# Dance Class Participant Safety Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from dance class participants about their medical conditions, emergency contacts, and acknowledgments of the class's safety information and emergency procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have understood the class's safety information and emergency procedures.
2. Fill in your personal information and any medical conditions or allergies that may affect your ability to participate in the class.
3. Indicate whether you have any allergies that may impact your participation in the class.
4. Provide the names and contact information of your emergency contacts.
5. Confirm that you have read and understood the class's safety information and emergency procedures.
6. Date and time-stamp your acknowledgement.
7. Provide any additional comments or concerns related to your acknowledgement.
8. Sign your name, confirming that you have read and understood the class's safety information and emergency procedures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info**: Please provide your name and any other relevant information.
* **Medical Info**: Please disclose any medical conditions or allergies that may affect your ability to participate in the class.
* **Allergies**: Indicate whether you have any allergies that may impact your participation in the class.
* **Emergency Contacts**: Provide the names and contact information of your emergency contacts.
* **Emergency Phone**: Provide your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Email**: Provide your emergency contact's email address.
* **Acknowledgement**: Confirm that you have read and understood the class's safety information and emergency procedures.
* **Acknowledgement Date**: Date and time-stamp your acknowledgement.
* **Acknowledgement Time**: Date and time-stamp your acknowledgement.
* **Acknowledgement Note**: Provide any additional comments or concerns related to your acknowledgement.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign your name, confirming that you have read and understood the class's safety information and emergency procedures.
* **Instructor Signature**: The instructor will sign their name, confirming that they have informed you of the class's safety information and emergency procedures.
* **Instructor Date**: The instructor will date their signature.
* **Instructor Time**: The instructor will time-stamp their signature.
* **Instructor Note**: The instructor may provide any additional comments or concerns related to your participation in the class.
