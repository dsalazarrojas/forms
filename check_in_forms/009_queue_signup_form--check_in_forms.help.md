# Queue Signup Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Queue Signup Form is used to collect information from customers or clients who wish to join a queue for services such as appointments, reservations, or other bookings. This form helps to gather essential details like contact information, service preferences, and queue number for efficient management of queue operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Email address in the email field.
2. Enter your Phone Number in the phone field.
3. Select your preferred Service Type from the dropdown menu.
4. Choose your desired Service Time for the queue.
5. Select a Preferred Time for your queue position.
6. Optionally, add any additional Notes about your visit or request.
7. Enter your Email Address to verify ownership.
8. Enter your Phone Number to verify ownership.
9. Enter a Queue Number, which will be assigned to you upon submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Queue Signup Form** (Queue Signup Form, text, required): This is the title of the form, and it's not a field that requires input.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address that you use to receive important updates and communications about your queue position.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for queue updates or notifications.
* **Service Type** (Service Type, select_one, optional): Select the type of service you require, such as appointment, reservation, or other bookings.
* **Service Time** (Service Time, time, required): Choose the time you would like to have your service.
* **Preferred Time** (Preferred Time, time, required): Select a specific time that you prefer for your queue position.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Enter any additional information about your visit or request, such as special requests or comments.
* **Verify Email** (Verify Email, email, required): Enter your email address again to verify ownership and ensure it matches your input in the Email field.
* **Verify Phone** (Verify Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number again to verify ownership and ensure it matches your input in the Phone Number field.
* **Queue Number** (Queue Number, number, required): Your queue number will be assigned to you upon submission and will be used to identify your position in the queue.
