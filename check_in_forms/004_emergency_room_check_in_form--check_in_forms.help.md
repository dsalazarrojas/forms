# Emergency Room Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Emergency Room Check In Form is used to collect information from patients and their emergency contacts in a quick and efficient manner during an emergency visit. This form is designed to gather essential details about the patient's medical history, reason for visit, and contact information. It helps the medical staff to quickly assess the situation and provide prompt treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and clear information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure all required information is provided.
3. If a field is marked as optional, provide the relevant information if possible, but it's not mandatory.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, optional): Enter the patient's basic details, such as their name and date of birth.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of the person who brought the patient to the emergency room.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions or allergies the patient has.
* **Reason for Visit** (`reason_for_visit`, select_one, optional): Select the reason for the patient's visit from the provided options (Headache, Fever, Broken Bone, or Other).
* **Emergency Room Number** (`emergency_room_number`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the number of the room where the patient is being treated.
* **Nurse Name** (`nurse_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the nurse who treated the patient.
* **Medical Staff Name** (`medical_staff_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medical staff member who treated the patient.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the patient's visit.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the patient's visit.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter any other phone numbers that may be relevant for contact.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields have been filled in.
* If you're unsure about any information, please ask the medical staff for assistance.
* If you need to add additional information, please provide it in the "Other" field under "Reason for Visit".
