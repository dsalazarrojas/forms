# Corporate Event Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees of corporate events, allowing event organizers to track attendee details in a structured and organized manner. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field, following the format: `example@example.com`.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
6. Enter your position or title in the "Position" field.
7. Select the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
8. Select the start time of the event in the "Event Start Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address in the format `example@example.com`. This will be used to contact you with event-related information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number for any important contact purposes.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company you represent or work for.
* **Position** (`position`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position within the company.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the event from the calendar.
* **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`, time, optional): Select the start time of the event from the 24-hour clock.

## Tips
Make sure to fill in all required fields (First Name, Last Name, and Email) to ensure that your event details are recorded accurately.
