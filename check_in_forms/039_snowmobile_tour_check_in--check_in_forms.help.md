# Snowmobile Tour Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snowmobile Tour Check In form is designed to collect participant information and medical details for a guided snowmobile tour.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all required fields carefully.
2. Ensure that you've provided accurate information for the participant and emergency contact.
3. Select "True" for the waiver acceptance if you've read and agree to the waiver.
4. Enter your participant's details (age, height, weight, email).
5. Enter your emergency contact's details (age, height, weight, email).
6. Specify the tour length and date.
7. Select the start and end time of the tour.
8. Sign the participant's signature if applicable.
9. Provide any necessary participant and emergency contact notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: Enter the participant's name, age, height, and weight to ensure we have accurate records.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the emergency contact's name, age, height, weight, and email to ensure we can contact them in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Specify the relationship between the participant and emergency contact.
* **Waiver Acceptance**: Select "True" to indicate that you've read and agree to the waiver.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign the participant's signature if applicable.
* **Medical Details**: Enter any medical conditions or treatments the participant is currently undergoing.
* **Tour Information**: Enter the tour length, date, start and end times.
* **Email**: Enter the participant and emergency contact's email addresses.
* **Medical Conditions**: Enter any medical conditions the participant may have.
* **Medical Treatment**: Enter any medical treatment the participant is currently undergoing.
* **Medical Prescription**: Enter any medical prescription the participant is taking.
* **Participant Notes**: Enter any additional notes for the participant.
* **Emergency Contact Notes**: Enter any additional notes for the emergency contact. 

Please complete the form carefully to ensure accurate information collection and a safe tour experience.
