# New Stylist Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Stylist Check In form is designed to collect essential information about new stylists, enabling our team to efficiently update their profiles and ensure smooth communication during their initial check-in process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as "Stylist" in the text field labeled "Stylist's Name".
2. Describe your style in a brief sentence or two in the text field labeled "Style".
3. Specify your level of experience in years as a number in the text field labeled "Stylist Experience".
4. Enter your email address in the email field labeled "Stylist Email".
5. Enter your phone number in the text field labeled "Stylist Phone".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stylist's Name** (New Stylist): Enter your name as a stylist.
* **Stylist's Style** (Style): Enter a brief description of your style, including any specific skills or techniques you bring to your work.
* **Stylist's Experience** (Stylist Experience): Enter your experience level as a stylist, measured in years.
* **Stylist Email** (new_stylist_email): Enter your email address for internal communication and updates.
* **Stylist Phone** (new_stylist_phone): Enter your phone number for internal communication and scheduling.

## Tips
This form is only to be completed once when you begin your first day at our salon. If you update your information, please contact us directly to ensure our records are accurate and up-to-date.
