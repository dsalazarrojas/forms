# Fitness Accountability Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Accountability Check In Form is designed to track and monitor users' fitness activities, providing a comprehensive overview of their progress and allowing for personalized feedback from administrators.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling out the General Information section, which includes:
	* **General Information**: A text field where you can provide any relevant background information about yourself.
2. Next, move on to the Exercise Frequency section, where you can report:
	* **Exercise Frequency**: A number field to record how often you exercise per week.
3. Continue by filling out the Exercise Duration section:
	* **Exercise Duration**: A number field to record how long you exercise per session.
4. The Exercise Intensity section is next:
	* **Exercise Intensity**: A text field where you can describe the intensity level of your workouts.
5. Then, report your Rest Time:
	* **Rest Time**: A number field to record how many hours you rest and recover each day.
6. Move on to the Water Intake section:
	* **Water Intake**: A text field where you can describe your daily water intake.
7. Provide your Nutrition Plan:
	* **Nutrition Plan**: A text field where you can describe your current nutrition plan.
8. Record your Sleep Quality:
	* **Sleep Quality**: A text field where you can describe your sleep quality and patterns.
9. Next, fill out the Weight and Height sections:
	* **Weight**: A number field to record your current weight.
	* **Height**: A number field to record your current height.
10. Provide your Workout Plan:
	* **Workout Plan**: A text field where you can describe your current workout plan.
11. Then, report your Workout Area:
	* **Workout Area**: A text field where you can describe the areas you workout in (e.g. legs, arms, etc.).
12. Record your Workout Intensity:
	* **Workout Intensity**: A text field where you can describe the intensity of your workouts.
13. Move on to the Pain Areas section:
	* **Pain Areas**: A text field where you can describe areas of your body that are currently experiencing pain.
14. Next, provide your Workout Notes:
	* **Workout Notes**: A text field where you can add any additional notes about your workouts.
15. Then, record your Exercise Notes:
	* **Exercise Notes**: A text field where you can add any additional notes about your exercises.
16. The Exercise Frequency Comments section is optional, but can be used to:
	* **Exercise Frequency Comments**: A text field where you can provide additional comments about your exercise frequency.
17. The Pain Comments section is optional, but can be used to:
	* **Pain Comments**: A text field where you can provide additional comments about your pain areas.
18. Finally, add any General Comments:
	* **General Comments**: A text field where you can provide any additional comments or information.
