# language_lesson_student_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record student information, session details, and attendance status for language lessons. It is intended for authorized personnel to track student progress accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Student Details section with the student's name and any other relevant information.
2. For each session, provide the Lesson Topic and choose whether it was attended (Yes/No).
3. Enter the Lesson Duration in minutes.
4. Specify the Session Date.
5. Enter the Session Time.
6. For each session, indicate whether the student was present or not (Student Attendance).
7. Record any additional notes about the student's performance.
8. Provide the School Email and School Phone number if available.
9. Specify who submitted the form and the time it was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Details**: Enter the student's name and any other relevant details.
* **Lesson Topic**: Select whether the session was attended (Yes/No) to track student attendance.
* **Lesson Duration**: Enter the duration of the lesson in minutes.
* **Session Date**: Enter the date of the session.
* **Session Time**: Enter the time of the session.
* **Student Attendance**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the student was present or not.
* **Student Notes**: Record any additional notes about the student's performance.
* **School Email**: Enter the school email address if available.
* **School Phone**: Enter the school phone number if available.
* **Form Submitted By**: Specify who submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted Time**: Enter the time the form was submitted.

## Tips
* Double-check student information for accuracy to avoid confusion.
* Ensure each session's details are correct to maintain a clear record.
* Complete each field carefully to avoid missing essential information.
* Review and submit the form only when required.
