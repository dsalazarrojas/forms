# Student Device Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Device Check In Form is designed to collect essential information about student devices during check-in and check-out procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the correct options for the device type and configuration.
3. Add any additional notes or comments if necessary.
4. Review and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your student ID number, if applicable.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name in this field.
* **Device Type** (`device_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of device you are checking in or out (e.g., Desktop, Laptop, or Tablet).
* **Device Model** (`device_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of your device.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text, optional): Enter the serial number of your device.
* **Device Status** (`device_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate the current status of your device (e.g., Checked In or Checked Out).
* **Check In Time** (`check_in_time`, date, optional): Record the time you checked in the device (if applicable).
* **Check Out Time** (`check_out_time`, date, optional): Record the time you checked out the device (if applicable).
* **Last Used** (`device_last_used`, date, optional): Note when the device was last used.
* **User ID** (`user_id`, text, optional): Enter your user ID number, if applicable.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or reasons for checking in or out the device.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any further comments or information about the device or its usage.
* **Student Name Check In** (`student_name_check_in`, note, optional): Note the student's name for the check-in process.
* **Device Check Out** (`device_check_out`, note, optional): Note the device's status for the check-out process.
* **Student Check Out** (`student_check_out`, note, optional): Note the student's status for the check-out process.
* **Device Status Check Out** (`device_status_check_out`, note, optional): Note the device's status for the check-out process.
* **Check In Device** (`check_in_device`, note, optional): Note the device's check-in status.
* **Check In Device Status** (`check_in_device_status`, note, optional): Note the device's check-in status.
* **Check Out Device** (`check_out_device`, note, optional): Note the device's check-out status.
* **Student Device Check In Form** (`student_device_check_in_form`, note, optional): A note field for general information about the check-in form.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, note, optional): A note field for general information about the form.

Tips:
* Ensure you have accurate and up-to-date information for each field before submitting the form.
* Use clear and concise language in the notes section to provide context and clarity.
* Double-check the device status and student information to avoid discrepancies.
* Save and review previous check-in/check-out records for easy tracking and reference.
