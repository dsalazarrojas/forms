# Art Class Student Project Progress Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Class Student Project Progress Check In form is designed to help students update their project progress in an art class. This form will help instructors track student's work, identify challenges, and provide support where needed. It's essential to fill this form out regularly to ensure you're on track to complete your project on time.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Click on the relevant section headers to open the corresponding fields.
2.  Complete each field according to the explanations below.
3.  Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked as "required: true" in the form.
4.  Double-check your responses before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Student Name** (text, required: true)
Enter your name as it appears in your student registration information.
### 2. **Student ID** (text, required: false)
If you have a student ID, please enter it here. If not, leave blank.
### 3. **Class Name** (text, required: true)
Enter the name of your art class, as assigned by your instructor.
### 4. **Check-In Date** (date, required: true)
Enter the date of your current check-in. This will help track your progress over time.
### 5. **Project Title** (text, required: true)
Provide a title for your current project, as assigned by your instructor or self-selected.
### 6. **Project Type or Medium** (select_one, required: true)
Select the type of project you're working on (e.g., Drawing, Painting, Sculpture).
### 7. **Brief Project Description** (text, required: true)
Give a concise description of your current project.
### 8. **Overall Progress Status** (select_one, required: true)
Choose one of the options to describe your current progress (On track, Slightly behind, Significantly behind, or Ahead of schedule).
### 9. **Project Completion Percentage** (number, required: true)
Enter a percentage value representing your current completion status.
### 10. **Current Work in Progress** (text, required: true)
Describe what you're actively working on at the moment.
### 11. **Recent Accomplishments or Milestones** (text, required: false)
List any notable achievements or milestones you've reached since your last check-in.
### 12. **Challenges or Obstacles Encountered** (text, required: false)
Describe any challenges or obstacles you've faced while working on your project.
### 13. **How have you addressed these challenges** (text, required: false)
Explain how you've overcome the challenges or obstacles mentioned above.
### 14. **Do you need assistance or feedback?** (select_one, required: true)
Choose if you need help or want feedback on your project.
### 15. **Details on Assistance Needed** (text, required: false)
If you need assistance, provide details on what type and level of help you require.
### 16. **Next Steps or Goals for This Project** (text, required: true)
Describe your next steps or goals for this project.
### 17. **Expected Completion Date** (date, required: false)
Enter the estimated date you expect to complete your project.
### 18. **Self-Assessment of Your Work** (select_one, required: true)
Choose how you self-assess your current progress (Excellent progress, Good progress, Satisfactory, Needs improvement).
### 19. **Additional Comments** (text, required: false)
Add any additional comments or notes about your project.

## Tips
*   Be honest about your progress and challenges.
*   Provide clear and concise answers to each question.
*   Double-check your responses before submitting.
*   Use this form as a regular check-in point to track your progress and receive feedback from instructors.
