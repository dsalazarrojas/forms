# Player Readiness Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Readiness Check In form is designed to collect necessary information from players to ensure they are ready for a specific event or competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out your athlete's name in the **Athlete Name** field.
2. Enter the date of the event or activity you will be participating in.
3. Select your **Availability** status for the event or activity.
4. Provide any additional **Notes** if required.
5. Enter your **Email** address for contact purposes.
6. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
7. Select your **Availability 2** status for any subsequent events or activities.
8. Add any additional **Notes 2** if required.
9. Enter your **Email 2** address for contact purposes.
10. Finally, enter your **Phone 2** number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Name** (`athlete_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it is written on your ID or registration.
* **Activity Date** (`activity_date`, date, required): The date of the event or activity you will be participating in.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, required): Select one or both of the options to indicate your availability for the event or activity.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about your participation or any special requirements.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Availability 2** (`availability_2`, select_one, required): Select your availability status for any subsequent events or activities.
* **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about your participation or any special requirements for subsequent events or activities.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, email, optional): Enter your secondary email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`, text, optional): Enter your secondary phone number for contact purposes.
