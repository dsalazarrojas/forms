# Library Patron Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about library patrons who have visited the library. This information will be used for internal tracking and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name.
2. Provide your Last Name.
3. Provide your Phone.
4. Provide your Email.
5. Select a Visit Date for your visit.
6. Select a Visit Time for your visit.
7. Select an Arrival Time for your visit.
8. Select a Leave Time for your visit.
9. Provide a Reason for your visit.
10. Provide the name of the Staff member who checked you in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (<code>first_name</code>):
* Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification.

### Last Name (<code>last_name</code>):
* Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification.

### Phone (<code>phone</code>):
* Enter your phone number as it is written on your phone or identification.

### Email (<code>email</code>)
* Enter your email address as it is written on your official identification or the one you have with your internet service provider.

### Visit Date (<code>visit_date</code>):
* Enter the date you visited the library.

### Visit Time (<code>visit_time</code>):
* Enter the time you visited the library, including the hour and minute.

### Arrival Time (<code>arrival_time</code>):
* Enter the time you arrived at the library.

### Leave Time (<code>leave_time</code>):
* Enter the time you left the library.

### Reason for Visit (<code>reason_for_visit</code>):
* Enter a brief description of the reason for your visit, if applicable.

### Staff Name (<code>staff_name</code>):
* Enter the name of the library staff member who checked you in.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you enter is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, please ask a library staff member for assistance.
