# Hotel Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from guests when they check in to the hotel. It helps us understand your identity and travel details so we can provide you with a comfortable and hassle-free stay. Please ensure you fill out this form accurately to avoid any issues during your stay.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details by filling out the text fields for "First Name" and "Last Name".
2. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your check-in date in the "Check In Date" field.
5. Select your check-out date in the "Check Out Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, **required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, **required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, **required**): Enter a valid email address that we can contact you with in case of any issues or updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, **required**): Enter your phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX` or `XXX XXX XXXX`.
* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, **required**): Select the date you plan to check in to the hotel.
* **Check Out Date** (`check_out_date`, **required**): Select the date you plan to check out of the hotel.

## Tips

* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to select your dates correctly, as this will affect the availability of rooms and services during your stay.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please contact us directly after filling out this form.
