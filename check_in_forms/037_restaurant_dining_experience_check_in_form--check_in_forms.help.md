# Restaurant Dining Experience Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Dining Experience Check In Form is designed to collect information from guests who have previously been in contact with the restaurant. This form ensures that the restaurant is prepared for the guest's visit and can provide the best possible dining experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Guest Check In page with your name and any other required information.
2. Select your preferred seating arrangement from the options provided.
3. If you have any special preferences or requests, please note them in the Guest Preferences field.
4. Provide the details of your expected dining experience, including the time you plan to arrive and the size of your party.
5. Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number, so that we can get in touch with you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Check In**: This is the first step in completing the form. Please fill out your name in this field.
* **Preferred Seating**: Select the seating arrangement that you prefer. Please note that the availability of seating options may vary.
* **Guest Preferences**: If you have any special requests or preferences, please list them here.
* **Dining Experience**: Describe your expected dining experience, including any specific requests or preferences.
* **Table Request**: If you have a specific table preference, please specify it here.
* **Arrival Time**: Enter the time you plan to arrive for your dining experience.
* **Party Size**: Indicate the number of people in your party.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, in case we need to contact you about your dining experience.
* **Phone Number**: This field is a redundant field and should be ignored. Please do not fill it out.
