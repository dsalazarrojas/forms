# GTF Weekly Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The GTF Weekly Check In form is designed to help users provide feedback, rate their coach's performance, and track their progress. This form is intended for regular check-ins to ensure that users are on track with their goals and provide valuable insights for coaches to improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form with your feedback, rating, and progress information.
2.  Ensure that all fields are filled out, but note that some fields are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Submitter feedback** (`submitter_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any feedback or comments about the experience.
    *   This field allows you to express your thoughts and opinions about the session or coaching service provided.
*   **Coach rating** (`coach_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your coach's performance out of, say, 5 or 10 points.
    *   Use this field to assess how well the coach understood your needs, communicated, and helped you achieve your goals.
*   **Overall health** (`overall_health`, `text`, required: false): Describe your overall health and wellness at this time.
    *   This field is intended for the user to report their overall state, not just physical but also mental and emotional.
*   **Coach feedback** (`coach_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide feedback for your coach.
    *   Use this field to tell your coach something they can improve on or to express appreciation for their help.
*   **Health progress** (`health_progress`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    *   Choose one of the following options: Progress 0 - 25%, Progress 26 - 50%, Progress 51 - 75%, Progress 76 - 100%.
    *   This field is meant to gauge your progress toward your goals, with higher percentages indicating greater success.
*   **Overall progress** (`overall_progress`, `text`, required: false): Describe your overall progress.
    *   This field allows you to give a brief overview of your progress, whether it's toward physical, mental, or emotional goals.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any comments or notes.
    *   Use this field to share any thoughts, observations, or insights you've had during or after the session.

## Tips
*   Ensure that you're honest in your assessments and feedback to help coaches improve their services.
*   Be specific and detailed in your comments and feedback to help the coach understand your needs and concerns.
*   This form is a tool for personal development, so use it regularly to track your progress.
