# Office Employee Safety Check-in Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help office employees check in daily, providing essential information about their status and whereabouts for the benefit of the workplace safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select your current status**:
	* Choose between "Remote", "Onsite", or "Other" to indicate your current location and availability.
2. **Enter your location**:
	* Type the exact location where you are currently (if you're onsite, this is where you report).
3. **Enter your name**:
	* Type your full name as it appears on your company ID or personnel records.
4. **Enter your department**:
	* Type the department you belong to or are responsible for.
5. **Check-in date**:
	* Select the date of your check-in.
6. **Check-in time**:
	* Select the time of your check-in.
7. **Onsite status**:
	* Indicate your onsite status by selecting one or more options from the list, such as "Onsite", "Confirmed Absent", or "Unconfirmed Absent".
8. **Onsite location** (if applicable):
	* Type the specific location you are at, if you are onsite.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest about your status and location to ensure the safety and well-being of yourself and your colleagues.
* If you are unsure about your onsite status or location, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* Regularly check in daily to keep your colleagues and management informed about your whereabouts and status.
