# monthly_coaching_call_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The monthly_coaching_call_check_in_form is used to record information about coaching calls. This form is intended to be filled out by coaches after each coaching call to track and analyze data over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the details of the coaching call, including the call date and any additional resources used.
2. Select the coaching call outcome from the dropdown list.
3. Enter the next steps for the participant.
4. Provide a brief summary of the coaching call in the coaching call notes field.
5. Enter the duration of the coaching call.
6. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Coaching Call Details**: A text field where you can enter the details of the coaching call.
* **Goals**: A dropdown list of pre-defined goals to select from.
* **Call Date**: A date field where you can select the date of the coaching call.
* **Coaching Call Notes**: A text field where you can enter any additional information about the coaching call.
* **Next Steps**: A text field where you can enter the next steps for the participant.
* **Coaching Call Duration**: A number field where you can enter the duration of the coaching call.
* **Coaching Call Outcome**: A dropdown list of pre-defined outcomes to select from.
* **Additional Resources**: A text field where you can enter any additional resources used during the coaching call.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* Only one form should be filled out per coaching call.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please reach out to your supervisor or support team.
* This form is a critical tool for tracking and analyzing coaching call data. Please fill it out thoroughly and accurately.
