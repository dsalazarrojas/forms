# Volunteer Activity Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information about volunteer activities, including check-in details, volunteer status, and shift information. It helps administrators track volunteer hours, manage shifts, and monitor volunteer engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields.
2. Provide your **Email** address, which will be used for communication.
3. Enter your **Phone** number for contact purposes.
4. Select your **Check In** status: Check In, Late Check In, or Not Attending.
5. Leave any additional **Comments** you may have.
6. Specify your **Volunteer Role**.
7. Choose the **Department** you are working in.
8. Identify your **Team Leader** (if applicable).
9. Report your **Volunteer Hours** worked.
10. Enter the **Volunteer Date** and **Volunteer Time** you worked.
11. Add any **Volunteer Comments** about your experience.
12. Enter the **Event Name** and **Shift** details.
13. Specify the **Shift Start** and **Shift End** times.
14. Choose your **Volunteer Status**: Active, Inactive, or Retired.
15. Select your **Volunteer Group** (if applicable).
16. Add any **Notes** about your shift.
17. Report the **Shift Hours** worked.
18. Specify the **Shift Length**.
19. Choose your **Volunteer Type**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Check In**: Select your check-in status: Check In, Late Check In, or Not Attending.
* **Comments**: Leave any additional comments about your experience.
* **Volunteer Role**: Specify your role within the volunteer team.
* **Department**: Choose the department you are working in.
* **Team Leader**: Identify your team leader (if applicable).
* **Volunteer Hours**: Report the hours you worked.
* **Volunteer Date**: Enter the date you worked.
* **Volunteer Time**: Enter the time you worked.
* **Volunteer Comments**: Add any comments about your experience.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you participated in.
* **Shift**: Specify the shift you worked.
* **Shift Start**: Enter the start time of your shift.
* **Shift End**: Enter the end time of your shift.
* **Volunteer Status**: Choose your volunteer status: Active, Inactive, or Retired.
* **Volunteer Group**: Select your volunteer group (if applicable).
* **Notes**: Add any notes about your shift.
* **Shift Hours**: Report the hours you worked on your shift.
* **Shift Length**: Specify the length of your shift in hours.
* **Volunteer Type**: Choose your volunteer type.

Note: Some fields are optional and may not be relevant to your specific situation.
