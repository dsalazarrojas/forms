# university_student_course_load_check_in - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Student Course Load Check-in form is designed to help track the course load and enrollment status of university students. It's a crucial tool for ensuring students' accurate enrollment and providing necessary support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct semester for the student's enrollment.
2. Enter the student's ID number.
3. Enter the student's name.
4. Choose the student's grade level (Undergraduate, Graduate, or Other).
5. Enter the student's course ID and course name.
6. Enter the credit hours for the course.
7. Choose the enrollment status (Currently Enrolled, Dropped, or Other).
8. Enter the date and time of the check-in.
9. Add any comments or notes about the student's enrollment.
10. Enter the student's email and phone number (if applicable).
11. Select any support needs the student requires (Academic Support, Financial Support, or Other).
12. Describe the support needs in detail.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Semester**: Select the correct semester for the student's enrollment. This field is used to determine which semester's courses the student is enrolled in.
* **Student ID**: Enter the unique student ID number. This field is used to identify the student in the system.
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on record.
* **Student Grade Level**: Choose the student's grade level (Undergraduate, Graduate, or Other). This field helps determine the student's program and eligibility for certain support services.
* **Course ID**: Enter the course ID number.
* **Course Name**: Enter the course name as it appears in the course catalog.
* **Credit Hours**: Enter the credit hours for the course. This field is used to track course load and determine student progress.
* **Enrollment Status**: Choose the current enrollment status (Currently Enrolled, Dropped, or Other). This field helps track student progress and provide support accordingly.
* **Check-in Date**: Enter the date of the check-in event.
* **Check-in Time**: Enter the time of the check-in event.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or notes about the student's enrollment. This field is for additional context or information that may be relevant to the student's support.
* **Email**: Enter the student's email address (if applicable).
* **Phone**: Enter the student's phone number (if applicable).
* **Support Needs**: Select any support needs the student requires (Academic Support, Financial Support, or Other). This field helps identify areas where the student needs assistance.
* **Support Type**: Describe the support needs in detail. This field provides more information about the type of support required.
