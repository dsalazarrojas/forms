# Telepsychology Session Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate check-ins during telepsychology sessions, ensuring that both the provider and patient are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Patient Name** in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the date of the session in the "Date" field.
3. Input the time of the session in the "Time" field.
4. Provide any necessary **Session Notes** in the "Session Notes" field.
5. Specify the **Patient Status** in the "Patient Status" field.
6. Schedule the next **Next Session** in the "Next Session" field.
7. Enter any **Provider Notes** in the "Provider Notes" field.
8. Update your **Provider Status** in the "Provider Status" field.
9. Summarize the **Outcome** of the session in the "Outcome" field.
10. Add any notes for the **Next Session** in the "Next Session Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: A required field where you will enter the name of the patient you are interacting with.
* **Date**: A required field where you will enter the date of the session.
* **Time**: A required field where you will enter the time of the session.
* **Session Notes**: A field where you can provide any notes or observations about the session.
* **Patient Status**: A field where you can update the status of the patient.
* **Next Session**: A field where you schedule the next session.
* **Provider Notes**: A field where you can provide any notes or observations from the session.
* **Provider Status**: A field where you can update your status as a provider.
* **Outcome**: A field where you can summarize the outcome of the session.
* **Next Session Notes**: A field where you can add any notes for the next session.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary information and authorization before completing this form.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Use this form as a guide to facilitate effective communication and collaboration with your patients.
