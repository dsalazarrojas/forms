# Driver Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for drivers to check in and provide information about their pick-up and delivery dates and times.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Pick the check-in date and time for your scheduled pick-up.
2. Pick the delivery date and time for your scheduled drop-off.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Check-in-date** (`check_in_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of your scheduled pick-up.
- **Check-in-time** (`check_in_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of your scheduled pick-up.
- **Delivery-date** (`delivery_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of your scheduled drop-off.
- **Delivery-time** (`delivery_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of your scheduled drop-off.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the dates and times before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any of the information, please contact your supervisor or administrator for assistance.

This guide is designed to be concise and easy to follow, with a clear explanation of each field and how to use it. It assumes that users understand the basic concepts of dates and times. If users have any further questions or need clarification, they can refer to this guide for assistance.
