# Virtual Preschool Academy Form November Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from students in the Virtual Preschool Academy for the month of November. It aims to understand their satisfaction levels with our virtual program and identify areas where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last" field.
3. Provide your **email address** in the "email" field.
4. Enter your **phone number** in the "phone" field.
5. Choose how **satisfied** you are with our virtual program from the "How satisfied are you with our virtual program?" dropdown menu.
6. Select any options that you think we could **improve** by choosing all relevant options from the "What are some of the things that we could do better?" dropdown menu.
7. If you'd like to add any additional comments or suggestions, you can do so in the "note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last** (text): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **email** (email): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **phone** (text): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **How satisfied are you with our virtual program?** (select_one): Choose how satisfied you are with our virtual program. Options include:
	+ Not at all
	+ Somewhat
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
* **What are some of the things that we could do better?** (select_multiple): Select all options that you think we could improve on.
* **note** (note): Add any additional comments or suggestions you have for us.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date you are completing this form. (Optional)
* **Time** (time): Enter the time you are completing this form. (Optional)

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to help us understand your experience.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please let us know in the "note" field.
* If you've already submitted this form, please do not submit again.
