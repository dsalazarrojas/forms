# Stylist Monthly Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stylist Monthly Check In Form is a vital tool for stylists to regularly update their status and provide essential information about themselves and their activities. This form helps to ensure accuracy and consistency in tracking and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Title** field with a brief description of your current status or activity.
2. Enter your **Client Name** to associate your check-in with your assigned clients.
3. Provide your **Email Address** as it appears on your professional profile or business card.
4. Fill in your **Phone Number** if you'd like to be contacted by us or your clients.
5. Add any **Notes** about your current status or activity, such as ongoing projects or notable events.
6. Select your **Date** of last check-in using the date field.
7. Optionally, enter your **Time** of last check-in if relevant.
8. Enter your **Location** where you're working or last checked in from, if applicable.
9. Choose your **Status** from the dropdown menu:
	* **New**: If you've recently joined or started a new project.
	* **Current**: If you're actively working on ongoing tasks.
	* **Lost**: If you've lost contact or are no longer working on a project.
	* **Missed**: If you've missed a scheduled check-in or appointment.
10. Add any **Comments** about your current status or activity, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (1, text, optional): A brief description of your current status or activity.
* **Client Name** (2, text, optional): The name of your assigned client or project.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): Your professional email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, optional): Your contact phone number for communication.
* **Notes** (5, text, optional): Any additional information about your current status or activity.
* **Date** (6, date, required): The date of your last check-in or activity.
* **Time** (7, time, optional): The time of your last check-in or activity.
* **Location** (8, text, optional): Where you're working or last checked in from, if applicable.
* **Status** (9, select_one, optional): Your current status:
	+ **New**: If you've recently joined or started a new project.
	+ **Current**: If you're actively working on ongoing tasks.
	+ **Lost**: If you've lost contact or are no longer working on a project.
	+ **Missed**: If you've missed a scheduled check-in or appointment.
* **Comments** (10, text, optional): Any additional comments about your current status or activity.
