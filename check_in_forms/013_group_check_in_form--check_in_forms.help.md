# Group Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for check-in purposes by groups arriving at a site. It helps staff collect necessary information from the group.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the relevant information about your group. Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Group Name** (`group_check_in_form_group_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your group.
* **Group Size** (`group_check_in_form_group_size`, number, required): Enter the number of people in your group.
* **Email** (`group_check_in_form_email`, email, required): Enter your group's email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`group_check_in_form_phone`, text, required): Enter your group's phone number.
* **Arrival Time** (`group_check_in_form_arrival_time`, time, required): Enter your group's estimated arrival time.
* **Arrival Date** (`group_check_in_form_arrival_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your group's arrival.
* **Arrival Location** (`group_check_in_form_arrival_location`, text, required): Enter the location of your group's arrival.
* **Additional Info** (`group_check_in_form_additional_info`, text, optional): Write anything else we should know about your group. 

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about your group. This will help us serve you better. 
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
