# RV Service Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the check-in process for RV service requests. It gathers essential information from the owner to provide efficient and effective service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your owner information, including your name and phone number (if desired).
2. Select the type of service you need, such as oil change, tire rotation, etc. (at least one service type must be selected).
3. Choose the time you would like to have your RV serviced at, with options from 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
4. Select the person or department this service will be assigned to (at least one must be selected).
5. Add any additional comments or notes regarding your service request.
6. If applicable, provide your email address and payment method (cash, credit, visa, mastercard, or american express).
7. Finally, select the type of vehicle you own (at least one type must be selected).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **owner_info** (text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant owner information.
* **vehicle_info** (text, optional): Enter your vehicle's details, such as make, model, and year.
* **service_request** (text, optional): Describe the specific service you need, such as oil change or brake pads replacement.
* **check_in_time** (select_one, optional): Choose the time you would like to have your RV serviced at, from 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
* **assigned_to** (select_one, required): Select the person or department this service will be assigned to (at least one must be selected).
* **comments** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes regarding your service request.
* **email** (email, optional): Provide your email address for future communication.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **service_notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments for the service team.
* **payment_method** (select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred payment method (cash, credit, visa, mastercard, or american express).
* **service_type** (select_multiple, required): Select at least one type of service you need, such as oil change or tire rotation.
* **notes** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **vehicle_type** (select_one, required): Select the type of vehicle you own, such as car, truck, motorcycle, etc.
* **owner_name** (text, optional): Enter your name if you want to be contacted by name.
* **owner_phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.

**Tips**

* Make sure to select at least one service type and vehicle type to ensure your request is processed correctly.
* Enter any additional comments or notes if you have specific requests or concerns.
* If you have any issues or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
