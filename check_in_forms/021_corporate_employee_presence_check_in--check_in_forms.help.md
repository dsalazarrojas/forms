# corporate_employees_presence_check_in - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate employees presence check-in form is a tool used to track employees' presence in the workplace. It helps ensure accurate records and efficient management of employee attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Employee Check In field with the employee's ID or name.
2. In the Employee Info field, provide any necessary details about the employee's status (e.g., "present" or "not present").
3. Click the Submit Information button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Check In** (`employee_check_in`, text, required):
  This field is where you enter the employee's ID or name for reference.
* **Employee Info** (`employee_info`, text, optional): 
  In this field, enter any relevant details about the employee's status, such as "present" or "not present".
* **Submit Information** (`submit_info`, text, required):
  Click this button to submit the form.
