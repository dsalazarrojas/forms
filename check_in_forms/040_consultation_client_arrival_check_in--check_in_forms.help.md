# Consultation Client Arrival Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consultation Client Arrival Check In form is used to record the arrival information of clients at a consultation. It includes details about the client's name, appointment time, arrival time, the service requested, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the form title, "Consultation Client Arrival Check In," to access the form.
2. Enter the full name of the client arriving in the "Client name" field.
3. Select the scheduled appointment time from the time picker for "Appointment time."
4. If applicable, enter the actual arrival time in the "Arrival time" field.
5. Briefly describe the consultation service in the "Service requested" field.
6. If a staff member is handling the check-in, enter their name in the "Check in staff" field.
7. Confirm if any waivers were signed during the check-in process by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Waivers signed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client name** (`client_name`, text, required): This field is for recording the full name of the client arriving for the consultation. Please enter the client's name as it should be spelled.
* **Appointment time** (`appointment_time`, time, required): This field is for selecting the scheduled appointment time using the time picker.
* **Arrival time** (`arrival_time`, time, optional): This field is for recording the actual arrival time of the client, if different from the scheduled appointment time.
* **Service requested** (`service_requested`, text, optional): This field is for a brief description of the consultation service being provided.
* **Check in staff** (`check_in_staff`, text, optional): This field is for recording the name of the staff member handling the check-in.
* **Waivers signed** (`waivers_signed`, select_one, optional): This field is for confirming whether waivers were signed during the check-in process. Please select "True" to indicate that waivers were signed and "False" otherwise.

## Tips
* Ensure that the client's name is spelled correctly.
* If the client's arrival time is different from the scheduled appointment time, please record the correct arrival time.
* Briefly and concisely describe the consultation service to ensure accurate recording.
* Select the correct staff member handling the check-in if applicable.
* Confirm the waiver status accurately to ensure correct documentation.
