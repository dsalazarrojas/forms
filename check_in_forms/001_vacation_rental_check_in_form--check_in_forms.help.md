# Vacation Rental Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacation Rental Check In Form is designed to gather essential information from guests checking in to a vacation rental property. This form is typically used by property owners or managers to collect contact and reservation details from arriving guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all necessary information ready, as some fields are required for a successful check-in process.
2. Start by entering the **Check In Date**, which is the date you are arriving at the property.
3. Fill in your **Guest Name** and **Guest Phone** for contact information.
4. Enter your **Guest Email** address, if you have one.
5. Provide the **Check Out Date**, which is the date you plan to depart from the property.
6. Indicate the **Rental Property** where you will be staying.
7. If you have any additional information or requests for your stay, you can include a **Guest Note** in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are arriving at the property.
- **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or reservation confirmation.
- **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you have one, this will be used for communication purposes.
- **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Check Out Date** (`check_out_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to depart from the property.
- **Rental Property** (`rental_property`, text, optional): Identify the property where you will be staying.
- **Guest Note** (`guest_note`, note, optional): Provide any additional information or requests you may have for your stay.
