# Office Vendor Visit Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record the arrival time and details of visiting vendors to the office.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Open the form and start by entering the details of the vendor visit.
*   In the first field, "Vendor Visit", enter a brief description of the visit.
*   In the second field, "Arrival Time", enter the time at which the vendor arrived at the office.
*   In the third field, "Vendor Details", enter any relevant details about the vendor, such as their contact person or contact number.
*   In the fourth field, "Visitor ID", enter the unique identifier of the vendor if available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Vendor Visit**
`text` (`1`, required: false)
This field is for providing a brief description of the visit, including the name of the vendor and any relevant context.
### **Arrival Time**
`time` (`2`, required: false)
Enter the exact time at which the vendor arrived at the office. Use the format `HH:MM`.
### **Vendor Details**
`text` (`3`, required: false)
Enter any relevant details about the vendor, such as their contact person or contact number.
### **Visitor ID**
`text` (`4`, required: false)
Enter the unique identifier of the vendor, if available. This can help with tracking and reporting purposes.

## Tips
*   Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in each field to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
*   Complete the form as soon as possible after the vendor visit, while the details are still fresh in your mind.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to your office's policies and procedures for guidance.
