# Resort Guest Special Request Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for guests to make special requests for their stay at the resort. It is used to capture important information about their arrival and contact preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it should appear on your room key.
2. If you have any special requests or comments, describe them in the "Special Request" field.
3. Select the date you plan to arrive at the resort from the calendar.
4. Choose the time you plan to arrive at the resort from the available options.
5. Select the method by which you would like to be contacted (either by phone or email).
6. If you would like to be contacted by email, enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. If you would like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (text): Your name as it should appear on your room key. This is the name we will use to address you during your stay.
* **Special Request** (text): If you have any special requests or comments, such as a preferred room location or amenities you would like to request, enter them here.
* **Arrival Date** (date): Select the date you plan to arrive at the resort from the calendar.
* **Arrival Time** (time): Choose the time you plan to arrive at the resort from the available options.
* **Contact Method** (select_one): Select how you would like to be contacted (phone or email). This will determine how we will contact you before your arrival.
* **Email** (email): If you selected phone as your contact method, you can enter your phone number here. This is not required, but we will use it to contact you if you do not answer your phone.
* **Phone** (text): If you selected email as your contact method, you can enter your phone number here. This is not required, but we will use it to contact you if you do not answer your email.

## Tips
* Make sure to include your arrival date and time so we can prepare for your arrival.
* If you have any special requests, such as a preferred room location or amenities you would like to request, be as detailed as possible.
* If you select phone as your contact method, ensure your phone number is correct and that you will receive notifications.
* If you select email as your contact method, ensure your email address is correct and that you check it regularly.
