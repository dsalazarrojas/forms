# Seminar Attendee Feedback Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees of a seminar, workshop, or training session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" if you attended the seminar, and "No" if you did not attend.
2. Enter the date of the session you attended, if applicable.
3. Enter the time of the session you attended, if applicable.
4. Enter the name of the session you attended.
5. Enter your name, as it should appear on the certificate of attendance.
6. Share your feedback and opinions about the seminar.
7. Provide any additional feedback or comments.
8. Indicate your status as an attendee by selecting "Attendee" or "Not Attendee".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seminar Attendee Feedback Check In Form** (`seminar_attendee_feedback_check_in_form`, select_multiple): 
  * Select "Yes" if you attended the seminar, and "No" if you did not attend.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): 
  * Enter the date of the session you attended.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): 
  * Enter the time of the session you attended.
* **Session Name** (`session_name`, text, optional): 
  * Enter the name of the session you attended.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): 
  * Enter your name, as it should appear on the certificate of attendance.
* **Attendee Feedback** (`attendee_feedback`, text, optional): 
  * Share your feedback and opinions about the seminar.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): 
  * Provide any additional feedback or comments.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, optional): 
  * This field is for your signature, but it is not required.
* **Attendee Status** (`attendee_status`, select_one): 
  * Select "Attendee" if you attended the seminar, and "Not Attendee" if you did not.
