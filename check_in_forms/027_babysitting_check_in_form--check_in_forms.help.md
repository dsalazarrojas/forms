# Babysitting Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by babysitters to record essential information from parents or guardians about the child, ensuring a safe and healthy experience for both the child and the sitter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out your information and that of your child.
* Enter your name, your child's name, and your relationship to the child.
* Provide the time you will be dropping off your child.
* If applicable, list any allergies or medications that your child may have.
* Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
* Specify any special instructions for the sitter to follow.
* List the names of any adults authorized to pick up your child.
* Sign your name to authorize the check-in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Parent name (`parent_name`, text, required)
Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child.

### Child name (`child_name`, text, required)
Enter the full name of the child.

### Check in time (`check_in_time`, time, required)
Enter the time you will be dropping off your child.

### Expected pickup time (`expected_pickup_time`, time, required/optional)
Enter the approximate time you expect to pick up your child. If unknown, leave blank.

### Allergies (`allergies`, text, required/optional)
List any known allergies or sensitivities your child may have.

### Medications to be administered (`medications`, text, required/optional)
List the medication name, dosage, and any specific administration instructions your child may require.

### Emergency contact name and phone (`emergency_contact`, text, required)
Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.

### Special instructions for sitter (`special_instructions`, text, required/optional)
Leave any special instructions or routines you would like the sitter to follow.

### Authorized pickup people (`authorized_pickup_people`, text, required)
List the names of any adults authorized to pick up your child.

### Parent signature name (`signature`, text, required)
Enter your name, which authorizes the sitter to take care of your child.

## Tips
* Ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you need help, contact the form administrators at form@gic.mx.
