# Tourism Services Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from tourists who have used the tourism services offered by the organization. The form aims to gather information about their overall experience, satisfaction with the services used, and any additional comments or feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions honestly and accurately. The form is designed to be self-explanatory, but if any questions or doubts arise, please don't hesitate to contact the organization for assistance. Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tour Feedback** (`tour_feedback`, text, optional): This field is designed to collect your feedback about the tour you took. Please provide a brief description of your experience.
* **Services Used** (`services_used`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the services you used during your tour from the list provided. This will help us understand your experience with our services.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall experience with our services.
* **Tour Guide** (`tour_guide`, text, optional): If you had a tour guide, please provide their name, phone number, and email. This will help us understand who provided your tour and how to contact them.
* **Satisfactory Services** (`services_satisfactory`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the services that you found satisfactory during your tour.
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_one, optional): Rate the communication from our staff or tour guide during your tour.
* **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness`, select_one, optional): Rate the cleanliness of the tour facilities or equipment used during your tour.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, optional): Rate the value you got from the services compared to the price you paid.
* **Tour Schedule** (`tour_schedule`, select_one, optional): Rate the schedule of your tour.
* **Tour Duration** (`tour_duration`, text, optional): If you had a tour with a specific duration, please provide the actual duration.
* **Tour Price** (`tour_price`, number, optional): If you paid a specific price for the tour, please provide the amount.
* **Recommend Tourism Service** (`recommend_tourism_service`, select_one, optional): Do you recommend our tourism services to others?
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Please provide any additional feedback or comments you have about your tour.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions for improvement, please provide them here.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, note, optional): If you would like to be contacted by our team, please provide your contact information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you would like to be contacted by our team, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by our team, please provide your phone number.
* **Other Feedback** (`other_feedback`, text, optional): If you have any other feedback or comments, please provide them here.
* **Submit Feedback** (`submit_feedback`, text, optional): Please click this button to submit your feedback.
* **Additional Services Used** (`services_used_optional`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional services you used during your tour.
