# Golf Trip Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from golfers about their preferences for a golf trip. The purpose of the form is to understand what golfers are looking for in a golf trip, such as the distance they are willing to travel, their preferred practice course, travel mode, and how often they would travel.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select your response to each question from the provided options.
2. For date and time fields, use the standard date and time format (e.g. MM/DD/YYYY or 12:00 PM).
3. If you have any other preferences or comments that are not listed, you can enter them in the "Golf Trip Purpose Other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Course Selection**: Select "Yes" if you would like to play at your home course. This will help us to understand if you are interested in playing at your home course or not.
* **How Far From Home**: Select all distances that apply to you, such as "Yes" for traveling up to 100 miles, 100-200 miles, 200-300 miles, etc.
* **Practice Course**: Select "Yes" if you would like to practice at a course other than your home course. This will help us to understand if you are interested in practicing at a different course or not.
* **Practice Distance**: Select all distances that apply to you, such as "Yes" for traveling up to 100 miles, 100-200 miles, 200-300 miles, etc.
* **Travel Mode**: Select "Yes" if you prefer to travel by car, plane, or public transport.
* **How Often Would You Travel**: Select all frequencies that apply to you, such as "Yes" for weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, etc.
* **Practice Day**: Select the day of the week you prefer to practice.
* **Practice Time**: Select the time of day you prefer to practice.
* **Golf Trip Purpose**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in golf trips for relaxation, socializing, or other purposes.
* **Golf Trip Purpose Other**: If you have any other preferences or comments that are not listed, you can enter them here.
