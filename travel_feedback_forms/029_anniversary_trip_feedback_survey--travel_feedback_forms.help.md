# Anniversary Trip Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from guests who have participated in an anniversary trip, to help us understand their experience and improve future trips.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Start by providing your personal details on the first page.
2.  Proceed to the second page and fill in the required fields for trip details.
3.  On the third page, rate your experience with the accommodation, activities, and food.
4.  On the fourth page, rate your experience with the service and overall satisfaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Guest Name** (`primary_guest_name`, `text`, required): The primary person in your couple.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for follow-up communications.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Your contact number for follow-up communications.
*   **Trip Dates** (`trip_dates`, `text`, optional): When did your trip occur.
*   **Destination** (`destination`, `text`, required): Where did you travel.
*   **Trip Duration** (`trip_duration`, `select_one`, required): Choose the duration of your trip.
*   **Type of Package** (`package_type`, `select_one`, optional): What kind of trip was booked.
*   **Package Name** (`package_name`, `text`, optional): The specific package or tour name.
*   **Hotel or Resort Rating** (`accommodation_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your lodging experience.
*   **Room Quality and Cleanliness** (`room_quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate the room quality and cleanliness.
*   **Amenities and Facilities** (`amenities_satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the available facilities.
*   **Quality of Activities and Excursions** (`activities_quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate the planned activities.
*   **Were the Activities What You Expected** (`activities_available`, `select_one`, optional): Did the activities match your expectations.
*   **Romantic Atmosphere and Setting** (`romantic_atmosphere`, `select_one`, required): Was it romantic and intimate.
*   **Food Quality and Variety** (`food_quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate the dining experience.
*   **Dining Options and Variety** (`dining_variety`, `select_one`, optional): Were there enough options.
*   **Staff Service** (`staff_service`, `select_one`, required): Rate the staff service.
*   **Overall Trip Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall experience.

Note: Please answer the questions as honestly as possible, and provide as much detail as you can. If you have any issues or concerns, contact us for assistance.
