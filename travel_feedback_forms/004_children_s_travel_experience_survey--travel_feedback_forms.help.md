# Children's Travel Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about a child's recent travel experience, including details about the trip and feedback on various aspects of the travel experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section one by one.
2. Provide accurate and honest answers to all required questions.
3. You can skip non-required fields if they do not apply to your situation.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
1. **Traveler Information**
	* (`traveler_information`, note, not required): This section is to provide general information about the young traveler. Please describe who is filling out this form.
2. **Child's Name**
	* (`child_name`, text, required): Please enter the child's name.
3. **Child's Age**
	* (`child_age`, number, required): Please enter the child's age.
4. **Parent or Guardian Name**
	* (`parent_name`, text, required): Please enter your name or the name of the child's guardian.
5. **How many people traveled together**
	* (`family_size`, number, required): Please enter the number of people who traveled with the child.
6. **Trip Details**
	* (`trip_details`, note, not required): This section is to provide information about the child's recent trip.
7. **Destination**
	* (`destination`, text, required): Please enter the city, state, or country that you visited.
8. **When did you travel**
	* (`travel_dates`, text, required): Please enter the approximate dates of the trip.
9. **How long was your trip**
	* (`trip_duration`, select_one, required): Please select the duration of your trip from the options provided.
10. **What type of trip was this**
	* (`travel_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of trip from the options provided.
11. **Transportation Feedback**
	* (`transportation_feedback`, note, not required): This section is to provide feedback on the child's transportation experience.
12. **How did you travel to your destination**
	* (`transportation_method`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the methods of transportation used during the trip.
13. **How would you rate the transportation experience**
	* (`transportation_rating`, select_one, required): Please rate the child's transportation experience from 1 to 5, with 1 being Poor and 5 being Excellent.
14. **Where did you stay**
	* (`accommodation_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of accommodation used during the trip.
15. **How would you rate where you stayed**
	* (`accommodation_rating`, select_one, required): Please rate the child's accommodation experience from 1 to 5, with 1 being Poor and 5 being Excellent.
16. **Activities and Experiences**
	* (`activities_feedback`, note, not required): This section is to provide feedback on the child's activities and experiences during the trip.
17. **What activities did the child enjoy most**
	* (`activities_enjoyed`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the activities that the child enjoyed during the trip.
18. **What was the child's favorite part of the trip**
	* (`favorite_part`, text, required): Please enter the child's favorite part of the trip.
19. **What was the least favorite part of the trip**
	* (`least_favorite_part`, text, not required): Please enter the child's least favorite part of the trip.
20. **Overall Satisfaction**
	* (`overall_satisfaction`, note, not required): This section is to provide feedback on the child's overall satisfaction with the trip.
21. **How would you rate this travel experience**
	* (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Please rate the child's travel experience from 1 to 5, with 1 being Poor and 5 being Excellent.
22. **Would you recommend this destination to other families**
	* (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Please select whether or not you would recommend this destination to other families.
23. **What could have made this trip better**
	* (`improvements_suggested`, text, not required): Please enter any suggestions you have for how this trip could be improved.
24. **Do you plan to travel to similar destinations in the future**
	* (`future_travel_plans`, select_one, not required): Please select whether or not you plan to travel to similar destinations in the future. 

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
* Your feedback will help us improve travel experiences for children in the future.
* Please take your time and answer each question thoroughly.
* If you need to leave a question out, please make sure to indicate it as 'Not applicable' or 'Not available'.
* This survey is intended to be completed once the trip is finished.
* We appreciate your participation and feedback.
