# Trip Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from travelers about their experiences on trips. Your input will help us understand what we can do better to enhance your future experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Select a date format that matches your trip dates (MM/DD or DD/MM).
2. Enter the location where you went on your trip.
3. Provide your feedback about your experience.
4. Choose the services you received during your trip (Service 1, Service 2, Service 3).
5. If you have any additional comments, write them in the note section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trip Dates (trip_dates)** (`date`, Optional): Please select the dates of your trip in MM/DD or DD/MM format.
* **Trip Location (trip_location)** (`text`, Optional): Enter the location where you went on your trip.
* **Client Feedback (client_feedback)** (`text`, Optional): Share your thoughts and opinions about your experience.
* **Services Provided (services_provided)** (`select_one`, Optional): Select the services you received during your trip (Service 1, Service 2, Service 3).
* **Note (note)** (`note`, Optional): If you have any additional comments, write them here.

## Tips
* Please provide honest and constructive feedback to help us improve our services.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* You can fill out this form for any trip you'd like to provide feedback on.
