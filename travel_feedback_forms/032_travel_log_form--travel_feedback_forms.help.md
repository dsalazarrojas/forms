# Travel Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Log Form is used to collect travel information for a company or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
* Select the form title as "Travel Log Form"
* Choose "Yes" or "No" for the following questions:
	+ Destinations: Were you traveling to any destinations during this trip?
	+ Transport: Did you use any transport during your trip?
	+ Activity: Did you engage in any activities during your trip?
	+ Accommodation: Did you stay in any accommodation during your trip?
* Enter the dates of your trip:
	+ Start date: Enter the start date of your trip
	+ End date: Enter the end date of your trip
* Enter the duration of your stay:
	+ Stay duration: Enter the duration of your stay in days
* Report expenses:
	+ Food expenses: Enter any food expenses you incurred during your trip
	+ Transport expenses: Enter any transport expenses you incurred during your trip
	+ Other expenses: Enter any other expenses you incurred during your trip
* Calculate your total expenses:
	+ Total expenses: This will be automatically calculated based on your previous answers

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **travel_log_form**: A text field to enter a brief title for your travel log.
* **destinations**: A multiple-select field to choose which destinations you visited during your trip.
* **start_date**: A date field to enter the start date of your trip.
* **end_date**: A date field to enter the end date of your trip.
* **stay_duration**: A number field to enter the duration of your stay in days.
* **transport**: A multiple-select field to choose which transport modes you used during your trip.
* **activity**: A single-select field to choose if you engaged in any activities during your trip.
* **accommodation**: A single-select field to choose if you stayed in any accommodation during your trip.
* **food_expenses**: A number field to enter any food expenses you incurred during your trip.
* **transport_expenses**: A number field to enter any transport expenses you incurred during your trip.
* **other_expenses**: A number field to enter any other expenses you incurred during your trip.
* **total_expenses**: A number field that will be automatically calculated based on your previous answers.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate reporting.
* If you encounter any issues or have questions, please contact your administrator or support team.
* This form is for internal use only, please do not share your responses with anyone outside of the organization.
* Please note that this form will be used to track your expenses for administrative and financial purposes.
