# Air Travel Compensation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from passengers about their experience with a particular airline. It will help us understand their satisfaction with the airline's handling of disruptions, delays, and other issues that may have occurred during their flight.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through the following steps:

1. Fill in your passenger name and contact email address.
2. Select the flight number and airline you traveled with.
3. Choose the type of disruption that occurred during your flight (e.g., flight cancellation, delay over 3 hours, etc.).
4. Choose the reason for the disruption (e.g., airline operational issue, weather, etc.).
5. Select the duration of the delay (e.g., under 2 hours, 2-3 hours, etc.).
6. Choose the timing of when you were notified about the disruption.
7. Select the assistance provided by the airline during the disruption.
8. Choose the compensation you expected and received from the airline.
9. Indicate whether you have submitted a claim to the airline and the airline's response to your claim.
10. Rate your satisfaction with the airline's response to your claim.
11. Rate the quality of communication from the airline.
12. Rate the clarity of information provided by the airline.
13. Select any additional expenses you incurred due to the disruption (e.g., meals, accommodation, etc.).
14. Enter the approximate total of additional expenses incurred.
15. Indicate whether you have receipts available for additional expenses.
16. Rate your likelihood of flying with this airline again.
17. Rate your likelihood of recommending this airline to others.
18. Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the airline.
19. Finally, enter the date of the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Passenger Name**: Your full name as a passenger.
  Enter your name as it appears on your passport or government ID.
- **Email Address**: Your contact email address.
  This will help us get in touch with you for further questions or updates.
- **Flight Number**: The number of the flight you took with the airline.
  Enter the flight number as it appears on your boarding pass or ticket.
- **Passenger Airline**: The name of the airline you traveled with.
  Enter the exact name of the airline as it appears on your ticket or boarding pass.
- **Travel Date**: The date of your flight.
  Enter the date of your flight in the format MM/DD/YY (e.g., 02/01/22).
- **Origin Airport**: The airport where your flight departed from.
  Enter the exact code of the airport as it appears on your ticket or boarding pass.
- **Destination Airport**: The airport where your flight arrived at.
  Enter the exact code of the airport as it appears on your ticket or boarding pass.
- **Type of Disruption**: Choose the type of disruption that occurred during your flight.
  Select from the following options: Flight cancellation, Delay over 3 hours, Denied boarding, Missed connection, Lost baggage, Damaged baggage, Downgrade in class, Other.
- **Cause of Disruption**: Choose the reason for the disruption.
  Select from the following options: Airline operational issue, Weather, Air traffic control, Mechanical problem, Crew issue, Security issue, Strike, Other, Not informed.
- **Delay Duration**: Choose the duration of the delay.
  Select from the following options: Under 2 hours, 2-3 hours, 3-5 hours, 5-10 hours, Over 10 hours, Overnight, Not applicable.
- **When Were You Notified**: Choose when you were notified about the disruption.
  Select from the following options: More than 24 hours before, 14-24 hours before, 7-14 days before, 2-7 days before, Less than 24 hours, At the airport, After arrival.
- **Assistance Provided**: Choose the assistance provided by the airline during the disruption.
  Select from the following options: Meal vouchers, Hotel accommodation, Transportation, Rebooking on next flight, Refund, Travel voucher, Cash compensation, Nothing.
- **Compensation Requested**: Choose the compensation you are seeking.
  Select from the following options: Monetary compensation, Travel voucher, Mileage points, Upgrade voucher, Refund, Apology only, Other.
- **Compensation Amount Expected (USD)**: Enter the approximate amount of compensation you are expecting.
  Enter the amount in USD.
- **Have You Submitted a Claim**: Indicate whether you have submitted a claim to the airline.
  Select from the following options: Yes, already submitted, Planning to submit, No, not yet.
- **Airline Response**: Choose the airline's response to your claim.
  Select from the following options: Full compensation offered, Partial compensation offered, Claim denied, No response yet, Still waiting, Not applicable.
- **Satisfaction with Airline Response**: Rate your satisfaction with the airline's response.
  Select from the following options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied, No response received.
- **Communication Quality**: Rate the quality of communication from the airline.
  Select from the following options: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very poor.
- **Information Clarity**: Rate the clarity of information provided by the airline.
  Select from the following options: Very clear and timely, Clear but delayed, Unclear information, No information provided.
- **Additional Expenses Incurred**: Select any additional expenses you incurred due to the disruption.
  Select from the following options: Meals, Accommodation, Transportation, Communication, Childcare, Lost work, None.
- **Total Additional Expenses (USD)**: Enter the approximate total of additional expenses incurred.
  Enter the amount in USD.
- **Receipts Available**: Indicate whether you have receipts for additional expenses.
  Select from the following options: Yes, all, Some, False.
- **Likelihood to Fly This Airline Again**: Rate your likelihood of flying with this airline again.
  Select from the following options: Very likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very unlikely.
- **Likelihood to Recommend This Airline**: Rate your likelihood of recommending this airline to others.
  Select from the following options: Very likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very unlikely.
- **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the airline.
  Enter any comments you may have about your experience.
- **Survey Date**: Enter the date of the survey.
  Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YY (e.g., 02/01/22).
