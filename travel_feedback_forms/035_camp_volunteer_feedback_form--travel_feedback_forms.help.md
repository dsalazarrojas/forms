# Camp Volunteer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from volunteers on their experience at the camp, allowing organizers to improve the volunteer program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your name (optional, but helpful for follow-up communication).
* Choose the date of your volunteer engagement.
* Describe the role you performed during the camp.
* Rate your overall experience (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* Rate the level of support you received from staff (Very Supported, Supported, Neutral, or Unsupported).
* Provide any suggestions for improving the volunteer experience.
* Indicate if you would volunteer again (True, Maybe, or False).
* If you felt additional training would be beneficial, describe what topics would be helpful.
* Rate your travel experience (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor), if applicable.
* Provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name**: Optional. If you're comfortable sharing your name, please enter it. This will help us follow up with you if we need more information.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date you participated in the camp.
* **Role Performed**: Describe the tasks and responsibilities you undertook during your volunteer engagement.
* **Overall Experience Rating**: Rate your overall experience from Excellent to Poor.
* **Support from Staff**: Rate the level of support you received from the camp staff. We want you to feel supported and valued.
* **Suggestions to Improve Volunteer Experience**: Share any practical suggestions on how we can improve the volunteer experience.
* **Would You Volunteer Again**: Indicate your interest in participating in future volunteer opportunities.
* **Additional Training Needed**: If there's anything else you feel would help you be a better volunteer, let us know.
* **Travel Experience Feedback**: Rate your experience with transport, if applicable.
* **Additional Comments**: Share any other thoughts or feedback you have about your experience at the camp.
