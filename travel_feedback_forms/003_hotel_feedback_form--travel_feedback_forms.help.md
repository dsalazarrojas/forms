# Hotel Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Feedback Form is designed to collect your feedback and suggestions on your recent stay at our hotel. Your input will help us to identify areas of improvement and make necessary changes to provide a better experience for our guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your hotel's name in the "Hotel Name" field.
2. Provide your room number, if you want to specify the room you stayed in.
3. Enter the date you checked in, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
4. Write your feedback about your stay in the "Feedback" field.
5. Rate your stay on a scale from 1 to 5.
6. Enter a valid email address in the "Contact Email" field, so we can get back to you with any questions or to follow up on your feedback.
7. If you'd like to be contacted by phone, you can enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Finally, click "Submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hotel Name**: <label> (Text, Required): Enter the name of the hotel where you stayed.
* **Room Number**: <label> (Text, Optional): If you want to specify which room you stayed in, enter the room number here.
* **Check-in Date**: <label> (Date, Required): Enter the date you checked in, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Feedback**: <label> (Text, Required): Write your honest feedback about your stay. This will help us identify areas of improvement.
* **Rating**: <label> (Number, Required): Rate your stay on a scale from 1 to 5. 
* **Contact Email**: <label> (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you with questions or follow up on your feedback.
* **Phone**: <label> (Text, Optional): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number here.
* **Submit**: <label> (Text, Required): Click to submit your feedback. Your input is greatly appreciated.
