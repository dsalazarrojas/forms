# travel_itinerary_planning_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel_itinerary_planning_feedback_form is designed to collect feedback from users on a travel itinerary. The form is used to gather information about a user's experience, including overall satisfaction, specific feedback, and travel details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a **Form Title** in the first field, if available. This will help us identify your feedback with your specific travel itinerary.
2. On the second page, select your feedback for each of the three questions using the provided dropdown menus.
3. On the third page, enter your **Travel Date**, the time of day you traveled, and click Submit when finished.
4. On the final pages, enter your email address and any additional travel comments you'd like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Title (Page 1)
* **Form Title** (form_title, text, required: false): Enter a brief title for your feedback, if available.
### Travel Itinerary (Page 2)
* **Travel Itinerary** (travel_itinerary, text, required: true): Describe your travel experience, including any notable events or activities.
### Feedback Question 1 (Page 3)
* **Feedback Question 1** (feedback_question_1, select_one, required: true): Select how satisfied were you with your travel experience? Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Bad.
### Feedback Question 2 (Page 3)
* **Feedback Question 2** (feedback_question_2, select_multiple, required: true): Select which aspects of your travel experience you'd like to improve. Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Bad.
### Feedback Question 3 (Page 4)
* **Feedback Question 3** (feedback_question_3, select_multiple, required: true): Select which aspects of your travel experience you'd like to improve. Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Bad.
### Travel Date (Page 4)
* **Travel Date** (travel_date, date, required: true): Enter the date of your travel.
### Travel Time (Page 4)
* **Travel Time** (travel_time, time, required: false): Enter the time of day you traveled. (Optional)
### Email (Page 5)
* **Email** (email, email, required: true): Enter your email address so we can follow up on your feedback.
### Travel Comments (Page 5)
* **Travel Comments** (travel_comments, text, required: true): Enter any additional travel comments or suggestions you'd like to share.
### Submit (Page 5)
* **Submit** (submit, text, required: false): Click this button to complete your feedback form.
### Form Category (Page 6)
* **Form Category** (form_category, text, required: true): Select the category of your feedback. (Optional)
### Form Title (Page 6)
* **Form Title** (form_title, text, required: false): Enter a brief title for your feedback. (Optional)
### Form Description (Page 6)
* **Form Description** (form_description, text, required: true): Enter a brief description of your travel experience.
### Form Output File (Page 7)
* **Form Output File** (form_output_file, text, required: true): Select a file to share as part of your feedback.
### Form Assigned Tool (Page 7)
* **Form Assigned Tool** (form_assigned_tool, text, required: true): Select the tool or resource used to create the travel itinerary.
### Form Form ID (Page 7)
* **Form Form ID** (form_form_id, text, required: true): Enter the ID of the travel itinerary.
### Form Output File Name (Page 8)
* **Form Output File Name** (form_output_file_name, text, required: true): Enter a name for the file you selected to share as part of your feedback.
### Form JotForm App ID (Page 8)
* **Form JotForm App ID** (form_jotform_app_id, text, required: true): Enter the JotForm App ID of the travel itinerary.
### Form Version (Page 8)
* **Form Version** (form_version, text, required: true): Enter the version of the travel itinerary.
### Form Created By (Page 9)
* **Form Created By** (form_created_by, text, required: true): Enter the person who created the travel itinerary.
### Form Modified By (Page 9)
* **Form Modified By** (form_modified_by, text, required: true): Enter the person who modified the travel itinerary.
### Form Modified On (Page 10)
* **Form Modified On** (form_modified_on, date, required: true): Enter the date the travel itinerary was modified.
### Form Created Time (Page 10)
* **Form Created Time** (form_created_time, time, required: true): Enter the time the travel itinerary was created.
### Form Modified Time (Page 10)
* **Form Modified Time** (form_modified_time, time, required: true): Enter the time the travel itinerary was modified.
### Form Modified Count (Page 10)
* **Form Modified Count** (form_modified_count, number, required: true): Enter the number of times the travel itinerary was modified.

Please note that this form is for feedback purposes only and should not be used for duplicate feedback.
