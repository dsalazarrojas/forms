# Client Trip Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to gather client feedback on their recent travel experience. Your input will help us improve our services and provide better experiences for future clients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your experience.
3. For fields with notes or additional information sections, feel free to add as much detail as you like.
4. Review each field carefully before submitting your form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, please enter your phone number (optional).
* **Type of Trip** (`trip_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of trip you took (e.g., Vacation, Business, Adventure, etc.).
* **Destination Country or Region** (`destination`, `text`, required): Enter the country or region where you traveled.
* **Trip Duration** (`trip_duration`, `number`, required): Enter the number of days you traveled.
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, `select_one`, optional): Select the time of year you traveled (if applicable).
* **Number of Travelers** (`number_of_travelers`, `number`, required): Enter the number of people who traveled with you.
* **Who Traveled with You?** (`travel_party`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select who traveled with you (e.g., Solo, Spouse or Partner, Family with Children, etc.).
* **Overall Booking Experience** (`booking_experience`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall experience with our booking process.
* **Staff Helpfulness** (`staff_helpfulness`, `select_one`, required): Rate how helpful our staff was in planning your trip.
* **Time Needed for Planning and Booking** (`planning_timeline`, `select_one`, optional): Select how much advance notice you needed for planning and booking.
* **Flight Experience** (`flight_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall flight experience.
* **Flight Comfort** (`flight_comfort`, `select_one`, required): Select your comfort level during flights.
* **Airline Service Quality** (`airline_service`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the quality of airline service you experienced.
* **Accommodation** (`accommodation_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of your accommodation.
* **Accommodation Met Expectations** (`accommodation_vs_expectations`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the accommodation met or exceeded your expectations.
* **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness`, `select_one`, required): Select the cleanliness level of your accommodation.
* **Satisfaction with Activities** (`activities_satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with available activities.
* **Good Activity Recommendations** (`activity_recommendations`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether you received good activity recommendations.
* **Best Activity or Experience** (`highlight_activity`, `text`, optional): If you have an exceptional experience or activity, share it here.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with your trip.

Please take a moment to read each field carefully and answer as honestly as possible. Your feedback will help us improve and make future experiences for our clients even better.
