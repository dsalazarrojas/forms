# Business Travel Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on business travel experiences, providing valuable insights for companies to improve their travel programs.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your employee's name and contact information.
2. Answer the questions regarding your travel destination, duration, and purpose.
3. Evaluate the quality of your accommodations, transportation, and travel arrangements.
4. Share your experience with booking and company support during travel.
5. Rate your satisfaction with travel vendors and the adequacy of your travel budget.
6. Describe any suggestions for improvement.
7. Indicate whether you are likely to travel again for business in the future.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as you would like to be identified on the survey results.
* **Email Address** (`employee_email`, `email`, required): Type your business email address for contact purposes.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Choose the department or team you belong to.
* **Travel Destination** (`trip_destination`, `text`, required): Enter the location you visited.
* **Trip Duration** (`trip_dates`, `date`, required): Select the dates of your trip.
* **Purpose of Travel** (`trip_purpose`, `text`, required): Describe the main reason for your trip.
* **Quality of Accommodations** (`accommodation_quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of your hotel or lodging (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Transportation Quality** (`transportation_quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of your travel arrangements (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Ease of Booking Travel** (`travel_booking_ease`, `select_one`, required): Rate how easy or difficult it was to book your travel (Very Easy, Easy, Neutral, Difficult, Very Difficult).
* **Company Support During Travel** (`company_support`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of company assistance you received during travel (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Satisfaction with Travel Vendors** (`vendor_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with hotels, airlines, and car rentals (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Was Your Travel Budget Adequate?** (`travel_budget_adequacy`, `select_one`, required): Rate whether your travel budget covered the trip (More than adequate, Adequate, Barely adequate, Inadequate, Very Inadequate).
* **Safety During Travel** (`travel_safety`, `select_one`, required): Rate how safe you felt during travel (Very Safe, Safe, Neutral, Unsafe, Very Unsafe).
* **How Can We Improve Business Travel?** (`travel_improvements`, `text`, optional): Share any suggestions or ideas for improving business travel experiences.
* **Likely to Travel Again for Business** (`likely_future_travel`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you plan to travel for business again (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Unsure, Probably No, Definitely No).
