# Participant Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Participant Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from participants on their travel experiences. It is used by the travel planning service to improve their planning and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the form with your honest and constructive feedback.
3. Answer all required fields, which are marked as required.
4. Select the correct options for the select_one and select_multiple fields.
5. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).
6. Provide a detailed description of your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Planning a trip (1)** (`planning_a_trip`, note, optional): This field is where you can type out any comments or thoughts you have about planning your trip. It's a free text area where you can write anything that comes to mind.
* **Feedback afterwards (2)** (`feedback_afterwards`, text, optional): In this field, please provide any feedback you have about your experience after the trip has taken place.
* **Tourists and adventurers (3)** (`tourists_and_adventurers`, select_multiple, optional): If you consider yourself a tourist or an adventurer, select the option that best describes you.
* **Want to live their trace behind (4)** (`want_to_live_their_trace_behind`, select_one, required): Please select one option from the list to indicate your level of interest in leaving a digital footprint or trail for the travel service to follow.
* **Email address (5)** (`email_address`, email, optional): If you have an email address, please enter it so we can contact you if needed. This field is only required if you want us to follow up with you.
* **Phone number (6)** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can contact you if needed. This field is only required if you want us to follow up with you.
* **Description (7)** (`description`, text, optional): In this field, provide a detailed description of your experience. This will help us understand your trip better and provide more accurate feedback.
* **Assigned Tool (8)** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Please select one option from the list to indicate which tool was assigned to you during the trip planning process.
