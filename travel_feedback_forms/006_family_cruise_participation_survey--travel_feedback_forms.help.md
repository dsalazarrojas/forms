# Family Cruise Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your family cruise plans, including travel dates, participant details, and cabin preferences. It's crucial that you complete this form accurately to help us tailor our services to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your travel date from the date picker.
2.  Enter the number of participants you are planning to bring on the cruise.
3.  Choose the number of adults and children participating from the respective dropdown menus.
4.  Select the type of cabin you prefer.
5.  If you have any special needs, provide a note explaining them.
6.  If you have a travel agent assigned to you, provide their name and email address.
7.  Optionally, add any comments about your trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, required: false): Enter the travel date for your family cruise.
    *   This is the date you plan to travel on the cruise.
*   **Number of Participants** (`number_of_participants`, number, required: false): Enter the total number of people in your group.
    *   Please provide the total number of people you plan to bring along.
*   **Number of Adults** (`adult_participants`, number, required: false): Enter the number of adults in your group.
    *   This is the number of adults that will be accompanying you on the cruise.
*   **Children** (`children`, select, required: false): Select the age group of the children.
    *   Please choose the age group of the children that will be traveling with you.
*   **Cabin Type** (`cabin_type`, select, required: false): Select the type of cabin you prefer.
    *   Choose the cabin type that suits your needs.
*   **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, note, required: false): If you have any special needs, provide a note explaining them.
    *   Describe any special needs you or your family may have that we should be aware of.
*   **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required: false): Enter your preferred travel date.
    *   This is your preferred date for the cruise.
*   **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, text, required: false): If you have an assigned travel agent, please list their name.
    *   Enter the name of your travel agent if assigned.
*   **Travel Agent Email** (`travel_agent_email`, email, required: false): If you have an assigned travel agent, please enter their email address.
    *   Enter the email address of your travel agent if assigned.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments about your trip.
    *   Share any further details or comments you'd like to mention about your travel plans.

This form is designed to be user-friendly and straightforward. Please answer the questions accurately to ensure we can tailor our services to your family's needs.
