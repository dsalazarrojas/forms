# Travel Website Customer Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This travel website's customer feedback survey aims to collect information from customers about their travel experiences and overall satisfaction with the website. The purpose of this form is to gather data to improve the quality and user experience of the website.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Answer the questions about your travel experience
To complete this form, please answer the questions about your recent travel experience. The form has several sections that ask about different aspects of your trip.

### Step 2: Provide detailed feedback
Please provide as much detail as possible in your answers. This will help us understand your experience better and make necessary improvements to the website.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Website Feedback Survey** (`travel_website_customer_feedback_survey_form`): This is the title of the form. Please do not fill in any information in this field.
* **Travel Booking ID** (`travel_booking_id`): Enter your Travel Booking ID if you have one. This helps us associate your feedback with your booking.
* **Travel Booking Date** (`travel_booking_date`): Enter the date of your travel booking.
* **Travel Booking Location** (`travel_booking_location`): Enter the location of your travel booking.
* **Travel Booking Type** (`travel_booking_type`): Select "Yes" if your travel booking was for leisure, "No" otherwise.
* **Booking Outlet** (`travel_booking_outlet`): Select all the outlets where you made your travel booking.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction with your travel experience on a scale.
* **Travel Experience** (`travel_experience_comment`): Tell us about your travel experience. What did you do? What did you see? What did you think?
* **Travel Experience Rating** (`travel_experience_rating`): Rate your travel experience with one or more of the following: Good, Fair.
* **Travel Website Experience** (`travel_website_experience`): Select "Yes" if you had a good experience with the travel website, "No" otherwise.
* **Travel Website Experience Rating** (`travel_website_experience_rating`): Rate your experience with the travel website with one or more of the following: Good, Fair.

### Tips
* Please answer all the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Travel Experience Comment" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
