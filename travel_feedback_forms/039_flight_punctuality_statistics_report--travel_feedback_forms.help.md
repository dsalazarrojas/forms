# Flight Punctuality Statistics Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Flight Punctuality Statistics Report" form is designed to collect information about flights and their punctuality. It is used to gather data that helps identify trends, patterns, and insights related to flight punctuality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  **Select the airline**: Choose the airline from the select_one field "airline_id".
2.  **Enter the flight date**: Enter the date of the flight in the date field "flight_date".
3.  **Enter the flight number**: Enter the flight number in the text field "flight_number".
4.  **Enter the arrival time**: Enter the arrival time in the time field "arrival_time".
5.  **Enter the departure time**: Enter the departure time in the time field "departure_time".
6.  **Enter the delay reason**: Enter the reason for the delay in the text field "delay_reason".
7.  **Select the delay type**: Choose the type of delay from the select_one field "delay_type".
8.  **Select flight status**: Choose the status of the flight from the select_multiple field "flight_status".
9.  **Enter delay duration**: Enter the duration of the delay in the number field "delay_duration".
10. **Enter flight ID**: Enter the ID of the flight in the text field "flight_id".
11.  **Enter airport ID**: Enter the ID of the airport in the text field "airport_id".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **airline_id** (Select One, required): Choose the airline associated with the flight.
*   **flight_date** (Date, required): Enter the date of the flight.
*   **flight_number** (Text, required): Enter the flight number.
*   **arrival_time** (Time, required): Enter the arrival time of the flight.
*   **departure_time** (Time, required): Enter the departure time of the flight.
*   **delay_reason** (Text, required): Enter the reason for the delay.
*   **delay_type** (Select One, required): Choose the type of delay (Delayed, Cancelled, or Other).
*   **flight_status** (Select Multiple, required): Choose the status of the flight (On Time, Delayed, Cancelled).
*   **delay_duration** (Number, required): Enter the duration of the delay in minutes.
*   **flight_id** (Text, required): Enter the ID of the flight.
*   **airport_id** (Text, required): Enter the ID of the airport.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
*   Double-check the select_one and select_multiple fields for correct selections.
*   Use the correct format for date and time entries.
*   Enter the delay duration in minutes.
