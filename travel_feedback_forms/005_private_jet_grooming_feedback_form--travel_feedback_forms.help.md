# Private Jet Grooming Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Private Jet Grooming Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from private jet users on their experience with the jet's grooming services. This form helps us identify areas of improvement to ensure that our private jet standards are maintained.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to save your feedback.
2. Fill out each field as accurately and truthfully as possible.
3. If you have any issues or concerns, please describe them in the "feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction (overall_satisfaction)** (`number`, Optional)
    - Rate your overall satisfaction with the grooming experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very satisfied".

* **Cleanliness (cleanliness)** (`text`, Optional)
    - Describe your experience with the jet's cleanliness, e.g., "The jet was extremely clean" or "There were some issues with trash on the floor".

* **Grooming Services (grooming_services)** (`select_one`, Optional)
    - Choose one of the options that best describes your experience with the jet's grooming services:
        - Cabin crew friendly
        - Cleanliness of cabin
        - Comfort of seat
        - Grooming staff friendly
        - Overall grooming experience
        - Punctuality
        - Timeliness of grooming

* **Feedback (feedback)** (`text`, Optional)
    - Share any additional comments or concerns about your experience.

* **Email (email)** (`email`, Optional)
    - Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive updates on improvements.

* **Phone Number (phone_number)** (`text`, Optional)
    - Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone for follow-up questions or to receive updates on improvements.

* **Submitted By (submitted_by)** (`text`, Optional)
    - Enter your name as it appears on the submitted feedback.

* **Submitted By Email (submitted_by_email)** (`email`, Optional)
    - Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive updates on improvements.

* **Submitted By Phone (submitted_by_phone)** (`text`, Optional)
    - Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone for follow-up questions or to receive updates on improvements.

* **Submitted At (submitted_at)** (`date`, Optional)
    - This field is automatically populated with the date and time you submit the form.
