# solo_travel_sign_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "solo_travel_sign_out_form" is designed to collect information about a traveler's trip, ensuring their safety and well-being, particularly in case of emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the travel date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` in the **travel_date** field.
2. Enter the travel time in the format `HH:MM` in the **travel_time** field.
3. Enter the duration of the travel in hours in the **travel_duration** field.
4. Provide the location of the travel in the **travel_location** field.
5. Enter any additional traveler details in the **traveler_details** field.
6. Enter the emergency contact person's details (name, phone number, and email) in the **emergency_contact_details**, **emergency_contact_name**, **emergency_contact_phone**, and **emergency_contact_email** fields.
7. Indicate the relationship between the traveler and the emergency contact person in the **emergency_contact_relationship** field.
8. Enter any additional comments or information about the trip in the **additional_comments** field.
9. Provide the status of the travel in the **travel_status** field.
10. Enter the relationship between the school, club, hostel, or family and the travel group in the **school_club_hostel_or_family_relationship** field.
11. Enter the phone number of the school, club, hostel, or family in the **school_club_hostel_or_family_phone** field.
12. Enter the email of the school, club, hostel, or family in the **school_club_hostel_or_family_email** field.
13. Enter any additional notes or information about the school, club, hostel, or family in the **school_club_hostel_or_family_notes** field.
14. Enter the name of the trip leader or person responsible for the travel in the **trip_leader** field.
15. Enter the phone number of the trip leader in the **trip_leaders_phone** field.
16. Enter the email of the trip leader in the **trip_leaders_email** field.
17. Enter the phone number of the school, club, hostel, or family in the **school_club_hostel_or_family_phone** field.
18. Enter the email of the school, club, hostel, or family in the **school_club_hostel_or_family_email** field.
19. Indicate the relationship between the school, club, hostel, or family and the travel group in the **school_club_hostel_or_family_relationship** field.
20. Enter any additional notes or information about the school, club, hostel, or family in the **school_club_hostel_or_family_notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, required): Enter the travel date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, time, required): Enter the travel time in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the travel in hours.
* **Travel Location** (`travel_location`, text, required): Provide the location of the travel.
* **Traveler Details** (`traveler_details`, text, required): Enter any additional traveler details.
* **Emergency Contact Details** (`emergency_contact_details`, text, required): Enter any emergency contact person's details.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required): Indicate the relationship between the traveler and the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationships** (`emergency_contact_relationships`, select_multiple, required): Indicate any additional relationships between the traveler and emergency contact persons.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Enter any additional information or comments about the travel.
* **Travel Status** (`travel_status`, text, required): Provide the status of the travel.
* **School Club Hostel or Family** (`school_club_hostel_or_family`, text, required): Enter any school, club, hostel, or family information.
* **Trip Leader** (`trip_leader`, text, required): Enter the name of the trip leader or person responsible for the travel.
* **Trip Leaders Phone** (`trip_leaders_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the trip leader.
* **Trip Leaders Email** (`trip_leaders_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the trip leader.
* **School Club Hostel or Family Phone** (`school_club_hostel_or_family_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the school, club, hostel, or family.
* **School Club Hostel or Family Email** (`school_club_hostel_or_family_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the school, club, hostel, or family.
* **School Club Hostel or Family Relationship** (`school_club_hostel_or_family_relationship`, select_one, required): Indicate the relationship between the school, club, hostel, or family and the travel group.
* **School Club Hostel or Family Relationships** (`school_club_hostel_or_family_relationships`, select_multiple, required): Indicate any additional relationships between the school, club, hostel, or family and the travel group.
* **School Club Hostel or Family Notes** (`school_club_hostel_or_family_notes`, text, required): Enter any notes or information about the school, club, hostel, or family.
* **Travel Group** (`travel_group`, text, required): Enter the name of the travel group.
* **Travel Group Size** (`travel_group_size`, number, required): Enter the size of the travel group.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
* Double-check the relationships between the traveler and emergency contact person, school, club, hostel, or family.
* Enter any additional comments or information that may be helpful for the travel leader or person responsible for the travel.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
