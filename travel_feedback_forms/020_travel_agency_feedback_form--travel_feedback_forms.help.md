# Travel Agency Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Travel Agency Feedback Form is designed to gather your feedback and comments about your experience with our travel agency. Your input will help us improve our services and make future travel experiences better for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your Guest Name and Email Address (if provided).
2. Provide your Feedback about your experience with our travel agency.
3. Rate your experience (optional).
4. Suggest any improvements or changes to our services.
5. Select the category of travel you were on (Hotels, Resorts, Tour Operators, or Airports).
6. Enter the value of your rating.
7. Choose the date of your travel.
8. Choose the time of your travel.
9. Enter the name of the travel agency you used.
10. Provide any additional details about your feedback (optional).
11. Enter the name of the travel agent you interacted with (if applicable).
12. Enter the contact number of the travel agency (if available).
13. Select the social media channels where you would like to share your feedback (optional).
14. Enter the website of the travel agency (if available).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you have one. This will help us contact you if we need to discuss your feedback.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and opinions about your experience with our travel agency. Be as detailed or brief as you like.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, optional): If you have an idea for how we can improve our services, enter it here.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category of travel you were on (Hotels, Resorts, Tour Operators, or Airports).
* **Rating Value** (`rating_value`, number, optional): Enter the value of your rating (1-5).
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your travel.
* **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your travel.
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, text, optional): Enter the name of the travel agency you used.
* **Feedback Details** (`feedback_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about your feedback.
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, text, optional): Enter the name of the travel agent you interacted with (if applicable).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact number of the travel agency (if available).
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the social media channels where you would like to share your feedback (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, or Reddit).
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the travel agency (if available).
