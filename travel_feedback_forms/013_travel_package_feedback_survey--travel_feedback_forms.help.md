# travel_package_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from travelers on our travel packages and services. Your input will help us make informed decisions to improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Please provide your honest feedback on the package you recently used.
2. Share any suggestions you have for us to improve the package.
3. Add any comments you'd like to make about your experience.
4. Rate the package and our service.
5. Indicate if you had a positive or negative travel experience.
6. Enter your contact information for us to follow up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Package Details
* **Package Details**: A brief description of the package you used.
* **Feedback**: Share your honest opinion on the package. What did you like or dislike about it?
* **Suggestions**: Suggest ways we can improve the package.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about your experience.
* **Rating Package**: Rate the package on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating Service**: Rate our service on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rated 5 Stars**: This rating indicates that you were extremely satisfied with the package.
* **Rating Value**: Enter a numerical value to rate the package and service.
* **Package Improvement**: Select all the ways we can improve the package.
	+ **Price**: Suggest ways to make the package more affordable.
	+ **Content**: Suggest adding or removing content features.
	+ **Design**: Suggest design improvements.
	+ **Service**: Suggest ways to improve our service.
	+ **Other**: Suggest anything else.
* **Service Improvement**: Select all the ways we can improve our service.
	+ **Communication**: Suggest ways to improve communication.
	+ **Response Time**: Suggest ways to improve our response time.
	+ **Price**: Suggest ways to make our service more affordable.
	+ **Other**: Suggest anything else.
* **Travel Experience**: Indicate if you had a positive travel experience. 
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number in case we need to get in touch with you.

Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback. Your input is invaluable to us!
