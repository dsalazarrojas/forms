# Airline Lounge Experience Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from passengers about their experience in the airline lounge. This information will help us identify areas of improvement and provide better services to our passengers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the "Visit Details" section at the top of the form.
2. Provide your visit details, including the date and location of your visit.
3. Indicate your membership status.
4. Rate your experience with check-in, seating comfort, food quality, and staff service.
5. Select any amenities you used during your visit.
6. Provide your overall satisfaction level.
7. Share any suggestions you have for improving the lounge experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest name** (`guest_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name, if you'd like to be acknowledged for your feedback.
* **Membership status** (`membership_status`, select_one, required): Select whether you are a member or a guest.
* **Visit date** (`visit_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Lounge location** (`lounge_location`, text, required): Enter the airport and terminal or lounge name.
* **Check in experience rating** (`check_in_experience_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your experience with check-in on a scale of 1-5.
* **Seating comfort rating** (`seating_comfort_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the comfort of the seating on a scale of 1-5.
* **Food quality rating** (`food_quality_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the food and beverage offerings on a scale of 1-5.
* **Staff service rating** (`staff_service_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the professionalism of the staff on a scale of 1-5.
* **Amenities used** (`amenities_used`, select_multiple, optional): Select any amenities you used during your visit, such as WiFi, showers, workspaces, etc.
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select your overall satisfaction level on a scale of 1-5.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving the lounge experience.
