# Disney World Planning And Booking Cheatsheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for planning and booking a Disney World trip. It's intended for individuals or travel agencies to gather details about a client's travel plans, including their preferences, dates, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select a travel agency (if applicable).
3. Enter your booking date.
4. Provide your contact information (name, email, and phone number).
5. Enter any additional travel notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, text, optional): If you're booking through a travel agency, enter their name here.
* **Booking Date** (`booking_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to book your Disney World trip.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter your name or the name of the person booking the trip.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where the agency can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where the agency can contact you.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to travel to Disney World.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, optional): Enter your home address or the address of the person booking the trip.
* **Travel Notes** (`travel_notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about your trip, such as special requests or preferences.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask your travel agent for clarification.
- This form is for general use and is not affiliated with any specific travel agency or service.
