# Travel Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Satisfaction Survey form is designed to gather feedback from travelers about their recent experiences. It helps travel companies to understand their customers' satisfaction levels, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions to enhance their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose.
2. Select your response for each question according to your experience.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Travel satisfaction** (`Satisfied`, `Dissatisfied`):
	+ Select "Satisfied" if your recent travel experience met your expectations.
	+ Select "Dissatisfied" if your experience fell short of your expectations.
* **2. Travel Feedback Forms** (`text`, required: false):
	+ Provide a brief description of your travel experience. This is an optional field, but it helps us understand your thoughts and feelings about your trip.
* **3. Traveler Experience** (`Yes`, `No`, required: false):
	+ Select "Yes" if you had a positive experience with travel-related activities (e.g., sightseeing, dining, or cultural events).
	+ Select "No" if you didn't participate in travel-related activities or had a negative experience.
* **4. Travel Type** (`Yes`, `No`, required: false):
	+ Select "Yes" if your recent trip was for leisure or entertainment purposes (e.g., vacation).
	+ Select "No" if your trip was for business or other purposes.
* **5. travel_date** (`date`, required: false):
	+ Enter the date of your recent trip.
* **6. travel_time** (`time`, required: false):
	+ Enter the time you traveled (e.g., morning, afternoon, or evening).
* **7. travel_note** (`note`, required: false):
	+ Share any additional thoughts or comments about your travel experience.
