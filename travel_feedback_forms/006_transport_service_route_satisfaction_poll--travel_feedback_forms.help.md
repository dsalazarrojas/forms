# Transport Service Route Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transport Service Route Satisfaction Poll is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with a specific transport route. This feedback will help the transport service provider to evaluate and improve the route's performance, frequency, and overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read the introduction and understand the purpose of the form.
2.  Rate the overall performance of the route (page 2).
3.  Select a rating for the route (page 3).
4.  Indicate how often you travel on this route (page 4).
5.  Provide any comments about your experience (page 5).
6.  Share any suggestions for improving the route (page 6).
7.  Identify any recurring issues you have encountered on the route (page 7).
8.  Enter your email address for contact purposes (page 8).
9.  Enter your phone number for contact purposes (page 9).
10.  Enter your email address again for contact purposes (page 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Introduction (Page 1)**: This is a brief introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and the information it collects.
*  **Overall Performance (Page 2)**: Rate the overall performance of this route, with a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
*  **Rating (Page 3)**: Rate this route, with a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
*  **Frequency (Page 4)**: Select how often you travel on this route, with options:
	+   Daily
	+   Weekly
	+   Monthly
	+   Rarely
	+   Never
*  **Comments (Page 5)**: Provide a brief explanation for your ratings and frequency selection, or any other comment about your experience.
*  **Suggestions (Page 6)**: Share any suggestions you have for improving the route.
*  **Recurring Issues (Page 7)**: Select any issues that regularly occur on this route, with options:
	+   Route delays
	+   Route cancellations
	+   Route disruptions
	+   Other
*  **Email (Page 8)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes, so we can get in touch with you.
*  **Phone (Page 9)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes, so we can get in touch with you.
*  **Email Address (Page 10)**: Enter your email address again, for our records.

Tips:

*   Please be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
*   If you have traveled on the route multiple times, feel free to answer as many times as you like, but make sure to fill out the form only once for each trip.
*   If you are unsure about any question, you can provide a brief explanation in the comments section.
*   Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve the route!
