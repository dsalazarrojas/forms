# travel_package_experience_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers about their travel experiences with our agency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions about your travel experience in the spaces provided.
2. Select the appropriate responses for the questions that require multiple choices.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
4. Enter your contact information so we can get back to you.
5. Submit the form to send us your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Travel Package Experience** (`travel_package_experience`, `note`, required: false)
	* This is a text field where you can describe your overall experience with your travel package. Try to be as detailed as possible to help us improve our services.
### 2. **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false)
	* This is a numerical field where you can rate your overall satisfaction with your travel package, on a scale of 1-5.
### 3. **Satisfaction Score** (`satisfaction_score`, `number`, required: false)
	* This is another numerical field where you can rate your satisfaction with different aspects of your travel package, such as accommodation and service. Again, please rate on a scale of 1-5.
### 4. **Rating** (`rating`, `select_one`, required: false)
	* Select a rating from the list below to indicate your satisfaction with your travel package. Options are:
		+ Excellent
		+ Very good
		+ Neutral
		+ Poor
		+ Terrible
### 5. **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false)
	* This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your travel experience. We value your input and will take it into consideration.
### 6. **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `email`, required: false)
	* Enter your email address so we can get back to you with any follow-up questions or to thank you for your feedback.
### 7. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false)
	* Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
### 8. **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	* Select all types of travel that apply to your experience. Options are:
		+ Domestic
		+ International
		+ Both
### 9. **Date of Travel** (`date_of_travel`, `date`, required: false)
	* Enter the date you traveled, if applicable.
### 10. **Time of Travel** (`time_of_travel`, `time`, required: false)
	* Enter the time you traveled, if applicable.
### 11. **Travel Agent Involved** (`travel_agent`, `select_one`, required: false)
	* Indicate whether a travel agent was involved in your travel arrangements. Options are:
		+ True
		+ False
### 12. **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	* Select all types of accommodation you used during your travel. Options are:
		+ Hotel
		+ Hostel
		+ Other
### 13. **Travel Agent Information** (`travel_agent_info`, `note`, required: false)
	* This is a text field where the travel agent can provide additional information about your travel package, if needed.
