# Thailand Travel Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Thailand Travel Experience Survey aims to gather valuable insights from travelers about their experiences in Thailand. By filling out this form, you will contribute to improving the travel experience for yourself and future visitors.

## How To Complete This Form

To provide your feedback effectively, follow these steps:

1.  Answer all fields that apply to your experience.
2.  Enter your email address and phone number if you wish to receive updates about changes to Thailand travel services.
3.  Rate your satisfaction level with a number between 1 and 10.
4.  Select activities you enjoyed during your trip.
5.  Choose recommendations for future travelers.
6.  Provide any comments or suggestions you might have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Travel Details**: Enter a brief description of your trip, including the duration and any notable experiences.
*   **Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the travel experience on a scale of 1 to 10.
*   **Activities**: Select all activities that you enjoyed during your trip, such as:
    +   Beach
    +   Cultural
    +   Foodie
    +   Nature
    +   Shopping
    +   Other (please specify)
*   **Recommendations**: Select activities or experiences you would recommend to future travelers.
*   **Satisfaction Score**: Rate your satisfaction with the travel experience on a scale of 1 to 10.
*   **Comments**: Share any additional comments or suggestions you might have.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address to receive updates about changes to Thailand travel services.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number to receive updates about changes to Thailand travel services.
*   **Trip Cost**: Enter the cost of your trip, if applicable.
*   **Travel Date**: Enter the dates of your trip.

## Tips

*   Please answer honestly and provide as much detail as possible.
*   If you have any issues or concerns during your trip, contact us at [support email/phone number].
*   Your feedback is invaluable and helps us improve the travel experience.
