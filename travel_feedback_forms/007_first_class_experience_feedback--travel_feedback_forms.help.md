# First Class Experience Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The First Class Experience Feedback form is used to gather input from passengers about their flight experiences, helping airlines and travel companies to identify areas for improvement and provide better service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `travel_route` field with the flight route you took (e.g., New York to Los Angeles).
2. Enter your overall experience on a scale of 1-5 in the `overall_experience` field.
3. Provide any additional comments about your experience in the `comments` field.
4. Select the date of your flight from the calendar in the `flight_date` field.
5. Rate the service quality of the airline using the following fields:
	* `airline_service`
	* `flight_attendant`
	* `cabin`
	* `seat`
	* `in_flight_entertainment`
	* `food_and_beverage`
	* `in_flight_service`
6. Finally, select "Submit" to send your feedback or "Cancel" to discard your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Route** (`travel_route`, text, required): This field asks for the flight route you took, including the departure and arrival cities.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, required): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Flight Date** (`flight_date`, date, required): Select the date of your flight.
* **Airline Service** (`airline_service`, number, optional): Rate the service quality of the airline.
* **Flight Attendant** (`flight_attendant`, number, optional): Rate the quality of service from the flight attendants.
* **Cabin** (`cabin`, text, optional): Describe the quality of the cabin amenities.
* **Seat** (`seat`, number, optional): Rate the comfort and quality of your seat.
* **In-Flight Entertainment** (`in_flight_entertainment`, number, optional): Rate the quality of in-flight entertainment options.
* **Food and Beverage** (`food_and_beverage`, number, optional): Rate the quality of in-flight meals and drinks.
* **In-Flight Service** (`in_flight_service`, number, optional): Rate the quality of in-flight service.
* **Overall** (`overall`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the flight experience.
* **Submit/Cancel** (`submit`, select_multiple, required): Select "Submit" to send your feedback or "Cancel" to discard your changes.
