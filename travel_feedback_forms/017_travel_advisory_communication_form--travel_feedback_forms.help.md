# Travel Advisory Communication Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect and communicate travel advisory information to internal stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please make sure to complete this form with accurate and up-to-date information about your travel plans, safety concerns, and any other relevant details.
*   If you are completing this form on behalf of another traveler, ensure you have their permission to do so.
*   Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact [insert contact information] for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Travel Advisory Protocol** (`id: 4`): Select the type of travel advisory or protocol associated with your trip (e.g., Travel Advisories, Travel Protocols).
    *   This field is required.
    *   Please choose one of the provided options to indicate the type of travel protocol or advisory associated with your trip.
*   **Safety Information** (`id: 5`): Provide any relevant safety information about your travel destination or activities.
    *   This field is not required but is highly recommended for the safety of the traveler and other relevant stakeholders.
    *   Please provide detailed information if available.
*   **Travel Updates** (`id: 6`): Enter the estimated duration of your trip, including the start and end dates.
    *   This field is not required but is useful for keeping stakeholders informed about your travel status.
    *   Format: Start Date - End Date.
*   **Travel Updates Date** (`id: 7`): Provide the date of the travel updates you are submitting.
    *   This field is not required but is useful for keeping records of when travel updates were made.
*   **Comments** (`id: 8`): Add any additional comments or details about your trip or travel plans.
    *   This field is optional.
    *   Use this space to clarify or provide extra information not covered in other fields.
*   **Submit** (`id: 9`): Confirm whether you wish to submit your travel update or advisory information.
    *   This field is required and must be checked to complete the form submission.
*   **Contact Information** (`id: 2`): Provide your contact information, including a number, to help internal stakeholders get in touch with you.
    *   This field is not required but is recommended for efficient communication.
    *   Please ensure your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.

This guide is to be used as a reference for users completing the travel advisory communication form. It's designed to be concrete and practical, ensuring users understand the form's purpose and requirements.
