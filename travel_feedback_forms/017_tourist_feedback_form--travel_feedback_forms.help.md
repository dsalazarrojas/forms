# Tourist Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tourist Feedback Form is a tool designed to collect feedback from tourists who have visited a particular location, destination, or attraction. This feedback will help us improve the experience and make the destination more enjoyable for future visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name.
2. Fill in your last name.
3. Enter your email address (if you wish to be contacted for further feedback).
4. Select your type of traveler (e.g., yes or no).
5. Rate your satisfaction level (on a scale, but no specific details provided).
6. Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
7. Add any additional comments.
