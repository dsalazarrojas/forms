# Local Attractions Tourist Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from tourists who have visited local attractions. The purpose of the form is to gather information about the tourist's experience, allowing the attraction to identify areas for improvement and provide better services to future visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2.  Provide your email address in the "Email" field, if you wish to receive a response to your feedback.
3.  Rate your experience by selecting a number from 1 to 5 in the "Ratingscale" field.
4.  Indicate whether you would recommend the attraction to others in the "Would you recommend?" field.
5.  Select the duration of your visit in the "Visit Duration" field.
6.  Indicate what you liked most about the attraction in the "What did you like most?" field.
7.  Indicate what you disliked most about the attraction in the "What did you dislike most?" field.
8.  Provide any questions or suggestions you may have in the "Any questions or suggestions?" field.
9.  Enter the date and time of your visit in the "Date" and "Time" fields, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification document.
*   **Email** (Email, Optional): Provide your email address if you want to receive a response to your feedback.
*   **How was your experience?** (Select Multiple, Optional): Describe your experience at the attraction, choosing from the provided options or providing your own response.
*   **Ratingscale** (Select One, Optional): Rate your experience by selecting a number from 1 to 5.
*   **Would you recommend?** (Select One, Optional): Indicate whether you would recommend the attraction to others.
*   **What did you like most?** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the aspect of the attraction that you enjoyed most, such as attractions, food and beverage, or services.
*   **What did you dislike most?** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the aspect of the attraction that you disliked most.
*   **Any questions or suggestions?** (Text, Optional): Provide any questions or suggestions you may have for the attraction.
*   **Follow up** (Time, Optional): This field is for selecting a follow-up action, such as scheduling a visit or requesting a callback.
*   **Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of your visit.
*   **Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of your visit.
