# Cross Campus Collaboration Data Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect consent information from participants in a cross-campus collaboration project. It helps in ensuring that all parties involved are aware of the data sharing and handling practices of the project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please select the applicable options from the given choices.
2. Fill in your contact details in the "Contact Details" section.
3. Provide project information in the "Project Info" section.
4. Choose the methods you are comfortable with for us to contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Please fill in your name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* **Project Info** (`project_info`, text, optional): In this section, please describe the project you are participating in, including its goals and objectives.
* **Data Sharing Consent** (`data_sharing_consent`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the methods you are comfortable with for your data to be shared:
	+ Label 1: Please specify what you are consenting to here.
	+ Label 2: Please specify what you are consenting to here.
	+ Label 3: Please specify what you are consenting to here.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose a method for us to contact you:
	+ Phone: We will contact you via phone to discuss your project.
	+ Email: We will contact you via email to discuss your project.
	+ Mail: We will contact you via mail to discuss your project.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all applicable sections to ensure we have accurate contact information.
- Select all the options that apply to your data sharing consent.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification.
- This form is for internal use only and is not intended for public distribution.
