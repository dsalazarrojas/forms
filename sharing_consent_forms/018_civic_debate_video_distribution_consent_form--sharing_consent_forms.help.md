# Civic Debate Video Distribution Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Civic Debate Video Distribution Consent Form is designed to document the consent and agreement of participants to record and distribute their debate performance for public use. This form ensures that participants are aware of and agree to the recording and sharing of their performance, as well as the channels in which it will be distributed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the **Participant name** field with your full name as recorded by the debate organizers.
2. Enter the **Event date** of the debate recording, as provided by the debate organizers.
3. Answer the **Consent to be recorded** question. If you agree to be recorded, select "I consent". If you do not wish to be recorded, select "I do not consent".
4. Answer the **Consent to distribute recording** question. If you agree to have your recording distributed publicly, select "I consent". If you do not wish to have your recording distributed, select "I do not consent".
5. If you wish to specify **Approved distribution channels**, select any or all of the channels you agree to allow for the distribution of your recording. This is optional and not required.
6. If applicable, indicate in the **Compensation agreement** field if any compensation applies to your participation in the debate.
7. Enter your **Signature acknowledgement** by typing your full name. This serves as a digital signature.
8. Provide your **Contact for questions** (if desired) with an email or phone number for follow-up inquiries.
9. Read and acknowledge the **Privacy and data usage note**, which explains how your recordings are stored and handled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (text): Your full name as recorded by the debate organizers.
* **Event date** (date): Date of the debate recording.
* **Consent to be recorded** (select_one): Do you agree to be recorded during the debate? (I consent / I do not consent)
* **Consent to distribute recording** (select_one): Do you agree to have your recording distributed publicly? (I consent / I do not consent)
* **Approved distribution channels** (select_multiple): Select channels you allow for distribution (Social media / Local TV / Website / Educational partners). Optional.
* **Compensation agreement** (select_one): Indicate if any compensation applies (No compensation / Honorarium / Other). Optional.
* **Signature acknowledgement** (text): Typed name serves as a digital signature.
* **Contact for questions** (text): Provide email or phone number for follow-up inquiries. Optional.
* **Privacy and data usage note** (note): Details on how recordings are stored and handled.
* **Submission date** (date): Use current date for submission. Required.
