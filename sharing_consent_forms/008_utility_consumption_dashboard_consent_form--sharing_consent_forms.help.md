# Utility Consumption Dashboard Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect your information and preferences to provide you with access to the Utility Consumption Dashboard.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **User Details** (Page 1)
	* Enter your name and other identifying information in the text field.
2. Enter your **Email Address** (Page 2)
	* Type your email address in the format `username@example.com`.
3. Select your **Utility Service** (Page 3)
	* Choose the utility service you use from the dropdown list (Electricity, Gas, or Water).
4. Select your **Dashboard Access** (Page 4)
	* Choose how you want to access the dashboard data: View usage data, View billing data, or both.
5. Select your **Analytics Access** (Page 5)
	* Choose how you want to access analytics data: View analytics reports or View analytics data.
6. Provide **Consent** (Page 6)
	* Choose how you want to use your data: Display my data, Analyze my data, or both.
7. Add **Notes** (Page 7)
	* Enter any additional comments or notes you have.
8. Enter your **Phone Number** (Page 8)
	* Type your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details**: Provide your name and other identifying information.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address in the format `username@example.com`.
* **Utility Service**: Choose the utility service you use from the dropdown list.
* **Dashboard Access**: Select how you want to access the dashboard data:
	+ View usage data
	+ View billing data
	+ View both usage and billing data
* **Analytics Access**: Select how you want to access analytics data:
	+ View analytics reports
	+ View analytics data
* **Consent**: Choose how you want to use your data:
	+ Display my data
	+ Analyze my data
	+ Both
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes you have.
* **Phone Number**: Type your phone number.

## Tips

* Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your administrator or contact us for assistance.
* This form is designed to be completed once; you don't need to fill it out again unless your information changes.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us at [support email/phone number].
