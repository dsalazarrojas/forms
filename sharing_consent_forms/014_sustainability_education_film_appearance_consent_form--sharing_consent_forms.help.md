# sustainability_education_film_appearance_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for participants, such as students or teachers, who will be featured in the sustainability education film. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Read through each page of the form carefully to understand the questions and fields.
2.  Fill out the form accurately and truthfully. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Introduction** (`Introduction`): A brief welcome message from the organization.
*  **name** (`name`): Your name as you want to be featured in the film. 
*  **email** (`email`): Your email address that will be used for communication. 
*  **phone** (`phone`): Your contact phone number. 
*  **work_usage** (`work_usage`): Select how you would like your work to be used in the film (`Image`, `Voice`, `Work`). 
*  **image_release** (`image_release`): Select how you would like your images to be released (`Still Images`, `Video`). 
*  **consent** (`consent`): Confirm your consent for the usage of your image, voice, and work in the film. 
*  **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`): Optional field for any additional comments or information you'd like to share. 
*  **participant_info** (`participant_info`): Optional field for any additional information about you. 
*  **participant_signature** (`participant_signature`): Your handwritten or printed signature. 
*  **participant_name** (`participant_name`): Your name for the film. 
*  **participant_school_npo_film** (`participant_school_npo_film`): The name of the school or nonprofit organization where you will be participating in the film. 
*  **other_school_npo_film** (`other_school_npo_film`): If your organization is not listed, enter the name of the school or organization where you will be participating. 
*  **participant_school_npo_email** (`participant_school_npo_email`): Your school or organization email address. 
*  **participant_school_npo_phone** (`participant_school_npo_phone`): Your school or organization contact phone number. 
*  **participant_school_npo_film_date** (`participant_school_npo_film_date`): The date of the film shoot. 
*  **participant_school_npo_film_time** (`participant_school_npo_film_time`): The time of the film shoot. 
*  **participant_school_npo_film_location** (`participant_school_npo_film_location`): The location of the film shoot. 
*  **participant_school_npo_film_additional_comments** (`participant_school_npo_film_additional_comments`): Optional field for any additional comments or information about your participation.
