# Student Testimonial Blog Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students to publish their testimonials on a blog, which will help to gather feedback and showcase student success stories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required information as prompted. Some fields are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: This is the name of the student who is providing the testimonial. Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: This is the email address associated with your university or institution. Please enter your university-issued email address.
* **Testimonial**: This is the content of your testimonial, describing your experience with the course, program, or service.
* **Date**: This is the date when you are providing your testimonial. Please use the date format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Publication Consent**: Please select one of the options provided, indicating your consent for the publication of your testimonial.
* **Student ID**: This is an optional field to identify you as a student. If you have a student ID, please enter it.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is used to identify the tool or resource that was assigned to you. Please enter the name of the tool or resource.
* **Category**: This is the category under which your testimonial will be published. Please enter a relevant category.
* **Category 2**: Another category under which your testimonial will be published.
* **Category 3**: Another category under which your testimonial will be published.
* **Department**: The department with which you have been associated. Please enter the department name.
* **School**: The school where you are enrolled. Please enter the school name.
* **Instructor**: The instructor who taught the course. Please enter the instructor's name.
* **Course**: The course under which you have been enrolled. Please enter the course name.
* **Course 2**: Another course under which you have been enrolled.
* **Course 3**: Another course under which you have been enrolled.
* **Program**: The program under which you have been enrolled. Please enter the program name.
* **Level**: The level of the program. Please enter the level (e.g., undergraduate, graduate).
* **Year**: The year of enrollment. Please enter the year.
* **Photo**: An optional field to attach a photo related to your testimonial.
* **Testimonial Blog URL**: The URL of the testimonial blog where your testimonial will be published.
* **Publication Consent Date**: The date of your publication consent. Please use the date format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Assigned Tool ID**: This is an optional field to identify the tool or resource that was assigned to you. If you have a tool ID, please enter it.
* **Form Type**: This is the type of form you are submitting. Please select one of the options provided.

Please note that some of the fields are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
