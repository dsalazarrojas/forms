# Public Art Mural Inclusion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide is designed to help you understand how to complete the Public Art Mural Inclusion Consent Form successfully.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Read the guide carefully** before proceeding.
2. Enter your First Name in the `first_name` field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the `last_name` field.
4. Enter your Email Address in the `email` field.
5. Enter the location of the mural you wish to be part of in the `mural_location` field.
6. Enter your Phone Number in the `phone_number` field.
7. Select one of the options for `mural_participation`:
	* Artwork
	* Mural
	* Both
8. Select one of the options for `permission`:
	* Permission granted
	* Permission pending
	* Permission denied
9. You may leave the Signature fields blank if not required or needed in your location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name**: A text field where you enter your First Name.
* **last_name**: A text field where you enter your Last Name.
* **email**: An email field where you enter your Email Address.
* **mural_location**: A text field where you enter the location of the mural you wish to be part of.
* **phone_number**: A text field where you enter your Phone Number.
* **mural_participation**: A selection field with options:
	+ **Artwork**: You are submitting artwork for inclusion.
	+ **Mural**: You are participating as an artist or designer for the mural.
	+ **Both**: You are both submitting artwork and participating as an artist or designer for the mural.
* **permission**: A selection field with options:
	+ **Permission granted**: You grant permission for your artwork to be displayed in the mural.
	+ **Permission pending**: Your permission is pending and will be confirmed later.
	+ **Permission denied**: You deny permission for your artwork to be displayed in the mural.
* **signature**: A timestamp field where a signature can be left if required.
* **signature2**: Another timestamp field for leaving a second signature, also optional.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have filled out the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out.
* You can reach us at form@gic.mx.
