# Digital App Notification Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital App Notification Consent Form is a form where users can provide their consent for receiving digital app notifications. This form is essential to ensure that users are aware of what types of notifications they will receive and how often they will be sent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the User Details section.
2. Choose the notification channels you would like to receive notifications from in the notification_channels section.
3. Select the frequency of the notifications in the notification_frequency section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Details
* **User Details (User Details)** (`text`, **required: false**): This section is for collecting the user's basic details. Please fill out this section with your name and any other relevant information you'd like to provide.

### Choose Channels
* **Choose Channels (notification_channels)** (`select_multiple`, **required: false**): In this section, you can choose which notification channels you would like to receive notifications from. Please select all the channels that apply to you, such as email, SMS, or in-app notifications. 

### How Often
* **How Often (notification_frequency)** (`number`, **required: false**): Here you can select how often you would like to receive notifications. Please choose from the available options or enter a custom frequency. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out this form carefully, as your consent is required for receiving digital app notifications.
* Review the notification channels and frequency options carefully before making your selection.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
