# Donor Recognition Wall Inclusion Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Donor Recognition Wall Inclusion Consent Form is used to collect information from donors to display on the Donor Recognition Wall, a public display of donors to the organization. By filling out this form, you are giving us permission to include your name and (optional) photo in the Donor Recognition Wall. Please review the following fields carefully to ensure accurate display of your information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name: Enter your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your organization (optional): If you are representing an organization, please enter its name.
3. Enter your email address: We will use this to contact you about your inclusion in the Donor Recognition Wall.
4. Provide your phone number (optional): This is for internal contact purposes.
5. Upload a photo (optional): You can upload a photo of yourself to display on the Donor Recognition Wall.
6. Determine sharing preferences: Choose how you would like your information to be shared.
   - "True" for Public: Share your information with everyone on the Donor Recognition Wall.
   - "False" for Private: Share your information only with internal stakeholders.
7. Choose specific sharing groups: Select which groups you would like to share your information with, including "Public", "Private", or "Internal".
8. Provide additional sharing information (optional): If you selected "Internal" or "Private", please enter specific individuals or groups you would like to share your information with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Recognition Wall Inclusion Consent** (`donor_recognition_wall_inclusion_consent`, note, optional):
	+ This field is not a typical "fill-in" field; it's simply a note to remind you of the purpose of this form.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required):
	+ Enter your first name to be displayed on the Donor Recognition Wall.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required):
	+ Enter your last name to be displayed on the Donor Recognition Wall.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, not required):
	+ If you are representing an organization, enter its name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required):
	+ Enter your email address for us to contact you about your inclusion in the Donor Recognition Wall.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required):
	+ Enter your phone number for internal contact purposes.
* **Photo** (`photo`, note, not required):
	+ You can upload a photo of yourself to display on the Donor Recognition Wall.
* **Share with** (`share_with`, select_one, not required):
	+ Choose how you would like your information to be shared:
		- "True" for Public: Share your information with everyone on the Donor Recognition Wall.
		- "False" for Private: Share your information only with internal stakeholders.
* **Share with** (`share_with_who`, select_multiple, not required):
	+ Select specific groups to share your information with:
		- Public
		- Private
		- Internal
* **Share with Others** (`share_with_who_other`, text, not required):
	+ If you selected "Internal" or "Private", provide specific individuals or groups you would like to share your information with.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required):
	+ Enter any additional information or comments you would like to share.
