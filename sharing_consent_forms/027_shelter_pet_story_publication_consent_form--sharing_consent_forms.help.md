# Shelter Pet Story Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from users and shelters for the publication of their stories and media. It's a necessary step to ensure that their content is used with their approval and consent.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the User Information section with your name, email, and phone number.
2.  Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
3.  Select the Type of Consent that applies to you (Shelter Pet Photo, Shelter Pet Story, or Both).
4.  Enter the URL of the shelter story you want to publish, if applicable.
5.  Enter the text of your story, if applicable.
6.  Enter the URL of any media associated with your story, if applicable.
7.  Add a caption and description for the media, if applicable.
8.  Sign and date your consent.
9.  Fill out the Shelter Information section with your shelter's name and address.
10. Fill out the Contact Information section with your contact's name and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information** (`User Information`, text, required): This is where you'll provide your basic contact information, including your name, email, and phone number. Please ensure you fill this out accurately to facilitate communication with you about your story.
*   **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, required): This is where you'll provide your contact details so we can get in touch with you for further discussion or questions related to your story.
*   **Consent Date** (`Consent Date`, date, required): Please enter the date you're giving your consent for the publication of your story and media. This is a formal record of when you're authorizing us to use your content.
*   **Type of Consent** (`Type of Consent`, select_one, required): Choose the type of consent that applies to you - Shelter Pet Photo, Shelter Pet Story, or Both. This will help us understand what content you're consenting to.
*   **Shelter Story URL** (`Shelter Story URL`, text, optional): If you're a shelter and this story is about your organization, please enter the URL of the story on your website.
*   **Story Text** (`Story Text`, text, optional): This is where you can enter the text of your story. If you have a longer story, consider attaching a link to it instead of typing it in here.
*   **Media URL** (`Media URL`, text, optional): If you've created media related to your story (e.g., videos, photos), enter the URL here so we can access it.
*   **Media Caption** (`Media Caption`, text, optional): Add a caption for your media to give context about what it's about and why you created it.
*   **Media Description** (`Media Description`, text, optional): Add a description of your media to help us understand its content and purpose.
*   **Consent Signature** (`Consent Signature`, note, required): This is where you'll sign and date your consent for the publication of your content. Please print your name and date it accurately.
*   **Shelter Name** (`Shelter Name`, text, optional): If you're a shelter, enter your official name here.
*   **Shelter Address** (`Shelter Address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your shelter so we can get in touch with you.
*   **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, text, optional): If you've designated someone at your shelter as a contact for questions or concerns, enter their name here.
*   **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, optional): Enter the email of your contact person at your shelter.
*   **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your contact person at your shelter.

## Tips

*   Always fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
*   Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
*   If you're unsure about any field, contact us for clarification.
*   This form is a legal document, so please handle it with care.
