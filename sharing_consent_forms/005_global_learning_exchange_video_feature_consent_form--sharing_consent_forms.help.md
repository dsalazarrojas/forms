# Global Learning Exchange Video Feature Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a video that you want to feature in the Global Learning Exchange. 
You will be asked to provide details about yourself and your video, which will help us review and consider your submission for publication on the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First name and Last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you regarding your submission.
3. Provide the Date of Birth and Time of Birth for your video feature.
4. Enter the URL of your video (e.g., YouTube, Vimeo, etc.).
5. Enter a title for your video.
6. Provide a brief description of your video.
7. Enter the duration of your video (e.g., length in minutes or hours).
8. If you'd like to add any additional comments or details about your video, you can do so in the optional Video Description field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is spelled on official documents such as your passport or ID.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is spelled on official documents such as your passport or ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address associated with your account on the platform or the email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your Date of Birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, required): Enter your Time of Birth in the format HH:MM AM/PM (e.g., 10:45 AM).
* **Video URL** (`video_url`, text, required): Enter the URL of the video you'd like to feature (e.g., YouTube, Vimeo, etc.).
* **Video Title** (`video_title`, text, required): Enter a short title for your video that summarizes its content.
* **Video Duration** (`video_duration`, text, required): Enter the length of your video in minutes or hours (e.g., 10 minutes, 1 hour).
* **Video Description** (`video_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your video that will help us evaluate it for publication.
* **Video Description** (`video_description`, text, optional): This field is optional but will help us get a better understanding of your video. Please feel free to add any additional comments or details that you think are relevant to your submission.
