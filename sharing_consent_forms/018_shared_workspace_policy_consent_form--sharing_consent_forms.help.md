# Shared Workspace Policy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect your consent to share your workspace with others. It will ask you for information about your preferences for sharing, including your contact details, notification settings, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out each field completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and implications.
3. Click the "Submit" button to submit your consent form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name? (Text)**: This field is where you should enter your name as it should be displayed in the shared workspace environment.
* **What is your email address? (Email)**: Please enter your email address where you can be reached for sharing and collaboration.
* **What is your phone number? (Text)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for sharing and collaboration.
* **Would you like to share your workspace? (Select Multiple)**: Choose when you would like to share your workspace, including options for "True", "False", and "Maybe".
* **What date would you like to start sharing your workspace from? (Date)**: Enter the date when you would like to start sharing your workspace.
* **What time would you like to share your workspace during? (Time)**: Enter the time of day when you would like to start sharing your workspace.
* **Do you have any additional comments or notes? (Text)**: Enter any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with the workspace administrators.
* **What is the start date of your availability? (Date)**: Enter the start date when you are available to share your workspace.
* **What is the end date of your availability? (Date)**: Enter the end date when you are no longer available to share your workspace.
* **Would you like to be notified via email or phone when someone tries to share your workspace? (Select Multiple)**: Choose how you would like to be notified, including options for "True", "False", and "Maybe".
* **What time do you like to be notified at? (Time)**: Enter the time of day when you would like to receive notifications.
* **Would you like to share your workspace with others on a specific date and time? (Select One)**: Choose whether you would like to share your workspace with others on a specific date and time, including options for "True" and "False".

## Tips

- If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to your workspace administrators for clarification.
- Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- Submit your form only when you're confident about the information you've provided.
