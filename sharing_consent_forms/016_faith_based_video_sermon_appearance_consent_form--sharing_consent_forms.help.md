# Faith Based Video Sermon Appearance Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent from users for the appearance of their faith-based video sermons. It is intended to capture information about the user's contact details, permissions, recording settings, livestream preferences, recording details, sharing preferences, and form submission. The goal is to ensure that users understand and agree to the terms of their content being shared with others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "User Input" field with your name and any additional information you'd like to share.
2. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
3. Specify your permissions in the "Permissions" field.
4. Choose your recording settings in the "Recording Settings" field.
5. Select your livestream preferences in the "Livestream" field.
6. Share your recording details in the "Recording Details" field.
7. Indicate your sharing preferences in the "Sharing Preferences" field.
8. Finally, click "Form Submission" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): A field for you to enter any additional information you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, such as your name, email, and phone number.
* **Permissions** (`permissions`, text, optional): Specify what you are willing to allow or deny in terms of content sharing and usage.
* **Recording Settings** (`recording_settings`, text, optional): Choose your recording settings, such as audio or video recording.
* **Livestream** (`livestream`, text, optional): Select your livestream preference, such as public or private.
* **Recording Details** (`recording_details`, text, optional): Share your recording details, such as date, time, and location.
* **Sharing Preferences** (`sharing_preferences`, text, optional): Indicate how you'd like your content to be shared, such as publicly or privately.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, text, optional): Click to submit your form.
