# Cost Allocation Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cost Allocation Sharing Consent Form is used to obtain approval for cost-sharing arrangements between the company and third-party entities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the provided instructions and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements of the form.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), which include:
	* Approver Name
	* Approver Signature
3. Fill out the optional fields as needed, which include:
	* Cost-Sharing Agreement (provide a brief description of the agreement)
	* Approval Status (choose from Approved, Denied, or Pending)
	* Date Approved (if approved)
	* Approval Date (if required)
	* Approver Comments (any additional information about the approval process)
	* Approver Email (if you want to provide contact information for the approver)
	* Approver Phone (if you want to provide contact information for the approver)
	* Approver Title (if you want to specify the title of the approver)
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cost-Sharing Agreement** (`cost_sharing_agreement`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the cost-sharing agreement, including the terms and conditions of the arrangement.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the approval (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* **Date Approved** (`date_approved`, date, optional): If the agreement has been approved, enter the date of approval.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, time, optional): If required, enter the time of approval.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the approval process.
* **Approver Comments** (`approver_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the approval process from the approver.
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person approving the cost-sharing agreement.
* **Approver Title** (`approver_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the person approving the cost-sharing agreement.
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person approving the cost-sharing agreement.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person approving the cost-sharing agreement.
* **Approver Signature** (`approver_signature`, time, required): Enter the signature of the person approving the cost-sharing agreement, with the date and time of signature.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask for clarification or guidance from the approver or relevant personnel.
* Keep a record of the completed form for reference and future use.
