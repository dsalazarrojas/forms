# Utility Supplier Data Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Utility Supplier Data Sharing Consent Form is used to obtain the consent of utility customers to share their data with third-party suppliers. This form ensures that customers are aware of and agree to the sharing of their data, which may include sensitive information such as personal details, consumption history, and account balances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each page and select the relevant options for each field.
2. Enter the required information in the relevant fields.
3. Make sure to check or uncheck the options that apply to you.
4. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Permission** (`customer_permission`, select_multiple, required: false):
  This field allows you to indicate whether you grant or deny permission for your data to be shared with third-party suppliers.
* **Utility Data** (`utility_data`, text, required: false):
  Enter your utility account information, such as your account number or account name.
* **Sharing Purpose** (`sharing_purpose`, text, required: false):
  Describe the specific purpose for which your data will be shared with third-party suppliers.
* **Sharing Duration** (`sharing_duration`, text, required: false):
  Specify the duration for which your data will be shared with third-party suppliers.
* **Customer Suppliers** (`customer_suppliers`, select_multiple, required: false):
  Indicate which of your suppliers you grant or deny permission to access your data.

## Tips
* Make sure to carefully review each field and select the options that apply to you.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, contact your utility supplier's customer service team.
* This form is for your consent only, and you can withdraw your consent at any time.
