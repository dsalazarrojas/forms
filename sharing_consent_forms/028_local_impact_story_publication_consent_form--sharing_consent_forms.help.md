# Local Impact Story Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Local Impact Story Publication Consent Form is used to collect contact information and consent for publishing local impact stories. This form helps us understand who to contact and share stories with in case of emergencies or next-of-kin notifications. Please complete this form to provide necessary information for the story publication process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you've understood the purpose and fields.
2. Fill in your personal details, such as your name, email, and contact information.
3. Provide emergency contact information, including relationship, name, phone number, and email.
4. Identify next-of-kin contact information, including relationship, name, phone number, and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1): A brief overview of the form's purpose and context. Please write a short paragraph describing why you're filling out this form.
* **Name** (Page 2): Your full name, as you'd like to be referred to in the story publication.
* **Email** (Page 3): Your email address, which we'll use for contact and publication notifications.
* **Date of Birth** (Page 4): Your date of birth, which helps us identify you in our records.
* **Time of Birth** (Page 5): Your time of birth, which helps us understand your age and identity.
* **Contact Phone** (Page 6): Your personal phone number, which we'll use for contact purposes.
* **Address** (Page 7): Your address, which helps us locate you in case of emergencies.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Page 8): The relationship between you and your emergency contact, such as "Aunt" or "Brother".
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Page 9): The name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Page 10): The phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Page 11): The email address of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (Page 12): The address of your emergency contact person, which helps us locate them in case of emergencies.
* **Next of Kin** (Page 13): The relationship between you and your next-of-kin contact, such as "Father" or "Mother".
* **Next of Kin Name** (Page 14): The name of your next-of-kin contact person.
* **Next of Kin Phone** (Page 15): The phone number of your next-of-kin contact person.
* **Next of Kin Email** (Page 16): The email address of your next-of-kin contact person.
