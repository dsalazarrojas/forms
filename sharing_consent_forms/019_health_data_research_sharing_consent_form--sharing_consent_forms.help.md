# Health Data Research Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Data Research Sharing Consent Form is designed to obtain consent from participants in health data research studies. This form ensures that participants understand how their health data will be used and shared, and obtain their explicit consent for such use and sharing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant information section, which includes demographic details about yourself.
2. Provide contact information, so that we can contact you for further information or to clarify any questions.
3. Describe the study details, including any relevant background information or research question.
4. Specify your sharing preferences for your health data.
5. Sign the consent form by choosing "I agree" from the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, required): This field is used to capture demographic details about you, including your name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Enter your contact information so that we can reach you if needed.
* **Study Details** (`study_details`, text, required): Describe the study details, including any relevant background information or research question.
* **Sharing Preferences** (`sharing_preferences`, text, optional): Specify how you wish to share your health data. For example, you can choose to share your data with other researchers, or keep it private.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): This field is used to obtain your explicit consent for sharing your health data. Please choose "I agree" to indicate your consent.
* **Consent 2** (`consent_2`, text, optional): This field is used to capture any additional comments or questions you may have about sharing your health data.
* **Consent 2** (`consent_2_2`, text, optional): This field is used to capture any additional comments or questions you may have about sharing your health data.
