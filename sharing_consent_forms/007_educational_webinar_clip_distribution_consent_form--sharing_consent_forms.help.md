# Educational Webinar Clip Distribution Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from participants for educational webinar clip distribution. It is used to ensure that participants understand how their data will be shared and used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) next to the label.
* For select_one fields, choose the correct option from the dropdown menu.
* Enter your email address in the correct format (username@domain.com).
* If you have any comments, type them in the text field below.
* Review your answers and ensure that you are comfortable with the terms of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (`participant_name`, `text`, *): This field is used to identify you as a participant. Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **What is your role?** (`participant_role`, `text`, *): This field is used to identify your role in the educational webinar, such as Student, Teacher, or Parent.
* **Do you consent to share educational webinar clips?** (`share_consent`, `select_one`, *): Choose 'True' to indicate that you consent to sharing your clips.
* **How would you like to be contacted for future webinars?** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, *): Choose how you would like to be contacted for future webinars (Email, Phone, or Other).
* **How will you be sharing the clips?** (`share_method`, `select_one`, *): Choose how you will be sharing the clips (Publicly, Privately).
* **What is your email address?** (`email_address`, `email`, *): This field is used to collect your email address for communication purposes.
* **How will you be using the clips?** (`use_purpose`, `select_one`, *): Choose how you will be using the clips (For personal use, For teaching, or For research).
* **Do you consent to share educational webinar clips with other institutions?** (`share_institutions`, `select_one`, *): Choose 'True' to indicate that you consent to sharing your clips with other institutions.
* **Would you like to provide any additional comments about the clips?** (`comments`, `text`, ): This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback about the clips.
* **Have you ever participated in a similar activity before?** (`previous_participation`, `select_one`, ): Choose 'True' if you have participated in a similar activity before.
* **Do you have any other questions or concerns about the consent form?** (`other_questions`, `text`, ): This field is optional and allows you to ask any questions or concerns about the consent form.
