# Smart Key Integration Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Smart Key Integration Consent Form is a critical step in setting up Smart Key Integration, a feature that allows you to control access to your smart lock. This form ensures that you understand the implications and permissions associated with the integration and grants access to the smart lock.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Smart Lock Integration**: Provide your consent for Smart Key Integration by confirming that you understand its implications and permissions.
2. **User Details**: Enter your user details as requested.
3. **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication and notifications.
4. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for emergency access and notifications.
5. **Confirm Password**: Confirm your password to secure your account.
6. **Smart Lock Model**: Select the model of your smart lock.
7. **Lock Access Code**: Enter the 6-digit code for your smart lock.
8. **Smart Lock Location**: Enter the location of your smart lock.
9. **Smart Lock Status**: Select the status of your smart lock (active or inactive).
10. **Smart Lock Access**: Decide whether to grant access to your smart lock.
11. **Smart Lock Notes**: Add any additional notes about your smart lock.
12. **Smart Lock Email**: Enter your email address for smart lock communication (optional).
13. **Smart Lock Phone**: Enter your phone number for smart lock emergencies (optional).
14. **Smart Lock Model**: Select the model of your smart lock.
15. **Smart Lock Access Type**: Select the access type (keycard or biometric).
16. **Smart Lock Access Level**: Choose the access level (low, medium, or high).
17. **Smart Lock Device**: Enter the device associated with your smart lock.
18. **Smart Lock Notes**: Add any additional notes about your smart lock.
19. **Smart Key Integration**: Decide whether to enable Smart Key Integration.
20. **Smart Lock Access Status**: Select the status of your smart lock access (granted or denied).
21. **Smart Lock Access Code**: Enter the 6-digit access code for your smart lock.
22. **Smart Lock Access Time**: Set the access time for your smart lock.
23. **Smart Lock Access Duration**: Set the duration of access for your smart lock.
24. **Smart Lock Access Frequency**: Enter the frequency of access for your smart lock.
25. **Smart Lock Access Comments**: Add any comments about access to your smart lock.
