# emotional_resilience_program_filming_consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain participant's consent for filming and recording activities within the Emotional Resilience Program. It ensures that participants' rights and interests are protected and that they understand the terms and conditions of filming and recording.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please carefully read each field and follow the instructions provided. Fill in all required fields and select options as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Identifier** (`participant_identifier`, text, required): Enter your participant identifier or ID number assigned to you by the program organizers.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter your contact information for the program organizers, if applicable.
* **Filming Permission** (`filming_permission`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options: 
	+ Filming: You grant permission for filming to take place.
	+ Not Filming: You do not grant permission for filming.
	+ No Preference: You have no preference for filming.
* **Photo Permission** (`photo_permission`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options: 
	+ Photos: You grant permission for photos to be taken.
	+ No Photos: You do not grant permission for photos.
	+ No Preference: You have no preference for photos.
* **Recording Permission** (`recording_permission`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options: 
	+ Recording: You grant permission for recording.
	+ No Recording: You do not grant permission for recording.
	+ No Preference: You have no preference for recording.
* **Video** (`video_permission`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options: 
	+ Video: You grant permission for video recording.
	+ No Video: You do not grant permission for video recording.
	+ No Preference: You have no preference for video recording.
* **Audio** (`audio_permission`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options: 
	+ Audio: You grant permission for audio recording.
	+ No Audio: You do not grant permission for audio recording.
	+ No Preference: You have no preference for audio recording.
* **Filming Location** (`filming_location`, text, optional): Enter the filming location, if applicable.
* **Filming Purpose** (`filming_purpose`, text, optional): Enter the filming purpose, if applicable.
* **Filming Duration** (`filming_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of filming.
* **Filming Frequency** (`filming_frequency`, number, required): Enter the filming frequency.
* **Recording Location** (`recording_location`, text, optional): Enter the recording location, if applicable.
* **Recording Purpose** (`recording_purpose`, text, optional): Enter the recording purpose, if applicable.
* **Recording Duration** (`recording_duration`, number, optional): Enter the recording duration.
* **Recording Frequency** (`recording_frequency`, number, optional): Enter the recording frequency.
* **Video Recording Format** (`video_recording_format`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following options: 
	+ 360 degree video
	+ 2D Video
	+ 3D Video
	+ Audio Recording
* **Photo Recording Format** (`photo_recording_format`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following options: 
	+ 2D Photos
	+ 3D Photos
* **Audio Recording Format** (`audio_recording_format`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following options: 
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Video Release** (`video_release`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options: 
	+ True: You grant permission for video release.
	+ False: You do not grant permission for video release.
	+ No Preference: You have no preference for video release.
* **Photo Release** (`photo_release`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options: 
	+ True: You grant permission for photo release.
	+ False: You do not grant permission for photo release.
	+ No Preference: You have no preference for photo release.
* **Audio Release** (`audio_release`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options: 
	+ True: You grant permission for audio release.
	+ False: You do not grant permission for audio release.
	+ No Preference: You have no preference for audio release.
* **Recording Agreement** (`recording_agreement`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options: 
	+ True: You agree to the recording agreement.
	+ False: You do not agree to the recording agreement.
	+ No Preference: You have no preference for recording agreement.
* **Filming Agreement** (`filming_agreement`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options: 
	+ True: You agree to the filming agreement.
	+ False: You do not agree to the filming agreement.
	+ No Preference: You have no preference for filming agreement.
* **Photo Agreement** (`photo_agreement`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options: 
	+ True: You agree to the photo agreement.
	+ False: You do not agree to the photo agreement.
	+ No Preference: You have no preference for photo agreement.
* **Audio Agreement** (`audio_agreement`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options: 
	+ True: You agree to the audio agreement.
	+ False: You do not agree to the audio agreement.
	+ No Preference: You have no preference for audio agreement.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have read and understood all the terms and conditions before signing this form. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the program organizers.
