# Hybrid Learning Session Replay Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hybrid Learning Session Replay Consent Form is designed to collect information from participants to record and replay their learning sessions. By filling out this form, participants provide their consent to be recorded and give permission for their sessions to be replayed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your name in the "Participant Name" field.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3.  Enter the name of the course you are participating in.
4.  Select the date of the session you are attending.
5.  Choose the time of the session.
6.  Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to send your consent form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents. This will help us identify you correctly.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your official email address. This will help us contact you for any further information or clarification.
*   **Name of the Course** (`name_of_the_course`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the course you are participating in. This will help us identify the course correctly.
*   **Date of the Session** (`date_of_the_session`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of the session you are attending. Please use the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   **Time of the Session** (`time_of_the_session`, `time`, required/optional): Choose the time of the session you are attending. Please use the 12-hour clock format (e.g., 01:00 PM).
