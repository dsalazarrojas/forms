# Content Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from content owners to share their work, ensuring they are aware of where and how it will be shared, credited, and for how long.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name and email address in the "Full Name of Content Owner" and "Email Address" fields, respectively.
2. Provide a brief description of the content you are allowing us to share, including its title or a brief description.
3. Enter the original URL or social media handle where the content was originally posted.
4. Select the platforms where you want your content to be shared (e.g., our website, Instagram, LinkedIn, Email Newsletters, or Digital Presentations/Webinars).
5. Choose how you want to be credited for your work (e.g., with your name and link to your profile, just with your name, or anonymously).
6. Select the duration you wish for us to share your content (e.g., 6 months, 1 year, 2 years, or indefinitely).
7. If you are receiving compensation for sharing your content, select the correct option from the "Is there any compensation for this sharing" field.
8. Finally, read through the "Any specific limitations or terms" field to add any additional terms or conditions you wish to include.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name of Content Owner** (`content_owner_name`, text, required): This field is where you enter your full name as the content owner.
- **Email Address** (`owner_email`, email, required): This field is where you enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Title or Brief Description of Content** (`content_title_description`, text, required): This is a brief description of the content you are allowing us to share.
- **Original URL or Social Media Handle** (`content_url_handle`, text, required): Enter the original URL or social media handle where the content was originally posted.
- **Approved Platforms for Sharing** (`approved_platforms`, select_multiple, required): Select all the platforms where you want your content to be shared.
- **Attribution Requirement** (`attribution_requirement`, select_one, required): Choose how you want to be credited for your work.
- **Duration of Consent** (`duration_of_consent`, select_one, required): Select how long you want us to share your content.
- **Is there any compensation for this sharing?** (`compensation_disclosure`, select_one, required): If you are receiving compensation for sharing your content, select the correct option.
- **Any specific limitations or terms** (`additional_terms`, text, optional): Add any additional terms or conditions you wish to include.

Note: Please ensure you have read and understood all the terms before signing. By signing, you confirm that you are the sole owner of the content and grant permission for us to share it as specified above.
