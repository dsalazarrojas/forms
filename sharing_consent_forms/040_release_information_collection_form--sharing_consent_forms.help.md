# Release Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Release Information Collection Form is designed to capture essential details about the release of sensitive information, ensuring accuracy and completeness in the record-keeping process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read and understand the form instructions and purpose.
2. Select the correct "Release Information Collection Form Type" that applies to your situation.
3. Fill out the "Release Information Collection Form Description" field with a brief description of the release information.
4. In the "Content Release Details" field, select the specific purpose of the release (e.g., business, agency, school, or nonprofit).
5. Enter the "Consent Form Purpose" field with a brief statement indicating your understanding of the form's purpose.
6. Provide the "Release Date" and "Release Time" fields with the exact date and time of the release.
7. Enter the "Release Location" field with the specific location of the release.
8. Enter the "Person In Charge" field with the name and title of the person responsible for the release.
9. Enter the "Email For Contact" and "Phone For Contact" fields with the contact information for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Release Information Collection Form Type** (`consent_form_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of release (Business, Agency, School, or Nonprofit) that applies to your situation.
* **Release Information Collection Form Description** (`release_information_form_description`, text, required: false): A brief description of the release information.
* **Content Release Details** (`content_release_details`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the specific purpose of the release (e.g., business, agency, school, or nonprofit).
* **Consent Form Purpose** (`consent_form_purpose`, text, required: false): A brief statement indicating your understanding of the form's purpose.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, required: false): Enter the exact date of the release.
* **Release Time** (`release_time`, time, required: false): Enter the exact time of the release.
* **Release Location** (`release_location`, text, required: false): Enter the specific location of the release.
* **Person In Charge** (`release_person_in_charge`, text, required: false): Enter the name and title of the person responsible for the release.
* **Email For Contact** (`email_for_contact`, email, required: false): Enter the email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone For Contact** (`phone_for_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number for contact purposes.
