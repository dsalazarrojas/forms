# talent_demo_reel_usage_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Demo Reel Usage Consent Form is used to obtain consent from users for the usage of a demo reel in a business or professional setting. It helps to ensure that users understand and agree to certain terms and conditions related to the demo reel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the terms and conditions.
2. Fill out the contact details section with your relevant information.
3. Provide any additional contact information if required.
4. Indicate your consent by selecting "I Agree" or "I Do Not Agree" for the terms and conditions.
5. Enter any additional details about the demo reel usage if required.
6. Review and submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): Enter your name, address, and other relevant contact information.
* **Do you agree** (`consent_agreement`): Select "I Agree" or "I Do Not Agree" to indicate your consent.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date you are providing this consent.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Additional Contact Information** (`contact_details_2`): Enter any additional contact information if required.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Additional Contact Information** (`additional_contact_info`): Enter any additional contact information if required.
* **Demo Reel Usage** (`demo_reel_usage`): Enter any details about the demo reel usage if required.
* **Demo Reel Link** (`demo_reel_link`): Enter the link to the demo reel if required.
* **Additional Agreement** (`additional_agreement`): A note field to add any additional agreements or comments.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_conditions`): A note field to add any terms and conditions.
* **Additional Contact Information 2** (`contact_details_3`): Enter any additional contact information if required.
* **Contact Number 2** (`contact_number_2`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email 2** (`contact_email_2`): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Info 2** (`contact_info_2`): Enter any additional contact information if required.
* **Signature 2** (`signature_2`): Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Terms of Agreement** (`terms_agreement`): Select "Agree" or "Disagree" to indicate your consent to the terms of agreement.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`): Select "Agree" or "Disagree" to indicate your consent to the terms of agreement.
* **Terms of Agreement 2** (`terms_agreement_2`): Select "Agree" or "Disagree" to indicate your consent to the terms of agreement.
* **Demo Reel Usage 2** (`demo_reel_usage_2`): Enter any details about the demo reel usage if required.
