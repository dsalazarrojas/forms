# pet_spa_media_publication_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Spa Media Publication Consent Form is used to capture consent and information about pets for publication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the relevant information about the pet, its owner, and other details.
2. Make sure to select the correct consent options.
3. Sign and date all relevant sections where necessary.
4. Review the form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Spa Media Publication** (`pet_spa_media_publication`): Enter the title of the publication.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the email address of the pet's owner.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the phone number of the pet's owner.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`): Enter the name of the pet's owner.
* **Photo** (`photo`): Upload a photo of the pet (optional).
* **Video** (`video`): Upload a video of the pet (optional).
* **Online Channels** (`online_channels`): List the online channels where the pet's information will be published.
* **Print Channels** (`print_channels`): List the print channels where the pet's information will be published.
* **Social Media Channels** (`social_media_channels`): List the social media channels where the pet's information will be published.
* **Consent** (`consent`): Select whether the pet's owner has given consent for publication. Options: True or False.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or information about the pet.
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`): Select the type of pet (Dog, Cat, Bird, Fish, Other).
* **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`): Enter the breed of the pet.
* **Color** (`color`): Enter the color of the pet.
* **Weight** (`weight`): Enter the weight of the pet.
* **Age** (`age`): Enter the age of the pet.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter the date of birth of the pet.
* **Date of Admission** (`date_of_admission`): Enter the date the pet was admitted or adopted.
* **Owner Signature** (`owner_signature`): Sign and date the owner's signature.
* **Owner Contact** (`owner_contact`): Enter the contact information of the pet's owner.
* **Vet Signature** (`vet_signature`): Sign and date the vet's signature.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`): Enter any additional information about the pet.
* **Consent Signature** (`consent_signature`): Sign and date the consent.
* **Date of Consent** (`date_of_consent`): Enter the date of consent.
* **Signature of Owner and Vet** (`signature_of_owner_and_vet`): Select whether both owner and vet signed the consent. Options: Both Owners, Both Owners and Vet, Only Owner, Only Vet.
* **Date of Signature** (`date_of_signature`): Enter the date of signature.
