# Entertainment Sponsorship Mention Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for entertainment sponsors to provide their consent for being mentioned or featured in various formats (such as social media, print, and online).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

* Fill in the form title (step 1).
* Enter the sponsor's name and email address (steps 2-3, if applicable).
* Select the formats in which they would like to be mentioned (step 4, select all applicable options).
* Check the box to consent to being mentioned (step 5).
* Optionally, specify their preferred mention format (step 6).
* Finally, provide any additional details about their mention preferences (step 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (Step 1): A text field to fill in a brief title for the sponsorship mention.
* **Sponsor Name** (Step 2): A text field to enter the sponsor's name.
* **Sponsor Email** (Step 3): An email field to enter the sponsor's email address.
* **Mention Formats** (Step 4): A select multiple field to choose the formats in which the sponsor would like to be mentioned (e.g., Social Media, Print, Online).
* **Consent** (Step 5): A select one field to confirm or decline being mentioned in any format.
* **Preferred Mention Format** (Step 6): A text field to optionally specify the sponsor's preferred mention format.
* **Mention Details** (Step 7): A note field to provide any additional information about the sponsor's mention preferences.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (Form Title and Consent).
* If a sponsor wants to be mentioned in multiple formats, select all applicable options in Step 4.
* If a sponsor has any specific mention preferences or details, use the note field in Step 7 to provide more information.
