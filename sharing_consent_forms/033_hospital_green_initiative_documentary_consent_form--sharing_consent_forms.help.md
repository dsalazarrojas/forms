The Hospital Green Initiative Documentary Consent Form is a document used to obtain consent from patients, staff, and visitors for various hospital activities, such as filming, photography, or other forms of documentation. This form serves as a record of consent, ensuring that all parties involved are aware of and agree to the hospital's policies and procedures regarding documentation. The form must be completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure that all necessary information is captured.

# Hospital Green Initiative Documentary Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Green Initiative Documentary Consent Form is used to obtain consent from patients, staff, and visitors for various hospital activities.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Gather all required information and signatures from patients, staff, and visitors.
2. Complete the form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring that all fields are filled in correctly.
3. Obtain signatures from patients, staff, and facility personnel as required.
4. Keep a copy of the form for future reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required):
  This field is where you will input the patient's name, date of birth, address, and other relevant contact information.
- **Staff Information** (`staff_info`, text, required):
  This field is where you will input the staff member's name, title, and department.
- **Visitor Information** (`visitor_info`, text, required):
  This field is where you will input the visitor's name, date of visit, and contact information.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required):
  This field is where you will input the contact person's name and contact information.
- **Patient Consent** (`patient_consent`, select_one, required):
  This field allows you to select whether the patient has given their consent for documentation.
- **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required):
  This field requires the patient's signature as proof of consent.
- **Staff Consent** (`staff_consent`, select_one, required):
  This field allows you to select whether the staff member has given their consent for documentation.
- **Staff Signature** (`staff_signature`, text, required):
  This field requires the staff member's signature as proof of consent.
- **Visitor Consent** (`visitor_consent`, select_one, required):
  This field allows you to select whether the visitor has given their consent for documentation.
- **Visitor Signature** (`visitor_signature`, text, required):
  This field requires the visitor's signature as proof of consent.
- **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required):
  This field is the date on which the consent form was signed.
- **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, required):
  This field is the time on which the consent form was signed.
- **Signed By** (`signed_by`, text, required):
  This field is where you will input the person who signed the consent form.
- **Printed By** (`printed_by`, text, required):
  This field is where you will input the person who printed the consent form.
- **Facility Signature** (`facility_signature`, select_one, required):
  This field allows you to select whether the facility has given their consent for documentation.
- **Facility Signer** (`facility_signer`, text, required):
  This field requires the facility's signature as proof of consent.
- **Document Number** (`document_number`, text, required):
  This field is the unique ID number assigned to the consent form.
- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required):
  This field is where you will input the patient's name.
- **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, required):
  This field is where you will input the staff member's name.
- **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required):
  This field is where you will input the facility's name.
- **Document Title** (`document_title`, text, required):
  This field is the title of the document.
- **Document Date** (`document_date`, date, required):
  This field is the date on which the document was created.
- **Document Version** (`document_version`, number, required):
  This field is the version number of the document.
- **Document Status** (`document_status`, text, required):
  This field is the status of the document.
- **Document ID** (`document_id`, text, required):
  This field is the unique ID number assigned to the document.

Note: This help guide is meant to be a general reference for users. Always ensure that you have the necessary permissions and follow hospital policies before completing this form.
