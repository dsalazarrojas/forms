# Public Health Social Media Campaign Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain public health social media campaign consent from individuals. It helps to understand the demographics and contact information of participants, and to gauge their willingness to share their data on social media.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name.
2. Provide your last name.
3. Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1990-01-01).
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Enter your address.
7. Enter your city.
8. Enter your state (province or region).
9. Enter your zip code or postal code.
10. Check the box to indicate your consent to share your data on social media.
11. (Optional) If you have any other contact information, provide it in the text field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your mailing address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city or town where you reside or work.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the province or region where you reside or work.
* **Zip/Postal Code** (`zip`, number, optional): Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Consent to Share** (`consent_to_share`, select one, required): Check the box to indicate your consent to share your data on social media.
* **Other Contact Info** (`other_contact_info`, text, optional): If you have any other contact information, enter it here.
