# Senior Testimonial Story Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Senior Testimonial Story Consent Form is a document used to collect sensitive information about the senior's personal details, preferences, and experiences, and to obtain their consent for sharing this information with others. This form helps healthcare providers understand the senior's needs and make informed decisions about their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* Enter your email address (if you have one).
* Provide a brief description of your senior's story.
* Choose the individuals or groups you would like to share your senior's story with.
* Enter your senior's date of birth and date of death (if applicable).
* Provide your phone number and time zone (if applicable).
* Provide any additional notes or comments about your senior's story.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (`senior_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address (if you have one). Note: This field is optional but recommended for communication purposes.
* **Senior Story** (`senior_story`, note, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your senior's story. This can include their medical history, preferences, and experiences.
* **Share With** (`share_with`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the individuals or groups you would like to share your senior's story with, such as Family and friends, Healthcare provider, or Other.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birthday`, date, required/optional): Enter your senior's date of birth. This information is essential for understanding their medical history.
* **Date of Death** (`date_of_death`, date, required/optional): Enter your senior's date of death (if applicable). Note: If not deceased, leave field blank.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your senior's phone number. This is optional but recommended for communication purposes.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter your senior's time zone. This is optional but recommended for scheduling purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter your senior's date of death (if applicable). Note: If not deceased, leave field blank.
* **Senior Testimonial Story Consent** (`senior_testimonial_story_consent`, select_multiple, required/true): Check the box to indicate that you consent to share your senior's story with the chosen individuals or groups.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your senior's story.
