# Digital Accessibility Campaign Feature Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect participant consent for digital accessibility campaigns, including the use of their photos, features, and contributions. It is essential to fill out this form to ensure that your participation in the campaign is voluntary and compliant with the terms of use.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in a clear and legible handwriting.
2. Answer each question accurately and truthfully.
3. Make sure to read and understand each question before filling it out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: This section is for providing basic participant information, such as name, email, and other relevant details.
* **Participant Photo Consent**: This section is for obtaining participant consent for the use of their photo in the campaign.
* **Digital Campaign Contribution**: This section is for describing your contribution to the campaign, such as text, images, or videos.
* **Feature Consent**: This section is for obtaining participant consent for their features to be used in the campaign.
* **Feature Consent Terms of Use**: This section is for understanding how your features will be used in the campaign.
* **Feature Consent What We Do**: This section is for understanding how your features will be used in the campaign.
* **Feature Consent Data Privacy**: This section is for understanding how your data will be handled in the campaign.
* **Feature Consent Terms of Service**: This section is for understanding the terms of service for your participation in the campaign.
* **Feature Consent Contact Us**: This section is for contacting the campaign team with questions or concerns.
* **Feature Consent Privacy Policy**: This section is for understanding the campaign's privacy policy.
* **Feature Consent Additional Information**: This section is for providing any additional information about your consent.
* **Contribution Agreement**: This section is for understanding the terms of your contribution to the campaign.
* **Contribution Acknowledgement**: This section is for acknowledging your contribution to the campaign.
* **Contribution Disclaimer**: This section is for understanding the disclaimer for your contribution.
* **Contribution Statement**: This section is for providing a statement about your contribution.
* **Contribution Licence Agreement**: This section is for understanding the licence agreement for your contribution.
* **Contribution Contract**: This section is for understanding the contract for your contribution.
* **Contribution Agreement Review**: This section is for reviewing your contribution agreement.
* **Contribution Agreement Version**: This section is for version number of your contribution agreement.
* **Contribution Agreement Date**: This section is for the date of your contribution agreement.
* **Contribution Agreement Signed**: This section is for signing your contribution agreement.

**Tips**

* Make sure to read and understand each section carefully before filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any section, please ask the campaign team for clarification.
* Fill out the form truthfully and accurately to ensure that your participation in the campaign is voluntary and compliant with the terms of use.
