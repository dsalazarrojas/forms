# Smart Lighting Usage Data Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant consent for smart lighting usage data. The data will be used for research purposes to improve smart lighting systems.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Fill out the participant information (name, email, phone number) and select either "Yes" or "No" for participant consent and parent consent.
3. If the participant is a minor, provide parent/guardian consent in addition to participant consent.
4. Enter the utility name, installer name, and property manager name (if applicable).
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the participant note field.
6. Review the participant agreement and enter your signature to confirm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**:
	+ Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Participant Consent**:
	+ Select "Yes" to agree to the data collection for research purposes.
* **Parent Consent**:
	+ If the participant is a minor, select "Yes" for parent/guardian consent.
* **Participant Email**:
	+ Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Participant Phone**:
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Utility Name**:
	+ Enter the name of the utility company or service provider.
* **Installer Name**:
	+ Enter the name of the installer or service provider.
* **Property Manager Name**:
	+ Enter the name of the property manager or owner.
* **Smart Home Program**:
	+ Enter the name of the smart home program or system.
* **Participant Note**:
	+ Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Participant Agreement**:
	+ Review and sign the agreement to confirm your consent.
