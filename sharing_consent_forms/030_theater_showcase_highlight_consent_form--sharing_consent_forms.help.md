# Theater Showcase Highlight Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theater Showcase Highlight Consent Form is designed to collect necessary information from participants' parents or guardians to facilitate recording and sharing of performances during the Theater Showcase Highlight.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately with the participant's information.
2. Select the appropriate options for the Parent Consent, Permission Type, School Permission, and Recording Permission fields.
3. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to record and share the participant's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, not required): Enter the participant's name as it is written on their official documents (ID, passport, etc.).
* **Parent Consent** (`parent_consent`, select_one, not required): Select one of the options to express your consent for the participant's recording and sharing:
	+ Parent Consent Option 1: Give consent for recording and sharing.
	+ Parent Consent Option 2: Do not give consent for recording and sharing.
	+ Parent Consent Option 3: Do not want to receive further information.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, not required): Enter the participant's age.
* **Participant DOB** (`participant_dob`, date, not required): Enter the participant's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Performance Date** (`performance_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the performance.
* **Performance Time** (`performance_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the performance.
* **Recorded By** (`recorded_by`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person who recorded or will record the performance.
* **Permission Type** (`permission_type`, select_one, not required): Select one of the options to specify the purpose of recording:
	+ Promotional: For promotional purposes (e.g., social media, advertising).
	+ Archival: For archival purposes (e.g., keeping for later use).
	+ Educational: For educational purposes (e.g., teaching, research).
* **Recording Location** (`recording_location`, text, not required): Enter the location where the recording took place.
* **Recording Device** (`recording_device`, text, not required): Enter the device used for the recording (e.g., camera, audio recorder).
* **Recording Duration** (`recording_duration`, text, not required): Enter the duration of the recording.
* **Sharing Permission** (`sharing_permission`, select_one, not required): Select whether you grant permission for sharing:
	+ True: Yes, you grant permission for sharing.
	+ False: No, you do not grant permission for sharing.
* **Recording For** (`recording_for`, select_multiple, not required): Select the purpose(s) for recording:
	+ Promotional: For promotional purposes.
	+ Archival: For archival purposes.
	+ Educational: For educational purposes.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Leave any additional comments or information about the participant's recording.
* **Participant School** (`participant_school`, text, not required): Enter the name of the participant's school.
* **Participant Teacher** (`participant_teacher`, text, not required): Enter the name of the participant's teacher.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, not required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, not required): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, not required): Enter the participant's address.
* **Participant City** (`participant_city`, text, not required): Enter the participant's city.
* **Participant State** (`participant_state`, text, not required): Enter the participant's state or province.
* **Participant Zip** (`participant_zip`, text, not required): Enter the participant's ZIP or postal code.
* **Participant Country** (`participant_country`, text, not required): Enter the participant's country.
* **School Permission** (`school_permission`, select_one, not required): Select whether you grant permission for the school:
	+ True: Yes, you grant permission for the school.
	+ False: No, you do not grant permission for the school.
* **Recording Permission** (`recording_permission`, select_one, not required): Select whether you grant permission for recording:
	+ True: Yes, you grant permission for recording.
	+ False: No, you do not grant permission for recording.
