# gender_equality_story_campaign_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from end users about their participation in a gender equality story campaign. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to help us track user engagement and avoid duplication of efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and make sure you are comfortable with the questions and options.
2. Complete all required fields, indicated by *required* label.
3. Answer each question based on your personal story and preferences.
4. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Text, Optional): A brief introduction to your story.
* **Gender Identity** (`Gender Identity`, `select_one`, *): Select your gender identity:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Sharing Preferences** (`Sharing Preferences`, `select_multiple`, *): Select your sharing preferences:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `email`, *): Enter your contact email address.
* **Sharing Status** (`Sharing Status`, `select_multiple`, *): Select your sharing status:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **Story Details** (`Story Details`, `note`, *): Briefly describe your story in this space.
* **Submission Tracking** (Text, Optional): If you wish to track your submission, please enter a unique identifier.
* **Sharing Agreement** (`Sharing Agreement`, `select_one`, *): Agree or disagree to share your story:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Consent** (`Consent`, `select_one`, *): Give your consent to share your story:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Contact Us** (Text, Optional): If you have any further questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us with your comments.
