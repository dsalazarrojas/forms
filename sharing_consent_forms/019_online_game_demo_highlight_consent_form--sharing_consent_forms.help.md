# online_game_demo_highlight_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The online game demo highlight consent form is designed to collect information from gamers for the purpose of obtaining their consent for online game demos. This form helps us to understand their preferences and requirements for demo uploads and access permissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your gamer name in the "Gamer Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Gamer Email" field.
3. Select your preferred game tag in the "Game Tag" field.
4. Specify your upload preferences in the "Highlight Uploads" field.
5. Choose your preferred permissions for the demo in the "Permissions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gamer Name** (`gamer_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as a gamer. This field is not required, but it helps us to identify you.
* **Gamer Email** (`gamer_email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you for updates on demo uploads and permissions.
* **Game Tag** (`gamer_game_tag`, text, optional): Select the game you are most interested in.
* **Highlight Uploads** (`highlight_uploads`, text, optional): Specify if you would like to upload any highlights from the game, such as screenshots or videos.
* **Permissions** (`permissions`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more options to grant access to your demo uploads. Please select "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3" to grant access. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have a valid email address to receive updates on demo uploads and permissions.
* Make sure to select the correct game tag to help us understand your preferences.
* You can select multiple options for permissions to grant access to different teams or individuals.
