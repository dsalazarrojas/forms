# Pedagogical Innovation Showcase Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect consent from project leads and collaborators to share their pedagogical innovation showcase projects with the Pedagogical Innovation Showcase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. **Project Title**: Enter the title of your project.
2. **Project Description**: Briefly describe your project.
3. **Project Type**: Select the type of your project (Innovative Use of Technology, Interdisciplinary Project, or Service-Learning Project).
4. **School/University**: Select the school or university associated with your project.
5. **Student Name**: Enter your name.
6. **Project Lead**: Enter the name of the project lead.
7. **Project Collaborators**: Enter the names of the project collaborators.
8. **Share Level**: Select the level of sharing you're comfortable with (School/Department, University/Public, or Project Lead).
9. **Share With**: Select the groups you'd like to share your project with (School, University, or Project Lead).
10. **Share Reason**: Explain why you'd like to share your project.
11. **Share Details**: Add any additional details about your project.
12. **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address.
13. **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
14. **Contact Name**: Enter your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title (Project Title, text, not required)**: The title of your project. It should be a short and descriptive title that summarizes the project.
* **Project Description (Project Description, text, not required)**: A brief description of your project. Please describe your project in a way that showcases its innovative aspects.
* **Project Type (Project Type, select_one, not required)**: Select the type of your project (Innovative Use of Technology, Interdisciplinary Project, or Service-Learning Project). This will help us understand the type of project you're showcasing.
* **School/University (School/University, select_one, not required)**: Select the school or university associated with your project. This will help us determine which audience to share your project with.
* **Student Name (Student Name, text, not required)**: Enter your name as the student or collaborator associated with the project.
* **Project Lead (Project Lead, text, not required)**: Enter the name of the project lead, who will be the primary contact person for your project.
* **Project Collaborators (Project Collaborators, text, not required)**: Enter the names of the other collaborators in your project.
* **Share Level (Share Level, select_one, not required)**: Select the level of sharing you're comfortable with (School/Department, University/Public, or Project Lead). This will help us determine which audience to share your project with.
* **Share With (Share With, select_multiple, not required)**: Select the groups you'd like to share your project with (School, University, or Project Lead). Be sure to select all relevant groups.
* **Share Reason (Share Reason, text, not required)**: Explain why you'd like to share your project. This will help us understand the purpose behind your sharing request.
* **Share Details (Share Details, text, not required)**: Add any additional details about your project that might be helpful for sharing.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email, email, not required)**: Enter your contact email address, where we can reach you for further questions or inquiries.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone, text, not required)**: Enter your contact phone number, where we can contact you for further questions or discussions.
* **Contact Name (Contact Name, text, not required)**: Enter your name as the primary contact person.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately.
* Double-check the selected options for "Share Level" and "Share With" to ensure you're sharing your project with the right audience.
* Provide a clear and concise description of your project in "Project Description" and "Share Reason".
* If you have any questions or concerns about sharing your project, please don't hesitate to contact us.
