# Educational Impact Story Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather consent from individuals who want to share their stories in an educational context. It ensures that their stories are shared according to their preferences and that they are attributed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each field carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select the options that best match your preferences.
3. Fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Do you agree to share your story** (`consent_to_share`, select_multiple, required): Answer "True" if you agree to share your story, and "False" if you do not.
* **How do you want to be attributed** (`attribution_preferences`, select_one, required): Choose how you want to be attributed: Anonymously, First name and last name, or First name and username.
* **How do you want to be followed up on** (`follow_up_permissions`, select_multiple, required): Select "True" to be followed up on and "False" to not be followed up on.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Enter your contact information so that we can reach out to you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Story Description** (`story_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your story.
* **Share a Story File** (`story_file`, text, optional): If you have a story file, upload it here (not required).
* **What type of story do you have?** (`story_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of story you have: Personal, Professional, or Academic.
* **Educational Organisation** (`educational_organisation`, text, required): Enter the name of the educational organisation.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the teacher.
* **Class Name** (`class_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the class.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Other Informations** (`other_informations`, text, optional): Enter any other information you want to share.
* **How do you want to be sharing your story** (`sharing_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Select how you want to be sharing your story.
* **Attribution Text** (`attribution_text`, text, optional): Enter any attribution text you want to share.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the person who will be contacting you.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Select when you want to be followed up on.
* **Follow-up Time Frequency** (`follow_up_time_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select how often you want to be followed up on.
* **Follow-up Time Details** (`follow_up_time_details`, text, optional): Enter any follow-up time details.
* **Sharing Consent** (`sharing_consent`, text, optional): Enter your sharing consent.
* **Story File Description** (`story_file_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the story file you're sharing.
* **Other Informations** (`other_informations`, text, optional): Enter any other information you want to share.
