# Legal Aid Case Data Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the client's consent for sharing data related to their legal aid case. It is essential that the client understands the purpose and implications of this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through the following sections:

*   "Client Information" section: Fill in your name, select the type of data you want to share, and specify the date you want to start sharing your data.
*   "Data Sharing Preferences" section: Indicate with whom you would like to share your data, select a method for sharing your data, and agree or disagree to receive data sharings from others.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **What is your name?** (client_information)
*   This is the client's name, which is a required field. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. **What kind of data do you want to share with us?** (data_to_share)
*   Select the type of data you are willing to share with us. This will help us understand what information is suitable for sharing.

### 3. **With whom would you like to share your data?** (sharing_with)
*   Select the entities you would like to share your data with, such as Legal Aid Agency, Court, or Solicitor.

### 4. **What date would you like to start sharing your data from?** (start_sharing_date)
*   Specify the date from which you would like to start sharing your data. This will help us know when to begin the sharing process.

### 5. **How do you prefer to receive your data sharings?** (receiving_method)
*   Select your preferred method for receiving data sharings, such as email, text message, or phone call.

### 6. **Do you agree to receive data sharings from us?** (agreement)
*   Please agree or disagree to receive data sharings from us. This will determine how we share your data with others.

### 7. **Do you have any medical records related to your case?** (medical_records)
*   Select yes or no to indicate if you have any medical records related to your case.

### 8. **If you have medical records, what is the name of your doctor or healthcare provider?** (doctor_name)
*   If you have medical records, enter the name of your doctor or healthcare provider.

### 9. **What is your email address for receiving data sharings?** (email_address)
*   Enter your email address where you would like to receive data sharings from us.

### 10. **Do you agree to share your data with others (e.g. family members, caregivers)?** (sharing_with_others)
*   Select whether you agree or disagree to share your data with others, such as family members or caregivers.

### 11. **What is the name of your family member or caregiver?** (other_names)
*   If you have chosen to share your data with others, enter the name of your family member or caregiver.

### 12. **Do you agree to receive data sharings from others (e.g. family members, caregivers)?** (receiving_sharing_from_others)
*   Select whether you agree or disagree to receive data sharings from others, such as family members or caregivers.

### 13. **What time of day would you like to receive data sharings from others?** (receiving_sharing_time)
*   Specify the time of day you would like to receive data sharings from others.

### 14. **What is the name of the person or organization you would like to share your data with?** (sharing_with_whom)
*   Enter the name of the person or organization you would like to share your data with.

### 15. **How do you want to share your data with others?** (sharing_method_others)
*   Select the method you prefer for sharing your data with others, such as email, text message, or phone call.
