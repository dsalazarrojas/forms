# Donor Quote Publication Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from donors for sharing and publishing their quotes, ensuring their approval and transparency throughout the process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have read the purpose and instructions provided at the beginning.
* Answer all required fields (Name, Email Address, Signature, and Signature Date).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Quote Publication Consent Form** (`donor_quote_publication_consent_form`): This is the title of the form.
* **Donor Consent** (`donor_consent`): Select the option that best reflects your preference for sharing your quote. You can choose to either grant or deny permission for Jotform to share and publish your quote.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your full name as it should be displayed when your quote is shared.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Provide your email address where you can be reached for any questions or further communication regarding your quote.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): If you would like to be contacted by phone for any reason, enter your phone number here. (Optional)
* **Signature** (`signature`): Sign this form to indicate your consent for sharing and publishing your quote. This is a required field.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`): Enter the date you signed this form. This is also a required field.
* **Publication Permission** (`publication_permission`): If you grant permission for your quote to be shared, select the formats in which you consent to it being published. You can select one or more of the options.
* **Publication Medium** (`publication_medium`): If you grant permission for your quote to be shared, select the mediums in which you consent to it being published. You can select one or more of the options.

## Tips

* Make sure to sign and date the form accurately to ensure your consent is valid.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at your email address provided in the Email Address field.
