# indoor_climate_control_data_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user consent for indoor climate control data. It is used to understand how users feel about sharing their data with different entities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Read the introduction to understand the form's purpose.
2. Answer the consent question by selecting the options that apply to you.
3. Select the purpose of monitoring (Research, Monitoring, or Optimization).
4. Select a period for monitoring (if applicable).
5. Choose the entities you want to share your data with (Research, Optimization, or Both).
6. Select the entities you want to share your data with (Others, Occupants, or Both).
7. Add any additional notes if you have any information to share.
8. Select your form completion status (Completed, Partial, or None).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, text, optional): A brief text field where you can write a short message or introduction.
* **Consent** (`I`, multiple-choice, optional): A multiple-choice question where you can express your consent for data sharing. Select "I" to agree, "Do not" to disagree.
* **Monitoring** (`Monitoring`, single-choice, optional): A single-choice question where you can select the purpose of monitoring (Research, Monitoring, or Optimization).
* **Monitoring Period** (`Monitoring Period`, date, optional): A date field where you can select a specific period for monitoring.
* **Share** (`Share`, multiple-choice, optional): A multiple-choice question where you can choose the entities you want to share your data with (Research, Optimization, or Both).
* **Share with Others** (`Share with Others`, multiple-choice, optional): A multiple-choice question where you can select the entities you want to share your data with (Others, Occupants, or Both).
* **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, text, optional): A free-text field where you can add any additional information.
* **Form Completion Status** (`Form Completion Status`, single-choice, optional): A single-choice question where you can select whether you have completed the form or not.

## Tips
* Take your time to read each question carefully and select the options that best fit your preferences.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to ask for help.
* The form is designed to be completed in a few minutes, and you can save your progress and come back later if needed.
