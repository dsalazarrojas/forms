# Gym Marketing Material Usage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Marketing Material Usage Consent Form is used to collect user consent for sharing marketing materials with the gym. This includes consent for receiving marketing communications, such as brochures, newsletters, and social media, as well as other marketing materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **User Information**: This field is optional and should only be filled out if you want to provide additional information about yourself.
2. **Consent Given**: Select one of the options to indicate whether you consent to receive marketing communications from the gym. Please make sure to select one option to complete the form.
3. **Email Address**: This field is required. Enter your email address where you would like to receive marketing communications from the gym.
4. **Phone Number**: This field is optional and should only be filled out if you want to provide your phone number for marketing communications.
5. **Marketing Content**: This field is optional and should only be filled out if you want to provide additional comments about the marketing content you are interested in.
6. **Preferred Contact Method**: Select one of the options (Email or Phone) to indicate your preferred contact method for marketing communications.
7. **Contact Information**: This field is optional and should only be filled out if you want to provide additional contact information about yourself.
8. **Agreement**: Select one of the options (I agree to receive marketing communications from the gym or I do not agree to receive marketing communications from the gym) to indicate your agreement to receive marketing communications.
9. **Marketing Materials**: Select all the marketing materials you are consenting to receive from the gym.
10. **Other Marketing Materials**: This field is optional and should only be filled out if you want to select additional marketing materials not listed.
11. **Other Marketing Materials Details**: If you selected "Yes" for other marketing materials, provide a brief description of the other marketing materials you are interested in.
12. **Marketing Agreement**: This field is optional and should only be filled out if you want to provide additional marketing agreement comments.
13. **Other Agreement**: This field is optional and should only be filled out if you want to provide additional agreement comments.
14. **Other Notes**: This field is optional and should only be filled out if you want to provide any additional notes about your consent.
