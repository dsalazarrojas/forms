# Patient Education Animation Voiceover Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from patients for the use and sharing of their voiceover animation, audio, and image content related to their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and any relevant contact information.
2. Select your consent status, which indicates whether you have given, declined, or are unsure about sharing your animation, audio, and image content.
3. If you have selected "Given" or "Declined", you will be asked to confirm your choice by clicking on the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Consent Status** (select_one, required): Select "Given" if you have provided consent for your care to be recorded and shared, "Declined" if you do not want your care to be recorded and shared, or "Unknown" if you are unsure or unable to make a decision.
* **Contact Information** (text, optional): Provide your contact information if you would like to be reached if there are any issues or concerns.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted.
* **Consent Date** (date, optional): Date you gave consent.
* **Animation Title** (text, optional): Enter a title of the animation that will be shared.
* **Animation Video** (text, optional): Enter the URL or path to the animation video that will be shared.
* **Animation Audio** (text, optional): Enter a description of the audio content that will be shared.
* **Animation Image** (text, optional): Enter a description of the image content that will be shared.
* **Consent to Use Audio** (select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you agree to the use of your audio content, "No" if you do not.
* **Consent to Share Audio** (select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you agree to the sharing of your audio content, "No" if you do not.
* **Consent to Use Image** (select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you agree to the use of your image content, "No" if you do not.
* **Consent to Share Image** (select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you agree to the sharing of your image content, "No" if you do not.
