# Fan Submission Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fan Submission Publication Consent Form is designed to collect necessary information from fans who have submitted their work for publication. This form helps to ensure that all parties involved are aware of the submission process and any associated rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your submission details.
2. Select the correct options for the "Publication Frequency", "Publication Purpose", "Consent Status", and "Publication Permission" fields.
3. Enter your contact information in the "Fan Name", "Fan Email", and "Fan Phone" fields.
4. Choose the correct "Assigned Tool" if applicable.
5. If necessary, add a "Note" to provide additional context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fan Submission**: This field is where you can enter a brief description of your submission.
* **Fan Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Fan Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Fan Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Publication Medium**: Select the medium in which your work will be published, such as print, online, or radio.
* **Publication Frequency**: Select the frequency at which you are willing to be published, such as once a year or multiple times a month.
* **Publication Purpose**: Enter a brief description of the purpose of your publication.
* **Consent Status**: Select the status of your consent, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted your work.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time you submitted your work.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or instructions related to your submission.
* **Publication Permission**: Select the permission you are granting for your publication, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool or equipment used in the creation of your work, if applicable.
* **Output File**: Enter the file type or format of your submitted work, if applicable.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant party.
* The submission process may involve multiple steps, and this form is only one part of it.
