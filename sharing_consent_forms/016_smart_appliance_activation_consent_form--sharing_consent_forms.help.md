# Smart Appliance Activation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for the activation of smart appliances, including customer and other relevant parties' details, consent, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer authorization section with your name and any other relevant information.
2. Provide details about the activation of the smart appliance, including dates and times.
3. Specify your scheduling preferences for the activation.
4. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
5. Provide details about the utility and retailer involved in the activation process.
6. Sign and date the form to indicate your consent for the activation.
7. Sign and date the form for other relevant parties, such as the installer and utility representatives.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Authorization** (`customer_authorization`, text, Required): Enter your name and any other relevant information for the activation process.
* **Activation Details** (`activation_details`, text, Optional): Provide details about the activation of the smart appliance, including dates and times.
* **Scheduling Preferences** (`scheduling_preferences`, text, Optional): Specify your scheduling preferences for the activation, such as a preferred date and time.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, Required): Enter your name as the customer.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Utility Details** (`utility_details`, text, Optional): Provide details about the utility involved in the activation process.
* **Retailer Details** (`retailer_details`, text, Optional): Provide details about the retailer involved in the activation process.
* **Installer Details** (`installer_details`, text, Optional): Provide details about the installer involved in the activation process.
* **Activation Date** (`activation_date`, date, Required): Enter the date of activation.
* **Activation Time** (`activation_time`, time, Required): Enter the time of activation.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, note, Required): Sign and date this section to indicate your consent for the activation.
* **Utility Signature** (`utility_signature`, note, Optional): Sign and date this section to indicate the utility's consent for the activation.
* **Retailer Signature** (`retailer_signature`, note, Optional): Sign and date this section to indicate the retailer's consent for the activation.
* **Installer Signature** (`installer_signature`, note, Optional): Sign and date this section to indicate the installer's consent for the activation.
* **Utility Notes** (`utility_notes`, note, Optional): Provide any additional notes from the utility.
* **Retailer Notes** (`retailer_notes`, note, Optional): Provide any additional notes from the retailer.
* **Activation Notes** (`activation_notes`, not present in the original form, but added here for completeness): Provide any additional notes about the activation process.

Note: The fields marked as "Optional" can be completed voluntarily, but are necessary for the activation process to be completed correctly.
