# Electric Vehicle Charging Data Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electric Vehicle Charging Data Consent Form is an optional form designed to collect information from users about their electric vehicle charging habits and preferences. This data will be used by our company to improve our charging infrastructure and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Form Fields" dropdown menu and select the options that best describe your electric vehicle charging habits and preferences.
2. Enter any additional information you feel is relevant to help us better understand your needs.
3. Click submit to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **What kind of EV charging you are using?** (text)
* This field allows you to specify the type of electric vehicle charging system you are using (e.g. public charging station, private charging station, home charging station).
* Please select the option that best describes your current charging method.

### 2. **How often do you charge your EV?** (select_one)
* This field helps us understand how frequently you charge your electric vehicle.
* Select the option that best describes your charging frequency (e.g. daily, weekly, monthly).

### 3. **What type of EV charging station do you use?** (text)
* This field is used to collect information about the type of charging station you typically use (e.g. wall-mounted, mobile, public).

### 4. **Submission** (text)
* This field is an optional text box where you can provide any additional comments or information that you feel is relevant to your electric vehicle charging habits.

### 5. **Notes** (note)
* This field is an optional text box where you can add any additional notes or comments that you feel are important for us to know.
* Please use this field to provide any additional information that will help us better understand your needs.
