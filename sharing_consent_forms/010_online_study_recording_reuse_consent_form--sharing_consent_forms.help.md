# online_study_recording_reuse_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain participant consent for the reuse of their online study recordings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant information section with your details.
2. In the contact information section, provide your contact details for the researchers to reach out to you.
3. Choose one option for participant consents.
4. Enter a unique Recording ID (if applicable).
5. Specify the research purpose of the reuse of the recording.
6. Select the data sharing options (Publicly or Privately).
7. Choose the storage duration (Long term, Short term, or Indeterminate).
8. Select the access control options (Public, Private, or Both).
9. Select the access users (Researchers, Participants, or Both).
10. Select the access groups (Researchers Group, Participant Group, or Both Group).
11. Select the access location (Online or Offline).
12. Select the access medium (Video, Audio, or Text).
13. Choose the access time (Immediately, Delayed, or Indeterminate).
14. Enter the consent date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (Participant Information, text, required): This is the first section where you need to enter your participant information.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required): Please provide your contact details so that researchers can reach out to you.
* **Participant Consents** (Participant Consents, select_one, required): Choose one option to indicate whether you consent to the reuse of the recording or not.
* **Recording ID** (Recording ID, number, required): If you have a unique Recording ID, enter it here.
* **Research Purpose** (Research Purpose, note, required): Enter a brief description of the purpose of reusing the recording.
* **Data Sharing** (Data Sharing, select_multiple, required): Select where you want the data to be shared (Publicly or Privately).
* **Storage Duration** (Storage Duration, select_one, required): Choose how long you want the data to be stored (Long term, Short term, or Indeterminate).
* **Access Control** (Access Control, select_multiple, required): Select who should have access to the data (Public, Private, or Both).
* **Access Users** (Access Users, select_multiple, required): Select who should have access to the data (Researchers, Participants, or Both).
* **Access Groups** (Access Groups, select_multiple, required): Select which groups should have access to the data (Researchers Group, Participant Group, or Both Group).
* **Access Location** (Access Location, select_one, required): Choose where the data should be accessible (Online or Offline).
* **Access Medium** (Access Medium, select_multiple, required): Select what medium the data should be accessible on (Video, Audio, or Text).
* **Access Time** (Access Time, select_one, required): Choose when the data should be accessible (Immediately, Delayed, or Indeterminate).
* **Consent Date** (Consent Date, date, required): Enter the date you are giving consent to the reuse of the recording.
