# Educational Video Licensing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals or organizations seeking to use educational video content. It ensures that they understand the terms and conditions of usage, including the purpose of use, consent, and any additional requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" field, including your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
2. Describe the purpose of use in the "Purpose of Use" field. Be specific about how you plan to use the video content and ensure it aligns with the provided options.
3. Provide a brief consent statement in the "Consent Text" field, confirming that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions of use.
4. Select a date in the "Date" field, indicating when you intend to use the video content.
5. If applicable, enter your signature in the "Signature" field to confirm your consent.
6. If you have any additional contact details or information, enter them in the "Other Contact Details" field.
7. If you want to provide an address, enter it in the "Address" field.
8. If you want to provide an alternative signature, enter it in the "Other Signature" field.
9. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Additional Notes" field.
10. Review and acknowledge the "Terms of Use" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
- **Purpose of Use** (`purpose_of_use`, text, required): Describe how you plan to use the video content, being specific about the intended use and ensuring it aligns with the provided options.
- **Consent Text** (`consent_text`, text, required): Provide a brief statement confirming you understand and agree to the terms and conditions of use.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date on which you intend to use the video content.
- **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Enter your signature to confirm your consent.
- **Other Contact Details** (`other_contact_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact details or information if necessary.
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address if you want to provide it.
- **Other Signature** (`other_signature`, text, optional): Enter an alternative signature if required.
- **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your use of the video content.
- **Terms of Use** (`terms_of_use`, text, optional): Review and acknowledge the terms of use, if applicable.
- **Other Purpose of Use** (`other_purpose_of_use`, text, optional): If not provided in the "Purpose of Use" field, enter any additional purpose of use here.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- Review the terms and conditions carefully before signing.
- If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, contact us for clarification.
- This form is for educational video content use only.
- The form is subject to change as terms and conditions evolve.
