# Learning Footage Data Science Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Learning Footage Data Science Consent Form is used to gather user consent for the use of their data in different purposes such as analytics, research, and training projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your consent in the "Learning Footage Data Science Consent Form" field. This is a free-form text field where you can enter any relevant details about your consent.
2. In the "give_us_your_consent" field, select one of the options that best describes your consent:
	* My data to be used for analytics.
	* My data to be used for research.
	* My data to be used for training projects.
	* Other (please specify)
3. In the "what_is_this_for" field, select all the purposes for which you give your consent:
	* Analytics
	* Research
	* Training projects
	* Other (please specify)
4. In the "how_do_you_want_to_be_contacted" field, select how you want to be contacted:
	* Phone
	* Email
	* Mail
	* Other (please specify)
5. If you selected "Other" in the previous field, enter your preferred contact method in the "other_specify" field.
6. If you want to provide an email address where you can be contacted, enter it in the "email" field.
7. If you want to provide a phone number where you can be contacted, enter it in the "phone" field.
8. If you want to provide any additional information, you can enter it in the "other_specify" field.
9. Finally, sign and date your consent in the "signature" field. You can use the date picker to select the date and time of completion.
10. Once you have completed all the fields, click submit to complete the form.
