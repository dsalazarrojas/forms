# Image And Word Usage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from individuals or organizations for the use of their images and words.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your organization's name and contact information in the Organization Name field.
2. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for this form in the Contact Name field.
3. Enter the title of the contact person in the Contact Title field.
4. Enter the email address of the contact person in the Email field.
5. Sign your name in the Signature field.
6. Enter the date you sign the form in the Date of Signature field.
7. Check the box for Permission to Share, indicating if you want to share the content on social media, email, print, or website.
8. Check the boxes for the channels you want to share the content on (Social Media, Email, Print, Website).
9. (Optional) Check the box for Permission to Publish, indicating if you want to publish the content on print, online, or social media.
10. Select the type of consent you are giving (Model Release, Property Release, Property Permission).
11. Enter the date of consent in the Consent Date field.
12. (Optional) You can add any additional notes in the Notes field if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Date of Signature** (`date_of_signature`, date, required): Enter the date you sign the form.
* **Permission to Share** (`permission_to_share`, select_one, required): Check the box if you want to share the content on social media, email, print, or website.
* **Channels to Share** (`channels_to_share`, select_multiple, required): Check the boxes for the channels you want to share the content on (Social Media, Email, Print, Website).
* **Permission to Publish** (`permission_to_publish`, select_multiple, required): (Optional) Check the boxes for the channels you want to publish the content on (Print, Online, Social Media).
* **Consent Type** (`consent_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of consent you are giving (Model Release, Property Release, Property Permission).
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date of consent.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): (Optional) Add any additional notes if needed.
