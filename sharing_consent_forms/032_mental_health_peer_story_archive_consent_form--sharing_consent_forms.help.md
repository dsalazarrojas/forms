# Mental Health Peer Story Archive Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Peer Story Archive Consent Form is a document that allows contributors to share their mental health stories in a peer archive. It's crucial to obtain consent from contributors before sharing their stories to ensure that their personal information and experiences are handled responsibly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each section and the implications of your responses.
2. Fill in your contributor information (ID number, name, and contact phone number or email).
3. Decide on your consent options (Story Consent, Use in Archive, Share with Peer Group, Share with Organization, and Share with Public).
4. Specify the archive location and type.
5. Provide any additional comments or explanations.
6. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Section**: This section is a note field for you to provide any additional information or context about your story or contributor details.
* **Contributor Information**: Please provide your ID number, name, and contact phone number or email to ensure we can reach you and handle any questions or concerns about your story.
* **Story Consent**: Choose whether you consent to share your story in the archive ('True' or 'False'). If Undecided, we will contact you for further clarification.
* **Use in Archive**: Choose whether you allow your story to be used in the archive ('Yes, with name removed', 'Yes, with name', or 'False').
* **Share with Peer Group**: Choose whether you share your story with other peers in the archive ('True' or 'False').
* **Share with Organization**: Choose whether you share your story with the organization maintaining the archive ('True' or 'False').
* **Share with Public**: Choose whether you share your story publicly ('True' or 'False').
* **Archive Location**: Specify where you want your story to be stored (e.g., online or offline).
* **Archive Type**: Choose whether you want your story to be stored in an online or offline archive, or both.
* **Contact Name**: Provide a name and contact information for the person or organization that will be responsible for handling your story.
* **Contact Email**: Provide your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Provide your contact phone number.
* **Contact Organization**: Provide the name of the organization that will be handling your story.
* **Contact Location**: Provide the location of the organization that will be handling your story.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or explanations about your story or contributor details.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by contributors, so please be honest and clear in your responses to ensure that your personal information and experiences are handled responsibly. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
