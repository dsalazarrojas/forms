# team_recruitment_video_appearance_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The team_recruitment_video_appearance_consent_form is a form designed to collect consent information from participants, parents, and teachers in the video recruitment process. This form is used to gather essential details such as participant names, contact information, and consent status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's name in the `participant_name` field.
2. Provide the participant's email address in the `email` field.
3. Enter the participant's phone number in the `phone_number` field.
4. Enter the name of the school the participant is associated with in the `school_name` field.
5. Describe the participant's role in the school in the `role_in_school` field.
6. Select either "Consent Given" or "Consent Not Given" for the `parent_consent` and `teacher_consent` fields to indicate the parent and teacher's consent status.
7. Enter the date of parent's consent in the `parent_consent_date` field.
8. Enter the date of teacher's consent in the `teacher_consent_date` field.
9. Add a signature from the participant in the `participant_signature` field.
10. Add a signature from the parent in the `parent_signature` field.
11. Add a signature from the teacher in the `teacher_signature` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant being recruited.
* **Email**: Provide the participant's email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the participant's phone number.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school the participant is associated with.
* **Role in School**: Describe the participant's role in the school.
* **Parent Consent**: Select either "Consent Given" or "Consent Not Given" to indicate the parent's consent status. This field is used to determine if the parent has given their consent for the participant's involvement.
* **Teacher Consent**: Select either "Consent Given" or "Consent Not Given" to indicate the teacher's consent status. This field is used to determine if the teacher has given their consent for the participant's involvement.
* **Parent Consent Date**: Enter the date the parent gave their consent.
* **Teacher Consent Date**: Enter the date the teacher gave their consent.
* **Participant Signature**: Add a signature from the participant.
* **Parent Signature**: Add a signature from the parent.
* **Teacher Signature**: Add a signature from the teacher.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* Ensure that the parent and teacher consent status is correctly selected.
* Enter dates in the correct format.
* Add clear and legible signatures from all participants.
