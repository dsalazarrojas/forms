# Disaster Relief Beneficiary Photo Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from disaster relief beneficiaries for sharing their photos. It helps ensure that beneficiaries are aware of and agree to the sharing of their photos, which may be used for various purposes such as documentation, fundraising, or reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and importance of the form.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and clearly.
3. Choose the correct options for each question.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is the first page of the form, which explains the purpose and importance of the form.
* **Beneficiary Name**: Please enter your name as a beneficiary of the disaster relief operation.
* **Photo Consent**: Please select "Yes" if you agree to share your photo, and "No" if you do not.
* **Contact Number**: Please enter your contact number, which can be a phone number or any other contact information.
* **Email Address**: Please enter your email address if you have one.
* **Emergency Contact**: Please select "Yes" if you have an emergency contact, and "No" if you do not. If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name in the next field.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: If you selected "Yes" in the previous field, please enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Other Contact Information**: If you have any other contact information, please enter it here.
* **Other Contact Phone**: Please enter any other phone number you want to share.
* **Emergency Information**: Please provide any emergency information you want to share.
* **Photo Description**: Please briefly describe the photo you are sharing.
* **Photo Taken Date**: Please enter the date the photo was taken.
* **Photo Taken Time**: Please enter the time the photo was taken.
* **Photo Taken Location**: Please enter the location where the photo was taken.
* **Photo Taken By**: Please enter the name of the person or organization that took the photo.
* **Photo Taken For**: Please select "Beneficiary" if the photo is for the beneficiary, and "Staff" if the photo is for staff use.
* **Photo Sharing Consent**: Please select "True" if you agree to share your photo, and "False" if you do not.
* **Other Photo Sharing**: If you have any other photo sharing information, please enter it here.
* **Other Photo Sharing Information**: Please provide any other photo sharing information you want to share.
* **Submit**: Please review your answers carefully and submit the form.
* **Emergency Contact Note**: If you have any emergency contact information, please enter any additional notes here.
* **Submitter**: Please enter your email address.
* **Email Note**: Please enter any additional notes or comments here.
* **Contact Email**: Please enter the email address of your contact.
* **Contact Phone**: Please enter the phone number of your contact.
* **Contact Address**: Please enter the address of your contact.
* **Contact Note**: If you have any additional contact information, please enter it here.
* **Emergency Contact Note**: Please enter any emergency contact information here.
* **Submitter Note**: Please enter any additional notes or comments here.

Note: This help guide is a general guide and may need to be tailored according to the specific context and requirements of your disaster relief operation.
