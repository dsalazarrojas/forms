# Pedagogical Reflection Collection Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent for the collection and use of pedagogical reflections. It is essential that you understand the purpose and requirements of this form before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the "Name" field with your full name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Confirm your consent by selecting "Yes" in the "Consent" field.
4. Select your "Date Of Consent" and "Time Of Consent" accurately.
5. Ensure your "Consent Status" is correctly marked as "Yes".
6. If applicable, add any "Additional Notes" about your consent.
7. If applicable, add a "Signature" by selecting "Yes" and include your "Signed By" name.
8. Capture the "Date Signed" and "Time Signed".
9. If applicable, provide "Signed Notes".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (1)** (`text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email (2)** (`email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (3)** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Date Of Birth (4)** (`date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Date Of Completion (5)** (`date`, required): Enter the date of completion.
* **Time Of Completion (6)** (`time`, required): Enter the time of completion.
* **Additional Notes (8)** (`note`, optional): Provide any additional information about your consent.
* **Consent (9)** (`select_one`, required): Confirm your consent by selecting "Yes".
* **Date Of Consent (10)** (`date`, required): Enter the date of consent.
* **Time Of Consent (11)** (`time`, required): Enter the time of consent.
* **Agreement (12)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" to confirm your agreement.
* **Consent Status (13)** (`select_one`, required): Mark your consent status as "Yes".
* **Date Signed (14)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date signed (if applicable).
* **Time Signed (15)** (`time`, optional): Enter the time signed (if applicable).
* **Signed Notes (16)** (`note`, optional): Provide signed notes (if applicable).
* **Signature (17)** (`select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" to add a signature.
* **Signed By (18)** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who signed.
* **Signature Date (19)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of signature.
* **Signature Time (20)** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of signature.
* **Signature Image (21)** (`select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" to add a signature image.
* **Location (22)** (`text`, optional): Enter the location of signature.
* **Title (23)** (`select_one`, required): Confirm if you are the signed by title.
* **Initials (24)** (`text`, required): Enter your initials.
* **Signature Image URL (25)** (`text`, required): Enter the URL of the signature image.
