# Contactless Entry Program Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and consent for the contactless entry program. The program aims to provide secure and convenient access to buildings and units for authorized personnel. By completing this form, you will be granting permission for keyless access to your building and unit, and providing your consent to share your data for this purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant details, including your full name, building and unit number, and email address.
2. Confirm that you understand how the contactless entry program works by selecting "True".
3. Consent to sharing your data for keyless access by selecting "True".
4. Agree to the privacy policy regarding your data by selecting "True".
5. Confirm that the information provided is accurate by selecting "I Agree".
6. Choose the date of consent by selecting the date on the calendar.
7. If you have any questions or comments, please provide them in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (full_name, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Building and Unit Number** (building_unit, text, required): Enter the building and unit number where you wish to have keyless access.
* **Email Address** (contact_email, email, required): Enter your email address to receive important updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number** (contact_phone, text, required): Enter your phone number to receive important updates and notifications.
* **I understand how the contactless entry program works** (program_understanding, select_one, required): Select "True" if you have read and understand the program's details. A user guide is attached for further reference.
* **I consent to my data being shared for keyless access** (data_sharing_consent, select_one, required): Select "True" to consent to sharing your data for keyless access.
* **I have read and agree to the privacy policy regarding my data** (privacy_policy_agreement, select_one, required): Select "True" to agree to the privacy policy.
* **I confirm that the information provided is accurate** (signature_confirmation, select_one, required): Select "I Agree" to confirm that the information is accurate.
* **Date of Consent** (date_of_consent, date, required): Choose the date of consent from the calendar.
* **Any questions or comments?** (additional_comments, text, optional): Provide any questions or comments you may have.
