> # Project Documentation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a required part of the project documentation process, intended for project participants to provide consent and share their information with the project leader and other project participants.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Participant Information** section with your name and any other relevant information.
2.  Choose your **Consent** by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the dropdown menu.
3.  Select your **Contact Method** by choosing one of the available options (Email, Phone, or Mail).
4.  If you wish, you can add **Contact Details** for your preferred contact method.
5.  In the **Participant Notes** section, you can add any additional information about your participation in the project.
6.  In the **Project Details** section, you can add any additional information about the project.
7.  Enter the **Project Leader Name** to ensure that the project leader has your information.
8.  Select 'Yes' or 'No' in the **Project Materials** section if you are willing to share project materials.
9.  Finally, sign and date the **Participant Signature** and **Project Leader Signature** sections to confirm your consent and agreement with the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Information**: Enter your name and any other relevant information to help the project leader and other participants contact you.
*   **Consent**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that you have read, understand, and agree to participate in the project, and 'No' to indicate that you do not wish to participate.
*   **Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Mail).
*   **Contact Details**: Enter your contact details for the chosen contact method.
*   **Participant Notes**: Add any additional information about your participation in the project.
*   **Project Details**: Add any additional information about the project.
*   **Project Leader Name**: Enter the project leader's name to ensure they have your information.
*   **Project Materials**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that you are willing to share project materials, or 'No' to indicate that you are not.
*   **Participant Signature**: Sign and date to confirm your consent and agreement with the project.
*   **Project Leader Signature**: (Optional) The project leader will sign and date to confirm their understanding of your consent and agreement.

## Tips

*   Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the project leader.
*   Remember to sign and date the **Participant Signature** and **Project Leader Signature** sections to confirm your consent and agreement with the project.
