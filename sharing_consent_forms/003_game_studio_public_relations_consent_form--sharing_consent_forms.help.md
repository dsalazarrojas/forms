# Game Studio Public Relations Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for obtaining public relations consent from game studio employees, contractors, or other individuals who may be representing the game studio in a public or professional capacity.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant category of public relations consent.
2. Enter a brief description of your role or position within the game studio.
3. Enter your date of birth.
4. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
5. Select your phone type (e.g., Home, Work, Mobile).
6. Select your phone carrier.
7. Enter your phone extension (if applicable).
8. Sign the consent section, indicating your agreement to share sensitive or confidential information about the game studio.
9. Select your agreement option (I agree or I do not agree).
10. Enter additional contact information, email, and phone number if needed.
11. Sign the second consent section, indicating your agreement to share sensitive or confidential information about the game studio.
12. Select your agreement option for the second section (I agree or I do not agree).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category**: Select the relevant category of public relations consent.
	+ This is used to determine the type of public relations consent required.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of your role or position within the game studio.
	+ This helps the game studio understand your position and the level of access you require.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
	+ This is not required, but it may be used for identification purposes.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
	+ This is how the game studio will contact you for public relations purposes.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	+ This is an optional field for additional contact information.
* **Contact Phone Type**: Select your phone type (e.g., Home, Work, Mobile).
	+ This helps the game studio understand how to contact you effectively.
* **Contact Phone Carrier**: Enter your phone carrier (e.g., AT&T, Verizon).

	+ This is a mandatory field to ensure you understand the terms of public relations consent.

## Tips
* Make sure you have been authorized to represent the game studio before filling out this form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the game studio's HR or management team for further guidance.
* This form is mandatory for all individuals representing the game studio in public or professional capacities.
