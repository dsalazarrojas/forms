# Grant Application Data Sharing Consent - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture the grant applicant's consent for data sharing related to their grant application. It is a critical component of the grant application process, ensuring that the grant applicant is aware of and consents to the sharing of their data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each page to understand the questions and options presented.
2. For each field, carefully enter or select the requested information.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided accurate and complete information.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields and reviewed your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Grant Applicant Info** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This field is intended for the grant applicant to provide general information about themselves.
* **Do you share your data** (`2`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field asks if you are willing to share your data with the grant maker and other parties. Select one of the options: `True`, `False`, or `Not Specified`.
* **Sharing Information** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for providing additional information about what data you are willing to share.
* **Grant Maker Info** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for providing general information about the grant maker.
* **Consent Given** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field asks for confirmation of consent for data sharing. Select multiple options: `True`, `False`, or `Not Specified`.
* **Email** (`6`, `email`, required/optional): This field is for entering your email address.

## Tips
* Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the grant maker or support team for clarification.
* Remember that submitting this form indicates your consent to share your data with the grant maker and other parties.
