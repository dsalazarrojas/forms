# Policyholder Story Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Policyholder Story Sharing Consent Form is designed to collect policyholders' consent and preferences regarding the sharing of their personal stories. This form helps the organization ensure that policyholders are aware of and agree to the sharing of their stories, promoting transparency and trust.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
*   Provide the required information for each field, following the guidelines below.
*   Review and submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Policyholder Information (Policyholder Information, text, required)**: This field is used to collect the policyholder's name or identifier used for internal purposes. Please enter the correct name or ID associated with your policyholder account.
*   **Consent (Select, optional)**: Select all the options that apply to your willingness to share your story. This field is optional and allows for multiple selection. You can choose one or more options.
    *   'Yes': You agree to share your story.
    *   'No': You do not agree to share your story.
*   **Story Sharing (Text, required)**: Describe the story you are willing to share with the organization or other stakeholders. This can be a personal story, a policyholder story, or any other relevant information you want to share.
*   **Story Sharing Permission (Select one, required)**: Choose the specific permission level you grant for sharing your story. You can choose one of the following options:
    *   'Yes': You grant permission to share your story.
    *   'No': You do not grant permission to share your story.
*   **Story Sharing Date (Date, required)**: Specify the date when you are willing to start sharing your story. Please format the date according to the given instructions.
*   **Follow-up Schedule (Text, required)**: Describe the schedule for follow-up activities related to story sharing. This could be a regular meeting or check-in schedule.
*   **Follow-up Status (Select multiple, optional)**: Select all the options that apply to your follow-up status. This field is optional and allows for multiple selection.
    *   'Active': You are actively participating in follow-up activities.
    *   'Inactive': You are not actively participating in follow-up activities.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you have not filled this form previously to avoid duplication of efforts.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the relevant contact person or department.
