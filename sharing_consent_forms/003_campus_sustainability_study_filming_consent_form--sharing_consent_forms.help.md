# Campus Sustainability Study Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather consent from participants in a campus sustainability study. The form aims to collect information about the study's purpose, filming details, and participant's preferences for using the recorded footage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your full legal name in the `Participant Name` field.
2. Enter your valid email address that you'd like to receive a copy of your consent.
3. If you are a student, faculty, staff, or community member, describe your affiliation with the university in the `Affiliation` field.
4. Provide a brief description of the filming purpose in the `Project Description` field.
5. Select a date for the filming to occur in the `Recording Date` field.
6. Choose the allowed uses of the footage in the `Usage Rights` field.
7. If applicable, select the type of compensation you will receive for participating in the study.
8. Describe the measures taken to protect your personal data in the `Confidentiality Measures` field.
9. Sign your name in the `Consent Signature Name` field to indicate your consent.
10. Enter the date you provided consent in the `Consent Date` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full legal name.
- **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that you'd like to receive a copy of your consent.
- **Affiliation** (`affiliation`, `text`, optional): If you are a student, faculty, staff, or community member, please describe your affiliation with the university.
- **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the filming purpose and how it relates to the study.
- **Recording Date** (`recording_date`, `date`, required): Select a date when filming will occur.
- **Usage Rights** (`usage_rights`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how you would like the footage to be used:
  • Research only
  • Educational presentations
  • Public outreach and website
  • Media and promotional materials
- **Compensation** (`compensation`, `select_one`, optional): If you will be compensated, choose from:
  • No compensation
  • Monetary compensation
  • Gift card or voucher
  • Other
- **Confidentiality Measures** (`confidentiality_measures`, `text`, optional): Describe how your personal data will be protected.
- **Consent Signature Name** (`consent_signature`, `text`, required): Type your name to indicate your consent.
- **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you provided consent.

## Tips
