# Open Resource Documentation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Resource Documentation Consent Form is a form used to gather information from users to understand their willingness to share their resources, such as personal data, documentation, or other materials, for documentation purposes. This form helps ensure that users are aware of and consent to the sharing of their resources with others, including the company or its affiliates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address.
2. Choose your preferred options for sharing your resources. You can select one or more options from the dropdown menu.
3. Provide a specific time for each sharing option, if applicable.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments about your resource sharing preferences.
5. Review and submit your form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address where you can be reached.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date for your resource sharing.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time for your resource sharing.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your resource sharing.
* **Options 1** (`options_1`, `select_one`, required): Choose one option for sharing your resources:
	+ Option 1: Share with Company A
	+ Option 2: Share with Company B
	+ Option 3: Share with Company C
* **Options 2** (`options_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose multiple options for sharing your resources:
	+ Option 1: Share with Company A
	+ Option 2: Share with Company B
	+ Option 3: Share with Company C
* **Options 3** (`options_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose multiple options for sharing your resources:
	+ Option 1: Share with Company A
	+ Option 2: Share with Company B
	+ Option 3: Share with Company C
* **Sharing 1-18** (`sharing_1-18`, `time`, optional): Enter specific times for each sharing option, if applicable.
* **Sharing 2-13** (`sharing_2-13`, `time`, optional): Enter specific times for each sharing option, if applicable.
* **Sharing 14-18** (`sharing_14-18`, `time`, optional): Enter specific times for each sharing option, if applicable.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be skipped if not applicable. Please ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
