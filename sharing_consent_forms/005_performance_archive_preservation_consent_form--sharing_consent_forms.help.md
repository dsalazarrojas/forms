# Performance Archive Preservation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users to obtain their consent for recording and storing their performance. This is a general-purpose form and can be used for various types of performances, such as music, sports, or art exhibitions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your User Name, which will be used to identify you as the performer or participant.
2. Enter your Email, which will be used to contact you if needed.
3. Select your choice for Consent to Recording: either Record and store or Do not record or store.
4. Provide any Performance Details in the text area, such as the event type, location, or any other relevant information.
5. Enter the Performance Date, which should be in YYYY-MM-DD format.
6. Select the Performance Time, which should be in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
7. Enter the Performance Duration, which should be the length of the performance in minutes or hours.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: A field to enter your name as the performer or participant.
* **Email**: A field to enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Consent to Recording**: A field to select whether you consent to recording and storing your performance. If you select "Record and store", you are giving permission for your performance to be recorded and stored. If you select "Do not record or store", your performance will not be recorded or stored.
* **Performance Details**: A field to provide any additional information about the performance, such as the event type, location, or any other details that might be relevant.
* **Performance Date**: A field to enter the date of the performance in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Performance Time**: A field to select the time of the performance in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Performance Duration**: A field to enter the length of the performance in minutes or hours.
 
**Tips**: 
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (marked as "required: true").
* Use the 24-hour format for Performance Time.
* Use the YYYY-MM-DD format for Performance Date.
* If you select "Record and store", ensure you understand that your performance will be recorded and stored.
* If you select "Do not record or store", your performance will not be recorded or stored.
