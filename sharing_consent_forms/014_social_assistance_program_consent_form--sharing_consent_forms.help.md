# Social Assistance Program Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Assistance Program Consent Form is designed to collect information and consent from individuals participating in a social assistance program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out your First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth in pages 1.
2.  Provide your Contact Email and Phone number in pages 2 and 3.
3.  Enter your Address in pages 4 and 5.
4.  Provide the Program Name and Program Start and End Dates in pages 6 and 7.
5.  Check the Consent box to confirm you have read and understood the program description in pages 8.
6.  Select how you wish to share your contact information with the program staff in pages 9.
7.  Choose whether you consent to recording of your voice and video in pages 10.
8.  Choose whether you consent to the collection of your data in pages 11.
9.  Enter any Participant Information in pages 12 and 13.
10.  Enter contact information for Program Staff in pages 14 and 15.
11.  Enter Emergency Contact information in pages 16 and 17.
12.  Select the contact relationship in pages 18 and 19.
13.  Enter Relationship Type in page 20.
14.  Enter Relationship Name in page 21.
15.  Choose the participant type in pages 22.
16.  Select the Relationship Status in pages 23 and 24.
17.  Enter Relationship Start and End Dates in pages 25 and 26.
18.  Enter any additional Notes in pages 27.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (page 1): Enter your first name as it is known to you.
*   **Last Name** (page 1): Enter your last name as it is known to you.
*   **Date of Birth** (page 1): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Contact Email** (page 2): Enter the email address you would like to use for contact with the program.
*   **Contact Phone** (page 3): Enter the phone number you would like to use for contact with the program.
*   **Address** (page 4): Enter your address where you would like to receive communication from the program.
*   **Program Name** (page 5): Enter the name of the social assistance program you are participating in.
*   **Program Start Date** (page 5): Enter the date you began participating in the program.
*   **Program End Date** (page 5): Enter the date you plan to end participating in the program.
*   **Consent** (page 6): Check the box to confirm you have read and understood the program description.
*   **Terms and Conditions** (page 6): Review the terms and conditions of the program.
*   **Sharing Consent** (page 7): Select how you would like to share your contact information with the program staff.
*   **Recording Consent** (page 8): Choose whether you consent to recording of your voice and video.
*   **Data Collection Consent** (page 9): Choose whether you consent to the collection of your data.
*   **Participant Information** (page 10): Enter any participant information you wish to share with the program.
*   **Program Staff** (page 11): Enter any contact information for the program staff you have.
*   **Emergency Contact** (page 12): Enter an emergency contact's name and phone number.
*   **Contact Relationship** (page 13): Select the type of relationship you have with the program.
*   **Relationship Type** (page 14): Enter the type of relationship you have with the program.
*   **Relationship Name** (page 15): Enter the name of the relationship.
*   **Participant Type** (page 16): Select whether you are an individual or group participant.
*   **Relationship Status** (page 17): Select the status of your relationship with the program.
*   **Relationship Start Date** (page 18): Enter the date your relationship with the program began.
*   **Relationship End Date** (page 19): Enter the date your relationship with the program ended.
*   **Notes** (page 20): Enter any additional notes about your participation in the program.

## Tips

*   Make sure to review and understand the program description before filling out this form.
*   Ensure you have provided accurate and up-to-date information about yourself and your relationship with the program.
*   Review the terms and conditions of the program before signing the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the program staff.
