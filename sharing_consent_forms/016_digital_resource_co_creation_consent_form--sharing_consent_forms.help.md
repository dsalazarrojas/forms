# digital_resource_co_creation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Digital Resource Co-Creation Consent Form" is used to collect consent from contributors to share and co-create digital resources. This form gathers essential information about a project and its contributors, enabling effective collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your project from the "Project Details" dropdown list.
2. Choose the contributors you want to work with by selecting their names from the "Contributor Details" list.
3. Enter the permissions you require for your project.
4. Fill in the contact email of your contributor(s).
5. You may leave the phone number field blank if not necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details (Select One)**: 
    Select your project from the dropdown list. Choose the option that best describes your project.
* **Contributor Details (Select Multiple)**: 
    Choose the contributors you want to work with by selecting their names from the list. You can select more than one contributor.
* **Permissions (Text Input)**: 
    Enter the permissions required for your project. Describe the level of access each contributor needs.
* **Email (Text Input)**: 
    Enter the email address of your contributor. This is a required field to ensure we can contact your contributor.
* **Phone (Text Input)**: 
    This field is optional. Enter the phone number of your contributor if you need to contact them.
