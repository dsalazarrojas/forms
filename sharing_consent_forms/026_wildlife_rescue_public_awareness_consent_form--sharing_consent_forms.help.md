# wildlife rescue public awareness consent form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from users who want to use images, videos, audio recordings, or documents from the wildlife rescue organization for public awareness campaigns. The organization uses this information to understand the scope and terms of use for these materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of content you want to use from the "permission type" dropdown menu.
2. Enter your name in the "name" field.
3. Provide the name of your organization in the "organization" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. Add your phone number in the "phone" field.
6. Describe how you intend to use the content in the "description" field.
7. Read and agree to the terms and conditions in the "terms and conditions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Permission Type**: Select the type of content you want to use (image, video, audio, document, or other).
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of your organization or entity.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you use to receive communications.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Description**: Briefly describe how you intend to use the content and what it will be used for.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to the terms of use for the content you selected. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to represent your organization. 
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact our support team. 
* Review your submission carefully before submitting, as you will not be able to edit your entry once submitted. 
* The organization may contact you to clarify any information provided in this form.
