# Animation Workshop Publicity Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture participants' consent to use their photos and testimonials for public purposes, such as marketing and promotion of the Animation Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name in the "Participant full name" field.
2. Enter your contact email in the "Contact email" field.
3. Select your age group from the "Age group" dropdown list.
4. Check the box to confirm that you consent to use photos and video from the workshop in the "Consent to use photos and video" field.
5. Check the box to confirm that you consent to use testimonials from the workshop in the "Consent to use testimonials" field.
6. Type your name in the "Typed name for consent" field to confirm your consent.
7. Select the date you signed the form in the "Date signed" field.
8. If you are under 18, enter the name and contact information of your guardian in the "Guardian contact" field.
9. If you have any additional restrictions on the use of your information, enter them in the "Additional restrictions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant full name** (`participant_name`, text, required): This is the first step to complete. Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address for publicity confirmation and other purposes.
* **Age group** (`age_group`, select_one, optional): Select your age group from the list:
	+ Under 18
	+ 18 to 25
	+ 26 to 40
	+ 41 to 65
	+ 66 and above
* **Consent to use photos and video** (`consent_photos`, select_one, required): Check the box to confirm that you consent to use images from the workshop.
* **Consent to use testimonials** (`consent_testimonials`, select_one, optional): Check the box to confirm that you consent to use testimonials from the workshop.
* **Typed name for consent** (`signature_name`, text, required): Type your name in this field to confirm your consent.
* **Date signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Select the date you signed this form.
* **Guardian contact** (`guardian_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your guardian if you are under 18.
* **Additional restrictions** (`additional_restrictions`, text, optional): If you have any additional restrictions on the use of your information, enter them here.
