# Digital Creator Highlight Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Creator Highlight Consent Form is a tool to collect information from digital creators about their work and their authority to grant permissions. This form helps us understand who has created a piece of digital content and what permissions they have granted or not granted for its use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Answer each question carefully and honestly**: Please answer every question on the form. If you're unsure about any question, try to answer based on your best understanding.
2. **Select the correct options for each question**: Use the dropdown menus to select the correct options for each question.
3. **Provide clear and accurate information in the text fields**: If you need to provide more information, please type it clearly and concisely in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **I am a digital creator**: This question helps us understand who has created the digital content. Please select "Yes" if you are the creator or have created the content, otherwise select "No".
* **What type of creator am I**: This question is about your role or position related to the creation of the digital content. Select all applicable options (e.g., "Author", "Artist", "Photographer", etc.).
* **I have the authority to act on behalf of**: This field is for providing additional context or information about your role or position. Type in any relevant details.
* **I can represent my own work**: This question helps us understand if you're the owner of the digital content. Select "Yes" if you have the rights to represent the content, otherwise select "No".
* **I have the right to grant permissions**: This question is about whether you have the authority to grant permissions for the use of the digital content. Select "Yes" if you have the rights, otherwise select "No".
