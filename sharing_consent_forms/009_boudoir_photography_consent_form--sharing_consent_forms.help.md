# Boudoir Photography Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to capture client information and consent for boudoir photography sessions. It outlines the terms and conditions of the photography session, including image sharing, editing, and usage rights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Provide your full name, email address, and phone number.
3. Choose your preferred photography session date.
4. Select your preferred session length (if applicable).
5. Decide on the level of styling and makeup services included (if applicable).
6. Choose where you'd like your session to take place (if applicable).
7. Select how you'd like your images to be shared (if applicable).
8. Agree to the terms and conditions outlined in this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Full Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Provide your primary email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Photography Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Choose the date for your photography session.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, select_one, optional): Select the approximate length of your session (30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours, or Full day).
* **Professional Styling and Makeup** (`styling_included`, select_one, optional): If you'd like professional styling and makeup services, select "True".
* **Session Location Type** (`location_type`, select_one, optional): Choose where you'd like your session to take place (Studio, Client provided location, Hotel room, Outdoor, or Other).
* **Content Sharing Permissions** (`sharing_consent`, select_multiple, required): Select where you'd like your images shared (Photographer portfolio, Social media, Website and online, Wedding/event websites, Industry publications, Photography competitions, Limited sharing, or Personal use only).
* **Specific Social Media Sharing Approval** (`social_media_consent`, select_multiple, optional): If you'd like to specify which social media platforms your images can be shared on, select them here (Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, Twitter, TikTok, LinkedIn, or No social media sharing).
* **Watermark Consent** (`watermark_consent`, select_one, required): If you'd like your images to have a watermark, select "Yes, approved".
* **Anonymity Preference** (`anonymity_preference`, select_one, required): If you'd like your identity to remain anonymous, select "Yes, anonymous only".
* **Image Editing Consent** (`editing_consent`, select_one, required): If you'd like to approve or limit image editing, select your preferred level of editing (Yes, full editing approved, Limited editing only, Approval required for major changes, or No editing without approval).
* **Confidentiality Understanding** (`confidentiality_understanding`, select_one, required): Please select "I understand" to acknowledge that these are artistic photos and may be shared professionally.
* **Third-Party Sharing** (`third_party_sharing`, select_one, required): If you'd like to approve or limit third-party sharing, select your preferred level (Yes, approved, Only with approval, or No third-party sharing).
* **Image Delivery Method** (`image_delivery`, select_one, optional): Choose how you'd like to receive your images (Digital download, USB drive, Online gallery, Physical prints, or Multiple formats).
* **Final Consent** (`final_consent`, select_one, required): Please select "I fully consent" to agree to the terms and conditions outlined in this form.

## Tips
- Please review each section carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your photographer.
- This form is a contract that outlines the terms and conditions of your photography session. By signing, you're agreeing to the outlined terms.
