# Digital Library Resource Case Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from users to share their digital library resources with various teams, groups, or individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand who will be sharing your resources with.
2. Select the options that apply to you.
3. Enter the date and time you would like to share your resources.
4. Review your selections and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Form
This is the main form section where you will enter your information.
- **Form** (`digital_library_resource_case_consent_form`, text, required): This is the main form section where you will enter your information. Please enter your response to the question of who you would like to share your resources with.

### Fields
Select the fields that you would like to share with.
- **Fields** (`digital_library_resource_case_consent_form_fields`, select_one, required): Please select all the fields that you would like to share with. The options are "Yes" and "No".

### Share with which team or group
Select the teams or groups to share with.
- **Share with which team or group** (`fields_to_share_with_which_team_or_group`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the teams or groups that you would like to share with. The options are "Yes" and "No".

### Share with specific organisations
Select the specific organizations to share with.
- **Share with specific organisations** (`fields_to_share_with_specific_organisations`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the specific organizations that you would like to share with. The options are "Yes" and "No".

### Date
Enter the date you would like to share your resources.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required): Please enter the date you would like to share your resources.

### Time
Enter the time you would like to share your resources.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required): Please enter the time you would like to share your resources.

### Share with specific individuals
Select the specific individuals to share with.
- **Share with specific individuals** (`fields_to_share_with_specific_individuals`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the specific individuals that you would like to share with. The options are "Yes" and "No".

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are only sharing your resources with those who need to access them.
* If you are unsure about who to share with, please consult with your supervisor or the digital library team.
* Please review your selections carefully before submitting the form.
* If you need to share your resources with someone not listed, please contact the digital library team to request approval.
