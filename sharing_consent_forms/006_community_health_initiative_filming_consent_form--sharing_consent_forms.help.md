# Community Health Initiative Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain explicit consent from participants before capturing any audio or video recordings of them. It is typically used for recording participants' opinions, experiences, or insights for research, educational, or promotional purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full legal name in the "Participant name" field.
2. Provide your contact email address in the "Contact email" field. This will allow us to notify you about any media use.
3. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "Phone number" field.
4. In "Consent to be recorded", select either "I consent" or "I do not consent" to indicate your willingness to be recorded.
5. Select all applicable options in "Permission for use of recordings" that you are comfortable with regarding the use of your recordings.
6. In "Duration of use", specify the time period or indicate that the recording will be used permanently.
7. In "Process to withdraw consent", provide a brief explanation of how you can withdraw your consent.
8. Finally, enter your printed name as a signature confirmation in the "Signature name" field and the date you are giving consent in the "Signature date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name** (Participant name, text, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Contact email** (Contact email, email, optional): Provide your email address for any notifications about media use.
* **Phone number** (Phone number, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Consent to be recorded** (Consent to be recorded, select_one, required): Select "I consent" if you agree to be recorded, and "I do not consent" if you do not agree.
* **Permission for use of recordings** (Permission for use of recordings, select_multiple, required): Choose all the applicable options for how you are comfortable with the use of your recordings.
* **Duration of use** (Duration of use, text, optional): Specify the time period or select "permanent" for permanent use.
* **Process to withdraw consent** (Process to withdraw consent, text, optional): Describe the process for withdrawing your consent.
* **Signature name** (Signature name, text, required): Enter your printed name as a signature confirmation.
* **Signature date** (Signature date, date, required): Enter the date you are giving consent.
