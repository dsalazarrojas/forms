# Emergency Preparadness Documentary Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Preparadness Documentary Consent Form is used to gather information about participants and their roles in a project, event, or program. This form is required for all participants and supporters to provide their consent for the use of their personal details in emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the participant's roles by choosing one or more of the options provided:
	* Participant
	* Supporter
	* Both
2. Check the box indicating whether you consent to the use of your personal details:
	* Yes (True)
	* No (False)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**: This field is used to collect additional details about the participant.
	+ Participant Name: Enter the participant's name.
	+ Participant Email: Enter the participant's email address.
	+ Participant Phone: Enter the participant's phone number.
	+ Participant Address: Enter the participant's address.
	+ Participant Role: Describe the participant's role in the project or event.
	+ Participant Description: Provide a brief description of the participant's involvement.
	+ Participant Image: Enter the participant's image (optional).
	+ Participant Video: Enter the participant's video (optional).
	+ Participant Audio: Enter the participant's audio (optional).
* Note: These fields are optional and can be filled in at your discretion.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure that your consent is valid.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the project or organization administering the form.
* This form is only required for participants and supporters; others may not be required to complete it.
