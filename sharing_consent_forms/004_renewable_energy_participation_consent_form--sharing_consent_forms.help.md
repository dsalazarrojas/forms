# Renewable Energy Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather participant information and consent for a renewable energy participation program. It helps to collect data in a controlled and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant_details fields on page 1 with your personal information.
2. On page 2, confirm your participation in the program by selecting "I consent to participate in the program" from the participant_consent field.
3. Fill in your contact information on page 3.
4. Sign the form by signing your name on the participant_signature field on page 4.
5. Date the signature on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details (Page 1)** (`Participant Details`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your personal details such as name, email, and phone number.
* **Participant Details (Page 2)** (`Participant Details`, `text`, required/optional): This field is identical to page 1 and should be ignored.
* **Participant Consent (Page 3)** (`Participant Consent`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please confirm your participation in the program by selecting "I consent to participate in the program".
* **Contact Information (Page 4)** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your contact information such as email, phone number, and physical address.
* **Contact Information (Page 5)** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required/optional): This field is identical to page 4 and should be ignored.
* **Participant Signature (Page 6)** (`Participant Signature`, `note`, required/optional): Please sign your name to confirm your participation.
* **Signature (Page 7)** (`Participant Signature`, `note`, required/optional): This field is identical to page 6 and should be ignored.
* **Signature (Page 8)** (`Signature`, `date`, required/optional): This field is identical to page 6 and should be ignored.
* **Participant Signature (Page 9)** (`Participant Signature`, `note`, required/optional): This field is identical to page 6 and should be ignored.
* **Participant Signature (Page 10)** (`Participant Signature`, `note`, required/optional): This field is identical to page 6 and should be ignored.
* **Signature Date (Page 11)** (`Signature Date`, `date`, required/optional): Please date your signature on this field.
