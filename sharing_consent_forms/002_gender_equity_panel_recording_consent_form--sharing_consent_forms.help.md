# genderEquityPanelRecordingConsentForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The genderEquityPanelRecordingConsentForm is designed to collect user information and recording details for the Gender Equity Panel, promoting diversity and inclusion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your user information in the User Information section.
2.  Select your recording location.
3.  Choose the Gender Equity Panel you are affiliated with.
4.  Enter the date and time of the recording.
5.  Specify the recording duration.
6.  Enter your email address.
7.  Provide your phone number.
8.  Confirm your consent to the sharing of your data.
9.  Add any additional notes if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (id: 1): Enter your name and other personal details in this field.
* **Recording Location** (id: 2): Provide the location where the recording will take place.
* **Gender Equity Panel** (id: 3): Choose the specific Gender Equity Panel you are affiliated with (e.g., Gender Equity Panel 1, 2, or 3).
* **Recording Date** (id: 4): Enter the date of the recording in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Recording Time** (id: 5): Enter the time of the recording in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
* **Recording Duration** (id: 6): Specify the length of the recording in minutes.
* **Email** (id: 7): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (id: 8): Provide your phone number for further contact or follow-up.
* **Consent** (id: 9): Confirm your consent to the sharing of your data by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
* **Notes** (id: 10): Add any additional notes or comments for the recording.
* **Additional Notes** (id: 11): Enter any further details or comments, if required.
* **Sharing Consent** (id: 12): Confirm your consent to share your data with others by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
