# Remote Workshop Recording Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant's consent for sharing remote workshop recordings. The form is part of the workshop management process and helps ensure that participants understand and agree to the sharing of their recorded sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form completely.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional):
    Please enter your name, email address, and any other relevant participant information.
    This field is optional but helps us keep track of who is completing the form.

- **Sharing Consent** (`sharing_consent`, select_one, optional):
    Select one of the three options:
    - **Consent given**: Select this option if you agree to share your recorded sessions.
    - **Consent not given**: Select this option if you do not agree to share your recorded sessions.
    - **No answer**: Select this option if you are unsure or do not know how to respond.

- **Recording Sharing** (`recording_sharing`, select_one, optional):
    Select one of the three options:
    - **Record**: Select this option if you want your session to be recorded.
    - **Share**: Select this option if you want your recorded session to be shared.
    - **Neither**: Select this option if you do not want your session to be recorded or shared.

- **Sharing Reason** (`sharing_reason`, text, optional):
    If you have chosen to share your recorded sessions, please provide a brief reason for your choice (e.g., for marketing purposes, for quality improvement).

- **Recording Sharing Reason** (`recording_sharing_reason`, text, optional):
    If you have chosen to record your session, please provide a brief reason for your choice (e.g., for review and analysis, for improvement).

Tips:
- Please take a moment to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your workshop administrators or support team.
- Your responses will be treated confidentially and used solely for the purpose of managing your workshop participation.
