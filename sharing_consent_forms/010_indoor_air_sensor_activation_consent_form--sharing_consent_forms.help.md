# Indoor Air Sensor Activation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather consent and relevant information for the activation of indoor air sensors. It is intended to be used by users who wish to activate indoor air sensors and provide the necessary details for the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by *) with the corresponding information.
2. Select the relevant monitoring programs that will be used for the indoor air sensor.
3. Choose the installer(s) who will be responsible for installing the sensor.
4. Enter the date the sensor was installed.
5. Enter the date the sensor will be activated.
6. Add any additional notes or comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, **optional**): Please enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Indoor Air Sensor Activation Consent** (`indoor_air_sensor_activation_consent`, text, **optional**): Please enter your consent to activate the indoor air sensor. This is a mandatory field for the activation process.
* **Monitoring Programs** (`monitoring_programs`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select the monitoring programs that will be used for the indoor air sensor. This will help us determine which programs to use for the activation process.
* **Installers** (`installers`, select_one, **optional**): Choose the installer(s) who will be responsible for installing the sensor. This will ensure that the correct person is contacted for any installation issues.
* **Date Installed** (`date_installed`, date, **optional**): Enter the date the sensor was installed. This will help us track the installation process.
* **Activation Date** (`activation_date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date the sensor will be activated. This will help us schedule the activation process.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, **optional**): Add any additional notes or comments you may have.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **optional**): Enter your email address. This will help us contact you in case of any issues or questions about the activation process.


## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure the activation process can move forward.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for clarification.
* Please make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
