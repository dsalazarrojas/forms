# Fitness Data Sharing Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user consent for sharing their fitness data with a specific recipient. This form helps ensure that users are aware of and agree to the sharing of their data with a trusted recipient for a legitimate purpose.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please fill out the required fields with your information.
*   Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the shared data.
*   Enter the email address of the person or organization receiving your data.
*   Specify the purpose for sharing your data (e.g., research, collaboration with healthcare providers).
*   Choose a reasonable time frame for sharing your data (e.g., specific dates or periods).
*   Indicate your consent to share your data with the specified recipient.
*   Optional fields are for recording the date and time the data was shared.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Name (Text)**: Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the shared data.
*   **Recipient Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the person or organization receiving your data.
*   **Recipient Email (Email)**: Enter a valid email address of the recipient.
*   **Purpose of Data Sharing (Text)**: Specify the reason or purpose for sharing your data.
*   **Time Frame (Text)**: Specify the time frame (e.g., specific dates or periods) for sharing your data.
*   **Sharing Consent (Select One)**: Indicate your consent to share your data with the specified recipient.
    *   **Yes**: Select this option to agree to share your data.
    *   **No**: Select this option to decline sharing your data.
*   **Date Shared (Date)**: Record the date the data was shared (optional).
*   **Time Shared (Time)**: Record the time the data was shared (optional).

## Tips

*   Be sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you have provided accurate and complete information.
*   Double-check the recipient's email address and name to ensure they are accurate and up-to-date.
*   Clearly specify the purpose and time frame for sharing your data to avoid any confusion.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about sharing your data, contact the specified recipient for clarification.
*   Remember to store a copy of the form for your records.
