# volunteer_recognition_video_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The volunteer recognition video consent form is used to obtain consent from volunteers to use their recorded video in the recognition program. This form helps to ensure that volunteers understand and agree to the terms of their video use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully to understand the questions being asked.
2. Answer all the required fields correctly.
3. Select the correct options for each field.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Recognition Video Consent** (`volunteer_recognition_video_consent_form_page_2`, required, select_one):
  + Agree: You are giving consent for the use of your recorded video in the recognition program.
  + Disagree: You are not giving consent for the use of your recorded video in the recognition program.

* **Role in the Recognition Program** (`volunteer_recognition_video_consent_form_page_3`, required, select_one):
  + Volunteer: You are a volunteer participating in the recognition program.
  + Team Lead: You are a team lead or lead volunteer participating in the recognition program.
  + Other: You are participating in the recognition program in another capacity.

* **Organization/Agency** (`volunteer_recognition_video_consent_form_page_4`, optional, text):
  + Enter the name of the organization or agency you are associated with.
  + This information will help us understand your role and context.

* **Start Date of Volunteering** (`volunteer_recognition_video_consent_form_page_5`, optional, date):
  + Enter the date you started volunteering in the recognition program.
  + Format: MM/DD/YYYY

* **Time of Day Volunteered** (`volunteer_recognition_video_consent_form_page_6`, optional, time):
  + Enter the time of day you started volunteering.
  + Format: HH:MM

* **Email** (`volunteer_recognition_video_consent_form_page_7`, required, email):
  + Enter your email address associated with your volunteer work.
  + This will help us contact you with further information about your recognition.

* **Brief Description** (`volunteer_recognition_video_consent_form_page_8`, optional, text):
  + Describe your volunteer work in the recognition program (up to 200 characters).
  + This will help us understand your contributions.

* **Hours Volunteered in Past 12 Months** (`volunteer_recognition_video_consent_form_page_9`, required, number):
  + Enter the number of hours you have volunteered in the recognition program in the past 12 months.
  + This will help us track your volunteer hours.

* **Hours Volunteered for this Event** (`volunteer_recognition_video_consent_form_page_10`, required, number):
  + Enter the number of hours you have volunteered for this event.
  + This will help us track your event-specific volunteer hours.

**Tips:**

* Ensure you understand and agree to the terms of the recognition program before filling out this form.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your recognition program administrator.
