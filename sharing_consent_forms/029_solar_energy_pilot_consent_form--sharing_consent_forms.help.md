# Solar Energy Pilot Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Solar Energy Pilot Consent Form is a mandatory document that participants must sign before joining the Solar Energy Pilot program. This form is designed to ensure that participants understand the program's terms and conditions and are aware of their rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please carefully read each field explanation below to understand what information is required.
2. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. Provide your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Sign and date the "Signature" field.
6. Read and acknowledge the participant consents (1-16) by selecting the appropriate checkboxes or entering your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Consent 1**: Read and acknowledge that you are aware of the program's terms and conditions.
* **Participant Consent 2**: Acknowledge that you understand the program's data collection and usage policies.
* **Participant Consent 3**: Confirm that you are aware of the program's confidentiality and data protection policies.
* **Participant Consent 4**: Acknowledge that you understand the program's communication policies.
* **Participant Consent 5**: Confirm that you are aware of the program's withdrawal policies.
* **Participant Consent 6**: Acknowledge that you understand the program's intellectual property policies.
* **Participant Consent 7**: Confirm that you are aware of the program's liability policies.
* **Participant Consent 8**: Acknowledge that you understand the program's warranty and disclaimer policies.
* **Participant Consent 9**: Confirm that you are aware of the program's security and data protection policies.
* **Participant Consent 10**: Acknowledge that you understand the program's audit and inspection policies.
* **Participant Consent 11**: Confirm that you are aware of the program's dispute resolution policies.
* **Participant Consent 12**: Acknowledge that you understand the program's data breach policies.
* **Participant Consent 13**: Confirm that you are aware of the program's data subject access request policies.
* **Participant Consent 14**: Acknowledge that you understand the program's cookie and tracking policies.
* **Participant Consent 15**: Confirm that you are aware of the program's third-party policies.
* **Participant Consent 16**: Acknowledge that you understand the program's miscellaneous policies.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you regularly check.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your primary contact number.
* **Signature**: Sign and date this form to confirm your consent.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review each participant consent carefully before signing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your program representative.
* This form is a requirement for participation in the Solar Energy Pilot program.
