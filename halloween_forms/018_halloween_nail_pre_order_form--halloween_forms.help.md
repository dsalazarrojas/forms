# Halloween Nail Pre-Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to pre-order Halloween-themed nail services, allowing them to reserve their desired services in advance of the holiday season.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please provide your **Form Information**.
2.  Next, select your **Customer Information**.
3.  Choose the **Pre-Order Service** you desire (Manicure, Pedicure, or Nail Polish).
4.  Select the **Date** and **Time of Service** for your appointment.
5.  Choose your **Nail Type** (French Tip, Acrylic, or Gel).
6.  Pick your **Nail Size** (Small, Medium, or Large).
7.  Select your desired **Nail Color** (Red, Pink, or Purple).
8.  Provide any **Special Instructions** for your service.
9.  Select your **Payment Method** (Cash, Credit, or Debit).
10.  Enter the **Payment Amount** for your service.
11.  Finally, enter any **Payment Comments**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form Information**: Enter your name, phone number, or email for easy contact.
    *   **Customer Information**: Enter your name, phone number, or email for our records.
    *   **Pre-Order Service**: Select one of the services offered to reserve your spot.
        +   **Manicure**: A service including nail polish application and shaping.
        +   **Pedicure**: A service including nail polish application and shaping for your feet.
        +   **Nail Polish**: A service focusing on nail color application and nail art.
    *   **Date**: The day you'd like to receive your services. Use the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
    *   **Time of Service**: The time you'd like to receive your services.
    *   **Nail Type**: Choose the type of nail application you desire.
        +   **French Tip**: A classic nail style with white tips.
        +   **Acrylic**: A durable, long-lasting nail style.
        +   **Gel**: A high-gloss, chip-resistant nail style.
    *   **Nail Size**: Choose the size of your nails.
        +   **Small**: For smaller nail beds.
        +   **Medium**: For average nail beds.
        +   **Large**: For larger nail beds.
    *   **Nail Color**: Choose the color of your nails.
        +   **Red**: A bold, Halloween-inspired color.
        +   **Pink**: A pastel, feminine color.
        +   **Purple**: A rich, mysterious color.
    *   **Special Instructions**: If you have a specific request or allergy, please let us know here.
    *   **Payment Method**: Choose how you'd like to pay for your services.
        +   **Cash**: Pay in cash at the time of service.
        +   **Credit**: Pay with a credit card.
        +   **Debit**: Pay with a debit card.
    *   **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you're paying for your services.
    *   **Payment Comments**: If you have any comments or questions about your payment, please enter them here.
    *   **Order Count**: The number of services you're pre-ordering.
    *   **Order Comments**: If you have any comments about your order, please enter them here.
