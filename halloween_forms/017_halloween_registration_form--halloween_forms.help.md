# halloween_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The halloween_registration_form is a user registration form used to collect contact information and other relevant details for a Halloween-themed event or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name in the First name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Provide your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select a date for the event or activity in the Date field.
6. Choose the time for the event or activity in the Time field.
7. Enter any additional notes in the Notes field.
8. Confirm your email address in the Confirm Email field.
9. Repeat your phone number in the second Phone field, which is a duplicate of the previous field, but this is likely a test or duplicate for a reason.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`First name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name in this field.
* **Last name** (`Last name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required: false): Select a date for the event or activity.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required: false): Choose a time for the event or activity.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about your registration.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Confirm your email address.
* **Confirm Email** (`Confirm Email`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select whether your email address is confirmed or not (True or False).
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Repeat your phone number from the previous field. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to avoid errors.
* Double-check your email and phone numbers for accuracy.
* If you have any issues with your registration, contact the event organizers.
* If you need to make any changes to your registration, please contact the event organizers as well.
