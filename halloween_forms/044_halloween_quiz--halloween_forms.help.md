# Halloween Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a lighthearted survey to gather information about users' Halloween preferences. It's meant to be a fun and engaging activity for those who enjoy the Halloween season.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Select your preferred options from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter your name and email address (if you'd like to be contacted).
4. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Halloween Quiz** (`title`, text, required: false): Enter a brief title or caption for your responses.
* **name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name to be included with your responses.
* **age** (`age`, number, required: false): Enter your age (optional, but fun to know!).
* **gender** (`gender`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you identify your gender (male, female, or other).
* **Favorite Halloween Movie** (`favorite_halloween_movie`, select_one, required: false): Select your favorite Halloween movie.
* **Favorite Halloween Character** (`favorite_halloween_character`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose multiple favorite Halloween characters.
* **How Do You Know Halloween?** (`how_do_you_know_halloween`, date, required: false): When did you start celebrating Halloween? (optional, but fun to know!).
* **time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you'd like to be contacted (optional).
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted).
* **goals** (`goals`, note, required: false): Add any additional goals or comments about Halloween.
* **Favorite Halloween Thing to Do** (`favorite_halloween_thing_to_do`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose multiple favorite Halloween activities.
* **Favorite Halloween Place** (`favorite_halloween_place`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose multiple favorite Halloween places.
* **Favorite Halloween Food** (`favorite_halloween_food`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose multiple favorite Halloween foods.
* **Favorite Halloween Activity** (`favorite_halloween_activity`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose multiple favorite Halloween activities.
* **Best Halloween Ever** (`best_halloween_ever`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the best Halloween ever experienced.

## Tips

* Be creative and have fun with your responses!
* You can select multiple options for most fields.
* If you have any feedback or suggestions, please use the "goals" field to share them.
