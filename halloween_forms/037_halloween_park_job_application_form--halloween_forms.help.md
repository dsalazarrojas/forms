# halloween_park_job_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for users to apply for jobs at a park during the Halloween season. It gathers information about their availability and job preferences to help us match them with suitable positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your email address in the "Email" field.
2. Briefly describe the job title and job description in the "Job Title" and "Job Description" fields.
3. Choose your job type by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Job Type" field.
4. Enter your availability using the time picker in the "Availability" field.
5. Choose how many hours you are willing to work in the "Job Hours" field.
6. Specify the length of your desired job duration using the number input in the "Job Duration" field.
7. Enter the location where you would like to work in the "Job Location" field.
8. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
9. Enter your contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
10. List any specific job requirements you have in the "Job Requirements" field.
11. Provide additional application instructions or comments in the "Application Instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (text, optional): Enter the title of the job you are applying for. This could be a specific role, a job function, or a general description of your job responsibilities.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your application.
* **Job Description** (text, optional): Briefly describe the job you are applying for, including your relevant experience and skills.
* **Job Type** (select_one, optional): Are you available for a day job or a night shift job? Select "Yes" for a job during the day, "No" for a night job.
* **Availability** (time, optional): Choose your available times using the time picker. You can select multiple time slots if needed.
* **Job Hours** (time, optional): Choose how many hours you are willing to work per day.
* **Job Duration** (number, optional): Enter the length of time you are available to work.
* **Job Location** (text, optional): Enter the specific location where you would like to work.
* **Contact Number** (text, optional): Enter your contact phone number in case we need to reach you.
* **Contact Email** (email, optional): Enter your contact email in case we need to reach you.
* **Job Requirements** (text, optional): List any specific requirements or qualifications you have for the job.
* **Application Instructions** (text, optional): Provide any additional information about your application or comments.
