# Halloween Pet Costume Contest Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Halloween Pet Costume Contest Form is designed to collect information about your pet's Halloween costume and your participation in the contest. This form helps us get to know you and your pet better and makes it easier for us to organize the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your pet's type (e.g., cat, dog, etc.).
3. Enter your pet's name and a brief description of the costume.
4. Enter your contact email and phone number (optional).
5. Upload a photo of your pet in its costume (optional).
6. Select the category for your pet's costume (e.g., Entertainment, Fashion, Food).
7. Check if your pet has participated in the contest before.
8. Confirm that you, as the owner, are giving permission for your pet to participate in the contest.
9. Sign your name as the participant.
10. Sign your name as the pet owner.
11. Enter your address, city, and state (optional).
12. Enter your zip code (optional).
13. Add any additional notes about the event date and time (optional).
14. Enter your notes as the owner (optional).
15. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, required): Select your pet's type (e.g., cat, dog, etc.).
* **Costume Name** (`costume_name`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your pet's costume.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Pet Image** (`pet_image`, text, optional): Upload a photo of your pet in its costume (optional).
* **Pet Description** (`pet_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your pet.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event (optional).
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event (optional).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool assigned to you (optional).
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required): Select the category for your pet's costume (e.g., Entertainment, Fashion, Food).
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your pet's costume.
* **Has this pet participated in the contest before?** (`first_time_participant`, select_one, required): Check if your pet has participated in the contest before.
* **Participant Permission** (`owner_permission`, select_one, required): Confirm that you, as the owner, are giving permission for your pet to participate in the contest.
* **Participant Signature** (`owner_signature`, text, optional): Sign your name as the participant.
* **Pet Owner Signature** (`pet_owner_signature`, text, optional): Sign your name as the pet owner.
* **Address** (`owner_address`, text, optional): Enter your address (optional).
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city (optional).
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state (optional).
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code (optional).
* **Event Date Note** (`event_date_note`, text, optional): Add any notes about the event date (optional).
* **Event Time Note** (`event_time_note`, text, optional): Add any notes about the event time (optional).
* **Owner Notes** (`owner_notes`, text, optional): Add any notes as the owner (optional).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the person who submitted the form (optional).
