# Halloween Costume Contest Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Costume Contest Voting Form is designed to collect user input for the Halloween costume contest. This form will help us understand user opinions and preferences on various aspects of the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your age in the "age" field.
4. Select your best costume choice from the "best_costume" field.
5. Select other costumes you liked from the "other_costumes" field.
6. Choose a date for the contest from the "voting_date" field.
7. Choose a time for the contest from the "voting_time" field.
8. Enter any additional notes about the contest in the "notes" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
11. Select your best category choice from the "best_category" field.
12. Select other characters you enjoyed from the "other_characters" field.
13. Select your favorite color from the "favorite_color" field.
14. Select your favorite haunted house from the "favorite_haunted_house" field.
15. Select the least favorite haunted house from the "least_favorite_haunted_house" field.
16. Select the least favorite costume from the "least_favorite_costume" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your age.
* **best_costume** (`best_costume`, select_one, required): Select your best costume.
* **other_costumes** (`other_costumes`, select_multiple, required): Select other costumes you liked.
* **voting_date** (`voting_date`, date, required): Choose a date for the contest.
* **voting_time** (`voting_time`, time, required): Choose a time for the contest.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes about the contest.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **best_category** (`best_category`, select_one, required): Select your best category.
* **other_characters** (`other_characters`, select_multiple, required): Select other characters you enjoyed.
* **favorite_color** (`favorite_color`, select_multiple, required): Select your favorite color.
* **favorite_haunted_house** (`favorite_haunted_house`, select_multiple, required): Select your favorite haunted house.
* **least_favorite_haunted_house** (`least_favorite_haunted_house`, select_multiple, required): Select the least favorite haunted house.
* **least_favorite_costume** (`least_favorite_costume`, select_multiple, required): Select the least favorite costume.

Note: This form is for internal use only and is not intended for public use. Please fill in the form carefully and accurately to ensure that your input is recorded correctly.
