# Virtual Halloween Cosplay Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for participants to submit their Halloween cosplay ideas to a virtual contest. Please fill out the form to enter your creation and have a chance to win a prize.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields and submit:

* **Category**: Select the category that best describes your Halloween cosplay entry.
* **Description**: Please describe your costume in 200-400 words.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Submission Image URL**: Enter the URL of your submission image.
* **Submission Video URL (optional)**: Enter the URL of your submission video (if you have one).
* **Submission Link (optional)**: Enter the URL of your submission link (if you have one).
* **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to submit your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category**: Please select the category that best describes your Halloween cosplay entry. This will help the judges to categorize your entry correctly. 
* **Description**: Please describe your costume in 200-400 words. This is an opportunity to showcase your creativity and imagination. 
* **Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport. 
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address where we can contact you if you win or need further information. 
* **Submission Image URL**: Enter the URL of your submission image (e.g., a Google Drive link or a photo hosting website). Make sure it is a clear and high-quality image of your costume. 
* **Submission Video URL (optional)**: If you have a video of your submission, enter the URL where it can be viewed. This will give the judges a better understanding of your costume. 
* **Submission Link (optional)**: If you have a website or social media link that showcases your submission, enter the URL here. This will give the judges more information about your work. 
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your entry. Once you click "Submit", your entry will be received by our team and reviewed by the judges. 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a clear and high-quality image of your submission.
* Use a valid email address where we can contact you if you win or need further information.
* If you have any issues with submitting your entry, contact our support team.
