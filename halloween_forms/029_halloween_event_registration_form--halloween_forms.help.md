# Halloween Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a Halloween event. It is required for all participants to complete this form to provide their personal and event-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked as required in the field label.
2. Enter your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your email address in the email address field, if you wish to receive updates about the event.
4. Enter your phone number, if you wish to receive calls or text about the event.
5. Select a date for the event from the calendar.
6. Choose a time for the event from the 24-hour clock.
7. Enter the location of the event.
8. Select all the participant interests that apply to you, such as Halloween, Costume, or Pumpkin Carving.
9. Select how you would like to participate in the event, such as Trick or Treat, Attend Event, or Stay Away.
10. Optionally, enter any additional notes about your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address here, if you wish to receive updates about the event.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number, if you wish to receive calls or text about the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Select a date for the event from the calendar.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Choose a time for the event from the 24-hour clock.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Participant Interest** (`participant_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select all the participant interests that apply to you, such as Halloween, Costume, or Pumpkin Carving.
* **Participant Treatment** (`participant_treatment`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to participate in the event, such as Trick or Treat, Attend Event, or Stay Away.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your participation.

Note: The participant interests and treatment fields are used to help the event organizers tailor the event to your needs and preferences. Please make sure to select all applicable options.
