# Halloween Candy Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Candy Donation Form is a simple online form designed to collect information from individuals interested in donating candy to a local food drive. The form is intended to gather contact details and other relevant information to facilitate the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the text field labeled "name".
2. Provide your email address in the text field labeled "email".
3. Enter your phone number in the text field labeled "phone".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Halloween Candy Donation Form** (`halloween_candy_donation`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form, which is not a field that you need to fill in.
* **name** (name, text, required/optional): Enter your name in this field.
* **email** (email, email, required/optional): Provide your email address in this format: `example@example.com`. This will be used to contact you about your donation.
* **phone** (phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number in this format: `123-456-7890`.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields correctly to ensure your donation is processed smoothly.
* Double-check your email address for formatting errors to avoid issues with email delivery.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us directly.
