# Halloween Candy Gram Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to place orders for Halloween-themed candy grams. The form collects customer information, order details, and candy gram preferences to facilitate a smooth and error-free order placement process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
3. Make sure to select the correct payment method and confirm it.
4. Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
5. Complete any other required fields as per the form instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (1)**: Enter your name and address to receive the candy gram at.
* **Order Details (2)**: Describe the candy gram you'd like to order, including any special requests.
* **Candy Gram Details (3)**: Choose the type of candy to be included in the gram, such as Grapes, Apples, Pears, or Oranges.
* **Shipping Address (4)**: Select the address where you'd like the candy gram to be shipped to.
* **Email (6)**: Enter a valid email address to receive updates on your order.
* **Phone (7)**: Enter your phone number for any additional information or updates.
* **Total Candy (8)**: Enter the total amount of candy to be included in the gram.
* **Payment Method (9)**: Choose your payment method, such as Cash, Credit, or PayPal.
* **Confirm Payment Method (10)**: Confirm your selected payment method.
* **Order Total (11)**: This will automatically be calculated after payment method selection.
* **Tax (12)**: Enter the tax amount.
* **Shipping Cost (13)**: Enter the shipping cost.
* **Total Cost (14)**: This will automatically be calculated after shipping cost entry.
* **Notes (15)**: Add any additional comments or notes for the order.
* **Order Date (16)**: Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time (17)**: Enter the time of the order.
* **Time Zone (18)**: Select your time zone.
* **Comments (19)**: Choose whether or not you'd like to receive comments on the order.
* **Status (20)**: View the status of your order, such as Completed, Processing, or Cancelled.
* **Source (21)**: Choose the source of the order, such as Online Order or Offline Order.
* **Order Number (22)**: This will be automatically generated and displayed.
* **Assigned User (23)**: Select the user assigned to handle the order.
* **Order ID (24)**: This will be automatically generated and displayed.
* **Created At (25)**: This will display the date and time the order was created.

Note: Some fields may not be required, but it's still essential to fill them out accurately for efficient order processing. Please review the form carefully before submission to avoid any errors.
