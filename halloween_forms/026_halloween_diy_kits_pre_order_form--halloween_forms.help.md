# halloween_diy_kits_pre_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to pre-order Halloween DIY kits. It captures the necessary information for the customer to place their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form submission section with your name and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your order details, including what you're interested in pre-ordering.
3. Enter your payment information to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, `text`, required): Please enter your name and any other relevant information about your order. This will help us get back to you as soon as possible.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, optional): Describe what you're interested in pre-ordering. This could be a specific kit, a customization request, or any other relevant details.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your payment method and any other payment-related information. Please note that this is a text field and does not support direct payment entry like credit card numbers or PayPal IDs.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your order can be processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
