# Fall Pumpkin Carving Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fall Pumpkin Carving Contest Entry Form is used to collect entries for a pumpkin carving contest. It is designed for participants to submit their pumpkin carving designs for judging.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by filling out the form**: Begin by selecting the "Entry Form" page.
2. **Provide your participant details**: Fill in your Participant Name, Participant Email, and Participant Phone in the respective fields.
3. **Submit your entry**: Fill in the Entry Title and Entry Description of your pumpkin carving design.
4. **Add your entry image**: Upload or link to a photo of your pumpkin carving.
5. **Review and submit**: Review your entry carefully and submit it for judging.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entry Form**: This is the main submission page for your entry.
* **Participant Name**: Your name as a participant in the contest.
* **Participant Email**: Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Participant Phone**: Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Entry Title**: A brief title of your pumpkin carving design.
* **Entry Description**: A description of your pumpkin carving design.
* **Entry Image**: A photo or link to a photo of your pumpkin carving design.
 
## Tips
* Ensure that your participant details are accurate to facilitate effective communication with contest organizers.
* Upload or link a clear and high-quality photo of your pumpkin carving design.
* Review your entry carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
