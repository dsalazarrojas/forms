# Halloween Treat Bag Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Treat Bag Request Form is designed for parents or guardians to request a treat bag for their child, schedule a visit from a technician, and provide essential contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your child's name in the "Child's Name" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Parent's Name" field.
3. Provide your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
6. Select the date and time for the visit in the "Date and Time" field.
7. Choose a suitable tool from the "Assigned Tool" field.
8. Select the assigned technician(s) from the "Assigned Technician" field.
9. Choose the assigned shift from the "Assigned Shift" field.
10. (Optional) Enter any additional notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trick or Treat Bag Request**: This field is the main request for the treat bag. Please enter the type or description of the treat bag you are requesting.
* **Childs Name**: Enter your child's name so we know who the treat bag is for.
* **Parents Name**: Enter your name so we know who to contact regarding the visit.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number so we can reach you to confirm the visit.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can communicate with you about the visit.
* **Address**: Enter your address so we can know where to deliver the treat bag.
* **Date and Time**: Select the date and time for the visit. This is crucial for scheduling purposes.
* **Notes**: This field is optional. If you have any additional information or special requests, please use this field.
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose a tool from the list that is suitable for your request.
* **Assigned Technician**: Select one or more technicians that you would like to be assigned to your visit.
* **Assigned Shift**: Choose a shift from the list that is available for the selected date and time.
* **Assigned Technician Notes**: This field is optional. If you have any additional information or special requests for the assigned technician, please use this field.
* **Customer Notes**: This field is optional. If you have any additional information or special requests, please use this field.

Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to avoid any miscommunication or misunderstandings. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
