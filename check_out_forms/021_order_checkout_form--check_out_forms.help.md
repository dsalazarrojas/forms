# Order Checkout Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Order Checkout Form is designed to collect order details and customer information for processing and shipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your order details and customer information.
2. Review your input carefully to ensure it is accurate and complete.
3. Click submit to send your order for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`checkout_form`, text, not required): This is the primary field for entering your order details. Please fill in any relevant information about your order, such as the order number, date, or any special instructions.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, not required): This field is for entering your name and contact information. Please provide your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, not required): If you are having your order shipped to a different address than your own, please enter the delivery address here. This includes your name, address, city, state, and postal code.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields for accurate processing.
* Double-check your information before submitting your order.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact our customer support.
