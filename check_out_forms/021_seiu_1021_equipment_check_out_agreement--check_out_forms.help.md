# seiu_1021_equipment_check_out_agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for checking out equipment from the inventory. It is a required field to document the check-out agreement between the equipment owner and the user.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Please answer all required fields (marked as 'true' in the form).
- Select "Yes" or "No" in the 'Equipment Type' field to indicate if the equipment is to be checked out.
- Enter the date you will return the equipment to the inventory in the 'Check-in Date' field.
- Enter your signature in the 'User Signed' field.
- Enter the date you will take the equipment in the 'Check Out Date' field.
- Enter the equipment's unique identifier or code in the 'Assigned Tool' field.
- Fill in the location where the equipment was last used (optional).
- Add any additional comments or notes for the equipment check-out agreement in the 'Notes' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the equipment is to be checked out.
* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you will return the equipment to the inventory.
* **User Signed** (`user_signed`, `text`, required): Enter your signature to confirm that you are aware of your responsibilities for the checked-out equipment.
* **Check Out Date** (`check_out_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you took the equipment out from the inventory.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required): Enter the equipment's unique identifier or code.
* **Equipment Location** (`equipment_location`, `text`, optional): Fill in the location where the equipment was last used.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes for the equipment check-out agreement.
