# Community Cleanup Volunteer Check-Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Cleanup Volunteer Check-Out Form is a survey designed to gather information from volunteers after participating in community cleanup events. This form helps organizers evaluate the event's effectiveness, assess volunteer engagement, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Provide your email address and phone number for follow-up communications.
3. Report your check-in and check-out times to track your volunteer hours.
4. Select the team you were assigned to and the name of your team leader.
5. Report the total hours you worked and the equipment you used or returned.
6. Estimate the number of trash bags collected and describe the area you cleaned.
7. If applicable, report any notable findings or safety incidents.
8. Rate your overall experience with the event.
9. Indicate your interest in participating in future events.
10. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions.
11. (Optional) Choose your T-Shirt size for volunteer appreciation.
12. Sign with your supervisor's initials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (1, Text, Required): Enter your legal first name.
- **Last Name** (2, Text, Required): Enter your legal last name.
- **Email Address** (3, Email, Required): Provide your email address for follow-up communications.
- **Phone Number** (4, Text, Optional): Enter your primary contact phone number.
- **Check-In Time** (5, Time, Required): Report the time you started volunteering.
- **Check-Out Time** (6, Time, Required): Report the current time when you finished volunteering.
- **Team Assignment** (7, Select One, Required): Choose the team you were assigned to from the options provided.
- **Team Leader Name** (8, Text, Required): Enter the name of your team leader.
- **Total Hours Worked** (9, Number, Required): Report the total hours you worked during the event.
- **Equipment Returned** (10, Select Multiple, Required): Select all equipment items you are returning.
- **Equipment Condition** (11, Text, Optional): Describe any damaged or missing equipment.
- **Number of Trash Bags Collected** (12, Number, Required): Estimate the number of trash bags collected.
- **Specific Area Cleaned** (13, Text, Required): Describe the area you cleaned.
- **Notable Findings** (14, Text, Optional): Report any unusual items or discoveries made during the event.
- **Were There Any Safety Incidents** (15, Select One, Required): Indicate if there were any safety incidents and provide a brief description if applicable.
- **Incident Description** (16, Text, Optional): Describe the safety incident if you reported yes.
- **How Would You Rate Your Experience** (17, Select One, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event.
- **Would You Volunteer Again** (18, Select One, Required): Indicate your interest in participating in future events.
- **Additional Feedback** (19, Text, Optional): Provide any suggestions or comments about the event.
- **T-Shirt Size** (20, Select One, Optional): Choose your T-Shirt size if you would like one.
- **Supervisor Initials** (21, Text, Required): Sign with your supervisor's initials.
