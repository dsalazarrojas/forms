# Airbnb Check In Check Out Policies Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Airbnb Check In Check Out Policies Agreement Form is a mandatory document that guests must complete before checking in to a property. Its purpose is to ensure that guests are aware of and agree to the host's policies and expectations regarding check-in and check-out times, cleaning, and responsibility for damages.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. **Provide your guest name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your booking reservation.
2. **Enter property address**: Type the exact address of the property you are visiting.
3. **Check-in time**: If you have a specific check-in time, enter it (though this is not required). If not, please leave this field blank.
4. **Check-out time**: If you have a specific check-out time, enter it (though this is not required). If not, please leave this field blank.
5. **Cleaning policy summary**: Read and agree to the cleaning expectations and fees outlined in this section. This may include details about trash disposal, cleaning schedules, and additional cleaning fees.
6. **Damage policy summary**: Read and agree to the explanation of responsibility for damages, including any potential fees or penalties for accidental or intentional damages.
7. **Agree to policies**: Select "True" to confirm that you have read and agree to abide by the check-in and check-out policies.
8. **Additional requests**: If you have any special requests or questions related to check-in or check-out, please list them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest name**: Your full name as it appears on your booking reservation.
* **Property address**: The exact address of the property you are visiting.
* **Check in time**: The official check-in time for the property. If you're unsure, please check with your host.
* **Check out time**: The official check-out time for the property. If you're unsure, please check with your host.
* **Cleaning policy summary**: This section outlines the cleaning expectations and fees for the property. Please read and agree to these terms.
* **Damage policy summary**: This section explains your responsibility for damages, including any potential fees or penalties.
* **Agree to policies**: Select "True" to confirm that you have read and agree to abide by the check-in and check-out policies.
* **Additional requests**: Any special requests or questions related to check-in or check-out can be listed here.
