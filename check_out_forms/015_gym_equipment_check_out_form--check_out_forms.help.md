# Gym Equipment Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the check-out and check-in dates of gym equipment, as well as member information and equipment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Member Name** in the first field. This is where you should enter your name as it appears on your membership card or profile.
2. Enter your **Member Email** in the second field. This is your email address associated with your membership account.
3. In the **Item Name** field, enter the name or code of the equipment you are checking out.
4. Select the **Check Out Date** when you took the equipment out from your calendar or schedule.
5. Select the **Check In Date** when you plan to return the equipment to the gym.
6. Choose the **Equipment Type** from the available options to categorize the equipment.
7. Select the **Equipment Status** as either **New Equipment** or **Used Equipment** based on its condition.
8. Choose the **Equipment Condition** as **Excellent**, **Good**, or **Poor** to indicate its current condition.
9. Enter any additional **Member Notes** if you need to include any specific instructions or comments about your usage.
10. Enter any **Equipment Notes** if you want to include any specific notes about the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Name**: Your name as it appears on your membership account.
* **Member Email**: Your email address associated with your membership account.
* **Item Name**: The name or code of the equipment you are taking out.
* **Check Out Date**: The date when you took the equipment out from your calendar or schedule.
* **Check In Date**: The date when you plan to return the equipment to the gym.
* **Equipment Type**: The category of the equipment (e.g., free weights, treadmills, etc.).
* **Equipment Status**: Whether the equipment is **New Equipment** or **Used Equipment**.
* **Equipment Condition**: The current condition of the equipment, as **Excellent**, **Good**, or **Poor**.
* **Member Notes**: Any additional comments or instructions about your usage of the equipment.
* **Equipment Notes**: Any specific notes about the equipment that may be useful for the gym staff.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct **Member Name** and **Member Email** to ensure accurate tracking and communication.
* Select the correct **Check In Date** to ensure you return the equipment on time.
* Be honest about the **Equipment Condition** to help the gym staff maintain the equipment properly. 
* Keep the **Member Notes** and **Equipment Notes** clear and concise to provide relevant information.
