# Student Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Check Out Form is a simple form used to collect information from students for check-out purposes. This form is meant to gather essential data from students before they leave the premises or participate in activities that require a record of their presence. Completing this form ensures that your school or institution can accurately track student attendance and ensure their safety.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
2.  Enter the student's name in the "Check Out Student Name" field.
3.  Provide the student's ID number in the "Student ID" field.
4.  Enter the guardian's name in the "Guardian Name" field.
5.  Enter the guardian's email address in the "Guardian Email" field.
6.  Enter the guardian's phone number in the "Guardian Phone" field.
7.  Click on the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Check Out Student Name** (`check_out_student_name`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the student who is checking out. This field is required.
*   **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, Required/Optional): Enter the student's ID number. This field is required.
*   **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of the student's guardian or contact person. This field is optional.
*   **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, email, Required/Optional): Enter the email address of the student's guardian or contact person. This field is optional.
*   **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, Required/Optional): Enter the phone number of the student's guardian or contact person. This field is optional.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill in all required fields.
*   Double-check that all entered information is accurate and up-to-date.
*   If you are unsure about any field, contact the designated staff member or supervisor for guidance.
