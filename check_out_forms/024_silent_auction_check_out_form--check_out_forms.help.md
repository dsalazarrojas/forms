# silent_auction_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The silent auction check-out form is used to gather contact and preference information from users interested in participating in the silent auction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your relevant information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address where we can contact you with more information about the silent auction.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where we can contact you with more information about the silent auction.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation`, number, required: false): Enter the amount you are willing to donate for the silent auction.
* **Bid Amount** (`bid_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the amount you are willing to bid on an item in the silent auction.
* **Bid Time** (`bid_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you would be available to bid on an item in the silent auction.
* **Bid Date** (`bid_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would be available to bid on an item in the silent auction.
* **Participation Type** (`silent_auction_participation`, select multiple, required: false): Select how you plan to participate in the silent auction, either as a donor, an online bidder, or both.
* **How Did You Hear About the Silent Auction?** (`how_did_you_hear`, select multiple, required: false): Select how you heard about the silent auction, either through social media, email, word of mouth, or other means.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact information and participation preferences before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
