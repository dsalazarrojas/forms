# Freight Shipment Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for checking out a freight shipment, allowing both the shipper and the payer to input relevant information. It is designed to collect details about the shipment, payment, and shipping methods.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the fields and select the applicable options for each section.
* Enter the shipment details, including the date and time of the shipment, the address, and any additional notes about the shipment.
* Select the payment method and enter the relevant payment information, including the total amount and currency.
* Choose the shipping method (Ground, Express, Air, or Sea).
* Enter any additional comments or notes about the shipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipment Details** (`shipment_details`): This section is for entering details about the shipment, including date, time, address, and notes.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`): This section is for entering payment information, including payment method, payment status, total amount, and currency.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`): Choose from the options Ground, Express, Air, or Sea.
* **Shipment Fee** (`payment_info_shipment_fee`): Enter the fee for the shipment.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select how you would like to pay (Pay with Credit Card or Pay with Cash).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_info_shipment_payment_status`): Select the status of the payment (Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid).
* **Total Amount** (`payment_info_shipment_total`): Enter the total amount of the shipment.
* **Currency** (`payment_info_shipment_currency`): Select the currency of the shipment (USD or GBP).

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for all fields to ensure correct processing of the shipment.
* Select the correct payment method and status to ensure proper handling of payment.
* Review and confirm all information before submitting the form.
