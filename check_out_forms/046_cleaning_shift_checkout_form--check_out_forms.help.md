# Cleaning Shift Checkout Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cleaning Shift Checkout Form is a tool used by cleaning staff to document their completion of a shift, including tasks, quality checks, and other essential details. This guide will walk you through the form's purpose, how to complete it, and each field's explanation.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the Cleaning Shift Checkout Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your facility's name in the "Facility Name" field (Field 1). This is the name of the building or facility where you worked.
2. Specify the area or zone you cleaned in the "Facility Area or Zone" field (Field 2).
3. Enter the date of your shift in the "Shift Date" field (Field 3).
4. Select the type of shift you completed in the "Shift Type" field (Field 4). Options include Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night, and Weekend Shifts.
5. Enter the time you finished your shift in the "Checkout Time" field (Field 5).
6. Provide your name in the "Team Member Name" field (Field 6).
7. Enter your supervisor's name in the "Supervisor Name" field (Field 7).
8. If applicable, list any incomplete tasks in the "List of Incomplete Tasks" field (Field 10).
9. Check if you completed a quality check in the "Self Quality Check Completed" field (Field 13).
10. If you found any quality issues, describe them in the "Quality Issues Found" field (Field 14).
11. If necessary, provide a corrective action taken in the "Corrective Action Taken" field (Field 15).
12. Check if you restocked any supplies in the "Supplies Restocked" field (Field 17).
13. List any supplies that are running low in the "Supplies Running Low" field (Field 18).
14. Confirm if all equipment was properly stored in the "Equipment Properly Stored" field (Field 19).
15. If you have any equipment issues, describe them in the "Equipment Issues or Malfunctions" field (Field 20).
16. If equipment needs repair or replacement, list it in the "Equipment Needs Repair or Replacement" field (Field 21).
17. Confirm if all areas were secured in the "All Areas Secured" field (Field 23).
18. Check if lights were adjusted appropriately in the "Lights Adjusted Appropriately" field (Field 24).
19. Confirm if windows were checked and secured in the "Windows Checked and Secured" field (Field 25).
20. If any safety hazards were reported, describe them in the "Safety Hazards Reported" field (Field 26).
21. Provide notes for the next shift in the "Notes for Next Shift" field (Field 28).
22. List any pending tasks for the next shift in the "Pending Tasks for Next Shift" field (Field 29).
23. Describe any client requests or complaints in the "Client Requests or Complaints" field (Field 30).
24. If you submitted maintenance requests, list them in the "Maintenance Requests Submitted" field (Field 31).
25. Check if all keys or access cards were returned in the "Keys or Access Cards Returned" field (Field 33).
26. Confirm if the alarm system was activated in the "Alarm System Set" field (Field 34).
27. Check if you completed a final walkthrough in the "Final Walkthrough Completed" field (Field 35).
28. Confirm your overall shift status in the "Checkout Status" field (Field 36).
29. Sign your name in the "Team Member Signature" field (Field 37).
30. If you're a supervisor, check if you've approved the shift in the "Supervisor Approval" field (Field 38).
31. Sign your name as supervisor in the "Supervisor Signature" field (Field 39).
32. Enter the date of your checkout in the "Checkout Date" field (Field 40).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Facility Name** (Field 1): Enter the name of the facility or building where you worked.
- **Facility Area or Zone** (Field 2): Specify the area or zone you cleaned in the facility.
- **Shift Date** (Field 3): Enter the date of your shift.
- **Shift Type** (Field 4): Select the type of shift you completed (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night, or Weekend Shift).
- **Checkout Time** (Field 5): Enter the time you finished your shift.
- **Team Member Name** (Field 6): Provide your name.
- **Supervisor Name** (Field 7): Enter your supervisor's name.
- **All Assigned Tasks Completed** (Field 9): Check if you completed all assigned tasks.
- **List of Incomplete Tasks** (Field 10): If you didn't complete tasks, list them here.
- **Reason for Incomplete Tasks** (Field 11): Provide an explanation for any incomplete tasks.
- **Self Quality Check Completed** (Field 13): Check if you performed a quality check.
- **Quality Issues Found** (Field 14): Describe any quality issues you found.
- **Corrective Action Taken** (Field 15): If necessary, provide a corrective action taken.
- **Supplies Restocked** (Field 17): Check if you restocked any supplies.
- **Supplies Running Low** (Field 18): List any supplies that are running low.
- **Equipment Properly Stored** (Field 19): Confirm if all equipment was properly stored.
- **Equipment Issues or Malfunctions** (Field 20): If you have equipment issues, describe them here.
- **Equipment Needs Repair or Replacement** (Field 21): If equipment needs repair or replacement, list it.
- **All Areas Secured** (Field 23): Confirm if all areas were secured.
- **Lights Adjusted Appropriately** (Field 24): Check if lights were adjusted correctly.
- **Windows Checked and Secured** (Field 25): Confirm if windows were checked and secured.
- **Safety Hazards Reported** (Field 26): If you found any safety hazards, describe them.
- **Notes for Next Shift** (Field 28): Provide notes for the next shift.
- **Pending Tasks for Next Shift** (Field 29): List any pending tasks for the next shift.
- **Client Requests or Complaints** (Field 30): Describe any client requests or complaints.
- **Maintenance Requests Submitted** (Field 31): If you submitted maintenance requests, list them.
- **Keys or Access Cards Returned** (Field 33): Confirm if all keys or access cards were returned.
- **Alarm System Set** (Field 34): Confirm if the alarm system was activated.
- **Final Walkthrough Completed** (Field 35): Check if you completed a final walkthrough.
- **Checkout Status** (Field 36): Confirm your overall shift status.
- **Team Member Signature** (Field 37): Sign your name as a team member.
- **Supervisor Approval** (Field 38): Check if you've approved the shift as a supervisor.
- **Supervisor Signature** (Field 39): Sign your name as a supervisor.
- **Checkout Date** (Field 40): Enter the date of your checkout.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.
* If you're unsure about a field, ask your supervisor or team lead for clarification.
* Keep the form up to date and accurate for future reference.
