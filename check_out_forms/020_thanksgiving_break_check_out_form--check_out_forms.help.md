# Thanksgiving Break Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document a student's check-out process during the Thanksgiving break. This form will be used to gather information from students and staff members for check-out and check-in purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the student's email address in the "Student Email" field.
3. Enter the student's phone number in the "Student Phone" field.
4. Select the check-out date from the calendar.
5. Select the check-in date from the calendar.
6. Provide a reason for check-out in the "Reason for Check Out" field.
7. Enter any comments or notes about the check-out process in the "Comments" field.
8. Select the time the student will be signing out (if applicable).
9. Select the time the staff member will be signing out (if applicable).
10. Select the date the staff member will be signing out (if applicable).
11. Enter any additional notes about the staff member's sign-out process in the "Staff Sign-out Note" field.
12. Select whether the student was informed about the check-out time or not.
13. Select whether an emergency contact was informed about the check-out process.
14. Enter the name of the student's emergency contact.
15. Select whether an alternate emergency contact was informed about the check-out process (if applicable).
16. Select the student's signature (if applicable).
17. Select the staff member's signature (if applicable).
18. Enter any additional comments or notes from the staff member in the "Staff Comments" field.
19. Enter any additional comments or notes from the student in the "Student Comments" field.
20. Enter any additional notes about the check-out process in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check Out** (`id: 1`, `label: Check Out`, `type: number`, `required: false`): 
  Enter the date and time the student will be checking out.

* **Student Name** (`id: 2`, `label: student_name`, `type: text`, `required: false`): 
  Enter the name of the student checking out.

* **Student Email** (`id: 3`, `label: student_email`, `type: email`, `required: false`): 
  Enter the student's email address.

* **Student Phone** (`id: 4`, `label: student_phone`, `type: text`, `required: false`): 
  Enter the student's phone number.

* **Check Out Date** (`id: 5`, `label: check_out_date`, `type: date`, `required: false`): 
  Select the date the student will be checking out.

* **Check In Date** (`id: 6`, `label: check_in_date`, `type: date`, `required: false`): 
  Select the date the student will be checking in.

* **Reason for Check Out** (`id: 7`, `label: reason_for_check_out`, `type: text`, `required: false`): 
  Provide a reason for the student's check out.

* **Comments** (`id: 8`, `label: comments`, `type: text`, `required: false`): 
  Enter any comments or notes about the check-out process.

* **Student Sign Out Time** (`id: 9`, `label: student_sign_out_time`, `type: time`, `required: false`): 
  Select the time the student will be signing out.

* **Staff Sign Out Time** (`id: 10`, `label: staff_sign_out_time`, `type: time`, `required: false`): 
  Select the time the staff member will be signing out.

* **Staff Sign Out Date** (`id: 11`, `label: staff_sign_out_date`, `type: date`, `required: false`): 
  Select the date the staff member will be signing out.

* **Staff Sign Out Note** (`id: 12`, `label: staff_sign_out_note`, `type: text`, `required: false`):

* **Emergency Contact Informed** (`id: 15`, `label: emergency_contact_informed`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`):

  Enter any additional comments or notes from the staff member.

* **Student Comments** (`id: 24`, `label: student_comments`, `type: text`, `required: false`): 
  Enter any additional comments or notes from the student.

* **Notes** (`id: 25`, `label: notes`, `type: text`, `required: false`): 
  Enter any additional notes about the check-out process.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask.
* The information collected from this form will be used to keep track of student check-out processes for the Thanksgiving break.
