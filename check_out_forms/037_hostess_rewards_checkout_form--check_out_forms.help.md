# Hostess Rewards Checkout Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hostess Rewards Checkout Form is a collection of fields used to track the status of hostess rewards, allowing hosts to accurately record rewards earned and redeemed by their customers. This form aims to provide a comprehensive overview of the reward status, facilitating efficient management and reporting for hosts.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete the Hostess Rewards Checkout Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields as needed, making sure to complete all required fields.
2. Review the form before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info (1)**: Enter the customer's name, email, or other identifying information to associate with their rewards points.
* **Date/Time (2)**: Enter the date and time of the customer's visit or reward redemption.
* **Reward Type (3)**: Select 'Yes' if the customer has earned or redeemed rewards, or 'No' if not.
* **Points Earned (4)**: Enter the total points earned by the customer.
* **Points Used (5)**: Enter the total points redeemed by the customer.
* **Customer ID (6)**: Enter the customer's unique identifier, often their email address.
* **Prophets Earned (7)**: This field appears to be a typo or placeholder; if you're unsure, please contact your administrator for clarification.
* **Reward Status (8)**: Select 'Active' if the customer has active rewards or 'Inactive' if they do not.
* **Points Status (9)**: Select 'Active' if the customer's points are active or 'Inactive' if not.
* **Customer Notes (10)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the customer's rewards status.
* **Customer Comments (11)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback from the customer.
* **Reward Program Notes (12)**: Enter any information about the reward program or its rules.
* **Assigned Tool (13)**: Enter the tool or system used to manage rewards.
* **Form ID (14)**: Enter the unique ID of this form submission.
* **Category (15)**: This field is empty; if you're unsure, please contact your administrator for clarification.
* **Description (16)**: Enter a brief description of the form or its purpose.
* **Customer (17)**: Enter the customer's name or other identifying information.
* **Reward Program (18)**: Enter the name of the reward program being used.
* **Notes (19)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the form submission.
* **Field 20 (20)**: This field is currently empty; if you're unsure, please contact your administrator for clarification.

Note: If you are unsure about any field or have questions about this form, please contact your administrator for assistance.
