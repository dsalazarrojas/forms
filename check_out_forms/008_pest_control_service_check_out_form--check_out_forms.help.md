# Pest Control Service Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers who have previously interacted with the pest control service. It is used to schedule a follow-up visit, confirm a service appointment, or to collect payment for services rendered.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Select the type of service you are scheduling or confirming (e.g. Ant Control, Bee Control, etc.).
3. Enter the date of the service visit.
4. Choose how you would like to pay for the service (e.g. Cash, Credit Card, etc.).
5. Enter your email address (for communication and confirmation purposes).
6. Enter your phone number (for follow-up calls or notifications).
7. If applicable, add any additional notes about your service visit (e.g. specific areas to focus on, special requests, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (Customer Name)** (Required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Service Type (Service Type)** (Required): Select the type of service you are scheduling or confirming (e.g. Ant Control, Bee Control, etc.).
* **Service Date (Service Date)** (Required): Enter the date of the service visit.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)** (Required): Choose how you would like to pay for the service (e.g. Cash, Credit Card, etc.).
* **Customer E-mail (Customer E-mail)** (Required): Enter your email address for communication and confirmation purposes.
* **Customer Phone (Customer Phone)** (Required): Enter your phone number for follow-up calls or notifications.
* **Notes (Notes)** (Optional): If applicable, add any additional notes about your service visit (e.g. specific areas to focus on, special requests, etc.). 

## Tips

* Double-check that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Make sure to use the correct payment method and confirm your service date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
