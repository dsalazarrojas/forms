# Hiking Trip Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hiking Trip Check Out Form is designed to collect information about hiker's experiences and feedback about a specific trail. This form helps us gather valuable data to improve and maintain our trails for future users.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct trail from the options provided.
2. Enter the trail conditions as you experienced them during your hike.
3. Choose the correct trail type and difficulty level.
4. Enter any relevant notes or comments about your experience.
5. Provide your user information, including email and phone number.
6. Finally, click "Submit" to submit your review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trail Name (`trail_name`, text, optional)**: Enter the name of the trail you hiked.
* **Trail Length (`trail_length`, number, optional)**: Enter the length of the trail in kilometers or miles.
* **Trail Difficulty (`trail_difficulty`, select_one, optional)**: Select the difficulty level of the trail from Hiking, Backpacking, or Mountaineering.
* **Check Out Date (`check_out_date`, date, optional)**: Enter the date you checked out of the trail.
* **End User Device Id (`end_user_device_id`, text, optional)**: Enter your device ID, if applicable.
* **Check Out Time (`check_out_time`, time, optional)**: Enter the time you checked out of the trail.
* **Trail Type (`trail_type`, select_multiple, optional)**: Select any applicable trail types, such as Hiking or Backpacking.
* **Trail Conditions (`trail_conditions`, text, optional)**: Enter any notes or comments about the trail conditions.
* **Trail Location (`trail_location`, text, optional)**: Enter the location of the trail.
* **User Id (`user_id`, text, optional)**: Enter your user ID, if applicable.
* **User Name (`user_name`, text, optional)**: Enter your name.
* **User Email (`user_email`, email, optional)**: Enter your email address.
* **User Phone (`user_phone`, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Notes (`notes`, text, optional)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your experience.
* **Assigned Tool (`assigned_tool`, text, optional)**: Enter any tools or equipment assigned to you during the trip.
* **Check Out User (`check_out_user`, text, optional)**: Enter the name of the person who checked you out.
* **Check Out User Name (`check_out_user_name`, text, optional)**: Enter the name of the person who checked you out.
* **Check Out Form Id (`check_out_form_id`, number, optional)**: Enter the check out form ID, if applicable.
* **Check Out Form Date (`check_out_form_date`, date, optional)**: Enter the check out form date.
* **Check Out Form Time (`check_out_form_time`, time, optional)**: Enter the check out form time.
* **Trail Status (`trail_status`, select_multiple, optional)**: Select the status of the trail, such as Active or Inactive.
* **Trail Status Note (`trail_status_note`, text, optional)**: Enter any notes about the trail status.
* **Trail Note (`trail_note`, text, optional)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the trail.
* **Comments (`comments`, text, optional)**: Enter any comments or feedback about your experience.

Note: This form is designed to be optional, so you can skip any fields that do not apply to your situation.
