# Guest Departure Notification - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Guest Departure Notification form is used to notify your hosts and other relevant staff members of your planned departure from their accommodation. Please use this form to provide accurate and detailed information to ensure a smooth handover and avoid any confusion during your check-out.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the unit number where you are currently residing.
2. Select a departure date.
3. Choose a departure time.
4. Provide any additional comments or notes, if needed.
5. If you have specific internal or external reporting requirements, please include them in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Unit Number** (`unit_number`, Number, Not Required): Enter the unit number of your accommodation where you will be staying during your visit.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, Date, Not Required): Select the date you plan to leave the accommodation.
* **Departure Time** (`departure_time`, Time, Not Required): Choose the time you plan to depart from the accommodation.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Not Required): Add any additional comments or notes that would be helpful for your host or staff to know about your departure.
* **Departure Time Comments** (`departure_time_comments`, Text, Not Required): Provide any specific comments about your departure, such as if you need assistance or special arrangements.
* **Internal Departure Reporting** (`internal_departure_reporting`, Text, Not Required): If your departure needs to be reported to internal stakeholders, please provide details.
* **External Report Required** (`external_report_required`, Text, Not Required): If external reporting is required for your departure, please specify the details.
