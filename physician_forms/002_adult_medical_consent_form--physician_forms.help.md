# Adult Medical Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is to obtain informed consent from an adult patient before undergoing any medical procedure or treatment. It ensures that the patient and their emergency contact understand the risks and benefits associated with the procedure, as well as any alternative treatments discussed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details in the required fields (Patient Full Name, Date of Birth, Address, Emergency Contact Name, and Emergency Contact Phone).
2. Provide a detailed description of the medical procedure you will undergo (Medical Procedure Description).
3. Indicate whether all potential risks have been explained to you (Risks Explained).
4. Confirm that you understand the potential benefits of the procedure (Benefits Understood).
5. Confirm that alternative treatments have been discussed with you (Alternative Treatments Discussed).
6. Give your consent to proceed with the procedure (Consent to Proceed).
7. Sign and date the form (Signature Date).
8. Enter the name of the physician performing the procedure (Physician Name).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter your legal full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format `month/day/year` (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Address** (Text, Required): Enter your current residential address where you can be contacted.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the person you would like to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Description of Medical Procedure** (Text, Required): Provide a detailed description of the medical procedure you will undergo, including any necessary steps or activities involved.
* **Risks Explained** (Select One, Required): Confirm that all potential risks associated with the procedure have been explained to you. Choose `Yes` if you have been informed.
* **Benefits Understood** (Select One, Required): Confirm that you understand the potential benefits of the procedure. Choose `Yes` if you have a clear understanding.
* **Alternative Treatments Discussed** (Select One, Required): Confirm that alternative treatments have been discussed with you. Choose `Yes` if you have been informed.
* **Consent to Proceed** (Select One, Required): Provide your consent to proceed with the described medical procedure. Choose `Yes` if you agree to proceed.
* **Signature Date** (Date, Required): Date and sign this form once you have read and understood all the information provided.
* **Physician Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the physician performing the medical procedure.
