# Symptom Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to screen patients for various medical conditions and gather relevant health information to support medical decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's ID, which can be found on their registration card or in their medical record.
2. Enter the patient's symptoms and medical history, such as their name, medical conditions, medications, and allergies.
3. Enter the patient's contact information, including their phone number and email address.
4. Enter any relevant medical tests or procedures that have been performed on the patient.
5. Enter any relevant medical history, such as surgeries or medical conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Id (1)**: Enter the patient's unique ID number.
* **Symptom Name (2)**: Enter the name of the patient's current symptom or condition.
* **Description (3)**: Enter a brief description of the patient's symptom or condition.
* **Symptom Category (4)**: Select the category of the patient's symptom or condition from the list provided.
* **Patient Age (5)**: Enter the patient's age.
* **Doctor Name (6)**: Enter the name of the doctor or medical professional who is treating the patient.
* **Doctor Phone (7)**: Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Id (8)**: Enter the doctor's unique ID number.
* **Doctor E-mail (9)**: Enter the doctor's email address.
* **Patient Phone (10)**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient E-mail (11)**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Medical History (12)**: Enter a brief description of the patient's medical history.
* **Treatment (13)**: Enter any relevant treatment information.
* **Patient Gender (14)**: Select the patient's gender from the list provided.
* **Patient Race (15)**: Select the patient's race from the list provided.
* **Patient Weight (16)**: Enter the patient's weight.
* **Blood Pressure (17)**: Enter the patient's blood pressure.
* **Blood Sugar (18)**: Enter the patient's blood sugar level.
* **Patient Allergies (19)**: Enter any allergies the patient has.
* **Patient Medications (20)**: Enter any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Patient Medical Conditions (21)**: Enter any medical conditions the patient has.
* **Patient Medical Tests (22)**: Enter any medical tests that have been performed on the patient.
* **Patient Surgeries (23)**: Enter any surgeries the patient has had.
* **Patient Medical History (24)**: Enter any relevant medical history.
