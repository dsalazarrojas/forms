# pediatric_patient_demographic_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and record essential demographic information from pediatric patients. This includes details such as name, date of birth, age, gender, weight, height, medical record number, contact number, address, emergency contact information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth using the date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
3. Enter the patient's age.
4. Select the patient's gender from the options provided.
5. Enter the patient's weight in kilograms.
6. Enter the patient's height in centimeters.
7. Enter the patient's medical record number (if applicable).
8. Enter the patient's contact number (if applicable).
9. Enter the patient's address.
10. Provide emergency contact information, including name, contact number, and relationship.
11. Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
12. Enter the doctor's name and specialty (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Name (1)
Enter the patient's full name, including first name and last name.

### Date of Birth (2)
Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Age (3)
Enter the patient's age.

### Gender (4)
Select the patient's gender from the options provided: Male, Female, Other.

### Weight (5)
Enter the patient's weight in kilograms.

### Height (6)
Enter the patient's height in centimeters.

### Medical Record Number (7)
Enter the patient's medical record number (if applicable).

### Contact Number (8)
Enter the patient's contact number (if applicable).

### Address (9)
Enter the patient's address.

### Emergency Contact (10)
Provide emergency contact information, including name and contact number.

### Emergency Contact Name (11)
Enter the name of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Number (12)
Enter the contact number of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Relationship (13)
Describe the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, guardian, friend).

### Patient Signature (14)
Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

### Doctor Name (15)
Enter the doctor's name.

### Doctor Specialty (16)
Enter the doctor's specialty (if applicable).

Tips:

* Ensure that the patient's information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Complete the form in a quiet and comfortable environment.
* Review the form carefully before signing and dating it.
* Make sure to keep the form secure and confidential.
