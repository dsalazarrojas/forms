# Doctor Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Doctor Information Form is designed to collect and update basic information about physicians.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the corresponding information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Title** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your title (e.g., Dr., Professor, etc.).
* **Address** (`address_1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your primary address.
* **Address 2** (`address_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional address information (e.g., suite number, apartment number, etc.).
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city where you reside or work.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state where you reside or work.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, required: false): Enter your zip code.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that your details are current in the database.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your administrator for guidance.
- Please note that this form is for physician use only, and only authorized personnel should have access to the database.
