# COVID 19 Vaccination Roster Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a COVID-19 vaccination roster request. The collected data will help the relevant authorities in organizing and fulfilling vaccination requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Requestor information section with your details.
2. Provide your organization's information in the Organization section.
3. Specify the purpose and number of individuals on the roster, as well as your preferred format for submitting the roster.
4. If applicable, indicate any additional needs or requirements.
5. Review the Logistics and Planning section if you have a specific venue for the vaccination sessions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Full Name**: Your name as the person or organization submitting the request.
* **Organization Name**: The name of the organization you represent.
* **Email Address**: Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number for easy communication.
* **Organization Type**: The type of organization you represent (e.g., School, Business, Healthcare facility, etc.).
* **Organization Street Address**: The street address of your organization.
* **City**: The city of your organization.
* **State**: The state where your organization is located.
* **Zip Code**: The zip code of your organization.
* **Purpose of Vaccination Roster**: The reason for requesting the vaccination roster (e.g., Employee vaccination, Student vaccination, etc.).
* **Estimated Number of Individuals on Roster**: The total number of people you plan to vaccinate.
* **Preferred Roster Format**: The format you would like the roster data to be provided in (e.g., Individual submissions, Batch upload, etc.).
* **Requested Submission Deadline**: The date by which you need the vaccination roster data submitted.
* **Priority Level**: The urgency level of your request (e.g., High - urgent, Medium - standard, Low - flexible timeline).
* **Vaccine Preference**: The type of vaccine you prefer for your roster (e.g., Pfizer, Moderna, etc.).
* **Priority Populations to Vaccinate**: If applicable, select the populations you would like prioritized for vaccination (e.g., Healthcare workers, Essential workers, etc.).
* **Accessibility or Special Accommodation Needs**: If you have any requirements for accessibility or special accommodations, please specify.
* **Languages Needed for Materials**: If you need materials in languages other than English, please specify.
* **Venue Available**: Indicate if you have a specific venue for the vaccination sessions.
* **Venue Address**: If you have a venue, provide its address.
* **Anticipated Number of Vaccination Sessions**: If applicable, estimate the number of sessions you will need.
* **Additional Notes or Requirements**: Any additional information or requirements you would like to share.
