# COVID 19 Patient Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Patient Screening Form is designed to gather information from patients about their symptoms and exposure history to help healthcare professionals identify potential cases and take necessary precautions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Enter your date of birth.
3. Answer the screening questions truthfully, selecting "True" or "False" for each symptom.
4. If you have traveled outside the country in the last 14 days, specify the location in the "Travel History" field.
5. Answer the "Have you been in close contact with someone who has a confirmed case of COVID-19?" question truthfully.

## Tips
* Answer truthfully to ensure accurate assessment of your health status.
* If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to reach out to a healthcare professional.
* This form is for patients who have visited the healthcare facility recently. If you are a healthcare worker or visitor, please refer to your organization's policy for screening procedures.
