# Urgent Care Work Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Urgent Care Work Release Form is used to document the release of a patient from an urgent care setting. It captures essential information about the patient's work status, reason for return, and other relevant details for patient follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the work release date.
2. Select the reason for return (if applicable).
3. Sign and date the form as the physician.
4. Enter the attending physician's name and title.
5. Enter the patient's name and date of birth.
6. Have the patient sign and date the form.
7. Enter the patient's address and phone number.
8. Select the patient's gender and race.
9. Enter the patient's height.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work Release Date**: This date indicates when the patient is cleared to return to work.
* **Reason for Return (if applicable)**: Select one or more reasons why the patient is being discharged.
* **Physician Signature**: The attending physician's signature confirms that the patient has been cleared to return to work.
* **Attending Physician Name**: The name and title of the attending physician.
* **Patient Name**: The name of the patient.
* **Patient Signature**: The patient's signature confirms they have been informed of their work status.
* **Patient Address**: The patient's home address.
* **Patient Phone**: The patient's phone number.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: The date of birth of the patient.
* **Patient Gender**: The patient's gender.
* **Patient Race**: The patient's racial category.
* **Patient Height**: The patient's height.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have obtained the patient's consent before completing this form.
- Review the patient's medical information carefully before filling out this form.
- Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
- Update the form if the patient's information changes.
