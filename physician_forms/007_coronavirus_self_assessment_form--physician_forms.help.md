# Coronavirus Self Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals who have recently traveled, been in contact with someone with coronavirus, or have symptoms related to the virus. This information will help the healthcare system to track and monitor the spread of the disease.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read the form carefully and provide accurate information about your general information, symptoms, travel history, contact history, medical conditions, medications, and test results.
2. Select all the options that apply to you.
3. Fill out the form completely and submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (page 1): This section is for providing general information about yourself, such as your name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Symptoms** (page 2): Select all the symptoms you have experienced in the past 7 days, such as cough, fever, chest pain, etc. Please be honest and accurate in your selection.
* **Travel History** (page 3): Select where you have traveled in the past 7 days, such as United States, Europe, Asia, etc.
* **Contact History** (page 4): Select your level of contact with someone who has the virus, such as close contact, casual contact, or no contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (page 5): If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, select all that apply.
* **Medications** (page 6): If you are taking any medications, select all that apply.
* **Previous Tests** (page 7): If you have previously been tested for coronavirus, select whether the test was positive, negative, or unknown.
* **Test Results** (page 8): Select whether you have any recent test results, such as positive, negative, or unknown.
* **Test Date** (page 9): Enter the date of your recent test, if applicable.
* **Test Time** (page 10): Enter the time of your recent test, if applicable.
* **Travel Last 7 Days** (page 11): Select whether you have traveled in the past 7 days.
* **Symptoms Last 7 Days** (page 12): Select all the symptoms you have experienced in the past 7 days.
* **Contact Last 7 Days** (page 13): Select your level of contact with someone who has the virus in the past 7 days.
* **Medical History** (page 14): If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, select all that apply.
* **Medications Last 7 Days** (page 15): If you have taken any medications in the past 7 days, select all that apply.
* **Previous Tests Last 7 Days** (page 16): If you have previously been tested for coronavirus in the past 7 days, select whether the test was positive, negative, or unknown.
* **Test Results Last 7 Days** (page 17): Select whether you have any recent test results, such as positive, negative, or unknown.
* **Test Date Last 7 Days** (page 18): Enter the date of your recent test, if applicable.
* **Test Time Last 7 Days** (page 19): Enter the time of your recent test, if applicable.
* **Contact Trace** (page 20): Select whether you are willing to be contacted by healthcare workers to trace your contacts.
* **Self-Isolation** (page 21): Select whether you are self-isolating due to the virus.
* **Travel Restrictions** (page 22): Select whether you have travel restrictions due to the virus.
* **Healthcare Worker** (page 23): Select whether you are a healthcare worker and have been exposed to the virus.
* **Test Results Awaiting** (page 24): Select whether you are awaiting test results.
* **Submit** (page 25): Complete this form by filling out all the fields and submitting it.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact a healthcare professional.
* Do not submit the form if you are experiencing an emergency or have severe symptoms.
* This form is for self-assessment purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for medical advice or diagnosis.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please contact a healthcare professional.
