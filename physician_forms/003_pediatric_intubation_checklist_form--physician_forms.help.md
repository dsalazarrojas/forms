# Pediatric Intubation Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Pediatric Intubation Checklist Form is used to document the process and status of pediatric intubation, ensuring accurate and thorough patient information. This form is essential for healthcare professionals to provide optimal patient care.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2.  Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3.  Record the patient's medical record number in the "Medical Record Number" field.
4.  Select the type of procedure performed in the "Procedure Type" field.
5.  List any anesthetics used during the procedure in the "Anesthetic Used" field.
6.  Record the patient's oxygen saturation level.
7.  Record the patient's blood pressure.
8.  Record the patient's heart rate.
9.  List any medications administered to the patient in the "Medications Administered" field.
10. List any allergies the patient has in the "Allergies" field.
11. Record the patient's breathing rate.
12. Describe the patient's respiratory rate quality.
13. Describe the patient's neurological status using the "Neurological Status Description" field.
14. List any neurological signs observed during the procedure.
15. Describe the patient's cardiac status using the "Cardiac Status" field.
16. Describe the patient's circulatory status using the "Circulatory Status" field.
17. List any other medical conditions in the "Other Medical Conditions" field.
18. Enter any comments or notes about the procedure in the "Comments" field.
19. Sign and date the form with your physician's signature and the current date.
20. Sign and time the form with your physician's signature and the current time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (1, text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Date of Birth** (2, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical Record Number** (3, number, required): Record the patient's medical record number.
* **Anesthetist Name** (4, text, optional): Enter the name of the anesthetist who performed the procedure, if applicable.
* **Procedure Type** (5, select_one, required): Select the type of procedure performed, such as Anesthesia or Surgery.
* **Anesthetic Used** (6, select_multiple, optional): Select any anesthetics used during the procedure.
* **O2 Saturation** (7, number, optional): Record the patient's oxygen saturation level.
* **Blood Pressure** (8, number, required): Record the patient's blood pressure.
* **Heart Rate** (9, number, required): Record the patient's heart rate.
* **Medications Administered** (10, text, required): List any medications administered to the patient.
* **Allergies** (11, select_multiple, optional): List any allergies the patient has.
* **Breathing Rate** (12, number, required): Record the patient's breathing rate.
* **Respiratory Rate Quality** (13, text, optional): Describe the patient's respiratory rate quality.
* **Neurological Status Description** (14, text, optional): Describe the patient's neurological status using this field.
* **Neurological Signs** (15, text, optional): List any neurological signs observed during the procedure.
* **Cardiac Status** (16, text, optional): Describe the patient's cardiac status.
* **Circulatory Status** (17, text, optional): Describe the patient's circulatory status.
* **Other Medical Conditions** (18, text, optional): List any other medical conditions.
* **Comments** (19, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes about the procedure.
* **Physician Signature** (20, text, required): Sign the form with your physician's signature.
* **Date Signed** (21, date, required): Date the form with the current date.
* **Time Signed** (22, time, required): Time the form with the current time.
* **Submit** (23, text, optional): This field is not necessary for completion, but may be used to submit the form for review or printing.
