# Medical Employment Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about an employee's medical employment, including their contact details, emergency contacts, and medical history. This information will be used to ensure the employee's well-being and safety in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form with the required information. If a field is marked as "optional", you can skip it if you don't have the information.
*   Make sure to answer all the questions truthfully and accurately to the best of your knowledge.
*   If you're unsure about any of the questions, please consult with your supervisor or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Information** (`employee_contact_information`, Text, Required): Enter the employee's contact information, including their name, address, and phone number.
*   **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_information`, Text, Optional): If you have emergency contact information for the employee, please enter it here. This can include their name, address, and phone number.
*   **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, Text, Optional): If the employee has medical insurance, please enter the name of their provider.
*   **Address** (`employee_address`, Number, Required): Enter the employee's address in the format "123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345".
*   **Phone** (`employee_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the employee's phone number.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, Text, Optional): Briefly describe the employee's medical history, including any conditions or allergies.
*   **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, Select One, Optional): Select any medical conditions the employee has from the list of options provided. If the condition is not listed, please select "Other" and enter a brief description.
*   **Date of Birth** (`employee_dob`, Date, Optional): Enter the employee's date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
*   **Medical Insurance Provider** (`medical_insurance_provider`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select any medical insurance providers the employee has from the list of options provided.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, Text, Optional): Describe your relationship to the emergency contact, such as "Spouse", "Parent", or "Child".
*   **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
