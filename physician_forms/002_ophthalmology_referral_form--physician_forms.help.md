# ophthalmology_referral_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The ophthalmology referral form is used to gather information about a patient's eye health and medical history, allowing healthcare providers to refer them to an ophthalmology specialist if necessary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information, including their name, date of birth, and contact details.
2. Provide medical history, including any previous diagnoses, treatments, or allergies.
3. Select the type of specialist you are referring the patient to (Ophthalmologist or Optometrist).
4. Add any additional information relevant to the patient's eye health.
5. Sign and date the form as the patient and provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, required): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous diagnoses, treatments, or allergies.
* **Referral Info** (`referral_info`, select_one, required): Select the type of specialist you are referring the patient to (Ophthalmologist or Optometrist).
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select_multiple, required): Select the contact methods that the patient can be reached at (Phone, Email, Fax, or Mail).
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required): Enter any additional information relevant to the patient's eye health.
* **Medical Specialties** (`medical_specialties`, text, required): Enter the patient's medical specialties or conditions.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, date, required): Sign and date the form as the patient.
* **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, date, required): Sign and date the form as the provider.
* **Referral Request** (`referral_request`, text, required): Enter a brief statement explaining the referral request.
* **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, text, required): Enter a brief statement explaining the referral reason.
* **Referral Comment** (`referral_comment`, text, required): Enter any additional comments about the referral.
* **Patient Relationship** (`patient_relationship`, text, required): Enter the patient's relationship to you (e.g., patient, family member, friend).
* **Patient Demographics** (`patient_demographics`, text, required): Enter the patient's demographic information (e.g., age, gender, ethnicity).
* **Insurance Info** (`insurance_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's insurance information.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, text, required): Enter any additional medical history information.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): Enter any allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, required): Enter the patient's medications and dosages.
* **Social History** (`social_history`, text, required): Enter the patient's social history.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, text, required): Enter the patient's family medical history.
* **Medical History 3** (`medical_history_3`, text, required): Enter any additional medical history information.
* **Chief Complaint** (`chief_complaint`, text, required): Enter the chief complaint or chief symptom.
* **Medical History 4** (`medical_history_4`, text, required): Enter any additional medical history information.
* **Medical History 5** (`medical_history_5`, text, required): Enter any additional medical history information.
* **Medical History 6** (`medical_history_6`, text, required): Enter any additional medical history information.
* **Medical History 7** (`medical_history_7`, text, required): Enter any additional medical history information.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure the patient receives the best possible care.
* Make sure to sign and date the form as both the patient and provider.
* If you are unsure about any information, please enter "unknown" or ask for clarification.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out.
