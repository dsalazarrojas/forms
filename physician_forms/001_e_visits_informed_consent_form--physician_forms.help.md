# E Visits Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients for electronic visits. It is intended to ensure that patients understand the risks and benefits of participating in these virtual consultations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the electronic visit in the "E Visits Informed Consent Form" field.
2. Enter a description of the visit in the "Description" field.
3. Select the category of the visit from the list in the "Category" field.
4. Enter any additional descriptions of the visit in the "Description 2", "Description 3", "Description 4", "Description 5", "Description 6", "Description 7", and "Description 8" fields.
5. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **E Visits Informed Consent Form**: This field is where you enter the title of the electronic visit.
* **Description**: This field is where you enter a description of the visit.
* **Category**: This field is where you select the category of the visit. You can choose from three options: "Physician Forms", "Other", or "Custom".
* **Description 2**, **Description 3**, **Description 4**, **Description 5**, **Description 6**, **Description 7**, **Description 8**: These fields are for providing additional descriptions of the visit, if necessary.

## Tips
* Be sure to enter accurate and complete information in all fields.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please contact the form administrators at form@gic.mx.
