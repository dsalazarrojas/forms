# Dry Needling Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a standard template used to obtain informed consent from patients before undergoing dry needling treatments. It helps ensure that patients are aware of the potential risks and benefits associated with the treatment and that they have given their consent to proceed.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please fill in the form carefully and accurately as you would in any other medical form.
*   Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
*   Review the form carefully before signing and dating it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name** (1): Enter the patient's full name as they would like to be referred to during the treatment.
    *   *Required field*
*   **Date** (2): Enter the date on which the form is being completed.
    *   *Required field*
*   **Physican** (3): Enter the name of the healthcare professional performing the dry needling treatment.
    *   *Required field*
*   **Treatment Location** (4): Enter the location where the treatment is being performed.
    *   *Required field*
*   **Treatment Description** (5): Briefly describe the dry needling treatment you will be receiving.
    *   *Required field*
*   **Understand** (6):
    *   Select all the ways in which the patient understands the information provided about the dry needling treatment.
    *   *Required field*
*   **Consent** (7):
    *   Select whether the patient understands the information provided about the dry needling treatment.
    *   *Required field*
*   **Signature** (8): Sign the form to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
    *   *Optional field (only required if necessary)*
*   **Patient ID** (9): Enter the patient's identification number or ID number as provided by your healthcare provider.
    *   *Required field*
*   **Physican ID** (10): Enter the healthcare provider's ID number as provided by their healthcare provider.
    *   *Required field*
*   **Date Completed** (11): Enter the date on which the form was completed.
    *   *Not required field (only for record-keeping purposes)*
