# Pediatric Patient Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Patient Registration Form is designed to collect essential information about pediatric patients, allowing healthcare providers to better understand their medical history, needs, and circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's demographic information (name, address, phone number, etc.) in the "Patient Details" page.
2. Provide family details, such as contact information for the patient's family members, in the "Family Details" page.
3. List the patient's allergies and medications in the "Allergies And Medications" page.
4. Record the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" page.
5. Report any developmental milestones in the "Developmental Milestones" page.
6. Provide insurance and social security information in the "Social Security" page.
7. List emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" page.
8. Record family history information in the "Family History" page.
9. Add any relevant medical background information in the "Medical Background" page.
10. Add any medical notes about the patient in the "Medical Notes" page.
11. Provide insurance information (payer, policy number, etc.) in the "Insurance Information" page.
12. Add a patient identifier (e.g., patient ID number) in the "Patient Identifier" page.
13. Obtain a digital signature from a parent or guardian in the "Signature" page.
14. Enter the date and time of the patient visit in the "Date" and "Time" page.
15. Add any notes from the physician in the "Physician Notes" page.
16. Enter the physician's signature in the "Physician Signature" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Details (id: 1)**
	+ Enter the patient's name, age, and other relevant demographic information.
* **Family Details (id: 2)**
	+ Enter contact information for the patient's family members, such as phone numbers and addresses.
* **Allergies And Medications (id: 3)**
	+ List any allergies or medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Medical History (id: 4)**
	+ Record any significant medical events or conditions affecting the patient.
* **Developmental Milestones (id: 5)**
	+ Report any developmental milestones or milestones in the patient's life.
* **Social Security (id: 6)**
	+ Enter any relevant social security information.
* **Emergency Contact (id: 7)**
	+ List contact information for emergency contacts.
* **Family History (id: 8)**
	+ Report any significant family medical history.
* **Medical Background (id: 9)**
	+ Add any relevant medical background information.
* **Medical Notes (id: 10)**
	+ Add any notes about the patient's medical status.
* **Insurance Information (id: 11)**
	+ Enter insurance information (payer, policy number, etc.).
* **Patient Identifier (id: 12)**
	+ Add a unique patient identifier.
* **Signature (id: 13)**
	+ Obtain a digital signature from a parent or guardian.
* **Date (id: 14)**
	+ Enter the date of the patient visit.
* **Time (id: 15)**
	+ Enter the time of the patient visit.
* **Physician Signature (id: 16)**
	+ Add a digital signature from the physician.

	+ Add any notes from the physician.

* **Policy Number (id: 19)**

* **Patient Phone (id: 20)**

# Tips
