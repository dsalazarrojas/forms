# Covid 19 Antigen Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid 19 Antigen Intake Form is a medical form designed for physicians and healthcare providers to collect information about a patient's COVID-19 antigen intake. This form helps in gathering essential details about the patient's medical history, treatment, and personal contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have reviewed and understood the requirements and guidelines for COVID-19 testing and treatment.
2. Enter your physician's name in the "Physician Name" field.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.
4. Enter the patient's contact number and email in the "Contact Number" and "Contact Email" fields respectively.
5. Select the antigen type from the dropdown menu in the "Antigen Type" field.
6. Enter the number of doses received by the patient in the "Number Of Doses" field.
7. Enter the date of dosage in the "Dosage Date" field.
8. Provide any adverse reactions experienced by the patient in the "Adverse Reactions" field.
9. Enter the follow-up date in the "Follow-Up Date" field.
10. Enter any comments or observations from the physician in the "Physician Comments" field.
11. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
12. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Patient Date Of Birth" field.
13. Enter the patient's contact number and email in the "Patient Contact Number" and "Patient Email" fields respectively.
14. Enter the patient's address in the "Patient Address" field.
15. Enter the patient's city in the "Patient City" field.
16. Enter the patient's state in the "Patient State" field.
17. Enter the patient's zip code in the "Patient Zip" field.
18. Provide any additional notes about the patient in the "Patient Notes" field.
19. Select your signature from the dropdown menu in the "Physician Signature" field.
20. Select the patient's signature from the dropdown menu in the "Patient Signature" field.
21. Select the physician who completed this form from the dropdown menu in the "Form Completed By" field.
22. Enter the date and time the form was completed in the "Date Completed" and "Time Completed" fields respectively.
23. Select the physician who completed this form from the dropdown menu in the "Completed By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Physician Name**: Enter your name as a physician.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Antigen Type**: Select the type of antigen the patient received (e.g. AstraZeneca, Johnson & Johnson, Pfizer, Moderna).
* **Number Of Doses**: Enter the number of doses received by the patient.
* **Dosage Date**: Enter the date of dosage.
* **Adverse Reactions**: Provide any adverse reactions experienced by the patient.
* **Follow-Up Date**: Enter the follow-up date.
* **Physician Comments**: Enter any comments or observations from the physician.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name.
* **Patient Date Of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient Contact Number**: Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Patient Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address.
* **Patient City**: Enter the patient's city.
* **Patient State**: Enter the patient's state.
* **Patient Zip**: Enter the patient's zip code.
* **Patient Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the patient.
* **Physician Signature**: Select your signature from the dropdown menu.
* **Patient Signature**: Select the patient's signature from the dropdown menu.
* **Form Completed By**: Select the physician who completed this form.
* **Date Completed**: Enter the date this form was completed.
* **Time Completed**: Enter the time this form was completed.
* **Completed By**: Select the physician who completed this form.

## Tips

* Ensure you have reviewed and understood the requirements and guidelines for COVID-19 testing and treatment.
* Verify the patient's information before entering it in the form.
* Use the correct antigen type and dosage information for the patient.
* Select the correct physician and patient signatures.
* Enter accurate dates and times for form completion.
* Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting.
