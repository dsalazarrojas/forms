# Flu Vaccine Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients about their medical history, allergies, and previous vaccinations in order to assess their risk factors and determine eligibility for the flu vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all the questions as accurately as possible.
2. For the "Medical History" section, provide a brief description of any past medical conditions or illnesses you have had.
3. In the "Allergies" section, select any allergies you have from the list provided.
4. In the "Medical Device Use" section, list any medical devices you have used in the past.
5. For the "Previous Vaccination" section, enter the date of your last flu vaccination.
6. Select the type of flu vaccine you received in the previous vaccination (if applicable).
7. For the "Flu Vaccine Type" section, select the type of flu vaccine you are interested in receiving this year.
8. Choose the doses of the flu vaccine you have received or are interested in receiving.
9. If you have had any reactions to a flu vaccine in the past, describe them in the "Flu Vaccine Reaction" section.
10. Finally, enter any additional next steps or concerns you have in the "Next Steps" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History**: A brief description of any past medical conditions or illnesses you have had.
* **Medical Condition**: A brief description of your current medical condition.
* **Allergies**: A list of any allergies you have, including options like "option 1", "option 2", "option 3".
* **Medical Device Use**: A list of any medical devices you have used in the past, e.g. pacemakers, dialysis machines.
* **Previous Vaccination**: The date of your last flu vaccination, if you have received one.
* **Flu Vaccine Type**: The type of flu vaccine you are interested in receiving this year, e.g. "flu vaccine type 1", "flu vaccine type 2", "flu vaccine type 3".
* **Flu Vaccine Dose**: The doses of the flu vaccine you have received or are interested in receiving, e.g. "flu vaccine dose 1", "flu vaccine dose 2", "flu vaccine dose 3".
* **Flu Vaccine Reaction**: A description of any previous reactions you have had to a flu vaccine, including any symptoms or concerns.
* **Next Steps**: Any additional next steps or concerns you have, including any follow-up questions or instructions for your healthcare provider.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and truthful as possible when completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any questions, ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form should not be used to gather information for multiple appointments, each submission is a separate instance of the form and should reflect your current medical status.
