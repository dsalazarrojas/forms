# COVID 19 Positive Diagnosis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record a patient's COVID-19 positive diagnosis. It captures essential information for patient care, contact tracing, and infection control. Ensure this form is filled out accurately and completely for each patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's full name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Record the date the positive test was performed in the **Date of Test** field.
3. Select the type of test used to diagnose the patient (PCR, antigen, or antibody) in the **Test Type** field.
4. List any symptoms the patient experienced when tested positive in the **Symptoms at Diagnosis** field. You may select multiple symptoms.
5. Indicate if hospitalization was required in the **Hospitalization Required** field.
6. If applicable, enter the primary clinician's name in the **Treating Physician Name** field.
7. Enter the date when the patient began isolation in the **Isolation Start Date** field.
8. Enter the date when the patient's isolation is expected to end in the **Isolation End Date** field.
9. Indicate if the patient's employer has been notified in the **Workplace Notified** field.
10. Provide any additional notes or comments in the **Additional Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Test**: Record the date the positive test was performed.
* **Test Type**: Select the type of test used to diagnose the patient (PCR, antigen, or antibody).
* **Symptoms at Diagnosis**: List any symptoms the patient experienced when tested positive. Select multiple symptoms as needed.
* **Hospitalization Required**: Indicate if hospitalization was required. Yes if hospitalized, no otherwise.
* **Treating Physician Name**: Enter the primary clinician's name if relevant. This field is optional.
* **Isolation Start Date**: Enter the date when the patient began isolation.
* **Isolation End Date**: Enter the date when the patient's isolation is expected to end.
* **Workplace Notified**: Indicate if the patient's employer has been notified.
* **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes for clinical or administrative purposes.
