# Hormone Therapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to inform patients about hormone therapy, its risks and benefits, and to obtain their informed consent before starting the treatment. This form ensures that you understand the therapy's goals, treatment plan, and any necessary medical precautions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Patient Name** (Field 1).
2.  Enter your **Date Of Birth** (Field 2), which is essential for your medical history.
3.  Provide information about your **Date Of Last Medical Visit** (Field 3), including the date and any relevant medical notes.
4.  Share your **Medical History** (Field 4), including any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies.
5.  List your current **Medications** (Field 5), including any prescribed medications, vitamins, or supplements.
6.  Inform us about any **Allergies** (Field 6) you may have.
7.  Provide the names and contact information of your **Medical Providers** (Field 7).
8.  Read and sign the **Informed Consent** (Field 8) carefully, ensuring you understand the hormone therapy's goals and potential risks.
9.  Specify your **Hormone Therapy Goals** (Field 9) to help us create a tailored treatment plan.
10. Describe your **Treatment Plan** (Field 10), including any specific treatment details and expectations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Date Of Birth** (`Date Of Birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Date Of Last Medical Visit** (`Date Of Last Medical Visit`, date, required): Provide the date of your last medical visit to ensure we have accurate medical history.
*   **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, required): Share any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies that may impact your hormone therapy.
*   **Medications** (`Medications`, text, required): List all prescribed medications, vitamins, or supplements you're currently taking.
*   **Allergies** (`Allergies`, text, required): Inform us about any known allergies, including medications or substances.
*   **Medical Providers** (`Medical Providers`, text, required): Enter the names and contact information of your current healthcare providers.
*   **Informed Consent** (`Informed Consent`, text, required): Review and sign this section carefully, acknowledging you understand the hormone therapy's goals, risks, and benefits.
*   **Hormone Therapy Goals** (`Hormone Therapy Goals`, text, required): Specify your personal goals for hormone therapy, including expected outcomes.
*   **Treatment Plan** (`Treatment Plan`, text, required): Describe your treatment plan, including any specific details or expectations.

## Tips
*   Please ensure accuracy and completeness in filling out the form to guarantee the best possible outcome from your hormone therapy.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
*   Review each field carefully before submitting the form to avoid missing important information.
