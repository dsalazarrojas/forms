# Medical Necessity Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Necessity Form is designed to gather information on medical necessity from patients or healthcare providers. This form will help healthcare professionals assess whether a medical treatment or procedure is medically necessary, taking into account the patient's health status, symptoms, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select whether the medical treatment or procedure is medically necessary (Yes/No) in the "Service Type" field.
2. If you select "No", you can provide additional information in the "Other Service Type" field.
3. Enter the date and time of the service or treatment in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
4. Enter the doctor's name who performed or will perform the service or treatment in the "Doctor Name" field.
5. Provide a brief explanation of the medical necessity reasons in the "Reasons" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Service Type
 * Service Type (Yes/No, required: false): Select "Yes" if the medical treatment or procedure is medically necessary, or "No" if it's not. If you select "No", please provide additional information in the "Other Service Type" field.

### Other Service Type
 * Other Service Type (text, required: false): This field is only visible if you selected "No" in the "Service Type" field. Please enter any additional information that explains why the medical treatment or procedure is not medically necessary.

### Date
 * Date (date, required: false): Enter the date of the service or treatment.

### Time
 * Time (time, required: false): Enter the time of the service or treatment.

### Doctor Name
 * Doctor Name (text, required: false): Enter the name of the doctor who performed or will perform the service or treatment.

### Reasons
 * Reasons (text, required: false): Provide a brief explanation of the medical necessity reasons for the service or treatment.

Tips: Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance from the healthcare team.
