# Patient Health Questionnaire PHQ9 - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Health Questionnaire PHQ9 is a comprehensive form designed to collect information about a patient's health status, medical history, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the guidelines below:

1. Read each section carefully and provide accurate information about your health status and medical history.
2. Fill out each section according to the guidelines and prompts provided.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Physical Health** (`physical_health`, `text`, required: false): Please describe your current physical health status, including any physical limitations or conditions you are experiencing.
* **Mental Health** (`mental_health`, `text`, required: false): Describe your current mental health status, including any mental health concerns or conditions you are experiencing.
* **General Health** (`general_health`, `text`, required: false): Summarize your overall health status, including any significant health concerns or conditions.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all medical conditions or health concerns you have experienced in the past, including but not limited to:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide any relevant patient information, such as medical allergies or sensitivities.
* **Social History** (`social_history`, `text`, required: false): Share any social factors that may impact your health, including relationships, living situation, or other relevant details.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, `text`, required: false): Describe your family medical history, including any significant health concerns or conditions affecting family members.
* **Medication** (`medication`, `text`, required: false): List all current medications you are taking, including dosage and frequency.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required: false): Report any known allergies or sensitivities.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required: false): List any current or past medical conditions, including but not limited to:
	+ Diabetes
	+ Hypertension
	+ Cancer
* **Patient Support** (`patient_support`, `text`, required: false): Provide any relevant information about your patient support system, including family, friends, or support groups.
* **Physician Notes** (`physician_notes`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional notes or concerns you have about your health or medical conditions.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `note`, required: false): Please sign and date this form to confirm your completion and understanding of its contents.
* **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, `note`, required: false): Please sign and date this form to confirm your review and recommendation. 

Note: Please sign and date both "Patient Signature" and "Physician Signature" sections after consulting with your healthcare provider.
