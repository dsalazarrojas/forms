# Doctor Visit Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doctor Visit Form Template is designed for healthcare professionals to collect and document patient information, medical history, and vital signs during a visit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering the patient's information, including any relevant medical history.
2. Record the patient's allergies and any medications they are currently taking.
3. Enter the patient's vital signs, such as blood pressure and temperature.
4. Describe the patient's chief complaint or reason for the visit.
5. Document any medical records or previous diagnoses.
6. Develop a treatment plan for the patient.
7. Sign the form to confirm your signature as the patient.
8. Sign the form again to confirm your signature as the doctor or healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Record any relevant medical history, including previous illnesses or conditions.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check if the patient has any allergies, with options "Yes" or "No".
*   **Medication** (`medication`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Record if the patient is currently taking any medications, with options "Yes" or "No".
*   **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's vital signs, such as blood pressure, temperature, and pulse.
*   **Chief Complaint** (`chief_complaint`, `text`, required: false): Describe the patient's chief complaint or reason for the visit.
*   **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, `text`, required: false): Document any previous medical records or diagnoses.
*   **Medical Diagnosis** (`medical_diagnosis`, `text`, required: false): Record any medical diagnosis or diagnosis made during the visit.
*   **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, `text`, required: false): Develop a treatment plan for the patient.
*   **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `select_one`, required: false): The patient signs to confirm they have read and understood the form, with options "Yes" or "No".
*   **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, `select_one`, required: false): The healthcare provider signs to confirm they have reviewed and completed the form, with options "Yes" or "No".
*   **Medical Note** (`medical_note`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional medical notes or comments.
 
## Tips
*   This form is intended for use by healthcare professionals, not for self-reporting or general information gathering.
*   Ensure that the form is completed in a way that is accurate and respectful of patient confidentiality.
*   Review and update the form as necessary to ensure it is current and complete.
