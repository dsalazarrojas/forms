# Ocular Surface Disease Index (OSDI) Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ocular Surface Disease Index (OSDI) Form is designed to collect information about a patient's ocular surface disease. This information is crucial for healthcare professionals to assess and diagnose ocular surface disease accurately.
## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out the form, please read through each section carefully to ensure you're providing the most accurate information. You can fill out this form as a healthcare professional or as a patient yourself, depending on your context. If you're unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare professional for guidance.

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Patient ID**: Enter the patient's ID number or other unique identifier.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
