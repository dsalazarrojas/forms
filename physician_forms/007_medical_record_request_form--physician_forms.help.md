# Medical Record Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Record Request Form is used to request access to a patient's medical record for review or reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the requested information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient whose medical record you are requesting.
* **Physician Name**: Enter the name of the physician who ordered the medical record.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility where the patient received treatment.
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter the unique identifier number for the patient's medical record.
* **Date of Service**: Enter the date on which the patient received treatment.

## Tips
- Double-check the fields to ensure you are requesting a medical record for a valid reason.
- Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are filled out.
- In case of any errors or unclear fields, contact the administrator for assistance.
