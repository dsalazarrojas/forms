# sculptra_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sculptra_consent_form is a medical consent form that collects necessary information from patients and clinicians for treatment and consent purposes. It is used to ensure that patients understand and agree to their treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the patient information page, including details such as name, contact, and other relevant fields.
* Enter the consent date and time if required.
* Provide a brief description of the treatment details in the text field.
* Sign and date the patient signature field to indicate that you have read and agreed to the treatment plan.
* Enter the clinician's name, signature, and contact information.
* Complete the clinic name and contact details if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`Patient Information`, `note`, required: false): Enter your name, contact details, and other relevant information.
* **Consent Date** (`Consent Date`, `date`, required: false): If applicable, enter the date and time of the consent.
* **Treatment Details** (`Treatment Details`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe the treatment you are undergoing.
* **Patient Signature** (`Patient Signature`, `text`, required: false): Sign and date to indicate that you have read and agreed to the treatment plan.
* **Clinician Name** (`Clinician Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the clinician providing your treatment.
* **Clinician Signature** (`Clinician Signature`, `text`, required: false): Enter the clinician's signature to confirm their involvement.
* **Clinic Name** (`Clinician Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the clinic where treatment is taking place.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any necessary contact details for the clinic or clinician.
* **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person for the clinic.
