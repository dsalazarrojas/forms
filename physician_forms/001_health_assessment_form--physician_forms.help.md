# health_assessment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect medical history information from patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Fill out each field thoroughly, but only if the field is required.
3. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please consult with your healthcare provider for clarification.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
