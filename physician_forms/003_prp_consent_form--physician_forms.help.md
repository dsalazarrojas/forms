# PRP Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PRP Consent Form is a document used to collect information and obtain consent from patients before undergoing a procedure or treatment. It is an essential tool for healthcare professionals to ensure that patients are aware of their rights and responsibilities during medical treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Patient Signature**: Date and sign the form to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided. This is an optional field but recommended for legal and medical record-keeping purposes.
2.  **Physician Signature and Date**: Date and sign the form to confirm that you, as a healthcare provider, have informed the patient about the procedure or treatment and have obtained their consent.

### Physician Date (`physician_date`)
*   *Label:*   **Physician Date**
*   *Type:*   *Date* 
*   *Required:*   Optional
*   *Explanation:*   The date on which you, as a healthcare provider, informed the patient about the procedure or treatment and obtained their consent.

## Tips
*   Always read and understand the information provided before signing the form.
*   Ensure that you, as a healthcare provider, have informed the patient about the procedure or treatment before obtaining their consent.
*   Keep a record of the form for future reference and medical record-keeping purposes.
