# National Urology Unit Service Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The National Urology Unit Service Audit Form is a tool used to evaluate the quality of services provided by healthcare providers in the urology unit. This form helps healthcare professionals track and analyze services, identify areas for improvement, and optimize patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a service audit form provider from the options provided (First Page, Service Audit Form Provider Details, Service Audit Form Note, and National Urology Unit Service Audit Form Note).
2. Enter the patient's identifier (Patient Identifier field).
3. Select the date of the service audit (Service Audit Date field).
4. Enter the time of day for the service audit (Time of Day field).
5. Select the date of the patient's visit (Date of Visit field).
6. Select the time of the patient's visit (Time of Visit field).
7. Enter any additional notes or comments regarding the service audit (Service Audit Form Note and National Urology Unit Service Audit Form Note fields).
8. Enter the healthcare provider's email address (Email field).
9. Enter the healthcare provider's phone number (Phone field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Page (National Urology Unit Service Audit Form 1)
* **First Page** (`national_urology_unit_service_audit_form_1`, Text, Optional): This field is the first page of the form where you will select a service audit form provider from the options provided.

### Service Audit Form Provider Details (service_audit_form_provider_details, Select One, Required)
* **Service Audit Form Provider Details** (`service_audit_form_provider_details`, Select One, Required): Select one of the provided options for the service audit form provider.

### Healthcare Service Provider (healthcare_service_provider, Select Multiple, Optional)
* **Healthcare Service Provider** (`healthcare_service_provider`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select multiple healthcare service providers from the options provided.

### Patient Identifier (patient_identifier, Text, Required)
* **Patient Identifier** (`patient_identifier`, Text, Required): Enter the patient's identifier, typically their patient ID or patient number.

### Service Audit Date (service_audit_date, Date, Optional)
* **Service Audit Date** (`service_audit_date`, Date, Optional): Select the date of the service audit.

### Time of Day (time_of_day, Time, Optional)
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, Time, Optional): Select the time of day for the service audit.

### Date of Visit (date_of_visit, Date, Optional)
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, Date, Optional): Select the date of the patient's visit.

### Time of Visit (time_of_visit, Time, Optional)
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, Time, Optional): Select the time of the visit.

### Service Audit Form Note (service_audit_form_note, Note, Optional)
* **Service Audit Form Note** (`service_audit_form_note`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments regarding the service audit.

### National Urology Unit Service Audit Form Note (national_urology_unit_service_audit_form_note, Note, Optional)
* **National Urology Unit Service Audit Form Note** (`national_urology_unit_service_audit_form_note`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments regarding the national urology unit service audit.

### Email (email, Email, Optional)
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter the healthcare provider's email address.

### Phone (phone, Text, Optional)
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the healthcare provider's phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct service audit form provider and healthcare service provider for accurate results.
* Enter accurate and complete information for the patient identifier, service audit date, time of day, date of visit, time of visit, and any additional notes or comments.
* Ensure that the healthcare provider's email address and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
* Review and edit the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the urology unit administration or IT department for assistance.
