# COVID 19 Test Result Reporting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is for reporting COVID-19 test results. It is intended to gather information about a patient's test result and any relevant medical history or circumstances surrounding the test. The form will be used to provide healthcare providers with critical information for further diagnosis or treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

This form is designed to be filled out by healthcare providers for reporting COVID-19 test results. To complete the form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the patient's information, including name, ID or medical record number, date of birth, and contact information.
2. Select the type of COVID-19 test performed.
3. Enter the date of the test and the date the result was received.
4. Select the outcome of the test (Positive, Negative, Inconclusive, or Pending).
5. Enter any relevant medical history or circumstances, such as symptoms, vaccination status, previous infection, or known exposure to COVID-19.
6. Confirm whether quarantine recommendations were provided to the patient.
7. Enter the name and contact information of the reporting healthcare provider.
8. Add any additional clinical notes or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Enter the patient's full legal name.
* **Patient ID or Medical Record Number** (`patient_id`): Enter the patient's ID or medical record number for reference.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter the patient's email address for result notifications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter the patient's phone number for follow-up contact.
* **Test Type** (`test_type`): Select the type of COVID-19 test performed.
* **Date of Test** (`test_date`): Enter the date the test was performed.
* **Test Result** (`result`): Select the outcome of the test (Positive, Negative, Inconclusive, or Pending).
* **Date Result Received** (`result_date`): Enter the date the result was received.
* **Testing Facility Name** (`facility_name`): Enter the name of the laboratory or clinic performing the test.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`): Enter the address of the testing location (optional).
* **Patient Had Symptoms** (`symptoms`): Select whether the patient was experiencing COVID-19 symptoms at the time of the test.
* **Symptoms Experienced** (`symptoms_list`): Check all symptoms the patient was experiencing.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`): Select the patient's vaccination status (Unvaccinated, Partially Vaccinated, Fully Vaccinated, or Boosted).
* **Previous COVID-19 Infection** (`previous_infection`): Select whether the patient had a previous COVID-19 infection.
* **Known Exposure to COVID-19** (`exposure_contact`): Select whether the patient had a known exposure to COVID-19 within 14 days of the test.
* **Hospitalization Required** (`hospitalization_required`): Select whether the patient required hospitalization due to COVID-19.
* **Quarantine Recommendations Provided** (`quarantine_recommendations`): Select whether quarantine recommendations were provided to the patient.
* **Reporting Physician Name** (`physician_name`): Enter the name of the healthcare provider reporting the result.
* **Physician Contact Information** (`physician_contact`): Enter the contact information of the healthcare provider.
* **Additional Clinical Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional clinical notes or information.

Note: All fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled out. Fields marked as "optional" can be filled out voluntarily.
