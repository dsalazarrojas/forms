# Vaccine Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vaccine Consent Form is designed to gather important information from patients and their parents or guardians about their health, medical history, and allergies. This information will help healthcare providers make informed decisions about treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

*   First, please provide your name as the patient.
*   If you are under 18, your parent or guardian must provide their consent.
*   Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   If you have any known medical conditions, please describe them in detail.
*   If you have any allergies, please list them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
*   **Date Of Birth** (`Date Of Birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
*   **Parent Consent** (`Parent/Guardian Consent`, select, optional): If you are under 18, please select "Parent/Guardian Consent." If you are 18 or older, select "Not a parent."
*   **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`, text, optional): Please describe any medical condition that may affect your treatment.
*   **Allergy** (`Allergy`, text, optional): List any allergies you have.

## Tips

*   Please provide accurate and honest information to ensure safe and effective treatment.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
