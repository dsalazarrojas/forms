# Consent To Point Of Care Testing POCT For Covid 19 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from patients for Point of Care (POC) testing for COVID-19. This includes gathering information about the patient's symptoms, medical history, and testing preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form in its entirety, including the patient's demographic information (page 1).
- Select the correct answers to each question, taking into account the patient's symptoms and medical history.
- Sign the form at the end, indicating that you have read and understood the information presented.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Patient Information
* **Consent Date**: Date of consent (required)
  * Date of when the patient provided consent for testing
* **Patient Name**: Full name of patient (required)
  * The patient's full name, as recorded in their medical record
* **Patient Date of Birth**: Patient date of birth (required)
  * The patient's date of birth, as recorded in their medical record
* **Patient ID Number**: Medical record number (required)
  * The patient's medical record number
* **Address**: Mailing address (required)
  * The patient's mailing address
* **Phone Number**: Contact phone number (required)
  * The patient's contact phone number
* **Email Address**: Contact email address (not required)
  * The patient's contact email address (optional)
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Name of emergency contact (required)
  * The name of the emergency contact person
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Emergency contact phone number (required)
  * The emergency contact person's phone number

### Page 2: Symptom and Exposure Information
* **Are You Experiencing Symptoms**: Do you have COVID-19 symptoms (required)
  * Yes or No answer, indicating if the patient is experiencing symptoms
* **List Your Symptoms**: Select all that apply (not required)
  * A list of possible COVID-19 symptoms to choose from, indicating if the patient is experiencing any of them
* **Symptom Onset Date**: When did symptoms start (not required)
  * The date when the patient first experienced symptoms
* **Known Exposure to COVID-19**: Have you been exposed to someone with COVID-19 (required)
  * Yes or No answer, indicating if the patient has been exposed to someone with COVID-19
* **Exposure Date**: When did exposure occur (not required)
  * The date when the patient was exposed to someone with COVID-19
* **Recent Travel History**: Have you traveled recently (required)
  * Yes or No answer, indicating if the patient has traveled recently
* **Travel Locations**: List places you traveled (not required)
  * A list of places the patient has traveled to recently
* **Type of Test**: Which test will be performed (required)
  * The type of test the patient will undergo (Rapid Antigen, Rapid Molecular, PCR, or Antibody)
* **Test Location**: Where will test be performed (required)
  * The location where the test will be performed
* **Test Provider Name**: Name of healthcare provider (required)
  * The name of the healthcare provider performing the test
* **Purpose of Testing**: Why are you being tested (required)
  * The reason for testing (Diagnostic - Symptoms Present, Screening - No Symptoms, Contact Tracing, Pre-Procedure, Travel Requirement, Work Requirement, or Other)

### Page 3: Testing Preferences and Risks
* **How Should Results Be Delivered**: Preferred method for receiving results (required)
  * The preferred method for receiving test results (Phone Call, Email, Text Message, Patient Portal, Mail, or Pick Up In Person)
* **Results Timeline Understood**: Do you understand when to expect results (required)
  * Yes or No answer, indicating if the patient understands when to expect results
* **Test Risks Understood**: Do you understand any risks or discomfort (required)
  * Yes or No answer, indicating if the patient understands the risks or discomfort associated with testing
* **Isolation Guidelines Understood**: Do you understand isolation requirements (required)
  * Yes or No answer, indicating if the patient understands the isolation requirements
* **Consent to Contact Tracing**: Do you consent to contact tracing if positive (required)
  * Yes or No answer, indicating if the patient consents to contact tracing
* **Consent to Share Results with Health Department**: Do you consent to reporting results (required)
  * Yes or No answer, indicating if the patient consents to reporting results
* **Financial Responsibility Understood**: Do you understand you may be responsible for cost (required)
  * Yes or No answer, indicating if the patient understands the financial responsibility
* **Consent Decision**: Do you consent to testing (required)
  * Yes or No answer, indicating if the patient consents to testing
* **Patient Signature**: Type your full name as signature (required)
  * The patient's full name as their signature
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Required if patient is minor (not required)
  * The signature of the parent or guardian if the patient is a minor
* **Provider Signature**: Name of healthcare provider (required)
  * The name of the healthcare provider performing the test
