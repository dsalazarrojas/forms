# Patient Information Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Patient Information Update Form" is used to update a patient's information in our system. This form helps to gather accurate and up-to-date details about the patient, including their name, contact information, medical condition, and emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the first name of the patient.
2. Fill in the last name of the patient.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth.
4. Enter the patient's email address (if applicable).
5. Enter the patient's phone number.
6. Describe the patient's medical condition.
7. Describe the patient's medical history.
8. List any medical allergies the patient has.
9. Enter the emergency contact's name.
10. Specify the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact.
11. Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
12. Enter the emergency contact's email address (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required: false): Enter the patient's first name as it appears on their identification or official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required: false): Enter the patient's last name as it appears on their identification or official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required: false): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter the patient's email address (if they have one).
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Medical Condition** (text, required: false): Describe the patient's medical condition or any chronic illnesses they may have.
* **Medical History** (text, required: false): Describe any past medical conditions or surgeries the patient has undergone.
* **Medical Allergies** (text, required: false): List any allergies the patient has, including medications or substances they are allergic to.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (text, required: false): Specify the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact (e.g., spouse, parent, child).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (email, required: false): Enter the email address of the emergency contact (if they have one).
