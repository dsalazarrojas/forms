# diabetes_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to monitor and record patient health data for diabetes management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

- Fill in the fields as required or as instructed by your healthcare professional.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
- Review the form carefully to ensure that you've entered all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Blood Sugar Level** (`blood_sugar_level`, Number): Record the current blood sugar level reading.
* **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`, Number): Record the current blood pressure reading.
* **Weight in kg** (`weight_in_kg`, Number): Record your current weight in kilograms.
* **Medication Notes** (`medication_notes`, Text): Record any notes or concerns regarding your current medications.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, Text): Record any dietary restrictions or allergies you have.
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, Text): Record your current exercise frequency.
* **Doctor Notes** (`doctor_notes`, Text): Record any comments or instructions from your doctor.
* **Next Appointment Date** (`next_appointment_date`, Date): Record your next appointment date with your doctor.
* **Medication Side Effects** (`medication_side_effects`, Text): Record any side effects you've experienced from your medications.
* **Medication Dosage** (`medication_dosage`, Text): Record the dosage of your current medications.
* **Medication Administered** (`medication_administered`, Date): Record the date and time your medications were last administered.
* **Doctor Comments** (`doctor_comments`, Text): Record any comments or instructions from your doctor.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, Text): Record your full name.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, Text): Record your phone number.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, Text): Record your doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, Email): Record your doctor's email address.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, Text): Record your doctor's name.
* **Doctor Title** (`doctor_title`, Text): Record your doctor's title or designation.
* **Medical Group** (`medical_group`, Text): Record the medical group or clinic you visit.
* **Facility** (`facility`, Text): Record the facility where you receive treatment.
* **Facility Phone** (`facility_phone`, Text): Record the facility's phone number.
* **Facility Fax** (`facility_fax`, Text): Record the facility's fax number.
* **Facility Email** (`facility_email`, Email): Record the facility's email address.
* **Lab Results** (`lab_results`, Text): Record any recent lab results or test results.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Text): Record any additional notes or comments.

**Tips**

- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your healthcare professional.
- Keep this form up to date with your current health information.
