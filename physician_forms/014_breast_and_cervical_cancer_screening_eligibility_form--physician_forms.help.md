# Breast And Cervical Cancer Screening Eligibility Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to determine the eligibility of patients for breast and cervical cancer screenings. It collects information on patient's medical history, risk factors, and other relevant details to guide healthcare providers in their clinical decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete this form for each patient before scheduling their screening appointment.
2. Ensure that you have the patient's full medical history and current medical status before submitting this form.
3. Review the form carefully and enter the correct information for each field.
4. If you are unsure about any field, please use your clinical judgment and consult with your colleagues or relevant medical resources.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, Text, Required): Enter the patient's full legal name.
* **Patient ID Number** (`Patient ID Number`, Text, Optional): Enter the patient's medical record or patient ID number if available.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, Date, Required): Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Patient Age** (`Patient Age`, Number, Required): Enter the patient's current age in years.
* **Scheduled Screening Date** (`Scheduled Screening Date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of the screening appointment.
* **Type of Screening** (`Type of Screening`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the type of screening the patient is eligible for (Breast cancer screening, Cervical cancer screening, or Both screenings).
* **Relevant Medical History** (`Relevant Medical History`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select any conditions that apply to the patient (Previous breast cancer, Previous cervical cancer, Previous abnormal pap smear, Hysterectomy, DCIS-Ductal carcinoma in situ, Hormonal therapy, Radiation therapy, or None).
* **Family Cancer History** (`Family Cancer History`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select any cancer history in the patient's family (Breast cancer in family, Cervical cancer in family, Ovarian cancer in family, Other cancer in family, or No known family history).
* **Previous Genetic Testing** (`Previous Genetic Testing`, Select One, Optional): Select if the patient has undergone genetic testing (Yes-BRCA positive, Yes-BRCA negative, Yes-Other genetic mutation, No genetic testing, or Not sure).
* **Screening Contrainications** (`Screening Contrainications`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select any reasons that prevent the patient from undergoing screening (Pregnancy, Recent miscarriage, Active infection, Severe illness, Patient refusal, or None).
* **Previous Cancer Screening History** (`Previous Cancer Screening History`, Select One, Required): Select the patient's previous cancer screening history (No previous screening, Last screening less than 1 year ago, Last screening 1-2 years ago, Last screening 2-3 years ago, Last screening over 3 years ago, or Unknown).
* **Previous Screening Results** (`Previous Screening Results`, Select One, Optional): Select the results of the patient's last screening (Normal, Abnormal, Unsatisfactory, Not applicable, or Unknown).
* **Current Symptoms** (`Current Symptoms`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select any symptoms the patient is currently experiencing (Breast pain or tenderness, Breast lump, Nipple discharge, Vaginal bleeding, Pelvic pain, or No symptoms).
* **Current Hormone Use** (`Current Hormone Use`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select if the patient is taking any hormonal medications (Hormone replacement therapy, Birth control, Tamoxifen, Other hormone therapy, or Not taking hormones).
* **Menopausal Status** (`Menopausal Status`, Select One, Optional): Select the patient's menopausal status (Pre-menopausal, Peri-menopausal, Menopausal, Post-menopausal, or Unknown).
* **Informed Consent** (`Informed Consent`, Select One, Required): Select if the patient understands and consents to the screening procedure and risks (Yes, patient consents, No, patient declines, or Patient needs more information).
* **High-Risk Assessment** (`High-Risk Assessment`, Select One, Optional): Select the patient's risk level (Low risk, Average risk, High risk, Very high risk, or Unknown).
* **Insurance Coverage Confirmed** (`Insurance Coverage Confirmed`, Select One, Optional): Select if the screening is covered by the patient's insurance (Yes, covered, No, not covered, Self-pay, Uninsured, or Unknown).
* **Provider Notes** (`Provider Notes`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional clinical notes about the patient's status.
* **Informed Consent for Screening** (`Informed Consent for Screening`, Select One, Required): This field is not actually required. However, this field should be removed from this guide.
* **High-Risk Assessment** (`High-Risk Assessment`, Select One, Optional): This field is not required. However, this field should be removed from this guide.
