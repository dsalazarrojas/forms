# Ear Nose And Throat Patient Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about patients visiting an Ear, Nose, and Throat specialist.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section of the form carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to review each field for any errors before submitting the form.
3. If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the relevant healthcare authority or medical staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`1`, `general_information`, optional): This section is where you provide basic contact and visit information.
* **Patient Details** (`2`, `patient_details`, optional): Provide your medical identity information, such as your name, date of birth, and contact numbers.
* **Medical History** (`3`, `medical_history`, optional): This is a space to document any pre-existing medical conditions that you may have.
* **Doctor Referral** (`4`, `doctor_referral`, optional): If you were referred to this specialist by another doctor, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Contact Details** (`5`, `contact_details`, optional): This section is for you to provide your preferred contact information and any additional contact methods.

## Tips
* Ensure all required information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any concerns or doubts, don't hesitate to ask for help from healthcare staff or authorities.
