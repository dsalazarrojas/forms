# patient_health_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient_health_questionnaire form is designed to collect essential information from patients to help healthcare providers understand their medical history, lifestyle habits, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter patient information**: Answer the questions in the "Patient Details" page.
2. **Describe medical history**: Provide a brief description of your medical history in the "Medical History" page.
3. **Share presenting complaints**: Explain your current health concerns or issues in the "Presenting Complaints" page.
4. **Discuss family history**: Share information about your family's medical history in the "Family History" page.
5. **Lifestyle habits**: Describe your lifestyle habits, including diet, exercise, and other relevant habits, in the "Lifestyle Habits" page.
6. **Add any additional information**: Provide any other relevant details that may be helpful for your healthcare provider to know in the "Additional Information" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Details**: Enter your name, age, and any other relevant patient information.
* **Medical History**: Describe your past medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies.
* **Presenting Complaints**: Explain your current health concerns or issues, such as symptoms, pain, or discomfort.
* **Family History**: Share information about your family's medical history, including any inherited conditions or health issues.
* **Lifestyle Habits**: Describe your lifestyle habits, including diet, exercise, sleep, and other relevant habits.
* **Additional Information**: Provide any other relevant details that may be helpful for your healthcare provider to know.
 
## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Remember to provide any relevant documents, such as medical records or test results, if required.
* If you're unsure about any question, ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
