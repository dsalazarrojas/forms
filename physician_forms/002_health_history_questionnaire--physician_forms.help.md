# Health History Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential medical information about a patient's contact details and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact details, including your name, address, and phone number, in the "Contact Details" section.
2. Provide a brief description of your medical history, including any previous illnesses or medical conditions, in the "Medical History" section.
3. If you have any medical contacts, such as a doctor or hospital, please list their names and contact information in the "Medical Contact" section.
4. List any allergies or sensitivities you have in the "Allergies" section.
5. Provide information about your current medications and medical conditions in the "Medications" section.
6. In the "Medical History Part 1", "Medical History Part 2", "Medical History Part 3", and "Medical History Part 4" sections, please provide a detailed description of your medical history, including any surgeries, illnesses, or medical conditions you have experienced.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, required/optional): This section is where you can enter your contact information, including your name, address, and phone number.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, required/optional): This section is where you can provide a brief description of your medical history, including any previous illnesses or medical conditions.
* **Medical Contact** (Medical Contact, text, required/optional): This section is where you can list any medical contacts, such as a doctor or hospital.
* **Allergies** (Allergies, text, required/optional): This section is where you can list any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Medications** (Medications, text, required/optional): This section is where you can provide information about your current medications and medical conditions.
* **Medical History Part 1** (Medical History Part 1, text, required/optional), **Medical History Part 2** (Medical History Part 2, text, required/optional), **Medical History Part 3** (Medical History Part 3, text, required/optional), **Medical History Part 4** (Medical History Part 4, text, required/optional): These sections are where you can provide a detailed description of your medical history, including any surgeries, illnesses, or medical conditions.

## Tips
* Please be as thorough and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* This form is designed to help your healthcare provider understand your medical history and provide the best possible care.
