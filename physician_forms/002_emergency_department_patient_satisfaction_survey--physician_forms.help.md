# Emergency Department Patient Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency department patient satisfaction survey is a tool to collect feedback from patients about their experience in the emergency department. The form is designed to gather information on various aspects of care, including the quality of care, communication with staff, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

1. Please select your answers to each question, making sure to answer all required fields.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select one or more options that best describe your experience.
3. For free-text questions, provide a brief description of your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **How would you rate the overall quality of care you received? (emergency_department_patient_satisfaction_survey_page_1)**: This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the care you received in the emergency department on a numerical scale.
- **Staff Nature of Encounter (staff_nature_of_encounter)**: This question asks you to select the type of staff you interacted with during your visit (e.g., nurse, doctor, other).
- **Staff Friendly Nature (staff_friendly_nature)**: This question asks you to rate how friendly and approachable the staff were during your visit.
- **Wait Time (wait_time)**: This question asks you to rate the waiting time you experienced in the emergency department.
- **Doctor Communication (doctor_communication)**: This question asks you to rate how well your doctor communicated with you during your visit.
- **Nurse Communication (nurse_communication)**: This question asks you to rate how well the nurses communicated with you during your visit.
- **Nurse Professionalism (nurse_professionalism)**: This question asks you to rate how professional the nurses were during your visit.
- **Doctor Professionalism (doctor_professionalism)**: This question asks you to rate how professional the doctors were during your visit.
- **Overall Experience (overall_experience)**: This question asks you to rate your overall experience in the emergency department.
- **Emergency Department Visit (emergency_department_visit)**: This question asks you to rate how likely you are to visit the emergency department again.
- **Would Visit (would_visit)**: This question asks you to rate how likely you are to recommend this emergency department to others.
- **Emergency Department Patient Satisfaction Survey Comments (emergency_department_patient_satisfaction_survey_comments)**: This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when answering each question.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the free-text field at the end.
* Your feedback is important and will help us improve our services.
