# COVID 19 RT PCR Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for ordering a COVID-19 RT-PCR test, a molecular test used to diagnose the SARS-CoV-2 virus. It gathers information from the ordering physician, medical facility, patient, and test requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name, NPI number, medical facility name, and address.
2. Provide your contact phone number and email for result delivery.
3. Enter the patient's full name, date of birth, gender, and contact phone number.
4. Describe the patient's symptoms and the date they started showing symptoms.
5. Specify the date and time you prefer the sample to be collected.
6. Choose the type of sample to collect (e.g., nasopharyngeal swab).
7. Select the urgency level of the test (e.g., routine, urgent, or stat).
8. If applicable, provide insurance information and additional clinical notes.
9. Sign the form with your date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ordering Physician Name**: Enter your full name with credentials.
* **Physician NPI Number**: Enter your National Provider Identifier.
* **Medical Facility Name**: Enter the name of the hospital or clinic where the test will be performed.
* **Facility Address**: Enter the street address of the medical facility.
* **City**: Enter the city location of the medical facility.
* **State**: Enter the state or province where the medical facility is located.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter the postal code of the medical facility.
* **Physician Phone**: Enter your direct line or office number.
* **Physician Email**: Enter your email for result delivery.
* **Patient Full Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Patient Gender**: Select the patient's gender (Male, Female, Other, or Prefer not to say).
* **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's home address.
* **Reason for Test**: Select the primary reason for the test (e.g., symptomatic patient, close contact exposure, etc.).
* **Symptom Details**: Describe the patient's symptoms if applicable.
* **Symptom Onset Date**: Enter the date when the patient started showing symptoms if applicable.
* **Exposure Date**: Enter the date of known exposure if applicable.
* **Preferred Collection Date**: Enter the date you prefer the sample to be collected.
* **Preferred Collection Time**: Enter the preferred time slot for sample collection (optional).
* **Specimen Type**: Select the type of sample to collect (e.g., nasopharyngeal swab).
* **Test Priority**: Select the urgency level of the test (e.g., routine, urgent, or stat).
* **Insurance Information**: Enter the provider and policy number if applicable.
* **Additional Clinical Notes**: Enter any relevant medical history or notes.
* **Physician Signature Date**: Sign the form with your date.
