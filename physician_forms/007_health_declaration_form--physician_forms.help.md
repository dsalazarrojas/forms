# Health Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect health-related information from individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2.  If you have no information to report for a particular field, you can leave it blank.
3.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out if you are able to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Travel History** (`travel_history`, text, optional): Report any recent or past travel, including countries visited, dates, and destinations.
*   **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, optional): List any symptoms you are experiencing, such as fever, cough, or shortness of breath.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Share any pre-existing medical conditions, such as diabetes, asthma, or allergies.
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information, including name, email, or phone number.
*   **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, text, optional): Report any travel-related details, including travel dates, destinations, and modes of transport.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide as much detail as possible when answering each question.
*   If you are unsure about any information, please ask a medical professional for guidance.
*   Ensure that your contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
