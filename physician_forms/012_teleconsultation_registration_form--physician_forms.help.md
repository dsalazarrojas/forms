# Teleconsultation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a teleconsultation session with the hospital's physicians. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field. 
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Choose your preferred contact method from the options in the "Preferred Contact Method" field.
6. Enter your appointment date in the "Appointment Date" field.
7. Enter your appointment time in the "Appointment Time" field.
8. Enter the name of the physician you are scheduling with.
9. Enter the department of the physician you are scheduling with.
10. Enter any relevant patient notes in the "Patient Notes" field.
11. Enter your medical condition or reason for the teleconsultation session in the "Medical Condition" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (First Name)**: Enter your first name here. This will help the physician know your name during the teleconsultation session.
* **Last Name (Last Name)**: Enter your last name here. This will help the physician know your full name during the teleconsultation session.
* **Date of Birth (Date of Birth)**: Enter your date of birth here. This will help the physician know your age and medical history during the teleconsultation session.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)**: Enter your phone number here. This will help the physician contact you if needed during the teleconsultation session.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address here. This will help the physician contact you if needed during the teleconsultation session.
* **Preferred Contact Method (Preferred Contact Method)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted for the teleconsultation session. You can choose from "Telephone", "Email", or "Message".
* **Appointment Date (Appointment Date)**: Enter the date of the teleconsultation session.
* **Appointment Time (Appointment Time)**: Enter the time of the teleconsultation session.
* **Physician Name (Physician Name)**: Enter the name of the physician you are scheduling with.
* **Physician Department (Physician Department)**: Enter the department of the physician you are scheduling with.
* **Patient Notes (Patient Notes)**: Enter any relevant information about your health condition or the reason for the teleconsultation session.
* **Medical Condition (Medical Condition)**: Enter your medical condition or reason for the teleconsultation session.

## Tips
Please ensure that the information you enter is accurate and up-to-date. If you need to make any changes after submitting the form, please contact the hospital's teleconsultation team.
