# new_patient_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a new patient's profile, including their name, birthday, phone number, medical history, allergies, medications, and various medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply fill in each field accurately and truthfully, and submit it when finished.
2. Start by filling in the "New Patient Name" field with the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
3. Enter the patient's birthday in the "Patient Birthday" field using the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Patient Phone" field.
5. In the "Medical History" field, briefly describe the patient's medical history, including any relevant information.
6. In the "Allergies" field, list any allergies the patient has, including the type of substance or medication that causes the allergy.
7. In the "Meditations" field (note: this field is likely a typo and should be "Medications"), list any medications the patient is currently taking.
8. For each of the "Medical Conditions" fields (1-18), describe any relevant medical conditions the patient has.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Patient Birthday**: Enter the patient's birthday using the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Medical History**: Briefly describe the patient's medical history, including any relevant information.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies the patient has, including the type of substance or medication that causes the allergy.
* **Meditations**: List any medications the patient is currently taking. (Note: This field is likely a typo and should be ignored.)
* **Medical Conditions 1-18**: Describe any relevant medical conditions the patient has. Note: These fields seem to be redundant and should be filled in only if explicitly instructed to do so by a healthcare professional.

## Tips
* Double-check that each field is filled in accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
