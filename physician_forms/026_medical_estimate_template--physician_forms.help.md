# Medical Estimate Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and process medical estimates in a structured and organized manner, helping healthcare providers, medical offices, or clinics streamline their workflow. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling in the **Patient Info** page.
2. Provide **Billing Info** and **Estimate Details** for the service or treatment rendered.
3. Enter **Payment Terms** to outline the financial conditions of the service or treatment.
4. Record **Medical History** to help the healthcare provider better understand the patient's past medical conditions.
5. Enter your **Billing Address** for payment processing purposes.
6. Provide a digital signature in the **Patient Signature** field to confirm your consent.
7. Finally, sign the form in the **Doctor Signature** field to confirm the accuracy of the estimate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (Required): Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant patient information for the healthcare provider.
* **Billing Info** (Required): Enter your billing address, name, and any other relevant payment information for the healthcare provider.
* **Estimate Details** (Required): Provide a detailed description of the services or treatments rendered.
* **Payment Terms** (Optional): Outline any specific payment conditions, such as payment methods or deadlines.
* **Medical History** (Optional): Enter any relevant medical history that may impact the treatment or service.
* **Billing Address** (Required): Enter your billing address for payment processing purposes.
* **Patient Signature** (Required): Provide a digital signature to confirm your consent to the treatment or service.
* **Doctor Signature** (Optional): The healthcare provider signs here to confirm the accuracy of the estimate.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to the healthcare provider.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the healthcare provider directly.
