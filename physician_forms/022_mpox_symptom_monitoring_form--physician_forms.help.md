# Mpox Symptom Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Symptom Monitoring Form is designed to collect information on symptoms associated with Mpox, a viral disease caused by the Parapoxvirus. This form helps healthcare professionals gather data to aid in the early detection, treatment, and management of the disease.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer each question accurately and honestly.
2.  If a symptom is not listed, do not add a new symptom. Contact your healthcare provider for guidance.
3.  Enter a "Yes" or "No" for each symptom as it applies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **symptoms** (Text, Required):
    *   A text field where you can report any symptoms you are experiencing.
*   **fever** (Number, Optional):
    *   Enter the number of days you have had a fever, if any.
*   **rash** (Select Multiple, Optional):
    *   Select all symptoms that apply from the listed choices.
    *   Please note that this field is for symptom selection, not for describing the rash's severity or duration.
*   **headache** (Text, Optional):
    *   Report any headaches you are experiencing, including their frequency and severity.
*   **muscle_aches** (Text, Optional):
    *   Report any muscle aches you are experiencing, including their frequency and severity.
*   **swollen_lymph_nodes** (Text, Optional):
    *   Report any swollen lymph nodes you are experiencing, including their location and severity.
*   **muscle_aches_2** (Text, Optional):
    *   Report any muscle aches you are experiencing, including their frequency and severity.
*   **other_symptoms** (Text, Optional):
    *   Report any other symptoms not listed above.
*   **note** (Note, Optional):
    *   Add any additional information about your symptoms or medical history.
*   **note_2** (Note, Optional):
    *   Add any additional information about your symptoms or medical history.
