# Procedure Visit Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Procedure Visit Intake Form is used to collect vital information about a patient's visit, including their contact details, medical records, and procedure specifics. This information will help the physician understand the patient's needs and provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Enter the patient's contact phone number in the **Contact Phone Number** field. This field is required.
3. Enter the patient's email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter the patient's medical record number in the **Medical Record Number** field. This field is required.
5. Select the procedure that the patient will undergo from the **Procedure Name** dropdown menu. This field is required.
6. Enter the date and time of the procedure in the **Date of Procedure** and **Time of Procedure** fields, if applicable.
7. Enter the patient's address in the **Patient Address** field.
8. Enter the name of the physician who will be performing the procedure in the **Physician Name** field.
9. Enter the name of the medical practice that the patient visits in the **Medical Practice Name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone_number`, text, required): Enter the patient’s contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, text, optional): Enter the patient’s email address.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, required): Enter the patient’s medical record number.
* **Procedure Name** (`procedure_name`, select_one, required): Select the procedure that the patient will undergo from the dropdown menu.
* **Date of Procedure** (`date_of_procedure`, date, optional): Enter the date of the procedure, if applicable.
* **Time of Procedure** (`time_of_procedure`, time, optional): Enter the time of the procedure, if applicable.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the physician who will be performing the procedure.
* **Medical Practice Name** (`medical_practice_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medical practice that the patient visits.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If the patient has a medical record number, enter it as a unique identifier.
* If the date and time of the procedure are not known, you can leave these fields blank.
* If the patient's address is not known, you can leave this field blank.
* Please ensure that the procedure selected is accurate and corresponds to the patient's needs.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the medical staff for assistance.
