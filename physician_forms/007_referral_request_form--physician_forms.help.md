# Referral Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about referral requests from physicians.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Referral Request Form Name** field with a brief description of the referral request.
2. Enter the **Patient Name** in the corresponding field.
3. Provide the **Referring Physician** name, if applicable.
4. Specify the **Referral Reason** in the field below.
5. Choose the **Referral Type** as either "new" or "follow-up".
6. Enter the **Patient Date of Birth** in the format of mm/dd/yyyy or mm/dd/yy.
7. Enter the **Patient's Address**, if applicable.
8. Enter the **Referring Physician Name** in the field below, if applicable.
9. Enter the **Patient's Phone Number**, including the 3 digit area code.
10. Enter the **Patient's Email**, if applicable.
11. Provide a brief **Patient Medical History**, if necessary.
12. Sign your **Physician's Signature** to confirm the referral request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referral Request Form Name**: Enter a brief description of the referral request.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name.
* **Referring Physician**: Enter the name of the referring physician, if applicable.
* **Referral Reason**: Enter a brief description of the referral reason.
* **Referral Type**: Choose "new" for a new referral or "follow-up" for a follow-up referral.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy or mm/dd/yy.
* **Patient's Address**: Enter the patient's address, if applicable.
* **Referring Physician Name**: Enter the name of the referring physician, if applicable.
* **Patient's Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number, including the 3 digit area code.
* **Patient's Email**: Enter the patient's email, if applicable.
* **Patient Medical History**: Provide a brief description of the patient's medical history, if necessary.
* **Physician's Signature**: Sign your name to confirm the referral request.
