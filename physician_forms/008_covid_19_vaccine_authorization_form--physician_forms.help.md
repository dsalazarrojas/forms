# COVID 19 Vaccine Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Vaccine Authorization Form is a mandatory submission for all employees who wish to receive the COVID-19 vaccine at the workplace. This form is used to gather information about your medical history, vaccine preferences, and other relevant details to ensure a safe and successful vaccination process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please complete the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are filled accurately.
2.  If you are unsure about any question, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
3.  Once completed, please review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Employee Full Name, Text, Required): Please provide your complete legal name as per your employee ID.
* **Employee ID Number** (Employee ID Number, Text, Required): Enter your organizational employee number.
* **Department** (Department, Text, Required): Enter your department or division.
* **Position Title** (Position Title, Text, Optional): Enter your job title or position.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, Email, Required): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, Text, Required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **I Authorize COVID-19 Vaccination at Workplace** (I Authorize COVID-19 Vaccination at Workplace, Select One, Required): Please select whether you authorize or decline the vaccine.
* **Preferred Vaccination Date** (Preferred Vaccination Date, Date, Optional): Choose your preferred vaccination date, if available.
* **Vaccine Type Preference if Available** (Vaccine Type Preference if Available, Select One, Optional): Choose your preferred vaccine type, if available.
* **Known Allergies to Vaccine Components** (Known Allergies to Vaccine Components, Text, Optional): If you have any allergies to vaccine ingredients, list them here.
* **Chronic Medical Conditions** (Chronic Medical Conditions, Text, Optional): If you have any chronic medical conditions, list them here.
* **Current Medications** (Current Medications, Text, Optional): If you are currently taking any medications, list them here.
* **Are You Immunocompromised** (Are You Immunocompromised, Select One, Required): Please indicate whether you have a compromised immune system.
* **Are You Pregnant or Breastfeeding** (Are You Pregnant or Breastfeeding, Select One, Optional): Please indicate your current pregnancy or breastfeeding status.
* **Have You Had COVID-19 in the Last 90 Days** (Have You Had COVID-19 in the Last 90 Days, Select One, Required): Please indicate whether you have had COVID-19 in the last 90 days.
* **If Declining, Reason for Declination** (If Declining, Reason for Declination, Text, Optional): If you are declining the vaccine, please provide a reason for your declination.
* **Date of Authorization** (Date of Authorization, Date, Required): Please date and sign this form to indicate your authorization.
* **I Acknowledge I Have Read and Understand This Form** (I Acknowledge I Have Read and Understand This Form, Select One, Required): Please acknowledge and agree to the terms in this authorization form.

Note that the required fields are marked as "Required" and are essential for the purpose of this form.
