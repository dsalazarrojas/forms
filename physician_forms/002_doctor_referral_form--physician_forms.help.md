# Doctor Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doctor Referral Form is designed to collect information from patients regarding their referral to a physician. This form is meant to gather essential details that will help healthcare providers better understand the patient's medical history, insurance status, and reasons for their referral.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient's information on the "patient_info" page.
2. Provide details about your physician referral on the "physician_referral" page.
3. Share your medical history on the "medical_history" page.
4. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have insurance on the "patient_insurance" page.
5. Enter your reason for referral on the "doctor_referral_reason" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_info (text)**: This is where you can enter your basic information, such as your name, contact details, and any other relevant patient data.
* **physician_referral (text)**: Here, you can provide details about your referral to a specific doctor or specialist.
* **medical_history (text)**: This is a text area where you can share your medical history, including any relevant health issues or concerns.
* **patient_insurance (select_multiple)**: Please answer "Yes" if you have insurance coverage, and "No" if you do not. This will help us better understand your insurance status.
* **doctor_referral_reason (text)**: Enter your reason for referral, including any specific symptoms, concerns, or goals for your medical care.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and honest as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to a healthcare professional.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, but it's also a good idea to fill out optional fields if you have the necessary information.
* Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness.
