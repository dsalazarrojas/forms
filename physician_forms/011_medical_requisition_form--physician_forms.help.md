# Medical Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Medical Requisition Form is used to gather information about patient medical needs and conditions from healthcare providers. This information will be used to help healthcare staff make informed decisions about patient care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have the necessary information to complete it accurately.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on the patient's medical needs and conditions.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields, indicated by an *asterisk*.
4. If a field has a dropdown menu, select all applicable options by using the checkboxes or by typing in the search field and selecting the correct option from the dropdown list.
5. Once you have completed the form, review it for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Patient Information
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, *required*): Enter the patient's name and any relevant information that is not already included in the patient's electronic health record.

### 2. Medical Equipment
* **Medical Equipment** (`medical_equipment`, select_multiple, *optional*): Check the boxes of all medical equipment that the patient requires. Options: 'Yes', 'No'.

### 3. Medical Supplies
* **Medical Supplies** (`medical_supplies`, select_multiple, *optional*): Check the boxes of all medical supplies that the patient requires. Options: 'Yes', 'No'.

### 4. Medications
* **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, *optional*): Check the boxes of all medications that the patient requires. Options: 'Yes', 'No'.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the healthcare team for guidance.
* Only one form is required per patient, so please make sure to check the patient's electronic health record for any existing forms before submitting this one.
