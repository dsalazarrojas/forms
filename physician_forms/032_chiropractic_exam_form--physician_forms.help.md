# Chiropractic Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chiropractic Exam Form is a comprehensive tool used by healthcare professionals to gather essential information about a patient's medical history, symptoms, and examination results. This form helps chiropractors and other healthcare providers assess a patient's condition, create a treatment plan, and track progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all required fields.
2. Enter your patient's information, including name, date of birth, age, and contact details.
3. Describe your chief complaint, including the area of pain or discomfort and any relevant medical history.
4. Report any previous injuries or accidents and prior treatments for this condition.
5. List all medications currently taken.
6. Identify any known allergies.
7. Answer questions about your occupation, daily activities, and physical activity level.
8. Report your posture assessment, range of motion, and palpation findings.
9. Create a recommended treatment plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Patient Information (Page 1)
* **Patient Full Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Age**: Enter the patient's age in years.
* **Gender**: Select the patient's gender (Male, Female, or Other).
* **Examination Date**: Enter the date of the examination.

### Chief Complaint and History (Page 2)
* **Chief Complaint Area**: Select the area where the pain or discomfort is located (Neck, Upper Back, Mid-Back, Lower Back, Shoulder, Arm, Leg, or Other).
* **Describe the Complaint**: Provide a detailed description of the symptoms and location of the pain or discomfort.
* **How long have you had this symptom**: Select the duration of the symptom (Less than 1 week, 1-4 weeks, 1-3 months, 3-6 months, or 6+ months).

### Medical History (Page 3)
* **Previous Injuries or Accidents**: Describe any previous injuries or accidents that may be relevant to the current condition.
* **Prior Treatments**: List any prior treatments for this condition, including medications, therapy, or surgery.
* **Current Medications**: List all medications currently taken.

### Occupational and Lifestyle Factors (Page 4)
* **Current Occupation**: Enter the patient's current occupation.
* **What daily activities aggravate the pain**: Describe any daily activities that aggravate the pain or discomfort.
* **Exercise or Physical Activity Level**: Select the level of physical activity (Sedentary, Light Activity, Moderate Activity, or Vigorous Activity).

### Physical Examination (Page 5)
* **Posture Assessment**: Select the posture assessment (Good, Fair, Poor, or Significant Deviations).
* **Range of Motion**: Select the range of motion (Normal, Limited on one side, Limited bilaterally, or Severely restricted).
* **Palpation Findings**: Describe any palpation findings, including tender points, misalignment, or muscle tension.

### Treatment Plan (Page 6)
* **Recommended Treatment Plan**: Describe the recommended treatment plan, including adjustments, therapy, stretches, or other treatments.

By following these instructions and completing each section carefully, healthcare professionals can gather essential information to create an effective treatment plan and improve patient outcomes.
