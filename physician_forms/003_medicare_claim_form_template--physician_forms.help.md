# Medicare Claim Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a Medicare claim, which is a request for reimbursement of healthcare expenses submitted to the Medicare program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information on page 1, including their name, date of birth, and contact details.
2. Provide claim details, including the claim type (e.g., inpatient, outpatient, etc.) and any relevant dates.
3. Enter the provider's information, including their name, address, and contact details.
4. Add billing information, including the patient's account number and billing address.
5. Check the claim status and update as necessary.
6. Enter payment information, if applicable.
7. Describe the patient's relationship with the provider.
8. Add any additional contact information for the provider.
9. Include any relevant claim notes or attachments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**:
	+ (Name, Text, Required): Enter the patient's full name and date of birth.
* **Claim Details**:
	+ (Claim Type, Text, Required): Enter the type of claim (e.g., inpatient, outpatient, etc.).
* **Provider Information**:
	+ (Provider Name, Text, Required): Enter the name and title of the healthcare provider.
* **Billing Information**:
	+ (Patient Account Number, Text, Required): Enter the patient's Medicare account number.
* **Claim Status**:
	+ (Claim Status, Text, Required): Check the status of the claim (e.g., pending, approved, denied).
* **Payment Information**:
	+ (Payment Method, Text, Optional): Enter the payment method, if applicable.
* **Patient Relationship**:
	+ (Relationship, Text, Optional): Describe the relationship between the patient and the provider.
* **Provider Contact Info**:
	+ (Provider Contact Info, Text, Optional): Enter any additional contact information for the provider.
* **Claim Notes**:
	+ (Claim Notes, Text, Optional): Enter any relevant notes or comments about the claim.
* **Claim Attachment**:
	+ (Claim Attachment, Text, Optional): Attach any supporting documents or files, if applicable.
