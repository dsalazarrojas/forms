# Cosmetic_Surgery_Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cosmetic_Surgery_Survey form is designed to collect information about a patient's experience with cosmetic surgery. It gathers feedback on survey trends, patient satisfaction, and overall experience, which will help healthcare professionals evaluate and improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and provide answers to the best of your knowledge.
* Enter all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
* For fields with options, select the most accurate answer from the provided choices.
* If a field has a note option, enter any additional comments or questions you may have about the patient.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Trends**: Enter any trends or observations about the patient's experience.
* **Patient Satisfaction**: Select how satisfied you are with the patient's overall experience:
	+ Option 1: Satisfied
	+ Option 2: Neutral
	+ Option 3: Dissatisfied
* **Overall Experience**: Describe the patient's overall experience with the surgery.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or questions about the patient.
* **Patient Email**: Enter the patient's email address (optional).
* **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number (optional).
* **Date of Surgery**: Enter the date of the surgery.
* **Surgeon Name**: Enter the name of the surgeon who performed the surgery.
* **Assistant Name**: Enter the name of the assistant who assisted with the surgery.
* **Anesthetist Name**: Enter the name of the anesthetist who administered anesthesia.
* **Surgical Team Name**: Enter the name of the surgical team that performed the surgery.
* **Location of Surgery**: Enter the location where the surgery was performed.
* **Type of Surgery**: Select the type of surgery performed:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Surgical Technology Used**: Enter the technology used during the surgery.
* **Complications Encountered**: Describe any complications that occurred during the surgery.
* **Post-Surgery Instructions**: Enter any post-surgery instructions given to the patient.
* **Follow-Up**: Enter the follow-up information for the patient (optional).
* **Patient Satisfaction Rating**: Select the patient's satisfaction rating:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Overall Rating**: Select the overall rating for the patient's experience:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Follow-Up Date**: Enter the date of the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-Up Time**: Enter the time of the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-Up Location**: Enter the location of the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-Up Notes**: Enter any follow-up notes (optional).
* **Surgeon Comments**: Enter any comments from the surgeon (optional).
* **Patient Comments**: Enter any comments from the patient (optional).
