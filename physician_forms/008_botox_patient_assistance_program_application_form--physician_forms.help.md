# Botox Patient Assistance Program Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help patients apply for assistance with Botox treatment. The Botox Patient Assistance Program is intended to provide financial assistance to patients who are experiencing difficulties in paying for Botox treatments. By filling out this form, you will be able to submit your application and potentially receive aid in paying for Botox treatments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out each field completely and accurately, using your full legal name and correct information.
2. Make sure to answer all questions truthfully, as this information will be used to assess your eligibility for assistance.
3. If you have any trouble with the form, do not hesitate to ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

**1. Patient First Name** (`patient_first_name`, text, required): Enter your legal first name as it appears on your government-issued identification.
**2. Patient Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, text, required): Enter your legal last name as it appears on your government-issued identification.
**3. Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
**4. Gender** (`patient_gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender as Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer not to say, or Other.
**5. Street Address** (`patient_address`, text, required): Enter your complete home address.
**6. City** (`patient_city`, text, required): Enter the city of your residence.
**7. State** (`patient_state`, select_one, required): Select your state of residence from the list provided.
**8. ZIP Code** (`patient_zip`, text, required): Enter your home ZIP code.
**9. Phone Number** (`patient_phone`, text, required): Enter your primary contact number.
**10. Email Address** (`patient_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for program communications.
**11. Last 4 Digits of SSN** (`ssn_last_four`, text, required): Enter the last four digits of your Social Security Number (SSN) for verification purposes.
**12. Household Size** (`household_size`, number, required): Enter the total number of people in your household.
**13. Annual Household Income** (`household_income`, select_one, required): Select the approximate annual income of your household before taxes from the provided options.
**14. Insurance Status** (`insurance_status`, select_one, required): Select whether you have private insurance, Medicare, Medicaid, Uninsured, or Underinsured.
**15. Medical Condition Requiring Treatment** (`medical_condition`, select_one, required): Select the diagnosed condition you are seeking treatment for from the provided options.
**16. Date of Diagnosis** (`diagnosis_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your diagnosis in MM/DD/YYYY format.
**17. Treating Physician Name** (`treating_physician`, text, required): Enter the full name of your treating physician.
**18. Physician Phone** (`physician_phone`, text, required): Enter your treating physician's contact number.
**19. Physician Practice Address** (`physician_address`, text, required): Enter your treating physician's practice address.
**20. Current Treatment Status** (`current_treatment`, select_one, required): Select whether you are currently receiving Botox treatment, yes or no.
**21. Financial Hardship Statement** (`financial_hardship`, text, required): Describe your financial situation and why you need assistance.
**22. Type of Assistance Requested** (`assistance_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of assistance you need, such as full coverage, partial co-pay assistance, or a payment plan.
**23. Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Select your current employment status from the provided options.
**24. Government Assistance Received** (`government_assistance`, select_multiple, required): Select any government assistance programs you receive.
**25. Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as a signature.
**26. Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your application.
**27. Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, text, required): Enter your treating physician's signature.

Note: Please make sure your treating physician signs and completes sections 17, 18, 19, 27, and provides their signature.
