# Mpox Vaccine Screening And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and manage patient information related to mpox vaccine screening, eligibility, consent, and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, please provide your **Screening Information** in the first field.
2.  After, select the **Vaccine Details** field and fill out all required information.
3.  In the **Screening Results** field, enter any relevant details.
4.  Choose your **Vaccination Status** and **Consent Status** accordingly, selecting from the provided options.
5.  Enter your **Date Of Birth** to identify the patient.
6.  Provide your **Contact Information** for follow-up purposes.
7.  Describe the **Next Steps** the patient should take or any reminders for you to follow up with the patient.
8.  Finally, sign and date the **Signature** field to authenticate the patient's consent.
9.  Enter your **Contact Number** for easy contact in case of emergencies.
10. Enter the **Patient Name** for easy identification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Screening Information**: This field is used to collect any relevant medical screening information.
*   **Vaccine Details**: Here, you will find specifics about the vaccine, such as dosage and administration schedule.
*   **Screening Results**: This field is used to record any reactions or issues after the vaccine.
*   **Vaccination Status**: Select from the provided options to indicate the patient's eligibility for the vaccine.
*   **Consent Status**: Similar to **Vaccination Status**, select from the provided options to indicate consent.
*   **Date Of Birth**: This is used to identify the patient.
*   **Contact Information**: Please provide your contact information for follow-up purposes.
*   **Next Steps**: Describe any additional instructions or reminders for the patient.
*   **Signature**: Sign and date this field to authenticate the patient’s consent.
*   **Contact Number**: Enter a contact number in case of emergencies.
*   **Patient Name**: Enter the patient’s name for easy identification.

## Tips

*   Be precise and accurate when filling out the form to avoid confusion.
*   Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
*   If you are unsure about any part of the form, please ask a healthcare professional for assistance.
*   Keep the form up-to-date and secure for future reference.
