# Pregnancy Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pregnancy Checklist is a form used to collect detailed information about a pregnancy, including expectant parents' details, due date, gestation age, symptoms, medical information, complications, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the expectant parents in the **Expectant Parents** field.
2. Enter the expected **Due Date** in YYYY-MM-DD format.
3. Enter the **Gestation Age** in numerical format.
4. Select the correct **Fetal Size** from the provided options.
5. Provide a detailed description of any **Symptoms** experienced during the pregnancy in the notes field.
6. Enter any **Medical Information** that may be relevant to the pregnancy in the notes field.
7. Select all applicable **Complications** that have occurred during the pregnancy from the options provided.
8. Enter the number of **Hospitalizations** the mother has experienced during the pregnancy.
9. Enter the **Birth Weight** of the baby in pounds.
10. Enter the **Birth Length** of the baby in centimeters.
11. Select the **Delivery Type** from the provided options.
12. Enter the **Birth Date** in YYYY-MM-DD format.
13. Select the **Blood Type** from the options provided.
14. Enter any additional **Notes** about the pregnancy in the notes field.
15. Enter the **Follow Up** date to schedule a follow-up appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Expectant Parents**: Enter the names of the mother and father, or single parent(s) if applicable.
* **Due Date**: Enter the expected date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Gestation Age**: Enter the number of weeks or months the baby has been in the womb.
* **Fetal Size**: Select the size of the baby from the provided options (1, 2, or 3).
* **Symptoms**: Describe any symptoms or concerns experienced during the pregnancy.
* **Medical Info**: Enter any relevant medical information or concerns.
* **Complications**: Select all applicable complications that have occurred during the pregnancy.
* **Hospitalizations**: Enter the number of times the mother has been hospitalized during the pregnancy.
* **Birth Weight**: Enter the weight of the baby in pounds.
* **Birth Length**: Enter the length of the baby in centimeters.
* **Delivery Type**: Select the method of delivery (Vaginal, C-Section, Other).
* **Birth Date**: Enter the date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Blood Type**: Select the blood type of the baby from the provided options.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or information about the pregnancy.
* **Follow Up**: Enter the date to schedule a follow-up appointment.
