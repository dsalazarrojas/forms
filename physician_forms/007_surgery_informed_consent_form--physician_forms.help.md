# Surgery Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients before surgery. It ensures that the patient understands the risks, benefits, and alternatives to the surgery.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out this form, please gather all necessary information and documents related to the patient, including but not limited to:
- Patient's full name, date of birth, and address
- Surgery details, including type, date, and time
- Anesthesia details, if applicable
- Patient's contact information, including phone number and email
- Medical history, allergies, and current medications
- Any additional medical conditions that may be relevant to the surgery

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Patient Information
* **Patient Information** (text): Enter the patient's full name, date of birth, and address.

### 2. Surgery Details
* **Surgery Details** (text): Enter the type of surgery, date, and time.

### 3. Anesthesia Details
* **Anesthesia Details** (text): If applicable, enter the details of the anesthesia procedure.

### 4. Patient Consent
* **Patient Consent** (text): The patient must sign this section to confirm they have read and understood the information provided.

### 5. Patient Signature
* **Patient Signature** (number): The patient's signature must be signed using a number (e.g., 1-10) to acknowledge they have read and understood the information provided.

### 6. Physician Signature
* **Physician Signature** (number): The physician must sign this section to confirm they have informed the patient of the risks, benefits, and alternatives to the surgery.

### 7. Date
* **Date** (date): Enter the date of the patient's informed consent.

### 8. Time
* **Time** (time): Enter the time of the patient's informed consent.

### 9. Physician Name
* **Physician Name** (text): Enter the name of the attending physician.

### 10. Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (text): Enter the contact information for the physician or medical institution.

### 11. Note 1
* **Additional Instructions** (note): If necessary, enter any additional instructions or comments about the patient's care.

### 12. Phone
* **Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the physician or medical institution.

### 13. Email
* **Email** (email): Enter the email address of the physician or medical institution.

### 14. Fax
* **Fax** (text): Enter the fax number of the physician or medical institution.

### 15. Address
* **Address** (text): Enter the address of the physician or medical institution.

### 16. Medical History
* **Medical History** (text): Enter any relevant medical history, allergies, or current medications.

### 17. Allergies
* **Allergies** (text): If applicable, enter any allergies that may affect the patient's treatment.

### 18. Medications
* **Medications** (text): If applicable, enter any current medications that may affect the patient's treatment.

### 19. Medical Condition
* **Medical Condition** (text): If applicable, enter any additional medical conditions that may affect the patient's treatment.

### 20. Physician Comments
* **Physician Comments** (text): If necessary, enter any comments from the physician about the patient's condition or treatment.

### 21. Patient Information Additional
* **Patient Information Additional** (text): If necessary, enter any additional patient information.

### 22. Consent Type
* **Consent Type** (select one): Select the type of consent (General, Minor, or Emergency).

### 23. Consent Date
* **Consent Date** (date): Enter the date of the patient's informed consent.

### 24. Consent Time
* **Consent Time** (time): Enter the time of the patient's informed consent.

### 25. Note 2
* **Note 2** (note): If necessary, enter any additional comments from the physician or patient.
