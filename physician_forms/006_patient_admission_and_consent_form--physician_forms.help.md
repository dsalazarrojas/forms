# Patient Admission And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Admission And Consent Form is a critical document used in the healthcare industry to record and verify patient information upon admission to the hospital. It is essential to have accurate and complete information on file for patient care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the relevant options for the "Patient Info" field that matches the patient's identity.
2. For the "Admission Date", enter the date and time of the patient's admission to the hospital.
3. For the "Doctor Info" field, select the attending doctor's name from the provided options or choose "Other" if the doctor is not listed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Admission and Consent**: This is a note section where patients or their representatives can provide any additional information or notes about the patient's condition or treatment.
* **Patient Info**: Choose one or more of the following options to match the patient's identity:
	+ Mr. John Doe
	+ Mrs. Jane Doe
	+ Mr. John Smith
* **Admission Date**: Enter the date and time of the patient's admission to the hospital in the following format: DD/MM/YYYY HH:MM.
* **Doctor Info**: Choose one of the following options:
	+ Dr. John Smith
	+ Other (for cases where the attending doctor is not listed)

## Tips
* Always ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and correctly.
* Double-check the patient's information to prevent any errors or discrepancies.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant staff or healthcare professional.
* If the patient's information has changed or updated, please update this form accordingly.
* This form must be completed in its entirety before proceeding with patient care.
