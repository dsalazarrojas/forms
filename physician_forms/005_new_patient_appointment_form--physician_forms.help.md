# New Patient Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Patient Appointment Form is designed to collect essential information from patients when booking a new appointment with a physician. This form serves as a starting point for the patient's care and helps the physician's office to gather necessary details for their records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "New Patient" field.
2. Choose whether you would like to submit your appointment by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Submit" field.
3. Select a date and time for your appointment in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
4. Enter your physician's name in the "Physician" field.
5. Provide a brief reason for your appointment in the "Reason" field.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about your appointment in the "Note" field.
7. Enter your contact phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Patient**: Enter your name in this field. This is a required field.
* **Submit**: Choose "Yes" if you are ready to submit your appointment, or "No" if you would like to cancel or reschedule.
* **Date**: Select the date for your appointment from the calendar.
* **Time**: Choose the time for your appointment from the available options.
* **Physician**: Enter the name of the physician you would like to schedule with.
* **Reason**: Provide a brief reason for your appointment, such as a routine check-up or a specific medical concern.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about your appointment, such as any medications or allergies.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number so we can reach you about your appointment.
