# mpox_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MPox Consent Form is designed to collect necessary information and consent from patients for MPox vaccine-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the page corresponding to the section you wish to fill out. The form has multiple pages to ensure that you do not have to fill out the entire form at once.
2. Fill out each field according to the instructions below:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Date (page 1)** (`consent_date`, date, required)
    * Please enter the date of the consent.
* **Patient Name (page 1)** (`name`, text, required)
    * Enter the patient's name as it appears on their government-issued ID or passport.
* **Contact Information (page 1)** (`contact_info`, text, optional)
    * If applicable, enter the patient's contact information to facilitate communication.
* **Patient Signature (page 1)** (`signature`, note, optional)
    * If applicable, have the patient sign this section.
* **Healthcare Provider (page 2)** (`healthcare_provider`, select_one, required)
    * Choose the healthcare provider from the available options.
* **Phone Number (page 2)** (`patient_phone`, text, optional)
    * Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email (page 2)** (`patient_email`, email, optional)
    * Enter the patient's email address for contact purposes.
* **Vaccine Information (page 2)** (`vaccine_info`, text, optional)
    * Enter any relevant vaccine information.
* **Treatment Information (page 2)** (`treatment_info`, text, optional)
    * Enter any relevant treatment information.
* **Consent Status (page 3)** (`consent_status`, select_one, required)
    * Choose the consent status from the available options.
* **Review (page 3)** (`review`, select_one, required)
    * Choose the review status from the available options.
* **Date of Birth (page 3)** (`patient_dob`, date, required)
    * Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Gender (page 3)** (`patient_gender`, select_one, required)
    * Choose the patient's gender from the available options.
* **Race (page 3)** (`patient_race`, select_one, required)
    * Choose the patient's race from the available options.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to collect and manage patient consent information.
* Review the form for completeness before submitting.
