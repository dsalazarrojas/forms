# Coronavirus Case Report Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for healthcare professionals to report cases of coronavirus, enabling the tracking and analysis of the disease's spread.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the location where the patient's symptoms first appeared in the "location" field.
3. Provide the date when the patient's symptoms first started in the "Date of Symptoms Onset" field.
4. Enter the number of days since the patient's symptoms began in the "Number of Days Since Onset" field.
5. Describe the patient's symptoms in the "Symptoms Reported" field.
6. Select the current case status from the options provided: "Active", "Recovered", or "No Longer Active".
7. Enter your name and role in the "Reported By" field, if required.
8. Enter the date and time when the report was submitted in the "Reporting Date" and "Reporting Time" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (text, optional)**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record or identification documents.
* **location (text, optional)**: Enter the location where the patient's symptoms first appeared, such as a city or town.
* **Date of Symptoms Onset (date, optional)**: Enter the date when the patient's symptoms first started.
* **Number of Days Since Onset (number, optional)**: Enter the number of days since the patient's symptoms began.
* **Symptoms Reported (text, optional)**: Describe the patient's symptoms, such as fever, cough, etc.
* **Case Status (select_one, required)**: Select the current case status from the options provided.
	+ **Active**: The case is active and the patient is still experiencing symptoms.
	+ **Recovered**: The case is no longer active and the patient has recovered.
	+ **No Longer Active**: The case is no longer active but may still be under observation.
* **Reported By (text, optional)**: Enter your name and role, such as "Dr. Smith, MD".
* **Reporting Date (date, optional)**: Enter the date when the report was submitted.
* **Reporting Time (time, optional)**: Enter the time when the report was submitted.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the report.
* If the case status is "Active", ensure that the patient is still receiving medical attention.
* If the case status is "Recovered", ensure that the patient has fully recovered and is no longer experiencing symptoms.
* If the case status is "No Longer Active", ensure that the patient is no longer experiencing symptoms but may still require observation.
