# Early Pregnancy Self Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Early Pregnancy Self Referral Form is designed for patients to provide information about their pregnancy. This form helps healthcare providers gather essential details about the patient's condition, medical history, and contact information. The purpose of this form is to ensure accurate and timely medical care during pregnancy.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Enter your contact information so that we can reach out to you for further questions or to schedule appointments.
3. Provide any relevant medical history and family history related to your pregnancy.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your name, date of birth, and any other identifying information.
* **Pregnancy Info** (`pregnancy_info`, text, optional): In this field, describe your current pregnancy status, including the expected due date, any previous pregnancies, and any complications or concerns you may be experiencing.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Here, you can list any medical conditions, allergies, or medications you are currently taking. This will help your healthcare provider understand your overall health and provide better care.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number and email address so that we can contact you for further questions or to schedule appointments.
* **Patient History** (`patient_history`, text, optional): In this field, describe any significant medical events or surgeries you've had in the past, including any previous pregnancies or births.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, text, optional): Describe any medical history or conditions that may affect your pregnancy or family dynamics.
* **Medical History 1** (`medical_history_1`, text, optional): This field is similar to the "Medical History" field above, but if you have any additional medical history you'd like to share, you can enter it here.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, text, optional): Another opportunity to list any additional medical history that may be relevant to your care.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form will help us provide you with the best possible care, so please be as thorough as possible.
