# Family Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Medical History Form is used to collect medical information about patients' families, helping doctors and medical professionals provide personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Patient ID**: Enter the patient's ID number in the field.
2. **Provide Family History**: Answer the question regarding your family history in the text field.
3. **List Medical Conditions**: Select any medical conditions that apply to your family from the dropdown menu.
4. **List Allergies**: Choose any known allergies from the dropdown menu.
5. **Enter Medical History Date**: Enter the date of the medical event, if applicable.
6. **Add Doctor Note**: Provide any additional medical notes from the doctor in the text field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Patient ID (1)**: Enter the patient's ID number to identify them in the system.
- **Family History (2)**: Provide a brief description of your family medical history.
- **Medical Conditions (3)**: Select 'Yes' if you have any known medical conditions in your family, 'No' otherwise.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us provide better care.
- **Allergies (4)**: Choose any known allergies from the dropdown menu.
  - This field is optional but crucial for our team to know about potential allergies.
- **Medical History Date (5)**: Enter the date of any significant medical event, such as a diagnosis or treatment.
  - This field is required for accurate record-keeping.
- **Doctor Note (6)**: Provide any additional comments or notes from the doctor, if necessary.
  - This field is optional but helps our team understand your situation better.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask your doctor for guidance.
- The form is designed for professional medical input only; patients should not fill it out themselves.
