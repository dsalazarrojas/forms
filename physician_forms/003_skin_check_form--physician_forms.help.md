# Skin Check Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Skin Check Form is a tool used to collect information about a patient's skin conditions, concerns, and related symptoms. This form helps healthcare professionals to quickly assess and document the skin health of patients, allowing for more efficient and effective care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options from each field to provide detailed information about the patient's skin conditions, concerns, and symptoms.
2. Enter any additional information or comments in the text fields.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
