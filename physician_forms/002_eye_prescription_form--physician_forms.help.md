# Eye Prescription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eye Prescription Form is a crucial document used by healthcare professionals to record and prescribe eye care instructions for patients. It is used to gather information from patients and prescribers to ensure accurate and efficient prescription processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's first name in the **Patient First Name** field.
2. Enter the patient's last name in the **Patient Last Name** field.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter the prescriber's first name in the **Prescriber First Name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Patient First Name** (`patient_first_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's first name.
### **Patient Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's last name.
### **Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
### **Prescriber First Name** (`prescriber_first_name`, text, optional): Enter the prescriber's first name.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If any field is marked as required, please fill it out.
- Double-check the patient's date of birth to ensure accuracy.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team.
