# Surgery Pre Op Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Surgery Pre Op Order Form is a collection of information required for patients about their medical history, condition, and upcoming surgery. This form helps medical professionals assess the patient's situation and prepare for the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each section carefully and thoroughly.
2. Enter the chief complaint(s) that led to the patient's admission (e.g., pain, injury, etc.).
3. Provide a brief history of the patient's illness or condition.
4. Report whether the patient has a history of medical issues related to the condition or similar conditions.
5. Describe the patient's current health status (e.g., fever, pain, etc.).
6. Sign your name as the attending physician.
7. Enter the patient's name.
8. Select a date for the surgery.
9. Specify the type of surgery the patient will undergo.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Chief Complaints**: Enter the main reason for the patient's admission. This is usually a brief description of the patient's current condition.
* **History of Illness**: Provide a concise and factual account of the patient's medical history, including any past illnesses or allergies.
* **Past Medical History (Yes/No)**: Indicate whether the patient has a history of medical issues related to the condition or similar conditions.
* **Review of Systems**: Describe the patient's current health status, including any symptoms or signs that need attention.
* **Signature of Physician**: Sign and date this section to confirm that you have reviewed and discussed the form with the patient.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Surgery**: Choose a date for the scheduled surgery.
* **Type of Surgery**: Select the specific procedure the patient will undergo.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure the best possible outcome for the patient.
* If you are unsure about any information, consult with colleagues or superiors before submitting the form.
* Remember to review and discuss the form with the patient to ensure they understand their situation and the procedure.
