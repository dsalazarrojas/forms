# Cervical Collar Removal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document the removal of a cervical collar in a patient. It is used to obtain patient consent, clinical details, and other relevant information before proceeding with the removal. This form is meant to be used as part of the standard procedure to ensure patient safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you have the necessary information to proceed with the patient's care.
2. Complete the form by providing the required information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (`patient_name`, text, required):** Enter the full legal name of the patient.
* **Patient ID (`patient_id`, text, optional):** Enter the hospital or record identifier for the patient, if applicable.
* **Attending Physician (`attending_physician`, text, required):** Enter the name of the clinician approving the removal.
* **Removal Date (`removal_date`, date, required):** Enter the scheduled date of collar removal.
* **Clinical Reason (`clinical_reason`, text, required):** Provide a brief description of the clinical justification for the removal.
* **Risks Explained (`risks_explained`, select_one, required):** Confirm that the patient has been discussed the risks and benefits of the procedure. Select "True" if the discussion was conducted, "False" otherwise.
* **Patient Consent (`patient_consent`, select_one, required):** Confirm patient consent for the procedure. Select "Consent given" if the patient agrees, "Consent withheld" if they do not.
* **Post-Removal Instructions (`post_removal_instructions`, text, optional):** Provide care and follow-up guidance for the patient after the procedure.
* **Clinician Signature (`clinician_signature`, text, required):** Enter your typed name as the clinician confirming the procedure.

## Tips
Be sure to review the form carefully before proceeding with the patient's care, and ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
