# Patient Encounter Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a patient's medical encounter, including their basic details and the date and time of the encounter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's basic information in the **patient_info** field.
2. Select the **encounter_date** field to choose the date of the medical encounter.
3. Select the **encounter_time** field to choose the time of the medical encounter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_info** (`patient_info`, text, required/optional: false): Enter the patient's name, age, and other relevant information.
* **encounter_date** (`encounter_date`, date, required/optional: false): Select the date of the medical encounter.
* **encounter_time** (`encounter_time`, time, required/optional: false): Select the time of the medical encounter.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the patient's information is recorded correctly.
* If you are unsure about any information, feel free to ask the medical staff for guidance.
* Once you have completed the form, submit it to the medical staff for review and processing.
