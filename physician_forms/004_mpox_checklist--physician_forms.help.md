# mpox_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mpox_checklist form is designed to gather information from healthcare professionals about Mpox, a rare and contagious viral disease. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate section to complete based on your experience and concerns.
2. Enter your information in the relevant fields. 
3. Ensure you complete all required fields (indicated by *).
4. Review your information for accuracy before submitting.
5. Sign and date the form to indicate completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Mpox Guidelines** (`mpox_guidelines`, `text`, *optional*): 
    Enter any relevant guidelines or protocols related to Mpox, such as those from the WHO or CDC.

- **Patient History** (`patient_history`, `text`, *optional*): 
    Describe the patient's medical history, including any pre-existing conditions that may be relevant to Mpox.

- **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `text`, *optional*): 
    List any symptoms or signs of Mpox that you have observed in patients.

- **Contact Trace** (`contact_trace`, `text`, *optional*): 
    Describe any contact tracing activities you have performed or plan to perform in response to Mpox.

- **Response Plan** (`response_plan`, `text`, *optional*): 
    Outline the steps you take in response to Mpox, including any containment efforts or public health measures.

- **Prevention** (`prevention`, `text`, *optional*): 
    Describe any prevention plans or strategies you have implemented to reduce the spread of Mpox.

- **Mpox Checklist Form** (`mpox_checklist_form`, `text`, *optional*): 
    This field is intended for any additional information or notes that you may have about Mpox.

- **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, `text`, *optional*): 
    Sign and date this field to indicate completion of the form.

- **Contact Trace Form** (`contact_trace_form`, `text`, *optional*): 
    Use this field for any additional contact tracing information.

- **Response Plan Form** (`response_plan_form`, `text`, *optional*): 
    Use this field for any additional response plan information.

- **Prevention Plan** (`prevention_plan`, `text`, *optional*): 
    Describe any additional prevention plans or strategies you have implemented.

- **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `text`, *optional*): 
    This field is intended for patient use, if applicable.

## Tips
* Ensure you complete all required fields.
* Review your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your local health authority.

* This form is intended for healthcare professionals only.
