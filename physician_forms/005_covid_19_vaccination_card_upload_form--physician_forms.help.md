# COVID 19 Vaccination Card Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather accurate and up-to-date information from your COVID-19 vaccination card. Please fill in the required fields accurately, and upload a clear photo or scan of your vaccination card.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the "First" section labeled "Patient Information --" and fill in the fields:
	* "Full Name": Your complete legal name as it appears on your identification.
	* "Date of Birth": Your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
	* "Email Address": Your current email address.
	* "Phone Number": Your current phone number.
	* "Patient ID or Medical Record Number": Your clinic patient ID if available (optional).
2. Next, select the "Second" section labeled "Vaccination Card Information --" and fill in the fields:
	* "Vaccine Manufacturer": Select the manufacturer of the vaccine you received from the options provided.
	* "First Dose Date": The date of your first COVID-19 vaccine dose.
	* "Second Dose Date" (if applicable): The date of your second COVID-19 vaccine dose.
	* "Booster Dose Date" (if applicable): The date of your first booster dose.
	* "Number of Additional Booster Doses": The number of additional boosters you have received.
	* "Vaccination Facility Name": The name of the facility where you received your vaccine.
	* "Vaccine Lot Numbers": Lot numbers from your vaccination card (if available).
3. Under the "Third" section labeled "Vaccination Card Upload --", upload a clear photo or scan of your vaccination card.
4. Select the "Fourth" section labeled "Information Verification --" and confirm:
	* "The Information I Provided is Accurate": Confirm that all information is accurate.
	* "I need to correct information": If you need to correct any information.
5. Finally, select the "Fifth" section labeled "Authorization for Record Keeping --" and confirm:
	* "I authorize": Confirm that you authorize this facility to maintain your vaccination records.
	* "I do not authorize": If you do not authorize this facility to maintain your vaccination records.
6. Finally, you may add any "Additional Notes or Comments" if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Patient Information -- Full Name, text, required): Your complete legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (Patient Information -- Date of Birth, date, required): Your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Email Address** (Patient Information -- Email Address, email, required): Your current email address.
* **Phone Number** (Patient Information -- Phone Number, text, required): Your current phone number.
* **Patient ID or Medical Record Number** (Patient Information -- Patient ID or Medical Record Number, text, optional): Your clinic patient ID if available.
* **Vaccine Manufacturer** (Vaccination Card Information -- Vaccine Manufacturer, select_one, required): Select the manufacturer of the vaccine you received from the options provided.
* **First Dose Date** (Vaccination Card Information -- First Dose Date, date, required): The date of your first COVID-19 vaccine dose.
* **Second Dose Date** (Vaccination Card Information -- Second Dose Date, date, optional): The date of your second COVID-19 vaccine dose.
* **Booster Dose Date** (Vaccination Card Information -- Booster Dose Date, date, optional): The date of your first booster dose.
* **Number of Additional Booster Doses** (Vaccination Card Information -- Number of Additional Booster Doses, number, optional): The number of additional boosters you have received.
* **Vaccination Facility Name** (Vaccination Card Information -- Vaccination Facility Name, text, optional): The name of the facility where you received your vaccine.
* **Vaccine Lot Numbers** (Vaccination Card Information -- Vaccine Lot Numbers, text, optional): Lot numbers from your vaccination card (if available).
* **Vaccination Card Image or Document** (Vaccination Card Upload -- Vaccination Card Image or Document, text, required): Upload a clear photo or scan of your vaccination card.
* **Card Image is Clear and Readable** (Vaccination Card Upload -- Card Image is Clear and Readable, select_one, required): Confirm the image quality is sufficient.
* **The Information I Provided is Accurate** (Information Verification -- The Information I Provided is Accurate, select_one, required): Confirm all vaccination information is correct.
* **I need to correct information** (Information Verification -- I need to correct information, select_one, optional): If you need to correct any information.
* **Authorization for Record Keeping** (Authorization for Record Keeping -- , select_one, required): Confirm that you authorize this facility to maintain your vaccination records.
* **I do not authorize** (Authorization for Record Keeping -- I do not authorize, select_one, optional): If you do not authorize this facility to maintain your vaccination records.
* **Additional Notes or Comments** (Additional Notes or Comments, text, optional): Any additional information about your vaccination.

## Tips

* Please ensure the uploaded image is clear and readable before confirming.
* If you have any doubts or uncertainties, please reach out to the facility staff for assistance.
