# Hip Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hip Referral Form is used by physicians to refer patients to specialists in the healthcare industry. This form collects relevant information from the patient and physician sides to facilitate effective communication and coordination between healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information section (Patient Information) with the relevant details.
2. Specify the referral reason for the patient's care.
3. Choose the specialist needed for the patient's case (e.g., Orthopedic Surgeon or Hospital).
4. Select the preferred referral method (e.g., Phone, Email, or In-person).
5. Provide any additional comments from the patient.
6. Enter comments from the physician.
7. If applicable, enter comments from the specialist.
8. Sign and stamp the form with the physician's signature.
9. Ensure that the specialist receives the referral information, and schedule a follow-up appointment if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, text, optional): Briefly explain the reason for the referral.
* **Specialist Needed** (`specialist_needed`, select_one, optional): Choose the specialist needed for the patient's care (e.g., Orthopedic Surgeon or Hospital).
* **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the referral.
* **Referral Method** (`referral_method`, select_one, optional): Select the preferred method of referral (e.g., Phone, Email, or In-person).
* **Patient Comments** (`patient_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments from the patient.
* **Physician Comments** (`physician_comments`, text, optional): Enter comments from the physician.
* **Specialist Comments** (`specialist_comment`, text, optional): If applicable, enter comments from the specialist.
* **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, text, optional): Sign and date the form with the physician's signature.
* **Specialist Signature** (`specialist_signature`, text, optional): Sign and date the form with the specialist's signature.
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, optional): Enter the physician's name.
* **Specialist Name** (`specialist_name`, text, optional): Enter the specialist's name.
* **Physician Title** (`physician_title`, select_one, optional): Select the physician's title (e.g., Dr, Dr. (M.D.), Dr. (D.O.), or Dr. (Ph.D.)).
* **Specialist Title** (`specialist_title`, select_one, optional): Select the specialist's title (e.g., Dr, Dr. (M.D.), Dr. (D.O.), or Dr. (Ph.D.)).
* **Specialist Email** (`specialist_email`, email, optional): Enter the specialist's email address.
* **Specialist Phone** (`specialist_phone`, text, optional): Enter the specialist's phone number.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Specialist Address** (`specialist_address`, text, optional): Enter the specialist's address.
* **Specialist Facility** (`specialist_facility`, text, optional): Enter the specialist's facility information.
* **Specialist Notes** (`specialist_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments from the specialist.
* **Referral Number** (`referral_number`, text, optional): Enter a unique referral number.
* **Date of Referral** (`date_of_referral`, date, optional): Enter the date of the referral.
* **Date of Last Contact** (`date_of_last_contact`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last contact with the specialist.
* **Next Date of Contact** (`next_date_of_contact`, date, optional): Enter the next date of contact with the specialist.
* **Physician Signature Stamp** (`physician_signature_stamp`, text, optional): Sign and date the form with the physician's signature stamp.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be filled in if relevant, but are not required for the form submission.
