# Medical Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Product Order Form is designed to be used by physicians and healthcare professionals to order medical products for their practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Data** field with any relevant information about your order.
2. Select one or multiple **Medical Products** from the provided list.
3. Enter the quantity of the selected medical products in the **Quantity** field.
4. Provide your **Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** contact information for billing purposes.
5. Select your preferred **Payment Method**: Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
6. Enter the **Payment Amount** for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (text, not required): A field for you to provide any additional information about your order.
* **Medical Product** (select_multiple, not required): Choose one or multiple medical products for your order. The available options are:
	+ product1
	+ product2
	+ product3
* **Quantity** (number, not required): Enter the quantity of the selected medical products.
* **Name** (text, not required): Enter your name for billing purposes.
* **Email** (email, not required): Enter your email address for billing and communication purposes.
* **Phone** (text, not required): Enter your phone number for billing and communication purposes.
* **Billing Address** (text, not required): Enter your billing address for shipping and taxation purposes.
* **Payment Method** (select_one, not required): Choose your preferred payment method: 
	+ Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Bank Transfer
* **Payment Amount** (number, not required): Enter the total payment amount for your order.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have a business agreement or partnership in place before using this form.
- Double-check your order details before submitting the form to avoid errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your account representative or support team.
