# COVID 19 Booster Shot Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The COVID 19 Booster Shot Form is used to gather information for patients who are interested in receiving a COVID-19 booster shot. This form helps healthcare providers understand a patient's medical history, safety concerns, and consent to receive the vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide the required personal information:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Date of Birth
	* Email Address
	* Phone Number

2. If you have any medical history or health conditions, please select all that apply:
	* Diabetes
	* Heart disease
	* Respiratory issues
	* Immunocompromised
	* Other conditions
	* None

3. Answer the following questions:
	* What type of vaccine did you receive initially?
	* Are you interested in receiving a booster dose?
	* Do you have any known allergies to vaccine components?
	* Did you experience any side effects from your initial vaccination?
	* If yes, please describe any side effects you experienced

4. If you have any safety concerns about the booster, please select one of the following options:
	* No concerns
	* Minor concerns
	* Significant concerns
	* Need to discuss with doctor

5. Finally, please indicate your consent to receive the COVID-19 booster vaccine:
	* I agree
	* I do not agree

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required): Your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Required): Your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Email Address** (Required): The email address where you would like to receive your vaccination records.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Initial COVID-19 Vaccination** (Required): The date you received your initial COVID-19 vaccine.
* **What type of vaccine did you receive initially?** (Required): Select the type of vaccine you received initially (Pfizer, Moderna, Johnson & Johnson, or Other).
* **Are you interested in receiving a booster dose?** (Required): Select one of the following options: Yes, definitely, Maybe, need more information, No, not at this time, or Unsure.
* **Do you have any known allergies to vaccine components?** (Required): Select one of the following options: No known allergies, Allergic to eggs, Allergic to gelatin, Other allergies, or No known allergies).
* **Do you have any current health conditions?** (Optional): Select all that apply: Diabetes, Heart disease, Respiratory issues, Immunocompromised, Other conditions, or None.
* **Did you experience any side effects from your initial vaccination?** (Required): Select one of the following options: No side effects, Mild side effects, Moderate side effects, Severe side effects.
* **Please describe any side effects you experienced** (Optional): If you selected any of the above side effects, please describe them in this field.
* **Do you have any safety concerns about the booster?** (Optional): Select one of the following options: No concerns, Minor concerns, Significant concerns, or Need to discuss with doctor.
* **Do you consent to receive the COVID-19 booster vaccine?** (Required): Select one of the following options: I agree or I do not agree.
* **Physician Notes** (Optional): For medical personnel use only.
* **Allergies to vaccine components?** (Optional): If you answered "Allergic to eggs" or "Allergic to gelatin," you may need to take extra precautions when receiving the vaccine.
