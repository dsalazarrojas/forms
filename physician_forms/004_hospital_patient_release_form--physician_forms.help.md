# Hospital Patient Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Patient Release Form is used to document the release of a patient from the hospital, ensuring accurate and up-to-date patient information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field. This is the name of the person being released from the hospital.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field. This is the date of the patient's birth.
3. Enter the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" field. This is a brief description of the patient's medical conditions, allergies, or other relevant information.
4. Enter the patient's contact information in the "Patient Contact" field. This can include the patient's address, phone number, or other contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person being released from the hospital.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the patient's birth, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the patient's medical conditions, allergies, or other relevant information.
* **Patient Contact** (`patient_contact`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's contact information, including their address, phone number, or other details.
