# Outpatient Imaging Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outpatient Imaging Order Form is used to order imaging procedures for patients in an outpatient setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the "Patient Information" field, including name, address, and any relevant medical history.
2. Select the imaging order details in the "Order Details" field, specifying the type of imaging procedure required.
3. Choose the imaging facility and radiologist for the exam in the "Imaging Facility" and "Radiologist Name" fields, respectively.
4. Schedule the exam by entering the exam date and time in the "Exam Date" and "Exam Time" fields.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the order in the "Order Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Outpatient Imaging Order**: Enter a brief description of the imaging order, such as "MRI for knee replacement" or "CT scan for lung cancer diagnosis".
* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, address, and any relevant medical history, such as current medications or allergies.
* **Order Details**: Select the type of imaging procedure required, such as "MRI", "CT scan", or "Ultrasound".
* **Imaging Type**: Choose the type of imaging modality, such as "MRI", "CT", or "X-ray".
* **Exam Date**: Enter the date of the exam, in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Exam Time**: Enter the time of the exam, in the format "HH:MM".
* **Exam Location**: Enter the location where the exam will take place, such as "Radiology Department" or "Outpatient Clinic".
* **Imaging Facility**: Choose the facility where the exam will be performed, such as "Hospital X" or "Private Clinic".
* **Radiologist Name**: Enter the name of the radiologist who will interpret the exam results.
* **Order Notes**: Enter any additional comments or instructions about the order, such as "Please perform contrast study" or "Use high-dose sedation".

Note: The patient's address is not strictly required, but it's recommended to enter it for billing and insurance purposes.
