# Palliative Care Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Palliative Care Form is a document that helps healthcare professionals gather and track vital information about a patient's medical history, current condition, and treatment plans. It is used to ensure that the patient receives comprehensive care that is tailored to their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and provide detailed information for each field.
2. If you are unsure about a section or have any questions, please consult with the healthcare team.
3. Use the provided fields to document the patient's medical history, current condition, and treatment plans.
4. Please note that all sections are optional, but providing as much information as possible will help the healthcare team better understand the patient's needs.
