# HPI Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information, including name, age, disease status, dates of treatment, physician's name, sex, medical notes, and medical history. It may be used by healthcare professionals to record and report patient data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Input the patient's age in the "Patient Age" field.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for each disease listed in the "Disease List" field.
4. Enter the start date of treatment in the "Start Date" field.
5. Enter the end date of treatment in the "End Date" field.
6. Input the physician's name in the "Physician Name" field.
7. Select the patient's sex from the options in the "Patient Sex" field.
8. Enter any relevant medical notes in the "Medical Notes" field.
9. Provide a brief medical history in the "Medical History" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical records.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, number, required: false): Input the patient's age in years.
* **Disease List** (`disease_list`, select_multiple, required: false): Select each disease that the patient has, using "Yes" for each condition that applies.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of treatment or condition onset.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of treatment or condition resolution.
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the treating physician or healthcare professional.
* **Patient Sex** (`patient_sex`, select_one, required: false): Select the patient's sex from the options: Male, Female, or Other.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant medical notes or comments.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Provide a brief medical history for the patient.
