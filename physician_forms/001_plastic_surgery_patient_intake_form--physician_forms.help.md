# Plastic Surgery Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for patients undergoing plastic surgery procedures to provide essential information about their medical history, condition, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand the purpose and fields.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
3. Answer all required fields (marked with an asterisk (\*).
4. Do not hesitate to ask if you have any questions or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for providing your personal details, such as name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for describing your medical history, including any previous surgeries, allergies, or medical conditions.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for explaining your current medical condition or reason for undergoing plastic surgery.
* **Procedure Understanding** (`procedure_understanding`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for understanding the procedure and its risks, benefits, and any questions you may have.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for confirming your understanding and consent for the procedure.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for providing your contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Medical History Allergies** (`medical_history_allergies`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for listing any allergies or sensitivities you may have.
* **Medical History Medications** (`medical_history_medications`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for listing any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical History Surgeries** (`medical_history_surgeries`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for listing any previous surgeries you have undergone.
* **Medical History Families** (`medical_history_families`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for providing information about your family medical history.
* **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for the doctor or medical staff to take notes about your consultation.
* **Doctor Notes** (`doctor_notes`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for the doctor or medical staff to take notes about your condition and treatment.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for you to sign and confirm that you have read and understood the form.

## Tips

* Please answer all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask the doctor or medical staff.
* This form is for your safety and well-being, and completing it carefully will help ensure a smooth and safe experience.
