# Referring Clinician Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referring Clinician Survey is a form used to capture information from referring clinicians about new patients being referred to our institution. This form helps us understand the context of each referral, including the patient's presenting condition and relevant background information. The data collected from this form will be used to support the care of the new patient.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Ensure you have all necessary information about the new patient before starting.
- Fill out the form in sections, working from top to bottom.
- Enter your contact information (Referring Clinician Phone and Referring Clinician Email) so we can get back to you with any questions or concerns.
- Fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible, as this information will be used to support the new patient's care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Presentation Date** (`new_presentation_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date the new patient first presented to you.
* **Presenting Physician** (`presenting_physician`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the physician who first saw the new patient.
* **New Patient Info** (`new_patient_info`, number, optional):
	+ This field appears to be intended for a unique identifier or a numerical value associated with the new patient. Please provide the relevant information.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, text, optional):
	+ Enter how the new patient came to be referred to our institution (e.g. emergency department, specialist referral, etc.).
* **New Patient Name** (`new_patient_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the new patient.
* **New Patient Address** (`new_patient_address`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the address of the new patient.
* **New Physician** (`new_physician`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the physician currently caring for the new patient.
* **Referring Clinician Phone** (`referring_clinician_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your clinic's phone number where you can be reached for follow-up.
* **Referring Clinician Email** (`referring_clinician_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address where you can be reached for follow-up.
* **New Presentation Diagnosis** (`new_presentation_diagnosis`, text, optional):
	+ Enter a brief description or diagnosis related to the new patient's presentation.
## Tips
- If you are unsure about any information, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- If you need to refer additional patients from the same clinician, you can simply duplicate this form, making sure to update the new patient's information accordingly.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form or the referral process, please don't hesitate to contact us.
