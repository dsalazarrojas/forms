# COVID 19 Test Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a COVID-19 test for yourself or someone else. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information so that healthcare workers can understand your needs and provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on "Start Form" to begin filling out the form.
2.  Fill out all required fields marked with \*.
3.  Make sure to enter correct dates in MM/DD/YYYY format.
4.  For select fields, choose the correct option from the dropdown menu.
5.  Click on "Submit" to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Request Date** (\*): Date of request for the test.
    *   Please enter the date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **Patient First Name** (\*): Legal first name of the patient.
    *   Enter the patient's first name.
*   **Patient Last Name** (\*): Legal last name of the patient.
    *   Enter the patient's last name.
*   **Date of Birth** (\*): Date of birth of the patient in MM/DD/YYYY format.
    *   Enter the date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **Gender**: Select the patient's gender.
    *   Choose from: Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer not to say, Other.
*   **Phone Number** (\*): Contact number of the patient.
    *   Enter the patient's phone number.
*   **Email Address** (\*): Contact email of the patient.
    *   Enter the patient's email address.
*   **Home Address** (\*): Street address of the patient.
    *   Enter the patient's home address.
*   **City** (\*): City of the patient's address.
    *   Enter the city of the patient's address.
*   **State** (\*): State of the patient's address.
    *   Enter the state of the patient's address.
*   **ZIP Code** (\*): Postal code of the patient's address.
    *   Enter the ZIP code of the patient's address.
*   **Occupation**: Job or profession of the patient.
    *   Enter the patient's occupation.
*   **Are you a healthcare worker**: Check if you are a healthcare worker.
    *   Choose from: True, False.
*   **Are you an essential worker**: Check if you are an essential worker.
    *   Choose from: True, False.
*   **Insurance Provider**: Insurance company of the patient.
    *   Enter the patient's insurance provider.
*   **Insurance Policy Number**: Member ID of the patient's insurance.
    *   Enter the patient's insurance policy number.
*   **Primary Care Physician Name**: Name of the patient's primary care physician.
    *   Enter the patient's primary care physician name.
*   **Physician Phone**: Office number of the patient's primary care physician.
    *   Enter the patient's physician phone number.
*   **Primary Reason for Test Request**: Reason for requesting the test.
    *   Choose from: Experiencing COVID-19 symptoms, Close contact with positive case, Required for work, Required for school, Required for travel, Required for medical procedure, Required for event attendance, Personal precaution.
*   **Describe your symptoms**: If applicable, describe your symptoms.
    *   Enter the patient's symptoms.
*   **When did symptoms begin**: Date when symptoms began.
    *   Enter the date of symptom onset.
*   **Describe your exposure**: If applicable, describe your exposure.
    *   Enter the patient's exposure details.
*   **Date of exposure**: Date of exposure.
    *   Enter the date of exposure if known.
*   **Have you been tested for COVID-19 before**: Check if you have been tested for COVID-19 before.
    *   Choose from: No never tested, Yes tested negative, Yes tested positive, Yes multiple times.
*   **Date of most recent test**: Date of most recent test.
    *   Enter the date of the most recent test if applicable.
*   **Result of most recent test**: Result of the most recent test if known.
    *   Choose from: Negative, Positive, Pending, Not sure.
*   **What is your vaccination status**: COVID-19 vaccination status.
    *   Choose from: Fully vaccinated with booster, Fully vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, Not vaccinated.
*   **Do you have any high-risk medical conditions**: Check if you have any underlying conditions.
    *   Choose from: None, Heart disease, Lung disease, Diabetes, Obesity, Cancer, Kidney disease, Immunocompromised, Pregnancy.
*   **Preferred Testing Method**: Preferred testing method for the patient.
    *   Choose from: Nasal swab at clinic, Drive-through testing, Home collection kit, Saliva test.
*   **How urgent is your test request**: Priority level of the test request.
    *   Choose from: Routine - within 5 days, Soon - within 3 days, Urgent - within 24 hours, STAT - immediate.
*   **Additional Comments**: Other information about the patient.
    *   Enter any additional comments.
*   **Signature Date**: Date the form was signed.
    *   Enter the date the form was signed in MM/DD/YYYY format.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Double-check all fields for errors before submitting.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask a healthcare worker for help.
- This form is only for COVID-19 test requests, not for other medical tests or services.
