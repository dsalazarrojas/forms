# Patient Supplies Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Supplies Order Form is used to collect and store information about medical supplies for patients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Information**: Provide details about the patient, including their name and ID (if applicable).
2. **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
3. **Patient ID**: Enter the patient's identification number (if applicable).
4. **Medical Supply Information**: Enter a brief description of the medical supply required, such as "gauze pads" or "bandages".
5. **Supply ID**: Enter the specific ID number for the medical supply (if applicable).
6. **Supply Name**: Enter the name of the medical supply, such as "antiseptic wipes" or "dressings".
7. **Cost**: Enter the cost of the medical supply.
8. **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the medical supply needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's identification number (if applicable).
* **Medical Supply Information** (`medical_supply_information`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the medical supply required.
* **Supply ID** (`supply_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the specific ID number for the medical supply (if applicable).
* **Supply Name** (`supply_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the medical supply, such as "antiseptic wipes" or "dressings".
* **Cost** (`cost`, number, required/optional): Enter the cost of the medical supply.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required/optional): Enter the quantity of the medical supply needed.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field to avoid errors or delays in processing the order.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the medical supply team for assistance.
