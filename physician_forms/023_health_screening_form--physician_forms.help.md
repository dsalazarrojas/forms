# Health Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your health status, contact details, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Begin by selecting a page from the available options. The current page is displayed first.
2. For each field, enter your response according to the options provided. If the field is a select option, choose one of the available choices. If the field is a date or time field, enter the correct date or time in the format specified.
3. When you have completed all the fields on the current page, click the "Next" button to proceed to the next page.
4. Continue filling out the form until you reach the last page.
5. Review your responses before submitting the form.
