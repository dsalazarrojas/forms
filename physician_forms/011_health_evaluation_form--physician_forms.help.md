# Health Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document a patient's health evaluation details in a structured and efficient manner. It's an essential tool for healthcare professionals to gather and analyze information to provide the best possible care for the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form to understand its sections and fields.
* Answer each question thoroughly and accurately, based on the patient's health status.
* Use the provided options for questions that require multiple choices (e.g., select_one, select_multiple).
* For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible.
* Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient History** (`patient_history`, note, optional): This field is a free-text area where you can document any relevant information about the patient's medical history, allergies, or previous medical conditions.
* **Lifestyle** (`lifestyle`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks if the patient has any lifestyle factors that may impact their health, such as smoking or excessive alcohol consumption. Choose all that apply.
* **Patient Lifestyle** (`patient_lifestyle`, text, optional): In this field, describe the patient's lifestyle habits, such as their diet, exercise routine, or stress levels.
* **Health Service** (`health_service`, select_one, optional): This field asks if the patient receives any health services, such as physiotherapy or counseling. Choose one that applies.
* **Needs** (`needs`, select_multiple, optional): This field identifies the patient's needs, such as requiring assistance with daily activities or having mobility issues. Choose all that apply.
* **Patient Needs** (`patient_needs`, text, optional): In this field, describe the patient's specific needs in more detail.
* **Patient Goals** (`patient_goals`, text, optional): This field allows the patient to set and describe their short-term and long-term goals.
* **Patient Obstacles** (`patient_obstacles`, text, optional): Describe any obstacles the patient is facing that may impact their progress towards their goals.
* **Physician Goals** (`physician_goals`, text, optional): This field is where the physician can describe their treatment goals and objectives.
* **Physician Obstacles** (`physician_obstacles`, text, optional): Describe any obstacles the physician is facing that may impact their treatment plan.
* **Physician Recommendations** (`physician_recommendations`, note, optional): In this field, provide any recommendations for the patient's treatment or care.
* **Communication Plan** (`communication_plan`, select_multiple, optional): This field determines the frequency and method of communication between the patient and healthcare provider. Choose all that apply.
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`, select_multiple, optional): This field identifies the medications the patient is currently taking. Choose all that apply.
* **Medication List Items** (`medication_list_items`, text, optional): In this field, list the specific medications the patient is taking, including dosages and frequencies.
