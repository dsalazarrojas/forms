# Mental Health Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare professionals understand your mental health status, allowing them to provide you with the most suitable support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. The survey consists of 16 questions, which are divided into several sections.
3. Questions 1-6 are about your current mental health status, while questions 7-16 are follow-up questions that will help us understand any changes or developments since the last assessment.
