**Hypnosis Intake Form - Help Guide**
=====================================

**Purpose**
-----------

The Hypnosis Intake Form is used to collect essential information from clients about their medical history, concerns, and goals. This form is crucial in helping our hypnotherapists understand the client's needs and provide personalized guidance and support.

**How To Complete This Form**
---------------------------

1. **Client Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Contact Number**: Provide your contact information to ensure we can reach you if needed.
3. **Email**: Enter your email address to enable us to communicate with you.
4. **Medical History**: Describe any medical conditions, allergies, or other health issues you have.
5. **Medical Notes**: Add any additional medical notes or details you believe are relevant.
6. **Reason For Visit**: Explain why you are seeking hypnotherapy services.
7. **Medical Procedure**: Describe any medical procedures you have undergone or are currently undergoing.
8. **Client Goals**: List your goals for seeking hypnotherapy services.
9. **Client Concerns**: Share any concerns or issues you are currently experiencing.
10. **Medical History Other**: If you have any other medical history not listed above, describe it here.
11. **Medical History Other Description**: Provide a brief description of your medical history.
12. **Medical History Other Notes**: Add any additional notes about your medical history.
