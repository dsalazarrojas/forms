# oxygen_and_enzyme_treatment_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from clients regarding oxygen and enzyme treatments. It ensures that clients are aware of the potential risks and benefits associated with these treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your phone number and the details of the oxygen treatment you wish to receive.
3. Indicate the duration of the treatment.
4. Report any potential risks of allergic reaction, pain, or burning you are aware of.
5. Follow the aftercare instructions as provided.
6. Specify how you can be reached for aftercare contact.
7. Arrange for a follow-up appointment if necessary.
8. Read and agree to the client understand section.
9. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client First Name (text, required)**: Your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Client Last Name (text, required)**: Your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Client Email (email, required)**: Your email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Client Phone (text, required)**: Your phone number where you can be reached for communication.
* **Oxygen Treatment (text, required)**: The type of oxygen treatment you wish to receive.
* **Enzyme Treatment (text, required)**: The type of enzyme treatment you wish to receive.
* **Treatment Duration (text, required)**: The length of time you will be receiving the treatment.
* **Risk of Allergic Reaction (text, required)**: If you are aware of any potential risks of allergic reaction, report them here.
* **Risk of Pain (text, required)**: If you are aware of any potential risks of pain, report them here.
* **Risk of Burning (text, required)**: If you are aware of any potential risks of burning, report them here.
* **Aftercare Instructions (text, required)**: Follow the aftercare instructions provided by the clinician.
* **Aftercare Contact (text, required)**: Specify how you can be reached for aftercare contact.
* **Follow Up (text, required)**: Arrange for a follow-up appointment if necessary.
* **Client Understand (text, required)**: Read and agree that you understand the risks and benefits of the treatment.
* **Client Agree (text, required)**: Sign to confirm that you agree to the treatment.
* **Client Sign Date (date, required)**: Date you sign the form to confirm your consent.
* **Client Sign Time (time, required)**: Time you sign the form to confirm your consent.
* **Client Signature (text, required)**: Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Clinician Sign Date (date, required)**: Date the clinician signs the form to confirm their endorsement of the treatment.
* **Clinician Name (text, required)**: The name of the clinician providing the treatment.
* **Clinician Title (text, required)**: The title of the clinician providing the treatment.
* **Clinician Signature (text, required)**: Sign the clinician's name to confirm their endorsement of the treatment.

## Tips

- Ensure you understand the risks and benefits of the treatment before signing the form.
- Review the aftercare instructions carefully.
- Report any potential risks you are aware of.
- Follow up with a clinician if necessary.
- Sign and date the form as required.
