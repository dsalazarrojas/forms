# Sample Telemedicine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template for patients to provide informed consent for telemedicine services. It collects essential information and allows patients to sign their consent in multiple ways.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to use the format "name@email.com".
4. Read the "Informed Consent" section and type in your agreement to it.
5. Select the desired signature option from the "Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address in the format "name@email.com" for contact purposes.
* **Informed Consent** (note, required): Type in your agreement to the informed consent, which is a statement of understanding and acceptance of the telemedicine services.
* **Signature** (select multiple, required): Choose one or more of the signature options to signify your agreement.
