# Telehealth Consent Form Colorado - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Telehealth Consent Form is designed to gather essential information from patients for telehealth services in Colorado. This form is used to ensure that the patient has the necessary details and consents in place for the telehealth provider to offer care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* **Patient Name**: Enter your name as it is written on your official identification.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
2. Provide contact information:
	* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address.
3. Add emergency contact details:
	* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you want to contact in case of an emergency.
	* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
	* **Relationship**: Select the relationship you have with your emergency contact.
4. Provide medical history:
	* **Medical Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of your medical insurance provider.
	* **Primary Care Provider**: Enter the name of your primary care provider.
	* **Address**: Enter your address.
	* **Medical History**: Enter any medical history you want to share with your healthcare provider.
	* **Medications**: Select any medications you are currently taking.
	* **Medical Condition**: Select any medical conditions you have.
5. Select telehealth services and provider details:
	* **Telehealth Services Used**: Select the telehealth services you have used.
	* **Provider Type**: Select the type of provider you have been seeing.
	* **Location**: Select where you have been receiving telehealth services.
6. Language and Insurance:
	* **Language**: Select your primary language.
	* **Insurance**: Select your insurance type.
7. Telehealth visit details:
	* **Date Last Telehealth Visit**: Enter the date of your last telehealth visit.
	* **Date First Telehealth Visit**: Enter the date of your first telehealth visit.
	* **Number of Telehealth Visits**: Enter the number of telehealth visits you have had.
8. Additional details:
	* **Provider Name**: Enter the name of your telehealth provider.
	* **Telehealth Consent**: Please sign this form to indicate your consent to receive telehealth services.
	* **Electronic Signature**: Date and sign this form to confirm your identity.
	* **Signature**: Date and sign this form to confirm your consent.

## Tips

* Please answer each field truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your healthcare provider.
* Please sign and date the form to confirm your identity and consent.
* If you have any medical conditions or medications that are not listed, please add them in the medical history field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your healthcare provider.
