# Emergency Visit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from patients during emergency visits. It captures details about the patient's identity, visit details, contact information, medical records, and insurance coverage. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure efficient and proper processing of the patient's emergency visit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their official documents or identification.
2. **Date of Visit**: Provide the date of the emergency visit. This date should match the date of the visit.
3. **Time of Arrival**: Enter the time the patient arrived for the emergency visit.
4. **Contact Person**: Enter the person to contact in case of emergencies or follow-ups.
5. **Patient Age**: Enter the patient's age.
6. **Sex**: Select the patient's sex.
7. **Visit Reason**: Provide a brief description of the reason for the emergency visit.
8. **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address.
9. **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the person listed as the contact person.
10. **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
11. **Medical Record ID**: If applicable, enter the patient's medical record ID number.
12. **Visit Notes**: Enter any important notes or information about the visit.
13. **Patient Insurance**: Select all applicable insurance coverage for the patient (e.g., Medicare, Medicaid, Private Insurance).
14. **Emergency Services**: Select all applicable emergency services provided (e.g., ER, Clinic, Hospital).
15. **Physician Name**: Enter the name of the physician attending to the patient.
16. **Physician Phone**: Enter the physician's phone number.
17. **Physician Email**: Enter the physician's email address.
18. **Hospital Location**: Select the hospital location (e.g., New York, Los Angeles, Chicago).
19. **Patient Address 2**: Enter the patient's alternate address.
20. **Visit Reason Additional**: Enter any additional information about the visit reason.
21. **Insurance Company**: If applicable, enter the patient's insurance company name.
22. **Insurance Card Number**: If applicable, enter the patient's insurance card number.
23. **Visit Notes 2**: Enter any additional notes or information about the visit.
24. **Sex 2**: Select the patient's sex.
25. **Insurance Card Copy**: If applicable, attach or upload a copy of the patient's insurance card.
26. **Medical Record ID**: If applicable, enter the patient's medical record ID number.
27. **Visit Notes 3**: Enter any additional notes or information about the visit.
