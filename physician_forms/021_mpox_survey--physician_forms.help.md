# Mpox Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Survey is designed to gather information on public awareness and understanding of the disease, its risk factors, symptoms, and the effectiveness of public health measures and control measures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and fill in the required information.
* For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
* For text fields, provide detailed answers to the best of your ability.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Public Awareness** (public_awareness, text, optional):
  This question asks about your level of awareness regarding Mpox and its symptoms.
* **Risk Factors** (risk_factors, select_multiple, optional):
  Please select all the risk factors that you believe are associated with Mpox.
* **Symptoms** (symptoms, select_one, optional):
  Select the symptom that you are experiencing or have experienced in the past.
* **Healthcare Access** (healthcare_access, text, optional):
  Provide information about your access to healthcare services related to Mpox.
* **Prevention Measures** (prevention_measures, number, optional):
  Answer with the number of prevention measures you have taken against Mpox.
* **Public Health Measures** (public_health_measures, select_one, optional):
  Select the public health measure you think is the most effective.
* **Control Measures** (control_measures, select_multiple, optional):
  Please select all control measures that you believe are effective against Mpox.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments, text, optional):
  Provide any additional comments or information about Mpox that you think is relevant.
* **Email** (email, email, optional):
  Enter a valid email address if you wish to be contacted for further information.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional):
  Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted for further information.
