# medical-summary-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather medical information from patients to aid healthcare providers in understanding their overall health status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To fill out this form, start by clicking on the first field, "Patient Name", and enter the patient's full name.
2. Click on the "Medical History" field and enter any previous medical history or health issues the patient has experienced.
3. For each subsequent "Medical Conditions" field, describe any conditions or health issues the patient has. There are multiple fields for this purpose to accommodate multiple conditions.
4. In the "Medical Summary" field, enter a brief summary of the patient's current medical status.
5. If applicable, list the patient's current medications.
6. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (1)**: Enter the patient's full name, including first and last name.
* **Medical History (2)**: Enter any previous medical history or health issues the patient has experienced. This can include previous surgeries, illnesses, or treatments.
* **Medications (3)**: If applicable, list the patient's current medications.
* **Social Security Number (4)**: If the patient has provided their social security number, enter it here. Please note that this field is optional.
* **Medical Conditions (5-10)**: For each of these fields, describe any conditions or health issues the patient has. There are multiple fields to accommodate multiple conditions.
* **Medical History 2-9 (6-19)**: For each of these fields, enter any additional medical history or health issues the patient has. These fields are similar to the medical history field but are for additional information.
* **Medical Summary (8)**: Enter a brief summary of the patient's current medical status.
* **Date of Birth (9)**: Enter the patient's date of birth in mm/dd/yyyy format.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have not already completed this form for the same patient.
* If you are unsure of any information, please contact the patient or their healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form is intended to be a supplement to, not a substitute for, a comprehensive medical evaluation.
* Please enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the best possible care for the patient.
