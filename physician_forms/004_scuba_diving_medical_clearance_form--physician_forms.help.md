**Scuba Diving Medical Clearance Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from a physician about a patient's medical history, clearance for scuba diving, and other relevant medical information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand each section.
2. Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
3. If you have any medical conditions or issues that are not listed, please specify them in the "Other Medical Issues" section.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have reviewed and completed it accurately.
5. Make sure to keep a copy of the completed form for your records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History**: This section is used to gather information about your medical history. Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
	+ **Medical Clearance**: Check "Yes" if you have been cleared for scuba diving or "No" if you have not been cleared.
	+ **Medical Conditions**: Select all conditions that apply to you.
	+ **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Certifications**: This section is used to gather information about your medical certifications. Please select all certifications that apply to you.
* **Diving History**: This section is used to gather information about your diving history. Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
	+ **Diving Environment**: Select the type of diving you are certified for (Open Water, Cavediver, Wreck Diving, Cave Diving, or Ice Cavern Diving).
	+ **Scuba Gear**: Select the type of scuba gear you will be using.
* **Emergency Contact**: This section is used to gather information about your emergency contact. Please provide the contact information of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
	+ **Emergency Contact Relation**: Select the relation of the emergency contact (e.g., spouse, parent, friend).
	+ **Emergency Contact Phone**: Provide the phone number of the emergency contact.
	+ **Emergency Contact Email**: Provide the email of the emergency contact.
* **Physician Signature**: This section is used to gather information about the physician who reviewed and cleared you for scuba diving. Please sign and date this section to confirm that you have been cleared.
	+ **Physician Name**: Provide the name of the physician who reviewed and cleared you.
	+ **Physician License Number**: Provide the license number of the physician who reviewed and cleared you.
* **Certification**: This section is used to confirm that you have been cleared for scuba diving. Please select "Certified" if you have been cleared or "Not Certified" if you have not been cleared.
* **Other Medical Issues**: This section is used to gather information about any other medical issues that are not listed. Please select all issues that apply to you and provide a description of each.

**Tips**

* Please review the form carefully before completing it.
* If you are unsure about any section, please ask a physician or medical professional for guidance.
* Make sure to keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
* If you have any medical conditions that are not listed, please specify them in the "Other Medical Issues" section.
* Please sign and date the form to confirm that you have reviewed and completed it accurately.
