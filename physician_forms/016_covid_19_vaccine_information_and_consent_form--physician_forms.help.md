# COVID 19 Vaccine Information And Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be completed by patients to provide information and consent for COVID-19 vaccination. It ensures that patients understand the purpose and benefits of vaccination, review the risks and side effects, and provide medical history information. The form is essential for healthcare providers to assess the patient's suitability for vaccination and to inform their decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully and ensure you have understood the questions and instructions.
2. Provide your personal details, including your full name, date of birth, email address, and phone number.
3. Answer questions about your medical history, such as allergies, chronic conditions, and previous COVID-19 infection.
4. Review and understand the risks and benefits of the COVID-19 vaccine.
5. Confirm that your questions have been answered by the healthcare provider.
6. Sign the form to authorize vaccination and acknowledge understanding of the risks and benefits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Your complete legal name that you wish to be used for vaccination records.
	+ A text field for you to enter your full name.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
	+ A date field where you enter your birth date.
* **Email Address**: Your email address where you can be contacted.
	+ A text field for your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number for follow-up information.
	+ A text field for your phone number.
* **Current Address**: Your current street address.
	+ A text field for your current address.
* **Important information about the COVID-19 Vaccine**: A note section to review the vaccine's purpose and benefits.
	+ A field to read and understand the vaccine's purpose and benefits.
* **I Understand the Purpose of COVID-19 Vaccination**: Confirm that you understand the purpose of COVID-19 vaccination.
	+ A select-one field with options: Yes, I understand; I have questions; False.
* **I Understand That COVID-19 Vaccines Are Effective**: Confirm that you understand the effectiveness of the COVID-19 vaccine.
	+ A select-one field with options: Yes, I understand; I have questions; False.
* **I Have Reviewed the Benefits of COVID-19 Vaccination**: Confirm that you have reviewed the benefits of COVID-19 vaccination.
	+ A select-one field with options: Yes, I reviewed benefits; I partially reviewed; I have not reviewed.
* **I Have Reviewed the Risks and Possible Side Effects of COVID-19 Vaccination**: Confirm that you have reviewed the risks and possible side effects of the COVID-19 vaccine.
	+ A select-one field with options: Yes, I reviewed risks; I partially reviewed; I have not reviewed.
* **My Questions About COVID-19 Vaccines Have Been Answered**: Confirm that your questions have been answered by the healthcare provider.
	+ A select-one field with options: Yes, all answered; Some answered; No questions asked.
* **Known Allergies**: A text field to list any known allergies to vaccine components.
	+ A field to list any allergies you may have.
* **Current Medications**: A text field to list all medications you are currently taking.
	+ A field to list all medications you are currently taking.
* **Chronic Medical Conditions**: A text field to list any ongoing health conditions.
	+ A field to list any ongoing health conditions.
* **Do You Have a Weakened Immune System**: Confirm that you do not have a weakened immune system.
	+ A select-one field with options: True; False; Unsure.
* **Are You Pregnant or Breastfeeding**: A select-one field to confirm your pregnancy or breastfeeding status.
	+ A select-one field with options: Pregnant; Breastfeeding; Neither; Prefer Not to Say.
* **Have You Had COVID-19 Before**: Confirm that you have not had COVID-19 before.
	+ A select-one field with options: True; False; Unsure.
* **Previous COVID-19 Vaccine Doses**: A number field to indicate the number of doses you have received previously.
	+ A field to enter the number of doses you have received previously.
* **Final Consent**: A select-one field to confirm that you authorize vaccination and understand the risks and benefits.
	+ A select-one field with options: I authorize vaccination; I do not authorize vaccination.
* **I Understand and Accept the Risks and Benefits**: Confirm that you understand and accept the risks and benefits of the COVID-19 vaccine.
	+ A select-one field with options: I understand and accept; I do not understand; I do not accept.
* **This Consent is Voluntary and Not Coerced**: Confirm that you understand that this consent is voluntary and not coerced.
	+ A select-one field with options: Voluntary and not coerced; Under pressure or coerced.
* **Date of Consent**: A date field to record the date of consent.
	+ A field to record the date you are providing consent.
