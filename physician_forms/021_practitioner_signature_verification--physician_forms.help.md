# Practitioner Signature Verification - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Practitioner Signature Verification form is used to verify the identity and authenticity of a practitioner's signature. This form collects necessary information from the practitioner, including clinic details, practitioner information, and verification details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the clinic information section, including any relevant details about the clinic where the practitioner works.
2. Provide your practitioner information, including your name and any other relevant details.
3. Select the verification method used to verify your signature, such as in-person verification or remote verification.
4. Enter the date and time of the verification process.
5. Add any notes or comments about the verification process, if necessary.
6. Provide your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
7. Upload a file containing your signature, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Clinic Information (Page 1)**: This section collects information about the clinic where you work.
* **Practitioner Information (Page 2)**: This section is where you enter your name and other relevant details about yourself.
* **Verification Method (Page 3)**: Select the method used to verify your signature, such as in-person or remote verification.
* **Signed Verification (Page 4)**: Enter the date and time of the verification process.
* **Verification Date (Page 5)**: Enter the date of the verification process.
* **Verification Time (Page 6)**: Enter the time of the day when the verification process took place.
* **Signed Verification Note (Page 7)**: Add any notes or comments about the verification process, if necessary.
* **Email (Page 8)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (Page 9)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Signature File (Page 10)**: Upload a file containing your signature, if required.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to prevent errors and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.
* Use the correct verification method and enter the correct date and time to ensure accurate verification.
* Add any relevant notes or comments to the signed verification note field, if necessary.
