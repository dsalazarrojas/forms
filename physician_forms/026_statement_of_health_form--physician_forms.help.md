# statement_of_health_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information for the purpose of recording and maintaining accurate patient data. It's essential to provide accurate and detailed information to ensure proper patient care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the date of the visit in the "Date Of Visit" field.
3. Enter the patient's ID number in the "Patient ID Number" field.
4. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.
5. Enter the patient's height in inches in the "Height In Inches" field.
6. Enter the patient's weight in pounds in the "Weight In Pounds" field.
7. Enter the patient's blood pressure in the "Blood Pressure" field.
8. List any allergies the patient has in the "Allergies" field.
9. List any medications the patient is currently taking in the "Medications" field.
10. List any medical conditions the patient has in the "Medical Conditions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): Please enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date Of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the visit or examination.
* **Patient ID Number** (`patient_id_number`, number, required: false): Enter the patient's ID number as it appears on their identification.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Height In Inches** (`height_in_inches`, number, required: false): Enter the patient's height in inches.
* **Weight In Pounds** (`weight_in_pounds`, number, required: false): Enter the patient's weight in pounds.
* **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`, text, required: false): Please enter the patient's blood pressure reading in mmHg.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required: false): List any allergies the patient has, including severity and any reactions.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, required: false): List any medications the patient is currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required: false): List any medical conditions the patient has, including diagnoses and current treatments.
