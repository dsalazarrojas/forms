# third_party_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Third-Party Authorization Form is used to obtain permission from a patient or their legal representative to access their medical records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals to access the patient's records.
* Fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Sign and date the form in the presence of the patient or their legal representative.
* Submit the form to the healthcare provider for processing.
