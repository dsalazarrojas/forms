# Foot And Ankle Care Center Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for new patients or visitors of the Foot and Ankle Care Center. It is designed to collect essential information to schedule appointments and provide care.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have all the required information handy before starting the form.
- Fill out the form in a clear and legible handwriting.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your active email address where you can be reached for appointment reminders and communication.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number where we can contact you for appointment scheduling or updates.
* **Date of Birth** (date): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 12/25/1999).
* **Medical History** (note): Briefly describe any relevant medical history or concerns you have.
* **Medical Condition** (select_one): Select the relevant medical condition you are experiencing (e.g., diabetes, foot pain, etc.).
* **Appointment Time** (time): Select a time that works best for your scheduled appointment.

Note: The fields marked as "text" are for free-formatted text entries, whereas the fields marked as "email" and "time" have specific format requirements. Please ensure you meet these requirements for accurate processing.
