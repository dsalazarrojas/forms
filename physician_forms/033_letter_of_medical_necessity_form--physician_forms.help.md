# Letter Of Medical Necessity Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Letter of Medical Necessity Form is a document that provides detailed information about a patient's medical necessity, which is required for various medical insurance coverage, treatments, or other benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.
3. Select the date of medical necessity certification in the "Date Of Medical Necessity Certification" field.
4. Enter the medical necessity certification details in the "Medical Necessity Certification" field.
5. Enter the name of the medical provider in the "Medical Provider" field.
6. Enter the medical insurance provider's name in the "Medical Insurance Provider" field.
7. Enter the patient's address in the "Patient Address" field.
8. Select the medical necessity date in the "Medical Necessity Date" field.
9. Choose the medical necessity type from the options provided in the "Medical Necessity Type" field.
10. Add any additional information or comments in the "Medical Necessity Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Date Of Medical Necessity Certification** (`date_of_medical_necessity_certification`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the medical necessity certification was issued.
* **Medical Necessity Certification** (`medical_necessity_certification`, text, required: false): Enter the details of the medical necessity certification, if applicable.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who issued the medical necessity certification.
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (`medical_insurance_provider`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the medical insurance provider.
* **Medical Insurance Provider Name** (`medical_insurance_provider_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the medical insurance provider.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's address, including street address and zip code.
* **Medical Necessity Date** (`medical_necessity_date`, date, required: false): Select the date when the medical necessity was determined.
* **Medical Necessity Type** (`medical_necessity_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of medical necessity from the options provided.
* **Medical Necessity Note** (`medical_necessity_note`, note, required: false): Add any additional information or comments about the medical necessity.

## Tips
* Ensure that you complete the form accurately and truthfully to avoid any issues with medical insurance coverage or other benefits.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the medical provider or institution for guidance.
* This form is for informational purposes only and is not intended for legal or official medical purposes.
