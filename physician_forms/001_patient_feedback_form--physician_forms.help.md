# Patient Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from patients after their visit to the clinic. This form is used to assess and improve the quality of care provided to patients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Provide accurate information about your visit and feedback.
3. Be thorough in providing your contact information, including your phone number and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, *): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Clinic Visited** (`clinic_visited`, `select_one`, *): Choose the clinic where you had your visit.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, `date`, *): Enter the date of your visit in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, `select_multiple`, *): Describe your overall experience at the clinic.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, ): Enter the name of your doctor.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, *): Share your thoughts and suggestions for improvement.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, ): Provide any additional comments or thoughts.
* **Patient Contact Info** (`patient_contact_info`, `text`, *): Enter your contact information, including your address and phone number.
* **Doctor Contact Info** (`doctor_contact_info`, `text`, ): Enter your doctor's contact information.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, `email`, *): Enter your email address.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, `text`, ): Enter your doctor's email address.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, `text`, *): Enter your phone number.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, `text`, ): Enter your doctor's phone number.
* **Follow up Visit** (`follow_up_visit`, `select_multiple`, *): Choose the follow-up visit option.
* **Next Visit Date** (`next_visit_date`, `date`, *): Enter the date of your next visit.
* **Follow up Status** (`follow_up_status`, `select_one`, *): Choose your follow-up status.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, `text`, *): Enter your patient ID number.
* **Doctor ID** (`doctor_id`, `text`, *): Enter your doctor's ID number.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, *): Enter the department where you visited.
* **Visit Type** (`visit_type`, `select_one`, *): Choose the type of visit.
* **Doctor Specialty** (`doctor_specialty`, `select_one`, *): Choose your doctor's specialty.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, `number`, ): Enter your age in years.
* **Doctor Gender** (`doctor_gender`, `select_one`, *): Choose your doctor's gender.
* **Patient Gender** (`patient_gender`, `select_one`, *): Choose your gender.
* **Follow up Visit Date** (`follow_up_visit_date`, `date`, *): Not applicable.
* **Doctor Contact Info** (`doctor_contact_info`, `text`, *): Not applicable.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, `text`, *): Not applicable.

Please note that some fields are optional but are marked with a colon (:). These fields are not required for form submission but may be useful for additional context and information.
