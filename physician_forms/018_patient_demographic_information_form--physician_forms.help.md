# Patient Demographic Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Patient Demographic Information Form" is used to collect essential details about a patient, including their last name, given names, date of birth, contact information, and medical-related data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the patient's last name in the "patient last name" field.
2. Enter the patient's given names in the "patient given names" field.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth (in the format MM/DD/YYYY) in the "patient dob" field.
4. Enter the patient's email address in the "patient email" field.
5. Enter the patient's phone number in the "patient phone" field.
6. Enter the patient's address in the "patient address" field.
7. If applicable, enter the patient's blood pressure measurement in the "blood pressure" field.
8. If applicable, enter the patient's pulse rate in the "pulse" field.
9. If applicable, enter the patient's artery pressure measurement in the "artery pressure" field.
10. If applicable, enter the patient's heart rate measurement in the "heart rate" field.
11. If applicable, select the type of medication form the patient is taking from the "medication form" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Last Name** (`patient last name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Patient Given Names** (`patient given names`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's given names.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (`patient dob`, date, required/optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Patient Email** (`patient email`, email, required/optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Address** (`patient address`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Blood Pressure** (`blood pressure`, number, required/optional): If applicable, enter the patient's blood pressure measurement.
* **Pulse** (`pulse`, number, required/optional): If applicable, enter the patient's pulse rate.
* **Artery Pressure** (`artery pressure`, number, required/optional): If applicable, enter the patient's artery pressure measurement.
* **Heart Rate** (`heart rate`, number, required/optional): If applicable, enter the patient's heart rate measurement.
* **Medication Form** (`medication form`, select one, required/optional): If applicable, select the type of medication the patient is taking.
