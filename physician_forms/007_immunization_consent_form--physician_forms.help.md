# Immunization Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Immunization Consent Form is a tool for healthcare professionals to collect patient information and obtain consent for immunizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Collect patient information, including their contact details and medical history.
2. Ask the patient about their immunization history, allergies, and current medications.
3. Ask the patient about their family history and medical conditions.
4. Record the patient's immunization record, if applicable.
5. Obtain consent from the patient for any proposed immunizations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's contact details, such as phone number and email.
* **Immunization History** (`immunization_history`, text, required/optional): Ask the patient about their previous immunizations and any relevant medical history.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required/optional): Ask the patient about any allergies or sensitivities they may have, especially related to vaccines or medications.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Ask the patient about any previous medical conditions or illnesses.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, required/optional): Ask the patient about any current medications they are taking.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, text, required/optional): Ask the patient about any family medical history, especially if there are any inherited conditions.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required/optional): Ask the patient about any current medical conditions they may have.
* **Immunization Record** (`immunization_record`, text, required/optional): Ask the patient about their immunization record, if applicable.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required/optional): Obtain consent from the patient for any proposed immunizations.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required/optional): Ask the patient to sign the form to confirm their consent.
