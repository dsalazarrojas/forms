# Teledermatology Patient Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a patient registration form for teledermatology services. It is designed to collect basic patient information for teledermatology consultations. The information provided will be used to schedule and conduct a teledermatology appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through each field carefully and fill in the required information.
- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Your first name.
- **Last Name**: Your last name.
- **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Email**: Your email address for communication and appointment reminders.
- **Phone**: Your phone number for follow-up calls or appointment reminders.
- **Address**: Your address for in-person consultations.
- **Specialty**: The medical specialty you are looking for (e.g. dermatology).
- **Medical License**: Your medical license number (if applicable).
- **Teledermatology Service Type**: Select the type of teledermatology service you are looking for (e.g. Dermatology, General, Skin Care, Cosmetic).
- **Preferred Language**: Select your preferred language for communication (e.g. English, Spanish, French, German).
- **Insurance**: Indicate if you have insurance coverage (True or False).
- **Medical History**: Briefly describe any medical history that may affect your consultation (optional).
- **Allergies**: List any allergies you have (e.g. penicillin, latex).
- **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking (optional).
- **Medical Provider**: Name of your medical provider (optional).
- **Appointment Date**: Date and time of your scheduled appointment (will be scheduled by the system).
- **Appointment Time**: Time of your scheduled appointment (will be scheduled by the system).
- **Notes**: Any additional information you want to provide to the medical provider (optional).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any medical history, allergies, or medications, please provide as much information as possible.
- If you are not sure about any field, please ask your medical provider for guidance.
