# COVID 19 Testing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Testing Consent Form is a document that asks patients to provide their personal and medical information, understand the testing process, and acknowledge their consent for the test.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your complete legal name in the "Patient Full Name" field.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in MM-DD-YYYY format.
3. Enter your current email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your current phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. If applicable, enter your current street address in the "Current Address" field.
6. Review the "Testing and Consent Information" section carefully.
7. Select "I understand and agree" if you understand the purpose of the COVID-19 test.
8. Select "Yes, I understand" if you understand the testing procedure.
9. If you have any health conditions that may affect testing, select the relevant options in the "Health Declaration" field.
10. If you are currently experiencing COVID-19 related symptoms, select the relevant option in the "Current Symptoms" field.
11. If you have any symptoms, describe them in detail in the "Describe Any Symptoms You Are Experiencing" field.
12. If you have any medical history or conditions, please provide relevant information in the "Medical History" field.
13. If you have any chronic medical conditions, select the relevant options in the "Do You Have Any Chronic Medical Conditions" field.
14. If you are taking any medications, list them in the "Current Medications" field.
15. If you have any medication allergies, list them in the "Medication Allergies" field.
16. Review the "Consent Acknowledgment" section carefully.
17. Select "I authorize this test" if you authorize the test.
18. Select "I agree" if you agree to the data privacy terms.
19. If you have health insurance, enter the name and type of insurance provider in the "Insurance Provider or Policy Type" field.
20. If you have an emergency contact, enter their name and phone number in the "Emergency Contact Name" and "Emergency Contact Phone Number" fields.
21. Sign the "Date of Consent" field with the date you are signing this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Full Name**: Enter your complete legal name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Email Address**: Enter your current email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your current phone number.
* **Current Address**: If applicable, enter your current street address.
* **Testing and Consent Information**: Review this section carefully.
* **I understand the purpose of COVID-19 testing**: Select "I understand and agree" if you understand the purpose of the test.
* **I understand the testing procedure**: Select "Yes, I understand" if you understand how the test will be conducted.
* **Health Declaration**: If you have any health conditions that may affect testing, select the relevant options.
* **Current Symptoms**: If you are currently experiencing COVID-19 related symptoms, select the relevant option.
* **Describe Any Symptoms You Are Experiencing**: If you have symptoms, describe them in detail.
* **Medical History**: Please provide relevant medical information.
* **Do You Have Any Chronic Medical Conditions**: Select any relevant conditions.
* **Current Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medication Allergies**: List any medication allergies you have.
* **Consent Acknowledgment**: Review this section carefully.
* **I Authorize the COVID-19 Test**: Select "I authorize this test" if you authorize the test.
* **I Agree to Data Privacy Terms**: Select "I agree" if you agree to the data privacy terms.
* **Insurance Provider or Policy Type**: If you have health insurance, enter the name and type of insurance provider.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Date of Consent**: Sign this form with the date you are signing it.

## Tips
* Please review each field carefully before signing the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, ask your healthcare provider or testing staff for assistance.
* This form is a legal document and should be filled out carefully and accurately.
