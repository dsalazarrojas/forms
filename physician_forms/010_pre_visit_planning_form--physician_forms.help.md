# Pre Visit Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This pre-visit planning form helps physicians gather essential information about the patient before their scheduled visit. Completing this form ensures that the physician has all necessary information about the patient's medical history, allergies, contact details, and other relevant factors to provide the best possible care during the visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name and date of visit.
2. Select the type of visit (e.g., New Patient Visit, Followup Visit, Return Visit, or Discharge).
3. Enter the patient's name, address, phone number, and email (if available).
4. Provide the patient's medical history, including any medical conditions they may have.
5. List the patient's medical allergies and any medications they are taking.
6. Add any additional notes or comments for the physician's reference during the visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Patient Name** (text)
Enter the patient's full name, including their first and last name.

### 2. **Visit Date** (text)
Enter the date of the patient's scheduled visit.

### 3. **Visit Time** (time)
Enter the time of the patient's scheduled visit.

### 4. **Physician Name** (text)
Enter your name and contact information for reference purposes.

### 5. **Medical Condition** (text)
List any pre-existing medical conditions the patient may have.

### 6. **Medical History** (text)
Provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical procedures or treatments they have undergone.

### 7. **Goals** (text)
List the patient's goals for the visit, including any specific concerns or questions they may have.

### 8. **Medical Allergies** (text)
List any allergies the patient may have, including any medications or substances they are allergic to.

### 9. **Patient Contact** (text)
Enter the patient's contact information, including their phone number and email (if available).

### 10. **Patient Phone** (text)
Enter the patient's phone number.

### 11. **Address** (text)
Enter the patient's address.

### 12. **Insurance** (text)
Enter the patient's insurance information, including their insurance provider and ID number.

### 13. **Patient Address** (text)
Enter the patient's address.

### 14. **Physician Note** (note)
Add any additional notes or comments for the physician's reference during the visit.

### 15. **Patient Email** (email)
Enter the patient's email address (if available).

### 16. **Medical ID** (text)
Enter the patient's medical ID number (if applicable).

### 17. **Visit Type** (select_one)
Select the type of visit the patient is scheduled for (e.g., New Patient Visit, Followup Visit, Return Visit, or Discharge).

### Tips

* Please ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, do not hesitate to reach out to the relevant healthcare team members for clarification.
* This form can be used as a reference during the actual visit to ensure that all necessary information is discussed and addressed.
