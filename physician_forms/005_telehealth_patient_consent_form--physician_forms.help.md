# Telehealth Patient Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telehealth Patient Consent Form is a mandatory form that patients must complete before starting telehealth services. This form is designed to ensure that patients understand the terms and conditions of their care and give their informed consent for the services they are about to receive.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name in the "patient-name" field. This is your legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field. This will be the email address we use to communicate with you about your care.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "date-of-birth" field. This is the date you were born.
4. Enter your address in the "address" field. This is your current address.
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field. This is your contact phone number.
6. Read the "consent" field and sign it if you agree to the terms and conditions.
7. Choose "Yes" or "No" in the "signature" field to indicate whether you have read and understood the form.
8. If you have a file to upload (e.g., ID documents), select "Yes" in the "upload_file" field.
9. If you have a document to upload (e.g., medical records), select "Yes" in the "upload_document" field.
10. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "submit" field to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient-name (text)**: Please enter your legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **email (email)**: Enter your email address that we will use to communicate with you about your care.
* **date-of-birth (date)**: Enter the date you were born.
* **address (text)**: Enter your current address.
* **phone (text)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **consent (note)**: Read and sign this field if you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **signature (select_multiple)**: Choose "Yes" if you have read and understood the form.
* **upload_file (select_multiple)**: Select "Yes" if you have a file to upload (e.g., ID documents).
* **upload_document (select_multiple)**: Select "Yes" if you have a document to upload (e.g., medical records).
* **submit (select_multiple)**: Choose "Yes" to complete the form and submit your information.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your healthcare provider or a trusted representative for clarification.
* Ensure that you have the necessary documents and information ready to upload before submitting the form.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully before signing the "consent" field.
