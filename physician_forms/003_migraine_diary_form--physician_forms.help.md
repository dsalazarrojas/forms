# Migraine Diary Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Migraine Diary Form is a personal and private space for individuals to record their migraines, including the frequency, duration, location, and other symptoms. This form helps users track and monitor their migraine attacks, providing valuable information to discuss with healthcare professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in the form fields with your experiences and information.
3. Be as detailed as possible, especially when describing symptoms and medications.
4. Review and update your form regularly to track changes and patterns in your migraine attacks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Migraine Diary Form** (`migraine_diary_form`, text, required: false): This is the main section where you can write a brief description of your migraine experience.
* **Headache Location** (`headache_location`, text, required: false): Describe the location of your headache, e.g., left side, right side, or entire head.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, text, required: false): Answer the question "How often do you have migraines?" and provide your answer, e.g., daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Duration** (`duration`, text, required: false): Describe how long your migraines last, e.g., hours, days, or weeks.
* **Pain** (`pain`, text, required: false): Rate your pain level from 1-10, where 1 is mild and 10 is severe.
* **Sensitivity to Light** (`sensitivity_to_light`, text, required: false): Describe how sensitive you are to light, e.g., very sensitive, moderately sensitive, or not sensitive at all.
* **Sensitivity to Sounds** (`sensitivity_to_sounds`, text, required: false): Describe how sensitive you are to sounds, e.g., very sensitive, moderately sensitive, or not sensitive at all.
* **Nausea** (`nausea`, text, required: false): Check if you experience nausea during your migraines, yes or no.
* **Vomiting** (`vomiting`, text, required: false): Check if you experience vomiting during your migraines, yes or no.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, required: false): List any medications you take for your migraines, e.g., triptans, antidepressants, or painkillers.
* **Other Symptoms** (`other_symptoms`, text, required: false): Describe any other symptoms you experience during your migraines, e.g., dizziness, fatigue, or visual disturbances.
* **Date Of Attack** (`date_of_attack`, date, required: false): Record the date of your last migraines attack, e.g., 2023-02-20.
* **Time Of Attack** (`time_of_attack`, time, required: false): Record the time of your last migraines attack, e.g., 14:30.

## Tips
* Be honest and consistent in your reporting to get accurate insights into your migraines.
* Review and update your form regularly to track changes and patterns in your migraines.
* Discuss your form with your healthcare professional to get personalized guidance and care.
