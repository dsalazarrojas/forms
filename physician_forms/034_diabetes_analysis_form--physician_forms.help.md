# Diabetes Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diabetes Analysis Form is designed to collect and analyze patient contact information, medical history, and doctor's notes for diabetes-related care. This form is used for analyzing and providing personalized recommendations for patients with diabetes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide contact information for the patient, including their name, phone number, and email address.
2. Choose how you would like to contact the patient (Phone or Email).
3. Enter a brief medical history related to diabetes.
4. Add any additional comments or notes from the doctor.
5. Enter the patient's date of birth (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
6. Enter the doctor's contact information, including phone number and email address.
7. Enter any additional medical history related to diabetes.
8. Review and submit the form for analysis and recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Contact Information** (`patient_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's contact information, including their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you would like to contact the patient, either by Phone or Email.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, required/optional): Enter a brief medical history related to diabetes.
* **Doctor Info** (`doctor_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the doctor's contact information, including their name and other relevant details.
* **Doctor Notes** (`doctor_notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes from the doctor.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name.
* **Contact Method Value** (`contact_method_value`, text, required/optional): Enter the value related to the contact method chosen (e.g. "Phone number" or "Email address").
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, text, required/optional): Enter the doctor's email address.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, text, required/optional): Enter additional medical history related to diabetes.
* **Patient DOB** (`patient_dob`, date, required/optional): Enter the patient's date of birth (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Doctor Phone Value** (`doctor_phone_value`, text, required/optional): Enter the value related to the doctor's phone number (e.g. "Phone number").
* **Doctor Email Value** (`doctor_email_value`, text, required/optional): Enter the value related to the doctor's email address (e.g. "Email address").

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for the patient and doctor contact information.
* Enter any relevant medical history related to diabetes.
* Use the contact method chosen to communicate with the patient.
* Review and submit the form carefully for accurate analysis and recommendations.
