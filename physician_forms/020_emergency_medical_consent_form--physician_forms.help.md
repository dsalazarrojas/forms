# emergency-medical-consent-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from patients or their representatives in emergency situations, enabling medical teams to provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information in the **patient-information** field.
2. Provide details about the patient's **condition**.
3. List any **medical-history** that may be relevant to the patient's care.
4. List any **allergies** the patient may have.
5. List the patient's **medications**.
6. Provide the patient's **emergency-contact** information.
7. Select the **relationship** between the emergency contact and the patient.
8. Fill in the **address**, **email**, and **phone** of the emergency contact.
9. Select the **emergency-services** options that apply to the patient's care.
10. Select the **medical-team** members that will be involved in the patient's care.
11. Select the **emergency-department-doctor** options that apply to the patient's care.
12. Read and sign the **consent** statement.
13. Select your **patient-acknowledgement** of the consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient-information**: Fill in the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **condition**: Describe the patient's current medical condition, including any symptoms or recent medical history.
* **medical-history**: Provide a list of the patient's medical history, including any relevant surgeries, illnesses, or injuries.
* **allergies**: List any substances or medications the patient is allergic to.
* **medications**: List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **emergency-contact**: Provide the emergency contact's name, address, phone number, and email.
* **relationship**: Select the relationship between the emergency contact and the patient (e.g., mother, father, sister, brother, spouse, etc.).
* **address**: Fill in the emergency contact's address.
* **email**: Fill in the emergency contact's email address.
* **phone**: Fill in the emergency contact's phone number.
* **emergency-services**: Select the emergency services that apply to the patient's care (e.g., hospital, emergency department, etc.).
* **medical-team**: Select the medical team members that will be involved in the patient's care.
* **emergency-department-doctor**: Select the emergency department doctor options that apply to the patient's care.
* **consent**: Read and sign the consent statement, indicating you understand and agree to the terms.
* **patient-acknowledgement**: Select your acknowledgement of the consent statement (i.e., "I agree," "I disagree," or "I understand but do not know").
