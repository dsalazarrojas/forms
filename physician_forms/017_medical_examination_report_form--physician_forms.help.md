# medical_examination_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical examination report form is a document used to record the details of a patient's medical examination. It helps healthcare professionals gather and document information about the patient's medical history, condition, and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Header** (id: 1) with the relevant information, such as the date and title of the examination.
2. Sign the **Sign Here** (id: 2) section with your signature, indicating that you have reviewed and completed the form.
3. Enter your **Name** (id: 3) and **Date of Exam** (id: 4) to identify yourself and the examination date.
4. Provide a brief description of the patient's **Medical History** (id: 5) and list any **Allergies** (id: 6) the patient may have.
5. Document the patient's current **Medical Condition** (id: 7) and indicate whether they are taking any **Medication** (id: 8).
6. Enter the name of the **Medical Examiner** (id: 9), their **Title** (id: 10), and the name of their **Office** (id: 11) and **Address** (id: 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header (id: 1)**: A brief title and date for the examination.
* **Sign Here (id: 2)**: Sign your name to indicate that you have completed the form.
* **Name (id: 3)**: Your name, as the person completing the form.
* **Date of Exam (id: 4)**: The date of the examination.
* **Medical History (id: 5)**: A description of the patient's medical history.
* **Allergies (id: 6)**: List any allergies the patient may have.
* **Medical Condition (id: 7)**: A description of the patient's current medical condition.
* **Medication Use (id: 8)**: Indicate whether the patient is taking any medication.
* **Medical Examiner's Name (id: 9)**: The name of the medical examiner.
* **Medical Examiner's Title (id: 10)**: The title of the medical examiner.
* **Medical Examiner's Office (id: 11)**: The name of the medical examiner's office.
* **Medical Examiner's Address (id: 12)**: The address of the medical examiner's office.
