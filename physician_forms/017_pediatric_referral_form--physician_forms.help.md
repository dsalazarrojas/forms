# Pediatric Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather and document information for referrals from one healthcare provider to another.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including the patient's name, date of birth, and referring provider's information.
2. Select the correct medical specialty for the referral.
3. Enter the date of the last visit, if applicable.
4. Provide any additional relevant information in the referral reason field.
5. Enter the patient's age, if applicable.
6. Enter the medical record number, if applicable.
7. Enter the contact name and phone number for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pediatric Referral Form**: This is the form title and header.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical record. (Required)
* **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY). (Required)
* **Referral Reason**: Enter a brief description of the reason for the referral, including any relevant medical history or concerns. (Optional)
* **Patient Age**: Enter the patient's current age in years. (Optional)
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's unique medical record number, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Referring Provider**: Enter the name and contact information of the referring provider. (Optional)
* **Date Last Visit**: Enter the date of the last visit, if applicable.
* **Medical Specialty**: Select the medical specialty to which the patient is being referred. (Required)
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact for follow-up, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person, if applicable. (Optional)

## Tips

* Double-check that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Make sure to enter the correct medical specialty for the referral.
* If the patient has multiple visits, you can enter multiple dates of last visit.
* If you are unsure about any information, ask the patient's primary care provider for clarification.
