# Physician Work Setting Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physician Work Setting Assessment is a form designed to gather information about your work setting as a healthcare professional. This includes your current work environment, duties, and responsibilities. The information collected will help us better understand your work context and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by selecting the first page, which will ask for general information about your work setting.
2.  Answer the questions on the first page, providing as much detail as possible.
3.  Once you have completed the first page, proceed to the second page, which will ask for additional information about your work setting.
4.  Repeat the process for the third and final page.
5.  Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

**Page 1**
* **Title** (optional): Enter a brief title for your work setting, such as the name of your clinic or hospital.
* **This is a text field to gather general information about your work setting.**

**Page 2**
* **Title** (optional): If you have not already entered a title, you can do so here.
* **This is a text field to gather more specific information about your work setting.**

**Page 3**
* **Title** (optional): Enter any additional details about your work setting that were not covered on the previous pages.
* **This is a text field to gather further information about your work setting.**
