> ### Daycare Allergy Form - Help Guide
> ## Purpose
> The Daycare Allergy Form is used to gather information about a child's known and suspected allergies, medical conditions, and emergency contacts in case of an allergic reaction. This information helps daycare staff provide safe and effective care for the child.

> ## How To Complete This Form
> To complete this form, follow these steps:
> 1. Enter any known allergies or medical conditions the child has.
> 2. Enter suspected allergies or medical conditions the child may have.
> 3. Add any medication allergies or medical conditions the child has.
> 4. Enter emergency contact information for the child.
> 5. Enter the contact phone number for the emergency contact.
> 6. Sign and date the form.

> ## Field-by-Field Explanation
> 
> * **Known Allergies** (Known Allergies, text, Optional): 
>   Enter any allergies the child is known to have, such as food or environmental allergies.
> * **Suspected Allergies** (Suspected Allergies, text, Optional): 
>   Enter any suspected allergies or medical conditions the child may have, but has not been confirmed.
> * **Medication Allergies** (Medication Allergies, text, Optional): 
>   Enter any allergies the child has to certain medications or treatments.
> * **Medical Condition** (Medical Condition, text, Optional): 
>   Enter any known medical conditions or allergies the child has, such as ADHD or asthma.
> * **Medical History** (Medical History, text, Optional): 
>   Enter any relevant medical history the child may have, such as surgeries or hospitalizations.
> * **Emergency Contacts** (Emergency Contacts, select_multiple, Optional): 
>   Select any emergency contacts for the child, such as parents or guardians.
> * **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, Optional): 
>   Enter the contact phone number for the emergency contact.
> * **Physician Signature** (Physician Signature, text, Optional): 
>   Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
> * **Physician Name** (Physician Name, text, Optional): 
>   Enter the name of the physician completing the form.
> * **Physician Title** (Physician Title, text, Optional): 
>   Enter the title of the physician completing the form.
> * **Date Signed** (Date Signed, date, Optional): 
>   Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
> * **Time Signed** (Time Signed, time, Optional): 
>   Enter the time the form was signed.
> * **Facility** (Facility, text, Optional): 
>   Enter the name of the facility where the child is being cared for.
> * **Facility ID** (Facility ID, text, Optional): 
>   Enter the ID of the facility where the child is being cared for.
> * **Date Entered** (Date Entered, date, Optional): 
>   Enter the date the form was entered.
> * **Time Entered** (Time Entered, time, Optional): 
>   Enter the time the form was entered.
> * **Physician Notes** (Physician Notes, text, Optional): 
>   Enter any additional notes or comments from the physician.

> ## Tips
> * Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the child's safety and well-being.
> * If you are unsure about any information, please contact the child's physician or parents/guardians for clarification.
> * This form must be signed and dated by a licensed physician to confirm completion.
