# Patient History Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient History Questionnaire is designed to collect and document a patient's medical history, including allergies, medical conditions, medications, and other relevant health information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. If you are unsure about the information required or have any doubts, please ask a healthcare provider for clarification.
3. Provide as much detail as possible for each question.
4. If you have any additional information that is not listed in the form, please use the "Additional Info" fields to provide context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient History** (`patient_history`, `note`, required: false): This section is a brief summary of the patient's medical history.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Describe any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or illnesses.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all the allergens you are allergic to (e.g. "Yes" to indicate a known allergy).
* **Medications** (`medications`, `select_multiple`, required: false): List any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all medical conditions you have or have had (e.g. "Yes" to indicate a condition).
* **Social History** (`social_history`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all relevant social factors that may impact your health.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all relevant family medical history that may impact your health.
* **Medical History Additional Info** (`medical_history_additional_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional information that may be useful for your medical history.
* **Patient History Additional Info** (`patient_history_additional_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional information that may be useful for your patient history.
* **Physician Notes** (`physician_notes`, `select_one`, required: false): Allow healthcare providers to note any important information about your visit or care.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask a healthcare provider for clarification.
* If you have any additional information that is not listed in the form, please use the "Additional Info" fields to provide context.
* This form is intended to be used as part of a larger set of tools for healthcare providers, and is not a standalone solution.
