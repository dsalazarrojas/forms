# Sleep Apnea Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through completing the Sleep Apnea Questionnaire form, which is used to gather information about your sleep apnea symptoms, diagnosis, and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each section of the form carefully, answering the questions as accurately as possible.
2. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your doctor or healthcare provider for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sleep Apnea Questionnaire** (`1`, `title`, `required`): This is the title of the form, which should not need to be filled out.
* **Sleep Apnea Questions** (`2`, `select_multiple`, `required`):
	+ Select the most common symptom you experience when you sleep from the options provided.
	+ You can select multiple options.
* **Sleep Apnea Frequency** (`3`, `number`, `required`: false): You can fill out a number to indicate how often you experience sleep apnea symptoms.
* **Sleep Apnea Duration** (`4`, `number`, `required`: false): You can fill out a number to indicate how long you experience sleep apnea symptoms.
* **Sleep Apnea Risk Factors** (`5`, `select_one`, `required`): Select one of the risk factors that may contribute to your sleep apnea.
* **Sleep Apnea Symptoms** (`6`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all the symptoms you experience from the options provided.
* **Sleep Apnea Medication** (`7`, `select_multiple`, `required`: false): Select any medications you are currently taking for sleep apnea.
* **Sleep Apnea Other Notes** (`8`, `note`, `required`: false): You can write any additional information about your sleep apnea in this field.
* **Sleep Apnea Email** (`9`, `email`, `required`: false): You can enter your email address if you want to be contacted by your healthcare provider.
* **Sleep Apnea Phone** (`10`, `text`, `required`: false): You can enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by your healthcare provider.
* **Sleep Apnea Date** (`11`, `date`, `required`: false): You can fill out the date of your last sleep apnea diagnosis or treatment.
* **Sleep Apnea Time** (`12`, `time`, `required`: false): You can fill out the time of your last sleep apnea diagnosis or treatment.
* **Sleep Apnea Note** (`13`, `note`, `required`: false): You can write any additional information about your sleep apnea in this field.
