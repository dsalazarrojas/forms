# Mpox Exposure Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Exposure Risk Assessment Form is used by healthcare professionals to gather information about an individual's risk of exposure to Mpox. This form helps healthcare professionals make informed decisions about follow-up actions and ensure the individual receives necessary care.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, fill out the form as completely and accurately as possible.
2.  For each field, select the relevant options that apply to the individual.
3.  If a field is marked as "No" and you have a "Yes" answer in another field, select "Yes" in that field instead.
4.  Save the form and submit it to the healthcare professional for review and follow-up actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Exposure History

*   **Exposure History (Date)**: Report the date of the individual's most recent exposure to Mpox.
*   **Travel History**: Select "Yes" if the individual has traveled to an area with a high risk of Mpox exposure.
*   **Contact History**: Select all contact history that applies to the individual, such as close contact with an infected person or contact with an infected animal.

### Travel History

*   **Travel History**: Select "Yes" if the individual has traveled to an area with a high risk of Mpox exposure.

### Contact History

*   **Contact History**: Select all contact history that applies to the individual, such as close contact with an infected person or contact with an infected animal.

### Exposure Risk Factors

*   **Exposure Risk Factors**: Select all risk factors that apply to the individual, such as exposure to an infected person or exposure to a high-risk environment.

### Symptoms

*   **Symptoms**: Report any symptoms the individual is experiencing.

### Exposure Risk Assessment

*   **Exposure Risk Assessment**: Rate the individual's risk of exposure to Mpox on a scale of 1-5, with 5 being the highest risk.

### Follow Up

*   **Follow Up**: Select "Yes" if follow-up is required after completing the form.

## Tips

*   Be as accurate and complete as possible when filling out the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, consult with a healthcare professional.
*   This form is intended for healthcare professionals only; if you are not a healthcare professional, do not fill out the form.
