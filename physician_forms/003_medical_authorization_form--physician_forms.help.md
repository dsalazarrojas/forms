# Medical Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Authorization Form is a document used to gather information about a patient's medical history, clearance, and facility details. This form is used to ensure that medical facilities have the necessary information to provide proper care and treatment to the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's data in the "Medical Authorization Form Data" field.
2.  Provide the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
3.  Enter the doctor's name in the "doctor_name" field.
4.  Enter the date of the last visit in the "date_of_last_visit" field in the correct date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
5.  Provide the reason for the visit in the "reason_for_visit" field.
6.  Enter the patient's medical history in the "medical_history" field.
7.  Check the "medical_clearance" field to indicate whether the patient has medical clearance. Select "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
8.  Provide the reason for medical clearance in the "medical_clearance_reason" field if applicable.
9.  Enter the medical certificate number in the "medical_certificate_number" field.
10. Enter the date of the medical certification in the "medical_certification_date" field in the correct date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
11.  Sign the form with your signature in the "signature" field.
12.  Enter your email address in the "email" field.
13.  Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
14.  Select the medical facility from the dropdown menu in the "medical_facility" field.
15.  Enter the name of the medical facility in the "medical_facility_name" field.
16.  Enter the address of the medical facility in the "medical_facility_address" field.
17.  Enter the city of the medical facility in the "medical_facility_city" field.
18.  Select the state of the medical facility from the dropdown menu in the "medical_facility_state" field.
19.  Enter the zip code of the medical facility in the "medical_facility_zip" field.
20.  Enter the phone number of the medical facility in the "medical_facility_phone" field.
21.  Enter the website of the medical facility in the "medical_facility_website" field.
22.  Enter the fax number of the medical facility in the "medical_facility_fax" field.
23.  Select the hours of operation for the medical facility from the dropdown menu in the "medical_facility_hours" field.
24.  Enter any additional details about the medical facility's hours in the "medical_facility_hours_details" field.
25.  Enter any notes about the medical facility in the "medical_facility_notes" field.
