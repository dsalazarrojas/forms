# COVID 19 Vaccination Screening Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to screen patients for COVID-19 vaccination, ensuring that they are suitable candidates for vaccination. The form gathers information about the patient's medical history, current health status, and previous vaccination experiences to identify potential risks and contraindications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Section 1: Patient Information**
	* Enter the patient's full name.
	* Enter the patient's date of birth.
	* Enter the patient's age.
2. **Section 2: Medical History**
	* Answer the questions about your medical history, current health status, and previous vaccination experiences.
	* If you have allergies to vaccine components, select "True" and describe the allergy.
	* If you have a history of anaphylaxis, select "True".
	* If you are immunocompromised, select "True" and describe your condition.
	* List any chronic medical conditions you have.
3. **Section 3: Vaccination Status**
	* Answer the questions about your previous vaccination experiences.
	* If you have had COVID-19, select "True" and enter the date of diagnosis.
	* If you have received a COVID-19 vaccine before, select "True".
	* If you have had an adverse reaction to a previous vaccine, select "True" and describe the reaction.
4. **Section 4: Current Health Status**
	* Answer the questions about your current health status.
	* Select "True" if you are currently experiencing COVID-19 symptoms.
	* If you are experiencing symptoms, check all that apply from the list.
	* Select "True" if you are currently taking blood thinners or anticoagulants.
	* Select "True" if you are taking immunosuppressive medications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Age**: Enter the patient's age.
* **Patient ID or Phone Number**: Enter the patient's ID or phone number.
* **Medical History Screening**: This section is a note field for healthcare providers to review the patient's medical history.
* **Do you have allergies to vaccine components**: Select "True" if you have allergies to any vaccine components, such as Polyethylene glycol or polysorbate.
* **History of anaphylaxis**: Select "True" if you have a history of anaphylaxis.
* **If yes, please describe**: If you selected "True" for the previous question, describe your allergy or condition.
* **Are you immunocompromised**: Select "True" if you are immunocompromised due to HIV, cancer, or immunosuppressive therapy.
* **Chronic medical conditions**: Select "True" if you have any chronic medical conditions, such as diabetes or lung disease.
* **List conditions**: If you selected "True" for the previous question, list your conditions.
* **Currently pregnant or planning to become pregnant**: Select "Pregnant", "Planning pregnancy", or "False".
* **Currently breastfeeding**: Select "True" if you are currently breastfeeding.
* **Current Medications**: This section is a note field for healthcare providers to review the patient's current medications.
* **Current medications you are taking**: List your current medications.
* **Taking blood thinners or anticoagulants**: Select "True" if you are taking blood thinners or anticoagulants.
* **Taking immunosuppressive medications**: Select "True" if you are taking immunosuppressive medications.
* **Vaccination History**: This section is a note field for healthcare providers to review the patient's vaccination history.
* **Have you had COVID-19**: Select "True" if you have had COVID-19.
* **If yes, when were you diagnosed**: Enter the date you were diagnosed with COVID-19.
* **Have you received a COVID-19 vaccine before**: Select "True" if you have received a COVID-19 vaccine before.
* **If yes, which vaccine and dates**: If you selected "True" for the previous question, enter the dates of your previous vaccine.
* **Had adverse reactions to previous vaccines**: Select "True" if you have had adverse reactions to previous vaccines.
* **Describe adverse reaction**: If you selected "True" for the previous question, describe your reaction.
* **Currently experiencing COVID-19 symptoms**: Select "True" if you are currently experiencing COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Symptoms**: If you selected "True" for the previous question, check all symptoms that apply from the list.
* **Current fever or temperature above 100.4 F**: Select "True" if you are currently experiencing a fever or temperature above 100.4 F.
* **Screening approval**: This field is for healthcare providers to indicate approval or deferral of vaccination.

Tips:

* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please discuss with your healthcare provider.
* Please answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form is designed to screen patients for COVID-19 vaccination, but it is not a substitute for medical evaluation and clearance.
