# Nutrition Diary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Nutrition Diary Form is used to collect information about a client's daily nutrition habits, including food intake, water intake, and physical activity. This data is used by health coaches to provide personalized guidance and support to clients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Provide your name** (`client_name`, text, **optional**): Enter your name to associate your form data with your account.
2. **Enter date** (`date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date of the day you are submitting data for (e.g., MM/DD/YY).
3. **Describe your food intake** (`food_intake`, text, **optional**): Describe the food and beverages you consumed on the day, including portion sizes.
4. **Indicate your water intake** (`water_intake`, number, **optional**): Estimate your total water intake in ounces.
5. **Specify your physical activity level** (`physical_activity`, text, **optional**): Describe your physical activity level, such as exercise, sports, or daily activities.
6. **Submit health coach feedback** (`health_coach_feedback`, text, **optional**): Share any comments or feedback you received from your health coach.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, **optional**): This field is used to associate your form data with your account.
* **Date** (`date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YY (e.g., 01/01/22).
* **Food Intake** (`food_intake`, text, **optional**): Describe the food and beverages you consumed on the day, including portion sizes.
* **Portion Size** (`portion_size`, number, **optional**): Estimate the portion size of each food item.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_one, **optional**): Choose how often you consumed each food item (e.g., 1 time/day, 2 times/day).
* **Water Intake** (`water_intake`, number, **optional**): Estimate your total water intake in ounces.
* **Physical Activity** (`physical_activity`, text, **optional**): Describe your physical activity level, such as exercise, sports, or daily activities.
* **Health Coach Feedback** (`health_coach_feedback`, text, **optional**): Share any comments or feedback you received from your health coach.

## Tips

* You can submit data for multiple days by completing this form multiple times.
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Your data will be used to provide personalized guidance and support from your health coach.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your health coach for assistance.
