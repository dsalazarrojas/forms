# Physical Therapy Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for patients to refer themselves to a physical therapist for further treatment and assessment. It collects information about the patient's medical history, chief complaints, and preferences for physical therapy.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form with accurate and relevant information about yourself.
- Ensure you answer all questions, as indicated by the required status of each field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask a healthcare professional for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: This section is where you'll provide basic information about yourself, such as your name, contact details, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Medical History**: Please provide a brief description of your medical history, including any previous injuries, illnesses, or treatments you've undergone.
* **Chief Complaint**: Describe your main concern or issue that you're experiencing. This could be related to pain, mobility, or any other symptoms.
* **Assessment**: Select the area of your body that you're experiencing issues with. This will help the physical therapist understand where to focus their assessment.
	+ 1.0 - Shoulder: Select this option if you're experiencing issues with your shoulder, such as pain, mobility, or limited range of motion.
	+ 2.0 - Back: Select this option if you're experiencing issues with your back, such as pain, posture, or discomfort.
	+ 3.0 - Knee: Select this option if you're experiencing issues with your knee, such as pain, instability, or limited mobility.
	+ 4.0 - Other: Select this option if you're experiencing issues with another area of your body that isn't listed above.
* **Physical Therapist**: Indicate whether you have a preference for working with a physical therapist. Select "Yes" if you're comfortable working with a physical therapist, or "No" if you'd prefer not to.
* **Referral Reason**: Provide a brief explanation for why you're referring yourself to a physical therapist. This could be for evaluation, treatment, or any other reason.
