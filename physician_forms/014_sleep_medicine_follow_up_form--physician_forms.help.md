# Sleep Medicine Follow Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect follow-up information from patients who are taking sleep medicine. It helps healthcare providers track the effectiveness of the treatment and any side effects or difficulties experienced by the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the required information for each field. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
2. Enter the patient's information in the "Patient Information" field.
3. Describe the patient's sleep patterns in the "Sleep Patterns" field.
4. List any medications the patient is currently taking in the "Medications" field.
5. Report any side effects experienced from medications in the "Side Effects of Medications" field.
6. Describe any symptoms the patient is experiencing in the "Symptoms" field.
7. Specify any sleep difficulties the patient is experiencing in the "Sleep Difficulties" field.
8. Provide any additional information about side effects not listed in the "Other Side Effects of Medications" field.
9. Report any frequencies of side effects in the "Side Effects Frequencies" field.
10. Specify the medication dosage in the "Medication Dosage" field.
11. Specify the frequency of medication usage in the "Medication Frequency" field.
12. Enter the start date of medication usage in the "Medication Start Date" field.
13. Enter the end date of medication usage in the "Medication End Date" field.
14. Provide any additional notes about medication in the "Medication Notes" field.
15. Enter any comments or feedback about medication in the "Medication Comments" field.
16. Provide any additional comments or information about medication in the "Additional Comments" field.
17. List any other medications or information not listed in the other fields in the "Other" field.
18. Enter any additional notes about other information in the "Other Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other relevant information.
* **Sleep Patterns** (`sleep_patterns`, text, required): Describe the patient's sleep patterns, including duration and quality of sleep.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, required): List any medications currently being taken by the patient.
* **Side Effects of Medications** (`medication_side_effects`, text, required): Report any side effects experienced from medications, such as dizziness or fatigue.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, required): Describe any symptoms experienced by the patient, such as insomnia or restlessness.
* **Sleep Difficulties** (`sleep_difficulties`, text, required): Specify any sleep difficulties the patient is experiencing, such as difficulty falling asleep or staying asleep.
* **Other Side Effects of Medications** (`medication_side_effects_other`, text, required): Provide any additional information about side effects not listed in the "Side Effects of Medications" field.
* **Side Effects Frequencies** (`medication_side_effects_frequencies`, number, optional): Report any frequencies of side effects, such as how often they occur.
* **Medication Dosage** (`medication_dosage`, text, optional): Specify the medication dosage.
* **Medication Frequency** (`medication_frequency`, text, optional): Specify the frequency of medication usage.
* **Medication Start Date** (`medication_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of medication usage.
* **Medication End Date** (`medication_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of medication usage.
* **Medication Notes** (`medication_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes about medication.
* **Medication Comments** (`medication_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback about medication.
* **Additional Comments** (`medication_additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about medication.
* **Other** (`medication_other`, text, optional): List any other medications or information not listed in other fields.
* **Other Notes** (`medication_other_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about other information.
