# Medical Device Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from users about a medical device. We want to understand your experience with the device and gather information that will help us improve its performance, usability, and safety.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. For rating questions, select the option that best describes your experience with the device.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you can.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the "Any additional comments about the device?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your rating of the device's performance?** (`performance_rating`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question asks you to rate the device's overall performance. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **How easy is the device to use?** (`usability_rating`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question asks you to rate how easy or difficult it is to use the device.
* **How safe do you consider this device?** (`safety_rating`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question asks you to rate the level of safety you associate with the device.
* **Is the device compliant with regulatory standards?** (`regulatory_compliance`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question asks you to indicate whether you are aware of the device's regulatory compliance status.
* **What is the name of the device?** (`device_name`, text, required: false)
	+ Please provide the name of the device you are rating.
* **Who is the manufacturer of the device?** (`manufacturer`, text, required: false)
	+ Please provide the name of the device's manufacturer.
* **Contact information for the manufacturer?** (`contact_info`, text, required: false)
	+ If you have any contact information for the manufacturer, please provide it.
* **Date the device was used?** (`date_used`, date, required: false)
	+ If you have used the device, please provide the date you used it.
* **Time the device was used?** (`time_used`, time, required: false)
	+ If you have used the device, please provide the time you used it.
* **Any additional comments about the device?** (`rating_comments`, note, required: false)
	+ If you have any additional feedback or comments about the device, please use this field.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately based on your experience with the device.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please select "Unknown" or provide a brief explanation.
* If you have any concerns or issues with the device, please provide as much detail as possible in the "Any additional comments about the device?" field.
