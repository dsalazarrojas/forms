# Medical Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from new medical staff members, including their new physician information, emergency contacts, physician experience, medical certifications, and other relevant details. It ensures consistency with our internal records and helps verify the qualifications and credentials of new medical staff members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately with your current information.
2. Ensure you answer all required fields.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask a supervisor or administrator for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Physician Information**: 
  * (`new_physician_information`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your name and any other identifying details as requested.
* **Emergency Contact**: 
  * (`emergency_contact`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all relevant information about your emergency contact, such as their first name, last name, relationship, phone number, and email address.
* **Physician Experience**: 
  * (`physician_experience`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of years you have been practicing medicine, including time in medical school and any relevant work experience.
* **Minimally Invasive Procedures**: 
  * (`minimalist`, `select_one`, required/optional): Check all relevant medical procedures you have performed, such as endoscopy or laparoscopy.
* **Medical Certifications**: 
  * (`medical_certifications`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check all relevant medical certifications you have obtained, such as yes or no.
* **Prophets**: 
  * (`prophets`, `note`, required/optional): Please note any relevant medical certifications or training you have received.
* **Physician Specialties**: 
  * (`physician_specialties`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check all relevant physician specialties you have, such as yes or no.
* **Address**: 
  * (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **Email**: 
  * (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Information**: 
  * (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your emergency contact's information for reference.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask a supervisor or administrator for assistance.
* Review and verify all information before submission.
* If you are already a registered medical staff member, please do not submit this form.
