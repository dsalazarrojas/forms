# COVID 19 Booster Shot Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Booster Shot Registration Form is a necessary step in the COVID-19 booster shot registration process. This form will help the healthcare team understand the patient's vaccination history, medical conditions, and eligibility for the booster shot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's Full Name in the **Full Name** field.
2. Enter the patient's Date of Birth in the **Date of Birth** field.
3. Enter the patient's ID Number in the **Patient ID Number** field.
4. Enter the patient's Phone Number in the **Phone Number** field.
5. Enter the patient's Email Address in the **Email Address** field.
6. Select the patient's preferred contact method from the **Preferred Contact Method** dropdown menu.
7. Enter the date the patient registered for the booster shot in the **Registration Date** field.
8. Enter the preferred date for the appointment in the **Preferred Appointment Date** field.
9. Select the preferred time for the appointment in the **Preferred Appointment Time** field.
10. Select the preferred location for the vaccination in the **Preferred Vaccination Location** dropdown menu.
11. Select the type of initial COVID-19 vaccine the patient received in the **Type of Initial COVID-19 Vaccine** dropdown menu.
12. Enter the date of the first dose of the vaccine in the **Date of First Dose** field.
13. If applicable, enter the date of the second dose of the vaccine in the **Date of Second Dose** field.
14. Indicate whether the patient meets the booster eligibility criteria in the **Do you meet booster eligibility criteria?** field.
15. If the patient has any medical conditions that may affect eligibility, describe them in the **Medical conditions that may affect eligibility** field.
16. Indicate whether the patient has any documented vaccine allergies in the **Any documented vaccine allergies?** field.
17. If the patient has allergies, describe them in the **Allergy Description** field.
18. Enter the patient's insurance provider in the **Insurance Provider** field.
19. Enter the patient's insurance ID number in the **Insurance ID Number** field.
20. Confirm that you understand the purpose and benefits of the booster shot in the **I understand the purpose and benefits of the booster shot** field.
21. Confirm that you consent to receive the COVID-19 booster vaccination in the **I consent to receive the COVID-19 booster vaccination** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Patient ID Number**: Enter the patient's unique identifier number.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter the patient's email address for contact purposes.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select how the patient would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Text Message).
* **Registration Date**: Enter the date the patient registered for the booster shot.
* **Preferred Appointment Date**: Select the preferred date for the appointment.
* **Preferred Appointment Time**: Select the preferred time for the appointment.
* **Preferred Vaccination Location**: Select the location where the patient would like to receive the vaccination (Main clinic, Community center, Mobile clinic, or Other).
* **Type of Initial COVID-19 Vaccine**: Select the type of vaccine the patient received as their initial COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Date of First Dose**: Enter the date the patient received their first dose of the vaccine.
* **Date of Second Dose**: If applicable, enter the date the patient received their second dose of the vaccine.
* **Do you meet booster eligibility criteria?**: Indicate whether the patient meets the eligibility criteria for a booster shot.
* **Medical conditions that may affect eligibility**: If the patient has any medical conditions that may affect eligibility, describe them in this field.
* **Any documented vaccine allergies?**: Indicate whether the patient has any documented vaccine allergies.
* **Allergy Description**: If the patient has allergies, describe them in this field.
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter the patient's insurance provider.
* **Insurance ID Number**: Enter the patient's insurance ID number.
* **I understand the purpose and benefits of the booster shot**: Confirm that you understand the purpose and benefits of the booster shot.
* **I consent to receive the COVID-19 booster vaccination**: Confirm that you consent to receive the COVID-19 booster vaccination.
