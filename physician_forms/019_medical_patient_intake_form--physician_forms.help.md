# Medical Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Patient Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from patients, including their medical history, medical history date, E-signature preferences, and medical provider details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Fill in your medical history in the **Medical History** text field (page 1).
2. Provide the date of your medical history in the **Medical History Date** field (page 1).
3. Select your E-signature preference from the options (page 1).
4. Choose your medical provider type from the options (page 1).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Medical History
**Medical History**: 
Enter a brief description of your medical history.

### Medical History Date
**Medical History Date**: 
Enter the date of your medical history.

### E-Signature
**E-Sign**: 
Select this option if you would like to digitally sign the form.
**No E-Sign**: 
Select this option if you do not want to digitally sign the form.

### Medical Provider
**Physician**: 
Choose this option if your medical provider is a physician.
**PA/Pharmacist**: 
Choose this option if your medical provider is a physician assistant or pharmacist.
