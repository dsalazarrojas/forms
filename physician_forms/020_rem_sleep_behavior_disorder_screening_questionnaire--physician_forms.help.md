# Rem Sleep Behavior Disorder Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rem Sleep Behavior Disorder Screening Questionnaire is designed to help clinicians identify and manage Rem Sleep Behavior Disorder (RBD) in patients. This form collects data on patient's sleep behavior, symptoms, and treatment plans to provide a comprehensive understanding of the condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Answer all questions to the best of your ability.
* Select the correct options for each question, using the multiple-choice options provided.
* If you need more space to provide information, use the text fields to describe your symptoms and experiences.
* Please answer each question honestly and accurately to provide the most helpful information for clinicians to diagnose and treat RBD.
