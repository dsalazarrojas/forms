# Mpox Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Intake Form is used to collect patient information related to mpox exposure and symptoms for medical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form with the patient's information, exposure history, and symptoms.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the healthcare professional responsible for your care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Info (patient_info)
* **Patient Info (patient_info)** (text, required/optional): This field is used to collect the patient's basic information, such as their name, contact details, and any other relevant medical history.

### Exposure (exposure)
* **Exposure (exposure)** (text, required/optional): This field is used to record the patient's exposure history, including any potential contact with mpox.

### Symptoms (symptoms)
* **Symptoms (symptoms)** (text, required/optional): This field is used to describe the patient's symptoms, such as any rash, itching, or other physical discomfort.

### Contact Info (contact)
* **Contact Info (contact)** (text, required/optional): This field is used to collect the patient's contact information, including their address and phone number.

### Exposure History (exposure_history)
* **Exposure History (exposure_history)** (text, required/optional): This field is used to collect more detailed information about the patient's exposure history, including dates and locations.

### Medical History (medical_history)
* **Medical History (medical_history)** (text, required/optional): This field is used to collect any relevant medical history that may be important for the patient's care.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate and truthful information in the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the healthcare professional responsible for your care.
* This form is intended for medical purposes and should only be used once for each patient. 

Note: Mpox is a medical condition, possibly referring to Monkeypox or Mpox (a genus of viruses).
