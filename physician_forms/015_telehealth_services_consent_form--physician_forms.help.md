# Telehealth Services Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to obtain patient consent for telehealth services. It captures essential information about the patient's medical history, allergies, and contact information while ensuring they understand and agree to terms of service.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely.
2.  Select the correct options for the required fields.
3.  Ensure you understand the terms of service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Telehealth Services Consent
*   **Telehealth Services Consent** (`telehealth_services_consent_form`): This is the main area where the patient can indicate their consent to telehealth services.
