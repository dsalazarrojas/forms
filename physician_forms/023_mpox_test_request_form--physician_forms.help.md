# Mpox Test Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Test Request Form is designed to collect information from physicians to request mpox tests. This form helps to streamline the process of requesting tests, ensuring that all necessary details are captured accurately. Please use this form to request mpox tests in a clear and concise manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and title.
2. Select the relevant information for the patient, including their name, date of birth, and contact details.
3. Choose whether an mpox test is required ('Yes') or not ('No').
4. Schedule the test date and time.
5. Specify the location for the test.
6. Enter the contact number and email address for communication.
7. Optionally, provide the test result.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Physician First Name** (text, Optional): Enter your first name as a physician.
* **Physician Last Name** (text, Optional): Enter your last name as a physician.
* **Patient First Name** (text, Optional): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Patient Last Name** (text, Optional): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (date, Required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Test Type** (Select One, Required): Choose 'Yes' if the mpox test is required, 'No' if not.
* **Test Date** (date, Required): Enter the date of the mpox test in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Test Time** (time, Required): Enter the time of the mpox test in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Location** (text, Required): Enter the location where the mpox test will take place.
* **Contact Number** (text, Required): Enter a contact number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (email, Required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Test Result** (text, Optional): Enter any relevant test results.
