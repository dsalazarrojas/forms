# Office Visit Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Office Visit Scheduling Form is designed to schedule an office visit for a patient. This form requires essential details about the visit, including the date, time, doctor's name, patient's name, and contact number. It also captures additional information such as responsible party, visit notes, and schedule preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by selecting the date of the scheduled visit using the "Visit Date" field.
2.  Choose the time slot for the visit from the "Schedule Visit" dropdown menu.
3.  Enter the doctor's name in the "Doctor's Name" field.
4.  Enter the patient's name in the "Patient's Name" field.
5.  Provide the contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
6.  Identify the responsible party (Self, Parent, Guardian, Spouse, or Other) and select it from the "Responsible Party" dropdown menu.
7.  If you select "Other" as the responsible party, provide additional information in the "Other Party Information" field.
8.  Enter any additional notes or comments about the visit in the "Visit Notes" field.
9.  Optionally, provide any additional information in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Scheduling Form** (`office_visit_scheduling_form`): This is the form header. It does not require any input but serves as the introduction to the rest of the form.
*   **Visit Date** (`office_visit_date`): Select the date for the scheduled visit.
*   **Visit Time** (`office_visit_time`): Choose the time slot for the visit from the dropdown menu.
*   **Doctor's Name** (`office_visit_doctor_name`): Enter the doctor's name who will be attending the visit.
*   **Patient's Name** (`office_visit_patient_name`): Enter the patient's name.
*   **Contact Number** (`office_visit_contact_number`): Provide the contact number for the patient or responsible party.
*   **Schedule Visit** (`office_visit_schedule`): Select a time slot for the visit.
*   **Visit Notes** (`office_visit_note`): Enter any notes or comments about the visit.
*   **Responsible Party** (`office_visit_responsible_party`): Identify the responsible party (Self, Parent, Guardian, Spouse, or Other).
*   **Additional Notes** (`office_visit_additional_notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the visit.
*   **Other Party Information** (`office_visit_responsible_party_other`): If you select "Other" as the responsible party, provide additional information.

## Tips
*   Be sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate scheduling.
*   Choose the correct time slot for the visit.
*   Enter accurate and detailed information for the responsible party and additional notes fields.
*   If you're unsure about any field, ask the office staff for assistance.
