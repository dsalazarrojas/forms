# Autopsy Report Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Autopsy Report Request Form is used to request an autopsy report for a deceased individual. This form is necessary to gather information about the requestor and the decedent, as well as to specify the type of report needed and the preferred delivery method.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relationship between you and the decedent from the options provided.
2. Enter your full name and any relevant information about your organization or institution (if applicable).
3. Provide a valid email address and phone number for contact purposes.
4. Enter the decedent's full name and date of birth.
5. Enter the decedent's date and place of death.
6. Specify the type of report needed and the purpose of the request.
7. Choose the urgency level and preferred delivery method for the report.
8. Select the number of copies of the report needed.
9. Provide any additional information that may be helpful in locating the case.
10. Confirm that you are authorized to make the request and sign off by selecting "Yes, I certify".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Type**: Select your relationship to the decedent (e.g., spouse, parent, child, etc.) to help us understand your connection to the decedent.
* **Requestor Full Name**: Enter your complete legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Organization or Institution**: If you represent an organization or institution, enter the name here.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where we can send the report.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter the address where you would like to receive physical copies of the report.
* **Decedent Full Name**: Enter the complete name of the deceased.
* **Decedent Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the deceased.
* **Decedent Date of Death**: Enter the date and year the deceased passed away.
* **Place of Death**: Enter the city and state or the name of the facility where the decedent died.
* **Case or File Number**: If known, enter the autopsy case number.
* **Medical Record Number (MRN)**: If applicable, enter the decedent's hospital MRN.
* **Type of Report Requested**: Select the type of report needed (e.g., full report, preliminary report, etc.).
* **Purpose of Request**: Specify why you need the report (e.g., personal records, legal proceedings, etc.).
* **Urgency Level**: Choose the urgency level for the report (e.g., routine, expedited, etc.).
* **Preferred Delivery Method**: Select how you would like to receive the report (e.g., secure email, regular mail, etc.).
* **Number of Copies Needed**: Enter the number of copies you require.
* **Additional Information**: Provide any additional details that may be helpful in locating the case.
* **I Certify I am Authorized to Request this Report**: Confirm that you are authorized to make the request by selecting "Yes, I certify".
* **Date of Request**: Select the current date.
