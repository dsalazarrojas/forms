# Hospice Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospice Consent Form is a document that helps us understand your needs and preferences as a patient or family member. It ensures we have the necessary information to provide personalized care and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information**: Enter your name, date of birth, and address.
2. **Patient Family History**: Share any relevant medical history or family history that may impact your care.
3. **Hospice Benefits**: Describe the benefits you are interested in receiving from the hospice.
4. **Care Team**: Identify your primary caregiver or family member who will be involved in your care.
5. **Patient Status**: Confirm your enrollment status in the hospice program.
6. **Admission and Discharge Dates**: Provide dates for when you were admitted and discharged from the hospice.
7. **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm you have read and understand the information.
8. **Patient Signature**: Sign the form to confirm your agreement to the care plan.
9. **Additional Information**: Provide any additional contact information for yourself and your caregivers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter your first and last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address**: Enter your address, including street number and postal code.
* **Patient Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the primary caregiver.
* **Primary Caregiver's Name**: Enter the name of your primary caregiver.
* **Primary Caregiver's Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of your primary caregiver.
* **Primary Caregiver's Address**: Enter the address of your primary caregiver.
* **Doctor's Name**: Enter the name of your doctor.
* **Doctor's Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of your doctor.
* **Doctor's Address**: Enter the address of your doctor.
* **Hospice Agency Name**: Enter the name of the hospice agency.
* **Hospice Agency Address**: Enter the address of the hospice agency.
* **Hospice Agency Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the hospice agency.
* **Hospice Agency Fax Number**: Enter the fax number of the hospice agency.
