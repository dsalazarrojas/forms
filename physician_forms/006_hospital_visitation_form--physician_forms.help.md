# Hospital Visitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Visitation Form is used for visitors to provide essential information about their visit, allowing healthcare professionals to track and manage visitations efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have reviewed the form carefully to understand what information is required and what fields are applicable for your visit.
2. Fill in your relationship to the patient, date of birth, and signature.
3. Fill in the visitor's relationship to the patient, date of birth, and signature.
4. If necessary, fill in the additional fields as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name (1)**: Enter the name of the patient being visited.
* **Relationship To Patient (2)**: Select the nature of your relationship to the patient (Child, Spouse, Parent, Sibling).
* **Date Of Birth (3)**: Enter the date of birth of the patient.
* **Visitor Signature (9)**: Sign in with your name and date to confirm your visit.
* **Visitor Name (10)**: Enter your name.
* **Visitor DOB (11)**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Visitor Relationship (13)**: Select your relationship to the patient (Child, Spouse, Parent, Sibling).
* **Staff Signature (14)**: Sign in with your name and date to confirm your visit.
* **Staff Name (15)**: Enter your name.
* **Staff DOB (16)**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Staff Phone (17)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Staff Email (18)**: Enter your email address.
* **Staff Signature (14)**: Sign in with your name and date to confirm your visit.
* **Visit Limit (20)**: Set the maximum duration of your visit in hours.
* **Visit Frequency (21)**: Enter visit frequency in hours.
* **Visit Start Time (23)**: Set the start time of your visit.
* **Visit End Time (24)**: Set the end time of your visit.
* **Medical Condition 1 (24)**: Enter any medical conditions related to the visit.
* **Medical Condition 2 (25)**: Enter any other medical conditions related to the visit.

Please note that some fields may be hidden or not applicable for your specific visit, so please make sure to review the form carefully before completing it. If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to the staff for assistance. 

Please ensure you have filled in all the required fields before submitting the form.
