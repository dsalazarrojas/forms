# Mpox Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Contact Form is a data collection tool used to gather information about patients who have been exposed to Mpox. This form helps healthcare professionals quickly and accurately record patient data, enabling timely medical interventions and reducing the risk of disease transmission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the **Patient Information** section (page 1).
2. Provide the exposure date in the **Exposure Date** section (page 2).
3. Describe the exposure details in the **Exposure Details** section (page 3).
4. Indicate whether the patient was in contact with a contact person in the **Contact Person** section (page 4).
5. Enter the contact person's phone number in the **Contact Number** section (page 5).
6. Specify the location where the exposure occurred in the **Location** section (page 6).
7. Add any relevant notes from the physician in the **Physician Notes** section (page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (Page 1)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant identification information.
* **Exposure Date (Page 2)**: Enter the date the patient was exposed to Mpox, or if the exposure is ongoing, enter the start date of the exposure.
* **Exposure Details (Page 3)**: Describe the circumstances surrounding the patient's exposure to Mpox, including any symptoms or signs exhibited by the patient.
* **Contact Person (Page 4)**: Indicate whether the patient had contact with another person who may have been exposed to Mpox.
* **Contact Number (Page 5)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person, if applicable.
* **Location (Page 6)**: Specify the location where the patient was exposed to Mpox.
* **Physician Notes (Page 7)**: Add any relevant medical notes or observations made by the treating physician.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required information is entered.
* If you have any questions or concerns while completing this form, contact the designated healthcare authority.
