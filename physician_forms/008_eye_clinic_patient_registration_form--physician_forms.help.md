# Eye Clinic Patient Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Eye Clinic Patient Registration Form is a crucial document that captures the essential information of each patient visiting the eye clinic. Completing this form accurately and thoroughly helps our staff understand your needs and provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
3. **Email**: Provide your valid email address so we can contact you for any updates or follow-ups.
4. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. **Appointment Date**: Select the date and time of your scheduled appointment.
6. **Doctor**: Choose the doctor who will be attending to your appointment from the provided options.
7. **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
8. **Address**: Select any relevant medical addresses or locations related to your appointment (if applicable).
9. **Notes**: Add any important medical history or special requests related to your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: A text input field for your first name.
* **Last Name**: A text input field for your last name.
* **Email**: A text input field for your email address.
* **Date of Birth**: A date input field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Appointment Date**: A date input field for selecting the date and time of your appointment.
* **Doctor**: A dropdown menu to choose the doctor who will attend to your appointment.
* **Phone**: A text input field for your contact phone number.
* **Address**: A dropdown menu to select relevant medical addresses or locations related to your appointment.
* **Notes**: A text area for adding any important medical history or special requests related to your appointment.
