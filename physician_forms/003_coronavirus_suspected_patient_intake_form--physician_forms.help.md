# Coronavirus Suspected Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coronavirus Suspected Patient Intake Form is designed to collect information from patients suspected of having the coronavirus. This form will help healthcare professionals gather essential data to assess and treat patients efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information in the "Patient Information" section.
2. List any symptoms you are currently experiencing in the "Symptoms" section. Please select all that apply.
3. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
4. Provide any relevant medical information in the "Medical Information" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's name, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required: false): List all the symptoms you are experiencing, such as:
	+ Fever
	+ Cough
	+ Shortness of breath
	+ Sore throat
	+ Runny nose or congestion
	+ Headache
	+ Fatigue
	+ Muscle aches
	+ Diarrhea
	+ Abdominal pain
	+ Vomiting
	+ Nausea
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact details, such as your name, phone number, and email.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide any relevant medical information, such as medications, allergies, or previous medical conditions.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any symptom or medical information, consult with a healthcare professional.
- This form is for suspected cases of coronavirus; if you have any other medical concerns, please contact your healthcare provider directly.
