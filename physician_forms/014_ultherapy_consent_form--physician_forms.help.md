# Ultherapy Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ultherapy Consent Form is a document designed to obtain informed consent from patients before undergoing the Ultherapy treatment. This form gathers essential information about the patient's medical history, contact details, and consent to proceed with the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3.  Check the optional fields only if necessary and relevant to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **patient_consent_1** (Text, Optional): This field is used to obtain a general consent from the patient to proceed with the Ultherapy treatment. Please write a clear and concise statement indicating your understanding and agreement to the treatment.
*   **patient_consent_2** (Text, Optional): This field is used to obtain more specific consent from the patient about their medical history and any allergies or conditions that may impact the treatment. Please be as detailed as necessary to ensure the doctor and medical staff are aware of any potential risks or concerns.
*   **medical_history_1** (Text, Optional): This field is used to provide a general description of the patient's medical history. This could include any previous surgeries, current medical conditions, or allergies that may affect the treatment. Please provide as much detail as possible to ensure the doctor and medical staff are aware of any potential risks or concerns.

## Tips
*   Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it, as the patient's consent and medical history information will be used to tailor the treatment and ensure their safety.
*   Be as detailed as possible when providing medical history information to ensure the doctor and medical staff have all the necessary information to provide the best possible care.
