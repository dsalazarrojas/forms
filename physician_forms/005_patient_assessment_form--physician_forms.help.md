# Patient Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information for assessment and evaluation. Fill in the form with accurate and truthful information about the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form with the patient's information as accurately as possible.
2. Answer questions in detail, including symptoms and allergies.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields.
4. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (Text, Optional):** Enter the patient's basic information, such as name, age, and contact details.
* **Symptoms (Text, Optional):** Describe the patient's current symptoms, including any health issues or concerns.
* **Patient Assessment (Text, Optional):** Provide a brief assessment of the patient's condition, including any relevant medical history.
* **Patient Allergies (Text, Optional):** List any known allergies or sensitivities of the patient.
