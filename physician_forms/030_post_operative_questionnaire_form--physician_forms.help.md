# Post Operative Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Post Operative Questionnaire Form is used to collect post-operative information from patients after a surgical procedure. This information helps the healthcare team to:

* Assess the patient's recovery progress
* Identify any potential issues or complications
* Make necessary adjustments to the patient's care plan
* Improve post-operative care and outcomes

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your feedback about your surgical experience.
2. Rate your pain level from 1 to 10.
3. Select the medications you have been taking.
4. Choose the members of the surgical team who were involved in your procedure.
5. Indicate the surgical site(s) where you experienced pain.
6. Provide your follow-up appointment details.
7. Select your preferred method of follow-up contact.
8. (Optional) Provide your email address and phone number for follow-up contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Surgical Procedure**: Describe your experience with the surgical procedure.
* **Patient Feedback**: Share your thoughts and feelings about your experience.
* **Medical Outcome**: Select 'Yes' if you are recovering well, or 'No' if you are experiencing issues.
* **Pain Level**: Rate your pain level from 1 to 10.
* **Medication**: Select all medications you have been taking.
* **Surgical Team**: Choose the members of the surgical team who were involved in your procedure.
* **Surgical Site**: Select all areas where you experienced pain.
* **Follow Up**: Provide your follow-up appointment details, including date and time.
* **Follow Up Contact**: Select your preferred method of follow-up contact.
* **Doctor Name**: (Optional) Provide your doctor's name.
* **Patient Name**: (Optional) Provide your name.
* **Medical History**: (Optional) Provide any relevant medical history.
* **Doctor Contact**: (Optional) Select your preferred method of doctor contact.
* **Patient E-mail**: (Optional) Provide your email address for follow-up contact.
* **Patient Phone**: (Optional) Provide your phone number for follow-up contact.
* **Follow Up Time**: (Optional) Provide your follow-up appointment time.
* **Follow Up Date**: (Optional) Provide your follow-up appointment date.
* **Patient Address**: (Optional) Provide your address for follow-up contact.
* **Follow Up Location**: (Optional) Provide the location of your follow-up appointment.
* **Follow Up Note**: (Optional) Provide any additional follow-up notes.
* **Medication Allergy**: (Optional) Provide any medication allergies.
* **Medical Notes**: (Optional) Provide any additional medical notes.
* **Follow Up Status**: (Optional) Indicate the status of your follow-up appointment.

Please fill in all required fields and provide any additional information as necessary.
