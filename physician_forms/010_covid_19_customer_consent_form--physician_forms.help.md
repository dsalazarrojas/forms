# COVID 19 Customer Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from customers visiting healthcare facilities related to COVID-19, ensuring they understand the risks and protocols in place for their health and safety.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the first field, which is a required field.
2. Enter your **Customer ID or Account Number**, if applicable, which is not a required field.
3. Provide your **Phone Number**, which is a required field.
4. Enter your **Email Address**, which is a required field.
5. Select the **Date of Service** when you visited or intend to visit the facility.
6. Choose the **Type of Service** provided by the facility among the options given.
7. Read the **Risk Acknowledgment** section to understand the potential risks associated with COVID-19. If you understand these risks, select **Yes, I Understand**.
8. Acknowledge that the facility's safety **Protocols** are explained to you.
9. Confirm if you have **Any COVID-19 symptoms currently present**.
10. If you do have symptoms, describe them in the **Symptoms Present** field.
11. Provide your **Vaccination Status** as per your current situation.
12. Select **I Consent** to acknowledge your understanding and agreement to the risks and protocols.
13. Provide the **Consent Date**, which is the date when you sign this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name as per your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Customer ID or Account Number** (Optional): If you have an account number or ID, please enter it here. This helps the facility to identify you and provide personalized service.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your contact phone number to ensure we can reach you for any updates or questions.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications from our facility.
* **Date of Service** (Required): Choose the date when you visited or intend to visit the facility.
* **Type of Service** (Required): Select the type of service you received or will receive from the facility.
* **Risk Acknowledgment** (Not Required): This section outlines the potential risks associated with COVID-19. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **I understand risks associated with COVID-19** (Required): Select this option if you understand the risks mentioned in the previous section.
* **I acknowledge facility safety protocols** (Required): Confirm that the safety protocols were explained to you.
* **Any COVID-19 symptoms currently present** (Required): Indicate if you have any COVID-19 symptoms currently.
* **Symptoms Present** (Optional): If you have COVID-19 symptoms, describe them briefly here.
* **Vaccination Status** (Required): Choose your current vaccination status among the given options.
* **I Consent and Acknowledge Risks** (Required): Select this option to confirm your understanding and agreement to the risks and protocols.
* **Consent Date** (Required): Enter the date when you sign this form.

This form is designed to ensure that customers are informed about the risks and safety protocols associated with COVID-19, allowing them to make informed decisions about their care.
