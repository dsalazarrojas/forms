# Patient Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect important medical information from patients to help healthcare providers understand their medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information, including your name and any other identifying details.
2. Provide your current weight.
3. List any allergies or sensitivities you may have.
4. Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses or conditions.
5. Choose any relevant operations you have undergone.
6. Select any healthy habits you practice, such as regular exercise or healthy eating.
7. List any unhealthy habits that may be impacting your health.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal info** (`Personal info`, `text`, required/optional): Please fill out your personal details, including your name and any other identifying information.
* **Weight** (`Weight`, `number`, required): Please enter your current weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose any allergies or sensitivities you may have. This will help healthcare providers take necessary precautions during treatment.
* **Medical history** (`Medical history`, `text`, optional): Describe any previous illnesses or medical conditions you may have experienced.
* **Operations** (`Operations`, `select_one`, required): Choose any operations or surgeries you have undergone.
* **Healthy habits** (`Healthy habits`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any healthy habits you practice regularly, such as exercise or healthy eating.
* **Unhealthy habits** (`Unhealthy habits`, `select_multiple`, optional): List any unhealthy habits that may be impacting your health, such as smoking or excessive drinking.

## Tips

* Please answer all fields that apply to you.
* If you are unsure of any information, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
