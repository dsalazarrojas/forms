# Covid Contact Tracing Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid Contact Tracing Form Template is used to record patient information for contact tracing purposes during the Covid-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's ID number in the "patient_id" field.
2. Fill in the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
3. Enter the patient's age in the "patient_age" field.
4. Select the patient's sex from the options provided in the "patient_sex" field.
5. Enter the patient's phone number in the "patient_phone" field.
6. Enter the patient's email address in the "patient_email" field.
7. Select whether the patient has a physician on record in the "physician_name" field.
8. Enter the date and time the patient last visited in the "date_visited" and "time_visited" fields.
9. Select the type of contact the patient had with the medical facility (in person, phone, or internet) in the "contact_type" field.
10. Describe any symptoms the patient is experiencing in the "symptoms" field.
11. Enter any relevant notes from the physician in the "physician_note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_id** (number, required): Enter the patient's ID number.
* **patient_name** (text, required): Fill in the patient's name.
* **patient_age** (number, required): Enter the patient's age.
* **patient_sex** (select one, required): Select the patient's sex (male, female, other).
* **patient_phone** (text, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **patient_email** (email, required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **physician_name** (select one, required): Select whether the patient has a physician on record.
* **date_visited** (date, required): Enter the date the patient last visited.
* **time_visited** (time, required): Enter the time the patient last visited.
* **contact_type** (select one, required): Select the type of contact the patient had (in person, phone, internet).
* **symptoms** (text, required): Describe any symptoms the patient is experiencing.
* **physician_note** (text, optional): Enter any relevant notes from the physician.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly for proper contact tracing and patient care.
- If the patient has already been traced or has no contact tracing required, please answer 'no' to the questions related to contact tracing.
- If the patient does not have a physician on record, select 'No'.
