# Medical Treatment Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a subject's medical treatment, including the treatment category, details, start and end dates, and other relevant details. It's a crucial document that helps healthcare providers or medical facilities to keep accurate records and track patient care.

## How To Complete This Form

- To start, fill out the Medical Category field by selecting one of the three options provided: Acute, Chronic, or Emergency.
- Next, enter the Subject Name and Subject Email address of the person undergoing medical treatment.
- If available, provide the Subject Phone number for contact purposes.
- Enter the Date of Birth of the subject for accurate medical record keeping.
- Provide detailed information about the Medical Treatment Details, including the treatment's nature, duration, and any other relevant details.
- Fill out the Treatment Start Date and Treatment End Date to mark the beginning and end of the treatment.
- If applicable, enter the Treatment Duration for precise record keeping.
- Enter the Medical Treatment Provider's name and address for accurate documentation.
- Choose the Medical Treatment Status as Active, Inactive, or Terminated.
- Enter the Medical Record Number if available.
- Fill out the Medical Treatment Note section with any additional information.
- Confirm the Subject Agreement by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
- Finally, sign and date the form using your digital signature. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Category** (`Medical Category`, Select One): Select the category of the medical treatment, which can be Acute, Chronic, or Emergency.
* **Subject Name** (`Subject Name`, Text, Required): Enter the full name of the subject undergoing medical treatment.
* **Subject Email** (`Subject Email`, Email, Required): Enter the subject's email address for contact purposes.
* **Subject Phone** (`Subject Phone`, Text, Optional): If the subject's phone number is available, enter it for further contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, Date, Optional): Enter the subject's date of birth for accurate medical record keeping.
* **Medical Treatment Details** (`Medical Treatment Details`, Text, Required): Provide detailed information about the medical treatment, including its nature, duration, and any other relevant details.
* **Treatment Start Date** (`Treatment Start Date`, Date, Optional): Enter the start date of the medical treatment.
* **Treatment End Date** (`Treatment End Date`, Date, Optional): Enter the end date of the medical treatment.
* **Treatment Duration** (`Treatment Duration`, Number, Optional): Enter the duration of the medical treatment for precise record keeping.
* **Medical Treatment Provider** (`Medical Treatment Provider`, Text, Optional): Enter the name and address of the medical treatment provider for accurate documentation.
* **Medical Treatment Status** (`Medical Treatment Status`, Select One, Optional): Choose the medical treatment status as Active, Inactive, or Terminated.
* **Medical Record Number** (`Medical Record Number`, Text, Optional): Enter the medical record number if available.
* **Medical Treatment Note** (`Medical Treatment Note`, Note, Optional): Fill out any additional information related to the medical treatment.
* **Subject Agreement** (`Subject Agreement`, Select One, Required): Confirm the subject's agreement by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, Text, Optional): Sign the form using your digital signature.
* **Signature Date** (`Signature Date`, Date, Optional): Date the form using the date of signing.
* **Signature IP Address** (`Signature IP Address`, Text, Optional): Enter the IP address from which you signed the form.
* **IP Location** (`IP Location`, Text, Optional): Fill out the IP location from which you signed the form.
* **Medical Record Facility** (`Medical Record Facility`, Text, Optional): Enter the medical record facility name and address.
* **Subject Consent** (`Subject Consent`, Select One, Required): Confirm the subject's consent by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
