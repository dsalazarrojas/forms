# Medical Volunteer Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Volunteer Application form is designed to collect information from medical professionals who want to volunteer their skills to assist in hospitals or medical facilities. This form is used to gather your contact and professional details to ensure that we can match your skills with the needs of our facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the third field (this will be used as your contact email).
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the fourth field (this can be your personal or professional number).
5. If you have a **Medical License**, please enter it in the fifth field.
6. Provide your **Address** in the sixth field.
7. Choose your **Medical Specialty** from the seventh field.
8. Select the hospital where you want to volunteer from the eighth field (this can be your own hospital or a specific location where you want to volunteer).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Please enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone**: Please enter a phone number where you can be reached during office hours or in case of emergencies.
* **Medical License**: If you have a valid medical license, please enter it. This will help us verify your credentials.
* **Address**: Please enter your current address where you can be reached during the application process.
* **Medical Specialty**: Choose the area of medicine you are trained in (e.g., surgery, pediatrics, cardiology, etc.).
* **Hospital**: Choose the hospital where you want to volunteer. You can select the hospital where you work or a specific location where you want to volunteer.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, feel free to contact us.
* If you have any prior experience as a volunteer, please mention it in the comments section.
