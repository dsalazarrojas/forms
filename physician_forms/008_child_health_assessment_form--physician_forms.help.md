# Child Health Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Child Health Assessment Form is designed to gather essential information about a child's health and medical history. This form helps healthcare professionals to quickly and accurately assess a child's condition and provide personalized guidance and care. Please complete this form to help us better understand your child's unique needs.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the Child Health Assessment Form:

1. Select the assessment date and time.
2. Enter the child's legal first name and last name.
3. Provide the child's date of birth.
4. Select the child's age in years.
5. Choose the child's gender.
6. Enter the legal first name and last name of the parent or guardian.
7. Provide the primary phone number for contact.
8. Select the primary reason for the visit.
9. List any current symptoms the child is experiencing.
10. Indicate the duration of the symptoms.
11. Answer if the child has had a fever.
12. Enter the highest temperature recorded.
13. List any chronic medical conditions the child has.
14. List any known allergies.
15. List all current medications with dosages.
16. Indicate the child's immunization status.
17. List any relevant family medical history.
18. Describe the child's typical diet and nutrition.
19. Describe the child's sleep patterns.
20. Select the child's physical activity level.
21. Share any concerns about the child's development.
22. Enter the name of the child's primary care physician.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Date**: Select the date and time of the health assessment.
* **Child First Name**: Enter the child's legal first name.
* **Child Last Name**: Enter the child's legal last name.
* **Child Date of Birth**: Enter the child's date of birth.
* **Child Age**: Select the child's current age in years.
* **Child Gender**: Choose the child's gender from the provided options.
* **Parent or Guardian First Name**: Enter the legal first name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name**: Enter the legal last name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the primary phone number for contact.
* **Reason for Visit**: Select the primary reason for this health assessment.
* **Current Symptoms**: List any current symptoms the child is experiencing.
* **Symptom Duration**: Indicate the duration of the symptoms.
* **Fever Present**: Answer if the child has had a fever.
* **Highest Temperature Recorded**: Enter the highest temperature recorded if yes.
* **Chronic Medical Conditions**: List any chronic medical conditions the child has.
* **Allergies**: List any known allergies.
* **Current Medications**: List all current medications with dosages.
* **Immunization Status**: Indicate if the child is up to date on immunizations.
* **Relevant Family Medical History**: List any relevant family medical history.
* **Diet and Nutrition Notes**: Describe the child's typical diet and nutrition.
* **Sleep Patterns**: Describe the child's typical sleep patterns.
* **Physical Activity Level**: Select the child's physical activity level.
* **Developmental Concerns**: Share any concerns about the child's development.
* **Primary Care Physician Name**: Enter the name of the child's primary care physician.

Remember, please answer each question to the best of your ability, and if unsure, select "Unknown" or "Not applicable" when prompted.
