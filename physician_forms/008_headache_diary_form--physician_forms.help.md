# Headache Diary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Headache Diary Form is a tool used by healthcare providers to record and track a patient's headache history, including their symptoms, medication history, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Headache Diary**: Please select the page titled "Headache Diary" and complete the form by describing your headaches, including their frequency, severity, and any other relevant details.
2. **Symptoms**: Select one of the symptoms from the available options that best describes your headache experience. 
3. **Medication**: Please select the medication or treatments you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
4. **Trigger**: Please describe any factors that trigger or cause your headaches.
5. **Headache Diary Form 2**: If applicable, select "Yes" or "No" depending on whether you are currently experiencing frequent or severe headaches.
6. **Trigger**: Please describe any factors that trigger or cause your headaches.
7. **Medication**: Please select the medication or treatments you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
8. **Notes**: Please add any additional comments or notes about your headaches.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Headache Diary** (Page 1):
	+ **Title**: Enter the title of this form.
	+ **Label**: Enter the label for this form.
* **Headache Diary** (Page 2):
	+ **Symptoms**: Select one of the following:
		- Migraine: Characterized by intense headaches with sensitivity to light and sound.
		- Tension headache: A common type of headache caused by tension in the neck or scalp.
		- Cluster headache: A brief but very severe headache that occurs in groups.
		- Silent migraine: A headache without the characteristic migraine symptoms.
		- Hemiplegic migraine: A rare and severe form of migraine that can lead to temporary paralysis.
		- Stroke-like migraine with weakness: A rare form of migraine that can cause temporary weakness.
		- Basilar migraine: A rare form of migraine with symptoms similar to a stroke.
		- Chronic migraine: Persistent and recurring headaches.
		- Status migrainosus: A prolonged migraine with symptoms lasting more than 3 hours.
		- Ophthalmic migraine: A headache affecting one or both eyes.
* **Medication** (Page 3):
	+ **Medication**: Enter the name of the medication you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
* **Trigger** (Page 4):
	+ **Trigger**: Enter any factors that trigger or cause your headaches.
* **Headache Diary Form 2** (Page 5):
	+ **Yes/No**: Select "Yes" if you are currently experiencing frequent or severe headaches, otherwise select "No".
* **Trigger** (Page 6):
	+ **Trigger**: Enter any factors that trigger or cause your headaches.
* **Medication** (Page 7):
	+ **Medication**: Enter the name of the medication you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
* **Notes** (Page 8):
	+ **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your headaches.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are accurately and completely filling out each field, especially if a field is marked as "required".
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is for your individual medical history and should not be shared with others. 
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider.
