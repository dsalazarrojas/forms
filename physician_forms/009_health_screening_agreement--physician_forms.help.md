# health_screening_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general information and health screening data from users to gather essential details about their health status, medical conditions, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the general information in the "General Information" page.
2. If applicable, provide details about your health conditions in the "Health Conditions" page.
3. If necessary, answer the health screening questions in the "Health Screening" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (General Information)** (`text`, required/optional): Please enter your general information, such as your name, age, and contact details.
* **Health Conditions (Health Conditions)** (`text`, required/optional): If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, please list them here.
* **Health Screening (Health Screening)** (`text`, required/optional): Answer the health screening questions as instructed. If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when providing information.
- If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
- Please note that this form is for general information only and may not be suitable for all purposes. Use it responsibly and according to your needs.
