# Pediatric Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pediatric Consent Form is a document used to obtain informed consent from parents or guardians for medical treatments, procedures, and research studies involving children. This form ensures that parents or guardians are fully aware of and agree to the proposed treatment, procedure, or research study before it is performed on their child.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the form**: Before starting, review each field carefully to ensure you understand what information is being requested.
2. **Fill out the fields**: Fill out each field according to the instructions below.
3. **Submit the form**: Once all fields are completed, submit the form to the relevant authorities for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information**: 
  * **Label:** Parent Information
  * **Type:** number
  * **Required:** True
  * **Purpose:** This section asks for basic information about the parent or guardian, including their name and contact details. Please enter the following:
	+ First and last name of the parent or guardian
	+ Address
	+ Phone number
	+ Email
* **Treatment Authorization**: 
  * **Label:** Treatment Authorization
  * **Type:** select_one
  * **Required:** True
  * **Purpose:** This section authorizes the treatment or procedure for the child. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Procedure Authorization**: 
  * **Label:** Procedure Authorization
  * **Type:** select_multiple
  * *Required:* True
  * **Purpose:** This section authorizes the procedures for the child. Please select all applicable procedures from the following:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Research Study**: 
  * **Label:** Research Study
  * **Type:** select_one
  * **Required:** True
  * **Purpose:** This section authorizes the research study for the child. Please select one of the following:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Medical Provider**: 
  * **Label:** Medical Provider
  * **Type:** text
  * **Required:** False
  * **Purpose:** This field is optional and is used to identify the medical provider performing the treatment or procedure. If not applicable, leave blank.
* **Contact Information**: 
  * **Label:** Contact Information
  * **Type:** note
  * **Required:** False
  * **Purpose:** This is an optional note field for contact information, such as phone numbers or email addresses of people involved in the child's care.
