# COVID 19 Vaccine Update Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from patients about their COVID-19 vaccination status, preferences, and accessibility needs. The goal is to help healthcare providers understand patient needs and provide personalized updates and services.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the required fields**: Ensure you have answered all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. **Answer each question accurately**: Be honest and thorough in answering each question.
3. **Double-check your selections**: Review your choices before submitting the form to ensure they are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address you want us to use for updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide an alternative contact method. If you have a preferred phone number, we may use it to reach you.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Text message, or Either email or phone).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth for vaccine eligibility verification purposes.
* **Current Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_one, required): Indicate your current vaccination status (Unvaccinated, One dose received, Two doses received, Fully vaccinated, Boosted).
* **Interest in COVID-19 Vaccine** (`vaccine_interest`, select_one, required): Show your level of interest in getting vaccinated (Very interested, Somewhat interested, Unsure, Not interested).
* **Vaccine Type Preference** (`vaccine_type_preference`, select_one, optional): If you have received a vaccine, specify which type (Pfizer-BioNTech, Moderna, Johnson & Johnson, No preference).
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, optional): Select your age group for accessibility planning purposes (18-29, 30-44, 45-59, 60-74, 75 or older).
* **Priority Medical Condition** (`medical_conditions`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have any priority medical conditions (Heart disease, Diabetes, Respiratory disease, Immunocompromised, Healthcare worker, Other essential worker, None).
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Check any accessibility needs you may have (Transportation assistance, Interpreter services, Physical accessibility, Large print materials, No special needs).
* **Preferred Language** (`language_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose the language you would like to communicate in (English, Spanish, Chinese, Vietnamese, Tagalog, Other).
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter your ZIP code for location-based updates.
* **Preferred Update Frequency** (`update_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you would like to receive updates (Daily, Weekly, As updates become available, Only when vaccine is available).
* **Consent to Receive Updates** (`consent_communication`, select_one, required): Confirm you consent to receive updates regarding vaccine availability and scheduling.

## Tips
- Review each field carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- If you are unsure about any question, please contact your healthcare provider for guidance.
- Completing this form does not guarantee vaccine accessibility or priority.
