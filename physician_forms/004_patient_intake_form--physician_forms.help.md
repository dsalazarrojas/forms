# Patient Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive information about patients for initial intake or updating patient records. It includes sections for patient contact details, medical history, and medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

* Review each section carefully and fill out all required fields.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (1)**: This is the initial patient information section where you can provide general details about the patient.
* **Medical History (2)**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous medical conditions or treatments.
* **Contact Info (3)**: Select the preferred contact method(s) for the patient, such as Email, Phone, or Fax.
* **Contact Info 2 (4)**: This is another contact information section, similar to the previous one.
* **Date of Birth (5)**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Visit (6)**: Enter the time of the patient's visit in the format HH:MM.
* **Date of Visit (7)**: Enter the date of the patient's visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Note (8)**: Add any relevant notes or comments about the patient's visit or condition.
* **Medical Condition (9)**: List any medical conditions the patient has.
* **Medical Condition 2 (10)**: List another medical condition the patient has.
* **Medical Condition 3 (11)**: List another medical condition the patient has.
* **Medical Condition 4 (12)**: List another medical condition the patient has.
* **Medical Condition 5 (13)**: Select multiple medical conditions from the provided list.
* **Date of Next Visit (14)**: Enter the date of the patient's next visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Info 3 (15)**: Select the preferred contact method(s) for the patient.
* **Medical History 2 (16)**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous medical conditions or treatments.
* **Medical History 3 (17)**: Describe another aspect of the patient's medical history.
* **Medical History 4 (18)**: Describe another aspect of the patient's medical history.
* **Medical Condition 6 (19)**: List another medical condition the patient has.
* **Medical Condition 7 (20)**: List another medical condition the patient has.
* **Medical Condition 8 (21)**: List another medical condition the patient has.
* **Submit (22)**: Click the submit button to complete the form.
* **Date of Visit 2 (23)**: Enter the date of the patient's visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Visit 2 (24)**: Enter the time of the patient's visit in the format HH:MM.
* **Medical History 5 (25)**: Describe another aspect of the patient's medical history.

Note: Please review each field carefully and fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
