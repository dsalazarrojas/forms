# medical_physical_exam_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather medical information from patients, including general information, medical history, medical conditions, and other relevant details. It is designed to help physicians and healthcare providers collect and store patient data efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, paying attention to required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
2. Enter accurate and up-to-date information about the patient's general information, medical history, and medical conditions.
3. Select the relevant medical conditions from the dropdown list.
4. Sign and date the form to validate the information collected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information**: Enter the patient's general information, including name, medical specialty, address, and other relevant details.
* **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's contact information, including phone number and date of birth.
* **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history, including allergies, medical certifications, and examination results.
* **Medical Certifications**: Enter any medical certifications or licenses held by the patient.
* **Medical Examination**: Enter any relevant medical examination details.
