# Patch Test Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form collects information from patients before undergoing a patch test to identify potential allergens and provide personalized treatment recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, fill out the form completely and accurately based on your personal information.
2.  Ensure you have a scheduled patch test appointment before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID. This information is required for our records and to identify you during the patch test.
*   **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact number so we can reach you with any questions or updates about your patch test. Providing your number is optional, but it helps us stay in touch with you.
*   **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with test results and follow-up information. Please use a valid email address that you check regularly.
*   **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your attending physician who has referred you for the patch test. This ensures our medical staff can review your test results and coordinate care with your physician.
*   **Patient Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth to verify your age. Please note that you must be 18 years or older to complete this form.
*   **Test Name** (`test_name`, text, optional): If you have a specific test or treatment in mind, please enter the name here.
*   **Test Date** (`test_date`, date, required): Enter the date you have scheduled for your patch test. This is crucial for our staff to prepare your test and ensure accurate record-keeping.

## Tips

*   Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
*   We will review and verify your form submission before proceeding with your patch test.
