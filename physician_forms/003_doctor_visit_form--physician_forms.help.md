# Doctor Visit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doctor Visit Form is a crucial document used to collect and document vital information during a patient's visit to a healthcare provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. If a field is not applicable or not relevant, please indicate this with a clear statement.
4. If you are unsure about any information, please consult your healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for providing your contact information and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or treatments.
* **Medical History Details** (`medical_history_details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide as much detail as possible about your medical history.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): List all allergies you have, including medications and foods.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): List all medications you are currently taking.
* **Doctor Notes** (`doctor_notes`, `text`, optional/optional): This field is for your healthcare provider to document any notes about your visit.
* **Medical Tests** (`medical_tests`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate if you have had any recent medical tests.
* **Diagnosis** (`diagnosis`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate if you have been diagnosed with any medical conditions.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the treatment plan recommended by your healthcare provider.
* **Follow Up Info** (`follow_up_info`, `text`, optional/optional): Provide any follow-up information or instructions from your healthcare provider.
* **Doctor Visit Form Signature** (`doctor_visit_form_signature`, `text`, optional/optional): This field is for your signature to confirm you have completed the form.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Sign this field to confirm you have reviewed and understood the form.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required/optional): Date you signed the form.
* **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, `time`, required/optional): Time you signed the form.
* **Doctor Visit Form Version** (`doctor_visit_form_version`, `text`, optional/optional): This field is for your healthcare provider to document the form version.
* **Doctor Visit Form Revision** (`doctor_visit_form_revision`, `number`, optional/optional): This field is for your healthcare provider to document the form revision number.
* **Doctor Visit Form Last Updated** (`doctor_visit_form_last_updated`, `date`, optional/optional): This field is for your healthcare provider to document the last update date.
* **Doctor Visit Form Last Updated By** (`doctor_visit_form_last_updated_by`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate who last updated the form.
* **Doctor Visit Form Last Updated By Name** (`doctor_visit_form_last_updated_by_name`, `text`, optional/optional): This field is for your healthcare provider to document the last updated by name.
* **Doctor Visit Form Last Updated By Email** (`doctor_visit_form_last_updated_by_email`, `email`, optional/optional): This field is for your healthcare provider to document the last updated by email.
* **Doctor Visit Form Last Updated At Time** (`doctor_visit_form_last_updated_at_time`, `time`, optional/optional): This field is for your healthcare provider to document the last updated at time.
* **Doctor Visit Form Last Updated By Department** (`doctor_visit_form_last_updated_by_department`, `text`, optional/optional): This field is for your healthcare provider to document the last updated by department.
* **Doctor Visit Form Last Updated By Tool** (`doctor_visit_form_last_updated_by_tool`, `text`, optional/optional): This field is for your healthcare provider to document the last updated by tool.

## Tips
* Please review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult your healthcare provider.
* This form is a critical tool for effective patient care, and accurate completion is essential.
