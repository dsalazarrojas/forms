# Telehealth Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This Telehealth Release Form is designed to document the release of a patient from telehealth services. It captures essential information about the patient's treatment, provider, and reason for discharge. The form is intended to be completed by healthcare providers for patients who have received care and services through telehealth platforms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form in a new tab**: You can access the form by opening a new tab on your web browser. This will allow you to complete the form without interfering with other browser tabs.
2. **Enter patient information**: Start by entering the patient's information in the "patient info" section. This includes providing the patient's name and other relevant details.
3. **Choose a release date**: Select the date of the patient's release from telehealth services. If the patient was transferred to another facility or provider, you can also enter a second release date.
4. **Enter the reason for release**: Describe the reason why the patient was released from telehealth services. This could be due to a variety of factors, such as the patient's recovery, transfer to another facility, or other reasons.
5. **Provide provider information**: Enter any relevant information about the provider who cared for the patient during their telehealth services.
6. **Enter patient's date of birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth, which is an essential piece of identifying information.
7. **Enter patient's phone number**: If you have the patient's phone number, enter it in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient info** (required)
  * The patient's name and other relevant identifying information.
* **release date** (required)
  * The date of the patient's release from telehealth services.
* **reason for release** (required)
  * A brief description of why the patient was released from telehealth services.
* **release provider** (optional)
  * Information about the provider who cared for the patient during their telehealth services.
* **date of birth** (required)
  * The patient's date of birth, which is an essential piece of identifying information.
* **provider info** (optional)
  * Additional information about the provider who cared for the patient during their telehealth services.
* **reason for discharge** (optional)
  * A description of why the patient was discharged from telehealth services.
* **reason for transfer** (optional)
  * A description of why the patient was transferred to another facility or provider.
* **reason for death** (optional)
  * A description of why the patient passed away.
* **reason for discharge other** (optional)
  * If the reason for discharge is not listed above, provide a brief description of the reason.
* **release provider info** (optional)
  * Additional information about the provider who cared for the patient during their telehealth services.
* **patient phone** (optional)
  * The patient's phone number, which can be used for follow-up contact.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information about the patient's treatment and release.
* Double-check the form for any required fields before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the patient or their representative for clarification.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
