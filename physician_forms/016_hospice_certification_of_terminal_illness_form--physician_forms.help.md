# Hospice Certification Of Terminal Illness Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to certify a terminal illness for a patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the certifying physician's name in the "Certifying Physician Name" field.
3. Enter the date of certification in the "Date of Certification" field.
4. Enter the medical record number in the "Medical Record Number" field.
5. Enter the date of admission in the "Date of Admission" field.
6. Enter the patient's residence in the "Patient Residence" field.
7. Select the hospice name from the drop-down menu in the "Hospice Name" field.
8. Select the patient's status as "Terminal" or "Non-Terminal" in the "Patient Status" field.
9. Enter the hospice ID in the "Hospice ID" field.
10. Enter the certifying physician's address in the "Certifying Physician Address" field.
11. Enter the certifying physician's phone number in the "Certifying Physician Phone" field.
12. Enter the certifying physician's fax number in the "Certifying Physician Fax Number" field.
13. Enter the certifying physician's license number in the "Certifying Physician License Number" field.
14. Enter the date of license issuance in the "Certifying Physician Date of License Issuance" field.
15. Enter the state where the physician's license was issued in the "Certifying Physician State License Issued" field.
16. Enter the medical school attended by the certifying physician in the "Certifying Physician Medical School Attended" field.
17. Enter the certifying physician's specialty in the "Certifying Physician Specialty" field.
18. Enter the certifying physician's NPI (National Provider Identifier) in the "Certifying Physician NPI" field.
19. Enter the certifying physician's address line 1 in the "Certifying Physician Address Line 1" field.
20. Enter the certifying physician's address line 2 in the "Certifying Physician Address Line 2" field.
21. Select the hospice name again in the "Hospice Name" field to confirm.
22. Sign and date the form with the certifying physician's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name** (Patient Name, Text, Optional): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
- **Certifying Physician Name** (Certifying Physician Name, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the certifying physician.
- **Date of Certification** (Date of Certification, Date, Optional): Enter the date of certification of the terminal illness.
- **Medical Record Number** (Medical Record Number, Text, Optional): Enter the patient's medical record number.
- **Date of Admission** (Date of Admission, Date, Optional): Enter the date of admission to the hospice.
- **Patient Residence** (Patient Residence, Text, Optional): Enter the patient's residence address.
- **Hospice Name** (Hospice Name, Select One, Optional): Select the name of the hospice that the patient is certified to.
- **Patient Status** (Patient Status, Select Multiple, Required): Select "Terminal" or "Non-Terminal" to indicate the patient's status.
- **Hospice ID** (Hospice ID, Text, Optional): Enter the ID number of the hospice that the patient is certified to.
- **Certifying Physician Date of Last Evaluation** (Certifying Physician Date of Last Evaluation, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the last evaluation by the certifying physician.
- **Hospice Provider Number** (Hospice Provider Number, Text, Optional): Enter the provider number of the hospice that the patient is certified to.
- **Certifying Physician Date of Last Visit** (Certifying Physician Date of Last Visit, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the last visit by the certifying physician.
- **Certifying Physician Signature** (Certifying Physician Signature, Text, Optional): Sign and date the form with the certifying physician's signature.
