# Gluten Free Prescription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gluten Free Prescription Form is designed to help physicians gather relevant medical information from patients suffering from gluten-related medical conditions, such as celiac disease or gluten intolerance. By filling out this form, patients can provide their medical history, dietary allergies, and other essential details required for a proper diagnosis and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the patient's personal details, such as their name and contact information.
*   Describe the reason for the visit and the medical condition being addressed.
*   Provide information about any medical conditions or allergies you have.
*   Explain your dietary habits and any gluten-related concerns.
*   Write down your doctor's signature, confirming you've received a copy of this form.
*   Sign the form with your own signature, confirming you've read and understood the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Information** (`Patient Information`, text, required): Please enter your full name, date of birth, and contact information (phone number and email).
*   **Physician Information** (`Physician Information`, text, required): Please enter your doctor's name and contact information (phone number and email).
*   **Reason** (`Reason`, text, required): Describe the reason for your visit and the medical condition being addressed (e.g., gluten intolerance, celiac disease).
*   **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`, text, required): Provide detailed information about any existing medical conditions, such as allergies or previous medical issues.
*   **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, required): Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or treatments.
*   **Dietary Allergies** (`Dietary Allergies`, text, required): Specify any dietary allergies or restrictions you have (e.g., gluten, lactose, nuts).
*   **Gluten-Free Diagnosis** (`Gluten-Free Diagnosis`, text, required): Explain how you were diagnosed with gluten-related issues (e.g., blood tests, symptoms, diagnosis by a doctor).
*   **Doctor's Signature** (`Doctor's Signature`, text, required): Write your doctor's signature, confirming they have received a copy of this form.
*   **Patient's Signature** (`Patient's Signature`, text, required): Sign your own signature, confirming you've read and understood the information provided.
*   **Date Submitted** (`Date Submitted`, date, optional): Record the date and time you submitted this form.

## Tips

*   Please be as detailed as possible when filling out this form to ensure your doctor can provide the best possible care.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your doctor.
*   Make sure to keep a copy of this form for your records.
