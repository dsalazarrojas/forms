# telehealth_intake_and_signature_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The telehealth_intake_and_signature_form is a form used for patient intake and signature capture during telehealth consultations. It collects essential information from both the patient and the healthcare provider. This form is designed to be easily accessible and completed by both parties, ensuring accurate and efficient data collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Patient Information**: Enter your personal information, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
2. **Consent to Participate**: Indicate whether you consent to participate in the telehealth consultation by selecting "Yes" or "No".
3. **Signature Information**: Sign your name, date, and time. You can select a date using the date picker field and a time using the time selector.
4. **Additional Patient Information**: Provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your health care.
5. **Consent to Share**: Indicate whether you consent to share your information with other parties (Insurance Company, Healthcare Organization, or Other).
6. **Provider Information**: Enter the healthcare provider's name, title, and contact information.
7. **Facility Information**: Enter the facility's name, address, city, state, zip, phone number, and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`Physician Name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it should appear on your medical record.
* **Consent to Participate** (`Consent to Participate in Telehealth Consultation`, `select_one`, required): Choose "Yes" to indicate your agreement to participate in the telehealth consultation.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Select the current date using the date picker field.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required): Choose the current time using the time selector.
* **Patient Signature** (`Patient Signature`, `note`, required): Sign your name in the designated space.
* **Physician Signature** (`Physician Signature`, `note`, required): Sign the healthcare provider's name in the designated space.
* **Physician Name** (`Physician Name`, `text`, required): Enter the healthcare provider's name.
* **Physician Title** (`Physician Title`, `text`, required): Enter the healthcare provider's title.
* **Facility** (`Facility`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the healthcare facility.
* **Facility Address** (`Facility Address`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's address.
* **Facility City** (`Facility City`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's city.
* **Facility State** (`Facility State`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's state.
* **Facility Zip** (`Facility Zip`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's zip code.
* **Facility Phone** (`Facility Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's phone number.
* **Facility Email** (`Facility Email`, `text`, required): Enter the facility's email address.
* **Additional Patient Information** (`Additional Patient Information`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information you feel is relevant to your health care.
* **Consent to Share** (`Consent to Share`, `select_one`, required): Choose "Yes" to indicate your agreement to share your information with other parties (Insurance Company, Healthcare Organization, or Other).
* **Share with** (`Share with`, `select_multiple`, required): Select which parties you consent to share your information with.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`Email`, `text`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Street** (`Street`, `text`, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`City`, `text`, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (`State`, `text`, required): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (`Zip`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code.
