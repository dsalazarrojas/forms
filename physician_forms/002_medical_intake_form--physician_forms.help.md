# medical_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical_intake_form is a comprehensive form used to gather detailed information about a patient's medical history, conditions, and treatments. This information will be used to provide an accurate diagnosis and treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant fields to fill out based on the patient's needs.
2. Fill out each field according to the field-by-field explanations provided.
3. Review and submit the form once all necessary fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Contact Info** (`patient_contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter the patient's contact information, including name, address, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the patient's medical history, including any illnesses, injuries, or conditions they have experienced.
* **Past Surgeries** (`past_surgeries`, `text`, required/optional): List any past surgeries the patient has undergone.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required/optional): Identify any allergies the patient has.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any ongoing or past medical conditions affecting the patient.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, `text`, required/optional): Share any notable family medical history.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required/optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Medical Procedures** (`medical_procedures`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any medical procedures the patient has undergone or is scheduled to undergo.
* **Patient Genetics** (`patient_genetics`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any relevant genetic information about the patient.
* **Medical Symptoms** (`medical_symptoms`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any current medical symptoms the patient is experiencing.
* **Medical Specialties** (`medical_specialties`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the patient's primary medical specialty (e.g., General Practice, Internal Medicine, Surgery, etc.).
* **Doctor Info** (`doctor_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any necessary information about the treating doctor or healthcare provider.

Note: Please refer to the provided options for the Medical Specialties field for a list of possible selections. 

## Tips

* Be as thorough as possible when filling out the form to provide accurate and comprehensive patient information.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out to ensure a complete medical history.
* If unsure about any field, please contact a healthcare professional for guidance.
