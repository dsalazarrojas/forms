# Toilet Training Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Toilet Training Checklist" form is used to gather information from parents or caregivers about a child's toilet training progress, status, and any associated observations or medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct gender of the child you are providing information about.
2. Choose whether or not the child's training has started, is in progress, or is completed.
3. Provide any additional next steps that need to be taken regarding the child's training.
4. Enter any notes or comments about the child's training progress.
5. Select any abnormal observations during the training process.
6. Choose the appropriate medical history for the child.
7. Enter any physician recommendations related to the child's training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Child Info** (`child_info`, select_one, required): Select the gender of the child you are providing information about.
* **Training Status** (`training_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether the child's training has not started, is in progress, or is completed.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required): Enter any next steps or actions that need to be taken regarding the child's training.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the child's training progress.
* **Observations** (`observations`, select_one, optional): Select any abnormal observations or issues that may have arisen during the training process.
* **Physician Recommendations** (`physician_recommendations`, text, optional): Enter any physician recommendations related to the child's training.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the child has any medical history related to the toilet training process.
