# COVID 19 Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a crucial document that ensures patients understand their COVID-19 treatment options, potential risks, and rights before commencing treatment. It's essential to complete this form carefully and accurately to ensure that you are making an informed decision about your treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, answering all questions accurately and truthfully.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand the questions and information being asked.
3. If you are unsure about any question or have concerns, please discuss them with your healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your medical record.
* **Medical Record Number**: Your hospital or medical ID number, if applicable.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in MM/DD format (e.g., 02/12/1990).
* **COVID-19 Diagnosis Date**: The date you were diagnosed with COVID-19.
* **Treating Physician Name**: The name of your treating physician.
* **-- Patient Education and Information --**: This section provides information about your condition and treatment options. Read this section carefully and ask your healthcare provider if you have any questions.
* **I understand my COVID-19 diagnosis and condition severity**: Please select "I agree" if you understand your condition and its severity.
* **I have been informed about the proposed treatment options**: Please select "I agree" if you have been informed about your treatment options.
* **The benefits and risks of treatment have been explained**: Please select "I agree" if you have been informed about the potential benefits and risks of your treatment.
* **-- Treatment Selection --**: This section outlines the proposed treatment options. Read this section carefully and discuss your options with your healthcare provider.
* **Proposed Treatment Type**: Select the treatment option that best suits your condition:
	+ **Antivirals**: Antiviral medications.
	+ **Monoclonal antibodies**: Monoclonal antibodies therapy.
	+ **Immunomodulators**: Immunomodulators therapy.
	+ **Oxygen therapy**: Oxygen therapy.
	+ **Mechanical ventilation**: Mechanical ventilation.
	+ **Experimental treatment**: Experimental treatment (please discuss with your healthcare provider).
	+ **Other**: Specify the treatment option you have chosen.
* **Specific Medication or Treatment Name**: If you have chosen an option other than "Other", please enter the name of the medication or treatment you have selected.
* **Estimated Treatment Duration**: If you have chosen an option other than "Other", please estimate the treatment duration.
* **-- Medication Allergies and History --**: This section collects information about your medical history and allergies.
* **Known medication allergies**: List any known allergies you have.
* **Current medications being taken**: List any current medications you are taking.
* **Other chronic medical conditions**: List any other chronic medical conditions you have.
* **-- Risks and Side Effects --**: This section provides information about potential risks and side effects.
* **I acknowledge potential risks and side effects**: Please select "I agree" if you understand and acknowledge the potential risks and side effects.
* **I understand alternative treatment options**: Please select "I agree" if you have been informed about alternative treatment options.
* **I understand the risks of refusing treatment**: Please select "I agree" if you understand the risks of refusing treatment.
* **-- Consent Statement --**: This section outlines the consent process.
* **I voluntarily consent to the proposed COVID-19 treatment**: Please select "I consent" if you agree to the treatment.
* **All my questions have been answered to my satisfaction**: Please select "True" if you have had all your questions answered.
* **I understand I have the right to withdraw consent at any time**: Please select "I understand" if you understand your right to withdraw consent.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: The name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: The phone number of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
