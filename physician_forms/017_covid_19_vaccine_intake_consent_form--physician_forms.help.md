# COVID 19 Vaccine Intake Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from patients before administering the COVID-19 vaccine. It will help the healthcare provider understand the patient's medical history, vaccination status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) to ensure accurate and complete data collection.
2. Enter your personal details in the "Patient Information" section.
3. Answer "Yes" or "No" for questions regarding medical history and vaccination status in the "Medical History" and "COVID-19 History" sections.
4. Select the correct options for "Immunocompromised" and "Pregnant or Breastfeeding" questions.
5. Confirm your consent in the "Patient Consent" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`intake_patient_name`): Enter your complete legal name as per your identity documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`intake_date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Email Address** (`intake_patient_email`): Enter your contact email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`intake_patient_phone`): Enter your contact phone number for communication purposes.
* **Are You Immunocompromised** (`intake_immunocompromised`): Select "Yes" if you have a weakened immune system, "No" otherwise.
* **Pregnant or Breastfeeding** (`intake_pregnancy`): Select "Pregnant", "Breastfeeding", "Neither", or "Prefer Not to Say" based on your current status.
* **Have You Had COVID-19** (`intake_previous_covid`): Select "Yes" if you have been infected with COVID-19 before, "No", or "Unsure".
* **Number of COVID-19 Vaccine Doses Received** (`intake_previous_vaccines`): Enter the number of doses you have received so far.
* **Dose Type for Today** (`intake_vaccine_dose_type`): Select the type of dose you will receive today (First Dose, Second Dose, Booster Dose, or Additional Dose).
* **Vaccine Product for Today** (`intake_vaccine_product`): Select the type of vaccine you will receive today (Pfizer-BioNTech, Moderna, Johnson & Johnson, Novavax, or To Be Determined).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`intake_emergency_contact`): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`intake_emergency_phone`): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Known Allergies** (`intake_allergies`): List any known allergies or sensitivities you may have.
* **Current Medications** (`intake_current_medications`): List all medications you are currently taking.
* **Chronic Medical Conditions** (`intake_chronic_conditions`): List any ongoing health conditions you may have.
* **Previous Adverse Vaccine Reactions** (`intake_adverse_reactions`): Report any previous adverse reactions you may have experienced from vaccines.
* **Consent** (`intake_consent_header`): Review and confirm your consent for the vaccination process.

Tips:
1. Please ensure you have filled in all required fields carefully and accurately.
2. If you're unsure or have questions, do not hesitate to ask the healthcare provider for assistance.
3. Complete the form carefully to avoid delays in the vaccination process.
