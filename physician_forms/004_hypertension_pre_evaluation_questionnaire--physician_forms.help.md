### Hypertension Pre Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide

### Purpose
The Hypertension Pre Evaluation Questionnaire is a form used to gather information from patients who have been diagnosed with hypertension (high blood pressure) or are at risk of developing high blood pressure. The form will help healthcare professionals assess the patient's blood pressure levels and identify potential risks associated with their condition.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Select the correct answer for each question based on the patient's information.
