# Student Disability Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from a student for disability verification purposes, typically used by educational institutions or organizations to verify a student's disability status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The student should start by completing the Physician Form and School Counselor Form sections, providing any required information from their physician or school counselor.
2. Once the necessary information is collected, the student should proceed to the Verification Form section.
3. In the Verification Form, the student must select one of the three options: Student Verified, Not Verified, or Unable to Verify.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Physician Form** (`physician_form`, text, optional)
This field is used to collect information from the student's physician. The student should provide any relevant medical information or documentation from their physician in this section.

### 2. **School Counselor Form** (`school_counselor_form`, text, optional)
This field is used to collect information from the student's school counselor. The student should provide any relevant information or documentation from their school counselor in this section.

### 3. **Verification Form** (`verification_form`, select_one, optional)
*   **Student Verified**: Select this option if the student's disability status has been verified by their physician or school counselor.
*   **Not Verified**: Select this option if the student's disability status has not been verified by their physician or school counselor.
*   **Unable to Verify**: Select this option if the student's disability status cannot be verified due to lack of information or other reasons.

## Tips
*   The student should ensure they have all necessary information and documentation before completing this form.
*   The student should review each section carefully and ensure accurate information is provided.
*   If any issues or questions arise, the student should contact their school counselor or physician for assistance.
