# Patient Exit Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Exit Survey is a tool used to gather patient feedback and insights about their experience at our healthcare facility. This survey aims to measure patient satisfaction, identify areas of improvement, and help staff refine their services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Survey Header**: This is a note field where you can provide any additional information about your visit or experience.
2. **Patient Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or medical record.
3. **Rating**: Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5, with 5 being the highest.
4. **Question 1**: Select 'Yes' if you experienced any issues with your appointment or care, or 'No' if everything went smoothly.
5. **Question 2**: Indicate which of the following issues you experienced during your visit (Select all that apply):
	* 'Yes' for each issue that occurred.
6. **Question 3**: Select 'Yes' if you experienced any discomfort, pain, or other issues during your treatment.
7. **Question 4**: Indicate which issues you experienced during your visit (Select all that apply):
	* 'Yes' for each issue that occurred.
8. **Additional Comments**: Share any additional comments or concerns you have about your visit.
9. **Patient Rating**: Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5, with 5 being the highest.
10. **Staff Rating**: Rate the staff's attitude, professionalism, and care on a scale of 1-5, with 5 being the highest.
11. **Staff Comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback about the staff who cared for you.
12. **Physician**: Enter the name of your physician.
13. **Clinic**: Enter the name of the clinic where you visited.
14. **Department**: Enter the department where you received care.
15. **Patient Type**: Indicate your patient type (e.g., new patient, follow-up, etc.).
16. **Date**: Enter the date of your visit.
17. **Time**: Enter the time of your visit.
18. **Email**: Enter your email address (optional).
19. **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
20. **Additional Comments**: Share any additional comments or concerns you have about your visit.

## Tips

* Be honest and open about your experience.
* Take your time to complete the form carefully.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please raise them in the space provided.
* Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback!
