# COVID-19 Vaccine First Dose Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information from patients for their first dose COVID-19 vaccine appointment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your patient's full name.
2.  Provide your patient's email address.
3.  Enter your patient's phone number.
4.  Enter your patient's date of birth.
5.  Select the preferred appointment date and time.
6.  Choose a preferred location for the appointment.
7.  Select the type of vaccine you prefer.
8.  If your patient is insured, select the insurance status.
9.  Enter the name of your patient's primary care provider.
10. If your patient has allergies or medical conditions, provide the necessary information.
11. Confirm your patient's pregnancy status.
12. Indicate if your patient has been previously diagnosed with COVID-19 or is experiencing symptoms.
13. Confirm if your patient has had close contact with a COVID-19 case recently.
14. Check if your patient has a current fever or elevated temperature.
15. Indicate if your patient has recently received other vaccines.
16. Confirm your consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Patient Full Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your patient's full name.
*  **Email Address** (`patient_email`, email, required): Provide your patient's email address.
*  **Phone Number** (`patient_phone`, text, required): Enter your patient's phone number.
*  **Date of Birth** (`patient_dob`, date, required): Enter your patient's date of birth.
*  **Preferred Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Select the preferred appointment date for your patient.
*  **Preferred Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required): Choose a preferred appointment time for your patient.
*  **Preferred Location** (`appointment_location`, text, required): Choose a preferred location for the appointment.
*  **Vaccine Type Preference** (`vaccine_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of vaccine you prefer.
*  **Insurance Status** (`insurance_status`, select_one, required): If your patient is insured, select their insurance status.
*  **Primary Care Provider Name** (`primary_care_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of your patient's primary care provider.
*  **Allergies to vaccine components** (`allergies_vaccines`, select_one, required): Confirm if your patient has allergies or is unsure about vaccine components.
*  **If yes, describe allergies** (`allergy_description`, text, optional): If your patient has allergies, describe them here.
*  **Chronic medical conditions** (`chronic_conditions`, select_one, required): Confirm if your patient has chronic medical conditions.
*  **If yes, list conditions** (`condition_list`, text, optional): If your patient has chronic conditions, list them here.
*  **Immunocompromised or immunosuppressed** (`immunocompromised`, select_one, required): Confirm if your patient is immunocompromised or immunosuppressed.
*  **Currently taking medications** (`current_medications`, text, optional): If your patient takes medications, enter them here.
*  **Pregnancy Status** (`pregnancy_status`, select_one, required): Confirm your patient's pregnancy status.
*  **Previously diagnosed with COVID-19** (`prior_covid`, select_one, required): Indicate if your patient has been previously diagnosed with COVID-19 or is unsure.
*  **If yes, date of diagnosis** (`covid_diagnosis_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of diagnosis if applicable.
*  **Experiencing COVID-19 symptoms** (`covid_symptoms`, select_one, required): Confirm if your patient is experiencing COVID-19 symptoms.
*  **Close contact with COVID-19 case recently** (`close_contact`, select_one, required): Confirm if your patient has had close contact with a COVID-19 case recently.
*  **Current fever or elevated temperature** (`fever`, select_one, required): Check if your patient has a current fever or elevated temperature.
*  **Recently received other vaccines** (`recent_vaccine`, select_one, required): Indicate if your patient has recently received other vaccines.
*  **If yes, date of other vaccine** (`other_vaccine_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the other vaccine if applicable.
*  **Consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine** (`consent`, select_one, required): Confirm your consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine.

## Tips

*   Be honest and accurate when providing information about your patient's medical history.
*   If you are unsure about any of the questions, choose "Unsure" and provide a brief explanation in the relevant field.
*   Ensure that all fields are completed accurately to ensure a successful and efficient vaccination process.
