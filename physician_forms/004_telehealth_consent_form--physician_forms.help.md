# Telehealth Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telehealth Consent Form is a mandatory document for patients undergoing remote consultations, treatments, or other telehealth services. It aims to establish a clear understanding of the patient's rights, responsibilities, and expectations in a virtual or remote healthcare environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Telehealth Consent Form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each question and its purpose.
2. Enter your information in the designated fields. Please ensure you fill out each section accurately and truthfully.
3. Sign and date the form, acknowledging that you have read and understood the content.
4. Submit the form to the healthcare provider or organization handling your telehealth session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required/optional): This section is where you will enter your basic contact information, including your name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Telehealth Information** (`telehealth_info`, text, required/optional): In this section, provide details about your telehealth experience, such as the type of services you are receiving or the platforms you are using.
* **Consent** (`consent`, number, required/optional): This field requires you to acknowledge your understanding of the telehealth consent terms. Please review and select the correct answer according to your understanding.
* **Electronic Signature** (`electronic_signature`, text, required/optional): Here, you will sign electronically, confirming that you have read and understood the consent form. Make sure to sign your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you are submitting this form, which is essential for record-keeping purposes.

## Tips
- Be sure to review and understand each question before answering.
- If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
- Ensure you save and print this form for your records, if needed.
- If you are experiencing difficulty filling out this form, consult your healthcare provider or the organization's support team for assistance.
