# New Patient Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document new patient information for the hospital's patient registry system. It will be used to create a patient profile, which will be used for administrative and clinical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following sections:

* Patient Information: Provide the patient's first and last name, date of birth, and contact information.
* Emergency Contact: Provide the name and contact information of the patient's emergency contact person.
* Medical History: Provide any relevant medical history, conditions, or medications that may be relevant to the patient's care.
* Physician Information: If applicable, provide the patient's current physician's name and hospital affiliation.
* Additional Information: If necessary, provide any additional information that may be relevant to the patient's care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**
	+ **First Name**: Enter the patient's first name.
	+ **Last Name**: Enter the patient's last name.
	+ **Date Of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy format).
	+ **Contact Information**: Enter the patient's contact information, including address, phone number, and email.
* **Emergency Contact**
	+ **Emergency First Name**: Enter the emergency contact person's first name.
	+ **Emergency Last Name**: Enter the emergency contact person's last name.
	+ **Relation**: Select the relation of the emergency contact person (e.g., Mother, Father, Spouse, Partner, Parent, Child, Other).
	+ **Emergency Phone**: Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
	+ **Emergency Email**: Enter the emergency contact person's email (if applicable).
* **Medical History**
	+ **Condition**: Enter any relevant medical conditions or health issues affecting the patient.
	+ **Medication**: Enter any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Physician Information**
	+ **Physician Name**: If applicable, enter the patient's current physician's name.
	+ **Hospital Affiliation**: If applicable, enter the hospital where the patient is currently being treated.
* **Additional Information**: If necessary, provide any additional information that may be relevant to the patient's care.
