# Patch Test Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patch Test Consent Form is used for patients to provide consent for a patch test, a diagnostic procedure used to check for allergic reactions or sensitivities to specific substances. This form is designed to ensure that the patient understands the potential risks and benefits of the test and agrees to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what information is required.
2. Fill in your name and contact information (client name, email, and phone number) as prompted.
3. Provide your date of birth for age verification purposes.
4. Enter your address so we can contact you with test results and any follow-up information.
5. Enter the name of the physician performing the test.
6. If you have any known medical conditions, please list them in the provided field.
7. Select a date and time for the patch test.
8. Review and sign the form to confirm your understanding and consent to the test.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patch Test Consent Form** (`patch_test_consent_form`, text, required/optional): This field is the title of the form, which you are required to fill in to acknowledge your understanding of the test.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with test results and any follow-up information.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with test results and any follow-up information.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Please enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address so we can contact you with test results and any follow-up information.
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of the physician performing the test.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required/optional): If you have any known medical conditions, please list them here.
* **Patch Test Date** (`patch_test_date`, date, required/optional): Select a date for the patch test.
* **Patch Test Time** (`patch_test_time`, time, required/optional): Select a time for the patch test.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what information is required.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form or the test, please don't hesitate to ask your physician or healthcare provider.
