# Dermatology Telehealth Medical Examination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dermatology Telehealth Medical Examination Form is a guide to help patients and physicians complete the necessary information during a telehealth medical examination in the field of dermatology. This form is designed to collect detailed information about the patient's medical history, examination, and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Complete each section with accurate and relevant information.
3. Be as thorough as possible, as this information will be used to guide the telehealth examination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`Patient Information`, `text`, required: false): 
This field is used to collect personal details about the patient, such as name, contact information, and other relevant details.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, required: false): 
This field is used to gather information about the patient's medical history, including any previous conditions, allergies, or treatments.
* **Examination** (`Examination`, `text`, required: false): 
This field is used to document the results of the examination, including any findings, diagnoses, and recommendations.
* **Treatment** (`Treatment`, `text`, required: false): 
This field is used to outline the treatment plan, including any prescribed medications, procedures, or further steps to be taken.
* **Photos** (`Photos`, `text`, required: false): 
This field is used to upload any relevant photos or images related to the patient's condition.
* **Payment** (`Payment`, `text`, required: false): 
This field is used to collect information about payment or insurance details.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, `text`, required: false): 
This field is used to obtain the patient's digital signature, indicating consent for the examination and any subsequent treatment.
* **Upload Photos** (`Upload Photos`, `text`, required: false): 
This field is used to upload any relevant photos or images related to the patient's condition.
* **Patient Consent** (`Patient Consent`, `text`, required: false): 
This field is used to obtain the patient's consent for the examination and any subsequent treatment.
* **Physician Information** (`Physician Information`, `text`, required: false): 
This field is used to collect information about the physician's details, including name, contact, and credentials.
* **Medical Record** (`Medical Record`, `text`, required: false): 
This field is used to document the patient's medical record, including any relevant notes or observations.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when completing each field.
* Ensure all required fields are completed before proceeding.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all necessary information is included.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your healthcare provider for clarification.
