# driver_medical_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a driver's medical history and current health status for medical evaluations.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Fill in the required fields marked as "required".
3. Provide accurate and honest information for each field.
4. Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Evaluation Form**: This field is not a field, but a section header.
* **Name**: A field to enter your name. Please provide your full name as it appears on your driver's license or ID.
* **Address**: A field to enter your address. Please provide your complete address as it appears on your driver's license or ID.
* **Medical History**: A field to provide any medical history or conditions you have been diagnosed with.
* **Address**: A field to enter your address. Please provide your complete address as it appears on your document or ID.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling in this form.
* If you have any medical conditions or history, please provide as much detail as possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form creator at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
