# Sexual Health Inventory For Men (SHIM) Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sexual Health Inventory For Men (SHIM) Questionnaire is designed to collect information about men's sexual health, including their experiences, habits, and preferences. The data collected will help researchers, clinicians, or educators understand the complexities of male sexual health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer as accurately and honestly as possible.
2. For text fields, provide as much detail as you need to answer the question.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrator at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Sexual History** (`sexual_history`, `text`, **optional**): This question is asking about your sexual history, including any past experiences, relationships, or events that may impact your current sexual health.
- **Sexual Benefits** (`sexual_benefits`, `text`, **optional**): Describe the benefits you experience or associate with sex, such as emotional fulfillment, physical pleasure, or intimacy.
- **Sexual Behaviors** (`sexual_behaviors`, `text`, **optional**): Please list any sexual behaviors you engage in, such as masturbation, sex with a partner, or other activities.
- **Sexual Health Status** (`sexual_health_status`, `text`, **optional**): Describe your current sexual health status, including any challenges or concerns you may have.
- **Sexual Dysfunctions** (`sexual_dysfunctions`, `text`, **optional**): If you experience any sexual dysfunctions, such as erectile dysfunction, premature ejaculation, or low libido, describe them in this field.
- **Sexual Relationships** (`sexual_relationships`, `text`, **optional**): Describe your current or past relationships, including partners, frequency, and satisfaction.
- **Sexual Preferences** (`sexual_preferences`, `text`, **optional**): List any preferences you have regarding sex, such as preferred positions, activities, or partners.
- **Sexual Dysfunctions 2** (`sexual_dysfunctions_2`, `text`, **optional**): This question is similar to "Sexual Dysfunctions" and is an opportunity for you to report any other issues you're experiencing.
Please answer these questions to the best of your ability and provide as much detail as you can.
