# Fitness For Duty Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness For Duty Form is used to document the return-to-work process for employees who have been absent due to medical reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your employee ID and name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter the date of your return to work, as recommended by your physician.
3. Enter your physician's name and signature in the fields provided.
4. Enter your employer's name and title if applicable.
5. Check the "Fitness for Duty" field to indicate whether you are fit to return to work.
6. Add any additional comments or information if necessary.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (2, `number`, required/optional: false): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Employee Name** (3, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee record.
* **Date of Return to Work** (4, `date`, required/optional: false): Enter the date you are recommended to return to work, as advised by your physician.
* **Physician Name** (5, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter the name of your attending physician.
* **Physician Signature** (6, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter the signature of your attending physician.
* **Employer Name** (7, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter the name of your employer, if applicable.
* **Employer Title** (8, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your job title, if applicable.
* **Date of Last Exam** (9, `date`, required/optional: false): Enter the date of your last medical examination.
* **Fitness for Duty** (10, `select_one`, required/optional: false): Select "True" if you are fit to return to work, or "False" if you are not fit to return to work.
* **Additional Comments** (11, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter any additional comments or information about your return-to-work status.
* **Form Footer** (12, `text`, required/optional: false): This field is reserved for any additional information or comments.
