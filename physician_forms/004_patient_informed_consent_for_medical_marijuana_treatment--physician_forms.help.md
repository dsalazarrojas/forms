# patient_informed_consent_for_medical_marijuana_treatment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for the purpose of obtaining informed consent from patients seeking medical marijuana treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions above and ensure that you provide accurate and detailed information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Marijuana Treatment** (`medical_marijuana_treatment`, text, required): Describe your medical condition and how medical marijuana will be used to treat it.
* **Medical Marijuana Contrainications** (`medical_marijuana_contraindications`, select_one, optional): Check off any conditions that you have or are suspected to have that may make you ineligible for medical marijuana treatment.
* **Risks of Medical Marijuana** (`risks_of_medical_marijuana`, select_multiple, optional): Check off any risks or side effects you are aware of or have experienced with medical marijuana.
* **Potential Side Effects** (`potential_side_effects`, text, optional): Describe any potential side effects you are aware of or have experienced with medical marijuana.
* **Potential Interactions** (`potential_interactions`, text, optional): Describe any potential interactions between medical marijuana and other medications you are taking.
* **Medical Marijuana Use** (`medical_marijuana_use`, date, required): Provide the date you will begin using medical marijuana, or when you have used medical marijuana in the past.
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, text, required): Provide a clear and detailed description of your consent for medical marijuana treatment, including any questions or concerns you have.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, required): Provide the date on which you completed this form.
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, required): Check off that you have read and understood the information provided and that you understand the risks and benefits associated with medical marijuana treatment.
* **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, select_one, optional): Check off that the physician has reviewed and discussed the information provided, and that you have been informed of the risks and benefits associated with medical marijuana treatment.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, select_one, required): Check off that you have read and understood the information provided and that you understand the risks and benefits associated with medical marijuana treatment.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Provide the date on which this form was signed.
