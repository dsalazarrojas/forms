# Telemedicine Patient Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather relevant information from patients during a telemedicine evaluation, helping healthcare professionals assess a patient's health and medical history.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Patient Name" field with the patient's name as it appears on their medical records.
2. Select the "Family Members" field by choosing from the provided options (e.g., John, Jane, or Joe) to indicate which family members are relevant to the patient's medical history.
3. In the "Medical History" field, provide a brief description of the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or relevant medical events.
4. Answer the "Allergies" question by selecting "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the patient has any allergies.
5. In the "Medications" field, select all medications the patient is currently taking, if applicable.
6. For "Medical Conditions", provide a brief description of the patient's current medical conditions or concerns.
7. Once you have completed all fields, review and submit the form for further evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (text): Provide the patient's name as it appears on their medical records.
* **Family Members** (select multiple): Select all family members relevant to the patient's medical history.
* **Medical History** (text): Describe the patient's medical history, including previous illnesses, surgeries, or relevant medical events.
* **Allergies** (select one): Answer "Yes" if the patient has any allergies, or "No" if they do not.
* **Medications** (select multiple): List all medications the patient is currently taking, if applicable.
* **Medical Conditions** (text): Describe the patient's current medical conditions or concerns.
