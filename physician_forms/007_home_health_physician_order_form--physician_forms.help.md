# Home Health Physician Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Health Physician Order Form is a document designed for physicians to collect essential information from patients about their medical history, medications, allergies, medical conditions, and home health care needs. This form helps physicians make informed decisions about patient care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter patient information on page 1, including any relevant details.
2. Provide a brief medical history on page 2.
3. List all current and prescribed medications on page 3.
4. Report any allergies on page 4.
5. Describe any relevant medical conditions on page 5.
6. Specify home health care needs on page 6.
7. Sign and date the form on page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Patient Information
* **Patient Information** (`name`, `type`, **required**: no): Enter the patient's name, address, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Medical History** (`name`, `type`, **required**: no): Provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history.
* **Medications** (`name`, `type`, **required**: no): List all current and prescribed medications, including dosages and frequency.
* **Allergy** (`name`, `type`, **required**: no): Report any known allergies or sensitivities.
* **Medical Conditions** (`name`, `type`, **required**: no): Describe any relevant medical conditions or concerns.
* **Home Health Care** (`name`, `type`, **required**: no): Specify any home health care needs or requirements.

### Page 2: Medical History
* **Medical History** (`name`, `type`, **required**: no): Provide a detailed medical history, including any relevant medical records or test results.

### Page 3: Medications
* **Medications** (`name`, `type`, **required**: no): List all current and prescribed medications, including dosages and frequency.

### Page 4: Allergy
* **Allergy** (`name`, `type`, **required**: no): Report any known allergies or sensitivities.

### Page 5: Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions** (`name`, `type`, **required**: no): Describe any relevant medical conditions or concerns.

### Page 6: Home Health Care
* **Home Health Care** (`name`, `type`, **required**: no): Specify any home health care needs or requirements.

### Page 7: Signature
* **Signature** (`name`, `type`, **required**: no): Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
* **Date** (`name`, `type`, **required**: no): Enter the date of completion.
