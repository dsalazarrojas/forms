# Influenza Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about patients' symptoms and exposure history related to influenza. The purpose of this form is to help healthcare professionals assess and manage patients who may be at risk of developing or have already developed flu symptoms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form sections and selecting the applicable fields for each section.
2. Answer each field based on your knowledge of the patient's symptoms and exposure history.
3. Be as accurate as possible when answering each field, as this information will help healthcare professionals assess the patient's risk level and provide appropriate care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Symptom Questions (<page 1>)
* **Symptom Questions (text)**: This field is used to collect any symptoms the patient is currently experiencing. Please list each symptom and its severity.
### Recent Travel (<page 2>)
* **Recent Travel (select_one)**: Select the type of recent travel the patient has had (Influenza, Flu-like illness, Common cold, Other, or None).
### Exposure Risk (<page 3>)
* **Exposure Risk (select_one)**: Choose the level of exposure risk the patient has (Low, Moderate, High, or Very High).
### Flu Symptoms (<page 4>)
* **Flu Symptoms (select_multiple)**: Select each symptom the patient is currently experiencing (Fever, Cough, Sore Throat, Runny Nose, Fatigue, Chills, Muscle Aches, Headache, Nausea/Vomiting, Diarrhea, Confusion/Disorientation, Loss of Appetite, Loss of Taste, Loss of Smell, Loss of Hearing).
### Recent Exposure (<page 5>)
* **Recent Exposure (select_one)**: Select the type of recent exposure the patient has had (Close contact with someone who has influenza, Close contact with someone who has a flu-like illness, Close contact with someone who has a different illness, or None).
### Exposure Duration (<page 6>)
* **Exposure Duration (select_one)**: Choose the duration of exposure (Less than 1 day, 1-2 days, 2-5 days, or More than 5 days).
### Recent Contact (<page 7>)
* **Recent Contact (select_multiple)**: Select each contact the patient has had (Health Care Worker, International Traveler, Family Member, Friend/Neighbor, or Other).
### Flu Vaccine (<page 8>)
* **Flu Vaccine (select_one)**: Select whether the patient has received flu vaccine this season (Received flu vaccine this season, Not received flu vaccine this season, or Unknown).
### Health Care Workplace (<page 9>)
* **Health Care Workplace (select_multiple)**: Select each healthcare workplace the patient has been in contact with (Hospital, Hospital Emergency Room, Hospital ICU, Hospital Clinic, Hospital Nursing Home, Hospital Rehabilitation Center, Hospital Long-term care, Outpatient clinic, Long-term care center, or Other).
### Flu Symptoms Duration (<page 10>)
* **Flu Symptoms Duration (select_one)**: Choose the duration of flu symptoms (Less than 48 hours, 48 hours to 4 days, 5-7 days, or More than 7 days).
### Flu Symptoms 2 (<page 11>)
* **Flu Symptoms 2 (select_multiple)**: Select each symptom the patient is currently experiencing (Fever, Cough, Sore Throat, Runny Nose, Fatigue, Chills, Muscle Aches, Headache, Nausea/Vomiting, Diarrhea, Confusion/Disorientation, Loss of Appetite, Loss of Taste, Loss of Smell, Loss of Hearing).
### Flu Exposure Risk (<page 12>)
* **Flu Exposure Risk (select_multiple)**: Select each risk level of exposure (Low, Moderate, High, or Very High).
### Healthcare Workplace Contact (<page 13>)
* **Healthcare Workplace Contact (select_multiple)**: Select each contact the patient has had with healthcare workers (True, False).
### Healthcare Workplace 2 (<page 14>)
* **Healthcare Workplace 2 (select_one)**: Select whether the patient has had contact with healthcare workers (True, False).
### Health Care Workplace Type (<page 15>)
* **Healthcare Workplace Type (select_one)**: Select the type of healthcare workplace the patient has been in contact with (Doctor/Nurse, Hospital Worker, Clinic Worker, Nursing Home Worker, Rehabilitation Worker, or Other).
### Flu Vaccination Date (<page 16>)
* **Flu Vaccination Date (date)**: Select the date the patient received flu vaccination (if applicable).
### Recent Flu Exposure (<page 17>)
* **Recent Flu Exposure (select_one)**: Select whether the patient has had recent flu exposure (True, False).
### Flu Symptoms Severity (<page 18>)
* **Flu Symptoms Severity (select_one)**: Choose the severity of flu symptoms (None, Mild, Moderate, or Severe).
### Recent Flu Symptoms (<page 19>)
* **Recent Flu Symptoms (select_multiple)**: Select each symptom the patient is currently experiencing (None, Mild, Moderate, or Severe).
### Health Care Worker Flu Exposure (<page 20>)
* **Health Care Worker Flu Exposure (select_multiple)**: Select whether the patient has had flu exposure with healthcare workers (True, False).
### Flu Vaccination (<page 21>)
* **Flu Vaccination (select_one)**: Select whether the patient has received flu vaccination (Received flu vaccine this season, Not received flu vaccine this season, or Unknown).
### Health Care Worker Flu Symptoms (<page 22>)
* **Health Care Worker Flu Symptoms (select_multiple)**: Select each symptom the patient is currently experiencing (None, Mild, Moderate, or Severe).
### Health Care Worker Flu Status (<page 23>)
* **Health Care Worker Flu Status (select_one)**: Select the flu status of healthcare workers (Positive, Negative).
### Flu Exposure Source (<page 24>)
* **Flu Exposure Source (select_multiple)**: Select each source of flu exposure (Health Care Worker, Family Member, Friend/Neighbor, or Other).

## Tips
* Be as accurate as possible when answering each field.
* If the patient has had recent travel or exposure, select the corresponding option.
* If the patient has had flu symptoms, select each symptom they are experiencing.
* If the patient has had contact with healthcare workers, select each type of contact.
* If the patient has received flu vaccination, select the date and whether they have received it this season.
