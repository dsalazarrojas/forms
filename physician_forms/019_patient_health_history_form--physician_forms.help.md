# Patient Health History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient health information in a structured and organized manner. The form is used to gather medical history, allergies, medical conditions, current medications, past treatments, medical procedures, medical specialties, doctor's contact information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please start by filling out the "Medical History" section, where you will be asked to provide a brief description of your medical history.
2. Next, list any allergies you have, such as peanut allergy, milk allergy, etc.
3. In the "Medical Conditions" section, list any medical conditions you currently have or have had in the past.
4. List your current medications, such as Aspirin, Vitamin C, etc.
5. If you have received any past medical treatments, list them in the "Past Treatments" section.
6. If you have undergone any medical procedures, select one from the "Medical Procedure" dropdown list.
7. If you have a medical specialty, select one from the "Medical Specialty" dropdown list.
8. Finally, provide the name, phone number, and facility name of your doctor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (text, not required): This is the section where you will be asked to provide a brief description of your medical history. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Allergies** (select_multiple, not required): Check any allergies you have, such as peanut allergy, milk allergy, etc.
* **Medical Conditions** (text, not required): This is the section where you will list any medical conditions you currently have or have had in the past. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Current Medications** (select_multiple, not required): This is the section where you will list your current medications. Please select all the medications you are currently taking.
* **Past Treatments** (text, not required): This is the section where you will list any past medical treatments you have received.
* **Medical Procedure** (select_one, not required): This is the section where you will select any medical procedures you have undergone, such as surgery, blood transfusion, etc.
* **Medical Specialty** (select_one, not required): This is the section where you will select your medical specialty, such as Cardiology, Dermatology, etc.
* **Doctor's Name** (text, not required): This is the section where you will enter your doctor's name.
* **Doctor's Phone** (text, not required): This is the section where you will enter your doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor's Title** (text, not required): This is the section where you will enter your doctor's title, such as Dr., MD, etc.
* **Doctor's Facility** (text, not required): This is the section where you will enter the name of your doctor's facility.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact your doctor.
* This form is intended to be a comprehensive and accurate representation of your health status.
* Please ensure that all sections are filled out completely before submitting the form.
