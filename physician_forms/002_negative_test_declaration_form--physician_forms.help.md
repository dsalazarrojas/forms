# Negative Test Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Negative Test Declaration Form is used to report the results of a COVID-19 test, ensuring that individuals are aware of their status and helping in the public health response to the pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Passenger Name
	* Travel Date
	* Test Date
	* Ship or Aircraft ID
	* Flight Number
	* Travel Destination
	* Test Result
	* Flight Number (Return)
	* Ship or Aircraft Type (Return)
	* Travel Date (Return)
	* Ship or Aircraft ID (Return)
2. Select the correct Test Status:
	* Positive
	* Negative
	* Inconclusive
3. Enter your Physician's Name and Signature:
	* This is the name of the healthcare professional who performed the test
	* This is a required field, which must be signed by the healthcare professional
4. Enter the Ship or Aircraft Type:
	* This is the type of ship or aircraft on which you traveled or worked

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Passenger Name** (`Passenger Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the individual who underwent the test.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you traveled or worked on the ship or aircraft.
* **Test Date** (`test_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the test was taken.
* **Ship or Aircraft ID** (`ship_or_aircraft_id`, `text`, required): Enter the ID of the ship or aircraft on which you traveled or worked.
* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, `text`, required): Enter the flight number on which you traveled.
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, `text`, required): Enter the destination of your travel.
* **Test Result** (`test_result`, `text`, required): Enter the result of your test (negative or positive).
* **Flight Number (Return)** (`flight_number_return`, `text`, required): Enter the flight number on which you returned.
* **Ship or Aircraft Type (Return)** (`ship_or_aircraft_type_return`, `text`, required): Enter the type of ship or aircraft on which you returned.
* **Travel Date (Return)** (`travel_date_return`, `date`, required): Enter the date you returned.
* **Ship or Aircraft ID (Return)** (`ship_or_aircraft_id_return`, `text`, required): Enter the ID of the ship or aircraft on which you returned.
* **Test Status** (`test_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your test:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Inconclusive
* **Test Status Other** (`test_status_other`, `text`, optional): If your test status is not listed, please enter the reason here.
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the healthcare professional who performed the test.
* **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, `text`, required): Enter your signature as the healthcare professional who performed the test.
* **Ship or Aircraft Type** (`ship_or_aircraft_type`, `text`, required): Enter the type of ship or aircraft on which you traveled or worked.
* **Flight Time** (`flight_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your flight.
* **Flight Time Zone** (`flight_time_zone`, `text`, required): Enter the time zone of your flight.
* **Flight Number (Alternative 1)** (`flight_number_alt`, `text`, required): Enter an alternative flight number.
* **Flight Number (Alternative 2)** (`flight_number_alt2`, `text`, required): Enter another alternative flight number.
* **Flight Number (Alternative 3)** (`flight_number_alt3`, `text`, optional): Enter yet another alternative flight number.
* **Ship or Aircraft ID (Return)** (`ship_or_aircraft_id_return`, `text`, required): Enter the ID of the ship or aircraft on which you returned.
* **Flight Number (Return)** (`flight_number_return`, `text`, required): Enter the flight number on which you returned.
* **Ship or Aircraft Type (Return)** (`ship_or_aircraft_type_return`, `text`, required): Enter the type of ship or aircraft on which you returned.
* **Travel Date (Return)** (`travel_date_return`, `date`, required): Enter the date you returned.
* **Flight Number (Return 2)** (`flight_number_return2`, `text`, required): Enter the flight number on which you returned.
* **Ship or Aircraft ID (Alternative 3)** (`ship_or_aircraft_id`, `text`, optional): Not required for return. Not included in the form for return.
