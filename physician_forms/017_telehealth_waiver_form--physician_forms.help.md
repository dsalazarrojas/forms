# Telehealth Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the completion of the telehealth waiver form, a crucial document for consenting to or declining certain aspects of your care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, `text`, required): Enter your patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Patient Email** (`Patient Email`, `email`, required): Enter your patient's email address.
* **Patient Phone** (`Patient Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your patient's phone number.
* **Physician Name** (`Physician Name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the treating physician.
* **Patient Signature** (`Patient Signature`, `note`, required): Sign this form to indicate your agreement to or decline certain aspects of your care.
* **Date Waiver Signed** (`Date Waiver Signed`, `date`, required): Enter the date you sign this waiver form.
* **Date Waiver Effective** (`Date Waiver Effective`, `date`, required): Enter the date this waiver takes effect.
* **Physician Signature** (`Physician Signature`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your level of licensure (physician, MD, or DO).
* **Waiver Type** (`Waiver Type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of waiver you are requesting (HIPAA, State, or Other).
* **Reason for Waiver** (`Reason for Waiver`, `text`, required): Explain why you are requesting this waiver.
* **Waiver Terms** (`Waiver Terms`, `text`, required): Read and agree to the terms of the waiver.
* **Date Form Submitted** (`Date Form Submitted`, `date`, required): Enter the date this form was submitted.

Please take note of the following tips:

* Ensure you have carefully reviewed the waiver form and understand its implications before completing it.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy.
* Make sure to sign the form with your full name and date it correctly.
