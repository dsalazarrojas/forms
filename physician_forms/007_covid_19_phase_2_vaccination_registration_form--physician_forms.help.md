## Purpose

The COVID 19 Phase 2 Vaccination Registration Form is designed to help individuals register for the Phase 2 COVID-19 vaccination. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to provide essential details for a smooth and efficient vaccination process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Full Name as per the "Full Name" field (1).
2. Provide your age in years (2).
3. Select your Gender from the list provided in the "Gender" field (3).
4. Enter your residential address (4).
5. Enter your Email Address (5).
6. Provide your Phone Number (6).
7. If you are part of a community group or organization, enter the name in the "Community Group or Organization" field (7). (Note: This is not a mandatory field.)
8. Indicate if you consider yourself at high risk for COVID-19 complications (8).
9. List any existing health conditions you have (9) (Note: This is not a mandatory field.).
10. Select your preferred appointment time from the list provided in the "Preferred Appointment Time" field (10).
11. Finally, select whether you certify that you are a resident of this community and eligible for Phase 2 (11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Age** (2, number, required): Enter your age in years.
* **Gender** (3, select_one, required): Select your gender from the list provided.
* **Residential Address** (4, text, required): Enter your full residential address.
* **Email Address** (5, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (6, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Community Group or Organization** (7, text, optional): If you are part of a community group or organization, enter the name here. (Note: This is not a mandatory field.)
* **Do you consider yourself to be at high risk for COVID-19 complications?** (8, select_one, required): Select whether you consider yourself at high risk for COVID-19 complications.
* **Please list any existing health conditions** (9, text, optional): List any existing health conditions you have. (Note: This is not a mandatory field.)
* **Preferred Appointment Time** (10, select_one, required): Select your preferred appointment time from the list provided.
* **I certify that I am a resident of this community and eligible for Phase 2** (11, select_one, required): Select whether you certify your residency and eligibility for Phase 2.
