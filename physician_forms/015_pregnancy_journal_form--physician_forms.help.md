# Pregnancy Journal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pregnancy Journal Form is a medical form used to collect and track various health-related information about a baby and mother during and after pregnancy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Due Date** in the first field, which is the expected date of birth for the baby.
2. Provide the **Mother's Name** and **Father's Name** in the second and third fields, respectively.
3. Select the **Delivery Method** from the options provided: Vaginal, Vaginal with Instrumental Assistance, or Cesarean.
4. Enter the **Baby's Weight**, **Baby's Length**, and **Baby's Height** in the subsequent fields, which are optional.
5. Enter the **Mother's Weight**, **Mother's Height**, and **Mother's Age** in the subsequent fields, which are also optional.
6. Record the **Baby's Weight at Birth**, **Baby's Length at Birth**, and **Baby's Head Circumference at Birth** in the next few fields.
7. Enter any additional information as prompted.
