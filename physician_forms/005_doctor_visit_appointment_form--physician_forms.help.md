# Doctor Visit Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doctor Visit Appointment Form is a medical form used to schedule appointments with doctors for patient visits. It collects information about the patient, doctor, and appointment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the doctor's specialty from the list of available options.
2. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
3. Enter the patient's phone number in the "patient_phone_number" field (optional).
4. Choose the doctor's name from the list of available options.
5. Select the appointment location from the list of available options.
6. Enter the appointment date and time in the "appointment_date" and "appointment_time" fields, respectively.
7. Add any additional comments about the appointment in the "additional_comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Doctor Visit Appointment Form**: This is the title of the form, which you are currently viewing.
* **Doctor Visit Appointment Form** (page 2): This is the second page of the form, where you will enter the patient's information.
	+ **patient_name**: Enter the name of the patient.
	+ **patient_phone_number**: Enter the patient's phone number (optional).
	+ **doctor_specialty**: Select the doctor's specialty from the list of available options.
* **Doctor Visit Appointment Form** (page 3): This is the third page of the form, where you will enter the appointment details.
	+ **appointment_date**: Enter the appointment date in the format MM/DD/YY or YY/MM/DD.
	+ **appointment_time**: Select the appointment time from the 24-hour clock (HH:MM AM/PM).
	+ **doctor_name**: Select the doctor's name from the list of available options.
* **Doctor Visit Appointment Form** (page 4): This is the fourth page of the form, where you will enter the appointment location and any additional comments.
	+ **appointment_location**: Select the appointment location from the list of available options.
	+ **additional_comments**: Add any additional comments about the appointment (optional).

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and access to complete this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your supervisor or the healthcare team for clarification.
* The data collected on this form will be used to schedule appointments and allocate resources.
