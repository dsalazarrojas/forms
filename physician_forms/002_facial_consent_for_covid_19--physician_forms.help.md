# Facial Consent For COVID 19 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facial Consent For COVID 19 form is used to obtain consent from clients for facial treatments during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's name and contact information (Client Name, Contact Number, Email).
2. Select the services being treated (Facial Treatment or Other).
3. Choose the status of the physician's consent (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
4. Sign the consent form with your signature and date.
5. Enter the physician's contact information (Physician Name, Title, Address, Phone, Email, License, and Consent Status).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facial Consent 1** (`facial_consent_1`, text, not required): This field is not required for completion and serves as a placeholder for any additional information that may be needed.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, not required): Enter the client's name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, not required): Enter the client's contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Date of Consent** (`date_of_consent`, date, not required): Enter the date of the consent.
* **Time of Consent** (`time_of_consent`, time, not required): Enter the time of the consent.
* **Services Treated** (`services_treated`, select multiple, required): Select all the services being treated (Facial Treatment or Other).
* **Consent Form Signed** (`consent_form_signed`, select one, required): Choose whether the client signed the consent form (Client Signed, Spa/Salon Signed, or Both).
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, not required): Leave a note for your signature (this field is for your reference only).
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, not required): Enter the date the consent form was signed.
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, not required): Enter the physician's name.
* **Physician Title** (`physician_title`, text, not required): Enter the physician's title.
* **Physician Address** (`physician_address`, text, not required): Enter the physician's address.
* **Physician Phone** (`physician_phone`, number, not required): Enter the physician's phone number.
* **Physician Email** (`physician_email`, email, not required): Enter the physician's email address.
* **Physician License** (`physician_license`, text, not required): Enter the physician's license number.
* **Physician Consent** (`physician_consent`, text, not required): Enter the physician's consent statement.
* **Physician License Number** (`physician_license_number`, number, not required): Enter the physician's license number.
* **Physician Consent Date** (`physician_consent_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the physician's consent.
* **Physician Consent Time** (`physician_consent_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the physician's consent.
* **Physician Consent Status** (`physician_consent_status`, select one, required): Choose the status of the physician's consent (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Spa/Salon Name** (`spa_salon_name`, text, not required): Enter the spa/salon name.
* **Spa/Salon Address** (`spa_salon_address`, text, not required): Enter the spa/salon address.
* **Spa/Salon Phone** (`spa_salon_phone`, number, not required): Enter the spa/salon phone number.
* **Spa/Salon Email** (`spa_salon_email`, email, not required): Enter the spa/salon email address.

Please note that some fields are not required for completion and are left for your reference.
