# Vaccination Plan Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vaccination Plan Survey is a crucial tool for healthcare professionals to track and manage vaccination records for patients. It helps to ensure that patients receive the necessary care and attention required for their health and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Vaccination Date field with the patient's scheduled vaccination date.
2. Enter the Vaccination Time field with the scheduled vaccination time.
3. Select the Vaccination Status field to indicate whether the patient is currently active or inactive for vaccinations.
4. Provide any relevant Medical Conditions in the Medical Condition field.
5. Select the Vaccination Plan field to indicate the plan for the patient's vaccinations.
6. Enter any necessary Follow Up information for the patient.
7. If necessary, provide additional Medical Conditions in the Medical Condition 2 field.
8. Check the Candidate for Vaccination field to indicate if the patient is a candidate for vaccination.
9. Add any Additional Info in the Additional Info field if necessary.
10. Finally, provide any Comments in the Comments field if you need to add any further information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`, Date, Required): Enter the patient's scheduled vaccination date.
* **Vaccination Time** (`vaccination_time`, Time, Required): Enter the scheduled vaccination time in HH:MM format.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, Select, Optional): Select whether the patient is active or inactive for vaccinations.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, Text, Required): Enter any relevant medical conditions for the patient.
* **Vaccination Plan** (`vaccination_plan`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the plan for the patient's vaccinations (Active, Inactive, True, False).
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, Text, Required): Enter any follow-up information for the patient.
* **Medical Condition 2** (`medical_condition_2`, Text, Required): Enter any additional relevant medical conditions for the patient.
* **Candidate for Vaccination** (`is_candidate_for_vaccination`, Checkbox, Required): Check if the patient is a candidate for vaccination.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, Note, Optional): Add any additional information about the patient if necessary.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the patient if necessary.
