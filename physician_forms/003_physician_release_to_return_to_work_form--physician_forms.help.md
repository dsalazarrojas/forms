# Physician Release To Return To Work Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document an employee's release to return to work after a medical examination. It captures essential information about the employee's medical condition, work status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have a valid medical reason for the employee's return to work.
2. Review the form carefully and complete all required fields accurately.
3. Enter the employee's personal details, including their name, address, date of birth, job title, occupation, and work status.
4. Enter the medical provider's information, including their name, address, license number, phone number, and email.
5. Select the relevant medical provider's certification from the options provided.
6. Enter the date released to work and return to work date.
7. Review and sign the form to confirm your medical assessment and recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information**:
	+ Employee Name: Enter the full name of the employee.
	+ Employee Address: Enter the employee's address.
	+ Date of Birth: Enter the employee's date of birth.
	+ Job Title: Enter the employee's job title.
	+ Occupation: Enter the employee's occupation.
	+ Work Status: Enter the employee's current work status.
* **Medical Information**:
	+ Medical Provider Name: Enter the name of the medical provider.
	+ Medical Provider Address: Enter the address of the medical provider.
	+ License Number: Enter the license number of the medical provider.
	+ Phone Number: Enter the phone number of the medical provider.
	+ Email: Enter the email of the medical provider.
	+ Date Examined: Enter the date the medical examination was performed.
	+ Nature of Injury: Enter the nature of the injury or condition.
	+ Remarks: Enter any additional remarks or comments about the employee's medical condition.
* **Physician's Certification**:
	+ Select the relevant medical certification from the options provided.
* **Date Released To Work**:
	+ Enter the date the employee is released to return to work.
* **Employee Signature**:
	+ Employee Signature: Sign this field to confirm that the employee has been informed of their return to work status.
* **Physician's Signature**:
	+ Medical Provider Signature: Sign this field to confirm that the medical assessment and recommendations are accurate.
* **Employee Return To Work Date**:
	+ Enter the date the employee is expected to return to work.
* **Employer Signature**:
	+ Sign this field to confirm that you, as the employer, have received the medical provider's assessment and recommendations.
* **Additional Comments**:
	+ Enter any additional comments or remarks about the employee's medical condition or return to work status.

## Tips
* Ensure you complete the form accurately and honestly.
* Review the form carefully before signing it.
* Make sure to keep the medical provider's signature confidential and secure.
* Store the completed form in a secure location, as required by your organization's policies.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, contact your HR department or medical provider for assistance.
