# Care Plan Meeting Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track care plan meetings between healthcare professionals and patients. It captures essential information about the patient's condition, goals, and treatment plans, ensuring a comprehensive and coordinated care approach.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the patient's personal and medical information.
2. Select the meeting date and time from the calendar.
3. Choose the meeting type and location from the dropdown options.
4. List any attending physicians and nursing staff.
5. Select other healthcare providers present, if applicable.
6. Document the patient's primary diagnosis and secondary diagnoses.
7. Outline the patient's short-term and long-term care goals.
8. Summarize any medication changes or concerns.
9. List any therapy services ordered, if applicable.
10. Document any durable medical equipment needs.
11. Schedule follow-up appointments, if applicable.
12. Specify the next care plan meeting date, if applicable.
13. Outline action items and responsibilities for each care team member.
14. Document the patient's consent to the care plan.
15. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Full Name** (`patient_full_name`, `text`, required)
The patient's full name.

### 2. **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, `text`, required)
The patient's medical record number.

### 3. **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required)
The patient's date of birth.

### 4. **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, `date`, required)
The date of this care plan meeting.

### 5. **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, `time`, required)
The time of this care plan meeting.

### 6. **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, `text`, required)
The location of this care plan meeting (e.g., room number or virtual link).

### 7. **Meeting Type** (`meeting_type`, `select_one`, required)
Select the type of care plan meeting (e.g., initial, quarterly, annual, discharge planning, care transition, emergency review, family conference).

### 8. **Attending Physicians** (`attending_physician`, `text`, required)
List all attending physicians present at this meeting.

### 9. **Attending Nursing Staff** (`attending_nurses`, `text`, required)
List the names and roles of attending nursing staff.

### 10. **Other Healthcare Providers** (`other_healthcare_providers`, `select_multiple`, required)
Select other healthcare providers present (e.g., physical therapist, occupational therapist, speech therapist).

### 11. **Family Members Present** (`family_members_present`, `text`, optional)
List the names and relationships of family members present (if applicable).

### 12. **Patient Advocate Present** (`patient_advocate_present`, `select_one`, required)
Select whether a patient advocate is present (yes or no).

### 13. **Primary Diagnosis** (`primary_diagnosis`, `text`, required)
The patient's primary diagnosis (main medical condition).

### 14. **Secondary Diagnoses** (`secondary_diagnoses`, `text`, required)
List the patient's secondary diagnoses (comorbidities).

### 15. **Current Health Status** (`current_health_status`, `text`, required)
Document the patient's current health status (brief summary).

### 16. **Functional Status** (`functional_status`, `select_one`, required)
Select the patient's functional status (e.g., independent, requires minimal assistance, requires moderate assistance).

### 17. **Cognitive Status** (`cognitive_status`, `select_one`, required)
Select the patient's cognitive status (e.g., alert and oriented, mild impairment, moderate impairment).

### 18. **Patient-Stated Goals** (`patient_goals`, `text`, optional)
Document the patient's self-stated goals (what they hope to achieve).

### 19. **Family Goals or Concerns** (`family_goals`, `text`, optional)
Document the family's goals or concerns (if applicable).

### 20. **Short-Term Care Goals** (`short_term_goals`, `text`, required)
Document the patient's short-term care goals (next 30 days).

### 21. **Long-Term Care Goals** (`long_term_goals`, `text`, required)
Document the patient's long-term care goals (next 90 days or more).

### 22. **Medication Review Summary** (`medication_review`, `text`, required)
Summarize any medication changes or concerns.

### 23. **Treatment Plan Changes** (`treatment_plan_changes`, `text`, optional)
List any treatment plan modifications.

### 24. **Therapy Services Ordered** (`therapy_services`, `select_multiple`, required)
Select the therapy services ordered (e.g., physical therapy, occupational therapy).

### 25. **Therapy Frequency** (`therapy_frequency`, `text`, optional)
Document the therapy sessions frequency (if applicable).

### 26. **Nutritional Needs and Diet Orders** (`nutritional_needs`, `text`, required)
Document the patient's nutritional needs and diet orders.

### 27. **Social Services Needs** (`social_services_needs`, `select_multiple`, required)
Select the patient's social services needs (e.g., discharge planning, financial assistance).

### 28. **Durable Medical Equipment Needs** (`equipment_needs`, `select_multiple`, required)
Select the patient's durable medical equipment needs (e.g., wheelchair, walker or cane).

### 29. **Follow-up Appointments Scheduled** (`follow_up_appointments`, `text`, required)
List the scheduled follow-up appointments and dates.

### 30. **Next Care Plan Meeting Date** (`next_meeting_date`, `date`, optional)
Document the next care plan meeting date (if applicable).

### 31. **Action Items and Responsibilities** (`action_items`, `text`, required)
Document the action items and responsibilities for each care team member.

### 32. **Patient Consents to Care Plan** (`patient_consent`, `select_one`, required)
Select whether the patient consents to the care plan (yes or no).

### 33. **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional)
Add any additional notes or comments.

### 34. **Meeting Facilitator** (`meeting_facilitator`, `text`, required)
Document the name and title of the meeting facilitator.

### 35. **Documentation Completed By** (`documentation_completed_by`, `text`, required)
Document who completed this care plan meeting template.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled in before finalizing the care plan.
* Use the patient's medical record number to ensure accurate and up-to-date information.
* Document any changes or modifications to the patient's treatment plan.
* Review and update the care plan regularly to ensure the patient's goals and needs are met.
* Communicate with the patient and their care team to ensure a coordinated approach to care.
