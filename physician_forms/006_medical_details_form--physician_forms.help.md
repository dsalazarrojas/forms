# Medical Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Details Form is designed to collect and document patient medical information. This form is meant to be completed by medical professionals or medical facilities to gather details about patients, including their allergies, medical conditions, and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Medical Details" page to start the form.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed.
3. Be thorough and accurate when providing information about the patient.
4. Review and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Details** (`medical_details_form`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the patient's medical details, including any relevant information about their medical history, procedures, or conditions.
* **Patient Allergies** (`patient_allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies the patient has, including medications, foods, or substances that trigger allergic reactions.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Document any medical conditions the patient is currently experiencing or has experienced in the past.
* **Patient Medications** (`patient_medications`, text, optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking or has taken in the past.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide a comprehensive summary of the patient's medical history, including past procedures, conditions, or illnesses.
* **Medical Procedure Details** (`medical_procedure_details`, text, optional): Enter any details about the medical procedures the patient has undergone or will undergo.

## Tips
* Be accurate and thorough when filling out this form to ensure the best possible care for the patient.
* Review and submit the form carefully to avoid any errors.
* If you're unsure about any information, consult with the patient or medical professionals before completing the form.
