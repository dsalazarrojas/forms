# Health Questionnaire For COVID 19 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your health status, symptoms, and contact history related to COVID-19. Please answer the questions honestly to help medical professionals assess and address your concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Enter your responses in the text fields provided.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked as "Required" in the field explanations below.
4. Once you've completed the form, submit it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (page 1)** (`general_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional information about your health status or concerns.
* **Symptoms (page 1)** (`symptoms`, text, optional):
	+ Describe any symptoms you're experiencing, such as cough, fever, or shortness of breath.
* **Contact History (page 1)** (`contact_history`, text, optional):
	+ Provide details about your contact with anyone who has tested positive for COVID-19 or has symptoms.
* **Travel History (page 1)** (`travel_history`, text, optional):
	+ List any recent travel, including dates and destinations.
* **Health Status (page 1)** (`health_status`, text, optional):
	+ Describe your current health status, including any underlying medical conditions.
* **Travel Restrictions (page 1)** (`travel_restrictions`, text, optional):
	+ If applicable, indicate any travel restrictions you're subject to or have experienced.
* **Exposure Risk (page 1)** (`exposure_risk`, text, optional):
	+ Describe any potential exposure risks to COVID-19.
* **Medical Condition (page 1)** (`medical_condition`, text, optional):
	+ If applicable, provide details about your current medical condition.
* **Test Result (page 1)** (`test_result`, text, optional):
	+ If you've taken a COVID-19 test, enter the results.
* **Follow Up (page 1)** (`follow_up`, text, optional):
	+ If you'd like to schedule a follow-up appointment, provide details.
* **Test Result 2 (page 2)** (`test_result_2`, text, optional):
	+ If you've taken additional COVID-19 tests, enter the results.
* **Test Result 3 (page 2)** (`test_result_3`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional test results.
* **Test Result 4 (page 2)** (`test_result_4`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any further test results.
* **General Information 2 (page 2)** (`general_info_2`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional information about your health status or concerns.
* **General Information 3 (page 2)** (`general_info_3`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any further information about your health status or concerns.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any question, ask for clarification.
* Once you've submitted the form, it will be reviewed by medical professionals to assess your concerns.
* If you have further questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form is intended to be used by individuals who need guidance on their health status related to COVID-19.
