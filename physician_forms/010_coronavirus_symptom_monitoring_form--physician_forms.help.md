# Coronavirus Symptom Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coronavirus Symptom Monitoring Form is designed to collect information about patients' symptoms to help healthcare professionals assess and treat coronavirus infections effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about the patient.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out completely.
3. Select all applicable symptoms from the options provided in each section.
4. Enter any additional symptoms not listed in the provided options in the "Other Symptoms" field.
5. Review and submit the form for further evaluation and treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`): Enter the patient's information, including their name and demographic details.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical records.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the patient's email address (if available).
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the patient's phone number (if available).
* **Temperature** (`temperature`): Record the patient's current body temperature (optional, but recommended for temperature monitoring).
* **Respiratory Rate** (`respiratory_rate`): Record the patient's current respiratory rate (optional, but recommended for respiratory monitoring).
* **Cough** (`cough`): Select all applicable symptoms from the options provided, including Fever, Cough, Runny nose, and Sore Throat.
* **Chest Pain** (`chest_pain`): Select all applicable symptoms from the options provided, including Chest tightness, Difficulty breathing, Shortness of breath, and Other.
* **Headache** (`headache`): Select the type of headache the patient is experiencing, from Migraine, Tension headache, No headache, or Other.
* **Nausea/Vomiting** (`nausea`): Select all applicable symptoms from the options provided, including Nausea, Vomiting, Diarrhea, and Other.
* **Muscle Pain** (`muscle_pain`): Select the type of muscle pain the patient is experiencing, from Muscle weakness, Muscle aches, or Other.
* **Fatigue** (`fatigue`): Select all applicable symptoms from the options provided, including Feeling tired, Fatigue, Weakness, and Other.
* **Diarrhea** (`diarrhea`): Select the type of diarrhea the patient is experiencing, from Diarrhea, Abdominal pain, Nausea, or Other.
* **Loss of Appetite** (`loss_of_appetite`): Select all applicable symptoms from the options provided, including Loss of appetite, Decrease in appetite, or Other.
* **Loss of Smell and Taste** (`loss_of_smell`): Select all applicable symptoms from the options provided, including Loss of smell and taste, Loss of smell, or Other.
* **Loss of Taste** (`loss_of_taste`): Select all applicable symptoms from the options provided, including Loss of smell and taste, Loss of taste, or Other.
* **Sore Throat** (`sore_throat`): Select the type of sore throat the patient is experiencing, from Sore Throat or Other.
* **Muscle Fatigue** (`muscle_fatigue`): Select all applicable symptoms from the options provided, including Muscle weakness, Muscle aches, or Other.
* **Shortness of Breath** (`shortness_breath`): Select the type of shortness of breath the patient is experiencing, from Difficulty breathing, Shortness of breath, Chest tightness, or Other.
* **Confusion** (`confusion`): Select all applicable symptoms from the options provided, including Confusion, Disorientation, or Other.
* **Chest Tightness** (`chest_tightness`): Select all applicable symptoms from the options provided, including Chest tightness, Difficulty breathing, or Other.
* **Loss of Concentration** (`loss_of_concentration`): Select all applicable symptoms from the options provided, including Loss of concentration, Confusion, or Other.
* **Memory Loss** (`memory_loss`): Select the type of memory loss the patient is experiencing, from Difficulty concentrating, Memory Loss, or Other.
* **Irritability** (`irritability`): Select all applicable symptoms from the options provided, including Irritability, Anger, or Other.
* **Other Symptoms** (`other_symptoms`): Enter any additional symptoms not listed in the provided options.

## Tips

* Always fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Select all applicable symptoms from the options provided.
* If you are unsure about any symptom or option, consult with a healthcare professional for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it for further evaluation and treatment.
