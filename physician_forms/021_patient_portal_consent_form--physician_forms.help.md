# Patient Portal Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Portal Consent Form is a document that allows you to obtain informed consent from patients before using our patient portal system. This form is essential for ensuring that patients understand how their data will be used, stored, and protected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to understand the questions and options presented.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Portal First Page
* **Name**, **Patient Portal First Page**, **Optional**: This is the first page of the form where you will be asked to provide some initial information. Please fill out this page carefully and honestly.
### Patient Portal Second Page
* **Consent to Use Patient Data**, **Consent to Use Patient Data**, **Optional**: Please select **Yes** if you consent to our use of your patient data for your treatment and healthcare management. If you select **No**, please do not worry, as we will not use your data without your explicit consent.
### Patient Portal Third Page
* **Date of Consent**, **Date of Consent**, **Optional**: This is the date on which you are providing consent to our use of your patient portal system. Please enter the current date using the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
### Patient Portal Fourth Page
* **Electronic Communication Preferences**, **Electronic Communication Preferences, **Optional**: Please select **Yes** if you wish to receive electronic communications from us, such as appointment reminders, medication lists, and lab results. If you select **No**, you will not receive any electronic communications.
### Patient Portal Fifth Page
* **Email Address**, **Email Address, **Optional**: This is the email address to which you would like our electronic communications to be sent. Please enter your primary email address or any secondary email address where you would like to receive messages.
### Patient Portal Sixth Page
* **Available Time Slots**, **Available Time Slots, **Optional**: This is the time of day when you are available to receive electronic communications. Please select the time slots that work best for you.
### Patient Portal Seventh Page
* **Additional Comments or Concerns**, **Additional Comments or Concerns, **Optional**: This is a text box where you can provide any additional comments or concerns you may have about our patient portal system.

## Tips
* Please review and understand each field carefully before proceeding.
* If you have any doubts or questions, don't hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
* Your consent is essential for us to provide you with high-quality care. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
