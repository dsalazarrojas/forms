# Pediatrics Medical Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatrics Medical Release Form is used to gather information about the parents or guardians of a child being seen by a physician.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are the primary parent or guardian (parent1).
2. If you are the primary parent or guardian, enter your name in the "parent1_name" field.
3. If you are related to the primary parent or guardian, select either "Yes" or "No" in the "parent1_relationship" field.
4. If you are a secondary parent or guardian, enter your name in the "parent2_name" field.
5. If you are related to the secondary parent or guardian, select either "Yes" or "No" in the "parent2_relationship" field.
6. Enter the name of the emergency contact person in the "emergency_contact_name" field.
7. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person in the "emergency_contact_phone" field.
8. Select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate the relationship between the child and the emergency contact person.
9. Enter the phone number of the child's guardian or parent who can be contacted in the "guardian_phone" field.
10. Enter the email address of the emergency contact person in the "emergency_contact_email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **parent_information**: This field allows you to select whether you are the primary parent or guardian (Yes) or not (No).
  • Type: Select multiple
  • Required: No
- **parent1_name**: This field is for the primary parent or guardian to enter their name.
  • Type: Text
  • Required: No
- **parent1_relationship**: This field is for the primary parent or guardian to indicate their relationship to the child.
  • Type: Select One
  • Required: No
- **parent2_name**: This field is for the secondary parent or guardian to enter their name.
  • Type: Text
  • Required: No
- **parent2_relationship**: This field is for the secondary parent or guardian to indicate their relationship to the child.
  • Type: Select One
  • Required: No
- **emergency_contact_name**: This field is for the parent or guardian to enter the name of the emergency contact person.
  • Type: Text
  • Required: No
- **emergency_contact_phone**: This field is for the parent or guardian to enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
  • Type: Text
  • Required: No
- **emergency_contact_relationship**: This field is for the parent or guardian to indicate the relationship between the child and the emergency contact person.
  • Type: Select One
  • Required: No
- **guardian_phone**: This field is for the parent or guardian to enter their own phone number.
  • Type: Text
  • Required: No
- **emergency_contact_email**: This field is for the parent or guardian to enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
  • Type: Email
  • Required: No
