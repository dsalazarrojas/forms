# eye_doctor_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule appointments with eye doctors for patients.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient information in the "Patient Information" field.
2. Choose a date for the appointment in the "Appointment Date" field.
3. Select a time for the appointment in the "Appointment Time" field.
4. Indicate whether you are selecting a date for the appointment in the "Selected Date" field.
5. Select any relevant time slots for the appointment in the "Selected Time" field.
6. Enter any relevant doctor information in the "Doctor Information" field.
7. Enter the patient's contact information, including email and phone number.
8. Add any additional notes about the appointment in the "Notes" field.
9. Confirm whether the appointment is confirmed in the "Confirm Appointment" field.
10. Indicate whether a follow-up is required in the "Confirm Follow-up" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Patient Information)**: This field is used to enter the patient's name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Appointment Date (Appointment Date)**: This field is used to select the date of the appointment.
* **Appointment Time (Appointment Time)**: This field is used to select the time of the appointment.
* **Selected Date (Selected Date)**: This field is used to indicate whether a date is selected for the appointment.
* **Selected Time (Selected Time)**: This field is used to select multiple time slots for the appointment.
* **Doctor Information (Doctor Information)**: This field is used to enter any relevant information about the doctor, such as their name and contact details.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)**: This field is used to enter the patient's contact information, including email and phone number.
* **Notes (Notes)**: This field is used to add any additional notes about the appointment.
* **Email (Email)**: This field is used to enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone (Phone)**: This field is used to enter the patient's phone number.
* **Confirm Appointment (Confirm Appointment)**: This field is used to confirm whether the appointment is confirmed.
* **Confirm Follow-up (Confirm Follow-up)**: This field is used to indicate whether a follow-up is required.
