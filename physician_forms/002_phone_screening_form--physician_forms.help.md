# Phone Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from candidates, including their phone numbers, availability for screening, and preferred contact details. It's a simple and straightforward form that ensures you have all the necessary details to proceed with the screening process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the candidate's information, including their phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
2. Choose a date and time for the phone screening in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
3. Add any additional notes or comments about the candidate in the "Note" field.
4. Select the phone screening questions you'd like to ask the candidate in the "Phone Screening Questions" field.
5. Finally, submit the form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Candidate Info** (`candidate_info`, Text, Required: No)
This field is for providing basic information about the candidate.

### **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Select One, Required: No)
Select the candidate's phone number from the provided options.

### **Date** (`date`, Date, Required: No)
Choose the date for the phone screening.

### **Time** (`time`, Time, Required: No)
Choose the time for the phone screening.

### **Note** (`note`, Note, Required: No)
Add any additional notes or comments about the candidate.

### **Email** (`email`, Email, Required: No)
Enter the candidate's email address for contact purposes.

### **Phone Screening Questions** (`phone_screening_questions`, Select Multiple, Required: Yes)
Select the questions you'd like to ask the candidate during the phone screening. 

### **Submit** (`submit`, Text, Required: No)
Submit the completed form to proceed with the screening process.

## Tips
* Ensure you have the necessary permission to collect and use the candidate's contact information.
* Be prepared to ask the selected questions during the phone screening.
* Review the candidate's information before proceeding with the screening process.
* Keep in mind that this form is for collecting data and not for storing sensitive or confidential information.
