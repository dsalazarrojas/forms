# Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect referral information for patients seeking medical care or services. It should only be used for patients who have a doctor referring them for a specific treatment or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Please fill in the referral information as requested by your doctor or healthcare provider.
- Ensure that you accurately enter the name of the doctor who is referring you (referrer).
- Select the appropriate referral type for your visit (Elective, Urgent, Emergency).
- Enter the date and time of your visit.
- If desired, provide additional comments for your doctor or healthcare provider.
- If you have any comments for your doctor, please provide them in the text box below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Referral**:
  * Type: text
  * Purpose: Briefly enter the referral information as requested by your doctor or healthcare provider.
- **Referrer**:
  * Type: select multiple
  * Purpose: Select the name of the doctor or healthcare provider who referred you. Options: Dr. Smith MD, Dr. Johnson MD, Other
- **Referee**:
  * Type: text
  * Purpose: Enter your name.
- **Date**:
  * Type: date
  * Purpose: Enter the date of your visit.
- **Time**:
  * Type: time
  * Purpose: Enter the time of your visit.
- **Doctor's Comment**:
  * Type: text
  * Purpose: If your doctor has any comments, you can enter them here.
- **Patient's Comment**:
  * Type: text
  * Purpose: If you have any comments for your doctor or healthcare provider, you can enter them here.
- **Referral Type**:
  * Type: select one
  * Purpose: Select the type of referral you have. Options: Elective, Urgent, Emergency

## Tips
- Make sure to accurately and truthfully complete this form.
- If you're unsure about any information, please ask your doctor or healthcare provider for guidance.
- If you need to make changes to the form, please contact the appropriate healthcare personnel.
- The information collected through this form will be used to provide accurate and efficient patient care.
