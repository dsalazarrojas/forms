# # medical_record_certification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Record Certification Form is used to certify the authenticity of a patient's medical record. It requires information about the patient, the healthcare professional, and the details of the medical record.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the healthcare professional's name and title in the "Healthcare Professional" field.
3. Enter the details of the medical record in the "Medical Record" field.
4. Indicate whether the medical record is authentic or not by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Authenti" field.
5. If you have a medical record number, enter it in the "Medical Record Number" field. (Optional)
6. Enter the date of the medical record in the "Medical Record Date" field. (Optional)
7. Enter the time of the medical record in the "Medical Record Time" field. (Optional)
8. Enter the location where the medical record was taken in the "Medical Record Location" field. (Optional)
9. Enter any additional notes about the medical record in the "Medical Record Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Healthcare Professional**: Enter the name and title of the healthcare professional who treated the patient.
* **Medical Record**: Enter the details of the medical record, including any relevant medical history, diagnoses, and treatments.
* **Authenti**: Select "Yes" if the medical record is authentic, or "No" if it is not.
* **Medical Record Number**: If you have a unique identifier for the medical record, enter it here. (Optional)
* **Medical Record Date**: Enter the date the medical record was created or updated. (Optional)
* **Medical Record Time**: Enter the time the medical record was created or updated. (Optional)
* **Medical Record Location**: Enter the location where the medical record was taken. (Optional)
* **Medical Record Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the medical record. (Required)

## Tips

* Make sure to complete the form accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any information, contact the healthcare professional who treated the patient.
* If the medical record is not authentic, explain the reason for certification "No" in the "Authenti" field and provide supporting documentation.
