# Hearing History Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about a patient's hearing history, including details about their hospitalization date, hearing loss type, location, description, and other relevant medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the **patient_name** field.
2. Enter the date of hospitalization in the **date_of_hospitalization** field.
3. Select the type of hearing loss from the options provided in the **hearing_loss_type** field.
4. Describe the location of the hearing loss in the **hearing_loss_location** field.
5. Provide a detailed description of the hearing loss in the **hearing_loss_description** field.
6. Check if the patient uses a hearing aid and select the relevant option in the **hearing_aid_use** field.
7. Enter the date of the hearing test in the **hearing_testing_date** field.
8. Select the result of the hearing test from the options provided in the **hearing_testing_result** field.
9. Enter the name of the healthcare provider who conducted the hearing test in the **hearing_testing_by** field.
10. Enter the location where the hearing test was conducted in the **hearing_testing_place** field.
11. Provide any relevant medical history in the **medical_history** field.
12. Mention any allergies or contraindications in the **allergy_or_contraindication** field.
13. Enter any surgical history in the **surgical_history** field.
14. Enter any additional medical history in the **other_medical_history** field.
15. Describe any previous tinnitus treatment in the **previous_tinnitus_treatment** field.
16. Enter the name of the healthcare provider who conducted the hearing test in the **hearing_tested_with** field.
17. Enter the date of the hearing test in the **hearing_test_date** field.
18. Select the result of the hearing test from the options provided in the **hearing_test_result** field.
19. Enter the name of the healthcare provider who conducted the hearing test in the **hearing_test_by** field.
20. Enter the location where the hearing test was conducted in the **hearing_test_place** field.
21. Enter any comments from the patient in the **patient_comment** field.
22. Enter any comments from the healthcare provider in the **provider_comment** field.
23. Enter any additional next steps or instructions in the **next_steps** field.
24. Schedule a follow-up appointment in the **follow_up** field.
25. Enter any other relevant notes in the **other_notes** field.
