# Health Status Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect comprehensive health information from patients or users, including their health status, medical history, and treatment plans. The information collected will be used for patient records, research, or other medical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in all required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Use the provided options for select fields such as Medical Board Certified Date and Submit.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
4. Save as Draft if you need to make changes or add more information later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info (1)** (`general_info`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your health status.
* **Health Status (2)** (`health_status`, text, required): Describe your current health status.
* **Symptoms (3)** (`symptoms`, text, optional): List any symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Treatment Plan (4)** (`treatment_plan`, text, optional): Outline your current treatment plan.
* **Medication (5)** (`medication`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergy (6)** (`allergy`, text, optional): List any allergies or sensitivities.
* **Medical History (7)** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe your medical history.
* **Medical Conditions (8)** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Tests (9)** (`medical_tests`, text, optional): List any medical tests you have undergone.
* **Medical Procedures (10)** (`medical_procedures`, text, optional): Describe any medical procedures you have undergone.
* **Patient Info (11)** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional patient information.
* **Medical Specialties (12)** (`medical_specialties`, text, optional): List any medical specialties you have.
* **Medical Equipment (13)** (`medical_equipment`, text, optional): List any medical equipment you use.
* **Medical Supplies (14)** (`medical_supplies`, text, optional): List any medical supplies you have.
* **Medical Facilities (15)** (`medical_facilities`, text, optional): Describe any medical facilities you have used.
* **Medical Personnel (16)** (`medical_personnel`, text, optional): List any medical personnel you have worked with.
* **Medical Education (17)** (`medical_education`, text, optional): Describe your medical education.
* **Medical Training (18)** (`medical_training`, text, optional): Describe any medical training you have.
* **Medical Certification (19)** (`medical_certification`, text, optional): List any medical certifications you have.
* **Medical License (20)** (`medical_license`, text, optional): List your medical license.
* **Medical Board Certified (21)** (`medical_board_certified`, text, optional): List any medical board certifications.
* **Medical Board Certified Date (22)** (`medical_board_certified_date`, date, optional): Date of your medical board certification.
* **Medical Degree (23)** (`medical_degree`, text, optional): List your medical degree.
* **Medical School (24)** (`medical_school`, text, optional): Name of your medical school.
* **Submit (25)** (`submit`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to submit the form: Save and Submit or Save as Draft.

Please ensure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
