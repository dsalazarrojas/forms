# Coronavirus Molecular Testing Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Coronavirus Molecular Testing Request Form is used to request molecular testing for a patient who has been tested for or is suspected of having a coronavirus infection. This form is intended to collect relevant information about the patient and their condition to ensure that the testing process is carried out efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

This form has two pages with several fields that need to be filled out in the following order:

1. **Patient Information (Page 1)**
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Patient Email**: Enter the patient's email address, if available.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number, if available.
* **Healthcare Provider**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider who is requesting the test.
* **Date of Symptom Onset**: Enter the date the patient first experienced symptoms.
* **Date of Sample Collection**: Enter the date the sample was collected.
* **Sample Type**: Select the type of sample being collected (e.g. type1, type2, type3).
* **Request Type**: Select the type of testing requested (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Sample Collection Method**: Select the method used to collect the sample (e.g. method1, method2, method3).
2. **Additional Information (Page 2)**
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional information about the patient or testing request that is not captured in the other fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, required): Enter the patient's email address, if available.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, required): Enter the patient's phone number, if available.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who is requesting the test.
* **Date of Symptom Onset** (`date_of_symptom_onset`, date, required): Enter the date the patient first experienced symptoms.
* **Date of Sample Collection** (`date_of_sample_collection`, date, required): Enter the date the sample was collected.
* **Sample Type** (`sample_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of sample being collected (e.g. type1, type2, type3).
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of testing requested (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Sample Collection Method** (`sample_collection_method`, select_multiple, required): Select the method used to collect the sample (e.g. method1, method2, method3).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Enter any additional information about the patient or testing request that is not captured in the other fields.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Enter accurate and legible handwriting.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with your supervisor or the laboratory staff.
