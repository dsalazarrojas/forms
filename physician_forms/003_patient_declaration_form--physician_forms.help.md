# patient-declaration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The patient declaration form is used to collect information from patients about their medical condition, treatment, and insurance information for medical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and contact information in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. If applicable, select your medical condition(s) from the "Medical Condition" dropdown menu.
4. Check the "Consent Given" field to indicate if you have given consent for treatment and medical care.
5. Enter the date and duration of your treatment in the "Date of Treatment" and "Duration of Treatment" fields respectively.
6. Enter your medical provider's information in the "Medical Provider" field.
7. Enter your medical facility's information in the "Medical Facility" field.
8. Provide any additional contact information you may have in the "Patient Contact Info" field.
9. Enter your medical insurance information in the "Medical Insurance" field.
10. Sign and date your declaration in the "Patient Signature" and "Date of Signature" fields.
11. Finally, your healthcare provider will review and sign the form in the "Physician Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_declaration_form_1`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`patient_declaration_form_2`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical Condition** (`patient_declaration_form_3`, text, optional): If applicable, select your medical condition from the dropdown menu.
* **Consent Given** (`patient_declaration_form_4`, select_one, optional): Check the box to indicate if you have given consent for treatment and medical care.
* **Medical Condition** (`patient_declaration_form_5`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, select your medical condition from the dropdown menu.
* **Date of Treatment** (`patient_declaration_form_6`, date, optional): Enter the date of your treatment.
* **Time of Treatment** (`patient_declaration_form_7`, time, optional): Enter the time of your treatment.
* **Duration of Treatment** (`patient_declaration_form_8`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your treatment in minutes.
* **Medical Provider** (`patient_declaration_form_9`, text, optional): Enter your healthcare provider's name and contact information.
* **Medical Facility** (`patient_declaration_form_10`, text, optional): Enter your medical facility's name and contact information.
* **Patient Contact Info** (`patient_declaration_form_11`, note, optional): Provide any additional contact information you may have.
* **Medical Insurance** (`patient_declaration_form_12`, text, optional): Enter your medical insurance information.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_declaration_form_13`, text, optional): Sign your declaration with your signature.
* **Date of Signature** (`patient_declaration_form_14`, date, optional): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Physician Signature** (`patient_declaration_form_15`, text, optional): Your healthcare provider will sign the form.
* **Physician Name** (`patient_declaration_form_16`, text, optional): Enter your healthcare provider's name.
* **Physician Title** (`patient_declaration_form_17`, text, optional): Enter your healthcare provider's title.
* **Physician Contact Info** (`patient_declaration_form_18`, text, optional): Enter your healthcare provider's contact information.
* **Date of Review** (`patient_declaration_form_19`, date, optional): Enter the date of review.
* **Review Status** (`patient_declaration_form_20`, select_one, optional): Check the box to indicate the review status.
* **Date of Revision** (`patient_declaration_form_21`, date, optional): Enter the date of revision.
* **Reason for Revision** (`patient_declaration_form_22`, text, optional): Enter the reason for revision.
* **Revision Comments** (`patient_declaration_form_23`, note, optional): Provide any revision comments.
* **Reviewer Name** (`patient_declaration_form_24`, text, optional): Enter the reviewer's name.
* **Reviewer Contact Info** (`patient_declaration_form_25`, text, optional): Enter the reviewer's contact information.
