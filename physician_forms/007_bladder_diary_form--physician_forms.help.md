# Bladder Diary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bladder Diary Form is a daily record of your bladder and urinary health. Please use this form to track your symptoms, fluid intake, and other factors that may affect your bladder health.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name and medical record number in the corresponding fields.
2. Record the date of entry in the "Date of Entry" field.
3. Report the time you woke up in the "Time You Woke Up" field.
4. Report the time you went to bed in the "Time You Went to Bed" field (if applicable).
5. Report the number of urination episodes throughout the day in the "Number of daytime urination episodes" and "Number of nighttime urination episodes" fields.
6. Report the number of incontinence episodes in the "Number of incontinence episodes" field.
7. Select the type of incontinence experienced from the "Type of incontinence experienced" field.
8. Report the total fluid intake in ounces in the "Total fluid intake (in ounces)" field (if applicable).
9. Select the types of fluids consumed from the "Types of fluids consumed" field (if applicable).
10. Report the color of your urine in the "Urine color" field.
11. Report your pain or discomfort level on a scale of 0-10 in the "Pain or discomfort level (0-10)" field (if applicable).
12. Report the location of any pain or discomfort in the "Location of pain (if any)" field (if applicable).
13. Report any medications taken today in the "Medications taken today" field (if applicable).
14. Provide any special diet or foods consumed in the "Special diet or foods consumed" field (if applicable).
15. Report your activity level today in the "Activity level today" field (if applicable).
16. Report your sleep quality today in the "Sleep quality" field (if applicable).
17. Report your stress level on a scale of 1-10 in the "Stress level today (1-10)" field (if applicable).
18. Provide any additional notes or observations about your bladder health in the "Additional notes or observations" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Daily Bladder Health Diary --**: This is a note field where you can record any general comments or reminders about your daily bladder health.
* **Patient Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your medical records.
* **Patient ID or Medical Record Number**: Please enter your medical record number.
* **Date of Entry**: Record the date of entry for each day.
* **Time You Woke Up**: Report the time you woke up in the morning.
* **Time You Went to Bed**: Report the time you went to bed (if applicable).
* **Number of daytime urination episodes**: Report the number of times you urinated during the day.
* **Number of nighttime urination episodes**: Report the number of times you urinated at night.
* **Number of incontinence episodes**: Report the number of times you experienced incontinence.
* **Type of incontinence experienced**: Select all types of incontinence you experienced.
* **Total fluid intake (in ounces)**: Report your total fluid intake for the day (if applicable).
* **Types of fluids consumed**: Select all types of fluids you consumed today (if applicable).
* **Urine color**: Report the color of your urine.
* **Pain or discomfort level (0-10)**: Report your pain or discomfort level on a scale of 0-10.
* **Location of pain (if any)**: Report the location of any pain or discomfort you experienced.
* **Pain or medications taken today**: Report any pain medications taken today (if applicable).
* **Special diet or foods consumed**: Report any special diet or foods consumed today (if applicable).
* **Activity level today**: Report your activity level today.
* **Sleep quality**: Report your sleep quality today.
* **Stress level today (1-10)**: Report your stress level on a scale of 1-10.
* **Additional notes or observations**: Provide any additional comments or observations about your bladder health.
