The purpose of this form is to collect information necessary for reimbursement of prescriptions. This form is used by physicians to provide details about a patient's prescription, including the patient's medical condition, treatment, and billing information. This form is typically used in medical settings to ensure accurate and efficient reimbursement processing.

Before completing this form, please ensure that you are the authorized physician for the patient and that you have the necessary information to fill out the form accurately. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your medical team or supervisor for clarification.

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

**How To Complete This Form**

1.  Fill out the patient information fields (fields 1-5) with the patient's details.
2.  Select the appropriate prescription type (field 2) based on the patient's treatment.
3.  Provide detailed information about the patient's medical condition (field 3).
4.  Select the patient's treatment options (field 7) if applicable.
5.  Enter the patient's and physician's contact information (fields 11-17).
6.  Enter the patient's and physician's signature (fields 19-20).
7.  Enter the prescription number (field 24) if applicable.
8.  Sign and date the form (field 25).
