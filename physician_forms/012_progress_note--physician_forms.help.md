# Progress Note - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Progress Note form is used to document the progress of a patient's treatment or care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the patient's chart or medical record to ensure accuracy and completeness.
2. Fill out the form with relevant information about the patient's condition, medical history, treatment plans, and other important details.
3. Be as detailed as possible, but concise and focused on the key points.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your role and completion date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Condition** (`patient_condition`, text, optional): Briefly describe the patient's current condition or medical status.
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the attending physician.
* **Patient History** (`patient_history`, text, optional): Summarize the patient's medical history, including previous illnesses, treatments, and outcomes.
* **Treatment Plans** (`treatment_plans`, text, optional): Outline the treatment plans or recommendations for the patient's care.
* **Next Visit** (`next_visit`, date, optional): Record the scheduled date for the next visit or appointment.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): List any prescribed medications or treatments.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): Document any known allergies or sensitivities.
* **Medications Allergies** (`medications_allergies`, text, optional): Specify any allergies or sensitivities related to medications.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide additional information about the patient's medical history.
* **Lab Results** (`lab_results`, text, optional): Enter any relevant lab test results or data.
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, text, optional): Record the patient's vital signs, such as blood pressure or temperature.
* **Other Info** (`other_info`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant information about the patient's care or condition.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible, but concise and focused on the key points.
* Use clear and legible handwriting to ensure readability.
* Sign and date the form to confirm your role and completion date.
* Keep the form up-to-date and accurate for future reference.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your healthcare team or supervisor.
