# Doctor Note Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture a doctor's notes for a patient, including key details about the patient, the doctor, and the reason for the visit. It's intended to be completed by a medical professional and serves as a record-keeping tool for the patient's medical history.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by filling in the required fields**: The form has several optional fields, but you are required to fill in the "Patient Name", "Doctor", and "Date" fields.
2. **Enter the patient's name**: This is the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
3. **Enter your name**: This is your name as a medical professional, which will be recorded on the patient's medical record.
4. **Enter the date of the visit**: This is the date of the patient's visit to your practice or clinic.
5. **Briefly describe the reason for the visit**: This can include the patient's symptoms, complaints, or any other relevant details.
6. **Add any additional medical notes or comments**: If you have any further information or comments about the patient's medical history or treatment, you can add them here.
7. **Sign the form**: Please sign the form to authenticate your role as a medical professional.
8. **Have the patient sign (if required)**: Depending on your practice or clinic's policies, you may need to have the patient sign the form as well, indicating they acknowledge the contents.
9. **Add any other relevant medical group information**: If you're part of a larger medical group or practice, you can add any necessary details here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Doctor** (`doctor_name`, text, required): This is your name as a medical professional, which will be recorded on the patient's medical record.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the patient's visit in a standard date format (e.g., MM/DD/YY).
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Describe the patient's symptoms, complaints, or any other relevant details about the visit.
* **Doctor** (`doctor_signature`, text, required): Sign your name as a medical professional, authenticating your role.
* **Patient** (`patient_signature`, text, optional): If required by your practice or clinic, have the patient sign to acknowledge the contents of the form.
* **Medical Group** (`medical_group`, text, optional): Add any relevant medical group information, if applicable.
* **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, note, optional): Enter any additional medical notes or comments about the patient's history or treatment.

## Tips

* Always review and update the form carefully before completing it.
* Use clear and concise language when filling in the fields.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions or approvals to complete this form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, consult with your medical group or practice's guidelines and policies.

Note: This guide is intended to provide a general understanding of the form and its fields. It's always best to consult with your medical group or practice's specific guidelines and policies for more detailed information and guidance.
