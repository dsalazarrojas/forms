# Respirator Medical Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for the medical evaluation of patients who are undergoing respiratory therapy. The form captures patient demographics, medical history, and relevant medical data. It also includes space for physician notes and signatures to ensure accurate and comprehensive assessment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Patient Name** field with the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
2. Provide a brief description of the patient's **Medical History** in the text field. Include any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or medications.
3. List the patient's current **Medications** if any.
4. Report any **Allergies** the patient has.
5. Select the type of **Medical Equipment** the patient is currently using or has used in the past, such as PAP, NIPPV, BiPAP, or CPAP.
6. Enter the patient's **Weight**, **Height**, and **Respiratory Rate** if applicable.
7. Enter the patient's **Oxygen Saturation** level if available.
8. Provide any additional **Medical Notes** regarding the patient's condition.
9. Sign and date your **Physician Signature** to confirm your evaluation.
10. Enter your **Physician Name** for identification purposes.
11. Provide your contact information, including **Physician Contact** (name, email, phone number).
12. Provide the patient's contact information, including **Patient Contact** (name, email, phone number).
13. Enter the **Date of Last Evaluation**, **Date of Next Evaluation**, and **Date of First Evaluation**, if applicable.
14. Finally, sign and date your **Physician Signature Date** to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Medical History**: Provide a brief description of the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or medications.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies the patient has.
* **Medications**: List the patient's current medications if any.
* **Medical Conditions**: Report any medical conditions affecting the patient.
* **Respirator History**: Enter a brief description of the patient's respiratory history.
* **Physician Comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes regarding the patient's condition.
* **Physician Signature**: Sign and date your signature to confirm your evaluation.
* **Physician Name**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Physician Contact**: Provide your contact information (name, email, phone number).
* **Medical Contact**: Provide the contact information (name, email, phone number) of the healthcare institution or organization.
* **Patient Contact**: Provide the patient's contact information (name, email, phone number).
* **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Date of Last Evaluation**: Enter the date of the last evaluation, if applicable.
* **Date of Next Evaluation**: Enter the date of the next evaluation, if applicable.
* **Date of First Evaluation**: Enter the date of the first evaluation, if applicable.
* **Medical Equipment**: Select the type of medical equipment the patient is currently using or has used in the past.
* **Weight**: Enter the patient's weight if applicable.
* **Height**: Enter the patient's height if applicable.
* **Respiratory Rate**: Enter the patient's respiratory rate if applicable.
* **Oxygen Saturation**: Enter the patient's oxygen saturation level if available.
* **Medical Notes**: Enter any additional medical notes regarding the patient's condition.
* **Physician Signature Date**: Sign and date your signature to complete the form.
