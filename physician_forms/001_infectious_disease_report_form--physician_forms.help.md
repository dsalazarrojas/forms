# Infectious Disease Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about patients with infectious diseases. It helps to collect data on various aspects of the disease, such as symptoms, medical history, exposure history, and disease characteristics. The form is used for monitoring, research, or educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

### 1. Patient Information (ID: 1)
* This field is used to collect general information about the patient, such as name, date of birth, contact information, etc.
* Please fill in the relevant details as per your institution's data collection policies.

### 2. Disease Symptoms (ID: 2)
* This field is used to record the symptoms exhibited by the patient, such as fever, cough, sore throat, etc.
* Please list all relevant symptoms, even if some may seem unrelated or insignificant.

### 3. Medical History (ID: 4)
* This field is used to collect information about the patient's past medical history, such as allergies, vaccinations, previous illnesses, etc.
* Please be as detailed as possible, as it helps in understanding the patient's medical background.

### 4. Exposure History (ID: 4)
* This field is used to record any potential exposure to infectious sources, such as travel, contact with infected persons or animals, etc.
* Please include dates and places of exposure.

### 5. Contact History (ID: 5)
* This field is used to gather information about any contacts with other infected individuals or animals.
* Please include names, dates, and places of contact.

### 6. Disease Outbreaks (ID: 6)
* This field is used to track any reported disease outbreaks in the patient's area.
* Please include dates and locations of outbreaks.

### 7. Disease Characteristics (ID: 7)
* This field is used to collect information about the disease's characteristics, such as its type, duration, and severity.
* Please be as specific as possible, and use any supporting documentation as necessary.

### 8. Medical Treatment (ID: 8)
* This field is used to record the patient's medical treatment, including any medications, surgeries, or other interventions.
* Please list all treatments received, even if some may not seem relevant.

### 9. Patient Outcomes (ID: 9)
* This field is used to collect information about the patient's outcome, such as recovery, death, or ongoing treatment.
* Please update this field as the patient's condition changes.

### 10. Physician Comments (ID: 10)
* This field is used for the physician to add notes and comments about the patient's case.
* Please be thorough in your comments, as they help other healthcare professionals understand the patient's situation.

### 11. Exposure Period (ID: 11)
* This field is used to record the period of exposure to the disease.
* Please include dates and duration of exposure.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all information collected is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your healthcare team or supervisor.
