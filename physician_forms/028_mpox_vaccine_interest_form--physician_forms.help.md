# Mpox Vaccine Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who are interested in receiving the Mpox vaccine. If you have been exposed to someone with Mpox or have had contact with the disease, please fill out this form to express your interest in the vaccine and provide necessary information for scheduling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the correct options for each field, as instructed.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Basic Information** (`basic_info`, text, required): Please enter your basic contact information, such as your name and phone number.
* **Health History** (`health_history`, text, required): Please describe your health status, including any medical conditions or allergies that may affect your ability to receive the vaccine.
* **Potential Exposure** (`potential_exposure`, select_one, required): Please select one of the following:
	+ Risk Category: If you have had contact with someone who has had Mpox.
	+ No Risk Category: If you have not had contact with someone who has had Mpox.
	+ Not Sure: If you are unsure whether you have had contact with someone who has had Mpox.
* **Vaccine Interest** (`vaccine_interest`, select_multiple, required): Please select one or both of the following:
	+ Interested: If you are interested in receiving the vaccine.
	+ Not Interested: If you are not interested in receiving the vaccine.
	+ Undecided: If you are unsure about receiving the vaccine.
* **Eligibility** (`eligibility`, text, required): This field is not currently used for eligibility checks, so please enter any relevant information about your medical condition that may affect your ability to receive the vaccine.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, text, required): Please enter your health insurance information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Please enter any relevant medical history that may affect your ability to receive the vaccine.
* **Doctor Recommended** (`doctor_recommended`, text, optional): If your doctor has recommended you for the vaccine, please enter their name.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional): If you have a doctor who has recommended you for the vaccine, please enter their name.
* **Doctor Phone Number** (`doctor_phone_number`, text, optional): If you have a doctor who has recommended you for the vaccine, please enter their phone number.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, text, optional): If you have a doctor who has recommended you for the vaccine, please enter their email address.
* **Date Last Seen Doctor** (`date_last_seen_doctor`, date, optional): If you have a doctor who has recommended you for the vaccine, please enter the date of your last visit.
* **Reason for Interest** (`reason_for_interest`, text, optional): Please explain why you are interested in receiving the vaccine.
* **Vaccine Side Effects** (`vaccine_side_effects`, text, optional): Please describe any concerns you may have about vaccine side effects.
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, select_one, optional): Please select how you would like to be contacted:
	+ Phone: If you would like to be contacted by phone.
	+ Email: If you would like to be contacted by email.
	+ Mail: If you would like to be contacted by mail.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, select_one, optional): Please select whether you would like to be followed up on:
	+ True: If you would like to be followed up on.
	+ False: If you do not want to be followed up on.
* **Vaccine Availability** (`vaccine_availability`, select_one, optional): Please select when you believe the vaccine is available:
	+ Available: If you believe the vaccine is available.
	+ Not Available: If you do not believe the vaccine is available.
	+ Not Sure: If you are unsure when the vaccine is available.
* **Vaccine Availability Date** (`vaccine_availability_date`, date, optional): If you believe the vaccine is available, please enter the date of availability.
* **Vaccine Availability Time** (`vaccine_availability_time`, time, optional): If you believe the vaccine is available, please enter the time of availability.
* **Next Vaccine Availability** (`next_vaccine_availability`, date, optional): If you are unsure or do not know when the vaccine will be available, please enter the expected date of the next availability.
* **Next Vaccine Availability Time** (`next_vaccine_availability_time`, time, optional): If you are unsure or do not know when the vaccine will be available, please enter the expected time of the next availability.
