# telemedicine_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telemedicine Consent Form is a document that a patient must complete before participating in a telemedicine consultation. This form ensures that patients understand their rights and responsibilities during telemedicine consultations and obtain their informed consent to proceed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the **Patient Information** field with your name, date of birth, and ID number (if applicable).
2. Choose your answer to the **Consent** question, selecting all options that best suit your needs.
3. Read and understand the **Telemedicine Information** section, which explains the risks and benefits of telemedicine consultations.
4. Fill out the **Physician Information** field with your name, title, and ID number (if applicable).
5. Sign the **Patient Signature** field to confirm that you have read and understood this form.
6. Sign the **Physician Signature** field to confirm that you have informed the patient of their rights and responsibilities and that they have given their consent.
7. Select the **Date** and **Time** of your telemedicine consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information (1)**: Please enter your name, date of birth, and ID number (if applicable).
- **Consent (2)**: Select one or more options to confirm your consent for the telemedicine consultation. Options are:
    - Yes
    - No
- **Telemedicine Information (3)**: This is a text field explaining the risks and benefits of telemedicine consultations. Please read and understand this section carefully.
- **Physician Information (4)**: Please enter your physician's name, title, and ID number (if applicable).
- **Patient Signature (5)**: This is a field where you will sign to confirm that you have read and understood this form.
- **Physician Signature (6)**: This is a field where your physician will sign to confirm that they have informed you of your rights and responsibilities and that you have given your consent.
- **Date (7)**: Please select the date of your telemedicine consultation.
- **Time (8)**: Please select the time of your telemedicine consultation.
