# Patient Health Questionnaire And Generalized Anxiety Disorder Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form aims to help patients report their symptoms of Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD) and collect general information for healthcare professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **General Information** page with your details.
2.  Answer the **Generalized Anxiety Disorder Questionnaire** questions to the best of your ability.
3.  Add any relevant notes in the **Note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Information (General Information, text, optional)**: This section is for you to provide some basic information about yourself.
*   **Generalized Anxiety Disorder Questionnaire (Generalized Anxiety Disorder Questionnaire, text, optional)**: This section is to assess your anxiety symptoms. Answer the questions honestly to help healthcare professionals understand your condition better.
*   **Note (Note, note, optional)**: If you have any additional comments or information about your symptoms or health status, please add them here.

## Tips
- Be honest when answering the questionnaire questions to ensure accurate assessment.
- If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
- The **Note** field is for any extra comments or information you'd like to add.
 
Please use this form to help healthcare professionals understand your anxiety symptoms better. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
