# Mpox Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Investigation Form is designed to gather information about Mpox outbreaks in order to facilitate prompt and accurate investigation and response.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information for each field. While some fields are marked as optional, providing accurate and complete information will help in the prompt investigation and response to the Mpox outbreak.
2. Review each field carefully and ensure that you have provided the correct information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the public health authority for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Case ID** (number, optional): Enter the unique identifier given to the case by the public health authority.
- **Case Number** (text, optional): Enter the unique number assigned to the case.
- **Date of Onset** (date, optional): Enter the date when the symptoms first appeared.
- **Location of Onset** (text, optional): Enter the location where the symptoms first appeared.
- **Patient Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the patient.
- **Date of Admission** (date, optional): Enter the date when the patient was admitted to the hospital.
- **Patient Symptoms** (text, optional): Describe the symptoms presented by the patient.
- **Date of Sample Submission** (date, optional): Enter the date when the sample was submitted for laboratory testing.
- **Laboratory Results** (text, optional): Enter the results of the laboratory testing, including any relevant findings.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form as accurately and completely as possible.
- Review each field carefully to ensure that you have provided the correct information.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the public health authority for guidance.
