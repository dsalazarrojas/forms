# COVID 19 Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Inquiry Form is a tool to gather information and assist individuals with inquiries related to COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Provide your contact information on **Page 1**:
	* Enter your **Full Name** in the **Full Name** field.
	* Enter your **Email Address** in the **Email Address** field.
	* Provide your **Phone Number** in the **Phone Number** field.
	* If applicable, enter the **Organization or Company** name in the **Organization or Company** field.
2. Select the **Type of Inquiry** on **Page 2**:
	* Choose one of the following options: **Vaccination information**, **Symptoms and testing**, **Treatment options**, **Prevention measures**, **Workplace safety**, **General information**, **Appointment request**, or **Other**.
3. Provide details about your **Subject of Inquiry** and **Detailed Message or Question** on **Page 3**:
	* Enter a brief description of your inquiry or concern in the **Subject of Inquiry** field.
	* Enter a detailed message or question in the **Detailed Message or Question** field.
4. Select your **Current COVID-19 status** and **How urgent is this inquiry** on **Page 4**:
	* Choose one of the following options for **Current COVID-19 status**: **No symptoms or diagnosis**, **Currently experiencing symptoms**, **Positive diagnosis**, **Recently recovered**, **Vaccinated**, or **Prefer not to answer**.
	* Choose one of the following options for **How urgent is this inquiry**: **Non-urgent**, **Moderately urgent**, **Urgent**, or **Emergency**.
5. Choose your **Preferred Method of Contact** and **Best Time to Contact** on **Page 5**:
	* Choose one of the following options for **Preferred Method of Contact**: **Email**, **Phone**, **Text message**, or **In-person appointment**.
	* Enter a preferred time for contact in the **Best Time to Contact** field.
6. Consider sharing your contact information with medical staff and consent to receive updates about COVID-19 on **Page 6**:
	* Check the box to allow medical staff to contact you.
	* Choose **True** or **False** to allow us to send you updates about COVID-19.
7. Review and submit your form to ensure you've filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (required): Enter your full name as it is written on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address** (required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (required): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Organization or Company** (optional): If you are representing a company or organization, enter the name here.
* **Type of Inquiry** (required): Choose one of the following options to describe your concern or question.
* **Subject of Inquiry** (required): Enter a brief description of your inquiry or concern.
* **Detailed Message or Question** (required): Enter a detailed message or question.
* **Current COVID-19 status** (optional): Choose one of the following options to describe your current status.
* **How urgent is this inquiry** (required): Choose one of the following options to describe the level of urgency.
* **Preferred Method of Contact** (required): Choose one of the following options to describe your preferred method of contact.
* **Best Time to Contact** (optional): Enter a preferred time for contact.
* **Consent to Share Information** (required): Check the box to allow medical staff to share your information.
* **Receive Updates** (optional): Choose **True** or **False** to allow us to send you updates about COVID-19.
* **Submission Date** (required): The date and time when this form is submitted.

Note: The **Submission Date** field is automatically populated with the current date and time.
