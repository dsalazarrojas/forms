# Medical Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Clearance Form is used to capture medical clearance information for patients undergoing medical procedures or treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select the reason for clearance in the "Reason For Clearance" field. You can choose from "Medical", "Surgical", or other options.
3. In the "Type of Clearance" field, select all applicable medical clearance types. For example, if the patient has both cardiac and pulmonary clearance, select both options.
4. Choose the duration for which the medical clearance is valid. If not selecting a date, leave this field empty.
5. Enter the date of the medical clearance in the "Medical Clearance Date" field.
6. Choose the clearance status in the "Clearance Status" field. You can choose from "Cleared" or "Not Cleared".
7. Enter your name, title, and contact information in the "Proctor's Name", "Proctor's Title", and "Proctor" field respectively.
8. In the "Medical Clearance Provider" field, enter your name and title.
9. Enter your contact information in the "Address", "City", "ZIP", "Phone", "Fax", and "Email" fields.
10. Enter "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have a phone number and fax number in the "State" and "ZIP" fields respectively.
11. Enter your phone number in the "Proctor's Phone" field.
12. Enter your fax number in the "Proctor's Fax" field.
13. Enter your email address in the "Proctor's Email" field.
14. In the "Medical Clearance Comments" and "Medical Clearance Notes" fields, enter any relevant comments or notes about the medical clearance.
