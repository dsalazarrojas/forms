# Laboratory Result Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laboratory Result Form is designed to collect and document laboratory test results for patient care and medical record keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the patient's information to ensure it is accurate and complete.
2. Fill in the test results, including test dates, times, and statuses.
3. Provide a specimen description and any other relevant clinical laboratory information.
4. Select the type of test performed (blood, urine, or saliva).
5. Enter the patient's contact information, including name, address, phone, and fax.
6. Review and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Test Results** (id: 1): Enter the test results for the patient.
* **Medical Officials** (id: 2): Select the medical official who performed the test (e.g., Dr. Smith, Dr. Johnson).
* **Test Date** (id: 3): Enter the date the test was performed.
* **Test Time** (id: 4): Enter the time the test was performed.
* **Specimen Description** (id: 5): Describe the specimen (e.g., blood, urine, saliva).
* **Results** (id: 6): Enter any additional test results or notes.
* **Clinical Laboratory** (id: 7): Enter the name of the clinical laboratory where the test was performed.
* **Medical Notes** (id: 8): Enter any additional medical notes or comments.
* **Test Results Notes** (id: 9): Enter any additional notes or comments about the test results.
* **Submit Form** (id: 10): Select "Submit" to complete and submit the form, or "Do Not Submit" to review and edit.
* **Email** (id: 11): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone** (id: 12): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Fax** (id: 13): Enter the patient's fax number.
* **Patient Name** (id: 14): Enter the patient's name.
* **Test Type** (id: 15): Select the type of test performed (e.g., blood test, urine test, saliva test).
* **Patient ID** (id: 16): Enter the patient's ID number.
* **Clinical Laboratory Name** (id: 17): Enter the name of the clinical laboratory where the test was performed.
* **Test Results Status** (id: 18): Select the status of the test results (e.g., positive, negative, inconclusive).
* **Test Results Date** (id: 19): Enter the date the test results were received.
* **Patient Address** (id: 20): Enter the patient's address.
* **Patient City** (id: 21): Enter the patient's city.
* **Patient State** (id: 22): Enter the patient's state.
* **Patient Zip** (id: 23): Enter the patient's zip code.
* **Medical Officials Name** (id: 24): Enter the name of the medical official who performed the test.
* **Specimen Type** (id: 25): Select the type of specimen (e.g., blood, urine, saliva).
