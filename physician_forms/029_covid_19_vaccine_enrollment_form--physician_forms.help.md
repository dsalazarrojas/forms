# COVID 19 Vaccine Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information from participants for the COVID-19 vaccination program. The form is used to determine the participant's eligibility, consent, and other necessary details for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter your age in the "Age" field.
4. Select your gender from the "Gender" field.
5. Fill in your contact information in the "Contact Information" field, which includes your email address and phone number(s).
6. Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code in the "Address Information" field.
7. Select your preferred vaccination location from the "Preferred Vaccination Location" field.
8. Select your preferred vaccination timeline from the "Preferred Vaccination Timeline" field.
9. Choose your preferred vaccine type from the "Vaccine Type Preference" field.
10. Answer the question "Do you have chronic health conditions" in the "Do you have chronic health conditions" field.
11. If you answered yes, describe your chronic conditions in the "If yes, describe conditions" field.
12. Answer the question "Are you immunocompromised" in the "Are you immunocompromised" field.
13. Answer the question "Have you previously diagnosed with COVID-19" in the "Previously diagnosed with COVID-19" field.
14. If you answered yes, enter the date of diagnosis in the "Date of diagnosis" field.
15. Answer the question "Are you insured" in the "Insurance Status" field.
16. If you answered yes, enter your insurance provider name in the "Insurance Provider Name" field.
17. Select your consent option in the "I consent to participate in the vaccination program" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (text, required): Fill in your full name.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Age** (number, required): Enter your age.
* **Gender** (select one, required): Select your gender from the options: Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer to specify, Prefer not to answer.
* **Contact Information** (note, required): Fill in your contact information, including your email address and phone number(s).
* **Street Address** (text, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State or Province** (text, required): Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **Zip Code** (text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Preferred Vaccination Location** (select one, required): Select your preferred vaccination location from the options: Doctor office, Clinic, Pharmacy, Hospital, Mobile clinic, No preference.
* **Preferred Vaccination Timeline** (select one, required): Select your preferred vaccination timeline from the options: Immediately, Within 1 week, Within 2 weeks, Within 1 month, Flexible.
* **Reason for Enrollment** (select one, required): Select your reason for enrollment from the options: Personal health - at-risk individual, Healthcare worker, Essential worker, Frontline worker, Community member, Caregiver to vulnerable person, Other.
* **Vaccine Type Preference** (select one, required): Select your preferred vaccine type from the options: Pfizer, Moderna, Johnson and Johnson, No preference.
* **Do you have chronic health conditions** (select one, required): Answer yes or no to the question.
* **If yes, describe conditions** (text, optional): If you answered yes, describe your chronic conditions.
* **Are you immunocompromised** (select one, required): Answer yes or no to the question.
* **Are you previously diagnosed with COVID-19** (select one, required): Answer yes or no to the question.
* **Date of diagnosis** (date, optional): If you answered yes, enter the date of diagnosis.
* **Insurance Status** (select one, required): Answer yes or no to the question.
* **Insurance Provider Name** (text, optional): If you answered yes, enter your insurance provider name.
* **I consent to participate in the vaccination program** (select one, required): Select your consent option from the options: I agree, I do not agree.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill in all required fields.
* Answer questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, select the option "Unsure" or "Prefer not to answer" and explain your reason.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the program administrator for assistance.
