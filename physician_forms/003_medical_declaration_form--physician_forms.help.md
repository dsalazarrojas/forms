The Medical Declaration Form is a document used to gather information about a patient's health status.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information about the patient on **General Info** (page 1).
2. Provide a detailed travel history on **travel_history** (page 2).
