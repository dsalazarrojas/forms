# Emergency Medicine Residency Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of residents in an emergency medicine residency program. It provides a framework for assessors to rate and provide feedback on a resident's performance, attendance, and progress during their rotations. This form helps program directors and evaluators make informed decisions about resident development and advancement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the resident's name in the "resident_name" field.
*   Enter the name of the medical school the resident attended in the "medical_school_name" field.
*   Enter the name of the rotation the resident completed in the "rotation_name" field.
*   Enter the start and end dates of the rotation in the "rotation_start_date" and "rotation_end_date" fields, respectively.
*   Enter the length of the rotation in the "rotation_length" field.
*   Enter any feedback or comments you have for the resident in the "feedback" field.
*   Enter the resident's attendance record in the "resident_attendance" field.
*   Enter the resident's performance during the rotation in the "resident_performance" field.
