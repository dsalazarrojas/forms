# Pediatric Medical Daycare Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to inspect pediatric daycares to ensure they meet the necessary standards and regulations for a safe and healthy environment for children. It is intended to gather information about the physical space, medical supplies, equipment, and procedures in place at the daycare center.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer each question honestly to provide a detailed account of your inspection findings.
2. For multiple-select fields, select all applicable answers.
3. Provide detailed explanations for text fields where necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Daycare Center Information** (`daycare_center_info`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing any additional information about the daycare center, such as its address, contact number, or hours of operation.
- **Pediatrician Information** (`pediatrician_info`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing information about the pediatrician on staff, including their name, contact number, and qualifications.

- **Medical Equipment** (`medical_equipment`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether the daycare center has the necessary medical equipment, such as thermometers, blood pressure monitors, or respirators.
- **Medical Staff** (`medical_staff`, `select_one`, optional): This field asks whether the daycare center has sufficient medical staff, such as a doctor or nurse, on site at all times.
- **Medical Records** (`medical_records`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field asks whether the daycare center maintains accurate and up-to-date medical records for each child.
- **Emergency Procedure** (`emergency_procedure`, `text`, optional): Provide a detailed description of the daycare center's emergency procedure, including the steps to be taken in case of an emergency.
- **Emergency Equipment** (`emergency_equipment`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field asks whether the daycare center has the necessary emergency equipment, such as defibrillators, oxygen tanks, or first aid kits.
- **Medical Staff Training** (`medical_staff_training`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether the medical staff at the daycare center has received necessary training in emergency procedures.
- **Facility Condition** (`facility_condition`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing information about the condition of the daycare center's physical space, including cleanliness, maintenance, and safety features.

- **Food and Water Temperature** (`food_and_water_temperature`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field asks whether the daycare center maintains proper food and water temperature control, such as refrigeration, hot water, and serving schedules.
