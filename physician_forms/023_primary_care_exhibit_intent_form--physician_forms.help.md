# Primary Care Exhibit Intent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Primary Care Exhibit Intent Form is a tool used to gather information from patients about their primary care exhibit status, enabling healthcare providers to make informed medical decisions and develop effective care plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Primary Care Exhibit Intent Form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and each field.
2.  Fill out any fields that apply to your situation, and leave blank any fields that do not apply.
3.  Answer each field based on your true and accurate information.
4.  Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and consistency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Exhibitor Contact Details
*   **exhibitor_contact_details**: (text, text, optional) Enter your contact details as required by your healthcare provider.

### Focus Area
*   **focus_area**: (select_one, Yes/No, optional) Indicate if you are currently in a focus area, such as intensive care or palliative care.

### Availability
*   **availability**: (date, optional) Provide your availability date, if applicable.

### Intent
*   **intent**: (text, optional) Describe your intent, as needed.

### Email
*   **email**: (email, optional) Enter your email address, if you want to be contacted by your healthcare provider.

### Phone
*   **phone**: (text, optional) Enter your phone number, if you want to be contacted by your healthcare provider.

### Focus Areas
*   **focus_areas**: (select_multiple, Yes/No, optional) Select any applicable focus areas, such as intensive care or palliative care.

### Primary Care Intent Form Description
*   **primary_care_intent_form_description**: (note, optional) Add any additional information about your primary care exhibit status.

### Submit
*   **submit**: (select_multiple, Yes/No, optional) Indicate if you are willing to submit your information.
