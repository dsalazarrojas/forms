# Anti Infectives Key Opinion Leader (KOL) Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information on your experiences and attitudes towards anti-infective medications, treatment practices, and innovative approaches in your field of work.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct answer for each question based on your personal experience and opinion.
2. For questions with a "Select all that apply" option, choose all relevant answers that apply to your situation.
3. If you are unsure or do not have an opinion on a question, select "Not applicable" or "Not informed" accordingly.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback you would like to share in the "Additional comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your professional title or position?** (1) (`professional_title`, `text`, required): Please enter your current role in healthcare or pharmaceutical industry.
* **Years of experience in anti-infectives field** (2) (`years_of_experience`, `number`, required): Approximately how many years you have experience in the anti-infectives field.
* **State of medical license** (3) (`medical_license_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state where you hold your medical license.
* **Primary practice setting** (4) (`practice_setting`, `select_one`, required): Select your main practice environment (Hospital, Private Practice, Clinic, Academic Medical Center, Pharmaceutical Company).
* **How frequently do you prescribe anti-infective medications?** (5) (`antibiotic_prescribing_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you prescribe anti-infective medications (Daily, Several times per week, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely).
* **Which treatment areas are you most involved with?** (6) (`treatment_areas`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all areas you are most involved with (Respiratory infections, Urinary tract infections, Surgical site infections, Bloodstream infections, Skin and soft tissue infections, Gastrointestinal infections).
* **What antibiotic resistance concerns are most pressing in your practice?** (7) (`resistance_concerns`, `text`, not required): Enter your most pressing concerns regarding antibiotic resistance.
* **Do you typically follow established antibiotic treatment guidelines?** (8) (`treatment_guidelines_adherence`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you follow established guidelines (Always, Mostly, Sometimes, Rarely, Never).
* **Which guidelines do you rely on?** (9) (`guideline_sources`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all guidelines you rely on (CDC guidelines, IDSA guidelines, Hospital protocols, Professional associations, Pharmaceutical representatives, Clinical trials data).
* **How informed are you about new anti-infective therapies?** (10) (`awareness_new_therapies`, `select_one`, required): Select how informed you are about new anti-infective therapies (Very informed, Well informed, Moderately informed, Somewhat informed, Not informed).
* **Primary sources for learning about new therapies** (11) (`information_sources`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all sources you rely on for learning about new therapies (Medical journals, Conferences, Continuing education, Pharmaceutical representatives, Professional organizations, Online resources).
* **What barriers do you face in implementing new anti-infective protocols?** (12) (`barriers_implementation`, `text`, not required): Enter any barriers you face in implementing new protocols.
* **Do you collaborate with infectious disease specialists?** (13) (`collaboration_infectious_disease`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you collaborate with infectious disease specialists (Regularly, Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, Never).
* **Are you involved in antimicrobial stewardship programs?** (14) (`stewardship_participation`, `select_one`, not required): Select your level of involvement in antimicrobial stewardship programs (Yes, leadership role, Yes, active participant, Yes, limited involvement, No, but interested, No, not interested).
* **How important is patient education about antibiotic use in your practice?** (15) (`patient_education_priority`, `select_one`, required): Select how important patient education about antibiotic use is in your practice (Very important, Important, Moderately important, Slightly important, Not important).
* **What innovations in anti-infectives are you most interested in?** (16) (`innovation_priorities`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all innovations you are most interested in (Combination therapies, Narrow spectrum agents, Novel mechanisms of action, Improved tolerability, Shorter treatment durations, Improved dosing regimens).
* **Do you track treatment outcomes and resistance patterns?** (17) (`treatment_outcome_tracking`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you track treatment outcomes and resistance patterns (Systematically, Regularly, Occasionally, Rarely, Never).
* **Additional comments or feedback on anti-infectives practice** (18) (`feedback_comments`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional comments or feedback on anti-infectives practice.
* **Email address for follow-up** (19) (`contact_email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address for follow-up, if desired.
* **Would you be willing to serve as a consultant on anti-infectives topics?** (20) (`willing_consultation`, `select_one`, not required): Select your willingness to serve as a consultant on anti-infectives topics (Yes, very interested, Yes, possibly interested, Maybe, No, not interested, Not at this time).

# Tips

* Be accurate and honest in your responses.
* Select the option that best applies to your personal experience and practice.
* If unsure or not informed, select "Not informed" accordingly.
* Enter any additional comments or feedback you would like to share.
