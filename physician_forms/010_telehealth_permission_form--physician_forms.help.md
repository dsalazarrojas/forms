# Telehealth Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telehealth Permission Form is a digital document that requires patients to provide their consent and agreement to participate in telehealth services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field, following the MM/DD/YYYY format.
3. Enter the patient's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Read and sign the "Telehealth Agreement" field, indicating your consent to participate in telehealth services.
6. Read and sign the "Telehealth Agreement Terms" field, indicating your understanding of the terms and conditions of telehealth care.
7. Select "Yes" to confirm that you have read and understood the telehealth agreement and terms.
8. Select "Yes" in the "Patient Signature" field to sign the form.
9. Select "Yes" in the "Provider Signature" field to confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the telehealth agreement and terms.
10. Enter the date and time of the telehealth session in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
11. If required, upload relevant documents in the "Upload Document" fields.
