# Patient Demographics And History Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information about a patient's demographics and medical history, which is crucial for healthcare providers to make informed decisions about patient care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and make sure you are in the correct section for the relevant information.
2. Fill out each field accurately and concisely, following the provided instructions.
3. Make sure to answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Patient Information (Page 1)** (`new_patient_info`, `text`, required: false): This page is meant to collect basic information about the patient. Please provide the patient's name, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Medical History (Page 1)** (`new_patient_history`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose from the available options to indicate the patient's medical history, such as Heart Disease, Diabetes, or Hypertension.
* **Allergies (Page 1)** (`allergies`, `text`, required: false): If the patient has any known allergies, please list them here.
* **Medications (Page 1)** (`medications`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate whether the patient takes any medications, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Medical Conditions (Page 1)** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the box to indicate whether the patient has any medical conditions.
* **Medical Procedures (Page 1)** (`medical_procedures`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose from the options to indicate whether the patient has undergone any medical procedures.
* **Family History (Page 1)** (`family_history`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate the patient's family history, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Medical Family History (Page 1)** (`medical_family_history`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the box to indicate the patient's medical family history.
* **Social History (Page 1)** (`social_history`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose from the options to indicate the patient's social history.
* **Education (Page 2)** (`education`, `text`, required: false): Provide information about the patient's educational background.
* **Occupation (Page 2)** (`occupation`, `text`, required: false): Indicate the patient's occupation or relevant work experience.
* **Contact Information (Page 2)** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide the patient's contact information, such as phone number or email.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* Double-check your responses to ensure they are correct and complete.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask for guidance from a healthcare professional.
