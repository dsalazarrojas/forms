# HIPAA Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HIPAA Waiver Form is a document used to obtain patient consent for the release of their protected health information (PHI). It is a legal requirement for healthcare providers to share PHI with designated parties.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the following fields:
	* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
	* **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's mailing address.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
	* **Physician Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider who will be releasing the PHI.
	* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare facility where the patient received care.
	* **Purpose of Release**: Describe the purpose for which the PHI will be released.
	* **Date of Signature**: Enter the date you sign this form.
	* **Patient Signature**: Sign this form to indicate your consent for the release of your PHI.
	* **Physician Signature**: Sign this form to indicate your agreement to release the PHI.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required): Enter your current address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your healthcare provider who will be releasing your PHI.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare facility where you received care.
* **Purpose of Release** (`purpose_of_release`, text, required): Describe the specific reason for which your PHI will be released.
* **Date of Signature** (`date_of_signature`, date, required): Enter the date you sign this form.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, date, required): Sign this form to confirm your consent for the release of your PHI.
* **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, text, required): Sign this form to confirm your agreement to release your PHI.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* Ensure that you understand the purpose for which your PHI will be released.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with your healthcare provider.
