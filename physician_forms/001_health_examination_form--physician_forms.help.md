# health_examination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for the health examination of a patient. It is designed to collect relevant medical history, symptoms, and test results to aid in the diagnosis and treatment of the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and clearance to access and use this form.
2. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and fields.
3. Complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
4. If you need assistance, do not hesitate to ask for help from a qualified medical professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Health Exam #** (`health_exam`, `number`, required: false): Please select the health exam number as per your hospital's protocol.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's address.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Provide any relevant medical history, including allergies, previous treatments, or medical conditions.
* **Chief Complaint** (`chief_complaint`, `text`, required: false): Describe the patient's main reason for visiting the doctor.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check any allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Test Results** (`test_results`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date and time of any recent test results.
* **Treatement Plan** (`treatment_plan`, `note`, required: false): Enter any treatment or therapy plans recommended.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `date`, required: false): Enter any follow-up appointments or check-ups scheduled.

Note: Please ensure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly. If you are unsure of any field, do not hesitate to ask for help.
