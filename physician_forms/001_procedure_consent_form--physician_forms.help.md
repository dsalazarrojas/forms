# Procedure Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Procedure Consent Form is used to document the informed consent of a patient for a medical procedure. This form ensures that the patient understands and agrees to the risks and benefits associated with the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Procedure Name" field with a brief description of the procedure you are consenting to.
2. In the "Risks" field, list any potential risks or complications associated with the procedure.
3. In the "Benefits" field, list any potential benefits or advantages of the procedure.
4. Enter the "Signature Date" in the format MM/DD/YYYY (Month, Day, Year).
5. Sign your name in the "Signature" field, indicating your consent to the procedure.
6. Enter the name of the physician or healthcare provider in the "Physician Name" field.
7. Enter the name of the patient in the "Patient Name" field.
8. Provide a brief description of the procedure in the "Procedure Description" field.
9. Enter the name of the facility where the procedure will be performed in the "Facility" field.
10. Enter the title of the physician or healthcare provider in the "Physician Title" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Procedure Name** (`procedure_name`, text, required): A brief description of the procedure you are consenting to.
* **Risks** (`risks`, text, optional): Potential risks or complications associated with the procedure.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, text, optional): Potential benefits or advantages of the procedure.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): The date you sign the form, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Your signature, indicating your consent to the procedure.
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, required): The name of the physician or healthcare provider.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): The name of the patient consenting to the procedure.
* **Procedure Description** (`procedure_description`, text, optional): A brief description of the procedure.
* **Facility** (`facility`, text, optional): The name of the facility where the procedure will be performed.
* **Physician Title** (`physician_title`, text, optional): The title of the physician or healthcare provider.

## Tips
* Be sure to review and sign the form carefully before completing it.
* Ensure that you understand the risks and benefits associated with the procedure.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, ask your physician or healthcare provider for clarification.
* Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
* Store the original in a secure location, as per facility policies.
