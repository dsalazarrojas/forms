# Medical Record Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medical Record Release Form is used to obtain the patient's permission to access their personal medical history, medical record number, and other confidential information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Select the correct options for each field as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other identifying information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, select_multiple, optional): Check "Yes" if the patient has a medical history that may impact their current treatment or care.
* **Medical Record Access** (`medical_record_access`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if you want to access the patient's medical records.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, note, optional): The patient's signature is required for the form to be valid. Please sign the form with your full name as printed above.
* **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, note, optional): This section is for the doctor to sign the form to verify that the patient's consent is obtained.
* **Medical Organization** (`medical_organization`, text, optional): Enter the name and address of the medical organization or hospital where the patient's record will be accessed.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date the form is signed.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time the form is signed.
* **Patient Contact** (`patient_contact`, select_multiple, optional): Select how the patient would like to be contacted if there is an issue with their records (Primary, Secondary, Tertiary).
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address for billing or other official purposes.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address for contact purposes.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, number, optional): Enter the patient's medical record number for reference.
* **Medical Record Date** (`medical_record_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the patient's medical record was accessed.
* **Medical Record Time** (`medical_record_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the patient's medical record was accessed.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your doctor or healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form is for patient use only and should not be used for any other purpose.
