# Surgery Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Surgery Reservation Form is designed to collect information for the purpose of reserving a surgery appointment. This form gathers details about the patient, their surgery preferences, and contact information to ensure a smooth and successful surgery experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required: false): Fill in the patient's personal details, such as name, address, and any other relevant information.
2. **Surgery Information** (`surgery_info`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the surgery, including the type of procedure and any relevant medical history.
3. **Surgery Details** (`surgery_details`, select multiple, required: false): Select multiple options from the list of available options, including:
	* **Surgeon**: Choose the surgeon you want to perform the surgery.
	* **Date**: Select the preferred date for the surgery.
	* **Time**: Select the preferred time for the surgery.
	* **Location**: Choose the location for the surgery.
4. **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select one, required: false): Choose how the patient would like to be contacted:
	* **Phone**: Provide a phone number to contact the patient.
	* **Email**: Provide an email address to contact the patient.
	* **Message**: Leave a personal message for the healthcare provider.
5. **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Fill in the date of the surgery.
6. **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Fill in the time of the surgery.
7. **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the surgery.
8. **Surgeon** (`surgeon`, select one, required: false): Choose the surgeon who will perform the surgery.
9. **Message** (`message`, text, required: false): Leave any additional comments or notes for the healthcare provider.
10. **Phone** (`phone`, select one, required: false): Choose the preferred method of contact:
	* **Home**: Provide a home phone number.
	* **Cell**: Provide a cell phone number.
	* **Work**: Provide a work phone number.

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any of the options, please consult with the healthcare provider.
* This form is for the purpose of reserving a surgery appointment, so please only fill it out once for each surgery.
