# Telemedicine Consultant Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from patients or clients about their experiences with telemedicine consultants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Select the overall rating that best describes your experience with the telemedicine consultant (if applicable).
3. Provide any additional feedback you may have about your experience (if applicable).
4. Choose the telemedicine consultant(s) you are evaluating (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required: false):
Select the overall rating that best describes your experience with the telemedicine consultant. This field has three options: High, Medium, and Low.

### 2. **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required: false):
Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with the telemedicine consultant. This is an optional field, but your feedback is valuable for us to improve our services.

### 3. **Telemedicine Consultant(s)** (`telemedicine_consultant`, select_multiple, required: false):
Select the telemedicine consultant(s) you are evaluating. If you are evaluating multiple consultants, you can select more than one option. The available options are Dr. Smith, Dr. Johnson, and Dr. Doe.

### 4. **Form** (`form`, text, required: false):
This is an empty field for any additional comments or information about your experience. While not required, it's a good place to provide more details if you need to.
