# Coronavirus_screening_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information and screen them for potential exposure to COVID-19. It is essential to provide healthcare professionals with the necessary data to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Patient Information**: Fill out the "Patient Information" section with your name, contact details, and any other relevant personal info.
2. **Describe Your Symptoms**: In the "Symptoms" section, describe any respiratory, cardiac, or other symptoms you may be experiencing.
3. **Share Your Contact Tracing**: In the "Contact Tracing" section, provide details about people you may have come into contact with who have shown COVID-19 symptoms.
4. **Disclose Your Travel History**: In the "Travel History" section, list any recent travel destinations or exposure to potential COVID-19 hotspots.
5. **Exposure and Test Results**: Choose all that apply from the "Exposure" and "Test Results" sections to indicate your potential exposure risk and any test results you have obtained.
6. **Medical History**: In the "Medical History" section, describe any pre-existing medical conditions that may be relevant to your COVID-19 risk assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (Required)**: This field is mandatory and collects your personal details to ensure we can contact you for follow-up care.
* **Symptoms**: Describe any symptoms you are experiencing, such as cough, fever, shortness of breath, or others.
* **Contact Tracing**: Provide details about people you've interacted with who have shown COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Travel History**: List any recent travel destinations or exposure to potential COVID-19 hotspots.
* **Exposure (Optional)**: If you've been exposed to someone with COVID-19 or recently traveled to an affected area, select the relevant option.
* **Test Results (Optional)**: If you've undergone a COVID-19 test, select "Positive" or "Negative" depending on your test outcome.
* **Medical History (Optional)**: Describe any pre-existing medical conditions that may impact your COVID-19 risk assessment.

## Tips
* If you are experiencing severe symptoms, seek immediate medical attention.
* If you have any concerns about your COVID-19 risk, contact your healthcare provider.
* This form is for informational purposes only and should not replace professional medical advice.
