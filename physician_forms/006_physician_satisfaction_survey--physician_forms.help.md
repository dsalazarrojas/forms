# Physician Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects feedback on the level of satisfaction of physicians with their job and work environment. It is designed to understand their perceptions and experiences in a variety of areas and help identify potential areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you will be asked about your level of satisfaction with various aspects of your job and work environment. Please select the option that best describes your level of satisfaction for each question. You can answer each question individually, and you don't have to complete all sections if you don't want to.
