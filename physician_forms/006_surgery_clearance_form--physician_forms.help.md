# Surgery Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Surgery Clearance Form is designed to collect information related to a patient's surgery, including their details and the surgeon's and anesthesist's information. This form is used to ensure that all necessary information is collected and recorded for administrative and clinical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth (DOB).
3. Enter the surgeon's name.
4. Select the surgeon's specialties (e.g., general surgery, cardiothoracic surgery).
5. Enter the anesthesist's name.
6. Select the anesthesist's specialties (e.g., general anesthesia, epidural anesthesia).
7. Select the type of surgery (e.g., elective, emergency).
8. Enter the surgery date.
9. Enter the surgery time.
10. Enter the name of the member of the surgical team (e.g., nurse, doctor).
11. Enter the status of the surgery (e.g., completed, cancelled).
12. Enter the patient's phone number.
13. Enter the patient's email address.
14. Enter the patient's address.
15. Enter the hospital name.
16. Enter the department's name.
17. Enter the room number (if applicable).
18. Enter the surgeon's phone number.
19. Enter the surgeon's email address.
20. Enter the surgeon's address.
21. Enter the phone number of the member of the surgical team.
22. Enter the email address of the member of the surgical team.
23. Enter the address of the member of the surgical team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient DOB** (`patient_dob`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Surgeon Name** (`surgeon_name`, text, required): Enter the surgeon's name.
* **Surgeon Specialties** (`surgeon_specialties`, select_multiple, required): Select the surgeon's specialties (e.g., general surgery, cardiothoracic surgery).
* **Anesthesist Name** (`anesthesist_name`, text, required): Enter the anesthesist's name.
* **Anesthesist Specialties** (`anesthesist_specialties`, select_multiple, required): Select the anesthesist's specialties (e.g., general anesthesia, epidural anesthesia).
* **Surgery Type** (`surgery_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of surgery (e.g., elective, emergency).
* **Surgery Date** (`surgery_date`, date, required): Enter the surgery date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Surgery Time** (`surgery_time`, time, required): Enter the surgery time in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
* **Surgical Team Member** (`surgical_team_member`, text, required): Enter the name of the member of the surgical team.
* **Surgical Status** (`surgical_status`, text, required): Enter the status of the surgery (e.g., completed, cancelled).
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required): Enter the patient's address.
* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the hospital.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the name of the department.
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, text, required): Enter the room number (if applicable).
* **Surgeon Phone** (`surgeon_phone`, text, required): Enter the surgeon's phone number.
* **Surgeon Email** (`surgeon_email`, email, required): Enter the surgeon's email address.
* **Surgeon Address** (`surgeon_address`, text, required): Enter the surgeon's address.
* **Surgical Team Member Phone** (`surgical_teammember_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the member of the surgical team.
* **Surgical Team Member Email** (`surgical_teammember_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the member of the surgical team.
* **Surgical Team Member Address** (`surgical_teammember_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the member of the surgical team.
