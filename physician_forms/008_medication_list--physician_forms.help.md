# Medication List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medication List form is designed to collect information about a patient's current medications, including name, dosages, start and end dates, and contact information. This information will help healthcare professionals make informed decisions about patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please ensure that you have reviewed and understand the patient's medical history before completing this form.
- If this is a follow-up visit, please refer back to previous medication lists for accuracy.
- For new patients, please complete all required fields and answer truthfully to ensure accurate records.
- Select 'No' for the 'Medications' and 'Dosages' fields if the patient is not currently taking any medications.
- Enter 'YYYY-MM-DD' format for dates.
- If the patient's contact information has changed, please update it accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (text, optional): Enter the patient's name.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in 'YYYY-MM-DD' format.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Medications** (select multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate that the patient is currently taking medications.
* **Dosages** (select multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if the patient has dosages for their medications, otherwise 'No'.
* **Start Date** (date, optional): Enter the start date of the patient's medications in 'YYYY-MM-DD' format.
* **End Date** (date, optional): Enter the end date of the patient's medications in 'YYYY-MM-DD' format.
* **Contact Info** (text, optional): Enter the patient's contact information.
* **Note** (note, optional): Add any relevant notes about the patient's medication list.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you answer all fields truthfully and accurately to maintain accurate patient records.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the primary care physician for guidance.
- This form is intended for follow-up visits, but can also be used for new patients.
