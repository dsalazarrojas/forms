# Clinical Trial Informed Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to inform participants about a clinical trial and have them provide their consent to participate. It is an essential document that outlines the study's purpose, procedures, risks, benefits, and other important information that the participant should understand before deciding to participate.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand all the information provided.
2. Answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Sign and date the form to indicate your consent to participate.
4. If you need more information or clarification on any section, do not hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Clinical Trial Informed Consent Form** (`consent_header`, `note`, not required): This is the heading of the form, which provides a brief overview of the purpose of the form.
* **Study Title** (`study_title`, `text`, required): The title of the study being conducted.
* **Study ID or Protocol Number** (`study_id`, `text`, required): A unique identifier for the study.
* **Principal Investigator** (`principal_investigator`, `text`, required): The name of the person responsible for conducting the study.
* **Institution or Facility** (`institution`, `text`, required): The name of the institution or facility where the study is being conducted.
* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Your full name as a participant.
* **Participant ID or MRN** (`participant_id`, `text`, not required): A unique identifier for you as a participant (if applicable).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Your date of birth.
* **I Understand the Purpose of This Study** (`study_purpose`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you understand the purpose of the study.
* **I Understand the Procedures Involved** (`study_procedures`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you understand the procedures involved in the study.
* **I Understand the Expected Duration of Participation** (`study_duration`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you understand the expected duration of participation.
* **I Understand the Potential Risks** (`risks_understood`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you understand the potential risks involved.
* **I Understand the Potential Benefits** (`benefits_understood`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you understand the potential benefits of participating.
* **I Understand There Is No Guarantee of Personal Benefit** (`no_guarantee_of_benefit`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you understand that there is no guarantee of personal benefit.
* **Alternative Treatments Have Been Explained** (`alternatives_explained`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether alternative treatments have been explained to you.
* **I Understand How My Information Will Be Protected** (`confidentiality`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you understand how your information will be protected.
* **I Understand Compensation for Injury Policies** (`compensation_for_injury`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you understand compensation for injury policies.
* **I Understand Participation Is Voluntary** (`voluntary_participation`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you understand that participation is voluntary.
* **I Understand I Can Withdraw at Any Time** (`right_to_withdraw`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you understand that you can withdraw at any time.
* **I Understand Any Costs or Payments** (`costs_and_payments`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you understand any costs or payments involved.
* **I Have Contact Information for Questions** (`contact_information`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you have contact information for questions.
* **Have All Your Questions Been Answered** (`questions_answered`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether all your questions have been answered.
* **Please List Remaining Questions** (`remaining_questions`, `text`, not required): If you have any remaining questions, list them here.
* **Have You Received a Copy of This Form** (`copy_of_form`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you have received a copy of this form.
* **Is This Form in a Language You Understand** (`language_understanding`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether this form is in a language you understand.
* **Interpreter Name** (`interpreter_name`, `text`, not required): If you require an interpreter, provide their name (if applicable).
* **Your Decision to Participate** (`participant_decision`, `select_one`, required): Answer whether you agree to participate or not.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name as the participant.
* **Date of Signature** (`participant_signature_date`, `date`, required): Date of your signature.
* **Legally Authorized Representative Name** (`legally_authorized_representative`, `text`, not required): If you are a legally authorized representative, provide their name (if applicable).
* **LAR Signature** (`lar_signature`, `text`, not required): If you are a LAR, provide their signature (if applicable).
* **Date** (`lar_signature_date`, `date`, not required): Date of LAR's signature (if applicable).
* **Investigator Name** (`investigator_name`, `text`, required): The name of the investigator in charge of the study.
* **Investigator Signature** (`investigator_signature`, `text`, required): The investigator's signature.
* **Date** (`investigator_signature_date`, `text`, required): The date of the investigator's signature.

Note: This guide is intended to assist users in filling out the Clinical Trial Informed Consent Form. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask the investigator or the institution conducting the study.
