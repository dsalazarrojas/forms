# Sleep Disorder Diagnosis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help physicians gather and document information about a patient's sleep habits, medical history, and sleep environment to aid in the diagnosis and treatment of sleep disorders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Gather the necessary information about the patient's general information, sleep habits, medical history, and sleep environment.
2. Enter the information into the corresponding fields on the form.
3. Add any additional comments or notes in the Physician Comments section, if necessary.
4. Review and finalize your submission before saving the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for entering the patient's basic information, such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Sleep Habits** (`sleep_habits`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for gathering information about the patient's sleep patterns, including the number of hours slept, sleep schedule, and any sleep disorders or issues.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for documenting the patient's medical history, including any previous diagnoses, treatments, or allergies.
* **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for assessing the patient's sleep quality, including difficulty falling asleep, staying asleep, or waking up refreshed.
* **Sleep Environment** (`sleep_environment`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for describing the patient's sleep environment, including sleep disorders or issues related to the bedroom or sleep environment.
* **Sleep Diagnosis** (`sleep_diagnosis`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for documenting the patient's sleep diagnosis or suspected diagnosis.
* **Physician Comments** (`sleep_disorder_diagnosis_form_physician_comments`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for adding any additional comments or notes from the physician.
* **Additional Comments** (`sleep_disorder_diagnosis_form_physician_comments_2`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for adding any additional comments or notes from the physician.
* **Physician Comments 2** (`sleep_disorder_diagnosis_form_physician_comments_3`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for adding any additional comments or notes from the physician.
* **Additional Comments 2** (`sleep_disorder_diagnosis_form_physician_comments_4`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for adding any additional comments or notes from the physician.
* ...

Please note that the fields labeled "Additional Comments 1-12" are for adding any additional information or comments from the physician, and can be filled in as needed.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields before saving the form.
* Use clear and concise language when entering information into the form.
* Review and finalize your submission before saving to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your healthcare professional for guidance.
