## Disability Assessment Form - Help Guide

### Purpose

This form is designed to help assess a patient's disability or condition, gathering information about their specific needs and circumstances. It is intended for use by healthcare professionals to aid in the evaluation and treatment of patients with disabilities.

### How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter the patient's name**: Please provide the patient's full name in the text field.
2. **Describe the disability or condition**: Type a clear and concise description of the patient's disability or condition in the note field.
3. **Assess the impact**: Provide a detailed description of how the disability or condition affects the patient in the note field.
4. **Provide patient contact information**: Enter the patient's contact information, including any relevant details such as phone number, email, or address. This information is required to ensure effective communication and follow-up.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name.
* **disability_condition** (`disability_condition`, note, required/optional): Describe the disability or condition being assessed.
* **impact_of_disability** (`impact_of_disability`, note, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of how the disability or condition affects the patient.
* **patient_contact_info** (`patient_contact_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's contact information, including any relevant details such as phone number, email, or address.
