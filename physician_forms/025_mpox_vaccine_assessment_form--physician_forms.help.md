# Mpox Vaccine Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your Mpox vaccine status and medical condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Date of Birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Provide a valid Contact Number where you can be reached.
4. Enter your Email Address where you can be contacted.
5. Enter your Address.
6. For Medical Condition, select any medical conditions you are currently experiencing.
7. Choose your current Mpox vaccine status from the available options (Active or Inactive).
8. If you like, you can provide a Reason for Assessment, otherwise you can leave this field blank.
9. Use the Additional Comments field to provide any extra information that may be helpful for your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Number** (text, required): Enter a valid contact number where we can reach you.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter your address where we can contact you.
* **Medical Condition** (select_multiple, required): Check any medical conditions you are currently experiencing.
*   *Yes*
*   *No*
* **Vaccine Status** (select_one, required): Choose your current Mpox vaccine status:
	+ *Active*: You are currently vaccinated against Mpox.
	+ *Inactive*: You are not currently vaccinated against Mpox.
* **Reason for Assessment** (text, optional): If you like, you can provide a reason for your assessment, otherwise you can leave this field blank.
* **Additional Comments** (text, optional): Use this field to provide any extra information that may be helpful for your assessment.
