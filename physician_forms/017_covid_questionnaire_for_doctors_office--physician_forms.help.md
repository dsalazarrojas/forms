# Covid Questionnaire For Doctors Office - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients in a doctor's office. It gathers details such as contact information, medical conditions, travel history, and symptoms. The form also includes sections for doctor and patient notes, as well as agreements and consents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin with the "Intro Section", where you can provide any initial comments or questions.
2. Fill in your **Name** in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your **Contact Information**, including phone number and email address in the "Contact Info" and "Phone Number" and "Email" fields respectively.
4. Describe your **Medical Conditions** in the "Medical Conditions" field.
5. Provide a brief account of your **Travel History** in the "Travel History" field.
6. List your **Symptoms** in the "Symptoms" field.
7. If applicable, select your **Vaccination Status**: Fully vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, or Not vaccinated.
8. Add any **Doctor Notes** in the "Doctor Notes" field.
9. Provide any additional **Patient Notes** in the "Patient Notes" field.
10. Finally, confirm your **Patient Agreement** and **Patient Consent** by selecting the corresponding options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (text, required: false)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Info (text, required: false)**: Enter your contact information, including your phone number.
* **Phone Number (text, required: false)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email (email, required: false)**: Enter your email address.
* **Medical Conditions (text, required: false)**: Describe any medical conditions you may have.
* **Travel History (text, required: false)**: Provide a brief account of your travel history.
* **Symptoms (text, required: false)**: List your symptoms.
* **Vaccination Status (select_multiple, required: false)**: Select your current vaccination status: Fully vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, or Not vaccinated.
* **Doctor Notes (note, required: false)**: Add any additional comments or notes for your doctor.
* **Patient Notes (note, required: false)**: Provide any further comments or notes for your patient.
* **Patient Agreement (select_one, required: false)**: Confirm your agreement to the patient consent form.
* **Patient Consent (select_one, required: false)**: Confirm your consent to the terms of this form.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields, but note that some fields are not required.
* Ensure you accurately describe your medical conditions and symptoms.
* Select your correct vaccination status from the options provided.
* Confirm your agreement and consent to the terms of this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider.
