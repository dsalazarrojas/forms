# Patient Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their satisfaction with the care provided by their physician. The goal is to gather information to improve the quality of service and patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Answer the questions on each page**. The form has multiple pages, each with its own set of questions.
2. **Rate your satisfaction**. On page 3, you will be asked to rate your satisfaction level. Choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest satisfaction level.
3. **Provide your comments**. On page 4, you will have the opportunity to share any additional comments or thoughts about your visit with your physician.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Physician Name**: Choose the physician you saw during your visit.
	+ Select your physician from the list provided.
* **Rating**: Rate your satisfaction level with the care provided.
	+ Choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest satisfaction level.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or thoughts about your visit.
	+ Be as detailed or brief as you like, but keep in mind that your responses will help us improve our service.
* **(Blank Page 1)**: This page is meant for gathering feedback about your satisfaction with your care. Please let us know if you have any comments or concerns.
	+ You can type your answer on the blank page and we will take it into account when reviewing your feedback.
* **(Blank Physician Name)**: Select your physician from the list.
	+ We have listed the names of our available physicians for your convenience.
* **(Blank Comments)**: You can add any additional comments or thoughts about your visit.
	+ Your feedback is valuable to us, and we will use it to improve our service.
* **(Blank Rating)**: Rate your satisfaction level with the care provided.
	+ Choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest satisfaction level.
