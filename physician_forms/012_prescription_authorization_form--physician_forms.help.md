# Prescription Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prescription Authorization Form is used to collect information from prescribers and authorized individuals to authorize prescriptions, ensuring that necessary details are captured accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
2. Ensure that you have the necessary authority to complete this form.
3. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
4. Enter the prescriber's name in the "Prescriber Name" field.
5. Enter the prescriber's title in the "Prescriber Title" field.
6. Enter the prescriber's phone number in the "Prescriber's Phone Number" field.
7. Enter the prescriber's email in the "Prescriber's Email" field.
8. Enter the prescriber's date in the "Prescriber's Date" field.
9. Select the pickup or refill option in the "Pickup or Refill" field.
10. Enter the pickup date and time in the "Pickup By" field.
11. Obtain the prescriber's signature in the "Prescriber's Signature" field.
12. Enter the authorized individual's name in the "Authorized Individual's Name" field.
13. Enter the authorized individual's title in the "Authorized Individual's Title" field (if applicable).
14. Enter the authorized individual's phone number in the "Authorized Individual's Phone Number" field.
15. Enter the authorized individual's email in the "Authorized Individual's Email" field.
16. Enter the authorized individual's date in the "Authorized Individual's Date" field.
17. Obtain the authorized individual's signature in the "Authorized Individual's Signature" field.
18. Enter the authorized individual's ID number (if applicable).
19. Enter the authorized individual's relationship to the patient in the "Authorized Individual's Relationship to Patient" field (if applicable).
20. Obtain the patient's signature in the "Patient's Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, \*): Enter the patient's name.
* **Prescriber Name** (`prescriber_name`, text, \*): Enter the prescriber's name.
* **Prescriber Title** (`prescriber_title`, text, ): Enter the prescriber's title.
* **Prescriber's Phone Number** (`prescriber_phone_number`, text, \*): Enter the prescriber's phone number.
* **Prescriber's Email** (`prescriber_email`, email, \*): Enter the prescriber's email address.
* **Prescriber's Date** (`prescriber_date`, date, \*): Enter the prescriber's date.
* **Pickup or Refill** (`pickup_or_refill`, select_one, \*): Select the pickup or refill option.
* **Pickup By** (`pickup_by_date`, time, \*): Enter the pickup date and time.
* **Prescriber's Signature** (`prescriber_signature`, note, \*): Obtain the prescriber's signature.
* **Authorized Individual's Name** (`authorized_individual_name`, text, \*): Enter the authorized individual's name.
* **Authorized Individual's Title** (`authorized_individual_title`, text, ): Enter the authorized individual's title (if applicable).
* **Authorized Individual's Phone Number** (`authorized_individual_phone_number`, text, \*): Enter the authorized individual's phone number.
* **Authorized Individual's Email** (`authorized_individual_email`, email, \*): Enter the authorized individual's email address.
* **Authorized Individual's Date** (`authorized_individual_date`, date, \*): Enter the authorized individual's date.
* **Authorized Individual's Signature** (`authorized_individual_signature`, note, \*): Obtain the authorized individual's signature.
* **Authorized Individual's ID Number** (`authorized_individual_id_number`, text, ): Enter the authorized individual's ID number (if applicable).
* **Authorized Individual's Relationship to Patient** (`authorized_individual_relationship`, text, ): Enter the authorized individual's relationship to the patient (if applicable).
* **Patient's Signature** (`patient_signature`, note, \*): Obtain the patient's signature.
* **Prescriber's Issuing Facility** (`prescriber_issuing_facility`, text, ): Enter the prescriber's issuing facility (if applicable).
* **Prescriber's NPI** (`prescriber_npi`, text, ): Enter the prescriber's NPI (if applicable).
* **Prescriber's License Number** (`prescriber_license_number`, text, ): Enter the prescriber's license number (if applicable).
* **Prescriber's Facility License Number** (`prescriber_facility_license_number`, text, ): Enter the prescriber's facility license number (if applicable).
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, ): Enter the facility name (if applicable).
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, ): Enter the facility address (if applicable).

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please contact the facility administration or your healthcare provider.
