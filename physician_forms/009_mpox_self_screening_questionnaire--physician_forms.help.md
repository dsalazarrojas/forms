## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Medical Facility

* **Medical Facility**: This is the location where you received medical attention or visited for any health issues. Please enter the name or identifier of the medical facility.

### Patient Name

* **Patient Name**: This is your name, which will be used to identify you in our records.

### Medical History

* **Medical History**: Briefly describe any pre-existing medical conditions or allergies you have.

### Symptoms

* **Symptoms**: Describe any symptoms you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.

### Contact Details

* **Contact Details**: Enter any contact information, such as phone number or email, where we can reach you if needed.

### Medical Staff

* **Medical Staff**: Identify any medical professionals who have treated or interacted with you during this episode.

### Travel History

* **Travel History**: List any recent travel destinations or locations where you have been present.

### Exposure Risk

* **Exposure Risk**: Select the level of risk you believe you have been exposed to:
	+ Exposure Low
	+ Exposure Moderate
	+ Exposure High

### Travel Destination

* **Travel Destination**: Enter the specific location you have traveled to or are currently located.

### Exposure Type

* **Exposure Type**: Describe how you believe you were exposed to the virus.

### Contact Frequency

* **Contact Frequency**: Describe the frequency of your contacts with others.

### Travel Dates

* **Travel Dates**: Enter the dates you traveled to or are currently located.

### Date of Last Contact

* **Date of Last Contact**: Mark the date of the last contact you had with anyone who may have been infected.

### Travel Duration

* **Travel Duration**: Enter the length of time you were away from home.

### Number of Contacts
