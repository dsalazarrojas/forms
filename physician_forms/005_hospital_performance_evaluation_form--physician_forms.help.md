# Hospital Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on the performance of the hospital. It includes questions about staff performance, patient care, and other relevant metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the evaluation option for the first question "Performance Evaluation Form".
2. Write a brief description of the form in the "Form Description" field.
3. Select the relevant metrics for staff performance in the "Staff Performance Metrics" field.
4. Enter any numerical values for patient care metrics in the "Patient Care Metrics" field.
5. Choose a time for the "Time of Visit" field.
6. Enter a date for the "Date of Visit" field.
7. Leave any feedback for your staff in the "Staff Feedback" field.
8. Add any submission notes in the "Form Submission" field.
9. Enter the email address of your staff member for the "Email Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance Evaluation Form** (`select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select one of the available evaluation options to describe the performance of the hospital.
* **Form Description** (`text`, required: false):
	+ Please provide a brief description of this form, explaining what it's used for and your experience with it.
* **Staff Performance Metrics** (`select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Choose multiple metrics that best describe the performance of your staff.
* **Patient Care Metrics** (`number`, required: false):
	+ Enter numerical values for patient care metrics, such as average wait times or quality of care scores.
* **Time of Visit** (`time`, required: false):
	+ Choose a specific time for the visit, using the format "HH:MM AM/PM" (e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **Date of Visit** (`date`, required: false):
	+ Enter the date of the visit, using the format "MM/DD/YYYY" (e.g., 12/31/2022).
* **Staff Feedback** (`text`, required: false):
	+ Leave feedback for your staff, such as suggestions or areas of improvement.
* **Form Submission** (`note`, required: false):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about your submission.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required: false):
	+ Enter the email address of your staff member, so we can follow up with them about their performance.
