# Release Of Medical Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from patients or their representatives regarding the release of their sensitive medical data. It may be used for administrative or regulatory purposes, such as HIPAA compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary access or permissions to submit this form.
2. If you are completing the form on behalf of someone else, ensure you have their authority to do so.
3. Fill out the form with the required information.
4. Submit the form to the designated recipient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Owner Name** (owner_name, text, optional)
The owner's name is the name of the individual whose medical information is being released. Enter the full name of the owner.

### **Owner Address** (owner_address, text, optional)
The owner's address is the address of the individual whose medical information is being released. Enter the address in the format you would use for mail delivery.

### **Medical Record Number** (medical_record_number, text, optional)
The medical record number is a unique identifier assigned to the owner's medical records. If you don't know the medical record number, you can leave this field blank.

### **Purpose** (purpose, text, optional)
The purpose of the release of medical information is described here. Enter a brief description of why the owner's medical information is being released.
