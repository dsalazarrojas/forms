# Hiv Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from patients to perform an HIV test. This is a legal requirement to ensure that the patient is aware and agrees to the test.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the patient's information on page 1.
2.  Enter the testing information on page 2.
3.  Answer the consent question on page 3.
4.  Enter any testing results on page 4.
5.  Record the patient's testing history on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name, address, and any other relevant information that will help with the test.
- **Testing Information** (`testing_information`, text, required/optional): Enter the date and details of the test to be performed.
- **Do You Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that the patient consents to the test, or "No" if they do not.
- **Testing Results** (`testing_results`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant results of the test.
- **Testing History** (`testing_history`, text, required/optional): Enter any previous test results or relevant medical history.
