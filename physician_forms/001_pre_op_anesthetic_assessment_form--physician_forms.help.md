# pre-op-anesthetic-assessment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pre-op-anesthetic-assessment-form is a patient-facing form used to provide essential information to the anesthetist and surgical team before undergoing anesthesia and surgery. This form helps ensure a safe and successful surgical experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review each field and provide accurate information about your health, medical history, and surgical procedures.
2. Answer each question in detail, including any allergies, medications, or previous medical conditions.
3. Provide as much information as possible about your upcoming surgery, including any potential complications or concerns.
4. Be sure to review and verify the information provided before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: Please enter your name, date of birth, and other relevant patient information.
* **Anesthesiologist Assessment**: Describe your medical history, previous surgeries, and any concerns about your upcoming anesthesia.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you have, including medications, foods, or substances that can cause an allergic reaction.
* **Medical History**: Provide a detailed account of your medical history, including any chronic conditions, diseases, or previous surgeries.
* **Anesthesia History**: Share your previous experience with anesthesia, including any complications or concerns.
* **Medications**: List all your current medications, including prescription and over-the-counter medications.
* **Surgical History**: Describe your previous surgical experiences, including any complications or concerns.
* **Vital Signs**: Enter your current vital signs, such as blood pressure, pulse, and body temperature.
* **Surgical Parameters**: Provide information about your upcoming surgery, including the procedure, location, and estimated duration.
* **Intraop Medications**: List any medications you will be receiving during surgery.
* **Postop Care**: Describe your post-operative care requirements, including any special instructions or concerns.
* **Patient Comments**: Share any comments or concerns you may have about your upcoming surgery.
* **Anesthetic Plan**: Discuss your anesthetic plan with your anesthetist, including any specific requirements or concerns.
* **Anesthesia Team Assessment**: Describe your interaction with the anesthesia team, including any concerns or questions you have.
* **Surgical Team Assessment**: Discuss your assessment of the surgical team, including their experience and qualifications.
* **Patient Authorization**: Verify your authorization for the form submission.
* **Surgical Procedure 1**: Enter information about your first surgical procedure.
* **Surgical Procedure 2**: Enter information about your second surgical procedure.
* **Surgical Procedure 3**: Enter information about your third surgical procedure.
* **Surgical Procedure 4**: Enter information about your fourth surgical procedure.
* **Surgical Team**: List the names and qualifications of your surgical team.
* **Anesthetists**: List the names and qualifications of your anesthetist.
* **Surgeons**: List the names and qualifications of your surgeon.
* **Nurses**: List the names and qualifications of your nurses.
* **Other Personnel**: List any other medical personnel involved in your care.
* **Anesthetists**: ( Duplicate field, please ignore)
