# Health Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive information about a patient's health survey, including medical history, treatments, and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each section and what information is required.
2. Enter your patient's information in the "Patient Info" section.
3. Select the relevant medical history, tests, and procedures that apply to the patient.
4. Enter any necessary comments or observations in the "Patient Comments" and "Doctor Comments" sections.
5. Review and submit the form for review by the healthcare team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (1, text, required): Enter your patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant identifying information.
* **Medical History** (2, select_multiple, required): Select all the medical conditions that the patient has been diagnosed with.
* **Symptoms** (3, text, optional): Describe the patient's symptoms, if any.
* **Medical Tests** (4, select_one, required): Select the medical test that was performed on the patient.
* **Medication** (5, text, optional): Enter any medication the patient is taking.
* **Allergies** (6, select_multiple, optional): Select any allergies the patient has.
* **Doctor Info** (7, text, optional): Enter the patient's doctor's name and contact information.
* **Medical Conditions** (8, select_multiple, optional): Select all the medical conditions the patient has.
* **Medical Specialties** (9, select_multiple, optional): Select any medical specialties relevant to the patient's care.
* **Medical Procedures** (10, select_multiple, optional): Select all the medical procedures the patient has undergone.
* **Medical Treatments** (11, select_multiple, optional): Select all the medical treatments the patient has received.
* **Medical History 2** (12, select_multiple, required): Select all the medical conditions that have been diagnosed in the past 2 years.
* **Medical Procedure 2** (13, select_multiple, optional): Select all the medical procedures the patient has undergone in the past 2 years.
* **Patient Comments** (14, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or concerns from the patient.
* **Doctor Comments** (15, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations from the healthcare team.
* **Medical Specialty 2** (16, select_multiple, optional): Select any medical specialties relevant to the patient's care.
* **Medical Test 2** (17, select_multiple, optional): Select all the medical tests that have been performed on the patient.
* **Medication 2** (18, text, optional): Enter any medication the patient is taking.
* **Medical Procedure 3** (19, select_multiple, optional): Select all the medical procedures the patient has undergone.
* **Medical Condition 2** (20, select_multiple, true): Select all the medical conditions the patient has been diagnosed with.
* **Medical Treatment 2** (21, select_multiple, optional): Select all the medical treatments the patient has received.
* **Medical Test 3** (22, select_multiple, optional): Select all the medical tests that have been performed on the patient.
* **Medical Condition 3** (23, select_multiple, optional): Select all the medical conditions the patient has been diagnosed with.
* **Medical Procedure 4** (24, select_multiple, optional): Select all the medical procedures the patient has undergone.
