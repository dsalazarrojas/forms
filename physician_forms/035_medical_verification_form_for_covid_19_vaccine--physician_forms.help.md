# Medical Verification Form For COVID 19 Vaccine - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medical verification information for COVID-19 vaccination purposes. It includes fields for patient demographics, medical history, vaccination status, medical provider information, and medical verification status.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:
- Fill in the patient demographic fields (patient age, gender, race, and ethnicity) as accurately as possible.
- Enter your medical provider information (name, license number, specialty, and signature).
- Provide any additional comments or notes about the patient's medical condition.
- Check the medical verification status field after submitting the form.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Enter the patient's information, including their name, address, and contact number.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical history or conditions for the patient.
* **Patient Vaccinations** (`patient_vaccinations`, text, optional): Enter any previous vaccinations or medical procedures the patient has undergone.
* **Medical Verification** (`medical_verification`, text, optional): Enter any medical verification or certification information for the patient.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, text, optional): Enter your medical provider information, including your name and title.
* **Medical Provider Details** (`medical_provider_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about your medical provider credentials.
* **Patient Consent** (`patient_consent`, text, optional): Obtain patient consent for the form and its purpose.
* **Medical Certification** (`medical_certification`, text, optional): Enter any medical certification or license number for your medical provider.
* **Medical Verification Status** (`medical_verification_status`, text, optional): Check the medical verification status after submitting the form.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the patient's medical condition.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, number, optional): Enter the patient's age as accurately as possible.
* **Patient Gender** (`patient_gender`, select_one, optional): Select the patient's gender from the provided options.
* **Patient Race** (`patient_race`, select_multiple, optional): Select the patient's racial or ethnic background from the provided options.
* **Patient Ethnicity** (`patient_ethnicity`, select_multiple, optional): Select the patient's ethnic background from the provided options.
* **Medical Provider License** (`medical_provider_license`, select_one, required): Select your medical provider license status from the provided options.
* **Medical Provider Address** (`medical_provider_address`, text, optional): Enter your medical provider address.
* **Medical Provider Phone** (`medical_provider_phone`, text, optional): Enter your medical provider phone number.
* **Medical Provider Email** (`medical_provider_email`, email, optional): Enter your medical provider email address.
* **Medical Provider Specialty** (`medical_provider_specialty`, select_multiple, optional): Select your medical provider specialty from the provided options.
* **Patient Medical Condition** (`patient_medical_condition`, text, optional): Enter the patient's medical condition.
* **Patient Medical Condition Note** (`patient_medical_condition_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the patient's medical condition.
* **Medical Provider Signature** (`medical_provider_signature`, text, optional): Enter your medical provider signature.
* **Medical Provider Date of Birth** (`medical_provider_dob`, date, optional): Enter your medical provider date of birth.
* **Medical Provider License Number** (`medical_provider_license_number`, text, optional): Enter your medical provider license number.
* **Medical Provider NPI** (`medical_provider_npi`, text, optional): Enter your medical provider NPI number.
