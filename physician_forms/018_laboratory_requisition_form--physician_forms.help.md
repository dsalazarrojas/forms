# Laboratory Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients for laboratory testing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please provide the following information:
	* Patient's personal details such as name, address, and contact information.
	* Medical conditions that are relevant to the laboratory tests requested.
	* Information about the laboratory tests requested, including the test frequency.
	* Results of the laboratory tests, including the test status and result date and time.
	* Notes from the doctor and laboratory technician.
2. Please ensure that all fields marked as "required" are completed accurately and thoroughly.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please consult with a healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`): This field captures the patient's personal details, including name, address, and contact information.
* **Test Request** (`test_request`): Select the laboratory tests requested from the available options.
* **Test Info** (`test_info`): Enter a brief description of the laboratory tests requested.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`): Select the medical condition that is relevant to the laboratory tests requested.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`): Enter the date when the laboratory tests were ordered.
* **Test Frequency** (`test_frequency`): Select the frequency of the laboratory tests requested.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`): Enter the patient's address.
* **Doctor Note** (`doctor_note`): Enter any additional notes from the doctor.
* **Lab Order** (`lab_order`): Enter the laboratory order details.
* **Test Result** (`test_result`): Enter the results of the laboratory tests.
* **Test Status** (`test_status`): Select the status of the laboratory tests (e.g., pending, completed, cancelled).
* **Result Date** (`result_date`): Enter the date when the laboratory results were received.
* **Result Time** (`result_time`): Enter the time when the laboratory results were received.
* **Lab Result Info** (`lab_result_info`): Enter any additional information about the laboratory results.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`): Enter the doctor's signature.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`): Enter the patient's signature.
* **Test Info** (`test_info`): Enter a brief description of the laboratory tests requested.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`): Select the status of the laboratory order (e.g., pending, completed, cancelled).
* **Result Status** (`result_status`): Select the status of the laboratory results (e.g., pending, completed, cancelled).
* **Lab Result Note** (`lab_result_note`): Enter any additional notes from the laboratory technician.
* **Lab Report** (`lab_report`): Enter the laboratory report details.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`): Enter the doctor's email address.
* **Lab Info** (`lab_info`): Enter any additional information about the laboratory.
