# Medical Student Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Student Evaluation Form is used to assess the performance and progress of medical students. This form allows professors to evaluate the medical knowledge, skills, and abilities of students, and provide recommendations on whether they are ready to move on to the next level of their medical education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Student Name in the field provided.
2. Enter the Evaluation Date if the form is not completed on the same day it is submitted.
3. Enter the Professor Name and their contact information is not required, but can be provided if desired.
4. Enter the Evaluation Score, which should be a numerical value between 0 and 100.
5. Enter Comments to describe the student's performance and progress.
6. If applicable, select the Medical School attended by the student.
7. Select Approve Next Level to indicate whether the student is ready to move on to the next level.
8. Enter Student Performance to describe the student's overall performance and progress.
9. Enter Additional Comments if needed.
10. Select Final Decision, which should be Not Recommended, Recommended, or another value not listed here.
11. Enter Student ID to track student progress.
12. Enter Professor ID to track professor progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Please enter the name of the student being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date the evaluation was completed.
*   **Professor Name**: Enter the name of the professor completing the evaluation.
*   **Evaluation Score**: Enter a numerical score between 0 and 100 to evaluate the student's performance. Please be honest and fair in your assessment.
*   **Comments**: Provide a description of the student's performance and progress. This is an opportunity to provide specific feedback on areas of strength and weakness.
*   **Medical School**: If the student has attended a medical school, enter the name of the school.
*   **Approve Next Level**: Select 'Yes' if the student is ready to move on to the next level, or 'No' if they are not yet ready.
*   **Student Performance**: Enter a description of the student's overall performance and progress.
*   **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional information that is relevant to the student's evaluation.
*   **Final Decision**: Select Not Recommended, Recommended, or another value not listed here to indicate your final decision.
*   **Student ID**: Enter the student's ID number to track their progress.
*   **Professor ID**: Enter your own ID number to track your progress.
