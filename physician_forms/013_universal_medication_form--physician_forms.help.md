# Universal Medication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's medication usage and medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide patient information in the designated fields.
2. List any allergies or medical conditions that affect your medication usage.
3. Indicate the start and end times for your current medication regimen.
4. Provide a list of medications you are currently taking, including prescription and non-prescription medications.
5. Specify the dosages for each medication.
6. Select the medical conditions that affect your medication usage.
7. Provide the name of each medication you are taking.
8. Indicate the medical provider who prescribed your medication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (patient_information)**: This section requires basic patient information such as name, contact details, etc.
* **Allergies (allergies)**: List any allergies or medical conditions that affect your medication usage.
* **Prescription Medications (prescription_medications)**: List any prescription medications you are currently taking.
* **Non-Prescription Medications (non_prescription_medications)**: List any non-prescription medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Conditions (medical_conditions)**: Select one or more medical conditions that affect your medication usage. Options include High Blood Pressure, Diabetes, Asthma, and Other.
* **Medications List (medications_list)**: Provide a list of your current medications, including both prescription and non-prescription medications.
* **Start Time (start_time)**: Indicate the start time of your current medication regimen.
* **End Time (end_time)**: Indicate the end time of your current medication regimen.
* **Dosages (dosages)**: Select the dosage frequency for each medication you are taking. Options include Daily, Every Other Day, Three Times A Day, and Other.
* **Medication Name (medication_name)**: Select the name of each medication you are taking. Options include Acetaminophen, Aspirin, and Other.
* **Medical Provider (medical_provider)**: Indicate the medical provider who prescribed your medication.

Note: The required fields are marked with a asterisk (*) and are essential for accurate completion of the form.
