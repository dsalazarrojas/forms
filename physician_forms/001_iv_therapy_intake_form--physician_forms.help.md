# Iv Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Iv Therapy Intake Form" is used to collect crucial information about patients undergoing IV therapy. This form is a critical document that guides healthcare providers in evaluating the patient's medical history, current condition, and treatment needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently:

1.  Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2.  Provide the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions in the "Medical History" field.
3.  Specify if the patient has any infectious diseases in the "Infectious Disease" field.
4.  Describe the patient's medical condition, including any current symptoms in the "Symptoms" field.
5.  List the patient's medications, if any, in the "Medications" field.
6.  Mention any allergies the patient has in the "Allergies" field.
7.  Check if the patient will be under medical team supervision in the "Medical Team" field.
8.  Enter the patient's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
9.  Provide the patient's email address in the "Email" field.
10.  Specify the patient's insurance details in the "Insurance" field.
11.  Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
12.  Enter the date and time of the visit in the "Date of Visit" and "Time of Visit" fields.
13.  Enter the patient's height and weight in the "Height/Weight" field.
14.  Describe the patient's medical record, including any laboratory tests or medical notes in the "Medical Record" field.
15.  Specify laboratory tests, if any, in the "Laboratory Tests" field.
16.  Provide any additional medical history notes in the "Medical History Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name accurately and completely.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions.
* **Infectious Disease** (`infectious_disease`, text, required): Specify if the patient has any infectious diseases.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): Describe the patient's current medical condition.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_multiple, required): Choose the symptoms the patient is experiencing, such as "Yes" for any of the listed symptoms or "No" for none.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List the patient's current medications, if any.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): Mention any allergies the patient has.
* **Medical Team** (`medical_team`, select_one, required): Check if the patient will be under medical team supervision.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Provide the patient's email address.
* **Insurance** (`insurance`, text, required): Specify the patient's insurance details.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required): Enter the date of the visit.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, required): Enter the time of the visit.
* **Height/Weight** (`height_weight`, text, required): Enter the patient's height and weight.
* **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, text, required): Describe the patient's medical record, including any laboratory tests or medical notes.
* **Laboratory Tests** (`laboratory_tests`, text, required): Specify any laboratory tests the patient has undergone.
* **Medical History Notes** (`medical_history_notes`, text, required): Provide any additional medical history notes.

## Tips
- Please complete the form carefully and accurately.
- Review each field carefully before proceeding.
- If any field is unclear or requires clarification, please ask for assistance.
- The form is a critical document in the IV therapy process; ensure it's completed thoroughly and with attention to detail.
