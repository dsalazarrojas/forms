# Mpox Vaccine Eligibility Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess whether an individual is eligible for the mpox vaccine. It will guide healthcare professionals through the necessary information to determine the vaccination status of each individual, ensuring accurate and efficient processing of patient data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your health status in the "Health Status" field. Provide a brief description of your overall health.
2. Indicate any risk factors you are experiencing or have experienced in the past in the "Risk Factors" field. You can select multiple options as needed.
3. Report your vaccination history in the "Vaccination History" field. This can include previous vaccinations you have received or are currently receiving.
4. Enter your date of birth to help us understand your age-related risk factors.
5. Provide any contact information for follow-up purposes in the "Contact Info" field.
6. If you have any medical history or conditions, please enter it in the "Medical History" field.
7. If you have any allergies, report them in the "Allergies" field.
8. List any existing medical conditions that may impact your vaccine eligibility in the "Medical Conditions" field.
9. Report any previous vaccinations you have received in the "Previous Vaccinations" field.
10. Provide a brief summary of your next steps in the process in the "Next Step" field.
11. Confirm the form completion status and submission status as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Health Status**: Describe your current health status, e.g., "Healthy", "Hospitalized", etc.
* **Risk Factors**: Select all applicable risk factors you are experiencing or have experienced in the past.
	+ High Blood Pressure
	+ Heart Disease
	+ Kidney Disease
	+ Liver Disease
	+ Cancer
	+ Weakened Immune System
* **Vaccination History**: Report your previous vaccination status, e.g., "Not Vaccinated", "Partially Vaccinated", "Fully Vaccinated", or "Unknown".
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Contact Info**: Provide any phone number, email, or address for follow-up purposes.
* **Medical History**: Report any existing medical history or conditions that may impact your vaccine eligibility.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you have, e.g., "Peanut allergy".
* **Medical Conditions**: List any existing medical conditions that may impact your vaccine eligibility.
* **Previous Vaccinations**: Report any previous vaccinations you have received.
* **Next Step**: Describe your next steps in the process, e.g., "Awaiting medical clearance".
* **Form Completion Status**: Confirm that this form is completed.
* **Submission Status**: Confirm your submission status, e.g., "Accepted", "Rejected", "Pending".
