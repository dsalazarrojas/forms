### Hearing Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide

#### Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a patient's hearing status, including medical history, current medications, hearing tests results, and recommendations for further treatment or follow-up.

#### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields according to your knowledge of the patient's condition. If you need help with any of the fields, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

#### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient information** (`patient_information`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
* **History** (`history`, `text`, required): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous hearing-related issues or conditions.
* **Medical history** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): List any relevant medical conditions or allergies that may affect the patient's hearing.
* **Current medication** (`current_medication`, `text`, required): List all the patient's current medications, including dosage and frequency.
* **Social history** (`social_history`, `text`, required): Discuss the patient's social history, including any relevant family medical history or cultural background.
* **Environmental** (`patient_environmental`, `text`, required): Describe the patient's work environment and any potential noise exposure risks.
* **Hearing tests** (`hearing_tests`, `select_one`, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the patient has undergone hearing tests.
* **Hearing tests hints** (`hearing_tests_hints`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about the hearing tests, if applicable.
* **Hearing tests results** (`hearing_tests_results`, `text`, optional): Describe the patient's hearing test results, if known.
