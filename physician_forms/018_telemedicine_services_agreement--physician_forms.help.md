# Telemedicine Services Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telemedicine Services Agreement form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of telemedicine services offered by a healthcare provider to a patient. It ensures that both the patient and the physician understand how communication will take place and what level of confidentiality can be expected.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Patient Identifier**: Enter your patient identifier, if any, which may be your Medicare/Medicaid ID, Health card number, or other identifying information. This will help the healthcare provider identify you properly.
2.  **Patient Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
3.  **Physician Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider you are working with.
4.  **Practice Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider's practice or clinic.
5.  **Agreement Date**: This is the date on which you are signing this agreement. This date will be used to track the start of your telemedicine services.
6.  **Patient Signature**: This is where you sign to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions outlined in this document.
7.  **Physician Signature**: This is where the healthcare provider signs to indicate their agreement to the terms and conditions outlined in this document.
8.  **Date of Service**: This is the date on which you will receive telemedicine services from the healthcare provider.
9.  **Services Agreed**: Select all the communication methods that you are comfortable with, such as Video Visits, Phone Consultations, Text Messaging, E-mail, Fax, or Secure Message. This will help the healthcare provider know how to communicate with you effectively.
10. **Communication Preferences**: Select all the communication methods that you are comfortable with, such as Video, Phone, Email, Text, Fax, or Secure Message. This will help the healthcare provider know how to contact you effectively.
11.  **Confidentiality Agreement**: Check the box to indicate that you understand and agree to the confidentiality terms of this contract.
12.  **Agreement Terms**: These are the terms and conditions that you and the healthcare provider agree to in this document. Please read this section carefully and sign below to indicate that you have read and understand these terms.
13.  **Agreement Terms Version**: This is the version number of this document. This is for record-keeping purposes only.
14.  **Agreement Date Version**: This is the date of this document version. This is for record-keeping purposes only.
15.  **Patient Acknowledgement**: This is where you confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of this document.
16.  **Physician Acknowledgement**: This is where the healthcare provider confirms that they have read and understood the terms and conditions of this document.
