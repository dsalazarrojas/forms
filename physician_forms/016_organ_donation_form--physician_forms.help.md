# Organ Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organ Donation Form is designed to collect information from potential organ donors, enabling medical professionals to assess their suitability and ensure that their wishes are respected during the organ donation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your Donor Information, including your name and any relevant contact details.
2.  Describe your Medical History, highlighting any existing medical conditions or allergies.
3.  Specify your Organ Donor Preferences, choosing the organs you are willing to donate or receive.
4.  Enter your Emergency Contact Information, including their name and contact details.
5.  Outline any relevant Medical Conditions that may impact your eligibility as a donor.
6.  Provide your consent for the organ donation process.
7.  Include any Additional Information that may be relevant to your donor status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Donor Information (Donor Information)** (`text`, required): Enter your name and any additional details relevant to your identity.
    *   **Contact Information (Contact Information)** (`text`, required): Provide your contact details, including phone numbers and email.
    *   **Medical History (Medical History)** (`text`, required): Describe your existing medical conditions or allergies.
    *   **Organ Donor Preferences (Organ Donation Preferences)** (`select_one`, required): Select the organs you are willing to donate or receive.
        +   **Kidney**
        +   **Liver**
        +   **Heart**
        +   **Lung**
        +   **Pancreas**
        +   **Spleen**
        +   **Bone Marrow**
        +   **Thyroid**
        +   **Other (Please specify)**: If none of the above options suit your needs, select "Other" and describe your preference.
    *   **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact)** (`text`, required): Provide the name and contact details of your emergency contact person.
    *   **Medical Conditions (Medical Conditions)** (`text`, required): Describe any medical conditions that may impact your eligibility as a donor.
    *   **Consent (Consent)** (`text`, required): Confirm your consent for the organ donation process.
    *   **Additional Information (Additional Information)** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional details that may be relevant to your donor status.

## Tips

*   Be honest and thorough when answering the questions to ensure accurate assessment.
*   If you have any doubts or questions, consult with a medical professional before proceeding.
*   Ensure that your emergency contact person is aware of this form and is willing to act on your behalf in case of emergencies.
*   This form is not a substitute for medical evaluation; always follow medical guidance and protocols.
*   Review the form carefully to ensure that you have completed it accurately and comprehensively.
