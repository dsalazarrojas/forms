# Patient Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information for medical purposes, including demographic details and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately for your own records and future reference.
2. Be sure to answer each question thoroughly, as this information is crucial for your medical treatment.
3. If you're unsure about any question, ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
4. Review your answers before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (`patient_info_1`, text, **required**): Provide a brief overview of your personal details, including name, date of birth, and contact information.
  - This field is used as an introduction to identify you and your contact information for future reference.
  - Please include your full name, address, and phone number (or email) in this field.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info_2`, text, **required**): Enter your current contact information for billing and medical purposes.
  - This field is crucial for us to communicate with you and send you reminders, bills, or updates.
  - Please ensure that your contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history_3`, text, **required**): Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions.
  - This field helps us understand your health background and provide better care.
  - Please be honest and thorough in your description, including any medication or treatments you're currently taking.
- **Billing Information** (`billing_info_3`, text, **required**): Provide your billing information, including insurance details or payment methods.
  - This field is necessary for us to process your bills and claims.
  - Please include your insurance provider's name, policy number, and any other relevant details.
- **Insurance Information** (`insurance_info_3`, text, **required**): Enter your insurance information, including type, provider, and policy details.
  - This field helps us understand your insurance coverage and process claims accurately.
  - Please ensure that your insurance information is up-to-date and accurate.
- **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts_2`, text, **required**): List your emergency contacts' names, phone numbers, and relationships.
  - This field is used in case of an emergency or if we need to reach you quickly.
  - Please include at least two emergency contacts, including a family member or close friend.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your answers, as this information is crucial for your medical treatment and billing purposes.
- Review your answers before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If you're unsure about any question, ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
