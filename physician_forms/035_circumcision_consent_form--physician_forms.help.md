# Circumcision Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent and ensure understanding from patients before performing a circumcision procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each section and field.
* Gather all necessary information from the patient before proceeding.
* Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, paying attention to required fields.
* Review the patient's responses to ensure they understand and agree to the procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name or Guardian Name (if minor)** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's name or the guardian's name if the patient is a minor.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's contact phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the patient's email address for communication purposes.
* **Reason for Procedure** (`procedure_reason`, `select_one`, required): Select the reason for the circumcision procedure from the provided options.
* **Medical Indication (if applicable)** (`medical_indication`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the medical indication for the procedure.
* **Any Current Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): List any current medical conditions the patient may have.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, `text`, optional): List any current medications the patient is taking.
* **Known Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, optional): List any known allergies or medication allergies.
* **History of Bleeding Disorders or Excessive Bleeding** (`bleeding_disorders`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the patient has a history of bleeding disorders or excessive bleeding.
* **Previous Surgeries** (`previous_surgeries`, `text`, optional): List any previous surgeries or medical procedures.
* **Understanding of Procedure** (`procedure_explanation`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the patient understands the procedure explanation.
* **Risks and Possible Complications Have Been Discussed** (`risks_discussed`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the risks and complications have been discussed with the patient.
* **Alternative Treatments or Options Have Been Discussed** (`alternatives_discussed`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether alternative treatments or options have been discussed with the patient.
* **Recovery and Care Instructions Understood** (`recovery_expectations`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the recovery and care instructions have been understood.
* **Acknowledge Risk of Bleeding or Hemorrhage** (`bleeding_risk`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the patient acknowledges the risk of bleeding or hemorrhage.
* **Acknowledge Risk of Infection** (`infection_risk`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the patient acknowledges the risk of infection.
* **Acknowledge Risks Associated with Anesthesia** (`anesthesia_risk`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the patient acknowledges the risks associated with anesthesia.
* **Acknowledge This is a Permanent Cosmetic Change** (`permanent_change_risk`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the patient acknowledges this is a permanent cosmetic change.
* **Voluntarily Consent to This Procedure** (`voluntary_consent`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the patient voluntarily consents to the procedure.
* **Confirm This Decision is Made Freely Without Coercion** (`no_coercion`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the patient confirms this decision is made freely without coercion.
* **Authorize the Surgeon to Make Any Necessary Decisions** (`surgeon_authority`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the patient authorizes the surgeon to make any necessary decisions.
* **Date of Consent** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of consent in MM/DD/YYYY format.

## Tips
* Ensure the patient understands each section and field before completing the form.
* Review the patient's responses to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Verify the patient's signature and date of consent before proceeding with the procedure.
