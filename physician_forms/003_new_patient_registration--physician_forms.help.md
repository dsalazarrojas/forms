# new_patient_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient registration information for new patients. It includes fields for medical history, contact details, medical practice preferences, assigned tools, output file options, description, and category selection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "patient_registration" field.
2. Provide your medical history in the "medical_history" field.
3. Enter your contact details in the "contact_details" field.
4. Select your preferred medical practice from the "medical_practice" dropdown menu.
5. Choose an assigned tool from the "assigned_tool" dropdown menu.
6. If necessary, provide additional information in the "output_file" field.
7. Add a description of the patient's condition or reason for visit.
8. Select a category for the patient's record from the "category" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Registration (new_patient_form)**: Enter your personal details, such as name and contact information.
* **Medical History**: Describe your medical history, including any allergies, conditions, or previous treatments.
* **Contact Details (contact_details)**: Enter your address, phone number, and email address.
* **Medical Practice (medical_practice)**: Select your preferred medical practice, which may include options like general practice, surgical practice, or pediatric practice.
* **Assigned Tool (assigned_tool)**: Choose the tool assigned to you by the healthcare provider.
* **Output File (output_file)**: If needed, provide additional information that will be included in the patient's file.
* **Description (description)**: Add a brief description of the patient's condition or reason for visit.
* **Category (category)**: Select a category for the patient's record, such as "physician forms" or another category.
