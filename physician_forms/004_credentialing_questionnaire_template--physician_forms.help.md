# Credentialing Questionnaire Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help verify the credentials and background of physicians.

## How To Complete This Form

### General Tips

* This form is designed to be completed by a physician who needs to be credentialed.
* Please fill out each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Physician Background** (`physician_background`, text, optional): 
  Provide a brief description of your background and qualifications.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): 
  List your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **Credentials** (`credentials`, text, optional): 
  List any licenses, certifications, or other credentials you hold.
* **Professional Experience** (`professional_experience`, text, optional): 
  Describe your professional experience in the field of medicine.
* **Certification and Board Certifications** (`certification_and_board_certifications`, text, optional): 
  List any board certifications or specialized training you have received.
* **Credentialing History** (`credentialing_history`, text, optional): 
  Explain your credentialing history, including any issues or revocations.
* **Verification** (`verification`, text, optional): 
  Describe the process you went through to verify your credentials.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): 
  Provide any additional information that is relevant to your credentialing.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): 
  Review and submit your completed form.
* **Review and Verification** (`review_and_verification`, text, optional): 
  Review and verify your answers before submitting.
* **Signature** (`signature`, date, optional): 
  Date and sign the form to confirm your authenticity.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): 
  Date the form to confirm when it was completed.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): 
  Record the time when the form was completed.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and verify your answers before submitting the form.
* Date and sign the form to confirm authenticity.
* This form is intended to be a guide for physicians. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to form@gic.mx.
