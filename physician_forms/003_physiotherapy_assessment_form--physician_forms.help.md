# Physiotherapy Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physiotherapy Assessment Form is used to assess patient satisfaction and other relevant information in a medical setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select one or more of the options under "patient_satisfaction" to indicate the patient's level of satisfaction.
2. Answer "Yes" or "No" to "physical_therapy_services" to indicate if the patient received or will receive physiotherapy services.
3. For "services_satisfaction", enter a numerical value between 0 and 10 to rate the patient's satisfaction with the services received or anticipated to receive.
4. In "overall_satisfaction", enter a numerical value between 0 and 10 to rate the patient's overall satisfaction.
5. In "additional_comments", provide any additional comments or remarks you would like to include.
6. For "date_of_last_visit", select the date of the patient's last visit.
7. In "next_steps", describe any next steps or follow-up actions you plan to take regarding the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **patient_satisfaction** (select_multiple): Indicate the patient's level of satisfaction with the services received or anticipated to receive. Select one or more of the options provided.
  - Satisfied: The patient is satisfied with the services.
  - Dissatisfied: The patient is dissatisfied with the services.
- **physical_therapy_services** (select_one): Indicate if the patient received or will receive physiotherapy services.
  - 'Yes': The patient received or will receive physiotherapy services.
  - 'No': The patient did not receive or will not receive physiotherapy services.
- **services_satisfaction** (number): Enter a numerical value between 0 and 10 to rate the patient's satisfaction with the services received or anticipated to receive.
- **overall_satisfaction** (number): Enter a numerical value between 0 and 10 to rate the patient's overall satisfaction.
- **additional_comments** (text): Provide any additional comments or remarks you would like to include.
- **date_of_last_visit** (date): Select the date of the patient's last visit.
- **next_steps** (text): Describe any next steps or follow-up actions you plan to take regarding the patient.
