# Pregnancy Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for patients to acknowledge their understanding of their medical condition and the risks associated with treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
3. Ensure that you answer all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, number, required/optional): Enter your age in years.
* **Are you Pregnant?** (`is_pregnant`, select_one, required/optional): Select "pregnant" if you are currently pregnant or "not pregnant" if you are not.
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Date of Last Visit** (`date_last_visit`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your last visit to the doctor.
* **Date of Next Visit** (`date_next_visit`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your next scheduled visit to the doctor.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required/optional): Please sign your name in the space provided to acknowledge that you have read and understood the form.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you signed this form.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have read and understood the entire form before signing.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your doctor or healthcare provider for clarification.
- This form is intended for patients who are not pregnant; if you are pregnant, please do not complete this form.
