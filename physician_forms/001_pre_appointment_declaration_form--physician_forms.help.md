# pre_appointment_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pre_appointment_declaration_form is a necessary document used to gather information about a patient before their scheduled appointment. This information will be reviewed by medical staff before and during the appointment to ensure the patient's needs are met.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting to complete the form, make sure to review the fields carefully and fill them out accurately. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for assistance from a medical staff member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: (`patient_name`, text, required): Please fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth**: (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY or MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Medical Condition**: (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Describe your current medical condition or reason for visiting the doctor.
* **Scheduled Date**: (`scheduled_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your scheduled appointment.
* **Appointment Time**: (`appointment_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your scheduled appointment (if applicable).
* **Doctor Name**: (`doctor_name`, text, required): Please fill in the name of your assigned doctor or the doctor you are scheduled to see.
* **Reason for Visit**: (`reason_for_visit`, text, optional): Describe the reason for your visit in a brief and clear manner.
* **Phone Number**: (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email**: (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Medical Insurance**: (`medical_insurance`, select_one, required): Select your current medical insurance type from the available options.
* **Emergency Contact**: (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person (if applicable).

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting the form.
- If you need assistance or have questions, don't hesitate to ask a medical staff member.
- Please provide truthful and accurate information to ensure the best possible care for your appointment.
