# Suturing Lab Skill Check Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Suturing Lab Skill Check Off Form is used to track the completion of a suture lab skill check-off by a trainee or practitioner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the text field with a brief description of the procedure you are checking off.
2.  For the question "Do you have the knowledge and skills to perform this procedure?", select one or more of the options "Yes" or "No".
3.  Enter the date and time when you completed the skill check-off.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Suturing Lab Skill Check Off** (`suturing_labskillcheckoffform_page_1`, note, required: false): This is the title of the form.
* **What is the procedure you are checking off?** (`suturing_labskillcheckoffform_page_2`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the procedure you are checking off.
* **Do you have the knowledge and skills to perform this procedure?** (`suturing_labskillcheckoffform_page_3`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more of the options "Yes" or "No" to indicate your level of knowledge and skills.
* **Date** (`suturing_labskillcheckoffform_page_4`, date, required: false): Enter the date when you completed the skill check-off.
* **Time** (`suturing_labskillcheckoffform_page_5`, time, required: false): Enter the time when you completed the skill check-off.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask a supervisor or instructor for guidance.
* This form is for personal or internal use, so there's no need to worry about security or data privacy concerns.
