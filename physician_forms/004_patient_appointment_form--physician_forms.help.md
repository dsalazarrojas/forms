# Patient Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Appointment Form is designed to collect information about a patient's appointment, which is necessary for efficient and organized patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select one of the following options for Patient Appointment Form 1.
2.  Select multiple options for Patient Appointment Form 2.
3.  Enter the appointment date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4.  Enter the appointment time.
5.  Enter the name of the physician assigned to the patient's appointment.
6.  Enter the name of the patient.
7.  Select one of the following options for Appointment Type.
8.  Select the appointment status (Active or Inactive).
9.  Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Appointment Form 1** (`patient_appointment_form_fields`, select_one, required/optional): This field allows you to select one of the options provided.
* **Patient Appointment Form 2** (`patient_appointment_form_fields_1`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field allows you to select multiple options.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the appointment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the appointment.
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the physician assigned to the patient's appointment.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the patient.
* **Appointment Type** (`appointment_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the following options for the type of appointment.
* **Appointment Status** (`appointment_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the appointment (Active or Inactive).
