# medical_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medical information for a medical report, including personal details, medical group, medical provider, and date of visit.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the first field. This is a text field where you can enter your full first name.
2. Fill in your **Last Name** in the second field. This is also a text field where you can enter your full last name.
3. Enter your **Birth Date** in the third field. This is a date field where you can select a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Select your **Medical Group** in the fourth field. This is a text field where you can enter your medical group (e.g., "Cardio").
5. Enter your **Medical Provider** in the fifth field. This is a text field where you can enter the name of your medical provider (e.g., "Dr. Smith").
6. Enter the **Date of Visit** in the sixth field. This is a date field where you can select the date of your visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your full last name.
* **Birth Date** (date): Enter your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical Group** (text): Select your medical group (e.g., "Cardio").
* **Medical Provider** (text): Enter the name of your medical provider (e.g., "Dr. Smith").
* **Date of Visit** (date): Enter the date of your visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields (if any).
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
