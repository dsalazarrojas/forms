# Prescription Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prescription Request Form is a standard form used by physicians to request a prescription for their patients. It gathers essential information from the patient and the healthcare professional requesting the prescription.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select the patient's date of birth in the "Patient DOB" field using the YYYY-MM-DD format.
3. Select the required medicines for the patient from the "Medicines Needed" dropdown list.
4. Enter the dosage of the medicine using the "Dosage" field.
5. Specify the quantity of the medicine required using the "Quantity" field.
6. Enter your name as the doctor's name in the "Doctor Name" field.
7. Enter your title as a doctor in the "Doctor Title" field.
8. Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email address, in the "Doctor Phone" and "Doctor Email" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient DOB** (`patient_dob`, date, optional): Select the patient's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Medicines Needed** (`medicines_needed`, select_multiple, required): Choose the required medicines from the list.
* **Dosage** (`dosage`, number, optional): Enter the dosage of the medicine prescribed.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Specify the quantity of the medicine required.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the doctor requesting the prescription.
* **Doctor Title** (`doctor_title`, text, optional): Enter your title as a doctor.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
