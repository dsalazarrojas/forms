# Covid Questionnaire For Patients - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients regarding their experiences and interactions with COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, select the appropriate options for each field, ensuring that all required fields are filled out. Provide accurate and honest answers to each question. Review and verify your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Has Exposed To Covid** (`exposed_to_covid`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the options that describe your exposure to COVID-19.
* **Has Symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Contact With Covid** (`contact_with_covid`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether you have had contact with someone who has had COVID-19.
* **Test Results** (`test_results`, select_one, required: false): Select the option that best describes your test results.
* **Traveled To Affected Areas** (`traveled_to_areas`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether you have traveled to areas affected by COVID-19.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of any relevant medical history you have.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required: false): List any medical conditions you have.
* **Medication Use** (`medication_use`, text, required: false): Report any medication you are currently using.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your travel history.
* **Recent Travel** (`recent_travel`, text, required: false): Indicate any recent travel you have taken.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_one, required: false): Indicate your current vaccination status.
* **Vaccination Type** (`vaccination_type`, select_one, required: false): If vaccinated, select the type of vaccine you received.
* **Vaccination Location** (`vaccination_location`, select_one, required: false): If vaccinated, select where you received your vaccination.
* **Follow Up Plan** (`follow_up_plan`, select_one, required: false): Indicate if you have a follow-up plan with your healthcare provider.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Provide any contact information you have.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Provide the contact information for your emergency contact.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, required: false): List your insurance provider, if any.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required: false): Provide the name of your doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, required: false): Provide the phone number of your doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, text, required: false): Provide the email address of your doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, required: false): Provide your personal contact phone number.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, text, required: false): Provide your personal email address.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required: false): Provide your address.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Provide any additional medical history.
* **Other** (`other`, text, required: false): Provide any other relevant information not covered by the previous fields.
