# Medical Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Supply Order Form is used to request medical supplies for hospitals, pharmacies, or stores. This form is intended for authorized personnel to order medical supplies for their facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the category for the medical supply order (e.g. "Physician Forms").
2.  Enter a brief description of the supplies needed.
3.  Choose the location where the supplies will be used (e.g. "Hospital", "Pharmacy", "Store").
4.  Enter the quantity of supplies needed.
5.  Indicate if medical supplies are required (yes/no).
6.  Enter the price per unit of the supplies.
7.  Optionally, select a date and time for the delivery or shipment of the supplies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Category** (`category`, required): Select the category for the medical supply order.
  - This field is required to ensure the form is completed for the correct type of facility (e.g. hospital, pharmacy, or store).
- **Description** (`description`, optional): Enter a brief description of the medical supplies needed.
  - This field helps the supply staff understand what you are requesting.
- **Location** (`hospital_pharmacy_store`, required): Choose the location where the supplies will be used.
  - This field ensures the supplies go to the correct facility.
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, required): Enter the amount of medical supplies needed.
  - A valid number is required here.
- **Medical Supplies** (`medical_supplies`, required): Indicate if medical supplies are being requested.
  - This field ensures the supplies you request are actually for medical use.
- **Price** (`pricing`, required): Enter the price per unit of the medical supplies.
  - A valid price per unit is required here.
- **Date and Time** (`date` and `time`, optional): Enter the date and time for the delivery or shipment of the supplies.
  - These fields are not required but can be useful for scheduling and logistics.
- **Comments** (`comments`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the order.
  - This field is for any additional information that may be helpful for the supply staff.
