### Physician Verification Form - Help Guide

### Purpose

The Physician Verification Form is a crucial document that confirms the accuracy of a patient's information and medical history. It is used to validate the patient's data before treatment or services are provided.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Physician Information section with your name, title, and license number.
2. Enter the Patient Name and Patient Address in the respective fields.
3. Select the Medical Board Certification for the patient from the provided options.
4. Choose the Date of Last Examination and Date of Last Contact from the calendar or enter in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. In the Medical History field, provide a brief description of the patient's medical condition.
6. Sign the form with your physician's signature.
7. Finally, review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Physician Name (physician_verification_form)** (Required: N/A): Enter your name as it appears on your medical license.
* **Patient Name (patient_name)** (Required: N/A): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their government-issued ID or medical record.
* **Medical Board Certification (medical_board_certification)** (Required): Select one or more of the following certifications: 
  • American Board of Surgery
  • American Board of Orthopedic Surgery
* **Date of Last Examination (date_of_last_examination)** (Required: N/A): Enter the date of the patient's last examination in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Physician Signature (physician_signature)** (Required: N/A): Sign with your professional medical signature.
* **Medical History (medical_history)** (Required: N/A): Describe the patient's medical condition, including any allergies, medical history, or previous treatments.
* **Patient Address (patient_address)** (Required: N/A): Enter the patient's home address as it appears on their government-issued ID or medical record.
* **Date of Last Contact (date_of_last_contact)** (Required: N/A): Enter the date of the last contact with the patient in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Physician Phone (physician_phone)** (Required: N/A): Enter your personal phone number for contact purposes.
* **Treatment Plan (treatment_plan)** (Required: N/A): Describe the patient's treatment plan, including any medications or therapies.
* **Procedure (procedure)** (Required: N/A): Briefly describe the medical procedure or service provided.
