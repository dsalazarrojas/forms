# Optometry Patient Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Optometry Patient Information Form is designed to collect patient's personal details for optometry services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your patient name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select your date of birth using the date format.
3. Enter the name of your eye doctor.
4. Provide your medical record number.
5. Enter your patient address.
6. Enter your contact email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): Enter your complete patient name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Select your date of birth using the date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Eye Doctor's Name** (`eye_doctor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your eye doctor.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, required: false): Enter your medical record number.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required: false): Enter your patient address.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your contact email address.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and consent to provide your personal data.
* If you have any doubts or concerns about the form's purpose or content, please contact <form@gic.mx>.
