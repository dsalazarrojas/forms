# Chronic Pain Assessment Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chronic Pain Assessment Questionnaire Form is used to gather information on your chronic pain experience. The information collected will help us understand your pain better, and aid in developing a personalized treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select all the relevant answers for each field.
3. If a field is not applicable or not relevant, select "None of the above" or "Not sure" as necessary.
4. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in the fields that allow text input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Where is your pain located (`pain_location`, select_multiple, required): Select all areas where you experience pain. Examples: Head, Neck, Shoulders, Upper Back, Lower Back, Arms, Hands, Legs, Feet, Abdomen, Chest.**
* **On a scale of 0 to 10, how would you rate your pain (`pain_intensity`, number, required): 0 means no pain, 10 means the worst pain imaginable.**
* **How long have you been experiencing this pain (`pain_duration`, select_one, required): Select the time frame that best describes your pain duration. Examples: Less than a week, 1 to 4 weeks, 1 to 3 months, 3 to 6 months, 6 months to 1 year, More than 1 year.**
* **What type of pain do you experience (`pain_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all types of pain you experience. Examples: Sharp, Dull, Burning, Throbbing, Shooting, Aching, Tingling, Numbness.**
* **What makes your pain worse (`pain_triggers`, select_multiple, required): Select all activities or factors that trigger your pain. Examples: Physical activity, Stress, Weather changes, Certain foods, Lack of sleep, Prolonged sitting, Prolonged standing, Lifting objects, None of the above.**
* **What helps relieve your pain (`pain_relief`, select_multiple, required): Select all methods that provide relief. Examples: Rest, Medication, Heat therapy, Cold therapy, Massage, Exercise, Meditation, Physical therapy, Nothing helps.**
* **How does pain affect your daily activities (`daily_activities_impact`, select_one, required): Select the impact of pain on your daily activities. Examples: No impact, Mild impact, Moderate impact, Severe impact, Completely unable to perform activities.**
* **Does pain interfere with your sleep (`sleep_quality`, select_one, required): Select how often pain interferes with your sleep. Examples: Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Always.**
* **What medications are you currently taking for pain (`current_medications`, text, required): List all prescription and over-the-counter medications you are currently taking.**
* **Have you received any previous treatments for this pain (`previous_treatments`, text, required): Include physical therapy, injections, surgeries, etc.**
* **How has chronic pain affected your mood (`mood_impact`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply. Examples: Increased irritability, Anxiety, Depression, Frustration, Mood swings, No noticeable effect.**
* **Has pain affected your ability to work (`work_impact`, select_one, required): Select the impact of pain on your work. Examples: No impact, Occasionally miss work, Reduced productivity, Changed job duties, Unable to work.**
* **Is there a family history of chronic pain conditions (`family_history`, select_one, required): Select if there is a family history of chronic pain conditions. Examples: True, False, Not sure.**
* **Any additional comments about your pain (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that may help us understand your pain better.**
