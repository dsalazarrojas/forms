# COVID 19 Vaccine Card Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and verify COVID-19 vaccination information from employees or staff members.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your details (Employee or Staff Member Name, Employee ID Number, and Email Address).
2. Select your COVID-19 vaccination status (Fully vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, or Not vaccinated).
3. Choose the type of vaccine you received (Pfizer, Moderna, Johnson and Johnson, AstraZeneca, Novavax, or Other).
4. If applicable, fill in the date and location of your first and second dose.
5. Indicate if you have your vaccine card.
6. Upload a photo or scan of your vaccine card or type "Lost" if you do not have it (PDF, JPG, or PNG format).
7. Confirm if the card is readable and legible.
8. Confirm that all the provided information is accurate and truthful.
9. Agree to sharing vaccination information for verification.
10. If applicable, select if you need a replacement vaccine card.
11. Indicate your plan to get the booster shot (True or False).
12. Confirm if you are claiming medical or religious exemption (True or False).
13. If claiming an exemption, select the type of exemption (Medical, Religious, or Other).
14. Upload documentation for the exemption claim (if required).
15. Finally, submit the form with your signature and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee or Staff Member Name** (1): Enter your name as recorded in the personnel database.
* **Employee ID Number** (2): Enter your employee or staff ID number.
* **Email Address** (3): Enter your work email address.
* **Department or Division** (4): Enter your department or division where you work.
* **-- COVID-19 Vaccination Status --** (5): This section is for indicating your COVID-19 vaccination status.
* **Vaccination Status** (6): Choose from Fully vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, or Not vaccinated.
* **Vaccine Type** (7): Select from Pfizer, Moderna, Johnson and Johnson, AstraZeneca, Novavax, or Other.
* **Date of First Dose** (8), **Location of First Dose** (9), **Date of Second Dose** (10), and **Location of Second Dose** (11): If you have received two doses, fill in the dates and locations.
* **Do you have your vaccine card** (15): Indicate if you have your vaccine card, type "Lost" if you don't have it, or upload a photo or scan.
* **Upload Vaccine Card Photo or Copy** (16): Upload a photo or scan of your vaccine card, or type "Lost" if you don't have it.
* **Card is readable and legible** (17): Confirm if the uploaded card is readable and legible.
* **All vaccination information is accurate and truthful** (19): Confirm that all provided information is accurate and truthful.
* **I consent to sharing vaccination information for verification** (20): Agree to sharing your vaccination information for verification.
* **Need replacement vaccine card** (21): Indicate if you need a replacement vaccine card.
* **Plan to get booster shot** (22): Indicate your plan to get the booster shot.
* **Claiming medical or religious exemption** (24): Confirm if you are claiming an exemption (True or False).
* **If yes, type of exemption** (25): Select Medical, Religious, or Other if claiming an exemption.
* **Upload exemption documentation** (26): Upload documentation for the exemption claim (if required).
* **Submission Date** (27): Fill in the date of submission.

**Tips**: Before submitting, double-check that all information is accurate and up-to-date. Ensure that you are not claiming a medical or religious exemption without valid documentation. If you need help or have questions, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
