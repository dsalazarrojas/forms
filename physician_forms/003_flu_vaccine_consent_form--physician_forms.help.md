# Flu Vaccine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flu Vaccine Consent Form is used by healthcare providers to gather essential information from patients before administering the flu vaccine. This form is required for every patient who will be receiving the flu vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the following sections:
	* Patient Information: Enter the patient's name and contact number.
	* Contact Information: Enter the patient's email address.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (Required: false): 
	+ Enter the patient's full name in this field.
	* **Contact Number** (Required: false):
	+ Enter the patient's phone number so the healthcare provider can contact them with any questions or updates.
* **Email Address** (Required: false):
	+ Enter the patient's email address so the healthcare provider can contact them with any questions or updates.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all entered information is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are unsure about any information, please contact the healthcare provider for assistance.
- The healthcare provider will review the form thoroughly before administering the flu vaccine.
- The flu vaccine will only be administered once this form is fully completed and reviewed.
