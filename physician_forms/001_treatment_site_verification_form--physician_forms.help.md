# Treatment Site Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treatment Site Verification Form is a medical form used to collect information from patients and their physicians to verify treatment sites. This form is used to track patient information and ensure consistency across multiple visits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth.
3. Enter the physician's name.
4. Enter the patient's medical record numbers (as available).
5. Enter the physician's email address (if applicable).
6. Enter the patient's phone number (if applicable).
7. Enter the patient's address.
8. Enter the treatment site (where the patient received treatment).
9. Enter the disease diagnosis.
10. Enter the patient's medical history.
11. Select the patient's status (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
12. Select whether the treatment site is verified (Yes or No).
13. Enter the date of the visit.
14. Enter the time of the visit.
15. Enter any additional notes from the physician (if applicable).
16. Select the physician's verification status (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Physician Name**: Enter the physician's name who treated the patient.
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter any available medical record numbers for the patient.
* **Physician Email**: Enter the physician's email address (if applicable).
* **Physician Phone**: Enter the physician's phone number (if applicable).
* **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address.
* **Treatment Site**: Enter the location where the patient received treatment.
* **Disease Diagnosis**: Enter the patient's disease diagnosis.
* **Patient Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history for the patient.
* **Patient Status**: Select the patient's status (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
* **Treatment Site Verification**: Select whether the treatment site is verified (Yes or No).
* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of the visit in the format MM/DD/YY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Time of Visit**: Enter the time of the visit in the format HH:MM (e.g., 12:00).
* **Physician Signature**: Enter any additional notes from the physician (if applicable).
* **Medical Record Notes**: Enter any additional medical record notes (if applicable).
* **Physician Verification**: Select the physician's verification status (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).

Note: The fields marked as "required" must be completed before proceeding with the rest of the form.

## Tips

* Double-check the information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* Make sure to enter the correct format for dates and times (MM/DD/YY and HH:MM respectively).
* If a field is not relevant, leave it blank.
* If a field is not completed, it may affect the form's submission and processing.
