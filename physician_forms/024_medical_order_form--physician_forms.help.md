# Medical Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Medical Order Form is used to gather necessary information about a patient's medical needs and create a medication order. This form helps healthcare professionals communicate with patients and ensure that the medication is dispensed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

- **Fill out each field completely and accurately**.
- **Double-check for spelling and grammar errors** before submitting.
- **Use the options available in the select fields to choose the correct answer**.
- **Make sure to review and edit as needed before final submission**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
  - **Example:** John Doe

- **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Describe the patient's medical condition that requires medication.
  - **Example:** High Blood Pressure

- **Medication Order** (`medication_order`, text, optional): Specify the type of medication ordered.
  - **Example:** Acetaminophen

- **Dosage Instructions** (`dosage_instructions`, text, optional): Provide clear instructions for the medication dosage.
  - **Example:** Take 2 tablets twice a day

- **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, number, optional): Enter the patient's age.
  - **Example:** 35

- **Medication Frequency** (`medication_frequency`, select one, optional): Choose the frequency of medication administration (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
  - **Example:** Morning

- **Medication Dosage** (`medication_dosage`, number, optional): Enter the dosage amount of the medication.
  - **Example:** 2

- **Physician Comments** (`physician_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's condition.
  - **Example:** Follow up with patient in 2 weeks

- **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
  - **Example:** 123-456-7890

- **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
  - **Example:** johndoe@example.com

## Tips

- **Double-check the form for errors before submission**.
- **Use clear and concise language in the fields**.
- **Make sure to save and submit the form when complete**.
