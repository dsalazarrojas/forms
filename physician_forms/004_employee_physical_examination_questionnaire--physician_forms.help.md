# Employee Physical Examination Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about an employee's medical history, physical examination, and work-related details. The information gathered from this form will be used to assess an employee's physical health and wellness, and to determine their eligibility for certain benefits or services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your HR or medical representative for clarification.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History**: Please provide a brief description of your medical history, including any previous injuries, illnesses, or medical conditions.
* **Current Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Medical Conditions**: Describe any ongoing medical conditions, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, or allergies.
* **Physical Examination**: Describe any physical examinations or tests you have undergone recently.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you have, including food, environmental, or medication allergies.
* **Medications Allergies**: List any medications you are allergic to.
* **Medical History Review**: Please provide a brief description of your medical history review, including any relevant details.
* **Medical Certifications**: List any relevant medical certifications or licenses you hold.
* **Medical License**: Please enter your medical license number.
* **Medical License Status**: Please select your current medical license status (Active or Expired).
* **Medical License Expiration Date**: Please enter your medical license expiration date (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Work Status**: Please select your current work status (Active, Inactive, or Retired).
* **Work Type**: Please select your current work type (Full-time, Part-time, or Intern).
* **Work Shift**: Please select your current work shift (Day, Night, or Rotating Shift).
* **Work Schedule**: Please select your current work schedule (9-5, 8-4, or 11-7).

## Tips

* Please answer all questions carefully and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your HR or medical representative for clarification.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely.
* If you have any additional medical information or concerns, please provide it in the "Additional Medical Information" section (not included in this form).

This guide will help employees understand what information is expected from them and how to complete the form accurately. The form's purpose, fields, and instructions will help HR and medical professionals gather the necessary information to assess an employee's physical health and wellness and determine their eligibility for certain benefits or services.
