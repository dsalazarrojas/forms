# Negative COVID 19 Test Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your negative COVID-19 test result. It's used to gather data about your test result, test date, and who reported the test result.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "User Information" page.
2. Fill in the required information, such as your name, email, and any other relevant details.
3. Move on to the "Test Result" page.
4. Choose your test result from the available options: "COVID 19 Negative", "COVID 19 Positive", or "Inconclusive".
5. Select the "Test Date" page.
6. Choose the date of your test from the calendar.
7. Select the "Reported By" page.
8. Choose the person who reported your test result from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (user_info, text, optional): Fill in your basic information, such as your name and email. If you don't have any personal data that you'd like to share, you can leave this field blank.
* **Test Result** (test_result, select_one, optional): Select the outcome of your COVID-19 test from the available options. Please choose "COVID 19 Negative" if your test result is negative, "COVID 19 Positive" if it's positive, or "Inconclusive" if it's inconclusive.
* **Test Date** (test_date, date, optional): Choose the date you took the test from the calendar. This can be any date in the past or future.
* **Reported By** (reported_by, select_one, optional): Select the person who reported your test result from the available options. You can choose from User A, User B, or User C.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and truthfully.
* Avoid duplication by not submitting multiple forms with the same test result.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for help or clarification.
