# pre_anesthetic_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pre-anesthetic questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about your medical history, allergies, medications, and anesthesia history. This information is crucial for the anesthesiologist to create a safe and effective anesthesia plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the relevant fields with your information and submit. You can navigate through the pages by clicking on the "Next" and "Back" buttons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Medical History

* **medical_history** (text): Please describe your medical history, including any previous surgeries, illnesses, or medical conditions.
### Allergies

* **allergies** (text): List any allergies you have, including any medications or substances that trigger allergic reactions.
### Medications

* **medications** (text): List any medications you are currently taking, including prescription medications, over-the-counter medications, and herbal supplements.
### Medications Allergies

* **medications_allergies** (select multiple): Select any medications that you are allergic to.
	+ Allergies to Penicillin
	+ Allergies to Sulfa drugs
	+ Allergies to Latex
### Anesthesia History

* **anesthesia_history** (text): Describe any previous experiences with anesthesia, including any complications or reactions.
### Anesthesia Medications

* **anesthesia_medications** (text): List any medications you have taken under previous anesthesia, including any medications that caused complications or reactions.
### Anesthesia Complications

* **anesthesia_complications** (text): Describe any complications or reactions you experienced under previous anesthesia.
### General Anesthetic

* **general_anaesthetic** (text): Describe any general anesthesia experiences or complications.
### Regional Anesthetic

* **regional_anaesthetic** (text): Describe any regional anesthesia experiences or complications.
### Local Anesthetic

* **local_anaesthetic** (text): Describe any local anesthesia experiences or complications.

## Tips
* Please answer all fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure the anesthesiologist has a complete understanding of your medical history.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
* Please submit the form only once you have completed all the fields.
