# Acupuncture Soap Notes Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to record and document the essential information for each acupuncture session. The goal is to gather and track the patient's subjective and objective symptoms, clinical impressions, and treatment plans to ensure accurate and effective care.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Start by filling out the form as soon as possible after each session, preferably during or immediately after. This will help ensure that you capture all relevant information while it is fresh.**
2.  **For each field, enter the required information as accurately and thoroughly as possible.**
3.  **Double-check your responses and ensure that they reflect the patient's situation correctly.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient name** (`patient_name`, text, **required**): This field is for the patient's full name, which will help identify and differentiate the patient's records.
* **Session date** (`session_date`, date, **required**): This field is for recording the date of the current acupuncture session.
* **Subjective complaint** (`subjective_complaint`, text, **required**): Describe the patient's reported symptoms, history, and any concerns they may have.
* **Objective findings** (`objective_findings`, text, **required**): Document your observations during the examination, including vital signs and any notable physical findings.
* **Assessment and clinical impression** (`assessment`, text, **required**): Summarize your professional assessment of the patient's condition and clinical impression.
* **Treatment provided and plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, **required**): Record the specific treatments performed and any recommendations or plans for future sessions.
* **Response to treatment** (`response_to_treatment`, text, **optional**): If applicable, note any immediate reactions or responses to the treatment. This field is optional and may not be relevant in all cases.
* **Practitioner name for signature** (`practitioner_signature`, text, **required**): This field is for you, the acupuncturist, to sign and date your name, indicating that you have completed the form.
* **Next appointment date** (`next_appointment`, date, **optional**): If you have scheduled or recommended a follow-up appointment, enter the date here. This field is optional and depends on your practice's scheduling and appointment management.

**Tips**

*  Always keep the form up-to-date and organized for easy reference and efficient patient care.
*  Review the form regularly to ensure that it is meeting your needs and those of your patients.
*  Consider adding or removing fields as necessary to accommodate your practice's specific requirements and patient needs.
