# sperm_donor_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed medical and personal information from potential sperm donors to ensure they meet the necessary health and eligibility standards for donation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and answer truthfully.
2. Fill out each field according to the label provided.
3. Ensure you have not previously completed a similar questionnaire or had any prior medical evaluations or screenings that would preclude you from filling out this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information** (`general_information`, text, required: false): This field is for providing general information about yourself. Please provide any relevant background details that might be useful for the screening process.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): This field is for describing your medical history. Please provide a detailed account of any medical conditions, treatments, or surgeries you have had in the past.
- **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, text, required: false): This is another field for further detailing your medical history.
- ... (continued for all 15 fields)

## Tips
- Be thorough and honest when filling out this form.
- If you are unsure about any medical information or have concerns, please discuss them with a medical professional or the relevant authority.
- Each field is crucial for the screening process, so please ensure you complete all required fields.
