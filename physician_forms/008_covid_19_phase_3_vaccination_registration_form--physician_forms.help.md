# COVID 19 Phase 3 Vaccination Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for essential workers and individuals in industries that require verification of employment to receive COVID-19 vaccination as part of Phase 3 of the vaccination program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, date of birth, and mobile phone number.
2. Select your industry of employment from the given options.
3. Enter your job role or title and the name of your business or organization.
4. Enter your business address.
5. Select your level of exposure to the public at work.
6. Indicate if you have received any previous doses of a COVID-19 vaccine.
7. Select the type of verification document you will bring to the appointment.
8. Confirm that you understand you must provide proof of employment at the time of vaccination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Industry of Employment**: Select your primary work sector, which may be:
	+ Restaurant/Food Service
	+ Hair/Beauty Salon
	+ Retail
	+ Transportation
	+ Hospitality
	+ Other Essential Service
* **Job Role / Title**: Enter your job title or role.
* **Name of Business**: Enter the name of your business or organization.
* **Business Address**: Enter your business address.
* **Mobile Phone Number**: Enter your mobile phone number for SMS appointment reminders.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Level of Exposure to Public at Work**: Select your level of exposure to the public at work:
	+ Low
	+ Moderate
	+ High
	+ Continuous
* **Have you received any previous doses of a COVID-19 vaccine?**: Indicate if you have received any previous doses of a COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Verification Document you will bring to appointment**: Select the type of verification document you will bring:
	+ Work ID
	+ Pay Stub
	+ Business License
	+ Letter from Employer
* **I understand I must provide proof of employment at the time of vaccination**: Confirm that you understand the requirement to provide proof of employment.

## Tips
* Be accurate and complete when filling out the form.
* Ensure you select the correct industry and level of exposure to the public at work.
* Bring the required verification document to the appointment.
* Confirm your understanding of the registration requirements.
