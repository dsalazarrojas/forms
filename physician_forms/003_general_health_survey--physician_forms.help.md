# general_health_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The general_health_survey form is designed to collect general health information from users, including demographic details and lifestyle habits.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling out the introduction section, which is a brief text field for you to provide any additional context or information about your health.
2. Enter your age in the **Age** field. This is a required field to ensure accurate representation of your demographic details.
3. Enter your height in the **Height** field. Although not required, this helps our system understand your stature for better representation.
4. Enter your weight in the **Weight** field. Similar to height, this is not required but helps our system to understand your body composition.
5. In the **Blood Pressure** field, enter your blood pressure reading if you have one. If not, leave it blank.
6. For the **Smoker** field, select one of the options:
	* 'True' if you are a smoker
	* 'False' if you are not a smoker
	* 'Other' if you have other smoking habits or if you are unsure
7. Similarly, for the **Drinker** field, select one of the options:
	* 'True' if you drink frequently
	* 'False' if you do not drink frequently
	* 'Other' if you have other drinking habits or if you are unsure
8. For the **Exercise** field, select one of the options:
	* 'True' if you exercise regularly
	* 'False' if you do not exercise regularly
	* 'Other' if you have other exercise habits or if you are unsure
9. In the **Medication** field, enter any current medication you are taking, if applicable.
10. Provide any medical conditions you have in the **Medical Condition** field, if applicable.
11. Mention any allergies you have in the **Allergies** field, if applicable.
12. For the **Medical History** field, enter any significant medical history you have, if applicable.
13. In the **Medical History Other** field, enter any other medical history not listed, if applicable.
14. In the **Medications** field, enter any medications you are taking, if applicable.
15. Select all applicable health insurance types in the **Health Insurance** field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction (Text)**: A text field for you to provide any additional context or information about your health.
* **Age (Number)**: Enter your age.
* **Height (Number)**: Enter your height in inches or centimeters.
* **Weight (Number)**: Enter your weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Blood Pressure (Text)**: Enter your blood pressure reading if you have one.
* **Smoker (Select One)**: Select if you are a smoker, not a smoker, or if you have other habits.
* **Drinker (Select One)**: Select if you drink frequently, do not drink frequently, or if you have other habits.
* **Exercise (Select One)**: Select if you exercise regularly, do not exercise regularly, or if you have other habits.
* **Medication (Text)**: Enter any current medication you are taking.
* **Medical Condition (Text)**: Enter any current medical conditions you have.
* **Allergies (Text)**: Enter any allergies you have.
* **Medical History (Text)**: Enter any significant medical history you have.
* **Medical History Other (Text)**: Enter any other medical history not listed.
* **Medications (Text)**: Enter any medications you are taking.
* **Health Insurance (Select Multiple)**: Select all health insurance types you have.
