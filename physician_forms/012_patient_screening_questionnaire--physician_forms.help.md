# Patient Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and store patient data for the purpose of conducting a comprehensive screening. The information gathered will help identify potential risks and ensure that the patient receives the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out this form with the following steps:

* Review the form carefully to understand what information is being requested.
* Fill out all required fields, which are marked as such in the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (1)**: Please enter the patient's full name.
* **birthdate (2)**: Enter the patient's birthdate in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Address (3)**: Provide the patient's current address.
* **Medical History (4)**: Enter any significant medical history, including previous treatments, surgeries, and illnesses.
* **Allergies (5)**: Select any allergies the patient has from the options provided.
* **Medications (6)**: Select any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Medical Conditions (7)**: List any known medical conditions the patient has.
* **Contact Number (8)**: Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Email (9)**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Emergency Contact (10)**: Enter the name and contact information of the patient's emergency contact.
* **Insurance Provider (11)**: Enter the name of the patient's insurance provider.
* **Insurance Number (12)**: Enter the patient's insurance number.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (13)**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name (14)**: Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Medical Provider (15)**: Enter the name of the patient's medical provider.
* **Medical Group (16)**: Enter the name of the patient's medical group.
* **Medical Record (17)**: Enter any relevant information about the patient's medical record.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (18)**: Describe the relationship between the emergency contact and the patient.
* **Patient Screening Form (19)**: Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the patient's screening.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to ensure the best possible care for the patient.
