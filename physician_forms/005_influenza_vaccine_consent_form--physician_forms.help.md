# Influenza Vaccine Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Influenza Vaccine Consent Form is used to obtain patient consent and other relevant information for influenza vaccine administration. This form ensures that patients are aware of the vaccine's purpose, risks, and benefits, and that they have provided their consent for the vaccine administration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient's interest in receiving the vaccine.
2. Enter your patient's name.
3. Enter your child's (if applicable) name.
4. Provide your patient's date of birth.
5. Enter your patient's contact phone number.
6. Enter your patient's email address (if applicable).
7. Sign the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the information provided.
8. Date and time-stamp the form when you have completed it.
9. Date and time-stamp the form when you enter it.
10. Date and time-stamp the form when you completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Interest** (`patient_interest`, text, optional): Enter a brief statement indicating your patient's interest in receiving the influenza vaccine.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter your patient's full name.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter your child's (if applicable) full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your patient's contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, text, optional): Enter your patient's email address (if applicable).
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): Sign this form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the information provided.
* **Date of Signature** (`date_signature`, date, optional): Date and time-stamp this form when you sign it.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Date and time-stamp this form when you have completed it.
* **Date Entered** (`date_entered`, date, optional): Date and time-stamp this form when you entered it.
