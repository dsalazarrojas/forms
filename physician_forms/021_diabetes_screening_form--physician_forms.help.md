# Diabetes Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients about their medical history, medications, and risk factors related to diabetes, as well as their blood pressure and glucose levels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Complete each section of the form according to the instructions provided.
3. For each field, please enter the relevant information, taking care to fill in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`Patient Information`, text, required: false): Enter your name, date of birth, and other relevant identifying information.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, required: false): Describe any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies you have.
* **Blood Pressure** (`Blood Pressure`, number, required: false): Record your current blood pressure reading.
* **Blood Glucose** (`Blood Glucose`, number, required: false): Record your current blood glucose reading.
* **Medications** (`Medications`, text, required: false): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, text, required: false): List any known allergies or sensitivities.
* **Medical Family History** (`Medical Family History`, text, required: false): Describe any relevant medical history in your family.
* **Risk Factors** (`Risk Factors`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose any of the following risk factors that apply to you:
	+ High blood pressure
	+ Obesity
	+ Physical inactivity
	+ Family history of diabetes
	+ Other
* **Screening Tests** (`Screening Tests`, select_one, required: true): Answer True or False to indicate whether you have undergone screening tests for diabetes.
* **Results** (`Results`, note, required: false): Use this field to record any results or comments from your healthcare provider.
* **Next Steps** (`Next Steps`, text, required: false): Describe any next steps or follow-up appointments you have scheduled with your healthcare provider.

Note: Please fill in all required fields, and if you're unsure about any field, consult your healthcare provider for guidance.
