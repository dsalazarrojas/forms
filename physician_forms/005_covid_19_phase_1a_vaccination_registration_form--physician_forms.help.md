# COVID 19 Phase 1A Vaccination Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help individuals register for the COVID-19 vaccination, specifically for Phase 1A. Phase 1A includes specific age groups and high-risk individuals. The form is designed to gather essential information from registrants to ensure they receive the correct vaccination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, users will need to provide the following information: Full name, date of birth, job title/occupation, employment category, employer name, primary phone number, email address, known allergies to vaccines or vaccine components, current pregnancy or breastfeeding status, consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccination, and confirmation of the accuracy of the provided information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Job Title/Occupation**: Describe your job title or occupation.
* **Employment Category**: Select one of the provided options that apply to you:
	+ Healthcare Worker
	+ First Responder
	+ Long-term Care Resident
	+ Long-term Care Staff
	+ Other Frontline Worker
* **Employer Name**: Enter your employer's name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Primary Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Primary Healthcare Provider Name**: If you have a primary healthcare provider, enter their name (optional).
* **Allergies to Vaccines or Components**: If you have any known allergies to vaccines or vaccine components, select the option that applies:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Unsure
* **Pregnancy Status**: Select one of the provided options that reflect your current pregnancy status:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Not Applicable
* **Consent for Vaccination**: Select one of the provided options to indicate your consent for vaccination:
	+ True
	+ I consent
	+ False
	+ I do not consent
* **Confirmation of Information**: Confirm that the information you provided is true and correct.

Please be aware that all fields are required for the purpose of Phase 1A. If you have any issues or concerns, contact your healthcare provider.
