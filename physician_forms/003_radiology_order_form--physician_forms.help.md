# Radiology Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Radiology Order Form is used to order radiology tests, which are medical imaging procedures that help diagnose or monitor diseases. This form collects information about the patient, including name, age, gender, and reason for imaging, to ensure accurate and efficient completion of radiology tests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the required fields: patient name and imaging requested (X-ray or MRI).
2. Optional fields are patient age, gender, reason for imaging, and radiologist name. You may skip these fields if not necessary.
3. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, number, optional): Enter the patient's age, if known.
* **Patient Gender** (`patient_gender`, select_one, optional): Select the patient's gender (male or female).
* **Imaging Requested** (`imaging_requested`, select_multiple, required): Select the imaging tests needed (X-ray or MRI).
* **Reason for Imaging** (`reason_for_imaging`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for the imaging test.
* **Radiologist Name** (`radiologist_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the radiologist or healthcare professional performing the test.
* **Patient Condition** (`patient_condition`, note, optional): Enter any relevant patient condition or medical history that may impact the test result.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with a medical professional or healthcare provider.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and clarity to ensure smooth processing of the radiology test order.
