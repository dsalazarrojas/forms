# Hipaa Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hipaa Medical History Form is a tool designed to gather information about a patient's medical history, which is used to support the diagnosis and treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide patient contact information**: Fill in the patient's contact details, including their name, phone number, and other relevant contact information.
2. **List current symptoms**: Describe any current symptoms or health issues you are experiencing.
3. **List current medications**: List any medications you are currently taking, including their dosage and frequency.
4. **Report allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities you have.
5. **Disclose drug use**: Answer questions about your drug use, including any history of substance abuse or addiction.
6. **Share family medical history**: Provide information about your family's medical history, including any relevant health conditions or genetic predispositions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Contact
* **Patient Contact (text)**: This field is for entering the patient's contact information, including their name, phone number, and other relevant details.

### Current Symptoms
* **Current Symptoms (text)**: Describe any current symptoms or health issues you are experiencing. Be as detailed as possible, including when symptoms started, how long you've had them, and any other relevant information.

### Current Medications
* **Current Medications (text)**: List any medications you are currently taking, including their dosage and frequency. This could include prescription medications, over-the-counter medications, and supplements.

### Allergies
* **Allergies (text)**: List any allergies or sensitivities you have, including any triggers or substances that cause a reaction.

### Drug Use
* **Drug Use (text)**: Answer questions about your drug use, including history of substance abuse or addiction, if applicable.

### Family Medical History
* **Family Medical History (text)**: Provide information about your family's medical history, including any relevant health conditions or genetic predispositions. 

## Tips
* Be as thorough as possible when filling out this form.
* Double-check the information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact a healthcare professional for clarification.
* This form is intended to support the diagnosis and treatment process, but it is not a substitute for a face-to-face consultation with a healthcare provider.
