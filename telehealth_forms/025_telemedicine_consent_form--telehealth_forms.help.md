# telemedicine_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The telemedicine_consent_form is a form used to obtain patient consent for telemedicine services. This form is typically filled out by the patient before starting a telemedicine appointment or session.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting the form, please make sure you have reviewed and understand the information provided. If you need assistance or have questions, please don't hesitate to contact your healthcare provider.

* **patient_information_form**: A text field where you can enter additional patient information.
