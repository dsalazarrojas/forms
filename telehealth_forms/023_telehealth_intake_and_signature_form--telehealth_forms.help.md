# Telehealth Intake And Signature Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telehealth Intake And Signature Form is designed to gather essential information from patients and obtain their consent for telehealth services. This form is a crucial document that ensures patients understand their role in the telehealth process and the services they will receive.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the form**: Take a moment to review the form and make sure you understand each field and its purpose.
2. **Submit your response**: Select the appropriate answers for each field and submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Text)**: This field is for providing your name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Do you consent to telehealth services? (Select Multiple)**: Please select "Yes" if you consent to receiving telehealth services, or "No" if you do not. If you select "No", you will not be eligible to receive telehealth services.
* **Medical History (Text)**: Describe any medical conditions, allergies, or other relevant health information.
* **Allergies (Text)**: List any allergies you have or have had in the past.
* **Medications (Text)**: List any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.
* **Medical Conditions (Text)**: Describe any medical conditions you have or have had in the past.
* **Signature (Select One)**: Please sign here to acknowledge that you have read and understood the telehealth services agreement. Select "Telehealth Services" if you are signing for telehealth services, or "Other" if you are signing for a different reason.
* **Patient Signature (Select One)**: Please sign here to confirm that you have reviewed and understood the telehealth services agreement.
* **Signature (Text)**: Your digital signature will be used to confirm that you have reviewed and understood the telehealth services agreement.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer each field honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the telehealth services staff for clarification.
* This form is only to be used for gathering patient information and consent for telehealth services.
