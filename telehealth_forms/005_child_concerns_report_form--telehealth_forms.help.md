# Child Concerns Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Concerns Report Form is used to gather information about a child's concerns, such as behavioral issues, academic performance, or physical well-being. The form is completed by educators and staff to report their observations and provide recommendations for next steps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Reporter Information section with your name and contact information.
2. Provide the child's full name and current classroom or grade level.
3. Select all areas of concern for the child (e.g., Behavioral Issues, Academic Performance, etc.).
4. Describe the observations made, providing specific examples and context.
5. Enter the date of the primary observation.
6. Indicate whether the parents have been notified and select the relevant status.
7. If applicable, suggest next steps for the child's support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Reporter Information
* **Reporter Name**: Enter your name as the person making the report.
* **Reporter Name (optional)**: This field is not required.

### Child Details
* **Child Full Name**: Enter the name of the child being observed.
* **Classroom or Grade Level**: Enter the current classroom or grade level of the child.

### Observation Details
* **Areas of Concern**: Select all areas that apply to the child's concerns (e.g., Behavioral Issues, Academic Performance, etc.).

### Description of Observations
* **Description of Observations**: Provide specific examples and context of the child's concerns.
* **(Hint: Provide as much detail as possible)**

### Actions and Recommendations
* **Date of Primary Observation**: Enter the date of the primary observation.
* **Have Parents Been Notified?**: Indicate whether the parents have been notified and select the relevant status.
* **Next Steps Recommended**: If applicable, suggest next steps for the child's support.

### Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing the observations and providing context.
* Select all relevant areas of concern for the child.
* Ensure that the parents are notified and involved in the process.
* Clearly state the next steps recommended for the child's support.
