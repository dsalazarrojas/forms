# New Patient Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The New Patient Enrollment Form is a crucial document used to collect vital information about patients enrolling in our telehealth services. This form ensures that we have accurate and up-to-date details about each patient, enabling us to provide the best possible care and support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the form in English.
* Enter accurate and detailed information for each field.
* Make sure to provide at least one emergency contact person.
* Review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Personal Information
* **Patient Personal Information** (`personal_information`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Please enter your name, date of birth, and any other personal details you feel are necessary for our records.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Provide your current address, phone number, and any other contact details you would like to share.

### 2. Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact People** (`emergency_contact`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Choose one person to be your emergency contact in case of an emergency.
	+ Options: Mother, Father, Sister, Brother, Spouse, Other

### 3. Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Share your medical history, including any health conditions, medications, or allergies.
	+ This information will help us understand your health better and provide personalized care.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you need help or have any questions, contact our team.
* We will review and follow up on this form submission to ensure a smooth enrollment process.
