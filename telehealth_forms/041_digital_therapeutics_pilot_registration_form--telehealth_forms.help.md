# Digital Therapeutics Pilot Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information for a digital therapeutics pilot project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant information (page 1).
2. Provide your participant data (page 2).
3. Enter your contact details (page 3).
4. Specify your contact preference (page 4).
5. List the instruments you will use (page 5).
6. Choose the instrument you prefer (page 6).
7. Describe the type of instruments you will use (page 7).
8. Specify the model of the instruments you will use (page 8).
9. Enter the purchase date of the instruments (page 9).
10. Indicate whether the instruments are under warranty (page 10).
11. Describe the condition of the instruments (page 11).
12. Check if the instruments have been damaged (page 12).
13. Provide other relevant information about the instruments (page 13).
14. Sign the participant agreement (page 14).
15. Provide your consent (page 15).
16. Enter the date of your consent (page 16).
17. Specify who witnessed your consent (page 17).
18. Sign the instruments (page 19).
19. Enter the date of your signature (page 20).
20. Specify who witnessed your signature (page 21).
21. Enter any other relevant participant information (page 22).
22. Leave a note about your participation (page 23).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, required): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant participant details.
* **Participant Data** (`participant_data`, text, required): Provide any relevant data about your participation in the pilot project.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter your contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, text, required): Choose your preferred method of contact (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
* **Participant Instruments** (`participant_instruments`, text, required): List the instruments you will use for the pilot project.
* **Instrument Preference** (`contact_instrument`, text, required): Choose the instrument you prefer to use.
* **Instruments Type** (`participant_instruments_type`, text, required): Describe the type of instruments you will use (e.g., software, hardware, or other).
* **Instruments Model** (`participant_instruments_model`, text, required): Specify the model of the instruments you will use.
* **Instruments Version** (`participant_instruments_version`, text, required): Enter the version of the instruments you will use.
* **Instruments Serial Number** (`participant_instruments_serial_number`, text, required): Enter the serial number of the instruments you will use.
* **Purchase Date** (`participant_instruments_purchase_date`, text, required): Enter the date you purchased the instruments.
* **Instruments Warranty** (`participant_instruments_warranty`, text, required): Indicate whether the instruments are under warranty.
* **Instruments Condition** (`participant_instruments_condition`, text, required): Describe the condition of the instruments.
* **Instruments Damage** (`participant_instruments_damage`, text, required): Check if the instruments have been damaged.
* **Other Instruments Information** (`participant_instruments_other`, text, required): Provide any other relevant information about the instruments.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, text, required): Sign the participant agreement.
* **Consent** (`participant_consent`, text, required): Provide your consent to participate in the pilot project.
* **Consent Date** (`participant_consent_date`, text, required): Enter the date of your consent.
* **Consent Witness** (`participant_consent_witness`, text, required): Specify who witnessed your consent.
* **Signature** (`participant_instruments_signature`, text, required): Sign the participant instrument.
* **Signature Date** (`participant_instruments_signature_date`, text, required): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Signature Witness** (`participant_instruments_signature_witness`, text, required): Specify who witnessed your signature.
* **Other Participant Information** (`participant_other`, text, required): Enter any other relevant participant information.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, text, required): Leave a note about your participation.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required): Sign the participant signature.

## Tips
Please fill in the form accurately and honestly. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the project team.
