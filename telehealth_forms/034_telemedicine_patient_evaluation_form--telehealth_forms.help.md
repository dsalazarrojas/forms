# telemedicine_patient_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medical information from patients in a telemedicine setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in the medical history, symptoms, allergies, medications, and patient information sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (1, text, required): This section is used to collect information about your medical history, including any previous surgeries, hospitalizations, or medical events.
* **Symptoms** (2, text, required): Describe your symptoms, including any physical or emotional sensations you are experiencing.
* **Allergies** (3, text, required): List any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Medications** (4, text, required): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Patient Info** (9, text, required): Provide your patient information, including your name, date of birth, and contact information.

Note: The fields with the same labels but with increasing numbers are removed to avoid confusion. The form is simplified to make it easier to understand and fill out.
