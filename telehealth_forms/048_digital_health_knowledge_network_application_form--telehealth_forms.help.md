# Digital Health Knowledge Network Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Health Knowledge Network Application Form is designed to help you join the Digital Health Knowledge Network, a platform that connects people with shared interests in digital health. Please read this help guide to understand how to complete the form and maximize your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

* Click on each field to select or enter your information.
* For text fields, type in your response.
* For select options, choose one or more of the available options.
* Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Contact Details**
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your contact information (e.g., name, email, phone number).

### 2. **Roles**
* **Roles** (`roles`, select multiple, optional): Select one or more roles that best describe your interests and expertise in digital health (e.g., Clinician, Researcher, Patient Advocate).

### 3. **Interests**
* **Interests** (`interests`, text, optional): Describe your interests in digital health (e.g., health informatics, health policy, digital therapeutics).

### 4. **Motivations**
* **Motivations** (`motivations`, text, optional): Explain why you are interested in joining the Digital Health Knowledge Network.

### 5. **Terms of Consent**
* **Terms of Consent** (`terms_consent`, select one, optional): Select the option that best describes your level of understanding of the Digital Health Knowledge Network's terms and conditions (e.g., I understand, I have questions, I'm not sure).

### 6. **Date of Birth**
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1990-01-01).

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
* Make sure to save your progress and revisit this form if needed.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask us for clarification.
