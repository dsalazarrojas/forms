## Purpose
This form is designed for clients and birth workers (doulas) to provide information and agreements for virtual doula services. Its purpose is to establish a clear understanding of the client's and birth worker's expectations, roles, and responsibilities in the virtual doula-client relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "Birth Worker Info" and "Client Info" sections with the necessary details.
2.  Provide the birth plan and any other relevant information in the "Birth Info" and "Birth Plan" sections.
3.  Enter the doula's information in the "Doula Info" section.
4.  Specify the contact information in the "Contact Info" section.
5.  In case of an emergency, provide the necessary contact information in the "Emergency Info" section.
6.  Read and agree to the "Birth Worker Agreement" and "Client Agreement" sections.
7.  Sign and date the "Birth Worker Signature" and "Client Signature" sections.
8.  Enter any additional information in the "Birth Worker Info 2" and "Contact Info 2" sections.
9.  Specify the payment details in the "Payment Info" and "Payment Details" sections.
10.  Agree to the payment terms and payment agreement in the "Payment Terms" and "Payment Agreement" sections.
11.  Sign and date the "Birth Worker Signature 2" and "Client Signature 2" sections.
