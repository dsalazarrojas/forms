# Telehealth Counseling Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telehealth Counseling Consent Form is a document that requires patients to provide consent for telehealth counseling services. This form ensures that patients understand their rights, responsibilities, and the services they will receive.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Read and understand the form**: Take a moment to review each section before you start filling out the form.
2. Fill out each section carefully**: Enter your information accurately and completely.
3. Sign and date the form**: Make sure to sign and date the form once you've completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Patient Consent
* **Patient Consent** (`Patient Consent`, `text`, required): This section requires the patient to acknowledge that they have read, understand, and agree to the terms and conditions of the telehealth counseling service. Please explain that you understand the services offered, any potential risks or benefits, and your role in the process.

### Page 2: Patient Information
* **Patient Information** (`Patient Information`, `text`, required): Here, you will enter your name, email, and other relevant contact information. Please ensure that your information is accurate and up-to-date.

### Page 3: Provider Information
* **Provider Information** (`Provider Information`, `text`, required): In this section, you will be asked to provide information about the provider(s) you will be working with. Please ensure that the provider's name, title, and contact information is accurate.

### Page 4: Provider Phone
* **Provider Phone** (`Provider Phone`, `text`, required): This section requires you to provide the provider's phone number. Ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
* This form is a requirement for participating in the telehealth counseling service. Please ensure that it is completed accurately and thoroughly.
