# Telemedicine Best Practices Forum Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for registering users for the Telemedicine Best Practices Forum. It is intended for healthcare professionals interested in or required to attend the forum.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your badge.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Confirm your email address.
4. Select all topics that interest you.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Confirm your phone number.
7. Enter any comments or additional information.
