# Telemedicine Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Telemedicine Appointment Form is designed to gather necessary information for scheduling a telemedicine appointment with a healthcare provider. Please complete this form to schedule your appointment and ensure that our team can efficiently process your request.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select a Doctor**: Choose the healthcare provider you would like to schedule an appointment with from the dropdown list. Please select Dr. Smith, Dr. Johnson, or Dr. Wilson.
2. **Select Date and Time**: Choose a date and time for your appointment that is convenient for you. This will be the date and time of your telemedicine appointment.
3. **Enter Email and Phone Number**: Enter your email address and phone number so our team can contact you with appointment details and schedule reminders.
4. **Add Note to Doctor**: If you have any specific questions or concerns, you can add a note for the doctor's reference.
5. **Confirm Information**: Confirm your email and phone number with our team to ensure that we have the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select a Doctor** (`Select Doctor`, `select_one`, required): Choose a healthcare provider from the dropdown list.
* **Select Date** (`Select Date`, `date`, required): Choose a date for your appointment.
* **Select Time** (`Select Time`, `time`, required): Choose a time for your appointment.
* **Note to Doctor** (`Note to Doctor`, `note`, optional): Enter any questions or concerns for the doctor's reference.
* **Patient Email** (`Patient Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so our team can contact you with appointment details and schedule reminders.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for our team to contact you.
* **Calendar** (`Calendar`, `text`, optional): Add any calendar events or reminders related to your appointment.
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, `text`, optional): Enter any other information you'd like to share with our team.
* **Confirm Email** (`Confirm Email`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that we have your correct email address.
* **Confirm Phone** (`Confirm Phone`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that we have your correct phone number.
