# Health Insurance Verification Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's and policyholder's insurance details for verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the patient's information in the fields marked as "Patient" and "Patient Insurance".
2. Enter the policyholder's information in the fields marked as "Policyholder" and "Policyholder Insurance".
3. If you have any other insurance details, please enter them in the fields marked as "Insurance Info".
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your information is accurately verified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient info**
*   **Patient name** (`patient_name`): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
*   **Patient birthdate** (`patient_birthdate`): Enter the patient's birthdate in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*   **Patient insurer name** (`patient_insurer_name`): Enter the name of the patient's insurance provider.
*   **Patient insurer start date** (`patient_insurer_date`): Enter the start date of the patient's insurance coverage.
*   **Policyholder info**
*   **Policyholder name** (`policyholder_name`): Enter the policyholder's name as it appears on their identification.
*   **Policyholder birthdate** (`policyholder_birthdate`): Enter the policyholder's birthdate in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*   **Policyholder insurer name** (`policyholder_insurer_name`): Enter the name of the policyholder's insurance provider.
*   **Policyholder insurer start date** (`policyholder_insurer_date`): Enter the start date of the policyholder's insurance coverage.
*   **Policyholder insurer phone** (`policyholder_insurer_phone`): Enter the policyholder's insurance provider phone number.
*   **Policyholder insurer email** (`policyholder_insurer_email`): Enter the policyholder's insurance provider email.
*   **Policyholder insurer end date** (`policyholder_insurer_date_2`): Enter the end date of the policyholder's insurance coverage.
*   **Policyholder employer name** (`policyholder_employer_name`): Enter the name of the policyholder's employer.
*   **Policyholder employer phone** (`policyholder_employer_phone`): Enter the policyholder's employer phone number.
*   **Policyholder relationship** (`policyholder_relationship`): Enter the relationship between the policyholder and the patient.
*   **Insurance info**
*   **Insurance info** (`insurance_info`): Enter any additional insurance details.
*   **Policyholder Insurer name 2** (`policyholder_insurer_name_2`): Enter the name of the policyholder's second insurance provider.
*   **Policyholder Insurer start date 2** (`policyholder_insurer_date_2`): Enter the start date of the policyholder's second insurance coverage.
*   **Policyholder Insurer end date 2** (`policyholder_insurer_date_2_2`): Enter the end date of the policyholder's second insurance coverage.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure that your information is verified correctly.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the instructions above or ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
