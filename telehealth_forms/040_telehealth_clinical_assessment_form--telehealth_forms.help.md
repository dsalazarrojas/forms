# telehealth Clinical Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "telehealth Clinical Assessment Form" is designed to collect information about a patient's medical condition, status, location, vital signs, symptoms, allergies, medication, and medical history. It helps healthcare professionals assess a patient's condition and provide accurate diagnosis and treatment recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the relevant fields for the patient's medical condition.
2. Choose the patient's status (Critical, Stable, or Unstable) in the "Patient Status" field.
3. Select the patient's current location (Home, Hospital, Clinic, or Ambulatory) in the "Patient Location" field.
4. Enter the patient's vital signs in the "Vital Signs" field.
5. Describe any symptoms the patient is experiencing in the "Symptoms" field.
6. List any allergies the patient has in the "Allergies" field.
7. Enter the name, dosage, and start date of any medications the patient is currently taking in the "Medication" field.
8. Provide a brief history of the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the patient's medical condition.
* **Patient Status** (`Patient Status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the patient's status (Critical, Stable, or Unstable) to help healthcare professionals understand their condition.
* **Patient Location** (`Patient Location`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the patient's current location (Home, Hospital, Clinic, or Ambulatory) to ensure that healthcare professionals understand their environment.
* **Vital Signs** (`Vital Signs`, `date`, required: false): Enter the patient's vital signs (e.g., blood pressure, temperature, etc.) to monitor their condition.
* **Symptoms** (`Symptoms`, `text`, required: false): Describe any symptoms the patient is experiencing to help healthcare professionals understand their condition.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, `select_one`, required: false): List any allergies the patient has, including pollen, dust, or medications.
* **Medication** (`Medication`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name, dosage, and start date of any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `note`, required: false): Provide a brief history of the patient's medical history to help healthcare professionals understand their condition.
