# New Patient Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Patient Appointment Form is designed to gather essential information from patients before scheduling an appointment. It helps healthcare providers prepare for the appointment and ensures that the patient's needs and preferences are met.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address**. These fields are required to identify you and ensure secure communication.
2. Enter your **Phone Number** so we can reach you to confirm your appointment.
3. Select your preferred **Date** for the appointment. Choose a date that works for you and let us know if you have any specific preferences.
4. Choose a **Time** for your appointment. Be as specific as possible, taking into account any time-sensitive procedures or requirements.
5. Specify the **Location** where you'd like to have your appointment.
6. Enter the desired **Appointment Date** and **Time** for your appointment. This will help us schedule your appointment efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or identification card.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or identification card.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for appointment confirmations and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach you by phone to confirm your appointment.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Choose a date that works for you, taking into account any scheduling conflicts or requirements.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a time for your appointment, considering any necessary preparation or constraints.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Specify the location where you'd like to have your appointment, such as your home or one of our offices.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you're requesting for your appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional): Choose the time for your appointment, considering any necessary preparation or constraints.
