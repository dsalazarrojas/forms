# Women's Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather comprehensive information about a patient's women's health, facilitating better care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Routine Check** (Routine Check, note, required: false): Provide any relevant information about the patient's routine check-up, including any notable findings or concerns.
2. **Medical History** (Medical History, number, required: false): Enter the number of years since your last medical check-up.
3. **Symptoms** (Symptoms, text, required: false): Describe any symptoms you are experiencing, such as pain, discomfort, or other health-related issues.
4. **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if you have any of the following medical conditions:
	* Yes
	* No
5. **Med Allergies** (Med Allergies, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if you have any of the following allergies:
	* Yes
	* No
6. **Med Prescriptions** (Med Prescriptions, select_multiple, required: false): Check if you are currently taking any of the following medications:
	* Yes
	* No
7. **Lab Results** (Lab Results, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if you have any of the following lab results:
	* Yes
	* No
8. **Social History** (Social History, text, required: false): Provide information about your social history, including any relevant relationships or living arrangements.
9. **Family Info** (Family Info, text, required: false): Enter any relevant family information.
10. **Med Notes** (Med Notes, text, required: false): Provide any additional medical notes or concerns.
