# Virtual Intake Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Intake Survey is used to gather information from clients or patients about their virtual sessions. This information will help providers tailor their services to meet the client's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is being collected.
2. Fill out the required fields (indicated by a *asterisk*).
3. Select the relevant options for each field.
4. Submit the form to provide your information to the provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (Text, required/optional): Enter your name as you would like to be referred to by the provider.
* **email** (Email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted by the provider or if you want to receive updates about your sessions.
* **session_type** (Select One, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you are comfortable with virtual sessions, and 'No' if you are not.
* **Session Type** (Select Multiple, optional): Select the types of sessions you are interested in: Virtual, In-person, or Phone.
* **session_length** (Number, optional): If you have a preferred session length, enter it here. This will help the provider plan your sessions accordingly.
* **date** (Date, optional): Choose a date for your session.
* **time** (Time, optional): Choose a time for your session.
* **consent** (Select One, optional): Choose 'Yes' to consent to the terms and conditions of your sessions, and 'No' if you do not.
* **Consent Details** (Text, optional): If you have any additional questions or concerns about consent, enter them here.
* **phone** (Text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by the provider.
* **preferred_contact** (Select One, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted: Phone, Email, or Text.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (Select Multiple, optional): Select the contact methods you are interested in: Phone, Email, or Text.
* **client_details** (Note, optional): Enter any additional details about yourself that you think the provider should know.
* **client_preferences** (Note, optional): Enter any preferences you have for your sessions, such as favorite times or topics.
* **session_frequency** (Select One, optional): Choose how frequently you would like to have sessions: Weekly, Every 2 weeks, or Every 4 weeks.
* **Session Frequency** (Select Multiple, optional): Select the frequencies you are interested in: Weekly, Every 2 weeks, or Every 4 weeks.
* **Client Session Preferences** (Select Multiple, optional): Select any preferences you have for your sessions, such as Weekdays or Weekends.
* **session_status** (Select One, optional): Choose the status of your sessions: Pending, In Progress, or Completed.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your provider for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you have completed all required fields.
