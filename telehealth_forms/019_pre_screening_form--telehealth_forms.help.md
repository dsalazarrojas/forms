# Pre Screening Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pre Screening Form is designed to collect information about a patient's contact and medical history. This form is used to gather essential details that will help our medical professionals assess a patient's condition before scheduling a consultation or appointment. Completing this form thoroughly and accurately will ensure that you receive the best possible care and attention from our medical team.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by selecting "Contact Info"**: Click on the "Contact Info" page to begin the form.
2. **Enter your contact information**: Fill in your name, phone number, and email address (if available) to help us get in touch with you if needed.
3. **Proceed to "Medical History" pages**: Click on each "Medical History" page to provide detailed information about your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions, medications, or allergies.
4. **Select your relevant medical history information**: Choose the options that best apply to you for each medical history page.
5. **Review and submit your form**: Double-check your responses and submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Contact Info

* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, phone number, and email address to enable us to contact you if needed.

### Medical History

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable medical conditions from the options provided.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history2`, select_one, required/optional): Choose a relevant medical condition from the options provided.
* This process continues for all other "Medical History" fields.

**Tips**

* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately to ensure your medical information is up-to-date and reliable.
* If you're unsure about any medical condition or history, consult with your primary care physician or healthcare provider for guidance.
* Review and submit your form carefully to avoid any potential issues or delays in your care.

Note: The fields with `required: false` are not mandatory, but it's still essential to provide as much information as possible to get the best care from our medical team.
