# Virtual Health Summit Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Health Summit Recording Consent Form is a mandatory form for participants to provide their information and recording preferences for the summit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the on-screen instructions and fill out the required fields. Each page has a brief explanation and instructions on the bottom.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Introduction**: This is the first page of the form, which provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
  * Required: false

- **Participant Information**: Please select whether you will be participating in the summit. 
  * Label: Participant Information
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: true
  * Options: Yes, No

- **Recording Consent**: Please choose whether you consent to the recording of your session.
  * Label: Recording Consent
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: true
  * Options: I have read, Do not allow the recording of my session

- **Date Time**: Enter your date and time of participation.
  * Label: Date Time
  * Type: date
  * Required: true

- **E-Mail**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
  * Label: E-Mail
  * Type: email
  * Required: false

- **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
  * Label: Phone
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

- **Name**: Enter your name.
  * Label: Name
  * Type: text
  * Required: true

- **Organization**: Select whether your organization is participating.
  * Label: Organization
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: false
  * Options: Yes, No

- **Title**: Enter your job title or position (optional).
  * Label: Title
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

- **Role**: Enter your role in your organization (optional).
  * Label: Role
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

- **Location**: Enter your location (optional).
  * Label: Location
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

- **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information (optional).
  * Label: Comments
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

## Tips

- Please fill out this form carefully and honestly.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields or options, please ask the organizers for clarification.
- The recording of sessions will only be made if you consent to it.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask the organizers for assistance.
