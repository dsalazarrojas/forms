# Telegroup Guidelines And Agreement (ENGSPANISH) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient information and medical history for a telehealth service. The purpose of this form is to provide the necessary information for the healthcare provider to assess the patient's medical needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the patient's information section, including their name, address, email, and phone number.
2. Answer the medical history questions, including any previous medical conditions or allergies.
3. Indicate your contact preferences, such as how you would like to be contacted.
4. Provide emergency contact information, such as a family member's name and phone number.
5. Review and agree to the patient consent statement.
6. Complete any additional medical history sections as needed.
7. Provide your signature and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required): This section is where you will enter your name, address, email, and phone number.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): This section is where you will provide any previous medical conditions or allergies.
* **Treatment Options** (`treatment_options`, select_one, required): Indicate your treatment preferences, such as whether you prefer in-person or online appointments.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, text, required): Specify how you would like to be contacted, such as phone, email, or mail.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Provide the name and phone number of a family member or other emergency contact.
* **Patient Consent** (`patient_consent`, select_multiple, required): Review and agree to the patient consent statement.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): This section is for any additional contact information, such as a friend or family member's name and phone number.
* **Insurance** (`insurance`, text, required): Specify your insurance information, such as provider and policy number.
* **Billing** (`billing`, text, required): This section is for billing information, such as payment method and account number.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Provide your digital signature and date.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have a medical condition.
* **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`, select_multiple, required): Specify the medical specialty of the healthcare provider.
* **Doctor** (`doctor`, text, required): Provide the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Medical History 1-7** (`medical_history_1-7`, text, required): Provide any additional medical history information.
* **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`, select_multiple, required): This section is for medical specialty information.
