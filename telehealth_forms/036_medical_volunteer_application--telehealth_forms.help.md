# Medical Volunteer Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Volunteer Application form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers for medical-related activities. This includes their personal and contact details, medical experience and license, availability for volunteering, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide information about your medical license, if applicable.
3. Describe your medical experience and skills.
4. Choose your availability for volunteering.
5. Select the specialty area you are interested in or skilled in.
6. Enter your address, city, and zip code.
7. Provide information about your emergency contact person, including their name, relation, phone number, and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it is spelled on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name as it is spelled on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Medical License**: If you have a medical license, please enter the details of your license, including your license number and expiration date.
* **Experience**: Describe your medical experience, including any relevant training or certifications.
* **Availability**: Select all the days and time slots you are available to volunteer.
* **Specialty**: Select the area of medicine you are most interested in or have experience in.
* **Address**: Enter your home address for contact and communication purposes.
* **City**: Enter the city where you live.
* **State**: Enter the state where you live.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code for mailing and shipping purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relation**: Select the relation of the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, spouse, friend).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.

**Tips**

* Please be honest and accurate when providing information about yourself and your medical experience.
* Ensure that you provide all required fields to avoid any delays in processing your application.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact us at [support email/phone number].
