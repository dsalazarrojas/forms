# Sleep Study Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for patients to provide their consent and information to participate in sleep-related research studies. It is a vital part of the research process, ensuring that both the patient and the researcher are on the same page regarding the study's details and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review the Form:** Carefully read through each section to understand the content and requirements.
2. **Gather Necessary Information:** Ensure you have all the necessary details before proceeding with the form.
3. **Select Relevant Answers:** Choose your answers accurately and honestly to the questions asked.
4. **Sign the Form:** When finished, sign your name to indicate your consent and understanding of the study details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for providing your basic contact information and any other relevant details requested by the researcher. Please ensure you fill this in accurately and completely.
  
- **Research Study** (`research_study`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question asks whether you are interested in participating in a research study. Select "Yes" if you are, "No" if you are not. Please answer honestly.
  
- **Study Details** (`study_details`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Here, you can select the details about the study that interest you or that you are available for. Please ensure you choose relevant options accurately.
  
- **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `note`, required/optional): This is where you sign your name to indicate that you have read and understood the study details and your commitment to the study. Please sign your name clearly and legibly.

- **Researcher Signature** (`researcher_signature`, `note`, required/optional): This section is for the researcher to sign, indicating their agreement with your participation and their commitment to the study's conduct. Please ensure the researcher signs accurately.

**Note:** The form's fields and explanations are designed to ensure clarity and understanding. If you have any questions or need further clarification, do not hesitate to contact the researcher or the relevant authorities.
