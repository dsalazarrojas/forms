# Telehealth Services Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telehealth Services Agreement Form is a document that patients must fill out to indicate their consent for receiving telehealth services, including video visits, phone calls, and messaging.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the form carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2.  Select the options that apply to you for each question.
3.  Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **I consent to receive telehealth services** (<type: select_one>, <name: telehealth_services_agreement>): This question asks if you consent to receive telehealth services. Select "Yes" if you are interested in telehealth services, and "No" if you are not.

- **I understand that telehealth services may involve** (<type: note, <name: patient_consent>): This section is a note that explains that telehealth services may involve various services, including video visits, phone calls, and messaging. This is not a question to answer.

- **I understand that my telehealth provider may** (<type: note, <name: patient_consent_2>): This section is a note that explains what your telehealth provider may do or ask you to do. This is not a question to answer.

- **If I am experiencing a medical emergency** (<type: select_one, <name: emergency_contact>): If you are experiencing a medical emergency, select the option that best suits your situation. Options include calling 911, contacting your emergency contact, or contacting your telehealth provider.

- **Contact person** (<type: select_multiple, <name: emergency_contact_2>): If you select the option to contact your emergency contact, you can choose multiple contact persons who will be informed of your situation.

- **Contact information** (<type: text, <name: emergency_contact_3>): If you select the option to contact your emergency contact, please provide their contact information, such as their phone number.

- **I am interested in the following telehealth services** (<type: select_multiple, <name: telehealth_services>): This question asks which telehealth services you are interested in. Select all that apply, such as video visits, phone calls, and messaging.

- **Which of the following telehealth services do you need or want** (<type: select_multiple, <name: telehealth_services_2>): Similar to the previous question, this question asks which specific services you are interested in.

- **I would like to schedule with a telehealth provider who** (<type: select_one, <name: telehealth_provider>): This question asks which characteristics you prefer in a telehealth provider. Select one that best suits your needs, such as someone who speaks your language, has your insurance, or has experience with your condition.

- **What type of telehealth provider would you like to schedule with** (<type: select_one, <name: telehealth_provider_2>): This question asks which type of provider you would like to schedule with, such as a doctor, nurse practitioner, or P.A.

- **What is your preferred method of communication** (<type: select_one, <name: telehealth_provider_3>): This question asks which method of communication you prefer, such as phone, video, or messaging.

- **How soon would you like to schedule your telehealth appointment** (<type: select_one, <name: telehealth_provider_4>): This question asks how soon you would like to schedule your telehealth appointment, such as the same day, next day, or this week.

- **On which days are you available for telehealth services** (<type: select_multiple, <name: telehealth_provider_5>): This question asks which days of the week you are available for telehealth services, such as Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, or Friday.

- **What time of day are you available for telehealth services** (<type: select_multiple, <name: telehealth_provider_6>): This question asks which time of day you are available for telehealth services, such as morning, afternoon, or evening.

# Tips
Please take the time to read and answer the questions carefully. This will help you get the most out of the telehealth services you are requesting. If you have any doubts or concerns, contact your telehealth provider directly.
