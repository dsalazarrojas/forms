# Telehealth Therapist Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to onboard new telehealth therapists into our system, collecting general information, contact details, and other necessary data for a smooth integration into our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as follows:
1. General Info: Enter your general information in this field.
2. Contact Details: Fill out your contact information, such as address and email.
3. Documentation: Please provide any relevant documentation or attachments.
4. Confirmation: Confirm the information you have entered above.
5. Additional Info: Provide any additional information that might be helpful for us.
6. Email: Enter your email address.
7. Phone: Enter your phone number.
8. Address: Enter your address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info (1)**: Enter your general information, such as your name and credentials.
* **Contact Details (2)**: Fill out your contact information, such as address and email. Please ensure that your email address is entered correctly.
* **Documentation (3)**: Please provide any relevant documentation or attachments that support your credentials or experience as a telehealth therapist.
* **Confirmation (4)**: Confirm the information you have entered above to ensure accuracy.
* **Additional Info (5)**: Provide any additional information that might be helpful for us in your onboarding process.
* **Email (6)**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and access to your account.
* **Phone (7)**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication and verification purposes.
* **Address (8)**: Enter your physical address, which will be used for shipping and billing purposes, if necessary.
