# ortal_joint_study_total_hip_replacement_initial_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect study information and patient details related to total hip replacement surgery. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide study information (page 1).
2. Fill out patient information fields (pages 2-5).
3. Enter date and time of visit (pages 6-7).
4. Add any relevant patient notes (page 8).
5. Specify patient goals (page 9).
6. Select patient status (page 10).
7. Enter surgical notes (page 11).
8. Choose the anesthesia method (page 12).
9. Select the surgical team (page 13).
10. Enter patient ID and medical chart number (pages 14-15).
11. Fill out additional patient information fields (pages 16-23).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Information** (page 1): Enter the study details as provided.
* **Patient Info 1** (page 2): Select the relevant option for the patient's status.
* **Patient Info 2** (page 3): Enter any relevant information about the patient.
* **Patient Info 3** (page 4): Enter the patient's email address, if applicable.
* **Date of Visit** (page 6): Enter the date of the visit in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time of Visit** (page 7): Enter the time of the visit in 24-hour format.
* **Patient Notes** (page 8): Enter any notes about the patient's condition or treatment.
* **Patient Goals** (page 9): Specify the patient's goals related to their surgery.
* **Patient Status** (page 10): Select the patient's status (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
* **Surgeon Notes** (page 11): Enter any notes about the surgery or patient's condition.
* **Anesthesia** (page 12): Enter the method of anesthesia used.
* **Surgical Team** (page 13): Select the surgical team member.
* **Patient ID** (page 14): Enter the patient's ID number.
* **Medical Chart Number** (page 15): Enter the patient's medical chart number.
* **Patient Info 5-13** (pages 16-23): Enter any additional relevant information about the patient.
