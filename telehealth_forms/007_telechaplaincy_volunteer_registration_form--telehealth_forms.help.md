# Telechaplaincy Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telechaplaincy Volunteer Registration Form is designed to collect information from potential telechaplaincy volunteers. This form helps us understand your interests and availability for telechaplaincy volunteer work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and email address.
2. Select your role in telechaplaincy (Chaplain, Volunteer, or Other).
3. Choose where you would like to be assigned (Home Office, Hospital, Clinic, or Other).
4. Select your availability for telechaplaincy volunteer work (no specific instructions are provided for this field, but it's assumed you have a standard 24-hour clock in mind).
5. Specify your volunteer start date.
6. Provide the time you would like to start your volunteer work.
7. Add any special notes or comments about your preferences.
8. If applicable, specify any tools you are comfortable using for telechaplaincy work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Telechaplaincy Form Title**: This is the title of the form, which is already filled out as "Telechaplaincy Volunteer Registration Form". You cannot edit this field.
* **Name (name)**: Enter your name as you would like to be known in the telechaplaincy system.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for telechaplaincy communications.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for urgent matters.
* **Role (role)**: Select your role in telechaplaincy. You can choose from Chaplain, Volunteer, or Other.
* **Location (location)**: Select where you would like to be assigned for telechaplaincy work. Choose from Home Office, Hospital, Clinic, or Other.
* **Availability (availability)**: This is likely a field for selecting your availability for telechaplaincy work. However, the options are not specified, so assume you can enter any value you like.
* **Volunteer Start Date (volunteer_start_date)**: Enter the date you would like to start your telechaplaincy volunteer work.
* **Volunteer Start Time (volunteer_start_time)**: Enter the time you would like to start your telechaplaincy volunteer work.
* **Note (note)**: Enter any special notes or comments about your preferences or experiences.
* **Assigned Tool (assigned_tool)**: This field is likely for specifying any tools or equipment you are comfortable using for telechaplaincy work. However, no further information is provided.
* **Form IDs (form_ids)**: This field is likely for tracking or referencing the form submission ID. It's not intended for end-user input.
