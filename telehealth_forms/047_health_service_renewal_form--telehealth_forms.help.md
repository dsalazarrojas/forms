# Health Service Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to renew a patient's health services. It collects essential information from the patient, which is then used to update their service status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** (required) in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your **Last Name** (required) in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your **Email** (required) in the field labeled "Email", following the standard email format (username@domain.com).
4. Enter your **Phone** (required) in the field labeled "Phone".
5. Select the **Service Type** (required) that you are renewing, from the options provided.
6. Enter your **Renewal Date** (required) in the date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
7. Select the **Renewal Period** (required) for your service renewal, from the options provided.
8. Select your **Preferred Contact** (required) method from the options provided.
9. If needed, provide any additional **Notes** (optional) about your service renewal.
9. Finally, select the person you would like your service to be **Assigned To** (required) from the options provided.

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address in the standard format (username@domain.com).
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Service Type** (Service Type, select_multiple, required): Select the type of service you are renewing. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Renewal Date** (Renewal Date, date, required): Enter the date you would like your service to be renewed in the date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Renewal Period** (Renewal Period, select_one, required): Select the renewal period for your service from the options provided. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Preferred Contact** (Preferred Contact, select_one, required): Select how you would like to be contacted from the options provided. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Provide any additional information about your service renewal.
* **Assigned To** (Assigned To, select_one, required): Select the person you would like to be assigned to handle your service renewal from the options provided. Options include:
