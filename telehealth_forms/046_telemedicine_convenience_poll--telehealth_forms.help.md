# Telemedicine Convenience Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user feedback on their experiences with telemedicine services, aiming to identify areas of improvement and optimize service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the frequency of your telemedicine usage.
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with your telemedicine experience on a scale of 1-5.
3. Rate the quality of services provided by the telehealth organization.
4. Provide a brief explanation for your rating of our services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How often do you use telemedicine?** (`telemedicine_convenience_poll_form`, `text`, optional):
	+ Please select how often you use telemedicine services. This will help us understand your usage patterns.
* **On a scale of 1-5, how would you rate your telemedicine experience?** (`telemedicine_convenience_rating`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with your telemedicine experience. Use the provided scale to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **How would you rate our services?** (`telemedicine_convenience_rating_how_did_we_do`, `text`, optional):
	+ Provide a rating for the quality of services provided by the telehealth organization.
* **Why did you rate our services as you did?** (`telemedicine_convenience_rating_why_did_we_rate`, `text`, optional):
	+ Please explain the reasoning behind your rating of our services.

## Tips
- Be honest in your ratings and responses. Your feedback is valuable to us.
- If you have any suggestions or comments, feel free to add them in the space provided.
