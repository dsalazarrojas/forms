# Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapy Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from therapy clients about their medical history, allergies, and other relevant details. This information helps the therapist understand the client's background and goals, allowing for more effective and personalized therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand each section and its purpose.
2. For each section, provide accurate and complete information about your medical history, allergies, and other relevant details.
3. Use the provided options for the "Allergies" section to select any allergies you have.
4. Be truthful and thorough in your answers, as this information will be used to provide you with the best possible care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Client Info
* **Client Info** (`client_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is intended for you to provide any additional information about yourself that may be helpful for your therapy sessions. This can include your contact information, medical insurance details, or any other relevant personal information.

### 2. Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): This section is a place to describe any medical conditions, previous illnesses, or surgeries you've experienced. Be as detailed as possible to ensure accurate information is provided.

### 3. Allergies
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select any allergies you have from the provided options (Peanut allergy, Pollen allergy, Shellfish allergy). If you have any other allergies not listed, please include them in the "Medical History" section.

### 4. Medications
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required: false): List any prescription medications, vitamins, or supplements you're currently taking. Include the dosage and frequency of use for each medication.

### 5. Reason for Visit
* **Reason for Visit** (`reason_for_visit`, `text`, required: false): Describe your reason for visiting the therapist in a few sentences or bullet points. This can include your goals for the therapy sessions, any concerns or issues you'd like to address, or your expectations from the therapy process.

### 6. Client Goals
* **Client Goals** (`client_goals`, `text`, required: false): Write down your goals and objectives for the therapy sessions. Be as specific and detailed as possible, including any areas you'd like to focus on or change during the therapy process.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers to ensure accurate information is provided.
* If you're unsure about any section or have questions, don't hesitate to ask your therapist for clarification.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure you've provided accurate and complete information.
