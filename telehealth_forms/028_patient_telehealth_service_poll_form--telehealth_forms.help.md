# Patient Telehealth Service Poll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Telehealth Service Poll Form is designed to collect feedback from patients who have received telehealth services. This form helps to gather information about the patient's experience, identify areas for improvement, and provide feedback on patient outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your name.
2. Enter the date of your telehealth visit.
3. Select the time of your telehealth visit.
4. Indicate the duration of your visit.
5. Rate your experience with the telehealth service.
6. Provide any additional comments about your visit.
7. If you experienced technical issues during your visit, select the type of issue you encountered.
8. Provide your email address and phone number.
9. Enter your feedback about your visit.
10. Enter the name of the clinic where you received service.
11. Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
12. Enter the name of the clinic staff.
13. Enter any technical notes about your visit.
14. Enter any additional feedback about your experience.
15. Enter the location of the clinic where you received service.
16. Select the type of visit (Routine, Emergency, Follow-up, or Other).
17. Enter the reason for your visit.
18. Enter the medical condition you were treated for.
19. Enter your current condition.
20. Select the outcome of your visit (Positive, Negative, or Neutral).
21. Indicate whether follow-up is needed.
22. Select the status of your follow-up (Scheduled, In Progress, Complete, or None).
23. Enter any comments or feedback about your experience.
24. Enter any other comments or feedback about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or medical records.
* **Visit Date**: Enter the date of your telehealth visit in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Visit Time**: Select the time of day of your telehealth visit.
* **Visit Duration**: Enter the length of your visit in minutes.
* **Rating**: Rate your experience with the telehealth service using the scale: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your visit.
* **Technical Issues**: If you experienced technical issues during your visit, select the type of issue you encountered (Audio, Video, or Both).
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Feedback**: Enter feedback about your visit.
* **Clinic**: Enter the name of the clinic where you received service.
* **Doctor**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
* **Staff**: Enter the name of the clinic staff member who assisted you.
* **Technical Notes**: Enter any technical notes about your visit.
* **Additional Feedback**: Enter any additional feedback about your experience.
* **Clinic Location**: Enter the location of the clinic where you received service.
* **Visit Type**: Select the type of visit (Routine, Emergency, Follow-up, or Other).
* **Reason for Visit**: Enter the reason for your visit.
* **Medical Condition**: Enter the medical condition you were treated for.
* **Patient Condition**: Enter your current condition.
* **Outcome**: Select the outcome of your visit (Positive, Negative, or Neutral).
* **Follow-up Needed**: Indicate whether follow-up is needed (True or False).
* **Follow-up Status**: Select the status of your follow-up (Scheduled, In Progress, Complete, or None).
* **Patient Survey**: Enter comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Additional Feedback**: Enter any other comments or feedback about your experience.
