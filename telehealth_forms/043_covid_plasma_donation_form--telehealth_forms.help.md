# COVID Plasma Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in donating plasma during the COVID-19 pandemic. The form gathers personal details, medical history, and consent for plasma donation. The purpose of this form is to ensure the safety and suitability of potential plasma donors.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1.  Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are essential for processing your application.
2.  Enter your name, date of birth, email address, phone number, and street address as per your identification documents.
3.  Report your COVID-19 infection details, including the date of positive test, hospitalization status, and severity of illness.
4.  If applicable, provide details about your antibody testing, including the date and results.
5.  Report any current health issues, medications, and medical conditions.
6.  Indicate your COVID-19 vaccination status.
7.  Confirm your understanding of the plasma donation process.
8.  Give your consent to plasma donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`plasma_donor_name`, text, required): Please enter your complete legal name.
*   **Date of Birth** (`plasma_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
*   **Email Address** (`plasma_donor_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone Number** (`plasma_donor_phone`, phone, required): Enter your contact phone number for follow-up calls.
*   **Street Address** (`plasma_donor_address`, text, required): Enter your street address as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Blood Type** (`plasma_blood_type`, select\_one, required): Select your ABO blood type from the provided options.
*   **Date of Positive COVID-19 Test** (`plasma_covid_positive_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your positive COVID-19 test result.
*   **Were You Hospitalized for COVID-19** (`plasma_covid_hospitalized`, select\_one, required): Indicate whether you were hospitalized due to COVID-19 infection.
*   **Date of Recovery or Test Negativity** (`plasma_covid_recovery_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your recovery or negativity test result.
*   **Severity of COVID-19 Illness** (`plasma_symptom_severity`, select\_one, required): Select the severity of your COVID-19 infection from the provided options.
*   **Have You Been Antibody Tested** (`plasma_antibody_tested`, select\_one, required): Indicate whether you have undergone antibody testing for COVID-19.
*   **Antibody Test Result** (`plasma_antibody_result`, select\_one, required): Select the result of your antibody test from the provided options.
*   **Date of Antibody Test** (`plasma_antibody_test_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your antibody test result.
*   **Currently Ill or Have Symptoms** (`plasma_current_illness`, select\_one, required): Indicate your current health status.
*   **Current Medications** (`plasma_medications`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
*   **Chronic Medical Conditions** (`plasma_medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any ongoing health conditions.
*   **COVID-19 Vaccination Status** (`plasma_vaccination_status`, select\_one, required): Select your COVID-19 vaccination status from the provided options.
*   **I Understand the Plasma Donation Process** (`plasma_understand_donation`, select\_one, required): Confirm your understanding of the plasma donation process.
*   **I Authorize COVID-19 Plasma Donation** (`plasma_authorize_donation`, select\_one, required): Confirm your authorization for plasma donation.

Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to facilitate the smooth processing of your plasma donation application.
