# Dermatology Virtual Visits Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the Dermatology Virtual Visits Consent Form, a crucial document that ensures the confidentiality and security of your personal and medical information during virtual visits. Please read this guide carefully to understand the form's purpose and how to complete it correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "User Input" page to begin.
2. Enter your date of birth and other required information.
3. Select your choice on the HIPAA compliance terms and conditions.
4. Choose whether you agree to participate in this virtual visit.
5. Fill out any other relevant fields as needed.
6. Sign the electronic signature form to confirm your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input (<name>, text, false)
* This field allows you to input any additional information that you believe is relevant to your virtual visit.

### Electronic Signature (<name>, select_one, false)
* This field is for electronic signatures. Select "I agree to participate in this virtual visit" to confirm your participation.

### HIPAA Compliance (<name>, select_one, false)
* This field is for understanding and agreeing to the HIPAA compliance terms and conditions. Select "I understand and agree to the HIPAA compliance terms and conditions" to acknowledge your understanding.

### Date of Birth (<name>, date, false)
* This field is for entering your date of birth.

### Medical Condition (<name>, text, false)
* This field is for describing your medical condition or condition you are visiting about.

### Contact Info (<name>, text, false)
* This field is for providing your contact information.

### Emergency Contact (<name>, text, false)
* This field is for listing your emergency contact information.

### Allergy/Medication (<name>, text, false)
* This field is for listing any allergies or medications you are taking.

## Tips
* Please note that this form is not intended to be a comprehensive medical questionnaire. If you have specific questions or concerns, contact your healthcare provider for further guidance.
* Make sure to read and understand the HIPAA compliance terms and conditions before signing.
* If you are unable to complete this form, contact your healthcare provider for assistance.
