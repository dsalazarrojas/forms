# Telehealth Consent Form Colorado - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information from users for telehealth services. This form is used to assess user's medical history, contact details, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out your personal details such as `first_name`, `last_name`, and `birthdate` (if applicable).
- Provide your contact information including `address`, `email`, and `phone`.
- Indicate any `emergency_contact` information, if applicable.
- If you have any existing medical condition, select the relevant medical condition from the `medical_condition` dropdown list.
- List any `medications` you are currently taking.
- Provide `emergency_contact_info` and `emergency_contact_relationship` if you have an emergency contact person.
- Enter the `emergency_contact_name`, `emergency_contact_phone`, and `emergency_contact_email` of your emergency contact person.
- Indicate your telehealth `consent` by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the `telehealth_consent` field.
- Sign the form digitally by filling out the `signature` field.
- Record the date and time you completed the form in the `date` and `time` fields.
- Add any additional `note` that you think is essential for your telehealth services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Birthdate** (`birthdate`, date, optional): Enter your birthdate.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your residential address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact person's name.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact person's information.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact person's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your emergency contact person's email address.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_one, optional): Select any existing medical condition from the dropdown list.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications you are taking.
* **Telehealth Consent** (`telehealth_consent`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your telehealth consent by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign the form digitally.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Signature Image** (`signature_image`, text, optional): Upload your digital signature.
* **Signature Image Date** (`signature_image_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you uploaded your signature.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you completed the form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you completed the form.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes you think are essential.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and consents to complete this form, especially the telehealth consent section. You can skip fields that are not relevant to your situation. 

Note: The fields with optional label are not required fields, and you can skip them if not applicable. 

Please review your form carefully before submitting it.
