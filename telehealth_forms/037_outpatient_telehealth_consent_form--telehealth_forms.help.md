# Outpatient Telehealth Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outpatient Telehealth Consent Form is used to obtain consent from patients for telehealth services. This form is designed to ensure that patients understand the terms and conditions of their telehealth care and to record their consent for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of services you are consenting to (Telehealth services or In-person services) on page 1.
2. Enter your name on page 2.
3. Select the date on which you are giving your consent on page 3.
4. Enter the name of the practitioner providing your care on page 4.
5. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have given your consent on page 5.
6. Enter your address on page 6.
7. Enter your contact number on page 7.
8. Enter your email address on page 8.
9. Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact on page 9.
10. Enter any relevant medical history on page 10.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consent to telehealth services**: Choose "Telehealth services" if you are willing to receive care via telehealth, or "In-person services" if you prefer to receive care in-person.
* **Patient name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of consent**: Please enter the date on which you are giving your consent. This should be the current date.
* **Practitioner name**: Please enter the name of the practitioner providing your care.
* **Consent given**: Answer "Yes" if you have given your consent, or "No" if you have not.
* **Patient address**: Please enter your home address.
* **Contact number**: Please enter a number where you can be reached.
* **Email address**: Please enter your email address.
* **Emergency contact**: Please provide the name and contact information of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Medical history**: Please provide any relevant medical history that may be important for your care.
