### Family Needs Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Family Needs Assessment Form is a tool used to gather information about a family's needs and circumstances. This form helps our organization understand the family's requirements and preferences, enabling us to provide more effective support and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form carefully**, as each field is essential for providing accurate and personalized support.
2. **Answer each question honestly** and to the best of your ability.
3. **Use the available options** to select the most suitable responses for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Parent Name**
**parent_name** (Text, Optional)
Enter the name of the parent or guardian completing the form.

### 2. **Child Names**
**child_names** (Select Multiple, Optional)
Select all applicable answers to indicate the presence or absence of each child:

* **Yes**: The child is currently living with the family.
* **No**: The child is not living with the family.

### 3. **In Counseling**
**in_counseling** (Select One, Optional)
Indicate whether any family members are currently in counseling:

* **Yes**: Family members are currently receiving counseling services.
* **No**: Family members are not currently receiving counseling services.

### 4. **Requests**
**requests** (Text, Optional)
Enter any specific requests or needs you have, such as housing assistance or educational support.

### Tips
* **Double-check your answers** to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* **Use clear and concise language** when filling out the form.
* **Don't hesitate to ask for help** if you're unsure about any field or question.
