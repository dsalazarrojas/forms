# Telehealth Infrastructure Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telehealth Infrastructure Assessment Form is designed to gather feedback from end-users about their experience with the telehealth infrastructure. This form will help us identify areas where the infrastructure excels and areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "phone" field.
5. Describe your experience with the telehealth infrastructure in the "telehealth_infrastructure" field.
6. Share any feedback or comments you have in the "feedback" field.
7. Rate how well the telehealth infrastructure performs in the "ratings_what_improve" field by selecting one of the options: "What we do well", "What we need to improve", or "What we don't do well".
8. If applicable, select a date when you completed your telehealth session or interaction in the "date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name**: Enter your first name to help us identify you.
* **last_name**: Enter your last name to help us identify you.
* **email**: Enter your email address, so we can contact you for follow-up questions or if we need more information.
* **phone**: If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can reach out to you.
* **telehealth_infrastructure**: Describe your experience with the telehealth infrastructure. Be as specific as possible.
* **feedback**: Share any feedback or comments you have about your experience with the telehealth infrastructure.
* **ratings_what_improve**: Rate how well the telehealth infrastructure performs in three categories: "What we do well", "What we need to improve", or "What we don't do well". Choose the category that best fits your experience.
* **date**: If applicable, select a date when you completed your telehealth session or interaction.
