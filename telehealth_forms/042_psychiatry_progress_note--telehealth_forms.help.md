# Psychiatry Progress Note - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a progress note for documenting patient information and treatment details. It helps healthcare providers to track patient progress, set goals, and plan for future appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's basic information, including name, date of birth, and other relevant details.
2. **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required): Record the date of the current visit.
3. **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, text, required): Enter the patient's ID number or unique identifier.
4. **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, text, required): Briefly summarize the key points discussed during the session.
5. **Chief Complaint** (`chief_complaint`, text, required): Describe the patient's main concern or issue.
6. **Assessment** (`assessment`, text, required): Summarize your assessment of the patient's current mental state.
7. **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, required): Outline the agreed-upon treatment goals and steps.
8. **Medications** (`medications`, text, required): List any prescribed medications and their dosages.
9. **Lab Results** (`lab_results`, text, required): Record any relevant lab results or test outcomes.
10. **Mental Status** (`mental_status`, text, required): Describe the patient's current mental status, including any concerns or changes.
11. **Treatment Goals** (`treatment_goals`, text, required): Outline the short-term and long-term goals for the patient's treatment.
12. **Progress Notes** (`progress_notes`, text, required): Record any notable progress or observations.
13. **Next Appointment** (`next_appointment`, text, required): Schedule the next appointment or follow-up date.
14. **Provider Info** (`provider_info`, text, required): Include your contact information and other relevant provider details.

## Tips

- Be thorough but concise in your entries.
- Use clear and respectful language when describing the patient's condition and treatment.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
- Review and update the form regularly to reflect changes in the patient's status.

Note: This form is a sample and should be reviewed and adapted according to your organization's specific policies and procedures.
