# Reiki Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reiki Intake Form is a crucial document that provides healthcare providers with information necessary to understand a patient's medical history, current health status, and Reiki treatment preferences. It is designed to be completed by patients before their first Reiki session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the first two fields.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** numbers in the next two fields.
3. Answer the E-Signature questions by selecting the corresponding options from the dropdown menus. These questions are designed to understand your preferences and agreements with your Reiki treatment.
4. If you have any comments or notes about your Reiki treatment, you can enter them in the **E-Signature Note** field.
5. Finally, submit the form to complete the Reiki Intake Form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name**: Enter your full first name in this field.
### Last Name
* **Last Name**: Enter your full last name in this field.
### Email
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further information or updates about your Reiki treatment.
### Phone
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for further information or updates about your Reiki treatment.
### E-Signature
* **E-Signature**: Select 'Yes' if you agree to participate in Reiki treatment; select 'No' otherwise.
### E-Signature Required
* **E-Signature Required**: Select 'Yes' if you require electronic signatures for future Reiki sessions; select 'No' otherwise.
### E-Signature Date
* **E-Signature Date**: Enter the date you are signing this form electronically.
### E-Signature Time
* **E-Signature Time**: Enter the time you are signing this form electronically.
### E-Signature Note
* **E-Signature Note**: Add any comments or notes about your Reiki treatment in this field (optional).
### E-Signature 1
