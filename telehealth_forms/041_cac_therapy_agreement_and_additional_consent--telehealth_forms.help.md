# CAC Therapy Agreement And Additional Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients about their therapy process, including their personal details, therapy preferences, and consent for emergency contact. It is essential to read and understand the form carefully to ensure you are aware of your responsibilities and boundaries in the therapy process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details, including your full name, date of birth, and contact information (email and phone number).
2. Select the type of therapy you would like to engage in (Individual, Group, Family, Couple, Online, or Other).
3. Choose the frequency of your therapy sessions (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, or As-needed).
4. Specify the length of each session (30 minutes, 45 minutes, 60 minutes, or 90 minutes).
5. Provide a brief description of the reasons you are seeking therapy.
6. If applicable, provide information about any previous mental health treatment or therapy experience.
7. Acknowledge that you understand the confidentiality limitations of therapy.
8. Indicate your consent for emergency contact and provide the name and phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
9. Acknowledge that you understand the risks and benefits of therapy.
10. Select your preferred payment method for therapy (Insurance, Credit Card, Debit Card, Cash, or Payment Plan).
11. If paying by insurance, provide the insurance carrier's name.
12. Sign and date the form to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address that you use regularly.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Therapist Name**: Enter the name of your assigned therapist.
* **Type of Therapy**: Select the type of therapy you would like to engage in (Individual, Group, Family, Couple, Online, or Other).
* **Therapy Start Date**: Enter the date you would like to begin therapy sessions.
* **Session Frequency**: Select how often you would like to attend therapy sessions (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, or As-needed).
* **Session Duration**: Select the length of each therapy session (30 minutes, 45 minutes, 60 minutes, or 90 minutes).
* **Presenting Concerns or Issues**: Briefly describe the reasons you are seeking therapy.
* **Previous Therapy Experience**: If applicable, describe any previous mental health treatment or therapy experience.
* **I Understand Confidentiality Limitations**: Acknowledge that you understand the confidentiality limitations of therapy.
* **Emergency Contact Consent**: Indicate your consent for emergency contact and provide the name and phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **I Acknowledge Risks and Benefits of Therapy**: Acknowledge that you understand the risks and benefits of therapy.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method for therapy (Insurance, Credit Card, Debit Card, Cash, or Payment Plan).
* **Insurance Carrier**: If paying by insurance, enter the name of your insurance carrier.
* **Consent Agreement Date**: Sign and date the form to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions.
