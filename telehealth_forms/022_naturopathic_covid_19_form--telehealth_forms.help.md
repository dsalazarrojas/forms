# Naturopathic COVID 19 Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Naturopathic COVID 19 Form is a document used to gather information from patients about their naturopathic treatment and medical history. This form will help naturopathic practitioners to gather essential information about the patient's medical condition and create a personalized treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information about your medical history and current conditions.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a detailed account of your medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions that may affect your treatment.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `text`, required: false): Describe your current symptoms, including any pain, discomfort, or other issues affecting your health.
* **Current Vaccination Status** (`current_vaccination_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate your current vaccination status, whether active or inactive.
* **Vaccination History** (`vaccination_history`, `text`, required: false): Provide information about your previous vaccination history, if any.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required: false): List any known allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required: false): Describe any medical conditions or diseases you are currently experiencing.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide your contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): List the name and contact information of your emergency contact person.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, `text`, required: false): Provide additional medical history information, if needed.
* **Medication** (`medication`, `text`, required: false): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Condition 2** (`medical_condition_2`, `text`, required: false): Describe any additional medical conditions or diseases you are experiencing.
* **Contact Info 2** (`contact_info_2`, `text`, required: false): Provide alternative contact information, if needed.

## Tips

* Please answer each question accurately and thoroughly to ensure the practitioner has a complete understanding of your medical history and condition.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your practitioner for guidance.
* If you have any additional information or concerns, please include them in the space provided at the end of the form.
