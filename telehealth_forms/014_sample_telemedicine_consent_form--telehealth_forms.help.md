# Sample Telemedicine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient information and consent for telemedicine services. Patients will provide their personal details and agree to the terms of their treatment. 

## How To Complete This Form

1.  The form is divided into several sections. Please review each section carefully and answer the questions as accurately and fully as possible.
2.  The form includes the following fields:
    - patient_name: Patient name
    - patient_email: Patient email (format: <patient's email address>)
    - date_of_birth: Date of birth (format: MM/DD/YY)
    - medical_condition: Medical condition or reason for consultation
    - consent: I, as a patient, give my consent for the telemedicine services provided. (Please type "yes" or "no")
    - e_signature: E-signature (please provide a scanned or typed signature)
    - provider_signature: Provider's signature (for authorized personnel)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **patient_name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
*   **patient_email**: Enter your email address that you'd like to use for communication. This will be used by your healthcare provider for any follow-up or appointment reminders.
*   **date_of_birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY (month/day/year). This will help us identify your age and medical history.
*   **medical_condition**: Describe your medical condition or reason for the consultation. This will help your healthcare provider understand your situation and provide the best possible care.
*   **consent**: Type "yes" if you give your consent for the telemedicine services. Type "no" if you do not give your consent.
*   **e_signature**: Please scan or type your electronic signature. This will serve as your formal agreement to the terms of your treatment.
*   **provider_signature**: Please enter your healthcare provider's signature for authorized personnel.

## Tips

*   Please answer the questions honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
*   If you're unsure about any question, please consult with your healthcare provider.
*   This form is designed to be used in a telemedicine setting. If you have any concerns or questions, please contact your healthcare provider or the form administrator.
*   The completed form should be submitted to your healthcare provider or the designated personnel for review and processing.
