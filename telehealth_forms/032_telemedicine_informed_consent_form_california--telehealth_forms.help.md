# telemedicine_informed_consent_form_california - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients for telemedicine services in California. It includes fields for patient and healthcare provider information, as well as terms and conditions for telemedicine services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your patient information, including name, address, and medical condition (if applicable).
2. Select the healthcare provider you are working with.
3. Provide your consent date.
4. Sign as a patient to indicate your understanding and agreement.
5. Sign as a healthcare provider to indicate their understanding and agreement.
6. Finally, confirm the telemedicine terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient.
* **Patient Address**: Enter the address of the patient (optional).
* **Medical Condition**: Enter any relevant medical condition that requires telemedicine services (optional).
* **Healthcare Provider**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider you are working with.
* **Consent Date**: Select the date you are providing your consent for telemedicine services.
* **Patient Signature**: Sign as a patient to indicate your understanding and agreement to the telemedicine terms.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature**: Sign as a healthcare provider to indicate their understanding and agreement to the telemedicine terms.
* **Telemedicine Terms**: Review and acknowledge the telemedicine terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete this form carefully and accurately.
- Ensure you understand the telemedicine terms and conditions before signing.
- Keep a copy of this form for your records.
- Review and update this form as necessary to reflect changes in state regulations or company policies.
