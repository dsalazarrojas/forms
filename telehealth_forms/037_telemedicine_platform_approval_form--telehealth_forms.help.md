# telemedicine_platform_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The telemedicine platform approval form is a crucial document that enables healthcare providers to onboard onto the platform. It is designed to collect necessary information about the provider's practice and credentials, ensuring that they can effectively provide telehealth services to patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and contact information, including your email and phone number.
2.  Provide your practice's address, including city and state.
3.  Choose your specialty and the telehealth services you will be offering.
4.  Verify your state license number and NPI.
5.  List any additional certifications you possess.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Provider Name**: This is the name of the healthcare provider, and will be used for internal and external communication. Please enter your name as it should be displayed on the platform.
* **Provider Type**: Please select the type of provider you are (Physician, Nurse Practitioner, or Physician Assistant/Nurse Practitioner)
* **Organization Name**: Enter the name of your practice or organization.
* **Practice Address**: Enter the address of your practice. This will be used to determine your location.
* **Practice Address 2**: Enter any additional address information, if applicable.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication with the platform.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication with the platform.
* **Practice Fax**: Enter your practice's fax number, if applicable.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person to contact for further information.
* **Telehealth Services**: Choose the telehealth services you will be offering (Virtual Visits, Patient Messaging, Video Visits, or Remote Patient Monitoring).
* **Practice Specialty**: Select your medical specialty from the provided list.
* **State License**: Enter your state license number.
* **License Number**: Enter your license number.
* **Practice License**: Enter your practice's license number, if applicable.
* **Practice Certifications**: Enter any additional certifications you possess, such as board certifications.
* **Board Credential**: Enter any board credentials you possess.
* **Address 3**: Enter any additional address information, if applicable.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Select your state from the provided list.
* **Zip**: Enter your practice's zip code.
* **Time Zone**: Enter the time zone of your practice.
* **Contact Person Title**: Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Practice NPI**: Enter your NPI number.
* **Telemed Certifications**: Enter any telemed certifications you possess.
* **Date**: Enter the date, which will be used to confirm your completion of this form.
