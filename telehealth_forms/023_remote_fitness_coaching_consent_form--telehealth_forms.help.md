# Remote Fitness Coaching Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients before starting a remote fitness coaching program. It collects client details, risks, consent, signatures, trainer details, client risks, and client consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. **Provide Client Details**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number in the "Client Details" section.
2. **Review and acknowledge Risks**: In the "Risks" section, read and acknowledge any potential risks associated with the remote coaching program.
3. **Provide Consent**: In the "Consent" section, write a brief statement of your understanding and agreement to participate in the program.
4. **Sign the form**: In the "Signatures" section, print your name and date to confirm your consent.
5. **Enter Trainer Details**: In the "Trainer Details" section, enter your trainer's name, email address, and phone number.
6. **List Client Risks**: In the "Client Risks" section, list any risks you are aware of that may affect your participation in the program.
7. **Client Consent**: In the "Client Consent" section, write a brief statement of your understanding and agreement to participate in the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Details
* **Client Details** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name, email address, and phone number to identify yourself as the client.
### Risks
* **Risks** (`text`, required/optional): This section is for you to acknowledge any potential risks associated with the remote coaching program. Please read and understand these risks before proceeding.
### Consent
* **Consent** (`text`, required/optional): Write a brief statement of your understanding and agreement to participate in the remote fitness coaching program.
### Signatures
* **Signatures** (`text`, required/optional): Print your name and date to confirm your consent and participation in the program.
### Trainer Details
* **Trainer Details** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your trainer's name, email address, and phone number, if applicable.
### Client Risks
* **Client Risks** (`text`, required/optional): List any risks you are aware of that may affect your participation in the program.
### Client Consent
* **Client Consent** (`text`, required/optional): Write a brief statement of your understanding and agreement to participate in the remote fitness coaching program.

## Tips

* Please complete this form carefully and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* This form is a legal document and your electronic or physical signature confirms your consent.
* Please note that the information collected through this form is confidential and will be used only for the purpose of providing remote fitness coaching services.
