# Telepsychiatry Session Recording Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from clients for telepsychiatry session recordings. It collects information about client consent, session details, and emergency contacts. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the client information section, including client name, session date, and session time.
2. Answer the recording permission question with one or multiple of 'Yes', 'No', or 'Not Sure'.
3. Explain why you are agreeing to recording in the 'Consent Reason' field.
4. Read and agree to the 'Client Agreement'.
5. If necessary, select the emergency contact's relationship and provide their contact information.
6. If necessary, provide the emergency contact's phone number and email address.
7. List any medical conditions or allergies you have.
8. If necessary, provide more details about your medical history.
9. Finally, select 'Submit' to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter your name to identify yourself as the client.
* **Session Date**: Enter the date of the telepsychiatry session.
* **Session Time**: Enter the time of the telepsychiatry session.
* **Can We Record?**: Choose 'Yes' if you are willing to have the session recorded, 'No' if you are not, or 'Not Sure' if you are unsure.
* **Why Are You Agreeing To Record?**: Briefly explain why you are consenting to recording.
* **Client Agreement**: Read and agree to the terms of the recording, including that it will be stored on a secure server.
* **Practitioner Agreement**: Read and agree to the terms of the recording, including that it will be used for your treatment.
* **Session Length**: Select the expected length of the session.
* **Recording Device**: Choose 'Yes' if you will be using a recording device, 'No' if you will not.
* **Contact Information**: Select how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Text, or Other).
* **Other Contact Info**: Provide any additional contact information not listed above.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Recording Location**: Select where the recording will take place (Home, Office, or Other).
* **Other Recording Location**: If recording at a location other than home or office, enter it here.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide information for an emergency contact person.
* **Relationship**: Select the relationship between you and the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number for the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address for the emergency contact.
* **Medical Condition**: List any medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Condition Details**: Provide more details about your medical condition.
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you have.
* **Medical History**: Provide more information about your medical history.
* **Submit**: Select 'Submit' to complete the form and give consent for the recording.
