# Teletherapy Consent Form Washington County Schools - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students in Washington County Schools to provide consent for teletherapy services. It's essential to complete this form accurately and carefully, as it affects the student's access to online telehealth services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your choice of telehealth forms from the "Consent Form" page (id: 1).
2. Enter the patient's information (id: 2), including name, contact details, and other relevant data.
3. Provide the therapist's information (id: 3) if applicable.
4. Enter emergency contact information (id: 6, id: 7, id: 8, id: 9, id: 10) for the student.
5. Choose whether you give consent for online telehealth services (id: 5).
6. Add any additional therapist notes (id: 11) or parent notes (id: 12) if necessary.
7. Set emergency instructions (id: 13) and follow-up information (id: 14).
8. Confirm your consent (id: 15) and sign the form (id: 18).
9. Complete the printed version of the form by signing (id: 17), printing the form (id: 20), and printing date/time (id: 21).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Form (id: 1)** (`select_multiple`):
	+ This is the first page of the form where you select the type of telehealth form to complete.
	+ Please choose the correct type from the options provided.
* **Patient Information (id: 2)** (`text`):
	+ Enter the student's name, date of birth, and other relevant details.
	+ Make sure to double-check the information for accuracy.
* **Therapist Information (id: 3)** (`text`):
	+ If applicable, provide the therapist's name, contact details, and other relevant information.
* **Guardian Information (id: 4)** (`text`):
	+ Enter the guardian's name, contact details, and other relevant information.
* **Consent Details (id: 5)** (`select_one`):
	+ Choose whether you give consent for online telehealth services.
* **Emergency Contact (id: 6)** (`text`):
	+ Enter the emergency contact person's name, phone number, and address.
* **Relationship (id: 7)** (`text`):
	+ Describe the relationship between the emergency contact and the student.
* **Contact Number (id: 8)** (`text`):
	+ Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Address (id: 9)** (`text`):
	+ Enter the emergency contact person's address.
* **Contact Email (id: 10)** (`text`):
	+ Enter the emergency contact person's email address.
* **Therapist Notes (id: 11)** (`note`):
	+ Add any additional notes from the therapist, if necessary.
* **Parent Notes (id: 12)** (`note`):
	+ Add any additional notes from the parent/guardian, if necessary.
* **Emergency Instructions (id: 13)** (`time`):
	+ Set any emergency instructions, such as contact numbers or procedures.
* **Follow Up Info (id: 14)** (`text`):
	+ Enter follow-up information, such as appointment schedules or next steps.
* **Consent Signed (id: 15)** (`select_one`):
	+ Confirm your consent for online telehealth services.
* **Consent Date (id: 16)** (`date`):
	+ Enter the date you signed the consent form.
* **Signed By (id: 17)** (`text`):
	+ Sign the printed version of the form.
* **Signature (id: 18)** (`date`):
	+ Sign the form electronically.
* **Printed By (id: 19)** (`text`):
	+ Enter the name of the person printing the form.
* **Printed Date (id: 20)** (`date`):
	+ Enter the date the form was printed.
* **Printed Time (id: 21)** (`time`):
	+ Enter the time the form was printed.
* **Form ID (id: 22)** (`number`):
	+ Enter a unique identifier for the form.
* **Form Version (id: 23)** (`number`):
	+ Enter the version number of the form.
* **Timestamp (id: 24)** (`date`):
	+ Enter the timestamp for the form completion.
