# Client Clinical Notes Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Clinical Notes Form is a tool for documenting client sessions, including telehealth and in-person interactions. This form ensures accurate and comprehensive recording of essential information for each session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information about the client and their session.
2. Make sure to select the correct session modality (e.g., Telehealth (Video), In-Person, or Phone Call).
3. Use the SOAP format for clinical observations (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, and Plan).
4. Assess the client's risk level (e.g., No Risk Identified, Low Risk, Moderate Risk, High Risk / Immediate Action Taken).
5. Confirm that this note is saved in a HIPAA-compliant secure system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Practitioner/Clinician Name** (required): Enter the name of the clinician providing the service.
* **Client Name** (required): Enter the client's full name.
* **Client Date of Birth** (required): Record the client's date of birth for identity verification.
* **Session Date** (required): Record the date of the session.
* **Session Time** (required): Record the start and end time of the session.
* **Duration (Minutes)** (required): Enter the total session time in minutes.
* **Session Modality** (required): Choose the session type (e.g., Telehealth (Video), In-Person, or Phone Call).
* **Subjective (S) - Client's reports and presentation** (required): Record the client's subjective experience and presentation.
* **Objective (O) - Observations and Measurable data** (required): Record any relevant observations or data gathered during the session.
* **Assessment (A) - Professional impressions and diagnosis** (required): Summarize the clinician's professional assessment and potential diagnosis.
* **Plan (P) - Treatment plan and next steps** (required): Develop a treatment plan and outline next steps for the client.
* **Risk Assessment (Harm to Self or Others)** (required): Evaluate the client's risk level.
* **ICD-10 or CPT Billing Codes** (optional): Enter relevant billing codes (if applicable).
* **Clinician Digital Signature** (required): Sign with your digital signature.
* **Time of Signature** (required): Record the time of signature.
* **I confirm this note is saved in a HIPAA-compliant secure system** (required): Confirm that the note is saved in a HIPAA-compliant secure system.
* **Note Footer** (optional): Record any additional notes or comments.
