# Upper Extremity Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Upper Extremity Consultation Form is designed to collect information from patients about their upper extremity condition, medical history, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your basic patient information (page 1).
2. Describe your symptoms and pain levels (page 2).
3. Provide detailed descriptions of your symptoms and pain locations (page 3).
4. Share your medical history and medications taken (page 4).
5. List any allergies or medical conditions you have (page 5).
6. Enter emergency contact information (page 6).
7. Specify the medical procedure(s) you would like to undergo (page 7).
8. Schedule a follow-up appointment (page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Patient Info
* **Patient Info**: This is the first field, where you will enter your basic patient information. 

### Page 2: Symptoms
* **Symptoms**: Here, you will describe your symptoms and how you are feeling. Try to be as detailed as possible.

### Page 3: Pain Level and Symptom Descriptions
* **Pain Level**: Enter a numerical value to indicate the severity of your pain.
* **Symptom Descriptions**: Provide a detailed description of your symptoms and how they affect you.

### Page 4: Pain Locations and Medical History
* **Pain Locations**: List the locations in your upper extremities where you are experiencing pain or discomfort.
* **Medical History**: Share any relevant medical history you have, including previous treatments or conditions.

### Page 5: Medications and Allergies
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies or medical conditions you have.

### Page 6: Emergency Contact Info
* **Emergency Contact Info**: Enter the contact information of a person to contact in case of an emergency.

### Page 7: Medical Procedure
* **Medical Procedure**: Select the medical procedure(s) you would like to undergo. Options include:
	+ MRI
	+ CT scan
	+ Ultrasound
	+ X-Ray

### Page 8: Follow-up Scheduling
* **Follow-up Scheduling**: Schedule a follow-up appointment with us:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Tips
* Please be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your healthcare provider.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask.
