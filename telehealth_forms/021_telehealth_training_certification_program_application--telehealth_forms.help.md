# Telehealth Training Certification Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals seeking certification in telehealth training. To be eligible, applicants must meet the program's specific requirements and criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your Applicant Information, including your name and contact details.
2. Describe your Program Details, including your experience and qualifications in telehealth training.
3. Share your Contact Details, including your email address and phone number.
4. Provide an overview of your Education Background, including your relevant degrees and certifications.
5. Outline your Clinical Experience, including your past experience in clinical settings.
6. Describe your Telehealth Training experience, including your past training programs or certifications.
7. List your References, including any relevant professionals or organizations that can vouch for your qualifications.
8. Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information (1)**: This field is for you to provide your name and contact details. Please ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Program Details (2)**: Describe your experience and qualifications in telehealth training. Be concise and clear in your response.
* **Contact Details (3)**: Provide your email address and phone number so that we can contact you for further information.
* **Education Background (4)**: Outline your relevant degrees and certifications related to telehealth training.
* **Clinical Experience (5)**: Describe your past experience in clinical settings.
* **Telehealth Training (6)**: Describe your past training programs or certifications in telehealth.
* **References (7)**: List any relevant professionals or organizations that can vouch for your qualifications.
* **Agreement (8)**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the program.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Double-check your answers for spelling and grammar errors.
* Proofread your answers before submitting the form.
* Be honest and transparent in your responses.
