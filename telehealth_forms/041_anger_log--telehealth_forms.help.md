# Anger Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Anger Log" form is designed to help individuals track and manage their anger episodes, becoming more aware of their anger patterns, identifying triggers, and developing effective coping strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the "Anger Log" form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Client Name / ID" field with your name or ID number.
2. Enter the date and time of the incident in the "Date of Incident" and "Time of Incident" fields, respectively.
3. Describe the incident that triggered your anger in the "Trigger - What happened just before the anger?" field.
4. Indicate the intensity of your anger on a scale from 1 to 10 in the "Rate Anger Intensity (1 to 10)" field.
5. For the "Physical Sensations Experienced" field, select all the physical sensations you experienced during the incident, such as rapid heartbeat, clenched fists, or muscle tension.
6. If applicable, enter your automatic thoughts or thoughts that contributed to your anger in the "Automatic Thoughts - What were you thinking?" field.
7. Describe your behavior or actions taken during or after the incident in the "Behavior - What did you do or say?" field.
8. Choose the coping skills you used to manage your anger from the "Coping Skills Used" field.
9. Describe the outcome or resolution of the incident in the "Outcome - How did the situation end?" field.
10. Select the primary feeling you experienced after the incident from the "Primary Feeling After the Incident" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name / ID**: Enter your name or ID number to identify yourself in the record.
* **Date of Incident**: Enter the date of the incident that triggered your anger.
* **Time of Incident**: Enter the time of the incident that triggered your anger.
* **Trigger - What happened just before the anger?**: Describe the incident that triggered your anger.
* **Rate Anger Intensity (1 to 10)**: Indicate the intensity of your anger on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Physical Sensations Experienced**: Select all the physical sensations you experienced during the incident, such as rapid heartbeat, clenched fists, or muscle tension.
* **Automatic Thoughts - What were you thinking?**: If applicable, enter your automatic thoughts or thoughts that contributed to your anger.
* **Behavior - What did you do or say?**: Describe your behavior or actions taken during or after the incident.
* **Coping Skills Used**: Choose the coping skills you used to manage your anger from the list of options.
* **Outcome - How did the situation end?**: Describe the outcome or resolution of the incident.
* **Primary Feeling After the Incident**: Select the primary feeling you experienced after the incident.
