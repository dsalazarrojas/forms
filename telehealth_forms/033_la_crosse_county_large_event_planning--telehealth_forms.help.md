# La Crosse County Large Event Planning - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to plan and manage large events in La Crosse County. It helps in gathering essential information about the event, such as its title, location, date and time, description, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the required fields:
	* **First Name**: Enter your name as it should be displayed on the event.
	* **Email**: Provide your email address where you can be reached for event updates.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for event updates.
	* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event (Event, Meeting, or Other).
	* **Location**: Enter the location of the event.
	* **Description**: Briefly describe the event.
	* **Capacity**: Estimate the number of attendees.
	* **Attendees**: Select the estimated number of attendees.
	* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the event.
2. If applicable, fill out the optional fields:
	* **Date**: Enter the start and end dates of the event.
	* **Time**: Enter the start and end times of the event.
	* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the event.
	* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the event.
	* **Recurrence**: Choose the frequency of the event (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
	* **Repeat Until**: Choose when the recurrence should end (Never, Specified Date, Until Specified Date).
	* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the assigned tool or resource for the event.
	* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the event.
	* **Contact E-mail**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for the event.
	* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the event.
	* **Status**: Choose the status of the event (Active, Inactive, Pending).
	* **Priority**: Choose the priority of the event (High, Low, Medium).
