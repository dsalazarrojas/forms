# Acupuncturist Telehealth Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for patients to provide consent for telehealth services. It ensures they understand the limitations and requirements of remote sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the patient's demographic information.
2. Enter the patient's contact email address, which will be used for telehealth links and materials.
3. Describe the device and location where the patient will be during the telehealth session.
4. If the patient has any specific privacy concerns, please list them.
5. Confirm your understanding of the telehealth limitations by selecting "I consent".
6. Enter the address and emergency contact information for the patient's location.
7. Review and acknowledge the billing and cancellation policies.
8. Sign the consent form by typing your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full legal name.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the patient's email address for telehealth links and materials.
* **Device and location during session** (`device_and_location`, text, required): Describe the location and device you will be using during the telehealth session.
* **Privacy concerns** (`privacy_concerns`, text, optional): List any specific privacy needs or concerns.
* **Consent for telehealth services** (`consent_for_telehealth`, select_one, required): Confirm your understanding of telehealth limitations by selecting "I consent".
* **Emergency plan and local contact** (`emergency_plan`, text, required): Enter the address and emergency contact information for your location.
* **Billing and cancellation policy** (`billing_and_cancellation`, note, required): Review and acknowledge the billing and cancellation policies.
* **Signature name** (`signature_name`, text, required): Sign the consent form by typing your name for the record.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of consent.
