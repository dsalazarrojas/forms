# COVID 19 Patient Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COVID-19 Patient Screening Questionnaire is a form designed to collect essential information from patients to assess their risk factors and determine the necessary steps for their care. This form will help identify potential health concerns and ensure that patients receive the required attention and care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by entering your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Date of Birth** in the corresponding fields. These fields are **required** for identification purposes.
2. Provide your **Email Address** so we can contact you if needed. This field is also **required**.
3. Answer the questions in the **Family Medical History** section regarding your family members who have been diagnosed with COVID-19 and if they have a history of chronic health conditions.
4. Disclose your **Personal Medical History**, including any chronic health conditions you may have.
5. Indicate your **COVID-19 Vaccination Status** and **Current Symptoms**, if any.
6. Describe your **Smoking Habits** and **Exercise Frequency** for further assessment.
7. Answer the questions in the **Family Medical History** section regarding the relationship to affected family members and if you have been diagnosed with COVID-19.
8. Provide **Current Medications** and **Known Allergies** if applicable.
9. Answer the questions in the **COVID-19 Screening** section regarding COVID-19 symptoms, diagnosis, and vaccination status.
10. Review and complete all sections carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, but this field is optional.
* **Family Members Diagnosed with COVID-19**: Answer 'True' if any family members have been diagnosed with COVID-19, otherwise 'False'.
* **Relationship to Affected Family Member**: Describe your relationship to the family member who has been diagnosed with COVID-19 (e.g., parent, sibling, etc.).
* **Family History of Chronic Conditions**: Answer 'True' if your family members have a history of chronic health conditions, otherwise 'False'.
* **Chronic Conditions**: Select all conditions that apply from the list (e.g., Diabetes, Heart disease, etc.).
* **Current Medications**: List all medications you are currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
* **Known Allergies**: List all known allergies and their severity level.
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status**: Select your current vaccination status from the options (Not vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, Fully vaccinated, or Prefer not to answer).
* **COVID-19 Symptoms**: Answer 'True' if you currently have COVID-19 symptoms, otherwise 'False' or 'Unsure'.
* **Symptoms Description**: If you have COVID-19 symptoms, describe them in detail.
* **Smoking Habits**: Answer 'True' if you currently smoke, 'False' otherwise.
* **Exercise Frequency**: Select your exercise frequency from the options (Rarely, 1-2 times per week, 3-4 times per week, Daily).

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the COVID-19 Patient Screening Questionnaire accurately and efficiently.
