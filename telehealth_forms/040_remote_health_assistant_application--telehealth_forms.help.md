# remote_health_assistant_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect patient information for telehealth services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Patient Details" section with your name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. In the "Availability" section, let us know when you are available to receive telehealth services.
3. In the "Preferences" section, tell us about any specific needs or requirements you have.
4. In the "Contact" section, provide us with your preferred contact method.
5. Finally, submit your application by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **1. Patient Details**
(Required)
* **Patient Name** (`title`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`type`, optional): Enter your date of birth for our records.
* **Contact Information** (`type`, optional): Provide your email address, phone number, or any other way we can reach you.

### **2. Availability**
(Required)
* **Availability** (`text`, required): Tell us when you are available to receive telehealth services. For example, "Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm".

### **3. Preferences**
(Required)
* **Preferences** (`text`, required): Describe any specific needs or requirements you have. For example, "I require a doctor who speaks Spanish" or "I need a specialist who can communicate with patients with hearing impairments".

### **4. Contact**
(Required)
* **Contact** (`text`, required): Choose a contact method for us to reach out to you. For example, "Phone", "Email", or "Text".

### **5. Submit**
(Required)
* **Submit** (`submit`, required): Click this button to submit your application.
 
## Tips
- Please make sure all fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
