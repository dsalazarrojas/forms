# Lifemark Informed Consent Telehealth Treatment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lifemark Informed Consent Telehealth Treatment form is a comprehensive document that outlines the rights and responsibilities of patients seeking telehealth treatment. This guide is designed to assist patients in navigating the form and ensuring they understand their obligations and rights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully and ensure you understand the information being requested.
2. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review the patient rights and responsibilities section to ensure you understand your rights and obligations as a patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Please enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Patient Informed Consent** (`patient_informed_consent`): This section is a critical part of the form, as it outlines your understanding of your rights and responsibilities as a patient. Please read through this section carefully and sign where indicated.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`): If you have emergency contacts, please list them in this section. This includes their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Briefly describe your relationship with each emergency contact.
* **Mental Health Providers** (`mental_health_providers`): If you have a mental health provider, please list their name and contact information.
* **Mental Health Provider Info** (`mental_health_provider_info`): If you have a mental health provider, please describe their role in your care and any relevant information about their practice.
* **Patient Privacy Rights** (`patient_privacy_rights`): This section outlines your rights as a patient, including your right to confidentiality and access to your medical records.
* **Electronic Signature** (`electronic_signature`): Please enter your electronic signature, which confirms that you have read and understood the patient rights and responsibilities outlined in this form.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`): Please sign this section to confirm that you have read and understood the patient rights and responsibilities outlined in this form.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Please enter your full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Please enter your date of birth.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`): Please enter the date of your last visit or service.
* **Date of Last Visit** (`date_of_last_visit`): Please enter the date of your last visit.
* **Time of Last Visit** (`time_of_last_visit`): Please enter the time of your last visit.
* **Patient Gender** (`patient_gender`): Please select your gender.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`): Please enter your age.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): If you have a preferred contact phone number, please enter it here.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): If you have a preferred email address, please enter it here.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Please list the name of each emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Please list the phone number of each emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Briefly describe your relationship with each emergency contact.
* **Mental Health Provider** (`mental_health_provider`): If you have a mental health provider, please list their name and contact information.
* **Mental Health Provider Info** (`mental_health_provider_info`): If you have a mental health provider, please describe their role in your care and any relevant information about their practice.
* **Patient Privacy Rights Info** (`patient_privacy_rights_info`): This section outlines your rights as a patient, including your right to confidentiality and access to your medical records.

## Tips
- Please review each section carefully to ensure you understand the information being requested.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to your mental health provider.
- This form is designed to ensure you are informed and aware of your rights and responsibilities as a patient.
