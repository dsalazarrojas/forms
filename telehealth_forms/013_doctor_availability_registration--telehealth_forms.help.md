# Doctor Availability Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a doctor's availability for appointments. It is used to register a doctor's schedule and availability for patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the doctor's schedule in the "Doctor Schedule" field.
2. Select whether the doctor is available for appointments by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "Appointment Type" field.
3. Enter the date for the appointment in the "Date" field.
4. Enter the start time for the appointment in the "Start Time" field.
5. Enter the end time for the appointment in the "End Time" field.
6. Enter the duration of the appointment in the "Duration" field.
7. Enter the doctor's name in the "Doctor Name" field.
8. Enter the location where the appointment will take place in the "Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Doctor Schedule** (`doctor_schedule`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's schedule in a text format.
* **Appointment Type** (`appointment_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that the doctor is available for appointments, "No" to indicate that the doctor is not available.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date for the appointment.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time for the appointment.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time for the appointment.
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the appointment.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional): Enter the doctor's name.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the appointment will take place.

Note: All fields are optional, except for the "Doctor Schedule" field, which can be left blank if not applicable.
