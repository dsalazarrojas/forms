# Cholesterol Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cholesterol Screening Form is a patient-facing form used for collecting personal and medical information for cholesterol screening and monitoring. The form is used for patients undergoing a cholesterol screening test and helps healthcare providers assess the patient's risk level and make recommendations for lifestyle changes and potential medication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Enter Patient Information**
    *   First Name: This field requires the patient's first name.
    *   Last Name: This field requires the patient's last name.
    *   Date of Birth: Enter the date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
    *   Age: Enter your age.
    *   Gender: Select your gender.
    *   Phone Number: Enter your phone number.
    *   Email Address: Enter your email address.
2.  **Enter Screening Information**
    *   Screening Date: Enter the date of the screening test.
    *   Type of Screening: Select the type of screening performed (in-person, telehealth, home test kit, or lab draw).
    *   Fasting Status: Select your fasting status before the screening test.
    *   Have you ever had high cholesterol? Select if you have or have not had high cholesterol.
    *   Family History of Heart Disease: Select if you have or have not had a family history of heart disease.
3.  **Enter Medical History**
    *   Current Medications: List your current medications, especially statins.
    *   Medical Conditions: Select all medical conditions you have, including high blood pressure, diabetes, heart disease, stroke, thyroid disorder, kidney disease, and liver disease.
4.  **Enter Lifestyle Factors**
    *   Smoking Status: Select your smoking status (never smoked, former smoker, or current smoker).
    *   Exercise Frequency: Select how often you exercise (daily, 3-5 times per week, 1-2 times per week, rarely, or never).
    *   Diet Quality: Select your diet quality (excellent, good, fair, or poor).
    *   Alcohol Consumption: Select your alcohol consumption status (none, occasional, moderate, or regular).
5.  **Enter Test Results and Risk Assessment**
    *   Blood Pressure: Enter your blood pressure reading (systolic over diastolic).
    *   Cholesterol Results: View your test results, including total cholesterol, LDL, HDL, and triglycerides.
    *   Cholesterol Ratio: View your cholesterol ratio (total/HDL).
    *   Risk Assessment: View your cardiovascular risk level (low, moderate, high, or very high).
6.  **Enter Recommendations and Follow-up**
    *   Recommended Lifestyle Changes: Select all lifestyle changes recommended for you.
    *   Is Medication Recommended: Select if medication is recommended.
    *   Follow-up Needed: Select if a follow-up is needed.

*   **Patient Information** (1) - This field is for entering the patient's personal details.

*   **Address** (10) - Enter the address in the format street, city, state, zip.
*   **Screening Information** (11) - This field is for viewing test details.
*   **Screening Date** (12) - Enter the date of the screening test.
*   **Screening Location** (13) - Enter the screening location.
*   **Type of Screening** (14) - Select the type of screening performed (in-person, telehealth, home test kit, or lab draw).
*   **Fasting Status** (15) - Select your fasting status before the screening test.
*   **Have you ever had high cholesterol?** (16) - Select if you have or have not had high cholesterol.
*   **Family History of Heart Disease** (17) - Select if you have or have not had a family history of heart disease.
*   **Current Medications** (18) - List your current medications, especially statins.
*   **Medical Conditions** (19) - Select all medical conditions you have, including high blood pressure, diabetes, heart disease, stroke, thyroid disorder, kidney disease, and liver disease.
*   **Lifestyle Factors** (20) - This field is for health habits.
*   **Smoking Status** (21) - Select your smoking status (never smoked, former smoker, or current smoker).
*   **Alcohol Consumption** (22) - Select your alcohol consumption status (none, occasional, moderate, or regular).
*   **Exercise Frequency** (23) - Select how often you exercise (daily, 3-5 times per week, 1-2 times per week, rarely, or never).

*   **Height** (26) - Enter your height in feet and inches or centimeters.
*   **Weight** (27) - Enter your weight in pounds or kilograms.
*   **Blood Pressure** (28) - Enter your blood pressure reading (systolic over diastolic).
*   **Cholesterol Results** (29) - View your test results, including total cholesterol, LDL, HDL, and triglycerides.
*   **Cholesterol Ratio** (30) - View your cholesterol ratio (total/HDL).
*   **Risk Assessment** (31) - View your cardiovascular risk level (low, moderate, high, or very high).
*   **Recommended Lifestyle Changes** (32) - Select all lifestyle changes recommended for you.
*   **Is Medication Recommended** (33) - Select if medication is recommended.
*   **Follow-up Needed** (34) - Select if a follow-up is needed.
*   **Provider Notes** (35) - Enter additional comments from your healthcare provider.
*   **I consent to cholesterol screening** (36) - Select if you consent to the screening.
*   **Patient Signature** (37) - Enter your digital signature.
*   **Provider Signature** (38) - Enter your healthcare provider's signature.
*   **Date** (39) - Enter the date.

## Tips

*   Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, ask your healthcare provider.
*   Follow your healthcare provider's recommendations for lifestyle changes and potential medication.
*   Keep your follow-up appointment as scheduled.
*   If you have any medical conditions or concerns, report them to your healthcare provider.
*   Keep a copy of your test results and recommendations for your records.
