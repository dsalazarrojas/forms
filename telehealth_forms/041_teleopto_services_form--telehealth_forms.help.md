# teleopto_services_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for teleopto services, including patient data, medical practice details, and monitoring settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow these steps:

* Fill in all required fields accurately and consistently.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid duplication.
* Ensure that authorized personnel only input information in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information
* **patient_first_name** (text, optional): Enter the patient's first name.
* **patient_last_name** (text, required): Enter the patient's last name.
* **birth_date** (date, required): Enter the patient's birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **patient_address** (text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **medical_practice** (text, optional): Enter the name of the medical practice.

### Telehealth Monitoring Information
* **teleopto_device_id** (number, required): Enter the teleopto device ID.
* **start_monitoring** (date, required): Enter the start date of monitoring.
* **end_monitoring** (date, optional): Enter the end date of monitoring.
* **monitoring_frequency** (select multiple, required): Choose how often the monitoring will be done (e.g., 1, 2, or 3 times a day).
* **monitoring_duration** (select one, required): Choose the duration of monitoring (e.g., 7, 14, or 30 days).
* **medical_practice_address** (text, optional): Enter the address of the medical practice.
* **assigned_technician** (text, optional): Enter the name of the assigned technician.
* **assigned_technician_phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the assigned technician.
* **assigned_technician_email** (email, optional): Enter the email of the assigned technician.

### Additional Information
* **teleopto_contact_person** (text, optional): Enter the contact person's name.
* **teleopto_contact_phone** (text, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **teleopto_contact_email** (email, optional): Enter the contact person's email.

### Signatures and Confirmations
* **patient_signature** (note, required): Obtain the patient's signature.
* **medical_practice_signature** (note, required): Obtain the medical practice's signature.
* **teleopto_services_signature** (note, required): Obtain the teleopto services signature.
* **technician_signature** (note, required): Obtain the technician's signature.
* **medical_practice_license** (select multiple, required): Choose the medical practice license (e.g., 1, 2, or 3).
* **monitoring_status** (select one, required): Choose the monitoring status (e.g., active, inactive, or completed).
* **technician_notification** (select one, required): Choose whether the technician will receive notifications (True or False).
* **patient_notification** (select one, optional): Choose whether the patient will receive notifications (True or False).
