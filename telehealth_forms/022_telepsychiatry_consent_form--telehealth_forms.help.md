# Telepsychiatry Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information from patients for telepsychiatry services, including patient and contact information, healthcare provider details, and consent status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Enter your personal information in the "Patient Information" section.
2.  Provide your contact and emergency contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
3.  Specify your healthcare provider in the "Healthcare Provider" section.
4.  Enter information for your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact" section.
5.  Read and agree to the terms of the telepsychiatry agreement in the "Telepsychiatry Agreement" section.
6.  Select your consent status in the "Consent Status" section.
7.  Enter the date and time of signing in the "Signed Date" and "Signed Time" sections respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Patient Information** (text, required/required): Enter your personal details as prompted, including name, email, phone number, and any other relevant information.
*  **Contact Information** (text, required/required): Enter your contact details as prompted, including name, phone number, and email.
*  **Healthcare Provider** (text, required/required): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
*  **Emergency Contact** (text, required/required): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
*  **Telepsychiatry Agreement** (text, required/required): Read and agree to the terms of the telepsychiatry agreement.
*  **Consent Status** (select_one, required/optional): Select whether you are fully signed, unsigned, or partially signed. Please note that this selection does not affect your actual consent status.
*  **Signed Date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date when you signed this form.
*  **Signed Time** (time, required/optional): Enter the time when you signed this form.
*  **Consent Status** (select_one, required/required): Select your consent status.
*  **Emergency Contact** (text, required/required): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
