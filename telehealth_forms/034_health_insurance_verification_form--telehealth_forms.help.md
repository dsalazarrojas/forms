# Health Insurance Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients about their health insurance coverage. It helps healthcare providers verify insurance coverage and ensure that patients have valid coverage for their treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your insurance card.
2. Enter your insurance card number.
3. Enter the name of your insurance provider.
4. Select the type of insurance coverage you have (e.g. Private, Medicare, Medicaid, etc.).
5. Enter the start date of your insurance coverage.
6. Enter the end date of your insurance coverage.
7. Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
8. Enter your healthcare provider's license number.
9. Enter your healthcare provider's address.
10. Provide any additional information about your insurance coverage.
11. Select any other insurance you may have.
12. Sign the form to indicate that you have verified your insurance information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name** (1): Enter your name as it appears on your insurance card.
- **Insurance Card Number** (2): Enter your insurance card number.
- **Insurance Provider** (3): Enter the name of your insurance provider (e.g. Blue Cross, United Healthcare, etc.).
- **Insurance Type** (4): Select the type of insurance coverage you have (e.g. Private, Medicare, Medicaid, etc.).
- **Insurance Start Date** (5): Enter the start date of your insurance coverage.
- **Insurance End Date** (6): Enter the end date of your insurance coverage.
- **Provider Name** (7): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
- **Provider License Number** (8): Enter your healthcare provider's license number.
- **Provider Address** (9): Enter your healthcare provider's address.
- **Additional Information** (10): Provide any additional information about your insurance coverage.
- **Other Insurance** (11): Select any other insurance you may have (e.g. Private, Medicare, etc.).
- **Signature** (12): Sign the form to indicate that you have verified your insurance information.
- **Date** (13): Enter the current date.
- **Time** (14): Enter the current time.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have your insurance card and other required documents with you when completing this form.
- If you are unsure about any of the information, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
- Please sign the form after you have verified your insurance information.
