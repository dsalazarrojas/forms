# Texas Telemedicine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent and contact information from patients before providing telehealth services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please read each field carefully and fill out the required information.
2. If you are unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
3. Make sure to save or print the completed form for your records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info** (patient_info): This is the first page of the form where you will find the following fields: 
  - **Medical History** (medical_history): This field is used to provide a brief medical history. Please include any relevant medical information, such as allergies, medical conditions, or previous surgeries.
  - **Telemedicine Services** (telemedicine_services): This field is used to select the type of telehealth services you are interested in receiving. You can choose from the following options: In-person visits, Phone calls, Video calls, Email messages, Text messages, Secure messaging, or None.
  - **Consent** (consent): This is where you will provide your digital signature to acknowledge that you have read and understood the telehealth services you have selected.
  - **Contact Method** (contact_method): This field is used to select how you would like to be contacted. You can choose from the following options: Phone, Email, Text, Video, or None.
  - **Contact Info** (contact_info): This field is used to provide your contact information for the selected contact method.
  - **Provider Info** (provider_info): This field is used to provide additional information about your healthcare provider. Please note that this is optional.
  - **Digital Signature** (signature): This is where you will provide your digital signature to acknowledge that you have read and understood the telehealth services you have selected.
  - **Patient Agreement** (patient_agreement): This field is used to acknowledge that you understand the telehealth services you will receive and that you agree to follow the guidelines set by your healthcare provider.

Note: This form has two fields that are not required, which are: Medical History, Provider Info, Digital Signature, and Patient Agreement. However, it's best to provide as much information as possible to ensure smooth communication and proper care.
