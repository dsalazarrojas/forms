# Nutrition Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about your nutritional needs, medical conditions, and goals. The data collected will help healthcare professionals create a tailored nutrition consultation plan to support your health and wellness objectives.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read through each section carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2.  Enter your medical conditions, including any relevant details.
3.  Select your nutrition goals, such as weight loss or muscle gain.
4.  Provide any necessary medical history or medication information.
5.  Enter your notes, if any additional information is required.
6.  Outline the next steps you'd like to take for your nutrition plan.
7.  Review and submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Page 1)** (`<patient_information`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Provide your personal and contact details for follow-up communication.

* **Nutrition Needs (Page 1)** (`<nutrition_needs`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Describe your current diet, eating habits, and nutritional requirements.

* **Medical Conditions (Page 2)** (`<medical_conditions`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Select any relevant medical conditions you have, such as Hypertension or Diabetes.

* **Medication (Page 2)** (`<medication`, `text`, required: false):
	+ List any medications you are currently taking.

* **Medical History (Page 2)** (`<medical_history`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Provide details about any significant medical events or surgeries in your past.

* **Nutrition Goals (Page 3)** (`<nutrition_goals`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select your goals, such as Weight Loss or Muscle Gain.

* **Notes (Page 3)** (`<notes`, `note`, required: false):
	+ Enter any additional comments, concerns, or thoughts that might be helpful for your nutrition consultation.

* **Next Steps (Page 4)** (`<next_steps`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Outline the next steps you'd like to take based on your consultation, such as scheduling appointments or purchasing supplements.

## Tips

- Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare professional for clarification.
- The information collected will be treated confidentially and used solely for the purpose of creating a personalized nutrition consultation plan.
