# COVID 19 Contact Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for the COVID-19 contact investigation process. It will help healthcare professionals to understand your interactions with others, identify potential risks, and take necessary precautions to prevent the spread of the virus.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your contact information (Contact Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Email Address, Phone Number, Address, City, State, ZIP Code) in the corresponding fields.
2.  Answer the questions about your exposure to COVID-19 (Exposure Date, Exposure Setting, Exposure Details, Exposure Duration, Close Contact, Mask Worn During Exposure).
3.  Provide information about your current health status (Current Symptoms, Symptom Onset Date, Tested for COVID-19, Test Date, Test Type, Vaccination Status, High Risk Medical Condition).
4.  Inform healthcare professionals about your quarantine status (Quarantine Status, Quarantine Start Date, Quarantine End Date).
5.  Specify your needs and preferences for support during quarantine (Quarantine Support Needed, Monitoring Method, Additional Contacts).
6.  Review and verify the accuracy of the information you have provided.
7.  Sign the form with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name (1)**: Enter your full legal name.
* **Date of Birth (2)**: Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Gender (3)**: Select the correct gender that matches your identity.
* **Email Address (4)**: Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone Number (5)**: Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Street Address (6)**: Enter your complete address where you reside.
* **City (7)**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State (8)**: Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **ZIP Code (9)**: Enter your postal code.
* **Interview Date (10)**: This field should be automatically filled by the healthcare professional who filled the form.
* **Interviewer Name (11)**: This field should be automatically filled by the healthcare professional who filled the form.
* **Associated Case ID (12)**: Enter the case number assigned to you.
* **Index Case Name (13)**: Enter the name of the person who exposed you to COVID-19 (if applicable).
* **Relationship to Case (14)**: Select how you know the person who exposed you to COVID-19 (e.g., household member, family member, friend, etc.).
* **Exposure Date (15)**: Enter the date of the exposure.
* **Exposure Setting (16)**: Select where the exposure occurred (e.g., household, workplace, school, etc.).
* **Exposure Details (17)**: Provide a description of the exposure.
* **Exposure Duration (18)**: Select how long you were exposed to the individual.
* **Close Contact (19)**: Check yes if you were within 6 feet of the infected person for 15 minutes or more.
* **Mask Worn During Exposure (20)**: Select if either you or the person exposed wore a mask.
* **Current Symptoms (21)**: Check all the symptoms you are experiencing now.
* **Symptom Onset Date (22)**: Enter the date your symptoms started (if you are experiencing symptoms).
* **Tested for COVID-19 (23)**: Select the outcome of the COVID-19 test (if you've taken one).
* **Test Date (24)**: Enter the date of the COVID-19 test (if you've taken one).
* **Test Type (25)**: Select the type of COVID-19 test taken.
* **Vaccination Status (26)**: Select your COVID-19 vaccination status.
* **High Risk Medical Condition (27)**: Select if you have any medical condition that increases your risk of COVID-19.
* **Quarantine Status (28)**: Select your current quarantine status.
* **Quarantine Start Date (29)**: Enter the date when you started quarantine.
* **Quarantine End Date (30)**: Enter the date you can end quarantine.
* **Quarantine Support Needed (31)**: Select the types of support you need during quarantine.
* **Monitoring Method (32)**: Select how you will be monitored during quarantine.
* **Additional Close Contacts (33)**: Provide the names and contact information of others you've exposed to COVID-19.
* **Interviewer Notes (34)**: Enter any additional comments or observations from the healthcare professional.
* **Follow-up Required (35)**: Select if you need follow-up with the healthcare professional.
* **Follow-up Date (36)**: Enter the date for follow-up communication.
* **Contact Signature (37)**: Sign the form with your full name.
* **Interviewer Signature (38)**: Sign the form with your full name.

## Tips

* Ensure that you answer honestly and accurately.
* Review the information before signing to ensure it is correct.
* Contact a healthcare professional if you are unsure about any of the questions or fields.
* This form should only be filled out by the person exposed to COVID-19 or their representative.
