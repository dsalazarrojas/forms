# telehealth patient consent form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The telehealth patient consent form is a mandatory form used for patients to provide consent for telehealth services. It is a crucial document that ensures patients understand their rights and responsibilities when receiving telehealth services. Please ensure you are completing this form for a legitimate reason and not duplicating effort.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each page carefully to ensure you have filled out all the required information.
2.  Complete each field according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Patient Information
#### 1. **Patient Information**: Provide your personal details as follows.
- **Patient Name** (text): Enter your full name as it appears on your official ID.
- **Date of Birth** (text): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Contact Information** (text): Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.

### Page 2: Telehealth Services
#### 2. **Telehealth Services**: You may choose to receive telehealth services or not. Select 'Yes' if you agree to receive telehealth services, or 'No' if you do not.
- **Telehealth Services**: This question is about whether you are comfortable with receiving telehealth services, which may include phone or video calls.

### Page 3: Consent to Telehealth Services
#### 3. **Consent to Telehealth Services**: Select your answer to this question carefully.
- **Consent**: This question is about whether you give your consent to telehealth services. Select 'Yes' if you consent, or 'No' if you do not.

### Page 4: Digital Signature
#### 4. **Digital Signature**: Please sign with your digital signature to confirm you have read and understood the form.
- **Digital Signature**: This is a virtual signature that serves as a legally binding agreement. Please sign with your digital device.

## Tips
- Please review each page carefully before proceeding.
- Ensure you understand the questions and instructions before answering.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your healthcare provider.
- This form is designed to be completed by you, the patient. Ensure you are the intended signatory.
- Save and print this form for your records.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your telehealth provider.
