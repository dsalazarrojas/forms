# Telemedicine Consultant Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the quality of telemedicine consulting services provided to patients. It is designed to gather feedback on the consultant's performance, including their overall rating, feedback, and additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name and contact information (phone number and email) to ensure the evaluation is linked to the correct patient.
2. Select the overall rating (1-10) and provide a brief reason for the rating.
3. If applicable, select the follow-up status (yes, no, or pending).
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback about the consultant's service.
5. Enter the date and time of the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears in their medical records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address for contact purposes.
* **Overall Rating** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the consultant's performance on a scale of 1-10.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any comments or feedback about the consultant's service.
* **Rating Date** (`rating_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Additional Note** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the consultant's service.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the patient's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Sent** (`email_sent`, select_one, optional): Indicate if an email was sent to the patient as part of the consultation.
* **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the telemedicine consultant.
* **Reason For Rating** (`rating_reason`, text, optional): Provide a brief reason for the rating.
* **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple evaluation options (excellent, good, fair, poor).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the consultant's service.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, select_one, optional): Indicate if follow-up actions were taken.
* **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the follow-up action.
* **Follow Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the follow-up action.
* **Consultant Notes** (`consultant_notes`, text, required): Enter any notes or comments about the consultant's service.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, text, required): Enter the patient's ID or unique identifier.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the evaluation.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, required): Enter the date the evaluation was submitted.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, time, required): Enter the time the evaluation was submitted.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who updated the evaluation.
* **Updated On** (`updated_on`, date, required): Enter the date the evaluation was updated.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, time, required): Enter the time the evaluation was updated.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, text, required): Enter the name or ID of the person or organization the evaluation was submitted to.

This guide is designed to be a comprehensive resource for users to complete the Telemedicine Consultant Evaluation Form accurately and efficiently.
