# Dermatology Telehealth Medical Examination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a telehealth medical examination form for dermatology-related issues, designed for remote consultations. It helps healthcare professionals assess and treat various dermatological conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Patient Info" section with your personal details, such as your name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. In the "Medical History" section, select the relevant options that apply to your medical history, such as Skin conditions, Allergies, and Medications.
3. In the "Exam" section, describe your current condition and any relevant details about your symptoms.
4. In the "Upload Photos" section, upload any photos of your skin condition to help the healthcare professional assess your condition better.
5. In the "Diagnosis" section, select the condition that you think you may have based on your symptoms and medical history.
6. In the "Treatment Plan" section, choose the recommended treatment plan for your condition.
7. In the "Next Steps" section, describe any next steps you would like to take, such as scheduling an appointment or follow-up appointment.
8. In the "Medical Info" section, provide any additional medical information that may be relevant to your condition.
9. In the "Payment Info" section, provide your payment information, such as credit card details or bank transfer information.
10. In the "Payment Method" section, select the payment method you would like to use for your consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (Text)**: Fill in your personal details, such as name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Medical History (Select Multiple)**: Select any relevant medical history options that apply to you, such as Skin conditions, Allergies, and Medications.
* **Exam (Text)**: Describe your current condition and any relevant details about your symptoms.
* **Upload Photos (Text)**: Upload any photos of your skin condition to help the healthcare professional assess your condition better.
* **Diagnosis (Select One)**: Select the condition that you think you may have based on your symptoms and medical history.
* **Treatment Plan (Select One)**: Choose the recommended treatment plan for your condition, such as Topical cream, Oral medication, or Phototherapy.
* **Next Steps (Text)**: Describe any next steps you would like to take, such as scheduling an appointment or follow-up appointment.
* **Medical Info (Text)**: Provide any additional medical information that may be relevant to your condition.
* **Payment Info (Text)**: Provide your payment information, such as credit card details or bank transfer information.
* **Payment Method (Select One)**: Select the payment method you would like to use for your consultation, such as Credit Card, Bank transfer, or PayPal.
