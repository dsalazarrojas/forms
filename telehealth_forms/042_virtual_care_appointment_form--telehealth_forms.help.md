# Virtual Care Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users for virtual care appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your user input fields:
	* **Name**: Enter your first and last name.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
2. Select your preferred:
	* **Date**: Choose the date of the appointment from the calendar.
	* **Time**: Choose the time of the appointment.
3. Provide provider information:
	* **Provider Name**: Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
	* **Specialty**: Enter the specialty of your healthcare provider (e.g. doctor, nurse practitioner, etc.).
	* **Phone Provider**: Enter the phone number of your healthcare provider.
4. Additional context:
	* **Reason for Visit**: Explain the reason for your visit.
	* **Location**: Enter the location where you will be visiting your healthcare provider.
5. Enter other details:
	* **Appointment Duration**: Enter the duration of the appointment.
	* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your appointment.
6. Confirm:
	* Review your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (Page 1): 
	+ **Name**: Enter your name for reference.
	+ **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
	+ **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Name** (Page 2): 
	+ Enter your healthcare provider's name.
* **Provider Information** (Page 2): 
	+ **Provider Name**: Enter your healthcare provider's name.
	+ **Specialty**: Enter your healthcare provider's specialty (e.g. doctor, nurse practitioner, etc.).
	+ **Phone Provider**: Enter your healthcare provider's phone number.
* **Date and Time** (Page 3): 
	+ **Date**: Choose the date of the appointment from the calendar.
	+ **Time**: Choose the time of the appointment.
* **Additional Context** (Page 4): 
	+ **Reason for Visit**: Explain the reason for your visit.
	+ **Location**: Enter the location where you will be visiting your healthcare provider.
* **Provider Information** (Page 5): 
	+ **Date Provider**: Choose the date of the provider's availability from the calendar.
	+ **Time Provider**: Choose the time of the provider's availability.
* **Appointment Details** (Page 6): 
	+ **Appointment Duration**: Enter the duration of the appointment.
	+ **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your appointment.
* **Language** (Page 7): 
	+ **Language**: Select your preferred language for the appointment.

## Tips

* Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* Enter accurate and complete information to ensure proper communication with your healthcare provider.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.
