## Purpose
This form is used to collect medical and client information for new clients. It is a crucial step in the onboarding process to ensure that our telehealth team has a comprehensive understanding of the client's medical history and current status. Please complete this form accurately to ensure a smooth and effective service experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and select the options that best describe the client's situation.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. If any questions or sections do not apply, select "No" or leave them blank.
