## Telehealth Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telehealth Release Form is used to obtain consent from patients for the release of their medical information to third-party providers for telehealth services. This form is a mandatory step in the telehealth process to ensure that patients understand and agree to the release of their information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information for each field:
	* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's details, such as their name, address, and date of birth.
	* **Get Patient Signatures**: Choose "Yes" to obtain the patient's signature for the release of their medical information.
	* **Release Treatment**: Choose "Yes" to release treatment information for the patient.
	* **Date**: Enter the date when the patient signed the release form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (Text Field): This field is where you will enter the patient's details. Please enter their full name, address, and date of birth as it appears on their medical record.
* **Get Patient Signatures** (Select One): This field is used to obtain the patient's signature for the release of their medical information. Select "Yes" to proceed.
* **Release Treatment** (Select One): This field is used to release treatment information for the patient. Select "Yes" to release treatment information.
* **Date** (Date Field): This field is used to enter the date when the patient signed the release form. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
