# Patient Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient information for telehealth services, including demographic details, medical history, and other relevant medical information. It is intended for patients to provide accurate and up-to-date information about themselves, which will be reviewed by healthcare providers before consultations or treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the Date of Birth field (MM/DD/YYYY format).
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter your email address in the Email field.
6. Enter your home address in the Address field.
7. Describe your medical condition in the Medical Condition field.
8. Provide your insurance provider's name in the Insurance Provider field.
9. Share your medical history in the Medical History field.
10. List your emergency contact's name, phone number, and relationship with you in the Emergency Contact field.
11. Write your consent for medical treatment in the Consent field.
12. Specify any medical treatment or procedures you are currently undergoing in the Medical Treatment field.
13. Describe the medical procedure you are interested in or have undergone in the Medical Procedure field.
14. Finally, please sign your name in the Signature field to confirm your identity and consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your given name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXX or (XXX) XXX XXXX.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format [username@domain.com](mailto:username@domain.com).
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your current address as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): Describe your medical condition, including any chronic or acute conditions you are experiencing.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, required): Enter the name of your insurance provider (e.g., Blue Cross).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Share your medical history, including any previous treatments, surgeries, or health concerns.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): List your emergency contact's name, phone number, and relationship with you (e.g., spouse, parent, sibling).
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required): Write your consent for medical treatment, indicating that you agree to receive care from our healthcare provider.
* **Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment`, text, required): Specify any medical treatment or procedures you are currently undergoing.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, text, required): Describe the medical procedure you are interested in or have undergone.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Please sign your name to confirm your identity and consent.
