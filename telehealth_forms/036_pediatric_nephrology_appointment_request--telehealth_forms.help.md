# pediatric_nephrology_appointment_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a pediatric nephrology appointment request.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter the patient's first name in the "First name" field.
* Enter the patient's last name in the "Last name" field.
* Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of birth" field in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* Enter the patient's contact email and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields, respectively.
* Indicate if the patient has a medical specialty in the "Medical specialty" field.
* Select the type of visit in the "Visit type" field, which may include "Yes" or "No".
* Select the frequency of visits in the "Visit frequency" field, which may include "Yes" or "No".
* Indicate the patient's preferred contact method in the "Preferred contact method" field.
* Select the date and time of the preferred visit in the "Visit preferred date" field.
* Indicate the type of appointment in the "Appointment type" field.
* Enter the doctor's first name in the "Doctor first name" field.
* Enter the doctor's last name in the "Doctor last name" field.
* Enter the doctor's phone number in the "Doctor phone" field.
* Enter the hospital and department in the "Hospital" and "Department" fields, respectively.
* Enter the doctor's specialties in the "Doctor specialty 1 to 9" fields, as applicable.
