## Coronavirus Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coronavirus Screening Form is a set of questions designed to identify potential risks of coronavirus exposure. This form helps public health officials understand your situation and provide guidance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct answer for each question from the provided options.
2. Answer all questions, even if you're unsure or do not have a history of the condition.
3. Provide any additional information in the "Note" field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **screen_1**: This is the first question. Select the answer that best describes your situation.
* **question_1**: Have you traveled outside the country? If you've traveled recently, answer "Yes".
* **symptom_1**: Do you have a fever? Select High, Low, or No, depending on your current condition.
* **symptom_2**: Do you have a cough? Select High, Low, or No, depending on your current condition.
* **question_2**: Do you have a history of respiratory disease? If you've been diagnosed or have a condition, answer "Yes".
* **symptom_3**: Do you experience shortness of breath? Select High, Low, or No, depending on your current condition.
* **question_3**: How long have you had your symptoms? Select the timeframe that best describes your situation (Less than 2 days, 3-4 days, 5-7 days).
* **note_1**: Is there anything else you'd like to add or mention about your current situation? Use this field for any additional information that's not covered in the questions.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
- If you're unsure or do not have a history of the condition, answer accordingly.
- If you have any additional information to share, use the "Note" field.
- This form is not a substitute for medical diagnosis. If you're experiencing severe symptoms, seek immediate medical attention.
