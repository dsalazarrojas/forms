# Telehealth Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telehealth Informed Consent Form is used to obtain informed consent from patients for telehealth services. This form is completed by the provider and is used to ensure that the patient understands their rights, responsibilities, and expectations for telehealth services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Make sure you are in a quiet, private space where you can focus on the form.
2. Review the form carefully and ask questions if you are unsure about any of the information.
3. Enter the required information in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Telehealth Informed Consent Form** (`telehealth_informed_consent_form`, note, required): This is the title of the form, which is not a field to fill in, but rather a heading to identify the form.
* **patient_id_label** (`patient_id`, number, optional): This is the patient's identification number. Enter the patient's ID number, if applicable.
* **patient_name_label** (`patient_name`, text, optional): This is the patient's name. Please enter the patient's full name.
* **provider_id_label** (`provider_id`, number, optional): This is the provider's identification number. Enter the provider's ID number, if applicable.
* **provider_name_label** (`provider_name`, text, optional): This is the provider's name. Please enter the provider's full name.
* **date_of_service_label** (`date_of_service`, date, optional): This is the date of the telehealth service. Enter the date of service in the format MM/DD/YY or MM/DD/YY.
