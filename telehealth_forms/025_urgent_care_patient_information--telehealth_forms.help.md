This form is used to collect information from patients seeking urgent care. The form is designed to gather details about the patient's contact information, emergency contacts, allergies, medical history, and other relevant information to provide prompt and appropriate care. 

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately to provide the best possible care.
2.  Please make sure to submit the form as soon as possible so that the medical team can process it and attend to your needs.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **Patient Details** (`patient_details`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the patient's condition and the reason for their urgent care visit.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's contact information, including phone number and address, so that we can reach them for further instructions or follow-up care.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required/optional): List any emergency contacts that we should be aware of, such as family members or caregivers, so that we can reach them in case of an emergency.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required/optional): If the patient has any allergies or sensitivities, please list them here so that we can take necessary precautions.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, required/optional): Describe the patient's symptoms or conditions that require urgent care.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Share any relevant medical history or previous conditions that may impact the patient's care.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required/optional): This field is for the patient or their representative to grant consent for treatment or procedures.
* **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, text, required/optional): Add any additional information or comments that will help the medical team provide the best possible care.
