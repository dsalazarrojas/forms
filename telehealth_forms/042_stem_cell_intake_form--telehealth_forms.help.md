# Stem Cell Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic and medical information from patients for telehealth purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each field explanation below to ensure you provide accurate information.
2. Fill out each field as instructed, and take your time to ensure accuracy.
3. If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities or healthcare professionals for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Age** (`Age`, number, optional): Enter your age in whole numbers (e.g., 30).
* **Height** (`Height`, text, optional): Enter your height in centimeters or meters (e.g., 175 cm or 1.75 m).
* **Weight** (`Weight`, text, optional): Enter your weight in kilograms or pounds (e.g., 70 kg or 154 lbs).
* **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`, text, optional): Describe your medical condition or any previous medical conditions you've experienced.
* **Medications** (`Medications`, text, optional): List any medications you're currently taking or have taken in the past.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies you have or have had in the past.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for secure communication.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, optional): Describe any previous medical treatments, surgeries, or conditions.
* **Surgical History** (`Surgical History`, text, optional): Describe any previous surgeries or surgical procedures you've had.
* **Medical Procedure** (`Medical Procedure`, text, optional): Describe any upcoming medical procedures or appointments.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, text, optional): Identify your emergency contact person's name.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`Emergency Contact Email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact person.
* **Medical Team** (`Medical Team`, text, optional): Identify your healthcare team's name.
* **Medical Specialty** (`Medical Specialty`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have a medical specialty, or 'No' if not.
* **Medical Department** (`Medical Department`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you're in a medical department, or 'No' if not.
* **Medical Department 2** (`Medical Department 2`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you're in medical department 2, or 'No' if not.
* **Medical Department 3** (`Medical Department 3`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you're in medical department 3, or 'No' if not.
* **Medical Department 4** (`Medical Department 4`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you're in medical department 4, or 'No' if not.
* **Medical Department 5** (`Medical Department 5`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you're in medical department 5, or 'No' if not.
