# Gut Guru Medical Consent And Indemnity Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect medical information from users, including their personal details, contact information, medical history, allergies, and medical conditions. This information will be used to assess their medical status and provide relevant care or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name and date of birth.
2. If applicable, select your contact information (e.g., email or phone number) to ensure we can contact you in case of an emergency.
3. Describe your medical history, including any past illnesses or injuries.
4. List any allergies or medical conditions you have.
5. Indicate whether you are currently taking any medication.
6. Provide information about any additional medical conditions you have.
7. List an emergency contact person and their contact information.
8. Indicate whether you have given consent for medical treatment or procedures.
9. Describe any other medical history you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, `text`, required/optional): Fill in your personal details, including your name and date of birth.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `select_one`, optional): If applicable, select your contact information (e.g., email or phone number) to ensure we can contact you in case of an emergency.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): Describe your medical history, including any past illnesses or injuries.
- **Consent** (`consent`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether you are currently consenting to medical treatment or procedures.
- **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_one`, optional): List any allergies you have.
- **Medication** (`medication`, `text`, optional): Indicate whether you are currently taking any medication.
- **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, optional): Provide information about any additional medical conditions you have.
- **Medical Condition 2** (`medical_condition_2`, `text`, optional): List any other medical conditions you have.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): List an emergency contact person and their contact information.
- **Other Consent** (`consent_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether you have given consent for other medical treatment or procedures.
- **Other Medical History** (`medical_history_2`, `text`, optional): Describe any other medical history you have.
- **Medical Condition 3** (`medical_condition_3`, `text`, optional): Provide information about any additional medical conditions you have.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields (marked with *) to ensure your form is complete.
- Use the provided fields to describe any medical conditions or allergies you have.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
