# Telehealth Technology Research Grant Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telehealth Technology Research Grant Application form is designed to collect necessary information from applicants for a telehealth technology research grant. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure your application is considered for the grant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read through the form carefully and ensure you understand each field's purpose.
* Fill out the form in a single session, as saving and resuming later may cause issues.
* Ensure you have all required information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant
* **Applicant** (`Applicant`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can enter your name or the name of the person submitting the application.
* **Project** (`Project`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your telehealth technology research project.
* **Project Details** (`Project Details`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional details about your project, such as goals, objectives, or methodology.
* **Organization** (`Organization`, `text`, required: false): If you're representing an organization, enter the name of the organization submitting the application.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
* Ensure you have all required information before submitting the form.
