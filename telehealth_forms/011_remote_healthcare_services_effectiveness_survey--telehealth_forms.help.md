# Remote Healthcare Services Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect feedback on the quality and effectiveness of telehealth services provided to you. Your input will help healthcare providers understand your needs and improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly, considering your recent experience with the telehealth service.
2. Select the best option that reflects your true feelings and experiences.
3. If you need assistance or clarification on any question, do not hesitate to reach out to the service provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Telehealth Form** (`telehealth_form`, text, not required): 
Please enter any comments, suggestions, or feedback about your telehealth experience. This is your opportunity to share your thoughts on how the service could be improved.

* **Patient Experience** (`patient_experience`, number, not required): 
Rate your overall experience with the telehealth service, considering how it met your needs and expectations.

* **Telemedicine Quality** (`telemedicine_quality`, select one, not required): 
Select from the options:
	+ High
	+ Low
	+ Very Low

* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select multiple, not required): 
Select all that apply:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

* **Communication** (`communication`, text, not required): 
Please provide any comments or feedback about the communication with the telehealth staff, such as response time, clarity, or helpfulness.

* **Staff Availability** (`staff_availability`, time, not required): 
Enter the time you were able to contact the telehealth staff or access the service.

* **Patient Satisfaction** (`patient_satisfaction`, select one, not required): 
Select one:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied

* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, not required): 
Please share any additional comments or suggestions for improving the telehealth service.

* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, select one, not required): 
Select one:
	+ Yes

* **Follow Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, date, not required): 
Enter the date and time of your last follow-up with the telehealth service.
