# telemental_health_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients and healthcare providers for telehealth services. It covers the basics of telehealth, patient rights, and responsibilities, as well as healthcare provider and healthcare organization requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information**: Enter your personal details and contact information.
2. Click "Next" to proceed to the consent section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (1)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number. This information is used to contact you for any follow-up questions or to send you appointment reminders.
* **Consent (2)**: Check the box to indicate that you have read and understood the terms of the telehealth services. This is a required field.
* **Healthcare Provider (3)**: Enter the name and contact information of your healthcare provider. This will help ensure that your healthcare provider is aware of the terms of the telehealth services.
* **Healthcare Organization (4)**: Enter the name and contact information of the healthcare organization providing the telehealth services. This will help ensure that you are aware of their policies and procedures.
* **Date (5)**: Enter the date you are completing this form. This helps track the date of your informed consent.
* **Signature (6)**: Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the terms of the telehealth services. This is not required, but it serves as a confirmation of your consent.
* **Healthcare Provider Informed Consent (7)**: Check the box to indicate that your healthcare provider has read and understood the terms of the telehealth services. This is not required, but it helps ensure that your healthcare provider is aware of the terms.
* **Patient Informed Consent (8)**: Check the box to indicate that you have read and understood the terms of the telehealth services. This is a required field.
* **Healthcare Organization Informed Consent (9)**: Check the box to indicate that you have read and understood the policies and procedures of the healthcare organization. This is not required, but it helps ensure that you are aware of their policies.
* **Telemental Health Consent Form (10)**: This is the actual informed consent form. Check the box to indicate that you have read and understood the terms of the telehealth services. This is a required field.

## Tips

* Make sure to read and understand the terms of the telehealth services before completing this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your healthcare provider or the healthcare organization providing the services.
* This form is not a substitute for a face-to-face consultation with your healthcare provider. Please follow their advice and instructions for your specific needs.
