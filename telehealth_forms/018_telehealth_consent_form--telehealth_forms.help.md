# telehealth_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for patients to provide their consent for telehealth services, which allow healthcare providers to offer medical care remotely through phone, video, and messaging. This form ensures that patients understand and agree to the terms of telehealth services, including the use of electronic communication methods and the sharing of their personal health information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Patient Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the email field.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** in the text field.
4. Select a **Date of Signature** in the calendar picker or manually enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Select a **Time of Signature** in the time picker or manually enter it in 12-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name (Patient Name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your active and valid email address that is checked regularly by the healthcare provider for communication and appointment reminders.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)**: Enter your active and valid phone number that the healthcare provider can use to contact you.
* **Date of Signature (Date of Signature)**: Select or manually enter the date you are signing this consent form, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Signature (Time of Signature)**: Select or manually enter the time you are signing this consent form, in 12-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).

## Tips
* Make sure to use your active and valid email address to ensure timely communication with your healthcare provider.
* Be cautious when selecting your phone number, as it will be used for important notifications and reminders.
* Double-check your **Date of Signature** and **Time of Signature** to ensure accuracy.
