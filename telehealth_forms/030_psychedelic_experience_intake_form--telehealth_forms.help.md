# Psychedelic Experience Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychedelic Experience Intake Form is a structured questionnaire designed to gather information about a user's psychedelic experience. This form is intended to help users provide a detailed account of their experience, which can be useful for research, treatment, or personal reflection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible. You may skip questions that do not apply to your experience or are not relevant to the specific questions being asked. Be honest and detailed in your responses, and try to provide specific examples or descriptions to support your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (page 1): This section gathers basic information about you, the person completing the form. Please enter your name and any other relevant details that might be helpful for future reference.
* **Psychedelic Type** (page 2): Please select the type of psychedelic you experienced from the list of options. If the type is not listed, please describe it in the text field provided.
* **Dosage** (page 3): If applicable, please enter the dosage of the psychedelic you consumed. This can be a helpful reference for understanding the effects of the drug.
* **Start Time** (page 4): Enter the time you began the experience, either in 24-hour clock format or using the provided date picker.
* **Duration** (page 5): Enter the length of time you were experiencing the effects of the psychedelic. This can help understand how long the effects lasted and how they developed over time.
* **Setting** (page 6): Describe the environment in which you experienced the psychedelic. This can include the physical location, weather, and any other relevant details that might have contributed to your experience.
* **Setting Type** (page 7): Select the type of setting from the provided options. If the setting is more complex or not listed, please describe it in the text field provided.
* **Other Setting** (page 8): If there was any other setting or factor that contributed to your experience, please describe it here.
* **Mood** (page 9): Select all the emotions or moods you experienced during the psychedelic from the list of options.
* **Visuals** (page 10): Select all the visual effects or sensations you experienced during the psychedelic from the list of options.
* **Feelings** (page 11): Select all the emotional or physical feelings you experienced during the psychedelic from the list of options.
* **Bodily Sensations** (page 12): Select all the bodily sensations you experienced during the psychedelic from the list of options.
* **Altered Senses** (page 13): Select all the altered senses you experienced during the psychedelic from the list of options.
* **Emotions** (page 14): Select all the emotions you experienced during the psychedelic from the list of options.
* **Thoughts** (page 15): Select all the thoughts or ideas you experienced during the psychedelic from the list of options.
* **Memories** (page 16): Select all the memories you experienced or were reminded of during the psychedelic from the list of options.
* **Music** (page 17): Select all the musical or auditory sensations or effects you experienced during the psychedelic from the list of options.
* **Visuals Permanent** (page 18): Select all the permanent visual effects or changes you experienced during the psychedelic from the list of options.
* **Visuals Transient** (page 19): Select all the temporary visual effects or changes you experienced during the psychedelic from the list of options.
* **Aftereffects** (page 20): Select all the aftereffects or lingering effects you experienced after the psychedelic from the list of options.
* **Suggestions** (page 21): If you have any suggestions or recommendations for future research or experiences, please describe them here.
* **Comments** (page 22): If you have any additional comments or thoughts about your experience, please describe them here.
* **Other** (page 23): If you have any other details or aspects of your experience that are not listed above, please describe them here.
* **Rating** (page 24): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being negative and 5 being positive.
* **Rating Other** (page 25): If you have any other thoughts or feelings about your experience that are not captured in the previous rating question, please describe them here.
