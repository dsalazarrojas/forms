# Teledermatology Patient Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teledermatology Patient Registration Form is designed to collect essential patient information for telemedicine consultations with dermatology providers. This form helps ensure that patients provide accurate and complete details about their medical history, condition, and appointment information. By filling out this form, patients can facilitate a smooth and efficient telemedicine consultation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your patient name, email address, and phone number to identify yourself.
2. Provide your birthdate to help us better understand your medical history.
3. Enter your patient ID, if applicable, to streamline the consultation process.
4. Choose "Yes" or "No" for Teledermatology Visit Type to indicate the purpose of your consultation.
5. Describe your medical condition and diagnosis to help us understand your situation.
6. Add any relevant patient notes to provide additional context.
7. Enter your provider's name and any notes they may have about your consultation.
8. Indicate if a follow-up is scheduled and choose the follow-up status.
9. Enter the follow-up date and provider, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Birthdate** (`birthdate`, date, optional): Provide your birthdate to help us better understand your medical history.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, number, optional): If you have a patient ID, please enter it here to expedite the consultation process.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, optional): Enter your provider's name for our records.
* **Patient Medical History** (`patient_medical_history`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions, allergies, or concerns you may have.
* **Teledermatology Visit Date** (`teledermatology_visit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your telemedicine consultation.
* **Teledermatology Visit Type** (`teledermatology_visit_type`, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" if this is an initial consultation, "No" if this is a follow-up appointment.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Describe your medical condition or concern.
* **Medical Diagnosis** (`medical_diagnosis`, text, optional): If you have been diagnosed with a medical condition, please enter the diagnosis here.
* **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional information about your condition or concerns.
* **Provider Notes** (`provider_notes`, text, optional): Please add any notes from your provider about your consultation.
* **Follow-Up Scheduled** (`follow_up_scheduled`, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" if you have a follow-up appointment scheduled, "No" otherwise.
* **Follow-Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your follow-up appointment, if applicable.
* **Follow-Up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_one, optional): Choose "Active" if your follow-up is active, "Inactive" otherwise.
* **Follow-Up Provider** (`follow_up_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of your follow-up provider, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Enter your information in a clear and concise manner.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form is for telemedicine consultations only, and your provider will guide you through the process.
* If you have any medical equipment or devices that may affect your consultation, please inform your provider before the appointment.
