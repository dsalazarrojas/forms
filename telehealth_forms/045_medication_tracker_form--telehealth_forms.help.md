# Medication Tracker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track a patient's medication schedule, including dosage instructions, frequency, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the medication in the "Medication Name" field.
3. Describe the dosage instructions for the medication in the "Dosage Instructions" field.
4. Enter the number of dosages for the medication in the "Dosages" field.
5. Choose the frequency of the medication from the "Frequency" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the start date of the medication in the "Start Date" field.
7. Enter the end date of the medication in the "End Date" field.
8. Enter the start time of the medication in the "Start Time" field.
9. Enter the end time of the medication in the "End Time" field.
10. Enter the medication dose in the "Medication Dose" field.
11. Enter any additional notes about the medication in the "Notes" field.
12. Enter the contact information for the medication in the "Contact" field.
13. Select the assigned user for the medication from the "Assigned User" dropdown menu.
14. Enter the patient's age in the "Patient Age" field.
15. Enter the contact email for the patient's healthcare provider in the "Contact Email" field.
16. Select the patient's relationship to the healthcare provider from the "Patient Relationship" dropdown menu.
17. Enter any relevant medical condition information for the patient in the "Medical Condition" field.
18. Enter the form of the medication (e.g. pill, liquid, etc.) in the "Medication Form" field.
19. Enter the unit of measurement for the medication dosage (e.g. milligrams, etc.) in the "Dosage Frequency Unit" field.
20. Enter any additional dosage instructions for the medication in the "Medication Form Dosage Instructions" field.
21. Enter the patient's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
22. Enter the patient's medical history in the "Patient Medical History" field.
23. Enter the medical record number for the patient in the "Medical Record Number" field.
24. Enter the strength of the medication (e.g. 10mg, etc.) in the "Medication Strength" field.
25. Enter a description of the medication dosage form (e.g. capsule, etc.) in the "Dosage Form Description" field.
