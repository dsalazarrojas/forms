# telemedicine_e_visit_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from patients before a virtual medical consultation (e-visit) with a healthcare provider. The form helps the provider to prepare for the e-visit and ensures that the patient has all the necessary information to provide accurate and safe medical care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide as much detail as possible, especially for any medical conditions or allergies.
3. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_header** (Form Header): A note field that serves as a header for the form.
* **patient_info** (Text): This is a text field where you can provide any additional information about yourself, such as your contact information or any previous medical history.
* **appointment_details** (Text): Enter your desired appointment date and time, or provide any other information about your appointment preferences.
* **medical_history** (Text): Please provide any relevant medical history, including allergies, previous diagnoses, or surgeries.
* **medical_condition** (Select Multiple): Check 'Yes' if you have any of the listed medical conditions.
* **appointment_date** (Date): Enter the date you would like to schedule the e-visit.
* **appointment_time** (Time): Enter the time you would like to schedule the e-visit.
* **doctor_info** (Select Multiple): Check 'Yes' if you would like the healthcare provider to have access to your personal health information, such as your medical history or medication list.
* **patient_notes** (Text): Leave any additional notes for the healthcare provider.
* **doctor_notes** (Text): This is where the healthcare provider will take notes during the e-visit. Please do not fill this out.
* **form_footer** (Form Footer): A note field that serves as a footer for the form.
* **phone_number** (Number): Enter your phone number, in case the healthcare provider needs to contact you directly.

## Tips
* If you are experiencing an emergency, please call your local emergency number (such as 911 in the US) or seek immediate attention from a healthcare professional.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
