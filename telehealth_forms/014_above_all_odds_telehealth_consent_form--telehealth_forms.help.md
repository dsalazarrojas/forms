# Above All Odds Telehealth Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the informed consent of clients for telehealth services provided by Above All Odds. It ensures that clients understand the terms and conditions of telehealth services, including limitations, confidentiality, and emergency procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Read each section carefully** before signing. This form is divided into two sections: "Understanding Telehealth" and "Consent Agreement." Each section explains your rights and responsibilities as a client.
2. **Enter your personal details**, including your full name, date of birth, email address, phone number, and current address. These are required fields.
3. **Indicate your understanding** of telehealth services, including its limitations, confidentiality, and emergency procedures. Select "Yes, I understand" for each of these options.
4. **Provide emergency contact information**, including the name, phone number, and relationship to the emergency contact. This is required.
5. **Sign and date the consent** by signing the "Date of consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or driver's license.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address**: Enter your personal email address where you can be reached for communication regarding your telehealth services.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your personal phone number where you can be reached for emergencies or appointments.
* **Current Address**: Enter your current address where you can be reached for delivery of services or emergencies.
* **I understand that telehealth involves the delivery of health services using electronic communications and technology**: This is a critical understanding of telehealth services.
* **I understand that telehealth has potential limitations, including technology failures and that it may not be suitable for all conditions**: Acknowledge the potential risks and limitations of telehealth services.
* **I understand that while reasonable steps are taken to protect confidentiality, electronic communication carries inherent risks**: Understand the risks associated with confidentiality in electronic communication.
* **I understand that telehealth is not appropriate for emergencies and I should call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room in case of an emergency**: Acknowledge the limitations of telehealth services in emergency situations.
* **Consent to Telehealth Treatment**: Indicate your agreement to receive telehealth services from Above All Odds.
* **I understand that I may withdraw my consent and discontinue telehealth services at any time**: Understand your right to revoke consent.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact**: Describe your relationship to the emergency contact.
* **Date of Consent**: Enter the date you are signing this consent form.

Note: This form is a legally binding document. Please sign and date it carefully before submitting it to the service provider.
