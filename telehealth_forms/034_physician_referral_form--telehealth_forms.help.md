# Physician Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Physician Referral Form" is used for referring patients to other physicians for further treatment or consultation. It is meant to gather information about the patient and the referral details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for each of the "Patient Referral", "Physician Referring", "Physician Title", and "Specialty" fields, depending on the information about the patient and the referring physician.
2. Enter the "Referral Reason" field if necessary, to provide additional context for the referral.
3. Choose a "Referral Date" if applicable, for tracking purposes.
4. Enter the "Physician Name", "Physician Email", and "Physician Phone" fields, if applicable, for direct communication with the referring physician.
5. Enter the "Patient Name", "Patient Email", and "Patient Phone" fields, if applicable, for direct communication with the patient or their family.
6. Enter the "Patient Address" field if necessary, for logistical or administrative purposes.
7. Enter the "Reason for Referral" field if necessary, to provide additional context for the referral.
8. Finally, enter any "Referral Note" if necessary, for additional information about the referral.
