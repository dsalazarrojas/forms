# Telehealth Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telehealth Waiver Form is used to obtain waivers or authorizations for medical care or services, typically in a telehealth context. It ensures that both the patient and healthcare provider understand their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the questions and sections.
2. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Sign and date the form as required.
4. Submit the form to the appropriate authority or healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Waiver Information (Page 1)
* **Waiver Information** (`telehealth_waiver_form`, text, optional):
    - This section is for additional information related to the waiver or authorization.
    - Please provide any relevant details that are not covered in other sections.

### Patient Information (Page 1)
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional):
    - Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
    - Ensure the name is spelled correctly and in the correct format.

### Patient Contact Information (Page 1)
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional):
    - Enter the patient's phone number.
    - This is for contact purposes only and does not affect the waiver or authorization.

### Patient Contact Information (Page 1)
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional):
    - Enter the patient's email address.
    - This is for contact purposes only and does not affect the waiver or authorization.

### Healthcare Provider (Page 1)
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, optional):
    - Enter the name and credentials of the healthcare provider (e.g., MD, DO, etc.).
    - Ensure the name is spelled correctly and in the correct format.

### Patient Signature (Page 1)
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, optional):
    - Sign and date the form to indicate acknowledgement and acceptance of the waiver or authorization.
    - This is a required field for patient acknowledgment.

### Healthcare Provider Signature (Page 1)
* **Healthcare Provider Signature** (`healthcare_provider_signature`, text, optional):
    - Sign and date the form to indicate acceptance of the waiver or authorization.
    - This is a required field for healthcare provider acceptance.

### Waiver Type (Page 1)
* **Waiver Type** (`waiver_type`, select_one, optional):
    - Select the type of waiver or authorization needed:
        - Emergency Medical Authorization
        - Medical Release
        - Waiver of Liability

### Effective Date (Page 2)
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, date, optional):
    - Enter the date the waiver or authorization begins.
    - This is for tracking purposes only.

### End Date (Page 2)
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional):
    - Enter the date the waiver or authorization ends.
    - This is for tracking purposes only.

### Telehealth Provider (Page 2)
* **Telehealth Provider** (`telehealth_provider`, text, optional):
    - Enter the name and credentials of the telehealth provider (e.g., MD, DO, etc.).
    - Ensure the name is spelled correctly and in the correct format.

### Waiver Terms (Page 2)
* **Waiver Terms** (`waiver_terms`, text, optional):
    - Enter any additional terms or conditions related to the waiver or authorization.
    - This is for information purposes only.

### Patient Acknowledgment (Page 2)
* **Patient Acknowledgment** (`patient_acknowledgment`, text, optional):
    - Sign and date the form to indicate acknowledgment and acceptance of the waiver or authorization.
    - This is a required field for patient acknowledgment.

### Healthcare Provider Acknowledgment (Page 2)
* **Healthcare Provider Acknowledgment** (`healthcare_provider_acknowledgment`, text, optional):
    - Sign and date the form to indicate acceptance of the waiver or authorization.
    - This is a required field for healthcare provider acknowledgment.

### Patient Informed Consent (Page 3)
* **Patient Informed Consent** (`patient_informed_consent`, text, optional):
    - Sign and date the form to indicate informed consent for the waiver or authorization.
    - This is a required field for patient informed consent.

## Tips

* Ensure you have read and understand the waiver or authorization before signing.
* Ask questions if you are unsure about any part of the form.
* Sign and date the form as required.
* Submit the form to the appropriate authority or healthcare provider.
