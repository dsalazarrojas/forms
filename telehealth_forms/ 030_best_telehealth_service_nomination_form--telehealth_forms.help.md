# Best Telehealth Service Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Telehealth Service Nomination Form is designed to collect user feedback on the quality and effectiveness of telehealth services. This form helps to identify areas of excellence and opportunities for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form thoroughly and accurately.
2. Be honest in your feedback and evaluation.
3. Ensure that you have used the telehealth service within the past 30 days.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (2): Enter your legal full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address** (3): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you for follow-up questions or clarification.
* **Phone number** (4): Enter your phone number, including the country code if it's outside your local region.
* **Date** (5): Select the date when you used the telehealth service.
* **Primary selection** (6): Choose the option that best describes your experience with the telehealth service. This will help us understand your overall satisfaction.
	+ Excellent: The service met or exceeded your expectations.
	+ Good: The service was satisfactory, but there was room for improvement.
	+ Fair: The service was adequate, but not exceptional.
	+ Poor: The service did not meet your expectations.
* **Quantity or rating** (7): If applicable, provide a whole number that represents your evaluation of the service. For example, if you're rating the service on a scale of 1-5, enter 4 if you selected 4 stars.
* **Additional details** (8): Provide any additional information that you think would be helpful in evaluating the service. This could be specific examples or comments about your experience.
* **Agreement** (9): Select all options that apply to indicate your level of agreement with our statements.
	+ I agree: You agree with our statements.
	+ I need follow-up: You need more information or clarification on our statements.
	+ Not applicable: You don't think the statements apply to your experience.
* **Estimated value** (10): If applicable, provide an estimated value of the service in decimal format. For example, if you think the service is worth $10.99, enter 10.99.
* **Preferred time** (11): If scheduling is required, enter a time that you prefer to receive the service.
* **Final comments or notes** (12): Provide any final thoughts or comments about your experience with the telehealth service.

## Tips
- Be thorough and honest in your feedback.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact information].
