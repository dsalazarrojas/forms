# restaurant_inventory_information_record_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and record information related to a restaurant's inventory, including stock levels, inventory takes, and general information about the restaurant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "restaurant_information" field with any relevant details about the restaurant.
2. Enter any relevant information about inventory takes in the "inventory_takes" field.
3. Record the current stock levels in the "stock_levels" field.
4. Confirm the submission with a message in the "submission_confirmation" field (optional).
5. Provide any additional submission details in the "submission_details" field (optional).
6. Enter the date and time of the submission (optional).
7. Enter the email address (optional) and phone number (optional) associated with the submission.
8. Leave a note about the submission (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **restaurant_information** (Text): Enter any relevant information about the restaurant, such as its name, address, or description.
* **inventory_takes** (Text): Enter any relevant information about inventory takes, such as quantities or dates.
* **stock_levels** (Text): Record the current stock levels of the inventory.
* **submission_confirmation** (Text): Confirm that the submission has been made or entered a message to note the status of the submission.
* **submission_details** (Text): Provide any additional information about the submission, such as context or circumstances surrounding it.
* **date** (Date): Enter the date of the submission (if applicable).
* **time** (Time): Enter the time of the submission (if applicable).
* **email** (Email): Enter the email address associated with the submission (if applicable).
* **phone** (Text): Enter the phone number associated with the submission (if applicable).
* **note** (Note): Leave a note about the submission (if applicable).
