# Canteen Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Canteen Subscription Form is designed to collect information from students who want to enroll in the canteen's nutritious meal program. This form helps the canteen provide tailored meal experiences for enrolled students.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your personal details, including your name, student ID, and grade level.
2.  Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.
3.  Choose your preferred meal plan and meal preferences.
4.  Specify any dietary restrictions or allergies you may have.
5.  Select your preferred payment method and subscription details.
6.  Provide consent for your child to participate in the canteen's program.
7.  Authorize the canteen to contact you in case of a food allergic reaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1.  **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional)

    *   Enter your first name.
    ### 2.  **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional)

    *   Enter your last name.
    ### 3.  **Student ID** (`student_id`, `text`, required/optional)

    *   Enter your school-issued ID.
    ### 4.  **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required/optional)

    *   Select your grade level.
    ### 5.  **Class or Section** (`class_section`, `text`, optional)

    *   If applicable, enter your class or section.
    ### 6.  **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required/optional)

    *   Enter your email address.
    ### 7.  **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional)

    *   Enter your contact phone number.
    ### 8.  **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required/optional)

    *   Enter your parent or guardian's name.
    ### 9.  **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, required/optional)

    *   Enter your parent or guardian's email address.
    ### 10. **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, required/optional)

    *   Enter your parent or guardian's contact phone number.
    ### 11. **Subscription Type** (`subscription_type`, `select_one`, required/optional)

    *   Select your subscription type (Daily, Three Days, Two Days, Custom Plan).
    ### 12. **Subscription Duration** (`subscription_duration`, `select_one`, required/optional)

    *   Select your subscription duration (Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annual, Annual, Custom dates).
    ### 13. **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required/optional)

    *   Enter the start date of your subscription.
    ### 14. **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, optional)

    *   Select all that apply (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Nut allergy, Shellfish allergy, Egg allergy, Soy allergy, Other allergies, No restrictions).
    ### 15. **Detailed Allergy Information** (`allergy_details`, `text`, optional)

    *   Enter any additional allergy information (if needed).
    ### 16. **Meal Type Preference** (`meal_type_preference`, `select_multiple`, optional)

    *   Select your preferred meal types (South Indian, North Indian, Continental, Chinese, Mexican, Italian, Asian Fusion, Vegetarian Special).
    ### 17. **Spice Level Preference** (`spice_level`, `select_one`, optional)

    *   Select your preferred spice level (Mild, Medium, Spicy, Very Spicy, No preference).
    ### 18. **Foods to Avoid** (`disliked_items`, `text`, optional)

    *   List any food items you dislike.
    ### 19. **Special Meal Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional)

    *   Enter any special meal requests.
    ### 20. **Subscription Cost (Display Only)** (`subscription_cost`, `number`, optional)

    *   This field displays your subscription cost (calculated based on your plan).
    ### 21. **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required/optional)

    *   Select your preferred payment method (Online payment portal, Bank transfer, Credit/Debit card, Check, Monthly invoice).
    ### 22. **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`, optional)

    *   Enter your billing address (for invoicing).
    ### 23. **I Consent to my Child Participating in the Canteen Subscription Program** (`consent_agreement`, `select_one`, required/optional)

    *   Select "Yes, I consent" to participate in the canteen's program.
    ### 24. **I Authorize the Canteen to Contact Me in Case of a Food Allergic Reaction** (`emergency_notification`, `select_one`, required/optional)

    *   Select "Yes, I authorize" for emergency contact.

## Tips

*   Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
*   Ensure all required fields are completed to avoid any issues with your subscription.
*   If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact the canteen staff.
