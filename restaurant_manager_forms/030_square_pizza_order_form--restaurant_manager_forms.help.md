# Square Pizza Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect order information from customers who are placing an order for a Square Pizza. Each field must be filled out to ensure accurate order fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Check the "Pizza" field to indicate whether you want a pizza.
2. Enter the quantity of pizza you want to order.
3. Select the desired toppings for your pizza.
4. Choose the type of crust you prefer.
5. Select the size of the pizza you want.
6. Indicate whether you want any side dishes.
7. Add your name (optional).
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your phone number (optional).
10. Add any additional notes about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page (Main page, Text, Optional):** This is the main section of the form where you'll be able to fill out other fields.
* **Pizza (Yes/No, Select One, True):** Check this box if you want a pizza.
* **Quantity (Number, Number, True):** Enter the number of pizzas you want to order.
* **Toppings (Yes/No, Select Multiple, True):** Select the toppings you want on your pizza by checking the boxes.
* **Crust (Yes/No, Select One, True):** Choose the type of crust you prefer. Options include 'Yes' and 'No' to indicate whether you want a specific type of crust or not.
* **Size (Yes/No, Select One, True):** Select the size of the pizza you want. Options include 'Yes' and 'No' to indicate whether you want a specific size or not.
* **Side (Yes/No, Select One, True):** Check this box if you want any side dishes with your order.
* **Name (Name, Text, False):** If you want to leave a note with your name, enter it here.
* **Email (Email, Email, True):** Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone (Phone, Text, False):** Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Notes (Notes, Note, False):** Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
