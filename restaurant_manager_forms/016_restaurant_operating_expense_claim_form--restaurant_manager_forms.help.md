# restaurant_operating_expense_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The restaurant operating expense claim form is used to report operating expenses incurred during the restaurant's daily operations. This form is typically used by restaurant managers to submit expense claims for reimbursement or approval from the restaurant's management or accounting department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the restaurant where the expense was incurred.
2. Check if the expense was actually incurred, i.e., "Yes" or "No".
3. Enter the date of the expense.
4. Enter the total amount of the expense.
5. Provide a brief description of the expense.
6. If the expense was incurred by someone other than the current submitter, enter their name.
7. Sign and date the form as the manager to confirm approval or rejection of the expense claim.
8. Sign and date the form as the reviewer to provide comments or approval status.
9. Review and update the approval status and comments as the approver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Restaurant Name** (Restaurant Name, text, required): Enter the name of the restaurant where the expense was incurred.
* **Expense Type** (Expense Type, select_one, not required): Check if the expense was actually incurred (Yes/No).
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Enter the date of the expense.
* **Amount** (Amount, number, required): Enter the total amount of the expense.
* **Description** (Description, text, not required): Provide a brief description of the expense.
* **Submitter** (Submitter, text, required): Enter the name of the person who is submitting the expense claim.
* **Submitter Email** (Submitter Email, email, not required): Enter the email address of the person who is submitting the expense claim.
* **Submitter Phone** (Submitter Phone, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the person who is submitting the expense claim.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about the expense.
* **Restaurant Address** (Restaurant Address, text, required): Enter the address of the restaurant where the expense was incurred.
* **Expense Claimed By** (Expense Claimed By, text, required): Enter the name of the person who actually incurred the expense.
* **Manager Signature** (Manager Signature, text, required): Sign and date the form as the manager to confirm approval or rejection of the expense claim.
* **Auditor Signature** (Auditor Signature, text, not required): Sign and date the form as the auditor to provide comments or approval status.
* **Reviewer Signature** (Reviewer Signature, text, not required): Sign and date the form as the reviewer to provide comments or approval status.
* **Approval Status** (Approval Status, select_one, required): Select the approval status of the expense claim (Active/Inactive).
* **Approved By** (Approved By, select_one, not required): Select if the expense claim was approved or not approved (Yes/No).
* **Denied By** (Denied By, select_one, not required): Select if the expense claim was denied (Yes/No).
* **Comments** (Comments, text, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about the expense.
* **Attachments** (Attachments, note, not required): Add any supporting documents or attachments about the expense.
* **Attachment** (Attachment, note, not required): Add any supporting documents or attachments about the expense.
* **Submitter Comments** (Submitter Comments, text, not required): Add any comments or notes from the submitter.
* **Reviewer Comments** (Reviewer Comments, text, not required): Add any comments or notes from the reviewer.
* **Footer** (Footer, text, not required): Add any additional comments or notes in the footer.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use the correct format for dates (MM/DD/YY).
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the restaurant management or accounting department for assistance.
