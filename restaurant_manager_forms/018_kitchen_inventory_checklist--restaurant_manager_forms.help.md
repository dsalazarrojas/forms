# Kitchen Inventory Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kitchen Inventory Checklist is a tool for restaurant managers to maintain accurate records of their kitchen inventory. This form helps you track stock levels, equipment, and supplies, ensuring you have a clear picture of what you need to restock and maintain.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and enter the current state of your kitchen inventory.
2. For each field, provide a brief description or quantity of the item, as applicable.
3. Ensure you answer all required fields (marked with *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe the overall state of your kitchen inventory.
* **Fridge** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contents of your fridge, including perishable and non-perishable items.
* **Freezer** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Record the contents of your freezer, including frozen goods and supplies.
* **Pantry** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Document the current state of your pantry, including dry goods and spices.
* **Dry Stores** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Report on dry food and supply storage conditions.
* **Equipment** (`6`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the current state of your kitchen equipment:
	+ C-01: In good working condition
	+ C-02: In need of repair or maintenance
	+ C-03: Out of order or broken
	+ C-04: Missing or misplaced
	+ C-05: Other (please specify)
* **Small Wares** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Enter a description or quantity of small wares, such as dishware, glassware, or cooking utensils.
* **Chemicals** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Record the quantity or state of chemicals, such as cleaning agents, sanitizers, or pest control.
* **Cleaning Supplies** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Document the condition and quantity of cleaning supplies, like brooms, mops, and disinfectants.
* **Kitchen Tools** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe the state of kitchen tools, such as knives, cutting boards, or pots and pans.
* **Maintenance** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Record any scheduled or pending maintenance tasks for kitchen equipment.
* **Waste Management** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select the current state of your waste management practices:
	+ W01: Regularly emptied and cleaned
	+ W02: Overdue for emptying and cleaning
	+ W03: Full and in need of replacement
	+ W04: Not applicable
* **Stock Levels** (`13`, `text`, required: false): Enter the current stock levels of non-perishable items.
* **Urgent Restocking** (`14`, `text`, required: false): Report any items that require urgent restocking.

## Tips

* Be thorough when completing this form to ensure accurate records.
* Review and update the form regularly to maintain current inventory levels.
* Use this form as a tool to identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions about restocking and maintenance.
