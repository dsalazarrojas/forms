# Pizza Toppings Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their desired pizza order.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

*   Enter a brief description of your order (optional).
*   Select the sauce you prefer for your pizza (up to 3 options).
*   Choose the cheese type for your pizza (select 1 option).
*   Select any desired pepperoni toppings (up to 3 options).
*   Choose any desired mushroom toppings (up to 3 options).
*   Select any desired onion toppings (up to 3 options).
*   Select any extra toppings you'd like (up to 3 options).
*   Choose the extra cheese type for your pizza (up to 3 options).
*   Select the crust type for your pizza (up to 3 options).
*   Choose the size of your pizza (1 option).
*   Enter any special instructions or comments about your order (optional).
*   Enter any additional comments about your order (optional).
*   Enter the date and time of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Order Pizza** (1, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your order, if you have any specific preferences or requests.
*   **Toppings Sauce** (2, select_multiple, required): Select up to 3 sauce options for your pizza. These options are:
    +   BBQ sauce
    +   Olive oil
    +   Marinara sauce
*   **Toppings Cheese** (3, select_one, required): Choose 1 cheese type for your pizza. Options are:
    +   Mozzarella
    +   Cheddar
    +   Feta
*   **Pepperoni Toppings** (4, select_multiple, required/optional): Select up to 3 pepperoni topping options. Options are:
    +   Pepperoni
    +   Sausage
    +   Ham
*   **Mushroom Toppings** (5, select_multiple, required/optional): Select up to 3 mushroom topping options. Options are:
    +   Mushrooms
    +   Shiitake
    +   Crimini
*   **Onions** (6, select_multiple, required/optional): Select up to 3 onion topping options. Options are:
    +   Onions
    +   Caramelized Onions
    +   Scallions
*   **Extra Toppings Sauce** (7, select_multiple, required/optional): Select up to 3 extra topping sauce options. Options are:
    +   Extra sauce
    +   Aioli
    +   Garlic Butter
*   **Extra Toppings Cheese** (8, select_multiple, required/optional): Select up to 3 extra cheese topping options. Options are:
    +   Extra cheese
    +   Feta
    +   Goat Cheese
*   **Crust Type** (9, select_multiple, required): Select up to 3 crust type options. Options are:
    +   Thin crust
    +   Thick crust
    +   Gluten-free crust
*   **Pizza Size** (10, select_one, required): Choose 1 size for your pizza. Options are:
    +   Small
    +   Medium
    +   Large
*   **Special Instructions** (11, text, required/optional): Enter any special instructions or comments about your order (optional).
*   **Order Comments** (12, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments about your order (optional).
*   **Order Date** (13, date, required): Enter the date of your order.
*   **Order Time** (14, time, required): Enter the time of your order.
