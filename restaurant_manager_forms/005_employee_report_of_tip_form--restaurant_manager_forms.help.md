# Employee Report Of Tip Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Report Of Tip Form is designed to collect accurate information on employee tips earned during shifts. Complete this form to report your tips and ensure that your employer accurately calculates and pays you for your earned tips.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your paystub or identification.
2. Enter your employee ID if required.
3. Choose the date of the reporting period.
4. Sign your name in the signature field to confirm you have completed the form accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (employee_name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your paystub or identification.
* **Employee ID** (employee_id, number, required): Enter your unique employee ID.
* **Date** (date, date, required): Choose the date of the reporting period.
* **Employee Signature** (employee_signature, note, required): Sign your name to confirm you have completed the form accurately.
* **Manager Name** (manager_name, text, optional): Enter your manager's name if required.
* **Manager Signature** (manager_signature, note, optional): Signature of the manager confirming the accuracy of the report.
* **Shift Manager Name** (shift_manager_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the shift manager if required.
* **Shift** (shift, text, optional): Describe the shift during which the tips were earned.
* **Hours Worked** (hours_worked, number, optional): Enter the total hours worked during the reporting period.
* **Gross Pay** (gross_pay, number, optional): Enter the gross pay earned during the reporting period.
* **Net Pay** (net_pay, number, optional): Enter the net pay earned during the reporting period.
* **Tax** (tax, number, optional): Enter the tax amount deducted from the gross pay.
* **Tax Rate** (tax_rate, number, optional): Enter the tax rate percentage applied.
* **Deductions** (deductions, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of deductions applied, such as Health Insurance, Other, or 401k.
* **Total Tips** (total_tips, number, optional): Enter the total tips earned during the reporting period.
* **Cash Tips** (cash_tips, number, optional): Enter the cash tips earned during the reporting period.
* **Credit Card Tips** (credit_card_tips, number, optional): Enter the credit card tips earned during the reporting period.
* **Cash Tips Breakdown** (cash_tips_breakdown, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of cash tips earned, such as Food and Beverage, Room Service, Bar, Parking, or Other.
* **Credit Card Tips Breakdown** (credit_card_tips_breakdown, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of credit card tips earned, such as Food and Beverage, Room Service, Bar, Parking, or Other.
* **Other Deductions** (other_deductions, text, optional): Enter any other deductions applied.
* **Total Tips Breakdown** (total_tips_breakdown, text, optional): Enter a breakdown of the total tips earned.
* **Employee Comments** (employee_comments, text, optional): Enter any comments from the employee about their report.
* **Manager Comments** (manager_comments, text, optional): Enter any comments from the manager about the report.

## Tips

* Ensure that you complete the form accurately and truthfully to ensure accurate tip reporting.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact your manager or HR representative.
* This form is for reporting tips earned during regular shifts, not for reporting overtime or other types of pay.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all fields are completed accurately.
