# mobile_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mobile order form is used to gather customer order information for mobile ordering purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Go to the form on the mobile ordering system.
2. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
3. Make sure to select the correct product.
4. Provide accurate customer information.
5. Submit the form after ensuring all the required fields are filled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, required: false): Please fill in the details of your order, including any special requests or messages.
* **Products** (`product_select`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the product you wish to order from the available options.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional information about your customer, such as their contact information.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your customer's phone number.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your customer's name.
