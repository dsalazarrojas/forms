# Seafood Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seafood Order Form is a tool for customers to provide their order details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the customer information section with your contact details.
*   In the "Order Details" section, provide any additional information about your order that you would like to share with us.
*   In the "Payment Details" section, provide your payment method and information.
*   Select your preferred seafood type from the "Seafood" section.
*   If you have a specific date and time for your order, enter it in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" fields.
*   Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
*   Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Customer Info
*   **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, `text`, optional): This is the contact information for the customer, such as name, address, and phone number.
*   **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, optional): This is an optional field where customers can provide any additional information about their order.
*   **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, optional): This is an optional field where customers can provide their payment method and information.
*   **Seafood** (`seafood`, `select_multiple`, optional): This is the main section where customers can select their preferred seafood type from the options provided.
*   **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, optional): This field is for entering the date when the order is desired.
*   **Order Time** (`order_time`, `time`, optional): This field is for entering the time when the order is desired.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): This is for entering the customer's email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): This is for entering the customer's phone number.
*   **Salmon**, **Tuna**, **Lobster**, **Shrimp**, **Crab**, **Lobster Roll**, **Crab Cakes**, **Fish**, and **Octopus** (`seafood`, `select_multiple`, optional): These are the options for selecting the preferred seafood type.
 

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth order process.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at your preferred contact method. 
*   Please note that this form is not designed for orders over the phone.
