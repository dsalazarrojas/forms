# Food Online Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Online Order Form is a tool for customers to place orders online. It collects information about the customer's meal preferences and delivery details to ensure accurate order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred meal from the "Select Meal" dropdown menu.
2. If you have any dietary restrictions, select "Yes" for "Diet Type" and specify which type of diet you require.
3. Choose the texture of your meal by selecting "Yes" or "No" for "Texture".
4. If you require your meal at a specific temperature, select "Yes" or "No" for "Temperature".
5. Enter the quantity of your order in the "Quantity" field.
6. Enter your delivery details, such as any special instructions or notes, in the "Delivery Details" field.
7. Select your preferred payment method by choosing "Yes" or "No" for "Payment Method".
8. If you need to enter any payment details, do so in the "Payment Details" field.
9. Enter your delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Meal**: This is a dropdown menu where you can select your preferred meal. Please choose one option from the list.
* **Diet Type**: If you have any dietary restrictions, select "Yes" and choose one or more of the options provided.
* **Texture**: Choose the texture of your meal by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Temperature**: If you require your meal at a specific temperature, select "Yes" and we'll do our best to accommodate your request.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of your order. Please note that this field is not required.
* **Delivery Details**: Enter any special instructions or notes about your delivery in this field. This is optional but helps us ensure your order is delivered correctly.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method by choosing "Yes" or "No".
* **Payment Details**: If you need to enter any payment details, please do so in this field. This is optional but helps us process your payment accurately.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter your delivery address in this field. This is required for us to deliver your order correctly.
