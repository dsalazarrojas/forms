# Taproom Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Taproom Order Form is designed to collect order details and payment information from customers. It helps restaurant managers to process orders efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the order.
2. Choose the time of the order.
3. Provide the customer's name.
4. Enter the customer's email address.
5. Enter the customer's phone number.
6. Enter any additional order details in the text box.
7. Select the payment method (cash, card, bank transfer, or other).
8. Enter the total cost of the order.
9. Choose whether the order includes items or not.
9. Enter the total cost of the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **date**: Enter the date of the order in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **time**: Select the time of the order from the available options.
* **customer_name**: Enter the customer's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **email**: Enter the customer's valid email address.
* **phone_number**: Enter the customer's phone number for contact purposes.
* **order_details**: Enter any additional comments or details about the order, such as special requests or allergy information.
* **payment_method**: Select the payment method used for the order, which can be:
	+ **cash**: Payment made in cash.
	+ **card**: Payment made using a credit or debit card.
	+ **bank transfer**: Payment made via bank transfer.
	+ **other**: Payment made through another means (please specify below).
* **order_total**: Enter the total cost of the order.
* **items**: Check this box if the order includes items. If not checked, the total will be zero.
* **total**: Enter the total cost of the order. This field is automatically populated if you selected items above.
