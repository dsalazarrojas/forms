# Restaurant Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Evaluation Form is a tool for restaurant managers to assess the overall quality of their restaurant's service and food. The feedback collected through this form will help them identify strengths and weaknesses, enabling data-driven decision-making to improve the customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the title of the restaurant that you are evaluating.
2. Choose the quality of the food you experienced.
3. Evaluate the overall service quality you received.
4. Rate the cleanliness of the restaurant.
5. Assess the accuracy of your order.
6. Select the speed of service provided by the staff.
7. Submit your feedback by typing in the provided text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Restaurant Evaluation Form (form_title)**: Enter the name of the restaurant you are evaluating.
* **Food Quality (food_quality)**: Choose how you would rate the quality of the food you were served.
* **Overall Service Quality (overall_service_quality)**: Select the overall quality of service you experienced, including how attentive the staff were and how responsive they were to your needs.
* **Cleanliness (cleanliness)**: Assess the overall cleanliness of the restaurant, including the kitchen, dining area, and restrooms.
* **Order Accuracy (order_accuracy)**: Rate the accuracy of your order, including whether the food was prepared correctly and if the server was knowledgeable about the menu.
* **Speed of Service (speed_of_service)**: Select how quickly you received your food and drinks.
* **Form Submit (form_submit)**: Please type in any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience. This is an optional field for you to provide more detailed feedback.
