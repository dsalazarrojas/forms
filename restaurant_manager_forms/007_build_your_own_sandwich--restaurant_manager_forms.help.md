# Build Your Own Sandwich - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to let you create a custom sandwich with your preferred ingredients and order details. Please select your options carefully to ensure your order is accurate and meets your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields:
	* Your name
	* Email address
	* Phone number
	* Select your bread type and size
	* Choose your protein (one or more)
	* Select your vegetables (one or more)
	* Choose your condiments (one or more)
	* Select your order type (Dine-in, Takeout, or Delivery)
	* Number of sandwiches
2. Select optional fields based on your preferences:
	* Bread size (Large or 6 inch)
	* Toasting preference
	* Extra toppings (additional charge may apply)
	* Special instructions or requests
3. Review your selections before submitting your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (customer_name, text, required): Enter your name to ensure accurate order processing.
* **Email Address** (customer_email, email, required): Enter your email address for order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (customer_phone, text, required): Enter your phone number for pickup or delivery contact.
* **Select Bread** (bread_type, select_one, required): Choose your preferred bread type:
	+ Wheat
	+ White
	+ Italian
	+ Ciabatta
	+ Sourdough
	+ Rye
	+ Gluten-free
* **Bread Size** (bread_size, select_one, required): Select your preferred bread size:
	+ Regular (6 inch)
	+ Large (12 inch)
	+ Wrap
	+ Flatbread
* **Protein** (protein_selection, select_multiple, required): Choose your preferred protein(s):
	+ Turkey
	+ Ham
	+ Roast beef
	+ Chicken breast
	+ Tuna
	+ Egg salad
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Double protein
* **Cheese** (cheese_type, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred cheese(s):
	+ Cheddar
	+ Swiss
	+ Provolone
	+ Mozzarella
	+ Pepper jack
	+ Feta
	+ No cheese
* **Vegetables** (vegetables, select_multiple, required): Choose your preferred vegetables:
	+ Lettuce
	+ Tomato
	+ Cucumber
	+ Onion
	+ Bell peppers
	+ Olives
	+ Spinach
	+ Carrots
	+ Sprouts
* **Condiments and Spreads** (condiments, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred condiments(s):
	+ Mayonnaise
	+ Mustard
	+ Oil and vinegar
	+ Hummus
	+ Pesto
	+ Ranch dressing
	+ Chipotle aioli
	+ No condiments
* **Extra Toppings** (extra_toppings, select_multiple, optional): Select any extra toppings (additional charge may apply):
	+ Bacon
	+ Avocado
	+ Cheese extra
	+ Nuts
	+ Seeds
	+ Hot sauce
* **Special Instructions or Requests** (special_instructions, text, optional): Enter any special instructions or requests (allergies, preferences, etc.)
* **Order Type** (order_type, select_one, required): Choose your preferred order type:
	+ Dine-in
	+ Takeout
	+ Delivery
* **Number of Sandwiches** (quantity, number, required): Enter the number of sandwiches you want to order.
