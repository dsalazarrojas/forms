# Restaurant Profile Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect restaurant information for the purpose of creating a comprehensive restaurant profile.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all relevant options that apply to your restaurant from the dropdown menus.
2. Enter text responses in the designated fields.
3. Fill out all required fields to ensure a complete and accurate restaurant profile.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **survey_questions**: Select all questions that apply to your restaurant. These questions will help us understand your restaurant's offerings and services.
* **customer_service**: Enter any special services or amenities your restaurant provides to enhance customer experience.
* **restaurant information**: Select how your restaurant is currently operating. This will help us understand your restaurant's current status.
* **restaurant_image**: Upload an image of your restaurant's logo or a photo that represents your establishment.
* **customer_experience**: Enter a numerical rating of your customers' experience at your restaurant (e.g., 1-5 stars).
* **rating**: Enter a numerical rating of your restaurant (e.g., 1-5 stars).
* **feedback**: Enter any feedback or comments about your restaurant.
* **restaurant_location**: Select the location where your restaurant is currently operating.
* **contact_number**: Enter your restaurant's contact phone number.
* **email**: Enter your restaurant's email address.
* **manager_name**: Enter the name of your restaurant's manager.
* **manager_image**: Upload an image of your restaurant's manager.
* **restaurant_description**: Enter a brief description of your restaurant.
* **restaurant_name**: Enter your restaurant's name.
* **restaurant_address**: Enter your restaurant's physical address.
* **restaurant_phone**: Enter your restaurant's phone number.
* **website**: Enter your restaurant's website URL.
* **social_media**: Enter your restaurant's social media handles.
* **restaurant_status**: Select the current status of your restaurant (e.g., Open, Closed, Temporarily Closed).
* **opening_time**: Enter the time when your restaurant opens.
* **closing_time**: Enter the time when your restaurant closes.
* **days_of_operation**: Select the days of operation for your restaurant (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, etc.).
* **hours_of_operation**: Enter the hours of operation for your restaurant.
* **menu**: Enter a brief description of your restaurant's menu.
* **survey_comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your restaurant.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure a comprehensive restaurant profile.
* Make sure to select all relevant options and enter all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
