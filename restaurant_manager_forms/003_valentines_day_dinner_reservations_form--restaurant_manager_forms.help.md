# valentines_day_dinner_reservations_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for Valentine's Day dinner reservations at a restaurant. It will help the management team to prepare for the special occasion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the guest's name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Select the date of the reservation in the "Reservation Date" field. The format is MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Choose the time of the reservation in the "Reservation Time" field.
4. Enter the number of guests attending in the "Number of Guests" field.
5. Select the table size required for the group from the "Table Size" options.
6. Choose the meal options for the guests from the "Meal Options" field. You can select multiple options.
7. Provide any special instructions or requests in the "Special Instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name**: Enter the name of the guest who made the reservation.
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date): Select the date you would like to make the reservation for.
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, time): Select the time you would like to make the reservation.
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the total number of guests attending the dinner.
* **Table Size** (`table_size`, select_one): Choose the size of the table required for the group from the options: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10.
* **Meal Options** (`meal_options`, select_multiple): Select the meal options you would like to offer for the guests. You can choose multiple options: Beef, Poultry, Fish, Veggie.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, note): Provide any special instructions or requests for the reservation.

## Tips
* Be sure to provide accurate information to avoid delays or misunderstandings.
* If you need to cancel or change a reservation, please contact the restaurant management directly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* The restaurant will do its best to accommodate your requests, but please be reasonable and respectful of other diners.
