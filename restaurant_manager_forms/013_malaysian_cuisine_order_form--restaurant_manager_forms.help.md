# Malaysian Cuisine Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture information for Malaysian cuisine food delivery and catering orders.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with the appropriate information for your order.
2. Review your submission for accuracy before clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to place an order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you would like to pick up or have your order delivered.
* **Orderer Name** (`orderer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person placing the order.
* **Orderer Email** (`orderer_email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for the person placing the order. This will help us contact you with any updates or issues.
* **Orderer Phone** (`orderer_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter a valid phone number for the person placing the order.
* **Food Item** (`food_item`, text, required/optional): Describe the type of Malaysian cuisine you would like to order.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required/optional): Enter the quantity of the food item you would like to order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, required/optional): Add any special instructions or notes about your order.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required/optional): Not applicable for this form.

## Tips
- Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy to ensure we can contact you with any issues or updates.
- Please be specific with your order details to help us fulfill your order correctly.
- If you have any special requests or instructions, please add them in the "Order Notes" field.
- We will review and validate your order before submitting it to our kitchen staff.
