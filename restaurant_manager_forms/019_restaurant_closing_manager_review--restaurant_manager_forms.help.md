# Restaurant Closing Manager Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Restaurant Closing Manager Review" form is used to collect feedback from managers on the closing performance of restaurants. This form helps to assess the efficiency and effectiveness of the closing process, and provides an opportunity for managers to share their thoughts and suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Please choose the correct rating for the manager's performance by selecting one of the options from the "manager_performance" field.
*   If you have any additional comments about the closing performance, please provide them in the "restaurant_closing_manager_review_comments" field.
*   Please provide a written review of the closing performance in the "restaurant_closing_manager_review" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **restaurant_closing_manager_review** (Text, Required: No)
    This field is for you to provide a written review of the closing performance. Please be as detailed as possible and provide specific examples or feedback.
*   **manager_performance** (Select One, Required: No)
    This field allows you to rate the manager's performance. Please select one of the options: Excellent, Good, or Fair.
*   **restaurant_closing_manager_review_comments** (Text, Required: No)
    If you have any additional comments or suggestions about the closing performance, please provide them here. Your feedback is valuable in helping us improve the closing process. 

## Tips

*   Please answer these questions honestly and objectively.
*   If you have any concerns or suggestions, please provide specific examples or examples of how the closing process can be improved.
*   If you are unsure about any of the questions or fields, please reach out to your supervisor for guidance.
