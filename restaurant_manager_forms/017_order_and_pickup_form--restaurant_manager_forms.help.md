# Order And Pickup Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to confirm their order status and pickup time for food from a restaurant or food establishment. It allows them to choose between delivery and pickup, specify the pickup time, and provide contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Order And Pickup Form, follow these steps:

1.  Select whether you would like to "Yes" or "No" to whether you would like to have your order delivered or picked up.
2.  Choose the desired "Pickup Time" for your order.
3.  If you have any specific instructions or notes for the delivery or pickup, you can add them in the "Note" field.
4.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field to be contacted by the restaurant for delivery.
5.  Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field in case the restaurant needs to contact you.
6.  Select the desired "Date" for your order.
7.  Specify the "Time" of your order.
8.  You can also add additional notes for the delivery or pickup in the "Note 1" to "Note 8" fields.
