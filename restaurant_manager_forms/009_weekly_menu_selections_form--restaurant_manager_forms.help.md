# Weekly Menu Selections Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Menu Selections Form is used to collect feedback and data on the menu selections, customer ratings, and restaurant feedback. This form helps restaurant managers to gather insights on customer preferences and satisfaction, and identify potential areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the requested information, including the menu items, customer feedback, and ratings.
2. Choose the menu items that you want to select from the list.
3. Enter your feedback and comments in the text fields.
4. Select the options for assigned tools, such as "Yes" or "No".
5. Enter the date and time of the form completion.
6. Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Menu Selections Form (`weekly_menu_selections_form`, Text, Not Required)**: Enter the title of the menu selections form.
* **Menu Item (`menu_item`, Select One, Not Required)**: Choose "Yes" if you want to select the menu item.
* **Menu Item 2 (`menu_item_2`, Select Multiple, Not Required)**: Choose the menu items that you want to select from the list.
* **Customer Feedback (`customer_feedback`, Number, Not Required)**: Enter a number to indicate the level of customer feedback, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **Restaurant Feedback (`restaurant_feedback`, Number, Not Required)**: Enter a number to indicate the level of restaurant feedback, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **Food Establishment Feedback (`food_establishment_feedback`, Number, Not Required)**: Enter a number to indicate the level of food establishment feedback, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **Customer Rating (`customer_rating`, Number, Not Required)**: Enter a number to indicate the level of customer satisfaction, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **Customer Comments (`customer_comments`, Text, Not Required)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback from the customer.
* **Restaurant Manager Comments (`restaurant_manager_comments`, Text, Not Required)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback from the restaurant manager.
* **Date (`date`, Date, Not Required)**: Enter the date of the form completion.
* **Time (`time`, Time, Not Required)**: Enter the time of the form completion.
* **Note (`note`, Note, Not Required)**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Email (`email`, Email, Not Required)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (`phone`, Text, Not Required)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Email (`customer_email`, Email, Not Required)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Restaurant Phone (`restaurant_phone`, Text, Not Required)**: Enter the restaurant's phone number.
* **Assigned Tool 1 (`assigned_tool`, Select One, Not Required)**: Choose "Yes" if you want to assign the tool.
* **Assigned Tool 2 (`assigned_tool_2`, Select One, Not Required)**: Choose "Yes" if you want to assign the tool.
* **Assigned Tool 3 (`assigned_tool_3`, Select Multiple, Not Required)**: Choose the tools that you want to assign from the list.
