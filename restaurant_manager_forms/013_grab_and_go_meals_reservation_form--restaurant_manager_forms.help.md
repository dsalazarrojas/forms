# Grab And Go Meals Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grab And Go Meals Reservation Form is used to collect information from customers for their meal orders, allowing restaurant managers to efficiently manage customer data and provide better service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the customer information page (page 1) with the customer's details.
2. Move on to the order information page (page 2) and fill in the requested meal selection, order comments, and order status.
3. On page 3, enter the customer's contact information, including phone number and email address.
4. For customers who have a message or notes, fill in the customer notes page.
5. Finally, submit the form to complete the reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info (id: 1)**: Enter the customer's name, phone number, and email address.
* **Order Info (id: 2)**: Select the type of meal the customer is ordering, such as Bread, Sandwich, or Salad.
* **Order Details (id: 3)**: Provide any additional details about the order, such as special requests.
* **Contact Info (id: 4)**: Enter the customer's phone number and email address.
* **Customer Phone (id: 8)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email (id: 22)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Name (id: 12)**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Order Total (id: 19)**: Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Order Status (id: 20)**: Select the status of the order, such as Pending, Confirmed, or Delivered.
* **Order Type (id: 21)**: Select the type of order, such as Food, Drink, or Package.
* **Customer Message (id: 25)**: Enter any additional message from the customer.
* **Customer Notes (id: 24)**: Enter any additional notes from the customer.
* **Customer Address (id: 16)**: Enter the customer's address.
* **Order Comments (id: 10)**: Enter any comments about the order.
* **Meal Selection (id: 9)**: Select the type of meal the customer is ordering.
* **Pickup Date (id: 6)**: Select the date for pickup.
* **Pickup Time (id: 7)**: Select the time for pickup.
* **Pickup Location (id: 18)**: Select the location where the customer will pick up their order.
* **Order ID (id: 13)**: Enter a unique identifier for the order.
* **Store Hours (id: 14)**: Enter the hours of operation for the store.
* **Special Instructions (id: 15)**: Enter any special instructions for the order.
* **Customer Phone (id: 23)**: Enter the customer's phone number. (Duplicate field, use either id: 8)
* **Customer Email (id: 22)**: Enter the customer's email address. (Duplicate field, use either id: 22)

Note: Fields with the same label and name may have identical functions and should only be filled once.
