# Restaurant Reopening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from restaurant owners about their current status and protocols for reopening after a lockdown or other restrictions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Is your restaurant open?** - Select the appropriate option from the dropdown menu:
	* Open
	* Limited
	* Closed
2. **Which days is your restaurant open?** - Choose all applicable days from the list:
	* Mon
	* Tue
	* Wed
	* Thu
	* Fri
	* Sat
	* Sun
3. **What time does your restaurant open?** - Enter the opening time in 12h or 24h format (e.g., 12:00 or 12:00h).
4. **What time does your restaurant close?** - Enter the closing time in 12h or 24h format (e.g., 22:00 or 22:00h).
5. **How many people do you allow in?** - Enter a number to indicate the maximum number of customers allowed at once.
6. **Social distancing measures in place?** - Select the status from the dropdown menu:
	* True
	* False
7. **Safety measures in place?** - Choose all applicable measures from the list:
	* Temperature checks
	* Sanitizing
	* Mask mandates
	* Hand sanitizer available
	* Reduced capacity
8. **Are there any specific safety precautions we should be aware of?** - Enter any additional safety measures or precautions not listed above.
9. **Any other comments or suggestions?** - Provide any additional information or feedback you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Is your restaurant open?** - Select your current status to inform customers and authorities about your reopening plans.
* **Which days is your restaurant open?** - This field is used to specify which days your restaurant is open, so customers can plan their visits accordingly.
* **What time does your restaurant open?** - Enter your opening time to help customers know when they can visit.
* **What time does your restaurant close?** - Enter your closing time to help customers know when to expect your restaurant to be closed.
* **How many people do you allow in?** - This field is used to inform customers about the maximum capacity of your restaurant.
* **Social distancing measures in place?** - Select True if your restaurant is implementing social distancing measures, False otherwise.
* **Safety measures in place?** - Choose all applicable safety measures to inform customers about your protocols.
* **Are there any specific safety precautions we should be aware of?** - Enter any additional safety measures or precautions not listed above.
* **Any other comments or suggestions?** - This field is for any additional information or feedback you'd like to share with us.
