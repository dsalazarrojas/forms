# Menu Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Menu Compliance Audit Form is used to collect information related to school menus for compliance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant menu date from the calendar picker.
2. Enter the name of the school or institution.
3. Add details about the menu item, including status, price, and description.
4. Enter contact information for the menu manager, including name, title, phone number, and email.
5. Repeat steps 2-4 for each school or location.
6. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school or institution.
* **Menu Date** (`menu_date`, date, required): Select the date of the menu.
* **Menu Item Name** (`menu_item_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the menu item.
* **Item Status** (`item_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the menu item (Active, Inactive, or Retired).
* **Item Description** (`item_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the menu item.
* **Item Price** (`item_price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the menu item.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the school or institution.
* **Menu Manager Email** (`menu_manager_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the menu manager.
* **Menu Manager Phone** (`menu_manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the menu manager.
* **Menu Manager Name** (`menu_manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the menu manager.
* **Menu Manager Title** (`menu_manager_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the menu manager.
* **School Name 2** (`school_name_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of a second school or institution.
* **Menu Manager Email 2** (`menu_manager_email_2`, email, optional): Enter the email address of a second menu manager.
* **Menu Manager Phone 2** (`menu_manager_phone_2`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of a second menu manager.
* **Menu Manager Name 2** (`menu_manager_name_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of a second menu manager.
* **Menu Manager Title 2** (`menu_manager_title_2`, text, optional): Enter the title of a second menu manager.
* **Menu Item Name 2** (`menu_item_name_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of a second menu item.
* **Item Status 2** (`item_status_2`, select_one, required): Select the status of the second menu item (Active, Inactive, or Retired).
* **Item Description 2** (`item_description_2`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the second menu item.
* **Item Price 2** (`item_price_2`, number, optional): Enter the price of the second menu item.
* **School Address 2** (`school_address_2`, text, optional): Enter the address of a second school or institution.
* **Menu Manager Email 3** (`menu_manager_email_3`, email, optional): Enter the email address of a third menu manager.
* **Menu Manager Phone 3** (`menu_manager_phone_3`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of a third menu manager.
* **Menu Manager Name 3** (`menu_manager_name_3`, text, optional): Enter the name of a third menu manager.
* **Menu Manager Title 3** (`menu_manager_title_3`, text, optional): Enter the title of a third menu manager.
* **School Name 3** (`school_name_3`, text, optional): Enter the name of a third school or institution.

**Tips:**

* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are completed.
* Use the provided field labels as a guide for which information to enter.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please contact your school or institution's compliance officer.
