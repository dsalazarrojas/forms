# Daily Kitchen Prep Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Kitchen Prep Checklist is a form used to document the daily tasks and activities performed by kitchen staff during food preparation. It helps kitchen managers and supervisors track the completion of tasks, monitor progress, and ensure that all necessary tasks are accomplished.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Review each page (1-9) to ensure you understand the tasks and questions presented.
2. Select the correct answer for each page using the options provided.
3. Enter any relevant information in the text fields.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Kitchen Preps** (`daily_kitchen_preps_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the tasks that were completed during the day's kitchen preps.
* **Morning Preps** (`morning_kitchen_preps_2`, select_one, required: false): Select one option to indicate if morning preps were completed.
* **Task 3** (`task_3`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant information or notes for this task.
* **Date** (`date_4`, date, required: false): Enter the date the tasks were completed.
* **Time** (`time_5`, time, required: false): Enter the time the tasks were completed.
* **Prep Time** (`prep_time_6`, number, required: false): Enter the amount of time spent on prepping.
* **Quantity** (`quantity_7`, number, required: false): Enter the quantity of items prepared.
* **Notes** (`notes_8`, text, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Task** (`task_9`, text, required: false): Enter any other relevant task or information.

## Tips
* Be sure to review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all necessary tasks are completed.
* Double-check the date and time fields to ensure accuracy.
* Use the text fields to provide any additional context or information as needed.
* If you are unsure about any field or task, ask your supervisor or kitchen manager for clarification.
