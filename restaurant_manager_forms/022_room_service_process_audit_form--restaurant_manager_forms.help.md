# Room Service Process Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the room service process in a hotel or restaurant setting. It is meant to be filled out after a room service experience to help managers and staff evaluate and improve the quality of service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections below to understand what each field is asking for.
2. Fill out the form by providing your honest feedback on the room service experience.
3. Make sure to answer all the required fields (marked as "Required" in the form).
4. Submit the form when you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, **Required**):
	+ Please provide a brief description of the order you received for room service, including the type of meal, time ordered, and any special requests.
* **Staff** (`staff`, text, **Required**):
	+ Please tell us about the staff member(s) who served you, including their name (if you remember it) and any notable interactions or experiences with them.
* **Quality Score** (`quality_score`, text, **Required**):
	+ Rate the quality of the room service experience on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "poor" and 5 being "excellent". Please be honest and provide any comments or suggestions on how the staff could improve.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback, but also be respectful and considerate in your comments.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documentation, please attach them to the form for further reference.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to the hotel or restaurant management for further assistance.
