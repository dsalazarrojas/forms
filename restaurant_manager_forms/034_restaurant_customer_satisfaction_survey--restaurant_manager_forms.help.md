# Restaurant Customer Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Restaurant Customer Satisfaction Survey is designed to measure the quality of our restaurant's food and service by gathering feedback from customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a different aspect of your dining experience.
2. Please answer all questions as accurately as possible.
3. If you have visited our restaurant before, select "Yes" on the "Have you visited this restaurant before?" question.
4. If you have not visited our restaurant before, select "No" on the "Have you visited this restaurant before?" question.
5. Choose multiple ratings for each rating question to provide more detailed feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Have you visited this restaurant before?** (Select One): 
	+ Answer this question truthfully, whether it is "Yes" or "No"
* **Overall Rating** (Number): 
	+ Rate your overall dining experience on a scale of 1-10
* **Service** (Select Multiple):
	+ Rate the quality of service you received during your visit. Choose one or multiple options: Good, Fair, Bad
* **Food Quality** (Select Multiple):
	+ Rate the quality of food you ordered. Choose one or multiple options: Good, Fair, Bad
* **How would you rate the service?** (Select Multiple):
	+ Rate the quality of service you received during your visit. Choose one or multiple options: Good, Fair, Bad
