# Cake Factory Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cake Factory Order Form is designed to collect information necessary to fulfill a cake order. This form has multiple sections: Customer Information, Cake Order Details, Delivery/Pickup, and Pricing and Payment. Each section is designed to collect specific information to ensure accurate and efficient order fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields in each section.
2.  Ensure you've filled in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3.  Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Customer Information

*   **Full Name** (2, text, \*): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Phone Number** (3, text, \*): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Email Address** (4, email, \*): Enter your email address for communication purposes.

### Cake Order Details

*   **Cake Type** (6, select_one, \*): Choose the type of cake you'd like to order. Options include Chocolate, Vanilla, Red Velvet, Strawberry, Carrot Cake, Cheesecake, Custom.
*   **Cake Size** (7, select_one, \*): Choose the size of the cake. Options include 6 inch (4-6 servings), 8 inch (8-12 servings), 10 inch (12-18 servings), 12 inch (18-24 servings), Custom size.
*   **Number of Servings Needed** (8, number, \*): Enter the number of servings you need.
*   **Frosting Type** (9, select_one, \*): Choose the type of frosting you'd like. Options include Buttercream, Cream Cheese, Ganache, Fondant, No frosting.
*   **Describe Your Flavor Preferences** (10, text, ): Optionally enter any specific flavor preferences or requests you have.
*   **Custom Message or Inscription** (11, text, ): Optionally enter a custom message or inscription for the cake.
*   **Dietary Requirements or Allergies** (12, text, ): Optionally enter any dietary requirements or allergies you have.

### Delivery/Pickup

*   **Delivery or Pickup?** (14, select_one, \*): Choose whether you'd like to delivery or pickup your order.
*   **Delivery Address** (15, text, ): Enter your delivery address if you've selected delivery.
*   **Delivery/Pickup Date** (16, date, \*): Enter the date you'd like to receive your order.
*   **Preferred Delivery/Pickup Time** (17, time, \*): Enter your preferred delivery/pickup time.

### Pricing and Payment

*   **Occasion** (18, select_one, ): Choose the occasion for your order. Options include Birthday, Wedding, Anniversary, Corporate Event, Graduation, Other.
*   **Estimated Cost** (20, number, ): Optionally enter an estimated cost for your order.
*   **Preferred Payment Method** (21, select_one, \*): Choose your preferred payment method. Options include Credit Card, Debit Card, Cash, Check.
*   **Deposit Amount** (22, number, ): Optionally enter a deposit amount for your order.
