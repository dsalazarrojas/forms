# Easter Meal Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Easter Meal Order Form is designed to help customers place their Easter meal orders. Please follow these steps to ensure a smooth and accurate ordering experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with your desired information.
2. Ensure you provide all required fields (if any).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Easter Meal Order** (`easter_meal_order_form`, text, required: false): Enter your Easter meal order here. Please describe the specific dish or meal you would like to order.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Select the date you would like to pick up your order.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Choose the time you would like to pick up your order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional details about your order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you would like to receive updates about your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for delivery or pick-up coordination.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required: false): You may enter any special instructions or comments about your order, or just leave this field blank if you're unsure about the total.

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately to ensure a smooth ordering experience.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
- We will contact you with any issues or updates about your order.
