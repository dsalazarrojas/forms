# employee_peer_review_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee peer review template is designed to facilitate open and constructive communication within teams, helping employees grow and improve their performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are evaluating a peer in good faith, and not someone who directly reports to you.
2. Complete the form thoroughly and honestly, providing specific feedback and suggestions for improvement.
3. Use the form to identify strengths and areas for growth.
4. Submit the completed form to the reviewer, manager, or HR.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Name (**name**, text, required)
Enter the employee being reviewed's name as it appears on their badge or personnel file.
### 2. Department (**department**, text, required)
Enter the department the employee belongs to.
### 3. Date (**date**, date, required)
Enter the date you conducted the review.
### 4. Rating (**rating**, number, required)
Rate the employee's performance on a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
### 5. Feedback (**feedback**, text, required)
Provide detailed, specific feedback on the employee's performance, focusing on strengths and areas for improvement.
### 6. Suggestions (**suggestions**, text, optional)
Provide suggestions for the employee's growth and development.
### 7. Action Items (**action items**, select multiple, optional)
Select one or more action items from the list:
	* Review and give feedback
	* Create an action plan
	* Recognize and reward
	* Other (please specify)
### 8. Reviewer Comments (**reviewer comments**, text, required)
Provide your thoughts and observations about the employee's performance.
### 9. Manager Comments (**manager comments**, text, required)
Enter any comments or suggestions from the manager for the reviewer.

### Tips
- Be specific, objective, and respectful when providing feedback.
- Keep the tone constructive and helpful.
- Focus on improving performance and growth, not personal opinions.
- Save the completed form to keep a record of your evaluations and suggestions.
