# Restaurant Catering Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information and catering order details for a restaurant catering order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **customer_name** field.
2. Provide your phone number in the **customer_phone** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **customer_email** field.
4. Provide your address in the **customer_address** field.
5. Describe your order details in the **order_details** field.
6. If you have any special requests, please specify them in the **special_requests** field.
7. Select the catering services you are interested in by choosing one or more from the **catering_service** dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Restaurant Catering Order Form Template**: This is the title of the form, but you do not need to fill in this field.
* **customer_name**: Your name.
* **customer_phone**: Your phone number where you can be reached.
* **customer_email**: Your email address where you can be contacted.
* **customer_address**: Your address where the catering order will be delivered.
* **order_details**: Please describe the catering order you would like to place.
* **special_requests**: If you have any special requests (e.g., dietary restrictions), please specify them here.
* **catering_service**: Select one or more catering services you are interested in (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
