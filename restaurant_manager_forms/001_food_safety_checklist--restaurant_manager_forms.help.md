# Food Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Safety Checklist is a tool used to evaluate and improve the food safety practices within a restaurant or food establishment. It helps to identify areas that require attention and ensures that all necessary protocols are in place to prevent foodborne illnesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review each section carefully and answer questions honestly.
* Answer "Yes" or "No" for questions that require a simple affirmation.
* For questions that require a detailed answer, provide as much information as possible.
* Make sure to complete all sections to get a comprehensive review of your food safety practices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1): This is a text field for any additional comments or notes you'd like to share about your food safety practices.
* **Food Handler Certification** (2): Check "Yes" if your food handlers have completed their certification program. This ensures that staff are trained to handle food safely.
* **Food Safety Training** (3): Record the date of your last food safety training. This keeps your staff up-to-date with the latest protocols and procedures.
* **Facility Safety Inspections** (4): Enter the number of facility safety inspections you've conducted in the past year. Regular inspections help identify potential hazards and prevent foodborne illnesses.
* **Equipment Cleaning Schedules** (5): Enter the frequency of your equipment cleaning schedules. Regular cleaning helps prevent the growth of bacteria and other microorganisms.
* **Personal Protective Equipment** (6): Describe the types of personal protective equipment (PPE) you use, such as gloves, hairnets, etc.
* **Emergency Procedures** (7): Select all emergency procedures that apply to your facility, such as fire evacuation plans, allergen response plans, and chemical spill response plans.
* **Employee Training** (8): Record the date of your last employee training. This ensures that staff are equipped to handle food safely.
* **Employee Compliance** (9): Check if all or some employees have completed the necessary training. This ensures that your staff are following food safety protocols.
* **Food Hazard Planning** (10): Check "Yes" if your facility has a food hazard planning in place. This helps identify and prevent potential hazards.

## Tips
* Regularly review and update your answers to ensure that your food safety practices are up-to-date and effective.
* Train staff on food safety protocols and procedures regularly.
* Conduct regular facility safety inspections to identify potential hazards.
* Keep accurate records of all food safety practices and procedures.
* Address any issues promptly and take corrective actions as needed.
