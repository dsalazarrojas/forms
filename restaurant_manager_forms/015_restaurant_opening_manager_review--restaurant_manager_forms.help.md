# Restaurant Opening Manager Review - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and ratings from the restaurant opening manager, allowing the management team to evaluate the restaurant's opening and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the restaurant's name in the "Restaurant Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the manager who managed the restaurant opening.
3. Select the date the restaurant opened.
4. Enter the restaurant's location.
5. Specify the restaurant's opening hours.
6. Rate your overall satisfaction with the manager's performance using the provided scale:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
7. Add any additional comments about the manager's performance.
8. If you have spent money on the restaurant's opening, enter it in the "Restaurant Opening Cost" field.
9. Rate your satisfaction with the overall quality of the restaurant's opening using the number rating system.
10. Click "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Restaurant Name** (Text, required): Enter the name of the restaurant that was opened.
* **Manager Name** (Text, required): Enter the name of the manager who managed the restaurant opening.
* **Date Opened** (Date, required): Select the date the restaurant opened.
* **Restaurant Location** (Text, required): Enter the location where the restaurant opened.
* **Restaurant Hours** (Time, required): Specify the restaurant's opening hours.
* **Rating** (Select One: Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the manager's performance.
* **Manager Comments** (Text, required): Add any comments about the manager's performance.
* **Restaurant Opening Cost** (Number, required): If you have spent money on the restaurant's opening, enter it here.
* **Restaurant Manager Rating** (Number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the overall quality of the restaurant's opening.
* **Restaurant Opening Cost** (Number, optional): Enter the amount of money you have spent on the restaurant's opening.
* **Manager Rating** (Select One: Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent, optional): Rate the manager's overall performance. 

Note: All the optional fields (Restaurant Opening Cost, Restaurant Manager Rating, and Manager Rating) should only be completed if applicable. 

## Tips

* Be as objective as possible when rating the manager's performance and providing comments.
* Enter accurate dates and times when selecting the "Date Opened" field.
* If you have spent money on the restaurant's opening, enter the correct amount in the "Restaurant Opening Cost" field.
* Rate the overall quality of the restaurant's opening based on your experience.
