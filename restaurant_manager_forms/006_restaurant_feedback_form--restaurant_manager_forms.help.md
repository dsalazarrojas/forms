# Restaurant Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their dining experience at a restaurant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the rating that best describes your experience (1-5).
2. Provide your contact information (name, etc.).
3. Fill out the fields to rate the restaurant's food quality, service quality, and cleanliness.
4. Write a brief description of your overall experience.
5. Add any additional comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select a rating**: This field asks you to rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest. Please select the number that best describes your experience.
* **Customer Info**: This field is for you to provide your name, email, and any other contact information.
* **Food quality**: This field is for you to rate the quality of the food you ordered.
* **Service quality**: This field is for you to rate the quality of the service you received at the restaurant.
* **Cleanliness**: This field is for you to rate the cleanliness of the restaurant.
* **Overall Experience**: This field is for you to write a brief description of your overall experience at the restaurant.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is for you to add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Tips
* Please fill out all the required fields to ensure that your feedback is complete and useful to the restaurant.
* Please be as specific as possible when providing your feedback.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, you can contact form@gic.mx.
