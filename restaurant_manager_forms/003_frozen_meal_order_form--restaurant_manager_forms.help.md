# Frozen Meal Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for frozen meal orders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review your form submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, email, or phone number.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional information related to the event or occasion.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, required/optional): List any dietary restrictions or preferences.
* **Meal Selection** (`meal_selection`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select multiple meals for the order. Options: 'Yes', 'No'.
* **Delivery Information** (`delivery_information`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information related to delivery.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any further comments or special requests.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all relevant fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your form submission before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant personnel.
