# Food Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The food pre-order form is designed to collect information from customers for food orders. This form is used to gather details such as customer contact information, order type, date, time, and any other important details that the customer may want to provide.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer info section with your details.
2. Choose the order type (yes or no).
3. Select the date and time you would like to place your order.
4. Enter your customer name and contact information.
5. Provide your customer email (optional).
6. If you have any additional order details or instructions, please let us know in the order details field.
7. If you have any special instructions for your order, please provide them in the order instructions field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer info** (id: 2, customer info, required): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Order type** (id: 3, order type, required): Indicate whether you are placing an order for pickup (yes) or delivery (no).
* **Date** (id: 4, date, required): Choose the date you would like to place your order.
* **Time** (id: 5, time, required): Select the time you would like to place your order.
* **Customer name** (id: 6, customer name, required): Enter the name of the person placing the order.
* **Customer contact** (id: 7, customer contact, required): Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you.
* **Customer email** (id: 8, customer email, optional): If you have a email, you can provide it for us to contact you with updates.
* **Order details** (id: 9, order details, optional): If you have any additional information about your order, such as special requests or modifications, please let us know.
* **Order instructions** (id: 10, order instructions, optional): If you have any specific instructions for your order, such as "please leave the soup at the door" or "please call me 10 minutes before arrival", please provide them here.

## Tips
* Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will contact you with any updates or confirmations about your order.
