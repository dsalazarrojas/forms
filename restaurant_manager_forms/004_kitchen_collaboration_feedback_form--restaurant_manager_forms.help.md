## Kitchen Collaboration Feedback Form - Help Guide
### Purpose
The Kitchen Collaboration Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their dining experience, helping the restaurant management team to identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance customer satisfaction.

### How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the form with your honest feedback on your recent visit to our restaurant.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, and be as specific as possible.
3. Select the options that best match your experience from the dropdown menus.
4. You can submit the form anonymously or provide your contact information if you want to be contacted for further discussion.
