# Restaurant Proposal Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Proposal Submission form is designed to collect detailed information about a proposed restaurant concept, including its concept, business plan, and contact details. This form is intended for individuals or entities interested in submitting a proposal for a specific restaurant management opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide a brief description of the restaurant concept in the "Restaurant Concept" field.
2.  Upload a comprehensive business plan for the proposed restaurant concept in the "Business Plan" field.
3.  Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, email, and address in the "Contact Details" field.
4.  Share details about the chef(s) responsible for the proposal in the "Chef Details" field.
5.  Enter the proposed restaurant's name, location, and image URL in the corresponding fields.
6.  Provide a URL for the restaurant's video presentation in the "Restaurant Video" field.
7.  Upload any relevant documents or supporting materials for the proposal in the "Restaurant Documents" field.
8.  Fill in your contact name, phone number, email, and address in the "Contact Name", "Contact Phone", "Contact E-mail", and "Contact Address" fields respectively.
9.  Add any additional note you want to share with the reviewer in the "Contact Note" field.
10. Finally, add a note for the reviewer in the "Restaurant Concept Note" field, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Restaurant Concept (Text)**: Enter a brief description of the restaurant's concept.
*   **Business Plan (Text)**: Upload a comprehensive business plan for the proposed restaurant concept.
*   **Contact Details (Text)**: Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, email, and address.
*   **Chef Details (Text)**: Share details about the chef(s) responsible for the proposal.
*   **Restaurant Name (Text)**: Enter the proposed restaurant's name.
*   **Location (Text)**: Enter the proposed restaurant's location.
*   **Restaurant Image (Text)**: Enter the URL of the restaurant's image.
*   **Restaurant Video (Text)**: Enter the URL of the restaurant's video presentation.
*   **Restaurant Documents (Text)**: Upload any relevant documents or supporting materials for the proposal.
*   **Contact Name (Text)**: Enter your name.
*   **Contact Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Contact E-mail (Text)**: Enter your email address.
*   **Contact Address (Text)**: Enter your address.
*   **Contact Note (Text)**: Add any additional note you want to share with the reviewer.
*   **Restaurant Concept Note (Text)**: Add a note for the reviewer, if needed.

## Tips

*   Be concise and comprehensive in your answers.
*   Ensure all fields are filled in as accurately as possible.
*   Do not share confidential or sensitive information unless explicitly required.
*   Review the form for any errors before submission.
