# thanksgiving_buffet_dining_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers book a table at our Thanksgiving buffet. Please fill out the form to let us know about your reservation details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields below. Please make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Reservation Details** (`reservation details`, text, required): This is the main section where you provide information about your reservation.
  - **Guest Count** (`guest count`, number, required): Please specify the number of guests attending your reservation.
  - **Time of Day** (`time of day`, time, required): Please select the time of day you plan to arrive at the buffet.
  - **Preferred Time** (`preferred time`, time, optional): If you have a preferred time, please select it. Please note that we will do our best to accommodate your request.
  - **Date** (`date`, date, required): Please select the date of your reservation.
  - **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you would like to receive a confirmation email, please provide your email address.
  - **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted before or after your reservation, please provide your phone number.
  - **Note** (`note`, note, optional): If you have any special requests or notes, please add them here.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- Please note that we will do our best to accommodate your request, but availability is subject to change.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
