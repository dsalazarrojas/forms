# Cafe Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Cafe Feedback Form" is designed to collect information about your experience at our cafe, allowing us to make improvements and provide better service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each field, and fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Your Name (customer_name)
* **Your Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.

### 2. Email Address (email_address)
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where we can follow up with you about your feedback. This is optional, but it helps us to reach out to you with any questions or to follow up on your feedback.

### 3. Date of Visit (visit_date)
* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date you visited our cafe.

### 4. Time of Visit (visit_time)
* **Time of Visit**: Choose the time of day you visited our cafe from the options provided.

### 5. Party Size (party_size)
* **Party Size**: Enter the number of people in your group that visited our cafe.

### 6. Dining Type (dining_type)
* **Dining Type**: Choose how you dined with us, such as dine-in, takeout, or delivery.

### 7. Food Quality (food_quality)
* **Food Quality**: Rate the quality of the food you ordered on a scale from Excellent to Poor.

### 8. Beverage Quality (beverage_quality)
* **Beverage Quality**: Rate the quality of the drinks you ordered on a scale from Excellent to Poor.

### 9. Service Speed (service_speed)
* **Service Speed**: Rate the speed of service on a scale from Very Fast to Very Slow.

### 10. Staff Friendliness (staff_friendliness)
* **Staff Friendliness**: Rate the friendliness of our staff on a scale from Very Friendly to Very Unfriendly.

### 11. Staff Knowledge (staff_knowledge)
* **Staff Knowledge**: Rate the knowledge of our staff about menu items on a scale from Very Knowledgeable to Very Uninformed.

### 12. Cleanliness (cleanliness)
* **Cleanliness**: Rate the cleanliness of our cafe on a scale from Very Clean to Very Dirty.

### 13. Atmosphere (atmosphere)
* **Atmosphere**: Rate the ambiance of our cafe on a scale from Excellent to Poor.

### 14. Value for Money (value_for_money)
* **Value for Money**: Rate how you feel about our prices on a scale from Excellent Value to Overpriced.

### 15. Menu Variety (menu_variety)
* **Menu Variety**: Rate the selection of items on our menu on a scale from Excellent Selection to Very Limited.

### 16. Favorite Item Ordered (favorite_item)
* **Favorite Item Ordered**: If you have a favorite item that you ordered, enter it here.

### 17. Least Favorite Item (least_favorite_item)
* **Least Favorite Item**: If you have a least favorite item that you ordered, enter it here.

### 18. Overall Experience (overall_experience)
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience at our cafe on a scale from Excellent to Poor.

### 19. Would You Recommend Us (would_recommend)
* **Would You Recommend Us**: Choose whether you would definitely, probably, or probably not recommend our cafe to others.

### 20. Will You Return (would_return)
* **Will You Return**: Choose whether you will definitely, probably, or probably not return to our cafe.

### 21. Additional Feedback (additional_feedback)
* **Additional Feedback**: If you have any other comments or suggestions, enter them here.
