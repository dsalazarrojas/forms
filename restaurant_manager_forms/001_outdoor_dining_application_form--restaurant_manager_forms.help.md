# outdoor_dining_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for outdoor dining applications at a restaurant. It is designed to gather information about the outdoor dining experience, including restaurant details, payment method, reservation date and time, reservation duration, number of guests, number of tables, table layout, special requests, event details, contact information, manager's signature, event date and time, and event duration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of restaurant you would like to dine at from the "Restaurant Information" dropdown menu.
2. Choose your preferred payment method.
3. Enter the date and time of your reservation.
4. Select the length of your reservation.
5. Choose the number of guests you will be dining with.
6. Select the number of tables you will need.
7. Choose the layout of the tables (circular, L-sharp, long, square, or rectangular).
8. Describe any special requests, such as music or decorations.
9. Choose the type of event (none, catering, music, decorations, or food and beverage service).
10. Enter your contact information.
11. If your event will be hosted by a manager, indicate that you will have their signature.
12. Enter the date and time of your event.
13. Select the duration of your event.
