# BlueSnap Pizza Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The BlueSnap Pizza Order Form is designed to collect information about your order preferences and payment details for a seamless and efficient ordering experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your preferred order type: Choose from Delivery, Pickup, or Dine-In.
2. Choose your pizza size: Select from Small (10 inch), Medium (12 inch), Large (14 inch), or Extra Large (16 inch).
3. Select your crust type: Pick from Hand Tossed, Thin Crust, Thick Crust, Stuffed Crust, or Gluten-Free.
4. Choose a pizza style: Select from pre-defined styles like Cheese Only, Pepperoni, Margherita, Hawaiian, Meat Lovers, Veggie Deluxe, or build your own pizza.
5. Add extra toppings: Select from Extra Cheese, Mushrooms, Onions, Bell Peppers, Sausage, Bacon, Black Olives, Spinach, Tomatoes, Jalapenos.
6. Specify the quantity of pizzas: Enter the number of pizzas you would like to order.
7. Add sides: Select from Garlic Bread, Chicken Wings, Mozzarella Sticks, Caesar Salad, Garden Salad, Breadsticks, or None.
8. Add beverages: Choose from Coca-Cola, Sprite, Fanta, Diet Coke, Bottled Water, Iced Tea, or None.
9. Enter your full name: Provide your name as it appears on your ID.
10. Enter your phone number: Enter your phone number for order confirmation and delivery updates.
11. Enter your email address: Enter your email address for order receipt.
12. Provide your delivery address (optional): Enter your full street address for delivery.
13. Add delivery instructions (optional): Enter gate codes, building numbers, etc.
14. Add special requests (optional): Enter any allergies or special instructions.
15. Choose your payment method: Select from Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Cash on Delivery, or Cash on Pickup.
16. Enter your card details (optional): Enter your card number, expiry date, and security code.
17. Specify the tip amount (optional): Choose from No Tip, $2, $5, $10, 15%, 20%, or enter a custom amount.
18. Enter a promo code (optional): Enter if you have a discount code.
