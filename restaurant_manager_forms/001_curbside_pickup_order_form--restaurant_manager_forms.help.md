# Curbside Pickup Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through completing the Curbside Pickup Order Form, which is designed to gather information from customers for their curbside pickup orders. This form is intended for customers who are unable to place their orders directly through our online ordering system. 

## How to Complete This Form

1. To start, review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information readily available.
2. Fill out each section completely and accurately, as indicated below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info (Text)**: Enter the customer's name, email, and phone number to ensure we can contact the customer and confirm their order.
* **Order Details (Text)**: Describe the order the customer wishes to place, including any special requests or instructions.
* **Payment Method (Select One)**: Choose how the customer would like to pay for their order, whether it's in cash, by card, or through another method.
* **Order Date (Date)**: Enter the date the order is needed for, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Order Time (Time)**: Select the time the order is needed for.
* **Note (Text)**: Leave any additional notes or comments related to the order.

## Tips

* Please review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, please ask the customer for clarification.
* Make sure to save a copy of the completed form for your records.
