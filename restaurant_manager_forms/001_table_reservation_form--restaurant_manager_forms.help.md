# Table Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Table Reservation Form is a tool for customers to make a table reservation at your restaurant. This form collects essential information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable dining experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your customer information, including your name and email address.
2. Enter your phone number, if you have one.
3. Provide the date and time you would like to make a reservation.
4. Specify the number of guests you will be dining with.
5. Choose a table number, if you have a specific preference.
6. If you have any special requirements, such as a birthday celebration, please let us know.
7. Add any additional notes or comments to help our staff prepare for your visit.
8. Confirm your reservation by selecting 'True' in the 'Confirmation' field.
9. Finally, select the 'Confirmation Email' options to receive a confirmation of your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (text): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued identification.
- **Customer Email** (email): Enter your email address to receive a confirmation of your reservation and any updates.
- **Customer Phone** (text): Enter your phone number for our staff to contact you if needed.
- **Reservation Date** (date): Choose the date you would like to make your reservation.
- **Reservation Time** (time): Select the time you would like to arrive for your reservation.
- **Number of Guests** (number): Specify the number of people in your party.
- **Table Number** (text): If you have a specific table number in mind, please enter it here.
- **Special Requirements** (text): If you have any special requests, such as a birthday celebration, let us know.
- **Reservation Notes** (text): Add any additional comments or notes to help our staff prepare for your visit.
- **Confirmation** (select_one): Select 'True' to confirm your reservation.
- **Additional Notes** (note): Add any additional information or comments about your reservation.
- **Confirmation Email** (select_multiple): Select the options you would like to receive confirmation emails for your reservation.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure we can prepare for your visit.
- If you have any questions or changes, please contact us directly.
- Our staff is committed to making your dining experience unforgettable!
