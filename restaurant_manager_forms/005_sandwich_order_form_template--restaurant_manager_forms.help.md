# Sandwich Order Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sandwich Order Form Template is a tool used by customers to place orders for sandwiches at a restaurant. It helps the restaurant staff to understand the customer's preferences and requirements, making it easier to fulfill the order correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name and email address in the `customer_name` and `customer_email` fields, respectively.
2. Select the type of sandwich the customer wants to order from the `sandwich_type` options.
3. Choose the menu item and quantity for the order from the `sandwich_menu`, `menu`, and `quantity` fields.
4. Enter the order details in the `order_details` field.
5. Provide the time and date of the order in the `order_time` and `date` fields.
6. Enter the customer's address, city, state, zip, and country in the `customer_address`, `customer_city`, `customer_state`, `customer_zip`, and `customer_country` fields.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes in the `customer_note` field.
8. If the order was submitted online, select the `form_submitted` field accordingly.
9. Finally, click the submit button to complete the form and place the order.
