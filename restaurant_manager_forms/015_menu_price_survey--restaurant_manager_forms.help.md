# menu_price_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The menu_price_survey form is used to collect customer feedback on menu prices. This form is designed to help restaurant managers and staff understand how customers perceive menu prices, allowing for better decision-making and menu item adjustments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please navigate to the "menu_price_survey" form in the [insert platform/application].
2. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
3. Complete each field as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Competition** (1, number, required/optional): Select a value between 1 and [insert max value, e.g., 10].
- **Customer** (2, select_one, required/optional): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the customer is the manager or staff member filling out the form.
- **Price** (3, number, required/optional): Report the current price of the menu item.
- **Survey** (4, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or survey questions you have regarding the menu price.
- **Menu Items** (5, select_multiple, required/optional): Select 'Yes' for each menu item that you are reporting on.
- **Additional Notes** (6, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the menu items or customer feedback.
