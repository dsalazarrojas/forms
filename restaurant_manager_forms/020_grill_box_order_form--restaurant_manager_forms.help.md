# Grill Box Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grill Box Order Form is used to collect information for a customer's order, including their delivery option, contact details, and order specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Delivery Option**: Select the desired delivery option for the order.
2. Fill out the **Name** field with the customer's name.
3. Fill out the **Phone** field with the customer's phone number.
4. Fill out the **Email** field with the customer's email address.
5. Use the **Order Details** field to provide any relevant information about the order.
6. Select the desired **Pickup Date** for the order.
7. Select the desired **Pickup Time** for the order.
8. Fill out the **Pickup Location** field with the location where the customer will pick up their order.
9. If necessary, use the **Special Instructions** field to provide any additional information about the order.
10. Finally, select the desired **Order Type** for the order (Grilled Box, Box, or Sandwich).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Option (Select One)**: Choose between Delivery, Pickup, and Catering options.
* **Name (Text)**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Order Details (Note)**: Provide any additional information about the order.
* **Pickup Date (Date)**: Select the date the customer will pick up their order.
* **Pickup Time (Time)**: Select the time the customer will pick up their order.
* **Pickup Location (Text)**: Enter the location where the customer will pick up their order.
* **Special Instructions (Note)**: Provide any special instructions for the order.
* **Order Type (Select One)**: Choose between Grilled Box, Box, and Sandwich options.
