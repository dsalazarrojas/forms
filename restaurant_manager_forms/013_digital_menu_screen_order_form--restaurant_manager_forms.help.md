# Digital Menu Screen Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Digital Menu Screen Order Form is designed to help restaurant managers configure the display settings for a digital menu screen. The form consists of three fields that determine which types of orders are displayed on the menu screen: dine-in, pickup, and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. For each field, select 'Yes' if the restaurant offers that type of service, or 'No' if it does not.
3. Complete all three fields, as they are all required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dinein**: Choose 'Yes' if your restaurant offers dine-in services, meaning customers can order and pick up their food at the restaurant. Choose 'No' if your restaurant does not offer dine-in services.
* **Pickup**: Choose 'Yes' if your restaurant offers pickup services, where customers can place orders and pick up their food at a designated time. Choose 'No' if your restaurant does not offer pickup services.
* **Delivery**: Choose 'Yes' if your restaurant offers delivery services, where customers can order and have their food delivered to their location. Choose 'No' if your restaurant does not offer delivery services.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the restaurant's operational policies and procedures before completing this form.
* Ensure that you have selected the correct options for your restaurant's services.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, consult with your manager or staff for guidance.
