# Drink Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Drink Order Form is designed to collect information from customers to place orders for drinks. This form helps restaurant staff gather the necessary details in a quick and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **name** in the provided text field.
2. If requested, provide your **date of birth** in the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. If you have a specific time in mind for your drink order, select your preferred **time**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name so the staff can greet you by name.
* **date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If you want to have a birthday treat, provide your date of birth for the staff to note.
* **time** (`time`, time, optional): If you have a specific time in mind for your drink order, let us know and we'll do our best to honor it.

## Tips
- Be honest when filling out the form, as the information provided will be used to prepare your drink order.
- Make sure to double-check your input for any errors before submission.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask a restaurant staff member for assistance.
