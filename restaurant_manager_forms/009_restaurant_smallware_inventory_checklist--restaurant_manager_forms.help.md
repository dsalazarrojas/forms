# Restaurant Smallware Inventory Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help restaurant owners and managers track and manage their smallware inventory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the sections for Restaurant Smallware Inventory and Restocking Needs.
2. In the Restaurant Smallware Inventory section, provide a detailed list of all the smallware items currently available in the kitchen.
3. In the Restocking Needs section, identify which items need to be restocked and describe the reason for the restocking.
4. Enter the required date for the next restocking in the Restocking Date field.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Restaurant Smallware Inventory** (`restaraunt_smallware_inventory`, text, required): List all the smallware items currently available in the kitchen.
* **Restocking Needs** (`restocking_needs`, text, required): Identify which items need to be restocked and describe the reason for the restocking.
* **Kitchen Equipment** (`kitchen_equipment`, text, optional): List any kitchen equipment that is not included in the smallware inventory.
* **Restaurant Smallware Inventory** (`restaurant_smallware_inventory`, text, required): This field is a duplicate of the previous field, but with a more general description.
* **Resticking Needs Description** (`restocking_needs_description`, text, optional): Add any additional information about the restocking needs.
* **Quantity Needs** (`quantity_needs`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of items that need to be restocked.
* **Resticking Date** (`restocking_date`, date, required): Enter the date for the next restocking.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the restocking process.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form regularly to ensure accuracy and stay on top of restocking needs.
* Keep the form up-to-date and use it to track changes in inventory over time.
* Use the Notes field to keep a record of any issues or concerns that may arise during the restocking process.
