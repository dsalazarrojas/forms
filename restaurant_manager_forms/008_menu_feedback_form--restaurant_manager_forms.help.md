# Menu Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Menu Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from customers about the menu of a restaurant. It helps restaurant managers to understand customer thoughts and make data-driven decisions to improve their menu offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the required fields**: Please fill in the required fields such as your customer name, email, phone number, and overall rating.
2. **Provide feedback**: Share your honest feedback about our menu. You can type as much or as little as you want.
3. **Choose your rating**: Select your overall rating for our restaurant's menu.
4. **Submit your feedback**: Click on the submit button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, text, required): This is the name of the person submitting the feedback.
* **Customer Email** (Customer Email, email, required): This is the email address where you can be reached for follow-up questions or for the restaurant to respond to your feedback.
* **Customer Phone** (Customer Phone, text, required): This is the phone number where you can be reached for follow-up questions.
* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, text, required): Please note that this field should not be confused with the "Restaurant Name" field. This is the name of the person submitting the feedback.
* **Overall Rating** (Overall Rating, number, required): Select a rating from 1 to 5 to express your overall satisfaction with our menu.
* **What do you think of our menu?** (Menu Feedback, text, required): Share your thoughts and feedback about our menu. Be as detailed or concise as you want.
* **Dish Id** (Dish Id, number, required): This is the ID of the dish you are providing feedback on. Please make sure to type it correctly.
* **Restaurant Name** (Restaurant Name, text, optional): This is the name of the restaurant. Please note that this field is not required.
* **Restaurant Email** (Restaurant Email, email, optional): This is the email address of the restaurant. Please note that this field is not required.
* **Restaurant Phone** (Restaurant Phone, text, optional): This is the phone number of the restaurant. Please note that this field is not required.
* **Food Quality** (Food Quality, text, optional): Share your thoughts on the quality of the food served by the restaurant.
* **Service Quality** (Service Quality, text, optional): Share your thoughts on the quality of the service provided by the restaurant.
* **Feedback** (Feedback, text, optional): Share any additional feedback or comments you might have about the restaurant or its services.
* **Menu Feedback** (Menu Feedback, text, optional): Share your thoughts on the menu and any suggestions you might have.
