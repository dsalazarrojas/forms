# Stripe Pizza Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect order details for a customer's pizza order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Order Details" field and fill in your order details.
2. In the "Toppings" field, select your favorite topping(s) by clicking on the corresponding option(s).
3. Enter the quantity of the pizza you are ordering in the "Quantity" field. This is a required field.
4. Choose your desired size of the pizza from the "Size" field.
5. Select your preferred crust type from the "Crust Type" field.
6. Choose your desired sauce from the "Sauce" field.
7. Select your order type (pickup, delivery, or in-store) from the "Order Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`Order Details`, text, optional): Enter your order details in this field.
* **Toppings** (`Toppings`, select_multiple, optional): Select your favorite topping(s) by clicking on the corresponding option(s).
* **Quantity** (`Quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of the pizza you are ordering.
* **Size** (`Size`, select_one, optional): Choose your desired size of the pizza.
* **Crust Type** (`Crust Type`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred crust type.
* **Sauce** (`Sauce`, select_one, optional): Choose your desired sauce.
* **Order Type** (`Order Type`, select_one, optional): Select your order type (pickup, delivery, or in-store).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about a field, refer to the form's help guide or contact our support team.
* If you have any special requests, please include them in the "Order Details" field.
* This form is only for pizza orders.
