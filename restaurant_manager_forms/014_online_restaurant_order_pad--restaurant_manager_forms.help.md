# Online Restaurant Order Pad - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Online Restaurant Order Pad" form is used to collect information from customers for their online orders in a restaurant. This includes collecting customer details and order specifics to facilitate efficient order management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name** in the text field provided. This will be the name of the person placing the order.
2. Provide your **Customer Email** in the email address format (e.g., `example@example.com`). This helps the restaurant keep in touch with you and inform you about your order status.
3. Enter your **Customer Phone** number in the text field provided. This will be the contact number the restaurant can use to reach you.
4. Describe the **Order Item** in the text field provided. Be as detailed as possible so the restaurant knows exactly what you want.
5. Specify the **Order Quantity** by entering a numeric value. This helps the restaurant prepare the right amount of items for you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (customer_name)**: Enter your personal name for identification purposes.
* **Customer Email (customer_email)**: Enter your email address for the restaurant to communicate with you.
* **Customer Phone (customer_phone)**: Enter a contact phone number for the restaurant to reach you.
* **Order Item (order_item)**: Clearly describe the item you want to order.
* **Order Quantity (quantity)**: Enter the number of items you want to order.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the **Order Item** to ensure the restaurant understands your order accurately.
* Double-check your **Customer Email** and **Customer Phone** for accuracy to ensure the restaurant can contact you efficiently.
* If you have any special requests or instructions for your order, you can include them in the **Order Item** field.
