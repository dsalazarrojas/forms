# Fast Food Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for restaurant staff to collect information about a customer's order, including their personal details and contact information. It will help us process your order accurately and ensure timely delivery or pickup of your food.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details such as first name, last name, and address.
2. Provide a valid email address for us to get in touch with you in case we need to reach you about your order.
3. Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
4. Review your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification or documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Not Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification or documents.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, Not Required): Enter your address where you would like to receive your order. This will help us ensure accurate delivery or pickup.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, Not Required): Provide a valid email address so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Not Required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case we need to reach you about your order.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you need assistance or have any questions, please ask a staff member for help.
