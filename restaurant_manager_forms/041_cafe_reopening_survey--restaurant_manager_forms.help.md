# Cafe Reopening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to help cafes understand their customers' preferences and concerns regarding their reopening. Your input will provide valuable insights for the cafe management to make informed decisions about their services and safety measures.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Select all the options that apply to your preferences.
3.  Enter your email address if you would like to receive updates from the cafe.
4.  Provide detailed feedback and suggestions in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Respondent name**: This is a field for you to provide your name. It is optional but helps us get in touch with you if needed.
*   **Likelihood to visit after reopening**: Choose how likely you are to visit the cafe after it reopens. Options include Very likely, Likely, Neutral, and Unlikely.
*   **Main safety concerns**: Select all the safety concerns that you have about visiting the cafe. Options are Social distancing, Cleanliness, Mask policy, and Capacity limits.
*   **Preferred services after reopening**: Choose the services you would prefer after the cafe reopens. Options are Dine in, Takeaway, and Delivery.
*   **Preferred operating hours**: This field is for you to specify your preferred operating hours. You can enter your preferred hours, e.g., morning, afternoon, or evening.
*   **Interest in menu changes**: Check if you would like to see new items introduced on the menu. Options are 'True' and 'False'.
*   **Feedback on safety measures**: This is a field for you to provide detailed feedback and suggestions on how the cafe can improve their safety measures.
*   **Would you like updates by email**: If you would like to receive updates from the cafe, please enter your email address.

## Tips

*   Be honest and accurate in your input.
*   Select all the options that genuinely apply to your preferences.
*   Use a detailed and constructive tone when providing feedback.
