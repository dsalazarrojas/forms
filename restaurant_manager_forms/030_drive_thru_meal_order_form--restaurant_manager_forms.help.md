# Drive Thru Meal Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Drive Thru Meal Order Form is designed to collect information for a drive-thru meal order. This form helps users input their order details, allowing for efficient processing and management of orders.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the Main Page (Page 1), where you can enter any additional information or comments.
2.  Move on to the Order Beverages (Page 2) and select the beverages you'd like to order.
3.  Proceed to the Order Side Sauce (Page 3) and choose your desired side sauces.
4.  Select your entrée options on the Order Entrée (Page 4).
5.  Add any toppings you'd like on the Order Toppings (Page 5).
6.  Choose your payment method on the Order Payment Method (Page 6).
7.  Enter any comments on the Order Comment (Page 7).
8.  The system will automatically calculate the Order Total (Page 8).
9.  Confirm your order details on the Order Confirmation (Page 9).
10.  Set the order status to either Pending, In Progress, Complete, or Cancelled on the Order Status (Page 10).
11.  Optionally, enter the assigned tool or user information on the Assigned Tool and Assigned User pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Main Page**: This page contains additional information or comments about your order.
*   **Order Beverages**: Choose your preferred beverage, such as Water, Soda, Juice, Coffee, or Tea.
*   **Order Side Sauce**: Pick one or more side sauces like Ketchup, Mustard, Hot Sauce, or None.
*   **Order Entrée**: Select one or more entrées such as Burger, Fries, Salad, Chicken, or Combo.
*   **Order Toppings**: Choose one or more toppings like Cheese, Pickles, Onions, Tomatoes, Mayo, Ketchup, or Mustard.
*   **Order Payment Method**: Select your payment method: Cash, Credit Card, or Debit Card.
*   **Order Comment**: Enter any additional comments about your order.
*   **Order Total**: This field is automatically populated based on your order selection.
*   **Order Confirmation**: Review your order details before proceeding.
*   **Order Status**: Set the order status to Pending, In Progress, Complete, or Cancelled.
*   **Assigned Tool**: If you've been assigned a specific tool for your order, enter it here.
*   **Assigned User**: If you've been assigned to a specific user, enter their name here.
