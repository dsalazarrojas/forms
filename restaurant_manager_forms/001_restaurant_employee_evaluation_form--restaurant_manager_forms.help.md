# Restaurant Employee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Employee Evaluation Form is used by supervisors to assess and document the performance of restaurant employees. This form helps supervisors provide constructive feedback and evaluate employee performance on a regular basis.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please select the appropriate page sections and fill in the required fields.
2. Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Review and submit the form when all necessary sections have been filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, `note`, not required): This section is for form header information.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, not required): Enter the employee's ID number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, not required): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Supervisor** (`supervisor`, `select_one`, not required): Select the supervisor's name from the dropdown list.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, not required): Rate the employee's performance (1-5).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, not required): Provide any additional comments or feedback for the employee.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Employee Rating** (`employee_rating`, `select_one`, not required): Select the employee's rating from the dropdown list.
* **Evaluation Form Completed** (`evaluation_form_completed`, `select_multiple`, not required): Indicate whether the form has been completed.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): Add any additional comments about the employee's performance.
* **Form Completed** (`form_completed`, `select_one`, not required): Indicate whether the form has been completed.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, not required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, `select_one`, not required): Select the employee's status (Active or Inactive).
* **Reason for Inactive Status** (`employee_status_reason`, `text`, not required): Provide a reason for the employee's inactive status.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, `text`, not required): Add a signature from the supervisor.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, `text`, not required): Add a signature from the employee.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `text`, not required): Enter a form ID (if applicable).
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `text`, not required): Enter the assigned to information (if applicable).
* **Tool Used** (`tool_used`, `text`, not required): Enter the tool used (if applicable).
* **Created At** (`created_at`, `date`, not required): Display the form creation date and time (auto-filled).
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, `date`, not required): Display the form update date and time (auto-filled).
* **User ID** (`user_id`, `text`, not required): Enter the user's ID (if applicable).
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, `text`, not required): Enter the form version (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all sections are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form before submitting it.
* Use the form for its intended purpose to evaluate and document employee performance.
* If you're unsure about any section, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
