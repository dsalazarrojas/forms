# Restaurant Customer Preferences Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your preferences and needs so that we can provide you a better dining experience at our restaurant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Click on the "Submit" button to start filling the form.
- Select your preferences and dietary needs from the available options.
- Choose your preferred dining experience and dining time.
- Provide any additional comments or feedback about your visit.
- Enter your contact information (email and phone number) so we can reach you back.
- Click on the "Submit" button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Preferences** (`form_01_preferences`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any specific preferences you have while visiting our restaurant. Please be as specific as possible so we can serve you better.
* **Dietary Needs** (`form_02_dietary_needs`, select multiple, optional): Choose your dietary needs from the available options. If you have any specific dietary requirements or restrictions, please select them here.
* **Dining Experience** (`form_03_dining_experience`, select one, optional): Choose your preferred dining experience from the available options. This will help us tailor our service to your taste.
* **Dining Time** (`form_04_dining_time`, date, optional): Select your preferred dining time for your visit. This will help us schedule your table accordingly.
* **Dining Date** (`form_05_dining_date`, time, optional): Select your preferred dining time for your visit. Please make sure to select the correct date and time.
* **Feedback** (`form_06_feedback`, note, optional): If you have any feedback or suggestions about our restaurant, please provide them here. Your input is valuable to us.
* **Email** (`form_07_email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address so we can contact you back about your reservation or any other inquiry.
* **Phone** (`form_08_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you back if needed.
* **Additional Comments** (`form_09_additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them here.
