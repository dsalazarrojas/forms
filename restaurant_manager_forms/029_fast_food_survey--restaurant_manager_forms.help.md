# Fast Food Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fast Food Survey is a tool designed to collect information about your fast food restaurant's details and characteristics. This form is intended to be completed by restaurant managers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the location name of your restaurant.
2. Enter your name and title.
3. Enter your phone number and email address.
4. Specify your restaurant's hours of operation.
5. Enter your restaurant's address.
6. Enter your manager's phone number and email address.
7. Specify your restaurant's manager's hours of operation.
8. Enter your manager's age and years of experience.
9. Indicate whether your restaurant offers a menu.
10. Specify your manager's education level.
11. Specify your manager's gender.
12. Specify your manager's marital status.
13. Specify your manager's family status.
14. Specify your manager's languages.
15. Enter the date your restaurant was opened.
16. Select whether your restaurant offers restaurant type (Yes/No).
For each field, ensure that you enter accurate information to the best of your abilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Location Name
* **Location Name** (`location_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your restaurant's location.

### 2. Manager Name
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as the manager of your restaurant.

### 3. Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.

### 4. Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.

### 5. Hours of Operation
* **Hours of Operation** (`hours_of_operation`, `text`, required/optional): Specify your restaurant's hours of operation.

### 6. Location Hours
* **Location Hours** (`location_hours`, `text`, required/optional): Specify your restaurant's location's hours of operation.

### 7. Manager Photo
* **Manager Photo** (`manager_photo`, `text`, required/optional): This field is not applicable and has been left empty.

### 8. Restaurant Name
* **Restaurant Name** (`restaurant_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your restaurant's name.

### 9. Location Address
* **Location Address** (`location_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your restaurant's address.

### 10. Manager Phone
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your manager's phone number.

### 11. Manager Email
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your manager's email address.

### 12. Manager Hours
* **Manager Hours** (`manager_hours`, `text`, required/optional): Specify your manager's hours of operation.

### 13. Manager Experience
* **Manager Experience** (`manager_experience`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your manager's years of experience.

### 14. Location Distance
* **Location Distance** (`location_distance`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the distance from your restaurant to the nearest landmark or transportation hub.

### 15. Manager Age
* **Manager Age** (`manager_age`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your manager's age.

### 16. Years of Experience
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your manager's years of experience.

### 17. Restaurant Type
* **Restaurant Type** (`restaurant_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate whether your restaurant offers a menu.

### 18. Menu Items
* **Menu Items** (`menu_items`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the types of food and drinks offered on your menu.

### 19. Manager Education
* **Manager Education** (`manager_education`, `text`, required/optional): Specify your manager's level of education.

### 20. Manager Gender
* **Manager Gender** (`manager_gender`, `text`, required/optional): Specify your manager's gender.

### 21. Manager Marital Status
* **Manager Marital Status** (`manager_marital_status`, `text`, required/optional): Specify your manager's marital status.

### 22. Manager Family Status
* **Manager Family Status** (`manager_family_status`, `text`, required/optional): Specify your manager's family status.

### 23. Manager Children
* **Manager Children** (`manager_children`, `text`, required/optional): Specify your manager's number of children.

### 24. Manager Languages
* **Manager Languages** (`manager_languages`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the languages spoken by your manager.

### 25. Date Opened
* **Date Opened** (`date_opened`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date your restaurant was opened.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate information for each field.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If a field has a required/optional label, it indicates whether the field is mandatory or optional.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or team member for clarification.
