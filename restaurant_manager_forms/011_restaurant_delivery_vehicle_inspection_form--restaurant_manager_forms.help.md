# Restaurant Delivery Vehicle Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to inspect and verify the safety and compliance of the restaurant delivery vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of the delivery vehicle from the options provided.
2. Record the vehicle's registration number.
3. Enter the year of the vehicle.
4. Attach any relevant photo documentation, such as pictures of the vehicle's condition.
5. Record the date of the vehicle inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Type** (`Vehicle Type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of vehicle being inspected. The options are 'Yes' and 'No'. If you are unsure, please select 'No' and discuss with the relevant authorities.
* **Vehicle Registration** (`Vehicle Registration`, text, required: false): Record the vehicle registration number accurately.
* **Year** (`Year`, number, required: false): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Photo Documentation** (`Photo Documentation`, note, required: false): Attach any relevant photos that show the vehicle's condition.
* **Inspection Date** (`Inspection Date`, date, required: false): Record the date of the vehicle inspection.

## Tips
* Be accurate and thorough when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with the relevant authorities or supervisors.
* This form is only for restaurant delivery vehicles, so ensure that you select the correct vehicle type.
* Attach clear and relevant photo documentation to support your claims.
