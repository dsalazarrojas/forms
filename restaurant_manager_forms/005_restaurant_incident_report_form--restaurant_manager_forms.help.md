# Restaurant Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Incident Report Form is a tool for reporting incidents that occur within the restaurant environment. It is essential to report incidents as soon as possible to ensure that the restaurant management team can investigate and address the issue promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the date and time of the incident in the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" fields, respectively.
2. Enter the location of the incident in the "Location" field.
3. Indicate if there were any witnesses to the incident in the "Witnesses" field. If there were witnesses, please list their names below.
4. Attach any supporting documents related to the incident in the "Supporting Documents" field.
5. Provide a detailed description of what happened in the "Incident Details" field.
6. Indicate if the incident was caused by a person or not in the "Incident Cause" field.
7. Enter the name of the restaurant where the incident occurred in the "Restaurant Name" field.
8. Enter the name of the employee involved in the incident in the "Employee Name" field.
9. Provide a contact number for the restaurant in the "Contact Number" field.
10. Enter a description of what happened in the "Incident Description" field.
11. If applicable, attach an image or video related to the incident in the "Incident Image" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (1): The date of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (2): The time of the incident.
* **Location** (3): The location where the incident occurred.
* **Witnesses** (4): Indicate if there were any witnesses to the incident. If yes, list their names below.
* **Supporting Documents** (5): Attach any relevant documents or photos related to the incident.
* **Incident Details** (6): Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Incident Cause** (7): Indicate if the incident was caused by a person or not.
* **Restaurant Name** (8): The name of the restaurant where the incident occurred.
* **Employee Name** (9): The name of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Contact Number** (10): A contact number for the restaurant.
* **Incident Description** (11): A description of what happened.
* **Incident Image** (12): If applicable, attach an image or video related to the incident.

## Tips
Please fill out this form as accurately and thoroughly as possible to ensure that the restaurant management team can investigate and address the issue efficiently.
