# Restaurant Health Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Health Screening Questionnaire is used to screen the health status of restaurant employees to ensure their well-being and the safety of the work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct employee name and location from the dropdown options.
2. Choose the employee's health status: Healthy or Unhealthy.
3. Enter the employee's phone number and email address for contact purposes.
4. Select the restaurant location where the employee works.
5. Enter the date of the last screening for the employee.
6. Enter the time of the last screening for the employee.
7. Select the date of the last screening for the restaurant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`restaurant_health_screening_questionnaire_2`, text, Required):
    Enter the name of the employee being screened. This is the name that will appear in the form header.
* **Employee Health Status** (`restaurant_health_screening_questionnaire_3`, select_one, Required):
    Choose the current health status of the employee. Options are Healthy or Unhealthy.
* **Employee Phone** (`restaurant_health_screening_questionnaire_4`, text, Optional):
    Enter the phone number of the employee for contact purposes.
* **Employee Email** (`restaurant_health_screening_questionnaire_5`, email, Optional):
    Enter the email address of the employee for contact purposes.
* **Restaurant Location** (`restaurant_health_screening_questionnaire_6`, select_one, Optional):
    Choose the location of the restaurant where the employee works from the dropdown options.
* **Last Screening Date** (`restaurant_health_screening_questionnaire_9`, date, Optional):
    Enter the date of the last health screening for the employee or the restaurant.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form for the correct employee and restaurant.
* Review and update the form regularly for accurate information.
* Ensure to store the form for future reference and follow-up screenings.
* Use this form to identify potential health risks and take necessary precautions to maintain a safe working environment.
