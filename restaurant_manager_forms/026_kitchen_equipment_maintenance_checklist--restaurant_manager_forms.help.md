# Kitchen Equipment Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kitchen Equipment Maintenance Checklist form is designed to track and record maintenance activities for kitchen equipment in a restaurant. This form helps ensure that maintenance tasks are performed regularly, reducing the risk of equipment malfunctions and accidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of kitchen equipment from the list (if provided).
2. Enter the last maintenance date for the selected equipment.
3. Set the next maintenance date for the equipment.
4. Check the maintenance status (Active, Inactive, or Retired).
5. Select the maintenance frequency (Yes or No).
6. Enter the cost of the maintenance.
7. Add any additional notes about the maintenance.
8. Provide contact information (Name, Phone, and Email) of the person responsible for the maintenance.
9. Select the date the entry was created.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Kitchen Equipment** (`equipment_type`): Enter the type of kitchen equipment being maintained (e.g., Refrigerator, Oven, etc.).
* **Last Maintenance Date** (`last_maintenance_date`): Record the date of the last maintenance performed on the equipment.
* **Next Maintenance Date** (`next_maintenance_date`): Set the date for the next scheduled maintenance.
* **Status** (`maintenance_status`): Check the status of the equipment maintenance (Active, Inactive, or Retired).
* **Maintenance Frequency** (`maintenance_frequency`): Select how often the equipment is maintained (Yes or No).
* **Cost** (`maintenance_cost`): Enter the cost of the last maintenance performed.
* **Notes** (`maintenance_notes`): Add any additional comments or notes about the maintenance.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of the person responsible for the maintenance.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the maintenance.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the email of the person responsible for the maintenance.
* **Created At** (`created_at`): Record the date the entry was created.
* **Last Updated At** (`last_updated_at`): Record the date the entry was last updated.
 
 **Tips**
* Only one entry should be made per piece of equipment.
* Ensure that the contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
* Regularly review and update the maintenance schedule to ensure that maintenance activities are performed on time.
* Store the completed form securely to maintain records and ensure compliance with food safety regulations.
