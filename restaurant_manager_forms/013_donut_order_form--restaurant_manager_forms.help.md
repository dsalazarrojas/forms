# Donut Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donut Order Form is a tool used to collect donut order information from customers. This information will help the restaurant to fulfill the customer's order accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the customer's details**:
	* In the "Customer Details" section, enter the customer's name and contact information (email and phone number).
2. **Select the donut type**:
	* In the "Donut Type" section, select the type of donut the customer would like to order (e.g., Sprinkles, Jelly, etc.).
3. **Enter order date and time**:
	* In the "Order Date" and "Order Time" sections, enter the date and time the customer would like to pick up their donut order.
4. **Enter order quantity**:
	* In the "Quantity" section, enter the number of donuts the customer would like to order.
5. **Add any customer notes**:
	* In the "Customer Notes" section, enter any additional information about the customer's order, such as special requests or allergies.
6. **Add order comments**:
	* In the "Order Comments" section, enter any comments or instructions for the customer or staff.
7. **Review and submit**:
	* Once all fields are completed, review the form for accuracy and submit it to the restaurant staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details (Customer Details)**: Enter the customer's name and contact information.
* **Order Date (Order Date)**: Enter the date the customer would like to pick up their donut order.
* **Order Time (Order Time)**: Enter the time the customer would like to pick up their donut order.
* **Donut Type (Donut Type)**: Select the type of donut the customer would like to order.
	+ Sprinkles: A type of donut with sprinkles on top.
	+ Sprinkles with Sprinkles: A type of donut with extra sprinkles on top.
	+ Jelly: A type of donut with jelly filling.
	+ Jelly with Sprinkles: A type of donut with jelly filling and sprinkles on top.
* **Quantity (Quantity)**: Enter the number of donuts the customer would like to order.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Notes (Customer Notes)**: Enter any additional information about the customer's order, such as special requests or allergies.
* **Order Comments (Order Comments)**: Enter any comments or instructions for the customer or staff.
* **Customer Notes (Customer Notes)**: This field is used to store any notes or messages about the customer. It is a text field and has no specific format or requirement.

Tips:

* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure the customer's order is fulfilled correctly.
* If the customer has any dietary restrictions or special requests, make sure to note them in the "Customer Notes" field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the restaurant staff for clarification.
