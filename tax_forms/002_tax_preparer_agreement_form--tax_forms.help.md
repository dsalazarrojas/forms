## Purpose
The Tax Preparer Agreement Form is a document that outlines the terms of the relationship between a client and a tax return preparer. It includes details on the services to be provided, payment terms, and communication preferences. This form ensures that both parties are aware of their roles and responsibilities in the tax return preparation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring that all required fields are completed accurately.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure that all the details are correct and agreeable to both parties.
3. Sign the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms of the agreement.
