# Dropbox Tax Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dropbox Tax Submission Form is used to submit tax-related information to the accounting department. This form is used to track the progress of tax submissions and provide additional context for the submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as applicable. You can submit the form online or offline, and it will be reviewed and updated by the accounting department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Submission Date (ID 1)
• **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, optional): The date the submission was made. This field is not required but is useful for tracking progress.

### Type (ID 2)
• **Type** (`submission_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
This field is used to classify the submission as either "yes" or "no" for tracking purposes.

### Status (ID 3)
• **Status** (`submission_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Received
	+ Rejected
	+ In Progress
This field is used to track the status of the submission.

### Document Count (ID 4)
• **Document Count** (`document_count`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of documents attached to the submission.

### Tax Year (ID 5)
• **Tax Year** (`tax_year`, `number`, optional): Enter the tax year being reported.

### Tax Type (ID 6)
• **Tax Type** (`tax_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Income Tax
	+ Sales Tax
	+ Estate Tax
This field is used to specify the type of tax being reported.

### Submission Notes (ID 7)
• **Submission Notes** (`submission_notes`, `note`, optional): Provide additional information or context for the submission.
