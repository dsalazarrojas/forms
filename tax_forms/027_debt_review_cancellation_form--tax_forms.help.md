# Debt Review Cancellation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information and facilitate the cancellation of debt. This form will help guide users through the cancellation process, ensuring that all necessary details are collected.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the "Name" field with the name of the individual or entity involved in the debt review process.
2. Provide contact details in the "Contact Details" field, including phone number and email (if applicable).
3. Select a reason for cancellation from the "Select Reason" dropdown, or provide a custom reason in the "Reason for Cancellation (Other)" field.
4. Enter the "Date of Enrollment" and "Date of Service" using the selected date format (e.g., month/day/year or year).
5. If necessary, provide additional contact details or agency information.
6. Confirm the cancellation by selecting "Confirm Cancellation" and providing a signature (if required).
