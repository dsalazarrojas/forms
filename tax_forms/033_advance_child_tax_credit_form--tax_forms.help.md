# Advance Child Tax Credit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advance Child Tax Credit Form is a tax credit form designed to help taxpayers claim the Advance Child Tax Credit. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own specific purpose and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
- Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
- Use the provided hints as guidance for each section.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Parent Full Name (Page 2)**: This field is required for identity verification. Please enter the parent's full name as it appears on their government-issued ID.
  - **Social Security Number - Last 4 (Page 3)**: This field is required for verification purposes. Enter the last four digits of the parent's Social Security number for identity verification.
  - **Mailing Address (Page 4)**: Enter the taxpayer's mailing address, including Street, City, State, and Zip for accurate delivery of the Advance Child Tax Credit.
  - **Contact Email (Page 5)**: This field is required for communication purposes. Enter the taxpayer's email address for any further communication or verification.
  - **Child Name (Page 7)**: Enter the child's full name as it appears on their birth certificate or government-issued ID.
  - **Child Date of Birth (Page 8)**: Enter the child's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
  - **School Currently Attending (Page 9)**: Enter the name of the school the child is currently attending.
  - **Education Level (Page 10)**: Select the current education level of the child, which could be Pre-K, Elementary, Middle School, High School, or Higher Education.
  - **Annual Tuition Expenses (Page 13)**: Enter the total annual tuition expenses for the child's education.
  - **Other Educational Costs (Page 14)**: Enter any additional education-related expenses, such as books, supplies, and transportation.
  - **Previously Claimed This Year (Page 14)**: Indicate whether the taxpayer has previously claimed the Advance Child Tax Credit for this child this year.
  - **Accuracy Confirmation (Page 15)**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate. This is a legal declaration and is required for the successful claim.
  - **Parent Legal Signature (Page 17)**: Sign the form with the taxpayer's full legal name to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

Note: All fields marked with "(Page X)" refer to the corresponding page number on the form.
