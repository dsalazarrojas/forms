# W4-Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The W4-Form is a form used to collect employee information for a W-4 form, which is a tax-related document used by employees to authorize a specific withholding rate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "employee-name" field.
2. Enter the employee's SNIP (Social Security Number or Identification Program) number in the "employee-snip" field.
3. Provide any additional information about the SNIP number in the "employee-snip-hint" field (optional).
4. Enter the employee's address in the "employee-address" field.
5. Enter the date of hire in the "employee-date-of-hire" field.
6. Select the employee's status (Active, Inactive, or Terminated) in the "employee-status" field.
7. Enter the employee's address (additional) in the "employee-address2" field.
8. Enter the employee's address (additional) in the "employee-address3" field.
9. Enter the employee's address (additional) in the "employee-address4" field.
10. Enter the employee's email address in the "employee-email" field.
11. Enter the employee's phone number in the "employee-phone" field.
12. Enter any additional information for the employee's signature in the "employee-signature" field.
13. Provide any additional information about the signature in the "employee-signature-hint" field (optional).
14. Enter any additional value for the employee's signature in the "employee-signature-value" field.
15. Enter any additional information for the employee's signature in the "employee-signature2" field.
16. Provide any additional information about the second signature in the "employee-signature2-hint" field (optional).
17. Enter any additional value for the employee's signature in the "employee-signature2-value" field.
18. Enter any additional information for the "W-4 form" in the "employee-w-4-form" field.
19. Provide any additional information about the "W-4 form" in the "employee-w-4-form-hint" field (optional).
20. Enter any additional value for the "W-4 form" in the "employee-w-4-form-value" field.
21. Enter the withholding information in the "w-4-withholding" field.
22. Provide any additional information about the withholding in the "w-4-withholding-hint" field (optional).
