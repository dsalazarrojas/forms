This is a user-facing help guide for the "tax_exemption_form" form.

**Purpose**
The tax_exemption_form is a form used to collect information about tax exemptions for an organization. This form is used to determine the tax exemption status of an organization, including the exemption status, reason for exemption, date of exemption, exemption type, tax ID number, exempted by, exemption amount, tax year, submitted by, submitted date, and tax authority.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the organization's name in the "Organization Name" field.
2.  Select the exemption status from the "Exemption Status" field.
3.  Enter the reason for exemption in the "Reason For Exemption" field.
4.  Enter the date of exemption in the "Date Of Exemption" field in the required format (YYYY-MM-DD).
5.  Select the exemption type from the "Exemption Type" field.
6.  Enter the tax ID number in the "Tax ID Number" field.
7.  Enter the name of the person or entity that exempted the organization in the "Exempted By" field.
8.  Enter the exemption amount in the "Exemption Amount" field.
9.  Enter the tax year in the "Tax Year" field.
10. Enter the name of the person or entity that submitted the exemption application in the "Submitted By" field.
11. Enter the date the exemption application was submitted in the "Submitted Date" field in the required format (YYYY-MM-DD).
12. Enter the tax authority in the "Tax Authority" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Organization Name (text)**: Enter the name of the organization.
- **Exemption Status (select_one)**: Select the current exemption status of the organization (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
- **Reason For Exemption (text)**: Enter the reason for which the organization is exempted.
- **Date Of Exemption (date)**: Enter the date on which the organization was exempted.
- **Exemption Type (select_multiple)**: Select all exemption types that apply to the organization (Individual, Organization, or Government Agency).
- **Tax ID Number (text)**: Enter the tax identification number of the organization.
- **Exempted By (text)**: Enter the name of the person or entity that exempted the organization.
- **Exemption Amount (number)**: Enter the exemption amount.
- **Tax Year (number)**: Enter the tax year for which the exemption applies.
- **Submitted By (text)**: Enter the name of the person or entity that submitted the exemption application.
- **Submitted Date (date)**: Enter the date the exemption application was submitted.
- **Tax Authority (text)**: Enter the tax authority that approved the exemption.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check the dates for accuracy.
- Ensure that the exemption status is accurate.
- Enter all relevant exemption types.
- If unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
