# Individual Tax Return Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about individual tax returns for the purpose of processing and tracking tax return submissions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter information about the tax return in the corresponding fields, as described in the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.
*   Fill out all required fields. The required fields are indicated by a asterisk (\*) in the Field-by-Field Explanation section.
*   Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Tax Return Information** (`tax_return_info`, `text`, not required): Enter a brief description of the tax return.
*   **Tax Return Type** (`tax_return_type`, `select_one`, not required): Select the type of tax return, such as W-2 or 1095.
*   **Income** (`income`, `number`, not required): Enter the total income for the tax return.
*   **Deductions** (`deductions`, `number`, not required): Enter any deductions related to the tax return.
*   **Expenses** (`expenses`, `text`, not required): Enter any expenses related to the tax return.
*   **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, not required): Enter the date the tax return was submitted.
*   **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, `time`, not required): Enter the time the tax return was submitted.
*   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `note`, not required): Enter the name of the person or entity submitting the tax return.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter the email address of the person or entity submitting the tax return.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter the phone number of the person or entity submitting the tax return.
*   **Tax Return Number** (`tax_return_number`, `text`, not required): Enter the tax return number.
*   **Tax Return Version** (`tax_return_version`, `select_one`, not required): Select the version of the tax return, such as 2018 or 2019.
*   **Tax Return Year** (`tax_return_year`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the year of the tax return, such as 2018, 2019, or 2020.
*   **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the financial institution related to the tax return.
*   **Tax Return Status** (`tax_return_status`, `select_one`, not required): Select the status of the tax return, such as Pending, Approved, or Rejected.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): Enter any comments or notes about the tax return.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the tax return.
*   **Tax Return Filing Date** (`tax_return_filing_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date the tax return was filed.
*   **Time Return Filing** (`time_return_filing`, `time`, not required): Enter the time the tax return was filed.
*   **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, `select_one`, not required): Select the entity the tax return was submitted to, such as Accountant or IRS.
*   **Submitted By User** (`submitted_by_user`, `select_one`, not required): Select the type of user submitting the tax return, such as Financial Expert or Individual.
*   **Financial Institution Name** (`financial_institution_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the financial institution related to the tax return.
*   **Financial Institution Address** (`financial_institution_address`, `text`, not required): Enter the address of the financial institution related to the tax return.
*   **Financial Institution Phone** (`financial_institution_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter the phone number of the financial institution related to the tax return.
*   **Financial Institution Email** (`financial_institution_email`, `email`, not required): Enter the email address of the financial institution related to the tax return.

## Tips

*   Be sure to double-check your entries before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authority for clarification.
*   This form is for informational purposes only and does not constitute professional tax advice. For official tax guidance, please consult with a tax professional.
