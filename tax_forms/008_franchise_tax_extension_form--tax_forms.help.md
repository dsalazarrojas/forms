# franchise_tax_extension_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "franchise_tax_extension_form" is used for requesting an extension for franchise tax, providing required information to support your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the above guide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Taxpayer Information (1)** (`taxpayer_information`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Tax Year (2)** (`tax_year`, number, optional): Specify the year for which you are requesting a tax extension.
* **Entity Type (3)** (`entity_type`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you are a Small Entity or Large Entity.
* **Entity Name (4)** (`entity_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your entity (if applicable).
* **Reason for Request (5)** (`reason_for_request`, text, optional): Provide a clear explanation of why you need the extension.
* **Franchise Tax Filing Date (6)** (`franchise_tax_filing_date`, date, optional): Specify the date you plan to file your franchise tax return.
* **Franchise Tax Return Due Date (7)** (`franchise_tax_return_due_date`, date, optional): Specify the due date for your franchise tax return.
* **Extension Request Date (8)** (`extension_request_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are requesting the extension for.
* **Requested Filing Date (9)** (`requested_filing_date`, date, optional): Specify the date you are requesting to file your tax return.
* **Reason for Request (10)** (`reason_for_request`, text, optional): Repeat the reason for your request (for confirmation).
* **Contact Information (11)** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter your address, contact number, and email address.
* **Address (12)** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Contact Number (13)** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Email (14)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Reason for Request (10)** (`reason_for_request`, text, optional): Enter a clear explanation of why you need the extension.
