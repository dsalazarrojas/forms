# Stock Transfer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stock Transfer Form is used to transfer stock from one entity to another. This form is used to capture the details of the stock transfer process, including the company name, dates, shares, reason, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name where the stock is currently held.
2. Select the date when the stock was obtained or last transferred.
3. Select the date when the stock is to be transferred.
4. Enter the symbol of the stock to be transferred.
5. Enter the symbol of the stock being transferred to.
6. Enter the number of shares to be transferred from and to.
7. Enter a brief reason for the transfer.
8. Enter any additional notes about the transfer.
9. Enter the email address of the user who initiated the transfer.
10. Enter the phone number of the user who initiated the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company that currently holds the stock.
* **From Date** (`from_date`, date, optional): Select the date when the stock was obtained or last transferred.
* **To Date** (`to_date`, date, optional): Select the date when the stock is to be transferred.
* **From Symbol** (`from_symbol`, text, optional): Enter the symbol of the stock to be transferred from.
* **To Symbol** (`to_symbol`, text, optional): Enter the symbol of the stock being transferred to.
* **From Shares** (`from_shares`, number, optional): Enter the number of shares to be transferred from.
* **To Shares** (`to_shares`, number, optional): Enter the number of shares to be transferred to.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Enter a brief reason for the stock transfer.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the transfer.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the user who initiated the transfer.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the user who initiated the transfer.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Not applicable for this form, this field is not used in the form.
