# Income Tax Information Form In Hindi - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect income tax information from taxpayers in Hindi. It is designed to be a brief and straightforward way to provide necessary details for tax purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the tax year for which you are providing information.
2. Provide your name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. Enter your address for verification purposes.
4. Enter your phone number for communication.
5. Enter your email address for contact.
6. Enter your annual income.
7. Select the type of tax return you are filing (e.g., Tax Return or Other).
8. If you select "Tax Return", provide additional details about your tax return.
9. If you select "Other", provide details about your other income sources (e.g., Rent, Interest, Dividend).
10. If you have other income sources, select them from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tax Year (`tax_year`, number, optional):** Enter the tax year you are providing information for.
* **Name (`name`, text, optional):** Provide your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address (`address`, text, optional):** Enter your address for verification purposes.
* **Phone (`phone`, text, optional):** Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Email (`email`, email, optional):** Enter your email address for contact.
* **Income (`income`, number, optional):** Enter your annual income.
* **Tax Return (`tax_return`, select_one, required):** Select the type of tax return you are filing (e.g., Tax Return or Other).
* **Tax Return Details (`tax_return_details`, text, optional):** If you select "Tax Return", provide additional details about your tax return.
* **Bank Details (`bank_details`, select_multiple, required):** If you select "Other", select Bank Name, Bank Account Number, and Bank Branch from the list.
* **Other Income Sources (`other_income_sources`, select_multiple, optional):** If you select "Other", select Rent, Interest, Dividend from the list.
* **Other (`tax_return_other`, text, optional):** If you have other income sources, describe them here.

## Tips

* Please ensure you are providing accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the hints provided for guidance.
* If you need further assistance, contact the relevant authorities or consult with a tax professional.
