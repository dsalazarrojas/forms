# Rental Property Tax Organizer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users accurately and efficiently provide rental property tax information. It guides users through the collection of essential tax details, ensuring that all required fields are filled out correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Rental Property Information** page, which includes the property's details.
2. Move on to the **Taxpayer Information** page, where you will provide your personal or business tax identification details.
3. On the **Tax Year**, **Tax Base**, and **Tax Rate** pages, input the relevant tax year, base, and rate information, respectively.
4. Choose the correct **Tax Rate Type** and indicate whether the property is **Tax Exempt**.
5. Input the **Tax Deduction**, **Tax Credit**, and **Rental Period**, pages for accurate tax calculation.
6. Select the **Tax Filing Status** and **Tax Filing Type** for your tax submission.
7. Provide any additional **Tax Notes** if needed.
8. Enter your **Email**, **Phone**, and **Address** for communication purposes.
9. Finally, sign the **Signature** page to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rental Property Information (1)**: This is the first page of the form. Enter the relevant information about the rental property, including its details.
* **Taxpayer Information (2)**: Provide your personal or business tax identification details on this page.
* **Tax Year (3)**: Input the tax year for which you are submitting the tax information.
* **Tax Base (4)**: Enter the tax base for the rental property.
* **Tax Rate (5)**: Input the tax rate applicable to the rental property.
* **Tax Rate Type (6)**: Choose the correct tax rate type for the property.
* **Tax Exempt (7)**: Indicate whether the property is tax-exempt.
* **Tax Deduction (8)**: Input the tax deduction amount.
* **Tax Credit (9)**: Enter the tax credit amount.
* **Rental Period (10)**: Input the rental period for the property.
* **Tax Filing Status (11)**: Select your tax filing status (e.g., Single, Joint).
* **Tax Filing Type (12)**: Choose your tax filing type (e.g., Personal, Business).
* **Tax Notes (13)**: Enter any additional tax notes if needed.
* **Email (14)**: Provide your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (15)**: Input your phone number.
* **Address (16)**: Enter your mailing address.
* **Signature (17)**: Sign the form to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Submit (18)**: Review and submit the form for processing.
