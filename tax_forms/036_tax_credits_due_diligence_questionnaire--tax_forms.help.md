# Tax Credits Due Diligence Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the purpose of assessing a company's or individual's eligibility for tax credits. It is used to gather details about general information, financial data, qualifying activities, supporting documents, and additional questions that may be relevant to tax credit qualification.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about yourself or your company, such as name, address, and contact details.
2. Answer questions about your financial data, including any relevant tax-related information.
3. Describe any activities that may be relevant to tax credits, such as investments or charitable donations.
4. Attach or describe any supporting documents that may be required for tax credit qualification.
5. Answer any additional questions that may be relevant to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)**: This field is used to collect your name, address, and contact details.
* **Financial Information (2)**: This field is used to gather information about your financial situation, such as income, expenses, and tax-related data.
* **Qualifying Activities (3)**: This field is used to describe any activities or investments that may be relevant to tax credits, such as charitable donations or business investments.
* **Supporting Documents (4)**: This field is used to attach or describe any supporting documents that may be required for tax credit qualification, such as invoices or receipts.
* **Additional Questions (5)**: This field is used to answer any further questions that may be relevant to your tax credit situation.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields accurately and completely.
* Attach any required supporting documents in the Supporting Documents field.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team for further assistance.
