# Capital Gains Tax Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Capital Gains Tax Claim Form is a form used to report capital gains and claim applicable tax deductions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your taxpayer information, including your first name, last name, Taxpayer ID or SSN, and contact details.
2. Select your filing status (e.g., Single, Married filing jointly, etc.).
3. Report the details of the capital gain, including the type of asset sold, its description, acquisition and sale dates, sale proceeds, and holding period.
4. Report any applicable tax deductions, such as capital improvement costs and other deductible expenses.
5. Confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`id: 4`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`id: 5`): Enter your last name.
* **Taxpayer ID or SSN** (`id: 6`): Enter your tax identification number or SSN.
* **Email Address** (`id: 7`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 8`): Enter your phone number.
* **Filing Status** (`id: 9`): Select your filing status (e.g., Single, Married filing jointly, etc.).
* **Tax Year** (`id: 10`): Enter the year of the capital gain.
* **Type of Asset Sold** (`id: 12`): Select the type of asset sold (e.g., Real estate, Stock, etc.).
* **Asset Description** (`id: 13`): Describe the asset sold.
* **Acquisition Date** (`id: 14`): Enter the date the asset was purchased.
* **Acquisition Cost** (`id: 15`): Enter the original purchase price of the asset.
* **Sale Date** (`id: 16`): Enter the date the asset was sold.
* **Sale Proceeds** (`id: 17`): Enter the amount received from the sale of the asset.
* **Holding Period** (`id: 18`): Select the holding period (e.g., Short-term, Long-term, etc.).
* **Adjusted Cost Basis** (`id: 20`): Enter the cost basis after any adjustments.
* **Selling Costs** (`id: 21`): Enter any commissions, fees, etc. related to the sale.
* **Capital Gain or Loss** (`id: 22`): This will be calculated automatically.
* **Capital Improvement Costs** (`id: 24`): Enter any capital improvement costs.
* **Other Deductible Expenses** (`id: 25`): Describe any other deductible expenses.
* **Primary Residence** (`id: 30`): Select whether this is the sale of a primary residence.
* **Section 121 Exclusion Amount** (`id: 33`): If qualified, enter the exclusion amount.
* **Is This an Installment Sale?** (`id: 35`): Select whether this is an installment sale.
* **Payments Received This Year** (`id: 36`): Enter any payments received this year.
* **Additional Information** (`id: 37`): Enter any other relevant details.
* **Confirmation** (`id: 38`): Confirm that all information is accurate and complete. 

## Tips
- Make sure to report all applicable tax deductions and expenses.
- Attach any supporting documentation for the capital gain or loss.
- If this is an installment sale, ensure that all payments received are reported accurately.
- Confirm that all information provided is accurate and complete before submitting the form.
