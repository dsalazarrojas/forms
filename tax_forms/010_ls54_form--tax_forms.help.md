# LS54 Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LS54 Form is used to collect information about an employee's work-related details and their employer's information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. For select_one fields, choose the correct option that best answers the question.
3. Ensure that all dates are in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
4. Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### employee_name
* **employee_name** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the employee.

### work_start_date
* **work_start_date** (`date`, required): Enter the date the employee started work.

### work_end_date
* **work_end_date** (`date`, required): Enter the date the employee ended work.

### employer_name
