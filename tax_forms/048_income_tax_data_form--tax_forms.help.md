# income_tax_data_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect income tax data from taxpayers.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field according to your information. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Taxpayer Name (required)
*   **Taxpayer Name** (`taxpayer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official government-issued identification.

### 2. Filing Status (not required)
*   **Filing Status** (`filing_status`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all applicable filing status options that apply to you.

### 3. Marital Status (not required)
*   **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, `select_one`, not required): Select your marital status.

### 4. Income Source (not required)
*   **Income Source** (`income_source`, `select_one`, not required): Select the primary source of your income.

### 5. Income Frequency (not required)
*   **Income Frequency** (`income_frequency`, `select_multiple`, not required): Indicate how frequently you receive income from your selected income source.

### 6. Tax Year (required)
*   **Tax Year** (`tax_year`, `date`, required): Enter the year for which you are submitting this tax data.

### 7. File Date (required)
*   **File Date** (`file_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are submitting this tax data.

### 8. File Time (required)
*   **File Time** (`file_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you are submitting this tax data.

### 9. Taxpayer Address (required)
*   **Taxpayer Address** (`taxpayer_address`, `text`, required): Enter your address.

### 10. City (required)
*   **City** (`taxpayer_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city of your address.

### 11. State (required)
*   **State** (`taxpayer_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state of your address.

### 12. Zip (required)
*   **Zip** (`taxpayer_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the zip code of your address.

### 13. Country (required)
*   **Country** (`taxpayer_country`, `text`, required): Enter the country of your address.

### 14. Phone (not required)
*   **Phone** (`taxpayer_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number.

### 15. Email (not required)
*   **Email** (`taxpayer_email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address.

## Tips
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are accurate and complete.
*   If you have any issues or concerns, contact your tax representative for assistance.
