# Intake Interview And Quality Review Sheet Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for intake interviews and quality reviews. It serves as a comprehensive sheet to document and verify critical details about volunteers, their experiences, and assigned tools. Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to facilitate efficient processing and quality control.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Intake Interview And Quality Review Sheet Form** title in the designated field.
2. Provide your **Volunteer Info** in the corresponding field.
3. Fill in the **Intake Interview Info** section with any relevant details from the interview.
4. Enter the **Review Sheet Info** date for the review.
5. Sign and date with your email address in the **Volunteer Signature** and **Volunteer Signature Date** fields.
6. Select the **Assigned Tool** as per the options provided.
7. Optionally, note down your **Goals** and/or sign with a second and possibly a third option in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Intake Interview And Quality Review Sheet Form (1)
* **Intake Interview And Quality Review Sheet Form**: A brief title to identify the form.

### Volunteer Info (2)
* **Volunteer Info**: Enter a brief description of the volunteer, including their name, role, or any other relevant details.

### Intake Interview Info (3)
* **Intake Interview Info**: A text field for recording any additional details from the interview.

### Review Sheet Info (4)
* **Review Sheet Info**: A date field to record the review date.

### Volunteer Signature (5)
* **Volunteer Signature**: A field where the volunteer can sign electronically with their email address.

### Volunteer Signature Date (6)
* **Volunteer Signature Date**: A date field for the volunteer's signature date.

### Assigned Tool (7)
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to the volunteer from the given options.

### Goals (8)
* **Goals**: Optionally, note down specific goals for the volunteer or project, if applicable.

### Volunteer Signature 2 (9)
*   **Volunteer Signature 2**: A second opportunity for the volunteer to sign, if required or desired.

### Volunteer Signature 3 (10)
*   **Volunteer Signature 3**: A third signature opportunity, typically for a secondary reviewer or confirmation, if applicable.
