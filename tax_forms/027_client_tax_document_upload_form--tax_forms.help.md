# Client Tax Document Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect necessary tax documents for tax preparation. Please provide all required documents for accurate and efficient tax filing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Upload all required tax documents.
3. Ensure you have filled out all necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Social Security Number** (`ssn`, text, required): Enter the last 4 digits of your Social Security Number for security purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Tax Year Being Prepared** (`tax_year`, number, required): Enter the year you are preparing taxes for.
* **Filing Status** (`filing_status`, select_one, required): Select your filing status:
	+ Single
	+ Married Filing Jointly
	+ Married Filing Separately
	+ Head of Household
	+ Qualifying Widow(er)
* **Spouse Name (if applicable)** (`spouse_name`, text, optional): If you are married, enter your spouse's name.
* **Spouse Social Security Number (if applicable)** (`spouse_ssn`, text, optional): If you are married, enter the last 4 digits of your spouse's Social Security Number.
* **Number of Dependents** (`dependents_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of dependents you have.
* **W2 Forms (if applicable)** (`w2_forms`, text, optional): Upload W2 forms from all employers.
* **Upload 1099 Forms (if applicable)** (`1099_forms`, text, optional): Upload 1099-NEC, 1099-INT, 1099-DIV, 1099-MISC, etc.
* **Business Income Documents (if applicable)** (`business_income`, text, optional): Upload profit and loss statements, schedule C, etc.
* **Rental Income Documents (if applicable)** (`rental_income`, text, optional): Upload schedule E documentation.
* **Investment Documents (if applicable)** (`investment_documents`, text, optional): Upload 1099-B, dividend statements, interest statements.
* **Mortgage Interest Statement (if applicable)** (`mortgage_interest`, text, optional): Upload Form 1098.
* **Property Tax Documents (if applicable)** (`property_tax`, text, optional): Upload property tax receipts or assessments.
* **Charitable Donation Records (if applicable)** (`charitable_donations`, text, optional): Upload receipts from charitable contributions.
* **Medical Expense Records (if applicable)** (`medical_expenses`, text, optional): Upload medical expense records if itemizing deductions.
* **Education Expense Documents (if applicable)** (`education_documents`, text, optional): Upload 1098-T forms or education costs.
* **Business Expense Documentation (if applicable)** (`business_expenses`, text, optional): Upload receipts, mileage logs, etc.
* **Estimated Tax Payment Records (if applicable)** (`estimated_tax_payments`, text, optional): Upload proof of quarterly payments.
* **Prior Year Tax Return (if applicable)** (`prior_year_return`, text, optional): Upload prior year tax return.
* **Additional Documents (if applicable)** (`additional_documents`, text, optional): Upload miscellaneous tax documents.

## Tips

* Ensure you have filled out all necessary fields and uploaded all required documents.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the tax professional preparing your return.
