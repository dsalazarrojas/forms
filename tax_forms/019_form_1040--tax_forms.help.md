# Form 1040 - Help Guide
## Purpose
Form 1040 is a tax return form used by individuals and businesses to report income and claim deductions. It helps taxpayers calculate their tax liability and claim eligible tax credits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete Form 1040, follow these steps:

1. Review your income and identify the income tax payable.
2. Claim any deductions you are eligible for.
3. Calculate your tax liability.
4. Report any payments and refunds made.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Income Taxable** (`income_taxable`, number, required: false): Enter the total amount of income taxable. This field is optional but is used to report income that is subject to tax.
* **Claim Deductions** (`claim_deductions`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all deductions you are eligible for. Options include:
	+ 1: Standard deductions
	+ 2: Itemized deductions
	+ 3: Other deductions
* **Calculate Tax** (`calculate_tax`, number, required: false): Enter the calculated tax liability. This field is automatically calculated based on your income and claimed deductions.
* **Payments and Refunds** (`payments_refunds`, select_one, required: false): Select one option:
	+ Refunds: Select this option to report refunds received.
	+ Payments: Select this option to report payments made.
* **Amount Owed** (`amount_owed`, number, required: false): Enter the total amount owed. This field is automatically calculated based on the tax liability and payments made.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your income and claim all eligible deductions to minimize your tax liability.
- Report all payments and refunds made to ensure accurate tax reporting.
- Double-check the calculated tax liability to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or need help, refer to the official IRS website or consult a tax professional.
