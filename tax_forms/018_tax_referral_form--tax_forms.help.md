# Tax Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Referral Form is a tool for individuals and tax firms to collect information about themselves and their referrals to a tax professional or firm.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Tax Referral Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the `first_name` field.
2. Enter your last name in the `last_name` field.
3. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
4. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
5. Enter the date of your tax return in the `tax_return_date` field.
6. Enter the name of the client you are referring in the `client_name` field.
7. Enter the name of the tax firm you are referring the client to in the `tax_firm_name` field.
8. Enter the name of the person or firm referring the client in the `referrer_name` field.
9. Enter the phone number of the person or firm referring the client in the `referrer_phone` field.
10. Enter the email of the person or firm referring the client in the `referrer_email` field.
11. Enter the name of the tax firm of the person or firm referring the client in the `referrer_tax_firm` field.
12. Enter a brief description of how the person or firm referring the client knew about the tax firm in the `how_did_you_know_us` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Tax return date** (`tax_return_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your tax return.
* **Client name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the client you are referring.
* **Tax firm name** (`tax_firm_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the tax firm you are referring the client to.
* **Referrer name** (`referrer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or firm referring the client.
* **Referrer phone** (`referrer_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person or firm referring the client.
* **Referrer email** (`referrer_email`, text, required): Enter the email of the person or firm referring the client.
* **Referrer tax firm** (`referrer_tax_firm`, text, required): Enter the name of the tax firm of the person or firm referring the client.
* **How did you know us?** (`how_did_you_know_us`, text, required): Enter a brief description of how the person or firm referring the client knew about the tax firm. 

Note: All fields are required for this form to be submitted.
