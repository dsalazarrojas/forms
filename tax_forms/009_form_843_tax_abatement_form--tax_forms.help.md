# Form 843 Tax Abatement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Form 843 Tax Abatement Form is a document used to request tax abatement for overpaid tax liabilities. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to increase the chances of your abatement request being approved.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, please ensure you have all the necessary information and supporting documents ready. The form consists of the following sections:

*   **Tax Abatement Form**: This is the main section of the form where you will enter your personal details.
*   **Submit this form**: Once you have filled out the form, click the "Submit" button to send it to the relevant authorities for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Tax Abatement Form (page 1)
*   **Tax Abatement Form (tax_abatement_form)**: This is the main section where you will enter your personal details, including your name, phone number (if applicable), email (if applicable), and tax year. Please fill out this section accurately and thoroughly.
*   **Claim Type (claim_type)**: Select the type of claim you are submitting:
    *   **Claim for refund**: If you are claiming for overpaid taxes.
    *   **Claim for Abatement**: If you are requesting abatement of taxes.
    *   **Claim for both**: If you are claiming both overpaid taxes and abatement.
*   **Amount Claimed (amount_claimed)**: Enter the exact amount you are claiming for abatement.
*   **Supporting Documents (supporting_documents)**: Attach any supporting documents relevant to your claim, such as receipts or invoices.

### Supporting Documents (page 2)
*   **Supporting Documents (supporting_documents)**: This is where you will attach any supporting documents related to your claim. Please ensure that all attached documents are legible and relevant to your claim.
*   **Phone Number (phone_number)**: This field is optional and only required if you want to include your phone number in the supporting documents section.
*   **Email (email)**: This field is optional and only required if you want to include your email in the supporting documents section.
*   **Tax Year (tax_year)**: This field is used to specify the tax year related to your claim. Please fill out this field accurately to ensure that your claim is processed correctly.

## Tips

*   Ensure that you have filled out the form accurately and thoroughly.
*   Make sure to attach all relevant supporting documents.
*   Double-check your claim details before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any part of the form, please contact your local tax authority for guidance.
