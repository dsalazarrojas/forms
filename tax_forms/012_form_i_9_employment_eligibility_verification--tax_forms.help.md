# Form I 9 Employment Eligibility Verification - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Form I 9 Employment Eligibility Verification is used to confirm an employee's or former employee's immigration status and employment eligibility. This form is typically completed by an employer or a representative to verify the employee's or former employee's employment eligibility under the Immigration and Nationality Act.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and documents required for completing the form.
2. Enter the employee's or former employee's name in the "name" field.
3. Enter the employee's or former employee's last name in the "last name" field.
4. Enter the employee's or former employee's address in the "address" field.
5. Enter the employee's or former employee's city in the "city" field.
6. Enter the employee's or former employee's state in the "state" field.
7. Enter the employee's or former employee's zip code in the "zip code" field.
8. Enter the date the employee was hired in the "date hired" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's or former employee's first name.
* **last name** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's or former employee's last name.
* **address** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's or former employee's full address.
* **city** (Text, Optional): Enter the city where the employee lives or last worked.
* **state** (Text, Optional): Enter the state where the employee lives or last worked.
* **zip code** (Text, Optional): Enter the zip code where the employee lives or last worked.
* **date hired** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the employee was first hired in the company. Please use the standard date format mm/dd/yyyy.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the form is completed correctly.
* If the employee has changed their address or other information since their last day of work, make sure to update the form accordingly.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, contact the relevant authorities or consult with a relevant expert.
