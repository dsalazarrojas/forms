# Tax Return Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant data for tax returns. It is intended for individuals and businesses to provide information for their respective tax returns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select "Individual Form Data" or "Business Form Data" based on your situation.
2. Fill in the "Tax Year" field with the relevant year.
3. Choose the type of tax return you are filing (if applicable).
4. Enter any additional comments or information in the "Tax Return Note" fields.
5. If applicable, enter your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Individual Form Data** (select_multiple): 
  Select this option if you are filing an individual tax return. Choose "Yes" or "No" based on your situation.
* **Business Form Data** (select_multiple): 
  Select this option if you are filing a business tax return. Choose "Yes" or "No" based on your situation.
* **Tax Year** (number): 
  Enter the tax year relevant to your tax return.
* **Tax Return Type** (select_one): 
  Select "Yes" or "No" based on your tax return type.
* **Business Tax Return Type** (select_one): 
  Select "Yes" or "No" based on your business tax return type.
* **Business Type** (select_one): 
  Select "Yes" or "No" based on your business type.
* **Tax Return Date** (date): 
  Enter the date of your tax return.
* **Tax Return Note** (note): 
  Enter any additional comments or information about your tax return.
* **Email** (email): 
  Enter your contact email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (text): 
  Enter your contact phone number (if applicable).
* **Tax Return Date** (date): 
  This field is a duplicate of "Tax Return Date", please enter the date of your tax return.
* **Tax Return Note** (note): 
  This field is for additional comments or information about your tax return.
* **Address** (text): 
  Enter your contact address (if applicable).
