# 2021 Tax Organizer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 2021 Tax Organizer Form is designed to collect necessary information for tax preparation. Please fill out this form to gather and submit your tax data accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Section 1: Taxpayer Information**
	* Enter your **Full name** (as shown on your Social Security card) in the "Full name" field.
	* Enter your **Social Security Number** in the "Social Security Number" field, in the format XXX-XX-XXXX.
	* Provide your **Primary email address** for correspondence about your return.
2. **Section 2: Dependents**
	* Enter the **Number of dependents** you claimed on your 2021 return.
	* List each dependent's **Name, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, and Relationship** on separate lines in the "Dependent details" field.
3. **Section 3: Income Summary**
	* Select the **Types of income** you received in 2021 from the list provided (W-2 wages, Self-employment income, Interest and dividends, etc.).
	* Enter the **Total wages (W-2)** for 2021 if applicable.
	* Enter your **Net self-employment income** (Schedule C) if self-employed.
4. **Section 4: Additional Information**
	* Choose your **Deduction preference** for 2021 (Standard deduction or Itemized deductions).
	* Enter your **Estimated tax payments** made for 2021.
5. **Section 5: Supporting Documents**
	* Indicate how you are providing supporting documents to the preparer (Yes, Electronic copies only, or No, to be provided later).
6. **Section 6: Feedback and Rating**
	* Select your satisfaction level with the 2021 Tax Organizer Form.
	* Rate your overall satisfaction with the form (1-10).
	* Provide any suggestions or improvements for the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your Social Security card.
* **Social Security Number**: Enter your Social Security Number in the format XXX-XX-XXXX.
* **Primary email address**: Provide your email address for correspondence about your return.
* **Primary phone number**: Enter your primary phone number, including the country code if outside the United States.
* **Filing status for 2021**: Select the status you used on your 2021 return (Single, Married filing jointly, Married filing separately, Head of household, or Qualifying widow(er)).
* **Spouse full name**: Enter your spouse's full name, if applicable.
* **Number of dependents**: Enter the total number of dependents you claimed on your 2021 return.
* **Dependent details**: List each dependent's details (Name, Date of Birth, Social Security Number, and Relationship) on separate lines.
* **Types of income**: Select all income types you received in 2021 (W-2 wages, Self-employment income, Interest and dividends, etc.).
* **Total wages (W-2)**: Enter combined wages from all W-2s in USD, if applicable.
* **Net self-employment income (Schedule C)**: Enter your net profit or loss from self-employment.
* **Deduction preference**: Choose whether you expect to take the standard deduction or itemize for 2021.
* **Estimated tax payments**: Enter the total of your federal estimated payments for 2021.
* **Supporting documents**: Indicate how you are providing supporting documents to the preparer.
* **Preparer or contact name**: Enter the name of the person preparing your return or primary contact.
* **Date signed / submitted**: Enter the date you signed this organizer or confirmed its accuracy.
* **Additional comments or notes**: Provide any other information the preparer should know.

# Tips

* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have all necessary supporting documents ready for the preparer.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your tax professional.
