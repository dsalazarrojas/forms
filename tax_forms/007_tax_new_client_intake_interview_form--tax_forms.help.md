# tax_new_client_intake_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a new client's intake interview, including their contact information, financial institutional data, tax firm involvement, and tax return details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your client's contact information, such as their name, phone number, and address.
3. Enter your client's financial institutional information, such as their business name and industry.
4. Describe your client's tax firm involvement, including any relevant details about their tax preparation.
5. Provide details about your client's tax returns, including the return type and filing status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **new_client_intake_form** (1): Enter a brief description of the new client's intake interview.
* **client_identification** (2): Provide your client's name, phone number, and address.
* **contact_information** (3): Enter your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **financial_institutional_info** (4): Enter your client's financial institutional information, such as their business name and industry.
* **tax_firm_involvement** (5): Describe your client's tax firm involvement, including any relevant details about their tax preparation.
* **tax_return** (6): Provide details about your client's tax return, including the return type and filing status.
* **tax_return_details** (7): Enter any additional details about your client's tax return.
* **tax_return_status** (8): Describe the status of your client's tax return, including any relevant deadlines or due dates.
* **tax_return_date** (9): Enter the date of your client's tax return.
* **tax_return_filing_status** (10): Enter the filing status of your client's tax return, such as "filed" or "not filed".

## Tips
* Please ensure that you understand the form's limitations and use it responsibly.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information provided, especially for sensitive topics like financial data.
* Use this form in conjunction with other relevant data and expert advice to make informed decisions.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please consult with a financial expert or tax professional.
