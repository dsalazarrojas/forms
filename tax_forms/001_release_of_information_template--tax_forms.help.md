# Release Of Information Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Release Of Information Template" is a form designed for clients to provide release of information on specific tax years. It helps ensure the accuracy and completeness of tax-related data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant tax year for which you are releasing information.
2. Fill in the name of your Limited Liability Company (LLC) as it appears on your tax return.
3. Review and complete the form according to your specific needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Release of information** (`release_of_information_template`, text, optional): This is the main field where you will provide the release of information.
* **Tax year** (`tax_year`, select_one, optional): Select the tax year for which you are releasing information. Please choose one of the provided options: 2020, 2021, or 2022.
* **LLC name** (`llc_name`, text, optional): Fill in the name of your Limited Liability Company (LLC) as it appears on your tax return.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately to avoid errors.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
