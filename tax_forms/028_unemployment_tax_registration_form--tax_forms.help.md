# Unemployment Tax Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Unemployment Tax Registration Form is used to register businesses or individuals with employees for unemployment tax purposes. It's a mandatory requirement for all businesses that have employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Employer Information** section with your business details.
2.  Provide your **Address**, if applicable.
3.  Indicate whether you have **Contact Information** and if it's **Yes** or **No**.
4.  Select **Tax Identification** and choose the relevant option.
5.  Enter your **Business Name** and **Business Address**, if applicable.
6.  Fill out your **Business Phone** and **Business Email**, if applicable.
7.  Provide your **Tax Agent** details, if applicable.
8.  Enter your **Tax Agent Phone** and **Tax Agent Email**, if applicable.
9.  Enter your **Business Fax**, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employer Information** (`employer_information`, text, required): This section requires you to enter your business name and contact details.
    Please provide the name of your business and any other relevant contact information.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, optional): If you have a physical address, please enter it here.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, select_one, optional): If you have a contact person or department for unemployment tax purposes, please select **Yes** or **No**.
*   **Tax Identification** (`tax_identification`, select_multiple, optional): Select the relevant tax identification option for your business. This field is optional but essential if you have the relevant information.
*   **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter your business name if it's not already included in the **Employer Information** section.
*   **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, optional): If you have a separate business address, please enter it here.
*   **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, optional): Enter your business phone number if you have one.
*   **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, optional): If you have a business email, please enter it here.
*   **Tax Agent** (`tax_agent`, text, optional): If you have a tax agent, please enter their name.
*   **Tax Agent Phone** (`tax_agent_phone`, text, optional): If your tax agent has a phone number, please enter it here.
*   **Tax Agent Email** (`tax_agent_email`, email, optional): If your tax agent has an email, please enter it here.
*   **Business Fax** (`business_fax`, text, optional): If you have a business fax number, please enter it here.
