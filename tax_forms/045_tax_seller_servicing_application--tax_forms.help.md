# Tax Seller Servicing Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Seller Servicing Application form is used to collect information from sellers about their tax history, debt concerns, and other tax-related details. This form helps us understand their tax status, which is essential for our internal processes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out. The following fields are marked as "required":
	* professional_history
	* tax_history
2. Answer each field according to your knowledge and understanding. 
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or assistance from your representative.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tax History** (`tax_history`, text, required): Please provide a detailed account of your tax history, including any previous tax experiences or concerns.
* **Insurance Tax** (`insurance_tax`, select_multiple, optional): Check 'Yes' if you have any insurance-related tax information.
* **Income Tax** (`income_tax`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you have any income tax information.
* **Online Purchases** (`online_purchases`, select_multiple, optional): Check 'Yes' if you have made any online purchases related to taxes.
* **Tax Documents** (`tax_documents`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' if you have any tax documents to provide.
* **Professional History** (`professional_history`, text, required): Please provide a brief account of your professional history, including your experience and qualifications.
* **Tax Debt Concerns** (`tax_debt_concerns`, select_multiple, optional): Check 'Yes' if you have any tax debt concerns.
