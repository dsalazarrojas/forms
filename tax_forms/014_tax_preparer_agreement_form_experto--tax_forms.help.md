# tax_preparer_agreement_form_experto - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and verify the understanding and agreement between a tax preparer and taxpayer.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill out the taxpayer information section with the correct details.
2. Fill out the preparer information section with the correct details.
3. Select the correct options for the agreement details section.
4. Sign and date the form to indicate your agreement and understanding of the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Taxpayer Name** (`taxpayer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the taxpayer.
- **Preparer Name** (`preparer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the tax preparer.
- **Preparer Taxpayer Agreement** (`preparer_taxpayer_agreement`, select one, required): Select the correct agreement option from the provided list.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of agreement.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of agreement (if applicable).
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the tax preparer (if applicable).
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the tax preparer (if applicable).
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the tax preparer (if applicable).
- **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of signature.
- **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of signature.
- **Taxpayer Signature** (`taxpayer_signature`, note, required): The taxpayer must sign to indicate their agreement and understanding of the terms.
- **Preparer Signature** (`preparer_signature`, note, required): The tax preparer must sign to indicate their agreement and understanding of the terms.
- **Preparer Firm** (`preparer_firm`, text, optional): Enter the name of the tax preparer firm (if applicable).
- **Taxpayer Address** (`taxpayer_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the taxpayer (if applicable).
- **Preparer Agreement Version** (`preparer_agreement_version`, select one, optional): Select the version of the agreement.
- **Taxpayer Agreement Version** (`taxpayer_agreement_version`, select one, optional): Select the version of the agreement.
- **Preparer Issuer** (`preparer_issuer`, select one, required): Select 'Yes' if the tax preparer agrees to be bound by the agreement.
- **Taxpayer Issuer** (`taxpayer_issuer`, select one, required): Select 'Yes' if the taxpayer agrees to be bound by the agreement.
- **Version** (`preparer_agreement_version`, select one, optional): Select the version of the agreement.
- **Version** (`taxpayer_agreement_version`, select one, optional): Select the version of the agreement.

## Tips
1. Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2. Ensure both the tax preparer and taxpayer sign the form to indicate their agreement and understanding of the terms.
3. If any fields are not applicable, do not fill them out.
4. Review the form for completeness before signing.
