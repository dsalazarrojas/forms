# Tax Preparation Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tax Preparation Information Form is a collection of fields that gather essential information about your tax-related activities. Please fill out this form to provide your tax preparation details.

## How to Complete This Form

1. Fill in each section with the required information.
2. For "Company Information", enter a brief description of your company.
3. In "Company Address", type your business address.
4. "Tax Year" is where you select the relevant tax year for this preparation.
5. "Tax Return" and "Employee Names" are related. If you're preparing a tax return, select "Yes" for "Tax Return" and list the employee names in "Employee Names".
6. If you're preparing a payment, select "Yes" for "Payment Information" and fill in the related fields (though this field is not explicitly linked to any other field).
7. "Tax Filing Status" should reflect your current tax filing status (Active or Inactive).
8. "Tax Filing Deadline" is the date by which you expect to file your tax return.
9. In "Note", you can add any additional comments or information that might be helpful.
10. Finally, provide your email address in "Email" and your phone number in "Phone".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tax Information**: Enter your tax-related information here.
* **Company Information**: Enter a brief description of your company.
* **Company Address**: Type your business address.
* **Tax Year**: Select the relevant tax year for this preparation.
* **Tax Return**: If you're preparing a tax return, select "Yes".
* **Employee Names**: If you're preparing a tax return, list the employee names here.
* **Payment Information**: If you're preparing a payment, select "Yes".
* **Tax Filing Status**: Select your current tax filing status (Active or Inactive).
* **Tax Filing Deadline**: The date by which you expect to file your tax return.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or information that might be helpful.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Type your phone number.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for help!
