# Tax Return Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Return Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information from taxpayers about their tax status and related details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing accurate and truthful information.
2. Ensure that you have a valid reason for completing this form and that the information provided is not already on file.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tax Return Questionnaire Form** (1, text, required: false): This is the form title and header.
* **Taxpayer Identification** (2, text, required: false): Enter your taxpayer identification number or other relevant tax identification details.
* **Tax Year** (3, number, required: false): Specify the tax year for which you are filing.
* **Tax Deductions** (4, select_multiple, required: false): Answer "Yes" or "No" if you claim tax deductions for the specified year.
* **Tax Lieu** (5, text, required: false): Enter any tax lieu details.
* **Taxpayer Address** (6, text, required: false): Enter your taxpayer address.
* **Tax Filing Status** (7, select_one, required: false): Select your current tax filing status (Active or Inactive).
* **Dependents** (8, number, required: false): Specify the number of dependents you have.
* **Total Income** (9, number, required: false): Enter your total income for the tax year.
* **Self-Employment Income** (10, number, required: false): Enter any self-employment income for the tax year.
* **Tax Liability** (11, number, required: false): Enter your tax liability for the tax year.
* **Payment Method** (12, select_one, required: false): Select your payment method (Yes or No).
