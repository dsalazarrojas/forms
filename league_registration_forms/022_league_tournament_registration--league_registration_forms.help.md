# League Tournament Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The League Tournament Registration form is used to collect team information for a league tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
2. Provide your team's Tournament Name.
3. Enter the Start Date and End Date of the tournament.
4. Enter your team's address and team number (if applicable).
5. Fill in your Phone number (if applicable).
6. Click Submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`user_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`user_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Tournament Name** (`tournament_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your team.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the tournament.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the tournament.
* **Tournament Address** (`tournament_address`, text, required): Enter your team's address.
* **Team Number** (`team_number`, number, required): Enter your team's number.
* **Email** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields to ensure proper registration.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact the league administrators.
- This form is intended for one-time use per team. If you want to register multiple teams, you must fill out the form for each team separately.
