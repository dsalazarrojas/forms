# Football League Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to register for the football league. It will ask for some basic contact and demographic information to help with communication and organization of the league.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Name on the first page.
2. Enter your Email on the second page.
3. Enter your Phone on the third page.
4. Fill in your Date of Birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY on the fourth page.
5. Enter your Time of Registration on the fifth page.
6. Write any additional Notes on the sixth page, if you want to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Text)**: Enter your full name as you would like to be known in the league.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your valid email address where you can be reached by us.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you, if needed.
* **Date of Birth (Date)**: Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Time of Registration (Time)**: Enter the time you are registering for the league.
* **Note (Note)**: If you have any additional information or comments, you can add them here.
