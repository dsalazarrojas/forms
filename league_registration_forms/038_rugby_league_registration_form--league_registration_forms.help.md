# Rugby League Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for players to register for the Rugby League.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Player Info" page with your details.
2. On the "Team" page, select whether your team is interested in registering for the league.
3. On the "Emergency Contact" page, provide your emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Info (Player Info, text, required: false)**: Provide your player information, such as your name and contact details.
* **Team (Team, select multiple, required: true)**: Select whether your team is interested in registering for the league. If "Yes" is selected, the team will be included in the league registration.
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact, text, required: false)**: Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person. This is the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.

## Tips
* Please make sure to select "Yes" on the "Team" page if you want your team to be included in the league registration.
* If you are not sure about any of the information, please contact the league administrators for clarification.
