# Player Trading Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Trading Form is used to document the details of a player trade between two teams in a sports league. This form captures essential information about the trade, including the players involved, their status, and the terms of the trade.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Player Trading Form** field with a descriptive title for the trade.
2. Enter the **Team Name** of the team trading away the player(s).
3. Enter the **Player Name** of the player being traded.
4. Select the **Position** of the player being traded (e.g., "Yes" for active or "No" for inactive).
5. Enter the **Value** of the trade (e.g., a monetary amount or other relevant value).
6. Enter the **Player Value** of the player being traded (e.g., a monetary amount or other relevant value).
7. Select the **Trade Date** and **Trade Time** of the trade.
8. Enter the **Player Note** field for any additional comments about the player being traded.
9. Enter the **Team Email** and **Player Email** fields for contact information.
10. Enter the **Team Phone** and **Player Phone** fields for phone numbers.
11. Select the teams involved in the trade using the **Team** and **Player** fields (e.g., "Yes" for active or "No" for inactive).
12. Select the **Trade Status** as "Active" or "Inactive".
13. Select the type of trade using the **Trade Type** field (e.g., "Yes" for valid or "No" for invalid).
14. Enter the **Trade Details** for any additional comments about the trade.
15. Enter any additional comments about the trade using the **Trade Notes** field.
16. Enter the names and labels of the teams involved in the trade using the **Team 1**, **Team 2**, **Player 1**, and **Player 2** fields.
17. Enter the names of the teams involved in the trade using the **Team Name 1** and **Team Name 2** fields.
18. Enter the names of the players involved in the trade using the **Player Name 1** and **Player Name 2** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Trading Form**: The form title.
* **Team Name**: The name of the team trading away the player(s).
* **Player Name**: The name of the player being traded.
* **Position**: The status of the player being traded (e.g., active or inactive).
* **Value**: The monetary amount or other relevant value of the trade.
* **Player Value**: The monetary amount or other relevant value of the player being traded.
* **Trade Date**: The date of the trade.
* **Trade Time**: The time of the trade.
* **Player Note**: Additional comments about the player being traded.
* **Team Email**: The email address of the team involved in the trade.
* **Player Email**: The email address of the player involved in the trade.
* **Team Phone**: The phone number of the team involved in the trade.
* **Player Phone**: The phone number of the player involved in the trade.
* **Team**: Select the teams involved in the trade (e.g., "Yes" for active or "No" for inactive).
* **Player**: Select the players involved in the trade (e.g., "Yes" for active or "No" for inactive).
* **Trade Status**: The status of the trade (e.g., "Active" or "Inactive").
* **Trade Type**: The type of trade (e.g., "Yes" for valid or "No" for invalid).
* **Trade Details**: Additional comments about the trade.
* **Trade Notes**: Additional comments about the trade.
* **Team 1**, **Team 2**, **Player 1**, **Player 2**: Enter the names of the teams and players involved in the trade.
* **Team Name 1**, **Team Name 2**, **Player Name 1**, **Player Name 2**: Enter the names of the teams and players involved in the trade.
