# youth_basketball_league_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The youth_basketball_league_registration_form is a registration form for players to join a youth basketball league. It collects essential information from players and their emergency contacts to ensure their safety and well-being during the league.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the player's name, email, phone number, and birth date.
2. Select the preferred team for the player.
3. Enter the emergency contact's name and phone number.
4. Provide any medical information, such as allergies or medical conditions.
5. Indicate the relationship between the emergency contact and the player.
6. Choose the player's grade and height.
7. Enter the player's weight and date of birth.
8. Provide any additional medical information, such as medical notes.
9. Select the team for the player.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the player.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the player's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the player's phone number.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the parent or guardian's name.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): Enter the player's birth date.
* **Team Preferred** (`team_preferred`, select_one, optional): Select the preferred team for the player.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, text, optional): Enter any medical information about the player.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required): Select the relationship between the emergency contact and the player.
* **Player Grade** (`player_grade`, select_one, optional): Select the player's grade.
* **Height** (`player_height`, number, required): Enter the player's height.
* **Weight** (`player_weight`, number, required): Enter the player's weight.
* **Date of Birth** (`player_dob`, date, optional): Enter the player's date of birth.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional medical notes about the player.
* **Team** (`team`, select_one, required): Select the team for the player.
* **Jersey Number** (`jersey_number`, number, optional): Enter the player's jersey number.
* **Weight Unit** (`player_weight_unit`, text, optional): Enter the weight unit (e.g., kg or lbs).
* **Additional Medical Info** (`medical_info_additional`, text, optional): Enter any additional medical information about the player.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
* If you have any questions, feel free to contact us.
* If you need help with filling out the form, do not hesitate to ask.
* The form will be reviewed and verified before the player can participate in the league.
