# League Branding Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participation and permission information from league participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out your name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "participant_email" field.
3. Check the boxes for the permissions you are granting:
	* "League Branding Permission" to use your team's branding in official league materials.
	* "Participant Image Permission" to use your image in official league materials.
	* "Participant Likenesses Permission" to use your likeness in official league materials.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Additional Notes" field (optional).
5. If required, sign and date the form using the "Participant Signature" and "Date Signed" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name (Participant Name, text, required)
* This is the field where you will enter your name as a participant in the league.

### Participant Email (participant_email, email, required)
* This is the field where you will enter your email address.

### League Branding Permission (League Branding Permission, select_one, optional)
* Check this box to grant permission for the league to use your team's branding in official league materials.

### Participant Image Permission (Participant Image Permission, select_one, optional)
* Check this box to grant permission for the league to use your image in official league materials.

### Participant Likenesses Permission (Participant Likenesses Permission, select_one, optional)
* Check this box to grant permission for the league to use your likeness in official league materials.

### Additional Notes (notes, note, optional)
* This is the field where you can provide any additional notes or comments about your participation.

### Participant Signature (Participant Signature, date, optional)
* If required, sign your name to confirm your participation and agreement to the permissions granted.

### Date Signed (Date Signed, date, optional)
* If required, enter the date you signed this form.

Note: Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
