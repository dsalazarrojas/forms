# Slinger Standing Qualifier Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Slinger Standing Qualifier Application is a form designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in the Slinger Standing Qualifier event. This form is essential for applicants to provide accurate and truthful information to avoid rejection of their application.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the corresponding fields.
2.  Enter your Phone number for contact purposes.
3.  Provide your experience related to the event in the Experience field.
4.  Indicate your availability for the event in the Availability field.
5.  Describe your event experience in the Event Experience field.
6.  Fill in the Emergency Contact Name, Emergency Phone number, and Emergency Email address.
7.  Select the consent options to confirm that you understand the implications of providing false information and that you consent to share your information with the event organizers.
8.  Enter your Date of Birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication purposes.
*   **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Experience** (text, optional): Describe your experience related to the event.
*   **Availability** (text, optional): Indicate your availability for the event.
*   **Event Experience** (text, optional): Describe your experience related to the event.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact person.
*   **Consent** (select multiple, optional): Select the options to confirm that you understand the implications of providing false information and that you consent to share your information with the event organizers.
*   **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you fill in all the required fields accurately and completely.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the relevant authorities.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any potential issues.
*   Be truthful and accurate when providing information to avoid rejection of your application.
