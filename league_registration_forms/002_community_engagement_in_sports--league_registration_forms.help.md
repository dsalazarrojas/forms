# Community Engagement In Sports - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential participants in community sports activities.

## How To Complete This Form

  To complete this form, please follow these steps:

  1. Fill out your full name and age group in the first two fields.
  2. Select all the sports activities you are interested in participating in.
  3. Choose your current activity level and availability.
  4. Indicate if you are interested in volunteering and select your preferred volunteer role if applicable.
  5. If you have coaching experience, indicate it in the field provided.
  5. Provide any feedback you have on local sports facilities.
  6. Enter your contact email to receive updates about community sports events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Age Group**: Select your current age range from the provided options.
* **Interested Sports Activities**: Select all the sports activities you are interested in participating in.
* **Current Activity Level**: Choose how often you participate in sports activities from the options provided.
* **General Availability**: Select all the times you are most likely to participate in sports activities.
* **Are you interested in volunteering?**: Indicate if you are interested in helping with events, coaching, or logistics.
* **Preferred Volunteer Role**: If you selected "Yes" or "Maybe" for the previous field, select your preferred volunteer role.
* **Do you have previous coaching experience?**: Check if you have any experience as a coach.
* **Feedback on Local Sports Facilities**: Provide any comments or suggestions you have about local sports facilities.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address to receive updates about community sports events.

## Tips

  * Please ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
  * Select all relevant options for the fields where multiple selections are allowed.
  * If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, don't hesitate to ask.
