# Hockey Ticket Draw Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hockey Ticket Draw Registration form is designed for users to register themselves and their team for the hockey ticket draw. It's a simple form that collects essential information to ensure that users are eligible for the draw.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`): Enter your name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for updates and confirmation of your registration.
* **Phone** (`Phone`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information.
* **Team Name** (`Team Name`): Enter the name of the team you are registering for.
* **Number of Players** (`Number of Players`): Enter the number of players in your team.
* **First Name** (`First Name`): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Age** (`Age`): Enter your age in years, if applicable. Please note that the minimum age requirement for this draw is usually 18 years.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check all information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us for assistance.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact us for clarification.
