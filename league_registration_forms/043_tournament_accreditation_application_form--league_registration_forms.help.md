# Tournament Accreditation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tournament Accreditation Application Form is a comprehensive form used by administrators or staff to manage and track tournament registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the tournament details in the first section, including the category selection and dates and times.
2. Provide contact information in the second section, including address, phone, email, and website.
3. Review and submit the form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Tournament Details
* **Tournament Category** (`Select a category for this tournament`): Select one of the four available categories (A, B, C, D) to categorize your tournament.
* **Tournament Dates** (`Dates`): Enter the start and end dates of the tournament in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Tournament Times** (`Times`): Enter the start and end times of the tournament in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Information** (`Contact`): Enter any additional contact information or notes for the tournament.
* **Email** (`Email`): Enter the email address associated with the tournament.
* **Phone** (`Phone`): Enter the phone number associated with the tournament.
* **Website** (`Website`): Enter the website associated with the tournament.

### Accreditation Status
* **Accreditation Status** (`Accreditation Status`): Select the accreditation status of your tournament (Pending, Approved, Denied).

### Submission Information
* **Submission Date** (`Submission Date`): This field is automatically populated with the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Submission Time** (`Submission Time`): This field is automatically populated with the time the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`): Select who submitted the form (Admin, Staff, User).
* **Submitted By Name** (`Submitted By Name`): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted By Email** (`Submitted By Email`): Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted By Phone** (`Submitted By Phone`): Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted By Website** (`Submitted By Website`): Enter the website of the person who submitted the form.

### Additional Information
* **Additional Info** (`Additional Info`): Enter any additional information about the tournament.

### Timestamps
* **Created At** (`Created At`): This field is automatically populated with the date and time the form was created.
* **Updated At** (`Updated At`): This field is automatically populated with the date and time the form was last updated.
* **Submitted At** (`Submitted At`): This field is automatically populated with the date and time the form was submitted.
