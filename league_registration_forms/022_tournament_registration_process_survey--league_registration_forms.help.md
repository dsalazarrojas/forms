# Tournament Registration Process Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and input from participants on their experience with the tournament registration process. This form aims to identify areas of improvement and provide valuable insights for future tournament registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Participant Feedback" page and choose whether you had a positive or negative experience during the registration process. This will help us understand your perspective on how we can improve.
2. On the "Registration Experience" page, select how you would rate your overall experience with the registration process. This will give us an idea of whether the process met your expectations.
3. If you encountered any issues during the registration process, select all that apply from the "Issues Encountered" page. This will help us identify common problems and work on solving them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Feedback
 Participant Feedback: **Yes/No**
	* Indicates whether you had a positive or negative experience during the registration process.
	* This will help us understand your perspective on how we can improve.

### Registration Experience
 Registration Experience: **Poor/Neutral/Good/Excellent**
	* Rates your overall experience with the registration process.
	* This will give us an idea of whether the process met your expectations.

### Issues Encountered
 Issues Encountered: **Yes/No**
	* Select all that apply if you encountered any issues during the registration process.
	* This will help us identify common problems and work on solving them.
