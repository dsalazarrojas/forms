# Sports League Expansion Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for applying to a sports league expansion. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your application is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the following sections: Organization Name, Division Name, Expansion History, and Team Formation.
2. If you are applying for a new division, choose the correct Division Location from the list.
3. If no teams already exist in the new division, choose all applicable areas for Division Location.
4. Enter the Number of Teams you plan to have in the new division.
5. If applicable, provide the Expansion Planned Date and Expansion Planned Time.
6. If you are applying for a team, enter the Team League ID and Team League Name of the existing team(s) you are merging with or expanding.
7. Choose the Expansion Reason for your application.
8. If necessary, enter Additional Comments to provide further information about your application.
9. Finally, enter your name and the Submission Date of your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name**: The name of your organization or entity applying for the expansion.
* **Division Name**: The name of the new division you are applying for.
* **Expansion History**: Briefly describe your league's expansion history.
* **Team Formation**: Describe the formation of your team or teams in the new division.
* **Division Location**: If you are applying for a new division, choose the correct location from the list. If no teams already exist in the new division, choose all applicable areas.
* **Division Location (if no team already exists, choose all applicable areas)**: If no teams already exist in the new division, enter all locations applicable to the new division.
* **Number of Teams**: The number of teams you plan to have in the new division.
* **Expansion Planned Date**: The date you plan to have the expansion, if applicable.
* **Expansion Planned Time**: The time you plan to have the expansion, if applicable.
* **Team League ID**: The ID of the existing team(s) you are merging with or expanding.
* **Team League Name**: The name of the existing team(s) you are merging with or expanding.
* **Team League Location**: The location of the existing team(s) you are merging with or expanding.
* **Expansion Reason**: Choose the reason for your expansion application (Financial, Market demand, or Other).
* **Additional Comments**: Any further comments or information you want to provide about your application.
* **Submitted By**: Your name and title.
* **Submission Date**: The date you are submitting your application.

## Tips
Please fill out the form completely and accurately. If you have any questions or need further information, contact the league administrators.
