# Practice Roster Attestation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information to validate the details of a practice session, ensuring all parties involved are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Complete Your Section
Each player, coach, and team member should complete their corresponding section of the form. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in each field.

### Tips

* Ensure that you are only completing the form for your own section.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask your team administrator or the relevant person for clarification.
* This form is not for sharing or distributing information outside of the intended recipients.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your team administrator or the form support team at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title (Page 1)**: Enter a title for this practice session that will be used for identification purposes.
* **Player First Name** (`player_first_name`): Enter your first name as a player.
* **Player Last Name** (`player_last_name`): Enter your last name as a player.
* **Player Email** (`player_email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`): Enter the name of the team you are part of.
* **Team Contact Details** (`team_contact_details`): Enter the contact details for your team.
* **Team Email** (`team_email`): Enter the email address of your team.
* **Practice Date** (`practice_date`): Enter the date of the practice session.
* **Practice Time** (`practice_time`): Enter the time of the practice session.
* **Practice Location** (`practice_location`): Enter the location of the practice session.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`): Enter the name of your coach.
* **Roster Attestation** (`roster_attestation`): Confirm that the roster is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`): Select the individuals or groups that have signed this form.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Provide a physical or digital signature to confirm your attestation.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Attestation ID** (`attestation_id`): Enter an ID for this attestation.
* **Team Signature** (`team_signature`): Provide a physical or digital signature to confirm the team's attestation.
* **Practice ID** (`practice_id`): Enter an ID for this practice session.
* **Date of Practice** (`date_practice`): Enter the date of the practice session.
* **Practice Status** (`practice_status`): Select the current status of the practice session.
* **Practice Comments** (`practice_comments`): Enter any comments or feedback about the practice session.
* **Player Comments** (`player_comments`): Enter any comments or feedback as a player.
* **Coach Comments** (`coach_comments`): Enter any comments or feedback as a coach.
* **Team Comments** (`team_comments`): Enter any comments or feedback from the team.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`): Enter the person or group that submitted this form.
