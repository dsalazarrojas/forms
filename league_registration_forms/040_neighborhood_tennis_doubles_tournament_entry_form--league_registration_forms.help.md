# Neighborhood Tennis Doubles Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neighborhood Tennis Doubles Tournament Entry Form is a form to register a team for the neighborhood tennis doubles tournament. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields in the form.
2. Ensure that the team member information is accurate and up-to-date.
3. Fill out the preferences and agreements sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name**: Team Name (`team_name`, text, required)
	+ Enter the name of your team.
* **Player 1 Name**: Player 1 Name (`team_member_1`, text, required)
	+ Enter the name of the first player on your team.
* **Player 1 Phone**: Player 1 Phone (`team_member_1_phone`, text, optional)
	+ Enter a phone number for Player 1.
* **Player 1 Email**: Player 1 Email (`team_member_1_email`, email, optional)
	+ Enter a valid email for Player 1.
* **Player 1 Experience**: Player 1 Experience (`team_member_1_experience`, select, optional)
	+ Select Player 1's level of experience as Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Player 2 Name**: Player 2 Name (`team_member_2`, text, required)
	+ Enter the name of the second player on your team.
* **Player 2 Experience**: Player 2 Experience (`team_member_2_experience`, select, required)
	+ Select Player 2's level of experience as Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Player 2 Phone**: Player 2 Phone (`team_member_2_phone`, text, required)
	+ Enter a phone number for Player 2.
* **Player 2 Email**: Player 2 Email (`team_member_2_email`, email, optional)
	+ Enter a valid email for Player 2.
* **Match Preference**: Match Preference (`team_member_1_match_preference`, select, optional)
	+ Select the preferred time of day for the match as Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Team Member 2 Match Preference**: Team Member 2 Match Preference (`team_member_2_match_preference`, select, optional)
	+ Select the preferred time of day for the match as Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Match Date**: Match Date (`team_member_1_match_date`, date, required)
	+ Enter the date of the match.
* **Team Member 2 Match Date**: Team Member 2 Match Date (`team_member_2_match_date`, date, required)
	+ Enter the date of the match.
* **Match Time**: Match Time (`team_member_1_match_time`, time, required)
	+ Enter the time of the match in 24-hour format (e.g., 13:00 for 1 pm).
* **Team Member 2 Match Time**: Team Member 2 Match Time (`team_member_2_match_time`, time, required)
	+ Enter the time of the match in 24-hour format (e.g., 13:00 for 1 pm).
* **Notes**: Notes (`team_member_1_notes`, note, optional)
	+ Enter any additional information or notes for Player 1.
* **Team Member 2 Notes**: Team Member 2 Notes (`team_member_2_notes`, note, optional)
	+ Enter any additional information or notes for Player 2.
* **Agreement**: Agreement (`team_member_1_agreement`, text, optional)
	+ Agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Team Member 2 Agreement**: Team Member 2 Agreement (`team_member_2_agreement`, text, optional)
	+ Agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Team Venue**: Team Venue (`team_member_1_venue`, select, required)
	+ Select the venue of the match as Tennis Club or Home Court.
* **Team Member 2 Venue**: Team Member 2 Venue (`team_member_2_venue`, select, required)
	+ Select the venue of the match as Tennis Club or Home Court.
* **Team Score**: Team Score (`team_member_1_score`, number, required)
	+ Enter the predicted score of the match.
* **Team Member 2 Score**: Team Member 2 Score (`team_member_2_score`, number, required)
	+ Enter the predicted score of the match.
* **Team ELO**: Team ELO (`team_member_1_elo`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the ELO rating of Player 1.
* **Team Member 2 ELO**: Team Member 2 ELO (`team_member_2_elo`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the ELO rating of Player 2.
* **Team UCLA**: Team UCLA (`team_member_1_ucla`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the UCLA rating of Player 1.
* **Team Member 2 UCLA**: Team Member 2 UCLA (`team_member_2_ucla`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the UCLA rating of Player 2.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If a field is optional, fill it out if it is relevant to your team.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the tournament organizers for assistance.
