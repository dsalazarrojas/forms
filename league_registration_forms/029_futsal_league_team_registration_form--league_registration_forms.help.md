# Futsal League Team Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Futsal League Team Registration Form is designed to collect information from teams participating in the Futsal League sports competition. Completing this form ensures that your team is registered and eligible for the competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Team Name field with your team's name.
2. Enter your team's email address in the Team Email field.
3. Enter your team's league name in the Team League Name field.
4. Provide the name of the contact person responsible for your team in the Contact Person field.
5. Enter the email of the contact person in the Contact Email field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter your team's name as it will be displayed in the competition.
* **Team Email** (`team_email`, email, optional): Enter your team's email address for communication purposes.
* **Team League Name** (`team_league_name`, text, optional): Enter your team's league name.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Provide the name of the person responsible for your team.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, optional): Enter the email of the contact person.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any).
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
