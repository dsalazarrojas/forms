# BlueSnap Church Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The BlueSnap Church Donation Form is a tool for making secure and easy online donations to the church. This form allows donors to support the church's various initiatives and programs by specifying their donation amount, type, and frequency. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in your full name in the "Full Name" field.
- Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
- Choose your donation amount from the provided options or enter a custom amount in the "Custom Amount" field.
- Select your donation type from the options provided.
- Indicate whether you want to set up a recurring donation.
- Choose the frequency of your recurring donation.
- Specify how you want to be recognized for your donation.
- If you have any prayer intentions, add them in the "Prayer Request (optional)" field.
- Decide if you need a tax receipt.
- Choose your payment method.
- Choose whether you want to subscribe to the church newsletter.
- Finally, select the date of your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (required): This field is used to enter your name as you want to be recognized by the church.
* **Email Address** (required): This field is used to contact you for any updates or to send you a tax receipt.
* **Phone Number** (optional): This field is optional and allows you to provide a phone number in case the church needs to contact you.
* **Mailing Address** (optional): This field is optional and allows you to provide your address for the purpose of sending you a tax receipt or other mailings.
* **Church Member Status** (required): This field helps the church understand your relationship with the church and how to process your donation.
* **Donation Amount** (required): This field allows you to specify the amount of your donation. You can choose from the provided options or select "Custom Amount" to enter your own amount.
* **Custom Amount** (optional): This field is used to enter a custom donation amount if you did not select one of the provided options.
* **Donation Type** (required): This field helps the church understand the purpose of your donation.
* **Set up recurring donation?** (required): This field allows you to specify whether you want to set up a recurring donation.
* **Recurring Frequency** (optional): This field is used to specify the frequency of your recurring donation.
* **How should we recognize your donation?** (required): This field allows you to decide how you want to be recognized for your donation.
* **Prayer Request (optional)**: This field is optional and allows you to specify any prayer intentions.
* **Tax receipt needed?** (required): This field allows you to decide if you need a tax receipt for your donation.
* **Payment Method** (required): This field allows you to specify how you want to make your donation.
* **Subscribe to church newsletter?** (optional): This field allows you to decide if you want to subscribe to the church newsletter.
* **Donation Date** (required): This field is used to specify the date of your donation.
