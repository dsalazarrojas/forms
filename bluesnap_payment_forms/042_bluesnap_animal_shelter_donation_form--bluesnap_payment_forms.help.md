# BlueSnap Animal Shelter Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The BlueSnap Animal Shelter Donation Form is designed to collect donations and support various aspects of the shelter's operations. Your contribution will help make a difference in the lives of animals in need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of this form to send your completed application.
2. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
3. Select the desired donation amount and frequency from the provided options.
4. Choose where you would like your donation to be allocated.
5. Confirm your donation details and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Full Name (<name>, <type: text>, required: true)
* This field collects your full name for recognition and communication purposes.

### Email Address (<name: donor_email, <type: email, required: true>)
* Enter your valid email address to receive updates and newsletters from the BlueSnap Animal Shelter.

### Phone Number (<name: donor_phone, <type: text, required: false>)
* Please enter your phone number for communication, but this field is optional.

### Mailing Address (<name: donor_address, <type: text, required: false>)
* Provide your mailing address for any further communication or recognition.

### Donation Amount (<name: donation_amount, <type: select_one, required: true)
* Select from the options to donate a fixed amount or choose "Custom amount" to specify a different amount.

### Custom Donation Amount (<name: custom_donation_amount, <type: number, required: false>)
* If you selected "Custom amount", enter the exact amount you wish to donate.

### Donation Type (<name: donation_frequency, <type: select_one, required: true)
* Choose how frequently you would like to make this donation: one-time, monthly recurring, quarterly, or annually.

### Where Would You Like Your Donation to Go? (<name: donation_focus, <type: select_one, required: false>)
* Select how you would like your donation to be allocated within the shelter's operations: medical care, food and supplies, shelter operations, adoption programs, or no preference.

### Is This a Corporate Donation? (<name: is_corporate_donor, <type: select_one, required: true)
* Indicate if this is a donation made on behalf of a company or organization.

### Company Name (<name: company_name, <type: text, required: false>)
* If this is a corporate donation, enter the name of the company.

### Need Tax Deduction Documentation? (<name: tax_deductible, <type: select_one, required: true)
* Choose if you would like to claim a tax deduction for your donation.

### Recognition Preference (<name: recognition_preference, <type: select_one, required: true)
* Select how you would like to be recognized for your donation: public recognition with name, public recognition without name, or no public recognition at all.

### Subscribe to Our Newsletter? (<name: newsletter_signup, <type: select_one, required: false>)
* Choose if you would like to receive regular updates and newsletters from the BlueSnap Animal Shelter.

### Payment Method (<name: payment_method, <type: select_one, required: true)
* Select how you would like to pay for your donation: credit card, debit card, or PayPal.

### Donation Date (<name: donation_date, <type: date, required: true)
* Enter the date you are making this donation.

### Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled in accurately to avoid any issues with your donation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the BlueSnap Animal Shelter directly.
* Thank you for your support!
