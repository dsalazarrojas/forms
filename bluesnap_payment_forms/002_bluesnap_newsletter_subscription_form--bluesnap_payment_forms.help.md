## BlueSnap Newsletter Subscription Form - Help Guide

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, **Required**): Enter the email address where you would like to receive the newsletter. We will send the newsletter to this address.
* **Subscription Format** (`subscription_format`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose how you would like to receive the newsletter:
    + **Digital Only**: Receive the newsletter as a digital copy only.
    + **Print Only**: Receive the newsletter as a physical print copy only.
    + **Both Digital and Print**: Receive both digital and print copies of the newsletter.
* **Subscription Duration** (`subscription_duration`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose how long you would like to subscribe for:
    + **1 Month**: Subscribe for one month.
    + **3 Months**: Subscribe for three months.
    + **6 Months**: Subscribe for six months.
    + **1 Year**: Subscribe for one year.
    + **2 Years**: Subscribe for two years.
    + **Ongoing**: Subscribe indefinitely.
* **Areas of Interest** (`interests`, `select_multiple`, **Optional**): Select topics you are most interested in:
    + **Business News**: Select this option if you are interested in business news.
    + **Technology**: Select this option if you are interested in technology.
    + **Health and Wellness**: Select this option if you are interested in health and wellness.
    + **Finance**: Select this option if you are interested in finance.
    + **Lifestyle**: Select this option if you are interested in lifestyle.
    + **Entertainment**: Select this option if you are interested in entertainment.
    + **Sports**: Select this option if you are interested in sports.
    + **Politics**: Select this option if you are interested in politics.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, **Optional**): If you are a business subscriber, enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, **Optional**): If you are a business subscriber, enter your job title.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, **Optional**): If you would like to be contacted, enter your phone number. This is optional for account verification.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, **Required for Print Subscriptions**): Enter your street address for print subscriptions.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter your city.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter your state or province.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (`country`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Select your country:
    + **United States**
    + **Canada**
    + **United Kingdom**
    + **Australia**
    + **Other**
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Help us understand how you found us:
    + **Social Media**
    + **Search Engine**
    + **Friend or Family**
    + **Advertisement**
    + **Email Campaign**
    + **Other**
* **Marketing Communications** (`marketing_consent`, `select_one`, **Required**): May we send you occasional promotional offers?
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select your payment method:
    + **Credit Card**
    + **Debit Card**
    + **PayPal**
    + **Bank Transfer**
* **Cardholder Name** (`cardholder_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the name as it appears on your card.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, `select_one`, **Required**): Review and accept the terms and conditions, and consent to receive marketing communications.

### Tips

*   Make sure to enter your email address correctly to avoid issues with receiving the newsletter.
*   Select only the topics that you are interested in to get the most relevant content.
*   If you are a business subscriber, make sure to enter your company name and job title correctly.
*   If you have any issues with the form, contact us at [support email](mailto:support@bluesnap.com).
