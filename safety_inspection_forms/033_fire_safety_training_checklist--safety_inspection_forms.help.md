# Fire Safety Training Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Safety Training Checklist is used to record and track the status of fire safety training sessions. This form is essential for ensuring that all required safety training is completed and reported accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the session participants' names.
2. Enter the date of the session.
3. Specify the location of the session.
4. Choose the topics that were covered during the session.
5. Add any notes about the session.
6. Identify the fire safety training officer who conducted the session.
7. Update the session status to reflect its current status.
8. Record the date the form was last updated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Session Participants
* **Session Participants** (`fire_safety_session_participants`, select_multiple, optional): List all the participants who attended the session. You can select multiple participants from the options provided.

### Session Date
* **Session Date** (`fire_safety_session_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the session. This should be in the standard date format (dd/mm/yyyy).

### Session Location
* **Session Location** (`fire_safety_session_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the session took place.

### Covered Topics
* **Covered Topics** (`fire_safety_topics_covered`, select_one, optional): Choose all the topics that were covered during the session. Select one or more options from the provided list.

### Session Notes
* **Session Notes** (`fire_safety_session_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the session.

### Fire Safety Training Officer
* **Fire Safety Training Officer** (`fire_safety_training_officer`, text, optional): Identify the fire safety training officer who conducted the session.

### Session Status
* **Session Status** (`fire_safety_session_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the session: Completed, Pending, or In Progress.

### Last Updated
* **Last Updated** (`fire_safety_last_updated`, date, optional): Record the date and time the form was last updated.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct participants, date, and location for each session.
* Update the session status regularly to reflect the current status.
* Keep the session notes concise but informative.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
