# Safety Reflection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Safety Reflection Form is designed to document safety incidents and near-miss events, helping the safety team to analyze and prevent future occurrences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select relevant categories and options from dropdown menus.
3. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
4. Review and revise your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Incident Details (id: 1)
* Incident Details (`text`, required: false): A brief description of the incident or near-miss.
* Hint: Keep it concise and descriptive.

### Near Miss Details (id: 2)
* Near Miss Details (`text`, required: false): Additional information about the near-miss incident.
* Hint: Elaborate on the incident if it occurred.

### Safety Team (id: 3)
* Safety Team (`select_one`, required: false): Select from: Safety Team, Other.
* Hint: Choose the applicable safety team.

### Manager Review (id: 4)
* Manager Review (`text`, required: false): Comments or suggestions from a manager or supervisor.
* Hint: Keep it professional and constructive.

### Prevention Steps (id: 5)
* Prevention Steps (`text`, required: false): Actions to prevent similar incidents.
* Hint: Be specific and actionable.

### Action Items (id: 6)
* Action Items (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select from: Item 1, Item 2, Item 3.
* Hint: Choose relevant items to address the incident.

### Notes (id: 7)
* Notes (`note`, required: false): Additional comments or explanations.
* Hint: Keep it concise and clear.

### Date (id: 8)
* Date (`date`, required: false): The date of the incident or near-miss.
* Hint: Enter in dd/mm/yyyy format.

### Time (id: 9)
* Time (`time`, required: false): The time of the incident or near-miss.
* Hint: Enter in 12-hour format.

### Email (id: 10)
* Email (`email`, required: false): Contact email.
* Hint: Enter a valid email address.

### Phone (id: 11)
* Phone (`text`, required: false): Contact phone number.
* Hint: Enter a valid phone number.

### Category (id: 12)

### Category Other (id: 13)
