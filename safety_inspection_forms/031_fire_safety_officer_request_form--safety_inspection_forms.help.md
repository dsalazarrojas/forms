# Fire Safety Officer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Safety Officer Request Form is used to request fire safety officer services from the relevant department. It is a crucial form that ensures the proper maintenance and inspection of fire safety equipment in the workplace, which is a top priority for a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and attachments before starting the form.
2. Fill in the requestor and submitter information fields with the relevant details.
3. Select the correct options for the contact information, fire safety equipment, and other fields as per your department's guidelines.
4. Upload any required attachments or supporting documents.
5. Double-check your form for any errors before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form** (`request_form`, text): This is the main description of the request.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_form`, select_multiple): Select the relevant contact information for your request, which may include "Yes" or "No" options.
* **Fire Safety Equipment** (`fire_safety_equipment`, select_one): Indicate if the fire safety equipment is present, "Yes" or "No", as per your observation.
* **Fire Safety Equipment List** (`fire_safety_equipment_list`, text): Describe the equipment that requires maintenance or inspection, and ensure to attach any supporting documents.
* **Fire Safety Equipment Condition** (`fire_safety_equipment_condition`, select_one): Select "Yes" if the equipment's condition is a concern, and "No" if it's in good condition.
* **Fire Safety Equipment Replaced** (`fire_safety_equipment_replaced`, select_one): Indicate if the equipment has been replaced, "Yes" or "No".
* **Fire Safety Equipment Replaced Date** (`fire_safety_equipment_replaced_date`, date): If the equipment has been replaced, enter the date it was replaced.
* **Fire Safety Equipment Replaced By** (`fire_safety_equipment_replaced_by`, text): Describe who replaced the equipment, if applicable.
* **Fire Safety Equipment Last Inspection Date** (`fire_safety_equipment_last_inspection_date`, date): Enter the date of the last inspection, if available.
* **Fire Safety Equipment Next Inspection Due** (`fire_safety_equipment_next_inspection_due`, date): Indicate when the next inspection is due, if applicable.
* **Fire Safety Equipment Notes** (`fire_safety_equipment_notes`, note): Enter any additional notes or comments regarding the equipment's condition.
* **Requestor** (`requestor`, text): Enter your name as the person requesting the fire safety officer services.
* **Phone** (`requestor_phone`, text): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple): Select the relevant location for the request, which may include "Yes" or "No" options.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text): Enter the name of the person submitting the request.
* **Email** (`submitter_email`, email): Enter the submitter's email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text): Enter the submitter's contact phone number.
* **Department** (`submitter_department`, text): Enter the department that the submitter belongs to.
* **Job Title** (`submitter_jobtitle`, text): Enter the submitter's job title.
* **Supervisor** (`submitter_supervisor`, text): Enter the name of the submitter's supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email** (`submitter_supervisor_email`, email): Enter the supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`submitter_supervisor_phone`, text): Enter the supervisor's contact phone number.
* **Submitter Department 2** (`submitter_department_2`, select_multiple): Select the department that the submitter belongs to, if applicable.
* **Submitter Job Title 2** (`submitter_jobtitle_2`, select_multiple): Select the submitter's job title, if applicable.
* **Supervisor Department** (`submitter_supervisor_department`, select_multiple): Select the supervisor's department, if applicable.

Note: Please ensure all mandatory fields are filled in for successful submission.
