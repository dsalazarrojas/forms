# Hose Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the results of a hose inspection, including the condition of the hose, location, and results of any tests or repairs. It is used by personnel responsible for maintaining and inspecting hoses in industries such as manufacturing, chemical processing, or water treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Check the condition of the hose and select the correct status from the options provided.
2. Indicate if the valve is functioning correctly or if there are any issues.
3. Select the location of the hose, such as "Yes" if it is located indoors or "No" if it is outdoors.
4. Record the result of any leak tests performed.
5. Take a photo of the hose if necessary for further reference.
6. Provide any additional comments or notes about the hose's condition.
7. Enter the date and time of the inspection.
8. Sign and date the form to confirm that it has been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hose Condition** (`hose_condition`): Describe the condition of the hose, using the available options to indicate its status (e.g., good, fair, poor).
* **Valve Condition** (`valve_condition`): Check if the valve is functioning correctly, selecting "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
* **Hose Location** (`hose_location`): Choose "Yes" if the hose is located indoors or "No" if it is outdoors.
* **Leak Test Result** (`leak_test_result`): Record the result of any leak tests performed, selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Photo** (`photo`): If necessary, take a photo of the hose to document its condition.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Provide any additional comments or notes about the hose's condition.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Employee Signed** (`employee_signed`): Sign and date the form to confirm that it has been completed.
* **Manager Signed** (`manager_signed`): Sign and date the form to confirm that it has been reviewed and approved.

## Tips

* Make sure to thoroughly inspect the hose and record its condition accurately.
* Take photos of any issues or concerns for future reference.
* Keep the form up-to-date and organized for easy reference.
