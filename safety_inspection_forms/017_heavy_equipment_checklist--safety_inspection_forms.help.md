## How To Complete This Form
This form is used to record and track heavy equipment inspections. To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Enter the equipment's ID to identify the specific equipment being inspected.
2.  Select the date of the inspection.
3.  Enter the name of the inspector.
4.  Choose whether the inspection is completed or not.
5.  List any conditions of the equipment (if any).
6.  Describe the equipment in detail.
7.  Check the category of the equipment (if applicable).
8.  Enter the location of the equipment.
9.  Add any comments about the inspection.
10.  If applicable, assign a tool to the equipment and select the type of tool assigned.
11.  Select if the tool is assigned to anyone (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Construction Equipment** (`construction_equipment`, `number`, required): Enter the unique identifier of the equipment being inspected.
- **Equipment Inspection Date** (`equipment_inspection_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the inspection was performed.
- **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, `text`, required): Type in the full name of the person performing the inspection.
- **Has Inspection Completed** (`has_inspection_completed`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if the inspection is finished, 'No' if it's ongoing.
- **Equipment Condition** (`condition`, `select_multiple`, required): Check 'Yes' for any conditions found on the equipment, such as wear and tear, damage, or malfunctions.
- **Equipment Description** (`equipment_description`, `text`, required): Describe the equipment in detail, including its type, model, and any distinguishing features.
- **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required): Check if the equipment falls under a specific category (if applicable).
- **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter the exact location where the equipment is stored or used.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required): Add any comments or notes about the inspection.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the tool assigned to the equipment (if applicable).
- **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, `select_multiple`, required): Check if the tool is assigned to anyone (if applicable).
