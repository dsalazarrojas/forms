# new_year_s_safety_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_year_s_safety_checklist_form is designed to gather information about fire safety procedures and protocols for the upcoming New Year's event. This form is intended to be completed by safety personnel or responsible individuals to ensure that all necessary fire safety measures are in place and functioning properly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Year's Safety Questions** (`new_year_s_safety_questions`): This field is for general safety questions related to the New Year's event.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`): Enter contact information for emergency situations, such as phone numbers and names of key contacts.
* **Fire Safety Procedures** (`fire_safety_procedures`): Describe the procedures in place for fire safety, including evacuation plans and protocols.
* **First Aid Kit** (`first_aid_kit`): Specify the location and availability of first aid kits.
* **Emergency Exit Plan** (`emergency_exit_plan`): Describe the plan for emergency exits, including routes and assembly points.
* **Fire Extinguishers** (`fire_extinguishers`): Specify the locations and types of fire extinguishers available.
* **Fire Safety Equipment** (`fire_safety_equipment`): Describe the fire safety equipment available, such as fire alarms and suppression systems.
* **Fire Safety Procedures** (`fire_safety_procedures`): Describe the procedures in place for regular fire safety checks and inspections.
* **Electrical Equipment** (`electrical_equipment`): Specify the types and locations of electrical equipment, including their safety features.
* **Electrical Hazard Control** (`electrical_hazard_control`): Describe the measures in place to control electrical hazards.
* **Chemical Safety Equipment** (`chemical_safety_equipment`): Specify the types and locations of chemical safety equipment, including their safety features.
* **Chemical Hazard Control** (`chemical_hazard_control`): Describe the measures in place to control chemical hazards.
* **First Aid Services** (`first_aid_services`): Specify the first aid services available, including the types and locations.
* **Emergency Phone Numbers** (`emergency_phone_numbers`): Enter emergency phone numbers, such as 911 or other contact numbers.
* **Fire Escape Routes** (`fire_escape_routes`): Describe the routes and locations for emergency exits.
* **Alarm System** (`alarm_system`): Describe the alarm system in place, including its features and locations.
* **Fire Drills** (`fire_drills`): Specify the types and frequency of fire drills, including training protocols.
* **Emergency Exit Signs** (`emergency_exit_signs`): Describe the locations and types of emergency exit signs.
* **Fire Supervision** (`fire_supervision`): Specify the types and locations of fire supervision, including monitoring systems.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter contact information for emergency situations.
* **Fire Alarm System** (`fire_alarm_system`): Describe the fire alarm system in place, including its features and locations.
* **Fire Extinguishers Locations** (`fire_extinguishers_locations`): Specify the locations of fire extinguishers.
* **Fire Safety Equipment Locations** (`fire_safety_equipment_locations`): Describe the locations of fire safety equipment.
* **Fire Safety Equipment Status** (`fire_safety_equipment_status`): Describe the status of fire safety equipment, including maintenance and testing schedules.
* **Other Fire Safety Measures** (`other_fire_safety_measures`): Specify any other fire safety measures not listed above.

Please note that this form is for informational purposes only and should be adapted according to your specific needs and protocols.
