# radio_message_log_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The radio_message_log_form is used to log and record radio messages sent or received by personnel, helping to maintain accurate and up-to-date information about these communications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of message you wish to log (Voice, Text, or Image).
2.  Enter the date and time the message was sent or received (if applicable).
3.  Enter the sender and recipient of the message (if applicable).
4.  Add any attachments to the message (if applicable).
5.  Provide any additional notes about the message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Message Type** (`message_type`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose the type of message that best describes the content of the message, such as Voice, Text, or Image.
* **Prophet's Date** (`message_date`, `date`, **Optional**): Enter the date the message was sent or received, in the format MM/DD/YY or MM/DD.
* **Message Time** (`message_time`, `time`, **Optional**): Enter the time the message was sent or received, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Sender** (`sender`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter the name or identifier of the person who sent the message.
* **Recipient** (`recipient`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter the name or identifier of the person who received the message.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter a brief description of the message content.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, `select_multiple`, **Optional**): Choose from a list of available attachments, such as 3G, 4G, 5G, or Other.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, **Optional**): Add any additional comments or notes about the message, such as relevant background information or follow-up actions.
