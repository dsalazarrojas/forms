# Scaffold Handover Certificate - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scaffold Handover Certificate is used to document the handover of a scaffold from one site manager to another, ensuring that all relevant details and information are recorded and transferred between the site manager who built the scaffold and the site manager who will be using it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form and ensure that you have all necessary information.
2. Complete the fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Scaffold Details** (`scaffolding_details`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter any relevant details about the scaffold, such as its location, size, or any other specific information that may be necessary for the site manager.
* **Inspection Status** (`inspection_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the inspection, either "Accepted" or "Rejected", to indicate whether the scaffold meets the necessary safety and quality standards.
* **Site Manager** (`site_manager_name`, select_multiple, optional): Select all site managers involved in the handover process, which may include the Construction Supervisor, Site Manager, and Project Manager.
* **Acceptance Date** (`acceptance_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the site manager accepted the scaffold, ensuring that all parties are aware of the timeline.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the inspection took place, allowing for accurate record-keeping and reference.
* **Site Location** (`site_location`, text, optional): Enter the site location where the scaffold will be used, ensuring that all parties are aware of its location.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the scaffold handover, such as any issues or concerns that need to be addressed.
* **Site Manager Email** (`site_manager_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the site manager, allowing for easy communication and reference.
* **Site Manager Phone** (`site_manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the site manager, allowing for easy contact and coordination.

Please remember to double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form. If you have any doubts or questions, please do not hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or relevant authority.
