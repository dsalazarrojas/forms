# hazard_identification_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help identify potential hazards in a workplace or facility. It collects information on the category, frequency, and severity of hazards, as well as measures to mitigate them. The form is meant to be completed by an inspector and reviewed by a designated reviewer.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and access to relevant documents before starting.
2. Answer all required questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Enter information in the format specified for each field.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Potential Hazards** (`1`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter a list of potential hazards found during the inspection.
* **Hazard Category** (`2`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select the category that best describes the hazard, from the options provided.
* **Hazard Frequency** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Indicate how often the hazard occurs, from the options frequent, occasional, or rare.
* **Severity Level** (`4`, `number`, required):
	+ Enter a numerical value to indicate the severity level of the hazard, with higher numbers indicating greater severity.
* **Hazard Description** (`5`, `text`, required):
	+ Provide a detailed description of the hazard, including its location and context.
* **Mitigation Measures** (`6`, `text`, required):
	+ List measures taken or recommended to mitigate the hazard.
* **Risk Level** (`7`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Assess the risk level of the hazard, from the options low, medium, or high.
* **Prevention Methods** (`8`, `text`, required):
	+ Describe measures taken or recommended to prevent the hazard.
* **Review Date** (`9`, `date`, required):
	+ Enter the date the inspection was reviewed.
* **Inspector Signature** (`10`, `date`, required):
	+ Sign and date the form as the inspector.
* **Reviewers Signature** (`11`, `date`, required):
	+ Sign and date the form as the reviewer.
