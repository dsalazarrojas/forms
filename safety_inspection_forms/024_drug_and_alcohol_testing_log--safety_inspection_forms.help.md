# Drug And Alcohol Testing Log - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Drug And Alcohol Testing Log" form is used for recording and documenting drug and alcohol testing events. This form helps in maintaining accurate and up-to-date records of testing instances.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the required fields:
	* **test_location**: Enter the location where the testing took place.
3. (Optional) Provide additional information in the **additional_tests** field if necessary.
4. Add any relevant notes in the **Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **test_location**: Enter the location where the testing event occurred. This could be a workplace, testing facility, or any other relevant location.
* **additional_tests**: (Optional) Provide any additional information about the testing event if required.
* **Notes**: Add any relevant notes or comments about the testing event.

## Tips

* Ensure the form is completed accurately and up-to-date.
* Review the form before submission to ensure all required fields are filled.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your organization's policies or guidelines for clarification.
