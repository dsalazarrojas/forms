# Lifting Sling Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lifting Sling Inspection Checklist is a critical document used to record the inspection findings of lifting slings on-site. It helps to identify potential hazards and ensures that the slings are fit for use. The form is completed by safety inspectors during regular inspections of the slings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Site Name field with the name of the location where the inspection was conducted.
2. Enter the date and time of the inspection in the Inspection Date and Inspection Time fields.
3. Enter your name and title/position in the Inspector Name field.
4. Record the Equipment ID of the sling being inspected.
5. Enter the sling's capacity in the Sling Capacity field.
6. Enter the safety factor of the sling in the Safety Factor field.
7. Select the inspection status from the options provided (Pass, Fail, or Review).
8. If you have any comments or additional information, enter them in the Comments field.
9. Upload any relevant photos or images of the sling in the Photos field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Site Name (`site_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the location where the inspection was conducted.

### Inspection Date (`inspection_date`, date, required)
Enter the date of the inspection in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### Inspection Time (`inspection_time`, time, required)
Enter the time of the inspection in the format `HH:MM:SS`.

### Inspector Name (`inspector_name`, text, required)
Enter your name and title/position.

### Equipment ID (`equipment_id`, number, required)
Enter the ID of the sling being inspected.

### Sling Capacity (`sling_capacity`, number, required)
Enter the capacity of the sling.

### Safety Factor (`safety_factor`, number, required)
Enter the safety factor of the sling.

### Inspection Status (`inspection_status`, select_one, required)
Select the inspection status from the options: Pass, Fail, or Review.

### Comments (`comments`, text, optional)
Enter any comments or additional information related to the inspection.

### Photos (`photos`, text, optional)
Upload any relevant photos or images of the sling.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* Ensure that you enter the correct equipment ID and capacity.
* Take photos of any defects or issues found during the inspection.
* Select the correct inspection status to reflect the actual condition of the sling.
* Keep the comments field for any additional information that may be necessary.
* If you have any questions or concerns, consult with your supervisor or colleague.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
