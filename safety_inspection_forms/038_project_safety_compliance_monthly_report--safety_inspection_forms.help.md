# Project Safety Compliance Monthly Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect safety compliance information on a monthly basis from project teams. It helps ensure that all projects are meeting safety regulations and identifies any concerns or incidents that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your project in the **Project Title** field.
2. If there were any incidents or accidents during the month, provide the **Incident Date**, **Incident Time**, and **Incident Description** fields.
3. Check the **Compliance Status** field to indicate whether your project is **In compliance with safety regulations**, **Not compliant**, or **Partially compliant**.
4. If you have any safety concerns, select all that apply from the **Safety concerns** field. You can also provide a brief description in the **Incident Details** field.
5. If there were any incidents, indicate their status in the **Follow-up Status** field.
6. Provide any additional information about follow-up actions or details in the **Follow-up Details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): Enter the name of your project here.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your submission, if applicable. If not, leave blank.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your project, if applicable. If not, leave blank.
* **Compliance Status** (`compliance_status`, select_one, optional): Check if your project is **In compliance with safety regulations**, **Not compliant**, or **Partially compliant**.
* **Safety concerns** (`safety_concerns`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the list of safety concerns. You can also provide a brief description in the **Incident Description** field.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of any incidents that occurred.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of any incidents that occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of any incidents that occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of any incidents that occurred.
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_one, optional): Check if you are **In progress** with follow-up actions, **Completed**, or if it's **Not applicable**.
* **Follow-up Details** (`follow_up_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about follow-up actions or details.

## Tips

* Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to help project leaders and safety experts monitor and improve safety performance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your safety team for guidance.
* Remember to review and submit this form on a monthly basis to ensure ongoing safety compliance and incident tracking.
