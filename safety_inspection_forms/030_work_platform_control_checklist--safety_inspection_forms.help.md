# Work Platform Control Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Platform Control Checklist is a safety inspection form used to ensure the safety of workers on work platforms. This form covers pre-operating checks, safety equipment, safety procedures, safety training, inspection of safety equipment, completion of safety procedures, and notes from the inspector. The form helps to identify potential hazards and prevent accidents on work platforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information to complete the form.
2. Review each field carefully and answer the questions accurately.
3. For fields with options, select the correct answer from the dropdown list.
4. Enter any necessary notes or comments in the "notes" field.
5. Fill out the "inspector" fields with your name, phone number, and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pre Operating Check** (`work_platform_control_checklist_pre_operating_check`): This is the first step in the safety inspection process. Check the work platform before operating to ensure it is in a safe condition.
* **Safety Equipment** (`work_platform_control_checklist_safety_equipment`): Select the safety equipment used on the work platform. Options include:
	+ Safety Harness
	+ Safety Harness with Fall Arrest
	+ Safety Net
	+ Safety Net with Fall Arrest
* **Safety Procedure** (`work_platform_control_checklist_safety_procedure`): Enter the safety procedure in place on the work platform. This is the sequence of steps taken to ensure worker safety.
* **Safety Training** (`work_platform_control_checklist_safety_training`): Select the safety training provided to workers on the work platform. Options include:
	+ Safety Harness
	+ Safety Harness with Fall Arrest
	+ Safety Net
	+ Safety Net with Fall Arrest
* **Safety Equipment Inspected** (`work_platform_control_checklist_safety_equipment_inspected`): Enter the number of safety equipment items inspected on the work platform.
* **Safety Procedure Completed** (`work_platform_control_checklist_safety_procedure_completed`): Enter the safety procedure followed on the work platform.
* **Notes** (`work_platform_control_checklist_notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
* **Inspector Name** (`work_platform_control_checklist_inspector_name`): Enter your name as the safety inspector.
* **Inspector Phone** (`work_platform_control_checklist_inspector_phone`): Enter your phone number as the safety inspector.
* **Inspector Email** (`work_platform_control_checklist_inspector_email`): Enter your email address as the safety inspector.
