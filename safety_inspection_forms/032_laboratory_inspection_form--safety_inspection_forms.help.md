# Laboratory Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on a laboratory's facilities and equipment, ensuring compliance with relevant regulations and industry standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact person's first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter the facility's name and address, including the city and state.
3. Provide the date and time of the inspection visit.
4. Select the type of equipment being inspected and its status.
5. Describe the condition of the equipment and facility.
6. Confirm if the regulatory requirements are met.
7. Add any additional comments or references.
8. List any action items for future improvements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_person_first_name** (Text, Required): Please enter the first name of the contact person.
* **contact_person_last_name** (Text, Required): Please enter the last name of the contact person.
* **facility_name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the laboratory or facility being inspected.
* **facility_address** (Text, Required): Enter the address of the laboratory or facility being inspected.
* **facility_city** (Text, Required): Enter the city of the laboratory or facility being inspected.
* **facility_state** (Text, Required): Enter the state of the laboratory or facility being inspected.
* **facility_zip** (Text, Required): Enter the zip code of the laboratory or facility being inspected.
* **date_of_visit** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the inspection visit.
* **time_of_visit** (Time, Required): Enter the time of the inspection visit.
* **inspector_name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the inspector conducting the inspection.
* **inspector_license_number** (Text, Required): Enter the license number of the inspector.
* **equipment_type** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the type of equipment being inspected.
* **equipment_status** (Select One, Required): Select the status of the equipment.
* **equipment_condition** (Select One, Required): Select the condition of the equipment.
* **facility_condition** (Select One, Required): Select the condition of the facility.
* **regulatory_requirements_met** (Select One, Required): Confirm if the regulatory requirements are met.
* **additional_comments** (Text, Required): Add any additional comments or observations.
* **regulatory_references** (Text, Optional): Add any regulatory references or standards.
* **action_item_1** (Text, Required): List the first action item for improvement.
* **action_item_2** (Text, Required): List the second action item for improvement.
* **action_item_3** (Text, Required): List the third action item for improvement.
* **action_item_4** (Text, Required): List the fourth action item for improvement.
