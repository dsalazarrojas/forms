# Incident Exposure Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incident Exposure Report form is designed to collect information about incidents that pose risks or dangers to employees, visitors, or the general public. This form is used to gather details about the incident, including the date, time, location, and description of the incident. The form also asks for the type of incident, status, reporter, category, investigator, and any witness statements or attachments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Incident Date** field with the date the incident occurred.
2. Enter the **Incident Time** field with the time the incident happened.
3. In the **Incident Location** field, specify the location where the incident took place.
4. Describe the **Incident Description** in the text box provided.
5. Choose the type of incident from the **Incident Type** dropdown menu. Options include:
	* Incident - Fire
	* Incident - Gas Leak
	* Incident - Electrical
	* Incident - Other
6. Select the current **Incident Status** from the dropdown menu. Options include:
	* Active
	* Resolved
	* Pending
7. Choose who reported the incident from the **Incident Reporter** dropdown menu. Options include:
	* Employee
	* Supervisor
	* Manager
8. Select the category of the incident from the **Incident Category** dropdown menu. Options include:
	* Electrical
	* Gas
	* Chemical
	* Physical
	* Other
9. Choose who investigated the incident from the **Incident Investigator** dropdown menu. Options include:
	* Employee
	* Supervisor
	* Manager
10. If there are any witness statements, enter them in the **Witness Statements** text box.
11. If any photos were taken during the incident, upload them as an **Incident Photos** attachment.
12. If witness statements or photos are attached, ensure that they are accurate and relevant to the incident.
13. Finally, submit the form once all required fields are filled out and accurate information is provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (Date): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (Time): Enter the time the incident happened.
* **Incident Location** (Text): Specify the location where the incident took place.
* **Incident Description** (Text): Describe the incident in detail.
* **Incident Type** (Select One):
	+ Incident - Fire
	+ Incident - Gas Leak
	+ Incident - Electrical
	+ Incident - Other
* **Incident Status** (Select Multiple):
	+ Active
	+ Resolved
	+ Pending
* **Incident Reporter** (Select Multiple):
	+ Employee
	+ Supervisor
	+ Manager
* **Incident Category** (Select Multiple):
	+ Electrical
	+ Gas
	+ Chemical
	+ Physical
	+ Other
* **Incident Investigator** (Select One):
	+ Employee
	+ Supervisor
	+ Manager
* **Witness Statements** (Text): Enter any witness statements.
* **Incident Photos** (Text): Upload incident photos.
* **Witness Statements Attachment** (Text): Attach witness statements.
* **Incident Photos Attachment** (Text): Attach incident photos.
* **Incident Video Attachment** (Text): Attach an incident video.
