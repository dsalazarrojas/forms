# Automotive Safety Verification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for an automotive safety verification request, which is a critical process to ensure the vehicle's safety and compliance with relevant regulations. The purpose of this form is to gather details about the vehicle, the requester, and the inspection requirements to facilitate efficient and effective safety verification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information in the "Requester Information" section.
2. Enter your vehicle's year, make, model, and Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) in the "Vehicle Information" section.
3. Specify the type of inspection required and the reason for the inspection in the "Inspection Details" section.
4. If there are any reported issues or concerns with the vehicle, describe them in the "Specific Issues or Concerns" section.
5. Assess the safety concern level and preferred inspection time in the "Safety Concern Assessment" and "Scheduling Preferences" sections, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, Text, Required): Enter your name as the requester for this safety verification request.
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for any follow-up questions or updates.
* **Phone Number** (`requester_phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you urgently.
* **Organization or Fleet Name** (`organization`, Text, Required): If you're representing a company or fleet, enter the name.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, Number, Required): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, Text, Required): Enter the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, Text, Required): Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle VIN** (`vehicle_vin`, Text, Required): Enter your vehicle's Vehicle Identification Number.
* **License Plate Number** (`license_plate`, Text, Required): Enter your vehicle's license plate number.
* **Current Mileage** (`mileage`, Number, Required): Enter your vehicle's current mileage.
* **Requested Inspection Date** (`requested_date`, Date, Required): Select the date you'd like to have the inspection conducted.
* **Type of Inspection Needed** (`inspection_type`, Select One, Required): Select the type of inspection required:
	+ Emissions Testing
	+ Safety Inspection
	+ Full Vehicle Inspection
	+ Mechanical Safety Check
	+ Electrical Systems Check
	+ Brake System Inspection
* **Reason for Inspection** (`inspection_reason`, Select One, Required): Select the reason for the inspection:
	+ Routine Maintenance
	+ Pre-Purchase Inspection
	+ Accident Investigation
	+ Compliance Check
	+ Fleet Management
	+ Warranty Service
	+ Other
* **Specific Issues or Concerns** (`reported_issues`, Text, Optional): Describe any known problems or concerns with your vehicle.
* **Are Any Warning Lights Illuminated?** (`warning_lights`, Select One, Required): Select if any warning lights are illuminated:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Unsure
* **Vehicle Has Been in Accident** (`accident_history`, Select One, Required): Select if your vehicle has been in an accident:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Unknown
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, Select One, Required): Select the urgency level:
	+ Critical - Safety Risk
	+ High - Major Issue
	+ Medium - Routine
	+ Low - General Check
* **Preferred Inspection Time** (`preferred_time`, Select One, Required): Select your preferred inspection time:
	+ Morning (8 AM - 12 PM)
	+ Afternoon (12 PM - 5 PM)
	+ Early Evening (5 PM - 7 PM)
	+ Flexible
* **Additional Notes or Special Instructions** (`additional_notes`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional information or special instructions for the inspection.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields with a "Required" label are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
* The inspection process will be conducted based on the information provided in this form.
* Please review your submission carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
