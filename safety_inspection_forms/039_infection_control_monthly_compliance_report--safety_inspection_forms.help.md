# Infection Control Monthly Compliance Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Infection Control Monthly Compliance Report is a form used to document infection control measures taken and reported incidents in a facility for the month. This information helps to identify areas of improvement and ensure compliance with infection control standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the facility address in the **Facility Address** field.
2. Select your **Reviewer Name** from the provided options.
3. Fill in the **Date Infection Control Monthly Compliance Report** field with the date you're reporting for.
4. Enter the **Infection Control Measures Taken** to report any actions taken for infection control during the month.
5. List any **Infection Control Measures Inadequacies** encountered, including any challenges faced or areas needing improvement.
6. Provide a brief description of any **Incident Reporting Infection Control Monthly Compliance** incidents that occurred during the month.
7. Describe the **Review Process** used for the report.
8. Sign with your **Reviewer Signature** and the date you completed the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility where the infection control measures were taken.
* **Reviewer Name**: Select the name of the reviewer who is submitting this report.
* **Date Infection Control Monthly Compliance Report**: The date the report is being submitted for.
* **Infection Control Measures Taken**: List any actions taken for infection control during the month.
* **Infection Control Measures Inadequacies**: List any challenges or areas needing improvement.
* **Incident Reporting Infection Control Monthly Compliance**: Describe any incidents that occurred during the month.
* **Review Process**: Describe the review process used for the report.
* **Reviewer Signature**: Sign with your name and the date you completed the report.
