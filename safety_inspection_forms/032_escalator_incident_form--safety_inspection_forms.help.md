# Escalator Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Escalator Incident Form is used to report incidents related to escalators, such as malfunctions, accidents, or other issues that require attention from maintenance or management teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the incident in the **Escalator Incident Description** field.
2. Answer the question about whether the escalator is currently in operation in the **Escalator Location** field.
3. Indicate whether a mechanic was involved in the incident in the **Escalator Mechanic** field.
4. Select whether an escalator rider was involved in the incident in the **Escalator Rider** field.
5. Enter the date and time of the incident in the **Escalator Date** and **Escalator Time** fields respectively.
6. Provide any additional comments or notes about the incident in the **Escalator Note** field.
7. If available, upload supporting images or videos of the incident in the **Escalator Incident Image** and **Escalator Incident Video** fields.
8. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number, in the **Escalator Email** and **Escalator Phone** fields respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Escalator Incident Form 1**: A text field where you can provide a brief description of the incident.
* **Escalator Incident Description**: A text field where you can provide more details about the incident.
* **Escalator Location**: Select "Yes" if the escalator is currently not in operation due to the incident, or "No" if it is still operational.
* **Escalator Mechanic**: Select "Yes" if a mechanic was involved in the incident, or "No" if not.
* **Escalator Rider**: Select "Yes" if an escalator rider was involved in the incident, or "No" if not.
* **Escalator Date**: Enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Escalator Time**: Enter the time of the incident in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Escalator Note**: A field for any additional comments or notes about the incident.
* **Escalator Incident Image**: If you have an image related to the incident, upload it here.
* **Escalator Incident Video**: If you have a video related to the incident, upload it here.
* **Escalator Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Escalator Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

Note: The fields marked as optional may be required depending on the incident's nature and your organization's specific policies. Please ensure you provide as much information as possible to help with the incident's investigation and resolution.
