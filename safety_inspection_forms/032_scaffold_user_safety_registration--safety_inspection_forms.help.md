# Scaffold User Safety Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for the safety registration of a scaffold. The goal is to understand the current safety status of the scaffold and ensure that it meets the necessary safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields.
2. Enter the scaffold details.
3. Provide your name and email address.
4. Enter the scaffold's phone number.
5. Confirm your status as a scaffold user.
6. Select the current safety status of the scaffold.
7. Add any additional comments about the scaffold.
8. Enter the date of the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Scaffold Details** (`scaffolddetails`): Enter a brief description of the scaffold, including its location and any relevant details.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name as the scaffold user.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the scaffold's phone number for contact purposes.
* **User Confirm** (`userconfirm`): Confirm that you are the scaffold user. Select "confirmed" if you are authorized to access the scaffold.
* **Safety Status** (`safetystatus`): Select the current safety status of the scaffold from the options "confirmed", "not confirmed", or "unknown".
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments about the scaffold's safety status.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the inspection.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask for clarification or assistance.
* This form is for internal use only, so please do not share it with anyone outside of the organization.
