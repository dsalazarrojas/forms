# Fire Incident Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents that have occurred in a facility, including fires, electrical issues, or other incidents that pose a risk to the facility and its occupants.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility where the incident occurred.
2. **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident, if it is relevant to the incident.
3. **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident, if it is relevant to the incident.
4. **Incident Summary**: Briefly describe the incident that occurred.
5. **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility where the incident occurred.
6. **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred within the facility.
7. **Incident Description**: Describe the incident in more detail, including any circumstances or contributing factors.
8. **Incident Cause**: Select the cause of the incident from the provided options or type in your own reason if it's not listed. If you select "Other", you can provide additional information in the next field.
9. **Other (Incident Cause)**: If you selected "Other" in the previous field, provide a brief description of the cause of the incident.
10. **Action Taken**: Describe any actions taken after the incident to prevent it from happening again.
11. **Fire Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the fire supervisor responsible for the incident.
12. **Fire Supervisor Contact**: Enter the contact information for the fire supervisor, including their phone number and any other relevant details.
13. **Reporting Officer**: Enter your name as the reporting officer.
14. **Reporting Officer Contact**: Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
15. **Reporting Officer Email**: Enter your email address.
16. **Reporting Officer Phone**: Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name**: The name of the facility where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Date**: The date of the incident, if it is relevant to the incident.
* **Incident Time**: The time of the incident, if it is relevant to the incident.
* **Incident Summary**: A brief description of the incident.
* **Facility Address**: The address of the facility where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location**: The location within the facility where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description**: A detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Cause**: The reason or source that caused the incident.
* **Other (Incident Cause)**: If you selected "Other" in the previous field, a description of the reason that caused the incident.
* **Action Taken**: A description of any actions taken after the incident.
* **Fire Supervisor Name**: The name of the fire supervisor responsible for the incident.
* **Fire Supervisor Contact**: The contact information for the fire supervisor.
* **Reporting Officer**: The name of the person completing the form.
* **Reporting Officer Contact**: The contact information for the reporting officer.
* **Reporting Officer Email**: The email address of the reporting officer.
* **Reporting Officer Phone**: The phone number of the reporting officer.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter all required fields.
* Double-check that you have selected the correct incident cause.
* Enter accurate contact information for the fire supervisor and reporting officer.
* If you are unsure about any information, consult with a supervisor or the facility's safety manager.
