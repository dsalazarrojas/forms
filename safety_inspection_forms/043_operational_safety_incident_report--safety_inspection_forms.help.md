# operational_safety_incident_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The operational safety incident report form is used to document and record incidents that occur during work hours, helping to ensure a safe working environment and enabling prompt action to be taken in response to such incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Incident Date** field with the date the incident occurred.
2. Enter the **Incident Time** when the incident took place.
3. Select the **Location** where the incident occurred from the provided options (Confined Space or Near Confined Space).
4. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the **Incident Description** field.
5. Identify the **Root Cause** of the incident from the provided options (Environmental Factor, Equipment Factor, Human Factor).
6. If necessary, suggest **Corrective Action** to prevent similar incidents in the future.
7. Specify the **Preventive Actions** to take to prevent similar incidents (Regular Review, Training).
8. Select the **Incident Type**(s) that best describes the incident (Lost Time, Injury, Damage).
9. Identify the **Personnel Involved** in the incident (Supervisor, Employee, Contractor).
10. Indicate whether there were **Witnesses** to the incident (True or False).
11. Optionally provide the **Incident Location** if it's a specific place (e.g., a room number or a building).
12. Select the **Incident Status** from the provided options (Active, Inactive).
13. Enter the name of the person who reported the **Incident Reporter**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (id 1, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (id 2, required): Enter the time the incident took place.
* **Location** (id 3, required): Select the area where the incident happened (Confined Space or Near Confined Space).
* **Incident Description** (id 4, required): Provide a detailed account of what happened.
* **Root Cause** (id 5, required): Choose the fundamental reason for the incident (Environmental Factor, Equipment Factor, Human Factor).
* **Corrective Action** (id 6, optional): Suggest steps to rectify the situation, if applicable.
* **Preventive Action** (id 7, required): Select actions to prevent similar incidents (Regular Review, Training).
* **Incident Type** (id 8, required): Select the type(s) of incident that occurred (Lost Time, Injury, Damage).
* **Personnel Involved** (id 9, required): Identify those who were present during the incident (Supervisor, Employee, Contractor).
* **Witnesses** (id 10, required): Indicate if anyone else witnessed the incident (True or False).
* **Incident Location** (id 11, optional): Provide specific details about the location of the incident, if applicable.
* **Incident Status** (id 12, required): Select the current status of the incident (Active, Inactive).
* **Incident Reporter** (id 13, required): Enter your name as the person reporting the incident.
