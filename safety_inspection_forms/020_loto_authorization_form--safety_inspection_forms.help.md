# LOTO Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LOTO Authorization Form is designed to ensure the safe and efficient execution of lockout/tagout operations. It serves as a record-keeping tool for gathering and tracking essential information related to lockout requests, personnel involvement, equipment, and location. This form is critical for maintaining accurate records and preventing potential safety hazards during these operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the LOTO Authorization Form, please follow these steps:
1. Select "Lockout Request" and choose the relevant option from the dropdown menu.
2. Provide a brief reason for the lockout request in the "Reason" field.
3. For "Lockout Tagging", choose the correct option from the dropdown menu.
4. List all personnel involved in the lockout/tagout activity in the "Personnel Involved" field.
5. Enter the equipment ID in the "Equipment ID" field.
6. Provide a detailed description of the equipment in the "Equipment Description" field.
7. Specify the location where the lockout/tagout activity will take place in the "Location" field.
8. Choose the lockout device to be used for the activity from the "Lockout Device" dropdown menu.
9. Select the maintenance type required for the lockout/tagout activity from the "Maintenance Type" dropdown menu.
10. Enter any additional details for "Other Maintenance Type" in the "Other" field.
11. Indicate whether the lockout authorizer is involved in the "Lockout Authorizer" field.
12. Record the date and time when the lockout/tagout activity will commence.
13. Specify the duration of the lockout/tagout activity in the "Duration" field.
14. Update the status of the lockout/tagout activity in the "Status" field.
15. Enter any comments or remarks related to the lockout/tagout activity in the "Comments" field.
16. If applicable, specify whether the activity is assigned to someone or not.
17. If applicable, indicate whether the activity is assigned by someone or not.
18. Set a review due date for the lockout/tagout activity.
19. Update the approval status of the activity.
20. Enter any comments from the reviewer.
21. If applicable, specify whether the reviewer is involved in the activity or not.
22. If applicable, indicate whether the reviewer is authorized to review or not.
23. If applicable, specify the reviewer's position or designation.
24. If applicable, enter the reviewer's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lockout Request**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether a lockout request is required or not.
	+ If you select "Yes", you will be prompted to provide a reason for the request.
* **Reason**: Enter a brief explanation for the lockout request.
* **Lockout Tagging**: Choose the correct lockout device to be used (Lockout Switch, Lockout Valve, or Lockout Valve and Switch).
* **Personnel Involved**: List all personnel participating in the lockout/tagout activity.
* **Equipment ID**: Enter the unique identifier of the equipment.
* **Equipment Description**: Provide a detailed description of the equipment.
* **Location**: Specify the room/section where the lockout/tagout will take place.
* **Lockout Device**: Choose the lockout device to be used.
* **Maintenance Type**: Select the type of maintenance required (Yes or No).
* **Other Maintenance Type**: Provide additional details for other maintenance types not listed.
* **Lockout Authorizer**: Check if the lockout authorizer is involved.
* **Date**: Record the date and time of the lockout/tagout activity.
* **Time**: Specify the duration of the activity.
* **Duration**: Enter the length of the lockout/tagout activity.
* **Status**: Update the status of the activity (Active, Completed, Cancelled).
* **Comments**: Enter any remarks or comments related to the activity.
* **Assigned To**: Indicate whether the activity is assigned to someone or not.
* **Assigned By**: Check if the activity is assigned by someone or not.
* **Review Due**: Set a review due date for the activity.
* **Approval Status**: Update the approval status of the activity.
* **Reviewer Comments**: Enter any comments from the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Involved**: Check if the reviewer is involved in the activity.
* **Reviewer Designation**: Specify the reviewer's designation or position.
* **Reviewer Signature**: Enter the reviewer's signature.
* **Review Status**: Indicate whether the reviewer is authorized to review or not.
* **Position**: Specify the reviewer's position or designation.
* **Signature**: Enter the reviewer's signature.
