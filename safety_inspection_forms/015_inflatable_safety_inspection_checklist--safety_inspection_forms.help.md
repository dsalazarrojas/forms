**Inflatable Safety Inspection Checklist - Help Guide**

### Purpose

This form is used to conduct an inflatable safety inspection checklist. The purpose of this form is to collect information about an inflatable's safety status and provide a record of its condition. It is intended for use by personnel responsible for inspecting and maintaining inflatables.

### How to Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, such as the event name, location, and date of inspection.
2. Evaluate the condition of the inflatable, including its model number, manufacturer, and equipment status (e.g., safety harnesses used).
3. Record the inspection results, including any notes or comments.
4. Provide details about the certification status, training status, and certification dates.
5. Document the availability of essential safety equipment, such as first aid kits and fire extinguishers.
6. Confirm emergency exit routes and emergency contact information.
7. Record the completion of the form, including the reviewer and completion dates and times.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name**: The name of the event where the inflatable was inspected.
* **Location**: The location where the inflatable was inspected.
* **Date of Inspection**: The date of the inspection.
* **Time of Inspection**: The time of the inspection.
* **Inflatable Model**: The model number of the inflatable.
* **Manufacturer**: The manufacturer of the inflatable.
* **Condition of Equipment**: The condition of the inflatable's safety equipment.
* **Safety Harnesses Used**: Whether safety harnesses were used during the inspection.
* **Inspection Results**: The results of the inspection.
* **Comments**: Any additional comments or notes.
* **Certification Status**: The certification status of the inflatable.
* **Training Status**: The training status of the personnel inspecting or operating the inflatable.
* **Certification Date**: The date of the certification.
* **Certification Expiration Date**: The expiration date of the certification.
* **Training Hours Completed**: The number of training hours completed.
* **First Aid Kit**: The availability of a first aid kit.
* **Fire Extinguisher**: The availability of a fire extinguisher.
* **Emergency Exits**: The clear marking of emergency exits.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: The emergency contact number.
* **Emergency Contact Person**: The emergency contact person.
* **Evacuation Routine**: The evacuation routine in place.
* **Reviewed by**: The person or personnel reviewing the inspection.
* **Completed by**: The person or personnel completing the inspection.
* **Date Completed**: The date the inspection was completed.
* **Time Completed**: The time the inspection was completed.
* **Attachment 1**: Any additional attachments or documentation.

### Tips

* Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form with the relevant personnel before completing it.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* Attach any necessary documentation or attachments to support the inspection results.
* Keep the form as a record of the inflatable's safety status for future reference.
