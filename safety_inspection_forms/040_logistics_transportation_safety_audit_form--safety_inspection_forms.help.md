The Logistics Transportation Safety Audit Form is used to gather information on the safety status of company vehicles. This form helps ensure that all necessary safety equipment is in good condition, and that vehicles are properly maintained and inspected.

Before completing this form, please ensure that it is not being completed unnecessarily. If the vehicle has already been inspected and audited recently, there is no need to fill out this form again.

### Logistics Transportation Safety Audit Form - Help Guide

### Purpose

This form is used to gather information on the safety status of company vehicles. The information collected will help identify any potential safety concerns and ensure that vehicles are properly maintained and inspected.

### How To Complete This Form

1. **General Information** (`id: 1`, `label: General Information`, `type: text`, optional)
	* Provide a brief description of the vehicle and its current condition.
2. **Driver Name** (`id: 2`, `label: driverName`, `type: text`, optional)
	* Enter the name of the driver who is operating the vehicle.
3. **Vehicle Registration Number** (`id: 3`, `label: vehicleRegistrationNumber`, `type: text`, optional)
	* Enter the vehicle's registration number.
4. **Vehicle Make** (`id: 4`, `label: vehicleMake`, `type: text`, optional)
	* Enter the make of the vehicle.
5. **Vehicle Model** (`id: 5`, `label: vehicleModel`, `type: text`, optional)
	* Enter the model of the vehicle.
6. **Vehicle Year** (`id: 6`, `label: vehicleYear`, `type: number`, optional)
	* Enter the year of the vehicle.
7. **Odometer Reading** (`id: 7`, `label: odometerReading`, `type: number`, optional)
	* Enter the current odometer reading.
8. **Safety Equipment** (`id: 8`, `label: safetyEquipment`, `type: select_multiple`, optional)
	* Select all safety equipment installed on the vehicle, such as Fire Extinguisher, First Aid Kit, and Safety Vest.
9. **Safety Equipment Status** (`id: 9`, `label: safetyEquipmentStatus`, `type: select_one`, optional)
	* Indicate the status of the safety equipment, either Working, Not Working, or N/A.
10. **Vehicle Condition** (`id: 10`, `label: vehicleCondition`, `type: text`, optional)
	* Describe the overall condition of the vehicle.
11. **Vehicle Location** (`id: 11`, `label: vehicleLocation`, `type: text`, optional)
	* Enter the current location of the vehicle.
12. **Vehicle Maintenance** (`id: 12`, `label: vehicleMaintenance`, `type: text`, optional)
	* Describe any recent maintenance performed on the vehicle.
13. **Safety Incident** (`id: 13`, `label: safetyIncident`, `type: select_multiple`, optional)
	* Indicate if any safety incidents have occurred, such as 'True' or 'False'.
14. **Safety Incident Detail** (`id: 14`, `label: safetyIncidentDetail`, `type: text`, optional)
	* Provide a brief description of any safety incidents.
15. **Vehicle Check Date** (`id: 15`, `label: vehicleCheckDate`, `type: date`, optional)
	* Enter the date of the last vehicle check.
16. **Vehicle Check Time** (`id: 16`, `label: vehicleCheckTime`, `type: time`, optional)
	* Enter the time of the last vehicle check.
17. **Vehicle Check By** (`id: 17`, `label: vehicleCheckBy`, `type: text`, optional)
	* Enter the name of the person who performed the last vehicle check.
18. **Notes** (`id: 18`, `label: notes`, `type: note`, optional)
	* Add any additional notes or comments about the vehicle.
19. **Signature** (`id: 19`, `label: signature`, `type: select_one`, optional)
	* Select whether a signature is required or not.
20. **Email Address** (`id: 20`, `label: emailAddress`, `type: email`, optional)
	* Enter the email address of the person completing the form.
21. **Phone Number** (`id: 21`, `label: phoneNumber`, `type: text`, optional)
	* Enter the phone number of the person completing the form.
22. **Vehicle Status** (`id: 22`, `label: vehicleStatus`, `type: select_one`, optional)
	* Indicate the current status of the vehicle, either In Use, Out of Use, or N/A.
23. **Vehicle Make Year** (`id: 23`, `label: vehicleMakeYear`, `type: number`, optional)
	* Enter the year the vehicle was manufactured.
24. **Vehicle Body Type** (`id: 24`, `label: vehicleBodyType`, `type: select_one`, optional)
	* Select the type of vehicle body, either Truck, Tractor, or N/A.
25. **Vehicle Body Length** (`id: 25`, `label: vehicleBodyLength`, `type: number`, optional)
	* Enter the length of the vehicle body.

### Tips

* Ensure all safety equipment is properly installed and in working condition.
* Describe the overall condition of the vehicle.
* Enter any relevant details about safety incidents, including dates and times.
* If a vehicle is not currently in use, mark it as Out of Use.
* If a signature is required, ensure the person signing is authorized to do so.
