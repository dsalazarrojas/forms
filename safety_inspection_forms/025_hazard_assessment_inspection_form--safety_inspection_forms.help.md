# hazard_assessment_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess and document the hazards found during an inspection. It aims to identify the type of hazard, its risk level, and the necessary measures to mitigate or control it. The form also allows for the observation of specific areas and notes, as well as the creation of an action plan for mitigation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the introduction page for context and background information.
2. Select the type of hazard found during the inspection (High Risk, Moderate Risk, Low Risk).
3. Choose the specific risk level associated with the hazard (High, Moderate, Low).
4. Describe the control measures taken or recommended for the hazard.
5. Provide any mitigation measures for the hazard.
6. Identify the risk factors contributing to the hazard (Physical, Chemical, Biological, Ergonomic, Psychosocial).
7. Enter the area observed during the inspection.
8. Take notes for the observed area.
9. Create an action plan for mitigation.
10. Specify next steps for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **hazard_type**: Select the type of hazard found during the inspection. This could be High Risk, Moderate Risk, or Low Risk.
* **risk_level**: Choose the specific risk level associated with the hazard. This could be High, Moderate, or Low.
* **control_measures**: Describe the control measures taken or recommended for the hazard. This could include procedures, equipment, or other measures to minimize the risk.
* **mitigation_measures**: Provide any additional measures to mitigate or reduce the hazard. This could include training, equipment, or other actions to reduce the risk.
* **risk_factors**: Identify the risk factors contributing to the hazard. This could include Physical hazards (e.g., falls, electrical shock), Chemical hazards (e.g., chemical spills, chemical exposure), Biological hazards (e.g., biohazards, disease transmission), Ergonomic hazards (e.g., poor lighting, noise exposure), or Psychosocial hazards (e.g., stress, burnout).
* **observation_area**: Enter the specific area observed during the inspection. This could include location, floor number, or room name.
* **observation_notes**: Take notes on the observed area, including any observations, findings, or concerns.
* **action_plan**: Create an action plan for mitigation, including specific steps, tasks, or deadlines.
* **next_steps**: Specify next steps for follow-up, including any further actions, inspections, or reviews.
 
## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the control measures, mitigation measures, and risk factors.
* Use specific examples or scenarios to illustrate your answers.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure accuracy and relevance.
