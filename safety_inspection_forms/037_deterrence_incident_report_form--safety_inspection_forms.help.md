# Deterrence Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Deterrence Incident Report Form is a tool used to document and track safety incidents. It collects information about the incident, its location, date, type, status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the details of the incident, including the date and location where it occurred.
2.  Select the type and subtype of the incident from the provided options.
3.  Enter the date you are reporting the incident.
4.  Select the status of the incident.
5.  Add any additional details or notes about the incident.
6.  Attach any relevant documents or photos to support your report.
7.  Enter the name of the officer investigating the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of incident from the provided options. This field helps categorize and track incidents.
*   **Date Of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
*   **Location Of Incident** (`location_of_incident`, text, required): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the incident from the provided options.
*   **Incident Subtype** (`incident_subtype`, select_multiple): Select any subtypes of the incident from the provided options.
*   **Date Of Report** (`date_of_report`, date): Enter the date you are reporting the incident.
*   **Incident Responsible** (`incident_responsible`, text): Enter the name of the person responsible for the incident.
*   **Incident Category** (`incident_category`, text): Enter any additional details or categories about the incident.
*   **Location Of Incident Location Details** (`location_of_incident_location_details`, text): Enter any additional details about the location of the incident.
*   **Incident Notes** (`incident_notes`, text): Add any additional notes or comments about the incident.
*   **Incident Attachments** (`incident_attachments`, note): Attach any relevant documents or photos to support your report.
*   **Incident Investigating Officer** (`incident_investigating_officer`, text): Enter the name of the officer investigating the incident.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
*   Use the provided options for fields like incident type and status to ensure consistent tracking and reporting.
*   Attach relevant documents or photos to support your report and provide additional context.
