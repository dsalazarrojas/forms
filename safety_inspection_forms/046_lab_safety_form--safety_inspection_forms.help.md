# Lab Safety Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lab Safety Form is designed to gather information about a laboratory inspection, including contact information, facility details, and safety concerns. This form is used to assess and improve the safety conditions of the laboratory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the contact information of the person filling out the form (Contact Information).
2.  Enter the name and address of the facility being inspected (Facility Name and Facility Address).
3.  Enter the name and contact details of the person responsible for the facility (Contact Person).
4.  Enter the name of the safety inspector (Safety Inspector).
5.  Select the date and time when the inspection took place (Date of Visit and Time of Visit).
6.  Assess the current condition of the facility (Facility Condition).
7.  List any hazards or safety concerns identified during the inspection (Hazards Identified).
8.  Document any actions taken to address the hazards (Actions Taken).
9.  Provide any additional information or follow-up actions required (Follow Up).
10.  Schedule the next visit or inspection (Next Visit).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, `required`): Enter the contact details of the person filling out the form.
*   **Facility Name** (`Facility Name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
*   **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name and contact details of the person responsible for the facility.
*   **Facility Address** (`Facility Address`, `text`, `required`): Enter the physical address of the facility.
*   **Safety Inspector** (`Safety Inspector`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the safety inspector or the person conducting the inspection.
*   **Date of Visit** (`Date of Visit`, `date`, `required`): Select the date when the inspection took place.
*   **Time of Visit** (`Time of Visit`, `time`, `required`): Select the time when the inspection occurred.
*   **Facility Condition** (`Facility Condition`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the current condition of the facility from the provided options.
*   **Hazards Identified** (`Hazards Identified`, `select_multiple`, `required`): List any hazards or safety concerns identified during the inspection.
*   **Actions Taken** (`Actions Taken`, `text`, `required`): Document any actions taken to address the hazards.
*   **Follow Up** (`Follow Up`, `text`, `required`): Provide any additional information or follow-up actions required.
*   **Next Visit** (`Next Visit`, `date`, `required`): Schedule the next visit or inspection.
