# Overcurrent protection checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Overcurrent Protection Checklist" form is designed to help you verify the status of overcurrent protection systems in facilities. This form ensures that critical systems are functioning properly, and provides a quick and easy way to assess their condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the facility name in the "facility_name" field.
2. Select the current status of the overcurrent protection system in the "overcurrent_protection_status" field. Choose "Active" if the system is currently functioning, or "Inactive" if it is not.
3. Record the date of your inspection in the "date" field. Use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter the time of your inspection in the "time" field. Use 24-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
5. If you're reporting this information for another technician, enter their name in the "technician_name" field. If no technician is responsible, leave this field blank.
6. In the "comments" field, add any additional information or notes about your inspection.
7. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **facility_name**: Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
* **overcurrent_protection_status**: Select "Active" if the overcurrent protection system is functioning correctly, or "Inactive" if it is not.
* **date**: Record the date of your inspection in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **time**: Enter the time of your inspection in 24-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **technician_name**: If this information is for a specific technician, enter their name here.
* **comments**: Add any additional notes or comments about your inspection.
