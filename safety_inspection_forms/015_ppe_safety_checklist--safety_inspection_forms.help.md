# PPE Safety Checklist - Safety Inspection Forms - Help Guide

## Purpose
The PPE Safety Checklist - Safety Inspection Forms is designed to ensure that Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) used on site is in good condition and properly utilized. This form helps safety inspectors to assess the type, condition, storage, and maintenance of PPE, which is crucial for employee protection and compliance with safety regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1.  Select all applicable types of PPE equipment used during the safety inspection.
2.  Check the condition of the PPE equipment, ensuring it is in good working order (Option A) or if it is not (Option B).
3.  Confirm whether the PPE equipment is properly worn (Option A) or not (Option B).
4.  Enter the label for the PPE equipment used, as this is a required field.
5.  Confirm whether the PPE equipment is stored in a clean, dry, and secure location, ensuring it is not exposed to potential hazards.
6.  Check if the PPE equipment is cleaned and sanitized regularly, following the recommended guidelines.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What type of PPE equipment used?
*   **What type of PPE equipment used?** (`ppe_equipment_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is used to select all types of PPE equipment used during the safety inspection. Select all applicable options from the provided list to ensure accurate recording of equipment types.

### 2. Is the PPE equipment in good condition?
*   **Is the PPE equipment in good condition?** (`ppe_equipment_condition`, checkbox, required): This question checks if the equipment is in good working order or if it is not. Choose Option A to confirm that the equipment is in good condition, or Option B to indicate otherwise.

### 3. Is the PPE equipment properly worn?
*   **Is the PPE equipment properly worn?** (`ppe_equipment_worn`, checkbox, required): This field is used to confirm whether the PPE equipment is properly worn during the safety inspection. Select Option A to confirm proper usage or Option B to indicate otherwise.

### 4. What is the PPE equipment label?
*   **What is the PPE equipment label?** (`ppe_equipment_label`, text, required): Enter the label or ID for the PPE equipment used. This helps in identifying the specific equipment being used.

### 5. Is the PPE equipment properly stored?
*   **Is the PPE equipment properly stored?** (`ppe_equipment_storage`, checkbox, required): This field is used to confirm that the PPE equipment is stored in a clean, dry, and secure location. Select Option A to confirm proper storage or Option B to indicate otherwise.

### 6. Is the PPE equipment cleaned and sanitized regularly?
*   **Is the PPE equipment cleaned and sanitized regularly?** (`ppe_equipment_cleaning`, checkbox, required): Confirm whether the PPE equipment is cleaned and sanitized regularly, following the recommended guidelines. Select Option A to confirm proper maintenance or Option B to indicate otherwise.

## Tips
- Always select all applicable types of PPE equipment used during the safety inspection.
- Ensure accurate recording of PPE equipment condition, storage, and maintenance.
- Enter the correct label for the PPE equipment used.
- Regularly clean and sanitize the PPE equipment to maintain its effectiveness and longevity.
- Follow recommended guidelines for cleaning and storing PPE equipment.
