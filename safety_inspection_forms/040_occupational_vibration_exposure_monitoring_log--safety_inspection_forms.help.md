# occupational_vibration_exposure_monitoring_log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and record employee exposure to occupational vibration. The goal of this form is to gather information about the type and frequency of monitoring, location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter general information about the employee, including their ID (if applicable), name, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide specific details about the job being performed, such as tasks and equipment used.
3. Document the level of vibration exposure, including the type and duration of exposure.
4. Specify the location where monitoring took place.
5. Record the date and time of monitoring.
6. If applicable, indicate the monitoring frequency (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
7. Add any additional notes about the monitoring process or findings.
8. Finally, select the person who assigned this task and submitted the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (1, `General Info`, required/optional): Enter basic information about the employee, such as their ID (if applicable) and name.
* **Employee Info** (2, `employee_info`, required/optional): Enter specific details about the employee being monitored.
* **Job Details** (3, `job_details`, required/optional): Describe the tasks being performed and equipment used during the monitoring.
* **Vibration Exposure** (4, `vibration_exposure`, required/optional): Record the level and type of vibration exposure.
* **Monitoring Location** (5, `monitoring_location`, required/optional): Specify the exact location where monitoring took place.
* **Date/Time** (6, `date_time`, required/optional): Enter the date and time of monitoring.
* **Monitoring Frequency** (7, `monitoring_frequency`, required/optional): Indicate the frequency of monitoring (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.).
* **Notes** (8, `notes`, required/optional): Add any additional information about the monitoring process or findings.
* **Assigned Tool** (9, `assigned_tool`, required/optional): Enter the tool or equipment used for monitoring.
* **Submitted By** (10, `submitted_by`, required): Select the person who assigned and submitted this report.
