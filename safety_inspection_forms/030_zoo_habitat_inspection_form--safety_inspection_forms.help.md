# Zoo Habitat Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data on the condition and well-being of animal habitats in zoos.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure that you are submitting data for a single animal habitat at a time.
2. Fill out each field as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
3. Use the options provided for each field to ensure consistency across submissions.
4. If a field is not relevant or applicable for a particular habitat, please leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Animal Habitat Name** (`animal_habitats`, text, required): Enter the name of the animal habitat being inspected.
* **Animal Species** (`animal_species`, select_one, optional): Select the species of animal housed in the habitat. If not applicable, leave blank.
* **Enclosure Type** (`enclosure_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of enclosure for the animal habitat. Options are: Open, Enclosed, Indoor.
* **Habitat Condition** (`habitat_condition`, text, optional): Describe the condition of the habitat, including any notable features or concerns.
* **Facility Condition** (`facility_condition`, select_one, optional): Assess the overall condition of the facility, choosing from: Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Animal Feeding Status** (`animal_feeding_status`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if the animals are currently being fed, not fed, or if feeding is in progress.
* **Facility Care Status** (`facility_care_status`, select_one, optional): Assess the overall care of the facility, choosing from: Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Observation Status** (`observation_status`, select_multiple, optional): Note if observations or checks have been conducted, or if not observed. Options are: Observed, Not Observed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the habitat or facility condition.
* **Inspection Date** (`animal_habitat_check_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (`animal_habitat_check_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the inspector.
* **Inspector Organization** (`inspector_organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the form was submitted.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out each field accurately and consistently.
* If you are unsure about a particular field, please consult with your supervisor or colleague.
* If you need to report any issues or concerns, please provide clear and detailed descriptions.
