# Pile Driving Compliance Affidavit - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pile Driving Compliance Affidavit form is used to document the details of pile driving work performed on a construction site, ensuring that the work is conducted in compliance with relevant regulations and standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form correctly, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Pile Driving Work Details on Page 1.
2. Select the Signed Attestations relevant to the work performed.
3. Enter the Date and Time of Completion.
4. Choose who completed the work.
5. Enter the Site ID.
6. Add any Inspector Comments.
7. Sign the Client Signature and Date of Signature fields.
8. Sign the Inspector Signature and enter the Date of Signature.
9. Enter the Client Name, Inspector Name, and Client Contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pile Driving Work Details** (`pile_driving_work_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the pile driving work performed, including any relevant details or notes.
* **Signed Attestations** (`signed_attestations`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all relevant attestations that apply to the work performed.
* **Date of Completion** (`date_of_completion`, date, optional): Enter the date the pile driving work was completed.
* **Time of Completion** (`time_of_completion`, time, optional): Enter the time the pile driving work was completed.
* **Completed By** (`completed_by`, select_one, optional): Choose who completed the pile driving work.
* **Site ID** (`site_id`, number, optional): Enter the site ID related to the pile driving work.
* **Inspector Comments** (`inspector_comments`, note, optional): Add any relevant comments or notes from the inspector regarding compliance.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, optional): Sign the client's signature.
* **Inspector Signature** (`inspector_signature`, text, optional): Sign the inspector's signature.
* **Date of Signature** (`date_of_signature`, date, optional): Enter the date of signature.
* **Time of Signature** (`time_of_signature`, time, optional): Enter the time of signature.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the client's name.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, optional): Enter the inspector's name.
* **Client Contact** (`client_contact`, text, optional): Enter the client's contact information.
