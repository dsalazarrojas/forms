# Step Hazard Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Step Hazard Assessment form is used to identify and assess potential hazards present on a given step of a process. This form is used to collect information about hazards found, their level of risk, and recommended actions to mitigate them. The goal is to identify and prioritize hazards to ensure a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each field as accurately and truthfully as possible.
2. Identify the hazards present on the step of the process being assessed.
3. Select the correct hazards from the list provided.
4. Rate the level of risk associated with each hazard using the hazard rating fields.
5. Describe any photos taken during the inspection.
6. Provide any recommendations for mitigating the identified hazards.
7. Enter the date and time the inspection was completed.
8. Enter any comments or notes about the assessment.
9. Attach photos of any hazards or mitigation methods.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Step 1 (step_hazard_assessment_1)**: This field allows you to enter a brief description of the step being assessed.
* **Hazards Found (hazards_found)**: Select any hazards present on the step from the provided list. These may include things like electrical hazards, chemical hazards, or physical hazards.
* **Hazard Rating 1 (hazard_rating_1)**: Rate the level of risk associated with the hazard selected in the previous field. Scale: 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
* **Hazard Rating 2 (hazard_rating_2)**: Rate the level of risk associated with the second selected hazard. Scale: 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
* **Hazard Rating 3 (hazard_rating_3)**: Rate the level of risk associated with the third selected hazard. Scale: 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
* **Recommendations (recommendations)**: Provide any recommendations for mitigating the identified hazards.
* **Photos (photos)**: Select any photos taken during the inspection that relate to the hazards identified.
* **Completed (completed)**: Enter the date and time the assessment was completed.
* **Date Completed (date_completed)**: Enter the date the assessment was completed.
* **Time Completed (time_completed)**: Enter the time the assessment was completed.
* **Assigned To (assigned_to)**: Enter the name and title of the person assigned to mitigate the hazard.
* **Inspector Name (inspector_name)**: Enter the name of the inspector who completed the assessment.
* **Inspector Signature (inspector_signature)**: Provide the signature of the inspector who completed the assessment.
* **Date Inspected (date_inspected)**: Enter the date the inspection was conducted.
* **Time Inspected (time_inspected)**: Enter the time the inspection was conducted.
* **Facility Name (facility_name)**: Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
* **Location (location)**: Enter the location where the inspection was conducted.
* **Comments (comments)**: Enter any additional comments about the assessment.
* **Photos Description (photos_description)**: Enter a brief description of any photos attached.
* **Hazard Frequency (hazard_frequency)**: Enter the frequency of the hazard.
* **Hazard Probability (hazard_probability)**: Enter the probability of the hazard.
* **Hazard Risk Level (hazard_risk_level)**: Enter the risk level of the hazard.
* **Action Planned (action_planned)**: Enter any actions planned to mitigate the hazard.
* **Follow-up Action (follow_up_action)**: Enter any follow-up actions planned.
* **Follow-up Frequency (follow_up_frequency)**: Enter the frequency of follow-up actions.

This form is used to identify and assess potential hazards in a step of a process. Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully to ensure a safe working environment.
