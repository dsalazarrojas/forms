# Employee Safety Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents that have occurred during the workday. It is designed to collect information about the incident, including its details, description, type, location, and other relevant information. This information will help management and safety teams identify potential hazards, track near-miss incidents, and take corrective measures to prevent future incidents.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Incident Details" field and enter a brief description of the incident.
2. In the "Description" field, provide a detailed account of what happened.
3. Select the type of incident that occurred from the "Type" field. This could be an accident, near miss, or other.
4. Enter the date and time of the incident in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
5. In the "Location" field, describe where the incident occurred.
6. Enter any injuries or illnesses that resulted from the incident in the "Injuries" and "Illnesses" fields.
7. Enter any property damage that occurred as a result of the incident in the "Property Damage" field.
8. If there is any other information you want to provide about the incident, enter it in the "Other" field.
9. If you have any supporting files or documents related to the incident, you can upload them in the "Supporting Files" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details**: Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Description**: Provide a detailed account of what happened.
* **Type**: Select the type of incident that occurred (Accident, Near Miss, or Other).
* **Date**: Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
* **Time**: Enter the time at which the incident occurred.
* **Location**: Describe where the incident occurred.
* **Injuries**: Enter any injuries that resulted from the incident (None, Minor, or Serious).
* **Illnesses**: Enter any illnesses that resulted from the incident (None, Yes).
* **Property Damage**: Enter the amount of property damage that occurred.
* **Other**: Enter any other information about the incident.
* **Supporting Files**: Upload any supporting files or documents related to the incident.

## Tips
* Make sure to be as detailed as possible when providing information about the incident.
* If you are unsure about the type of incident, select "Other" and provide a brief explanation.
* If you have any supporting files or documents, upload them to help with the investigation.
* If you have any concerns or questions about the incident, contact your supervisor or safety team.
