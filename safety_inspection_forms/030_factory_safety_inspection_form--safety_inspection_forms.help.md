# Factory Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory Safety Inspection Form is designed to collect information about a factory's safety protocols and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and fill in the required information.
2. Answer each field with the requested details.
3. Ensure that the submitted information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Factory Information**:
	+ Label: Factory Information
	+ Type: Text Field
	+ Required: Optional
	+ Explanation: This field is for providing general information about the factory, such as its name, address, or contact details.
* **Safety Inspections**:
	+ Label: Safety Inspections
	+ Type: Text Field
	+ Required: Optional
	+ Explanation: Here, report any recent or upcoming safety inspections, including the date, scope, and outcome.
* **Safety Risks**:
	+ Label: Safety Risks
	+ Type: Text Field
	+ Required: Optional
	+ Explanation: In this field, list any identified safety risks or hazards present in the factory, and describe the mitigating measures taken to address them.
* **Submission Information**:
	+ Label: Submission Information
	+ Type: Text Field
	+ Required: Optional
	+ Explanation: Provide any relevant submission details, such as the submission date, submitted by, or comments.
* **Signature**:
	+ Label: Signature
	+ Type: Text Field
	+ Required: Optional
	+ Explanation: This field is for optional signature or verification, but it's not a required field.
