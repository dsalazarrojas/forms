# COVID 19 Ambulance Check Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to inspect an ambulance's condition and readiness for emergency services. It will help ensure that the vehicle is equipped and prepared for safe and effective medical care, especially during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please complete all required fields, indicated by a ***, as accurately and thoroughly as possible.**
2.  Review each section and answer all questions carefully, ensuring that you are checking all relevant aspects of the ambulance's condition.
3.  If any issues or concerns are found, take notes and provide details in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **EMS Agency Name** (`ems_agency_name`): Enter your organization's name, as this will help identify your agency and provide context for the inspection.
* **Unit Number** (`unit_number`): Type the ambulance's unique identifier, which distinguishes it from other vehicles in your agency.
* **Check Date** (`check_date`): Enter the current date to track when the inspection was conducted.
* **Check Time** (`check_time`): Enter the time of the inspection to record when it was done.
* **EMT Name** (`emt_name`): Type the name of the EMT performing the inspection.
* **EMT ID Number** (`emt_id`): Enter the ID number of the EMT performing the inspection.
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`): If applicable, type the name of the partner on duty.
* **Shift** (`shift`): Select the current shift of the EMT performing the inspection.
* **-- Vehicle Exterior --**: Review the exterior of the vehicle, checking for damage, cleanliness, and functionality of lights, sirens, and tires.
	+ **Exterior Condition** (`exterior_condition`): Assess the overall condition of the vehicle's exterior, considering body damage, cleanliness, and overall appearance.
	+ **Lights and Sirens** (`lights_sirens`): Check if the lights and sirens are functional and in good working condition.
	+ **Tires** (`tires`): Inspect the condition and pressure of the tires, ensuring they are in good condition for safe operation.
	+ **Fuel Level** (`fuel_level`): Record the current fuel level of the vehicle, indicating whether it needs refueling or not.
* **-- Vehicle Interior --**: Inspect the interior of the vehicle, checking the cleanliness and functionality of the cabin and patient compartment.
	+ **Cab Cleanliness** (`cab_cleanliness`): Assess the overall cleanliness of the cab area, considering the condition and cleanliness of the interior.
	+ **Patient Compartment** (`patient_compartment`): Evaluate the condition and cleanliness of the patient compartment, ensuring it is ready for patient care.
	+ **Stretcher Condition** (`stretcher_condition`): Check the functionality of the stretcher, considering any issues or concerns.
* **-- Medical Equipment --**: Review all medical equipment in the vehicle, checking for functionality, availability, and expiration dates.
	+ **Oxygen System** (`oxygen_system`): Check the supply and functionality of the oxygen system, ensuring it is fully stocked and ready for use.
	+ **Bag Valve Mask** (`bag_valve_mask`): Assess the availability and functionality of the BVM, considering any issues or concerns.
	+ **Suction Unit** (`suction_unit`): Evaluate the condition and functionality of the suction unit, considering any issues or concerns.
	+ **Defibrillator Monitor** (`defibrillator`): Check the AED or manual defibrillator, ensuring it is ready and charged for use.
	+ **Airway Equipment** (`airway_equipment`): Inspect the availability and functionality of intubation supplies, ensuring they are fully stocked and ready for use.
	+ **IV Supplies** (`iv_supplies`): Review the availability and condition of IV supplies, ensuring they are fully stocked and ready for use.
	+ **Medications** (`medications`): Check the expiration dates and availability of medications, ensuring they are present and valid for use.
* **-- COVID-19 Specific Checks --**: Review the ambulance's readiness for COVID-19-specific protocols.
	+ **PPE Supply** (`ppe_supply`): Assess the availability and functionality of PPE, considering masks, gloves, gowns, and other essential items.
	+ **N95 Masks** (`n95_masks`): Record the quantity of N95 masks available, ensuring they are sufficient for safe operation.
	+ **Disinfectant Supplies** (`disinfectant_supplies`): Check the availability and condition of disinfectant supplies, ensuring they are fully stocked and ready for use.
* **-- Documentation --**: Review the required documents and forms, ensuring they are present and complete.
	+ **Run Report Forms** (`run_reports`): Check the availability and condition of PCR forms, ensuring they are fully stocked and ready for use.
* **-- Overall Readiness --**: Evaluate the overall readiness and condition of the vehicle, considering the entire inspection report.
* **Radio Communication** (`radio_functional`): Check the functionality of radio communication, ensuring it is fully functional and ready for use.
* **GPS System** (`gps_functional`): Evaluate the condition and functionality of the GPS system, ensuring it is fully functional and ready for use.
* **Issues Found** (`issues_found`): Describe any issues or concerns found during the inspection.
* **Corrective Action Taken** (`corrective_action`): Record any corrective actions taken to address issues or concerns.
* **Vehicle Status** (`vehicle_status`): Evaluate the overall readiness and condition of the vehicle, considering the entire inspection report.
* **Supervisor Notification Required** (`supervisor_notification`): Indicate if the supervisor needs to be notified about any issues or concerns.
* **EMT Signature** (`emt_signature`): Type the full name of the EMT performing the inspection.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`): Type the full name of the supervisor, if applicable.
