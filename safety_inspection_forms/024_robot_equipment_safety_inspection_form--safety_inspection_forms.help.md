# Robot Equipment Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record information about the safety inspection of robot equipment. It collects details on the equipment's model number, manufacturer, year model, location installed, description, hazard rating, hazard identified, and other essential details. This form helps ensure that the equipment is regularly inspected and any safety issues are addressed promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide basic information about the robot equipment, including its model number, manufacturer, year model, location installed, and description.
2. Answer the question about whether any safety hazards were identified during the inspection.
3. If a hazard was identified, describe the action taken to address it.
4. Select the person responsible for assigning tasks related to the equipment and the person who completed the inspection.
5. Provide a review date and time (if applicable).
6. Select the person who reviewed the inspection and provide any comments.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: Briefly introduce yourself and the purpose of the form.
* **Machinery Model**: Enter the model number of the robot equipment.
* **Inspection Frequency**: Select how frequently the equipment is inspected (e.g., every day, week, month).
* **Last Inspection Date**: Record the date of the last inspection (if applicable).
* **Next Inspection Date**: Record the date of the next inspection (if applicable).
* **Safety Hazard Identified**: Indicate if any safety hazards were identified during the inspection.
* **Action Taken to Address Hazard**: Describe the steps taken to address any identified hazards.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the inspection.
* **Assigned To**: Select the person responsible for assigning tasks related to the equipment.
* **Completed By**: Select the person who completed the inspection.
* **Review Date**: Record the date of the review (if applicable).
* **Review Time**: Record the time of the review (if applicable).
* **Reviewed By**: Select the person who reviewed the inspection.
* **Review Comment**: Add any comments from the reviewer.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the inspection.
* **Signature**: Record your signature.
* **Robot Equipment Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the equipment.
* **Robot Equipment Model Number**: Enter the model number of the equipment.
* **Robot Equipment Manufacturer**: Enter the manufacturer of the equipment.
* **Robot Equipment Year Model**: Enter the year model of the equipment.
* **Robot Equipment Location Installed**: Describe the location where the equipment is installed.
* **Robot Equipment Description**: Describe the equipment's purpose and functionality.
* **Robot Equipment Hazard Rating**: Rate the equipment's hazard level (if applicable).
* **Robot Equipment Hazard**: Identify any safety hazards associated with the equipment.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the equipment.
