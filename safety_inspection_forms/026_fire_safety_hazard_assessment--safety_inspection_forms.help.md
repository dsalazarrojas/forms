# Fire Safety Hazard Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help individuals responsible for maintaining fire safety in a specific location to quickly and efficiently gather information about on-site risk reviews, hazard identifications, risk levels, risk assessments, risk control measures, and recommendations related to fire safety hazards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions carefully and honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Complete all the fields that apply to your specific situation.
3. If you're unsure about any of the questions, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **On Site Risk Reviews** (`on_site_risk_reviews`, text, optional): Describe the on-site risk reviews that have been conducted, including any relevant dates, times, and locations.
  - This field is for noting any reviews or assessments that have been done on-site regarding fire safety risks.
  - Be as detailed as possible, but keep it concise.

- **Hazard Identifications** (`hazard_identifications`, text, optional): Identify any potential fire safety hazards on-site, including their locations and descriptions.
  - This field is for listing any hazards that could pose a risk to people on-site.
  - Be specific about where these hazards are located and what makes them hazardous.

- **Risk Levels** (`risk_levels`, select_multiple, optional): Select the risk levels for each identified hazard:
  - This field is for assessing the level of risk posed by each hazard.
  - You can select one or more of the following options: Low, Moderate, High.

- **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, select_one, optional): Assess the overall risk level for each hazard:
  - This field is for evaluating the overall risk level of each hazard.
  - You can select one of the following options: Low, Moderate, High.

- **Risk Control Measures** (`risk_control_measures`, text, optional): Describe any risk control measures that are in place, including their effectiveness and limitations:
  - This field is for noting any control measures taken to mitigate the risk of fire hazards.
  - Be specific about what measures are in place and how effective they are.

- **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Make any recommendations for additional risk control measures that could be taken:
  - This field is for suggesting further control measures that could be taken to address identified hazards.
  - Be specific and provide supporting evidence or reasoning for your suggestions.
