# Pre Pour Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about the conditions of foundation, road, and sidewalk areas on a construction site. This data will be used to identify potential safety risks and inform corrective actions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Go through each area (foundation, road, and sidewalk) and answer the questions based on your observations.
2. For each question, choose the most accurate answer from the provided options.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Foundation Areas

* **Foundation Areas**: Describe the areas where you will be inspecting.
* **Foundation Surface Type** (`Concrete`, `Aggregate`, `Steel`, `Brick`, `Gravel`): Select the material type of the foundation surface.
* **Foundation Surface Condition** (`Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): Select the condition of the foundation surface.
* **Foundation Surface Damage** (`None`, `Hairline Cracks`, `Spalls`, `Settling`): Select any damage or issues observed on the foundation surface. (Required)
* **Foundation Surface Clearance** (Measurement in feet): Measure the clearance of the foundation surface from any nearby obstacles. (Optional)
* **Foundation Surface Clearance (Inch)** (Measurement in inches): Measure the clearance of the foundation surface from any nearby obstacles in inches. (Optional)
* **Foundation Surface Levelness** (Measurement in feet): Measure the levelness of the foundation surface. (Optional)
* **Foundation Surface Levelness (Inch)** (Measurement in inches): Measure the levelness of the foundation surface in inches. (Optional)
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the foundation areas.
