# Aviation Safety Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aviation Safety Incident Form is used to report and document safety incidents that occur in the aviation industry. This form helps gather information that can be used to identify trends and patterns in safety incidents, which can inform training, procedures, and policies to prevent similar incidents in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information about the incident.
2. Be as thorough as possible in providing descriptions and details.
3. Select relevant options for incident type and contributing factors.
4. Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Person Reporting Information --**
	+ **Reporter Full Name**: Enter the full name of the person reporting the incident.
	+ **Reporter Email Address**: Enter the email address of the person reporting the incident.
	+ **Reporter Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the person reporting the incident (optional).
* **-- Incident Time and Location --**
	+ **Date of Incident**: Enter the date the incident occurred (YYYY-MM-DD format).
	+ **Time of Incident**: Enter the time the incident occurred in UTC or local time.
	+ **Location of Incident**: Enter the exact location where the incident occurred (airport, navaid, or lat/long).
* **-- Aircraft Details --**
	+ **Aircraft Tail Number**: Enter the tail number of the aircraft involved in the incident (e.g. N12345).
	+ **Aircraft Type**: Enter the type of aircraft involved in the incident (e.g. Boeing 777, Cessna 172).
* **Incident Severity Rating**
	+ Select the level of severity of the incident (Minor, Moderate, Major, Critical).
* **Type of Incident**
	+ Select all that apply from the list of options (Engine or Powerplant Issue, Avionics or System Failure, etc.).
* **Detailed Description of Incident**
	+ Provide a full account of what happened during the incident.
* **Potential Contributing Factors**
	+ Select all that apply from the list of options (Human Factors, Mechanical Failure, etc.).
* **Immediate Actions Taken**
	+ Describe the steps taken to resolve the situation.
* **Final Incident Outcome**
	+ Select the outcome of the incident (Normal Landing/Resumption, Emergency Landing/Diversion, etc.).
* **Recommendations for Prevention**
	+ Provide suggestions on how to prevent similar incidents in the future.

Note: Please fill out each field accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the form can effectively gather the necessary information to identify trends and patterns in safety incidents.
