# vehicle_reversing_safety_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect safety inspection data for vehicles during the reversing process. It's intended for specific vehicles, and the driver information is required for every single vehicle inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Vehicle ID.
2. Provide the Vehicle Number.
3. Describe the vehicle in the Vehicle Description field.
4. Select the Driver License from the options provided.
5. Enter the Driver Name and Driver Address.
6. Choose the Vehicle Type from the options provided.
7. Select the status for each of the 9 reversing checklist items.
8. Review and confirm the form before submission.
