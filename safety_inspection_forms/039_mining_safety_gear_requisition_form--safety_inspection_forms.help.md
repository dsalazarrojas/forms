# Mining Safety Gear Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request safety gear for mining operations. The goal is to ensure that all necessary safety equipment is requisitioned and approved before use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Submitter Name**, which is your name and title (e.g., "John Doe, Safety Officer").
2. Describe the **Equipment Requested**, including the type and quantity of gear needed.
3. Indicate your department and if the equipment is needed for an in-department or off-department location.
4. Check if your **Department** needs the equipment.
5. Check if the equipment should be sent to multiple **Locations**.
6. Enter a detailed **Equipment Description** of the gear requested.
7. Confirm if your **Supervisor Approval** is required.
8. Fill in your **Submitted By** name and title (e.g., "John Doe, Safety Officer").
9. Select the **Submission Date** and **Submission Time** for the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Submitter Name
* **Submitter Name** (text, required): Your name and title (e.g., "John Doe, Safety Officer").

### 2. Equipment Requested
* **Equipment Requested** (text, required): Describe the type and quantity of safety gear needed.

### 3. Quantity Requested
* **Quantity Requested** (number, optional): Enter the number of units of the equipment needed.

### 4. Department
* **Department** (select, required): Check if your department needs the equipment.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'

### 5. Location
* **Location** (select, required): Check if the equipment should be sent to multiple locations.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'

### 6. Equipment Description
* **Equipment Description** (text, required): Enter a detailed description of the equipment.

### 7. Supervisor Approval
* **Supervisor Approval** (select, required): Check if supervisor approval is required for the equipment.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'

### 8. Submitted By
* **Submitted By** (text, required): Fill in your name and title (e.g., "John Doe, Safety Officer").

### 9. Submission Date
* **Submission Date** (date, required): Select the date of the submission.

### 10. Submission Time
* **Submission Time** (time, required): Select the submission time.

### 11. Department Options (optional)
* **Department Options** (select, optional): If your department needs the equipment.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'

### 12. Location Options (optional)
* **Location Options** (select, optional): If the equipment should be sent to multiple locations.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'

### 13. Equipment Requested Options (optional)
* **Equipment Requested Options** (select, optional): If the equipment is needed.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'

### 14. Submitter Name Options (optional)
* **Submitter Name Options** (select, optional): If you have options for your name.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'

Note: If you're unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or safety officer for clarification.
