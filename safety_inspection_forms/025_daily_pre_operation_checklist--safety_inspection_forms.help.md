# Daily Pre Operation Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Pre Operation Checklist form is a safety inspection tool used to evaluate and document the status of critical areas and equipment before operation begins. Its primary goal is to identify potential safety hazards and ensure a risk-free working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
2. Select the appropriate options for each field based on the actual state of equipment and areas inspected.
3. Provide thorough and detailed responses for text fields.
4. Verify your submission by reviewing the form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Inspections** (`equipment_inspection`): 
  * Select "Yes" if the equipment inspected meets the required standards and is ready for use.
  * Select "No" if the equipment is not inspected or does not meet the required standards.

* **Critical Areas Inspection** (`critical_areas_inspection`): 
  * Choose the correct option based on the status of the critical areas inspected.

* **Pre-Operation Checklist** (`pre_operation_checklist`): 
  * Select "Yes" if the pre-operation checklist has been completed.
  * Select "No" if the pre-operation checklist is incomplete or not started.

* **Pre-Operation Checklist Review** (`pre_operation_checklist_review`): 
  * Enter a detailed review of the pre-operation checklist, mentioning any issues or discrepancies found.

* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`): 
  * Enter any additional comments or observations regarding the safety status of the area.

* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): 
  * Enter the date of submission in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): 
  * Select the correct option indicating who submitted the form.

* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`): 
  * Enter the time of submission.

* **Form Completion** (`form_completion`): 
  * Select "Complete" if the form has been finished accurately and thoroughly.

* **Notes** (`notes`): 
  * Enter any additional notes or comments about the form completion.

* **Submitter** (`submitter`): 
  * Select the correct option indicating who submitted the form.

Please ensure you've reviewed the form carefully and completed it accurately to maintain a safe working environment.
