# Crash Cart Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Crash Cart Checklist form is designed to ensure the safety features of the crash cart are properly inspected and maintained.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Select the Unit Number of the Crash Cart

* Enter the unit number of the crash cart being inspected.

### Step 2: Specify the Location of the Crash Cart

* Identify the location of the crash cart.

### Step 3: Identify the Contents of the Crash Cart

* Specify the contents of the crash cart.

### Step 4: Check the Condition of the Batteries

* Check the condition of the batteries on the crash cart.

### Step 5: Assess the Level of Oxygen (O2)

* Select the level of O2 on the crash cart.
  - High: The O2 level is high.
  - Low: The O2 level is low.

### Step 6: Check the Age of the Batteries

* Check whether the batteries on the crash cart are old or new.
  - Yes: The batteries are old.
  - No: The batteries are new.

### Step 7: Check the Age of Other Equipment

* Check whether other equipment on the crash cart is old or new.
  - Yes: The equipment is old.
  - No: The equipment is new.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **unit_number (Text, Required):** Enter the unit number of the crash cart.
* **crashCartLocation (Text, Required):** Identify the location of the crash cart.
* **crashCartContents (Text, Required):** Specify the contents of the crash cart.
* **crashCartBatteries (Text, Required):** Check the condition of the batteries on the crash cart.
* **crashCartO2 (Text, Required):** Select the level of O2 on the crash cart.
  - High: The O2 level is high.
  - Low: The O2 level is low.
* **crashCartBatteriesAge (Select Multiple, Required):** Check whether the batteries on the crash cart are old or new.
  - Yes: The batteries are old.
  - No: The batteries are new.
* **crashCartO2Age (Select Multiple, Required):** Check whether the O2 on the crash cart is old or new.
  - Yes: The O2 is old.
  - No: The O2 is new.
* **crashCartBelt (Text, Required):** Check the condition of the belts on the crash cart.
* **crashCartOtherEquipment (Text, Required):** Identify other equipment on the crash cart.
* **crashCartBeltAge (Select Multiple, Required):** Check whether the belts on the crash cart are old or new.
  - Yes: The belts are old.
  - No: The belts are new.
* **crashCartOtherEquipmentAge (Select Multiple, Required):** Check whether other equipment on the crash cart is old or new.
  - Yes: The equipment is old.
  - No: The equipment is new.
* **crashCartLocationAdequate (Select One, Required):** Assess whether the location of the crash cart is adequate.
  - Yes: The location is adequate.
  - No: The location is not adequate.
* **crashCartContentsAdequate (Select One, Required):** Assess whether the contents of the crash cart are adequate.
  - Yes: The contents are adequate.
  - No: The contents are not adequate.
* **crashCartBatteriesAdequate (Select One, Required):** Assess whether the batteries on the crash cart are adequate.
  - Yes: The batteries are adequate.
  - No: The batteries are not adequate.
* **crashCartO2Adequate (Select One, Required):** Assess whether the O2 on the crash cart is adequate.
  - Yes: The O2 is adequate.
  - No: The O2 is not adequate.
* **crashCartBeltAdequate (Select One, Required):** Assess whether the belts on the crash cart are adequate.
  - Yes: The belts are adequate.
  - No: The belts are not adequate.
* **crashCartOtherEquipmentAdequate (Select One, Required):** Assess whether other equipment on the crash cart is adequate.
  - Yes: The equipment is adequate.
  - No: The equipment is not adequate.
* **crashCartBatteriesExpiration (Date, Optional):** If applicable, select the date when the batteries on the crash cart expire.
* **crashCartO2Expiration (Date, Optional):** If applicable, select the date when the O2 on the crash cart expires.
* **crashCartBeltExpiration (Date, Optional):** If applicable, select the date when the belts on the crash cart expire.
* **crashCartOtherEquipmentExpiration (Date, Optional):** If applicable, select the date when other equipment on the crash cart expires.
* **crashCartBatteriesReplaced (Time, Optional):** If applicable, select the time when the batteries on the crash cart were last replaced.
* **crashCartO2Replaced (Time, Optional):** If applicable, select the time when the O2 on the crash cart was last replaced.
* **crashCartBeltReplaced (Time, Optional):** If applicable, select the time when the belts on the crash cart were last replaced.
* **crashCartOtherEquipmentReplaced (Time, Optional):** If applicable, select the time when other equipment on the crash cart were last replaced.

### Tips

* Make sure to carefully inspect the crash cart and answer all fields accurately.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the relevant personnel.
* The form is designed to be completed by authorized personnel.
* The form will be reviewed and signed by an authorized representative.
