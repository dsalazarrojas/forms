# Waterway Safety Work Method Statement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather safety-related information and documentation for waterway projects. It is intended to provide a comprehensive overview of the safety aspects of the project, including potential hazards, safety measures, and emergency procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the project details and ensure you have all relevant information before filling out the form.
2. Fill out the General Info page with any general information about the project, such as project location and start date.
3. Provide a detailed Work Method Statement, including any necessary safety precautions and procedures.
4. Describe any Safety Precautions taken to ensure a safe working environment.
5. Perform a Site Inspection and document any findings or concerns.
6. Create a Method Statement, detailing any necessary steps to follow for safe working practices.
7. Develop a Training Plan to ensure all workers are properly trained and equipped for the job.
8. Establish an Emergency Procedure in case of accidents or unexpected events.
9. Schedule and maintain regular Equipment Maintenance to prevent accidents or equipment failures.
10. Create an Incident Reporting process to document and address any incidents that occur.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info**: This section is for entering general information about the project, such as project location, start date, and any other relevant details.
* **Work Method Statement**: Describe the method and approach you will use for the waterway safety work, including any necessary safety precautions and procedures.
* **Safety Precautions**: Describe any safety measures you will take to ensure a safe working environment, including personal protective equipment (PPE), safety harnesses, and any other necessary safety equipment or protocols.
* **Site Inspection**: Conduct a thorough site inspection to identify any potential hazards, and document any findings or concerns.
* **Method Statement**: Create a detailed document that outlines the necessary steps to follow for safe working practices, including any specific procedures or protocols to be used.
* **Training Plan**: Develop a plan for training and equipping all workers with necessary safety skills and knowledge to complete the waterway safety work safely.
* **Emergency Procedure**: Establish a procedure in case of accidents or unexpected events, including emergency contact information and evacuation procedures.
* **Equipment Maintenance**: Schedule and maintain regular equipment maintenance to prevent accidents or equipment failures.
* **Incident Reporting**: Establish a process for documenting and addressing any incidents that occur during the project, including reporting and investigation procedures.
* **Safety Plan**: Create a comprehensive safety plan that outlines all safety measures, protocols, and procedures to be followed during the project.
* **Training Plan**: Develop a plan for training and equipping all workers with necessary safety skills and knowledge to complete the waterway safety work safely.
* **Risk Analysis**: Describe the potential risks and hazards that may occur during the project, and propose solutions or mitigation measures.

## Tips

* Consult with your project team or supervisor if you are unsure about any aspect of the form or project.
* Ensure that you have reviewed all previous submissions and relevant project documents to avoid duplication of effort and ensure consistency in your responses.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form, and provide as much detail as possible.
