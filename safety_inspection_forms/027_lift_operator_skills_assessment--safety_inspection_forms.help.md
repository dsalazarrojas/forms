# Lift Operator Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lift Operator Skills Assessment form is designed to evaluate a lift operator's skills and knowledge in various aspects of their work, including first aid training, fall protection, confined space entry, lift equipment inspection, general inspection requirements, and rescue procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. For select_one fields, choose the option that best applies to your situation.
3. For text fields, provide a brief description or answer in the space provided.
4. Make sure to answer all questions, even if you're unsure or don't know the answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Aid Training** (`first_aid_training`): This field asks if you have received first aid training. Answer 'True' if you have, 'False' if you haven't.
* **Fall Protection** (`fall_protection`): This field asks about your knowledge of fall protection methods. Choose the type of fall protection you are trained in or familiar with.
* **Confined Space Entry** (`confined_space_entry`): This field asks if you have received training in confined space entry procedures. Answer 'True' if you have, 'False' if you haven't.
* **Lift Equipment Inspection** (`lift_equipment_inspection`): This field asks if you inspect lift equipment daily, weekly, or monthly. Choose the frequency that best applies to your work.
* **General Inspection Requirements** (`general_inspection_requirements`): This field is for providing a brief description of any general inspection requirements you have to follow.
* **General Inspection Frequency** (`general_inspection_frequency`): This field asks how often you inspect the general areas of the workplace. Choose the frequency that best applies to your work.
* **Lift Operator License** (`lift_operators_license`): This field is for providing a brief description of any lift operator license you hold.
* **Lift Operator License Number** (`lift_operator_license_number`): This field is for providing the number of your lift operator license.
* **Lift Operator License Expiry Date** (`lift_operator_license_expiry_date`): This field asks for the date your lift operator license will expire.
* **Lift Operator License Issuing Authority** (`lift_operator_license_issuing_authority`): This field is for providing the authority that issued your lift operator license.
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`): This field asks about the types of safety equipment you use, such as hard hats, safety vests, or safety glasses. Choose all that apply.
* **Personal Protection Equipment** (`personal_protection_equipment`): This field asks about the types of personal protection equipment you use, such as harnesses, fall protection equipment, or hard hats. Choose all that apply.
* **Rescue Procedure** (`rescue_procedure`): This field is for providing a brief description of your rescue procedure in case of an emergency.
* **Confined Space Entry Procedure** (`confined_space_entry_procedure`): This field asks for a brief description of your confined space entry procedure.
* **Rescue Equipment** (`rescue_equipment`): This field is for providing a brief description of your rescue equipment.
* **Lift Equipment Inspection Frequency** (`lift_equipment_inspection_frequency`): This field asks how often you inspect lift equipment, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Equipment Testing Frequency** (`equipment_testing_frequency`): This field asks how often you test equipment, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Training Frequency** (`training_frequency`): This field asks how often you receive training, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Lift Operator Training Frequency** (`lift_operator_training_frequency`): This field asks how often you receive lift operator training, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Rescue Training Frequency** (`rescue_training_frequency`): This field asks how often you receive rescue training, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Confined Space Entry Training Frequency** (`confined_space_entry_training_frequency`): This field asks how often you receive confined space entry training, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Confined Space Entry Procedure Frequency** (`confined_space_entry_procedure_frequency`): This field asks how often you perform confined space entry procedures, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Rescue Equipment Testing Frequency** (`rescue_equipment_testing_frequency`): This field asks how often you test rescue equipment, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Safety Equipment Testing Frequency** (`safety_equipment_testing_frequency`): This field asks how often you test safety equipment, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
