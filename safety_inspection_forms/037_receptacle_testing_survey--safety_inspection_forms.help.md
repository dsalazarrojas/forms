# Receptacle Testing Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Receptacle Testing Survey is designed to collect information on the condition and results of receptacle testing, a critical process to ensure proper functionality and safety in various environments. This form gathers data on the location, receptacle identifier, test time, test results, and any issues encountered during the testing process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill in the required fields:
	* Location: Enter the specific location where the receptacle was tested.
	* Receptacle Identifier: Enter the unique identifier for the tested receptacle.
	* Test Time: Select the date and time the test was performed.
3. For the following fields, you can choose or enter information as needed:
	* Test Result: Select the outcome of the test (Pass, Fail, or Incomplete).
	* Test Result Other: If the test result is not Pass, Fail, or Incomplete, please describe the result.
	* Issue: If any issues were encountered during the testing process, describe them.
4. Test Date: Enter the date the test was performed.
