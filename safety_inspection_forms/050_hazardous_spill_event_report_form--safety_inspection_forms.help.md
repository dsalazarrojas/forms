# Hazardous Spill Event Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hazardous Spill Event Report Form is used to document and report hazardous spills on a facility's premises. This form collects information about the spill, including its location, date, and description, to help facility personnel and management take appropriate measures to contain and clean up the spill.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the location where the spill occurred.
2. Enter the date the spill occurred.
3. Enter your name and contact information as the reporter.
4. Enter the contact person responsible for the spill.
5. Enter the contact number for the person responsible for the spill.
6. Enter the facility where the spill occurred.
7. Check if the spill was a hazardous event.
8. Check if hazardous materials were involved in the spill.
9. Describe the spill in detail.
10. Enter the coordinates of the spill location.
11. Enter the volume of the spill.
12. Check if the spill had an impact on the environment.
13. Check if the spill was contained.
14. Check the method used to contain the spill.
15. Enter the location where the spill was contained.
16. Check if the cleanup cost is known.
17. Check the status of the spill.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Spill Location**: Enter the specific location where the spill occurred, including the building, floor, or area where the spill took place.
* **Spill Date**: Enter the date and time the spill occurred, using the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Reporter**: Enter your name and contact information as the reporter of the spill.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person responsible for the spill.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number for the person responsible for the spill.
* **Facility**: Enter the name of the facility where the spill occurred.
* **Spill Type**: Check if the spill was a hazardous event. This will help in prioritizing the response and taking necessary measures.
* **Materials Hazardous**: Check if hazardous materials were involved in the spill. This will help in assessing the risk level of the spill.
* **Spill Description**: Describe the spill in detail, including the cause, extent, and any other relevant information.
* **Spill Location Coordinates**: Enter the coordinates of the spill location, if available.
* **Spill Volume**: Enter the volume of the spill, in units of volume or quantity.
* **Spill Impact**: Check if the spill had an impact on the environment.
* **Spill Containment**: Check if the spill was contained.
* **Spill Containment Method**: Enter the method used to contain the spill.
* **Spill Containment Location**: Enter the location where the spill was contained.
* **Spill Cleanup Cost**: Check if the cleanup cost is known.
* **Spill Status**: Check the status of the spill as Active or Inactive.
