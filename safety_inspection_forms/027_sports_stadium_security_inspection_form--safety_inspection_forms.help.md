# Sports Stadium Security Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Stadium Security Inspection Form is designed to assess the security and safety measures in place at the sports stadium. This form is for stadium staff and security personnel to evaluate the stadium's compliance with safety and security standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go to the corresponding section of the stadium and inspect each field accordingly. Make sure to answer all required questions truthfully. If you are unsure about any field, please seek guidance from your supervisor or a qualified member of the stadium staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Security Check** (`security_check`, select_one, required: false)
Check the overall security of the stadium, including any visible security personnel, cameras, and access control measures.

### 2. **Security Team Responsibility Area** (`security_team_responsibility_area`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select all areas where the security team has responsibility, such as the north, east, west, and south sections of the stadium, as well as the main entrance.

### 3. **Fire Suppression** (`fire_suppression`, select_one, required: false)
Check the fire alarm system and other fire suppression measures in place, such as reporter alarm systems or other.

### 4. **Access Control** (`access_control`, select_one, required: false)
Check if there are measures in place to control access to the stadium, such as gates, doors, or other barriers.

### 5. **Emergency Exit Signs** (`emergency_exit_signs`, select_one, required: false)
Check for emergency exit signs, including green lights or arrows, or other signs.

### 6. **Emergency Exit Path** (`emergency_exit_path`, select_one, required: false)
Check for the straight path, staircase, or ramp that leads to emergency exits.

### 7. **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, select_one, required: false)
Check for safety equipment, such as fire extinguishers, fire alarms, first aid kits, or other.

### 8. **Safety Equipment Type** (`safety_equipment_type`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select the type of safety equipment found, such as fire suppression, safety alarm, first aid equipment, or other.

### 9. **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_one, required: false)
Check for emergency contact information, such as security office, stadium manager, first aid team, or other.

### 10. **Emergency Contact Type** (`emergency_contact_type`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select the type of emergency contact person or team, such as office staff, security team, first aid staff, or event staff.

## Tips
* Please complete the form honestly and accurately based on your inspection.
* If you are unsure about any field, please seek guidance from your supervisor or a qualified member of the stadium staff.
* This form should not be used for other purposes, such as general event planning or maintenance requests.
