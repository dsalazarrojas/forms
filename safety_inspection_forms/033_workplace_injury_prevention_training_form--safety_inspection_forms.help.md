# Workplace Injury Prevention Training Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about workplace injury prevention training events. It's intended for supervisors, employees, and instructors to report and document the completion of training sessions, ensuring that all necessary protocols and procedures are in place to prevent workplace injuries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus where applicable.
2. Fill in the text fields with the required information.
3. Choose the correct date and time options where applicable.
4. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workplace Injury Prevention Training** (`1`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the training session or event.
* **Workplace Injury Prevention Training Questions** (`2`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the options: Safety Protocols, Safety Procedures, Hazard Reporting, Incident Reporting, or Training Feedback.
* **Training Status** (`3`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable options (Completed, In Progress, Not Started).
* **Training Feedback** (`4`, text, required/optional): Enter any feedback or comments about the training session.
* **Training Comments** (`5`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments about the training session.
* **Who Attended** (`6`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable options (True, False).
* **Who Is Responsible** (`7`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable options (True, False).
* **Notes** (`8`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the training session.
* **Additional Comments** (`9`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or questions about the training session.
* **Date Completed** (`10`, date, required/optional): Choose the date the training was completed.
* **Time Completed** (`11`, time, required/optional): Choose the time the training was completed.
* **Duration Completed** (`12`, text, required/optional): Enter the duration of the training session.
* **Date Started** (`13`, date, required/optional): Choose the date the training started.
* **Time Started** (`14`, time, required/optional): Choose the time the training started.
* **Duration Started** (`15`, text, required/optional): Enter the duration of the training session.
* **Who Is Responsible 1** (`16`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the options: Supervisor, Employee, or Manager.
* **Who Is Responsible 2** (`17`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the options: Supervisor, Employee, or Manager.
* **Instructor** (`18`, select_one, required/optional): Select if the training was led by a true or false statement.
* **Qualified Instructor** (`19`, select_one, required/optional): Select if the instructor was qualified or not.
* **Completed Correctly** (`20`, select_one, required/optional): Select if the training was completed correctly or not.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`21`, text, required/optional): Enter the supervisor's signature.
* **Employee Signature** (`22`, text, required/optional): Enter the employee's signature.
* **Instructor Signature** (`23`, text, required/optional): Enter the instructor's signature.
* **Date Submitted** (`24`, date, required/optional): Choose the date the form was submitted.
* **Time Submitted** (`25`, time, required/optional): Choose the time the form was submitted.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Be honest and transparent when answering questions and providing feedback.
* If unsure about any field, refer to the relevant policies and procedures.
* Review and edit the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
