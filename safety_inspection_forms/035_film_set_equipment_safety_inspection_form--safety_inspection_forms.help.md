# Film Set Equipment Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to conduct regular safety inspections on film set equipment. It is designed to identify and document any issues or hazards found during the inspection, and to ensure that the equipment is properly maintained and safe for use. The form is used by film set inspectors to gather information and take action on any potential safety risks.

## How To Complete This Form

* Ensure you have all necessary information and materials before starting the inspection.
* Read each section carefully and answer all required fields.
* Enter your contact information so that you can be reached in case of follow-up questions or actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (ID 2): Enter your name, phone number, and email so that the production team can contact you with any follow-up questions or actions.
* **Equipment Description** (ID 3): Describe the equipment being inspected, including its make, model, and any relevant details.
* **Inspection Date** (ID 4): Enter the date of the inspection in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Inspection Time** (ID 5): Enter the time of the inspection in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Inspector Name** (ID 18): Enter your name as the inspector.
* **Signature Date** (ID 15): Enter the date you sign the form in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Signature Time** (ID 16): Enter the time you sign the form in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Inspector Signature** (ID 17): Sign your name as the inspector.
* **Department** (ID 19): Check "Yes" if the equipment is part of a department (e.g. props, wardrobe, etc.).
* **Location** (ID 20): Check "Yes" if the equipment is located at a specific location (e.g. soundstage, set, etc.).
* **Inspector Phone Number** (ID 21): Enter your phone number so that the production team can contact you.
* **Inspector Email** (ID 22): Enter your email so that the production team can contact you.
* **Production Company** (ID 23): Check "Yes" if the equipment is part of a production company.
* **Production Contact** (ID 24): Enter the production contact name and phone number.
* **Form ID** (ID 25): Enter a unique number for this form submission.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate in your inspection and description of the equipment.
* If you find any issues, check "Issues Found" and describe the problem.
* If you take any action on the issues, check "Follow-up Action" and describe the action taken.
* If the equipment is not active, check "Equipment Status" and select "Inactive".
* If you have any additional comments, enter them in "Additional Comments".
