# public_transit_safety_incident_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for reporting public transit safety incidents to track and analyze safety incidents, identify contributing factors, and prevent similar incidents in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the date and time the incident occurred using the date and time fields.
2. Provide a brief description of the incident in the incident description field.
3. Enter any relevant images or videos related to the incident in the incident image or incident video fields (optional).
4. Enter information about the vehicle involved in the incident, including its registration, make and model, license plate number, and year.
5. Enter information about the incident reporter, including their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
6. Enter information about the incident investigator, including their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
7. Choose the incident status from the select one options and provide a brief description of the status in the incident status description field.
8. Enter the incident status code and date and time the status was updated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description**: Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Image**: Enter any relevant images related to the incident (optional).
* **Incident Video**: Enter any relevant videos related to the incident (optional).
* **Vehicle Registration**: Enter the vehicle's registration number.
* **Vehicle Make Model**: Enter the vehicle's make and model.
* **Vehicle License Plate**: Enter the vehicle's license plate number.
* **Incident Reporter Information**: Enter information about the person reporting the incident, including their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the vehicle's year.
* **Incident Investigator Information**: Enter information about the person investigating the incident, including their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Incident Investigator Name**: Enter the name of the incident investigator.
* **Incident Investigator Phone**: Enter the phone number of the incident investigator.
* **Incident Investigator E-mail**: Enter the email of the incident investigator.
* **Date Reported**: Enter the date the incident was reported (optional).
* **Time Reported**: Enter the time the incident was reported (optional).
* **Date Completed**: Enter the date the incident was completed (optional).
* **Time Completed**: Enter the time the incident was completed (optional).
* **Incident Status**: Choose the incident status from the select one options.
* **Incident Status Description**: Enter a brief description of the incident status.
* **Incident Status Code**: Enter the incident status code.
* **Incident Status Date**: Enter the date the incident status was updated (optional).
* **Incident Status Time**: Enter the time the incident status was updated (optional).
* **Incident Status User**: Enter the user who updated the incident status (optional).

Note: The optional fields can be left blank if not applicable.
