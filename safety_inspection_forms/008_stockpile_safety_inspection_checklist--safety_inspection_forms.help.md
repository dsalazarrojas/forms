# Stockpile Safety Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stockpile Safety Inspection Checklist is a tool used to ensure the safety of stockpiles by identifying and tracking potential hazards and corrective actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the Stockpile ID to associate the inspection with the correct stockpile.
* Provide the location ID of the stockpile being inspected.
* Enter the date and time of the inspection.
* Select any hazards found during the inspection.
* Select the status of any corrective actions taken.
* Enter any comments or notes about the inspection.
* Provide the name, email, and phone number of the safety manager responsible for the stockpile.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stockpile ID** (`stockpile_id`, number, required): Enter the unique identifier for the stockpile being inspected.
* **Location ID** (`location_id`, text, required): Enter the location where the stockpile is stored.
* **Date of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection`, date, required): Enter the date and time of the inspection.
* **Hazards Found** (`hazards_found`, select_multiple, required): Select any hazards identified during the inspection. Options include:
	+ Hazard 1
	+ Hazard 2
	+ Hazard 3
* **Corrective Action Status** (`corrective_action_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of any corrective actions taken. Options include:
	+ Status 1
	+ Status 2
	+ Status 3
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Safety Manager Name** (`safety_manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the safety manager responsible for the stockpile.
* **Safety Manager Email** (`safety_manager_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the safety manager responsible for the stockpile.
* **Safety Manager Phone** (`safety_manager_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the safety manager responsible for the stockpile.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to complete this form.
* Review the stockpile's safety procedures and protocols before completing the form.
* Complete all required fields to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
* Use this form to identify and address potential safety hazards and implement corrective actions.
