# ppe_upgrade_deduction_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PPE Upgrade Deduction Form is used to request approval for an employee's PPE upgrade.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you will need to provide the following information:

* Your personal details as the submitter, including your name, ID, email, phone number, company, address, job title, and department.
* The employee's details, including their name, ID, and department.
* The upgrade details, including the upgrade date and cost.
* The reviewer's details, including their name, ID, email, phone number, company, address, job title, and department.
* A brief note on why the upgrade is being requested.

Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1.  Enter the employee's name and ID.
2.  Enter the upgrade date and cost.
3.  Select the department where the employee works.
4.  Enter the reviewer's name and ID.
5.  Enter the reviewer's email, phone number, company, address, job title, and department.
6.  Enter a brief note on why the upgrade is being requested.
7.  Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee requesting the upgrade.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter the ID number of the employee requesting the upgrade.
* **Upgrade Date** (`upgrade_date`, date, required): Enter the date the upgrade is needed.
* **Upgrade Cost** (`upgrade_cost`, number, required): Enter the cost of the upgrade.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department where the employee works.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee's supervisor.
* **Supervisor ID** (`supervisor_id`, number, required): Enter the ID number of the employee's supervisor.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, required): Enter the date the upgrade is approved.
* **Approval Note** (`approval_note`, note, required): Enter a brief note on why the upgrade is approved.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter ID** (`submitter_id`, number, required): Enter your ID as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter your email as the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as the submitter.
* **Submitter Company** (`submitter_company`, text, required): Enter your company as the submitter.
* **Submitter Address** (`submitter_address`, text, required): Enter your address as the submitter.
* **Submitter Jobtitle** (`submitter_jobtitle`, text, required): Enter your job title as the submitter.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, text, required): Enter your department as the submitter.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer ID** (`reviewer_id`, number, required): Enter the ID number of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email** (`reviewer_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Phone** (`reviewer_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Company** (`reviewer_company`, text, required): Enter the company of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Address** (`reviewer_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Jobtitle** (`reviewer_jobtitle`, text, required): Enter the job title of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Department** (`reviewer_department`, text, required): Enter the department of the reviewer.
