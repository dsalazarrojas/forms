# Aircraft Cabin Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide is intended to help you understand the purpose and structure of the Aircraft Cabin Safety Inspection Form. The form is designed to collect data on the safety inspection of an aircraft cabin, covering various aspects of safety and condition. The information gathered from this form will help ensure that the aircraft is safe and ready for flight.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Review the form to understand the sections and fields it includes.
2.  Complete each field according to your findings, using the options provided in the relevant fields.
3.  Ensure that all required fields are filled accurately and completely.
4.  Use the provided guidelines to ensure you are capturing all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, required)

*   This field is for capturing the full name of the inspector conducting the inspection.

### 2. **Inspector ID** (`inspector_id`, text, required)

*   Enter the employee or badge number of the inspector.

### 3. **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required)

*   Record the date of the inspection.

### 4. **Aircraft Registration** (`aircraft_registration`, text, required)

*   Enter the aircraft's tail number.

### 5. **Aircraft Type** (`aircraft_type`, text, required)

*   Provide the make and model of the aircraft.

### 6. **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, text, optional)

*   Enter the flight number, if applicable.

### 7. **Passenger Seat Condition** (`seat_condition`, select_one, required)

*   Evaluate the overall condition of the passenger seats.

### 8. **Seatbelt Inspection** (`seatbelts_check`, select_one, required)

*   Assess the functionality of all seatbelts.

### 9. **Non-Functional Seatbelts** (`seatbelt_count`, number, optional)

*   Record the number of non-functioning seatbelts.

### 10. **Overhead Bins** (`overhead_bins`, select_one, required)

*   Evaluate the overall condition and functionality of the overhead bins.

### 11. **Emergency Exits** (`emergency_exits`, select_one, required)

*   Assess the condition and accessibility of the emergency exits.

### 12. **Exit Signage** (`exit_signage`, select_one, required)

*   Evaluate the visibility and condition of exit signs.

### 13. **Emergency Lighting** (`emergency_lighting`, select_one, required)

*   Assess the functionality of the emergency lighting.

### 14. **Life Vests** (`life_vests`, select_one, required)

*   Evaluate the presence and condition of life vests.

### 15. **Oxygen Masks** (`oxygen_masks`, select_one, required)

*   Assess the condition of the oxygen mask compartments.

### 16. **Fire Extinguishers** (`fire_extinguishers`, select_one, required)

*   Evaluate the presence and charge of fire extinguishers.

### 17. **First Aid Kits** (`first_aid_kits`, select_one, required)

*   Assess the presence and condition of first aid kits.

### 18. **Megaphone** (`megaphone`, select_one, required)

*   Evaluate the presence and functionality of the megaphone.

### 19. **Emergency Axe Crowbar** (`axe_crowbar`, select_one, required)

*   Assess the presence and accessibility of the emergency axe crowbar.

### 20. **Slide Rafts** (`slide_rafts`, select_one, required)

*   Evaluate the condition and readiness of the slide rafts.

### 21. **Cabin Cleanliness** (`cabin_cleanliness`, select_one, required)

*   Assess the overall cleanliness of the cabin.

### 22. **Lavatory Condition** (`lavatory_condition`, select_one, required)

*   Evaluate the cleanliness and functionality of the lavatory.

### 23. **Galley Condition** (`galley_condition`, select_one, required)

*   Assess the cleanliness and security of the galley.

### 24. **Carpet Condition** (`carpet_condition`, select_one, required)

*   Evaluate the wear and cleanliness of the carpet.

### 25. **Interior Panels** (`panel_condition`, select_one, required)

*   Assess the condition and security of the interior panels.

### 26. **Window Condition** (`window_condition`, select_one, required)

*   Evaluate the cleanliness and undamage of the windows.

### 27. **Window Shades** (`shade_condition`, select_one, required)

*   Assess the functionality of the window shades.

### 28. **Tray Tables** (`tray_tables`, select_one, required)

*   Evaluate the functionality and cleanliness of the tray tables.

### 29. **Entertainment System** (`entertainment_system`, select_one, optional)

*   Assess the functionality of the entertainment system, if applicable.

### 30. **Discrepancies Found** (`discrepancies_found`, text, required)

*   List all discrepancies or issues found during the inspection.

### 31. **Corrective Action Required** (`corrective_action`, text, required)

*   Provide suggestions for corrective actions to be taken.

### 32. **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, select_one, required)

*   Evaluate the urgency of the repairs needed.

### 33. **Aircraft Cleared for Service** (`aircraft_cleared`, select_one, required)

*   Record whether the aircraft is cleared for service after the inspection.

### 34. **Inspector Signature** (`inspector_signature`, text, required)

*   Enter a digital signature of the inspector.

### 35. **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, text, optional)

*   Enter the approval signature of the supervisor, if applicable.

## Tips

- Ensure that all required fields are filled accurately and completely.
- Review each field carefully to understand the options provided.
- Document all discrepancies or issues found during the inspection.
- Use the provided guidelines to ensure you are capturing all necessary information.
- Double-check the aircraft's readiness for flight after the inspection.
