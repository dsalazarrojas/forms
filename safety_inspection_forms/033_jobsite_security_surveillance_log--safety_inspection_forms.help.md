# jobsite_security_surveillance_log_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record security surveillance log information for a job site, including incidents reported during patrols and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the Job Id of the job site being surveilled.
2. Enter the Security Officer Id who conducted the patrol.
3. Enter the Patrol Date and Start/End Time of the patrol.
4. Check if an Incident was Reported and select the corresponding option.
5. Select all Locations where the incident occurred.
6. Provide a Description of the incident.
7. Enter any Actions Taken to address the incident.
8. Enter any additional Security Officer Notes.
9. Enter Shift Notes for the security officer.
10. Enter the Weather Conditions.
11. Enter the Shift Start and End Time.
12. Enter a list of Personnel On Site during the patrol.
13. Enter the Vehicle Type, Color, License Plate, and Make/Model.
14. Enter any additional Notes.
15. Enter the Contact Information for follow-up (Email and Phone).
16. Enter the Date and Time of the patrol.
17. Review and update the Notes as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Id**: Enter the unique identifier for the job site being surveilled.
* **Security Officer Id**: Enter the id of the security officer who conducted the patrol.
* **Patrol Date**: Enter the date the patrol was conducted.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the patrol.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the patrol.
* **Patrol Notes**: Enter any notes regarding the patrol.
* **Incident Reported**: Check if an incident was reported during the patrol.
* **Incident Location**: Select all locations where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Action Taken**: Enter any actions taken to address the incident.
* **Security Officer Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the security officer.
* **Shift Notes**: Enter shift notes for the security officer.
* **Weather Conditions**: Enter the weather conditions during the patrol.
* **Shift Start**: Enter the start time of the shift.
* **Shift End**: Enter the end time of the shift.
* **Personnel On Site**: Enter a list of personnel on site during the patrol.
* **Vehicle Type**: Enter the type of vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Color**: Enter the color of the vehicle involved.
* **Vehicle License Plate**: Enter the license plate of the vehicle involved.
* **Vehicle Make Model**: Enter the make and model of the vehicle involved.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes.
* **Email**: Enter the contact email for follow-up.
* **Phone**: Enter the contact phone number for follow-up.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the patrol.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the patrol.
