# Wire Management Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the organization, condition, and management of cables in the data center or electrical infrastructure. This information will help identify areas for improvement and provide a clear overview of the cable's status.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Select the appropriate option for each field based on your observation:
	* Cable Organization: Choose the level of organization of the cables (e.g., well-organized, somewhat organized, poorly organized, not organized at all).
	* Cable Clutter: Identify any exposed or exposed cables.
	* Cable Labeling: Select the level of labeling (e.g., proper labeling, no labeling, partial labeling).
	* Cable Location: Choose the correct location of the cable (e.g., proper location, improper location, no location).
	* Cable Condition: Select the condition of the cable (e.g., good, fair, poor).
	* Cable Label Quality: Evaluate the quality of the cable label (e.g., good, fair, poor).
	* Cable Color Quality: Assess the color of the cable (e.g., good, fair, poor).
	* Form Comments: Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the cables.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cable Organization**: This field asks you to evaluate the level of organization of the cables. Choose the option that best describes your observation.
* **Cable Clutter**: Check all that apply for any exposed or partially exposed cables.
* **Cable Labeling**: Select the level of labeling that you've observed for the cable.
* **Cable Location**: Identify the correct location of the cable.
* **Cable Condition**: Assess the condition of the cable by choosing the option that best describes it.
* **Cable Label Quality**: Evaluate the quality of the cable label.
* **Cable Color Quality**: Assess the color of the cable.
* **Form Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the cables.
