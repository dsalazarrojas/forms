# Corridor Safety Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corridor Safety Assessment Form is a tool used to evaluate the safety conditions of corridors within facilities. Its primary objective is to identify potential hazards and take corrective actions to mitigate risks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the corridor's safety conditions carefully, considering all potential hazards.
2. Fill out the form with accurate information about the corridor's hazards, risk severity, corrective actions, and any other relevant details.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed, and optional fields are filled out if applicable.
4. Select the correct facility manager and safety inspection date from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Corridor Hazards
* **Corridor Hazards** (`corridor_hazards`, text, required/optional): Describe the hazards present in the corridor, such as tripping hazards, uneven flooring, or other safety concerns.

### Risk Severity
* **Risk Severity** (`risk_severity`, number, required/optional): Rate the level of risk associated with each hazard on a scale (e.g., 1-5).

### Corrective Actions
* **Corrective Actions** (`corrective_actions`, text, required/optional): Outline the actions taken to mitigate or eliminate the hazards, such as repairing the flooring or installing handrails.

### Safety Inspection Date
* **Safety Inspection Date** (`safety_inspection_date`, date, required/optional): Record the date of the safety inspection, which will help track the progress and history of assessments.

### Facility Manager
* **Facility Manager** (`facility_manager`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the correct facility manager responsible for the corridor's safety and maintenance.

### Building Condition
* **Building Condition** (`building_condition`, text, required/optional): Describe the overall condition of the building, including any recent repairs or maintenance.

### Hazard Type
* **Hazard Type** (`hazard_type`, text, required/optional): Identify the type of hazard present in the corridor, such as tripping hazards or fire hazards.

### Safety Equipment
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the safety equipment currently installed or available in the corridor, such as emergency exits or fire extinguishers.

### Location
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Provide the specific location of the corridor within the facility, including floor and room numbers.

Tips:
* Be thorough when describing hazards and corrective actions to ensure accurate risk assessment.
* Select the correct facility manager to ensure accountability and follow-up actions.
* Use the safety inspection date to track progress and history of assessments.
* Choose the most accurate hazard type and safety equipment to ensure accurate records.
