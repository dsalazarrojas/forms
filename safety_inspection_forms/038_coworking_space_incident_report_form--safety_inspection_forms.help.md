# Coworking Space Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for reporting incidents, issues, or concerns that have occurred in the coworking space. It helps to collect information about the incident, gather details about the issue, and track follow-up activities. The purpose of this form is to ensure accurate and efficient documentation of incidents, facilitate communication among stakeholders, and enable timely follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate section for the incident you are reporting.
2. Fill in the required fields with as much detail as possible.
3. Choose relevant options from the drop-down menus as necessary.
4. Enter your comments and notes in the corresponding text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shared Office Issues** (Page 1, id: 1): Describe any shared office issues that may be causing problems.
* **Reported By** (Page 1, id: 2): Enter the name of the person who reported the issue.
* **Incident Date** (Page 1, id: 3): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (Page 1, id: 4): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Issue Description** (Page 1, id: 5): Describe the issue in as much detail as possible.
* **Reported Details** (Page 1, id: 6): Provide any additional details about the reported issue.
* **Resolved By** (Page 1, id: 7): Check if the issue has been resolved.
* **Follow Up Status** (Page 1, id: 8): Select the follow-up status of the issue (Active or Inactive).
* **Follow Up Date** (Page 1, id: 9): Enter the date of the follow-up activity.
* **Follow Up Comment** (Page 1, id: 10): Enter any comments about the follow-up activity.
* **Staff Involved** (Page 1, id: 11): List the staff members involved in the incident.
* **Members Involved** (Page 1, id: 12): List the members involved in the incident.
* **Incident Reported By** (Page 1, id: 13): Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Resolved By** (Page 1, id: 14): Enter the name of the person who resolved the incident.
* **Follow Up Contacted** (Page 1, id: 15): Check if follow-up contact was made.
* **Follow Up Contacted Date** (Page 1, id: 16): Enter the date follow-up contact was made.
* **Follow Up Contacted By** (Page 1, id: 17): Enter the name of the person who made the follow-up contact.
* **Incident Location** (Page 1, id: 18): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Details** (Page 1, id: 19): Enter any additional details about the incident.
* **Staff Comments** (Page 1, id: 20): Enter any comments from staff members.
* **Follow Up Status Details** (Page 1, id: 21): Enter any details about the follow-up status.
* **Action Taken** (Page 1, id: 22): Enter any actions taken to resolve the issue.
* **Follow Up Date Details** (Page 1, id: 23): Enter any details about the follow-up date.
* **Follow Up Contact** (Page 1, id: 24): Enter any contact information for follow-up activities.
* **Follow Up Notes** (Page 1, id: 25): Enter any additional follow-up notes.

Note: Please review the form carefully and ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting.
