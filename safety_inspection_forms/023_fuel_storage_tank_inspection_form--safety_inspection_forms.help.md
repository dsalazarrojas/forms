# Fuel Storage Tank Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fuel Storage Tank Inspection Form is designed to collect information about a fuel storage tank's general information, size, contents, and inspection status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information page with the tank's details, including its size and contents.
2. Choose the correct tank size from the options provided.
3. Select the current tank contents from the available options.
4. Enter the date and time of the inspection.
5. Choose the current inspection status.
6. Add any additional remarks or comments about the tank's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (Page 1)
* **General Information**: Enter the tank's name, location, and any other relevant details.
### Tank ID (Page 1)
* **Tank ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the tank. This can be a serial number or any other distinct identifier.
### Tank Size (Page 1)
* **Tank Size**: Choose the correct size of the tank from the options provided:
	+ **Minimalist**: Small tanks with limited storage capacity.
	+ **Medium**: Standard tanks with average storage capacity.
	+ **Large**: Extra-large tanks with increased storage capacity.
### Tank Contents (Page 1)
* **Tank Contents**: Select all relevant contents of the tank from the available options:
	+ **Diesel Fuel**: Fuel for diesel engines.
	+ **Jet Fuel**: Fuel for jet engines.
	+ **Other**: Any other type of fuel or substance stored in the tank.
### Inspection Date (Page 2)
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection in the correct format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
### Inspection Time (Page 2)
* **Inspection Time**: Enter the time of the inspection in the correct format (e.g., 12:00 PM).
### Inspection Status (Page 3)
* **Inspection Status**: Choose the current status of the inspection from the options provided:
	+ **Complete**: The inspection is finished and all tasks are complete.
	+ **Partial**: The inspection is not finished, but some tasks are complete.
	+ **Not Started**: The inspection has not started yet.
### Remarks (Page 3)
* **Remarks**: Add any additional comments or notes about the tank's condition, any issues, or actions to be taken.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the tank's operator or facility manager.
* This form is a general guideline and may need to be adapted to your specific situation.
* Save a copy of the filled form for future reference.
