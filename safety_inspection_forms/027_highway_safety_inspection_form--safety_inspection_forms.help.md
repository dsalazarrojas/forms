# Highway Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Highway Safety Inspection Form is designed to document the condition of vehicles and roadways to ensure safety standards are met during inspections.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant options for each field according to your inspection findings.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and clear information for all fields.
3. Fill out the form as thoroughly and truthfully as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Vehicle Condition**
* **Vehicle Condition** (select_multiple, optional): Check all the boxes that apply to the vehicle's condition during the inspection:
	+ Yes: The vehicle shows signs of wear and tear or damage.
	+ No: The vehicle appears to be in good condition.

### 2. **Roadway Condition**
* **Roadway Condition** (select_multiple, optional): Check all the boxes that apply to the road's condition during the inspection:
	+ Yes: The road shows signs of wear and tear or damage.
	+ No: The road appears to be in good condition.

### 3. **Inspection Date**
* **Inspection Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection for record-keeping purposes. This helps track and schedule future inspections.

### 4. **Inspector Name**
* **Inspector Name** (text, optional): Enter your name for identification and accountability.

### 5. **Vehicle Number**
* **Vehicle Number** (text, optional): If applicable, enter the vehicle's license plate number for reference.

### 6. **Vehicle Location**
* **Vehicle Location** (text, optional): If applicable, provide the exact location of the vehicle for future reference.

### 7. **Comments**
* **Comments** (note, optional): Add any additional information or notes from the inspection for future reference or clarification.

### 8. **Safety Issues**
* **Safety Issues** (select_multiple, optional): Check all the boxes that apply to any safety issues found during the inspection:
	+ Yes: Safety issues were present.
	+ No: No safety issues were found.

## Tips
* Be sure to accurately report the condition of both vehicles and roadways.
* If unsure about any field or condition, consult relevant guidelines or seek advice from colleagues.
* Review the form thoroughly before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
