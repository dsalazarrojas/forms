# subwaysafetyincidentform - Help Guide
## Purpose
The subwaysafetyincidentform is a tool used to collect incident information and safety data from subways. This form helps to track and analyze incidents, identify potential safety issues, and improve overall safety measures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the incident details page (page 1) and enter a brief description of the incident, including any relevant details such as date, time, location, and involved parties.
2. On the incident summary page (page 2), enter a concise summary of the incident.
3. On the incident type page (page 3), select the type of incident that occurred (Accident, Near Miss, or Other).
4. On the incident cause page (page 4), enter the cause of the incident.
5. On the incident impact page (page 5), select the impact of the incident (Minor, Major).
6. On the incident severity page (page 6), enter the severity level of the incident on a scale of 1-5.
7. On the incident reporter page (page 7), enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
8. On the incident status page (page 8), select the status of the incident (Open, Closed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details** (Page 1, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Summary** (Page 2, text, required/optional): Enter a concise summary of the incident.
* **Incident Type** (Page 3, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of incident that occurred (Accident, Near Miss, or Other).
* **Incident Cause** (Page 4, text, required/optional): Enter the cause of the incident.
* **Incident Impact** (Page 5, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the impact of the incident (Minor, Major).
* **Incident Severity** (Page 6, number, required/optional): Enter the severity level of the incident on a scale of 1-5.
* **Incident Reporter** (Page 7, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Incident Status** (Page 8, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the incident (Open, Closed).
