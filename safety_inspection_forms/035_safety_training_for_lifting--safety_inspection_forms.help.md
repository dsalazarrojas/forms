# Safety Training For Lifting - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for safety training purposes, specifically for lifting. It's essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your safety and well-being are protected during lifting operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant information, including your name and email address.
2. Select your training program, which indicates whether you have undergone safety training for lifting or not.
3. Enter your participant name, which is the name of the person receiving the safety training.
4. Enter your participant's email address, which is used for communication purposes.
5. Enter your participant's phone number, which is used for emergency contact purposes.
6. Select the training date, which is the date you received the safety training.
7. Enter the training time, which is the time you received the safety training.
8. Enter the training location, which is the site where you received the safety training.
9. Select whether you have received the safety training certificate or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Information** (Text, Required: false): This field is used to collect your participant information. Please enter your name and email address accurately.
- **Training Program** (Select One, Required: false): Please select 'Yes' if you have undergone safety training for lifting, 'No' otherwise.
- **Participant Name** (Text, Required: false): Enter the name of the person receiving the safety training.
- **Participant Email** (Email, Required: false): Enter the email address of the person receiving the safety training, which will be used for communication purposes.
- **Participant Phone** (Text, Required: false): Enter the phone number of the person receiving the safety training, which will be used for emergency contact purposes.
- **Training Date** (Date, Required: false): Enter the date you received the safety training.
- **Training Time** (Time, Required: false): Enter the time you received the safety training.
- **Training Location** (Text, Required: false): Enter the location where you received the safety training.
- **Safety Training Certificate** (Select Multiple, Required: false): Please select 'Yes' if you have received the safety training certificate, 'No' otherwise.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your safety and well-being are protected during lifting operations.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your supervisor or the relevant department before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you have not already submitted a similar form for this specific training purpose to avoid duplication and unnecessary processing delays.
