# Fire Hazard Report Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Hazard Report Contact Form is designed to report any safety hazards, particularly fire hazards, encountered by employees while working. This form helps the safety team gather essential information to address the hazard effectively.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to initiate the form.
2. Enter the location of the fire hazard in the "Fire Hazard Location" field.
3. Select the type of fire hazard encountered using the "Fire Hazard Type" field (e.g., Electrical, Gas, Water, or Other).
4. Provide a detailed description of the hazard in the "Fire Hazard Description" field.
5. If available, upload a photo of the hazard (if not, proceed to the next step).
6. Enter your contact information, including your phone number, name, and email address (if applicable).
7. Select the preferred contact method (Emergency, Phone, or Email).
8. Provide any additional message or comments in the "Message" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
###  Fire Hazard Location
* **Fire Hazard Location**: This is the address or location where the hazard was found. Please enter the exact address or building name.

* **Report Date**: This field is to record the date on which you reported the hazard.
###  Contact Number
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information or assistance.

* **Contact Name**: Please enter your name as a reference for follow-up contact.

* **Contact Email**: If you have an email address, enter it so we can reach out to you.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill out all required fields to ensure that the safety team receives accurate and complete information.
- Be as detailed as possible when describing the fire hazard.
