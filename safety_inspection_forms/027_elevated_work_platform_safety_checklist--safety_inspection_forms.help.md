# Elevated Work Platform Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elevated Work Platform Safety Checklist is a crucial document that helps ensure the safe operation of elevated work platforms. It is designed to guide inspectors in conducting regular inspections, identifying potential hazards, and documenting corrective actions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Ewp Inspection**: Provide a brief summary of the EWP inspection, describing any issues found or concerns raised.
2. **Ppe Check**: Select "Correct" if the PPE check was completed and satisfactory, "Incorrect" if issues were found, or "Not Applicable" if the item was not checked.
3. **Hazards**: List any hazards identified during the inspection, selecting all relevant options (e.g., Fall from height, Electrocution, etc.).
4. **Corrective Action**: Describe the actions taken to correct any issues found during the inspection.
5. **Date Of Insptn**: Record the date of the inspection.
6. **Signature**: Sign and date this form to confirm that the inspection was completed.
7. **Assigned To**: Record the name of the person assigned to complete this form.
8. **Date Of Signature**: Record the date the form was signed.
9. **Time Of Signature**: Record the time the form was signed.
10. **Time Zone**: Record the time zone where the inspection was conducted (e.g., EST, PST, CST, etc.).
11. **Note**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
12. **Ewp Make**: Record the make of the EWP (if applicable).
13. **Ewp Model**: Record the model of the EWP (if applicable).
14. **Ewp Year**: Record the year the EWP was manufactured (if applicable).
15. **Safety Inspector**: Select the safety inspector's name who conducted the inspection.
16. **Ewp Location**: Record the location where the EWP was inspected.
17. **Weather**: Select the weather condition during the inspection (e.g., Sunny, Cloudy, etc.).
18. **Remarks**: Provide any additional remarks or comments about the inspection.
19. **Supervisor Signature**: Sign and date this section to confirm that the supervisor has reviewed the form.
20. **Operator Signature**: Sign and date this section to confirm that the operator has reviewed the form.
21. **Assigned To**: Record the name of the person assigned to complete this form.
22. **Form Id**: Record a unique identifier for this form (if required).
23. **Category**: Record any category or classification for this EWP (if applicable).
24. **Description**: Provide a brief description of the EWP.
25. **Assigned To**: Record the name of the person assigned to complete this form ( duplicate of #21).
