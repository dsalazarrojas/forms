# Safety Hazard Resolution Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Hazard Resolution Report Form is designed to collect information about safety hazards and their resolution. It's used to identify, document, and address potential risks and incidents in the workplace. This form is a tool for safety managers, inspectors, and other stakeholders to gather accurate and consistent data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2.  Fill in the form with the required information, starting from the first field.
3.  For fields that are not required, you can skip them if you don't have the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`id: 1`, text, required/optional):
    *   This is the first field on the form where you can enter any general information about the hazard or incident.
*   It's a plain text field where you can type in any relevant details.
*   You can skip this field if you don't have any specific information to share.
*   **Hazard Identification** (`id: 2`, select_multiple, required/optional):
    *   This field is used to select multiple options that describe the hazard or incident.
*   Choose the options that best match your situation and add any additional details if needed.
*   For this field, you can choose one or more options.
*   **Hazard Severity** (`id: 3`, select_one, required/optional):
    *   This field is used to rate the severity of the hazard or incident.
*   Select the option that best describes the severity level.
*   You can choose only one option for this field.
*   **Risk Factors** (`id: 4`, select_multiple, required/optional):
    *   This field is used to identify the risk factors associated with the hazard or incident.
*   Choose the options that apply to your situation and add any additional details if needed.
*   For this field, you can choose one or more options.
*   **Corrective Actions** (`id: 5`, text, required/optional):
    *   This is a field for you to describe the corrective actions taken to resolve the hazard or incident.
*   Enter any additional details about the actions taken.
*   You can skip this field if you don't have any information to provide.
*   **Follow-up Actions** (`id: 6`, text, required/optional):
    *   This field is used to record any follow-up actions taken to address the hazard or incident.
*   Enter any additional details about the actions taken.
*   You can skip this field if you don't have any information to provide.
*   **Review and Approval** (`id: 7`, text, required/optional):
    *   This field is used to record any reviews and approvals related to the hazard or incident.
*   Enter any additional details about reviews and approvals.
*   You can skip this field if you don't have any information to provide.
*   **Incident Status** (`id: 8`, select_one, required/optional):
    *   This field is used to report the current status of the hazard or incident.
*   Choose the option that best describes the status.
*   You can choose only one option for this field.
*   **Incident Closure Date** (`id: 9`, date, required/optional):
    *   This field is used to record the date when the hazard or incident was closed.
*   Enter the date in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
*   You can skip this field if you don't know the closure date.
*   **Incident Closure Reason** (`id: 10`, text, required/optional):
    *   This field is used to describe the reason for closing the hazard or incident.
*   Enter any additional details about the closure reason.
*   You can skip this field if you don't have any information to provide.
