# Fire Safety Inspection Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide safety inspectors in documenting their findings on fire safety inspections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the first page, which covers identifying potential fire hazards.
2. Choose one or more hazards that were observed during the inspection.
3. On the second page, review and verify your answers from the first page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fire Hazards** (`section_1`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
    This field asks you to identify the fire hazards found during the inspection. Choose one or more hazards that were observed, such as Hazard 1, Hazard 2, or Hazard 3.
* **First Page** (`fire_safety_inspection_report_form_page`, `note`, required: `false`): 
    This page is a general note section meant for the inspector to provide any additional information not captured in other fields.
* **Second Page** (`fire_safety_inspection_report_form_page_2`, `note`, required: `false`): 
    Similar to the first page, this is another general note section for the inspector to provide any additional information not captured in other fields.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when identifying fire hazards.
- Take your time to ensure all fields are filled out properly.
- If you're unsure about a particular hazard or field, refer to your inspection protocol or consult with a supervisor.
