# Parade Safety Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for assessing the safety of an event, specifically a parade. It is intended for use by event organizers to identify and evaluate potential risks and develop a plan to mitigate them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form and enter your answers in the corresponding text fields.
2. For the `Risk Controls` field, select all applicable controls that will be implemented to mitigate risks.
3. For the `Weather Conditions` field, select the current weather conditions on the day of the event.
4. For the `Review Responses` field, indicate your level of agreement with the assessment.
5. Finally, select "Yes" to submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Risk Risks**: This field is where you will list all potential risks associated with the event. Enter a brief description of each risk in the text field.
* **Risk Controls**: This field is where you will select all controls that will be implemented to mitigate risks. Choose all options that apply.
* **Event Planning**: This field is where you will enter any additional information about the event plan, such as the event schedule, participant information, or any specific safety considerations.
* **Safety Plan**: This field is where you will outline the overall safety plan for the event, including any measures to be taken in case of emergencies.
* **Contact Info**: This field is where you will enter the contact information of the person in charge of the event.
* **Phone Number**: This field is where you will enter the phone number of the person in charge of the event.
* **Location Info**: This field is where you will enter the location and any relevant details about the event site.
* **Emergency Plan**: This field is where you will outline the emergency plan, including procedures for responding to injuries, illnesses, or other incidents.
* **Weather Conditions**: This field is where you will select the current weather conditions on the day of the event.
* **Risk Factors**: This field is where you will enter any additional information about factors that may increase the risk of injury or harm.
* **Review Responses**: This field is where you will indicate your level of agreement with the assessment. Choose "Agree", "Disagree", or "Undecided".
* **Submit Form**: This field is where you will confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the assessment. Choose "Yes" to submit the form.

## Tips

* Please be thorough and accurate when filling out this form to ensure the safety of participants and attendees.
* If there are any changes to the original form, please resubmit the form to ensure that the safety plan is up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please seek guidance from your supervisor or safety officer.
