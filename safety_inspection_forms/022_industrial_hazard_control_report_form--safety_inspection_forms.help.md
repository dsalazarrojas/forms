# industrial_hazard_control_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Industrial Hazard Control Report Form** is designed to facilitate the reporting and control of hazards in industrial workplaces. This form is used to collect information about hazards, their control measures, and review status to ensure safe working conditions and compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **General Information** section, which includes fields for **Work Site**, **Job Title**, **Department**, **Shift**, and **Work Hours**.
2. Enter the **Work Date** and **Work Time**.
3. Complete the **Supervisor Information** section by entering the **Supervisor Name**, **Supervisor Email**, and **Supervisor Phone**.
4. Select the **Work Location** from the options provided.
5. Enter **Hazard Control Measurements**, **Safety Equipment**, **Safety Equipment Location**, **Notes**.
6. Set the **Review Date**, **Next Review**, and **Review By**.
7. Enter any **Review Comments**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Work Site** (`work_site`, text, required): Enter the name of the site where the hazard was identified.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title or role.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department or team.
* **Shift** (`shift`, text, required): Enter your shift or work schedule.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, text, required): Enter your work hours.
* **Work Date** (`work_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the hazard control.
* **Work Time** (`work_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the hazard control.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, required): Enter the email of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your supervisor.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, select_multiple, required): Select the location where the hazard was identified.
* **Hazard Control Measurements** (`hazard_control_measurements`, number, required): Enter any measurements related to the hazard control.
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, text, required): Enter the safety equipment used to control the hazard.
* **Safety Equipment Location** (`safety_equipment_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the safety equipment.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes related to the hazard control.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required): Set the review date for the hazard control.
* **Next Review** (`next_review`, date, required): Set the next review date for the hazard control.
* **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by`, text, required): Enter the person who reviewed the hazard control.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, note, required): Enter any comments from the reviewer.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required): Select the approval status for the hazard control.
