# structural_failure_incident_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about structural failures that have occurred. It helps in documenting incidents and analyzing their root causes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in the **Incident Summary** field, which provides a brief overview of the incident. This section helps in summarizing the key points of the structural failure.
2.  In the **Incident Date** field, enter the date when the incident occurred.
3.  The **Structural Elements** field is a multiple-choice section where you select all the structural elements that were involved in the incident. Choose from the available options to accurately represent the elements affected.
4.  In the **Failure Type** field, select the type of failure that occurred.
5.  Finally, provide a detailed description of the **Root Cause** in the provided text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Summary** (incident_summary): A brief summary of the structural failure incident.
* **Incident Date** (incident_date): Enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **Structural Elements** (structural_elements): Select all structural elements involved in the incident (e.g., beams, columns, foundations, etc.).
* **Failure Type** (failure_type): Choose the type of failure that occurred (e.g., collapse, damage, malfunction, etc.).
* **Root Cause** (root_cause): Provide a detailed explanation of the root cause of the structural failure.

## Tips

*   Be as specific as possible when describing the incident and its root cause.
*   Ensure that you are providing accurate information to facilitate correct analysis and decision-making.
*   If you're unsure about any field, please feel free to contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
*   This form can be used by anyone, and any feedback or suggestions for improvement are welcome.

Note: Please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly, as it is designed to assist in understanding and addressing structural failure incidents efficiently.
