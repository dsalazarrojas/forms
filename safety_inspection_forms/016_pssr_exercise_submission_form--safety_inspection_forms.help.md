# pssr_exercise_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to facilitate the submission of exercises to assess the effectiveness of emergency procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide a clear and concise summary of the exercise, including the title and outcome.
2. Indicate whether the exercise was successful ('Yes' or 'No').
3. Add any further notes or observations that may be helpful in the review process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exercise Summary** (pssr_exercise_summary): Enter a brief description of the exercise, including the title and outcome.
* **Exercise Outcome** (pssr_exercise_outcome): Select 'Yes' if the exercise was successful, 'No' if it was not.
* **Exercise Notes** (pssr_exercise_notes): Enter any additional notes or observations that may be helpful in the review process.

## Tips
* Ensure that this form is completed only once per exercise, to prevent duplication of effort.
* Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
