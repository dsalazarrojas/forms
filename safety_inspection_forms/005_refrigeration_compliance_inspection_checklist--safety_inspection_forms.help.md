# Refrigeration Compliance Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide inspectors through the process of evaluating refrigeration units for compliance with relevant regulations and standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the location of the refrigeration unit in the "Facility Location" field.
2. Provide the name of the refrigeration unit in the "Facility Name" field.
3. Select the correct refrigeration unit type from the "Refrigeration Unit" dropdown menu.
4. Record the temperature reading in the "Temperature" field.
5. Enter the temperature range if applicable in the "Temperature Range" field.
6. Check the refrigerant level and record it in the "Refrigerant Level" field.
7. Conduct a leak test and select the test results in the "Leak Test Results" field.
8. Evaluate the maintenance status of the unit and select it from the "Maintenance Status" field.
9. Record any comments or notes in the "Comments" field.
10. Enter the inspector's signature and date in the "Signature" field.
11. Enter your name and job title in the "Name" and "Job Title" fields.
12. Enter your company and department in the "Company" and "Department" fields.
13. Provide your contact information in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
14. Enter the inspection location in the "Inspection Location" field.
15. Record the inspection status in the "Inspection Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Refrigeration Checklist** (Field 1): A brief description of the refrigeration unit's condition and status.
* **Location** (Field 2): The location of the refrigeration unit.
* **Inspection Date** (Field 3): The date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (Field 4): The time of the inspection.
* **Temperature** (Field 5): The current temperature reading of the refrigeration unit.
* **Temperature Range** (Field 6): The temperature range of the refrigeration unit if applicable.
* **Refrigeration Unit** (Field 7): The type of refrigeration unit being inspected.
* **Refrigerant Level** (Field 8): The current refrigerant level of the unit.
* **Leak Test Results** (Field 9): The results of the leak test.
* **Maintenance Status** (Field 10): The maintenance status of the unit.
* **Comments** (Field 11): Any additional comments or notes.
* **Signature** (Field 12): The inspector's signature and date.
* **Name** (Field 13): The inspector's name.
* **Job Title** (Field 14): The inspector's job title.
* **Company** (Field 15): The company name.
* **Department** (Field 16): The department name.
* **Email** (Field 17): The inspector's email address.
* **Phone** (Field 18): The inspector's phone number.
* **Facility Location** (Field 19): The location of the facility.
* **Facility Name** (Field 20): The name of the facility.
* **Inspection Location** (Field 21): The location of the inspection.
* **Inspection Status** (Field 22): The status of the inspection.

Note: The fields marked as "Required" must be completed for the form to be considered valid.
