# Manufacturing Safety Protocol Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manufacturing Safety Protocol Evaluation form is used to assess the safety protocols of a manufacturing facility, ensuring they meet regulatory and company standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Start by selecting the "Manufacturing Safety Protocol" page**. This page will provide a general overview of the facility's safety protocols.
2. **Review the options on the "Evaluation Form" page**. Select the most accurate option that describes your evaluation of the facility's safety protocols.
3. **Provide detailed safety review notes on the "Safety Review" page**. This is an opportunity to provide any additional comments or observations about the facility's safety protocols.
4. **Select the relevant Safety Inspection Forms from the "Safety Inspection Forms" page**. Make sure to choose all applicable forms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Manufacturing Safety Protocol** (`Manufacturing Safety Protocol`, text, false): This field is where you should provide a general overview of the facility's safety protocols.
* **Evaluation Form** (`Evaluation Form`, select_one, false): This field asks you to choose the most accurate option that describes your evaluation of the facility's safety protocols.
* **Safety Review** (`Safety Review`, text, false): This is where you can provide detailed safety review notes. Use this space to elaborate on any observations or comments you have about the facility's safety protocols.
* **Safety Inspection Forms** (`Safety Inspection Forms`, select_multiple, true): This field allows you to choose which Safety Inspection Forms are relevant to the facility's safety protocols. Make sure to select all applicable forms.
