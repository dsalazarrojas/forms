# Workplace Safety Innovation Award Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workplace Safety Innovation Award Nomination Form is a tool for nominating a workplace safety initiative, innovation, or technology for recognition and potential award. The form is used to collect information about the nomination, including the title, description, nomination date, and submission status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the Nomination Form section with a brief description of the nominated safety initiative.
2. Select the Nomination Type (Safety Initiative, New Technology, or Other).
3. Choose a Nomination Category from the provided options.
4. Enter the Nomination Date and Time.
5. Provide the Nomination Email and Phone number.
6. Include an image or video URL (optional).
7. Optionally, enter any additional information or comments about the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Form** (`nomination_form`): Enter a brief description of the nominated safety initiative.
* **Company Information** (`company_info`): Enter the name and contact information of the company submitting the nomination.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Nomination Title** (`nomination_title`): Enter a title for the nomination.
* **Description** (`nomination_description`): Enter a brief description of the nominated safety initiative.
* **Nomination Type** (`nomination_type`): Select the type of nomination from the provided options (Safety Initiative, New Technology, or Other). If "Other", you can enter additional information in the **Type** field.
* **Type** (`nomination_type_other`): Enter the additional information about the nomination type (for "Other" selections).
* **Nomination Category** (`nomination_category`): Choose a category for the nomination from the provided options.
* **Category** (`nomination_category_other`): Enter any additional information about the nomination category (for "Other" selections).
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`): Enter the date the nomination was submitted.
* **Date** (`nomination_date_other`): Enter any additional information about the nomination date (for "Other" selections).
* **Nomination Time** (`nomination_time`): Enter the time the nomination was submitted.
* **Time** (`nomination_time_other`): Enter any additional information about the nomination time (for "Other" selections).
* **Nomination Email** (`nomination_email`): Enter the email address associated with the nomination.
* **Email** (`nomination_email_other`): Enter any additional information about the nomination email (for "Other" selections).
* **Nomination Phone** (`nomination_phone`): Enter the phone number associated with the nomination.
* **Phone** (`nomination_phone_other`): Enter any additional information about the nomination phone number (for "Other" selections).
* **Nomination Image** (`nomination_image`): Enter the URL of an image or video related to the nomination.
* **Image** (`nomination_image_other`): Enter any additional information about the image or video (for "Other" selections).
* **Nomination URL** (`nomination_url`): Enter the URL of the nomination.
* **URL** (`nomination_url_other`): Enter any additional information about the nomination URL (for "Other" selections).
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`): Select the current status of the submission from the provided options (In Process, Approved, or Rejected). If "Other", you can enter additional information in the **Status** field.
* **Status** (`submission_status_other`): Enter any additional information about the submission status (for "Other" selections).

## Tips

* Before submitting the form, ensure that you have not already submitted a nomination for the same project or idea.
* Double-check that all fields are filled out correctly to avoid errors.
* If you need to provide additional information, use the "Other" fields to enter your comments or explanations.
