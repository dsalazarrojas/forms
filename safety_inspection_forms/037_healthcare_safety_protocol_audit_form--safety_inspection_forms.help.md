# Healthcare Safety Protocol Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the safety protocols in place in a healthcare setting. It is used to ensure that the necessary safety measures are in place to protect patients, employees, and visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what each question is asking.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
3. Use the options provided in the select multiple and select one fields.
4. If a field is not required, you can skip it if it does not apply to your situation.
5. Use the date and time fields to enter dates and times as needed.
6. Submit the form when you have completed all the questions.
