# Workplace Safety Compliance Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify that your workplace safety management system is in compliance with regulatory requirements and industry standards. Please answer the questions honestly, and if you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact your safety officer or supervisor for clarification.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose the correct option from the list provided.
3. For text questions, enter your answer in the provided text box.
4. Please indicate whether you have completed or partially completed each question, as applicable.
5. If you are unsure or cannot answer a question, please contact your safety officer or supervisor for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Training** (`Employee Training`, select_one, required):
	+ This question is about the training provided to employees on workplace safety practices. Please indicate whether your employees have received training on all necessary aspects of workplace safety.
* **Safety Practices** (`Safety Practices`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ This question is about the safety practices followed in your workplace. Please indicate which practices are currently in place.
* **Regulatory Requirements** (`Regulatory Requirements`, number, optional):
	+ This question is about the regulatory requirements that need to be met. Please enter the number of regulatory requirements that your workplace is currently meeting.
* **Hazards** (`Hazards`, text, optional):
	+ This question is about the potential hazards present in your workplace. Please describe any hazards that are present, and take note of any measures taken to mitigate them.
* **Incident Reporting** (`Incident Reporting`, select_one, optional):
	+ This question is about incident reporting. Please indicate whether incidents are reported and investigated in your workplace.
* **Safety Equipment** (`Safety Equipment`, select_one, optional):
	+ This question is about the safety equipment present in your workplace. Please indicate whether all necessary equipment is available and functional.
* **Employee Involvement** (`Employee Involvement`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ This question is about employee involvement in safety practices. Please indicate which employees are involved in safety practices.
* **Training Programs** (`Training Programs`, select_one, optional):
	+ This question is about training programs for employees. Please indicate whether training programs are in place.
* **Safety Equipment Testing** (`Safety Equipment Testing`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ This question is about the safety equipment testing process. Please indicate which equipment are currently being tested.
* **Safety Inspection Frequency** (`Safety Inspection Frequency`, select_one, optional):
	+ This question is about the frequency of safety inspections. Please select the frequency of safety inspections in your workplace.
