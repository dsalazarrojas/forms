# SCBA Safety Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The SCBA Safety Assessment Survey is designed to gather information about the status and conditions of Self-Controlling Breathing Apparatus (SCBA) equipment in the workplace. This survey is intended to ensure that SCBA equipment is properly maintained, inspected, and used in accordance with safety regulations and guidelines. By completing this form, you will be contributing to the upkeep and safety of your work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the current status of the SCBA equipment from the options provided.
2. Indicate whether the SCBA equipment is in active or inactive status.
3. Identify the SCBA equipment that is currently available and in use.
4. Enter the location where the SCBA equipment is stored or kept.
5. Specify the date when the SCBA last used.
6. Enter the date when the SCBA last underwent maintenance.
7. Enter the date when the SCBA last underwent inspection.
8. Add any additional notes about the SCBA equipment status or condition.
9. Provide the name of the assessor who conducted the SCBA inspection or assessment.
10. Select the type of SCBA inspection or assessment performed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Scba Status** (`scba_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the current status of the SCBA equipment from the options provided. The options are "Active" and "Inactive".
* **Scba Equipment** (`scba_equipment`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose all SCBA equipment that are currently available and in use.
* **Scba Location** (`scba_location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location where the SCBA equipment is stored or kept. This can be a specific room or area in the facility.
* **Scba Last Use Date** (`scba_last_use_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the SCBA equipment last used. If not used, select a date indicating "Never Used".
* **Scba Last Maintenance Date** (`scba_last_maintenance_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the SCBA equipment last underwent maintenance.
* **Scba Last Inspection Date** (`scba_last_inspection_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the SCBA equipment last underwent inspection.
* **Scba Status Notes** (`scba_status_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the SCBA equipment status or condition.
* **Scba Assessor Name** (`scba_assessor_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the assessor who conducted the SCBA inspection or assessment.
* **Scba Inspection Type** (`scba_inspection_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of SCBA inspection or assessment performed. The options are "Yes" and "No".
