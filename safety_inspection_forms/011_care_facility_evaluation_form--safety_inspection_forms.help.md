# Care Facility Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Care Facility Evaluation Form is designed to assess the safety and quality of care provided by residential care facilities. This form is used by inspectors or surveyors to evaluate facilities and identify areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field based on your observation or evaluation.
2. Be as specific as possible when filling out fields that require text or number input.
3. If you need to provide additional information or comments, use the "Deficiencies Noted" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility being evaluated.
* **Facility Address**: Enter the full address of the facility.
* **Facility Type**: Select the type of facility being evaluated (e.g., Assisted Living Facility, Nursing Home, etc.).
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluator Name**: Enter your name and title/position.
* **Evaluator Role**: Select your role during the evaluation (e.g., State Inspector, Accreditation Surveyor, etc.).
* **Facility Capacity**: Enter the number of residents or residents capacity the facility has.
* **Current Occupancy**: Enter the number of residents currently living in the facility.
* **Overall Cleanliness Rating**: Select the overall cleanliness rating of the facility (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Safety Features Present**: Select all safety features that are present in the facility (e.g., handrails, emergency call systems, etc.).
* **Staff Responsiveness**: Select the level of staff responsiveness (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Staff to Resident Ratio**: Select the staff to resident ratio (Excellent, Good, Adequate, Below adequate).
* **Medication Management**: Select the level of medication management (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Not Observed).
* **Dining Services Quality**: Select the quality of dining services (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Dietary Accommodations Available**: Select all dietary accommodations available to residents (e.g., low sodium options, diabetic-friendly meals, etc.).
* **Activity Programs**: Select the level of activity programs (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Social Engagement Opportunities**: Select the level of social engagement opportunities (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Resident Room Conditions**: Select the overall condition of resident rooms (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Bathroom Accessibility**: Select the level of bathroom accessibility (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Outdoor Spaces**: Select the quality and accessibility of outdoor spaces (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Infection Control Measures**: Select the level of infection control measures in place (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Complaint Resolution Process**: Select the clarity and effectiveness of the complaint resolution process (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Family Involvement Opportunities**: Select the level of family involvement opportunities (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Overall Facility Rating**: Select the overall rating of the facility (5 stars, 4 stars, 3 stars, 2 stars, 1 star).
* **Deficiencies Noted**: If you have noted any deficiencies during the evaluation, list them here.
* **Recommendations for Improvement**: If you have any recommendations for improvement, list them here.
* **Follow-up Inspection Required**: Select the follow-up inspection frequency required (Yes, within 30 days, 90 days, 1 year, No routine follow-up).
