# Pre Task Safety Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Task Safety Assessment form is used to evaluate the safety risks associated with a particular task. This form helps identify potential hazards and control measures that need to be taken before commencing the task.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the following questions about the task:
	* Do you have identified any hazards associated with the task? 
	* Are there any control measures that need to be taken to mitigate these hazards?
	* Do you have attended a safety brief?
	* Briefly describe the task
	* Enter the ID of the task and the ID of the supervisor responsible for the task
	* Enter the start and end times of the task in 24 hour format
	* Enter the duration of the task
	* Enter the date of the task
	* What is the risk level of the task (High or Low)?

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pre-Task Safety Assessment** (`pre_task_safety_assessment`): This field is a text box where you can enter any notes or comments about the safety assessment of the task.
* **Hazards** (`hazards`): Select all hazards associated with the task. Please choose 'Yes' or 'No' for each hazard.
* **Control Measures** (`control_measures`): Select the control measures that need to be taken to mitigate the hazards. Please choose 'Yes' or 'No' for each control measure.
* **Safety Briefings** (`safety_briefings`): Select all safety briefings that you have attended. Please choose 'Yes' or 'No' for each briefing.
* **Task Description** (`task_description`): Briefly describe the task in a few words.
* **Task ID** (`task_id`): Enter the ID of the task.
* **Supervisor ID** (`supervisor_id`): Enter the ID of the supervisor responsible for the task.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`): Enter the start time of the task in 24 hour format.
* **End Time** (`end_time`): Enter the end time of the task in 24 hour format.
* **Duration** (`duration`): Enter the duration of the task.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the task.
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`): Select the risk level of the task. Please choose 'High' or 'Low'.

## Tips
- Please provide detailed and accurate information for each field.
- Please make sure to answer all required fields.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
