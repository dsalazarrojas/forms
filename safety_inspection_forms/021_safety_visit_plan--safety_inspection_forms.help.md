# Safety Visit Plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Visit Plan is a form used to document and record visit details, risk levels, and safety measures taken to ensure a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the required and optional fields.
2. Fill out each field accurately and consistently with the company's information.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the company being visited.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the visit in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the visit in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Visit Duration** (`visit_duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the duration of the visit in minutes.
* **Safety Inspector** (`safety_inspector`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the safety inspector conducting the visit.
* **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): Select the frequency of visits from the options provided (daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, select_one, required/optional): Select the risk level from the options provided (low, medium, high).
* **Safety Measures Taken** (`safety_measures`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the safety measures taken during the visit from the options provided (measure1, measure2, measure3).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the visit.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the company being visited.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the user assigned to the visit.

Tips:
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and consistently with the company's information.
* Review the form carefully to understand the required and optional fields.
* Double-check the information entered to ensure accuracy and consistency.
