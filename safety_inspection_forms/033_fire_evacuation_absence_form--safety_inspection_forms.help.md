# Fire Evacuation Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record incidents of employees being absent from their workstations due to fire evacuations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you have the necessary permissions and approvals to access and complete this form.
2. Select the correct options for "Fire Alarm Activated" and "Fire Evacuated" based on the actual events that occurred.
3. Enter your Employee ID, Date Time, and other relevant details accurately.
4. Provide a brief Reason for the employee's absence.
5. If applicable, enter the Supervisor Name, Employee Name, Employee Number, Department, Building Name, and Room Number for the affected employee.
6. If applicable, provide the contact number and location of the employee during the incident.
7. Select the correct status for "Employee Status" and "Employee Absent".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fire Evacuation Absence Form** (`fire_evacuation_absence_form`): This is the title of the form.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter the ID of the employee who was absent due to the fire evacuation.
* **Date Time** (`date_time`): Enter the date and time of the fire evacuation.
* **Reason** (`reason`): Enter a brief reason for the employee's absence due to the fire evacuation.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`): If applicable, enter the name of the supervisor who authorized the absence.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): If applicable, enter the name of the employee who was absent.
* **Employee Number** (`employee_number`): If applicable, enter the ID number of the employee who was absent.
* **Department** (`department`): If applicable, enter the department where the employee works.
* **Building Name** (`building_name`): If applicable, enter the name of the building where the employee was located.
* **Room Number** (`room_number`): If applicable, enter the room number where the employee was located.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): If applicable, enter the contact number of the employee during the incident.
* **Location** (`location`): If applicable, enter the location where the employee was during the incident.
* **Fire Alarm Activated** (`fire_alarm`): Select "Yes" if the fire alarm was activated during the incident.
* **Fire Evacuated** (`fire_evacuated`): Select "Yes" if the employee was evacuated during the incident.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`): Select the status of the employee (Active or Inactive).
* **Employee Absent** (`employee_absent`): Select "Yes" if the employee was absent during the incident.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): If applicable, enter the emergency contact information of the employee.
* **Fire Alarm Activated** (`fire_alarm`) and **Fire Evacuated** (`fire_evacuated`) are related fields and should be completed accordingly.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`) and **Employee Absent** (`employee_absent`) are related fields and should be completed accordingly.

## Tips

* If the employee was evacuated, make sure to check the "Fire Evacuated" field accordingly.
* If the employee was not evacuated, make sure to check the "Fire Evacuated" field to "No".
* If the employee was absent, make sure to check the "Employee Absent" field accordingly.
* If the employee was present, make sure to check the "Employee Absent" field to "No".
