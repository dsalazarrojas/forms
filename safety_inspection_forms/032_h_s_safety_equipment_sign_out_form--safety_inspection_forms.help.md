# H₂S Safety Equipment Sign Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The H₂S Safety Equipment Sign Out Form is designed to track the sign out and return of safety equipment in a workplace. This form helps maintain accurate records of equipment availability, ensuring that employees can access the equipment they need for tasks and projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the equipment you wish to sign out using the "Equipment Selected" field.
2. Enter your name in the "User Name" field.
3. Choose a date and time when you signed out the equipment in the "Date Signed Out" and "Time Signed Out" fields.
4. Select the current status of the equipment from the options provided.
5. Add any additional comments or notes regarding the equipment's condition or usage.
6. Finally, choose a date and time when you expect to return the equipment in the "Date Return" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Equipment** (`Equipment Selected`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the safety equipment you wish to sign out from the available options. This ensures that the correct equipment is tracked and maintained accurately.
* **User Name** (`User Name`, `text`, required): Enter your name to associate it with the equipment sign out. This helps track usage and maintain records.
* **Date Signed Out** (`Date Signed Out`, `date`, required): Choose the date you signed out the equipment. This helps maintain a record of usage and ensures the correct tracking of availability.
* **Date Return** (`Date Return`, `date`, required): Select a date when you expect to return the equipment. This helps plan for its maintenance and ensures it's back in stock.
* **Time Signed Out** (`Time Signed Out`, `time`, required): Indicate the time when you signed out the equipment.
* **Equipment Status** (`Equipment Status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of the equipment from the options provided (Available, Unavailable, In Use).
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, optional): Add any comments or notes regarding the equipment's condition or usage.
* **Date Return** is also used to track when the equipment is expected to be returned, which helps plan for its maintenance and ensures it's back in stock.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct equipment to avoid any confusion or errors in tracking.
* Enter accurate dates and times to maintain precise records.
* Use the notes field to add any important information regarding the equipment's condition or usage.
* Always return the equipment on the selected date to ensure it's available for the next user.
