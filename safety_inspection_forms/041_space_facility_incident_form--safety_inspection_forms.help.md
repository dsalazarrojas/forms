# space_facility_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The space_facility_incident_form is used to report incidents or near-miss incidents within a space facility. This form helps gather relevant information about the incident, including details about the incident itself, the location, and the person who reported it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, start by providing the basic details of the incident, such as the location and the incident date and time. Then, provide a detailed description of what happened, including any images or relevant information that may help with the investigation. Finally, identify yourself and provide your contact information in case you need to be reached for further questions or follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details** (Incident Details): Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Location** (Location): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Date** (Incident Date): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (Incident Time): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (Incident Description): Enter a detailed description of what happened, including any images or relevant information that may help with the investigation.
* **Incident Image** (Incident Image): If applicable, attach any images related to the incident.
* **Incident Reported By** (Incident Reported By): Enter your name as the person reporting the incident.
* **Incident Reported By Email** (Incident Reported By Email): Enter your email address so we can reach you for further questions or follow-up.
* **Incident Reported By Phone** (Incident Reported By Phone): Enter your phone number so we can reach you for further questions or follow-up.

## Tips
- Please provide accurate and detailed information about the incident.
- If you have any images or relevant information that may help with the investigation, attach them to the form.
- Make sure to enter your contact information so we can reach you for further questions or follow-up.
