# Temporary Electrical Safety Certification Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request temporary electrical certification for a project or equipment, ensuring it meets the necessary safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Project Title** in the first field. This is the title of the project for which you are requesting temporary certification.
2. If applicable, enter the **Project Number** in the second field. This number is used to identify the project within our system.
3. Enter your **Customer Name** in the third field. This is the name of the customer or person requesting the certification.
4. Select the relevant **Electrical Certification** option from the dropdown list in the fourth field. This indicates the type of certification you are requesting.
5. In the fifth field, provide a list of the **Equipment** used for the project, one item per line.
6. Describe the **Equipment Description** in the sixth field, including any relevant details about the equipment.
7. Enter the **Equipment Location** in the seventh field, where the equipment will be used.
8. Enter the **Equipment Serial Number** in the eighth field, if applicable.
9. If applicable, select the **Approval Status** in the eleventh field, indicating the current status of your request.
10. Enter the **Certification Date** in the ninth field, if you have a specific certification date.
11. Enter the **Certification Number** in the tenth field, if you have a specific certification number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Title (1)**: Enter the title of the project for which you are requesting temporary certification.
* **Project Number (2)**: If applicable, enter the project number to identify the project within our system.
* **Customer Name (3)**: Enter the name of the customer or person requesting the certification.
* **Electrical Certification (4)**: Select the type of certification you are requesting from the dropdown list.
* **Equipment List (5)**: Provide a list of the equipment used for the project, one item per line.
* **Equipment Description (6)**: Describe the equipment used, including any relevant details.
* **Equipment Location (7)**: Enter the location where the equipment will be used.
* **Equipment Serial Number (8)**: Enter the serial number of the equipment, if applicable.
* **Certification Date (9)**: Enter the date of the certification, if you have a specific date.
* **Certification Number (10)**: Enter the certification number, if you have a specific number.
* **Approval Status (11)**: Select the current status of your request from the dropdown list.

## Tips
* Ensure you have all necessary information before completing the form.
* Double-check the form for errors before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authority or project administrator.
