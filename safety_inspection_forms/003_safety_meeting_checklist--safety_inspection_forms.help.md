# Safety Meeting Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Safety Meeting Checklist form is a tool used to document important information about safety meetings. It helps teams and managers keep track of meeting dates, attendees, and action items, which can improve safety processes and prevent accidents.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the meeting from the calendar.
2. Choose whether the meeting was held at a specific location or not.
3. Select the attendees for the meeting.
4. Identify if there were leaders present for the meeting.
5. Check if any hazards were found during the meeting.
6. Enter any action plans that were discussed or agreed upon during the meeting.
7. Describe the safety equipment that was available or used during the meeting.
8. Enter the date of the next meeting.
9. Provide the location of the next meeting.
10. Enter the leaders of the next meeting.
11. Enter any notes about the next meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the safety meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the meeting was held at a specific location or not.
* **Meeting Attendees** (`meeting_attendees`, select_multiple, optional): Select all attendees for the meeting.
* **Meeting Leaders** (`meeting_leaders`, select_one, optional): Identify if there were leaders present for the meeting.
* **Hazards Found** (`hazards_found`, select_multiple, optional): Check if any hazards were found during the meeting.
* **Action Plans** (`action_plans`, text, optional): Enter any action plans that were discussed or agreed upon during the meeting.
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, text, optional): Describe the safety equipment that was available or used during the meeting.
* **Next Meeting Date** (`next_meeting_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the next meeting.
* **Next Meeting Location** (`next_meeting_location`, text, optional): Provide the location of the next meeting.
* **Next Meeting Leaders** (`next_meeting_leaders`, text, optional): Enter the leaders of the next meeting.
* **Next Meeting Notes** (`next_meeting_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes about the next meeting.
* **Safety Meeting Notes** (`safety_meeting_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the safety meeting.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you complete the form for each safety meeting separately.
* Be thorough when describing the safety equipment and action plans.
* Enter the next meeting date and location as early as possible to avoid confusion.
* Use the provided fields to keep track of all the important details of the safety meeting.
