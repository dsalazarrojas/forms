# University Dormitory Health Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for regular health inspections of residence halls, allowing inspectors to document observations, findings, and follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Leave optional fields blank if not applicable.
3. Use the provided options (e.g., select one from the dropdown list) to ensure accurate data entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Residence Hall ID** (`residence_hall_id`, number, not required): Enter the unique ID of the residence hall being inspected.
* **Inspector ID** (`inspector_id`, number, not required): Enter your inspector ID, if applicable.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, not required): Enter your name, if applicable.
* **Inspector Department** (`inspector_department`, text, not required): Enter your department, if applicable.
* **Residence Hall Name** (`residence_hall_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the residence hall being inspected.
* **Room Block** (`room_block`, text, not required): Enter the room block number, if applicable.
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, text, not required): Enter the room number, if applicable.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Inspection Status** (`inspection_status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the inspection: Available, Not Available, or Deferred.
* **Findings** (`findings`, text, not required): Document any issues or observations during the inspection.
* **Next Follow-up** (`next_follow_up`, date, not required): Enter the next follow-up date, if applicable.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Add any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, not required): Enter your name, if submitting the form.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, not required): Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Submitted Department** (`submitted_department`, text, not required): Enter your department, if submitting the form.
* **Submitted By Name** (`submitted_by_name`, text, not required): Enter your name, if submitting the form.
* **Submitted By Position** (`submitted_by_position`, text, not required): Enter your position, if submitting the form.
* **Submitted By Contact No.** (`submitted_by_contact_no`, text, not required): Enter your contact number.
* **Submitted By Contact Number** (`submitted_by_contact_no_alt`, text, not required): Enter your contact number.
* **Submitted By Email** (`submitted_by_email`, email, not required): Enter your email.
* **Submitted By Department** (`submitted_by_department`, text, not required): Enter your department, if submitting the form.
* **Follow-up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`, text, not required): Add any follow-up comments, if applicable.
