# Lockout/Tagout Procedures Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and manage lockout/tagout procedures training for employees. It helps track the training process, including employee attendance, trainer involvement, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely and honestly**. Each field is required to ensure that we have accurate information.
2. **Enter your employee name** in the "Employee Name" field, which is used to identify you as the individual who attended the training.
3. **Enter the date of your training** in the "Training Date" field. This is the date when you received the training.
4. **Enter the name of your trainer** in the "Trainer Name" field. This is the person who provided the training.
5. **Enter the training session** details in the "Training Session" field. This should include the session number, date, and other relevant information.
6. **Select your attendance** status in the "Employee Attendance" field:
	* Attended: If you attended the training session
	* Did not attend: If you did not attend the training session
	* Unable to determine: If you cannot determine whether you attended or not
7. **Enter the location** where you received the training in the "Training Location" field.
8. **Indicate whether you received training during the lockout/tagout** procedures in the "Training During Lockout/Tagout" field:
	* True: If you received training during the lockout/tagout procedures
	* False: If you did not receive training during the lockout/tagout procedures
9. **Sign your supervisor's approval** in the "Supervisor Signature" field. This confirms that you have received the required training.
10. **Sign your own acknowledgment** in the "Employee Signature" field. This confirms that you have received the training and understand its requirements.
