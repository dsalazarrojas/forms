# Outdoor Seating Safety Test Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outdoor Seating Safety Test Application Form is used to assess the safety and condition of outdoor seating areas on properties. This information helps maintain a safe and healthy environment for users and ensures compliance with relevant safety regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2. Select the correct category(ies) for the outdoor seating area you're reporting.
3. Provide a description of the outdoor seating area.
4. Enter the facility manager's name and contact information.
5. Upload any relevant documentation or notes (if applicable).
6. Select the date and time of the inspection.
7. Ensure all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more categories that describe the outdoor seating area. For example, "Yes" or "No" to indicate if it's accessible, has amenities like tables or chairs, etc.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe the outdoor seating area, including its location, size, and condition.
* **Facility Manager Name** (`facility_manager_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the facility manager responsible for maintaining the outdoor seating area.
* **Facility Manager Email** (`facility_manager_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the facility manager.
* **Facility Manager Phone** (`facility_manager_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the facility manager.
* **Facility Manager Notes** (`facility_manager_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments from the facility manager.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date of the safety inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time of the safety inspection.
* **Upload Documentation** (`upload_documentation`, `note`, required: false): Upload any supporting documentation or evidence related to the safety inspection.

Note: If a field is marked as "required: false", it's not mandatory to fill out, but it's recommended for completeness.
