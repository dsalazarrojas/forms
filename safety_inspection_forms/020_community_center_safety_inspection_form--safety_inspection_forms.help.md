# Community Center Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Center Safety Inspection Form is a tool used to conduct regular safety inspections of community centers to identify and assess potential hazards, ensure compliance with safety standards, and take corrective actions as needed. This form helps inspectors to document their findings, prioritize necessary repairs or fixes, and schedule follow-up inspections to confirm compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Inspector name** (Field 1) with the name of the person performing the inspection.
2. Enter the **Inspection date** (Field 2) in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Identify the **Area inspected** (Field 3) by entering the specific location within the community center that was inspected (e.g., kitchen, main hall, storage).
4. If you observed any **Hazards** (Field 4), list them in detail.
5. Assess the **Compliance status** (Field 5) as:
	* **Compliant**: The community center is in full compliance with safety standards.
	* **Non-compliant**: The community center is not in full compliance with safety standards.
	* **Needs attention**: The community center is partially compliant with safety standards, but some issues need to be addressed.
6. If **Corrective actions** (Field 6) are required, provide a brief description of recommended repairs or fixes.
7. Select the **Priority level for corrective actions** (Field 7) as:
	* **Low**: The issue is minor and can be addressed in the near future.
	* **Medium**: The issue requires attention in the next inspection cycle or soon.
	* **High**: The issue requires immediate attention.
	* **Critical**: The issue poses an imminent risk and must be addressed as soon as possible.
8. If a **Follow-up date** (Field 8) is necessary, schedule a date to re-inspect or confirm fixes.
9. Finally, sign your **Inspector signature name** (Field 9) by typing your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspector name**: The person performing the inspection, which should be your name.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Inspection date**: The date of the inspection, in MM/DD/YYYY format.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: Yes
* **Area inspected**: The specific location within the community center that was inspected (e.g., kitchen, main hall, storage).
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Hazards observed**: A list of any safety hazards found during the inspection.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Compliance status**: An assessment of the community center's compliance with safety standards.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Yes
* **Corrective actions**: Recommended repairs or fixes to address identified hazards.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
* **Priority level for corrective actions**: The urgency of the issue.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Yes
* **Follow-up date**: A scheduled date to re-inspect or confirm fixes.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: No
* **Inspector signature name**: The full name of the inspector, serving as a signature.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes
