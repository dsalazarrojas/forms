# Workplace Safety Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Safety Report Form is used to report hazards and incidents in the workplace, ensuring a safe work environment is maintained.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Report the Hazard or Incident

* Fill in the "Hazard/Incident Reported" field with a brief description of the hazard or incident.

### Step 2: Provide Additional Details

* Enter the location where the hazard or incident occurred in the "Hazard/Location" field.
* Describe the hazard or incident in more detail in the "Additional Comments" field.

### Step 3: Maintenance Schedule and Status

* Select the maintenance schedule for safety equipment in the "Safety Equipment Maintenance Schedule" field.
* Check the maintenance status of safety equipment in the "Safety Equipment Maintenance Status" field.

### Step 4: Supervisor and Inspector Information

* Enter your supervisor's name and contact information in the "Supervisor Name" and "Supervisor Contact" fields.
* Record any comments from your supervisor in the "Supervisor Comments" field.
* Enter the name and contact information of the safety inspector in the "Safety Inspector Name" and "Safety Inspector Contact" fields.
* Record any comments from the safety inspector in the "Safety Inspector Comments" field.

### Step 5: Sign Off

* Sign the form in the "Signature" field to confirm that you have reported the hazard or incident.
* Enter your name and job title in the "Employee Name" and "Employee Title" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hazard/Incident Reported** (`workplace_safety_report_form`, text, required): A brief description of the hazard or incident.
* **Hazard/Location** (`hazard_location`, text, required): The location where the hazard or incident occurred.
* **Hazard Description** (`hazard_description`, text, required): A detailed description of the hazard or incident.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, text, required): A description of the actions taken to address the hazard or incident.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Any additional information or comments about the hazard or incident.
* **Safety Equipment Maintenance Date** (`safety_equipment_maintenance_date`, date, required): The date of the last maintenance of safety equipment.
* **Safety Equipment Maintenance Schedule** (`safety_equipment_maintenance_schedule`, select_one, required): The maintenance schedule for safety equipment (e.g., every 2 weeks, every 4 weeks, etc.).
* **Safety Equipment Maintenance Status** (`safety_equipment_maintenance_status`, select_multiple, required): The current maintenance status of safety equipment (e.g., not required, due soon, overdue, done).
* **Safety Equipment Maintenance Last Maintenance Date** (`safety_equipment_maintenance_last_maintenance_date`, date, required): The date of the last maintenance of safety equipment.
* **Safety Equipment Maintenance Next Maintenance Date** (`safety_equipment_maintenance_next_maintenance_date`, date, required): The date of the next maintenance of safety equipment.
* **Safety Equipment Maintenance Notes** (`safety_equipment_maintenance_notes`, text, required): Any notes about the maintenance of safety equipment.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): The name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Contact** (`supervisor_contact`, text, required): The contact information of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, text, required): Any comments from your supervisor.
* **Safety Inspector Name** (`safety_inspector_name`, text, required): The name of the safety inspector.
* **Safety Inspector Contact** (`safety_inspector_contact`, text, required): The contact information of the safety inspector.
* **Safety Inspector Comments** (`safety_inspector_comments`, text, required): Any comments from the safety inspector.
* **Safety Officer Name** (`safety_officer_name`, text, required): The name of the safety officer.
* **Safety Officer Contact** (`safety_officer_contact`, text, required): The contact information of the safety officer.
* **Safety Officer Comments** (`safety_officer_comments`, text, required): Any comments from the safety officer.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Your name.
* **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, text, required): Your job title.
* **Employee Sign Off** (`employee_sign_off`, text, required): Your electronic signature.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Your physical signature.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all necessary information is included.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or safety officer.
