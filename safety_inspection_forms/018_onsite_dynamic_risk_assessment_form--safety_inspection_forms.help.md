# Onsite Dynamic Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Onsite Dynamic Risk Assessment Form is designed to identify potential risks that may impact projects or operations on site, ensuring that our team is informed and prepared to take action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill out the required information for each field.
2. Start by selecting "First Page" and "Second Page", which will guide you through the risk assessment process.
3. On the "Risk Assessment" page, select the level of risk you perceive from the options (High, Medium, Low).
4. Provide a detailed description of the risk on the "Risk Description" page.
5. If applicable, identify any additional risk factors on the "Risk Factors" page.
6. Create a mitigation plan on the "Mitigation Plan" page.
7. Assign the risk to a team member on the "Assigned To" page.
8. If applicable, estimate the risk frequency, probability, and impact on the "Risk Frequency", "Risk Probability", and "Risk Impact" pages.
9. Summarize the risk information on the "Risk Summary" page.
10. Outline the next steps to take on the "Next Steps" page.
11. Add any additional comments or observations on the "Comments" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page (First Page, text, required):** Select this option to start the risk assessment process. This page serves as a guide to help you navigate the form.
* **Second Page (Second Page, text, required):** Select this option to proceed with the risk assessment process. This page provides additional guidance on how to complete the form.
* **Risk Assessment (Risk Assessment, select_one, required):** Choose the level of risk you perceive from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Risk Description (Risk Description, text, required):** Provide a detailed description of the risk you've identified.
* **Risk Level (Risk Level, select_multiple, optional):** Select any additional risk levels you'd like to identify.
* **Risk Factors (Risk Factors, text, required):** List any additional risk factors that may impact the risk.
* **Mitigation Plan (Mitigation Plan, note, required):** Describe your plan to mitigate the risk.
* **Assigned To (Assigned To, select_one, required):** Assign the risk to a team member for follow-up and action.
* **Risk Frequency (Risk Frequency, number, optional):** Estimate the frequency of the risk.
* **Risk Probability (Risk Probability, number, optional):** Estimate the probability of the risk.
* **Risk Impact (Risk Impact, number, optional):** Estimate the potential impact of the risk.
* **Risk Summary (Risk Summary, text, required):** Summarize the risk information you've collected.
* **Next Steps (Next Steps, text, required):** Outline the next steps to take to mitigate the risk.
* **Comments (Comments, note, required):** Add any additional comments or observations about the risk.
