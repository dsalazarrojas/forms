# Gate Control Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for performing safety inspections on gates, ensuring that they are properly controlled and functioning according to established standards. By completing this form, users can report on the state of a gate, including any issues or concerns they have identified during their inspection.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to the Gate Control Checklist page.
2. Select the access point being inspected from the dropdown menu.
3. Describe the location of the gate, including any relevant details.
4. Assess the condition of the gate by selecting one of the options (Good, Poor, or Unavailable).
5. If necessary, provide a description of the issue and its priority level (High, Medium, or Low).
6. Decide on the action to take by selecting one of the options (Accept, Reject, Done, or Not done).
7. Update the status of the gate by selecting one of the options (Open or Closed).
8. If relevant, provide a date and time for when the gate was opened or closed.
9. Add any additional comments or notes about the gate's condition.
10. Attach any supporting photos or videos that illustrate the gate's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Access Point**: Select the access point being inspected from the dropdown menu. This will help track which gate is being inspected.
* **Location**: Enter a brief description of the location where the gate is situated. This could include any nearby landmarks or contextual information that might be relevant to the inspection.
* **Condition**: Assess the condition of the gate by selecting one of the options (Good, Poor, or Unavailable). This will help identify whether the gate is functioning properly or if it's in need of attention.
* **Issue Description**: If there's an issue with the gate, provide a brief description of the problem. This could include symptoms or specific details about the issue.
* **Priority**: If an issue is identified, select a priority level (High, Medium, or Low) to indicate its severity.
* **Action Planned**: Decide on the course of action to take regarding the gate's condition. Select one of the options (Accept, Reject, Done, or Not done).
* **Action Taken**: Update the action taken field to reflect the outcome of the inspection, selecting one of the options (Done, Not done).
* **Issue Status**: Update the gate's status by selecting one of the options (Open or Closed).
* **Gate Opening Date**: Enter a date for when the gate was opened, if applicable.
* **Gate Closing Date**: Enter a date for when the gate was closed, if applicable.
* **Gate Opening Time**: Enter a time for when the gate was opened, if applicable.
* **Gate Closing Time**: Enter a time for when the gate was closed, if applicable.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about the gate's condition.
* **Photos/Videos**: Attach any supporting photos or videos that demonstrate the gate's condition.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the gate's condition.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, refer to the guidelines provided.
* Take photos or videos of the gate to support your inspection and help with decision-making.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
