# Anesthesia Equipment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on the status of anesthesia equipment, ensuring that it is functioning correctly and is safe for use. Medical professionals use this form to assess the status of critical equipment, such as oxygen supply, suction, and monitors, and to identify any issues that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please review the checklist carefully and provide the following information:
	* Confirm the current status of critical equipment, including oxygen supply, suction, and monitors.
	* Record any issues or concerns you have about the equipment.
	* Sign off on the completion of the checklist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Checked by
* **Checked by**: Enter the name of the staff member who performed the inspection. 
* **Required**: This field is required to ensure that the inspection is performed by a staff member.

### Date Checked
* **Date Checked**: Enter the date of the inspection. 
* **Type**: Date field.

### Oxygen Supply Status
* **Oxygen Supply Status**: Confirm the current status of the oxygen supply, selecting one of the following options:
	+ **OK**: Oxygen supply is functioning correctly.
	+ **Needs maintenance**: Oxygen supply requires maintenance or attention.
	+ **N/A**: Oxygen supply is not applicable or not available.

### Suction Status
* **Suction Status**: Confirm the current status of the suction, selecting one of the following options:
	+ **OK**: Suction is functioning correctly.
	+ **Needs maintenance**: Suction requires maintenance or attention.
	+ **N/A**: Suction is not applicable or not available.

### Monitors Functional
* **Monitors Functional**: Confirm the current status of the monitors, selecting one of the following options:
	+ **OK**: Monitors are functioning correctly.
	+ **Needs maintenance**: Monitors require maintenance or attention.
	+ **N/A**: Monitors are not applicable or not available.

### Notes on Equipment
* **Notes on Equipment**: Enter any issues or concerns you have about the equipment. 

### Sign off Complete
* **Sign Off Complete**: Confirm that you have completed the inspection and review of the equipment. 
* **Type**: Select one of the following options:
	+ **True**: Inspection completed and no issues identified.
	+ **False**: Inspection incomplete or issues identified.
