# Fire Safety Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants on their fire safety training experiences. It is intended for users who have undergone fire safety training and are providing insight into their understanding of fire safety protocols and equipment used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the following pages:
	* Page 1: What fire safety training you received (required)
	* Page 2: Date of training (optional)
	* Page 3: Fire safety protocol understood (required)
	* Page 4: Fire safety equipment used (required)
	* Page 5: Comments (optional)
	* Page 6: Participant name (optional)
	* Page 7: Participant email (optional)
	* Page 8: Participant phone (optional)
	* Page 9: Fire safety protocol improvement ideas (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What fire safety training received (<type:text, required: true>):**
	+ Please select all the fire safety training you have received.
	* Example: Fire Extinguisher Training
* **Date of training (<type:date, optional>):**
	+ If you have undergone fire safety training, enter the date you received it.
* **Fire safety protocol understood (<type:select_one, required: true>):**
	+ Have you understood the fire safety protocol?
	+ Yes, No, Not Sure
* **Fire safety equipment used (<type:select_multiple, required: true>):**
	+ Select all the fire safety equipment you have used during the training.
	+ Fire Extinguisher, Fire Alarm, Emergency Exit
* **Comments (<type:text, optional>):**
	+ Please provide any additional comments or feedback on your fire safety training experience.
* **Participant name (<type:text, optional>):**
	+ If you would like to provide your name, please enter it here.
* **Participant email (<type:email, optional>):**
	+ If you would like to provide your email, please enter it here.
* **Participant phone (<type:text, optional>):**
	+ If you would like to provide your phone number, please enter it here.
* **Fire safety protocol improvement ideas (<type:text, optional>):**
	+ If you have any suggestions on how to improve the fire safety protocol, please enter them here.

**Tips**

* Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to provide valuable information for fire safety improvement initiatives.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
