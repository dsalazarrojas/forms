# Blasting Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Blasting Safety Checklist is a crucial document used to ensure that all necessary safety protocols are in place before conducting a blasting operation. This form is designed to verify that the blast area is secure, communication with all relevant teams is confirmed, equipment is properly checked, and any potential weather conditions or additional actions required to be taken before the blast are documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Inspector name" field with the name of the person completing the form.
2. Enter the date and time of the inspection in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
3. Confirm whether the blast area is secured by selecting "True" or "False" from the "Blast area secured" dropdown menu.
4. Confirm whether communication with all relevant teams has been confirmed by selecting "True" or "False" from the "Communication confirmed" dropdown menu.
5. Check if the monitoring and detonation gear has been verified by selecting "True" or "False" from the "Equipment checked" dropdown menu.
6. If necessary, note any weather conditions that may impact the blast operation in the "Weather conditions" field.
7. If there are any outstanding actions required to be taken before the blast, enter them in the "Additional actions required" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Inspector name (`inspector_name`, text, required)
 Enter the name of the person completing the form.

### Date (`date`, date, required)
 Enter the date of the inspection.

### Time (`time`, time, required)
 Enter the time of the inspection.

### Blast area secured (`blast_area_secured`, select_one, required)
 Confirm whether the blast area is secure by selecting "True" or "False".

### Communication confirmed (`communication_confirmed`, select_one, required)
 Confirm whether communication with all relevant teams has been confirmed by selecting "True" or "False".

### Equipment checked (`equipment_checked`, select_one, required)
 Check if the monitoring and detonation gear has been verified by selecting "True" or "False".

### Weather conditions (`weather_conditions`, text, optional)
 If there are any weather conditions that may impact the blast operation, enter them here.

### Additional actions required (`additional_actions_required`, text, optional)
 If there are any outstanding actions required to be taken before the blast, enter them here.
