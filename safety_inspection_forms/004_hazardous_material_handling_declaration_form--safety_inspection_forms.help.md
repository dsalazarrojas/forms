# Hazardous Material Handling Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hazardous Material Handling Declaration Form is a safety document used to report the handling and disposal procedures for materials that pose a risk to health and safety. This form is used to document and ensure proper handling and disposal of these materials to minimize risks associated with their use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the form from the options provided.
2. Provide a brief description of the materials being handled.
3. Detail the specific handling procedures and precautions taken when handling the materials.
4. Specify the storage location for the materials.
5. Include any special instructions for handling or disposal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the category of the form from the options provided. This will help determine the specific procedures and guidelines that apply to your situation.
- **Description** (`description`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the materials being handled. This should include the name, type, and quantity of the materials.
- **Hazardous Materials** (`hazardous_materials`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the specific hazardous materials being handled. This should include details about their chemical properties, potential risks, and any special handling requirements.
- **Handling Procedure** (`handling_procedure`, `text`, required/optional): Detail the specific steps taken when handling the materials, including any protective gear required and procedures for minimizing risks.
- **Safety Precautions** (`safety_precautions`, `text`, required/optional): List any safety precautions that should be taken when handling the materials, including personal protective equipment (PPE) required and emergency procedures.
- **Storage Location** (`storage_location`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the storage location for the materials. This should include details about the container, labeling, and any special storage requirements.
- **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, required/optional): Include any special instructions for handling or disposal of the materials. This may include specific procedures for spill response, disposal, or other unique circumstances.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy and completeness of your entries.
- Ensure all necessary information is included.
- Proofread your form for errors before submitting.
- If unsure about any information, consult with your supervisor or safety team.

Note: This guide is provided as a general aid to completing the form and should not be used as a substitute for proper training or certification in hazardous material handling. Completing this form should be done with care and attention to detail to avoid errors that may lead to accidents or other safety issues.
