# Dormitory Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to conduct a safety inspection of the dormitory and identify potential hazards. The inspection includes checking for safety equipment, electrical, fire, medical, structural, environmental, and other hazards. The form is completed by a safety officer and is used to document the results of the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "dormitory_safety_inspection" field and enter the date of the inspection.
2. Enter the name of the safety officer who completed the form in the "safety_officer_name" field.
3. Enter the ID of the safety officer who completed the form in the "safety_officer_id" field.
4. Enter the name of the dorm in the "dorm_name" field.
5. Enter the building number in the "building_number" field.
6. Enter the room number in the "room_number" field.
7. Select the safety equipment that is present or missing from the "safety_equipment" field.
8. Select the electrical hazards found during the inspection from the "electrical_hazards" field.
9. Select the fire hazards found during the inspection from the "fire_hazards" field.
10. Select the medical hazards found during the inspection from the "medical_hazards" field.
11. Select the structural hazards found during the inspection from the "structural_hazards" field.
12. Select the environmental hazards found during the inspection from the "environmental_hazards" field.
13. Enter any other hazards found during the inspection in the "other_hazards" field.
14. Enter any comments or notes about the inspection in the "comments" field.
15. Enter the date the form was completed in the "date_completed" field.
16. Enter the time the form was completed in the "time_completed" field.
17. Enter the name of the person who submitted the form in the "submitted_by" field.
18. Enter the title of the person who submitted the form in the "submitted_by_title" field.
19. Enter the safety officer's signature in the "safety_officer_sign" field.
20. Enter the safety officer's title in the "safety_officer_title" field.
21. Enter the date the form was completed in the "date_completed" field.
22. Enter the time the form was completed in the "time_completed" field.
