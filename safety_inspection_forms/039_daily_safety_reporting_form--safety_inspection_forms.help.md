# Daily Safety Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report any incidents that have occurred during the workday.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if an incident occurred on the specified date by checking the "Incident occurred" field. If you're unsure, select "No" to indicate that no incident occurred on the specified date.
2. If an incident did occur, fill in the "Incident location" field with the location where the incident occurred.
3. In the "Description of the incident" field, provide a brief description of what happened.
4. Enter the "Date of incident" field with the date the incident occurred.
5. Determine if the incident was reported by someone else (e.g., a supervisor or manager) by checking the "Reported by" field. If someone else reported the incident, select "Yes" to indicate that it was reported by someone else.
6. Fill in the "Safety Hazards" field with any potential safety hazards that were identified during the incident.
7. Check the "Was the incident escalated" field to indicate if the incident was escalated to a higher authority.
8. Select the "Outcome of the incident" field to indicate the outcome of the incident.
9. In the "Remediation taken for the incident" field, describe any actions taken to mitigate or correct the incident.
10. In the "Follow up actions" field, describe any follow-up actions taken or planned to prevent similar incidents in the future.
11. In the "Comments" field, provide any additional comments or observations about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Incident occurred
* **Incident occurred** (`Yes/No`, required): Indicate if an incident occurred on the specified date. If no incident occurred, select "No".

### Incident location
* **Incident location** (`Text`, optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.

### Description of the incident
* **Description of the incident** (`Text`, optional): Provide a brief description of what happened during the incident.

### Date of incident
* **Date of incident** (`Date`, optional): Enter the date the incident occurred.

### Reported by
* **Reported by** (`Yes/No`, optional): Determine if the incident was reported by someone else (e.g., a supervisor or manager).

### Safety Hazards
* **Safety Hazards** (`Text`, optional): Identify any potential safety hazards that were present during the incident.

### Was the incident escalated
* **Was the incident escalated** (`Yes/No`, optional): Indicate if the incident was escalated to a higher authority.

### Outcome of the incident
* **Outcome of the incident** (`Yes/No`, optional): Select the outcome of the incident.

### Remediation taken for the incident
* **Remediation taken for the incident** (`Text`, optional): Describe any actions taken to mitigate or correct the incident.

### Follow up actions
* **Follow up actions** (`Text`, optional): Describe any follow-up actions taken or planned to prevent similar incidents in the future.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`Text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or observations about the incident.
