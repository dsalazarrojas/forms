# Trailer Safety Inspection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trailer Safety Inspection Survey is a tool used to inspect trailers for their overall safety and condition. This form helps identify potential issues and ensures that trailers are in a safe state for use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review each section carefully, and answer accordingly.
* For sections with options, select the relevant option that best describes the trailer's condition.
* If an option is not applicable, choose 'Not Applicable' and provide a brief reason in the 'Notes' section.
* Fill out the 'Last Trailer Check Date' and 'Last Trailer Check Time' fields with the dates and times when the trailer was last inspected.
* Fill out the 'Next Trailer Check Date' and 'Next Trailer Check Time' fields with the dates and times when the trailer is due for its next inspection.
* Fill out the 'Notes' section with any additional comments or issues found during the inspection.
