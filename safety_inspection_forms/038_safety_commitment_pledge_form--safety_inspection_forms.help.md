# Safety Commitment Pledge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Commitment Pledge Form is used to collect information from employees about their safety-related commitments. This form helps to promote a culture of safety in the workplace and ensures that employees understand their responsibilities in maintaining a safe and healthy work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information accurately and honestly.
2. Select your department(s) from the "Department" field, if applicable.
3. Fill in your job title and job status if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false)
Enter your name as it appears on your official company documents.

### 2. **Email** (`email`, text, required: false)
Enter your official company email address.

### 3. **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false)
Enter your work phone number.

### 4. **Safety Pledge** (`safety_pledge`, text, required: false)
Describe your commitment to maintaining a safe work environment. Please be specific and provide examples if possible.

### 5. **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select the department(s) you belong to from the list provided. If you're not sure which department to select, please contact your HR representative.

### 6. **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false)
Enter your official job title as it appears on your company documents.

### 7. **Date of Joining** (`date_of_joining`, date, required: false)
Enter the date you joined the company.

### 8. **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select your current job status from the list provided.

### 9. **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false)
Enter your manager's name as it appears on official company documents.

### 10. **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required: false)
Enter your manager's contact phone number.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form accurately and honestly.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult your HR representative.
* This form is not mandatory, but it's recommended for all employees to complete it.
