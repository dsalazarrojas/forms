# Warehouse Fire Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a warehouse fire incident.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the facility where the incident occurred.
2. Provide the date when the incident happened.
3. Enter the time of the incident.
4. Describe the incident in detail.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Facility
 * **Facility** (`facility`, text, required/optional): Enter the name or location of the facility where the incident occurred.

### 2. Warehouse Location
 * **Warehouse Location** (`warehouse_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the address or location of the warehouse where the incident occurred.

### 3. Incident Date
 * **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the incident happened.

### 4. Incident Reported By
 * **Incident Reported By** (`reported_by`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.

### 5. Reported On
 * **Reported On** (`reported_on`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the incident was reported.

### 6. Incident Time
 * **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the incident.

### 7. Incident Description
 * **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when filling in the fields.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
- If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to reach out for assistance.
