# logistics_vehicle_roadworthiness_inspection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The logistics_vehicle_roadworthiness_inspection form is used to collect information about the roadworthiness inspection of logistics vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form and ensuring you have all the necessary information about the vehicle.
2. Fill in the vehicle number (id: 2) of the vehicle being inspected.
3. Enter the date of the inspection (id: 3) using the date format.
4. For the mileage (id: 4), enter the current mileage of the vehicle.
5. Check the engine oil level (id: 5) and enter the current level.
6. Select the engine oil type (id: 6) from the options provided.
7. Indicate if the engine oil level is critical (id: 7).
8. Check the condition of the brakes (id: 8) and select all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **logistics_vehicle_roadworthiness_inspection** (id: 1): Enter a brief description of the vehicle being inspected.
* **vehicle_number** (id: 2): Enter the vehicle number or registration number.
* **date** (id: 3): Enter the date of the inspection in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **mileage** (id: 4): Enter the current mileage of the vehicle.
* **engine_oil_level** (id: 5): Check the current level of the engine oil. A higher number indicates a fuller oil level.
* **engine_oil_type** (id: 6): Select the type of engine oil used in the vehicle.
* **engine_oil_level_critical** (id: 7): Indicate if the engine oil level is critical, i.e., low enough to cause engine damage.
* **brakes_worn** (id: 8): Check all that apply for brake issues, including worn brake pads, worn brake rotors, or other issues.

## Tips
* Always refer to the vehicle's manual for specific maintenance recommendations.
* If unsure about any of the questions, consult a mechanic or the vehicle's manufacturer.
* This form is intended to be completed for each vehicle, ensuring consistent data collection.
