# Forklift Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Forklift Safety Inspection Form is used to identify potential safety concerns and provide recommendations for improvement on forklifts in a workplace or facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions accurately and thoroughly. Ensure that you have access to the forklift's documentation, training records, and any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Forklift Safety Inspection Form 1
* **safety_inspection_questions** (Text, Optional):
	+ Please provide a detailed answer to the safety inspection questions.

### Forklift Equipment Checklist
* **forklift_equipment_checklist** (Select Multiple, Required):
	+ Check all the parts that need inspection:
		+ Forklift Controls
		+ Forklift Tires
		+ Forklift Wheels

### Forklift Operating Procedures
* **forklift_operating_procedures** (Text, Optional):
	+ Describe the operating procedures for the forklift, including any pre-use checks, operating procedures, and emergency procedures.

### Forklift Training Records
* **forklift_training_records** (Text, Optional):
	+ Please provide the training records of the forklift operator, including dates, instructor names, and certification numbers.

### Forklift License
* **forklift_license** (Select One, Optional):
	+ Please select the current license status of the forklift:
		+ Valid
		+ Invalid

### Forklift Defects Found
* **forklift_defects_found** (Number, Optional):
	+ Count the number of defects found during the inspection.

### Forklift Safety Inspection Report
* **forklift_safety_inspection_report** (Text, Optional):
	+ Summarize the findings and results of the inspection, including any safety concerns.

### Forklift Safety Inspection Recommendations
* **forklift_safety_inspection_recommendations** (Text, Optional):
	+ Provide recommendations for improvement and any necessary corrective actions.
 
## Tips
- Please complete this form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
- Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and access to the forklift and its documentation.
- If you are unsure about any question or field, please ask for clarification or assistance.
