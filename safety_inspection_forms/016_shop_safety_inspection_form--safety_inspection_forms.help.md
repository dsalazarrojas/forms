# Shop Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to conduct safety inspections of shops and offices, ensuring compliance with relevant safety regulations and guidelines.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Complete all required fields (indicated by *Required* in the field explanation below).
2. Answer each question or section according to your findings during the safety inspection.
3. Ensure you provide a detailed and accurate description of your findings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. *shop_safety_inspection_form* (Text, Optional)
This field is a text entry field for you to describe the shop or office you inspected.
### 2. *safety_inspection_form* (Text, Optional)
This field is a duplicate of the first field, likely for historical or legacy purposes.
### 3. *safety_inspection_form_2* (Text, Required)
This field is for the inspector to describe the specific inspection they performed (e.g., "Fire Safety Equipment Inspect").
### 4. *safety_inspection_form_3* (Select Multiple, Optional)
This field allows the inspector to select multiple options from a list (e.g., "Fire Safety Equipment Inspect", "Chemical and Waste Management Inspect").
### 5. *safety_inspection_form_5* (Date, Optional)
This field is for the inspector to enter the date of the last safety inspection.
### 6. *safety_inspection_form_6* (Time, Required)
This field requires the inspector to enter the time of the inspection.
### 7. *safety_inspection_form_8* (Text, Optional)
This field is for the inspector to describe any additional comments or observations related to the safety inspection.
### 8. *safety_inspection_form_9* (Text, Optional)
This field is for the inspector to provide a detailed report of their findings and any recommended actions or improvements.

## Tips
- Please ensure to complete all required fields.
- Be as detailed as possible when describing your findings and observations.
- If you are unsure about any section or field, contact your supervisor or relevant authorities for guidance.
