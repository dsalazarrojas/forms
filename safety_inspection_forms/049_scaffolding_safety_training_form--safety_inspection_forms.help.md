# Scaffolding Safety Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect safety information from scaffolding training participants. It helps to assess their safety experience and identify areas where they need further training or support. This form will be used to provide participants with relevant safety recommendations and training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant information, including your name and contact details.
2. Answer the questions regarding your safety equipment, procedures, and training requirements.
3. Choose the relevant training programs and certifications you have completed or are interested in.
4. Add any additional comments or concerns you have about your safety experience.
5. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm your completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`scaffolding_safety_training_form_participant_information`): This field is used to collect your name and contact details. Please provide your name, phone number, and email address to ensure we can contact you with any questions or recommendations.
* **Safety Equipment** (`scaffolding_safety_training_form_safety_equipment`): This field asks if you have any safety equipment or personal protective equipment (PPE) for scaffolding work. Please choose 'Yes' if you have the necessary equipment, or 'No' if you do not.
* **Safety Procedures** (`scaffolding_safety_training_form_safety_procedures`): This field inquires about your experience and adherence to safety procedures. Please choose 'Yes' if you have a good understanding of the procedures, or 'No' if you need further training or clarification.
* **Training Requirements** (`scaffolding_safety_training_form_training_requirements`): This field is used to assess your training needs. Please enter the number of years of experience you have with scaffolding work, or the level of training you require (e.g., basic, intermediate, advanced).
* **Training Programs** (`scaffolding_safety_training_form_training_programs`): This field allows you to select the training programs you have completed or are interested in. Please choose 'Yes' for each program you have completed or are interested in.
* **Training Certifications** (`scaffolding_safety_training_form_training_certifications`): Similar to the previous field, this one inquires about your certifications or interests in specific training areas. Please choose 'Yes' for each certification you have or are interested in.
* **Participant Comments** (`scaffolding_safety_training_form_participant_comments`): This field is for any additional comments or concerns you have about your safety experience. Please be as clear and concise as possible.
* **Participant Signature** (`scaffolding_safety_training_form_participant_signature`): Please sign and date this form to confirm your completion and commitment to following safety procedures.
