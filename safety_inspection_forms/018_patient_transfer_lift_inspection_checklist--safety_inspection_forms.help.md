# Patient Transfer Lift Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Transfer Lift Inspection Checklist is used to inspect patient lifts and ensure they are in good working order and compliant with safety guidelines. This form is required for all patients transfers and is used to identify and document any deficiencies or issues found during the inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are in the correct location for the patient transfer, such as near the patient's bed or in a designated transfer area.
2. Review the patient's medical record to understand their specific needs and any special considerations for the transfer.
3. Inspect the patient lift according to the checklist provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **2. patient_transfer_lift_inspection_checklist_2** (`2`, `text`, required/optional: false): Check if the lift is properly assembled and all parts are securely attached.
* **3. patient_transfer_lift_inspection_checklist_3** (`3`, `text`, required/optional: false): Verify that the lift's weight capacity is clearly displayed and is within the recommended range for the patient's weight.
* **4. patient_transfer_lift_inspection_checklist_4** (`4`, `date`, required/optional: false): Record the date of the last inspection or maintenance of the patient lift.
