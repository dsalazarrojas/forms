# Risk Assessment And Method Statement Rams Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Assessment and Method Statement RAMS Form is a critical tool used to identify and assess potential hazards on construction sites. It helps you document and prioritize risks, as well as outline control measures to mitigate them.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To get started, enter the **Job Number** (field 1) to help identify the specific job or project being assessed.
2.  Describe the **Hazard** (field 2) you've identified, being as specific as possible.
3.  For each hazard, indicate the **Control Measures** (field 3) that will be taken to mitigate it.
4.  Assess the **Risk Level** (field 4) of each hazard, selecting from options like High, Low, or Medium.
5.  Evaluate the **Control Effectiveness** (field 5) of the control measures, indicating their impact on the risk level, from 100 (High) to 50 (Low).
6.  Record the **Review Date** (field 6) when the risk assessment was conducted.
7.  Provide your **Reviewer Name** (field 7), so the risk assessment is associated with your identity.
8.  Write a concise **Risk Assessment** (field 8) to summarize your findings, including the hazard, its risk level, and proposed control measures.
9.  Outline the **Method Statement** (field 9) for each risk, detailing the steps to be taken to mitigate or eliminate it.
10. Identify if the **Safety-Critical Control** (field 10) is in place for each risk, indicating whether it's essential for safety.
11. Assess the **Risk Level Frequency** (field 11) of each hazard, selecting from options like Low, Medium, or High.
12. Evaluate the **Risk Level Severity** (field 12) of each hazard, indicating its potential impact.
13. Assess the **Control Measures Frequency** (field 13) of each control, selecting from options like Low, Medium, or High.
14. Evaluate the **Control Measures Severity** (field 14) of each control, indicating its effectiveness.
15. Lastly, add your **Reviewer Signature** (field 15) and **Contractor Signature** (field 16) to acknowledge you've reviewed and agree with the risk assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Number** (1): Enter the specific job or project number associated with this risk assessment.
* **Hazard** (2): Describe the identified hazard in clear and specific terms.
* **Control Measures** (3): Outline the measures to be taken to mitigate or eliminate the hazard.
* **Risk Level** (4): Assess the risk level of the hazard as High, Low, or Medium.
* **Control Effectiveness** (5): Evaluate the effectiveness of control measures on a scale from 100 (High) to 50 (Low).
* **Review Date** (6): Record the date when the risk assessment was conducted.
* **Reviewer Name** (7): Enter your name for identification and accountability.
* **Risk Assessment** (8): Summarize your findings and proposed control measures for each hazard.
* **Method Statement** (9): Outline the steps to be taken to mitigate or eliminate each risk.
* **Safety-Critical Control** (10): Identify if the control is essential for safety.
* **Risk Level Frequency** (11): Assess the frequency of each hazard, selecting from Low, Medium, or High.
* **Risk Level Severity** (12): Evaluate the potential impact of each hazard, selecting from Low, Medium, or High.
* **Control Measures Frequency** (13): Assess the frequency of control measures, selecting from Low, Medium, or High.
* **Control Measures Severity** (14): Evaluate the effectiveness of control measures, selecting from Low, Medium, or High.
* **Reviewer Signature** (15): Add your signature to acknowledge your role in the risk assessment.
* **Contractor Signature** (16): Add your contractor's signature to acknowledge their acceptance of the risk assessment.
* **Contractor Name** (17): Enter the contractor's name for their identity.
