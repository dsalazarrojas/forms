# Safety Recall Information Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Safety Recall Information Request form is designed to gather information about a customer's product recall concern. It is used to collect details about the reason for the recall request, product identification, and customer contact information. This form is intended to be filled out by customers who have concerns about their product's safety and want to initiate a recall process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the reason for your recall request in the "What is the reason for this recall request" field. Please provide as much detail as possible.
2. Provide product identification details, such as the product model and serial number, in the "Product Identification" field.
3. If you would like to share additional contact information with us, fill out the "Customer Contact Information" field.
4. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Customer E-mail" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
7. Enter a message for us in the "Customer Message" field.
8. If you have any additional information you'd like to share, enter it in the "Additional Customer Notes" field.
9. Indicate whether you agree to our terms and conditions by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Customer Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is the reason for this recall request**: Enter the reason why you are requesting a recall. This could be due to a defect, a concern about the product's safety, or any other issue that is causing you to request a recall.
- **Product Identification**: Provide details about the product that is being recalled, including the model number, serial number, or any other relevant information.
- **Customer Contact Information**: If you would like to share additional contact information with us, such as a phone number or address, you can enter it here.
- **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Customer E-mail**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
- **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **Customer Message**: Enter a brief message explaining your concerns and any other information you would like to share with us.
- **Additional Customer Notes**: Enter any additional information you would like to share with us.
- **Customer Agreement**: Indicate whether you agree to our terms and conditions by selecting "Yes" or "No".
