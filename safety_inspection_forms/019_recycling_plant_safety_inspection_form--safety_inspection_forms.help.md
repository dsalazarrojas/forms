# Recycling Plant Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to conduct a thorough safety inspection at a recycling plant. It gathers information on various aspects of the plant's safety, including its name, address, and status of its safety and electrical and mechanical equipment.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the facility's name and address in the respective fields.
2. Enter the date and time of the inspection.
3. Check the status of the safety equipment, electrical equipment, and mechanical equipment.
4. Provide notes and observations about the plant's safety status.
5. Determine if any corrective actions are needed.
6. Record follow-up information (if applicable).
7. Enter the name and date of the person who conducted the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the facility being inspected.
* **Date of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection`, date, required): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Time of Inspection** (`time_of_inspection`, time, required): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Safety Equipment Status** (`safety_equipment_list`, select_multiple, required): Check if the safety equipment is functioning properly.
	+ Select 'Yes' if the equipment is functioning properly.
	+ Select 'No' if the equipment is not functioning properly.
* **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, select_one, required): Check the condition of the equipment.
	+ Select 'Yes' if the equipment is in good condition.
	+ Select 'No' if the equipment is not in good condition.
* **Electrical Equipment Status** (`electrical_equipment_list`, select_multiple, optional): Check the status of the electrical equipment.
	+ Select 'Yes' if the equipment is functioning properly.
	+ Select 'No' if the equipment is not functioning properly.
* **Electrical Equipment Notes** (`electrical_equipment_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes about the electrical equipment.
* **Mechanical Equipment Status** (`mechanical_equipment_list`, select_multiple, optional): Check the status of the mechanical equipment.
	+ Select 'Yes' if the equipment is functioning properly.
	+ Select 'No' if the equipment is not functioning properly.
* **Mechanical Equipment Notes** (`mechanical_equipment_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes about the mechanical equipment.
* **Safety Equipment Notes** (`safety_equipment_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes about the safety equipment.
* **Electrical Risk Factors** (`electrical_risk_factors`, text, required): Enter any electrical risk factors.
* **Mechanical Risk Factors** (`mechanical_risk_factors`, text, required): Enter any mechanical risk factors.
* **Observation** (`observation`, note, required): Enter any observation about the plant's safety status.
* **Corrective Action** (`corrective_action`, select_one, required): Determine if any corrective actions are needed.
	+ Select 'Yes' if corrective actions are needed.
	+ Select 'No' if no corrective actions are needed.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, text, optional): Enter any follow-up information (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the follow-up date (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Enter the follow-up time (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, text, optional): Enter any follow-up notes (if applicable).
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_one, optional): Check the status of the follow-up.
	+ Select 'Active' if the follow-up is active.
	+ Select 'Inactive' if the follow-up is inactive.
* **Follow-up Status Description** (`follow_up_status_description`, text, optional): Enter any description of the follow-up status.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who conducted the inspection.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, required): Enter the date of the inspection submission.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, time, optional): Enter the time of the inspection submission.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* Double-check the information provided.
* Enter any relevant notes or observations to provide detailed information.
* Ensure that the follow-up information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Save the form after completion to ensure that the information is saved.
