# Zone Compliance Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Zone Compliance Checklist form is a comprehensive tool used for tracking the safety inspection status of various zones within a facility or organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Choose the zone for which you want to report compliance (Section 8).
2. Enter your name as the team leader or submitter (Section 30).
3. Select the team members involved in the inspection (Section 31).
4. Enter the date and time of the inspection (Section 26 and 42).
5. Select the status of the inspection (Section 32).
6. Enter any comments or notes regarding the inspection (Section 33).
7. Enter your email and phone number (Section 35 and 36).
8. Enter your department and position (Section 37 and 38).
9. Enter your location (Section 39).
10. Enter any additional comments for the submitter (Section 40).
11. Enter the date and time of submission (Section 41).
12. Enter your IP address (not explicitly mentioned in the original YAML but present in other forms, Section 43).
12. Enter your user agent (Section 44).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Zone Selection (Section 8)**: Choose the zone to be inspected.
* **Submitter Name (Section 30)**: Enter your name as the team leader or submitter.
* **Safety Team (Section 31)**: Select the team members involved in the inspection.
* **Date (Section 26)**: Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Time (Section 42)**: Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Inspection Status (Section 32)**: Select the status of the inspection.
* **Comments (Section 33)**: Enter any comments or notes regarding the inspection.
* **Submitter Email (Section 35)**: Enter your email.
* **Submitter Phone (Section 36)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Department (Section 37)**: Enter your department.
* **Submitter Position (Section 38)**: Enter your position.
* **Submitter Location (Section 39)**: Enter your location.
* **Submitter Comments (Section 40)**: Enter any additional comments for the submitter.
* **Submitter Date (Section 41)**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitter IP (Section 43)**: Enter your IP address.
* **Submitter User Agent (Section 44)**: Enter your user agent.

## Tips

* Ensure that you select the correct zone for the inspection.
* Verify that you've entered accurate and complete information for all fields.
* Enter comments and notes regarding any issues or concerns with the inspection.
* Double-check your email, department, position, and location before submission.
* Make sure to submit at the correct date and time.
* Be clear and concise in your comments for the submitter.
* Review and submit the form accurately to avoid any errors.
