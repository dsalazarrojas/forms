# Surveillance Tour Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Surveillance Tour Checklist form is used to document the details of a patrol or inspection tour. The form captures information about the tour, including patrol details, date and time, officer name, officer ID, and checklists of checkpoints and incidents.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the patrol details, including the date and time of the tour.
2. Enter the officer's name and ID who conducted the tour.
3. Complete the checklists for each checkpoint, noting any incidents or issues encountered.
4. Add any additional comments or notes about the tour.
5. Submit the form to complete the tour report.
