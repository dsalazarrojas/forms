# Scaffolding Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the safety of scaffolding on site. It is designed to ensure that scaffolding is properly installed, maintained, and monitored to prevent accidents and injuries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the scaffolding setup and identify any potential hazards.
2. Answer the questions in this form based on your observation and knowledge of the scaffolding.
3. Make sure to answer all questions truthfully and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of scaffolding is being used?** (Scaffolding type, Scaffold, Support scaffolding, Independent scaffolding)
	+ Select the correct type of scaffolding being used on site.
* **Is scaffolding located on a solid surface?** (True, False)
	+ Answer true if the scaffolding is on a solid surface, such as a concrete or metal base.
* **Are there adequate access holes?** (True, False)
	+ Answer true if there are sufficient access holes for safe egress and egress from the scaffolding.
* **Are fall protection devices required?** (True, False)
	+ Answer true if fall protection devices, such as harnesses, nets, or other safety gear, are required to prevent falls.
* **What is the maximum height of scaffolding?** (Number)
	+ Enter the maximum height of the scaffolding, measured in feet or meters.
* **What is the maximum width of scaffolding?** (Number)
	+ Enter the maximum width of the scaffolding, measured in feet or meters.
* **Is there an access path available for the scaffolding?** (True, False)
	+ Answer true if there is a safe and accessible path for entering and exiting the scaffolding.
* **Are safety signs displayed at each level?** (True, False)
	+ Answer true if safety signs, such as warning signs or instructions, are displayed at each level of the scaffolding.
* **Was a scaffold safety training given?** (True, False)
	+ Answer true if a safety training or briefing has been given to employees working on or near the scaffolding.
* **Is an emergency plan established?** (True, False)
	+ Answer true if an emergency plan, including procedures and protocols for emergency situations, is in place.

## Tips

* Make sure to thoroughly inspect the scaffolding setup before completing this form.
* Answer questions based on your observation and knowledge of the scaffolding.
* Review and confirm the form with your supervisor or site manager to ensure accuracy.
* Update this form as needed to reflect changes in the scaffolding setup or safety protocols.
