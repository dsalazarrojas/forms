# Firearm Fault Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you report a fault with your firearm. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to help our service team resolve the issue efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Report A Fault**: Describe the issue you are experiencing with your firearm.
2. **Select Fault Type**: Choose the type of fault you are reporting (e.g., Malfunctioning Trigger, Malfunctioning Safety, etc.).
3. **Select Date of Fault**: Enter the date the fault occurred.
4. **Select Time of Fault**: Enter the time the fault occurred.
5. **Fault Description**: Provide a detailed description of the fault.
6. **Supporting File 1-4**: If you have any supporting files or documentation related to the fault, upload them here.
7. **Select Date of Last Use**: If you are reporting a fault that occurred during the last use of the firearm, enter the date of last use.
8. **Select Time of Last Use**: If applicable, enter the time the fault occurred during the last use.
9. **Select Range or Retailer**: Choose the range or retailer where the fault occurred.
10. **Select Service Team**: Choose the service team that will be handling your case.
11. **Select Service Technician**: Choose the service technician who will be working on your case.
12. **firearm_fault_report_form_22**: Enter any additional information related to the fault.
13. **Select Supporting File 5**: If you have any additional supporting files or documentation, upload them here.
14. **firearm_fault_report_form_24**: Enter any additional information related to the fault.
15. **firearm_fault_report_form_25**: Enter any final notes or comments about the fault.
