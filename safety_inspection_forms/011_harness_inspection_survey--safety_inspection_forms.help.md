# Harness Inspection Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Harness Inspection Survey is a crucial tool for ensuring the safety and reliability of harness systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the harness's current state and identify any changes or updates that may impact its safety.
2. Determine if the harness meets the necessary criteria for safe operation.
3. Select the correct answers for each field based on your observations and findings.
4. Provide any additional comments or notes relevant to the harness's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Harness Check List (id: 1)
* **Harness Check List** (`Harness Check List`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the areas where the harness meets the necessary criteria for safe operation:
	+ Yes: The harness is safe and compliant with all relevant standards and regulations.
	+ No: The harness does not meet the necessary criteria for safe operation.

### Safety Inspection Checklist (id: 2)
* **Safety Inspection Checklist** (`Safety Inspection Checklist`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the answer that best describes the harness's safety status:
	+ Yes: The harness is safe and compliant with all relevant standards and regulations.
	+ No: The harness does not meet the necessary criteria for safe operation.

### Outcomes (id: 3)
* **Outcomes** (`Outcomes`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any outcomes or results related to the harness's inspection, such as repairs or modifications made.

### Site Name (id: 4)
* **Site Name** (`Site Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the site where the harness is located.

### Date (id: 5)
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the inspection.

### Time (id: 6)
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the inspection.

### Comments (id: 7)
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `note`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or notes related to the harness's condition.

### Assigned To (id: 8)
* **Assigned To** (`Assigned To`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the personnel assigned to the harness:
	+ Yes: The harness is assigned to this personnel.
	+ No: The harness is not assigned to this personnel.

### Team Members (id: 9)
* **Team Members** (`Team Members`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select any team members involved in the harness's maintenance and upkeep:
	+ Yes: These team members are involved in the harness's maintenance and upkeep.
	+ No: These team members are not involved in the harness's maintenance and upkeep.

## Tips
* Review the harness's history and previous inspections to ensure consistency and continuity.
* Complete this form regularly to maintain a culture of safety and responsibility.
* Ensure that all team members are aware of the harness's safety status and any actions required for maintenance.
