# Power Line Integrity Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Power Line Integrity Inspection Form is designed to provide a structured way to collect safety-related information about electrical power lines. This form ensures that critical data is captured and documented consistently, which aids in identifying potential issues and preventing safety hazards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the inspection date for the power line being inspected.
* Enter the name of the inspector conducting the inspection.
* Enter the facility's ID number.
* Describe the equipment being inspected.
* Report the condition of the equipment.
* List any hazards or concerns identified during the inspection.
* Provide any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* Identify if the inspection has been assigned to a specific user.
* Specify the type of facility being inspected (e.g., substation, transformer, etc.).
* Enter the facility's location.
* Indicate the status of the facility (Active or Inactive).
* Determine if the facility has a high priority for inspection.
* Provide any additional subtypes or descriptions for the facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection, ensuring it corresponds to the actual date the inspection took place.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the individual conducting the inspection, for accountability and record-keeping purposes.
* **Facility ID**: Enter the unique ID number assigned to the facility being inspected.
* **Equipment Description**: Provide a clear and concise description of the equipment being inspected.
* **Equipment Condition**: Describe the condition of the equipment, using the available options (e.g., good, fair, or poor).
* **Hazards**: List any safety hazards or concerns identified during the inspection.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection, for reference and follow-up purposes.
* **Assigned User**: Indicate if the inspection has been assigned to a specific user for follow-up or action items.
* **Facility Type**: Select the type of facility being inspected (e.g., substation, transformer, etc.).
* **Facility Location**: Enter the location of the facility being inspected.
* **Facility Status**: Specify the status of the facility (Active or Inactive).
* **Facility Priority**: Determine if the facility has a high priority for inspection, for targeted attention and follow-up.
* **Facility Subtype 1-10**: Enter any additional subtypes or descriptions for the facility, as applicable.
