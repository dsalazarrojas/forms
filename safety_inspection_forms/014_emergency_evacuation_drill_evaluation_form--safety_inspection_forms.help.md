# emergency_evacuation_drill_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency_evacuation_drill_evaluation_form is used to assess the effectiveness and conduct of an emergency evacuation drill. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Read the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
- Complete each field accurately and thoroughly, providing details about each question as required or specified.
- Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure that you have not missed any critical information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`note`): This is a note field where you can briefly describe the purpose and context of the drill. 
  - Please provide a short introduction to the drill, including its purpose and any relevant background information.
  - Keep it concise, but clear and relevant to the drill's objectives.
- **drill_date** (`date`): Enter the date of the drill. 
  - Ensure you enter the correct date of the drill in the required format.
  - If the drill was conducted across multiple days, provide the range of dates or specify each day separately.
- **drill_time** (`time`): Enter the time of the drill. 
  - Enter the time in the 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
  - If the drill was conducted across multiple time slots, provide each time separately.
- **drill_location** (`text`): Enter the location where the drill was conducted. 
  - Specify the exact location where the drill took place, including any relevant details about the site.
- **evacuation_plan** (`text`): Describe the evacuation plan that was used during the drill. 
  - Explain the procedures and protocols followed during the drill, including any specific evacuation routes or procedures.
  - Ensure you provide clear and accurate information about the plan's objectives and any deviations.
- **safety_equipment** (`select_one`): Indicate whether safety equipment was used during the drill. 
  - Choose 'Yes' if safety equipment was used, or 'No' if it was not.
  - Be specific about the type of equipment and its usage.
- **drill_conduct** (`select_multiple`): Indicate which personnel participated in the drill conduct. 
  - Choose multiple options if applicable, or select 'No' for each option if not applicable.
  - Be accurate and specific about the personnel involved.
- **drill_supervise** (`number`): Enter the number of people supervising the drill. 
  - Enter the correct number of people supervising the drill.
  - If the drill had multiple supervisors, specify each one separately.
- **evacuation_route** (`text`): Describe the evacuation route followed during the drill. 
  - Explain the route taken by participants, including any specific directions or landmarks.
- **emergency_phone** (`text`): Enter the emergency contact phone number. 
  - Provide the correct and accessible emergency contact number.
  - Ensure it's a phone number, not a different type of contact information.
- **first_aid_station** (`text`): Specify the location of the first aid station. 
  - Enter the exact location of the first aid station, including any relevant details.
- **drill_participants** (`number`): Enter the number of participants. 
  - Enter the correct number of participants in the drill.
  - If the drill had multiple groups or teams, specify each one separately.
- **drill_supervisor** (`text`): Enter the name of the drill supervisor. 
  - Provide the correct name of the drill supervisor.
  - If there were multiple supervisors, list each one separately.
- **drill_comments** (`note`): Provide any additional comments about the drill. 
  - Add any relevant comments or observations about the drill, including suggestions for improvement.
  - Keep it concise and focused on the drill's objectives and conduct. 

## Tips
- Review this form carefully before starting to ensure you have all the necessary information.
- If unsure about any field, refer to the drill's purpose, procedures, and objectives.
- Complete each field accurately and thoroughly, providing details as required or specified.
- Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure you have not missed any critical information.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form or the drill, contact the relevant authorities or safety officers.
