# Incident Severity Classification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to classify the severity of an incident, which is a critical process to ensure the safety and well-being of our team members and the organization as a whole. The goal of this form is to quickly and accurately assess the severity of an incident, so we can take prompt and appropriate action to mitigate any potential risks or consequences.

Before proceeding, please ensure that you have not previously filled out this form for this incident. If you are unsure, please review your previous responses or ask your supervisor for guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct answer for "Severity Classification" from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand the severity of the incident.
2. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field. Be as thorough and accurate as possible, so we can get a clear understanding of what happened.
3. If the incident occurred on a specific date, enter the date in the "Incident Date" field. If not, simply leave it blank.
4. If the incident occurred at a specific time, enter the time in the "Incident Time" field. If not, simply leave it blank.
5. Enter the location where the incident occurred in the "Incident Location" field. This will help us understand the context of the incident.
6. For "Incident Severity", select "Yes" if the incident had significant consequences or risks, and "No" if it did not.
7. Provide a description of the incident's severity in the "Incident Severity Description" field. This will help us understand the level of risk or consequence associated with the incident.
8. Finally, indicate if follow-up is required for the incident by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
