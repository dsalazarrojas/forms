# Workplace Hazard Assessment Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to schedule a workplace safety inspection. It gathers essential information from the user to facilitate the inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information, including your contact email and phone number.
2. Select a date and time for the inspection.
3. Choose the type of assessment you need (e.g., General Hazard, Ergonomic, or Confined Space).
4. Select the office location(s) where the assessment will take place.
5. Add any additional comments or notes for the assessor.
6. Review and confirm your submission to schedule the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_input`, Text, Required): Enter your name, title, and department.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, Date, Required): Select a date for the inspection.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, Time, Required): Select a time for the inspection.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, Email, Required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, Required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Assessment Type** (`assessment_type`, Select One, Required): Select the type of assessment you need (e.g., General Hazard, Ergonomic, or Confined Space).
* **Assessment Location** (`assessment_location`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the office location(s) where the assessment will take place.
* **Assessment Notes** (`assessment_notes`, Note, Required): Enter any additional comments or notes for the assessor.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, Text, Required): Review and confirm your submission to schedule the inspection.
* **Confirm** (`confirm_button`, Text, Required): Confirm your submission to schedule the inspection.
* **Cancel** (`cancel_button`, Text, Required): Cancel your submission and start over.

**Tips**

* Please be accurate when selecting the assessment location, as this will affect the schedule.
* Be clear with additional comments or notes to facilitate the assessment process.
