# Operational Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Operational Safety Checklist form is used to gather information about the operational safety status of equipment, areas, and personnel in a specific environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Select the correct status of the equipment in question.
3. Provide details about any hazards identified in the area.
4. Specify any safety measures taken or planned to address the hazards.
5. Indicate the next actions required to maintain or improve safety.
6. Add any comments or additional information relevant to the safety assessment.
7. Select the assigned user(s) responsible for the safety check.
8. If applicable, provide the date and time when the form was submitted and last updated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **is_equipment_in_good_working_order**: 
  Check if the equipment is in good working order. Choose 'True' if the equipment is functioning properly, 'False' if it's not, 'Partial' if it's operational but with some issues, or 'Unknown' if you're unsure.
* **area_checked**: 
  Describe the area you are assessing for safety.
* **hazard_identified**: 
  Identify any hazards present in the area or associated with the equipment.
* **safety_measures**: 
  List any safety measures you have taken or plan to take to address the hazards identified.
* **next_actions**: 
  Specify the next steps required to maintain or improve safety.
* **comments**: 
  Add any additional comments or information relevant to the safety assessment.
* **area_comments**: 
  Provide any comments specific to the area assessed.
* **assigned_user**: 
  Select the user(s) responsible for the safety check.
* **submitted_on**: 
  Date and time when the form was submitted.
* **last_updated**: 
  Date and time when the form was last updated.

## Tips
- Be thorough in your assessment and answer each field as accurately as possible.
- Review and update the submitted information regularly to reflect any changes in the safety status.
- Communicate clearly and effectively with the assigned user(s) about the safety concerns and next steps.
