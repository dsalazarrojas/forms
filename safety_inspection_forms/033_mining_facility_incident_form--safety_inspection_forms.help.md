# Mining Facility Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mining Facility Incident Form is used to report incidents that occurred at the mining facility. This form helps collect information about the incident, including its date, location, and details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you are reporting an incident that occurred at the mining facility.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Report** (required):
	+ This field is for the main incident report. Please provide a brief description of what happened.
* **Date** (not required):
	+ Enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (not required):
	+ Enter the time of the incident in the format HH:MM (12-hour format, e.g., 01:30).
* **Location** (not required):
	+ Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Type of Incident** (not required):
	+ Select 'Yes' if the incident involved a type of incident that is typically reported in this form, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Description** (required):
	+ Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Injuries** (required):
	+ Enter the number of injuries sustained during the incident.
* **Damage** (required):
	+ Enter the extent of the damage caused by the incident.
* **Weather** (not required):
	+ Select 'Yes' if the incident occurred during adverse weather conditions, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Employees Involved** (not required):
	+ Select all employees who were involved in the incident.
* **Equipment Involved** (not required):
	+ Select all equipment that was involved in the incident.
* **Witness List** (required):
	+ Enter the names of all witnesses to the incident.
* **Witness Notes** (required):
	+ Enter any additional notes from the witnesses.
* **Witness Signature** (required):
	+ The witness's signature, usually the person who filled out this form or the person who witnessed the incident.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact your supervisor or the person responsible for incident reporting.
* This form is intended to help in investigating and addressing the incident, so please provide as much detail as possible.
