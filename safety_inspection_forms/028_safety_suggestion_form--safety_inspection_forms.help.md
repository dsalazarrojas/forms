# Safety Suggestion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect safety inspection suggestions from employees. It helps identify potential hazards and suggest ways to address them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Select the correct department and category for your suggestion.
3. Choose a priority level for your suggestion.
4. Set a due date for completion.
5. Select a status for your suggestion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Suggestion (Safety Suggestion Form)** (`Safety Suggestion Form`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your safety suggestion.
* **Description (Description)** (`text`, required): Provide a detailed description of your safety suggestion.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact.
* **Employee Name (Employee Name)** (`text`, required): Enter your name as an employee.
* **Department (Department)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the department related to your suggestion.
* **Location (Location)** (`text`, required): Enter the location related to your suggestion.
* **Suggestion Date (Suggestion Date)** (`date`, required): Enter the date you made the suggestion.
* **Submission Date (Submission Date)** (`date`, required): Enter the date you submitted the suggestion.
* **Status (Status)** (`select_one`, required): Choose the status of your suggestion.
* **Submitter Name (Submitter Name)** (`text`, required): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email (Submitter Email)** (`email`, required): Enter your email address as the submitter.
* **Assignee Name (Assignee Name)** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the assignee.
* **Assignee Email (Assignee Email)** (`email`, required): Enter the email address of the assignee.
* **Assigned Date (Assigned Date)** (`date`, required): Enter the date the suggest was assigned.
* **Due Date (Due Date)** (`date`, required): Enter the due date for completion.
* **Priority (Priority)** (`select_one`, required): Choose the priority level for your suggestion.
* **Category (Category)** (`select_one`, required): Select the category of your suggestion.
* **Notes (Notes)** (`note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Submitter Phone (Submitter Phone)** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Submitter External (Submitter External)** (`text`, optional): Enter any external details related to your suggestion.
* **Assignee Phone (Assignee External Phone)** (`text`, optional): Enter the external phone number of the assignee.
* **Assignee External (Assignee External)** (`text`, optional): Enter any external details related to the assignee.
* **Assignee External Email (Assignee External Email)** (`email`, optional): Enter the external email address of the assignee.
