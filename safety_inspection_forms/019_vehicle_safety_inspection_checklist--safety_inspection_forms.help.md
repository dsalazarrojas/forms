# vehicle_safety_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle_safety_inspection_checklist form is used to document the safety inspection of a vehicle. It captures details about the vehicle's condition, safety status, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the vehicle_year field with the vehicle's year of manufacture.
2. Enter the vehicle's Vin Number in the vin_number field.
3. Select the date of the safety inspection in the safety_inspection_date field.
4. (Optional) Enter the vehicle's make in the vehicle_make field.
5. (Optional) Enter the vehicle's model in the vehicle_model field.
6. If the vehicle has a second safety inspection date, enter it in the safety_inspection_date_2 field.
7. If the vehicle has a second make, enter it in the vehicle_make_2 field.
8. If the vehicle has a second model, enter it in the vehicle_model_2 field.
9. Enter the year of the second inspection in the year_2 field.
10. For the Inspection Date field, select the date of the second inspection.
11. Enter the vehicle type in the vehicle_type field.
12. Select the Inspection Status as Pass, Fail, or Pending.
13. If necessary, add additional notes in the Additional Notes field.
14. Enter the vehicle's weight in the Vehicle Weight field.
15. Enter the vehicle's length in the Vehicle Length field.
