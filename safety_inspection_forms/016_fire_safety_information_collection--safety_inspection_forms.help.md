# Fire Safety Information Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Safety Information Collection form is used to gather information about fire safety at specific sites. This form is part of an ongoing process to collect data for analysis and decision-making purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the site details section and enter the required information about the site.
2. In the equipment status section, select all the equipment that you have inspected or checked.
3. In the inspection dates section, enter the dates of the last inspection or audit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Details (Required)** (`site_details`): Enter a brief description of the site, including its name and location.
* **Equipment Status (Required)** (`equipment_status`): Select all the equipment that you have inspected or checked. This includes:
	+ Good: The equipment is functioning correctly and safely.
	+ Bad: The equipment is not functioning correctly or is not safe.
	+ Unknown: You are unsure or unable to determine the status of the equipment.
* **Exits (Optional)** (`exits`): Enter a brief description of the exits at the site, including their locations and any notable features.
* **Inspection Dates (Required)** (`inspection_dates`): Enter the dates of the last inspection or audit.
