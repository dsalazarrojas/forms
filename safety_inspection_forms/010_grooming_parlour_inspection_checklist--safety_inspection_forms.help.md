# Grooming Parlour Inspection Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to inspect and evaluate the safety and hygiene standards of an animal grooming facility.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each field according to the guidelines and instructions provided.
2. Be thorough and accurate in your observations and answers.
3. Take note of any observations or issues that may impact the facility's safety and hygiene.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Animal Facilities** (Page 1): This field is a note field where you can add any observations related to the animal facilities. Please provide a brief description of what you observed.
* **Cleanliness** (Page 2): This field is for providing text-based feedback on the cleanliness of the facility. Please describe your observations regarding the cleanliness of the facilities, equipment, and overall environment.
* **Animal Handling** (Page 3): Select one of the options to indicate your assessment of the animal handling procedures. Please choose the option that best describes your observation.
	+ Animal handling: The facility has an adequate animal handling system in place.
	+ Inadequate animal handling: The facility's animal handling procedures are not adequate.
	+ Proper animal handling: The facility's animal handling procedures are excellent.
* **Animal Records** (Page 4): This field is for providing numerical feedback on the completeness and accuracy of animal records. Please enter the number of records reviewed.
* **Animal Health** (Page 5): Select all that apply for your assessment of the animal health. Please choose the options that best describe your observation.
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Facility License** (Page 6): This field is for providing numerical feedback on the facility's license status. Please enter the number of licenses checked.
* **Staff Training** (Page 7): Select one of the options to indicate your assessment of the staff's training. Please choose the option that best describes your observation.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Overall Assessment** (Page 8): This field is for providing time-based feedback on your overall assessment of the facility. Please enter the time spent observing and evaluating the facility.
* **Comments** (Page 9): This field is for providing text-based feedback on any additional comments or issues that may impact the facility's safety and hygiene.
* **Animal Welfare** (Page 10): Select one of the options to indicate your assessment of the animal welfare. Please choose the option that best describes your observation.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Veterinary Care** (Page 11): Please provide text-based feedback on the veterinary care provided at the facility.
* **Emergency Procedure** (Page 12): Please provide text-based feedback on the emergency procedures in place at the facility.
* **Staff Hygiene** (Page 13): Please provide text-based feedback on the staff's personal hygiene.
* **Staff Vaccinations** (Page 14): Please provide text-based feedback on the staff's vaccinations.
* **Animal Handling Area** (Page 15): This field is a note field where you can add any observations related to the animal handling area. Please provide a brief description of what you observed.
* **Equipment Maintenance** (Page 16): Select all that apply for your assessment of the equipment maintenance. Please choose the options that best describe your observation.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Waste Disposal** (Page 17): Select one of the options to indicate your assessment of the waste disposal. Please choose the option that best describes your observation.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Cleanliness Hazardous Areas** (Page 18): Select one of the options to indicate your assessment of the cleanliness of hazardous areas. Please choose the option that best describes your observation.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Animal Rest** (Page 19): This field is a note field where you can add any observations related to the animal rest area. Please provide a brief description of what you observed.
* **Animal Shelter** (Page 20): This field is a note field where you can add any observations related to the animal shelter. Please provide a brief description of what you observed.
* **Food Storage** (Page 21): Please provide text-based feedback on the food storage conditions at the facility.
* **Staff Awareness** (Page 22): Please provide text-based feedback on the staff's awareness of animal safety and welfare.
* **Staff First Aids** (Page 23): Please provide text-based feedback on the staff's knowledge and use of first aid.
