# Electrical Safety Test Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record the results of an electrical safety test on a system. It's used to document the test date, the inspector's name, and the system's details. The form helps ensure that electrical systems are safe and functioning correctly.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Test Date** of the safety test, using the date format mm/dd/yyyy.
2. Enter your **Inspector Name** and **System Name** to identify yourself and the system being tested.
3. For **System Type**, select 'Yes' if the system is rated correctly or 'No' if it's not.
4. Enter the **Circuit Rating** number, if applicable.
5. For **Electrical Inspection Results**, select 'Yes' for passed and 'No' for failed, and indicate which results apply by selecting all that apply.
6. If necessary, select 'Yes' for **Corrective Action Taken** and list the actions taken.
7. Enter any **Comments** about the test, the system, or the inspection process.
8. Sign and date your **Inspector Signature** to confirm the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Test Date**: Enter the date of the safety test, in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter your name, which helps identify you as the person conducting the test.
* **System Name**: Type the name or identifier of the system being tested to help with future reference.
* **System Type**: Select 'Yes' if the system was rated correctly; 'No' otherwise. This ensures that system ratings are accurate and compliant with safety regulations.
* **Circuit Rating**: Enter the circuit rating number, if applicable. This helps ensure that the system's electrical rating is correct and meets safety standards.
* **Electrical Inspection Results**: Indicate all applicable results, including 'Yes' or 'No' for passed or failed. This helps identify any issues with the system.
* **Corrective Action Taken**: If necessary, select 'Yes' and document the corrective actions taken. This shows that you addressed any problems or issues found during the inspection.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the test, the system, or the inspection process. This helps with further evaluation and decision-making.
* **Inspector Signature**: Sign and date your signature as the inspector, confirming that you conducted the test and documented the results accurately.
