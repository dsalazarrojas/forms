# tunnel_accident_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tunnel accident incident form is used to gather information about incidents that occur in tunnel environments. This form helps in tracking and analyzing data on the frequency, causes, and consequences of such incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are reporting a new incident and not a duplicate.
2. Fill in the required fields as prompted, starting with the incident date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date**: This field captures the date on which the incident occurred. It is recommended to use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY) for consistency.
* **Incident Location**: Enter the exact location where the incident occurred within the tunnel. This could be the name of the tunnel, a specific section, or a reference point.
* **Incident Description**: Provide a clear and concise description of what happened during the incident. This field helps in understanding the context and causes of the incident.
