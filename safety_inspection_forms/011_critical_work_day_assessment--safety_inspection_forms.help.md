# Critical Work Day Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Critical Work Day Assessment form is designed to evaluate the risk level and critical factors associated with a workday. This form helps individuals identify potential hazards and take necessary steps to mitigate them, ensuring a safe and efficient work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the provided options for each field to understand the possible answers.
2. Assess each field based on your knowledge and expertise.
3. Enter your assessments in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workday Risk Level** (`workday_risk_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select all possible risk levels for the workday:
	+ High: Indicates a high level of risk associated with the workday.
	+ Low: Indicates a low level of risk associated with the workday.
* **Resource Impact Level** (`resource_impact_level`, select_one, optional): Choose the resource impact level for the workday:
	+ High: Indicates a high impact on resources associated with the workday.
	+ Low: Indicates a low impact on resources associated with the workday.
* **Workday Frequency** (`workday_frequency`, number, optional): Enter the frequency of the workday:
	+ A numerical value indicating the frequency of the workday.
* **Critical Risk Factor** (`critical_risk_factor`, text, optional): Enter any critical risk factors associated with the workday:
	+ A text field for describing critical risk factors.
* **Resource Availability** (`resource_availability`, number, optional): Enter the resource availability for the workday:
	+ A numerical value indicating the resource availability.
* **Workday Duration** (`workday_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the workday:
	+ A numerical value indicating the duration of the workday.
* **Workday Start Time** (`workday_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the workday:
	+ A time value indicating the start time of the workday.
* **Workday End Time** (`workday_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the workday:
	+ A time value indicating the end time of the workday.
* **Critical Workday Risk Factors** (`critical_workday_risk_factor_1`-`critical_workday_risk_factor_15`, select_multiple, optional): Select all possible risk factors associated with the workday:
	+ 'Yes' indicates the presence of the risk factor.
	+ 'No' indicates the absence of the risk factor.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are familiar with the workday schedule and associated risks before completing this form.
* If unsure about any field, refer to your supervisor or relevant documentation for guidance.
* This form is a subjective assessment and should be used as a guide for identifying potential hazards.
* Review and revise your assessments regularly to ensure accuracy and effectiveness.
