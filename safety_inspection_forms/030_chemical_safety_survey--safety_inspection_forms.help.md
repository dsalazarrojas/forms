# Chemical Safety Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to assess your perception of safety when handling chemicals in the workplace. It will help management understand your experiences and concerns regarding chemical safety, allowing for targeted improvements to be made.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and accurately based on your experiences.
2. For questions with multiple options, select the one that best describes your current state or experience.
3. If you have no response for a question, select the "Never available" or "No" option.
4. If you need help or clarification, please refer to the provided hints for each question.
