# swppp_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The swppp_inspection_form is used to record safety inspections for equipment, machinery, or other safety-related items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the company name in the "Company" field.
2. Enter the name of the inspector in the "Inspector" field.
3. Enter the address in the "Address" field.
4. If applicable, enter the contact person's name in the "Contact Person" field.
5. If applicable, enter the contact person's email address in the "Email" field.
6. If applicable, enter the contact person's phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Enter the date the inspection was completed in the "Date Completed" field.
8. Enter the time the inspection was completed in the "Time Completed" field.
9. Enter any notes about the inspection in the "Inspector Notes" field.
10. If necessary, select any safety standards that were met in the "Safety Standards Met" field.
11. If necessary, select any safety standards that were not met in the "Safety Standards Not Met" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company that owns or is responsible for the equipment or machinery being inspected.
* **Inspector**: Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Address**: Enter the address where the inspection was conducted.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the equipment or machinery.
* (Note: This field is optional, but it's recommended to provide contact information for follow-up.)
* **Email**: Enter the contact person's email address. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Date Completed**: Enter the date the inspection was completed. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Time Completed**: Enter the time the inspection was completed. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Inspector Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the inspection. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Safety Standards Met**: Select any safety standards that were met during the inspection.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Safety Standards Not Met**: Select any safety standards that were not met during the inspection.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'

## Tips
* Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting the form.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information in each field.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with a supervisor or expert before proceeding.
