# Electrical Hazard Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Electrical Hazard Acknowledgement Form is used to document and acknowledge electrical hazards present on a job site, and to record the control measures taken to mitigate these hazards. This form is used to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and internal safety protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and requirements.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information, paying attention to required fields.
3. Sign and date the form to indicate that you have reviewed and acknowledged the electrical hazards present on the job site.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Electrical Hazard Acknowledgement
* **Electrical Hazard Acknowledgement (electrical_hazard_acknowledgement)**: Acknowledge the electrical hazards present on the job site.
* **Date Submitted (date_submitted)**: Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Form Number (electrical_hazard_acknowledgement_form_number)**: Enter the unique form number for this submission.
* **Job Number (job_number)**: Enter the job number assigned to this project.
* **Employee ID (employee_id)**: Enter your employee ID number.
* **Job Supervisor (job_supervisor)**: Enter the name of your job supervisor.
* **Job Location (job_location)**: Enter the location of the job site.
* **Job Supervisor Signature (job_supervisor_signature)**: Sign to confirm that you have reviewed and acknowledged the electrical hazards present on the job site.
* **Job Date (job_date)**: Enter the date of the job.
* **Job Time (job_time)**: Enter the time of the job (optional).
* **Electrical Hazard Control Measures (electrical_hazard_control_measures)**: Describe the control measures taken to mitigate the electrical hazards.

### Page 2: Employee Information
* **Employee Name (employee_name)**: Enter your name.
* **Employee Signature (employee_signature)**: Sign to confirm that you have reviewed and acknowledged the electrical hazards present on the job site.
* **Electrical Hazard Acknowledgement Date (electrical_hazard_acknowledgement_date)**: Enter the date you acknowledged the electrical hazards.
* **Electrical Hazard Acknowledgement By (electrical_hazard_acknowledgement_by)**: Enter your name as the person acknowledging the electrical hazards.
* **Electrical Hazard Acknowledgement Form Number (electrical_hazard_acknowledgement_form_number)**: Enter the unique form number for this submission.
* **Job Notes (job_notes)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the job.
* **Hazards Identified (hazards_identified)**: Describe any electrical hazards identified on the job site.
* **Hazards Controlled (hazards_controlled)**: Describe the control measures taken to mitigate the electrical hazards.
* **Electrical Hazard Acknowledgement Form Version (electrical_hazard_acknowledgement_form_version)**: Enter the version of this form.
* **Form Submitted (form_submitted)**: Indicate if this form has been submitted (Yes/No).
* **Form Submitter (form_submitter)**: Enter the name of the person submitting this form.
* **Form Submission Date (form_submission_date)**: Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Submitted By (submitted_by)**: Enter your name as the person submitting this form.

### Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and completely.
- Keep a record of this form for future reference.
- If you are unsure about any part of this process, consult with your supervisor or safety officer.
