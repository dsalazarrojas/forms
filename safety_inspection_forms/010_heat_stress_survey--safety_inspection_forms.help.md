# Heat Stress Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Heat Stress Survey is designed to collect information about your workplace environment, work schedule, and individual experiences with heat stress. This information will help us better understand the conditions that contribute to heat stress and identify potential areas for improvement to ensure a safer work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. For each field, enter the relevant information according to the field type (e.g., text, number, select_one).
3. If a field has options (e.g., "Heat Stress Level" with options "High", "Medium", "Low"), select the option that best describes your current experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workplace Location** (`Workplace Location`, text, required/optional): This field is used to indicate the location of your workplace. Please enter the name or description of your workplace.
* **Workday** (`Workday`, number, required/optional): This field is used to indicate the number of days you worked during this period. If you worked multiple days, enter the total number of days.
* **Temperature** (`Temperature`, number, required/optional): This field is used to report the average temperature of your workplace during your work period. Please enter the temperature in degrees Celsius or Fahrenheit.
* **Time Worked** (`Time Worked`, number, required/optional): This field is used to report the number of hours you worked during this period. If you worked multiple shifts, enter the total number of hours.
* **Heat Stress Level** (`Heat Stress Level`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the level of heat stress you experienced during your work period. Select one of the following options:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Heat Stress Symptoms** (`Heat Stress Symptoms`, text, required/optional): This field is used to report any symptoms you experienced due to heat stress. Please enter your symptoms in the text field.
* **Heat Stress Precautions** (`Heat Stress Precautions`, text, required/optional): This field is used to report any precautions you took to alleviate heat stress. Please enter your precautions in the text field.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide additional comments or feedback about your experience with heat stress.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, text, required/optional): This field is used to report any tools or equipment provided to you for heat stress prevention. Please enter the name or description of the tool.
* **Created By** (`Created By`, text, required/optional): This field is not applicable and is only for internal use.

## Tips
* Be as accurate as possible when reporting your experience with heat stress.
* If you are unsure about the temperature or time worked, use your best estimate.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Comments" field.
* If you need help or have questions, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
