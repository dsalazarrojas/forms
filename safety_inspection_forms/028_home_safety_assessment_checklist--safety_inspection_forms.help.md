# Home Safety Assessment Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the safety of a residence, identifying potential hazards, necessary improvements, and required safety equipment. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure the home's safety and prevent accidents.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required information for the property manager's contact details.
2. Evaluate the residence's safety by selecting any identified hazards from the list.
3. List the necessary safety improvements for the residence.
4. Provide a plan for the residence's safety.
5. Record the date and time of the safety assessment.
6. Enter the contact information for further communication.
7. Enter the property address.
8. Add any additional notes about the property.
9. Answer the safety inspection questions.
10. List the safety equipment used in the residence.
11. Specify the types of safety equipment used.
12. Indicate if the safety equipment is used.
13. List the conditions of the safety equipment.
14. Specify the maintenance frequency for the safety equipment.
15. Indicate the maintenance status of the safety equipment.
16. Record the maintenance schedule for fire safety equipment.
17. Check if smoke detectors are working properly.
18. Check if fire extinguishers are available and accessible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Manager Information** (`property_manager_info`, `select_one`): Enter the property manager's name and contact details to keep them informed about the safety assessment.
* **Residence Safety Evaluation** (`residence_safety_evaluation`, `note`): This field is for the property manager's evaluation of the residence's safety.
* **Identified Hazards** (`residence_safety_hazards`, `select_multiple`): Select any hazards identified during the safety assessment.
* **Safety Improvements** (`residence_safety_improvements`, `select_multiple`): List the necessary safety improvements for the residence.
* **Residence Safety Plan** (`residence_safety_plan`, `text`): Provide a plan for the residence's safety, outlining steps to be taken and deadlines.
* **Date of Safety Assessment** (`date_of_safety_assessment`, `date`): Record the date of the safety assessment.
* **Time of Safety Assessment** (`time_of_safety_assessment`, `time`): Record the time of the safety assessment.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`): Enter the contact number for further communication.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`): Enter the contact email for further communication.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, `text`): Enter the property address.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, `note`): Add any additional notes about the property.
* **Safety Inspection Questions** (`safety_inspection_questions`, `select_multiple`): Answer the safety inspection questions to identify potential hazards and improvements.
* **Safety Inspection Answers** (`safety_inspection_answers`, `select_multiple`): Enter your answers to the safety inspection questions.
* **Home Safety Equipment** (`home_safety_equipment`, `text`): List the safety equipment used in the residence.
* **Types of Safety Equipment** (`home_safety_equipment_types`, `select_multiple`): Specify the types of safety equipment used.
* **Used** (`home_safety_equipment_used`, `select_one`): Indicate if the safety equipment is used.
* **Safety Equipment Conditions** (`home_safety_equipment_conditions`, `select_multiple`): List the conditions of the safety equipment.
* **Maintenance Frequency** (`home_safety_equipment_maintenance`, `select_multiple`): Specify the maintenance frequency for the safety equipment.
* **Maintenance Frequency** (`home_safety_equipment_maintenance_freq`, `text`): Record the maintenance frequency for the safety equipment.
* **Maintenance Status** (`home_safety_equipment_maintenance_status`, `select_multiple`): Indicate the maintenance status of the safety equipment.
* **Fire Safety Equipment Maintenance** (`fire_safety_equipment_maintenance`, `text`): Record the maintenance schedule for fire safety equipment.
* **Fire Safety Equipment Maintenance Frequency** (`fire_safety_equipment_maintenance_freq`, `text`): Record the maintenance frequency for fire safety equipment.
* **Smoke Detectors** (`smoke_detectors`, `select_multiple`): Check if smoke detectors are working properly.
* **Fire Extinguisher** (`fire_extinguishers`, `select_multiple`): Check if fire extinguishers are available and accessible.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately to ensure the residence's safety.
* Make sure to keep the contact information up-to-date for further communication.
* Record any additional notes about the property for future reference.
* Regularly review and update the safety assessment and plans to ensure the residence's safety.
