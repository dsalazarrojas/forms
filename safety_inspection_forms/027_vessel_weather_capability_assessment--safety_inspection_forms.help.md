# Vessel Weather Capability Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the weather capability of a vessel. It collects information about the user's vessel, its safety equipment, and the weather conditions it operates in. The form is used to evaluate the vessel's preparedness and ability to handle adverse weather conditions.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Provide your contact information and vessel details on **Page 1: User Information**
* Describe the current weather conditions on **Page 2: Weather Conditions**
* Choose the safety equipment available on board from the list on **Page 4: Safety Equipment**
* Describe the details of each listed safety equipment on **Page 7: Safety Equipment Details**
* Enter any additional information about the vessel's performance on **Page 6: Vessel Performance**
* Review the weather forecast (if applicable) on **Page 8: Weather Forecast**
* Finally, review your submission and confirm on **Page 9: Submission Confirmation**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vessel Details** (`vessel_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your vessel, including its type, size, and any other relevant information.
* **Weather Conditions** (`weather_conditions`, text, optional): Describe the current weather conditions you are operating in, including any notable weather patterns or forecasts.
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from the list:
	+ Emergency Position Indicating (EPIRB)
	+ Emergency Position Indicating Radio (EPIRB)
	+ Flares
	+ Life jacket
	+ Satellite phone
	+ Satellite communication device
* **Safety Equipment Details** (`safety_equipment_details`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of each safety equipment item listed.
* **Vessel Performance** (`vessel_performance`, text, optional): Enter any information about the vessel's performance, such as its speed, course, and any notable performance metrics.
* **Weather Forecast** (`weather_forecast`, text, optional): Review and enter any information about the current weather forecast, such as expected weather patterns or alerts.
* **Submission Confirmation** (`submission_confirmation`, text, optional): Review and confirm your submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
