# Workplace Safety Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to ensure that workplaces are safe and compliant with relevant regulations. It helps workplaces identify potential hazards and take necessary steps to address them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each section carefully to determine which areas of the workplace require attention.
2. For each section, choose the correct options from the provided list.
3. Enter the number of items or the number of people that apply to each option.
4. Make sure to review all sections carefully and enter accurate information.
5. Submit the form once you have reviewed all sections.
