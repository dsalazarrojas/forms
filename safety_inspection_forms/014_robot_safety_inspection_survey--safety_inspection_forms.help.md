# Robot Safety Inspection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a robot safety inspection survey. The purpose of this form is to gather data on the condition of a robot, its model, number, and other relevant details to ensure the robot is functioning safely and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the facility name where the robot is located.
2. Enter the date of the last inspection.
3. Enter the next inspection due date.
4. Enter the robot model.
5. Enter the robot number.
6. Enter the serial number.
7. Indicate if the facility is in a hazardous area (yes/no).
8. Enter the facility location (if applicable).
9. Enter the robot type.
10. Report any hazards found during the inspection.
11. Describe any corrective actions taken.
12. Enter the next action date.
13. Enter any comments.
14. Enter the contact information for the person submitting the form.
15. Enter the contact information for the person or team assigned to address any issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility name**: Enter the name of the facility where the robot is located.
* **Date last inspection**: Enter the date of the last inspection performed on the robot.
* **Next inspection due date**: Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection for the robot.
* **Robot model**: Enter the model number of the robot.
* **Robot number**: Enter the unique number assigned to the robot.
* **Serial number**: Enter the serial number of the robot.
* **Facility area**: Indicate if the facility where the robot is located is in a hazardous area (yes/no).
* **Facility location**: Enter the location of the facility where the robot is located (if applicable).
* **Robot type**: Enter the type of robot (e.g. industrial, service, etc.).
* **Condition of inspection**: Report any hazards found during the inspection, and indicate if they have been addressed (yes/no).
* **Hazards found**: Describe any hazards found during the inspection.
* **Corrective action taken**: Describe any corrective actions taken to address the hazards found.
* **Next action date**: Enter the date of the next action planned to address any outstanding issues.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the robot's condition.
* **Submitted by**: Enter your name, title, and contact information.
* **Assigned to**: Enter the name, title, contact information, and department of the person or team assigned to address any issues.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification.
* If you have already filled out this form for the same robot, please do not resubmit.
