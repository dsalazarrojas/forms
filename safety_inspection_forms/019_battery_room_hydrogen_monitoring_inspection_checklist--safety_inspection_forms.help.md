# Battery Room Hydrogen Monitoring Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data on the status of safety-critical elements in the battery room during regular safety inspections. The information collected will help ensure that the battery room is safe and in compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **Inspection ID**, which is a unique identifier for the inspection.
2. Enter the **Inspection Date** and **Inspection Time** to record when the inspection was performed.
3. Enter the **Inspector Name** to record who performed the inspection.
4. Enter the **Facility Name** to record the location where the inspection was performed.
5. Select the **Ventilation System Status** to report the status of the ventilation system, which is essential for maintaining a safe atmosphere in the battery room.
6. Select the **Hydrogen Detector Status** to report the status of the detector, which helps detect leaks and ensure safety.
7. Enter the **Hydrogen Level Reading** to record the current ppm reading.
8. Set the **Alarm Threshold** to report the level at which the alarm would go off.
9. Report whether the **Alarm** was **Tested** and if it's working or failed.
10. Report the **Emergency Lighting** status, which is critical for emergency situations.
11. Report whether the **Fire Extinguisher** is available and in date.
12. Report whether the **Eyewash Station** is accessible and working.
13. Report whether **Warning Signs** are posted and visible.
14. Report the **Floor Condition**, which should be clean and dry.
15. Report the **Battery Condition** after a visual inspection.
16. Report whether any **Leaks** were detected.
17. Report the **Temperature Reading** in Celsius.
18. Rate the **Overall Safety Rating** of the room.
19. Enter any **Corrective Actions** needed to improve safety.
20. Enter the **Next Inspection Date** when the room is due for the next inspection.
