# Gym Safety Compliance Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess the gym's safety compliance level and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review each page carefully before starting to fill out the form.
2. Enter the information as accurately as possible.
3. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact your supervisor or safety officer for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This page is an overview of the survey and an introduction to the importance of safety compliance in the gym.
* **Safety Procedures**: This field is where you will enter the gym's safety procedures, including emergency contact information and other relevant details.
* *Type: Text* (Note: This field should be filled out by typing in the text)
* **Emergency Contacts**: This field is where you will select the emergency contacts that are available in the gym.
* *Type: Select Multiple* (Note: Select all applicable options)
* **Fire Drills**: This field is where you will enter the last fire drill date and time.
* *Type: Date* (Note: Enter in the date format mm/dd/yyyy)
* **First Aid**: This field is where you will enter the location and availability of first aid equipment.
* *Type: Text* (Note: Enter in a brief description)
* **Evacuation Plan**: This field is where you will indicate if the gym has an evacuation plan.
* *Type: Select One* (Note: Select Yes or No)
* **Emergency Contacts Email**: This field is where you will enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* *Type: Email* (Note: Enter in the email format)
* **Safety Meetings**: This field is where you will select the frequency of safety meetings.
* *Type: Select One* (Note: Select Yes or No)
* **Fire Extinguishers**: This field is where you will select the location of fire extinguishers.
* *Type: Select Multiple* (Note: Select all applicable options)
* **Exit Routes**: This field is where you will enter the number of exit routes available in the gym.
