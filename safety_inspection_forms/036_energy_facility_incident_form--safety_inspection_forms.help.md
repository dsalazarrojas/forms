# energy_facility_incident_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about incidents that occur at energy facilities. It is a general template for documenting and reporting incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Facility Name" field with the name of the facility where the incident occurred.
2. Enter the date of the incident in the "Incident Date" field.
3. Enter the time of the incident in the "Incident Time" field.
4. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the facility where the incident occurred. For example, "ABC Power Plant".
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the incident in the format 24-hour clock: HH:MM.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including what happened, who was involved, and any relevant circumstances.
