# Quarterly Food Safety Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help you, as a food service provider, assess and document the safety of your food handling and storage practices for the quarter. This form is used to evaluate the risk level of various aspects of food safety, including food temperature, cross-contamination, proper storage conditions, cleanliness, pest control, food handling, and chemical control.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter the Food Service Provider**: In the "Food Service Provider" field, enter the name of the food service or establishment where the food is being provided.
2. **Enter the Location**: In the "Location" field, enter the physical address or location where the food is being stored or handled.
3. **Enter the Last Review Date**: In the "Last Review Date" field, enter the date of the last review or assessment of the food safety practices.
4. **Assess the Safety Risk Level**: In the "Safety Risk Level" field, select a number from 1 (low) to 5 (high) to indicate the level of risk associated with the food safety practices.
5. **Assess Food Temperature**: In the "Food Temperature" field, enter a number to indicate the average temperature (in degrees Fahrenheit) of the food storage or handling areas.
6. **Assess Cross Contamination**: In the "Cross Contamination" field, select a number from 1 (low) to 5 (high) to indicate the level of cross contamination risk.
7. **Assess Proper Storage Conditions**: In the "Proper Storage Conditions" field, enter a brief description of the condition of the storage areas, such as "clean" or " dirty".
8. **Assess Cleanliness**: In the "Cleanliness" field, select a number from 1 (low) to 5 (high) to indicate the level of cleanliness of the food handling and storage areas.
9. **Assess Pest Control**: In the "Pest Control" field, select a number from 1 (low) to 5 (high) to indicate the level of pest control measures in place.
10. **Assess Food Handling**: In the "Food Handling" field, select a number from 1 (low) to 5 (high) to indicate the level of food handling risk.
11. **Assess Chemical Control**: In the "Chemical Control" field, enter a brief description of the control measures in place, such as "regularly checked" or "none".
