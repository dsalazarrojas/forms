# Waste Bin Weight Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the waste bin weight audit process. It is intended for waste management personnel to accurately record the weight of waste bins and their condition. This form helps in maintaining accurate records and facilitates the process of monitoring and managing waste bins.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the necessary fields with the required information.
2. Make sure to accurately record the weight of the waste bin and its condition.
3. Select the relevant options for the bin condition and any additional remarks.
4. Verify that all fields are filled in before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Waste Bin Weight Audit Form** (`waste_bin_weight_audit_form`, text, required): Enter the title of the form being completed.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of the waste bin, such as the room or building.
* **Bin Number** (`bin_number`, text, required): Enter the unique number assigned to the waste bin.
* **Weight (kg)** (`weight`, number, required): Accurately record the weight of the waste bin in kilograms.
* **Audit Date** (`audit_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the audit.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the audit.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, note, required): Enter any additional comments about the bin condition.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in case of any follow-up questions.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for any emergency contact.
* **Which of the following best describes the bin condition?** (`select_one`, select_one, required): Select the bin condition from the options: Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Which of the following apply?** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): Select any of the following options that apply to the bin condition: Overfilled, Not Clean, Broken, or Leaking.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the audit.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the audit.
* **Remarks** (`remarks_1`, note, required): Enter any additional comments about the bin condition.

## Tips
* Make sure to accurately record the weight of the waste bin to ensure accurate tracking.
* Select the correct bin condition based on your observation.
* Use the email and phone number fields for follow-up contact.
* Use the remarks fields for any additional comments or suggestions.
