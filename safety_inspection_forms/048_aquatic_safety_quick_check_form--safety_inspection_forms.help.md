# Aquatic Safety Quick Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps facility management and safety personnel quickly assess the safety of an aquatic facility. It ensures that all necessary checks are performed, and any issues are addressed promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form:

1. Provide the details of the inspection, including the date and time.
2. Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
3. Specify the area or pool location within the facility.
4. Record the name of the inspector conducting the inspection.
5. Check off the results of each safety check, ensuring you are aware of any hazards or concerns.
6. If issues are found, describe them in the "Issues or Concerns Found" field.
7. Finally, select the required actions needed to address the issues or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Details** (`inspection_details`): A free-text field for capturing the details of the inspection.
* **Date of Safety Check** (`check_date`): Enter the date of the inspection in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time of Check** (`check_time`): Enter the time of the inspection in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`): Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
* **Area or Pool Location** (`location_area`): Specify the area or pool within the facility that was inspected.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`): Enter the name of the inspector conducting the inspection.
* **Safety Checks** (`safety_checks`): A free-text field for capturing any safety concerns or issues found during the inspection.
* **Water Clarity Acceptable** (`water_clarity`): Check if the water clarity meets the required standards (`Yes`/`No`).
* **Chemical Levels Within Range** (`chemical_levels`): Check if the chemical levels are within the acceptable range (`Yes`/`No`/`Not Tested`).
* **All Equipment Functional** (`equipment_functional`): Check if all equipment is functioning correctly (`Yes`/`No`).
* **Deck Free of Hazards** (`deck_hazards`): Check if the deck is free of hazards (`Yes`/`No`).
* **Lifeguard Present and Alert** (`lifeguard_present`): Check if a lifeguard is present and alert (`Yes`/`No`/`Not Applicable`).
* **Emergency Equipment Available** (`emergency_equipment`): Check if emergency equipment is available (`Yes`/`No`).
* **Issues or Concerns Found** (`issues_found`): A free-text field for capturing any issues or concerns found during the inspection.
* **Action Required** (`action_required`): Select the action required to address any issues or concerns found (`None`/`Minor Maintenance`/`Immediate Attention`).

Please note that the fields marked as "required" are necessary for a proper completion of the form.
