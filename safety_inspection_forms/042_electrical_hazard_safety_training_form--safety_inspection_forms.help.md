# electrical_hazard_safety_training_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track the completion of electrical safety training for employees. It is essential to complete this form accurately and honestly to ensure that training is properly recorded and that employees are aware of their responsibility to maintain electrical safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
2. Select whether or not you have completed the training. 
3. Enter the date of when you completed the training.
4. Enter the time you spent in training (hours).
5. Enter the name of the person who led the training.
6. Enter the number of the course.
7. Enter where the training took place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: This field is where you enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Training Completed**: Select "Completed" if you have attended and finished the electrical safety training. Select "Not Completed" if you have not attended or have not finished the training.
* **Training Date**: Enter the date on which you completed the training.
* **Training Time**: Enter the number of hours spent in training.
* **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the person who led the training session.
* **Course Number**: Enter the number of the course.
* **Training Location**: Enter the location where you completed the training.

## Tips
* Please be accurate and honest when filling out this form.
* Ensure that you have actually attended and completed the training before marking "Completed".
* If you have any issues or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or training administrator.
