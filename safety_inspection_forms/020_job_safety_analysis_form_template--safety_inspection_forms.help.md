# Job Safety Analysis Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Safety Analysis Form Template is a tool used to collect and document safety-related information about a job or task. It is designed to help organizations identify and assess potential safety hazards and develop measures to mitigate them.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  The form consists of multiple pages, each with its own set of fields. Please fill out each page in order from 1 to 7.
2.  The form requires no prior knowledge or preparation, so feel free to ask if you need any clarification.
3.  Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate risk assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Job Title (`form_title`):** Enter a descriptive title for the job or task you are analyzing.
    *   This field is optional, but it helps in organizing and referencing the analysis later.
*   **Category (`category`):** Select a relevant category for your job or task.
    *   This helps in grouping similar tasks or jobs and identifying common hazards.
*   **Description (`description`):** Write a detailed description of the job or task.
    *   This field should cover any relevant details such as equipment, tools, and procedures used.
*   **Assigned Tool (`assigned_tool`):** Identify the tools, equipment, or machinery involved in the job or task.
    *   Be specific about the tool or equipment used, including brand and model if possible.
*   **Created By (`created_by`):** Identify the person responsible for creating this analysis.
    *   This field is typically used to track who created the analysis and when.
*   **Created Date (`created_date`):** Enter the date when the analysis was created.
    *   Use the standard date format to avoid confusion.
*   **Modified By (`modified_by`):** Identify the last person who modified this analysis.
    *   This field helps track any updates or changes made to the analysis.
*   **Modified Date (`modified_date`):** Enter the last date this analysis was modified.
    *   Similar to the created date, use the standard date format.

## Tips

*   Always fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
*   Use clear and descriptive language when filling out the form fields.
*   If you're unsure about any field, do not hesitate to reach out to form@gic.mx for support.
