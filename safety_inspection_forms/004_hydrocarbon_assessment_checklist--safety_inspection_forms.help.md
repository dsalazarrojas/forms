# Hydrocarbon Assessment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hydrocarbon Assessment Checklist is a form designed to assess the risks associated with hydrocarbon storage and handling on your site. This form helps identify potential hazards, assess risks, and ensure that your site is prepared for emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply review each section and provide information about your site's specific situation. Each section is designed to capture important details about your site's hydrocarbon storage and handling practices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General** (`1`, `General`, `Note`, `[]`): Enter a brief overview of your site's general information, such as company name, site location, and contact information.
* **Storage** (`2`, `Storage`, `Note`, `[]`): Describe your site's storage practices, including types of hydrocarbons stored, storage capacity, and any relevant safety procedures.
* **Spill Response** (`3`, `Spill Response`, `Note`, `[]`): Outline your site's spill response procedures, including emergency contact information, spill response team members, and equipment.
* **Staff Training** (`4`, `Staff Training`, `Note`, `[]`): Describe the training received by your site's staff on hydrocarbon handling and safety procedures.
* **Hydrocarbon Storage** (`5`, `Hydrocarbon Storage`, `Number`, `[]`): Indicate the number of hydrocarbons stored on your site.
* **Spill Response Planning** (`6`, `Spill Response Planning`, `Note`, `[]`): Outline your site's spill response planning, including emergency procedures and containment methods.
* **Emergency Procedures** (`7`, `Emergency Procedures`, `Note`, `[]`): Describe your site's emergency procedures, including evacuation routes and emergency contact information.
* **Hydrocarbon Handling** (`8`, `Hydrocarbon Handling`, `Note`, `[]`): Explain your site's hydrocarbon handling practices, including transfer, storage, and disposal methods.
* **Spill Recovery** (`9`, `Spill Recovery`, `Note`, `[]`): Describe your site's spill recovery procedures, including containment and mitigation methods.
* **Site Specifics** (`10`, `Site Specifics`, `Note`, `[]`): Enter site-specific information, such as local regulations and environmental conditions.
* **Site Characteristics** (`11`, `Site Characteristics`, `Note`, `[]`): Describe your site's characteristics, including terrain, climate, and surrounding features.
* **Hydrocarbon Assessment** (`12`, `Hydrocarbon Assessment`, `Note`, `[]`): Assess the hydrocarbon risks on your site, including potential hazards and risks.
* **Risk Control** (`13`, `Risk Control`, `Note`, `[]`): Explain your site's risk control measures, including procedures and protocols.

* **Spill Containment** (`16`, `Spill Containment`, `Note`, `[]`): Outline your site's spill containment procedures, including containment methods and materials.
* **Incident Reporting** (`17`, `Incident Reporting`, `Note`, `[]`): Describe your site's incident reporting procedures, including reporting requirements and deadlines.

* **Site Environmental Impacts** (`20`, `Site Environmental Impacts`, `Note`, `[]`): Describe your site's environmental impacts, including potential effects on local ecosystems.
* **Hydrocarbon Hazards** (`21`, `Hydrocarbon Hazards`, `Note`, `[]`): Identify hydrocarbon hazards on your site, including potential risks and consequences.
* **Spill Recovery Plan** (`22`, `Spill Recovery Plan`, `Note`, `[]`): Outline your site's spill recovery plan, including recovery methods and procedures.
* **Spill Prevention** (`23`, `Spill Prevention`, `Note`, `[]`): Explain your site's spill prevention measures, including practices and procedures.
* **Spill Supervision** (`24`, `Spill Supervision`, `Note`, `[]`): Describe your site's spill supervision procedures, including monitoring and reporting requirements.
