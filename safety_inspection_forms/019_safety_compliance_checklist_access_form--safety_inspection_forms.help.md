# Safety Compliance Checklist Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the safety compliance checklists used in your organization. It is intended to be used by safety officers to access and manage safety compliance data, ensuring that your organization is meeting its safety standards. This form helps to identify potential risks and areas of non-compliance, and provides a systematic approach to maintaining safety records and reporting incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Form Title** field with a brief description of the safety compliance checklist.
2. In the **Purpose of the Safety Compliance Checklist** field, describe the purpose and scope of the checklist.
3. Enter the **Responsible Officer** name and designation in the corresponding field.
4. Select all the **Inspection Areas** relevant to the checklist (e.g. Fire Safety, Electrical Safety, etc.).
5. Enter the **Inspection Schedule** date of the last inspection.
6. Describe the **Incident Reporting** procedures and documentation process in the text field.
7. Choose the type of **Safety Training** provided to your staff (e.g. On-the-job, Class Room, Online Course, etc.).
8. Select all the relevant **Safety Records Maintenance** areas (e.g. Maintenance Records, Incident Records, etc.).
9. Describe the **Non-Compliance Consequences** of not meeting safety standards.
10. Choose the **Incident Reporting Process** used in your organization (e.g. Immediate Reporting, Investigation, Reporting to Authorities, etc.).
11. Select all the **Role of Safety Officer** areas (e.g. Conducting Training, Conducting Inspections, etc.).
12. Choose the **Safety Compliance Monitoring** method used in your organization (e.g. Regular Inspections, Audits, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Form Title** (`form_title`, Text, Required)
Enter a brief description of the safety compliance checklist.

### **Purpose of the Safety Compliance Checklist** (`purpose`, Text, Required)
Describe the purpose and scope of the checklist.

### **Responsible Officer** (`responsible_officer`, Text, Required)
Enter the name and designation of the officer responsible for the checklist.

### **Inspection Areas** (`inspection_areas`, Select Multiple, Required)
Select all relevant areas to be inspected (e.g. Fire Safety, Electrical Safety, etc.).

### **Inspection Schedule** (`inspection_schedule`, Date, Required)
Enter the date of the last inspection.

### **Incident Reporting** (`incident_reporting`, Text, Required)
Describe the procedures and documentation process for reporting and investigating safety incidents.

### **Safety Training** (`safety_training`, Select One, Required)
Choose the type of safety training provided to your staff (e.g. On-the-job, Class Room, Online Course, etc.).

### **Safety Records Maintenance** (`safety_records`, Select Multiple, Required)
Select all relevant areas of safety records maintenance (e.g. Maintenance Records, Incident Records, etc.).

### **Non-Compliance Consequences** (`non_compliance_consequences`, Text, Required)
Describe the consequences of non-compliance with safety standards.

### **Incident Reporting Process** (`incident_reporting_process`, Select Multiple, Required)
Choose the incident reporting process used in your organization (e.g. Immediate Reporting, Investigation, etc.).

### **Role of Safety Officer** (`safety_officer_role`, Select Multiple, Required)
Select all areas of the safety officer's role (e.g. Conducting Training, Conducting Inspections, etc.).

### **Safety Compliance Monitoring** (`safety_compliance_monitoring`, Select One, Required)
Choose the safety compliance monitoring method used (e.g. Regular Inspections, Audits, etc.).
