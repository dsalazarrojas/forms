# Hospital Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on safety inspections conducted in hospitals and other healthcare settings. It is meant to help identify potential safety risks, document incidents, and track completed inspections.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by entering the patient's ID number.
2.  List all the potential safety risks encountered during the inspection.
3.  Indicate the level of risk (high, medium, or low) for each risk.
4.  Select the location where the inspection was conducted.
5.  Provide a detailed description of the incidents or observations made during the inspection.
6.  Enter the date and time of the inspection.
7.  Specify the person or team assigned to complete the inspection.
8.  Enter the person or team that completed the inspection.
9.  Enter the date the inspection was completed.
10.  List the individuals who conducted the inspection.
11.  Enter the address of the hospital.
12.  Enter the patient's name.
13.  Enter the patient's phone number.
14.  Enter the patient's email (if applicable).
15.  Select the patient's room number (if applicable).
16.  Add any additional comments or notes from the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient ID**: A unique identifier for the patient.
*   **Safety Risks**: A list of potential safety risks encountered during the inspection. Examples: Fire Hazards, Fall Hazards, Chemical Spill, Slip/Trip Hazards, Other
*   **Risk Factor**: The level of risk (High, Medium, Low)
*   **Location**: The location of the inspection (Hospital, Clinics, Outpatient, Laboratory, Other)
*   **Description**: A detailed description of the incidents or observations made during the inspection.
*   **Date Visited**: The date of the inspection.
*   **Time Visited**: The time of the inspection.
*   **Assigned To**: The person or team assigned to complete the inspection.
*   **Completed By**: The person or team that completed the inspection.
*   **Completed Date**: The date the inspection was completed.
*   **Safety Inspectors**: The individuals who conducted the inspection.
*   **Hospital Address**: The address of the hospital.
*   **Patient Name**: The name of the patient.
*   **Patient Phone**: The phone number of the patient.
*   **Patient Email**: The email of the patient (if applicable).
*   **Patient Room**: The room number of the patient (if applicable).
*   **Notes**: Any additional comments or notes from the inspection.

## Tips

*   Make sure to complete each field accurately and truthfully.
*   Be specific when describing incidents or observations.
*   List all potential safety risks and their corresponding risk factors.
*   Enter the correct date and time of the inspection.
*   Ensure that the assigned and completed fields are accurately filled out.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it.
