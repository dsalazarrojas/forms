# Public Safety Program Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Safety Program Waiver form is used to collect information from participants about their safety and emergency contact information. This form ensures that participants are aware of and agree to the safety guidelines and rules for the program or event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the General Information field with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide information about the participant, including their emergency contact details.
3. Sign and agree to the waiver as the participant.
4. If the participant has an emergency contact, fill out their details, sign, and agree to the waiver.
5. Enter the date and time of the event or program.
6. If needed, fill out any additional emergency details.
7. Select whether you or your emergency contact agree to the waiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information (Field 1)
* General Information (`general_info`): A field to enter basic participant information.

### Participant Details (Field 2)
* Participant Details (`participant_details`): A field to enter information about the participant.

### Emergency Contact (Field 3)
* Emergency Contact (`emergency_contact`): A field to enter emergency contact information.

### Participant Sign Up (Field 4)
* Participant Sign Up (`participant_sign_up`): A field for the participant to confirm they have read and agree to the waiver.

### Participant Signature (Field 5)
* Participant Signature (`participant_signature`): A field for the participant to sign their name and confirm they have read and agree to the waiver.

### Emergency Contact Sign Up (Field 6)
* Emergency Contact Sign Up (`emergency_sign_up`): A field for the emergency contact to confirm they have read and agree to the waiver.

### Emergency Contact Signature (Field 7)
* Emergency Contact Signature (`emergency_signature`): A field for the emergency contact to sign their name and confirm they have read and agree to the waiver.

### Date and Time (Field 8)
* Date and Time (`date_and_time`): A field to enter the date and time of the event or program.

### Emergency Details (Field 9)
* Emergency Details (`emergency_details`): A field for additional emergency details (if needed).

### Emergency Phone (Field 10)
* Emergency Phone (`emergency_phone`): A field to enter the emergency contact's phone number.

### Participant Info (Field 11)
* Participant Info (`participant_info`): A field to enter any additional participant information.

### Emergency Email (Field 12)
* Emergency Email (`emergency_email`): A field to enter the emergency contact's email address.

### Emergency Note (Field 13)
* Emergency Note (`emergency_note`): A field for any additional emergency contact information.

### Participant Agree (Field 14)
* Participant Agree (`participant_agree`): A field to select whether the participant agrees to the waiver.

### Emergency Agree (Field 15)
* Emergency Agree (`emergency_agree`): A field to select whether the emergency contact agrees to the waiver.

### Participant Signature 2 (Field 16)
* Participant Signature 2 (`participant_signature2`): A field for the participant to re-sign to confirm they have read and agree to the waiver.

### Emergency Contact Signature 2 (Field 17)
* Emergency Contact Signature 2 (`emergency_signature2`): A field for the emergency contact to re-sign to confirm they have read and agree to the waiver.

### Date (Field 18)
* Date (`date`): A field to enter the date of the event or program.

### Time (Field 19)
* Time (`time`): A field to enter the time of the event or program.

### Participant Date (Field 20)
* Participant Date (`participant_date`): A field to enter the participant's date.

### Emergency Date (Field 21)
* Emergency Date (`emergency_date`): A field to enter the emergency contact's date.

### Participant Time (Field 22)
* Participant Time (`participant_time`): A field to enter the participant's time.

### Emergency Time (Field 23)
* Emergency Time (`emergency_time`): A field to enter the emergency contact's time.

### Participant Agree 2 (Field 24)
* Participant Agree 2 (`participant_agree2`): A field to select whether the participant agrees to the waiver (again).

### Emergency Agree 2 (Field 25)
* Emergency Agree 2 (`emergency_agree2`): A field to select whether the emergency contact agrees to the waiver (again).
