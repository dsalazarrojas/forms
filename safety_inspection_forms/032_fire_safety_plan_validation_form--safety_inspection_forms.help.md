# Fire Safety Plan Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Safety Plan Validation Form is a tool used to validate fire safety plans for facilities. This form is designed to gather essential information to assess fire safety risks and ensure compliance with local regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand each section and field.
2. Enter accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions or access rights to complete this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Information**: Enter a brief description of the facility, including its name, address, and a contact person's name.
* **Facility Information 1-20**: These fields are for entering additional facility-specific information. Please enter any relevant details about the facility, such as its size, type, or number of occupants.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Facility Information 21-22**: These fields are for entering any additional facility information not covered in the previous fields.
* **Note**: If you're unsure about any field, please consult your local fire safety authorities or experts for guidance.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness.
* Use the correct format for dates and times.
* If you need help with any field, contact your local fire safety authorities or experts.
* This form is a supplement to your facility's fire safety plan, and it's essential to maintain accurate and up-to-date information.
