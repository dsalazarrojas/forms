# Hospital Safety Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Safety Compliance Audit Form is a one-time audit form used to assess a facility's compliance with hospital safety regulations. This form should be completed for a specific facility at the scheduled audit time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the facility name and address in the corresponding text fields.
2. Provide your facility's phone number and email address for contact information.
3. Select the Safety Team Lead and Team Members who will be participating in the audit.
4. Choose the Safety Inspector(s) responsible for conducting the audit.
5. Enter the date and time of the audit.
6. Complete the form by entering your name and signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility being audited.
* **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility being audited.
* **Facility Phone**: Enter the phone number of the facility for contact information.
* **Facility Email**: Enter the email address of the facility for contact information.
* **Facility Date**: Enter the date of the audit in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Facility Time**: Enter the time of the audit in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Safety Team Lead**: Select the Safety Team Lead responsible for the audit.
* **Safety Team Members**: Select all members of the safety team participating in the audit.
* **Safety Inspector**: Select all Safety Inspectors conducting the audit.
* **Audit Date**: Enter the date of the audit in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Audit Time**: Enter the time of the audit in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Completed By**: Enter your name as the person completing the form.
* **Review By**: Enter your name as the person reviewing the form.
* **Signature**: Enter your name and signature for verification.

## Tips

* Be sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for your facility.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure you've selected the correct Safety Team Lead and Team Members for the audit.
* Enter the correct audit date and time.
* Sign and date the form for verification purposes.
