# Fire Safety Daily Work Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Safety Daily Work Report is a form used to document and report daily work activities and safety concerns related to fire safety in a facility. This form helps to ensure that all staff and supervisors are aware of the current safety status of the facility and can take necessary actions to mitigate any risks identified.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant location from the drop-down menu.
2. Enter the date of the daily work activities.
3. Enter the time you started and finished work.
4. Describe any fire safety concerns or issues you encountered during your work.
5. Identify the severity of the risk and describe it in detail.
6. Outline any necessary actions or procedures to be taken to mitigate the risk.
7. Document any safety equipment used or noted during your work.
8. Provide any additional notes or comments regarding safety procedures.
9. Enter the emergency contact information, including phone numbers and email addresses.
10. Sign the form with your supervisor's signature to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fire Safety Daily Work Report (text)**: This field is where you can describe the daily work activities related to fire safety.
* **Facility (select_one)**: Select the location where you worked on the fire safety report.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date of the daily work activities.
* **Time In/Time Out (time)**: Enter the time you started and finished work for the day.
* **Location (select_one)**: Select the location where you worked on the fire safety report.
* **Fire Safety Inspection (text)**: Describe any fire safety inspections you conducted during the day.
* **Fire Safety Risk (text)**: Identify any fire safety risks encountered during the workday.
* **Risk Description (text)**: Provide a detailed description of the fire safety risk.
* **Severity (text)**: Select the severity of the risk.
* **Action (text)**: Outline any necessary actions or procedures to be taken to mitigate the risk.
* **Safety Equipment (text)**: Document any safety equipment used or noted during the workday.
* **Safety Equipment Notes (text)**: Provide any additional notes or comments regarding safety equipment.
* **Fire Safety Procedure (text)**: Describe any fire safety procedures followed during the day.
* **Fire Safety Procedure Notes (text)**: Provide any additional notes or comments regarding fire safety procedures.
* **Emergency Contact (text)**: Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter the emergency contact phone number.
* **Alt Phone (text)**: Enter an alternative emergency contact phone number.
* **Email (text)**: Enter the emergency contact email address.
* **Other (text)**: Enter any other emergency contact information.
* **Supervisor Signature (note)**: Sign the form with your supervisor's signature to confirm completion.
* **Supervisor (text)**: Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Facility Manager Signature (note)**: Sign the form with the facility manager's signature to confirm completion.
* **Facility Manager (text)**: Enter the name of the facility manager.

Please make sure to complete all required fields and provide as much detail as possible to ensure accurate and effective use of this form.
