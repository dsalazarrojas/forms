# Housing Development Site Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Housing Development Site Audit Form is a crucial tool for assessing the safety status of a housing development site. It is designed to identify potential hazards, document actions taken to mitigate them, and track the site's safety inspection history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Enter Site Address**: Fill in the site address, which includes the street address, number, and any additional notes about the site's location.
2. **List Contact Person**: Provide the contact person's name, title, and other relevant contact information.
3. **Safety Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the safety inspection, which will serve as a reference for future actions.
4. **Hazard Identified**: Select the relevant hazard identified during the inspection from the provided options. This could be a potential fall hazard, environmental concern, or other safety risks.
5. **Action Taken**: Briefly describe the actions taken to address the hazard identified, including any necessary corrective measures.
6. **Next Steps**: List the next steps planned for the site, including any upcoming inspections or actions required to ensure the site's safety.
7. **Safety Inspection Form Status**: Select the current status of the form, indicating whether the site has passed, failed, or is pending.
8. **Safety Inspection Comments**: Add any additional comments or concerns related to the site's safety status.
9. **Photo Evidence**: Attach any relevant photos of the site or hazards identified.
10. **Document Attachment Evidence**: Attach any supporting documents or records related to the site's safety status.
11. **Site Map Evidence**: Attach a site map or diagram highlighting the hazards or issues identified.
12. **Video Evidence**: Attach a video recording of the site or hazards identified.
