# ESD Safety Equipment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ESD Safety Equipment Survey is used to collect information about the safety equipment used to mitigate electrostatic discharge in a facility. This survey helps ensure that the equipment is properly maintained and up-to-date, reducing the risk of electrostatic discharge-related accidents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by reading the Introduction page, which provides context and background information about the survey.
2. Proceed to Section A Safety Controls, where you'll be asked to confirm whether each safety control measure is in place.
3. Section B Equipment Status asks you to select the status of each piece of safety equipment.
4. In Section C Equipment Inspection Frequency, enter the date of the last inspection or the recommended inspection frequency.
5. In Section D Equipment Inspection Comments, provide any additional comments about the equipment's condition.
6. Section E Safety Equipment asks you to confirm whether each piece of safety equipment is present.
7. Section F Safety Equipment Location asks you to provide the location of each piece of safety equipment.
8. In Section G ESD Equipment Certifications, select whether each piece of equipment has the necessary certifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Section A Safety Controls
* **Section A Safety Controls (no label)**: Confirm whether each safety control measure is in place.

### Section B Equipment Status
* **Section B Equipment Status (Section B Equipment Status)**: Select the status of each piece of safety equipment:
	+ Active: The equipment is active and in use.
	+ Inactive: The equipment is not active and not in use.

### Section C Equipment Inspection Frequency
* **Section C Equipment Inspection Frequency (Section C Equipment Inspection Frequency)**: Enter the date of the last inspection or the recommended inspection frequency.

### Section D Equipment Inspection Comments
* **Section D Equipment Inspection Comments (Section D Equipment Inspection Comments)**: Provide any additional comments about the equipment's condition.

### Section E Safety Equipment
* **Section E Safety Equipment (Section E Safety Equipment)**: Confirm whether each piece of safety equipment is present:
	+ Yes: The equipment is present.
	+ No: The equipment is not present.

### Section F Safety Equipment Location
* **Section F Safety Equipment Location (Section F Safety Equipment Location)**: Provide the location of each piece of safety equipment.

### Section G ESD Equipment Certifications
* **Section G ESD Equipment Certifications (Section G ESD Equipment Certifications)**: Select whether each piece of equipment has the necessary certifications:
	+ Yes: The equipment has the necessary certifications.
	+ No: The equipment does not have the necessary certifications.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authority to complete this survey.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, contact the relevant authority for guidance.
* Complete the survey accurately and thoroughly to help maintain the safety and efficiency of the facility.
