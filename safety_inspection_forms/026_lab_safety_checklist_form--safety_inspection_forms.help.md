# Lab Safety Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and ensure that all necessary safety measures are in place in a lab setting. This includes emergency exit routes, fire extinguishers, first aid kits, personal protection equipment, electrical equipment, chemical and biological hazards, physical hazards, and other potential hazards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the lab's safety policies and procedures to ensure that all necessary measures are in place.
2. Check the boxes for each section, selecting the options that apply to your lab.
3. Provide any additional comments or notes about the lab's safety situation.
4. Enter the date and time the form was completed.
5. Assign the form to a reviewer or responsible person.
6. The reviewer will review the form and add any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Safety Info (Required: No)
* General Safety Info (`general_safety_info`, none, required: no): Enter any general safety information or notes about the lab's safety situation.

### Emergency Exit Route (Required: Yes)
* Emergency Exit Route (`emergency_exit_route`, required: yes): Identify the emergency exit route in your lab. Select all options that apply:
	+ Option 1: The lab has a clear, well-marked emergency exit route.
	+ Option 2: The emergency exit route is clearly identified with signs and arrows.

### Fire Extinguisher (Required: Yes)
* Fire Extinguisher (`fire_extinguishers`, required: yes): Identify your lab's fire extinguishers:
	+ Option 1: We have at least one fire extinguisher in the lab.
	+ Option 2: We have at least one fire extinguisher on each floor/level of the building.
	+ Option 3: We have fire extinguishers that have been inspected and are in good working condition.

### First Aid Kit (Required: Yes)
* First Aid Kit (`first_aid_kits`, required: yes): Identify your lab's first aid kits:
	+ Option 4: We have at least one first aid kit in the lab.
	+ Option 5: We have at least one first aid kit on each floor/level of the building.
	+ Option 6: Our first aid kits are stocked and easily accessible.

### Spill Containment (Required: Yes)
* Spill Containment (`spill_containment`, required: yes): Identify your lab's spill containment measures:
	+ Option 7: We have a spill containment kit in the lab.
	+ Option 8: We have spill containment mats or other protective measures.

### Personal Protection Equipment (Required: Yes)
* Personal Protection Equipment (`personal_protection_equipment`, required: yes): Identify your lab's personal protection equipment:
	+ Option 9: We have at least one face shield in the lab.
	+ Option 10: We have at least one lab coat in the lab.
	+ Option 11: Our lab coats are in good condition and easily accessible.

### Electrical Equipment (Required: Yes)
* Electrical Equipment (`electrical_equipment`, required: yes): Identify your lab's electrical equipment:
	+ Option 12: We have at least one electrical panel or circuit breaker.
	+ Option 13: Our electrical equipment is inspected and maintained regularly.

### Chemical Hazards (Required: Yes)
* Chemical Hazards (`chemical_hazards`, required: yes): Identify your lab's chemical hazards:
	+ Option 14: We have a chemical hazard list or chemical inventory.
	+ Option 15: Our chemicals are stored and handled properly.
	+ Option 16: Our lab is equipped with a chemical splash shield or spill pan.

### Biological Hazards (Required: Yes)
* Biological Hazards (`biological_hazards`, required: yes): Identify your lab's biological hazards:
	+ Option 17: We have a biological hazard list or biohazard inventory.
	+ Option 18: Our biological samples are handled properly.
	+ Option 19: Our lab has a biohazard disposal procedure in place.

### Physical Hazards (Required: Yes)
* Physical Hazards (`physical_hazards`, required: yes): Identify your lab's physical hazards:
	+ Option 20: We have a physical hazard list or physical hazard inventory.
	+ Option 21: Our physical hazards are marked or addressed.
	+ Option 22: Our lab has a physical hazard mitigation plan in place.

### Other Hazards (Required: Yes)
* Other Hazards (`other_hazards`, required: yes): Identify your lab's other hazards:
	+ Option 23: We have an other hazard list or other hazard inventory.
	+ Option 24: Our other hazards are addressed or mitigated.
	+ Option 25: Our lab has an other hazard reporting procedure in place.

### Comments (Required: No)
* Comments (`comments`, none, required: no): Add any additional comments or notes about the lab's safety situation.

### Date Completed (Required: No)
* Date Completed (`date_completed`, none, required: no): Enter the date this form was completed.

### Time Completed (Required: No)
* Time Completed (`time_completed`, none, required: no): Enter the time this form was completed.

### Assigned To (Required: No)
* Assigned To (`assigned_to`, none, required: no): Enter the name of the person responsible for this form.

### Reviewer (Required: No)
* Reviewer (`reviewer`, none, required: no): Enter the name of the reviewer or person to review this form.

### Reviewer Comments (Required: No)
* Reviewer Comments (`reviewer_comments`, none, required: no): Add any comments or notes from the reviewer.
