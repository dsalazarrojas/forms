# Maneuver Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maneuver Safety Checklist form is designed to aid in ensuring the safety of vehicles and their operators. It helps in identifying potential hazards, assessing vehicle conditions, and verifying the presence of necessary safety measures, equipment, and training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the different sections and questions.
2. For each field, select the most accurate answer based on your knowledge of the vehicle's status and safety features.
3. Provide clear and concise answers to help the form reviewers make informed decisions.
4. Complete all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Status** (`vehicle_status`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the vehicle's current status, such as "Operating normally" or "Under maintenance".
* **Safety Measures** (`safety_measures`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the most relevant safety measures applied to the vehicle, such as "Properly worn personal protective equipment (PPE)" or "Regular vehicle maintenance checks".
* **Hazard Preconditions** (`hazard_preconditions`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all applicable hazard preconditions that may pose a risk to the vehicle or its operator, such as "Wet or icy conditions" or "Heavy traffic".
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, select_one, optional):
	+ Indicate the current condition of the vehicle, such as "Good" or "Needs immediate maintenance".
* **Risk Control** (`risk_control`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the types of risk control measures that are currently applied, such as "Regular risk assessments" or "Emergency response plans".
* **Emergency Procedure** (`emergency_procedure`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the most relevant emergency procedure for the vehicle, such as "Fire extinguisher training" or "First aid kit availability".
* **Vehicle Equipment** (`vehicle_equipment`, select_one, optional):
	+ Indicate the type and condition of the vehicle's equipment, such as "Functioning" or "Needs replacement".
* **Safety Training** (`safety_training`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the type and level of safety training provided to the vehicle's operator, such as "Basic" or "Advanced".
