# Hotel Fire Escape Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hotel Fire Escape Inspection Form is used to assess the safety and readiness of a hotel's fire escape route and emergency procedures. This form helps identify potential risks and hazards, ensuring that the hotel is prepared in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have reviewed the hotel's emergency procedures and are familiar with its fire escape route.
2. Answer the following questions based on your observations:
3. Provide details about the fire escape route, such as its status and conditions.
4. Report on any urgent issues or hazards found during the inspection.
5. Document the status of fire extinguishers, fire alarms, and emergency exit doors.
6. Confirm the presence and functionality of emergency call points and exit points.
7. Record your signature and the date and time of completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info (1)**: Enter a brief description of the hotel's general information, such as location, floor number, etc.
* **Fire Exit Route (2)**: Select "Yes" if the fire exit route is functional and "No" if not.
* **Escape Route Conditions (3)**: Report any conditions that may affect the fire exit route's usability, such as obstructions or damage.
* **Urgent Issues (4)**: List any urgent issues or hazards found during the inspection, such as missing fire extinguishers or malfunctioning alarms.
* **Escape Route Status (5)**: Select "Active" if the fire exit route is functional and "Inactive" if not.
* **Emergency Exit Door Condition (6)**: Select "Yes" if the exit door is functional and "No" if not.
* **Emergency Exit Door Rating (7)**: Rate the emergency exit door's usability, with higher numbers indicating better conditions.
* **Fire Extinguisher (8)**: Select "Yes" if fire extinguishers are present and functional, "No" otherwise.
* **Fire Extinguisher Rating (9)**: Rate the fire extinguisher's usability, with higher numbers indicating better conditions.
* **Fire Alarm (10)**: Select "Yes" if the fire alarm is functional and "No" if not.
* **Emergency Exit Light (11)**: Select "Yes" if the emergency exit light is functional and "No" if not.
* **Fire Extinguisher Location (12)**: Enter the location of the fire extinguisher.
* **Fire Exit Route Location (13)**: Enter the location of the fire exit route.
* **Exit Door (14)**: Enter the location of the exit door.
* **Emergency Call Point (15)**: Select "Yes" if the emergency call point is functional and "No" if not.
* **Emergency Exit Point (16)**: Select "Yes" if the emergency exit point is functional and "No" if not.
* **Signature (17)**: Confirm that you have completed the inspection and that the information is accurate.
* **Date Completed (18)**: Enter the date of completion.
* **Time Completed (19)**: Enter the time of completion.
* **User Signature (20)**: Confirm that you are the user who completed the inspection.
* **Completed By (21)**: Confirm that you have completed the inspection.
* **Date Submitted (22)**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Time Submitted (23)**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Submitted By (24)**: Confirm that you are the submitter of the inspection report.
* **Notes (25)**: Enter any additional comments or recommendations.
