# Terminal Safety Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and report safety incidents that occur in terminal settings, such as power plants, transportation hubs, and other facilities. The form helps to gather accurate and detailed information about the incidents, which can be used to prevent future occurrences, identify root causes, and take corrective actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "incident_type" field to choose the type of safety incident that occurred (e.g., Hazardous Condition, Injury, Near Miss).
2. Describe the incident in the "incident_description" field, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Select the current "incident_status" of the incident (e.g., Open, Closed).
4. If the incident has already been closed, select the "closed_by" field to identify the person who closed the incident.
5. Fill in the "followup_date" and "followup_time" fields if a follow-up action is required.
6. Enter any additional notes or details about the incident in the "followup_note" field.
7. If necessary, add images or attachments related to the incident in the "incident_image" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **terminal_safety_incident_form** (Page 1, text): This field is required and should include a brief description of the safety incident.
- **date** (Page 1, date): Enter the date the incident occurred.
- **time** (Page 1, time): Enter the time the incident occurred.
- **reported_by** (Page 1, text): Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
- **reported_by_phone** (Page 1, text): Enter the phone number of the person who reported the incident (if available).
- **facility** (Page 1, text): Enter the name of the terminal or facility where the incident occurred.
- **location** (Page 1, text): Enter the location within the terminal or facility where the incident occurred.
- **incident_type** (Page 2, select_multiple): Choose the type of safety incident that occurred (e.g., Hazardous Condition, Injury, Near Miss).
- **incident_description** (Page 2, text): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
- **incident_status** (Page 2, select_one): Select the current status of the incident (e.g., Open, Closed).
- **incident_details** (Page 2, text): Enter any additional details about the incident.
- **action_taken** (Page 2, text): Enter any actions taken as a result of the incident.
- **action_followup_status** (Page 2, text): Enter the status of any follow-up actions.
- **followup_date** (Page 2, date): Enter the date of the follow-up action.
- **followup_time** (Page 2, time): Enter the time of the follow-up action.
- **followup_note** (Page 2, text): Enter any notes or comments about the follow-up action.
- **closed_by** (Page 2, text): Enter the name of the person who closed the incident.
- **closed_date** (Page 2, date): Enter the date the incident was closed.
- **closed_time** (Page 2, time): Enter the time the incident was closed.
- **closed_by_phone** (Page 2, text): Enter the phone number of the person who closed the incident (if available).
- **terminal_safety_manager** (Page 3, text): Enter the name of the terminal safety manager.
- **terminal_operations_supervisor** (Page 3, text): Enter the name of the terminal operations supervisor.
- **terminal_safety_coordinator** (Page 3, text): Enter the name of the terminal safety coordinator.
- **terminal_security** (Page 3, text): Enter the name of the terminal security personnel.
- **incident_image** (Page 3, text): Upload any images or attachments related to the incident.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled in for the form to be considered complete.
