# Demolition Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the safety measures and protocols in place for a demolition site. It helps in assessing the risks and hazards associated with the site and ensuring a safe demolition process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start by providing general information about the site.
2.  Select the relevant safety plan and emergency response plan for the site.
3.  List any special equipment that will be used during the demolition process.
4.  Choose the methods of demolition that will be used, such as mechanical or explosive.
5.  Confirm if there are special hazards present on the site.
6.  Provide information about the site location and its details.
7.  Answer questions about hazardous substance handling, hazardous hazards, personal protective equipment, and environmental hazards.
8.  Finally, provide any additional comments or explanations about the site safety.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (1): This field is for entering general information about the site, such as its name, location, and description.
* **Site Details** (2): This field is for providing more specific details about the site, such as its address, size, and other relevant information.
* **Site Location** (3): This field is for specifying the location of the site, including its latitude, longitude, and any other relevant coordinates.
* **Demolition Safety Plan** (4): This field is for selecting the type of safety plan that will be used for the site, such as Mechanical, Explosive, or Other.
* **Hazardous Substance Handling** (5): This field is for indicating if hazardous substances will be handled on the site.
* **Hazardous Hazards** (6): This field is for specifying if there are any hazardous hazards present on the site, such as Fire, Explosive, Chemical, or Electrical.
* **Personal Protective Equipment** (7): This field is for listing the personal protective equipment that will be used by workers on the site.
* **Special Equipment** (8): This field is for specifying any special equipment that will be used on the site.
* **Emergency Response Plan** (9): This field is for providing information about the emergency response plan in place for the site.
* **Demolition Methods** (10): This field is for selecting the methods of demolition that will be used, such as Mechanical, Explosive, or Other.
* **Special Hazards** (11): This field is for indicating if there are any special hazards present on the site.
* **Environmental Hazards** (12): This field is for providing information about any environmental hazards present on the site.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all fields truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any information, please seek guidance from your supervisor or relevant authorities.
* This form is for assessment purposes only and should not be used for any other purpose.
