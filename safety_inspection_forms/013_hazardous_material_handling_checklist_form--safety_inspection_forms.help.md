# Hazardous Material Handling Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hazardous Material Handling Checklist Form is designed to ensure that businesses that handle hazardous materials adhere to safety standards and regulations. This form is used to gather information about a business's hazardous material handling practices, training, and compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your business name and address in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide the contact information for the person responsible for handling hazardous materials at your business.
3. List the types of hazardous materials used at your business.
4. Describe the safety procedures in place for handling these materials.
5. Outline your training programs for employees handling hazardous materials.
6. Indicate whether employees have been informed about the risks associated with handling hazardous materials.
7. Provide the frequency and duration of training for employees.
8. List the topics covered in your training programs.
9. Indicate whether your training programs have been evaluated.
10. Describe the frequency with which employees conduct their activities.
11. Indicate whether training has been completed.
12. Specify the frequency of completed training.
13. Indicate whether regulatory training has been completed.
14. List the frequencies of regulatory training.
15. List the topics covered in regulatory training programs.
16. Indicate whether employees are aware of these topics.
17. Describe the frequency with which employees conduct regulatory activities.
18. Specify the form submitter's name and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your business as it is registered.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter the physical address of your business.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for handling hazardous materials at your business.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `text`, required): Describe the type of business that handles hazardous materials.
* **Hazardous Materials Used** (`hazardous_materials_used`, `text`, required): List the types of hazardous materials used at your business.
* **Safety Procedures In Place** (`safety_procedures_in_place`, `text`, required): Describe the safety procedures in place for handling hazardous materials.
* **Training Programs** (`training_programs`, `text`, required): Outline the training programs for employees handling hazardous materials.
* **Employee Informed** (`employee_informed`, `text`, required): Indicate whether employees have been informed about the risks associated with handling hazardous materials.
* **Training Frequency** (`training_frequency`, `text`, required): Specify the frequency of training for employees.
* **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, `text`, required): Specify the duration of training for employees.
* **Training Topics** (`training_topics`, `text`, required): List the topics covered in your training programs.
* **Training Evaluated** (`training_evaluated`, `text`, required): Indicate whether your training programs have been evaluated.
* **Employee Conducted** (`employee_conducted`, `text`, required): Describe the frequency with which employees conduct their activities.
* **Employee Conducted Frequency** (`employee_conducted_frequency`, `text`, required): Specify the frequency with which employees conduct their activities.
* **Training Completed** (`training_completed`, `text`, required): Indicate whether training has been completed.
* **Training Completed Frequency** (`training_completed_frequency`, `text`, required): Specify the frequency of completed training.
* **Regulatory Training Completed** (`regulatory_training_completed`, `text`, required): Indicate whether regulatory training has been completed.
* **Regulatory Training Frequencies** (`regulatory_training_frequencies`, `text`, required): List the frequencies of regulatory training.
* **Regulatory Training Topics** (`regulatory_training_topics`, `text`, required): List the topics covered in regulatory training programs.
* **Training Topics Awareness** (`training_topics_awareness`, `text`, required): Indicate whether employees are aware of these topics.
* **Employee Conducted Regulatory** (`employee_conducted_regulatory`, `text`, required): Describe the frequency with which employees conduct regulatory activities.
* **Employee Conducted Regulatory Frequency** (`employee_conducted_regulatory_frequency`, `text`, required): Specify the frequency with which employees conduct regulatory activities.
* **Form Submitter** (`form_submitted`, `text`, required): Specify the name and any other relevant details of the form submitter.

Please note that some fields may not be applicable to your business or may require further explanation. If you have any questions, please contact your supervisor or the relevant regulatory authority.

**Tips**

* Ensure that all fields are accurately and thoroughly completed.
* Double-check your entries to ensure they are correct and up-to-date.
* If you need help or clarification on any field, please do not hesitate to ask.
