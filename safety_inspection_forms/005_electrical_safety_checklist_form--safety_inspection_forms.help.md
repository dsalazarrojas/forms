# Electrical Safety Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Electrical Safety Checklist Form is designed to evaluate the safety and certification status of electrical systems in a facility or workplace. This form helps ensure that electrical systems are properly maintained, certified, and meet safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
3. Use the options and explanations provided as guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Electrical System Assessment** (electrical_system_assessment, select_one, Required): Indicates whether the electrical system is in good condition or requires assessment.
  * Answer: True (yes) or False (no)
* **Circuit Breaker Inspection** (circuit_breaker_inspection, select_one, Required): Indicates whether the circuit breaker inspection has been performed.
  * Answer: True (yes) or False (no)
* **Electrical System Documentation** (electrical_system_documentation, note, Required): Provide any necessary documentation related to the electrical system.
  * Please include any relevant documentation or information.
* **Arc Flash Protection Device Inspection** (arc_flash_protection_device_inspection, select_one, Required): Indicates whether the arc flash protection device inspection has been performed.
  * Answer: True (yes) or False (no)
* **Circuit Cables and Wiring Inspection** (circuit_cables_and_wiring_inspection, select_one, Required): Indicates whether the circuit cables and wiring inspection has been performed.
  * Answer: True (yes) or False (no)
* **Electrical Systems Testing** (electrical_systems_testing, select_one, Required): Indicates whether electrical system testing has been performed.
  * Answer: True (yes) or False (no)
* **Electrical Systems Labeling** (electrical_systems_labeling, select_one, Required): Indicates whether electrical systems are properly labeled.
  * Answer: True (yes) or False (no)
* **Electrical Outlet Inspection** (electrical_outlet_inspection, select_one, Required): Indicates whether electrical outlet inspection has been performed.
  * Answer: True (yes) or False (no)
* **Emergency Exit Lights Inspection** (emergency_exit_lights_inspection, select_one, Required): Indicates whether emergency exit lights inspection has been performed.
  * Answer: True (yes) or False (no)
* **Electrical Systems Certification** (electrical_systems_certification, select_one, Required): Indicates whether electrical systems are certified.
  * Answer: True (yes) or False (no)
* **Electrical Systems Certification Date** (electrical_systems_certification_date, date, Required): Provide the date of the last electrical systems certification.
  * Enter the date in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Electrical Systems Certification Expiration Date** (electrical_systems_certification_expiration_date, date, Required): Provide the expiration date of the electrical systems certification.
  * Enter the date in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Electrical Systems Certification Status** (electrical_systems_certification_status, select_one, Required): Indicates the status of the electrical systems certification.
  * Answer: Active or Inactive
* **Electrical Systems Certification Number** (electrical_systems_certification_number, number, Required): Provide the certification number for the electrical systems.
  * Enter the number as a whole number.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (emergency_contact_info, text, Required): Provide the emergency contact information.
  * Enter first name, last name, and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (emergency_contact_person, select_one, Required): Provide the name of the emergency contact person.
  * Answer: Joe or Jane
* **Electrical Systems Certification Issue Date** (electrical_systems_certification_issue_date, date, Required): Provide the date of the last electrical systems certification issue.
  * Enter the date in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Electrical Systems Certification Issue Description** (electrical_systems_certification_issue_description, text, Required): Provide a description of any issues with the electrical systems certification.
  * Enter a brief description.
* **Electrical Systems Certification Resolution Date** (electrical_systems_certification_resolution_date, date, Required): Provide the date of the last electrical systems certification resolution.
  * Enter the date in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Electrical Systems Certification Resolution Status** (electrical_systems_certification_resolution_status, select_one, Required): Indicates the status of the electrical systems certification resolution.
  * Answer: Active or Inactive
* **Electrical Systems Certification Re-Test Date** (electrical_systems_certification_retest_date, date, Optional): Provide the date of the last electrical systems certification re-test.
  * Enter the date in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Electrical Systems Certification Re-Test Status** (electrical_systems_certification_retest_status, select_one, Optional): Indicates the status of the electrical systems certification re-test.
  * Answer: Active or Inactive
* **Electrical Systems Certification Re-Test Number** (electrical_systems_certification_retest_number, number, Optional): Provide the re-test number for the electrical systems certification.
  * Enter the number as a whole number.
* **Electrical Systems Certification Comments** (electrical_systems_certification_comments, text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or observations about the electrical systems certification.
  * Enter any relevant comments or observations.

## Tips

* Use this form as a reference guide to ensure that electrical systems are properly maintained, certified, and meet safety standards.
* Review and update the form regularly to reflect any changes in the electrical systems or workplace.
* If you are unsure about any field or question, please seek guidance from your supervisor or relevant authority.
