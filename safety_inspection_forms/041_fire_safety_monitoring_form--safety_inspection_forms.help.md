# Fire Safety Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Safety Monitoring Form is a tool used to monitor and record fire safety conditions in a location.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and answer each question based on your current situation.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, taking into account your specific fire safety conditions.
3. Ensure you're honest and accurate in your responses, as this information will be used to take proactive measures to mitigate potential fires.
4. Review your completed form to ensure it's complete and accurate before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Safety Inspection Form** (Field 1): This is the title of the form and is used to identify the form's purpose. Please do not modify this field.
* **Fire Hazards Present** (Field 2): Indicate if there are any fire hazards present in the area. Choose 'Yes' if there are hazards, 'No' otherwise.
* **Fire Hazards Risk Level** (Field 3): Select the risk level of the fire hazards present. Choose from 'Low', 'Medium', 'High' based on the severity of the hazards.
* **Safety Equipment in Good Working Condition** (Field 4): Select which safety equipment is in good working condition. Choose from 'All', 'Most', 'Some', or 'None' based on your assessment.
* **Safety Equipment Testing** (Field 5): This field is not applicable for this form, as it's meant for a different purpose. Please skip this field.
* **Fire Extinguishers** (Field 6): Indicate if fire extinguishers are present and fully functional. Choose from 'Full', 'Partial', or 'None' based on your assessment.
* **Fire Drills** (Field 7): Select if fire drills have been conducted in the past year. Choose from 'Yes' or 'No' based on your knowledge.
* **Fire Evacuation Plan** (Field 8): Indicate if a fire evacuation plan is in place. Choose from 'Yes' or 'No' based on your assessment.
* **Fire Safety Manual** (Field 9): Select if a fire safety manual is available and up-to-date. Choose from 'Yes' or 'No' based on your knowledge.
* **Fire Safety Training** (Field 10): Indicate if fire safety training has been conducted for personnel. Choose from 'Yes' or 'No' based on your assessment.
* **Fire Safety Equipment** (Field 11): Select the safety equipment available in the area. Choose from 'Sprinkler System', 'Fire Alarm', 'Fire Extinguisher', or 'Fire Alarm System' based on your knowledge.
* **Fire Safety Risk** (Field 12): Select the risk level of fires in the area. Choose from 'Low', 'Medium', 'High' based on your assessment.
* **Fire Safety Inspection Interval** (Field 13): Select the frequency of fire safety inspections conducted in the area. Choose from 'Daily', 'Weekly', 'Monthly', 'Quarterly', 'Every 6 Months', or 'Yearly' based on your knowledge.

## Tips
1. Ensure you're thorough and accurate in your responses.
2. If you're unsure about any of the questions, please consult with your fire safety officer or relevant authorities.
3. Review your completed form carefully before submitting it.
4. Update your response if there are any changes in the fire safety conditions since the last submission.
