# Power Line Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Power Line Safety Inspection Form is a document used to record information about the safety of power lines. It is essential for ensuring that power lines are in good working order and safe for use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about the power line, including any relevant details that may affect its safety.
2. Enter information about the equipment used on the power line, such as its type and condition.
3. Describe the condition of the power line, including any potential hazards or issues that have been identified.
4. Record any compliance information that is relevant to the power line, such as certifications or permits.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments about the power line.
6. Indicate whether there are any hazards present on the power line.
7. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm that it has been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the power line, including its purpose and any relevant details that may affect its safety.
* **Equipment Information** (`equipment_info`, text, optional): Describe the type and condition of the equipment used on the power line.
* **Condition Info** (`condition_info`, text, optional): Describe the condition of the power line, including any potential hazards or issues that have been identified.
* **Compliance Info** (`compliance_info`, text, optional): Record any compliance information that is relevant to the power line, such as certifications or permits.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the power line.
* **Hazards** (`hazards`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply regarding the presence of hazards on the power line.
	+ 'True': Indicate that a hazard is present
	+ 'False': Indicate that a hazard is not present
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm that it has been completed.
