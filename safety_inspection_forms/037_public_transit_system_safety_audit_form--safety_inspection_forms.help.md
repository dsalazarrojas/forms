# Public Transit System Safety Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Transit System Safety Audit Form is used to conduct safety audits of stations, stops, and vehicles in public transit systems. This form helps ensure that passengers and staff are safe while using or working on these facilities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the **Station Name**, **Stop Name**, and **Vehicle Number** to identify the specific station, stop, or vehicle being audited.
2. Select the **Audit Type** (e.g., routine, special, or emergency).
3. List any **Safety Concerns** that were observed during the audit.
4. Describe the **Corrective Action** taken or planned to address the safety concerns.
5. Report the **Station Inspection Status** (e.g., passed, failed, or pending).
6. Add any additional **Comments** about the audit.
7. Enter the **Completed By** (e.g., auditor's name or ID).
8. Date and time of completion.
9. If necessary, specify the **Assigned Tool** used for the audit.
10. Enter the **Form ID** and **Agency ID** (if applicable).
11. Record the **Station Address**, **Stop Address**, and **Vehicle Address** (if applicable).
12. Note any **Safety Inspections** performed.
13. Document the **Next Action** planned.
14. Record the **Follow-up Date**, **Follow-up Time**, and any **Follow-up Comments** (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Station Name** (`station_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the station being audited.
  - This helps identify the station being inspected.
- **Stop Name** (`stop_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the stop being audited.
  - This helps identify the stop being inspected.
- **Vehicle Number** (`vehicle_number`, text, optional): Enter the number of the vehicle being audited.
  - This helps identify the vehicle being inspected.
- **Station Type** (`station_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of station being audited (e.g., bus, train, or tram).
  - This helps categorize the station for future reference.
- **Audit Type** (`audit_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of audit performed (e.g., routine, special, or emergency).
  - This helps classify the audit for future reference.
- **Safety Concerns** (`safety_concerns`, select_multiple, optional): List any safety concerns observed during the audit.
  - This helps identify potential hazards and areas for improvement.
- **Corrective Action** (`corrective_action`, select_multiple, optional): Describe the actions taken or planned to address the safety concerns.
  - This helps track progress and ensure that actions are taken to address safety issues.
- **Station Inspection Status** (`station_inspection_status`, select_one, required): Report the status of the station inspection (e.g., passed, failed, or pending).
  - This helps track the inspection status and ensure that the station meets safety standards.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the audit.
  - This helps provide additional context and information.
- **Completed By** (`completed_by`, text, optional): Enter the name or ID of the person or team completing the audit.
  - This helps track who completed the audit and ensures accountability.
- **Completed Date** and **Completed Time** (`completed_date` and `completed_time`, date and time, optional): Enter the date and time of completion.
  - This helps track when the audit was completed.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If necessary, specify the tool used for the audit.
  - This helps track the tool used and ensures consistency.
- **Form ID** and **Agency ID** (`form_id` and `agency_id`, text, optional): Enter the form ID and agency ID (if applicable).
  - This helps track the form and agency information.
- **Station Address**, **Stop Address**, and **Vehicle Address** (`station_address`, `stop_address`, and `vehicle_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the station, stop, or vehicle being audited.
  - This helps identify the location of the station, stop, or vehicle.
- **Safety Inspections** (`safety_inspections`, text, optional): Record any safety inspections performed.
  - This helps track safety inspections and ensure that safety standards are met.
- **Next Action** (`next_action`, text, optional): Document the next action planned.
  - This helps track progress and ensure that follow-up actions are taken.
- **Follow-up Date**, **Follow-up Time**, and **Follow-up Comments** (`follow_up_date`, `follow_up_time`, and `follow_up_comments`, date, time, and text, optional): Record the follow-up date, time, and comments (if applicable).
  - This helps track follow-up actions and ensure that issues are resolved.
- **Form Last Updated** (`form_last_updated`, text, optional): Enter the last updated date and time of the form.
  - This helps track form updates and changes.
