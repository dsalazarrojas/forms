# workplace_safety_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on workplace safety assessments, including location, supervisor's name, date, time, safety protocols, hazards, and recommendations for improvement. It's intended to be used by authorized personnel for regular workplace safety checks or assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "What is your name?" field (page 1).
2. Enter the location of the workplace (page 2).
3. Provide the supervisor's name (page 3).
4. Enter the date and time of the assessment (page 4).
5. Describe the safety protocols in place (page 5).
6. List any hazards identified during the assessment (page 6).
7. Document any observations or notes (page 7).
8. Provide recommendations for improvement (page 8).
9. List any action items or tasks (page 9).
10. Select relevant safety training areas (page 10).
11. Rate the risk level (page 11).
12. Select the risk level (page 12).
13. Describe the risk control method (page 13).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (page 1): Enter your name so we can associate the assessment with your name.
* **Workplace location** (page 2): Enter the exact location where the assessment is taking place.
* **Supervisor name** (page 3): Enter the name of your direct supervisor or the person in charge of the workplace area.
* **Date** (page 4): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Time** (page 4): Enter the time of the assessment.
* **Safety protocols** (page 5): Describe the safety protocols, procedures, and guidelines in place at the workplace.
* **Hazards** (page 6): List any hazards or potential risks identified during the assessment.
* **Observations** (page 7): Document any observations or notes from the assessment.
* **Recommendations** (page 8): Provide any recommendations for improvement or changes to be made.
* **Action items** (page 9): List any action items or tasks that need to be addressed.
* **Safety training** (page 10): Select relevant safety training areas that the workplace needs.
* **Risk rating** (page 11): Rate the level of risk (Low, Medium, High).
* **Risk level** (page 12): Select the risk level (Low, Medium, High).
* **Risk control** (page 13): Describe the method used to control or mitigate the risk.
* **Risk control method** (page 13): Describe the method used to control or mitigate the risk.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form only if you're authorized to do so and if the assessment is not already documented or in progress.
* Make sure to be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance.
