# Freight Terminal Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to assess the safety conditions of a freight terminal. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the terminal meets or exceeds the required safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and authorizations to complete this form.
2. Review the terminal's current safety records and previous inspection reports to avoid duplication of effort.
3. Be thorough and accurate when answering each question, using the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: This is the first field, required to identify the terminal and its location. Please enter the name of the terminal exactly as it appears on its official documents and signage.
* **Phone**: Enter the terminal's contact phone number for follow-up or reference purposes.
* **Email**: Enter the terminal's email address for communication and notification.
* **General Safety**: Select one of the three options:
	+ **Very Safe**: The terminal meets all safety standards and requirements.
	+ **Safe**: The terminal meets most safety standards, but with some minor issues or deficiencies.
	+ **Not Safe**: The terminal has significant safety issues or non-compliance with standards.
* **Specific Safety**: Select all applicable options from the list to report specific safety issues or concerns. For example, if you observe uneven or loose flooring, select both "Option 1" and "Option 2".
* **Equipment Condition**: Select the condition of the terminal's equipment:
	+ **Good**: The equipment is functioning correctly and in good condition.
	+ **Fair**: The equipment is functional but has minor issues or wear and tear.
	+ **Poor**: The equipment is non-functional or in poor condition.
* **Facility Condition**: Select the condition of the terminal's facility:
	+ **Good**: The facility is well-maintained and in good condition.
	+ **Fair**: The facility has some minor issues or wear and tear.
	+ **Poor**: The facility is in poor condition or requires significant maintenance.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the terminal's safety conditions.
* **Date Completed**: Enter the date the inspection was completed, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

Note to the reader: This form is a guide, and your specific organization's policies and procedures should be followed for completion.
