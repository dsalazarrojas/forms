# Water Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Safety Checklist is a form used to report on the status of water safety in a facility. It is used to record any issues with water quality or safety equipment, and to schedule follow-up inspections or actions to address these issues.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the facility's name and address.
3. Select the date the form is being completed.
4. Check the boxes for any water quality issues identified, such as Algae or slime growth in pipes, Coliforms or E. coli, or Dissolved oxygen levels low.
5. Select the safety equipment that is currently available and in use at the facility, such as Fire extinguishers or First aid kits.
6. Record the number of trained personnel who are available to address any water safety issues.
7. Enter the maximum number of people allowed in the facility at any one time, if applicable.
8. List any emergency procedures or contact numbers that are in place at the facility.
9. Enter the date of the next follow-up inspection or action.
10. Enter the name and title of the person completing the form.
11. Enter the name and title of the person to whom the form is being submitted.
12. Enter the location of the facility.
13. Enter any comments or notes about the status of water safety in the facility.
14. If applicable, attach photos or other supporting documentation to the form.
15. Enter any other issues or concerns that need to be addressed.
16. Enter the maximum water temperature allowed in the facility.
17. Select any other equipment available at the facility, such as Water testing equipment.
18. Select any other emergency procedures in place at the facility.
19. Enter any follow-up notes or comments.
20. Enter the time the form was submitted.
21. Select the follow-up status, such as Completed, Not Required, or Scheduled.
22. Enter the contact person and their title for follow-up actions.
23. Enter any additional notes or comments.
24. Enter the date and time of the next follow-up action, if applicable.
25. Enter the time of the next follow-up action, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the facility.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, required): Enter the date the form is being completed.
* **Water Quality Issues** (`water_quality_issues`, select_multiple, required): Check the boxes for any water quality issues identified, such as Algae or slime growth in pipes, Coliforms or E. coli, or Dissolved oxygen levels low.
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, select_one, required): Select the safety equipment that is currently available and in use at the facility, such as Fire extinguishers or First aid kits.
* **Trained Personnel** (`trained_personnel`, number, required): Record the number of trained personnel who are available to address any water safety issues.
* **Facility Capacity** (`facility_capacity`, number, optional): Enter the maximum number of people allowed in the facility at any one time, if applicable.
* **Emergency Procedures** (`emergency_procedure`, note, optional): List any emergency procedures or contact numbers that are in place at the facility.
* **Next Follow-Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the next follow-up inspection or action.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter the name and title of the person completing the form.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, text, required): Enter the name and title of the person to whom the form is being submitted.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of the facility.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or notes about the status of water safety in the facility.
* **Photos** (`photos`, note, optional): If applicable, attach photos or other supporting documentation to the form.
* **Other Issues** (`other_issues`, note, optional): Enter any other issues or concerns that need to be addressed.
* **Water Temperature** (`water_temperature`, number, optional): Enter the maximum water temperature allowed in the facility.
* **Other Equipment** (`other_equipment`, select_multiple, optional): Select any other equipment available at the facility, such as Water testing equipment.
* **Other Emergency Procedure** (`other_emergency_procedure`, select_multiple, optional): Select any other emergency procedures in place at the facility.
* **Follow-Up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, note, optional): Enter any follow-up notes or comments.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Follow-Up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_one, required): Select the follow-up status, such as Completed, Not Required, or Scheduled.
* **Follow-Up By** (`follow_up_by`, text, optional): Enter the contact person and their title for follow-up actions.
* **Follow-Up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Follow-Up Date 2** (`follow_up_date_2`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the next follow-up action, if applicable.
* **Follow-Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the next follow-up action, if applicable.
