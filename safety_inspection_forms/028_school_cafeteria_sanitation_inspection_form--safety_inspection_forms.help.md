# School Cafeteria Sanitation Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Cafeteria Sanitation Inspection Form is designed to assess the cleanliness and safety of a school cafeteria.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct option for each section to indicate whether the condition is met or not.
2. Use the "Notes" section to provide additional comments or observations.
3. Submit the completed form to the school administration or health inspector for evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Page: School Cafeteria Sanitation Inspection Form
* **School Cafeteria Sanitation Inspection Form** (`title`, select_one): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the form is complete and accurate.
* **Location** (`location`, select_one): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the inspection is conducted in the correct location.
* **Date/Time** (`date_time`, date): Enter the date and time of the inspection.
* **School ID** (`school_id`, number): Enter the school ID number (not applicable for this form).

### Section 1 - Equipment
* **Section 1 - Equipment** (`section_1`, select_multiple): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each equipment item to indicate its cleanliness and functionality.
*   - Dish Room
*   - Water Temperature Control
*   - Sanitation Sinks
*   - Dishwashing Machine
*   - Trash Receptacles

### Section 2 - Utensils

## Tips
* Please ensure that you answer each question honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any section, please take a moment to review the section's description and options.
