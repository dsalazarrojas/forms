# Fire Safety Compliance Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information on fire safety compliance within your facility. Please provide accurate and honest responses to help ensure the safety of our workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all applicable options for the "Fire Equipment Condition" field. This field is not required.
2. Choose your facility's drill frequency from the options provided.
3. Enter your contact information in the "Emergency Contact Information" field. This field is required.
4. Enter the frequency of your fire safety equipment inspections in the "Fire Safety Equipment Inspection Frequency" field. This field is required.
5. Enter the dates for the following inspections:
	* Fire Extinguisher Inspection Date
	* Fire Alarm Inspection Date
	* Fire Sprinkler Inspection Date (optional)
	* Fire Alarm Panel Inspection Date (optional)
	* Fire Pump Inspection Date (optional)
6. Describe the overall safety practices of your facility in the "Overall Safety Practices" field. This field is not required.
7. Enter the frequency of your safety training in the "Safety Training Frequency" field. This field is required.
8. Check the box indicating whether you have attended fire safety training in the "Fire Safety Training Attendance" field. This field is required.
9. Describe your facility's fire safety plan and procedures in the "Fire Safety Plan" and "Fire Safety Procedures" fields. These fields are required.
10. Answer the following questions:
	* Have you tested fire safety equipment? (Fire Safety Equipment Testing)
	* How often do you test fire safety equipment? (Fire Safety Equipment Testing Frequency)
	* What are the results of your fire safety equipment testing? (Fire Safety Equipment Testing Results)
	* Are you prepared for emergency situations? (Emergency Situation)
	* How would you respond to an emergency? (Emergency Response)
	* Do you report incidents? (Incident Reporting)
	* How often do you report incidents? (Incident Reporting Frequency)
	* Is the evacuation route marked? (Fire Evacuation Route)
	* Where is the fire alarm located? (Fire Alarm Location)
	* Is there an alternate fire alarm location? (Alternate Fire Alarm Location)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* Fire Equipment Condition: Select all applicable options for the current condition of your fire safety equipment.
* Drill Frequency: Choose the frequency at which your facility conducts regular drills.
* Emergency Contact Information: Enter your contact information in case of an emergency.
* Fire Safety Equipment Inspection Frequency: Enter the frequency of your fire safety equipment inspections.
* Fire Extinguisher Inspection Date: Enter the date of your last fire extinguisher inspection.
* Fire Alarm Inspection Date: Enter the date of your last fire alarm inspection.
* Fire Sprinkler Inspection Date: Enter the date of your last fire sprinkler inspection (if applicable).
* Fire Alarm Panel Inspection Date: Enter the date of your last fire alarm panel inspection (if applicable).
* Fire Pump Inspection Date: Enter the date of your last fire pump inspection (if applicable).
* Overall Safety Practices: Describe the overall safety practices of your facility.
* Safety Training Frequency: Enter the frequency of your safety training.
* Fire Safety Training Attendance: Check the box indicating whether you have attended fire safety training.
* Fire Safety Plan: Describe your facility's fire safety plan.
* Fire Safety Procedures: Describe your facility's fire safety procedures.
* Fire Safety Equipment Testing: Answer whether you have tested fire safety equipment.
* Fire Safety Equipment Testing Frequency: Answer how often you test fire safety equipment.
* Fire Safety Equipment Testing Results: Describe the results of your fire safety equipment testing.
* Emergency Situation: Answer whether you are prepared for emergency situations.
* Emergency Response: Describe how you would respond to an emergency.
* Incident Reporting: Answer whether you report incidents.
* Incident Reporting Frequency: Answer how often you report incidents.
* Fire Evacuation Route: Check if the evacuation route is marked.
* Fire Alarm Location: Describe the location of your fire alarm.
* Alternate Fire Alarm Location: Describe the location of your alternate fire alarm.
