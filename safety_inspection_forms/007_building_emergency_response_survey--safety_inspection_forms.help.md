This help guide will explain the purpose and instructions for completing the "Building Emergency Response Survey" form, which is used to gather information from building staff about their awareness and preparedness in emergency situations.

The form's purpose is to assess the level of emergency preparedness and safety awareness among building staff. It aims to identify areas where staff may need additional training or support to ensure a smooth and effective response to emergencies.

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

## Purpose
The Building Emergency Response Survey is designed to gauge the level of emergency preparedness and safety awareness among building staff. This survey helps identify areas where staff may need additional training or support to ensure a safe and effective response to emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the date you are completing this form (page 1).
2. Indicate the building or area where you work (page 2).
3. If applicable, select your department or work location (page 3).
4. Answer questions about your awareness and familiarity with emergency procedures, including evacuation routes, assembly points, and drill effectiveness (pages 4-7).
5. Indicate if you have participated in a previous evacuation drill (page 8).
6. Provide feedback on the effectiveness of previous drills (page 8).
7. Answer questions about your knowledge of emergency equipment, such as fire extinguishers, first aid kits, and AEDs (page 9).
8. If applicable, indicate if you need mobility assistance in an emergency (page 10).
9. If applicable, select your special needs or medical conditions that staff should be aware of (page 11).
10. Express your confidence level in the building's safety (page 12).
11. Provide suggestions for improving emergency preparedness (page 13).
12. Share any safety concerns or issues you have noticed (page 14).
13. Enter your name and email address (pages 15-16), which are optional fields for follow-up communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date** (page 1): Select the date you are completing this form to ensure accurate records.
* **Building or Floor** (page 2): Identify the specific area or building where you work.
* **Your Department** (page 3): If applicable, specify your department or work location to help with targeted support.
* **Emergency Evacuation Awareness** (page 4): Rate your awareness of emergency evacuation routes.
	+ Very Aware: You have a complete understanding of evacuation routes.
	+ Somewhat Aware: You have some knowledge of evacuation routes, but may need a refresher.
	+ Not Very Aware: You are not familiar with evacuation routes.
	+ Unaware: You do not know the evacuation routes.
* **Familiarity With Emergency Procedures** (page 5): Rate your familiarity with emergency procedures.
	+ Very Familiar: You are thoroughly familiar with emergency procedures.
	+ Somewhat Familiar: You have some knowledge of emergency procedures, but may need a refresher.
	+ Not Very Familiar: You are not familiar with emergency procedures.
	+ Unfamiliar: You do not know emergency procedures.
* **Assembly Point Awareness** (page 6): Indicate if you know the assembly point after evacuation.
	+ Yes, Clearly: You know the assembly point.
	+ Somewhat: You have some idea of the assembly point.
	+ False: You do not know the assembly point.
	+ Unsure: You are unsure about the assembly point.
* **Participated in Evacuation Drill** (page 7): Check if you have participated in a previous evacuation drill.
* **Drill Effectiveness** (page 8): Rate the effectiveness of previous drills.
	+ Very Effective: Drills were well-organized and effective.
	+ Effective: Drills were somewhat effective.
	+ Needs Improvement: Drills were not effective or required improvement.
	+ Not Effective: Drills were not effective at all.
* **Emergency Equipment Awareness** (page 9): Indicate your knowledge of emergency equipment, such as fire extinguishers and first aid kits.
	+ Yes, All: You are familiar with all emergency equipment.
	+ Yes, Most: You are familiar with most emergency equipment.
	+ Some: You are familiar with some emergency equipment.
	+ None: You do not know the location of emergency equipment.
* **Mobility Assistance** (page 10): Indicate if you need mobility assistance in an emergency.
* **Hearing or Visually Impaired** (page 11): Select if you have hearing or visual impairments that require special consideration.
* **Medical Conditions** (page 12): Indicate if you have medical conditions that staff should be aware of in emergency situations.
* **Confidence Level** (page 13): Rate your confidence level in the building's safety.
	+ Very Confident: You are very confident in the building's safety.
	+ Confident: You are somewhat confident in the building's safety.
	+ Somewhat Confident: You are not very confident in the building's safety.
	+ Not Confident: You are not confident in the building's safety.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (page 14): Provide any suggestions for improving emergency preparedness.
* **Safety Concerns or Issues** (page 15): Share any safety concerns or issues you have noticed.
* **Your Name** (page 16): Enter your name, which is optional.
* **Email Address** (page 17): Enter your email address, which is optional for follow-up communication.

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the Building Emergency Response Survey effectively and provide valuable information to help improve building safety and emergency preparedness.
