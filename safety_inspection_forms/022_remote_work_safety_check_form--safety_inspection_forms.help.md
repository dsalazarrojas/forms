# Remote Work Safety Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees ensure that their remote workspaces are safe and risk-free.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field and its corresponding options carefully.
2. Fill out each field according to your knowledge of your workspace and safety procedures.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workspace Location** (`workspace_location`, text, optional): Enter the name or address of your remote workspace.
* **Workspace Address** (`workspace_address`, text, optional): Enter the full address of your remote workspace.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your supervisor or manager.
* **Safety Risk Assessment** (`safety_risk_assessment`, select_one, optional): Choose the risk level of your workspace (Risk 1, Risk 2, Risk 3, or Risk 4).
* **Safety Risk Assessment - Why** (`safety_risk_assessment_why`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the reasons why your workspace is at that risk level.
* **Hazards** (`hazards`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the hazards present in your workspace (Hazard 1, Hazard 2, Hazard 3).
* **Safety Control Measures** (`safety_control_measures`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the safety measures being taken to mitigate those hazards (Control 1, Control 2, Control 3).
* **Workplace** (`workplace`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your remote workspace.
* **Safety Training** (`safety_training`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of safety training received (Option 1, Option 2).
* **Remote Work Environment** (`remote_work_environment`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the remote work environments you are in (Option 1, Option 2).
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the emergency contacts available (Option 1, Option 2).
* **Equipment** (`equipment`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the safety equipment available (Option 1, Option 2).
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the safety equipment available (Option 1, Option 2).
* **Emergency Exit Paths** (`emergency_exit_paths`, text, optional): Describe your emergency exit paths.
* **Safety Equipment Maintenance** (`safety_equipment_maintenance`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the safety equipment maintenance routines (Option 1, Option 2).
* **Safety Equipment Inspection** (`safety_equipment_inspection`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the safety equipment inspections (Option 1, Option 2).
* **Emergency Response Plan** (`emergency_response_plan`, text, optional): Describe your emergency response plan.
* **Fire Drill Frequency** (`fire_drill_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose the frequency of fire drills (Option 1, Option 2).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact information.
* **Supervisor Approval** (`supervisor_approval`, select_one, optional): Choose the level of supervisor approval (Option 1, Option 2).
* **Employee Acknowledgement** (`employee_acknowledgement`, select_one, optional): Choose your employee acknowledgement (Option 1, Option 2).
* **Supervisor Acknowledgement** (`supervisor_acknowledgement`, select_one, optional): Choose your supervisor's acknowledgement (Option 1, Option 2).
* **Safety Check Frequency** (`safety_check_frequency`, number, optional): Enter the frequency of safety checks.
* **Safety Check Date** (`safety_check_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last safety check.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your remote workspace.

## Tips
* Please be thorough and accurate in your responses.
* If unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or HR representative.
* Review and submit the form after filling out all fields.
* Make sure to save your responses for future reference.
