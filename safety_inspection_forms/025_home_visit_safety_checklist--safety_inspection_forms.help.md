# Home Visit Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides an overview of the Home Visit Safety Checklist form, which is used to gather information about safety assessments and visits to clients' homes. The form includes sections for collecting client data, safety assessments, follow-up actions, reviewer notes, and next review dates.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of visit from the "Visit Type" dropdown menu:
    *   Home Care: A visit for general home care purposes.
    *   Social Services: A visit for social services purposes.
    *   Property Team: A visit for property team purposes.

2.  Enter the date and time of the visit using the "Date Visited" and "Time Visited" fields.

3.  Enter any notes from the visit in the "Notes" field.

4.  Select and list any safety assessments from the "Safety Assessments" dropdown menu:
    *   Smoke and fire hazards: Assessments related to fire and smoke safety.
    *   Slip and fall hazards: Assessments related to slip and fall safety.
    *   Sharp objects or hazards: Assessments related to sharp objects or hazards.
    *   Electrical or gas hazards: Assessments related to electrical or gas safety.
    *   Other (please specify): Select this option if none of the above apply.

5.  Select and list any risk factors from the "Risk Factors" dropdown menu:
    *   History of falls: Assessments related to falls.
    *   History of fires: Assessments related to fires.
    *   History of medical conditions: Assessments related to medical conditions.
    *   Other (please specify): Select this option if none of the above apply.

6.  Select and list any action taken from the "Action Taken" dropdown menu:
    *   None: No action was taken.
    *   Advised client to: Provide guidance or advice to the client.
    *   Installed safety measures: Installed safety measures to prevent future incidents.
    *   Other (please specify): Select this option if none of the above apply.

7.  Enter the client's name and contact number in the respective fields.

8.  Enter the next visit date and time in the respective fields.

9.  Enter any follow-up notes in the "Follow-up Notes" field.

10.  Enter the next visit date and time in the "Follow-up Date" and "Follow-up Time" fields.

11.  Enter any follow-up action taken in the "Follow-up Action" field.

12.  Enter the follow-up contact information in the "Follow-up Contact" field.

13.  Select the review status from the "Review Status" dropdown menu:
    *   Not Reviewed: The form has not been reviewed.
    *   In Progress: The form is currently being reviewed.
    *   Complete: The form has been reviewed and completed.

14.  Enter any notes for the reviewer in the "Notes for Reviewer" field.

15.  Enter the reviewer's notes in the "Reviewer Notes" field.

16.  Select the reviewer status from the "Reviewer Status" dropdown menu:
    *   Not Reviewed: The form has not been reviewed by a reviewer.
    *   Complete: The form has been reviewed by a reviewer.
    *   Partially Reviewed: The form has been partially reviewed by a reviewer.

17.  Enter the review date and time in the "Review Date" and "Review Time" fields.

18.  Enter any reviewer comments in the "Reviewer Comments" field.

19.  Enter the next review date and time in the "Next Review Date" and "Next Review Time" fields.
