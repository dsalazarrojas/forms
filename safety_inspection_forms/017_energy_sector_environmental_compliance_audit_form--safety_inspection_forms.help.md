# Energy Sector Environmental Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Energy Sector Environmental Compliance Audit Form is designed to collect information about a facility's energy usage and environmental compliance status. This form is intended for internal use within the organization, likely for regulatory or reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have permission to access and input information about the facility.
2. Be as accurate and detailed as possible when completing the form.
3. Have relevant documentation or evidence to support your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Facility Information
* **Facility ID** (`facility_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the unique identifier assigned to this facility by the organization.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the facility.
* **Facility Location** (`facility_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the facility.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the full address of the facility.
* **Facility City** (`facility_city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city of the facility.
* **Facility State** (`facility_state`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state of the facility.
* **Facility Zip** (`facility_zip`, `text`, required: false): Enter the zip code of the facility.
* **Facility Contact** (`facility_contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the facility's contact person.
* **Facility Contact Number** (`facility_contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the facility's contact person.
* **Facility Notes** (`facility_notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the facility.
* **Facility Status** (`facility_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current status of the facility from the options Active, Inactive, or Suspended.
* **Facility Status (2)** (`facility_status_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the current status of the facility from the options Active, Inactive, or Suspended. **Note:** This field is a duplicate of `facility_status`, and its value will be ignored.
* **Facility Status (3)** (`facility_status_3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the current status of the facility from the options Active, Inactive, or Suspended. **Note:** This field is a duplicate of `facility_status_2`, and its value will be ignored.
* **Facility Status (4)** (`facility_status_4`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current status of the facility from the options Active, Inactive, or Suspended.
* **Facility Status (5)** (`facility_status_5`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current status of the facility from the options Active, Inactive, or Suspended.
* **Facility Status (6)** (`facility_status_6`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current status of the facility from the options Active, Inactive, or Suspended.
* **Facility Status (7)** (`facility_status_7`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the current status of the facility from the options Active, Inactive, or Suspended. **Note:** This field is a duplicate of `facility_status_6`, and its value will be ignored.
* **Facility Status (8)** (`facility_status_8`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the current status of the facility from the options Active, Inactive, or Suspended. **Note:** This field is a duplicate of `facility_status_7`, and its value will be ignored.
* **Facility Status (9)** (`facility_status_9`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current status of the facility from the options Active, Inactive, or Suspended.
