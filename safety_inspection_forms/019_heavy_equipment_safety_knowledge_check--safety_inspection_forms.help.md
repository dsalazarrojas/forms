# Heavy Equipment Safety Knowledge Check - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Heavy Equipment Safety Knowledge Check is a series of questions designed to assess your familiarity with procedures and policies related to the use of heavy equipment. This form is intended to ensure that users are aware of and follow proper safety protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question with either "Yes" or "No" based on your level of familiarity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Heavy Equipment Safety Knowledge Check** (`general_questions`, text, not required): This is the title of the form and serves as a brief introduction to the assessment.
* **Do you use PPE (Personal Protective Equipment)?** (`ppe_use`, select_multiple, required: false): Answer "Yes" if you use PPE when operating heavy equipment, and "No" if you do not use PPE.
* **Are you familiar with safety procedures for heavy equipment?** (`procedure_familiarity`, select_one, required: true): Answer "Yes" if you are familiar with safety procedures for heavy equipment, and "No" if you are not.
* **Have you ever used a backhoe?** (`procedure_familiarity_question_1`, select_one, required: true): Answer "Yes" if you have used a backhoe, and "No" if you have not.
* **Do you use PPE for backhoe operation?** (`ppe_used`, select_multiple, required: false): Answer "Yes" if you use PPE for backhoe operation, and "No" if you do not.
* **Are you familiar with safety policies for backhoe operation?** (`policy_familiarity_question_1`, select_one, required: true): Answer "Yes" if you are familiar with safety policies for backhoe operation, and "No" if you are not.
* **Have you used other heavy equipment?** (`heavy_equipment_used`, select_multiple, required: false): Answer "Yes" if you have used other heavy equipment, and "No" if you have not.
* **Are you familiar with safety procedures for other heavy equipment?** (`procedure_familiarity_question_2`, select_one, required: true): Answer "Yes" if you are familiar with safety procedures for other heavy equipment, and "No" if you are not.
* **Do you use PPE for other heavy equipment operations?** (`ppe_used`, select_multiple, required: false): Answer "Yes" if you use PPE for other heavy equipment operations, and "No" if you do not.
* **Are you familiar with safety policies for other heavy equipment operations?** (`policy_familiarity_question_2`, select_one, required: true): Answer "Yes" if you are familiar with safety policies for other heavy equipment operations, and "No" if you are not.
* **Is there a specific policy that you are familiar with?** (`policy_familiarity_question_3`, select_one, required: true): Answer "Yes" if you are familiar with a specific policy, and "No" if you are not.

Note: The answers to these questions will help assess your safety knowledge and identify areas where you may need additional training or guidance.
