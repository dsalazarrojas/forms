1. Start by entering the general information about the facility, including its name, address, phone number, and email.
2. Evaluate the condition of the facility, focusing on the floor conditions, secure areas, and other relevant aspects.
3. Check the availability and condition of safety equipment, such as fire extinguishers, first aid kits, and emergency exit lights.
