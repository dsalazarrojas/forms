# Equipment Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Equipment Inspection Form is used to record the inspection status of equipment, ensuring that it is safe to operate and maintain.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the inspection date for the equipment being inspected.
2. Enter the name of the equipment being inspected.
3. Describe the equipment being inspected, including its location and any other relevant details.
4. Choose the inspection frequency for the equipment (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
5. Check the condition status of the equipment (Safe, Not Safe, or Not Applicable).
6. Add any comments or notes about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date** (1, Date, required: no): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Equipment Name** (2, text, required: no): Enter the name of the equipment being inspected.
* **Equipment Description** (3, text, required: no): Describe the equipment, including its location and any other relevant details.
* **Inspection Frequency** (4, select one, required: no): Choose the frequency of the inspection (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Condition Status** (5, select multiple, required: no): Check the status of the equipment (Safe, Not Safe, or Not Applicable).
* **Comments** (6, text, required: no): Add any comments or notes about the inspection.

## Tips
* Ensure you are inspecting the correct equipment and have all the necessary information.
* Be thorough and accurate when describing the equipment and its condition.
* Choose the correct inspection frequency and condition status.
* Add any comments or notes that will help in future inspections.
