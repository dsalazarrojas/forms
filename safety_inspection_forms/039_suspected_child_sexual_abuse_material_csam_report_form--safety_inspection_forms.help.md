# Suspected Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about suspected cases of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) from users who have witnessed or have knowledge of such incidents. The goal is to gather accurate and comprehensive details to facilitate investigation and potential further action.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Report Type**: Select the type of report you are submitting (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3"). This will help us identify the nature of the report.
2. **Report Date**: Enter the date when the incident occurred or when you became aware of it.
3. **Report Location**: Provide the location where the incident occurred or where the incident was discovered.
4. **Report Details**: Give a brief description of the incident, including any relevant details that might be helpful for the investigation.
5. **Report Officer Name**: If you are reporting on behalf of someone else, please provide the name of the person who made the report.
6. **Report Officer Contact**: Enter the contact information of the person who made the report.
7. **Report Officer Email**: Enter the email address of the person who made the report.
8. **Report Officer Agency**: If the report is being made on behalf of an organization, please provide the agency's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Report Type
* Report Type (`report_type`): Select the type of report you are submitting to help identify the nature of the incident.

### Report Date
* Report Date (`report_date`): Enter the date when the incident occurred or when you became aware of it. This helps us understand the timeline of the incident.

### Report Location
* Report Location (`report_location`): Provide the location where the incident occurred or where the incident was discovered. This could be a physical address, website, or any other location relevant to the incident.

### Report Details
* Report Details (`report_details`): Give a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant context, circumstances, or information that might be helpful for the investigation.

### Report Officer Name
* Report Officer Name (`report_officer_name`): If you are reporting on behalf of someone else, provide the name of the person who made the report.

### Report Officer Contact
* Report Officer Contact (`report_officer_contact`): Enter the contact information of the person who made the report. This helps us get in touch with them for further information or clarification.

### Report Officer Email
* Report Officer Email (`report_officer_email`): Enter the email address of the person who made the report. This helps us communicate with them regarding the report.

### Report Officer Agency
* Report Officer Agency (`report_officer_agency`): If the report is being made on behalf of an organization, provide the agency's name. This helps us understand the scope and context of the report.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly, as the information provided will be used to facilitate investigation and potential further action.
- If you are reporting on behalf of someone else, make sure to provide accurate contact information for the person making the report.
- If you have any additional information or context that might be helpful for the investigation, please include it in the "Report Details" field.
- If you are unsure about any part of the form, please contact us for clarification or guidance.
