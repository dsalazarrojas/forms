# Water Supply Drill Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Supply Drill Feedback Survey is a tool for evaluating the effectiveness of water supply drills. This form helps us understand how well a drill was conducted, what worked and what didn't, and how we can improve future drills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the location name of the drill site.
2. Enter the start and end times of the drill.
3. Select the number of evaluators who participated.
4. Rate the water quality of the drill (Good, Fair, Poor).
5. Rate the water temperature (a number).
6. Select the duration of the drill (Less than 30 minutes, 30-60 minutes, 60-90 minutes, More than 90 minutes).
7. Rate the water pressure (a number).
8. Rate the water flow (a number).
9. Enter the date and time when the drill started and ended.
10. Select the location of the drill.
11. Select the status of the drill (Complete, Incomplete, Not Started).
12. Enter the number of evaluators who attended.
13. Enter a total score for the drill (a number).
14. Enter the maximum possible points for the drill (a number).
15. Enter the minimum possible points for the drill (a number).
16. Enter the total score given by evaluators (a number).
17. Enter a unique form ID and version number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Location Name
* **Location Name** (`location_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the drill site.
### Location Address
* **Location Address** (`location_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the drill site.
### Start Time
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the drill in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00).
### End Time
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the drill in 24-hour format (e.g., 17:00).
### Evaluators Participated
* **Evaluators Participated** (`evaluators_participated`, number, optional): Select the number of evaluators who participated in the drill.
### Evaluators Count
* **Evaluators Count** (`evaluators_count`, number, optional): Select the number of evaluators who participated in the drill (this field is redundant, please ignore).
### Location ID
* **Location ID** (`location_id`, select_one, optional): Select the location ID of the drill site (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
### Evaluator Comments
* **Evaluator Comments** (`evaluator_comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments from the evaluators.
### Water Quality
* **Water Quality** (`water_quality`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the water quality of the drill (Good, Fair, Poor).
### Water Temperature
* **Water Temperature** (`water_temperature`, number, optional): Rate the water temperature of the drill (a number).
### Drill Duration
* **Drill Duration** (`drill_duration`, select_one, optional): Select the duration of the drill (Less than 30 minutes, 30-60 minutes, 60-90 minutes, More than 90 minutes).
### Water Pressure
* **Water Pressure** (`water_pressure`, number, optional): Rate the water pressure of the drill (a number).
### Water Flow
* **Water Flow** (`water_flow`, number, optional): Rate the water flow of the drill (a number).
### Drill Start
* **Drill Start** (`drill_start`, date, optional): Enter the date and time when the drill started (YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM).
### Drill End
* **Drill End** (`drill_end`, date, optional): Enter the date and time when the drill ended (YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM).
### Drill Location
* **Drill Location** (`drill_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the drill.
### Drill Status
* **Drill Status** (`drill_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the drill (Complete, Incomplete, Not Started).
### Evaluators Attended
* **Evaluators Attended** (`evaluators_attended`, number, optional): Select the number of evaluators who attended the drill.
### Total Points
* **Total Points** (`total_points`, number, optional): Enter the total score for the drill (a number).
### Max Points
* **Max Points** (`total_points_max`, number, optional): Enter the maximum possible points for the drill (a number).
### Min Points
* **Min Points** (`total_points_min`, number, optional): Enter the minimum possible points for the drill (a number).
### Evaluators Score
* **Evaluators Score** (`evaluators_score`, number, optional): Enter the score given by evaluators (a number).
### Form ID
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): Enter a unique form ID for the drill.
### Form Version
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, number, optional): Enter the version number of the form.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
* If a field is not applicable, please select the "Not Applicable" option.
* If you have any questions or issues while filling out this form, please contact us at [contact email or phone number].
