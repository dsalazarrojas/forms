# Project Safety Protocol Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Safety Protocol Assessment Form is used to evaluate project sites for potential hazards and assess their adherence to safety protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please fill out all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*) if required.
- Ensure that each field is accurate and complete.
- Use the provided explanations for each field to help guide your completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Safety Protocol Assessment Form** (`project_safety_protocol_assessment_form`, text, not required): This is the title of the form.
* **Supervisor's Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, not required): Please enter the name of the supervisor responsible for this project.
* **Project Number** (`project_number`, text, not required): Enter the unique identifier for this project.
* **Site Location** (`site_location`, text, not required): Enter the location where the project is taking place.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, not required): Enter the date of the visit.
* **Rating of Safety Practices** (`safety_practices_rating`, select_one, not required): Rate the project's safety practices as Good or Fair.
* **Rating of Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment_rating`, select_one, not required): Rate the condition of safety equipment as Good or Fair.
* **Condition of Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment_condition`, select_multiple, not required): Check all that apply regarding the condition of safety equipment:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Observation of Worksite** (`observation_of_worksite`, text, not required): Provide a brief description of what was observed during the visit.
* **Supervisor's Notes** (`supervisor_notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments from the supervisor.
* **Safety Concerns** (`safety_concerns`, select_multiple, not required): Check all that apply regarding safety concerns:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Follow-up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`, select_multiple, not required): Check all that apply regarding follow-up actions:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Supervisor's Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, email, not required): Enter the supervisor's email address for verification purposes.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, not required): Enter the date the form was signed.
* **Project Manager's Signature** (`project_manager_signature`, select_one, not required): Rate whether the project manager signed the form as Yes or No.

Please ensure to review the form carefully and complete it accurately to help ensure the project site's safety.
