# Outdoor Safety Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Outdoor Safety Assessment form is a tool used for gathering information about hazards and safety-related incidents that occur in outdoor environments. This form helps in identifying and reporting hazards, ensuring that necessary precautions and measures are taken to prevent accidents and ensure a safe working condition.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Gather all necessary information and answers for the questions below.
2. Select the relevant answers from the options provided.
3. Fill in the text fields with concise and clear information.
4. Select the appropriate hazard risk level and safety equipment as needed.
5. Provide the emergency contact person's name and phone number.
6. Enter the emergency phone number.
7. Add any additional notes or comments.
8. Complete the submitter and submit date/time information.
9. Finally, review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`General Information`, `text`, required/optional): Briefly describe the incident or hazard situation.
* **Hazard Location** (`Hazard Location`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the exact location where the hazard was encountered.
* **Hazard Description** (`Hazard Description`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide a detailed description of the hazard.
* **Hazard Risk Level** (`Hazard Risk Level`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the risk level of the hazard from the options: Low, Medium, High.
* **Hazard Precautions** (`Hazard Precautions`, `text`, required/optional): Add any relevant precautions or safety measures taken or recommended.
* **Safety Equipment** (`Safety Equipment`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the safety equipment related to the hazard from the options: Hard Hat, Safety Glasses, Steel Toe Boots, First Aid Kit, Fire Extinguisher.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the name and phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone** (`Emergency Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact phone number.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes regarding the hazard.
* **Submitter** (`Submitter`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submit Date** (`Submit Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submit Time** (`Submit Time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, `text`, required/optional): Identify the person submitting the form.

Please review the form carefully and ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the assessment.
