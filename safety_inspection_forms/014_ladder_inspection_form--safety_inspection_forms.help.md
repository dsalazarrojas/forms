# Ladder Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to report and document safety inspections of ladders.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the ladder being inspected in the "Ladder Name" field.
2. Enter the location of the ladder in the "Ladder Location" field.
3. Select the appropriate checklist for the inspection from the "inspection_checklist" field.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments about the inspection in the "Notes" field.
5. Provide your email address in the "Email Contact" field.
6. (Optional) Provide your telephone number in the "Telephone Contact" field.
7. (Optional) Enter the name and title of the person assigned to the ladder (e.g. "John Doe, Safety Inspector") in the "Assigned To" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ladder Name** (`ladder_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the ladder being inspected.
* **Ladder Location** (`ladder_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the ladder.
* **inspection_checklist** (`inspection_checklist`, select one, required): Select the appropriate checklist for the inspection.

# Tips

* Make sure to select the correct checklist for the inspection to ensure accurate reporting.
* Use the "Notes" field to provide any additional information that may not be captured in the other fields.
* If you are providing your email address in the "Email Contact" field, ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are providing your telephone number in the "Telephone Contact" field, ensure that it is accurate and relevant to the inspection.
