# Safety Equipment Demonstration Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from participants for a safety equipment demonstration. It's essential to fill it out accurately and thoroughly to ensure the consent is recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the required information, including participant details, demonstration date and time, location, and any additional notes.
2. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all fields are completed accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (participant_information): This is the first page of the form where you need to fill out your personal details.
* **Demonstration Date** (demonstration_date): Enter the date of the demonstration in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Demonstration Time** (demonstration_time): Enter the time of the demonstration in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Demonstration Location** (demonstration_location): Enter the location where the demonstration will take place.
* **Safety Equipment** (safety_equipment): Enter a brief description of the safety equipment being demonstrated.
* **Trainer Name** (trainer_name): Enter the name of the trainer who will be conducting the demonstration.
* **Organization** (organization): Enter the name of the organization hosting the demonstration.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address to ensure you can be reached for any further communication.
* **Phone** (phone): Enter your phone number in case the organization needs to contact you.
* **Notes** (notes): Add any additional notes or comments related to the demonstration.
