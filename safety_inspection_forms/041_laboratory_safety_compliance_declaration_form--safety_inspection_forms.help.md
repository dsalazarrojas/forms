# laboratory_safety_compliance_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The laboratory_safety_compliance_declaration_form is used to declare safety and equipment usage in a laboratory setting. This helps in maintaining a safe and compliant environment for all staff and students.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your First Name
2. Enter your Last Name
3. Enter your Job Title
4. Enter your Department
5. Enter your Email
6. Enter your Phone
7. Enter your Address
8. Declare your Personal Safety Equipment usage:
	* Fire Exinguishable Chemical Bib
	* Fire Extinguisher
	* Fire Alarm
9. Declare your Personal Protective Equipment usage:
	* Lab Cohort
	* Lab Gloves
	* Lab Goggles
10. Declare your Fire Safety Equipment:
	* Fire Extinguisher
	* Fire Alarm
11. Declare your Electrical Equipment:
	* Fume Hood
	* Safety Shutter
	* Safety Glass
12. Declare your Gas Cylinder:
	* Gas Cylinder
	* Gas Valve
13. Declare your Chemical Hydrant:
	* Gas Valve
	* Gas Tank
14. Declare your Electrical Outlet:
	* Grounded Outlet
	* Grouding
15. Declare your Electrical System:
	* Grounded System
	* Ground System
16. Declare your Electrical Box:
	* Grounded Box
	* Ground Box
17. Declare your Electrical Wire:
	* Grounded Wire
	* Ground Wire
18. Declare your Electrical Device:
	* Grounded Device
	* Ground Device
19. Click submit to submit your declaration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: Enter your first name here.
*   **Last Name**: Enter your last name here.
*   **Job Title**: Enter your job title here.
*   **Department**: Enter your department here.
*   **Email**: Enter your email here. This should be in the format of `yourname@example.com`.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number here.
*   **Address**: Enter your address here.
*   **Personal Safety Equipment**: Declare your use of fire safety equipment, including Fire Exinguishable Chemical Bib, Fire Extinguisher, and Fire Alarm.
*   **Personal Protective Equipment**: Declare your use of lab safety equipment, including Lab Cohort, Lab Gloves, and Lab Goggles.
*   **Fire Safety Equipment**: Declare your use of fire safety equipment, including Fire Extinguisher and Fire Alarm.
*   **Electrical Equipment**: Declare your use of electrical equipment, including Fume Hood, Safety Shutter, and Safety Glass.
*   **Gas Cylinder**: Declare your use of gas cylinders, including Gas Cylinder and Gas Valve.
*   **Chemical Hydrant**: Declare your use of chemical hydrants, including Gas Valve and Gas Tank.
*   **Electrical Outlet**: Declare your use of grounded electrical outlets, including Grounded Outlet and Grouding.
*   **Electrical System**: Declare your use of electrical systems, including Grounded System and Ground System.
*   **Electrical Box**: Declare your use of grounded electrical boxes, including Grounded Box and Ground Box.
*   **Electrical Wire**: Declare your use of grounded electrical wires, including Grounded Wire and Ground Wire.
*   **Electrical Device**: Declare your use of grounded electrical devices, including Grounded Device and Ground Device.
*   **Submit**: Click this button to submit your declaration.
*   **Cancel**: This button will cancel your declaration.
