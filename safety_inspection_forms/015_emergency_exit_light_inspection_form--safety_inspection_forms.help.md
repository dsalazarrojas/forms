# Emergency Exit Light Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Exit Light Inspection Form is designed to record the status of emergency exit lights in a building or facility. This form helps to ensure that exit lights are functioning properly and are inspected regularly to maintain safety and compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form and ensuring you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Begin with the first page of the form, which includes the form title and a blank space for your comments or notes.
3. Move on to the next page, where you will find fields to complete the inspection details.
4. Complete the fields as follows:
	* **Exit Light Location**: Enter the location of the exit light being inspected.
	* **Exit Light Status**: Select the status of the exit light from the options provided (Active, Inactive, or Out of service).
	* **Last Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the last inspection or maintenance performed on the exit light.
	* **Next Inspection Due Date**: Enter the date by which the next inspection or maintenance is due for the exit light.
	* **Exit Light Type**: Select the type of exit light from the options provided (LED, Halogen, or Fluorescent).
	* **Exit Light Model**: Enter the model number or description of the exit light being inspected.
5. Review your completed form to ensure all fields are filled in correctly and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exit Light Location**: This field is used to record the location of the exit light being inspected. Please enter the specific area or room where the exit light is installed.
* **Exit Light Status**: This field is used to indicate the current status of the exit light. Select the option that best describes its current condition (Active, Inactive, or Out of service).
* **Last Inspection Date**: This field is used to record the date of the last inspection or maintenance performed on the exit light. If the exit light has never been inspected or maintained, enter "Never" or "Unknown".
* **Next Inspection Due Date**: This field is used to record the date by which the next inspection or maintenance is due for the exit light. If no maintenance is required, enter "Never" or "Unknown".
* **Exit Light Type**: This field is used to select the type of exit light being inspected (LED, Halogen, or Fluorescent).
* **Exit Light Model**: This field is used to record the model number or description of the exit light being inspected.
* **Emergency Exit Light Inspection Form Page 2**: This field is a blank space for any additional comments or notes related to the inspection.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
* If a field is not applicable or not known, enter "Unknown" or "Never".
