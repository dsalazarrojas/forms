# Fire Safety Equipment Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to conduct a fire safety equipment checklist to ensure that the facilities and equipment are in compliance with safety regulations and guidelines. This helps to identify any potential hazards and ensure that the facilities are safe for employees, visitors, and occupants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the facility in the `Facility Name` field.
2. Select the inspector's name from the `inspector_name` field.
3. Answer the questions regarding the presence or absence of fire safety equipment, such as fire exits, fire extinguishers, fire sensors, fire hoses, fire alarm systems, fire sprinkler systems, and fire hydrants.
4. Specify whether fire safety training has been provided to personnel.
5. Enter the date and time of the last inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the facility that is being inspected.
- **inspector_name** (`inspector_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the inspector conducting the inspection.
- **Fire Exits** (`fire_exits`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if there are fire exits available in the facility.
- **Fire Extinguishers** (`fire_extinguishers`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if fire extinguishers are available and in working condition in the facility.
- **Fire Sensors** (`fire_sensors`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if fire sensors are installed and functional in the facility.
- **Fire Hoses** (`fire_hoses`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if fire hoses are available and in working condition in the facility.
- **Fire Alarm Systems** (`fire_alarm_systems`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if fire alarm systems are installed and functional in the facility.
- **Fire Sprinkler Systems** (`fire_sprinkler_systems`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if fire sprinkler systems are installed and functional in the facility.
- **Fire Hydrants** (`fire_sprinkler_hydrants`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if fire hydrants are available and in working condition in the facility.
- **Smoke Alarms** (`smoke_alarms`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if smoke alarms are installed and functional in the facility.
- **Fire Safety Training** (`fire_safety_training`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if fire safety training has been provided to personnel.
- **Last Inspection Date** (`last_inspection_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the last inspection.
- **Last Inspection Time** (`last_inspection_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the last inspection.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fire safety equipment is properly installed, maintained, and functional.
- Verify that all personnel have received fire safety training.
- Schedule regular inspections to ensure the facilities remain safe and compliant with regulations.
- Document all findings and recommendations for improvement.
- Review and update the form regularly to reflect changes and updates to the facilities and equipment.
