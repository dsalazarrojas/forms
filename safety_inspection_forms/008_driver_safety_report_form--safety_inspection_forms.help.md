# driver_safety_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The driver_safety_report_form is used for reporting incidents or events related to driver safety. It helps to document and track safety-related events that may have occurred during a vehicle's operation or maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the vehicle status of the vehicle involved in the incident/event. Choose from "Active" or "Inactive".
2. Enter the name of the driver who was driving the vehicle at the time of the incident/event.
3. Enter the vehicle's number or identifier.
4. Choose the date of the incident/event in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
5. Select the time of the incident/event in the format HH:MM.
6. If applicable, enter the location where the incident/event occurred.
7. Describe what happened in the incident/event.
8. If not already indicated, indicate if the vehicle was in good condition.
9. Select the status of the driver after the incident/event. Choose from "Active" or "Inactive".
10. Indicate if there was a safety risk involved in the incident/event. Choose "Yes" or "No".
11. Enter any safety comments related to the incident/event.
12. If applicable, enter any corrective action taken after the incident/event.
13. If applicable, enter any action taken after the incident/event.
14. If applicable, enter any safety standards or regulations related to the incident/event.
15. Indicate if the driver's conduct was in accordance with company policies. Choose from "Yes" or "No".
16. Enter any other relevant information not already captured.
17. Sign and date your signature.
18. Sign and time your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Status** (`vehicle_status`): Select whether the vehicle was active or inactive at the time of the incident/event.
* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`): Enter the name of the driver who was driving the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Number** (`vehicle_number`): Enter the vehicle's number or identifier.
* **Date Of Event** (`date_of_event`): Choose the date of the incident/event in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time Of Event** (`time_of_event`): Choose the time of the incident/event in the format HH:MM.
* **Location Of Event** (`location_of_event`): If applicable, enter the location where the incident/event occurred.
* **Description Of Event** (`description_of_event`): Describe what happened in the incident/event.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`): If not already indicated, indicate whether the vehicle was in good or poor condition.
* **Driver Status** (`driver_status`): Select the status of the driver after the incident/event. Choose from "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Safety Risk** (`safety_risk`): Indicate if there was a safety risk involved in the incident/event. Choose "Yes" or "No".
* **Safety Comments** (`safety_comments`): Enter any safety comments related to the incident/event.
* **Corrective Action** (`corrective_action`): If applicable, enter any corrective action taken after the incident/event.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`): If applicable, enter any action taken after the incident/event.
* **Safety Standards** (`safety_standards`): If applicable, enter any safety standards or regulations related to the incident/event.
* **Driver Conduct** (`driver_conduct`): Indicate if the driver's conduct was in accordance with company policies. Choose from "Yes" or "No".
* **Other Information** (`other_information`): Enter any other relevant information not already captured.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`): Sign and date your signature.
* **Signature Time** (`signature_time`): Sign and time your signature.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Sign your name to confirm that the incident/event was reported accurately.
* **Driver Id** (`driver_id`): Enter the driver's identifier.
* **Vehicle Id** (`vehicle_id`): Enter the vehicle's identifier.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`): If applicable, indicate the type of vehicle involved in the incident/event.
* **Driver Status 2** (`driver_status_2`): If applicable, enter the status of the driver after the incident/event.
* **Vehicle Status 2** (`vehicle_status_2`): If applicable, enter the status of the vehicle after the incident/event.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the incident/event.
