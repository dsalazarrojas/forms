# scissor_lift_safety_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scissor lift safety checklist form is a systematic tool for inspecting and ensuring the safety of scissor lifts. It is designed to be completed by an authorized inspector prior to each operation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the relevant information in each field as required.
2. Ensure you complete all sections of the form thoroughly and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Pre-Operation Checks** (`pre_operation_checks`, text, optional):
	* This section is for listing any pre-operation checks performed on the scissor lift, such as checking for wear and tear, proper assembly, and other relevant safety measures.
- **Lift Operation** (`lift_operation`, text, optional):
	* Describe the lift operation that was performed, including any notable issues or concerns encountered.
- **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, optional):
	* Record the date of the inspection for future reference.
- **Location** (`location`, text, optional):
	* Enter the location where the scissor lift was inspected and operated.
- **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, optional):
	* List the name of the authorized inspector who performed the safety check.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional):
	* Capture any additional comments or remarks relevant to the safety inspection.
- **Operational Checks** (`operational_checks`, text, optional):
	* Record any operational checks performed on the scissor lift, such as oil levels, hydraulic pressures, or other relevant measures.
- **Inspection Date 2** (`inspection_date_2`, date, optional):
	* (Note: This field seems to be a duplicate of `inspection_date`, but it's left here for reference. Please use the original `inspection_date` field instead.)
- **Inspection Date 2** can be ignored, as it is not necessary to record the same date twice. Please disregard this field and use the original `inspection_date` field.
