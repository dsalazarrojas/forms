# Inhalant Abuse Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inhalant Abuse Incident Report Form is used to document incidents of inhalant abuse in various settings such as schools, workplaces, and communities. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the incident is investigated and addressed properly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the date and time of the incident in the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" fields.
2. Select the correct location of the incident from the "Incident Location" dropdown menu.
3. Describe the details of the incident in the "Incident Details" field.
4. Indicate the involvement of the suspect(s) and witness(es) by selecting the appropriate options in the "Suspect Involvement" and "Witness Involvement" fields.
5. Select the follow-up action(s) to be taken in the "Follow-up Action" field.
6. Choose the reporting unit that handled the incident from the "Incident Reporting Unit" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the date and time the report was submitted in the "Incident Reporting Date" and "Incident Reporting Time" fields.
8. Enter the name of the person submitting the report in the "Submitted By" field.
9. Enter the unit to whom the report was submitted in the "Submitted To" field.
10. Indicate the status of the report by selecting the correct option in the "Incident Reporting Status" field.
11. Enter any additional comments about the report in the "Incident Reporting Comments" field.
12. Enter the name of the reporting unit in the "Incident Reporting Unit Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Report** (`incident_report`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Suspect Involvement** (`suspect_involvement`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the involvement of the suspect(s) in the incident. Choose from the options: None, Suspect, Involuntary.
* **Witness Involvement** (`witness_involvement`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the involvement of witness(es) in the incident. Choose from the options: None, Suspect, Witness, Involuntary.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, select_one, optional): Select the correct location of the incident. Choose from the options: Yes, No.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, optional): Describe the details of the incident.
* **Follow-up Action** (`follow_up_action`, select_multiple, optional): Select the follow-up action(s) to be taken. Choose from the options: None, Follow-up, Escalate.
* **Incident Reporting Unit** (`incident_reporting_unit`, select_one, optional): Choose the reporting unit that handled the incident. Choose from the options: School, Workplace, Community.
* **Incident Reporting Date** (`incident_reporting_date`, date, required): Enter the date the report was submitted.
* **Incident Reporting Time** (`incident_reporting_time`, time, required): Enter the time the report was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the report.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, text, optional): Enter the unit to whom the report was submitted.
* **Incident Reporting Status** (`incident_reporting_status`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the status of the report. Choose from the options: Draft, Completed, Submitted.
* **Incident Reporting Comments** (`incident_reporting_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the report.
* **Incident Reporting Unit Name** (`incident_reporting_unit_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the reporting unit.
