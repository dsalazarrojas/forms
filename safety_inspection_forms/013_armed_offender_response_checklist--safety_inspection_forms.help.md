# Armed Offender Response Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Armed Offender Response Checklist is a form designed to collect information about incidents involving an armed offender on campus. This form helps gather details about the incident, including the date and time it occurred, the location, and the response actions taken by the responding team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and provide the requested information.
2. Ensure you answer all required fields accurately and completely.
3. Use the options provided to select the correct response for each field.
4. If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the designated response team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Section 1: Incident Information
*   **Date of Incident** (`incident_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of the incident.
*   **Time of Incident** (`incident_time`, Time, Required): Enter the time of the incident.
*   **Location of Incident** (`incident_location`, Text, Required): Enter the location where the incident occurred, including the building, facility, or address.
*   **Officer or Staff Member Reporting** (`reporting_officer`, Text, Required): Enter the name and badge number of the officer or staff member who is reporting the incident.

### Section 2: Threat Assessment
*   **Description of Offender** (`offender_description`, Text, Required): Describe the physical appearance and any identifying marks of the offender.
*   **Type of Weapon Observed** (`weapon_type`, Select One, Required): Select the type of weapon observed, including Firearm, Knife, Explosive device, Blunt object, or Other weapon.
*   **Is Weapon Still Present** (`weapon_confirmation`, Select One, Required): Indicate if the weapon is still present or if this information is unknown.
*   **Threat Level Assessment** (`threat_level`, Select One, Required): Assess the threat level as Low, Medium, High, or Critical.

### Section 3: Response Actions
*   **Emergency Services Called** (`emergency_services_called`, Select One, Required): Indicate if emergency services were called, including True, False, or In Progress.
*   **Evacuation Initiated** (`evacuation_initiated`, Select One, Required): Indicate if evacuation was initiated, including True, False, or In Progress.
*   **Lockdown or Shelter-in-Place Initiated** (`lockdown_initiated`, Select One, Required): Indicate if lockdown or shelter-in-place was initiated, including True, False, or In Progress.
*   **Staff and Occupant Communication Issued** (`communication_issued`, Select One, Required): Indicate if staff and occupant communication was issued, including Yes - PA system, Yes - email alert, or other method.

### Section 4: Personnel and Injuries
*   **Any Injuries Sustained** (`injuries_sustained`, Select One, Required): Report any injuries sustained, including No injuries, Minor injuries, Moderate injuries, Severe injuries, or Fatalities.
*   **Number of People Injured** (`persons_injured_count`, Number, Optional): Enter the number of people injured.
*   **First Aid Provided** (`first_aid_provided`, Select One, Optional): Indicate if first aid was provided, including True, False, or In Progress.

### Section 5: Documentation
*   **Witnesses Present** (`witnesses_present`, Select One, Required): Indicate if witnesses are present, including True, False.
*   **Number of Witnesses** (`witness_count`, Number, Optional): Enter the number of witnesses.
*   **Incident Recorded on Security Video** (`video_recorded`, Select One, Optional): Indicate if the incident was recorded on security video, including True, False, or Unknown.
*   **Additional Details or Observations** (`additional_details`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional details or observations about the incident.
