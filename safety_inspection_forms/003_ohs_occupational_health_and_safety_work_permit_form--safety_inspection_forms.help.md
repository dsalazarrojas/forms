# OHS (Occupational Health And Safety) Work Permit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to document the results of a safety inspection and ensure that necessary measures are taken to mitigate hazards and prevent injuries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answers for each field based on your inspection findings.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, even if they are marked as optional.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Safety Inspection Form (Page 1)
* **Safety Inspection Form** (`Safety Inspection Form`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable options that relate to the hazards or risks identified during the inspection.

* **Hazard Assessment** (`Hazard Assessment`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if there are hazards or risks present, and 'No' if there are no hazards or risks.

* **Job Details** (`Job Details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the task or job being performed, including any relevant details about the equipment or materials used.

* **Supervisor Signature** (`Supervisor Signature`, text, optional): This field is reserved for your supervisor's signature, which is typically required to confirm that they have reviewed and approved the inspection results.

* **Safety Inspection Date** (`Safety Inspection Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the safety inspection.

* **Safety Inspection Time** (`Safety Inspection Time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the safety inspection.

* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Record any additional notes or comments about the inspection, including any actions to be taken to address hazards or risks.

* **Employee Signature** (`Employee Signature`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have read and understood the inspection results, and 'No' if you have not.

### Tips
* Ensure that you complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the workplace remains safe for all employees.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or safety officer for guidance.
* Keep a record of all completed forms for future reference and compliance with regulatory requirements.
