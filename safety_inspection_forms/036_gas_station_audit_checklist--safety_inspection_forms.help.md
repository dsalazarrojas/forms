# Gas Station Audit Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for gas stations to self-audit their safety and cleanliness standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer every question truthfully.
2. For each field, select the best response from the provided options.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
**<label> Introduction</label> (ID 1, **note**): This is a placeholder section to introduce the purpose and scope of the audit.

### Site Name
**<label> Site Name</label> (ID 2, text): Enter the name of the gas station being audited.

### Location Address
**<label> Location Address</label> (ID 3, text): Enter the physical address of the gas station.

### Safety Equipment
**<label> Safety Equipment</label> (ID 4, select_multiple): Check which safety equipment are available at the gas station. Options are: 
* Fire Extinguisher
* Fire Alarm
* Emergency Exit Sign

### Safety Equipment Hazards
**<label> Safety Equipment Hazards</label> (ID 5, select_multiple): Check which safety equipment hazards are present at the gas station. Options are: 
* Rusted Equipment
* Damaged Equipment
* Broken Equipment

### Cleanliness Standards
**<label> Cleanliness Standards</label> (ID 6, select_one): Rate the cleanliness standards of the gas station. Options are: 
* High
* Medium
* Low

### Cleanliness Standards Description
**<label> Cleanliness Standards Description</label> (ID 7, text): Provide a description of the cleanliness standards at the gas station.

### Employee Training
