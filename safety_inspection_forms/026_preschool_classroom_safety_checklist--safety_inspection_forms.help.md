# Preschool Classroom Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive safety inspection checklist for preschool classrooms, aimed at identifying potential hazards and ensuring a safe environment for children and staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Review each page to understand the specific hazards listed.
*   Check the corresponding box for each hazard you observe in the classroom.
*   For each hazard found, record the relevant details and remarks in the "Remarks" field.
*   If a specific hazard is not listed, use the "Other Hazards" field to report any other issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Teacher Signature** (Select Multiple, 1): This is the section where the teacher confirms they have reviewed the form and understand its purpose.
    *   Select "Yes" if you have reviewed the form and understand its purpose.
    *   Select "No" if you have not yet reviewed the form or do not understand its purpose.

- **Emergency Contact** (Text, 2): In the event of an emergency, this contact person will be called upon for assistance.
    *   Enter the full name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
    *   This is for reference in case of an emergency.

- **Fire Hazards** (Select One, 3): Check for fire hazards such as frayed electrical cords and exposed wiring.
    *   Select the appropriate option that best describes the fire hazards observed.
    *   Use the "Remarks" field to note any additional information about the fire hazards.

- **Electrical Hazards** (Select One, 4): Check for electrical hazards such as exposed wiring and frayed cords.
    *   Select the appropriate option that best describes the electrical hazards observed.
    *   Use the "Remarks" field to note any additional information about the electrical hazards.

- **Physical Hazards** (Select One, 5): Check for physical hazards such as sharp objects or heavy furniture.
    *   Select the appropriate option that best describes the physical hazards observed.
    *   Use the "Remarks" field to note any additional information about the physical hazards.

- **Chemical Hazards** (Select One, 6): Check for chemical hazards such as cleaning supplies and paints.
    *   Select the appropriate option that best describes the chemical hazards observed.
    *   Use the "Remarks" field to note any additional information about the chemical hazards.

- **Other Hazards** (Select One, 7): Check for any other hazards not listed above.
    *   Select the appropriate option that best describes the other hazards observed.
    *   Use the "Remarks" field to note any additional information about the other hazards.

- **Date** (Date, 8): Record the date of the last safety inspection.
    *   Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

- **Time** (Time, 9): Record the time of the last safety inspection.
    *   Enter the time in the format HH:MM:SS.

- **Remarks** (Note, 10): Use this field to note any additional comments or observations about the safety inspection.
    *   Enter any additional comments or observations about the safety inspection.

- **Assigned Tool** (Text, 11): Enter the name or description of the tool or equipment used during the safety inspection.
    *   Enter the name or description of the tool or equipment used.

- **Created By** (Text, 12): Record the name of the person who created this report.
    *   Enter the full name of the person who created this report.

# Tips
*   Always review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
*   If a hazard is noted, take immediate action to mitigate or address it to ensure the classroom safety.
