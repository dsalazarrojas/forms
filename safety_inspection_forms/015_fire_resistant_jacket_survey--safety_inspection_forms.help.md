Fire Resistant Jacket Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Resistant Jacket Survey form is designed to gather information about fire-resistant jackets and their performance characteristics. This form will help gather data on various parameters such as performance, comfort, safety features, manufacturer, and other relevant details. Please ensure that each field is filled in correctly to provide accurate information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction page, which provides context and background information on fire-resistant jackets.
2. Rate the performance of the jacket using the provided scale (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
3. If you are a user, select how you rate the comfort of the jacket.
4. In the "Safety Features" field, provide a list of all the safety features of your jacket.
5. Select the manufacturer of your jacket from the provided options (3M, Alpha PPE, 3M PPE, or Others).
6. If you want to add any additional comments about your jacket, use the "Comments" field.
7. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
8. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
9. Finally, provide your signature for authentication and completion of the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (text): This field provides context and background information on fire-resistant jackets. Please read and understand this page before proceeding.
* **Jacket Performance** (select_one): Rate the performance of the jacket using the scale Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Comfort** (number): Rate how comfortable you are with the jacket. Please enter a number to indicate your level of comfort. 
* **Safety Features** (text): Provide a list of all the safety features of your jacket. This can include features such as flame retardancy, thermal resistance, etc.
* **Manufacturer** (select_multiple): Select the manufacturer of your jacket from the provided options (3M, Alpha PPE, 3M PPE, or Others).
* **Comments** (note): If you want to add any additional comments about your jacket, use this field to provide more information.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Signature** (text): Provide your signature to authenticate and complete the survey.
