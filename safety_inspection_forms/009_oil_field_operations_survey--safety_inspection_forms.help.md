# Oil Field Operations Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about the oil field operations, including safety conditions, performance issues, weather conditions, crew size, equipment count, personnel injuries, vehicles moving, parked, operational, and maintenance. The collected data will help in identifying trends and areas for improvement in oil field operations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully based on your experience or observations during the oil field operations.
2. Fill in the form according to the instructions provided for each field.
3. Double-check your answers before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shift Conditions** (`shift_conditions`, text, optional): Describe the shift conditions experienced during the oil field operations.
* **Safety Observation** (`safety_observation`, select multiple, optional): Indicate if safety hazards were found during the shift, with options:
	+ Yes, no hazards found
	+ No, minor hazards found
	+ No, major hazards found
* **Performance Issues** (`performance_issues`, select one, optional): Report performance issues experienced during the shift, with options:
	+ No issues reported
	+ Equipment not functioning as expected
	+ Team was delayed due to issues
* **Weather Conditions** (`weather_conditions`, date, optional): Record the weather conditions observed during the shift.
* **Time Of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, optional): Report the time of day when the shift took place.
* **Crew Size** (`crew_size`, number, optional): Indicate the number of personnel in your crew.
* **Equipment Count** (`equipment_count`, number, optional): Report the number of equipment on site.
* **Personnel Injured** (`personnel_injured`, number, optional): Indicate the number of personnel injured during the shift.
* **Vehicles Moving** (`vehicles_moving`, select multiple, optional): Report on the vehicles moving with or without personnel, with options:
	+ Yes, with personnel
	+ Yes, without personnel
	+ 'No'
* **Vehicles Parked** (`vehicles_parked`, select multiple, optional): Indicate if vehicles were parked on site, with options:
	+ Yes, on site
	+ 'No'
* **Vehicles Operational** (`vehicles_operational`, select multiple, optional): Report on the vehicles operational status, with options:
	+ Yes, on site
	+ 'No'
* **Vehicles Maintenance** (`vehicles_maintenance`, select multiple, optional): Indicate if vehicles were undergoing maintenance, with options:
	+ Yes, on site
	+ 'No'

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when filling in the fields.
* If unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately to provide valuable insights for improvement.
