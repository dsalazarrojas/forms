# Food Safety Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assist in monitoring and maintaining food safety standards in your facility. It helps you evaluate the current status of your food safety protocols and ensure compliance with regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each field based on your current food safety practices and records. 
2. Use the options provided for each field to select the appropriate status.
3. Ensure that all fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Safety Standards**: Describe the current food safety standards in place at your facility.
* **Storage And Labeling**: Confirm if food products are stored and labeled correctly according to guidelines.
* **Date Last Reviewed**: Record the date of the last food safety review.
* **Date Next Review**: Schedule the next review date for the food safety standards.
* **Safety Standards Reviewed**: Confirm if the safety standards have been reviewed.
* **Safety Standards Review Status**: Confirm if the food safety standards have been reviewed and are active or inactive.
* **Corrective Actions**: Confirm if corrective actions have been taken for any food safety issues.
* **Preventive Actions**: Confirm if preventive actions have been taken to prevent food safety issues.
* **Status of Food Hazard Control**: Confirm the status of food hazard control in the facility.
* **Corrective Actions Taken**: Confirm if corrective actions have been taken for food safety issues.
* **Corrective Action Effective**: Confirm if the corrective actions have been effective.
* **Corrective Action Documented**: Confirm if the corrective actions have been documented.
* **Corrective Action Repeated**: Confirm if the corrective actions have been repeated.
* **Food Hazard Control Effective**: Confirm if food hazard control is effective.
* **Food Hazard Control Documented**: Confirm if food hazard control is documented.
* **Corrective Action Followup**: Confirm if follow-up actions have been taken for food safety issues.
* **Corrective Action Closed**: Confirm if corrective actions have been closed.
* **Preventive Action Status**: Confirm the status of preventive actions.
* **Corrective Action Status**: Confirm the status of corrective actions.
* **Food Hazard Control Status**: Confirm the status of food hazard control.
* **Corrective Action Document Status**: Confirm the status of corrective action documentation.
* **Corrective Action Frequency**: Select the frequency at which corrective actions are taken (e.g., once, every 30 days, etc.).
* **Corrective Action Frequency 2**: Select the frequency at which corrective actions are taken (e.g., once, every 30 days, etc.).
* **Food Hazard Control Frequency 1**: Confirm if food hazard control is in place.
* **Food Hazard Control Frequency 2**: Confirm if food hazard control frequency 2 is in place.
* **Followup Frequency**: Confirm if follow-up actions are taken for food safety issues.
* **Corrective Action Closed**: Confirm if corrective actions have been closed.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately to avoid any discrepancies.
* Review the form regularly to maintain up-to-date records.
* Use the form as a reference guide for future reviews and assessments.
