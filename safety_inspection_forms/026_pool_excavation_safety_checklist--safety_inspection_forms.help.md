# Pool Excavation Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pool Excavation Safety Checklist is a document used to inspect and verify the safety of a pool excavation site before construction or maintenance begins. This form helps ensure that all necessary safety checks are performed, reducing the risk of accidents and ensuring a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Complete all sections as instructed.
3. Review and verify the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pool Excavation** (`pool_excavation_safety_checklist_form`): Enter a brief description of the pool excavation site, including location and any relevant details.
* **Supervisor's Name** (`supervisor_name`): Enter the name of the person responsible for performing the inspection.
* **Supervisor's Email** (`supervisor_email`): Enter the email address of the person responsible for performing the inspection.
* **Supervisor's Phone** (`supervisor_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for performing the inspection.
* **Date Inspected** (`date_inspected`): Enter the date the pool excavation inspection was performed.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`): Enter the time the pool excavation inspection was completed.
* **Pool Type** (`pool_type`): Select the type of pool being inspected (Residential, Commercial, or Other).
* **Hazards Observed** (`hazards_observed`): List any hazards or safety concerns observed during the inspection.
* **Hazards Corrected** (`hazards_corrected`): Describe any hazards or safety concerns that were corrected or addressed.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when completing this form to ensure a safe working environment.
* Review and verify the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
* Keep a record of completed forms for future reference and to track progress.
