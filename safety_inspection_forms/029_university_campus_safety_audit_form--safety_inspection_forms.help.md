# University Campus Safety Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Campus Safety Audit Form is used to gather information about potential hazards and improvements on campus. The audit form is used to help identify areas of concern and guide further actions to improve campus safety.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the building where the audit will be conducted from the dropdown list.
2. Choose a floor number (if applicable) for the selected building.
3. Choose a zone within the building where the audit will be conducted.
4. Enter the date and time of the audit.
5. List any hazards found on site in the "Hazards" field.
6. Select the priority level of improvement required.
7. Choose if follow-up action is necessary.
8. Select a date and time for the next scheduled audit.
9. Choose the person assigned to conduct the audit and assigned tool.
10. Select a location for the next audit.
11. Enter any comments about the audit.
12. Evaluate the condition of the building, zone, and floor.
13. Select the level of action required for the next step.
14. Enter a date and time for the next action.
15. Select the person assigned to conduct the next action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Which Building?** (id: 2): Select the building where the audit will be conducted.
*   **Floor** (id: 3): Enter the floor number (if applicable) for the selected building.
*   **Zone** (id: 4): Choose a zone within the building where the audit will be conducted.
*   **Date of Audit** (id: 5): Enter the date and time of the audit.
*   **Hazards** (id: 10): List all hazards found on site. This could include any potential risks or hazards that could impact safety.
*   **Priority Improvement** (id: 11): Select the priority level of improvement required for the audit.
*   **Follow Up** (id: 12): Choose if follow-up action is necessary.
*   **Next Audit Date** (id: 13): Enter the date and time for the next scheduled audit.
*   **Next Audit Time** (id: 14): Enter the date and time for the next scheduled audit.
*   **Assigned To** (id: 15): Select the person assigned to conduct the audit.
*   **Assigned Tool** (id: 16): Select the tool assigned to conduct the audit.
*   **Next Audit Location** (id: 17): Select the location for the next audit.
*   **Comments** (id: 18): Enter any comments about the audit.
*   **Building Condition** (id: 19): Evaluate the condition of the building.
*   **Zone Condition** (id: 20): Evaluate the condition of the zone.
*   **Floor Condition** (id: 21): Evaluate the condition of the floor.
*   **Next Action** (id: 22): Select the level of action required for the next step.
*   **Next Action Date** (id: 23): Enter the date and time for the next action.
*   **Next Action Time** (id: 24): Enter the date and time for the next action.
*   **Assigned User** (id: 25): Select the person assigned to conduct the next action.

## Tips

*   Make sure to select the correct building, zone, and floor for the audit.
*   Be thorough when listing hazards on site.
*   Ensure the next audit date and time is scheduled correctly.
*   Assign the correct person and tool for the audit.
*   Evaluate the condition of the building, zone, and floor accurately.
*   Plan the next action correctly, including the level of action required and the date and time for the next action.
