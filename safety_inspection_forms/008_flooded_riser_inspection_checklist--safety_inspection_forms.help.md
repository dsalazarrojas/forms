# Flooded Riser Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users inspect flooded risers and document their status. 

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete each field with the relevant information regarding the flooded riser inspection. 
2.  Start by indicating the status of the flooded riser, either Flooded or Not Flooded. 
3.  If the riser is flooded, complete the fields regarding inspection date, location, and other details.
4.  If the riser is not flooded, fill out the corresponding fields for that status.
