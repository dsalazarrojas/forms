# Auto Lift Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auto Lift Inspection Form is a comprehensive document used to assess the safety and functionality of automotive lifts. It is used to identify any potential issues, deficiencies, or hazards, and to provide recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review and understand the form's purpose and sections.
2. Fill out the form in a clear and accurate manner.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
