# Gym Facility Safety Inspection Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a client's consent for a gym facility safety inspection. The client must review and acknowledge the form to confirm their understanding of the safety inspection process and consent to the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements of the safety inspection.
2. Select the correct options for the frequency of the inspections and the type of inspection.
3. Provide your email address and contact information for the client and the inspector.
4. Sign and date the form to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options to indicate your consent for the inspection. This confirms that you understand the safety inspection process and consent to the inspection.
* **Inspection Frequency** (`frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the frequency of the inspections (e.g., every 1 month, every 3 months, every 6 months, or every 12 months).
* **Inspection Type** (`inspection_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of inspection (e.g., General, Specialized, or Fire Safety).
* **Inspecting Officer** (`facility_inspector`, `select_one`, required): Choose the role of the inspector (e.g., Facility Manager, Facility Supervisor, or Facility Safety Officer).
* **Inspecting Officer Email** (`facility_facility_inspector_email`, `select_one`, required): Choose the email address of the inspector.
* **Facility Review** (`facility_review`, `date`, required): Date and sign the form to confirm your consent and review.
* **Facility Signature** (`facility_signature`, `date`, required): Date and sign the form to confirm your consent.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, optional): Provide your name or the name of the organization.
* **Client Organization** (`client_organization`, `text`, optional): Provide the name of your organization.
* **Facility ID** (`facility_id`, `text`, optional): Provide the facility ID number.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, `text`, optional): Provide the facility address.
* **Facility Phone** (`facility_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide the facility phone number.
* **Facility Email** (`facility_email`, `text`, optional): Provide the facility email address.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, `text`, optional): Provide your email address.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number.
* **Inspection Results** (`inspection_results`, `text`, optional): Describe your inspection results.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Provide emergency contact information.
* **Facility Notes** (`facility_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional facility notes.

Please note that some fields are optional and are only required for specific purposes, such as providing additional information for the inspector or client. Make sure to review the form carefully and provide the necessary information to ensure accurate and complete data collection.
