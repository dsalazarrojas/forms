# Fire Drill Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Fire Drill Checklist" form is a critical tool for emergency preparedness and response. It helps gather essential information from employees and emergency contacts in the event of a fire drill or actual emergency situation. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out this form thoroughly and accurately**. Make sure to double-check the information provided before submitting it.
2. **Use the "Employee Name" field to enter your name**.
3. **Enter your "Contact Info" in the "Contact Info" field. This is the best way for emergency responders to reach you in case of an emergency.
4. **Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact** in the "Emergency Contact" field.
5. **Provide the location of your work area** in the "Employee Location" field.
6. **Enter your supervisor's name** in the "Supervisor Name" field.
7. **Enter the location of your emergency contact** in the "Emergency Location" field.
8. **Enter the location where you will conduct the fire drill** in the "Drill Location" field.
9. **Provide the time of the fire drill** in the 24-hour clock format in the "Drill Time" field.
10. **Enter the date of the fire drill** in the format MM/DD/YY in the "Drill Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it is spelled.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information, including your phone number and/or email.
* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, email, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Employee Location** (Employee Location, text, required/optional): Enter your work location, including the building name and room number.
* **Supervisor Name** (Supervisor Name, text, required/optional): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (Emergency Contact Info, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Location** (Emergency Location, text, required/optional): Enter the location of your emergency contact, including the building name and room number.
* **Drill Location** (Drill Location, text, required/optional): Enter the location where you will conduct the fire drill.
* **Drill Time** (Drill Time, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the fire drill in the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 1400).
* **Drill Date** (Drill Date, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the fire drill in the format MM/DD/YY.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form carefully and thoroughly.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information you provide.
* Ensure that you have the contact information of your emergency contact.
* Keep in mind that this form is for emergency use only.
* This form is for informational purposes only and not for regular usage.
