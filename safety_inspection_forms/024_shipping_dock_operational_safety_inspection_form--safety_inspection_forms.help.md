# shipping_dock_operational_safety_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the operational safety of the shipping dock and ensure that it is conducted in a safe and secure manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the "General" section and add any general comments or observations about the dock.
2. In the "Location" section, select the specific dock being inspected from the dropdown list.
3. In the "Hazards" section, select any potential hazards that were found during the inspection.
4. In the "Findings" section, add any findings or notes about the inspection.
5. If necessary, add a follow-up date and time in the "Follow-up" section.
6. Enter the inspector's contact information in the "Inspector Name", "Inspector Email", and "Inspector Phone" fields.
7. Enter the name of the person responsible for the dock's maintenance in the "Assigned To" field.
8. Specify the equipment or tool used during the inspection in the "Assigned Tool" field.
9. Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection in the "Comments" field.
10. Sign the form digitally in the "Signature" field.
11. Enter the date and time of the inspection in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
12. Enter the geolocation information in the "Location Latitude", "Location Longitude", "Location Altitude", "Location Address", "Location City", "Location State", and "Location Zip" fields.
13. Add any additional notes about the location in the "Location Notes" field.
14. Enter the contact information of the person responsible for the dock's maintenance in the "Assigned To Name", "Assigned To Email", and "Assigned To Phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General**: A free-form text field for adding any general comments or observations about the dock.
* **Location**: A dropdown list of available docks for selection.
* **Hazards**: A list of potential hazards that were found during the inspection.
* **Findings**: A free-form text field for adding any findings or notes about the inspection.
* **Follow-up**: An optional field for adding a follow-up date and time for the inspection.
* **Inspector Name**: The contact information of the inspector.
* **Inspector Email**: The contact information of the inspector.
* **Inspector Phone**: The contact information of the inspector.
* **Assigned To**: The name of the person responsible for the dock's maintenance.
* **Assigned Tool**: The equipment or tool used during the inspection.
* **Comments**: Any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Signature**: The inspector's digital signature.
* **Date**: The date of the inspection.
* **Time**: The time of the inspection.
* **Location Latitude**: The latitude of the location.
* **Location Longitude**: The longitude of the location.
* **Location Altitude**: The altitude of the location.
* **Location Address**: The address of the location.
* **Location City**: The city of the location.
* **Location State**: The state of the location.
* **Location Zip**: The zip code of the location.
* **Location Notes**: Any additional notes about the location.
* **Assigned To Name**: The name of the person responsible for the dock's maintenance.
* **Assigned To Email**: The contact information of the person responsible for the dock's maintenance.
* **Assigned To Phone**: The contact information of the person responsible for the dock's maintenance.
