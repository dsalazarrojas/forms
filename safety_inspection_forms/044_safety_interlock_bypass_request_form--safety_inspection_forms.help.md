# Safety Interlock Bypass Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Interlock Bypass Request Form is used to request a temporary bypass of a safety interlock to perform maintenance or repairs that cannot be done while the safety interlock is enabled.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for "Maintenance Team": This field determines whether the request is from a designated maintenance team.
2. Provide a brief reason for the request in the "Reason for Request" field.
3. Enter the start time of the maintenance or repair in the "Start Time" field, using the date and time format.
4. Enter the end time of the maintenance or repair in the "End Time" field, using the time format.
5. Enter the duration of the maintenance or repair in the "Duration" field, using a numeric value.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Risk Controls" to indicate whether adequate risk controls are in place.
7. Select the risk level associated with the maintenance or repair, choosing between "High" or "Low".
8. Add any additional comments or explanations for the request in the "Comments" field.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Safety Inspector" to indicate if a safety inspector has been assigned to supervise the task.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Maintenance Team** (maintenance_team, select_one, required: false): This field helps determine whether the request is from an authorized maintenance team. Select "Yes" if the request is from a designated maintenance team.
* **Reason for Request** (reason_for_request, text, required: false): This field allows you to provide a brief explanation for the request. 
* **Start Time** (start_time, date, required: false): Enter the start time of the maintenance or repair in the format of date and time.
* **End Time** (end_time, time, required: false): Enter the end time of the maintenance or repair in the format of time.
* **Duration** (duration, number, required: false): Enter the duration of the maintenance or repair in numeric value.
* **Risk Controls** (risk_controls, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether adequate risk controls are in place by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Risk Level** (risk_level, select_one, required: false): Choose the risk level associated with the maintenance or repair, selecting between "High" or "Low".
* **Comments** (comments, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or explanations for the request.
* **Safety Inspector** (safety_inspector, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether a safety inspector has been assigned to supervise the task by selecting "Yes" or "No".
