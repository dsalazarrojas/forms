# Laboratory Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laboratory Safety Checklist is a tool used to verify that a laboratory is equipped and prepared for potential hazards and incidents. This form aims to ensure that all necessary safety equipment, procedures, and protocols are in place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question based on your knowledge and understanding of the laboratory's safety conditions.
2.  Ensure you are familiar with the relevant safety standards and protocols.
3.  Complete the form thoroughly and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Lab Safety Checklist (1)**: A brief overview of the laboratory's safety conditions. This field is not required.
*   **Lab Equipment (2)**: List the equipment available in the laboratory for safety purposes. This field is not required.
*   **Electrical Hazards (3)**: Select all hazards that are present or potential in the laboratory, such as exposed wiring, frayed cords, or electrical panels. This field is required.
*   **Fire Safety (4)**: Select one option that best describes the laboratory's fire safety measures, such as fire extinguishers available, fire alarm systems, or fire suppression systems. This field is required.
*   **Emergency Exit Routes (5)**: Describe the designated emergency exit routes in the laboratory. This field is required.
*   **Fire Extinguishers (6)**: List the location and availability of fire extinguishers in the laboratory. This field is not required.
*   **Spill Containment (7)**: Select all methods used for spill containment in the laboratory, such as spill kits or spill pads. This field is required.
*   **Lab Safety Manual (8)**: Describe the laboratory's safety manual, including its location and accessibility. This field is required.
*   **Personal Protective Equipment (9)**: List the personal protective equipment available for use in the laboratory, such as gloves, goggles, and masks. This field is not required.
*   **Chemical Handling (10)**: Describe the chemicals handled and stored in the laboratory, including their location and proper handling procedures. This field is not required.
*   **Spill Containment Supplies (11)**: List the supplies available for spill containment, such as absorbent pads or spill kits. This field is not required.
*   **Chemical Spill Procedures (12)**: Describe the procedures for responding to chemical spills in the laboratory. This field is not required.
*   **Electrical Lockout Tagging (13)**: Describe the procedures for locking out electrical equipment in the laboratory. This field is not required.
*   **Confined Spaces (14)**: Select one option that best describes the laboratory's confined spaces, such as areas with restricted access or areas where ventilation is limited. This field is required.
*   **Gas Cylinders (15)**: Select all gases stored or handled in the laboratory, such as compressed gases or cylinders. This field is required.
*   **Compressed Gases (16)**: List the compressed gases available in the laboratory, such as oxygen, nitrogen, or argon. This field is not required.
*   **Fire Hazards Supplies (17)**: List the supplies available for fire hazards, such as fire extinguishers or fire blankets. This field is not required.
*   **Spill Response Program (18)**: Describe the laboratory's spill response program, including its protocols and procedures. This field is not required.
*   **Electrical Equipment (19)**: List the electrical equipment available in the laboratory, such as generators or electrical panels. This field is not required.
*   **Chemical Hazards (20)**: Describe the chemicals handled or stored in the laboratory, including their potential hazards. This field is not required.
*   **Lab Safety Procedures (21)**: Describe the laboratory's safety procedures, including protocols for handling chemicals, electrical equipment, and other laboratory activities. This field is required.
*   **Spill Response Training (22)**: Describe the training provided for spill response in the laboratory. This field is not required.
*   **Electrical Equipment Maintenance (23)**: Describe the maintenance schedules and procedures for electrical equipment in the laboratory. This field is not required.
*   **Lab Safety Standards (24)**: Describe the laboratory's adherence to relevant safety standards and protocols. This field is not required.
*   **Lab Equipment Inspection (25)**: Select one option that best describes the laboratory's equipment inspection procedures, such as regular scheduled inspections or ad-hoc inspections. This field is required.
