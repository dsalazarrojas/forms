# Chipper Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chipper Inspection Checklist is a form used to collect information about a safety inspection of a chipper, which is a heavy machinery used for logging and construction. This form helps to identify potential safety hazards and ensure the machine is in a safe condition for operation.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct date and time of the inspection from the calendar.
2. Enter the inspector's name who performed the inspection.
3. Provide the chipper's identifier or serial number.
4. Check if safety guards are in place.
5. Check the emergency stop function of the chipper.
6. Check for hydraulic leaks, and report the severity (none, minor, or major).
7. Check if the operators are trained and certified.
8. Provide any safety improvements or repairs needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector Name**: (`inspector`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter the name of the person performing the inspection.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, `date`, required)
	+ Select the date of the inspection from the calendar.
* **Chipper Identifier** (`chipper_id`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter the asset tag or serial number of the chipper.
* **Safety Guards in Place** (`guards_in_place`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Check if safety guards are installed and functional. Choose 'True' if they are present, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Emergency Stop Function** (`emergency_stop`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Test the emergency stop operation and select 'Operational' if it works correctly, 'Faulty' if it's not working, or 'Not Present' if it's missing.
* **Hydraulic Leaks Observed** (`hydraulic_leaks`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Check for fluid leaks and select 'None' if no leaks are found, 'Minor' if there are minor leaks, or 'Major' if there are significant leaks.
* **Operator Training Current** (`operator_training_current`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Check if operators are trained and certified to use the chipper. Choose 'True' if they are, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `text`, optional)
	+ Provide any safety improvements or repairs needed to ensure the chipper is safe to operate.
## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
- Be thorough in checking for safety hazards and reporting any issues found.
- Keep a record of the inspection to track the machine's safety status over time. 

This guide is designed to be a quick reference to help you complete the Chipper Inspection Checklist form effectively and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or maintenance team for assistance.
