# Biohazard Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document biohazard safety inspections and corrective actions to ensure a safe working environment. Fill out the form to report any issues or incidents, and follow up on any required actions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `Inspector name`, providing your full name as the inspector.
2. Enter the `Inspection date`, the date on which the inspection was performed.
3. Provide the `Facility location` where the inspection was conducted (e.g., building, room, or lab number).
4. Specify the `Area or unit inspected` (e.g., hood, lab bench, storage).
5. Indicate whether `PPE compliance` was observed (Compliant, Non-compliant, Not applicable).
6. Evaluate the `Biohazard waste storage condition` as Acceptable, Needs attention, or Unacceptable.
7. Mark whether any `Spill or incident observed` (True, False).
8. If a spill or incident occurred, take `Immediate actions` if necessary and provide a brief description.
9. Check off `Corrective actions required` if any are necessary (True, False).
10. If corrective actions are required, specify `Corrective action details` (e.g., tasks, responsible party, and due dates).
11. If follow-up is necessary, set a target `Follow up date` for verification of corrective actions.
12. Add any `Inspector comments` and recommendations in the provided text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector name**:
	+ Full name of the inspector.
* **Inspection date**:
	+ Date inspection was performed.
* **Facility location**:
	+ Building and room or lab identifier.
* **Area or unit inspected**:
	+ Specific area such as hood, lab bench, or storage.
* **PPE compliance**:
	+ Select one of:
		- Compliant: Personnel using required PPE.
		- Non-compliant: Personnel not using required PPE.
		- Not applicable: PPE not required or applicable in this area.
* **Biohazard waste storage condition**:
	+ Select one of:
		- Acceptable: Waste properly stored and labeled.
		- Needs attention: Waste not properly stored or labeled.
		- Unacceptable: Waste not stored or labeled at all.
* **Spill or incident observed**:
	+ Select one of:
		- True: Evidence of spills or incidents found.
		- False: No evidence of spills or incidents.
* **Immediate actions**:
	+ Describe any actions taken at the time of inspection.
* **Corrective actions required**:
	+ Check off if corrective actions are necessary.
* **Corrective action details**:
	+ Provide tasks, responsible party, and due dates for corrective actions.
* **Follow up date**:
	+ Target date for verification of corrective actions.
* **Inspector comments**:
	+ Additional notes or recommendations.
