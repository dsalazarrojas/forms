# Innovation Process Evaluation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Innovation Process Evaluation Request Form is used to collect project information and evaluation requests from project managers and team members. It helps project sponsors and evaluators understand project status, submission method, and submission status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details in the `Contact Details` field.
2. Provide a brief description of your project in the `Project Description` field.
3. Select the `Project Type` from the options provided (Internal or External).
4. Choose the `Submission Method` from the options (Online or Offline).
5. Indicate the `Submission Status` of your project (Open or Closed).
6. If your project requires evaluation, select the type of evaluation you are requesting (Innovation, Improvement, or Implementation).
7. Fill in the `Submission Date`, `Submission Time`, and `Submission Time Zone`.
8. If applicable, indicate if your project involves `Project Manager`, `Team Member`, `Project Lead`, `Project Coordinator`, `Project Sponsor`, `Project Reviewer`, or `Submission Method`.
9. Click submit to send your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text): Please fill in your name, email, and phone number so that we can contact you for further clarification or to send updates about your project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text): Provide a brief description of your project, including its goal, objectives, and any relevant details.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_one): Choose whether your project is Internal or External. This will help us understand your project's context.
* **Submission Method** (`submission_method`, select_multiple): Select how you plan to submit your project (Online or Offline).
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one): Indicate whether your project is Open or Closed. This will help us understand your project's status.
* **Evaluation Request** (`evaluation_request`, select_multiple): Choose the type of evaluation you are requesting (Innovation, Improvement, or Implementation). This will help us understand your project's evaluation needs.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, select_one): Choose where your project is located (On-site or Off-site).
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, select_multiple): Indicate if you have any comments about your submission (Yes or No).
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, select_one): Indicate if your project has a project manager (Yes or No).
* **Team Member** (`team_member`, select_one): Indicate if you are a team member (Yes or No).
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, select_one): Indicate if you are the project lead (Yes or No).
* **Project Coordinator** (`project_coordinator`, select_one): Indicate if you are the project coordinator (Yes or No).
* **Project Sponsor** (`project_sponsor`, select_one): Indicate if you have a project sponsor (Yes or No).
* **Project Reviewer** (`project_reviewer`, select_one): Indicate if you have a project reviewer (Yes or No).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date): Fill in the date of your submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time): Fill in the time of your submission.
* **Submission Time Zone** (`submission_time_zone`, select_one): Choose the time zone of your submission.
* **Submission Time Zone**: Choose your local time zone.
* **Submission Method** (`submission_method`, select_multiple): Select how you plan to submit your project (Online or Offline).
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one): Indicate whether your project is Open or Closed.
* **Submission Note** (`submission_note`, note): Provide any additional comments or notes about your submission.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields.
* Use clear and concise language in your responses.
* Ensure that your project details are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the project sponsor or coordinator for guidance.
