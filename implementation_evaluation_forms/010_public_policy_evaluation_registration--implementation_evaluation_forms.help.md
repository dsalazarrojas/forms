# Public Policy Evaluation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a public policy evaluation. It is intended to collect information about the policy being evaluated, including its title, start date, location, evaluation type, duration, and evaluation frequency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the public policy being evaluated in the "Program Title" field.
2. Select the date when the program started from the calendar provided in the "Program Started" field.
3. Enter the location where the program took place in the "Program Location" field.
4. Choose how often you were evaluated (e.g., Yes or No) from the dropdown menu in the "Evaluation Type" field.
5. Enter the duration of the evaluation in the "Evaluation Duration" field.
6. Choose how often the policy is evaluated (e.g., Yes or No) from the dropdown menu in the "Evaluation Periodicity" field.
7. Enter the frequency of evaluation (e.g., a number of times per year) in the "Evaluation Frequency" field.
8. Select the program evaluators from the dropdown menu in the "Program Evaluators" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form**: Enter a brief title for the evaluation, such as "Evaluation of XYZ Policy".
* **Program Title**: Enter the official title of the public policy being evaluated.
* **Program Started**: Select the start date of the program using the calendar provided.
* **Program Location**: Enter the location where the program took place.
* **Evaluation Type**: Choose how the policy was evaluated (e.g., Yes or No).
* **Evaluation Duration**: Enter the number of days, weeks, or months the policy was evaluated.
* **Evaluation Periodicity**: Choose how often the policy is evaluated (e.g., Yes or No).
* **Evaluation Frequency**: Enter how often the policy is evaluated (e.g., number of times per year).
* **Program Evaluators**: Select the program evaluators from the dropdown menu (e.g., Yes or No).
