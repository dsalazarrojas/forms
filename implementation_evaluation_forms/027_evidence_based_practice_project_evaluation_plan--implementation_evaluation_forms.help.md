# Evidence Based Practice Project Evaluation Plan - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Evidence Based Practice Project Evaluation Plan is a tool used to assess the success and effectiveness of your project. It will help project leads and team members evaluate the project's progress, identify areas of improvement, and provide guidance on future project development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the project title and category in the respective fields.
2. Select the project lead and team members involved in the project.
3. Enter the evaluation date and start and end dates (if applicable).
4. Select the evidence-based practices and evaluation methods used in the project.
5. Choose the evaluation frequency and project status.
6. Enter any notes or email, phone, and address information (if applicable).
7. Provide project notes and evaluation criteria.
8. Enter evaluation outcomes and recommendations (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title (1)**: Enter the title of your project.
* **Category (2)**: Select the category that best describes your project. The options are "Implementation Evaluation Forms" and "Project Evaluation Forms".
* **Project Lead (3)**: Enter the name of the person leading your project.
* **Team Members (4)**: Select the team members involved in your project.
* **Evaluation Date (5)**: Enter the date when the evaluation will take place.
* **Start Date (6)**: Enter the start date of your project (if applicable).
* **End Date (7)**: Enter the end date of your project (if applicable).
* **Evidence Based Practices (8)**: Select the evidence-based practices used in your project.
* **Evaluation Methods (9)**: Select the evaluation methods used in your project.
* **Evaluation Frequency (10)**: Choose the frequency of evaluation for your project.
* **Project Status (11)**: Enter the current status of your project.
* **Notes (12)**: Enter any additional notes about your project.
* **Email (13)**: Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Phone (14)**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Address (15)**: Enter your project's address (if applicable).
* **Project Notes (16)**: Enter any additional project notes.
* **Evaluation Criteria (17)**: Enter the evaluation criteria for your project.
* **Evaluation Outcomes (18)**: Enter any evaluation outcomes for your project.
* **Evaluation Recommendations (19)**: Enter any evaluation recommendations for your project.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information about your project.
* Review and revise your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask for help.
