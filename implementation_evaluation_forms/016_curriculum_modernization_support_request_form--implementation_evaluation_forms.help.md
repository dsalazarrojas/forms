# Curriculum Modernization Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user feedback and support requests related to the implementation of a curriculum modernization project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Click on "Submit" at the end of the form to send your request.
2.  Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
3.  Provide any additional comments or information in the "Comments" field if you have any further thoughts or details to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): Please enter your name and any other relevant user information that you'd like to share. This could include your role or department within the institution.
* **Curriculum Information** (`curriculum_info`, text, optional): Describe the curriculum that you're requesting support with. Be as specific as possible about the topic, course, or program.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide contact information so that we can reach out to you with any questions or updates. This could include your email, phone number, or department name.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): This is an optional field where you can add any additional comments or suggestions you have for the curriculum modernization project.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to receive updates or follow up on your request, please enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number if you'd like to receive a call or text from us with any updates.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Clicking on this button will send your request to us.

## Tips
* Please make sure to proofread your submission before clicking on "Submit" to ensure that all the information is accurate and complete.
* If you have any attachments or files to share, please attach them to your submission or mention them in the "Comments" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us directly.
* We'll get back to you as soon as possible with any updates or next steps.
