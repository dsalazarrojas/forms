The form's purpose is to help users develop a logic model for their project or program. A logic model is a visual representation of a project or program's goals, activities, outputs, and outcomes. It's used to identify and analyze the logical relationships between these elements. This form is designed to guide the user through the process of defining their project's goal, activities, outputs, and outcomes, and creating objective statements that describe the desired results of their project.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the "What is your goal" field with a brief description of your project or program's main objective.
2.  List the activities that will be performed to achieve your goal.
3.  Describe the outputs or products that will be produced as a result of these activities.
4.  Identify the outcomes that you expect to happen as a result of your project or program.
5.  List any assumptions that may impact your project or program.
6.  Create objective statements for each of the 10 objective_statement fields, which describe the desired results of your project or program.

Field-by-Field Explanation:

*   **What is your goal** (text, required: false)
    This field is where you should describe your project or program's main objective. Be as specific as possible and keep it brief.
*   **Activities** (text, required: false)
    In this field, list the activities that will be performed to achieve your goal. This could include tasks, events, or any other actions that are essential to your project.
*   **Outputs** (text, required: false)
    Describe the products or results that will be produced as a result of your activities. This could include reports, products, services, or any other output that is relevant to your project.
*   **Outcomes** (text, required: false)
    Identify the outcomes that you expect to happen as a result of your project or program. This could include changes in behavior, improvements in performance, or any other result that is desired.
*   **Assumptions** (text, required: false)
    In this field, list any assumptions that may impact your project or program. This could include assumptions about the target audience, resources, or any other factor that may affect the outcome of your project.
*   **Objective Statement 1** (text, required: false)
