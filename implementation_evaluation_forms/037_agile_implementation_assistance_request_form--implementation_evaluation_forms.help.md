# Agile Implementation Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help individuals or teams seek assistance with their Agile implementation. Please fill out this form to provide information about your project, team, and needs, and our team will be happy to offer tailored assistance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Contact Information**: Please fill out the "Requestor Name", "Requestor Email", and "Requestor Phone" fields with your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. **Project Details**: Enter the "Project Name" and "Team Size" fields with your project's name and the number of people on your team. Choose the current "Current Agile Implementation Stage" that best describes your team's current Agile implementation stage.
3. **Assistance Needed**: Select your "Primary Assistance Need" and list any "Specific Agile Challenges" you are facing. Describe your "Desired Outcomes" for seeking Agile assistance.
4. **Support and Resources**: If applicable, indicate if you have "Internal Agile Expertise" available within your team. Choose the "Executive Support Level" for this initiative. If you have previous experience with Agile methodologies, describe it in the "Previous Agile Experience" field.
5. **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional information about your Agile implementation needs, please provide it in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Requestor Email** (`requestor_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Requestor Phone** (`requestor_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Department/Team** (`department_team`, text, required): Enter your department or team name.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your project needing Agile assistance.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, required): Enter the number of people on your team.
* **Current Agile Implementation Stage** (`current_agile_stage`, select_one, required): Choose the current stage of your Agile implementation (Just Starting, Early Implementation, Mid-Implementation, Advanced Implementation, Optimization Phase).
* **Agile Framework Currently Used** (`agile_framework_currently_used`, select_one, required): Choose the Agile framework you are currently using (Scrum, Kanban, SAFe, LeSS, XP, Mixed Approach, Not Using Any).
* **Primary Assistance Need** (`primary_assistance_need`, select_one, required): Select your primary need for Agile assistance (Training, Coaching, Implementation Guidance, Process Optimization, Team Facilitation, Tool Selection, Metrics Definition, Other).
* **Specific Agile Challenges** (`specific_agile_challenges`, select_multiple, required): List any specific Agile challenges you are facing (Team Resistance, Lack of Management Support, Unclear Requirements, Scaling Issues, Tool Complexity, Communication Problems, Estimation Difficulties, Undefined Roles, Other).
* **Desired Outcomes** (`desired_outcomes`, text, required): Describe the outcomes you hope to achieve with Agile assistance.
* **Timeline Expectations** (`timeline_expectations`, select_one, required): Choose when you need this assistance to begin (Immediately, Within 2 Weeks, Within 1 Month, Within 3 Months, No Urgent Timeline).
* **Budget Availability** (`budget_availability`, select_one, optional): If applicable, choose your budget range for Agile assistance (Under $5,000, $5,000-$15,000, $15,000-$30,000, $30,000-$50,000, Over $50,000, Budget Not Determined).
* **Internal Agile Expertise** (`internal_agile_expertise`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have internal Agile expertise available within your team (Yes, Certified Scrum Master, Yes, Agile Coach, Some Agile Experience, No Internal Expertise, Not Sure).
* **Executive Support Level** (`executive_support_level`, select_one, required): Choose the level of executive support for this initiative (Strong Support, Moderate Support, Limited Support, No Support, Unknown).
* **Previous Agile Experience** (`previous_agile_experience`, text, optional): Describe your team's previous experience with Agile methodologies.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional information about your Agile implementation needs, provide it here.

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible to help us understand your specific needs.
* If you have any attachments (e.g., diagrams, screenshots), feel free to include them in the "Additional Comments" field.
* Our team will review your form submission and provide tailored assistance based on your needs.
