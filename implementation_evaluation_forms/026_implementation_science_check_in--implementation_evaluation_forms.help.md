# Implementation Science Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Implementation Science Check In form is designed to gather information from project team members about the implementation status of a project. This form helps to identify potential barriers, strategies, and support needs, and track project outcomes and quality improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions truthfully, based on your current project status.
2. For each field, select the most accurate option or enter your response in the text box.
3. Make sure to save your progress regularly, as you may need to access your previous responses later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, text, optional): Describe the current status of your project. If you're unsure, just let us know that you're not sure yet.
* **Barriers** (`barriers`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the barriers that apply to your project.
	+ Yes: You're experiencing challenges that hinder your project progress.
	+ No: You're not experiencing any significant challenges at this time.
* **Strategies** (`strategies`, text, optional): Describe any strategies you've implemented or plan to implement to overcome the barriers you've identified.
* **Support Needs** (`support_needs`, select_one, optional): Let us know if you have any support needs for your project.
	+ Yes: You would like support on your project.
	+ No: You don't need any support at this time.
* **Project Outcomes** (`project_outcomes`, text, optional): Describe the expected outcomes of your project. If you're unsure, just let us know that you're not sure yet.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the team members who are working on your project.
	+ Yes: Team members are actively involved in the project.
	+ No: There are no team members actively involved in the project at this time.
* **Quality Improvement** (`quality_improvement`, select_one, optional): Evaluate the quality of your project.
	+ Good: The project is progressing well and meeting expectations.
	+ Poor: The project is not progressing well and is behind schedule.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, date, optional): Enter the date for your next scheduled check-in or follow-up meeting.
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, number, optional): Enter the time spent on your project so far.
* **Total Hours** (`total_hours`, number, optional): Enter the total number of hours spent on your project so far.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your project.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further updates.
