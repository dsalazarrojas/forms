# RPA Implementation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RPA Implementation Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback on the success and areas for improvement of Robotic Process Automation (RPA) projects. This form helps us understand how well the project was executed, what worked well, and what could be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: `automation_outcomes`, `project_satisfaction`, and `email`. These fields are marked as required and are necessary for us to receive your feedback.
2. Select one or more outcomes from the `automation_outcomes` field, depending on what happened during the project.
3. Choose your level of satisfaction with the project from the `project_satisfaction` field.
4. If you have any email address, please enter it in the `email` field.
5. Fill in any additional feedback you have in the `note` fields, if applicable.
6. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **RPA Implementation Feedback Form** (`title`): This is the title of the form, which is displayed at the top of the form. You cannot modify this field.
* **Automation Outcomes** (`automation_outcomes`): This field allows you to select one or more outcomes that occurred during the project. Outcomes are:
	+ Outcome 1
	+ Outcome 2
	+ Outcome 3
	+ ... (Up to 14 outcomes in total)
* **Project Satisfaction** (`project_satisfaction`): This field allows you to rate your level of satisfaction with the project. You can choose from:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Email** (`email`): This field is for you to enter your email address, if you have one.
* **Phone** (`phone`): This field is for you to enter your phone number, but it's not required.
* **Additional Feedback 1-14** (`note_1`, `note_2`, ..., `note_14`): These fields are for you to provide any additional feedback you have on the project. You can use them to elaborate on your answers or provide more information.
* **Date** (`date`): This field is for you to enter the date of the project implementation.
* **Time** (`time`): This field is for you to enter the time of the project implementation.
* **Future Improvement** (`future_improvement`): This field is for you to provide any feedback on how to improve the project in the future.
