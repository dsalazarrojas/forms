# Healthcare Policy Implementation Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the implementation of healthcare policies in various settings. It helps to assess the effectiveness, impact, and challenges associated with policy implementations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with relevant information about the healthcare policy you are evaluating.
2. Select the appropriate options for each field based on your knowledge and understanding of the policy implementation.
3. Answer each question in a clear and concise manner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. healthcare policy evaluation** (`healthcare_policy_implementation_evaluation`, text, required: false): 
  This field is used to briefly describe the policy evaluation.
* **2. policy name** (`policy_name`, text, required: false): 
  Enter the name of the policy being evaluated.
* **3. policy category** (`policy_category`, text, required: false): 
  Describe the category of the policy (e.g. patient safety, quality improvement, etc.)
* **4. policy owner** (`policy_owner`, text, required: false): 
  Identify the person or team responsible for the policy implementation.
* **5. policy effectiveness** (`policy_effectiveness`, select_one, required: false): 
  Evaluate the overall effectiveness of the policy (Effective or Not Effective).
* **6. policy impact** (`policy_impact`, text, required: false): 
  Describe the impact of the policy on patients, staff, or the organization.
* **7. policy outcomes** (`policy_outcomes`, text, required: false): 
  Enter any positive or negative outcomes associated with the policy implementation.
* **8. policy challenges** (`policy_challenges`, text, required: false): 
  Identify any challenges or barriers encountered during policy implementation.
* **9. policy actions taken** (`policy_actions_taken`, text, required: false): 
  Describe any actions taken to implement the policy.
* **10. policy actions planned** (`policy_actions_planned`, text, required: false): 
  Outline any planned actions for policy implementation or review.
* **11. policy communication plan** (`policy_communication_plan`, text, required: false): 
  Describe the plan for communication with stakeholders regarding the policy.
* **12. policy evaluation** (`policy_evaluation`, date, required: false): 
  Enter the date of policy evaluation.
* **13. policy review date** (`policy_review_date`, date, required: false): 
  Enter the date of policy review.
* **14. policy review frequency** (`policy_review_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false): 
  Select the frequency of policy review (Quarterly, Half-yearly, Yearly).
* **15. policy rationale** (`policy_rationale`, text, required: false): 
  Enter the rationale for policy implementation or review.
* **16. policy goals and objectives** (`policy_goals_and_objectives`, text, required: false): 
  Describe the goals and objectives of the policy.
* **17. policy benefits** (`policy_benefits`, text, required: false): 
  Identify the benefits of the policy implementation.
* **18. policy challenges and barriers** (`policy_challenges_and_barriers`, text, required: false): 
  Describe any challenges or barriers associated with policy implementation.
* **19. policy sustainability** (`policy_sustainability`, select_one, required: false): 
  Evaluate the sustainability of the policy (Sustainable or Not Sustainable).
* **20. policy sustainability rationale** (`policy_sustainability_rationale`, text, required: false): 
  Enter the rationale for policy sustainability.
* **21. policy recommended actions** (`policy_recommended_actions`, text, required: false): 
  Outline any recommended actions for policy implementation or review.
* **22. policy recommended actions planned** (`policy_recommended_actions_planned`, text, required: false): 
  Describe any recommended actions for policy implementation or review.
* **23. policy communication plan** (`policy_communication_plan`, text, required: false): 
  Describe the plan for communication with stakeholders regarding policy implementation or review.
* **24. policy evaluation plan** (`policy_evaluation_plan`, text, required: false): 
  Outline the plan for policy evaluation or review.
* **25. policy review plan** (`policy_review_plan`, text, required: false): 
  Describe the plan for policy review.
