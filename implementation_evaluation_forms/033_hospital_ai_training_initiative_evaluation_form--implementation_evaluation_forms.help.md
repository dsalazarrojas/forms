# Hospital AI Training Initiative Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluations from users on their experience with AI models in a hospital setting. It aims to collect both quantitative and qualitative data to improve the performance and functionality of these models in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete this form to share your experiences with AI models in the hospital setting.
2. Select the relevant fields based on your feedback, and ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Use the "Overall Success" and "Recommendation" fields to rate the performance of the AI models you've interacted with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Experience** (id: 1, required): This field is for providing an overall impression of your experience with the AI model in the hospital setting.
* **Primary Goals and Objectives** (id: 2, required): Describe the primary goals and objectives you aimed to achieve with the AI model.
* **AI Model Performance** (id: 3, optional): If you've interacted with multiple AI models, select the one that best performed during your experience.
* **Note1** (id: 4, optional): Additional note field for any additional comments or feedback.
* **Note2** (id: 5, optional): Additional note field for any additional comments or feedback.
* **Note3** (id: 6, optional): Additional note field for any additional comments or feedback.
* **Note4** (id: 7, optional): Additional note field for any additional comments or feedback.
* **Note5** (id: 8, optional): Additional note field for any additional comments or feedback.
* **Note6** (id: 9, optional): Additional note field for any additional comments or feedback.
* **AI Model Comparison** (id: 10, required): List the AI models you've interacted with, selecting the ones that met your expectations.
* **Selected Model** (id: 11, required): Select the model you've selected for the project.
* **Note7** (id: 12, optional): Additional note field for any additional comments or feedback.
* **AI Model Performance Satisfactory** (id: 13, optional): Assess whether the performance of the AI model met your expectations.
* **Documentation Clarity** (id: 14, optional): Select whether the documentation provided with the AI model was clear and understandable.
* **Overall Success** (id: 15, required): Rate the overall success of your experience with the AI model.
* **Recommendation** (id: 16, required): Select whether you recommend the use of the AI model in the hospital setting.
* **Suggestions** (id: 17, optional): Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Interaction Frequency** (id: 18, optional): Specify the frequency of interaction with the AI model.
* **Model Recommendation** (id: 19, optional): Select whether you recommend using the AI model as a model for future projects.

Note: The optional fields can be skipped, and users should not be required to fill them in. However, providing feedback in these fields can enhance the evaluation process.
