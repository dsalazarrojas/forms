# implementation_training_session_record - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and evaluate the details of an implementation training session, including the number of participants, session date and time, location, and various outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Main Topic" field to specify the main topic of the session.
2. Enter the "Number of Participants" field to indicate how many people attended the session.
3. Choose the "Session Date" field to select the date of the session.
4. Enter the "Session Time" field to specify the start time of the session.
5. Select the "Session Location" field to indicate where the session was held.
6. Check the "Participants Experienced" field to indicate whether participants were able to fully engage with the material.
7. Check the "Reachable for Questions" field to indicate whether participants were able to ask questions during the session.
8. Check the "Topics Relevant" field to indicate whether the topics covered were relevant to the participants' needs.
9. Check the "Can Provide Feedback" field to indicate whether participants were able to provide feedback to the trainer.
10. Select the "Session Notes" field to record any important notes about the session.
11. Check the "Can Reach Participants" field to indicate whether participants were able to reach out to each other after the session.
12. Check the "Topics Up-to-Date" field to indicate whether the topics covered were up-to-date with current events.
13. Check the "Follow-up Questions" field to indicate whether participants were able to follow-up with the trainer after the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Topic**: The main topic of the session, briefly describe the main subject matter covered during the session.
* **Number of Participants**: The number of people who attended the session.
* **Session Date**: The date of the session, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Session Time**: The start time of the session, in the format hh:mm AM/PM.
* **Session Location**: The location where the session was held, e.g. a meeting room, online platform, etc.
* **Participants Experienced**: Whether participants were able to fully engage with the material.
* **Reachable for Questions**: Whether participants were able to ask questions during the session.
* **Topics Relevant**: Whether the topics covered were relevant to the participants' needs.
* **Can Provide Feedback**: Whether participants were able to provide feedback to the trainer.
* **Session Notes**: Any important notes about the session, such as notable discussions or issues that arose.
* **Can Reach Participants**: Whether participants were able to reach out to each other after the session.
* **Topics Up-to-Date**: Whether the topics covered were up-to-date with current events.
* **Follow-up Questions**: Whether participants were able to follow-up with the trainer after the session.
