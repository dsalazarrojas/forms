# Care Continuity Improvement Project Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Care Continuity Improvement Project Registration Form is designed to collect information about Care Continuity Improvement Projects, a type of project aimed at improving care services in healthcare settings. This form gathers essential details to facilitate project registration and tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in your project title, lead organization, and project start date.
3. Provide your contact information, including your name, role, and email.
4. Specify the target population that the project will serve.
5. Indicate the expected outcomes of the project.
6. If applicable, mention the resources needed for the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required)
This field collects the official name of your project. Please enter a clear and concise title that accurately represents your project.

### **Lead Organization** (`lead_organization`, text, required)
Enter the name of the primary implementing organization responsible for your project.

### **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, required)
Select the anticipated start date of your project.

### **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, date, optional)
If known, enter the expected completion date of your project.

### **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required)
Enter your name and role in the project.

### **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required)
Provide your email for project communications.

### **Target Population** (`target_population`, text, required)
Describe the population that your project aims to serve.

### **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, text, required)
List the key measurable outcomes of your project.

### **Resources Needed** (`resources_needed`, text, optional)
If applicable, mention the staffing, technology, or budget resources required for your project.

## Tips

* Be thorough when filling in your project details to ensure accurate registration.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions or approvals before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out to [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
