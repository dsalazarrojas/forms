# Smart Label Implementation Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from organizations interested in implementing smart labels. It is part of the grant's application process and will be reviewed by the grant administrators to assess your eligibility.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" if you are interested in applying for the grant.
2. Fill in your name as the applicant.
3. Enter your address.
4. Provide your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Specify the grant amount you are applying for.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" if you are applying for a specific type of grant.
8. Enter the date you plan to start implementing smart labels.
9. Enter the date you plan to finish implementing smart labels.
10. Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate your current status of smart label implementation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form** (Select "Yes", Required): Select "Yes" if you are interested in applying for the grant. This will determine if you are eligible to proceed with the application.
* **Applicant Name** (Text, Optional): Fill in your name as the applicant. This information will be used to contact you regarding your application.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your address. This will help us to contact you regarding your application.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Provide your email address. This will be used to contact you regarding your application.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number. This will be used to contact you regarding your application.
* **Grant Amount** (Number, Optional): Specify the grant amount you are applying for. This will help us to assess your eligibility.
* **Grant Type** (Select "Yes" or "No", Optional): Select "Yes" or "No" if you are applying for a specific type of grant. This will help us to determine your eligibility.
* **Grant Period** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you plan to start implementing smart labels. This will help us to assess your timeline.
* **Implementation Start** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you plan to finish implementing smart labels. This will help us to assess your progress.
* **Implementation End** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you plan to finish implementing smart labels. This will help us to assess your completion time.
* **Status** (Select "Active" or "Inactive", Optional): Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate your current status of smart label implementation. This will help us to assess your current state.
