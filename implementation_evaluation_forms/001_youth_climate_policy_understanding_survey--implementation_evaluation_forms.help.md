# Youth Climate Policy Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to assess an individual's understanding of youth climate policy, its implementation, and its effectiveness in addressing climate change.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer the questions as they appear.
2. Select the best answer that corresponds to your thoughts, beliefs, or experiences.
3. You can choose more than one option when prompted.
4. Please answer all questions, but do not feel obligated to answer questions that you are not comfortable answering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (id: 1, type: note): This is a brief overview of the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Climate Change** (id: 2, type: select_one, options: high, medium, low): Select one of the options to express your level of concern about climate change.
* **Climate Policies** (id: 3, type: select_multiple, options: national, local, global): Select all the policy levels that you believe are effective in addressing climate change.
* **Sources of Info** (id: 4, type: select_multiple, options: family, friends, school, social media, internet, government): Select all the sources of information that you use to learn about climate policies.
* **Climate Policy** (id: 5, type: select_one, options: strong, weak): Select one of the options to express your confidence in the current climate policy.
* **Policy Priority** (id: 6, type: number): Select a number to indicate your priority for addressing climate change.
* **Policy Solutions** (id: 7, type: select_multiple, options: reduce consumption, change consumption habits, invest in re, transition to renew, other): Select all the policy solutions that you think are effective in addressing climate change.
* **Policy Support** (id: 8, type: select_multiple, options: True, False): Select one of the options to express your level of support for climate policy.
* **Communication** (id: 9, type: select_multiple, options: government, social media, school, family, friends): Select all the channels that you use to learn about climate policy.
* **Policy Implementation** (id: 10, type: select_one, options: good, bad): Select one of the options to express your opinion on climate policy implementation.
* **Policy Effectiveness** (id: 11, type: number): Select a number to indicate your level of confidence in the effectiveness of climate policy.
* **Policy Relevance** (id: 12, type: number): Select a number to indicate your relevance of climate policy.
* **Policy Sustainability** (id: 13, type: number): Select a number to indicate your level of commitment to climate policy.
* **Policy Inclusiveness** (id: 14, type: number): Select a number to indicate your level of inclusiveness of climate policy.
* **Policy Ambitiousness** (id: 15, type: number): Select a number to indicate your level of ambitiousness of climate policy.
* **Policy Clarity** (id: 16, type: number): Select a number to indicate your level of understanding of climate policy.
* **Policy Flexibility** (id: 17, type: number): Select a number to indicate your level of flexibility of climate policy.
* **Policy Participatation** (id: 18, type: text): Write a short description of your participation in climate policy.
* **Policy Innovation** (id: 19, type: number): Select a number to indicate your level of innovation of climate policy.
* **Policy Effectiveness 2** (id: 20, type: number): Select a number to indicate your level of effectiveness of climate policy.
* **Policy Impact** (id: 21, type: select_one, options: positive, negative): Select one of the options to express your opinion on the impact of climate policy.
* **Climate Policy Satisfaction** (id: 22, type: number): Select a number to indicate your level of satisfaction with climate policy.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately.
* You can skip questions that do not apply to you.
* Please do not use this survey as a formal, scientifically validated tool.
