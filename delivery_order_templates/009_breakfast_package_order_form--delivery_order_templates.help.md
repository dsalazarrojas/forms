# Breakfast Package Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Breakfast Package Order Form is designed to gather information from businesses and organizations placing orders for breakfast packages. This form includes fields for contact information, delivery details, and preferences for the package contents and presentation.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company or organization's name in the "Company or Organization Name" field.
2. If applicable, enter the name of the department responsible for placing the order in the "Department" field.
3. Enter the name of the person placing the order in the "Contact Person Name" field.
4. Enter the email address of the person placing the order in the "Contact Email Address" field. This will be used for order confirmation.
5. Enter the phone number of the person placing the order in the "Contact Phone Number" field. This will be used for delivery coordination.
6. Enter the complete delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
7. Select the date for the delivery of the breakfast package in the "Delivery Date" field.
8. Choose a preferred delivery time in the "Preferred Delivery Time" field.
9. Enter the number of people to be served in the "Number of People" field.
10. Select the type of breakfast package from the "Breakfast Package Type" field.
11. Choose the package components that should be included in the "Package Components" field.
12. Select any dietary variations or special requests in the "Dietary Variations Needed" field.
13. Select the serving style and serving ware preferences in the "Serving Style" and "Serving Ware" fields.
14. Choose the type of beverage service in the "Beverage Service" field.
15. Enter any special requests or instructions in the "Special Requests or Instructions" field.
16. If necessary, indicate whether setup and cleanup services are required in the "Setup and Cleanup" field.
17. Finally, choose an estimated budget range from the "Estimated Total Budget" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company or Organization Name** (1, required): Enter the name of your business or organization.
* **Department** (2, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the department responsible for placing the order.
* **Contact Person Name** (3, required): Enter the name of the person placing the order.
* **Contact Email Address** (4, required): Enter the email address of the person placing the order for order confirmation.
* **Contact Phone Number** (5, required): Enter the phone number of the person placing the order for delivery coordination.
* **Delivery Address** (6, required): Enter the complete delivery address.
* **Delivery Date** (7, required): Select the date for the delivery of the breakfast package.
* **Preferred Delivery Time** (8, required): Choose a preferred delivery time.
* **Number of People** (9, required): Enter the number of people to be served.
* **Breakfast Package Type** (10, required): Select the type of breakfast package from the options provided.
* **Package Components** (11, required): Choose the package components that should be included in the order.
* **Dietary Variations Needed** (12, optional): Select any dietary variations or special requests.
* **Serving Style** (13, required): Select the serving style preference.
* **Serving Ware** (14, optional): Select the serving ware preference.
* **Beverage Service** (15, optional): Choose the type of beverage service.
* **Special Requests or Instructions** (16, optional): Enter any special requests or instructions.
* **Setup and Cleanup** (17, optional): Indicate whether setup and cleanup services are required.
* **Estimated Total Budget** (18, optional): Choose an estimated budget range from the options provided.
