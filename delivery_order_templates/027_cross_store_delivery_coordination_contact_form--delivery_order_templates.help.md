# Cross Store Delivery Coordination Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to request delivery coordination with stores for receiving or sending goods.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by selecting the page "Request for delivery coordination".
2. In the "Request for delivery coordination" page, fill out the form as follows:
	* Enter your name as per your identification. This field is required.
	* Enter the customer name for whom the goods are being delivered or picked up. This field is required.
	* Enter the name of the store where you need assistance with delivery. This field is required.
3. On the next page (page 2), fill out the form as follows:
	* Choose the date of delivery. This field is required and should be in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'. 
	* Select the time of delivery. This field is required and should be in the format 'HH:MM'. 
	* Enter the address or location of the store where delivery will occur. This field is required.
	* Choose the status of the delivery. This field is required and should be set to 'Active' if the delivery is ongoing or 'Inactive' if the delivery is not happening.
	* Choose the status of the store. This field is required and should be set to 'Active' if the store is open, 'Inactive' if the store is closed.
	* Enter any additional details regarding the delivery. This field is not required but recommended for extra information.
	* Enter your contact details so that we can get in touch with you if needed. This field is required.
	* Add any additional notes about your contact. This field is not required but recommended for extra information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request for delivery coordination**: This is the first page of the form and is used for the main information.
	+ **Customer name**: Enter the name of the customer for whom the delivery is being made or picked up.
	+ **Store name**: Enter the name of the store where you need assistance with delivery.
* **Contact information**: Enter your contact details so that we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact notes**: This field is for extra information about your contact.
