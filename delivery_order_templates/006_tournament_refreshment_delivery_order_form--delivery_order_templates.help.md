# Tournament Refreshment Delivery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tournament Refreshment Delivery Order Form is a digital form designed for ordering refreshments for a tournament event. This form is used to collect important details from the customer to ensure accurate order processing and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Event Name** field with the correct name of the tournament event.
2. Select the **Delivery Time** date and time for the refreshment delivery.
3. Enter the **Location** where the refreshments will be delivered.
4. Optionally, provide additional **Payment Details** if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (event_name): Enter the name of the tournament event.
* **Delivery Time** (delivery_time): Select the date and time for the refreshment delivery.
* **Location** (location): Enter the address or location where the refreshments will be delivered.
* **Payment Details** (payment_details): If payment is required, enter the payment information here.
