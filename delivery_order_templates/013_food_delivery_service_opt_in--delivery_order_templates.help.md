# Food Delivery Service Opt In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Food Delivery Service Opt In" form is used to collect specific information from customers to facilitate food delivery services. This form captures details that are essential for restaurants to prepare and deliver food as per customer preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Food Delivery Service Opt In" form link.
2. A new window or tab will open with the form.
3. Fill in the required fields as prompted, which are marked as "required" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Restaurant Preferences**: This field allows you to specify any food preferences or allergies you have, such as vegetarian, non-vegetarian, or gluten-free options.
* **Order Details**: Enter the specific order details such as the type of food you want to order (e.g., takeout, dine-in) or any other special requests.
* **Customer Preferences**: Indicate any special preferences you have, such as extra sauces or no pickles.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter your delivery address to ensure accurate and prompt delivery.
* **Special Instructions**: If you have any special requests or instructions for the delivery person, type them in this field.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number to ensure that we can contact you in case of any issues with your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any specific requests or instructions, use the "special_instructions" field to communicate them to us.
* Double-check your phone number to ensure that we can reach out to you in case of any issues.
