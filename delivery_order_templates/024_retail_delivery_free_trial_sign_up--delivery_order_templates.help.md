# Retail Delivery Free Trial Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to be a fast and easy way for retailers and delivery teams to sign up for a free trial of delivery services. It collects contact information, confirms eligibility, and gathers delivery preferences to ensure a smooth trial experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Confirm that you are eligible for the trial by selecting "I am eligible" from the "Confirm Eligibility" field.
2. Choose your preferred delivery days and times from the "Delivery Preferences" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (delivery_order_templates_registration_forms, select_one): This field is not required and allows you to select a category for your trial sign up. You can choose from "delivery order templates" or "registration forms".
* **Description** (Retail Delivery Free Trial Sign Up Form, note): This field is not required and provides a brief description of the form's purpose.
* **Confirm Eligibility** (Confirm Eligibility, select_multiple): This field is not required and allows you to confirm your eligibility for the trial. Choose "I am eligible" to proceed.
* **Delivery Preferences** (Delivery Preferences, select_multiple): This field is not required and allows you to select your preferred delivery days and times. Choose from Monday to Sunday.
* **Date** (Date, date): This field is not required and allows you to select a date for your delivery.
* **Time** (Time, time): This field is not required and allows you to select a time for your delivery.
* **Email** (Email, email): This field is not required but recommended. Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about your trial.
* **Phone** (Phone, text): This field is not required but recommended. Enter your phone number to receive updates and notifications about your trial.
