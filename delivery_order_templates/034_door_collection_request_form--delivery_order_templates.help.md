# door_collection_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "door_collection_request_form" form is designed to collect relevant information about the delivery process. This form is intended for use by warehouse or logistics teams to document and communicate details about pickup and delivery events.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have all the necessary information before completing this form.
- Select the correct delivery method (Pickup or Delivery) from the "Delivery Method" dropdown field.
- Enter the delivery date and time in the "Delivery Date" and "Pickup Time" fields.
- Enter the pickup location and contact number in the "Pickup Location" and "Contact Number" fields, respectively.
- Provide any additional notes about the delivery in the "Additional Notes" field.
- Make sure to review all the fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Delivery Date
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you want the item to be delivered.

### Time of Day
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, optional): Enter the time you want the item to be delivered.

### Pickup Location
* **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the item will be picked up.

### Delivery Name
* **Delivery Name** (`delivery_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or entity receiving the delivery.

### Pickup Time
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the item will be collected from the delivery location.

### Delivery Method
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, optional): Select the method of delivery (Pickup or Delivery).

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter a contact number for the delivery recipient.

### Additional Notes
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about the delivery.
