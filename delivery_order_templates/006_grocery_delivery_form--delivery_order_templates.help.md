# Grocery Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grocery Delivery Form is used to collect information from customers for a smooth and successful grocery delivery experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required information, marked as "required".
2.  Provide a valid email address in the "delivery_email" field if you want to receive updates about your delivery.
3.  If you need to specify a phone number, enter it in the "delivery_phone" field.
4.  Select a valid payment method from the "payment_gateway" field (e.g. Stripe or PayPal).
5.  Enter the date for your delivery in the "delivery_date" field.
6.  Choose the delivery time that suits you best in the "delivery_time" field.
7.  You can add any additional delivery notes in the "delivery_note" field.
8.  Make sure all fields are completed and correct before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **delivery_order_form (Text)**: Enter your delivery order information here (required).
*   **customer_details (Text)**: Provide your customer details.
*   **customer_phone (Text)**: Enter your customer phone number (optional).
*   **customer_email (Text)**: Enter your customer email address (optional).
*   **payment_gateway (Select One)**: Choose your preferred payment method (e.g. Stripe or PayPal) (required).
*   **payment_details (Text)**: Enter your payment details.
*   **delivery_date (Date)**: Select the date for your delivery.
*   **delivery_time (Time)**: Choose the delivery time that suits you best.
*   **delivery_note (Note)**: Add any additional delivery notes.
*   **delivery_email (Email)**: Enter a valid email address to receive updates about your delivery (optional).
*   **delivery_phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number to receive updates about your delivery (optional).
