# Health Food Store Organic Order Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Health Food Store Organic Order Request form is used to collect and track organic order requests from customers. This form helps store staff to efficiently manage and fulfill customer needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title or description of your organic order request in the text field labeled "Organic Order Request".
2. Provide details about the delivery, including the delivery date, time, and location, in the "Delivery Details" field.
3. Choose the preferred delivery timing from the options provided in the "Delivery Timing" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the delivery address where the order will be received.
5. Choose the delivery time from the available options in the "Delivery Time" field.
6. Select the store details that apply to the order.
7. Choose a preferred delivery date in the "Preferred Delivery Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organic Order Request** (text): Enter a brief description of your request.
* **Delivery Details** (text): Describe the delivery details, including any specific requirements.
* **Delivery Timing** (select one): Select the preferred delivery timing from the options provided.
* **Delivery Address** (text): Enter the address where the order will be delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (time): Choose the time of day for delivery.
* **Store Details** (select multiple): Select the store details that apply to the order.
* **Preferred Delivery Date** (date): Choose the date for preferred delivery.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you complete all required fields accurately to avoid any confusion or delays.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the store staff for assistance.
