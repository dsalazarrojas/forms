# weather_station_equipment_delivery_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The weather_station_equipment_delivery_form is a form designed to collect information related to the delivery of weather station equipment. This form is meant to be used for internal and external purposes, including customer-facing and administrative tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the delivery address where the equipment will be delivered.
2.  Choose if the equipment is to be delivered or not.
3.  Enter the equipment's name.
4.  Select a delivery date (if the equipment is being delivered).
5.  Enter the delivery time.
6.  Provide the contact name and phone number of the recipient.
7.  Add any delivery note or message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address where the equipment will be delivered.
*   **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose 'Yes' if the equipment is to be delivered, or 'No' if it's not.
*   **Equipment Name** (`equipment_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the equipment being delivered.
*   **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required: false): Select a date for the delivery (if applicable).
*   **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required: false): Choose the time of delivery.
*   **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact phone number.
*   **Delivery Note** (`delivery_note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional delivery information or message.

## Tips

*   Double-check the delivery address to ensure it's correct and complete.
*   Be accurate when selecting the equipment type to ensure it matches the actual delivery situation.
*   Enter the equipment's name clearly to avoid confusion.
*   If selecting a delivery date and time, make sure they're correct to avoid delays or miscommunication.
*   Enter the contact person's name and phone number accurately to ensure they receive the delivery on time. 
*   Add any necessary delivery notes or messages to ensure the recipient is prepared for the delivery.
