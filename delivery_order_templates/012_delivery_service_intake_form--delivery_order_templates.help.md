# delivery_service_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The delivery service intake form is a collection of fields that gathers information for a delivery service. The form is used to record details about the delivery, including contact information, delivery schedule, and any additional comments or notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the required information for the contact field.
2. Enter the delivery address in the delivery address field.
3. Select the delivery date and time from the calendar.
4. Estimate the delivery time.
5. Enter the order ID.
6. Choose a service level (High, Medium, or Low).
7. Add any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title (delivery_service_intake_form)**: This field is not used in the form, but it is an internal title.
* **Contact (contact)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (First and last name, Phone number, Email address, or Physical address).
* **Delivery Address (delivery_address)**: Enter the delivery address where the service is to be delivered.
* **Delivery Date (delivery_date)**: Select the date of the delivery service.
* **Delivery Time (delivery_time)**: Select the time of the delivery service.
* **Estimated Delivery Time (estimated_delivery_time)**: Estimate how long the delivery will take.
* **Order ID (order_id)**: Enter the ID of the order.
* **Service Level (service_level)**: Choose a service level (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Additional Comments (additional_comments)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the delivery service.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address if you have one.
* **Notes (notes)**: Add any additional notes about the delivery service.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the support team for assistance.
* This form is intended for single users, so make sure to check for existing delivery services before submitting a new entry.
