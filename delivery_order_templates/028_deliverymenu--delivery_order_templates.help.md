# deliverymenu - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "deliverymenu" form is used to configure delivery settings for a system. This form consists of four pages, each designed to gather specific information related to delivery services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Navigate to the "deliverymenu" form and select the relevant pages to complete.
2. Complete each page according to the instructions provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **restaurants-or-other-stores-that-deliver**: On this page, please list or search for restaurants or other stores that deliver in your area. Enter the names of these stores as they appear on their respective websites or marketing materials.
* **delivery**: This page has two fields. Select "Yes" if you would like to offer delivery to customers for this store. If you don't want to offer delivery, select "No". This setting applies to the store selected on the previous page.
* **restaurants-or-other-stores-accepting**: On this page, select "Yes" or "No" for each store to indicate whether it accepts delivery. This will help determine which stores are valid for delivery settings.
* **delivery**: (again) This field is similar to the one on page 2. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether to offer delivery to the selected store on the previous page.
Tips:
* Ensure that you're selecting the correct stores and settings for your delivery services.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about a store's delivery policy, it's best to contact the store directly or seek assistance from a relevant authority.
