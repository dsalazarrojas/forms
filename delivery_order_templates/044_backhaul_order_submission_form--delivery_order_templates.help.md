## Backhaul Order Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide detailed information for a backhaul delivery order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions for each field, and ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
The following explanations provide detailed information about each field:

* **Carrier or Company Name** (`carrier_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the carrier or company.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person or dispatcher.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email for order confirmation.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the direct contact number.
* **Motor Carrier Number** (`mc_number`, `text`, required): Enter the motor carrier number.
* **Department of Transportation Number** (`dot_number`, `text`, required): Enter the Department of Transportation ID.
* **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`, `text`, required): Enter the full address for pickup.
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date for pickup.
* **Pickup Time Window** (`pickup_time`, `time`, required): Enter the available time for pickup.
* **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`, `text`, required): Enter the full address for delivery.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date for delivery.
* **Delivery Time Window** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required): Enter the available time for delivery.
* **Trailer Type** (`trailer_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the equipment type.
* **Trailer Length** (`trailer_length`, `select_one`, required): Select the length of the trailer.
* **Load Type** (`load_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the cargo type.
* **Load Weight** (`weight`, `number`, required): Enter the weight in pounds.
* **Number of Pallets** (`number_of_pallets`, `number`, required): Enter the total pallet count.
* **Temperature Control Required** (`temperature_required`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if refrigeration is needed.
* **Temperature Setting** (`temperature_setting`, `text`, not required): Enter the required temperature if applicable.
* **Loading Requirements** (`loading_requirements`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any special requirements.
* **Rate Requested** (`rate_requested`, `number`, required): Enter your rate in dollars.
* **Additional Comments or Instructions** (`additional_accessories`, `text`, not required): Enter any special notes for this load.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of this order.
