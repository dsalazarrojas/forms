# Gift Delivery Address Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for gift delivery purposes, including recipient details, delivery date and time, sender information, and other relevant details. It's essential to provide accurate information to ensure a smooth delivery process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the recipient's details, including their name, delivery date and time, and email address.
2. Provide the sender's address and delivery service provider information.
3. Add any additional notes or comments as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Recipient Details
* **Recipient Name** (text, required): The name of the person receiving the gift.
* **Delivery Date** (date, required): The date on which the gift will be delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (time, required): The time at which the gift will be delivered.
* **Recipient Email** (email, required): The recipient's email address.
* **Recipient Phone** (text, required): The recipient's phone number.
* **Recipient Phone Extension** (text, required): The recipient's phone extension.
* **Recipient Notes** (note, required): Any additional comments or notes for the recipient.

### Sender Information
* **Sender Address** (text, required): The sender's address.
* **Delivery Service Provider** (select one, required): The provider of the delivery service (Online Shop, Florist, Gift Service).
* **Delivery Service Name** (select one, required): Whether the delivery service is used (Yes, No).
* **Sender Address 1** (text, required): The sender's address.
* **Delivery Date 1** (date, required): The first delivery date.
* **Delivery Date 2** (date, required): The second delivery date.
* **Delivery Date 3** (date, required): The third delivery date.
* **Delivery Date 4** (date, required): The fourth delivery date.
* **Delivery Service ID** (number, required): The ID of the delivery service.
* **Order Number** (text, required): The order number.
* **Gift Type** (text, required): The type of gift being delivered.
* **Delivery Date 1** (date, required): The first delivery date.
* **Delivery Date 2** (date, required): The second delivery date.
* **Delivery Date 3** (date, required): The third delivery date.
* **Delivery Date 4** (date, required): The fourth delivery date.
* **Sender Address 1** (text, required): The sender's address.

### Additional Information
* **Notes** (note, optional): Any additional notes or comments about the delivery.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): The phone number of the sender.
* **Email** (email, optional): The email of the sender.
* **Delivery Service Provider** (select one, optional): The provider of the delivery service (Online Shop, Florist, Gift Service).
* **Delivery Service Name** (select one, optional): Whether the delivery service is used (Yes, No).
* **Delivery Notes** (note, optional): Any additional notes or comments about the delivery.
* **Delivery Date 1** (date, optional): The first delivery date.
* **Delivery Date 2** (date, optional): The second delivery date.
* **Delivery Date 3** (date, optional): The third delivery date.
* **Delivery Date 4** (date, optional): The fourth delivery date.
* **Delivery Date 5** (date, optional): The fifth delivery date.
