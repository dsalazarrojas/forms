# Food Drive Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Drive Form is a template used to create and manage delivery orders. This form helps you create and customize delivery orders with details such as food items, amount, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the delivery order template**: Enter the desired delivery order template in the "Delivery Order Template" field.
2. **Select the food items**: Choose the food items you want to include in the order from the "Food" field.
3. **Add toiletries and baby items**: If required, select the toiletries and baby items you want to include in the order from the "Toiletries" and "Baby Items" fields.
4. **Enter the amount**: Enter the quantity of each food item and toiletry or baby item in the "Amount" field.
5. **Enter the contact information**: Enter the recipient's contact information in the "Zip", "Street", "State", and "Country" fields.
6. **Enter the email and phone number**: Enter the recipient's email and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
7. **Set the date and time**: Enter the desired date and time for the delivery in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
8. **Add a note**: Enter any additional notes or instructions in the "Note" field.
9. **Submit the form**: Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Order Template** (`delivery_order_template`, text, optional): Enter the desired delivery order template in this field.
* **Food** (`food_item`, select_multiple, required): Select the food items you want to include in the order.
* **Toiletries** (`toiletries`, select_multiple, optional): Select the toiletries you want to include in the order, if required.
* **Baby Items** (`baby_items`, select_multiple, optional): Select the baby items you want to include in the order, if required.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, required): Enter the quantity of each food item and toiletry or baby item.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the recipient's zip code.
* **Street** (`street`, text, optional): Enter the recipient's street address.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the recipient's state.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter the recipient's country.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the recipient's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the recipient's phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the desired date for the delivery.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the desired time for the delivery.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required): Enter any additional notes or instructions.
* **Help** (`help`, text, optional): Enter any additional help or comments.

Note: This form has no additional tips, but make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate delivery information.
