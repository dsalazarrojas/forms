# Same Day Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to facilitate same-day delivery orders. It collects information from customers to coordinate and expedite the delivery process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all necessary information ready before starting.
2.  Scroll through the pages of the form to review the fields that need to be filled out.
3.  Fill in the required fields as per your knowledge.
4.  Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's name as per your knowledge.
*   **Order Details** (`order_details`, `note`, required/optional): Briefly describe the order details to ensure accurate delivery.
*   **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of the order.
*   **Order Time** (`order_time`, `time`, required/optional): Choose the time of the order.
*   **Delivery Time Window** (`delivery_time_window`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the time frame within which you want to deliver the order:
    \- Within 2 hours
    \- Within 4 hours
    \- Within 6 hours
*   **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the method of delivery:
    \- Same Day Delivery
    \- Next Day Delivery
*   **Delivery Status** (`delivery_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the status of the delivery:
    \- Available
    \- Not Available
*   **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
*   **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address.
*   **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the business.
*   **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's address.
*   **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or information about the customer.
*   **Business Comment** (`business_comment`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or information about the business.
*   **Delivery Comment** (`delivery_comment`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or information about the delivery.

## Tips

*   Review the form carefully before submitting it.
*   Ensure accuracy in filling out all fields to avoid delays or issues with delivery.
*   If you are unsure about any field, ask for assistance or clarification.
*   Submit the form only after you have filled out all required fields.
