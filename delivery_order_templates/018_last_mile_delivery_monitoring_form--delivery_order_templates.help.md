# Last Mile Delivery Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to track and monitor deliveries, ensuring that important details are captured and recorded accurately. It helps in streamlining the process and maintaining transparency throughout the delivery journey.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the **Delivery Order** field with the order number or reference number.
2.  Enter the **Customer Info** field with any relevant details about the customer, such as name and contact information.
3.  In the **Delivery Tracking** field, mention any specific delivery instructions or tracking numbers.
4.  The **Delivery Details** field is for recording any additional delivery information.
5.  The **Customer Feedback** field allows customers to provide feedback about their delivery experience.
6.  Select the **Order Status** field to update the delivery status.
7.  In the **Customer Notes** field, note down any specific customer requests or concerns.
8.  Enter the **Delivery Date** and **Delivery Time** fields to document the delivery schedule.
9.  Specify the **Delivery Location** where the delivery was made.
10. Enter the **Customer Name**, **Customer Email**, and **Customer Phone** fields with the customer's details.
11. The **Customer Note** field is for any notes about the customer's interaction.
12. Enter the **Product Details** field with any relevant product information.
13. In the **Driver Info** field, provide details about the driver assigned to the delivery.
14. Select the **Delivery Status** field to update the delivery status.
15. Enter the **Signature Date** and **Signature Time** fields to document the delivery confirmation.
16. Specify the **Signature Location** where the signature was taken.
17. Record any **Delivery Note** in this field.
18. The **Customer Signature** field is for capturing the customer's signature.
19. In the **Photo** field, upload any relevant photos about the delivery.
20. Use the **Comments** field for any additional comments or notes.
