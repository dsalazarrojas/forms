# Pickup Time Slot Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for selecting a pickup time slot for an order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the desired time slot from the dropdown menu. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
2.  If you select 'Yes', enter your desired pickup date in the 'pickup_time' field.
3.  Enter your contact details in the 'contact_details' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pickup_time_slot_selection** (Select One, Required): Select whether you would like to select a time slot for your order. This will allow us to schedule your order for pickup at the time you choose.
* **pickup_time** (Date, Optional): If you selected 'Yes' for the time slot, enter your desired pickup date here. This is the date you would like to pick up your order.
* **contact_details** (Text, Required): Enter your contact details. This will help us get in touch with you regarding your order.

## Tips
* Please select a time slot that suits your schedule.
* Make sure to enter your correct contact details so we can get in touch with you.
* If you have any issues with your order, contact us with your contact details and we will be happy to assist you.
