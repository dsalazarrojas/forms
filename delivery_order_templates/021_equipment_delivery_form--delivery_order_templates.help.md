# equipment_delivery_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general information, shipping information, and equipment details for deliveries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the General Information page, enter your contact details such as your name, email, and phone number.
2. On the Shipping Information page, provide additional shipping details like the shipping address, city, state, and zip code if required.
3. On the Equipment Information page, enter the equipment type, model, and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (General Information, text, required)**: Enter your contact information to help us get in touch with you.
* **Shipping Information (Shipping Information, text, optional)**: If you have any additional shipping instructions or details, provide them here.
* **Equipment Information (Equipment Information, text, required)**: Describe the equipment you are delivering, including its type and model.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can process your delivery correctly.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.
