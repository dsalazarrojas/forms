# Distributor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distributor Registration Form is a form used to collect information from distributors for various business-related activities, such as orders, payments, or other administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the required information.
2. Fill in your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Provide your address in the "Address" field.
6. Specify the city in the "City" field.
7. Enter the state in the "State" field.
8. Enter your ZIP code in the "ZIP Code" field.
9. Fill in the "Order Details" field with relevant information about your order.
10. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Terms and Conditions" field, indicating your acceptance of the terms.
11. Provide payment details in the "Payment Details" field.
12. Choose your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown list.
13. If applicable, provide the name of the representative in the "Representative Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your account or organization.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your business or residential address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Specify the city where you are located.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where you are located.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Provide relevant information about your order.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" to accept the terms and conditions.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Provide payment details related to your order or transaction.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose from Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal as your payment method.
* **Representative Name** (`representative_name`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the name of the representative handling your order or payment.
