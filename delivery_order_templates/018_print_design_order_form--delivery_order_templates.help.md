# Print & Design Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture the preferences and requirements for printing and designing custom order forms. It is intended for use in business-to-business (B2B) or internal processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields as needed. Each field is optional, and you can submit the form without filling out any of the fields. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lettering Preferences (Lettering)** (<font_preferences>, text, optional): Please provide any specific preferences for lettering, such as font style, size, or font color.
* **Numbering (Numbering)** (<numbering>, text, optional): Enter any numbering preferences, such as start page, font size, or color for numbering.
* **Coloring Preferences (Coloring)** (<coloring>, text, optional): Enter any specific preferences for coloring, such as background color, text color, or color scheme.
* **Font Preferences (Font Preferences)** (<font_preferences>, text, optional): Enter any specific preferences for font style, size, or color. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out only the fields that apply to your specific use case.
* You can use this form as a template and adapt it to your specific needs.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, contact us at form@gic.mx.
