# Raw Dog Food Delivery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Raw Dog Food Delivery Order Form is designed to collect necessary information from pet owners for raw dog food delivery orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Scroll down through the two pages.
2. On the "Pet Info" page, enter your pet's name, their species, and any other relevant information regarding your pet's health or dietary needs.
3. On the "Delivery Info" page, provide the delivery address and any special instructions for the delivery personnel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Info**: (`pet_info`, `text`, Optional)
	+ Enter your pet's name and any other relevant details regarding their species, age, health, or dietary needs.
* **Delivery Info**: (`delivery_info`, `text`, Optional)
	+ Enter the delivery address and any special instructions for the delivery personnel to follow.

## Tips
- Please be as descriptive as possible when providing "Pet Info" to help the delivery personnel tailor the order to your pet's specific needs.
- Ensure you provide accurate delivery information to avoid any delivery issues.
