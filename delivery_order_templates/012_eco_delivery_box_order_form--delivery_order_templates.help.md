# Eco Delivery Box Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Delivery Box Order Form is used to collect information for the Eco Delivery Box order process. This form is meant to be completed by the customer or the person placing the order on behalf of the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Order Form** (`delivery_order_form`, text, optional): This field is used to enter a unique identifier for the order.
* **Recurring Order** (`recurring_order_form`, text, optional): This field is used to enter a recurring order number (if applicable).
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): Enter customer name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, optional): Enter shipping information, including shipping address and any other relevant details.
* **Order Items** (`order_items`, text, optional): Enter the items being delivered, including quantity and any other relevant information.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter payment method and any other relevant payment information.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Enter the delivery date.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Enter the delivery time.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the order or customer.
