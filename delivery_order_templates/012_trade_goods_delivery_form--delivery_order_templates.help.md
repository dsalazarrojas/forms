# Trade Goods Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Goods Delivery Form is used to input and manage trade goods delivery schedules, shipment details, and other necessary information for efficient logistics and order management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Delivery Date" field to choose the date of the delivery.
2. In the "Goods Name", "Goods Description", and "Shipping Address" fields, provide the required details for the goods being delivered.
3. For the "Shipping Contact" field, enter the relevant contact information for the shipping company or person handling the delivery.
4. In the "Delivery Method" field, select the method of delivery from the provided options (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
5. Enter any additional shipping or delivery notes in the "Shipping Note" field.
6. If necessary, add a delivery note for the recipient in the "Delivery Note" field.
7. Finally, specify the "Assigned User" field with the person responsible for managing the delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Date (1)** (`delivery_date`, Optional): Enter the date of the delivery to schedule it accurately.
* **Goods Name (3)** (`goods_name`, Optional): Enter the name of the goods being delivered.
* **Goods Description (4)** (`goods_description`, Optional): Provide a description of the goods being delivered.
* **Shipping Address (5)** (`shipping_address`, Optional): Enter the shipping address where the goods will be delivered.
* **Shipping Contact (6)** (`shipping_contact`, Optional): Enter the shipping contact information for the delivery.
* **Delivery Method (7)** (`delivery_method`, Required): Select the method of delivery from the available options.
* **Shipping Note (8)** (`shipping_note`, Optional): Add any additional shipping or delivery notes.
* **Delivery Note (9)** (`delivery_note`, Optional): Enter a delivery note for the recipient if necessary.
* **Assigned User (10)** (`assigned_user`, Optional): Specify the user responsible for managing the delivery.

## Tips
Make sure to complete all required fields accurately to avoid errors in the delivery process. Double-check dates and delivery methods to ensure timely and correct delivery.
