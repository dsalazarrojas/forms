# Restaurant Food Photos - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Restaurant Food Photos" form is used to capture information about photos taken of food items at a restaurant. This form is typically used to document and track photos of menu items, ensuring consistency and accuracy in food presentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Click on the "Restaurant Food Photos" form to access it.
- Select the relevant options from the available pages.
- Fill in the required and optional fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Restaurant Food Photos
* **Restaurant Food Photos** (`restaurant_food_photos`, `note`, not required): This is a general description of the food photos. Enter a brief note about the photos taken.
 
### Take Phone Photo
* **Take Phone Photo** (`take_phone_photo`, `number`, not required): Take a photo of the food item using your phone camera or mobile device. This field is not required, but recommended for better image quality.
 
### Menu Item Name
* **Menu Item Name** (`menu_item_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the food item being photographed.
 
### Photo Taken
* **Photo Taken** (`photo_taken`, `time`, not required): Record the time and date when the photo was taken.
