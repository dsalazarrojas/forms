# Mushroom Delivery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a mushroom delivery order. It helps mushroom farms or businesses deliver fresh mushrooms to customers' homes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure timely and efficient delivery of your order.
2. Enter your farm name in the **Farm Name** field. This is where the mushrooms are from.
3. Enter your **Customer Name** to let the delivery team know who to deliver to.
4. Select a **Delivery Date** for your order. This is the date you want the mushrooms delivered.
5. Choose a **Delivery Time** for your order. This is when you want the delivery to take place.
6. Select a **Delivery Time of Day**. This is when the delivery person will arrive. Options are 6:00 AM, 12:00 PM, or 6:00 PM.
7. Add any additional **Delivery Note** to help the delivery team know specific instructions or requests for your order.
8. Enter your **Email** address to receive updates or notifications from the delivery team.
9. Enter your **Phone** number in case the delivery team needs to reach you.
10. Choose your preferred **Payment** method for the order. Options are Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal.
11. Enter your **Delivery Address** where the mushrooms will be delivered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farm Name** (`farm_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the farm where the mushrooms were grown.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person or business receiving the order.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false): Select the date you want to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required: false): Select the time you want to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time of Day** (`delivery_time_of_day`, select_one, required: true): Select when you want the delivery to take place (6:00 AM, 12:00 PM, or 6:00 PM).
* **Delivery Note** (`delivery_note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional information or requests for your order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to receive updates or notifications from the delivery team.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in case the delivery team needs to contact you.
* **Payment** (`payment`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose your preferred payment method (Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal).
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address where you want the order delivered.

## Tips

* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
* Make sure to select a valid payment method to complete the order.
* If you have any special requests or instructions for the delivery, add them in the **Delivery Note** field.
