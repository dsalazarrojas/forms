# Restaurant Delivery Settings Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Delivery Settings Form is used to configure settings for restaurant delivery services, allowing you to customize your delivery options and ensure efficient service to your customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your service areas from the available options.
2. Set your delivery fee, if applicable.
3. Choose your delivery channels (e.g., online, phone, etc.).
4. Enter notes about your services, if necessary.
5. Review and submit your settings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Areas**: Select the areas where you offer delivery services. This field is not required, but selecting options here can help you better target your services to specific locations or customer segments.
* **Delivery Fee**: Set the delivery fee for your services, if applicable. This field is not required, but setting a fee can help you manage customer expectations and charge accordingly.
* **Channels**: Choose the channels through which you offer delivery services, such as online ordering, phone orders, etc. Selecting options here helps determine how customers can order from your restaurant.
* **Prophet ID**: This field is currently not clearly defined, so we'll assume it's used for some form of ID or identifier. Please enter the relevant ID or value as instructed.
* **Availability**: Set the time period when your delivery services are available. This field is not required, but setting availability can help customers plan their orders and ensure timely delivery.
* **Availability End Date**: Set the end date for your delivery availability period. This field is not required, but setting an end date can help manage seasonal or promotional periods.
* **Availability Start Date**: Set the start date for your delivery availability period. This field is not required, but setting a start date can help manage seasonal or promotional periods.
* **Prophet Name**: This field is currently not clearly defined, so we'll assume it's used for some form of name or description. Please enter the relevant name or value as instructed.
* **Notes** (various): Enter any notes or comments about your services, delivery processes, or customer feedback. These fields are not required, but providing notes can help you track and manage customer interactions and feedback. 

Note: The fields with "Notes" labels are intended for free-form text entry, while fields with "Prophet" labels seem to be intended for more structured input (e.g., ID or name). Please review each field carefully to understand the expected input format. 

## Tips
* Always review and update your delivery settings regularly to ensure they remain accurate and relevant.
* Use this form as part of your ongoing management and administration of delivery services.
* If you're unsure about a field or setting, please seek guidance from your restaurant management or support team.
