# Delivery Impact Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information on the impact of a delivery on various aspects such as service quality, environmental impact, community impact, and overall satisfaction. This helps delivery teams to track their performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Environmental Impact Scale (Field 10)
	* Service Quality Score (Field 11)
	* Environmental Score (Field 12)
	* Community Score (Field 13)
	* Overall Score (Field 14)
	* Incident Reporting Frequency Scale (Field 16)
	* Service Quality Frequency (Field 17)
	* Environmental Frequency (Field 18)
	* Community Frequency (Field 19)
	* Delivery Status (Field 20)
2. Choose the relevant options for the following fields:
	* Service Quality (Field 2)
	* Environmental Impact (Field 3)
	* Community Impact (Field 4)
	* Delivery Type (Field 25)
	* Incident Reporting Frequency (Field 9)
3. Provide any additional comments or details in the text fields:
	* Service Quality Comments (Field 5)
	* Environmental Comments (Field 6)
	* Community Comments (Field 7)
	* Incident Details (Field 8)
	* Comments from Shipper (Field 23)
	* Comments from Receiver (Field 24)
4. Leave the optional fields blank unless necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Delivery Impact
* **Delivery Impact**: This field is not applicable for this form. Please ignore.

### 2. Service Quality
* **Service Quality**: Assess the quality of service provided. Choose one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### 3. Environmental Impact
* **Environmental Impact**: Select all that apply:
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High

### 4. Community Impact
* **Community Impact**: Assess the impact on the community. Choose one of the following options:
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High

### 5. Service Quality Comments
* **Service Quality Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the service quality.

### 6. Environmental Comments
* **Environmental Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the environmental impact.

### 7. Community Comments
* **Community Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the community impact.

### 8. Incident Details
* **Incident Details**: Provide any details about the incident that may be relevant to the assessment.

### 9. Incident Reporting Frequency
* **Incident Reporting Frequency**: Select all that apply:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Not Applicable

### 10. Environmental Impact Scale
* **Environmental Impact Scale**: This field is not a scale, but rather a date field. Please enter the date of the assessment.

### 11. Service Quality Score
* **Service Quality Score**: Enter a number between 1 and 10 to assess the service quality.

### 12. Environmental Score
* **Environmental Score**: Enter a number between 1 and 10 to assess the environmental impact.

### 13. Community Score
* **Community Score**: Enter a number between 1 and 10 to assess the community impact.

### 14. Overall Score
* **Overall Score**: Enter a number between 1 and 10 to assess the overall satisfaction.

### 15. Comments
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments.

### 16. Incident Reporting Frequency Scale
* **Incident Reporting Frequency Scale**: Enter a number between 1 and 10 to assess the incident reporting frequency.

### 17. Service Quality Frequency
* **Service Quality Frequency**: Enter a number between 1 and 10 to assess the service quality frequency.

### 18. Environmental Frequency
* **Environmental Frequency**: Enter a number between 1 and 10 to assess the environmental frequency.

### 19. Community Frequency
* **Community Frequency**: Enter a number between 1 and 10 to assess the community frequency.

### 20. Delivery Status
* **Delivery Status**: Choose one of the following options:
	+ Not Started
	+ In Progress
	+ Completed

### 21. Delivery Notes
* **Delivery Notes**: Provide any additional comments about the delivery.

### 22. Delivery Due Date
* **Delivery Due Date**: This field is not applicable for this form. Please ignore.

### 23. Comments from Shipper
* **Comments from Shipper**: Provide any comments from the shipper.

### 24. Comments from Receiver
* **Comments from Receiver**: Provide any comments from the receiver.

### 25. Delivery Type
* **Delivery Type**: Choose one of the following options:
	+ Standard
	+ Expedited
	+ Express
