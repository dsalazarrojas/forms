# Pizza Hut Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pizza Hut Order Form is a tool designed to collect and track information about customer orders, enabling efficient order fulfillment and assignment of tasks to staff members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Select a delivery method**: Choose between "delivery" or "carryout" to indicate how the order will be fulfilled.
2. **Enter customer information**: Provide the customer's name, address, email address, and phone number.
3. **Choose a pizza type**: Select the size of pizza ordered (large, medium, or small).
4. **Select optional toppings**: Choose any additional toppings for the pizza.
5. **Enter order method details**: Add any additional comments or instructions for fulfilling the order.
6. **Schedule delivery**: Enter the date and time for delivery.
7. **Assign order**: Fill out the staff assignment fields to assign the order to a specific staff member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`): Choose between "delivery" or "carryout" to indicate how the order will be fulfilled.
* **Customer Name** (`name`): Enter the customer's name accurately.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter the customer's address where they will receive their order.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter the customer's phone number for communication.
* **Pizza Type** (`pizza_type`): Select the size of pizza ordered (large, medium, or small).
* **Toppings** (`pizza_topping1` and `pizza_topping2`): Choose any additional toppings for the pizza.
* **Order Method Details** (`order_method_details`): Add any additional comments or instructions for fulfilling the order.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`): Enter the date for delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`): Enter the time for delivery.
* **Note** (`note`): Add any additional comments or information about the order.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Tip** (`tip`): Enter any tip amount for the staff member.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`): Select the status of the order (pending, completed, or cancelled).
* **Assignee** (`assignee`): Enter the staff member's name to be assigned to the order.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Enter the staff member's name assigned to the order.
* **Assigned By** (`assigned_by`): Enter the staff member's name who assigned the order.
* **Assigned At** (`assigned_at`): Enter the date and time the order was assigned.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`): Enter the date and time the order was last updated.
* **Created At** (`created_at`): Enter the date and time the order was created.
* **Created By** (`created_by`): Enter the staff member's name who created the order.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`): Enter the staff member's name who last updated the order.
* **Assigned To Name** (`assigned_to_name`): Enter the name of the staff member assigned to the order.
* **Note 2** (`note2`): Add any additional comments or information about the order.
