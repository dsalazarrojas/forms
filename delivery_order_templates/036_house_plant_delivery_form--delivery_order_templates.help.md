# House Plant Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for house plant deliveries. It's intended for customers who are ordering plants for delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes:
	* Customer Information (name, email, phone number)
	* Delivery details (delivery date, time, address)
	* Payment method (credit card, PayPal, bank transfer)
	* Plant ordering information (select the plants you want to order)
2. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (1)**: Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Delivery Date (2)**: Choose the date you want to receive the delivery.
* **Delivery Time (3)**: Choose the time you want to receive the delivery.
* **Payment Method (4)**: Select how you want to pay for the delivery (credit card, PayPal, bank transfer).
* **Customer Email (5)**: Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone (6)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Delivery Address (7)**: Enter your delivery address.
* **Order Plants (8)**: Select the plants you want to order.
* **Order Plots (9)**: Select whether you want to order plots or not.
* **Notes (10)**: Enter any additional notes for the delivery.
* **Total (11)**: This field is not applicable for customer completion.
* **Grand Total (12)**: This field is not applicable for customer completion.
* **Payment Gateway (13)**: This field is not applicable for customer completion.
* **Currency (14)**: This field is not applicable for customer completion.
* **Payment Status (15)**: This field is not applicable for customer completion.
* **Delivery Status (16)**: This field is not applicable for customer completion.
* **Payment Date (17)**: This field is not applicable for customer completion.
* **Payment Time (18)**: This field is not applicable for customer completion.
* **Delivery Date (19)**: This field is not applicable for customer completion.
* **Delivery Note (20)**: Enter any additional delivery notes.
* **Order Note (21)**: Enter any additional notes for the order.
* **Payment Note (22)**: This field is not applicable for customer completion.
* **Delivery Date 2 (23)**: This field is not applicable for customer completion.
* **Delivery Time 2 (24)**: This field is not applicable for customer completion.
* **Customer Name (25)**: Enter your name.
