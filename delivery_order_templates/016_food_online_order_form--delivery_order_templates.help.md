# Food Online Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for online food orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select your preferred meal options, choose your preferred delivery details, and confirm your payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Choose Your Meal** (`meal_selection`, select_multiple, required: false):
  Choose your preferred meal options by selecting multiple items from the list.
  * Select as many meal options as you like.
* **Preferred Diet** (`diet_type`, select_one, required: false):
  Choose your preferred diet from the list.
  * Select one diet option.
* **Texture** (`texture`, select_one, required: false):
  Choose your preferred texture from the list.
  * Select one texture option.
* **Temperature** (`temperature`, select_one, required: false):
  Choose your preferred temperature from the list.
  * Select one temperature option.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false):
  Input the quantity of food you want to order.
  * Enter a whole number (e.g. 1, 2, 3).
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, note, required: false):
  Enter any additional delivery details or special instructions.
  * Use this field to provide any additional information about your delivery.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required: false):
  Choose your preferred delivery method from the list.
  * Select one delivery method option.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, select_one, required: false):
  Choose your preferred payment method from the list.
  * Select one payment option.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false):
  Input your name.
  * Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false):
  Input your email address.
  * Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false):
  Input your phone number.
  * Enter your phone number.
* **Order Online** (`order_online`, select_one, required: false):
  Choose if you want to order online.
  * Select one option.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false):
  Choose your preferred payment method from the list.
  * Select one payment option.
* **Confirm Payment** (`confirm_payment`, select_one, required: false):
  Confirm your payment method.
  * Select one option.

Note: This form is not required for customers to fill out, and it is intended to be a convenience tool for customers to provide their order information.
