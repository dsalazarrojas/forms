# product_delivery_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information for product delivery, ensuring accurate and timely delivery of products to customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the `Contact Information` page, including your name and relevant contact details.
2. Provide your shipping address in the `Shipping Address` page, including the street address, city, state, and zip code.
3. Set the desired `Delivery Date` and `Delivery Time` for your product.
4. Choose the methods of contact you prefer (e.g., Email and/or Phone) and provide any additional contact details.
5. Optionally, include delivery instructions or additional notes for the delivery team in the `Delivery Instructions` and `Delivery Notes` fields.
6. Verify your email address is correct in the `Email` field.
7. Verify your phone number is correct in the `Phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)** (`contact_information`, `text`, required): Enter your name and contact details, such as your phone number or any other contact information you want to provide.
* **Shipping Address (Shipping Address)** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required): Enter your shipping address, including the street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Delivery Date (Delivery Date)** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you would like to receive your product.
* **Delivery Time (Delivery Time)** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required): Select the time you would like to receive your product.
* **Contact Method (Contact Method)** (`contact_method`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your preferred contact methods, such as Email or Phone (or both).
* **Contact Details (Contact Details)** (`contact_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional contact details you want to provide for the delivery team.
* **Delivery Instructions (Delivery Instructions)** (`delivery_instructions`, `text`, optional): Provide any special instructions for the delivery team.
* **Delivery Notes (Delivery Notes)** (`delivery_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes for the delivery team.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you regarding your order.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for the delivery team to contact you.
