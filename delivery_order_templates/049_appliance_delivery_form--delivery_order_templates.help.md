# Appliance Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for customers to provide information about their appliance delivery. The form is divided into several sections to make it easier to fill out and ensure that all required fields are completed.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by entering your delivery ID number.
2.  Enter the date of your order.
3.  Choose a scheduled delivery date from the provided calendar.
4.  Fill out all the required fields with your personal information, including:
   *   Full name
   *   Email address
   *   Phone number
   *   Alternative contact information (if applicable)
5.  Provide information about the appliance being delivered, including:
   *   Appliance type
   *   Brand
   *   Model
   *   Serial number (if available)
   *   Color or finish
   *   Quantity
   *   Dimensions (if applicable)
   *   Weight (if applicable)
6.  Specify your preferred delivery options, including:
   *   Delivery type (e.g., Curbside, Threshold, Room of Choice)
   *   Installation required (yes or no)
   *   Installation type (if required)
   *   Haul away old appliance (yes or no)
7.  Choose your preferred delivery time frame and any specific access information.
8.  Select the type of residence (e.g., house, apartment, condominium, townhouse, other).
9.  Provide information about your property, including:
   *   Floor number
   *   Availability of elevator (if applicable)
   *   Number of stairs to entry
   *   Doorway width concerns (if applicable)
   *   Parking availability (if applicable)
   *   Gate code (if applicable)
   *   Building contact (if applicable)
10. Select your preferred payment method (e.g., credit card, debit card, cash, check, financing).
11. Fill out payment information, including:
   *   Total amount due
   *   Amount paid as deposit
   *   Balance due
12. Add any additional delivery notes or remarks.
13. Sign the form digitally with your customer signature.
14. Enter the date of your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Delivery ID**: Enter a unique number provided to you by your delivery service provider.
    *   **Order Date**: Enter the date you placed your order.
    *   **Scheduled Delivery Date**: Choose a date for delivery.
    *   **Customer Full Name**: Enter your first and last name.
    *   **Customer Email**: Enter your contact email address.
    *   **Customer Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
    *   **Alternate Contact**: Enter an alternative contact name and number (if applicable).
    *   **Delivery Address**: Enter your full address where the appliance will be delivered.
    *   **City**: Enter your city or town.
    *   **State**: Enter your state or province.
    *   **ZIP Code**: Enter your postal code.
    *   **Appliance Type**: Choose the type of appliance being delivered.
    *   **Appliance Brand**: Enter the manufacturer of the appliance.
    *   **Appliance Model**: Enter the model number of the appliance.
    *   **Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the appliance (if available).
    *   **Appliance Color**: Enter the color or finish of the appliance (if applicable).
    *   **Quantity**: Enter the number of appliances being delivered.
    *   **Dimensions**: Enter the dimensions of the appliance (if applicable).
    *   **Weight**: Enter the weight of the appliance (in pounds, if applicable).
    *   **Delivery Type**: Choose your preferred delivery type.
    *   **Installation Required**: Specify if installation is needed.
    *   **Installation Type**: Choose any necessary installation types (if required).
    *   **Haul Away Old Appliance**: Specify if you want to haul away the old appliance.
    *   **Preferred Delivery Time**: Choose your preferred delivery time frame.
    *   **Property Type**: Choose the type of residence (e.g., house, apartment, condominium, townhouse, other).
    *   **Floor Number**: Enter your floor number (if applicable).
    *   **Elevator Available**: Specify if an elevator is available.
    *   **Stairs to Entry**: Enter the number of stairs to entry (if applicable).
    *   **Doorway Width Concerns**: Specify if there are any concerns regarding the doorway width (if applicable).
    *   **Parking Availability**: Specify if parking is available for the delivery truck.
    *   **Gate Code**: Enter any gate code (if applicable).
    *   **Building Contact**: Enter the contact person for the building (if applicable).
    *   **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method.
    *   **Total Amount Due**: Enter the total amount due.
    *   **Deposit Paid**: Enter the amount paid as deposit.
    *   **Balance Due**: Enter the balance due.
    *   **Delivery Notes**: Add any additional delivery notes or remarks.
    *   **Customer Signature**: Sign the form digitally with your customer signature.
    *   **Signature Date**: Enter the date you sign the form.
    *   **Driver Signature**: Sign the form digitally with the driver's signature (if applicable).
    *   **Delivery Status**: Choose the outcome of the delivery.
    *   **Driver Name**: Enter the name of the delivery personnel (if applicable).
    *   **Driver Signature**: Sign the form digitally with the driver's signature (if applicable).
    *   **Customer Acceptance**: Sign the form digitally to confirm receipt (if applicable).
    *   **Driver Notes**: Enter any issues or observations made by the delivery personnel (if applicable).

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled out in order to ensure successful delivery.
*   If there are any issues with the delivery, please contact your delivery service provider.
*   The delivery personnel will review the form and ensure that all details are accurate before delivering the appliance.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact your delivery service provider for assistance.
*   This form is intended for your convenience and to help ensure smooth delivery experiences. Please follow the instructions carefully to get the best results.
