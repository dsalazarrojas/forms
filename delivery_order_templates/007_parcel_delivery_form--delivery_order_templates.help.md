# Parcel Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for parcel delivery purposes. It is intended to be a template for customers to provide their delivery details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field. Please use a valid email address.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
6. Enter your City in the "City" field.
7. Select your State from the list in the "State" field.
8. Enter your Zip Code in the "Zip Code" field.
9. Enter the desired Delivery Date in the "Delivery Date" field.
10. Select your preferred Delivery Time in the "Delivery Time" field.
11. Choose your preferred Delivery Method from the list in the "Delivery Method" field.
12. Provide any additional Shipping Notes in the "Shipping Notes" field.
13. If applicable, select if an assigned tool will be used in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your complete address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city name.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, optional): Select your state from the list.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Enter the date for the delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Select your preferred delivery time.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred delivery method.
* **Shipping Notes** (`shipping_notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional shipping notes.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select if an assigned tool will be used.
