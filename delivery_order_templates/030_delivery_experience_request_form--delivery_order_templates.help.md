# Delivery Experience Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Delivery Experience Request Form" is a tool used to collect information about customer experiences with deliveries. It is designed to help monitor and improve the quality of service provided to customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions honestly and provide as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure you have not submitted a similar request for the same order.
3. Fill out all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Experience Request Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Delivery Team Member**: Please select 'Yes' if you had a delivery team member assigned to your order, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Delivery Order Number**: Enter the order number for the delivery you are referring to.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date of the delivery.
* **Delivery Time**: Enter the time of the delivery (optional).
* **Delivery Rating**: Select your rating for the delivery experience (Good, Fair).
* **Delivery Comments**: Provide any comments or feedback about your delivery experience.
* **Customer Service Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the customer service representative who handled your inquiry.
* **Customer Service Email**: Enter the email address of the customer service representative.
* **Customer Service Phone**: Enter the phone number of the customer service representative.
* **Delivery Issues**: Select 'Yes' if you experienced any issues during the delivery, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Delivery Service Rating**: Select your rating for the delivery service (Good, Fair).
* **Delivery Service Comments**: Provide comments about your experience with the delivery service.
* **Delivery Rating**: Enter a numerical rating for the delivery experience (1-5).
* **Delivery Service Level**: Select your rating for the delivery service level (High, Low).
* **Delivery Comments**: Provide any comments or feedback about your delivery experience.
* **Delivery Satisfaction**: Select your level of satisfaction with the delivery (Satisfied, Dissatisfied).
* **Customer Service Satisfaction**: Select your level of satisfaction with the customer service (Satisfied, Dissatisfied).
* **Customer Service Rating**: Enter a numerical rating for the customer service experience (1-5).
* **Customer Service Comments**: Provide any comments or feedback about your experience with the customer service.
* **Order ID**: Enter the order ID for the delivery you are referring to.
* **Assigned User ID**: Select 'Yes' if the delivery was assigned to a specific user, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date and time you submitted this form (optional).
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name and title/position.
