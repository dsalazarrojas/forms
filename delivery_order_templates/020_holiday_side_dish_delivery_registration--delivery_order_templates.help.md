# Holiday Side Dish Delivery Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register holiday side dish delivery preferences for customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose whether or not you want to receive holiday side dishes by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the **Delivery Preferences** section.
2. Enter your customer details in the **Customer Details** section, including any relevant information such as your name and contact number.
3. In the **Side Dish Selections** section, select your preferred side dishes by choosing "Yes" or "No" for each option.
4. Choose a delivery date and time in the **Delivery Date** and **Delivery Time** sections, respectively.
5. Enter your email address and phone number in the **Email** and **Phone** sections, respectively.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the **Notes** section, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Preferences** (Select Multiple, Required: False):
	+ Choose whether or not you want to receive holiday side dishes.
* **Customer Details** (Text, Required: False):
	+ Enter your customer details, including your name and contact information.
* **Side Dish Selections** (Select Multiple, Required: False):
	+ Select your preferred side dishes by choosing "Yes" or "No" for each option.
* **Delivery Date** (Date, Required: False):
	+ Choose a date for your holiday side dish delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (Time, Required: False):
	+ Choose a time for your holiday side dish delivery.
* **Email** (Email, Required: False):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Required: False):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (Note, Required: False):
	+ Add any additional notes or comments, if needed.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have all the necessary information before filling out the form.
* Consult with your colleagues or managers if you are unsure about any of the fields.
* Double-check your selection in the **Side Dish Selections** section to ensure you are choosing the correct options.
* Enter accurate and complete information in the **Email** and **Phone** sections to ensure timely communication.
* Use the **Notes** section to provide any additional context or information about your delivery preferences.
