# Holiday Side Dish Delivery Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register holiday side dish delivery preferences for customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose whether or not you want to receive holiday side dishes by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the **Delivery Preferences** section.
2. Enter your customer details in the **Customer Details** section, including any relevant information such as your name and contact number.
3. In the **Side Dish Selections** section, select your preferred side dishes by choosing "Yes" or "No" for each option.
4. Choose a delivery date and time in the **Delivery Date** and **Delivery Time** sections, respectively.
5. Enter your email address and phone number in the **Email** and **Phone** sections, respectively.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the **Notes** section, if needed.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have all the necessary information before filling out the form.
