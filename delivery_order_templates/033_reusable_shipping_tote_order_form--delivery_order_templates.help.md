# Reusable Shipping Tote Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for reusable shipping totes orders. It captures essential details such as delivery preferences, return choices, and contact information for seamless communication and order management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of your order in the `shipping_tote_order` field.
2. Provide your delivery preferences, such as any specific instructions or requests for delivery, in the `delivery_preferences` field.
3. Select your return choices from the available options in the `return_choices` field.
4. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
5. Enter your phone number in the `phone_number` field.
6. Choose a date for your order in the `date` field.
7. Select a time for your order in the `time` field.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the `note` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **shipping_tote_order (Text, Optional)**: Enter a brief description of your order.
* **delivery_preferences (Text, Optional)**: Provide any specific delivery instructions or requests.
* **return_choices (Text, Optional)**: Select one or more return options that apply to your order.
* **email (Email, Required)**: Enter a valid email address to receive order updates and confirmation.
* **phone_number (Text, Optional)**: Enter your phone number to receive order updates by phone.
* **date (Date, Optional)**: Choose a date for your order.
* **time (Time, Optional)**: Select a time for your order.
* **note (Text, Optional)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
