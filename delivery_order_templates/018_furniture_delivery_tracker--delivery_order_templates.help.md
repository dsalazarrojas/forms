# Furniture Delivery Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Furniture Delivery Tracker form is used for tracking and managing the status of furniture deliveries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the delivery status from the options provided in the "Delivery Status" field.
2. Choose the relevant confirmation status for the delivery team, warehouse, and retailer in the "Delivery Team", "Warehouse Confirmation", and "Retailer Confirmation" fields, respectively.
3. Fill in the "Delivery Date" and "Delivery Time" fields with the actual delivery date and time.
4. Add any relevant notes about the delivery in the "Notes" field.
5. Enter the contact number and email of the person handling the delivery in the "Contact Number" and "Contact Email" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Delivery Status
* **Delivery Status** (Required: Optional): Select the current status of the delivery from the options provided.

### Delivery Confirmation
* **Delivery Confirmation** (Required: Optional): Select the status of delivery confirmation from the options provided.

### Delivery Date
* **Delivery Date** (Required: Optional): Enter the actual date of the delivery. This field accepts date values in the YYYY-MM-DD format.

### Delivery Time
* **Delivery Time** (Required: Optional): Enter the actual time of the delivery in 24-hour format (HH:MM).

### Delivery Team
* **Delivery Team** (Required: Optional): Check if the delivery team is involved in the delivery.

### Warehouse Confirmation
* **Warehouse Confirmation** (Required: Optional): Select the status of warehouse confirmation from the options provided.

### Retailer Confirmation
* **Retailer Confirmation** (Required: Optional): Select the status of retailer confirmation from the options provided.

### Notes
* **Notes** (Required: Optional): Add any relevant notes about the delivery.

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (Required: Optional): Enter the contact number of the person handling the delivery.

### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (Required: Optional): Enter the contact email of the person handling the delivery.
