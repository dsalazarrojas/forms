# Jurisdiction And Limitation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the primary jurisdiction and limitation affecting a particular decision. The purpose of this survey is to gather data that will help us understand the regulatory context and potential restrictions that impact our business operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions and introduction provided on the first page.
2. On the first page, answer the question "What is the primary jurisdiction that affects this decision?" in the text field.
3. On the second page, answer the question "What is the primary limitation that affects this decision?" in the text field.
4. If you have any options to choose from, select the corresponding option for each question.
5. Click the "Submit" button to submit your answers.

* **Welcome to the Survey**: This is the introduction page of the survey.

* **Jurisdiction**: This field is where you will answer the question "What is the primary jurisdiction that affects this decision?" This is a text field where you can provide a brief description of the jurisdiction that applies to your decision.

### Limitation (Page 1)
