* **Site Name** (`site_name`, text, required/optional): Enter a unique name for the website being assessed.

* **Site ID** (`site_id`, text, required): Enter a unique identifier for the website.
* **Score** (`score`, number, required): Enter a score between 1 and 10 for the website's performance.
