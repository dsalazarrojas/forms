# Executive Coaching Program Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from program participants about their experiences and perceptions of the Executive Coaching Program. Your input will help us understand what worked well and what can be improved in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete this form in a clear and concise manner.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the open-ended fields.
4. Review and submit your form before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Participants** (`program_participants`, text, not required): This field is for you to list the program participants you have had the opportunity to work with through the Executive Coaching Program.
* **Coaching Goals** (`coaching_goals`, text, not required): Describe the goals you had for the coaching sessions, including any specific objectives or outcomes you aimed to achieve.
* **Goals Achieved** (`goals_achieved`, select_multiple, not required): Indicate which of the coaching goals you felt were met or partially met, and which ones you did not achieve. Please select all that apply.
* **Feedback Rating** (`feedback_rating`, number, not required): Rate your overall experience with the program on a scale of 1 (low) to 10 (high). Be honest and provide a number that reflects your perception.
* **Coaching Effectiveness** (`coaching_effectiveness`, select_one, not required): Indicate whether you found the coaching sessions effective, somewhat effective, or not effective at all.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, not required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a scale of 1 (low) to 10 (high). Be honest and provide a number that reflects your perception.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, not required): Share any additional insights or suggestions you have for improving the Executive Coaching Program.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, not required): Provide any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share about your experience with the program.

## Tips
- Please be thorough and honest in your responses.
- If you are unsure about a question, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
- Your input is valuable and will contribute to the improvement of the Executive Coaching Program.
