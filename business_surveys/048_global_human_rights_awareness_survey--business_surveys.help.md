# Global Human Rights Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey aims to assess your awareness and attitudes towards human rights in various aspects of your life. The survey consists of multiple questions that cover different dimensions of human rights, including knowledge, attitudes, experiences, and beliefs. Your responses will help us better understand human rights issues and promote awareness and understanding of human rights.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reading each question carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Select the answer that best reflects your opinion or attitude towards each question.
3. If you are unsure or have no clear opinion, choose "Undecided."
4. If your answer depends on specific circumstances, choose "Depends."
5. For questions with "Yes" and "No" options, choose the one that best reflects your agreement or disagreement with the statement.
6. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
7. Finally, review your answers carefully before submitting the survey.
