# Tenant Retention Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Tenant Retention Motivation Survey is a tool used by landlords to gauge the level of satisfaction and motivation among their tenants. The data collected from this form will help landlords identify areas for improvement and inform decisions to increase tenant retention and satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start with the introduction page, where you will see a brief explanation of the purpose and scope of the survey.
2. Please fill out the fields as instructed in the field-by-field explanation section below.
3. Be honest in your responses, as this will ensure that the data collected is accurate and useful for our landlord-tenant relationship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, not required): This is a brief explanation of the survey's purpose and scope. Please take a moment to read it carefully before proceeding.
*   **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as a tenant.
*   **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `number`, not required): This measures how satisfied you are with your current rental experience.
    *   A value of 1 represents 'Not Satisfied' and 2 represents 'Satisfied'
*   **Likely to Renew** (`likely_to_renew`, `select_one`, not required): Do you intend to renew your lease when it ends?
    *   Please choose one of the options: 'Yes' or 'No'
*   **Reasons for Not Renewing** (`reasons_for_not_renewing`, `select_multiple`, not required): Why would you not renew your lease if you chose 'No' for the previous question?
    *   This is a multiple select field, where you can select one or more reasons.
    *   Options include: 'Yes' and 'No'
*   **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, not required): Please provide any suggestions you have for our landlord-tenant relationship.
*   **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, `number`, not required): How would you rate your overall experience with our rental property?
    *   A value of 1 represents 'Poor' and 2 represents 'Excellent'
*   **Renewal Intent** (`renewal_intent`, `select_one`, not required): Do you intend to renew your lease when it ends?
    *   Please choose one of the options: 'Yes' or 'No'
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please share them here.
*   **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, not required): How would you like to be contacted if we need to follow up on this survey?
    *   Please choose one of the options: 'Yes' or 'No'
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, not required): If you would like to be contacted via phone, please provide your phone number.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, not required): If you would like to be contacted via email, please provide your email address.

## Tips
*   Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about any of the fields, feel free to contact us.
*   Your input is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our services.
