# Accessory Dwelling Unit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accessory Dwelling Unit Survey is designed to gather information about your ADU. Please answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer all questions, but do not worry about grammar or spelling mistakes.
2.  If you are unsure about any question, please select "Don't Know" or "Other" and provide additional information in the comments section.
3.  If you have any comments or additional information about your ADU experience, please use the "Additional ADU Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Property Owner Name** (`property_owner_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the property owner.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for follow-up questions or inquiries.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for follow-up questions or inquiries.
*   **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the complete address of your property with the ADU.
*   **ADU Type** (`adu_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of ADU you have (Detached ADU, Attached ADU, Converted Garage, Converted Basement, Converted Attic, or Other).
*   **ADU Size (Square Feet)** (`adu_size_square_feet`, number, required): Enter the approximate square footage of your ADU.
*   **Number of Bedrooms** (`adu_bedrooms`, number, required): Enter the number of bedrooms in your ADU.
*   **ADU Year Constructed** (`adu_year_constructed`, number, required): Enter the year your ADU was constructed or converted.
*   **ADU Monthly Rent** (`adu_monthly_rent`, number, optional): Enter the monthly rental amount for your ADU (if applicable).
*   **ADU Occupancy Status** (`adu_occupancy_status`, select_one, required): Select how your ADU is currently occupied (Rented to tenants, Occupied by family members, Used as home office, Vacant, Seasonal use, or Other).
*   **ADU Utility Arrangements** (`adu_utility_arrangements`, select_multiple, required): Select how utilities are handled for your ADU (Separate meters, Shared with main house, Included in rent, Paid separately by tenant, or Other).
*   **ADU Parking Arrangements** (`adu_parking_arrangements`, select_one, required): Select how parking is arranged for your ADU (Dedicated parking space, Street parking, Shared driveway, Garage space, No dedicated parking, or Other).
*   **ADU Ownership Type** (`adu_ownership_type`, select_one, required): Select who owns the ADU (Same owner as main house, Different owner, Rental company, or Other).
*   **ADU Rental Income** (`adu_rental_income`, number, optional): Enter the annual rental income from your ADU (if applicable).
*   **ADU Main Purpose** (`adu_main_purpose`, select_multiple, required): Select the main purpose of your ADU (Additional rental income, Family housing, Home office, Guest accommodation, Senior living solution, Downsize main house, or Other).
*   **ADU Regulatory Compliance** (`adu_regulatory_compliance`, select_one, required): Select whether your ADU is compliant with local zoning and building codes (Fully Compliant, Mostly Compliant, Partially Compliant, Not Compliant, Don't Know).
*   **ADU Challenges Faced** (`adu_challenges_faced`, select_multiple, required): Select the challenges you have faced with your ADU (Financing construction, Obtaining permits, Neighbor disputes, Insurance issues, Maintenance costs, Tenant issues, Regulatory compliance, None, or Other).
*   **ADU Satisfaction Level** (`adu_satisfaction_level`, select_one, required): Select your satisfaction level with your ADU (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
*   **ADU Recommendation Likelihood** (`adu_recommendation_likelihood`, select_one, required): Select how likely you are to recommend ADU development to others (Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very Unlikely).
*   **Additional ADU Comments** (`additional_adu_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about your ADU experience.

Please note that all fields are required unless marked as "optional." You can ask for clarification on any question if you're unsure.
