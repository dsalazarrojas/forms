# Business Sustainability Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Sustainability Checklist is designed to help organizations assess their progress in sustainable business practices. This form evaluates various aspects of corporate sustainability, including environmental, social, and governance (ESG) factors.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in your company's basic information:
    *   **Company Name**: Enter your organization's name as it appears on your business cards or marketing materials.
    *   **Assessment Date**: Select the date on which you are completing this survey, as this will help us understand when you began implementing sustainable practices.
    *   **Assessor Name**: Enter the name of the person completing this assessment.
    *   **Assessor Role or Title**: Describe your role or title within the company.
2.  Review the sustainability metrics and practices below and answer accordingly.
    *   Note that some questions are multiple-choice answers, while others require a numerical input.
3.  Review and adjust your answers as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company Name (1)**: This is the name of your organization as it appears on your business cards or marketing materials.
*   **Assessment Date (2)**: This is the date on which you are completing this assessment.
*   **Assessor Name (3)**: Enter the name of the person completing this survey.
*   **Assessor Role or Title (4)**: Describe your role or title within the company.
*   **Number of Employees (5)**: If applicable, enter the number of employees you have in your organization.
*   **Industry or Sector (6)**: Identify the industry or sector in which your organization operates.
*   **Environmental Policy (7)**: Answer:
    *   *   **Yes, documented**: You have a written policy in place that details your environmental practices.
    *   *   **Yes, informal**: You have an unwritten policy or set of practices for environmental management.
    *   *   **No, not yet**: You do not have a formal environmental policy.
*   **Carbon Footprint Tracking (8)**: Answer:
    *   *   **Yes, regularly**: You regularly track your carbon footprint or emissions.
    *   *   **Yes, periodically**: You track your carbon footprint or emissions but do not do so regularly.
    *   *   **No, not tracked**: You do not track your carbon footprint or emissions.
*   **Renewable Energy (9)**: Enter the percentage of renewable energy used in your organization.
*   **Waste Reduction (10)**: Answer:
    *   *   **Yes, comprehensive**: You have a comprehensive waste reduction program in place.
    *   *   **Yes, basic**: You have a basic waste reduction program.
    *   *   **No program**: You do not have a waste reduction program.
*   **Recycling Program (11)**: Answer:
    *   *   **Yes, facility-wide**: Your recycling program applies to all areas of your organization.
    *   *   **Yes, limited areas**: Your recycling program applies to some areas of your organization.
    *   *   **No recycling program**: You do not have a recycling program.
*   **Water Conservation (12)**: Answer:
    *   *   **Yes, active program**: You have an active program for water conservation.
    *   *   **Yes, some measures**: You have some water conservation measures in place.
    *   *   **No initiatives**: You do not have water conservation initiatives.
*   **Supplier Sustainability (13)**: Answer:
    *   *   **Yes, thoroughly**: You thoroughly assess your suppliers for sustainability.
    *   *   **Yes, some assessment**: You perform some assessment of your suppliers.
    *   *   **No assessment**: You do not assess your suppliers.
*   **Social Responsibility (14)**: Answer:
    *   *   **Yes, comprehensive**: You have a comprehensive social responsibility program.
    *   *   **Yes, basic program**: You have a basic social responsibility program.
    *   *   **No program**: You do not have a social responsibility program.
*   **Employee Wellbeing (15)**: Answer:
    *   *   **Strong program**: You have a strong employee wellbeing program.
    *   *   **Some initiatives**: You have some employee wellbeing initiatives.
    *   *   **Minimal program**: You have a minimal employee wellbeing program.
    *   *   **No programs**: You do not have any employee wellbeing programs.
*   **Diversity and Inclusion (16)**: Answer:
    *   *   **Yes, active and measurable**: You have active and measurable diversity and inclusion programs in place.
    *   *   **Yes, some initiatives**: You have some diversity and inclusion initiatives.
    *   *   **No formal program**: You do not have a formal diversity and inclusion program.
*   **Governance and Ethics (17)**: Answer:
    *   *   **Yes, documented and enforced**: You have documented and enforced governance and ethics guidelines.
    *   *   **Yes, informal**: You have informal governance and ethics guidelines.
    *   *   **No guidelines**: You do not have governance and ethics guidelines.
*   **Sustainability Reporting (18)**: Answer:
    *   *   **Yes, annually**: You publish your sustainability reports annually.
    *   *   **Yes, sometimes**: You publish sustainability reports occasionally.
    *   *   **No reports**: You do not publish sustainability reports.
*   **Measurable Sustainability Goals (19)**: Answer:
    *   *   **Yes, clear targets set**: You have clear targets set for sustainability goals.
    *   *   **Yes, some goals**: You have some sustainability goals.
    *   *   **No formal goals**: You do not have any formal sustainability goals.
*   **Top Areas for Improvement (20)**: Select all areas that apply for sustainability improvement.

## Tips

*   Be truthful and accurate when answering. This is a self-assessment tool, and the results of your efforts will reflect your progress in sustainable business practices.
*   If you are unsure of a term or practice, refer to your company's policies, reports, or previous assessments for guidance.
