# treasury_forecast_confidence_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users about their confidence in their business forecast. The purpose of this form is to understand the current state of user's confidence in their forecast and identify areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out each field as required, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. first_page (\*): This field is optional.
### 2. select_one (\*): 
* select_one (required): This field is a selection field. Please choose the option that best describes your current confidence level. The options available are:
* option 1
* option 2
* option 3

### 3. select_multiple (\*): This field is a multiple selection field. Choose all options that apply to your risk factors. The options available are:
* option 4
* option 5
* option 6

### 4. risk_1 (\*): This field is a number field. Please enter your risk score for this factor.
### 5. risk_2 (\*): This field is also a number field. Please enter your risk score for this factor.
### 6. date (\*): This field is a date field. Please enter the date of your forecast.
### 7. time (\*): This field is a time field. Please enter the time of your forecast.
### 8. note (\*): This field is a note field. Please enter any additional comments or notes about your forecast.
### 9. email (\*): This field is an email field. Please enter your email address.
### 10. phone (\*): This field is a text field. Please enter your phone number.
### 11. risk_1 (\*): This field is a text field. Please enter your risk factor 1.
### 12. risk_2 (\*): This field is also a text field. Please enter your risk factor 2.
### 13. risk_3 (\*): This field is a text field. Please enter your risk factor 3.
### 14. risk_4 (\*): This field is another text field. Please enter your risk factor 4.

# Tips
* Please be accurate and clear when filling out each field.
* If unsure, leave fields blank or ask for help.
* The survey will be reviewed and used to identify areas that may need improvement.
* The information will be kept private and secure.
* If you have any feedback, please provide it in the note field.
