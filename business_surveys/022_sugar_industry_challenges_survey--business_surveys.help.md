# sugar_industry_challenges_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from the sugar industry on their pain points and priorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your role in the industry (Association, Business, Other, Researcher).
2. Choose the region you operate in (Americas, Asia, Europe, Other).
3. Enter up to 2 pain points that you are experiencing in the industry.
4. Assign a priority level (1-5) to each pain point.
5. Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further information.
6. (Optional) If you wish, you can also provide additional information about your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Industry Pain Points**: Please list up to 2 specific challenges you are currently facing in the sugar industry.
* **Priorities**: Assign a priority level (1-5) to each pain point. Please note that 1 is the highest priority and 5 is the lowest.
* **Role**: Please select your role in the sugar industry (Association, Business, Other, Researcher).
* **Region**: Please choose the region you operate in (Americas, Asia, Europe, Other).
* **Submissions**: This field is for additional comments or information about your submission.
* **Pain Point 1**, **Pain Point 2**, **Priority 1**, and **Priority 2**: Please enter numbers to correspond to the challenges and priorities you listed above.
* **Submitted By**: Please enter your email address so that we can contact you for further information.
* **Submitted At** and **Submitted Time**: These fields are optional and will be used to track when and who submitted the form. Please be aware that these fields are not required.
