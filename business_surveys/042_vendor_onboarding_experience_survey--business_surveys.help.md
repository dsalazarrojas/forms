# Vendor Onboarding Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from vendors about their onboarding experience with our company. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions on each page as accurately as possible.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the answer that best represents your experience.
3. If you have any comments or additional feedback, please use the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Information** (Text, Optional)
	+ This field is for providing your vendor name or company name.
* **Supplier Information** (Text, Optional)
	+ This field is for providing additional information about your supplier, if needed.
* **Supplier Address** (Text, Optional)
	+ This field is for providing your supplier's address.
* **Contact Details** (Text, Optional)
	+ This field is for providing your contact details, including name, email, and phone number.
* **Onboarding Experience** (Select Multiple, Optional)
	+ This question asks if you had a positive or negative onboarding experience with us.
	+ Please select "Yes" if you had a positive experience, "No" if you had a negative experience, or "N/A" if you don't have an experience with us.
* **Time To First Shipment** (Select One, Optional)
	+ This question asks about the time it took for your first shipment to be delivered.
	+ Please select "Yes" if your first shipment was delivered within the expected time frame, "No" if it was delayed, or "N/A" if it was canceled or postponed.
* **Shipment Frequency** (Select One, Optional)
	+ This question asks about the frequency of your shipments with us.
	+ Please select "Yes" if your shipments are frequent and regular, "No" if they are infrequent, or "N/A" if it was a one-time shipment.
* **First Shipment Date** (Date, Optional)
	+ This field is for providing the date of your first shipment.
* **First Shipment Time** (Time, Optional)
	+ This field is for providing the time of your first shipment.
* **Onboarding Process** (Text, Optional)
	+ This field is for providing any additional information about the onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Experience Rating** (Select One, Optional)
	+ This question asks for your overall rating of the onboarding experience.
	+ Please select "Good" for a positive experience, "Fair" for a neutral experience, or "Poor" for a negative experience.
* **Shipment Frequency Rating** (Select One, Optional)
	+ This question asks for your rating of the shipment frequency.
	+ Please select "Good" for a positive experience, "Fair" for a neutral experience, or "Poor" for a negative experience.
* **Onboarding Support** (Select One, Optional)
	+ This question asks if you received support during the onboarding process.
	+ Please select "Yes" if you received support, "No" if you didn't receive support, or "N/A" if it was not applicable.
* **Overall Rating** (Select One, Optional)
	+ This question asks for your overall rating of our company.
	+ Please select "Good" for a positive experience, "Fair" for a neutral experience, or "Poor" for a negative experience.
* **Comments** (Text, Optional)
	+ This field is for providing any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Email** (Email, Optional)
	+ This field is for providing your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional)
	+ This field is for providing your phone number.
