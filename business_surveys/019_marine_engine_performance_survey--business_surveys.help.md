# Marine Engine Performance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marine Engine Performance Survey is designed to gather information about the status of your marine engine's performance. This form will help identify areas that require attention, track performance trends, and inform future maintenance and optimization efforts.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct engine type (Gasoline or Diesel) that best describes your marine engine.
2. Enter your assessment of the engine's fuel efficiency (in units of miles per gallon).
3. Indicate the engine capacity (in liters or horsepower).
4. Report your assessment of the engine's horsepower, if applicable.
5. Enter the engine's fuel consumption, if applicable.
6. Choose the type of transmission (Manual or Automatic) associated with the engine.
7. Evaluate the engine's condition using the provided scale (Good, Fair, Poor).
8. Select the maintenance tasks that have been performed or are required for the engine, if applicable.
9. Enter the engine's age in years.
10. Enter the engine's mileage in kilometers or miles.
11. Indicate if the engine has required any repairs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a placeholder to provide an introduction to the form. Please fill it in concisely and accurately.
* **engine_type**: Select the type of engine (Gasoline or Diesel) that best describes your marine engine.
* **fuel_efficiency**: Enter your assessment of the engine's fuel efficiency, in units of miles per gallon or liters per kilometer.
* **engine_capacity**: Enter the engine capacity in liters or horsepower, if applicable.
* **horsepower**: Enter the engine's horsepower, if applicable.
* **fuel_consumption**: Enter the engine's fuel consumption, in units of gallons or liters.
* **transmission_type**: Choose the type of transmission (Manual or Automatic) associated with the engine.
* **engine_condition**: Evaluate the engine's condition using the provided scale (Good, Fair, Poor).
* **maintenance**: Select the maintenance tasks that have been performed or are required for the engine, if applicable.
* **engine_year**: Enter the engine's age in years.
* **engine_mileage**: Enter the engine's mileage in kilometers or miles.
* **engine_repair**: Indicate if the engine has required any repairs.
