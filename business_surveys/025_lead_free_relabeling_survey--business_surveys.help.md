# Lead Free Relabeling Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lead Free Relabeling Survey is a form designed to gather customer feedback and input on their experiences with Lead Free products. This form collects information on various aspects of the customer's interactions, such as satisfaction, product labeling, and overall experience, to help the development team improve the quality and relabeling of the products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and answer all the questions that apply to your experience.
2. For each question, choose the relevant response from the provided options.
3. Be as specific as possible when providing your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lead Free Product Survey** (`lead_free_product_survey`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about your experience with the Lead Free products. Select all the options that apply to your experience.
* **Customer Survey** (`customer_survey`, text, required: false): Share your thoughts and feedback about your experience with the Lead Free products.
* **Experience With Products** (`experience_with_products`, select_one, required: false): This question is about your experience with the Lead Free products. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Product Relabeling Process** (`product_relabeling_process`, text, required: false): Share any feedback or suggestions you have about the relabeling process of the Lead Free products.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the Lead Free products, using a number from 1 to 5.
* **Lead Free Relabeling Survey** (`lead_free_relabeling_survey`, select_one, required: false): This question is about the relabeling survey itself. Select the option that best describes your experience with this form.
* **Customer Suggestion** (`customer_suggestion`, text, required: false): Share any suggestions or ideas you have about how to improve the Lead Free products or services.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, required: false): Rate your overall experience with the Lead Free products, using a number from 1 to 5.
* **Product Relabeling Frequency** (`product_relabeling_frequency`, select_one, required: false): This question is about how often you have interacted with the Lead Free products. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Customer Experience Score** (`customer_experience_score`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate your experience with the Lead Free products in each of the provided areas.
* **Lead Free Product Quality** (`lead_free_product_quality`, select_one, required: false): Rate the quality of the Lead Free products, using the provided options.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, text, required: false): Share your thoughts about the customer service you received from the Lead Free team.
* **Customer Service Quality** (`customer_service_quality`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate the quality of the customer service you received, using the provided options.
* **Lead Free Product Characteristics** (`lead_free_product_characteristics`, text, required: false): Share any specific characteristics you think are important for the Lead Free products.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you have about the Lead Free products or services.
