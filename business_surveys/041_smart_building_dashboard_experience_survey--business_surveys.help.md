# Smart Building Dashboard Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Building Dashboard Experience Survey is a short, user-friendly form designed to gather feedback and insights from users about their experience with the Smart Building Dashboard. Your input will help us understand your needs and preferences to improve the dashboard.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
2. Select the options that best reflect your experience with the dashboard.
3. Make sure to fill out all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
4. If you have any feature requests or suggestions, type them in the "Do you have any feature requests for the dashboard" field.
5. Enter your email and phone number (if available) so we can get in touch with you for any follow-up questions or to provide updates.
