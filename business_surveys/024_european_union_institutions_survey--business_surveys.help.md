# European Union Institutions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about European Union institutions and preferences. It is intended for external use and may be used for research, data collection, or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your company name in the company field.
3. Select your preferred European Union institution from the eu body field.
4. Select any other European Union institutions you are interested in from the eu_body_2 field.
5. Enter your contact information in the contact field.
6. Enter your email address in the email field.
7. Add any additional notes in the note field.
8. Select a date and time for the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name here.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company name here.
* **EU Body** (`eu_body`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the main European Union institution you are interested in. Options include EU Commission, EU Parliament, and EU Court of Auditors.
* **Other EU Body** (`eu_body_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any other European Union institutions you are interested in. Options include EU Commission, EU Parliament, and EU Court of Auditors.
* **Contact** (`contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional contact information here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address here.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes here.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Select a date for the survey.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Select a time for the survey.
* **Eu Body 3** (`eu_body_3`, `select_one`, required: false): Select another European Union institution you are interested in. Options include EU Commission, EU Parliament, and EU Court of Auditors.
* **Eu Body 4** (`eu_body_4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select more European Union institutions you are interested in. Options include EU Commission, EU Parliament, and EU Court of Auditors.
* **Eu Body 5** (`eu_body_5`, `select_one`, required: false): Select another European Union institution you are interested in. Options include EU Commission, EU Parliament, and EU Court of Auditors.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions, contact the relevant European Union institution or organization.
* If you need to submit the form multiple times, please make sure to fill it in completely and accurately each time.
