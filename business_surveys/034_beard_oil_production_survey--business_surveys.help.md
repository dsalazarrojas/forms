# Beard Oil Production Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from companies producing beard oil products to improve the overall quality and standardization of these products in the market.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, providing accurate and detailed information about your company's production practices.
2. Select all that apply for multi-select questions.
3. Use the options provided as a starting point, but feel free to add additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" section if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent Name**: Enter your company's name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your company's email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter your company's name.
* **Your Role** (`role`): Select the title of the person completing the form (e.g., Owner, Production Manager, Quality Control, Formulator, Marketing, etc.).
* **Monthly Production Volume** (`production_volume`): Select the volume of beard oil produced by your company per month (e.g., Under 100 bottles, 100-500 bottles, 501-1000 bottles, Over 5000 bottles, etc.).
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`): Select the number of years your company has been operating (e.g., Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, 3-5 years, Over 10 years, etc.).
* **Primary Carrier Oils Used** (`primary_ingredients`): Select all carrier oils used in your beard oil production (e.g., Jojoba Oil, Argan Oil, Coconut Oil, Sweet Almond Oil, Grapeseed Oil, Castor Oil, Hemp Seed Oil, Other, etc.).
* **Essential Oils Used** (`essential_oils_used`): Select all essential oils used for fragrance purposes (e.g., Cedarwood, Sandalwood, Peppermint, Eucalyptus, Tea Tree, Bergamot, Lavender, Unscented Options, etc.).
* **Production Method** (`production_method`): Select the method used to produce your beard oil (e.g., Hand Mixed Small Batches, Semi-Automated, Fully Automated, Contract Manufacturing, etc.).
* **Quality Control Measures** (`quality_control`): Select all quality control measures in place (e.g., Ingredient Testing, Batch Testing, Stability Testing, Microbial Testing, Visual Inspection, Customer Feedback, None, etc.).
* **Primary Packaging Type** (`packaging_type`): Select the type of packaging used for your beard oil products (e.g., Amber Glass Dropper, Clear Glass Dropper, Plastic Dropper, Pump Bottle, Other, etc.).
* **Biggest Production Challenges** (`production_challenges`): Select all production challenges your company faces (e.g., Ingredient Sourcing, Cost Management, Quality Consistency, Scaling Production, Regulatory Compliance, Packaging Supply, Storage, Other, etc.).
* **Certifications Held** (`certification_status`): Select all certifications held by your company (e.g., Organic Certified, GMP Certified, Cruelty Free, Vegan Certified, None, In Process, etc.).
* **How Do You Collect Feedback** (`customer_feedback`): Select the method used to collect customer feedback (e.g., Product Reviews, Surveys, Social Media, Direct Contact, Multiple Methods, Not Systematically, etc.).
* **Customer Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`): Select the average customer satisfaction rating (e.g., 4.5 to 5 stars, 4 to 4.5 stars, 3.5 to 4 stars, Below 3.5 stars, Not Tracked, etc.).
* **Future Production Plans** (`future_plans`): Select the future plans for your company's production (e.g., Expand Production, Maintain Current Level, Add New Products, Improve Quality, Reduce Costs, etc.).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_feedback`): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your company's production practices.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and accurately to provide valuable insights.
* If you have any further questions or need clarification, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* Thank you for participating in this survey! Your feedback is greatly appreciated.
