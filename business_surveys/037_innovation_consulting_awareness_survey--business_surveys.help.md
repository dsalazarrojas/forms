# Innovation Consulting Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge users' awareness, perception, and involvement in innovation within their organization. The survey is intended to gather information about respondents' opinions on innovation services, communication, and investment in innovation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each section carefully and accurately. You may ask for assistance if you have any questions or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name (1)**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Company Name (2)**: Type your company's name as it is known in the market or industry.
* **Email (4)**: Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone (5)**: Enter your business phone number.
* **Department (6)**: Type the name of your department or team within your company.
* **Job Title (7)**: Enter your job title within your company.
* **Company (8)**: Select your company from the list.
* **Role (9)**: Select all roles that apply to you within your company.
	+ This field helps us understand your involvement in innovation within your company. Please select all roles that you are involved in or responsible for.
* **Awareness of Innovation (10)**: Indicate whether you are aware of innovation within your company.
* **Perception of Innovation (11)**: Select how you perceive innovation within your company.
* **Innovation Services (12)**: Select services that you have used or are familiar with within innovation.
* **Frequency of Communication (13)**: Indicate how frequently you communicate with the innovation team or department.
* **Level of Involvement in Innovation (14)**: Choose your level of involvement in innovation within your company.
* **Confidence in Innovation (15)**: Indicate your confidence level in innovation within your company.
* **Satisfaction with Innovation (16)**: Select your level of satisfaction with innovation within your company.
* **Innovation Capacity (17)**: Indicate whether your company has the capacity to innovate.
* **Innovation Readiness (18)**: Select whether your company is innovation ready.
* **Innovation Strategy (19)**: Select your evaluation of your company's innovation strategy.
* **Innovation Investment (20)**: Enter a numerical value indicating your company's investment in innovation.
* **Innovation Partnership Opportunity (21)**: Indicate whether you see an opportunity for partnership in innovation within your company.
* **Innovation Capacity of Partners (22)**: Select whether your partners have the capacity to innovate.
* **Innovation Investment in Capacity (23)**: Indicate whether you invest in your partner's capacity to innovate.
* **Innovation Partnership Readiness (24)**: Select whether your partners are ready for innovation.
* **Innovation Strategy Execution (25)**: Select your evaluation of the execution of your company's innovation strategy.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* This survey is intended to gauge your opinions and perceptions on innovation within your company. Your input is valuable to us.
