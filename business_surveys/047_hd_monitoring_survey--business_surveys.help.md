# hd_monitoring_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey for customers to provide feedback and information about their experience with the service offered by the business. It aims to monitor and improve the service by understanding customer usage and satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the type of service you use from the "Service Type" dropdown menu.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. If you have a contact email address, enter it in the "Email" field.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Choose how you perceive the quality of service you receive from the "Quality of Service" dropdown menu.
6. Describe how often you use the service in the "Service Type Frequency" dropdown menu.
7. Select how often you send emails to the business in the "Email Frequency" dropdown menu.
8. If you would like to request a service, select "True" in the "Would you like to request a service?" question.
9. If you have any additional comments or feedback, you can type them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one): This field allows you to choose the type of service you use.
* **Name** (`name`, text): Enter your name to help us know who you are.
* **Email** (`email`, email): If you have a contact email address, enter it here.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text): If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Quality of Service** (`service_quality`, select_one): Choose how you perceive the quality of service you receive.
* **Additional Comments** (`comment`, note): Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **How often do you use HD Monitoring Service?** (`service_type_frequency`, select_one): Describe how often you use the service.
* **How often do you send emails?** (`email_frequency`, select_one): Select how often you send emails to the business.
* **Would you like to request a service?** (`service_request`, select_one): If you would like to request a service, select "True".
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text): Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Service Type Frequency** (`service_type_frequency`, select_one): This field is not clearly labeled, but likely asks for the frequency of using the service. It is recommended to choose how often you use the service.

**Tips**

* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Your feedback is highly appreciated and will help us improve our service.
