# Team Superlative Nominations Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Superlative Nominations Survey is used to collect information about team members who deserve recognition and awards for their outstanding work and achievements. This form helps to gather details about the nominated team, including their name, nominee's name, reason for nomination, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the team's name and nominee's name in the respective fields.
2.  Enter the nominee's email address in the Nominee Email field.
3.  Provide a brief reason for nominating the team member in the Reason for Nominating field.
4.  Select the team member's department and the nominator's department from the available options (if any).
5.  Enter the nominator's email address and name in the Nominator Email and Nominator fields.
6.  Specify the team size and division in the Team Size and Team Division fields.
7.  Enter the team's location in the Team Location field.
8.  Choose a date for the nomination in the Nomination Date field.
9.  Enter the nominator's title in the Nominator Title field.
10. Select the nominee's supervisor from the available options (if any).
11. Enter the nominator's supervisor in the Nominator Supervisor field.
12. Enter a reason for the recognition in the Reason for Recognition field.
13. Add any comments from the nominator in the Nominator Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the team being nominated.
* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the team member being nominated.
* **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the team member being nominated.
* **Reason for Nominating** (`reason_for_nominating`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief reason for nominating the team member.
* **Nominee Department** (`nominee_department`, text, required/optional): Select the department of the team member being nominated.
* **Nominator** (`nominator`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person making the nomination.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person making the nomination.
* **Nominator Department** (`nominator_department`, text, required/optional): Select the department of the person making the nomination.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, required/optional): Enter the size of the team being nominated.
* **Team Division** (`team_division`, text, required/optional): Select the division of the team being nominated.
* **Team Location** (`team_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the team being nominated.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, required/optional): Choose a date for the nomination.
* **Nominator Title** (`nominator_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the person making the nomination.
* **Nominee Supervisor** (`nominee_supervisor`, text, required/optional): Select the supervisor of the team member being nominated.
* **Nominator Supervisor** (`nominator_supervisor`, text, required/optional): Enter the supervisor of the person making the nomination.
* **Reason for Recognition** (`reason_for_recognition`, text, required/optional): Enter a reason for the recognition.
* **Nominator Comments** (`nominator_comments`, text, required/optional): Add any comments from the nominator.
