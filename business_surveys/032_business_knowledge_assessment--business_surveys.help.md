# Business Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Knowledge Assessment form is designed to evaluate your expertise in various business-related areas. Please answer the questions honestly to help identify your strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
2. Enter your job title or position.
3. Rate your expertise in the industry, from Novice to Expert.
4. Rate your understanding of financial concepts, from Low to High.
5. Rate your familiarity with marketing principles, from Low to High.
6. Rate your operational management skills, from Low to High.
7. If you have specific areas where you would like further training or support, type them in the text box.
8. If you have recommended resources or tools that you find helpful, type them in the text box.
9. Enter your email address (if you'd like to receive follow-up on your assessment results).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Role** (`role`, text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Industry knowledge** (`industry_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate your expertise in the industry, from Novice to Expert.
	+ Novice: You have limited knowledge in the industry.
	+ Intermediate: You have some knowledge, but it's not up-to-date or in-depth.
	+ Advanced: You have strong knowledge, but there's still room for improvement.
	+ Expert: You have extensive knowledge in the industry.
* **Financial literacy** (`financial_literacy`, select_one, required): Rate your understanding of financial concepts, from Low to High.
	+ Low: You have limited understanding of financial concepts.
	+ Medium: You have some understanding, but it's not comprehensive.
	+ High: You have strong understanding of financial concepts.
* **Marketing understanding** (`marketing_understanding`, select_one, required): Rate your familiarity with marketing principles, from Low to High.
	+ Low: You have limited familiarity with marketing principles.
	+ Medium: You have some familiarity, but it's not up-to-date or in-depth.
	+ High: You have strong familiarity with marketing principles.
* **Operations skills** (`operations_skills`, select_one, required): Rate your operational management skills, from Low to High.
	+ Low: You have limited operational management skills.
	+ Medium: You have some operational management skills, but there's still room for improvement.
	+ High: You have strong operational management skills.
* **Development needs** (`development_needs`, text, optional): If you have specific areas where you would like further training or support, type them in this text box.
* **Recommended resources** (`recommended_resources`, text, optional): If you have recommended resources or tools that you find helpful, type them in this text box.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive follow-up on your assessment results.
This form is designed to help you evaluate your knowledge and skills in various areas related to business. Please answer honestly, and feel free to suggest resources or tools that you find helpful.
