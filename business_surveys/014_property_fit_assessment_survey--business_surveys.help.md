# Property Fit Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Property Fit Assessment Survey is designed to gather detailed information about a property to facilitate a property fit assessment. This assessment helps determine how well a property meets the needs and preferences of a specific use case. Please complete this form with the correct and relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each field carefully, ensuring that the information provided is accurate and relevant.
2. Select the correct options from the provided lists for questions with multiple options.
3. Use the provided fields to describe your needs and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Property Id**
 (`property_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the ID of the property to be assessed.
 
### 2. **Property Location**
 (`property_location`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the property's location.
 
### 3. **Property Type**
 (`property_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the types that apply to the property:
 • Option 1: Residential
 • Option 2: Commercial
 • Option 3: Industrial

### 4. **Contact Info**
 (`contact_info`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the primary contact method:
 • Option 1: Email
 • Option 2: Phone
 • Option 3: Mobile

### 5. **Budget**
 (`budget`, `number`, required: false): Enter the property's budget.
 
### 6. **Needs and Preferences**
 (`needs_preferences`, `text`, required: false): Describe the needs and preferences of the property.
 
### 7. **Property Match**
 (`property_match`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all that apply to the property:
 • Option 1: Yes
 • Option 2: No
 • Option 3: Maybe

### 8. **Property Match Score**
 (`property_match_score`, `number`, required: false): Enter a score out of 10 for how well the property matches your needs.
 
Please note that all fields are optional but will greatly aid in the assessment process.
