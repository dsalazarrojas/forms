# E Catalog Registration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Catalog Registration Survey is a form designed to collect information from business contacts, such as their contact details, interests, and communication preferences. This data is used to create a catalog entry for the business, which will be used for internal reference and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Provide your contact title in the "Contact Title" field.
3. Enter your business email address in the "Email" field.
4. List your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
6. Visit your business website in the "Website" field.
7. Select your areas of interest in the "Interests" field.
8. Choose your preferred method of communication in the "Communication Preferences" field.
9. Specify your product interests in the "Product Interests" field.
10. Choose your preferred follow-up frequency in the "Follow-up" field.
11. Add any additional notes about your business in the "Notes" field.
12. Review and adjust your information as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact title or position within the company.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): List your business phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required/optional): Enter your company name.
* **Website** (`website`, text, required/optional): Visit your business website.
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your areas of interest (e.g., Interest 1, Interest 2, Interest 3).
* **Communication Preferences** (`communication_preferences`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., Phone, Email, Mail).
* **Product Interests** (`product_interests`, select_multiple, required/optional): Specify the products you are interested in (e.g., Product 1, Product 2, Product 3).
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how often you would like to be contacted (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, Never).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your business.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, required/optional): Not applicable for end-users.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, required/optional): Not applicable for end-users.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, required/optional): Not applicable for end-users.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, required/optional): Not applicable for end-users.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required/optional): Not applicable for end-users.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, required/optional): Not applicable for end-users.

Note: All fields marked as "required" are mandatory fields and must be completed.
