# Market Feasibility Research Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather all necessary information for market feasibility research requests, ensuring that your request is processed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief research brief describing the market feasibility research you are requesting.
2. Select the market segment for your research.
3. Choose the marketing strategy for your research.
4. Specify the target audience for your research.
5. Provide the research duration and frequency.
6. Outline the research objectives.
7. Specify the submission details.
8. Set a submission deadline.
9. Enter your contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Research Brief** (research_brief, text, required): Briefly describe the market feasibility research you are requesting. This will help us understand the nature of your request.
* **Market Segment** (market_segment, select_one, required): Choose the market segment for your research. This will help us identify the target market for your analysis.
* **Marketing Strategy** (marketing_strategy, select_one, required): Choose the marketing strategy for your research. This will enable us to tailor our approach to your specific needs.
* **Target Audience** (target_audience, select_multiple, optional): Specify the target audience for your research. This will help us identify the key stakeholders involved.
* **Market Feasibility** (market_feasibility, number, optional): Provide a numerical value for market feasibility. This will help us understand the level of feasibility.
* **Marketing Budget** (marketing_budget, text, optional): Enter your marketing budget for the research.
* **Research Duration** (research_duration, time, optional): Specify the duration of your research.
* **Research Frequency** (research_frequency, select_multiple, required): Choose the research frequency. This will help us understand how often you need the research to be conducted.
* **Research Objectives** (research_objectives, text, optional): Outline the research objectives.
* **Submission Details** (submission_details, select_one, required): Choose the submission details.
* **Submission Deadline** (submission_deadline, date, optional): Set a submission deadline for your research.
* **Contact Information** (contact_number, text, optional): Provide your contact information, including phone number.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email, email, optional): Enter your contact email.
* **Contact Person** (contact_person, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments, text, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **File Upload** (file_upload, text, optional): Upload any relevant files.
* **Signature Upload** (signature_upload, text, optional): Upload your signature.
* **File Download** (file_download, text, optional): Specify the file to be downloaded.
* **Signature Download** (signature_download, text, optional): Specify the signature to be downloaded.
* **Submitter Name** (submitter_name, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Email** (submitter_email, email, optional): Enter your email.
* **Submitter Phone** (submitter_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Comments** (submitter_comments, text, optional): Enter any comments.
