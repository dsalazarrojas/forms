# Geometric Dimensioning And Tolerancing (GD&T) Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing (GD&T) Survey is a tool used to collect information about a company's GD&T practices, knowledge, and submission processes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best describe your company's current GD&T practices.
2. Answer the questions about your company's GD&T knowledge and submission processes.
3. Fill in the text fields with relevant information about your company's GD&T survey template and submission processes.
