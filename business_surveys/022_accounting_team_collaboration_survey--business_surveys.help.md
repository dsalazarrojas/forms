# Accounting Team Collaboration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accounting Team Collaboration Survey is designed to gather feedback from team members on their experiences with collaboration within the team. Your input will help identify areas for improvement and contribute to creating a more effective team environment.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please answer each question with your honest and constructive feedback.
- Ensure you complete the form carefully, as your feedback is essential for us to understand your perspective.
- If you have any suggestions or comments, please include them in the "Suggestions for Better Teamwork" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Member Name** (team_member_name, text, optional): Please enter your name as a team member.
* **Rate Team Communication** (communication_rating, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of team communication on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Task Distribution Fairness** (task_distribution_fairness, select_one, required): Indicate if you feel tasks are distributed fairly or not.
* **Collaboration Tools Used** (collaboration_tools_used, select_multiple, optional): Select all collaboration tools used by the team, such as Slack, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Internal Wiki, or Project Board.
* **Frequency of Team Meetings** (frequency_of_team_meetings, select_one, required): Choose how often the team meets, such as Daily, Weekly, Bi-weekly, or Monthly.
* **Meeting Effectiveness** (meeting_effectiveness, select_one, required): Assess the productivity of team meetings, considering whether they are Not Effective, Moderately Effective, or Highly Effective.
* **Collaboration Barriers** (collaboration_barriers, text, optional): Identify any obstacles that hinder the team's collaboration.
* **Support from Management** (support_from_management, select_one, required): Evaluate the level of support received from management, choosing from Insufficient, Sufficient, or Outstanding.
* **Suggestions for Better Teamwork** (suggestions_for_better_teamwork, text, optional): Share any ideas or suggestions for improving team collaboration.
* **Feedback (Anonymous)** (anon_note, note, optional): Provide feedback that will help us understand your perspective without revealing your name. If you choose to provide your name, your feedback will be attributed to you.
