# Follower Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended for businesses who want to collect information about their followers. It will help you understand their demographics, engagement levels, and contact preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

1. Read the guide carefully before starting.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and in the required format.
3. Select the options that best fit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What do you want to talk about** (`title`, `text`, `optional`): This is a text field where you can provide any information you'd like to discuss with us.
* **Which of the following categories are you interested in** (`which_categories`, `select_multiple`, `true`): Choose all the categories that interest you from the list:
	+ Category A
	+ Category B
	+ Category C
* **What type of follower are you?** (`follower_type`, `select_one`, `true`): Select one option from the list:
	+ Social Media
	+ Influencer
	+ Content Creator
* **What is your age group?** (`age_group`, `select_one`, `true`): Choose one option from the list:
	+ 18-24
	+ 25-34
	+ 35-44
	+ 45-54
	+ 55+
* **How often do you engage with us?** (`engagement_level`, `select_one`, `true`): Select one option from the list:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Rarely
* **How do you like to contact us?** (`contact_method`, `select_multiple`, `true`): Choose all the methods you prefer from the list:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ In-person
* **Do you have any additional comments or concerns?** (`additional_comments`, `note`, `false`): If you have anything else you'd like to share, please use this field to provide additional comments or concerns.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and truthfully.
* Choose the options that best fit your response.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, please ask us for clarification.
