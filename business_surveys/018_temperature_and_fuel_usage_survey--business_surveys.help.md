# Temperature And Fuel Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a questionnaire designed to collect information about your residential heating systems, including the types of fuel used, thermostat settings, energy saving habits, and energy consumption patterns. Your input will help in understanding and improving the efficiency of heating systems.

## How To Complete This Form

This form consists of multiple pages with various questions. Please answer each question carefully, and if you have any doubts, do not hesitate to ask. You can skip any question that you do not have an answer for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Heating Fuel Types**: This question is about the types of fuel used in your heating system, such as Gas, Oil, Electricity, or Other. Please select all that apply.
* **Thermostat Settings**: This question is about the temperature settings of your heating system, ranging from 1-5°C to 25°C or warmer. You can select one or multiple options that best describe your settings.
* **Energy Saving Habits**: This question is about your habits for reducing energy consumption when using your heating system, such as Reduce usage, Use a timer or timer switch, Turn off the radiator, Lowering the temperature, or Use a smart radiator valve. Please select all that apply.
* **Temperature Range (Winter/Summer)**: These questions are about the temperature ranges you usually set on your heating system in winter and summer, in °C. Please enter a number that best describes the range.
* **Hours of Usage (Winter/Summer)**: These questions are about the number of hours your heating system works in winter and summer. Please enter a number that best describes the hours.
* **Energy Source of Heating System 1/2**: This question is about the type of energy source of your first/second heating system, such as Gas boiler, Oil boiler, Electricity, Heat pump, or Radiator. Please select one option.
* **Frequency of Usage (Heating System 1/2)**: This question is about how often you use your first/second heating system, such as Daily, Weekly, Fortnightly, Monthly, or Rarely. Please select all that apply.
* **Usage by Location**: These questions are about the areas of your home where you use your first/second heating system, such as Living room, Kitchen, Bedroom, Bathroom, Study or office, or Other. Please select all that apply.
* **Energy Savings Habits (Heating System 1/2)**: This question is about your habits for reducing energy consumption when using your first/second heating system, similar to Energy Saving Habits.
* **Total Energy Consumption (Winter)**: These questions are about the estimated energy consumption of your first/second heating system in winter, in kWh. Please enter a number that best describes your consumption.
* **Total Fuel Consumption (Winter)**: These questions are about the estimated fuel consumption of your first/second heating system in winter, in liters. Please enter a number that best describes your consumption.
* **Carbon Footprint**: These questions are about the estimated carbon footprint of your first/second heating system, in tons of CO2 per year. Please enter a number that best describes your footprint.
* **Other Heating Sources**: This question is about other heating sources you use, such as Central heating system, Portable heater, or Other. Please select all that apply.
* **Comments**: This is an optional field for any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
