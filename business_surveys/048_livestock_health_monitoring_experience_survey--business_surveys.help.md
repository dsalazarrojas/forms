# Livestock Health Monitoring Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Livestock Health Monitoring Experience Survey is designed to gather information and feedback from farmers and ranchers about their experiences with monitoring the health of their livestock. This survey aims to understand their challenges, improvements, and methods used in monitoring their animals' health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select all applicable options for the questions that ask for multiple selections.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
4. Make sure to submit the form when you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Monitoring Methods** (`monitoring_methods`, `text`, optional): This field is used to collect information about the methods used to monitor the health of your livestock. Please provide a brief description of the methods you use, such as regular checks, vaccinations, or veterinary visits.
* **Monitoring Frequency** (`monitoring_frequency`, `number`, optional): This field is used to collect information about how often you monitor the health of your livestock. Please enter a number that represents how many times a month you monitor your animals' health.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to collect information about the challenges you face in monitoring the health of your livestock. Please select one of the options that best describes your experience:
	+ Option 1: Insufficient resources
	+ Option 2: Limited access to information
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify in the text field below)
* **Improvements** (`improvements`, `text`, optional): This field is used to collect suggestions or ideas for improving the health monitoring methods or programs. Please provide a brief description of what you think could be improved.
* **Animal Health Programs** (`animal_health_programs`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is used to collect information about the animal health programs you are or have been part of. Please select all applicable options:
	+ Program 1
	+ Program 2
	+ Program 3
* **Challenges Date** (`challenges_date`, `date`, optional): This field is used to collect information about the date you experienced the challenges in monitoring the health of your livestock.
* **Challenges Time** (`challenges_time`, `time`, optional): This field is used to collect information about the time you experienced the challenges in monitoring the health of your livestock.
* **Monitoring Methods Frequency** (`monitoring_methods_frequency`, `number`, optional): This field is used to collect information about how often you monitor the health of your livestock in relation to the methods used.
* **Animal Health Programs Improvements** (`animal_health_programs_improvements`, `text`, optional): This field is used to collect suggestions or ideas for improving the animal health programs.
* **Programs Improvements** (`program_improvements`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is used to collect information about improvements for animal health programs. Please select all applicable options:
	+ Improvement 1
	+ Improvement 2
* **Challenges 1** (`challenges_1`, `text`, optional): This field is used to collect additional information about the challenges you face in monitoring the health of your livestock.
* **Challenges 2** (`challenges_2`, `text`, optional): This field is used to collect more information about the challenges you face in monitoring the health of your livestock.
* **Monitoring Methods 2** (`monitoring_methods_2`, `text`, optional): This field is used to collect information about the methods you use to monitor the health of your livestock.
* ... (and so on)

Note that all fields are optional, and you can provide as much information as you are comfortable with. Thank you for participating in this survey!
