# Impact Of Heat Wave Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Impact of Heat Wave Survey is designed to gather information on the effects of heat waves on businesses, focusing on local and individual needs, coping strategies, and data collection frequency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and questions.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, providing the best available information.
3. For select_multiple, select all applicable options that apply to your business.
4. For select_one, choose the most relevant option that best describes your situation.
5. For text fields, provide a clear and concise answer in the space provided.
6. For number fields, enter a numerical value as requested.
7. Proofread your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, optional): This is a general introduction to your response. Please provide a brief overview of your business and its operations.

### Heat Wave Impact
* **Heat Wave Impact** (`heatwave_impact`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about the effects of heat waves on your business. Select all options that apply to your experience.
	+ Heat Wave Impact
	+ Other

### Coping Strategies
* **Coping Strategies** (`coping_strategies`, `text`, optional): Describe the ways in which your business coped with the heat wave event. This may include any adaptation or adjustments you made to your operations or services.

### Local Needs
* **Local Needs** (`local_needs`, `select_one`, optional): Identify the specific local needs your business addressed during the heat wave event.
	+ Local Needs
	+ Other

### Individual Needs
* **Individual Needs** (`individual_needs`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all individual needs your business addressed during the heat wave event.
	+ Individual Needs
	+ Other

### Data Collection Frequency
* **Data Collection Frequency** (`data_collection_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how often you would like to receive data collection updates during heat wave events.
	+ Data Collection Frequency
	+ Other

### Collection Frequency
* **Collection Frequency** (`collection_frequency`, `number`, optional): If other than daily, please specify the desired frequency of data collection updates.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the heat wave event or this form.

Note: All fields are optional unless otherwise specified as "required".
