# Property Safety Training Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us evaluate the effectiveness of property safety training programs. It will help us identify areas where the training is strong and areas where it can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the type of person responsible for the property (Property Manager, Safety Team, or Other).
2. Choose how relevant each section of the training was to you (1-5, with 5 being very relevant).
3. Rate the quality of each section of the training (1-5, with 5 being excellent).
4. Provide the number of trainees and total number of trainees for each section.
5. Enter the number of training hours completed for each section.
6. Select if the section was completed or not.
7. Enter the date of the last training session for each section.
8. Leave any comments about the training experience.
9. Enter the contact information of the training provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **property_managers** (Property Manager, optional):
    This section is to identify the type of person responsible for the property (Property Manager, Safety Team, or Other). Please select the one that applies to you.
* **safety_teams** (Safety Team, Supervisor, or Other, optional):
    This section is to identify the type of person who attended the training (Safety Team, Supervisor, or Other). Please select the one that applies to you.
* **quality_of_training** (1-5, optional):
    Rate how good the training was for this section, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent. 
* **relevance_of_training** (1-5, optional):
    Rate how relevant the training was for this section, with 1 being not relevant and 5 being very relevant.
* **confidence_levels** (1-5, optional):
    Rate how confident you are in the training outcomes, with 1 being not confident and 5 being very confident.
* **other_trainings** (text, optional):
    Enter any other trainings that are relevant to the property safety.
* **date_of_last_train** (date, optional):
    Enter the date of the last training session.
* **last_train_location** (text, optional):
    Enter the location of the last training session.
* **training_provider** (text, optional):
    Enter the provider of the last training session.
* **trainees_attended** (number, optional):
    Enter the number of trainees for each section.
* **total_trainees** (number, optional):
    Enter the total number of trainees for each section.
* **training_hours** (number, optional):
    Enter the number of training hours completed for each section.
* **trainings_completed** (Completed, Not Completed, On Going, optional):
    Select if the training was completed or not.
* **last_train_date** (date, optional):
    Enter the date of the last training session.
* **trainee_comments** (text, optional):
    Leave any comments about the training experience.
* **training_provider_email** (email, optional):
    Enter the email of the training provider.
* **training_provider_phone** (text, optional):
    Enter the phone number of the training provider.
* **assigned_tool** (text, optional):
    Enter the assigned tool or equipment for the training.
