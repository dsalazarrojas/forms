# recognition_and_appreciation_culture_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from employees on the company's recognition and appreciation culture. It will help the company identify areas of improvement and best practices to enhance the work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best describe your experiences and opinions. Make sure to answer all required questions, and if you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can add them in the text fields at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Survey** (1): Select the type of relationship you have with your manager or team members (Manager Recognition, Team Member Recognition, Colleague, Supervisor, or Other).
* **Recognition Frequency** (2): Choose how often you receive recognition from your manager or team members (1-2 times a month, 2-3 times a month, Weekly, Rarely, or Never).
* **Appreciation Frequency** (3): Choose how often you receive appreciation from your manager or team members (1-2 times a month, 2-3 times a month, Weekly, Rarely, or Never).
* **Recognition Type** (4): Select the type of recognition you prefer (Verbal, Written, or Monetary).
* **Feedback Frequency** (5): Choose how often you receive feedback from your manager or team members (1-2 times a month, 2-3 times a month, Weekly, Rarely, or Never).
* **Feedback Sources** (6): Select who provides you feedback (Manager, Colleague, Supervisor, HR, or Self).
* **Fairness Rating** (7): Rate how fair you feel the recognition and appreciation culture is (on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being completely unfair and 10 being completely fair).
* **Appreciation Program** (8): Check if the company has an appreciation program in place (In place or Not In Place).
* **Program Ideas** (9): Provide any ideas you have for an appreciation program (text field).
* **Suggestions** (10): Share any suggestions you have for improving the recognition and appreciation culture (text field).
* **Fairness Comments** (11): Add any comments about fairness issues (text field).
* **Appreciation Comments** (12): Add any comments about appreciation issues (text field).
* **Employee Satisfaction** (13): Rate your overall satisfaction with the recognition and appreciation culture (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Unsatisfied, or Very Unsatisfied).
* **Suggestions For Improvement** (14): Share any suggestions for improvement (text field).
* **Open Comments** (15): Add any open-ended comments (text field).
* **Overall Fairness** (16): Rate the overall fairness of the recognition and appreciation culture (on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being completely unfair and 10 being completely fair).
* **Overall Appreciation** (17): Rate the overall appreciation of the recognition and appreciation culture (on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being completely lacking and 10 being completely appreciated).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (18): Rate your overall satisfaction with the recognition and appreciation culture (on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being completely dissatisfied and 10 being completely satisfied).
* **Overall Ideas** (19): Rate the overall number of ideas you have for improving the recognition and appreciation culture (on a scale of 1-10).
* **Open Comments 2** (20): Add any additional comments (text field).
* **Open Comments 3** (21): Add any further comments (text field).
* **Open Comments 4** (22): Add any more comments (text field).
* **Open Comments 5** (23): Add any additional comments (text field).
* **Open Comments 6** (24): Add any further comments (text field).
* **Open Comments 7** (25): Add any final comments (text field).

## Tips
* Please answer all required questions.
* Make sure to provide specific and detailed comments and suggestions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your HR representative or management team.
* Your responses will be kept confidential and anonymous.
* If you have already filled out this survey, do not submit another response.
