# Academic Freedom Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gauge your perception of academic freedom on campus. Your responses will help us understand your concerns, experiences, and opinions about the academic climate and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select your primary role from the list.
2. If required, provide your campus or unit.
3. Rate your overall perception of academic freedom on campus.
4. Select any concerns about restrictions that apply to you.
5. If applicable, describe your experience with censorship or pressure.
6. Rate the level of institutional support for academic freedom.
7. If relevant, specify what training or resources you would need to improve the academic freedom climate.
8. Indicate your preference for anonymity.
9. Provide any final thoughts or examples in the "Additional comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent role** (`respondent_role`, select_one, required): Select your primary role on campus, such as Faculty, Graduate student, Undergraduate student, Staff, or Administrator.
* **Campus or unit** (`campus`, text, optional): Enter your main campus or organizational unit (e.g., department, school, or college).
* **Perception of academic freedom** (`perception_of_academic_freedom`, select_one, required): Rate your overall perception of academic freedom on campus as Very positive, Somewhat positive, Neutral, Somewhat negative, or Very negative.
* **Concerns about restrictions** (`concerns_about_restrictions`, select_multiple, optional): Select any concerns about restrictions that apply to you, such as Administrative constraints, Funding or donor influence, Legal or policy restrictions, Self-censorship, or Other.
* **Experience with censorship or pressure** (`experience_with_censorship`, text, optional): Describe any incidents involving censorship or pressure.
* **Institutional support for academic freedom** (`institutional_support_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the level of institutional support for academic freedom as Strong, Moderate, Weak, or None.
* **Training and resources needed** (`training_and_resources_needed`, text, optional): Specify what you would need to improve the academic freedom climate.
* **Preference for anonymity** (`anonymity_preference`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you prefer your response to be anonymous (True), not anonymous (False), or No preference.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any final thoughts or examples.

**Tips:**

* Please be honest and candid in your responses.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to ask.
* Your responses will help us improve the academic climate on campus.
* If you prefer your response to be anonymous, your answers will not be associated with your identity.
* You can provide examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
