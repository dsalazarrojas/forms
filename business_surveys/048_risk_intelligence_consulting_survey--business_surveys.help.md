# risk_intelligence_consulting_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect risk intelligence and consulting information from users. It is a crucial tool for risk assessment, analysis, and mitigation strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant risk concerns from the list.
2. Choose the correct risk maturity level (High, Medium, or Low) based on your assessment.
3. Enter your business objectives and risk description in the respective fields.
4. Select the risk assessment level (High, Medium, or Low) for each identified risk.
5. Choose the maturity level (High, Medium, or Low) for each selected risk.
6. (Optional) Enter the next steps to be taken regarding the identified risks.
7. (Optional) Enter the risk score.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Risk Concerns
* **Risk Concerns** (`risk_concerns`, `select_multiple`): Select all the relevant risk concerns that apply to your business. This field helps us understand the scope of your risk management.
### Risk Maturity
* **Risk Maturity** (`risk_maturity`, `select_one`): Choose the correct risk maturity level (High, Medium, or Low) based on your assessment. This field helps us evaluate the risk's current state.
### Business Objectives
* **Business Objectives** (`business_objectives`, `text`): Enter a brief description of your business objectives. This field provides context to the risk assessment.
### Risk Description
* **Risk Description** (`risk_description`, `text`): Enter a detailed description of the identified risk. This field helps us understand the risk's nature.
### Risk Assessment
* **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, `select_multiple`): Select the risk assessment level (High, Medium, or Low) for each identified risk. This field helps us evaluate the risk's potential impact.
### Maturity Level
* **Maturity Level** (`maturity_level`, `select_one`): Choose the maturity level (High, Medium, or Low) for each selected risk. This field helps us determine the risk's current state.
### Engagement Process
* **Engagement Process** (`engagement_process`, `date`): (Optional) Enter the date of the engagement process. This field is used to track the timeline of risk management activities.
### Next Steps
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`): (Optional) Enter the next steps to be taken regarding the identified risks. This field helps us understand the plan for risk mitigation.
### Risk Score
* **Risk Score** (`risk_score`, `number`): (Optional) Enter the risk score for each selected risk. This field helps us evaluate the risk's overall impact.
