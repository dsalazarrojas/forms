# Donor Data Protection Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your awareness and understanding of data protection practices as a donor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:
1. Answer each question in the space provided.
2. Read each question carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
3. Answer honestly and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Data Protection Awareness Survey**: This is the title of the form, and you can read it as is.
* **Introduction**: Please read and understand this introduction, which explains the context and purpose of the survey.
* **What is the Privacy Statement?**: Please explain the definition of a privacy statement.
* **Data Collection**: Describe the types of data collected and how it is handled.
* **Data Security**: Explain the measures in place to secure donor data.
* **Sensitive Data**: Define sensitive data and its handling.
* **Data Access**: Discuss who has access to donor data.
* **Data Retention**: Describe the policy for retaining donor data.
* **Data Sharing**: Explain how donor data is shared.
* **Data Anonymization**: Define data anonymization and its application.
* **Data Breach**: Describe the consequences and response to a data breach.
* **Form Completion**: Please confirm completion of the form.

Note: Each field is optional, but please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
