# Movember Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participation information from Movember participants. It is essential to fill out this form to help us track and analyze engagement with the Movember initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter your participant name in the "participant_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "participant_email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "participant_phone" field.
5. If you are representing an organization, enter the name of your organization in the "participant_organization" field.
6. Answer the "what_motivates_you" question to help us understand your motivations for participating.
7. Answer the "future_interest" question to help us understand your interests for the future.
8. Optionally, provide feedback in the "feedback" field.
9. In the final section, select one of the three options:
	* Submit: This will save your entry and make it visible to others.
	* Save for later: This will save your entry but will not make it visible to others.
	* Do not save: This will discard your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **movember_participation_survey_header** (Text): This is the header of the form.
* **participant_name** (Text): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **participant_email** (Email): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **participant_phone** (Text): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **participant_organization** (Text): If you are representing an organization, enter the name of the organization.
* **what_motivates_you** (Text): Answer this question to help us understand why you are participating in Movember.
* **future_interest** (Text): Answer this question to help us understand your interests for the future.
* **feedback** (Text): Optionally, provide any feedback or comments you would like to share.
* **submit** (Select One): Select one of the three options to save or discard your entry.
	+ **Submit**: This will save your entry and make it visible to others.
	+ **Save for later**: This will save your entry but will not make it visible to others.
	+ **Do not save**: This will discard your entry.
