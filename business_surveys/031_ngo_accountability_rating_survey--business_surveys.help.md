# NGO Accountability Rating Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NGO Accountability Rating Survey is designed to assess the transparency, engagement, and accountability of NGOs. This form is intended to gather user perceptions on the level of these aspects, providing valuable insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Provide answers based on your genuine experience with the NGOs you are rating.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
4. You may skip questions that do not apply to your experience.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`text`, required: false): Please enter a brief introduction to your rating, explaining the context of your interaction with the NGO (e.g., "I volunteered with this organization for a few months").
* **Transparency** (`number`, required: false): Rate the level of transparency you experienced during your interaction with the NGO, where 1 means "Low" and 5 means "High".
* **Engagement** (`text`, required: false): Describe your overall impression of the NGO's engagement with you, including their communication, responsiveness, and collaboration (e.g., "I felt well-informed, and they were approachable and responsive to my questions").
* **Accountability** (`select_one`, required: false): Select one of the two options:
	+ **NGO Accountability Practices**: This option is related to the NGO's internal policies, processes, and standards for accountability (e.g., financial reporting, audit, and compliance).
	+ **Stakeholder Engagement**: This option focuses on the NGO's involvement and communication with stakeholders, including their responsiveness to concerns and feedback.
* **Ratings** (`number`, required: false): Rate your overall impression of the NGO's accountability, where 1 means "Very Poor" and 5 means "Excellent".
* **NGO Transparency** (`number`, required: false): Rate the level of transparency displayed by the NGO during your interaction, where 1 means "Low" and 5 means "High".
* **NGO Accountability** (`number`, required: false): Rate the level of accountability displayed by the NGO during your interaction, where 1 means "Low" and 5 means "High".
* **NGO Engagement** (`number`, required: false): Rate the level of engagement displayed by the NGO during your interaction, where 1 means "Low" and 5 means "High".

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your ratings and answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.

Please note that the form's required fields and options are indicated in the YAML schema. The explanations provided are based on the best-effort understanding of the fields, but the actual meaning may vary depending on the organization's context and purpose.
