# Technology Adoption Index Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect data on an organization's technology adoption, including tech usage, barriers to adoption, openness to change, and other related aspects. This data will help us better understand the current state of technology adoption in various industries and company types.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For select multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
3. For numerical questions, enter a whole number without any decimal points.
4. Please complete all the questions on each page before moving on to the next.
