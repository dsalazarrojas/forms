# employer_cost_analysis_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `employer_cost_analysis_survey` form is designed to gather information from employers about the costs and challenges associated with their workforce. This data will be used to better understand the current financial situations and pain points of employers, helping us improve the support and resources we offer to them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Benefits" option you currently offer to your employees.
2. Enter a number for your average monthly labor costs.
3. Describe any challenges you are facing with employee benefits in the text field.
4. Choose all the challenges that apply to your benefits management.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Benefits** (`employee_benefits`, select_one, required): Select the type of benefits you currently offer to your employees. This will help us understand what kinds of support you need the most.
* **Labor Costs** (`labor_costs`, number, required): Enter your average monthly labor costs. This will help us understand your financial situation.
* **Expense Management Challenges** (`expense_management`, text, optional): Describe any challenges you are facing with managing expenses related to your workforce. This could include issues with budgeting, forecasting, or implementing cost-saving measures.
* **Employee Benefits Challenges** (`benefits_challenges`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the challenges you are experiencing with managing employee benefits. This will help us identify areas where we can offer more support and resources. 

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses, as this data will be used to improve our support and resources.
* If you're unsure about a field or have questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.
