# Facility Efficiency Benchmark Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Efficiency Benchmark Survey is designed to gather information about your facility's energy consumption and usage patterns to help you assess and improve your energy efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Use the provided options for questions with multiple options.
3. If you need assistance, refer to the help section or reach out to your supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What is the primary use of the facility?
* **What is the primary use of the facility?** (`facility_efficiency_benchmark_survey_1`, text, required): This question is asking you to describe the main purpose or function of your facility. Please provide a brief description of the type of business or activity that takes place within the facility.

### 2. How many square feet is the facility?
* **How many square feet is the facility?** (`facility_efficiency_benchmark_survey_2`, number, optional): This question is asking for the total square footage of your facility. If you don't know the exact square footage, please provide an approximate value.

### 3. How many employees work at the facility?
* **How many employees work at the facility?** (`facility_efficiency_benchmark_survey_3`, number, required): This question is asking for the number of people who work at your facility. Please provide the actual number, not an approximate value.

### 4. What is your facility's current energy consumption per square foot?
* **What is your facility's current energy consumption per square foot?** (`facility_efficiency_benchmark_survey_4`, number, required): This question is asking for your facility's current energy consumption per square foot. Please provide the actual value, not an estimated one.

### 5. What is your facility's current water consumption per square foot?
* **What is your facility's current water consumption per square foot?** (`facility_efficiency_benchmark_survey_5`, number, required): This question is asking for your facility's current water consumption per square foot. Please provide the actual value, not an estimated one.

### 6. What is your facility's energy efficiency target?
* **What is your facility's energy efficiency target?** (`facility_efficiency_benchmark_survey_6`, text, optional): This question is asking for your facility's goals or targets for reducing energy consumption. Please provide a brief description of your energy efficiency targets.

### 7. How many facilities does your company own or operate?
* **How many facilities does your company own or operate?** (`facility_efficiency_benchmark_survey_7`, select_one, required): This question is asking for the number of facilities your company owns or operates. Please select the correct option that best represents your company's situation.

### 8. What is your primary method for tracking energy efficiency?
* **What is your primary method for tracking energy efficiency?** (`facility_efficiency_benchmark_survey_8`, text, optional): This question is asking for the process or method you use to monitor or track energy consumption at your facility. Please provide a brief description of your current method.

### 9. What are the major obstacles to achieving energy efficiency?
* **What are the major obstacles to achieving energy efficiency?** (`facility_efficiency_benchmark_survey_9`, select_multiple, required): This question is asking for any challenges or barriers you face in reducing energy consumption. Please select all the options that apply to your situation.

### 10. What are your plans for improving energy efficiency?
* **What are your plans for improving energy efficiency?** (`facility_efficiency_benchmark_survey_10`, text, optional): This question is asking for any initiatives, projects, or strategies you have or plan to implement to improve energy efficiency at your facility. Please provide a brief description of your plans.

## Tips
- If you need assistance, refer to the help section or reach out to your supervisor.
- Please answer honestly and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure or have any questions, please let us know.
