# Energy Leak Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help energy management teams identify and assess energy leaks in commercial and residential buildings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the building description to ensure you have all the relevant information about the building.
2. Take photos and upload them to the uploaded photos field.
3. Select the location of the energy leak from the drop-down menu in the "Energy Leak Location" field.
4. Describe the energy leak and its location in the "Energy Leak Description" field.
5. Enter the number of energy leaks present in the building in the "Building Energy Leaks Count" field.
6. If necessary, provide recommendations for improvement in the "Energy Leak Recommendations" field.
7. Upload any relevant files to the "Uploaded Files" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Building Description** (`building_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the building, including its address, size, and any relevant details about its energy management systems.
* **Building Photos** (`building_photos`, text, optional): Upload photos of the building's energy management systems, such as HVAC units or boilers.
* **Energy Leak Location** (`energy_leak_location`, select_one, optional): Select the location where the energy leak is present from the drop-down menu.
* **Energy Leak Description** (`energy_leak_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the energy leak, including its cause and severity.
* **Building Energy Leaks Count** (`building_energy_leaks_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of energy leaks present in the building.
* **Uploaded Files** (`uploaded_files`, text, optional): Upload any relevant files related to the energy leak, such as reports or diagrams.
* **Uploaded Photos** (`uploaded_photos`, text, optional): Upload photos of the specific energy leak location.
* **Energy Leak Recommendations** (`energy_leak_recommendations`, note, optional): Provide any recommendations for improvement or mitigation of the energy leak.
