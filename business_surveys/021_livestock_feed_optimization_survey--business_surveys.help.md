# Livestock Feed Optimization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about livestock feed optimization on farms. It is intended for farmers and livestock producers to provide details about their feed sources, nutrient balances, and feeding frequencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions and select your answers accordingly.
2. Fill in the required information, including farm name, farm type, feed source, feed frequency, and survey date.
3. Provide additional details about your feed sources, nutrient balances, and feeding frequencies.
4. Select the frequency of nutrition and the frequency of nutrient distribution.
5. Provide information about the nutrition and farm location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farm Name** (farm_name, text, required): Enter your farm name to identify your farm.
* **Farm Type** (farm_type, select_one, required): Select the type of farm you operate (Beef, Dairy, or Other).
* **Feed Source** (feed_source, select_multiple, required): Select the sources of feed for your livestock (Corn, Soybeans, or Other).
* **Other Feed Source** (feed_source_other, text, required if "Other" is selected): If you selected "Other", provide the name of the other feed source.
* **Feed Challenge** (feed_challenge, select_one, required): Select the challenge you are currently experiencing with your feed (Nutrient imbalance, Cost, or Other).
* **Nutrient Balance** (feed_nutrient_balance, number, required if "Nutrient imbalance" is selected): If you selected "Nutrient imbalance", enter the current balance of your feed.
* **Cost** (feed_cost, number, required if "Cost" is selected): If you selected "Cost", enter the current cost of your feed.
* **Feed Nutrient Balance Other** (feed_nutrient_balance_other, text, required if "Nutrient imbalance" is selected): If you selected "Nutrient imbalance" and are unable to enter a balance, provide a description.
* **Feed Frequency** (feed_frequency, select_one, required): Select the frequency of your feed distribution (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Feed Frequency Other** (feed_frequency_other, text, required if "Other" is selected): If you selected "Other", provide the frequency of your feed distribution.
* **Feed Amount** (feed_amount, number, required): Enter the total amount of feed provided.
* **Feed Amount Other** (feed_amount_other, text, required if "Other" is selected): If you selected "Other", provide the amount of feed provided.
* **Feed Nutrition** (feed_nutrition, select_one, required): Select the nutrition level of your feed (High Quality, Medium Quality, or Low Quality).
* **Feed Nutrition Other** (feed_nutrition_other, text, required if "Other" is selected): If you selected "Other", provide the nutrition level of your feed.
* **Frequency of Nutrition** (feed_nutrition_frequency, date, required): Enter the frequency of nutrition provided.
* **Feed Nutrition Frequency Other** (feed_nutrition_frequency_other, text, required if "Other" is selected): If you selected "Other", provide the frequency of nutrition.
* **Frequency** (feed_frequency_of_nutrition, select_multiple, required): Select the frequency of feed distribution.
* **Feed Frequency Of Nutrition Other** (feed_frequency_of_nutrition_other, text, required if "Other" is selected): If you selected "Other", provide the frequency of feed distribution.
* **Nutrition** (nutrition, select_one, required): Select the level of nutrition.
* **Nutrition Other** (nutrition_other, text, required if "Other" is selected): If you selected "Other", provide the level of nutrition.
* **Farm Location** (farm_location, select_one, required): Select the location of your farm (North, South, East, or West).
* **Farm Location Other** (farm_location_other, text, required if "Other" is selected): If you selected "Other", provide the location of your farm.
* **Survey Date** (survey_date, date, required): Enter the date of the survey.
* **Survey Time** (survey_time, time, required): Enter the time of the survey.

## Tips
- Please answer questions honestly and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact the organization for guidance.
- If you have any additional information or comments, please provide them in the "Other" text fields.
