# Desert Sustainable Tourism Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Desert Sustainable Tourism Survey is designed to gather information about your travel habits and behaviors related to sustainable tourism practices. This survey aims to help us better understand how you contribute to or impact the desert environment through your travels. Your participation will help us identify areas for improvement and inform strategies to promote sustainable tourism practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2. Select the options that best describe your habits or behaviors.
3. For text fields, provide a brief description or explanation.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
5. Submit your responses when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Desert Sustainable Tourism Survey** (`introduction`): This is a required text field where you can describe the purpose of this survey or leave it blank to move on to the next question.
* **Traveler Type** (`traveler_type`): Select all that apply: 
  * Yes: I travel for pleasure.
  * Yes: I travel for business.
  * Yes: I travel for both pleasure and business.
* **Gender** (`gender`): Select one:
  * Yes: Male
  * Yes: Female
  * No: Other/Prefer not to say
* **Age** (`age`): Enter your age in years.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Travel Frequency** (`travel_frequency`): Select one:
  * Yes: Frequently (more than once a month)
  * Yes: Occasionally (1-3 times a year)
  * No: Rarely (less than 1 time a year)
* **Travel Mode** (`travel_mode`): Select one:
  * Yes: By plane
  * Yes: By bus
  * Yes: By car
  * Yes: On foot
  * Yes: On bicycle
  * No: Other/Prefer not to say
* **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`): Enter a brief description of why you travel.
* **Stay Duration** (`stay_duration`): Enter the average number of days you stay in the desert.
* **Travel Distance** (`travel_distance`): Enter the average distance you travel each trip.
* **Water Usage** (`water_usage`): Select all that apply:
  * Yes: Drink bottled water
  * Yes: Use tap water
  * Yes: Collect rainwater
  * No: Other/Prefer not to say
* **Food Consumption** (`food_consumption`): Select all that apply:
  * Yes: Eat local food

  * No: Other/Prefer not to say
* **Waste Disposal** (`waste_disposal`): Select all that apply:
  * Yes: Dispose of waste in trash cans

  * No: Other/Prefer not to say
* **Transportation** (`transportation`): Select all that apply:
  * Yes: Drive an electric vehicle
  * Yes: Drive a gasoline-powered vehicle
  * Yes: Use public transportation
  * Yes: Walk or bike
  * No: Other/Prefer not to say
* **Energy Consumption** (`energy_consumption`): Select all that apply:
  * Yes: Use renewable energy
  * Yes: Use non-renewable energy
  * No: Other/Prefer not to say
* **Accommodation** (`accommodation`): Select all that apply:
  * Yes: Stay in eco-friendly hotels
