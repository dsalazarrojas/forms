# Wardrobe Design Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on wardrobe design preferences from users. Your feedback will help us understand your preferences and improve our designs accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred style by choosing one or more of the options provided in the "Style Preferences" field.
2. Provide your thoughts on the space constraints you have in your current wardrobe or workspace. You can type your answer in the text field.
3. If you have a specific budget in mind for your wardrobe design, select "Yes" from the "Budget" dropdown menu.
4. If you have custom furniture preferences, choose "Yes" from the "Custom Furniture" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Style Preferences** (`style_preferences`, `select_multiple`): This field asks you to choose one or more options from the list to express your preferred style. Select one or more options that best describe your style.
* **Space Constraints** (`space_constraints`, `text`): Describe the space constraints you have in your current wardrobe or workspace. This could be limited space, specific dimensions, or any other constraint that affects your wardrobe design.
* **Budget** (`budget`, `select_one`): If you have a specific budget for your wardrobe design, select "Yes" from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand your financial constraints.
* **Custom Furniture** (`custom_furniture`, `select_multiple`): If you have custom furniture preferences, choose "Yes" from the dropdown menu. This will help us tailor our design to your specific needs.

## Tips
* Please provide clear and specific answers to help us understand your preferences better.
* If you have any suggestions or feedback, feel free to comment below or contact the business team directly.
* This form should take a few minutes to complete, and your input is greatly appreciated!
