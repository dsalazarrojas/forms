# Building And Space Generation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from employees and users about their building usage and satisfaction with the space.

## How To Complete This Form

Please complete this form by following the steps below:

* Fill out the "Respondent Information" section with your basic information.
* Answer the questions in the "Building and Space Generation" section about your building usage and satisfaction.
* Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your responses.
* You can add any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Suggestions" field at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Respondent Information
* **Your name** (text, required): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the response summary.
* **Department or team** (text, required): Enter the department or team you belong to.
* **Your role** (text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Email address** (email, required): Enter your work email address.
