# Business Permit Processing Time Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Permit Processing Time Survey Form is designed to collect data on business permit application processing times. It aims to understand how long it took for businesses to receive their permits, their satisfaction with the processing time, and any issues they encountered during the process. This information helps us improve our services and provide better support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your business's name in the "Business Name" field. This is the name of your company or business as it appears in our records.
2. Select the correct business type from the list provided in the "Business Type" field. This helps us understand the specific type of business you are submitting an application for.
3. Enter your business's full address in the "Business Address" field.
4. Provide your full name in the "Contact Name" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Contact Email" field. This is the email address we will use to contact you for any updates or additional information.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Contact Phone" field, if required.
7. Select the type of permit you applied for from the list in the "Permit Type Applied For" field.
8. Provide the date you submitted your application in the "Application Submission Date" field.
9. Select the expected timeline you were given for the processing time in the "Expected Timeline Given" field.
10. Choose how long it actually took for the processing in the "Actual Processing Time" field.
11. Rate your satisfaction with the processing time in the "Processing Time Satisfaction" field.
12. Rate your experience with communication during the process in the "Communication Rating" field.
13. Choose how complex your application was in the "Application Complexity" field.
14. Select any issues you encountered during the process in the "Issues Encountered" field.
15. Rate the helpfulness of our staff in the "Staff Helpfulness Rating" field.
16. Rate the online system's functionality in the "Online System Rating" field.
17. Rate how clear the requirements were in the "Clarity of Requirements" field.
18. Select whether you would recommend our process to others in the "Would Recommend Process" field.
19. (Optional) Provide any suggestions for improving the process in the "Suggestions for Improvement" field.
20. (Optional) Add any additional comments about your experience in the "Additional Comments" field.
