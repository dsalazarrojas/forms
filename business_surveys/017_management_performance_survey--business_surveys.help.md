# Management Performance Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees or team members about their manager's performance. Your input will help identify areas of strength and weakness in management, enabling managers to make informed decisions to improve work environment and employee satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the answers that best describe your experience and perceptions.
2. For each field, choose the option that best fits your response.
3. Be honest and thorough in your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Survey** (1, select_one): Choose 'Yes' if you think the company policies and procedures are being followed effectively. Choose 'No' if you think there are issues with company policies and procedures.
* **Manager Survey** (2, select_multiple): Select all the 'Yes' options if you think your manager is performing well in these areas. Select 'No' for areas where you have concerns.
* **Overall Performance** (3, number): Rate your overall experience with your manager, using a scale of 1-10.
* **Performance Rating** (4, select_one): Choose 'Good' if you think your manager's performance is satisfactory. Choose 'Fair' if there are areas for improvement.
* **Communication Skills** (5, select_multiple): Select 'Yes' for areas where you feel your manager communicates effectively. Select 'No' for areas where you feel your manager's communication is poor.
* **Leadership Skills** (6, select_multiple): Select 'Yes' for areas where you feel your manager leads effectively. Select 'No' for areas where you feel your manager's leadership is weak.
* **Management Style** (7, select_one): Choose 'Yes' if you think your manager's management style is effective. Choose 'No' if you have concerns.
* **Employee Motivation** (8, select_multiple): Select 'Yes' for areas where you feel motivated by your manager's actions. Select 'No' for areas where you feel unmotivated.
* **Work Quality** (9, select_one): Choose 'Good' if you think the work quality is satisfactory. Choose 'Poor' if there are issues with work quality.
* **Job Satisfaction** (10, select_one): Choose 'Satisfied' if you are satisfied with your job. Choose 'Dissatisfied' if you are not satisfied.
* **Work-Life Balance** (11, select_one): Choose 'Yes' if you feel your work-life balance is satisfactory. Choose 'No' if you have concerns.
* **Work-Life Satisfaction** (12, select_one): Choose 'Satisfied' if you feel satisfied with your work-life balance. Choose 'Dissatisfied' if you are not satisfied.

## Tips
Please be honest and thorough in your answers. Your feedback will help shape management decisions and improve the work environment.
