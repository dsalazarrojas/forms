# Digital Access Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Access Survey is designed to assess your household's access to digital technologies and services. It helps us understand your needs and areas where we can provide support to ensure everyone has access to the digital tools they need.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct options for each question to the best of your ability.
2. Please answer all questions to ensure you receive accurate results.
3. Use the options that apply to you and your household.
4. You can skip questions that do not apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Household type** (`household_type`, `select_one`, required): Please select the type that best describes your household composition (e.g. single adult, family with children, shared housing, etc.).
* **Internet access at home** (`internet_access`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of internet connection you have at home (e.g. no access, dial-up, DSL, cable, fiber, cellular data).
* **Devices owned in household** (`device_ownership`, `select_multiple`, required): Check all the devices you own in your household (e.g. smartphone, tablet, laptop, desktop, none).
* **Data limit or affordability issues** (`data_limit_issues`, `select_one`, required): Check if data cost limits affect your internet usage.
* **Areas where support is needed** (`digital_skills_needs`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the areas where you would like to receive support (e.g. basic computing, online safety and privacy, job search tools, remote learning support).
* **Suggestions to improve digital access** (`suggestions`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional suggestions you have to improve digital access in your community.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate with your answers to ensure we can provide the right support.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* Your input will help us identify areas of need and provide targeted support.
