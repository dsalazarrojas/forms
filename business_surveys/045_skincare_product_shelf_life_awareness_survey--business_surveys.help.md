# Skincare Product Shelf Life Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skincare Product Shelf Life Awareness Survey aims to understand how users manage and perceive the shelf life of skincare products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question honestly, and try to provide as much detail as possible.
2. If you have not purchased a skincare product recently, please answer the questions based on your general knowledge or practices.
3. You can skip questions that are not applicable to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please feel free to write any additional information that might be helpful in understanding your response.
* **Product Usage** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Describe how you usually use your skincare products (e.g., daily, weekly, as needed).
* **Product Purchase** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Tell us about your last skincare product purchase (e.g., where did you buy it, when did you buy it).
* **Purchase Frequency** (`4`, `number`, required: false): How often do you typically buy skincare products?
	+ Please respond with a number (e.g., 1-2 times a week, once a month, etc.).
* **Purchase Location** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false): Where do you usually buy your skincare products?
	+ Please select one of the following options:
		- Beauty store
		- Online store
		- Pharmacy
		- Other (please specify)
* **Product Storage** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): How do you store your skincare products?
	+ Please select all applicable options:
		- Refrigerate
		- Room temperature
		- Freezer
		- Other (please specify)
* **Expiration Date** (`7`, `date`, required: false): Do you check the expiration date on your skincare products before use?
* **Product Condition** (`8`, `time`, required: false): How long do you usually use a skincare product before considering it expired or expired?
	+ Please respond with a time period (e.g., 1-2 months, 3-4 months, etc.).
