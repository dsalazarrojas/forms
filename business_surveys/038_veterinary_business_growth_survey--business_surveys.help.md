# Veterinary Business Growth Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Business Growth Survey aims to gather information from veterinary businesses to identify areas of growth and provide insights for improvement. This data will help veterinary businesses understand their current challenges, goals, and marketing strategies, enabling them to make informed decisions for their practice's growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all the required information for each page. 
2. For each field, provide accurate and relevant information about your veterinary business.
3. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access and use the data collected from this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Information**:
	+ **Owner Name**: This field is for the name of the owner of the veterinary business.
	+ **Owner Email**: This field is for the email address of the owner.
	+ **Owner Phone**: This field is for the phone number of the owner.
* **Practice Information**:
	+ **Practice Name**: This field is for the name of the veterinary practice.
	+ **Practice Address**: This field is for the address of the veterinary practice.
	+ **Practice Type**: This field is for the type of veterinary practice (e.g., animal hospital, clinic, private practice, etc.)
* **Challenges**:
	+ **Describe the challenges you are currently facing**: This field is for you to describe the challenges your veterinary business is currently facing.
* **Goals**:
	+ **Describe your business goals**: This field is for you to describe your veterinary business's goals.
* **Marketing Strategy**:
	+ **Describe your current marketing strategy**: This field is for you to describe your current marketing strategy for your veterinary business. 

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when answering each question.
* Ensure that your responses are accurate and relevant to your veterinary business.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
