# Franchise Marketing Expert Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from franchise marketing experts on their views and experiences related to marketing strategies and trends in the franchise industry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions carefully and honestly.
2. For select questions, choose the option that best aligns with your opinion or experience.
3. Select multiple options for questions that allow it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Franchise Marketing Expert Survey** (`marketing_expert_survey`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or information about your experience as a franchise marketing expert. Please write your answer in the text box.
* **Marketing Strategy** (`marketing_strategy`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  + Good: Do you think the marketing strategy of the franchise is effective?
  + Fair: Do you think the marketing strategy of the franchise is somewhat effective?
* **Marketing Trends** (`marketing_trends`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  + Yes: Are you up-to-date with the latest marketing trends in the franchise industry?
  + No: Are you not up-to-date with the latest marketing trends in the franchise industry?
* **Franchise Growth** (`franchise_growth`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  + Yes: Do you think the franchise has grown or expanded its market in the past year?
  + No: Do you think the franchise has not grown or expanded its market in the past year?
* **Promotional Efforts** (`promotional_efforts`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  + Yes: Have you participated in or seen any promotional efforts that have contributed to franchise growth?
  + No: Have you not participated in or seen any promotional efforts that have contributed to franchise growth?
* **Marketing Experts** (`marketing_experts`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  + Yes: Do you think having marketing experts in the franchise industry is beneficial?
  + No: Do you not think having marketing experts in the franchise industry is beneficial?
* **Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  + Yes: Are you using or have you used effective marketing channels in the franchise industry?
  + No: Are you not using or have you not used effective marketing channels in the franchise industry?
* **Business Partners** (`business_partners`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  + Yes: Do you work with or have you worked with business partners in the franchise industry?
  + No: Do you not work with or have you not worked with business partners in the franchise industry?
* **Target Market** (`target_market`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  + Yes: Is the franchise targeting its market effectively?
  + No: Is the franchise not targeting its market effectively?
* **Marketing Budget** (`marketing_budget`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  + Yes: Do you think the franchise's marketing budget is sufficient?
  + No: Do you think the franchise's marketing budget is insufficient?

## Tips
* Please answer all questions carefully and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any question, you can select "No" or "Not Applicable".
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can include them in the field "Franchise Marketing Expert Survey".
