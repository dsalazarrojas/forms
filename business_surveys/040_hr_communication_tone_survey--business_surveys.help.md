# HR Communication Tone Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey aims to understand how often employees perceive HR's tone as helpful and supportive, and to gather suggestions for improvement. Your feedback will help refine HR's communication strategy to better meet the needs of all employees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select the options that best represent your experience with HR's communication.
3. If you have additional comments or suggestions, use the text fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro_question`, note, required: false): This is a short introduction to the survey.
* **Tone Perceived** (`tone_perceived`, select_one, required: false): Please select the frequency with which you feel that HR's tone is helpful and supportive:
	+ Very Often
	+ Often
	+ Sometimes
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the levels that best describe your satisfaction with HR's communication:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
* **Tone to Improve** (`tone_improve`, text, required: false): If you have suggestions for improvements, please describe them here.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required: false): Any additional comments or suggestions for HR's communication?
* **How do you usually receive HR's communication?** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the channels through which you usually receive HR's communication:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Text
	+ Intranet
	+ Other
* **How long did it take you to respond?** (`time_response`, time, required: false): Please select the time it takes you to respond to HR's communication:
	+ Never
	+ Less than once a month
	+ Once a month
	+ 2-3 times a month
	+ 1 time in 2 weeks
	+ Weekly or more
* **How often do you respond?** (`frequency_response`, select_one, required: false): How often do you respond to HR's communication:
	+ Never
	+ Less than once a month
	+ Once a month
	+ 2-3 times a month
	+ 1 time in 2 weeks
	+ Weekly or more
* **Why are you satisfied or dissatisfied?** (`satisfaction_why`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the reasons for your satisfaction or dissatisfaction:
	+ Tone
	+ Content
	+ Format
	+ Other
* **Channels to Improve** (`communication_channels_improve`, text, required: false): If you have suggestions for improvements, please describe them here.
* **Tone to Improve** (`tone_improve_channels`, text, required: false): If you have suggestions for improvements, please describe them here.
