# rural_development_awareness_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from respondents about their awareness and knowledge of rural development programs, schemes, and initiatives. It is intended to collect data on the respondents' understanding of these programs and provide insights on the impact and progress of rural development initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
2. Select the department that you work for or are affiliated with in the "department" field.
3. Provide your designation or job title in the "designation" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
6. Select your level of awareness about rural development programs in the "awareness" field.
7. Provide a brief description of your knowledge about rural development in the "knowledge" field.
8. Share any suggestions you might have in the "suggestion" field.
9. Enter the date of submission in the "submission" field.
10. Provide the time of follow-up in the "follow_up" field.
11. Leave any additional comments in the "comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (`name`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **department** (`department`, required): Please select the department or organization you work for or are affiliated with.
* **designation** (`designation`, required): Please enter your job title or position.
* **email** (`email`, required): Please enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **phone** (`phone`, required): Please enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **awareness** (`awareness`, required): Select one of the options to indicate your level of awareness about rural development programs.
	+ rural development program
	+ government schemes
	+ other
* **knowledge** (`knowledge`, required): Please provide a brief description of your knowledge about rural development.
* **suggestion** (`suggestion`, required): Share any suggestions you might have about rural development.
* **submission** (`submission`, required): Enter the date of submission of your response.
* **follow_up** (`follow_up`, required): Provide the time of follow-up for any further communication.
* **comments** (`comments`, optional): Leave any additional comments you might have.
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, optional): Select the tool assigned to you for this task (e.g., jotform, jotpad).
* **output_file** (`output_file`, optional): Please provide the name of the output file (if any).
* **form_ids** (`form_ids`, required): Please enter a unique identifier for this form response.
* **assigned_user** (`assigned_user`, optional): Please provide the name of the user assigned to you for this task.
* **description** (`description`, optional): Leave any additional description or context.
