# Strategic Capabilities Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strategic Capabilities Assessment form is designed to help teams or organizations evaluate their current strengths, challenges, and priorities. The information collected from this form will be used to gain insights into the team's or organization's strategic positions and areas where they can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your team's or organization's current position from the provided options in the "Team" field.
2. Describe your team's or organization's current strengths in the "Strengths" field.
3. Mention any significant challenges your team is currently facing in the "Challenges" field.
4. List your team's or organization's top priorities in the "Priorities" field.
5. If you have any supporting documents related to this assessment, you can upload them in the "Upload Documents" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team**: Select your team's or organization's current position from the options provided. This field helps us understand your team's or organization's current state.
* **Strengths**: Describe your team's or organization's current strengths. This could include your team's skills, expertise, or any advantages you have over others.
* **Challenges**: Mention any significant challenges your team is currently facing. This could include internal or external factors that affect your team's performance.
* **Priorities**: List your team's or organization's top priorities. These could be short-term or long-term objectives that drive your team's or organization's strategy.
* **Upload Documents**: If you have any supporting documents related to this assessment, you can upload them here. This could include reports, presentations, or other materials that help explain your team's or organization's position.
* **Centralize Results**: Although not required, this field allows you to choose whether the results of this assessment will be centralized or reported back to the team or organization.
