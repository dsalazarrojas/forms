# salon_rebooking_behavior_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information on rebooking behavior to help salons better understand their customers' needs and tailor their services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the relevant options for each field as instructed.
2. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "note" field.
3. Provide your email address for further communication.
4. Enter your phone number for additional contact.
5. Select the frequency and motivation for rebooking.
6. Provide any relevant additional details, such as frequency and important reasons for rebooking.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **email**: Enter your email address for further communication. 
* **phone_number**: Enter your phone number for additional contact.
* **note**: Enter any additional notes or comments you would like to provide.
* **rebooking_habits**: Select the rebooking habits that apply to you. 
* **rebooking_motivations**: Select the motivations for rebooking. 
* **rebooking_preferences**: Select the rebooking preferences. 
* **rebooking_frequency**: Select the frequency of rebooking. 
* **rebooking_frequency_frequency_type**: Select the type of frequency. 
* **rebooking_frequency_time**: Enter the time of rebooking. 
* **rebooking_frequency_time_2**: Enter the second time of rebooking. 
* **rebooking_frequency_time_3**: Enter the third time of rebooking. 
* **rebooking_frequency_time_4**: Enter the fourth time of rebooking. 
* **rebooking_frequency_important**: Select the important reason for rebooking. 
* **rebooking_frequency_important_2**: Select the important reasons for rebooking. 
* **rebooking_frequency_important_3**: Select the important reason for rebooking. 
* **rebooking_habits_frequency**: Enter the frequency of rebooking habits. 
* **rebooking_preferences_frequency_important**: Enter the important frequency of rebooking preferences. 

## Tips

* Please answer each field carefully and accurately to ensure your responses are helpful for the salon.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask a salon staff member for assistance.
* The form is for your convenience and will help the salon better understand your rebooking behavior.
