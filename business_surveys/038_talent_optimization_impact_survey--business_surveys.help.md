# Talent Optimization Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Optimization Impact Survey is designed to gather information from employees on their experiences and perceptions regarding talent development within the organization. This form aims to help identify areas of improvement and optimize talent acquisition, growth, and retention strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Review the form carefully**: Take a few minutes to read through the questions and understand what is being asked.
2. **Answer questions accurately**: Respond to each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. **Select the correct options**: For questions with select options, choose the most relevant answer.
4. **Submit your responses**: Once you've completed the form, click the submit button to send your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **Role**: Select the title of your role within the organization. (Director, Manager, Analyst, Employee, Other)
	+ Please choose the most accurate description of your position within the organization.
* **Department**: Select all departments that relate to your role. (Finance, IT, Marketing, Sales, HR)
	+ Please choose all departments that you are directly associated with.
* **Talent initiatives**: Describe any talent-related initiatives you are involved in or aware of. (Text box)
	+ Please provide a brief description of talent initiatives you are involved in or have information about.
* **Results**: If you have a specific date or deadline for talent-related goals or projects, enter it here. (Date)
	+ Please provide a date or deadline if you have one.

### Page 2
* **Page 2**: This is an optional page, please fill it out if you feel it is necessary to provide further information. (Text box)
	+ Please provide any additional thoughts or comments you feel are relevant to the survey.
