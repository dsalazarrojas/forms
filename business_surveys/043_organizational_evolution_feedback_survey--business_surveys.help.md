**Organizational Evolution Feedback Survey - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from employees on the organization's evolution, including positive and negative impacts of change initiatives, suggestions for future changes, and other important information. This feedback will help the organization understand areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Answer the questions on each page**: Each page has one or more questions with corresponding fields. Please answer each question as thoroughly as possible.
2. **Provide accurate information**: Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and truthful.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you have completed all the questions, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Feedback
* **Employee Feedback**: A text field where you can provide your thoughts and opinions about the organization's evolution.
### Change Initiative
* **Change Initiative**: A dropdown menu where you select the reason for the change initiative you are experiencing. Please choose one of the following options:
	+ New product launch
	+ Merger or acquisition
	+ Restructuring
### Positive Impacts
* **Positive Impacts**: A list of multiple-choice fields where you can select the positive effects of the change initiative you are experiencing. Please choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Increased revenue
	+ Improved efficiency
	+ Enhanced customer satisfaction
### Negative Impacts
* **Negative Impacts**: A list of multiple-choice fields where you can select the negative effects of the change initiative you are experiencing. Please choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Loss of jobs
	+ Increased competition
	+ Regulatory challenges
### Suggestions
* **Suggestions**: A text field where you can provide suggestions for future change initiatives. Please be creative and provide any ideas you think could benefit the organization.
### Submission
* **Submission**: A dropdown menu where you can select who the feedback is intended for. Please choose one of the following options:
	+ HR
	+ Management
	+ Both
### Submission Date
* **Submission Date**: A date field where you can enter the date you are submitting the feedback.
### Submission Time
* **Submission Time**: A time field where you can enter the time of submission.
### Email
* **Email**: An email field where you can enter your email address.
### Phone
* **Phone**: A text field where you can enter your phone number.

Note: Please ensure that the form is submitted before the deadline to be counted in the feedback statistics.
