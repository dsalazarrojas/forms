# Nonprofit Impact Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us better understand how you perceive our communications and what you expect from them. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and share your thoughts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form to understand what it asks and make sure you're comfortable with the questions.
2. Select the answers that best fit your preferences for each question.
3. Fill out the form accordingly and don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### How did you hear about us?
* <label> How did you hear about us? </label> (`other`, `text`, required/optional): Please share how you learned about our nonprofit organization. This will help us understand the channels we use to communicate with you effectively.

### How do you usually get our communications?
* <label> How do you usually get our communications? </label> (`communication_method`, `select_one`, required/true): You can choose how you typically receive our communications from the options provided.

### How often do you get our communications?
* <label> How often do you get our communications? </label> (`communication_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select the frequency at which you usually get our communications.

### What value do you get from our communications?
* <label> What value do you get from our communications? </label> (`communication_value`, `select_one`, required/true): Please select the value you derive from our communications.

### How much help do you get from our communications?
* <label> How much help do you get from our communications? </label> (`communication_help`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose how much assistance you usually receive from our communications.

### How clear are our communications?
* <label> How clear are our communications? </label> (`communication_clarity`, `select_one`, required/true): Indicate the level of clarity in our communications.

### How timely are our communications?
* <label> How timely are our communications? </label> (`communication_timeliness`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate the time sensitivity of our communications.

### How often would you like to get our communications?
* <label> How often would you like to get our communications? </label> (`communication_frequency_desired`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select the ideal frequency of our communications for you.

### How much help would you like to get from our communications?
* <label> How much help would you like to get from our communications? </label> (`communication_help_desired`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose the level of help you expect from our communications.

### How clear would you like our communications to be?
* <label> How clear would you like our communications to be? </label> (`communication_clarity_desired`, `select_one`, required/true): Select how clear our communications should be for you.

### How timely would you like our communications to be?
* <label> How timely would you like our communications to be? </label> (`communication_timeliness_desired`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate the ideal time sensitivity of our communications.

### Any comments?
* <label> Any comments? </label> (`comments`, `text`, required/false): This is an optional section where you can share any additional thoughts or comments about our communications.

### Email?
* <label> Email? </label> (`email`, `text`, required/false): If you would like to be contacted by email, please enter your email address here.

### Phone?
* <label> Phone? </label> (`phone`, `text`, required/false): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number here.

### Social media?
* <label> Social media? </label> (`social_media`, `text`, required/false): If you would like to be contacted through social media, please enter your social media handle here.

### How did you hear about us?
* <label> How did you hear about us? </label> (`other`, `text`, required/false): You can select this option if you learned about us through a different channel, and then specify the other method.

### Other?
* <label> Other? </label> (`other_method`, `text`, required/false): If you have any other method for how you heard about us, please specify here.

### Nonprofit Impact Communication Survey
* <label> Nonprofit Impact Communication Survey </label> (`submission`, `text`, required/false): This is a confirmation field to ensure you've completed the survey.

Note: The fields marked as required/true are mandatory and must be filled out. The fields marked as required/false are optional and can be skipped if you don't have any information to provide.
