# Commercial Openings Prioritization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to help surveyors collect and prioritize commercial opening sites. The information collected will be used to inform business decisions and ensure that the most suitable location for commercial openings is chosen.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out each section in sequence, starting with "section_project_info" and then "surveyor_name", "survey_date", and "region_under_review".
- Answer each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
- For the site location fields (site_location_a, site_location_b, site_location_c), provide the address or a brief description of the site.
- When choosing the "Primary Prioritization Criteria", select the most important factor for this market.
- For the score fields (site_a_score, site_b_score, site_c_score), enter a number between 1 and 10 to represent the priority score of each site.
- Select the estimated complexity of local permitting for each site.
- Answer "Does any site require immediate action/commitment?" accordingly.
- Summarize the prioritization in the "Executive Summary of Prioritization" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Commercial Expansion Project --** (`section_project_info`, note, required: false): This is an overview of the commercial expansion project.
* **Surveyor Name/ID** (`surveyor_name`, text, required: true): Enter the name or ID of the person filling out the survey.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required: true): Enter the date the survey was completed.
* **Region/Market Area Under Review** (`region_under_review`, text, required: true): Enter the region or market area being reviewed.
* **-- Site Evaluation and Ranking --** (`section_site_evaluation`, note, required: false): This section will be used to evaluate and rank potential opening sites. Please skip for now.
* **Potential Opening Site A - Address/Description** (`site_location_a`, text, required: true): Enter the address or description of potential opening site A.
* **Potential Opening Site B - Address/Description** (`site_location_b`, text, required: false): Enter the address or description of potential opening site B.
* **Potential Opening Site C - Address/Description** (`site_location_c`, text, required: false): Enter the address or description of potential opening site C.
* **Primary Prioritization Criteria** (`prioritization_criteria`, select_one, required: true): Select the most important factor for this market.
* **Site A Priority Score (1-10)** (`site_a_score`, number, required: true): Enter a number between 1 and 10 to represent the priority score of site A.
* **Site B Priority Score (1-10)** (`site_b_score`, number, required: false): Enter a number between 1 and 10 to represent the priority score of site B.
* **Site C Priority Score (1-10)** (`site_c_score`, number, required: false): Enter a number between 1 and 10 to represent the priority score of site C.
* **-- Market Readiness --** (`section_market_readiness`, note, required: false): This section will be used to evaluate market readiness. Please skip for now.
* **Estimated Complexity of Local Permitting** (`local_permit_complexity`, select_one, required: true): Select the estimated complexity of local permitting for each site.
* **Does any site require immediate action/commitment?** (`immediate_action_required`, select_one, required: true): Answer "Yes" for any site that requires immediate action/commitment.
* **Executive Summary of Prioritization** (`prioritization_summary`, text, required: false): Summarize your prioritization of sites in this field.

# Tips
- If you have any questions or concerns about the survey, please contact the project team.
- This survey should only be filled out if the user has not previously completed a similar survey for the same project.
- Ensure that the surveyor's name and date are accurate and up-to-date.
