# Manager Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manager Survey form is designed to gather information from managers in a company, likely for performance evaluation, feedback, or other managerial purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Begin by reading this guide carefully to understand each field's purpose.
2. Ensure you have all the required information before starting.
3. For each question or field, select the most accurate answer based on your knowledge.
4. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1: Page 1** (`page_title`,  `note`, required: false): This is the first page of the survey. You will not need to fill in any specific information on this page; it's just an introductory page.
* **Questions 1** (`questions_1`,  `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that apply to you from the list of options provided. You can select one or more options.
* **Questions 2** (`questions_2`,  `select_one`, required: false): Choose one option from the list that best answers your response.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`,  `date`, required: true): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`,  `time`, required: false): This field is not required. If you want to be more accurate, you can enter the time you are submitting this form.
* **Email** (`email`,  `email`, required: true): Enter your email address as it appears in your business contact information.
* **Phone** (`phone`,  `text`, required: false): If you want to provide your phone number for communication, enter it here.
* **Page 2: Page 2** (`page_title_2`,  `note`, required: false): This is the second page of the survey. Like the first page, it's just an introduction.
* **Questions 3** (`questions_3`,  `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the options that apply to you from the list of options provided.
* **Questions 4** (`questions_4`,  `select_one`, required: false): Choose one option from the list that best answers your response.
* **Comments** (`comments`,  `note`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or remarks, you can enter them here.
* **Page 3: Page 3** (`page_title_3`,  `note`, required: false): This is the third and final page of the survey.
 
## Tips
- Double-check all your answers before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
