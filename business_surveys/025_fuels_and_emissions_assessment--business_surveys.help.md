# Fuels And Emissions Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess a company's fuels and emissions performance in the context of the business survey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
- Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about your company.
- Select the correct fuel type and vehicle type for your company.
- Enter the correct fuel consumption and emissions rating for your company.
- Enter any additional information that may be relevant to your company's fuels and emissions performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This is the name of your company.
- **Contact Person** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): This is the person responsible for filling out this form.
- **Email** (`3`, `email`, required/optional): This is the email address of the contact person.
- **Phone Number** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): This is the phone number of the contact person.
- **Fuel Type** (`5`, `select_one`, required/optional): This is the type of fuel your company uses. Please select from the options Gasoline, Diesel, or Other.
- **Fuel Consumption** (`6`, `number`, required/optional): This is the amount of fuel your company consumes per unit.
- **Emissions Rating** (`7`, `number`, required/optional): This is your company's emissions rating.
- **Vehicle Type** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This is the type of vehicle your company uses. Please select from the options Car, Truck, or Other.
- **Fuel Emissions Rating** (`9`, `number`, required/optional): This is your company's fuel emissions rating.
- **Company Description** (`10`, `note`, required/optional): This is a note about your company's fuels and emissions performance.
- **Date of Birth** (`11`, `date`, required/optional): This is not applicable for companies. Please do not fill out this field.
- **Time of Birth** (`12`, `time`, required/optional): This is not applicable for companies. Please do not fill out this field.
- **Vehicle Registration Number** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): This is the registration number of your vehicle.
- **Company Address** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): This is the address of your company.
- **Location** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): This is the location of your company.
- **Other Fuel Type** (`16`, `text`, required/optional): This is an optional field for specifying other types of fuel not listed in the previous options.
- **Fuel Consumption Unit** (`17`, `select_one`, required/optional): This is the unit of measurement for your fuel consumption. Please select from the options Liters, Tons, or Other.
- **Vehicle Model** (`18`, `text`, required/optional): This is the model of your vehicle.
- **Vehicle Year** (`19`, `text`, required/optional): This is the year of your vehicle.
- **Vehicle Color** (`20`, `text`, required/optional): This is the color of your vehicle.
- **Vehicle Size** (`21`, `text`, required/optional): This is the size of your vehicle.
- **Vehicle Weight** (`22`, `text`, required/optional): This is the weight of your vehicle.
- **Fuel Efficiency** (`23`, `text`, required/optional): This is a note about your company's fuel efficiency performance.
- **Emissions Reductions** (`24`, `text`, required/optional): This is a note about your company's emissions reductions performance.
- **Submission Date** (`25`, `date`, required/optional): This is the date you are submitting this form.

## Tips
- Please fill out this form accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please contact the relevant authorities.
- This form should only be filled out by companies that have a vehicle or are involved in the transportation industry.
