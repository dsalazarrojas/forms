# Ethanol Production Process Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from ethanol production sites to help us track and improve ethanol production processes. It's essential to provide accurate responses as this information will be used internally to identify areas for improvement and optimize processes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "production site" where you are currently working or have access to.
2. Enter the production capacity of your facility. Please ensure you provide accurate numbers, as this information will help us estimate the impact on the environment.
3. Specify the units used for production capacity (e.g., "tons per year" or "gallons per day").
4. Choose a report period (month, quarter, or six months) for the data you are providing.
5. Select the yield of your current process (high, moderate, or low).
6. Describe the yield deviation of your process.
7. Enter the deviation in units specific to your process (e.g., "percentage points" or "tons per year").
8. Enter the production cost of your current process.
9. Specify the units used for production cost (e.g., "dollars per liter" or "euros per ton").
10. Choose the currency used for production cost (e.g., "USD" or "EUR").
11. Enter the energy efficiency of your current process.
12. Specify the units used for energy efficiency (e.g., "percentage points" or "kilojoules per liter").
13. Describe the energy consumption of your process.
14. Specify the units used for energy consumption (e.g., "megawatt hours" or "liters per day").
15. Enter the water efficiency of your current process.
16. Specify the units used for water efficiency (e.g., "percentage points" or "cubic meters per ton").
17. Describe the water consumption of your process.
18. Enter the waste efficiency of your current process.
19. Specify the units used for waste efficiency (e.g., "percentage points" or "kilograms per liter").
20. Describe the waste consumption of your process.
21. Enter any additional comments about your process or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **production_site** (1, text, required): Enter the name or ID of the production site where you are currently working or have access to.
* **production_capacity** (2, number, required): Enter the production capacity of your facility. Please ensure you provide accurate numbers, as this information will help us estimate the impact on the environment.
* **production_capacity_units** (3, text, required): Specify the units used for production capacity (e.g., "tons per year" or "gallons per day").
* **report_period** (4, select_one, required): Choose a report period (month, quarter, or six months) for the data you are providing.
* **ethanol_yield** (5, select_multiple, required): Select the yield of your current process (high, moderate, or low).
* **ethanol_yield_deviation** (6, text, optional): Describe the yield deviation of your process.
* **yield_deviation** (7, number, required): Enter the deviation in units specific to your process (e.g., "percentage points" or "tons per year").
* **production_cost** (8, number, required): Enter the production cost of your current process.
* **production_cost_units** (9, text, required): Specify the units used for production cost (e.g., "dollars per liter" or "euros per ton").
* **production_cost_currency** (10, text, required): Choose the currency used for production cost (e.g., "USD" or "EUR").
* **energy_efficiency** (11, number, required): Enter the energy efficiency of your current process.
* **energy_efficiency_units** (12, text, required): Specify the units used for energy efficiency (e.g., "percentage points" or "kilojoules per liter").
* **energy_consumption** (13, text, required): Describe the energy consumption of your process.
* **energy_consumption_units** (14, text, required): Specify the units used for energy consumption (e.g., "megawatt hours" or "liters per day").
* **water_efficiency** (15, number, required): Enter the water efficiency of your current process.
* **water_efficiency_units** (16, text, required): Specify the units used for water efficiency (e.g., "percentage points" or "cubic meters per ton").
* **water_consumption** (17, text, required): Describe the water consumption of your process.
* **waste_efficiency** (18, number, required): Enter the waste efficiency of your current process.
* **waste_efficiency_units** (19, text, required): Specify the units used for waste efficiency (e.g., "percentage points" or "kilograms per liter").
* **waste_consumption** (20, text, required): Describe the waste consumption of your process.
* **comments** (21, text, required): Enter any additional comments about your process or concerns.
