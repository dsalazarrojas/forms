# Agent Commitment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agent Commitment Survey is designed to collect feedback from agents to help the organization better understand their engagement, satisfaction, and commitment. Your input will contribute to creating a more positive and productive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name, if you feel comfortable doing so (it's optional).
2. Answer the questions honestly, and select the most accurate option for each question.
3. For questions with a scale (e.g., "How proud are you to represent this organization?"), choose the option that best describes your feelings or experiences.
4. For questions asking for specific feedback, be as detailed and honest as possible.
5. Review your answers before submitting to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Agent Name** (Agent Name - Optional, text): Enter your name if you feel comfortable sharing it, otherwise, you can remain anonymous.
- **Years of Service** (Years of service with the organization, number, required): Indicate how many years you have been working with the organization.
- **How proud are you to represent this organization?** (select_one, required): Choose how proud you are to represent the organization, using the scale provided.
  - Very Proud: I feel extremely proud to represent this organization.
  - Proud: I feel proud to represent this organization.
  - Neutral: I have no strong feelings about representing this organization.
  - Not very proud: I do not feel proud to represent this organization.
- **Likelihood of staying with the company for the next 12 months** (select_one, required): Choose how likely you are to stay with the company in the next 12 months.
  - Extremely Likely: I am very likely to stay with the company.
  - Likely: I am likely to stay with the company.
  - Unsure: I am not sure about my future with the company.
  - Unlikely: I am unlikely to stay with the company.
- **Satisfaction with professional development opportunities** (select_one, required): Choose how satisfied you are with the opportunities for growth and development within the company.
  - Highly Satisfied: I am very satisfied with the opportunities.
  - Satisfied: I am satisfied with the opportunities.
  - Dissatisfied: I am not satisfied with the opportunities.
  - Highly Dissatisfied: I am very dissatisfied with the opportunities.
- **Clarity of organizational goals and values** (select_one, required): Choose how clear you are about the company's goals and values.
  - Very Clear: The goals and values are very clear.
  - Clear: The goals and values are clear.
  - Somewhat Clear: The goals and values are somewhat clear.
  - Not Clear at all: The goals and values are not clear.
- **Support received from leadership and peers** (select_one, required): Choose how much support you receive from your leadership and peers.
  - Excellent Support: I receive excellent support.
  - Good Support: I receive good support.
  - Fair Support: I receive fair support.
  - Poor Support: I receive poor support.
- **Balance between workload and compensation** (select_one, required): Choose how balanced you feel your workload and compensation are.
  - Perfectly Balanced: My workload and compensation are perfectly balanced.
  - Acceptable: My workload and compensation are acceptable.
  - Unbalanced: My workload and compensation are unbalanced.
  - Extremely Stressful: My workload and compensation are extremely stressful.
- **What motivates you most in your daily work?** (text, optional): Provide an honest description of what motivates you most in your daily work.
- **One thing the company could do to improve your commitment** (text, optional): Provide specific and honest feedback on areas that could improve your commitment.
- **Words that describe the current company culture** (select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to describe the current company culture.
  - Innovative: The company culture is innovative.
  - Collaborative: The company culture is collaborative.
  - Competitive: The company culture is competitive.
  - Supportive: The company culture is supportive.
  - Bureaucratic: The company culture is bureaucratic.
  - Fast-paced: The company culture is fast-paced.
