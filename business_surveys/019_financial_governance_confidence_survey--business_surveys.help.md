# Financial Governance Confidence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their perception of the company's financial governance and information management. The survey aims to understand their level of confidence in the company's financial processes, procedures, and reporting. The information collected will help identify areas of improvement and ensure that financial information is provided in a clear, concise, and effective manner.
