# Influential Companies Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Influential Companies Survey is designed to collect and categorize data on influential companies across various sectors, including retail, financial, and technology.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the category that best describes the company's main area of business.
2. Fill in the company's name, location, and other relevant details as needed.
3. Provide accurate information about the company's size, employee count, and year established.
4. Answer questions about the company's revenue growth, employee satisfaction, and leadership style.
5. Enter the company's social media links and website.
6. Add any additional comments or descriptions about the company in the "Company Description" field.
7. Ensure that all required fields are filled out and that any optional fields are filled out as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select one or multiple categories that best describe the company's main area of business.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of the company.
* **Size of Business** (`size_of_business`, number, required): Enter the number of employees the company has.
* **Employee Count** (`employee_count`, number, required): Enter the number of employees the company has.
* **Year Established** (`year_established`, time, required): Enter the year the company was established.
* **Revenue Growth** (`revenue_growth`, number, required): Enter the revenue growth of the company.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate the employee satisfaction level of the company (High, Medium, Low).
* **Leadership** (`leadership`, select_multiple, required): Rate the leadership style of the company (Strong, Somewhat strong, Weak, Very weak).
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, required): Enter the URL of the company's website.
* **Company Twitter** (`company_twitter`, text, required): Enter the company's Twitter URL.
* **Company Facebook** (`company_facebook`, text, required): Enter the company's Facebook URL.
* **Company Email** (`company_email`, text, required): Enter the company's email address.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text, required): Enter the company's phone number in the format 123-456-7890.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): Enter the company's address.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee filling out the form.
* **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the employee filling out the form.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, text, required): Enter the email address of the employee filling out the form.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the employee filling out the form.
* **Company Facebook URL** (`company_facebook_url`, text, required): Enter the company's Facebook URL.
* **Company Instagram URL** (`company_instagram_url`, text, required): Enter the company's Instagram URL.
* **Company Twitter URL** (`company_twitter_url`, text, required): Enter the company's Twitter URL.
* **Company YouTube URL** (`company_youtube_url`, text, required): Enter the company's YouTube URL.
* **Company LinkedIn URL** (`company_linkedin_url`, text, required): Enter the company's LinkedIn URL.
* **Company Description** (`company_description`, note, required): Add any additional comments or descriptions about the company.
