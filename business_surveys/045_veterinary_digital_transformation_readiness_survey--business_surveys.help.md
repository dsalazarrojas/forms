# Veterinary Digital Transformation Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Digital Transformation Readiness Survey is designed to collect information about your veterinary practice, helping us assess its readiness for digital transformation. Please answer the questions as accurately as possible to help us better understand your practice's digital needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Survey Form" tab.
2. Fill out the form completely, answering all the required fields.
3. Click on "submit" to submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Form** (`survey_form`, text, optional): This is the main section of the form where you can provide your practice's information.
* **Contact Information** (`form_page_2`, text, optional): Please enter your contact details, including your name, title, and contact number.
* **Address** (`form_page_3`, text, optional): Enter your practice's address.
* **Phone Number** (`form_page_4`, text, optional): Enter your practice's phone number.
* **Email** (`form_page_5`, email, optional): Enter your practice's email address.
* **Website** (`form_page_6`, text, optional): Enter your practice's website.
* **Business Name** (`form_page_7`, text, optional): Enter your practice's name.
* **Number of Years in Practice** (`form_page_8`, number, optional): Enter the number of years your practice has been in operation.
* **Number of Clinics** (`form_page_9`, number, optional): Enter the number of clinics your practice operates.
* **Number of Employees** (`form_page_10`, number, optional): Enter the number of employees in your practice.
* **Practice Type** (`form_page_11`, text, optional): Select the type of veterinary practice you operate (e.g., general, specialty, etc.).
* **Years in Business** (`form_page_12`, number, optional): Enter the number of years your practice has been in business.

## Tips
* Please answer all the questions accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* This form will help us better understand your practice's digital needs and provide you with guidance on how to improve your digital transformation journey.
