# Compensation Fairness Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Compensation Fairness Perception Survey is designed to collect employee feedback on pay fairness, equity, and transparency within the company. The survey is anonymous and aims to help us understand employee perceptions on compensation practices. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions below to help us improve our compensation policies.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question with the option that best represents your feelings.
2.  You can choose one option per question, unless otherwise specified.
3.  If you have additional feedback or comments, use the "Suggestions for improvement" or "Additional Comments" text fields.
4.  Take the "Overall pay fairness rating" question at the end to give us a general idea of your perception on pay fairness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Field 1: Employee ID (Optional)
**Employee ID** (Number, Optional): Enter your employee ID, if you have one. If not, you can leave this field blank.

### Field 2: Department
**Department** (Select one, Required): Select your department from the list.

### Field 3: Length of Employment
**Length of Employment** (Select one, Required): Choose how long you've worked here.

### Field 4: Job Level
**Job Level** (Select one, Required): Select your current level in the company.

**Overall pay is fair** (Select one, Required): Rate your agreement with the statement "Overall, I think pay is fair."

### Field 9: Pay Fairness for Experience

### Field 10: Pay Fairness Compared to Colleagues

### Field 11: Pay Fairness Compared to Market
