# Animal Transport Safety Standards Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experiences and perceptions of animal transport safety standards. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and contribute to the development of safer animal transport practices.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the number of years you have been transporting animals.
2. Choose all species of animals you regularly transport.
3. Rate your awareness of current safety regulations.
4. Indicate how frequently you perform vehicle safety checks.
5. Describe the biggest challenge you face in maintaining animal transport safety.
6. Rate the effectiveness of current regulatory enforcement.
7. Share any suggestions for new or revised safety standards.
8. Indicate your use of integrated temperature monitoring systems.
9. Rate your emergency response preparedness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Years of Experience in Animal Transport** (1, `number`, required): Please select the number of years you have been transporting animals.
* **Primary Species Transported** (2, `select_multiple`, required): Please choose all species you regularly transport, such as Companion Animals - Dogs and Cats, Equine - Horses, or Livestock - Cattle, Swine, Sheep, etc.
* **Awareness of Current Safety Regulations** (3, `select_one`, required): Please rate your level of awareness of current safety regulations. Options: Very High, High, Moderate, Low.
* **Frequency of Vehicle Safety Checks** (4, `select_one`, required): Please select how frequently you perform vehicle safety checks. Options: Before Every Trip, Daily, Weekly, Monthly.
* **Biggest Challenge in Transport Safety** (5, `text`, required): Describe the most significant barrier you face in maintaining animal transport safety. Please provide detailed information about your experiences.
* **Effectiveness of Current Regulatory Enforcement** (6, `select_one`, required): Please rate the effectiveness of current regulatory enforcement. Options: Very Effective, Effective, Neutral, Ineffective, Very Ineffective.
* **Suggestions for New or Revised Safety Standards** (7, `text`, optional): Please provide any suggestions or ideas for new or revised safety standards. This section is optional, but your input is valuable.
* **Use of Integrated Temperature Monitoring Systems** (8, `select_one`, required): Please indicate your use of integrated temperature monitoring systems. Options: Yes - Always in use, Sometimes - For long hauls only, No - Manual checks only.
* **Emergency Response Preparedness Rating** (9, `select_one`, required): Please rate your emergency response preparedness. Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
