# Academic Collaboration Across Borders Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experiences and challenges in international academic collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the correct options for each field, if applicable.
3. If you need help with a field, refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name (Respondent Name)**: (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Your full name, which is not required but can help us track your responses.
* **Email address (Respondent Email)**: (`respondent_email`, email, required): Your email address, which is necessary to contact you for follow-up questions or to confirm your participation.
* **Institution or University (Institution)**: (`institution`, text, required): The name of your institution or university.
* **Country of your institution (Country)**: (`country`, text, required): The country where your institution or university is located.
* **Your academic role (Academic Role)**: (`academic_role`, select_one, required): Choose your academic role from the list:
	+ Professor
	+ Associate Professor
	+ Assistant Professor
	+ Lecturer
	+ Researcher
	+ Post-doctoral fellow
	+ Graduate student
	+ Other
* **Primary discipline or field (Discipline)**: (`discipline`, text, required): The primary discipline or field you are working in.
* **Have you participated in international academic collaborations? (Has International Collaborations)**: (`has_international_collaborations`, select_one, required): Select your answer from the list:
	+ Yes - currently active
	+ Yes - in the past
	+ No - but interested
	+ No - not interested
* **How many international partnerships have you been involved in? (Number of Partnerships)**: (`number_of_partnerships`, select_one, required): Choose how many international partnerships you have been involved in:
	+ None
	+ 1 to 2
	+ 3 to 5
	+ 6 to 10
	+ More than 10
* **Types of international collaborations you have engaged in (Collaboration Types)**: (`collaboration_types`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Joint research projects
	+ Co-authored publications
	+ Student exchange programs
	+ Faculty exchange
	+ Joint conferences or workshops
	+ Grant co-applications
	+ Curriculum development
* **Which regions have you collaborated with? (Regions Collaborated With)**: (`regions_collaborated_with`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ North America
	+ South America
	+ Europe
	+ Africa
	+ Asia
	+ Middle East
	+ Oceania
* **What are the biggest challenges in cross-border academic collaboration? (Major Challenges)**: (`major_challenges`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Language barriers
	+ Time zone differences
	+ Funding limitations
	+ Bureaucratic or regulatory hurdles
	+ Cultural differences
	+ Technology infrastructure gaps
	+ Intellectual property concerns
* **How well does your institution support international collaboration? (Institutional Support)**: (`institutional_support`, select_one, required): Choose your answer from the list:
	+ Very well supported
	+ Somewhat supported
	+ Neutral
	+ Poorly supported
	+ Not supported at all
* **What improvements would most benefit international academic collaboration? (Desired Improvements)**: (`desired_improvements`, text, optional): Share any thoughts or suggestions on what would improve international academic collaboration.
* **Additional comments or insights (Additional Comments)**: (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or insights you may have.
