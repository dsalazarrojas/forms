# Water Generation Market Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about user preferences and habits related to water consumption.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **How Do You Prefer Your Water?**
	* Please select one of the options for "how_do_you_prefer_your_water" (Water Bottled, Tap, Filtered Water, Well Water, or Spring Water).
2. **How Often Do You Buy Water?**
	* Select all that apply from the list for "how_often_do_you_buy_water" (Often, Occasionally, Rarely, Never).
3. **How Much Do You Spend On Water?**
	* If you have a specific amount in mind, you can enter it in the number field. (Optional)
4. **What Type Of Water Do You Prefer?**
	* Select one option for "what_type_of_water_do_you_prefer" (Bottled, Filtered, Spring, Well).
5. **How Do You Rate Your Current Water Source?**
	* Please rate your current water source on a scale from 1 to [insert range].
6. **What Do You Prefer The Taste Of?**
	* Select one option for "what_do_you_prefer_the_taste_of" (Fresh, Mild, Strong, Neutral).
7. **When Do You Buy Water?**
	* Select all that apply from the list for "when_do_you_buy_water" (Every Time, Occasionally, Rarely, Never).
8. **Note**
	* This is a text area for any additional comments or notes.
9. **Time**
	* Enter the time you usually buy water.
10. **Date**
	* Enter the date you last bought water.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How Do You Prefer Your Water? (Water Bottled, Tap, Filtered Water, Well Water, or Spring Water)**: Please select your preferred type of water.
* **How Often Do You Buy Water? (Often, Occasionally, Rarely, Never)**: Select all the frequencies at which you buy water.
* **How Much Do You Spend On Water?**: This field is optional, enter the amount you usually spend on water.
* **What Type Of Water Do You Prefer? (Bottled, Filtered, Spring, Well)**: Select your preferred type of water.
* **How Do You Rate Your Current Water Source?**: Rate your current water source on a scale from 1 to [insert range].
* **What Do You Prefer The Taste Of? (Fresh, Mild, Strong, Neutral)**: Select your preferred taste of water.
* **When Do You Buy Water? (Every Time, Occasionally, Rarely, Never)**: Select all the frequencies at which you buy water.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes you have.
* **Time**: Enter the time you usually buy water.
* **Date**: Enter the date you last bought water.
