# Veterinary Industry Diversity & Inclusion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Industry Diversity & Inclusion Survey aims to gather information on the current state of diversity and inclusion practices within the veterinary industry. Your responses will help us identify areas for improvement and contribute to creating a more inclusive and welcoming work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions honestly, and select all that apply where relevant.
2. Review each page carefully to ensure you haven't missed any important questions.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible in your response.
4. If you're unsure about any question or need clarification, please don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Workplace Culture
* **Workplace Culture** (`workplace_culture`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question asks about the current state of your workplace culture. Select all that apply:
	+ Yes: Our workplace has an inclusive and respectful culture.
	+ No: Our workplace has a culture that is not inclusive or respectful.
* This question helps us understand the overall atmosphere of your workplace.
