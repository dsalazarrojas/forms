# Organizational Climate Research Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Climate Research Application Form is a tool for organizations to provide information about themselves and their research interests to the research team. The form helps the research team to understand the organization's needs and goals, and to explore potential collaboration opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your organization's details, including name, department, and contact person information.
2. Specify your research interests and areas of collaboration.
3. Select the type(s) of research inquiry you are interested in (Research, Partnership, or Collaboration).
4. If applicable, provide additional information about your organization and research interests.
5. Agree to the terms of service by reading and accepting the terms below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Details** (`organization_details`, text, optional): This is a free-text field for you to provide any additional information about your organization.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for this inquiry.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): If applicable, specify the department within your organization that is interested in collaborating with the research team.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your organization's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Research Interest** (`research_interest`, text, optional): Describe your research interests and areas you are interested in exploring with the research team.
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Research: You are interested in conducting research with the research team.
	+ Partnership: You are interested in partnering with the research team on a research project.
	+ Collaboration: You are interested in collaborating with the research team on a specific research project.
* **Research Areas** (`research_areas`, select_one, optional): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Organizational Development
	+ Team Effectiveness
	+ Organizational Learning
	+ Innovation
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your research interests and needs.
* **Terms of Service** (`terms_of_service`, note, optional): Read and accept the terms of service to proceed with the form submission.
