# Payroll Communication Flow Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand your experience with company payroll communication. Your feedback will help us identify areas to improve our communication flow and provide better services to you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please take approximately 10-15 minutes to complete this survey.
2. Answer each question carefully, selecting the best option that reflects your genuine experience.
3. For open-ended questions, please provide detailed and clear feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What pay channels do we use**: Select all the pay channels you use to receive your payroll information (e.g., payroll email, payroll portal, manager).
* **How clear are our current pay channels**: Please rate the clarity of our current pay channels on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **How satisfied are you with our current pay channels**: Rate your satisfaction with our current pay channels on a 5-point scale.
* **What improvements do you want**: Describe the changes or improvements you would like to see in our pay channels.
* **How often do managers communicate with you**: Select how often you feel you receive communication from your manager (e.g., often, occasionally, rarely).
* **How satisfied are you with your manager**: Rate your satisfaction with your manager's communication on a 5-point scale.
* **How often do you feel you get information about team changes**: Select how often you receive information about team changes (e.g., often, occasionally, rarely).
* **How satisfied are you with team communication**: Rate your satisfaction with team communication on a 5-point scale.
* **Do you have any feedback**: Provide any comments, suggestions, or concerns about our payroll communication.
* **Comments**: Please provide any additional comments or insights about your experience with payroll communication.

Note: Please keep your answers honest and specific to help us improve our communication flow.
