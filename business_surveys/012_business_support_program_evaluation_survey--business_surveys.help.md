# Business Support Program Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather your feedback on the Business Support Program, which will help us to improve and enhance our programs for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your name, email address, and company or organization name.
2. Select the program you participated in and answer the questions regarding your participation duration and frequency.
3. Rate the program's relevance, expectations, content quality, instructor quality, and overall relevance.
4. Answer questions about the applicability, business impact, and skills gained from the program.
5. Indicate whether you successfully implemented what you learned and the quality of the support and guidance received.
6. Recommend the program to others and suggest areas for improvement.
7. Answer questions about your overall satisfaction with the program and willingness to participate in future programs.
8. Provide any additional topics you would like to see covered in future programs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name (participant_name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Email Address (participant_email)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Company or Organization Name (company_name)**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Which program did you participate in? (program_name)**: Select the name of the program you participated in.
* **How long did you participate? (participation_duration)**: Choose the duration of your participation.
* **How frequently did you participate? (participation_frequency)**: Choose the frequency of your participation.
* **How relevant was this program to your needs? (program_relevance)**: Rate the relevance of the program to your needs.
* **Were your expectations met? (expectations_met)**: Rate the fulfillment of your expectations.
* **Quality of program content (content_quality)**: Rate the quality of the program content.
* **Quality of instruction or facilitation (instructor_quality)**: Rate the quality of the instructor or facilitation.
* **How applicable is the learning to your business? (practical_application)**: Rate the applicability of the program to your business.
* **What skills or knowledge did you gain? (skills_gained)**: Select all the skills or knowledge you gained from the program.
* **Have you successfully implemented what you learned? (implementation_success)**: Indicate whether you successfully implemented what you learned.
* **Quality of support and guidance received (support_quality)**: Rate the quality of the support and guidance received.
* **Would you recommend this program to others? (recommendation_likelihood)**: Indicate whether you would recommend the program to others.
* **How could the program be improved? (improvement_suggestions)**: Provide any suggestions on how the program could be improved.
* **Would you participate in future programs? (future_participation)**: Indicate your willingness to participate in future programs.
* **What additional topics would you like covered? (additional_topics)**: Provide any additional topics you would like to see covered in future programs.
* **Overall satisfaction with the program (overall_satisfaction)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the program.

Please take a few minutes to complete this form, and be as specific as possible in your answers. Your feedback is crucial to helping us improve our Business Support Programs.
