# Business Continuity Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess and improve your organization's business continuity plans. It will help you identify your organization's current state of continuity, threats, and areas that require attention.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your organization's details, including its name and department.
2. Fill in your personal contact information, including your name, email, and position.
3. Select your current business continuity plan status and identify your critical recovery capabilities.
4. Choose the key threats that could disrupt your business operations.
5. Choose your maximum acceptable downtime and select the critical business functions that must continue.
6. Answer questions about plan testing frequency and date of last test.
7. Identify any gaps and challenges your business faces.
8. Select your main continuity challenge and improvement priority.
9. If applicable, fill in the annual budget for continuity and additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Organization Name**: Your organization's name.
- **Department or Division**: The department or division you belong to in your organization.
- **Your Name**: The full name of the person completing this form.
- **Your Email**: Your business email address.
- **Your Position**: Your job title within the organization.
- **Current Business Continuity Plan Status**: The current status of your organization's continuity plan.
  - **No Plan in Place**: No business continuity plan is in place.
  - **Plan Under Development**: A plan is being developed but not yet in use.
  - **Plan in Place but Outdated**: A plan is in place but outdated and not updated regularly.
  - **Current Plan in Use**: A business continuity plan is currently being used.
  - **Plan Recently Updated**: The plan was recently updated.
- **Critical Recovery Capabilities**: Critical capabilities your organization has to recover from a disruption. Choose all that apply.
  - **Data Backup System**: A system for backing up important data.
  - **Alternate Work Location**: A location where work can continue in case of disruption.
  - **Emergency Communication Plan**: A plan for emergency communication.
  - **Supplier Continuity Agreements**: Agreements with suppliers for continuity.
  - **Vendor Continuity Agreements**: Agreements with vendors for continuity.
  - **Key Personnel Cross-Training**: Cross-training of key personnel for continuity.
- **Key Threats**: Potential threats that could disrupt business operations. Choose all that apply.
  - **Natural Disaster**: A natural disaster.
  - **Cyber Attack**: A cyber attack.
  - **System Failure**: A system failure.
  - **Pandemic**: A pandemic.
  - **Supply Chain Disruption**: A supply chain disruption.
  - **Key Personnel Loss**: Loss of key personnel.
  - **Facility Damage**: Damage to the facility.
- **Maximum Acceptable Downtime**: The maximum time the organization can be down before it's unacceptable.
  - **Immediate**: The organization cannot be down.
  - **1-4 Hours**: The organization can be down for up to 4 hours.
  - **4-24 Hours**: The organization can be down for up to 24 hours.
  - **1-7 Days**: The organization can be down for up to 7 days.
  - **More than 7 Days**: The organization can be down for more than 7 days.
- **Critical Business Functions**: Functions that must continue operating. Enter a brief description.
- **Plan Testing Frequency**: How often the plan is tested.
  - **Never Tested**: The plan has never been tested.
  - **Annually**: The plan is tested once a year.
  - **Semi-Annually**: The plan is tested twice a year.
  - **Quarterly**: The plan is tested every 3 months.
  - **Monthly**: The plan is tested every month.
- **Date of Last Plan Test**: The date of the last test of the plan.
- **Main Continuity Challenge**: Identify the primary obstacle to continuity.
- **Top Improvement Priority**: The area that requires the most improvement.
  - **Data Protection**: Improving data protection.
  - **Technology Resilience**: Improving technology resilience.
  - **Staff Training**: Improving staff training.
  - **Supplier Agreements**: Improving supplier agreements.
  - **Communication Plan**: Improving the communication plan.
  - **Resource Allocation**: Improving resource allocation.
- **Annual Budget for Continuity**: The amount allocated for continuity.
- **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or information.
