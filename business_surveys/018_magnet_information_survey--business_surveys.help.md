# Magnet Information Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to collect information about your usage habits, satisfaction, and purchasing behavior in relation to our services. This will help us understand your needs and improve our offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question as accurately as possible.
2. Select all applicable options for each field.
3. Fill out every page of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Usage Habits** (`usage_habits`, note, optional): Briefly describe your usage habits related to our services.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_one, optional):
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	Select the option that best describes your current satisfaction level.
* **Purchase Behavior** (`purchase_behavior`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Regularly
	+ Occasionally
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
Select all the options that apply to your purchase behavior.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select your date of birth or a date relevant to your interaction with our services.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select your time of day or a time relevant to your interaction with our services.
* **Usage Frequency** (`usage_frequency`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Several Times a Week
	+ Daily
	+ Several Times a Day
Select all the options that apply to your usage frequency.
* **How Often** (`how_often`, number, optional): Enter the number of times you use our service within the given time period.
* **Purchase Amount** (`purchase_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you spent on our service within the given time period.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate with your answers.
* If you're unsure about a question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please include them in the "Usage Habits" field.
