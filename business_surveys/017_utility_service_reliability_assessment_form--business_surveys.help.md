# Utility Service Reliability Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Utility Service Reliability Assessment Form is designed to gather feedback from customers on the reliability and quality of utility services provided to them.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best fit your experience with the utility service.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please feel free to share them in the "Comments" field.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Outages**: This field asks about the frequency and duration of outages you have experienced with the utility service. Please select the options that best fit your experience.
* **Frequency of Outages**: This field asks about how often you have experienced an outage in the past month. Select the option that best reflects your experience.
* **Duration of Outages**: This field asks about the length of time you have experienced an outage. Select the option that best reflects your experience.
* **Cause of Outages**: This field asks about the reasons you have experienced an outage. Please select all options that apply.
* **Reliability of Services**: This field asks about your perception of the reliability of the utility service. Select the option that best reflects your experience.
* **Customer Service**: This field asks about your satisfaction with the customer service provided by the utility service. Select the option that best reflects your experience.
* **Communication**: This field asks about your experience with communication from the utility service. Select the option that best reflects your experience.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: This field asks about your overall satisfaction with the utility service. Select the option that best reflects your experience.
* **Comments**: This field is where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Contact Information**: This field is optional and only for customers who wish to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions.

Please note that all fields are optional, but the more information you provide, the more helpful it will be for us to understand your experience with the utility service.
