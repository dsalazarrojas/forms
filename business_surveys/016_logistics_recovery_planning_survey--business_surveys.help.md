# Logistics Recovery Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the current status of logistics recovery planning within an organization. It is designed to be completed by team members or team leaders to provide an update on the progress of recovery efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the team(s) involved in the recovery efforts from the list of options.
2. Choose the current status of the recovery planning from the list of options.
3. Enter the date of the recovery status, if applicable.
4. Enter the time of the recovery status, if applicable.
5. Add any next steps or actions that need to be taken.
6. List the team members involved in the recovery efforts, if applicable.
7. Enter any contact information, if applicable.
8. Enter the email address for follow-up, if applicable.
9. Provide any recovery status comments or concerns, if applicable.
10. Check the box to indicate if there are any recovery status concerns.
11. Provide a description of the recovery status concerns, if applicable.
12. Enter the team leader's response to the recovery status concerns, if applicable.
13. Enter the date and time of the recovery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team** (`Team`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the team(s) involved in the recovery efforts.
* **Recovery Planning Status** (`Recovery Planning Status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the current status of the recovery planning from the list of options.
* **Recovery Status Date** (`Recovery Status Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the recovery status.
* **Recovery Status Time** (`Recovery Status Time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the recovery status.
* **Next Steps** (`Next Steps`, `text`, required: false): Add any next steps or actions that need to be taken.
* **Team Members** (`Team Members`, `select_multiple`, required: false): List the team members involved in the recovery efforts, if applicable.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any contact information, if applicable.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address for follow-up, if applicable.
* **Recovery Status Comments** (`Recovery Status Comments`, `note`, required: false): Provide any recovery status comments or concerns, if applicable.
* **Recovery Status Concerns** (`Recovery Status Concerns`, `select_one`, required: false): Check the box to indicate if there are any recovery status concerns.
* **Recovery Status Concerns Description** (`Recovery Status Concerns Description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a description of the recovery status concerns, if applicable.
* **Team Leader's Response** (`Team Leader's Response`, `text`, required: false): Enter the team leader's response to the recovery status concerns, if applicable.
* **Recovery Date** (`Recovery Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the recovery date.
* **Recovery Time** (`Recovery Time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the recovery time.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct team(s) and status from the options provided.
- Enter accurate dates and times for the recovery status.
- Be sure to list all team members involved in the recovery efforts.
- Enter valid email addresses for follow-up purposes.
- Provide clear and concise comments or concerns to ensure effective communication.
- Make sure to respond to recovery status concerns with a clear and concise answer.
- Enter the correct recovery date and time.
