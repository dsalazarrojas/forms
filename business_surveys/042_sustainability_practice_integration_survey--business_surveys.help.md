# Sustainability Practice Integration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides instructions on how to complete the Sustainability Practice Integration Survey form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the answers that best apply to your situation for each question.
2. Enter any additional comments or suggestions in the text field at the end of the form.
3. Click submit to submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sustainability Practice Integration Form (1)**: This field is used to confirm that you have selected the correct options for your organization's sustainability practice integration level.
	+ Select 'Yes' if your organization's sustainability practice integration level is high.
	+ Select 'No' if your organization's sustainability practice integration level is not high.
* **Introduction (2)**: This field is used to provide a brief background about your current role and position in the organization.
	+ Enter a short paragraph about your job and why you are taking this survey.
* **What is your sustainability practice integration level? (3)**: This field is used to determine your level of knowledge and experience with sustainability practice integration.
	+ Select 'High' if you have extensive knowledge and experience in sustainability practice integration.
	+ Select 'Medium' if you have some knowledge and experience in sustainability practice integration.
	+ Select 'Low' if you have little to no knowledge and experience in sustainability practice integration.
* **What is your role in the organization? (4)**: This field is used to determine your position within the organization and level of authority you have over sustainability practice integration.
	+ Select 'Sustainability Manager' if you are responsible for sustainability practice integration.
	+ Select 'Operations Manager' if you are responsible for operations related to sustainability practice integration.
	+ Select 'Employee' if you are not in a leadership position for sustainability practice integration.
* **What is the frequency of your sustainability practice integration meetings? (5)**: This field is used to determine how often you participate in sustainability practice integration meetings.
	+ Enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD for the last meeting you attended.
* **What is the duration of your sustainability practice integration meetings? (6)**: This field is used to determine the length of time you typically spend on sustainability practice integration meetings.
	+ Enter the duration in the format HH:MM for the longest meeting you have attended.
* **What are the benefits of sustainability practice integration? (7)**: This field is used to provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the benefits of sustainability practice integration.
	+ Enter any comments or suggestions you may have.
* **What are the challenges of sustainability practice integration? (8)**: This field is used to provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the challenges of sustainability practice integration.
	+ Enter any comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Additional comments (9)**: This field is used to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
	+ Enter any comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Current state of sustainability practice integration (10)**: This field is used to determine your perception of your organization's current state of sustainability practice integration.
	+ Select 'Excellent' if you feel that your organization's sustainability practice integration is meeting your expectations.
	+ Select 'Good' if you feel that your organization's sustainability practice integration is partially meeting your expectations.
	+ Select 'Fair' if you feel that your organization's sustainability practice integration is not meeting your expectations.
* **Is the current state of sustainability practice integration meeting your expectation? (11)**: This field is used to determine whether you feel that your organization's sustainability practice integration is meeting your expectations.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you feel that your organization's sustainability practice integration is meeting your expectations.
	+ Select 'No' if you feel that your organization's sustainability practice integration is not meeting your expectations.
* **How often do you review your organization's progress on sustainability practice integration? (12)**: This field is used to determine how often you review your organization's progress on sustainability practice integration.
	+ Select 'Every day' if you review your organization's progress daily.
	+ Select 'Weekly' if you review your organization's progress weekly.
	+ Select 'Monthly' if you review your organization's progress monthly.
	+ Select 'Quarterly' if you review your organization's progress quarterly.
	+ Select 'Yearly' if you review your organization's progress yearly.
* **Additional comments or suggestions (13)**: This field is used to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
	+ Enter any comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Has this been done before? (14)**: This field is used to determine whether you have completed this survey before.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have completed this survey before.
	+ Select 'No' if this is the first time you are completing this survey.
* **What is your age? (15)**: This field is used to determine your age.
	+ Enter your age as a whole number.
* **What department do you work in? (16)**: This field is used to determine the department you work in.
	+ Select 'Sustainability' if you work in the sustainability department.
	+ Select 'Operations' if you work in the operations department.
	+ Select 'HR' if you work in the human resources department.
	+ Select 'Finance' if you work in the finance department.
* **How many years of experience do you have in sustainability practice integration? (17)**: This field is used to determine the amount of experience you have in sustainability practice integration.
	+ Enter the number of years you have experience in sustainability practice integration.
* **How many years of experience do you have in your current role? (18)**: This field is used to determine the amount of experience you have in your current role.
	+ Enter the number of years you have experience in your current role.
* **What is your job title? (19)**: This field is used to determine your job title.
	+ Enter your job title as it appears on your business card or resume.
* **What is your level in the company? (20)**: This field is used to determine your position within the company.
	+ Select 'Director' if you are a director-level employee.
	+ Select 'Manager' if you are a manager-level employee.
	+ Select 'Employee' if you are an employee.
* **What is your education level? (21)**: This field is used to determine your education level.
	+ Select 'High School' if you only have a high school education.
	+ Select 'Bachelor' if you have a bachelor's degree.
	+ Select 'Master' if you have a master's degree.
	+ Select 'Ph.D.' if you have a Ph.D.
* **How many years of experience do you have in sustainability practice integration? (21)**: This field is used to determine the amount of experience you have in sustainability practice integration.
	+ Enter the number of years you have experience in sustainability practice integration.
* **How many years of experience do you have in your current role? (22)**: This field is used to determine the amount of experience you have in your current role.
	+ Enter the number of years you have experience in your current role.
* **How many employees do you manage? (23)**: This field is used to determine the number of employees you are responsible for.
	+ Enter the number of employees you manage.
* **What is the size of your team? (24)**: This field is used to determine the number of people on your team.
	+ Enter the number of people on your team.
* **Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey (25)**: This field is used to thank you for completing the survey.
	+ Enter any final thoughts or comments.
	+ Select 'Thank you' if you have no comments or suggestions.
