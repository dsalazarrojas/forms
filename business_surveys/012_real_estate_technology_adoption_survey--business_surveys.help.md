# Real Estate Technology Adoption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on real estate technology adoption from businesses. The information collected will help us understand the current state and future trends in the industry.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your real estate company's information in the **company_info** field.
2. Provide your contact information in the **contact_info** field.
3. Answer yes or no to the question about whether you currently use real estate technology in the **tech_usage** field.
4. Choose all that apply for your future adoption plans in the **future_adoption_plans** field.
5. Describe your future adoption plans in the **future_adoption_plans_description** field.
6. Answer yes or no to the question about whether you currently face any challenges in the **current_challenges** field.
7. Choose all that apply for the challenges you currently face in the **challenges** field.
8. Answer yes or no to the question about whether you expect to face any challenges in the future in the **future_challenges** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **company_info** (Text): Enter your real estate company's information. This field is not required.
* **contact_info** (Text): Provide your contact information. This field is not required.
* **tech_usage** (Select One): Answer yes or no to whether you currently use real estate technology. This field is not required.
* **future_adoption_plans** (Select Multiple): Choose all that apply for your future adoption plans. This field is not required.
* **future_adoption_plans_description** (Text): Describe your future adoption plans. This field is not required.
* **challenges** (Text): Describe the challenges you face. This field is not required.
* **current_challenges** (Select Multiple): Answer yes or no to whether you currently face any challenges. This field is required.
* **future_challenges** (Select One): Answer yes or no to whether you expect to face any challenges in the future. This field is not required.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* This form is intended to be completed by business users, so please ensure that you're representing your business accurately.
