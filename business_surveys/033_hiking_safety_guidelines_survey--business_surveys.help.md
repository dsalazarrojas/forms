# Hiking Safety Guidelines Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hiking Safety Guidelines Survey is designed to collect information about hikers' safety habits, education, and experiences related to safety procedures. This data will be used to identify areas for improvement in hiking safety guidelines and to assess risk factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide honest and accurate responses.
2. Fill out the form only once.
3. Enter your responses in the text fields by typing in the required information.
4. Select the appropriate options for multiple-choice fields.
5. Submit the form when you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional comments or notes you may have about your hiking experience or safety concerns.
* **Hiking Group** (`hiking_group`, text, required: false): Enter your hiking group or organization name, if applicable.
* **Experience** (`experience`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ 'Yes': You have previous experience with hiking and safety procedures.
	+ 'No': You have little to no experience with hiking and safety procedures.
* **Safety Habits** (`safety_habits`, text, required: false): Describe your safety habits, such as always carrying a first aid kit or knowing how to navigate using a map and compass.
* **Safety Education** (`safety_education`, select_one, required: false):
	+ 'Yes': You have received safety education or training, such as a wilderness first aid course.
	+ 'No': You have not received safety education or training.
* **Emergency Procedure** (`emergency_procedure`, select_one, required: false):
	+ 'Yes': You know the emergency procedure for responding to common hiking hazards, such as inclement weather or injuries.
	+ 'No': You are not familiar with the emergency procedure.
* **Equipment Use** (`equipment_use`, text, required: false): Describe the safety equipment you use while hiking, such as a GPS device or bear spray.
* **Survey Completion** (`survey_completion`, select_one, required: false):
	+ 'Yes': You completed this survey accurately and truthfully.
	+ 'No': You did not complete this survey accurately and truthfully.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person, such as a spouse or family member.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or concerns you may have about your hiking experience or safety procedures.
