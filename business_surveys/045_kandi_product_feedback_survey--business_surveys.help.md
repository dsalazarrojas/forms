# Kandi Product Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kandi Product Feedback Survey is designed to collect honest feedback from customers about their experience with our product. Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time in filling out this survey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction to understand the purpose of the form.
2. Select one of the five options for "How was your experience with our product?" that best describes your feelings.
3. If you have any improvement ideas for our product, type them in the text field provided.
4. Check the box next to any issue reports you have encountered with our product.
5. Enter your email address so we can contact you for further clarification or to thank you for your feedback.
6. Enter your phone number (optional) in case we need to contact you.
7. Fill in your date and time of interaction with our product (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, optional): This section is a brief welcome message. Please read it before proceeding with the survey.
* **How was your experience with our product?** (`product_satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the five options that best describe your feelings about our product.
	+ Very Satisfied: You are extremely happy with our product and its performance.
	+ Satisfied: You are generally happy with our product, but there is room for improvement.
	+ Neutral: You are neither happy nor unhappy with our product.
	+ Dissatisfied: You are unhappy with our product, but we can still fix things.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: You are extremely unhappy with our product and its performance.
* **What improvement ideas do you have?** (`product_improvement_ideas`, `text`, optional): Share any ideas you have for how we can improve our product. Be as specific as possible.
* **Do you have any issue reports?** (`issue_reports`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the box next to any issues you have encountered with our product. You can select multiple issues if applicable.
* **E-mail** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further clarification or to thank you for your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your interaction with our product.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your interaction with our product.
