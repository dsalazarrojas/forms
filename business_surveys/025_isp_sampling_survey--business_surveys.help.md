# isp_sampling_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and data from internet service providers (ISPs) about their experience with our sampling methods.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select the correct options for each question, taking into account your experience with our sampling methods.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "survey_comments" or "survey_comments_other" fields to provide your input.
4. Once you have completed all the fields, click "Submit" to send your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Internet Provider Name** (`internet_provider_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your internet service provider.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options to indicate your overall satisfaction with our sampling methods:
	+ very satisfied
	+ somewhat satisfied
	+ neutral
	+ somewhat dissatisfied
	+ very dissatisfied
* **Interest in Sampling** (`interest_in_sampling`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Maybe
* **Trial Period Days** (`trial_period_days`, number, optional): Enter the number of days you have been participating in our sampling methods.
* **Current ISP** (`current_isp`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options to indicate your current internet service provider:
	+ isp1
	+ isp2
	+ isp3
* **Current ISP (Other)** (`current_isp_other`, text, optional): If your current internet service provider is not listed, enter the name of your current provider here.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for completing this form.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person responsible for completing this form.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person responsible for completing this form.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about our sampling methods.
* **Survey Comments** (`survey_comments`, text, optional): If you have any other comments or feedback, please include them here.
* **Survey Comments (Other)** (`survey_comments_other`, text, optional): If you have any other comments or feedback that do not fit in the above field, please include them here.
