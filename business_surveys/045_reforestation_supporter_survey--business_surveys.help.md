# reforestation_supporter_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather insights from supporters of reforestation efforts to refine communication strategies and improve the overall reforestation experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and accurately.
2. Select the options that best fit your preferences and experiences.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
4. Please confirm your submission before proceeding to the next step.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in the reforestation effort?** (`1`, `text`, optional): Describe your role in the reforestation effort in a few words. This will help us understand the different perspectives and contributions of our supporters.
* **What are your preferred communication methods?** (`2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all the methods you prefer to receive updates and information from us. This will help us tailor our communication to your needs.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address to be contacted by us.
* **Phone Number** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number to be contacted by us.
* **Supporter Name** (`5`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as a supporter of reforestation efforts.
* **Organization Name** (`6`, `text`, optional): If you are representing an organization, enter your organization's name.
* **What is your role in the reforestation effort?** (`7`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you contribute to reforestation efforts. This will help us categorize our supporters and tailor our approach to their specific needs.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`8`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you first heard about reforestation efforts.
* **How did you join the reforestation effort?** (`9`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you became involved in reforestation efforts.
* **How often do you participate in reforestation efforts?** (`10`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how frequently you engage with reforestation activities.
* **How long have you been supporting reforestation efforts?** (`11`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how long you have been supporting reforestation activities.
* **What are your reforestation goals?** (`12`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your reforestation goals to help us tailor our support to your needs.
* **Favorite tree species?** (`13`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your favorite tree species to help us better understand your preferences.
* **Favorite way to plant trees?** (`14`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your favorite method to engage with reforestation activities.
* **Years of reforestation experience?** (`15`, `text`, optional): Describe your level of experience with reforestation efforts.
* **How would you like to be contacted?** (`16`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose how you prefer to be contacted for updates and information.
* **Supporter status?** (`17`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your current supporter status to help us understand your level of engagement.
* **Additional notes** (`18`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please share them with us.
* **Confirm** (`19`, `select_one`, optional): Please confirm that you have completed the form accurately and honestly.
