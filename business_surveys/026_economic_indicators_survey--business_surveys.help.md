# Economic Indicators Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data on economic indicators, such as economic sentiment and outlook, from various regions and departments within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the Audience option that best describes your role within the organization.
2. Choose your country from the list of options.
3. Rate your economic sentiment on a scale from 1 to 5.
4. Enter your expected economic outlook in the given date format.
5. Add any additional comments or notes if needed.
6. Fill in the assigned tool, user, and email address (if applicable).
7. Enter your contact details, including email, phone number, and job title.
8. Choose your department and region (if applicable).
9. Select your manager's name and email address (if applicable).
10. Choose your team leader's name and email address (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Audience** (`select_multiple`, **required: false**): Choose the role you currently hold within the organization, e.g., Employee, Manager, Team Leader, etc.
* **Country** (`select_one`, **required: true**): Select your country from the list of options.
* **Economic Sentiment** (`number`, **required: true**): Rate your economic sentiment on a scale from 1 to 5, where:
	+ 1: Very negative
	+ 2: Somewhat negative
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat positive
	+ 5: Very positive
* **Economic Outlook** (`date`, **required: true**): Enter your expected economic outlook in the given date format (e.g., 2024-01-01).
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, **required: false`): Add any additional comments or notes if needed.
* **Assigned Tool** (`text`, **required: false`): If assigned a tool, enter the tool name here.
* **Assigned User** (`select_one`, **required: true`): Choose 'Yes' if you are assigned a user, and enter their name or email address.
* **Email** (`email`, **required: true`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`text`, **required: true`): Enter your phone number.
* **Time** (`time`, **required: true`): Enter the time zone you are in (e.g., UTC+0).
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, **required: false`): Add any additional comments or notes if needed.
* **Company** (`text`, **required: false`): Enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (`text`, **required: true`): Enter your job title.
* **Employee Number** (`number`, **required: true`): Enter your employee number.
* **Department** (`select_one`, **required: true`): Choose your department from the list of options.
* **Manager Name** (`text`, **required: false`): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`email`, **required: false`): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Team Leader** (`select_one`, **required: false`): Choose your team leader's name from the list of options.
* **Team Leader Email** (`email`, **required: false`): Enter your team leader's email address.
* **Region** (`select_multiple`, **required: false`): Choose your region from the list of options.
* **Region 2** (`select_multiple`, **required: false`): Choose your second region from the list of options.
* **Region 3** (`select_multiple`, **required: false`): Choose your third region from the list of options.
* **Region 4** (`select_multiple`, **required: false`): Choose your fourth region from the list of options.
* **Region 5** (`select_multiple`, **required: false`): Choose your fifth region from the list of options.
