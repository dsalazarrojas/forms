# Workforce Collaboration Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather insights into team collaboration experiences and challenges to improve team performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions honestly and provide as much detail as possible. You may skip any questions that do not apply to you. Please enter your email address and phone number if you want to be contacted by your manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Member Information** (`team_member_info`): Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
* **How would you rate your collaboration level with your team members?** (`collaboration_level`): Choose from the options: Team Collaboration, Limited Collaboration, or No Collaboration.
* **How often do you collaborate with your team members?** (`collaboration_frequency`): Choose all that apply: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely.
* **How would you rate the effectiveness of your team's collaboration?** (`collaboration_effectiveness`): Choose from the options: Highly Effective, Somewhat Effective, Not Very Effective, or Not Effective At All.
* **How important do you think collaboration is in your job?** (`collaboration_importance`): Choose from the options: Very Important, Somewhat Important, Not Very Important, or Not Important At All.
* **Which of the following tools does your team use for collaboration?** (`collaboration_tools`): Choose all that apply: Email, Chat, Video Conference, or Shared Drive.
* **Which of the following challenges does your team face for collaboration?** (`collaboration_barriers`): Choose all that apply: Communication issues, Time zone differences, Cultural differences, or Technical issues.
* **Which of the following opportunities does your team see for collaboration?** (`collaboration_opportunities`): Choose all that apply: Improved teamwork, Increased productivity, Better communication, or Improved work environment.
* **What would improve team collaboration?** (`collaboration_recommendations`): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Additional comments** (`collaboration_comments`): Enter any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Email** (`team_member_email`): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`team_member_phone`): Enter your work phone number.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`): Enter your manager's title.
* **Team Member Title** (`team_member_title`): Enter your team member title.
* **Department** (`department`): Enter your department name.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`): Enter your job function or role.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`): Enter your job level or seniority level.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`): Enter your job type or category.
* **How long have you been with the team?** (`team_member_since`): Choose from the options: Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, or More than 10 years.
* **How long have you been with the team? options** (`team_member_since_options`): Choose from the options: Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, or More than 10 years.
* **Historical options** (`team_member_since_historical`): Enter historical details.
* **Submission** (`submission`): Choose from the options: Submit, Cancel, or Preview.

Please submit the form when you are ready.
