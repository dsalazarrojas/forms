# Vacant Land Listing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for gathering information about vacant land listings.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the property details section, including the property's location, size, and use.
2. Move on to the listing agent's contact information, including their name, email, phone number, and address.
3. Provide any additional details about the property, such as its features and notes.
4. Finally, confirm the submission date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Details** (`1`, `property_details`, required: `false`): Enter the details about the property, including its location, size, and use.
* **Listing Type** (`2`, `listing_type`, required: `false`): Select the type of listing (Land, Residential, Commercial).
* **Land Size** (`3`, `land_size`, required: `false`): Enter the size of the land in numbers.
* **Land Use** (`4`, `land_use`, required: `false`): Select the use of the land (Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Agricultural).
* **Property Features** (`5`, `property_features`, required: `false`): Select the features of the property (Fenced, Leased, Unfenced, Leased and Fenced).
* **Property Location** (`6`, `property_location`, required: `false`): Select the location of the property (North, East, South, West).
* **Property Price** (`7`, `property_price`, required: `false`): Enter the price of the property in numbers.
* **Property Status** (`8`, `property_status`, required: `false`): Select the status of the property (Active, Sold, Under Contract).
* **Contact Name** (`9`, `contact_name`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the listing agent.
* **Contact Email** (`10`, `contact_email`, required: `false`): Enter the email of the listing agent.
* **Contact Phone** (`11`, `contact_phone`, required: `false`): Enter the phone number of the listing agent.
* **Contact Address** (`12`, `contact_address`, required: `false`): Enter the address of the listing agent.
* **Property Address** (`13`, `property_address`, required: `false`): Enter the address of the property.
* **Property City** (`14`, `property_city`, required: `false`): Enter the city of the property.
* **Property State** (`15`, `property_state`, required: `false`): Enter the state of the property.
* **Property Zip** (`16`, `property_zip`, required: `false`): Enter the zip code of the property.
* **Property Neighborhood** (`17`, `property_neighborhood`, required: `false`): Enter the neighborhood of the property.
* **Property Map** (`18`, `property_map`, required: `false`): Enter the map of the property.
* **Property Map URL** (`19`, `property_map_url`, required: `false`): Enter the URL of the property map.
* **Listing Agent** (`20`, `listing_agent`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the listing agent.
* **Listing Agency** (`21`, `listing_agency`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the listing agency.
* **Listing Agent Phone** (`22`, `listing_agent_phone`, required: `false`): Enter the phone number of the listing agent.
* **Property Image** (`23`, `property_image`, required: `false`): Enter the image of the property.
* **Property Notes** (`24`, `property_notes`, required: `false`): Enter any additional notes about the property.
* **Submission Date** (`25`, `submission_date`, required: `false`): This is the date and time the form was submitted.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for help.
* This form is not designed for repeat submissions. Once you've filled it out, you're done!
* If you need help or have questions, feel free to reach out to the listing agent or our support team for assistance.
