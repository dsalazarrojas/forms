# National Salary Survey For Technology - Help Guide

## Purpose
The National Salary Survey For Technology form is designed to collect data on employee salaries and benefits in the technology industry. This data will be used to analyze and provide insights on industry trends, compensation, and benefits.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your base information, including any relevant details about yourself.
2. Enter your level of experience in the technology industry.
3. Share your current salary information.
4. Select the benefits you receive from your current or previous employers (health insurance, stock options, retirement plan, or other benefits).
5. Enter your company's information, including any relevant details.
6. Provide your manager's information, if applicable.
7. Optionally, upload any supporting documents, such as contracts or agreements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Base Information (Base Information, Text, Optional)**: This field is where you can provide any additional details about yourself, such as your job title, department, or any other relevant information.
* **Experience (Experience, Number, Required)**: This field is used to gauge your level of experience in the technology industry. Please provide your number of years of experience.
* **Salary Information (Salary Information, Number, Required)**: Enter your current or expected salary.
* **Benefits (Benefits, Select One, Required)**: Select one or more benefits you receive from your current or previous employers. This field will help us understand what benefits are common in the industry.
* **Company Information (Company Information, Text, Required)**: Provide any relevant details about your company, such as its size, location, or industry.
* **Manager Information (Manager Information, Text, Required)**: If applicable, please provide your manager's information, including their contact details.
* **Upload Documents (Upload Documents, Text, Optional)**: If you have any supporting documents, such as contracts or agreements, please upload them here. This field is optional, and you can skip it if not necessary.
