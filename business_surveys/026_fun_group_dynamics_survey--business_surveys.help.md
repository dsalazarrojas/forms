## Fun Group Dynamics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fun Group Dynamics Survey is designed to gather information about the dynamics within teams. It helps teams reflect on their communication styles, collaboration, and conflict resolution strategies. This survey is meant to be a tool for teams to self-assess and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly, and provide the best possible information about your team's dynamics.
2. Select the options that best describe your team's communication style, collaboration, and conflict resolution strategies.
3. Be as specific as possible when describing any open-ended questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your team.
* **Team Role** (`team_role`, select_one, required): Choose the role you play within the team (Team Lead or Team Member).
* **Communication Style** (`communication_style`, select_multiple, optional): Describe how you usually communicate with your teammates (Very open and approachable, Friendly and approachable, Somewhat open and approachable, Not very open and approachable, Not at all open and approachable).
* **Team Culture** (`team_culture`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the culture of your team (Fun, Positive and supportive, Respectful, Helpful, Competitive, Other).
* **Collaboration Style** (`collaboration_style`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your team's collaboration (Very good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Work Environment** (`work_environment`, select_one, optional): Choose the environment where you usually work (Office, Home, Virtual, Other).
* **Time Spent Working** (`time_spent_working`, number, optional): Estimate the average time you spend working on projects.
* **Team Leadership** (`team_leadership`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the leadership of your team (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the ways you usually communicate with teammates (Email, Phone, In-person, Chat, Video conference, Other).
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your overall experience with the team (Very good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions or areas for improvement.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (not publicly visible).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (not publicly visible).
* **Time Commitment** (`time_commitment`, number, optional): Estimate the average time you commit to projects.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the size of your team (Small, Medium, Large, Very Large).
* **Team Dynamics** (`team_dynamics`, select_one, optional): Rate your team's dynamics (Very good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Conflict Resolution** (`conflict_resolution`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you usually resolve conflicts within the team (Collaborative, Competitive, Avoiding, Confrontational).
* **Conflict Resolution What** (`conflict_resolution_what`, text, optional): Describe what triggers conflicts in your team.
* **Conflict Resolution How** (`conflict_resolution_how`, text, optional): Describe how you usually resolve conflicts within the team.
* **Collaboration What** (`collaboration_what`, text, optional): Describe how you usually collaborate with your teammates.
* **Collaboration How** (`collaboration_how`, text, optional): Describe how you usually collaborate with your teammates.
* **Team Goals** (`team_goals`, text, optional): Describe your team's goals.
* **Team Mission** (`team_mission`, text, optional): Describe your team's mission.
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or thoughts.
