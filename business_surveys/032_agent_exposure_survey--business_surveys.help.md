# Agent Exposure Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agent Exposure Survey is a form used to collect information about exposures, which are incidents or situations where an individual comes into contact with a hazardous substance or environment. This form is used by supervisors to gather necessary details to report and address such incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, marked with an asterisk (\*), on each page.
2. Review each field carefully and ensure that you provide accurate and complete information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Agent name** (`agent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person reporting the exposure.
- **Incident date** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the exposure occurred.
- **Type of exposure** (`exposure_type`, select_one, required): Select the category of exposure from the options provided.
  - Choose "Chemical" for chemical exposure.
  - Choose "Biological" for biological exposure.
  - Choose "Physical" for physical exposure.
  - Choose "Other" for other types of exposure.
- **Symptoms present** (`symptoms_present`, text, required): Describe any symptoms observed during the exposure.
- **Immediate actions taken** (`immediate_actions_taken`, text, required): Describe the first aid or containment steps taken after the exposure.
- **Reported to supervisor** (`reported_to_supervisor`, select_one, required): Confirm whether the exposure was reported to a supervisor.
  - Choose "True" if reported to a supervisor.
  - Choose "False" otherwise.
- **Follow up required** (`follow_up_required`, select_one, required): Confirm whether further investigation is required.
  - Choose "True" if further investigation is required.
  - Choose "False" otherwise.
- **Follow up notes** (`follow_up_notes`, text, required): Describe the follow-up actions planned.
- **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number for follow-up.
- **Follow-up notes** (`follow_up_notes`, text, optional): Describe any additional follow-up actions planned.
