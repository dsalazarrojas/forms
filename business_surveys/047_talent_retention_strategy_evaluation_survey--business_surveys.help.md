## Talent Retention Strategy Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of your talent retention strategies within your organization. It aims to gather information on various aspects of job satisfaction, company climate, leadership support, communication, career development opportunities, work-life balance, employee feedback, and retention strategies. This will help you identify areas where your organization can improve talent retention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions as honestly as possible.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the answer that best represents your experience.
3. For open-ended questions, provide a brief description of your thoughts.
4. Be specific when describing areas for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Satisfication** (`job_satisfaction`, text, required): 
  Please describe your overall job satisfaction, using the space provided. This will help us understand how happy you are with your job.
* **Company Climate** (`company_climate`, text, optional): 
  Describe your experience of the company culture and environment.
* **Leadership Support** (`leadership_support`, select_one, optional): 
  Please select whether you feel supported by your leaders.
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_multiple, optional): 
  Select which types of communication you experience with your colleagues and management, such as email, phone calls, or in-person meetings.
* **Career Development Opportunities** (`career_development_opportunities`, number, optional): 
  Please indicate how many career development opportunities you have access to, such as training, mentorship, or education programs.
* **Work-Life Balance** (`work_life_balance`, select_one, optional): 
  Please select how well your work-life balance is achieved.
* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, text, required): 
  Please describe any positive or negative feedback you have received from your colleagues or management.
* **Retention Strategies** (`retention_strategies`, select_multiple, optional): 
  Select which retention strategies are in place within your company, such as bonuses, promotions, or flexible work arrangements.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, select_one, optional): 
  Please select whether there are any areas where the company can improve talent retention.
* **Survey Completion** (`survey_completion`, time, optional): 
  Please estimate how much time you spent completing this survey.
* **Manager Support** (`manager_support`, select_multiple, optional): 
  Select which types of support you receive from your manager.
* **Feedback Received** (`feedback_recieved`, select_one, optional): 
  Please select whether you received feedback on your performance.
* **Retention Strategies Improvement** (`retention_strategies_improve`, number, required): 
  Please provide the number of retention strategies that you think need improvement.
* **Page 2** (`page_2`, note, optional): 
  This is a note page where you can add any additional comments or suggestions.
