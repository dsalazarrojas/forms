# Reality Check Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reality Check Survey form is designed to gather information about your thoughts, feelings, and experiences related to your current reality. This form helps you reflect on your goals, actions, and expectations, and is useful for personal or professional development, goal-setting, or self-reflection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Use the text fields to provide your thoughts, feelings, and experiences.
3. Select the options that best match your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. What is the purpose of this form?**: This field is not used in this form, but it's included to guide you through the form's purpose.
* **2. What would you like to know more about?**: Please share the specific topic or area you're interested in learning more about.
* **3. What are you currently doing?**: Describe your current actions, behaviors, or activities related to the topic.
* **4. What do you wish to accomplish?**: State your goals, objectives, or outcomes you're trying to achieve.
* **5. What are some challenges you face?**: List any obstacles, difficulties, or hindrances you're experiencing.
* **6. How would you rate the importance?**: Select the level of importance for the current task or activity (High, Medium, Low).
* **7. What is your current reality?**: Provide a detailed description of your current situation, state of mind, or environment.
* **8. What would you like to see change in?**: Specify areas or aspects you'd like to change or improve.
* **9. What would you do differently?**: Describe alternative actions or approaches you would take if you could.
* **10. What can I do to help you?**: This field is not directly applicable to this form.
* **11. What is the next action?**: Indicate the next step or task you plan to take.
* **12. What is the next step?**: Describe the next action or activity you'll take.
* **13. What do you expect from me?**: This field is not applicable to this form.
* **14. What do you expect from others?**: This field is not applicable to this form.
* **15. What have you learned?**: Share any insights, knowledge, or breakthroughs you've gained.
* **16. What would you do differently if you could?**: Describe alternative actions or approaches you would take if you had the opportunity.
* **17. What would you do more often?**: Indicate activities or behaviors you'd like to increase or repeat.
* **18. What would you do less often?**: Describe actions or behaviors you'd like to decrease or avoid.
* **19. What have you done recently?**: Provide details about your recent activities or experiences.
* **20. What will you do this week?**: Indicate the next actions or tasks you plan to take.
* **21. What is the most important thing?**: Identify the most critical aspect or activity.
* **22. What is the least important thing?**: Describe the least critical aspect or activity.
* **23. What is the next most important thing?**: Indicate the next most important task or activity.
* **24. What would you like to do next?**: Specify the next action or step you'd like to take.
* **25. Do you have any other thoughts?**: Share any additional thoughts, ideas, or concerns you have.
