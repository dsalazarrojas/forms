# Market Research Completion Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the completion of a market research study, ensuring that all necessary information is captured and recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form title field with the title of the market research study.
*   Enter your name in the participant name field.
*   Record the date and time of the survey in the participant survey date and participant survey time fields.
*   Provide your researcher's name in the researcher name field.
*   Enter your researcher's ID in the researcher ID field (if applicable).
*   Fill out the research project name and research project ID fields if they are relevant to the study.
*   Enter the research study ID if it is relevant to the study.
*   Add any relevant notes about the research study in the research study notes field.
*   Provide any comments from the participant and researcher in their respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Form Title** (Form Title): Enter the title of the market research study.
*   **Participant Name** (Participant Name): Enter your name as the participant in the study.
*   **Participant Survey Date** (Participant Survey Date): Record the date of the survey.
*   **Participant Survey Time** (Participant Survey Time): Record the time of the survey.
*   **Researcher Name** (Researcher Name): Enter the name of the researcher who conducted the study.
*   **Researcher ID** (Researcher ID): Enter the ID of the researcher (if applicable).
*   **Research Project Name** (Research Project Name): Enter the name of the research project.
*   **Research Project ID** (Research Project ID): Enter the ID of the research project.
*   **Research Study ID** (Research Study ID): Enter the ID of the research study.
*   **Research Study Notes** (Research Study Notes): Add any relevant notes about the research study.
*   **Participant Comments** (Participant Comments): Enter any comments from the participant.
*   **Researcher Comments** (Researcher Comments): Enter any comments from the researcher.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate recording of the market research study completion.
*   If you're unsure about any field, refer to the study's documentation or contact the researcher for clarification.
*   Keep the participant comments and researcher comments fields for any important notes or feedback.
