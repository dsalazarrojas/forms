# Vegetable Rationing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on households' vegetable rationing practices, availability, and accessibility. It's used to gather information on various aspects of vegetable rationing, including household demographics and individual responses to survey questions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by reviewing the survey questions**: Take a few moments to read through the questions carefully to ensure you understand what each one is asking.
2. **Answer each question to the best of your ability**: For each question, provide an honest and accurate response.
3. **Make sure to complete all required fields**: Some fields are marked as "required," which means they must be completed for the survey to be submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Household formation**: A description of your household, including the number of people living there.
* **Rationing methods**: Indicate whether you and your household practice rationing vegetables. Select all options that apply.
* **Vegetable use**: Please describe your household's use of vegetables, including any relevant details.
* **Challenges and barriers**: Describe any challenges or barriers you face when it comes to vegetable rationing.
* **Household size**: The number of people living in your household.
* **Survey date**: The date you're completing this survey.
* **Survey time**: The time you're completing this survey.
* **Household income**: The income of your household.
* **Surveyer email**: Your email address, used to contact you if needed.
* **Surveyer phone**: Your phone number, used to contact you if needed.
* **Surveyer name**: Your name, used to identify you in the survey.
* **Surveyer note**: Any additional information you'd like to provide.
* **Surveyer organization**: The organization you're representing (if applicable).
* **Surveyer title**: Your title or position within the organization (if applicable).
* **Household address**: The address of your household.
* **Household city**: The city where your household is located.
* **Household state**: The state where your household is located.
* **Household zip**: The zip code of your household.
* **Vegetable type**: The type of vegetables you have access to.
* **Vegetable rationing frequency**: How often you practice vegetable rationing.
* **Vegetable importance**: The importance of vegetables to you and your household.
* **Vegetable accessibility**: How accessible vegetables are to you and your household.
* **Vegetable availability**: The availability of vegetables to you and your household.
* **Vegetable storage**: How you store vegetables.
* **Other vegetables**: List any other vegetables that are not listed (select all that apply).

Note: Please be honest and accurate when answering these questions. This survey is designed to collect data for research purposes only.
