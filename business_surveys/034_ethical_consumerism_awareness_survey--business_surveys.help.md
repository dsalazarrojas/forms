# Ethical Consumerism Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on consumers' awareness and practices related to ethical consumerism, specifically regarding the environmental impact and sustainability of purchasing decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions to the best of your ability and honestly.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best describes your behavior or attitude.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Survey Form (id: 1)
* **Survey Form** (`text`, not required): This is the main section of the form where you can provide any additional comments or information about your purchasing habits and preferences.

### Introduction (id: 2)
* **Introduction** (`note`, not required): This section is a brief introduction to the survey and its purpose. Please read it carefully before proceeding.

### Ethical Purchasing Behaviors (id: 3)
* **Ethical Purchasing Behaviors** (`select_multiple`, not required):
	+ **Always consider the environmental impact of my purchasing decisions**: You consider the environmental impact of your purchasing decisions all the time.
	+ **Often consider the environmental impact of my purchasing decisions**: You usually think about the environmental impact of your purchasing decisions.
	+ **Sometimes consider the environmental impact of my purchasing decisions**: You occasionally think about the environmental impact of your purchasing decisions.
	+ **Rarely consider the environmental impact of my purchasing decisions**: You hardly ever think about the environmental impact of your purchasing decisions.
	+ **Never consider the environmental impact of my purchasing decisions**: You never think about the environmental impact of your purchasing decisions

### Purchasing Patterns (id: 4)
* **Purchasing Patterns** (`select_one`, not required):
	+ **Buy only from brands I know are sustainable**: You only buy from brands that are known for their sustainable practices.
	+ **Buy mostly from brands I know are sustainable**: You mostly buy from brands that are known for their sustainable practices.
	+ **Buy some from brands I know are sustainable**: You occasionally buy from brands that are known for their sustainable practices.
	+ **Buy only from non-sustainable brands**: You only buy from brands that are not known for their sustainable practices.
	+ **Buy mostly from non-sustainable brands**: You mostly buy from brands that are not known for their sustainable practices.
	+ **Buy some from non-sustainable brands**: You occasionally buy from brands that are not known for their sustainable practices.

### Social Awareness (id: 5)
* **Social Awareness** (`select_multiple`, not required):
	+ **I have a very good understanding of where my money goes**: You have a clear idea of where your money is spent.
	+ **I have a good understanding of where my money goes**: You have some idea of where your money is spent.
	+ **I have some understanding of where my money goes**: You have a basic understanding of where your money is spent.
	+ **I have little understanding of where my money goes**: You have a limited understanding of where your money is spent.
	+ **I have no idea where my money goes**: You are not sure where your money is spent.
