# Team Productivity Barriers Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Productivity Barriers Survey is designed to help us understand the challenges that affect team productivity and performance. By completing this survey, you will be contributing to our efforts to identify and address potential obstacles that hinder team success.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and provide honest answers.
2. Select all that apply for questions that have multiple options.
3. Enter your thoughts and suggestions in the text fields.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by a '*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Performance** (`team_performance`, `text`, optional): Describe your team's overall performance and how you feel it is currently.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate which team members are currently experiencing challenges or obstacles that impact productivity. Select all that apply.
* **Obstacles to Performance** (`obstacles_to_performance`, `select_one`, required): Select the single most significant obstacle to your team's performance.
* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, `text`, optional): Share any feedback or suggestions you have regarding what you think would improve team productivity.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, optional): Suggest ideas for overcoming the obstacles you've identified.
* **Team Dynamics** (`team_dynamics`, `text`, optional): Describe any changes in team dynamics that you think are impacting productivity.
* **Communication Barriers** (`communication_barriers`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate which communication barriers are affecting team productivity. Select all that apply.
* **Resource Availability** (`resource_availability`, `date`, optional): If you've experienced any challenges with resource availability, please select a date when the issue occurred.
