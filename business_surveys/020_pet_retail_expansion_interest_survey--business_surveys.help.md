# Pet Retail Expansion Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your interest in expanding your pet retail business. We want to know about your preferences regarding regions, pet types, and other relevant information that will aid us in providing better services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "Submit" to start the survey.
2. Answer the questions according to your business interests.
3. You can select multiple regions and pet types that you are interested in.
4. Enter your email and phone number (if you're willing to share).
5. Provide any feedback in the "Feedback" field.
6. Click "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Retailer**: Enter your pet retail business name (if applicable).
* **Regions of Interest**: Select the regions where you are interested in expanding your business.
	+ This field is not required, but it will help us understand where you are most interested in exploring new opportunities.
* **Feedback**: Enter any comments or suggestions you have about our services or expansion plans.
	+ This is an optional field, but it will help us provide better services tailored to your needs.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if you're willing to share).
	+ This is not required, but it will help us contact you with more information about our services.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if you're willing to share).
	+ This is not required, but it will help us contact you with more information about our services.
* **Pet Store**: Select the type of pet store you currently own or represent.
	+ This field will help us understand which types of pet stores are most popular.
* **Pet Type**: Select the type of pets you sell or are interested in selling (e.g., Dog, Cat, Other).
* **Pet Retail Expansion Interest**: Enter a numerical value (1-100) indicating how interested you are in expanding your pet retail business.
* **Pet Retail Expansion Regions**: Select the regions you are interested in expanding your business to.
* **Pet Retail Expansion Feedback**: Enter any feedback or comments about our services or expansion plans.
* **Region**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in exploring new regions.
	+ This field will help us understand if you are open to new opportunities and willing to explore new regions for your business.
