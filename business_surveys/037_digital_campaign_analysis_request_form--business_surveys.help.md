# Digital Campaign Analysis Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Campaign Analysis Request Form is a tool used to collect information about digital campaigns for analysis purposes. It helps in identifying the campaign's objectives, target audience, budget, and other relevant details. This form will help our team to better understand your campaign's performance and provide valuable insights to improve future campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing a unique name for your digital campaign.
2.  Choose the objective of your campaign from the given options.
3.  Select the target audience for your campaign.
4.  Provide the campaign duration, including start and end dates.
5.  Enter the total budget for your campaign.
6.  Choose the marketing channels used for your campaign.
7.  Select the current status of your campaign.
8.  Decide if you want to receive feedback on this campaign.
9.  Add any additional comments about your campaign.
10. Lastly, assign a user to receive the campaign report and analysis.

*   *Campaign Name* (`Campaign Details`, required/optional): This field is for providing a name for your digital campaign. Please provide a unique name for easy identification.

### Objective
*   *Objective* (`Objective`, required/optional): This field is for selecting the objective of your campaign. You can choose from the following options: Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3.

### Campaign Duration
*   *Campaign Duration* (`Campaign Duration`, required/optional): This field is for providing the start and end dates of your campaign.

### Budget

### Campaign Status
