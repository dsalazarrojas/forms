# donation_channel_trustworthiness_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the trustworthiness of a donation channel. It asks for an assessment of the channel's communication, helpfulness, timeliness, confidence in sharing, and submission processes. This information helps in evaluating and improving the effectiveness of the donation channel.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the multiple-choice questions about the donation channel, such as whether it is trustworthy, helpful, and timely.
2. Select the level of trustworthiness you have in the channel.
3. Assess the overall experience with the channel.
4. Evaluate the communication quality and effectiveness of the channel.
5. Choose whether the channel's processes are easy to use and submit information.
6. Provide additional comments about your experience with the channel, if any.
7. Enter your email address to ensure we can follow up on your feedback.
8. Optionally, enter your phone number for further contact.
9. Confirm your submission to complete the form.
