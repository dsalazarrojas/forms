# Transparency Communication Practices Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about the transparency communication practices within an organization. It helps us understand how often employees communicate with their organization, which communication channels they use, with whom they share information, and what data they share.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions based on your experiences.
2. For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose the options that best describe your answers.
3. For text and note questions, provide clear and concise answers.
4. Make sure to save your progress as you go, as you can only submit the form once.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **organization_size** (`organization_size`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about the size of your organization. Please choose one of the options: Small, Medium, or Large.
* **communication_practices** (`How often do you communicate with your organization?`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about how often you communicate with your organization. Please choose one or more of the options: Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely.
* **communication_channels** (`What communication channels do you use?`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about the communication channels you use. Please choose one or more of the options: Email, Phone, Video calls, Text messages.
* **information_sharing** (`With whom do you share information?`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about with whom you share information. Please choose one or more of the options: Colleagues, Management, Clients, Suppliers.
* **information_sharing_frequency** (`How often do you share information with others?`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about how often you share information with others. Please choose one or more of the options: Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely.
* **data_sharing** (`What data do you share with others?`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about what data you share with others. Please choose one or more of the options: Financial information, Performance information, Other data.
* **data_sharing_reason** (`Why do you share data?`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about why you share data. Please choose one or more of the options: To help others make decisions, For reporting or auditing, For research.
* **feedback** (`Have you received any feedback?`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about if you have received any feedback. Please choose one of the options: True or False.
* **feedback_sources** (`Who provided the feedback?`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about who provided the feedback. Please choose one or more of the options: Colleagues, Management, Clients, Suppliers.
* **communication_style** (`How would you describe your communication style?`, text, required: false): This question asks about your communication style. Please provide a clear and concise answer.
* **communication_channels_quality** (`What do you think about these communication channels?`, time, required: false): This question asks about your opinion on communication channels. Please enter the time you have spent thinking about this.
* **communication_channels_frequency** (`How often do you use these communication channels?`, number, required: false): This question asks about how often you use communication channels. Please enter a number.
* **information_sharing_quality** (`What do you think about information sharing?`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about your opinion on information sharing. Please choose one of the options: Good, Fair, Poor.
* **overall_satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, note, required: false): This question asks about your overall satisfaction. Please write a note about your feelings.
* **comments** (`Do you have any other comments?`, note, required: false): This question is for any additional comments you may have. Please write a clear and concise answer.
