# Parish Book Distribution Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track the distribution of parish books, providing information on the number of books sent, received, and remaining. It is used to monitor and manage the parish book distribution process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select 'Parish' as either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether a parish has received books.
2. Choose the 'Parish Book Distribution Status' as either 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the current status of book distribution to the parish.
3. Enter the number of books distributed ('Total Books Distributed'), sent ('Total Books Sent'), and remaining ('Remaining Books').
4. Provide contact information for the parish, including name ('Contact Name'), phone ('Contact Phone'), email ('Contact Email'), address ('Contact Address'), and location ('Church Location').
5. Select the 'Distribution Date' and 'Distribution Date Sent' to record the dates of book distribution.
6. Enter the 'Received Parish Name', 'Received Parish Address', 'Received Parish Email', and 'Received Parish Phone' and location.
7. Enter any feedback or comments on the distribution process ('Feedback').
8. Finally, select the 'Received Books Status' as either 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the status of the books received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parishes**: Select 'Yes' if the parish has received books, 'No' otherwise.
* **Parish Book Distribution Status**: Choose 'Active' if the distribution process is ongoing, 'Inactive' if it's not.
* **Received Books**: Select 'Yes' if the parish has received books, 'No' otherwise.
* **Feedback**: Enter any comments or feedback on the distribution process.
* **Distribution Date**: Enter the date when books were distributed to the parish.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person at the parish.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Address**: Enter the address of the contact person.
* **Church Location**: Enter the location of the church where the parish is situated.
* **Total Books Distributed**: Enter the total number of books distributed to the parish.
* **Total Books Sent**: Enter the total number of books sent to the parish.
* **Remaining Books**: Enter the number of books still remaining after distribution.
* **Distribution Status**: Choose 'Active' if the distribution is ongoing, 'Inactive' if it's not.
* **Received Parish Name**: Enter the name of the parish that received the books.
* **Received Parish Address**: Enter the address of the parish where the books were received.
* **Received Parish Email**: Enter the email of the contact person at the received parish.
* **Received Parish Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person at the received parish.
* **Received Parish Location**: Enter the location of the parish where the books were received.
* **Total Books Remaining**: Enter the number of books still remaining after distribution.
* **Received Books Count**: Enter the number of books received by the parish.
* **Given Feedback**: Enter any comments or feedback on the distribution process (optional).
* **Received Books Status**: Choose 'Active' if the books are active, 'Inactive' if they are not.
* **Distribution Date Sent**: Enter the date when books were sent to the parish.
* **Received Parish Name**: Enter the name of the received parish.
* **Received Parish Address**: Enter the address of the received parish.
