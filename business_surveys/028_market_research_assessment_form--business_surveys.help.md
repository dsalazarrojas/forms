# Market Research Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers about their experience and preferences regarding our company's products and services. This data will help us understand their needs and expectations, enabling us to make informed decisions to improve our offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. For fields that allow multiple selections, choose all the options that apply.
3. Enter your date and time of assessment.
4. Provide a brief note about your experience (optional).
5. Submit the form to complete the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Company** (`company`, text, Required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, Optional): Enter your job title or position at your company.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Required): Enter your business phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, Required): Select the date of your assessment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, Required): Select the time of your assessment.
* **Note** (`note`, note, Optional): Provide a brief note about your experience.
* **What do you like most about the company?** (`select_one`, select_one, Required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Quality of products and services
	+ Price
	+ Customer service
* **Which services do you prefer?** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, Required): Select all the options that apply:
	+ Yes (to services 1)
	+ Yes (to services 2)
	+ No (to services 3)
* **When?** (`date_time`, date, Optional): Select the date and time of your assessment.
* **Revenue** (`decimal`, number, Optional): Enter the revenue generated by our services.
* **Number** (`integer`, number, Optional): Enter a number related to your assessment.
