# Staple Food Crops Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staple Food Crops Survey is designed to collect information from growers about their staple food crops, including production details, farm location, and contact information. The survey is used to gather data for research and policy-making purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in your grower information, including your name and contact details.
3. Provide production details, such as farm location, crop type, and harvest season.
4. Enter farm size and land area information.
5. Select your region, country, state, district, and village.
6. Add any additional information about your farm, such as farm type and crop name.
7. Enter your contact details, including phone and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Growers Info** (`grower_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact details.
* **Farm Location** (`farm_location`, text, optional): Provide your farm's location, including city, state, or region.
* **Crop Info** (`crop_info`, text, optional): Enter details about the crop you are growing, such as type and name.
* **Production Details** (`production_details`, text, optional): Describe your production process and any relevant details.
* **Yield** (`yield`, number, optional): Enter the yield of your crop.
* **Harvest Season** (`harvest_season`, select multiple, optional): Indicate whether you harvest your crop during the regular season or not.
* **Crop Type** (`crop_type`, select one, optional): Choose the type of crop you are growing.
* **Farm Size** (`farm_size`, select one, optional): Select the size of your farm.
* **Other Farm Size** (`farm_size_other`, text, optional): If your farm size is not listed, describe it in this field.
* **Farm Land Area** (`farm_land_area`, number, optional): Enter the area of your farm land.
* **Other Farm Land Area** (`farm_land_area_other`, text, optional): If the area of your farm land is not listed, describe it in this field.
* **Region** (`region`, text, optional): Enter the region where your farm is located.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter the country where your farm is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where your farm is located.
* **District** (`district`, text, optional): Enter the district where your farm is located.
* **Village** (`village`, text, optional): Enter the village where your farm is located.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person to contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person to contact.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person to contact.
* **Farm Type** (`farm_type`, text, optional): Describe the type of farm.
* **Crop Name** (`crop_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the crop you are growing.
* **Crop Yield 1** (`crop_yield_1`, number, optional): Enter the yield of your crop 1.
* **Crop Yield 2** (`crop_yield_2`, number, optional): Enter the yield of your crop 2.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Once you have completed all the fields, click submit to submit your survey.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required.
