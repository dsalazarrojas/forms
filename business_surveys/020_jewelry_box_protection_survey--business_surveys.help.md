# Jewelry Box Protection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experiences with jewelry box protection and overall satisfaction with the product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the most relevant storage habits from the options provided.
3. Choose the protection features that best fit your needs.
4. Rate your customer satisfaction level on a scale.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Storage Habits** (storage_habits, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the method you use to store your jewelry box, such as in a display case, a drawer, or a box with a lock.
* **Protection Features** (protection_features, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose the features that you feel are essential for protecting your jewelry box, such as anti-tarnish coating, velvet lining, or a protective case.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (customer_satisfaction, note, optional):
	+ This field is for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about your overall satisfaction with the jewelry box.
* **Customer Satisfaction Rating** (customer_satisfaction_rating, number, optional):
	+ Rate your level of satisfaction with the product on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "not satisfied" and 5 being "very satisfied."
* **Customer Satisfaction Comments** (customer_satisfaction_comments, text, optional):
	+ Provide any comments or feedback you have about your experience with the jewelry box.
* **Product Team Feedback** (product_team_feedback, text, optional):
	+ Share any suggestions or comments you have about the product's functionality or features.
* **Product Team Feedback Comments** (product_team_feedback_comments, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional details or explanations about your feedback.
