# Product feedback survey template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is designed to gather feedback from product users about their experience and satisfaction with our product. Your input will help us improve the product and provide a better service.

## How to Complete This Form
1. Answer the first question about your overall satisfaction with a single selection from the options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
2. Select all relevant features from the list that influenced your opinion of the product. This can be more than one feature, depending on your response.
3. Answer the third question about recommending the product with a single selection: true or false.
4. Describe the primary problem that our product helps you solve in the text field.
5. Share any suggestions or ideas on what you would improve or change about the product in the text field.
6. Finally, add any additional comments or feedback you may have about the product in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied are you with our product overall?**
	+ (Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied)
	+ This question helps us understand your overall satisfaction with the product. Please choose the option that best describes your feelings.
* **Which features or aspects most influenced your opinion of the product? (Select all that apply)**
	+ Design
	+ Price
	+ Performance
	+ Core Features
	+ Ease of Use
	+ Customer Support
* **Would you recommend this product to others?**
	+ (true or false)
	+ This question is about whether you would recommend our product to friends, family, or colleagues based on your experience.
* **What is the primary problem our product helps you solve?**
	+ (text field for your answer)
	+ This question is about how our product addresses a specific need or issue you face.
* **What would you improve or change about the product?**
	+ (text field for your answer)
	+ This is your chance to suggest improvements or changes you think we should make to the product.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback for us?**
	+ (text field for your answer)
	+ This is an open-ended question where you can share any other thoughts or ideas you have about the product.
