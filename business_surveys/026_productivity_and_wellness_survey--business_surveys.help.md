# Productivity And Wellness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Productivity And Wellness Survey is designed to gather information about your productivity and wellness at work. This data will help your organization understand areas where employees can improve their work experience and overall well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Work Hours**: Please enter the number of hours you work per week.
2. **Stress Level**: On a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very low" and 5 is "very high", please rate your current stress level.
3. **Sleep Quality**: Rate your sleep quality from 1-5, where 1 is "poor" and 5 is "excellent".
4. **Exercise Frequency**: How often do you engage in exercise per week, on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "less than once a week" and 5 is "daily or almost daily".
5. **Self-Care Activities**: Select one of the self-care activities that you enjoy, such as "Self-Care Activity 1", "Self-Care Activity 2", "Self-Care Activity 3", or "Self-Care Activity 4".
6. **Productivity Level**: On a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very low" and 5 is "very high", please rate your current productivity level.
7. **Work Lifespan**: How many hours you work per day, on a scale of 1-10.
8. **Work Life Quality**: Rate your current work-life quality from 1-5, where 1 is "very poor" and 5 is "excellent".
9. **Work Life Quantity**: How many hours of work you feel you can perform before feeling overworked, on a scale of 1-10.
10. **Work Life Quality 2**: This field is similar to "Work Life Quality", but with a different scale.
11. **Employee Feedback**: Please provide any additional feedback you have about your work experience or any suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Work Hours**: Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **Stress Level**: Rate your current stress level on a scale of 1-5.
* **Sleep Quality**: Rate your sleep quality from 1-5.
* **Exercise Frequency**: Rate how often you exercise per week on a scale of 1-5.
* **Self-Care Activities**: Choose one self-care activity that you enjoy.
* **Productivity Level**: Rate your current productivity level on a scale of 1-5.
* **Work Lifespan**: Enter the number of hours you work per day.
* **Work Lifequality**: Rate your current work-life quality on a scale of 1-5.
* **Work Lifequantity**: Enter the number of hours of work you feel you can perform before feeling overworked.
* **Work Lifequality2**: This field is similar to "Work Lifequality", with a different scale, and is meant to rate the quality of your work-life balance.
* **Employee Feedback**: Please provide any feedback or suggestions you have for your work experience.
