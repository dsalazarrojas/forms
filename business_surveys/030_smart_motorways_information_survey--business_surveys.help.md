# Smart Motorways Information Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and information from users about their experience with smart motorways. This data will help improve the design and functionality of the motorways to provide a more efficient and convenient driving experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2.  Be honest and accurate in your responses.
3.  Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Road Network** (`id: 9`, `select_one`, `required`: true): This field asks you to select the type of road network you use. Select one of the following options: Smart Motorways, Motorways, Road, or None.
*   **Driver Type** (`id: 10`, `select_multiple`, `required`: true): Please select all the types of vehicles you drive.
*   **Contact Method** (`id: 11`, `select_multiple`, `required`: true): Choose the method you used to contact us. Select all that apply: Phone, Email, or None.
*   **Awareness Level** (`id: 1`, `number`, `required`: false): Please rate your awareness level of smart motorways on a scale.
*   **Frequency of Use** (`id: 2`, `number`, `required`: false): Rate your frequency of use of smart motorways.
*   **Overall Satisfaction** (`id: 3`, `number`, `required`: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with smart motorways.
*   **Improvement Ideas** (`id: 4`, `text`, `required`: false): Share any ideas you have for improving the smart motorway system.
*   **Feedback Comments** (`id: 5`, `text`, `required`: false): Provide any additional feedback you may have about your experience with smart motorways.
*   **Category Awareness** (`id: 6`, `text`, `required`: false): Share your level of awareness about smart motorway categories.
*   **Category Experience** (`id: 7`, `text`, `required`: false): Share your experience with smart motorway categories.
*   **Category Improvement** (`id: 8`, `text`, `required`: false): Share your ideas for improving smart motorway categories.
*   **Category Experience Details** (`id: 20`, `text`, `required`: false): Share more details about your experience with smart motorway categories.
*   **Category Improvement Details** (`id: 21`, `text`, `required`: false): Share more details about your ideas for improving smart motorway categories.
*   **Road Network Details** (`id: 22`, `text`, `required`: false): Share more details about the road network you use.
*   **Overall Satisfaction Details** (`id: 23`, `text`, `required`: false): Share more details about your overall satisfaction with smart motorways.
*   **Submitted By Details** (`id: 24`, `text`, `required`: false): If you'd like, please share more details about yourself.
*   **Survey Date** (`id: 17`, `date`, `required`: false): Not required but if you'd like to, please share the date you completed this survey.
