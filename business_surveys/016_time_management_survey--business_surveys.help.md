# Time Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about how employees manage their time. It covers various aspects of time management, including work hours, breaks, focus, time wasters, time management goals, and tools used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your employee name in the "employee name" field.
2. Provide your employee email address in the "employee email" field.
3. Answer questions about your working hours per week, breaks per day, and daily breaks.
4. Share your thoughts on your daily focus, time wasters, and time management goals.
5. List any time management tools you use (e.g., online survey, offline survey).
6. If you've been assigned a tool by your manager, select it from the options or enter "Other" and provide more details.
7. If you've used other tools for time management, specify them in the "other tools used" field.
8. Take a moment to note any additional information or comments about your time management practices.
9. Finally, indicate whether this survey is of the "free" or "paid" type. If it's not listed, enter "Other" and describe.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee name**: Enter your full name as an employee.
* **employee email**: Enter your company email address.
* **working hours per week**: Estimate the number of hours you work per week.
* **breaks**: Enter the number of breaks you take per day.
* **daily breaks**: Enter the number of daily breaks you take.
* **focus**: Describe what you focus on during the workday.
* **time wasters**: List any activities that distract you from your work.
* **time management goals**: Share your goals for managing your time effectively.
* **time management tools**: Select the tools you use for time management (e.g., online survey, offline survey).
* **assigned tool**: If you've been assigned a tool by your manager, select it from the options or enter "Other" and provide more details.
* **other tools used**: If you've used other tools for time management, specify them here.
* **notes**: Take a moment to note any additional information or comments about your time management practices.
* **survey type**: Indicate whether this survey is of the "free" or "paid" type. If not listed, enter "Other" and describe.
* **date**: This field is not relevant to the survey's purpose; you can leave it blank.
