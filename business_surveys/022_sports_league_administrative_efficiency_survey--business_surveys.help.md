# Sports League Administrative Efficiency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports League Administrative Efficiency Survey is designed to collect feedback on the current administrative processes and resources used by a sports league. This survey aims to identify areas of improvement and provide data-driven insights for optimizing the league's administration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Introduction**: This section provides a brief overview of the survey and its purpose. Read through it to understand the context and objectives of the survey.
2. Select all applicable **administrative tasks** (Task 1, Task 2, Task 3) that are currently being performed by the league's administrative staff.
3. Choose the most effective **communication channel** (Channel 1, Channel 2, Channel 3) used for internal and external communication.
4. Provide a numerical value for **administrative efficiency**, representing the percentage of tasks completed efficiently.
5. Enter a numerical value for **communication efficiency**, representing the percentage of communication effectively completed.
6. Describe the **resource utilization** in a text field.
7. Select all applicable **administrative resources** (Resource 1, Resource 2, Resource 3) used for administration.
8. Select all applicable **communication resources** (Resource 1, Resource 2, Resource 3) used for communication.
9. Choose the most effective **administrative staff** (Staff 1, Staff 2, Staff 3) currently leading the league's administration.
10. Provide any additional **feedback** regarding the survey or the sports league's administration.
