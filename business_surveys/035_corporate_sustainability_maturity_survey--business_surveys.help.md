# Corporate Sustainability Maturity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the Corporate Sustainability Maturity Survey, which is designed to assess your company's progress in implementing sustainable practices. The survey is used to gather insights on your organization's sustainability strategy, areas of interest, and challenges faced.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by reading this guide to understand the purpose and structure of the survey.
2.  Begin by answering the questions on Page 1: "Introduction". This page is not required, and you can skip it if you prefer.
3.  On Page 2, "Strategy", you will be asked to describe your company's sustainability strategy. Please provide a brief description.
4.  On Page 3, "Domain Maturity Levels", you will be presented with a selection of options. Choose the domains that your company is most interested in.
5.  On Page 4, "Challenges", you will be asked to describe the challenges you are currently facing in your sustainability journey.
6.  On Page 5, "Submission", you will choose how you would like to submit your survey. You can either upload a file, send an email, or save your answers.
7.  On Page 6, "Email", you will be asked to provide your email address.
8.  On Page 7, "Phone", you can optionally provide your phone number.
9.  On Page 8, "Submission Terms and Conditions", please carefully read and accept the terms and conditions for submission.
10. Review your answers to ensure you are comfortable with your submissions before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1, Not Required): This page is a note field where you can provide any background information about your company and the purpose of your sustainability survey.
* **What is your sustainability strategy?** (Page 2, Required): Please describe your company's current sustainability strategy, focusing on your main goals and objectives.
* **Which sustainability domain(s) are you most interested in?** (Page 3, Optional): Select the areas of sustainability that your company is currently focused on, such as Energy and Climate Change, Biodiversity, Human Rights and Labour, or Supply Chain.
* **What are you currently facing in your sustainability journey?** (Page 4, Required): Describe the challenges you are encountering during your sustainability development process.
* **Submission** (Page 5, Optional): Choose how you would like to submit your survey: Upload a file, send an email, or save your answers.
* **Email** (Page 6, Required): Provide your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Page 7, Optional): You can optionally provide your phone number.
* **Submission Terms and Conditions** (Page 8, Required): Carefully review and accept the terms and conditions for submission to ensure a smooth completion of the survey.
