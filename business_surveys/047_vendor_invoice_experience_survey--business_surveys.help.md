# Vendor Invoice Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for vendors to provide feedback on their invoice experience with your organization. The form is used to gather information on how often and in what method payments are made, and any comments or feedback vendors may have.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all necessary information ready, such as your invoice ID, vendor name, and payment details.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Use the correct payment terms and methods to ensure correct data collection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Invoice ID (1)** (Required): Enter the ID number of the invoice for which you are providing feedback.
* **Vendor Name (2)** (Required): Enter the name of the vendor or supplier for which you are providing feedback.
* **Invoice Date (3)** (Required): Enter the date the invoice was created or paid.
* **Payment Terms (4)** (Required): Select how often you make payments (e.g., Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly).
* **Payment Method (5)** (Required): Select how you make payments (e.g., Bank Transfer, Check, Other).
* **Contact Method (6)** (Required): Select how you prefer to be contacted (e.g., Phone, Email, In Person).
* **Comments (7)** (Optional): Add any additional comments or feedback you have about the payment process.
* **Contact Email (8)** (Optional): Enter your preferred email address for communication.
* **Contact Phone (9)** (Optional): Enter your preferred phone number for communication.
* **Payment Terms Frequency (10)** (Required): Select how often you make payments (e.g., Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly).
* **Payment Amount (11)** (Required): Enter the amount paid for the invoice.
* **Payment Frequency (12)** (Required): Select how often you pay (e.g., Once, Reoccurring, On Demand).
* **Payment Type (13)** (Required): Select how you make payments (e.g., Bank Transfer, Check, Online Transfer).
* **Payment Status (14)** (Required): Select the status of the payment (e.g., Paid, Unpaid, Partially Paid).
* **Payment Notes (15)** (Optional): Add any additional comments about payments.
* **Vendor Comments (16)** (Optional): Add any comments or feedback about the vendor.
* **Vendor Feedback (17)** (Optional): Add any feedback or comments about the vendor.
* **Contact Title (18)** (Optional): Enter your title or position.
* **Contact First Name (19)** (Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Contact Last Name (20)** (Optional): Enter your last name.
* **Report (21)** (Optional): Add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Payment Method Other (22)** (Optional): If other than bank transfer, check, or online transfer, specify the method used.
* **Payment Terms Other (23)** (Optional): If other than weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, annually, or on demand, specify the frequency.
* **Contact Method Other (24)** (Optional): If other than phone, email, or in person, specify the method used.
* **Comments Other (25)** (Optional): Add any additional comments or feedback.
