# Livestock Welfare Advocacy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Livestock Welfare Advocacy Survey is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations about their experiences and opinions on livestock welfare advocacy efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Form Title** in the text box provided. This will help identify the specific survey and ensure it is correctly associated with your responses.
2. Select the **Category** that best describes your response (e.g., Business Surveys, Other, Unknown).
3. Provide a brief **Description** of your experience or opinion on livestock welfare advocacy efforts (optional).
4. Enter a description of the **Form Description** (optional).
5. Enter a unique **Form ID** (required).
6. Select the **Assigned Tool** that was used to complete the form (e.g., web, mobile, paper).
7. Enter your **Email** address (required).
8. Enter your **Phone** number (required).
9. If you would like, provide the name of the person who **Submitted the form** (optional).
10. If you like, enter the date and time you **Submitted the form** (optional).
