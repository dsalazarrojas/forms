# Strategic Advisory Outcome Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strategic Advisory Outcome Survey is a form designed to collect feedback and satisfaction from clients after an advisory engagement with a consultant. This survey aims to gauge the effectiveness of the consultation process and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each section honestly and thoroughly.
2. For select-one questions, choose one of the provided options.
3. For select_multiple questions, choose all applicable options.
4. For note questions, provide a brief description.
5. For text fields, enter your contact information and any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This section is an introductory note that explains the purpose of the survey.
* **Satisfaction**: How satisfied are you with our services? Choose one of the options: Satisfied, Dissatisfied, Very Satisfied, Very Dissatisfied.
* **Outcomes**: What were the key outcomes of our advisory engagement? Choose all applicable options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **Impact**: What impact did our advisory engagement have on your organization? Choose one of the options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **Future Needs**: A note section for you to describe your future needs and expectations.
* **Consultant Feedback (Knowledge and Expertise)**: How would you rate your consultant's knowledge and expertise? Enter a number from 1 to 5.
* **Team Feedback (Responsiveness and Communication)**: How would you rate your consultant's team's responsiveness and communication? Enter a number from 1 to 5.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: How would you rate your overall satisfaction with our advisory engagement? Choose one of the options: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied.
* **Future Improvement**: A note section for you to describe any areas for future improvement.
* **Next Steps**: What are your next steps or expectations for future engagements? Choose all applicable options: 'Yes', 'No'.
* **Follow Up**: Do you prefer to be contacted for follow-up purposes? Choose one of the options: 'Yes', 'No'.
* **Comments**: A note section for you to provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Additional Comments**: A note section for any further comments or feedback.
* **Contact**: How do you prefer to be contacted? Choose all applicable options: 'Yes', 'No'.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, if you prefer to be contacted via email.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, if you prefer to be contacted via phone.
* **Other**: Enter any other contact information you'd like to provide.
* **Additional Contact Information**: Enter any additional contact information you'd like to provide.
* **Consultant Name**: Enter the name of your consultant.
* **Consultant Role**: Enter the role of your consultant.
