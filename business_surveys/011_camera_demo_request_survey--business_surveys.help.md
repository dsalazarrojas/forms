# Camera Demo Request Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a camera demo for your organization or group. Please fill out the form to help us better understand your demo requirements and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Requester Name** in the field below. This is the name of the person submitting the request.
2. Enter your **Organization** name in the field below. This is the company or group hosting the demo.
3. Enter a valid **Contact Email** in the field below. This will be used to confirm demo scheduling.
4. Select a **Preferred Demo Date** in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This can be a single date or a date range.
5. If you have a **Preferred Time** for the demo, select it from the available options.
6. If you have specific **Camera Models of Interest**, enter them in the field below.
7. Enter the expected number of **Attendees** for the demo, including yourself.
8. Enter the **Demo Location** where the demo will take place, including any necessary address or virtual platform details.
9. If you have any **Additional Requirements**, enter them in the field below.
9. Select your preferred **Follow Up Method** by choosing one of the options: Email, Phone, or No Preference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Name**: 
  * Label: Requester Name
  * Type: text
  * Explanation: Enter the name of the person submitting the request.
* **Organization**: 
  * Label: Organization
  * Type: text
  * Explanation: Enter the company or group hosting the demo.
* **Contact Email**:
  * Label: Contact Email
  * Type: email
  * Explanation: Enter a valid email to confirm demo scheduling.
* **Preferred Demo Date**:
  * Label: Preferred Demo Date
  * Type: date
  * Explanation: Select a date or date range for the demo.
* **Preferred Time**:
  * Label: Preferred Time
  * Type: time
  * Explanation: Select a time of day for the demo.
* **Camera Models of Interest**:
  * Label: Camera Models of Interest
  * Type: text
  * Explanation: If you have specific camera models you would like to see, enter them here.
* **Expected Number of Attendees**:
  * Label: Expected Number of Attendees
  * Type: number
  * Explanation: Enter the number of people expected to attend the demo.
* **Demo Location**:
  * Label: Demo Location
  * Type: text
  * Explanation: Enter the address or virtual platform details where the demo will take place.
* **Additional Requirements**:
  * Label: Additional Requirements
  * Type: text
  * Explanation: If you have any technical requirements or special requests, enter them here.
* **Follow Up Preference**:
  * Label: Follow Up Preference
  * Type: select_one
  * Explanation: Select how you would like to be followed up with for demo scheduling.
