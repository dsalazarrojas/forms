# Retail Home Improvement Preferences Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather your opinions and preferences as a customer, helping the store to better understand your needs and improve its products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form with the requested information.
2. Make sure to answer each question as accurately as possible.
3. If you are unsure about a question, choose the option that best matches your answer.
4. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Store ID** (`store_id`, number, optional): If you know your store ID, enter it here. Otherwise, you can leave it blank.
* **Purchase Frequency** (`purchase_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you frequently purchase home improvement products, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Satisfaction with Products** (`satisfaction_with_products`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply to describe your satisfaction with our products. Select 'Satisfied' if you are satisfied, 'Dissatisfied' if you are dissatisfied, or both if you are neutral.
* **Product Search** (`product_search`, text, optional): Describe how you usually search for home improvement products.
* **Purchase Channel** (`purchase_channel`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have ever purchased home improvement products through our store, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply to rate our customer service. Select 'Yes' if you are satisfied, 'No' if you are not satisfied, or both if you are neutral.
* **Product Quality** (`product_quality`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply to rate the quality of our products. Select 'Good' if you are satisfied, 'Poor' if you are not satisfied, or both if you are neutral.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, optional): If you are able to rate the price of our products, enter the price you would be willing to pay.
* **Store Experience** (`store_experience`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have had a positive experience in our store, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Store Atmosphere** (`store_atmosphere`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if our store atmosphere meets your expectations, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Employee Knowledge** (`employee_knowledge`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply to rate our employee's knowledge. Select 'Yes' if you are satisfied, 'No' if you are not satisfied, or both if you are neutral.

Remember to submit the form once you have filled out all the fields. Your feedback and preferences will help the store to better understand your needs and improve its products and services.
