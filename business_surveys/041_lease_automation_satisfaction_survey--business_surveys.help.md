# Lease Automation Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lease Automation Satisfaction Survey is designed to gather your opinions and feedback on the lease automation system. Your input will help us to identify what works well and what needs improvement, allowing us to make necessary changes to enhance your overall experience with our system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have a good understanding of each question.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Be as detailed as possible when responding to open-ended questions.
4. Submit your survey to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Feedback** (`user_feedback`, text, required: false): This is your opportunity to provide any comments or feedback about your experience with the lease automation system.
* **Lease Automation** (`lease_automation`, select_one, required: false): Please select how satisfied you are with the lease automation system:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
* **Issue Identification** (`issue_identification`, note, required: false): If you have encountered any issues with the lease automation system, describe them here.
* **Enhancement Priority** (`enhancement_priority`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select which of the following enhancements you would like to see in the system:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of any issues you have encountered with the lease automation system.
* **Suggested Solution** (`suggested_solution`, text, required: false): If you have a solution to any issues you've encountered, suggest it here.
* **System Usability** (`system_usability`, select_one, required: false): Please select how easy or difficult you found the system to use:
	+ Very Easy
	+ Somewhat Easy
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Difficult
	+ Very Difficult
* **User Recommendation** (`user_recommendation`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select your recommendations for improvements to the system:
	+ Improve
	+ Keep
	+ Discontinue
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Is there anything else you would like to share about your experience with the lease automation system?
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as you would like it to appear in the results of this survey.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): If you are representing a company, please enter your company name as you would like it to appear in the results of this survey.
