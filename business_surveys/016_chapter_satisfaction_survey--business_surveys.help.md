# Chapter Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chapter Satisfaction Survey is designed to collect your feedback and suggestions to improve the chapter's services and activities. Your input will help the chapter understand your needs and preferences, allowing us to make informed decisions to benefit our members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please take a few minutes to read each question carefully and select the answer that best reflects your thoughts and feelings.
2. For questions with a select_one type, choose the option that best describes your experience or sentiment.
3. For questions with a number type, rate your experience from 1 (Very Dissatisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied).
4. For questions with a text type, provide a brief and specific answer to help us understand your thoughts.
5. Once you have completed all the questions, review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Years of Membership** (`chapter_membership_years`, select_one, required): Select the number of years you have been a member of this chapter. This will help us understand your long-term engagement with the chapter.
* **Level of Participation** (`member_participation_level`, select_one, required): Rate your level of participation in chapter activities. Choose the option that best describes your level of involvement.
* **Overall Chapter Experience Satisfaction** (`chapter_experience_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the chapter's experience from 1 (Very Dissatisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied).
* **Value of Membership vs. Dues** (`value_of_membership_rating`, number, required): Rate whether you feel the value of membership justifies the cost of dues.
* **Chapter Leadership Effectiveness** (`chapter_leadership_effectiveness`, number, required): Rate how well the chapter is being led.
* **Quality and Relevance of Chapter Events** (`event_quality_and_relevance`, number, required): Rate the quality and relevance of chapter events.
* **Communication from Leadership** (`communication_from_leadership`, select_one, required): Rate the clarity and timeliness of communication from chapter leadership.
* **Feeling of Welcome and Inclusion** (`welcome_inclusion_for_members`, select_one, required): Rate how welcome and included you feel in the chapter community.
* **Opportunity for Leadership Roles** (`opportunity_for_leadership_roles`, select_one, required): Rate whether there are clear pathways to serve in leadership roles.
* **Primary Benefit of Membership** (`primary_benefit_of_membership`, select_one, required): Select the most important factor that drives your membership.
* **Suggestions for New Events or Programs** (`suggestions_for_new_events`, text, required): Provide suggestions for new events or programs you would like to see the chapter offer.
* **Areas Needing Most Improvement** (`areas_needing_most_improvement`, text, required): Identify specific areas where the chapter can improve.
* **Likelihood of Membership Renewal** (`likelihood_of_renewal_membership`, select_one, required): Rate your likelihood of renewing your membership.
* **Likelihood of Recommending the Chapter** (`recommendation_to_others_rating`, number, required): Rate your likelihood of recommending the chapter to a friend or colleague.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`additional_comments_satisfaction`, text, optional): Provide any other thoughts or suggestions you may have.
* **Date of Completion** (`date_of_survey_completion`, date, required): Enter the current date.
* **Confirmation of Feedback** (`accuracy_confirmation_member`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are providing honest and fair feedback.
* **Welcome and Final Thoughts** (`additional_comments_satisfaction`, text, optional): Provide final thoughts or comments, if any.
