# Project Cargo Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Project Cargo Assessment Survey is used to gather information about cargo projects. Project managers and team members can use this survey to provide details about cargo projects, including project details, cargo details, shipment details, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the project details in the corresponding field.
2. Provide cargo details such as weight, length, width, height, and diameter.
3. Indicate whether the cargo is yes or no for cargo type and subtype.
4. Select the shipment date and time.
5. Choose the shipment status as active or inactive.
6. Select the project status as active or inactive.
7. Add any additional notes.
8. Enter contact information, including email and name.
