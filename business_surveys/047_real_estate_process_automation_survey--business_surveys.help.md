# Real Estate Process Automation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on user experiences and opinions about the real estate process automation in your organization. The goal is to understand how users perceive the effectiveness and efficiency of the current process, identify potential challenges, and gauge overall satisfaction with the current state of the real estate process automation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions based on your experiences and opinions.
2. Use the next and back buttons to navigate through the 4 pages of the form.
3. You can submit the form once you have completed all the pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Real Estate Process Automation Survey** (`title`, `text`, **Required: No**)
This is the title of the form, and it will appear in the tab or title bar of your browser. You can use this field to understand the context of the form and its purpose.

### **Real Estate Survey Tools** (`real_estate_survey_tools`, `select_multiple`, **Required: No**)
Select one or more options that apply to you. These options are:
- Option 1
- Option 2
- Option 3

### **Real Estate Survey Challenges** (`challenges`, `text`, **Required: No**)
Describe the challenges you face when using the current real estate process automation. Please provide specific examples or details about the issues you encounter.

### **User Satisfaction** (`user_satisfaction`, `select_one`, **Required: No**)
Select one of the following options to rate your overall satisfaction with the current state of the real estate process automation:
- Very satisfied
- Somewhat satisfied
- Not satisfied

## Tips
- Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
