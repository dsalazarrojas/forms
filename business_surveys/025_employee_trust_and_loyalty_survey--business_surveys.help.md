# Employee Trust And Loyalty Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect opinions and feelings of employees about their workplace experiences, specifically regarding trust and loyalty.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question to the best of your abilities, providing honest and accurate information about your experiences.
2. Select the department where you work from the list provided.
3. Answer the questions regarding your trust in different aspects of the company, such as job satisfaction, manager relationship, and work-life balance.
4. Answer the questions regarding your loyalty to the company, such as years with the company and employee perks.
5. Select your level of engagement and satisfaction with different aspects of your work and job, such as manager relationship, team trust, and communication.
6. Answer the questions regarding your feelings about your work environment, such as company culture and employee safety.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department**: 
  * (`Department`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the department where you work. This will help us understand the specific experiences and perceptions of different teams within the company.
* **Employee Trust**: 
  * (`Employee Trust`, number, required/optional): This question is about how much you trust your colleagues and the company in general. Please answer this question by selecting a number that best represents your sentiment.
* **Employee Loyalty**: 
  * (`Employee Loyalty`, text, required/optional): This question is about how committed you are to the company. Please type in your honest answer to this question.
* **Engagement**: 
  * (`Engagement`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question is about your level of engagement with your work and the company. Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **Years with the Company**: 
  * (`Years with the Company`, number, required/optional): This question is about how long you have been with the company. Please enter the number of years you have been with the company.
* **Job Satisfaction**: 
  * (`Job Satisfaction`, text, required/optional): This question is about your feelings about your job. Please type in your honest answer to this question.
* **Job Security**: 
  * (`Job Security`, select_one, required/optional): This question is about how secure you feel about your job. Please select one of the options provided.
* **Employee Perks**: 
  * (`Employee Perks`, text, required/optional): This question is about any special benefits or incentives you enjoy as an employee. Please type in your honest answer to this question.
* **Manager Relationship**: 
  * (`Manager Relationship`, select_multiple, required/optional): This question is about your feelings about your manager. Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **Team Trust**: 
  * (`Team Trust`, text, required/optional): This question is about how much you trust your team members. Please type in your honest answer to this question.
* **Communication**: 
  * (`Communication`, text, required/optional): This question is about how well you communicate with your colleagues and management. Please type in your honest answer to this question.
* **Work Life Balance**: 
  * (`Work Life Balance`, text, required/optional): This question is about how balanced you feel your work and personal life are. Please type in your honest answer to this question.
* **Company Culture**: 
  * (`Company Culture`, text, required/optional): This question is about your feelings about the company culture. Please type in your honest answer to this question.
* **Employee Safety**: 
  * (`Employee Safety`, text, required/optional): This question is about how safe you feel in the workplace. Please type in your honest answer to this question.
