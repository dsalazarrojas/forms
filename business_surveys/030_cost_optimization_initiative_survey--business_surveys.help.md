# Cost Optimization Initiative Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and ideas on cost-saving initiatives within the organization. The data collected will be used to inform decision-making and identify areas for cost-saving opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into 8 pages, each with its own unique characteristics and requirements. Please review each page carefully and provide the requested information.
2. Start by reading each page carefully and ensure you understand the question or prompt before proceeding.
3. Answer each question or provide an idea in the designated field.
4. Use the available options or input fields to provide the necessary information.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, note, required: false): This page provides an overview of the form and its purpose.
* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, text, required: false): This field is for providing general feedback on cost-saving initiatives.
	+ Please type in your thoughts on cost-saving initiatives.
* **Impact Score** (`impact_score`, number, required: false): This field is for rating the impact of cost-saving initiatives.
	+ Use the available options to rate the impact.
* **New Ideas** (`new_ideas`, text, required: false): This field is for providing new ideas for cost-saving initiatives.
	+ Please provide suggestions for cost-saving initiatives.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required: false): Select the department impacted by the cost-saving initiative.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): This field is for submitting the form.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the category for the cost-saving initiative.
* **Form Footer** (`form_footer`, note, required: false): This page provides an overview of the form's submission and next steps.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any question or prompt, please ask your supervisor or manager for clarification.
* The data collected will be used to inform decision-making and identify areas for cost-saving opportunities.
