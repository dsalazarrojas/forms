# Superannuation Contribution Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about superannuation contributions made by employees and their employers. It is used to track and record superannuation contributions for internal use within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Introduction

1. This is the introduction section, where you can read about the purpose and importance of the superannuation contribution survey.

### Step 2: Employee and Employer Details

* **Employee ID**: Please enter your employee ID number. This is a unique identifier assigned to you by the organization.
* **Employer ID**: Please enter your employer's ID number. This is a unique identifier assigned to your employer by the organization.
* **Employee Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employer Name**: Please enter your employer's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Department**: Please enter your department name.
* **Job Title**: Please enter your job title.

### Step 3: Contribution Details

* **Contribution Frequency**: Please select the frequency at which you make superannuation contributions, such as monthly, quarterly, or bi-annually.
* **Contribution Amount**: Please enter the amount of superannuation contributions made by you and your employer.
* **Employer Contributions**: Please enter the amount of superannuation contributions made by your employer.
* **Employee Contributions**: Please enter the amount of superannuation contributions made by you.
* **Total Contribution**: Please enter the total amount of superannuation contributions made by you and your employer.

### Step 4: Additional Information

* **Comments**: Please provide any additional comments or notes about your superannuation contributions.
* **Date Submitted**: Please enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Notes**: Please provide any additional information or notes about your superannuation contributions.

### Step 5: Signature and Contact Information

* **Email**: Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Please enter your phone number.
* **Signature**: Please select one of the options to indicate that you have signed the form.
* **Employee Name**: Please re-enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Manager Name**: Please enter your manager's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Manager Email**: Please enter your manager's email address.
* **Location**: Please enter the location where you work.

### Step 6: Submit Form

Once you have filled in all the required fields, please click the submit button to complete and submit the form.
