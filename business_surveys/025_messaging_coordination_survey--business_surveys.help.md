# messaging_coordination_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "messaging_coordination_survey" form is designed to collect information for internal business messaging and coordination purposes. It helps to understand the effectiveness of current messaging and coordination tools, identify areas for improvement, and provide suggestions for better communication and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start on the "Introduction" page, where you can provide a brief overview of your team's current messaging and coordination practices.
2. Answer all required questions, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. For each question, provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
4. Select the correct option for the "messaging_tool" question.
5. Enter your email address and phone number in the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (page 1): A brief overview of your team's current messaging and coordination practices.
* **Tools Rating** (page 2): Rate the effectiveness of your current messaging and coordination tools. Please select a number from 1 to 10.
* **Challenges** (page 3): List any challenges you are currently facing with messaging and coordination.
* **Improvements** (page 4): Suggest any improvements you would like to see in your messaging and coordination practices.
* **Communication** (page 5): Describe your current communication methods and frequency.
* **Collaboration** (page 6): Describe your current collaboration methods and frequency.
* **Suggestions** (page 7): Provide any suggestions for improving messaging and coordination.
* **Team Size** (page 8): Estimate the size of your team.
* **Team Location** (page 9): Describe your team's location.
* **Messaging Tool** (page 10): Select the messaging tool you currently use.
* **Email** (page 11): Enter your email address. (Required)
* **Phone** (page 12): Enter your phone number. (Required)
* **Notes** (page 13): Provide any additional comments or notes about your messaging and coordination practices.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required questions to ensure accurate results.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
* Select the correct option for the "messaging_tool" question.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* Use this form for internal business communication and coordination purposes only.
