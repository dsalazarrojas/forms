# leadership_alignment_consulting_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about team leadership styles and preferences for consulting services. It helps us gain insight into a team's alignment and leadership styles, which will aid in providing more effective support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Home" to begin the form.
2. Answer the "Team Leadership" question in the text field.
3. Choose one of the provided options for the "Leadership Style" field.
4. Optionally answer the "Team Alignment" question in the text field.
5. Optionally select multiple options for the "Leadership Leadership" question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Leadership** (text, required): This question is meant to understand a team's current leadership and how they function in the context of the project. Provide a brief explanation of what leadership style your team currently has.
* **Leadership Style** (select one, required): Choose one of the three options that most closely aligns with your team's leadership style.
* **Team Alignment** (text, optional): If you wish, you can provide more information about how your team aligns, such as any goals or objectives you are working towards.
* **Leadership Leadership** (select multiple, optional): If you wish, you can select multiple leadership styles that you believe your team has or would benefit from. This can give us a better understanding of your team's leadership strengths and areas for improvement.

## Tips
* Please answer the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* You may not need to complete all fields if you don't have information to provide.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the support team.
