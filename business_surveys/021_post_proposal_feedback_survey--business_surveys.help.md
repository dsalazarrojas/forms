# Post Proposal Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Post Proposal Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from reviewers on a proposal submission. This form helps to gather information on the reviewer's overall rating, comments, and sentiment of the proposal, as well as any actionable suggestions for improvement. The survey is intended to be completed by individuals who have reviewed a proposal and provides valuable insights to the proposal owner to help refine and enhance their proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the rating that best describes your overall assessment of the proposal (if you've reviewed multiple proposals, you can answer for each one).
2. Provide a brief description of your thoughts on the proposal, highlighting your likes and dislikes.
3. Choose the sentiment that best describes your feedback (Positive, Negative, or Neutral).
4. Offer any actionable suggestions for improvement, if applicable.
5. Select a date for when you reviewed the proposal.
6. Enter your name as the reviewer.
7. Enter your email address (this will help us contact you with any follow-up questions or to provide updates on proposal progress).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Post Proposal Feedback Survey** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title summarizing the purpose of this survey.
- **Overall Rating** (`reviewer_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate your overall impression of the proposal, using a numeric scale.
- **Your Thoughts** (`reviewer_comments`, text, required/optional): Share your thoughts and feedback on the proposal, including any likes or dislikes.
- **Sentiment** (`reviewer_sentiment`, select_one, required/optional): Select the sentiment that best describes your feedback (Positive, Negative, or Neutral).
- **Actionable Suggestions** (`actionable_suggestions`, text, required/optional): Provide any actionable suggestions for improvement, if applicable.
- **Date of Review** (`review_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when you reviewed the proposal.
- **Name of Reviewer** (`reviewer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the reviewer.
- **Email of Reviewer** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for follow-up contact.
