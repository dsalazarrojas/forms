# Saddle Bench Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with the saddle bench. Your input will help us improve the design and functionality of the saddle bench.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields. You can leave the optional fields blank if you do not have any feedback to provide. When you are finished, click the submit button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title that describes your experience with the saddle bench.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name so we can contact you if we need more information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information.
* **How many people rode on the bench?** (`prophets`, number, optional): Please enter the number of people that rode on the bench during your experience.
* **Overall Comfort** (`comfort_prophets`, number, optional): Please rate your overall comfort level on the bench, where 1 is "Not at all comfortable" and 5 is "Very Comfortable".
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`satisfaction_prophets`, number, optional): Please rate your overall satisfaction level on the bench, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, text, optional): If you have any ideas on how we can improve the design and functionality of the saddle bench, please enter them here.
* **Other Suggestions** (`suggestions_ideas`, text, optional): If you have any other suggestions, please enter them here.
* **How Comfortable** (`comfort_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your comfort level on the bench, where:
	+ "Not at all" means you were not comfortable at all
	+ "Somewhat Comfortable" means you were somewhat comfortable
	+ "Very Comfortable" means you were very comfortable
* **How Satisfied** (`satisfaction_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your satisfaction level on the bench, where:
	+ "Very Dissatisfied" means you were not satisfied at all
	+ "Somewhat Satisfied" means you were somewhat satisfied
	+ "Neutral" means you were neutral about your experience
	+ "Very Satisfied" means you were very satisfied

## Tips
* Please be honest and constructive in your feedback. Your input will help us improve the design and functionality of the saddle bench.
* If you have any further questions or need clarification, please contact us.
