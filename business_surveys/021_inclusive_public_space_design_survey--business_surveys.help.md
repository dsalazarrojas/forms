# Inclusive Public Space Design Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a user experience survey aimed at gathering feedback on the accessibility and usability of public spaces in our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the pages of the survey that you would like to respond to.
2. Answer the questions or make your selections in the relevant fields.
3. Optionally provide additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
4. Enter your contact details in the "Contact details" field to help us respond to your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Survey questions
* **survey_questions** (text, optional): Answer any question or provide feedback that you feel is important to our public spaces. This is a free-text field, so please write as much or as little as you need.

### Public spaces
* **public_spaces** (select_multiple, optional): Select all public spaces that are relevant to your response. For example, if you've had a positive or negative experience in a specific room or area, please select it here.

### Barriers
* **barriers** (select_one, optional): Select the barrier that you feel is most relevant to your experience or opinion. Be as specific as possible to help us understand your perspective.

### Comments
* **comments** (note, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them here.

### Contact details
* **contact_details** (email, optional): Enter your contact information to help us respond to your feedback and follow up with further questions or discussions. Please note that we will only use this information to contact you for this specific survey.

## Tips
* Be as specific and detailed as possible when answering the questions or providing feedback.
* If you have experiences or opinions on multiple public spaces, please select all relevant options in the "Public spaces" field.
* If you have further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us at your organization.
