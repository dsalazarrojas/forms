# Focus Areas Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information on the focus areas and priorities of various departments within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the category for which you are submitting the survey. The two options are "Business Surveys" and "Other Surveys".
2.  Provide a brief description of the project or task you are submitting the survey for.
3.  Enter the date and time you completed the survey.
4.  Add any comments or notes related to the survey.
5.  Select the focus areas that are relevant to your department.
6.  If applicable, assign a tool or user to the project.
7.  Provide any additional notes or comments.
8.  Enter any contact information (email or phone number).
9.  Add any other comments or notes.
10.  Provide information on the business unit, department, project manager, project lead, and project status.
11.  Enter the project name and description.
12.  Select the priority level of the project.
13.  Add any comments or notes related to the focus areas.
14.  Finally, provide any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the category for which you are submitting the survey.
*   **Description** (`description`, `note`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the project or task you are submitting the survey for.
*   **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you completed the survey.
*   **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you completed the survey.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any comments or notes related to the survey.
*   **Focus Areas** (`focus_areas`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the focus areas that are relevant to your department.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, assign a tool or user to the project.
*   **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, assign a user to the project.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional notes or comments.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your contact email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any other comments or notes.
*   **Other Notes** (`other_notes`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional notes or comments.
*   **Business Unit** (`business_unit`, `text`, required: false): Provide information on the business unit.
*   **Department** (`department`, `text`, required: false): Provide information on the department.
*   **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, `text`, required: false): Provide information on the project manager.
*   **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, `text`, required: false): Provide information on the project lead.
*   **Project Status** (`project_status`, `text`, required: false): Provide information on the project status.
*   **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the project name.
*   **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, required: false): Enter the project description.
*   **Priority** (`priority`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the priority level of the project.
*   **Focus Area Comments** (`focus_area_comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any comments or notes related to the focus areas.
*   **Focus Area Notes** (`focus_area_notes`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional notes or comments.
*   **Focus Area Priority** (`focus_area_priority`, `text`, required: false): Enter the priority level of the focus areas.
