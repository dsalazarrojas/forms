# Social Media Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data on your social media performance and strategy. It's designed to be a regular check-in to help you monitor your progress and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the social media platforms you are currently using.
2. Enter the number of current posts on each platform.
3. Enter the engagement rate (likes, comments, etc.) for each platform.
4. Select the demographics and language of your target audience.
5. Enter the number of current followers on each platform.
6. Enter the follower growth rate for each platform.
7. Enter the average number of posts made per month on each platform.
8. Select the engagement boosting strategy and average engagement boosting rate for each platform.
9. Enter the average posts growth rate for each platform.
10. Enter the cost of boosting for each platform.
11. Enter the average cost of boosting for each platform.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Social Media Platform(s) Used**: Select all social media platforms you are currently using (e.g. Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn).
* **Current Posts**: Enter the number of posts currently on your social media platforms.
* **Engagement Rate**: Enter the engagement rate for each platform (e.g. likes, comments, etc.).
* **Audience Demographics**: Select the age range and language of your target audience.
* **Audience Language**: Select the language your audience speaks.
* **Current Followers**: Enter the number of followers on each platform.
* **Follower Growth Rate**: Enter the growth rate of followers for each platform.
* **Average Posts per Month**: Enter the average number of posts made on each platform per month.
* **Engagement Boosting Strategy**: Select the strategy you use to boost engagement on each platform (e.g. High, Medium, Low).
* **Average Engagement Boosting Rate**: Enter the average rate at which you boost engagement on each platform.
* **Average Posts Growth Rate**: Enter the average rate at which posts grow on each platform.
* **Boosting Cost**: Enter the cost of boosting on each platform.
* **Average Boosting Cost**: Enter the average cost of boosting on each platform.
