# Marketplace MVP Requirements Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather essential information about your marketplace's requirements, which will be used to create a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) that meets your business needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section below, providing clear and concise answers to each question.
2. For each text field, feel free to add as much detail as you need, but keep in mind that shorter answers are also welcome if they're sufficient.
3. You can always come back to edit your answers if you need more time or want to add more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **marketplace_ideas**:
	+ Label: marketplace ideas
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Description: Describe the key concepts, features, and functionalities you envision for your marketplace. What makes your marketplace unique? What problem does it solve?
* **core_features**:
	+ Label: core features
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Description: List the essential features that your marketplace must have to be viable. What are the minimum requirements for a customer to use and benefit from your platform?
* **monetization_models**:
	+ Label: monetization models
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Description: How do you plan to monetize your marketplace? What fees, subscription plans, or advertising strategies do you envision?
* **launch_priorities**:
	+ Label: launch priorities
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Description: List your key launch priorities for the MVP. What milestones are most important for achieving a successful launch?

## Tips
- Please answer as concisely as possible while still providing enough information for a clear understanding.
- If you're unsure about a particular question, it's okay to skip it or come back to it later if you need more time.
- This survey is intended to be a starting point for your MVP development, and your input will help shape the platform's core features and priorities.
