# ICO And Presale Overview Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "ICO And Presale Overview Survey" form is designed to collect relevant information related to Initial Coin Offerings (ICOs) and presales. This form appears to be part of a business survey, which might be used for analysis, reporting, or other business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for the survey in the "ICO And Presale Overview Survey" field.
2. Select a category from the dropdown menu to indicate the type of business survey this form is related to.
3. Enter a description of the survey in the "Description" field.
4. If required, upload a file as the "Output File".
5. If needed, assign a tool to the survey from the dropdown options in the "Assigned Tool" field.
6. Enter any relevant Form ID and Category ID (if applicable) if they are required.
7. Finally, enter any other relevant ID information, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **ICO And Presale Overview Survey** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for the survey.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category related to the business survey.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Enter a description of the survey.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): Upload a file related to the survey (if required).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Select a tool assigned to the survey (if needed).
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter a Form ID if required.
* **Category ID** (`category_id`, text, optional): Enter a Category ID if required.
* **Description ID** (`description_id`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant ID information (if necessary).
