# Customer Retention Post Campaign Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather data on customer retention after a marketing or outreach campaign. It helps businesses or organizations track and evaluate the success of their campaign, making it possible to refine and improve future campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information about your campaign, including the campaign ID and start and end dates.
2. Select the correct customer segment that the campaign targeted.
3. Enter the campaign outcomes, including any notable events or results.
4. Enter the customer retention rate, acquisition cost, engagement rate, and customer satisfaction rate, as relevant.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes about the campaign, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Campaign ID** (`customer_retention_campaign_id`, `number`, optional): This field is used to input the unique identifier of the campaign. Please enter the campaign ID number.
* **Customer Segment** (`customer_segment`, `text`, optional): This field is used to select the customer segment that the campaign targeted. Please choose the relevant segment from the options.
* **Customer Segment ID** (`customer_segment_id`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to input the ID of the customer segment selected.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): This field is used to input the start date of the campaign.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): This field is used to input the end date of the campaign.
* **Campaign Outcomes** (`campaign_outcomes`, `note`, optional): This field is used to input any notable events or results of the campaign.
* **Customer Retention Rate** (`customer_retention_rate`, `number`, optional): This field is used to input the customer retention rate.
* **Customer Acquisition Cost** (`customer_acquisition_cost`, `number`, optional): This field is used to input the customer acquisition cost.
* **Customer Engagement Rate** (`customer_engagement_rate`, `number`, optional): This field is used to input the customer engagement rate.
* **Customer Satisfaction Rate Goals** (`customer_satisfaction_rate_goals`, `number`, optional): This field is used to input the customer satisfaction rate.
* **Customer Segment** (`customer_segment`, `text`, optional): This field is used to input the ID of the customer segment selected.
* **Customer Segment ID** (`customer_segment_id`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is used to input multiple IDs of the customer segment selected.
* **Customer Retention Post Campaign Report** (`customer_retention_post_campaign_report`, `text`, optional): This field is used as the title of the form and is not editable.

## Tips
- Double-check the campaign ID and dates for accuracy.
- Ensure you are selecting the correct customer segment and ID for the campaign.
- Provide clear and concise descriptions for campaign outcomes and customer satisfaction rates.
