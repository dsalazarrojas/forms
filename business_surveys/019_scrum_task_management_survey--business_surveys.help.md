# Scrum Task Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your team's Scrum task management process. It will help identify areas of improvement and optimize the process for better outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required field `team_size`.
2. Answer the optional questions about `collaboration`, `task_tracking`, `process_satisfaction`, `communication`, `continuous_improvement`, `team_leadership`, and `feedback` in as much detail as you feel necessary.
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, `text`, required): Please enter the number of team members currently working on your project.
* **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, `text`, optional): Describe your team's collaboration process, including how members interact and communicate with each other.
* **Task Tracking** (`task_tracking`, `text`, optional): Explain how you currently track and follow up on tasks and projects within your team.
* **Process Satisfaction** (`process_satisfaction`, `text`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the current Scrum process, including any areas that need improvement.
* **Communication** (`communication`, `text`, optional): Describe your team's communication process, including how information is shared and discussed.
* **Continuous Improvement** (`continuous_improvement`, `text`, optional): Explain how your team approaches continuous improvement and innovation within the Scrum process.
* **Team Leadership** (`team_leadership`, `text`, optional): Describe your team's leadership structure and how it supports the Scrum process.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Share any feedback or suggestions you have for improving the Scrum process within your team.
