# oem_production_scaling_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from OEM partners on their experiences with production scaling, allowing us to gather insights and make data-driven decisions to improve efficiency and quality.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select Multiple - oem_partner**
	* Choose all applicable OEM partners you have interacted with regarding production scaling.
2. **Text - scaling_challenges**
	* Describe any challenges you faced during production scaling, such as production capacity, quality control, or other issues.
3. **Number - capacity**
	* Provide a numerical value indicating your current production capacity.
4. **Text - support_needs**
	* Specify any support needs you have, such as training, documentation, or equipment maintenance.
5. **Note - comments**
	* Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have regarding production scaling.
6. **Text - contact_name**
	* Enter the name of the contact person who will be able to follow up on this feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Oem_partner**: Select all applicable OEM partners you have interacted with regarding production scaling.
* **Scaling_challenges**: Describe any challenges you faced during production scaling.
* **Capacity**: Provide a numerical value indicating your current production capacity.
* **Support_needs**: Specify any support needs you have.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have regarding production scaling.
* **Contact_name**: Enter the name of the contact person who will be able to follow up on this feedback.
