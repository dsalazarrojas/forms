# Policy Improvement Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Policy Improvement Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from users on policy improvements. This survey is a crucial tool for the organization to gauge the effectiveness and relevance of its policies. Your input is invaluable in helping us create policies that better serve your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and provide as much detail as possible. Your feedback is confidential and will not be used for any other purpose than to improve our policies.
2. You will be directed to a specific page for each section, and each page will have fields relevant to that section. Follow the instructions on each page carefully.
3. If you have any difficulty in answering a question, feel free to ask for clarification or assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policy Improvement Feedback** (`policy_improvement_feedback`, select_multiple, 1): Select all the policies that you believe need improvement. This will help us understand which policies are causing issues for you.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, 1): Choose the category that best describes the policy you're providing feedback on. This will help us group your feedback by category.
* **Description** (`description`, text, ): Please describe the policy you're providing feedback on, being as specific as possible. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
* **Impact** (`impact`, number, 1-5): On a scale of 1-5, please rate how impactful you believe this policy update is.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, number, 1-5): On a scale of 1-5, please rate how often you use this policy.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, note, ): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you have for improvement. This is your chance to suggest changes that might make our policies more effective.
* **Email** (`email`, email, ): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to provide more information, please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, ): If you'd prefer to be contacted over the phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, ): Please enter the date you're submitting this feedback.
* **Time** (`time`, time, ): Please enter the time you're submitting this feedback.
* **Submission Date-Time** (`date_time`, date, ): Please enter the date and time you're submitting this feedback.
* **Submission User** (`submission_user`, select_one, ): Choose the user who submitted this feedback.

Note: Some fields might seem repetitive, but each field is designed to provide additional information on a specific aspect of the policy. Please make sure to fill out all relevant fields to provide a comprehensive feedback.
