# System Configuration Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The System Configuration Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from end-users on the system's configuration, ensuring that it meets their needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and ensure you are answering the questions that pertain to your specific experience with the system.
2. Select the options that best describe your feedback on each page.
3. Enter any additional comments or information in the text boxes provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title**: This form title is a placeholder and should not be edited. It is meant to identify the form's purpose.
* **Feedback Form Page 1**: This page is a general feedback page where you can provide comments on the system's overall performance and usability.
* **System Setup**: This section allows you to choose from multiple options to describe your system setup. Select the option that best suits your experience.
* **System Configuration Feedback**: Provide detailed feedback on the system's configuration, highlighting areas that need improvement.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you for further clarification or follow-up questions.
* **System Questions**: Select the option that best answers your question about the system.
* **Feedback Type**: Choose the type of feedback you are providing (e.g., bug report, feature request, etc.).
* **Additional Feedback**: Provide any additional comments or information that might be helpful in understanding your feedback.
* **Email Confirmation**: Enter your email address to confirm that you are providing feedback and so we can contact you if needed.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for follow-up questions or further clarification.
* **Feedback Form Page 2**: This page is an additional feedback page for any further comments or information.
* **System Config Feedback 2**: Provide detailed feedback on specific aspects of the system's configuration.
* **Additional Contact Number**: Enter an additional contact number for further contact.
