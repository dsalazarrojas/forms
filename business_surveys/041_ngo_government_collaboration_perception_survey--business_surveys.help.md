# NGO Government Collaboration Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the collaboration experiences and perceptions of NGOs with government institutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each section of the form in sequence.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your knowledge.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **NGO Government Collaboration Perception Survey (form_1)**: A text field where you can express your general thoughts and opinions about your collaboration experience with government institutions.
* **Government Partnership Challenges (form_2)**: A text field where you can describe any challenges you faced while collaborating with government partners.
* **Collaboration Ideas (form_3)**: A text field where you can provide suggestions or ideas for improving your collaboration experience with government partners.

## Tips
* Please be honest and concise in your responses.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to provide them in the "Collaboration Ideas" field.
* If you're unsure about any question or field, please ask form@gic.mx for clarification.
