## customer_acquisition_strategy_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer_acquisition_strategy_feedback_form is a tool designed to gather feedback from customers on their experience with your business. This form will help you understand their perspective on your customer acquisition strategy, which can be used to improve your business operations and customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully, and answer them based on your actual experience with the company.
2. Be honest in your feedback, as it will help us improve our services.
3. Use the provided options for select questions, and type your answer in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Acquisition Strategy** (`customer_acquisition_strategy`, note, required: false): This field is a general description of your customer acquisition strategy experience with our company. Please provide your thoughts on how our strategy has helped or hindered your customer acquisition efforts.
* **Acquisition Channels** (`acquisition_channels`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the channels you used to acquire our product or service. For example, if you visited our website, checked out our social media, or attended a trade show.
* **Marketing Campaigns** (`marketing_campaigns`, text, required: false): Describe any marketing campaigns or promotions you were aware of or interacted with during your customer acquisition process.
* **Customer Engagement** (`customer_engagement`, select_one, required: false): Rate your level of engagement with our customer support team or representatives. Was it satisfactory?
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): Rate your level of satisfaction with our product or service. Were you happy with your purchase?
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the channels you used to communicate with our customer support team or representatives. For example, email, phone, or live chat.
* **Sales Process** (`sales_process`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the sales processes you experienced during your interaction with our company. For example, did you receive a call from a sales representative, or did you interact with a salesperson through email or online chat?
* **Customer Acquisition Goals** (`customer_acquisition_goals`, text, required: false): Describe your goals for acquiring our product or service. Was it for personal use, for business, or other?
* **Customer Engagement Channels** (`customer_engagement_channels`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the channels you used to engage with our customer support team or representatives. For example, email, phone, or online chat.
* **Marketing Budget** (`marketing_budget`, number, required: false): Provide an estimate of the marketing budget you allocated for customer acquisition. Was it high, medium, or low?
* **Customer Satisfaction Level** (`customer_satisfaction_level`, select_one, required: false): Rate your level of satisfaction with our product or service. Were you satisfied or dissatisfied?
* **Acquisition Process** (`acquisition_process`, text, required: false): Describe your experience with our customer acquisition process. Was it easy, difficult, or something in between?
* **Customer Acquisition Process** (`customer_acquisition_process`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the steps you experienced during your customer acquisition process. For example, did you interact with a sales representative, or did you self-service online?
* **Sales Process** (`sales_process`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the sales processes you experienced during your customer acquisition process. For example, did you receive a call from a sales representative, or did you interact with a salesperson through email or online chat?
* **Customer Acquisition Cost** (`customer_acquisition_cost`, number, required: false): Provide an estimate of the cost you incurred for customer acquisition. Was it high, medium, or low?
* **Customer Acquisition Strategy Ideas** (`customer_acquisition_strategy_ideas`, text, required: false): Provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improving our customer acquisition strategy.
