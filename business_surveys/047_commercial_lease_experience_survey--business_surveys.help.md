# Commercial Lease Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your commercial lease experience. Your responses will help us improve our services and provide better support to our tenants.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Take your time to consider each question before submitting your responses.
4. You can skip questions if they don't apply to your experience.
5. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Additional Comments" field to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name**: This is your name as the tenant.
	+ Your name is optional, but it will help us identify your responses.
* **Company Name**: This is your company's name.
	+ This information helps us understand the size and type of business you operate.
* **Email Address**: This is your email address for follow-up contact.
	+ We will use this to get in touch with you if we need clarification or more information.
* **Phone Number**: This is your phone number.
	+ We will use this to get in touch with you if we need to.
* **Leased Property Address**: This is the address of the property you leased.
	+ Please ensure that you enter the correct address.
* **Lease Start Date**: This is the date your lease began.
	+ Use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Lease Duration**: Choose the length of your lease from the options provided.
	+ Select the most accurate duration of your lease.
* **Type of Space**: Choose the type of commercial space you leased.
	+ Select the most accurate type of space.
* **Square Footage**: Enter the size of the leased space.
	+ Use numerical value.
* **Overall Landlord Rating**: Rate your overall experience with your landlord.
	+ Select the most accurate rating.
* **Landlord Responsiveness**: Rate the responsiveness of your landlord.
	+ Select the most accurate rating.
* **Maintenance Handling**: Rate the maintenance handling by your landlord.
	+ Select the most accurate rating.
* **Communication Quality**: Rate the communication quality from your landlord.
	+ Select the most accurate rating.
* **Initial Property Condition**: Rate the initial condition of the property.
	+ Select the most accurate rating.
* **Current Property Condition**: Rate the current condition of the property.
	+ Select the most accurate rating.
* **Building Amenities**: Rate the amenities provided by the building.
	+ Select the most accurate rating.
* **Parking Facilities**: Rate the parking facilities provided by the building.
	+ Select the most accurate rating.
* **Rent Value**: Rate the rent value for the space.
	+ Select the most accurate rating.
* **Lease Flexibility**: Rate the flexibility of the lease terms.
	+ Select the most accurate rating.
* **Renewal Terms**: Rate the renewal terms of the lease.
	+ Select the most accurate rating.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the lease.
	+ Select the most accurate rating.
* **Would You Renew**: Answer if you would renew the lease.
	+ Select the most accurate answer.
* **Would You Recommend**: Answer if you would recommend the lease to others.
	+ Select the most accurate answer.
* **Best Aspect of Lease**: Describe the best aspect of your lease experience.
	+ Enter a brief description.
* **Worst Aspect of Lease**: Describe the worst aspect of your lease experience.
	+ Enter a brief description.
* **Suggested Improvements**: Describe any improvements you would suggest.
	+ Enter a brief description.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback.
	+ Enter a brief description.
