# Sync Settings Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to help users provide detailed information about the sync settings they are using in their organization. By completing this survey, you will be able to provide feedback and suggestions for improvement to our development team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each page of the form to determine which sections are relevant to your current sync settings.
2. Select the sync frequency that best describes your current setting in the "Sync Frequency" section.
3. For the "What type of sync issue do you have?" section, choose the option that best describes the type of issue you are experiencing.
4. In the "Sync Behavior" section, you may enter any additional information about your sync behavior that you feel is necessary.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" section so we can contact you with any follow-up questions or to provide updates.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" section so we can contact you with any urgent matters.
7. In the "Describe the issue you are experiencing with sync behavior" section, provide a brief description of the issue.
8. If you experience any specific sync issues, describe them in the detail in the "Sync Issue" section.
9. If you have any additional notes on your sync frequency, enter them in the "Note on sync frequency" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sync Frequency** (sync_frequency_label, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the frequency at which you are syncing data. This could be daily, weekly, monthly, or never.
* **What type of sync issue do you have?** (issue_type_label, select_one, required/optional): This will help us understand the type of issue you are experiencing with sync behavior.
* **Sync Behavior** (sync_behavior_label, note, required/optional): Enter any additional information about your sync behavior that you feel is necessary.
* **Email** (email_label, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with any follow-up questions or to provide updates.
* **Phone Number** (phone_label, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any urgent matters.
* **Describe the issue you are experiencing with sync behavior** (sync_issue_description_label, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the issue.
* **Sync Issue** (sync_issue_label, text, required/optional): Describe the specific sync issue you are experiencing.
* **Note on sync frequency** (sync_frequency_note_label, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about your sync frequency.
* **Note** (sync_frequency_note_label, note, required/optional): This field is not actively used in the form, but it's a placeholder for any additional information.
