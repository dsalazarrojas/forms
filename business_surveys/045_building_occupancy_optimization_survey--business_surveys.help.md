# Building Occupancy Optimization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information to help optimize space usage and occupant comfort in your building. It will help us understand how you use your workspace and identify areas that can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this survey in its entirety, as this will provide us with the most valuable information.
2. Answer the questions honestly, and to the best of your ability.
3. Select the correct answer for each question from the provided options.
4. If you don't see a suitable option for a question, you can type a custom answer in the text box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your name**: This is your name, which will help us identify you and your responses.
* **Email address**: This is your email address, which will help us contact you with any follow-up questions or if we need more information.
* **Department or team**: Please select the department or team you belong to.
* **Your primary workspace location**: This is where you usually work, so please select the floor or area.
* **Current Space Usage**: This section is just a note, as we want to get a brief understanding of your space usage.
* **How often are you in the building?**: Please select how often you are physically present in the building.
* **How often do you work from home?**: Select how often you work from home, as this will help us understand your work habits.
* **Desk or workspace assignment**: Please select your current desk or workspace assignment.
* **Is your assigned workspace adequate?**: Select how adequate you feel your assigned workspace is.
* **Frequency of shared space use**: Please select how often you use shared spaces like conference rooms or collaboration areas.
* **Are shared spaces adequate?**: Select how adequate you feel shared spaces are.
* **When is the building most crowded?**: Select from the options when the building is typically most crowded.
* **Areas you notice are often underutilized**: Type in any areas that you feel are underutilized.
* **Areas that feel crowded or congested**: Type in any areas that you feel are overcrowded or congested.
* **Effectiveness of collaboration spaces**: Select how effective you feel collaboration spaces are for your work.
* **Building amenities available**: Please select from the options which amenities are available in the building.
* **Which amenities are most important to you?**: Select the amenities that are most important to you.
* **Primary commuting method**: Select your primary method of commuting to the building.
* **Average commute time**: Select your average commute time.
* **Parking satisfaction**: Select how satisfied you are with the parking facilities.
* **Interest in flexible work schedule**: Select how interested you are in having a flexible work schedule.
* **Optimal work schedule for you**: Select your optimal work schedule.
* **Recommended space improvements**: Type in any suggestions you have for improving the space.
* **Ideas for better space utilization**: Type in any ideas you have for improving space usage.
* **Suggestions to improve efficiency**: Type in any suggestions you have for improving the efficiency of your workspace.
* **Additional comments or feedback**: Please feel free to add any additional comments or feedback you may have.

Note: Please keep in mind that this is just a guide, and you may adjust the content according to your specific form needs. 

Also, this survey is intended to be a living document, it's subject to change as your team grows and learns more about you. 

The more honest and thorough you are with your responses, the better we can help optimize the building's space and make it more comfortable for you and your colleagues.
