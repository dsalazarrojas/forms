Theatre Strategic Planning Survey - Help Guide
=====================================

## Purpose
This form is intended for users to provide insights and feedback on the theatre's strategic planning and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information**:
	+ **General Information 1**: This field is for entering any relevant information about the theatre.
* **Challenges**:
	+ **Challenges**: This field is for selecting all applicable challenges that the theatre is facing.
* **Priorities**:
	+ **Priorities**: This field is for selecting all applicable priorities for the theatre's development and improvement.
* **Resources**:
	+ **Resources**: This field is for selecting all applicable resources available for the theatre's development and improvement.
* **Audience Engagement**:
	+ **Audience Engagement**: This field is for selecting all applicable audience engagement levels.
* **Additional Notes**:
	+ **Additional Notes**: This field is for entering any additional comments or notes about the theatre.
* **Contact Information**:
	+ **Contact Information**: This field is for entering the contact information of the person filling out this form.
* **Email**:
	+ **Email**: This field is for entering a valid email address.
* **Submission Date**:
	+ **Submission Date**: This field is for selecting the desired submission date.
* **Submission Time**:
	+ **Submission Time**: This field is for selecting the desired submission time.
* **Submission Emacs**:
	+ **Submission Emacs**: This field is for entering any additional submission information.
* **General Information 4-9**:
	+ **General Information 4-9**: This field is for entering any additional general information about the theatre.

## Tips
* Ensure that each field is filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* Duplicate entries should be avoided.
* If unsure about any field, contact the theatre's management team for clarification.
* This form is for informational purposes only and will not be used for any form of solicitation or marketing.
