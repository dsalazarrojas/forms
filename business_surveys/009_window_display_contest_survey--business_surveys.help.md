# Window Display Contest Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Window Display Contest Survey is a form designed to collect information from store owners or representatives about their business. The survey aims to gather insights on various aspects of the store, including contact details, store information, and a photo. This information will help us better understand the store's characteristics and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully before filling out the form.
2. Ensure that you have all the necessary information before starting.
3. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter your details** (`entry_form`, text, optional): This is the main section where you can provide a brief description of your store, including any relevant information about your business.
	+ Please provide a brief overview of your store, including its unique selling points, products, or services offered.
* **Contact details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): In this section, you can provide your contact information so that we can get in touch with you about any questions or follow-up inquiries.
	+ Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date contact details, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Display description** (`display_description`, text, optional): This section is where you can describe your store's display window or storefront, including any notable features or attractions.
	+ Try to be as specific as possible when describing your store's display window or storefront.
* **Photo** (`photo`, text, optional): In this section, you can upload or paste a link to a photo of your store's display window or storefront.
	+ Please ensure that the photo is clear and well-lit, and that it accurately represents your store's display window or storefront.
* **Store name** (`store_name`, text, optional): This is the name of your store, which will be used to identify it in the survey results.
	+ Please use the exact name of your store as it appears on your business cards, website, or other marketing materials.
* **Contact person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): This is the person responsible for handling customer inquiries or questions.
	+ Please provide the name and contact information of the person who will be able to respond to customer inquiries or questions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): The phone number of your store, where customers can call to get more information or place orders.
	+ Please ensure that this is the correct phone number for your store.
* **Email** (`email`, text, optional): The email address of your store, where customers can contact you for more information or inquiries.
	+ Please ensure that this is the correct email address for your store.
* **Store address** (`store_address`, text, optional): The physical address of your store, which will be used to identify its location.
	+ Please use the exact address of your store as it appears on your business cards, website, or other marketing materials.
* **Store city** (`store_city`, text, optional): The city where your store is located.
	+ Please ensure that this is the correct city where your store is located.
* **Store state** (`store_state`, select_one, optional): Whether your store has a physical presence in the United States or not.
	+ Please select 'Yes' if your store has a physical presence in the United States, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Store zip** (`store_zip`, text, optional): The postal code of your store's location.
	+ Please use the exact zip code for your store as it appears on your business cards, website, or other marketing materials.
* **Store country** (`store_country`, text, optional): The country where your store is located.
	+ Please use the exact country where your store is located.
* **Store region** (`store_region`, text, optional): The region or area where your store is located.
	+ Please use the exact region or area where your store is located.
* **Photo description** (`photo_description`, text, optional): A brief description of the photo you've uploaded or linked to.
	+ Please provide a brief description of the photo, including any notable features or attractions.
* **Store hours** (`store_hours`, text, optional): The hours of operation for your store.
	+ Please ensure that this is the correct schedule of operation for your store.

## Tips

* Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
* We appreciate your participation in the Window Display Contest Survey and look forward to reviewing your submission!
