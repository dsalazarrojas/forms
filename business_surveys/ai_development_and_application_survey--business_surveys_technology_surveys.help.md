# AI Development And Application Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on your experiences with AI development and application. Your input will help us better understand the current state of AI adoption in various industries and organizations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your demographic information (full name, email address, and phone number)
2. Provide your date of birth
3. Enter your home address
4. Share your organization's name
5. Choose your primary industry
6. Select your job role
7. Enter your years of experience in AI/ML
8. Choose your AI experience level
9. Select all AI use cases you have implemented
10. List the AI tools or platforms you have used
11. Enter the average project budget (if applicable)
12. Enter the average project timeline (if applicable)
13. Choose your primary challenge in AI adoption
14. Select the anticipated business impact
15. Choose your overall satisfaction with AI initiatives
16. Add any additional comments or observations
17. Provide consent to share your survey data

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, required): Enter your legal first and last name.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, optional): Include your country code. (Note: This field is optional but recommended for further contact.)
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, required): Select your birth date.
* **Home address** (`address`, required): Enter your street, city, state, and ZIP.
* **Organization name** (`organization_name`, required): Enter the name of your company or institution.
* **Industry** (`industry`, required): Select your primary industry (e.g., Healthcare, Finance, Retail).
* **Job role** (`job_role`, required): Choose your primary professional title.
* **Years of experience in AI/ML** (`years_experience`, required): Enter a whole number.
* **AI experience level** (`ai_experience_level`, required): Choose the level that best describes your experience (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert).
* **AI use cases implemented** (`ai_use_cases`, optional): Select all AI use cases you have implemented.
* **AI tools or platforms used** (`ai_tools_used`, optional): List the AI tools or platforms you have used, separated by commas.
* **Average project budget** (`ai_project_budget`, optional): Enter the average project budget in USD, using decimal format if needed.
* **Average project timeline** (`ai_project_timeline_months`, optional): Enter the average project timeline in months.
* **Primary challenge in AI adoption** (`primary_challenge`, required): Choose the most significant obstacle you face in AI adoption.
* **Anticipated business impact** (`anticipated_impact`, required): Select the expected return on investment.
* **Overall satisfaction with AI initiatives** (`satisfaction_rating`, required): Choose your overall satisfaction with AI initiatives (1-10).
* **Additional comments or observations** (`additional_comments`, optional): Share any extra insights you have.
* **Consent to share survey data** (`consent`, required): Choose to share your survey data for research purposes.
