# Landlord Tenant Relationship Health Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Landlord Tenant Relationship Health Survey is a tool designed to gauge the satisfaction and frequency of communication between landlords and tenants. This survey aims to collect insights on the quality of the landlord-tenant relationship, helping landlords and property managers to identify areas of improvement and foster better communication.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the questions carefully and select the best answer that reflects your experience with your landlord.
2. Select one answer option for each question, as applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you communicate with your landlord?** (`Landlord Tenant Communication`, Select Multiple, Optional)
This question asks about your frequency of communication with your landlord, such as when you need something, ask questions, or discuss issues.
* **How responsive is your landlord when you need something?** (`Landlord Tenant Responsiveness`, Select One, Optional)
This question assesses your landlord's attentiveness and willingness to respond to your needs, questions, or concerns.
* **Have you ever had any privacy concerns with your landlord?** (`Privacy Concerns`, Select One, Optional)
This question seeks to understand if you have ever felt that your privacy has been compromised or violated by your landlord, whether in terms of personal space or data.
* **How satisfied are you with the communication from your landlord?** (`Communication Satisfaction`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Rate your level of satisfaction with the communication you receive from your landlord, considering factors like clarity, frequency, and responsiveness.
* **How satisfied are you with your tenancy?** (`Tenant Satisfaction`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Evaluate your overall satisfaction with being a tenant, encompassing aspects like rent, utilities, and living conditions.
* **How satisfied are you with your overall tenancy experience?** (`Overall Satisfaction`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Assess your overall satisfaction with the tenancy experience, considering both positive and negative aspects.
* **How often do you communicate with your landlord through?** (`Communication Channels`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Select the channels through which you typically communicate with your landlord, such as phone, email, text message, or video call.
* **How often do you communicate with your landlord?** (`Communication Frequency`, Select Multiple, Optional)
This is similar to the previous question, asking about the frequency of your communication with your landlord, but does not specify the channel.
