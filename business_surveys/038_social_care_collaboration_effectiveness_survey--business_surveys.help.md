# social_care_collaboration_effectiveness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the effectiveness of team collaboration in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For questions with options, select the one that best represents your opinion.
3. For numerical questions, enter a number that best represents your frequency or satisfaction level.
4. Please provide your honest and constructive feedback.
