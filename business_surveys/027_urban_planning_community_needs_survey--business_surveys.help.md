# urban_planning_community_needs_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from community members about their needs and concerns regarding urban planning and development in their neighborhood. It aims to identify areas for improvement and facilitate community engagement and participation in the planning process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For multiple choice questions, select all the options that apply.
3. For free-text questions, type your responses clearly and concisely.
4. If you have no preference or opinion, you can leave the question blank or select "Not very important" for the corresponding question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How satisfied are you with your neighborhood?** (`neighborhood_satisfaction`, `text`, required: `false`): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with your neighborhood. Please provide a brief answer to this question.
- **What is your main mode of transportation to work?** (`transportation`, `select_one`, options: `Bus`, `Car`, `Walking`, `Other`): This question asks about your primary mode of transportation to work. Please select the option that best describes how you usually travel to work.
- **How important is having facilities in your neighborhood?** (`community_facilities`, `select_multiple`, options: `Very important`, `Moderately important`, `Not very important`): This question asks about the importance of having facilities in your neighborhood. Please select all the options that apply to you.
- **Do you have any comments or feedback about this project?** (`resident_feedback`, `note`, required: `false`): This question is for any additional comments or feedback you may have about this project.
- **Would you like to be contacted about this project?** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: `false`): This question is for your contact information if you would like to be kept updated about this project.
- **What is your email address?** (`email`, `email`, required: `false`): This question asks for your email address so we can contact you if needed.
- **What is your expected completion date for this project?** (`date`, `date`, required: `false`): This question asks about your expected completion date for this project. Please use the YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **What time of day do you expect to be done with this project?** (`time`, `time`, required: `false`): This question asks about your expected completion time of day. Please use the HH:MM AM/PM format.
- **What location do you expect to be done with this project?** (`location`, `select_multiple`, options: `Downtown`, `Uptown`, `Downtown/Uptown`, `Other`): This question asks about your expected location of completion. Please select all the locations that apply.
- **What is your top priority for this project?** (`priority`, `select_one`, options: `Safety`, `Affordability`, `Accessibility`, `Quality`, `Other`): This question asks about your top priority for this project. Please select the option that best represents your priority.
- **Can you provide more information about this project?** (`project_details`, `text`, required: `false`): This question is for any additional details you may want to provide about this project.
- **Who is your assigned tool?** (`assigned_tool`, `select_multiple`, options: `chatjimmy`, `chat bob`, `Other`): This question asks about your assigned tool for this project. Please select all the tools that apply.
- **What category do you belong to?** (`category`, `select_multiple`, options: `Business Surveys`, `Community Surveys`, `Personal Surveys`): This question asks about your category. Please select all the categories that apply.
- **What is the name of your output file?** (`output_file`, `text`, required: `false`): This question asks for your output file name.
- **Do you have any comments or feedback about this form?** (`comments`, `note`, required: `false`): This question is for any additional comments or feedback you may have about this form.

## Tips

- You can skip questions that are not relevant to you.
