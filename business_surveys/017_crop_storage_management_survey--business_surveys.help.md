# Crop Storage Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from farmers about their current crop storage methods, capacity, challenges, and facilities. This data will be used to identify areas where improvements can be made to better support farmers in their crop storage management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your current storage method from the options provided for "Current Storage Method".
2. Enter your storage capacity in the "Storage Capacity" field.
3. Describe your storage challenges in the "Storage Challenges" field.
4. Select your storage facility from the options provided for "Storage Facility".
5. Choose the storage practices you currently use from the "Storage Practices" list.
6. Describe any other challenges you are facing with storage in the "Other Challenges" field.
7. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Current Storage Method 
* **Current Storage Method** (storage_practices, select_one, required): Select the current method you are using to store your crops. This could be Storage 1, Storage 2, or Storage 3.

### Storage Capacity 
* **Storage Capacity** (storage_capacity, number, required): Enter the current capacity of your storage in the number field. This will help us understand the scale of your storage needs.

### Storage Challenges 
* **Storage Challenges** (storage_challenges, text, required): Describe any challenges you are facing with storage, such as pests, moisture, or other issues.

### Storage Facility 
* **Storage Facility** (storage_facility, select_one, required): Select the facility where you are storing your crops. This could be Facility 1, Facility 2, or Facility 3.

### Storage Practices 
* **Storage Practices** (storage_practices_1, select_multiple, required): Choose the storage practices you currently use. These practices are important for maintaining crop quality and safety. Please select all that apply.

### Storage Challenges 1 
* **Storage Challenges 1** (storage_challenges_1, text, required): Describe any specific challenges you face with storage, such as pests, moisture, or other issues.

### Storage Facility 1 
* **Storage Facility 1** (storage_facility_1, select_one, required): Select the facility where you store your crops. This could be Facility 1, Facility 2, or Facility 3.

### Other Challenges 
* **Other Challenges** (other_challenges, text, optional): If there are any other challenges you face with storage that are not listed, please describe them here.
