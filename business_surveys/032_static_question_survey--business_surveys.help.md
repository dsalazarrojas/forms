# static_question_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with our feature or content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer it as accurately as possible.
2. Provide clear and concise answers to each question.
3. Fill out all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction (text)**: This is a brief description of your experience with our feature or content. Please provide a short paragraph or sentence that summarizes your experience.
* **Satisfaction (number)**: Rate your satisfaction with our feature or content on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Feature Usage (number)**: Tell us how often you use our feature or content.
* **Recommendation (select_one)**: Suggest an improvement or a feature that you would like to see added to our feature or content.
	+ Option 1: [Option 1]
	+ Option 2: [Option 2]
	+ Option 3: [Option 3]
* **Feedback (note)**: This is an open-ended field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Contact Email (email)**: If you would like to be contacted for further discussion, please provide your email address.
* **Contact Phone (text)**: If you would like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Additional Comments (text)**: This is an optional field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions.

Note: All fields are optional, but it's essential to provide clear and concise answers to help us better understand your feedback.
