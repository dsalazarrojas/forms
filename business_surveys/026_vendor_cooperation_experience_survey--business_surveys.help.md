# Vendor Cooperation Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from vendors about their experience in partnering with your business. It helps to gauge their satisfaction, identify areas for improvement, and foster a stronger relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Answer the questions based on your experience with our company.
3. If you have any concerns or issues, please be specific and provide examples.
4. If you are unsure about any question, you can leave it blank or contact the person who sent you this form for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name** (text, required): Please enter your company's name as a supplier.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for any follow-up questions.
* **Department** (text, required): Enter the department or team within your company that you interact with us.
* **Job Title** (text, required): Enter your job title to help us understand your role within the partnership.
* **How do you feel about our communication?** (number, required): Rate your satisfaction with our communication from 1-5, where 1 is not satisfactory and 5 is very satisfactory.
* **How would you rate the timeliness of our payments?** (number, required): Rate your experience with our payment schedule from 1-5, where 1 is not timely and 5 is very timely.
* **How do you rate the level of support we provide?** (select_one, required): Choose one of the options to rate our level of support:
	+ Option 1: Good
	+ Option 2: Fair
	+ Option 3: Poor
* **How would you rate the level of support we provide?** (select_multiple, required): Choose multiple options to rate our level of support:
	+ Option 1: Good
	+ Option 2: Fair
	+ Option 3: Poor
* **How do you rate the level of support we provide?** (select_one, required): This question is intended to gauge your experience with our support. Please choose one of the options:
	+ Option 1: Good
	+ Option 2: Fair
	+ Option 3: Poor
* **Have we met our commitments and deadlines?** (select_one, required): Rate your experience with meeting commitments and deadlines:
	+ Option 1: Yes
	+ Option 2: No
	+ Option 3: Mostly
* **How would you rate the overall experience?** (time, required): Rate your overall experience with us, with 1 being not satisfactory and 5 being very satisfactory.
* **How would you rate the collaboration?** (select_one, required): Rate your experience with our collaboration:
	+ Option 1: Good
	+ Option 2: Fair
	+ Option 3: Poor
* **Have we exceeded your expectations?** (date, required): Enter a date when we exceeded your expectations, or leave blank if not applicable.
* **What are some areas for improvement?** (note, required): Provide specific examples or suggestions on how we can improve our partnership.
* **Is this partnership beneficial?** (select_one, optional): If you have time, rate your experience with this partnership:
	+ Option 1: Yes
	+ Option 2: No
	+ Option 3: Unsure
* **Have you had any issues with payments or invoices?** (select_multiple, required): If you have encountered any issues, choose all that apply:
	+ Option 1: Good
	+ Option 2: Fair
	+ Option 3: Poor
* **What would make this partnership even better?** (text, required): Provide suggestions or feedback on how we can improve our partnership.
