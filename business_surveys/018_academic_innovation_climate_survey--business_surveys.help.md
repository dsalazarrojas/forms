# Academic Innovation Climate Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information on the perceived support and resources available for innovation within the institution, as well as the challenges and barriers that respondents face. Your feedback will help us better understand the needs and concerns of our community and inform strategies to improve the innovation climate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select your role within the institution from the available options.
2. If applicable, enter the approximate number of years you have been at the institution.
3. Rate the institutional support for innovation.
4. Select all that apply from the list of common barriers to innovation.
5. Briefly describe any notable successes or innovations you have implemented or witnessed.
6. Indicate the areas where professional development needs improvement.
7. Choose up to three resource priorities that would help improve innovation.
8. Select the likelihood of your participation in improvement activities.
9. Enter any additional feedback or comments on the innovation climate.
10. Review and submit your responses to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent role** (`respondent_role`, select_one, required): Select your role within the institution (Faculty, Student, Staff, Administrator, or External partner).
* **Years at institution** (`years_at_institution`, number, optional): Enter the approximate number of years you have been at the institution.
* **Institutional support for innovation** (`innovation_support_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the institutional support for innovation as Very strong, Strong, Moderate, Weak, or Very weak.
* **Key barriers to innovation** (`barriers_to_innovation`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply (Funding constraints, Lack of time, Administrative burden, Lack of incentives, Cultural resistance).
* **Examples of successful innovation** (`successful_innovation_examples`, text, optional): Briefly describe any notable successes or innovations you have implemented or witnessed.
* **Professional development needs** (`professional_development_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate areas where professional development is needed (Design thinking, Project management, Data skills, Teaching innovation, Leadership training).
* **Resource priorities** (`resource_priorities`, select_multiple, optional): Choose up to three resource priorities that would help improve innovation (Seed funding, Staff time, Technical support, Collaboration spaces, Recognition and awards).
* **Commitment to implement changes** (`commitment_to_change`, select_one, optional): Select the likelihood of your participation in improvement activities (Very likely, Somewhat likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very unlikely).
* **Additional feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Enter any additional feedback or comments on the innovation climate.
