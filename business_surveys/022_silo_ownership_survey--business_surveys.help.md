# SILo Ownership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SILo Ownership Survey form is designed to collect information about the ownership and usage of silos. This form is used to gather data on the location, capacity, and other relevant details about the silos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields (if any) for each section.
2.  Provide accurate and complete information about the silo, including its location, capacity, and usage patterns.
3.  Enter the name and contact information of the owner of the silo.
4.  Select the type of agency or organization that is involved with the silo (if applicable).
5.  Add any additional notes or comments about the silo.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### silo_ownership_survey
*   **SILO Ownership Survey** (`text`, required): A brief description of the silo and its purpose.
### silo_location
*   **siloLocation** (`text`, required): The location of the silo.
### silo_capacity
*   **siloCapacity** (`number`, required): The capacity of the silo.
### silo_usage
*   **siloUsage** (`time`, required): The usage pattern of the silo.
### silo_owner_name
*   **siloOwnerName** (`text`, required): The name of the silo owner.
### silo_owner_contact
*   **siloOwnerContact** (`email`, required): The email contact information of the silo owner.
### silo_owner_phone
*   **siloOwnerPhone** (`text`, optional): The phone number of the silo owner.
### silo_owner_business
*   **siloOwnerBusiness** (`select_one`, optional): The type of agency or organization that is involved with the silo (Co-op, Agribusiness, Agency).
### silo_ownership_notes
*   **siloOwnerNotes** (`note`, optional): Additional notes about the silo owner.
### silo_other_notes
*   **siloOtherNotes** (`note`, optional): Additional notes about the silo.
### silo_location_notes
*   **siloLocationNotes** (`note`, optional): Additional notes about the silo location.
### silo_usage_patterns
*   **siloUsagePatterns** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all applicable usage patterns for the silo.
### silo_capacity_notes
*   **siloCapacityNotes** (`note`, optional): Additional notes about the silo capacity.
### assigned_tool
*   **assignedTool** (`text`, optional): The tool assigned to the silo.
### assigned_user
*   **assignedUser** (`text`, optional): The user assigned to the silo.
### created_at
*   **createdAt** (`date`, required): Date and time the record was created.

## Tips
*   Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
*   Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
*   If unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or the survey administrator for clarification.
*   Use this form to report any changes or updates to the silo ownership and usage information.
