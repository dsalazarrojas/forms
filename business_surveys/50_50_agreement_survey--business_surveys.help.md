# 50:50 Agreement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the respondent's understanding and sentiment towards a 50:50 agreement. The agreement is a business partnership or collaboration where the profits are split equally between the parties involved. This form is intended to gather feedback on the perceived fairness and effectiveness of the 50:50 arrangement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as the respondent.
2. Choose the role or affiliation of your company/partnership/stakeholder.
3. Provide your contact email and phone number for follow-up questions.
4. Select the option that best describes your understanding of the 50:50 agreement terms.
5. Rate how fair you consider the 50:50 split to be on a scale of 1 to 10.
6. Check all that apply for any primary concerns regarding the 50:50 arrangement.
7. Suggest specific modifications to improve fairness if needed.
8. Choose the likelihood of accepting the 50:50 agreement.
9. Select mitigation measures you consider most effective for the partnership.
10. Provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent full name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the respondent.
* **Role or affiliation** (`respondent_role`, `text`, required): Choose the role or affiliation of your company/partnership/stakeholder, e.g., Co-owner, Team Lead, Investor.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email for follow-up questions.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, `phone`, optional): Enter your contact phone number including country code if outside local area.
* **Do you understand the terms of the proposed 50:50 split?** (`agreement_understanding`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best matches your understanding of the 50:50 terms:
	+ Yes, fully understood
	+ Mostly understood, minor questions
	+ Not fully understood
	+ No, need full explanation
* **How fair do you consider a 50:50 split?** (`fairness_rating`, `integer`, required): Rate the fairness of the 50:50 split on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Primary concerns about the 50:50 arrangement** (`primary_concerns`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check all that apply for any primary concerns regarding the 50:50 arrangement:
	+ Unequal effort or contribution
	+ Unclear decision-making authority
	+ Financial risk distribution
	+ Exit or dissolution terms
	+ Intellectual property ownership
	+ Tax or legal implications
* **Suggested modifications to improve fairness** (`proposed_modifications`, `text`, optional): Suggest specific clause changes or mechanisms to improve fairness.
* **Likelihood you would accept this 50:50 agreement** (`acceptance_likelihood`, `select_one`, required): Choose the likelihood of accepting the 50:50 agreement:
	+ Very likely
	+ Somewhat likely
	+ Unsure
	+ Unlikely
	+ Definitely not
* **Recommended mitigation measures** (`mitigation_measures`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply for recommended mitigation measures:
	+ Vesting schedule
	+ Mediation/arbitration clause
	+ Performance milestones
	+ Buy-sell provisions
	+ Third-party oversight
* **Additional comments or detailed feedback** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or detailed feedback.
