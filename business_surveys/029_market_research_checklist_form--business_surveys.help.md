# market_research_checklist_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The market research checklist form is designed to collect information about a project's market research needs. It helps to identify key areas to investigate in a project, ensuring that all necessary market research has been conducted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the project name and research location fields with the relevant information.
2. Select the target audience that the research is aimed at.
3. Choose the research methodology and data collection method that best suits the project's needs.
4. Describe the research objectives, scope, limitations, and conclusion in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Project Name (project_name)
* **Project Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the project you are conducting market research for.

### Research Location (research_location)
* **Research Location** (text, required): Specify the location where the research will take place or the market that will be studied.

### Research Date (research_date)
* **Research Date** (date, required): Enter the date on which the research will be conducted or the start date of the research project.

### Target Audience (target_audience)
* **Target Audience** (select multiple, required): Identify the audience that the research is aimed at. You can select multiple audiences by clicking on the "+" button next to "Target Audience".

### Research Methodology (research_methodology)
* **Research Methodology** (text, required): Describe the approach you will take to conduct the research, such as surveys, interviews, or focus groups.

### Research Objectives (research_objectives)
* **Research Objectives** (text, required): Outline the specific goals you aim to achieve through the research, such as identifying market trends or understanding customer needs.

### Data Collection Method (data_collection_method)
* **Data Collection Method** (select one, required): Choose the method you will use to collect data, such as option 1 or option 2.

### Research Scope (research_scope)
* **Research Scope** (text, required): Describe the extent or range of the research, including any specific areas or markets that will be studied.

### Research Limitations (research_limitations)
* **Research Limitations** (text, required): Identify any limitations or challenges that may affect the research, such as time or resource constraints.

### Research Conclusion (research_conclusion)
* **Research Conclusion** (text, required): Summarize the main findings or results of the research, and any recommendations for future projects.

### Data Collection Method Options
If you select "Data Collection Method", please note that you will be asked to choose between Option 1 and Option 2. However, we do not know which option is more suitable for your research at this point.
