This form is designed to gather feedback on the usability and performance of category filters on a platform. It is intended to help the platform's developers understand how users interact with and perceive the filtering system, allowing for improvements to be made to enhance the user experience. This form is not affiliated with or derived from any specific form platform, but rather generated using AI and public survey topic metadata as inspiration.

The survey is divided into three main sections: "Filter Usability Feedback", "Technical Performance", and "Improvement Suggestions". The "Filter Usability Feedback" section aims to gauge users' experience and perceptions of the filtering system, including the ease of finding filters, the clarity of filter category names, and the usefulness of filter options. The "Technical Performance" section assesses the speed and visual design of the filter menu, while the "Improvement Suggestions" section invites users to provide feedback on how to improve the filtering system.

Before starting the survey, ensure you have all the necessary information and access to the filtering system. This survey should take approximately 5-10 minutes to complete.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the device you are currently using (e.g., Mobile, Tablet, Desktop/Laptop).
2. Rate the ease of finding the category filters (Very Easy, Somewhat Easy, Difficult, I could not find them).
3. Rate the clarity of the filter category names (Yes, very clear, Mostly clear, Somewhat confusing, No, very confusing).
4. Rate the usefulness of the current filter options (5 - Extremely Useful, 4, 3, 2, 1 - Not Useful).
5. If you did not see a filter you expected, describe it in the text box.
6. Rate the speed of results updating when a filter is applied (Instant, Acceptable, Slow, Extremely Slow/Crashed).
7. Rate the visual design of the filter menu (Professional and Clean, Functional but Boring, Cluttered/Hard to read).
8. Rate your overall satisfaction with the filtering experience (Highly Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied).
9. Share any improvement suggestions for the filtering system.
10. If interested, join our Beta Testing group and provide your email address.
11. Finally, submit your response by providing the current date.
