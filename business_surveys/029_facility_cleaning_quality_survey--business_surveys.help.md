# facility_cleaning_quality_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the quality of facility cleaning in your workplace. It's a self-assessment tool that helps facility managers and maintenance teams to determine the cleanliness of different areas, identify areas of improvement, and develop strategies to maintain a clean and safe environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and answer them truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. Answer each question based on your personal experience and observation.
3. Be as detailed as possible when providing comments or descriptions.
4. Use the provided options or select "No" or "Never" when answering questions.
5. Don't hesitate to add any additional comments or observations if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Introduction** (1): This is a descriptive page that explains the purpose of the form and how it will be used.
 
- **Location** (2): Describe the area(s) you are evaluating, including the location name or description.
 
- **Cleanliness** (3): Select "Yes" if the area is clean, and "No" if it's not. You can also add comments or descriptions to provide further information.
 
- **Rating** (4): Rate the cleanliness of the area from 1 to 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
 
- **Comment** (5): Add any comments or observations related to cleanliness.
 
- **Inspector** (6): Check "Yes" if an inspector was present during the evaluation, and "No" if not.
 
- **Staff** (7): Describe the facility cleaning staff's presence and efficiency.
 
- **Frequency** (8): Select the frequency of facility cleaning, such as "daily", "weekly", "monthly", or "never".
 
- **Date** (9): Provide the date of the evaluation.
 
- **Time** (10): Record the time of the evaluation.
 
- **Email** (11): Enter your email address or contact information.
 
- **Comments** (12): Add any additional comments or observations.
 
- **Signature** (13): Sign and date your name to confirm the evaluation.
 
- **Location Comments** (14): Provide any comments or observations related to the location.
 
- **Location** (15): Select "Yes" for areas that are clean, and "No" for areas that are not.
 
- **Area** (16): Check "Yes" if the area is clean, and "No" if it's not.
 
- **Section** (17): Describe the section or area being cleaned.
 
- **Section Comments** (18): Add any comments or observations related to the section or area.
 
- **Equipment** (19): Describe the facility cleaning equipment used.
 
- **Section Frequency** (20): Select the frequency of cleaning in the section or area.
 
- **Section Time** (21): Record the time spent cleaning the section or area.
 
- **Section Location** (22): Check "Yes" for areas that are clean, and "No" for areas that are not.
 
- **Section Comments** (23): Add any comments or observations related to the section or area.
 
- **Facility Cleaning** (24): Rate the overall facility cleaning quality from excellent to poor.
 
- **Facility Cleaning Comments** (25): Add any final comments or observations about the facility cleaning quality.
