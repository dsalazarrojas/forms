# property_contract_renewal_process_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and feedback from property owners or managers about their experience with the property contract renewal process. Your responses will help us improve the process and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions and questions carefully.
2. Answer each question based on your experience and understanding of the property contract renewal process.
3. If you're unsure about any question, ask for assistance or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Contract Renewal Process Survey Form** (`introduction`, `note`, `optional`): This is a brief introduction to the form and the purpose of the survey. Please read this carefully before proceeding.
* **Tenant Satisfaction** (`tenant_satisfaction`, `number`, `optional`): This question asks about your level of satisfaction with the current property contract renewal process. Please select a number from 1 to 10, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 10 is very satisfied.
* **Renewal Intent** (`renewal_intent`, `select_one`, `optional`): This question asks about your intention to renew the property contract. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1: Definitely renew the contract
	+ Option 2: Maybe renew the contract
	+ Option 3: Definitely not renew the contract
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, `optional`): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about the property contract renewal process.
* **Lease Expiration Date** (`lease_expiration_date`, `date`, `optional`): This question asks for the date of the lease expiration. Please select a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `note`, `optional`): This is a note field where you can provide any contact information or additional comments.

## Tips

* Please answer each question based on your genuine experience and understanding of the property contract renewal process.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the feedback field.
* If you're unsure about any question, ask for assistance or clarification.
* Please submit your completed form for review and analysis.
