# Business Vision Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Business Vision Assessment form is designed to gauge your level of understanding and alignment with your organization's vision, mission, and values. This form will help your organization's leadership and stakeholders understand your perceptions and attitudes towards the company's vision and goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your abilities.
2. Please provide clear and concise answers for text-based questions.
3. Select all that apply for questions with multiple options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents or business cards.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, `email`, required): Enter your official contact email address.
* **Company or Organization Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Your Role or Title** (`respondent_role`, `text`, required): Enter your role or title within the organization.
* **How Long Have You Been With This Organization?** (`tenure_years`, `number`, optional): If applicable, enter how many years you have been with the organization. If not, please leave this field blank.
* **Organizational Level** (`organizational_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your current level within the organization:
	+ Executive or C-suite
	+ Senior management
	+ Middle management
	+ Team lead or supervisor
	+ Individual contributor
	+ Other
* **How Clear Is The Organization Vision?** (`vision_clarity`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how clear the organization's vision is to you:
	+ Very clear
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat clear
	+ Unclear
	+ Very unclear
* **Is The Vision Effectively Communicated?** (`vision_communication`, `select_one`, required): Rate the effectiveness of the vision communication:
	+ Excellent communication
	+ Good communication
	+ Fair communication
	+ Poor communication
	+ No communication
* **Do You Understand The Organization Mission?** (`mission_understanding`, `select_one`, required): Rate your understanding of the organization's mission:
	+ Complete understanding
	+ Good understanding
	+ Fair understanding
	+ Poor understanding
	+ No understanding
* **How Well Do You Align With Organizational Values?** (`values_alignment`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how well you align with the organization's values:
	+ Highly aligned
	+ Aligned
	+ Somewhat aligned
	+ Poorly aligned
	+ Not aligned
* **Clarity of Strategic Goals** (`strategic_goals_clarity`, `select_one`, required): Rate the clarity of the strategic goals:
	+ Very clear
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat clear
	+ Unclear
	+ Very unclear
* **Do You Know Short-Term Priorities?** (`short_term_priorities`, `select_one`, required): Rate your knowledge of short-term priorities:
	+ Definitely yes
	+ Mostly yes
	+ Somewhat
	+ Not really
	+ Not at all
* **Does The Vision Inspire You To Do Your Best?** (`vision_inspiration`, `select_one`, required): Rate the level of inspiration you feel towards the company vision:
	+ Highly inspiring
	+ Inspiring
	+ Somewhat inspiring
	+ Not inspiring
	+ Demotivating
* **Do You Believe The Vision Is Achievable?** (`vision_believability`, `select_one`, required): Rate your confidence in the achievability of the vision:
	+ Very believable
	+ Believable
	+ Somewhat believable
	+ Questionable
	+ Not believable
* **Effectiveness Of The Strategy To Achieve Vision** (`strategy_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required): Rate the effectiveness of the strategy to achieve the vision:
	+ Very effective
	+ Effective
	+ Somewhat effective
	+ Not very effective
	+ Not effective
* **Gaps Between Vision And Current Reality** (`vision_gaps`, `text`, optional): If applicable, describe the gaps between the vision and current reality.
* **How Could The Vision Be Improved?** (`vision_improvements`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide suggestions for refining the vision.
* **Challenges To Vision Alignment** (`alignment_challenges`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the challenges you face in aligning with the vision:
	+ Unclear communication
	+ Lack of buy-in
	+ Resource constraints
	+ Misaligned incentives
	+ Conflicting priorities
	+ Leadership inconsistency
	+ Market changes
	+ No significant challenges
* **Your Commitment To The Vision** (`support_commitment`, `select_one`, required): Rate your level of commitment to the vision:
	+ Fully committed
	+ Committed
	+ Somewhat committed
	+ Not very committed
	+ Not committed
* **Additional Vision-Related Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the vision.
