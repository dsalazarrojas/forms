# Chapter Updates Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Chapter Updates Survey" form is designed to gather information from chapter members about their chapter's progress and status. This information is used by the chapter leadership and national or regional organization to monitor and support chapter growth, challenges, and financial health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the chapter identity section, which includes:
	* Member Identity
	* Member Full Name
	* Your Current Chapter Title
2. Provide contact information to receive updates:
	* Contact Email for Updates
3. Report your chapter's progress:
	* Most Recent Chapter Achievement
	* Status of Current Service Project
	* Chapter Financial Standing Self-Rate
	* Dues Collection Status Update
4. Confirm your chapter's status:
	* Accuracy Confirmation of Update Submission
	* Date of Submission
5. Add any additional comments or information:
	* Additional Comments or Information

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Identity** (`section_member_identity`, `text`, required):
	* This section is to confirm your identity as a chapter member.
* **Member Full Name** (`member_full_name_identity`, `text`, required):
	* Enter your full name for chapter records.
* **Your Current Chapter Title** (`member_current_chapter_title`, `text`, required):
	* Indicate your current chapter title or position.
* **Contact Email for Updates** (`contact_email_for_updates`, `email`, required):
	* Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Most Recent Chapter Achievement** (`most_recent_chapter_achievement`, `text`, required):
	* Briefly describe your chapter's most recent achievement or success.
* **Status of Current Service Project** (`status_of_current_service_project`, `select_one`, required):
	* Select the current status of your chapter's service project:
		+ Just Starting
		+ In Progress
		+ Nearing Completion
		+ Completed and Logged
		+ Planning Phase
* **Chapter Financial Standing Self-Rate** (`chapter_financial_standing_self_rate`, `number`, required):
	* Indicate your self-assessed chapter finances, on a 1-10 scale.
* **Dues Collection Status Update** (`dues_collection_status_update`, `select_one`, required):
	* Select the current status of dues collection:
		+ 100 percent Collected
		+ Over 75 percent Collected
		+ Over 50 percent Collected
		+ Less than 50 percent Collected
* **Chapter Meeting Frequency Update** (`chapter_meeting_frequency_update`, `select_one`, required):
	* Indicate if the meeting schedule has changed recently:
		+ No Change - Remaining Same
		+ Changing to Monthly
		+ Changing to Bi-Monthly
		+ Changing to Quarterly
* **Primary Chapter Challenge Currently** (`primary_chapter_challenge_currently`, `text`, required):
	* Identify the biggest obstacle your chapter is currently facing.
* **Support Needed from National or Regional** (`support_needed_from_national_regional`, `text`, optional):
	* Describe how the higher-level organization can support your chapter.
* **Chapter Financial Standing Self-Rate** (`chapter_financial_standing_self_rate`, `number`, required):
	* Indicate your self-assessed chapter finances, on a 1-10 scale.
* **Dues Collection Status Update** (`dues_collection_status_update`, `select_one`, required):
	* Select the current status of dues collection:
		+ 100 percent Collected
		+ Over 75 percent Collected
		+ Over 50 percent Collected
		+ Less than 50 percent Collected
* **Confirmation of Update Submission** (`accuracy_confirmation_member_update`, `select_one`, required):
	* Confirm that the updates are accurate and complete:
		+ I Confirm and Submit Update
		+ Other
* **Date of Submission** (`date_of_update_log_submission`, `date`, required):
	* Enter the date of your submission.
* **Additional Comments or Information** (`additional_comments_update`, `text`, optional):
	* Provide any additional comments or information about your chapter.
* **Accuracy Confirmation of Update Submission** (`accuracy_confirmation_member_update`, `select_one`, required):
	* Confirm that the updates are accurate and complete:
		+ I Confirm and Submit Update
		+ Other
