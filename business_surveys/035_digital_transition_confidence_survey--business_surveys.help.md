# digital_transition_confidence_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The digital transition confidence survey is a tool used to gauge the confidence and expectations of business users regarding a digital transition in your organization. The data collected from this form will be used by IT and business leadership to identify areas of improvement and inform future digital transformations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please read the introduction section for a brief overview of the purpose and scope of the digital transition.
2. Answer each section according to your level of confidence and expectation regarding the digital transition.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### This is an introduction (Section 1)
* **This is an introduction** (`text`, required: false): This section is a brief overview of the purpose and scope of the digital transition.

### Section 1 (Section 2)
* **Section 1** (`select_one`, required: false): Select one option that best describes your confidence in the organization's ability to adapt to the digital transition.

### Section 2 (Section 3)
* **Section 2** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select all options that apply to your expectations regarding the digital transition.

### What is your expected completion date for the digital transition? (Section 3)
* **Section 3** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date you expect the digital transition to be completed.

### What time of day do you expect the digital transition to be completed? (Section 4)
* **Section 4** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time of day you expect the digital transition to be completed.

### Please note any additional comments or concerns (Section 5)
* **Section 5** (`note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or concerns you may have regarding the digital transition.

### What is your email address? (Section 6)
* **Section 6** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you regarding any questions or updates.

### Do you have any additional information or feedback? (Section 7)
* **Section 7** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional information or feedback you may have regarding the digital transition.
