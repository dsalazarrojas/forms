# Leadership Communication Consistency Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate how well team leaders communicate with their team members, ensuring that everyone stays aligned and informed. The results will help us identify areas for improvement and create more positive work experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

- To begin, please select the correct team leader from the list on **team-leader** page.
- Next, answer the questions on each page to the best of your ability.
- For multiple-choice questions, please select all that apply.
- You can add any additional comments or notes on the **form-notes** page.
- Finally, enter your **email** and **phone number**, so we can contact you with your results and follow up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **team-leader**: Please select the team leader with whom you work most closely.
* **team-member-1-label**: Please select all options that apply to the leader's communication style:
	+ Option 1: Frequent, clear, and timely communication.
	+ Option 2: Some, but could be improved.
	+ Option 3: Rare or unclear communication.
* **team-member-2**: Provide any additional comments or insights on the leader's communication style. 
* **frequency-question-label**: How often does the leader check in with you about progress?
	+ Option 1: Daily.
	+ Option 2: Weekly.
	+ Option 3: Rarely.
* **question-4-1**: How would you rate the leader's ability to explain complex topics?
	+ Option 1: Excellent, always clear.
	+ Option 2: Some, but could be improved.
	+ Option 3: Poor, often confusing.
* **question-4-2**: How would you rate the leader's ability to follow up on commitments?
	+ Option 1: Excellent, always follow up.
	+ Option 2: Some, but could be improved.
	+ Option 3: Poor, rarely follow up.
* **question-5-1**: How would you rate the leader's communication style?
	+ Option 1: Frequent, clear, and timely communication.
	+ Option 2: Some, but could be improved.
	+ Option 3: Rare or unclear communication.
* **alignment-question-label**: How often does the leader ensure team alignment on goals and objectives?
	+ Option 1: Daily.
	+ Option 2: Weekly.
	+ Option 3: Rarely.
* **question-8-1**: How would you rate the leader's ability to listen actively?
	+ Option 1: Excellent, always listens.
	+ Option 2: Some, but could be improved.
	+ Option 3: Poor, rarely listens.
* **form-notes**: You can add any additional comments or insights about the leader's communication style.
* **email**: Enter your contact email so we can follow up with your results.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for any further discussions or follow-up questions.
