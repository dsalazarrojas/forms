# Smart City Real Estate Opinion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart City Real Estate Opinion Survey is designed to help us understand your level of satisfaction with our Smart City Real Estate services and gather your feedback on potential improvements. This survey is crucial in helping us improve our services and better serve our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your level of satisfaction with the Smart City Real Estate from the scale provided.
2. Choose which features are very or somewhat important to you from the list of options.
3. Provide open-ended comments on the desired improvements in the text field.
4. Scale the importance of different features on the "Smart City Real Estate Improvement Scale" section.
5. Enter a numeric value for the improvement scale (e.g., 1-10) in the corresponding field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Smart City Real Estate Satisfaction Level** (`1`, `select_one`, `required`): This section measures your current level of satisfaction with the Smart City Real Estate services. Please select the option that best describes your feelings.
* **Smart City Real Estate Features** (`2`, `select_multiple`, `not required`): This section lists various features of the Smart City Real Estate. Select all the features that are very or somewhat important to you.
* **Desired Improvements** (`3`, `text`, `not required`): This is an open-ended text field where you can provide any comments or suggestions on how we can improve our services.
* **Smart City Real Estate Improvement Scale** (`4`, `select_one`, `not required`): This section assesses how important you think certain aspects of the Smart City Real Estate are.
* **Smart City Real Estate Improvement Scale Value** (`5`, `number`, `not required`): This field is a numeric value corresponding to the importance of the features listed in the previous section.
* **Smart City Real Estate Improvements** (`6`, `text`, `not required`): This section is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Smart City Real Estate Improvement Scale 2** (`7`, `select_multiple`, `not required`): This is another section to scale the importance of features.
* **Smart City Real Estate Improvement Scale Value Scale** (`8`, `number`, `not required`): Similar to the previous section, this field is a numeric value corresponding to the importance of features.
* **Smart City Real Estate Improvement Scale Value 2** (`9`, `text`, `not required`): Another field for any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Open-ended Comments** (`10`, `text`, `not required`): This is an open-ended text field where you can provide any other feedback you may have.
