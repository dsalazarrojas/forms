# Vinegar Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your vinegar usage habits and preferences. It's intended to help us better understand your behavior and needs regarding vinegar purchases. Please answer the questions honestly, and only fill out the form if you have experience with vinegar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about your vinegar usage habits, including which type of vinegar you use, how often you use it, and where you purchase it.
2. Rate your satisfaction with the type of vinegar you purchased.
3. Provide details about any other brand or type of vinegar you usually purchase, if applicable.
4. Fill out the form with your preferred frequency of purchase and location of purchase for other types of vinegar.
5. Indicate the reasons why you purchase other types of vinegar.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Which type of vinegar do you use?** (`vinegar_types`, select_one)
* This question asks you to select the type of vinegar you commonly use. Please choose one of the options: apple cider, balsamic, balsamic vinegar, white wine, or white vinegar.

### 2. **How often do you use vinegar?** (`usage_habits`, select_multiple)
* This question asks you to select how often you use vinegar. You can choose one or more of the options: every day, every other day, rarely, or never.

### 3. **Where do you purchase vinegar?** (`purchase_locations`, select_multiple)
* This question asks you to select where you usually purchase vinegar. You can choose one or more of the options: online, grocery store, health food store, or specialty food store.

### 4. **How satisfied are you with the type of vinegar you purchased?** (`satisfaction`, select_one)
* This question asks you to rate your level of satisfaction with the type of vinegar you usually purchase. Please select one of the options: very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, not satisfied, neutral, or dissatisfied.

### 5. **How often do you purchase vinegar?** (`purchase_frequency`, select_one)
* This question asks you to select how often you purchase vinegar. Please choose one of the options: very often, sometimes, rarely, or never.

### 6. **What brand do you usually purchase?** (`brand_purchased`, text)
* This question asks you to enter the brand of vinegar you usually buy, if you have one. You can type a brand name or a generic description.

### 7. **Is there another brand you usually purchase?** (`brand_purchased_other`, text)
* If you have another brand in your regular rotation, this question allows you to list it.

### 8. **What is the value of the vinegar you usually purchase?** (`purchase_value`, number)
* This question asks for the price range or value of the vinegar you usually buy. Please enter a number.

### 9. **How often do you purchase other types of vinegar?** (`purchase_frequency_other`, select_multiple)
* This question asks you to select how often you purchase other types of vinegar. You can choose one or more of the options: very often, sometimes, rarely, or never.

### 10. **Where do you purchase other types of vinegar?** (`purchase_location_other`, select_multiple)
* This question asks you to select where you usually purchase other types of vinegar. Please choose one or more of the options: online, grocery store, health food store, or specialty food store.

### 11. **Why do you purchase other types of vinegar?** (`purchase_reason`, select_multiple)
* This question asks you to select the reasons you purchase other types of vinegar. You can choose one or more of the options: for culinary use, for health reasons, for other reasons, for specialty use, or for other reasons.

Please fill out the form honestly, and thank you for your time!
