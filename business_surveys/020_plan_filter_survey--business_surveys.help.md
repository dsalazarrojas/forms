# Plan Filter Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Plan Filter Survey" form is designed to collect information for planning purposes. It's not intended for real-time data collection but rather for gathering data that will be used to filter and prioritize plans. Providing accurate and relevant information will help ensure that your responses are useful for planning and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the "Plan Filter Survey" form efficiently and accurately, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you're providing relevant information.
2. Fill out each page with the requested information, taking into account any required fields.
3. Use the options available to select the best response for each field.
4. Double-check your responses to ensure accuracy and relevance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First page** (`first_page`, text, optional): This page is a free-text field where you can provide any additional information that's relevant to your planning needs.
* **Second Page** (`second_page`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options provided to indicate your primary preference or choice.
* **Third page** (`third_page`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to your preferences or choices.
* **Fourth page** (`fourth_page`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options provided to indicate your primary preference or choice.
* **Fifth page** (`fifth_page`, time, optional): This field is for recording a specific time or duration. If you're unsure, you can leave it blank.
* **Sixth page** (`sixth_page`, date, optional): This field is for recording a specific date. If you're unsure, you can leave it blank.
* **Seventh page** (`seventh_page`, number, optional): This field is for recording a numerical value. If you're unsure, you can leave it blank.
* **Eighth page** (`eighth_page`, text, optional): This page is another free-text field where you can provide any additional information that's relevant to your planning needs.

## Tips

* Double-check your responses to ensure accuracy and relevance.
* Use the options available to select the best response for each field.
* If you're unsure about any field, it's okay to leave it blank.
* Review each page carefully to ensure you're providing the most accurate and useful information for planning purposes.
