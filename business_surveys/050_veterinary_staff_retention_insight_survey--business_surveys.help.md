# Veterinary Staff Retention Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help veterinary staff provide honest feedback on their reasons for staying or leaving the practice, and suggestions for improvement to increase staff retention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each page and question carefully.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Be honest and provide as much detail as you are comfortable sharing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Satisfaction** (`1`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with your job as a staff member. Please answer on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Reasons For Staying** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Provide reasons why you are satisfied with your role or the practice. This could include any positive experiences, benefits, or aspects of your work that you enjoy.
* **Reasons For Leaving** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Provide reasons why you are not satisfied with your role or the practice. This could include any challenges, difficulties, or concerns that you have experienced.
* **Staff Retention Ideas** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Suggest any ideas or initiatives that you think would help improve staff retention and job satisfaction.
* **Suggestions For Improvement** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Provide any suggestions you have for improving the practice, including processes, policies, or work environment.
* **Manager Support** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your level of agreement with the level of support you receive from your manager. Please answer on a scale of 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree).
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`7`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with your job on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Work Life Balance** (`8`, `number`, required: false): Rate your balance between your work and personal life on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Unbalanced" and 5 is "Very Balanced".

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and as accurately as possible.
* Remember, this form is for your own benefit, so don't worry about sharing your concerns or opinions.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
