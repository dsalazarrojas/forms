# home_phone_fundraising_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The home_phone_fundraising_survey form is designed to collect information about your home phone fundraising experience. This form is used to gather data that will help us improve our services and better understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and submit it. You can navigate through the pages using the next and previous buttons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **required**): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **required**): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, **required**): Please enter your phone number.
* **How Long** (`how_long`, number, **required**): Please select the number of minutes you spent on the phone call.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, text, **required**): Please select the frequency of your home phone fundraising calls.
* **Concerns** (`concerns`, select_multiple, **optional**): If you have any concerns, please select them from the list.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, **optional**): Please provide any additional comments about your experience.
