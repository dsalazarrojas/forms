# eco_friendly_product_preference_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your shopping habits, preferences, and behaviors related to eco-friendly products. Your input will help us improve our products and services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Select all applicable options for the "sustainable shopping habits" question.
3. Choose one option for the "decision drivers" question.
4. Enter your email address in the "contact info" field (if you have any additional comments, you can optionally provide it).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Do you practice sustainable shopping habits?** (`sustainable_shopping_habits`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about your shopping habits in relation to eco-friendly products. Please select all that apply:
	+ Sustainable products: Products that are environmentally friendly and sustainable.
	+ Eco-friendly products: Products that are environmentally friendly but may not be sustainable.
	+ Locally sourced products: Products that are sourced locally.
	+ Certified products: Products that have been certified as eco-friendly.
* **How sensitive are you to price?** (`pricing_sensitivity`, `number`, required): This question asks about your price sensitivity in relation to eco-friendly products. Please enter a number between 1 and 10, where 1 means you are very sensitive to price and 10 means you are not sensitive to price at all.
* **What drives your purchasing decision?** (`decision_drivers`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about what drives your purchasing decision when buying eco-friendly products. Please choose one of the following:
	+ Quality
	+ Price
	+ Brand
	+ Reviews
* **Contact info** (`contact_info`, `email`, optional): If you have any contact information (email), please enter it here. This will help us reach out to you with any follow-up questions or offers.
* **Any additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or thoughts about your shopping habits or preferences, please enter them here.

## Tips
* Please answer all required questions honestly and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* This form is a one-time survey, and you will not receive any follow-up forms or emails.
