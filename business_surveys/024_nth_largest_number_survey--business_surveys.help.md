# nth_largest_number_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report the accuracy of the numbers provided in the survey on the nth largest number. The goal is to collect feedback on the confidence of the user in their response, which will help improve the quality of the survey questions and data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the survey questions and answer them honestly.
2. Fill out the form with the required information.
3. Provide a confidence rating for your response.
4. Select the methods used to calculate the nth largest number.
5. Indicate whether you believe the numbers provided are accurate or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, `text`, optional):
	+ This field is used to provide a title for your response.
	+ Please enter a brief description of your response.
* **Number Lists** (`number_lists`, `text`, optional):
	+ In this field, list the numbers you reported for the nth largest survey.
	+ Separate each number with a comma.
* **Nth Largest** (`nth_largest`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you are certain about the accuracy of the numbers reported.
	+ Select 'No' if you are not confident about the accuracy of the numbers reported.
* **Methods Used** (`methods_used`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select all methods that apply to how you calculated the nth largest number.
	+ Please choose from the options provided.
* **Confidence Rating** (`confidence_rating`, `number`, optional):
	+ Rate your confidence in the accuracy of your response.
	+ The scale ranges from 0 to 100, where 0 means you are not confident at all and 100 means you are very confident.
* **Confidence Rating 2** (`confidence_rating_2`, `number`, optional):
	+ (This field is currently not being used. It can be ignored.)

Note: The Confidence Rating 2 field is not being used in the form, so you can ignore it.
