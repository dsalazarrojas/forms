# customer_retention_metrics_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer_retention_metrics_monitoring_form is a survey designed to monitor and analyze customer retention metrics, including retention rates, customer satisfaction, and other relevant metrics. This information helps businesses understand customer loyalty and behavior, enabling informed decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. For fields with options, choose the relevant option that best describes your situation.
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Retention Rates**: Enter your current customer retention rate as a percentage.
* **Customer Satisfaction**: Rate your customer satisfaction with the options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Net Promoter Score**: Enter your current net promoter score.
* **Customer Acquisition Costs**: Enter the costs associated with acquiring customers.
* **Customer Retention Costs**: Enter the costs associated with retaining customers.
* **Customer Satisfaction Costs**: Enter the costs associated with customer satisfaction.
* **Retention Rates**: Enter your current retention rates.
* **Customer Segment**: Select the customer segment that best describes your customers (e.g., Segment 1, Segment 2, Segment 3).
* **Customer Segment Other**: If your customer segment is not listed, describe it here.
* **Customer Segment Other Description**: Provide a brief description of your customer segment.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Acquisition Channel**: Select the channel through which you acquired your customers (e.g., Online, Offline, Social Media, Referral, Other).
* **Customer Referral Source**: If you acquired your customers through referrals, describe the source here.
* **Customer Referral Source Description**: Provide a brief description of the source of the referrals.
* **Additional Metric**: If you have any additional metrics you'd like to share, enter them here.
