**Crime Control Support Fund Survey - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information on individual support or non-support for the Crime Control Support Fund. Your feedback and opinions are invaluable in helping us understand community sentiment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what information we are seeking.
2. Provide honest and accurate information in all fields.
3. If a field is not relevant to you, you can leave it blank or indicate that you do not wish to provide information on that topic.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional)
* This section is for you to provide your contact information, in case we need to follow up with you regarding your survey submission.

### **Support Levels** (`support_levels`, number, optional)
* This field allows you to indicate on a scale of 1 to 10 how strongly you support or do not support the Crime Control Support Fund.

### **Engagement Preferences** (`engagement_preferences`, text, optional)
* This section is for you to provide information on how you would like to be engaged regarding updates and information on the Crime Control Support Fund.

### **Do you support the Crime Control Support Fund?** (`support_fund`, select_one, optional)
* This question is to gather your direct stance on supporting the Crime Control Support Fund. You can select "True", "False", or "Undecided" based on your opinion.

### **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
* This field is for you to provide your email address, in case we need to contact you regarding your survey submission.

### **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
* This field is for you to provide your phone number, in case we need to contact you regarding your survey submission.

### **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional)
* This section is for you to provide any additional comments or thoughts you have on the Crime Control Support Fund.

**Tips**
* Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you are unsure of any information, it's okay to indicate that you do not wish to provide it.
* We value your feedback and opinions, and your responses will be kept confidential.
