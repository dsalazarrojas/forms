# Routing Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users about their experience with our routing system. This feedback will help us identify areas for improvement and make our system more efficient and user-friendly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions and select the options that best describe your experience with our routing system.
2. Provide any additional feedback or comments in the text fields.
3. Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
4. Confirm your email address and phone number for verification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Routing Feedback** (`routing_feedback_survey`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is where you can describe your overall experience with our routing system.
* **Experience** (`experience`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Please select all the options that best describe your experience with our system:
		- Very Satisfied
		- Somewhat Satisfied
		- Neutral
		- Somewhat Dissatisfied
		- Very Dissatisfied
* **What is your experience with our routing system?** (`feedback_question_1`, text, required: false)
	+ Please describe your experience with our system in a few sentences.
* **Our system's navigation efficiency** (`routing_system_1`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select an option that best describes your experience with our system's navigation:
		- Very Efficient
		- Somewhat Efficient
		- Neutral
		- Somewhat Inefficient
		- Very Inefficient
* **Can our system help you reach your destination?** (`feedback_question_2`, text, required: false)
	+ Please describe how our system helped or hindered your ability to reach your destination.
* **Our system's information quality** (`routing_system_2`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select an option that best describes your experience with our system's information quality:
		- Highly Accurate
		- Somewhat Accurate
		- Neutral
		- Somewhat Inaccurate
		- Highly Inaccurate
* **Additional feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, required: false)
	+ Please share any additional comments or suggestions you have about our routing system.
* **What did we do well?** (`routing_feedback_survey_question_1`, text, required: false)
	+ Please describe something our system did well.
* **How did we do poorly?** (`routing_feedback_survey_question_2`, text, required: false)
	+ Please describe something our system did poorly.
* **Any other comments?** (`routing_feedback_survey_question_3`, text, required: false)
	+ Please share any other comments or suggestions you have about our system.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false)
	+ Please enter your contact information, including your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
	+ Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false)
	+ Please enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Please confirm your email address:
		- Is correct
		- Is not correct
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Please confirm your phone number:
		- Is correct
		- Is not correct

Note: This help guide is intended to be a general explanation of the form and its fields. Some fields may have specific instructions or requirements not covered in this guide. If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
