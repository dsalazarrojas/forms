# Group And Role Information Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a group and its members. It's used to gather data on the group's details, member list, role assignments, member tenure, and role responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Group Details section with the required information about the group.
2. Select the correct answer for Member List, indicating whether the group has members or not.
3. For Role Assignments, choose the roles that are assigned to this group.
4. Enter the member tenure (duration of membership) in the Member Tenure field.
5. In the Role Responsibilities field, describe the specific responsibilities assigned to this group's role.
6. Finally, enter the role tenure (duration of the role assignment) in the Role Tenure field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Group Details**: This section is for entering the group's details such as name, description, or any other identifying information.
  * Type: text input field
  * Required: false
  * Hint: Enter the group's name, description, or any other relevant details.
- **Member List**: This question asks whether the group has members or not.
  * Type: select multiple-choice field
  * Required: false
  * Options: 'Yes', 'No'
- **Role Assignments**: This field allows you to select the roles assigned to this group.
  * Type: select one field
  * Required: false
  * Options: 'Yes', 'No'
- **Member Tenure**: This field asks for the duration of a member's membership in the group.
  * Type: number input field
  * Required: false
  * Hint: Enter the member's tenure in months or years.
- **Role Responsibilities**: This field is for describing the specific responsibilities assigned to this group's role.
  * Type: text input field
  * Required: false
  * Hint: Enter a brief description of the role's responsibilities.
- **Role Tenure**: This field asks for the duration of the role assignment.
  * Type: number input field
  * Required: false
  * Hint: Enter the role's tenure in months or years.
