# market_research_survey_for_study_spaces - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect market research data for a study on study spaces, gathering information on user demographics, preferences, and habits related to study spaces.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and fields.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
3. For select-one and select-multiple fields, choose the most accurate options.
4. Enter text fields as instructed in the hints.
5. Date and time fields should be filled in the required formats (dd/mm/yyyy and hh:mm AM/PM).
