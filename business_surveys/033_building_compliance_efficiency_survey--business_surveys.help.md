# Building Compliance Efficiency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to assess the current state of compliance efficiency within your organization. It gathers information on various aspects of compliance processes, tools, and communication to identify areas for improvement and develop strategies for increasing efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your name and department to help us identify your role and department.
2. Answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Select the most accurate response for each question.
4. Add any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" section.
