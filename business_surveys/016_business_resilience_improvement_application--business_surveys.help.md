# Business Resilience Improvement Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Resilience Improvement Application is a comprehensive form designed to help businesses identify areas for improvement and gather information for potential support or assistance. This form collects detailed information on business details, challenges, and needs to determine the level of support required and the expected timeline for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the business name, registration number, and contact person's name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the primary industry sector of your business.
3. Enter the total workforce size and approximate annual revenue.
4. Choose the primary improvement objectives for your business.
5. Describe your main resilience challenges and any previous disruptions or crises experienced.
6. Indicate the urgency of your improvement needs.
7. Provide the budget available for improvements, if applicable.
8. Select the desired improvement timeline.
9. Specify the types of support needed for improvement.
10. Identify the key decision maker involved in this application.
11. Indicate if you have received external support before.
12. Describe how you will measure success.
13. Provide the date of this application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Business Name (Required)
**Business Name**: Enter the official name of your business.

### 2. Business Registration Number (Required)
**Business Registration Number**: Provide your government-issued registration number.

### 3. Contact Person Name (Required)
**Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the primary contact person for this application.

### 4. Contact Email (Required)
**Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the primary contact person.

### 5. Contact Phone Number (Required)
**Contact Phone Number**: Enter the primary phone number of the contact person.

### 6. Business Industry (Required)
**Business Industry**: Select the primary industry sector of your business.

### 7. Employee Count (Required)
**Employee Count**: Enter the total number of employees in your business.

### 8. Annual Turnover (Optional)
**Annual Turnover**: Enter your approximate annual revenue, if applicable.

### 9. Improvement Objectives (Required)
**Improvement Objectives**: Select the primary improvement objectives for your business.

### 10. Current Resilience Challenges (Required)
**Current Resilience Challenges**: Describe your main resilience challenges.

### 11. Previous Disruptions or Crises (Optional)
**Previous Disruptions or Crises**: If you have experienced disruptions or crises, describe them.

### 12. Urgency of Improvement Needs (Required)
**Urgency of Improvement Needs**: Indicate how urgent your improvement needs are.

### 13. Budget Available (Optional)
**Budget Available for Improvements**: If you have a budget for improvement, enter it.

### 14. Desired Improvement Timeline (Required)
**Desired Improvement Timeline**: Select your desired improvement timeline.

### 15. Types of Support Needed (Required)
**Types of Support Needed**: Specify the types of support you need for improvement.

### 16. Key Decision Maker (Required)
**Key Decision Maker Involved**: Identify the key decision maker involved in this application.

### 17. Previous Support or Assessments (Optional)
**Previous Support or Assessments**: If you have received external support before, describe it.

### 18. Success Metrics or Goals (Required)
**Success Metrics or Goals**: Describe how you will measure success.

### 19. Application Submission Date (Required)
**Application Submission Date**: Enter the date of this application.

**Tips**

- Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to ensure a smooth review process.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the support team for assistance.
- The provided information will be used to assess your business's resilience needs and provide relevant support.
