# Digital Donation Behavior Study - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Donation Behavior Study is a form designed to collect information about your donation behavior. This form aims to gather insights on what motivates individuals to donate, their preferred frequency of giving, and other details about their donation experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the questions on each page and answer them accordingly.
2.  Ensure that you complete all required fields, as indicated by the "required" label in each field.
3.  If you have any questions or need help with a particular field, feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What motivates you to donate (Introduction Questions)** (`What motivates you to donate`, select_multiple, required): This question asks about the primary reasons why you donate. Please select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Frequency of Giving (Frequency of Giving)** (`Frequency of Giving`, number, required): This field asks about your donation frequency. Enter a numerical value to indicate how often you donate.
* **Amount of Donation (Amount of Donation)** (`Amount of Donation`, number, required): This field asks about the amount you typically donate. Enter a numerical value to indicate the amount you usually give.
* **Online Giving Preference (Online Giving Preference)** (`Online Giving Preference`, select_one, required): This field asks about your preferred method of giving. Select the option that best represents your preference.
* **Social Media Influence (Social Media Influence)** (`Social Media Influence`, select_multiple, required): This question asks about the influence of social media on your donation decisions. Please select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Email Inquiry (Email Inquiry)** (`Email Inquiry`, email, required): This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Phone Inquiry (Phone Inquiry)** (`Phone Inquiry`, text, required): This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Date of Donation (Date of Donation)** (`Date of Donation`, date, required): This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Time of Donation (Time of Donation)** (`Time of Donation`, time, required): This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Frequency of Donation Time (Frequency of Donation Time)** (`Frequency of Donation Time`, select_multiple, required): This field asks about the time of day when you usually donate. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Amount of Donation Amount (Amount of Donation Amount)** (`Amount of Donation Amount`, number, required): This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Amount of Donation Converted (Amount of Donation Converted)** (`Amount of Donation Converted`, number, required): This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Amount of Donation Currency (Amount of Donation Currency)** (`Amount of Donation Currency`, select_one, required): This field asks about the currency of your donation. Select the option that best represents your currency.
* **Amount of Donation Time (Amount of Donation Time)** (`Amount of Donation Time`, time, required): This field is not applicable for this form.
