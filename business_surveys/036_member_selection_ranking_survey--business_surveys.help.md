# Member Selection Ranking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Member Selection Ranking Survey is used to gather user preferences and opinions on which members are of most importance or value to them. Users are asked to select up to three members and assign a ranking number to each selected member.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select up to three members from the dropdown list.
2. Assign a ranking number to each selected member.
3. Click the "Submit" button to confirm your submission.
4. If you have any additional comments or notes, use the "Any Additional Notes" field to enter them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Select Ranking
* **Select Ranking** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select up to three members from the provided options.
### Select Member 1
* **Select Member 1** (`text`, optional): Enter the name or identifier of the first member you want to select.
### Ranking for Member 1
* **Ranking for Member 1** (`number`, optional): Assign a ranking number to the selected member, with 1 being the highest and 10 being the lowest.
### Select Member 2
* **Select Member 2** (`text`, optional): Enter the name or identifier of the second member you want to select.
### Ranking for Member 2
* **Ranking for Member 2** (`number`, optional): Assign a ranking number to the selected member, with 1 being the highest and 10 being the lowest.
### Select Member 3
* **Select Member 3** (`text`, optional): Enter the name or identifier of the third member you want to select.
### Ranking for Member 3
* **Ranking for Member 3** (`number`, optional): Assign a ranking number to the selected member, with 1 being the highest and 10 being the lowest.
### Any Additional Notes
* **Any Additional Notes** (`note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your selection or ranking.
### Submit
* **Submit** (`text`, optional): Click to confirm your submission and complete the form.
### Confirm Submission
* **Confirm Submission** (`text`, optional): A confirmation message after clicking the "Submit" button.
