# Statement Ranking Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect user feedback and opinions on various statements. It is designed to gather information about your preferences, habits, and demographics. The survey will ask you to select your favorite options from a list of statements, which will help us understand your interests and behaviors. Please answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please follow these steps:
1. Review the statements presented to you.
2. Select your preferred options for each statement.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, but you don't have to answer them all if they are marked as "optional".
4. Enter your contact information, if requested.
5. Submit your survey once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select your favorite food** (statement_1, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your favorite food from the options provided.
* **Select your favorite color** (statement_2, select_one, required: false): Pick one color that you like the most.
* **Age** (statement_3, number, required: false): Please enter your age.
* **Select your job title** (statement_4, text, required: false): Enter your current job title or position.
* **Select your height** (statement_5, number, required: false): Enter your height in inches.
* **Select your contact information** (statement_6, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more from the options to provide your contact information.
* **Select your work time** (statement_7, time, required: false): Please enter your regular work hours in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00).
* **Hours slept** (statement_8, number, required: false): Enter the number of hours you sleep per day.
* **Select your phone number** (statement_9, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Select your address** (statement_10, text, required: false): Enter your current address.
* **Birthday** (statement_11, date, required: false): Please enter your birthdate in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Select your preference** (statement_12, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more from the options to express your preference.
* **Statement 16** (statement_13, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Select your lunch time** (statement_14, number, required: false): Enter the time you usually eat lunch in 24-hour format (e.g., 12:00).
* **Select your break time** (statement_15, number, required: false): Enter the time you usually take a break in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
* **Select your finish work time** (statement_16, time, required: false): Enter the time you finish work in 24-hour format (e.g., 17:00).
