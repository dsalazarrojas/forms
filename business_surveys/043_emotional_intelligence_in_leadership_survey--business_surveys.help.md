# Emotional Intelligence In Leadership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emotional Intelligence In Leadership Survey is designed to measure the level of emotional intelligence in leadership among employees in an organization. It assesses key components of emotional intelligence, including self-perception, self-monitoring, self-control, personal motivation, role modeling, leadership, teamwork, communication, problem-solving, adaptability, and other skills essential for effective leadership.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and choose the best answer that reflects your thoughts and feelings.
2. For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose all that apply.
3. For text questions, type your response in the provided text box.
4. For email and phone questions, enter your email address and phone number, respectively.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
