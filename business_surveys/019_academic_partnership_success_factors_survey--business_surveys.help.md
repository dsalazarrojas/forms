# Academic Partnership Success Factors Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback from academic partnership participants on their experiences and perceptions of the partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please read each question carefully and provide your honest and detailed responses. You can select one option for multiple-choice questions and can provide multiple answers for questions that ask for multiple selections. If you have any suggestions or comments, please use the "Improvement Suggestions" field to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Academic Partnership Success Factors Survey** (`title`, `note`, `object`): This is the title of the survey.
* **Your Role in the Partnership** (`respondent_role`, `select_one`, `required`): Please select one of the options that best describes your role in the partnership. This will help us understand your perspective and involvement in the partnership.
* **Partnership Duration** (`partnership_duration`, `select_one`, `required`): Indicate how long the partnership has been in existence.
* **Type of Partnership** (`partnership_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the type of partnership that best describes your collaboration.
* **Communication Effectiveness Rating** (`communication_effectiveness_rating`, `number`, `required`): Rate the effectiveness of communication between partners on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not effective at all" and 5 is "very effective".
* **Goal Alignment Rating** (`goal_alignment_rating`, `number`, `required`): Rate how well the partnership goals align between partners on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not at all aligned" and 5 is "very well aligned".
* **Resource Sharing Rating** (`resource_sharing_rating`, `number`, `required`): Rate the effectiveness of resource sharing between partners on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not at all effective" and 5 is "very effective".
* **Decision Making Process Rating** (`decision_making_process_rating`, `number`, `required`): Rate the fairness and effectiveness of the decision-making process on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not at all fair" and 5 is "very fair and effective".
* **Outcome Quality Rating** (`outcome_quality_rating`, `number`, `required`): Rate your satisfaction with the outcomes achieved on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not satisfied at all" and 5 is "very satisfied".
* **Mutual Benefits Rating** (`mutual_benefits_rating`, `number`, `required`): Rate how well the partnership benefits all parties involved on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not at all beneficial" and 5 is "very beneficial".
* **Trust Level Rating** (`trust_level_rating`, `number`, `required`): Rate your level of trust in your partnership counterparts on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not at all trusted" and 5 is "very trusted".
* **Conflict Resolution Rating** (`conflict_resolution_rating`, `number`, `required`): Rate how effectively conflicts are resolved on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not at all effectively" and 5 is "very effectively".
* **Key Success Factors** (`partnership_success_factors`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select up to three factors that you consider the most important for the partnership.
* **Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all that apply to the challenges you have faced in the partnership.
* **Sustainability Confidence** (`sustainability_confidence`, `number`, `required`): Rate your confidence in the long-term sustainability of the partnership on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not confident at all" and 5 is "very confident".
* **Recommendation Likelihood** (`recommendation_likelihood`, `number`, `required`): Rate how likely you are to recommend this partnership model to others on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not at all likely" and 5 is "very likely".
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, `required`): Provide any suggestions or comments on how to improve the partnership.
