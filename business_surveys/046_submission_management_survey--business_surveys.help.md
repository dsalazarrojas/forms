# Submission Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with the submission management process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your honest feedback about your experience with the submission management process.
2. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability, but feel free to skip any questions that are not relevant to your experience.
3. If you have any improvement ideas or comments, please provide them in the "Comments" field.
4. If you have a submission status update, please select "Completed" in the "Submission Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission Management Survey Form** (`submission_management_survey_form`): This field is the title of the form. Please ignore and do not fill it in.
* **Submission Process** (`submission_management_survey_form_submission_process`): This field asks you to describe your experience with the submission process.
* **Satisfaction** (`submission_management_survey_form_satisfaction`): This field asks you to rate your satisfaction with the submission process on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Dissatisfied".
* **Satisfaction 2** (`submission_management_survey_form_satisfaction_2`): This field is a duplicate of the "Satisfaction" field, and you can ignore it.
* **Ease of Use** (`submission_management_survey_form_ease_of_use`): This field asks you to provide feedback on how easy or difficult it was to complete the submission process.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`submission_management_survey_form_improvement_ideas`): This field is for you to provide any ideas you have on how to improve the submission management process.
* **Satisfaction 3** (`submission_management_survey_form_satisfaction_3`): Another duplicate field, please ignore it.
* **Email Address** (`submission_management_survey_form_email_address`): If you want to be contacted for further information or follow-up questions, please enter your email address.
* **Username** (`submission_management_survey_form_username`): This field is not required and can be left blank.
* **Phone Number** (`submission_management_survey_form_phone_number`): If you have any other way to be contacted, please enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (`submission_management_survey_form_comments`): Please provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the submission management process.
* **Date** (`submission_management_survey_form_date`): Please enter the date you completed the submission process.
* **Time** (`submission_management_survey_form_time`): Please enter the time you completed the submission process.
* **Note** (`submission_management_survey_form_note`): This field is for any additional notes you have about the submission process.
* **Date 2** (`submission_management_survey_form_date_2`): Another date field, please ignore it.
* **Time 2** (`submission_management_survey_form_time_2`): Another time field, please ignore it.
* **Date 3** (`submission_management_survey_form_date_3`): Yet another date field, please ignore it.
* **Time 3** (`submission_management_survey_form_time_3`): Another time field, please ignore it.
* **Date 4** (`submission_management_survey_form_date_4`): The last date field, please ignore it.
* **Time 4** (`submission_management_survey_form_time_4`): The last time field, please ignore it.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_management_survey_form_submission_status`): This field asks you to select the status of your submission.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_management_survey_form_submission_comments`): This field is for you to provide any comments about your submission.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_management_survey_form_submission_date`): The date you submitted your form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_management_survey_form_submission_time`): The time you submitted your form.
* **Submitter** (`submission_management_survey_form_submitter`): This field is not required and can be left blank.

Note: This help guide is subject to change as the form evolves. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact the form administrator.
