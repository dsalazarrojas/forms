# Enablement Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enablement Insight Survey form is designed to gather information on various aspects of enablement programs, helping to inform business decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information and resources.
2. Select the correct options for each field, providing truthful and detailed information.
3. Fill out the form accurately, paying close attention to required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Enablement Programs
* **Enablement Programs** (Select Multiple, `enablement_programs`, Not Required): Choose the relevant programs for this survey.
### 2. Category
* **Category** (Select One, `category`, Not Required): Select a category that best describes this program.
### 3. Program Name
* **Program Name** (Text, `program_name`, Not Required): Enter the name of the program.
### 4. Description
* **Description** (Text, `description`, Not Required): Provide a brief description of the program.
### 5. Goals And Objectives
* **Goals And Objectives** (Text, `goals_and_objectives`, Not Required): Enter the goals and objectives of the program.
### 6. Current Status
* **Current Status** (Select One, `current_status`, Not Required): Choose the current status of the program.
### 7. Expected Completion Date
* **Expected Completion Date** (Date, `expected_completion_date`, Not Required): Enter the expected completion date of the program.
### 8. Actual Completion Date
* **Actual Completion Date** (Date, `actual_completion_date`, Not Required): Enter the actual completion date of the program.
### 9. Enablement Resources
* **Enablement Resources** (Select Multiple, `enablement_resources`, Not Required): Choose whether enablement resources were used.
### 10. Enablement Outcomes
* **Enablement Outcomes** (Select Multiple, `enablement_outcomes`, Not Required): Choose the outcomes of the enablement program.
### 11. Enablement Metrics
* **Enablement Metrics** (Select Multiple, `enablement_metrics`, Not Required): Choose whether enablement metrics were used.
### 12. Enablement Impact
* **Enablement Impact** (Select Multiple, `enablement_impact`, Not Required): Choose the impact of the enablement program.
### 13. Program Owner
* **Program Owner** (Text, `program_owner`, Not Required): Enter the program owner's name.
### 14. Enablement Owner Response
* **Enablement Owner Response** (Text, `enablement_owner_response`, Not Required): Enter the enablement owner's response.
### 15. Business Stakeholders
* **Business Stakeholders** (Select Multiple, `business_stakeholders`, Not Required): Choose the business stakeholders involved.
### 16. Business Stakeholders Response
* **Business Stakeholders Response** (Text, `business_stakeholders_response`, Not Required): Enter the business stakeholders' response.
### 17. Business Outcomes
* **Business Outcomes** (Select Multiple, `business_outcomes`, Not Required): Choose the business outcomes.
### 18. Business Metrics
* **Business Metrics** (Select Multiple, `business_metrics`, Not Required): Choose the business metrics used.
### 19. Business Impact
* **Business Impact** (Select Multiple, `business_impact`, Not Required): Choose the business impact.
### 20. Program Participants
* **Program Participants** (Select Multiple, `program_participants`, Not Required): Choose whether participants were involved in the program.
### 21. Program Participants Response
* **Program Participants Response** (Text, `program_participants_response`, Not Required): Enter the program participants' response.
### 22. Enablement Participants
* **Enablement Participants** (Select Multiple, `enablement_participants`, Not Required): Choose whether enablement participants were involved.
### 23. Enablement Participants Response
* **Enablement Participants Response** (Text, `enablement_participants_response`, Not Required): Enter the enablement participants' response.
### 24. Business Stakeholders Participants
* **Business Stakeholders Participants** (Select Multiple, `business_stakeholders_participants`, Not Required): Choose whether business stakeholders participated.
### 25. Enablement Stakeholders Participants
* **Enablement Stakeholders Participants** (Select Multiple, `enablement_stakeholders_participants`, Not Required): Choose whether enablement stakeholders participated.
