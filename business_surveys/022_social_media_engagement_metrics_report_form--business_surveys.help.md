# Social Media Engagement Metrics Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on social media engagement metrics for a specific platform and time range. It helps users to gather insights on the performance of their social media presence and identify areas for improvement. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the **Social Media Platform** from the options provided. This will determine the specific metrics you will be asked about.
2.  Choose the **Time Range** for which you want to gather engagement metrics. This could be a specific date or a more extensive time period.
3.  Select the **Frequency** at which you'd like to gather metrics for, which could be **Hourly, Daily, Weekly, Monthly** for more granular data.
4.  Choose which **Engagement Platforms** to include in your metrics report, which could be **Posts, Stories, Live Streaming** or more.
5.  Select which **Engagement Metrics** to display in your report, this could be **Post Reach, Engagement Rate, Comment Count, Post Click-Through Rate, Sentiment Analysis, Audience Growth Rate, Top Engagement Content, Hashtags Used, Engagement Index**.
6.  Select the **Metric Interval** you'd like to gather metrics for, which could be **Week, Month, Quarter, Year** or for the total period.
7.  If you would like your report to be sent to your email or saved somewhere, please enter your **Email Address** or **Phone Number**.
8.  Provide any **Additional Comments** you have about your request.
9.  Finally, choose the format you'd like your report in, which could be **PDF, CSV, JSON**.
10.  Optionally, you can **Assign a tool** to use for the analysis.
11.  You can also specify a path where you want to **Output the file**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Social Media Platform** 
   *   Select the social media platform you are interested in. This could be Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, or Twitter.

### 2. **Time Range**
   *   Enter the start date for the time range you are interested in. The date should be in a format that can be understood by your system.

### 3. **Time Range End**
   *   Enter the end date for the time range you are interested in. The date should be in a format that can be understood by your system.

### 4. **Metric Frequency**
   *   Choose how often you'd like to gather metrics for, this could be Hourly, Daily, Weekly, Monthly for more granular data.

### 5. **Engagement Platforms**
   *   Select which type of engagement platforms you'd like to gather data for, which could be Posts, Stories, Live Streaming or more.

### 6. **Engagement Metrics**
   *   Choose which metrics you'd like to display in your report, this could be Post Reach, Engagement Rate, Comment Count, Post Click-Through Rate, Sentiment Analysis, Audience Growth Rate, Top Engagement Content, Hashtags Used, Engagement Index.

### 7. **Metric Interval**
   *   Select the interval you'd like to gather metrics for, this could be Week, Month, Quarter, Year or for the total period.

### 8. **Email Address**
   *   Enter your email address where you'd like to receive your report.

### 9. **Phone Number**
   *   Enter your phone number where you'd like your report to be sent.

### 10. **Comments Section**
   *   Enter any additional information about your request.

### 11. **Additional Comments**
   *   Add any additional comments you have about your request.

### 12. **Output File**
   *   Choose the file format you'd like your report to be in, this could be PDF, CSV, JSON.

### 13. **Assigned Tool**
   *   Optionally, you can assign a tool to use for analysis.

### 14. **Output File Path**
   *   If you want to save your report somewhere, enter the path where it should be saved.
