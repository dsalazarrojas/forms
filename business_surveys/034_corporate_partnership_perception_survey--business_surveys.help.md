# Corporate Partnership Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from business partners about their perception of the corporate partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions on the form as accurately as possible, providing concrete examples from your experience with the partner.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields. The required fields are marked with a *.
3. Select the options that best fit your response to the question.
4. Do not skip any questions.
5. If you're unsure about any question, it's best to answer based on your understanding of the partnership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, not required): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully before answering the questions.
* **Partner Satisfaction** (`partner_satisfaction`, `number`, not required): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the partner. Please rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Partner Value** (`partner_value`, `text`, not required): This question asks about the value you've received from the partner. Please provide a brief description of your experience.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question asks about any challenges you've faced with the partner. Please select all that apply.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, `text`, not required): This question asks for any suggestions you have for how the partnership could be improved.
* **Long Term Partnership** (`long_term_partnership`, `select_one`, not required): This question asks about your expectation for the long-term partnership. Please select one of the options.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Partner Relationship** (`partner_relationship`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question asks about your relationship with the partner. Please select all that apply.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Communication** (`communication`, `select_one`, not required): This question asks about the communication with the partner. Please select one of the options.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `text`, not required): This question asks for any follow-up questions or concerns you have about the partnership.
* **Corporate Partnership** (`corporate_partnership`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question asks about your perception of the corporate partnership. Please select one of the options.
	+ Good
	+ Fair
