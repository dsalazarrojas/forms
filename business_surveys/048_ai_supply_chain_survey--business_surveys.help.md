# AI Supply Chain Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information on the adoption and use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in supply chain operations. The goal is to collect data on various aspects of AI adoption, including the benefits, challenges, and future plans for AI investment in supply chain.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the correct options for each field according to your company's current situation.
2. Answer all questions that apply to your company.
3. Do not leave any fields blank unless explicitly instructed to do so.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (2): This is the name of your company.
* **Primary Industry** (3): Select the primary industry your company operates in.
* **Annual Revenue Range** (4): Choose the range of your company's annual revenue.
* **Supply Chain functions currently using AI** (6): Select all supply chain functions currently using AI.
* **Percentage of supply chain operations currently assisted by AI** (7): Estimate the overall footprint of AI in your operations.
* **Years since first AI implementation in supply chain** (8): Select the number of years since your company first implemented AI in your supply chain.
* **Impact on Demand Forecasting Accuracy** (10): Choose the impact of AI on demand forecasting accuracy.
* **Impact on Inventory Carrying Costs** (11): Select the impact of AI on inventory carrying costs.
* **Improvement in Lead Time / Delivery Speed** (12): Rate the improvement in lead time / delivery speed.
* **Overall ROI observed from AI investments** (13): Select the overall return on investment observed from AI investments.
* **Main barriers to AI adoption in your supply chain** (15): Select up to three main barriers to AI adoption.
* **Biggest challenge during implementation** (16): Select the biggest challenge you experienced during AI implementation.
* **Plans for AI investment in the next 24 months** (18): Choose your plans for AI investment in the next 24 months.
* **Future AI strategic priorities** (19): Select all that apply, if applicable.
* **General comments on the future of AI in supply chain** (20): This is an open-ended question for any additional comments on the future of AI in supply chain operations.

## Tips
* Please select one option per field, unless multiple options are provided.
* If a field does not apply to your company, please leave it blank.
* Select all that apply for multiple choice questions.
* Provide general comments if you have any additional insights or concerns about the future of AI in supply chain operations.
