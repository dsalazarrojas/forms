# Timber Building Advertising Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Timber Building Advertising Survey" form helps us understand your advertising needs and preferences to improve our advertising strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" (e.g. Last Name, Contact Email, Advertisement Type, Date, Time, Rating, and Feedback).
2. Select the appropriate options for "Advertisement Type" and "Advertisement Frequency" if applicable.
3. Provide your contact information (optional).
4. Leave the non-required fields blank if not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Phone 2** (`contact_phone_2`, text, optional): Enter additional contact phone numbers (e.g. work or cell phone).
* **Building Name** (`building_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the building or project you're associated with.
* **Advertisement Type** (`advertisement_type`, select one, required): Select the type of advertisement you're interested in (e.g. Billboard, Facebook, Instagram, Print, or TV).
* **Advertisement Frequency** (`advertisement_frequency`, select multiple, optional): Select the frequency of the advertisement you're interested in (e.g. Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the advertisement will be displayed.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you'd like the advertisement to be displayed.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you'd like the advertisement to be displayed.
* **Note** (`note`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your advertising needs.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with our advertising services.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Provide any feedback about your experience with our advertising services.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, optional): Suggest any improvements for our advertising services.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Job Title** (`contact_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the contact person.
* **Contact Department** (`contact_department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the contact person.
* **Contact Company** (`contact_company`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's company.
* **Contact Phone 2** (`contact_phone2`, text, optional): Enter additional contact phone numbers (e.g. work or cell phone).
* **Contact Email 2** (`contact_email2`, text, optional): Enter additional contact email addresses.
* **Contact Phone 3** (`contact_phone3`, text, optional): Enter additional contact phone numbers.
* **Contact Email 3** (`contact_email3`, text, optional): Enter additional contact email addresses.
* **Contact Phone 4** (`contact_phone4`, text, optional): Enter additional contact phone numbers.
* **Contact Email 4** (`contact_email4`, text, optional): Enter additional contact email addresses.

**Tips**

* Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* Be specific when providing feedback and suggestions.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
