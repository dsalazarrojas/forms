# corporate.report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees about corporate crime awareness in various departments, including finance, human resources, information technology, and environmental services. It aims to gauge employees' levels of awareness about these critical areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read through each page carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2.  Select the correct options for each question based on your role and level of awareness.
3.  Make sure to complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) next to the label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Welcome to this survey** (`introduction`, `note`, required): This is an introductory page that explains the purpose of the form.
*   **What kind of crimes are we dealing with?** (`crime_types`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the types of crimes that are relevant to your department or role.
    *   Select all that apply: Financial Crime, Human Trafficking, Cyber Crime, Environmental Crime.
*   **What is your role in corporate finance?** (`crime_finance`, `select_one`, required): Choose your role in corporate finance.
    *   Select one option: CEO, CFO, CCO, Compliance Officer, Risk Manager.
*   **What is your role in corporate human resources?** (`crime_human`, `select_one`, required): Choose your role in corporate human resources.
    *   Select one option: HR Manager, Recruitment Manager, Training Manager, Compliance Officer, Risk Manager.
*   **What is your level of awareness about corporate crime in finance?** (`crime_finance_details`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your level of awareness about corporate crime in finance.
    *   Select all that apply: High, Medium, Low.
*   **What is your role in corporate information technology?** (`crime_it`, `select_one`, required): Choose your role in corporate information technology.
    *   Select one option: IT Manager, Network Administrator, Database Manager, Security Manager, Compliance Officer.
*   **What is your level of awareness about corporate crime in IT?** (`crime_it_details`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your level of awareness about corporate crime in IT.
    *   Select all that apply: High, Medium, Low.
*   **What is your role in corporate environmental services?** (`crime_environment`, `select_one`, required): Choose your role in corporate environmental services.
    *   Select one option: EHS Manager, Sustainability Manager, Compliance Officer, Environmental Manager, Risk Manager.
*   **What is your level of awareness about corporate crime in the environment?** (`crime_environment_details`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your level of awareness about corporate crime in the environment.
    *   Select all that apply: High, Medium, Low.

## Tips
*   Make sure to answer questions based on your actual role and level of awareness, not hypothetical scenarios.
*   If you're unsure about a question, try to choose the option that best fits your role and situation.
*   Remember to complete all required fields to ensure your feedback is recorded accurately.
