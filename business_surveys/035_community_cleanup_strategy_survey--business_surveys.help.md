# Community Cleanup Strategy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Cleanup Strategy Survey is designed to gather information from residents on their opinions and suggestions on how to maintain a clean and beautiful community. Your input is vital to helping us create a comprehensive community cleanup strategy that meets the needs and preferences of our community members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions on each page of the form, following the instructions provided for each field.
2. Make sure to provide your name and email address to ensure we can follow up with you about your participation.
3. Indicate your preferred method of communication (e.g., email, phone, or in-person) for any follow-up questions or updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Resident (1)**: Please provide your full name as you would like it to be listed in our records.
* **Email Address (2)**: Enter your email address to enable us to follow up with you about your participation and any updates on our community cleanup initiatives.
* **Primary Contact Phone Number (3)**: Provide your primary phone number for any further communication or questions.
* **Residential Address (4)**: Enter your residential address to help us identify your location within the community.
* **-- Cleanup Priorities and Areas -- (5)**: This section is a brief introduction to the next part of the form.
* **Which area in our community needs the most cleanup attention (6)**: Select the area that you believe requires the most attention for maintenance and upkeep.
* **What are the biggest litter or cleanup issues you see (7)**: Choose all that apply to help us identify the most pressing issues affecting our community.
* **-- Volunteer Availability and Preferences -- (8)**: This section is a brief introduction to the next part of the form.
* **How often would you like to see community cleanup events (9)**: Select the frequency of events that you would prefer to participate in.
* **Which days of the week are you most likely to participate (10)**: Choose all the days of the week that you are available to participate in community cleanup events.
* **Which volunteer role would you be most interested in (11)**: Select the role that you would be interested in or willing to perform during cleanup events.
* **-- Effective Cleanup Strategies -- (12)**: This section is a brief introduction to the next part of the form.
* **Which of these strategies do you think would be most effective (13)**: Choose all that apply to help us identify the most effective strategies for our community cleanup initiatives.
* **Share any other ideas or suggestions for our cleanup strategy (14)**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have for improving our community cleanup efforts.
* **-- Final Acknowledgment and Submission -- (15)**: This section is a confirmation of your participation and submission of your response.
* **Digital Signature (16)**: Please type your full name to confirm that the information provided is correct.
* **Date of Submission (17)**: Enter the date you are submitting your survey.
* **I would like to receive the community center's newsletter (18)**: Choose yes to stay updated on our programs and events.
* **How did you hear about this cleanup strategy survey (19)**: Select the method through which you became aware of this survey.

Please submit your completed form to us by providing your digital signature and date of submission. We appreciate your participation and look forward to incorporating your input into our community cleanup strategy.
