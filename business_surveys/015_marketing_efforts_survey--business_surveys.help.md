# Marketing Efforts Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect data about marketing efforts, such as business surveys and their effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the required fields to provide a complete and accurate response.
2. Select all relevant options that apply to the marketing surveys you are reporting on.
3. Fill in the effectiveness score and date/time fields as requested.
4. Add any additional notes or comments you feel are relevant to your response.
5. Finally, review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Marketing Surveys** (`marketing_efforts_survey_business_surveys`, select_multiple, optional): Select all marketing surveys that apply to the marketing efforts you are reporting on.
* **Effectiveness** (`marketing_efforts_survey_effectiveness`, number, optional): Enter a numerical score to indicate how effective you feel the marketing efforts were.
* **Effectiveness Score** (`marketing_efforts_survey_effectiveness_score`, text, optional): Provide a description of the effectiveness score you chose (e.g., "High" or "Low").
* **Date** (`marketing_efforts_survey_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the marketing efforts were performed.
* **Time** (`marketing_efforts_survey_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of day the marketing efforts were performed.
* **Note** (`marketing_efforts_survey_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the marketing efforts.
* **Email** (`marketing_efforts_survey_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact or follow-up purposes.
* **Phone** (`marketing_efforts_survey_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact or follow-up purposes.
* **Note 2** (`marketing_efforts_survey_note_2`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the marketing efforts.

## Tips
- Make sure to select all relevant marketing surveys and enter accurate dates and times.
- Be as detailed as possible in your notes and comments.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or contact the marketing team.
