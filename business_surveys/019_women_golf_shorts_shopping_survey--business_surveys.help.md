# Women Golf Shorts Shopping Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey aims to gather information on your preferences, shopping habits, and attitudes towards golf shorts. Your input will help us better understand your needs and improve our products and marketing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select all the applicable shopping channels that you use to purchase golf shorts. 
2. Choose the brands you are most familiar with or prefer to buy from.
3. Indicate your purchase frequency in terms of how often you buy golf shorts.
4. Select your level of satisfaction with your current golf shorts.
5. Choose the features that are most important to you when purchasing golf shorts.
6. Select the price range that you are most comfortable with when buying golf shorts.
7. Identify the channels you use to learn about golf shorts (e.g., social media, online ads, friends).
8. Enter the number of times you shop in-store and online for golf shorts each week.
9. Select your age group.
10. Choose your income level.
11. Indicate the importance of shopping channels to you.
12. Rate your level of loyalty to specific brands.
13. Select your frequency of communication with brands.
14. Indicate your level of satisfaction with your purchases.
15. Choose your level of confidence when making purchases.
16. Select your shopping habits in-store (e.g., try before you buy, online research).
17. Select your shopping habits online (e.g., try before you buy, online research).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Shopping Channels** (`shopping_channels`, select multiple): Select all the shopping channels that you use to purchase golf shorts, such as Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Twitter, Google Ads, and Print Ads.
- **Preferred Brands** (`preferred_brands`, select multiple): Choose the brands you are most familiar with or prefer to buy from, such as Nike, Adidas, Under Armour, Reebok, and Other.
- **Purchase Frequency** (`purchase_frequency`, select one): Indicate how often you buy golf shorts, options include Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, or Never.
- **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select one): Rate your level of satisfaction with your current golf shorts, options include Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
- **Preferred Features** (`preferred_features`, select multiple): Choose the features that are most important to you when purchasing golf shorts, such as Comfortable Fit, Affordable, Style, Functionality, Color, and Other.
- **Price Point** (`price_point`, select one): Select the price range that you are most comfortable with when buying golf shorts, options include $0-$20, $20-$50, $50-$100, or More.
- **Shopping Influencers** (`shopping_influencers`, select multiple): Identify the channels you use to learn about golf shorts, such as Family and Friends, Social Media, Online Ads, Magazine Ads, and Other.
- **Shopping Frequency In-Store** (`shopping_frequency_instore`, number): Enter the number of times you shop in-store for golf shorts each week.
- **Shopping Frequency Online** (`shopping_frequency_online`, number): Enter the number of times you shop online for golf shorts each week.
- **Age Group** (`age_group`, select one): Select your age group, options include 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, 65+, or Other.
- **Income Level** (`income_level`, select one): Choose your income level, options include Less than $20, $20, 000-$40, 000, $40, 000-$60, 000, $60, 000-$80, 000, or $80, 000+.
- **Shopping Channel Importance** (`shopping_channel_importance`, select one): Indicate the importance of shopping channels to you, options include Very Important, Somewhat Important, Not Very Important, Not at All Important.
- **Brand Loyalty** (`brand_loyalty`, select one): Rate your level of loyalty to specific brands, options include Very Loyal, Somewhat Loyal, Neutral, Not at All Loyal.
- **Communication Frequency** (`communication_frequency`, select one): Select your frequency of communication with brands, options include Very Frequently, Somewhat Frequently, Not Very Frequently, Not at All Frequently.
- **Purchase Satisfaction** (`purchase_satisfaction`, select one): Rate your level of satisfaction with your purchases, options include Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied.
- **Purchase Confidence** (`purchase_confidence`, select one): Choose your level of confidence when making purchases, options include Very Confident, Somewhat Confident, Neutral, Somewhat Insecure, Very Insecure.
- **Purchase Habits In-Store** (`purchase_habits_instore`, select multiple): Select your shopping habits in-store, such as Try Before You Buy, Online Research, Buy on Impulse, Recommendation, Other.
- **Purchase Habits Online** (`purchase_habits_online`, select multiple): Select your shopping habits online, such as Try Before You Buy, Online Research, Buy on Impulse, Recommendation, Other.
- **Purchase Location** (`purchase_location`, select one): Indicate the location where you usually make purchases, options include In-Store, Online.
- **Shopping Frequency In-Store** (`shopping_frequency_instore`, number): Enter the number of times you shop in-store for golf shorts each week.
- **Shopping Frequency Online** (`shopping_frequency_online`, number): Enter the number of times you shop online for golf shorts each week.
