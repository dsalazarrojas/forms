# Smart Home Feature Value Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Home Feature Value Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from users about their perceptions and expectations of smart home features. It is intended to gauge the importance and expectation of different smart home features and how much users are willing to pay for them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the survey.
2. For each field, select the relevant option that best answers your questions.
3. Make sure to fill in all the required fields, marked as "required" in the field label.
4. You can skip fields marked as "optional" if you wish, but filling them out will provide more accurate and useful feedback.
5. Enter your email address and phone number for us to contact you for further clarification if needed.
