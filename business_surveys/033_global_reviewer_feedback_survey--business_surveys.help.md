# Global Reviewer Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for global reviewers to provide their feedback on the review process.

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete the form in one sitting. The form has 11 fields that require your input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Review type** (`review_type`, select_one, required): This field asks you to confirm whether this is a review or not. Please select 'Yes' if this is a review and 'No' if it's not.
* **Reviewer type** (`reviewer_type`, select_multiple, required): This field asks you to confirm whether you are a reviewer or not. Please select all that apply.
* **Reviewer name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required): This field asks for your name as a reviewer.
* **Reviewer company** (`reviewer_company`, text, optional): This field is optional and asks for your company's name.
* **Feedback** (`reviewer_feedback`, note, optional): This field is optional and asks for any additional feedback you may have.
* **Satisfaction level** (`reviewer_satisfaction`, select_one, required): This field asks you to rate your satisfaction level with the review process. Please select 'Satisfied' or 'Dissatisfied'.
* **Review date** (`review_date`, date, required): This field asks for the date of the review.
* **Review time** (`review_time`, time, optional): This field is optional and asks for the time of the review.
* **Reviewer email** (`reviewer_email`, email, optional): This field is optional and asks for your email address.
* **Reviewer phone** (`reviewer_phone`, text, optional): This field is optional and asks for your phone number.
* **Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, note, optional): This field is optional and asks for any additional comments you may have.
