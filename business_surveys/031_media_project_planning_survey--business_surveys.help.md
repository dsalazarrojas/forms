# Media Project Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help project teams gather and record project details, objectives, timelines, and other relevant information in one place. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that project stakeholders are on the same page and that the project progresses smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each page to input your project information.
2. Enter your project details in the designated fields.
3. For fields with multiple options (like Objectives), select all applicable choices.
4. Enter dates using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
5. For text fields, enter a brief description or relevant information.
6. Ensure that you fill out all required fields to ensure accurate project planning.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details**: Enter a brief description of your project, including its title, genre, or any other relevant details.
* **Objectives**: Select all objectives that apply to your project (Project Goal, Media Type, Product Type).
* **Project Timelines**: Enter the project timelines, including start and end dates.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your project using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your project using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Production Studio**: Enter the name of your production studio.
* **Marketing Agency**: Enter the name of your marketing agency.
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of your client.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of your contact person.
* **Client Email**: Enter the email address of your client.
* **Client Phone**: Enter the phone number of your client.
* **Production Company**: Enter the name of your production company.
* **Production Address**: Enter the address of your production company.
* **Project Manager**: Enter the name of your project manager.
* **Client Notes**: Enter any additional notes from your client.
* **Marketing Team**: Enter the name of your marketing team.
* **Production Team**: Enter the name of your production team.
* **Resources**: Enter the resources needed for your project (people, equipment, locations, etc.).
* **Project Budget**: Enter the budget for your project.
* **Resource People**: Enter the names of people needed for your project.
* **Resource Equipment**: Enter the equipment needed for your project.
* **Resource Locations**: Enter the locations needed for your project.
* **Resource Other**: Enter any other resources needed for your project.
* **Resource Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your resources.

Note: This guide is for informational purposes only and not a substitute for professional advice. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your project manager or support team.
