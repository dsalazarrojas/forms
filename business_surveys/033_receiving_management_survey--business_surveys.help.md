# Receiving Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Receiving Management Survey is a tool designed to gather feedback from users about their receiving management experience. This form helps the management team understand strengths and weaknesses of the current receiving management processes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Please take a few minutes to carefully read each question and choose the option that best fits your experience.
3. If a question doesn't fit your experience, you can answer "N/A" or "Not Applicable".
4. Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send your feedback.
