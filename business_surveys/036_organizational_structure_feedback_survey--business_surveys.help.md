# Organizational Structure Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the organizational structure of a company, focusing on company roles and reporting hierarchies. The feedback will help the organization understand its current structure and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Use the space provided for each field to elaborate on your thoughts.
3. If you're unsure about any question, provide a brief explanation or ask for clarification.
4. Submit the form once you've completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Company Roles** (`company_roles`, `text`, required: false)
Company roles refer to the different departments, teams, or positions within the organization. Describe the roles you've encountered or been part of in the company.

### 2. **Reporting Hierarchy** (`reporting_hierarchy`, `note`, required: false)
Describe the reporting structure within your team or department, including who reports directly to whom and any notable relationships.

### 3. **Feedback Message** (`feedback_message`, `text`, required: false)
Share any additional comments or thoughts you have about the organizational structure. This is an opportunity to provide specific, constructive feedback on areas of improvement.
