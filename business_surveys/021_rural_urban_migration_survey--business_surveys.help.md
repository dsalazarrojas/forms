# rural_urban_migration_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rural-urban migration survey is designed to collect information about your reasons for migrating from a rural to an urban area, the challenges you faced during the migration process, and your plans and experiences after settlement. This survey will help us understand your story and improve the lives of others who may be undergoing similar experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all the questions honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Select all the reasons that apply to you for migrating from a rural to an urban area.
3. Choose all the settlement plans that apply to you.
4. If you faced any challenges during the migration process, select all the challenges you faced.
5. Enter the duration of your migration in the format of dd/mm/yy.
6. Enter the time of your migration in the format of hh:mm.
7. Enter the distance you traveled during your migration.
8. Enter the duration of your stay in the urban area.
9. Enter any additional details about your settlement plans.
10. Enter any additional details about your migration reasons.
11. Enter any additional comments or details about your experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **migration_reasons** (Select One): 
    * Financial reasons: If you migrated for better financial opportunities or to escape financial difficulties.
    * Job opportunities: If you migrated for job opportunities or career advancement.
    * Family reasons: If you migrated for family reasons such as reuniting with family members or to join family.
    * Healthcare reasons: If you migrated for better healthcare facilities or services.
    * Educational reasons: If you migrated for education or to access better educational facilities.
    * Personal reasons: If you migrated for personal reasons such as to experience city life or to escape rural isolation.
* **settlement_plans** (Select Multiple): 
    * Urban: If you plan to settle in an urban area.
    * Rural: If you plan to settle in a rural area.
* **migration_challenges** (Select Multiple): 
    * Language barrier: If you faced language barriers during the migration process.
    * Job opportunities: If you faced difficulties finding job opportunities in your new location.
    * Housing difficulties: If you faced difficulties finding suitable housing in your new location.
    * Financial difficulties: If you faced financial difficulties during the migration process.
    * Safety and security concerns: If you faced safety and security concerns during the migration process.
* **settlement_duration** (Date): Enter the duration of your stay in the urban area in the format of dd/mm/yy.
* **settlement_time** (Time): Enter the time of your arrival in the urban area in the format of hh:mm.
* **migration_distance** (Number): Enter the distance you traveled during the migration.
* **migration_duration** (Number): Enter the duration of your stay in the urban area.
* **settlement_costs** (Number): Enter any additional costs you incurred during the migration process.
* **migration_challenges_reasons** (Text): Enter any additional details about the challenges you faced during the migration process.
* **settlement_plans_details** (Text): Enter any additional details about your settlement plans.
* **migration_reasons_details** (Text): Enter any additional details about your migration reasons.
* **settlement_costs_details** (Text): Enter any additional details about any additional costs you incurred during the migration process.
* **note** (Note): Enter any additional comments or details about your experiences.

## Tips
* Please answer all the questions honestly and accurately to provide valuable information for research and analysis.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please enter your best guess or provide any additional details that may help us understand your experiences.
* You can enter any additional comments or details in the "note" field.
