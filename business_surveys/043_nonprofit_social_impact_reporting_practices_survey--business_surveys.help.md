# Nonprofit Social Impact Reporting Practices Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Nonprofit Social Impact Reporting Practices Survey is a tool to collect insights on impact measurement methods, reporting frequency, challenges, and support needs from nonprofits, funders, and social enterprises. This survey aims to help organizations better understand their reporting practices and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and answer the questions that are relevant to your organization's practices. If a question does not apply, please select the "N/A" or "None" option when available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nonprofit Social Impact Reporting Practices Survey Form** (`reporting_methods`, `text`, required: false): This is the main question that introduces the survey's purpose and scope. Please provide a brief description of your organization's practices.
* **How often do you report your impact?** (`reporting_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about the frequency of your reporting practices. Select one of the options: Never, Monthly, Quarterly, Semiannually, or Annually.
* **What are some of the key challenges you experience when reporting your impact?** (`reporting_challenges`, `text`, required: false): This question asks about the challenges you face when reporting your impact. Please provide a brief description of the challenges you experience.
* **What kind of support do you need to enhance your reporting practices?** (`reporting_support`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about the type of support you need to improve your reporting practices. Select one or more of the options: Data analysis and visualization, Technical support, Financial support, or Strategic guidance.
* **How often do you face challenges with reporting your impact?** (`reporting_frequency_challenges`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about the frequency of challenges you face when reporting your impact. Select one of the options: Never, Monthly, Quarterly, Semiannually, or Annually.
* **How often do you report your impact?** (`reporting_frequency_2`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about the frequency of your reporting practices. Select one of the options: Never, Monthly, Quarterly, Semiannually, or Annually.
* **What impact areas do you report on?** (`impact_areas`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about the areas of impact you report on. Select one or more of the options: Program, Operational, Financial, or Strategic.
* **How often do you report your impact?** (`reporting_frequency_3`, `select_one`, required: false): This question is a duplicate of question 2, asking about the frequency of your reporting practices. Select one of the options: Never, Monthly, Quarterly, Semiannually, or Annually.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions that are relevant to your organization's practices.
* If a question does not apply, please select the "N/A" or "None" option when available.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the space below.
