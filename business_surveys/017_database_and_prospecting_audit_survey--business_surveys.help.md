# Database And Prospecting Audit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help businesses evaluate the quality and effectiveness of their database and prospecting process. It will provide insights into the database's condition, data collection and quality, and other key performance indicators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

- Carefully review each field and ensure you have accurate information for each question.
- Answer each question truthfully, based on your current business processes and performance.
- If you are unsure of any question, please refer back to your business's documentation or consult with your team members.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: Please enter a brief introduction to your business and its prospecting process. This will help us understand the context of your database and prospecting activities.
* **Company Info**: Please select all the companies that apply to your business. This will help us understand the scope of your database and prospecting efforts.
* **Database Quality**: Please enter the quality of your current database. This will help us understand the condition of your data and potential areas for improvement.
* **Data Collection**: Enter the date of your last data collection effort. This will help us understand the frequency and schedule of your data collection.
* **Probing Efficiency**: Please select if your sales team is probing or not. This will help us understand the effectiveness of your prospecting approach.
* **Sales Team Performance**: Please enter the performance of your sales team, in hours. This will help us understand their effectiveness and productivity.
* **Marketing Team Performance**: Please select all the performance areas that apply to your marketing team. This will help us understand their contribution to the prospecting process.
* **Operations Team Performance**: Please enter a brief description of your operations team's performance. This will help us understand their support and contribution to the prospecting process.
* **Customer Relationships**: Please enter a brief description of your customer relationships. This will help us understand the quality and depth of your customer relationships.
* **Lead Generation**: Please enter the number of leads generated in your last prospecting effort. This will help us understand the effectiveness of your prospecting strategy.
* **Data Collection Process**: Please describe your data collection process in detail. This will help us understand the method and frequency of your data collection.
* **Database Growth Rate**: Please enter the growth rate of your database. This will help us understand the rate of growth of your sales pipeline.
* **Lead Quality Rating**: Please select all the qualities that apply to your leads. This will help us understand the quality and characteristics of your leads.
* **Sales Pipeline Growth Rate**: Please enter the growth rate of your sales pipeline. This will help us understand the effectiveness of your prospecting strategy.
* **Conversion Rate**: Please select the conversion rate of your leads. This will help us understand the effectiveness of your prospecting strategy.
* **Sales Pipeline Value**: Please enter the value of your sales pipeline. This will help us understand the revenue and growth potential of your business.
* **Customer Acquisition Rate**: Please select all the qualities that apply to your customer acquisition rate. This will help us understand the effectiveness of your prospecting strategy.
* **Customer Satisfaction**: Please enter a brief description of customer satisfaction. This will help us understand the quality and depth of your customer relationships.
* **Sales Team Size**: Please enter the size of your sales team. This will help us understand the human resources allocated to the prospecting process.
* **Data Security**: Please select if your data is secure or not. This will help us understand the risk and potential threats to your data.
* **Sales Team Efficiency**: Please enter the efficiency of your sales team, in hours. This will help us understand their productivity and effectiveness.
* **Customer Relationship Quality**: Please enter a brief description of your customer relationships. This will help us understand the quality and depth of your customer relationships.
* **Sales Pipeline Efficiency**: Please enter the efficiency of your sales pipeline. This will help us understand the effectiveness and productivity of your prospecting strategy.
* **Customer Interaction**: Please describe your customer interaction in detail. This will help us understand how you engage with your customers.
* **Customer Engagement**: Please enter a brief description of customer engagement. This will help us understand how your customers are engaged with your business.
