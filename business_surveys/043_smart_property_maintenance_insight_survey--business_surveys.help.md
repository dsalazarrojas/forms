# Smart Property Maintenance Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Property Maintenance Insight Survey is designed to gather information from facility managers and property owners about their maintenance concerns, preferences, and contact information. This form will help us understand the current state of property maintenance, identify areas for improvement, and provide valuable insights for our company.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the maintenance concerns questions (Questions 1-2) honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select your preferred options for maintenance updates and communication (Questions 4-6).
3. Provide your contact information (Questions 7-10) to ensure we can reach out to you for any follow-up questions or concerns.
4. Fill in the property information (Questions 16-21) to help us better understand your property's specifics.
5. Add any additional comments or notes (Questions 22-25) if you have any further information or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Maintenance Concerns**
* `maintenance_concerns`: A text field where you can describe your maintenance concerns or issues with your property.
* **Hint:** This question is not required, but your honest answers will help us understand the current state of maintenance in your property.

### 2. **Concern Description**
* `maintenance_concerns_description`: A text field where you can describe your maintenance concerns in more detail.
* **Hint:** This question is required to help us better understand your maintenance concerns.

### 3. **Overall Satisfaction**
* `satisfaction`: A select-one field where you can choose one of the options to describe your overall satisfaction with the maintenance of your property.
* **Hint:** This question is required and helps us understand your overall satisfaction.

### 4. **How often do you maintain your property?**
* `maintenance_frequency`: A select-multiple field where you can choose how often you maintain your property.
* **Hint:** This question is optional but will help us understand your maintenance frequency.

### 5. **How do you prefer to receive maintenance updates?**
* `maintenance_preferences`: A select-multiple field where you can choose how you prefer to receive maintenance updates.
* **Hint:** This question is optional but will help us understand your update preferences.

### 6. **How do you prefer to communicate?**
* `communication_preferences`: A select-multiple field where you can choose how you prefer to communicate.
* **Hint:** This question is optional but will help us understand your communication preferences.

### 7. **Contact Name**
* `contact_name`: A text field where you can enter your contact name.
* **Hint:** This question is required to allow us to reach out to you for any follow-up questions or concerns.

### 8. **Contact Phone**
* `contact_phone_number`: A text field where you can enter your contact phone number.
* **Hint:** This question is optional but will help us reach out to you for any urgent maintenance concerns.

### 9. **Contact Email**
* `contact_email`: A text field where you can enter your contact email.

### 10. **Contact Note**

* **Hint:** This question is optional but will help us understand any additional contact information.

### 11. **Facility Manager**

### 12. **Manager Name**

* **Hint:** This question is optional but will help us understand your team's structure.

### 13. **Manager Phone**

### 14. **Manager Email**
