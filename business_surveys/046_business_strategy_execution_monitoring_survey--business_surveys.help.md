**Business Strategy Execution Monitoring Survey - Help Guide**
===============================================

## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather information about your understanding and experience with business strategy execution within your department. The goal is to identify strengths and weaknesses, as well as areas for improvement in execution and strategy alignment.

**How To Complete This Form**

1. Review the questions carefully and provide honest and thoughtful responses.
2. Fill in the required fields as indicated.
3. For text fields, provide concise and specific answers.
4. Use the provided options for select_one fields.
5. Save your progress and submit when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Respondent Information
**Your Name** (`respondent_name`, Text, **Required**): Please enter your name for tracking purposes.

### Department
**Department** (`department`, Text, **Required**): Enter the department you belong to.

### Role Level
**Role Level** (`role_level`, Select One, **Required**):
	* **Executive**: Senior management or leadership role.
	* **Manager**: Mid-level management role.
	* **Team Lead**: Team leader or supervisor role.
	* **Individual Contributor**: Non-management role.
	* **Other**: Other role levels.

### Survey Date
**Survey Date** (`survey_date`, Date, **Required**): Enter the date you are completing this survey.

### Strategy Clarity and Understanding
**Understanding of Company Strategy** (`strategy_understanding`, Select One, **Required**):
	* **Very poor understanding**: Not clear or confused about the strategy.
	* **Poor understanding**: Some understanding, but not clear.
	* **Adequate understanding**: Basic understanding of the strategy.
	* **Good understanding**: Clear understanding of the strategy.
	* **Excellent understanding**: Very clear understanding of the strategy.

### Strategy Clarity and Understanding
**Effectiveness of Strategy Communication** (`strategy_communication`, Select One, **Required**):
	* **Very ineffective**: Strategy not communicated effectively.
	* **Ineffective**: Strategy not communicated well.
	* **Neutral**: Strategy communicated adequately.
	* **Effective**: Strategy communicated clearly.
	* **Very effective**: Strategy communicated very clearly.

### Strategy Clarity and Understanding
**Personal Role Alignment with Strategy** (`personal_alignment`, Select One, **Required**):
	* **Not aligned**: Role not aligned with the strategy.
	* **Somewhat aligned**: Role somewhat aligned with the strategy.
	* **Moderately aligned**: Role moderately aligned with the strategy.
	* **Well aligned**: Role well aligned with the strategy.
	* **Perfectly aligned**: Role perfectly aligned with the strategy.

### Execution and Progress
**Strategy Execution Progress** (`execution_progress`, Select One, **Required**):
	* **Falling behind**: Not meeting execution targets.
	* **Somewhat behind schedule**: Meeting execution targets, but behind schedule.
	* **On schedule**: Meeting execution targets on schedule.
	* **Ahead of schedule**: Meeting execution targets ahead of schedule.
	* **Exceeding targets**: Exceeding execution targets.

### Execution and Progress
**Progress on Strategic Goals** (`goal_progress`, Select One, **Required**):
	* **No progress**: No progress on strategic goals.
	* **Minimal progress**: Some progress on strategic goals.
	* **Moderate progress**: Moderate progress on strategic goals.
	* **Good progress**: Good progress on strategic goals.
	* **Excellent progress**: Excellent progress on strategic goals.

### Obstacles and Challenges
**Main Obstacles to Execution** (`main_obstacles`, Text, **Required**): Describe the main obstacles to execution.

### Obstacles and Challenges
**Internal Barriers Identified** (`internal_barriers`, Text, **Optional**): Describe any internal barriers.

### Obstacles and Challenges
**External Challenges** (`external_challenges`, Text, **Optional**): Describe any external challenges.

### Recommendations
**Needed Improvements** (`needed_improvements`, Text, **Optional**): Suggest improvements to strategy execution.

### Additional Feedback
**Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, Text, **Optional**): Provide any other observations or feedback.

## Tips
