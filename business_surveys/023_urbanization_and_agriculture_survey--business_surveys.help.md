# Urbanization And Agriculture Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on urbanization and its effects on agriculture from respondents with experience or knowledge in the field. The form aims to understand the process of urbanization, current agriculture practices, community outcomes, and stakeholder outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the introduction page (page 1) to understand the purpose and scope of the form.
2. Select the urbanization process that best describes your experience or knowledge (page 2).
3. If you have experience or knowledge in agriculture practices, input your answer in the text field (page 3).
4. Describe the community outcomes you have observed or experienced (page 4).
5. If relevant, describe the stakeholder outcomes you have observed or experienced (page 5).
6. Select a research recommendation that you think is most relevant to your experience or knowledge (page 6).
7. Select the local government support that you think is most relevant to your experience or knowledge (page 7).
8. If you have experience or knowledge of community engagement, input your answer in the text field (page 8).
9. If you have experience or knowledge of research teams, input your answer in the text field (page 9).
10. If you have experience or knowledge of planners and experts, input your answer in the text field (page 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, optional): This page is a brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **Urbanization process** (`urbanization_process`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the urbanization processes that you have experienced or know. This field is required to ensure that you have relevant experience or knowledge.
* **Agriculture practices** (`agriculture_practices`, `text`, optional): If you have experience or knowledge of agriculture practices, input your answer in this text field.
* **Community outcomes** (`community_outcomes`, `text`, required): Describe the community outcomes you have observed or experienced.
* **Stakeholder outcomes** (`stakeholder_outcomes`, `text`, optional): If you have experience or knowledge of stakeholder outcomes, input your answer in this text field.
* **Research recommendations** (`research_recommendations`, `select_one`, optional): Select a research recommendation that you think is most relevant to your experience or knowledge.
* **Local government support** (`local_government_support`, `select_one`, required): Select the local government support that you think is most relevant to your experience or knowledge.
* **Community engagement** (`community_engagement`, `text`, optional): If you have experience or knowledge of community engagement, input your answer in this text field.
* **Research teams** (`research_teams`, `text`, optional): If you have experience or knowledge of research teams, input your answer in this text field.
* **Planners and experts** (`planners_and_experts`, `text`, optional): If you have experience or knowledge of planners and experts, input your answer in this text field.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals before completing this form.
* Be honest and accurate when answering the form's questions.
* If you are unsure of any question, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
* This form is meant to be completed by one person at a time. Please do not share your answers with others.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
