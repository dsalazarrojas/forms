# Business Advisory Insight Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Advisory Insight Request Form is designed to collect information from business leaders and decision-makers who are seeking advisory services to address specific challenges and issues within their organization. This form helps to understand the nature of their challenge, their expectations for advisory services, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Select your job title from the "Position" field.
3. Enter the name of your organization in the "Organization" field.
4. Choose your industry from the list in the "Industry" field.
5. Enter your work email in the "Email Address" field.
6. Enter your contact number in the "Phone Number" field.
7. Select the size of your organization from the "Organization Size" field.
8. Choose your approximate annual revenue in the "Annual Revenue" field (if applicable).
9. Enter the type of guidance you need in the "Advisory Needs" field (if applicable).
10. Select the main area where you need guidance in the "Primary Advisory Area" field.
11. Describe your specific challenge in the "Specific Challenge" field.
12. Choose the duration of the challenge in the "Challenge Duration" field.
13. If you have previously attempted to address this challenge, describe your efforts in the "Previous Attempts to Address" field (if applicable).
14. Enter the results you are hoping for in the "Desired Outcome" field.
15. Choose the urgency level of your request in the "Urgency Level" field.
16. Select your preferred engagement type in the "Preferred Engagement Type" field.
17. Enter your expected budget range for advisory services in the "Budget Range" field (if applicable).
18. Choose the timeline for the advisory engagement in the "Preferred Timeline" field.
19. If there are other stakeholders involved, enter their names in the "Key Stakeholders" field (if applicable).
20. If you are the decision-maker, select "True" in the "Are You the Decision Maker" field.
21. Enter any additional information relevant to your request in the "Additional Information" field (if applicable).
