# Government Relationship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Relationship Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from the public on their experience and perceptions of the government services provided. The goal of this survey is to help the government understand how well it is serving the public and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your experience and perceptions.
2. For questions with options, select the one that best describes your experience or feelings.
3. You may skip questions that do not apply to you.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (government_relationship_survey_form_intro)**: This is an introductory section of the survey, providing background information on the purpose and goal of the survey.
* **How satisfied with services (government_relationship_survey_service_satisfaction)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the services provided by the government on a scale of 1-5.
* **Services accessibility (government_relationship_survey_service_accessibility)**: Rate the accessibility of the services provided by the government on a scale of 1-5.
* **Government transparency (government_relationship_survey_transparency)**: Describe your expectations for government transparency.
* **How often use services (government_relationship_survey_services_frequency)**: Select the frequency with which you use the services provided by the government.
* **Importance of services (government_relationship_survey_services_importance)**: Select the importance of the services provided by the government.
* **Which areas surveyed (government_relationship_survey_surveyed_areas)**: Select the areas of services that have been surveyed.
* **Satisfaction with services provided (government_relationship_survey_satisfaction_with_services_provided)**: Rate your satisfaction with the services provided by the government on a scale of 1-5.
* **How frequently surveyed areas surveyed (government_relationship_survey_surveyed_areas_frequency)**: Select the frequency with which the surveyed areas are surveyed.
* **Services use (government_relationship_survey_services_use)**: Select the frequency with which you use the services provided by the government.
* **Importance of services to residents (government_relationship_survey_services_importance_to_residents)**: Select the importance of the services provided by the government to residents.
* **Satisfaction of services for residents (government_relationship_survey_services_satisfaction_for_residents)**: Rate your satisfaction with the services provided by the government for residents on a scale of 1-5.
* **Transparency in government decision (government_relationship_survey_transparency_in_government_decision)**: Describe your expectations for government decision-making transparency.
* **Accessibility of services (government_relationship_survey_services_accessibility_services)**: Rate the accessibility of the services provided by the government on a scale of 1-5.
* **Satisfaction with services transparency (government_relationship_survey_satisfaction_with_services_transparency)**: Rate your satisfaction with the services transparency on a scale of 1-5.
* **Services transparency (government_relationship_survey_services_transparency)**: Describe your expectations for services transparency.
* **Frequency of services transparency (government_relationship_survey_services_transparency_frequency)**: Select the frequency with which services transparency is provided.
* **Importance of services transparency (government_relationship_survey_services_transparency_importance)**: Select the importance of services transparency.
* **Additional comments (government_relationship_survey_additional_comments)**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Services surveyed (government_relationship_survey_services_surveyed)**: Describe the services that have been surveyed.
* **Government employees (government_relationship_survey_employees)**: Describe your experience with government employees.
* **Frequency of services surveyed (government_relationship_survey_services_frequency_surveyed)**: Select the frequency with which the services are surveyed.
* **Importance of employees (government_relationship_survey_services_importance_of_services)**: Select the importance of government employees.
* **Satisfaction with employees (government_relationship_survey_services_satisfaction_with_services)**: Rate your satisfaction with government employees on a scale of 1-5.
* **Employees surveyed (government_relationship_survey_surveyed_areas_employees)**: Describe the services that have been surveyed by government employees.
* **Services transparency employees (government_relationship_survey_services_transparency_employees)**: Describe your expectations for services transparency by government employees.
* **Importance of employees (government_relationship_survey_services_importance_of_employees)**: Select the importance of government employees.
* **Satisfaction with employees transparency (government_relationship_survey_services_satisfaction_with_employees_transparency)**: Rate your satisfaction with government employees transparency on a scale of 1-5.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and to the best of your abilities.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the government department responsible for this survey.
* Your feedback is valuable to us and will help us improve our services. Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey.
