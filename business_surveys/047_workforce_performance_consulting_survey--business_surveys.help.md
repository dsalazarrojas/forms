# Workforce Performance Consulting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about an individual's job performance, role, and evaluation history for HR management and performance evaluation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Choose your current role from the list of options.
*   Fill in your current location (if applicable).
*   Select all applicable job functions and departments related to your work.
*   Select your current job grade level.
*   Fill in any additional job grade level details (if applicable).
*   Enter your manager's name (if applicable).
*   Select your manager's job grade level.
*   Fill in your manager's job grade level details (if applicable).
*   Enter the date of your last job evaluation.
*   Fill in the comments about your last job evaluation (if applicable).
*   Enter the date of your next job evaluation.
*   Fill in the comments about your next job evaluation (if applicable).
*   Enter your performance score (if applicable).
*   Enter your last evaluation's performance score (if applicable).
*   Enter your next evaluation's performance score (if applicable).
*   Fill in comments about your last and next performance scores (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **What is your current role?**: Choose your current role from the list of options to describe your position in the company.
*   **Current Location:** Enter your current location if it is relevant to your work or role.
*   **Job Functions:** Select all applicable job functions related to your work, such as Operations Management, Sales, HR, IT, and Marketing.
*   **Departments:** Choose all applicable departments related to your work, such as Operations, Sales, Marketing, HR, and IT.
*   **Job Grade Level:** Select your current job grade level, which could be Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3.
*   **Job Grade Level 1 & 2:** Fill in any additional job grade level details, such as specific tasks or responsibilities.
*   **Manager's Name:** Enter your manager's name if they are involved in your performance evaluations.
*   **Manager's Job Grade Level:** Select your manager's job grade level, which could be Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3.
*   **Manager's Job Grade Level 1 & 2:** Fill in any details about your manager's job grade level, such as specific tasks or responsibilities.
*   **Last Job Evaluation Date:** Enter the date of your last job evaluation.
*   **Last Job Evaluation Comment:** Fill in comments about your last job evaluation, if applicable.
*   **Next Job Evaluation Date:** Enter the date of your next job evaluation.
*   **Next Job Evaluation Comment:** Fill in comments about your next job evaluation, if applicable.
*   **Performance Score:** Enter your current performance score (if applicable).
*   **Last Evaluation's Performance Score:** Enter your last evaluation's performance score (if applicable).
*   **Next Evaluation's Performance Score:** Enter your next evaluation's performance score (if applicable).
*   **Last Evaluation's Performance Comment:** Fill in comments about your last evaluation's performance score, if applicable.
