# Mergers And Acquisitions Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mergers And Acquisitions Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback and comments from employees on the M&A deal and integration process. This form will help the organization identify areas of improvement and ensure a smooth integration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and provide feedback on the M&A deal and integration process.
2. Enter your company information, including company name, department, and job function.
3. Enter contact information, including your name, title, and email.
4. Provide detailed information about the deal, including transaction details and integration plans.
5. Enter any additional comments or feedback.
6. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter the department name.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, `text`, optional): Enter your job function.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name.
* **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your title.
* **Employee Role** (`employee_role`, `text`, optional): Enter your role.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, `text`, optional): Enter your email.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Department Email** (`department_email`, `text`, optional): Enter department email.
* **Department Phone** (`department_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter department phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the deal.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the deal.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Transaction Information** (`transaction_info`, `text`, optional): Provide detailed information about the transaction.
* **Integration Plans** (`integration_plans`, `text`, optional): Provide detailed information about integration plans.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Enter your feedback on the M&A deal and integration process.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Provide your contact information.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your company email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your company phone number.
* **Submit** (Submit, `select_one`, required): Review your submission and select "Submit Form" to complete the form.

## Tips
* Please be thorough and honest in your feedback.
* Ensure your submission is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
