# Investment Policy Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gauge the level of awareness and understanding of investment policies among employees of the finance, compliance, and HR departments. The survey is meant to be a tool for awareness and education, and is not a comprehensive audit of financial activities or compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select all the options that apply to the Finance Understanding question.
2. Choose the options that best describe the investment policies that you are familiar with.
3. Answer the Compliance Conformity question with either 'Yes' or 'No', indicating if you are in compliance or not.
4. Enter your thoughts on the overall satisfaction with the investment policies in place.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback about the investment policies.
6. Select the department that handles finance for your organization.
7. Answer the Compliance Department question with either 'Yes' or 'No', indicating if you are in compliance or not.
8. Answer the HR Department question with either 'Yes' or 'No', indicating if you are in compliance or not.
9. Indicate if a reporting officer is available for your organization.
10. Enter the name and contact information of the person who can provide additional information.
11. Enter the contact person's phone number.
12. Select the department that you report to.
13. Indicate if a reporting officer is available for your organization.
14. Indicate if a finance manager is available for your organization.
15. Enter the name of the contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Finance Understanding** (`finance_understanding`, number, **Required**): This question is designed to gauge your level of understanding of finance-related policies and procedures. Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **Investment Policies** (`investment_policies`, select_multiple, **Required**): This question asks you to choose all the investment policies that you are familiar with. Please select all that apply.
* **Compliance Conformity** (`compliance_conformity`, select_one, **Optional**): This question is asking if you are in compliance with investment policies. Answer with either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **HR Understanding** (`hr_understanding`, number, **Required**): This question is designed to gauge your level of understanding of HR-related policies and procedures. Please enter your answer.
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`, text, **Optional**): This question is for you to provide any additional feedback on training needs related to investment policies.
* **Finance Feedback** (`finance_feedback`, email, **Required**): Please enter your email address so that we can contact you for further information.
* **Compliance Feedback** (`compliance_feedback`, select_multiple, **Required**): This question asks you to choose all the compliance issues that you are familiar with. Please select all that apply.
* **HR Feedback** (`hr_feedback`, select_one, **Optional**): This question is asking if you are compliant with HR policies. Answer with either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, **Required**): This question asks how satisfied you are with the investment policies in place.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, **Optional**): This is an opportunity for you to provide any additional feedback or comments on the investment policies.
* **Finance Department** (`finance_department`, select_one, **Required**): This question asks you to select the department that handles finance for your organization.
* **Compliance Department** (`compliance_department`, select_one, **Required**): This question asks you to answer if you are compliant with compliance policies. Answer with either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **HR Department** (`hr_department`, select_one, **Required**): This question asks you to answer if you are compliant with HR policies. Answer with either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Reporting Officer** (`reporting_officer`, select_one, **Required**): This question asks if a reporting officer is available for your organization.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, **Required**): This question asks you to enter the name of the person who can provide additional information.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, **Optional**): This question asks you to enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, **Required**): This question asks you to select the department that you report to.
* **Finance Phone** (`finance_phone`, text, **Required**): This question asks you to enter the finance department's phone number.
* **Finance Phone 2** (`finance_phone_2`, text, **Optional**): This question is for you to enter additional finance phone number.
* **Finance Email** (`finance_email`, email, **Required**): Please enter the finance department's email address.
* **Reporting Officer 2** (`reporting_officer_2`, select_one, **Required**): This question asks you to answer if a reporting officer is available for your organization.
* **Reporting Officer 3** (`reporting_officer_3`, select_one, **Required**): This question asks you to answer if a reporting officer is available for your organization.
* **Finance Manager** (`finance_manager`, select_one, **Required**): This question asks you to answer if a finance manager is available for your organization.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, **Optional**): This question asks you to enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, **Optional**): This question asks you to enter the contact person's phone number.
