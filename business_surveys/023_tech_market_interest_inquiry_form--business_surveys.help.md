# Tech Market Interest Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge your interest in the tech market. It will ask you to provide information about your company, department/team, preferred technology, industry, budget, time period, and any additional comments. This form will help us understand your needs and interests in the tech market.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Form Header" section to begin.
2. Enter your company's name in the "Company Name" field.
3. Select your department or team name in the "Department/Team" field.
4. Choose your company from the list in the "Company" field.
5. Choose your preferred technology in the "Technology" field.
6. Select your industry in the "Industry" field.
7. Select your budget in the "Budget" field.
8. Choose one or more time periods in the "Time Period" field.
9. Click on the "Submit" button to complete the form.
10. If you have any additional comments, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header (1)**: This is the title of the form and an introduction to get you started.
* **Company Name (2)**: Enter the name of your company. This will help us understand which company you represent.
* **Department/Team (3)**: Select your department or team name. This will help us understand which team is interested in the tech market.
* **Company (4)**: Choose your company from the list. This will help us understand which company you are representing.
* **Technology (5)**: Select your preferred technology. This will help us understand which technology interest you have.
* **Industry (6)**: Select your industry. This will help us understand which industry you are interested in.
* **Budget (7)**: Select your budget. This will help us understand your budget constraints or interests.
* **Time Period (8)**: Choose one or more time periods. This will help us understand your time frame for interest in the tech market.
* **Submit (9)**: This is the submit button to complete the form.
* **Additional Comments (10)**: Enter any additional comments you have. This is optional but will provide more context to your interest in the tech market.
