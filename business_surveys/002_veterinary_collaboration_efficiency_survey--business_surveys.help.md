# Veterinary Collaboration Efficiency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information on the methods used for collaboration between veterinarians, researchers, and other healthcare professionals, as well as any challenges faced in these settings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Teamwork methods" field to select the methods your team uses for collaboration. You can choose multiple methods by holding down the Ctrl key (Windows) or Command key (Mac) while selecting.
2. Next, click on the "Communication methods" field to select the methods your team uses for communication. Again, you can choose multiple methods by holding down the Ctrl key (Windows) or Command key (Mac) while selecting.
3. The "Challenges" field is meant to capture the single most significant challenge faced by your team. Please select the option that best describes your experience.
4. For the "Hospital X" fields, rate the efficiency of collaboration and communication methods at each hospital on a scale from 1-20, with 1 being very poor and 20 being excellent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teamwork methods (Select Multiple)**: This field allows you to select the methods used for collaboration between veterinarians and other healthcare professionals in your hospital. Choose all that apply.
* **Communication methods (Select Multiple)**: This field is similar to the teamwork methods field, but for communication methods.
* **Challenges (Select One)**: Identify the single most significant challenge faced by your team.
* **Hospital X (Number)**: Rate the efficiency of collaboration and communication methods at each hospital on a scale from 1-20.
* **Teamwork methods at hospital (Select Multiple)**: This is another field for selecting teamwork methods, but specifically for hospital settings.
