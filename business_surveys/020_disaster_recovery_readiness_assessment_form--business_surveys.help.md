# Disaster Recovery Readiness Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the Disaster Recovery Readiness of your organization. It's essential to complete this form to evaluate your company's ability to recover from disasters and disruptions. This assessment will help you determine your organization's strengths and weaknesses in disaster recovery, identify areas for improvement, and ensure you're prepared for any unexpected events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Go through each page and provide information about your organization's disaster recovery capabilities.
2. Answer each question honestly, using the options provided.
3. If you're unsure about any question, use the space provided to explain your answer.
4. Review and complete all pages to get an accurate assessment of your organization's disaster recovery readiness.
