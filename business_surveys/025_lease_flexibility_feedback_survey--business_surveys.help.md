# Lease Flexibility Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a feedback survey designed to collect information on lease flexibility from tenants. The survey aims to gather opinions on lease agreements, lease terms, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and provide honest feedback.
2. Select the relevant options for each question from the provided choices.
3. Provide clear and concise answers for the text-based questions.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled in, as indicated by the "required" label next to each field.
5. Once you've completed the form, click the "submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Start**: This is an introductory note that explains the purpose of the form.
* **Tenant Feedback**: This is a text-based field where you can provide any feedback or comments about your lease experience.
* **Lease Agreement**: Please select the lease agreement that you are currently under. This will help us understand your specific situation.
* **Lease Term**: Choose the lease term that best describes your current situation. This will give us insight into the duration of your lease.
* **Tenant Satisfaction**: Select how satisfied you are with your lease from the provided options. This will help us understand your level of satisfaction.
* **Suggestions**: This is a text-based field where you can provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Submit**: This is the final step to submit your feedback. Please ensure that you've filled in all required fields before submitting.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide clear feedback.
* If you're unsure about any question, please feel free to ask.
* You can submit your feedback anonymously, but if you'd like to share your name and contact information, we appreciate it to follow up on your suggestions.
