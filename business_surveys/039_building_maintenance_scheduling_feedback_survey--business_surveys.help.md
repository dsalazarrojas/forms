# Building Maintenance Scheduling Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form was created to help us improve our building maintenance scheduling process. We want to understand how our maintenance scheduling process is working and what can be done better. Your feedback is valuable to us, and your participation will help us create a smoother and more efficient maintenance scheduling experience for you and our building users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your name and email address to identify yourself.
2. Answer the following questions regarding your experience with our maintenance scheduling process:

   * Did you have recent maintenance scheduled?
   * How easy was scheduling the maintenance?
   * How did you schedule the maintenance?
   * What was the quality of communication about scheduling?
   * How much advance notice did you get?
   * Were flexible time slot options available?
   * What was the time window for the appointment?
   * Did the technician arrive at the promised time?
   * What is your preferred time for maintenance?
   * What level of disruption did you experience during the scheduling process?
   * What is your ideal advance notice for scheduling?
   * Do you want to receive maintenance notifications?
   * What is your overall satisfaction with the scheduling process?
   * Would you recommend our maintenance scheduling process?
   * What are your suggestions for improvements?
   * What are the main issues you experienced with the scheduling process?
   * Are there any specific changes you would like to see made to the scheduling process?

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your name**: Please provide your name to help us identify you.
* **Email address**: Please provide your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Department or area**: Please specify the department or area you scheduled maintenance for.
* **Recent maintenance**: Please tell us if you had maintenance scheduled recently.
* **Did you have maintenance scheduled recently?**: This question helps us understand how recently you had maintenance scheduled.
* **Type of maintenance**: Please select the type of maintenance you had, such as HVAC service or plumbing repair.
* **Scheduling experience**: This is a note field for you to add any comments about your experience with scheduling.
* **How easy was scheduling the maintenance?**: Rate how easy or difficult you found the maintenance scheduling process to be.
* **How did you schedule the maintenance?**: Please select how you scheduled the maintenance (e.g., online, phone, email, or in-person).
* **Quality of communication about scheduling**: Rate the quality of communication you received about scheduling.
* **Timeliness of notification**: Rate how much advance notice you received for the maintenance appointment.
* **Were flexible time slot options available?**: This question helps us understand if you had options for different time slots.
* **What was the time window for the appointment?**: Please select the time window you had for the maintenance appointment.
* **Did the technician arrive at the promised time?**: Rate if the technician arrived at the promised time.
* **Preferred time for maintenance**: Please select your preferred time for maintenance.
* **Level of disruption caused by scheduling**: Rate the level of disruption you experienced during the scheduling process.
* **Ideal advance notice for scheduling**: Please select your ideal advance notice for scheduling.
* **Preference for maintenance frequency notifications**: Please select how you would like to receive maintenance notifications.
* **Overall satisfaction with the scheduling process**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the scheduling process.
* **Would you recommend the maintenance scheduling process?**: Rate if you would recommend our maintenance scheduling process to others.
* **Suggested improvements**: Please provide any suggestions for improving the maintenance scheduling process.
* **Main issues with the scheduling process**: Please describe any main issues you experienced with the scheduling process.
* **What would make scheduling better?**: Please select any specific changes you would like to see made to the maintenance scheduling process.
* **Interest in online scheduling system**: Please rate your interest in using an online scheduling system.
* **Preferred notification methods**: Please select your preferred methods for receiving maintenance notifications.
* **Additional feedback or comments**: Please add any additional feedback or comments about the maintenance scheduling process.
* **Would you like to suggest anything?**: Please provide any additional comments or suggestions about the maintenance scheduling process.
