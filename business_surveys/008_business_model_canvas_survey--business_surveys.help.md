# Business Model Canvas Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help business owners and stakeholders evaluate their business model and identify areas for improvement. It asks questions about various aspects of the business, including its value proposition, customer segments, revenue streams, key resources, key activities, partnerships, cost structure, competitive advantage, market position, growth potential, and areas needing improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly, considering your business's current state and future goals.
2. If you are unsure about a question, select the option that best describes your situation.
3. For questions with multiple selections (e.g., select_multiple), choose all options that apply.
4. Please provide clear and concise answers to the text-based questions (e.g., improvement areas, competitive advantage).
5. If a question is not relevant to your business, please select "Not Applicable" or skip it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): This is where you can enter your name and title/position for reference.
* **Your Role in the Business** (`respondent_role`, `text`, required): Describe your role or position within the business.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the business being evaluated.
* **Stakeholder Type** (`stakeholder_type`, `select_one`, required): Select your relationship to the business:
	+ Founder/Owner
	+ Employee
	+ Investor
	+ Partner
	+ Customer
	+ Advisor
	+ Other
* **Value Proposition Clarity** (`value_proposition`, `select_one`, required): How clear is the value proposition to customers?
	+ Very Clear
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat Clear
	+ Unclear
	+ Very Unclear
* **Customer Segments Understanding** (`customer_segments`, `select_one`, required): How well-defined are customer segments?
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Adequate
	+ Poor
	+ Needs Work
* **Revenue Streams Identified** (`revenue_streams`, `number`, required/optional): If applicable, enter the number of revenue streams you have.
* **Revenue Stream Viability** (`revenue_viability`, `select_one`, required): Are revenue streams sustainable?
	+ Highly Viable
	+ Viable
	+ Moderately Viable
	+ Questionable
	+ Not Viable
* **Key Resources Required** (`key_resources`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all key resources critical to the business:
	+ Financial Capital
	+ Human Resources
	+ Technology
	+ Intellectual Property
	+ Physical Assets
	+ Brand
* **Key Activities Complexity** (`key_activities`, `select_one`, required): How complex are core activities?
	+ Very Simple
	+ Simple
	+ Moderate
	+ Complex
	+ Very Complex
* **Key Partnerships Strength** (`key_partnerships`, `select_one`, optional): Are partnerships strong and stable?
	+ Very Strong
	+ Strong
	+ Adequate
	+ Weak
	+ No Partnerships
* **Cost Structure Efficiency** (`cost_structure`, `select_one`, required): Are operational costs under control?
	+ Very Efficient
	+ Efficient
	+ Acceptable
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Very Inefficient
* **Competitive Advantage** (`competitive_advantage`, `text`, required): Describe what differentiates your business from competitors.
* **Market Position Assessment** (`market_position`, `select_one`, required): How strong is your market position?
	+ Market Leader
	+ Strong Position
	+ Competitive
	+ Emerging
	+ At Risk
* **Growth Potential** (`growth_potential`, `select_one`, required): What is the potential for growth?
	+ Excellent
	+ Strong
	+ Good
	+ Limited
	+ Minimal
* **Areas Needing Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, `text`, required): Describe areas that need improvement.
* **Strategic Recommendations** (`strategic_recommendations`, `text`, optional): Provide suggestions for improvement.
