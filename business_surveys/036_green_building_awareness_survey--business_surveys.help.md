# Green Building Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Green Building Awareness Survey form. The form is a self-assessment tool used to gauge your knowledge, practices, and perceptions regarding green building and sustainability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each question carefully and select the most accurate response based on your understanding.
2. For select multiple options, choose all that apply or select "not sure" if you're unsure.
3. For select one option, choose the most relevant answer.
4. Fill in any text fields with brief, descriptive answers.
5. Submit the form once you've answered all questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Green Building Facts** (`green_building_facts`, text, optional): This question is asking about your understanding of basic green building facts. Answer with a brief description of your current knowledge.
* **What is Green Building Importance** (`what_is_green_building_importance`, number, optional): This question gauges your understanding of the importance of green building practices. Answer with a number indicating your level of understanding (e.g., 1-10).
* **Sustainability Commitment** (`sustainability_commitment`, select multiple, optional): Choose all that apply regarding your organization's or your individual commitment to sustainability.
* **Knowledge of Energy Efficiency** (`knowledge_of_energy_efficiency`, number, optional): This question evaluates your understanding of energy-efficient practices. Answer with a number indicating your level of understanding (e.g., 1-10).
* **Knowledge of Water Efficiency** (`knowledge_of_water_efficiency`, select multiple, optional): Choose all that apply regarding your understanding of water-efficient practices.
* **Green Building Expertise** (`green_building_expertise`, select multiple, optional): Select all that apply regarding your level of expertise in green building practices.
* **Green Building Experience** (`green_building_experience`, select multiple, optional): Choose all that apply regarding your experience with green building projects or practices.
* **Education Programs** (`education_programs`, select multiple, optional): Select all that apply regarding education programs or workshops you've attended related to green building and sustainability.
* **Green Building Stakeholders** (`green_building_stakeholders`, select multiple, optional): Choose all that apply regarding your organization's or your individual stake in green building projects or practices.
* **Green Building Certifications** (`green_building_certifications`, select multiple, optional): Select all that apply regarding any green building certifications you hold.
* **Green Building Incentives** (`green_building_incentives`, select one, optional): Choose the most relevant incentive for green building practices.
* **Green Building Challenges** (`green_building_challenges`, select multiple, optional): Select all that apply regarding challenges you face in implementing green building practices.
* **Green Building Benefits** (`green_building_benefits`, select multiple, optional): Choose all that apply regarding the benefits of green building practices.
* **Further Steps** (`green_building_further_steps`, select multiple, optional): Choose all that apply regarding further actions you plan to take to improve your green building practices.
* **Green Building Awareness** (`green_building_awareness`, select multiple, optional): Select all that apply regarding your current level of awareness regarding green building and sustainability.
* **Action Plan** (`green_building_action_plan`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your action plan to improve your green building practices.
* **Green Building Commitment** (`green_building_commitment`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your organization's or your individual commitment to green building and sustainability.
* **Green Building Awareness Score** (`green_building_awareness_score`, number, optional): Answer with a number indicating your current level of awareness (e.g., 1-10).
* **Green Building Importance Score** (`green_building_importance_score`, number, optional): Answer with a number indicating your level of understanding of green building importance (e.g., 1-10).
