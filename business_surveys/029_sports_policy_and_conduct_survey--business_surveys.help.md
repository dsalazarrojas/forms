# Sports Policy And Conduct Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and information about sports policies and conduct practices within your organization. It's a tool to help you assess and refine these policies, ensuring they align with your organization's goals and values.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully, and answer honestly to the best of your ability.
2. For questions with multiple options, select the one that best describes your current situation or perspective.
3. If a question doesn't apply to you, or you're unsure about the answer, please provide an explanation in the text field provided.
4. Use the provided options for questions that ask for specific choices (e.g., select one or multiple options).
5. If you're unable to complete the form in one sitting, don't worry! You can save your progress and come back to it later.
