# Time Certification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Time Certification Survey form is designed to gather essential information related to employee certifications, ensuring accurate and up-to-date records within the organization. This includes certifications for time-off, vacation, and other types of absences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the relevant information for the certification period, including the date and time period (First Quarter, Second Quarter, etc.).
2. Enter the certification start and end dates, if applicable.
3. Specify the employee's name and department.
4. Choose the certification status (Certified, Not Certified, Pending).
5. Enter any comments or feedback about the certification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **time_certification_survey_form** (`time_certification_survey_form`, text, Optional): This field is the main form section, where you can enter any additional comments or information related to the certification.
* **date** (`date`, date, Optional): Enter the date of the certification.
* **time_period** (`time_period`, select_one, Optional): Choose the time period for which the certification applies (First Quarter, Second Quarter, etc.).
* **certification_start_date** (`certification_start_date`, date, Optional): If applicable, enter the start date of the certification.
* **certification_end_date** (`certification_end_date`, date, Optional): If applicable, enter the end date of the certification.
* **employee_name** (`employee_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the employee for whom the certification is being made.
* **department** (`department`, select_one, Optional): Choose the department for which the certification is related.
* **certification_status** (`certification_status`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the certification status (Certified, Not Certified, Pending).
* **manager_name** (`manager_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the manager responsible for the certification.
* **certification_note** (`certification_note`, text, Optional): Enter any notes or comments about the certification.
* **certification_comment** (`certification_comment`, text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the certification.
* **certification_rating** (`certification_rating`, number, Optional): Enter a rating for the certification (if applicable).
* **certification_feedback** (`certification_feedback`, text, Optional): Enter any feedback or suggestions about the certification.
* **certification_remarks** (`certification_remarks`, text, Optional): Enter any additional remarks or comments about the certification.
* **certification_suggestions** (`certification_suggestions`, text, Optional): Enter any suggestions or ideas for future certifications.
* **certification_review** (`certification_review`, text, Optional): Enter a review or evaluation of the certification process.
* **certification_approval** (`certification_approval`, select_one, Optional): Select the approval status of the certification (Approved, Not Approved, Pending).
* **time_tracking** (`time_tracking`, select_one, Optional): Choose the time tracking status of the certification (On Time, Off Time, Late).
* **certification_type** (`certification_type`, select_one, Optional): Choose the type of certification (Overtime, Vacation, Sick Leave).
* **certification_hours** (`certification_hours`, number, Optional): Enter the number of hours related to the certification (if applicable).
