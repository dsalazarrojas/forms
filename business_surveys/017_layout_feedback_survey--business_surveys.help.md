# layout_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback on the layout of a specific design. It helps us understand how users perceive and interact with the layout to identify areas of improvement and inform future design decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Select the page number you are providing feedback on.
* Choose your answer to the question on that page.
* Enter any relevant feedback or comments.
* Enter your contact information.
* Add any additional comments if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **layout_feedback_survey (page 1)**: The name of this field is the main question being asked, and it is asking for an overall assessment of the layout.
* **layout_feedback (page 2)**: This question asks how much you agree or disagree with the current layout on a scale of Not at all, Somewhat, or Very much.
* **feedback_1 (page 3)**: Select all that apply from the options provided to give feedback on specific aspects of the layout.
* **rating (page 4)**: This field is asking for your rating of the layout on a scale of 1-5.
* **feedback_2 (page 5)**: Enter any additional feedback you have about the layout.
* **layout_feedback_2 (page 6)**: Enter any other feedback you have about the layout.
* **feedback_3 (page 7)**: This field is for any additional comments you would like to make about the layout.
* **contact_info (page 8)**: Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you for further clarification or discussion.
* **additional_comments (page 9)**: Enter any additional comments or thoughts about the layout.
* **layout_feedback_3 (page 10)**: Select all that apply from the options provided to give feedback on specific aspects of the layout.
