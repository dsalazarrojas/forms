# Financial Forecast Accuracy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help finance teams and business leaders evaluate the accuracy of their financial forecasts. It asks about the level of confidence in forecast accuracy, the causes of forecast variance, and the potential financial impact of inaccurate forecasts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by reading the introductory text to understand the purpose and context of the survey.
2. Rate the level of confidence in the accuracy of your financial forecasts.
3. Identify the causes of forecast variance.
4. Share any ideas or suggestions for improving forecast accuracy.
5. Provide actual and forecast values for the current period.
6. Estimate the potential error in your forecasts.
7. Estimate the deviation between actual and forecast values.
8. Describe the potential financial impact of forecast errors.
9. Choose the level of financial impact.
10. Estimate the amount of the financial impact.
11. Choose the units for measuring the financial impact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro** (`intro`, `text`, not required): A brief introduction to the survey and its purpose.
* **Rate** (`accuracy_rate`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the level of confidence in the accuracy of your financial forecasts:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Confidence** (`confidence_interval`, `number`, not required): Estimate the level of confidence in the accuracy of your financial forecasts as a percentage.
* **Causes of variance** (`causes_of_variance`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose the causes of forecast variance:
	+ Financial model
	+ Economic factors
	+ Other
* **Ideas for improvement** (`ideas_for_improvement`, `text`, not required): Share any ideas or suggestions for improving forecast accuracy.
* **Accuracy** (`forecast_accuracy`, `number`, not required): Enter the actual and forecast values for the current period.
* **Actual** (`actual_forecast`, `number`, not required): Enter the actual values for the current period.
* **Error** (`forecast_error`, `number`, not required): Estimate the potential error in your forecasts.
* **Deviation** (`forecast_deviation`, `number`, not required): Estimate the deviation between actual and forecast values.
* **Financial Impact** (`financial_impact`, `text`, not required): Describe the potential financial impact of forecast errors.
* **Financial Impact** (`financial_impact`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the level of financial impact:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Amount** (`financial_impact_amount`, `number`, not required): Estimate the amount of the financial impact.
* **Units** (`financial_impact_units`, `text`, not required): Choose the units for measuring the financial impact.
