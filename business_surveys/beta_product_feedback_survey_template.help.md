# beta_product_feedback_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This beta product feedback survey is designed to collect your opinions on the beta product experience. Your participation will help the development team identify areas for improvement and create a better product for all users.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide feedback, complete the following steps:

1. **Rate Your Overall Satisfaction**: Select one of the six options that best describes your level of satisfaction with the beta product experience.
2. **Identify Useful Features**: Choose all the features you used or evaluated during your beta product experience. Select all that apply.
3. **Aspects Influencing Your Opinion**: Select all the aspects that most influenced your opinion of the product.
4. **Recommendation**: Indicate whether you would recommend the beta product to others.
5. **Improvement Suggestion**: Provide a single, concise suggestion for improvement.
6. **Additional Comments**: Share any additional comments or suggestions for the development team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How Satisfied Are You?** (`very_satisfied`, `single`, required): This question helps us understand your overall satisfaction with the beta product experience. Choose one of the six options that best describes your feelings.
* **Which Features Did You Use or Evaluate?** (`core_functionality`, `user_interface`, `performance`, `integration`, `reporting`, `security`, multiple, required): Select all the features you used or evaluated during your beta product experience. This will help us understand which areas need improvement.
* **Which Aspects Influenced Your Opinion?** (`ease_of_use`, `performance`, `design`, `feature_set`, `reliability`, `customer_support`, multiple, required): This question helps us understand what aspects of the product most influenced your opinion. Choose all that apply.
* **Would You Recommend the Beta Product?** (`true`, `false`, `maybe`, single, required): Indicate whether you would recommend the beta product to others.
* **Single Most Important Improvement Suggestion**: (`single`, text, optional): Provide a single, concise suggestion for improvement. This will help us prioritize development efforts.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions**: (`text`, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions for the development team. This is an opportunity to provide more detailed feedback.
