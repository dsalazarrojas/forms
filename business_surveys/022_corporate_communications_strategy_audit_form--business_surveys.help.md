# Corporate Communications Strategy Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Communications Strategy Audit Form is designed to help assess the current state of corporate communications within the organization. It collects information on various aspects of corporate communications and helps identify areas that need improvement. This form will guide you through each section to ensure a thorough and accurate assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Review each section carefully and select the most accurate response for each field.
2. Ensure you answer all required questions to ensure a comprehensive assessment.
3. Use the options provided for each field as a guide for your response.
4. If you're unsure about a field, please contact the relevant team member for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Messaging Consistency
* **Messaging Consistency** (`Yes/No`, Optional): This field assesses how consistent the organization's messaging is across different channels and teams. Please select 'Yes' if the messaging is consistent, or 'No' otherwise.

### 2. Performance
* **Performance** (`Yes/No`, Optional): This field evaluates the performance of corporate communications in meeting its goals and objectives. Select 'Yes' if the performance is satisfactory, or 'No' otherwise.

### 3. Stakeholder Communication
* **Stakeholder Communication** (Note, Required): This field is a free-text field where you can provide any information or insights about stakeholder communication within the organization. Please provide any relevant details or concerns.

### 4. Messaging Channels
* **Messaging Channels** (`Yes/No`, Required): This field determines the availability and effectiveness of messaging channels within the organization. Select 'Yes' if messaging channels are available and effective, or 'No' otherwise.

### 5. Performance Indicators
* **Performance Indicators** (`Yes/No`, Required): This field evaluates the use and relevance of performance indicators for corporate communications. Select 'Yes' if performance indicators are used and relevant, or 'No' otherwise.

### 6. Stakeholder Communication Channels
* **Stakeholder Communication Channels** (`Yes/No`, Required): This field assesses the communication channels used to engage with stakeholders. Select 'Yes' if the channels are effective, or 'No' otherwise.

### 7. Communication Channels Performance
* **Communication Channels Performance** (`Yes/No`, Required): This field evaluates the performance of communication channels in achieving corporate goals. Select 'Yes' if the performance is satisfactory, or 'No' otherwise.

### 8. Messaging Consistency
* **Messaging Consistency** (`Yes/No`, Required): This field is similar to Field 1, assessing messaging consistency across different channels and teams. Select 'Yes' if the messaging is consistent, or 'No' otherwise.

### 9. Online Data Collection
* **Online Data Collection** (Note, Optional): This field is a free-text field where you can provide any information or insights about online data collection within the organization. Please provide any relevant details or concerns.

### 10. Clearer Insights
* **Clearer Insights** (Note, Optional): This field is a free-text field where you can provide any information or insights about clearer insights within the organization. Please provide any relevant details or concerns.
