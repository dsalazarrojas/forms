# Risk And Opportunity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk And Opportunity Assessment form is used to identify and document potential risks and opportunities within the organization. This form helps assess the impact, likelihood, and actions needed to address threats, as well as the owner and their contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form and ensure you have a clear understanding of the items you are assessing.
2. Select the "Risk Title" field to describe the specific risk or opportunity being assessed.
3. Choose the relevant "Risk Category" for the item being assessed.
4. Evaluate the potential "Impact" and "Likelihood" of the risk or opportunity.
5. Indicate whether "Actions" are being taken to address the risk or opportunity.
6. Select the "Actions Owner" to indicate who is responsible for addressing the risk or opportunity.
7. Provide a detailed "Risk Description" of the specific risk or opportunity.
8. Describe the "Actions Description" for addressing the risk or opportunity.
9. Enter the "Owner Name" and "Owner Email" and "Owner Phone" for the person responsible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Risk Title** (`title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for the risk or opportunity being assessed.
* **Risk Category** (`risk_category`, select_multiple, required): Select the category that best describes the type of risk or opportunity (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **Impact** (`impact`, number, required): Evaluate the potential impact of the risk or opportunity on the organization (e.g., 1-10).
* **Likelihood** (`likelihood`, number, required): Evaluate the likelihood of the risk or opportunity occurring (e.g., 1-10).
* **Actions** (`actions`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether actions are being taken to address the risk or opportunity (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Actions Owner** (`actions_owner`, select_one, required): Select the person responsible for addressing the risk or opportunity.
* **Owner** (`owner`, select_one, required): Select who is the owner of the risk or opportunity.
* **Risk Description** (`risk_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the risk or opportunity.
* **Actions Description** (`actions_description`, text, required): Describe the actions being taken to address the risk or opportunity.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the owner of the risk or opportunity.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the owner of the risk or opportunity.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the owner of the risk or opportunity.

## Tips
- Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- Use clear and concise language in the descriptions and titles.
- Ensure the risk or opportunity is properly categorized and prioritized.
- Verify contact information for the owner is up-to-date.
