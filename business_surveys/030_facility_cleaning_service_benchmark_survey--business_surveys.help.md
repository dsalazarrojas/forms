# Facility Cleaning Service Benchmark Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and suggestions from facility owners and managers on the performance of the facility cleaning service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your facility's name and location in the "Facility Name" and "Location Name" fields.
2. Rate your satisfaction level with the cleaning service by selecting a number from 1 to 5 in the "Satisfaction Level" field.
3. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please type them in the "Improvement Suggestions" field.
4. Provide your assigned manager's name and title in the "Assigned Manager Name" and "Assigned Manager Title" fields.
5. Select the type of facility you manage in the "Facility Type" field.
6. Enter your contact email and phone number in the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" fields.
7. Provide any additional notes about your facility in the "Facility Notes" field.
8. Enter the facility's address and city in the "Facility Address" and "Facility City" fields.
9. If applicable, select any other facility type not listed in the "Facility Type" field in the "Other" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required/optional): This is the name of your facility. Enter the name of the facility you are evaluating.
* **Location Name** (`location_name`, text, required/optional): This is the location of your facility.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction level with the cleaning service by selecting a number from 1 to 5.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, required/optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them here.
* **Assigned Manager Name** (`assigned_manager_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the manager assigned to your facility.
* **Assigned Manager Title** (`assigned_manager_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the manager assigned to your facility.
* **Facility Type** (`facility_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of facility you manage.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Facility Location** (`facility_location`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about your facility's location.
* **Facility Notes** (`facility_notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about your facility.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of your facility.
* **Facility City** (`facility_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city of your facility.
* **Facility State** (`facility_state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state of your facility.

Note: Some fields are marked as required or optional, please complete all required fields to submit the form.
