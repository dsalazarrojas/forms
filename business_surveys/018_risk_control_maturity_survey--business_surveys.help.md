# Risk Control Maturity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Control Maturity Survey form is designed to evaluate the risk control maturity of an organization. This form is used to assess the effectiveness of risk control practices and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Risk Control Maturity Form** field, which is a multiple-choice question with three options.
2. Answer the **Risk Category** field by selecting one or more categories that best describe the risk.
3. Answer the **Risk Impact** field by selecting one or more impacts that best describe the risk.
4. Choose the **Risk Frequency** field by selecting one of the three frequency options.
5. Answer the **Risk Probability** field by entering a number between 0 and 100.
6. Answer the **Risk Likelihood** field by entering a number between 0 and 100.
