# Waste Management Services Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and ratings from customers about the waste management services provided by the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Answer all the questions and ratings according to your experience with the waste management services.
2. Make sure to select the relevant options for each question from the provided choices.
3. Fill in any additional comments or details in the "comments" field if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Waste Management Services Poll** (`waste_management_services_poll`): This is the title of the form which is not a field to fill, but a description of the form purpose.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`): Rate the quality of service provided by the waste management services. Please rate from 1 to 10.
* **Issues Encountered** (`issues_encountered`): Select all the issues you encountered with the waste management services. You can select multiple options.
* **Improvement Priorities** (`improvement_priorities`): Select the priority for improvements you would like to see in the waste management services. Please select one option.
* **Service Quality Rating** (`service_quality_rating`): Rate the overall quality of service provided by the waste management services. Please rate from 1 to 10.
* **Time Rating** (`time_rating`): Rate the time taken by the waste management services to complete the job. Please rate from 1 to 10.
* **Date of Last Service** (`date_of_last_service`): Please enter the date of your last waste management service.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Please provide any additional contact information that may be helpful for the organization to reach you back.
* **Email** (`email`): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Please enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about the waste management services.
* **Service Providers** (`service_providers`): Select the service provider you received the waste management service from. You can select only one option.
* **Service Frequency** (`service_frequency`): Select the frequency of your waste management service. You can select only one option.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`): Select the method you used to contact the waste management services. You can select only one option.
