# donor_demographic_research_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The donor demographic research survey is designed to gather information about our donors to better understand their demographic characteristics, behaviors, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2.  Ensure that you only submit valid and accurate information.
3.  Review each question carefully to ensure that you understand the options available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction** (`introduction`, note, required: false): This is a note field to provide a brief introduction to the form.
* **name** (`name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on our donor records.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Please enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **address** (`address`, text, required: false): Please enter your physical address or the address where you would like to receive communications from us.
* **occupation** (`occupation`, text, required: false): Please enter your occupation or the type of work you do.
* **income** (`income`, number, required: false): Please enter your annual income.
* **donation_frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Please select how frequently you make donations.
* **donation_amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required: false): Please enter the amount you typically donate.
* **donation_method** (`donation_method`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select the methods you use to make donations (credit card, check, bank transfer).
* **favorite_nonprofit** (`favorite_nonprofit`, select_one, required: false): Please select your favorite nonprofit organization.
* **why_cause** (`why_cause`, text, required: false): Please enter why you support our cause.
* **motivation** (`motivation`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select the reasons why you support our cause (personal, family, community, social, other).
* **demographics** (`demographics`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select the demographics that you identify with (gender, age, location, income, occupation).
* **survey_completed** (`survey_completed`, select_one, required: false): Please confirm if you have completed this survey before.
* **favorite_charity** (`favorite_charity`, select_one, required: false): Please select your favorite charity organization.
* **how_did_you_hear** (`how_did_you_hear`, text, required: false): Please enter how you heard about our organization.
* **how_often** (`how_often`, select_one, required: false): Please select how often you interact with our organization.
* **motivation_reason** (`motivation_reason`, text, required: false): Please enter the reason for your support.
* **nonprofit_relationship** (`nonprofit_relationship`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select your relationship with the nonprofit organization.
* **survey_completed_date** (`survey_completed_date`, date, required: false): Please enter the date you completed this survey.
* **favorite_program** (`favorite_program`, select_one, required: false): Please select your favorite program or service we offer.
* **donation_frequency_important** (`donation_frequency_important`, select_one, required: false): Please select how important donation frequency is to you.
* **motivation_description** (`motivation_description`, text, required: false): Please enter a brief description of your motivation for supporting our organization.
* **survey_comments** (`survey_comments`, text, required: false): Please enter any additional comments or feedback about this survey.

Please note that all fields are optional, but providing accurate and up-to-date information will help us better understand your needs and preferences. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
