# Economic Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to collect information on economic conditions and priorities from employees or stakeholders. It contains multiple sections with different types of questions, including select multiple, select one, text, and email.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please ensure that you have read through the form completely before starting to fill it out.
- Answer each question according to your actual knowledge and experience.
- If a question is not applicable, mark the "No" option or leave it blank.
- You can save your progress and come back later if needed.
- Once you've completed the form, click the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **economic_conditions** (`select_multiple`, required): This section covers economic conditions. Select all conditions that apply to your experience.
  - Please choose all that apply from the given options.
- **Priorities** (`select_one`, required): This question asks about your priorities in relation to economic conditions.
  - Choose one of the given options that best describes your priorities.
- **Outlook** (`text`, required): Describe your current outlook on the economic conditions.
  - Type a brief description of your current outlook.
- **organization** (`select_one`, required): This question asks about the current state of your organization.
  - Choose one of the given options that best describes your organization's condition.
- **comments** (`note`, optional): Add any additional comments related to the economic conditions or your experience.
  - You can type any comments or observations you want to share.
- **email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email for further communication.
  - This is an optional field, but it's recommended to provide your email so we can get in touch with you.
- **phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
  - This is an optional field, but it's recommended to provide your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
- **Economic Priority 1** (`select_one`, required): Choose one of the given options that best describes the first economic priority.
  - Select one option from the list.
- **Economic Priority 2** (`text`, required): Describe the second economic priority.
  - Type a brief description of the second economic priority.
- **phone** (`text`, optional): This field is currently not used and can be ignored.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
- If you're unsure about any of the questions, ask for clarification.
- You can ask for help if you need it.
- Once you've submitted the form, you can't edit it.
