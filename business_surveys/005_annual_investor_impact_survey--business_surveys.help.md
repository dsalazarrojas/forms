# Annual Investor Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Investor Impact Survey is designed to gather data from investors about their experiences and perceptions regarding investments made in the past year. This information will help the organization better understand the impact of their investments and make informed decisions for future growth.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form honestly and accurately based on your genuine experiences and perceptions.
2. Review each question carefully and ensure you understand what's being asked.
3. Ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
4. Submit the form after filling out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`investor_name`, Text, Required): Your full name as it appears in investor records.
* **Email Address** (`investor_email`, Email, Required): Your contact email for follow-up communication.
* **Type of Investment** (`investment_type`, Select One, Required): The type of investment you made, such as equity, fixed income, or other.
* **Date of Initial Investment** (`investment_date`, Date, Optional): When you made your initial investment.
* **Investment Amount** (`investment_amount`, Text, Optional): The dollar amount or approximate range of the investment.
* **How long have you been an investor** (`years_investor`, Select One, Optional): How long you've been investing, from less than a year to more than 10 years.
* **My understanding of investment impact has improved** (`impact_understanding`, Select One, Required): Rate your agreement on a scale.
* **Impact metrics clearly demonstrate value** (`impact_metrics`, Select One, Required): Rate your agreement on a scale.
* **Financial performance meets expectations** (`financial_performance`, Select One, Required): Rate your agreement on a scale.
* **I am comfortable with the risk profile** (`risk_comfort`, Select One, Required): Rate your comfort level with the risk profile.
* **Communication frequency is adequate** (`communication_frequency`, Select One, Required): Rate your agreement on a scale.
* **Communication is clear and informative** (`communication_quality`, Select One, Required): Rate your agreement on a scale.
* **I have confidence in management team** (`management_confidence`, Select One, Required): Rate your confidence in the management team.
* **Investment aligns with my values and mission** (`mission_alignment`, Select One, Required): Rate your agreement on a scale.
* **Transparency level of reporting** (`transparency_level`, Select One, Required): Rate the transparency level of reporting on a scale.
* **Likelihood to recommend this investment** (`likelihood_recommend`, Select One, Required): Rate your likelihood of recommending this investment.
* **Areas for improvement** (`areas_improvement`, Text, Optional): Suggest areas where the investment or its management could improve.
* **Additional concerns or questions** (`additional_concerns`, Text, Optional): Express any additional concerns or questions you may have.
* **Would you consider additional investment** (`additional_investment`, Select One, Optional): Rate your agreement on a scale.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, Select One, Optional): Choose your preferred method of communication.

## Tips

* Answer each question based on your genuine experiences and perceptions.
* Ensure you answer all required fields to provide valuable insights.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to ask or seek clarification.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
