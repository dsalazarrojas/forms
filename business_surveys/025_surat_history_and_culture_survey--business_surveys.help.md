# surat_history_and_culture_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information about the user's interests and engagement with Surat's heritage and culture.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reading the introduction to understand the purpose of the form.
2. Select all the heritage interests that apply to you from the multiple-choice options.
3. Choose all the cultural engagement activities that you participate in from the multiple-choice options.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback on the education section if you have any.
5. Enter your name, email, and phone number if you wish to provide contact information.
6. Finally, submit the form to complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header**: 
	+ Enter any information or comments you'd like to include about the purpose of this form.
* **Introduction**: 
	+ Please read this section to understand the purpose and instructions for this form.
* **Heritage interests**: 
	+ Select all the aspects of Surat’s heritage that you are interested in.
	+ Example: You might be interested in history, culture, or traditions.
* **Cultural engagement**: 
	+ Choose all the cultural activities you participate in.
	+ Example: You might participate in festivals, music, or sports activities.
* **Education**: 
	+ Write any comments or feedback about your educational experiences with Surat's heritage and culture.
* **Community feedback**: 
	+ This is a note section where you can provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Name**: 
	+ Enter your name for contact information.
* **Email**: 
	+ Enter your email for contact information.
* **Phone**: 
	+ Enter your phone number for contact information.
* **Submission Date**: 
	+ This will be automatically populated with the current date.
* **Submission Time**: 
	+ This will be automatically populated with the current time.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, you can ask for help.
* Remember to submit the form to complete it.
