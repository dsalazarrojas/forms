# Manifestation Techniques Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manifestation Techniques Survey is a form designed to gather information about your experiences with various manifestation techniques. This data will be used to better understand how users engage with these techniques and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review the survey questions and select the options that best describe your experience.
2. For questions with a scale (e.g., "Please rate your experience with the following manifestation techniques on a scale from 1 to 5"), choose the number that best reflects your level of experience.
3. For questions with a "yes/no" or "select one" option, choose the answer that best applies to you.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the text fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **techniques_used** (`techniques_used`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about the manifestation techniques you have used in the past. Select all the techniques that apply to you.
* **manifestation_experience** (`manifestation_experience`, select_one, required: false): Please rate your experience with the following manifestation techniques on a scale from 1 to 5. Choose the number that best reflects your level of experience.
* **manifestation_method** (`manifestation_method`, text, required: false): Describe the method you used for manifestation.
* **manifestation_frequency** (`manifestation_frequency`, number, required: false): Report the frequency of your manifestation attempts.
* **manifestation_time** (`manifestation_time`, date, required: false): Record the date of your last manifestation attempt.
* **manifestation_location** (`manifestation_location`, text, required: false): Indicate the location where you attempted manifestation.
* **comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide your email address for future contact.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for follow-up.
* **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience.
* **additional_phone** (`additional_phone`, text, required: false): Enter any additional phone numbers for follow-up.
* **additional_email** (`additional_email`, email, required: false): Enter any additional email addresses for follow-up.

Note: Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly to ensure accurate data collection. Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey!
