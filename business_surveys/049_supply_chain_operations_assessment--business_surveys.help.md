# Supply Chain Operations Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess the performance of a company's supply chain operations. It includes various sections that evaluate different aspects of the supply chain management processes, such as bottlenecks, rating scales, and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out each section of the form according to your company's supply chain performance.
- Answer all the questions truthfully to provide accurate insights.
- Use the rating scales to rate your company's performance on each aspect of supply chain operations.
- Provide feedback and comments where necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department Name** (Department Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the department responsible for supply chain operations.
* **Assessment Rating** (Assessment Rating, number, optional): Enter a numerical rating for the company's overall supply chain performance.
* **Bottleneck 1 - 1** (Bottleneck 1 - 1, select_one, optional): Indicate if there is a bottleneck in this aspect of your supply chain operations (yes/no).

* **Bottleneck 2 - 2** (Bottleneck 2 - 2, select_one, optional): Indicate if there is a bottleneck in this aspect of your supply chain operations (yes/no).
* **Bottleneck 3 - 1** (Bottleneck 3 - 1, select_one, optional): Indicate if there is a bottleneck in this aspect of your supply chain operations (yes/no).
* **Feedback** (Feedback, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback about your company's supply chain operations.
* **Date of Assessment** (Date of Assessment, date, optional): Enter the date this assessment was performed.
* **Time of Assessment** (Time of Assessment, time, optional): Enter the time this assessment was performed.
* **Rating Scale 1** (Rating Scale 1, select_multiple, optional): Rate your company's performance on this aspect of supply chain operations (Good/Fair).
* **Rating Scale 2** (Rating Scale 2, select_multiple, optional): Rate your company's performance on this aspect of supply chain operations (Good/Fair).

* **Note 1** (Note 1, note, optional): Add any additional note about your company's supply chain operations.
