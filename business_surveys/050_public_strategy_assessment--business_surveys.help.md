# Public Strategy Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about individuals and their roles within an organization for public strategy assessment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First name and Last name in the top section.
2. Enter your Email address and Company name in the top section.
3. Choose your role within the organization from the options provided (CEO, Marketing, Operations).
4. For each additional section, fill in the First name, Last name, Email, Company name, and choose your role within the organization.
5. Enter your budget and Timeline for the strategy, if applicable.
6. Provide Comments on the strategy, if any.
7. Repeat the process for each additional section.
8. Finally, click on the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Company name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, required): Choose your role within the organization from the options provided (CEO, Marketing, Operations).
* **Strategy** (`strategy`, text, optional): Enter a description of your strategy.
* **Goal** (`goal`, text, optional): Enter your goal related to the strategy.
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, optional): Enter your budget for the strategy.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, time, optional): Enter your timeline for the strategy.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments on the strategy.
* **First name 1** (`first_name_1`, text, required): Enter the first name of the additional stakeholder.
* **Last name 1** (`last_name_1`, text, required): Enter the last name of the additional stakeholder.
* **Email 1** (`email_1`, email, required): Enter the email address of the additional stakeholder.
* **Company name 1** (`company_name_1`, text, optional): Enter the company name of the additional stakeholder.
* **Role 1** (`role_1`, select_one, required): Choose the role of the additional stakeholder within the organization.
* **First name 2** (`first_name_2`, text, optional): Enter the first name of the additional stakeholder.
* **Last name 2** (`last_name_2`, text, optional): Enter the last name of the additional stakeholder.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the additional stakeholder.
* **Company name 2** (`company_name_2`, text, optional): Enter the company name of the additional stakeholder.
* **Role 2** (`role_2`, select_one, required): Choose the role of the additional stakeholder within the organization.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Use the provided options for the roles.
- Be concise in your comments and strategy description.
- Use proper email format for the email address.
