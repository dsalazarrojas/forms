# Compensation Transparency Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Compensation Transparency Perception Survey is designed to gather information about employees' perceptions of pay transparency within the organization. This survey aims to understand employees' comfort levels with discussing pay with colleagues, their understanding of pay ranges and decision criteria, and their overall perception of transparency.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the department that best represents your work area.
2. Choose how long you have worked with the company.
3. Indicate your current job level.
4. Answer questions about your comfort level with discussing pay with colleagues and understanding of pay ranges and decision criteria.
5. Rate your perception of HR communication and manager discussions about compensation.
6. Indicate your access to compensation documentation.
7. Answer questions about your awareness of company pay philosophy, market position, and desired level of transparency.
8. Rate the impact of transparency on your trust, job satisfaction, and performance.
9. Rate the current and desired level of transparency within the organization.
10. If you have any concerns about transparency, select all that apply.
11. If you have any additional comments or feedback, enter your thoughts in the text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, required: false): This field is an optional identifier for the survey response, allowing the organization to track individual feedback without linking it to the actual employee's identity.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose your department from the list of options provided. This will help us understand which department's perspectives are most relevant.
* **Years with Company** (`tenure`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the number of years you have worked with the company. This will help us gauge your familiarity with company policies and procedures.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose your current job level from the provided options. This will help us understand your role and responsibilities.
* **Comfort discussing pay with colleagues** (`transparency_comfort`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate your comfort level with discussing pay with colleagues. This will help us understand if you feel comfortable sharing pay information with your peers.
* **Awareness of pay range for my role** (`pay_range_knowledge`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose how aware you are of your own pay range. This will help us gauge your understanding of your compensation.
* **Awareness of pay ranges for similar roles** (`pay_range_knowledge_others`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose how aware you are of pay ranges for similar roles. This will help us understand if you have a clear view of market standards.
* **Understanding of pay decision criteria** (`criteria_understanding`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate your understanding of how pay decisions are made. This will help us identify potential areas for improvement in transparency.
* **Promotion pay increase criteria are clear** (`promotion_criteria_clear`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how clear promotion pay increase criteria are. This will help us understand if promotions are fair and transparent.
* **Raise criteria are clearly communicated** (`raise_criteria_clear`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how clearly raise criteria are communicated. This will help us understand if pay raises are fair and transparent.
* **Bonus criteria are transparent** (`bonus_criteria_clear`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate how transparent bonus criteria are. This will help us identify potential biases in bonus allocation.
* **HR communicates compensation well** (`hr_communication_rating`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate the quality of HR communication. This will help us understand if HR is providing clear and helpful information.
* **Manager discusses compensation openly** (`manager_communication_rating`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate your manager's openness with discussing compensation. This will help us understand if managers are providing clear and transparent information.
* **Can access compensation documentation** (`documentation_access`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose how easily you can access compensation documentation. This will help us understand if information is readily available.
* **Company pay philosophy is known to me** (`pay_philosophy_known`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate your understanding of the company's pay philosophy. This will help us understand if you feel aligned with company goals.
* **Know where company pays vs market** (`market_position_known`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose how aware you are of the company's market position. This will help us understand if you feel the company is fair in its compensation.
* **Transparency increases my trust** (`transparency_impact_trust`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate the impact of transparency on your trust in the company. This will help us understand if transparency has a positive effect on employee relationships.
* **Transparency increases job satisfaction** (`transparency_impact_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate the impact of transparency on your job satisfaction. This will help us understand if transparency has a positive effect on employee morale.
* **Transparency motivates better performance** (`transparency_impact_performance`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate the impact of transparency on your performance. This will help us understand if transparency has a positive effect on productivity.
* **Current level of transparency** (`current_transparency_level`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate the current level of transparency within the organization. This will help us understand if we are meeting employees' expectations.
* **Desired level of transparency** (`desired_transparency_level`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate the desired level of transparency within the organization. This will help us understand if we are meeting employees' expectations.
* **Concerns about transparency** (`concerns_about_transparency`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose any concerns you have about transparency. This will help us understand potential issues and areas for improvement.
* **Concerns about transparency (Select all that apply)** (`transparency_concerns`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all concerns you have about transparency. This will help us understand specific worries and areas for improvement.
* **Transparency recommendations** (`transparency_recommendations`, `text`, required: false): Enter any suggestions or recommendations for improving transparency. This will help us understand your ideas and feedback.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have. This will help us understand your thoughts and concerns.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly, as your responses will help us create a more accurate picture of pay transparency within the organization.
* If you have any concerns or feedback, use the "Concerns about transparency" or "Additional comments" fields to provide more information.
* We value your input and appreciate your participation in this survey.
