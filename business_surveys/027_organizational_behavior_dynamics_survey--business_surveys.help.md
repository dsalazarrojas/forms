# Organizational Behavior Dynamics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data about employees' behaviors, department, job titles, job functions, job levels, and other organizational dynamics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your Employee ID, which is a unique identifier assigned to you by your organization.
2. Select your Department from the provided options.
3. Provide your Job Title and Job Function.
4. Choose your Job Level (High or Low).
5. Enter your Team ID, if applicable.
6. Enter your Supervisor ID, if applicable.
7. Select your Work Status (Active or Inactive).
8. Choose whether you have Work Type (Yes or No).
9. Enter your Work Hours, if applicable.
10. Select your Work Week (Yes or No).
11. Choose whether you have Work Location (Yes or No).
12. Select your Work Shift (Yes or No).
13. Enter your Role, if applicable.
14. Enter your Manager ID, if applicable.
15. Enter your Organization Unit, if applicable.
16. Enter your Organization Level, if applicable.
17. Select your Work Type 2 (Yes or No).
18. Choose whether you have Work Location 2 (Yes or No).
19. Enter your Job Title 2, if applicable.
20. Enter your Job Function 2, if applicable.
21. Enter your Team Name, if applicable.
22. Enter your Supervisor Name, if applicable.
23. Enter your Organization Name, if applicable.
24. Enter your Organization Email, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your unique Employee ID assigned to you by your organization.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select your department from the provided options.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, required): Enter your job function.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_one, required): Choose your job level (High or Low).
* **Team ID** (`team_id`, text, optional): Enter your team ID, if applicable.
* **Supervisor ID** (`supervisor_id`, text, optional): Enter your supervisor ID, if applicable.
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, select_one, optional): Select your work status (Active or Inactive).
* **Work Type** (`work_type`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you have work type (Yes or No).
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, optional): Enter your work hours, if applicable.
* **Work Week** (`work_week`, select_multiple, optional): Select your work week (Yes or No).
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you have work location (Yes or No).
* **Work Shift** (`work_shift`, select_multiple, optional): Select your work shift (Yes or No).
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Enter your role, if applicable.
* **Manager ID** (`manager_id`, text, optional): Enter your manager ID, if applicable.
* **Organization Unit** (`org_unit`, text, optional): Enter your organization unit, if applicable.
* **Organization Level** (`org_level`, text, optional): Enter your organization level, if applicable.
* **Work Type 2** (`work_type_2`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you have work type 2 (Yes or No).
* **Work Location 2** (`work_location_2`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you have work location 2 (Yes or No).
* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, text, optional): Enter your job title 2, if applicable.
* **Job Function 2** (`job_function_2`, text, optional): Enter your job function 2, if applicable.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter your team name, if applicable.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter your supervisor name, if applicable.
* **Organization Name** (`org_name`, text, optional): Enter your organization name, if applicable.
* **Organization Email** (`org_email`, email, optional): Enter your organization email, if applicable.
