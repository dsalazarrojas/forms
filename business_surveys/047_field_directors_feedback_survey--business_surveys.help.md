# field_directors_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Directors Feedback Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from Field Directors on their overall satisfaction with the support they receive from the organization and any challenges they are facing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select one of the options for "Field Directors are satisfied with the overall support they receive from the organization."**: Choose one of the three options:
	* Field Directors are satisfied with the overall support they receive from the organization.
	* Field Directors are not satisfied with the overall support they receive from the organization.
	* Neutral.
2. **Enter any improvement ideas you have**: In the "improvement_ideas" field, type any suggestions or ideas you have for how the organization can improve its support.
3. **Describe any challenges you are facing**: In the "challenges" field, provide a detailed description of any challenges you are facing.
4. **Provide any additional feedback**: In the "feedback" field, provide any additional comments or feedback you have for the organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Field Directors are satisfied with the overall support they receive from the organization.** (`select_one`, required/optional): This field asks you to indicate your level of satisfaction with the overall support you receive from the organization.
- **improvement_ideas** (`text`, required/optional): This field is where you can enter any ideas or suggestions you have for how the organization can improve its support.
- **challenges** (`note`, required/optional): This field is for you to describe any challenges you are facing. Use the text area to provide as much detail as necessary.
- **feedback** (`text`, required/optional): This field is for any additional feedback or comments you have for the organization.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when describing any challenges you are facing.
* When entering improvement ideas, try to focus on concrete solutions rather than just suggestions.
* Remember to provide any additional context or background information that may be helpful for the organization to understand your feedback.
