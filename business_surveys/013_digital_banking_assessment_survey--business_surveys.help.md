# Digital Banking Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and ratings from users regarding the digital banking services provided by an institution.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the required fields as prompted.
2. Ensure that you have not already submitted a survey for the same branch and account.
3. Enter your feedback and ratings honestly and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Id** (1, number, required: false): Enter your unique User Id.
* **User Name** (2, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Branch Id** (3, select_one, required: false): Select the branch you interacted with using the options provided.
* **Account Id** (4, text, required: false): Enter your account number or other unique account identifier.
* **User Rating** (5, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the services you received, using a scale of your choice.
* **Branch Rating** (6, number, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the branch you interacted with, using a scale of your choice.
* **Feedback** (7, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have regarding your experience with the digital banking services.
* **Satisfaction** (8, number, required: false): This field is intended to measure your overall satisfaction with the services you received, but its purpose is unclear. Please use your discretion when filling this field.
* **Email** (9, email, required: false): Enter your email address for us to contact you regarding your feedback.
* **Phone** (10, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for us to contact you regarding your feedback.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when filling in your feedback and ratings.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your time and effort in helping us improve our services.
