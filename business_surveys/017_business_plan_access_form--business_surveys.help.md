# Business Plan Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Business Plan Access Form" is used to collect information from requesters who wish to access a business plan. This form helps us understand the reason for accessing the plan and ensures that the request is legitimate. By filling out this form, you will be asked to provide details about your identity, the business you are interested in, and the purpose of your request. This form is essential for ensuring confidentiality and maintaining a secure and transparent process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the "Business Plan Access Form," follow these steps:

1.  **Read the form carefully**: Understand the purpose and requirements of the form.
2.  Fill out your **Requester Information** (Section 1) with your name, title/role, organization, and email address.
3.  In the **Access Request Details** section (Section 2), specify the business plan you are interested in and the reason for your access request.
4.  Select your relationship to the business from the given options (Section 3).
5.  If applicable, fill out the **Investment Details** section (Section 4) with the amount and timeframe for your potential investment.
6.  Confirm that you agree to keep the business plan confidential by selecting "Yes, I agree" in Section 5.
7.  Confirm that you have read and accept the NDA terms by selecting "Yes, I accept" in Section 6.
8.  In the **Additional Information** section (Section 7), provide any relevant background information or relevant experience related to the business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`requester_name`, `text`, required): Enter your complete name.
* **Title or Role** (`requester_title`, `text`, required): Type in your professional title or position.
* **Organization Name** (`requester_organization`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the company or entity you represent.
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address associated with you or your organization.
* **Phone Number** (`requester_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your contact phone number (optional).
* **Business Plan - Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Type in the name of the business covered in the plan.
* **Reason for Accessing Business Plan** (`access_reason`, `select_one`, required): Choose a reason for accessing the business plan from the given options.
* **Your Relationship to the Business** (`relationship_type`, `select_one`, required): Select your relationship to the business from the given options.
* **Potential Investment Amount** (`investment_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount you are potentially investing, if applicable.
* **Investment Timeframe** (`investment_timeframe`, `select_one`, optional): Select the investment timeframe from the given options.
* **Agree to Keep the Business Plan Confidential** (`confidentiality_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to keep the business plan confidential.
* **Confirm NDA Acceptance** (`nda_acceptance`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have read and accept the NDA terms.
* **Background Information** (`background_information`, `text`, optional): Provide any background information about yourself or your experience related to the business.
* **Relevant Experience in This Industry** (`relevant_experience`, `text`, optional): Describe your experience in the industry related to the business.
* **Request Submission Date** (`request_submission_date`, `date`, optional): Select today's date.
