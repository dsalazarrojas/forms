# Business Agility Consulting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help business leaders assess their organization's current state of agility and identify areas for improvement. It's a self-assessment tool that will help you understand your organization's strengths and weaknesses and provide insights on how to achieve higher levels of agility.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly, and don't worry about right or wrong answers - your responses will help us understand your needs.
2. For multi-select questions (e.g., `select_multiple`, `select_one`), select all that apply or choose one option that best describes your situation.
3. For text fields (e.g., `text`, `note`), provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select your industry sector.
* **Organization Size** (`organization_size`, select_one, required): Choose the approximate number of employees in your organization.
* **Your Role** (`respondent_role`, select_one, required): Describe your position within the organization.
* **Agility Maturity** (`agility_maturity`, note, optional): Briefly describe your organization's current state of agility (e.g., low, moderate, high).
* **Agility Experience** (`agility_experience`, select_one, required): Choose how long your organization has practiced agility.
* **Current Frameworks** (`current_frameworks`, select_multiple, required): Select all frameworks used within your organization.
* **Teams Using Agile** (`teams_using_agile`, select_one, required): Choose the percentage of teams using agile.
* **Leadership Support** (`leadership_support`, select_one, required): Rate how supportive your leadership is.
* **Strategic Clarity** (`strategic_clarity`, select_one, required): Rate how clear your organizational strategy is.
* **Customer Focus** (`customer_focus`, select_one, required): Rate how customer-centric your organization is.
* **Decision Making Speed** (`decision_making_speed`, select_one, required): Choose how quickly decisions are made.
* **Team Autonomy** (`team_autonomy`, select_one, required): Rate how much autonomy teams have.
* **Cross-Functional Collaboration** (`collaboration`, select_one, required): Rate how well teams collaborate.
* **Innovation Culture** (`innovation_culture`, select_one, required): Rate how innovative your organization culture is.
* **Change Adaptability** (`change_adaptability`, select_one, required): Rate how well your organization adapts to change.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, note, optional): List any challenges your organization faces.
* **Main Challenges** (`main_challenges`, select_multiple, required): Select your top challenges.
* **Biggest Obstacle** (`biggest_obstacle`, text, required): Describe your single biggest obstacle.
* **Consulting Needs** (`consulting_needs`, note, optional): Briefly describe what help you need.
* **Services Needed** (`services_needed`, select_multiple, required): Select all services you might need from a consultant.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Enter your expected investment range.
* **Implementation Timeline** (`implementation_timeline`, select_one, required): Choose when you want to start implementing change.
* **Success Metrics** (`success_metrics`, text, optional): Describe how you will measure success.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any other insights or thoughts.

Note: The form's questions and labels are designed to be as clear and concise as possible, but if you're unsure about any question or option, please ask for clarification or describe your best effort understanding of the question's intent.
