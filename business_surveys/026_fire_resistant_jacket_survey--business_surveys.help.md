# fire_resistant_jacket_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fire_resistant_jacket_survey form is designed to gather user feedback on the performance and usability of fire resistant jackets. This form helps us understand how well the jackets meet your expectations and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:
1. Answer each question according to your experience and perception of the fire resistant jacket.
2. Please select one option for each question, as indicated by the label.
3. You may skip the "value_for_money" question if it does not apply to your experience.
4. For "manufacturer," you can select one or multiple options, as they are all relevant to this type of product.
5. Enter the model number for the fire resistant jacket you are evaluating.
6. Feel free to provide any additional comments about the jacket in the "comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How would you rate the overall performance of this fire resistant jacket?** (`jacket_performance`, `select_one`, required): This question asks for your opinion on the overall performance of the jacket, considering factors such as its ability to provide protection and functionality.
* **How comfortable is this fire resistant jacket?** (`comfort_level`, `select_one`, required): This question assesses how comfortable you felt when wearing the jacket.
* **How would you rate the weight of this fire resistant jacket?** (`weight_rating`, `select_one`, required): This question evaluates the perceived weight of the jacket, considering its usability and mobility.
* **What size is this fire resistant jacket?** (`size`, `select_one`, required): This question determines the fit and sizing of the jacket, which is crucial for its practicality.
* **How safe do you feel when wearing this fire resistant jacket?** (`safety_rating`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about your level of confidence in the jacket's safety features.
* **How would you rate the value for money of this fire resistant jacket?** (`value_for_money`, `select_one`, required=False): This question considers the jacket's price-to-performance ratio, but you may skip this question if it does not apply.
* **Would you recommend this fire resistant jacket to a colleague?** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, required=False): This question gauges your willingness to endorse the jacket to others.
* **What is your preferred manufacturer of fire resistant jacket?** (`manufacturer`, `select_multiple`, required): This question explores your preferred brand or manufacturer of fire resistant jackets.
* **What is the model number of the fire resistant jacket?** (`model_number`, `text`, required): Enter the exact model number of the jacket you are evaluating.
* **Do you have any comments about the fire resistant jacket?** (`comments`, `note`, required=False): This field is for providing any additional, more detailed feedback or suggestions about the jacket.
