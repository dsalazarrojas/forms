# Business Partner Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your business partner's details. It's an essential tool for our partnership collaboration, ensuring we have a clear understanding of your company's profile.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the Company Information section, which includes your company's name, type, years in business, and primary industry.
2. Move on to the Contact Information section, where you'll provide your contact person's name, title, email address, phone number, and business address.
3. In the Partnership Details section, select the areas of partnership interest and describe your services or products offered, target market, geographic service area, and partnership expectations.
4. Finally, provide any business references you're willing to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's official legal business name.
* **Type of Business** (`company_type`, select_one, required): Select your business structure or category from the options provided. This will help us understand your company's nature and how it operates.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, number, required): Enter the number of years your company has been in operation.
* **Primary Industry** (`primary_industry`, text, required): Describe your main business sector or field.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, number, required): Enter the current number of employees in your company.
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, number, required): Approximate your yearly revenue.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your primary contact person.
* **Title or Position** (`contact_title`, text, required): Enter the title or position of your primary contact person.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your company's primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your company's business phone number.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Enter your full business street address.
* **Areas of Partnership Interest** (`partnership_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select the applicable areas of partnership interest from the options provided.
* **Services or Products Offered** (`services_offered`, text, required): Describe your main services or products in a brief manner.
* **Target Market or Client Base** (`target_market`, text, required): Describe your primary customers or target market.
* **Geographic Service Area** (`geographic_reach`, text, required): Describe the regions you serve.
* **Partnership Expectations** (`partnership_expectations`, text, required): Describe what you seek from our partnership.
* **Can You Provide Business References** (`reference_provided`, text, required): If yes, provide company name and contact info.
