# Representative Compensation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about representative compensation surveys, which are essential for managers and surveyors to gauge their effectiveness and improve their feedback sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked as "Required" in the form.
2. For select options, choose the most suitable answer from the available choices.
3. Enter any additional information or comments where applicable.
4. Ensure that you answer all questions honestly and accurately to provide valuable insights.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, `text`, Optional): This is a brief title for the survey, which helps to identify its purpose and scope.
* **Survey Description** (`survey_description`, `text`, Optional): Briefly describe the purpose and objectives of the survey.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, Optional): Enter the department associated with the survey.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, Optional): Enter the job title associated with the survey.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter the name of the employee associated with the survey.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, `text`, Optional): Enter the job category associated with the survey.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, `text`, Optional): Enter the job level associated with the survey.
* **Base Pay** (`base_pay`, `number`, Optional): Enter the base pay associated with the survey.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, `text`, Optional): Enter any additional benefits associated with the survey.
* **Bonus** (`bonus`, `text`, Optional): Enter any bonuses associated with the survey.
* **Equity** (`equity`, `text`, Optional): Enter any equity associated with the survey.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, Optional): Enter the date the survey was conducted.
* **Manager Surveyed** (`manager_surveyed`, `select_one`, Optional): Choose how the manager was surveyed:
	+ Manager
	+ Supervisor
	+ Not Surveyed
* **How did you feel about the survey?** (`manager_surveyed_how`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Choose how you felt about the survey:
	+ Positive experience
	+ Negative experience
	+ No answer
* **How frequently do managers conduct surveys?** (`manager_surveyed_frequency`, `select_one`, Optional): Choose how frequently managers conduct surveys:
	+ Frequently
	+ Occasionally
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **What is the purpose of the survey?** (`manager_surveyed_purpose`, `select_one`, Optional): Choose the purpose of the survey:
	+ To collect feedback
	+ To evaluate performance
	+ To identify issues
	+ Other (specify)
* **Other Purpose** (`other_purpose_specify`, `text`, Optional): Enter any other purpose of the survey.
* **Manager Surveyed Comments** (`manager_surveyed_comments`, `text`, Optional): Enter any comments about the survey.
* **Surveyors Surveyed** (`surveyors_surveyed`, `select_one`, Optional): Choose if the surveyor was surveyed:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Surveyors Surveyed Comments** (`surveyors_surveyed_comments`, `text`, Optional): Enter any comments about the surveyor surveying.
* **How frequently do surveyors conduct surveys?** (`surveyors_surveyed_frequency`, `select_one`, Optional): Choose how frequently surveyors conduct surveys:
	+ Frequently
	+ Occasionally
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Surveyors Surveyed Purpose** (`surveyors_surveyed_purpose`, `select_one`, Optional): Choose the purpose of the surveyor surveying:
	+ To collect feedback
	+ To evaluate performance
	+ To identify issues
	+ Other (specify)
* **Other Purpose Specify Surveyors** (`other_purpose_specify_surveyors`, `text`, Optional): Enter any other purpose of the surveyor surveying.
* **Surveyors Surveyed Comments 2** (`surveyors_surveyed_comments_2`, `text`, Optional): Enter any comments about the surveyor surveying.
* **Surveyor** (`surveyor`, `text`, Optional): Enter the surveyor's name.
* **Other Purpose Specify** (`other_purpose_specify`, `text`, Optional): Enter any additional information or comments about the survey.
