# Accounting Ethics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accounting Ethics Survey is designed to gauge the opinions and perceptions of accountants within your firm on various aspects of ethics and reporting practices. This survey aims to gather information on respondents' roles, experience, familiarity with ethics codes, and confidence in reporting mechanisms, as well as their suggestions for improvements and preferences for anonymity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Respond to each question**: Answer all the questions truthfully, and provide specific examples if applicable.
2. **Choose your answers carefully**: Select the options that best fit your situation from the provided choices.
3. **Provide additional comments (optional)**: If you have any further thoughts or suggestions, you can add them in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Respondent role
* **Respondent role**: Select the role you hold within the firm or organization.
	+ Options: 
		- Staff accountant
		- Manager
		- Partner
		- Intern
		- Other

### 2. Years of accounting experience
* **Years of accounting experience**: Please enter the number of years you have been working as an accountant.
	+ Hint: Round to the nearest whole year.

### 3. Familiarity with code of ethics
* **Familiarity with code of ethics**: Indicate your level of familiarity with the firm's code of ethics.
	+ Options:
		- Very familiar
		- Somewhat familiar
		- Not very familiar
		- Not familiar at all

### 4. Have you witnessed unethical behavior
* **Have you witnessed unethical behavior**: Check "True" if you have witnessed any unethical behavior.
	+ Hint: You can include examples in the comments if you are comfortable doing so.

### 5. Confidence in reporting mechanisms
* **Confidence in reporting mechanisms**: Rate your confidence in the reporting mechanisms within your firm.
	+ Options:
		- Very confident
		- Somewhat confident
		- Neutral
		- Not confident
		- Not at all confident

### 6. Ethical training received
* **Ethical training received**: Indicate whether you have received any formal ethical training.
	+ Options:
		- Yes within the last 12 months
		- Yes over 12 months ago
		- No formal training

### 7. Adequacy of ethics policies
* **Adequacy of ethics policies**: Indicate whether the firm's current policies cover common ethical issues.
	+ Options:
		- Adequate
		- Partially adequate
		- Inadequate

### 8. Suggestions for improvement
* **Suggestions for improvement**: If you have any suggestions for improving ethics and reporting within the firm, please provide them in this field.

### 9. Anonymity preference
* **Would you like to remain anonymous?**: Choose whether you would prefer to remain anonymous or be identified.
	+ Options:
		- Yes remain anonymous
		- No, identify me

### 10. Additional comments
* **Additional comments**: If you have any further thoughts or feedback, please add them here.
