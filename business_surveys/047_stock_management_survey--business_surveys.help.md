# stock_management_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on stock management practices and policies within the organization. It aims to gather information on current stock levels, purchasing habits, and overall satisfaction with stock management practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Complete each page of the form, which covers various aspects of stock management practices.
3. Select the most relevant options for each question.
4. Ensure that you answer each question to the best of your ability, even if it's not always clear-cut.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the current stock level of our inventory?** (`stock_level`, `text`, optional): This question asks for a brief description of the current stock level of your inventory. Please provide a concise answer that accurately reflects your current stock situation.
* **How often do we order stock from suppliers?** (`frequent_ordering`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks how frequently you order stock from suppliers. Select all the frequencies that apply to your stock management practices. Possible answers include:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Never
* **What is your level of satisfaction with our current stock management practices?** (`satisfaction_level`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks for your level of satisfaction with the current stock management practices. Select one of the following answers:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **How often do we rotate stock to ensure freshness?** (`stock_rotation`, `text`, optional): This question asks for a description of how often you rotate stock to ensure freshness.
* **What is your level of control over stock purchases?** (`purchasing_control`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks for your level of control over stock purchases. Select all that apply:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **How often do we need to return stock to suppliers?** (`return_frequency`, `time`, optional): This question asks for a time-based answer on how often you need to return stock to suppliers. Please provide a specific time interval (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **What methods do we use to purchase stock?** (`purchase_method`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks for the methods you use to purchase stock. Select one of the following answers:
	+ Regular
	+ Irregular
* **What is the current stock return policy?** (`return_policy`, `text`, optional): This question asks for a description of the current stock return policy.
* **How often do we purchase stock?** (`purchase_frequency`, `number`, optional): This question asks for a numerical answer on how often you purchase stock. Please provide a specific number (e.g., weekly, daily, monthly).
* **How often do we need to purchase stock?** (`stock_purchasing`, `number`, optional): This question asks for a numerical answer on how often you need to purchase stock. Please provide a specific number (e.g., weekly, daily, monthly).
* **What is your level of satisfaction with our current stock inventory management?** (`stock_satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks for your level of satisfaction with the current stock inventory management practices. Select one of the following answers:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **How do we currently purchase stock, through regular or irregular methods?** (`purchase_method`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks for the methods you use to purchase stock. Select one of the following answers:
	+ Regular
	+ Irregular
