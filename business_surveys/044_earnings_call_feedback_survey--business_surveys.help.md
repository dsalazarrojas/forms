# Earnings Call Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Earnings Call Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback on company-specific earnings calls. It's a tool to help analyze the performance of companies such as Apple, Google, Amazon, and others.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the company for which you want to provide feedback (Apple, Google, Amazon, or Other).
2. Choose the date of the earnings call you're providing feedback on.
3. Select the time of the earnings call.
4. Evaluate the engagement level, communication level, presentation level, and overall impact of the call.
5. Provide feedback on presentation skills and communication skills.
6. Rate the team's work and preparedness.
7. Provide any additional comments on the call.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company** (`company`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the company for which you want to provide feedback. Choose from the list of available options: Apple, Google, Amazon, or Other.
* **Date of Earnings Call** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the earnings call.
* **Time of Earnings Call** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time of the earnings call.
* **Engagement Level** (`engagement_level`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the level of engagement during the call. Choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Communication Level** (`communication_level`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the level of communication during the call. Choose from Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Presentation Level** (`presentation_level`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from Strong, Fair, or Poor.
* **Overall Impact** (`overall_impact`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Confidence Level** (`confidence_level`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the level of confidence during the call. Choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Revenue Confidence Level** (`confidence_level_revenue`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the level of revenue confidence during the call. Choose from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Presentation Skills** (`presentation_skills`, `text`, required: false): Provide feedback on the presentation skills displayed during the call.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, `text`, required: false): Provide feedback on the communication skills displayed during the call.
* **Team Work** (`team_work`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from Excellent, Good, or Fair.
* **Preparedness** (`preparedness`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the level of preparedness during the call. Choose from Highly, Somewhat, or Not.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments on the call.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose whether you would like follow-up on any of the issues mentioned.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, required: false): Provide any next steps or actions that could be taken as a result of the feedback.
* **Next Steps Follow-up** (`next_steps_follow_up`, `text`, required: false): Provide any next steps or actions that could be taken as a result of the follow-up.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments on the call.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any comments on the call.
* **Comments Additional** (`comments_additional`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments on the call.
* **Comments Follow-up** (`comments_follow_up`, `text`, required: false): Provide any comments on the follow-up.
* **Comments Additional Comments** (`comments_additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments on the follow-up.
* **Comments Follow-up Additional** (`comments_follow_up_additional`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments on the follow-up.
