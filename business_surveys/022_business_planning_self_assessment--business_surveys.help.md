# Business Planning Self Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Planning Self Assessment is a comprehensive form that helps you evaluate your business's current state and plan for its future. This form is designed to assess your business's clarity on objectives, market analysis, financial planning, risk assessment, and access to support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. For questions with options, select the one that best describes your current state or approach.
3. If you're unsure about any question, use your best judgment and provide a clear answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (id: 2, label: Your Name, type: text, required: true): Enter your name as it appears on your business license or ID.
* **Business Name** (id: 3, label: Business Name, type: text, required: true): Enter your business's name as it appears on your business license or ID.
* **Assessment Date** (id: 4, label: Assessment Date, type: date, required: true): Select the date you're completing this assessment.
* **How Clear Are Your Business Objectives** (id: 6, label: How Clear Are Your Business Objectives, type: select_one, required: true): Rate the clarity of your business objectives. Options:
	+ Very Unclear
	+ Somewhat Unclear
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Clear
	+ Very Clear
* **Are Your Objectives Documented in Writing** (id: 7, label: Are Your Objectives Documented in Writing, type: select_one, required: true): Indicate if you've formalized your goals in writing.
	+ True
	+ Partially
	+ False
* **Do You Have Timelines for Your Objectives** (id: 8, label: Do You Have Timelines for Your Objectives, type: select_one, required: true): Rate the level of detail in your timelines for your objectives. Options:
	+ Yes, detailed timelines
	+ Yes, but vague timelines
	+ No timelines
* **Understanding of Your Market** (id: 10, label: Understanding of Your Market, type: select_one, required: true): Rate your understanding of your market. Options:
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Very Good
	+ Excellent
* **Awareness of Competitors** (id: 11, label: Awareness of Competitors, type: select_one, required: true): Rate your awareness of your competitors. Options:
	+ Not aware
	+ Somewhat aware
	+ Aware
	+ Very aware
	+ Expert level
* **Understanding of Market Trends** (id: 12, label: Understanding of Market Trends, type: select_one, required: true): Rate your understanding of market trends. Options:
	+ No understanding
	+ Limited understanding
	+ Moderate understanding
	+ Good understanding
	+ Excellent understanding
* **Confidence in Financial Projections** (id: 14, label: Confidence in Financial Projections, type: select_one, required: true): Rate your confidence in your financial projections. Options:
	+ Very Low
	+ Low
	+ Moderate
	+ High
	+ Very High
* **Cash Flow Management Readiness** (id: 15, label: Cash Flow Management Readiness, type: select_one, required: true): Rate your preparedness for cash flow management. Options:
	+ Not prepared
	+ Somewhat prepared
	+ Moderately prepared
	+ Well prepared
	+ Fully prepared
* **Funding Strategy Clarity** (id: 16, label: Funding Strategy Clarity, type: select_one, required: true): Rate the clarity of your funding approach. Options:
	+ Not clear
	+ Somewhat clear
	+ Clear
	+ Very clear
* **Have You Identified Key Risks** (id: 18, label: Have You Identified Key Risks, type: select_one, required: true): Rate if you've identified key risks in your business. Options:
	+ No risks identified
	+ Few risks identified
	+ Some risks identified
	+ Most risks identified
	+ All major risks identified
* **Risk Mitigation Plans in Place** (id: 19, label: Risk Mitigation Plans in Place, type: select_one, required: true): Rate if you have contingency plans for risks. Options:
	+ No plans
	+ Basic plans
	+ Adequate plans
	+ Comprehensive plans
* **Access to Business Advisors or Mentors** (id: 21, label: Access to Business Advisors or Mentors, type: select_one, required: true): Rate your access to business advisors or mentors. Options:
	+ No access
	+ Limited access
	+ Good access
	+ Excellent access
* **Top Areas Needing Improvement** (id: 22, label: Top Areas Needing Improvement, type: text, required: false): List areas that need improvement in your business.
