# Financial Reporting Timeline Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the frequency and nature of financial reporting tasks within an organization. The data collected will help the organization understand its financial reporting timeline and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the applicable options for each field that accurately reflect your experience with financial reporting within your department or team.
2. For fields with multiple options, choose all that apply.
3. Fill out all required fields, as indicated by the "required" label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Reporting Frequency (ID 1)
* **Reporting Frequency**: This field asks about the frequency of financial reporting tasks within your department or team. Select all the options that apply to your experience.
  - **Yes**: Financial reports are regularly prepared and submitted on time.
  - **No**: Financial reports are not regularly prepared and submitted on time.

### Reporting Deadlines (ID 2)
* **Reporting Deadlines**: This field asks about whether financial reports have deadlines that must be met. Select all the options that apply to your experience.
  - **Yes**: Financial reports have specific deadlines that must be met.
  - **No**: Financial reports do not have specific deadlines that must be met.

### Preparation Time (ID 3)
* **Preparation Time**: This field asks about the time it takes to prepare financial reports. Select all the options that apply to your experience.
  - **Yes**: It takes a significant amount of time to prepare financial reports.
  - **No**: It does not take a significant amount of time to prepare financial reports.

### Challenges (ID 4)
* **Challenges**: This field asks about any challenges you experience when preparing financial reports. Select all the options that apply to your experience.
  - **Yes**: There are challenges in preparing financial reports.
  - **No**: There are no challenges in preparing financial reports.

### Preparation Time Range (ID 5)
* **Preparation Time Range**: This field asks you to estimate the range of time it takes to prepare financial reports. Please enter the number of minutes it takes to prepare a financial report.
