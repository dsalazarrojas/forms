# Driver Income Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Income Survey is a tool designed to collect information about a driver's income and work-related experiences. This form will help gather data on various aspects of a driver's income and job, which will aid in providing insights and support to drivers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select your job type from the options provided. This will help categorize your income source.
2. Enter your annual income, if applicable.
3. Enter your weekly hours worked.
4. Enter your weekly wages.
5. Select the frequency of your job (e.g., 1-2 days, 3-5 days, 6-7 days).
6. Select your income source (e.g., Regular, Overtime, Bonus, Other).
7. Select the frequency of your income (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Biweekly, Monthly).
8. Enter the range of your income, if applicable.
9. Select your level of financial stress (e.g., High, Moderate, Low).
10. Select the level of your financial stress (e.g., High, Moderate, Low).
11. Select your financial influence, if applicable.
12. Select the level of your job insecurity (e.g., High, Moderate, Low).
13. Select the level of your financial safety (e.g., High, Moderate, Low).
14. Select the level of your income urgency (e.g., High, Moderate, Low).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Type (2)** (Driver, Rider, Delivery, Owner-Operator): Select your job type to categorize your income source.
* **Annual Income (3)**: Enter your total annual income.
* **Weekly Hours (4)**: Enter the number of hours you work weekly.
* **Weekly Wages (5)**: Enter your weekly wages.
* **Job Frequency (6)**: Select the frequency of your job (e.g., 1-2 days, 3-5 days, 6-7 days).
* **Income Source (7)** (Regular, Overtime, Bonus, Other): Select your income source.
* **Income Frequency (8)** (Daily, Weekly, Biweekly, Monthly): Select the frequency of your income.
* **Income Range (9)** (Number): Enter the range of your income, if applicable.
* **Financial Stress (10)** (High, Moderate, Low): Select your level of financial stress.
* **Financial Stress Level (11)** (High, Moderate, Low): Select your financial stress level.
* **Financial Influence (12)** (Number): Enter your financial influence, if applicable.
* **Job Insecurity (13)** (High, Moderate, Low): Select your level of job insecurity.
* **Job Insecurity Level (14)** (High, Moderate, Low): Select your job insecurity level.
* **Financial Safety (15)** (High, Moderate, Low): Select your level of financial safety.
* **Financial Safety Level (16)** (High, Moderate, Low): Select your financial safety level.
* **Income Urgency (17)** (High, Moderate, Low): Select your level of income urgency.
* **Income Urgency Level (18)** (High, Moderate, Low): Select your income urgency level.
