# Capital Access Training Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your interest in capital access training programs.
## How To Complete This Form
Please answer all questions in the form to ensure we understand your needs and interests.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter your business name.
* **Business Stage** (`business_stage`, select_one, required): Select the stage of your business (Startup, Early stage, Growth stage, Established, Other).
* **Industry or Sector** (`industry`, text, required): Enter the industry or sector of your business.
* **Number of Employees** (`employee_count`, select_one, required): Select the number of employees in your business (Self-employed, 2-5, 6-10, 11-20, 21-50, 50 plus).
* **Annual Revenue Range** (`annual_revenue`, select_one, optional): Select the annual revenue range of your business (Less than 50K, 50K-100K, 100K-500K, 500K-1M, 1M-5M, 5M plus, Prefer not to say).
* **Do you currently need capital?** (`capital_needed`, select_one, required): Select whether you currently need capital (Yes, urgently, Yes, within 6 months, Maybe within a year, Planning future growth, Unsure).
* **Which capital access topics interest you?** (`topics_of_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select all capital access topics that interest you (Bank financing, Small business loans, SBA loans, Venture capital, Angel investment, Grants and competitions, Crowdfunding, Microfinance, Equipment financing, Line of credit, Alternative lending, Financial planning, Business planning, Credit building).
* **Current Knowledge of Financing Options** (`financing_knowledge`, select_one, required): Select your current level of knowledge of financing options (Beginner - No knowledge, Basic - Some awareness, Intermediate - Familiar with options, Advanced - Experienced).
* **Business Plan Status** (`business_plan_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your business plan (None, In progress, Preliminary, Complete, Professional version).
* **Preferred Training Format** (`preferred_format`, select_multiple, required): Select all preferred training formats (In-person workshop, Online webinar, Self-paced online course, One-on-one mentoring, Group coaching, Hybrid format, Virtual classroom).
* **Available Time Commitment** (`time_commitment`, select_one, required): Select the available time commitment for training (Half day workshop, Full day workshop, Multiple sessions (2-3 days), Weekly sessions, Self-paced, Flexible).
* **Language Preference** (`language_preference`, select_one, optional): Select the language preference for training (English, Spanish, Other language, Bilingual).
* **Additional Services of Interest** (`additional_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select all additional services of interest (Business plan assistance, Financial statement preparation, Application preparation, Credit coaching, Pitch preparation, Network/connections, Ongoing mentoring, No additional services).
* **Suggestions for Training Topics** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Enter your suggestions for additional training topics.
* **May we contact you regarding this training program?** (`follow_up`, select_one, required): Select whether we can contact you regarding this training program (Yes, contact me, No, thanks).

## Tips

* Please answer all questions in the form to ensure we understand your needs and interests.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
* Please select all options that apply to each question.
