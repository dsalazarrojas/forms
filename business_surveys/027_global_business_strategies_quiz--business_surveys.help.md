# Global Business Strategies Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Business Strategies Quiz is designed to assess your company's global business strategies. This form will guide you through a series of questions to identify your company's strengths and weaknesses in different areas.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select the correct answer for each question**. Please choose only one answer per question, even if there are multiple options provided.
2. **Answer every question**. All questions are optional, but your input will be more useful to us if you complete them all.
3. **Be honest and accurate** in your assessments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role in the company?** (role_in_company): A field to enter your role in the company. Please type your role, e.g., CEO, manager, or employee.
* **What is the current state of your industry?** (current_state_of_industry): A dropdown menu with two options: "Yes" or "No". Please select whether your industry is experiencing growth or decline.
* **Which statement about your company is true?** (company_strategy_question1): A dropdown menu with two options: "Good" or "Fair". This questions assesses your company's overall strategy.
* **Which of the following best describes your company's global business strategy?** (company_strategy_question2): A multiple-select menu with two options: "Good" or "Fair". This questions evaluates the company's global approach.
* **What is the current state of your business strategy?** (company_strategy_question3): A dropdown menu with two options: "Good" or "Fair". This question checks the company's strategy at this point.
* **Company Strategy Question4** (company_strategy_question4): A multiple-select menu with two options: "Good" or "Fair". This questions assesses multiple aspects of the company strategy.
* **Which statement is true about your company's strategy?** (company_strategy_question5): A multiple-select menu with three options: "This company is growing rapidly", "This company is struggling financially", or "This company has a high market share". This question evaluates multiple aspects of the company strategy.
* **What is your company's global business strategy?** (company_strategy_question6): A dropdown menu with two options: "Good" or "Fair". This questions assesses the global approach of your company.
* **Role In Company Text** (role_in_company_text): A text field to type in your role in the company.
