# scale_assessment_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scale assessment survey is designed to gather data on customer satisfaction, recommendation, quality rating, and other relevant aspects of a product or service. The gathered data will be used to improve the product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Choose the appropriate responses for each section, taking into account the options provided.
3. Fill out all required fields (indicated by *) and provide optional information for non-required fields.
4. Once completed, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`1`, `select_one`, optional): 
  Choose how satisfied are you with our product or service. Select from: 
  - Excellent 
  - Good 
  - Fair 
  - Poor
* **Recommendation** (`2`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
  Indicate if you would recommend this product or service to others. Select from: 
  - 'True' 
  - 'False'
* **Quality Rating** (`3`, `number`, optional): 
  Rate the quality of our product or service on a scale of 1-10.
* **Comments** (`4`, `text`, optional): 
  Provide any additional comments or feedback about our product or service.
* **Contact Email** (`5`, `email`, optional): 
  Enter your email address to contact you if we need more information.
* **Product Name** (`6`, `text`, optional): 
  Enter the name of the product or service you are evaluating.
* **Company Name** (`7`, `text`, optional): 
  Enter the name of the company providing the product or service.
