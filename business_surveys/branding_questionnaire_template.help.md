# Branding questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and insights on brand perception and strategy from individuals who have interacted with the brand. The collected data will help the brand understand how they are perceived by their audience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select your answers from the options provided for each question, and be sure to read the explanation below for each field to understand what they mean.
3. For text fields, provide your thoughts and comments freely.
4. If you have trouble understanding a question or field, feel free to ask for clarification or help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How would you describe your overall perception of our brand?** (`Perception`, single, Required)
	+ This question asks about your general feeling or impression of the brand.
* **Which of the following elements most influence your perception of our brand?** (`Elements`, multiple, Required)
	+ Select all the elements that you think have a significant impact on your perception of the brand. These might include product/service quality, customer service, marketing/advertising, company values, brand reputation, or price.
* **Would you recommend our brand to others?** (`Recommendation`, single, Required)
	+ This question asks about your willingness to suggest the brand to others.
* **Which words best describe our brand?** (`Description`, multiple, Required)
	+ Choose up to three words that you think accurately describe the brand. These might include words like innovative, trustworthy, modern, affordable, premium, friendly, reliable, or dynamic.
* **How unique do you perceive our brand to be compared to competitors?** (`Uniqueness`, single, Required)
	+ This question asks about how distinct or different the brand is compared to others in the same industry.
* **What aspects of our brand do you think could be improved or changed?** (`Suggestions`, text, Optional)
	+ Provide specific areas or features of the brand that you think could be improved or modified for better results.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback regarding our brand?** (`Feedback`, text, Optional)
	+ Share any thoughts, suggestions, or observations you have about the brand that are not captured in the other questions.
