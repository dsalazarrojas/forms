# Conference Schedule Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from conference attendees to help us improve our conference schedule. Your input will help us make informed decisions about future session planning and time slot selection.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your full name (if you'd like to share it). This is optional, but it helps us understand who's giving us feedback.
2. Select the session you most recently attended.
3. Rate the content of that session, using the scale: 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
4. Choose how convenient the time slot was for you.
5. Select your preferred time slots for future sessions.
6. Provide any feedback on the speaker's performance.
7. Choose how you'd rate the pace of the session.
8. Select how satisfied you are with the conference schedule overall.
9. If you have any suggestions for improvements for the next conference schedule, please let us know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Name (Optional)** (`name`, `text`, Optional): Please enter your full name (if you'd like to share it).
* **Most Recently Attended Session**: This is the session you most recently attended. You can choose one of the options provided.
* **Session Content Rating**: This question asks you to rate the content of the most recently attended session, using a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Session Time Slot Convenience**: Choose how convenient the time slot was for you. Options range from Very Convenient to Very Inconvenient.
* **Preferred Time Slots**: Select all the time slots that you think would be productive for future sessions.
* **Speaker Feedback** (Optional): If you'd like, you can provide any feedback on the speaker's performance.
* **Session Pace**: Rate how fast or slow you felt the session was. Options range from Too Fast to Too Slow.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Select how satisfied you are with the conference schedule overall. Options range from Extremely Satisfied to Extremely Dissatisfied.
* **Improvement Suggestions**: Share any suggestions you have for improving the next conference schedule.
* **Contact for Further Details**: We'd like to know if it's okay to contact you for further details on your feedback. Please choose yes or no.
