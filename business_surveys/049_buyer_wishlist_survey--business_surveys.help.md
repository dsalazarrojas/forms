# Buyer Wishlist Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your preferences and requirements for buying products or services. It will help us better understand your needs and provide you with more targeted recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and company or organization (if applicable) on pages 1 and 2.
2. Select your type of buyer (e.g., individual, business, government, etc.) on page 3.
3. Choose all the product or service categories that interest you on page 4.
4. Describe the specific products or services you are interested in on page 5.
5. Select the reasons why you are interested in something new on page 6.
6. List the features or functionalities you want on page 7.
7. Choose a budget range on page 8.
8. Decide on a purchase timeline on page 9.
9. Select how you typically make decisions on page 10.
10. Provide any additional comments or suggestions on page 11.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Your Name**
 (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Your full name.

### 2. **Email Address**
 (`respondent_email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.

### 3. **Company or Organization**
 (`respondent_company`, `text`, optional): If you work for a company or organization, enter its name here.

### 4. **Your Title or Position**
 (`respondent_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title or position.

### 5. **Type of Buyer**
 (`buyer_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following types:
	* Individual consumer
	* Business or company
	* Government or institutional
	* Non-profit
	* Reseller or distributor
	* Other

### 6. **Product or Service Categories of Interest**
 (`product_category_interest`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the categories that interest you:
	* Technology and electronics
	* Software or applications
	* Office equipment
	* Business services
	* Marketing and advertising
	* Consulting services
	* Industrial equipment
	* Other category

### 7. **Specific Products or Services You Want**
 (`specific_product_interest`, `text`, required): Describe the specific products or services you are interested in.

### 8. **Current Solution or Product Used**
 (`current_solution`, `text`, optional): If you are currently using a product or service, describe it here.

### 9. **Why Are You Interested in Something New?**
 (`dissatisfaction_reasons`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the reasons why you are interested in something new:
	* Cost is too high
	* Quality issues
	* Lack of features
	* Poor customer service
	* Need upgrade
	* Searching for options
	* Other reason

### 10. **Features or Functionality You Want**
 (`desired_features`, `text`, required): List the features or functionalities you want.

### 11. **Budget Range**
 (`budget_range`, `select_one`, required): Choose a budget range:
	* Under $500
	* $500 - $2500
	* $2500 - $10000
	* $10000 - $50000
	* $50000 - $100000
	* Over $100000
	* Flexible
	* Not determined

### 12. **When Do You Plan to Purchase?**
 (`purchase_timeline`, `select_one`, required): Choose a purchase timeline:
	* Immediately
	* Within 1 month
	* Within 3 months
	* Within 6 months
	* Within 12 months
	* Not soon
	* Unsure

### 13. **Decision-Making Process**
 (`decision_process`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you typically make decisions:
	* Individual decision
	* Team or committee decision
	* Need approval from management
	* RFP or competitive bidding
	* Other process

### 14. **Current or Preferred Vendors**
 (`key_vendors`, `text`, optional): If you have a current or preferred vendor, enter its name here.

### 15. **Competitor Products You Like or Dislike**
 (`competitor_products`, `text`, optional): If you have a competitor product you like or dislike, describe it here.

### 16. **Quality or Premium Features Importance**
 (`quality_importance`, `select_one`, required): Choose the importance of quality or premium features:
	* Very important
	* Important
	* Somewhat important
	* Not very important
	* Not important

### 17. **Importance of Customer Support and Service**
 (`support_service`, `select_one`, required): Choose the importance of customer support and service:
	* Very important
	* Important
	* Somewhat important
	* Not very important
	* Not important

### 18. **Environmental or Sustainability Concerns**
 (`environmental_concerns`, `select_one`, required): Choose the importance of environmental or sustainability concerns:
	* Very important
	* Important
	* Somewhat important
	* Not very important
	* Not a concern

### 19. **Additional Comments or Suggestions**
 (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions here.

### 20. **May We Follow Up Regarding These Products?**
 (`follow_up_contact`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you want us to follow up on these products:
	* Yes, please contact me
	* No, do not contact
	* Only if relevant product
