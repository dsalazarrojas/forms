# Facility Equipment Lifecycle Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Equipment Lifecycle Survey is a tool used to track and analyze the life cycle of facility equipment, including maintenance history, asset condition, replacement needs, and budgeting requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and provide detailed answers to the questions.
2. Ensure that you answer all required fields.
3. If a field is not applicable, enter "N/A" or a brief explanation.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **facility_equipment_lifecycle_survey_form_1** (`Facility Equipment Lifecycle Survey Form 1`, text, required): This field is the first page of the survey, which includes general information about the equipment.
* **maintenance_history_1** (`Maintenance History 1`, text, required): Describe the maintenance history of the equipment, including any repairs, replacements, or upgrades.
* **maintenance_history_2** (`Maintenance History 2`, text, required): Continue describing the maintenance history of the equipment.
* **asset_condition_1** (`Asset Condition 1`, text, required): Report the current condition of the asset, including any issues or concerns.
* **asset_condition_2** (`Asset Condition 2`, text, required): Describe any additional information about the asset's condition.
* **replacement_needs_1** (`Replacement Needs 1`, text, required): Identify any replacement needs or requirements for the equipment.
* **replacement_needs_2** (`Replacement Needs 2`, text, required): Continue identifying replacement needs or requirements.
* **replacement_plans_1** (`Replacement Plans 1`, text, required): Describe any replacement plans or strategies for the equipment.
* **replacement_plans_2** (`Replacement Plans 2`, text, required): Continue describing replacement plans or strategies.
* **budgeting_needs_1** (`Budgeting Needs 1`, text, required): Specify any budgeting requirements or constraints for the equipment replacement.
* **budgeting_needs_2** (`Budgeting Needs 2`, text, required): Continue specifying budgeting requirements or constraints.
* **asset_tags_1** (`Asset Tags 1`, text, required): List any relevant asset tags or classifications for the equipment.
* **asset_tags_2** (`Asset Tags 2`, text, required): Continue listing asset tags or classifications.
* **facility_equipment_lifecycle_survey_form_2** (`Facility Equipment Lifecycle Survey Form 2`, text, required): This field is the second page of the survey, which includes additional information about the equipment.
* ... and so on.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when completing each field.
- If you're unsure about a particular field, refer to the form's creator or a relevant facility management expert.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are complete.
