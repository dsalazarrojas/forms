# rooftop_farming_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather insights from rooftop farming practitioners on their experiences, methods, and perspectives on the future of rooftop farming. Your input will help us better understand the challenges and opportunities in this emerging field and inform decision-making for urban planning and research.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Provide detailed answers to all required fields.
3. Use the options provided for select fields to help you make your selection.

* **What is your experience with rooftop farming? (grower_experience)**: This question helps us understand your background and expertise in rooftop farming.
	+ Please provide a brief description of your experience with rooftop farming.
