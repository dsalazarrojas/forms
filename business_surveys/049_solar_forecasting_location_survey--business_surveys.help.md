# Solar Forecasting Location Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a specific location's suitability for solar forecasting. The form is designed to gather data on various aspects of the location, including its climate, geography, and infrastructure.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the "Survey location" field with the name or identifier of the location being surveyed.
2. In the "Solar resource availability" field, select the level of solar resource availability at the location, using the options provided.
3. For each of the "Site details", "Coordinate 1 (Latitude)", "Coordinate 2 (Longitude)", and "Coordinate 3 (Elevation)" fields, provide the relevant information about the location.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments about the site in the "Site notes" field.
5. If applicable, upload photos of the site for each of the "Site Photo 1", "Site Photo 2", and "Site Photo 3" fields.
6. Attach visual maps or plans of the site to the "Site map" and "Site plan" fields, if applicable.
7. For the "Zoning status", "Zoning notes", and "Zoning contact" fields, provide information about local zoning regulations and relevant contacts.
8. Enter the local wind direction and speed for the "Solar wind direction" and "Solar wind speed" fields.
9. Assess the local cloud cover and its impact on solar energy generation in the "Cloud cover" field.
10. Enter the site's solar insolation, irradiance, and capacity in the "Solar insolation", "Solar irradiance", and "Solar capacity" fields.
11. Provide any additional notes or comments about the site's capacity in the "Solar capacity notes" field.
12. Enter any other capacity information in the "Other capacity notes" field.
13. Enter any other capacity information in the "Other capacity" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey location** (`survey_location`, text, optional): Enter the name or identifier of the location being surveyed.
* **Solar resource availability** (`solar_resource_availability`, select_multiple, optional): Select the level of solar resource availability at the location, using the options provided.
* **Site details** (`site_details`, text, optional): Provide additional information about the site, such as its terrain, vegetation, and other relevant details.
* **Coordinate 1 (Latitude)** (`coordinate_1_north`, number, optional): Enter the precise location of the site's latitude.
* **Coordinate 2 (Longitude)** (`coordinate_2_east`, number, optional): Enter the precise location of the site's longitude.
* **Coordinate 3 (Elevation)** (`coordinate_3_elevation`, number, optional): Enter the precise location of the site's elevation.
* **Site notes** (`site_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the site.
* **Site Photo 1** (`site_photo1`, text, optional): Upload a photo of the site.
* **Site Photo 2** (`site_photo2`, text, optional): Upload a photo of the site.
* **Site Photo 3** (`site_photo3`, text, optional): Upload a photo of the site.
* **Site Map** (`site_map`, text, optional): Attach a visual map of the site.
* **Site Plan** (`site_plan`, text, optional): Attach a visual plan of the site.
* **Zoning status** (`zoning_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of local zoning regulations.
* **Zoning notes** (`zoning_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about local zoning regulations.
* **Zoning contact** (`zoning_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for local zoning authorities.
* **Solar wind direction** (`solar_wind_direction`, text, optional): Enter the local wind direction.
* **Solar wind speed** (`solar_wind_speed`, text, optional): Enter the local wind speed.
* **Cloud cover** (`cloud_cover`, text, optional): Assess the local cloud cover and its impact on solar energy generation.
* **Solar insolation** (`solar_insolation`, text, optional): Enter the site's solar insolation.
* **Solar irradiance** (`solar_irradiance`, text, optional): Enter the site's solar irradiance.
* **Solar capacity** (`solar_capacity`, text, optional): Enter the site's solar capacity.
* **Solar capacity notes** (`solar_capacity_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the site's capacity.
* **Other capacity notes** (`other_capacity_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about other capacity information.
