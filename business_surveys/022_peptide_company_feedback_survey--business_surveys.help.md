# Peptide Company Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with Peptide Company products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter the Date of your feedback in the "Date" field.
5. Specify the Time of your feedback in the "Time" field.
6. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Note" field.
7. Select the type of Peptide product you use from the "What type of product do you use?" dropdown menu.
8. Choose the features that you find most useful from the "Which of the following do you find most useful?" dropdown menu.
9. Rate your experience by selecting a number from 1 to 10 in the "How would you rate your experience?" field.
10. Repeat the same rating process for the additional "How would you rate your experience?" fields.
11. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Note" fields.
12. Enter the Date for the additional "Date" fields.
13. Provide the Time for the additional "Time" fields.
