# Microfinance Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Microfinance Awareness Survey is designed to collect information about microfinance awareness from different stakeholders in the microfinance sector. This survey aims to gather insights about the services provided by microfinance institutions, the interest levels, knowledge levels, and opinions on other services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter a name for the survey in the "Survey Name" field.
2.  Select the category of the survey (Business Surveys or Other).
3.  Provide a description of the survey in the "Description" field.
4.  Enter the name of the organization associated with the survey.
5.  Select the contact person(s) responsible for the survey from the "Contact Person" field.
6.  Indicate the level of interest in microfinance services (Interest Level) and knowledge about microfinance (Knowledge Level).
7.  Select the services in which you are interested in (Services Interested).
8.  Select the services you are not interested in (Services Not Interested).
9.  If you have any other microfinance services, please describe them in the "Other Services" field.
10.  Rate your satisfaction with other services on a scale of 1 to 5.
11.  Enter the contact details of the contact person (Organization, Contact Person, Email, Phone).
12.  Provide the date and time of the survey.
13.  Add any comments or observations.
14.  Schedule a follow-up if necessary (Follow Up).
15.  Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Survey Name** (1, text, required): Enter a name for the survey.
*   **Category** (2, select_one, required): Select the category of the survey (Business Surveys or Other).
*   **Description** (3, text, required): Provide a description of the survey.
*   **Organization** (4, text, required): Enter the name of the organization associated with the survey.
*   **Contact Person** (5, select_multiple, required): Select the contact person(s) responsible for the survey.
*   **Interest Level** (6, number, required): Indicate the level of interest in microfinance services (1-5).
*   **Knowledge Level** (7, number, required): Indicate your knowledge level about microfinance (1-5).
*   **Services Interested** (8, select_multiple, required): Select the services you are interested in (Loan, Credit Card, Savings Account, Money Transfer, Other).
*   **Services Not Interested** (9, select_multiple, required): Select the services you are not interested in (Loan, Credit Card, Savings Account, Money Transfer, Other).
*   **Other Services** (10, text, optional): Describe any other microfinance services you have used.
*   **Other Services Frequency** (13, time, optional): Indicate how often you use other services.
*   **Other Services Importance** (14, number, optional): Rate the importance of other services (1-5).
*   **Other Services Confidence** (15, number, optional): Rate your confidence level in other services (1-5).
*   **Other Services Satisfaction** (16, select_one, optional): Rate your satisfaction with other services (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neither Satisfied nor Dissatisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
*   **Contact Person Title** (17, select_multiple, optional): Select the title of the contact person (Microfinance Manager, Microfinance Officer, Researcher).
*   **Contact Person Organization** (18, text, required): Enter the name of the organization associated with the contact person.
*   **Contact Person Email** (19, email, optional): Enter the email of the contact person.
*   **Contact Person Phone** (20, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
*   **Date** (21, date, required): Enter the date of the survey.
*   **Time** (22, time, required): Enter the time of the survey.
*   **Comments** (23, text, optional): Add any comments or observations.
*   **Follow Up** (24, select_multiple, optional): Schedule a follow-up in 1-5 weeks.
*   **Submit** (25, select_one, required): Confirm that you have completed the survey.
