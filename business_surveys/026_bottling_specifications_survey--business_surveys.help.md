# Bottling Specifications Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect and document the specifications of bottles for production and packaging purposes. It is intended for internal use within a business organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Start from the top and work down to each section.
3. Make sure to review and submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client and Project Info** (`client_project_header`): This section is used to gather information about the client and project details.
  - *Company Name* (`company_name`): Please enter the name of the company associated with this project.
  - *Project Title / Product Name* (`project_title`): Enter the title or name of the product associated with this project.
  - *Contact Email* (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the contact person associated with this project.
* -- Bottle Specifications --
* *Bottle Material* (`bottle_material_select`): Select the material that the bottle is made of (e.g. Glass, PET Plastic, HDPE Plastic, Aluminum, Stainless Steel).
* *Bottle Shape / Type* (`bottle_type_select`): Select the shape or type of bottle (e.g. Round, Square, Oval, Boston Round, Wine Bottle).
* *Fill Volume - ml* (`fill_volume_ml`): Enter the volume of the bottle in milliliters.
* -- Closure and Cap --
* *Closure / Cap Type* (`cap_type_select`): Select the type of cap or closure used on the bottle (e.g. Screw Cap, Flip Top, Pump, Spray, Cork, Crown Cap).
* *Preferred Cap Color* (`cap_color_pref`): Enter the preferred color of the cap or closure, if applicable.
* -- Labeling and Packaging --
* *Label Application Method* (`label_meth`): Select the method of label application used on the bottle (e.g. Pressure Sensitive, Shrink Sleeve, Glue Applied, Direct Print).
* *Secondary Packaging Needs* (`packaging_needs`): Select all secondary packaging needs for the project (e.g. Cardboard Case, Shrink Wrap, Display Box, Palletized).
* *Additional Technical Specifications* (`technical_specs`): Enter any additional technical specifications or notes for the bottle.
* *Target Production Date* (`target_dt`): Enter the target production date for the project.

## Tips

- Make sure to review and validate your answers carefully before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the project owner or team before proceeding.
- This form is for internal use only and should not be shared with external parties without proper authorization.
