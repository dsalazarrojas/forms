# mode_selection_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mode selection survey is designed to collect feedback on modes of operation or selection within an organization. This form aims to gather information on the frequency, satisfaction, and suggested improvements for each mode, allowing businesses to better understand their operational modes and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all relevant modes of operation or selection from the options provided in the "Mode Selection" field.
2. Choose the frequency of usage for each selected mode from the "Mode Usage Frequency" field.
3. Rate your satisfaction with each mode in the "Mode Satisfaction" field.
4. Provide any comments or suggestions for the "Mode Comments" field.
5. Select a category for each mode from the options provided in the "Category Selection" field.
6. Rate your satisfaction with each category in the "Category Satisfaction" field.
7. Provide any comments or suggestions for the "Category Comments" field.
