# Trade Opportunities And Challenges Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Trade Opportunities And Challenges Survey is designed to gather information about trade opportunities and challenges faced by businesses across different borders. This survey aims to identify areas where businesses face difficulties in conducting international trade and to provide insights into the support needs of businesses for cross-border trade.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin by reading through each section carefully.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Select the relevant options for each question.
4. Fill in any text fields with relevant information.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
