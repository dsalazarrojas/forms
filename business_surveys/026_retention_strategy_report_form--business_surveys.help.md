# retention_strategy_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about customer retention strategies and related metrics. It is designed to help analyze and improve customer retention, satisfaction, and overall business performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question truthfully.
2. Select the customer segment that best describes your current customer base.
3. Choose the customer retention strategy that you believe is most effective.
4. Indicate the customer satisfaction level and retention period.
5. Provide reasons for customer churn (if applicable).
6. Select the preferred customer interaction method (phone, email, or in-person).
7. Add any additional comments or notes about customer interactions.
8. Enter the name and date of the person submitting the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Segment** (`Customer Segment`, select_one, **required**): Select the customer segment that best describes your current customer base. This could be 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Customer Segment Options** (`Customer Segment Options`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all options that apply to your customer segment.
* **Customer Retention Strategy** (`Customer Retention Strategy`, text, **required**): Describe the customer retention strategy you believe is most effective.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`Customer Satisfaction`, number, **required**): Enter a number between 1 and 10 to indicate the level of customer satisfaction.
* **Retention Period** (`Retention Period`, select_one, **required**): Select the retention period that best describes the time customers stay with your business. This could be 'Less than 1 month', '1-3 months', '3-6 months', or 'More than 6 months'.
* **Reasons for Churn** (`Reasons for Churn`, select_one, **required**): Select 'Yes' if customer churn is a significant issue, 'No' otherwise.
* **Reasons For Churn Options** (`Reasons For Churn Options`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all options that apply for reasons for customer churn.
* **Retention Strategy** (`Retention Strategy`, select_one, **required**): Select 'Good' or 'Fair' for the overall retention strategy.
* **Retention Strategy Options** (`Retention Strategy Options`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all options for retention strategy.
* **Customer Interaction** (`Customer Interaction`, select_one, **required**): Choose the preferred customer interaction method: 'Phone', 'Email', or 'In-person'.
* **Customer Interaction Details** (`Customer Interaction Details`, text, **optional**): Provide additional information about customer interactions.
* **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, text, **optional**): Add any additional comments or notes about customer interactions.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the person submitting the report.
* **Submitted Date** (`Submitted Date`, date, **required**): Enter the date the report was submitted.
