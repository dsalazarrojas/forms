# veterinary_waste_management_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The veterinary_waste_management_survey form is designed to gather information about veterinary waste management practices and needs. This form will help us better understand the current state of veterinary waste management and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your general information (page 1).
2. Select the types of waste you manage (page 2).
3. Choose the waste disposal practices you use (page 3).
4. Describe any collection challenges you face (page 4).
5. Describe any disposal challenges you face (page 5).
6. Indicate your training needs and preferences (page 6).
7. Select the topics you would like to receive training on (page 7).
8. Add any additional comments or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This section is used to gather your general information, such as your contact details.
	+ **Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Waste Types**: Select all the types of waste you manage, such as Animal waste, Human waste, or Other waste.
* **Waste Disposal Practices**: Select all the practices you use for waste disposal, such as Landfill, Recycling, Incinerator, or Other practices.
* **Collection Challenges**: Describe any challenges you face while collecting waste.
* **Disposal Challenges**: Describe any challenges you face while disposing of waste.
* **Training Needs**: Indicate whether you need training or are unsure about training needs.
* **Training Topics**: Select the topics you would like to receive training on, such as Waste handling or Waste management.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or questions you have about veterinary waste management.
