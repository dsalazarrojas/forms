# Veterinary Cybersecurity Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help veterinary practices assess their cybersecurity readiness. The survey aims to gather information about employee awareness, security concerns, and security measures in place. This will help the practice evaluate their current cybersecurity posture and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: This is a note type field that serves as an introduction to the survey.
2. **Practice Name**: Enter the name of your veterinary practice.
3. **Number of Employees**: Enter the total number of employees in your practice.
4. **Is Your Employee Surveilled?**: Answer "True" if your employees are currently surveilled, "False" if not, or "Maybe" if unsure.
5. **Security Concerns**: Select all the security concerns that apply to your practice, such as Email, Internet, Cloud, Network, or Other.
6. **Email Address**: Enter the email address of your practice.
7. **Phone Number**: Enter your practice's phone number (optional).
8. **Security Measures**: Select all the security measures that are currently in place, such as Firewalls, Antivirus, Encryption, Intrusion Detection, or Backup.
9. **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about your practice's cybersecurity.
10. **Practice Survey Completed**: Leave this field blank as it is not intended to be filled in by the user.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Practice Name** (`practice_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your veterinary practice.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_employees`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of employees in your practice.
* **Is Your Employee Surveilled?** (`employee_survey`, `select_one`, required): Answer "True" if your employees are currently surveilled, "False" if not, or "Maybe" if unsure.
* **Security Concerns** (`security_concerns`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the security concerns that apply to your practice, such as Email, Internet, Cloud, Network, or Other.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of your practice.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your practice's phone number (if applicable).
* **Security Measures** (`security_measures`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the security measures that are currently in place, such as Firewalls, Antivirus, Encryption, Intrusion Detection, or Backup.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your practice's cybersecurity.
* **Practice Survey Completed** (`practice_survey_completed`, `note`, optional): Leave this field blank as it is not intended to be filled in by the user.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and honest answers to ensure an accurate assessment of your practice's cybersecurity readiness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* This survey is intended to be completed by the person responsible for your practice's cybersecurity.
