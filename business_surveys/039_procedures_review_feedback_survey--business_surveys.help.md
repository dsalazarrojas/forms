# Procedures Review Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Procedures Review Feedback Survey is a form used to collect feedback on procedures within a business process. It helps identify areas where procedures need improvement or are effective.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the procedure being reviewed.
2. Provide feedback on whether staff is involved (Yes/No).
3. Indicate whether stakeholders are involved (Yes/No).
4. Rate the procedure (1-5).
5. Provide comments on the procedure (optional).
6. Enter the date the procedure was received.
7. Enter the date the procedure is due.
8. Enter the time the procedure was received.
9. Enter the time the procedure is due.
10. Enter the email associated with the procedure.
11. Enter the phone number associated with the procedure.
12. Indicate the category of the procedure (Yes/No).
13. Indicate the subcategory of the procedure (Yes/No).
14. Enter the priority of the procedure (1-5).
15. Select the status of the procedure (Active/Inactive).
16. Indicate whether the procedure was assigned to someone (Yes/No).
17. Indicate whether the procedure was assigned by someone (Yes/No).
18. Enter a description of the procedure (optional).
19. Indicate the type of feedback (Yes/No).
20. Indicate whether the procedure was submitted by someone (Yes/No).
21. Indicate the type of procedure (Yes/No).
22. Enter any additional notes on the procedure.
23. Enter the date and time the procedure was submitted.
24. Enter the submit date of the procedure.
25. Enter the submit time of the procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Procedures Name**: Enter the name of the procedure being reviewed.
* **Staff**: Indicate whether staff is involved in the procedure (Yes/No).
* **Stakeholders**: Indicate whether stakeholders are involved in the procedure (Yes/No).
* **Rating**: Rate the procedure on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments**: Provide comments on the procedure.
* **Date Received**: Enter the date the procedure was received.
* **Date Due**: Enter the date the procedure is due.
* **Time Received**: Enter the time the procedure was received.
* **Time Due**: Enter the time the procedure is due.
* **Email**: Enter the email associated with the procedure.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number associated with the procedure.
* **Category**: Indicate whether the procedure is a category (Yes/No).
* **Subcategory**: Indicate whether the procedure is a subcategory (Yes/No).
* **Priority**: Enter the priority of the procedure on a scale of 1-5.
* **Status**: Select the status of the procedure (Active/Inactive).
* **Assigned to**: Indicate whether the procedure was assigned to someone (Yes/No).
* **Assigned by**: Indicate whether the procedure was assigned by someone (Yes/No).
* **Procedure Description**: Enter a description of the procedure (optional).
* **Feedback Type**: Indicate the type of feedback (Yes/No).
* **Submitted by**: Indicate whether the procedure was submitted by someone (Yes/No).
* **Procedure Type**: Indicate the type of procedure (Yes/No).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes on the procedure.
* **Submitted at**: Enter the date and time the procedure was submitted.
* **Submit Date**: Enter the submit date of the procedure.
* **Submit Time**: Enter the submit time of the procedure.
