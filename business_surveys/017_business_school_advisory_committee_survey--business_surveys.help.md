# Business School Advisory Committee Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business School Advisory Committee Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from advisory committee members on various aspects of the business school, including curriculum relevance, graduate skill development, and faculty qualifications. Your input will help the school administration understand the effectiveness of their programs and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please make sure you have authority and expertise to complete this form.
2.  Complete the form thoroughly and accurately, providing honest and constructive feedback.
3.  Answer each question carefully, considering the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Full Name**: (`respondent_name`, `text`, required)
    Your name should be provided as per the hint, which is "Name of advisory committee member".
*   **Your Title or Position**: (`respondent_title`, `text`, required)
    Please provide your title or position as per the hint "Your role or position".
*   **Organization Name**: (`organization`, `text`, not required)
    This is where you can mention your affiliated organization.
*   **Years on Advisory Committee**: (`committee_tenure`, `number`, not required)
    Provide the number of years you've served on the committee.
*   **Survey Completion Date**: (`survey_date`, `date`, required)
    Please provide the date you're completing this survey.
*   **Curriculum Relevance to Industry**: (`curriculum_relevance`, `select_one`, required)
    Answer how relevant the curriculum is to the industry using the provided options:
    *   Highly Relevant
    *   Relevant

    *   Somewhat Outdated
    *   Outdated
