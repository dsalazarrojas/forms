# Project Execution Efficiency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Project Execution Efficiency Survey" form is designed to evaluate the efficiency of projects in various aspects. It is used to gather data on how projects are executed, including their efficiency, category, project status, type, size, duration, cost, team size, scope, quality, risk, satisfaction, completion rate, execution speed, and other relevant metrics. This data will help organizations understand where they can improve their project execution processes to increase efficiency and productivity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the "Project Execution Efficiency" option from the first question.
2. In the "Category" question, select all categories related to your project (e.g., Project Initiation, Project Planning, Project Execution, etc.).
3. For "Project Status", select the current status of your project.
4. Choose the type of your project (Internal, External, Joint Venture, or Project Portfolio).
5. Fill in the "Project Size", "Project Duration", "Project Cost", "Project Team Size", "Project Scope", "Project Quality", "Project Risk", "Project Satisfaction", "Project Completion Rate", "Project Execution Speed", and "Survey Date" fields with the relevant information related to your project.
6. For "Project Team Size", please enter the number of team members involved in the project.
7. Fill in the "Survey Time" field with the time of day the survey was completed.
8. In the "Survey Notes" field, add any additional comments or information about your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Execution Efficiency** (`project_execution_efficiency_survey`, select_one): Select the level of efficiency of your project from "Efficiency Very Poor", "Efficiency Fair", "Efficiency Good", or "Efficiency Excellent".
* **Category** (`project_execution_efficiency_category`, select_multiple): Select all categories related to your project, such as Project Initiation, Project Planning, Project Execution, etc.
* **Project Status** (`project_execution_efficiency_project_status`, select_one): Choose the current status of your project from "Not Started", "In Progress", "Completed", "On Hold", or "Canceled".
* **Project Type** (`project_execution_efficiency_project_type`, select_one): Select the type of your project from "Internal Project", "External Project", "Joint Venture", or "Project Portfolio".
* **Project Size** (`project_execution_efficiency_project_size`, number): Enter the size of your project.
* **Project Duration** (`project_execution_efficiency_project_duration`, number): Enter the duration of your project.
* **Project Cost** (`project_execution_efficiency_project_cost`, number): Enter the cost of your project.
* **Project Team Size** (`project_execution_efficiency_project_team_size`, number): Enter the number of team members involved in the project.
* **Project Scope** (`project_execution_efficiency_project_scope`, number): Enter the scope of your project.
* **Project Quality** (`project_execution_efficiency_project_quality`, number): Enter the quality of your project.
* **Project Risk** (`project_execution_efficiency_project_risk`, number): Enter the level of risk associated with your project.
* **Project Satisfaction** (`project_execution_efficiency_project_satisfaction`, number): Enter the level of satisfaction with your project.
* **Project Completion Rate** (`project_execution_efficiency_project_completion_rate`, number): Enter the completion rate of your project.
* **Project Execution Speed** (`project_execution_efficiency_project_execution_speed`, number): Enter the speed of project execution.
* **Survey Date** (`project_execution_efficiency_survey_date`, date): Enter the date the survey was completed.
* **Survey Time** (`project_execution_efficiency_survey_time`, time): Enter the time of day the survey was completed.
* **Survey Notes** (`project_execution_efficiency_survey_notes`, note): Add any additional comments or information about your project.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled in, while the fields not marked as "required" are optional and can be skipped if not applicable.
