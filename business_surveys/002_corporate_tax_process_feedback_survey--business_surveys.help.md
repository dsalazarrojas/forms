# corporate_tax_process_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from users on the corporate tax process. Your input will help us improve the efficiency and effectiveness of the tax process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the appropriate answers and provide as much detail as possible for each field.
2. Fill out all the fields, especially those marked as required.
3. If you have any suggestions or comments, please make sure to provide concrete and actionable ideas.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Corporate Tax Process Survey** (`corporate_tax_process_survey`, text, optional): A text field where you can provide a brief overview of your experience with the tax process.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted regarding your feedback, please provide your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): A field to provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): A text field where you can provide detailed feedback on the tax process.
* **Tax Year** (`tax_year`, number, optional): The year you are providing feedback on.
* **Tax Return Type** (`tax_return_type`, select_one, optional): Select whether you're providing feedback on 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Tax Return Date** (`tax_return_date`, date, optional): The date you are providing feedback on.
* **Tax Return Deadline** (`tax_return_deadline`, date, optional): The deadline for the tax return.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, time, optional): The date and time you reviewed the tax process.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): The date the tax return was submitted.
* **Tax Process Rating** (`tax_process_rating`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the tax process as 'Good' or 'Fair'.
* **Tax Process Comment** (`tax_process_comment`, note, optional): A field for you to provide any comments on the tax process.
* **Tax Process Suggestions** (`tax_process_suggestions`, text, optional): A field to provide suggestions on how to improve the tax process.
* **Tax Process Agreement** (`tax_process_agreement`, select_one, optional): Select whether you agree with the tax process 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Tax Process Suggestions 1-11** (`tax_process_suggestions_1`-`tax_process_suggestions_11`, text, optional): Multiple fields for you to provide suggestions on how to improve the tax process.

Note: Please make sure to fill out all the fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your feedback is actionable and comprehensive. Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time.
