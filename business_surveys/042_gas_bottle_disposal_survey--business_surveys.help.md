This form aims to collect data on the gas bottle disposal methods used by individuals or households in a given area, as well as their awareness and perception of these methods. The purpose is to understand the effectiveness and accessibility of different gas bottle disposal methods, which can help identify areas for improvement and inform policy decisions. 

# Gas Bottle Disposal Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the fields and steps required to complete the Gas Bottle Disposal Survey.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select all the methods you use for disposing of gas bottles from the options provided on page 1.
2. Choose the location where you collect gas bottles from the options on page 2.
3. Select the method you use to collect gas bottles from the options on page 3.
4. Indicate your awareness of the methods used for gas bottle disposal on page 4.
5. Answer if you believe local improvements are needed on page 5.
6. Provide any additional comments about gas bottle disposal on page 6.
7. (Optional) Suggest local improvements on page 7.
8. Answer if your waste operator is aware of local disposal methods on page 8.
9. Provide comments about your waste operator's awareness on page 9.
10. Suggest solutions for improving gas bottle disposal on page 10.
11. (Optional) Provide more information on how your waste operator can improve on page 11.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gas Bottle Disposal Methods** (Page 1):
	+ Select all the ways you dispose of gas bottles.
	+ Options are: Gas bottles, Empty recyclables, Other (please specify in the comments).

* **Gas Bottle Collection** (Page 2):
	+ Please choose where you collect gas bottles from.
	+ Options are: Home, Street, Recycling, Other (please specify in the comments).

* **Method of Gas Bottle Collection** (Page 3):
	+ Choose how you collect gas bottles.
	+ Options are: Curbside, Drop-off, Depot, Other (please specify in the comments).

* **Awareness of Disposal Methods** (Page 4):
	+ Indicate if you are aware of the different methods used for gas bottle disposal.
	+ Options are: True, False.

* **Local Improvement Need** (Page 5):
	+ Answer if you believe local improvements are needed for gas bottle disposal.
	+ Options are: True, False.

* **Comments** (Page 6):
	+ Write any additional comments about gas bottle disposal.

* **Local Improvement Ideas** (Page 7):
	+ Provide any ideas for improving local gas bottle disposal (if you believe improvements are needed).

* **Awareness of Waste Operator** (Page 8):
	+ Indicate if your waste operator (e.g. trash collector) is aware of local disposal methods.
	+ Options are: True, False.

* **Comments for Waste Operator Awareness** (Page 9):
	+ Write any comments about your waste operator's awareness of local disposal methods.

* **Improvement Solutions** (Page 10):
	+ Suggest solutions for improving gas bottle disposal.
	+ Options are: Recycling, Waste Education, Other (please specify in the comments).

* **Improvement Ideas for Waste Operator** (Page 11):
	+ (Optional) Provide more ideas for your waste operator to improve gas bottle disposal.

Note: If you believe improvements are needed, we encourage you to provide specific and practical suggestions. If you are unsure or need help, do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
