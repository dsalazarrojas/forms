# Establishment Configuration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential configuration details about an establishment, allowing our system to accurately manage and configure its settings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, providing information for all relevant fields.
2. Double-check that you are only completing this form once for your establishment.
3. If you need help or have questions, contact your administrator for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Establishment Configuration Survey** (Note): This is just a title to remind you that you are filling out the establishment configuration form. You can ignore this field.
* **Establishment Type** (Select): Choose the type of establishment from the options provided.
	+ This field helps us understand the type of establishment you are configuring, which affects how the system will manage its settings.
* **Location Details** (Text): Enter the address, city, state, or any other relevant location details for your establishment.
	+ This field is used to accurately pinpoint the location of your establishment on our system map.
* **Operating Hours** (Time): Enter your establishment's operating hours (e.g., 9am-5pm) or select a pre-defined schedule.
	+ This field helps us understand your establishment's availability and schedule.
* **Service Requirements** (Text): If you have any specific service requirements for your establishment (e.g., food service, wheelchair accessibility), list them here.
	+ This field is used to ensure our system can accommodate your establishment's unique needs.
* **Contact Details** (Text): Enter your establishment's contact information, such as phone number, email, or website.
	+ This field helps us connect with your establishment and provide support when needed.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your establishment's email address.
	+ This field is used for internal communication and to keep you informed about system updates.
* **Address** (Text): Enter your establishment's address.
	+ This field helps us accurately locate your establishment on our system map.
* **Notes** (Text): If you have any additional comments or remarks about your establishment, add them here.
	+ This field is used to capture any special requests or concerns you may have about your establishment.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to avoid errors.
- If you need help with any part of this form, contact your administrator for assistance.
- Ensure you have not already completed this form for your establishment to avoid duplication.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to us and we'll be happy to help.
