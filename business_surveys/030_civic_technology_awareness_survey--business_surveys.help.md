# Civic Technology Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from respondents about their familiarity with civic technology, their use of civic tech tools, and their interest in partnerships for civic tech development. The data collected will inform future civic tech initiatives and identify areas where support is needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please select the type of organization you represent from the "Organization Type" dropdown menu.
2.  Indicate your level of familiarity with civic technology tools by selecting a number from 1 (not familiar) to 5 (very familiar).
3.  Select any civic technology tools you currently use.
4.  If applicable, describe any barriers you face when adopting civic technology.
5.  Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are interested in partnerships for civic tech development.
6.  Identify your top priority technology needs.
7.  If you have a budget for technology, please estimate your annual approximate budget.
8.  Indicate your level of interest in capacity building for civic tech.
9.  Provide your contact information for follow-up.
10.  Enter the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Type** (Government, NGO, Private Sector, Academic, Other): This field allows you to select the type of organization you represent.
* **Familiarity with Civic Tech** (1 not familiar to 5 very familiar): This question assesses your level of familiarity with civic technology.
* **Civic Tech Tools Currently Used** (Data Dashboards, Open Mapping, SMS Platforms, Online Engagement Portals): This field allows you to select any civic technology tools you currently use.
* **Barriers to Adopting Civic Tech** (Text field): This field is for describing any barriers you face when adopting civic technology.
* **Interest in Partnerships** (Yes, No): This field allows you to indicate your interest in partnerships for civic tech development.
* **Priority Technology Needs** (Data Collection, Citizen Engagement, Service Delivery, Transparency Tools): This field allows you to select your top priority technology needs.
* **Budget Estimate** (Annual approximate budget): This field is for estimating your annual approximate budget for technology.
* **Interest in Capacity Building** (High, Medium, Low): This field allows you to indicate your level of interest in capacity building for civic tech.
* **Contact for Follow-up** (Text field): This field is for providing your contact information for follow-up.
* **Submission Date** (Date): This field is for entering the current date.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* If you have any other questions or concerns, please contact us.
