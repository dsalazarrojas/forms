# Facility Technology Adoption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the adoption of technology within facilities. Your input will help us understand the types of technologies being used, their adoption rates, and any challenges or benefits experienced by facility management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the form with accurate and complete information. If you are unsure about any field, please provide your best effort or indicate that it's not applicable.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form. If you need assistance, please reach out to our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **facility_technology_adoption_survey**:
	+ This is the title of the survey.
* **facility_name**:
	+ Provide the name of the facility.
* **facility_type**:
	+ Select 'Yes' if the facility uses technology, 'No' otherwise.
* **technology_adoption**:
	+ If your facility uses technology, select the number of years you have been using it.
* **technology_type**:
	+ Select 'Yes' if the technology used by your facility is the type listed in the options.
* **technology_rating**:
	+ Rate your satisfaction with the technology used, using a scale from 0-100.
* **comments**:
	+ Provide any comments or feedback about the technology used or your experience with it.
* **email**:
	+ Enter your email address (if you have one).
* **phone**:
	+ Enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **submission_date**:
	+ Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
* **submission_time**:
	+ Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **submitter_name**:
	+ Enter your name.
* **facility_id**:
	+ Enter a unique identifier for your facility (if applicable).

Please note that all fields are optional unless otherwise specified. If you need further assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
