# Air Cargo Route Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from air cargo companies about their current routes, challenges, and needs to improve air cargo services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your demographic information: Respondent Name, Company Name, Job Title, Email Address.
2. Select your industry sector and monthly air cargo volume.
3. Choose your primary shipping routes.
4. Rate your current route satisfaction and answer questions about transit time importance, budget range, and long-term partnership interest.
5. Select all challenges you are currently facing.
6. Answer questions about new route demand, desired new routes, flight frequency needs, capacity requirements, special handling requirements, carrier preference, technology needs, and additional feedback.
7. Finally, fill in the survey date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, **required**): Enter your company's name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, **required**): Enter your job title or position.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter your contact email address.
* **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, select_one, **required**): Select the industry your company operates in.
	+ Freight forwarding
	+ Logistics
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Retail
	+ E-commerce
	+ Healthcare
	+ Automotive
	+ Perishables
	+ Other
* **Monthly Air Cargo Volume** (`cargo_volume`, select_one, **required**): Select your approximate monthly air cargo volume.
	+ Under 1 ton
	+ 1-10 tons
	+ 10-50 tons
	+ 50-100 tons
	+ Over 100 tons
* **Primary Shipping Routes** (`primary_routes`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all your primary shipping routes.
	+ North America domestic
	+ Transatlantic
	+ Transpacific
	+ Asia-Europe
	+ Middle East
	+ Africa
	+ South America
	+ Intra-Asia
* **Current Route Satisfaction** (`route_satisfaction`, select_one, **required**): Rate your current route satisfaction.
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Transit Time Importance** (`transit_time_importance`, select_one, **required**): Rate the importance of fast transit time.
	+ Critical
	+ Very important
	+ Important
	+ Somewhat important
	+ Not important
* **Cost Importance** (`cost_importance`, select_one, **required**): Rate the importance of cost.
	+ Critical
	+ Very important
	+ Important
	+ Somewhat important
	+ Not important
* **Reliability Importance** (`reliability_importance`, select_one, **required**): Rate the importance of reliability.
	+ Critical
	+ Very important
	+ Important
	+ Somewhat important
	+ Not important
* **Current Challenges** (`current_challenges`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all challenges you are currently facing.
	+ Limited route options
	+ High costs
	+ Long transit times
	+ Capacity constraints
	+ Customs delays
	+ Poor tracking
	+ Damage or loss
	+ Communication issues
* **New Route Demand** (`new_route_demand`, select_one, **required**): Answer whether you are interested in new routes.
	+ Yes, multiple
	+ Yes, one or two
	+ No, current routes sufficient
* **Desired New Routes** (`desired_new_routes`, text, **required**): Enter any desired new routes.
* **Flight Frequency Needs** (`frequency_needs`, select_one, **required**): Select how often you need flights.
	+ Daily
	+ Multiple times per week
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ On-demand
* **Capacity Requirements** (`capacity_requirements`, select_one, **required**): Select your capacity requirements.
	+ Small parcels
	+ Palletized cargo
	+ Full containers
	+ Oversized cargo
	+ Mixed
* **Special Handling Requirements** (`special_handling`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all special handling requirements.
	+ Temperature controlled
	+ Hazardous materials
	+ Live animals
	+ Pharmaceuticals
	+ High value goods
	+ Perishables
	+ None
* **Carrier Preference** (`carrier_preference`, text, **required**): Enter your carrier preference.
* **Technology Needs** (`technology_needs`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all technology needs.
	+ Real-time tracking
	+ Automated notifications
	+ Digital documentation
	+ API integration
	+ Analytics dashboard
	+ Mobile app
* **Monthly Air Cargo Budget** (`budget_range`, select_one, **required**): Select your approximate monthly air cargo budget.
	+ Under $10,000
	+ $10,000-$50,000
	+ $50,000-$100,000
	+ $100,000-$500,000
	+ Over $500,000
* **Long-term Partnership Interest** (`partnership_interest`, select_one, **required**): Rate your long-term partnership interest.
	+ Very interested
	+ Somewhat interested
	+ Neutral
	+ Not interested
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, **required**): Enter any additional feedback.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of completion.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when filling in the form.
* If you have any additional feedback, use the "Additional Feedback" field.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
