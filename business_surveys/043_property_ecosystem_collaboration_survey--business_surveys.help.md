# Property Ecosystem Collaboration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps property owners and organizations share their information, enabling more effective collaboration and communication within the property ecosystem.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose your property from the list of options on Page 1: **Property Organisations**.
2. Provide contact details for your organization, selecting multiple options for **Contact Person** and entering a valid email and phone number in **Contact Email** and **Contact Phone**.
3. Enter the specifics of your property, including **Property Type**, **Property Address**, **Property Postal Code**, **Property Road Name**, **Property Road Number**, **Property Road Postal Code**, **Property Floor Number**, **Property Floor Area**, **Property Entrance Gate**, **Property Unit**, and **Property Facilities**.
4. Optionally, select additional information about your property's **Land Use** and add any **Property Notes**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: **Property Organisations**
- **Property Organisations**: Select your property from the available options.
 
### Page 2: **Contact Person**
 
- **Contact Person**: Choose multiple people who will act as contact points for your organization.
 
### Page 3: **Contact Email**
 
- **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address for your organization.
 
### Page 4: **Contact Phone**
 
- **Contact Phone**: Enter a valid phone number for your organization.
 
### Page 5: **Property Type**
 
- **Property Type**: Select the type of property you are representing (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
 
### Page 6: **Property Address**
 
- **Property Address**: Enter the address of your property.
 
### Page 7: **Property Postal Code**
 
- **Property Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of your property.
 
### Page 8: **Property Road Name**
 
- **Property Road Name**: Enter the name of the road where your property is located.
 
### Page 9: **Property Road Number**
 
- **Property Road Number**: Enter the number of the road where your property is located.
 
### Page 10: **Property Road Postal Code**
 
- **Property Road Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of the road where your property is located.
 
### Page 11: **Property Floor Number**
 
- **Property Floor Number**: Enter the floor number of your property.
 
### Page 12: **Property Floor Area**
 
- **Property Floor Area**: Enter the floor area of your property.
 
### Page 13: **Property Entrance Gate**
 
- **Property Entrance Gate**: Choose multiple options for the entrance gate of your property.
 
### Page 14: **Property Unit**
 
- **Property Unit**: Enter the unit number of your property.
 
### Page 15: **Property Facilities**
 
- **Property Facilities**: Enter any facilities available at your property.
 
### Page 16: **Property Land Use**
 
- **Property Land Use**: Select the land use of your property (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
 
### Page 17: **Property Land Use Hint**
 
- **Property Land Use Hint**: Enter any additional information about your property's land use.
 
### Page 18: **Property Notes**
 
- **Property Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your property.
