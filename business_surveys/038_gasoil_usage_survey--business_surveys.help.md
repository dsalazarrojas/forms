# Gasoil Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data about gasoil usage from employees in the company. This data will be used to better understand and optimize gasoil usage within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions in the "To Complete This Form" section above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (text, required/optional)**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Name (text, required/optional)**: Enter your name as a contact person.
* **Email (email, required/optional)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (text, required/optional)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Start Date (date, required/optional)**: Enter the start date of your gasoil usage.
* **End Date (date, required/optional)**: Enter the end date of your gasoil usage.
* **Gasoils Consumption (number, required/optional)**: Enter your gasoil consumption.
* **Fuel Type (select_one, required/optional)**: Select the type of fuel you use.
* **Vehicle Type (select_multiple, required/optional)**: Select the type of vehicle you use.
* **Location (select_one, required/optional)**: Select the location where you use the gasoil.
* **Shift (select_one, required/optional)**: Select your shift.
* **Equipment (select_multiple, required/optional)**: Indicate whether you use any equipment.
* **Usage Frequency (select_multiple, required/optional)**: Select how often you use the gasoil.
* **Usage Time (time, required/optional)**: Enter the time of day you use the gasoil.
* **Consumption/Day (number, required/optional)**: Enter your daily gasoil consumption.
* **Consumption/Week (number, required/optional)**: Enter your weekly gasoil consumption.
* **Review (note, required/optional)**: Add a review or comments about your gasoil usage.
* **Submit (text, required/optional)**: Click the submit button to send the form.
