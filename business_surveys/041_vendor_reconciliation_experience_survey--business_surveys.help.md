# Vendor Reconciliation Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Reconciliation Experience Survey is designed to gather feedback from vendors on their experience with the vendor reconciliation process. The goal is to understand how well the team is communicating with vendors, identify areas for improvement, and make adjustments to enhance the vendor relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the answers that best describe your experience with vendor reconciliation processes.
2. Provide specific ideas or feedback on how to improve the process.
3. Fill out the remaining fields with your contact information so that we can follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your experience with vendor reconciliation processes?** (`vendor_reconciliation_experience`): This question asks about your experience with vendor reconciliation processes. Select 'Yes' if you have had a good experience, or 'No' if you have not.
* **Who is your point of contact for procurements and vendor communications?** (`procurement_team`): This question asks who you typically communicate with when it comes to procurement and vendor matters. Select 'Yes' if you have a point of contact, or 'No' if you do not.
* **How would you rate the quality of communication from our team?** (`communication_quality`): This question asks how well our team has communicated with you. Select 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Poor' to rate your experience.
* **What are some ideas for improvement for our reconciliation process?** (`improvement_ideas`): This is an open-ended question where you can provide specific ideas on how to improve the process. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **When did you last have a reconciliation meeting with our team?** (`date`): This question asks about your last reconciliation meeting with us. If you have not had a meeting, select 'Not Applicable'.
* **What time of day did you last have a reconciliation meeting with our team?** (`time`): This question asks about the time of day of your last reconciliation meeting with us. If you have not had a meeting, select 'Not Applicable'.
* **What email address do you typically use to communicate with us?** (`email`): This question asks for your primary email address that you use to contact us.
* **What is your preferred phone number to contact you at?** (`phone_number`): This question asks for your preferred phone number that you would like us to use when contacting you.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback for us?** (`comments`): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any additional comments or feedback you have for us.
* **How often do you have vendor reconciliation meetings?** (`vendor_reconciliation`): This question asks about how often you have vendor reconciliation meetings with us. Select 'Yes' if you have meetings, or 'No' if you do not.
