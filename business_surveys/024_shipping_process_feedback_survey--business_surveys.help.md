# shipping_process_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The shipping_process_feedback_survey form is designed to gather feedback from customers regarding their experiences with our shipping process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
2.  Select the date of the shipment from the calendar in the "Shipping Date" field.
3.  Choose the time of the shipment from the "Shipping Time" field.
4.  Provide feedback about your experience with the shipment in the "Feedback" field.
5.  If you have any improvement suggestions, enter them in the "Improvement Suggestions" field.
6.  Select your rating for the shipment from the options in the "Rating" field.
7.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field (if available).
8.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (if available).
9.  Enter any comments about the shipment in the "Comments" field.
10.  Select the category of the shipment from the options in the "Category" field (required).
11.  Enter the form ID if you have it in the "Form ID" field (if available).
12.  If you'd like to save the output to a file, enter a filename in the "Output File" field (if available).
13.  Enter a description of the shipment in the "Description" field (if available).
14.  Enter the title of the form in the "Form Title" field (if available).
15.  Enter the category of the form in the "Form Category" field (if available).
16.  Enter the assigned tool for the form in the "Form Assigned Tool" field (if available).
17.  Enter the form's form ID in the "Form Form ID" field (if available).
18.  Enter the output filename for the form in the "Form Output File" fields (if available).
19.  Enter the name of the category in the "Category Name" field (if available).
20.  Enter the title of the form again in the "Form Title 2" field (if available).
21.  Finally, enter the output filename for the form again in the "Output Filename 2" field (if available).
22.  You can also optionally select Jimmy as the assigned person in the "Assigned Jimmy" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (text)**: This is where you enter your name.
* **Shipping Date (date)**: Select the date of the shipment.
* **Shipping Time (time)**: Choose the time of the shipment.
* **Feedback (text)**: Share your feedback about your experience with the shipment.
* **Improvement Suggestions (text)**: If you have any suggestions for how we can improve our shipping process, enter them here.
* **Rating (select multiple)**: Select your rating for the shipment from the options available.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address if you have one.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Comments (text)**: Share any additional comments about the shipment.
* **Category (select one)**: Select the category of the shipment from the options.
* **Form ID (text)**: Enter the form ID if you have it.
* **Output File (text)**: If you'd like to save the output to a file, enter a filename here.
* **Description (text)**: Enter a description of the shipment.
* **Form Title (text)**: Enter the title of the form.
* **Form Category (text)**: Enter the category of the form.
* **Form Assigned Tool (text)**: Enter the assigned tool for the form.
* **Form Form ID (text)**: Enter the form's form ID.
* **Form Output File (text)**: Enter the output filename for the form.
* **Category Name (text)**: Enter the name of the category.
* **Form Title 2 (text)**: Enter the title of the form again.
* **Output Filename (text)**: Enter the output filename for the form.
* **Output Filename 2 (text)**: Enter the output filename again.
* **Assigned Jimmy (text)**: You can optionally select Jimmy as the assigned person.
