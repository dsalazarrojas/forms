# Mission Statement Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from employees on the organization's mission statement. The form asks questions to gauge how the mission statement aligns with the employees' understanding, expectations, and perceptions. It aims to gather insights to refine and adjust the organization's mission to better align with its strategic goals.

## How To Complete This Form

To provide feedback, simply fill in the text boxes with your thoughts on the mission statement and goals of the organization. You can type as much or as little as you like in each box. If a field is not required, feel free to leave it blank. Once you've completed all sections, click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Mission Statement** (`mission_statement`, `text`, optional): Describe your understanding of the organization's mission statement.
- **Feedback On Mission** (`feedback_on_mission`, `text`, optional): Share your thoughts on how well the mission statement aligns with your expectations and understanding.
- **Feedback On Goals** (`feedback_on_goals`, `text`, optional): Provide feedback on how the mission statement relates to the organization's goals and objectives.
- **Feedback On Values** (`feedback_on_values`, `text`, optional): Share your thoughts on how the organization's values align with its mission and goals.
- **Feedback On Strategies** (`feedback_on_strategies`, `text`, optional): Offer suggestions on how the organization can improve its strategies to achieve its mission and goals.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the mission statement and goals.

## Tips

* Be honest and specific with your feedback.
* Keep your answers brief and concise.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas, feel free to share them.
* Remember, this form is a safe space for you to share your thoughts and opinions.
