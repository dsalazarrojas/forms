# Property Staff Responsiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to measure the responsiveness and quality of service provided by property staff. It will help us understand how well we are serving our properties and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Fill in all required fields, denoted by a red asterisk (*).
3. If you have additional comments or feedback, use the "Additional comments" field.
4. Submit your responses when you have completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Property Name (1) 
* **Property Name** (`property_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the property you are rating.

### How responsive is the property staff? (2)
* **How responsive is the property staff** (`staff_responsiveness`, number, required): Rate the responsiveness of the property staff on a scale from 0 to 100.

### How often is staff responsive via email? (3)
* **How often is staff responsive via email** (`communication`, number, required): Rate how often the property staff is responsive via email, with higher numbers indicating more frequent response times.

### Response Time (4) 
* **Response Time** (`response_time`, time, required): Enter the time it took for property staff to respond to your inquiries.

### What communication method have you used to contact property staff? (5) 
* **What communication method** (`communication_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose all methods you have used to contact property staff.

### How helpful is the property staff? (6)
* **How helpful is the property staff** (`staff_helpfulness`, number, required): Rate the helpfulness of the property staff on a scale from 0 to 100.

### Overall Service Quality (7) 
* **Overall Service Quality** (`overall_service_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the overall service quality of the property staff.

### Additional Comments (8) 
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have.

### Email (9) 
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the property staff.

### Phone (10) 
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the property staff.

### Time (11) 
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you spent waiting for a response from property staff.

### Date (12) 
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you made contact with property staff.

### Staff Rating (13) 
* **Staff Rating** (`staff_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of service provided by property staff.

### Response Time (14) 
* **Response Time** (`response_time`, time, optional): Enter the time it took for property staff to respond to your inquiry.

### Follow-up Action Taken (15) 
* **Follow-up Action Taken** (`follow_up_action`, select_one, required): Choose the action you took after waiting for a response from property staff.

### Follow-up Method (16) 
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, select_one, required): Choose the method you used for follow-up.

### Follow-up Result (17) 
* **Follow-up Result** (`follow_up_result`, select_one, required): Rate the outcome of your follow-up action.

### Follow-up Comments (18) 
* **Follow-up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments about your follow-up action.

### Property Manager (19) 
* **Property Manager** (`property_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of the property manager.

### Property Owner (20) 
* **Property Owner** (`property_owner`, text, optional): Enter the name of the property owner.

### Property Contact (21) 
* **Property Contact** (`property_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name of property contact.

### Property Address (22) 
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): Enter the property address.

### Property City (23) 
* **Property City** (`property_city`, text, optional): Enter the city where the property is located.

### Property State (24) 
* **Property State** (`property_state`, text, optional): Enter the state where the property is located.
