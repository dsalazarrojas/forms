# real_estate_innovation_awareness_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the level of awareness and innovation adoption within the real estate industry. It aims to gather insights on the current state of proptech firms, agency participants, and industry perceptions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.**
2. **Select all that apply** for multiple-choice questions.
3. **Provide clear and concise answers** for text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Background** (`survey_background`, text, optional):
	+ This field is a brief description of the survey's purpose and objectives.
* **Proptech Firms** (`proptech_firms`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose all types of proptech firms you are familiar with:
		+ Real estate tech
		+ Property tech
		+ Other
* **Agency Participants** (`agency_participants`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose the types of agency participants you are familiar with:
		+ Proptech agency
		+ Non-proptech agency
		+ Other
* **Adoption Level** (`adoption_level`, select_one, required):
	+ What is your current level of adoption of proptech innovations:
		+ High
		+ Medium
		+ Low
* **Innovation Adoption** (`innovation_adoption`, select_one, required):
	+ How do you perceive the level of innovation adoption in your industry:
		+ Fully
		+ Partially
		+ Not at all
* **Perceptions & Opinions** (`perceptions_and_opinions`, text, required):
	+ Share your perceptions and opinions on the current state of the real estate industry.
* **Industry Groups** (`industry_groups`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Which industry groups do you belong to or have an affiliation with:
		+ Real estate tech
		+ Property tech
		+ Other
* **Proptech Ideas** (`proptech_ideas`, text, optional):
	+ Share any proptech ideas you have or have heard about.
* **Proptech Challenges** (`proptech_challenges`, text, optional):
	+ Share any proptech-related challenges you face or have heard about.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
* Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
* Provide clear and concise answers for text fields.
* If you're unsure about a question, try to answer based on your general knowledge and experience.
* If you have any further questions or need clarification, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
