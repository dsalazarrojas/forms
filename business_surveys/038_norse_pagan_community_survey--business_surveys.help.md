# Norse Pagan Community Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from members of the Norse Pagan community about their interests, needs, and preferences. Your input will help us better understand your community and inform the development of new events, resources, and outreach efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions below.
2. Be honest and accurate when providing your information.
3. You can submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

* **Beliefs** (`beliefs`, text, optional): Describe your core beliefs or values that are important to you.
* **Practices** (`practices`, text, optional): Share your current practices or rituals that you find meaningful.
* **Demographics** (`demographics`, text, optional): Provide some basic demographic information about yourself.

* **Group Size** (`group_size`, number, optional): If you are part of a group, please estimate the size of your group.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, number, optional): Enter your age group or range.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted or receive updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted or receive updates.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address if you'd like to be contacted or receive updates.
