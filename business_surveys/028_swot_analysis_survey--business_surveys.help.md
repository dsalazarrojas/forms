# Swot Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help identify the Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats (SWOT) of an individual and the company. A SWOT analysis is a framework used to identify and prioritize these elements to help make informed business decisions. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and concisely.
2. Start with the "Introduction" page, which explains the purpose of the SWOT analysis.
3. In the "Personal Strengths" and "Personal Weaknesses" sections, describe your personal attributes and characteristics that you think are relevant to your professional role.
4. In the "Company Strengths" and "Company Weaknesses" sections, describe the company's attributes and characteristics that you think are relevant.
5. In the "Opportunities" and "Threats" sections, describe the potential future events, trends, or situations that may affect the company.
6. In the "Summary of SWOT analysis" section, summarize your analysis and identify the key points.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required/optional): This is a brief overview of the purpose and scope of the SWOT analysis.
- **What are your personal strengths?** (`Personal Strengths`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your personal attributes and characteristics that are relevant to your professional role.
- **What are your personal weaknesses?** (`Personal Weaknesses`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your personal attributes and characteristics that you think are areas of improvement.
- **What opportunities does our company have to grow in the future?** (`Opportunities`, `text`, required/optional): Describe potential future events, trends, or situations that may benefit the company.
- **What threats does our company face in the future?** (`Threats`, `text`, required/optional): Describe potential future events, trends, or situations that may harm the company.
- **What are the company's strengths?** (`Company Strengths`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the company's attributes and characteristics that are relevant to its success.
- **What are the company's weaknesses?** (`Company Weaknesses`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the company's attributes and characteristics that are areas of improvement.
- **What opportunities does the company have to grow in the future?** (`Company Opportunities`, `text`, required/optional): Describe potential future events, trends, or situations that may benefit the company.
- **What threats does the company face in the future?** (`Company Threats`, `text`, required/optional): Describe potential future events, trends, or situations that may harm the company.
- **Summary of SWOT analysis** (`Summary of SWOT analysis`, `text`, required/optional): Summarize your analysis and identify the key points.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing your personal and company strengths and weaknesses.
* Think about the company's mission, vision, and values when answering the questions.
* Consider the internal and external factors that may affect the company.
* Be honest and objective in your analysis.
* Use bullet points or numbered lists to organize your answers if it helps.
* Proofread your answers for accuracy and clarity.
* Keep your answers concise and focused on the essential points.
