# Business Process Improvement Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Process Improvement Program Registration Form is designed to gather information from interested individuals who want to participate in a business process improvement program. The form is used to collect demographic and program participation information to help program administrators better understand the needs and preferences of potential participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Fill in your personal details in the **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** fields.
2. Enter your **Organization Name** and **Job Title**.
3. Select your area of focus for process improvement from the **Area of Process Improvement Focus** dropdown menu.
4. Describe the main challenges you are facing in the **Current Process Challenges** field.
5. Specify what you hope to achieve through the program in the **Improvement Goals** field.
6. Select your preferred level of involvement in the program, including the format for your participation.
7. If applicable, enter the **Team Size Involved** and **Budget Allocated for Improvement** fields.
8. Choose your desired **Timeline for Improvements**.
9. Optionally, specify any **Expected Outcomes** you expect from the program.
10. Finally, enter the **Registration Date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Your complete name as it appears on official records.
- **Email Address** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Your email address for program communication and updates.
- **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required): Your contact phone number for program communication.
- **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): Name of your company or organization.
- **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Your department or division within the organization.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Your position in the organization.
- **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, optional): Your experience in your current field.
- **Area of Process Improvement Focus** (`improvement_area`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary area you want to improve.
- **Current Process Challenges** (`current_challenges`, `text`, required): Describe the main challenges you face in your current process.
- **Improvement Goals** (`improvement_goals`, `text`, required): What you hope to achieve through the program.
- **Team Size Involved** (`team_size`, `number`, optional): Number of people in your improvement team.
- **Participation Level Preference** (`program_participation_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred involvement level in the program.
- **Preferred Program Format** (`program_format`, `select_multiple`, required): How would you like to participate in the program.
- **Budget Allocated for Improvement** (`budget_allocation`, `number`, optional): Total budget available for this project.
- **Desired Timeline for Improvements** (`timeline`, `select_one`, required): When do you want to implement changes.
- **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, `text`, optional): What specific results do you expect.
- **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): Date of program registration.
