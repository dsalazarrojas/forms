# Asset Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Asset Survey form is a tool for collecting data about an asset's condition, maintenance needs, and other relevant details. It's essential to accurately and comprehensively fill out this form to help manage and maintain your assets effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the survey form for the correct asset**: Ensure you're completing the form for the asset being surveyed.
2. **Enter accurate and detailed information**: Provide precise answers to each question to ensure data accuracy.
3. **Use the hints for guidance**: Pay attention to the hints provided with each question to help you understand what information is required.
4. **Save and review your answers**: Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Surveyor Name (1)**: Enter your name and title or position.
* **Survey Date (2)**: Enter the date of this survey.
* **Survey Location (3)**: Enter the physical location of the survey.
* **Facility or Site Name (4)**: Enter the name of the facility or site being surveyed.
* **Department or Area (5)**: Enter the department or area responsible for the asset.
* **Asset ID Number (6)**: Enter the unique identifier of the asset.
* **Asset Name (7)**: Enter the name of the asset.
* **Asset Description (8)**: Enter a detailed description of the asset.
* **Specific Location of Asset (9)**: Enter the exact location of the asset within the facility or site.
* **Is Asset Visible and Accessible? (10)**: Answer if the asset is visible and accessible or not.
* **Overall Condition (11)**: Rate the asset's condition as excellent, good, fair, poor, or damaged.
* **Functionality Status (12)**: Rate the asset's functionality as fully functional, partially functional, non-functional, or unknown.
* **Maintenance or Repair Needed? (13)**: Answer if the asset needs maintenance or repair.
* **Details of Maintenance Needed (14)**: If the asset needs maintenance, describe the required work.
* **Cleanliness Level (15)**: Rate the asset's cleanliness as clean, moderate dust/dirt, significant dirt, or very dirty.
* **Any Safety Concerns? (16)**: Answer if there are any safety concerns.
* **Safety Concerns Details (17)**: If there are safety concerns, describe them.
* **Missing or Damaged Components? (18)**: List any missing or broken parts.
* **Last Inspection Date (19)**: Enter the date of the last inspection.
* **Next Inspection Due Date (20)**: Enter the scheduled date for the next inspection.
* **Person Responsible for Asset (21)**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the asset.
* **Documentation Present (22)**: Answer if documentation is available for the asset.
* **Additional Observations (23)**: Enter any other relevant notes or observations.
* **Completion Time (24)**: Enter the time it took to complete the survey.

Note that fields 14, 17, 19, and 20 are optional and only required if necessary.
