# Employee Motivation Research Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Motivation Research Application Form is designed to help employees submit their research applications for employee motivation. This form is used to gather information from employees about their job motivation levels, factors that drive their motivation, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your job title and department.
3. Select the factors that motivate you from the "Motivation Factors" dropdown list.
4. Answer the feedback questions in the "Feedback Questions" field.
5. Enter the date and time of submission.
6. Enter your email address in the "Submission Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Submission Phone" field.
8. Provide any additional comments in the "Submission Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as an employee.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department or team name.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your job function or responsibilities.
* **Motivation Level** (`motivation_level`, text, required): Enter your level of motivation on a scale (e.g., high, medium, low).
* **Motivation Factors** (`motivation_factors`, select multiple, optional): Select the factors that motivate you (e.g., Recognition, Rewards, Feedback).
* **Feedback Questions** (`feedback_questions`, text, optional): Answer any additional feedback questions.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are submitting this form.
* **Submission Email** (`submission_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Submission Phone** (`submission_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments.

## Tips
* Please enter accurate and honest information about your motivation level and factors.
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly.
* Enter a valid phone number.
* Provide any additional comments if necessary.
