# Sustainability Crew Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainability Crew Availability Survey is a form designed to help the sustainability team understand your availability and interests for volunteer opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill out the form with your first and last name.
2. Enter your email address so that we can contact you about volunteer opportunities.
3. Indicate if you are interested in volunteering.
4. Select the days you are available to volunteer.
5. Choose your available times on those days.
6. Add any additional notes about your availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you about volunteer opportunities.
* **What are your interests?** (select multiple, optional): Check the boxes for what type of activities you are interested in.
* **What days are you available?** (time, optional): Select the days you are free to volunteer.
* **What time on those days?** (time, optional): Enter the time you are available to volunteer on those days.
* **Availability Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes about your availability. 

## Tips
Please fill out the form only if you are interested in volunteering. If you have already submitted the form, you can update your information by resubmitting the form.
