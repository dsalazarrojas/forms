# Risk Culture Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the risk culture of your organization by gathering information about your risk awareness, reporting practices, and ideas for improvement. The survey consists of 10 questions that will help us understand your current risk culture.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
- Select the most accurate option for each question.
- If you have any comments or additional information, please use the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This section is a brief overview of the survey purpose and instructions.
* **Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter your official email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your official phone number.
* **Risk Awareness**: This question assesses your level of awareness about risks in your work area. Answer on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 means "not aware at all" and 10 means "very aware".
* **Reporting**: Please select the method you use to report risks or incidents:
	+ Option 1: Report to my manager only.
	+ Option 2: Report to both manager and risk management team.
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify in comments).
* **Improvement Ideas**: Please select all ideas that apply for improving risk culture:
	+ Option 4: More training on risk awareness.
	+ Option 5: More resources for risk reporting.
	+ Option 6: Other (please specify in comments).
* **Date**: Please enter the date you completed this survey.
* **Time**: This field is currently empty, please do not fill it out.
* **Comments**: Please use this field to provide any additional information or comments about risk culture in your organization.
