# Corporate Funding Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Corporate Funding Feedback Survey is designed to gather insights from users about corporate funding, helping the organization evaluate and improve the funding process.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by selecting the type of funding you received or are interested in, as this will help us understand the context of your feedback.
- Provide your overall satisfaction level with the corporate funding process, using the numbers provided.
- Choose any areas where you experienced impact, such as increased revenue or job creation.
- Share any improvement ideas you have to enhance the funding process.
- Enter your contact information to help us reach out to you for further clarification or discussion, if needed.
- Finally, provide any additional feedback you may have about your experience with corporate funding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Funding Type** (`funder_type`, Select One, **Optional**): Choose the type of funding you received or are interested in. This could be Direct Grant, Indirect Grant, Loan, or Other.
- **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, Number, **Optional**): Rate your overall satisfaction level with the corporate funding process, using the scale provided.
- **Impact** (`impact`, Select Multiple, **Optional**): Check any areas where you experienced impact, such as Increased Revenue, Job Creation, or Increased Efficiency.
- **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, Note, **Optional**): Share any ideas or suggestions you have to enhance the corporate funding process.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, Email, **Optional**): Enter your contact information to help us reach out to you for further discussion or clarification.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, Text, **Optional**): Provide any additional feedback or comments you may have about your experience with corporate funding.
