# Role Specific Training Needs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Role Specific Training Needs Survey is a form designed to collect information from end-users about their training needs for specific roles within the organization. This form helps identify areas where training programs can be improved to better support employee growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the Introduction page to understand the purpose and context of the survey.
2. Enter your Role Name in the `roleName` field. This is a required field, so please make sure to fill it in accurately.
3. Select the training needs that are relevant to your role from the list in the `trainingNeeds` field.
4. Identify the skills that are currently assessed and not covered in your current role from the list in the `skillsAssessed` field.
5. Provide additional comments or feedback in the `additionalComments` field, if needed.
6. Select one of the options in the `submit` field to submit or save your responses. If you want to submit the form, select "Submit". If you want to save a draft, select "Save Draft". If you want to cancel your responses, select "Cancel".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Role Name** (`role_name`, text, required): Enter your current role within the organization.
* **Training Needs** (`trainingNeeds`, select_multiple, required): Select the training needs that are relevant to your role. Choose from the list of options provided.
* **Skills Assessed** (`skillsAssessed`, select_multiple, required): Identify the skills that are currently assessed in your role. Select from the list of options provided.
* **Skills Not Covered** (`skillsNotCovered`, text, optional): Provide additional comments or feedback about skills not covered in your current role.
* **Additional Comments** (`additionalComments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about your training needs and experiences.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Select one of the options to submit or save your responses.
