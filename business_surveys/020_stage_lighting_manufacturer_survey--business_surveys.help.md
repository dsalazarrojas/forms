# Stage Lighting Manufacturer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Stage Lighting Manufacturer Survey" is designed to collect information about your company, contact person, contact email, and contact phone. This will help us better understand your business and provide more effective support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form by entering your company name in the "company" field.
2. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for your company.
3. Enter the email address of the contact person.
4. Enter the phone number of the contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company (company, text, optional):** Enter your company name as it appears on official documents or your website.
* **Contact Person (contact_person, text, optional):** Enter the name of the person responsible for your company, ideally the one who can be reached for further questions.
* **Contact Email (contact_email, email, optional):** Enter the email address of the contact person, using the standard email format (e.g., name@domain.com).
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone, text, optional):** Enter the phone number of the contact person, using the standard phone format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
