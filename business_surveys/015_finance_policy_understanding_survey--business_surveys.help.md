# Finance Policy Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand how well our employees comprehend our company's finance policies. Your responses will help us identify areas of improvement and inform our training and education programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. Select the most accurate response from the options provided for each question.
3. If a question doesn't apply to you, select "Not Applicable" or leave the field blank.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field if you'd like to receive a copy of your responses.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Employee Survey
* **Employee Survey** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): This question asks how well you, as an employee, understand our company's finance policies. Please select all that apply to indicate your level of understanding. You can choose 'Yes' if you feel you have a good understanding, 'No' if you don't, or leave it blank if you're unsure.
### 2. policy_clarity
* **policy_clarity** (`select_one`, required/optional): This question assesses how clear our finance policies are to you. Please select 'Yes' if you find our policies clear and well-defined, 'No' if you don't, or leave it blank if you're unsure.
### 3. Finance Guidelines
* **Finance Guidelines** (text, required/optional): This question gives you the opportunity to share any thoughts or observations about our finance guidelines. Enter your comments or suggestions, if any. This can be as simple as "I need more information about [specific policy]" or "I think we're doing a great job with [policy]!"
### 4. Employee Training
* **Employee Training** (`select_one`, required/optional): This question helps us understand if you've received sufficient training on our finance policies. Please select 'Yes' if you feel you have the necessary knowledge, 'No' if you don't, or leave it blank if you're unsure.
### 5. Feedback
* **Feedback** (text, required/optional): This is an open note field for you to share any additional thoughts or concerns you may have about our finance policies. Enter your comments, suggestions, or ideas here.
### 6. Email
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive a copy of your responses. This way, you can access your survey results at a later time.
### 7. Phone
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions about your survey responses.
### 8. Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (date, required/optional): This field is not currently in use, please leave it blank.
