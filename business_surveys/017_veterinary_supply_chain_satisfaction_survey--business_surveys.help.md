# Veterinary Supply Chain Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Supply Chain Satisfaction Survey is a tool used to collect feedback from veterinarians and other medical professionals in the veterinary supply chain. This form helps identify areas where the supply chain can be improved to provide better service, delivery, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For each question, select the option that best matches your experience with the supplier.
3. Make sure to provide specific examples or details to support your answers where possible.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback that are not covered in this form, please use the "Additional Comments" section at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Suppliers**: Please select all suppliers that you have interacted with in the last quarter/year/period. Selecting "Yes" indicates that you have worked with them.
* **Supplier Name**: Enter the name of the supplier you have selected.
* **Supplier Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with the supplier from 1 (Good) to 3 (Fair).
* **Availability and Delivery**: Rate your satisfaction with the supplier's availability and delivery from 1 (Option 1) to 3 (Option 3). 
* **Quality and Service**: Rate your satisfaction with the supplier's quality and service from 1 (Option 1) to 3 (Option 3).
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them in this section.
* **Feedback**: If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please provide them in this section.
