# Acrylic Signage Solutions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback and information about our acrylic signage solutions. Your input will help us improve our products and services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your company name if you're comfortable sharing. If not, you can leave this field blank.
2. Specify your role within your company (e.g., Facilities manager, designer, or buyer).
3. Select the primary use cases for our signage solutions (select all that apply).
	* Wayfinding
	* Branding
	* Safety signage
	* Promotional displays
4. Rate your satisfaction with the durability of our solutions:
	* Very satisfied
	* Satisfied
	* Neutral
	* Dissatisfied
	* Very dissatisfied
5. Describe your experience with our installation process:
	* Easy
	* Moderate
	* Difficult
6. Choose your preferred finish options (select all that apply):
	* Gloss
	* Matte
	* Frosted
	* Textured
7. Estimate the typical volume of signs you order from us.
8. Indicate your priority level for sustainable materials:
	* High priority
	* Medium priority
	* Low priority
9. Share any ideas you have to improve our products or services.
10. Add any additional comments or feedback about our acrylic signage solutions.
