# Beauty Tool Sanitization Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your awareness and behaviors related to the sanitization of your beauty tools. It will help us understand how frequently you clean your tools, how you clean them, and what barriers you may face in maintaining good hygiene practices. Your responses will be anonymous and used to inform education and outreach efforts on tool sanitization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each question and select the most accurate answer from the provided options. You can also add any additional comments or insights in the "Open Comments" field at the end. Please be honest and take the time to thoughtfully answer each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date** (`<date>`, `date`, required): This is the date you completed this survey.
* **How often do you clean your personal beauty tools?** (`<select_one>`, required): This question asks how frequently you clean your personal beauty tools, such as brushes, sponges, and tweezers. Choose from the options: After every use, Once a week, Once a month, Every few months, Never.
* **What is your primary method for cleaning tools?** (`<select_multiple>`, required): This question asks what method you typically use to clean your beauty tools. Choose all that apply from the options: Soap and Water, Specialized Brush Cleanser, Isopropyl Alcohol (70%+), UV Sanitizer Box, Disposable Applicators.
* **How often do you replace tools like makeup sponges or loofahs?** (`<select_one>`, required): This question asks how frequently you replace beauty tools that are disposable or have a limited lifespan. Choose from the options: Monthly, Every 3 months, Every 6 months, Once a year, Only when visibly damaged.
* **How aware are you of the risks of bacteria buildup on beauty tools?** (`<select_one>`, required): This question assesses your understanding of the risks associated with bacteria growth on beauty tools. Choose from the options: Very Aware, Somewhat Aware, Slightly Aware, Not Aware at All.
* **Have you ever experienced a skin reaction you suspect was from dirty tools?** (`<select_one>`, required): This question asks if you have had any adverse skin reactions that you suspect were caused by dirty or contaminated tools. Choose from the options: Yes, definitely, I suspect so, False, Unsure.
* **What is the biggest barrier to cleaning your tools more often?** (`<select_one>`, optional): This question asks what obstacles you face in maintaining good tool hygiene practices. Choose from the options: Lack of time, Forgetting to do it, Not knowing how to clean them properly, Cost of cleaning supplies, Too many tools to manage.
* **When receiving services, how closely do you observe the professional's sanitization?** (`<select_one>`, required): This question evaluates how closely you observe the professionals' sanitization procedures when receiving services. Choose from the options: Very closely, Somewhat closely, I trust their process without checking, I don't pay attention.
* **How important is it that a salon displays its sanitization certification?** (`<select_one>`, required): This question asks how much you value a salon's display of sanitization certification. Choose from the options: Extremely Important, Important, Neutral, Not Important.
* **Would you be interested in learning more about home tool sanitization?** (`<select_one>`, optional): This question gauges your interest in education on home tool sanitization. Choose from the options: Yes, via email, Yes, in-person workshop, No thanks.
* **Share any tips or concerns regarding beauty tool hygiene** (`<text>`, optional): This is an open field for you to share any additional comments, tips, or concerns regarding tool sanitization.
* **Open Comments** (`<text>`, optional): This is an optional field for you to provide any further comments or insights.
 
Note: Please be honest and take the time to thoughtfully answer each question. Your responses will help us better understand your behaviors and attitudes towards tool sanitization.
