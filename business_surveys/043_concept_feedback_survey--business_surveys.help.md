# Concept Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Concept Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback on a specific concept, helping to gauge its perceived value, primary benefits, and potential concerns. This survey aims to gather insights from respondents to improve and refine the concept.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): If you're not comfortable sharing your name, you can leave this field blank.
2. **Industry** (`industry`, text, optional): Please specify the sector or domain you belong to.
3. **Perceived Value** (`perceived_value`, select_one, required): Rate the concept's value to you on a scale of High, Moderate, or Low.
4. **Primary Benefit** (`primary_benefit`, text, optional): Share the main advantage you see in the concept.
5. **Main Concerns** (`main_concerns`, text, optional): List any risks or limitations you foresee with the concept.
6. **Likelihood to Use** (`likelihood_to_use`, select_one, required): Indicate how likely you are to adopt or recommend the concept on a scale of Very Likely, Likely, Maybe, or Unlikely.
7. **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Share any ideas to improve the concept or pitch.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent Name**: This field is optional, and you can leave it blank if you prefer anonymity.
* **Industry**: Please specify the industry or sector you belong to, as this will help us understand the context.
* **Perceived Value**: Rate the concept's value to you using the provided scale. This will help us gauge your perception of the concept's worth.
* **Primary Benefit**: Share the main advantage you see in the concept. This will help us identify the key benefits of the concept.
* **Main Concerns**: List any risks or limitations you foresee with the concept. This will help us understand potential areas of improvement.
* **Likelihood to Use**: Indicate how likely you are to adopt or recommend the concept using the provided scale. This will help us gauge the concept's potential adoption.
* **Suggestions**: Share any ideas to improve the concept or pitch. This is an opportunity to suggest enhancements or new features that could make the concept more appealing.
 
## Tips
* Take your time to reflect on the concept before answering the questions.
* Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your responses.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask us for clarification.
