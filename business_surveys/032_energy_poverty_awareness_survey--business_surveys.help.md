# Energy Poverty Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Poverty Awareness Survey is a tool used to collect information about respondents' experiences, awareness, and opinions related to energy poverty. Energy poverty refers to a situation where households or individuals face challenges in accessing reliable and sufficient energy services, making it difficult for them to meet their basic needs such as lighting, cooking, and heating. This survey aims to gather data to better understand energy poverty, its causes, impact, and ways to alleviate it.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Respondent Information**: Provide a brief description of yourself, including your name and any relevant contact information (e.g., email, phone number, or other).
2. **Household Information**: Briefly describe your household's energy situation, including energy sources used and any challenges you face in accessing energy services.
3. **Energy Poverty Experience**: Describe any challenges or difficulties you face in accessing energy services, such as lack of income, high energy costs, or limited access to energy sources.
4. **Awareness of Energy Poverty**: Select one of the provided options that best describes your understanding of energy poverty.
5. **Causes of Energy Poverty**: Select all that apply from the provided options regarding the causes of energy poverty.
6. **Impact of Energy Poverty**: Select one of the provided options to describe the impact energy poverty has on your daily life.
7. **Duration of Energy Poverty Impact**: If applicable, provide a numerical value (e.g., 1-10 years) to indicate how long you have been experiencing energy poverty.
8. **Other Energy Poverty Impact**: If applicable, describe in detail any additional challenges or difficulties you face due to energy poverty.
9. **Support Systems**: Select all that apply from the provided options indicating any support systems you have or need to cope with energy poverty.
10. **Opinion**: Choose one of the provided options regarding your level of agreement or disagreement with the statement "I feel comfortable asking for help when facing energy poverty challenges".
11. **Satisfaction Level**: Choose one of the provided options regarding your level of satisfaction with the current support systems or services you have access to due to energy poverty.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, `text`, optional)
This is the title of your response to the survey. Please provide a brief and descriptive title that summarizes your answers.

### **Respondent Information** (`respondent_info`, `text`, optional)
Briefly describe yourself, including your name and any relevant contact information.

### **Household Information** (`household_info`, `text`, optional)
Describe your household's energy situation, including energy sources used and any challenges you face in accessing energy services.

### **Energy Poverty Experience** (`energy_poverty_experience`, `text`, optional)
Describe any challenges or difficulties you face in accessing energy services, such as lack of income, high energy costs, or limited access to energy sources.

### **Awareness of Energy Poverty** (`awareness_of_energy_poverty`, `select_one`, required)
Select one of the following options:
*   Poverty is a lack of money
*   Poverty is a lack of opportunities
*   Poverty is when you cannot afford energy
*   Other

### **Causes of Energy Poverty** (`causes_of_energy_poverty`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all that apply:
*   Lack of income
*   High energy costs
*   Poor access to energy sources
*   Other

### **Impact of Energy Poverty** (`energy_poverty_impact`, `select_one`, optional)
Select one of the following options:
*   High
*   Medium
*   Low

### **Duration of Energy Poverty Impact** (`energy_poverty_impact_duration`, `number`, optional)
If applicable, provide a numerical value (e.g., 1-10 years) indicating how long you have been experiencing energy poverty.

### **Other Energy Poverty Impact** (`energy_poverty_impact_other`, `text`, optional)
Describe in detail any additional challenges or difficulties you face due to energy poverty.

### **Support Systems** (`respondent_support`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all that apply:
*   Financial support
*   Emotional support
*   Social support
*   Other

### **Opinion** (`respondent_opinion`, `select_one`, optional)
Choose one of the following options:
*   Strongly agree
*   Somewhat agree
*   Neither agree nor disagree
*   Somewhat disagree
*   Strongly disagree

### **Satisfaction Level** (`respondent_satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional)
Choose one of the following options regarding your level of satisfaction with the current support systems or services you have access to due to energy poverty.
*   Very satisfied
*   Somewhat satisfied
*   Neither satisfied nor dissatisfied
*   Somewhat dissatisfied
*   Very dissatisfied

### **Other Energy Poverty Impact** (`energy_poverty_impact_description`, `note`, optional)
Describe in detail any additional challenges or difficulties you face due to energy poverty.
