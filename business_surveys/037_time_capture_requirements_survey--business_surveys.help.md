# Time Capture Requirements Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Time Capture Requirements Survey is a form used to gather information about an employee's time capture requirements. This form helps employees report their specific needs and preferences related to time capture, which is essential for effective task management and productivity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's instructions and requirements to ensure you understand what is needed.
2. Fill out the form with the requested information, making sure to include all required fields.
3. Select the correct category for your time capture requirements.
4. Provide a clear and concise description of your needs and preferences.
5. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (text, optional): Your name or identifier as an employee, which helps us to identify you and link your submission to your account.
* **Category** (select_one, optional): Select the category that best describes your time capture requirements, such as "Business Surveys".
* **Description** (text, optional): Provide a clear and concise description of your time capture requirements and needs, including any relevant details or context. This will help us to accurately understand your needs and address them accordingly.
