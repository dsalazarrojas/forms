# Livestock Health Data Collection Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Livestock Health Data Collection Survey is designed to collect general data on livestock farms for non-real-time analysis. This form is used to gather information about farm practices, animal health, and other related metrics.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the farm and its livestock.
2. Select the relevant options for each field based on your knowledge and experience.
3. Fill in the form with the required information and select options for each field.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Farm Name (1)
* **Farm Name**: Enter the name of the farm.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No

### Farm Size (2)
* **Farm Size**: Enter the size of the farm.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No

### Total Cattle (3)
* **Total Cattle**: Enter the total number of cattle on the farm.
  * Type: number
  * Required: No

### Herd Health (4)
* **Herd Health**: Select the overall health of the herd.
  * Type: select multiple
  * Options: High, Medium, Low
  * Required: No

### Vaccination Status (5)
* **Vaccination Status**: Select whether the farm has vaccinated its cattle.
  * Type: select one
  * Options: True, False
  * Required: No

### Farm Practices (6)
* **Farm Practices**: Enter any special practices or procedures used on the farm.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No

### Veterinary Care (7)
* **Veterinary Care**: Enter any notable veterinary care or treatments used on the farm.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No

### Monitoring Frequency (8)
* **Monitoring Frequency**: Select how often the farm monitors its livestock.
  * Type: select multiple
  * Options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly
  * Required: No

### Data Collection Method (9)
* **Data Collection Method**: Enter how the farm collects its data.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No

### Animal Count (10)
* **Animal Count**: Enter the total number of animals on the farm.
  * Type: number
  * Required: No

## Tips

- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- Ensure you have the necessary information before submitting.
- If unsure, select "Unknown" or enter "Not Applicable" where necessary.
- Submit the form when you have completed it to the best of your ability.
