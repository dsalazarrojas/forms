# Building Compliance Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey aims to assess employees' understanding of compliance requirements in the workplace, including fire safety, emergency procedures, accessibility, and chemical handling. Your responses will help us identify areas where we can improve our compliance programs and provide targeted training and resources to enhance your safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Respond to the survey questions honestly, and answer based on your actual experience and knowledge.
2. Please answer all questions, as your feedback is invaluable to us.
3. If you have any suggestions or recommendations for improving compliance awareness, feel free to share them in the "Improvement Suggestions" field.
4. If you need any resources or support to better understand compliance requirements, please list them in the "Resources Needed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Information** (`respondent_info`, `text`, required: false): This is the beginning of the survey, and we ask for your contact information to ensure we can follow up with any questions or concerns.
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required: true): Please enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Department or Team** (`department`, `text`, required: true): Please enter the department or team you belong to.
* **Your Position or Role** (`position`, `text`, required: true): Enter your position or role within the organization.
* **How Long Have You Worked Here?** (`tenure`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the length of time you have worked with us.
* **Overall Awareness of Building Compliance Requirements** (`overall_awareness`, `select_one`, required: true): Please rate your overall awareness of building compliance requirements.
* **Knowledge of Safety Procedures** (`safety_procedures`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate your knowledge of safety procedures.
* **Understanding of Emergency Procedures** (`emergency_procedures`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate your understanding of emergency procedures.
* **Awareness of Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_awareness`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate your awareness of accessibility requirements.
* **Understanding of Fire Safety Protocols** (`fire_safety`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate your understanding of fire safety protocols.
* **Aware of Evacuation Routes** (`evacuation_routes`, `select_one`, required: true): Are you aware of the evacuation routes in the building?
* **Know Location of Emergency Equipment** (`emergency_equipment`, `select_one`, required: true): Are you aware of the location of emergency equipment, such as fire extinguishers and first aid kits?
* **Knowledge of Hazardous Material Handling** (`chemical_safety`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate your knowledge of hazardous material handling.
* **Awareness of Ergonomics Requirements** (`ergonomics_awareness`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate your awareness of ergonomic requirements.
* **Know How to Report Compliance Issues** (`reporting_procedures`, `select_one`, required: true): Are you aware of the procedures for reporting compliance issues?
* **Training Received** (`training_received`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Which types of training have you received on compliance requirements?
* **Frequency of Training Provided** (`training_frequency`, `select_one`, required: true): How often do you receive compliance training?
* **Effectiveness of Compliance Training** (`training_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required: true): Rate the effectiveness of our compliance training.
* **Topics Needing More Training** (`needed_training`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Which topics do you feel need more training on compliance requirements?
* **Preferred Training Method** (`preferred_training`, `select_one`, required: false): How would you prefer to receive compliance training?
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, required: false): Share your suggestions for improving compliance awareness and knowledge.
* **Resources Needed** (`resource_needs`, `text`, required: false): If you need any resources to better understand compliance requirements, please list them.
* **Is Compliance Information Easily Accessible?** (`accessible_information`, `select_one`, required: true): Is compliance information easily accessible to you?
