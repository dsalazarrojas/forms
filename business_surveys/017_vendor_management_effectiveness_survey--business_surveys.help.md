# Vendor Management Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Management Effectiveness Survey is designed to assess the effectiveness of your vendor management processes and the performance of your vendors. This survey helps to identify areas of improvement and provides valuable insights for better vendor management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the relevant answers for each question and fill in any required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Management Effectiveness Survey**: This is a multiple-choice question that asks about the effectiveness of your vendor management processes. Please select all that apply.
* **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the vendor you are evaluating.
* **Vendor Rating**: Rate the performance of the vendor (Good or Fair).
* **Vendor Performance**: Indicate if the vendor is performing well (Yes or No).
* **Contract Start Date**: Enter the start date of the contract with the vendor.
* **Contract End Date**: Enter the end date of the contract with the vendor.
* **Vendor Category**: Indicate if the vendor is active or inactive (Yes or No).
* **Vendor Status**: Select all statuses that apply to the vendor (Active or Inactive).
* **Vendor Rating 1**: Rate the performance of the vendor (Good or Fair).
* **Vendor Management Effectiveness Score**: Enter a score between 0 and 100 to evaluate the overall effectiveness of the vendor.
* **Vendor Management Effectiveness 1**: Indicate if the vendor management processes are effective (Yes or No).

* **Vendor Rating 2**: Rate the performance of the vendor (Good or Fair).
