# Operations Transformation Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Operations Transformation Feedback Survey is a form used by operations teams to gather feedback on the progress and outcomes of specific business operations. The feedback gathered will help to identify areas of improvement and optimize future operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the operation you are providing feedback on in the "Operation Name" field.
2. Select one or more outcomes from the "Outcomes" field that apply to your operation.
3. Provide a detailed description of any challenges you faced during the operation in the "Challenges" field.
4. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please enter them in the "Suggestion" field.
5. Select one of the future outcomes that you expect from the operation in the "Future Outcomes" field.
6. Enter the date when you expected the operation to be completed in the "Expected Date" field.
7. Enter the actual date when the operation was completed in the "Actual Date" field.
8. Estimate the time spent on the operation in the "Time Spent" field.
9. If you have any additional notes on the time spent, please enter them in the "Time Spent Note" field.
10. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
11. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
