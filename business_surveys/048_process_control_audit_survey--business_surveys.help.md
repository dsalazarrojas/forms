# Process Control Audit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Control Audit Survey form is designed for collecting information on the status and effectiveness of internal process controls within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start the form, please select the "Process" tab or click on any of the other pages that interest you.
2. Answer each question thoroughly, using the provided options when available.
3. Be sure to provide all required information. Required fields are marked as such.
4. Review your form submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Process Control Audit Survey

* **Process Control Level** (`process_control_level`, select_one, required): Choose the level of process control from the options provided: Very High, High, Medium, Low, or None.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the team responsible for the process.
* **Audit Date** (`audit_date`, date, required): Select the date of the audit or assessment.
* **Audit Scope** (`audit_scope`, select_one, required): Choose the scope of the audit from the options: Entire Organization, Department, Process.
* **Process Step** (`process_step`, text, required): Describe the step of the process being audited.
* **Control Measures** (`control_measures`, text, required): List the measures taken to ensure process control.
* **Observations and Recommendations** (`observations_and_recommendations`, text, required): Record any observations and recommendations made during the audit.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required): Describe the next steps planned or taken.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the review from the options: Complete, Incomplete, Not Applicable.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required): Select the date of the review.
* **Review By** (`review_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or team reviewing the process.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments made during the review.
* **Auditor Comments** (`auditor_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments from the auditor.
* **Audit Status** (`audit_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the audit from the options: Active, Inactive, Complete.
* **Auditor Name** (`auditor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the auditor.
* **Team Member Name** (`team_member_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the team member.
* **Team Member Email** (`team_member_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the team member.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department associated with the process.
* **Process** (`process`, text, required): Enter the name of the process being audited.
* **Control** (`control`, text, required): Enter the control measures taken.
* **Risk** (`risk`, text, required): Enter any risk associated with the process.
* **Impact** (`impact`, text, required): Enter the impact on the process.
* **Likelihood** (`likelihood`, text, required): Enter the likelihood of the risk.
* **Review By Date** (`review_by_date`, date, required): Select the date the review was made by.

### Tips
