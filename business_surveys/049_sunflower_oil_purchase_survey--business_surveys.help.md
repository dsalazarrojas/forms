# Sunflower Oil Purchase Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about sunflower oil purchasing habits from customers. This information will be used to better understand customer preferences and behaviors related to sunflower oil sales and distribution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

1. Answer each question carefully, as your responses will help us better understand your habits and preferences.
2. Please select one answer per question.
3. You may skip any questions that do not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Do you currently buy sunflower oil?** (`sunflower_oil_purchase_survey_1`, select_one, required): This question asks if you have ever purchased sunflower oil. Please select either "Yes" or "No" to answer this question.
- **Sunflower oil price** (`sunflower_oil_price_range_1`, `sunflower_oil_price_range_2`, number, required): This question asks about your experience with sunflower oil prices. Please select one of the two options provided, either "Less than 1 USD" or "1-2 USD".
- **Where do you purchase sunflower oil?** (`sunflower_oil_purchase_1`, `sunflower_oil_purchase_2`, `sunflower_oil_purchase_3`, select_multiple, required): This question asks about your typical purchase location. Please select all applicable options from the list, either "Local store", "Online store", or "Other".
- **Additional comments** (`other_info_1`, text, required): This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional comments or information about your sunflower oil purchasing habits.
