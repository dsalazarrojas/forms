# Cafe Market Research Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers about their experience with our cafe. The goal is to understand their demographics, behavior, and preferences to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the correct age bracket from the options provided.
2. Choose how often you visit our cafe.
3. Indicate your main purchase type.
4. If you wish to provide an approximate amount spent per visit, enter a number. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
5. Select all features that apply to your preferences for our cafe.
6. If you've heard about our cafe through a referral, social media, or by walking in, type your answer.
7. If you wish to provide an indication of your likelihood to recommend our cafe, enter a number from 0 to 10.
8. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Age Group** (`respondent_age_group`, select_one, required): Select your age group from the provided options.
* **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you visit our cafe.
* **Main Purchase Type** (`main_purchase`, select_one, required): Indicate your main purchase type when visiting our cafe.
* **Average Spending Per Visit** (`spending_per_visit`, number, optional): If you wish to provide an approximate amount spent per visit, enter a number. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
* **Preferred Cafe Features** (`preferred_features`, select_multiple, required): Select all features that apply to your preferences for our cafe.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`brand_awareness`, text, optional): If you've heard about our cafe through a referral, social media, or by walking in, type your answer.
* **Likelihood to Recommend** (`likelihood_to_recommend`, number, optional): If you wish to provide an indication of your likelihood to recommend our cafe, enter a number from 0 to 10.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

Note: All fields with "required" as a property are mandatory and must be filled out.
