# Youth Climate Policy Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Climate Policy Understanding Survey is a tool for gathering information on youth perspectives on climate policy. This form is designed to be filled out by individuals who have some level of understanding of climate policies and their impact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read through each section carefully to understand what is being asked
* Fill out each section with your thoughts and opinions on the topic
* Be as specific and detailed as possible in your responses
* If you have any additional comments or information that you would like to share, you can add them in the blank spaces provided

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (text): This is where you can start by briefly introducing yourself and explaining why you are interested in climate policy.
* **Awareness** (text): This section is where you can talk about your level of awareness on climate policy, including what you know and what you don't know.
* **Attitudes** (text): Share your attitudes towards climate policy, including any positive or negative feelings you have about it.
* **Ideas** (text): This is where you can share any ideas you have on climate policy, including any suggestions or proposals you think would be effective.
* **National Policies** (text): Describe any national policies you are aware of related to climate policy.
* **NGO-Schools-Advocacy** (text): Share any experiences or knowledge you have about NGOs (Non-Governmental Organizations) advocating for climate policy.
* **Other Policies** (text): This is where you can share any other policies not covered in the previous sections.
* **Conclusion** (text): Summarize your main points and reiterate your interest in climate policy.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate in your responses
* Avoid repeating the same answers in different sections
* Use specific examples and details to illustrate your points
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask the administrator
