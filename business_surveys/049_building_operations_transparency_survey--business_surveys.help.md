# Building Operations Transparency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your experience and perceptions of building operations. It is designed to help management understand what areas need improvement in terms of communication, transparency, and overall operations. Your input will help us identify areas of strength and weakness, and make informed decisions to enhance the working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: Please read the introductory note, which explains the purpose of this survey.
2. **Respondent Information**: Provide your name and email address. This is a required field to ensure we can contact you if needed.
3. **Type of Occupancy**: Choose the type of occupancy that best describes you (e.g., tenant, owner-occupant, etc.).
4. **Length of Stay**: Select how long you have been in the building.
5. **Awareness of Building Operations**: Rate your overall awareness of building operations.
6. **Management Transparency**: Rate the current level of management transparency.
7. **Information Access**: Rate how easy it is to access operations information.
8. **Communication Effectiveness**: Rate the quality of management communication.
9. **Update Frequency**: Rate how frequently you receive operational updates.
10. **Timeliness of Notifications**: Rate the timeliness of important notifications.
11. **Communication Channels**: Select the communication methods you prefer or use (e.g., email, phone, in-person, etc.).
12. **Preferred Communication Method**: Choose your preferred method of communication (e.g., email, phone, etc.).
13. **Maintenance and Repair Transparency**: Rate the transparency of maintenance and repair activities.
14. **Financial Operations Transparency**: Rate the transparency of financial operations.
15. **Future Planning Transparency**: Rate the transparency of future plans and changes.
16. **Decision-Making Process Transparency**: Rate the transparency of decision-making processes.
17. **Important Information**: Choose the topics that are most important to you for building operations.
18. **Transparency Gaps**: Describe areas where you feel transparency is lacking.
19. **Missing Information**: Explain what specific information you need but don't have access to.
20. **Unclear Areas**: Describe any areas where communication is unclear.
21. **Suggestions for Improvement**: Provide suggestions for how we can improve transparency.
22. **Additional Communication Improvements**: Suggest ways to improve communication.
23. **Trust Level**: Rate your level of trust in management.
24. **Confidence in Operations**: Rate your confidence that operations are managed well.
25. **Additional Feedback**: Add any additional comments or feedback you have.
