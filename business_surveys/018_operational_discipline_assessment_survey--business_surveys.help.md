# Operational Discipline Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a team's operational discipline, including their communication, safety practices, and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each page and answer the questions as they appear.
2.  For multiple-choice questions, select the answer that best describes your experience or situation.
3.  For text fields, provide a brief explanation or description.
4.  Ensure you provide accurate and honest answers, as they will help improve the team's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, Required: No): Please select all the team members who are part of the team being assessed.
* **Team Leaders** (`team_leaders`, select_one, Required: No): Select the team leader or leaders who are part of the team being assessed.
* **Punctuality** (`punctuality`, number, Required: No): Please enter the average time team members arrive at work or are punctual.
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, Required: No): Provide a brief description of the team's communication practices, such as how they communicate with each other, with management, or with external partners.
* **Procedures** (`procedures`, text, Required: No): Describe the procedures the team uses to complete tasks, such as protocols, checklists, or workflows.
* **Safety Practices** (`safety_practices`, select_multiple, Required: No): Select all the safety practices that are currently in use within the team.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, Required: No): Share any feedback or suggestions on how the team can improve their performance or practices.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, Required: No): Provide any additional comments or explanations for the team's assessment.
* **Date** (`date`, date, Required: No): Enter the date when the assessment was completed.
* **Time** (`time`, time, Required: No): Enter the time when the assessment was completed.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by team members or leaders to provide a comprehensive view of the team's operational discipline.
