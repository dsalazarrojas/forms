# Company Philanthropy Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Company Philanthropy Assessment is a tool to help organizations evaluate and improve their philanthropic practices. It collects information on various aspects of corporate giving, including respondent role, giving priorities, employee engagement, impact measurement, and budget allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Respond to each question carefully and honestly.
2.  Choose the most accurate answer for each question based on your company's current practices.
3.  If you are unsure about a question, use your best judgment and explain your choice in the "Additional Comments" section.
4.  Be as specific as possible when providing numerical values (e.g., percentage or amount).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Respondent role
- **Respondent role** (`respondent_role`, text, optional): Please select the role you play within the company (e.g., Executive, HR, CSR, Employee).

### 2. Giving priorities
- **Giving priorities** (`giving_priorities`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the causes that are currently prioritized by your company (e.g., Education, Health, Environment, Social services, Arts).

### 3. Employee engagement in giving
- **Employee engagement in giving** (`employee_engagement`, select_one, optional): Indicate your company's level of employee participation in philanthropic efforts (e.g., High, Moderate, Low).

### 4. Measurement of impact
- **Measurement of impact** (`measurement_of_impact`, text, optional): Describe how your company measures the effectiveness of their philanthropic efforts.

### 5. Annual budget allocation for philanthropy
- **Annual budget allocation for philanthropy** (`budget_allocation`, text, optional): Provide an approximate percentage or amount of your company's annual budget allocated for philanthropy.

## Tips

- Be honest and accurate when answering these questions.
- If you are unsure about a question, explain your reasoning in the "Additional Comments" section.
- This form is intended for internal use, and your responses will help your organization improve its philanthropic practices.
