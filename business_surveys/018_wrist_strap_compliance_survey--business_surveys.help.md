# Wrist Strap Compliance Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wrist Strap Compliance Survey is designed to help management teams monitor and assess the compliance of manufacturing, lab, and electronics teams with specified guidelines. It's essential to fill it out thoroughly and accurately to guarantee that the collected data is valid.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and answer all questions truthfully.
- Make sure to provide the correct team name (manufacturing, lab, or electronics team) and a detailed description of the wrist straps used.
- If you're unsure about any question, ask for help or clarification before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Manufacturing Team** (`manufacturing_team`, text, optional): Enter the name of your manufacturing team. This field is not mandatory but will help us to better understand the compliance with the guidelines in the manufacturing department.
* **Lab Team** (`lab_team`, text, optional): Enter the name of your lab team. This field is optional and will aid in understanding the lab's compliance with the guidelines.
* **Electronics Team** (`electronics_team`, text, optional): Enter the name of your electronics team. This field is not mandatory but will help us in understanding the electronics department's compliance with the guidelines.
* **Supervisor Review** (`supervisor_review`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the wrist strap compliance in this field. This field is not mandatory but can be helpful for the management team to get a better understanding of the situation.
