# inventory_management_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the current state of your inventory. It helps us understand the name, price, description, type, stock status, location, and owner of each item in your inventory. This data will be used to analyze and improve our inventory management practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field with the requested information. All fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete data is crucial for the best results.
2. Make sure to select the correct stock status from the dropdown menu for each item.
3. For multi-select fields, such as "owner", select as many relevant options as apply.
4. Double-check your inputs before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **item_name** (text): Enter the name of the item in your inventory.
* **item_price** (number): Enter the current price of the item.
* **item_description** (text): Enter a brief description of the item.
* **item_type** (text): Select the type of the item (e.g., "Product", "Service", etc.).
* **stock_status** (select_one): Choose the current stock status of the item:
	+ **available**: The item is in stock and available for sale.
	+ **out of stock**: The item is currently not in stock.
	+ **backordered**: The item is currently backordered and not available for sale.
	+ **discontinued**: The item is no longer available for sale.
* **item_location** (text): Enter the location where the item is currently located.
* **owner** (select_multiple): Select all applicable owners of the item.
* **count** (number): Enter the current stock count of the item.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and complete as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We appreciate your help in maintaining the accuracy of our inventory data.
