# Cost Allocation Efficiency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information on the efficiency of cost allocation processes within your department or division. This data will help management identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to optimize resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select the correct department that best describes your role or function.
2. If applicable, choose the specific departments that are relevant to your role or function.
3. Provide a brief description of the current transparency levels of cost allocation within your department or division.
4. Identify any challenges you are facing with cost allocation.
5. Enter your email address and/or phone number for feedback purposes.
6. Provide any additional comments or observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cost Allocation Processes**: (`cost_allocation_efficiency_survey_1`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the cost allocation processes within your department or division.
* **Cost Allocation Transparency** (`cost_allocation_transparency`, note, required/optional): Provide detailed description of current transparency levels within your department or division.
* **Cost Allocation Challenges** (`cost_allocation_challenges`, select_one, required): Identify the level of challenges you are facing with cost allocation. Options are High, Medium, and Low.
* **Department 1** (`department_1`, select_one, required): Select the department that best describes your role or function. Options are Operations and Finance.
* **Department 2** (`department_2`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, choose additional departments that are relevant to your role or function. Options are Operations, Finance, and Other.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or observations about cost allocation processes within your department or division.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for feedback purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for feedback purposes.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, optional): Enter the time you submitted this form.
