# Faith Based Social Cohesion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to measure the level of social cohesion within a faith-based community. The survey collects opinions on various aspects of community life, including feelings of belonging, interfaith interaction, leadership, involvement, trust, conflict, communication, and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question with the response that best reflects your current feelings or experiences. You can choose from the provided options for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1): This section serves as an introduction to the survey. You do not need to answer this question as it is a note-type field.
* **Does the Community Feel Like Home?** (2): Choose one or more answers that reflect how you feel about your community. 
	+ Community of friends
	+ Family
	+ Church
	+ School
* **Interfaith Interaction** (3): Select one option that reflects your level of interfaith interaction:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Sometimes
* **The Community is Like a Big Family** (4): Select one option that reflects your level of agreement:
	+ Strongly Agree

	+ Don't Know

* **I Am in Conflict with Other Community Members** (8): Select one option that reflects your level of conflict:

	+ Strongly Agree

	+ Don't Know

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your responses.

* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form creator.
