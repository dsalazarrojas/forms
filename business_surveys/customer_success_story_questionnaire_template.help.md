# customer_success_story_questionnaire_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights and quantifiable results for crafting compelling customer success stories. It helps businesses and organizations understand their customers' experiences, benefits, and achievements when using their solution. By filling out this form, you will be providing valuable information to help us understand what works well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Answer each question thoroughly and honestly.
* For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply for each option that best describes your experience.
* Use the text field to provide a specific achievement or quantifiable result that stands out from your experience with our solution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### How satisfied are you with the results achieved using our solution?
* **How satisfied are you with the results achieved using our solution?** (`How satisfied are you with the results achieved using our solution?`, `single`, required):
	+ Choose the option that best describes your level of satisfaction with the results achieved using our solution.
* **What primary business challenge were you facing before using our solution?** (`What primary business challenge were you facing before using our solution?`, `text`, optional):
	+ Describe the main business challenge you were facing before using our solution.
* **Which of the following benefits have you realized since implementing our solution?** (`Which of the following benefits have you realized since implementing our solution?`, `multiple`, required):
	+ Select all the benefits that apply to your experience with our solution.
* **Which features of our solution have been most impactful for you?** (`Which features of our solution have been most impactful for you?`, `multiple`, optional):
	+ Choose all the features that have been most impactful for you.
* **How likely are you to recommend our solution to a colleague or peer?** (`How likely are you to recommend our solution to a colleague or peer?`, `single`, required):
	+ Choose the level of likelihood that best describes your willingness to recommend our solution.
* **What specific achievement or quantifiable result stands out from your experience with our solution?** (`What specific achievement or quantifiable result stands out from your experience with our solution?`, `text`, optional):
	+ Describe a specific achievement or quantifiable result that stands out from your experience with our solution.

## Tips
* Be specific and honest when answering each question.
* Use examples or metrics to support your answers whenever possible.
* Remember, the goal of this form is to help us understand your genuine experience with our solution. Your input will help us create better customer success stories!
