# Budgeting Process Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to evaluate the budgeting process within your organization, gathering insights on how well the process is working, what's working well, and areas that require improvement. Your input will help identify strengths and weaknesses, enabling data-driven decisions to refine the budgeting process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, answering each question based on your personal experience and involvement in the budgeting process.
2. Be honest and provide clear, concise responses to each question.
3. Ensure you've selected all relevant options for questions that ask for multiple selections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name (`respondent_name`, text, optional)**: Your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Department (`department`, text, required)**: Your department or division within the organization.
* **Your Role (`role`, text, required)**: Your job title or position within the organization.
* **Email Address (`email`, email, required)**: Your official contact email address.
* **Involvement in Budgeting Process (`involvement_level`, select_one, required)**: Select how involved you are in the budgeting process:
	+ Lead the Budgeting Process
	+ Actively Participate
	+ Provide Input Occasionally
	+ Review Only
	+ Minimal Involvement
* **Years of Budgeting Experience (`years_experience`, select_one, required)**: Select how long you've been involved in the budgeting process:
	+ Less Than 1 Year
	+ 1-3 Years
	+ 4-6 Years
	+ 7-10 Years
	+ More Than 10 Years
* **Process Clarity (`process_clarity`, select_one, required)**: Rate how clear the budgeting process is:
	+ Very Clear
	+ Somewhat Clear
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Unclear
	+ Very Unclear
* **Timeline Adequacy (`timeline_adequacy`, select_one, required)**: Rate whether the budgeting timeline is reasonable:
	+ More Than Adequate
	+ Adequate
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Rushed
	+ Too Rushed
* **Communication Effectiveness (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, required)**: Rate the effectiveness of communication during the budgeting process:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
	+ Poor
* **Resource Availability (`resource_availability`, select_one, required)**: Rate the availability of resources for budgeting:
	+ More Than Enough
	+ Adequate
	+ Somewhat Limited
	+ Very Limited
	+ Insufficient
* **Collaboration Quality (`collaboration_quality`, select_one, required)**: Rate the quality of cross-department collaboration:
	+ Excellent Collaboration
	+ Good Collaboration
	+ Average Collaboration
	+ Poor Collaboration
	+ No Collaboration Occurs
* **Budgeting Tools Effectiveness (`tools_effectiveness`, select_one, required)**: Rate the effectiveness of current budgeting tools:
	+ Very Effective
	+ Somewhat Effective
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Ineffective
	+ Very Ineffective
* **Challenges Faced (`challenges_faced`, select_multiple, required)**: Select all challenges you face during the budgeting process:
	+ Unclear Guidelines
	+ Tight Deadlines
	+ Lack of Data
	+ Poor Communication
	+ Inadequate Tools
	+ Limited Training
	+ Changing Requirements
	+ Resource Constraints
	+ Lack of Leadership Support
	+ Other
* **Process Strengths (`strengths_identified`, text, optional)**: List what works well in the budgeting process.
* **Areas for Improvement (`improvement_areas`, text, required)**: List areas that need improvement in the budgeting process.
* **Training Needs (`training_needs`, select_multiple, optional)**: Select additional training that would be helpful for you:
	+ Budgeting Software Training
	+ Financial Analysis Skills
	+ Forecasting Techniques
	+ Communication Skills
	+ Strategic Planning
	+ Data Analysis
	+ No Additional Training Needed
* **Overall Satisfaction (`satisfaction_overall`, select_one, required)**: Rate overall satisfaction with the budgeting process:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Recommendations for Improvement (`recommendations`, text, optional)**: Suggest improvements to the budgeting process.
* **Willingness to Participate in Future (`willingness_participate_future`, select_one, optional)**: Select your willingness to participate in process improvements:
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Maybe
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No

## Tips

1. Be honest and provide clear, concise responses to each question.
2. Ensure you've selected all relevant options for questions that ask for multiple selections.
3. If you're unsure about a particular field, please ask your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Recommendations for Improvement" field.
