# Wedding Artist Fee Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Artist Fee Survey is designed to gather information from artists about their fees and preferences for weddings. This data helps wedding planners and coordinators understand the costs and time commitments involved in hiring an artist for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out all the required fields and provide as much detail as possible. You can submit the form once and update your profile with new information as your needs and preferences change.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Performer Fee ($)
* **Performer Fee** (number, required): Enter your base fee for performing at a wedding. This is the minimum amount you charge for your services.

### Travel Preference
* **Travel Preference** (select_one, optional): Choose your preferred mode of travel for weddings:
	+ **Local**: You are available to travel within your local area.
	+ **National**: You are available to travel within your country.
	+ **International**: You are available to travel outside of your country.

### Booking Priority
* **Booking Priority** (select_multiple, optional): Indicate your priority level for booking requests:
	+ **High**: You prioritize booking requests above all others.
	+ **Medium**: You consider booking requests on a case-by-case basis.
	+ **Low**: You only accept bookings for high-paying clients or emergencies.

### Travel Cost ($)
* **Travel Cost** (number, optional): Enter any additional cost associated with traveling to a location, such as gas, lodging, or meals.

### Travel Time (HH:MM)
* **Travel Time** (time, optional): Enter your estimated travel time to the destination. This helps us understand your time commitment and plan accordingly.

### Artist Name
* **Artist Name** (text, optional): Enter your name as you would like to be displayed on marketing materials and event programs.

### Artist Contact (email)
* **Artist Contact** (email, optional): Enter your email address for event coordinators and wedding planners to contact you.

### Artist Description
* **Artist Description** (note, optional): Enter a brief description of your services, such as your musical style or specialties.

### Travel Distance (miles)
* **Travel Distance** (number, optional): Enter the maximum distance you are willing to travel for a wedding.

### Travel Time To Destination
* **Travel Time To Destination** (time, optional): Enter your estimated travel time to the destination, including any layovers or time zone changes.

## Tips
* Please update your profile with any changes to your fees, preferences, or availability to avoid duplicate submissions.
* Be as specific as possible when entering your travel costs and times to ensure accurate estimates.
* Use a consistent format for your artist name and contact information to ensure easy communication.
