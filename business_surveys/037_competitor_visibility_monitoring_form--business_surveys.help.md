# Competitor Visibility Monitoring Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to track and monitor activities performed by competitors that could impact our business. By filling out this form, you'll help us identify trends and opportunities to respond to competitor actions, ensuring we stay competitive in the market.

## How To Complete This Form

Please fill out the form for each competitor activity you have observed. If you've noticed multiple activities, feel free to submit multiple entries. Ensure you're accurate and thorough in your reporting to help us better understand the landscape.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Observer Name 
**Observer Name** (`observer_full_name`, text, required): 
Please enter the name of the team member capturing this observation.

### Date of Observation 
**Date of Observation** (`date_of_observation_log`, date, required): 
When was this activity noticed? Please select a date.

### Competitor Name 
**Competitor Name** (`observed_competitor_name`, text, required): 
Enter the company name performing the activity.

### Channel of Visibility 
**Channel of Visibility** (`visibility_channel_type`, select_multiple, required): 
Please select all applicable channels where you've seen this activity:
* Social Media (LinkedIn/X/IG)
* Search Engine Ad (Google/Bing)
* Industry News / PR
* Physical Billboard / Signage
* Event / Conference Sponsorship
* TV / Radio Ad
* Direct Email / Outreach

### Description of Activity 
**Description of Activity** (`activity_description_details`, text, required): 
Provide details on the campaign, messaging, or offer.

### Link to Evidence (Optional) 
**Link to Evidence (Optional)** (`evidence_url_link`, text, optional): 
If available, provide the URL to a post, article, or shared folder for further reference.

### Estimated Visibility Impact 
**Estimated Visibility Impact** (`impact_rating_visibility`, select_one, required): 
Assess how prominent this activity is:
* 1 low
* 2
* 3
* 4
* 5 high

### Strategic Threat Level 
**Strategic Threat Level** (`strategic_threat_level_mon`, select_one, required): 
Evaluate the level of concern:
* Critical (Requires immediate counter-campaign)
* Moderate (Monitor engagement)
* Low (Routine activity)

### Recommended Internal Follow-up 
**Recommended Internal Follow-up** (`recommended_follow_up_action`, text, optional): 
Please suggest actions for internal follow-up, if applicable.

### Evidence Notation 
**Evidence Notation** (`evidence_notification_note`, note, required): 
Please remember to save any supporting evidence for the Shared Strategy Folder.

### Date Logged 
**Date Logged** (`logging_submission_date`, date, required): 
Indicate the date of submission.
