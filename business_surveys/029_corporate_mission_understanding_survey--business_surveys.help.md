# Corporate Mission Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Mission Understanding Survey is designed to gauge the understanding and alignment of corporate mission among various aspects of the organization. This survey aims to collect feedback and insights on the current state of mission understanding within the organization, allowing for a better grasp of where the team stands in terms of aligning with the corporate mission.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant options for each field that best describe your understanding or alignment with the corporate mission.
2. Provide detailed feedback and comments in the text fields where requested.
3. Ensure that you answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Corporate Mission Understanding** (`mission_understanding`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select all options that describe your understanding of the corporate mission.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter any additional feedback or comments about your understanding of the corporate mission.
* **Internal Communication** (`internal_communication`, `text`, optional):
	+ Provide any comments or examples about internal communication within the organization.
* **Leadership Alignment** (`leadership_alignment`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you are aligned with leadership on the corporate mission.
* **Departmentalization** (`departmentalization`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter any comments or examples about departmentalization and its relation to the corporate mission.
* **Team Alignment** (`team_alignment`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter a number to indicate how aligned you are with your team on the corporate mission (1-5, where 1 is "Not at all" and 5 is "Completely").
* **Manager Alignment** (`manager_alignment`, `text`, optional):
	+ Provide any comments or examples about alignment with your manager on the corporate mission.
* **How Frequently Do You Communicate?** (`communication_frequency`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you communicate with colleagues and teams frequently about the corporate mission.
* **Mission Understanding 1-5** (`mission_understanding_1-5`, `text`, optional):
	+ Provide any comments or examples about your understanding of the corporate mission for each specific aspect.
* **Feedback 1-3** (`feedback_1-3`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter any additional feedback or comments about your understanding of the corporate mission.
* **Internal Communication 1-3** (`internal_communication_1-3`, `text`, optional):
	+ Provide any comments or examples about internal communication within the organization.
* **Leadership Alignment 1-2** (`leadership_alignment_1-2`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter any comments or examples about alignment with leadership on the corporate mission.
* **Departmentalization 1-2** (`departmentalization_1-2`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter any comments or examples about departmentalization and its relation to the corporate mission.
* **Team Alignment 1-3** (`team_alignment_1-3`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter a number to indicate how aligned you are with your team on the corporate mission (1-5, where 1 is "Not at all" and 5 is "Completely").

Note: This survey is designed to be completed by employees of the organization.
