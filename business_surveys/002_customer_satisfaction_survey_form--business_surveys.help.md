# Customer Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Customer Satisfaction Survey Form" is designed to collect feedback and satisfaction levels from customers about their experience with a company or business. This form aims to gather essential information to help improve customer satisfaction and overall business performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information, including your name, company, and job title.
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with the company (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
3. Choose the company size that best represents your company (Small, Medium, or Large).
4. Select your job function (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
5. Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.
6. Enter your company URL, if applicable.
7. Rate your job satisfaction (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
8. Add any additional comments or notes about your experience.
9. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (text): Enter your name, company, and job title.
* **Satisfaction** (number): Rate your overall satisfaction with the company, with 1 being the lowest and 3 being the highest.
* **Rating** (select_one): Rate your satisfaction with the company, selecting one of the three options provided.
* **Comments** (text): Enter any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number, which will be used to contact you if needed.
* **Date** (date): Select the date you completed the form.
* **Time** (time): Select the time you completed the form.
* **Note** (note): Enter any additional notes about your experience.
* **Business Info** (text): Enter your company information, such as size and location.
* **Name** (text): Enter your name.
* **Company** (text): Enter your company name.
* **Position** (text): Enter your position or role in the company.
* **Department** (text): Enter your department or area of work.
* **Job Title** (text): Enter your job title.
* **Company Size** (select_one): Select the size of your company, with Small, Medium, or Large being the options.
* **Location** (select_multiple): Select multiple options for your location.
* **Industry** (select_one): Select the industry that best represents your company.
* **Job Function** (select_one): Select your job function or role in the company.
* **Contact Person** (text): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (email): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Company URL** (text): Enter your company URL, if applicable.
* **Job Satisfaction** (select_one): Rate your job satisfaction, with Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3 being the highest.
* **Submit** (text): Review and submit the form.

Remember to review the form carefully before submitting to ensure you've provided all the required information. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
