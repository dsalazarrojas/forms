# Uniform Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Uniform Management Survey is designed to gather information about uniforms usage, satisfaction level, inventory, and other relevant details to help the management team make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the "Uniform usage" field to describe how you use the uniforms.
2. Provide your satisfaction level with the uniforms by choosing a value in the "Satisfaction level" field.
3. If you have any uniforms in your inventory, enter the quantity in the "Uniform inventory" field.
4. In "What do you want to do with a uniform?", choose one option from the list:
	* Buy
	* Rent
	* Return
5. In "Which of the following do you want to do?", choose multiple options from the list:
	* Buy
	* Rent
	* Return
6. If you have purchased or rented a uniform, provide the date in the "Date of purchase/rental" field.
7. If you have purchased or rented a uniform, provide the time of purchase/rental in the "Time of purchase/rental" field.
8. Provide any additional comments or questions in the "Additional comments" field.
9. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Uniform usage** (Text, required: false): Describe how you use the uniforms.
* **Satisfaction level** (Number, required: false): Provide your satisfaction level with the uniforms, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Uniform inventory** (Number, required: false): Enter the quantity of uniforms in your inventory.
* **What do you want to do with a uniform?** (Select one, required: false): Choose one option from the list:
	+ Buy
	+ Rent
	+ Return
* **Which of the following do you want to do?** (Select multiple, required: false): Choose multiple options from the list:
	+ Buy
	+ Rent
	+ Return
* **Date of purchase/rental** (Date, required: false): Provide the date of purchase or rental of a uniform.
* **Time of purchase/rental** (Time, required: false): Provide the time of purchase or rental of a uniform.
* **Additional comments** (Text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or questions.
* **Contact email** (Email, required: false): Enter your contact email address.
