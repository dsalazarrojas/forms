# Treasury Budget Review Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and insights from finance stakeholders on the treasury budget, including allocation, priorities, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all the finance stakeholders that will be participating in the treasury budget review. Choose from "Yes" or "No" depending on their involvement.
2. If you are the person responsible for budget allocation, enter a number that represents the allocated budget. This field is optional.
3. Select your top priority for funding from the provided options: "Budget", "Cost", or "Other". This field is required.
4. Provide any improvement suggestions you have for the treasury budget.
5. Enter any additional feedback you have on the treasury budget.
6. Provide details on any improvements you suggest should be implemented.
7. Enter any feedback you have from finance leaders.
8. Enter any feedback you have from treasury teams.
9. Enter feedback from both finance leaders and treasury teams (if applicable).
10. Finally, select the current planning cycle (e.g., Q1, Q2, or Q3).
11. Provide any additional details on your improvement suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **finance_stakeholders** (select_multiple, required): This field allows you to select all the finance stakeholders that will be participating in the treasury budget review. Choose "Yes" or "No" depending on their involvement.
* **budget_allocation** (number, optional): If you are the person responsible for budget allocation, enter a number that represents the allocated budget.
* **funding_priorities** (select_one, required): This field allows you to select your top priority for funding. Choose from "Budget", "Cost", or "Other".
* **improvement_suggestions** (text, optional): Provide any improvement suggestions you have for the treasury budget.
* **feedback** (text, optional): Enter any additional feedback you have on the treasury budget.
* **budget_allocation_details** (text, optional): Provide details on any improvements you suggest should be implemented.
* **finance_leaders_feedback** (text, optional): Enter any feedback you have from finance leaders.
* **treasury_teams_feedback** (text, optional): Enter any feedback you have from treasury teams.
* **finance_leaders_and_treasury_teams_feedback** (text, optional): Enter feedback from both finance leaders and treasury teams (if applicable).
* **planning_cycles** (select_one, required): Select the current planning cycle (e.g., Q1, Q2, or Q3).
* **improvement_suggestions_details** (text, optional): Provide any additional details on your improvement suggestions.
