# Audit Trail Documentation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about an event or incident that has occurred within an organization. It helps in documenting and reporting on such events, providing valuable insights for future reference and risk assessment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address.
2. Select your department from the given options.
3. Enter your job title.
4. Provide the date of the event.
5. Choose the type of event that occurred.
6. Enter the date and time of the event.
7. Describe the event in detail.
8. Specify which systems and users were involved.
9. Indicate the action taken.
10. Rate the severity level and impact assessment of the event.
11. Assess the documentation completeness and evidence attachment.
12. Determine if follow-up actions are required.
13. Check if there are related events.
14. Mention any additional notes or comments.
15. Specify who will review this documentation.
16. Finally, provide your electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Your Name** (Required)

* Enter your name as it appears on your official ID or business card.

### 2. **Email Address** (Required)

* Enter your official email address associated with your organization.

### 3. **Department** (Required)

* Select the department you belong to from the given options:
	+ Internal Audit
	+ IT
	+ Compliance
	+ Risk Management
	+ Finance
	+ Operations
	+ Other

### 4. **Role Title** (Required)

* Enter your official job title within the organization.

### 5. **Survey Date** (Required)

* Enter the date you are completing this survey.

### 6. **Event Type** (Required)

* Choose the type of event that occurred from the given options:
	+ System access
	+ Data modification
	+ Transaction processing
	+ Security incident
	+ Configuration change
	+ User activity
	+ Other

### 7. **Event Date** (Required)

* Enter the date the event occurred.

### 8. **Event Time** (Required)

* Enter the time the event occurred.

### 9. **Event Description** (Required)

* Provide a detailed description of the event.

### 10. **System Involved** (Required)

* Specify which system was involved.

### 11. **User Involved** (Required)

* Enter the user ID or name of the person involved.

### 12. **Action Taken** (Required)

* Describe the action you took in response to the event.

### 13. **Severity Level** (Required)

* Rate the severity of the event from the given options:
	+ Critical
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ Informational

### 14. **Impact Assessment** (Required)

* Assess the impact of the event from the given options:
	+ Significant impact
	+ Moderate impact
	+ Minor impact
	+ No impact
	+ Potential impact only

### 15. **Documentation Completeness** (Required)

* Assess the completeness of documentation from the given options:
	+ Fully documented
	+ Mostly documented
	+ Partially documented
	+ Minimally documented
	+ Incomplete

### 16. **Evidence Attached** (Required)

* Indicate if supporting evidence is attached from the given options:
	+ Yes, full evidence
	+ Yes, partial evidence
	+ No, evidence not available
	+ No, evidence not required

### 17. **Evidence Type** (Optional)

* Specify the type of evidence attached, if any, from the given options:
	+ Screenshots
	+ Log files
	+ Email correspondence
	+ System reports
	+ Witness statements
	+ Other

### 18. **Follow-Up Required** (Required)

* Determine if follow-up actions are required from the given options:
	+ Yes, immediate action
	+ Yes, within 24 hours
	+ Yes, within 1 week
	+ No follow-up needed

### 19. **Related Events** (Required)

* Check if there are any related events from the given options:
	+ Yes, multiple related events
	+ Yes, one related event
	+ No, isolated event

### 20. **Additional Notes** (Optional)

* Provide any additional notes or comments about the event.

### 21. **Reviewer Assigned** (Required)

* Specify who will review this documentation.

### 22. **Respondent Signature** (Required)

* Provide your electronic signature as a type of "Type your full name as electronic signature".
