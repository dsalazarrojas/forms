This form is intended to collect information and feedback from oil field workers regarding their shift conditions, safety observations, operational performance, time worked, start time, end time, and notes. The purpose of this form is to ensure that the oil field operations team has a clear understanding of the conditions of each shift, including any safety concerns, performance metrics, and notable events.

This form will be used to collect both required and optional information, depending on the nature of the data being collected. All fields are designed to be completed by the oil field workers themselves, ensuring that the information is accurate and first-hand.

Before proceeding, please ensure that you have reviewed the form instructions and understood the purpose and requirements of the form.

# Oil Field Operations Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Oil Field Operations Survey is a form designed to collect information from oil field workers about their shift conditions, safety concerns, and operational performance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each field, and answer the questions truthfully and accurately.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shift Conditions** (`shift_conditions`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any general information about your shift, such as weather conditions, equipment used, or notable events.
* **Safety Observations** (`safety_observations`, text, optional): This field is for you to report any safety concerns or observations from your shift, such as near misses, incidents, or hazards.
* **Operational Performance** (`operational_performance`, text, optional): This field is for you to report on your performance during the shift, such as tasks completed, targets met, or challenges faced.
* **Time Worked** (`time_worked`, number, optional): This field is for you to report the amount of time you worked during the shift, in hours or minutes.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): This field is for you to report the time you started your shift.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): This field is for you to report the time you ended your shift.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): This field is for you to add any additional comments or notes about your shift.
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, date, optional): This field is for you to report the date and time your shift started.
