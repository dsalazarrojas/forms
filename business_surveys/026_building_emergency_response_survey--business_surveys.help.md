# Building Emergency Response Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to assess your knowledge, awareness, and preparedness in responding to emergency situations in the building where you work. It will help us identify areas for improvement to better prepare for potential emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting.
2. Answer each question based on your genuine experiences and knowledge.
3. Use the provided options to select your response for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
1. **Your name**: Enter your name, which is required to complete the survey.
2. **Department or Floor**: Enter your department or floor where you work, which is also required.
3. **How long have you worked in this building?**: Choose the duration of your work experience in the building from the options provided.
4. **Awareness of emergency procedures**: This is a note section, please enter any thoughts or comments about your awareness of emergency procedures.
5. **Awareness of evacuation procedures**: Answer how aware you are of the evacuation procedures, with options from Very aware - Know procedures well to Not aware - No knowledge.
6. **Do you know your evacuation route?**: Answer if you can describe your evacuation route, know its general idea, or are unsure.
7. **Know location of nearest emergency exit**: Select the option that best describes your level of awareness of the emergency exit location.
8. **Know designated assembly point**: Choose your level of awareness of the assembly point, from Clearly marked to Unknown.
9. **Know building emergency contacts**: Answer how familiar you are with the emergency contacts in your building.
10. **Know location of fire extinguishers**: Select the option that describes your level of awareness of the fire extinguishers' location.
11. **Know location of first aid kit**: Answer where you can find the first aid kit, from Clear location to Unsure.
12. **Know location of AED (Automated External Defibrillator)**: Choose the option that best describes your awareness of the AED location.
13. **Understand emergency alarm sound**: Select the option that describes your level of familiarity with the emergency alarm sound.
14. **Evacuation drill experience**: This is a note section, please enter any information or comments about your evacuation drill experience.
15. **Participated in drill**: Answer if you participated in a drill recently or some time ago.
16. **Drill frequency**: Select the frequency of drills conducted in your building.
17. **Effectiveness of last drill**: Answer how effective the last drill was, from Very effective to Not effective.
18. **Estimated time to evacuate**: Select the estimated time it would take you to evacuate your area.
19. **Completed emergency response training**: Answer if you have completed emergency response training, when, and how effective it was.
20. **Training topics covered**: Select the topics covered in your emergency response training.
21. **Training effectiveness**: Answer how effective the training was for you.
22. **Confidence in responding to emergency**: Answer your level of confidence in responding to an emergency.
23. **Readiness to respond**: Select if you are ready to help others in emergency situations.
24. **Special considerations**: Enter any special considerations or needs you may have during emergency response.
25. **Accessibility concerns**: Enter any concerns you may have about accessibility during emergency response.
26. **Improvements needed**: Select the improvements that you think are necessary for emergency readiness.
27. **Additional feedback**: Enter any additional feedback or suggestions you may have about improving emergency response in your building.
