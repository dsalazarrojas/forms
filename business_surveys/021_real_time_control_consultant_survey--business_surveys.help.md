# real_time_control_consultant_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from real-time control consultants, allowing the organization to better understand their needs and improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out this form with as much detail as you can about your current activities and goals.
- Provide clear and concise answers for each question.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **what_do_you_do** (text, optional): Briefly describe your current activities as a real-time control consultant.
- **what_are_your_goals** (text, optional): Share your short-term and long-term goals as a consultant.
- **what_is_your_name** (text, optional): Provide your name or identifier.
- **how_do_you_plan** (text, optional): Explain how you plan to achieve your goals.
- **what_are_your_expectations** (text, optional): Share your expectations from our services.
- **what_would_you_like_me_to_know** (text, optional): Suggest any additional information we should know about you.
- **do_you_have_any_other_comments** (text, optional): Share any other comments or feedback you have.
- **what_do_you_expect_from_us** (text, optional): Describe what you expect from us in terms of improvement.
- **what_can_we_improve_on** (text, optional): Suggest areas where we can improve our services.
- **do_you_have_a_suggested_improvement** (text, optional): Offer a specific suggestion for improvement.
- **do_you_have_any_other_comments_or_comments** (text, optional): Share any other comments or feedback you have.
- **do_you_have_comments_on_the_services_we_provide** (text, optional): Provide comments on the services we provide.
- **what_do_you_expect_from_us_in_the_future** (text, optional): Describe what you expect from us in the future.

## Tips
- Please answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
- If you need help or clarification on any question, contact us.
- Your input is invaluable in helping us improve our services.
- Thank you for taking the time to fill out this form and providing your feedback.
