# Livestock Worker Health Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to collect information from livestock workers about their health and safety conditions. This will help us better understand and address the health and safety issues faced by livestock workers.
## How To Complete This Form
- Please take a few minutes to read through each section carefully.
- Answer the questions honestly and accurately.
- Ensure that the information you input is relevant to your situation.
- You can fill out this form only once.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief introduction to the survey and its purpose.
* **Health Issues**: Please describe any health issues you have faced while working with livestock. This can include injuries, illnesses, or any other health-related problems.
* **Safety Practices**: Please select all the safety practices that you follow while working with livestock. These practices are designed to keep you safe while working with animals.
* **Access to Care**: Please select the option that best describes your access to care when you are injured or ill while working with livestock. This will help us understand how you receive medical attention when you need it.
* **Farm Characteristics (1)**: Please select the option that best describes your farm's size and type.
* **Farm Characteristics 2**: Please describe any other relevant characteristics of your farm, such as its location or climate.
* **Farm Characteristics 3**: Please select the option that best describes your farm's level of use of technology (e.g., machinery, etc.).
* **Contact Information**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you with any further questions or follow-up information.

## Tips
- Answer the questions honestly and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification.
- If you need help with anything, please contact us.
- This survey is completely anonymous, and your responses will be kept confidential.
