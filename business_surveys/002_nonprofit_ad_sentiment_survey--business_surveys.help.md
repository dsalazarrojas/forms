# Nonprofit Ad Sentiment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the nonprofit ad's effectiveness and public perception. Your input will help us understand how well the ad conveys the message and how engaging it is.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Fill out each field carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
- Rate each field's effectiveness based on your honest opinion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What do you think of this ad?** (`non_profit_ad_sentiment_survey_form`, text, optional): Please provide a short description of your overall opinion of the ad.
* **How would you rate the ad's effectiveness?** (`ad_effectiveness`, number, optional): Rate the ad's effectiveness on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being not effective at all and 10 being very effective.
* **How does the ad make you feel?** (`emotional_impact`, select_one, optional): Please select how the ad makes you feel, such as positive, negative, or neither.
* **How likely is it that this ad will motivate you to donate to this nonprofit?** (`donation_intent`, number, optional): Rate how likely you are to donate to the nonprofit based on the ad's message.
* **What do you think of the ad's design?** (`ad_design`, text, optional): Please provide a short description of your opinion on the ad's design.
* **What message does the ad convey?** (`ad_message`, text, optional): Please describe the message the ad conveys.
* **How engaging are the visuals in the ad?** (`ad_visuals`, text, optional): Rate the visuals in the ad, providing details on their effectiveness.
* **What is the name of the nonprofit featured in this ad?** (`non_profit_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the nonprofit featured in the ad.
* **What is the contact information for the nonprofit featured in this ad?** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide the contact information for the nonprofit.
* **What is the website of the nonprofit featured in this ad?** (`non_profit_website`, text, optional): Provide the website of the nonprofit.
* **What is the social media handle of the nonprofit featured in this ad?** (`non_profit_social_media`, text, optional): Provide the social media handle of the nonprofit.
* **Is the nonprofit on Facebook?** (`non_profit_facebook`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if the nonprofit has a presence on Facebook.
* **Is the nonprofit on Twitter?** (`non_profit_twitter`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if the nonprofit has a presence on Twitter.
* **Is the nonprofit on Instagram?** (`non_profit_instagram`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if the nonprofit has a presence on Instagram.
* **Is the nonprofit on LinkedIn?** (`non_profit_linkedin`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if the nonprofit has a presence on LinkedIn.
* **Is the nonprofit on YouTube?** (`non_profit_youtube`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if the nonprofit has a presence on YouTube.
* **Is the nonprofit on Pinterest?** (`non_profit_pinterest`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if the nonprofit has a presence on Pinterest.
* **Is the nonprofit on Tumblr?** (`non_profit_tumblr`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if the nonprofit has a presence on Tumblr.
* **Is the nonprofit on Flickr?** (`non_profit_flickr`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if the nonprofit has a presence on Flickr.
* **Is the nonprofit on Etsy?** (`non_profit_etsy`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if the nonprofit has a presence on Etsy.
* **Is the nonprofit on Wordpress?** (`non_profit_wordpress`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if the nonprofit has a presence on Wordpress.

* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in each field.
