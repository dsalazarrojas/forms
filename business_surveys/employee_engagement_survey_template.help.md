# employee_engagement_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from employees on their experience and satisfaction with their role in the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select all options that apply to questions with multiple choices.
3. Type your thoughts and comments in the text fields.
4. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your role using the options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
* **Management Communication**: Rate how satisfied you are with the communication from management using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Development Support**: Indicate whether you feel supported in your professional development with the options: true, false, Somewhat.
* **Engagement Factors**: Select all factors that positively contribute to your engagement, choosing from: Meaningful work, Team collaboration, Flexible hours, Recognition, Opportunities for growth, Work-life balance.
* **Work-Life Balance**: Rate your current work-life balance using the options: Excellent, Good, Acceptable, Needs improvement, Poor.
* **Additional Comments**: Share any additional comments or suggestions you have to improve your overall experience at work.
* **Additional Suggestions**: Type any additional comments or suggestions you have to improve your experience at work.
