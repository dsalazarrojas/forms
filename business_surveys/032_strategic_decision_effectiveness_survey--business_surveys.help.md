# Strategic Decision Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strategic Decision Effectiveness Survey is a tool used to evaluate the effectiveness of strategic decisions made within a business context. It collects information on various aspects of decision-making processes, such as the level of difficulty in making a decision, the perceived effectiveness of the decision, and the communication and execution of the decision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select all the options that apply to your experience with strategic decisions. You will be presented with a series of questions, each with multiple-choice options. Please answer as accurately and honestly as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Surveys** (id: 1, select_multiple, optional): Select all the business surveys that you have participated in or have experience with.
* **Execution** (id: 2, number, optional): Please rate the level of difficulty in executing the decision (e.g., 1-5).
* **Perception** (id: 3, text, optional): Describe your understanding or perception of the decision-making process (e.g., 1-10).
* **Communication** (id: 4, select_one, optional): Choose the method of communication you used to execute the decision (e.g., email, phone, in-person, other).
* **Time** (id: 5, time, optional): Enter the date and time you made the decision.
* **Date** (id: 6, date, optional): Enter the date the decision was executed.
* **Description** (id: 7, note, optional): Provide a brief description of the decision (e.g., 1-2 sentences).
* **Assigned Tool** (id: 8, text, optional): Describe the tool or resource used to make the decision.
* **Category** (id: 9, select_multiple, optional): Select the category or type of decision (e.g., Business Surveys, Evaluation Surveys).
* **Title** (id: 10, text, optional): Enter a title for the decision (e.g., "Decision to Launch New Product").

## Tips
* Please be as accurate and honest as possible in your answers.
* If you are unsure about an option, choose the one that best represents your experience.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the description field.
