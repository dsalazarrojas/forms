# Expense Category Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to expense categories, which will be used for managing and tracking expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the first name and last name fields with your full names.
2. Enter your company name in the Company field.
3. Enter your job title in the Job Title field.
4. Select the relevant category for your expense from the Category field.
5. Choose a date for your expense in the Date field.
6. Optionally, select a time for your expense in the Time field.
7. If necessary, provide additional comments or explanations in the Note field.
8. Enter your email address in the Email field.
9. Fill in your phone number in the Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company you work for or the organization you represent.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position within your company or organization.
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes your expense from the options provided.
* **Date**: Choose the date when the expense occurred.
* **Time**: Select the time when the expense occurred (if applicable).
* **Note**: Provide any additional comments or explanations about the expense.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if necessary.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate data collection.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your official identification documents for guidance.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
