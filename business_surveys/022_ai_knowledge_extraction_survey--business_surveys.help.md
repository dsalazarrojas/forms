# AI Knowledge Extraction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form aims to gather your information and experiences regarding AI-powered knowledge extraction within your organization. Your input will help us better understand the current state of knowledge landscapes, challenges, and interests in this technology.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, simply follow the steps:

1. Provide your full name and job role.
2. Select your department.
3. Identify your current primary knowledge sources.
4. Describe your biggest challenges in finding information.
5. Indicate your average hours spent searching for information per week.
6. Express your interest in an AI-powered 'Knowledge Extraction' tool.
7. Select the desired features of such a tool.
8. Specify the sensitivity of the data you handle.
9. Identify your primary concerns regarding AI knowledge extraction.
10. Describe a specific scenario where AI extraction would be helpful.
11. Indicate your interest in participating in a pilot program.
12. Enter your email for follow-up (optional).
13. Provide the date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your full name as it should be spelled and written.
* **Job Role / Title**: A description of your current job role within the organization.
* **Department**: Select the department you belong to in order for us to better understand your context.
* **Primary Knowledge Sources**: Select all the knowledge sources that are relevant to your team.
* **Biggest Challenges**: Identify the biggest challenges you face when searching for information.
* **Average Hours Spent Searching**: Indicate how many hours you spend looking for information on average per week.
* **AI Tool Interest**: Indicate your level of interest in an AI-powered 'Knowledge Extraction' tool.
* **Desired Features**: Select the features you desire the most in a 'Knowledge Extraction' tool.
* **Data Sensitivity**: Indicate the sensitivity level of the data you handle.
* **Primary Concerns**: Identify your biggest concerns regarding AI knowledge extraction.
* **Use Case**: Describe a scenario where AI extraction would be helpful.
* **Pilot Participation**: Indicate your interest in participating in a pilot program for this technology.
* **Email**: Provide your email for follow-up (optional).
* **Date**: Indicate the date for this response.
