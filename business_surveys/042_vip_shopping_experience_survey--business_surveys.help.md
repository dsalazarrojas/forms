# VIP Shopping Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from valued partners (VIPs) to improve their shopping experience at our store.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your role in the organization from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose your organization from the list.
3. Provide your email address and phone number.
4. Rate your experience with the store staff, products, and facilities.
5. Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
6. (Optional) Choose how often you visit the store and how many times you've visited.
7. (Optional) Select your last purchase and its date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your role in our organization?**: Choose the option that best describes your position within the organization.
	+ Customer: You are a customer of our store.
	+ Employee: You are an employee of our organization.
	+ VIP Guest: You are a special guest of our organization.
* **What is your organization?**: Choose all the organizations you are associated with.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Store Experience**: Rate your experience with the store staff, products, and facilities from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very Poor" and 5 is "Very Good".
* **Employee Knowledge**: Rate the store staff's knowledge from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very Poor" and 5 is "Very Good".
* **Product Knowledge**: Rate your knowledge about our products from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very Poor" and 5 is "Very Good".
* **Staff Attitude**: Rate the store staff's attitude from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very Poor" and 5 is "Very Good".
* **Service Quality**: Rate the quality of our services from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very Poor" and 5 is "Very Good".
* **Store Facilities**: Rate the facilities and amenities at our store from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very Poor" and 5 is "Very Good".
* **Store Facilities Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the store facilities.
* **Welcoming Staff**: Rate the staff's welcoming behavior from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very Poor" and 5 is "Very Good".
* **Staff Professionalism**: Rate the staff's professionalism from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very Poor" and 5 is "Very Good".
* **Staff Communication**: Rate the staff's communication from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Very Poor" and 5 is "Very Good".
* **Would you recommend us?**: Choose "Yes" if you would recommend our store to others.
* **Any suggestions for improvements?**: Provide any comments or suggestions for improvements.
* **How often do you visit our store?**: Choose how often you visit our store.
* **How many times have you visited our store?**: Enter the number of times you've visited our store.
* **Last visit date**: Enter the date of your last visit.
* **Last visit time**: Enter the time of your last visit.
* **Store visit frequency**: Choose how often you visit our store.
* **Last purchase**: Choose the type of your last purchase (online or in-store).
* **Last purchase date**: Enter the date of your last purchase.
* **Purchase frequency**: Choose how often you make purchases from us.
* **Last purchase amount**: Enter the amount of your last purchase.

## Tips
* Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
* Use the "Any suggestions for improvements?" field for any comments or suggestions.
* Please keep the form confidential.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact our customer support team.
