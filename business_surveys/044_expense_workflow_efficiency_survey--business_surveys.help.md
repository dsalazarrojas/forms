# Expense Workflow Efficiency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Workflow Efficiency Survey is a tool used to gather feedback on the efficiency of your expense processing workflow. This survey aims to identify areas of improvement and provide insights to enhance the overall experience of submitting and processing expenses within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the option that best represents your role at the company from the "What is your role at the company?" dropdown menu.
2. Choose how big your company is from the "How big is your company?" dropdown menu.
3. Select the option that best describes how you currently handle expenses from the "How do you currently handle expenses?" dropdown menu.
4. If prompted, rate the efficiency and satisfaction of your expense process on a scale of 1-5.
5. Provide any suggestions or feedback on how to improve the expense process.
6. If you encounter any issues with submitting or processing expenses, select all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role at the company?** (`expense_workflow_efficiency_survey`, select_one, required): This question will help us understand your perspective on the expense workflow from your position within the company.
* **How big is your company?** (`company_size`, select_multiple, required): This question will help us understand the dynamics of different company sizes and their impact on expense workflows.
* **How do you currently handle expenses?** (`expense_process`, select_one, required): This question will help us understand your current experience with the expense process.
* **Do you think your expense process has improved since the new system was implemented?** (`expense_process_2`, number, required): This question will help us gauge your subjective experience with the new system.
* **What time of month do you submit your expenses?** (`expense_process_3`, select_one, required): This question will help us understand the timing and cadence of expense submissions.
* **How many days are you able to process an expense report?** (`expense_process_4`, number, required): This question will help us understand the time taken to process expenses.
* **How much time do you spend on expense processing per day?** (`expense_process_5`, select_multiple, required): This question will help us understand the time commitment to expense processing.
* **On a scale of 1-5, how would you rate the overall efficiency of your expense process?** (`expense_process_6`, note, required): This question will help us understand your subjective experience with the expense process.
* **On a scale of 1-5, how would you rate the overall satisfaction of your expense process?** (`expense_process_7`, note, required): This question will help us understand your subjective experience with the expense process.
* **Do you have any suggestions on how to improve the expense process?** (`expense_process_8`, text, required): This question will help us gather actionable feedback for improvement.
* **Do you have any feedback on the current expense process?** (`expense_process_9`, note, required): This question will help us understand your experience with the current process.
* **Are there any issues with submitting or processing expenses?** (`expense_process_10`, select_multiple, required): This question will help us identify pain points and areas for improvement.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately to provide the most valuable insights for improvement.
* If you need help or have questions, please reach out to the support team.
* Thank you for taking the time to participate in this survey! Your input is highly valued.
