# Sales Strategy Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and insights from sales teams about their current sales strategies, which will help management identify areas of strength and weakness and inform future decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer each question carefully and honestly to provide valuable insights.
* For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose the most relevant option(s) from the list.
* For text, number, and date fields, enter relevant information as prompted.
* For email and phone fields, enter your contact information for follow-up purposes.
* Take as much time as needed to complete the form, but it should take approximately 10-15 minutes to complete.
* Submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **what_is_the_currently_used_sales_technology**: This question asks about the currently used sales technology that is most important to your sales process. Please select the technology that best describes your current process.
* **what_is_the_currently_used_sales_channel**: This question asks about the currently used sales channel that is most important to your sales process. Please enter the channel that best describes your current process.
* **how_many_sales_channels_do_you_use**: This question asks about the number of sales channels you use. Please enter the total number of channels you currently use.
* **budget_allocated_for_sales**: This question asks about the budget allocated for sales activities. Please enter the total budget allocated for sales.
* **average_time_spent_on_sales**: This question asks about the average time spent on sales activities. Please enter the average time spent per sales representative.
* **which_of_the_following_are_used_in_your_sales_process**: This question asks about the sales activities that are currently used in your process. Please select all the activities that are relevant to your sales process.
* **which_sales_technology_is_most_important**: This question asks about the most important sales technology used in your process. Please select the technology that best describes your current process.
* **email**: This question asks for your email address for follow-up purposes. Please enter your email address.
* **phone**: This question asks for your phone number for follow-up purposes. Please enter your phone number.
* **start_date**: This question asks for the start date of your sales process. Please enter the start date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **end_time**: This question asks for the end time of your sales process. Please enter the end time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **note**: This question provides space for any additional comments or feedback you may have about your sales process. Please enter any relevant information here.

Remember, your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve our sales strategies. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
