# Product Market Fit Confirmation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand and confirm the product market fit of your business. It will guide you through a series of questions that will help us assess how well your product or service aligns with its target audience's needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Fill in your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Fill in your company's name in the "Company" field (if you have one).
6.  Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field (if applicable).
7.  Select your department or team in the "Department" field (if applicable).
8.  Describe your job description in the "Job Description" field (if applicable).
9.  Enter your expected annual revenue in the "Expected Annual Revenue" field (if applicable).
10.  Enter your expected growth rate in the "Expected Growth Rate" field (if applicable).
11.  Describe your expected user base in the "Expected User Base" field (if applicable).
12.  Describe your expected customer base in the "Expected Customer Base" field (if applicable).
13.  Select your expected revenue streams from the options provided in the "Expected Revenue Streams" field.
14.  Choose your target audience from the options provided in the "Target Audience" field.
15.  Describe your competitors in the "Competitors" field (if applicable).
16.  List your marketing channels in the "Marketing Channels" field (if applicable).
17.  Select your pricing model from the options provided in the "Pricing Model" field.
18.  Select your revenue model from the options provided in the "Revenue Model" field.
19.  Describe your cost structure in the "Cost Structure" field (if applicable).
20.  Describe your operating costs in the "Operating Costs" field (if applicable).
21.  Enter your selling price in the "Selling Price" field (if applicable).
22.  Describe your profit margin in the "Profit Margin" field (if applicable).
23.  Select your revenue streams from the options provided in the "Revenue Streams" field.
24.  Describe your customer base in the "Customer Base" field (if applicable).
25.  Select your business goals from the options provided in the "Business Goals" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for further questions or clarifications.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further questions or clarifications.
* **Company**: If you have a company, enter its name. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Job Title**: If you have a job title, enter it in this field. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Department**: If you work in a department or team, enter its name in this field. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Job Description**: Describe your job responsibilities in this field, if applicable.
* **Expected Annual Revenue**: Enter your expected annual revenue, if applicable.
* **Expected Growth Rate**: Enter your expected growth rate, if applicable.
* **Expected User Base**: Describe your expected user base, if applicable.
* **Expected Customer Base**: Describe your expected customer base, if applicable.
* **Expected Revenue Streams**: Select the revenue streams that apply to your business, such as Sales, Advertising, Subscriptions, etc.
* **Target Audience**: Choose the target audience that aligns with your product or service.
* **Competitors**: Describe your competitors in this field, if applicable.
* **Marketing Channels**: List the marketing channels you use or plan to use for your business, if applicable.
* **Pricing Model**: Select the pricing model that applies to your business, such as Subscription-based, One-time Payment, Free, etc.
* **Revenue Model**: Select the revenue model that applies to your business, such as Subscription, Sales, Advertising, etc.
* **Cost Structure**: Describe your cost structure, if applicable.
* **Operating Costs**: Describe your operating costs, if applicable.
* **Selling Price**: Enter your selling price, if applicable.
* **Profit Margin**: Enter your profit margin, if applicable.
* **Revenue Streams**: Select the revenue streams that apply to your business, such as Sales, Advertising, Subscriptions, etc.
* **Customer Base**: Describe your customer base, if applicable.
* **Business Goals**: Select the business goals that align with your product or service, such as Increasing Revenue, Reducing Costs, etc.

**Tips**

* Please be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any question, do not hesitate to contact us for clarification.
* This form is a confidential document and is only meant for internal use.
* By completing this form, you are confirming that you have the authority to represent your company and make commitments on its behalf.
