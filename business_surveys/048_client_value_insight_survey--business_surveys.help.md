# Client Value Insight Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form aims to measure the effectiveness of our services in delivering value to your business. By answering these questions, you will help us understand how we've impacted your operations and what we can improve to deliver more value in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please review the questions carefully and answer them based on your personal experience working with us.
2. For questions that have a dropdown menu, select the option that best describes your answer.
3. For text fields, provide specific details and examples to support your answers.
4. If you have any additional feedback or comments, please use the "Additional Feedback" field.
5. Once you've completed the form, click "Submit" to send it to us.

* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Engagement Duration with Us** (Engagement Duration with Us, select_one, required): Choose the duration of our engagement:
	+ Less than 3 months

* **Perceived Value of Our Services** (Perceived Value of Our Services, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your perception of our services:

	+ No value
