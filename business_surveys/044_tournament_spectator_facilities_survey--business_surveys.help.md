# Tournament Spectator Facilities Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather information from event organizers and attendees about the spectator facilities of a tournament event. Your honest answers will help us understand the spectator facilities and improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions based on your experience with the facilities. If you don't have experience with the facility, please don't complete the survey.
2. Select the options that best fit your experience with the spectator facilities.
3. Fill in the required fields, such as Name, Email, Phone and event details.
4. Submit your survey once completed.
