# Project Success Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Success Feedback Survey Form is designed to gather feedback on the success of a project. It should only be filled out by authorized personnel who have access to the project's information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the project information, including the title, description, and current status.
2. Select the current status of the project (Active or Inactive).
3. Enter your comments on the project.
4. Select the responsible person for the improvement actions.
5. Select the status of the improvement actions (Active or Inactive).
6. Select the due date of the improvement actions.
7. Enter your comments on the improvement actions.
8. Select the important fields for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Title** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_title`): Enter the title of the project.
* **Project Description** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_description`): Enter a brief description of the project.
* **Project Success Score** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_success_score`): Rate the project's success score (optional).
* **Project Status** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_status`): Select the current status of the project (Active or Inactive).
* **Success Score Important** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_success_score_important`): Indicates whether the success score is important or not.
* **Success Score Justification** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_success_score_justification`): Enter your justification for the success score.
* **Success Score Justification Important** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_success_score_justification_important`): Indicates whether the justification is important or not.
* **Project Comments** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_comments`): Enter any comments on the project.
* **Success Score Suggestions** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_success_score_suggestions`): Enter any suggestions for improvement.
* **Success Score Improvement Actions** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_success_score_improvement_actions`): Select whether there are improvement actions or not.
* **Success Score Improvement Actions Important** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_success_score_improvement_actions_important`): Indicates whether the improvement actions are important or not.
* **Success Score Improvement Actions Status** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_success_score_improvement_actions_status`): Select the status of the improvement actions (Active or Inactive).
* **Success Score Improvement Actions Due Date** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_success_score_improvement_actions_due_date`): Select the due date of the improvement actions.
* **Success Score Improvement Actions Comments** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_success_score_improvement_actions_comments`): Enter any comments on the improvement actions.
* **Success Score Improvement Actions Comments Important** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_success_score_improvement_actions_comment_important`): Indicates whether the comments are important or not.
* **Success Score Improvement Actions Responsible Person** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_success_score_improvement_actions_responsible_person`): Select whether the responsible person is assigned or not.
* **Success Score Improvement Actions Responsible Person Important** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_success_score_improvement_actions_responsible_person_important`): Indicates whether the responsible person is important or not.
* **Success Score Improvement Actions Responsible Person Title** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_success_score_improvement_actions_responsible_person_title`): Enter the title of the responsible person.
* **Success Score Improvement Actions Responsible Person Status** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_success_score_improvement_actions_responsible_person_status`): Select the status of the responsible person (Active or Inactive).
* **Success Score Improvement Actions Responsible Person Due Date** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_success_score_improvement_actions_responsible_person_due_date`): Select the due date of the responsible person.
* **Success Score Improvement Actions Responsible Person Due Date Important** (`project_success_feedback_survey_form_business_surveys_project_success_score_improvement_actions_responsible_person_due_date_important`): Indicates whether the due date is important or not.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately.
* If you need more information or clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask.
* This form should only be filled out by authorized personnel who have access to the project's information.
* If you are unsure about the project or your role in providing feedback, please contact the project manager or team lead for clarification.
