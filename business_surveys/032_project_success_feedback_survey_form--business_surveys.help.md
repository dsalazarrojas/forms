# Project Success Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Success Feedback Survey Form is designed to gather feedback on the success of a project. It should only be filled out by authorized personnel who have access to the project's information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the project information, including the title, description, and current status.
2. Select the current status of the project (Active or Inactive).
3. Enter your comments on the project.
4. Select the responsible person for the improvement actions.
5. Select the status of the improvement actions (Active or Inactive).
6. Select the due date of the improvement actions.
7. Enter your comments on the improvement actions.
8. Select the important fields for each section.
