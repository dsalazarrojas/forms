# monthly_marketing_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on a company's marketing and sales performance for a specific month. It helps marketing managers track and analyze their team's progress, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions to optimize their marketing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Marketing Manager` field with the name of the person responsible for the marketing efforts.
2. Enter the `Report Date` field with the date of the month for which you are reporting.
3. Enter the actual `Budget` spent on marketing efforts for the month.
4. Enter the `Revenue` generated for the month.
5. Describe the `Customer Satisfaction` experience in a few words or sentences.
6. Select the marketing `Marketing Channels` used for the month (e.g., Social Media, Email Marketing, Print Media, Event Marketing).
7. Enter a brief `Marketing Message` that summarizes the main marketing efforts for the month.
8. Select the `Sales Channels` used for the month (e.g., Online, Offline, Both).
9. Enter the actual `Sales Revenue` for the month.
10. Describe the `Customer Service` experience in a few words or sentences.
11. Enter the actual `Marketing Cost` spent on marketing efforts for the month.
12. Describe the `Customer Acquisition` experience in a few words or sentences.
13. Enter the `Customer Satisfaction Rating` on a scale of 1-10.
14. Select the awareness level of `Sales Channels Awareness` for the month (e.g., High, Low).
15. Describe the `Customer Service Quality` experience in a few words or sentences.
16. Describe the `Customer Fulfillment` experience in a few words or sentences.
17. Describe the `Marketing Fulfillment` experience in a few words or sentences.
18. Enter a comment from the `Marketing Manager` on any notable events or insights from the month.
19. Describe the awareness level of `Customer Satisfaction Rating Awareness` for the month.
20. Describe the awareness level of `Sales Revenue Awareness` for the month.
21. Describe the awareness level of `Customer Fulfillment Awareness` for the month.
22. Describe the awareness level of `Sales Channels Awareness` for the month.
23. Describe the awareness level of `Customer Service Awareness` for the month.
24. Enter the `Customer Satisfaction Score` out of 10.
25. Enter the total number of `Total Customers` acquired or reached during the month.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Marketing Manager**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the marketing efforts for the month. This field is required.
* **Report Date**: Enter the date of the month for which you are reporting. This field is required.
* **Budget**: Enter the actual budget spent on marketing efforts for the month.
* **Revenue**: Enter the actual revenue generated for the month.
* **Customer Satisfaction**: Describe the customer satisfaction experience in a few words or sentences.
* **Marketing Channels**: Select the marketing channels used for the month (e.g., Social Media, Email Marketing, Print Media, Event Marketing). 
* **Marketing Message**: Enter a brief summary of the main marketing efforts for the month.
* **Sales Channels**: Select the sales channels used for the month (e.g., Online, Offline, Both).
* **Sales Revenue**: Enter the actual sales revenue for the month.
* **Customer Service**: Describe the customer service experience in a few words or sentences.
* **Marketing Cost**: Enter the actual marketing cost spent on marketing efforts for the month.
* **Customer Acquisition**: Describe the customer acquisition experience in a few words or sentences.
* **Customer Satisfaction Rating**: Enter the customer satisfaction rating on a scale of 1-10.
* **Sales Channels Awareness**: Select the awareness level of sales channels used for the month (e.g., High, Low).
* **Customer Service Quality**: Describe the customer service quality experience in a few words or sentences.
* **Customer Fulfillment**: Describe the customer fulfillment experience in a few words or sentences.
* **Marketing Fulfillment**: Describe the marketing fulfillment experience in a few words or sentences.
* **Marketing Manager Comment**: Enter a comment from the marketing manager on any notable events or insights from the month.
* **Customer Satisfaction Rating Awareness**: Describe the awareness level of customer satisfaction rating for the month.
* **Sales Revenue Awareness**: Describe the awareness level of sales revenue for the month.
* **Customer Fulfillment Awareness**: Describe the awareness level of customer fulfillment for the month.
* **Sales Channels Awareness**: Describe the awareness level of sales channels awareness for the month.
* **Customer Service Awareness**: Describe the awareness level of customer service awareness for the month.
* **Customer Satisfaction Score**: Enter the customer satisfaction score out of 10.
* **Total Customers**: Enter the total number of customers acquired or reached during the month.

Note: All fields are optional unless explicitly stated as required.
