# Event Fee Structure Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Fee Structure Survey is a form used to collect information about events and their associated fees. This form is used to gather details from event organizers and submitters, ensuring that all necessary information is accurately captured and utilized for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Event Organiser" field with the name of the person responsible for the event.
2. Enter the "Event Organiser Email" field with the event organizer's email address.
3. Select the correct "Event Date" and "Event Time" for the event.
4. Provide the "Event Location" where the event will take place.
5. Determine the "Event Fee Structure" by selecting one of the provided options.
6. Select one or multiple "Event Fees" that apply to the event.
7. If necessary, provide any "Event Organiser Note" relevant to the event.
8. Enter the "Submitter" name, which will be the person submitting the form.
9. Enter the "Submitter Email" address for the person submitting the form.
10. Indicate the "Submitter Department" the person belongs to.
11. Enter the "Submitter Title" for the person submitting the form.
12. Enter the "Submitter Organization" where the person works.
13. If necessary, provide any "Submitter Note" relevant to the submission.
14. Enter the "Submitter Phone" number for the person submitting.
15. Enter the "Submitter Location" where the person is located.
16. Provide any "Submitter Comments" or feedback related to the form.
17. If not provided in the previous fields, select the "Event Type" relevant to the event from the options provided.
18. If not provided in the previous fields, enter the "Event Type Other" if it doesn't fit into the previous options.
19. Enter the "Event Venue" where the event will take place.
20. If necessary, provide any "Event Fee Structure Other" details not captured by the previous fields.
21. Read and agree to the "Submitter Agreement" terms.
