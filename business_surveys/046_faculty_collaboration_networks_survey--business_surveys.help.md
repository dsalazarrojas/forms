# Faculty Collaboration Networks Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help faculty members establish and expand their collaboration networks within the faculty community. It collects information about their department affiliation, collaboration goals, expected duration of collaboration, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  In the first page, select your department of affiliation (e.g., Department of Computer Science).
2.  Select all departments with which you would like to collaborate (e.g., Department of Engineering and Department of Math and Computer Science).
3.  Provide the expected duration of the collaboration (e.g., 2 weeks, 1 month).
4.  Add any additional comments or notes you want to share about your collaboration goals or plans.
5.  On the second page, provide your contact email address.
6.  Enter your contact phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **What is this form about?** (`1`, `text`, required): This is the title of your collaboration project. Please provide a brief description of your project and its goals.
  - **Which department are you from?** (`2`, `select_one`, required): Select your department of affiliation from the options provided.
  - **Which departments are you collaborating with?** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all departments with which you would like to collaborate.
  - **What is the expected duration of the collaboration?** (`4`, `time`, required): Provide the expected duration of your collaboration in a format that makes sense (e.g., 2 weeks, 1 month).
  - **Additional comments?** (`5`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes you want to share about your collaboration goals or plans.
  - **Faculty Collaboration Networks Survey** (`6`, `text`, required): This field is for the title of the second page of your collaboration project. Please provide a brief description of your project and its goals.
  - **Your contact email** (`7`, `email`, required): Provide your work email address to facilitate communication with other collaborators.
  - **Your contact phone number** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter your work phone number for further communication.
