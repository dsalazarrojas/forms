# Outdoor Activity Safety Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your outdoor activity safety habits and awareness. Please answer all questions truthfully to help us better understand your outdoor safety practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Fill in all required fields (marked with *) as they are necessary for us to understand your safety habits.
3. For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. For the first prophet and last prophet questions, choose the correct option from the list.
5. If you have any additional comments or notes about your outdoor activities, use the note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Introduction** 
  * (Text field, Required, id: 1)
This field is a free-text area where you can type a brief introduction or statement about your outdoor activities. This will help us understand the context of your responses.

### 2. **activity_frequency** 
  * (Number field, Required, id: 2)
This field asks about how often you engage in outdoor activities. Please select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 means less than once a month and 5 means every day.

### 3. **safety_equipment_used** 
  * (Select multiple field, Required, id: 3)
Check all the safety equipment you frequently use or have used during your outdoor activities. 

### 4. **emergency_training** 
  * (Select multiple field, Required, id: 4)
Check all the emergency training you have received or undergone. 

### 5. **outdoor_habits** 
  * (Number field, Required, id: 5)
This field asks about your outdoor habits, similar to the frequency question above.

### 6. **first_prophets** 
  * (Select one field, Required, id: 6)
Choose the first option you would use in case of an emergency. Please choose the correct option from the list.

### 7. **last_prophet** 
  * (Select multiple field, Required, id: 7)
Choose all the options you would use in case of an emergency. 

### 8. **note** 
  * (Note field, Optional, id: 8)
If you have any additional comments or notes about your outdoor activities, use this field.

### 9. **email** 
  * (Email field, Required, id: 9)
Please provide your email address. This will help us contact you in case of an emergency.

### 10. **phone** 
  * (Text field, Required, id: 10)
Please provide your phone number. This will help us contact you in case of an emergency.

### 11. **submit** 
  * (Text field, Not Required, id: 11)
Click this button to submit your form and complete the survey.
