# Beauty Brand Trust Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights on your perception of beauty brands, specifically on the factors that influence your trust in these brands.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form with your information and responses.
*   Answer all questions truthfully, as your input will help businesses better understand your views on beauty brands.
*   Select all options that apply to your preferences and experiences.
*   Provide additional feedback if you have any comments or concerns that are not covered in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Your Name (`respondent_name`, text, Optional):** Enter your name for follow-up purposes.
*   **Email Address (`email_address`, email, For follow-up):** This is for follow-up purposes, but you can also use a generic email address if you prefer.
*   **Age Group (`age_group`, select_one, True):** Select your age group, and it will help us understand how trust perceptions differ across age groups.
*   **Gender (`gender`, select_one, True):** Choose your gender, and it will help us understand the impact of gender on trust perceptions.
*   **Beauty Brands You Currently Use (`beauty_brand_used`, text, True):** List the beauty brands you currently use, and it will help us understand your preferences.
*   **How Often Do You Switch Brands (`brand_switching`, select_one, True):** Select how often you switch brands, and it will help us understand your loyalty patterns.
*   **What Makes You Trust a Beauty Brand (`trust_factors`, select_multiple, True):** Select all the factors that make you trust a brand, and it will help us understand the importance of product quality, transparency, and other attributes.
*   **How Important Is Product Quality (`quality_importance`, select_one, True):** Select how important product quality is to you, and it will help us understand the priority of product quality in your decision-making process.
*   **Does Higher Price Mean More Trust (`price_vs_trust`, select_one, True):** Select if you think a higher price means more trust, and it will help us understand the relationship between price and trust.
*   **How Important Is Ingredient Transparency (`ingredient_transparency`, select_one, True):** Select how important ingredient transparency is to you, and it will help us understand the priority of transparency in your decision-making process.
*   **Does Social Responsibility Affect Trust (`social_responsibility`, select_one, True):** Select if social responsibility affects your trust in a beauty brand, and it will help us understand the impact of CSR on trust.
*   **How Important Is Cruelty-Free Status (`cruelty_free_importance`, select_one, True):** Select how important cruelty-free status is to you, and it will help us understand the priority of cruelty-free status in your decision-making process.
*   **Do You Trust Influencer Recommendations (`influencer_recommendations`, select_one, True):** Select if you trust influencer recommendations, and it will help us understand the influence of influencers on trust.
*   **Do You Trust Online Reviews (`online_reviews`, select_one, True):** Select if you trust online reviews, and it will help us understand the role of online reviews in shaping trust.
*   **What Makes You Loyal to a Brand (`brand_loyalty_reason`, text, True):** Enter the factors that make you loyal to a beauty brand, and it will help us understand the drivers of loyalty.
*   **What Would Make You Lose Trust (`trust_breakers`, select_multiple, True):** Select all the factors that would make you lose trust in a beauty brand, and it will help us understand the trust breakers.
*   **Is Your Trust in Beauty Brands Increasing or Decreasing (`overall_trust_trend`, select_one, True):** Select if your trust in beauty brands is increasing, staying the same, or decreasing, and it will help us understand the overall trust trend.
*   **Additional Feedback (`additional_feedback`, text, False):** Provide any additional comments or concerns that are not covered in the form, and it will help us understand your views further.

## Tips
*   Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
*   You can select all options that apply to your experiences.
*   Provide additional feedback if you have any comments or concerns that are not covered in the form.
*   Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and help us understand your views on beauty brands!
