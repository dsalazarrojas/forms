# Digital Talent Strategy Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to collect your feedback and input on the company's digital talent strategy. Your responses will help the management team understand your perspectives and opinions on how to improve the strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the Category you are most relevant to.
2. Write a brief Description of your area of expertise or what you would like to see changed about the digital talent strategy.
3. Enter your Name (optional but recommended for your contact information).
4. Provide your Email address (optional but recommended for communication).
5. Enter your Phone number (optional but recommended for follow-up contact).
6. Leave your Feedback in the text field about the digital talent strategy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (select one):
	+ A category is a way to specify which area of the business you are most relevant to, i.e., "Business Surveys" or "Feedback Surveys".
* **Description** (text):
	+ Please write a brief description about your area of expertise or what you would like to see changed about the digital talent strategy.
* **Name** (text, optional):
	+ If you have a name you would like associated with your feedback, please enter it here. This will help the management team contact you for follow-up.
* **Email** (email, optional):
	+ If you have an email address you would like to be reached at for follow-up, please enter it here.
* **Phone** (text, optional):
	+ If you have a phone number you would like to be reached at for follow-up, please enter it here.
* **Feedback** (text):
	+ This is a free text field where you can leave any feedback you have about the digital talent strategy. 

## Tips
- Please be as specific and concrete as possible when providing feedback.
- Do not repeat what is already being done or planned.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the management team for guidance.
