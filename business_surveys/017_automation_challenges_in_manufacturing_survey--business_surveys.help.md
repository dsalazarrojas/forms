# Automation Challenges In Manufacturing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather information on the current state of automation in manufacturing industries, identifying key challenges, and exploring potential future investments and priorities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions honestly, and provide accurate information about your company.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please select the option that best reflects your current situation.
3. You can select multiple options for questions that allow it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your company's name as it appears on your business cards or website.
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your business cards or email.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, `email`, Required): Enter your company email address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, Required): Enter your job title or position within your company.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, Required): Select the department you belong to within your company. Options are Operations, Manufacturing, Engineering, Management, IT, or Other.
* **Years of Operation** (`years_in_operation`, `number`, Required): Enter the number of years your company has been in operation.
* **Facility Size** (`facility_size`, `select_one`, Required): Select the size of your facility. Options are Small (1-50 employees), Medium (51-250 employees), Large (251-1000 employees), or Enterprise (1000+ employees).
* **Industry Type** (`industry_type`, `select_one`, Required): Select the type of industry your company operates in. Options are Automotive, Electronics, Pharmaceuticals, Food and Beverage, Consumer Goods, Chemicals, Metals and Mining, or Other.
* **Annual Production Volume** (`production_volume`, `text`, Optional): Enter your company's annual production volume. This is optional but helps us understand your company's production capacity.
* **Current Automation Level** (`automation_percentage`, `select_one`, Required): Select the percentage of your production that is currently automated. Options are Less than 10%, 10-25%, 25-50%, 50-75%, or More than 75%.
* **Current Automation Technologies Used** (`automation_technologies`, `select_multiple`, Required): Select all automation technologies currently used in your company. Options are Robotics, PLC/CNC Systems, Industrial IoT, AI/Machine Learning, SCADA Systems, ERP Systems, MES Systems, Vision Systems, or Other.
* **Automation Implementation Timeline** (`automation_timeline`, `select_one`, Required): Select the time frame when your company implemented or plans to implement automation. Options are Within Last 2 Years, 2-5 Years Ago, 5-10 Years Ago, More than 10 Years Ago, or Not Yet Implemented.
* **Major Automation Challenges** (`major_challenges`, `select_multiple`, Required): Select all major challenges your company faces with automation. Options are High Capital Investment, Lack of Skilled Workforce, Integration Complexity, Maintenance and Support, Outdated Legacy Systems, ROI Uncertainty, Supply Chain Issues, Regulatory Compliance, Data Security, or Other.
* **Single Biggest Challenge** (`biggest_challenge`, `text`, Required): Enter the single biggest challenge your company faces with automation.
* **Annual Automation Investment** (`current_investment`, `select_one`, Optional): Select your company's annual automation investment. Options are Less than 50000, 50000-100000, 100000-500000, 500000-1000000, or More than 1000000.
* **Future Automation Investment Plans** (`future_investment_plans`, `select_one`, Required): Select whether your company plans to increase automation investment. Options are Yes, significantly, Yes, moderately, Unsure, or No plans.
* **Skilled Workforce** (`skilled_workforce`, `select_one`, Required): Select your company's current skilled workforce for automation. Options are Fully Skilled, Partially Skilled, Limited Skills, or No Technical Expertise.
* **Top Automation Priorities** (`automation_priorities`, `select_multiple`, Required): Select up to 3 priorities for your company's future automation investments. Options are Increase Production Capacity, Reduce Labor Costs, Improve Quality, Reduce Downtime, Improve Worker Safety, Reduce Energy Consumption, Enable Customization, or Other.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, Optional): Enter any other insights or concerns related to automation in your company.

## Tips

- Answer honestly and accurately to provide valuable insights.
- Select all applicable options for questions that allow multiple selections.
- If unsure about any question, select the option that best reflects your current situation.
- If a field has a specific instruction, please follow it strictly.
- Take approximately 10-15 minutes to complete the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact information].
