# Component Breakdown Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed feedback on a specific component within a project. It helps in assessing its performance, reliability, cost-effectiveness, ease of integration, maintenance requirements, scalability, compatibility, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Survey ID**: A unique identifier for this survey.
2. **Project Name**: The name of the project being surveyed.
3. **Department**: Select your department.
4. **Respondent Name**: Your name.
5. **Survey Date**: The date of this survey.
6. **Component Category**: Select the component category being evaluated.
7. **Component Name**: The specific name of the component.
8. **Component Function**: Describe the primary function of this component.
9. **Performance Rating**: Rate the overall performance.
10. **Reliability Score**: Rate the reliability.
11. **Cost Effectiveness**: Rate the cost effectiveness.
12. **Ease of Integration**: Rate how easy it is to integrate this component.
13. **Maintenance Requirements**: Rate the maintenance burden.
14. **Scalability**: Rate how well this component scales.
15. **Compatibility Issues**: Select whether you've experienced any compatibility issues.
16. **Issues Description**: Describe any issues encountered (optional).
17. **Improvement Suggestions**: Suggest improvements (optional).
18. **Replacement Consideration**: Select whether you're considering replacing this component.
19. **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction.
20. **Additional Comments**: Any other feedback about this component (optional).

## Tips

* Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly.
* Ensure you have sufficient information about the component to complete this form.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant department or project lead for guidance.
* This form is intended for project managers, team members, or stakeholders who need to assess components within their project.
