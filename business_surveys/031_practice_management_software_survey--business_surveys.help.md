# Practice Management Software Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from users of practice management software. The goal is to understand their current systems and user experience, gather improvement ideas, and make product decisions based on vendor feedback and firm feedback. Completing this form will help us better understand your needs and preferences, and ultimately improve our practice management software.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully read each question and answer the ones that apply to your situation.
2. Select all options that match your experience or preferences.
3. Provide detailed responses for text fields as needed.
4. Submit your answers when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Systems** (`current_systems`, text, required/optional): Describe your current practice management systems, including any software, tools, or methods you are currently using.
* **User Satisfaction** (`user_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the options that apply to your user experience. Choose 'Satisfied' if you are generally happy with your current system, 'Dissatisfied' if you are not, or select both if your experience varies.
* **Key Features** (`key_features`, text, required/optional): List the features or functionalities you consider essential or would like to see in our practice management software.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, text, required/optional): Share any ideas or suggestions you have for improving our practice management software.
* **Product Decisions** (`product_decisions`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether or not you would recommend our practice management software to others based on your experience.
* **Vendor Feedback** (`vendor_feedback`, text, required/optional): Provide any feedback or comments you have about our software or services.
* **Firm Feedback** (`firm_feedback`, text, required/optional): Share any feedback or comments you have about our company or services.
* **Practice Feedback** (`practice_feedback`, text, required/optional): Provide any feedback or comments you have about our practice or services.
* **Practice Decisions** (`practice_decisions`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether or not you would recommend our practice management software to others based on your experience.
