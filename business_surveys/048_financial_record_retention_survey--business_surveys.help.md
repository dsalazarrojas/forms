# Financial Record Retention Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool to collect data on financial records, their storage, retention, and disposal methods from businesses. This data will help in understanding compliance with financial data regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. Review and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Information** (`financial_record_retention_survey_page_1`, text, required): Provide your company's name, address, and contact information.
* **Financial Records** (`financial_record_retention_survey_page_2`, text, required): Describe your financial records management system, including the types of records kept, storage methods, and access control procedures.
* **Records Storage** (`financial_record_retention_survey_page_3`, text, required): Explain the location where financial records are stored, including the storage duration, security measures, and backup procedures.
* **Retention and Disposal** (`financial_record_retention_survey_page_4`, text, required): Describe the company's retention and disposal policies for financial records, including the duration and methods of retention, and disposal procedures.
* **Records Security** (`financial_record_record_retention_survey_page_5`, text, required): Explain the security measures in place to protect financial records, including access controls, authentication, and authorization procedures.
* **Compliance** (`financial_record_record_retention_survey_page_6`, text, required): Indicate the company's compliance with financial data regulations, including laws and industry standards.
* **Financial Data** (`financial_record_retention_survey_page_7`, text, required): Describe the financial data management processes, including data collection, processing, and analysis procedures.
* **Review and Approval** (`financial_record_retention_survey_page_8`, text, required): Confirm that the company's financial records are accurate and up-to-date, and that they are properly maintained and reviewed.
* **Record-Keeping Best Practices** (`financial_record_retention_survey_page_9`, text, required): Discuss best practices for financial record-keeping, including storage, security, and compliance procedures.
* **Survey Submission** (`financial_record_retention_survey_page_10`, text, required): Confirm that the submission of the financial records is complete and accurate.
