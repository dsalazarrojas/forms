# Hospital Digital Innovation Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate a hospital's readiness for digital innovation, covering various aspects of the organization, including IT infrastructure, collaboration, training, digital transformation, innovation maturity, innovation roadmap, technology adoption, digital innovation, leadership, culture, finance, human resources, operations, security, environment, IT, innovation, digital innovation plan, budget, and sustainability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by reading the introduction page and understanding the purpose of the survey.
2.  Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3.  For select_multiple fields (e.g., hospital_management, hospital_finance, hospital_human_resources), select all applicable options.
4.  For text fields (e.g., hospital_it_infrastructure, data_management), provide a brief but clear description of your current state or plans.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, text, required): Briefly read and understand the introduction, which sets the context for the rest of the survey.
* **What is digital transformation?** (`2`, text, required): Describe your hospital's understanding of digital transformation.
* **Benefits** (`3`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether your hospital is experiencing any benefits from digital transformation.
* **What is your hospital's current IT infrastructure like?** (`4`, text, required): Describe your hospital's current IT infrastructure.
* **Data management** (`5`, text, required): Describe your current data management practices.
* **Collaboration** (`6`, text, required): Describe your hospital's collaboration practices.
* **Training and support** (`7`, text, required): Describe your hospital's training and support for digital innovation.
* **Digital transformation** (`8`, text, required): Describe your hospital's approach to digital transformation.
* **What is your hospital's digital innovation maturity?** (`9`, text, required): Assess your hospital's current digital innovation maturity level.
* **Innovation roadmap** (`10`, text, required): Outline your hospital's innovation roadmap.
* **Technology adoption** (`11`, text, required): Describe your hospital's technology adoption process.
* **Digital innovation readiness** (`12`, text, required): Evaluate your hospital's digital innovation readiness.
* **Hospital leadership** (`13`, text, required): Describe your hospital's leadership approach to digital innovation.
* **Organizational culture** (`14`, text, required): Describe your hospital's organizational culture.
* **Hospital management** (`15`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable management approaches that your hospital employs.
* **Hospital finance** (`16`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable finance approaches that your hospital employs.
* **Hospital human resources** (`17`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable human resources approaches that your hospital employs.
* **Hospital operations** (`18`, text, required): Describe your hospital's current operations.
* **Hospital security** (`19`, text, required): Describe your hospital's current security measures.
* **Hospital environment** (`20`, text, required): Describe your hospital's current environment.
* **Hospital IT** (`21`, text, required): Describe your hospital's current IT department.
* **Hospital innovation** (`22`, text, required): Describe your hospital's current innovation approach.
* **Hospital digital innovation plan** (`23`, text, required): Outline your hospital's digital innovation plan.
* **Hospital digital innovation budget** (`24`, text, required): Describe your hospital's digital innovation budget.
* **Hospital digital innovation sustainability** (`25`, text, required): Describe your hospital's digital innovation sustainability approach.

By carefully following these steps and explanations, you'll be able to provide a comprehensive and accurate assessment of your hospital's digital innovation readiness.
