# Wallpaper Change Interval Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is intended to gather information about your habits and preferences when it comes to wallcovering, including how often you change your wallpaper, what influences your decisions, and what your typical budget is for such changes. The information collected will help us understand your behavior and provide insights to improve our product offerings and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Select one or multiple options for each field as per your actual practices.
3. If you're unsure about an option, feel free to select "Others" and provide more information in the text box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How often do you change your wallpaper?** (wallpaper_change_frequency): How often you replace your current wallpaper with a new one.
  - Frequently: You usually change your wallpaper every few months.
  - Occasionally: You may change your wallpaper occasionally, e.g., when renovating a room or for special occasions.
  - Rarely: You don't often change your wallpaper.
  - Never: You've never changed your wallpaper.

- **Why do you typically change your wallpaper?** (reason_for_change): Select all reasons that apply to why you usually change your wallpaper.

  - Aesthetics: You prefer a change in look or style.
  - Practicality: You want to update your wallpaper for functional reasons, e.g., to cover damages or stains.
  - Maintenance: You're concerned about the condition of your current wallpaper.
  - Others: Specify your reasons.
  - None of the above: None of the above options apply to you.

- **What influences your wallpaper decisions?** (influences_decisions): Check all sources that influence your wallpaper choices.

  - Online research: You research and compare different options online.
  - Others: Specify your sources.
  - None of the above: None of the above sources apply to you.
