# Customer Product Research Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Product Research Survey is designed to gather feedback on your experience with our product. Your input will help us understand how to improve and better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate options for the "Customer Information" and "Product Information" fields.
2. Choose how often you use the product and how satisfied you are with it.
3. Decide if you would recommend the product.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" field.
5. Enter your contact details accurately, including email, phone numbers, and job titles.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`Customer Information`, `select_multiple`): Select one or more of the titles (Mr, Mrs, Ms, Dr) that apply to you.
* **Product Information** (`Product Information`, `select_multiple`): Select one or more of the product names (Apple, Microsoft, Google) that you have experience with.
* **How often do you use this product?** (`How often do you use this product?`, `select_one`): Choose one of the options (Daily, Weekly, Rarely) that best describes your product usage frequency.
* **How satisfied are you with this product?** (`How satisfied are you with this product?`, `select_one`): Select one of the satisfaction levels (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied) that best describe your experience with the product.
* **Would you recommend this product?** (`Would you recommend this product?`, `select_one`): Choose "Yes" if you would recommend the product, and "No" if you wouldn't.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`): Provide any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share with us.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`): Enter a valid email address in the correct format.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`): Enter your contact phone number in the correct format.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`): Enter your contact address.
* **Company** (`Company`, `text`): Enter your company name.
* **Role** (`Role`, `text`): Enter your job title.
* **Manager** (`Manager`, `text`): Enter your manager's name.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`): Select the date in the correct format.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`): Select the time in the correct format.
* **Phone Work** (`Phone`, `text`): Enter your work phone number.
* **Mobile Phone** (`Mobile Phone`, `text`): Enter your mobile phone number.
* **Website** (`Website`, `text`): Enter your company website.
* **Social Media** (`Social Media`, `text`): Enter your company social media handle.
* **Work Email** (`Work Email`, `email`): Enter your company email address.
* **Home Email** (`Home Email`, `email`): Enter your home email address.
* **Company 2** (`Company 2`, `text`): Enter the name of your second company.
* **Job Title 2** (`Job Title 2`, `text`): Enter your job title in your second company.
* **Manager** (`Manager`, `text`): Enter the name of your manager in your second company.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and genuinely reflect your experience with the product.
* Double-check the format of the date and time fields for correct input. 
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, provide them in the "Additional Comments" field. 
* Enter contact details accurately and consistently with your company's format.
