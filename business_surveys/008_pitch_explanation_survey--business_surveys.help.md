## pitch_explanation_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on a pitch, allowing us to understand what people think of it and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction to understand the context of the pitch.
2. Select whether or not you think the pitch is good.
3. If you have any feedback or areas for improvement, select one or more options.
4. Provide any suggestions for the pitch.
5. Describe the next steps for the pitch.
6. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
7. If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, enter your preferred email address or phone number.
8. If you have any other additional information to share, enter it in the "Additional Information" field.
9. Lastly, enter the current status of the pitch.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Introduction** (Text): Enter a brief introduction to the pitch.
    Enter a short paragraph that summarizes the pitch and provides context.
- **What did you think of the pitch?** (Select One): Select whether or not you think the pitch is good.
    This question is to gauge your overall opinion of the pitch.
- **What do you think is the main improvement point for the pitch?** (Select One): Select one or more areas for improvement.
    This question is to help us identify specific areas that need improvement.
- **Do you have any suggestions for the pitch?** (Text): Provide any suggestions or ideas you have for the pitch.
    This is an open-ended question for you to share your thoughts.
- **What are the next steps for the pitch?** (Text): Describe the next steps for the pitch.
    This question is to help us understand what's next for the pitch.
- **Do you have any other comments on the pitch?** (Text): Provide any additional comments or thoughts.
    This is an open-ended question for you to share any additional information.
- **How can I follow up with you about the pitch?** (Text): Enter a preferred email address or phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
    This question is to let us know how to follow up with you.
- **Email** (Email): Enter a valid email address.
    This is a required field for follow-up purposes.
- **Phone Number** (Text): Enter a valid phone number.
    This is a required field for follow-up purposes.
- **Additional Information** (Text): Provide any additional information you'd like to share.
    This is an open-ended question for you to share any additional thoughts.
- **What is the status of the pitch?** (Text): Enter the current status of the pitch.
    This question is to help us understand the current state of the pitch.
- **Follow-up** (Text): Enter how you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.
    This question is to let us know how to follow up with you.
- **Your phone number** (Text): Enter a valid phone number.
    This is a required field for follow-up purposes.
- **Your email address** (Email): Enter a valid email address.
    This is a required field for follow-up purposes.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form carefully and only once.
* Be honest and provide your true opinion.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* We'll review your feedback and use it to improve the pitch.
