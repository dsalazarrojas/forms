# Cloud Systems Analyst Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cloud Systems Analyst Survey is a tool to gather information from cloud systems analysts to help us better understand their background, experience, and perspectives in the field.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all the questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
2. Use the provided options as a guideline, but feel free to add any additional information if you have more to share.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please provide a brief explanation or description in the text box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or introduction materials.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your work email address. This will help us contact you with any follow-up questions or to provide you with relevant information.
* **Current Job Title** (`current_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title, e.g., Cloud Systems Analyst, Senior Cloud Systems Analyst, etc.
* **Years in Cloud Systems - Analytics** (`years_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been working in cloud systems analysis.
* **Type of Organization** (`organization_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of organization you are working for (e.g., Enterprise, Mid-market, Small business, etc.).
* **Primary cloud platforms you work with** (`primary_platforms`, select_multiple, required): Select all the cloud platforms you have experience with (e.g., AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, etc.).
* **Which platform are you most expert in?** (`platform_expertise`, text, required): Select the cloud platform you have the most experience with.
* **Multi-cloud environment experience?** (`multi_cloud_experience`, select_one, required): Answer if you have experience working in multi-cloud environments.
* **Monitoring and analytics tools used** (`monitoring_tools`, text, required): List the monitoring and analytics tools you are using or have experience with.
* **Automation and orchestration tools** (`automation_tools`, text, required): List the automation and orchestration tools you are using or have experience with.
* **Security and compliance tools experience** (`security_compliance_tools`, text, required): List the security and compliance tools you are using or have experience with.
* **Main challenges in your role** (`main_challenges`, select_multiple, required): Select the main challenges you are currently facing in your role.
* **Biggest pain point currently** (`biggest_pain_point`, text, required): Describe the biggest pain point you are currently facing in your role.
* **Overall job satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your overall job satisfaction from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Most satisfying aspects of the role** (`satisfaction_factors`, text, required): Describe the most satisfying aspects of your role.
* **Areas for improvement in your organization** (`improvement_areas`, text, required): Describe the areas where you think your organization can improve.
* **Training or certifications you want to pursue** (`training_needs`, text, required): List the trainings or certifications you are interested in pursuing.
* **Career goals in cloud systems** (`career_goals`, text, required): Describe your career goals in cloud systems for the next 2-3 years.
