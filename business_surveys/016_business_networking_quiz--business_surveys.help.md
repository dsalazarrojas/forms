# Business Networking Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you understand your current networking habits, preferences, and goals. By completing this quiz, you'll gain insights into areas where you need to improve and discover opportunities for personal and professional growth.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly, using the options provided for each field.
2. For questions with a "select_multiple" or "select_one" type, choose the option that best fits your current situation or preference.
3. For questions with a "text" type, provide a brief answer or description.
4. Make sure to answer all required fields (marked as required).
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name (participant_name)**: Enter your full name, first and last name.
* **Email Address (participant_email)**: Enter your primary email contact information.
* **Company Name (company_name)**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Networking Experience Level (networking_experience)**: Choose how experienced you are with networking, from "Beginner" to "Expert".
* **How Often Do You Network (networking_frequency)**: Select how frequently you engage in networking activities, from "Weekly" to "Never".
* **Comfort Level with Networking (networking_comfort_level)**: Rate your comfort level with networking, from 1 (very uncomfortable) to 5 (very comfortable).
* **Confidence in Making New Connections (networking_confidence)**: Rate your confidence in making new connections, from 1 (very low) to 5 (very high).
* **Preferred Networking Format (preferred_networking_format)**: Choose your preferred method of networking, from "In-person events", "Virtual meetings", "Social media", "One-on-one calls", or "Mixed format".
* **Primary Networking Goals (networking_goals)**: Select all applicable goals you hope to achieve through networking, from "Find business partners", "Generate leads", "Build friendships", "Learn from peers", "Grow professional community", or "Career advancement".
* **Biggest Networking Challenge (biggest_networking_challenge)**: If applicable, describe the biggest challenge you face with networking.
* **Have You Made Successful Business Connections (past_successful_connections)**: Choose whether you've made successful connections or not.
* **Comfort with Starting Conversations (conversation_initiation)**: Rate your comfort level with starting conversations, from 1 (very uncomfortable) to 5 (very comfortable).
* **Follow Up Consistency (follow_up_consistency)**: Select how consistently you follow up after meetings, from "Always" to "Never".
* **Your Networking Strengths (networking_strengths)**: If applicable, describe your strengths in networking.
* **Areas for Improvement (improvement_areas)**: If applicable, mention areas where you'd like to improve your networking.
* **Willing to Help Other Networkers (willing_to_help_others)**: Choose your willingness to help other networkers, from "Yes, Very Much" to "Not Really".
* **Networking Topics You Want to Learn (learning_interests)**: Select topics you're interested in learning more about, from "Communication Skills", "Building Relationships", "Online Networking", "Industry Connections", "Personal Branding", or "Confidence Building".
