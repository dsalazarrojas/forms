# sports_event_vendor_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from sports event vendors about their experience with a sports event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section as instructed:

1. Go through each page (section) one by one, ensuring you provide information for each relevant field.
2. For the "Event Details" section, provide any relevant information about the event you participated in, such as date, location, or event type.
3. For the "Vendor Info" section, describe your experience with the event organization process, including any interactions with our team.
4. In the "Communication" section, select one or more of the options that best describe your perception of our communication efforts.
5. For the "Overall Satisfaction" field, rate your overall satisfaction with the event using a numerical value.
6. In the "Outcome Measures" section, provide any specific measures or metrics related to your experience with the event.
7. If relevant, provide further feedback in the "Outcome Measures 1", "Outcome Measures 2", and "Outcome Measures 3" fields.
8. Finally, in the "Communication" fields, provide any additional comments about your communication experience with our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (text, optional): Provide a brief overview of your experience with the event.
* **Event Details** (text, optional): Describe any relevant details about the event, such as date, location, or event type.
* **Vendor Info** (text, optional): Describe your experience with the event organization process and any interactions with our team.
* **Communication** (select multiple, optional): Select one or more options that best describe our communication efforts to you:
	+ Option 1: Not at all
	+ Option 2: Somewhat
	+ Option 3: Neutral
	+ Option 4: Often
	+ Option 5: Very often
	+ Option 6: Almost always
* **Overall Satisfaction** (number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event using a numerical value (e.g., 0-5 or -5).
* **Outcome Measures** (text, optional): Provide any specific measures or metrics related to your experience with the event.
* **Outcome Measures 1**, **Outcome Measures 2**, and **Outcome Measures 3** (number, optional): Provide further feedback on specific measures or metrics related to your experience with the event.
* **Communication 1** through **Communication 15** (text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your communication experience with our team.
