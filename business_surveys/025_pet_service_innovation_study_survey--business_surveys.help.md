# Pet Service Innovation Study Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and ideas from customers on the company's pet services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Please provide feedback on your current experience with the company's pet services.
3. If you have any new ideas for service improvements, feel free to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Pet Service Innovation Study Survey Form** (`Pet Service Innovation Study Survey Form`, `note`, optional): This field is a placeholder for the form title. Please do not fill it out.
- **Feedback On Pet Service** (`Feedback On Pet Service`, `text`, optional): In this field, please provide a brief description of your experience with the company's pet services. Be specific about what you liked or disliked.
- **Satisfaction On Current Services** (`Satisfaction On Current Services`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about your level of satisfaction with the current services offered by the company. Select the option that best represents your feelings.
- **New Service Ideas** (`New Service Ideas`, `text`, optional): If you have any ideas for new services that the company could offer, please describe them in this field.
- **How Many New Service Ideas** (`How Many New Service Ideas`, `number`, optional): If you have more than one new service idea, please estimate the number of ideas you have.
- **New Service Ideas Description** (`New Service Ideas Description`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or explanations for your new service ideas, please provide them here.

## Tips
- Please be honest and sincere in your feedback.
- If you have any suggestions or ideas, we value your input and appreciate your participation.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
