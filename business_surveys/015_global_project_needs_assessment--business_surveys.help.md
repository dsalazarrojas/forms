# global_project_needs_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The global_project_needs_assessment form is designed to gather information about a project's objectives, challenges, resource needs, project scope, project scope details, and business impact. This form is essential for project stakeholders to understand the project's needs and plan accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Click on the "Submit" button to start filling in the form.
* Scroll through the form's pages and select the page you want to answer.
* For each page, fill in the relevant fields with your answers.
* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible for each field.
* Once you have filled in all the required fields, click on the "Submit" button to save your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Objectives** (Project Objectives, text, optional)
	+ This field is where you should describe the project's objectives, goals, and objectives.
* **Project Challenges** (Project Challenges, text, optional)
	+ This field is where you can list the project's challenges, obstacles, or constraints.
* **Resource Needs** (Resource Needs, text, optional)
	+ This field is where you should describe the resources required to complete the project, such as personnel, equipment, or materials.
* **Project Scope** (Project Scope, text, optional)
	+ This field is where you should describe the project's scope, boundaries, and limitations.
* **Project Scope Details** (Project Scope Details, text, optional)
	+ This field is where you can provide more details about the project's scope, if needed.
* **Business Impact** (Business Impact, number, optional)
	+ This field is where you should estimate the potential business impact of the project, using a numerical value.
