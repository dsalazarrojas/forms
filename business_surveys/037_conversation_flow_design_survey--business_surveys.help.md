# Conversation Flow Design Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information and feedback from conversation designers and developers to better understand their practices, preferences, and challenges in conversation design. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions in the order they appear.
2. Provide honest and detailed responses based on your current practices and preferences.
3. Take around 10-15 minutes to complete the survey.
4. Do not skip any questions, and you can go back and change your answers at any time before submitting the form.
5. If you have any questions or concerns, please let us know and we will be happy to help.
