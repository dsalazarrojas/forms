# Social Media Engagement Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you track and analyze social media engagement metrics for your company or organization. You can use it to evaluate and prioritize social media platforms, accounts, or campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct date range for the data you want to audit.
2. Enter the email address associated with the social media account(s) you want to audit.
3. Enter the URL of the company website related to the social media account(s).
4. Identify the social media platforms your company is active on, such as Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn, and YouTube.
5. Specify the type of social media account(s) you are auditing, such as Personal, Business, or Other.
6. Select the current status of the social media account(s), such as Active, Inactive, or Pending.
7. Provide a brief description of the social media account(s).
8. Add any additional notes about the social media account(s).
9. Tag or categorize the social media account(s) with relevant keywords or tags.
10. Specify the category of the social media account(s), such as Category1, Category2, etc.
11. Choose the priority level of the social media account(s), such as High, Medium, or Low.
12. Rate the overall score of the social media account(s) on a scale from 1-3, Excellent, Good, or Bad.
13. Enter the percentage score for the social media account(s).
14. Enter any additional notes about the score.
15. Specify the frequency of score updates, such as Monthly, Quarterly, or Semiannually.
16. Select the basis of the score, such as Overall, Performance, or Engagement.
17. Enter the weightage for the score, if applicable.
18. Enter any additional notes about the score weightage.
19. Enter the date of the last score update, if applicable.
20. Enter the date of the next score update, if applicable.
21. Choose the frequency of score updates, such as Daily, Weekly, Monthly, etc.
22. Enter the average score, if applicable.
23. Enter any additional notes about the average score.
24. Enter the weightage for the average score, if applicable.
25. Enter the frequency of average score updates, if applicable.
26. Enter any other notes or comments about the social media account(s).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Surveys Form** (1): Enter any notes or comments about this section.
* **Date Range** (2): The date range for the data you want to audit, in the format mm/dd/yyyy to mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email Address** (3): Enter the email address associated with the social media account(s).
* **Company Website** (4): Enter the URL of the company website related to the social media account(s).
* **Social Media Platforms** (5): Identify the social media platforms your company is active on (e.g. Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn, etc.).
* **Account Name** (6): Enter the name of the social media account(s).
* **Account Type** (7): Specify the type of social media account(s), such as Personal, Business, or Other.
* **Account Status** (8): Select the current status of the social media account(s), such as Active, Inactive, or Pending.
* **Account Description** (9): Provide a brief description of the social media account(s).
* **Account Notes** (10): Add any additional notes about the social media account(s).
* **Account Tags** (11): Tag or categorize the social media account(s) with relevant keywords or tags.
* **Account Keywords** (12): Enter any other relevant keywords or tags for the social media account(s).
* **Account Category** (13): Specify the category of the social media account(s), such as Category1, Category2, etc.
* **Account Category Type** (14): Choose the type of category for the social media account(s), such as Category1, Category2, etc.
* **Account Priority** (15): Choose the priority level of the social media account(s), such as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Account Score** (16): Rate the overall score of the social media account(s) on a scale from 1-3, Excellent, Good, or Bad.
* **Account Score Percentage** (17): Enter the percentage score for the social media account(s).
* **Account Score Notes** (18): Enter any additional notes about the score.
* **Account Score Frequency** (19): Specify the frequency of score updates, such as Monthly, Quarterly, or Semiannually.
* **Account Score Basis** (20): Choose the basis of the score, such as Overall, Performance, or Engagement.
* **Account Score Weightage** (21): Enter the weightage for the score, if applicable.
* **Account Score Last Score Date** (22): Enter the date of the last score update, if applicable.
* **Account Score Next Score Date** (23): Enter the date of the next score update, if applicable.
* **Account Score Score Frequency** (24): Choose the frequency of score updates, such as Daily, Weekly, Monthly, etc.
* **Account Score Average** (25): Enter the average score, if applicable.
* **Account Score Average Notes** (26): Enter any additional notes about the average score.
* **Account Score Average Weightage** (27): Enter the weightage for the average score, if applicable.
* **Account Score Average Frequency** (28): Choose the frequency of average score updates, if applicable.
* **Account Score Last Score Date** (29): Enter the date of the last average score update, if applicable.
* **Account Score Next Score Date** (30): Enter the date of the next average score update, if applicable.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields for accurate results.
- Use the correct date range to ensure accurate data.
- Use relevant keywords or tags to categorize social media accounts.
- Be specific when rating the score and providing additional notes.
- Regularly update scores and averages to track changes in social media engagement.
