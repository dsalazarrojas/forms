# partnership_facilities_audit_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data on a partner's facilities, specifically their locations, compliance, and quality ratings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all relevant locations for the partner's facilities when asked for "Partner Locations".
2. Choose the compliance ratings for each location by selecting all relevant options that apply from the dropdown menu.
3. In the "Compliance Observations" field, provide specific and accurate observations or notes related to compliance for each location.
4. Select all relevant quality ratings for each facility by choosing all applicable options from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partner Locations**: Select all relevant locations for the partner's facilities.
* **Compliance Ratings**: Choose the compliance ratings for each location by selecting all relevant options that apply from the dropdown menu. Options are "Good" and "Fair".
* **Compliance Observations**: Provide specific and accurate observations or notes related to compliance for each location.
* **Quality Ratings**: Select all relevant quality ratings for each facility by choosing all applicable options from the dropdown menu. Options are "Good" and "Poor".
* **Audit Data**: This field is not required, but if you have any additional information or notes related to the audit, please include them here.
