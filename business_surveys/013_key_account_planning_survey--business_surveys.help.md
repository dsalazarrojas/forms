# Key Account Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about key customer accounts, their objectives, challenges, and relationships. The collected data will help you better understand their needs and expectations, enabling you to develop effective sales strategies and plans.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the **Survey Title** field with a brief description of the account.
2.  In the **Objectives** field, list the customer's main goals and objectives.
3.  Select the **Stakeholders** involved in the account by choosing from the provided options.
4.  In the **Challenges** field, describe any challenges the customer is currently facing.
5.  In the **Growth Opportunities** field, outline potential areas for growth and improvement.
6.  Select the **Customer Accounts** by choosing from the provided options.
7.  In the **Account Managers** field, select the individuals responsible for managing the account.
8.  In the **Sales Teams** field, provide an overview of the teams involved in the sale process.
9.  In the **Customer Contacts** field, list the key contacts involved in the account.
10.  In the **Customer Relationships** field, describe the relationships between the customer and your company.
11.  In the **Sales Strategy** field, outline the overall sales strategy for the account.
12.  In the **Customer Commitment** field, describe the level of commitment from the customer.
13.  In the **Customer Support** field, outline the level of support provided to the customer.
14.  In the **Customer Feedback** field, describe the feedback mechanisms in place.
15.  In the **Sales Performance** field, evaluate the sales performance of the account.
16.  In the **Customer Expectations** field, outline the customer's expectations.
17.  In the **Sales Process** field, describe the sales process followed for the account.
18.  In the **Account Plan** field, create a detailed plan for the account.
19.  In the **Account Reviews** field, schedule review meetings with the account.
20.  In the **Sales Outlook** field, provide a forecasted sales outlook for the account.
21.  In the **Customer Goals** field, outline the customer's short-term goals.
22.  In the **Customer Objectives** field, outline the customer's long-term objectives.
23.  In the **Customer Commitment Level** field, evaluate the customer's level of commitment.
24.  In the **Customer Satisfaction** field, evaluate the customer's satisfaction level.
25.  In the **Customer Feedback Frequency** field, describe the frequency of customer feedback.

## Tips

*  Be as detailed and accurate as possible while filling out the form.
*  Review and verify the information with the customer before submitting the form.
*  Use this form as a basis for your sales strategy and account planning.
*  Update the form regularly to reflect changes in the customer's needs and expectations.
