# marketplace_export_consultation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from business owners about their exporting readiness, products or services offered, and challenges faced when exporting. It is intended for those with existing businesses looking to expand their international presence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by answering the question in the "How do you want to start this survey?" field. This section is optional and allows you to introduce your thoughts.
2. Provide the size of your business in the "What is the size of your business?" field. This field requires a numeric value.
3. Choose all the export products you offer from the list in the "What export products do you offer?" field.
4. Select all the export services you offer from the list in the "What export services do you offer?" field.
5. Rate how easily customers will access your products or services in the "How easily do you think customers will access your products or services?" field. This field requires a numeric value.
6. Answer if you are ready for international markets in the "Are you ready for international markets?" field. This field requires a single selection.
7. Choose all the export challenges you face from the list in the "What export challenges do you face?" field.
8. Select if you are ready for international markets in the "Are you ready for international markets?" field. This field requires a single selection.
9. Choose all the export services you offer from the list in the "What export services do you offer?" field. This field seems to be a duplicate of the previous question, but we will follow the provided label.
10. Select all the export challenges you face from the list in the "What export challenges do you face?" field. This field seems to be a duplicate of the previous question, but we will follow the provided label.
11. Select if you have export planning in place in the "Do you have export planning in place?" field. This field is optional.
12. Choose all the export services you have planning in place from the list in the "What export planning do you have in place?" field. This field is optional.
