# Project Outcome Measurement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Project Outcome Measurement Survey is designed to collect feedback about a project's outcome, measuring its impact, and understanding what worked well and what didn't. This survey will help the project team to identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions for future projects.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully and thoroughly, providing specific examples or examples when possible.
2. For questions with multiple options, select the one that best describes your experience with the project.
3. If a question does not apply to your experience, please mark the "Not Applicable" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the primary goal of this project** (`project_outcome_measurement_survey_1`, text, required): Describe the main objective of the project. This will help us understand what we aimed to achieve.
* **What was the primary outcome of this project** (`project_outcome_measurement_survey_2`, text, required): Describe the actual outcome of the project. Compare it with the primary goal to identify any deviations or successes.
* **How likely are you to recommend this project to a colleague** (`project_outcome_measurement_survey_3`, number, required): Rate your confidence in recommending this project to others.
* **What was the level of project impact** (`project_outcome_measurement_survey_4`, text, required): Describe the project's impact on the organization, team, or customers.
* **Did this project meet your expectations** (`project_outcome_measurement_survey_5`, select_one, required): Select whether the project met or didn't meet your expectations. If not met, describe why.
* **What did you like most about this project** (`project_outcome_measurement_survey_6`, text, optional): Share the most positive aspect of the project.
* **What did you like least about this project** (`project_outcome_measurement_survey_7`, text, optional): Share the least positive aspect of the project.
