The "Small Business Financial Assessment" form is a tool used to gauge the financial health of small businesses. It is essential to understand that this form should only be filled out by business owners or authorized personnel who are familiar with the financial aspects of their company. Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authority to complete this form accurately.

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

## Purpose
The form is used to gather information about the financial situation of small businesses. This includes revenue, expenses, and financial goals. The goal of this form is to help identify areas where the business can improve its financial stability and growth.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Review the form**: Before starting, review the form's fields to understand what information is being requested.
2. **Fill out the form accurately**: Answer the questions honestly and accurately, using the provided options or entering your own values for the numeric fields.
3. **Select relevant options**: When selecting from multiple-choice options, choose the ones that best describe your business's situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Revenue**: This field is used to record your business's annual revenue. Please enter a numerical value in the provided field.
*   **Expense**: Enter your business's annual expenses here. This includes all the costs associated with running your business, such as rent, equipment, and employee salaries.
*   **Goal**: This field is used to choose the primary financial goal of your business. Please select one of the options provided:
	+ Option 1: Increase revenue by 10%
	+ Option 2: Reduce expenses by 15%
	+ Option 3: Expand product line
*   **Annual Revenue**: Enter your business's annual revenue here. This is used to track changes in your business's revenue over time.
*   **Annual Expenses**: Enter your business's annual expenses here. This includes all the costs associated with running your business, such as rent, equipment, and employee salaries.
*   **Goal**: This field is used to choose the financial goals of your business. Please select all the options that apply:
	+ Option 1: Increase revenue
	+ Option 2: Reduce expenses
	+ Option 3: Expand product line

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly.
*   If you're unsure about any of the options, please contact a financial advisor.
*   The information collected from this form will be used to provide guidance on improving the financial stability and growth of your business.
