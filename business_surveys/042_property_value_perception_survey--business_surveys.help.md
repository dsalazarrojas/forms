# Property Value Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Value Perception Survey is designed to gather feedback on the perception of a specific geographic area, focusing on the quality and features of the area.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question based on your personal experience and opinions.
2. Select one option for questions with a "select_one" type, such as "How often do you visit this area?" or "How would you rate the overall safety of this area?"
3. For "select_multiple" types, such as "What features do you value most in a property?", select all applicable options.
4. For "text" type, provide a short and clear answer to "What do you like least about this area?"
5. Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by "required: true").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you visit this area?** (`property_visit_frequency`, select_one, required): Answer how frequently you visit this area, such as Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of property you are referring to, such as House, Condo, Townhouse, or Duplex.
* **How would you rate the quality of local public transportation?** (`public_transportation`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of local public transportation as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What do you like least about this area?** (`area_features`, text, required): Describe what you dislike or find least appealing about the area in a short sentence.
* **What features do you value most in a property?** (`valued_features`, select_multiple, required): Select all the features that you value most, such as Parks, Public transportation, Schools, Shopping, Safety, or Proximity to work.
* **What amenities would you like to see in this area?** (`desired_amenities`, select_multiple, required): Select all the amenities you would like to see in the area, such as Parks, Public transportation, Schools, Shopping, Restaurants, or Gyms.
* **How would you rate the overall safety of this area?** (`area_safety`, select_one, required): Rate the overall safety of the area as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of local amenities in this area?** (`amenities_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of local amenities as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How important is the availability of parking to you in this area?** (`parking_importance`, select_one, required): Rate the importance of parking availability in the area as Very important, Somewhat important, Not very important, or Not at all important.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of local businesses in this area?** (`business_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of local businesses as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How often do you think new developments/changes should be made in this area?** (`developments_frequency`, select_one, required): Rate the frequency of new developments as Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.
* **How would you rate the overall sense of community in this area?** (`sense_of_community`, select_one, required): Rate the sense of community as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.

## Tips

* Answer honestly and based on your personal experiences.
* If you have any suggestions or comments about the area, feel free to add them to "What do you like least about this area?".
* This survey is designed to understand your perception of the area and help improve it.
