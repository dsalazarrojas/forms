# ride-hailing-company-evaluation-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from riders about their experience with a ride-hailing company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start with the Introduction section, which provides a brief overview of the form's purpose.
2. In the **rate-your-experience** section, rate your overall experience with the ride-hailing company on a scale of 1-5.
3. In the **Overall Satisfaction** section, select how satisfied you are with your experience.
4. In the **City** section, select all the cities where you've used the ride-hailing service.
5. In the **Price** section, rate the price of your ride.
6. In the **Service** section, select whether you received the service you requested.
7. In the **Cleanliness** section, rate the cleanliness of the vehicle(s) you used.
8. In the **Safety** section, select how often you felt safe during your ride.
9. In the **Communication** section, select whether you received timely and clear communication from the driver.
10. In the **Rating** section, rate your overall rating for the ride-hailing company.
11. In the **Satisfaction** section, rate your satisfaction with the service.
12. In the **Comments** section, provide any additional comments or suggestions you'd like to share.
13. In the **Contact Info** section, provide a valid email address so we can reach out to you for follow-up questions or to provide updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Note): This is a brief overview of the form's purpose.
* **rate-your-experience** (Number): Rate your overall experience with the ride-hailing company on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Select One): Select how satisfied you are with your experience. Options: Satisfied, Dissatisfied.
* **City** (Select Multiple): Select all the cities where you've used the ride-hailing service. Options: New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Houston, Phoenix, Philadelphia, San Antonio, San Diego, San Jose, Austin, Dallas, San Francisco, Jacksonville, Seattle, Indianapolis, Boston.
* **Price** (Number): Rate the price of your ride.
* **Service** (Select One): Select whether you received the service you requested. Options: Yes, No.
* **Cleanliness** (Number): Rate the cleanliness of the vehicle(s) you used.
* **Safety** (Select Multiple): Select how often you felt safe during your ride. Options: Always, Most of the time, Occasionally, Seldom, Never.
* **Communication** (Select One): Select whether you received timely and clear communication from the driver. Options: Yes, No.
* **Rating** (Number): Rate your overall rating for the ride-hailing company.
* **Satisfaction** (Number): Rate your satisfaction with the service.
* **Comments** (Text): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you'd like to share.
* **Contact Info** (Email): Provide a valid email address so we can reach out to you for follow-up questions or to provide updates.

## Tips
* Please be as specific and honest as possible when answering each question.
* If you're unsure about any question, just select the option that best reflects your experience.
* You can select multiple cities where you've used the service.
