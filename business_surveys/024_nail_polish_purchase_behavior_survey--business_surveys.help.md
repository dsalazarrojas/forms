# Nail Polish Purchase Behavior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nail Polish Purchase Behavior Survey is designed to collect data on the purchase behavior of nail polish users. This form will help businesses in the nail care industry understand their target audience's preferences and behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the frequency of your nail polish purchases.
2. Choose all the shopping channels you use to purchase nail polish.
3. Enter your preferred brand of nail polish.
4. Select all the factors that influence your purchases.
5. Choose the type of nail polish product you are interested in.
6. Enter the frequency of your in-store purchases.
7. Enter the frequency of your online purchases.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Purchase Frequency** (`purchase_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks you to select how often you purchase nail polish. Please choose one of the options: Nail Polish, Nail care, Nail art, or Other.
* **Shopping Channels** (`shopping_channels`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field asks you to select all the channels where you purchase nail polish. Please choose from: Online, In store, Mobile app, or Other.
* **Purchase Preferences** (`purchase_preferences`, `text`, required: false): This field is open text where you can describe your preferred brand of nail polish. 
* **Brand Preferred** (`brand_preferred`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks you to select your preferred brand of nail polish. Please choose from: OPI, Sally, Sally Beauty, or Other.
* **Purchase Influenced** (`purchase_influenced`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field asks you to select all the factors that influence your purchases. Please choose from: Social Media, Online ads, In store promotion, or Other.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks you to select the type of nail polish product you are interested in. Please choose from: Regular, Nail care, Nail art, or Nail polish remover.
* **Shopping Frequency In Store** (`shopping_frequency_in_store`, `number`, required: false): This field asks you to enter the frequency of your in-store purchases. Please enter a whole number.
* **Shopping Frequency Online** (`shopping_frequency_online`, `number`, required: false): This field asks you to enter the frequency of your online purchases. Please enter a whole number.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully to help us better understand your purchase behavior.
* You can select multiple options for each field, as needed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
