# management_communication_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Management Communication Feedback Form" is a tool for employees to provide feedback on the effectiveness and quality of management communication within the organization. This form will help the management team better understand the strengths and weaknesses of their communication and make informed decisions to improve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide your honest feedback.
2. Ensure you fill in all required fields.
3. Select only the relevant options for each field.
4. Enter your comments in the comments field if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **page_1** (`page_1`, none, required): This is the start of the feedback form. Please confirm that you have read and understood the instructions before proceeding.
* **communication_effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, select multiple, required): This field asks about the overall effectiveness of management communication. You should select all the options that you believe apply.
* **communication_strategy** (`communication_strategy`, select one, required): In this field, you should choose the strategy that best describes how management typically communicates.
* **frequency** (`frequency`, number, required): Enter the frequency with which management communicates with you. Please be as specific as possible.
* **communication_channels** (`communication_channels`, select multiple, required): Select all the channels through which management communicates with you.
* **style** (`style`, select multiple, required): Choose the styles of communication you experience from management.
* **team_leadership** (`team_leadership`, select multiple, required): Select all the leadership styles you experience from management.
* **communication_skills** (`communication_skills`, select multiple, required): Choose the communication skills you see from management.
* **comments** (`comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have about management communication.
