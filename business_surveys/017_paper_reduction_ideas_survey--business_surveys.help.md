# Paper Reduction Ideas Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect ideas and feedback from business users on strategies for reducing paper usage within their teams. The input collected will help in identifying effective ways to decrease paper consumption and implement sustainable practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully, as each section collects specific information on different areas of paper reduction.
2. Answer every question that applies to your team's situation.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
4. For open-ended questions, provide clear and concise answers.
5. Use the "Save" or "Submit" buttons to review and finalize your answers before submitting the form.
