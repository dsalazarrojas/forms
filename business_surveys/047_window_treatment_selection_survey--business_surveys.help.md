# Window Treatment Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Window Treatment Selection Survey is designed to collect user preferences and requirements for window treatments. This form will help us understand your needs and preferences regarding window treatments, which will aid us in providing better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your preferred option for each question. You can choose multiple options if the field is a select_multiple type.
2.  Fill in the text fields with the requested information.
3.  Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your submission is complete.
4.  Submit the form when you are ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Window Treatment Selection (`window_treatment_survey_form`)

*   This question asks whether you prefer window treatments.

*   You can select one or multiple options depending on your preference.

### 2. Room Details (`room_details`)

*   Please provide a brief description of the room where you would like to install the window treatment.

*   This will help us understand the context of your needs.

### 3. Client Preferences (`client_preferences`)

*   If you have any specific preferences or requests for your window treatment, please provide them here.

### 4. Budget (`budget`)

*   You can enter your budget for the window treatment installation.

*   This is an optional field, but it will help us provide you with more accurate quotes.

### 5. Window Dimensions (`window_dimensions`)

*   Please select the dimensions of your window to ensure we can provide you with the correct treatment size.

*   Options are 1x2, 2x3, and 2x4.

### 6. Drag and Drop (`drag_and_drop`)

*   Select how you prefer to interact with this form.

*   Options are 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5.

### 7. Organized Form Submissions (`organized_form_submissions`)

*   Select whether you prefer your form submissions to be organized.

*   Options are Yes and No.

### 8. Easy Data Collection (`easy_data_collection`)

*   Choose how easy you find data collection on this form.

*   Options are Yes and No.

### 9. Drag and Drop Interface (`drag_and_drop_interface`)

*   Select your preference for the drag and drop interface.

*   Options are Yes and No.

### 10. Organized Form Builder Form Templates (`organized_form_builder_form_templates`)

*   Enter any specific form templates you prefer for building forms.

### 11. Jotform Form Builder Form Templates (`jotform_form_builder_form_templates`)

*   Enter any specific Jotform form templates you prefer for building forms.

### 12. Collection Client Preferences, Room Details, Budgets, Window Dimensions (`collection_client_preferences_room_details_budgets_window_dimensions`)

*   Please enter any other information you'd like to collect related to the client, room, budget, and window dimensions.

### 13. Drag and Drop (`drag_and_drop`)

*   Select whether you want to interact with this form using drag and drop.

*   Options are Yes and No.

### 14. Easy Data Collection Organizer (`easy_data_collection_organizer`)

*   Choose how easy you find it to use an easy data collection tool.

*   Options are Yes and No.
