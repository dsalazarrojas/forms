# Property Renovation Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Renovation Quality Survey is designed to collect feedback on the quality of property renovation projects. This survey helps property owners, managers, or occupants evaluate the performance of contractors, assess the quality of work, and provide valuable insights for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the survey questions carefully and answer them truthfully to provide an accurate assessment of the renovation project.
2. Answer all the questions, but note that some questions are optional.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
4. Select the appropriate options for the multiple-choice questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Home Page (Page 1)
* **Home Page** (`home_page`, text, not required): This field is for any comments or feedback about the project's home page, where the work was done.

### Renovation Project (Page 2)
* **Renovation Project** (`renovation_project`, text, not required): Describe the renovation project in details, including its scope and nature.

### Project Details (Page 3)
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, not required): Provide a detailed description of the project, including any notable issues or challenges encountered during the project.

### Contractor Performance (Page 4)
* **Contractor Performance** (`contractor_performance`, select_one, not required): Evaluate the contractor's performance based on the provided options:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'

### Quality Rating (Page 5)
* **Quality Rating** (`quality_rating`, number, not required): Rate the overall quality of the work, on a numerical scale (e.g., 1-10).

### Satisfaction Level (Page 6)
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_multiple, not required): Evaluate your satisfaction level with the project based on the provided options:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied

### Communication (Page 7)
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, not required): Describe the level of communication between you and the contractor, including any issues or challenges encountered.

### Punctuality (Page 8)
* **Punctuality** (`punctuality`, text, not required): Evaluate the contractor's punctuality and timeliness of work, including any issues or delays.

### Value for Money (Page 9)
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, number, not required): Rate the value of the work, on a numerical scale (e.g., 1-10).

### Overall Rating (Page 10)
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_multiple, not required): Evaluate the overall rating of the project based on the provided options:
	+ Good
	+ Fair

### Comments (Page 11)
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the project, including suggestions for improvement.
