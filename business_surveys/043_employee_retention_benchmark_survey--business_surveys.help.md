# Employee Retention Benchmark Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to help organizations gather data about employee retention rates and strategies.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and consider the questions before answering.
2. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Select the options that apply to you or your experience.
4. Submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Turnover Rate** (`turnover_rate`, `number`, optional): 
This question is asking about the current turnover rate in your team or department. Please enter a numerical value to represent this rate.

### 2. **Retention Strategies** (`retention_strategies`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
This question asks about the retention strategies you have employed. Please select all the options that apply to you.

*   **Good**: We have employed good retention strategies such as offering competitive pay and benefits.
*   **Fair**: We have employed fair retention strategies such as providing opportunities for growth and development.
*   **Other**: (Please specify)

### 3. **Challenges** (`challenges`, `select_one`, optional): 
This question asks about the most common type of challenge faced by employees. Please select the option that best represents your experience.

*   **Option 1**: We have faced challenges such as limited training opportunities.
*   **Option 2**: We have faced challenges such as lack of clear expectations.
*   **Option 3**: We have faced challenges such as poor communication.

### 4. **Strategies Used** (`strategies_used`, `select_one`, optional): 
This question asks about the most common type of retention strategy employed by you. Please select the option that best represents your experience.

*   **Option 1**: We have employed strategies such as offering bonuses and incentives.
*   **Option 2**: We have employed strategies such as providing flexible work arrangements.
*   **Option 3**: We have employed strategies such as recognizing and rewarding employee contributions.

### 5. **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
This question asks about the most common type of feedback given to employees. Please select all the options that apply to you.

*   **Yes**: We receive feedback from employees on their performance.
*   **No**: We do not receive feedback from employees on their performance.
*   **Other**: (Please specify)

### 6. **Organizational Challenges** (`organizational_challenges`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
This question asks about the most common type of organizational challenge you face. Please select all the options that apply to you.

*   **Yes**: We face organizational challenges such as budget constraints.
*   **No**: We do not face organizational challenges such as budget constraints.
*   **Other**: (Please specify)

### 7. **Employee Departures** (`employee_departures`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
This question asks about the most common type of reason for employee departures. Please select all the options that apply to you.

*   **Yes**: We have seen employee departures due to lack of challenge.
*   **No**: We have not seen employee departures due to lack of challenge.
*   **Other**: (Please specify)

### 8. **Retention Programs** (`retention_programs`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
This question asks about the types of retention programs you participate in. Please select all the options that apply to you.

*   **Yes**: We participate in retention programs such as training and development.
*   **No**: We do not participate in retention programs such as training and development.
*   **Other**: (Please specify)

## Tips

*   Please be honest and complete the form as accurately as possible.
*   If you are unsure about any of the questions, please select "Other" and specify your thoughts.
*   If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to your manager or HR department.
