# Light Pollution Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Light Pollution Awareness Survey is a tool used to gather information about the use of outdoor lighting in residential or commercial settings and its impact on the night-time environment. This survey is designed to help understand the current state of light pollution and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please answer each question carefully and accurately, based on your personal experience or knowledge.
* If you have any questions or concerns about a particular question, please contact the form administrators before proceeding.
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* If a question is not applicable to your situation, please select "Other" or "N/A" as a placeholder, and briefly explain why you've chosen this option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required: false): This is an optional text field where you can briefly introduce yourself or provide any background information that might be relevant to your answers. Please keep your introduction concise and to the point.

* **Night Time Lighting** (`nighttime_lighting`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select one of the following options:
	+ True: You use or have used nighttime lighting in your current or past residential or commercial setting.
	+ False: You do not use or have not used nighttime lighting in your current or past residential or commercial setting.
	+ Other: You use or have used nighttime lighting, but the options above do not apply to your situation.

* **Sky Glow** (`skyglow`, `text`, required: false): This field is optional and allows you to describe the amount of light pollution you've observed in your current or past residential or commercial settings. Please be as detailed as possible, providing a brief but descriptive answer.

* **Sky Glow Level** (`skyglow_level`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all that apply from the following options:
	+ None: You have not observed any skylight pollution in your current or past residential or commercial settings.
	+ Low: You have observed some light pollution, but it is relatively low.
	+ Moderate: You have observed noticeable light pollution, but it is not extreme.
	+ High: You have observed extreme light pollution.

* **Energy Use** (`energy_use`, `number`, required: false): This field is optional and asks for a numerical value representing your energy use in terms of watt-hours per night. If you're unsure or do not have access to this information, please answer "N/A".

* **Energy Source** (`energy_source`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select one of the following energy sources you've used or are familiar with:
	+ Electricity
	+ Gas
	+ Solar
	+ Other

* **Submit** (`submit`, `note`, required: false): Please use this field to submit your responses. 

This help guide has been created to assist you in completing the Light Pollution Awareness Survey accurately and effectively. If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the form administrators.
