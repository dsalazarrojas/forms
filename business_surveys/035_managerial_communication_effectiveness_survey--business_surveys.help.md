# Managerial Communication Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of managerial communication within our organization. Your honest feedback will help us improve communication and make our organization a better place to work.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the options that best represent your thoughts and feelings about the statements.
2. For text fields, please provide your answer in the box.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **page_one** (page 1, note, no): This is the first page of the survey, which provides some background information. Please read it carefully and proceed to the next section.
* **employee_information** (page 2, email, no): Please enter your email address to help us keep your responses anonymous.
* **question_1** (page 3, select_one, no): How often do you feel your manager communicates with you? 
  * Strongly Agree: I feel my manager communicates with me often enough.
  * Somewhat Agree: I feel my manager communicates with me somewhat often.
  * Neither Agree nor Disagree: My manager does not communicate with me often, but not rarely either.
  * Somewhat Disagree: I feel my manager does not communicate with me often enough.
  * Strongly Disagree: I feel my manager rarely communicates with me.
* **question_2** (page 4, select_multiple, no): How would you rate your satisfaction with your manager's communication style?
  * Strongly Agree: I am very satisfied with my manager's communication style.
  * Somewhat Agree: I am somewhat satisfied with my manager's communication style.
  * Neither Agree nor Disagree: My manager's communication style is neutral.
  * Somewhat Disagree: I am somewhat dissatisfied with my manager's communication style.
  * Strongly Disagree: I am very dissatisfied with my manager's communication style.
* **question_3** (page 5, time, no): How much time do you spend communicating with your manager each week?
  * Please answer in hours.
* **question_4** (page 6, date, no): When was your most recent communication with your manager?
  * Please answer in date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **question_5** (page 7, text, no): What do you think is the most effective way for your manager to communicate with you?
* **question_6** (page 8, text, no): What do you think would improve the communication between you and your manager?
* **question_7** (page 9, text, no): Are there any other comments or suggestions you would like to share about your manager's communication?
* **question_8** (page 10, text, no): Do you have any other feedback or thoughts on how to improve managerial communication?
