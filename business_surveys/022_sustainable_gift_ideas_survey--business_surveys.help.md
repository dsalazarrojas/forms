# Sustainable Gift Ideas Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your gifting habits, preferences, and behaviors to help us create more effective and sustainable gifting solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully and answer each question based on your true preferences and habits.
2. Select all that apply for the "What motivates you to give gifts?" question.
3. Choose one option that best describes your preference for each question that requires selection.
4. Enter a number in the "What is your budget for gifting?" field.
5. For the open-ended question "Do you have any open-ended gift ideas?", type out your idea in the text field.
6. For any questions that are not relevant or do not apply to you, simply select "Rarely" and move on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Welcome to the Sustainable Gift Ideas Survey (Page 1)
* **Welcome to the Sustainable Gift Ideas Survey**: This page simply welcomes you to the survey and provides a brief introduction.

### What motivates you to give gifts? (Page 2)
* **What motivates you to give gifts?**: This question is asking about your reasons for giving gifts. Select all the options that apply to you, such as:
	+ To show love and appreciation
	+ To celebrate special occasions
	+ To support a cause or charity
	+ To build relationships
	+ Other (please specify)

### What type of gift do you like to give? (Page 3)
* **What type of gift do you like to give?**: This question is asking about your gift preference. Choose one option that best describes your preference:
	+ Experience gift
	+ Personalized gift
	+ Sustainable gift
	+ Other (please specify)

### How often do you gift? (Page 4)
* **How often do you gift?**: This question is asking about your gifting frequency. Select one option that best describes your gifting frequency:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Yearly
	+ Rarely

### What is your budget for gifting? (Page 5)
* **What is your budget for gifting?**: This question is asking about your budget for gifting. Enter a number in dollars.

### How often do you give this type of gift? (Page 6)
* **How often do you give this type of gift?**: This question is asking about your gifting frequency for this specific type of gift. Select one option that best describes your gifting frequency:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Yearly
	+ Rarely

### Do you have any open-ended gift ideas? (Page 7)
* **Do you have any open-ended gift ideas?**: This question is asking for any gift ideas that you have. Please describe your idea in the text field.

### Gifting Frequency3 (Page 8)
* **Gifting Frequency3**: This question is asking about your gifting frequency for this specific type of gift. Select one option that best describes your gifting frequency:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Yearly
	+ Rarely

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and accurately based on your true preferences and habits.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* This survey is designed to be completed in about 10-15 minutes.
* Thank you for taking the time to help us understand your gifting habits and preferences!
