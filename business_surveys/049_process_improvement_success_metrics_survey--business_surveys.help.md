# Process Improvement Success Metrics Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on process improvement success metrics. It is intended for employees to provide their thoughts and opinions on the effectiveness of our current processes. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your role from the options provided in the first field.
2. Enter your responses to the first field (open text field).
3. Enter your numerical responses to the second and third fields.
4. Select one of the options provided in the fourth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role?**
	+ Select One of:
		- Leader
		- Team Member
		- Other
	+ This field is used to categorize your position within the organization.
* **First Field**
	+ A text field for you to provide your thoughts and opinions.
	+ Please keep your response concise, around 1-2 paragraphs.
* **second field**
	+ A numerical field for you to provide a quantity value.
	+ Please enter a whole number only.
* **third field**
	+ A numerical field for you to provide another quantity value.
	+ Please enter a whole number only.
* **fourth field**
	+ Select One of:
		- Select One Option 1
		- Select One Option 2
	+ This field is used to gather additional feedback.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer honestly and accurately.
- You can refer to the process improvement success metrics documentation for more information on the metrics we are trying to measure.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your team lead or process owner.
- Please keep your response private and confidential.
