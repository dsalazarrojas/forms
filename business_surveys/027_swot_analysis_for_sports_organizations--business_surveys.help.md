# SWOT Analysis For Sports Organizations - Help Guide

## Purpose
The SWOT Analysis For Sports Organizations form is designed to help sports organizations assess their internal and external strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats. This form guides you through a structured analysis process to identify key areas of improvement and potential challenges that may impact your organization's performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions based on your sports organization's current situation.
2. Be honest and thorough in your answers, as the results will be used to inform decision-making and improvement efforts.
3. Use the provided fields to describe each strength, weakness, opportunity, and threat in detail.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Strengths** (`strengths`, `text`, required: false): Describe your sports organization's internal and external strengths that help you achieve your goals.
- **Weaknesses** (`weaknesses`, `text`, required: false): Identify internal weaknesses that hinder your organization's performance.
- **Opportunities** (`opportunities`, `text`, required: false): Describe external opportunities that can help your organization grow or improve.
- **Threats** (`threats`, `text`, required: false): Identify external threats that may impact your organization's performance.
- **Internal Factors** (`internal_factors`, `text`, required: false): Describe internal factors that influence your organization's performance.
- **External Factors** (`external_factors`, `text`, required: false): Describe external factors that impact your organization's performance.
- **Internal Threats** (`internal_threats`, `text`, required: false): Identify internal threats that hinder your organization's performance.
- **External Threats** (`external_threats`, `text`, required: false): Describe external threats that may impact your organization's performance.
- **SWOT Analysis** (`swot_analysis`, `text`, required: false): Summarize your analysis of strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.
- **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `text`, required: false): Suggest potential actions or changes to address identified areas of improvement.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or observations that may be helpful for future reference.
- **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `text`, required: false): Specify the user responsible for completing this form.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): Specify the tool used for completing this form.
- **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, required: false): Specify the file format of the output.
- **Created At** (`created_at`, `date`, required: false): Date and time when the form was created.
- **Updated At** (`updated_at`, `date`, required: false): Date and time when the form was last updated.

## Tips
- Be thorough and honest in your answers to ensure accurate analysis.
- Review and revise your answers as necessary.
- Use clear and concise language when describing each factor.
- This form is a guide, not a replacement for expert analysis or professional advice. If you're unsure about any aspect of your organization, consult with a relevant expert.
