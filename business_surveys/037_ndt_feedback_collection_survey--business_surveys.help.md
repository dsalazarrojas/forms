# NDT Feedback Collection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with the NDT team, including their services used, rating, and other relevant information. The purpose of this form is to gather insights on how the team can improve its services and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

* Please fill out each section of the form completely, even if it's just to indicate that a service was not used.
* Answer each question to the best of your ability, and provide as much detail as you need to give a clear picture of your experience.
* If you are unsure about a question, feel free to ask for clarification or assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Experience** (`participant_experience`): Describe your overall experience with the NDT team. This is an opportunity to share your thoughts, feelings, and impressions of our services.
* **Services Used** (`services_used`): Select all services you have used from the list below. This will help us understand which services are being used and how effective they are.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Comments** (`comments`): Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about our services.
* **Rating** (`rating`): Rate your overall satisfaction with our services, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number, if you would like us to follow up with you.
* **Date of Last Contact** (`date_of_last_contact`): Enter the date of your last contact with the NDT team.
* **Time of Last Contact** (`time_of_last_contact`): Enter the time of your last contact with the NDT team.
* **Services Wanted** (`services_wanted`): Select the services you are interested in using in the future. This will help us understand your needs and expectations.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Comments Wanted** (`comments_wanted`): Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about services you are interested in using in the future.
* **Rating Wanted** (`rating_wanted`): Rate your interest level in services you are interested in using, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Email Address Wanted** (`email_address_wanted`): Enter your email address, if you would like to be contacted about these services.
* **Phone Number Wanted** (`phone_number_wanted`): Enter your phone number, if you would like to be contacted about these services.
* **Date of Last Contact Wanted** (`date_of_last_contact_wanted`): Enter the date of your last contact with the NDT team regarding these services.
* **Time of Last Contact Wanted** (`time_of_last_contact_wanted`): Enter the time of your last contact with the NDT team regarding these services.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort in completing this form.
