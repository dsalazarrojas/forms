# Internal Training Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants about their experience with internal training sessions. It aims to assess the usefulness, satisfaction, and overall impact of the training sessions on the participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the response for each question based on your experience with the internal training sessions.
2. Fill in the text fields with your opinions and comments.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### How useful were the training sessions? (Required)
* **How useful were the training sessions?** (`training_usefulness`, select_one, required): Rate the usefulness of the training sessions. Choose 'Yes' if you found the sessions useful, and 'No' otherwise.

### What is your level of satisfaction with the training?
* **What is your level of satisfaction with the training?** (`satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the training on a scale from 'Very Good' to 'Bad'.

### Did you gain any new skills through the training?
* **Did you gain any new skills through the training?** (`skill_gain`, select_multiple, required): Check 'Yes' if you gained new skills, and 'No' otherwise.

### Note (Optional)
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any comments or additional information about the training.

### Email Address (Required)
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address.

### Phone Number (Optional)
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).

### Date (Required)
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the training session.

### Time (Required)
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the training session.

### How would you rate the overall satisfaction with the training?
* **How would you rate the overall satisfaction with the training?** (`training_satisfaction_scale`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the training on a scale from 1 to 5.

### Level of skill gained (Required)
* **Level of skill gained** (`skill_level`, number, required): Rate the level of skill gained through the training on a scale from 1 to 5.

### How would you rate the workshop organizers?
* **How would you rate the workshop organizers?** (`workshop_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Rate the workshop organizers on a scale from 'Good' to 'Fair'.

### How would you rate the quality of the workshop?
* **How would you rate the quality of the workshop?** (`workshop_quality`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of the workshop on a scale from 'Good' to 'Poor'.

### Were the workshop materials good?
* **Were the workshop materials good?** (`workshop_materials`, select_one, optional): Check 'Yes' if the materials were good, and 'No' otherwise.

### Was the workshop duration optimal?
* **Was the workshop duration optimal?** (`workshop_duration`, select_one, optional): Check 'Yes' if the duration was optimal, and 'No' otherwise.

### How often would you attend workshops?
* **How often would you attend workshops?** (`workshop_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Check 'Yes' if you would attend workshops often.

### What topics were covered in the workshop?
* **What topics were covered in the workshop?** (`workshop_content`, text, required): Provide a comma-separated list of the topics covered in the workshop.

### What was the workshop venue?
* **What was the workshop venue?** (`workshop_venue`, text, optional): Enter the workshop location.

### How would you rate the facilitator?
* **How would you rate the facilitator?** (`workshop_facilitator`, select_one, optional): Rate the facilitator on a scale from 'Good' to 'Fair'.

### Facilitator Name (Optional)
* **Facilitator name** (`workshop_facilitator_name`, text, optional): Enter the facilitator's name.

### Facilitator Title (Optional)
* **Facilitator title** (`workshop_facilitator_title`, text, optional): Enter the facilitator's title.
