# urban_sustainability_awareness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about urban sustainability awareness and behaviors from users.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Details section with your name, email, and any other relevant information.
2. In the Awareness section, provide your level of awareness about urban sustainability. You can choose from three levels: Very High, Medium, or Low.
3. In the Behaviors section, select the actions you take in relation to urban sustainability. You can choose from three options: Very High, Medium, or Low.
4. In the Interest section, indicate your level of interest in urban sustainability, choosing from Strong, Neutral, or Not Very.
5. In the Knowledge section, enter a number to represent your level of knowledge about urban sustainability.
6. Enter scores 1, 2, and 3 to indicate your level of agreement with certain statements about urban sustainability.
7. In the Date and Time section, enter the date and time you completed the form.
8. In the Email and Phone sections, enter your contact information.
9. In the Note section, enter any additional comments or feedback you have.
10. In the User Notes section, enter any further notes or comments.
11. In the City, Organization, and Assigned To sections, select your relevant information from the provided options.
12. In the Assigned Tool and Output File sections, select your preferred options.
13. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant information.
* **Awareness**: Choose your level of awareness about urban sustainability (Very High, Medium, or Low).
* **Behaviors**: Select the actions you take in relation to urban sustainability (Very High, Medium, or Low).
* **Interest**: Indicate your level of interest in urban sustainability (Strong, Neutral, or Not Very).
* **Knowledge**: Enter a number to represent your level of knowledge about urban sustainability.
* **Score 1**, **Score 2**, and **Score 3**: Enter numbers to indicate your level of agreement with certain statements about urban sustainability.
* **Date**: Enter the date you completed the form.
* **Time**: Enter the time you completed the form.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **User Notes**: Enter any further notes or comments.
* **City**: Select your city of residence.
* **Organization**: Select your type of organization (Cities, NGOs, Schools, or ESG Teams).
* **Assigned To**: Select the person you want to be assigned to (chatjimmy or chatjimmy2).
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool you want to be assigned (chatjimmy or other).
* **Output File**: Select the output file you want to receive (034 urban sustainability awareness survey--business surveys or other).
* **Assigned Category**: Select the category you want to be assigned to (Business Surveys or other).

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are filling out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your supervisor or the form's administrator.
* The form is designed to be completed by one person at a time.
* If you need help or have questions, please contact your supervisor or the form's administrator.
