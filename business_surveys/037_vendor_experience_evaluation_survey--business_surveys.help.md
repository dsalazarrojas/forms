# Vendor Experience Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Experience Evaluation Survey is designed to collect feedback from vendors or their representatives on their experience with the service provided by our organization. This form helps us identify areas of improvement and areas of strength in our interactions with vendors.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer all questions as honestly as possible.
* Fill in the required fields (marked as `true` in the form).
* You can select multiple options for select_multiple fields.
* If you need to add extra information that doesn't fit in one of the fields, you can write it in the `comments2` and `comments3` fields.
* Once you've completed all the fields, click submit to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Vendor Experience Evaluation** (`vendor_experience_evaluation`)
------------------------------------------

* A free-text field for you to describe your experience with our organization.

### 2. **Satisfaction with Vendor** (`satisfaction_with_vendor`)
-----------------------------------------------

* Rate your overall satisfaction with the vendor on a scale of 1-5 (1 = Not satisfied, 2 = Somewhat satisfied, 3 = Neutral, 4 = Somewhat dissatisfied, 5 = Very satisfied).

### 3. **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`)
-------------------------------

* Select all areas where the vendor could have done better. You can choose one or more:
	+ Communication
	+ Quality
	+ Cost

### 4. **Comments** (`comments`)
-------------------------

* Add any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with the vendor.

### 5. **Email** (`email`)
-----------------------

* Enter the email of the vendor or their representative who completed this form.

### 6. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`)
-------------------------------

* Enter the phone number of the vendor or their representative who completed this form.

### 7. **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`)
-------------------------

* Enter the name of the vendor or their representative who completed this form.

### 8. **Vendor Contact Person** (`vendor_contact_person`)
----------------------------------------------

* Enter the name of the person you usually contact with the vendor.

### 9. **Contact Method** (`contact_method`)
--------------------------------------

* Select how you contact the vendor:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ In Person

### 10. **Vendor Rating** (`vendor_rating`)
-------------------------

* Rate the quality of the service provided by the vendor on a scale of 1-5 (1 = Poor, 2 = Average, 3 = Good, 4 = Excellent, 5 = Outstanding).

### 11. **Vendor Recommendation** (`vendor_recommendation`)
-----------------------------------------

* Select if you would highly recommend, not recommend, or are neutral about recommending the vendor. You can select one or more:
	+ Highly
	+ Not
	+ Neither

### 12. **Comments 2** (`comments2`)
---------------------------

* Add any additional comments or feedback you have about the vendor.

### 13. **Rating** (`rating`)
-------------------------

* Rate the overall experience with the vendor on a scale of 1-5 (1 = Poor, 2 = Average, 3 = Good, 4 = Excellent, 5 = Outstanding).

### 14. **Comments 3** (`comments3`)
---------------------------

* Add any additional comments or feedback you have about the vendor.
