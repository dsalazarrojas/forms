# Government Store Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Store Management Survey is designed to collect feedback from store managers and customers to improve store operations and customer service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form page-by-page.
2. Each page will ask you for specific information about your store and feedback.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information for each field.
4. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Location** (`store_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of your store. This will help us understand which location you are providing feedback for.
* **Manager ID** (`manager_id`, number, required: true): Enter the ID of your store manager. This will help us track your manager's performance and ensure we are addressing the correct manager's feedback.
* **Store Manager ID** (`store_manager_id`, number, required: false): Enter the ID of your store manager. This field is similar to the previous one, but it's optional.
* **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, number, required: true): Rate how often you provide feedback to your store. This will help us understand your feedback pattern and improve our services.
* **Feedback Method** (`feedback_method`, text, required: false): Choose how you usually provide feedback to your store. This will help us understand your preferred communication channel.
* **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of feedback you want to provide. Please choose one from the following options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, number, required: false): Enter the number of hours your store operates. This will help us understand your store's operational hours.
* **Store Hours Sunday** (`store_hours_sunday`, number, required: false): Enter the number of hours your store operates on Sundays. This will help us understand your store's operational hours on Sundays.
* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, date, required: true): Enter the date you are providing feedback for.
* **Feedback Time** (`feedback_time`, time, required: true): Enter the time you are providing feedback for.
* **Feedback Note** (`feedback_note`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about your feedback.
* **Feedback Email** (`feedback_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further information.
* **Feedback Phone** (`feedback_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for further information.
* **Feedback Location** (`feedback_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where you are providing feedback.
* **Store Manager Note** (`store_manager_note`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about your store manager.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information for each field.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact our support team.
