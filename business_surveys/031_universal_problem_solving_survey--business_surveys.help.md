# Universal Problem Solving Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Universal Problem Solving Survey is a self-assessment tool that helps employees evaluate their ability to tackle common business problems and their job satisfaction level.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully and honestly.
2. Answer all questions to the best of your ability.
3. Use your judgment when selecting the options for the select_multiple fields.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please leave it blank or provide an explanation in the text fields.
