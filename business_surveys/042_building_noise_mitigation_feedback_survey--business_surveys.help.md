# Building Noise Mitigation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect feedback from you, as an occupant of the building, on the noise levels and mitigation measures in place in the building. Your input will help us understand how noise affects you and identify areas for improvement to create a quieter and more comfortable environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing honest and detailed feedback.
2. Answer each question based on your personal experience in the building.
3. Select the most suitable options for your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as an occupant of the building.
* **Email address** (`respondent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to allow us to follow up with you if needed.
* **Floor or area** (`floor_area`, text, required): Type the name or number of the floor or area where you occupy.
* **Occupancy duration** (`occupancy_duration`, select_one, required): Choose how long you have occupied this space.
	+ Less than 3 months
	+ 3-6 months
	+ 6-12 months
	+ 1-2 years
	+ More than 2 years
* **Type of space** (`space_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of space you occupy.
	+ Office
	+ Residential apartment
	+ Retail
	+ Industrial
	+ Educational
	+ Healthcare
	+ Hospitality
	+ Other
* **Noise Perception** (`noise_perception`, note, required): Please describe your overall perception of the noise levels in your space.
* **Overall noise level** (`overall_noise_level`, select_one, required): Choose the overall noise level in your space.
	+ Very quiet - ideal
	+ Quiet - acceptable
	+ Moderate - sometimes noisy
	+ Loud - frequently disruptive
	+ Very loud - constantly disruptive
* **Noise impact** (`noise_impact`, select_one, required): Choose the impact of noise on your activities.
	+ No impact - very quiet
	+ Minimal impact
	+ Moderate impact
	+ Significant impact
	+ Severe impact - very disruptive
* **Primary sources of noise** (`primary_noise_sources`, select_multiple, required): Select all the primary sources of unwanted noise you experience.
	+ Voices and conversations
	+ HVAC systems
	+ Machinery or equipment
	+ Building structure sounds
	+ Outside traffic
	+ Music or entertainment
	+ Footsteps or movement
	+ Construction
	+ Plumbing
	+ Other
* **Noise frequency** (`noise_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose the frequency of noise problems.
	+ Never
	+ Rarely - occasional
	+ Sometimes - weekly
	+ Frequently - daily
	+ Constantly - throughout the day
* **Time of day** (`time_of_day`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the times of day when noise is most problematic.
	+ Early morning
	+ Mid-morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Early evening
	+ Late evening
	+ Night
	+ All times equally
	+ No specific pattern
* **Current noise mitigation measures** (`current_measures`, select_multiple, required): Select all the current measures you are using to reduce noise.
	+ Soundproofing materials
	+ Acoustic panels
	+ Noise barriers
	+ Insulation upgrades
	+ HVAC modifications
	+ Double-pane windows
	+ Door seals
	+ Carpeting
	+ Rugs and furniture
	+ White noise machines
	+ None
	+ Unsure
* **Effectiveness of current measures** (`measure_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Choose how effective you find the current measures in reducing noise.
	+ Very effective - significant improvement
	+ Effective - noticeable improvement
	+ Somewhat effective - minor improvement
	+ Not effective - no improvement
	+ No measures in place
* **Satisfaction with noise control efforts** (`satisfaction_with_measures`, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied you are with the current noise control efforts.
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Additional improvements needed** (`additional_improvements`, select_multiple, required): Select all the additional improvements you think are needed to reduce noise.
	+ Better insulation
	+ Acoustic treatments
	+ HVAC improvements
	+ Window upgrades
	+ Door improvements
	+ Structural soundproofing
	+ Noise policies enforcement
	+ Quiet areas designation
	+ Sound masking systems
	+ Construction noise control
	+ Other
* **Top priority improvements** (`priority_improvements`, text, required): Describe your top priorities for noise reduction.
* **Realistic goals for noise reduction** (`realistic_expectations`, text, optional): Describe any realistic goals you have for noise reduction.
* **Support for improvements even if costly** (`budget_for_improvements`, select_one, optional): Choose your level of support for noise-reducing improvements, even if they come at a cost.
	+ Yes, very supportive
	+ Somewhat supportive
	+ Neutral
	+ Reluctant
	+ No, too expensive
* **Personal measures for noise reduction** (`personal_actions`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the personal measures you use to reduce noise.
	+ Wear headphones
	+ Use earplugs
	+ Sound machines
	+ Quiet hours at home
	+ Adjust schedule
	+ Move to quieter location
	+ Close doors
	+ Use rugs/curtains
	+ Nothing
	+ Other
* **Willingness to cooperate** (`willingness_cooperate`, select_one, optional): Choose how willing you are to follow noise reduction guidelines.
	+ Yes, very willing
	+ Willing
	+ Neutral
	+ Reluctant
	+ 'False'
* **Awareness of building noise policies** (`policy_awareness`, select_one, required): Choose how aware you are of building noise policies.
	+ Yes, clearly understood
	+ Yes, somewhat aware
	+ Vaguely aware
	+ Not aware
	+ No policies exist
* **Noise policy enforcement** (`policy_enforcement`, select_one, required): Choose how well the noise policies are enforced.
	+ Strictly enforced
	+ Generally enforced
	+ Inconsistently enforced
	+ Rarely enforced
	+ Not enforced
	+ Unsure
* **Effectiveness of noise complaint process** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Choose how effective you find the noise complaint process.
	+ Very effective - issues resolved
	+ Effective - usually resolved
	+ Somewhat effective
	+ Not effective
	+ No complaint process
* **Specific suggestions for noise reduction** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, required): Describe specific suggestions you have for noise reduction.
* **Suggested noise policies or changes** (`policy_suggestions`, text, optional): Describe any noise policies or changes you suggest.
* **Additional comments about noise in building** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about noise in the building.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and thoroughly to help us understand your experience with noise in the building.
* If you are unsure about any options, choose the one that best represents your experience.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and your input will help us improve the building environment.
