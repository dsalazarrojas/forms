# Preference Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preference Selection Survey is used to collect information about users' preferences, such as language, product, and category selections. This information will help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category that best fits your preferences (Category field).
2. Choose the product that you are interested in (Product field).
3. Choose the language in which you would like to receive our communication (Language field).
4. Optionally, enter any additional comments you have (Comments field).
5. Optionally, select the date and time of your preference (Date and Time fields).
6. Enter your contact phone number, if you would like us to get in touch with you (Phone field).
7. Enter your email address (Email field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Category** (`preference_selection_survey_category`, select_multiple): Select one or more categories that best fit your preferences.
- **Product** (`preference_selection_survey_product`, select_one): Choose the product that you are interested in.
- **Language** (`preference_selection_survey_language`, select_one): Select the language in which you would like to receive our communication.
- **Comments** (`preference_selection_survey_comments`, text): Enter any additional comments you have about your preferences.
- **Date** (`preference_selection_survey_date`, date): Optionally, select a date when you would like to receive our communication.
- **Time** (`preference_selection_survey_time`, time): Optionally, select a time when you would like to receive our communication.
- **Phone** (`preference_selection_survey_phone`, number): Enter your contact phone number, if you would like us to get in touch with you.
- **Email** (`preference_selection_survey_email`, email): Enter your email address (you can enter both email fields, but only the second field will be saved).

## Tips
- You are not required to fill out this form, and your responses will only be used for internal purposes.
- You can always contact us directly if you need help or clarification about any field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [insert contact email].
