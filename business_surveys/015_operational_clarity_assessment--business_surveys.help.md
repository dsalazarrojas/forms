# Operational Clarity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Operational Clarity Assessment form is designed to help you evaluate the operational clarity within your team or business unit. It covers essential aspects such as goals, communication, resources, and employee insights to provide a comprehensive view of operational clarity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best describe your current situation for each field.
2. Provide detailed answers for the text fields (communication, Operational Gap, Employee Insights, and Feedback).
3. Be as accurate and honest as possible in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Goals** (Select Multiple):
	+ Select all goals that are relevant to your current operational clarity evaluation.
	+ If none of the options apply, select "None".
* **Roles** (Text):
	+ Describe the different roles involved in your operational process.
* **communication** (Select One):
	+ Indicate if communication is clear or not among team members.
* **Resources** (Select Multiple):
	+ Check all the resources that are currently available or needed to improve operational clarity.
* **Employee Insights** (Select Multiple):
	+ Indicate if employee insights are available or not for operational clarity.
* **Operational Gap** (Text):
	+ Describe the gaps or issues that hinder operational clarity.
* **Clarity** (Select Multiple):
	+ Select all the factors that contribute to operational clarity.
* **Manager Involvement** (Select One):
	+ Indicate the level of manager involvement in operational clarity.
* **Consultant Involvement** (Select One):
	+ Indicate the level of consultant involvement in operational clarity.
* **Feedback** (Text):
	+ Provide detailed feedback on how to improve operational clarity.
* **Next Steps** (Select Multiple):
	+ Identify the next steps needed to improve operational clarity.
	+ If none, select "None".
