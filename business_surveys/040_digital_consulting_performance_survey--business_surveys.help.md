# Digital Consulting Performance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from clients on the digital consulting services provided by the agency. Your responses will help us understand your experiences and provide valuable insights to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the text fields provided.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best represents your experience or opinion.
4. If you have no experience or opinion, please select "Not sure" or "Other" as applicable.

* **Introduction** (Page 1): This page serves as an introduction to the form and provides a space for any initial comments or questions.
* **What did you learn from our services?** (Page 2): Please select all that apply from this list:
	+ Yes

* **Overall, how would you rate this agency?** (Page 5): Please provide a numerical rating from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

	+ True

* **Do you have any further comments about our services?** (Page 7): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **What was your favorite part about our services?** (Page 8): Please provide any information about your favorite part of our services.

	+ True
