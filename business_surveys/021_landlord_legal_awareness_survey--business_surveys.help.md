# Landlord Legal Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landlord Legal Awareness Survey is designed to gather information from landlords to assess their legal awareness and understanding of their responsibilities. This form helps us understand their needs and concerns, which can inform our policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply select the applicable options and provide any additional information where requested.

1. Start by selecting whether you have a property manager organization (yes or no).
2. Choose all that apply from the list of legal responsibilities that you believe you need training on.
3. If other legal responsibilities are not listed, specify them in the text field.
4. Select all that apply for any other training needs you have.
5. If you have a partnership, select yes or no.
6. If you have a partnership, enter the name of the organization you are partnering with.
7. Select yes or no for association.
8. If you have an association, enter the name of the organization you are associated with.
9. Select yes or no for certification.
10. Select yes or no for licensure.
11. Select yes or no for certification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Manager Organization**: Please enter the name of your property management organization if you have one.
* **Legal Responsibilities**: Choose all legal responsibilities that you believe you need training on. Select "Other" if none of the provided options apply.
* **Training Needs**: Choose all that apply for any other training needs you have.
* **Other Training Needs**: If you selected "Other" for Training Needs, please provide a brief description of what you need training on.
* **Has Partnership**: Select yes if you have a partnership.
* **Partnership Organization**: If you selected "Has Partnership", enter the name of the organization you are partnering with.
* **Has Association**: Select yes if you have an association.
* **Association Organization**: If you selected "Has Association", enter the name of the organization you are associated with.
* **Has Certificate**: Select yes if you have a certificate.
* **Has Licensure**: Select yes if you have a licensure.
* **Has Certification**: Select yes if you have a certification.
