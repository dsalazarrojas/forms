# Application Architect Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from application architects about their experiences, challenges, and preferences. It is intended to help organizations or communities better understand the current state and needs of their architecture community.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling in your name, the name of your organization, and your years of experience as an application architect.
2.  Choose your primary technology stack and select one preferred architecture style.
3.  Briefly describe your biggest challenge and what architecture goals you have for the next 12 months.
4.  Mention any scaling strategies you use and if you have any contact information for follow-up discussions.
5.  Complete all fields as accurately as possible to help provide valuable insights.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name (Respondent name, text, Optional - leave blank for anonymous)**: Enter your name or leave it blank if you prefer to remain anonymous.
* **Organization (Organization, text, Company or team name)**: This is your current or previous work place where you have applied your architectural skills. If you're a freelancer or consultant, enter a name that reflects your work.
* **Years of experience (Years of experience, number, Whole number of years)**: This is your total number of years you have been working as an application architect.
* **Primary technology stack (Primary technology stack, text, Main languages and frameworks used)**: Select one or list the main technologies you've worked with in your architectural work.
* **Preferred architecture style (Preferred architecture style, select_one, Choose the style most used)**: This is your preference or most used architectural style, e.g., Monolithic, Microservices, Event-driven, or Serverless.
* **Biggest challenge (Biggest challenge, text, Brief description of main challenges)**: Describe the biggest difficulty you've faced in your work as an application architect.
* **Architecture goals for next 12 months (Architecture goals for next 12 months, text, Key priorities and initiatives)**: Outline key initiatives or improvements you plan to focus on in the next 12 months.
* **Scaling strategies used (Scaling strategies used, text, How systems are scaled in practice)**: Describe how your architecture team or you scale systems in practice, e.g., horizontal, vertical, or through migration.
* **Contact for follow-up (Contact for follow-up, email, Provide email if follow up is welcome)**: Provide your email address if you're open to follow-up discussions or questions.
