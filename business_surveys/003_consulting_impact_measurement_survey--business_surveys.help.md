# Consulting Impact Measurement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients on the impact of consulting services provided to them. It is used to measure the effectiveness of the consultants and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name or company name in the "Client Name" field.
2. If you are unsure of the engagement period, you can leave the "Engagement Period" field blank.
3. Enter the name of your primary contact from the consulting firm in the "Project Consultant" field.
4. For the remaining fields, please provide your honest feedback on the consulting services provided to you. Please rate the professionalism and communication of the consultant from 1 to 5, where 5 is excellent.
5. Rate the expertise and timeliness of the consultant from 1 to 5, where 5 is excellent.
6. Describe the primary project outcome achieved through the consulting services in the "Primary Project Outcome" field.
7. Rate the impact of the consulting services on operational efficiency from 1 to 5, where 1 means no change and 5 means more than 25% improvement.
8. Select the correct answer regarding the impact on company revenue.
9. Please indicate your likelihood to recommend the consulting services to others using the Net Promoter Score.
10. Describe any areas for improvement in the "Areas for Improvement" field.
11. Select any future areas of consulting needs.
12. Indicate whether or not the firm can use your feedback as a case study.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter your name or company name.
* **Engagement Period**: Enter the month and year when the project took place. (optional)
* **Project Consultant**: Who was your primary contact?
* **Professionalism and Communication**: Rate from 1 to 5, where 5 is excellent.
* **Industry Knowledge and Expertise**: Rate from 1 to 5, where 5 is excellent.
* **Timeliness and Deadlines**: Rate from 1 to 5, where 5 is excellent.
* **Primary Project Outcome**: What was the main result achieved?
* **Impact on Operational Efficiency**: Rate from 1 to 5, where 1 means no change and 5 means more than 25% improvement.
* **Impact on Company Revenue**: Select the correct answer.
* **Likelihood to Recommend**: Select your Net Promoter Score.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Describe areas where the firm could improve.
* **Future Needs**: Select any future areas of consulting needs.
* **Testimonial or Case Study**: Indicate if the firm can use your feedback as a case study.
