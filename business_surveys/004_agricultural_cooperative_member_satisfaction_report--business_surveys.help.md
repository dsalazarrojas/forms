# Agricultural Cooperative Member Satisfaction Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from agricultural cooperative members to gauge their satisfaction with the services provided by the cooperative.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name as a member of the cooperative (optional).
2. Provide your cooperative membership number (if applicable).
3. Estimate the number of years you have been a member of the cooperative.
4. Choose your overall satisfaction level with the cooperative services.
5. Rate the quality of services received from the cooperative.
6. Evaluate the effectiveness of communication from the cooperative.
7. Share any suggestions or feedback for improvement.
8. Indicate your likelihood to recommend the cooperative to others.
9. Provide any additional comments or testimonials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Member name**: Enter your name, or simply leave this field blank if you prefer.
- **Member ID**: Enter your cooperative membership number, if you have one.
- **Membership duration in years**: Estimate the number of years you have been a member of the cooperative.
- **Overall satisfaction**: Choose your overall satisfaction level with the services provided by the cooperative. This will help us understand how well we are meeting your needs.
	+ Very satisfied: You are very happy with our services.
	+ Satisfied: You are somewhat happy with our services.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral about our services.
	+ Dissatisfied: You are somewhat unhappy with our services.
	+ Very dissatisfied: You are very unhappy with our services.
- **Quality of services received**: Rate the quality of services you received from the cooperative. This will help us understand how well we are performing.
	+ Excellent: You received excellent services from the cooperative.
	+ Good: You received good services from the cooperative.
	+ Fair: You received fair services from the cooperative.
	+ Poor: You received poor services from the cooperative.
- **Communication effectiveness**: Evaluate how well the cooperative communicates updates to you.
	+ Very effective: The cooperative communicated updates very effectively.
	+ Effective: The cooperative communicated updates effectively.
	+ Somewhat effective: The cooperative communicated updates somewhat effectively.
	+ Not effective: The cooperative did not communicate updates effectively.
- **Suggestions for improvement**: Share any ideas or feedback that can help the cooperative improve its services and communication.
- **Likelihood to recommend**: Indicate how likely you are to recommend the cooperative to others.
	+ Very likely: You are very likely to recommend the cooperative.
	+ Likely: You are likely to recommend the cooperative.
	+ Uncertain: You are uncertain about recommending the cooperative.
	+ Unlikely: You are unlikely to recommend the cooperative.
- **Additional comments**: Provide any additional comments or testimonials about your experience with the cooperative.
