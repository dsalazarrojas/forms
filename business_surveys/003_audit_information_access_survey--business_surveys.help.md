# Audit Information Access Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audit Information Access Survey is designed to gather information about access and permissions within a system or tool. This form will help assess the current state of access controls and identify potential security risks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page to understand the purpose and scope of the survey.
2. Enter your name and email address in the required fields.
3. Answer the questions about your access frequency and assessment of access controls.
4. Provide any additional information or suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Access and Permissions Survey --** (`intro_note`, `note`, required: false)
	+ This page provides context and an introduction to the survey.
* **respondent name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on follow-up communications.
* **respondent email** (`respondent_email`, `email`, required: false)
	+ Enter your work email address for follow-up communications and notifications.
* **System/Tool Access** (`system_accessed`, `text`, required: true)
	+ Enter the name of the system or tool being evaluated.
* **Access Frequency** (`access_frequency`, `select_one`, required: true)
	+ Select how often you access this information:
		- Daily
		- Weekly
		- Monthly
		- Rarely
* **Adequacy of Access Controls** (`adequacy_of_access_controls`, `select_one`, required: true)
	+ Select your assessment of the current access controls:
		- Strong
		- Adequate
		- Weak
		- Unknown
* **Incidents Reported Last Year** (`incidents_reported_last_year`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Enter the number of access incidents you are aware of.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Provide brief suggestions for improving information access controls.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Provide concrete examples and specific details where possible.
* Remember to keep your responses brief and to the point.
* If you have any suggestions or concerns, feel free to share them.
