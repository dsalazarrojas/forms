# Market Season Planning Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather insights into market season planning from business users. It is intended to be used by users to provide information on the seasons they are interested in planning for, specific dates related to the planning process, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the seasons you are interested in planning for from the "planning_season_interest" field.
2. Enter the specific dates related to the planning process in the "season_dates" field.
3. Choose the product you want to focus on from the "product_focus" field.
4. Provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improvements in the "improvements" field.
5. Add any additional feedback or comments in the "feedback" field.
6. Enter a valid email address in the "email" field.
7. Enter a valid phone number in the "phone" field.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the "note" field.
9. Enter the relevant dates for each date input field, if necessary (although note that the duplication of effort in this section may be unnecessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **planning_season_interest (Select Multiple)**: Select all the seasons you are interested in planning for.
* **season_dates (Number)**: Enter specific dates related to the planning process.
* **product_focus (Select One)**: Choose the product you want to focus on.
* **improvements (Text)**: Provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improvements.
* **feedback (Text)**: Add any additional feedback or comments.
* **email (Email)**: Enter a valid email address.
* **phone (Text)**: Enter a valid phone number.
* **note (Note)**: Add any additional notes or comments.
* **dates_1 (Date)**: Enter a specific date related to the planning process.
* **dates_2 (Date)**: Enter another specific date related to the planning process.
* **dates_3 (Date)**: Enter yet another specific date related to the planning process.
* **dates_4 (Date)**: Enter a fourth specific date related to the planning process.
* **dates_5 (Date)**: Enter a fifth specific date related to the planning process.
* **dates_6 (Date)**: Enter a sixth specific date related to the planning process.
* **dates_7 (Date)**: Enter a seventh specific date related to the planning process.
* **dates_8 (Date)**: Enter an eighth specific date related to the planning process.
