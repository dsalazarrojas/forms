# Agency Collaboration Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from internal project managers on the performance and partnership quality of external agencies. This information will help to identify strengths and areas for improvement for future collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the internal project manager's name under "Internal Project Manager Name".
2. Type in the name of the external agency under "Name of the External Agency".
3. Enter the project name or ID under "Project Name or ID".
4. Select the completion date of the project under "Project Completion Date".
5. Evaluate the quality of deliverables provided by the agency under "Quality of deliverables provided by the agency".
6. Rate the agency's adherence to timelines and deadlines under "Adherence to project timelines and deadlines".
7. Assess the agency's communication frequency and clarity under "Communication frequency and clarity".
8. Rate the agency's budget management and transparency under "Budget management and transparency".
9. Evaluate the agency's proactivity in problem-solving under "Proactivity in problem-solving".
10. Rate the overall value for money under "Overall value for money".
11. Specify the agency's strengths under "What did the agency do particularly well?" (if applicable).
12. Identify areas for improvement under "What areas should the agency focus on for improvement?" (if applicable).
13. Finally, select your likelihood of collaborating with this agency again under "Likelihood of collaborating with this agency again".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 2. Internal Project Manager Name
* **Internal Project Manager Name** (`Internal Project Manager Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your internal project manager's name.

### 3. Name of the External Agency
* **Name of the External Agency** (`agency_name`, `text`, required): Type in the name of the external agency you worked with.

### 4. Project Name or ID
* **Project Name or ID** (`project_id`, `text`, required): Enter the name or ID of the project you've completed with the agency.

### 5. Project Completion Date
* **Project Completion Date** (`completion_date`, `date`, required): Select the completion date of the project.

### 6. Quality of deliverables provided by the agency
* **Quality of deliverables provided by the agency** (`quality_deliverables`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the quality of the deliverables provided by the agency.

### 7. Adherence to project timelines and deadlines
* **Adherence to project timelines and deadlines** (`timeline_adherence`, `select_one`, required): Rate the agency's adherence to project timelines and deadlines.

### 8. Communication frequency and clarity
* **Communication frequency and clarity** (`communication_clarity`, `select_one`, required): Assess the agency's communication frequency and clarity.

### 9. Budget management and transparency
* **Budget management and transparency** (`budget_management`, `select_one`, required): Rate the agency's budget management and transparency.

### 10. Proactivity in problem-solving
* **Proactivity in problem-solving** (`problem_solving`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the agency's proactivity in problem-solving.

### 11. Overall value for money
* **Overall value for money** (`value_money`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the overall value for money.

### 12. What did the agency do particularly well?
* **What did the agency do particularly well?** (`agency_strengths`, `text`, optional): If applicable, specify the agency's strengths.

### 13. What areas should the agency focus on for improvement?
* **What areas should the agency focus on for improvement?** (`improvement_areas`, `text`, optional): If applicable, specify the agency's areas for improvement.

### 14. Likelihood of collaborating with this agency again
* **Likelihood of collaborating with this agency again** (`future_collaboration`, `select_one`, required): Select your likelihood of collaborating with this agency again.
