# innovation_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The innovation audit form is a tool to help business leaders assess and evaluate their organization's innovation processes, goals, and challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete the form in order, starting from the "Business Surveys" section.
2. Answer each question carefully, taking into consideration the field's description and required fields.
3. Review your progress regularly to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Surveys**: This section is intended to gather information about your company's current state. Please answer the questions thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
	+ **Company Name**: The name of your company.
	+ **Email**: Your work email address.
	+ **Phone**: Your work phone number.
	+ **Date**: The current date (yyyy-mm-dd).
	+ **Time**: The current time (hh:mm).
	+ **Note**: Any additional comments or notes regarding the innovation process.
* **Select Multiple**: You can choose one or multiple options from the list:
	+ Option 1: Select one option.
	+ Option 2: Select another option.
	+ Option 3: Select a third option.
* **Select One**: You can choose one option from the list:
	+ Option 1: Select one option.
	+ Option 2: Select another option.
	+ Option 3: Select a third option.
* **Decimal**: A numerical field for decimal numbers.
* **Integer**: A numerical field for whole numbers.
* **Time Zone**: The time zone of your location.
* **Date Time**: The current date and time (yyyy-mm-dd hh:mm).
* **Innovation Process**: A text field for describing your innovation process.
* **Survey Questions**: A text field for describing your survey questions.
* **Business Processes**: A text field for describing your business processes.
* **Innovation Ideas**: A text field for describing your innovation ideas.
* **Innovation Goals**: A text field for describing your innovation goals.
* **Business Objects**: A text field for describing your business objects.
* **Survey Results**: A text field for describing your survey results.
* **Feedback**: A text field for providing feedback.
* **Innovation Process Improvement**: A text field for describing how to improve your innovation process.
* **Innovation Leadership**: A text field for describing your innovation leadership.
* **Innovation Culture**: A text field for describing your innovation culture.
* **Innovation Roadmap**: A text field for describing your innovation roadmap.

Please note that all fields are optional, but providing accurate and thorough information is crucial for a successful innovation audit. If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to your innovation team for clarification or guidance.
