# workforce_agility_benchmark_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The workforce agility benchmark survey is designed to evaluate the current state of business agility and employee engagement across different departments within an organization. It assesses various aspects of business agility, including company awareness, team adaptability, change management, communication, and employee engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's purpose and scope to ensure you understand its goals and objectives.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. For questions with multiple options (e.g., "Adaptation and learning", "Collaboration and teamwork"), select all that apply.
4. For questions with numerical ratings (e.g., "Departmental Adaptability Score"), enter a number between 1 and 10, with 1 being Low and 10 being High.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Awareness** (`1`, text, optional): Describe how well-informed your department is about the company's goals, vision, and overall direction.
* **Team Adaptability** (`2`, text, optional): Share your department's ability to adapt to change, innovate, and stay up-to-date with industry trends.
* **Change Management** (`3`, text, optional): Explain how your department handles organizational change, such as restructuring, process updates, or policy changes.
* **Communication** (`4`, text, optional): Describe how information is shared and communicated within your department, including meetings, updates, and feedback loops.
* **Employee Engagement** (`5`, text, optional): Share how employees are invested in their work, participate in decision-making, and feel motivated to contribute to the organization.
* **Adaptability** (`6`, number, optional): Rate your department's adaptability on a scale from 1 (Low) to 10 (High).
* **Leadership** (`7`, text, optional): Describe your department's leadership style, including their ability to guide and motivate team members.
* **Feedback** (`8`, note, optional): Share any feedback or suggestions for improvement within your department.
* **Challenges** (`9`, select multiple, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Adaptation and learning
	+ Collaboration and teamwork
	+ Communication and information sharing
	+ Conflict resolution
	+ Decision-making and decision speed
	+ Flexibility and adaptability
	+ Organizational change management
	+ Time management
* **Departmental Adaptability** (`10`, select one, required): Rate your department's adaptability as:
	+ High
	+ Low
	+ None
* **Departmental Adaptability Score** (`11`, number, optional): Enter a numerical rating between 1 (Low) and 10 (High).
* **Departmental Leadership** (`12`, select one, required): Rate your department's leadership as:
	+ High
	+ Low
	+ None
* **Departmental Leadership Score** (`13`, number, optional): Enter a numerical rating between 1 (Low) and 10 (High).
* **Overall Adaptability** (`14`, number, optional): Rate your department's overall adaptability on a scale from 1 (Low) to 10 (High).
* **Overall Leadership** (`15`, number, optional): Rate your department's overall leadership on a scale from 1 (Low) to 10 (High).
* **Overall Score** (`16`, number, optional): Enter a numerical rating between 1 (Low) and 10 (High).
* **Action Plan** (`17`, text, optional): Describe any actions you plan to take based on the results of this survey.
* **Departmental Adaptability Scale** (`18`, text, optional): Describe your department's adaptability scale.
* **Departmental Leadership Scale** (`19`, text, optional): Describe your department's leadership scale.
* **Overall Adaptability Scale** (`20`, text, optional): Describe your department's overall adaptability scale.
* **Overall Leadership Scale** (`21`, text, optional): Describe your department's overall leadership scale.

* **Overall Leadership Score** (`23`, number, optional): Enter a numerical rating between 1 (Low) and 10 (High).
* **Overall Score 2** (`24`, number, optional): Enter a numerical rating between 1 (Low) and 10 (High).
* **Overall Score 3** (`25`, number, optional): Enter a numerical rating between 1 (Low) and 10 (High).
