# Corporate Social Responsibility Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Survey is a form designed to help organizations assess their commitment to social and environmental responsibility. It is used to evaluate the current state of CSR within a company and identify areas for improvement. This survey is essential for businesses to ensure that they are meeting their social and environmental obligations and to make informed decisions about future CSR initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have answered all questions honestly and accurately.
2. For each question, select the correct answer from the provided options.
3. If a question is not applicable, choose "Not Applicable" or "N/A".
4. Ensure that you have filled out all required questions (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**:
	+ **Page One** (text): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please provide a short description of your company's current CSR policy and practices.
* **Question 1: Does your company prioritize CSR?**
	+ **Question One** (select_one): This question asks whether your company prioritizes CSR. Choose "Yes" if your company has a strong focus on CSR, "No" if it does not.
* **Question 2: What is your company's current CSR strategy?**
	+ **Question Two** (select_multiple): This question is asking about your company's current CSR strategy. Please select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Question 3: What is the last date you conducted a CSR audit or review?**
	+ **Question Three** (date): This question is asking for the last date your company conducted a CSR audit or review. Please enter the date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Question 4: What time of day is your work day typically completed?**
	+ **Question Four** (time): This question is asking about your typical work day completion time. Please enter the time in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Question 5: Please provide any additional comments about your company's CSR practices.**
	+ **Question Five** (note): This is an open-ended question for any additional comments or information about your company's CSR practices.
* **Question 6: What is your company's email address for CSR-related inquiries?**
	+ **Question Six** (email): This question is asking for your company's email address associated with CSR-related inquiries. Please enter the address in the format "[email]@company.com".

## Tips
- Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any question, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
- This survey is confidential and your responses will be used to improve our company's CSR practices.
