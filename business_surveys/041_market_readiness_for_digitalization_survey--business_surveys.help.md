# Market Readiness For Digitalization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us assess your company's market readiness for digitalization. Your responses will help us understand your current state and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. For most questions, select the option that best describes your company's current state.
3. For questions with a numerical value (e.g., "Customer Satisfaction" and "Company Growth"), use numbers to indicate your company's current performance.
4. You can add any additional comments or thoughts in the "Submission Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Market Readiness Survey** (Page 1, Text Input)
	+ A brief description of your company's current market readiness for digitalization.
* **Company Details** (Page 1, Text Input)
	+ Provide your company's name.
* **Digital Transformation** (Page 2, Select One)
	+ Select the option that best describes your company's current level of digital transformation: 
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Business Model** (Page 2, Text Input)
	+ Describe your company's current business model.
* **Business Process** (Page 3, Text Input)
	+ Describe your company's current business processes.
* **Customer Experience** (Page 3, Select One)
	+ Select the option that best describes your company's current customer experience:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Employee Skills** (Page 4, Select Multiple)
	+ Select the skills that best describe your company's current employee skills:
	+ IT
	+ Finance
	+ Operations
	+ Other
* **Company Performance** (Page 4, Text Input)
	+ Describe your company's current performance.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (Page 5, Number)
	+ Enter a numerical value between 0 and 100 to indicate your company's current customer satisfaction.
* **Company Growth** (Page 5, Number)
	+ Enter a numerical value between 0 and 100 to indicate your company's current growth.
* **Market Trends** (Page 6, Time)
	+ Enter the date of your last market trends assessment.
* **Company Compliance** (Page 6, Select One)
	+ Select the option that best describes your company's current compliance level:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Digitalization Barometer Score** (Page 7, Number)
	+ Enter a numerical value between 0 and 100 to indicate your company's current digitalization readiness score.
* **Notes** (Page 7, Note)
	+ Add any additional comments or thoughts about your company's market readiness.
* **Email Address** (Page 8, Email)
	+ Enter your company's email address.
* **Phone Number** (Page 8, Text Input)
	+ Enter your company's phone number.
* **Submission Comments** (Page 9, Note)
	+ Add any additional comments or thoughts about the survey.
* **Company Name** (Page 9, Text Input)
	+ Enter your company's name.
* **Company Website** (Page 10, Text Input)
	+ Enter your company's website.
* **Business Unit** (Page 10, Text Input)
	+ Describe the business unit that submitted this survey.
* **Company Address** (Page 11, Text Input)
	+ Enter your company's address.
* **City** (Page 11, Text Input)
	+ Enter your city.
* **State** (Page 12, Text Input)
	+ Enter your state.
* **Country** (Page 12, Text Input)
	+ Enter your country.
* **Submission Date** (Page 13, Date)
	+ Select the date of submission.

Note: The fields that appear on each page are determined by the page ID number in the provided YAML form.
