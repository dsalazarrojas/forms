# Ice Skating Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers or employees on their ice skating experience at our facility. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and provide a better experience for all our visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select your level of satisfaction with the skating experience.
3. Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information or clarification.
4. Provide your phone number for us to reach out to you if needed.
5. Enter the date and time of your visit to the facility.
6. Enter the time you spent skating.
7. Share any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience.
8. Add any additional feedback you may have about your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Email (Email, <email>, required):** Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information or clarification about your feedback.
- **Phone (Phone, <text>, required):** Enter your phone number for us to reach out to you if needed.
- **Date (Date, <date>, required):** Enter the date of your visit to the facility.
- **Time (Time, <time>, required):** Enter the time you spent skating.
- **Skating Experience (Skating Experience, <select_multiple>, not required):** Select your level of satisfaction with the skating experience.
  - 1 - Very Satisfied
  - 2 - Somewhat Satisfied
  - 3 - Not Satisfied
  - 4 - Very Not Satisfied
- **Comments (Comments, <text>, not required):** Share any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience.
- **Additional Feedback (Additional Feedback, <note>, not required):** Add any additional feedback you may have about your visit.
- **What did you think (What did you think, <note>, not required):** Share any thoughts or opinions about your experience.
- **Additional Feedback (Additional Feedback, <note>, not required):** Add any additional feedback you may have about your visit.
