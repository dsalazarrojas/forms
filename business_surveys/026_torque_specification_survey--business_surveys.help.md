# Torque Specification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about torque specification practices within your organization. It aims to collect feedback on your current procedures, common issues, and standards used in the quality control process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the following questions to the best of your knowledge and experience.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. Enter a value for numerical fields (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
4. Complete all questions on each page before proceeding to the next.
5. Once you have filled out all the questions, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **engineering_team_surveyed**: Are your engineering teams regularly surveyed to identify and address issues with torque specifications?
	+ Choose 'Yes' if your engineering teams are regularly surveyed.
	+ Choose 'No' if your engineering teams are not regularly surveyed.
* **common_challenges**: Which of the following challenges are common with your current torque specifications?
	+ Choose all that apply:

* **torque_values**: Please enter the current torque value (e.g., 1.5, 2.2, etc.).
* **torque_units**: Are you currently using the correct units for torque specification (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No')?

	+ Choose 'No' if you are not using the correct units.
* **torque_standards_used**: Which of the following standards are used for torque specification?
	+ Choose all that apply:
		- 'Yes' for each standard you are using.
