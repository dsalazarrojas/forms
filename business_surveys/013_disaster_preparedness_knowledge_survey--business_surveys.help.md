# Disaster Preparedness Knowledge Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the disaster preparedness knowledge of a business or organization. It will help you gauge your level of preparedness in case of emergencies, including having emergency contacts, employee training, and emergency plans in place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each section and answer the questions accordingly. You can select "Yes" or "No" for most questions, and provide your business phone number and email address if applicable. Make sure to answer based on your business's current situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, optional): This is a brief overview of the survey. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you have an emergency contact person within your business.
* **Family Emergency Contact** (`family_emergency_contact`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you have an emergency contact person outside your business.
* **Business Employees** (`business_employees`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of employees in your business.
* **Business Employees Onsite** (`business_employees_onsite`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of employees who work onsite.
* **Business Employees Offsite** (`business_employees_offsite`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of employees who work offsite.
* **Business Location** (`business_location`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate if your business has a location that is accessible to emergency services.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your business email address.
* **Emergency Plan** (`emergency_plan`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you have an emergency plan in place.
* **Plan Frequency** (`plan_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate how often you review your emergency plan.
* **Plan Review** (`plan_review`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate how often you review your emergency plan.
* **Plan Document** (`plan_document`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you have a written emergency plan document.
* **Plan Location** (`plan_location`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if your emergency plan includes the location of your business.
* **Employee Training** (`employee_training`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if your employees have received disaster preparedness training.
* **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate the total number of hours of disaster preparedness training provided to employees.
* **Training Frequency** (`training_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate how often disaster preparedness training is provided to employees.
* **Training Status** (`training_status`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate the status of your business's disaster preparedness training.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you have a designated emergency contact person.
