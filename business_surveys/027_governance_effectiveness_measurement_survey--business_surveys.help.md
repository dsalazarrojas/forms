# Governance Effectiveness Measurement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to measure the effectiveness of governance in an organization, specifically in the areas of decision-making processes, transparency, accountability, communication, and reporting. It helps in identifying areas that need improvement and provides a basis for future governance development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your organization's name in the "Enter Organization Name" field.
2. Select the decision-making process used by your organization (Direct, Indirect, or Mixed).
3. Choose the level of transparency (Low, Medium, or High) that your organization maintains.
4. Indicate the level of board participation (High, Medium, or Low) and accountability (High, Medium, or Low).
5. Select the level of communication (High, Medium, or Low) and choose the reporting frequency (Quarterly, Annually, or Semi-annually).
6. Choose the method of reporting (Email, Phone, or In-person).
7. Select the responsibility for reporting (Board, Leadership, or Department).
8. Enter the timing of reporting (date).
9. Choose the evaluation frequency (Quarterly, Annually, or Semi-annually).
10. Select the method of evaluation (Online, Offline, or Hybrid).
11. Choose the frequency of board meetings (Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
12. Select the length of board meetings (Less than 1 hour, 1-2 hours, or More than 2 hours).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Organization Name** (1) (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter your organization's name.
* **What is the decision-making process?** (2) (`decision_making_process`, select_one, required): Choose how your organization makes decisions (Direct, Indirect, or Mixed).
* **Level of Transparency** (3) (`transparency_level`, select_multiple, required): Select the level of transparency your organization maintains (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Board Participation** (4) (`board_participation`, number, required): Indicate the level of board participation (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Level of Accountability** (5) (`accountability_level`, select_one, required): Choose the level of accountability (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Level of Communication** (6) (`communication_level`, select_multiple, required): Select the level of communication (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Reporting Frequency** (7) (`reporting_frequency`, time, required): Enter the frequency of reporting (date, in the format YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Reporting Method** (8) (`reporting_method`, select_one, required): Choose the method of reporting (Email, Phone, or In-person).
* **Reporting Responsibility** (9) (`reporting_responsibility`, select_multiple, required): Select the responsibility for reporting (Board, Leadership, or Department).
* **Reporting Timing** (10) (`reporting_timing`, date, required): Enter the timing of reporting (date, in the format YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Evaluation Frequency** (11) (`evaluation_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select the frequency of evaluation (Quarterly, Annually, or Semi-annually).
* **Evaluation Method** (12) (`evaluation_method`, select_one, required): Choose the method of evaluation (Online, Offline, or Hybrid).
* **Board Meeting Frequency** (13) (`board_meeting_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select the frequency of board meetings (Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
* **Board Meeting Length** (14) (`board_meeting_length`, select_one, required): Choose the length of board meetings (Less than 1 hour, 1-2 hours, or More than 2 hours).
