# Emergency Fundraising Benchmarking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Fundraising Benchmarking Survey is a form to collect data on an organization's emergency fundraising efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you fill out the form only once for each organization.
2. Enter the organization's details in the "Organization" field.
3. Provide the total donations received by the organization in the "Total Donations" field.
4. Specify the frequency of donations made to the organization in the "Donations Frequency" field.
5. Enter the average donation amount received by the organization in the "Donation Amount" field.
6. Enter the average time taken to receive a donation in the "Average Donation Time" field.
7. Enter the total time taken to receive all donations in the "Total Donation Time" field.
8. Specify the average donation period in the "Average Donation Period" field.
9. Specify the average donation frequency in the "Average Donation Frequency" field.
10. Enter the total donations frequency in the "Total Donations Frequency" field.
11. Provide any additional notes or comments about the organization's fundraising efforts in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization** (Organization, text, required): Enter the name of the organization.
* **Campaign Name** (campaign_name, text, required): Enter the name of the campaign.
* **Total Donations** (total_donations, number, required): Enter the total amount of money received from donations.
* **Donations Frequency** (donations_frequency, select_one, required): Specify the frequency of donations made to the organization.
* **Donation Amount** (donation_amount, number, required): Enter the average amount of money received from each donation.
* **Average Donation Amount** (average_donation_amount, number, required): Enter the average donation amount.
* **Donation Frequency 2** (donation_frequency_2, select_multiple, required): Specify the frequency of donations made to the organization.
* **Average Donation Report** (average_donation_report, date, required): Enter the date of the most recent donation report.
* **Average Donation Time** (average_donation_time, time, required): Enter the average time taken to receive a donation in minutes.
* **Total Donation Time** (total_donation_time, time, required): Enter the total time taken to receive all donations in minutes.
* **Average Donation Period** (average_donation_period, date, required): Enter the average donation period.
* **Average Donation Frequency** (average_donation_frequency, select_multiple, required): Specify the average donation frequency.
* **Total Donations Frequency** (total_donations_frequency, select_multiple, required): Specify the total donations frequency.
* **Notes** (notes, note, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about the organization's fundraising efforts.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately to provide reliable data for comparison and evaluation.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
* This form is designed to be filled out once for each organization. If you need to fill out the form again for the same organization, please contact [support email/department].
