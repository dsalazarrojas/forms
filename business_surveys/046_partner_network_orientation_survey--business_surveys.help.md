# Partner Network Orientation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand your current level of knowledge and engagement within our partner network. Your feedback will be used to provide personalized support and guidance to ensure your success in the network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and select the options that best fit your current situation.
2. Answer all questions, but you can skip questions that are not applicable to you.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
4. If you have any feedback or suggestions, please use the "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Onboarding Questions** (`onboarding_questions`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
  This question asks about your current level of knowledge on specific topics related to our partner network. Please select all the options that apply to you.
* **Partner Programs** (`partner_programs`, `text`, required: false):
  Describe the partner programs you are currently using or have used in the past.
* **Resources Provided** (`resources_provided`, `number`, required: false):
  If applicable, enter a numerical value indicating the number of resources provided to you as a partner.
* **Onboarding Process** (`onboarding_process`, `text`, required: false):
  Share your experience or thoughts on your onboarding process. What worked well and what could be improved?
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, `select_one`, required: false):
  Please select the communication channel(s) you usually use to stay in touch with us or our partner network (e.g., email, phone, or our community portal).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false):
  Provide any feedback or suggestions you have about our partner network or onboarding process.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false):
  Enter your email address for us to contact you with updates or information relevant to your partner status.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false):
  Enter your phone number for us to reach you for urgent matters.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false):
  If you have any specific dates for upcoming events or deadlines related to our partner network, please enter them here.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false):
  If you have any specific times for upcoming events or deadlines related to our partner network, please enter them here.

Note: Please fill in all fields accurately and thoroughly, as the information you provide will help us tailor our support and guidance to better meet your needs.
