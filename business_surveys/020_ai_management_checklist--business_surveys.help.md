# AI Management Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Management Checklist is a tool designed to assess your organization's AI management practices. It provides a framework to identify key areas of improvement and helps in providing a comprehensive overview of your organization's AI strategy. This checklist covers essential aspects of AI management, including strategic alignment, data strategy, governance, talent, budgeting, and risk assessment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer the questions accurately and thoroughly, providing the most up-to-date information about your organization's AI initiatives.
2.  Review each page carefully, ensuring you understand the question or prompt before proceeding.
3.  If you are unsure about a question, please provide your best estimate or answer based on your current knowledge.
4.  For questions with multiple options, select the one that best matches your organization's current situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization name** (`organization_name`, text, required): This field is for you to enter your organization's legal or operating name.
* **Contact person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Please provide the name of the person responsible for AI management or leadership in your organization.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the work email of the contact person to ensure smooth communication and follow-up.
* **AI strategic alignment with business goals** (`strategic_alignment`, select_one, required): Select the best match from the given options to describe your organization's current alignment of AI initiatives with business goals.
	+ **Not defined**: Your organization has not yet defined its AI strategy in relation to business goals.
	+ **Emerging**: Your organization is starting to explore AI initiatives but has not yet fully aligned them with business goals.
	+ **Aligned to specific units**: Some departments or units within your organization have AI initiatives aligned with their specific goals.
	+ **Organization-wide**: Your organization has a well-defined AI strategy aligned with overall business goals.
* **Data strategy maturity** (`data_strategy`, select_one, required): Indicate the level of maturity for data governance in your organization.
	+ **Nascent**: Your organization is just starting to develop its data strategy.
	+ **Developing**: Your organization has a basic data strategy in place.
	+ **Operational**: Your organization has a well-established data strategy.
	+ **Mature**: Your organization has a highly developed data strategy.
* **Governance policies in place** (`governance_policies`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable governance policies currently implemented in your organization.
	+ **Data privacy policy**: A data privacy policy is in place to protect sensitive information.
	+ **Model validation policy**: A policy for validating AI models is established.
	+ **Security controls**: Your organization has security measures to ensure the integrity of AI systems.
	+ **Ethical review board**: An ethical review board reviews AI-related decisions.
* **Talent availability for AI projects** (`talent_and_skills`, select_one, required): Choose the statement that best describes the current talent availability for AI projects in your organization.
	+ **No internal talent**: Your organization does not have internal talent for AI projects.
	+ **Small internal team**: Your organization has a small team with some AI expertise.
	+ **External partners engaged**: Your organization has partnered with external experts or firms for AI projects.
	+ **Strong internal capability**: Your organization has a well-established internal AI team.
* **Budget allocated for AI initiatives this year** (`budgeting`, number, optional): Enter the estimated annual budget for AI initiatives in USD.
* **Key risks identified** (`risk_assessment`, text, optional): Provide a short list of the top risks identified in your organization's AI management.
* **Recommended next steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Suggest actions your organization can take after completing this checklist.

Please review the form carefully and provide accurate information to help ensure that your organization has a comprehensive AI management framework.
