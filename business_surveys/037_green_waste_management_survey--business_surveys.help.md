# Green Waste Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Green Waste Management Survey is a form designed to gather information from companies about their waste reduction strategies and practices. This data will be used to identify areas for improvement and best practices in the industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field (required).
* Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field (required).
* Enter your Company Name in the "Company Name" field (required).
* Enter your Email address in the "Email" field (required, must be a valid email format).
* Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field (required).
* If you have an address, enter it in the "Address" field (optional).
* Select the type of waste management system your company has implemented from the options provided in "What type of waste management system does your company have?" (required).
* If you have information on the percentage of your company's waste that is recycled, enter it in the "What percentage of your company's waste is recycled?" field (optional).
* If you have any additional information you would like to provide, you can enter it in the "Additional Information" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your company's records.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your company's records.
* **Company Name** (text, required): Enter the full name of your company.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address for the person filling out this form.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Address** (text, optional): If you would like to provide a physical address for your company, please enter it here.
* **What type of waste management system does your company have?** (select_one, required): Select the type of waste management system your company has implemented. Options include:
	+ Recycling program
	+ No program
	+ Other (please specify in "Additional Information" field)
* **What percentage of your company's waste is recycled?** (number, optional): If you have information on the percentage of your company's waste that is recycled, please enter it here.
* **Additional Information** (note, optional): If you have any additional information you would like to provide, please enter it here. This can include details about your company's waste management practices, such as the types of materials you recycle or how you measure your progress.
