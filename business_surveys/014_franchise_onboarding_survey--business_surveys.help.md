## Purpose
The Franchise Onboarding Survey is designed to collect information from new franchisees to help us understand their business needs and provide them with relevant support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Make sure to provide your current contact information.
* Answer the questions about your business to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**:
	+ This is a text field where you can provide any additional information you'd like to share with us.
* **Franchise Name** (`franchise_name`):
	+ This is the name of your franchise.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`):
	+ This is the first name of the person who will be the primary point of contact for your franchise.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`):
	+ This is the last name of the person who will be the primary point of contact for your franchise.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`):
	+ Please enter the email address of the person who will be the primary point of contact for your franchise.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`):
	+ This is the phone number of the person who will be the primary point of contact for your franchise.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`):
	+ This is the address of the person who will be the primary point of contact for your franchise.
* **What is your position** (`what_is_your_position`):
	+ This is your position or title within the franchise.
* **Years with Firm** (`years_with_firm`):
	+ This is the number of years you have been with the firm.
* **How long has been in business** (`how_long_has_been_in_business`):
	+ This is how long your business has been operational.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`):
	+ This is the number of employees that work for your franchise.
* **Average Annual Revenue** (`average_annual_revenue`):
	+ This is the average annual revenue of your franchise.
* **Top Product or Service** (`top_product_or_service`):
	+ This is your top-selling or most popular product or service.
* **Type of Top Product or Service** (`top_product_or_service_type`):
	+ This is the type of product or service you offer (product, service, etc.).
* **Top Product or Service Subcategory** (`top_product_or_service_subcategory`):
	+ This is a subcategory of your top-selling or most popular product or service.
* **Average Hourly Rate** (`average_hourly_rate`):
	+ This is the average hourly rate of your services.
* **Average Daily Rate** (`average_daily_rate`):
	+ This is the average daily rate of your services.
* **Average Weekly Rate** (`average_weekly_rate`):
	+ This is the average weekly rate of your services.
* **Average Monthly Rate** (`average_monthly_rate`):
	+ This is the average monthly rate of your services.
* **Average Annual Rate** (`average_annual_rate`):
	+ This is the average annual rate of your services.
* **Average Daily Rate Weekends** (`average_daily_rate_weekends`):
	+ This is the average daily rate on weekends.
* **Average Daily Rate Holidays** (`average_daily_rate_holidays`):
	+ This is the average daily rate on holidays.
* **Average Daily Rate Others** (`average_daily_rate_others`):
	+ This is the average daily rate for other days.
* **Average Annual Hourly Rate** (`average_annual_hourly_rate`):
	+ This is the average daily rate for an hour.
* **What do you use to process invoices** (`what_do_you_use_to_process_invoices`):
	+ This is the tool or software you use to process invoices for your business.
 
## Tips

* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, please let us know and we'll do our best to clarify.
* If you have any additional information you'd like to share with us, you can do so in the "Introduction" field.
