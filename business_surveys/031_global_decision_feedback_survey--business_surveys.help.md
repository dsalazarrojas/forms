# Global Decision Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from global decision-makers on their decision-making processes. The collected data will help the organization improve its decision-making processes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each question carefully before selecting an answer.
2. Choose the option that best reflects your answer to each question.
3. For questions with text fields, please provide a brief and clear explanation of your answer.
4. Click "Submit" to send the form.
