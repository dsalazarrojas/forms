# Business Innovation Adoption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the business innovation adoption survey. The survey aims to gather insights from business stakeholders on their current and future innovation adoption stages, barriers, decision timeframes, and expected benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and answer all questions as they relate to your current organization.
- Provide your name and title/department as the respondent.
- If you have previously responded to this survey, you can choose to skip it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Organization name**: Provide the name of your company participating in this survey.
- **Respondent role**: Enter your job title or department in your organization.
- **Current innovation tools**: Describe the tools or platforms your organization currently uses for innovation.
- **Adoption stage**: Select the stage of innovation adoption that best describes your organization's current state:
  - **Awareness**: Your organization is aware of the innovation but not yet adopted.
  - **Trial**: Your organization is testing or piloting the innovation.
  - **Adoption**: Your organization has fully adopted the innovation.
  - **Scaling**: Your organization is scaling up the innovation.
- **Barriers to adoption**: Select all the applicable barriers that hinder your organization's innovation adoption:
  - **Budget constraints**: Limited budget for innovation implementation.
  - **Lack of skills**: Insufficient skills or expertise to implement the innovation.
  - **Cultural resistance**: Resistance to change within your organization.
  - **Regulatory concerns**: Regulatory compliance issues that hinder innovation adoption.
- **Decision timeframe**: Select the timeframe for when you plan to implement new innovations:
  - **Within 3 months**: Implement new innovations within the next three months.
  - **3 to 12 months**: Implement new innovations within the next three years.
  - **1 to 2 years**: Implement new innovations within one to two years.
  - **No plans**: No current plans for innovation implementation.
- **Expected benefits**: Describe the expected improvements or benefits from adopting new innovations.
- **Contact for follow up**: Provide your email address if you consent to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions.
