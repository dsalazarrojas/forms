# Marine Technology Research Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Marine Technology Research Survey form, which is designed to collect feedback from individuals or organizations involved in or interested in marine technology research.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through each question to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. For text fields, provide a brief but clear answer in the format requested.
3. For select fields, choose one or more options that best apply to your experience or perspective.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Research Roles** (`research_roles`, text, required): Describe your involvement in marine technology research, including any roles you play or organizations you represent.
* **Focus Areas** (`focus_areas`, select_multiple, required): Select all the areas of marine technology research that interest you, such as:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, text, required): Share your experiences or difficulties you've encountered in marine technology research.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required): Provide any suggestions you have for improving marine technology research.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Roles** (`roles`, select_one, required): Select one of the following roles:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Focus Areas Other** (`focus_areas_other`, text, required): If there are any other areas of marine technology research that you are interested in or involved with, please specify here.
* **Suggestions Other** (`suggestions_other`, text, required): If you have any other suggestions for improving marine technology research, please share them here.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Provide the date of your most recent involvement in marine technology research.
