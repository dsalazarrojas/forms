> ### 401k Quality Assurance Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gauge employee satisfaction with the 401(k) plan provided by your organization. It will help the management to assess the quality of the plan and make improvements based on your feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Provide your full name, email address, department, and job title to identify yourself as an employee.
2. Answer the following questions honestly and to the best of your ability, selecting the relevant options for each question.
3. If you feel there's anything else you'd like to share, provide additional comments in the 'Additional comments or notes' section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name**: A required field to confirm your identity as an employee. Enter your legal first and last name.
* **Email address**: We'll use this to send survey results to you. Enter a valid email address.
* **Department**: Enter your current department, for example, Finance, Marketing, or Engineering.
* **Job title**: Your current position title in the organization.
* **Date of hire**: If you are unsure of your hire date, leave this field blank.
* **Years employed**: Approximate number of years you've worked at the organization. If you're new, you can leave this field blank.
* **Satisfaction with 401(k) plan quality**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the 401(k) plan.
* **Management rating**: Rate your experience with management's communication about the 401(k) plan.
* **Supported in professional development**: Rate your agreement with the statement that you feel supported in professional development related to retirement planning.
* **Work-life balance**: Rate your current work-life balance, where 1 is very poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Features used**: Select all the 401(k) plan features you use, such as Traditional, Roth, or Safe Harbor.
* **Plan communication rating**: Rate the overall effectiveness of communications about plan updates.
* **Annual plan fees**: Enter the annual fees as a percentage of assets, if you know them.
* **Plan fees comprehension**: Rate your understanding of plan fees.
* **Plan education sessions**: Enter the number of educational sessions you've attended in the past year.
* **Plan education satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the educational resources provided.
* **Improvement suggestions**: Provide specific ideas on how to improve your experience with the 401(k) plan.
* **Additional comments**: Share anything else you'd like to share or comment about.
