# Pharmaceutical Technology Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about the pharmaceutical technology industry, specifically about company size, type, and technology interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions in each section, one by one.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
3. For open-ended questions, enter your answer in the space provided.
4. Be as accurate and detailed as possible with your answers.
5. Do not leave any blank fields, as they are all required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role within the company?** (`title`, text, required): Please enter a brief description of your role within the company.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_multiple, required): Please select all that apply from the following options: Small, Medium, Large, Other.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, select_one, required): Please select one of the following options: Pharmaceutical Company, Medical Device Company, Research Organization, Other.
* **What technology areas are you most interested in?** (`technology_interests`, select_multiple, required): Please select all that apply from the following options: Automation and Robotics, Artificial Intelligence, Blockchain, Data Science, Cloud Computing, Other.
* **On a scale of 0-10, what level of adoption do you have for each technology?** (`adoption_levels`, number, required): Please enter a score between 0-10, indicating your level of adoption for each technology.
* **Which of the following implementation challenges do you face when adopting new technology?** (`implementation_challenges`, select_multiple, required): Please select all that apply from the following options: Cost and ROI, Change Management, Security and Compliance, Training and Support, Other.
* **Other implementation challenges** (`implementation_challenges_2`, text, required): Please enter any other implementation challenges you face when adopting new technology.
* **How long did it take to implement the technology?** (`implementation_timeframe`, select_one, required): Please select one of the following options: Less than 1 month, 1-3 months, 4-6 months, 7-12 months, More than 1 year.
* **What is the status of your current project or implementation?** (`implementation_timeframe_2`, select_one, required): Please select one of the following options: Not started, In Progress, Completed, On hold, Postponed.
* **Would you like to share any industry insights or observations?** (`industry_insights`, text, required): Please enter any industry insights or observations you have.
* **Would you like to share any other industry insights or observations?** (`industry_insights_2`, text, required): Please enter any other industry insights or observations you have.
* **Any additional comments?** (`comments`, text, required): Please enter any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **Your Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Your Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Your Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your name.
* **Your Department** (`department`, text, required): Please enter your department name.
* **Your Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Please enter your job title.
* **Your Company** (`company`, text, required): Please enter your company name.
* **Your Role** (`role`, text, required): Please enter your role within the company.
* **What level of experience do you have with the technologies mentioned?** (`technology_experience`, select_multiple, required): Please select all that apply from the following options: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert, Other.
* **Other Technology Experience** (`technology_experience_2`, text, required): Please enter any other technology experience you have.
* **How would you like to be contacted for follow-up questions?** (`contact_details`, text, required): Please enter your preferred contact method (e.g., email, phone).
