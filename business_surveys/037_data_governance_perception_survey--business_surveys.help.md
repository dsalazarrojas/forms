# Data Governance Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Governance Perception Survey is a self-assessment tool used to gauge employees' perceptions and attitudes towards data governance practices within the organization. The survey aims to gather insights into how employees perceive the effectiveness and importance of data governance policies, procedures, and tools.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to understand the questions and options provided.
2. Answer each question honestly, and to the best of your ability.
3. Use the dropdown menu to select one or more options for questions that ask for multiple choice.
4. Enter your email address in the format "username@example.com" if you have one.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have in the "Feedback" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Data Governance Practices** (Page 1)
• **<label>Data Governance Practices</label>** (`<name>data_governance_practices</name>`, `<type>note`): This field is a free-text area where you can provide feedback or comments about the data governance practices within the organization. Please be as specific as possible and provide concrete examples.

### **How Important Is It** (Page 2)
• **<label>How Important Is It</label>** (`<name>how_important_is_it</name>`, `<type>select_one`): This question asks about the importance of data governance practices. Select one of the options provided, and choose the one that best reflects your opinion. 

### **Feedback** (Page 3)
• **<label>Feedback</label>** (`<name>feedback</name>`, `<type>note`): This field is another free-text area for you to provide any additional comments or suggestions about data governance practices.

### **Email** (Page 4)
• **<label>Email</label>** (`<name>email</name>`, `<type>email`): Enter your email address in the format "username@example.com". This is optional, but it will help us to contact you for any follow-up questions or to provide updates about our data governance initiatives.

### **Phone** (Page 5)
• **<label>Phone</label>** (`<name>phone</name>`, `<type>text`): Please enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by phone for any follow-up questions or to provide more information about our data governance practices.

### **Comments** (Page 6)
• **<label>Comments</label>** (`<name>comments</name>`, `<type>note`): Another free-text area for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about data governance practices.

### **Submission** (Page 7)
• **<label>Submission</label>** (`<name>submission</name>`, `<type>text`): Please provide any additional information about your submission, such as any files or attachments related to your answer.

### **Submission Date** (Page 8)
• **<label>Submission Date</label>** (`<name>submission_date</name>`, `<type>date`): Please enter the date you are submitting this form in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".

### **Submission Time** (Page 9)
• **<label>Submission Time</label>** (`<name>submission_time</name>`, `<type>time`): Please enter the time you are submitting this form in the format "HH:MM AM/PM".

### **Submission File** (Page 10)
• **<label>Submission File</label>** (`<name>submission_file</name>`, `<type>text`): This field is for uploading any files related to your submission, such as documents or images.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Use the format "username@example.com" for your email address.
* Make sure to review each page carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the questions, please feel free to contact us.
* Your feedback and comments are valuable and help us improve our data governance practices.
