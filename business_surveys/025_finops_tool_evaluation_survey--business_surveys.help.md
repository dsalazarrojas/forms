# FinOps Tool Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the effectiveness of various FinOps tools.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please answer the questions according to the provided guidelines.
2. Fill in the relevant information for each field, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Be sure to answer all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
