# Sports Facility Accessibility Improvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from sports facility users about accessibility challenges they face when visiting these facilities. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and create a more accessible and enjoyable experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of sports facility you visit most often.
2. Answer the questions about the accessibility challenges you face, using the provided scale for question 4.
3. Provide any suggested improvements for the facility's accessibility in the text fields at the end.
4. Add any additional comments or feedback in the final text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which type of sports facility do you visit most often?** (School, Gym, Stadium, Park, Arena, Other (please specify))
	+ This question helps us understand which type of facility you are most likely to visit and what kind of accessibility challenges you may face.
* **If other, please specify** (text)
	+ If you don't see your facility type listed, please specify it here so we can make sure to include it in our analysis.
* **How often do you encounter difficulty entering your facility due to accessibility challenges?** (1-5)
	+ On a scale from 1 (less than once a month) to 5 (daily), rate how often you face difficulty entering the facility.
* **How often do you encounter difficulty finding accessible facilities or services?** (Never, Rarely, Occasionally, Often, Always)
	+ Think about the times you have difficulty finding facilities or services that are accessible to you.
* **How often do you have difficulty getting assistance or support for accessibility needs?** (Never, Rarely, Occasionally, Often, Always)
	+ Rate how often you need help or support for accessibility-related issues.
* **How often do you have difficulty getting information about accessibility features or services?** (Never, Rarely, Occasionally, Often, Always)
	+ Think about the times you have difficulty finding information about accessible features or services.
* **What improvements would you suggest for our facility's accessibility?** (text)
	+ This is your chance to suggest any improvements you think would enhance accessibility at the facility.
* **Do you have any other ideas or suggestions?** (text)
	+ If you think of something that's not listed here, please share it with us.
* **Additional comments or feedback** (text)
	+ If you have any other thoughts or comments, this is the place to share them.
