# Snowmobile Equipment Exchange Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by selecting the "What Type Of Equipment" option that best matches your snowmobile.
2. Next, choose the condition of your snowmobile from the options provided.
3. If applicable, upload photos of your snowmobile by clicking on the "Photos Upload" field.
4. Enter the make and model of your snowmobile in the "Equipment Make" and "Equipment Model" fields.
5. Provide the year of your snowmobile in the "Equipment Year" field.
6. Enter the price of your snowmobile in the "Equipment Price" field.
7. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
8. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
