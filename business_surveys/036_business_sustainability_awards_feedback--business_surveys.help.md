# Business Sustainability Awards Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees and participants of business awards events, helping us improve future events and better serve our attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name as it should appear on your feedback response.
2.  Provide your organization or company name (if applicable).
3.  Enter your email address.
4.  Select your type of participation (e.g., Nominee, Award Winner, Sponsor, Attendee, Organizer, Media, or Other).
5.  Enter the date of the awards event.
6.  Enter the date you are submitting your feedback (today's date).
7.  Select the quality of the event's organization and planning.
8.  Select the quality of the venue.
9.  Select how fair you thought the award selection process was.
10.  Select how well the event focused on sustainability issues.
11.  Select how relevant you found the content.
12.  Select the quality of networking opportunities.
13.  (Optional) Select the quality of the catering and refreshments. (If applicable)
14.  Select the pace of the program schedule.
15.  Select the quality of the award recognition.
16.  Select your overall satisfaction with the event.
17.  (Optional) Provide suggestions for improvement.
17.  Select your likelihood of attending future events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Full Name**: Enter your full name as it should appear on your feedback response.
*   **Organization Name**: Provide your organization or company name (if applicable).
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address for future contact and reference.
*   **Type of Participant**: Select your role in the event (e.g., Nominee, Award Winner, Sponsor, Attendee, Organizer, Media, or Other).
*   **Event Date**: Enter the date of the awards event.
*   **Feedback Submission Date**: Enter today's date.
*   **Event Organization and Planning**: Select the quality of the event's organization and planning.
*   **Venue Quality**: Select the quality of the venue.
*   **Award Selection Process Fairness**: Select how fair you thought the award selection process was.
*   **Sustainability Focus**: Select how well the event addressed sustainability issues.
*   **Content Relevance and Value**: Select the relevance and value of the content.
*   **Networking Opportunities**: Select the quality of networking opportunities.
*   **Catering and Refreshments Quality**: (Optional) Select the quality of the catering and refreshments.
*   **Program Schedule**: Select the pace of the program schedule.
*   **Quality of Award Recognition**: Select the quality of the award recognition.
*   **Overall Satisfaction**: Select your overall satisfaction with the event.
*   **Suggestions for Improvement**: (Optional) Provide specific suggestions for improvement.
*   **Future Event Participation**: Select your likelihood of attending future events.

**Tips**

*   Be honest in your feedback, as it will help us improve the event and serve you better in the future.
*   Please answer the required questions before submitting your feedback.
*   You can skip optional questions, but your feedback will be more valuable if you complete all required fields.
