# Co-Op Marketing Opportunities Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your business and its potential for co-op marketing opportunities. This will help our team understand your business needs and identify potential collaboration opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your business information, including your business name, primary contact name, and industry type.
2. Select the co-op marketing initiatives you are interested in, such as shared social media campaigns, co-branded events, or referral programs.
3. Specify your primary goal for participating in co-op marketing, which can be to reach new audiences, reduce marketing costs, or align your brand.
4. If you have resources to contribute, such as graphic design or social media reach, indicate them in the "Resource Contribution" section.
5. Estimate your monthly budget for collaborative marketing efforts.
6. Describe your target customer demographic.
7. List up to three brands you would like to collaborate with.
8. If you're interested in scheduling a discovery call for potential partnerships, select "True".
9. Finally, choose a preferred time for an initial partnership meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: Please enter your company name as it appears on your business cards, website, or other marketing materials.
* **Primary Contact Name**: Enter the name of the primary contact person for your business.
* **Business Type or Industry**: Describe the type of business or industry your company operates in.
* **-- Marketing Collaboration Interests --**: Select all the co-op marketing initiatives you're interested in, such as:
	+ Shared Social Media Campaigns
	+ Co-Branded Events
	+ Joint Email Newsletters
	+ Shared Advertising Space
	+ Referral Programs
* **What is your primary goal for participating in co-op marketing?**: Choose the reason why you want to participate in co-op marketing:
	+ Reach New Audiences
	+ Reduce Marketing Costs
	+ Brand Alignment
	+ Increased Sales
* **-- Resource Contribution --**: If your business has resources to contribute, such as:
	+ Graphic Design
	+ Social Media Reach
	+ Physical Event Space
	+ Budget
	+ Customer Mailing List
	Select all that apply.
* **What is your estimated monthly budget for collaborative marketing?**: Enter your estimated monthly budget for co-op marketing efforts in USD.
* **-- Strategic Alignment --**: This is a note section for you to describe how your business aligns with our organization.
* **Please describe your target customer demographic**: Explain the characteristics of your ideal customer, including demographics, interests, and behavior.
* **List up to 3 brands you would ideally like to collaborate with**: Enter the names of up to three brands you would like to partner with.
* **-- Next Steps --**: This is a note section for you to describe any additional information about next steps.
* **Would you like to schedule a discovery call for potential partnerships?**: Select "True" if you're interested in scheduling a call.
* **Preferred time for an initial partnership meeting**: Choose a time of day when you're available for a meeting.
