# Decode E Numbers Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Decode E Numbers Survey is a tool designed to assess businesses' knowledge and usage of E-numbers. The survey will help us better understand how companies are currently utilizing E-numbers in their decision-making processes and identify areas where awareness can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the options for E-numbers awareness on **E Numbers Awareness**.
2. Provide a brief answer to **How do you currently use E-numbers in your work?**.
3. Describe your **Concerns** related to E-numbers.
4. Rate the importance of E-numbers awareness in your business decisions on a scale from 0 to 100 for **How important is E-numbers awareness in your business decisions?**.
5. Share your thoughts on how your business could improve E-numbers awareness in **How do you think your business could improve E-numbers awareness?**.
6. Indicate whether you are currently using alternative solutions or alternatives to E-numbers on **Are you currently using alternative solutions or alternatives to E-numbers?**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **E Numbers Awareness**: This question asks you to select the level of your business's awareness of E-numbers. Please choose from the options:
	+ E-numbers: Your business has a clear understanding of E-numbers.
	+ Low: Your business has a basic understanding of E-numbers.
	+ High: Your business has a thorough understanding of E-numbers.
* **How do you currently use E-numbers in your work?**: Provide a brief description of how your business is currently using E-numbers in its decision-making processes. This can include examples or specific use cases.
* **Concerns**: Share any concerns or challenges your business faces when dealing with E-numbers. This can include difficulties in understanding E-numbers, issues with implementation, or any other related concerns.
* **How important is E-numbers awareness in your business decisions?**: Rate the importance of E-numbers awareness in your business decisions on a scale from 0 to 100. This will help us understand how much weight you place on E-numbers in your business decisions.
* **How do you think your business could improve E-numbers awareness?**: Share your thoughts on how your business could improve its E-numbers awareness. This can include suggestions for training, education, or process changes.
* **Are you currently using alternative solutions or alternatives to E-numbers?**: Indicate whether your business is using alternative solutions or alternatives to E-numbers. You can select 'True' if you are using an alternative solution, 'False' if not, or 'Undecided' if you are unsure.
