**Supply Control Survey - Help Guide**
================================

## Purpose
This form is used to collect data about stock levels, stockout reasons, and ordering performance to monitor and analyze stock control.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct options for each field as instructed below.
2. Answer each field according to your knowledge and understanding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Stock Levels**: Enter the current stock levels.
  • (`stock_levels`, number, required/optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Stockout Reasons**: Select all applicable reasons for stockouts.
  • (`stockout_reasons`, select_multiple, required/optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Ordering Performance**: Select the performance of your current ordering process.
  • (`ordering_performance`, select_one, required/optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Departments**: Select all departments that may be affected by stockouts.
  • (`departments`, select_multiple, required/optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Stockouts**: Enter the number of stockouts experienced.
  • (`stockouts`, number, required/optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Stockout Frequency**: Enter the frequency of stockouts.
  • (`stockout_frequency`, date, required/optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Stockout Description**: Enter a description of the stockout.
  • (`stockout_description`, text, required/optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

## Field-by-Field Explanation
