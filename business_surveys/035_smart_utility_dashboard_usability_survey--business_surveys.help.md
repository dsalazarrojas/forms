# Smart Utility Dashboard Usability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Utility Dashboard Usability Survey is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from users on the usability of the Smart Utility Dashboard. This survey will help us understand how often users access the dashboard, their roles within the organization, and their overall experience with the dashboard's features and functionality.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields marked with an asterisk (*) in the field label.
2. Select one of the provided options for each select field.
3. Enter your email and phone number for contact purposes.
4. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **UserRoles** (Select One, required): This field asks you to select your role within the organization. Please choose the option that best represents your job title or position.
* **Usage Frequency** (Select Multiple, required): This field asks you to select how often you access the Smart Utility Dashboard. Please choose the frequency that best describes your usage.
* **Usability Ratings** (Number, required): This field asks you to rate your overall experience with the dashboard's features and functionality. Please rate on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Feature Feedback** (Text, not required): This field is optional and allows you to provide feedback on specific features or functionality of the dashboard.
* **Improvement Ideas** (Text, not required): This field is optional and allows you to provide suggestions on how to improve the dashboard's usability.
* **Form Submit** (Note, required): This field is a submission confirmation field. Please enter your email address to confirm your submission.
* **Form Comment** (Text, not required): This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Form Email** (Email, required): This field asks for your email address for contact purposes.
* **Form Phone** (Text, required): This field asks for your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Form File** (Text, not required): This field is optional and allows you to attach a file or document for reference.
* **Form Date** (Date, not required): This field is optional and asks for the date of your submission.
