# Makeup Storage And Hygiene Habit Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your makeup storage and hygiene habits. Please answer the questions honestly to help us understand your habits and provide valuable insights for our business.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select the relevant options for each question.
3. Fill in the required fields.
4. Submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Makeup Storage And Hygiene Habit** (`1`, `text`, `required`): This is the main question of the form, where you will describe your general storage and hygiene habits regarding makeup.
* **Storage Location** (`2`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Choose all the locations where you store your makeup, such as Home, Clinic, Salon, and Online.
* **Usage Frequency** (`3`, `number`, `required`): Report how often you use your makeup, using numbers (e.g., every day, every few weeks, rarely, etc.).
* **Usage Time** (`4`, `time`, `required`): Record the time of day when you usually use your makeup.
* **Storage Duration** (`5`, `number`, `required`): Indicate how long you keep your makeup stored, in terms of the number of days or weeks.
* **Cleaning Frequency** (`6`, `number`, `required`): State how frequently you clean your makeup, including the number of times per week or month.
* **Cleaning Duration** (`7`, `number`, `required`): Report the duration of each cleaning session.
* **Makeup Type** (`8`, `select_one`, `optional`): Identify the type of makeup you use, from Foundation to Blush.
* **Stored With** (`9`, `select_one`, `optional`): Choose if you store your makeup alone or with others.
* **Stored In Airtight Container** (`10`, `select_one`, `optional`): Report if you store your makeup in an airtight container, and choose the type of container used.
* **Stored In Clean Area** (`12`, `select_one`, `optional`): Answer whether you store your makeup in a clean area, like a specific room or on a specific surface.
* **Stored** (`13`, `number`, `required`): Record the number of makeup products you store.
* **Used Within** (`14`, `number`, `required`): Indicate how many times you use each product within a certain timeframe.
* **Usage Area** (`15`, `select_one`, `optional`): Report where you apply your makeup, such as Face, Eyes, Lips, or Skin.
* **Stored In Direct Sunlight** (`16`, `select_one`, `optional`): Answer whether you store your makeup in direct sunlight.
* **Cleaned Daily** (`17`, `select_one`, `optional`): Report if you clean your makeup daily.
* **Cleaned With Brush** (`18`, `select_one`, `optional`): Choose if you clean your makeup with a brush.
* **Cleaned With Water** (`19`, `select_one`, `optional`): Report if you clean your makeup with water.
* **Cleaned With Soap** (`20`, `select_one`, `optional`): Answer whether you clean your makeup with soap.
* **Stored In Fridge** (`21`, `select_one`, `optional`): Report if you store your makeup in the fridge.
* **Used On Face** (`22`, `select_one`, `optional`): Answer whether you use your makeup on your face.
* **Used On Eyes** (`23`, `select_one`, `optional`): Report if you use your makeup on your eyes.
* **Used On Lips** (`24`, `select_one`, `optional`): Choose if you use your makeup on your lips.
* **Used On Skin** (`25`, `select_one`, `optional`): Report if you use your makeup on your skin.
