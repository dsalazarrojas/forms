## Financial Control Review Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Financial Control Review Survey is designed to help business owners review and evaluate their financial control measures, assess potential financial risks, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer questions to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure that you are familiar with the relevant financial regulations and laws applicable to your business.
3. Select only the options that apply to your business.
4. Enter your answers in the relevant fields.
5. Scroll down to the next page to review and complete additional questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Financial Control Effectiveness (Page 1)
* **Financial Control Effectiveness** (Number, Optional): This field asks you to evaluate the effectiveness of your financial control measures on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is "not effective" and 10 is "very effective".

### Financial Risks (Page 1)
* **Financial Risks** (Select One, Optional): Select one of the following financial risks that apply to your business:
	+ Risk 1: Regulatory non-compliance
	+ Risk 2: Market volatility
	+ Risk 3: Financial crime or corruption

### Financial Improvement Ideas (Page 2)
* **Financial Improvement Ideas** (Text, Optional): In this field, describe any ideas or suggestions you have for improving your financial control measures.

### Staff Feedback (Page 2)
* **Staff Feedback** (Note, Optional): In this field, provide any feedback or comments from your staff regarding the financial control measures in place.

This guide is intended to be a helpful resource for completing the Financial Control Review Survey. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
