# sports_league_fan_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about sports league fans' experiences to help us better understand their needs and preferences.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each section and answer the questions accordingly. You can skip any questions that don't apply to you or your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully before starting.
- **Demographic Info** (`demographic_info`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your demographic info (Male, Female, or Other) to help us better understand your experience.
- **Fan Satisfaction** (`fan_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with different aspects of the league on a scale of Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
- **Attendance** (`attendance`, `number`, required: false): If you attend games, please let us know how many games you attend per season.
- **Frequency of Visit** (`frequency_of_visit`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how often you visit the games, from Less than 1/4 of games to All games.
- **Favorite Team** (`favorite_team`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your favorite team(s) to help us understand your loyalties.
- **Improvement Feedback** (`improvement_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Share with us what you think is the most impactful improvement for your experience.
- **Engagement** (`engagement`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your engagement level with the league on a scale of Very Engaged to Not Very Engaged.
- **Season Ticket Price** (`season_ticket_price`, `number`, required: false): If you buy season tickets, please let us know how much you expect to pay for them.
- **Merchandise Purchases** (`merchandise_purchases`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select how often you purchase merchandise from the league.
- **Fan Suggestion** (`fan_suggestion`, `text`, required: false): Share any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the league experience.
- **Fan Suggestion 2** (`fan_suggestion_2`, `text`, required: false): If you have more suggestions or ideas, share them here.
- **Game Day Experience** (`game_day_experience`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your game day experience on a scale of Very Good to Very Bad.
- **Parking Experience** (`parking_experience`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how good or bad your parking experience is at the games.
- **Ticket Purchasing** (`ticket_purchasing`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate how easy or hard it is to purchase tickets from the league.
- **Concession Stands** (`concession_stands`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select how clean you find the concession stands at the games.
- **Parking Experience 2** (`parking_experience_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how good or bad your parking experience is at the games.
- **Food and Beverage** (`food_and_beverage`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select how good or bad you think the food and beverage options are at the games.
- **Merchandise Purchases 2** (`merchandise_purchases_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select how often you purchase merchandise from the concession stands.
- **Merchandise Purchases 3** (`merchandise_purchases_3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select how often you purchase merchandise from the league's official store.
- **Merchandise Purchases 4** (`merchandise_purchases_4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select how often you purchase merchandise from other sources.
- **Merchandise Purchases 5** (`merchandise_purchases_5`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select how often you purchase merchandise from online platforms.
- **Merchandise Purchases 6** (`merchandise_purchases_6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select how often you purchase merchandise from social media platforms.
- **Merchandise Purchases 7** (`merchandise_purchases_7`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select how often you purchase merchandise from other sources.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional thoughts or feedback you have about the league experience.
