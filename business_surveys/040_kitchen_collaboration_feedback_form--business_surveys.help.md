# Kitchen Collaboration Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for gathering feedback and suggestions from kitchen users on collaboration aspects of the kitchen. It aims to improve user experience and encourage users to share their thoughts on the project's progress, details, and overall collaboration. This form will help us understand what is working well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in the form.
2. Provide feedback on the project details.
3. Rate your experience (if you have had one).
4. Add any comments you'd like to share.
5. Suggest improvements or new ideas.
6. Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up).
7. If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details (project_details)**: Please share any specific feedback or information about the project, such as its purpose, progress, or any challenges you're facing.
* **Rating (rating)**: Use the rating scale to express your satisfaction with the collaboration experience. This is optional, but your feedback will help us understand what works and what doesn't.
* **Comments (comments)**: Write any additional thoughts or observations you'd like to share about your collaboration experience.
* **Suggestions (suggestions)**: This is your chance to suggest new ideas or improvements to help us make the collaboration process better.
* **Email (email)**: If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up or further information, please enter your email address.
* **Phone (phone)**: If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number.

Note: Please fill in the required information as per your discretion, and don't hesitate to skip optional fields if you're not comfortable sharing them.
