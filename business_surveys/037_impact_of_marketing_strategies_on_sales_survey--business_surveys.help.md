# Impact Of Marketing Strategies On Sales Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information on the impact of marketing strategies on sales performance. The goal is to gather data on various marketing channels, strategies, and budget allocations used by businesses to achieve sales goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information, such as your company name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the marketing channels you use, such as online advertising, social media, email marketing, and more.
3. Choose the marketing strategy that best describes your business approach, such as inbound, outbound, content, or relationship marketing.
4. Indicate your sales channel, such as online, offline, direct, or telemarketing sales.
5. Select the customer segment your business targets, such as B2B or B2C.
6. Report your sales performance, including high, medium, or low levels of sales.
7. Enter your marketing budget and lead time, if applicable.
8. Provide any additional information or feedback on customer relationships and sales performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for your company or a brief description of your business.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Type the name of your company.
* **Email** (`company_email`, email, optional): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone** (`company_phone`, text, optional): Enter your company phone number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Type your job title.
* **Department** (`department_name`, text, optional): Enter the department name of the person completing this form.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, text, optional): Select the size of your company, such as small, medium, or large.
* **Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the marketing channels you use, such as online advertising, social media, email marketing, and more.
* **Other Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels_other`, text, optional): If you use other marketing channels not listed above, enter them here.
* **Marketing Strategy** (`marketing_strategy`, select_one, optional): Choose the marketing strategy that best describes your business approach, such as inbound, outbound, content, or relationship marketing.
* **Other Marketing Strategy** (`marketing_strategy_other`, text, optional): If you use other marketing strategies not listed above, enter them here.
* **Sales Channel** (`sales_channel`, select_one, optional): Choose the sales channel you use, such as online, offline, direct, or telemarketing sales.
* **Other Sales Channel** (`sales_channel_other`, text, optional): If you use other sales channels not listed above, enter them here.
* **Customer Segment** (`customer_segment`, select_one, optional): Choose the customer segment you target, such as B2B or B2C.
* **Other Customer Segment** (`customer_segment_other`, text, optional): If you target other customer segments not listed above, enter them here.
* **Sales Performance** (`sales_performance`, select_one, optional): Report your sales performance, including high, medium, or low levels.
* **Other Sales Performance** (`sales_performance_other`, text, optional): If your sales performance is not listed above, enter a description.
* **Marketing Budget** (`marketing_budget`, number, optional): Enter your marketing budget.
* **Other Marketing Budget** (`marketing_budget_other`, text, optional): If you have other marketing budgets not listed above, enter them here.
* **Lead Time** (`lead_time`, time, optional): Enter the time it takes to lead a sales transaction.
* **Other Lead Time** (`lead_time_other`, text, optional): If you have other lead times not listed above, enter them here.
* **Sales Time** (`sales_time`, time, optional): Enter the time it takes to complete a sales transaction.
* **Other Sales Time** (`sales_time_other`, text, optional): If you have other sales times not listed above, enter them here.
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or feedback on customer relationships and sales performance.
* **Customer Follow-up** (`customer_follow_up`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you follow up with customers, such as True or False.
* **Other Customer Follow-up** (`customer_follow_up_other`, text, optional): If you follow up with customers in other ways not listed above, enter them here.
