# Resource Collaboration Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from collaborators regarding their experience with resource allocation and collaboration on projects. The purpose of this survey is to understand how well resources are shared and utilized across teams, and to identify areas for improvement in our collaboration processes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer them as honestly as possible.
2. For each question, select or input the most accurate response from the options provided.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions that are not captured in the questions, feel free to add them in the "Project Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Resource Collaboration Feedback

* **Resource Collaboration Feedback**: This field is a text input where you can provide feedback on your experience with resource allocation and collaboration on the project. Please keep your response concise and focused on the collaboration aspects of the project. This field is not required, but your feedback is valuable and helps us improve our collaboration processes.

### Shared Resources

* **Shared Resources**: This field has two options:
	+ **Yes**: You believe that shared resources have been effective in helping you complete your tasks.
	+ **No**: You do not believe that shared resources have been effective in helping you complete your tasks.
Select the option that best reflects your experience on this project.

### Collaboration Outcomes

* **Collaboration Outcomes**: This field is a text input where you can describe the positive outcomes or benefits you experienced as a result of working with the resources assigned to you on this project. Please focus on the specific outcomes and be as specific as possible.

### Project Outcome Rating

* **Project Outcome Rating**: This field has four options:
	+ **Very Poor**: The project did not meet your expectations.
	+ **Fair**: The project met some of your expectations, but there were significant issues.
	+ **Good**: The project met most of your expectations.
	+ **Excellent**: The project exceeded your expectations.
Select the option that best reflects your overall experience with the project.

### Project Description

* **Project Description**: This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the project or resource collaboration process. This field is not required, but your feedback is valuable and helps us improve our processes.
