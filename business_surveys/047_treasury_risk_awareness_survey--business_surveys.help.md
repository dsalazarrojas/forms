# Treasury Risk Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Risk Awareness Survey is designed to assess employees' awareness and understanding of treasury risk factors, including fraud prevention, detection, and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all that apply for each question.
2. Enter a brief answer to each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Risk Factors**: This section asks about the risk factors that are currently a priority for the organization.
	+ **Risk of fraud**: This is the risk of fraudulent activities, such as scams, phishing, or other types of deception.
	+ **Risk of financial loss**: This is the risk of losing money or resources due to various factors, such as market fluctuations or poor investment decisions.
	+ **Risk of regulatory non-compliance**: This is the risk of failing to meet regulatory requirements or standards.
* **Risk Factors Answer**: This field is for providing a brief description of the risk factors selected.
* **Risk Factors Awareness**: This section asks about the level of awareness of the risk factors.
	+ **High**: This indicates a high level of awareness and understanding of the risk factors.
	+ **Medium**: This indicates a moderate level of awareness and understanding of the risk factors.
	+ **Low**: This indicates a low level of awareness and understanding of the risk factors.
* **Risk Factors Awareness Reason**: This field is for providing a brief explanation of the level of awareness selected.
* **Fraud**: This section asks about the methods of preventing fraud.
	+ **What is fraud?**: This is a definition of fraud.
	+ **How is fraud perpetrated?**: This is how fraud is carried out, such as through identity theft or phishing.
	+ **How can we prevent fraud?**: This is how to prevent fraud, such as through education or internal controls.
* **Fraud Answer**: This field is for providing a brief description of the methods of preventing fraud.
* **Fraud Prevention**: This section asks about the methods of preventing fraud.
	+ **Awareness and education**: This is educating employees about fraud.
	+ **Internal controls**: This is implementing controls to prevent internal fraud.
	+ **Regulatory compliance**: This is ensuring that internal controls and procedures are in place to prevent regulatory non-compliance.
* **Fraud Prevention Answer**: This field is for providing a brief description of the methods of preventing fraud.
* **Fraud Prevention Reason**: This field is for providing a brief explanation of the methods of preventing fraud selected.
* **Fraud Prevention Consequences**: This section asks about the consequences of not preventing fraud.
* **Fraud Prevention Consequences Answer**: This field is for providing a brief description of the consequences of not preventing fraud.
* **Fraud Detection**: This section asks about the methods of detecting fraud.
	+ **What are some signs of fraud?**: This is identifying indicators of potential fraud.
	+ **What are some red flags for fraud?**: This is identifying specific behaviors or actions that indicate potential fraud.
	+ **How often should we check for fraud?**: This is the frequency of fraud detection activities.
* **Fraud Detection Answer**: This field is for providing a brief description of the methods of detecting fraud.
* **Fraud Detection Method**: This field is for providing a brief description of the methods of detecting fraud.
* **Fraud Reporting**: This section asks about the methods of reporting fraud.
	+ **Who should report fraud?**: This is who is responsible for reporting fraud.
	+ **How soon should fraud be reported?**: This is the time frame for reporting fraud.
	+ **Where should fraud be reported?**: This is the location for reporting fraud.
* **Fraud Reporting Answer**: This field is for providing a brief description of the methods of reporting fraud.
* **Fraud Reporting Method**: This field is for providing a brief description of the methods of reporting fraud.
* **Fraud Reporting Consequences**: This section asks about the consequences of not reporting fraud.
* **Fraud Reporting Consequences Answer**: This field is for providing a brief description of the consequences of not reporting fraud.
* **Fraud Reporting Consequences Reason**: This field is for providing a brief explanation of the consequences of not reporting fraud.
