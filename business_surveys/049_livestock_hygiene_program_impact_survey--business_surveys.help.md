# Livestock Hygiene Program Impact Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather information about the Livestock Hygiene Program's impact on farms, with the goal of improving the program's effectiveness and providing valuable insights for future development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), with accurate and detailed information.
3. For fields with options, select the most relevant choice for your situation.
4. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (id: 1, required: false)
	+ This field is for capturing any general comments or remarks about the Livestock Hygiene Program.
* **Contact Instructions** (id: 2, required: false)
	+ Share any contact information or instructions for the surveyor to reach you or your organization.
* **Farm Name** (id: 3, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of your farm or organization.
* **Region** (id: 4, required: false)
	+ Specify the region where your farm or organization is located.
* **Contact Person** (id: 5, required: false)
	+ Provide the name of the person to contact in case of any questions or concerns.
* **Email** (id: 6, required: false)
	+ Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (id: 7, required: false)
	+ Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Farm Type** (id: 8, required: false)
	+ Select the type of farm or organization (Farm, Livestock, or Other).
* **Farm Size** (id: 9, required: false)
	+ Describe the size of your farm or organization.
* **Livestock** (id: 10, required: false)
	+ Select all types of livestock you have on your farm or organization (Cattle, Sheep, Pigs, Horses, Goats, Chickens, Ducks, or Other).
* **Contact Email** (id: 11, required: false)
	+ Enter the email address of the contact person again, if different from previous entry.
* **Contact Phone** (id: 12, required: false)
	+ Enter the phone number of the contact person again, if different from previous entry.
* **Contact Address** (id: 13, required: false)
	+ Provide the address of the contact person or organization.
* **Contact Notes** (id: 14, required: false)
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about your contact person or organization.
* **Program Type** (id: 15, required: false)
	+ Select the type of the Livestock Hygiene Program (Livestock, or Other).
* **Hygiene Sessions** (id: 16, required: false)
	+ Describe any sessions or events related to the Livestock Hygiene Program.
* **Sessions Held** (id: 17, required: false)
	+ Specify the number of sessions or events held.
* **Next Session Date** (id: 18, required: false)
	+ Enter the date of the next session or event.
* **Next Session Time** (id: 19, required: false)
	+ Specify the time of the next session or event.
* **Session Duration** (id: 20, required: false)
	+ Describe the duration of the next session or event.
* **Next Session Location** (id: 21, required: false)
	+ Provide the location of the next session or event.
* **Program Funding** (id: 22, required: false)
	+ Describe the funding sources for the Livestock Hygiene Program.
* **Program Cost** (id: 23, required: false)
	+ Enter the cost of the Livestock Hygiene Program.
* **Farm Subsidies** (id: 24, required: false)
	+ Discuss any farm subsidies received.
* **Other Questions** (id: 25, required: false)
	+ Add any additional comments or questions about the Livestock Hygiene Program.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
