# Seller Discovery Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seller Discovery Survey is a form used to collect information about sellers. It is designed to be completed by the seller themselves or by an administrator on their behalf. The data collected through this form will be used to identify and categorize sellers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
5. Enter your zip code in the "Zip Code" field.
6. Select your date of birth from the calendar in the "Date of Birth" field.
7. Select your time of live in the "Time of Live" field.
8. Select your seller type from the "Seller Type" field.
9. Choose your preferred contact method(s) in the "Seller Preferences" field.
10. Provide a note about yourself in the "Note" field.
11. Select your survey status in the "Survey Status" field.
12. Enter your user ID in the "User ID" field.
13. Select your user type from the "User Type" field.
14. Enter the survey ID in the "Survey ID" field.
15. Enter the form ID in the "Form ID" field.
16. Select the form version from the "Form Version" field.
17. Enter the date and time the form was last updated in the "Updated At" field.
18. Enter the date and time the form was created in the "Created At" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (1)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (2)**: Enter your email address that you use to receive notifications and updates.
* **Phone (3)**: Enter your phone number that you would like to be contacted on.
* **Address (4)**: Enter your physical address where you can be contacted.
* **Zip Code (5)**: Enter your zip code as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (6)**: Select your date of birth from the calendar.
* **Time of Live (7)**: Select your time of live from the available options.
* **Seller Type (8)**: Select your type of seller (Individual, Company, or Other).
* **Seller Preferences (9)**: Choose your preferred contact method(s) (Email, Phone, or Other).
* **Note (10)**: Provide a note about yourself that will help us understand your profile.
* **Survey Status (11)**: Select the status of your survey (Active, Inactive, or Other).
* **User ID (12)**: Enter your user ID that is used to track your account.
* **User Type (13)**: Select your user type (Seller, Administrator, or Other).
* **Survey ID (14)**: Enter the ID of the survey that this form is part of.
* **Form ID (15)**: Enter the ID of the form that this survey is using.
* **Form Version (16)**: Select the version of the form that is currently being used.
* **Updated At (17)**: Enter the date and time the form was last updated.
* **Created At (18)**: Enter the date and time the form was created.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all the information you provide before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your administrator for clarification.
* Provide accurate and honest information to the best of your knowledge.
* If you need to update any information, please contact your administrator for assistance.
