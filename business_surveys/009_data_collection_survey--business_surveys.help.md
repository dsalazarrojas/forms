# Data Collection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Collection Survey is designed to collect demographic and personal data from individuals or businesses. This form is meant to gather information about the respondent, including their name, contact details, preferences, and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the required fields (indicated by an asterisk (*) in the field label) as much as possible.
2. For each field, follow the label and instructions provided:
   - First Name: Enter your full first name.
   - Email: Enter your business email address.
   - Category: Select your category (Business or Feedback).
   - Tags: Select one or more tags that best describe your area of expertise.
   - Description: Provide a brief description of your business or feedback.
   - Time: If applicable, enter the time you are most available or prefer to be contacted.
   - Date: Enter your date of birth or anniversary.
   - Salary: If applicable, enter your annual salary.
   - Status: Select the status of your business (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
   - Languages: Select one or more languages you are proficient in.
   - Phone: Enter your phone number.
   - Age: If applicable, enter your age.
   - Education: Select your highest level of education (High School Diploma, College, University).
   - Country: Select your country of residence.
   - Hobbies: Select one or more hobbies you enjoy.
   - Birthday: Enter your date of birth.
   - Mobile: Enter your mobile phone number.
   - Note: Provide any additional notes or comments.
