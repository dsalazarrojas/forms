# Workplace Inclusivity Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Inclusivity Index Survey is a tool used to measure how inclusive and supportive employees feel in their workplace. It aims to collect information about various aspects of employee experience, including job satisfaction, team satisfaction, and inclusivity. This survey helps managers and HR teams identify areas for improvement and promote a more inclusive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and question carefully to ensure you answer all the required questions.
2. Answer all questions honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Select the correct option for each question that best reflects your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (1): This is a brief note about the survey's purpose and objectives.
* **Demographics** (2): Please provide your basic demographic information, such as job role and job type.
* **Job Satisfaction** (3): Rate your job satisfaction on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Strongly Disagree" and 5 is "Strongly Agree".
* **Team Satisfaction** (4): Rate your team satisfaction on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Strongly Disagree" and 5 is "Strongly Agree".
* **Inclusivity** (5): Select the option that best reflects your agreement level with the statement "I feel included and supported in my workplace" on a scale from 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree).
* **Belonging** (6): Select all the options that best describe how you feel about belonging in your workplace, on a scale from 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree).
* **Feedback** (7): Share any additional comments or concerns you have about your workplace experience.
* **Contact Info** (8): Enter your contact information, including your email address, in case we need to follow up with you.
* **Job Role** (9): Select your job role from the options provided.
* **Job Type** (10): Select your job type from the options provided.
* **Department** (11): Enter your department name.
* **Manager Info** (12): Enter any relevant information about your manager.
* **Job Title** (13): Enter your job title.
* **Work Hours** (14): Rate the number of work hours you have per week.
* **Work Week** (15): Select the days you work during the week.
* **Work Shift** (16): Select your work shift from the options provided.
* **Breaks** (17): Rate the number of breaks you take per day.
* **Work from Home** (18): Select how often you work from home.
* **Team Size** (19): Select the size of your team.
* **Job Satisfaction Level** (20): Rate your job satisfaction level on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Team Satisfaction Level** (21): Rate your team satisfaction level on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Inclusivity Level** (22): Rate your sense of inclusion in the workplace on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Belonging Level** (23): Rate how well you feel you belong in your workplace on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Work Pace** (24): Select your preferred work pace from the options provided.
* **Submission** (25): Confirm your submission, indicating whether you'd like to share your feedback with your manager or keep it anonymous.
