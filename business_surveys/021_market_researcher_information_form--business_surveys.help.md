# Market Researcher Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from market researchers about their personal contact details and work experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that you regularly use for work-related purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter a phone number where you can be reached for work-related discussions.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and honestly to facilitate smooth communication and collaboration with our team.
* If you experience any issues while filling out this form, please reach out to our support team for assistance.
