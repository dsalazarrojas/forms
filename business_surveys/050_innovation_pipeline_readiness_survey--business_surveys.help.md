# Innovation Pipeline Readiness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Innovation Pipeline Readiness Survey is designed to assess the maturity and effectiveness of your organization's innovation pipeline.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question according to your organization's current state.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Does your organization have an innovation pipeline in place?** (`has_innovation_pipeline`, select_one): This question assesses whether your organization has a structured process for driving innovation and growth.
* **Please rate your organization's innovation pipeline readiness (on a scale of 1-5)** (`has_innovation_pipeline_score`, number): This question gauges your organization's current level of innovation pipeline readiness, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **What are the stages of the innovation pipeline that your organization is currently using?** (`business_sightings`, text): This question asks you to describe the stages of the innovation pipeline that your organization is actively utilizing.
* **Do you have a clear understanding of the innovation pipeline and its benefits?** (`has_innovation_pipeline_readiness`, select_one): This question evaluates your organization's comprehension of the innovation pipeline and its advantages.
* **How do you measure the performance of your innovation pipeline?** (`innovation_pipeline_score_1`, number): This question inquires about your organization's metrics or methods for evaluating the innovation pipeline's performance.
* **How does your organization's innovation pipeline support strategic decision-making?** (`has_innovation_pipeline_readiness_1`, select_one): This question assesses whether your organization uses the innovation pipeline to inform strategic decisions.
* **What are some recent business sightings that have come from the innovation pipeline?** (`business_sightings_1`, text): This question seeks examples of recent business sightings or successes that have resulted from the innovation pipeline.
* **Does your organization have a clear understanding of the innovation pipeline and its benefits?** (`has_innovation_pipeline_1`, select_one): This question evaluates your organization's understanding of the innovation pipeline's value.
* **Innovation Pipeline Stages 1** (`innovation_pipeline_stages_1`, select_one): This question gauges your organization's adoption and utilization of the innovation pipeline's initial stages.
* **How does your organization's innovation pipeline align with your organization's goals?** (`innovation_pipeline_stages_2`, select_one): This question assesses the alignment between the innovation pipeline and your organization's objectives.
* **What are the challenges faced by your organization in developing and implementing its innovation pipeline?** (`business_sightings_2`, text): This question inquires about any obstacles or difficulties your organization has encountered while developing or implementing the innovation pipeline.
* **What is the score for your organization's innovation pipeline readiness?** (`innovation_pipeline_readiness_score`, number): This question seeks your organization's current level of innovation pipeline readiness, with 1 being low and 5 being high.
* **Innovation Pipeline Stages** (`innovation_pipeline_stages`, select_one): This question evaluates your organization's adoption and utilization of the innovation pipeline's subsequent stages.
* **Has Innovation Pipeline** (`has_innovation_pipeline_2`, select_multiple): This question gauges your organization's overall presence and use of the innovation pipeline.
* **Innovation Pipeline Readiness** (`innovation_pipeline_readiness`, select_one): This question assesses your organization's current level of innovation pipeline readiness, with 'Yes' indicating a clear understanding and high readiness.
