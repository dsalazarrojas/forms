# Business Studies Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Studies Assessment form is a tool used to evaluate students' understanding and performance in the Business Studies course.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Student Name field with your full name as it appears on your student ID.
2. In the Student ID field, enter your student ID number.
3. In the Course Name field, type the name of the course you are enrolled in.
4. In the Course Code field, enter the course code or number (if applicable).
5. In the Instructor Name field, type the name of your instructor.
6. In the Assessment Date field, select the date of the assessment.
7. In the Type of Assessment field, select the type of assessment you are taking.
8. For each section of the form, rate your understanding or skills from Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, or Poor.
9. In the Overall Assessment Score field, enter a percentage or grade for your overall performance.
10. If desired, provide your Instructor Feedback and Recommendations for Improvement in the respective text fields.
