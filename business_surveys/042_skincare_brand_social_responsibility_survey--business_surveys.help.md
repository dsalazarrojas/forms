# Skincare Brand Social Responsibility Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your level of awareness and perception of our company's social responsibility efforts. We strive to understand your thoughts on what matters most when making choices about skincare brands, and how we can improve our social responsibility performance. Your input will help us to better serve your needs and create a more positive impact in the market.

## How To Complete This Form

To provide valuable insights, please answer each question to the best of your ability. The form is divided into multiple sections, each focusing on a different aspect of our social responsibility efforts. You can navigate through the form using the next and previous buttons. When you reach the end of the form, your answers will be collected and displayed for review.
