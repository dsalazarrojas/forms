# International Buyer Return Policy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from international buyers on their experiences with our return policy. Your input will help us understand your challenges and improve our return policy to provide a better experience for you and all our international buyers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the relevant options that apply to your experiences with our return policy. For each question, provide a clear and concise response that reflects your honest opinion. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the "Additional comments" field (not shown in this guide).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How clear is the return policy on your website?** (Return Clarity): Please describe your understanding of the return policy as displayed on our website. This will help us understand if our policy is clear and easy to understand.
* **What challenges do you face when returning items?** (Return Challenges): Share any difficulties or obstacles you have encountered when trying to return items purchased from us. This will help us identify areas for improvement in our return process.
* **How satisfied are you with the return process?** (Return Satisfaction): Rate your satisfaction level with the return process, from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **How often do you return items?** (Return Frequency): Select all the options that apply to your return frequency. This will help us understand how often you return items and what we can do to improve our return process.
* **How satisfied are you with the return frequency?** (Return Satisfaction Frequency): Rate your satisfaction level with the return frequency, from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **How satisfied are you with the return process globally?** (Global Return Satisfaction): Choose one of the options above that best describes your satisfaction level with our global return process.
* **How clear is the return process for global returns?** (Return Process Clarity): Please describe your understanding of the global return process. This will help us understand if our process is clear and easy to understand for international returns.
