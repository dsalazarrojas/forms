# Habilitation Program Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from participants in the habilitation program. Your input will help us understand your overall experience and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the option that best describes your overall experience in the program.
2. For questions with multiple choice options, select all that apply.
3. Be as honest and thorough as possible in your answers, as your feedback is valuable for our program's improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Experience**: This question asks you to rate your overall experience in the program. Choose the option that best fits your feelings:
  • Very Good: You are very satisfied with your experience.
  • Average: You are fairly neutral, and your experience is okay.
  • Poor: You are not satisfied with your experience.
* **Facility Rating**: This question asks you to rate the facilities provided by the program. Choose a number that best fits your perception:
  • A higher number indicates better facilities.
* **Instructor Attitude**: This question asks you to rate the attitude of the instructors. Choose the option that best fits your feelings:
  • Very Positive: The instructors were very supportive and knowledgeable.
  • Positive: The instructors were supportive and knowledgeable.
  • Negative: The instructors were not supportive or knowledgeable.
* **Support Services**: This question asks you to choose all the support services that you found useful:
  • Very Good: You found the support services very helpful.
  • Somewhat Good: You found the support services somewhat helpful.
  • Very Poor: You found the support services not helpful.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the program. Choose the option that best fits your feelings:
  • Very Satisfied: You are very satisfied with the program.
  • Satisfied: You are fairly satisfied with the program.
  • Not Satisfied: You are not satisfied with the program.
* **Facilities Quality**: This question asks you to rate the quality of the facilities provided by the program. Choose the option that best fits your perception:
  • Very Good: The facilities were very good.
  • Average: The facilities were fairly neutral.
  • Poor: The facilities were not very good.
* **Instructor Support**: This question asks you to choose all the support services that you found helpful:
  • Very Helpful: You found the support services very helpful.
  • Somewhat Helpful: You found the support services somewhat helpful.
  • Not Very Helpful: You found the support services not helpful.
* **Program Location**: This question asks you to rate the location of the program. Choose the option that best fits your perception:
  • Very Good: You are very satisfied with the program location.
  • Average: You are fairly satisfied with the program location.
  • Very Poor: You are not satisfied with the program location.
* **Program Attitude**: This question asks you to rate the attitude of the program. Choose the option that best fits your feelings:
  • Very Positive: The program has a very positive attitude.
  • Positive: The program has a positive attitude.
  • Negative: The program has a negative attitude.
* **Instructor Availability**: This question asks you to choose all the support services that you found helpful:
  • Very Good: You found the instructors available and helpful.
  • Somewhat Good: You found the instructors somewhat available.
  • Very Poor: You found the instructors not available.
* **Program Location**: This question asks you to rate the location of the program. Choose the option that best fits your perception:
  • Very Good: You are very satisfied with the program location.
  • Average: You are fairly satisfied with the program location.
  • Very Poor: You are not satisfied with the program location.

Please note that this is a mandatory form, and all fields are required.
