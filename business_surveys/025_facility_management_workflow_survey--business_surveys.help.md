# Facility Management Workflow Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and insights from end-users on the current facility management workflow. It aims to identify areas for improvement and help develop more efficient and effective processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select the best option for each question, even if it's a simple "Yes" or "No".
3. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" section.
5. Date and time the submission to ensure it's accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Management Workflow Questionnaire** (`facility_management_workflow_questionnaire`, select_one): This question asks about your overall experience with the facility management workflow. Choose "Yes" if you've had a positive experience, and "No" otherwise.
* **Current Location** (`current_location`, select_one): This question asks about your current location in relation to the facility management workflow. Choose "Yes" if you're currently using the workflow, and "No" if you're not.
* **Workflow Satisfaction** (`workflow_satisfaction`, number): This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with the current workflow on a scale of 1-10.
* **Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, text): This question asks you to describe any challenges you've faced while using the facility management workflow. Please provide a detailed explanation.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, text): This question asks you to share any suggestions or ideas for improving the facility management workflow. Please provide a detailed explanation.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text): This question asks you to provide any additional feedback or comments about the facility management workflow. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text): This question is for any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email): This question asks for your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text): This question asks for your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date): Please provide the date you're submitting this form.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time): Please provide the time you're submitting this form.
