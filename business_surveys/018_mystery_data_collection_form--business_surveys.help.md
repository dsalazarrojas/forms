# Mystery Data Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data for business purposes, likely for research or analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required and optional fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name (Text)**: Please fill in your first name.
*   **Last Name (Text)**: Please fill in your last name.
*   **Email Address (Email)**: Please provide a valid email address.
*   **Phone Number (Text)**: Please fill in your phone number.
*   **Date (Date)**: Please enter a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Time (Time)**: Please enter a time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
*   **Note (Note)**: Please provide any additional information you'd like to share.
*   **Additional Note (Note)**: Please provide any further information you'd like to share.
*   **What is your favorite food? (Select One)**: Please select one option from the list.
*   **Which of the following is correct? (Select Multiple)**: Please select all applicable options from the list.
