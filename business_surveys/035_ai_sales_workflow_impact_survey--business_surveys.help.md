# AI Sales Workflow Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Sales Workflow Impact Survey is designed to gather feedback from sales teams on their experiences with AI tools used in their sales workflow. This information will help shape future investments in AI to ensure that AI sales platforms meet the real-world needs of sales teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the guidelines below:

* Please answer all required fields (denoted by an asterisk `*`).
* Select all options that apply for multiple selection questions.
* Be as descriptive as possible in your answers.
* You can skip questions that do not apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Sales Team Information** (`--sales_team_information--`, `note`, optional): This is an introductory section providing context for the survey.
* **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name, which will be used to provide attribution for your responses.
* **Sales team size** (`team_size`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of people in your sales team.
* **AI tools currently in use** (`ai_tools_in_use`, `select_multiple`, true*): Select all AI tools currently used by your team (Lead scoring, Automated outreach, Conversational agents, CRM augmentation, Forecasting).
* **Observed benefits from AI tools** (`observed_benefits`, `select_multiple`, true*): Select all benefits observed from using AI tools (Higher conversion, Faster response, Better lead qualification, Time saved on admin tasks).
* **Workflow disruptions experienced** (`workflow_disruptions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all workflow disruptions experienced (Integration issues, Incorrect recommendations, Data syncing problems, User adoption resistance).
* **Estimated ROI from AI tools** (`roi_estimate`, `number`, optional): Enter an estimate of the ROI improvement or dollar value from AI tools.
* **Training needs for sales team** (`training_needed`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all training needs for your sales team (Tool usage, Interpreting recommendations, Data hygiene, Change management).
* **Priority for further investment in AI** (`implementation_priority`, `select_one`, true*): Select the priority for further investment in AI (High, Medium, Low).
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experiences with AI tools.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and thoroughly.
* This information will help guide future investments in AI tools.
* If unsure, skip questions that do not apply to your experience.
* If you have additional comments or feedback, enter them in the "Additional comments" field.
