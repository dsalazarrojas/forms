# documentation_notes_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and documentation notes on business processes or products. The collected data will help in identifying areas that require improvement and inform further development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. For each section, review the fields carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Select all applicable options for the "Business Surveys" section.
3. Answer the "Feedback Surveys" section questions with a simple "Yes" or "No".
4. In the "Documentation Notes Survey" form, type any notes or comments related to the documentation.
5. Provide the product or process name in the "Product or Process Documentation" field.
6. Rate the clarity of the documentation on a scale of 1 to 5 in the "Clarity" field.
7. Rate the usefulness of the documentation on a scale of 1 to 5 in the "Usefulness" field.
8. Provide any specific improvements or recommendations in the "Target Improvements" field.
9. Indicate if any documentation was collected by "Yes" or "No" in the "Collected By" field.
10. If collected by someone else, provide their name or initials in the "Collected By Other" field.
11. Select all applicable options for the "Improvement Action" section.
12. Select all applicable options for the "Submitted By" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Surveys** (`business_surveys`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all applicable options to indicate the current state of business processes or products.
* **Feedback Surveys** (`feedback_surveys`, Select Multiple, Required): Answer with "Yes" or "No" to provide feedback on business processes or products.
* **Documentation Notes Survey** (`documentation_notes_survey_form`, Text, Required): Type any notes or comments related to the documentation.
* **Product or Process Documentation** (`product_or_process_documentation`, Text, Required): Provide the name of the product or process being documented.
* **Clarity** (`clarity`, Number, Required): Rate the clarity of the documentation on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Usefulness** (`usefulness`, Number, Required): Rate the usefulness of the documentation on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Target Improvements** (`target_improvements`, Text, Required): Provide any specific improvements or recommendations.
* **Collected By** (`collected_by`, Select One, Required): Indicate if any documentation was collected by "Yes" or "No".
* **Collected By Other** (`collected_by_other`, Text, Optional): If collected by someone else, provide their name or initials.
* **Improvement Action** (`improvement_action`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all applicable options for further action.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all applicable options for submitted by.
