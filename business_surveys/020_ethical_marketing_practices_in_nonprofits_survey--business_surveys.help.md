# Ethical Marketing Practices In Nonprofits Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the awareness and understanding of ethical marketing practices among nonprofit professionals. It aims to collect data on the challenges and barriers to adopting these practices, as well as their attitudes towards marketing practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully, and answer the questions based on your understanding of ethical marketing practices.
2. For the awareness level questions, rate your level of awareness on a scale of 1-100.
3. For the attitude scale questions, rate your attitude on a scale of 1-5.
4. Make sure to answer all questions honestly and accurately.
