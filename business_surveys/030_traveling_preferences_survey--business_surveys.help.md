# Traveling Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to understand your preferences when it comes to traveling. It will help businesses provide better services for your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and select the option that best fits your preferences.
2. Fill in the answers to each question, and make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.
3. Take your time, and don't hesitate to ask for help if you need it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Traveling Frequency** (`Often`, `Occasionally`, `Rarely`): This question asks about how often you travel, which will help businesses understand your travel habits.
* **Destination Type** (`Beaches`, `Cities`, `Nature reserves`): This question asks about the type of destinations you prefer to visit, such as relaxing on beaches, exploring cities, or visiting nature reserves.
* **Mode of Transportation** (`Air`, `Land`, `Sea`): This question asks about your preferred mode of transportation, such as flying, driving, or taking a boat or ship.
* **Budget** (`Low`, `Medium`, `High`): This question asks about your budget for traveling, from budget-friendly options to more expensive ones.
* **Type of Accommodation** (`Hotel`, `Hostel`, `Airbnb`): This question asks about your preferred type of accommodation, such as staying in a hotel, hostel, or renting an apartment through a platform like Airbnb.
* **Comfort Level** (`High`, `Medium`, `Low`): This question asks about your comfort level when traveling, from luxury to basic comfort.
* **Conclusion** (text): This is a final open-ended question where you can share any additional thoughts or comments about your preferences.
