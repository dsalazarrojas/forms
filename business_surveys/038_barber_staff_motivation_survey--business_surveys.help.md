# Barber Staff Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us make this shop a better place for you to work. Your responses are confidential and will help us identify areas of improvement to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and select the answer that best reflects your opinion.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (*).
3. You can select multiple options for questions that allow it (e.g., "Select all that apply").
4. Enter your comments and feedback in the relevant text fields.
5. Finally, review your responses before submitting the form.
