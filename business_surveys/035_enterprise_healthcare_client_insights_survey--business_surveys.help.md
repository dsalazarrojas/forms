# Enterprise Healthcare Client Insights Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from Enterprise Healthcare clients about their experiences with the service, which will help the business to make data-driven decisions and improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and contact number, which will help us to reach out to you if needed.
2. Provide your email address so we can contact you with any updates or follow-ups.
3. Rate your satisfaction level with our service (Satisfied or Dissatisfied).
4. Share any improvement ideas you have for our service.
5. Let us know if you would like to refer others to our service (Yes or No).
6. Enter the date of your last contact with our service.
7. Enter the time of your last contact with our service.
8. Provide any service decisions made for you.
9. Add any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Name
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached at.

### Client Email
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached at.

### Client Satisfaction
* **Client Satisfaction** (`client_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction level with our service (Satisfied or Dissatisfied).

### Improvement Ideas
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, `text`, required: false): Share any ideas you have for us to improve our service.

### Referral Intent
* **Referral Intent** (`referral_intent`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate if you would like to refer others to our service (Yes or No).

### Date of Last Contact
* **Date of Last Contact** (`date_of_last_contact`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your last contact with our service.

### Time of Last Contact
* **Time of Last Contact** (`time_of_last_contact`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your last contact with our service.

### Service Decisions
* **Service Decisions** (`service_decisions`, `note`, required: false): Provide any service decisions made for you.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any comments or feedback you may have about our service.
