# Sales Cycle Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Cycle Awareness Survey is designed to gather information about your team's knowledge and awareness of the sales cycle process. This survey helps us understand your team's strengths and weaknesses, allowing us to provide targeted support and training to improve performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the answers that apply to your team's sales cycle experience. For each question, choose the options that best describe your team's current state. You can select one or multiple options for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sales Cycle Knowledge**: Please select all the areas where your team has sales cycle knowledge. This will help us understand what your team already knows about the sales cycle process.
	+ Select 'Yes' if your team has knowledge in this area.
* **Biggest Challenge**: Identify the biggest challenge your team is currently facing with the sales cycle process.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you face this challenge.
* **Sales Cycle Process**: Indicate areas where your team follows the sales cycle process.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you follow this process.
* **Sales Cycle Phases**: List all the phases your team is familiar with in the sales cycle process.
	+ Select 'Yes' for each phase you are familiar with.
* **Sales Cycle Phases Details**: Provide any additional details about your team's understanding of the sales cycle phases.
* **Sales Cycle Objectives**: Identify the sales cycle objectives your team strives to achieve.
	+ Select 'Yes' for each objective.
* **Sales Cycle Leaders Role**: Indicate how sales leaders contribute to the sales cycle process.
	+ Select 'Yes' for each role.
* **Sales Cycle Leaders Challenges**: Identify the challenges sales leaders face in the sales cycle process.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you face this challenge.
* **Sales Cycle Leaders Support**: Indicate how sales leaders support the sales cycle process.
	+ Select 'Yes' for each support option.
* **Sales Cycle Coaches Role**: Indicate how sales coaches contribute to the sales cycle process.
	+ Select 'Yes' for each role.
* **Sales Cycle Coaches Challenges**: Identify the challenges sales coaches face in the sales cycle process.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you face this challenge.
* **Sales Cycle Coaches Support**: Indicate how sales coaches support the sales cycle process.
	+ Select 'Yes' for each support option.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your team's sales cycle experience.
* **Additional Sales Cycle Knowledge Information**: Is there any additional information about your team's sales cycle knowledge that you'd like to share? 
	+ Select 'Yes' to provide more information.
