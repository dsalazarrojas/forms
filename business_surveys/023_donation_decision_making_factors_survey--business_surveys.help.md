# donation_decision_making_factors_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about factors that influence donation decisions. It will help us understand donor demographics, donation frequency, motivations, and barriers to making donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Carefully review each question and ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. For questions with options, select one or multiple answers that best describe your situation.
3. If you encounter any issues or have difficulty completing a question, please contact us for assistance.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Introduction** (1/1)
*   **Introduction** (note): This section provides an overview of the survey purpose and objectives.

### **demographic_information** (2/1)
*   **demographic_information** (text): Please provide a brief description of your demographic information.

### **donation_frequency** (3/1)
*   **donation_frequency** (text): Describe your donation frequency, e.g., how often you make donations.

### **donation_amount** (4/1)
*   **donation_amount** (number): Enter the amount you typically donate or would like to donate.

### **donor_motive** (5/1)
*   **donor_motive** (select one):
    -   Financial need
    -   Personal relationship
    -   Social responsibility
    -   Other

### **donor_barriers** (6/1)
*   **donor_barriers** (select multiple):
    -   Lack of information
    -   Lack of time
    -   Lack of trust
    -   Other

### **preferences** (7/1)
*   **preferences** (select one):
    -   Donating online
    -   Donating via phone
    -   Donating at a store
    -   Other

## Tips
*   Please ensure your responses are honest and accurate to help us improve our donation processes.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
*   This survey is for internal use and will help us better understand your needs and preferences.
