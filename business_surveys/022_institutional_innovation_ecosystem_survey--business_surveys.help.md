# Institutional Innovation Ecosystem Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Institutional Innovation Ecosystem Survey is a form designed to gather information about institutions and their innovation ecosystems. This form helps to understand how institutions are innovating and what challenges they face.

## How To Complete This Form

Before you start filling out this form, please make sure to read through it carefully. The form is divided into multiple sections with different questions and options. Each section is a separate part of the survey, and you will be able to navigate through them easily. If you have any questions or concerns, please refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.
