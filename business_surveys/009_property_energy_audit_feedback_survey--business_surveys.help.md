# Property Energy Audit Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Property Energy Audit Feedback Survey form, a tool designed to collect feedback from customers or visitors after a property energy audit has been conducted. The feedback collected through this form will help the organization improve their services and better meet the needs of their customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter the Property Address where the energy audit was conducted.
5. Select the Date you visited the property from the calendar.
6. Choose the Time of your visit.
7. Report your Energy Consumption for the property.
8. Provide the Total Floor Area of the property.
9. Rate the property's Energy Star Rating (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
10. Share any Feedback about your experience.
11. Add Additional Comments about your visit or the energy audit service.
12. Enter the name of the Contact Person who accompanied you during the energy audit.
13. Provide the Contact Phone number for the Contact Person.
14. Enter the Contact Email for the Contact Person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (First Name)**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (Last Name)**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter the email address associated with your contact information.
* **Property Address (Property Address)**: Type the full address of the property where the energy audit was conducted.
* **Date Visited (Date Visited)**: Select the date of your visit using a calendar or entering it in a specific format (e.g., mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Time Visited (Time Visited)**: Choose the time of your visit using a 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Energy Consumption (Energy Consumption)**: Report your total energy consumption for the property.
* **Total Floor Area (Total Floor Area)**: Enter the total floor area of the property in square meters or feet.
* **Energy Star Rating (Energy Star Rating)**: Choose one of the four available ratings: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Feedback (Feedback)**: Share any comments or suggestions you have about your experience with the energy audit service.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments)**: Add any additional thoughts or comments about your visit or the service.
* **Contact Person Name (Contact Person Name)**: Enter the name of the person who was in contact with you during the energy audit.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.

## Tips
* Ensure that you're filling out the form with accurate and honest information.
* If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact us for clarification.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we'll use it to improve our services.
