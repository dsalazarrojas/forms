# Psychological Safety At Work Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help your organization understand the psychological safety levels of your team members. It is part of an initiative to improve work-related well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and answer the questions accordingly. The questions are self-explanatory and should take about 5-10 minutes to complete. Make sure to answer each question truthfully to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Safety** (`Team Safety`, number, required false): This field asks about your perception of your team's overall psychological safety. Please answer on a scale from 0 to 5, where 0 means very low and 5 means very high.
* **Trust** (`Trust`, select_one, required false): This question asks about the level of trust you have with your team members. Please select one of the options: Open, High, Medium, or Low.
* **Openness** (`Openness`, number, required false): This field asks about how often you feel you can be open and honest with your team members about your thoughts and feelings. Please answer on a scale from 0 to 5, where 0 means very rarely and 5 means very often.
* **Communication** (`Communication`, select_multiple, required false): This question asks about the communication style of your team members. Please select all the options that apply to you: Regular, Sporadic, Rare, None.
* **Psychological Safety** (`Psychological Safety`, number, required false): This field asks about your perception of your own psychological safety. Please answer on a scale from 0 to 5, where 0 means very low and 5 means very high.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`Job Satisfaction`, select_one, required false): This question asks about your level of job satisfaction. Please select one of the options: High, Medium, Low.
* **Manager Safety** (`Manager Safety`, number, required false): This field asks about your perception of your manager's psychological safety. Please answer on a scale from 0 to 5, where 0 means very low and 5 means very high.
* **Team Dynamics** (`Team Dynamics`, select_multiple, required false): This question asks about the overall dynamics within your team. Please select all the options that apply to you: Good, Bad, Mixed.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, select_multiple, required false): This question asks about how often you receive feedback from your team members. Please select all the options that apply to you: Always, Mostly, Rarely, Never.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, required false): This field is for any additional comments you may have about your team's psychological safety.
* **Date Last Updated** (`Date Last Updated`, date, required false): This field is the date the form was last updated.
* **Time Last Updated** (`Time Last Updated`, time, required false): This field is the time the form was last updated.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your team lead or HR representative.
* Remember, this survey is anonymous and confidential.
