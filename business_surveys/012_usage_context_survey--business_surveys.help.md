# usage_context_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your usage context of our product. The goal is to understand how often you use our product, where you typically use it, and what motivates you to use it. This data will help us improve and tailor our product to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer them as accurately as possible.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. If a question does not apply to you, feel free to leave it blank.
4. Provide as much detail as you can in the text fields (e.g., "How often do you use product?" and "Additional Information").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How often do you use product?** (`introduction`, `text`, required: false): This question asks about your frequency of use. Try to provide a brief description of how often you use our product.
- **Where do you typically use product?** (`usage_location`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all locations where you typically use our product (e.g., home, office, while traveling).
- **Frequency of use** (`usage_frequency`, `text`, required: false): Describe your frequency of use, e.g., daily, weekly, occasionally.
- **What motivates you to use product?** (`usage_motivation`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one reason why you use our product (e.g., necessity, pleasure, curiosity).
- **How can we improve product?** (`improvement_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Suggest ways we can improve our product to better meet your needs.
- **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `note`, required: false): Provide any other information you think is relevant to help us understand your usage context.
- **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted for further discussion).
- **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted for further discussion).
- **Time spent with product** (`time_spent`, `number`, required: false): Estimate the average time you spend using our product.
- **Time of day** (`time_of_day`, `time`, required: false): Indicate when you usually use our product (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
