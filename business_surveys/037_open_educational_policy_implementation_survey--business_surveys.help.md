# Open Educational Policy Implementation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Educational Policy Implementation Survey is designed to assess the current status of Open Educational Policy (OEP) implementation in educational institutions. This survey helps us understand the awareness, effectiveness, challenges, and relationships between key stakeholders in the OEP process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question honestly, taking into account your current role and involvement with OEP.
- Select all applicable options for each question, as multiple options are allowed.
- Use the provided dropdown menus to select your answers.
- You may save your progress and return to the form later, but please complete it in one session for accurate results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Policy awareness** (`policy_awareness`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about your current awareness of the Open Educational Policy. Select all options that apply to you, such as "Yes" if you're well-informed, or "No" if you need more information.
* **Policy effectiveness** (`policy_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how effective you think the current Open Educational Policy is, on a scale from "Yes" (very effective) to "No" (not effective at all).
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, `note`, required: false): Describe any challenges you face in implementing or understanding the Open Educational Policy. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Key stakeholders** (`key_stakeholders`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate which stakeholders (e.g., institutions, teachers, students) you believe are key to the successful implementation of the Open Educational Policy.
* **Stakeholder relationships** (`stakeholder_relationships`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all applicable options describing your relationships with key stakeholders, such as "Yes" for collaboration or "No" for lack of communication.
* **Awareness and challenges** (`awareness_and_challenges`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all options that apply to your current experience with Open Educational Policy, including "Yes" for awareness and "No" for challenges.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly, and do not worry about giving "incorrect" responses.
- If you're unsure about an option or have a comment, use the "Challenges" field to provide more details.
- If you have any further questions or need help, contact your institution's Open Educational Policy team.
