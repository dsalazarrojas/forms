# Workforce Voice Participation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workforce Voice Participation Survey is a tool used to gather feedback and opinions from employees on various aspects of their work experience. This survey aims to improve team communication, engagement, and workplace culture by providing a platform for employees to voice their thoughts and suggestions.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4. Select your department from the "department" field. Please choose from the following options:
	* Human Resources
	* Marketing
	* Operations
	* Sales Reporting
	* Finance
5. Answer the question "Are you a member of a team?" by selecting either "Yes" or "No" in the "team_members" field.
6. In the "communication" field, choose from the following options:
	* Regular updates from managers
	* Regular updates from team members
	* Regular updates from HR
	* Regular updates from leadership
	* Regular updates from other team members
7. For the "engagement" field, choose from the following options:
	* I am engaged
	* I am somewhat engaged
	* I am not engaged
	* I am neutral
8. In the "culture" field, select from the following options:
	* I think this team has a positive culture
	* I think this team has a neutral culture
	* I think this team has a negative culture
9. Choose one or more ways to improve the workplace from the "workplace_improvement" field:
	* Improve communication
	* Improve team building
	* Improve work-life balance
	* Improve job satisfaction
	* Improve employee morale
10. Write any additional comments in the "comments" field.
11. Finally, confirm that the submission was made by selecting "Daily" or choosing "Weekly", "Monthly", "Quarterly" or "Semi-annually" in the "reporting" field.

* **First Name** (First name, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

* **Department** (Department, select_one, optional): Select your department from the list:
	+ Human Resources

	+ Sales Reporting
	+ Finance
* **Team Members** (Team members, select_multiple, optional): Are you a member of a team?
	+ Yes
	+ No
