# social-media-performance-report-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect performance metrics related to social media engagement and advertising campaigns. It is designed to gather data on social media engagement, campaign performance, content distribution channels, and target audience characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review each field carefully to understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill in the fields with the relevant data for your social media campaigns.
3. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus as required.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled in.
5. Submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Social Media Engagement** (`number`, `optional`): Enter the level of engagement you have observed on social media platforms.
* **2. Social Media Performance Metrics** (`number`, `optional`): Enter the performance metrics of your social media campaigns, such as likes, shares, comments, etc.
* **3. Social Media Campaigns** (`select_multiple`, `optional`): Select the social media campaigns you are reporting on, either "Yes" or "No".
* **4. Target Audience** (`text`, `optional`): Enter a brief description of your target audience, including demographics and characteristics.
* **5. Content Type** (`select_one`, `optional`): Select the type of content you are using for your social media campaigns, such as images, videos, etc.
* **6. Engagement Type** (`select_one`, `optional`): Select the type of engagement you are measuring, such as likes, comments, shares, etc.
* **7. Engagement Score** (`number`, `optional`): Enter a score reflecting the level of engagement you have observed.
* **8. Content Performance Score** (`number`, `optional`): Enter a score reflecting the performance of your content.
* **9. Engagement Duration** (`time`, `optional`): Enter the duration of engagement you have observed.
* **10. Content Distribution Channel** (`select_one`, `optional`): Select the channel through which you are distributing your content, such as Facebook, Twitter, etc.
* **11. Social Media Platforms** (`select_multiple`, `optional`): Select the social media platforms you are using for your campaigns, such as Instagram, YouTube, etc.
* **12. Engagement Metric** (`select_one`, `optional`): Select the engagement metric you are using, such as likes, views, etc.
* **13. Content Creation Date** (`date`, `optional`): Enter the date your content was created.
* **14. Social Media Ad Campaigns** (`select_multiple`, `optional`): Select the ad campaigns you are running, either "Yes" or "No".
* **15. Ad Campaign Budget** (`number`, `optional`): Enter the budget for your ad campaign.
* **16. Ad Campaign Distribution Channel** (`select_one`, `optional`): Select the channel through which you are distributing your ad campaign.
* **17. Ad Campaign Duration** (`time`, `optional`): Enter the duration of your ad campaign.
* **18. Engagement Lead Time** (`time`, `optional`): Enter the lead time for engagement you have observed.
* **19. Engagement Frequency** (`select_one`, `optional`): Select the frequency of engagement you are measuring, such as daily, weekly, etc.
* **20. Content Performance Metric** (`select_one`, `optional`): Select the metric you are using to measure content performance, such as engagement, clicks, etc.
* **21. Content Performance Ratio** (`number`, `optional`): Enter a ratio reflecting the performance of your content.
* **22. Content Performance Score** (`number`, `optional`): Enter a score reflecting the performance of your content.
* **23. Ad Campaign Lead Time** (`time`, `optional`): Enter the lead time for your ad campaign.
* **24. Ad Campaign Frequency** (`select_one`, `optional`): Select the frequency of your ad campaign, such as daily, weekly, etc.
* **25. Target Audience Description** (`text`, `optional`): Enter a brief description of your target audience, including demographics and characteristics.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in.
- Review each field carefully to ensure you are selecting the correct options.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your administrator for clarification.
