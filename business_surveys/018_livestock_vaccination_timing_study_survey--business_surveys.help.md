# Livestock Vaccination Timing Study Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information from participants on their experiences with vaccinating livestock. Your input will help improve our understanding of vaccination timing and frequency, and inform better practices for animal health.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions carefully and honestly.
2. If you have any issues or questions, contact the research team.
3. You can fill out this form as many times as you need to, but please ensure each submission is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (note): This is a brief note to introduce yourself as a participant. Please provide a short description of your role and experience with livestock vaccination.
* **Participant Info** (number): Please enter a unique participant ID number to identify your responses. This will help us keep track of your data.
* **Participant Type** (select_one): Please select one of the following options: 
	+ farmer
	+ veterinarian
	+ other
* **Vaccination Schedule** (date): Enter the date when your livestock typically receives vaccinations.
* **Vaccination Frequency** (select_one): Please select one of the following:
	+ daily
	+ weekly
	+ monthly
* **Vaccination Type** (text): Describe the type of vaccinations your livestock typically receives.
* **Vaccination Location** (select_multiple): Select all locations where your livestock typically receives vaccinations:
	+ farm
	+ clinic
	+ other
* **Challenge 1** (text): Describe one challenge you face with vaccinating your livestock.
* **Challenge 2** (text): Describe another challenge you face with vaccinating your livestock.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the research team.
- If you're unsure about a field, try to provide the best answer you can, and don't worry if you're not sure.
