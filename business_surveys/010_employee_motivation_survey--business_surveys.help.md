# employee_motivation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about employee motivation and satisfaction. This includes feedback, engagement, and other relevant metrics.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Welcome to Employee Motivation Survey**: This page provides a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully and click "Next" to continue.
2. Select your answers from the following pages:
	* **Job Satisfaction**: Select "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied" to indicate your level of job satisfaction.
	* **Team Communication**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you receive regular feedback from your team.
	* **Feedback**: Please provide any feedback you may have.
	* **Motivation**: Enter a number value to indicate your current motivation level (e.g., 1-10).
	* **Engagement**: Enter a number value to indicate your current engagement level (e.g., 1-10).
	* **Work-Life Balance**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you feel your work-life balance is satisfactory.
	* **Overall Motivation**: Select "Yes" or "No" for your overall motivation level.
	* **Employee Suggestions**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have suggestions for your work.
	* **Manager Comments**: Enter a date for the last time your manager commented on your performance.
	* **Employee Comments**: Enter a time for the last time you commented on your performance.
	* **Last Updated**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if your data is up-to-date.
	* **Frequency**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you receive regular feedback from your manager.
	* **Motivation Factor**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if motivation level is a factor in your job.
	* **Motivation Level**: Select "High" or "Low" for your current motivation level.
	* **Motivation Score**: Enter a number value to indicate your current motivation score.
	* **Employee ID**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are the employee with this ID.
	* **Team Name**: Enter your team name.
	* **Date**: Enter the date of your last performance review.
	* **Time**: Enter the time of your last performance review.
	* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your performance.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Manager**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if your manager is available for feedback.
	* **Status**: Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate your current status.
	* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments.
