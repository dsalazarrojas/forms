# Treasury Resource Allocation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Resource Allocation Survey is designed to gather information from business users to help inform resource allocation decisions. This survey is intended to be completed by users in various departments within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out each page individually, and be sure to answer all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
2. For text fields, type your response in the designated area.
3. For select fields (e.g., What is your department), choose the most accurate answer from the provided options.
4. For multiple select fields (e.g., What is your role), select all applicable options.
5. For numerical fields (e.g., What is your team size), enter a numeric value.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your title** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): This field is asking for your job title or position within the organization. Please enter your official title or position.
* **What is your department** (`department`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is asking for the department you belong to. Choose the department that best represents your work group.
* **What is your role** (`role`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all roles that apply to your position within the organization.
* **What is your team size** (`team_size`, `number`, required/true): Enter the number of people in your team.
* **What is your budget** (`budget`, `number`, required/true): Enter your budget for the current period.
* **What is your priority** (`priority`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is asking for your priority for resources. Select 'Yes' if resources are high priority for you, otherwise select 'No'.
* **What is your funding need** (`funding_need`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all funding areas where you have a need.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for any additional comments or information you'd like to provide about your resource needs.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when providing information, as this will help the resource allocation process.
* Ensure you answer all required fields to provide a complete picture of your resource needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the survey, please reach out to your administrator or resource allocation team.
