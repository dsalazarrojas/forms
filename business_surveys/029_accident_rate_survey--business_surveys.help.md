# Accident Rate Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on accidents that occurred within your organization during a specific time period. The information collected will help us understand the types of accidents, their severity, locations, and contributing factors. This data will be used to identify trends and areas for improvement in your organization's safety protocols.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to provide a clear picture of your organization's safety performance.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. If you're unsure of a question or answer, please ask for clarification or consult your organization's safety protocols.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Survey Period** (`survey_period`, text, required): Specify the time period covered by this survey.
* **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, select_one, required): Select the industry sector your organization operates in. Examples: Manufacturing, Construction, Healthcare, etc.
* **Total Employees** (`total_employees`, number, required): Enter the total number of employees during the survey period.
* **Total Work Hours** (`total_work_hours`, number, required): Enter the total number of work hours during the survey period.
* **Total Accidents** (`total_accidents`, number, required): Enter the total number of accidents during the survey period.
* **Accident Categories** (`accident_categories`, select_multiple, required): Select the categories of accidents that occurred. Examples: Slip/Trip/Fall, Struck by Object, etc.
* **Accident Locations** (`accident_locations`, select_multiple, required): Select the locations where accidents most commonly occurred. Examples: Production Floor, Warehouse, Office, etc.
* **Accident Severity Distribution** (`accident_severity_distribution`, text, required): Describe the distribution of accident severity levels.
* **Time Periods High Incidence** (`time_periods_high_incidence`, select_multiple, required): Select the time periods during which accidents occurred most frequently. Examples: Early Morning (6-10 AM), Afternoon (12-5 PM), etc.
* **Departments Affected** (`departments_affected`, select_multiple, required): Select the departments with the highest accident rates. Examples: Production, Maintenance, etc.
* **Contributing Factors** (`contributing_factors`, select_multiple, required): Select the factors that contributed to the accidents. Examples: Equipment failure, Inadequate training, etc.
* **Accident Precursors** (`accident_precursors`, select_multiple, required): Select the warning signs that preceded the accidents. Examples: Equipment malfunctions, Safety violations, etc.
* **Safety Program Effectiveness** (`safety_program_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Select the effectiveness of your current safety program. Examples: Very Effective, Effective, etc.
* **Safety Training Frequency** (`safety_training_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often safety training is provided. Examples: Monthly, Quarterly, etc.
* **Safety Equipment Adequacy** (`safety_equipment_adequacy`, select_one, required): Select the adequacy of your safety equipment. Examples: More than adequate, Adequate, etc.
* **Accident Reporting Compliance** (`accident_reporting_compliance`, select_one, required): Select the level of compliance with accident reporting requirements. Examples: Excellent, Good, etc.
* **Safety Improvement Priorities** (`safety_improvement_priorities`, select_multiple, required): Select the top priorities for improving safety. Examples: Equipment upgrades, Training programs, etc.
* **Budget Allocated to Safety** (`budget_allocated_safety`, number, required): Enter the percentage of budget allocated to safety measures.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or observations about accident rates or safety measures.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* If unsure of a question or answer, please ask for clarification or consult your organization's safety protocols.
* Please provide any additional comments or observations about accident rates or safety measures in the "Additional Comments" field.
* This form is a tool to help us better understand your organization's safety performance.
