# Staff Examination Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation of employee performance. It is intended to be used for performance evaluations, promotion considerations, or other purposes related to employee development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the employee's details in the "Employee Details" section.
2. Fill out the "Strengths" and "Areas for Improvement" sections with specific feedback.
3. Select the correct evaluation scale for the employee's performance.
4. Enter clear and actionable recommendations for the employee's growth.
5. Enter the date and time of the last review.
6. Enter the reviewer's name and position.
7. Provide detailed comments for the employee's review.
8. Enter the date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details (Text)**: Enter the employee's name, position, and any other relevant details.
* **Name (Text)**: Enter the employee's name correctly.
* **Position (Select One)**: Select the employee's position from the options provided (Manager, Supervisor, Team Member).
* **Strengths (Text)**: Enter specific strengths of the employee.
* **Areas for Improvement (Text)**: Enter areas where the employee can improve.
* **Promotion Recommendations (Text)**: Enter clear and actionable recommendations for the employee's growth.
* **Employee Evaluation (Number)**: Rate the employee's performance using the evaluation scale.
* **Evaluation Scale (Select One)**: Select the correct evaluation scale for the employee's performance (Excellent, Good, Fair).
* **Next Steps (Text)**: Enter clear and actionable next steps for the employee's growth.
* **Date Last Review (Date)**: Enter the date of the last review.
* **Time Last Review (Time)**: Enter the time of the last review.
* **Reviewer Name (Text)**: Enter the reviewer's name correctly.
* **Reviewer Position (Text)**: Enter the reviewer's position correctly.
* **Reviewer Comments (Text)**: Enter detailed comments for the employee's review.
* **Date Submitted (Date)**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Time Submitted (Time)**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Submitted By (Text)**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Staff Review Comments (Text)**: Enter any additional comments from the staff reviewer.
* **Review Time (Time)**: Enter the time of the review.
* **Reviewer Email (Email)**: Enter the reviewer's email address.
* **Reviewer Phone (Text)**: Enter the reviewer's phone number.
* **Date Submitter (Date)**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Time Submitter (Time)**: Enter the time of submission. 

Please note that the following fields have no options:

* **Employee Details**
* **Name**
* **Date Last Review**
* **Time Last Review**
* **Date Submitted**
* **Time Submitted**
* **Date Submitter**
