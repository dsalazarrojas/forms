# Staff Examination Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation of employee performance. It is intended to be used for performance evaluations, promotion considerations, or other purposes related to employee development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the employee's details in the "Employee Details" section.
2. Fill out the "Strengths" and "Areas for Improvement" sections with specific feedback.
3. Select the correct evaluation scale for the employee's performance.
4. Enter clear and actionable recommendations for the employee's growth.
5. Enter the date and time of the last review.
6. Enter the reviewer's name and position.
7. Provide detailed comments for the employee's review.
8. Enter the date and time of submission.
