# DIY Beauty Practice Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your at-home beauty practices, including what motivates you to maintain a consistent routine, your current routine, challenges you face in your routine, and your level of satisfaction with the current practice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the "what motivates your at-home beauty routine" question with a brief description.
2.  Describe your current at-home beauty routine in the "describe your at-home beauty routine" field.
3.  Identify any challenges you face in maintaining your routine, if any.
4.  Select one option to answer "how satisfied are you with your at-home beauty routine" and "what challenges do you face in your at-home beauty routine".
5.  Answer the "how much time do you spend on your at-home beauty routine" question with a brief description.
6.  Select the amount of time you spend on your routine from the options provided.
7.  You can add any additional comments or notes in the "do you have any other comments" field.
8.  Provide your email address.
9.  Enter your phone number.
10.  Enter the current date.
11.  Enter the current time.
12.  Add any additional comments or notes in the "other comments" field.
13.  Enter your email address again.
14.  Enter your phone number again.
15.  Enter the current date again.
16.  Enter the current time again.
17.  Add any additional comments or notes in the "other comments 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **what motivates your at-home beauty routine** (intro_q1): A description of what motivates you to maintain a consistent beauty routine at home.
*   **motivation** (motivation): Select one option to indicate what motivates you to maintain a consistent beauty routine.
    +   *satisfaction*: You feel that maintaining a consistent beauty routine brings you satisfaction.
    +   *convenience*: You prefer the convenience of doing it at home.
    +   *time saving*: Maintaining a consistent beauty routine saves you time.
    +   *variety*: You enjoy trying new things and trying different products.
    +   *creativity*: You like to express yourself through your beauty routine.
*   **describe your at-home beauty routine** (routine): A description of your current at-home beauty routine.
*   **what challenge do you face in your at-home beauty routine** (challenge_page): A description of any challenges you face in maintaining your beauty routine.
*   **challenges** (challenges): Select one or more options to indicate the challenges you face in your beauty routine.
    +   *dry skin*: You experience dry skin while following your routine.
    +   *skin sensitivity*: You experience skin sensitivity while following your routine.
    +   *uneven terrain*: Your skin has an uneven terrain.
    +   *other*: Other challenges.
*   **how satisfied are you with your at-home beauty routine** (satisfaction_page): Select one option to indicate how satisfied you are with your at-home beauty routine.
    +   *very satisfied*: You are very satisfied with your routine.
    +   *satisfied*: You are satisfied with your routine.
    +   *neutral*: Your routine is just okay.
    +   *dissatisfied*: You are dissatisfied with your routine.
    +   *very dissatisfied*: You are very dissatisfied with your routine.
*   **how much time do you spend on your at-home beauty routine** (time_page): A description of how much time you spend on your at-home beauty routine.
*   **how satisfied are you with your at-home beauty routine** (satisfaction): Select one option to indicate how satisfied you are with your beauty routine.
*   **how much time do you spend on your at-home beauty routine** (time_spend): Select one option to indicate how much time you spend on your routine.
*   **do you have any other comments** (note_page): Additional comments or notes about your at-home beauty routine.
*   **do you have any other comments 2** (note2): Additional comments or notes about your at-home beauty routine.
*   **do you have any other comments 3** (note3): Additional comments or notes about your at-home beauty routine.
*   **your email address** (email): Your email address for contact.
*   **your phone number** (phone): Your phone number.
*   **current date** (date): The current date.
*   **current time** (time): The current time.
*   **your email address 2** (email2): Your email address.
*   **your phone number 2** (phone2): Your phone number.
*   **current date 2** (date2): The current date.
*   **current time 2** (time2): The current time.
*   **your email address 3** (email3): Your email address.
*   **your phone number 3** (phone3): Your phone number.
*   **current date 3** (date3): The current date.
*   **current time 3** (time3): The current time.
