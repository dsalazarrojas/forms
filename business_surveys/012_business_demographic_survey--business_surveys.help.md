# Business Demographic Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects demographic information about your business to provide insights and support for business development initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question to the best of your knowledge and understanding.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. If you are unsure about any information, please leave it blank or provide an explanation in the notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: Provide the name of your business.
* **Years in Operation**: Specify how long your business has existed.
* **Business Structure**: Select your business structure, e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, Corporation, Non-Profit, Cooperative.
* **Primary Industry**: Choose your main industry, e.g., Agriculture, Technology, Healthcare, Manufacturing, Retail, Services, Finance, Education, Other.
* **Total Number of Employees**: Enter the current number of full and part-time staff.
* **Full-Time Employees**: Specify the number of full-time staff.
* **Part-Time Employees**: Specify the number of part-time staff.
* **Management Structure**: Select the management levels in your business, e.g., Executive/Owner, Management, Supervisory, Entry Level.
* **Employee Growth**: Choose how your team size has changed in the past year, e.g., Decreased, Stable, Slight Growth, Moderate Growth, Significant Growth.
* **Annual Revenue**: Select your total annual revenue, e.g., Less than $100K, $100K - $500K, $500K - $1M, $1M - $5M, $5M - $10M, $10M - $50M, More than $50M.
* **Revenue Growth**: Choose how your revenue is trending, e.g., Declining, Flat, Modest Growth, Strong Growth, Rapid Growth.
* **Estimated Profit Margin**: Specify the percentage of revenue that is profit.
* **Primary Market**: Select where you primarily operate, e.g., Local, Regional, National, International.
* **Target Customer Demographics**: Describe your primary customers, e.g., age range, location.
* **Number of Active Customers**: Enter an approximate customer count.
* **Customer Concentration**: Choose how your customers are distributed, e.g., Highly Concentrated, Moderately Concentrated, Fairly Distributed, Well Distributed.
* **Number of Owners**: Specify the number of owners in your business.
* **Owner Backgrounds**: Describe the owner's background and experience in the industry.
