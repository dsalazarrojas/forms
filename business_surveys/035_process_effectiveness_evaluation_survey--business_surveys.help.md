# process_effectiveness_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "process_effectiveness_evaluation_survey" form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of business processes within an organization. It aims to assess respondents' awareness of the current workflow, its impact, and potential improvements to enhance overall efficiency and productivity.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reading the introduction, which provides context for the survey questions.
2. Select the name of your team lead from the dropdown menu in **What is the name of your team lead?**.
3. Choose your level of awareness of the current workflow in **How aware are you about the current workflow?**.
4. Answer the question **How has the current workflow impacted your work?** in a text field.
5. Provide suggestions for process improvements in **What improvements would you suggest for the current workflow?**.
6. Rate your level of team collaboration in **How does team collaboration impact your work?**.
7. If you have any feedback, type it in **Is there any feedback you would like to share?** (optional).
8. If you've completed this survey before, provide the date and time of your last survey in **When was your last survey?** and **Time of last survey** (optional).
9. Finally, enter your email address in **Email of last survey**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief introduction to the survey purpose and context.
* **What is the name of your team lead?** (`select_one`): Choose the name of your team lead from the dropdown menu.
* **How aware are you about the current workflow?** (`select_multiple`): Rate your level of awareness about the current workflow.
	+ Not at all
	+ Somewhat
	+ Highly
	+ Very highly
* **How has the current workflow impacted your work?** (`text`): Describe the impact of the current workflow on your work.
* **What improvements would you suggest for the current workflow?** (`text`): Provide suggestions for process improvements.
* **How does team collaboration impact your work?** (`select_one`): Rate the level of team collaboration in your work.
	+ Rarely
	+ Somewhat
	+ Highly
	+ Very highly
* **Is there any feedback you would like to share?** (`note`): Type any feedback or comments about the survey or process.
* **When was your last survey?** (`date`): Enter the date of your last survey.
* **Time of last survey** (`time`): Provide the time of your last survey (optional).
* **Email of last survey** (`email`): Enter your email address associated with the survey response.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions as honestly and accurately as possible.
* If you have completed this survey before, please provide the required metadata to help track your responses.
* If you have any feedback or suggestions, feel free to share them in the **Is there any feedback you would like to share?** field.
