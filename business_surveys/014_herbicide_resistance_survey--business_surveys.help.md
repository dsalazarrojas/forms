# Herbicide Resistance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Herbicide Resistance Survey form is designed to collect data on herbicide use and resistance in agricultural settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the location where herbicides are being used.
2. Choose the crop species being managed.
3. Indicate whether herbicides are used.
4. Choose the management practices employed.
5. Select the weed species present.
6. Enter the location where the survey was submitted from.
7. Enter any additional comments or information.
8. Optionally, provide contact information for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **field_location**:
	+ **Field Location**: A text input field to record the location where herbicides are being used. This is a required field for the purpose of tracking herbicide use in specific locations.
* **crops**:
	+ **Crops**: A multiple-choice select field with options for different crop species (Cotton, Corn, Soybean, Alfalfa, Other). This field is optional and allows users to report multiple crop species.
* **herbicide_use**:
	+ **Herbicide Use**: A single-choice select field with options for whether herbicides are used (Yes, No). This field is optional and helps track herbicide use patterns.
* **management_practices**:
	+ **Management Practices**: A text input field for users to describe their management practices. This field is optional and allows users to provide additional context about their herbicide use.
* **weed_species**:
	+ **Weed Species**: A multiple-choice select field with options for different weed species (Weed 1, Weed 2, Weed 3, Other). This field is optional and allows users to report multiple weed species.
* **location**:
	+ **Location**: A date field to record the date and time when the survey was submitted. This field is optional and is used to track the submission time and date.
* **submitted_by**:
	+ **Submitted By**: A text input field to record the name or identifier of the person submitting the survey. This field is optional.
* **contact_information**:
	+ **Contact Information**: A text input field for users to provide additional contact information for follow-up. This field is optional.
* **additional_comments**:
	+ **Additional Comments**: A text input field for users to provide any additional comments or information about their herbicide use. This field is optional.
Note: All fields are optional except for **field_location** which is required for form submission.
