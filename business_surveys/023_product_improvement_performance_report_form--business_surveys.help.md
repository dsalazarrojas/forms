# Product Improvement Performance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Improvement Performance Report Form is used to collect feedback from customers on the performance of a product or service offered by the business. This form will help the business to identify areas that need improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance its products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the product or service you are evaluating in the `form_title` field.
2. Select the category of the product or service from the options provided in the `category` field.
3. Provide a brief description of your experience with the product or service in the `description` field.
4. Answer the feedback questions provided in the `feedback_question_1`, `feedback_question_2`, `feedback_question_3`, `feedback_question_4`, and `feedback_question_5` fields.
5. Rate the performance of the product or service on a scale of 1-10 in the `rating_scale_1`, `rating_scale_2`, `rating_scale_3`, and `rating_scale_4` fields.

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the title of the product or service you are evaluating.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the product or service you are providing feedback on.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the category that best describes the product or service:
	+ Business
	+ Service
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your experience with the product or service.
* **Feedback Question 1** (`feedback_question_1`, `text`, required: false): Answer this question to provide more information about your experience with the product or service.
* **Feedback Question 2** (`feedback_question_2`, `text`, required: false): Answer this question to provide more information about your experience with the product or service.
* **Feedback Question 3** (`feedback_question_3`, `text`, required: false): Answer this question to provide more information about your experience with the product or service.
* **Feedback Question 4** (`feedback_question_4`, `text`, required: false): Answer this question to provide more information about your experience with the product or service.
* **Feedback Question 5** (`feedback_question_5`, `text`, required: false): Answer this question to provide more information about your experience with the product or service.
* **Rating Scale 1** (`rating_scale_1`, `number`, required: false): Rate the performance of the product or service on a scale of 1-10.
* **Rating Scale 2** (`rating_scale_2`, `number`, required: false): Rate the performance of the product or service on a scale of 1-10.
* **Rating Scale 3** (`rating_scale_3`, `number`, required: false): Rate the performance of the product or service on a scale of 1-10.
* **Rating Scale 4** (`rating_scale_4`, `number`, required: false): Rate the performance of the product or service on a scale of 1-10.
