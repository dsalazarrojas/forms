# Social Care Accessibility Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Social Care Accessibility Survey. The survey is designed to collect information about your experiences with social care services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure you have read and understood the purpose of the survey.
2. Answer each question accurately and thoroughly.
3. For each field, follow the instructions provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **service_awareness** (Page 2, select_one): This question asks about your awareness of social care services. Choose 'Yes' if you are aware of the service, or 'No' if you are not. 
* **service_accessibility** (Page 3, select_multiple): This question asks about the accessibility of social care services. Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **satisfaction** (Page 4, number): This question asks for your level of satisfaction with the service. Please enter a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **contact_info** (Page 5, text): This is a text field where you can provide your contact information if you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **service_barriers** (Page 6, text): This question asks about any barriers you experienced while accessing the service. Please provide a detailed description.
* **service_support** (Page 7, date): This question asks about any support provided by the service. Please enter the date you received the support.
* **follow_up** (Page 8, note): This is a thank you note for your participation in the survey.
* **survey_completion** (Page 9, time): This question asks how long it took you to complete the survey.
* **additional_comments** (Page 10, text): This question allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback about the survey.
