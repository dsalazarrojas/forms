# Collateral ROI Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you evaluate the effectiveness of marketing collateral in generating revenue and promoting your products or services. By filling out this form, you'll provide valuable insights into the performance of your marketing materials, which can help inform future investments and improve sales strategies.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Ensure you have a clear understanding of the goals and objectives you're trying to achieve with your marketing collateral.
*   Read through each section carefully, and answer all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   *Your Name* (Page 1): **Required, text** - Enter your name as you want it to appear on the assessment report.
*   *Email Address* (Page 1): **Required, email** - Enter your contact email address so that the report can be sent to you.
*   *Department* (Page 1): **Required, text** - Enter your department or team name.
*   *Your Role* (Page 1): **Required, text** - Enter your job title or role.
*   *Assessment Period* (Page 2): **Required, select_one** - Select the time frame being assessed (e.g., Last Quarter, Last 6 Months, Last Year, Year to Date, or Custom Period).
*   *Type of Marketing Collateral* (Page 2): **Required, select_one** - Choose the type of marketing collateral being assessed (e.g., Brochures, Flyers, Business Cards, etc.).
*   *Collateral Name or Title* (Page 2): **Required, text** - Enter the specific name or title of the collateral being assessed.
*   *Distribution Channels Used* (Page 3): **Required, select_multiple** - Select all the distribution channels used for the collateral (e.g., Email Campaigns, Direct Mail, Trade Shows, etc.).
*   *Quantity Distributed* (Page 3): **Required, number** - Enter the total quantity distributed.
*   *Total Production Cost* (Page 3): **Required, text** - Enter the total production cost.
*   *Total Distribution Cost* (Page 4): **Optional, text** - Enter the total distribution cost.
*   *Total Investment* (Page 4): **Required, text** - Enter the total investment for the collateral.
*   *Leads Generated* (Page 4): **Optional, number** - Enter the number of leads generated.
*   *Sales Opportunities Created* (Page 4): **Optional, number** - Enter the number of sales opportunities created.
*   *Deals Closed* (Page 4): **Optional, number** - Enter the number of deals closed.
*   *Revenue Attributed* (Page 5): **Optional, text** - Enter the revenue attributed to the collateral.
*   *Calculated ROI Percentage* (Page 5): **Optional, text** - Enter the calculated ROI percentage.
*   *Audience Engagement Level* (Page 5): **Required, select_one** - Select the audience engagement level (e.g., Very High, High, Moderate, Low, Very Low).
*   *Brand Awareness Impact* (Page 5): **Required, select_one** - Select the brand awareness impact (e.g., Significant Increase, Moderate Increase, Slight Increase, No Change, Decrease).
*   *Message Clarity* (Page 6): **Required, select_one** - Select the message clarity rating (e.g., Very Clear, Clear, Somewhat Clear, Unclear, Very Unclear).
*   *Design Quality* (Page 6): **Required, select_one** - Select the design quality rating (e.g., Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   *Content Relevance* (Page 6): **Required, select_one** - Select the content relevance (e.g., Highly Relevant, Relevant, Somewhat Relevant, Not Very Relevant, Irrelevant).
*   *Call to Action Effectiveness* (Page 6): **Required, select_one** - Select the call to action effectiveness (e.g., Very Effective, Effective, Somewhat Effective, Not Effective, No Call to Action).
*   *Sales Team Feedback* (Page 7): **Required, select_one** - Select the sales team feedback (e.g., Very Useful, Useful, Somewhat Useful, Not Useful, Not Used by Sales).
*   *Customer Feedback* (Page 7): **Optional, text** - Enter any customer feedback received.
*   *Competitive Advantage* (Page 7): **Required, select_one** - Select the competitive advantage provided (e.g., Significant Advantage, Moderate Advantage, Slight Advantage, No Advantage, Disadvantage).
*   *Reuse Potential* (Page 8): **Required, select_one** - Select the reuse potential (e.g., High Reuse Value, Moderate Reuse Value, Low Reuse Value, Single Use Only).
*   *Update Frequency* (Page 8): **Required, select_one** - Select the update frequency needed (e.g., Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually, As Needed, Never Update).
*   *Continue Production* (Page 8): **Required, select_one** - Select whether to continue producing the collateral (e.g., Yes Definitely, Yes With Improvements, Unsure, No Discontinue).
*   *Improvements Needed* (Page 9): **Optional, text** - Enter any improvements needed for the collateral.
*   *Budget Recommendation* (Page 9): **Required, select_one** - Select the budget recommendation for future production (e.g., Increase Budget, Maintain Budget, Reduce Budget, Eliminate Budget).
*   *Additional Comments* (Page 9): **Optional, text** - Enter any additional comments or feedback.

## Tips

*   Please answer all required fields accurately and completely.
*   For optional fields, provide as much detail as possible.
*   If unsure about any field, refer to your organization's marketing strategy or consult with a team member.
*   This form is designed to help you evaluate the effectiveness of marketing collateral. Please be honest and provide valuable insights.
