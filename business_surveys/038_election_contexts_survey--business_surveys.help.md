# election_contexts_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the election context, including participant information, participation habits, sources of information, and fairness perceptions. This data will help researchers and analysts understand the election context and inform their studies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the election context questions in the section below.
2. For the participant information section, enter your participant ID, email, and phone number (if applicable).
3. Provide any additional participant comments or notes you would like to share.
4. Enter any researcher comments or notes you would like to add.
5. Finally, enter any research group information (if applicable) and provide any research notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Election Context
* **Election Context** (`Election Context`, `text`, required/optional): Please describe the election context you are responding to.
### Participant Info
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select how often you participate in elections.
	+ Option 1: Always
	+ Option 2: Sometimes
	+ Option 3: Rarely
	+ Option 4: Never
* **Info Sources** (`info_sources`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select where you usually get election-related information from.
	+ Option 1: Social Media
	+ Option 2: Friends
	+ Option 3: Family
	+ Option 4: News
	+ Option 5: Online
	+ Option 6: Offline
* **Fairness Perceptions** (`fairness_perceptions`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select how fair you perceive the election process to be.
	+ Option 1: Fair
	+ Option 2: Not Fair
	+ Option 3: Neutral
### Participant Info
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, `number`, required/optional): Please enter your participant ID.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required/optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide any additional participant comments or notes.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide any additional comments you would like to make about your participation in the election.
### Researcher Info
* **Researcher ID** (`researcher_id`, `number`, required/optional): Please enter your researcher ID.
* **Researcher Email** (`researcher_email`, `email`, required/optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Researcher Phone** (`researcher_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Research Notes** (`research_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide any additional researcher comments or notes.
* **Researcher Comments** (`researcher_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide any additional comments you would like to make about your role as a researcher.
* **Research Group** (`research_group`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter the name of your research group (if applicable).
* **Research Group ID** (`research_group_id`, `number`, required/optional): Please enter your research group ID (if applicable).
* **Research Group Comments** (`research_group_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide any additional comments about your research group.
* **Research Group Email** (`research_group_email`, `email`, required/optional): Please enter your research group email (if applicable).
* **Research Group Phone** (`research_group_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your research group phone number (if applicable).
### Other
* **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide any additional comments or notes.
