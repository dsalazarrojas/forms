# Global Giving Behavior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about an individual's or organization's giving behavior. It has multiple pages with various input types to gather specific details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill out each page according to the instructions and guidelines provided.
3. Make sure to select all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Contact Us
* **Contact Us**: This page allows you to provide contact information, such as email and phone number. If you wish to be contacted, please fill out this page.

### Name
* **Name**: Enter your full name as per your preference. This field is not required.

### Email
* **Email**: Provide your email address. This is not required.

### Phone
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This field is not required.

### Address
* **Address**: Enter your address if you'd like to be contacted at this location. This field is not required.

### Date
* **Date**: Select the date you are willing to participate. This field is not required.

### Time
* **Time**: Select the time you are available to participate. This field is not required.

### Select
* **Select**: Choose one option from the available choices. This field is required.

### Select Multiple
* **Select Multiple**: Choose one or multiple options from the available choices. This field is not required.

### Date and Time
* **Date and Time**: Enter both date and time you are available. This field is not required.

### Integer
* **Integer**: Enter a numerical value. This field is not required.

### Decimal
* **Decimal**: Enter a decimal numerical value. This field is not required.

### Note
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes you'd like to share. This field is not required.

Please review your submission before submitting the form to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
