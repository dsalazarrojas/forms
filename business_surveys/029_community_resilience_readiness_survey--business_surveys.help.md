# Community Resilience Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to assess the community's ability to bounce back from challenges and identify areas for improvement. It aims to understand individual and household preparedness, awareness of local resources, and perception of community readiness. Your input will help us better understand our community's strengths and weaknesses, informing our planning and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page and understand the questions and options provided.
2. Answer the questions based on your personal experiences and perceptions.
3. Provide honest and accurate information to help us better understand our community's needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resilience Readiness Survey** (`resilience_intro`, note):
	+ This page provides an introduction to the survey, explaining its purpose and importance.
* **Name of Respondent** (`respondent_name`, text):
	+ If you are filling out the survey on behalf of yourself, enter your name. This information will be used to track your responses and provide updates.
* **Your Neighborhood or Community** (`residency_neighborhood`, text):
	+ Enter your neighborhood or community name. This information will help us understand the context of your responses.
* **Rate your personal level of emergency preparedness** (`emergency_preparedness_level`, select_one):
	+ Answer this question based on your level of preparedness for emergencies, with 1 being Not Prepared and 5 being Fully Prepared.
* **Does your household have a 3-day emergency kit?** (`household_emergency_kit_check`, select_one):
	+ Answer this question based on whether your household has a 3-day emergency kit or not.
* **Does your family have an emergency communication plan?** (`emergency_communication_plan_check`, select_one):
	+ Answer this question based on whether your family has a plan in place to communicate during emergencies.
* **What is your primary concern regarding community resilience?** (`primary_resilience_concern`, select_multiple):
	+ Select up to two concerns from the list provided, as this will help us understand your perspective on the community's resilience.
* **Other concern - please specify** (`resilience_concern_other`, text):
	+ If none of the provided options are relevant, enter your concern here.
* **Rate the strength of your neighborhood support network** (`neighborhood_support_network_rating`, select_one):
	+ Answer this question based on the strength of your neighborhood support network, with 1 being Weak and 5 being Very Strong.
* **Are you aware of local resilience resources?** (`resilience_resources_awareness`, select_one):
	+ Answer this question based on whether you are aware of local resources such as community centers, food banks, or emergency shelters.
* **How ready is our community for a major crisis?** (`community_readiness_perception`, select_one):
	+ Answer this question based on your perception of the community's readiness for a major crisis, with 1 being Not Ready and 5 being Very Ready.
* **Would you be interested in community resilience training?** (`resilience_training_interest`, select_one):
	+ Answer this question based on whether you would be interested in training programs that promote community resilience.
* **What is one thing that would make you feel more resilient?** (`resilience_improvement_suggestions`, text):
	+ Enter one thing that you think would improve the community's resilience.
* **Contact Email Address** (`follow_up_email`, email):
	+ Enter your email address to receive updates and survey results.
* **Today's Date** (`readiness_date`, date):
	+ Enter the current date to help us track your responses.
* **Thank You for Your Input** (`survey_footer`, note):
	+ This is a message of appreciation for your contribution to the community planning process.
