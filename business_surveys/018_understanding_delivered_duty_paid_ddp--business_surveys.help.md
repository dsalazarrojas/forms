# understanding_delivered_duty_paid_ddp - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "understanding_delivered_duty_paid_ddp" form is designed to collect feedback and gather insights from users about their experience with Delivered Duty Paid (DDP). This form helps us understand the challenges, benefits, and areas of improvement related to DDP, ensuring we can provide better support and services to our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question carefully, providing detailed responses to share your genuine thoughts and experiences.
2.  Use the provided options for the select fields, if applicable.
3.  Fill in the text fields with your comments and suggestions.
4.  Review and submit your responses to ensure you're providing accurate and complete information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **DDP Familiarity** (DDP Familiarity, select_one, required/optional): Please select the level of your familiarity with DDP.
	+ Very High: You have extensive knowledge about DDP and its concepts.
	+ High: You have good understanding of DDP and its applications.
	+ Medium: You have basic knowledge about DDP, but may need further clarification.
	+ Low: You have limited or no knowledge about DDP.
* **DDP Usage** (DDP Usage, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate your level of usage of DDP.
	+ Always: You use DDP frequently.
	+ Sometimes: You use DDP occasionally.
	+ Never: You do not use DDP.
* **DDP Challenges** (DDP Challenges, text, required/optional): Describe your challenges or difficulties you face when using DDP.
* **DDP Benefits** (DDP Benefits, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the benefits you have gained from using DDP.
	+ Faster Clearance: You experience faster clearance times with DDP.
	+ Reduced Fees: You pay reduced fees with DDP.
	+ Improved Tracking: You track your progress better with DDP.
	+ None: You do not experience any benefits with DDP.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Provide your email address for communication and follow-up purposes.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Date of Interest** (Date of Interest, date, required/optional): Choose a date that is of interest to you.
* **Time of Interest** (Time of Interest, time, required/optional): Select a time of interest to you.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about DDP.
* **Would you like to learn more about DDP?** (Would you like to learn more about DDP?, select_one, required/optional): Indicate your interest in learning more about DDP.
	+ True: You are interested in learning more about DDP.
	+ False: You do not want to learn more about DDP.
* **Would you like to receive an DDP Roadmap?** (Would you like to receive an DDP Roadmap?, select_multiple, required/optional): Select if you want to receive an DDP roadmap.
	+ True: You want to receive an DDP roadmap.
	+ False: You do not want to receive an DDP roadmap.
* **Would you like to receive additional DDP resources?** (Would you like to receive additional DDP resources?, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate if you want to receive additional DDP resources.
	+ True: You want to receive additional DDP resources.
	+ False: You do not want to receive additional DDP resources.
* **Additional Feedback** (Additional Feedback, note, required/optional): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions about DDP.
* **What are your next steps with DDP?** (What are your next steps with DDP?, text, required/optional): Describe your next steps or actions you plan to take regarding DDP.
* **Follow-up** (Follow-up, select_one, required/optional): Indicate if you want to schedule a follow-up.
	+ True: You want to schedule a follow-up.
	+ False: You do not want to schedule a follow-up.
* **Do you have any suggestions for us?** (Do you have any suggestions for us?, text, required/optional): Provide any suggestions or feedback for improving DDP.
* **Do you agree with DDP?** (Do you agree with DDP?, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your level of agreement with DDP.
	+ Agree: You agree with DDP.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral about DDP.
	+ Disagree: You disagree with DDP.
