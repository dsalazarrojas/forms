# Property Management Transparency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Management Transparency Survey is designed to collect feedback and information on various aspects of property management, such as communication and issue resolution. This form aims to gather input from respondents to help property managers and stakeholders better understand their experiences and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's structure and ensure you understand the purpose of each section.
2. For each section, please provide a brief description or answer in the text field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Communication**: Please provide any feedback or concerns about the communication within your property management team or department.
* **Transparency**: Share any instances where you felt you had a clear understanding of what was happening or what was being communicated to you.
* **Issue Resolution**: Report any issues that you have encountered and how they were resolved (or not resolved).
* **Communication 4, 6, 7**: These fields are duplicates of the initial **Communication** field but with different numbers. Please consider them as additional points to elaborate on your feedback or concerns about communication within the property management team or department.
* **Issue Resolution 5, 7**: These fields are duplicates of the initial **Issue Resolution** field but with different numbers. Report any issues that you have encountered and how they were resolved (or not resolved) for these sections.
* **Communication 8**: This field is another instance of the **Communication** field. Please use it to provide feedback or concerns about communication with the property management team or department.
* **Communication 6**: This field is another duplicate of the **Communication** field, likely intended to be used in a different context or section. Use it as an opportunity to elaborate on your thoughts about communication.

## Tips
- Be as detailed and specific as possible when answering the fields.
- If you have any further concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- Thank you for taking the time to provide your valuable feedback and input!
