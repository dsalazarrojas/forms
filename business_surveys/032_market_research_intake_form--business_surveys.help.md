# Market Research Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather market research information from customers to help our team better understand their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure you have been requested to fill out this form by a representative of our company.
2. Carefully read each field description to ensure you provide accurate and relevant information.
3. Fill out each field with the required information, following the format and instructions provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Market Research Intake Form (Page 1)
* **Market Research Intake Form** (`form_section`, note, not required): This is the title of the form. You can disregard this field as it's not required.
* **customer-info** (`customer_info`, text, not required): Please provide a brief description of the research project or study you are interested in or have already participated in.
* **customer-name** (`customer_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., driver's license, passport).
* **customer-email** (`customer_email`, email, not required): Enter your work or personal email address.
* **customer-phone** (`customer_phone`, text, not required): Enter your work or personal phone number.
* **customer-address** (`customer_address`, text, not required): Enter your home or work address.
* **customer-city** (`customer_city`, text, not required): Enter the city where you reside or work.
* **customer-state** (`customer_state`, text, not required): Enter the state where you reside or work.
* **customer-zip** (`customer_zip`, text, not required): Enter your zip code.
* **customer-company** (`customer_company`, text, not required): Enter the name of your company (if applicable).
* **customer-job-title** (`customer_job_title`, text, not required): Enter your job title or profession.
