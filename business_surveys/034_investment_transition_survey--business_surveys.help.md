# investment_transition_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The investment transition survey is used to collect feedback on the investment transition experience of clients. This includes information on the purpose of the survey, the type of investment, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by reading through each question carefully to understand what information is being requested.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as possible to ensure accurate feedback.
3. If you are unsure about a question or its relevance, please provide your best estimate or answer based on your experience.
4. Be as specific as possible when providing additional comments or feedback.
