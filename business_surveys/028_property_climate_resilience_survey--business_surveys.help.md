# Property Climate Resilience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about a property's climate resilience, including its ability to withstand and adapt to climate-related risks and changes. The survey aims to gather data on a property's building type, age, and existing measures in place to mitigate climate risks. The goal is to assess the property's climate resilience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the appropriate option for each field.
2. Fill in any required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol.
3. Use the available space in the "Comments" field to provide any additional context or information about your property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Name**: This field is where you can input the name of the property.
* **Building Type**: Select the type of building you are surveying, such as High-rise, Low-rise, Mixed-use, or Other.
* **Building Age**: Input the age of the building.
* **Climate Risk**: Choose all the climate risks that are relevant to your property, such as Severe weather, Coastal flooding, Drought, or Other.
* **Resilience Measures**: This field is for any additional measures you have taken or plan to take to improve the property's climate resilience.
* **Existing Measures**: Select any existing measures that are already in place to mitigate climate risks.
* **Climate Impacts**: Choose all the climate impacts that are relevant to your property, such as Storm surges, Sea level rise, or Other.
* **Property Owner**: This field is where you can input the name of the property owner.
* **Property Manager**: This field is where you can input the name of the property manager.
* **Consultant**: This field is where you can input the name of the consultant involved in the project.
* **Comments**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or context about your property.
* **Email**: Input your email address.
* **Phone**: Input your phone number.
* **Date**: Input the date you are submitting this form.
* **Time**: Input the time you are submitting this form.
* **Submit**: Select "Submit" to complete and submit the survey.
* **Notes**: Use this field to add any additional comments or notes.
* **Followup**: Select "True" if you want to be contacted for follow-up, or "False" otherwise.
* **Followup Date**: Input the date for the follow-up.
* **Followup Time**: Input the time for the follow-up.
* **Followup Comments**: Add any comments for the follow-up.
* **Property Value**: Input the value of the property.
* **Climate Change**: Select "True" if you think climate change is a concern for your property, or "False" otherwise.
* **Climate Mitigation**: Select "True" if you have taken or plan to take measures to mitigate climate change, or "False" otherwise.
* **Climate Adaptation**: Select "True" if you have taken or plan to take measures to adapt to climate change, or "False" otherwise.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use the available space in the "Comments" field to provide any additional context or information about your property.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can leave it blank and provide an explanation in the "Comments" field.
* If you have any questions or need help with this form, please contact us.
