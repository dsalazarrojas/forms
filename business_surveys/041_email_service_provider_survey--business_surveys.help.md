# Email Service Provider Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gather feedback and information about your email service provider experience. Your input will help us improve our services and better understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your current email service provider from the dropdown menu.
2. Rate your service provider's performance on a scale from 1 to 10.
3. Choose how often you use email.
4. Select the features you currently use for email marketing automation, email list management, customer service, and email analytics.
5. Rate your service provider's open rate, bounce rate, complaint rate, and spam rate.
6. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
7. Enter any other comments or suggestions you may have about your email service provider experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **current_service** (Select One): Select the email service provider you are currently using.
* **service_rating** (Number): Rate your email service provider's performance on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **email_frequency** (Number): Choose how often you use email.
* **email_frequency_what** (Select One): Select how often you use email.
* **email_preferences** (Select Multiple): Choose the features you currently use for email marketing automation, email list management, customer service, and email analytics.
* **open_rate** (Number): Rate your email service provider's open rate.
* **bounce_rate** (Number): Rate your email service provider's bounce rate.
* **complaint_rate** (Number): Rate your email service provider's complaint rate.
* **spam_rate** (Number): Rate your email service provider's spam rate.
* **feedback** (Text): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **comments** (Text): Enter any other comments or feedback you may have about your email service provider experience.
* **suggestions** (Text): Enter any other comments or suggestions you may have about your email service provider experience.
* **email_service_id** (Text): Enter the email service provider ID.
* **email_service_id_hint** (Text): Enter a brief description of the email service ID.
* **service_providers** (Select One): Select the email service provider you are currently using.
* **comments_hint** (Text): Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about the email service provider experience.
* **suggestions_hint** (Text): Enter any other comments or suggestions you may have about the email service provider experience.
* **form_submission_id** (Text): Enter the form submission ID.

Note: The fields marked as "Hint" are for additional comments or feedback, and are optional.
