# Property Digital Experience Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Digital Experience Index Survey is a form designed to collect user feedback and ratings about properties. This survey helps businesses to understand how their properties are perceived by their customers and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your property ID.
2. Rate your experience (on a scale) with the property.
3. Select the category of your interaction with the property.
4. Enter a comment about your experience with the property.
5. Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted about your feedback).
6. Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted about your feedback).
7. Provide the date you visited the property.
8. Provide the time of your visit to the property.
9. Enter any additional notes about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property ID**: <number> Enter the ID of the property you're providing feedback on. This is a unique identifier for the property.
* **Rating**: <number> Rate your experience with the property on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Category**: <select_one> Select the category of your interaction with the property. This will help us understand how you used the property.
* **Comment**: <text> Enter a brief comment about your experience with the property. This will help us understand your specific experience.
* **Email**: <email> If you want to be contacted about your feedback, please enter your email address. This will allow us to reach out to you with any follow-up questions.
* **Phone**: <text> If you want to be contacted about your feedback, please enter your phone number. This will allow us to reach out to you with any follow-up questions.
* **Date**: <date> Enter the date you visited the property. This will help us understand your timeline of interaction with the property.
* **Time**: <time> Enter the time of your visit to the property. This will help us understand your specific experience with the property.
* **Notes**: <note> Enter any additional notes or comments about your experience with the property. This is an optional field for any further details you'd like to provide.
