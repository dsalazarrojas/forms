# Weekly Delivery Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect delivery preferences information from customers, allowing us to better understand their delivery time windows and instructions for smooth delivery experience. Please answer these questions to help us serve you better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the sections below, providing your delivery preferences and feedback.
2. For "Delivery Instructions", select the option that best describes your delivery experience.
3. For "Time Windows", select all the time slots that are convenient for you.
4. Provide your contact information, including phone number and email.
5. Click submit to send your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences_survey_1`, text, optional): Describe your delivery preferences, such as any specific instructions or requests you have.
* **Time Windows** (`time_windows_1`, select multiple, optional): Select all time slots that are convenient for you to receive delivery.
* **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions_1`, select one, optional): Choose how you would like to receive your delivery instructions, such as "No special instructions" or "Self service at designated pick-up area".
* **Satisfaction Feedback** (`satisfaction_feedback_1`, note, optional): Provide any feedback or comments about your delivery experience.
* **Phone** (`phone_1`, number, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email_1`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Delivery Preferences 2** (`delivery_preferences_survey_2`, text, optional): Describe any additional delivery preferences or requests you have.
* **Time Windows 2** (`time_windows_2`, select multiple, optional): Select all time slots that are convenient for you to receive delivery.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and complete information to help us serve you better.
* If you have any special delivery instructions or requests, please specify them in the "Delivery Instructions" field.
* If you have any feedback or comments about your delivery experience, please share them in the "Satisfaction Feedback" field.
