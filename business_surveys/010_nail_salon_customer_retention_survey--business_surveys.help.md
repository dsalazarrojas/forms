# Nail Salon Customer Retention Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nail Salon Customer Retention Survey is designed to help us understand how you, our valued customers, experience our services. Your feedback is vital in helping us identify areas of improvement and enhance your overall experience with our salon. Please take a few minutes to provide your honest answers, and we will use them to make informed decisions to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start.
2. Answer each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. Select "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text, Required):
	+ Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, Number, Required):
	+ Indicate how often you visit our salon. Choose a number that best describes your frequency, e.g., "Once a month" or "Every two weeks".
* **Last Visit Date** (`last_visit_date`, Date, Required):
	+ Enter the date of your last visit to our salon.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, Select, Required):
	+ Rate your satisfaction level with our services. Choose between "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".
* **Services Purchased** (`services_purchased`, Select Multiple, Required):
	+ Check all the services that you purchased during your last visit, e.g., "Nail polish change", "Manicure", etc.
* **Loyalty Program Enrolled** (`loyalty_program_enrolled`, Select, Required):
	+ If you are enrolled in our loyalty program, please select "Yes". If not, select "No".
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required):
	+ Enter your email address where we can reach you for updates and promotions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required):
	+ Enter your phone number where we can contact you for any last-minute updates or promotions.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Note, Required):
	+ Share your thoughts, concerns, or suggestions about your experience with our salon. This is your opportunity to provide detailed feedback.
* **Rating** (`rating`, Number, Required):
	+ Rate your experience out of 5. A higher number indicates greater satisfaction.
* **Loyalty Program Comments** (`loyalty_program_comments`, Note, Required):
	+ If enrolled in our loyalty program, please provide any additional comments about your experience with the program.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
