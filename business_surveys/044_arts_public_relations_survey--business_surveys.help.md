# Arts Public Relations Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Arts Public Relations Survey is a tool designed to gather information from arts organizations and institutions about their experiences with public relations (PR) campaigns. This form aims to identify trends and insights that can inform future PR strategies and initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and title/role within the organization.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Select the type of arts organization or institution you represent.
4. Rate your awareness of current PR campaigns.
5. Evaluate the reach and visibility of current PR campaigns.
6. Assess the clarity of PR messaging.
7. Assess the quality of media coverage.
8. Evaluate the effectiveness of social media campaigns.
9. Rate the level of audience engagement.
10. Rate the effectiveness of communication with stakeholders.
11. Identify areas for PR improvement.
12. (Optional) Provide additional feedback or suggestions.
13. Grant permission to follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Your Title or Role** (`respondent_title`, Text, Required): Enter your official title or role within the organization.
* **Organization or Arts Institution** (`organization_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your organization or institution.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, Required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Type of Arts Organization** (`organization_type`, Select One, Required): Select from the list to indicate the type of arts organization or institution you represent (Museum, Theater, Gallery, Music Organization, Dance Company, Festival, Arts Nonprofit, Educational Institution).
* **Awareness of Current PR Campaigns** (`pr_awareness`, Select One, Required): Rate your level of awareness of current PR campaigns. Options: Very Aware, Somewhat Aware, Minimally Aware, Not Aware.
* **Perceived Campaign Reach and Visibility** (`campaign_reach`, Select One, Required): Rate the reach and visibility of current PR campaigns. Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Clarity of PR Messaging** (`messaging_clarity`, Select One, Required): Rate the clarity of PR messaging. Options: Very Clear, Clear, Somewhat Clear, Unclear.
* **Quality of Media Coverage** (`media_coverage`, Select One, Optional): Rate the quality of media coverage. Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Social Media PR Effectiveness** (`social_media_effectiveness`, Select One, Required): Rate the effectiveness of social media campaigns. Options: Very Effective, Effective, Somewhat Effective, Not Effective.
* **Audience Engagement Level** (`audience_engagement`, Select One, Required): Rate the level of audience engagement. Options: High, Moderate, Low, Very Low.
* **Stakeholder Communication** (`stakeholder_communication`, Select One, Required): Rate the effectiveness of communication with stakeholders. Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **PR Improvement Areas** (`pr_improvements`, Text, Required): Identify areas where PR can be improved.
* **Current PR Strengths** (`pr_strengths`, Text, Optional): Identify what is working well with PR.
* **PR Budget Adequacy** (`budget_adequacy`, Select One, Optional): Rate the adequacy of the PR budget. Options: More Than Adequate, Adequate, Slightly Inadequate, Significantly Inadequate.
* **Priority Initiatives for Next Year** (`priority_initiatives`, Text, Optional): Suggest priority initiatives for next year.
* **Effectiveness of PR Team or Department** (`pr_team_effectiveness`, Select One, Optional): Rate the effectiveness of the PR team or department. Options: Very Effective, Effective, Somewhat Effective, Not Effective.
* **Perceived Community Perception of Organization** (`community_perception`, Select One, Optional): Rate the perceived community perception of your organization. Options: Very Positive, Positive, Neutral, Negative.
* **Additional Feedback or Suggestions** (`additional_feedback`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions.
* **May We Follow Up With You** (`follow_up_permission`, Select One, Optional): Grant permission to follow up with you. Options: Yes, No.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please provide a brief explanation.
* You can skip questions that are not applicable to your situation.
* If you have any other questions or need clarification, please contact form@gic.mx.
