# leadership_maturity_assessment_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Leadership Maturity Assessment Survey is a tool used to evaluate an individual's leadership style and approach to management. It assesses their leadership maturity by asking questions about their decision-making, team development, and feedback practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate options for each question by clicking on the corresponding buttons.
2. Review each section carefully and make sure to answer all questions honestly.
3. Submit the form once you have finished answering all the questions.

* **Section 1**: This is the header section of the survey.
	+ **Leadership Maturity Assessment Survey**: This is the title of the survey.
	+ **Section 1**: This is the first section of the survey.
* **Section 2**: This section assesses your approach to leadership style.
	+ **What is your role in the organization?**: This question asks about your current position in the organization.
