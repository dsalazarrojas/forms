# Curriculum Relevance For Future Skills Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand the relevance and alignment of our training and development programs with your future skills needs.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly to the best of your abilities.
2. For each question, select only one answer from the provided options.
3. If you have any further comments or suggestions, use the text fields provided.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your role in the company?** (`question1`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Please select your current role in the company from the provided options.
* **Do you currently have access to training or resources that support your learning and development needs?** (`question2`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Check all options that apply to your situation.
* **How often do you participate in training or professional development activities?** (`question3`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the option that best describes your participation frequency.
* **How would you rate the relevance of our training and development programs to your future career goals?** (`question4`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Choose the option that best reflects your assessment of the programs' relevance.
* **How many years do you think it will take to gain proficiency in the new skills required for your current role?** (`question5`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Enter a numerical value representing the expected time to gain proficiency.
* **How many months do you think it will take to achieve proficiency in 1-2 areas of new skills required for your current role?** (`question6`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Enter a numerical value representing the expected time to achieve proficiency in 1-2 areas.
* **How many months do you think it will take to achieve proficiency in 3-5 areas of new skills required for your current role?** (`question7`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Enter a numerical value representing the expected time to achieve proficiency in 3-5 areas.
* **What do you think is the most important factor in our training and development programs?** (`question8`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the option that you consider most important for your training needs.
* **How likely are you to participate in the programs we offer?** (`question9`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Check all options that apply to your willingness to participate.
* **Do you currently use any online learning platforms or tools?** (`question10`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Check all options that apply to your current usage.
* **How do you think our training and development programs can be improved?** (`question11`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Check all options that apply to your suggestions for improvement.
* **Do you have a preferred way of communicating with us?** (`question13`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Check all options that apply to your preferred communication method.
* **Do you have any further comments or suggestions?** (`question14`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you have for our training and development programs.
