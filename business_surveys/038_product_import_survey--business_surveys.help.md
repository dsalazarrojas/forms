## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **product_name** (product name, text, required): Enter the name of the product.
- **product_description** (product description, text, required): Enter a brief description of the product.
- **product_image** (product image, text, required): Enter or upload an image of the product.
- **product_price** (product price, number, required): Enter the price of the product.
- **product_status** (product status, select_one, required): Select the status of the product (Active or Inactive).
- **product_category** (product category, text, required): Enter the category of the product.
- **supplier_rating** (supplier rating, number, required): Enter the rating of the supplier.
- **product_rating** (product rating, number, required): Enter the rating of the product.
- **product_import_date** (product import date, date, required): Enter the date the product was imported.
- **product_import_time** (product import time, time, required): Enter the time the product was imported.
- **product_import_notes** (product import notes, note, required): Add any additional notes about the product import.
- **product_id** (product id, number, required): Enter the internal product ID.
- **product_code** (product code, number, required): Enter the internal product code.
- **supplier_email** (supplier email, email, required): Enter the supplier's email address.
- **supplier_phone** (supplier phone, text, required): Enter the supplier's phone number.
- **supplier_address** (supplier address, text, required): Enter the supplier's address.
- **supplier_city** (supplier city, text, required): Enter the supplier's city.
- **supplier_country** (supplier country, text, required): Enter the supplier's country.
- **supplier_contact** (supplier contact, text, required): Enter the supplier's contact information.
- **supplier_website** (supplier website, text, required): Enter the supplier's website.
- **product_description_long** (product description long, text, required): Enter a long description of the product.

Note: The required fields are marked as "required" in the YAML, but in the explanation above, they are also marked as "required" to emphasize their importance.
