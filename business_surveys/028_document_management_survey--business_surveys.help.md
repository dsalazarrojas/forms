# Document Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your document management habits, preferences, and challenges. Your feedback will help us improve the document management system to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Take a few minutes to review each question carefully.
2. Select the best option for each question based on your experience and preferences.
3. Enter your answers in the required fields.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have in the "Any other feedback or suggestions" field at the end of the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: Please take a few minutes to complete this survey.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Purpose: Introduction to the survey.
* **What type of documents do you mostly handle?**: Select the type of documents you most frequently work with (e.g., pdf, doc, xls).
	+ Type: Select One field
	+ Purpose: To identify the types of documents you handle.
* **How often do you use documents in your work?**: Select how often you use documents in your work (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
	+ Type: Select One field
	+ Purpose: To understand your document usage frequency.
* **How much time do you spend on document management?**: Enter the amount of time you spend on document management (in hours).
	+ Type: Number field
	+ Purpose: To understand your document management time commitment.
* **What is your biggest document management challenge?**: Describe your biggest document management challenge or problem.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Purpose: To identify areas for improvement in the document management system.
* **How many documents do you typically handle per day?**: Enter the number of documents you usually handle in a day.
	+ Type: Number field
	+ Purpose: To gauge your document handling volume.
* **How do you usually access documents?**: Select the methods you use to access documents (e.g., file cabinet, cloud storage, email).
	+ Type: Select Multiple field
	+ Purpose: To understand your document access habits.
* **Can you access documents from anywhere?**: Check if you can access documents from anywhere (e.g., office, home, or on-the-go).
	+ Type: Checkbox field
	+ Purpose: To understand your document access flexibility.
* **Do you think document management could be improved?**: Select if you believe the document management system can be improved.
	+ Type: Select One field
	+ Purpose: To gauge your opinion on the document management system.
* **Would you like to provide additional comments?**: Use this field to provide any further feedback or suggestions.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Purpose: To gather any additional feedback you may have.
* **How likely are you to use the proposed document management system?**: Select your likelihood of using the proposed system (e.g., very likely, not very likely).
	+ Type: Select One field
	+ Purpose: To gauge your interest in the proposed system.
* **What do you think about the proposed document management system?**: Provide your thoughts or feedback on the proposed system.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Purpose: To gather your opinions on the proposed system.
* **Any other feedback or suggestions?**: Use this field to provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Purpose: To gather any other feedback you may have.
* **How satisfied are you with the current document management system?**: Enter your level of satisfaction with the current system.
	+ Type: Number field
	+ Purpose: To gauge your satisfaction with the current system.
* **Would you be willing to pay for the proposed document management system?**: Select if you would be willing to pay for the proposed system.
	+ Type: Select One field
	+ Purpose: To gauge your willingness to pay for the proposed system.
* **Do you have any other feedback or suggestions?**: Use this field to provide any additional feedback or suggestions.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Purpose: To gather any other feedback you may have.
