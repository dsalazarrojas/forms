# Residential Energy Efficiency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a residential building's energy efficiency. It is used by auditors or assessors to conduct energy efficiency surveys and identify potential areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide basic user information.
2. Enter household details.
3. Select the type of energy used (e.g., Electricity, Gas, or Water).
4. Choose potential energy upgrades (e.g., Refrigerators, Water Heaters, or Air Conditioners).
5. Add any additional notes or comments.
6. Enter your email address and contact number.
7. Add any further notes or comments for the auditor.
8. Provide an energy efficiency rating (High, Medium, or Low).
9. Add any final notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_info**: Enter your name and other contact information.
* **household_info**: Describe the building you are surveying (e.g., number of bedrooms, square footage, etc.).
* **energy_use**: Select the type of energy used in the building (e.g., Electricity, Gas, or Water).
* **upgrades**: Choose potential energy upgrades that could be implemented in the building.
* **notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the building's energy efficiency.
* **email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your energy efficiency survey.
* **phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **user_notes**: Add any final notes or comments about your energy efficiency survey.
* **auditor_notes**: Add any notes or comments for the auditor's reference.
* **energy_use_notes**: Add any additional notes about the building's energy use.
* **energy_efficiency**: Rate the building's energy efficiency (High, Medium, or Low).
* **upgrade_notes**: Add any notes about potential energy upgrades.
* **energy_efficiency_notes**: Add any final notes or comments about the building's energy efficiency.
* **auditor_info**: Add any notes about the auditor's visit or survey process.
