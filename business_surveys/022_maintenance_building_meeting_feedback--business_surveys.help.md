# Maintenance Building Meeting Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps you collect feedback from participants after a maintenance building meeting. It's essential to provide accurate and relevant information about the meeting, including attendance, discussion topics, and action items. By filling out this form, you'll help improve future meetings and ensure that tasks are followed up on.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the meeting that you are providing feedback about.
2. Indicate whether each participant attended the meeting.
3. Describe the topic(s) discussed during the meeting.
4. Enter any action items that came out of the meeting.
5. For each action item, choose its status and provide any additional comments.
6. Enter the contact information for the person responsible for maintenance, including their email and phone number (if applicable).
7. Enter any follow-up information, such as whether to follow up with this person.
8. Finally, enter any additional information about the meeting facilitator and participants, including their email and phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required/optional: required): Select the date of the meeting you are providing feedback about.
* **Meeting Attendance** (`meeting_attendance`, select_multiple, required/optional: required): Indicate whether each participant attended the meeting by choosing 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Meeting Topic** (`meeting_topic`, text, required/optional: required): Describe the topic(s) discussed during the meeting.
* **Action Items** (`action_items`, text, required/optional: required): Enter any action items that came out of the meeting.
* **Action Item 1** (`action_item_1`, select_one, required/optional: required): For each action item, choose its status by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Status** (`action_item_1_status`, select_multiple, required/optional: optional): Enter the status of the action item, such as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_one, required/optional: optional): Indicate whether to follow up with the person responsible for maintenance.
* **Maintenance Contact** (`maintenance_contact`, text, required/optional: optional): Enter the contact information for the person responsible for maintenance.
* **Email** (`maintenance_contact_email`, email, required/optional: optional): Enter the email address of the maintenance contact.
* **Phone** (`maintenance_contact_phone`, text, required/optional: optional): Enter the phone number of the maintenance contact.
* **Notes** (`maintenance_contact_notes`, text, required/optional: optional): Enter any additional comments about the maintenance contact.
* **Meeting Facilitator** (`meeting_facilitator`, text, required/optional: optional): Enter the name of the meeting facilitator.
* **Meeting Facilitator Email** (`meeting_facilitator_email`, email, required/optional: optional): Enter the email address of the meeting facilitator.
* **Meeting Facilitator Phone** (`meeting_facilitator_phone`, text, required/optional: optional): Enter the phone number of the meeting facilitator.
* **Meeting Facilitator Notes** (`meeting_facilitator_notes`, text, required/optional: optional): Enter any additional comments about the meeting facilitator.
* **Participant 1** (`meeting_participant_1`, text, required/optional: optional): Enter the name of the first participant.
* **Participant 1 Email** (`meeting_participant_1_email`, email, required/optional: optional): Enter the email address of the first participant.
* **Participant 1 Phone** (`meeting_participant_1_phone`, text, required/optional: optional): Enter the phone number of the first participant.
* **Participant 1 Notes** (`meeting_participant_1_notes`, text, required/optional: optional): Enter any additional comments about the first participant.
* **Participant 2** (`meeting_participant_2`, text, required/optional: optional): Enter the name of the second participant.
* **Participant 2 Email** (`meeting_participant_2_email`, email, required/optional: optional): Enter the email address of the second participant.
* **Participant 2 Phone** (`meeting_participant_2_phone`, text, required/optional: optional): Enter the phone number of the second participant.
* **Participant 2 Notes** (`meeting_participant_2_notes`, text, required/optional: optional): Enter any additional comments about the second participant.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required/optional: optional): Select the date you are submitting this feedback.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask the person who facilitated the meeting for guidance.
- If you are providing feedback for a meeting with multiple participants, fill out each participant's information separately.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting to ensure they accurately reflect the meeting.
