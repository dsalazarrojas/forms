# innovation_strategy_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from users on the company's innovation strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer all questions that apply.
2. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
3. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Introduction
* **Introduction** (`note`, required: false): This is a text area for you to provide any background information about your experience with the company's innovation initiatives.

### 2. Innovation Initiatives
* **Innovation Initiatives** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the options below:
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3

### 3. Feedback
* **Feedback** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Choose one or more of the options below:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### 4. Date Submitted
* **Date Submitted** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date you submitted this feedback.

### 5. Submission Time
* **Submission Time** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time you submitted this feedback.

### 6. Email
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address for follow-up contact.

### 7. Phone
* **Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.

### 8. Submission Comments
* **Submission Comments** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have about the company's innovation initiatives.
