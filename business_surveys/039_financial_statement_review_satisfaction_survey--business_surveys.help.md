# financial_statement_review_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The financial statement review satisfaction survey is a tool used to gauge the satisfaction of users who have reviewed financial statements. The collected feedback and insights will be used to improve the financial statement review process and overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your honest and thorough feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Financial Statement** (`1`, `text`, optional): Please describe the financial statement you reviewed in your own words.
- **Review Satisfaction** (`2`, `text`, optional): Rate your satisfaction level with the review process (e.g., satisfied, neutral, dissatisfied).
- **Comments** (`3`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them here.
- **Date Reviewed** (`4`, `date`, optional): If you remember the date you reviewed the financial statement, please enter it here.
- **Review By** (`5`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you reviewed the financial statement as part of a team, please select all members who were involved.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough with your feedback.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please share them in the comments field.
* If you have already provided feedback, please do not repeat yourself; we value new and different feedback.
