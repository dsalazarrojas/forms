# Growth Strategy Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from team members regarding the growth strategy of the business. Fill out this form to provide your thoughts, suggestions, and opinions on how to improve the company's growth strategy.

## How To Complete This Form

This form consists of several fields that are divided into several categories. Please make sure to fill out each section carefully and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Save** (`form_submit_button`, text, required): Click this button to submit your feedback once you have completed all the fields. Make sure to review all the fields carefully before submitting your form.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): If you are submitting feedback on behalf of a specific business, enter the name of the business here.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name to be associated with your feedback.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): If you have a job title, enter it here to provide context to your feedback.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): If you have a specific role within the company, enter it here.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the name of the department you belong to, if applicable.
* **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of feedback you are providing (e.g., suggestion, concern, idea, etc.).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Enter your feedback, thoughts, or opinions on the company's growth strategy.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or context to your feedback.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the company's growth strategy (e.g., 1-5).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your work email for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your work phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you are providing your feedback.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time when you are providing your feedback.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes or context to your feedback.

## Tips
* Make sure to be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* Be respectful and constructive in your feedback.
* If you have any suggestions, try to provide concrete ideas or solutions.
* Review all the fields carefully before submitting your form.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to reach out to us.
