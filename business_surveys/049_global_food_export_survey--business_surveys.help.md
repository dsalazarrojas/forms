# Global Food Export Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from businesses that export food products, aiming to understand their market, product, export channels, shipping, logistics, export terms, contact, and company details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all the export markets you are involved in (Export Markets).
2. Choose the product(s) you are exporting (Product Info).
3. Choose the export channel(s) you use (Export Channels).
4. Provide more details about your shipping and logistics (Shipping Details). This field is a text box for any additional information.
5. Enter the cost of logistics for your export (Logistics Costs).
6. Specify any export terms you apply (Export Terms). This field is a text box for any additional information.
7. Provide your contact information (Contact Info). This field is a text box for any additional information.
8. Enter a brief description of your company (Company Info). This field is a text box for any additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Export Markets** (export_market, select_multiple, optional): Select all the markets you are involved in, e.g., the countries you export to.
* **Product Info** (product_info, select_one, optional): Choose the product(s) you are exporting. For example, "Yes for meat products and No for dairy products."
* **Export Channels** (export_channels, select_one, optional): Choose the export channel(s) you use, e.g., "Yes for direct-to-customer and No for brokered".
* **Shipping Details** (shipping_details, text, optional): Provide more details about your shipping and logistics, e.g., "We use refrigerated trucks and ships."
* **Logistics Costs** (logistics_costs, number, optional): Enter the cost of logistics for your export.
* **Export Terms** (export_terms, text, optional): Specify any export terms you apply, e.g., "FASDA and FASOS standards".
* **Contact Info** (contact_info, text, optional): Provide your contact information, e.g., "Phone number and email address."
* **Company Info** (company_info, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your company, e.g., "Company name, location, and size."
