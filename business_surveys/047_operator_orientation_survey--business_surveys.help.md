# operator_orientation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from operators about their experience and satisfaction with their onboarding process. This will help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Select the correct option for the Assigned Operator field, if applicable.
4. Enter your contact details, including phone number and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (``,  , optional): This is a brief description of the form and its purpose. Please read it to understand what we're asking you to do.
* **Operator Experience** (`operator_experience`, optional): Rate your overall experience with our onboarding process, on a scale of 0-10.
* **Onboarding Quality** (`onboarding_quality`, optional): Rate the quality of your onboarding process, on a scale of 0-10.
* **Operator Satisfaction** (`operator_satisfaction`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with your onboarding process, on a scale of 0-10.
* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback you have about your onboarding process.
* **Assigned Operator** (`assigned_operator`, optional): Select the operator who was assigned to your onboarding process. This can help us track communication and feedback.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, optional): Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, optional): Enter your email address.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, please share them in the Feedback Comments field.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your contact information and we'll be happy to help.
