# Beauty Subscription Retention Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to understand your motivations and satisfaction with your subscription services. Your responses will help us identify areas for improvement and implement changes to retain you as a subscriber.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. For select_one questions, choose the option that best represents your current situation.
3. For select_multiple questions, select all options that apply.
4. For number questions, choose a value between 1 (not important) and 5 (very important).
5. For text questions, provide as much detail as you like.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subscription Status** (Section 1, id: 2): Your current subscription status, whether active, paused, or recently cancelled.
* **How long have you been a subscriber?** (Section 1, id: 4): Select how long you have been subscribed to our service.
* **What is your current subscription status?** (Section 1, id: 3): Choose the status of your subscription (active, paused, or recently cancelled).
* **Why do you stay?** (Section 2, id: 4): Although there is no options provided, this section is intended for you to explain why you stay subscribed.
* **Product variety and discovery** (Section 2, id: 5): Rate how important product variety and discovery are for your subscription (1 not important, 5 very important).
* **Overall value for money** (Section 2, id: 6): Rate the importance of value for money for your subscription (1 not important, 5 very important).
* **Convenience of delivery** (Section 2, id: 7): Rate the importance of convenience of delivery for your subscription (1 not important, 5 very important).
* **Single favorite feature** (Section 2, id: 8): Select the single feature that you enjoy the most about our subscription service.
* **Areas for Improvement** (Section 3, id: 9): Although there are no options provided, this section is intended for you to suggest areas where we can improve.
* **Product redundancy frequency** (Section 3, id: 10): Select how often you receive products you already own or don't use (never, rarely, sometimes, or frequently).
* **Cancellation motivation** (Section 3, id: 11): Although there are many options provided, this question is not required, so only answer it if you plan to cancel or have cancelled.
* **New features** (Section 3, id: 12): Select all features that would encourage you to stay longer.
* **Overall satisfaction** (Section 3, id: 13): Rate your overall satisfaction with your last 3 boxes (0 to 10 scale).
* **Additional comments** (Section 3, id: 14): Provide any feedback you may have for our retention team.
