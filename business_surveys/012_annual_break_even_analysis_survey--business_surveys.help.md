# Annual Break-Even Analysis Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data for the annual break-even analysis of a business.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your business's information.
2. Select your industry from the options provided.
3. Choose your business's size from the options provided.
4. Select the year this analysis covers.
5. Enter your business's total fixed costs and total variable costs.
6. Enter your business's total revenue.
7. Calculate your business's break-even point.
8. Enter the date when you broke even.
9. Enter any additional comments or information.
10. Select all applicable cost reduction initiatives.
11. Enter your business's revenue growth rate.
12. Select the biggest cost driver.
13. Select all applicable financial challenges.
14. Select the accuracy of your break-even analysis.
15. Select how often you perform break-even analysis.
16. Enter any financial advisors you work with.
17. Enter any tools used for analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter your business's official name.
* **Contact Person Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person completing this survey.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your business's contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your business's contact phone number.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select your business's industry from the options provided.
* **Business Size** (`business_size`, select_one, required): Select your business's size from the options provided.
* **Reporting Year** (`reporting_year`, select_one, required): Select the year this analysis covers.
* **Total Fixed Costs** (`total_fixed_costs`, number, required): Enter your business's total fixed costs in dollars.
* **Total Variable Costs** (`total_variable_costs`, number, required): Enter your business's total variable costs in dollars.
* **Total Revenue** (`total_revenue`, number, required): Enter your business's total revenue in dollars.
* **Break-Even Point** (`break_even_point`, number, required): Calculate your business's break-even point.
* **Break-Even Date Achieved** (`break_even_date`, date, not required): Enter the date when you broke even.
* **Average Profit Margin** (`profit_margin`, number, not required): Enter your business's average profit margin.
* **Annual Unit Sales Volume** (`unit_sales_volume`, number, not required): Enter your business's total units sold.
* **Average Unit Price** (`average_unit_price`, number, not required): Enter your business's average selling price per unit.
* **Contribution Margin** (`contribution_margin`, number, not required): Enter your business's contribution margin per unit or percentage.
* **Cost Reduction Initiatives** (`cost_reduction_initiatives`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable cost reduction initiatives.
* **Revenue Growth Rate** (`revenue_growth_rate`, number, not required): Enter your business's revenue growth rate.
* **Biggest Cost Driver** (`biggest_cost_driver`, select_one, required): Select the biggest cost driver.
* **Financial Challenges** (`financial_challenges`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable financial challenges.
* **Break-Even Analysis Accuracy** (`break_even_accuracy`, select_one, required): Select the accuracy of your break-even analysis.
* **Analysis Frequency** (`analysis_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you perform break-even analysis.
* **Software or Tools Used** (`software_used`, text, not required): Enter any tools used for analysis.
* **Advisor Support** (`advisor_support`, select_one, not required): Select if you work with a financial advisor.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): Enter any other relevant information.
