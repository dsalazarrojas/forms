# Outline Creation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Outline Creation Survey" form is a tool used to gather information for creating outlines in a business context. It helps users define and organize their outline structure, content, and submission status. This form is used by business users to create outlines for their projects or documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of your project or document.
2. Provide a brief description of your outline creation needs.
3. Choose the structure type for your outline (Hierarchical or Linear).
4. Select the type and number of elements you need in your outline.
5. Determine the order and positioning of elements in your outline.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about your outline.
7. Specify the tool assigned to your outline creation process.
8. Set the submission status of your outline.
9. Provide details about the user who submitted and updated the outline.
