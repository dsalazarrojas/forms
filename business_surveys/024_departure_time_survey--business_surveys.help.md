# departure_time_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The departure_time_survey form is designed to collect information about employee or user travel dates, methods, and departure times. This form helps in improving business operations and employee travel management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information for each field.
2. Ensure that you only fill out the form once for each travel event.
3. Answer all fields accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Travel Date** (1, date, required/optional): Enter the date you traveled or are planning to travel.
- **Travel Time** (2, time, required/optional): Enter the time you traveled or are planning to travel.
- **Travel Location** (3, text, required/optional): Enter the location you traveled from or are planning to travel to.
- **Travel Method** (4, select_one, required): Select how you traveled (Airplane, Bus, Train, Car, or Other).
- **Travel Details** (5, text, required/optional): Enter any additional travel details, such as travel documents used or mode of transportation taken.
- **Departure Time** (6, time, required/optional): Enter the departure time of your travel event.

## Tips
- Be accurate and complete when filling out the form to ensure the data is helpful for business operations and employee travel management.
- Do not fill out the form more than once for the same travel event.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact the form administrator at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
