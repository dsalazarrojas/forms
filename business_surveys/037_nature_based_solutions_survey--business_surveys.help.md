# Nature Based Solutions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and information from users about nature-based solutions, their awareness barriers, benefits of interest, local authorities, and NGOs researchers.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below each field.
- Make sure to answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the form owner for clarification.
- Please fill out the form completely before submitting.

* **Nature based solutions** (`nature_based_solutions_survey`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect general information about nature-based solutions. Type in your answer in the provided text box.
* **Awareness barriers** (`awareness_barriers`, select_multiple, required: false): In this field, select all that apply. Please choose all the awareness barriers that you think are relevant to nature-based solutions. The options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Benefits interest** (`benefits_interest`, select_one, required: false): Select one benefit that is of interest to you from the provided options. The options are:

* **Local authorities** (`local_authorities`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect information about local authorities. Type in your answer in the provided text box.
