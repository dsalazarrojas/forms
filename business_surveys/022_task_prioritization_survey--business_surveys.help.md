# task_prioritization_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for task prioritization to help organizations determine the order of tasks to be completed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this task prioritization survey, follow these steps:

1. On **Page 1: Task Prioritization**, enter a brief description of your task (e.g., project, initiative, or goal).
2. On **Page 2: Task Prioritization Scale**, select the priority level for your task:
	* **Low**: for tasks with minimal impact or urgency.
	* **Medium**: for tasks with moderate impact or urgency.
	* **High**: for tasks with significant impact or high urgency.
3. On **Page 3: Selected Tasks**, select all tasks that you have chosen to work on.
4. On **Page 4: Comments**, enter any additional comments or explanations related to your task.
5. On **Page 5: Date**, enter the date when you expect to start and complete your task.
6. On **Page 6: Time**, enter the time when you expect to complete your task (e.g., hours, minutes).
7. On **Page 7: Email**, enter your email address.
8. On **Page 8: Phone**, enter your phone number.
9. On **Page 9: Task Description**, enter a detailed description of your task.
10. On **Page 10: Additional Information**, enter any additional information.
11. On **Page 11: Task Prioritization Scale 2**, select the priority level for your task:
	* **Low**: for tasks with minimal impact or urgency.
	* **Medium**: for tasks with moderate impact or urgency.
	* **High**: for tasks with significant impact or high urgency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1: Task Prioritization**: Enter a brief description of your task to help us understand what you will be working on.
* **Page 2: Task Prioritization Scale**: This question helps us determine the level of priority your task should be given.
* **Page 3: Selected Tasks**: Select all tasks that you have chosen to focus on.
* **Page 4: Comments**: Enter any additional comments or explanations related to your task.
* **Page 5: Date**: Enter the date when you expect to start and complete your task.
* **Page 6: Time**: Enter the time when you expect to complete your task.
* **Page 7: Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Page 8: Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to call you.
* **Page 9: Task Description**: Enter a detailed description of your task.
* **Page 10: Additional Information**: Enter any additional information you think is relevant.
* **Page 11: Task Prioritization Scale 2**: This question is similar to **Page 2: Task Prioritization Scale**, used to determine the priority level of your task.
* **Page 12: Task Description**: This field is not actually used in the form, it seems to be a duplicate field. Please ignore it.

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when selecting the priority level for your task.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at the email address provided.
* This form is designed to be used for task prioritization, please do not enter irrelevant information.
