The Batch Feedback Form - Help Guide
===============================

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants on various aspects of a training batch, including the instructor's expertise, quality of materials, and overall satisfaction. The feedback will help improve future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the Batch ID or Code in the first field. This is a required field.
2.  If you wish to provide your name, type it in the second field. Otherwise, you can leave it blank.
3.  Rate the instructor's expertise from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional) in the third field.
4.  Choose one of the options for the quality of training materials from the drop-down list in the fourth field.
5.  Select the pace of the session from the drop-down list in the fifth field.
6.  Type in the topic or module you found most valuable in the sixth field.
7.  If you wish, type in the topic or module you found least valuable in the seventh field. (This field is optional.)
8.  Rate your overall satisfaction with the batch from 0 to 10 in the eighth field.
9.  Provide one suggestion on how to improve the batch in the ninth field.
10. Answer the tenth field by selecting whether you would recommend this training to a colleague.
11.  Rate the cleanliness and comfort of the facilities from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional) in the eleventh field. (This field is optional.)
12. Select the date you are providing feedback on in the last field. This is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **-- Training Batch Feedback Survey --**: This is a header to explain the purpose of the form.
*   **Batch ID or Code**: Enter the batch ID or code for the training session you are providing feedback for.
*   **Name (Optional)**: If you wish to provide your name, type it in. Otherwise, you can leave it blank.
*   **How would you rate the instructor expertise?**: Rate the instructor's expertise from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional).
*   **Quality of training materials and resources**: Select one of the options for the quality of the training materials.
*   **The pace of the sessions was**: Select one of the options for the pace of the sessions.
*   **Which topic or module did you find most valuable?**: Type in the topic or module you found most valuable.
*   **Which topic or module did you find least valuable?**: If you wish, type in the topic or module you found least valuable. (This field is optional.)
*   **Overall satisfaction with this batch**: Rate your overall satisfaction from 0 to 10.
*   **What is one thing we could change to improve this batch?**: Provide one suggestion on how to improve the batch.
*   **Would you recommend this training to a colleague?**: Select whether you would recommend this training to a colleague.
*   **Rate the cleanliness and comfort of the facilities**: Rate the facilities from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Exceptional). (This field is optional.)
*   **Date of Feedback**: Select the date you are providing feedback on.

This form requires careful completion to provide accurate feedback.
