# Employee Decision Involvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Decision Involvement Survey is designed to collect feedback from employees on their experiences with decision-making processes within the company. This survey aims to gauge the level of involvement and satisfaction employees have with these processes, which will help managers and employees identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on each page to navigate through the form, and answer the questions accordingly.
- For select multiple/one options, choose the answer that best reflects your experience or opinion.
- For number fields, enter a numerical value between 1-10, with 1 being "Not at All" and 10 being "Extremely Important" or "Strongly Agree".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start** (`form_start`, text, not required): This page is the starting point of the survey. It is used to begin the form completion process. Simply click "Start" to start the survey.
* **employee_involvement** (`employee_involvement`, select multiple, not required): Answer "Yes" if you are involved in the decision-making process for the project.
* **decision_influence** (`decision_influence`, select one, not required): Choose "Yes" if you were involved in the decision-making process for the project.
* **decision_influence_scale** (`decision_influence_scale`, number, not required): Rate the level of influence you had on the decision-making process. Enter a number between 1-10, with 1 being "Not at All" and 10 being "Extremely Important".
* **decision_influence_frequency** (`decision_influence_frequency`, select one, not required): Answer "Yes" if you influence the decision-making process for the project.
* **decision_influence_scale_important** (`decision_influence_scale_important`, number, not required): Rate the level of importance you attached to the decision-making process. Enter a number between 1-10, with 1 being "Not at All" and 10 being "Extremely Important".
* **decision_influence_scale_not_important_at_all** (`decision_influence_scale_not_important_at_all`, number, not required): Rate the level of influence you had on the decision-making process. Enter a number between 1-10, with 1 being "Not at All" and 10 being "Extremely Important".
* **feedback_frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, select one, not required): Answer "Yes" if you provide feedback on the decision-making process.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, select one, not required): Answer "Yes" if you provide feedback on the decision-making process.
* **employee_satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, select one, not required): Choose "Satisfied" if you are satisfied with the decision-making process.
* **employee_commitment** (`employee_commitment`, select one, not required): Answer "Yes" if you are committed to the decision-making process.
* **employee_involvement_impact** (`employee_involvement_impact`, select one, not required): Answer "Yes" if you believe you had an impact on the decision-making process.
* **employee_recommend** (`employee_recommend`, select one, not required): Answer "Yes" if you would recommend the decision-making process.
* **employee_ideas** (`employee_ideas`, select one, not required): Answer "Yes" if you provided ideas for the decision-making process.
* **manager_satisfaction** (`manager_satisfaction`, select one, not required): Choose "Satisfied" if your manager is satisfied with the decision-making process.
* **manager_commitment** (`manager_commitment`, select one, not required): Answer "Yes" if your manager is committed to the decision-making process.
* **manager_involvement_impact** (`manager_involvement_impact`, select one, not required): Answer "Yes" if your manager had an impact on the decision-making process.
* **manager_recommend** (`manager_recommend`, select one, not required): Answer "Yes" if you would recommend the decision-making process.
* **overall_satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select one, not required): Choose "Satisfied" if you are overall satisfied with the decision-making process.
