# Machinery Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about the machines they operate. By providing your honest and detailed feedback, you contribute to the improvement of the machines' performance and overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, fill in your user details:
	* Enter your email address in the "user-email" field.
	* Provide your name in the "user-name" field.
2. Rate your experience with each machine:
	* Choose the machine type by selecting one of the listed options (e.g., Machine 1, Machine 2, Machine 3, Machine 4, or Machine 5).
	* Rate the machine's performance on a scale of 1-5, where 5 is excellent and 1 is poor.
	* Enter any comments or suggestions about the machine.
3. Repeat the same steps for the second machine (if applicable).
4. Click the "submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **machine-id** (Number): Enter the ID number of the machine you're providing feedback about.
* **user-email** (Email): Enter your email address for our records.
* **user-name** (Text): Provide your name for us to address you personally.
* **satisfaction** (Number): Rate how satisfied you are with the machine's performance, using the scale 1-5.
* **performance** (Number): Rate the machine's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **comments** (Text): Share any comments or suggestions you have about the machine.
* **machine-type** (Select One): Select the type of machine you're providing feedback about.
* **submit** (Note): Click to submit your feedback and complete the form.
* **machine-id-2** (Number): Enter the ID number of the second machine you're providing feedback about (if applicable).
* **performance-2** (Number): Rate the performance of the second machine you're providing feedback about (if applicable).
* **comments-2** (Text): Enter any comments or suggestions about the second machine (if applicable).
* **performance-2** (Number): Repeat the same steps for the second machine as the first machine.
