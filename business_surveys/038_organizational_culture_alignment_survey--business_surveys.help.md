# Organizational Culture Alignment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Culture Alignment Survey aims to assess the level of cultural alignment between an individual's personal values and work habits and the organization's culture. This survey is designed to help employees understand their own cultural alignment and work style, as well as facilitate team collaboration and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the introduction page for background information on the purpose and objectives of the survey.
2.  For each field, enter your information accurately and completely.
3.  Ensure you have completed all required fields (indicated by *) before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (employee_name, text, required*)
    Your name as it appears in company records.
* **Manager Name** (manager_name, text, required*)
    The name of your direct supervisor.
* **Department** (department, text, optional)
    The department in which you are currently working.
* **Team** (team, select_multiple, optional)
    Indicate whether you are part of a team and if so, whether you are the team lead.
*   • Yes: Indicate that you are part of a team.
    • No: Indicate that you are not part of a team.
* **Location** (location, text, required*)
    The location where you work (e.g., office, home, remotely).
* **Job Title** (job_title, text, optional)
    Your current job title or position.
* **Work Hours** (work_hours, time, required*)
    Your regular work hours (e.g., 08:00 - 17:00).
* **Email** (email, email, optional)
    Your work email address.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional)
    Your work phone number.
* **Rating 1 to 10** (rating_scale_1_to_10, number, required*)
    Rate your cultural alignment with the organization on a scale of 1 to 10.
*   • 1: Very low alignment.
    • 10: Very high alignment.
* **Comments** (comments, text, optional)
    Add any additional comments or feedback about your cultural alignment.

## Tips
- Ensure that you answer all required fields to ensure accurate results.
- Be as specific and honest as possible when answering questions.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please contact your HR representative for assistance.
