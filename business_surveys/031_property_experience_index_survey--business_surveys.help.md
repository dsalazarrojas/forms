# Property Experience Index Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Property Experience Index Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from visitors to a property. It helps property managers and administrators understand the visitor's experience and identify areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Property Id**: Enter the unique ID of the property you are visiting or interacting with.
2. **Select Property Type**: Choose the type of property you are visiting or interacting with from the dropdown menu.
3. **Enter Property Address**: Enter the physical address of the property.
4. **Select Property Manager**: Choose the person responsible for managing the property from the dropdown menu.
5. **Rate Your Experience**: Rate your overall experience with the property using the numerical scale provided.
6. **Identify Improvement Areas**: In the text field, describe any areas you think could be improved.
7. **Add Comments**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, enter them in this text field.
8. **Enter Email**: Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you.
9. **Enter Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
10. **Select Date**: Choose the date of your visit from the calendar picker.
11. **Select Time**: Choose the time of your visit from the time picker.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Id (1)**: Enter the unique ID of the property you are visiting or interacting with.
* **Property Type (2)**: Choose the type of property you are visiting or interacting with from the dropdown menu.
 	+ *Example*: If you are visiting a hotel, select "Hotel".
* **Property Address (3)**: Enter the physical address of the property.
* **Property Manager (4)**: Choose the person responsible for managing the property from the dropdown menu.
* **Visitor Experience (5)**: Rate your overall experience with the property using the numerical scale provided.
* **Overall Satisfaction (6)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the property using the numerical scale provided.
* **Improvement Areas (7)**: Describe any areas you think could be improved in the text field.
* **Comments (8)**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, enter them in this text field.
* **Email (9)**: Enter your email address so that we can follow up with you.
* **Phone (10)**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Date (11)**: Choose the date of your visit from the calendar picker.
* **Time (12)**: Choose the time of your visit from the time picker.
