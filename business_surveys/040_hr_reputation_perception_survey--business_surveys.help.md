# HR Reputatioin Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback about the HR department's performance and service. It is intended to understand employee perceptions and opinions about HR's values, interactions, responsiveness, feedback, and overall performance. The survey aims to identify areas of improvement and help the HR department better serve the employees and the organization as a whole.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Select the options that best describe your opinions and experiences with HR.
2. Choose from the provided options for each field.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, even if you don't have an opinion on a particular aspect.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What aspects of HRs performance do you value most?** (`employee_values`, select_multiple): This field asks you to select the most important aspects of HR's performance. Choose the options that best describe what you value most in your interactions with HR.
	+ Communication: This aspect of HR's performance refers to the clarity and timeliness of HR's communication with you.
	+ Responsiveness: This aspect of HR's performance refers to how quickly and effectively HR responds to your needs and concerns.
	+ Leadership: This aspect of HR's performance refers to the guidance and support provided by HR to help you achieve your goals and objectives.
	+ Teamwork: This aspect of HR's performance refers to how well HR collaborates with other departments to address your needs and concerns.
	+ Problem-solving: This aspect of HR's performance refers to HR's ability to analyze and resolve issues efficiently and effectively.
	+ Organization: This aspect of HR's performance refers to the clarity and structure of HR's work processes and procedures.
	+ Time management: This aspect of HR's performance refers to how well HR manages its time and resources to address your needs and concerns.
	+ Other: This option allows you to select any other aspects of HR's performance that are not listed above.
* **How many of you have had interactions with HR?** (`interactions_with_hr`, select_one): This field asks about your frequency of interactions with HR. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Always: You interact with HR regularly and frequently.
	+ Sometimes: You interact with HR occasionally, but not frequently.
	+ Rarely: You interact with HR infrequently.
	+ Never: You have never interacted with HR.
* **How many of you have had positive interactions with HR?** (`positive_interactions`, select_one): This field asks about your experience with positive interactions with HR. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Always: You always have positive interactions with HR.
	+ Sometimes: You sometimes have positive interactions with HR.
	+ Rarely: You rarely have positive interactions with HR.
	+ Never: You never have positive interactions with HR.
* **How would you rate HRs responsiveness?** (`hr_responsiveness`, select_one): This field asks about your opinion on HR's responsiveness. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Excellent: HR responds very quickly and effectively.
	+ Good: HR responds in a timely manner, but not always promptly.
	+ Fair: HR responds, but the response time is variable.
	+ Poor: HR rarely responds or not at all.
* **How often do you receive feedback from HR?** (`feedback_frequency`, select_one): This field asks about your experience with feedback from HR. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Always: You always receive feedback from HR.
	+ Sometimes: You sometimes receive feedback from HR.
	+ Rarely: You rarely receive feedback from HR.
	+ Never: You never receive feedback from HR.
* **How would you rate HRs willingness to listen to and act upon feedback?** (`feedback_willingness`, select_one): This field asks about HR's openness to feedback. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Highly: HR is very willing to listen to and act upon feedback.
	+ Somewhat: HR is somewhat willing to listen to and act upon feedback.
	+ Neutral: HR has a neutral stance on feedback.
	+ Somewhat unhelpful: HR is not very willing to listen to or act upon feedback.
	+ Not at all: HR is not willing to listen to or act upon feedback at all.
* **How likely are you to recommend HRs services to a colleague or friend?** (`recommendation`, select_one): This field asks about your likelihood to recommend HR's services. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Very likely: You would highly recommend HR's services.
	+ Somewhat likely: You would somewhat recommend HR's services.
	+ Not likely: You would not recommend HR's services.
	+ Not at all likely: You would not recommend HR's services at all.
* **Overall, how would you rate HRs performance and service?** (`overall_rating`, select_one): This field asks about your overall opinion on HR's performance. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Excellent: HR performs very well and effectively.
	+ Good: HR performs well, but not exceptionally.
	+ Fair: HR performs somewhat adequately.
	+ Poor: HR performs poorly and ineffectively.

## Tips
Please answer this survey honestly and provide your genuine opinions about HR's performance. Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve and better serve you and the organization.
