# Company Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from respondents about their experience with the company. It will help us understand your satisfaction, areas for improvement, and future plans.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address in the **Your Name** and **Email Address** fields.
2. Select your relationship with the company (e.g., Current Employee, Customer, etc.) in the **I am a** field.
3. If you are an employee, select your department from the list of options in the **Department (if employee)** field.
4. Rate your relationship length with the company in the **How long have you been associated with the company?** field.
5. Rate your overall satisfaction with the company in the **Overall, how satisfied are you with the company?** field.
6. Answer the multiple-choice questions in the **-- Quality Assessment --** section, rating the quality of products/services and value for money.
7. Answer the multiple-choice questions in the **-- Service Assessment --** section, rating customer service, responsiveness, and communication clarity.
8. In the **-- Communication --** section, rate the frequency of communication and its clarity.
9. In the **-- Areas for Improvement --** section, select all areas where you think the company needs improvement.
10. Specify the details of areas for improvement in the **Please specify improvement areas** field.
11. In the **-- Company Strengths --** section, select all areas where the company excels.
12. Elaborate on the company's strengths in the **Please elaborate on strengths** field.
13. In the **-- Additional Feedback --** section, provide any additional comments or suggestions.
14. Answer the multiple-choice question in the **What is the biggest challenge you face with the company?** field.
15. Provide any suggestions for improvement in the **What suggestions do you have for improvement?** field.
16. In the **-- Future Outlook --** section, answer the multiple-choice question about future plans with the company.
17. In the **-- Future Expectations --** section, specify your expectations for the future.
18. In the **Any additional comments?** field, provide any thoughts or comments you may have.

* **What areas need improvement?**: Select all areas where you think the company needs improvement.

* **What does the company do well?**: Select all areas where the company excels.
* **Please elaborate on strengths**: Elaborate on the company's strengths.
* **What is the biggest challenge you face with the company?**: Answer the multiple-choice question.
* **What suggestions do you have for improvement?**: Provide any suggestions for improvement.

## Tips

* Be honest and objective when answering the questions.
* Rate your answers based on your personal experience and opinion.
* Provide concrete and specific answers whenever possible.
* If you are unsure about a question, select the "Not applicable" option.
