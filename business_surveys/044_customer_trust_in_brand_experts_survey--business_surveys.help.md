# Customer Trust In Brand Experts Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form, "Customer Trust In Brand Experts Survey", is used to assess your perception of our company's customer service team. It consists of several questions designed to gauge your level of satisfaction and trust in our experts, as well as gather additional feedback and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and select the answer that best matches your sentiment. For multiple-choice questions, choose one response. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you'd like in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How Well Do You Know Our Customer Service Team** (`customer_trust_in_brand_experts`, `select_one`): Select one response that reflects your level of knowledge about our customer service team. Choose "Very Well" if you feel confident in their expertise, "Well" if you have some familiarity but may not be fully informed, "Somewhat" if you're unsure, or "Not Well" if you don't know much about them.
- **Overall How Much Do You Trust Our Brand Experts** (`trust_in_experts`, `number`): This question is not required, but if you choose to answer, rate how much you trust our brand experts on a scale from 0 to 10.
- **Based On Your Experience With Us, How Would You Rate Our Brand Experts** (`expert_experience`, `select_one`): Select one response that reflects your overall experience with our brand experts. Choose "Excellent" for exceptional service, "Good" for satisfactory service, "Fair" for average service, or "Poor" for subpar service.
- **Please Share Any Additional Comments You May Have** (`customer_comments`, `note`): Provide any additional feedback or comments you may have about our customer service team or overall experience.
- **How Would You Like To Be Contacted** (`contact_method`, `select_multiple`): Select all communication methods you'd like to be contacted through (e.g., Phone, Email, SMS).
- **How Was Your Overall Experience With Our Customer Service** (`customer_service`, `select_one`): Select one response that reflects your overall experience with our customer service. Choose "Excellent" for exceptional service, "Good" for satisfactory service, "Fair" for average service, or "Poor" for subpar service.
- **How Often Do You Expect Our Experts To Be Available** (`expert_availability`, `select_multiple`): Select all response periods you expect our experts to be available (e.g., Always, Most Days, Some Days, Never).
- **Based On Your Experience, How Would You Rate Our Overall Customer Service** (`customer_experience`, `number`): This question is not required, but if you choose to answer, rate how good or bad your overall experience was with our customer service on a scale from 0 to 10.
- **Please Share Any Additional Comments You May Have** (`additional_comments`, `note`): Provide any additional feedback or comments you may have.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`): If you'd like to be contacted via email, enter your email address here.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`): If you'd like to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number here.
