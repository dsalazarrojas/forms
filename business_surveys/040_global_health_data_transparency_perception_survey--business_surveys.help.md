# global_health_data_transparency_perception_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess individuals' perceptions of global health data transparency.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each section and answer the questions accordingly. For each question, select the option that best reflects your perception or attitude.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trust** (`Trust`, `select_one`, required): This question asks how much you trust global health data. Select the option that best reflects your level of trust. 
  * Very willing: You have a high level of trust in global health data.
  * Somewhat trust: You have a moderate level of trust in global health data.
  * Not very much trust: You have a somewhat lower level of trust in global health data.
  * Not at all trust: You do not trust global health data at all.
* **Data Sharing Willingness** (`Data Sharing Willingness`, `select_one`, required): This question asks how willing you are to share global health data. Select the option that best reflects your level of willingness.
  * Very willing: You are very willing to share global health data.
  * Somewhat willing: You are somewhat willing to share global health data.
  * Not very willing: You are not very willing to share global health data.
  * Not at all willing: You are not willing to share global health data at all.
* **Data Sharing Concerns** (`Data Sharing Concerns`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks what concerns you have about sharing global health data. Select all the options that apply to you.
  * Lack of data quality
  * Data not shared on time
  * Lack of standardization
  * Data is not secure
  * Lack of transparency
* **Privacy and Security** (`Privacy and Security`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks what privacy and security concerns you have about global health data. Select all the options that apply to you.
  * Data not protected
  * Data shared without consent
  * Information leaked
* **Communication** (`Communication`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about the communication you experience about global health data. Select all the options that apply to you.
  * Inadequate communication
  * Communication not clear
  * Information not shared
* **Global Health Data Transparency** (`Global Health Data Transparency`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about your perception of global health data transparency. Select all the options that apply to you.
  * Transparency in data collection
  * Transparency in data storage
  * Transparency in data analysis
  * Transparency in data sharing
* **Data Quality** (`Data Quality`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question asks about your perception of global health data quality. Select all the options that apply to you.
  * High quality
  * Medium quality
  * Low quality

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and truthfully.
- Select all options that apply to you.
- If you have any concerns or questions, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
