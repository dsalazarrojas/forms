# Animal Shelter Resource Allocation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data on animal shelter operations, enabling the organization to better understand their resource allocation and capacity status.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the survey:

1. **Shelter Name**: Enter the name of your organization.
2. **Your Name**: Provide your full name as the contact person.
3. **Your Role**: Enter your position title within the shelter.
4. **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
5. **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
6. **Shelter Location**: Enter the city and state of your shelter.
7. **Shelter Type**: Select the type of your shelter (e.g., Municipal, Private Nonprofit, Rescue Organization, etc.).
8. **Annual Animal Capacity**: Enter the number of animals your shelter serves per year.
9. **Current Animal Population**: Enter the number of animals currently in your care.
10. **Population Breakdown**: Select all applicable species categories (e.g., Dogs, Cats, Rabbits, etc.).
11. **Current Dog Population**: (Optional) Enter the number of dogs in your care. (Note: This field is only required if you have dogs.)
12. **Current Cat Population**: (Optional) Enter the number of cats in your care. (Note: This field is only required if you have cats.)
13. **Number of Staff Members**: Enter the total number of staff members, including full and part-time workers.
14. **Number of Active Volunteers**: Enter the number of active volunteers.
15. **Annual Budget**: Select your approximate annual operating budget range.
16. **Primary Budget Sources**: Select your top 3 primary budget sources (e.g., Government Funding, Individual Donations, etc.).
17. **Current Resource Needs**: Select all applicable categories that your shelter currently needs help with (e.g., Food and Nutrition, Medical Supplies, etc.).
18. **Most Critical Resource Need**: Select the one resource need that you consider most critical.
19. **Estimated Annual Funding Gap**: Select the estimated difference between your needs and resources.
20. **Current Capacity Status**: Select the status of your current capacity.
21. **Average Monthly Intake**: Enter the average number of animals you intake per month.
22. **Average Monthly Adoptions**: Enter the average number of animals adopted per month.
23. **Average Length of Stay**: (Optional) Enter the average length of stay for animals in your care in days. (Note: This field is only required if applicable.)
24. **Type of Support**: Select all applicable types of support that would help your shelter most (e.g., Financial Grants, Food Donations, etc.).
25. **Partnership Interest**: Indicate your interest level in partnership opportunities.
26. **Additional Comments**: Share any other relevant information or context about your shelter's operations.
