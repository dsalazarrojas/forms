# public_services_disruption_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about disruptions to public services. If you have experienced a disruption, please fill out this form to provide as much detail as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure all fields apply to your situation.
2. Fill out the form accurately and honestly.
3. For each field, use the provided options or enter free text to describe your experience.
4. If a field does not apply, leave it blank or select "No" if required.
5. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please use this space to provide any necessary context for your response.

### How_was_your_service_disrupted
* **How_was_your_service_disrupted** (`How_was_your_service_disrupted`, `text`, required: false): Describe the circumstances surrounding the disruption, including any relevant details.

### Which_service_was_affected
* **Which_service_was_affected** (`Select Multiple`, required: false): If the disruption affected one or more services, please select each service that was impacted.

### What_date_was_the_service_disrupted
* **What_date_was_the_service_disrupted** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date of the disruption.

### What_time_was_the_service_disrupted
* **What_time_was_the_service_disrupted** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time of the disruption.

### Why_was_the_service_disrupted
* **Why_was_the_service_disrupted** (`text`, required: false): Provide an explanation for why the service was disrupted.

### How_long_was_the_service_disrupted
* **How_long_was_the_service_disrupted** (`text`, required: false): Describe the duration of the disruption.

### What_is_the_status_of_the_disruption
* **What_is_the_status_of_the_disruption** (`Select One`, required: false): Indicate the current status of the disruption (Active or Inactive).

### Do_you_want_to_be_contacted_for_follow_up
* **Do_you_want_to_be_contacted_for_follow_up** (`text`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted for follow-up, please indicate so and provide any additional information.
