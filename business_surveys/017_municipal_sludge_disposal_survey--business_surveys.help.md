# Municipal Sludge Disposal Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Municipal Sludge Disposal Survey is a tool designed for municipalities to share their sludge disposal practices, including treatment processes, storage capacities, types of treatment facilities, and regulatory compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through the questions below, providing accurate information about your municipality's sludge disposal practices. You can select multiple options for certain questions as needed. Ensure to answer every question, but remember that some are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your current sludge treatment process?** (`municipal_sludge_disposal_survey`): Describe your municipality's current sludge treatment process.
* **What is the capacity of your sludge storage tanks?** (`municipal_sludge_storage_capacity`): Indicate the capacity of your sludge storage tanks in the appropriate unit (e.g., gallons, cubic meters, etc.).
* **What type of treatment facility do you have?** (`municipal_treatment_facility_type`): Select from the provided options: Raw, Stabilized, Anaerobic, Aerobic, or Other.
* **How long ago was your treatment facility built?** (`municipal_treatment_facility_age`): Enter the number of years ago the facility was constructed.
* **What is the capacity of your treatment facility?** (`municipal_treatment_facility_capacity`): Enter the capacity of your treatment facility in the appropriate unit (e.g., gallons, cubic meters, etc.).
* **What is your primary sludge disposal method?** (`municipal_sludge_disposal_method`): Select from the provided options: Landfill, Landfill and Land Application, Ocean Disposal, or Other.
* **How often is sludge disposed of?** (`municipal_sludge_disposal_frequency`): Select from the options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly, or Other.
* **What is the volume of sludge disposed of per year?** (`municipal_sludge_disposal_capacity`): Enter the volume of sludge disposed of annually.
* **Do you provide regular public education on sludge disposal practices?** (`municipal_public_education`): Confirm whether your municipality engages in regular public education on sludge disposal practices.
* **Do you involve your community in sludge disposal practices?** (`municipal_community_involvement`): Confirm whether your municipality involves the community in sludge disposal decision-making.
* **Are there local regulations on sludge disposal?** (`municipal_regulations`): Confirm whether your municipality has regulations governing sludge disposal.
* **Are local regulations on sludge disposal enforced?** (`municipal_regulations_enforcement`): Confirm whether local regulations on sludge disposal are enforced.
* **Are there fines for non-compliance with local sludge disposal regulations?** (`municipal_regulations_fines`): Confirm whether your municipality levies fines for non-compliance with local regulations.
* **How often are fines levied for non-compliance with local sludge disposal regulations?** (`municipal_regulations_fines_frequencies`): Select from the options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly, or Other.
* **Are there other local regulations or permits required for sludge disposal?** (`other_regulations`): Confirm whether your municipality has other regulations or permits for sludge disposal.
* **Which department handles sludge disposal in your municipality?** (`public_works_department`): Select from the provided options: Public Works, Health, Environmental, or Other.
* **Which other departments are involved in sludge disposal decision-making?** (`other_departments_involved`): Select from the options: True or False, indicating involvement or not.
* **Do you review form submission results for sludge disposal practices?** (`municipal_sludge_disposal_survey_results`): Confirm whether your municipality reviews form submission results.
* **How often do you review form submission results for sludge disposal practices?** (`municipal_sludge_disposal_survey_frequency`): Select from the options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly, or Other.
* **Do you receive feedback from the public on sludge disposal practices?** (`municipal_sludge_disposal_survey_feedback`): Confirm whether your municipality receives feedback from the public.
* **How often do you receive feedback from the public on sludge disposal practices?** (`municipal_sludge_disposal_survey_feedback_frequencies`): Select from the options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly, or Other.
