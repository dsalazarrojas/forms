# Business Operations Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from business operations employees on their experiences and opinions on various aspects of the company's operations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your name for follow-up purposes (optional).
2. Select your department from the list.
3. Answer the questions on process efficiency, highlighting areas that could benefit from automation, and providing suggestions for improvement.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the work environment and tools/resources.
5. Indicate how effective you find internal communication.
6. Identify the main operational challenges and suggest improvements.
7. Specify any training or development needs.
8. Finally, assess your understanding of the company's business strategy and how aligned your work is with it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, Optional): Enter your name for follow-up purposes.
* **Department** (`respondent_department`, select_one, Required): Choose from Operations, Management, Finance, Human Resources, IT, Sales, Marketing, or Other.
* **Your Role** (`respondent_role`, text, Optional): Describe your position in the company.
* **Years with Company** (`tenure_years`, number, Optional): Indicate how long you have been employed with the company.
* **Process Effectiveness** (`process_effectiveness`, select_one, Required): Rate overall process effectiveness on a scale from Very Ineffective to Very Effective.
* **Process Bottlenecks** (`process_bottlenecks`, select_multiple, Optional): Identify key areas of inefficiency, including Unclear Procedures, Outdated Systems, Poor Communication, Lack of Resources, Insufficient Training, or Unclear Responsibilities.
* **Automation Potential** (`automation_potential`, select_multiple, Optional): Select areas that could benefit from automation, such as Data Entry, Report Generation, Approval Workflows, Scheduling, Communications, or Inventory Management.
* **Satisfaction with Work Environment** (`work_environment_satisfaction`, select_one, Required): Rate the physical and organizational environment on a scale from Very Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied.
* **Satisfaction with Tools and Resources** (`tools_resources_satisfaction`, select_one, Required): Rate the availability and quality of work tools on a scale from Very Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied.
* **Internal Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, Required): Rate internal communication on a scale from Very Poor to Excellent.
* **Main Challenges** (`main_challenges`, text, Optional): Identify the top 3 areas of concern.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, Optional): Provide suggestions for improvement.
* **Training or Development Needs** (`training_needs`, text, Optional): Specify any training or development needs.
* **Understanding of Business Strategy** (`strategy_understanding`, select_one, Required): Rate how clear the company's business strategy is to you on a scale from Not at all clear to Very clear.
* **Alignment with Business Strategy** (`alignment_with_strategy`, select_one, Required): Indicate how connected your role is to company goals on a scale from Not at all to Extremely.
