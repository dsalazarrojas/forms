# IT Awareness And Trust Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to measure IT awareness and trust among IT professionals and users who have interacted with our IT department. The survey is used to gauge the level of understanding about our IT services and support, as well as gather feedback about potential improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. For questions that have options, select the one that best describes your sentiment.
3. Be honest in your responses, as they are completely anonymous.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the corresponding text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief welcome message that explains the purpose and scope of the survey.
* **IT Services**: A question to gauge your awareness and agreement about our current IT services. Please select the option that best describes your sentiment:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **IT Support**: A question to measure your confidence and satisfaction with our IT support. Please select all the options that best describe your sentiment:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Security Concerns**: Please enter a number that best describes your level of concern about security issues.
* **IT Training**: A question to gauge your interest and need for IT training. Please select all the options that best describe your sentiment:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Communication**: A text field where you can write any comments or feedback about our communication.
* **Feedback**: A text field where you can write any general feedback about our IT department.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Phone**: A text field where you can write any comments or feedback about our phone support.
* **Comments 1-16**: Additional text fields where you can write any comments or feedback about our IT department.

Note: Please do not submit the form if you are not a part of our organization or have not interacted with our IT department.
