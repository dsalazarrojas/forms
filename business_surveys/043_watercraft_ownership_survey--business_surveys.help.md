# Watercraft Ownership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your watercraft ownership status, helping us understand your relationship with your vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. If unsure about any question, please provide an estimate or description.
3. Review and submit your responses before proceeding to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (General Information)** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ This is the first step in the form where you'll start by providing general information about yourself.
* **Ownership** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Please describe your watercraft ownership status (e.g., owned, leased, rented, or have no knowledge).
* **Usage** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Describe how often you use your watercraft, such as daily, weekly, or occasionally.
* **Insurance** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Check if you have insurance coverage for your watercraft and describe its details.
* **Maintenance** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Report on any regular or occasional maintenance activities for your watercraft.
