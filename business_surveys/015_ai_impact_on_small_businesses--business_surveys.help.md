# AI Impact On Small Businesses - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the impact of AI on small businesses. The data collected will help inform decision-making and resource allocation within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out your business profile section, which includes your business name and industry sector.
2. Next, provide your estimated headcount of employees.
3. Then, indicate the level of AI adoption in your business operations (e.g. None, Pilot, Operational, Strategic).
4. After that, select the main use cases of AI in your business (e.g. Customer service, Marketing, Operations, Sales, HR).
5. You may choose to provide perceived benefits of AI in your business (e.g. Cost savings, Efficiency, New revenue, Improved decisions).
6. Optionally, provide any main challenges to adoption, such as barriers or limitations.
7. Finally, indicate what kind of support you need to adopt AI in your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Business Name (Business name)
 * **Business Name** (Text, Required): A brief description of your business.
### Industry Sector (Industry sector)
 * **Industry Sector** (Text, Required): A brief description of your industry or sector.
### Number of Employees (Number of employees)
 * **Number of Employees** (Number, Required): An approximate headcount of employees in your business.
### AI Adoption Level (AI adoption level)
 * **AI Adoption Level** (Select One, Required): The level of AI adoption in your business operations (e.g. None, Pilot, Operational, Strategic).
### Main AI Use Cases (Main AI use cases)
 * **Main AI Use Cases** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all main use cases of AI in your business (e.g. Customer service, Marketing, Operations, Sales, HR).
### Perceived Benefits of AI (Perceived benefits of AI)
 * **Perceived Benefits of AI** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all perceived benefits of AI in your business (e.g. Cost savings, Efficiency, New revenue, Improved decisions).
### Main Challenges to Adoption (Main challenges to adoption)
 * **Main Challenges to Adoption** (Text, Optional): Describe any main challenges to adoption.
### Support Needed to Adopt AI (Support needed to adopt AI)
 * **Support Needed to Adopt AI** (Text, Optional): Indicate what kind of support you need to adopt AI in your business.

## Tips
- Please be as accurate as possible when filling out your business profile.
- Be specific when describing your industry or sector.
- Estimate your headcount of employees as accurately as possible.
- Choose the most relevant AI adoption level for your business.
- Select all main use cases and perceived benefits that apply to your business.
- Be as descriptive as possible when describing any main challenges to adoption and support needed to adopt AI.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
