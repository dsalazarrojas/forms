# Jitney Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jitney Usage Survey is designed to collect feedback and survey data from users about their experiences with Jitney services. This form helps service providers understand user behavior, satisfaction, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the first page, which asks about your recent Jitney trip.
2. Answer each question carefully, and provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
3. For the select options fields, choose the answer that best matches your experience.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
5. Finally, provide your email address and phone number (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive a copy of your survey results).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Jitney Usage Survey** (`jitney_usage_survey_page_1`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the survey, which you can ignore as it's just a heading.
* **How often do you use Jitney services?** (`usage_frequency`, number, optional): Please select the number that best represents how often you use Jitney services.
* **How satisfied are you with your latest Jitney trip?** (`satisfaction_rating`, select_one, optional): Choose how satisfied you were with your latest trip:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **What service level of service did you experience?** (`service_level_of_service`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the service levels that apply to your experience:
	+ On Time
	+ Late
	+ Cancelled
* **What was the condition of the Jitney vehicle?** (`vehicle_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the vehicle conditions that apply to your experience:
	+ Clean
	+ Dirty
	+ Damaged
* **What was the level of communication from the Jitney provider?** (`communication_level`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the communication levels that apply to your experience:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **How would you rate your overall experience with Jitney services?** (`vehicle_rating`, select_one, optional): Choose how you'd rate your overall experience:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **How would you rate your overall experience with Jitney services?** (`jitney_service_rating`, select_one, optional): This field is identical to the previous one, you can ignore it.
* **Is there anything you would like to see improved?** (`jitney_service_improvement`, text, optional): Please provide any suggestions or feedback you have for Jitney services.
* **Do you have any other comments about your Jitney experience?** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive a copy of your survey results.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
