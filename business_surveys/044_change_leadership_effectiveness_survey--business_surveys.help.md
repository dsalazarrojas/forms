# Change Leadership Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to collect feedback on the leadership effectiveness of a leader in your organization during a change process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete all the questions honestly and based on your direct experience with the leader.
2. Select the answers that best reflect your assessment of the leader's performance.
3. For questions with multiple options (e.g., Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly Disagree), choose the one that best fits your opinion.
4. For questions that ask for specific feedback or comments, provide a brief description or example to support your answer.
5. Please note that the survey is anonymous and your input will be kept confidential.

# Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* Provide specific examples or reasons to support your answers where possible.
* If you are unsure about a question, choose the answer that best reflects your opinion.
* You can choose only one answer for multiple-choice questions, but if you want to provide more feedback, you can use the "Leader Strengths" and "Development Areas" fields.
