# Coworking Space Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience at a coworking space.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by answering the first field, "how_did_you_hear_about_us", with a brief description of how you heard about the coworking space.
2.  Scroll down to the "Amenities" section and select one or more amenities you used during your visit. Choose all that apply.
3.  Move on to the "Coworking Space" section and select multiple amenities you found during your visit. Choose all that apply.
4.  Answer the "Overall Experience" field by rating your overall experience on a scale of 1-5.
5.  In the "Pricing Feedback" field, rate your satisfaction with the pricing of the coworking space on a scale of 1-5.
6.  If you wish to provide your contact information, enter your email address and/or phone number in the respective fields.
7.  Finally, provide any additional comments or feedback you may have in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **how_did_you_hear_about_us** (text, optional): Please describe how you heard about the coworking space.
*   **amenities** (select_one, required): Select one or more amenities you used during your visit:
    *   Parking
    *   Food and Beverage
    *   Wi-Fi
    *   Meeting Rooms
*   **coworking_space** (select_multiple, required): Select multiple amenities you found during your visit:
    *   Comfortable
    *   Well-lit
    *   Quiet
    *   Spacious
*   **overall_experience** (number, required): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5.
*   **pricing_feedback** (number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the pricing of the coworking space on a scale of 1-5.
*   **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **feedback** (note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
*   **comments** (text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
