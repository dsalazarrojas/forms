# Transformation Consulting Success Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transformation Consulting Success Survey is designed to gather feedback on the success of transformation projects. It aims to evaluate project impact, feedback, and overall success factors from project stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions based on your experience and perception of the project.
2. Provide honest and constructive feedback.
3. Take note of any questions you're unsure about, and ask for clarification if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Impact (1)** (`project_impact`, number): Estimate the overall impact of the project on your role, team, or organization.
* **What feedback do you want to give about this project? (2)** (`project_feedback`, text): Provide your honest feedback about the project.
* **Project Impact Score (3)** (`project_impact_score`, number): Rate the project's impact on a scale of 1-10.
* **Project Outcome (4)** (`project_outcome`, select_one): Select the project outcome based on the options provided (Success, Partially, Failure).
* **What feedback do you want to give about the team? (5)** (`team_feedback`, text): Provide your feedback about the team's performance.
* **Team Effectiveness (6)** (`team_effectiveness`, number): Rate the team's effectiveness on a scale.
* **Project Manager Performance (7)** (`project_manager_performance`, select_multiple): Select the project manager's performance based on the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Team Member Performance (8)** (`team_member_performance`, select_multiple): Select the team member performance based on the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Overall Satisfaction (9)** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one): Select your overall satisfaction with the project on a scale of High, Medium, Low.
* **Project Duration (10)** (`project_duration`, time): Estimate the project duration.
* **Project Budget (11)** (`project_budget`, number): Estimate the project budget.
* **Project Scope (12)** (`project_scope`, text): Describe the project scope.
* **Project Goals (13)** (`project_goals`, text): Describe the project goals.
* **Project Phases (14)** (`project_phases`, select_multiple): Select the project phases based on the options provided (Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3).
* **Client Relationship (15)** (`client_relationship`, select_multiple): Select the client relationship based on the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Client Satisfaction (16)** (`client_satisfaction`, select_one): Select the client satisfaction on a scale of High, Medium, Low.
* **Client Expectation (17)** (`client_expectation`, select_one): Select the client expectation on a scale of High, Medium, Low.
* **Team Relationship (18)** (`team_relationship`, select_multiple): Select the team relationship based on the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Team Satisfaction (19)** (`team_satisfaction`, select_one): Select the team satisfaction on a scale of High, Medium, Low.
* **Project Success Factors (20)** (`project_success_factors`, select_multiple): Select the project success factors based on the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Project Success Score (21)** (`project_success_score`, number): Rate the project's success on a scale.
* **Project Success Outcome (22)** (`project_success_outcome`, select_one): Select the project success outcome based on the options provided (Success, Partial, Failure).
* **Project Recommendation (23)** (`project_recommendation`, select_multiple): Select the project recommendation based on the options provided (Highly, Moderately, Slightly, Not at all).
* **Project Improvement Points (24)** (`project_improvement_points`, number): Estimate the project improvement points.
* **Final Comments (25)** (`final_comments`, note): Provide any final comments or suggestions.

Please note that this form is intended for internal purposes only. All information collected will be used to improve future projects.
