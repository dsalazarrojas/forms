# Non Payment Information Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information regarding instances where payment has not been made. The survey collects details on the reasons for non-payment, the amount involved, the frequency of non-payments, the method of non-payment, and related refund information.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. For multiple choice questions, select all applicable options.
3. If a question does not apply, you can skip it.
4. Enter any additional information that is not covered by the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Non Payment Reason (1)
 * **non_payment_reason**: This field is for providing a brief explanation of the reason why payment was not made. Please enter a clear and concise statement that describes the reason for non-payment.

### Non Payment Amount (2)
 * **non_payment_amount**: Enter the exact amount involved in the non-payment transaction.

### Non Payment Frequency (3)
 * **non_payment_frequency**: Select all applicable options that represent how often payments are not made in this context. Please choose from the following options: '1', '2', '3', '4'.

### Non Payment Method (4)
 * **non_payment_method**: This field is for selecting the method through which payment was not made. Choose one of the options: Bank transfer, Credit card, Cheque.

### Non Payment Service (5)
 * **non_payment_service**: This field is for providing any additional information related to the non-payment service.

### Non Payment Frequency Other (6)
 * **non_payment_frequency_other**: If the frequency of non-payment does not match any of the provided options, enter a brief description of the non-payment frequency in this field.

### Non Payment Refund (7)
 * **non_payment_refund**: Enter the amount of refund applied or in progress.

### Non Payment Refund Status (8)
 * **non_payment_refund_status**: Select the status of the refund: Applied, In progress, Not applied.

### Non Payment Refund Amount (9)
 * **non_payment_refund_amount**: Enter the amount of the refund.

### Non Payment Other Info (10)
 * **non_payment_other_info**: Provide any additional information about the non-payment situation that is not covered by the other fields.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed and accurate as possible in your responses.
- If unsure about any field, consult with your team or relevant documentation.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your support team.
