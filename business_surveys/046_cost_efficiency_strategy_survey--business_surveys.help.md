## Purpose
The "Cost Efficiency Strategy Survey" form helps business owners evaluate their operational costs and gather feedback on efficiency strategies. It is intended for businesses to identify areas where they can improve their cost efficiency and implement changes to reduce waste and increase productivity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct responses to the first question "Business Surveys" (Page 1). You will be asked whether the business has a formal business survey process in place.
2. Read and provide feedback on the purpose statement on Page 2.
3. Enter a numeric value for the "Operational Costs" field (Page 3) if you have relevant data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Surveys** (`<business_surveys_form>`, Select Multiple, required/optional): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Yes: The business has a formal business survey process in place.
	+ No: The business does not have a formal business survey process.
* **The Cost Efficiency Strategy Survey helps businesses evaluate operational costs and gather feedback on efficiency strategies making data collection easy with Jotform's Form Builder**
	+ This is a description of the purpose statement of the form. Please read and provide feedback on its clarity and relevance.
* **Operational Costs** (`<operational_costs>`, Number, required/optional): Enter the numeric value of the business's operational costs.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when providing feedback on the purpose statement.
* Ensure that you are answering the question about whether the business has a formal business survey process.
* Enter only numeric values for the "Operational Costs" field.
