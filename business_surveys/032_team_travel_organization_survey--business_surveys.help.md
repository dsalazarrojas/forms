# Team Travel Organization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about team travel, including details about the team, travel dates, travel types, travel purposes, team leaders, travel locations, and other relevant information. This information will be used to facilitate organization and coordination of team travel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the team's name in the "Team Name" field.
2. Enter the manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
3. Enter the traveler's details in the "Traveler Details" field.
4. Select the travel dates in the "Travel Dates" field.
5. Select the departure date in the "Departure Date" field.
6. Choose the travel type in the "Travel Type" field.
7. Enter the travel purpose in the "Travel Purpose" field.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" for whether the travel is led by the team leader in the "Team Leaders" field.
9. Enter the travel location in the "Travel Location" field.
10. Enter the travel length in the "Travel Length" field.
11. Enter the travel cost in the "Travel Cost" field.
12. Enter the emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the team.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the team's manager.
* **Traveler Details** (`travel_details`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the traveler.
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, date, required: false): Select the travel dates.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, required: false): Select the departure date.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, text, required: false): Choose the type of travel.
* **Travel Purpose** (`travel_purpose`, text, required: false): Enter the purpose of the travel.
* **Team Leaders** (`team_leaders`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the travel is led by the team leader.
* **Travel Location** (`travel_location`, text, required: false): Enter the travel location.
* **Travel Length** (`travel_length`, number, required: false): Enter the length of travel in units of time (e.g., days, hours).
* **Travel Cost** (`travel_cost`, number, required: false): Enter the cost of the travel.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your team leader or manager for clarification.
* This form is for internal use only, please do not share any information with external parties.
