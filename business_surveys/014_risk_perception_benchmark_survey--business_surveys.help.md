# Risk Perception Benchmark Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help you, as an employee of the organization, understand how you perceive the potential risks that the organization may face. Your responses will contribute to a better understanding of the organization's overall risk perception.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out this form in a way that reflects your honest and genuine perception of the risks that the organization may face.
- Be as detailed as possible while answering the questions.
- Make sure to answer all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief introduction or explanation of your understanding of the risks that the organization may face.
* **Risk Perception** (`risk_perception`, `number`, required: false): This field is to help us understand how you rate the overall risk perception. Rate it on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is "Low" and 10 is "High".
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select all the risk factors that you perceive as potential risks to the organization. You can select multiple factors.
	+ Financial
	+ Market
	+ Regulatory
	+ Operational
	+ Cyber Security
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the level of risk you perceive for each selected risk factor. You can choose from "Low", "Medium", and "High".
* **Impact Frequency** (`impact_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Indicate how frequently you perceive the impact of each risk factor. You can select multiple frequencies.
	+ Never
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Annually
	+ More frequently
* **Risk Factors Frequency** (`risk_factors_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select how frequently you perceive each risk factor to occur.
* **Impact Level** (`impact_level`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose the impact level you perceive for each risk factor. You can choose from "Low", "Medium", and "High".
* **Risk Consequence** (`risk_consequence`, `text`, required: true): Please describe the potential consequence of each risk factor, if any.
* **Risk Mitigation** (`risk_mitigation`, `text`, required: true): Describe any existing mitigating measures to minimize or manage the risks selected.
* **Risk Prevention** (`risk_prevention`, `text`, required: true): Describe the prevention measures you suggest to minimize or prevent the identified risk factors.

By following this guide, you'll be able to submit your risk perception data accurately and thoroughly.
