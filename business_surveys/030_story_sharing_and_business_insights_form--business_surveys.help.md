# Story Sharing And Business Insights Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Story Sharing And Business Insights Form is designed to collect feedback and insights from business users on story sharing and its impact on business growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields and submit the form at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **business_surveys** (`business_surveys`, select multiple, required: false): This field asks you to indicate whether or not you have participated in business surveys. Please select all that apply.
* **story_sharing** (`story_sharing`, text, required: false): Describe your story sharing experiences, including any insights you've gained from sharing your story.
* **growth** (`growth`, select one, required: false): Indicate whether or not you believe that sharing your story has contributed to business growth. Please select only one option.
* **growth_through** (`growth_through`, select one, required: false): Indicate whether or not you believe that sharing your story has led to growth through effective data collection. Please select only one option.
* **effective_data_collection** (`effective_data_collection`, select one, required: false): Indicate whether or not you believe that your story sharing has contributed to effective data collection. Please select only one option.
* **enhance_engagement** (`enhance_engagement`, select one, required: false): Indicate whether or not you believe that sharing your story has enhanced engagement. Please select only one option.
* **drive_growth** (`drive_growth`, select one, required: false): Indicate whether or not you believe that sharing your story has driven business growth. Please select only one option.
* **business_insights** (`business_insights`, text, required: false): Share any additional insights you have on the impact of story sharing on business growth.
