## Academic Publishing Barriers Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to identify the main barriers that researchers experience when publishing their work. The data collected will be used to inform policy and operational changes that can help to reduce these barriers and improve the research publication process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Start by selecting your role in the research lifecycle and primary field of study.
2. Choose up to three main barriers you experience when publishing your work.
3. Describe the specific impacts or missed opportunities that these barriers have had on your career.
4. Suggest practical solutions to reduce these barriers.
5. Select the types of support that would help you overcome these barriers.
6. Indicate your willingness to participate in follow-up discussions.
7. If you are willing to be contacted, provide your email address for follow-up purposes.
8. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Role in the research lifecycle** (`role`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best describes your role in the research lifecycle. For example, Early career researcher, Senior researcher, Librarian, Editor, or Administrator.
* **Academic discipline** (`discipline`, `text`, required): Describe your primary field of study.
* **Main barriers experienced when publishing** (`main_barriers`, `select_multiple`, required): Select up to three barriers that you experience when publishing your work. For example, High publication costs, Long review times, Access to reviewers, Language barriers, or Lack of institutional support.
* **How have these barriers affected your career** (`impact_of_barriers`, `text`, required): Describe specific impacts or missed opportunities that these barriers have had on your career. For example, "I had to take a longer time to publication" or "I missed opportunities to collaborate with colleagues".
* **Suggested solutions to reduce barriers** (`solutions_suggested`, `text`, required): Provide practical policy or operational recommendations that could help reduce these barriers.
* **Support needed from institutions or funders** (`support_needed`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the types of support that would help you overcome these barriers. For example, Funding for APCs, Reviewer training, Editorial support, or Language editing.
* **Willingness to join follow-up discussions** (`willingness_to_participate`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you are open to further engagement on this topic.
* **Contact email for follow-up** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): If you are willing to be contacted, provide your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required): Share any additional comments or feedback you have about the publishing process.
