To create a user-facing help guide for the "Financial Statement Clarity Survey" form, we need to break down the form's purpose, completion instructions, and field explanations in a clear and concise manner.

**Purpose**

The Financial Statement Clarity Survey form is designed to gather feedback from users about the clarity of financial statements. This form is used to help organizations understand how well their financial information is presented, allowing them to make improvements and provide clearer and more informative financial reports.

**How To Complete This Form**

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Company Finance Team" field and select the name of the company's finance team member responsible for preparing the financial statement.
2. For the "Rating of Clarity" field, choose a rating that best describes your perception of the financial statement's clarity. You can select from "Low", "Medium", or "High".
3. In the "Comments on Clarity" field, you can provide any additional comments or feedback about what makes the financial statement unclear or what could be improved.
4. For the "Rating of Reporting" field, choose a rating that best describes your perception of the reporting quality of the financial statement. You can select from "Low", "Medium", or "High".
5. In the "Comments on Reporting" field, you can provide any additional comments or feedback about what makes the financial statement's reporting quality unclear or what could be improved.
6. Finally, choose an overall rating for the "Overall Clarity Rating" field, selecting from "Low", "Medium", or "High".

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **Company Finance Team**: This field allows you to select the name of the company's finance team member responsible for preparing the financial statement.
* **Rating of Clarity**: This field asks you to rate the clarity of the financial statement. Select a rating that best describes your perception of how clear the statement is. A lower rating means the statement is not clear, while a higher rating means it is clear.
* **Comments on Clarity**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about what makes the financial statement unclear or what could be improved.
* **Rating of Reporting**: This field asks you to rate the reporting quality of the financial statement. Select a rating that best describes your perception of how good or bad the reporting is.
* **Comments on Reporting**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about what makes the financial statement's reporting quality unclear or what could be improved.
* **Overall Clarity Rating**: Choose an overall rating for the financial statement's clarity, selecting from "Low", "Medium", or "High". This rating should reflect your overall impression of the statement's clarity.
