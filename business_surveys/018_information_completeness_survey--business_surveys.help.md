# information_completeness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the quality and completeness of information provided by an individual or organization. It evaluates various aspects of the information, including its clarity, thoroughness, organization, quality, and purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer each question based on your evaluation of the information provided.
* Select the corresponding ratings for each aspect of the information.
* You may provide more feedback in the "Additional Feedback" section, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the overall purpose of the information provided?** (`page_9`, select_multiple, required): This question assesses the overall purpose of the information. Choose one of the options: "Good Purpose", "Somewhat Good Purpose", or "Not Good Purpose".
* **How would you rate the overall quality of the information?** (`page_6`, select_multiple, required): This question asks for your evaluation of the information's quality. Select one of the options: "Good Quality", "Somewhat Good Quality", or "Not Good Quality".
* **How would you rate the thoroughness of the information?** (`page_4`, select_one, required): This question assesses how thorough the information is. Choose one of the options: "Comprehensive", "Somewhat Comprehensive", or "Not Comprehensive".
* **How would you rate the organization of the information?** (`page_5`, select_multiple, required): This question asks for your evaluation of the information's organization. Select one or more of the options: "Well Organized", "Somewhat Organized", or "Not Well Organized".
* **How would you rate the clarity of the information?** (`page_2`, select_multiple, required): This question asks for your evaluation of the information's clarity. Select one or more of the options: "Clear", "Somewhat Clear", or "Not Clear".
* **Is the information up-to-date?** (`page_8`, select_multiple, required): This question assesses whether the information is up-to-date. Choose one of the options: "Yes", "No", or "Not Sure".
* **Would you like to provide more feedback on this information?** (`page_7`, note, optional): This is an optional section for providing additional feedback on the information.
* **How would you rate the overall purpose of the information provided?** (`page_9`, select_multiple, required): This question is a duplicate of the first question and should be skipped if already answered.
* **Additional Feedback** (`page_6`, note, optional): This section is for providing any additional feedback or comments about the information.

Note: This form has some redundant questions, but they are kept as they are in the original YAML. If you want to remove or modify any of them, please let me know.
