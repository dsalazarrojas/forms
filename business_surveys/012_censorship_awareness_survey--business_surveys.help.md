# Censorship Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Censorship Awareness Survey is a tool for gathering data on individual and organizational knowledge, experiences, and concerns regarding censorship. Your responses will help us better understand the current state of awareness and understanding of censorship.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please select all that apply.
- Fill out each page of the survey, one field at a time.
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Age Group

* Age Group (Required): Select one of the age categories to identify your age group.

### Profession

* Profession (Required): Select the category that best describes your work or profession.

### Awareness of Information Rights

* Awareness of Information Rights (Very High, High, Moderate, Low, Very Low) (Required): Indicate your level of understanding of information rights.

### Forms of Censorship

* Forms of Censorship (Required, multiple selection): Select all forms you are most familiar with.

### Have You Witnessed or Experienced Censorship

* Have You Witnessed or Experienced Censorship (Required) (True, False, Unsure): Indicate your experience with censorship.

### Describe the Incident

* Describe the Incident: Provide a brief description if you answered yes to the previous question.

### Concern Levels

* Concern in Your Field (Required): Select your level of concern about censorship in your profession.

### Organizational Policy

* Organizational Policy on Freedom of Information (Required) (True, False, Unsure): Indicate if your organization has a clear policy on freedom of information.

### Recommendations and Education

* Tools for Awareness (Optional, multiple selection): Select all tools that would increase your understanding of censorship issues.

### Optional Contact

* Email Address (Optional): Provide an email address for survey results and updates.
