# Internal Process Friction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to understand the internal processes that cause friction, identify pain points, and determine the implementation status of suggested solutions. Your honest and thorough responses will help us pinpoint areas for improvement and develop strategies to enhance our internal processes.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as you need to describe your experience.
2.  For fields with multiple options, select the one that best represents your situation.
3.  If a field does not apply to you, mark it as "Not applicable" or leave it blank.
4.  Please provide comments or descriptions where necessary to support your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Workflow bottlenecks** (`workflow_bottlenecks`, text, required): Describe any bottlenecks you experience within your workflow. A bottleneck is a point in the process where work is significantly delayed or hindered.
*   **Pain points** (`pain_points`, select_multiple, required): Select all the pain points you experience:
    *   Painful tasks
    *   Delays
    *   Inaccurate information
    *   Communication failures
    *   Other (please specify)
*   **How often do bottlenecks happen** (`workflow_bottlenecks_frequency`, number, required): Indicate how frequently you encounter bottlenecks.
*   **Frequency of impact** (`impact_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose the frequency of the impact of bottlenecks on your work:
    *   Never
    *   Occasionally
    *   Often
    *   Always
*   **How much impact do bottlenecks have** (`workflow_bottlenecks_impact`, select_one, required): Select the level of impact bottlenecks have on your work:
    *   Low
    *   Medium
    *   High
    *   Very high
*   **Describe the most painful point** (`pain_point_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the most painful point in your workflow.
*   **Suggested solution** (`suggested_solution`, text, required): Offer any suggestions you have for resolving the bottlenecks you've identified.
*   **Implementation status** (`implementation_status`, select_one, required): Indicate the current implementation status of suggested solutions:
    *   Not started
    *   In progress
    *   Done
    *   Not applicable
*   **Barriers to implementation** (`implementation_barriers`, text, required): Describe any obstacles that hinder the implementation of suggested solutions.
*   **Comments about implementation** (`implementation_comments`, text, required): Provide any additional comments about the implementation status.
*   **Team size** (`team_size`, text, required): Describe your team's size.
*   **Team size impact** (`team_size_impact`, text, required): Explain how team size affects your workflow.
*   **Process complexity** (`process_complexity`, text, required): Describe the complexity of your process.
*   **Process complexity impact** (`process_complexity_impact`, text, required): Explain how process complexity affects your workflow.
*   **Describe process complexity** (`process_complexity_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the complexity of your process.
*   **Comments about implementation status** (`implementation_status_comments`, text, required): Share any additional comments about the implementation status.
*   **Team lead** (`team_lead`, text, required): Describe your team lead.
*   **Team lead impact** (`team_lead_impact`, text, required): Explain how the team lead affects your workflow.
*   **Describe team lead** (`team_lead_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the team lead's role.
*   **Team member** (`team_member`, text, required): Describe your team member.
*   **Team member impact** (`team_member_impact`, text, required): Explain how the team member affects your workflow.
*   **Describe team member** (`team_member_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the team member's role.
*   **Manager** (`manager`, text, required): Describe your manager.
*   **Manager impact** (`manager_impact`, text, required): Explain how your manager affects your workflow.
*   **Describe manager** (`manager_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the manager's role.

Please note that this guide will be updated based on the form's actual fields.
