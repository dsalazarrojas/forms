# Community Insights Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Insights Survey is designed to gather feedback from community members, business owners, and other stakeholders to improve local services and initiatives. Your participation is crucial in helping us understand the community's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your role within the community by choosing one of the four options in the "Respondent type" field.
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with local services by selecting one of the five options in the "Satisfaction with local services" field.
3. Select up to three top priorities for the community by choosing from the available options in the "Top priorities for the community" field. You can select multiple options.
4. If you are a business owner, please provide information about your business needs in the "Business needs in the community" field.
5. Indicate your preferred communication channels with officials by selecting up to two options in the "How do you prefer to engage with officials" field.
6. Answer "Very likely" or "Somewhat likely" if you are willing to participate in initiatives or "Unlikely" if you do not feel motivated.
7. In the "Suggestions for improvement" field, provide any suggestions you think would benefit the community.
8. If you prefer to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your email address in the "Contact email for follow up" field.
9. Finally, indicate your consent to share anonymized responses in the "Consent to share anonymized responses" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent type** (`respondent_type`, Select One, required): This field determines which type of respondent you are within the community. Please choose one of the four available options:
	+ Resident: You are a member of the community.
	+ Business owner: You own a business within the community.
	+ Community leader: You are a leader within the community or have a leadership role.
	+ Other: This category is for any other type of community stakeholder.
* **Satisfaction with local services** (`satisfaction_with_services`, Select One, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with local services:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Top priorities for the community** (`top_priorities`, Select Multiple, required for some respondents): Select up to three top priorities for the community:
	+ Economic development
	+ Public safety
	+ Parks and recreation
	+ Transportation
	+ Education
* **Business needs in the community** (`business_needs`, Text, optional): If you are a business owner, please provide information about your business needs in the community.
* **How do you prefer to engage with officials** (`engagement_preference`, Select Multiple, optional): Indicate your preferred communication channels with officials:
	+ Public meetings
	+ Email updates
	+ Social media
	+ Surveys
* **Likelihood to participate in initiatives** (`likelihood_to_participate`, Select One, optional): Indicate your willingness to participate in initiatives:
	+ Very likely
	+ Somewhat likely
	+ Unlikely
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, Text, optional): Provide any suggestions you think would benefit the community.
* **Contact email for follow up** (`contact_for_follow_up`, Email, optional): If you prefer to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your email address.
* **Consent to share anonymized responses** (`consent_to_share`, Select One, required): Agree to allow the use of your anonymized responses for reporting purposes:
	+ True
	+ False
