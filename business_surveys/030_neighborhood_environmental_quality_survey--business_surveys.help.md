# Neighborhood Environmental Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neighborhood Environmental Quality Survey is a tool designed to gather information about the environmental quality of your neighborhood. This survey aims to understand your level of satisfaction and concerns related to various aspects of your neighborhood.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please read the introduction carefully.
2. Fill out the required fields (marked as **required** in the form):
	* Name: A brief description of your name.
	* Zip Code: The zip code of your neighborhood.
	* Questions 1-20: Answer each question with either 'Yes' or 'No' depending on your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief introduction to the survey, explaining its purpose and importance.
* **Name**: A required field to collect your name for reference purposes.
* **Zip Code**: A required field to collect the zip code of your neighborhood for geographical reference.
* **Questions 1-20**:
	+ Questions 1-5: Each question has a 'Yes' or 'No' option. Please answer with the option that best represents your response.
	+ Questions 6-7: These questions require a date and time of your available responses, respectively.
	+ Questions 8-19: These questions also have a 'Yes' or 'No' option. Please answer with the option that best represents your response.
	+ Question 20: This question requires you to select one option.

Please note that some fields are not marked as 'required', but it is highly recommended to fill out all questions to provide a comprehensive response. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
