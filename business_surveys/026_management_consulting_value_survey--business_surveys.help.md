# Management Consulting Value Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather valuable feedback from clients and stakeholders about the management consulting services provided. It helps to evaluate the project's outcomes, consultant's performance, and overall satisfaction, which will aid in the continuous improvement of the service offered.

## How To Complete This Form

To get the most out of this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Answer each field carefully and accurately.
2. Use the options provided for select type fields.
3. If a field is blank, please don't select an option or enter any text.
4. Please use the provided hints for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional):
	+ Enter a brief description of the project you received management consulting services for.
* **Consultant Details** (`consultant_details`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the details about the consultant(s) who worked on the project.
* **Project Value** (`project_value`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the value the project brought to your business.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select one of the options to indicate your overall satisfaction with the project:
		- Very Satisfied
		- Somewhat Satisfied
		- Somewhat Dissatisfied
		- Very Dissatisfied
* **Outcomes** (`outcomes`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the project's outcomes, such as improvements or results.
* **Consultant Notes** (`consultant_notes`, text, optional):
	+ Add any notes about the consultant's performance or interaction.
* **Client Feedback** (`client_feedback`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select one or more of the options to provide feedback about your experience:
		- Very Satisfied
		- Somewhat Satisfied
		- Somewhat Dissatisfied
		- Very Dissatisfied
* **Form Completion** (`form_completion`, text, optional):
	+ Please enter any additional comments or observations about your experience with the form.

## Tips
- Take a few minutes to think about each question carefully before providing your feedback.
- If you have any questions or need clarification about any field, please don't hesitate to contact us.
- Your input is crucial in helping us improve our services.
