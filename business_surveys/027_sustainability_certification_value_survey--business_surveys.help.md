# Sustainability Certification Value Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainability Certification Value Survey is a form designed to gather data on the value of sustainability certifications to business stakeholders. The survey aims to understand the categories that are most important to stakeholders, the preferred form titles and descriptions, and the data collection methods used in forms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best represents the type of certification you are interested in.
2. Choose whether or not you are interested in business surveys.
3. Enter a title for your form, a description of its purpose, and the name of your organization.
4. Provide feedback on the stakeholder experience.
5. Select the types of sustainability certifications that are relevant to your business.
6. Describe your form submission data collection methods.
7. Answer additional questions about your experience with forms.
