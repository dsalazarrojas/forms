# Leadership Communication Flow Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to assess leadership communication habits and challenges within teams.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one option for each question.
2. You will be asked about your communication frequency, effectiveness, communication channels, common barriers, and some general information about your team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How many times per week do you usually communicate with your team?** (`communication_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about the frequency of your interactions with your team.
* **How would you rate the effectiveness of your communication with your team?** (`communication_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your communication effectiveness using the provided options.
* **Which communication channel(s) do you usually use to communicate with your team?** (`communication_channels`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the communication channels you use most often.
* **Which of the following barriers affect your ability to communicate effectively with your team?** (`communication_barriers`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more barriers you face.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant information about who or what you are assigned to.
* **Assigned from** (`assigned_from`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant information about who or what assigned to you.
* **Team members involved** (`team_members`, `text`, required: false): List the members of your team.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
- Select all relevant information for each field.
- If you are unsure about a question, feel free to ask for clarification.
