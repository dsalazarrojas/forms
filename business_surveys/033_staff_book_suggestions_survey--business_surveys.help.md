# Staff Book Suggestions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect book suggestions from staff members. It's meant to gather relevant information about books that staff might find interesting or useful for work or personal development. This form will help us identify popular book titles, authors, and publishers that staff would like to read or explore.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the form.
2. Answer each question on the page by typing in the provided input fields.
3. Click on the "Submit" button to send your responses to us.
4. Fill out all the fields on each page as thoroughly as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1**: This page is for general information about your book suggestions. 
* **Book Title** (`page_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a title of a book you suggest that you think staff might find interesting.
* **Book Author** (`page_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the author of the book you suggest that you think staff might find interesting.
* **Book Publisher** (`page_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the publisher of the book you suggest that you think staff might find interesting.
* **Year of Publication** (`page_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the year the book was published that you think staff might find interesting.
* **Book Description** (`page_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the book you suggest, including why you think staff might find it interesting.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and thorough as possible when providing information about the book you suggest.
* If you have any personal connections or biases towards the book (e.g., you've read it, you own it, etc.), try not to let that influence your suggestions.
* We encourage you to suggest books that are relevant to our work or interests, but also feel free to suggest books that are just generally interesting.
