# Corporate Transformation Pulse Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Transformation Pulse Survey is a tool designed to gauge the current state of your department's effectiveness and your own confidence levels in achieving goals and transformation initiatives. By answering this survey, you will help us understand the pulse of your department and identify areas where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your thoughts and opinions.
2. Use the provided instructions and hints as a guide.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Manager** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your department.
* **Employee Sentiment** (`employee_sentiment`, number, required): Rate your current employee sentiment on a scale of 1-5.
* **Departmental Effectiveness** (`departmental_effectiveness`, number, required): Rate your department's overall effectiveness on a scale of 1-5.
* **Confidence Level** (`confidence_level`, number, required): Rate your confidence level in the company's transformation on a scale of 1-5.
* **Change Initiative** (`change_initiative`, select one, required): Choose the initiative you think is most important for the company's transformation: Transformation, Innovation, or Growth.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your team.
* **Departmental Effectiveness 2** (`departmental_effectiveness_2`, number, required): Rate your team's effectiveness on a scale of 1-5.
* **Confidence Level 2** (`confidence_level_2`, number, required): Rate your confidence level in your team's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Manager Feedback** (`manager_feedback`, note, required): Provide feedback on what you would like to tell your manager.
* **Team Performance** (`team_performance`, number, required): Rate your team's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Departmental Effectiveness 3** (`departmental_effectiveness_3`, number, optional): Rate your department's effectiveness in achieving its goals on a scale of 1-5.
* **Confidence Level 3** (`confidence_level_3`, number, optional): Rate your confidence level in achieving your department's goals on a scale of 1-5.
* **Manager's Manager** (`manager_name_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of your manager's manager.
* **Team Name 2** (`team_name_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of your team's goal.
* **Confidence Level 4** (`confidence_level_4`, number, optional): Rate your confidence level in achieving your team's goal on a scale of 1-5.
* **Departmental Effectiveness 4** (`departmental_effectiveness_4`, number, optional): Rate your department's effectiveness in achieving its goal on a scale of 1-5.
* **Confidence Level 5** (`confidence_level_5`, number, optional): Rate your confidence level in achieving your department's goal on a scale of 1-5.
* **Manager's Manager's Manager** (`manager_name_3`, text, optional): Enter the name of your manager's manager's manager.
* **Team Name 3** (`team_name_3`, text, optional): Enter the name of your team's goal.
* **Confidence Level 6** (`confidence_level_6`, number, optional): Rate your confidence level in achieving your team's goal on a scale of 1-5.
* **Departmental Effectiveness 5** (`departmental_effectiveness_5`, number, optional): Rate your department's effectiveness in achieving its goal on a scale of 1-5.
* **Confidence Level 7** (`confidence_level_7`, number, optional): Rate your confidence level in achieving your department's goal on a scale of 1-5.

Please note that the optional fields are for optional questions, but we appreciate any input you can provide to help us better understand your thoughts and opinions.

## Tips
