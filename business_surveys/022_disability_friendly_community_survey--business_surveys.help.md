# Disability Friendly Community Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disability Friendly Community Survey is a one-time opportunity for community members to share their experiences and suggestions about accessibility in our facilities. Your feedback will help us identify areas of improvement to make our community more inclusive and welcoming to all members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your honest and accurate information.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each field.
3. Check your email address for the "Email" field to ensure it is correct.
4. If you have any suggestions or comments, write them in the "Suggestions" field.
5. Click "Submit" to send your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Community Name** (`community_name`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of the community where you are currently or recently visited.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required: false): If you are visiting a specific organization, please enter its name.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required: false): If you are in a specific facility, please enter its name.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, required: false): If you are in a specific facility, please enter its address.
* **Accessibility Barriers** (`accessibility_barriers`, text, required: false): Describe any accessibility barriers you encountered or suggest ways to improve accessibility.
* **Physical Benefits** (`physical_benefits`, text, required: false): Share any physical benefits you have experienced or suggest ways to improve physical accessibility.
* **Communication Benefits** (`communication_benefits`, text, required: false): Share any communication benefits you have experienced or suggest ways to improve communication accessibility.
* **Emotional Benefits** (`emotional_benefits`, text, required: false): Share any emotional benefits you have experienced or suggest ways to improve emotional accessibility.
* **Transportation Benefits** (`transportation_benefits`, text, required: false): Share any transportation benefits you have experienced or suggest ways to improve transportation accessibility.
* **Accommodations and Support Services** (`accommodations_and_support_services`, text, required: false): Share any accommodations or support services you have used or suggest ways to improve them.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required: false): Write any additional comments, suggestions, or ideas you have about making our community more accessible.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to allow us to get back to you with any follow-up questions or to provide updates on our improvements.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you for any follow-up questions or to provide updates on our improvements.
