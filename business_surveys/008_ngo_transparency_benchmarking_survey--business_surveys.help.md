# NGO Transparency Benchmarking Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The NGO Transparency Benchmarking Survey is a tool designed to gather data on NGOs and their practices in terms of transparency. This form is used to collect information on various aspects of NGO operations, including financial disclosures, transparency practices, and governance structures.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting your organization type:
	* Non-profit: A traditional non-profit organization that operates for the public good.
	* For-profit: A for-profit organization that operates for the benefit of its shareholders.
	* Hybrid: A organization that combines elements of both non-profit and for-profit structures.
	* Government: A government agency or department.
2. Choose the financial disclosures you provide:
	* Select one or more options that apply to your organization, such as Income statement, Balance sheet, or Cash flow statement.
3. Select your transparency practices:
	* Choose one practice that best describes your organization's level of transparency, such as Open financial data, Public data portal, or Annual reports.
4. Select your governance structure:
	* Choose one option that best describes your organization's governance structure, such as Board size and composition, CEO compensation, or Conflict of interest policies.
5. Provide feedback on areas of improvement:
	* Select one or more areas where your organization would like to improve its transparency and governance practices.
6. Leave any additional comments or notes:
	* This is a text field where you can provide any further information or comments you would like to share.
7. Provide your contact information:
	* Enter your email address and phone number for further communication and follow-up questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Type**: Select the type of organization you represent (Non-profit, For-profit, Hybrid, or Government).
* **Financial Disclosures**: Select one or more of the following options: Income statement, Balance sheet, or Cash flow statement.
* **Transparency Practices**: Choose one practice that best describes your organization's level of transparency: Open financial data, Public data portal, or Annual reports.
* **Governance**: Choose one option that best describes your organization's governance structure: Board size and composition, CEO compensation, or Conflict of interest policies.
* **Feedback**: Provide feedback on areas where your organization would like to improve its transparency and governance practices.
* **Notes**: Leave any additional comments or notes you would like to share.
* **Contact**: Enter your email address and phone number for further communication and follow-up questions.
