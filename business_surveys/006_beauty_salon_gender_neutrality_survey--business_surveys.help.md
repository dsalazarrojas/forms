# Beauty Salon Gender Neutrality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from customers on the gender neutrality of our beauty salon. Your input will help us create a more welcoming environment for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the response that best matches your feelings or experiences.
3. If you have any specific feedback or suggestions, please use the "Specific Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Salon Inclusivity Survey --** (`section_intro`, note, optional): This section is a general introduction to the survey, providing context and explaining the importance of your feedback.
* **How do you identify your gender?** (`gender_identity`, select_one, optional): Please select how you identify your gender from the provided options.
* **How comfortable do you feel in our salon's current atmosphere?** (`comfort_level_atmosphere`, number, required): Answer on a scale of 1 (very uncomfortable) to 5 (very comfortable).
* **How important is gender-neutral pricing to you?** (`gender_neutral_pricing`, select_one, required): Please select how important gender-neutral pricing is to you, from Very Important to Not Important.
* **Do you feel our marketing and signage are inclusive?** (`inclusivity_perception`, select_one, required): Please select whether you feel our marketing and signage are inclusive or not.
* **How would you rate the accessibility of our restrooms for all genders?** (`restroom_access`, select_one, optional): Answer on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or N/A.
* **Have you ever felt gender-based bias during a consultation or service?** (`staff_interactions`, select_one, required): Please select how often you have felt gender-based bias during a consultation or service.
* **Does our staff consistently use your preferred pronouns?** (`preferred_pronouns_usage`, select_one, optional): Please select whether our staff consistently use your preferred pronouns or not.
* **Please share any specific experiences or suggestions for improvement** (`specific_feedback`, text, optional): This is an open-ended field for you to share any specific experiences or suggestions for improvement.
* **Would you like this feedback to remain anonymous?** (`anonymous_submission`, select_one, required): Please select whether you would like your feedback to remain anonymous.

Note: Please answer honestly and thoroughly. Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our services.
