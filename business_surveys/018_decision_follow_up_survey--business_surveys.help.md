# decision_follow_up_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for decision follow-up, which is a crucial part of the decision-making process in project management. It helps to track the progress of decisions made and ensures that they are followed up on accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill out the required fields.
2. Select the correct options for each field as per your understanding.
3. Provide accurate information in the "suggestion" field for any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Decision Leader** (`decision_leader`, select_one, optional): Choose whether there is a decision leader for this project or not.
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the prophets that were involved in the decision-making process.
* **Decision Date** (`decision_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the decision was made.
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, number, optional): Estimate the time spent on making the decision.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction level with the decision made.
* **Impact** (`impact`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the decision had a positive or negative impact on the project.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the decision.
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the project manager was involved in the decision-making process.
* **Team Leader** (`team_leader`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the team leader was involved in the decision-making process.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the team members that were involved in the decision-making process.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_one, optional): Choose whether follow-up is required or not.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the follow-up is due.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Estimate the time spent on follow-up activities.
* **Follow-up Location** (`follow_up_location`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the locations where follow-up activities will take place.
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, select_one, optional): Choose the method of follow-up activities.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose the frequency of follow-up activities.
* **Impact on Project** (`decision_impact`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the decision had a positive or negative impact on the project.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the decision.
* **Project ID** (`project_id`, number, optional): Enter the project ID number.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): Enter the form ID number.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, text, optional): Enter the form version number.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the form was created by you or not.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the form was updated by you or not.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, optional): Enter the date when the form was last updated.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be left blank if not applicable.
