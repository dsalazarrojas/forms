# youtube_brand_presence_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The YouTube Brand Presence Survey is a form designed to help businesses assess their presence and effectiveness on YouTube. This form is meant to understand how well businesses have established a presence on YouTube and the effectiveness of their content strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible. You will be asked about various aspects of your YouTube presence, including your brand's familiarity, engagement, content preferences, and content distribution strategies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This field is for a brief description of your business and its YouTube presence.
* **Brand Familiarity**: Please indicate how familiar customers are with your brand on YouTube. Choose one or both of the options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Engagement**: This is a numerical field for measuring the level of engagement of your content with customers.
* **Content Preferences**: This field is for selecting one of the options for how you prefer your content to be distributed on YouTube. Choose one of the options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Channel Strategy**: This field is for a brief description of your channel's strategy on YouTube.
* **Content Creation**: This field is for a brief description of the process of creating content on your channel.
* **Content Distribution**: This field is for a brief description of how you distribute your content on YouTube.
* **Channel Promotion**: This field is for a brief description of how you promote your channel on YouTube.
* **Social Media**: This field is for selecting one or more of the social media channels you use to promote your brand.
* **Content Purpose**: This field is for describing the purpose of your content on YouTube.
* **Content Length**: This is a numerical field for measuring the length of your content.
* **Report Frequency**: This field is for selecting one or both of the options for how frequently you report on your content. Choose one or both of the options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Content Format**: This field is for selecting one of the options for the format of your content on YouTube. Choose one of the options: "Yes" or "No".
* **Target Audience**: This field is for describing the target audience of your content on YouTube.
* **Content Tactics**: This field is for describing the tactics you use to promote your content on YouTube.
* **Content Effectiveness**: This is a numerical field for measuring the effectiveness of your content.
* **Social Media Channels**: This field is for selecting one or more of the social media channels you use to promote your content on YouTube.
* **Content Tactics Cost**: This is a numerical field for measuring the cost of promoting your content on YouTube.

## Tips
* Please answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, please feel free to ask for clarification.
* The information gathered from this form will help us understand your YouTube presence and content strategies.
