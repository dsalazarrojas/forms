# Ethical Storytelling In Nonprofits Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from nonprofit organizations about their experiences and practices related to ethical storytelling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction and ensuring you understand the purpose of the form.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Use the options provided for multiple-choice questions, and feel free to skip any questions that are not relevant to your organization.
4. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **Nonprofit Background**: Please provide a brief description of your nonprofit organization, including its mission, goals, and any relevant information about your organization's background.
* **Storytelling Practices**: Describe the ways in which your organization uses storytelling as a tool for communication and engagement.
* **Challenges Facing**: Indicate which of the following challenges your organization faces when it comes to storytelling: 
  • Yes, our organization faces this challenge
  • No, we do not face this challenge
* **Strategies Implementing**: Select the strategy that best describes how your organization approaches storytelling: 
  • Good: Our organization uses a formal, well-planned approach to storytelling.
  • Fair: Our organization uses a moderate approach to storytelling.
* **Impact Measuring**: Describe how your organization measures the impact of its storytelling efforts.
* **Next Steps**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions for how the form could be improved.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you with any additional questions or information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for any follow-up questions.
* **Comments**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback about the form.
* **Form Submission**: This field is automatically populated with the date and time of submission.
* **Form Submission Time**: This field is automatically populated with the time of submission.
* **Form Submission IP**: This field is automatically populated with the IP address of the submission.
* **Form Submission User Agent**: This field is automatically populated with the user agent of the submission.
* **Form Submission Postal Code**: This field is automatically populated with the postal code of the submission.
* **Form Submission User**: This field is automatically populated with the user's name and email address.
* **Form Submission Referrer**: This field is automatically populated with the referrer URL.
* **Form Submission Browser**: This field is automatically populated with the browser type and version.
* **Form Submission OS**: This field is automatically populated with the operating system type and version.
