# pmi_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on Project Management Initiatives (PMI) from project participants, helping us improve our project management practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter the PMI project ID number to associate your feedback with the specific project.
2. Enter the name of the project manager who oversaw the project.
3. Enter the ID of the PMI initiative being evaluated.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the project outcome on a scale from 1 to 10.
5. Provide feedback about your experience with the project.
6. Rate your satisfaction with the PMI initiative on a scale from 1 to 10.
7. Describe the outcome of the project.
8. Enter your name and email address (if you'd like us to contact you for further feedback).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pm_i** (`pmi_project_id`, text, required): Enter the PMI project ID number associated with this feedback.
* **project_manager** (`project_manager_id`, text, required): Enter the name of the project manager who oversaw this project.
* **pmi_initiative** (`pmi_initiative_id`, text, required): Enter the ID of the PMI initiative being evaluated.
* **rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the project outcome on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your feedback about your experience with the project.
* **pmi_satisfaction** (`pmi_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the PMI initiative on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **project_outcome** (`project_outcome`, text, optional): Describe the outcome of the project.
* **participant_name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name so we can contact you for further feedback.
* **participant_email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further feedback.

## Tips
Please make sure to complete all required fields (project iD, project manager, and PMI initiative). This will help us accurately associate your feedback with the correct project and PMI.
