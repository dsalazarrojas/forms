# Latin America Historical Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Latin America Historical Survey form is designed to gather information from users about their interests, knowledge, and preferences related to the history of Latin America. This form is intended for educators, researchers, or students who wish to contribute their expertise and experiences in this area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each page and select the relevant options for each question.
2. Ensure that you are completing the form for the right purpose, i.e., to contribute to the research on Latin America history.
3. Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **educator_interests** (`educator_interests`, text, optional): Please select your interests related to Latin America history as an educator. This could include topics like teaching methods, course development, or educational resources.

- **interests_level** (`interests_level`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your level of interest in different Latin American countries. You can select more than one option, such as Brazil, Mexico, or both.

- **history_knowledge_level** (`history_knowledge_level`, select_one, required): Rate your level of knowledge in Latin America history. You can choose High, Medium, or Low, depending on your confidence in your knowledge.

- **knowledge_areas** (`knowledge_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select the areas of Latin America history you are knowledgeable about. You can choose from Latin American culture, Latin American history, or Latin American politics.

- **knowledge_level_1**, **knowledge_level_2**, **knowledge_level_3** (`knowledge_level_1`, `knowledge_level_2`, `knowledge_level_3`, number, optional): These fields are intended for users to rate their level of knowledge in specific areas, but they are not currently populated with options. If you would like to provide additional feedback on these areas, please do so.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Ensure that you are completing the form for the right purpose, i.e., to contribute to the research on Latin America history.
* If you have any difficulty or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
