# Expense Approval Workflow Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Approval Workflow Survey is designed to gather information and feedback from employees on the current expense approval workflow in the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure that you have completed every page, as some pages may be required.
3. If you're unsure about any question or field, contact the form administrator for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Page
* **First Page** (`first_page`, text, false): This is the first page of the form, which is optional. You can enter your thoughts on the current expense approval workflow here.

### Second Page
* **Second Page** (`second_page`, note, false): This page is also optional and allows you to provide additional comments or information about the current expense approval workflow.

### Third Page
* **Option 1** (`third_page`, select_multiple, false): Choose up to 3 options from the list:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### Fourth Page
* **Option** (`fourth_page`, number, false): Enter a number between 1 and 5 to rate your satisfaction with a specific aspect of the expense approval workflow.

### Fifth Page
* **Option** (`fifth_page`, time, false): Select a time that represents how often you perform a specific task in the expense approval workflow.

### Sixth Page
* **Option** (`sixth_page`, number, false): Enter a number that represents the average time spent on a specific task in the expense approval workflow.

### Seventh Page
* **Option 1/Option 2** (`seventh_page`, select_one, false): Select one of the two options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2

### Eighth Page
* **E-mail** (`eighth_page`, email, false): Enter your company email address.

### Ninth Page
* **Additional Information** (`ninth_page`, text, false): Enter any additional comments or information about the expense approval workflow.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you complete every page of the form, as some pages may be required.
* If you have any feedback or questions, contact the form administrator for assistance.
