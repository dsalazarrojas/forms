# Process Automation Insight Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Process Automation Insight Survey" is designed to gather information about your workflows, automation tools, and survey participation preferences. Your responses will help us better understand your needs and improve our process automation capabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the instructions carefully before starting the survey.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately, taking into account your current workflow experiences.
3. Be honest and transparent in your responses.
4. Do not duplicate responses or answer on behalf of others without their permission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the primary goal of your workflow?** (`workflow_goal`, text, required) - Describe the main objective of your workflow.
* **How many workflows do you have?** (`number_of_workflows`, number, required) - Provide the number of workflows you currently manage.
* **Which tools are you currently using for your workflows?** (`tools_used`, select_multiple, required) - List the tools used for your workflows (e.g., Zapier, Automata, Other).
* **What is the primary benefit of your automated workflows?** (`benefit_of_automation`, select_one, required) - Choose the main advantage of your automated workflows (e.g., Increased efficiency, Improved accuracy, Other).
* **On what schedule is this survey expected to be completed?** (`survey_schedule`, select_one, required) - Select the expected completion time (e.g., ASAP, Within a few days, Within a week).
* **What would be the best time to conduct this survey according to your perspective?** (`survey_time`, select_one, required) - Choose the best time for the survey (e.g., Now, Later today, Tomorrow).
* **Have you conducted similar surveys in the past?** (`similar_surveys`, select_multiple, required) - Answer yes or no to indicate if you've conducted similar surveys.
* **What is the URL of the survey you have conducted most recently?** (`recent_survey_url`, text, required) - Provide the URL of the most recent survey you've completed.
* **Do you want to participate in future surveys?** (`participate_in_future`, select_multiple, required) - Answer yes or no to indicate your willingness to participate in future surveys.
* **Do you have any comments or suggestions?** (`comments`, text, optional) - Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **What is your contact email address?** (`contact`, email, required) - Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Follow-up question 1** (`follow_up_question_1`, select_multiple, optional) - Answer according to your understanding (Note: This question is optional and not required).
* **What is your contact information?** (`contact_answer`, text, optional) - Provide any additional contact information you may have.
