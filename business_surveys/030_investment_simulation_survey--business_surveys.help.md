# Investment Simulation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Simulation Survey is a form designed to collect information about your investment preferences and goals. It will help you simulate investment scenarios and provide personalized results.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Introduction**: This is the first page of the form where you can briefly describe your investment background and goals.
2. **Simulation Type**: Please select one of the following options for the type of investment you are interested in:
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3
	* Option 4
3. **Investment Amount**: Enter the amount you are willing to invest.
4. **Investment Frequency**: Please select how frequently you are willing to invest:
	* Daily
	* Weekly
	* Monthly
	* Quarterly
5. **Risk Tolerance**: Enter a number between 0 and 100 to indicate how much risk you are willing to take on.
6. **Investment Horizon**: Please select one of the following options for the length of your investment horizon:
	* Short-term
	* Medium-term
	* Long-term
7. **Investment Goal**: This is an optional field where you can describe your investment goals.
8. **Investment Sector**: If you have multiple investment goals, please select all the sectors that apply (e.g. Financial services, Real estate, Technology, Other):
	* Financial services
	* Real estate
	* Technology
	* Other
9. **Investment Size**: Enter the size of your investment.
10. **Confidence Level**: Enter a number between 0 and 100 to indicate how confident you are in your investment decision.
11. **Investment Return**: Please select one of the following options for the expected return on your investment:
	* Low
	* High
	* Medium

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **simulation_type** (required): This field asks you to select the type of investment you are interested in.
* **investment_amount** (required): This field asks you to enter the amount you are willing to invest.
* **investment_frequency** (required): This field asks you to select how frequently you are willing to invest.
* **risk_tolerance** (required): This field asks you to enter a number between 0 and 100 to indicate how much risk you are willing to take on.
* **investment_horizon** (required): This field asks you to select the length of your investment horizon.
* **investment_goal** (optional): This is an optional field where you can describe your investment goals.
* **investment_sector** (optional): This field asks you to select all the sectors that apply to your investment goals.
* **investment_size** (required): This field asks you to enter the size of your investment.
* **confidence_level** (required): This field asks you to enter a number between 0 and 100 to indicate how confident you are in your investment decision.
* **investment_return** (required): This field asks you to select the expected return on your investment.

## Tips
* Please answer these questions truthfully and accurately to get the most relevant results.
* You can skip any optional fields if they do not apply to your situation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
