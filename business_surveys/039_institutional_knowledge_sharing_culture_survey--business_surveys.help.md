**Institutional Knowledge Sharing Culture Survey - Help Guide**

## Purpose

This survey is designed to understand the current state of knowledge sharing and collaboration within your organization. Your honest feedback is crucial in helping management identify areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question based on your honest assessment of your work experience within the organization.
2. Select the options that best match your perception of the situation.
3. Be as accurate as possible, but don't worry if you're unsure.
