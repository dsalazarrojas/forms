# Managed Services Billing Software Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluation from customers about their experience with the managed services billing software.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Please take a few minutes to complete this form**, as your input will help us improve the managed services billing software.
- **Read each field carefully** before entering your response.
- **Fill in the required fields** with as much detail as possible.
- **Save your progress** if you don't finish the form in one sitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`managed_services_billing_software_evaluation_survey_first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name, which will help us personalize our communication with you.
* **Last Name** (`managed_services_billing_software_evaluation_survey_last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name, which will help us personalize our communication with you.
* **Email** (`managed_services_billing_software_evaluation_survey_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your business email address to ensure we contact you about your feedback.
* **Billing Software** (`managed_services_billing_software_evaluation_survey_billing_software`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the managed services billing software you are using. Please select from the options below:
	+ Billing Software 1
	+ Billing Software 2
	+ Billing Software 3
* **Prioritize Improvements** (`managed_services_billing_software_evaluation_survey_prioritize_improvements`, `number`, required: false): Enter the top 3 improvements you would like to see in the managed services billing software. Use numbers 1, 2, or 3 to prioritize your suggestions.
* **Satisfaction** (`managed_services_billing_software_evaluation_survey_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Tell us about your overall satisfaction with the managed services billing software. Please select from the options below:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Slightly Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
	+ Not At All Satisfied
* **Comments** (`managed_services_billing_software_evaluation_survey_comments`, `note`, required: false): Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about the managed services billing software.
* **Phone** (`managed_services_billing_software_evaluation_survey_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number to allow us to follow up on your feedback.

## Tips
- Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible about your experience with the managed services billing software.
- If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please share them with us.
- If you have any feedback or comments about our communication, please let us know.
