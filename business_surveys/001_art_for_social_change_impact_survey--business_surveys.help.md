# Art For Social Change Impact Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Art For Social Change Impact Survey is a questionnaire designed to gather information about arts projects with social impact. This form is intended for arts organizations or individuals implementing projects that aim to bring about positive social change.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Art For Social Change Impact Survey, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, email, and the name of your organization or group in the corresponding fields.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as possible to help us better understand your project's impact.
3. Select the relevant options for your project from the dropdown menus.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the space provided at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as the person filling out this form.
* **Organization or Group Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization or group.
* **Art Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): Provide a brief title of your art project.
* **Project Duration - Months** (`project_duration`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the length of time your project has been running.
* **Primary Audience Reached** (`primary_audience`, select_one, required): Select the primary audience your project is designed to impact. Options: Community Members, Students, Underserved Populations, Mixed Audience, Other.
* **Areas of Social Impact** (`social_impact_areas`, select_multiple, required): Select all the areas where your project has had a significant impact. Options: Economic Empowerment, Education, Health and Wellness, Community Engagement, Social Justice, Environmental Awareness, Cultural Preservation.
* **Estimated Number of Participants Reached** (`participants_reached`, number, optional): If applicable, enter an estimate of the number of people your project has affected.
* **Primary Funding Sources** (`funding_sources`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the primary sources of funding for your project. Options: Grants, Donations, Earned Revenue, Government Funding, Corporate Sponsorship, Personal Investment.
* **Rate the Overall Social Impact - Scale 1-10** (`impact_rating`, number, required): Assess the overall impact of your project on a scale of 1-10, with 1 indicating minimal impact and 10 indicating significant impact.
* **Measurable Outcomes Achieved** (`measurable_outcomes`, text, optional): Describe any measurable outcomes or results of your project.
* **Key Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any key challenges you faced while implementing your project.
* **Future Plans for the Project** (`future_plans`, text, optional): Share any future plans or intentions for your project.
* **Open to Collaboration Opportunities** (`collaboration_opportunities`, select_one, optional): Indicate your willingness to collaborate with others on future projects. Options: Yes, Very Interested, Possibly, Not at This Time.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication and updates (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Use this space to provide any additional comments or feedback about your project.
