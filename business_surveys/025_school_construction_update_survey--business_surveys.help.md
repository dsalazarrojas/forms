# School Construction Update Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a school construction project, including its name, address, and update frequency. It helps school staff and administrators track the progress and status of the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the construction project in the "construction_project_name" field.
2. Enter the address of the construction project in the "construction_project_address" field.
3. Select the update frequency for the project from the options provided in the "update_frequency" field.
4. If the update frequency is not listed in the options, enter the custom update frequency in the "update_frequency_other" field.
5. Enter any additional comments about the project in the "comments" field.
6. Provide any submission comments about the form in the "submission_comments" fields.
7. Select the stage of the project from the options provided in the "project_stage" field.
8. If the project stage is not listed in the options, enter the custom stage in the "submission_comments_other" field.
9. Enter the school ID in the "school_id" field.
10. Enter the contact email in the "contact_email" field.
11. Enter the contact phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
12. Enter the contact title in the "contact_title" field.
13. Enter the contact name in the "contact_name" field.
14. Enter the contact position in the "contact_position" field.
15. Enter the contact department in the "contact_department" field.
16. Enter the school name in the "school_name" field.
17. Enter the school address in the "school_address" field.
18. Enter the school city in the "school_city" field.
19. Enter the school state in the "school_state" field.
20. Enter the school zip code in the "school_zip" field.
