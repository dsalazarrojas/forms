# Drone Interference Impact Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us gather information about incidents where drones may have interfered with your business operations. Please complete this form to describe the incident, its impact on your business, and provide any additional information we need to follow up.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out each field as instructed and submit the form when you are finished. If you need help with any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of incident?**: Please describe the type of incident that occurred, including any relevant details such as date, time, and location. This will help us understand the context of the incident.
* **Drone Type**: Choose the type of drone that was involved in the incident from the list of options. This will help us determine the potential causes and effects of the incident.
* **Date of Incident**: Enter the date the incident occurred. If you are not sure of the exact date, you can estimate the closest date it happened.
* **Time of Incident**: Enter the time of day the incident occurred. If you are not sure of the exact time, you can estimate the closest hour it happened.
* **Impact on Business**: Select all the ways the incident affected your business. Please be honest and detailed in your response, as this will help us understand the full scope of the impact.
* **Description of Incident**: Please provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details such as the location, duration, and any other relevant circumstances.
* **Contact Person**: Please select the contact person responsible for the incident. This will help us follow up with them directly.
* **Email**: Enter your company email address so we can contact you with any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your company phone number so we can contact you with any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Company Website**: If you have a website, please enter it so we can look up more information about your company.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or information about the incident, please add them here.
* **Comments**: Please indicate whether you would like us to follow up with you about the incident.
* **Follow-up Information**: Indicate if you would like us to follow up with you regarding any next steps or further actions.
* **Next Steps**: If you have any suggestions or next steps you think we should take regarding the incident, please add them here.
* **Submitter Information**: You can add any information about yourself, such as name, title, and department.
* **Created At**: This field is automatically generated and shows when the form was submitted.
* **Updated At**: This field is automatically generated and shows when the form was last updated.
* **Comment**: Please add any further comments or questions you have about the incident.
* **Attachments**: If you have any attachments or files related to the incident, please select this option.
* **Comment**: This field is a repeat of the previous one, please add any further comments or questions you have about the incident.
