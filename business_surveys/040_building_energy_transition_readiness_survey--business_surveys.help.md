# Building Energy Transition Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Building Energy Transition Readiness Survey is designed to assess the readiness of buildings to transition to energy-efficient practices and technologies. This survey will help organizations understand their readiness to transition and identify potential areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information, including your name, job title, organization, email, and phone number.
2. Provide information about the building, including its name, address, type, size, and year built.
3. Answer questions about your role in energy management, the building's energy sources and usage patterns, and your organization's sustainability goals.
4. Select all relevant options for questions about existing energy efficiency measures, barriers to transition, and priority projects.
5. Answer questions about your organization's budget availability, incentive awareness, and timeline for action.
6. Select any support needed to aid in the transition process.
7. Self-assess your organization's overall readiness to transition to energy-efficient practices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your full name to confirm your identity.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position within your organization.
* **Organization**: Enter your organization's name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Survey Date**: Enter the date of the survey.
* **Building Name**: Enter the name of the building.
* **Building Address**: Enter the full address of the building.
* **Building Type**: Select the type of building (e.g. office, residential, retail, etc.).
* **Building Size**: Enter the size of the building in square footage.
* **Year Built**: Enter the year the building was constructed.
* **Your Role in Energy Management**: Select your role in energy management (e.g. primary decision-maker, influencer, etc.).
* **Current Energy Sources**: Select all energy sources currently used by the building (e.g. grid electricity, natural gas, etc.).
* **Annual Energy Spend**: Select the approximate annual energy spend of the building.
* **Sustainability Goals**: Select whether your organization has set sustainability goals.
* **Target Year**: Enter the year by which your organization aims to achieve its sustainability goals (if applicable).
* **Energy Audit Status**: Select whether you have had an energy audit recently (e.g. within the past year, 1-3 years ago, etc.).
* **Existing Efficiency Measures**: Select all energy efficiency measures already implemented in the building.
* **Electrification Status**: Select the current state of electrification in the building (e.g. fully electrified, mostly electrified, etc.).
* **Barriers to Transition**: Select all barriers to energy transition that apply to your organization.
* **Budget Availability**: Select the availability of budget for energy projects.
* **Incentive Awareness**: Select your level of awareness of energy incentives.
* **Priority Projects**: Select priority projects for energy-efficient upgrades and improvements.
* **Timeline for Action**: Select when your organization plans to take action on energy-efficient projects.
* **Support Needed**: Select any support needed for the transition process (e.g. energy audit, financial analysis, etc.).
* **Overall Readiness**: Select your self-assessment of your organization's overall readiness to transition to energy-efficient practices.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure of an answer, select the option that best reflects your current situation.
* You can ask for clarification or assistance if you are unsure about any question or answer.
* This survey is intended for internal use by organizations to assess their readiness to transition to energy-efficient practices.
