# Internal Communications Transparency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from employees on the internal communications within the organization, including the quality, frequency, and style of communication. The collected data will help management identify areas of improvement and provide insights to enhance employee engagement and satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your answers for each section from the options provided.
2.  For questions with a rating scale (number options), choose a number that best represents your opinion.
3.  Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Business Surveys** (Yes, No): This section asks if you receive regular surveys from the company on business-related topics.
    *   Select "Yes" if you receive regular surveys.
    *   Select "No" if you do not receive regular surveys.
*   **Employee Surveys** (Employee, Business): This section asks about the type of surveys you receive from the company.
    *   Select "Employee" if the surveys focus on employee-related topics.
    *   Select "Business" if the surveys focus on business-related topics.
*   **Survey Effectiveness** (Rating Scale): Rate the effectiveness of our internal surveys based on your experience.
    *   A higher rating indicates that you find the surveys effective.
*   **Communication Channels** (email, phone, intranet): Choose the channels through which you typically receive internal communications.
    *   Select all applicable channels.
*   **Trust Level** (High, Medium, Low): Rate your level of trust in internal communications.
    *   A higher rating indicates that you trust the communications more.
*   **Transparency Level** (High, Medium, Low): Rate the transparency of internal communications.
    *   A higher rating indicates that you perceive internal communications as more transparent.
*   **Internal Communications** (Rating Scale): Rate the quality of internal communications.
    *   A higher rating indicates that you find the communications of good quality.
*   **Communication Frequency** (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly): Choose the frequency of internal communications you receive.
    *   Select all applicable frequencies.
*   **Survey Frequency** (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly): Choose the frequency of internal surveys you receive.
    *   Select all applicable frequencies.
*   **Feedback Channel** (email, phone, chat): Choose the channels through which you prefer to provide feedback.
    *   Select all applicable channels.
*   **Communication Style** (Rating Scale): Rate the style of internal communications.
    *   A higher rating indicates that you find the communications style effective.

Note: This help guide is based on the provided YAML and aims to provide clear explanations for each field. However, if you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to management or the survey administrators.
