# space_organization_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The space_organization_survey form is used to collect and track information about an organization's spaces.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all the necessary permissions to access and submit the form.
2. Select the relevant fields and enter the required information for your workspace or office.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **workspace_id** (`workspace_id`, **number**, optional): Enter a unique identifier for this workspace or office.
* **space_id** (`space_id`, **number**, optional): Enter the ID number for this space.
* **space_type** (`space_type`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select one or multiple options for the type of space (e.g. office, room, area).
* **space_capacity** (`space_capacity`, **number**, optional): Enter the capacity of this space (e.g. number of people that can fit).
* **space_description** (`space_description`, **text**, optional): Briefly describe this space.
* **space_floor** (`space_floor`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select whether this space is on the ground floor or not.
* **space_status** (`space_status`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the current status of this space (e.g. Active or Inactive).
* **space_employees** (`space_employees`, **text**, optional): Enter a list of employees assigned to this space.
* **space_equipment** (`space_equipment`, **text**, optional): List any notable equipment installed in this space.
* **space_notes** (`space_notes`, **note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about this space.
* **space_email** (`space_email`, **email**, optional): Enter the email address for this space or office's contact.
* **space_phone** (`space_phone`, **text, optional): Enter the phone number for this space or office's contact.
* **space_location** (`space_location`, **text**, optional): Enter the physical location of this space or office.
* **space_manager** (`space_manager`, **text, optional): Enter the name or title of this space or office's manager.
* **space_manager_email** (`space_manager_email`, **email, optional): Enter the email address of this space or office's manager.
* **space_manager_phone** (`space_manager_phone`, **text, optional): Enter the phone number of this space or office's manager.
* **space_last_updated** (`space_last_updated`, **date, optional): Record the last time this space was updated or modified.
* **space_created_at** (`space_created_at`, **date, optional): Record the date this space was created.
* **space_updated_at** (`space_updated_at`, **date, optional): Record the date this space was last updated.
* **space_deleted_at** (`space_deleted_at`, **date, optional): Record the date this space was deleted.
* **space_last_used** (`space_last_used`, **date, optional): Record the last time this space was used.
* **space_first_used** (`space_first_used`, **date, optional): Record the first time this space was used.
* **space_last_visited** (`space_last_visited`, **date, optional): Record the last time this space was visited.
* **space_first_visited** (`space_first_visited`, **date, optional): Record the first time this space was visited.
* **space_last_accessed** (`space_last_accessed`, **date, optional): Record the last time this space was accessed.
