# Business Intelligence Roadmap Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to gather information from organizations on their current business intelligence capabilities, strategic priorities, and requirements for a roadmap to support their business needs. This information will be used to determine the scope and timeline of the roadmap.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your organization's name and current business capabilities.
2. Select the top three strategic priorities to support with the BI roadmap.
3. Choose the desired timeframe for the roadmap delivery.
4. Provide an approximate budget for the roadmap implementation.
5. List the key stakeholders involved.
6. Describe the expected business outcomes from the roadmap.
7. Enter your contact email for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Current capabilities** (`current_capabilities`, text, optional): Provide a brief overview of your current BI capabilities.
* **Strategic priorities** (`strategic_priorities`, select_multiple, required): Select up to three priorities to support with the BI roadmap.
	+ Reporting: This includes generating reports and dashboards to support business decision-making.
	+ Advanced analytics: This includes in-depth analysis of data to support complex business decisions.
	+ Data governance: This includes managing and maintaining data quality, security, and compliance.
	+ Self-service: This includes empowering users to access and analyze data independently.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, select_one, required): Choose the desired timeframe for the roadmap delivery: 3 months, 6 months, 12 months, or 18 months.
* **Budget estimate** (`budget_estimate`, number, optional): Enter an approximate budget for the roadmap implementation.
* **Stakeholders** (`stakeholders`, text, optional): List the key stakeholders involved in the roadmap project.
* **Expected benefits** (`expected_benefits`, text, optional): Describe the expected business outcomes from the roadmap.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email for follow-up.
