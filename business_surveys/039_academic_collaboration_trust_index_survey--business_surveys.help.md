# Academic Collaboration Trust Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help users complete the Academic Collaboration Trust Index Survey, a form designed to measure trust in academic collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your email address in the "Email address" field.
2. Select your role in the collaboration from the "Your role" dropdown menu.
3. Select the context of your primary collaboration from the "Context of your primary collaboration" dropdown menu.
4. Answer the questions about your trust in various aspects of your collaboration, rating your level of agreement from 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree).
5. Select all factors that contribute to building trust and all factors that erode trust in the "Building Factors" and "Eroding Factors" sections, respectively.
6. Provide any suggestions for improving trust in academic collaborations in the "Suggestions" field.
7. Finally, rate your overall trust level in your collaboration and indicate if it has changed over time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email address** (`respondent_email`, email): Enter your email address in this field. This is a required field to identify you as the respondent.
* **Institution or organization** (`institution`, text): Enter the name of the institution or organization you are affiliated with.
* **Your role** (`role`, select_one): Select your role in the collaboration from the dropdown menu:
	+ Principal Investigator
	+ Co-Investigator
	+ Research Associate
	+ Post-doctoral researcher
	+ Graduate student
	+ Administrator
	+ Other
* **Context of your primary collaboration** (`collaboration_context`, select_one): Select the context of your primary collaboration from the dropdown menu:
	+ Within my department
	+ Across departments at the same institution
	+ With another institution domestically
	+ International collaboration
	+ Industry-academia partnership
* **I trust that my collaborators have the expertise and skills needed for the project** (`trust_competence`, select_one): Rate your level of agreement from 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree) on the statement "I trust that my collaborators have the expertise and skills needed for the project".
* **I trust that my collaborators act with honesty and integrity** (`trust_integrity`, select_one): Rate your level of agreement from 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree) on the statement "I trust that my collaborators act with honesty and integrity".
* **I trust that my collaborators deliver on their commitments and deadlines** (`trust_reliability`, select_one): Rate your level of agreement from 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree) on the statement "I trust that my collaborators deliver on their commitments and deadlines".
* **I feel comfortable sharing ideas and preliminary findings with my collaborators** (`trust_openness`, select_one): Rate your level of agreement from 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree) on the statement "I feel comfortable sharing ideas and preliminary findings with my collaborators".
* **I believe credit and authorship are distributed fairly among collaborators** (`trust_fairness`, select_one): Rate your level of agreement from 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree) on the statement "I believe credit and authorship are distributed fairly among collaborators".
* **Communication with my collaborators is open, timely, and effective** (`trust_communication`, select_one): Rate your level of agreement from 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree) on the statement "Communication with my collaborators is open, timely, and effective".
* **Overall, how would you rate the level of trust in your current collaboration** (`overall_trust_level`, select_one): Rate your overall level of trust from 1 (Very low) to 5 (Very high).
* **Has trust in your collaboration changed over time** (`trust_changed_over_time`, select_one): Select the option that best describes the change in trust over time.
	+ Increased significantly
	+ Increased somewhat
	+ Stayed the same
	+ Decreased somewhat
	+ Decreased significantly
* **What factors most contribute to building trust in academic collaborations** (`trust_building_factors`, select_multiple): Select all factors that contribute to building trust in your collaboration from the list:
	+ Clear expectations and agreements
	+ Regular communication
	+ Shared values and goals
	+ Track record of reliability
	+ Transparent decision-making
	+ Fair credit and recognition
* **What factors most erode trust in collaborations** (`trust_erosion_factors`, select_multiple): Select all factors that erode trust in your collaboration from the list:
	+ Missed deadlines
	+ Unfair credit distribution
	+ Poor communication
	+ Withholding information
	+ Conflicting agendas
	+ Lack of transparency
* **What suggestions do you have for improving trust in academic collaborations?** (`suggestions`, text): Enter any suggestions you have for improving trust in academic collaborations.
