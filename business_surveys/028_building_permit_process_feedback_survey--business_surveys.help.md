# Building Permit Process Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback on your experience with the building permit process. Your input will help us improve the process and provide better service to applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each page of the form as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
2. For each field, please answer the question based on your experience with the building permit process.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the "Suggestions for Process Improvement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, required): Enter the date you completed this survey.
* **Your Name** (`applicant_name`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, optional): Enter your contact phone number (if applicable).
* **Company or Organization** (`company_name`, optional): Enter your business or organization name (if applicable).
* **Type of Permit Applied For** (`permit_type`, required): Select the type of permit you applied for from the list.
* **Municipality or Jurisdiction** (`municipality`, required): Enter the municipality or jurisdiction where the permit was issued.
* **Permit Application Date** (`application_date`, optional): Enter the date you applied for the permit.
* **Permit Issuance Date** (`permit_issuance_date`, optional): Enter the date you received the permit.
* **Ease of Submitting Application** (`application_process_ease`, required): Select how easy or difficult you found the application process.
* **Clarity of Application Instructions** (`application_instructions`, required): Select how clear or unclear the application instructions were.
* **Documentation Requirements Were Clear** (`documentation_requirements`, required): Select how clear or unclear the documentation requirements were.
* **Online Submission System Quality** (`online_submission`, optional): Select the quality of the online submission system (if applicable).
* **Helpfulness of Permit Staff** (`staff_helpfulness`, required): Select how helpful or unhelpful the permit staff were.
* **Response Time to Permit Application** (`response_time`, required): Select how quickly or slowly you received a response to your application.
* **Overall Speed of Permit Approval** (`approval_speed`, required): Select how fast or slow the permit approval process was.
* **Were Revision Requests Clear and Reasonable** (`revision_requests`, optional): Select if the revision requests were clear and reasonable (if applicable).
* **Fee Costs Were Transparent and Fair** (`costs_transparent`, required): Select how transparent and fair the permit costs were.
* **Were There Hidden or Unexpected Requirements** (`hidden_requirements`, required): Select if there were any hidden or unexpected requirements.
* **Suggestions for Process Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, optional): Enter any suggestions or comments you have for improving the permit process.
* **Overall Satisfaction With Permit Process** (`overall_satisfaction`, required): Select your overall satisfaction with the permit process.
