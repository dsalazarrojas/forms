# Agreement Specialist Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients or users who have interacted with an agreement specialist. It helps the specialist to continuously improve their services and better serve their clients in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your name and email address (if you choose to provide it)
* Fill out the fields about your interaction with the agreement specialist:
	+ Enter the name of the agreement specialist you worked with
	+ Enter the name of your company
	+ Select the type of service you received
	+ Enter the date of your interaction with the specialist
* Rate your satisfaction and experience with the specialist:
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the service provided
	+ Rate the specialist's communication effectiveness
	+ Rate the specialist's expertise and knowledge
	+ Rate the specialist's responsiveness to your needs
	+ Rate the specialist's professionalism
* Provide feedback on the quality of the service:
	+ Rate the accuracy of the specialist's work
	+ Rate how well the specialist met the agreed-upon deadlines
	+ Rate whether the service provided was good value for money
* Provide feedback on the specialist's understanding of your requirements:
	+ Rate how well the specialist understood your requirements
* Provide any additional comments about your experience:
	+ What did you appreciate most about the service?
	+ What aspects of the service could be improved?
	+ Any additional comments about your experience
* Rate your likelihood to recommend the specialist to others and your interest in working with them again:
	+ How likely are you to recommend this specialist to others?
	+ Would you be interested in working with this specialist again?

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`): Your name (optional)
* **Respondent Email** (`respondent_email`): Your email address (optional)
* **Agreement Specialist Name** (`agreement_specialist_name`): The name of the agreement specialist you worked with (required)
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): The name of your company (required)
* **Interaction Date** (`interaction_date`): The date of your interaction with the agreement specialist (required)
* **Type of Service** (`service_type`): What type of agreement service did you receive? (required)
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating** (`overall_satisfaction_rating`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service provided (required)
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`): Rate the specialist's communication effectiveness (required)
* **Expertise and Knowledge** (`expertise_knowledge`): Rate the specialist's expertise and knowledge (required)
* **Responsiveness** (`responsiveness`): Rate the specialist's responsiveness to your needs (required)
* **Professionalism** (`professionalism`): Rate the specialist's professionalism (required)
* **Accuracy of Work** (`accuracy_of_work`): Rate the accuracy of the specialist's work (required)
* **Timeliness of Delivery** (`timeliness_of_delivery`): Rate whether the specialist met the agreed-upon deadlines (required)
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`): Rate whether the service provided was good value for money (required)
* **Understanding of Requirements** (`understanding_of_requirements`): Rate how well the specialist understood your requirements (required)
* **Positive Aspects** (`positive_aspects`): What did you appreciate most about the service? (optional)
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`): What aspects of the service could be improved? (optional)
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Any additional comments about your experience (optional)
* **Recommendation Likelihood** (`recommendation_likelihood`): How likely are you to recommend this specialist to others? (required)
* **Future Engagement Interest** (`future_engagement_interest`): Would you be interested in working with this specialist again? (required)

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when providing feedback. Your input helps the agreement specialist to improve their services.
* Take your time and carefully read each field to ensure you provide the most accurate information.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can leave it blank and provide any additional comments in the **Additional Comments** field.
