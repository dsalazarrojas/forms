# Site Signal Testing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on their experiences with signal testing, allowing us to evaluate and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant service provider from the options listed.
2. Choose the signal strength for each service provider.
3. Identify any signal issues that occurred during the incident.
4. Select the signal performance for each incident.
5. Provide detailed information about the date and time of the incident, if applicable.
6. Record the duration of the incident.
7. Enter the location of the incident.
8. Add any relevant notes to supplement your answers.
9. Enter your email and phone number for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Signal Rating**: Enter a number between 1 and 5 to rate the site signal strength, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Signal Strength**: Type a short description of the signal strength for each service provider.
* **Service Provider**: Select the service provider that was being used during the incident.
* **Signal Issues**: Choose any issues that you experienced with the signal, such as Interference, Noise, or Delay.
* **Signal Performance**: Select the performance of the signal for each incident, such as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Date of Incident**: Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY when applicable.
* **Time of Day**: Enter the time in the format HH:MM when applicable.
* **Duration of Incident**: Enter the duration of the incident in minutes.
* **Location**: Enter a detailed description of the location where the incident occurred.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or details about the incident.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Service Provider 2**: Select the service provider that was being used during the second incident.
* **Signal Issues 2**: Choose any issues that you experienced with the signal, such as Interference, Noise, or Delay.
* **Signal Performance 2**: Select the performance of the signal for each incident, such as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Date of Incident 2**: Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY when applicable.
* **Time of Day 2**: Enter the time in the format HH:MM when applicable.
* **Duration of Incident 2**: Enter the duration of the incident in minutes.
* **Location 2**: Enter a detailed description of the location where the second incident occurred.
* **Notes 2**: Add any additional comments or details about the second incident.
* **Email 2**: Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Phone 2**: Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
