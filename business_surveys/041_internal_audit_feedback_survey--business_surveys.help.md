# Internal Audit Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from internal auditors about their experiences and perceptions during the internal audit process. This survey aims to collect insights on the effectiveness and quality of the audit process, identify areas for improvement, and facilitate continuous enhancement of the internal audit function.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the questions carefully and provide honest and constructive feedback.
2. Answer each question to the best of your knowledge and experience.
3. You can skip questions that are not relevant to your experience with the internal audit process.
4. Submit your response once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Survey** (`feedback_survey`, text, required/optional): 
This field is a free-text response where you can provide your overall feedback and suggestions about the internal audit process. Write your comments in a clear and concise manner.
 
* **Audit Teams** (`audit_teams`, select_one, required/optional): 
Select all the audit teams you have worked with during the internal audit process. This will help us understand which teams were involved and their experiences.
 
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_multiple, required/optional): 
Indicate your level of satisfaction with the audit process. You can select more than one option if your experience varied across different aspects of the process.
 
* **Risk Areas** (`risk_areas`, text, required/optional): 
If there are specific areas that you believe require improvement, please list them in this field. Provide concrete examples or suggestions for addressing these risks.
 
* **Tips for Improvement** (Implicit. This field is not explicitly labeled in the provided YAML, but it can be assumed that end-users will use this field to provide additional feedback, even though its name is not provided.): 
Use this field to suggest any improvements or changes that you think would enhance the internal audit function and process. Be specific and provide concrete ideas.
