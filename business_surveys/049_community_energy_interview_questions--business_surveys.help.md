# Energy Interview Questions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals in the energy sector to understand the main concerns and challenges surrounding energy adoption and development in their communities or organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are eligible to fill out this form by identifying as a professional or community leader in the energy sector.
2. Fill out each field with the requested information, following the guidelines and hints provided for each field.
3. Answer all required fields to complete the form submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Interviewee name** (`interviewee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name for confidentiality purposes.
* **Organization or affiliation** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter your organization or affiliation name, such as "XYZ Corporation" or "ABC Community Group".
* **Main energy concerns in the community** (`main_energy_concerns`, text, required): Describe the main energy concerns in your community, including issues like cost, reliability, or access.
* **Readiness for renewable energy adoption** (`readiness_for_renewables`, select_one, required): Rate your organization's or community's readiness for renewable energy adoption on a scale from High to Low.
* **Barriers to energy project adoption** (`barriers_to_adoption`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more barriers that hinder energy project adoption in your area, such as Cost, Lack of information, Technical limitations, or Regulatory barriers.
* **Recommended interventions** (`recommended_interventions`, text, optional): Suggest policy programs or investments that could support energy development in your community or organization.

Note: Please answer each field carefully and accurately to provide valuable insights for energy sector professionals and researchers.
