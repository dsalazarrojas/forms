# Nonprofit Technology Adoption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Technology Adoption Survey is designed to collect information from non-profit organizations about their adoption of technology. This survey aims to gather insights on which tools have been adopted, the challenges faced during adoption, training needs, implementation time, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Nonprofit Technology Adoption Survey**: Enter a brief description of the survey or your thoughts on the technology adoption in your non-profit organization.
2. **What tools have you adopted?**: Select all the tools that your non-profit organization has adopted from the available options (tool-1, tool-2, tool-3).
3. **What challenges did you face when adopting this technology?**: Provide a written note on the challenges you faced during the technology adoption process.
4. **What training needs do you have for technology?**: Select the option that best describes your training needs (option-1, option-2).
5. **How long did it take to implement the technology?**: Enter the date of when the technology was implemented.
6. **By whom was the technology adopted?**: Select the person or department that adopted the technology (option-1, option-2).
7. **Which organization level adopted the technology?**: Select the level within your non-profit organization that adopted the technology (option-1, option-2).
8. **Which department adopted the technology?**: Select the department within your non-profit organization that adopted the technology (option-1, option-2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nonprofit Technology Adoption Survey** (`name`: `nonprofit_technology_adoption_survey`, `type`: `text`): Enter a brief description of the survey or your thoughts on the technology adoption in your non-profit organization.
* **What tools have you adopted?** (`name`: `nonprofit_adopted_technology`, `type`: `select_multiple`): Select all the tools that your non-profit organization has adopted from the available options (tool-1, tool-2, tool-3).
* **What challenges did you face when adopting this technology?** (`name`: `technology_adopted_challenge`, `type`: `note`): Provide a written note on the challenges you faced during the technology adoption process.
* **What training needs do you have for technology?** (`name`: `nonprofit_training_needs`, `type`: `select_one`): Select the option that best describes your training needs (option-1, option-2).
* **How long did it take to implement the technology?** (`name`: `nonprofit_implementation_time`, `type`: `date`): Enter the date of when the technology was implemented.
* **By whom was the technology adopted?** (`name`: `nonprofit_adopted_by`, `type`: `select_one`): Select the person or department that adopted the technology (option-1, option-2).
* **Which organization level adopted the technology?** (`name`: `nonprofit_organizational_level`, `type`: `select_one`): Select the level within your non-profit organization that adopted the technology (option-1, option-2).
* **Which department adopted the technology?** (`name`: `nonprofit_department_name`, `type`: `select_one`): Select the department within your non-profit organization that adopted the technology (option-1, option-2).

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses to ensure accurate information is collected.
* Please select all relevant tools that your non-profit organization has adopted.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
