# healthcare_leadership_digital_literacy_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the digital literacy levels, skills, and training needs of healthcare leaders within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. For select_multiple questions, select all applicable options that apply to you.
3. For text questions, provide a brief description or explanation.
4. Ensure that you complete all sections and questions as accurately as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`): Enter the name of your hospital or healthcare organization.
* **Leader Title** (`leader_title`): Enter your current leadership title or position within the organization.
* **Digital Literacy Level** (`digital_literacy_level`): Select the level of digital literacy you feel you possess, from High to Low.
	+ High: You possess a strong understanding of digital technologies and tools.
	+ Low: You may need additional training or support to effectively use digital technologies.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`): Enter the number of years you have been in your current leadership position.
* **Technology Skills** (`technology_skills`): Select the technology skills you currently possess or are proficient in, such as Yes or No for each option.
	+ Yes: You are proficient in this technology skill.
	+ No: You are not proficient in this technology skill.
* **Technology Barrier** (`technology_barrier`): Select the technology barriers you face in your daily work, from Yes or No for each option.
	+ Yes: You face this technology barrier.
	+ No: You do not face this technology barrier.
* **Technology Barrier Reason** (`technology_barrier_reason`): If you selected Yes for Technology Barrier, please provide a brief explanation for each barrier.
* **Goals** (`goals`): Enter your current goals and objectives for your organization or department.
* **Technology Training** (`technology_trainee`): Select whether you have received or plan to receive technology training, from Yes or No for each option.
	+ Yes: You have received or plan to receive technology training.
	+ No: You have not received or do not plan to receive technology training.
* **Hospital IT Support** (`hospital_it_support`): Select whether your hospital or organization provides sufficient IT support, from Yes or No for each option.
	+ Yes: Your hospital or organization provides sufficient IT support.
	+ No: Your hospital or organization does not provide sufficient IT support.
* **Communication Plan** (`communication_plan`): Select whether you have a communication plan in place, from Yes or No for each option.
	+ Yes: You have a communication plan in place.
	+ No: You do not have a communication plan in place.
* **Training Plan** (`training_plan`): Select whether you have a training plan in place, from Yes or No for each option.
	+ Yes: You have a training plan in place.
	+ No: You do not have a training plan in place.
* **Technology Coordination** (`technology_coordination`): Select whether you coordinate technology efforts, from Yes or No for each option.
	+ Yes: You coordinate technology efforts.
	+ No: You do not coordinate technology efforts.
* **Leadership Development** (`leadership_development`): Select whether you are involved in leadership development, from Yes or No for each option.
	+ Yes: You are involved in leadership development.
	+ No: You are not involved in leadership development.
* **Organizational Change** (`organizational_change`): Select whether you are leading or participating in organizational change, from Yes or No for each option.
	+ Yes: You are leading or participating in organizational change.
	+ No: You are not leading or participating in organizational change.
* **Technology Integration** (`technology_integration`): Select whether you have integrated technology into your work, from Yes or No for each option.
	+ Yes: You have integrated technology into your work.
	+ No: You have not integrated technology into your work.
* **Data Security** (`data_security`): Select whether you are concerned about data security, from Yes or No for each option.
	+ Yes: You are concerned about data security.
	+ No: You are not concerned about data security.
* **Elearning** (`elearning`): Select whether you have participated in elearning, from Yes or No for each option.
	+ Yes: You have participated in elearning.
	+ No: You have not participated in elearning.
* **Elearning Frequency** (`elearning_frequency`): Select how often you participate in elearning, from Yes or No for each option.
	+ Yes: You participate in elearning.
	+ No: You do not participate in elearning.
* **Technology Integration Plan** (`technology_integration_plan`): Enter a brief description of your technology integration plan.
* **Hospital Leadership Plan** (`hospital_leadership_plan`): Enter a brief description of your hospital leadership plan.
* **Technology Prophet Plan** (`technology_prophet_plan`): Enter a brief description of your technology prophet plan.
* **E-Learning Plan** (`e_learning_plan`): Enter a brief description of your E-Learning plan.
* **Communication Plan** (`communication_plannn`): Enter a brief description of your communication plan.
* **Hospital Leadership Prophets** (`hospital_leadership_prophets`): Enter a brief description of your hospital leadership prophets.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* Please ensure that you complete all sections and questions as accurately as possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or designated contact person.
* This form is designed to assess your current state of digital literacy, technology skills, and training needs. The information collected will be used to support your development and training needs within the organization.
