# Consulting Partnership Value Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to assess the value and performance of our consulting partnership with your organization. The survey is designed to gather feedback on our alignment with your organizational goals, collaboration, trust, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing honest and detailed answers to each question.
2. For select-one questions, choose the option that best describes your experience or perspective.
3. For text fields, provide a clear and concise description or example.
4. If you have any additional feedback or comments, feel free to use the "Any Additional Feedback or Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): Enter your organization's name for identification purposes.
* **Your Role in the Partnership** (`respondent_role`, `text`, not required): Briefly describe your position within the partnership.
* **Duration of Partnership** (`duration_of_partnership`, `select_one`, required): Choose how long we have worked together.
* **-- Partnership Performance --** (`section_partnership_performance`, `note`, not required): This is a section header to separate the performance questions.
* **Alignment with Organizational Goals** (`rating_alignment`, `select_one`, required): Choose how well we understand your mission.
* **Level of Collaboration** (`rating_collaboration`, `select_one`, required): Choose how effectively we work with your team.
* **Level of Trust and Transparency** (`rating_trust`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you rate the trust between us.
* **-- Value Assessment --** (`section_value_assessment`, `note`, not required): This is a section header to separate the value questions.
* **Perceived Value for Investment** (`perceived_value_for_money`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you're getting a good return on your spend.
* **Specific Example of Partnership Value** (`specific_value_example`, `text`, not required): Describe a key success or win.
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating** (`rating_overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Choose your general satisfaction with the partnership.
* **-- Looking Forward --** (`section_improvements`, `note`, not required): This is a section header to separate the improvements questions.
* **Primary Area for Improvement** (`primary_improvement_area`, `text`, required): Specify the single biggest thing we can improve.
* **Interest in Future Collaborative Projects** (`future_collaboration_interest`, `select_one`, not required): Choose whether you're interested in working with us again.
* **Any Additional Feedback or Comments** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, not required): Provide any final thoughts or comments.

Please fill out this survey and provide honest feedback. Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort.
