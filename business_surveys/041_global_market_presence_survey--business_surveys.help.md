# global_market_presence_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Market Presence Survey is a form designed to collect information about a company's global market presence. This data will help us understand your company's performance and reputation in the global market.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Company Name field with your company's official name.
2. Enter your company's website URL in the Company Website field.
3. Provide your company's phone number in the Phone field.
4. Enter your company's email address in the Email field (make sure to use the correct format, e.g., `example@example.com`).
5. Enter your company's brand name in the Brand Name field.
6. Enter your product name in the Product Name field.
7. Provide a numerical value between 1 and 10 for Market Reputation, representing your company's reputation in the market.
8. Enter a numerical value between 1 and 10 for Market Potential, representing your company's potential in the market.
9. Select one or more options for Product Availability, representing where your product is available (e.g., online, offline, both).
10. Select one option for Brand Recognition, representing your brand's recognition level (e.g., high, low, medium).
11. Select one option for Market Standing, representing your market standing (e.g., strong, weak, medium).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Global Market Presence Survey** (Introduction): This is the title of the form, which is not a field to fill, but an introduction to the form.
* **Company Name (Company Name)**: Enter your company's official name.
* **Company Website (Company Website)**: Enter your company's website URL.
* **Phone (Company Phone)**: Enter your company's phone number.
* **Email (Company Email)**: Enter your company's email address (use the correct format, e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **Brand Name (Brand Name)**: Enter your company's brand name.
* **Product Name (Product Name)**: Enter your company's product name.
* **Market Reputation (Market Reputation)**: Enter a numerical value between 1 and 10 representing your company's reputation in the market.
* **Market Potential (Market Potential)**: Enter a numerical value between 1 and 10 representing your company's potential in the market.
* **Product Availability (Product Availability)**: Select one or more options for where your product is available (e.g., online, offline, both).
* **Brand Recognition (Brand Recognition)**: Select one option for your brand's recognition level (e.g., high, low, medium).
* **Market Standing (Market Standing)**: Select one option for your market standing (e.g., strong, weak, medium).

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and relevant information will help us better understand your company's market presence.
