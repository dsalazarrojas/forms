# Client Success Measurement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help measure the level of engagement, satisfaction, and value that clients have with your services. It aims to identify potential risks and opportunities for growth and tailor your services and support to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
2. Be as specific as possible when answering questions, especially when it comes to measurable results or improvements achieved.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
4. If a question does not apply to your situation, please leave it blank or note "Not applicable".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name or Company** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the client or company that this survey is about.
* **Your Name** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter your name as the person completing this survey.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person completing this survey.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): Enter the date this survey is being completed.
* **Overall Engagement Level with our services** (`engagement_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of engagement you have with our services: Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, or Very High.
* **Engagement Trend over the past 6 months** (`engagement_trend`, select_one, required): Select the engagement trend you've observed over the past 6 months: Declining, Stable, Growing, or Significantly Growing.
* **How many product features are they actively using?** (`feature_adoption`, number, required): Enter the percentage of available features the client is actively using.
* **Current Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, number, required): Rate your current satisfaction with our services, from 1 (Very unsatisfied) to 10 (Very satisfied).
* **Net Promoter Score** (`nps_score`, number, required): Rate the likelihood of the client recommending our services to a colleague, from 0 to 10.
* **Perceives Strong Value from our services** (`value_perception`, select_one, required): Select how the client perceives the value of our services: Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, or Strongly Agree.
* **Achieving Positive Return on Investment** (`roi_perception`, select_one, required): Select how the client perceives their return on investment with our services: Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, or Strongly Agree.
* **Initial Goals when they signed up** (`initial_goals`, text, required): Enter the initial goals that the client had when they signed up.
* **Progress toward achieving those initial goals** (`goal_progress`, select_one, required): Select the progress the client has made toward achieving their initial goals: Not Started, Early Stages, Moderate Progress, Significant Progress, or Completed.
* **Measurable Results or Improvements Achieved** (`measurable_results`, text, required): Enter specific measurable results or improvements achieved by the client.
* **Churn Risk Assessment** (`churn_risk`, select_one, required): Select the churn risk the client presents: Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, or Very High.
* **Primary Reason for Churn Risk** (`churn_reason`, text, required): Enter the primary reason for the client's churn risk, if assessed as high.
* **Likelihood of Renewal or Continued Engagement** (`retention_likelihood`, select_one, required): Select the likelihood of the client renewing or continuing their engagement: Very Unlikely, Unlikely, Neutral, Likely, or Very Likely.
* **Overall Client Health Score** (`client_health_score`, number, required): Enter the overall client health score, from 1 to 100.
* **Expansion or Upsell Opportunity Score** (`expansion_opportunity`, number, required): Enter the likelihood of the client's expansion or upsell opportunity, from 1 to 10.
* **Likelihood They Will Advocate for Us** (`advocacy_likelihood`, select_one, required): Select the likelihood the client will advocate for our services: Very Unlikely, Unlikely, Neutral, Likely, or Very Likely.
* **What Advocacy Activities Would They Be Willing to Do?** (`advocacy_activities`, text, required): Enter the advocacy activities the client would be willing to do, such as providing a case study or testimonial.
* **Recommended Next Steps for Client Success** (`recommended_actions`, text, required): Enter any recommended next steps for the client's success.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when answering each question.
* If a question does not apply, please leave it blank or note "Not applicable".
* Take as much time as needed to complete this survey for the best results.
* Once completed, return the survey to the relevant team members for review and discussion.
* Use this survey results to inform and improve services and support offered to clients.
