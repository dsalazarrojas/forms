# office_information_coordination_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your office's current communication and coordination processes. It will help us understand how you currently work with your team and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. You can choose multiple options for the communication challenges and select one option for the communication tools.
3. Leave the "improvement ideas" field for any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Office Name** (`office_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your office or department name where you work.
* **Communication Challenges** (`communication_challenges`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check all challenges that apply to your team:
	+ Yes for challenges you experience
	+ No for challenges you do not experience
* **Communication Tools** (`communication_tools`, select_one, required/optional): Which of the following tools do you primarily use for communication?
	+ Email
	+ Slack
	+ Teams
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, note, required/optional): If you have any ideas for improving communication or coordination within your team, please list them here.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your department or team.
* **Manager** (`manager`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your manager or supervisor.
* **Team Member Count** (`team_member_count`, number, required/optional): Enter the approximate number of people on your team.
* **Team Member Names** (`team_member_names`, text, required/optional): List the names of all team members.
* **Team Member Emails** (`team_member_emails`, text, required/optional): List the emails of all team members.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form as thoroughly as possible to help us gather accurate information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
