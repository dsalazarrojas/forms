# Gym Staff Training Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback on the effectiveness of training programs provided to gym staff, aiming to evaluate the quality and relevance of the training sessions, identify areas of improvement, and inform future training initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the available options for each question and select the ones that best describe your experience with the training programs.
2. Provide feedback on the effectiveness of each training program by selecting multiple options.
3. Evaluate the effectiveness of each program by selecting one option.
4. Describe any training outcomes or results you have achieved as a result of the training.
5. Select the frequency with which you are evaluated on your training performance.
6. Describe how you are evaluated on your training performance.
7. Indicate the frequency of training you receive.
8. Provide the total hours spent on training.
9. Share any comments on the evaluation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Programs** (`training_programs`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the training programs you have attended, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3", etc.
* **Program Effectiveness** (`program_effectiveness`, `number`, optional): Rate the effectiveness of the training programs on a scale (assuming the scale is not explicitly defined, please rate as you feel it best represents the effectiveness).
* **Training Goals** (`training_goals`, `select_one`, optional): Select the primary goal of the training program you attended.
* **Training Outcomes** (`training_outcomes`, `text`, optional): Describe any outcomes or results you have achieved as a result of the training program.
* **Training Evaluations** (`training_evaluations`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the frequency of evaluations you receive on your training performance.
* **Evaluation Frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select the frequency with which you are evaluated on your training performance (e.g., weekly, monthly, quarterly).
* **Evaluation Methods** (`evaluation_methods`, `text`, optional): Describe how you are evaluated on your training performance.
* **Training Frequency** (`training_frequency`, `number`, optional): Indicate how frequently you receive training sessions.
* **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, `number`, optional): Enter the total hours you spend on training.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, `text`, optional): Share any comments on the evaluation process.
* **Training Hours Frequency** (`training_hours_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select the frequency of training hours you receive.

Note: All fields are optional, so you do not need to fill out all of them. Provide the information that is relevant to your experience with the training programs.
