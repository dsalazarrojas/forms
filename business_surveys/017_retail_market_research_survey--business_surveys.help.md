# Retail Market Research Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on your shopping habits and preferences to help us better understand your needs and provide a better shopping experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each section:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Choose your preferred type of stores.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with your current shopping experience.
4. Indicate how often you visit stores.
5. Select your favorite store.
6. Enter your age group.
7. Select your preferred store location.
8. Choose your preferred store type.
9. Select your gender.
10. Enter your annual income.
11. Choose your education level.
12. Select your employment status.
13. Choose your marital status.
14. Enter your occupation.
15. Share your store experience.
16. Rate your customer service experience.
17. Check if you participate in any loyalty programs.
18. Choose your preferred communication channel.
19. Select how you prefer to contact the store.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (contact_info)**: Please enter your contact details to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* **What kind of stores do you visit? (shopping_preferences)**: Select all types of stores you visit, such as department stores, clothing stores, and electronics stores.
* **Overall Satisfaction (satisfaction_score)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your current shopping experience, on a scale of 1-5.
* **How often do you shop? (shopping_frequency)**: Select how often you visit stores, such as daily, weekly, or rarely.
* **Favorite Store (preferred_store)**: Enter the name of your favorite store.
* **Store Visit Details (store_visit_details)**: Enter the date of your last store visit.
* **What's your budget? (purchase_amount)**: Enter your monthly or annual budget for shopping.
* **Age Group (age_group)**: Select your age group, such as 18-24 or 45-54.
* **Store Location (location)**: Select where you prefer to shop, such as city, suburbs, or online.
* **Store Type (store_type)**: Select the type of store you prefer, such as department stores or electronics stores.
* **Gender (gender)**: Select your gender, such as male or female.
* **Annual Income (income)**: Enter your annual income.
* **Education Level (education_level)**: Select your education level, such as high school or bachelor's degree.
* **Employment Status (employment_status)**: Select your employment status, such as employed or self-employed.
* **Marital Status (marital_status)**: Select your marital status, such as single or married.
* **Occupation (occupation)**: Enter your occupation.
* **Store Experience (store_experience)**: Share your experience with the store, such as positive or negative.
* **Customer Service (customer_service)**: Rate your customer service experience, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Do you participate in any loyalty programs? (loyalty_program)**: Check if you participate in any loyalty programs.
* **How do you prefer to be contacted? (communication_channel)**: Select all communication channels you prefer, such as email or SMS.
* **How do you prefer to contact the store? (contact_method)**: Select how you prefer to contact the store, such as phone or in person.
