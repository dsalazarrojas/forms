# Hair Mask Usage Frequency Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the frequency of hair mask usage. Please answer the questions honestly to help us better understand the usage habits of individuals in your demographic.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Read the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Answer the questions truthfully, selecting the correct options for each question.
3. If you have any additional information you'd like to share, use the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): This question asks how satisfied you are with your current hair mask usage. Please select one of the options provided.
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about your preferences when it comes to hair masks. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional information you'd like to share about your hair mask usage, please type it here.
* **Hair Mask Habits** (`hair_mask_habits`, `text`, optional): This question asks about your hair mask habits. Please provide a brief description of your current habits.
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required by default is false, but it's a note section, so it's not necessary to explain): This is a note section that provides context and background information about the survey. You can read it to understand the purpose and objectives of the survey.
