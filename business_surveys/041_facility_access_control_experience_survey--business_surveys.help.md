# Facility Access Control Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your feedback on your experience with facility access control. Your input will help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the questions below and provide your honest answers.
2. Select the options that best fit your experiences and opinions.
3. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please share them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is an optional introduction section where you can briefly describe your context or any previous experience with facility access control.
* **What Type of Facility**: Please select all types of facilities that you have experience with, such as Offices, Warehouses, or other facilities.
* **What is Size of Facility**: Choose the size of the facility you visit, such as 10 or less, 11-50, 51-100, or 101 or more.
* **How Many People**: If applicable, enter the number of people you typically visit the facility with.
* **How Frequent Do You Visit**: Select how often you visit the facility, such as Rarely, Regularly, or Almost Daily.
* **Last Visit Duration**: If applicable, enter the length of your last visit to the facility.
* **How Would You Rate Your Experience**: Rate your overall experience with facility access control, such as Very Good, Good, Average, or Bad.
* **Overall Experience of Facility Access Control**: Rate your experience with facility access control, such as Very Good, Good, Average, or Bad.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or thoughts you have about your experience with facility access control.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you if needed.
