# world_management_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The world_management_feedback form is a survey used to gather feedback from team members regarding their management team. The goal is to understand team members' experiences, strengths, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your title in the "World Management Feedback" text field.
2. Select the type of feedback you want to provide (Positive, Negative, or Neutral).
3. Choose the department you are providing feedback on.
4. Rate your manager's performance on a scale (e.g., 1-10).
5. Provide comments on your manager's performance.
6. Rate your team's performance on a scale (e.g., 1-10).
7. Provide comments on your team's performance.
8. Rate your overall experience on a scale (e.g., 1-10).
9. Provide any additional comments.
10. Indicate how often you give feedback on this topic (e.g., Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Always).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **World Management Feedback**: (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title for your feedback. This will help us understand the context of your response.
* **Feedback Type**: (`feedback_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of feedback you want to provide (Positive, Negative, or Neutral).
* **Department**: (`department`, text, required/optional): Select the department you are providing feedback on.
* **Manager Rating**: (`manager_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate your manager's performance on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Manager Comments**: (`manager_comments`, text, required/optional): Provide comments on your manager's performance.
* **Team Rating**: (`team_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate your team's performance on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Team Comments**: (`team_comments`, text, required/optional): Provide comments on your team's performance.
* **Overall Rating**: (`overall_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate your overall experience on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Overall Comments**: (`overall_comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments on your overall experience.
* **Feedback Frequency**: (`feedback_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate how often you give feedback on this topic (e.g., Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, Always).
