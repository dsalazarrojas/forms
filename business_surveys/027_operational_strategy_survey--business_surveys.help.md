# operational_strategy_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The operational strategy survey is a tool used to gather information about a company's current and future operational strategies, objectives, and performance. It is designed to help identify areas of improvement and provide suggestions for future development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, simply fill out each section with the requested information. For each question, provide as much detail as possible to ensure accurate and thorough results. You can select multiple options from the dropdown menus where applicable. Make sure to answer all questions marked as "required" as they are necessary for the survey's purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the company's operational strategy?** (1, "operational_strategy"): Describe the company's overall approach to achieving its objectives and goals. 
* **What are the company's operational objectives?** (2, "operational_objectives"): List the specific, measurable goals and objectives that the company is working towards. 
* **What are the performance metrics used to measure the company's performance?** (3, "performance_metrics"): Identify the metrics that the company uses to evaluate its progress and success. 
* **What are the operational processes used in the company?** (4, "operational_processes"): Describe the steps and procedures that the company follows to achieve its objectives. 
* **How does the company measure its efficiency and effectiveness?** (5, "efficiency_and_effectiveness"): Indicate whether the company's operational processes are highly effective, moderately effective, or not effective at all. 
* **What feedback do you have for the stakeholders?** (6, "stakeholders_feedback"): Share any comments or suggestions you have for the individuals or teams involved in the company's operations. 
* **What recommendations and actions would you suggest for the company?** (7, "recommendations_and_actions"): Propose specific steps or recommendations that the company can take to improve its operational strategy. 
* **How does the company conduct review and follow-up?** (8, "review_and_followup"): Explain the frequency and method of review and follow-up meetings or processes. 
* **What key performance indicators (KPIs) are used by the company?** (9, "key_performance_indicators"): Select all the relevant metrics that the company uses to evaluate its performance. 
* **How often does the company conduct review and follow-up meetings?** (10, "review_and_followup_frequency"): Choose the frequency of review and follow-up meetings. 
* **What is the role of the company's leadership in the operational strategy?** (11, "leadership_role"): Describe the leadership's involvement and responsibilities in the operational strategy. 
* **What are the main challenges faced by the company in its operational strategy?** (12, "main_challenges"): Identify the key obstacles or challenges that the company encounters in its operational strategy. 
* **What are the future plans of the company for its operational strategy?** (13, "future_plans"): Outline the company's plans for future development and growth in its operational strategy.
