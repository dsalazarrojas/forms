# Geographic Information Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a visitor's geographic location, demographics, and interaction with the business or institution. The information collected will help us improve our services, products, or experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Where did you visit?**: Select the location you visited.
2. **How old are you?**: Enter your age if you want to share this information.
3. **Income level**: Select your current income level.
4. **Gender**: Choose your gender.
5. **Occupation**: Enter your profession or job title.
6. **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
7. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
8. **Time of Visit**: Choose the time of day you visited.
9. **Time of Visit (Time)**: Choose the time you arrived and departed.
10. **Visit Length**: Estimate the length of your visit.
11. **How did you hear about us?**: Select all the methods that apply (e.g., social media, referral, search engine).
12. **Recommendation**: Select all the ways you would recommend our business or institution to others.
13. **Additional Comments**: Share any further comments or suggestions.
14. **Contact Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
15. **Visit Frequency**: Estimate how often you visit our location.
16. **Visit Duration**: Estimate the average duration of your visits.
17. **Visit Time**: Choose the time you usually visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Where did you visit?** (`survey_location`, select_one, required): This field asks you to select the location you visited. Please choose the correct location from the options provided.
* **How old are you?** (`age`, number, optional): If you want to share your age, enter it here.
* **Income level** (`income_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select your current income level (high, medium, low).
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Choose your gender.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, optional): Enter your profession or job title.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, date, optional): Choose the time of day you visited.
* **Time of Visit (Time)** (`time_of_visit_time`, time, optional): Choose the time you arrived and departed.
* **Visit Length** (`visit_length`, number, optional): Estimate the length of your visit.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the methods that apply (e.g., social media, referral, search engine).
* **Recommendation** (`how_would_you_recommend`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the ways you would recommend our business or institution to others.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Share any further comments or suggestions.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, number, optional): Estimate how often you visit our location.
* **Visit Duration** (`visit_duration`, number, optional): Estimate the average duration of your visits.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, optional): Choose the time you usually visit.

## Tips
- Please answer truthfully and accurately.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Additional Comments" field.
- If you would like to be contacted, please fill in the "Contact Email" or "Contact Phone Number" fields.
