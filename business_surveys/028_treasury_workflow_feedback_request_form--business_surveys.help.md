# treasury_workflow_feedback_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The treasury workflow feedback request form is a survey designed to gather feedback from users on the treasury workflow's efficiency, clarity, and areas for improvement. Your input will help us assess the current state of the workflow and make adjustments to optimize it for better performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best describe your experience with the treasury workflow.
2. Provide a brief explanation of your answers for each question.
3. Submit your feedback by selecting the "Yes" option in the "submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workflow Efficiency** (workflow_efficiency): Please select all the options that best describe your experience with the treasury workflow.
	+ "Yes": The workflow is meeting the required standards.
	+ "No": The workflow is not meeting the required standards.
* **Clarity** (clarity): Describe your experience with the clarity of the treasury workflow.
* **Issues** (issues): Describe any issues or problems you've encountered while using the treasury workflow.
* **Process Improvements** (process_improvements): If you have any suggestions for improving the treasury workflow, please describe them here.
* **Comments** (comments): If you have any additional comments or feedback about the treasury workflow, please provide them here.
* **Submit** (submit): Please select "Yes" to submit your feedback.
* **Email** (email): Please enter your email address so that we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Phone** (phone): If you'd like to be contacted by phone for further discussion, please enter your phone number here.

## Tips
* Please provide specific examples or scenarios that illustrate your points.
* If you have feedback on a specific step or process within the treasury workflow, please be as detailed as possible.
* Your input is crucial in helping us improve the treasury workflow, and we appreciate your time and feedback!
