# Business Identification Number Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Identification Number Survey collects information about your business to help us understand your company's details for legal and regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as 'true' in the form configuration).
3. Enter your business' legal name, business identification number, and country of registration accurately.
4. Choose the correct business structure from the available options (Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Corporation, or Limited Liability Company).
5. If applicable, enter the date of official registration.
6. Enter the name and email of the primary contact person responsible for registration queries.
7. Add any additional notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Legal Business Name** (`legal_business_name`, `text`, required): Enter your company's registered name as per government records.
- **Business Identification Number** (`business_identification_number`, `text`, required): Enter your business registration or tax ID number.
- **Country of Registration** (`country_of_registration`, `text`, required): Enter the country where your business is registered.
- **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of official registration if known.
- **Business Structure** (`business_structure`, `select_one`, required): Choose from Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Corporation, or Limited Liability Company.
- **Primary Contact Name** (`primary_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for registration queries.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
- **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any extra information about your business.

## Tips
- Be accurate and thorough in your responses to ensure the form is completed correctly.
- Ensure you have the necessary information before submitting the form.
