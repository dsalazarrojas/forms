# Jira Integration Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jira Integration Feedback Form is designed to gather user feedback on the integration of Jira with our services. This form will help us understand how our integration affects your experience and gather valuable insights to make improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the category that best describes the purpose of your feedback.
2.  Enter a short title that summarizes your experience.
3.  Fill in your email address to receive any follow-up communication.
4.  Optionally, provide your phone number for us to contact you.
5.  Add any comments or suggestions you may have.
6.  Choose a rating for your overall experience.
7.  Enter the date and time of your interaction with Jira.
8.  Optionally, provide any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Category** (`category`: Category): Select the category that best describes the purpose of your feedback. This will help us understand the nature of your feedback.
*   **Title** (`title`: Title): Enter a brief title that describes your experience with the Jira integration. This title will help us identify your feedback and associate it with the correct category.
*   **Email** (`email`: Email): If you'd like to receive any follow-up communication, please provide your email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`: Phone): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number. (Optional)
*   **Comments** (`comments`: Comments): Add any comments or suggestions you may have about the Jira integration.
*   **Rating** (`rating`: Rating): Choose a rating that reflects your overall experience with the Jira integration. Choose from Very satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied.
*   **Date** (`date`: Date): Enter the date of your interaction with Jira.
*   **Time** (`time`: Time): Enter the time of your interaction with Jira.
*   **Form Comments 2** (`comments_2`: Form Comments 2): Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
