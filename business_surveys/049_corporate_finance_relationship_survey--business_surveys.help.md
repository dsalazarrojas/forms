# Corporate Finance Relationship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Finance Relationship Survey is a form designed to gauge the quality of collaboration and communication between the finance team and other departments within the company. This survey aims to identify areas of improvement and opportunities for enhanced relationships between finance and other teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand the questions and options.
2. Select the options that best describe your relationship with the finance team.
3. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
4. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable) for us to contact you with any follow-up questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Corporate Finance Relationship Survey** (`title`, `note`, required: false): This is the title of the form, which is intended to help you understand the purpose of the survey.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks you to select all that apply from the options provided (Finance Team, Customer Support, Accounting, IT, Other) to describe your relationship with the finance team.
* **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, select_one, required: false): This question asks you to select the frequency of your collaboration with the finance team, using the options (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Other).
* **Finance Support** (`finance_support`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks you to select all that apply from the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor) to describe the support you receive from the finance team.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): This is a text box where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about your relationship with the finance team.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): This is a field where you can enter your email address for us to contact you with any follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): This is a field where you can enter your phone number for us to contact you with any follow-up questions.

## Tips
* Please answer this form honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, please use the feedback field to provide them.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us at the email or phone number you provided.
