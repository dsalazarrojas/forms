# Best Radio Journalist Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Radio Journalist Survey is designed to collect feedback on a radio journalist's performance, presentation, and reach. The survey is used to evaluate the strengths and weaknesses of the journalist and provide scores for each evaluation criterion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and email address to identify yourself as a respondent.
2. Select whether you are a professional in the radio industry or a regular listener.
3. Nominate the radio journalist you are evaluating.
4. Select the primary beat or topic of the journalist's focus.
5. Evaluate the journalist's performance on a scale of 1 to 5 for Vocal Clarity and Delivery, Content Accuracy and Objectivity, Interviewing Skills, Engaging Presentation Style, and Depth of Research.
6. Describe the most memorable segment or report by the journalist and how it has influenced your community or understanding.
7. State what makes this journalist stand out from their peers.
8. Finally, select whether you would listen to this journalist's show again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### -- Respondent Information --
* **Your Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name to identify yourself as a respondent.
* **Your Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your email address to identify yourself as a respondent.
* **Are you a professional in the radio industry** (Select One, Required): Select whether you are a professional in the radio industry or a regular listener.

### -- Radio Journalist Nomination --
* **Journalist Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the journalist you are evaluating.

### -- Performance Evaluation --
* **Vocal Clarity and Delivery** (Select One, Required): Evaluate the journalist's vocal clarity and delivery on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Content Accuracy and Objectivity** (Select One, Required): Evaluate the journalist's content accuracy and objectivity on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Interviewing Skills** (Select One, Required): Evaluate the journalist's interviewing skills on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Engaging Presentation Style** (Select One, Required): Evaluate the journalist's engaging presentation style on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Depth of Research** (Select One, Required): Evaluate the journalist's depth of research on a scale of 1 to 5.

### -- Impact and Narrative Reach --
* **Describe the most memorable segment or report by this journalist** (Text, Required): Describe a specific story that stayed with you.
* **How has this journalist influenced your community or understanding** (Text, Required): Describe their impact on local or global issues.
* **What makes this journalist stand out from their peers** (Text, Required): State what makes this journalist unique.

### -- Final Submission --
* **Would you listen to this journalist's show again** (Select One, Required): Select whether you would listen to this journalist's show again.
* **Date of Response** (Date, Required): Enter the date you are submitting this form.

Please note that each field is required to be completed accurately to get a thorough evaluation of the radio journalist.
