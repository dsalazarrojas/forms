# Accounting Team Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from the accounting team on various aspects of their work environment, job satisfaction, and team dynamics. It is intended to help management understand the team's strengths and weaknesses, and to identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Please select the options that best match your current state and feelings.
3. Be as specific as possible when providing further comments or suggestions.
4. If you have any additional remarks, please share them in the "Further comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: This field is for optional reference, and your identity will not be revealed.
* **Tenure with Company**: Please provide the number of years you have been with the company.
* **Job Satisfaction Level**: Rate your overall job satisfaction level.
* **Support from Team**: Select an option that best describes the level of support you receive from your team.
* **Management Communication Clarity**: Rate the clarity of communication from management.
* **Workload Balance**: Select an option that best describes your current workload balance.
* **Training Needs**: If you have specific areas where you feel you need training, please describe them in this field.
* **Recognition and Rewards**: Select an option that best describes how you feel your contributions are recognized.
* **Suggestions for Change**: If you have concrete suggestions to improve team functioning, please provide them in this field.
* **Further Comments**: Share any additional remarks or suggestions you might have.

## Tips
* Please keep your answers concise and clear.
* If you need more space for your answers, use the "Further comments" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to management.

Note: This guide is intended to be a helpful resource for team members to complete the form efficiently and effectively. If you have any further questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please do not hesitate to ask.
