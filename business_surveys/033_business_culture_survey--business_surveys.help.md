# Business Culture Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback on the current state of your company culture. It will help us understand your perceptions of our mission, values, leadership, teamwork, innovation, and overall job satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. For questions with a scale (e.g., Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly Disagree), choose the response that best reflects your opinion.
3. For questions with a "Select One" type, choose only one response per question.
4. For questions with a "Text" type, provide a brief answer to explain your thoughts.
5. Ensure that you're answering as a representative of your company, and not as an individual.
