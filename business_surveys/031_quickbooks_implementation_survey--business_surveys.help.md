# QuickBooks Implementation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The QuickBooks Implementation Survey is designed to gather information about your organization's QuickBooks implementation experience. This survey will help us understand your implementation phase, benefits realized, challenges faced, and suggestions for improvement. Your input will aid us in providing more effective support and guidance for future implementations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields (marked with \* in the label) and provide any optional answers as well. Please make sure to choose one answer from the dropdown menu for select type fields (Implementation Phase, Team Size, Benefits Realized, and Challenges Faced). You can provide multiple answers to these fields if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What is QuickBooks?
* This field is optional and is meant to help us understand your basic familiarity with QuickBooks.
### 2. \* Company Name
* This is the name of your company or organization.
### 3. QuickBooks Version
* This field is optional and is used to gather information about the version of QuickBooks you are using.
### 4. \* Implementation Date
* This field is required and is used to track the date of your QuickBooks implementation.
### 5. Team Size
* Select the approximate number of people using QuickBooks in your organization.
	+ 1-10
	+ 11-50
	+ 51-100
	+ 101-200
	+ 201-500
	+ 501+
### 6. \* Implementation Phase
* This field is required and is meant to gather information about the phase of your QuickBooks implementation.
	+ Planning
	+ Design
	+ Development
	+ Testing
	+ Deployment
	+ Maintenance
### 7. QuickBooks Training
* This field is optional and is used to gather information about the level of QuickBooks training your team has received.
### 8. Benefits Realized
* Select the benefits you have realized from using QuickBooks (you can select multiple answers).
	+ Cost Savings
	+ Productivity Increase
	+ Improved Communication
	+ Enhanced Reporting
	+ Other (please describe)
### 9. Challenges Faced
* Select the challenges you faced during QuickBooks implementation (you can select multiple answers).
	+ Difficulty in getting team members on board
	+ Data loss
	+ Integration issues
	+ Training and Support
	+ Technical difficulties
	+ Other (please describe)
### 10. Suggestions for Improvement
* This field is optional and provides a place for you to suggest any areas of improvement for future QuickBooks implementations.
