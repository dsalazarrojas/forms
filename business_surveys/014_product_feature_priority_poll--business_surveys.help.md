# Product Feature Priority Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your opinions and feedback on a specific product feature poll.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the features that you consider high or low priority.
2. Fill in your email and phone number (if applicable) for contact purposes.
3. If you'd like, add any additional comments or notes.
4. Select the product name and feature name (if known) for reference.
5. Choose your customer name and provide any additional comments if desired.
6. Finally, submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Feature Priority Poll** (`select_multiple`, optional): This is the main question of the form where you get to decide which features are high or low priority to you. Choose from the list of features below:
	+ Feature 1
	+ Feature 2
	+ Feature 3
* **Email** (`email`, optional): This is a required field for us to contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): Provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Note** (`note`, optional): If you'd like to add a comment or note, this is the field for you.
* **Feature 1-3** (`number`, optional): Select the numbers of the features that correspond to Feature 1, Feature 2, or Feature 3.
* **Date** (`date`, optional): Select the date you'd like to use for reference.
* **Time** (`time`, optional): Select the time you'd like to use for reference.
* **Feature Priority** (`select_one`, optional): Choose whether the features are High or Low priority.
* **Product Name** (`text`, optional): If you know the product name, please let us know.
* **Customer Comments** (`text`, optional): If you'd like to add any additional comments, this is the field.
* **Customer Name** (`text`, optional): Please enter your customer name.
* **Form Submitted** (`number`, optional): This field is automatically filled out by our system.
* **Form Completed** (`number`, optional): This field is automatically filled out by our system.
* **Form ID** (`number`, optional): This field is automatically filled out by our system.
* **Form Category** (`text`, optional): This field is automatically filled out by our system.
* **Form Description** (`text`, optional): This field is automatically filled out by our system.

Please note that some fields are not required, as indicated by the word "optional" in parentheses next to each field. However, please fill out as much information as you can to help us better understand your opinions and feedback.
