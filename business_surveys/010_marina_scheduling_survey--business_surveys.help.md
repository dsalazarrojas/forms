# marina_scheduling_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The marina scheduling survey form is designed to gather information from users about their preferences for arriving and departing from the marina. This information will help marina staff optimize scheduling and provide better service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully and fill out the required information.
2. Select your preferred arrival and departure dates and times.
3. Choose your preferred arrival days.
4. Select your preferred contact method.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes.
6. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, required: false): Enter your planned arrival date.
* **Earliest Arrival Time** (`earliest_arrival_time`, time, required: false): Enter your earliest arrival time.
* **Preferred Arrival Date** (`preferred_arrival_date`, date, required: false): Enter your preferred arrival date.
* **Preferred Arrival Time** (`preferred_arrival_time`, time, required: false): Enter your preferred arrival time.
* **Preferred Arrival Day** (`preferred_arrival_day`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your preferred arrival days from the list.
* **Earliest Arrival Time Preference** (`earliest_arrival_time_preference`, select_one, required: false): Choose your preferred arrival time (morning, afternoon, or evening).
* **Latest Departure Time Preference** (`latest_departure_time_preference`, select_one, required: false): Choose your preferred departure time (morning, afternoon, or evening).
* **Preferred Length of Stay** (`preferred_length_of_stay`, number, required: false): Enter your preferred length of stay (in hours).
* **Earliest Arrival** (`earliest_arrival`, date, required: false): Enter your earliest arrival date.
* **Latest Departure** (`latest_departure`, date, required: false): Enter your latest departure date.
* **Boat Length** (`boat_length`, select_one, required: false): Select the length of your boat (15 feet, 20 feet, 25 feet).
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your preferred contact method (email, phone, text).
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your preferred contact method (call, email, text).
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments.
* **Boat Type** (`boat_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of boat (sailboat, powerboat, other).
* **Boat Size** (`boat_size`, number, required: false): Enter the size of your boat.
* **Marina Location** (`marina_location`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your preferred marina location (location1, location2, location3).
