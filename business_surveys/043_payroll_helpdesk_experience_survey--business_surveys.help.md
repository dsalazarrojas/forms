# Payroll Helpdesk Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payroll Helpdesk Experience Survey is designed to collect feedback from payroll employees on their experience with the helpdesk. This survey aims to gather information on the quality of service, response time, and other key aspects of the helpdesk experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, navigate to the designated helpdesk experience survey page.
- Select the relevant ratings and answers according to your actual experience with the helpdesk.
- Ensure that the required fields are filled out, as indicated by the asterisk (*) in the field label.
- If you have any issues or concerns, you may describe them in the Issue Description field.
- Click "Submit" to send the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Response Time** (`response_time`, number, optional):
	+ Rate the time it took for the helpdesk team to respond to your issue.
* **Professionalism** (`professionalism`, number, optional):
	+ Rate the professionalism of the helpdesk team member who assisted you.
* **Issue Resolution** (`issue_resolution`, number, optional):
	+ Rate the effectiveness of the helpdesk team in resolving your issue.
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, optional):
	+ Describe how well the helpdesk team communicated with you.
* **Support Service** (`support_service`, select_one, optional):
	+ Rate the quality of the support service provided.
*   - **Very Good**
*   - **Good**
*   - **Neutral**
*   - **Bad**
* **Email Support** (`email_support`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Indicate whether you received helpful email support.
*   - **True**
*   - **False**
* **Phone Support** (`phone_support`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Indicate whether you received helpful phone support.
*   - **True**
*   - **False**
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, optional):
	+ Describe any issues or concerns you experienced with the helpdesk.

## Tips
- Be honest and objective when rating and answering the questions.
- Take a few minutes to review each question carefully.
- If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask the helpdesk team for guidance.
- Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our helpdesk service.
