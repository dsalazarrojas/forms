# Audit Scheduling Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Audit Scheduling Experience Survey" is designed to collect feedback on the effectiveness of the audit scheduling process. It aims to identify areas of improvement and gauge overall satisfaction with scheduling.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in all required fields.
3. If a question does not apply to your experience, select "N/A" or "Not Applicable".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (1): A text field where you can optionally input your name.
* **Email Address** (2): A text field for your contact information, optional.
* **Department** (3): A dropdown menu where you select your department.
* **Role Level** (4): A dropdown menu where you select your role level in the organization.
* **Survey Date** (5): A date field where you input the date of the survey.
* **Recent Audit Name** (6): A text field where you input the name of the recent audit you experienced.
* **Audit Scheduling Date** (7): A date field where you input when the audit was scheduled.
* **Scheduling Ease** (8): A dropdown menu where you rate how easy the scheduling process was.
* **Scheduling Timeline** (9): A dropdown menu where you select the length of time the scheduling took.
* **Communication Quality** (10): A dropdown menu where you rate the quality of scheduling communications.
* **Scheduling Flexibility** (11): A dropdown menu where you rate how flexible the scheduling process was.
* **Advance Notice** (12): A dropdown menu where you select how much advance notice you received.
* **Preferred Dates Accommodated** (13): A dropdown menu where you select how well your preferred dates were accommodated.
* **Scheduling Conflicts** (14): A dropdown menu where you rate the presence of scheduling conflicts.
* **Rescheduling Required** (15): A dropdown menu where you rate whether rescheduling was necessary.
* **Scheduling Tools** (16): A dropdown menu where you select the tools used for scheduling.
* **Tool Effectiveness** (17): A dropdown menu where you rate the effectiveness of the scheduling tools.
* **Information Clarity** (18): A dropdown menu where you rate the clarity of scheduling information.
* **Scheduling Improvements** (19): A text field where you input suggestions for improvements.
* **Overall Scheduling Satisfaction** (20): A dropdown menu where you rate overall satisfaction with scheduling.

Note: Fields marked as "required" must be filled out. Fields marked as "optional" can be left blank.
