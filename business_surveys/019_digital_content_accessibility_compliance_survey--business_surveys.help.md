# Digital Content Accessibility Compliance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Content Accessibility Compliance Survey is a form used to evaluate and track the accessibility of digital content submitted to the accessibility compliance team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the category for the digital content you are submitting.
2. Choose the type of digital content you are submitting.
3. Provide a brief description of the content.
4. Check the accessibility checklist to ensure the content meets the necessary accessibility standards.
5. Select the practices you have used to ensure accessibility.
6. Select the accessibility standards your content adheres to.
7. Enter the date of the last review and the date the content was last checked for accessibility.
8. Provide the name of the last reviewer.
9. Enter any comments or notes from the reviewer.
10. Add any additional accessibility comments.
11. Select the status of the form submission.
12. Enter your name and contact information as the submitter.
13. Enter the date of submission.
14. Enter your email address.
15. Enter your phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Category (1)**: Select the category for the digital content you are submitting.
  - This field allows you to specify the category of the digital content.
  - Please select the corresponding category number that matches your content type.
- **Category (2)**: Select the category for the digital content you are submitting (repeat of 1).
  - This is a repeat of the category field, please ensure you select the same category you selected in the previous field.
- **Form Type (2)**: Select the type of digital content you are submitting.
  - This field helps us determine the type of content you are submitting.
  - Please select the corresponding number that matches your content type.
- **Description (4)**: Enter a brief description of the digital content.
  - This field allows you to provide a brief summary of the content.
  - Please keep the description concise and relevant to the content.
- **Accessibility Checklist (5)**: Select the accessibility standards your content meets.
  - This field is a list of accessibility standards your content should meet.
  - Please select all the standards that apply to your content.
- **Accessibility Practice (6)**: Select the accessibility practices you have followed.
  - This field helps us understand how you ensured accessibility in your content.
  - Please select all the practices you have used.
- **Accessibility Standards (7)**: Select the accessibility standards your content adheres to.
  - This field is a list of accessibility standards your content should meet.
  - Please select all the standards that apply to your content.
- **Date of Last Review (8)**: Enter the date of the last review.
  - This field is used to track the date of the last review.
  - Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Last Review Date Checked (9)**: Enter the date the content was last checked for accessibility.
  - This field is used to track the date the content was last checked for accessibility.
  - Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Last Reviewer (10)**: Enter the name of the last reviewer.
  - This field is used to track the name of the last reviewer.
  - Please enter the name as it appears on their badge or ID.
- **Reviewer Comments (11)**: Enter any comments or notes from the reviewer.
  - This field is used to capture any comments or feedback from the reviewer.
  - Please enter any relevant information.
- **Accessibility Comments (12)**: Enter any additional accessibility comments.
  - This field is used to capture any additional accessibility comments.
  - Please enter any relevant information.
- **Form Submission Status (13)**: Select the status of the form submission.
  - This field is used to track the status of the form submission.
  - Please select the corresponding number that matches your current status.
- **Submitter (14)**: Enter your name as the submitter.
  - This field is used to track the submitter's name.
  - Please enter your name as it appears on your badge or ID.
- **Submit Date (15)**: Enter the date of submission.
  - This field is used to track the date of submission.
  - Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Submitter E-mail (16)**: Enter your email address.
  - This field is used to capture your email address.
  - Please enter your work email address.
- **Submitter Phone (17)**: Enter your phone number (optional).
  - This field is used to capture your phone number (optional).
  - Please enter your work phone number.
- **Reviewer Signature (18)**: This field is not applicable for this form.

Note: The date fields are in the format MM/DD/YYYY. Please ensure you enter the date in this format. If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please reach out to the accessibility team for guidance.
