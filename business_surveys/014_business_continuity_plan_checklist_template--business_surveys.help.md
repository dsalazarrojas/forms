# Business Continuity Plan Checklist Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is designed to help organizations assess and document their business continuity plans, ensuring they have a comprehensive plan in place to ensure continuity and minimize disruptions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Organization Information** page, which captures basic information about your organization.
2. Move on to the **Organization Name** field, where you'll enter the name of your organization.
3. Proceed to the **Assessment Date** field, where you'll select the date of the assessment.
4. Enter the name of the person leading the assessment in the **Assessment Coordinator** field.
5. Answer the questions in the **Governance and Planning** section, which includes questions on executive sponsorship, plan scope definition, risk assessment, and critical functions identification.
6. The **Recovery Capabilities** section captures information on data backup systems, alternate work facilities, recovery software, and vendor contact lists.
7. Move on to the **Communication and Training** section, where you'll answer questions on communication plans, staff awareness, and role assignments.
8. The **Documentation** section covers the status of plan documentation, distribution method, and version control.
9. Finally, fill out the **Testing and Maintenance** section, including questions on testing schedules and maintenance activities.
