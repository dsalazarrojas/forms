# Pros And Cons Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pros And Cons Evaluation Form is a tool used to gather feedback and opinions from users about a product or service. It aims to collect information about the key benefits and drawbacks of the product/service, the user's frequency of use, their opinion about the value for money, and their willingness to recommend it to others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each field carefully and fill in the required information.
2. Start with the first field "What are the key pros of the product/service?" and provide a brief description of the benefits you've experienced with the product/service.
3. Move on to the next field "What are the key cons of the product/service?" and describe the drawbacks you've encountered.
4. In the "How often do you think you will use this product/service?" field, select one of the options that best suits your frequency of use.
5. In the "What is your opinion about the value for money of the product/service?" field, select one of the options that best describes your opinion.
6. For the "Would you recommend this product/service to others?" field, select 'True' if you would recommend it, and 'False' otherwise.
7. If you'd like to provide additional comments or feedback, use the "Additional comments or feedback?" field.
8. You can also provide further comments or feedback in the "Do you have any other comments or feedback?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are the key pros of the product/service?** (`pros`, `text`, required): This field is where you provide a brief description of the benefits you've experienced with the product/service.
* **What are the key cons of the product/service?** (`cons`, `text`, required): This field is where you describe the drawbacks you've encountered with the product/service.
* **How often do you think you will use this product/service?** (`frequency`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the options that best suits your frequency of use.
* **What is your opinion about the value for money of the product/service?** (`value_for_money`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options that best describes your opinion about the value for money.
* **Would you recommend this product/service to others?** (`recommendation`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' if you would recommend it, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Would you choose this product/service again?** (`will_use_again`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' if you would choose it again, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Additional comments or feedback?** (`comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them here.
* **Do you have any other comments or feedback?** (`comments2`, `text`, optional): You can provide any other comments or feedback here.
* **Comments3** (`comments3`, `text`, optional): Please provide any other comments or feedback.
* **Comments4** (`comments4`, `text`, optional): Please provide any other comments or feedback.
* **Comments5** (`comments5`, `text`, optional): Please provide any other comments or feedback.

Note: The comments fields are not required, but they are useful if you have any additional information or feedback to share about the product/service.
