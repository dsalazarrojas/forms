# Animal Control Community Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Control Community Perception Survey is designed to collect feedback and opinions from residents about the effectiveness and quality of animal control services in their community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the name field (optional).
2. Enter your email address (optional).
3. Select the zip code that best corresponds to your area.
4. Read the survey introduction and continue with the survey.
5. Answer the questions honestly and accurately.
6. Select all that apply for multi-select questions.
7. Leave comments or suggestions in the "Do You Have Any Additional Feedback or Suggestions" field if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Name (Optional)
* **Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, optional): Your name or a name you'd like to be known by in the responses.

### 2. Email Address (Optional)
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, optional): Your email address for follow-up or additional information.

### 3. ZIP Code
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): The zip code that best represents your area.

### 4. Animal Control Community Perception Survey
* **Animal Control Community Perception Survey** (`survey_intro`, `note`, not required): A brief introduction to the survey.

### 5. How Long Have You Lived in This Community
* **How Long Have You Lived in This Community** (`residency_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the options to indicate how long you've lived in your area.

### 6. Do You Currently Own Pets?
* **Do You Currently Own Pets?** (`pet_ownership`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the options to indicate whether you have pets.

### 7. How Aware Are You of Animal Control Services?
* **How Aware Are You of Animal Control Services?** (`awareness_services`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the options to indicate your level of awareness.

### 8. Have You Ever Contacted Animal Control?
* **Have You Ever Contacted Animal Control?** (`contact_history`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the options to indicate if you have contacted animal control services.

### 9. If Yes, What Was the Reason
* **If Yes, What Was the Reason** (`contact_reason`, `text`, not required): Provide the reason for your contact.

### 10. How Satisfied Were You with the Response?
* **How Satisfied Were You with the Response?** (`response_satisfaction`, `select_one`, not required): Choose one of the options to indicate your satisfaction level.

### 11. How Would You Rate the Overall Quality of Animal Control Services?
* **How Would You Rate the Overall Quality of Animal Control Services?** (`service_quality`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the options to indicate your quality rating.

### 12. How Would You Rate Response Times?
* **How Would You Rate Response Times?** (`response_time`, `select_one`, not required): Choose one of the options to indicate your speed rating.

### 13. How Would You Rate Officer Professionalism?
* **How Would You Rate Officer Professionalism?** (`officer_professionalism`, `select_one`, not required): Choose one of the options to indicate your professionalism rating.

### 14. Do You Feel Animal Control Makes Your Community Safer?
* **Do You Feel Animal Control Makes Your Community Safer?** (`community_safety`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the options to indicate your safety perception.

### 15. What Animal-Related Issues Are Most Common in Your Area?
* **What Animal-Related Issues Are Most Common in Your Area?** (`common_issues`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply.

### 16. How Concerned Are You About Stray Animals in Your Neighborhood?
* **How Concerned Are You About Stray Animals in Your Neighborhood?** (`concern_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the options to indicate your concern level.

### 17. What Should Be the Top Priority for Animal Control?
* **What Should Be the Top Priority for Animal Control?** (`enforcement_priority`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the options to indicate your priority.

### 18. Which Programs Would You Support?
* **Which Programs Would You Support?** (`program_interest`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all that apply.

### 19. How Would You Prefer to Receive Information?
* **How Would You Prefer to Receive Information?** (`communication_preference`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all that apply.

### 20. Would You Be Interested in Volunteering?
* **Would You Be Interested in Volunteering?** (`willingness_volunteer`, `select_one`, not required): Choose one of the options to indicate your interest level.

### 21. Would You Support Increased Funding for Animal Control?
* **Would You Support Increased Funding for Animal Control?** (`funding_support`, `select_one`, not required): Choose one of the options to indicate your funding support.

### 22. Do You Have Any Additional Feedback or Suggestions?
* **Do You Have Any Additional Feedback or Suggestions?** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, not required): Provide your comments or suggestions.

### 23. Would You Be Willing to Participate in a Follow-up Interview?
* **Would You Be Willing to Participate in a Follow-up Interview?** (`follow_up_interest`, `select_one`, not required): Choose one of the options to indicate your willingness to participate in a follow-up interview.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and accurately.
* Select all that apply for multi-select questions.
* Leave comments or suggestions in the "Do You Have Any Additional Feedback or Suggestions" field if you have any.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
