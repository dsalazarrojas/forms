# franchise_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to measure the satisfaction of a franchise with a company's services or products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the franchise in the text field labeled "franchise_name".
2. Type in the email address of the franchise in the field labeled "email".
3. Provide the contact number of the franchise in the field labeled "phone_number".
4. Answer the questions on page 2, rating your satisfaction with the options provided.
5. Provide any additional feedback in the text field labeled "feedback" on page 2.
6. Select the appropriate date from the date picker.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **franchise_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the franchise.
* **email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the franchise.
* **phone_number** (text, required): Enter the contact number of the franchise.
* **feedback** (text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the franchise.
* **question1** (select_one, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the question "Very satisfied" to "Very dissatisfied".
* **question2** (select_one, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the question "Excellent" to "Poor".
* **question3** (select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply with the question "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", "Poor".
* **question4** (select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply with the question "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", "Poor".
* **date** (date, optional): Select the date.

Note: Fields marked as "required" must be completed, while fields marked as "optional" can be left blank.
