# Tenant Empowerment Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand your perceptions of our tenant empowerment efforts, including communication, renter ideas, and overall quality of service. Your input will help us improve our services and provide a better experience for all renters.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review each question below and select the options that best fit your experiences and opinions.
2. You can choose more than one option for questions that allow multiple selections.
3. Please provide as much detail as you can in the text fields (e.g., "renter_ideas" and "tenant_suggestions").

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **tenant_empowerment** (`tenant_empowerment`, select_multiple, required): How often do you feel empowered as a renter in our building? Please select all that apply: 
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **renter_ideas** (`renter_ideas`, text, optional): If you have any ideas or suggestions on how we can improve our service, please share them with us.
* **communication_satisfaction** (`communication_satisfaction`, number, optional): How would you rate your satisfaction with communication from our team?
	+ (Use a scale from 1 to 5, with 1 being very dissatisfied and 5 being very satisfied)
* **empowerment** (`empowerment`, select_one, optional): Which of the following statements best describes your level of empowerment as a renter?
	+ Renters have full knowledge about the building's maintenance schedule.
	+ Landlords provide a 24-hour contact phone number.
	+ Renters are able to communicate with landlords through multiple channels.
	+ None of the above
* **improvement_ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, text, optional): If you have any ideas on how we can improve our service, please share them with us.
* **housing_provider** (`housing_provider`, select_multiple, optional): Which of the following services do you feel are essential to your renting experience? 
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **tenant_satisfaction** (`tenant_satisfaction`, text, optional): Please describe your overall satisfaction with our service.
* **communication** (`communication`, select_one, optional): How satisfied are you with communication from our team?
	+ Very dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Very satisfied
* **suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions for us, please share them below.
* **ideas** (`ideas`, text, optional): Do you have any ideas for how we can improve our service?
