# Business Partner Accountability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help evaluate the performance and accountability of your business partners. It's essential to complete this survey to provide a comprehensive and accurate evaluation of your partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and email address in the respective fields.
2.  Enter the name of the company you represent.
3.  Enter the name of the partner company being evaluated.
4.  Select the duration of your relationship with the partner.
5.  Choose the type of partnership you have with the partner.
6.  Evaluate the reliability, communication, deliverable quality, and other attributes of your partner using the provided rating scales.
7.  Select all issues you have encountered with your partner.
8.  Rate the partner's ability to resolve issues.
9.  State whether you would continue the partnership and recommend the partner to others.
10.  Provide any additional feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): This is your name that will be associated with the survey responses.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Your Company** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.

## Tips

* Be honest and objective when filling out this survey.
* Select all issues you have encountered with your partner.
* Take your time when filling out this survey, as it's crucial for providing accurate and helpful feedback.
* If you are unsure about any of the ratings, please choose the option that best reflects your current experience.
* This survey is intended to be a helpful tool for both you and your partner. It's not meant to be used as a punishment or a way to criticize your partner.
