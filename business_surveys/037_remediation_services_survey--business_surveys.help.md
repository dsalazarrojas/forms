# Remediation Services Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from customers on their remediation services experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you are filling it out correctly.
3. If you are unsure about a field, you can refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select One Service** (`select_one_service`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of our services that you received. Selecting a service will help us understand which specific service you experienced.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached for follow-up questions or surveys.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for follow-up questions or surveys.
* **Select One Rating** (`select_one_rating`, select_one, required: false): Choose a rating for your overall experience (e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4).
* **Select Multiple Services** (`select_multiple_services`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose multiple services that you received. You can enter multiple values separated by commas (e.g., option 1, option 2, option 3).
* **Service Rating** (`service_rating`, number, required: false): Rate your experience with the selected service from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, number, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the selected service from 1 to 5, where 1 is dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Share any comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you received the remediation services.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you received the remediation services.
* **Select One1** (`select_one1`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the services that you received.
* **Select Multiple1** (`select_multiple1`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose multiple services that you received. You can enter multiple values separated by commas (e.g., option 1, option 2, option 3).
* **Select One2** (`select_one2`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the services that you received.
* **Select Multiple2** (`select_multiple2`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose multiple services that you received. You can enter multiple values separated by commas (e.g., option 1, option 2, option 3).
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Rating1** (`rating1`, text, required: false): Enter your rating for this experience.
* **Rating2** (`rating2`, text, required: false): Enter your rating for this experience.
* **Select One3** (`select_one3`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the services that you received.
* **Select Multiple3** (`select_multiple3`, select_multiple, required: false): This field does not exist in the form.

## Tips

* Please answer each field carefully and honestly.
* You can always refer back to this guide if you are unsure about any field.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact us.
