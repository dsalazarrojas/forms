# Social Promotion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey aims to gather information about a company's social media promotion activities, including the types of posts made, the engagement generated, and the duration of promotions. This form is designed to be completed by users who want to provide feedback on the company's social media promotion efforts.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2.  Select the correct options for each field based on your knowledge of the company's social media activities.
3.  Fill in the required fields accurately.
4.  Ensure that you provide clear and concise feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Social Promotion Survey** (`id: 1`, `name: social_promotion_survey`, `label: Social Promotion Survey`, `type: text`, `required: false`)
    \[Enter a brief description of the purpose of this form. For example: "This form aims to gather information about our company's social media promotion activities."]
*   **social media channels** (`id: 2`, `name: social_media_channels`, `label: social media channels`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`)
    \[Choose the social media channels that the company uses for promotion. Select all that apply. For example: Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, etc.]
*   **What were we posting** (`id: 3`, `name: social_media_channels_what_were_we_posting`, `label: What were we posting`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`)
    \[Choose one or more of the following options: Images, Videos, Links, Text, or Other. Select the one that best describes the content of the company's posts.]
*   **Why did you see us post** (`id: 4`, `name: social_media_channels_why`, `label: Why did you see us post`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`)
    \[Choose one or more of the following options: To promote a product, To engage with customers, To share news, or Other. Select the one that best describes the reason for the company's posts.]
*   **What were we doing** (`id: 5`, `name: social_media_channels_what_were_we_doing`, `label: What were we doing`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`)
    \[Choose the following options that best describe the company's actions on social media: Posting updates, Sharing news, Engaging with customers, Other. Select all that apply.]
*   **Why did we** (`id: 6`, `name: social_media_channels_why_we`, `label: Why did we`, `type: text`, `required: false`)
    \[Enter a brief description of why the company posts on social media. For example: "We post on social media to engage with customers and increase brand awareness."]
*   **Did we really post** (`id: 7`, `name: social_media_channels_did_we_really_post`, `label: Did we really post`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`)
    \[Choose one of the following options: Yes, No. Select Yes if the company has posted on social media recently.]
*   **Engagement** (`id: 8`, `name: engagement`, `label: Engagement`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`)
    \[Choose the following options that best describe the engagement generated by the company's social media activities: Comments, Likes, Shares, Other. Select all that apply.]
*   **Promotion posting frequency** (`id: 9`, `name: promotion_posting_frequency`, `label: Promotion posting frequency`, `type: text`, `required: false`)
    \[Enter the frequency of the company's social media promotion activities, for example: "Daily, Weekly, Monthly, etc."]
*   **Promotion posting time** (`id: 10`, `name: promotion_posting_time`, `label: Promotion posting time`, `type: date`, `required: false`)
    \[Enter the time of day when the company posts on social media, for example: "Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM."]
*   **Promotion posting duration** (`id: 11`, `name: promotion_posting_duration`, `label: Promotion posting duration`, `type: time`, `required: false`)
    \[Enter the duration of the company's social media posts, for example: "15 minutes to 1 hour."]
*   **Promotion posting days** (`id: 12`, `name: promotion_posting_days`, `label: Promotion posting days`, `type: date`, `required: false`)
    \[Enter the days of the week when the company posts on social media, for example: "Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, etc."]
*   **Comments** (`id: 13`, `name: comments`, `label: Comments`, `type: text`, `required: false`)
    \[Enter any additional comments about the company's social media activities. For example: "We post on social media to engage with customers and increase brand awareness."]

## Tips

*   This form is designed to gather information about the company's social media promotion activities.
*   Please answer each field accurately and clearly.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
*   Thank you for taking the time to complete this form. Your feedback is greatly appreciated.
