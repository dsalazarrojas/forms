# Diversity And Equity In Research Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather demographic information and suggestions from individuals on the topic of diversity and equity in research. Your participation in this survey is crucial in helping us create a more inclusive and equitable research environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and fill out the required fields.
2. Provide your demographic information, including your contact details.
3. Share any suggestions or comments you may have.
4. Confirm your submission by typing "I have read and agree to these terms."

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Header** (Note): This section is a placeholder for a note or introduction to the survey. You can ignore it, as it's not a required field.
* **Demographic Information** (`demographic_info`): This is the first section where you will provide your demographic information, such as your background, experiences, and perspectives.
* **Inclusion Questions** (`inclusion_questions`): Unfortunately, this field is empty. You can ignore it as it's not currently active.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`): Share any suggestions or comments you may have on diversity and equity in research. This will help us better understand your concerns and ideas.
* **Submission Confirmation** (`submission_confirmation`): Confirm that you have read and agree to these terms. Type "I have read and agree to these terms" to proceed.
* **Contact Information** (Email and Name): Provide your email address and name to help us contact you regarding your submission.
* **Institution Affiliation** (`contact_institution`): This field is currently not active. You can ignore it as it's not currently available for selection.
* **Additional Comments** (`submission`): If you have any additional comments or concerns regarding diversity and equity in research, please share them here.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed and honest as possible when sharing your demographic information and suggestions.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
