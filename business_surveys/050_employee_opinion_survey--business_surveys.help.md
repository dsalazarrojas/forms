# Employee Opinion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Opinion Survey is designed to gather feedback and opinions from employees about their work experience, department, and overall company performance. This survey is intended to help the management team understand employee concerns, suggestions, and ratings on various aspects of the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Email (if applicable).
3. Select your Job Title.
4. Choose your Department from the list.
5. Share your thoughts in the Opinion field.
6. Provide any Suggestion or Feedback you have.
7. Rate your overall satisfaction (optional).
8. Select a Date and Time of your completion (optional).
9. Provide any additional Comments (optional).
10. Enter the Name of the Company (optional).
11. Enter the Name of your Manager (optional).
