# Prairden Room Inventory Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Prairden Room Inventory Survey form is designed to collect information about a specific room in a garden, used for various gardening activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Garden Room Name in the text field.
2. Type in the Garden Room Address in the text field.
3. Select all the items that are currently in the room from the list of options (Lawn Mowers, Pruning Shears, Watering Cans, etc.).
4. Choose the condition of the room from the options (Good, Bad, Unknown).
5. Indicate if there is any equipment missing or not.
6. Choose if there is any equipment in the room that is damaged or not.
7. Enter any notes about the room in the text area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Garden Room Name (text)
Enter the name of the room in the garden.

### Garden Room Address (text)
Enter the address of the room in the garden.

### Items In Room (select multiple)
Select all the items that are currently in the room. This field allows you to list multiple items at once. Some options include:
* Lawn Mowers
* Pruning Shears
* Watering Cans

### Condition (select one)
Choose the condition of the room from the options:
* Good
* Bad
* Unknown

### Has Any Equipment Missing (select one)
Indicate if there is any equipment missing or not:
* Yes
* No
* Unknown

### Has Damaged Equipment (select one)
Indicate if there is any equipment in the room that is damaged or not:
* Yes
* No
* Unknown

### Garden Room Notes (note)
Enter any notes or comments about the room in this text area. This field is optional and can be used to add any additional information about the room.
