# Vision Statement Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a team's vision statement, which is a brief description of their goals, values, and mission. This form is used for internal purposes only and will help our team understand each team's objectives and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the team's name, description, and organization.
2. Select whether the team has a team leader.
3. Indicate the team's organizers (yes or no).
4. Choose the submit action (submit or save for later).
5. Optionally, provide the date and time of submission, and your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **team_leader** (text): Enter the name of the team leader, if applicable.
* **team_name** (text): Enter the name of the team.
* **team_description** (text): Enter a brief description of the team.
* **organization** (text): Enter the organization's name associated with the team.
* **team_organizers** (select multiple, yes or no): Check if the team has organizers. If yes, you can provide names.
* **submit_button** (select one, submit or save for later): Choose whether to submit the form or save it for later.
* **date_submitted** (date): Optionally, provide the date of submission.
* **time_submitted** (time): Optionally, provide the time of submission.
* **email_submitted** (email): Optionally, provide the email address of submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information to help our team understand each team's objectives.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are filled in.
* If you're saving the form for later, make sure to review and submit it before the end of the deadline.

Note: This help guide does not include fields `phone_submitted` as no explanation was possible without inventing unsupported business logic.
