# Migrant Workforce Rights Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gauge the level of awareness and understanding among migrant workers in a specific industry regarding their rights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions on each page:

- Introduction: Read through the instructions and understand the survey's purpose.
- migrant_workers_knowledge: Answer "Yes" if you are aware of your rights, "No" otherwise.
- rights: Select the option that best describes your understanding of your rights.
- migrant_rights: Describe the rights you are aware of.
- employer_responsibilities: Explain the responsibilities of your employer regarding your rights.
- employee_awareness: Select the option that best describes your employer's awareness of your rights.
- workplace_rights: Answer "Yes" if your workplace has clear policies on your rights, "No" otherwise.
- employee_consequences: Select the option that best describes the consequences of non-compliance with your rights.
- migrant_workers: Describe the number of migrant workers you are aware of.
- migrant_employees: Describe the number of migrant employees you know.
- employer_name: Enter the name of your employer.
- employer_phone: Enter the phone number of your employer.
- employer_email: Enter the email address of your employer.
- employer_address: Enter the address of your employer.
- employer_country: Select the country where your employer is located.
- employer_province: Select the province where your employer is located.
- employer_city: Enter the city of your employer.
- employer_state: Enter the state of your employer.
- employer_postcode: Enter the postcode of your employer.
- employer_website: Enter the website of your employer.
- date: Enter the date you completed this survey.
- time: Enter the time you completed this survey.
- notes: Add any additional comments or notes about your experience.
- submit: Once you have completed the survey, click this button to submit your responses.
