# Consulting Workflow Improvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from consultants on the current workflow, identifying areas of improvement, and suggestions for changes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely, providing your honest opinions and suggestions.
2. Select the department or team you belong to.
3. Estimate the time lost weekly due to bottlenecks.
4. Rate the efficiency of current tools.
5. Suggest a tool most in need of update.
6. Describe your idea for workflow improvement.
7. Assess the expected impact on productivity.
8. Indicate your interest in a pilot program to test new workflows.
9. Add any additional comments or suggestions.
10. Finally, fill in your submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name to ensure your feedback is attributed to you.
* **Department or Team** (`department`, select_one, required): Select your department or team from the options provided.
* **Primary Workflow Bottleneck** (`primary_workflow_bottleneck`, select_one, required): Identify the process that slows you down the most.
* **Estimated Time Lost Weekly (Hours)** (`estimated_time_lost_weekly`, number, required): Estimate the amount of time lost due to bottlenecks per week.
* **Efficiency of Current Tools** (`rating_tool_efficiency`, select_one, required): Rate how well current tools help you work.
* **Tool Most in Need of Update** (`tool_most_in_need_of_update`, text, required): Suggest the tool that needs improvement.
* **Workflow Improvement Idea** (`workflow_improvement_idea`, text, required): Describe your idea for improving the process.
* **Expected Impact on Productivity** (`expected_impact_on_productivity`, select_one, required): Assess the expected impact on productivity.
* **Interest in a Pilot Program** (`interest_in_pilot_program`, select_one, required): Indicate if you would be willing to test a new workflow.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any further comments or suggestions.
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`, date, required): Fill in today's date.
