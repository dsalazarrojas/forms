# Budget Forecasting Confidence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Budget Forecasting Confidence Survey is designed to gather information from respondents about their confidence in the current forecasting practices and procedures in their organization. This survey aims to help improve the accuracy and effectiveness of forecasts by identifying areas for improvement and potential challenges faced by respondents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Indicate your role within the organization.
4. Select your department.
5. Answer the questions about your confidence in the current forecasting accuracy, preferred forecasting time period, confidence in the methodology, confidence in data quality, and confidence in the forecasting technology.
6. Select any major challenges you are facing with forecasting (if applicable).
7. Choose how often your forecasts match actuals.
8. Specify the acceptable variance from your forecasts (if applicable).
9. Identify areas where you believe need improvement.
10. Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
11. Finally, indicate your overall confidence in budget forecasting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name (1)**: Enter your name as it appears on your organizational ID or business card.
* **Email Address (2)**: Enter your email address as it is registered with your organization.
* **Your Role (3)**: Select your role within the organization, e.g., Manager, Analyst, etc.
* **Department (4)**: Select the department you belong to within the organization.
* **Confidence in Current Forecasting Accuracy (5)**: Indicate your confidence in the accuracy of the current forecasting practices, ranging from Very Confident to Not Confident.
* **Preferred Forecasting Time Period (6)**: Select the preferred time period for your forecasts, which can be Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, or Annually.
* **Confidence in Forecasting Methodology (7)**: Indicate your confidence in the current forecasting methodology, ranging from Very Confident to Not Confident.
* **Confidence in Data Quality (8)**: Indicate your confidence in the accuracy and completeness of the data used for forecasting, ranging from Very Confident to Not Confident.
* **Major Forecasting Challenges (9)**: Select all the challenges you face with forecasting, such as Limited historical data, Market volatility, Inadequate tools, etc. (if applicable).
* **How Often Do Forecasts Match Actuals (10)**: Choose how often your forecasts match actuals, ranging from Consistently accurate to Never accurate.
* **Acceptable Variance from Forecast (11)**: Specify the acceptable percentage variance from your forecasts (if applicable).
* **Areas Needing Improvement (12)**: Select the top three areas you believe need improvement for forecasting, such as Data collection, Analysis tools, etc.
* **Adequacy of Forecasting Technology (13)**: Indicate the adequacy of the forecasting technology used, ranging from Very Adequate to Very Inadequate.
* **Training or Development Needs (14)**: If you have any training or development needs, type them in the space provided.
* **Overall Confidence in Budget Forecasting (15)**: Indicate your overall confidence in budget forecasting, ranging from Very High to Very Low.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions (16)**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the forecasting process.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by respondents who have experience with the forecasting process within their organization.
