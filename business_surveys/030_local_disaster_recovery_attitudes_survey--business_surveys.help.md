# Local Disaster Recovery Attitudes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information on local residents' attitudes and concerns regarding disaster recovery in their area.
## How To Complete This Form
1. The survey has several sections with questions that cover different aspects of disaster recovery.
2. Please answer each question according to your actual experiences and concerns.
3. You can select only one or multiple options for the select-type questions.
4. For the text-type question, provide a brief answer in the provided space.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How often do you think disasters occur in your local area?**: This question is about your personal experience or observation of natural disasters in your area.
* **In the event of a disaster, what role do you think local governments should play?**: Check one or multiple options that best describe your expectations. Please select "Yes" if you think local government should play that role, and "No" if you don't.
* **What is your biggest concern when it comes to disaster preparedness?**: Please provide a brief answer to this open-ended question.
* **What is your biggest concern when it comes to disaster recovery?**: Check one option that best describes your concern.
* **How do you think local government can best support disaster recovery in your community?**: Please provide a brief answer to this open-ended question.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
- If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us.
- The survey should take approximately 5-10 minutes to complete.
