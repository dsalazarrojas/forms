# Team Process Mapping Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Process Mapping Survey is designed to gather information about your team's performance and identify areas where improvement is needed. By answering the questions, you'll help your department to refine and optimize its processes, making it more efficient and effective.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go through each question and answer the ones that apply to you.
2. For the "process_steps" field, describe each step in your current process in as much detail as possible.
3. In "challenges", select all the pain points that hinder your team's performance.
4. In "improvement_ideas", share any ideas you have for improving your department.
5. In "team_leaders", select your team leader from the options.
6. In "department", select the department you belong to.
7. In "comments", you can provide any additional comments or thoughts you have.
8. Finally, read and acknowledge the submission confirmation page to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro_page`, note, required: false): This is the welcome page where you'll find more information about this survey.
* **Process Steps** (`process_steps`, text, required: false): Describe each step in your team's process in as much detail as possible.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the pain points that hinder your team's performance. These could be bottlenecks, inefficiencies, or difficulties that affect the team's overall performance.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, text, required: false): Share any ideas you have for improving your department. This is a great opportunity to provide innovative and practical suggestions.
* **Team Leaders** (`team_leaders`, select_one, required: false): Select your team leader from the options provided. This will help your team lead to better understand your perspective.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required: false): Select the department you belong to. This will help us to better understand the scope of the issues and opportunities for improvement.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or thoughts you have. This is an optional field, but it's a great way to add more context and depth to your responses.
* **Submission Confirmation** (`submission_confirmation`, note, required: false): This is the final page where you'll read and acknowledge that you've completed the form.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Share as much detail as you feel comfortable with.
* Don't worry if you're unsure or don't know the answers to some questions. Just answer what you can, and we'll take care of the rest.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
