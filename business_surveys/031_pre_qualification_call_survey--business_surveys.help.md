# Pre Qualification Call Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about potential customers or leads to assess their fit with our services or products.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately for each potential customer or lead.
2. Make sure to only fill out one survey per lead or individual.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant department or team member for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: This is the individual's first name.
* **Last Name**: This is the individual's last name.
* **Company**: This is the name of the company the individual is associated with.
* **Job Title**: This is the individual's job title or position.
* **Phone**: This is the individual's contact phone number.
* **Email**: This is the individual's contact email address. (Please ensure it is a valid email address)
* **Business Survey**: This is the type of survey the individual is interested in.
* **Best Contact Method**: This is the preferred method of contact for the individual (Phone, Email, or Social Media).
* **Lead Source**: This is how the individual came across our services or products (Referral, Trade Show, Online Search).
* **Company Size**: This is the size of the company the individual is associated with (Small, Medium, Large, Enterprise).
* **Industry**: This is the industry the company or individual is associated with (Healthcare, Finance, Technology).
* **Sales Stage**: This is the current stage of the lead (Lead, Qualified, Opportunity, Lost).
* **Lead Status**: This is the current status of the lead (New, Qualified, Qualified - Opportunity, Lost).
* **Follow-up Call**: This is whether a follow-up call is required (True, False, No Response).
* **Follow-up Email**: This is whether a follow-up email is required (True, False).
* **Lead Owner**: This is the individual or team member responsible for the lead (Sales, Marketing).
* **Follow-up Time**: This is the time at which follow-up is required (Format: HH:MM).
* **Follow-up Method**: This is the method of follow-up contact (Phone, Email, In-person).
* **Next Step**: This is the next action to be taken for the lead (Schedule Call, Send Email, Send Proposal, Follow up via Phone, Follow up via Email, No Follow up).
* **Next Step Time**: This is the time at which the next step is to be taken (Format: HH:MM).
* **Next Step Method**: This is the method of next step contact (Follow-up via Phone, Follow-up via Email).
* **Lead Value**: This is an optional field to capture a lead value.
* **Follow-up Frequency**: This is how often follow-up is required (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Follow-up Channel**: This is the channel to be used for follow-up (Phone, Email).
* **Lead Source Description**: This is an optional field to capture a lead source description. 

Please note that this form is designed to be completed for each potential customer or lead, and the information collected will be used to assess their fit with our services or products. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
