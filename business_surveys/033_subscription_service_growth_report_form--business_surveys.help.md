# subscription_service_growth_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The subscription_service_growth_report_form is designed to collect data on the growth and engagement of a subscription-based service. This form will help administrators understand the overall performance of their service, enabling them to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability.
2.  Each question is optional, unless marked as required.
3.  You can submit the completed form to generate a report on subscription service growth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Engagement** (`1`, number, required: `false`): Enter the level of engagement of users with your service.
	+ This field is intended to capture data on the level of user interaction with your service.
* **Total Users** (`2`, number, required: `false`): Enter the total number of users currently subscribed to your service.
	+ This field is intended to capture the total number of users currently using your service.
* **Subscription Growth** (`3`, number, required: `false`): Enter the growth rate of subscriptions to your service.
	+ This field is intended to capture data on the increase or decrease in subscription numbers over time.
* **Subscription Churn** (`4`, number, required: `false`): Enter the churn rate of subscriptions to your service.
	+ This field is intended to capture data on the proportion of users who have unsubscribed from your service.
