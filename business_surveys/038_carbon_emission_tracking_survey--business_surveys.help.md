# Carbon Emission Tracking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on carbon emissions tracking for an organization. The purpose of this form is to gather information about an individual's energy consumption, transportation habits, and overall sustainability awareness level.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the required fields (marked as required).
3. Select the appropriate options for each question.
4. Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your name** (`name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you if needed.
* **Department or area** (`department`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your department or area of work, if applicable.
* **Survey completion date** (`survey_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date when you completed this form.
* **Tracking period** (`tracking_period`, `select_one`, **required**): Select how often you track your energy consumption:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Annually
* **Monthly electricity usage (kWh)** (`energy_consumption`, `number`, **optional**): Enter your monthly electricity usage in kilowatt-hours (kWh).
* **Monthly natural gas usage (therms)** (`natural_gas_usage`, `number`, **optional**): Enter your monthly natural gas usage in therms.
* **Primary heating fuel type** (`heating_fuel`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select your primary heating fuel type:
	+ Electricity
	+ Natural Gas
	+ Oil
	+ Heat Pump
	+ Other
	+ Not Applicable
* **Heating system efficiency efforts** (`heating_efficiency`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select all that apply:
	+ Programmable Thermostat
	+ Insulation Improvements
	+ Weatherization
	+ Regular Maintenance
	+ Smart Controls
* **Primary commute method** (`commute_method`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your primary way to commute:
	+ Personal Vehicle
	+ Carpool
	+ Public Transit
	+ Bike
	+ Walk
	+ Work From Home
	+ Other
* **Commute distance (miles one way)** (`commute_distance`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the distance of your daily commute in miles.
* **Commute frequency per week** (`commute_frequency`, `number`, **optional**): Enter how often you commute per week.
* **Vehicle type (if personal vehicle)** (`vehicle_type`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select the type of vehicle you use for your commute:
	+ Sedan
	+ SUV
	+ Truck
	+ Hybrid
	+ Electric
	+ Motorcycle
	+ Other
* **Vehicle fuel efficiency (MPG if known)** (`vehicle_fuel_efficiency`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the fuel efficiency of your vehicle in miles per gallon (MPG).
* **Business travel - flights per month** (`business_travel`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the number of flights you take per month for business travel.
* **Monthly water usage (gallons)** (`water_usage`, `number`, **optional**): Enter your monthly water usage in gallons.
* **Weekly waste generation (pounds)** (`waste_generated`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the amount of waste you generate per week in pounds.
* **Do you participate in recycling?** (`recycling_participation`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select how often you participate in recycling:
	+ Yes - Regularly
	+ Yes - Sometimes
	+ False
	+ Not Available
* **Waste reduction efforts (select all that apply)** (`waste_reduction_efforts`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select all that apply:
	+ Reduce Consumption
	+ Reuse Items
	+ Recycle Materials
	+ Compost
	+ Use Reusable Products
	+ Minimize Packaging
* **Overall sustainability awareness level** (`overall_sustainability`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select your level of sustainability awareness:
	+ Very Aware
	+ Aware
	+ Somewhat Aware
	+ Not Aware
* **Suggestions for reducing carbon emissions in our organization** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any suggestions you have for reducing carbon emissions in our organization.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your supervisor or manager for guidance.
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please enter them in the "Suggestions for reducing carbon emissions in our organization" field.
