# internal_systems_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The internal systems feedback survey is a tool for gathering feedback from employees about the internal systems used in the organization. This survey aims to understand the overall satisfaction and effectiveness of these systems, which will help the management team to identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the employee who has access to the internal systems. (Required)
2. Select whether or not the current tool is used for this tool. (Required)
3. Provide a brief feedback about the current tool. (Required)
4. Enter any comments or suggestions you may have about the current tool. (Required)
5. Click the "Submit" button to send your response. (Optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee**: Select the employee who has access to the internal systems. This is a required field to ensure that the feedback is attributed to the correct person.
* **Tool name**: Select whether or not the current tool is used for this tool. This field helps to understand which tools are being used and which ones are not.
* **Feedback**: Provide a brief feedback about the current tool. This field is where you can express your opinion about the tool's effectiveness, usability, or any other aspect that you think is relevant.
* **Comment**: Enter any comments or suggestions you may have about the current tool. This field is a place for more detailed feedback or suggestions that you may not have time to express in the "Feedback" field.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send your response and submit your feedback.
