# Data Analytics Utilization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on your organization's use and perception of data analytics tools and practices. The insights gathered will help us better understand how you utilize data and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Go through each page and answer the questions as accurately and honestly as possible.
2. For each question, select the best response that reflects your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tools Used** (`data_analytics_tools_used`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question asks about the data analytics tools you use. Choose all the tools that apply to your situation.
    * If you select "Yes" for any tool, indicate which tools are being used.
* **Most Liked** (`tools_liked`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about the most liked data analytics tool used. If you do not like any tool, select "No".
* **Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, `text`, required: false): Describe any challenges you faced while using data analytics tools or data practices.
* **Benefits Realized** (`benefits_realized`, `text`, required: false): Describe the benefits you have realized from using data analytics tools or data practices.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the size of your team. Choose from the options: Small, Medium, Large, or Other.
* **Team Location** (`team_location`, `select_one`, required: false): Describe the location of your team. Choose from the options: North, South, East, West, or Other.
* **Team Department** (`team_department`, `select_one`, required: false): Describe the department your team belongs to. Choose from the options: Sales, Marketing, Other.
* **Data Source** (`data_source`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate your data source. Choose from the options: Google Drive, Google Sheets, Other.
* **Most Used Analytics Tool** (`analytics_tool_used`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks about the most used analytics tool. If you do not use any analytics tool, select "No".
* **How Often Challenges Occur** (`challenge_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate how often you face challenges while using data analytics tools or practices. Choose from the options: Rarely, Occasionally, Frequently, Always.
* **Impact On Team** (`challenge_impact`, `select_one`, required: false): Assess the impact of challenges on your team. Choose from the options: High, Low, None.
* **Benefit Awareness** (`benefit_awareness`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate your level of awareness of the benefits of data analytics. Choose from the options: High, Low, None.
* **Tool Satisfaction** (`tool_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the tools used. Choose from the options: Highly, Somewhat, Not At All.
* **Team Communication** (`team_communication`, `text`, required: false): Describe the level of communication within your team related to data analytics practices.
* **Manager Support** (`manager_support`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the level of support you receive from your manager on data analytics practices. Choose from the options: High, Low, None.
* **Data Quality** (`data_quality`, `select_one`, required: false): Evaluate the quality of the data used in your analytics practices. Choose from the options: High, Low, None.
* **Data Security** (`data_security`, `select_one`, required: false): Assess the security of your data practices. Choose from the options: High, Low, None.
* **Data Privacy** (`data_privacy`, `select_one`, required: false): Evaluate the level of data privacy in your organization. Choose from the options: High, Low, None.
* **Data Accuracy** (`data_accuracy`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the accuracy of your data analytics practices. Choose from the options: High, Low, None.
* **Team Implementation** (`team_implementation`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate how well your team is implementing data analytics practices. Choose from the options: High, Low, None.
* **Data Utilization** (`data_utilization`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate how well your organization utilizes data analytics. Choose from the options: Highly, Somewhat, Not At All.
* **Team Involvement** (`team_involvement`, `select_one`, required: false): Assess the level of team involvement in data analytics practices. Choose from the options: High, Low, None.
* **Data Training** (`data_training`, `select_one`, required: false): Evaluate the level of training received on data analytics practices. Choose from the options: High, Low, None.
* **Data Adoption** (`data_adoption`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the level of data adoption in your organization. Choose from the options: High, Low, None.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with data analytics practices. Choose from the options: High, Low, None.

## Tips
* Please answer questions accurately and honestly to ensure we get a clear picture of your situation.
* If you are unsure about any question, select the option that best represents your situation.
* This form is designed to be as straightforward as possible. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
