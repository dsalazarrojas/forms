# Productivity App Recommendation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your opinions and preferences on productivity apps, tools, and features. Your input will help us understand your habits and needs, which can inform our product development to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the tools you currently use for work from the list on Page 1.
2. Select your preferred tool for each task on Page 2.
3. Choose your preferred device for work on Page 3.
4. Select the features that are most important to you on Page 4.
5. If you don't see your preferred tool, device, or feature listed, select 'Other (specify)' and provide a brief description in the 'Other (please specify)' field on Page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What tools do you use for work?** (Page 1)
	+ This question asks you to select all the tools you currently use for work, such as email, chat, phone, collaboration tools, or others not listed.
* **Tool 1, 2, and 3** (Page 2)
	+ These questions ask you to select your preferred tool for each specific task. For example, Task Management and Productivity are listed as options, which could mean you prefer Asana or Trello over Microsoft Planner. 
* **What device do you use?** (Page 3)
	+ This question asks you to select your preferred device for work, such as a mobile, desktop, tablet, or laptop.
* **Mobile, Device 2, and Device 3** (Page 4)
	+ These questions ask you to select your preferred device, but be aware that 'Device 2' and 'Device 3' are not explicitly labeled, potentially leading to confusion.
* **Which feature do you find most important?** (Page 5)
	+ This question asks you to select up to three features that are most important to you, such as Task Management, Productivity, Team Collaboration, Time Management, or Focus.
* **Feature 1, 2, 3, and 6** (Page 5)
	+ These questions ask you to select your preferred features, with some options not explicitly explained. 
* **Other (please specify)** (Page 5)
	+ If you don't see your preferred tool, device, or feature listed, select 'Other (please specify)' and provide a brief description in this field.
* **Tool 5, 7, Device 5, and Feature 9** (Page 5)
	+ These questions seem to be duplicates of previously asked questions, potentially leading to redundancy.
* **What is your preferred tool?** (Page 6)
	+ This question asks you to select your preferred tool from a list, but the tool names are not explicitly defined.
* **What is your preferred device?** (Page 6)
	+ This question asks you to select your preferred device from a list, but the device names are not explicitly defined.
* **What is your preferred feature?** (Page 6)
	+ This question asks you to select your preferred feature from a list, but the feature names are not explicitly defined.
* **Other (please specify)** (Page 6)
	+ Similar to previous question, if you don't see your preferred tool, device, or feature listed, select 'Other (please specify)' and provide a brief description in this field.

## Tips
- Please answer each question truthfully to help us better understand your preferences.
- If you're unsure about a question, select 'Other (specify)' and provide a brief description.
- You can select more than one option in 'What tools do you use for work?' and 'Which feature do you find most important?' fields. 

Note: I've tried to address redundancy and potential confusion by grouping similar questions together and rephrasing them for clarity. I've also provided clear instructions for completion. Please review and adjust as needed to ensure the guide is comprehensive and clear for end-users.
