# Onion Shed Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Onion Shed Management Survey is a tool to gather information from farmers about the management practices and challenges faced by their onion sheds. This survey aims to help farmers identify areas of improvement and provide insights for targeted support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form to report on your onion shed's management practices and challenges.
2. Start with the introduction page, which provides background information about the survey.
3. On the "Shed Details" page, provide a brief description of your shed, including its name, location, and size.
4. On the "Shed Capacity" page, report your shed's capacity, which is the total amount of onions that can be stored in your shed.
5. On the "Storage Challenges" page, select all the challenges you are currently facing with storage, such as pests, mold, or water damage.
6. On the "Storage Conditions" page, select the storage conditions of your shed, such as good, fair, or poor.
7. On the "Practices" page, select all the practices you have implemented to manage your onions, such as temperature control, humidity control, and cleaning and disinfection.
8. On the "Training Hours" page, report the total number of hours spent on training related to onion management.
9. On the "Location" page, provide the location of your shed, including city, state, and zip code.
10. On the "Contact Person" page, provide your name and contact information, including phone number and email.
11. On the "Comments" page, add any additional comments or notes about your onion shed.
12. Finally, on the "Location Coordinates" page, provide the coordinates of your shed.
