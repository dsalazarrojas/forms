# Building Technology Upgrade Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the current state of your building's technology infrastructure and identify potential upgrade priorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question to the best of your ability, being as detailed as possible.
2.  Make sure to answer the required questions first before proceeding to the non-required ones.
3.  For questions that require more than one answer, use the provided options to select all that apply.
4.  Be honest and provide accurate information about your current systems and needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Date**: This is where you enter the date and time you are completing this survey. Please use the provided date picker to select the correct date.
* **Your Name**: This field is for your full name, which will help us know who is answering this survey.
* **Email Address**: This is your contact email, which will be used for follow-up questions or to provide additional information.
* **Your Role**: This field is for your job title or position within the building or organization.
* **Building Location**: This is where you enter the location of the building you are answering this survey for.
* **Department**: This field is for your department or team name.
* **Current Technology Infrastructure**: This is a description of the current systems and tools used in the building.
* **Age of Current Systems**: This field asks about the age of the current systems, which will help us understand their condition and potential need for upgrades.
* **Satisfaction With Current Systems**: This question assesses your overall satisfaction with the current systems.
* **Current System Problems**: This field is for any issues or problems you are experiencing with the current systems.
* **Upgrade Priorities**: This question allows you to select all the upgrade priorities that are most important to you.
* **Top Priority Upgrade**: This field is for the most important upgrade you are currently considering.
* **Interest in Smart Building Technology**: This question gauges your interest in smart building technology, including IoT and automation.
* **Cybersecurity Concerns**: This field assesses how important cybersecurity is to you.
* **Budget Available for Upgrades**: This question helps us understand your estimated budget for upgrades.
* **Desired Timeline for Upgrades**: This field asks about your desired timeline for implementing upgrades.
* **Anticipated Implementation Challenges**: This field is for any challenges or obstacles you anticipate during the implementation process.
* **Staff Training Required**: This question assesses the level of training required for staff.
* **Vendor or Technology Preference**: This field is for any preferred vendor or technology.
* **System Integration Requirements**: This question gauges the importance of system integration.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide detailed and accurate information about your current systems and needs.
- If you are unsure about any question, please answer to the best of your ability or mark "Unsure" in the relevant field.
- If you have any additional comments or questions, please include them in the "Current System Problems" or "Anticipated Implementation Challenges" fields.
