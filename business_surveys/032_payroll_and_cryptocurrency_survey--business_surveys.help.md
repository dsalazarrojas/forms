# Payroll And Cryptocurrency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about payroll and cryptocurrency-related topics.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the instructions carefully and fill out the form honestly.
- Each field is optional, so feel free to skip fields that don't apply to you.
- Type or paste your answers in the fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Payroll preferences** (`payroll_preferences`, text, Optional): This field is for you to express your thoughts on your current payroll preferences. Type or paste your answer in the field.
- **Crypto payments** (`crypto_payments`, text, Optional): This field is for you to share your experience or opinion on cryptocurrency payments. Type or paste your answer in the field.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, Optional): This field is for you to add any additional comments or thoughts you have about the topic. You can write as much or as little as you'd like.
 
## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
