# Arduino And IoT Usage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your experience and usage of Arduino and IoT (Internet of Things) technologies. The survey aims to collect data on your background as an individual, your involvement with Arduino and IoT, and your future plans for these technologies. Your input will help us better understand the current state of Arduino and IoT usage and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your personal information, including your name and email address.
2. Provide your job title or role and the name of your company or organization.
3. Choose your industry sector from the provided options.
4. Answer the questions regarding your experience with Arduino and IoT, including whether you currently use Arduino boards, your level of experience, and which boards you have used.
5. Select the primary IoT applications you work with and the connectivity technologies you use.
6. Indicate your main challenges with Arduino and IoT projects and the average project budget.
7. Let us know about your future plans to expand your Arduino and IoT usage.
8. Finally, share any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Enter your name to identify yourself in the survey.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, required): Provide a valid email address where you can be contacted for any follow-up questions or to receive survey results.
* **Job Title or Role** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or role to provide more context about your involvement with Arduino and IoT.
* **Company or Organization** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company or organization where you work with Arduino and IoT.
* **Industry Sector** (`industry`, select_one, required): Choose the sector where you work with Arduino and IoT from the provided options (e.g., Manufacturing, Healthcare, etc.).
* **Do you currently use Arduino or Arduino-compatible boards?** (`use_arduino_currently`, select_one, required): Answer whether you actively use Arduino boards, use them irregularly, have used them in the past, or have never used them.
* **Experience Level with Arduino** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Indicate your level of experience with Arduino, from Beginner to Expert.
* **Which Arduino boards have you used?** (`arduino_boards_used`, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of Arduino boards you have used.
* **Primary IoT applications you work with** (`iot_applications`, select_multiple, required): Choose the main applications you work with that involve IoT.
* **Connectivity technologies you use** (`connectivity_technologies`, select_multiple, optional): Select the connectivity technologies you use for your IoT projects.
* **Main challenges with Arduino and IoT projects** (`main_challenges`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the main challenges you face with Arduino and IoT projects.
* **Average project budget** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Provide an estimate of the average project budget for your Arduino and IoT projects.
* **Do you plan to expand Arduino/IoT usage?** (`expansion_plans`, select_one, required): Answer whether you plan to significantly expand, moderately expand, do not have plans, or are evaluating options.
* **What improvements would help your projects?** (`desired_improvements`, text, optional): Share any specific improvements you need for your Arduino and IoT projects.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any other relevant information or feedback you have about Arduino and IoT.

**Tips:**

* Please answer each question to the best of your knowledge and experience.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to provide an explanation or ask for clarification.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the "Additional Comments or Feedback" field to share them.
