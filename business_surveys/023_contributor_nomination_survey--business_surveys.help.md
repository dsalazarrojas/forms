# Contributor Nomination Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for nominating a deserving individual for a prestigious award. The form gathers details about the nominee, including their name, contact information, and achievements, as well as the nominator's relationship with the nominee and their reasons for the nomination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter the nominee's information, including their full name, email address, and contact number.
3. Select the award category for which you are nominating the person.
4. Provide a brief reason for nominating the individual.
5. Describe the nominee's key contributions and the impact of their work.
6. Specify the period during which the nominee made their contributions.
7. If applicable, provide a specific date of achievement.
8. List any supporting documents you are including with the nomination.
9. Indicate whether references are available and provide their names and contact information.
10. Select your relationship with the nominee and how long you have known them.
11. Confirm whether the nominee has consented to public recognition.
12. If the nominee has been nominated before, indicate whether or not.
13. Finally, provide any additional comments or information about the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed if you are recognized as a nominator.
* **Your Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for more information.
* **Your Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Nominee Full Name**: Enter the name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Email**: Enter the email address of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Organization**: Enter the company or institution the person is affiliated with.
* **Nominee Job Title**: Enter the person's position or role.
* **Award Category**: Select the category for which you are nominating the person (e.g., Outstanding Contribution, Innovation Award, Leadership Excellence).
* **Reason for Nomination**: Describe why you are nominating the individual.
* **Key Contributions**: Describe the nominee's main achievements.
* **Impact of Their Work**: Explain how the nominee's work has made a difference.
* **Period of Contribution**: Specify the time frame during which the nominee made their contributions.
* **Specific Date of Achievement**: If applicable, provide a specific date of achievement.
* **Supporting Documents**: List any documents you are including with the nomination.
* **References Available**: Indicate whether references are available to support the nomination.
* **Reference 1 Name**: If references are available, enter their name.
* **Reference 1 Contact**: Enter their email or phone number.
* **Reference 2 Name**: If additional references are available, enter their name.
* **Reference 2 Contact**: Enter their email or phone number.
* **Your Relationship to Nominee**: Select how you know the person (e.g., colleague, supervisor, direct report, client, partner, community member, other).
* **How Long Have You Known Them**: Select how long you have known the person.
* **Nominee Consent to Public Recognition**: Confirm whether the nominee has consented to public recognition.
* **Has This Person Been Nominated Before**: Indicate whether the person has been nominated before.
* **Permission to Follow Up**: Confirm whether you are available to be contacted for more information.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about the nomination.

This guide should help you complete the form efficiently and accurately. If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
