# Militarism And Conflict Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experiences and opinions on militarism and conflict. It is intended to help researchers understand the nature of conflict in your community and how to effectively resolve it. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, and feel free to skip questions that do not apply to you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and provide your honest answer.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. If a question does not apply to you, simply leave it blank and move on.
4. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you are comfortable sharing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional):
	+ This is a general question to help us get a better understanding of your background and experiences.
* **Have you ever had an experience with conflict in your community** (`question_1`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select one of the options that best describes your experience with conflict in your community.
* **Which of the following statements best describes your attitude towards armed conflict** (`question_2`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ This question is asking about your general attitude towards armed conflict. Select all that apply.
* **How do you think peaceful conflict resolution is best achieved in your community** (`question_3`, text, optional):
	+ Please provide a detailed explanation of how you think conflict resolution should be achieved in your community.
* **Can you tell us more about your experience with armed conflict** (`question_4`, text, optional):
	+ This is an opportunity to share more about your experiences with armed conflict, if you feel comfortable doing so.
* **Do you have any other comments or concerns about armed conflict** (`question_5`, text, optional):
	+ If you have any other thoughts or concerns that you would like to share, please include them here.
