# Advisory Partnership Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to evaluate the performance of a partnership or collaboration by assessing its various aspects. It helps in identifying areas of strength and weakness, providing valuable feedback to the partner and improving future collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer all questions honestly, and make sure to provide clear and concise answers.
2.  The form consists of several sections: Partnership Information, Performance Evaluation, Future Intent, and Open Feedback.
3.  Start by filling out the Partnership Information section, providing details about the partner organization and your role.
4.  Then, evaluate the partner's performance across different aspects, rating them as Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
5.  Finally, provide open feedback and comments about the partnership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Partner Organization Name** (`partner_name`, text, required): This field is to identify the partner organization you are evaluating. Please enter the name of the organization.
*   **Your Role** (`evaluator_role`, text, required): This field is to identify your role within the partnership. Please describe your position or responsibility within the partnership.
*   **Duration of Partnership** (`partnership_duration`, select_one, required): This field is to determine the length of the partnership. Choose from Less than 6 months, 6-12 months, 1-3 years, or Over 3 years.
*   **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, required): This field evaluates how effectively the partner communicated with you. Rate their communication as Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
*   **Alignment with Goals** (`goal_alignment`, select_one, required): This field assesses if the partner's goals aligned with yours. Rate them as Completely Aligned, Mostly Aligned, Somewhat Aligned, Misaligned, or Not Aligned at all.
*   **Value Delivered** (`value_delivery`, select_one, required): This field evaluates if the partner delivered the expected value. Rate them as Exceeded Expectations, Met Expectations, Below Expectations, or Not Met Expectations.
*   **Responsiveness to Issues** (`responsiveness`, select_one, required): This field assesses how well the partner handles issues or problems. Rate them as Very Responsive, Responsive, Slow to Respond, or Unresponsive.
*   **Future Intent** (`renew_partnership`, select_one, required): This field evaluates your likelihood to renew the partnership. Rate yourself as Definitely, Probably, Unsure, Probably Not, or Definitely Not.
*   **Additional Comments** (`open_feedback`, text, required): This field is for any specific comments or areas of improvement you have for the partner. Please provide open-ended feedback.

## Tips

*   Make sure to answer all the required fields truthfully.
*   Be as specific as possible with your answers.
*   Use the open feedback field to provide detailed comments about the partner's strengths and weaknesses.
