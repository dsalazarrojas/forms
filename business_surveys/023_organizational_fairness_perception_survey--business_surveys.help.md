# Organizational Fairness Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to gauge the fairness perception of the organization from an employee's perspective. It collects data on employee opinions, experiences, and perceptions regarding fairness in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by answering the questions on the "Employee Feedback" page (Page 1).
2. Move on to the "Demographic Information" page (Page 2), where you will provide your basic demographic details.
3. On the "Fair Scales" page (Page 3), you will be presented with a set of scales, and for each scale, you will provide a response.
4. On the "Response" pages (Pages 4-6), you will elaborate on your responses to the scales.
5. Finally, on the "Organizational Fairness" page (Page 7), you will provide an additional response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Feedback** (Page 1):
	+ This section is where you will share your thoughts and opinions about your overall experience with the organization.
	+ It is not a required field, but providing your honest feedback is encouraged to help improve the organization.
* **Demographic Information** (Page 2):
	+ This section collects your basic demographic details, such as your name, department, job title, and years of service.
	+ Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Fair Scales** (Page 3):
	+ The Fair Scales section presents a set of questions with a scale format (e.g., "Very Unfair", "Fair", "Very Fair").
	+ Choose the scale that best represents your opinion.
* **Response** (Pages 4-6):
	+ These pages are for elaborating on your responses to the scales.
	+ Be as detailed as possible in your explanation.
* **Organizational Fairness** (Page 7):
	+ In this final section, provide an additional response to the overall fairness of the organization.
	+ This is an opportunity to share any final thoughts or opinions.
