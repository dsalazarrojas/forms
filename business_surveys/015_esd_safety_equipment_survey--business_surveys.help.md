# ESD Safety Equipment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ESD Safety Equipment Survey is designed to gather information about safety equipment and gear used in various environments. This data will be used to improve safety protocols and procedures within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1.  Read through each page carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2.  For each question, provide the requested information in the corresponding field.
3.  Be as accurate and truthful as possible when answering.
4.  If you are unsure about any question, ask for clarification or assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): Start by providing a brief introduction or background information about yourself or your work environment.
*   **Safety Equipment Controlled** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe any safety equipment that is under strict control, such as equipment that is subject to strict usage and maintenance protocols.
*   **Frequency of Controlled Equipment** (`3`, `time`, required: `true`): Provide the frequency at which you use controlled equipment. This could be daily, weekly, monthly, or never.
*   **Safety Gear** (`4`, `select_one`, required: `true`): Answer whether or not you have any safety gear available.
*   **Frequency of Safety Gear** (`5`, `time`, required: `true`): Provide the frequency at which you use safety gear.
*   **Location of Controlled Equipment** (`6`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe the location where controlled equipment is kept.
*   **Location of Safety Gear** (`7`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe the location where safety gear is kept.
*   **Frequencies of Controlled Equipment** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): Answer whether or not you use controlled equipment for 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Other Safety Equipment Frequency** (`9`, `text`, required: `false`): If you have any other frequency for controlled equipment not listed, describe it here.
*   **Other Controlled Equipment** (`10`, `text`, required: `false`): If you have any other controlled equipment not listed, describe it here.

## Tips
*   Please be as accurate as possible when answering.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, ask for clarification or assistance.
*   This form is designed to be completed quickly and efficiently, so please don't hesitate to ask if you need help.
