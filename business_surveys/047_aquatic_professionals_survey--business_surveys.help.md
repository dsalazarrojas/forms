# Aquatic Professionals Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from aquatic professionals about their professional experiences, challenges, and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Read each field carefully and answer accurately.
*   Provide detailed responses in the text fields.
*   Select only one primary profession that best describes your work.
*   List all relevant main challenges faced in the profession.
*   Choose one industry trend that affects your work the most.
*   Select your technology adoption level.
*   Provide suggestions for industry improvement.
*   Enter your years of experience in the field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (Full Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your professional email address.
*   **Facility Name** (Facility Name, text, required): Enter the name of the facility where you work.
*   **Job Title** (Job Title, text, required): Enter your job title or position in the facility.
*   **Primary Profession** (Primary Profession, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your primary profession.
*   **Main Challenges You Face** (Main Challenges You Face, select_multiple, optional): List all relevant main challenges faced in the profession.
*   **Industry Trends Affecting Your Work** (Industry Trends Affecting Your Work, text, optional): Describe the industry trend that affects your work the most.
*   **Technology Adoption Level** (Technology Adoption Level, select_one, optional): Select your technology adoption level.
*   **Training or Development Needs** (Training or Development Needs, text, optional): Provide suggestions for training or development needs.
*   **Suggestions for Industry Improvement** (Suggestions for Industry Improvement, text, optional): Offer suggestions for industry improvement.
*   **Years of Experience in Field** (Years of Experience in Field, number, optional): Enter your years of experience in the aquatic field.
