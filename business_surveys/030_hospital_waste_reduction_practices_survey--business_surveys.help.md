# Hospital Waste Reduction Practices Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to collect information from hospital staff on their current practices and suggestions for reducing waste in the hospital. This will help identify areas of improvement and provide insights for future waste reduction initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page carefully. You will be asked to provide information on current waste reduction efforts, barriers to these efforts, and suggestions for improvement. You can type your answers in the provided text fields.

* **Current Barriers** (`current_barriers`, text, required/optional): Identify any barriers you face in implementing waste reduction efforts in the hospital.
