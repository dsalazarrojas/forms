# Avocado Consumption Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your avocado consumption habits, preferences, and purchasing behaviors. Your anonymous data will help us understand consumer insights for market research.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. Answer all questions to the best of your ability.
3. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, `true`)
Full name is your name as you want to be known in the context of this survey.

### 2. **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, `true`)
Your primary email address for contact purposes.

### 3. **Age Group** (`age_group`, `select_one`, `true`)
Please select the age group that best represents you:

  * Under 18
  * 18 - 24
  * 25 - 34
  * 35 - 44
  * 45 - 54
  * 55 or older

### 4. **Gender** (`gender_id`, `select_one`, `true`)
Please select the option that best describes your gender:

  * Female
  * Male
  * Non-binary
  * Prefer not to say

### 5. **How Often Do You Eat Avocados?** (`consumption_frequency`, `select_one`, `true`)
How often do you eat avocados?

  * Daily
  * 2-3 times per week
  * Once per week
  * 2-3 times per month
  * Rarely or Never

### 6. **Primary Way of Eating Avocados** (`primary_usage`, `select_multiple`, `true`)
Select all the primary ways you eat avocados:

  * Avocado Toast
  * Guacamole or Dips
  * Salads and Toppings
  * Smoothies and Beverages
  * Plain with salt/pepper
  * Sushi or Asian Cuisine

### 7. **Where Do You Usually Buy Avocados?** (`purchase_location`, `select_one`, `true`)
Where do you usually buy avocados?

  * Supermarket/Grocery Store
  * Farmers Market
  * Organic or Specialty Store
  * Online/Delivery Service
  * Membership Club (e.g. Costco)

### 8. **Average Number of Avocados Purchased Per Week** (`purchase_count_weekly`, `number`, `true`)
Please enter the average number of avocados you buy per week.

### 9. **Preferred Ripeness at Purchase** (`ripeness_preference`, `select_one`, `true`)
How ripe do you prefer avocados to be at the time of purchase?

  * Hard (Green/Unripe)
  * Ready in 1-2 days
  * Ripe (Ready to eat now)
  * No preference

### 10. **Factors Influencing Your Purchase** (`purchase_factors`, `select_multiple`, `true`)
Select all the factors that influence your avocado purchase decisions:

  * Price and Value
  * Ripeness and Texture
  * Specific Brand
  * Organic Certification
  * Country of Origin
  * Size of Fruit

### 11. **Maximum Price You Would Pay for One Avocado** (`max_unit_price`, `number`, `true`)
Please enter the maximum price you would pay for one avocado.

### 12. **Health Perception of Avocados** (`health_perception`, `select_one`, `true`)
Rate how healthy avocados are to you:

  * 1 - Unhealthy
  * 2 - Slightly Healthy
  * 3 - Moderate
  * 4 - Healthy
  * 5 - Extremely Healthy

### 13. **Any Feedback on Avocado Availability?** (`availability_feedback`, `text`, `false`)
Please provide any comments or feedback on the availability of avocados at your preferred store.

### 14. **Consent to Share Anonymous Data** (`survey_participation_consent`, `select_one`, `true`)
Do you consent to share your anonymous data for market research purposes?

  * Yes, I agree
  * No, I do not agree
