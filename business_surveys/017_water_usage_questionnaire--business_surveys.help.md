# Water Usage Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Water Usage Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about an individual's water usage habits and consumption patterns. This data will help us better understand water management and conservation efforts in the region.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus for questions that have multiple options.
3. Enter your name, phone number, and email address in the "Contact Details" section so we can reach out to you if needed.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Household Details** (`household_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your household, including the number of people living there and any unique features that might affect your water usage.
- **Water Sources** (`water_sources`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Rainwater
	+ Public Water
	+ Wells
	+ Other (please specify)
- **Water Used For** (`water_used_for`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the ways you use water in your household:
	+ Drinking
	+ Cooking
	+ Cleaning
	+ Other (please specify)
- **Daily Consumption** (`daily_consumption`, number, optional): Enter the average amount of water used by your household per day. If you're unsure, estimate or provide a range.
- **Daily Water Bills** (`daily_water_bills`, number, optional): Enter the average daily water bill for your household. If you don't have a daily bill, estimate or provide a range.
- **Monthly Bills** (`monthly_bills`, number, optional): Enter the average monthly water bill for your household. If you don't have a monthly bill, estimate or provide a range.
- **Years Of Service** (`years_of_service`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you've had your current water service.
- **Average Consumption** (`average_consumption`, number, optional): Enter the average monthly water consumption for your household. If you're unsure, estimate or provide a range.
- **Average Water Billed** (`average_water_billed`, number, optional): Enter the average monthly water bill for your household. If you don't have a monthly bill, estimate or provide a range.
- **Monthly Consumption** (`monthly_consumption`, number, optional): Enter the average monthly water consumption for your household. If you're unsure, estimate or provide a range.
- **Daily Usage Patterns** (`daily_usage_patterns`, note, optional): Describe your daily usage patterns, such as when and how you use water in your household.
- **Conservation Strategies** (`conservation_strategies`, note, optional): Describe any conservation strategies or habits you have in place to reduce water usage.
- **Follow Up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, number, optional): Select how often you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or updates:
	+ 1-2 times a month
	+ 3-6 times a month
	+ Weekly
	+ Never
- **Conservation Status** (`conservation_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate your household's conservation status:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Not Sure
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your household's water usage.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your name, phone number, and email address so we can contact you if needed.
- **Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
- **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Alternate Email** (`contact_email_2`, email, optional): Enter an alternate email address (if you have one).
- **Comments** (`contact_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your contact information.
