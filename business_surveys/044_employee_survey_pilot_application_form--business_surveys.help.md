# Employee Survey Pilot Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Employee Survey Pilot Application Form is designed to collect feedback and data from employees to help us better understand their experiences and improve our company's employee engagement. The data collected will be used to inform future initiatives and decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to provide valuable feedback.
2. Make sure to provide your correct email address for any communication or follow-ups.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data**: This is a general text field where you can provide any additional information that is not covered in the other fields.
* **Application ID**: This field is not used in the current version of the form, so you can disregard it.
* **Email**: Please enter your actual email address to receive any communication or follow-ups.
* **First name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your official company documents.
* **Last name**: Please enter your last name as it appears on your official company documents.
* **Department**: Please select the department you belong to from the provided options.
* **Role**: Please select the role you currently hold from the provided options.
* **Team**: Please select all the teams you belong to from the provided options.
* **Survey pilot**: This field is not used in the current version of the form, so you can disregard it.
* **Consent**: Please sign this field to indicate that you've read and understand the form's purpose and data collection.
* **Notes**: This field is for any additional comments or observations you may have.
* **Phone**: Please enter your actual phone number for any follow-ups or communication.
* **Created at**: This field is automatically populated and should not be filled out.
