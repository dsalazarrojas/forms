# PESTEL Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PESTEL Analysis Survey is a tool used to evaluate the factors that may impact an organization or business. This guide will walk you through how to complete the form in a step-by-step manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form sections that correspond to the five categories: Political, Economic, Environmental, Technological, and Social.
2. For each category, select "Yes" if the factor is present or "No" if it is not present in your organization or business.
3. Enter a numerical value for the PESTEL score, which represents the overall score of your organization or business based on the selected factors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Political (1. political_factors)** (`political_factors`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if your organization or business is influenced by or has a significant impact on political factors, such as government policies, regulations, or public opinion.
- **Economic (2. economic_factors)** (`economic_factors`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if your organization or business is influenced by or has a significant impact on economic factors, such as market trends, inflation, or interest rates.
- **Environmental (3. environmental_factors)** (`environmental_factors`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if your organization or business is influenced by or has a significant impact on environmental factors, such as climate change, pollution, or natural resource depletion.
- **Technological (4. technological_factors)** (`technological_factors`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if your organization or business is influenced by or has a significant impact on technological factors, such as digitalization, automation, or innovation.
- **Social (5. social_factors)** (`social_factors`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if your organization or business is influenced by or has a significant impact on social factors, such as consumer behavior, cultural trends, or social media.
- **PESTEL Score (8. pestel_score)** (`pestel_score`, number, required: false): Enter a numerical value between 0 and 100 that represents the overall score of your organization or business based on the selected factors.
