# Facility Service Innovation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a survey designed to gather feedback and ideas from facility staff, managers, and customers on how to improve facility services. The survey aims to identify areas of improvement and understand the needs and concerns of different groups within the facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to provide valuable feedback.
2. Select the options that best describe your role, department, and interests.
3. Provide specific ideas and comments in the "What specific ideas do you have?" field.
4. Check the boxes for any additional comments or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role at your facility?** (`Manager`, `Employee`, `Customer`, `Other`): Choose the option that best describes your position within the facility.
* **Which department are you in?** (`Facilities`, `Operations`, `Maintenance`, `Administration`, `Other`): Select the department where you work or are most familiar with.
* **What type of improvement are you interested in?** (`Energy Efficiency`, `Water Management`, `Waste Reduction`, `Productivity`, `Cost Reduction`, `Other`): Choose the areas you believe are most important for improvement.
* **What specific ideas do you have?** (free text): Provide detailed and specific ideas on how to improve facility services.
* **Do you need help implementing these ideas?** (`True`, `False`): Indicate whether you would like assistance with implementing your ideas.
* **What is the status of your idea?** (`Under Review`, `Approved`, `Rejected`, `On Hold`, `Other`): Choose the current status of your idea.
* **Do you have any comments about your idea?** (free text): Add any additional comments or feedback about your idea.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to share?** (`True`, `False`): Indicate whether you have any additional information or feedback to share.
* **Do you have any follow-up questions?** (`True`, `False`): Check this box if you have further questions or need clarification on any aspect of this survey.
* **Do you have any other comments?** (free text): Add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Is there any other information you'd like to share?** (free text): Provide any other information you think might be useful for this survey.
* **Would you like to be contacted?** (`True`, `False`): Indicate whether you would like to be contacted about this survey or your idea.
* **Do you have any further comments?** (free text): Add any additional comments or feedback about this survey.
* **Facility Service Innovation Survey Page 15**, **Facility Service Innovation Survey Page 16**, ... (various field explanations): These fields are meant to capture additional comments or feedback. Please use them as needed.
