# customer_survey_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and inquiries from customers to help us understand their experience and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your honest answers to the questions.
2. For questions with options, choose one or multiple options that best describe your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or notes, please provide them in the text fields.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Questions** (`form_questions`, text, optional): This field is a brief description of the customer's experience with our organization. Please provide a short summary of your experience.
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, text, optional): This field is for any additional feedback or comments you may have about your experience with our organization.
* **Customer Needs** (`customer_needs`, text, optional): This field is for any specific needs or concerns you have as a customer.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, text, optional): This field is for you to rate your overall satisfaction with our organization.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or thoughts you may have about your experience with our organization.
* **How would you rate your satisfaction** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): This field is for you to rate your satisfaction on a scale from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
* **Which of the following best describe your experience?** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for you to choose one or multiple options that best describe your experience.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): This field is for you to enter the date of your experience with our organization.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): This field is for you to enter the time of your experience with our organization.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is for you to enter your email address, if you wish to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted by phone.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): This field is for you to enter any additional notes or comments about your experience.
* **Note2** (`note2`, note, optional): This field is for you to enter any additional notes or comments about your experience.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate with your answers.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to reach out to us.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our services.
