# Product Usage Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's experience with a product. It's crucial to understand how users interact with the product, how often they use it, and what they think about it. This data will help the product team gather valuable insights to improve and refine the product.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Fill out the form completely, but do not fill it out more than once per product.
3. If you have already filled out this form for a product, do not fill it out again.
4. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): This is the title of the form that you are filling out. Please leave this field blank.
* **Product Usage** (`product_usage`, `number`, required/optional): Please select the frequency of your product usage from the options provided.
* **Frequency of Use** (`frequency_of_use`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the options that apply to your product usage frequency.
* **Time Spent on Product** (`time_spent_on_product`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the average amount of time you spend on the product per day.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the option that best describes your overall satisfaction with the product.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or thoughts about the product, please enter them here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and honestly.
* If you have any questions, please contact us.
* If you have already filled out this form for a product, do not fill it out again.
