# Amazon Experience Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a customer feedback survey designed to gather information about your experience with Amazon. The data collected will be used to improve Amazon's services and enhance your overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the survey questions that apply to your experience.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Be as specific as possible in your answers.
4. You can skip questions that do not apply to your experience.
5. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Number** (Optional - for follow-up): Please enter your order number to help us track your experience.
* **Order Date** (Approximate date): Enter the approximate date you made your purchase.
* **Website Ease of Use** (Required): Rate how easy it was to find what you were looking for on Amazon. Use the scale: 1 (Very Difficult) to 5 (Very Easy).
* **Product Selection** (Required): Rate how satisfied you are with the product selection. Use the scale: 1 (Very Dissatisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied).
* **Product Information** (Required): Rate how helpful the product descriptions and images were. Use the scale: 1 (Not Helpful) to 5 (Extremely Helpful).
* **Customer Reviews** (Required): Rate how helpful the customer reviews were. Use the scale: Very Helpful, Somewhat Helpful, Neutral, Not Very Helpful, Not Helpful at All.
* **Pricing** (Required): Rate how satisfied you are with the pricing. Use the scale: 1 (Very Dissatisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied).
* **Checkout Process** (Required): Rate how smooth the checkout process was. Use the scale: 1 (Very Difficult) to 5 (Very Smooth).
* **Delivery Speed** (Required): Rate how satisfied you are with the delivery speed. Use the scale: 1 (Very Dissatisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied).
* **Delivery Accuracy** (Required): Rate whether your order was delivered on time. Choose from: Yes - Earlier than expected, Yes - On time, No - Slightly Late, No - Significantly Late, Not Yet Delivered.
* **Package Condition** (Required): Rate the condition of your package. Choose from: Excellent - Perfect condition, Good - Minor wear, Fair - Some damage, Poor - Significant damage.
* **Delivery Tracking** (Optional): Rate how satisfied you are with delivery tracking. Use the scale: 1 (Very Dissatisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied).
* **Delivery Options** (Optional): Rate how satisfied you are with delivery options. Choose from: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied.
* **Product Quality** (Required): Rate how you would rate the product quality. Choose from: Excellent - Exceeded expectations, Good - Met expectations, Fair - Below expectations, Poor - Significantly Below expectations.
* **Product Accuracy** (Required): Rate whether you received the correct item. Choose from: Yes - Exactly as ordered, Yes - Minor variation, No - Wrong item, No - Missing items.
* **Value for Money** (Required): Rate how you would rate the value for money. Choose from: Excellent Value, Good Value, Fair Value, Poor Value.
* **Customer Service** (Optional): Rate how satisfied you are with customer service. Choose from: No - No issues, Yes - Chat support, Yes - Phone support, Yes - Email support, Yes - Multiple channels.
* **Customer Service Rating** (Optional): Rate how you would rate customer service. Choose from: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor, Did Not Contact.
* **Issue Resolution** (Optional): Rate whether your issue was resolved satisfactorily. Choose from: Yes - Completely resolved, Partially resolved, No - Not resolved, Still pending, Not applicable.
* **Response Time** (Optional): Rate how satisfied you are with response time. Choose from: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied, Not applicable.
* **Additional Feedback** (Optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Likelihood to Recommend** (Required): Rate how likely you are to recommend Amazon. Use the scale: 0 (Not at all likely) to 10 (Extremely likely).
* **Likelihood to Repurchase** (Required): Rate how likely you are to shop with Amazon again. Choose from: Definitely will, Probably will, Might or might not, Probably will not, Definitely will not.
* **Best Aspect** (Optional): Describe what you liked best about your experience.
* **Areas for Improvement** (Optional): Describe what areas you think need improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (Optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Contact Permission** (Optional): Check if you give permission to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Email for Follow-up** (Optional): If you gave permission to be contacted, provide your email address.
