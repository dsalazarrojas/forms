**Control Head and Button Survey - Purpose**
The Control Head and Button Survey is designed to gather feedback on various aspects of the product's control interface, including its control head, buttons, and overall usability. This survey aims to understand users' experiences and opinions on the product's control head display visibility, readability, intuitiveness, responsiveness, and button characteristics such as tactile feedback, size, spacing, labeling, and durability. The survey also seeks to gauge users' overall satisfaction and willingness to recommend the product.

**How To Complete This Form**
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the survey.
2. Scroll through the sections and questions, filling in the relevant information as needed.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select one answer from the provided options.
4. For open-ended questions, provide detailed answers in the text fields.
5. Once you have filled out all the questions, click the "Submit" button to complete the survey.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **Your Name** (respondent_name, text, Optional - your name): Your name is used to identify you as a respondent for follow-up purposes.
* **Email Address** (email, email, For follow-up if needed): Enter your email address to receive follow-up communications about your responses.
* **-- Product Information --**
*   **Product Model** (product_model, select_one, Which device are you evaluating): Choose the model of the product you are evaluating.
*   **How long have you used this product** (usage_duration, select_one, First time, Less than 1 week, 1-4 weeks, 1-6 months, Over 6 months): Tell us about your experience with the product.
*   **How often do you use this product** (usage_frequency, select_one, Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Occasionally, Rarely): Report on your usage frequency of the product.
*   **-- Control Head Evaluation --**
*   **Control head display visibility** (control_head_visibility, select_one, Very Easy, Easy, Neutral, Difficult, Very Difficult): Evaluate how easy or difficult it is to see the control head display.
*   **Control head readability in different lighting** (control_head_readability, select_one, Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor): Assess the readability of the control head in various lighting conditions.
*   **Control head interface intuitiveness** (control_head_intuitiveness, select_one, Very Intuitive, Somewhat Intuitive, Neutral, Confusing, Very Confusing): Rate the control head's interface intuitiveness.
*   **Control head touch responsiveness** (control_head_responsiveness, select_one, Very Responsive, Responsive, Neutral, Unresponsive, Very Unresponsive): Evaluate the responsiveness of the control head's touch interface.
*   **-- Button Evaluation --**
*   **Button tactile feedback** (button_tactile_feedback, select_one, Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor): Describe the physical feedback from buttons.
*   **Button size** (button_size, select_one, Too Large, Slightly Large, Just Right, Slightly Small, Too Small): Evaluate the size of the buttons.
*   **Button spacing** (button_spacing, select_one, Too Much Space, Good Spacing, Adequate, Too Close Together, Overlapping): Assess the spacing between buttons.
*   **Button labeling clarity** (button_labeling, select_one, Very Clear, Clear, Neutral, Unclear, Very Unclear): Evaluate the clarity of button labels.
*   **Perceived button durability** (button_durability, select_one, Very Durable, Durable, Neutral, Fragile, Very Fragile): Rate the perceived durability of the buttons.
*   **-- Overall Usability --**
*   **Overall ease of use** (ease_of_use, select_one, Very Easy, Easy, Neutral, Difficult, Very Difficult): Rate the overall ease of use of the product's controls.
*   **How easy was it to learn the controls** (learning_curve, select_one, Immediately Intuitive, Quick to Learn, Moderate Learning, Difficult to Learn, Very Difficult to Learn): Describe how easily you were able to learn the controls.
*   **How often do you make mistakes** (error_frequency, select_one, Never, Rarely, Occasionally, Frequently, Very Frequently): Report on mistake frequency.
*   **-- Satisfaction --**
*   **Overall satisfaction with controls** (overall_satisfaction, select_one, Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied): Rate your overall satisfaction with the controls.
*   **Would you recommend this product** (would_recommend, select_one, Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, Definitely No): Indicate your willingness to recommend the product.
*   **-- Additional Feedback --**
*   **What do you like best about the controls** (best_features, text, Any other feedback): Share your favorite features or aspects of the controls.
*   **What needs improvement** (areas_for_improvement, text, Any other feedback): Identify areas where the controls could be improved.
*   **Any features you wish were included** (missing_features, text, Any other feedback): Suggest features that you would like to see added.
*   **Additional Comments** (additional_comments, text, Any other feedback): Provide any other feedback or comments about the product's controls.
