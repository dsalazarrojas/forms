# Building Maintenance Frequency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Building Maintenance Frequency Survey aims to collect information about your building's maintenance schedule. This survey will help identify areas that need improvement and provide valuable insights for maintaining a safe and healthy environment. Please answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **Survey Date** in the first field, which represents when this survey is being completed.
2. Type the **Building Location** in the second field, which is the name or address of the building.
3. If applicable, provide the **Floor/Area** in the third field, which is the section or area of the building you are answering about (e.g., floor 1, floor 2, etc.).
4. Select your **Role** in the fourth field, which could be Building Occupant, Tenant, Manager, Facility Staff, Visitor, or Other.
5. Choose how long you have used the building in the fifth field, which is Less than 6 months, 6 months - 1 year, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, or Over 5 years.
6. Select the **Frequency of Floor Cleaning** in the sixth field, which could be Daily, Several Times Weekly, Weekly, Biweekly, Monthly, or As Needed.
7. Choose the **Restroom Maintenance Frequency** in the seventh field, which could be Multiple Times Daily, Daily, Several Times Weekly, Weekly, or Less Frequently.
8. Select the **Trash and Recycling Collection Frequency** in the eighth field, which could be Multiple Times Daily, Daily, Several Times Weekly, or Weekly.
9. Choose the **HVAC System Maintenance Frequency** in the ninth field, which could be Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually, As Needed, or Unknown.
10. Select the **Window Cleaning Frequency** in the tenth field, which could be Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Biannually, Annually, or Rarely.
11. Choose the **Exterior Area Maintenance Frequency** in the eleventh field, which could be Daily, Weekly, Biweekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or As Needed.
12. Select the **Carpet Cleaning Frequency** in the twelfth field, which could be Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually, As Needed, or Not Applicable.
13. Select the **Plumbing System Checks** in the thirteenth field, which could be Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually, As Needed, or Unknown.
14. Choose the **Electrical System Inspections** in the fourteenth field, which could be Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually, As Needed, or Unknown.
15. Select your **Overall Satisfaction with Current Maintenance** in the fifteenth field, which could be Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
16. If you have any **Suggested Maintenance Improvements**, you can provide them in the sixteenth field. This is optional.
17. If you have any **Current Maintenance Concerns**, you can list them in the seventeenth field. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Date**: Enter the date on which this survey is being completed.
* **Building Location**: Type the name or address of the building or property.
* **Floor/Area**: If applicable, type the section or area of the building you are answering about (e.g., floor 1, floor 2, etc.). This is an optional field.
* **Role**: Select your role in the building, such as Building Occupant, Tenant, Manager, Facility Staff, Visitor, or Other.
* **How Long Have You Used This Building**: Choose how long you have used the building: Less than 6 months, 6 months - 1 year, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, or Over 5 years.
* **Frequency of Floor Cleaning**: Select the frequency of floor cleaning: Daily, Several Times Weekly, Weekly, Biweekly, Monthly, or As Needed.
* **Restroom Maintenance Frequency**: Choose the maintenance frequency for restrooms: Multiple Times Daily, Daily, Several Times Weekly, Weekly, or Less Frequently.
* **Trash and Recycling Collection Frequency**: Select the frequency of trash and recycling collection: Multiple Times Daily, Daily, Several Times Weekly, or Weekly.
* **HVAC System Maintenance Frequency**: Choose the maintenance frequency for HVAC systems: Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually, As Needed, or Unknown.
* **Window Cleaning Frequency**: Select the window cleaning frequency: Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Biannually, Annually, or Rarely.
* **Exterior Area Maintenance Frequency**: Choose the maintenance frequency for exterior areas: Daily, Weekly, Biweekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or As Needed.
* **Carpet Cleaning Frequency**: Select the carpet cleaning frequency: Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually, As Needed, or Not Applicable.
* **Plumbing System Checks**: Select the maintenance frequency for plumbing systems: Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually, As Needed, or Unknown.
* **Electrical System Inspections**: Choose the maintenance frequency for electrical systems: Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually, As Needed, or Unknown.
* **Satisfaction With Current Maintenance**: Select your overall satisfaction level with maintenance: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **Suggested Maintenance Improvements**: If you have any suggestions for maintenance improvements, type them in this field. This is optional.
* **Current Maintenance Concerns**: If you have any current concerns about maintenance, list them in this field. This is optional.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions based on your actual experience with the building.
- Be as detailed as possible in the "Floor/Area" field if you are answering on behalf of a specific area or section of the building.
- If you are unsure about any of the maintenance frequencies, choose "As Needed" for those fields.
- If you have any concerns or improvements, please provide as much detail as possible to help improve the building's maintenance schedule.
