# Workforce Capability Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Capability Index Survey is designed to collect information from employees about their manager's feedback and assessment of their skills, performance, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Make sure to answer all questions and provide feedback where necessary.
3. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure you've completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your manager who will be providing feedback on your performance.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address for easy contact and communication.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter your manager's phone number for quick contact.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your own name as the employee being assessed.
* **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or role within the organization.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department where you work.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, optional): Describe your job function or responsibilities.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you work.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you've been working in your current role or at the company.
* **Skills Assessed** (`skills_assessed`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate which skills were assessed during this evaluation (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Assessment Type** (`assessment_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of assessment (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the assessment or evaluation.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback from your manager.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback from your manager.
* **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback from the employee being assessed.
* **Departmental Comments** (`departmental_comments`, text, optional): Add any departmental comments or feedback.
* **Action Items** (`action_items`, text, optional): List any action items or tasks assigned to the employee.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback.

Note: All fields marked as "optional" are not required, but it's recommended to fill them out for a more comprehensive assessment.
