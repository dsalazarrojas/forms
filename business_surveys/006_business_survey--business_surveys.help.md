# Business Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Survey form is designed to gather detailed information about your business, including its structure, operations, and future plans. This data will help us provide you with relevant and targeted support, ensuring you get the most out of our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your company name, industry sector, and size.
2. Select the correct industry sector that best describes your business.
3. Choose the number of employees you have in your company.
4. Enter the year your company was founded.
5. Select the annual revenue range that applies to your business.
6. Provide your contact person's name, email, phone number, and a brief description of your role.
7. Enter your company's primary contact person's email address.
8. Enter your company's phone number.
9. Write a brief description of your business's primary products or services.
10. Identify your target market.
11. Provide your company's primary business location (city and state).
12. If applicable, select your company's expansion plans.
13. Optionally, mention your main business challenges.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (1)**: Enter your company's official name as registered.
* **Industry Sector (2)**: Choose the primary industry that best describes your business (e.g., Technology, Manufacturing, Retail).
* **Company Size (3)**: Select the number of employees in your company.
* **Year Founded (4)**: Enter the year your company was established.
* **Annual Revenue Range (5)**: Select the correct revenue range that applies to your business.
* **Contact Person Name (6)**: Enter the name of your primary contact person.
* **Contact Email (7)**: Enter your business email address.
* **Contact Phone Number (8)**: Enter your company's phone number.
* **Brief Business Description (9)**: Write a short description of your business's primary products or services.
* **Key Products or Services (10)**: List your company's main offerings.
* **Target Market (11)**: Identify your primary customers.
* **Primary Business Location (12)**: Enter your company's city and state.
* **Expansion Plans (13)**: If you have plans for expansion, select the timeframe for your plans.
* **Main Business Challenges (14)**: Optionally, mention your biggest obstacles.
