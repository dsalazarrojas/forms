# Canopy Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
<thinking>
This form is used to collect information about a client's requirements for a canopy solution. The goal is to gather details about the project type, size, material, and other essential features to provide a tailored recommendation for a canopy that meets their needs.
</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the intro section, which provides a brief overview of the project.
2. Fill in your name and email address in the "Customer Information" section.
3. Select the type of project you are planning in the "Project Type" section.
4. Indicate the primary use of the canopy in the "Primary Use of the Canopy" section.
5. Specify the estimated coverage area needed in the "Estimated Coverage Area Needed" section.
6. Choose the flexibility of the size in the "Size Flexibility" section.
7. Select the shape preference in the "Shape Preference" section.
8. Choose the type of canopy that interests you in the "Canopy Type Selection" section.
9. Specify the wind resistance capability in the "Wind Resistance Capability" section.
10. Select any desired special features in the "Desired Special Features" section.
11. Choose the maintenance level acceptable in the "Maintenance Preferences" section.
12. Specify the expected lifespan of the canopy in the "Expected Lifespan" section.
13. Select the approximate budget range in the "Approximate Budget Range" section.
14. Choose the local climate type in the "Local Climate Type" section.
15. If necessary, add any additional notes or requirements in the "Additional Notes or Requirements" section.
16. Finally, select the decision timeline in the "Decision Timeline" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (Name)** (`text`, required): Enter your name to ensure we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Email Address (Email Address)** (`email`, required): Enter your email address to facilitate communication.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number for additional contact options.
* **What type of project are you planning? (Project Type)** (`select_one`, required): Select one of the following options: 
  * Residential backyard
  * Residential deck
  * Commercial space
  * Event/temporary use
  * Parking area
  * Outdoor dining
  * Sports facility
  * Other
* **Primary use of the canopy (Primary Use of the Canopy)** (`select_one`, required): Select one of the following options: 
  * Shade and sun protection
  * Rain protection
  * Storage area
  * Outdoor entertaining
  * Business/commercial use
  * Event venue
  * Multiple purposes
* **Estimated Coverage Area Needed (Coverage Area)** (`number`, required): Enter the approximate square footage needed for coverage.
* **How flexible is the size? (Size Flexibility)** (`select_one`, not required): Select one of the following options: 
  * Must be exact size
  * Can be slightly larger
  * Can be slightly smaller
  * Flexible within range
  * Unsure
* **Shape Preference (Shape Preference)** (`select_one`, not required): Select one of the following options: 
  * Square
  * Rectangular
  * Hexagonal
  * Octagonal
  * Circular
  * Custom shape
  * No preference
* **What type of canopy interests you? (Canopy Type Selection)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply:
  * Fixed permanent structure
  * Retractable canopy
  * Portable tent
  * Shade sail
  * Pergola
  * Marquee
  * Pop-up tent
  * Custom design
* **Installation Preference (Installation Preference)** (`select_one`, required): Select one of the following options: 
  * Ground mounted
  * Wall mounted
  * Post mounted
  * Freestanding
  * Mobile/on wheels
  * Flexible
* **Fabric Type Preference (Fabric Preference)** (`select_one`, required): Select one of the following options: 
  * Canvas
  * Polyester
  * Vinyl
  * Acrylic
  * Mesh
  * Polycarbonate
  * Metal
  * Other
* **UV Protection Level Needed (UV Protection Level Needed)** (`select_one`, not required): Select one of the following options: 
  * Basic protection
  * Standard protection
  * Maximum UV protection
  * Not important
* **Water Resistance Needed (Water Resistance Needed)** (`select_one`, required): Select one of the following options: 
  * None - sun only
  * Water resistant
  * Waterproof
  * Extremely durable
* **Wind Resistance Capability (Wind Resistance Capability)** (`select_one`, required): Select one of the following options: 
  * Light winds only
  * Moderate winds
  * Strong winds
  * Extreme weather
  * Unsure of needs
* **Desired Special Features (Desired Special Features)** (`select_multiple`, not required): Select all that apply:
  * Motorized/automated
  * Lighting included
  * Heating capability
  * Sound system
  * Removable panels
  * Easy maintenance
  * Eco-friendly materials
  * No special features
* **Maintenance Level Acceptable (Maintenance Level Acceptable)** (`select_one`, not required): Select one of the following options: 
  * Very low maintenance
  * Low maintenance
  * Moderate maintenance
  * High maintenance acceptable
  * No preference
* **Expected Lifespan (Expected Lifespan)** (`select_one`, required): Select one of the following options: 
  * Temporary - less than 1 year
  * Short term - 1-3 years
  * Medium term - 3-5 years
  * Long term - 5-10 years
  * Very long term - 10 plus years
* **Approximate Budget Range (Budget Range)** (`select_one`, not required): Select one of the following options: 
  * Under 500
  * 500-1500
  * 1500-5000
  * 5000-15000
  * 15000 plus
  * Flexible budget
* **Local Climate Type (Local Climate Type)** (`select_multiple`, not required): Select all that apply:
  * Tropical/humid
  * Arid/desert
  * Temperate
  * Cold/snowy
  * Coastal/salt air
  * High altitude
  * Other
* **Any Additional Notes or Requirements? (Additional Notes or Requirements)** (`text`, not required): Add any additional notes or requirements that are not covered in the other fields.
* **Decision Timeline (Decision Timeline)** (`select_one`, not required): Select one of the following options: 
  * Immediately
  * Within 1 week
  * Within 1 month
  * Within 3 months
  * No rush
