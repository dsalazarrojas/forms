# Expense Management Process Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help evaluate the current expense management process within your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and choose the options that best describe your current experience with the expense management process.
2. Select all the options that apply to you for each section.
3. For the "Comments" field, provide any additional information or insights you have about the expense management process.
4. Make sure to complete all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Expense Workflow * 
* **Expense Workflow** (`expense_workflow`, `select_multiple`): Select all the options that best describe your current expense workflow.

### Reporting Frequency
* **Reporting Frequency** (`reporting_frequency`, `select_one`): Choose the frequency of reporting that best suits your needs.

### Reporting Method
* **Reporting Method** (`reporting_method`, `select_multiple`): Select all the methods you use for reporting.

### Submission Status
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `select_multiple`): Choose the status of your submissions.

### Reporting Deadline
* **Reporting Deadline** (`reporting_deadline`, `date`): Enter the deadline for submitting reports.

### Reporting Time
* **Reporting Time** (`reporting_time`, `time`): Enter the time of day you typically submit reports.

### Training Needs
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`, `text`): Provide any additional information about your training needs related to the expense management process.

### Tool Performance
* **Tool Performance** (`tool_performance`, `number`): Evaluate the performance of the tools used for expense management on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`): Provide any additional comments or insights about the expense management process.

**Tips:**

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Make sure to complete all the required fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please feel free to ask for clarification.
