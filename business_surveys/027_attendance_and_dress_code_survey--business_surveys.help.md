# Attendance And Dress Code Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees about their attendance and dress code for events. It helps us ensure that we can accommodate everyone's needs and provide a smooth experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

* Read through each section carefully and provide accurate information where applicable.
* For select-one fields, choose one option that best answers your question.
* For select_multiple fields, select all applicable options.
* Use text fields to provide detailed responses or explanations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the event you are attending.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the event will be held.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, required): Enter the venue where the event will be held.
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Company/Organization** (`company_organization`, `text`, optional): If you represent a company or organization, enter its name.
* **Title/Position** (`title_position`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title or role.
* **Will You Be Attending?** (`will_attend`, `select_one`, required): Select if you plan to attend the event.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, `number`, optional): If you are bringing guests, enter the number of attendees.
* **Guest Names (if applicable)** (`guest_names`, `text`, optional): Enter the names of your guests.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Preferences** (`dietary_needs`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your dietary restrictions, if any.
* **Specific Dietary Details** (`specific_dietary_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional dietary information.
* **Dress Code (as communicated)** (`dress_code_understanding`, `select_one`, required): Select the dress code communicated by the event organizers.
* **Do you understand the dress code requirements?** (`dress_code_confirmation_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your understanding of the dress code.
* **Accessibility or Mobility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional): If you require any accessibility accommodations, describe your needs.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional): If you have any special requests, describe them.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any other information you would like to share.
