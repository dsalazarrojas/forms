# Business Support Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the quality and effectiveness of our business support services. Your input will help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. If a question is unclear or requires further explanation, contact us for assistance.
4. Review the form before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Your full name.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, `email`, required): Your contact email address.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): The name of your organization.
* **Support Contact or Account Manager Name** (`support_contact`, `text`, optional): The name of the person you've interacted with, if applicable.
* **How long have you been with us?** (`relationship_duration`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Less than 3 months
	+ 3-6 months
	+ 6-12 months
	+ 1-2 years
	+ 2-5 years
	+ 5+ years
* **How frequently do you use support services?** (`support_type_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ As needed
	+ Rarely
* **How quickly does support respond to your inquiries?** (`responsiveness_rating`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Immediate
	+ Within 1 hour
	+ Within 4 hours
	+ Within 1 day
	+ Within several days
	+ Very slow
* **How effectively are issues resolved?** (`resolution_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Completely resolved
	+ Usually resolved
	+ Partially resolved
	+ Rarely resolved
	+ Not resolved
* **Support staff knowledge and expertise** (`staff_knowledge`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Expert knowledge
	+ Knowledgeable
	+ Adequate knowledge
	+ Limited knowledge
	+ Lack of knowledge
* **Clarity of communication from support** (`communication_clarity`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Excellent clarity
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat clear
	+ Unclear
	+ Very unclear
* **Staff professionalism and courtesy** (`professionalism`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Professional
	+ Acceptable
	+ Unprofessional
	+ Very unprofessional
* **Quality of documentation and resources** (`documentation_quality`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Adequate
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Availability of support channels** (`support_channels`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Excellent - many options
	+ Good - several options
	+ Adequate - few options
	+ Limited options
	+ Not available
* **Value for the price paid** (`price_value`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Excellent value
	+ Good value
	+ Fair value
	+ Poor value
	+ Very poor value
* **How often do you encounter issues?** (`issue_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Rarely have issues
	+ Few issues
	+ Some issues
	+ Frequent issues
	+ Constant issues
* **What improvements would you suggest?** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **Effectiveness of training or onboarding** (`training_effectiveness`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Adequate
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **Would you recommend our support to others?** (`recommendation_likelihood`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Definitely would
	+ Probably would
	+ Unsure
	+ Probably would not
	+ Definitely would not
* **What additional support services would help?** (`additional_services_needed`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional support services you think would be helpful.
* **Overall satisfaction with support** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied

## Tips
1. Answer each question honestly and carefully.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. If a question is unclear, contact us for assistance.
