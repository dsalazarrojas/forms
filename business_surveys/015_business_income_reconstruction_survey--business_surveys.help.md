# Business Income Reconstruction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Income Reconstruction Survey is a form designed to collect detailed information about a business' income and expenses for a given reporting period. This information will be used to reconstruct the business' income, which is essential for financial audits, tax purposes, or other accounting tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Business name** field with the legal or trading name of your company.
2.  Enter the **Start date** and **End date** of the reporting period for which you're reconstructing the income.
3.  Describe the **Revenue sources** that contributed to your income during the reporting period.
4.  Estimate the **Average monthly income** across the reporting period.
5.  If applicable, list the **Major expenses** that impacted your income during the period.
6.  Choose the **Accounting method** used during the period (either **Cash** or **Accrual**).
7.  Indicate if you have **Supporting documents** available (such as invoices or bank statements).
8.  Enter your **Contact email** for any follow-up questions or discussions related to the reconstructed income.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Business name** (`text`, **required**: yes): Enter the legal or trading name of your company.
*  **Reporting period start** (`date`, **required**: yes): Enter the start date of the reporting period for which you're reconstructing the income.
*  **Reporting period end** (`date`, **required**: yes): Enter the end date of the reporting period for which you're reconstructing the income.
*  **Revenue sources** (`text`, **required**: yes): Describe the main revenue streams that contributed to your income during the period.
*  **Estimated monthly income** (`number`, **required**: yes): Estimate the average monthly income across the period.
*  **Major expenses** (`text`, **required**: no): This field is optional but may be helpful for providing clarity on significant expenses.
*  **Accounting method** (`select_one`, **required**: yes): Choose between **Cash** and **Accrual** accounting methods used during the period.
*  **Supporting documents available** (`select_one`, **required**: yes): Indicate if you have supporting documents available to back up your reconstructed income.
*  **Contact email** (`email`, **required**: no): This field is for contact information and is optional.
