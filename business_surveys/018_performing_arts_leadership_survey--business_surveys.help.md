# Performing Arts Leadership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about performing arts leadership experiences, skills, and satisfaction levels. Your input will help create a comprehensive database for the performing arts industry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name, email address, and job-related information (company, department, job title).
2. Answer questions about your leadership experiences, including time spent leading, job and leadership satisfaction, and number of people you lead.
3. Provide additional leadership skills or focus areas you'd like to mention.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your work email address.
* **Company**: Enter the name of your current or past employer.
* **Department**: Enter the name of your department or team within your company.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current or past job title.
* **Job Type**: Select 'Yes' if you currently hold a leadership role, 'No' otherwise.
* **Job Status**: Select 'Active' if you're currently leading, 'Inactive' if not.
* **Job Level**: Select 'High' if you have a high-level leadership position, 'Low' otherwise.
* **Years with Leadership Role**: Enter the number of years you've spent in a leadership position.
* **Leadership Skills**: Enter any skills you use or have used in a leadership role.
* **Leadership Style**: Select 'Yes' if you use a leadership style that focuses on [insert leadership style], 'No' otherwise. Note: Please specify your leadership style in the "Other Leadership Focus Area" field.
* **Management Level**: Select 'High' if you have a high-level management position, 'Low' otherwise.
* **Job Satisfaction**: Enter a number (0-10) to rate your job satisfaction level.
* **Leadership Satisfaction**: Enter a number (0-10) to rate your leadership satisfaction level.
* **Time Spent Leading**: Enter the number of hours you spend leading per week.
* **Time Spent Leading per Week**: Enter the average number of hours you spend leading per week.
* **Number of People Leading**: Enter the number of people you currently lead or have led in the past.
* **Hours Spent Leading**: Enter the average number of hours you spend leading per week.
* **Leadership Hours Spent**: Enter the average number of hours you spend leading per week.
* **Leadership Level**: Select 'High' if you have a high leadership position, 'Low' otherwise.
* **Leadership Focus**: Select 'Yes' if you focus on [insert leadership focus], 'No' otherwise. Note: Please specify your leadership focus in the "Leadership Focus Area" field.
* **Leadership Focus Area**: Select 'Yes' for each area you focus on, such as [insert areas], 'No' otherwise.
* **Other Leadership Focus Area**: Enter any additional leadership focus areas not listed above.
* **Other Leadership Focus**: Enter any additional leadership skills or focus areas not listed above.
