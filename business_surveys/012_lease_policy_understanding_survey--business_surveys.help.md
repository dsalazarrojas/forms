# lease_policy_understanding_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about lease policies. It is used to gather data on various aspects of leases, including survey title, questions, landlord name, tenant name, lease start and end dates, lease policy URL, rent amount, lease term length, lease term unit, and lease term type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the survey in the "Survey Title" field.
2. Select the relevant questions from the list provided in the "Survey Questions" field.
3. Enter the name of the landlord in the "Landlord Name" field.
4. Enter the name of the tenant in the "Tenant Name" field.
5. Enter the start date of the lease in the "Lease Start Date" field.
6. Enter the end date of the lease in the "Lease End Date" field.
7. Enter the URL of the lease policy in the "Lease Policy URL" field.
8. Enter the total rent amount for the lease in the "Rent Amount" field.
9. Enter the length of the lease term in the "Lease Term Length" field.
10. Select the unit of the lease term from the options provided in the "Lease Term Unit" field.
11. Select the type of lease term from the options provided in the "Lease Term Type" field.
12. Enter a description of the lease term in the "Lease Term Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a title for the survey.
* **Survey Questions** (`survey_questions`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the questions that apply to the survey.
* **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the landlord.
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the tenant.
* **Lease Start Date** (`lease_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the lease.
* **Lease End Date** (`lease_end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of the lease.
* **Lease Policy URL** (`lease_policy_url`, text, required/optional): Enter the URL of the lease policy.
* **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the total rent amount for the lease.
* **Lease Term Length** (`lease_term_length`, number, required/optional): Enter the length of the lease term.
* **Lease Term Unit** (`lease_term_unit`, select_one, required/optional): Select the unit of the lease term.
* **Lease Term Type** (`lease_term_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of lease term.
* **Lease Term Description** (`lease_term_description`, text, required/optional): Enter a description of the lease term.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information you enter in each field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with the relevant parties or seek guidance from a knowledgeable person.
