# ESG Reporting Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of the ESG Reporting Awareness Survey is to gauge the awareness and understanding of Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) reporting among respondents. The survey aims to collect information about their knowledge and views on ESG reporting, its importance, and how it is conducted.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Read through each page carefully**: Take your time to review each section of the survey carefully.
2. **Answer all questions**: Respond to every question honestly, as your answers will help us understand your perspective.
3. **Submit your responses**: Once you've filled out the entire form, click the submit button to send your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is the first page of the survey, where you will find a brief introduction to the topic of ESG reporting.
* **This is the introduction section**: This field is a text box where you will provide your thoughts or comments about the introduction.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: No
* **What do you know about ESG**: This field is a time-based question that asks about your knowledge or experience with ESG reporting.
	+ Type: Time field
	+ Required: No
* **About the Reporting Process**: This field is a date-based question that inquires about your views or understanding of the ESG reporting process.
	+ Type: Date field
	+ Required: No

## Tips

* Take your time to answer each question carefully.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* Remember, your responses are anonymous and will be used solely for educational purposes.
