# Urban Redevelopment Trust Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Urban Redevelopment Trust Survey is a tool for collecting feedback from the community regarding urban development issues such as housing, infrastructure, and public health. This form helps the Urban Redevelopment Trust to understand your concerns and opinions on these matters.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the survey completely and honestly.
2. Please answer all required fields, which are marked as required in the form.
3. For questions with options, choose the one that best represents your opinion or concern.
4. If you have additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional comments or suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Urban Redevelopment Trust** (`page1`, `text`, required): This field is not applicable for explanation as it is a simple text input field. Please type your name and title in this field.
* **Survey Introduction** (`page2`, `text`, required): This field is not applicable for explanation as it is a simple text input field. Please write a brief introduction or comment about your response.
* **What is your role in the community?** (`page_3`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the options that best represents your role in the community:
	+ Urban Planner: You are a professional involved in planning and development of urban projects.
	+ Community Leader: You are a leader or representative of a community group or organization.
	+ Resident: You are a community member or resident of the area.
* **How do you rate the level of community engagement?** (`page4`, `number`, required): Please rate your answer on a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.
* **What are your top three concerns about urban development?** (`page5`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select up to three options that best represent your top concerns about urban development:
	+ Housing
	+ Infrastructure
	+ Public Health
* **How do you rate the quality of life in your neighborhood?** (`page6`, `number`, required): Please rate your answer on a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.
* **How do you rate the level of community satisfaction?** (`page7`, `number`, required): Please rate your answer on a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.
* **Additional comments or suggestions** (`page8`, `note`, required): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about urban development in your area.
