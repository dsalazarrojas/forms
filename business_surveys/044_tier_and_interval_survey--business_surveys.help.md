# Tier And Interval Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a customer's pricing tiers and billing intervals. It aims to gather feedback on the customer's satisfaction with the current pricing tiers and intervals, as well as their preferences for future pricing tiers and intervals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all applicable pricing tiers by choosing the "Yes" option for each tier that applies to you.
2. Choose the billing interval that best describes your current or preferred billing cycle.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the current tier pricing and billing interval.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the tier pricing and billing interval scale.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback about the tier pricing and billing interval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pricing Tiers** (`tier_and_interval_survey_form`, `select_multiple`): Choose all the pricing tiers that apply to you.
* **Billing Intervals** (`billing_interval_form`, `select_multiple`): Choose the billing interval that best describes your current or preferred billing cycle.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction_form`, `select_one`): Rate your satisfaction with the current tier pricing as either "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".
* **Preferred Pricing Tier** (`preferred_pricing_tier`, `select_one`): Choose whether you would prefer the current tier pricing.
* **Billing Interval** (`billing_interval`, `select_one`): Rate your satisfaction with the current billing interval as either "Yes" or "No".
* **Tier Pricing Satisfaction** (`tier_pricing_satisfaction`, `select_one`): Rate your satisfaction with the tier pricing as either "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".
* **Tier Pricing Satisfaction Scale** (`tier_pricing_satisfaction_scale`, `select_one`): Rate your satisfaction with the tier pricing scale as either "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".
* **Tier Pricing Satisfaction Scale Other** (`tier_pricing_satisfaction_scale_other`, `select_one`): If you have any other comments about the tier pricing scale, enter them here.
* **Preferred Pricing Tier Comments** (`preferred_pricing_tier_comments`, `text`): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your preferred pricing tier.
* **Billing Interval Comments** (`billing_interval_comments`, `text`): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your preferred billing interval.
* **Tier Pricing Satisfaction Comments** (`tier_pricing_satisfaction_comments`, `text`): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your satisfaction with the tier pricing.
* **Tier Pricing Satisfaction Scale Comments** (`tier_pricing_satisfaction_scale_comments`, `text`): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your satisfaction with the tier pricing scale.
* **Tier Pricing Satisfaction Scale Other Comments** (`tier_pricing_satisfaction_scale_other_comments`, `text`): If you have any other comments about your satisfaction with the tier pricing scale, enter them here.
