# Facility Management Modernization Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey is designed to help us understand the current state of your facilities management practices. Your responses will help us identify areas for modernization and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best describe your current facilities management practices.
3. Leave any additional comments or notes in the spaces provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the current state of your facilities?** (`page_1`, `note`, required: false): This section asks you to briefly describe the current state of your facilities. Please provide an overview of your facility's current condition, including any challenges or issues you are facing.
* **Current Challenges** (`current_challenges`, `text`, required: false): Describe the challenges you are currently facing with your facilities. This could include budget constraints, limited resources, or other obstacles.
* **Prioritization** (`prioritization`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the level of prioritization you currently give to your facilities management practices. Options are High, Medium, or Low.
* **What types of facilities do you have?** (`page_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the types of facilities you have, such as Manufacturing, Office, etc.
* **Facilities Size** (`facilities_size`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the size of your facilities from Small, Medium, or Large.
* **Facilities Type** (`facilities_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the types of facilities you have, such as Office Building, Warehouse, or Other.
* **Facilities Equipment** (`facilities_equipment`, `text`, required: false): Describe your facilities' equipment and systems.
* **What is your current modernization level?** (`page_3`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the level of modernization you currently have for your facilities management practices. Options are Basic, Standard, or Advanced.
* **Facilities Budget** (`facilities_budget`, `number`, required: false): Enter your current facilities budget.
* **Current Budget** (`current_budget`, `number`, required: false): Enter your current budget for facilities management.
* **Facilities Sustainability** (`facilities_sustainability`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the level of sustainability in your facilities management practices. Options are High, Medium, or Low.
* **Facilities Environmental Impact** (`facilities_environmental_impact`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the level of environmental impact in your facilities management practices. Options are High, Medium, or Low.
* **Facilities Security** (`facilities_security`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the level of security in your facilities management practices. Options are High, Medium, or Low.
* **Facilities Sustainability Goals** (`facilities_sustainability_goals`, `text`, required: false): Describe your facilities sustainability goals and objectives.
* **Facilities Maintenance** (`facilities_maintenance`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the level of maintenance in your facilities management practices. Options are High, Medium, or Low.
* **Facilities Training and Professional Development** (`facilities_training_and_professional_development`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the level of training and professional development in your facilities management practices. Options are High, Medium, or Low.
* **Facilities Waste and Recycling** (`facilities_waste_and_recycling`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the level of waste and recycling in your facilities management practices. Options are High, Medium, or Low.
* **What initiatives would you like to modernize?** (`page_4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the initiatives you would like to modernize, such as Asset Management, Energy Management, or Other.
* **Additional Comments** (`page_5`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about your facilities management practices.
* **Facility Manager** (`facility_manager`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your facility manager.
* **Facility Administrator** (`facility_administrator`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your facility administrator.
* **Facility Supervisor** (`facility_supervisor`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your facility supervisor.
* **What are the major concerns of your facility operations team?** (`page_6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the major concerns of your facility operations team, such as Budget Constraints, Limited Resources, or Other.
* **Which of the following best describes the role of your facilities team?** (`page_7`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the role of your facilities team from Strategic, Tactical, or Operational.
* **Additional Comments** (`page_8`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about your facilities management practices.
