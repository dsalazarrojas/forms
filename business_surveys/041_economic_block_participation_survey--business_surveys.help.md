# Economic Block Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about your participation in the economic block. Your responses will help us understand your preferences and involvement in this area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact details, including any additional contact information if applicable.
2. Select your roles or responsibilities related to the economic block.
3. Indicate your motivations for participating in the economic block.
4. Specify your expectations from the economic block.
5. If you have any other roles or responsibilities not listed, describe them and provide further details.
6. If you have any other expectations not listed, describe them and provide further details.
7. If you have any other information not covered in the previous steps, use the "Other" section to provide details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can provide your name, email, and other contact information.
* **Contact Details Additional** (`contact_details_additional`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any additional contact details that you may want to provide.
* **Roles** (`roles`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the roles or responsibilities that apply to you related to the economic block. You can choose multiple options.
* **Motivations** (`motivations`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the motivations that best describes why you are participating in the economic block.
* **Expectations** (`expectations`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the expectations that best describes your expectations from the economic block.
* **More Roles** (`roles_additional`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If any of the listed roles do not apply, select any additional roles that you want to add.
* **Other Roles** (`roles_other`, `text`, required: false): Describe any other roles or responsibilities that you may have related to the economic block.
* **Other Roles Description** (`roles_other_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the other role or responsibility you selected.
* **Other Roles Category** (`roles_other_category`, `text`, required: false): If you want to categorize the other role or responsibility, use this field.
* **Other Roles Frequency** (`roles_other_frequency`, `number`, required: false): If you have an estimate of how often you perform this other role or responsibility, provide a number.
* **Other Roles Frequency Other** (`roles_other_frequency_other`, `text`, required: false): If the frequency is not a number, describe it in this field.
* **Other Roles Frequency Other Description** (`roles_other_frequency_other_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the other frequency.
* **Other Roles Frequency Other Other** (`roles_other_frequency_other_other`, `text`, required: false): If you want to add any additional details about the other frequency.
* **Other Roles Frequency Other Other Description** (`roles_other_frequency_other_other_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the other other frequency.
* **Other Roles Frequency Other Other Frequency** (`other_frequency_other_other_frequency`, `number`, required: false): If you want to add any other frequency details, provide a number.
* **Other Roles Frequency Other Other Other** (`other_frequency_other_other_other`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional details about the other other frequency.
* **Other** (`other`, `note`, required: false): Use this field to add any other information that is not covered in the previous fields.
* **Other Description** (`other_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the other information you added.
* **Other Frequency** (`other_frequency`, `number`, required: false): If you have an estimate of how often you perform this other activity, provide a number.
* **Other Frequency Other** (`other_frequency_other`, `text`, required: false): If the frequency is not a number, describe it in this field.
* **Other Frequency Other Description** (`other_frequency_other_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the other frequency.
* **Other Frequency Other Other** (`other_frequency_other_other`, `text`, required: false): If you want to add any additional details about the other frequency.
* **Other Frequency Other Other Description** (`other_frequency_other_other_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the other other frequency.
* **Other Frequency Other Other Other** (`other_frequency_other_other_other`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional details about the other other frequency.
* **Other Frequency Other Other Other Frequency** (`other_frequency_other_other_other_frequency`, `number`, required: false): If you want to add any additional details about the other other frequency, provide a number.

Note: This help guide is a general explanation and might not cover all possible fields or edge cases. If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
