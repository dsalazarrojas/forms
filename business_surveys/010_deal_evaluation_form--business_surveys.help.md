# deal_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The deal_evaluation_form is used to gather information to evaluate deals. It's likely used for business or investment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and fill it out for each deal you want to evaluate.
2. Start with the introduction page.
3. Answer each question truthfully and provide as much detail as possible.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **deal_overview** (`deal_overview`, text, optional): Briefly describe the deal in a few words.
* **deal_details** (`deal_details`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the details that apply to the deal.
* **risk_assessment** (`risk_assessment`, select_one, optional): Assess the risk level of the deal.
	+ Choose 'Yes' for high risk or 'No' for low risk.
* **financials** (`financials`, number, optional): Provide a numerical value for the deal's financials.
* **deal_value** (`deal_value`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value to represent the deal's value.
* **deal_strategy** (`deal_strategy`, text, optional): Describe the deal's strategy.
* **deal_outcome** (`deal_outcome`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the outcomes that apply to the deal.
* **deal_review** (`deal_review`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value for the deal's review.
* **deal_rating** (`deal_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the deal's performance, choose 'Good' or 'Fair'.
* **additional_notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the deal.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Use concrete values for fields like financials and deal_value.
* If unsure about the risk level, choose 'Yes' or 'No' based on your best judgment.
* Review each field carefully to ensure your answers make sense and are consistent.
