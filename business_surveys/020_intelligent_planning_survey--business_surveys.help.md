# Intelligent Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and data from users about their planning processes and overall satisfaction. Your feedback will help stakeholders improve the planning processes and overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields.
3. If a field is not required, you can skip it if you're not sure or don't have an answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **survey_start**: This is the beginning of the survey, where you can enter any relevant comments or information.
* **planning_processes**: Please select all the planning processes that apply to you. Options include:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **feedback_questions**: This is a free-text field for you to provide any additional feedback or comments about the planning processes.
* **decision_making**: Please select how often you use decision-making in your planning processes. Options include:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **strategic_improvements**: Enter any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the planning processes.
* **planning_process_satisfaction**: Please select how satisfied you are with the current planning processes. Options include:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **overall_satisfaction**: Please select how satisfied you are with your overall experience. Options include:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied
* **assigned_tool**: This field is currently not in use, but please do not leave it blank.

## Tips
* Please answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you're unsure or have no answer for a question, you can skip it.
* Your feedback is valuable and will help improve the planning processes and overall user experience.
