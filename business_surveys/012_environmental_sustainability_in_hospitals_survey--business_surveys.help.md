# Environmental Sustainability In Hospitals Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a hospital's environmental sustainability initiatives, green teams, and ideas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the hospital facility and provide the name of the hospital in the "Hospital Facility" field.
2. Check all the environmental sustainability initiatives that are currently in place, such as Sustainability, Energy Efficiency, and Water Conservation.
3. Indicate whether or not a green team is in place and provide justification for your answer.
4. If a green team is in place, provide information about its composition in the "Team Composition" field.
5. Describe the leadership structure of the green team in the "Team Leaders" field.
6. Provide an organizational chart for the green team in the "Organizational Chart" field.
7. List any challenges the hospital is facing in terms of sustainability in the "Challenges" field.
8. Provide a date for the hospital's sustainability performance metrics in the "Performance Across Facilities" field.
9. Enter the hospital's sustainability goals in the "Goals" field.
10. Enter the hospital's sustainability budget in the "Budget" field.
11. Check whether or not incentives are in place to support sustainability initiatives and provide a description of the incentives in the "Incentives Description" field.
12. Select the frequency of incentives, such as monthly or quarterly, in the "Incentives Frequency" field.
13. Add any comments or additional information about the hospital's sustainability initiatives in the "Comments" field.
14. Enter the hospital's email address in the "Email" field.
15. Enter the hospital's phone number in the "Phone" field.
16. List any ideas the hospital has for improving its sustainability in the "Ideas" field.
17. Provide justification for each idea in the "Justification" field.
18. Describe the frequency of the ideas in the "Frequency" field.
19. Add any comments or additional information about the ideas in the "Comments" field.
20. Describe the next steps the hospital plans to take to implement the ideas in the "Next Steps" field.
21. Check the status of each idea, such as implemented or not started, and select all the statuses that apply in the "Status" field.
22. Provide justification for each status in the "Status Justification" field.
23. Describe the frequency of the ideas statuses in the "Frequency" field.
24. Add any additional comments about the status of the ideas in the "Status Comments" field.
