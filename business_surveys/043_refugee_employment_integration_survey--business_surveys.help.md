# Refugee Employment Integration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on refugee employment status, language skills, and support needs. It is designed to help organizations working with refugees to better understand their integration into the workforce and identify areas where they may require assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each field. If you feel that a particular field does not apply to you, simply select "None" for that field. You may choose to provide as much or as little information as you feel comfortable with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Status (Jobless/Unemployed/Employed/Self-employed)**: Select the option that best describes your current employment status.
* **Language Skills - English (None/Basic/Intermediate/Advanced/Proficient)**: Indicate your level of proficiency in English, the language of the country where you will be integrating.
* **Language Skills - Spanish (None/Basic/Intermediate/Advanced/Proficient)**: If you are not fluent in English, indicate your level of proficiency in Spanish, the language of your country of origin.
* **Language Skills - French (None/Basic/Intermediate/Advanced/Proficient)**: If you are not fluent in English or Spanish, indicate your level of proficiency in French, another language that may be spoken in your country of origin.
* **Support Needs (None/Financial/Housing/Employment/Transportation)**: If you are in need of support, select the options that apply to you.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* You may skip any questions that do not apply to you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
