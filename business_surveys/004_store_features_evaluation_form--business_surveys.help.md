# Store Features Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback on various aspects of a store's features. Your responses will help store owners and management teams understand what they can improve to enhance your overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Choose the page corresponding to the feature you want to evaluate.
2. Select the option that best describes your experience with that feature.
3. Repeat the process for each feature until you have completed all 12 pages.
4. Submit your form when you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Product Quality** (`select_multiple`, required: false) - Choose the options that best describe your experience with the product quality, such as "Very Good" or "Fair".
* **Store Ambiance** (`select_one`, required: false) - Select the option that best describes your experience with the store ambiance, such as "Very Comfortable" or "Uncomfortable".
* **Staff Friendliness** (`select_one`, required: false) - Choose the option that best describes your experience with the staff friendliness, such as "Very Friendly" or "Unfriendly".
* **Product Variety** (`select_multiple`, required: false) - Select the options that best describe your experience with the product variety, such as "Very Good" or "Fair".
* **Prices** (`select_one`, required: false) - Choose the option that best describes your experience with the store prices, such as "Very Reasonable" or "Unreasonable".
* **Accessibility** (`select_one`, required: false) - Select the option that best describes your experience with the store accessibility, such as "Very Convenient" or "Inconvenient".
* **Opening Hours** (`select_one`, required: false) - Choose the option that best describes your experience with the store opening hours, such as "Very Convenient" or "Inconvenient".
* **Product Knowledge of Staff** (`select_multiple`, required: false) - Select the options that best describe your experience with the staff's product knowledge, such as "Very Good" or "Fair".
* **Returns and Exchanges Policy** (`select_one`, required: false) - Choose the option that best describes your experience with the store returns and exchanges policy, such as "Very Fair" or "Unfair".
* **Loyalty Program** (`select_one`, required: false) - Select the option that best describes your experience with the store loyalty program, such as "Very Good" or "Poor".
* **Overall Experience** (`select_one`, required: false) - Choose the option that best describes your overall experience with the store, such as "Very Good" or "Poor".
* **Navigation** (`select_one`, required: false) - Select the option that best describes your experience with the store navigation, such as "Very Easy" or "Hard to navigate".
