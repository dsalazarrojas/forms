# Cabinet Color Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from respondents about their cabinet color selection preferences for their business or personal projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): 
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of business or project you are working on. This will help us understand the context of your cabinet color selection.
* **Preferred Cabinet Style** (`preferred_style`, select_one, required): Select your preferred cabinet style. This will help us understand the aesthetic you are aiming for in your cabinet selection.
* **Top Color Choices** (`color_choices`, text, required): List or rank your top three color choices for the cabinets. Please be specific and provide the colors in a format that makes sense to you (e.g., ranked or listed).
* **Finish Preference** (`finish_preference`, select_one, required): Select your preferred finish on the cabinets. This will help us understand the look and feel you are after.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): If you have a budget in mind for the cabinets, select the approximate range that best fits your planned expenses.
* **Purchase Timeline** (`purchase_timeline`, select_one, optional): Choose when you plan to make the purchase for the cabinets.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or preferences you may have about your cabinet color selection.
