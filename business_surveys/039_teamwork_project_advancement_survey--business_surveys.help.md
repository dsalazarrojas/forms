# Teamwork Project Advancement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for gathering feedback and input from team members on various aspects of a project, including project collaboration, milestones, challenges, and improvement ideas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Read through each section carefully and think about your experiences and thoughts related to the project.
2.  Type your answers to the text fields (project\_collaboration and challenges).
3.  Select one or multiple options from the dropdown list for project\_milestones.
4.  Share your improvement ideas for the project in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Project collaboration**: <small>(project_collaboration, text, optional)</small>: Share your thoughts and experiences about the project's collaboration. This can include your feelings about the team's dynamics, communication, and overall collaboration process.
*   **Project milestones**: <small>(project_milestones, select\_multiple, required)</small>: Choose one or multiple options that best describe the project's current state or progress. Please select from the provided list: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
*   **Challenges**: <small>(challenges, text, optional)</small>: Share your thoughts and experiences related to challenges faced during the project.
*   **Improvement ideas**: <small>(improvement\_ideas, text, optional)</small>: Share your suggestions on how to improve the project or the team's performance.

## Tips
*   Be honest and constructive in your input.
*   Keep your responses concise and clear.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the project team for clarification.
