# Fashion Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your fashion preferences, including your favorite colors, sizes, brands, and shopping habits. Your input will help us understand your style better and provide you with more tailored recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. Fill in the text field with your answer if there is no multiple-choice option available.
4. Once you have completed all the questions, click the "Submit" button to send your survey response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Top Categories**: This question asks if you have interest in a particular fashion category. Select "Yes" if you are interested in that category.
* **Top Subcategories**: Select all subcategories that you are interested in. This will help us understand your specific interests within the category.
* **Brand**: Please enter the brand name that you prefer (e.g., Gucci, Louis Vuitton).
* **Color**: This question asks if you prefer a particular color. Select "Yes" for yes, "No" for no.
* **Size**: Please select the size that you prefer (e.g., S, M, L, XL).
* **Price**: This question asks if you prefer a particular price range. Select "Yes" for yes, "No" for no.
* **Gender**: This question asks if you are interested in a particular gender-based fashion category. Select "Yes" for yes, "No" for no.
* **Age**: This question asks if you are interested in fashion that caters to a particular age group. Select "Yes" for yes, "No" for no.
* **Hobbies**: Select all hobbies that you are interested in.
* **Shopping Frequency**: This question asks how frequently you shop for fashion items. Select the frequency that best describes your shopping habits.
* **Shopping Frequency 2**, **Shopping Frequency 3**, ..., **Shopping Frequency 12**: These questions ask about your shopping frequency for specific fashion items. Select the frequency that best describes your shopping habits for each item.
* **Submit**: Once you have completed all the questions, click the "Submit" button to send your survey response. 

**Tips**

* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Take your time and answer all the questions carefully.
* If you are unsure about a particular question, try to provide the best answer you can.
