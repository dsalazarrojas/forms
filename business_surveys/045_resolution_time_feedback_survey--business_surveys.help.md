# Resolution Time Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resolution Time Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback on the speed and quality of support team performance. Your input helps us understand how well we're doing and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question based on your personal experience with the support team.
2. For select fields, choose the response that best matches your experience.
3. If a field does not apply to your situation, you can leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resolution Time Feedback Survey** (`resolution_time_feedback_survey`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form. Please leave this field blank.
* **How quickly did the team respond to your issue?** (`question_1`, select_one, required): Choose the response that best matches your experience:
	+ Immediately
	+ Within 1 day
	+ Within 3 days
	+ Within 1 week
	+ Never
* **How quickly was the issue resolved?** (`question_2`, select_one, required): Choose the response that best matches your experience:
	+ Immediately
	+ Within 1 day
	+ Within 3 days
	+ Within 1 week
	+ Never
* **How often do you usually contact support teams?** (`question_3`, select_one, optional): Choose the response that best matches your experience:
	+ Rarely
	+ Occasionally
	+ Frequently
	+ Almost always
* **How quickly do you usually get a response from the support team?** (`question_4`, select_one, required): Choose the response that best matches your experience:
	+ Immediately
	+ Within 1 hour
	+ Within 1-2 hours
	+ Within 6-12 hours
	+ Never
* **Any additional feedback?** (`feedback`, text, optional): Please share any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the support team.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted with a summary of your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted with a summary of your feedback.

## Tips
- Answer honestly and accurately to provide valuable feedback.
- If you have any concerns or issues not addressed in this form, please reach out to us directly.
- We appreciate your input and will use it to improve our support services.
