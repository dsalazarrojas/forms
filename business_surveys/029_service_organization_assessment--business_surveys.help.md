# Service Organization Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients or customers about the service organization, allowing it to continuously improve its service delivery based on the feedback received.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Service Delivery" section, indicating your level of satisfaction with the services you received.
2. Enter your level of satisfaction in the "Customer Satisfaction" section, on a scale of 1-5.
3. In the "Customer Satisfaction Rating" section, select one or more ratings that match your level of satisfaction with the service received.
4. Provide detailed feedback on areas where you think the service organization can improve in the "Areas for Improvement" section.
5. Add any additional comments or suggestions in the "Comments and Suggestions" section.
6. Finally, enter your contact information in the "Contact Details" section so that the service organization can get back to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* Service Delivery (Service Delivery): 
  • This is the first section of the form where you can rate your overall satisfaction with the services you received.
* Customer Satisfaction (Customer Satisfaction):
  • This is a numeric input where you can enter your level of satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* Customer Satisfaction Rating (Customer Satisfaction Rating):
  • This section is a dropdown select multiple input where you can select one or more ratings from 1-5 that match your level of satisfaction with the service received.
* Areas for Improvement (Areas for Improvement):
  • This section is a text input where you can provide detailed feedback on areas where you think the service organization can improve.
* Comments and Suggestions (Comments and Suggestions):
  • This section is a text input where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions you might have.
* Contact Details (Contact Details):
  • This section is an email input where you can provide your contact information so that the service organization can get back to you.
