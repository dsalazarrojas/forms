# Project Execution Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Execution Readiness Survey is a form designed to assess the readiness of your project for execution. It helps project teams and stakeholders evaluate key aspects of the project, including its launch, team, resources, budget, timeline, and more. This survey aims to ensure that all necessary factors are in place before the project begins, ensuring a successful and efficient project execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and fill in the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Launch Readiness** (`project_launch_readiness`): Describe your project's readiness for launch, including any necessary preparations, dependencies, or obstacles that may affect its start.
* **Team Readiness** (`team_readiness`): Provide information about your team's readiness for the project, including their availability, skills, and experience.
* **Resource Availability** (`resource_availability`): List the resources (people, materials, equipment) available for the project, including their quantity and quality.
* **Project Launch Timing** (`project_launch_timing`): Enter the expected start date and duration of the project.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`): Provide the total budget allocated for the project.
* **Project Timeline** (`project_timeline`): Enter the expected project timeline, including milestones and deadlines.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`): Choose the current status of your project among the options provided: Active, In Progress, or Completed.
* **Business Case for Change** (`business_case_for_change`): Describe the business case for undertaking the project, including its benefits and expected outcomes.
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`): List potential risks associated with the project, including their likelihood and impact.
* **Resource Plan** (`resource_plan`): Describe the resources (people, materials, equipment) required for the project, including their allocation and utilization.
* **Project Plan** (`project_plan`): Provide a detailed project plan, including its scope, objectives, and deliverables.
* **Business Case for Investment** (`business_case_for_investment`): Describe the investment justification for the project, including its expected return on investment.
* **Change Mgt Process** (`change_mgt_process`): Describe the process for managing changes to the project scope, schedule, or budget.
* **Stakeholder Engagement** (`stakeholder_engagement`): Identify key stakeholders and their level of engagement in the project.
* **Project Scope** (`project_scope`): Describe the project's scope, including its goals, objectives, and deliverables.
* **Project Success Criteria** (`project_success_criteria`): Define the criteria for determining project success, including key performance indicators (KPIs).
* **Resource Allocation** (`resource_allocation`): Describe how resources will be allocated and managed during the project.
* **Project Control** (`project_control`): Describe the project control processes, including quality control, risk management, and issue tracking.
* **Project Close** (`project_close`): Describe the project closure process, including its activities and timelines.
* **Project Status Update** (`project_status_update`): Enter the date and time of the last project status update.
* **Project Risk Register** (`project_risk_register`): List potential risks and their mitigation strategies.
* **Project Scope Change** (`project_scope_change`): Describe the process for managing changes to the project scope.
* **Project Closure** (`project_closure`): Choose whether the project is closed or not among the options provided: True, False, or Unknown.
* **Post-Implementation Review** (`post_implementation_review`): Describe the process for reviewing the project's outcome and lessons learned.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Choose your overall satisfaction level with the project among the options provided: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
