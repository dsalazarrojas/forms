# Customer Segmentation Data Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Segmentation Data Collection Form is designed to gather detailed information about customers for business development and marketing purposes. This form is used to collect demographic data, employment details, and customer behavior, which helps businesses create targeted marketing campaigns and tailor their products or services to specific customer segments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your company's name in the "Company Name" field, if applicable.
3. Provide your job title in the "Job Title" field.
4. Enter your company's website in the "Company Website" field, if applicable.
5. Select your industry from the "Industry" dropdown menu.
6. Choose your job function from the list of options in the "Job Function" field.
7. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
8. Select your time zone from the "Time Zone" dropdown menu.
9. Choose your customer segment from the list of options in the "Customer Segment" field.
10. Identify your segment interest(s) from the list of options in the "Segment Interest" field.
11. Select your target audience(s) from the list of options in the "Target Audience" field.
12. (Optional) Choose additional customer segments from the list of options in the "Customer Segment 2" field.
13. Enter any additional information about yourself or your company in the "Additional Info" field, if needed.
14. Check the "Consent Given" field to confirm you've provided accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your company email address.
* **Company Name** (Text): Enter your company's name, if applicable.
* **Job Title** (Text): Enter your job title.
* **Company Website** (Text): Enter your company's website, if applicable.
* **Industry** (Select One): Select the industry you work in or your company operates in.
* **Job Function** (Select Multiple): Choose your job functions, as applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (Date): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time Zone** (Time): Select your time zone.
* **Customer Segment** (Select One): Choose your primary customer segment.
* **Consent Given** (Note): Confirm you've provided accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Segment Interest** (Select Multiple): Identify your customer segments of interest.
* **Target Audience** (Select Multiple): Select the target audience(s) for your business or marketing efforts.
* **Customer Segment 2** (Select Multiple): (Optional) Identify additional customer segments, if applicable.
* **Additional Info** (Text): Enter any additional information about yourself or your company.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required.
