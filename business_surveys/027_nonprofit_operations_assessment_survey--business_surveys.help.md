# Nonprofit Operations Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Operations Assessment Survey is a tool designed to gather feedback from nonprofit organizations about their operational challenges, financial practices, and governance processes. This survey helps identify areas of improvement to optimize nonprofit operations and provide a more efficient and effective service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of your nonprofit organization (Business Surveys or Other).
2. Provide a brief description of your organization.
3. Choose the operational challenges your nonprofit faces (select one or more from the list).
4. Provide any additional help or context you'd like to offer.
5. Enter the current version of the form and date and time of completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Nonprofit Form Name** (`form_name`, `text`, required: false)
Provide a brief name for the form that you'd like to be associated with this submission.

### 2. **Category** (`select_category`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select the category of your nonprofit organization (Business Surveys or Other).

### 3. **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false)
Describe your nonprofit organization in a few words.

### 4. **Operational Challenges** (`operational_challenges`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Choose one or more operational challenges your nonprofit faces. These options include:
* Governance
* Financial Practices
* Program Delivery
* Operational Challenges

### 5. **Nonprofit Form Help** (`form_help`, `text`, required: false)
Provide any additional help or context you'd like to offer.

### 6. **Nonprofit Form Version** (`form_version`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the current version of the form.

### 7. **Nonprofit Date** (`form_date`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the date of completion.

### 8. **Nonprofit Time** (`form_time`, `time`, required: false)
Enter the time of completion.

### 9. **Nonprofit Note** (`form_note`, `note`, required: false)
Add any additional notes about this submission.

### 10. **Nonprofit Email** (`form_email`, `email`, required: false)
Enter your contact email.

### 11. **Nonprofit Phone** (`form_phone`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your contact phone number.

This help guide is designed to assist you in completing the Nonprofit Operations Assessment Survey. If you have any additional questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
