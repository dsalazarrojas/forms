## Purpose

The Employee Satisfaction Drivers Survey is a tool to help management understand the key factors that contribute to employee engagement and satisfaction within the organization. It aims to identify areas of improvement and support employee well-being by providing a platform for employees to share their thoughts, feelings, and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select all relevant options**: In the "Engagement Drivers" section, choose all the options that apply to you, such as autonomy, mastery, purpose, etc.
2. **Rate your score**: In the "Autonomy", "Mastery", and "Purpose" sections, rate your score on a scale of 1-5.
3. **Choose your feeling**: Select your feeling for each day of the week in the "Monday Morning Feeling", "Tuesday Feeling", "Wednesday Feeling", "Thursday Feeling", "Friday Feeling", and "Saturday/ Sunday Feeling" sections.
4. **Add comments**: In the "Monday Morning Comments", "Tuesday Comments", "Wednesday Comments", "Thursday Comments", "Friday Comments", and "Saturday/ Sunday Comments" sections, you can add any comments or feedback you'd like to share.
5. **Enter contact info**: Finally, enter your email and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" sections, if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.
