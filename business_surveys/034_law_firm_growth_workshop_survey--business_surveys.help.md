# Law Firm Growth Workshop Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Law Firm Growth Workshop Survey is designed to gather information from law firms about their experiences, needs, and goals. This information will help us better understand their perspectives and provide insights for the workshop's success.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form's required fields, such as Firm Name, Workshop Topic, Rating, Email, and Workshop Date.
2. Fill out any optional fields that you feel are relevant to your firm's experiences and goals.
3. For select questions (e.g., Select Multiple, Select One), choose the most relevant option(s) from the provided list.
4. If you have any additional comments or notes about your firm, please use the Note field.
5. Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
