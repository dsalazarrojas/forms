# Product Stocking Retailer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from retailers about their experience stocking our products. Your input helps us to identify areas for improvement and provide better service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and click "Submit" at the bottom of the page. You can skip any questions that do not apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Retailer Information
* **Retailer Information** (`Retailer Information`, text, required: false): This is the title of the form. Please enter your name or your store's name to help us identify you.

### 2. Stocking Experience
* **Stocking Experience** (`Retailer Stocking`, number, required: false): Rate your overall experience with stocking this product. Please select a number between 1 (poor) and 5 (excellent).

### 3. Ordering Information
* **Ordering Information** (`Ordering Information`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all the ways in which you ordered this product:
		- Yes: You ordered this product from our website, phone, or in-person.
		- No: You did not order this product from any of our channels.

### 4. Delivery Issues
* **Delivery Issues** (`Delivery Issues`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select yes if there were any issues with your delivery, such as delays or damage. Select no if there were no issues.

### 5. Promotional Needs
* **Promotional Needs** (`Promotional Needs`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all the promotional needs you have for this product:
		- Yes: You would like to receive more promotions or discounts on this product.
		- No: You do not have any promotional needs for this product.

### 6. Overall Satisfaction
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the product and our service. Please select a number between 1 (poor) and 5 (excellent).

### 7. Comments
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, required: false): Leave any additional comments or feedback about your experience with this product.

### 8. Email
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions.

### 9. Phone
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for follow-up questions.

### 10. Store Hours
* **Store Hours** (`Store Hours`, time, required: false): Enter your store's operating hours so we can better understand your availability.

### 11. Store Name
* **Store Name** (`Store Name`, text, required: false): Enter your store's name so we can identify you.

### 12. Manager Name
* **Manager Name** (`Manager Name`, text, required: false): Enter your manager's name so we can identify you.

### 13. Feedback
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, text, required: false): Leave any additional feedback about your experience.

Note: You can skip any questions that do not apply to your experience. Your input is valuable, and we appreciate your time.
