# Feedback On Denial Process - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees who have experienced a denial in the company's processes. The feedback will help us understand the reasons behind the denial and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field in the form with the required information.
2. Make sure to provide detailed and objective feedback in the "Feedback Message" field.
3. If you are a manager, ensure that you have included your name and contact information (email and phone number) in the relevant fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Denial Process Feedback** (`denial_process_form`, `note`, required: false): This field should contain a general description of the denial process that led to your feedback. Please provide a brief overview of the situation.
* **Date of Denial** (`denial_decision_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when the denial occurred.
* **Reason for Denial** (`denial_decision_reason`, `text`, required: false): Describe the reason why you were denied. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: false): Your name as an employee.
* **Feedback Message** (`feedback_message`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can provide more detailed and objective feedback about the denial process. Please be specific and try to avoid personal opinions or biases.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required: false): The name of your manager who was involved in the denial process.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, required: false): The email address of your manager.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, required: false): The phone number of your manager.

Note: The fields with `required: false` are optional and can be skipped if not applicable.
