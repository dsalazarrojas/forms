# Business Future Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand your organization's future-readiness, which is the ability to adapt and thrive in a rapidly changing business landscape. The survey will help us gather insights on various aspects of your organization's capabilities, such as digital transformation, agility, innovation culture, and future challenges. Your input will aid us in providing guidance and support to help your organization stay ahead in the ever-evolving business world.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions based on your current organizational status.
2. For questions with options, select the most accurate description that reflects your organization's current state.
3. If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification or provide additional information in the "Additional Comments" section.
4. Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure we receive a complete understanding of your organization's future-readiness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name_readiness`): This field is required and asks for your organization's name. Please type in the full name as it appears on your business cards or letterhead.
* **Your Current Role** (`respondent_role_readiness`): This field is required and asks about your current role within your organization. Choose the description that best fits your position, e.g., Executive, Manager, or Individual Contributor.
* **Awareness of Future Industry Trends** (`future_trend_awareness_score`): This field is required and asks you to rate how well-informed you are about future industry trends. Use the scale from 1 (Unaware) to 5 (Expertly Informed).
* **Digital Transformation Maturity Level** (`digital_transformation_maturity_level`): This field is required and asks about your organization's current state of digital transformation. Choose the description that best fits your organization's level of digital adoption.
* **Agility of Current Processes** (`operational_agility_rating`): This field is required and asks about your processes' flexibility to adapt to change. Choose the rating from High (Very flexible and rapid) to Low (Rigid and difficult to change).
* **Significant Future Challenges** (`key_future_challenges_identified`): This field is required and asks you to select up to three concerns that are most important to your organization.
* **Innovation Culture Rating** (`innovation_culture_score`): This field is required and asks you to rate how much your organization encourages new ideas. Use the scale from 1 to 5.
* **Investment in Emerging Technology** (`emerging_tech_investment_status`): This field is required and asks about your organization's investment in emerging technologies, such as AI, Blockchain, or IoT. Choose the description that best fits your organization's current investment level.
* **Training Programs for Future Skills** (`skill_development_readiness`): This field is required and asks about the availability of training programs for future skills. Choose the description that best fits your organization's current state.
* **Strategic Planning Horizon** (`strategic_planning_horizon_length`): This field is required and asks about the length of time your organization plans for the future. Choose the time frame from 1 year or less to 10+ years.
* **Additional Comments on Future Readiness** (`additional_readiness_comments`): This field is optional and allows you to provide any specific areas of concern or strength related to your organization's future-readiness.
* **Survey Completion Date** (`readiness_survey_date`): This field is required and asks for the date you completed this survey.
