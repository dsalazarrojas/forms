# Construction Efficiency Benchmark Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us assess your company's efficiency in construction projects. It will gather data on your company's performance in terms of project size, schedule adherence, cost variance, and various areas of improvement. This information will help us benchmark your company's efficiency against industry standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form**: Answer each question in the corresponding field.
2. **Provide accurate information**: Answer questions based on your company's general practices and typical projects.
3. **Select only one answer for select fields**: When you see a select-one or select-multiple field, choose only one answer from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter your company's legal name as it appears on government documents and licenses.
* **Respondent role** (`respondent_role`, `text`, optional): Describe your role or title within the company.
* **Average project size value** (`project_size_average`, `number`, optional): Enter the typical value of contracts your company completes.
* **Typical schedule adherence rate** (`typical_schedule_adherence`, `number`, required): Estimate the percentage of projects completed on schedule. For example, 80% or 90%.
* **Average cost variance percentage** (`cost_variance_average`, `number`, optional): Enter the typical percentage deviation from budget. For example, 5% or 10%.
* **Primary bottlenecks** (`primary_bottlenecks`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the top issues affecting efficiency in your company.
	+ Select one or more of the following options: Material delays, Labor shortages, Design changes, Equipment downtime, Permitting.
* **Use of technology** (`use_of_technology`, `select_one`, required): Describe your company's adoption of technology. Choose one of the following options: Low, Moderate, High.
* **Training frequency** (`training_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate how often staff receive skills training. Choose one of the following options: Quarterly, Semi-annually, Annually, Ad hoc.
* **Improvement priorities** (`improvement_priorities`, `text`, optional): List your company's top areas of improvement.
