# glacier_information_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The glacier information survey is a form used to collect data on glaciers, their locations, sizes, conditions, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, simply select each field with the relevant information for the glacier you are surveying.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by a star (*) at the end of the label.
3. If a field is optional, fill it out if you have additional information that you believe will be helpful for the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Glacier Location** (1, select_one, required/optional): Choose the location of the glacier you are surveying, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Date of Observation** (2, date, required/optional): Enter the date you observed the glacier. This could be the date you visited the glacier or the date you received the information.
* **Glacier Size** (3, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate if the glacier is "Yes" or "No" in terms of size, such as its area or volume.
* **Prophets** (4, note, required/optional): Add any additional information or comments about the glacier that are not included in other fields.
* **Glacier Condition** (5, select_one, required/optional): Choose the condition of the glacier, such as "Yes" or "No", indicating its state or status.
* **Glacier Coloration** (6, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" for each coloration of the glacier, such as white, blue, or black.
* **Observation Method** (7, select_one, required/optional): Choose the method of how you observed the glacier, such as "Yes" or "No", indicating if it was done in person, via satellite, or other means.
* **Glacier Altitude** (8, select_one, required/optional): Select the altitude of the glacier, "Yes" or "No", indicating its height or presence.

## Tips
* Make sure to be as specific as possible with your answers, as this will help provide the most accurate information for the survey.
* If you are unsure about a field, leave it blank or contact the survey team for clarification. 
* If you have any additional comments or information not included in this form, add them to the "Prophets" field.
