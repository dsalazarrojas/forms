## Purpose
The Luxury Customer Experience Audit Form is a survey designed to assess the quality of service standards, staff friendliness, facilities cleanliness, and overall customer satisfaction in luxury establishments. This form helps businesses identify areas for improvement and provide a comprehensive evaluation of their services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: Location, Date, Time, Service Standards, Staff Attendance, Staff Friendly, Facilities Cleanliness, Facilities Quality, Amenities, Amenities Availability, Facilities Condition, Facilities Equipments, Facilities Security, Facilities Parking, Staff Willingness, Staff Communication, Staff Helpfulness, Customer Satisfaction, and Total Scores.
2. Select the correct Audit Type (Mystery Visit, Audit, or Re-audit) and Frequency (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly) from the dropdown menus.
3. Choose the location(s) where the audit was performed from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter any additional Notes about the audit.
5. Enter the name of the person the audit was assigned to and the person who submitted the audit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Location** (`Location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the audit was performed.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the audit in the correct format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the audit in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
* **Service Standards** (`Service Standards`, `number`, required): Rate the quality of service standards (e.g., 1-5).
* **Staff Attendance** (`Staff Attendance`, `number`, required): Rate the staff's attendance to work (e.g., 1-5).
* **Staff Friendly** (`Staff Friendly`, `number`, required): Rate the staff's friendliness (e.g., 1-5).
* **Facilities Cleanliness** (`Facilities Cleanliness`, `number`, required): Rate the cleanliness of facilities (e.g., 1-5).
* **Facilities Quality** (`Facilities Quality`, `number`, required): Rate the quality of facilities (e.g., 1-5).
* **Amenities** (`Amenities`, `number`, required): Rate the availability of amenities (e.g., 1-5).
* **Amenities Availability** (`Amenities Availability`, `number`, required): Rate the availability of amenities (e.g., 1-5).
* **Facilities Condition** (`Facilities Condition`, `number`, required): Rate the condition of facilities (e.g., 1-5).
* **Facilities Equipments** (`Facilities Equipments`, `number`, required): Rate the quality of facilities equipments (e.g., 1-5).
* **Facilities Security** (`Facilities Security`, `number`, required): Rate the security of facilities (e.g., 1-5).
* **Facilities Parking** (`Facilities Parking`, `number`, required): Rate the parking facilities (e.g., 1-5).
* **Staff Willingness** (`Staff Willingness`, `number`, required): Rate the staff's willingness to help (e.g., 1-5).
* **Staff Communication** (`Staff Communication`, `number`, required): Rate the staff's communication skills (e.g., 1-5).
* **Staff Helpfulness** (`Staff Helpfulness`, `number`, required): Rate the staff's helpfulness (e.g., 1-5).
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`Customer Satisfaction`, `number`, required): Rate the customer's satisfaction (e.g., 1-5).
* **Total Scores** (`Total Scores`, `number`, required): Calculate the total scores for all the above fields.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the audit.
* **Assigned To** (`Assigned To`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person the audit was assigned to.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who submitted the audit.
* **Audit Type** (`Audit Type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of audit (Mystery Visit, Audit, or Re-audit).
* **Frequency** (`Frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the frequency of the audit (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Location** (`Location`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the location(s) where the audit was performed.
* **Assigned To** (`Assigned To`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person the audit was assigned to.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who submitted the audit.
