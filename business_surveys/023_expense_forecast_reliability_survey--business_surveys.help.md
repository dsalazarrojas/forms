# Expense Forecast Reliability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather information about your demographic characteristics and your experience with expense forecasting. Your responses will help us improve our forecasting process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all the questions honestly and accurately. 
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose one or multiple options that best fit your situation.
3. For date and time fields, enter the date and time of your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Introduction** (`form_intro`, note, not required): This section provides an introduction to the form. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **demographic_info** (`demographic_info`, select_one, not required): 
	+ Select "Yes" if you identify as part of a demographic group (e.g., male, female, other). 
* **Age** (`demographic_age`, number, not required): Enter your age in years. 
* **gender** (`demographic_gender`, select_one, not required): 
	+ Select "Male", "Female", or "Other" for your gender. 
* **Income Range** (`demographic_income_range`, select_one, not required): 
	+ Select the income range that best fits your annual income.
* **Job Type** (`demographic_job_type`, select_one, not required): 
	+ Select the job type that best fits your current job status (Employee, Self-Employed, Consultant, Retiree).
* **Job Industry** (`demographic_job_industry`, select_one, not required): 
	+ Select the job industry that best fits your job field (Finance, Healthcare, Technology, Creative).
* **section_budgeting_accuracy** (`section_budgeting_accuracy`, text, not required): Enter any thoughts or comments on your budgeting accuracy. 
* **budgeting_accuracy_1** (`budgeting_accuracy_1`, select_multiple, not required): 
	+ Select how accurate you are with your budgeting (Always accurate, Sometimes accurate, Rarely accurate, Never accurate).
* **budgeting_accuracy_2** (`budgeting_accuracy_2`, select_multiple, not required): 
	+ Select how accurate you are with your budgeting (Always accurate, Sometimes accurate, Rarely accurate, Never accurate).
* **budgeting_accuracy_3** (`budgeting_accuracy_3`, select_multiple, not required): 
	+ Select how accurate you are with your budgeting (Always accurate, Sometimes accurate, Rarely accurate, Never accurate).
* **section_streamline_submission** (`section_streamline_submission`, text, not required): Enter any thoughts or comments on your submission frequency. 
* **submission_frequency** (`submission_frequency`, select_multiple, not required): 
	+ Select how often you submit your expense reports (Never, Less than 3 times a month, More than 3 times a month).
* **submission_time** (`submission_time`, date, not required): Enter the date of your submission.
* **submission_time_2** (`submission_time_2`, time, not required): Enter the time of your submission.
* **submission_time_3** (`submission_time_3`, time, not required): Enter the time of your submission.
* **submission_time_4** (`submission_time_4`, time, not required): Enter the time of your submission.
* **submission_time_5** (`submission_time_5`, time, not required): Enter the time of your submission.
* **submission_time_6** (`submission_time_6`, time, not required): Enter the time of your submission.
* **submission_time_7** (`submission_time_7`, time, not required): Enter the time of your submission.
* **section_improving_expense_data** (`section_improving_expense_data`, text, not required): Enter any thoughts or comments on how to improve the expense data collection process.
* **forecast_accuracy** (`forecast_accuracy`, select_one, not required): 
	+ Select how accurate you are with your expense forecasting (Highly accurate, Somewhat accurate, Not very accurate).
* **forecast_accuracy_2** (`forecast_accuracy_2`, select_multiple, not required): 
	+ Select how accurate you are with your expense forecasting (Highly accurate, Somewhat accurate, Not very accurate, Not at all accurate).
* **forecast_accuracy_3** (`forecast_accuracy_3`, select_multiple, not required):
