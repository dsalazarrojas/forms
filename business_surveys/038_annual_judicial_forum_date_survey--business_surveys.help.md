# Annual Judicial Forum Date Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Judicial Forum Date Survey is a form designed to collect information from potential attendees about their interest and availability for the Annual Judicial Forum. The survey will help us better understand the needs and preferences of potential attendees, which will inform the planning and organization of the forum.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields, marked as required in the form.
2. Select your preferred options for the date, format, location, and topics of interest for the forum.
3. If you have any conflicts or scheduling conflicts, please let us know in the "Scheduling Conflicts" field.
4. If you have any dietary restrictions or accessibility needs, please select all that apply in the respective fields.
5. Finally, add any additional comments or information you think will help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (id: 1, `respondent_name`, required): Your full name.
* **Email Address** (id: 2, `email`, required): Your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (id: 3, `phone`, optional): Your best contact phone number.
* **Organization** (id: 4, `organization`, optional): The court or organization you represent.
* **Position Title** (id: 5, `position_title`, optional): Your role.
* **Forum Year** (id: 6, `forum_year`, required): Select the year this is for.
* **Attendance Interest** (id: 7, `attendance_interest`, required): Indicate if you will attend.
* **Preferred Month** (id: 8, `preferred_month`, required): Select the best month for the forum.
* **Preferred Quarter** (id: 9, `preferred_quarter`, required): Select the best quarter for the forum.
* **Preferred Day of Week** (id: 10, `preferred_day_of_week`, required): Select the best day of the week for the forum.
* **Date Option 1 Availability** (id: 11, `date_option_1`, optional): Indicate if you are available on the first proposed date.
* **Date Option 2 Availability** (id: 12, `date_option_2`, optional): Indicate if you are available on the second proposed date.
* **Date Option 3 Availability** (id: 13, `date_option_3`, optional): Indicate if you are available on the third proposed date.
* **Scheduling Conflicts** (id: 14, `scheduling_conflicts`, optional): Provide any known conflicts.
* **Format Preference** (id: 15, `format_preference`, required): Select how the forum should be held.
* **Location Preference** (id: 16, `location_preference`, optional): Enter your preferred city or venue.
* **Session Topics of Interest** (id: 17, `session_topics`, optional): Select all topics that interest you.
* **Topic Suggestions** (id: 18, `topic_suggestions`, optional): Provide any topic suggestions.
* **Speaker Interest** (id: 19, `speaker_interest`, optional): Indicate if you would like to speak.
* **Speaker Topic** (id: 20, `speaker_topic`, optional): Enter the topic you would present.
* **Panel Participation** (id: 21, `panel_interest`, optional): Indicate if you would join a panel.
* **Networking Events** (id: 22, `networking_events`, optional): Select which events interest you.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (id: 23, `dietary_restrictions`, optional): Select any dietary restrictions.
* **Accessibility Needs** (id: 24, `accessibility_needs`, optional): Enter any accessibility needs.
* **Additional Comments** (id: 25, `additional_comments`, optional): Provide any additional information.

## Tips
* Please select only the options that apply to you.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please choose the best available option.
* If you have any conflicts or scheduling conflicts, please provide as much information as possible.
* We appreciate your input and will use it to plan the best possible forum for everyone.
