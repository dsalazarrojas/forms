# industrial_waste_management_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting data about an organization's industrial waste management practices and policies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Introduction**: This page is a note field and will not have any input fields to fill out. It is meant to be read and acknowledged by the user.
2. **Company Information**: This is a text field where you should enter your company's name and other relevant details.
3. **Waste Management Policies**: This is a select-one field with three options: "Yes", "No", or "N/A". Please select "Yes" if your company has a formal waste management policy, "No" if you do not have a policy, and "N/A" if your company does not have a policy or procedure in place.
4. **Management Challenges**: This is a select-multiple field with three options: "Waste management is a top priority", "Waste management is a low priority", or "N/A". Please select all priorities that apply.
5. **Satisfaction Score**: This is a numeric field where you should enter a score between 1 and 10, with 1 being the lowest satisfaction score and 10 being the highest. This represents how satisfied you are with your current waste management policies.
6. **Operations Manager**: This is an email field where you should enter the name and email address of the operations manager responsible for your company's waste management practices.
7. **Facilities Manager**: This is a select-one field with three options: "Yes", "No", or "N/A". Please select "Yes" if your facilities manager is responsible for waste management, "No" if they are not, and "N/A" if you do not have a facilities manager.
8. **EHS Manager**: This is a select-one field with three options: "Yes", "No", or "N/A". Please select "Yes" if your EHS manager is responsible for waste management, "No" if they are not, and "N/A" if you do not have an EHS manager.
9. **Other Contacts**: This is a text field where you can enter any other contacts that may be relevant to your company's waste management practices.
10. **Comments**: This is a text field for any additional comments or information you may want to provide about your company's waste management practices.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions or fields, please select "N/A".
* This form is for your organization's information only and will not be shared publicly.
