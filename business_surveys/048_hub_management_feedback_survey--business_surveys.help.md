# hub-management-feedback-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers or users on your organization's service, staff, or products. It allows you to gather insights and opinions on specific areas of improvement or recognition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company's name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Select the specific topic you would like to provide feedback on using the "Feedback Topic" field.
3. If required, input your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Provide a brief comment or feedback in the "Comment" field.
6. Rate your experience using the "Rating" field.
7. If you have a second company name, enter it in the "Company Name 2" field.
8. Select one option from the "Select One" field.
9. Select multiple options from the "Select Multiple" field.
10. If applicable, input a specific date in the "Date" field.
11. Choose a time for the event or event-related feedback in the "Time" field.
12. If needed, add a note in the "Note" field.
13. For specific feedback on a particular date, input it in the "Date Picker" field.
