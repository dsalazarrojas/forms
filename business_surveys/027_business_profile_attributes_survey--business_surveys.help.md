# Business Profile Attributes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect business profile attributes from respondents, helping us understand their company's characteristics and interests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully, as the information provided will help us tailor our services to your specific needs.
2. Review the form sections to ensure you are providing the correct type of information.
3. Use the hints provided for each field as a guide to help you understand what we are looking for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Client Type** (`client_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of your company: Individual, Small Business, Medium Business, Large Corporation, Non-Profit, Government, or Other.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter your company or organization name.
* **Industry or Sector** (`business_industry`, text, optional): Enter your primary business category.
* **Business Size** (`business_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the number of employees in your company: Solo, 2-5, 6-20, 21-50, 51-100, 100+.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, number, optional): Enter how long your business has been operating.
* **Services or Products of Interest** (`interested_services`, select_multiple, required): Choose the services or products you are interested in: Consulting, Technology Solutions, Marketing Services, Financial Services, Training Programs, Supply and Materials.
* **Estimated Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Choose your estimated budget range: Under $1,000, $1,000 - $5,000, $5,000 - $10,000, $10,000 - $25,000, $25,000+.
* **Purchase Decision Timeline** (`decision_timeline`, select_one, optional): Choose when you plan to decide: Immediate, 1-3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, 12+ months, Still exploring.
* **Preferred Communication Method** (`preferred_communication`, select_one, optional): Choose how you like to be contacted: Email, Phone Call, Text Message, Video Call, In-Person Meeting.
* **Decision-Making Role** (`decision_maker`, select_one, required): Choose your role in purchasing decisions: Final Decision Maker, Key Influencer, Information Gatherer, End User, Other.
* **Company Location** (`company_location`, text, optional): Enter your city and state or country.
* **Website or Social Media** (`website`, text, optional): Enter your web presence.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information we should know.

## Tips

* Please answer each question carefully, as the information provided will help us tailor our services to your specific needs.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on a particular field, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
