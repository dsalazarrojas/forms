# port_innovation_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The port_innovation_feedback_survey form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from users on the services and tools offered by the Port team. This information will be used internally to improve and refine our services and tools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the survey link to access the form.
2. Fill in the required information such as Email and Phone numbers.
3. Rate your experience with the Service Quality question.
4. Select the categories that best describe your experience with the services and tools.
5. Choose the tool that you were assigned to use during your interaction with our services.
6. Select any additional comments you have on your experience.
7. Add any suggestions or comments you have on how we can improve our services and tools.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email (contact_email)** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address to contact you in case we need to follow up on any issues.
* **Phone (contact_phone)** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number to contact you in case we need to follow up on any issues.
* **Rate (rating)** (`rating`, `number`, required/optional): Rate your experience with the services and tools offered by the Port team. Choose a number between 1 and 5.
* **Service Quality (service_quality)** (`service_quality`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the level of service quality you experienced. This will help us identify areas of improvement.
* **Suggestions (suggestions)** (`suggestions`, `text`, required/optional): Add any suggestions you have on how we can improve our services and tools.
* **Additional comments (additional_comments)** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or feedback you have on your experience.
* **Category (category)** (`category`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the categories that best describe your experience. This will help us identify areas of improvement.
* **Assigned Tool (assigned_tool)** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the tool that you were assigned to use during your interaction with our services.
* **Comments (comments)** (`comments`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select any comments that best describe your experience. This will help us identify areas of improvement.
* **Comments (comments)** (`comments`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select any comments that best describe your experience. 

Note: The form is intended to be a free-form input, so you can add as much or as little information as you like. The more details you provide, the better we can tailor our services and tools to meet your needs. 

Note: This form is not intended to be a complaint form. If you are experiencing technical difficulties or issues with our services, please contact us directly through our support channel.
