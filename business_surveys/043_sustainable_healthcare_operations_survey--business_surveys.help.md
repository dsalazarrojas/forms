# Sustainable Healthcare Operations Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainable Healthcare Operations Survey is a tool designed to collect data about your perceptions of sustainable healthcare operations. This information will help us understand your thoughts on how we can improve our services and operations to better support you and the healthcare community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each page carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. Enter your information in the format requested (e.g., date, time, or email address).
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First-page (#page_1, text, not required)**: Please start by answering the first question on the first page. This is an introductory page that sets the stage for the rest of the survey.
* **Second-page (#page_2, select_one, not required)**: On this page, you will find a multiple-choice question. Select one option that best answers the question posed.
* **Third-page (#page_3, select_multiple, not required)**: This page presents a question with multiple options. Select all that apply to answer this question.
* **Fourth-page (#page_4, number, not required)**: Please enter a numerical value to answer this question.
* **Fifth-page (#page_5, email, not required)**: Enter your email address as requested in this field.
* **Sixth-page (#page_6, text, not required)**: Enter a short response to this open-ended question.
* **Seventh-page (#page_7, time, not required)**: Use the time picker to enter a specific time value.
* **Eighth-page (#page_8, note, not required)**: Take a moment to add any additional comments or thoughts.
* **Ninth-page (#page_9, date, not required)**: Enter the date in the requested format.
* **Tenth-page (#page_10, text, not required)**: Enter a short response to this open-ended question.
* **Eleventh-page (#page_11, text, not required)**: Enter another short response to this open-ended question.
* **Twelfth-page (#page_12, text, not required)**: Final thoughts or comments.

## Tips
- Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
- Take your time to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
