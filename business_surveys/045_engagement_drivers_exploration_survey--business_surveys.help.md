# Engagement Drivers Exploration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to explore the drivers and satisfaction of employees and managers within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps listed above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee being surveyed.
* **Enter Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department the employee works in.
* **Enter Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the employee.
* **Enter Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the manager of the employee being surveyed.
* **Enter Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the manager being surveyed.
* **Enter Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the manager being surveyed.
* **Enter Manager Job Title** (`manager_job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the manager being surveyed.
* **Enter Manager Department** (`manager_department`, text, required): Enter the department of the manager being surveyed.
* **Enter Manager Location** (`manager_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the manager being surveyed.
* **Enter Employee Job Title** (`employee_job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the employee being surveyed.
* **Enter Employee Department** (`employee_department`, text, required): Enter the department of the employee being surveyed.
* **Enter Employee Location** (`employee_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the employee being surveyed.
* **Enter Employee Engagement Drivers** (`employee_engagement_drivers`, select_multiple, required): Select the factors that drive employee engagement, such as job security, communication, and recognition.
* **Enter Manager Engagement Drivers** (`manager_engagement_drivers`, select_multiple, required): Select the factors that drive manager engagement, such as job security, communication, and development.
* **Enter Employee Job Satisfaction** (`employee_job_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied the employee is with their job, such as "Very Satisfied" or "Somewhat Satisfied."
* **Enter Manager Job Satisfaction** (`manager_job_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied the manager is with their job, such as "Very Satisfied" or "Somewhat Satisfied."
* **Enter Manager Job Security** (`manager_job_security`, select_one, required): Choose how secure the manager's job is, such as "Highly Secure" or "Not at All Secure."

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* Select all relevant factors that drive employee and manager engagement.
* Choose the correct level of satisfaction and security for the employee and manager.
