# Workplace Culture Trends Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the current state of workplace culture, employee satisfaction, and manager satisfaction within an organization. It will help identify trends and areas of improvement for the organization's leadership and HR team to make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field, or provide detailed answers in the text fields.
2. Ensure that you have permission to fill out this form, as some questions may require sensitive or confidential information.
3. Answer each question truthfully and accurately, based on your personal experience and knowledge of the organization.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Employee Satisfaction
* **Employee Satisfaction**: Select all the ways in which you feel satisfied with your job. This could include feeling supported, valued, and engaged in your work. Options: Satisfied, Dissatisfied
* **Manager Name**: Please enter the name of your manager, as they may be involved in making decisions that affect your work experience.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or role within the organization.
* **Department**: Enter the department or team you belong to within the organization.

### Page 2: Employee and Manager Diversity and Inclusion
* **Employee Diversity and Inclusion**: Select all the ways in which you believe the organization supports diversity and inclusion, such as promoting a positive work environment and respecting different backgrounds and beliefs. Options: Yes, No
* **Employee Diversity and Inclusion Details**: Please provide any additional information about how the organization supports diversity and inclusion, such as training programs or policies.
* **Manager Diversity and Inclusion**: Select all the ways in which your manager supports diversity and inclusion, such as being a role model for diversity and inclusion.
* **Manager Diversity and Inclusion Details**: Please provide any additional information about how your manager supports diversity and inclusion, such as their own experiences and challenges.

### Page 3: Organizational Culture
* **Organizational Culture**: Select all the ways in which you believe the organization's culture supports a positive work environment, such as promoting a sense of community and teamwork. Options: Yes, No
* **Organizational Culture Details**: Please provide any additional information about the organization's culture, such as company values and mission statements.
* **Work Life Balance**: Select all the ways in which you believe the organization supports a healthy work-life balance, such as flexible work arrangements and paid time off. Options: Yes, No
* **Work Life Balance Details**: Please provide any additional information about how the organization supports a healthy work-life balance, such as policies and programs.

### Page 4: Manager's Perspective
* **Manager Satisfaction**: Select all the ways in which you believe your manager is satisfied with their role and responsibilities. Options: Satisfied, Dissatisfied
* **Manager Satisfaction Details**: Please provide any additional information about your manager's satisfaction, such as their own experiences and challenges.
* **Overall Organization Performance**: Select all the ways in which you believe the organization is performing well, such as meeting goals and targets. Options: Yes, No
* **Overall Organization Performance Details**: Please provide any additional information about the organization's performance, such as strengths and areas for improvement.
* **Employee Satisfaction Score**: Enter a numerical value between 1-5 to rate your satisfaction with your job.
* **Manager Satisfaction Score**: Enter a numerical value between 1-5 to rate your manager's satisfaction.
* **Overall Organization Performance Score**: Enter a numerical value between 1-5 to rate the organization's performance.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your answers, as the goal is to gather truthful and reliable data.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they are complete and accurate.
* If you are unsure about any question, please provide as much information as you can, and indicate that you are unsure.
* Remember that this form is for anonymous and confidential use, so please feel free to answer honestly without fear of repercussions.
