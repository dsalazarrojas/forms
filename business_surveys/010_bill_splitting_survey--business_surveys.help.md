# Bill Splitting Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your habits and preferences when it comes to splitting bills in social situations. The data collected will be used to improve bill-splitting apps and understand social dynamics around financial interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select your preferred method for splitting a bill from the provided options.
3. For some questions, you may need to choose multiple options. In those cases, select all applicable options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you dine out with groups?** (`dining_frequency`, select_one, required): This question is designed to understand your frequency of dining out with groups, which can influence your preferences for splitting bills. Choose the option that best describes your habits.
* **What is your preferred method for splitting a bill?** (`primary_splitting_method`, select_one, required): Here, you can select how you usually split a bill when dining with a group. This will help us understand your approach to bill splitting.
* **How should shared items or drinks be handled?** (`shared_items_handling`, select_one, required): This question explores how you handle shared items or drinks when splitting a bill. Choose the option that best reflects your usual practice.
* **How do you usually handle the tip when splitting?** (`tip_calculation`, select_one, required): We're interested in how you calculate tips when splitting a bill. Select the method you usually use.
* **What is your biggest frustration when splitting a bill?** (`biggest_frustration`, select_multiple, required): This question helps us understand challenges you face when splitting bills. Select all that apply from the provided options.
* **Do you use apps to help split bills?** (`tool_usage`, select_one, required): We'd like to know if you use apps to split bills and how frequently you do so. Choose the option that best describes your behavior.
* **How often do you use cash to settle group bills?** (`cash_handling`, select_one, required): This question explores how often you use cash to settle group bills. Select the option that best describes your habits.
* **How comfortable are you discussing splitting before ordering?** (`discussing_split_comfort`, select_one, required): This question assesses your comfort level when discussing splitting bills before ordering. Select the level that best describes your comfort.
* **Does income disparity affect how you split?** (`income_disparity_impact`, select_one, required): We're interested in how income disparity influences your bill-splitting habits. Select the option that best describes your approach.
* **What feature would make a bill-splitting app perfect for you?** (`app_feature_suggestion`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for features that would improve bill-splitting apps, feel free to share them here.
* **I consent to my anonymous data being used for research purposes** (`survey_consent`, select_one, required): This is a survey, and all answers will be kept confidential for research purposes. Please select the option that reflects your consent.

Please answer all questions to the best of your ability, and thank you for participating in this survey!
