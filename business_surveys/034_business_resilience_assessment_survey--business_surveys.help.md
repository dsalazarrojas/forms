# Business Resilience Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Resilience Assessment Survey is designed to assess your company's current level of business resilience. It consists of a series of questions that cover key areas of business resilience, including strategic resilience, operational resilience, financial resilience, and technology resilience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question based on your company's current practices and policies.
2. For each question, select the best answer that reflects your company's current level of resilience.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your supervisor or colleagues for guidance.
4. Please complete the form carefully and accurately, as the results will be used to identify areas for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification card.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your company's name.
* **Your Role** (`respondent_role`, `text`, required): Please enter your position within the company.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): If you want to specify the department where you work (optional).
* **Do You Have a Business Continuity Plan?** (`business_continuity_plan`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about the existence and development of a business continuity plan.
* **Have You Completed a Risk Assessment?** (`risk_assessment_done`, `select_one`, required): This question assesses your company's risk management practices.
* **Crisis Management Readiness** (`crisis_management_readiness`, `select_one`, required): This question evaluates your company's preparedness for crises.
* **Supply Chain Diversification** (`supply_chain_diversity`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about the diversification of your supply chain.
* **Backup Systems and Redundancy** (`backup_systems`, `select_one`, required): This question assesses your company's backup systems and redundancy.
* **Staff Cross-Training** (`staff_cross_training`, `select_one`, required): This question evaluates the cross-training practices within your company.
* **Emergency Financial Reserve** (`emergency_fund`, `select_one`, required): This question assesses your company's financial reserve for emergencies.
* **Access to Credit Facilities** (`credit_facilities`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about your company's access to credit facilities.
* **Appropriate Insurance Coverage** (`insurance_coverage`, `select_one`, required): This question evaluates your company's insurance coverage.
* **Data Backup and Recovery** (`data_backup`, `select_one`, required): This question assesses your company's data backup and recovery practices.
* **Cybersecurity Measures** (`cybersecurity`, `select_one`, required): This question evaluates your company's cybersecurity measures.
* **Remote Work Capability** (`remote_work_capability`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about your company's remote work capability.
* **Top Areas for Improvement** (`top_improvement_areas`, `text`, optional): This question is for you to identify areas where your company can improve its resilience.
* **Support or Resources Needed** (`support_needed`, `text`, optional): This question is for you to suggest resources or support that can help improve your company's resilience.

Note: Please answer each question based on your current practices and policies. The answers should reflect your company's actual level of business resilience.
