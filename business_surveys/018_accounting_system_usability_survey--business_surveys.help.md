# Accounting System Usability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to complete the Accounting System Usability Survey form. The survey aims to collect feedback about your experience with the accounting system's usability.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the survey honestly, based on your actual experience with the accounting system.
2.  Answer questions that are relevant to your experience and interactions with the system.
3.  If you haven't used the system, please do not fill out this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User name** (`user_name`, text, Optional for anonymous feedback): Enter your name, but feel free to leave it blank if you wish.
*   **Role** (`role`, text, Job title or team): Enter your job title or team name, if applicable.
*   **System used** (`system_used`, text, Name of accounting software being evaluated): Type the name of the accounting software you have used.
*   **Frequency of use** (`frequency_of_use`, select\_one, How often you use the system): Select how often you use the system. Choose from the options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely.
*   **Ease of navigation** (`ease_of_navigation`, select\_one, Rate how easy it is to find features): Select your rating. Choose from the options: Very easy, Somewhat easy, Neutral, Somewhat difficult, Very difficult.
*   **Data entry efficiency** (`data_entry_efficiency`, select\_one, How efficient is entering transactions): Select your rating. Choose from the options: Very efficient, Efficient, Neutral, Inefficient, Very inefficient.
*   **Reporting capabilities satisfaction** (`reporting_capabilities`, select\_one, Satisfaction with reporting features): Select your rating. Choose from the options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
*   **Integration with other tools** (`integration_with_other_tools`, select\_one, Is integration sufficient for your needs): Select true if integration meets your needs, otherwise partially or false.
*   **Training and support quality** (`training_and_support`, select\_one, Rate available training and support): Select your rating. Choose from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
*   **Top usability issues** (`top_issues`, text, List up to three primary usability problems): Write up to three primary issues you face with the system.
*   **Suggested improvements** (`suggested_improvements`, text, Ideas for enhancing usability): Suggest any ideas you have to improve the system's usability.
*   **Willingness to participate in testing** (`willingness_to_participate_in_testing`, select\_one, If you can help test improvements): Select true if you are willing to participate, otherwise false.
*   **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, Any other feedback about the system): Add any other feedback or suggestions you have.
