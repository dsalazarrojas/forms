# Audit Coordination Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audit Coordination Communication Survey is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of coordination between the audit team and stakeholders. It aims to gather feedback from audit coordinators to identify strengths and weaknesses in the coordination process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each question and answer based on your personal experience and perceptions.
2. For questions with rating options (e.g., Very Effective, Effective, Neutral, Ineffective), select the option that best reflects your level of satisfaction or experience.
3. For open-ended questions (e.g., text fields), provide detailed descriptions or examples whenever possible.
4. If you are unsure about a question, skip it and move on to the next one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Project Name
**Audit Project Name**: This field is required to identify the specific project being evaluated. Enter the name of the project as it is known.

### Audit Coordinator Name
**Audit Coordinator Name**: This field is required to identify the coordinator who managed the project schedule and requests. Enter your name as the audit coordinator.

### Coordination Performance
#### Effectiveness of the Kickoff Meeting
**Effectiveness of the Kickoff Meeting**: Did the kickoff meeting set clear expectations? Select one of the following options:
	* Very Effective: The meeting was well-organized, and expectations were clearly set.
	* Effective: The meeting was generally well-organized, but some expectations were unclear or missing.
	* Neutral: The meeting was just okay, and expectations were somewhat clear.
	* Ineffective: The meeting was poorly organized, and expectations were not clear.

#### Coordination between different departments
**Coordination between different departments**: How well did teams work together? Select one of the following options:
	* Seamless: Teams worked together very effectively with minimal issues.
	* Good with minor issues: Teams generally worked together well, but some minor issues arose.
	* Poor - siloed behavior: Teams did not work well together, with significant issues or conflict.

#### Satisfaction with Audit Scheduling
**Satisfaction with Audit Scheduling**: Was the timeline realistic and respected? Select one of the following options:
	* Very Satisfied: The timeline was well-planned, and all team members respected it.
	* Satisfied: The timeline was generally well-planned, but some issues arose.
	* Dissatisfied: The timeline was poorly planned, and team members did not respect it.

#### Effectiveness of Audit Management Tools
**Effectiveness of Audit Management Tools**: How helpful were the tools used for the audit project? Select one of the following options:
	* High - Very Helpful: The tools were very effective and helped significantly with coordination.
	* Moderate: The tools were somewhat helpful but did not provide much support.
	* Low - More work than help: The tools were not helpful and actually caused more work.

### Information and Document Flow
#### Efficiency of the Document Request Process
**Efficiency of the Document Request Process**: How long did it take to request and provide documents? Select one of the following options:
	* Excellent: The process was very efficient, and documents were provided quickly.
	* Good: The process was generally efficient, but some delays occurred.
	* Needs Optimization: The process was slow, and delays were frequent.
	* Very slow: The process was extremely slow, and it took a long time to request and provide documents.

#### Were there any issues with information sharing?
**Were there any issues with information sharing?**: Describe any issues that arose with information sharing, such as lack of access, missing files, or wrong versions.

### Future Coordination
#### What was the primary bottleneck in coordination?
**What was the primary bottleneck in coordination?**: Describe the main obstacle that hindered coordination on the project.

#### Suggestions for the Audit Coordinator
**Suggestions for the Audit Coordinator**: Provide suggestions for improvement for the audit coordinator, including any recommendations for changes to procedures or processes.

### Survey Date
**Survey Date**: Enter the date you completed this survey. This field is required to track when the survey was completed.

Note: This guide is meant to be a starting point for completing the form. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact form@gic.mx.
