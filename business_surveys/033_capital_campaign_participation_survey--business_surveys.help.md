This form is designed to gather information from potential supporters about their interest and potential involvement in a capital campaign. The purpose is to gauge the level of interest in supporting the campaign, understand the individual's awareness and knowledge of the campaign goals, and gather information on their preferred ways to support the campaign. It also aims to capture their availability and interest in contributing financially, in-kind, or through volunteering.

It's essential to complete this form carefully, as it will help the campaign organizers understand the needs and expectations of potential supporters. The form is divided into several sections, each addressing a specific aspect of the campaign. Please read each section carefully to ensure you provide accurate and relevant information.

**How to Complete This Form**

1. Read each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. Enter your name, last name, and email address accurately.
3. Select your preferred way of supporting the campaign (financial, in-kind, or volunteering).
4. Indicate your level of awareness and understanding of the campaign goals.
5. Choose your preferred frequency and timing for contributions.
6. Provide any additional information or comments you may have.
