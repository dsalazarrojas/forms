# Social Exposure Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Exposure Impact Survey is designed to measure the effectiveness of social media campaigns in terms of reach, awareness, and influence on the target audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select one or multiple social media channels that your campaign was exposed through (options 1-3).
2. Add brief comments about each selected channel (survey_comments field).
3. Choose a rating for the overall survey impact (survey_rating field).
4. Add any additional notes about the survey (survey_notes field).
5. Select one social channel comment option (social_channels_comments field).
6. Choose the level of awareness of the selected social channels (options 1-3, survey_awareness field).
7. Select multiple or one option for the influence of the selected social channels (options 1-3, social_channels_influence field).
8. Choose the level of awareness of the social media channels (options 1-3, social_media_channels_awareness field).
9. Select multiple or one option for the influence of the social media channels (Yes/No, social_media_channels_influence field).
10. Confirm if participants were affected by social media (Yes/No, social_media_channels_influence field).
11. Enter the name of the campaign (campaign_name field).
12. Add any additional notes about the campaign (campaign_notes field).
13. Select one option for the participants' awareness of the campaign (options 1-3, survey_participants_awareness field).
14. Select one option for the participants' influence of the campaign (options 1-3, survey_participants_influence field).
15. Select multiple or one option for the participants' influence of the campaign (options Yes/No, survey_participants_influence field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **survey_questions** (`survey_questions`, select_multiple, not required): Please select one or multiple social media channels that your campaign was exposed through.
* **survey_comments** (`survey_comments`, text, not required): Add brief comments about each selected channel.
* **social_media_channels** (`social_media_channels`, select_multiple, not required): Please select multiple social media channels that your campaign was exposed through.
* **survey_rating** (`survey_rating`, number, not required): Choose a rating for the overall survey impact.
* **survey_notes** (`survey_notes`, note, not required): Add any additional notes about the survey.
* **social_channels_comments** (`social_channels_comments`, text, not required): Select one social channel comment option.
* **survey_awareness** (`survey_awareness`, select_one, not required): Choose the level of awareness of the selected social channels.
* **social_channels_influence** (`social_channels_influence`, select_multiple, not required): Select multiple or one option for the influence of the selected social channels.
* **social_media_channels_awareness** (`social_media_channels_awareness`, select_one, not required): Choose the level of awareness of the social media channels.
* **social_media_channels_influence** (`social_media_channels_influence`, select_multiple, not required): Select multiple or one option for the influence of the social media channels.
* **survey_participants** (`survey_participants`, select_one, not required): Select one option for the participants' awareness of the campaign.
* **survey_participants_awareness** (`survey_participants_awareness`, select_one, not required): Select one option for the participants' influence of the campaign.
* **survey_participants_influence** (`survey_participants_influence`, select_multiple, not required): Select multiple or one option for the participants' influence of the campaign.
* **campaign_name** (`campaign_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the campaign.
* **campaign_notes** (`campaign_notes`, note, not required): Add any additional notes about the campaign.
