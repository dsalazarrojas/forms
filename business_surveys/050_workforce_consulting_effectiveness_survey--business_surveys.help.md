# Workforce Consulting Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback on the effectiveness of our workforce consulting team. It's essential to fill out this form only if you have had direct interactions with the team, as this will help us understand how well we've managed to engage with you and meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the category that best describes your role in the organization: (CEO, Account Manager, Business Analyst)
2. Provide your Satisfaction Score on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is "not satisfactory" and 10 is "very satisfactory".
3. Enter your comments about your experience with the team.
4. Choose your department from the options provided.
5. If you're part of a department not listed, you can specify it in the text field.
6. Select the consultant you've interacted with from the options provided.
7. Provide any impact measurements of the team's work, or enter other relevant metrics.
8. If you can't find any specific metrics, you can enter other relevant information in the text field.
9. If you'd like to specify a different role, you can do so in the text field.
10. Enter the date you completed this form.
11. Enter the time you completed this form.
12. Enter your email address for follow-up.
13. Enter your phone number for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Engagement Experience** (`engagement_experience`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your experience with the team.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select the category that best describes your role in the organization.
* **Satisfaction Score** (`satisfaction_score`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the team, with 0-10 being the score.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with the team.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Select all departments you're part of.
* **Other (please specify)** (`department_other`, text, optional): If you're part of a department not listed, enter it here.
* **Consultant** (`consultant`, select_one, required): Select the consultant you've interacted with.
* **Impact Measurements** (`impact_measurements`, time, optional): Enter impact measurements, or other relevant metrics.
* **Other (please specify)** (`impact_measurements_other`, text, optional): If you can't find any specific metrics, enter other relevant information here.
* **Other (please specify)** (`role_other`, text, optional): If you'd like to specify a different role, enter it here.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you completed this form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up.
