# Internal Audit Cooperation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internal Audit Cooperation Survey is designed to help management understand the current state of collaboration within the company. It aims to gather information on the frequency and effectiveness of internal communication and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the relevant fields.
2. Select your job title and department from the provided options.
3. Choose your location of work.
4. Answer the questions regarding collaboration, communication, and information sharing by selecting the most suitable options from the provided lists.
5. You can choose multiple options for the questions where allowed.
6. Provide any feedback or comments in the "Feedback" field at the end of the form.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when completing the form.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to contact your supervisor or HR representative.
* This form is meant to be a starting point for internal collaboration assessment, not a comprehensive audit.
* Your feedback and input are valuable and will help improve internal collaboration within the company.
