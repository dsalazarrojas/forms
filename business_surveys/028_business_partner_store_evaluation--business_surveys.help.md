# Business Partner Store Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Partner Store Evaluation form is a tool used to assess the performance of business partner stores. It helps to identify areas of improvement and provide constructive feedback to store managers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the date of your store visit.
2. Enter your name and any other evaluator's name(s) if applicable.
3. Enter the name of the store being evaluated.
4. Enter the store's address or location.
5. Rate the store's cleanliness (1-5).
6. Rate the product display and organization (1-5).
7. Rate the staff's courtesy and helpfulness (1-5).
8. Rate the staff's product knowledge (1-5).
9. Rate the inventory levels (1-5).
10. Rate the pricing accuracy (1-5).
11. Rate the checkout experience (1-5).
12. Rate the overall quality of products sold (1-5).
13. Rate the overall store rating (1-10).
14. Describe any cleanliness issues found.
15. Report any inventory or stocking issues.
16. Report any product quality issues.
17. Provide comments on staff performance.
18. Offer recommendations for improvement.
19. Share positive observations about the store.
20. Indicate if a follow-up is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Evaluation Date (1)**: Date of your store visit.
* **Evaluator Name (2)**: Your name and any other evaluator's name(s) if applicable.
* **Store Name (3)**: Name of the store being evaluated.
* **Store Location (4)**: Address or city of the store.
* **Store Manager Name (5)**: Manager on duty.
* **Store Cleanliness (6)**: Rate cleanliness of the store (1-5).
* **Product Display and Organization (7)**: Rate product display and organization (1-5).
* **Staff Courtesy and Helpfulness (8)**: Rate the staff's courtesy and helpfulness (1-5).
* **Staff Product Knowledge (9)**: Rate the staff's product knowledge (1-5).
* **Inventory Levels (10)**: Rate inventory levels (1-5).
* **Pricing Accuracy (11)**: Rate pricing accuracy (1-5).
* **Checkout Experience (12)**: Rate the checkout experience (1-5).
* **Product Quality (13)**: Rate the overall quality of products sold (1-5).
* **Overall Store Rating (14)**: Rate the overall performance of the store (1-10).
* **Cleanliness Issues (15)**: Describe any issues found during the evaluation.
* **Inventory or Stocking Issues (16)**: Report any inventory or stocking issues.
* **Product Quality Issues (17)**: Report any product quality issues.
* **Staff Performance Notes (18)**: Provide comments on staff performance.
* **Recommendations for Improvement (19)**: Offer specific suggestions for improvement.
* **Positive Observations (20)**: Share positive observations about the store.
* **Follow-up Required (21)**: Indicate if a follow-up is required.
