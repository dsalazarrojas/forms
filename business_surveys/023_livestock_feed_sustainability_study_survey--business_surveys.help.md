# Livestock Feed Sustainability Study Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about farm operations and their feed sustainability practices. It will help gather insights to inform decision-making and improve the management of resources within the livestock feed industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and fill in the requested information accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farm Information** (`farm_info`, text, optional): This section is for providing general information about your farm, including any relevant details that can help us understand your operations.
* **Feed Type** (`feed_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of feed you use for your livestock (Livestock, Grain, Forage).
* **Feed Source** (`feed_source`, select_one, optional): Indicate where you source your feed (Local, Imported, Mixed).
* **Feed Formulation** (`feed_formulation`, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of feed formulation you use for your livestock (None, Other).
* **Farm Size** (`farm_size`, number, optional): Provide the size of your farm operation.
* **Sustainability Measures** (`sustainability_measures`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate any sustainability measures you have implemented or plan to implement (None, Other).
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, select_multiple, optional): List any challenges you face related to sustainability (None, Other).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or information regarding your farm operations.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by individual farm operations and should take approximately 10-15 minutes to complete. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
