# Consumer Loyalty Index Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to measure customer loyalty, satisfaction, and overall experience with the brand. By providing feedback through this form, you will help the brand improve its services and better understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all required questions (marked with *) as thoroughly as possible.
2. For questions with dropdown menus, select the option that best describes your experience.
3. For questions with text fields, provide detailed and honest answers.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
