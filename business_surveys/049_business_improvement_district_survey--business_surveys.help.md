# Business Improvement District Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Improvement District Survey is a tool for business owners and other stakeholders to provide feedback on the district. Your input will help us understand your needs and preferences and improve the district's services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please provide your basic information in the `Respondent Information` section.
2.  If you are a business owner, provide your business information in the `Business/Location Information` section.
3.  Answer the questions in the `District Services and Needs` section about your satisfaction with various aspects of the district.
4.  Provide a general assessment of the district in the `Overall District Assessment` section.
5.  Share your thoughts on the sense of community and interest in district events in the `Community and Events` section.
6.  Finally, provide any additional comments or concerns in the `Additional Comments or Concerns` section and let us know if you are willing to be contacted about this survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Respondent Information

*   **Your Name**: Your name will help us understand who is providing the feedback.
*   **Email Address**: This is how we will contact you if needed.
*   **Phone Number**: Your contact number, if you are willing to share it.
*   **Respondent Type**: Please choose how you are associated with the district: Business Owner, Resident, or Both.

### Business/Location Information

*   **Business Name (if applicable)**: If you are a business owner, provide your business name here.
*   **Type of Business**: Describe the type of business you own or are associated with.
*   **Property/Business Address**: Your business address in the district.
*   **Years in This District**: How long have you been in the district?

### District Services and Needs

*   **Satisfaction with District Cleanliness**: Please choose how satisfied you are with the district's cleanliness.
*   **Feeling of Safety**: Rate your feeling of safety in the district.
*   **Quality of Maintenance and Infrastructure**: Rate the quality of maintenance and infrastructure in the district.
*   **Parking Availability**: Rate the availability of parking in the district.
*   **Priority Improvements Needed**: Select up to 3 priority improvements you think are needed.
*   **Specific Improvement Suggestions**: Share any specific improvement suggestions you have.

### Overall District Assessment

*   **District Economic Vitality**: Rate the economic vitality of the district.
*   **Appeal of the Neighborhood**: Rate how appealing you think the neighborhood is.
*   **Satisfaction with Overall District**: Provide a general assessment of the district.
*   **May we follow up with you**: Let us know if you are willing to be contacted about this survey.

### Community and Events

*   **Sense of Community**: Rate the sense of community in the district.
*   **Interest in District Events**: Let us know if you would attend community events.

### Additional Comments or Concerns

*   **Any additional comments or concerns**: Share any other thoughts or comments you have about the district.
