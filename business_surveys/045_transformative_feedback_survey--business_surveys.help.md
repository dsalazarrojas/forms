## Transformative Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from customers on their overall experience with our company. The goal is to understand areas where we excel and areas where we need improvement. Your input will help us refine our services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of your experience. Please answer each question thoroughly and honestly to provide us with valuable insights. Start by filling out the first page with your name and email address, then move on to the subsequent pages to rate your experience and provide feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you if necessary.
* **Rating** (Number, Required): Choose a number from 1 to 5 to rate your overall experience. 
*   - 1: Very Poor
  - 2: Poor
  - 3: Neutral
  - 4: Good
  - 5: Very Good
* **Rating: Feedback Frequency** (Select One, Required): Choose how often you use or engage with our services.
  - Good
  - Fair
*   **Rating: Areas of Improvement** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the areas where we could improve from the list below:
  - 1 - Very Poor
  - 2 - Poor
  - 3 - Neutral
  - 4 - Good
  - 5 - Very Good
*   (Note: This field is meant to help identify areas where we can improve.)
* **Comments** (Text, Optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience. This is an opportunity to provide more detailed feedback and suggestions.

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields to ensure we receive a complete and accurate survey.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback! Your input is invaluable to us.
