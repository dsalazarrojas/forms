# industry_insights_feedback_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The industry_insights_feedback_survey_form is a tool to gather industry experts' experiences and opinions on the current state of their field. It aims to collect feedback on various aspects of the industry, including company size, job title, and department. The form's results will help identify trends and areas for improvement within the industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill it out.
2. Answer each question truthfully and provide detailed explanations when necessary.
3. Be sure to select the correct options for each question and provide clear and concise answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`intro`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief note to introduce yourself and provide any necessary context for your responses.
* **Industry Experience** (`industry_experience`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select "Yes" if you have industry experience, and "No" if you don't.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the size of your company:
	+ Large
	+ Medium
	+ Small
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your job title.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select "Yes" if you play a leadership role, and "No" if you don't.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your department or area of expertise.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your company name.
* **Field 8** (`field_8`, `text`, required: false): This field is currently empty. You can provide any additional comments or feedback here.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide detailed explanations when necessary.
* Make sure to review your responses carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
