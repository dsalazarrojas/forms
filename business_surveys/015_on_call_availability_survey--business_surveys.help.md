# On Call Availability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The On Call Availability Survey is used to gather information about your on-call availability. This form helps teams and managers understand who is available and when, allowing for efficient communication and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select your preferred contact method(s): Phone, Email, or Chat.
2. Choose the days you are available to be on call.
3. Enter your start and end times for on-call shifts.
4. Select your preferred language(s).
5. If applicable, choose the tool assigned to you for on-call duties.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Prefered Contact Methods** (`preferred_contact_methods`, select_one, **Required**): Select one or multiple ways you are available to be contacted, such as Phone, Email, or Chat.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, select_multiple, **Required**): Choose the days you are available to be on call, selecting one or multiple days from the list of options: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday.
* **On Call Availability** (`on_call_availability`, date, **Optional**): If you are not available on any of the selected days, you can enter a date when you are not available.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, **Optional**): Enter the start time for your on-call shifts.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, **Optional**): Enter the end time for your on-call shifts.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, select_multiple, **Required**): Select one or multiple languages you prefer to communicate in, such as English, Spanish, or French.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, **Required**): If you have been assigned a specific tool for on-call duties, choose it from the list of options, or select "Other" if it's not listed.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, **Optional**): If you have any additional information or comments, enter them here.
