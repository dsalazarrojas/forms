# Market Alignment Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Market Alignment Review Form is a tool used to assess a product's alignment with the market. It's designed to evaluate a product's market fit, strategy, and competitor landscape. This form helps identify areas of improvement, opportunities, and challenges.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with relevant information about the product.
2. Answer each section with as much detail as possible.
3. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the correct option from the provided list.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
5. Review your submission before submitting it.
