# Monitoring Performance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monitoring Performance Survey is a form designed to collect feedback and opinions from employees about their team's performance over a specific period. 

## How To Complete This Form

- Please select the relevant survey date from the calendar or select a specific date to input manually.
- Select a survey period from the available options to determine how often the survey is performed.
- Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
- Choose your team leader from the options provided.
- Answer on a scale from 1 to 100 to rate your team's performance.
- Enter any comments or feedback about your team's performance.
- Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- Enter your phone number for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): Select the date for which you are submitting the survey.
* **Survey Period** (`survey_period`, number, optional): Select the frequency of the surveys, e.g. daily, weekly, monthly etc.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Team Leader** (`team_leader`, select_one, optional): Your team leader's name from the options provided.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, number, optional): Answer on a scale from 1 to 100 to rate your team's performance.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or feedback about your team's performance.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.

## Tips

- Please be as specific and honest as possible when answering the survey questions.
- The answers will be kept confidential and anonymous.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your team leader or HR.
