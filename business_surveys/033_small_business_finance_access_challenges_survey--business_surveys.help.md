# Small Business Finance Access Challenges Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your experience with finance access challenges as a small business owner. Your responses will help us better understand the challenges you face and inform our internal efforts to improve finance access for small businesses.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
2. Select all that apply from the options provided.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Start**: This page serves as an introduction to the survey. Please read through the survey questions carefully and proceed when ready.
* **Business Type**: This field asks about the type of business you own. Please select all that apply from the options:
	+ Small Business
	+ Micro Business
	+ Small Business with Employees
	+ Non Profit
* **Owner Type**: This field asks about the ownership structure of your business. Please select all that apply from the options:
	+ Sole Owner
	+ Joint Owners
	+ Partners
	+ Corporation
	+ LLC
	+ Partnership
* **Location**: This field asks about the location of your business. Please provide a brief description of where your business is located.
* **Financing Type**: This field asks about the type of financing your business has or is seeking. Please select all that apply from the options:
	+ Loan
	+ Grant
	+ Crowdfunding
* **Current Financing**: This field asks about the financing your business currently has. Please respond with a number.
* **Financial Obstacles**: This field is for you to describe any financial obstacles your business is currently facing.
* **Years In Business**: This field asks about the number of years your business has been running. Please respond with a number.
* **Number of Employees**: This field asks about the number of employees your business has. Please respond with a number.
* **Business Assets**: This field is for you to describe your business's assets.
* **Financial Challenges**: This field is for you to describe any financial challenges your business is currently facing.
* **Financial Institutions**: This field asks about the financial institutions you have dealt with. Please select all that apply from the options:
	+ Community Banks
	+ Credit Unions
	+ Commercial Banks
	+ Microfinance
* **Government Agencies**: This field asks about the government agencies you have interacted with. Please select all that apply from the options:
	+ SBA
	+ Local Governments
	+ Non Profit Organizations
* **Other Financial Institutions**: This field asks about any other financial institutions you have interacted with. Please respond 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Other Sources**: This field asks about any other sources of finance you have accessed. Please respond 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Challenges to Financing**: This field is for you to describe any challenges you face when accessing finance.
* **Financing Goals**: This field is for you to describe your financing goals.
* **Access to Funding**: This field is for you to describe your access to funding.
* **Credit Score**: This field asks about your credit score. Please respond with a number.
* **Business Credit**: This field asks about your business's credit score. Please respond with a number.
* **Cash Flow**: This field is for you to describe your business's cash flow.
* **Collateral**: This field is for you to describe any collateral your business has.
* **Financial Partners**: This field asks about your financial partners. Please select all that apply from the options:
	+ Lender
	+ Investor
	+ Financial Advisor
* **Financial Products**: This field is for you to describe any financial products your business has or is using.
* **Survey End**: This page serves as a confirmation page after completing the survey. Please review your responses carefully before submitting.

Please take the time to complete this survey accurately and honestly, as it will help us better understand your finance access challenges. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
