# SaaS Industry Benchmark Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SaaS Industry Benchmark Survey is a tool to help companies compare their performance against their peers in the industry. This form collects information on ARR, revenue range, churn rate, company size, priorities, company name, contact email, contact phone, company profits, notes, comments, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the ARR Range field with the current ARR for your company.
2. Select the Revenue Range that best reflects your company's current revenue.
3. Enter your company's current Churn Rate.
4. Choose the Company Size that best describes your company's current size.
5. Select all priorities that apply to your company from the list.
6. Enter your company's name.
7. Enter your contact email address.
8. Enter your contact phone number.
9. Enter your company's profits for the current period.
10. Enter any additional notes you'd like to provide.
11. Enter any comments about your company.
12-49. Enter any additional information about your company (Other Details 1-10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **ARR Range** (`arr_range`, `number`, required: false): Please fill out the current ARR for your company.
* **Revenue Range** (`revenue_range`, `number`, required: false): Select the Revenue Range that best reflects your company's current revenue.
* **Churn Rate** (`churn_rate`, `number`, required: false): Enter your company's current Churn Rate.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the Company Size that best describes your company's current size.
* **Priorities** (`priorities`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all priorities that apply to your company from the list.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company Profits** (`company_profits`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's profits for the current period.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional notes you'd like to provide.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any comments about your company.
* **Other Details 1-10** (`other_details_1-10`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about your company. Please note that these fields are for any other details that do not fit into the previous categories.

## Tips

* Please fill out all fields that apply to your company.
* You can skip any fields that are not relevant to your company.
* If you have any other information that you'd like to provide, please use the "Other Details" fields.
* If you have any questions, please contact us.
