# Performance Metrics Clarity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information on the current state of performance metrics within your organization, ensuring that everyone is on the same page regarding performance targets and metrics.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your current understanding and experience.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
4. Submit the form when you have completed all the sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief overview of the purpose and context of the survey.
* **Metrics Understood**: This question assesses your understanding of the performance metrics currently in use. Please respond with your best answer.
* **Targets Clear**: This question gauges the level of clarity around performance targets. Please respond with your best answer.
* **Targets Measurable**: In this question, you are asked if you believe that the performance targets are measurable.
	+ 'Yes': The targets are clear and can be measured.
	+ 'No': The targets are not clear or are not measurable.
* **Targets Relevant**: This question asks if you believe the performance targets are relevant to your role or department.
	+ 'Yes': The targets are relevant and align with your role or department.
	+ 'No': The targets are not relevant or do not align with your role or department.
* **Metrics Clear**: This question gauges the level of clarity around performance metrics.
	+ 'Yes': The metrics are clear.
	+ 'No': The metrics are not clear.
* **Feedback**: Please provide any additional feedback or comments you have about the performance metrics.
* **Next Steps**: This question asks if you believe there are next steps that need to be taken to improve the clarity of performance metrics.
	+ 'Yes': There are next steps that need to be taken.
	+ 'No': There are no next steps that need to be taken.
* **Performance Metrics Clarity Score**: Please enter a numerical value between 0 and 10 to indicate your perception of the current state of performance metrics within your organization. A score of 0 indicates complete confusion, while a score of 10 indicates perfect clarity.
