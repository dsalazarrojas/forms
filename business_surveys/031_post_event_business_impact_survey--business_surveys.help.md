# post event business impact survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "post event business impact survey" form is designed to capture information about an event that has occurred. The form helps to identify the purpose and impact of the event on the business. It provides a platform for gathering feedback and insights that can be used to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the event ready.
2. Select the correct event date and time from the calendar.
3. Enter the name of the event in the "Event Name" field.
4. Choose the type of event from the "Event Type" field.
5. Provide a brief description of the event purpose in the "Event Purpose" field.
6. Enter the outcome of the event in the "Event Outcome" field.
7. Rate your satisfaction with the event in the "Event Satisfaction" field.
8. Select the correct answer to the questions in the "Question 1" to "Question 10" fields.
9. Enter your feedback and recommendations in the "Event Feedback" and "Event Recommendations" fields.
10. Provide any additional comments about the event in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, text, required): Choose the type of event from the options provided.
* **Event Purpose** (`event_purpose`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the event purpose.
* **Event Outcome** (`event_outcome`, text, required): Enter the outcome of the event.
* **Event Goal** (`event_goal`, text, required): Enter the goal of the event.
* **Expected Outcome** (`expected_outcome`, text, required): Enter the expected outcome of the event.
* **Actual Outcome** (`actual_outcome`, text, required): Enter the actual outcome of the event.
* **Event Satisfaction** (`event_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Event Feedback** (`event_feedback`, text, required): Enter your feedback about the event.
* **Event Recommendations** (`event_recommendations`, select_multiple, required): Select any recommendations for future events.
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, number, required): Enter the number of prophets who attended the event.
