# Distribution Acceptance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from various roles within a company about their distribution performance. Your input will help us better understand your needs and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the fields as completely as possible.
2. Choose the best option that fits your role and location from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter your email address for our response.
4. Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
5. Rate your distribution performance from 1-10.
6. Add any comments or feedback about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name?** (1) - Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Your Role** (2) - Select the best description of your role within the company. This will help us understand your perspective on our services.
* **Your Department** (3) - Choose the department you work for to help us know which team we're serving.
* **Your Location** (4) - Select all relevant locations for our teams serving you. This could be a single city, region, or country.
* **Email** (5) - Enter your company email address so we can respond to your feedback.
* **Phone** (6) - Enter your company phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Distribution Performance Rating** (7) - Rate your satisfaction with our distribution services on a scale of 1-10.
* **Comments** (8) - Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our services.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
- If you have any trouble completing the form, don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
- We appreciate your time and feedback!
