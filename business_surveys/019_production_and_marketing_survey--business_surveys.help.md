# Production And Marketing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from end-users on production and marketing aspects of the business. This survey aims to collect information about whether a particular activity is being used for production purposes or for marketing activities, and to gather feedback from customers and products used in the production process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions and ensure you are providing accurate information.
2. Choose the correct option for each question.
3. Fill in any additional comments or feedback where required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Production Survey** (`production_survey`, select_one, required/optional): Choose 'Yes' if the activity is being used for production purposes, and 'No' otherwise.
- **Marketing Survey** (`marketing_survey`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose all the activities that are being used for marketing purposes.
- **Product Feedback** (`product_feedback`, text, required/optional): Provide additional feedback or comments about the product or service being used in the production process.
- **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, text, required/optional): Provide feedback or comments about the customer experience with the product or service being used in the production process.

## Tips
- Be honest in your responses to provide accurate and helpful feedback.
- Ensure you are providing relevant information for each question.
