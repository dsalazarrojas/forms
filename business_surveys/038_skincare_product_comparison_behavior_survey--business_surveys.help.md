# Skincare Product Comparison Behavior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather insights on how customers make decisions when purchasing skincare products. Your participation will help us better understand your habits and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form by answering each question as accurately as possible.
2. Make sure to answer all required questions (marked as required).
3. You can leave optional questions blank if you don't want to provide an answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: This is the first section of the form. Please provide basic information about yourself.
* **Skincare Products**: Select all the skincare products you know about. This will help us understand your familiarity with various products.
* **Frequency of Use**: Indicate how often you use skincare products. This will help us understand your usage habits.
* **Purchase Decision**: Choose the factor that influences your purchase decision for skincare products. This can be Brand, Price, Quality, or Recommendation.
* **How Many Skincare Products Do You Use?**: Please answer how many skincare products you use regularly.
* **How Often Do You Use Skincare Products?**: Indicate how often you use skincare products in a typical week.
* **How Many Skincare Products Have You Purchased in the Last Year?**: Answer how many skincare products you've purchased in the last 12 months.
* **Which Factor Influenced Your Purchase Decision?**: Choose all the factors that influenced your last skincare product purchase decision.
* **How Much Skincare Product Research Do You Do Before Making a Purchase?**: Answer how much research you do before making a skincare product purchase.
* **Who Do You Most Often Ask for Skincare Product Recommendations?**: Select all the people you usually ask for skincare product recommendations.
* **Where Do You Usually Buy Skincare Products?**: Choose where you buy skincare products.
* **How Often Do You Purchase Skincare Products?**: Indicate how often you purchase skincare products.
* **What Is Your Main Reason for Buying Skincare Products?**: Choose why you buy skincare products.
* **Where Do You Usually Purchase Skincare Products?**: Select where you usually buy skincare products.
* **Where Do You Purchase Skincare Products?**: This is a similar question to the previous one, please answer consistently with your previous answer.

Please note that the last two questions seem to be duplicates, and you may want to consider removing one of them to avoid confusing the user.
