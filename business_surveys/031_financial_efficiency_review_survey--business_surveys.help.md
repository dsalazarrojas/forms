# Financial Efficiency Review Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Efficiency Review Survey is designed to gather feedback and scores on the department's financial processes. This form is completed by staff members to provide an objective view of the current state of financial processes and suggest areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing as much detail as possible in each field.
2. The form is divided into several sections, each covering a different aspect of financial efficiency.
3. For numerical fields, please enter scores based on your level of satisfaction with the current state of each process.
4. For text fields, please provide specific examples or comments on each process.
5. Make sure to save your progress regularly to avoid losing any entered data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Challenges** (`challenges_identified`, text, required): Please describe any challenges or issues you have experienced with the current financial processes.
* **Financial Management** (`financial_management`, text, required): Share your thoughts on the effectiveness and efficiency of financial management within the department.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, text, optional): Provide your general satisfaction level with the current financial processes.
* **Financial Processes** (`departmental_financial_processes`, text, optional): Add any general comments about the financial processes in your department.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Suggest ways to improve the financial processes within your department.
* **Financial Efficiency Score** (`financial_efficiency_score`, number, required): Enter a score from 1 to 5 to indicate your level of satisfaction with the current financial efficiency.
* **Financial Efficiency Review Score** (`financial_efficiency_review_score`, number, required): Enter a final score for the review, based on your overall satisfaction.
* **Financial Efficiency Review Date** (`financial_efficiency_review_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the review.
* **Financial Efficiency Review Time** (`financial_efficiency_review_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the review.
* **Financial Efficiency Review Note** (`financial_efficiency_review_note`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or observations about the review.
* **Financial Efficiency Review Email** (`financial_efficiency_review_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for any follow-up discussions.
* **Feedback** (`feedback_from_staff`, select_multiple, optional): Please select from "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the current financial processes are meeting your needs.
* **Financial Processes Score** (`financial_processes_score`, number, required): Enter a score for the effectiveness of the financial processes.
* **Staff Engagement** (`staff_engagement`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on staff engagement and involvement in financial processes.
* **Financial Management Score** (`financial_management_score`, number, required): Enter a score for the effectiveness of financial management.
* **Challenges Score** (`challenges_identified_score`, number, required): Enter a score to rate the challenges identified.
* **Improvement Suggestions Score** (`improvement_suggestions_score`, number, required): Enter a score for the effectiveness of improvement suggestions.
* **Staff Engagement Score** (`staff_engagement_score`, number, required): Enter a score for staff engagement.

Please note that all fields marked as required must be completed to submit the form.
