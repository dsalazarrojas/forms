# Garment Waste Audit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about garment waste in facilities. It aims to identify common challenges and improvement ideas to reduce waste and enhance sustainability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form for each facility that needs to be audited.
2. Ensure you have access to relevant information about the facility's waste quantities, production process, and disposal methods.
3. Enter accurate and detailed information in each field to help the auditor understand the facility's situation.
4. If a field is not applicable, select "Not applicable" as the response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Information** (`<name>`, `number`, required): Enter the name and ID number of the facility being audited.
* **Production Process** (`<text>`, required): Describe the facility's production process, including all the steps involved in producing garments.
* **Waste Quantities** (`<number>`, required): Report the total quantity of waste generated by the facility.
* **Disposal Methods** (`<select_multiple>`, not required): Select all the methods used for disposing of waste, including Landfill, Recycling, Energy recovery, Incineration, Other, or Not applicable.
* **Common Challenges** (`<text>`, required): Identify common challenges faced by the facility related to garment waste.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`<select_multiple>`, not required): Suggest improvement ideas to reduce waste, such as Improved supply chain collaboration, Improved production processes, Reduced material usage, Other, or Not applicable.
* **Comments** (`<text>`, not required): Add any additional comments or information about the facility's waste management practices.
* **Facility Contact** (`<text>`, not required): Enter the contact information of the facility, including the name and address.
* **Contact Person** (`<text>`, not required): Enter the name and title of the contact person responsible for the facility's waste management.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Select all applicable disposal methods and improvement ideas.
* Enter detailed and specific information in the Production Process field.
* Use the "Not applicable" option when a field is not relevant to the facility's waste management practices.
* This form is an essential tool for identifying areas of improvement and developing sustainable practices in the garment industry. Please fill it out carefully to contribute to this goal.
