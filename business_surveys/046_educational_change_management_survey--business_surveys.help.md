# Educational Change Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a tool to help educational institutions and individuals track, document, and manage changes made within their school or campus environment. By filling out this form, you will provide valuable information to aid in the understanding and improvement of the change management process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill in the required fields and submit the form.
2.  Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
3.  Select the correct options for the select multiple fields.
4.  If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the note fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **School Name**: This field is used to identify the school or institution that the change is being made within.
*  **Campus Name**: This field is used to identify the specific campus within the school that the change is being made within.
*  **Initiative Name**: This field is used to name the specific initiative or project being managed.
*  **Description**: This field is used to describe the change being managed, including its scope and goals.
*  **Department Name**: This field is used to identify the department or unit within the school or campus that the change is being managed within.
*  **Team Name**: This field is used to identify the team members involved in the change management process.
*  **Role**: This field is used to describe the specific role that the individual submitting the form holds in the change management process.
*  **Email**: This field is used to collect the email address of the individual submitting the form or the contact person for the change.
*  **Phone**: This field is used to collect the phone number of the individual submitting the form or the contact person for the change.
*  **Start Date**: This field is used to record the start date of the change or initiative.
*  **End Date**: This field is used to record the end date of the change or initiative.
*  **Change Management System**: This field is used to determine whether a change management system is used or not.
	+  Please select 'Yes' if a formal system is in place.
	+  Please select 'No' if there is no formal system in place.
*  **Change Management Approvals**: This field is used to describe the approval process for the change.
*  **Change Management Process**: This field is used to describe the specific process used to manage the change.
	+  Please select all applicable options, e.g., 'Yes' for processes that are applicable.
*  **Change Management Ownership**: This field is used to describe who owns the change management process.
*  **Feedback**: This field is used for any additional comments or feedback about the change management process.
*  **Comments**: This field is used for any additional comments or notes about the change or initiative.
