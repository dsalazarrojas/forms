# Entrepreneurship Coaching Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entrepreneurship Coaching Feedback form is designed to gather feedback from clients on their experiences with the entrepreneurship coaching program. This form is used to assess the relevance and effectiveness of the coaching sessions, identify areas for improvement, and provide a follow-up opportunity for the coach to discuss next steps with the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly to provide the coach with valuable feedback.
2. Fill out the form on one page at a time, using the provided fields as a guide.
3. Review each question carefully before providing your response to ensure you provide the most accurate information possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Topic (<name>)**: This field is used to describe the topic of the current coaching session. Please provide a brief and concise description of what was discussed during the session.
* **Relevance (<name>)**: This field asks you to rate the relevance of the session topic, with higher scores indicating higher relevance. Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 10.
* **Coach Guidance (<name>)**: This field asks you to rate the guidance provided by the coach during the session, with higher scores indicating more effective guidance. Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 10.
* **Client Feedback (<name>)**: This field allows you to provide any comments or feedback you have for the coach or the session. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Areas For Improvement (<name>)**: This field asks you to indicate which areas you believe need improvement. Please select 'Yes' for each area that requires improvement.
* **Session Outcome (<name>)**: This field asks you to rate the outcome of the session, selecting 'Yes' if you felt it was successful.
* **Follow Up (<name>)**: This field is for scheduling a follow-up meeting or discussion with the coach.
* **Next Steps (<name>)**: This field requires you to describe the next steps you will take after the coaching session. Please provide specific and actionable plans.
* **Comments (<name>)**: This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Session Topic 2 (<name>)**: This field is used to describe the topic of the next coaching session (if applicable).
* **Relevance 2 (<name>)**: This field asks you to rate the relevance of the next session topic, with higher scores indicating higher relevance.
* **Coach Guidance 2 (<name>)**: This field asks you to rate the guidance provided by the coach during the next session, with higher scores indicating more effective guidance.
* **Client Feedback 2 (<name>)**: This field allows you to provide any comments or feedback you have for the coach or the next session. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Areas For Improvement 2 (<name>)**: This field asks you to indicate which areas you believe need improvement for the next session. Please select 'Yes' for each area that requires improvement.
* **Next Steps 2 (<name>)**: This field requires you to describe the next steps you will take after the next coaching session. Please provide specific and actionable plans.
