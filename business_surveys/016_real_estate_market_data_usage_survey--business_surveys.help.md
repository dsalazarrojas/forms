# real_estate_market_data_usage_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the usage of real estate market data from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions truthfully and completely.
2. For questions with options, select the options that best describe your situation.
3. For text fields, provide as much detail as possible.
4. For time fields, enter the time frequency of data use (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header**: This field is not editable. It provides an introduction to the form.
* **demographic_info**: Please provide your demographic information, such as your name, job title, job type, industry, and organization.
* **company_info**: If you are affiliated with a company, provide their name.
* **job_function**: Check "Yes" if you use real estate market data as part of your job function.
* **job_classification**: If you use real estate market data for your job, classify your job function by checking "Yes".
* **data_provider**: Indicate who provides you with real estate market data, by checking "Yes" if you use data from providers like Zillow, Realtor.com, etc.
* **data_type**: Select the types of real estate market data you use, by checking "Yes" for each type.
* **data_frequency**: Tell us how often you use real estate market data. For example, daily, weekly, monthly.
* **data_licensing**: Check "Yes" if you have a license to use real estate market data.
* **data_satisfactory**: Rate the quality of real estate market data you use, from 1 to 10.
* **data_quality**: Rate the quality of real estate market data you use, from 1 to 10.
* **data_frequency_2**: Indicate how often you use real estate market data for a second set of data.
* **data_licensing_2**: Check "Yes" if you have a license to use real estate market data for a second set.
* **data_satisfactory_2**: Rate the quality of real estate market data for a second set, from 1 to 10.
* **data_quality_2**: Rate the quality of real estate market data for a second set, from 1 to 10.
* **additional_info**: If you have any additional comments about the use of real estate market data, please provide them here.
* **survey_comments**: If you have any comments or suggestions for how we can improve our services, please provide them here.
* **email**: Provide your email for contact.
* **phone**: Provide your phone number for contact.
* **name**: Provide your name.
* **job_title**: Provide your job title.
* **job_type**: Provide your job type.
* **job_status**: Provide your job status.
* **industry**: Provide the industry you work in.
* **organization**: Provide the organization you work for.
