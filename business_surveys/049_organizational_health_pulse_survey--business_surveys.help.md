# Organizational Health Pulse Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Health Pulse Survey is a tool to help employees provide feedback on the overall health and well-being of their organization. This survey is designed to be completed by employees to provide insights on the culture and work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best describe your experiences and perceptions.
3. Be as detailed as possible in the "Feedback" and "Comments" sections.
4. Use the "Additional Comments" section for any further information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What's your general satisfaction with the organization? (`survey_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required)
* This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the organization. Select all options that apply to you:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Low
	+ Very Low

### 2. How would you describe the quality of communication within the organization? (`communication`, `select_one`, required)
* This question asks about the quality of communication within the organization. Select one option that best describes your experience:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ Very Low

### 3. How would you rate the level of support from our leaders? (`leadership_support`, `select_multiple`, required)
* This question asks about the level of support from leaders in the organization. Select all options that apply to you:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ Very Low

### 4. What's your well-being and resilience at work? (`team_wellness`, `select_one`, required)
* This question asks about your well-being and resilience at work. Select one option that best describes your experience:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ Very Low

### 5. What's your overall satisfaction with your team? (`team_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required)
* This question asks about your overall satisfaction with your team. Select all options that apply to you:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
	+ Very Low

### 6. Feedback (no default value, `note`, optional)
* This section is for you to provide any feedback you'd like to share with the organization.

### 7. Comments (no default value, `note`, optional)
* This section is for you to provide any additional comments or thoughts about your experience in the organization.

### 8. Additional Comments (no default value, `note`, optional)
* This section is for you to provide any further comments or suggestions you'd like to share with the organization.
