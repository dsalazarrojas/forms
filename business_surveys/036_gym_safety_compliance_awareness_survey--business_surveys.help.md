# Gym Safety Compliance Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to assess employee awareness and compliance with safety protocols within the gym environment.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the required fields with your name, email, phone number, supervisor's name, and job title.
2. Select the relevant options for safety procedures and emergency contacts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or company records.
* **Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Job Title** (`employee_job_title`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your job title.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, optional): If applicable, enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Supervisor Job Title** (`supervisor_job_title`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your supervisor's job title.
* **Supervisor Department** (`supervisor_department`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your supervisor's department.
* **Supervisor Department** is likely a duplicate field and may not be necessary. Please disregard if not applicable.
* **Safety Procedure** (`safety_procedure`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the safety procedure you follow for emergencies.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): If applicable, enter the emergency contact person's email address.
* **Emergency Contact External** (`emergency_contact_ext`, select_one, optional): If applicable, select 'Yes' if the emergency contact is external.
* **Emergency Contact External Options** (`emergency_contact_ext_options`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, select multiple options for external emergency contact information.
* **Emergency Contact External External** (`emergency_contact_ext_ext`, select_one, optional): This field seems to be a duplicate of "Emergency Contact External".
* **Safety Training** (`safety_training`, select_one, optional): If applicable, select 'Yes' if you have received safety training.
* **Safety Training Options** (`safety_training_options`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, select multiple options for safety training information.
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, select_one, optional): If applicable, select 'Yes' if you have access to safety equipment.
* **Safety Equipment Options** (`safety_equipment_options`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, select multiple options for safety equipment information.
* **Safety Equipment External** (`safety_equipment_ext`, select_one, optional): This field seems to be a duplicate of "Safety Equipment".
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, number, optional): This field is likely redundant and can be disregarded.
* **Emergency Contact External Options** (`emergency_contact_ext_options`, select_multiple, optional): This field is likely a duplicate and can be disregarded.
* **Emergency Contact External External** (`emergency_contact_ext_ext`, select_one, optional): This field is likely a duplicate and can be disregarded.
