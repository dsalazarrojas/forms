# Drone Flight Log Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Drone Flight Log Survey is a form designed to collect information about each flight performed by drones. This form will help in maintaining a centralized log of flight activities, which can be used by management to track and analyze flight performance, identify safety issues, and improve overall drone operation efficiency.

The information collected through this form is used to:

* Monitor drone usage and flight frequency
* Identify potential safety risks and incidents
* Analyze flight performance and distance covered
* Record weather conditions during flight
* Track emergency procedures followed

Please ensure you complete all mandatory fields to ensure accurate and thorough reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the flight from the calendar provided.
2. Enter the time of the flight.
3. Type or paste the location where the flight was conducted.
4. Enter the distance covered during the flight (in meters or kilometers).
5. Provide notes about the flight, if any (e.g., any notable events or issues encountered during the flight).
6. Select the weather conditions from the provided options.
7. If an incident or emergency occurred during the flight, provide details in the "Incident Report" field.
8. Mark the box "Emergency Procedure Followed" if any emergency procedures were followed during the flight.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Flight Date** (`flight_date`): Enter the date of the flight in the calendar format provided. This is an important field as it helps in tracking flight frequency and duration.
* **Flight Time** (`flight_time`): Enter the start and end times of the flight in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00-10:00).
* **Flight Location** (`flight_location`): Type or paste the exact location where the flight was conducted. This can be in the form of an address, GPS coordinates, or a descriptive phrase (e.g., "Field near Johnson Farm").
* **Flight Distance** (`flight_distance`): Enter the distance covered during the flight in meters or kilometers. This helps in analyzing flight performance and identifying potential issues.
* **Flight Notes** (`flight_notes`): Provide any additional notes or comments about the flight, if any. This can include any issues or concerns raised during or after the flight.
* **Weather Conditions** (`weather_conditions`): Select the weather conditions during the flight from the provided options:
	+ Clear Sky
	+ Partly Cloudy
	+ Cloudy
	+ Windy
	+ Raining
* **Incident Report** (`incident_report`): If an incident or emergency occurred during the flight, provide details in this field. This helps in identifying potential safety risks and improving future flights.
* **Emergency Procedure Followed** (`emergency_procedure_followed`): Mark the box if any emergency procedures were followed during the flight. This helps in tracking the effectiveness of emergency procedures and improving response times.
* **Pilot Notes** (`pilot_notes`): Provide any additional comments or notes from the pilot about the flight. This can include insights or observations that may be useful for future flights.

## Tips
* Ensure all mandatory fields are completed to ensure accurate and thorough reporting.
* If an incident occurred during the flight, please provide detailed information to help improve safety and response procedures.
* Double-check the accuracy of the flight details to prevent errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to your supervisor or support team for guidance.
