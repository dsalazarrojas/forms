# socioeconomic_status_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The socioeconomic_status_survey form is designed to collect socioeconomic data about individuals or households. This data may be used for research, statistical analysis, or other business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your knowledge and understanding.
2. Fill in the required fields, denoted by asterisks (\*). If a field is optional, you can choose not to fill it in.
3. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **household_income** (number): Please enter your household's annual income. This field is optional.
* **household_size** (number): Enter the number of people living in your household. This field is optional.
* **occupation** (select_one): Check if anyone in your household has a job. This field is optional.
	+ 'Yes' means that at least one person in the household has a job.
	+ 'No' means that no one in the household has a job.
* **education_level** (select_multiple): Indicate the level of education of the household members. This field is optional.
	+ High: Household members have achieved a higher level of education.
	+ Low: Household members have achieved a lower level of education.
* **employment_status** (select_one): Check the employment status of the household members. This field is optional.
	+ Active: At least one household member is actively employed.
	+ Inactive: No household member is actively employed.
* **income_frequency** (number): Please enter the frequency of income for the household. This field is optional.
* **household_assets** (number): Enter the number of assets your household possesses. This field is optional.
* **education_field_of_study** (text): Describe the field of education or study of the household members. This field is optional.
* **occupation_field_of_study** (text): Describe the field of occupation or work experience of the household members. This field is optional.
* **employment_field_of_study** (text): Describe the field of employment or work experience of the household members. This field is optional.
* **education_level_of_education_field_of_study** (select_multiple): Indicate the level of education related to the field of education or study. This field is optional.
	+ High: The household members have a high level of education related to the field.
	+ Low: The household members have a low level of education related to the field.
* **employment_status_of_employment_field_of_study** (select_one): Check the employment status related to the field of employment or work experience. This field is optional.
	+ Active: At least one household member is actively employed in the related field.
	+ Inactive: No household member is actively employed in the related field.
