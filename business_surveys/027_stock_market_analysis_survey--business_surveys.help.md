# Stock Market Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stock Market Analysis Survey is a tool for users to provide their investment preferences, risk tolerance, and market insights. This information will help financial experts analyze and provide personalized advice on investments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by navigating through the form's different pages, each with its own set of questions.
2. For each page, review the questions carefully and answer accordingly.
3. Use the options provided for each question to select the most accurate response.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled before proceeding to the next page.
