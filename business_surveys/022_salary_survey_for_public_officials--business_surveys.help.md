# Salary Survey For Public Officials - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the salaries and benefits of public officials. This information will be used to help the organization understand the current state of compensation for public officials and make informed decisions to improve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your public official role from the options listed.
2. Enter your current salary range for the past year.
3. Enter your current salary range for the present year.
4. Enter your current salary range for the next year (if applicable).
5. List any benefits you currently receive (e.g. health insurance, retirement plan, etc.).
6. Rate your overall satisfaction with your current compensation.
7. List any additional benefits you receive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Public Official Roles
* **Public Official Roles**: Select your current or previous role(s) as a public official, for example, Mayor, City Council Member, etc.

### Salary Range 1
* **Salary Range 1 Label**: Enter the range of your current annual salary for the past year. For example, if your salary is between $50,000 and $75,000, enter "50000-75000".

### Salary Range 2
* **Salary Range 2 Label**: Enter the range of your current annual salary for the present year. This field is optional but helps us better understand your current compensation.

### Salary Range 3
* **Salary Range 3 Label**: Enter the range of your current annual salary for the next year (if applicable). This field is optional but helps us understand future plans or projections.

### Benefits 1
* **Benefits 1 Label**: List any benefits you currently receive, such as health insurance, retirement plan, etc. This field is optional but helps us understand the types of benefits provided by your current employer.

### Benefits 2
* **Benefits 2 Label**: List any additional benefits you receive, such as paid time off, etc. This field is optional but helps us better understand the types of benefits provided by your current employer.

### Benefits 3
* **Benefits 3 Label**: List any other benefits you receive. This field is optional but helps us better understand the types of benefits provided by your current employer.

### Benefits 4
* **Benefits 4 Label**: This field is optional and will be used to collect any other benefits you receive. You can list additional benefits you receive that are not mentioned above.

### Satisfaction
* **Satisfaction Label**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your current compensation on a scale of 1-10. 

### Benefits 5
* **Benefits 5 Label**: This field is optional and will be used to collect any additional information about benefits you receive. 

Note: The form is designed to be completed by public officials only, and the information collected will be used to improve compensation for this group. 

Tips: 

* Please be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the survey administrators. 
* The information collected will be kept confidential and used to improve compensation for public officials. 

This document should serve as a guide to help public officials complete the form in a clear and accurate manner. The form is designed to be easy to use and understand, and the fields should be filled out with care. 

Please note that this is a public official's survey and should only be completed by public officials. If you are not a public official, do not complete this form.
