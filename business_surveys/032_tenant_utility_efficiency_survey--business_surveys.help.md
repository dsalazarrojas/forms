# Tenant Utility Efficiency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Utility Efficiency Survey is designed to gather information about a tenant's utility usage and efficiency. This form helps us understand how efficiently you use utilities such as electricity, gas, and water, and provides valuable insights to optimize your utility usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best describe your energy usage (e.g., Electricity, Gas, Solar, Other).
2. Choose the options that best describe your water usage (e.g., Tap Water, Well Water, Other).
3. For the heating field, enter a brief description of your heating method (e.g., Gas, Oil, Electric).
4. In the comments field, provide any additional information about your utility usage or any suggestions you may have.
5. If you have an email address, please enter it for contact purposes.
6. Enter your phone number for any follow-up communications.
7. If applicable, assign this survey to a specific person or department.
8. Select your level of agreement with the tenant survey questions.
9. Choose your energy efficiency level (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
10. Choose your energy consumption level (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
11. Choose your water consumption level (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
12. Enter your monthly rent amount.
13. Enter your average utility bills (e.g., electricity, water, gas).
14. Enter your average energy bills.
15. Enter your average water bills.
16. Enter the date and time you submitted this survey.
17. Enter the time of submission.
18. In the description field, provide any additional information or comments about your utility usage.
