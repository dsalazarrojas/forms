# System Diagnostic Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The System Diagnostic Survey is a tool used to identify and diagnose potential issues with a system. This form will help you to provide a comprehensive overview of the system's status, enabling our team to take the necessary steps to resolve any problems that may be affecting it.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly, based on your current knowledge and understanding of the system.
2. For questions with a 'Yes' or 'No' option, select the most accurate answer.
3. For numerical scale questions (e.g., performance_scale_1, issue_scale_1), select a number between 0 and 10, where 0 indicates no issue and 10 indicates a severe issue.
4. Enter your details in the 'Form Submitter' field so that we can contact you for further clarification if needed.
5. Fill out any other relevant details such as 'Email' and 'Phone' if you have them.
