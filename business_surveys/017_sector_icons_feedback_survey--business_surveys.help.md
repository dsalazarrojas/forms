# Sector Icons Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on sector icons used by various organizations. The purpose of this survey is to collect data on the effectiveness of sector icons in communicating an organization's identity, their overall design quality, and potential areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  For each question, please choose the best response that aligns with your experience with sector icons.
2.  If you have any specific feedback or suggestions, please provide detailed explanations in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which of the sector icons is the most effective in communicating the organization's identity?** (`sector_icon_effectiveness`, `select_one`): Please select the sector icon that you think is the most effective in communicating the organization's identity.
* **How would you rate the overall design effectiveness of the sector icons?** (`overall_design_effectiveness`, `number`): Please rate on a scale of 1-5.
* **What specific improvements could be made to the icon design itself?** (`icon_design_improvement`, `text`): If you have any specific feedback or suggestions on the icon design, please provide detailed explanations.
* **What kind of insights do you get when looking at the sector icons?** (`user_insights`, `text`): Please provide detailed feedback.
* **How does the sector icon contribute to the overall user experience?** (`overall_user_experience`, `number`): Please rate on a scale of 1-5.
* **What would you rate the quality of the sector icon design?** (`icon_quality`, `number`): Please rate on a scale of 1-5.
* **Which organization is using sector icons?** (`organization`, `select_multiple`): Please select the organization.
* **What is the design style of the sector icon?** (`icon_style`, `select_one`): Please select from Modern, Classic, or Minimalist.
* **What is the color scheme of the sector icon?** (`icon_color_scheme`, `select_one`): Please select from Monochromatic, Multicolored, or Grayscale.
* **What is the size of the sector icon?** (`icon_scale`, `select_one`): Please select from Small, Medium, or Large.
* **How does the sector icon make you feel?** (`icon_feeling`, `select_multiple`): Please select from Not at all, Somewhat, Very, or Extremely.
* **What specific feedback have you received about the sector icon?** (`feedback`, `text`): If you have any specific feedback or suggestions, please provide detailed explanations.
* **Which organization is being surveyed in this sector icon feedback?** (`organization_surveyed`, `select_multiple`): Please select the organization.
* **Is there an improvement that could be made to the color scheme of the sector icon?** (`sector_improvement`, `select_one`): Please select from Yes or No.
* **Are there any overall improvements that could be made to the sector icon design?** (`overall_improvement`, `select_one`): Please select from Yes or No.
* **What specific improvement could be made to the overall user interface?** (`user_interface_improvement`, `text`): If you have any specific feedback or suggestions on the user interface, please provide detailed explanations.
* **What specific improvement could be made to the sector icon design itself?** (`icon_feeling_improvement`, `text`): If you have any specific feedback or suggestions on the icon design, please provide detailed explanations.
* **User Experience Improvement** (`user_experience_improvement`, `select_multiple`): Please select from Not at all, Somewhat, Very, or Extremely.

## Tips

* Please take your time to answer each question carefully.
* If you have any specific feedback or suggestions, please provide detailed explanations in the text fields.
* If you're unsure about any question, feel free to provide an answer based on your best judgment.
