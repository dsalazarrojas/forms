# nonprofit_board_governance_practices_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the governance practices of non-profit boards. Your responses will help us understand how non-profit boards operate and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question based on your actual experiences and practices within your non-profit organization.
2. For select-type questions, choose one or more options that best describe your current practices.
3. Provide detailed responses for text-type questions, as these allow for more specific and detailed information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Field 1** (`Field 1`, text, required: false): This field is a placeholder for any other important information about your non-profit organization that you'd like to share with us.
* **Governing Board Composition** (`governing_board_composition`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the ways in which your governing board is constituted. For example, all or most members are men, all or most members are women, diverse in age, diverse in ethnicity.
* **Board Size** (`board_size`, number, required: false): Enter the number of members on your board of directors.
* **Board Meetings** (`board_meetings`, select_one, required: false): Choose how often your board holds meetings. This could be monthly, quarterly, annually, as needed, or never.
* **Board Meeting Length** (`board_meeting_length`, text, required: false): Describe the typical length of your board meetings.
* **Meeting Minutes** (`meeting_minutes`, text, required: false): Describe how your board meetings are recorded, if at all.
* **Board Debate** (`board_debate`, text, required: false): Describe the level of debate and discussion during your board meetings.
* **Decision Making Process** (`decision_making_process`, text, required: false): Describe the process your board uses to make decisions.
* **Conflict of Interest** (`conflict_of_interest`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether or not there is a conflict of interest during your board meetings.
* **Board Evaluations** (`board_evaluations`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose how your board evaluates itself and its members. For example, self-evaluation, peer evaluation, evaluation by an external agency.
* **Evaluation Frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Choose how often your board evaluates itself and its members. This could be annually, biannually, or never.
* **Board Evaluation Process** (`board_evaluation_process`, text, required: false): Describe how your board conducts evaluations of itself and its members.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any specific comments about your board's evaluation process.
* **Evaluation Criteria** (`evaluation_criteria`, text, required: false): Describe the criteria your board uses to evaluate itself and its members. 
* **Evaluation Frequency 2** (`evaluation_frequency_2`, text, required: false): Enter a more detailed description of how often your board evaluates itself and its members. 

Note: This form is for the purpose of benchmarking and improving non-profit board governance practices. Please be honest and provide as much information as possible to help us improve.
