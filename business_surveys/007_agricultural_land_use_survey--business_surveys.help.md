# Agricultural Land Use Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agricultural Land Use Survey is a form designed to gather information on agricultural land use from farmers or landowners. This information is used to help us better understand their practices and the impact on the environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information. The form has two main sections: "Farm name" and "Respondent contact". The "Farm name" field is optional, but it's recommended that you provide it if you have a specific name for your farm or plot.
2. Enter the total land area used for agricultural production in the "Land area in hectares" field. This field is required.
3. Select the primary land use for your farm or plot from the dropdown menu. This field is required.
4. If applicable, select any secondary land uses from the dropdown menu.
5. If you irrigate your land, select the method used for irrigation from the dropdown menu.
6. Describe any soil management practices you use, such as tillage or cover cropping.
7. Describe any conservation measures in place, such as erosion control or reforestation.
8. Provide details on your crop rotation sequence, if applicable.
9. Add any additional comments or information about your farm or plot.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Farm name** (Optional): This field is where you can enter a name for your farm or plot.
* **Respondent contact** (Optional): This field is for your phone or email, in case we need to follow up with you for more information.
* **Land area in hectares** (Required): This field is where you enter the total area used for agricultural production.
* **Primary land use** (Required): This field is where you select the main activity on your land, such as crop production or livestock grazing.
* **Secondary land use** (Optional): This field is where you select any other significant land uses, such as irrigation ponds or timber.
* **Irrigation type** (Optional): This field is where you select the method used for irrigation, if applicable.
* **Soil management practices** (Optional): This field is where you describe any soil management practices you use, such as tillage or cover cropping.
* **Conservation measures** (Optional): This field is where you describe any conservation measures you have in place, such as erosion control or reforestation.
* **Crop rotation** (Optional): This field is where you provide details on your crop rotation sequence, if applicable.
* **Additional comments** (Optional): This field is where you can add any other relevant information about your farm or plot.
