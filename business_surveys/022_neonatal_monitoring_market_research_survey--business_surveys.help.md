# neonatal_monitoring_market_research_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information on your experiences and perceptions regarding neonatal monitoring devices. Your input will help us understand your needs and preferences, which will contribute to the development and improvement of these devices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select the options that best fit your experiences or preferences.
3. Provide more details when requested in the text fields.
4. Ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required/optional): This is the start of our survey where we want to ensure you're aware of the purpose and goals of this research.
* **What is this survey about** (`What is this survey about`, `text`, required/true): Please provide a brief explanation of what this survey is about.
* **How often do you use a neonatal monitoring device?** (`device_usage`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select from the options: Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, Never.
* **On a scale of 1-10, how satisfied are you with the neonatal monitoring device you use?** (`device_satisfaction`, `number`, required/true): Rate your satisfaction level with the device you're using.
* **Can you provide more details about your satisfaction with the neonatal monitoring device?** (`device_satisfaction_details`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide any additional comments or thoughts about your satisfaction.
* **On a scale of 1-10, how often do you buy a new neonatal monitoring device?** (`buying_cycle`, `number`, required/optional): Rate your frequency of buying new devices.
* **Can you provide more details about your buying cycle with the neonatal monitoring device?** (`buying_cycle_details`, `text`, required/optional): Please elaborate on your buying process.
* **What barriers do you face when adopting a neonatal monitoring device?** (`adoption_barriers`, `select_one`, required/true): Select from the options: Lack of awareness, Cost, Regulatory, Other.
* **Can you provide more details about your adoption barriers with the neonatal monitoring device?** (`adoption_barriers_details`, `text`, required/optional): Share any additional insights or challenges you face.
* **What are the key characteristics of the neonatal monitoring device you use?** (`device_characteristics`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose from the options: Accuracy, Ease of use, Cost, Other.
* **How many years of experience do you have in the healthcare industry?** (`demographic_1`, `number`, required/true): Rate your years of experience.
* **What is your job title or role?** (`demographic_2`, `select_one`, required/true): Select from the options: Respiratory Therapist, Nurse Practitioner, Doctor, Other.
* **What is your level of authority in purchasing decisions?** (`demographic_3`, `select_one`, required/true): Select from the options: Low, Medium, High.
* **What is your level of expertise with neonatal monitoring devices?** (`demographic_4`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose from the options: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced.
* **What is your primary role in the organization?** (`demographic_5`, `select_one`, required/true): Select from the options: Research, Clinical, Management, Other.
* **What is the size of your healthcare organization?** (`demographic_6`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose from the options: Less than 100, 100-500, 500-1000, More than 1000.
* **What is your annual budget for neonatal monitoring devices?** (`demographic_7`, `number`, required/true): Rate your annual budget.
* **How many neonatal monitoring devices do you currently use?** (`demographic_8`, `number`, required/true): Count the number of devices you're using.
* **Any follow-up question or concern?** (`follow_up_question`, `text`, required/optional): Share any additional thoughts or questions you have.

Please note: The required fields are marked with `required/true` and should be filled out. The optional fields are marked with `required/optional` and can be left blank if not applicable.
