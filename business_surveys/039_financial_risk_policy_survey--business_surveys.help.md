# Financial Risk Policy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is used to gather information about the financial risk policy of your business, helping us to understand your company's approach to risk management and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
*   Please answer all the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
*   Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
*   If a field is not applicable, please select "None" or leave it empty.
*   Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
*   Submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **1. Financial Risk Policy** (<code>financial risk policy</code>, <code>text</code>, required/optional): This field is used to describe your company's current financial risk policy. Please provide a brief explanation of your company's financial risk management practices.
*   **2. Review Frequency** (<code>review frequency</code>, <code>select_multiple</code>, required/optional): Here, you can select the frequency at which your financial risk policy is reviewed. Please choose from the following options: 1-6 Months, 6-12 Months, 1-2 Years, 2-5 Years, or Never.
*   **3. Satisfaction** (<code>satisfaction</code>, <code>number</code>, required/optional): This field is used to gauge your level of satisfaction with your current financial risk policy. Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Satisfied".
*   **4. Review Comments** (<code>review comments</code>, <code>text</code>, required/optional): Here, you can provide any additional comments about your financial risk policy.
*   **5. Risk Rating** (<code>risk rating</code>, <code>number</code>, required/optional): Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 10, where 1 is "Low Risk" and 10 is "High Risk".
