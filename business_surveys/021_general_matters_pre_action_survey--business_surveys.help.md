# General Matters Pre-Action Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Matters Pre-Action Survey is a pre-action assessment tool used to gather information on internal changes, readiness to support or change, and other concerns that may arise before taking any significant action. This form helps to ensure that all parties involved are aware of the potential implications of their actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and select your answers for each section. You can choose from multiple select options or provide a note for certain fields as needed. Ensure that you answer each question carefully and accurately to help facilitate a thorough assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Internal Change**: Check if an internal change is expected or necessary before proceeding. You can select "Yes" if you anticipate any internal changes, otherwise select "No".
* **Readiness to Support**: Indicate how ready you are to support a particular action or decision. You can select "Yes", "No", or choose multiple options for "Support" and "Change" depending on your level of readiness.
* **Capture Concerns**: Add any concerns or issues that you have about the current situation or proposed action. You can type a brief note to express your thoughts.
* **Readiness to Change**: Provide a numerical value indicating your level of readiness to change, with higher values indicating greater readiness.
* **Readiness to Support**: Indicate your level of support for a particular action or decision. You can select "Yes" or "No".
* **General Matters Pre-Action Survey Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the situation or proposed action. You can type a brief note to provide more information.

# Tips
* Be thorough and honest when completing this form to ensure accurate and informative responses.
* Take your time to carefully consider each question and provide clear and concise answers.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, do not hesitate to seek clarification before submitting your responses.
