# Non Produce Inventory Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about non-produce inventory items in a business setting.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Item Information**
    * **Item Name**: Enter the name of the item you are tracking.
    * **Item Description**: Provide a brief description of the item.
    * **Item Type**: Enter the type of item (optional).
2. **Supplier Information**
    * **Supplier**: Enter the name of the supplier.
    * **Supplier Location**: Enter the location of the supplier.
    * **Supplier Phone**: Enter the phone number of the supplier.
3. **Inventory Metrics**
    * **Purchase Price**: Enter the price of the item when purchased.
    * **Sale Price**: Enter the price at which the item is sold.
    * **Quantity**: Enter the current stock quantity of the item.
    * **Stock Level**: Enter the current stock level (optional).
4. **Restedocking Information**
    * **Restocking Level**: Enter the level at which the item should be restocked (optional).
    * **Restedocking Frequency**: Enter the frequency at which the item should be restocked (optional).
5. **Delivery Information**
    * **Delivery Date**: Enter the date of the next expected delivery.
    * **Delivery Time**: Enter the time of the next expected delivery.
6. **Additional Information**
    * **Non-Produce**: Check if the item is non-produce (optional).
    * **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the item.
