# Hampers Collection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hampers Collection Survey is designed to collect information about your hamper preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form below, following the instructions for each field.
2. Make sure to enter accurate information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Customer Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Type of Hamper** (`hamper`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of hamper you are interested in: Hampers or Non-hampers.
* **Quantity of Hampers** (`quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of hampers you would like to collect.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date you would like to receive your hampers.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, optional): Select the time of day you would like to receive your hampers.
