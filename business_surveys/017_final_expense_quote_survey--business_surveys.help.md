# final_expense_quote_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Final Expense Quote Survey is used to gather information about a business entity and its operations to generate an expense quote for the policy period.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the survey. You will be directed to the first page.
2. Read the introductory note carefully and ensure that you understand the purpose of the survey.
3. Provide accurate and complete information about the business entity, its address, contact information, and other relevant details.
4. Review and complete each page carefully, as incomplete or incorrect information may lead to inaccurate quotes.
5. Click on the "Next" button to proceed to the next page.
6. Continue to complete each page until you have answered all questions.
7. Once you have completed the survey, click on the "Submit" button to finalize the quote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Final Expense Quote Survey** (`id: 1`): This page provides an introduction to the survey and explains its purpose.
* **First Name** (`id: 2`): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`id: 3`): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Address** (`id: 4`): Enter the business entity's address in this field.
* **City** (`id: 5`): Enter the city where the business entity is located in this field.
* **State** (`id: 6`): Select the state where the business entity is located from the dropdown menu.
* **Zip** (`id: 7`): Enter the business entity's zip code in this field.
* **Phone** (`id: 8`): Enter the business entity's phone number in this field.
* **Email** (`id: 9`): Enter the business entity's email address in this field.
* **Date of Birth** (`id: 10`): Enter the date of birth of the business owner or representative in this field.
* **Insurance Type** (`id: 11`): Select the type of insurance the business entity is seeking in this field.
* **Annual Income** (`id: 12`): Enter the annual income of the business entity in this field.
* **Years Living in City** (`id: 13`): Enter the number of years the business entity has been living in the city in this field.
* **Years Living in Area** (`id: 14`): Enter the number of years the business entity has been living in the area in this field.
* **Employment Status** (`id: 15`): Select the employment status of the business owner or representative in this field.
* **Industry** (`id: 16`): Select the industry the business entity operates in from the dropdown menu.
* **Years Experience with Insurance** (`id: 17`): Enter the number of years the business entity has experience with insurance in this field.
* **Annual Income Source** (`id: 18`): Select the source of the business entity's annual income from the dropdown menu.
* **Business Entity Name** (`id: 19`): Enter the name of the business entity in this field.
* **Business Entity Type** (`id: 20`): Select the type of business entity from the dropdown menu.
* **Business Entity Address** (`id: 21`): Enter the address of the business entity in this field.
* **Business Entity Phone** (`id: 22`): Enter the phone number of the business entity in this field.
* **Business Entity Email** (`id: 23`): Enter the email address of the business entity in this field.
* **Policy Start Date** (`id: 24`): Enter the start date of the policy period in this field.
* **Policy End Date** (`id: 25`): Enter the end date of the policy period in this field.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you complete all required fields to ensure an accurate expense quote.
* If you are unsure about any question, please contact the representative for assistance.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you need to save your progress, you can do so by clicking on the "Save and Continue Later" button.
