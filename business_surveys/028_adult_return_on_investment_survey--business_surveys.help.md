# Adult Return On Investment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to evaluate the impact of adult programs or training sessions on participants' careers and lives. It is essential for us to gather feedback from actual attendees to refine and improve our offerings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you are an actual participant of the program or session you are evaluating.
2. Fill out the form carefully, providing honest and accurate feedback.
3. Be specific when answering questions, especially when describing skills gained or areas of improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Program Attended**: A text field where you will enter the name of the program or session you attended.
* **Completion Date**: A date field where you will enter the date when you completed the program.
* **Improvement in Technical Skills**: A select-one field where you will assess the level of improvement in technical skills you gained, such as significant, moderate, slight, or none.
* **Improvement in Soft Skills**: A select-one field where you will assess the level of improvement in soft skills, like communication, leadership, etc.
* **Specific Skills Gained**: A text field where you can describe the most valuable skills you learned and gained during the program.
* **Current Employment Status**: A select-one field where you will report your current employment status, such as employed full-time, part-time, self-employed, or unemployed.
* **Salary Increase Since Program**: A select-one field where you will answer whether your salary has increased since the program, true or false.
* **Estimated Percentage Increase**: A number field where you will estimate the percentage increase in your salary (if applicable).
* **Promotion Received**: A select-one field where you will answer whether you received a promotion since the program, true or false.
* **Program Value Relative to Cost**: A select-one field where you will assess the program's value relative to its cost, such as very high, high, moderate, or low.
* **Would Recommend to Colleagues**: A select-one field where you will answer whether you would recommend the program to colleagues, definitely, probably, maybe, or not.
* **Additional Feedback on ROI**: A text field where you can provide any other comments on the program's impact.

By following these steps and providing accurate feedback, you will be helping us to refine and improve our programs and services to better support adult learners.
