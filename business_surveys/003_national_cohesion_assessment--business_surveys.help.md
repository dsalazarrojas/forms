# national_cohesion_assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The National Cohesion Assessment form is designed to gather information about individuals' perceptions of their region and country's political, economic, and social cohesion. This form aims to collect data on trust in government institutions, regional identity, sense of belonging, and overall feelings about the region and country.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and select the relevant information for your region and country.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the option that best represents your level of trust, satisfaction, or feeling.
3. Provide your name, email, and contact information if you would like to be contacted.
4. Write any additional comments about your overall feelings in the "Do you have any comments about your overall feelings?" section.
