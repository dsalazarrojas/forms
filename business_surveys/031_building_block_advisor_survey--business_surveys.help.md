# Building Block Advisor Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Building Block Advisor Survey is a form designed to gather information from project advisors about a client's project. This form helps to identify key strengths and areas of concern, risk factors, and recommendations to improve the project's status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information.
2. Choose the project name and client name.
3. Describe the current project stage.
4. Select the budget and timeline status.
5. List any key strengths identified in the project.
6. Identify any areas of concern.
7. Choose any risk factors present.
8. Provide priority recommendations.
9. Specify any additional resources needed.
10. Assess the stakeholder alignment.
11. Provide an overall project assessment.
12. Add any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Advisor Name** (`advisor_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Company or Firm** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or firm.
* **Job Title** (`title`, text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the survey.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the client being advised.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the project.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_one, required): Choose the project type (e.g., New Construction, Renovation, etc.).
* **Area of Advisory** (`advisory_area`, select_multiple, required): Choose the area of advisory (e.g., Financial Planning, Design Review, etc.).
* **Current Project Stage** (`project_stage`, select_one, required): Choose the current project stage (e.g., Conceptual Planning, Design Development, etc.).
* **Budget Status** (`budget_status`, select_one, required): Choose the current budget status (e.g., On Budget, Under Budget, etc.).
* **Timeline Status** (`timeline_status`, select_one, required): Choose the current timeline status (e.g., Ahead of Schedule, On Schedule, etc.).
* **Key Strengths Identified** (`key_strengths`, text, required): List any key strengths identified in the project.
* **Areas of Concern** (`areas_of_concern`, text, required): Identify any areas of concern.
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, select_multiple, required): Choose any risk factors present (e.g., Budget Overruns, Schedule Delays, etc.).
* **Priority Recommendations** (`recommendations_priority`, text, required): Provide priority recommendations.
* **Resource Needs** (`resource_needs`, text, optional): List any additional resources needed (optional).
* **Stakeholder Alignment** (`stakeholder_alignment`, select_one, required): Choose the level of stakeholder alignment (e.g., Fully Aligned, Not Aligned, etc.).
* **Decision Timeline** (`decision_timeline`, text, optional): Specify when key decisions are needed (optional).
* **Follow-up Needed** (`follow_up_needed`, select_one, required): Choose if further advisory is needed (e.g., Yes - Within 1 Week, No - Current Support Sufficient, etc.).
* **Overall Project Assessment** (`overall_assessment`, select_one, required): Provide an overall project assessment (e.g., Excellent - On Track, Poor - Significant Issues, etc.).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
