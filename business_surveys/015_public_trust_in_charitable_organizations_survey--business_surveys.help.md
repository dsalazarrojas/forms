# Public Trust In Charitable Organizations Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback and opinions from the public about charitable organizations. Your responses will help us understand how well charitable organizations are doing in terms of public trust, confidence, and overall reputation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in each section as accurately and honestly as possible.
2. For select_multiple questions, select all that apply.
3. For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose one or multiple options as applicable.
4. For text fields, provide as much information as you need to answer the question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Introduction**
This section is meant to give you an overview of the survey and its purpose.

*   The introduction explains the purpose of the survey and why we need your input.

### 2. **Trust drivers**
Please select all that apply to the factors that contribute to your trust in charitable organizations.

*   **Charity's mission and goals**: The charity's mission and goals are the reasons why it was established and the outcomes it hopes to achieve.
*   **Charity's financial health**: The charity's financial health is a measure of its stability and ability to manage its finances effectively.
*   **Charity's accountability and transparency**: The charity's accountability and transparency refer to its ability to be open and honest about its activities and finances.
*   **Board of directors' accountability**: The board of directors is responsible for overseeing the charity's activities and decisions.
*   **CEO/leadership accountability**: The CEO/leadership is responsible for making key decisions and leading the organization.
*   **Charity's financial reporting and disclosure**: The charity's financial reporting and disclosure refer to its ability to provide accurate and timely financial information.
*   **Charity's reputation and public image**: The charity's reputation and public image are how it is seen by the public.
*   **Charity's relationship with government and political entities**: The charity's relationship with government and political entities is how it interacts with these entities.
*   **Charity's relationship with other NGOs**: The charity's relationship with other NGOs is how it collaborates with other organizations.
*   **Charity's social media engagement**: The charity's social media engagement is how it uses social media to communicate with the public.
*   **Charity's website and online presence**: The charity's website and online presence is how it presents itself to the public.
*   **Charity's fundraising practices**: The charity's fundraising practices are how it raises money for its activities.
*   **Donor feedback and complaint system**: The charity's donor feedback and complaint system is how it handles feedback and complaints from donors.

### 3. **Confidence**
Please select the level of confidence you have in charitable organizations.

*   **Neutral**: You have neutral confidence in charitable organizations.
*   **Somewhat low**: You have somewhat low confidence in charitable organizations.
