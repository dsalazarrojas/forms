# Faculty Collaboration Effectiveness Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the effectiveness of faculty collaboration within a university setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your university's name in the "University Name" field.
2. Select your department from the "Department" field.
3. Choose the level of collaboration you typically experience with your peers in the "Collaboration Level" field.
4. Select the types of communication you usually engage in with your peers in the "Communication Level" field.
5. Choose the types of challenges you face in your work in the "Challenge Level" field.
6. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Suggestion" field.
7. Fill in your faculty member name and role in the "Faculty Member" field.
8. Choose the impact of your collaboration on your department in the "Departmental Impact" field.
9. Choose the overall impact of your collaboration on the institution in the "Institutional Impact" field.
10. Select the frequency of your collaborations in the "Collaboration Frequency" field.
11. Choose the duration of your collaborations in the "Collaboration Duration" field.
12. Select the impact of your collaborations on your productivity in the "Impact on Productivity" field.
13. Select the impact of your collaborations on your job satisfaction in the "Impact on Job Satisfaction" field.
14. Select the impact of your collaborations on student learning in the "Impact on Student Learning" field.
15. Select the impact of your collaborations on innovation in the "Impact on Innovation" field.
16. Select the impact of your collaborations on diversity and inclusion in the "Impact on Diversity and Inclusion" field.
17. Select the time commitment required for your collaborations in the "Time Commitment" field.
18. Choose the frequency of communication with your peers in the "Communication with Faculty" field.
19. Choose the frequency of communication with staff in the "Communication with Staff" field.
20. Choose the frequency of communication with students in the "Communication with Students" field.
21. Select the types of collaborations you engage in with other colleagues in the "Collaboration with Others" field.
22. Choose the resources required for your collaborations in the "Additional Resources" field.
23. Provide any final comments in the "Final Comments" field.
