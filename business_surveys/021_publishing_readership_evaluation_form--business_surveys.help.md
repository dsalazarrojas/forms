# Publishing Readership Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Publishing Readership Evaluation Form is a survey designed to gather information on readers' experiences with published content. This form aims to collect feedback on various aspects of the content, including type, frequency, satisfaction, format, and quality.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions based on your actual experience with the content you read.
2. Select the most relevant options that best describe your experience.
3. Be as detailed as possible in the "Additional Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **What type of content did you read?** (`reader_content_type`, `select_one`, required)
Select the type of content you read, such as article, blog post, book, or newsletter.

### 2. **How often do you read this type of content?** (`reader_frequency`, `select_one`, required)
Indicate how frequently you read the content, including daily, weekly, monthly, rarely, or never.

### 3. **How would you rate your level of satisfaction?** (`reader_satisfaction`, `number`, required)
Rate your level of satisfaction on a scale.

### 4. **What is your preferred format for reading this type of content?** (`reader_format`, `select_multiple`, required)
Select the formats you prefer, such as digital, print, audio, or video.

### 5. **How would you rate the quality of the content?** (`reader_quality`, `number`, required)
Rate the quality of the content on a scale.

### 6. **Do you have any feedback or comments about the content?** (`reader_feedback`, `note`, optional)
Please provide any additional feedback or comments about the content.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and as accurately as possible to provide valuable feedback.
* If unsure about any question, select the option that best reflects your experience.
* The more detailed feedback, the better we can understand your needs and improve the content.
