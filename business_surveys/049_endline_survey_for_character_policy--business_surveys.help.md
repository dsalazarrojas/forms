# # endline-survey-for-character-policy - Help Guide
## Purpose
The endline-survey-for-character-policy form is a tool used to collect feedback and opinions from key stakeholders in a school setting. The form is designed to gather information about the character policy, including awareness, effectiveness, and challenges faced by teachers, parents, students, and other stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant category (e.g., school survey, key stakeholders survey, etc.) and complete the corresponding questions.
2. Answer each question briefly and clearly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields (although most fields are optional).
4. Review and submit your answers for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Survey** (`school_survey`, text, optional): This section is for school administrators, teachers, and other school staff to share their thoughts on the character policy.
* **Key Stakeholders Survey** (`key_stakeholders_survey`, text, optional): This section is for individuals who are directly involved in the character policy, such as school board members or district officials.
* **Parent Stakeholders Survey** (`parent_stakeholders_survey`, text, optional): This section is for parents and guardians to share their thoughts on the character policy.
* **Teachers Survey** (`teachers_survey`, text, optional): This section is for teachers to share their experiences and opinions on the character policy.
* **Students Survey** (`students_survey`, text, optional): This section is for students to share their thoughts and opinions on the character policy.
* **Other Stakeholders Survey** (`other_stakeholders_survey`, text, optional): This section is for any other individuals involved in the character policy, such as community members or alumni.
* **Awareness** (`awareness`, text, optional): This section is for you to share your understanding of the character policy.
* **Effectiveness** (`effectiveness`, text, optional): This section is for you to share your thoughts on how effective the character policy has been in achieving its goals.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, text, optional): This section is for you to share any challenges you have faced while implementing the character policy.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, text, optional): This section is for you to suggest any improvements to the character policy.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Provide as much detail as possible.
* If you have any suggestions or improvements, share them in the Improvement Ideas section.
* If you have any difficulties or concerns, contact the form creators at form@gic.mx.
