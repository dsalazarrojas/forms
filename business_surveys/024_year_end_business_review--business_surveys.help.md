# Year End Business Review - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Year End Business Review form is designed to help business owners and managers reflect on the past year's performance, set goals for the next year, and identify areas for improvement. This form is a comprehensive review of the company's business objectives, financial performance, key performance indicators, business risks, and other important aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the designated text fields. For the select_multiple and select_one fields, choose one or multiple options from the provided list.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as required).
3. Take your time to provide thorough and accurate answers, especially for the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Business Overview (Page 1)
* **Business Overview** (text, required): This field is for providing a brief summary of your business's overall performance, highlighting key achievements and milestones from the past year.

### Financial Performance (Page 1)
* **Financial Performance** (text, required): Describe your business's financial performance, including revenue growth, expenses, and any notable financial achievements or challenges.

### Business Objectives (Page 2)
* **Business Objectives** (select_multiple, required): Choose one or multiple objectives that align with your business's goals for the next year. The options are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### Key Performance Indicators (Page 2)
* **Key Performance Indicators** (select_one, required): Select one key performance indicator that is most relevant to your business. The options are:
	+ Indicator 1
	+ Indicator 2
	+ Indicator 3

### Business Risks (Page 3)
* **Business Risks** (text, required): Identify any risks that your business faced or anticipates facing in the next year. Be as specific as possible, including potential causes and consequences.

### Action Items (Page 3)
* **Action Items** (text, required): Outline the steps you plan to take to address the identified risks and achieve business objectives, including any initiatives or projects that are already underway.

### Key Success Factors (Page 4)
* **Key Success Factors** (select_multiple, required): Choose one or multiple factors that contribute to your business's success. The options are:
	+ Success Factor 1
	+ Success Factor 2
	+ Success Factor 3

### Challenges Faced (Page 4)
* **Challenges Faced** (text, required): Reflect on any challenges you faced in the past year, including any obstacles that hindered or threatened your business's progress.
