# operations_advisory_impact_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The operations advisory impact survey is designed to gather information on the advisory engagement of team members. It is intended for use by operations teams and leaders to assess the level of advisory engagement within their team and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2.  Provide detailed and accurate information in the text fields.
3.  Select all the options that apply to your situation in the select_one and select_multiple fields.
4.  Ensure that you fill out the form once and do not duplicate the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Advisory Engagement
* **Advisory Engagement** (`advisory_engagement`, `text`, optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of the advisory engagement of the team. Please provide a concise and accurate answer.

### Measured Impact
* **Measured Impact** (`measured_impact`, `number`, optional): This field is used to report the measured impact of the advisory engagement. Please provide a numerical value.

### Suggestions for Improvement
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `select_one`, optional): Select all the options that apply to your situation. This field is used to provide suggestions for improvement for the advisory engagement.

### Perceived Impact
* **Perceived Impact** (`perceived_impact`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is used to report the perceived impact of the advisory engagement. Select all the options that apply to your situation.

### Operations Leader
* **Operations Leader** (`operations_leader`, `text`, optional): This field is used to provide the name and title of the operations leader.

### Feedback Frequency
* **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select all the options that apply to your situation. This field is used to report the feedback frequency.

### Engagement Type
* **Engagement Type** (`engagement_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select all the options that apply to your situation. This field is used to report the engagement type.

### Engagement Duration
* **Engagement Duration** (`engagement_duration`, `number`, optional): This field is used to report the engagement duration in terms of hours or days.

### Team Members
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the team members involved in the advisory engagement.

### Team Members Notes
* **Team Members Notes** (`team_members_notes`, `note`, optional): This field is used to provide additional notes for the team members involved in the advisory engagement.
