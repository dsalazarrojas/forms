# Cross Team Collaboration Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cross Team Collaboration Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback on cross-team collaboration practices within your team or organization. This form aims to improve collaboration, communication, and overall team performance by understanding individual experiences and perceptions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through each page and select the options that best reflect your experiences and perceptions. You can choose one or multiple options per question, depending on the field type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`title`, `note`, , optional): This is an introductory page providing context about the form and its purpose.
- **What is your collaboration method?** (`collaboration_method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one option from 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate how often you collaborate with others.
- **What is your team size?** (`team_size`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose multiple options from 'Yes', 'No', and 'Maybe' to describe your team size.
- **How do you collaborate with others?** (`collaboration_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose multiple options from 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Poor' to rate your collaboration frequency.
- **How do you prefer to communicate?** (`communication_style`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose multiple options from 'Yes', 'No', or 'Sometimes' to indicate your communication preferences.
- **What style do you prefer for collaboration?** (`collaboration_style`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose multiple options from 'Yes', 'No', or 'Agree' to describe your collaboration style preferences.
- **What collaboration tools do you use?** (`collaboration_tool`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose multiple options from 'Yes', 'No', or 'Maybe' to describe the tools you use for collaboration.
- **What is your collaboration goal?** (`collaboration_goal`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose multiple options from 'Yes', 'No', or 'Agree' to describe your collaboration goals.
- **What collaboration challenges do you face?** (`collaboration_challenges`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose multiple options from 'Yes', 'No', or 'Agree' to describe your collaboration challenges.
- **Do you have any feedback?** (`feedback`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one option from 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have any feedback.
- **How effective is your collaboration?** (`collaboration_effectiveness`, `number`, optional): Rate your collaboration effectiveness on a scale of 1-5.
- **How satisfied are you with your collaboration?** (`collaboration_satisfaction`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with collaboration on a scale of 1-5.
- **How frequent is your collaboration?** (`collaboration_frequency_scale`, `number`, optional): Rate your collaboration frequency on a scale of 1-5.

## Tips
- Please select one or multiple options for each question, depending on the field type.
- Rate your answers on the provided scales for numerical fields.
- Answer honestly and accurately to provide valuable feedback.
- If you have any additional feedback, please provide it in the "Feedback" field.
