# Designer Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback and satisfaction from designers who have worked on a project with our design team. Your responses will help us improve our design process and better meet the needs of our clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. Use the options provided for select fields.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think about this design** (design_satisfaction_survey):
	+ This is an open-ended question where you can share your thoughts about the design process and what you think we did well or poorly.
	+ Please keep your response concise and focused on the specific design project you worked on.
* **Who did you work with** (design_team):
	+ Choose one of the design team members you worked with on this project.
	+ If you worked with multiple team members, please select "Other" and provide their names in the "Comments" field.
* **How many hours did you work** (work_hours):
	+ Enter the number of hours you worked on this project.
	+ Please make sure to fill out this field as accurately as possible.
* **Comments** (comments):
	+ This is an optional field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about the project.
	+ Please use this field to provide any relevant information that is not covered by the other questions.
* **Email** (email):
	+ Enter your email address.
	+ Please make sure to fill out this field as it will be used to contact you if we need more information.
* **Phone** (phone):
	+ Enter your phone number.
	+ Please note that this field is optional, but it will be helpful if we need to contact you urgently.
* **Company** (company):
	+ Choose the company you worked with on this project.
	+ If you're unsure or don't see your company listed, please select "Other" and provide the name in the "Comments" field.
* **Date completed** (date_completed):
	+ Enter the date you completed the project.
	+ Please make sure to fill out this field as accurately as possible.
* **Time completed** (time_completed):
	+ Enter the time you completed the project.
	+ Please note that this field is optional, but it will be helpful if we need to know the exact time.
* **Time worked** (time_worked):
	+ Enter the number of hours you worked on this project.
	+ Please make sure to fill out this field as accurately as possible.
* **Satisfaction** (satisfaction):
	+ Choose one of the satisfaction levels that best describes your experience with this project.
* **Submission type** (submission_type):
	+ Choose the type of submission this project represents (New, Update, or Re-submission).
	+ If you're unsure, please select "Other" and provide a description in the "Comments" field.
* **Submitter** (submitter):
	+ Choose who submitted the project (John, Jane, or Other).
	+ If you're unsure, please select "Other" and provide the name in the "Comments" field.
* **Submitted by** (submitted_by):
	+ Choose who completed the submission (Jim, Jane, or Other).
	+ If you're unsure, please select "Other" and provide the name in the "Comments" field.
* **Comments submitted** (comments_sub):
	+ Choose one or more of the options that best describe the comments submitted.
	+ You can also provide additional comments or feedback in the "Additional comments" field.
* **Additional comments** (additional_comments):
	+ This is an optional field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about the project.

* **Time spent** (time_spent):

* **Hours worked** (hours_worked):

* **Time** (time):
