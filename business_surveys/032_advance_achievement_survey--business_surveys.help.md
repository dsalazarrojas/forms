# Advance Achievement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to provide users with a clear understanding of how to complete the Advance Achievement Survey form and what information it captures. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and data required to fill out the form.
2. Start by completing the Department Name field, which is a required field that asks for the name of the department or unit responsible for the progress and achievements reported.
3. In the Reporting Period field, enter the specific time period for which you are reporting progress and achievements.
4. In the Top Achievement of the Period field, describe the most significant accomplishment or achievement made during the reporting period.
5. Select the Target Attainment Level for the reported period, indicating whether the department exceeded, met, or partially met the established Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).
6. If applicable, select the Contributing Factors to Success field to identify the primary drivers that contributed to the success.
7. Enter the Revenue Growth Percentage for the reported period.
8. Enter the number of New Clients Acquired during the reported period.
9. Enter the Project Completion Rate as a percentage of projects delivered on time.
10. In the Primary Objective for Next Period field, describe the main goal or objective for the upcoming period.
11. Select the Confidence in Meeting Future Goals, indicating how confident you are that the department will achieve its goals in the next period.
12. Finally, enter any Support Needed for Advance Achievement, describing any resources or changes required to achieve future success.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Department Name** (Text, required): Enter the name of the department or unit responsible for the progress and achievements reported.
* **Reporting Period** (Text, required): Enter the specific time period for which you are reporting progress and achievements.
* **Top Achievement of the Period** (Text, required): Describe the most significant accomplishment or achievement made during the reporting period.
* **Target Attainment Level** (Select One, required): Select one of the following options: Exceeded, Met, Partially Met, Not Met, indicating whether the department exceeded, met, or partially met the established KPIs.
* **Contributing Factors to Success** (Select Multiple, optional): Select the primary drivers that contributed to the success (e.g., Team Collaboration, Process Innovation, Resource Allocation, Leadership Support, External Conditions).
* **Revenue Growth Percentage** (Number, required): Enter the revenue growth percentage for the reported period.
* **New Clients Acquired** (Number, required): Enter the number of new clients acquired during the reported period.
* **Project Completion Rate** (Number, required): Enter the percentage of projects delivered on time.
* **Primary Objective for Next Period** (Text, required): Describe the main goal or objective for the upcoming period.
* **Confidence in Meeting Future Goals** (Select One, required): Select one of the following options: Very High, High, Moderate, Low, indicating how confident you are that the department will achieve its goals in the next period.
* **Support Needed for Advance Achievement** (Text, optional): Describe any resources or changes required to achieve future success.
