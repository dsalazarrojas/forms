# Salesforce Integration Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Salesforce Integration Feedback Form is a tool used to gather feedback and insights on the Salesforce integration experience. This form helps us understand your thoughts, opinions, and experiences with the integration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes your experience with the Salesforce integration.
2. Provide a brief description of your experience with the Salesforce integration.
3. Choose one option from the list of "What do you like most about Salesforce?".
4. Select all that apply from the list of "Salesforce Integration Feedback" options.
5. Choose one option from the list of "What do you like least about Salesforce?".
6. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have contacted Salesforce support.
7. Choose "True", "False", or "Maybe" from the list of "Do you plan to use Salesforce?" options.
8. Provide any additional comments about your experience with the Salesforce integration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Category**:
	+ This field asks you to select the category that best describes your experience with the Salesforce integration. Please choose one or more of the following options:
		- Yes
		- No
* **Description**:
	+ This field allows you to provide a brief description of your experience with the Salesforce integration. Please keep your answer concise and clear.
* **Salesforce Integration Feedback Form Title**:
	+ This field is not currently in use, but please ignore it.
* **What do you like most about Salesforce?**:
	+ This field asks you to choose one option that best describes what you like most about Salesforce. Please select one of the following options:
		- Option 1
		- Option 2
		- Option 3
* **Salesforce Integration Feedback**:
	+ This field asks you to select all that apply from the list of options. Please choose all that are relevant to your experience:
		- Yes
		- No
* **What do you like least about Salesforce?**:
	+ This field asks you to choose one option that best describes what you like least about Salesforce. Please select one of the following options:
		- Option 4
		- Option 5
		- Option 6
* **Have you contacted Salesforce support?**:
	+ This field asks you to select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have contacted Salesforce support.
* **Do you plan to use Salesforce?**:
	+ This field asks you to choose "True", "False", or "Maybe" to indicate if you plan to use Salesforce in the future.
* **Why didn't you use Salesforce?**:
	+ This field is an open-ended text field where you can provide any additional comments about why you didn't use Salesforce.
* **Any other comments**:
	+ This field is an open-ended text field where you can provide any additional comments about your experience with the Salesforce integration.
