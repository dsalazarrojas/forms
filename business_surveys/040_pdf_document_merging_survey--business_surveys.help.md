# pdf_document_merging_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on their experiences with PDF merging, helping us improve the service and better understand current workflows, satisfaction, and opportunities for enhancement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions and select options as prompted.
2. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional comments" field.
3. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes, if you wish to be contacted for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome to our PDF Merging Survey** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the form, providing context and background information.
* **How do you currently merge PDFs?** (`pdf_merging_workflows`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the options provided to describe your current PDF merging workflow.
* **What method do you use to merge PDFs?** (`merging_method`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all applicable options to describe your method for merging PDFs.
* **Additional comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional thoughts or feedback you have about your experience with our PDF Merging Survey.
* **Your email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up or further discussion.
* **Your phone number** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for follow-up or further discussion.
* **How much time do you spend on PDF merging per week?** (`time_spent`, `time`, required: false): Estimate the amount of time you spend on PDF merging activities per week.
* **How satisfied are you with our PDF Merging Survey?** (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how satisfied you are with our PDF Merging Survey.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers, as your feedback is valuable to us.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us using the provided email or phone number.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
