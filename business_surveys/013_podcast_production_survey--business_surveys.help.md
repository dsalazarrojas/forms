# Podcast Production Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by producers to submit their podcast show information for review and planning purposes within the company. It will help our team understand your show's title, producer contact details, production requirements, and target audience details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your podcast show title in the **Podcast Show Title** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Producer Email** field.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Producer Phone** field.
4. Select your show category from the options provided in the **Show Category** field.
5. Select whether your show has a fixed episode frequency in the **Episode Frequency** field.
6. Enter the length of each episode in the **Episode Length** field.
7. Describe your target audience in the **Target Audience** field.
8. Enter the estimated number of listeners for your show in the **Number of Listeners** field.
9. Provide any specific production needs for your show in the **Production Needs** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Podcast Show Title** (`title`, text): Enter your podcast show title.
* **Producer Email** (`producer_email`, email): Enter your email address in a format like `example@example.com`. This field is required.
* **Producer Phone** (`producer_phone`, text): Enter your phone number in international format like `+1-123-456-7890`. This field is not required.
* **Show Category** (`show_category`, select_one): Select your show's category from the options provided.
* **Show Duration** (`show_duration`, time): Currently not implemented in this form, you can ignore this field.
* **Episode Frequency** (`episode_frequency`, select_multiple): Select whether your show has a fixed episode frequency.
* **Episode Length** (`episode_length`, number): Enter the length of each episode in minutes.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text): Describe your show's target audience.
* **Number of Listeners** (`number_of_listeners`, number): Enter the estimated number of listeners for your show.
* **Production Needs** (`production_needs`, text): Describe any specific production needs for your show.
