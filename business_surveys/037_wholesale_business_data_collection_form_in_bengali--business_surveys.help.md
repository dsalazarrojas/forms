# wholesale_business_data_collection_form_in_bengali - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wholesale_business_data_collection_form_in_bengali is a form designed to collect business data from wholesale businesses. This form aims to gather information about the business details, product information, transaction details, and other relevant data from wholesale companies for analysis and decision-making purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "contact_details" page and enter the company's contact information such as name, address, and email.
2. On the "business_details" page, provide the business details like business name, industry, and business type.
3. On the "product_details" page, describe the products or services offered by the business.
4. On the "transaction_details" page, enter the details of the transaction such as date, amount, and type of product sold.
5. On the "contact_person_details" page, enter the contact person's information such as name, title, and contact number.
6. On the "notes" page, add any additional comments or notes about the business.
7. On the "additional_notes" page, add any further notes or comments.
8. Finally, review and confirm the transaction details on the "confirm_transaction_details" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_details (contact_details)**: Enter the business's contact information such as name, address, and email.
* **business_details (business_details)**: Provide the business details like business name, industry, and business type.
* **product_details (product_details)**: Describe the products or services offered by the business.
* **transaction_details (transaction_details)**: Enter the details of the transaction such as date, amount, and type of product sold.
* **contact_person_details (contact_person_details)**: Enter the contact person's information such as name, title, and contact number.
* **notes (notes)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the business.
* **additional_notes (additional_notes)**: Add any further notes or comments.
* **confirm_transaction_details (confirm_transaction_details)**: Review and confirm the transaction details entered in the previous page.
