# Wildlife Photography Impact Study Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on wildlife photography impact studies. It helps researchers and enthusiasts in the field understand the effects of photography on wildlife and the impact it has on the environment and the people involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Survey Title**: This is the title of your wildlife photography impact study. Enter a brief and descriptive title that summarizes the purpose and focus of your study.
2. Enter the **Survey Description**: Provide a detailed description of your study, including its goals, methods, and expected outcomes.
3. Select the **Survey Type**: Choose the type of survey that best fits your study, such as Wildlife Photography or Wildlife Conservation.
4. Enter **Survey Tags**: Add relevant tags to categorize and make your survey more discoverable.
5. Set the **Survey Created At**: Enter the date when you created your survey.
6. Set the **Survey Updated At**: Enter the date when you last updated your survey.
7. Set the **Survey Deleted At**: Enter the date when you deleted your survey (if applicable).
8. Enter the **Survey Status**: Describe the current status of your survey (e.g., "Active", "Inactive", or "Deprecated").
9. Enter **Survey Version**: Enter the version number of your survey.
10. Select the **Survey Language**: Choose the language in which your survey will be conducted (e.g., English, French, or Other).
11. Enter **Survey Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your survey.
