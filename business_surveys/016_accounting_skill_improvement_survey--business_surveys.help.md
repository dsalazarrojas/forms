# Accounting Skill Improvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from accounting professionals about their skills and interests in training. The data collected will help the training department plan relevant and effective training programs for accounting professionals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields.
3. If you need help or clarification on any question, please contact [support contact].

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): This is your name, and it will help us understand who is responding to this survey.
* **Role in organization** (`role_in_organization`, text, optional): Please enter your job title or position in the organization.
* **Years of experience** (`years_experience`, number, optional): Please round your experience to the nearest whole year.
* **Proficiency in financial reporting** (`proficiency_financial_reporting`, select_one, required): Rate your proficiency level in financial reporting as:
	+ Advanced
	+ Intermediate
	+ Beginner
* **Proficiency in audit and compliance** (`proficiency_audit_and_compliance`, select_one, required): Rate your proficiency level in audit and compliance as:
	+ Advanced
	+ Intermediate
	+ Beginner
* **Proficiency in accounting software** (`proficiency_software_skills`, select_one, required): Rate your proficiency level in accounting software as:
	+ Advanced
	+ Intermediate
	+ Beginner
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Training interests** (`training_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select all topics you would like training on:
	+ Financial reporting
	+ Tax updates
	+ Auditing techniques
	+ Excel for accounting
	+ Accounting systems integration
* **Preferred training format** (`preferred_training_format`, select_one, required): Choose one preferred format for training:
	+ In person
	+ Online live
	+ On demand
* **Availability for training** (`availability_for_training`, text, optional): Please specify the days or times that work best for you.
* **Barriers to training** (`barriers_to_training`, text, optional): If you have any obstacles to participation, please describe them.
* **Expected outcomes from training** (`expected_outcomes`, text, optional): What improvements would you expect from training?
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other comments or suggestions you would like to make.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when rating your proficiency levels.
* Think about your current skills and what you would like to improve on.
* Consider your work schedule and preferred training format.
* If you have any other comments or suggestions, please feel free to add them.
