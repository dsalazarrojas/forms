# Business Plan Overview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Plan Overview Form is designed to provide a concise and comprehensive overview of a business, capturing its key details and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above and fill in each field as per the guidance provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Fill in the legal name of your business.
* **Type of Business** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary business category (Retail, Services, Manufacturing, Technology, Consulting, or Other).
* **Startup or Transition Date** (`startup_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when your business started or transitioned.
* **Owner or Principal** (`business_owner`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the primary business owner.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, `email`, required): Enter the primary contact email address.
* **Owner Phone Number** (`owner_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Business Vision Statement** (`business_vision`, `text`, required): Describe your long-term vision.
* **Mission Statement** (`mission_statement`, `text`, required): Outline the core purpose of your business.
* **Target Market Description** (`target_market`, `text`, required): Describe your primary customers.
* **Key Products or Services** (`key_products_services`, `text`, required): List your main offerings.
* **Competitive Advantage** (`competitive_advantage`, `text`, required): Explain what sets you apart from competitors.
* **Startup Capital Needed** (`startup_capital_needed`, `number`, required): Enter the total funds required to launch.
* **Planned Funding Sources** (`funding_sources`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose from Personal Savings, Bank Loan, Investor Funding, Government Grants, or Family Loans.
* **Break-even Timeline (months)** (`break_even_timeline`, `number`, optional): Estimate the expected months to profitability.
* **First Year Revenue Projection** (`first_year_revenue_projection`, `number`, optional): Estimate the first-year revenue.
* **Number of Employees Planned** (`num_employees_planned`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of employees you plan to hire initially.
* **Business Location** (`business_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of your business.
* **Major Challenges Anticipated** (`major_challenges`, `text`, optional): List the challenges you foresee.
* **Plan Submission Date** (`plan_submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date this plan is being submitted.
