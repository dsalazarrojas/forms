# National Security Expert Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your background and expertise in national security matters. It is intended to help us better understand your qualifications and experience in the field.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the instructions and explanations provided for each field below.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. If a field is marked as optional, consider whether it is relevant to your expertise and whether filling it out would add value to your profile.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`general_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for you to provide a brief description of your general background and experience in national security.
* **Security Focus** (`security_focus`, `text`, required/optional): Please describe your specific focus or area of expertise in national security.
* **Publications** (`publications`, `text`, required/optional): List any relevant publications or academic works that demonstrate your expertise.
* **Research Areas** (`research_areas`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the areas of research you have conducted or are currently conducting in national security.
* **Languages** (`languages`, `text`, required/optional): If you have experience or proficiency in languages other than your native language, please list them here.
* **Awards** (`awards`, `text`, required/optional): List any awards or recognitions you have received for your work in national security.
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, required/optional): Provide information about your academic background and any relevant degrees or certifications you have earned.
* **Previous Projects** (`previous_projects`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any previous projects or work you have completed in national security.
* **Previous Certifications** (`previous_certifications`, `text`, required/optional): List any previous certifications or licenses you have obtained that are relevant to your expertise.
* **Research Areas Link** (`research_areas_link`, `text`, required/optional): If you have a link to a website or document that showcases your research areas, please provide it.
* **Awards Link** (`awards_link`, `text`, required/optional): If you have a link to a website or document that showcases your awards, please provide it.
* **Education Link** (`education_link`, `text`, required/optional): If you have a link to a website or document that provides more information about your education, please provide it.
* **Previous Projects Link** (`previous_projects_link`, `text`, required/optional): If you have a link to a website or document that showcases your previous projects, please provide it.
* **Previous Certifications Link** (`previous_certifications_link`, `text`, required/optional): If you have a link to a website or document that provides more information about your previous certifications, please provide it.
* **Research Areas 2** (`research_areas_2`, `text`, required/optional): This field is similar to "Research Areas", please provide information about your current or past research in national security.

Please note that this form is not a comprehensive survey, and you may not need to fill out all fields. However, filling out all fields will help us get a complete picture of your expertise.
