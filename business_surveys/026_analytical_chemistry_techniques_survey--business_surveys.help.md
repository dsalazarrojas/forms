# Analytical Chemistry Techniques Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect information about the analytical techniques and practices employed by professionals in the field of analytical chemistry. Your participation is invaluable in helping us understand your needs and challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the appropriate options for each question.
2. Fill in any text fields with your relevant information.
3. Provide your overall satisfaction with current equipment and suggested upgrades or purchases.
4. Select all areas where you would like to receive further training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Professional Title** (`Professional Title`, **text**, required): This field is where you enter your professional title, such as Senior Chemist or Lab Manager.
  - Enter your current job title that reflects your role in the laboratory.
- **Laboratory / Institution Affiliation** (`Laboratory / Institution Affiliation`, **text**, required): This field is for your laboratory or institution affiliation.
  - Enter the name of your laboratory or institution.
- **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, **email**, optional): This field is where you enter your contact email address.
  - This is optional, but provides a way for us to get in touch with you with any further questions or to provide updates.
- **Analytical Techniques Regularly Used** (`Analytical Techniques Regularly Used`, **select_multiple**, required): Select all techniques you frequently use.
  - This is a multiple-choice field where you can choose from HPLC, GC-MS, NMR, ICP-OES, UV-Vis Spectroscopy, and Titration.
- **Frequency of Instrument Calibration** (`Frequency of Instrument Calibration`, **select_one**, required): Choose the frequency of instrument calibration for you.
  - Select one of the options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Per Project, or Quarterly.
- **What is your most challenging analytical matrix?** (`What is your most challenging analytical matrix?`, **text**, optional): Describe the most challenging matrix you handle in your work.
  - This field is for providing detailed information about the matrix you find most challenging to work with.
- **Overall Satisfaction with Current Analytical Equipment** (`Overall Satisfaction with Current Analytical Equipment`, **select_one**, required): Choose how satisfied you are with your current equipment.
  - This is a single-choice field where you can choose Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
- **Suggested Equipment Upgrades or Purchases** (`Suggested Equipment Upgrades or Purchases`, **text**, optional): Describe the upgrades or purchases you think would improve lab efficiency.
  - This field is for providing suggestions on how to improve lab equipment.
- **Desired Areas for Further Professional Training** (`Desired Areas for Further Professional Training`, **select_multiple**, optional): Select all areas where you would like to receive further training.
  - This is a multiple-choice field where you can choose from Method Development, Troubleshooting, Data Analysis Software, Lab Safety, and so on.
- **Additional Comments on Lab Efficiency** (`Additional Comments on Lab Efficiency`, **text**, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions on improving lab efficiency.
  - This field is for providing any extra feedback on how lab operations can be improved.
