# Livestock Veterinary Response Time Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data on the response times and other relevant information from veterinarians and their practices. This data will help in analyzing and understanding the response times and satisfaction levels of the veterinarians in their practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through the following steps:

1.  Answer questions about your practice and its operations. This includes your practice hours, location, and response times.
2.  Provide details about your response times and your level of satisfaction with the services provided by your practice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farmer Survey** (`farmer_survey`, `text`, `false`): Enter a brief description or note about your practice.
* **Veterinarian Survey** (`vet_survey`, `text`, `false`): Enter any other information or comments about your practice.
* **Vet Office Hours** (`vet_office_survey`, `select_one`, `true`): Select the time period when your practice operates (e.g. 8-8, 8-12, etc.).
* **Office Hours** (`office_hours`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Select the hours when your office is available for appointments and consultations.
*   **Vet Hours** (`vet_hours`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Select the hours when your veterinarian is available for consultations and surgeries.
*   **Vet Response Time** (`vet_response_time`, `number`, `false`): Enter an estimate of the time it takes your practice to respond to emergencies.
*   **Veterinarian Satisfaction** (`vet_satisfaction`, `number`, `false`): Enter your level of satisfaction with your practice's services.
*   **Practice Hours** (`practice_hours`, `select_one`, `true`): Select the hours when your practice operates (e.g. 8-12, 12-16, etc.).
*   **Practice Location** (`practice_location`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Select the location of your practice (e.g. City, Town, etc.).
*   **Response Time** (`response_time`, `number`, `false`): Enter an estimate of the time it takes your practice to respond to emergencies.
*   **Vet Clinic Survey** (`vet_clinic_survey`, `select_one`, `true`): Select the number of clinics you have in your practice (e.g. 1-5, 5-10, etc.).

Note: Ensure you answer all required questions accurately. This will help in generating reliable data for analysis.
