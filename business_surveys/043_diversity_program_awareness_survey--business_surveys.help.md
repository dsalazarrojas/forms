# Diversity Program Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your perceptions and experiences with our company's diversity and inclusion programs.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each section header to navigate to the relevant page.
2. Read each field carefully and answer accordingly.
3. Use the provided options for select-one and select-multiple fields.
4. Enter your suggestions in the text fields.
5. Click "Submit" when you have completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Awareness and Participation**: This field is a free-text field for you to describe your level of awareness and participation in our diversity and inclusion programs.
* **On a scale of 1-4**: This field asks you to rate your level of awareness and participation in our diversity and inclusion programs, with 1 being "No" and 4 being "Yes".
* **Actions Taken**: In this field, please select all the actions you have taken to support or participate in our diversity and inclusion programs.
* **Recommendations**: Please provide any suggestions or recommendations for improving our diversity and inclusion programs.
* **Category**: Please select the category that best fits your role or department within the company.
* **E-mail**: This field is for your contact email address. 
* **Phone**: This field is for your contact phone number.
* **Date**: This field is for the date of your submission.
* **Time**: This field is for the time of your submission.
 
**Tips**: Please answer the fields as accurately and honestly as possible. The more detailed your answers, the better we can understand your experiences and suggestions.
