# Departmental SWOT Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Departmental SWOT Analysis Form is a tool designed to help departments identify and assess their internal and external strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats, and create an action plan to address the identified issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the first section, "Departmental SWOT Analysis Form", which is a general description of your department's current situation.
2. In the "Internal Environment" section, describe your department's internal factors, such as cost, culture, leadership, and strategy.
3. In the "Industry Trends" section, describe the current trends in your industry, including innovation, regulation, globalization, and diversification.
4. In the "Company Performance" section, assess your department's current performance, including revenue, growth, profit, and productivity.
5. In the "External Environment" section, describe the external factors that affect your department, such as market, technology, government, and economy.
6. In the "Competitive Analysis" section, select the factors that you consider to be your department's strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.
7. In the "Internal Factors" section, select the internal factors that affect your department's performance.
8. In the "External Factors" section, select the external factors that affect your department's performance.
9. In the "Action Plan" section, describe the actions your department plans to take to address the identified issues.
10. In the "Next Steps" section, describe the next steps your department will take to implement the action plan.
11. Finally, in the "Review" section, review and comment on your department's current situation and the action plan.

## Tips

* Be as thorough and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* Consider multiple perspectives and viewpoints when identifying strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.
* Prioritize the most critical issues and create an action plan to address them.
* Review and update this form regularly to stay on top of your department's current situation and performance.
* Use this form as a starting point for ongoing strategic planning and improvement.
* Consider seeking input from multiple stakeholders, including team members, customers, and external experts.
