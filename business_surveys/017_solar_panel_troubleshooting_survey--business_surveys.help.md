# solar-panel-troubleshooting-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The solar-panel-troubleshooting-survey form is designed to gather information from users about issues they are experiencing with their solar panels. This form will help our team identify and resolve problems, ensuring that your solar panel system runs smoothly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form for each issue you are experiencing with your solar panel system.
2. Provide a clear and concise description of the problem in the "issue-description" field.
3. Attach photos of the issue by clicking on the "upload-photos" field.
4. If applicable, provide your contact information (email, phone number, or other) in the "contact-info" field.
5. Complete any other relevant fields as requested, such as describing the issue or providing additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Troubleshooting Survey** (`solar_panel_troubleshooting_survey`): Please provide a brief description of the problem you are experiencing with your solar panel system.
* **issue-description** (`issue-description`): Describe the issue you are experiencing with your solar panel system, including any error messages or symptoms you have noticed.
* **photo-description** (`photo-description`): Attach photos of the issue to help our team better understand the problem.
* **upload-photos** (`upload-photos`): Upload photos of the issue to provide our team with visual evidence.
* **contact-info** (`contact-info`): If you are available and willing, provide your email address, phone number, or other contact information to allow us to follow up with you about your issue.
* **email-address** (`email-address`): If you provided contact information, enter your email address here.
* **phone-number** (`phone-number`): If you provided contact information, enter your phone number here.
* **issue-report** (`issue-report`): Please provide any additional information about the issue you are experiencing.
* **issue-report-2** (`issue-report-2`): If the issue is ongoing, please provide any additional information about the situation.
* **issue-report-3** (`issue-report-3`): If the issue has been ongoing, please provide any additional information about the situation.
* **submit-report** (`submit-report`): Click submit to send your report to our team. We will review and follow up with you as needed.
