# initial_process_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from participants on a business project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each field based on your experience and expectations.
2. Ensure that you fill out all required fields.
3. Use the provided hints if you need help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you describe your experience on this project** (`participant_experience`, `text`, required)
  This field is used to describe your experience on the project.
* **What outcome are you expecting from this project** (`expected_outcome`, `text`, required)
  This field is used to describe the outcome you expect from the project.
* **What do you hope to achieve through this project** (`participant_expectation`, `text`, required)
  This field is used to describe what you hope to achieve through the project.
* **Do you have any suggestions** (`participant_suggestions`, `text`, required)
  This field is used to provide any suggestions you have for the project.
* **Additional comments** (`participant_comments`, `text`, optional)
  This field is used to provide any additional comments or thoughts about the project.
* **How would you rate your experience** (`participant_experience_scale`, `number`, required)
  This field is used to rate your experience on the project, with higher numbers indicating a better experience.
* **How would you rate the expected outcome** (`participant_outcome_scale`, `number`, required)
  This field is used to rate the expected outcome of the project, with higher numbers indicating a better outcome.
* **How would you rate your expectations** (`participant_expectation_scale`, `number`, required)
  This field is used to rate your expectations for the project, with higher numbers indicating higher expectations.
* **How would you rate your suggestions** (`participant_suggestions_scale`, `number`, required)
  This field is used to rate how important your suggestions are, with higher numbers indicating higher importance.
* **How would you rate additional comments** (`participant_comments_scale`, `number`, optional)
  This field is used to rate how useful you find additional comments, with higher numbers indicating higher usefulness.
* **When did your experience start** (`participant_experience_date`, `date`, required)
  This field is used to specify when your experience on the project started.
* **When do you expect the outcome** (`participant_outcome_date`, `required`)
  This field is used to specify when you expect the outcome of the project.
* **When do you expect it to happen** (`participant_expectation_date`, `required`)
  This field is used to specify when you expect the project to be completed.
* **How long did your experience last** (`participant_experience_time`, `time`, required)
  This field is used to specify how long your experience on the project lasted.
* **How long do you expect the outcome to take** (`participant_outcome_time`, `time`, required)
  This field is used to specify how long you expect the outcome of the project to take.
* **How long do you expect it to take** (`participant_expectation_time`, `time`, required)
  This field is used to specify how long you expect the project to take.
* **How long do you expect it to take** (`participant_suggestions_time`, `time`, optional)
  This field is used to specify how long you expect the outcome of your suggestions to take.
* **Participant email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required)
  This field is used to store the participant's email address.
* **Participant phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, optional)
  This field is used to store the participant's phone number.
* **Participant note** (`participant_note`, `note`, optional)
  This field is used to store additional notes about the participant.
* **Participant notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`, optional)
  This field is used to store additional notes about the participant.
* **Participant note on expectations** (`participant_expectations_note`, `note`, optional)
  This field is used to store notes about participant's expectations.
* **Participant note on suggestions** (`participant_suggestions_note`, `note`, optional)
  This field is used to store notes about the participant's suggestions.
* **Participant note on comments** (`participant_comments_note`, `note`, optional)
  This field is used to store notes about the participant's comments.
