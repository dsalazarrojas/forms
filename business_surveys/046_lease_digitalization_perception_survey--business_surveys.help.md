# Lease Digitalization Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form aims to collect feedback and perceptions about the digitalization of processes in a business environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions on each page carefully, providing honest and detailed feedback.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. For questions with a date field, enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. For questions with a text field, enter a clear and concise response.
5. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lease Digitalization Perception Survey** (`main_page`, text, required):
	+ This is the introductory page with a brief description of the form's purpose.
* **What Is Digitalization?** (`introduction`, text, required):
	+ This section is to gather your understanding of what digitalization means in the business environment.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, select_multiple, not required):
	+ Select all benefits that you consider important for digitalization, such as improved efficiency, increased accuracy, or enhanced collaboration.
* **Current Process** (`current_process`, text, required):
	+ Describe your current process, highlighting any pain points or challenges you face.
* **Digital Process** (`digital_process`, text, required):
	+ Describe your ideal digital process, including any benefits you envision.
* **Ease of Access** (`ease_of_access`, select_multiple, not required):
	+ Select all ways in which you think digitalization can make access to information easier, such as mobile apps, online portals, or automation.
* **Support** (`support`, select_multiple, not required):
	+ Select all types of support you think are essential for digitalization, such as technical support, training, or online resources.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, required):
	+ Rate your overall experience with digitalization, from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, not required):
	+ Share any suggestions you have for improving digitalization in your business environment.
* **Submission** (`submission`, date, not required):
	+ Enter the date you are submitting the form, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, not required):
	+ Enter the time you are submitting the form, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Submission Note** (`submission_note`, note, not required):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about your submission.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required):
	+ Enter your email address, if you have one.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required):
	+ Enter your phone number, if you have one.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required):
	+ Enter your name.
* **Company** (`company`, text, not required):
	+ Enter your company name.
* **Department** (`department`, text, not required):
	+ Enter your department name.
* **Position** (`position`, text, not required):
	+ Enter your position or job title.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, not required):
	+ Enter your job title.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, select_one, required):
	+ Select the job function you perform.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_one, not required):
	+ Select the level of your job, such as entry-level or senior manager.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, not required):
	+ Select the industry you work in.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, not required):
	+ Select the size of your company.
* **Job Tenure** (`job_tenure`, select_one, not required):
	+ Select your job tenure.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required):
	+ Add any additional comments about your experience with digitalization.
