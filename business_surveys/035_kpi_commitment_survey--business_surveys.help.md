# KPI Commitment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The KPI Commitment Survey is designed to help users understand and commit to key performance indicators that are crucial for business operations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select all relevant options for "performance_indicators" that apply to your role or area of responsibility.
2. For "commitment_level", enter a numeric value between 1-2, 3-5, or 6-8, depending on your level of commitment.
3. Choose one option from "current_commitment" that accurately reflects your current commitment level.
4. Select all relevant options from "expected_commitment" that apply to your future goals.
5. Enter a numeric value for "performance_improvement" that reflects your expected improvement rate.
6. In "improvement_action_plan", describe a concrete plan for improving performance.
7. Enter a numeric value for "improvement_progress" that reflects your progress on the improvement action plan.
8. For "performance_indicators_awareness", select all relevant options that apply to your awareness of performance indicators.
9. Choose one option from "kpi_commitment_level" that accurately reflects your overall commitment level.
10. Rate your performance indicators using a numeric value in "performance_indicators_rating".
11. Select all relevant options from "overall_commitment" that apply to your overall commitment level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **performance_indicators** (`performance_indicators`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant options that apply to your role or area of responsibility.
* **commitment_level** (`commitment_level`, number, required): Enter a numeric value between 1-2, 3-5, or 6-8 that reflects your level of commitment.
* **current_commitment** (`current_commitment`, select_one, required): Choose one option that accurately reflects your current commitment level.
* **expected_commitment** (`expected_commitment`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant options that apply to your future goals.
* **performance_improvement** (`performance_improvement`, number, required): Enter a numeric value that reflects your expected improvement rate.
* **improvement_action_plan** (`improvement_action_plan`, text, required): Describe a concrete plan for improving performance.
* **improvement_progress** (`improvement_progress`, number, required): Enter a numeric value that reflects your progress on the improvement action plan.
* **performance_indicators_awareness** (`performance_indicators_awareness`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant options that apply to your awareness of performance indicators.
* **kpi_commitment_level** (`kpi_commitment_level`, select_one, required): Choose one option that accurately reflects your overall commitment level.
* **performance_indicators_rating** (`performance_indicators_rating`, number, required): Rate your performance indicators using a numeric value.
* **overall_commitment** (`overall_commitment`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant options that apply to your overall commitment level.
