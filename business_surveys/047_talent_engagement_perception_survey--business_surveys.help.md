# Talent Engagement Perception Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about your perception of your work and the organization. Your input will help the management team understand the current state of employee engagement and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have a clear understanding of your current role and department.
2. Answer the questions truthfully, and select the best option that reflects your feelings.
3. If you're unsure about a question, try to make an educated guess.
4. Fill out all the required fields. (Fields marked as "required" are marked with an asterisk \*).
