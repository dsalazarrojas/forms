# Global Marketing Leadership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Global Marketing Leadership Survey is designed to gather information on marketing leadership strategies, challenges, and experiences from professionals in the field. This data will help us understand the current state of marketing leadership and identify areas of improvement for the industry as a whole.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete each field according to the instructions below.
2. Take approximately 10-15 minutes to complete the survey.
3. Be as specific and honest as possible in your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Marketing strategies** (`marketing_strategies`, text, optional):
	+ Briefly describe your current marketing strategies and approaches.
* **Best practices** (`best_practices`, text, optional):
	+ Share your best practices or tips for effective marketing leadership.
* **Challenges faced** (`challenges_faced`, text, optional):
	+ List any challenges you've faced as a marketing leader.
* **Organizational growth** (`organizational_growth`, number, optional):
	+ Estimate the growth rate of your organization over the past year.
* **Marketing leadership style** (`marketing_leadership_style`, select-one, optional):
	+ Choose the style that best describes your approach:
		- Transformational: Focus on innovation and long-term vision.
		- Transactional: Focus on short-term results and quick wins.
		- None of the above: Other (please specify).
* **Marketing leadership role** (`marketing_leadership_role`, text, optional):
	+ Describe your current marketing leadership role or position.
* **Survey length** (`survey_length`, number, optional):
	+ Estimate how long you've been in a marketing leadership role.
* **How long have you been in a marketing leadership role?** (`marketing_leadership_survey_length`, select-one, optional):
	+ Choose one of the following:
		- 1-5 years
		- 5-10 years
		- 10+ years
		- None of the above
* **How often do you complete these surveys?** (`marketing_leadership_survey_frequency`, select-one, optional):
	+ Choose one of the following:
		- Always
		- Rarely
		- Sometimes
