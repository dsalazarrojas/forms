# retail_shopping_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The retail_shopping_experience_survey form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their shopping experience at a retail store. This form is meant to gather insights on various aspects of the customer's visit, including their overall satisfaction, staff helpfulness, product quality, return policy, communication, store ATM, store hours, customer service, store layout, and staff friendliness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer each field according to your experience.
2.  Select from the options provided for fields like customer_satisfaction, staff_helpfulness, product_quality, return_policy, communication, customer_service, and staff_friendly.
3.  Enter a date for fields like store_atm and a time for store_hours if you have any information about those services.
4.  Answer honestly, based on your actual experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Store Visit (Store Visit, number, not required)**: This field is meant to collect the number of times you visited the store.
*   **Overall Satisfaction (Overall Satisfaction, select_one, not required)**: This field is designed to gauge your overall satisfaction after visiting the store.
*   **Staff Helpfulness (Staff Helpfulness, select_multiple, not required)**: This field asks if the staff was helpful during your visit.
*   **Product Quality (Product Quality, select_one, not required)**: This field inquires about the quality of the products you purchased or looked at during your visit.
*   **Return Policy (Return Policy, select_one, not required)**: This field is meant to ask if the store had a clear return policy.
*   **Communication (Communication, select_multiple, not required)**: This field asks if the store's communication was helpful during your visit.
*   **Store ATM (Store ATM, date, not required)**: This field is meant to collect the time or day you used the store's ATM service, if you did.
*   **Store Hours (Store Hours, time, not required)**: This field is meant to collect the time you visited the store.
*   **Customer Service (Customer Service, select_multiple, not required)**: This field asks if the customer service was helpful during your visit.
*   **Store Layout (Store Layout, select_one, not required)**: This field is meant to inquire about the store's layout and if it was clear and easy to navigate.
*   **Staff Friendly (Staff Friendly, select_multiple, not required)**: This field asks if the store staff was friendly and welcoming during your visit.
*   **Product Selection (Product Selection, select_one, not required)**: This field is meant to ask if the store had a wide selection of products.
