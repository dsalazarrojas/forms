# Pet Brand Loyalty Study Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about your interactions with pet brands. Your input will help us better understand how pet owners like you interact with our brand.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand each section.
2. Fill in your name and pet owner information.
3. Answer the questions honestly, keeping in mind that your responses will help us improve our brand's services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Owners Name** (Required, Text): Enter your name as the pet owner.
* **Pet Type** (Required, Select One): Choose the type of pet you own (Pet, Cat, or Dog).
* **Brand Awareness** (Required, Number): Rate your level of brand awareness on a scale of 1-5.
* **Purchase Frequency** (Required, Number): How often do you purchase our products or services? Rate on a scale of 1-5.
* **Satisfaction Level** (Required, Select Multiple): Choose how satisfied you are with our brand (High, Medium, Low).
* **Loyalty Drivers** (Required, Note): Describe what drives your loyalty to our brand.
* **Email** (Required, Email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Required, Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (Optional, Text): Share any additional comments or thoughts about our brand.
* **Additional Comments** (Optional, Text): Provide any additional information that might be helpful for us.
* **Date Last Contact** (Required, Date): Enter the date of your last contact with our brand.
* **Time Last Contact** (Required, Time): Enter the time of your last contact with our brand.
* **Frequency Of Contact** (Optional, Number): Rate how often you contact us. 
* **Last Contact Method** (Optional, Select One): Choose how you last contacted our brand (Social Media, In Store, Online).
* **Last Contact Location** (Optional, Select Multiple): Choose where you last contacted our brand (Home, Store, Online, Other).
* **Last Contact Details** (Optional, Text): Provide any additional details about your last contact.
* **How Did You Hear** (Optional, Text): Describe how you first heard about our brand.
* **Last Contact Source** (Optional, Select Multiple): Choose where you last heard about our brand (Social Media, In Store, Online, Other).
* **Last Contact Date** (Optional, Date): Enter the date of your last contact with our brand.
* **Last Contact Time** (Optional, Time): Enter the time of your last contact with our brand.
* **Contact Method** (Optional, Select Multiple): Choose how you usually contact our brand (Social Media, In Store, Online).
* **Contact Source** (Optional, Select Multiple): Choose where you usually hear about our brand (Social Media, In Store, Online, Other).
* **Last Contact Date/Time** (Optional, Date): Enter the date and time of your last contact with our brand.
* **Additional Contact Details** (Optional, Text): Provide any additional details about your contact history with our brand.
* **Survey Comments** (Optional, Text): Share any feedback or comments about our survey.

Note: Required fields are marked as such and are necessary to complete the form. Optional fields allow you to provide additional information if you wish. 

Please answer honestly and keep in mind that your responses will help us improve our brand's services. If you have any issues or questions, feel free to ask.
