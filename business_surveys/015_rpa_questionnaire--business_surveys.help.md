# RPA Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a business survey designed to collect information from potential clients or customers about their business needs and preferences. The purpose of this form is to gather data that will help the business to better understand its target market and tailor its services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each page sequentially, ensuring that the information provided is accurate and complete. Some fields are marked as optional, but providing complete and accurate information will significantly improve the quality of the data collected and the relevance of the services offered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (`question_1_text`, text, required/optional): This field asks for the client's or customer's name. Please provide your name as it appears on your business license or ID. If you don't have a business license or ID, please provide your name as it appears on your company's website or other public platform.
* **Age of the business** (`question_2_integer`, number, required/optional): This field asks for the age of the business. Please provide the number of years the business has been operating.
* **Date of the business** (`question_3_date`, date, required/optional): This field asks for the date the business was established. Please provide the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **What time do you want to meet** (`question_4_time`, time, required/optional): This field asks for the preferred time of meeting. Please provide the time in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
* **Comments** (`question_5_note`, note, required/optional): This field is for any additional comments or notes about the business or meeting. Please keep your comments brief and concise.
* **Email** (`question_6_email`, email, required/optional): This field asks for the business's email address. Please provide the email address associated with the business's domain or a personal email address used for business purposes.
* **Phone** (`question_7_phone`, text, required/optional): This field asks for the business's phone number. Please provide the phone number associated with the business's domain or a personal phone number used for business purposes.
* **What kind of business do you have** (`question_8_select_one`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks for the type of business the client or customer has. Please select 'Yes' if the business has the listed services; otherwise, select 'No'.
* **What services do you need** (`question_9_select_multiple`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field asks for the services needed from the business. Please select all services that apply to your business needs.

Note: This help guide assumes that the form pages and fields are presented in order. If the form has a different layout, the field explanation may need to be adjusted accordingly.
