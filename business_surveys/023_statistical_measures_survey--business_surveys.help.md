# Statistical Measures Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to understand your knowledge, familiarity, and confidence in statistical measures, which are essential tools for making data-driven decisions in business settings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form by entering your responses in the corresponding fields.
2. Ensure that you have relevant experience with statistical measures to provide accurate feedback.
3. If you have already completed this survey, please do not re-complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title summarizing the purpose of this survey.
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category under which this survey falls. Options:
	+ Business Surveys
	+ Personal Finance
	+ Other
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Provide a short description of why you're interested in this survey.
* **Familiarity with Statistical Measures** (`form_familiarity_familiarity`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Very familiar
	+ Somewhat familiar
	+ Not familiar
* **How Often Do You Use Statistical Measures?** (`form_usage_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Almost always
	+ Often
	+ Occasionally
	+ Never
* **Confidence in Statistical Measures** (`form_confidence`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Very confident
	+ Somewhat confident
	+ Not confident
* **Skill Gaps for Statistical Measures** (`form_skill_gap`, text, optional): If you have any areas where you feel you need improvement in statistical measures, please elaborate.
* **Recommendations for Improving Statistical Measures** (`form_recommendations`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for enhancing the curriculum, please share them with us.

Note: All fields are optional, but please ensure that you're completing this survey with your best effort and relevant experience.
