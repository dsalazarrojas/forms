# Corporate Ethics Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to help users understand and complete the Corporate Ethics Perception Survey. The survey collects information about employees' perception of overall ethics concept, reporting channels, and overall reporting behavior in the organization. It is designed to gather feedback from employees about their experiences and opinions on corporate ethics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form pages and sections to understand the layout and organization of the questions.
2. Carefully read each question and its corresponding label and options.
3. Select the correct options or answer for each question based on your experience and opinions.
4. Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall ethics concept** (`1`, `number`, required: false):
This question asks about your overall perception of the ethics concept in the organization. Please rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "poor" and 5 is "excellent".
* **Reporting channel** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
Select all the reporting channels you use to report incidents or issues in the organization. Choose from: Email, Phone, In-person.
* **Reporting frequency** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false):
Choose how often you report incidents or issues in the organization. Choose from: Daily, Weekly, Monthly.
* **Reporting behavior when faced with an incident** (`4`, `text`, required: false):
Describe your reporting behavior when you are faced with an incident in the organization.
* **Reporting via email** (`5`, `number`, required: false):
Please rate how often you report via email in the organization. 
* **Reporting via phone** (`6`, `number`, required: false):
Please rate how often you report via phone in the organization.
* **Reporting frequency via email** (`7`, `select_one`, required: false):
Choose how often you report via email in the organization. Choose from: Daily, Weekly, Monthly.
* **Reporting frequency via phone** (`8`, `select_one`, required: false):
Choose how often you report via phone in the organization. Choose from: Daily, Weekly, Monthly.
* **Overall reporting behavior** (`9`, `text`, required: false):
Describe your overall reporting behavior in the organization.
* **Behavior faced with incident** (`10`, `text`, required: false):
Describe your behavior when faced with an incident in the organization.
* **Overall reporting** (`11`, `note`, required: false):
Note any additional comments or suggestions about reporting behavior in the organization.
* **Reporting Frequency 2** (`12`, `select_one`, required: false):
Choose how often you report, Choose from: Daily, Weekly, Monthly.

Tips:
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Take your time to carefully read each question and choose the correct answer.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, use the note field.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
* Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the questions.
* This form is designed to gather feedback from employees, so your input is valuable and appreciated.
