# Decision Analysis Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Decision Analysis Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from users on their experience with our decision-making processes. This form helps us evaluate the effectiveness, clarity, and confidence in our decision-making processes. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the options that apply to your experience with our decision-making processes.
2. Enter a numerical value for Clarity, Effectiveness, and Confidence.
3. Provide any suggestions or comments in the text field.
4. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prophets (Select all that apply)**:
    * This field asks you to identify whether our decision-making processes are helpful in your work. Select all the options that apply to your experience:
        + Yes
        + No

* **Clarity**:
    * Rate the level of clarity in our decision-making processes, with higher scores indicating clearer processes. For example:
        + 1 (Very unclear)
        + 2 (Somewhat unclear)
        + 3 (Neutral)
        + 4 (Somewhat clear)
        + 5 (Very clear)

* **Effectiveness**:
    * Rate the effectiveness of our decision-making processes in achieving their goals, with higher scores indicating more effective processes. For example:
        + 1 (Not effective at all)
        + 2 (Somewhat effective)
        + 3 (Neutral)
        + 4 (Somewhat effective)
        + 5 (Very effective)

* **Confidence**:
    * Rate the confidence you have in our decision-making processes, with higher scores indicating more confidence. For example:
        + 1 (Not confident at all)
        + 2 (Somewhat confident)
        + 3 (Neutral)
        + 4 (Somewhat confident)
        + 5 (Very confident)

* **Suggestions**:
    * If you have any suggestions or comments on how we can improve our decision-making processes, enter them here. Please be as specific as possible and provide any relevant details.
