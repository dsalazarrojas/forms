# Treasury Satisfaction Benchmark Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and satisfaction data from users about the treasury operations. It is meant to help the treasury team gain insights to improve their services and operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please make sure you have experience with treasury operations.
2.  Review each question carefully and select the most suitable options.
3.  Provide honest and detailed answers to each question.
4.  You can choose to keep your feedback anonymous or provide your name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
*   **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: `false`): This section is an introductory note to the survey. Please read through it before proceeding with the rest of the survey.

### Questions
*   **What is your current role in the treasury operations?** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
    - Select all the roles that apply to you in the treasury operations.
    - Example: Cash Management, Accounting, Risk Management
*   **How satisfied are you with the current treasury operations?** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
    - Select the level of satisfaction you experience with the treasury operations.
    - Options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied
*   **What is the primary reason for your level of satisfaction?** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`):
    - Write a brief description of the primary reason for your level of satisfaction.
    - Example: "Good communication from the team", "Clear and efficient processes", "Lack of resources"
*   **What do you think is the biggest challenge facing the treasury operations today?** (`6`, `text`, required: `false`):
    - Write a brief description of the biggest challenge you face in the treasury operations.
    - Example: "Insufficient budget", "Lack of personnel", "Complexity in the system"
*   **Do you have any suggestions for improvement?** (`7`, `text`, required: `false`):
    - Provide any suggestions you have for improving the treasury operations.
    - Example: "Improve communication channels", "Increase resources", "Simplify the processes"

### Contact Information
*   **Is this feedback anonymous?** (`8`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
    - Choose whether you want to keep your feedback anonymous or not.
    - Options: Yes, No

Note that the survey is designed to be completed by experienced users, and all answers are optional. You are free to provide your name and contact information if you wish, but you can also keep your feedback anonymous.
