# post_promotion_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The post-promotion feedback survey is designed to collect valuable information about our promotional activities. This feedback will help us refine our strategies and improve future promotions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Type your company name (if applicable).
4. Enter your email address (if you provided it in the promotion).
5. If you received the promotion via phone, enter the phone number.
6. Indicate the type of promotion you received (e.g., Social Media, Print, Email, Direct Mail, or TV).
7. Enter the date and time you received the promotion (if applicable).
8. Select your rating of the promotion (on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the least satisfactory and 5 being the most satisfactory).
