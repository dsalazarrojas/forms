# Loyalty Program Impact Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Loyalty Program Impact Report Form is a tool to collect data on the effectiveness of a company's loyalty program. The data collected will help businesses assess and improve their loyalty programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question in the space provided, being as accurate and detailed as possible.
2. If a question does not apply to your business, you can select "N/A" or "Not Applicable" as a valid answer.
3. Make sure to provide contact information so that we can follow up with you for any further questions or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Employees**: Please provide the number of employees that have participated in your company's loyalty program.
* **Customer Satisfaction**: Select the level of customer satisfaction with your company's products or services. Choose from "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".
* **Program Participation**: Indicate the level of participation in your loyalty program. Select "Yes" if most customers participate, "No" if few customers participate.
* **Employee Satisfaction**: Enter the average satisfaction score of your employees with the loyalty program.
* **Program Impact**: Describe the impact of your loyalty program on your business, including any notable achievements or challenges.
* **Customer Satisfaction (Date)**: Enter the date of the customer satisfaction survey.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company's name as it appears on your website or business cards.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address where we can contact you for any questions or follow-ups.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you for any questions or follow-ups.
* **Company Website**: Enter your company's website URL.
* **Company Industry**: Enter your company's industry as it is classified in your business registration.
* **Company Description**: Describe your company in a few sentences, including your mission, products, or services.

Note: This guide is based on the provided YAML fields and assumes that the form has not been changed since last update.
