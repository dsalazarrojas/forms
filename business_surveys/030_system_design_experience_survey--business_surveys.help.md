**System Design Experience Survey - Help Guide**
=====================================

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your system design experience. It will help us better understand your design skills, experience, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "System Design Experience" field as a number.
2. Choose your "Design Experience Level" from the provided options (Low, Medium, High).
3. Select one or more of the "Skills" options that best describe your experience (Architecture, Analysis, Requirements, Prototyping, Documentation).
4. Enter the number of years you have experience with system design.
5. Describe any challenges you have faced with system design in the "Challenges" field.
6. Choose your preferred "Design Process" (Agile, Waterfall, Hybrid).
7. Select one or more of the "Collaboration" options that best describe your experience (Individual, Team, Remote, Distributed).
8. Choose whether you use "Technology" (Low-fidelity, False, True).
9. Select one or more of the "Communication" options that best describe your experience (Regular, Ad-hoc, None).
10. Check whether you have attended "Training Programs" (True, False).
11. Enter any resources you use for system design in the "Resources" field.
12. Select one or more of the "Design Principles" options that best describe your experience (Human-Centered, Lean, Agile).
13. Choose your preferred "Feedback" option (Regular, Ad-hoc, None).
14. Select one or more of the "Review Process" options that best describe your experience (Formal, Informal, None).
15. Select one or more of the "System Design Tools" options that best describe your experience (UML, BPMN, Other).
16. Select one or more of the "Documentation" options that best describe your experience (UML, Text, Other).
17. Select one or more of the "Tools" options that best describe your experience (Trello, Asana, Other).
18. Select one or more of the "Tools" options that best describe your experience (Slack, MS Teams, Other).
19. Choose your preferred "Development Process" (Agile, Waterfall, Hybrid).

24. Select one or more of the "Maintainability" options that best describe your experience (High, Medium, Low).
25. Select one or more of the "Testability" options that best describe your experience (High, Medium, Low).
