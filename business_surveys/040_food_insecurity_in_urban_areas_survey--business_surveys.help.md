# Food Insecurity In Urban Areas Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about the experiences and needs of households in urban areas regarding food access, challenges, and coping mechanisms.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:
1. Fill out the form accurately and carefully, providing information about your household.
2. Answer each question according to your household's experiences and circumstances.
3. Select the options that best describe your household's situation.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Household Id** (`household_id`, Number, required): This is a unique identifier for your household. Please enter a number that is not already used by another household.
* **Household Size** (`household_size`, Number, required): Please indicate the number of people living in your household.
* **Household Income** (`household_income`, Number, required): This is the total monthly income of your household. Please enter a number.
* **Food Access** (`food_access`, Select Multiple, required): Please select all the options that apply to your household's food access:
	+ Often
	+ Sometimes
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Food Challenge** (`food_challenge`, Select Multiple, required): Please select all the options that apply to your household's food challenges:
	+ No challenge
	+ Food insecurity
	+ Lack of money
	+ Limited access
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Food Coping** (`food_coping`, Select Multiple, required): Please select all the options that apply to your household's food coping mechanisms:
	+ No coping
	+ Food bank
	+ Government help
	+ Other help
	+ Self help
* **Food Source** (`food_source`, Select Multiple, required): Please select all the options that apply to your household's food sources:
	+ Store
	+ Supermarket
	+ Farm
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Household Location** (`household_location`, Select Multiple, required): Please select the location of your household:
	+ Urban
	+ Rural
	+ Both
* **Household Income Source** (`household_income_source`, Select Multiple, required): Please select the main source of income for your household:
	+ Employment
	+ Government aid
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Household Income Frequency** (`household_income_frequency`, Select Multiple, required): Please select the frequency of your household's income:
	+ Monthly
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi weekly
	+ Fortnightly
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Survey Response** (`survey_response`, Date, optional): If you would like to provide a date for when you completed this survey, please do so.
* **Household Income Type** (`household_income_type`, Select One, required): Please select the type of your household's income:
	+ Fixed income
	+ Variable income
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, Date, required): Please enter the date on which you are completing this survey.
* **Note** (`note`, Text, optional): If you would like to provide any additional comments or notes, please enter them here.
