# Business Travel Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Travel Survey is designed to gather feedback from employees on their experiences and perceptions about business travel. This form aims to collect information on various aspects of business travel, including frequency, duration, satisfaction, and improvements needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Please answer all questions to the best of your ability**. This will help us understand your experiences and perceptions accurately.
2. **Select the most appropriate response for each question**. If you're unsure, choose the option that best reflects your feelings or experiences.
3. **Be honest and thorough in your answers**. Your feedback is valuable to us, and your input will help us improve business travel for all employees.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required)
Your full name as an employee.

### 2. **Email Address** (`employee_email`, email, required)
Your work email address.

### 3. **Department** (`department`, text, required)
Your department or division within the company.

### 4. **How Often Do You Travel for Business?** (`travel_frequency`, select_one, required)
Select one of the options to describe how often you travel for business:

* **Weekly**
* **2-3 times per month**
* **Monthly**
* **Quarterly**
* **Once or twice per year**
* **Rarely**

### 5. **Are You Responding About a Recent Trip?** (`recent_trip`, select_one, required)

* **No, general feedback**

* **One week**
* **2+ weeks**
* **Varies widely**
