# Warehouse Supervisor Performance Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Warehouse Supervisor Performance Survey is designed to assess the performance of warehouse supervisors. The survey is meant to collect feedback on a supervisor's leadership, communication, and safety compliance. The survey is typically completed by team members or managers to evaluate the supervisor's effectiveness in their role.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the response for each section**: Answer the questions based on your experience and interaction with the supervisor.
2. **Rate the supervisor's performance**: Use numbers to rate the supervisor's performance in leadership, communication, and safety compliance sections.
3. **Provide comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the supervisor's performance.
4. **Submit the form**: Click "Submit" to complete and send the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Leadership Rating** (`leadership_rating`): Rate the supervisor's leadership performance on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Leadership Comments** (`leadership_notes`): Add any comments about the supervisor's leadership.
* **Communication Rating** (`communication_rating`): Rate the supervisor's communication performance on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Communication Comments** (`communication_comments`): Add any comments about the supervisor's communication.
* **Safety Compliance Rating** (`safety_compliance_rating`): Rate the supervisor's safety compliance performance on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Safety Comments** (`safety_comments`): Add any comments about the supervisor's safety compliance.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Add any additional comments or feedback about the supervisor.
* **Leadership Rating 2** (`leadership_rating_2`): Rate the supervisor's leadership performance on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Communication Rating 2** (`communication_rating_2`): Rate the supervisor's communication performance on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Safety Compliance Rating 2** (`safety_compliance_rating_2`): Rate the supervisor's safety compliance performance on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Additional Comments 2** (`additional_comments_2`): Add any additional comments or feedback about the supervisor.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the supervisor's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the supervisor's phone number.

## Tips

* Be honest and objective when answering the questions.
* Use specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
* Keep your comments concise and clear.
* Do not include any identifying information about other team members.
* If you are unsure about any section, please contact the survey administrator for clarification.
