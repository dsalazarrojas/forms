# Catalogue Design Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the design of a catalogue.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and specifically.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as possible.
3. If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please write them down in the relevant fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required: false): This field is for your name, which will help us understand who provided the feedback.
* **Your Role in the Project** (`reviewer_role`, select_one, required: true): Please select your role in the project, such as "Designer", "Marketing Manager", "Product Owner", "Sales Team", "External Consultant", or "Customer".
* **Rate the overall visual appeal of the catalogue** (`overall_appeal_rating`, select_one, required: true): Please rate the overall visual appeal of the catalogue on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Quality of images and photography** (`image_quality_rating`, select_one, required: true): Please rate the quality of the images and photography on a scale from "Excellent" to "Poor".
* **Does the color palette align with our brand** (`color_palette_feedback`, select_one, required: true): Please select whether the color palette aligns with our brand, "Yes, perfectly", "Somewhat", or "No, needs adjustment".
* **Rate the flow and organization of the pages** (`layout_flow_rating`, select_one, required: true): Please rate the flow and organization of the pages on a scale from "Very Logical" to "Confusing".
* **How would you rate the readability of the text** (`text_readability`, select_one, required: true): Please rate the readability of the text on a scale from "Easy to read" to "Very hard to read".
* **How easy is it to find specific products** (`product_search_ease`, select_one, required: false): Please rate how easy it is to find specific products on a scale from "Very Easy" to "Difficult".
* **Are the call-to-action points clear** (`cta_clarity`, select_one, required: true): Please select whether the call-to-action points are clear, "Clear and Prominent", "Visible but not clear", or "Hidden or unclear".
* **Is this design ready for print** (`finalized_status`, select_one, required: true): Please select whether the design is ready for print, "Yes, ready", "Yes, with minor changes", or "No, needs major revision".
