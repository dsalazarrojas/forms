# Price Control Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about customers' perceptions and expectations related to price control, allowing you to understand their price-related needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions truthfully to provide accurate feedback.
2. For the **price_sensitivity** and **price_confidence** fields, enter numerical values as requested.
3. For the **pricing_strategy** field, select one of the provided options that best aligns with your current price control approach.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### customer_feedback
* **customer-feedback** (`customer_feedback`, text, required: false): Please provide any additional feedback about your price control experience or expectations.

### pricing_strategy
* **pricing-strategy** (`pricing_strategy`, select_one, required: false): Select the pricing strategy that best aligns with your current approach. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### price_sensitivity
* **price-sensitivity** (`price_sensitivity`, number, required: false): Enter a numerical value to indicate your price sensitivity. For example, if you feel that small changes in price have a significant impact on your purchasing decisions, enter a higher value.

### price_confidence
* **price-confidence** (`price_confidence`, number, required: false): Enter a numerical value to indicate your confidence in your current price understanding. For example, if you feel that you have a good understanding of prices and their impact on your decisions, enter a higher value.
