# Key Checkout Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers after they have checked out from a business or service. The information collected will help us understand their experience and make improvements to provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information:
	* First name
	* Last name
	* Email address
	* Phone number
	* Date and time of visit
	* Time spent
	* Rating of your experience
2. Select your options for your favorite items.
3. If you have any comments, please add them in the comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Date visited** (`date_visited`, date, required): Please select the date and time you visited us.
* **Time spent** (`time_spent`, time, required): Please select the amount of time you spent with us.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required): Rate your experience:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions, please add them here.
* **Favorite item 1** (`select_one_options`, select_one, optional): Which of the following items was your favorite?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Favorite item 2** (`select_one_options_2`, select_one, optional): Which of the following items was your favorite?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Favorite item 3** (`select_one_select_one_options_2`, select_multiple, optional): Which of the following items was your favorite?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Please add any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Favorite item 4** (`select_multiple_options_2`, select_multiple, optional): Which of the following items were your favorites?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Favorite item 5** (`select_multiple_select_multiple_options_2`, select_multiple, optional): Which of the following items were your favorites?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Favorite item 6** (`select_multiple_select_multiple_options_3`, select_multiple, optional): Which of the following items were your favorites?
	+ Yes
	+ No

Note: The optional fields (Comments, Favorite item 1-6) are not required to be filled out. If you don't have any comments or favorite items, please leave them blank.
