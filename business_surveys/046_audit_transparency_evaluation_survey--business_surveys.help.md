# Audit Transparency Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the transparency of the audit process. It is used to gather feedback from respondents about their experiences with audits, focusing on the level of transparency during each step of the process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your information and answers to the questions.
2. Be honest in your answers, as they will help us improve the audit process.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields.
4. If you are unsure about any question, leave a note in the "Transparency Improvements" field.
5. Once you have completed the form, click submit to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Respondent Information

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): This is a required field, but you can leave it blank if you prefer to be anonymous. 
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, optional): This field is optional and will help us contact you for follow-up questions. 
* **Stakeholder Type** (`stakeholder_type`, select_one, required): This field helps us understand your relationship to the audit. Please select one of the options:
	+ Board Member
	+ Senior Management
	+ Department Head
	+ Audit Committee Member
	+ External Stakeholder
	+ Internal Auditor
	+ Auditee
	+ Other

### Page 2: Audit Information

* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): This is the date of this survey.
* **Audit Name** (`audit_name`, text, required): This is the name of the audit being evaluated.
* **Audit Completion Date** (`audit_completion_date`, date, required): This is the date the audit was completed.
