# Team Role Ownership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand team dynamics and identify areas where team members may need additional support or resources to excel in their roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are filling it out only once.
2. Select the correct answer for each question from the provided options.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, but do not feel obligated to answer questions that are marked as optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Ownership** (team_ownership, select_one, optional):
  This question asks if you own or identify strongly with your current role. Please select "Yes" if you feel a strong sense of ownership and responsibility towards your role, or "No" if you don't.
* **Gauging Comfort Level** (gauging_comfort_level, select_multiple, optional):
  This question helps us gauge your comfort level with your current role. Select all that apply from the options "High", "Low", or "Neutral" to indicate your comfort level.
* **Interested In New Roles** (interested_in_new_roles, select_one, optional):
  This question is to determine if you are interested in taking on new roles or responsibilities within the team. Please select "Yes" if you are interested and "No" if you are not.
