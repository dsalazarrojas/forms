# global_drumming_community_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The global_drumming_community_survey form is designed to understand the level of engagement and interest of the global drumming community. It is intended to gather information on the community's demographics, drumming experience, and preferences regarding drumming programs and styles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your experience and preferences.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. If you have any feedback or comments, please use the "feedback" field to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Global Drumming Community Survey** (`introduction`, `note`): This is an introductory note explaining the purpose of the form.
* **What's your drumming experience?** (`experience`, `number`): Please indicate your level of experience in drumming, on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **What drumming styles do you typically play?** (`styles`, `select_multiple`): Choose all the drumming styles you usually play, such as Jazz, Rock, Classical, etc.
* **How involved are you with the global drumming community?** (`involvement`, `select_one`): Select the level of your involvement with the drumming community, i.e., Slightly involved, Moderately involved, or Highly involved.
* **What types of drumming programs would you like to see more?** (`programs`, `select_multiple`): Check all the types of drumming programs you would like to see more, such as Workshops, Lessons, Meetups, etc.
* **Which of the following drumming styles would you like to see more?** (`styles_programs`, `select_multiple`): Choose all the drumming styles you would like to see more, such as Jazz, Rock, Classical, or Other.
* **How would you describe your relationship with the drumming community?** (`community`, `select_multiple`): Select how you perceive your relationship with the drumming community, i.e., Very close, Somewhat close, or Not very close.
* **Do you have any other feedback for the drumming community?** (`feedback`, `note`): Use this field to provide any other comments or suggestions you may have for the drumming community.
