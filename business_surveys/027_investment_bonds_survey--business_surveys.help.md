# Investment Bonds Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about investment bonds from participants. It's meant to gauge awareness, knowledge, interest, and attitudes related to investment bonds. The goal is to understand trends in these areas and gather data that can inform business decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the survey you're responding to (Business Surveys or Financial Surveys).
2. Provide a brief description of what you're aware of, know, or interested in when it comes to investment bonds.
3. Choose the awareness trends that apply to you (awareness, knowledge, interest, or attitudes).
4. Rate the value of each awareness trend you selected.
5. Select the motivations trends that apply to you (motivations or satisfaction).
6. Rate the value of each motivation trend you selected.
7. Choose the satisfaction trends that apply to you (satisfaction or awareness).
8. Rate the value of each satisfaction trend you selected.
9. Select the demographics that apply to you (demographics or location).
10. Enter the value of each demographic you selected.
11. Answer the following questions:
	* What is your awareness about investment bonds?
	* What is your knowledge about investment bonds?
	* What is your interest in investment bonds?
	* What is your attitudes about investment bonds?
	* What is your confidence in investment bonds?
	* What is your risk perception of investment bonds?
	* What is your investment experience with investment bonds?
