# Sports Viewing Habits Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your sports viewing habits, including the types of sports you watch, devices you use, and the duration you spend watching sports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Select the types of sports you enjoy watching in the "sports" field. You can choose one or multiple options.
6. Choose the devices on which you watch sports in the "Watch sports on" field. Again, you can select one or multiple options.
7. Choose the additional devices on which you watch sports in the "Watch sports on 2" field. This is another opportunity to list the devices you use for sports viewing.
8. Select the duration of time you typically spend watching sports in the "How much" field.
9. Choose how often you watch sports in the "How often" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address that you want to be contacted on.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number that you can be reached on.
* **Sports** (select multiple, optional): Select the types of sports you enjoy watching. Options include:
	+ NFL
	+ NBA
	+ MLB
	+ NHL
	+ Golf
	+ Boxing
	+ UFC
	+ Soccer
	+ Tennis
	+ Cricket
* **Watch sports on** (select multiple, optional): Choose the devices on which you watch sports. Options include:
	+ Phone
	+ Tablet
	+ Laptop
	+ Computer
	+ Smartwatch
	+ Gaming console
	+ TV
	+ Radio
	+ Live streaming
* **Watch sports on 2** (select multiple, optional): Choose additional devices on which you watch sports. Again, you can select one or multiple options.
* **How much** (select one, required): Choose the duration of time you typically spend watching sports, from less than 1 hour to more than 6 hours.
* **How often** (select one, required): Choose how often you watch sports, from never to almost every day.
