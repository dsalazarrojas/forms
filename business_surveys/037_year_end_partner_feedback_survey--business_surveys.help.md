# Year End Partner Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from partners at the end of the year. This feedback is essential in helping us understand our partner's perspective and experiences, which will inform our strategies and improvements for the coming year.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please select all that apply to the questions provided.
2.  In the "Comments" field, you can provide any additional information or thoughts you'd like to share.
3.  Fill in your name and email address in the respective fields.
4.  Rate your overall experience with a number from 1 to 5.
5.  Once you've completed all the necessary fields, click "Submit" to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**:
This is a note field where you can read a brief introduction to the survey.
* **Partner**:
Select all that apply to the following questions:
	+ "Yes" - If you have been a partner of the company this year.
	+ "No" - If you were not a partner this year.
* **Questions**:
Select all that apply to the following questions:
	+ "Yes" - If the following events or activities were part of your partnership.
	+ "No" - If the following events or activities were not part of your partnership.
* **What would you like to see us do differently in the coming year?**:
Select all that apply to the following questions:
	+ "Yes" - If you would like us to take certain actions or provide certain services in the coming year.
	+ "No" - If you would not like us to take certain actions or provide certain services in the coming year.
* **Comments**:
This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or thoughts about your partnership.
* **Name**:
Please fill in your name as it appears on your business card or correspondence.
* **Email**:
Please fill in your email address as it appears in your business email.
* **Rating (1-5)**:
Rate your overall experience with the company on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Submit**:
Click this button to submit your feedback.
