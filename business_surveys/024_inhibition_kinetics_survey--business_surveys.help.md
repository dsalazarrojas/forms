# Inhibition Kinetics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Inhibition Kinetics Survey" is designed to collect information about inhibition kinetics, which is a crucial aspect of chemical reactions. This form aims to gather details from users about enzymes, their inhibitors, and the parameters that affect the reaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the "Survey Details" field with a brief description of the survey or study you are conducting.
3. Provide information about the enzyme, its structure, and its function in the "Enzyme Details" field.
4. Describe the inhibition details, including the type of inhibitor, its structure, and its effects on the enzyme, in the "Inhibition Details" field.
5. Enter the kinetic parameters, such as rates, rates constants, and other relevant data, in the "Kinetic Parameters" field.
6. If any additional details are relevant to your survey or study, enter them in the "Other Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Details (id: 1)**: This field is where you can provide a brief description of the survey or study you are conducting. Be as specific as possible to help others understand the context of your research.
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** no
* **Enzyme Details (id: 2)**: This field is where you can provide information about the enzyme, its structure, and its function. This will help the researcher understand the enzyme's role in the reaction.
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** no
* **Inhibition Details (id: 3)**: This field is where you can describe the inhibition details, including the type of inhibitor, its structure, and its effects on the enzyme. This will help the researcher understand the inhibition mechanism.
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** no
* **Kinetic Parameters (id: 4)**: This field is where you can enter kinetic parameters, such as rates, rates constants, and other relevant data. This will help the researcher understand the kinetic aspects of the reaction.
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** no
* **Other Details (id: 5)**: This field is where you can provide any additional information that is relevant to your survey or study. This can include background information, methods, or other context.
	+ **Type:** text
	+ **Required:** no

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely to provide the best possible information for analysis.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
* The data collected from this form will be used to analyze and understand inhibition kinetics.
