# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
The <string> form is used to collect and manage project information for research projects. It helps project leads and researchers to provide detailed descriptions of their projects, including their goals, objectives, and outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the project details page.
2. Fill in the project title in the "Project Title" field. This field is required and should be a short summary of the project.
3. Select the research focus area from the options provided in the "Research Focus Area" field. This field is required and will help the project lead and other team members understand the project scope.
4. In the "Project Goals and Objectives" field, provide a detailed description of the project goals and objectives. This field is required and should be a comprehensive overview of what the project aims to achieve.
5. Enter the project methodology used in the "Research Methodology" field. This field is required and should provide information on the approach and methods used to conduct the research.
6. Choose the project status from the options provided in the "Project Status" field. This field is required and will help in tracking the project progress.
7. Enter the next steps in the "Next Steps" field. This field is required and should provide information on the next actions to be taken in the project.
8. Enter the project lead name in the "Project Lead" field. This field is required and should be the person responsible for the project.
9. Select the research area from the options provided in the "Research Area" field. This field is required and will help in understanding the project scope.
10. Enter the project start date in the "Project Start Date" field. This field is required and should be the date when the project starts.
11. Enter the project end date in the "Project End Date" field. This field is required and should be the date when the project completes.
12. Enter the project duration in the "Project Duration" field. This field is required and should be the length of time the project will take.
13. Enter the project location in the "Project Location" field. This field is required and should be the location where the project is being conducted.
14. Enter the project funding source in the "Project Funding" field. This field is required and should provide information on who is funding the project.
15. Enter the project outcome in the "Project Outcome" field. This field is required and should provide a description of the project outcome.
16. Enter the names of team members in the "Team Members" field. This field is required and should include all team members involved in the project.
17. Enter the research team name in the "Research Team" field. This field is required and should be the name of the research team.
18. Enter the project outcome status in the "Project Outcome Status" field. This field is required and should provide information on the project outcome status.
19. Enter the review date in the "Review Date" field. This field is required and should be the date of the last review.
20. Enter the next review date in the "Next Review Date" field. This field is required and should be the date of the next review.
21. Choose the next review status from the options provided in the "Next Review Status" field. This field is required and will help in tracking the project progress.
22. Enter the evaluation score in the "Evaluation Score" field. This field is required and should provide a measure of the project evaluation.
23. Enter the evaluation comment in the "Evaluation Comment" field. This field is required and should provide any comments or feedback from the evaluation process.
24. (Optional) Enter the final review status in the "Final Review Status" field. This field is not required but will help in tracking the project completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title** (`project_title`, `text`, required): A short summary of the project.
* **Research Focus Area** (`research_focus_area`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the research focus area of the project.
* **Project Goals and Objectives** (`project_goals_objectives`, `note`, required): A detailed description of the project goals and objectives.
* **Research Methodology** (`research_methodology`, `select_one`, required): Choose the research methodology used in the project.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, `number`, required): Choose the project status.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `note`, required): Information on the next actions to be taken in the project.
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the project lead.
* **Research Area** (`research_area`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the research area of the project.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the project starts.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the project completes.
* **Project Duration** (`project_duration`, `number`, required): Enter the length of time the project will take.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the project is being conducted.
* **Project Funding** (`project_funding`, `text`, required): Enter the source of project funding.
* **Project Outcome** (`project_outcome`, `text`, required): Enter a description of the project outcome.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, `text`, required): Enter the names of team members involved in the project.
* **Research Team** (`research_team`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the research team.
* **Project Outcome Status** (`project_outcome_status`, `text`, required): Enter the status of the project outcome.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the last review.
* **Next Review Date** (`next_review_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the next review.
* **Next Review Status** (`next_review_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the next review status.
* **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`, `number`, required): Enter the evaluation score.
* **Evaluation Comment** (`evaluation_comment`, `note`, required): Enter any comments or feedback from the evaluation process.
* **Final Review Status** (`final_review_status`, `select_one`, optional): (Optional) Choose the final review status.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are necessary to complete the form. The other fields are optional and can be completed as needed.
