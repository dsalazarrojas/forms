# Business Transition Consulting Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather essential information about your business transition plans, allowing us to better understand your organization's current state, strengths, and weaknesses. This will help us tailor our support and consulting services to meet your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question carefully, ensuring you provide as much detail as possible. If you're unsure about any of the questions, please provide an explanation or clarification. Don't worry if you're unable to answer a question; simply proceed to the next one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, required): Enter your company or organization email address.
* **Company or Organization Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Your Role** (`respondent_role`, text, required): Describe your current position within the organization.
* **How Long Have You Been With This Company?** (`years_with_company`, number, optional): If applicable, please provide the number of years you've been with this company. If not applicable, you can skip this question.
* **Years of Experience in This Industry** (`years_in_industry`, number, optional): If applicable, please provide the number of years you've been in this industry. If not applicable, you can skip this question.
* **Type of Business Transition** (`transition_type`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options: Ownership succession or sale, Leadership transition or retirement, Merger or acquisition, Restructuring, Expansion, Market pivot, or Other transition.
* **Transition Timeline** (`transition_timeline`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options: Immediate - within 3 months, Near-term - 3-6 months, Medium-term - 6-12 months, Long-term - 1-3 years, or Undetermined.
* **Current Readiness for Transition** (`transition_readiness`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options: Very prepared, Mostly prepared, Somewhat prepared, Not very prepared, or Not prepared.
* **Key Challenges You Anticipate** (`key_challenges`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Finding qualified successor
	+ Knowledge transfer
	+ Team retention
	+ Financial planning
	+ Customer retention
	+ Maintaining continuity
	+ Regulatory requirements
* **Company Strengths That Will Aid Transition** (`key_strengths`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Strong leadership team
	+ Documented processes
	+ Stable customer base
	+ Financial stability
	+ Positive culture
	+ Modern technology
	+ Experienced staff
	+ Clear strategic vision
* **Current Financial Condition** (`financial_condition`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options: Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, or Needs improvement.
* **Documentation and Systems Status** (`documentation_status`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options: Well documented, Mostly documented, Partially documented, Poorly documented, or Not documented.
* **Do you have a Knowledge Transfer Plan?** (`knowledge_transfer_plan`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options: Yes, comprehensive plan, Yes, basic plan, No plan yet, or In development.
* **Succession Plan Status** (`succession_plan_status`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options: Formal plan in place, Informal understanding, Under development, or Not yet addressed.
* **Stakeholder Readiness for Change** (`stakeholder_readiness`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options: Very ready, Ready, Somewhat ready, Not very ready, or Resistant.
* **External Support and Consulting Needed** (`external_support_needed`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply:
	+ Strategic planning
	+ Financial advisory
	+ Legal consultation
	+ Change management
	+ Leadership coaching
	+ Market analysis
	+ Operations optimization
	+ HR and talent management
* **Communication Strategy for Transition** (`communication_plan`, text, optional): Describe your plan for communicating the changes to stakeholders.
* **What Will Define Success?** (`success_factors`, text, required): Share your vision for a successful transition.
* **Additional Concerns or Questions** (`additional_concerns`, text, optional): If you have any additional concerns or questions, please share them here.

## Tips
- Please be as specific and detailed as possible when answering each question.
- If you're unsure or have a question about any of the questions, don't hesitate to ask us.
- This information will help us provide tailored support and consulting services to meet your organization's unique needs.
