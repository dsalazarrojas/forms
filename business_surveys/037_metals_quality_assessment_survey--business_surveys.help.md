# metals quality assessment survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about the quality of metal samples received from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have the relevant information about the sample, such as the sample ID, customer name, and customer phone number.
2. Fill in the required fields, including Sample ID, customer name, and sample weight.
3. Provide any additional information you believe is necessary to give a complete assessment of the sample quality.
4. Review and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Sample ID** (`sample_id`, number, required): Enter the unique ID of the sample.
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the customer who submitted the sample.
- **Metal Type** (`metal_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of metal in the sample, if applicable.
- **Sample Weight** (`sample_weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight of the sample, if provided.
- **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, number, optional): Enter the customer's ID number, if applicable.
- **Metal Grade** (`metal_grade`, text, optional): Enter the grade of the metal in the sample, if known.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number, if available.
- **Sample Received Date** (`sample_received_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the sample was received, if known.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address, if available.
- **Sample Received Time** (`sample_received_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the sample was received, if known.
- **Metal Quality** (`metal_quality`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value for the quality of the sample, if applicable.
- **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the sample from the customer.
- **Sample ID 2** (`sample_id_2`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the second sample, if applicable.
- **Customer Phone 2** (`customer_phone_2`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the second customer, if known.
- **Customer Email 2** (`customer_email_2`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the second customer, if available.
- **Sample Received Date 2** (`sample_received_date_2`, date, optional): Enter the date the second sample was received, if known.
- **Sample Received Time 2** (`sample_received_time_2`, time, optional): Enter the time the second sample was received, if applicable.
- **Customer Note 2** (`customer_note_2`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the second sample from the customer.

## Tips
- Ensure accuracy and precision when filling out the form to provide the best quality assessment of the sample.
- If a field has a label with a suffix, it likely pertains to a second sample or customer.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or support team for assistance.
