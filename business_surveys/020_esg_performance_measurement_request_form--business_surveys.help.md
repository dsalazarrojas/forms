# ESG Performance Measurement Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
<thinking>
This form is intended to capture essential details about the ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) performance measurement request submitted by employees. The purpose of this form is to gather accurate and consistent information about the ESG performance measurement requests across different employees, ensuring timely and efficient submission management. This is crucial for maintaining a centralized and up-to-date ESG performance measurement system.
</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form effectively, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the title bar.
2. Enter your ESG performance measurement request in the corresponding fields, such as 'ESG Performance Measurement Request Form', 'Business Surveys', 'ESG Data Collection', etc.
3. Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number (if available).
4. Select the appropriate options for 'ESG Form Version', 'ESG Form Status', and 'ESG Form Status 2'.
5. Add any additional comments, if necessary.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **ESG Performance Measurement Request Form (*)**: Enter a brief description of your ESG performance measurement request.
* **Business Surveys (*)**: Describe the business surveys related to your ESG performance measurement request.
* **ESG Data Collection (*)**: Explain the ESG data collection methods or tools you plan to use.
* **ESG Template (*)**: Provide any relevant ESG templates or examples.
* **Jotform Form Builder (*)**: Describe your experience with Jotform Form Builder or any specific features used.
* **ESG No-Code Drag-and-Drop Interface (*)**: Explain how you plan to use the ESG No-Code Drag-and-Drop Interface.
* **ESG Data Collection Efficient Submission Management (*)**: Describe your approach to efficient submission management for ESG data collection.
* **ESG Performance Measurement Request Form Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the form.
* **ESG Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the ESG performance measurement process.
* **ESG Submitter Info**: Enter any relevant information about yourself as the submitter.
* **ESG Submitter**: Enter your name as the submitter.
* **ESG Submitted By**: Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
* **ESG Form Submitted Date (*)**: Select the date the form was submitted.
* **ESG Form Submitted At (*)**: Select the time the form was submitted.
* **ESG Form Version (*)**: Choose whether the form version is Yes or No.
* **ESG Form Version 2 (*)**: Choose the relevant options for this field.
* **ESG Form Status (*)**: Select the current status of the form, either Active or Inactive.
* **ESG Form Status 2 (*)**: Select the relevant options for this field.
* **ESG Form Comments**: Add any comments or feedback about the form.
* **ESG Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the ESG performance measurement process.
* **ESG Submitter Email**: Enter your email address as the person submitting the form.
* **ESG Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number as the person submitting the form.

**Tips**

* Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Select the correct options for 'ESG Form Version', 'ESG Form Status', and 'ESG Form Status 2' according to your form requirements.
* Use the 'ESG Form Comments' field for any general comments or feedback about the form.
* Use the 'ESG Additional Comments' field for any specific comments or suggestions about the ESG performance measurement process.
