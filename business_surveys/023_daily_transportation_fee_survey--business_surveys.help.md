# daily transportation fee survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The daily transportation fee survey is designed to gather information about your daily commuting habits, specifically regarding methods, costs, and satisfaction levels. This data will help our organization make informed decisions on transportation policies and benefits for employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To get started, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully to understand what is being asked.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Ensure you fill out all required fields (indicated by *required*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Commute Method**:
  * Label: Commute Method
  * Type: Select One
  * Explanation: This field asks you to select the methods you use for commuting, such as 'Yes' if you take public transportation or 'No' if you drive alone.
* **Frequency**:
  * Label: Frequency
  * Type: Number
  * Explanation: Report the number of days you commute per week. This helps us understand how often you use transportation services or methods.
* **Satisfaction**:
  * Label: Satisfaction
  * Type: Number
  * Explanation: Rate your satisfaction level from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 10 (very satisfied) with your current transportation methods or arrangements.
* **Cost**:
  * Label: Cost
  * Type: Number
  * Explanation: Report the approximate average cost of your daily commute. This can include fuel, public transport, or other expenses related to your commute.
* **Transportation Mode**:
  * Label: Transportation Mode
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Explanation: Indicate which modes of transportation you use, such as driving alone, public transportation, or carpooling.
* **Distance**:
  * Label: Distance
  * Type: Number
  * Explanation: Report the average distance you cover during your daily commute.
* **Hours Commute**:
  * Label: Hours Commute
  * Type: Number
  * Explanation: Report the average time you spend commuting each day.
* **Travel Time**:
  * Label: Travel Time
  * Type: Time
  * Explanation: Report the time of your daily commute, including departure and arrival times.
* **Cost Frequency**:
  * Label: Cost Frequency
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Explanation: Indicate how frequently you pay fees or costs related to your commute, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Cost Frequency 2**:
  * Label: Cost Frequency 2
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Explanation: This field seems to be a duplicate or continuation of the previous 'Cost Frequency' field, likely asking about the same frequency of cost-related activities but with more specific options.
* **Cost Vehicles**:
  * Label: Cost Vehicles
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Explanation: Indicate which vehicles you might incur costs for, such as buses, trains, or cars.
* **Satisfaction Mode**:
  * Label: Satisfaction Mode
  * Type: Select One
  * Explanation: Rate your level of satisfaction with your current transportation arrangements, whether 'Satisfied' or 'Dissatisfied'.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and accurately to help us better understand your commuting habits.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, feel free to contact us for assistance.
- This data will be used to inform our organization's transportation policies and benefits for employees.
