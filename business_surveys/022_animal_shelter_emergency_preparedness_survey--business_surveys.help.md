# Animal Shelter Emergency Preparedness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to help animal shelters assess their emergency preparedness and identify areas for improvement. The goal is to gather information on the shelter's capacity to respond to emergencies, such as natural disasters, animal disease outbreaks, and other crises. This information will help shelters develop effective emergency plans and provide a baseline for future assessments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct answer for each question based on your shelter's current status.
2. For multiple-select questions, choose all applicable options.
3. Provide additional comments or concerns in the "Additional Comments or Concerns" section if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shelter Name** (`shelter_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Your Role** (`respondent_role`, `text`, required): Enter your position title.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Typical Animal Capacity** (`shelter_capacity`, `number`, required): Enter the average number of animals your shelter typically cares for.
* **Do You Have a Written Emergency Plan** (`emergency_plan_status`, `select_one`, required): Select how up-to-date and complete your emergency plan is.
* **When Was Your Emergency Plan Last Updated** (`plan_last_updated`, `select_one`, required): Enter when your emergency plan was last updated (if never, select "Never").
* **Which Emergencies Does Your Plan Cover** (`emergency_types`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all types of emergencies your plan covers (e.g., natural disasters, disease outbreaks, etc.).
* **Do You Have an Evacuation Plan** (`evacuation_plan`, `select_one`, required): Select how prepared you are for evacuation.
* **Can You Evacuate All Animals Within 2 Hours** (`evacuation_capacity`, `select_one`, required): Select your capacity to evacuate all animals within 2 hours.
* **Do You Have Access to Emergency Transport** (`transport_resources`, `select_one`, required): Select how you have access to emergency transport vehicles.
* **Emergency Supplies Status** (`emergency_supplies`, `select_one`, required): Select your emergency supplies status (e.g., 7+ days, 3-7 days, etc.).
* **Backup Power Availability** (`backup_power`, `select_one`, required): Select if you have backup power availability.
* **Staff Emergency Training Status** (`staff_training`, `select_one`, required): Select how much staff training you have on emergency procedures.
* **How Often Do You Conduct Emergency Drills** (`training_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you conduct emergency drills.
* **Do You Have Agreements with Other Shelters** (`partner_agreements`, `select_one`, required): Select if you have agreements with other shelters for mutual aid.
* **Emergency Contact List Status** (`emergency_contacts`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your emergency contact list.
* **Emergency Communication Plan** (`communication_plan`, `select_one`, required): Select your emergency communication plan (e.g., multiple methods, basic plan, etc.).
* **Animal Identification System** (`animal_identification`, `select_one`, required): Select your animal identification system (e.g., microchips, collars, etc.).
* **Medical Records Accessibility** (`medical_records`, `select_one`, required): Select how easily you can access medical records during emergencies.
* **Emergency Fund Availability** (`funding_available`, `select_one`, required): Select if you have dedicated emergency funds.
* **What Are Your Biggest Emergency Preparedness Challenges** (`biggest_challenges`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all challenges you face in emergency preparedness (e.g., limited budget, limited staff, etc.).
* **What Support Would Help Most** (`support_needed`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all support areas that would help most in emergency preparedness (e.g., training, funding, etc.).
* **Additional Comments or Concerns** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or concerns you have.

Please note that this is a sample guide, and you can adjust it according to your specific needs.
