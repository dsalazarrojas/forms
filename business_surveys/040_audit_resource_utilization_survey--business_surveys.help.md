# Audit Resource Utilization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the resource utilization during an audit project. It is intended to gather data on various aspects of resource utilization, including planned and actual hours spent, budget variance, staff allocation, technology resources, tool utilization, external resources needed, overtime required, resource constraints, resource efficiency, utilization rate, improvement areas, and overall resource satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your name in the "Your Name" field. If you wish to remain anonymous, you can leave this field blank.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field, which is optional but helpful for follow-up purposes.
3. Select your department from the list in the "Department" field.
4. Choose your role in the audit from the list in the "Role in Audit" field.
5. Enter the date of the survey in the "Survey Date" field.
6. Type the name of the audit project in the "Audit Project" field.
7. Enter the total planned hours for the audit project in the "Planned Hours" field.
8. Enter the total actual hours spent on the audit project in the "Actual Hours" field.
9. Select how the actual hours compare to the budget in the "Budget Variance" field.
10. Rate your satisfaction with staff allocation in the "Staff Allocation" field.
11. Choose how well the skills matched the requirements in the "Skill Match" field.
12. Select how adequate the technology resources were in the "Technology Resources" field.
13. Choose how well the audit tools were utilized in the "Tool Utilization" field.
14. Select whether external resources were needed in the "External Resources" field.
15. Choose how much overtime was required in the "Overtime Required" field.
16. Select which resource constraints existed in the "Resource Constraints" field. You can select multiple options.
17. Rate your satisfaction with resource efficiency in the "Resource Efficiency" field.
18. Choose the utilization rate in the "Utilization Rate" field.
19. Select areas that need improvement in the "Improvement Areas" field. You can select multiple options.
20. If you have any recommendations for resource utilization, type them in the "Recommendations" field, which is optional.
21. Rate your overall satisfaction with resources in the "Overall Resource Satisfaction" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name for follow-up purposes. If you wish to remain anonymous, you can leave this field blank.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address, which is helpful for follow-up purposes, but not required.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select your department from the list.
* **Role in Audit** (`role_in_audit`, `select_one`, required): Choose your role in the audit from the list.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the survey.
* **Audit Project** (`audit_project`, `text`, required): Type the name of the audit project.
* **Planned Hours** (`planned_hours`, `number`, required): Enter the total planned hours for the audit project.
* **Actual Hours** (`actual_hours`, `number`, required): Enter the total actual hours spent on the audit project.
* **Budget Variance** (`budget_variance`, `select_one`, required): Select how the actual hours compare to the budget.
* **Staff Allocation** (`staff_allocation`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with staff allocation.
* **Skill Match** (`skill_match`, `select_one`, required): Choose how well the skills matched the requirements.
* **Technology Resources** (`technology_resources`, `select_one`, required): Select how adequate the technology resources were.
* **Tool Utilization** (`tool_utilization`, `select_one`, required): Choose how well the audit tools were utilized.
* **External Resources** (`external_resources`, `select_one`, required): Select whether external resources were needed.
* **Overtime Required** (`overtime_required`, `select_one`, required): Choose how much overtime was required.
* **Resource Constraints** (`resource_constraints`, `select_multiple`, required): Select which resource constraints existed. You can select multiple options.
* **Resource Efficiency** (`resource_efficiency`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with resource efficiency.
* **Utilization Rate** (`utilization_rate`, `select_one`, required): Choose the utilization rate.
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Select areas that need improvement. You can select multiple options.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `text`, optional): If you have any recommendations for resource utilization, type them in this field.
* **Overall Resource Satisfaction** (`overall_resource_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with resources.
