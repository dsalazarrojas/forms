# Community Waste Segregation Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to understand community residents' waste segregation practices, knowledge, and willingness to improve their waste management habits.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please select the "Not sure" or "Not applicable" option.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Any additional comments or suggestions?" section at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`resident_name`, text, Optional): This is your name for the purpose of this survey. It is not required for the survey to be completed, but it will help us understand who is responding.
* **Email Address** (`resident_email`, email, For follow-up): This is your email address for any follow-up communication or education about waste management.
* **Household Address** (`household_address`, text, Required): This is your physical address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Household Size** (`household_size`, number, Required): This is the number of people living in your household.
* **Housing Type** (`housing_type`, select_one, Required): This is the type of housing you live in, such as Single-family home, Apartment/Condo, etc.
* **Current Practice** (`current_practice`, select_one, Required): This questions asks about your current waste segregation practice, whether you always, sometimes, rarely, or never segregate your waste.
* **Separation Method** (`separation_method`, select_multiple, Optional): If you segregate your waste, please select all the methods you use, such as recycling, composting, etc.
* **Knowledge Level** (`knowledge_level`, select_one, Required): This is a self-assessment of your knowledge about waste segregation, from Very knowledgeable to No knowledge at all.
* **Recyclable Materials** (`recyclable_materials`, select_multiple, Required): This is a list of materials that you know are recyclable. Please select all that apply.
* **Composting Practice** (`composting_practice`, select_one, Required): This question asks if you compost organic waste, and if so, through what method (at home or through a community program).
* **Disposal Challenges** (`disposal_challenges`, select_multiple, Required): This questions asks about any challenges you face with waste segregation, such as lack of space, confusion, etc.
* **Additional Comments** (`challenge_details`, text, Optional): This is an opportunity to provide additional information about your challenges, if any.
* **Education Source** (`education_source`, select_multiple, Required): This is where you learned about waste segregation, such as school, local government, etc.
* **Education Effectiveness** (`education_effectiveness`, select_one, Optional): This question asks about the effectiveness of the education you received, from Very effective to Not effective at all.
* **Interest in Learning** (`interest_in_learning`, select_one, Required): This is how interested you are in learning more about waste segregation, from Very interested to Not interested at all.
* **Preferred Education Format** (`preferred_education_format`, select_multiple, Optional): This is how you would prefer to receive education about waste segregation, such as printed guides, online resources, etc.
* **Bin Availability** (`bin_availability`, select_one, Required): This question asks about the availability of separate bins for different waste types in your area.
* **Pickup Service** (`pickup_service`, select_one, Required): This is about the regularity of waste pickup service for recyclables in your area.
* **Willingness to Improve** (`willingness_to_improve`, select_one, Required): This question asks about your willingness to improve your waste segregation habits, from Very willing to Not willing at all.
* **Incentive Interest** (`incentive_interest`, select_one, Optional): This questions asks about whether incentives would encourage you to segregate waste, and if so, what type of incentive would motivate you.
* **Incentive Type** (`incentive_type`, select_multiple, Optional): This is a list of incentives that could motivate you to segregate waste, such as tax reduction, etc.
* **Community Program Interest** (`community_program_interest`, select_one, Optional): This question asks about your interest in participating in a community waste program.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, Optional): This is an opportunity to provide any additional comments or suggestions about waste segregation.
* **Survey Completion Date** (`survey_date`, date, Required): This is the date you completed this survey, automatically filled in for your convenience.
