# Painting Project Scope Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about painting projects for internal use. It collects various details about the project, including project name, type, status, client contact, project location, budget, scope, and contract details. This guide will walk you through the form's fields and explain each section's purpose.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Project Name**: Enter the name of the painting project.
2. **Project Type**: Select the type of project from the given options (Painting, Renovation, Repainting, or Other).
3. **Project Status**: Choose the current status of the project from the options (Active, Planned, Completed).
4. **Client Name**: Provide the client's name.
5. **Client Contact**: Enter the client's contact information (email).
6. **Project Location**: Specify the project location.
7. **Project Description**: Describe the project in detail.
8. **Project Scope**: Note down the project's scope.
9. **Project Budget**: Enter the project budget.
10. **Project Scope Items**: Select the items included in the project scope.
11. **Project Scope Notes**: Add any additional notes about the project scope.
12. **Contract Type**: Choose the type of contract (Lump Sum, Hourly, Unit Price).
13. **Contract Duration**: Select the duration of the contract (1-30 days, 31-90 days, More than 90 days).
14. **Contract Status**: Choose the status of the contract (Active, Inactive, Completed).
15. **Contract Start Date**: Enter the start date of the contract.
16. **Contract End Date**: Enter the end date of the contract.
17. **Contractor Name**: Provide the contractor's name.
18. **Contractor Email**: Enter the contractor's email address.
19. **Contractor Phone**: Enter the contractor's phone number.
20. **Contract Price**: Enter the contract price.
21. **Project Manager**: Enter the project manager's name.
22. **Project Manager Email**: Enter the project manager's email address.
23. **Project Manager Phone**: Enter the project manager's phone number.
24. **Payment Terms**: Describe the payment terms.
25. **Save and Submit**: Once you've filled out all the necessary fields, click 'Save and Submit' to complete the form.
