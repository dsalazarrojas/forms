# NGO Technology Capacity Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to assess the technology capacity of Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs) to effectively use and leverage technology to achieve their goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Carefully review each section and determine which questions apply to your organization.
2. Ensure you have the authority to complete this form on behalf of your NGO.
3. Answer each question honestly and accurately, based on your current technology capabilities.
4. Select the options that best fit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Introduction** 
* `title`: Introduction
* `type`: Note
* `required`: No
The introduction section is a brief description of your organization. Please provide a short summary of your NGO, including its mission, goals, and objectives.

### 2. **Tech Stack**
* `label`: Tech Stack
* `type`: Select Multiple
* `required`: No
This question asks if your NGO uses any specific technology or platforms that help you achieve your goals. Choose all that apply from the options provided.

### 3. **Tech Budget**
* `label`: Tech Budget
* `type`: Select One
* `required`: No
Indicate the level of technology budget your NGO has available for the current year.

### 4. **Tech Support**
* `label`: Tech Support
* `type`: Select Multiple
* `required`: No
Describe the level of technology support your NGO has, including help desk, email, phone, or online support.

### 5. **Digital Skills**
* `label`: Digital Skills
* `type`: Select Multiple
* `required`: No
Indicate the level of digital skills your NGO staff has, including programming, web development, data analysis, and other related areas.

### 6. **Staff**
* `label`: Staff
* `type`: Select Multiple
* `required`: No
Describe the number of staff members with technical expertise, including developers, analysts, and other related roles.

### 7. **Tech Needs**
* `label`: Tech Needs
* `type`: Note
* `required`: No
If you need any additional technology, please describe them and provide any supporting details.

### 8. **Action Plan**
* `label`: Action Plan
* `type`: Note
* `required`: No
Explain your technology action plan for the next 3-6 months, including any new initiatives, projects, or goals.

# Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when answering these questions.
* If you're unsure about any question, contact your supervisor or technology department for guidance.
* Ensure that the information you provide is up-to-date and reflects your current technology capacity.
