# Soft Material Crafting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soft Material Crafting Survey is a form designed to gather information about your soft material crafting projects. This includes data on project types, material types, colors, and other relevant details. Please answer the questions truthfully to help us better understand the soft material crafting market.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose a project type by selecting "Yarn", "Fiber", or "Other". If "Other", please provide more details in the "Project type other" field.
2. Select the types of materials used for your project (e.g., "Yarn", "Fiber", "Felt", "Thread").
3. Choose the colors used in your project from the list provided.
4. Select any patterns used in your project (e.g., "Stripe", "Dot", "Zigzag", "Solid").
5. If a pattern was not listed, please describe it in the "Pattern other" field.
6. Choose your level of craft skill by selecting "Basic", "Intermediate", or "Advanced".
7. Select your level of experience in craft by choosing a time range.
8. Enter your project's name and description.
9. Upload a project image, if possible.
10. Provide your contact information: name, email, and phone number.
11. If you want to provide feedback or additional information, please use the "Review", "Additional info", and "Feedback" fields.
12. Enter a reported value, if applicable, and describe it if it's not a standard option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Type** (2, select_one): Choose the type of project you are working on, such as "Yarn" or "Fiber".
* **Project Type Other** (3, text): If "Other" was selected, provide more details about your project type.
* **Material Type** (4, select_multiple): Select the types of materials used for your project.
* **Material Color** (5, select_multiple): Choose the colors used in your project.
* **Pattern** (6, select_multiple): Select any patterns used in your project.
* **Pattern Other** (7, text): If a pattern was not listed, describe it here.
* **Craft Skill** (8, select_one): Choose your level of craft skill.
* **Craft Experience** (9, select_one): Select your level of experience in craft.
* **Project Name** (10, text): Enter your project's name.
* **Project Description** (11, text): Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Project Image** (12, select_multiple): If applicable, upload a project image.
* **Contact Name** (13, text): Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (14, email): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (15, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Review** (16, text): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the survey.
* **Additional Info** (17, text): Provide any additional information about your project.
* **Feedback** (18, text): Enter any feedback about the form or survey.
* **Reported Value** (19, number): Enter a reported value, if applicable.
* **Reported Value Other** (20, text): If the reported value was not listed, describe it here.
* **Price Range** (21, select_one): Select a price range for your project.
* **Price Range Other** (22, text): If a price range was not listed, describe it here.
