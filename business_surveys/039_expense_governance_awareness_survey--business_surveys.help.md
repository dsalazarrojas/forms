# Expense Governance Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge employee awareness and understanding of expense governance policies within the organization. It's essential to fill out this form to help the organization identify areas of improvement and ensure effective expense management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the category of expense that best describes the type of expense you typically manage or are familiar with. (Category)
2. Briefly describe your primary function or role within the organization. (What is your primary function?)
3. Answer the following questions:
	* Have you received training on our expense policies? (Yes, No, Not sure)
	* Have you read our expense policy handbook? (Yes, No, Not sure)
	* How often do you expense personal purchases? (Never, Occasionally, Regularly, Always)
	* Do you have any suggestions for improving our expense governance? (text field)
	* Have you had any issues with our expense system? (Yes, No)
	* Do you have any feedback about our expense policy? (text field)
	* Do you know where to report a concern? (Yes, No)
	* Do you know who to contact for questions or concerns? (text field)
4. Provide any additional feedback or comments you have. (Is there anything else you'd like to tell us?)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Category`, `select_one`): This field allows you to select the category of expense that best describes the type of expense you typically manage or are familiar with. Please choose one of the options: Air Travel, Hotel, Dining, or Other.
* **What is your primary function?** (`What is your primary function?`, `text`): This field is for you to briefly describe your primary function or role within the organization. Please provide a brief answer.
* **Have you received training on our expense policies?** (`Have you received training on our expense policies?`, `select_one`): This question is to determine if you have received training on our expense policies. Choose one of the options: Yes, No, or Not sure.
* **Have you read our expense policy handbook?** (`Have you read our expense policy handbook?`, `select_one`): This question is to assess your awareness of our expense policy handbook. Choose one of the options: Yes, No, or Not sure.
* **How often do you expense personal purchases?** (`How often do you expense personal purchases?`, `select_multiple`): This question is to gauge how frequently you expense personal purchases. Select all the options that apply: Never, Occasionally, Regularly, or Always.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improving our expense governance?** (`Do you have any suggestions for improving our expense governance?`, `text`): This field is for providing any suggestions or feedback you have on improving our expense governance processes.
* **Have you had any issues with our expense system?** (`Have you had any issues with our expense system?`, `select_multiple`): This question is to determine if you have encountered any problems with our expense system. Choose all the options that apply: Yes, or No.
* **Do you have any feedback about our expense policy?** (`Do you have any feedback about our expense policy?`, `text`): This field is for providing any feedback or comments you have about our expense policy.
* **Do you know where to report a concern?** (`Do you know where to report a concern?`, `select_one`): This question is to gauge your understanding of the reporting process. Choose one of the options: Yes, or No.
* **Do you know who to contact for questions or concerns?** (`Do you know who to contact for questions or concerns?`, `text`): This field is for indicating who you would contact for questions or concerns.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to tell us?** (`Is there anything else you'd like to tell us?`, `text`): This is the final section for providing any additional feedback or comments you have.
