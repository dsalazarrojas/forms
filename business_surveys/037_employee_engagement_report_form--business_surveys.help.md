# Employee Engagement Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Engagement Report Form is designed to collect information about employees, including their personal details, departmental and managerial relationships, and survey responses. This form will help the organization to better understand employee engagement and improve workplace experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, paying attention to each field's requirements.
2. Enter your employee name, department, job title, years of service, and contact information (email, phone number, and date of birth).
3. Select your department and engagement level from the provided options.
4. Provide honest and thoughtful responses to the survey questions for yourself, your manager, reporting director, and supervisor.
5. Submit the form when completed.
