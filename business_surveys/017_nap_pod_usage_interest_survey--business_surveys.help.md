# Nap Pod Usage Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nap Pod Usage Interest Survey is a tool to gather information on users' preferences and behavior related to the use of Nap Pods. The data collected will help us understand how often you use Nap Pods, your preferred time of day to use them, and your overall satisfaction with the service. We will use this information to improve and better serve you and other users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select the correct answer for each question.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the "Nap Pod Suggestions" field.
4. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How Often Do You Use Nap Pods** (`Nap Pod Frequency`, select_one): 
Choose how often you use Nap Pods. Select from the options:
	+ Never
	+ Less than once a month
	+ 1-2 times a month
	+ 1-2 times a week
	+ Daily
* **What Time Of Day Do You Prefer To Use Nap Pods** (`preferred_time_nap_pod_usage`, time):
	+ Please select the time of day you prefer to use Nap Pods. You can choose any time using the time format (e.g., 08:00, 12:00, etc.).
* **Nap Pod Location** (`nap_pod_location`, select_one): 
Choose where you prefer to use Nap Pods. Select from the options:
	+ On campus
	+ Near my office
	+ At home
	+ In my backyard
	+ In a park
* **How Do You Like The Nap Pods** (`nap_pod_feedback`, note):
	+ Please provide any feedback or comments about your experience with Nap Pods.
* **Do You Have Any Feedback or Suggestions** (`nap_pod_suggestions`, text):
	+ Please provide any suggestions or feedback you have about Nap Pods. This is an open-ended field for you to share any thoughts or ideas you may have.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* Your feedback and suggestions are highly valued and will help us improve the Nap Pod service.
