# Urban Housing Policy Impact Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to understand the effects of urban housing policies on individuals and communities in terms of their opinions, experiences, and perceptions. Your input will help policymakers create more effective policies that meet the needs of everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, taking your time to answer each question carefully.
2. Start with your name and email, which are required fields.
3. Answer the questions about your housing experiences and perceptions, including how long you live in the city, the effect of city policies, and your current housing status.
4. Express your opinions on the city's housing policies, including how much they affect you and what they impact you.
5. Finally, provide demographic information, including your organization or group affiliation, job title, phone number, and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your personal or professional email address.
* **How long do you live in the city** (`survey_start`, `number`, **required**): Answer this question to help us understand your connection to the city and its policies.
* **How much effect does the city policy have** (`policy_effect`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Rate the impact of the city's policies on a scale of 1-5.
* **What is your current housing status** (`housing_status`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose your current housing status (Renting, Home ownership, or Other).
* **What are the most pressing housing conditions in your neighborhood** (`housing_conditions`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Rate the most pressing housing conditions in your area (Poor maintenance, Unaffordable housing, Lack of affordable housing, or Other).
* **How likely are you to fill out this survey in the future** (`survey_finish`, `time`, **required**): Rate your likelihood of filling out this survey again.
* **How relevant is this survey to you** (`policy_relevance`, `number`, **required**): Rate the relevance of this survey to your personal experiences.
* **How much do you feel that this policy will affect you** (`policy_effect`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Rate your personal experience with the policy.
* **What do you think this policy will impact** (`policy_impact`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Rate the impact of the policy on your opinions.
* **Other policy impacts** (`other_impact`, `text`, **optional**): Share any other effects you feel the policy has on you.
* **How aware of this policy are you** (`policy_awareness`, `number`, **required**): Rate your awareness of the policy.
* **How much do you support this policy** (`policy_support`, `number`, **required**): Rate your support for the policy.
* **What is your opinion on this policy** (`policy_opinion`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose your opinion on the policy (Strongly Opposed, Neutral, Strongly in favor, or Other).
* **Would you recommend this policy** (`policy_recommend`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose whether you would recommend the policy (Strongly oppose, Neutral, Strongly recommend, or Other).
* **Do you have any comments on this policy** (`policy_comment`, `text`, **optional**): Share any additional thoughts or concerns about the policy.
* **How satisfied are you with this policy** (`policy_satisfaction`, `select_one`, **required**): Rate your satisfaction with the policy (Very dissatisfied, Somewhat dissatisfied, Neutral, Somewhat satisfied, or Very satisfied).
* **Any other comments on this policy** (`policy_impact_comment`, `text`, **optional**): Share any other thoughts or concerns about the policy.
* **Organization/Group you are part of** (`contact_organization`, `text`, **required**): Enter your current organization or group affiliation.
* **Job** (`contact_job`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your job title or position.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your contact email address.
* **Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your contact address.
* **City** (`contact_city`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your city or region.
