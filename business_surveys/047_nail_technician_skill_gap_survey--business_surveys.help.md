# Nail Technician Skill Gap Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help nail technicians identify their skill gaps and provide valuable feedback on areas where they need more training or support. Your input will be used to improve the quality of training and resources provided by our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please provide your title and years of experience in the nail industry.
2. Rate your confidence level in the skills listed below on a scale from 1 to 3 (High, Medium, Low).
3. Select the skills you have experience with.
4. Choose the areas where you need additional training or support.
5. Add any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nail Technician Title**: This field is where you can enter your official title or position in the nail industry.
* **Years Of Experience**: This field is where you can enter the number of years you have worked in the nail industry.
* **Confidence Level**:
	+ High: I am confident in my ability to perform the skill.
	+ Medium: I have some experience with the skill, but may need additional practice or training.
	+ Low: I have limited or no experience with the skill.
* **Training Needs**:
	+ Basic: I have some general knowledge, but need more training or hands-on experience.
	+ Intermediate: I have some practical experience, but may need more guidance or review.
	+ Advanced: I have extensive experience and can perform the skill with confidence.
	+ Expert: I have exceptional experience and can train others.
* **Skills Assessed**:
	+ You can select all the skills that you have experience with from the options below.
* **Experience Areas**:
	+ You can select all the areas where you have experience from the options below.
* **Additional Comments**: This is where you can add any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience or areas where you need more training or support.

Note: All fields are optional, and you can skip any sections that you don't feel are relevant to your experience.
