# Organizational Process Audit Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your organization's processes. It will help us understand how you work, what processes you follow, and what areas need improvement. Please answer these questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and answer the questions accordingly. If you're unsure about a question, feel free to ask for clarification. Please make sure to submit the form completely and accurately, as your input will help us understand your organization's processes better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Introduction
* **Page 1: Introduction** (Page 1, required: false) - This is just a brief introduction to the survey.

* **Contact Information** (Page 2, type: text, required: true) - Please enter your name, title, and email address here.
