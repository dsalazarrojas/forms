# Aircraft Specifications Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather detailed information about an aircraft's specifications and details for various business use cases, such as insurance, sale, or fleet planning. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the following fields:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent Name**: Your full name as the owner or representative of the aircraft.
* **Company Name**: Name of the organization that owns or operates the aircraft.
* **Email Address**: Contact email address for further communication.
* **Phone Number**: Contact number for follow-up questions or requests.
* **Survey Date**: Date of completion of the survey.
* **Aircraft Registration**: The tail number of your aircraft.
* **Aircraft Make**: The manufacturer of your aircraft.
* **Aircraft Model**: The model designation of your aircraft.
* **Aircraft Year**: The year of manufacture of your aircraft.
* **Serial Number**: The manufacturer's serial number of your aircraft.
* **Aircraft Category**: Select the type of aircraft you own (e.g., Single engine piston, Multi-engine piston, etc.).
* **Primary Use**: Select the main purpose of your aircraft (e.g., Corporate transport, Charter operations, etc.).
* **Total Airframe Hours**: Total time your aircraft has spent in service.
* **Maximum Seating Capacity**: The number of seats in your aircraft.
* **Typical Seating Configuration**: The usual number of passengers your aircraft can carry.
* **Engine Make**: The manufacturer of your aircraft's engine.
* **Engine Model**: The model of your aircraft's engine.
* **Number of Engines**: The total number of engines in your aircraft.
* **Engine Hours Since New**: The total time your aircraft's engines have spent in service.
* **Engine Hours Since Overhaul**: The time since your aircraft's engines were last overhauled.
* **Maximum Range (nautical miles)**: The maximum range of your aircraft in nautical miles.
* **Typical Range with Passengers**: The usual range of your aircraft with passengers.
* **Typical Cruise Speed (knots)**: The speed at which your aircraft usually cruises.
* **Maximum Operating Altitude**: The highest altitude your aircraft can operate at.
* **Fuel Capacity (gallons)**: The total fuel capacity of your aircraft.
* **Baggage Capacity (cubic feet)**: The volume of cargo your aircraft can carry.
* **Maximum Takeoff Weight (lbs)**: The maximum weight of your aircraft when taking off.
* **Avionics Package**: The avionics system installed in your aircraft.
* **Autopilot System**: The type of autopilot system installed in your aircraft.
* **Weather Radar**: Whether your aircraft is equipped with a weather radar system.
* **TCAS (Traffic Collision Avoidance)**: Whether your aircraft is equipped with a TCAS system.
* **EGPWS (Terrain Awareness)**: Whether your aircraft is equipped with an EGPWS system.
* **ADS-B Out**: Whether your aircraft is equipped with an ADS-B Out system.
* **WiFi Installed**: Whether your aircraft has WiFi installed.
* **Cabin Type**: The configuration of your aircraft's cabin.
* **Interior Condition**: Select the overall condition of your aircraft's interior.
* **Exterior Condition**: Select the overall condition of your aircraft's exterior.
* **Last Paint Year**: The year your aircraft was last painted.
* **Maintenance Program**: Select the current maintenance program your aircraft is part of.
* **Primary Hangar Location**: Where your aircraft is currently based.
* **Additional Equipment**: List any additional notable equipment installed in your aircraft.
* **Known Deficiencies**: List any known issues or deficiencies in your aircraft.
* **Estimated Market Value (USD)**: Select the estimated market value of your aircraft.
* **Survey Purpose**: Select the reason why you are completing this survey.
