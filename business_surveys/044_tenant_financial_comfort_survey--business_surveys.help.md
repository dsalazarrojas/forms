# Tenant Financial Comfort Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Financial Comfort Survey is designed to gather information about your experience and perception of your financial comfort and satisfaction with your rental property. Your feedback will help us understand your needs and make informed decisions to improve the property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page for a brief overview of the form.
2. Answer the questions on each page by selecting the corresponding options or entering your responses.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by *).
4. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, optional): This page provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **Rent Affordability** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select from the options how you perceive the rent affordability of this property:
	+ Highly Affordable
	+ Somewhat Affordable
	+ Not Very Affordable
* **Financial Comfort** (`3`, `select_one`, required): Please indicate your level of financial comfort with this property:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Tenant Satisfaction** (`4`, `date`, optional): If you're comfortable sharing, please enter the date of your move-in or lease start date.
* **Contact Info** (`5`, `text`, optional): Please enter any contact information you'd like to provide for follow-up communication.
* **Landlord Contact Info** (`6`, `text`, optional): Please enter any contact information for your landlord or property manager.
* **Property Info** (`7`, `text`, optional): If you have any information about the property that you'd like to share (e.g., unit number, building name), please enter it here.
