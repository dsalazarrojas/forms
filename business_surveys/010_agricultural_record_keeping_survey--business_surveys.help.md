# Agricultural Record Keeping Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agricultural Record Keeping Survey aims to gather information from agricultural professionals about their record keeping practices, challenges, and preferences for record keeping tools and training. This survey will help inform the development of more effective record keeping solutions that cater to the needs and challenges of the agricultural community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name and contact information (optional).
2. Select your farm size in hectares (approximate area).
3. Indicate the type(s) of records you keep (e.g., planting dates, harvest volumes, etc.).
4. Choose how you currently keep your records (e.g., paper, spreadsheet, farm management software, etc.).
5. Specify how often you update your records (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, etc.).
6. Share any challenges you face with record keeping (e.g., time, cost, literacy, etc.).
7. Indicate whether you would be interested in training on record keeping.
8. Choose the record keeping tools you prefer to use (e.g., mobile app, paper templates, etc.).
9. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about record keeping.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name** (1): This field is for your name and contact information. You can leave it blank if you prefer to respond anonymously.
* **Farm Size (hectares)** (2): Please estimate the area of your farm.
* **Record types kept** (3): Select all the types of records you maintain, such as planting dates, harvest volumes, etc.
* **Record keeping format** (4): Choose how you currently keep your records, like paper, spreadsheet, or farm management software.
* **Frequency of record updates** (5): Specify how often you update your records.
* **Main challenges with record keeping** (6): Share any challenges you face with keeping records, such as time, costs, literacy, or technology barriers.
* **Interest in record keeping training** (7): Indicate whether you would be interested in training on record keeping practices.
* **Preferred record keeping tools** (8): Select the tools you prefer to use for record keeping, like mobile app, paper templates, etc.
* **Additional comments** (9): Provide any other feedback or comments about your record keeping practices.
