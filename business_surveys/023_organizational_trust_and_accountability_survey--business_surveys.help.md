# Organizational Trust And Accountability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Trust And Accountability Survey is designed to collect feedback from employees about their perceptions of trust and accountability within their organization. The survey aims to gather information on various aspects of employee relationships, such as trust, transparency, communication, and reporting. This data will help management understand the current state of organizational culture and make informed decisions to improve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read through each page carefully to understand the questions and options.
* Select all relevant options for each question, as each page allows select_multiple, select_one, or other types of input.
* Enter relevant information in fields that require text or numerical input, such as email addresses and feedback.
* Make sure to save your progress before leaving the page or submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1)
	+ This page serves as an introduction to the survey, providing context and explaining its purpose.
* **Trust** (Page 2)
	+ Select all options that apply to your perception of trust within the organization:
		- Option 1: You feel trusted and supported by your colleagues and superiors.
		- Option 2: You feel some level of trust, but there are instances where you feel uncertain or unsupported.
		- Option 3: You do not feel trusted or supported by your colleagues and superiors.
* **Transparency** (Page 3)
	+ Enter the date of your last transparent communication with your superior or HR representative.
* **Accountability** (Page 4)
	+ Estimate the time you spend on tasks that require accountability and transparency.
* **Communication** (Page 5)
	+ Select the option that best describes your communication style with your colleagues:
		- Option 1: You communicate regularly and openly with your colleagues.
		- Option 2: You communicate occasionally, but not as frequently as you'd like.
		- Option 3: You rarely communicate with your colleagues.
* **Reporting** (Page 6)
	+ Enter your email address for reporting concerns or issues to your superiors or HR representative.
* **Feedback** (Page 7)
	+ Rate your experience with feedback mechanisms within the organization:
		- This field is a number input, where you can rate your experience on a scale.
* **Suggestions** (Page 8)
	+ Rate your experience with suggestions and ideas submitted to the organization:
		- This field is a number input, where you can rate your experience on a scale.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact your HR representative or superior for guidance.
