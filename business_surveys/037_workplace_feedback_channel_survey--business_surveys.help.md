# workplace_feedback_channel_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the usage and effectiveness of employee feedback channels within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one of the options for each question, as applicable.
2. Provide any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What feedback channel do you use for employee feedback** (`employee_feedback_channel`): Select the channel you use to provide feedback to your colleagues, such as "Yes" for using a specific feedback portal or "No" for not using any particular channel.
* **Management level** (`management_level`): Choose the level of management you interact with, such as "High" for senior management or "Low" for junior management.
* **Department** (`department`): Select any departments you work with or have interacted with in the past. This will help us understand the breadth of your feedback interactions.
* **How often do you use this feedback channel** (`frequency_of_use`): Indicate how often you use the chosen feedback channel, such as "Yes" for regular use or "No" for infrequent use.
* **What is your communication style** (`communication_style`): Choose the type of communication style you prefer, such as "Yes" for formal or "No" for informal.
* **Satisfaction level** (`satisfaction_level`): Express your satisfaction level with the current feedback channel, like "Satisfied" for positive feedback or "Dissatisfied" for negative feedback.
* **What type of communication would you like to have in this channel** (`communication_type`): Select the type of communication you would like to have in the feedback channel, such as "Yes" for one-to-one or "No" for group communication.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`): If you have any other comments or suggestions regarding the feedback channel, please enter them here. This is an optional field.
