# equity_and_international_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about participants' backgrounds, experiences, and perceptions in relation to equity and international topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in each section with the requested information about the participant.
2. Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out the form.
3. If you are unsure about any of the questions, refer to the field-by-field explanations below for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Background
* **Background** (`background`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the participant's background information.

### Experience
* **Your Experience** (`experience`, `text`, optional): Describe the participant's experience related to equity and international topics.

### International Context
* **International Context** (`international_context`, `text`, optional): Explain the participant's international experience and how it relates to the equity topic.

### Perception
* **Perception** (`perception`, `text`, optional): Describe how the participant perceives the equity topic.

### Organization
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, optional): Provide the participant's current or past workplace organization.

### Institution
* **Institution** (`institution`, `text`, optional): List the participant's current or past institution.

### Analysis
* **Analysis** (`analysis`, `text`, optional): Summarize the participant's analysis on the equity topic.

### Data Sources
* **Data Sources** (`data_sources`, `text`, optional): List any relevant data sources the participant has used for their analysis.

### Research NGO/Institutional
* **Research NGO/Institutional** (`research_ngo_institutional`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether the participant is doing research in a NGO, institution, or something else.
*   Research
*   NGO
*   Institution

### Research Other
* **Other** (`research_ngo_institutional_other`, `select_multiple`, optional): If not doing research in a NGO or institution, choose all that apply.
*   Research
*   NGO
*   Institution

### Participant ID
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, `text`, optional): Provide a unique participant ID.

### Participant Type
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the participant's type.
*   Individual
*   Representative
*   Organization

### Participant Location
* **Participant Location** (`participant_location`, `text`, optional): Describe the participant's location.

### Participant Organization
* **Participant Organization** (`participant_organization`, `text`, optional): Provide the participant's current or past workplace organization.

### Participant Job
* **Participant Job** (`participant_job`, `text`, optional): Describe the participant's job.

### Participant Language
* **Participant Language** (`participant_language`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all languages spoken by the participant.
*   English
*   French
*   Spanish

### Participant Country
* **Participant Country** (`participant_country`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the participant's country of residence.
*   United States
*   Canada
*   Mexico

### Participant Gender
* **Participant Gender** (`participant_gender`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the participant's gender.
*   Male
*   Female
*   Other

### Participant Educational Background
* **Participant Educational Background** (`participant_educational_background`, `text`, optional): Describe the participant's educational background.

### Participant Job Title
* **Participant Job Title** (`participant_job_title`, `text`, optional): Describe the participant's job title.

### Participant Job Type
* **Participant Job Type** (`participant_job_type`, `text`, optional): Describe the participant's job type.

### Participant Job Sector
* **Participant Job Sector** (`participant_job_sector`, `text`, optional): Describe the participant's job sector.

### Participant Job Employer
* **Participant Job Employer** (`participant_job_employer`, `text`, optional): Provide the participant's job employer.

### Participant Job Salary
* **Participant Job Salary** (`participant_job_salary`, `number`, optional): Enter the participant's current job salary.

## Tips

* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, refer to the field-by-field explanations above for clarification.
* Make sure to save your progress frequently to avoid losing your work.
