# Business Support Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Business Support Satisfaction Poll is a survey designed to help us understand your experience with our support services. Your feedback will help us improve and refine our services to better meet the needs of businesses like yours.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each field carefully, making sure to provide as much detail as possible.
2. If a field does not apply to your experience, please select "Does not apply" or "Not relevant" where relevant.
3. You may select multiple options for "Select all that apply" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (Required): Please enter your business name as it appears on your business cards or other official documents.
* **Contact Person Name** (Required): Please enter the name of the person representing your business who is filling out this form.
* **Contact Email Address** (Required): Please enter your business email address or that of the representative filling out this form.
* **Phone Number** (Optional): If you would like to provide a phone number, please do so. This will help us contact you in case we need additional information.
* **Business Size** (Required): Please select the number of employees in your business.
	+ Solo entrepreneur: A business with only one owner/operator.
	+ 2-5 employees: A business with 2-5 employees.
	+ 6-10 employees: A business with 6-10 employees.
	+ 11-50 employees: A business with 11-50 employees.
	+ 51-100 employees: A business with 51-100 employees.
	+ 100+ employees: A business with over 100 employees.
* **What support services have you used?** (Required): Select all support services that apply to your business.
	+ Business consulting: We provided business consulting services.
	+ Financing or loans: We provided financing or loan services.
	+ Marketing assistance: We provided marketing assistance.
	+ Training programs: We provided training programs.
	+ Mentoring: We provided mentoring services.
	+ Technology support: We provided technology support.
	+ Networking events: We hosted networking events.
	+ Legal services: We provided legal services.
