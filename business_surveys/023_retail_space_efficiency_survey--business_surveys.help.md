# Retail Space Efficiency Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Retail Space Efficiency Survey is a tool used to measure the overall efficiency and effectiveness of your retail store in terms of store layout, store hours, space usage, store performance, customer experience, employee performance, and other key aspects. This survey aims to help you identify areas of improvement and opportunities for growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the store information: Provide the store's ID, name, address, city, country, store manager's contact information, and store manager's job title.
2. Answer the store layout question: Indicate whether your store layout is efficient or not.
3. Report on store hours: Enter your store's operational hours.
4. Evaluate space usage: Choose whether you use your space effectively or not.
5. Rate store performance: Provide a numerical rating of your store's overall performance.
6. Share customer experiences: Provide a text description of your customer experiences.
7. Evaluate employee performance: Rate your employees' performance using numbers.
8. Share employee experiences: Provide a text description of your employee experiences.
9. Identify improvements: Check any areas where you feel your store's space usage can be improved.
10. Evaluate store performance improvement: Choose whether you think your store's performance can be improved.
11. Evaluate customer experiences: Select whether you think customer experiences can be improved.
12. Identify manager improvements: Check any areas where you feel your store manager's performance can be improved.
13. Rate manager satisfaction: Provide a numerical rating of your store manager's satisfaction.
14. Confirm store manager's job title: Select whether your store manager is effective in their job.
14. Confirm manager's job satisfaction: Provide a numerical rating of your store manager's satisfaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store ID** (id: 15, required): Enter the ID number of the store.
* **Store Name** (id: 16, required): Enter the name of the store.
* **Store Address** (id: 17, required): Enter the address of the store.
* **City** (id: 18, required): Enter the city where the store is located.
* **Country** (id: 19, required): Enter the country where the store is located.
* **Store Manager** (id: 20, required): Enter the name of the store manager.
* **Store Manager Phone** (id: 21, required): Enter the phone number of the store manager.
* **Store Manager Email** (id: 22, required): Enter the email address of the store manager.
* **Store Manager Job** (id: 23, required): Select whether your store manager is effective in their job.
* **Store Performance** (id: 13, required): Rate the overall performance of your store.
* **Store Layout** (id: 1, optional): Indicate whether your store layout is efficient or not.
* **Store Hours** (id: 2, required): Enter your store's operational hours.
* **Space Usage** (id: 3, required): Choose whether you use your space effectively or not.
* **Customer Experience** (id: 5, optional): Provide a text description of your customer experiences.
* **Employee Performance** (id: 6, optional): Rate your employees' performance using numbers.
* **Employee Experience** (id: 7, optional): Provide a text description of your employee experiences.
* **Store Layout Improvement** (id: 8, optional): Provide any suggestions on how to improve your store's space usage.
* **Space Usage Improvement** (id: 9, optional): Check any areas where you feel your space usage can be improved.
* **Employee Improvement** (id: 10, optional): Check any areas where you feel your employees can be improved.
* **Customer Improvement** (id: 12, optional): Select whether you think customer experiences can be improved.
* **Manager Improvement** (id: 24, optional): Check any areas where you feel your store manager can be improved.
* **Manager Satisfaction** (id: 25, required): Rate your store manager's satisfaction using numbers.
* **Store Performance Improvement** (id: 11, optional): Choose whether you think your store's performance can be improved.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (id: 13, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with your store.

Tips:

* Please provide honest and accurate information to help us identify areas of improvement.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please use the "Store Layout Improvement", "Space Usage Improvement", "Employee Improvement", "Customer Improvement", "Manager Improvement" fields.
* Please provide numerical ratings for fields that have numerical options.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [contact email or phone number].
