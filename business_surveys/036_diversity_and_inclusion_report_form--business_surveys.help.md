# Diversity and Inclusion Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diversity and Inclusion Report Form is designed to gather information about your experiences and perspectives on diversity and inclusion in the workplace. This form helps to identify areas for improvement and opportunities for growth in creating an inclusive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
2. Select all relevant options for the Demographic Information and Inclusion Practices sections.
3. Enter your comments and concerns in the Open-Ended section.
4. Provide your employee information, manager's name and email, and signature.
5. Enter the date and time you completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required: false): Your organization's name.
* **Demographic Information** (`demographic_info`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all relevant options that describe your demographic characteristics, such as Asian, Black or African American, White or Caucasian, etc.
* **Inclusion Practices** (`practices`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all relevant options that describe the inclusion practices your organization has, such as flexible work hours, remote work options, etc.
* **Experiences** (`experiences`, text, required: false): Describe any experiences you have had or have seen in the workplace related to diversity and inclusion.
* **Concerns** (`concerns`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all relevant options that describe any concerns or issues you have with diversity and inclusion in the workplace, such as microaggression, inadequate representation, etc.
* **Open-Ended** (`open_ended`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or concerns you have about diversity and inclusion in the workplace.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): Sign your name, as proof of completion.
* **Employee Info** (`employee_info`, number, required: false): Enter your employee number, which can be used to identify your role or position in the organization.
* **Manager Info** (`manager_info`, number, required: false): Enter your manager's name or ID, which can be used to identify your direct supervisor.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you completed this form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about this form or the diversity and inclusion initiatives in your organization.

Please note that all fields are optional, and you should only answer questions that are relevant to your experiences and perspectives. Thank you for your input!
