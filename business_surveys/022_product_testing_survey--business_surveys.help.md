# Product Testing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on a product, allowing us to understand your overall impressions, quality perception, and willingness to recommend it to others.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Be as specific as possible when answering questions.
3. For questions with options, select only the options that apply to your experience.
4. If a question does not apply, please indicate that it does not apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Impressions (1)**: Share your honest feedback on what you think about the product.
* **Product Quality (2)**: Rate the product's quality on a scale of 1-5, with 5 being excellent.
* **Recommendation (3)**: Indicate how likely you are to recommend the product on a scale of 1-5, with 5 being very likely.
* **Favorite Feature (4)**: Specify the feature of the product that you like most.
* **Least Liked Feature (5)**: Specify the feature of the product that you like least.
* **Usage Frequency (6)**: Select how often you use the product (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Never).
* **New Features (7)**: Check all the options that you would like to see added to the product:
	+ Yes, add more features like this
	+ Yes, add features that are similar to this
	+ Yes, add features that are totally different from this
	+ No, do not recommend any new features
* **Expectations Met (8)**: Check all the options that describe how the product meets your expectations:
	+ Yes, it exceeded my expectations
	+ Yes, it met my expectations
	+ No, it was below my expectations
	+ No, I'm not sure
* **Price (9)**: Specify what you think of the product's price.
* **Improvements (10)**: Suggest improvements to the product, if any.
* **Comments (11)**: Share any other comments or suggestions you have for the product.
* **Additional Comments (12)**: Share any other comments or suggestions you have for the product.
