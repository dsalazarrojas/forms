# Vendor Payment Cycle Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Payment Cycle Survey is used to collect information about the payment cycle of vendors. This includes the frequency of payments, payment terms, and other relevant details. The purpose of this form is to gather accurate and comprehensive data to improve vendor management and payment processes within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Vendor Payment Cycle Survey, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each question and its relevance to your company's vendor payment cycle.
2. Provide accurate and honest answers to each question, based on your company's current practices and policies.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed, as indicated by the "required" label.
4. If you have any issues or questions while completing the form, contact the designated person for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your company's name? (`question_1`, text, optional):** Please enter your company's name as it appears on public records.
* **question 2 (`question_2`, select_one, optional):** Please select one of the options that best describes your company's payment cycle frequency.
* **question 3 (`question_3`, select_multiple, optional):** Select all the options that apply to your company's payment cycle frequency.
* **question 4 (`question_4`, date, optional):** Please enter the date of the last payment cycle.
* **question 5 (`question_5`, time, optional):** Please enter the time of the last payment cycle.
* **question 6 (`question_6`, note, optional):** This field is a text box for any additional information or comments about the payment cycle.
* **Vendor email (`question_7`, email, optional):** Please enter the email address associated with your company's payment information.
* **Additional comments (`question_8`, text, optional):** Please provide any additional comments or questions you have about the payment cycle.
* **Frequency of payment (`question_9`, number, optional):** Please enter the frequency of payments made to vendors in the last 12 months.
* **Total payments made (`question_10`, number, optional):** Please enter the total number of payments made in the last 12 months.
* **Average payment amount (`question_11`, number, decimal, optional):** Please enter the average payment amount made to vendors in the last 12 months.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact the designated person for guidance.
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please report them to the designated person.
