# Employee Collaboration Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess employee satisfaction with collaboration within the company, helping us to identify areas of improvement and enhance teamwork and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Please review each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Fill in the required information for all fields marked as "required".
3. You may leave blank any field that is not relevant to your experience.
4. Click submit to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **teamwork_satisfaction (Required)**: This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the collaboration experience. Please select one of the three options: Teamwork is good, Teamwork is bad, or Teamwork is average.
* **communication_satisfaction (Required)**: This is an open-ended question where you can share your thoughts about the effectiveness of communication within the team. Please type in your feedback.
* **feedback (Required)**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional feedback about your experience.
* **email (Optional)**: Please enter your email address so that we can follow up with you if needed.
* **phone (Optional)**: Please enter your phone number so that we can reach you if necessary.
* **department (Required)**: Select your department from the list (Sales, Marketing, HR, or IT) so we can better understand your feedback in relation to your specific team.
* **manager_satisfaction (Required)**: This question asks about your overall satisfaction with your manager's support and responsiveness. Please select one of the three options: Manager is supportive, Manager is unresponsive, or Manager is average.
* **employee_collaboration_satisfaction_survey**: This is an internal field for our use and does not affect your feedback. Please ignore this field.
