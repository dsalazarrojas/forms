# Chief Security Officer Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chief Security Officer (CSO) feedback form is designed to gather qualitative feedback on the performance and leadership of the CSO within an organization. This form is intended to be used by various stakeholders, including board members, executives, managers, and team members, to provide their input on the CSO's effectiveness in key areas of security management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are:
	* Feedback date
	* Feedback provider name
	* CSO name being evaluated
	* Your role in the organization
	* CSO tenure duration
2. Rate the CSO's performance in each of the select_one fields, using the scales provided. These include:
	* Effectiveness of security strategy development
	* Incident response and crisis management
	* Risk assessment and mitigation effectiveness
	* Compliance and governance implementation
	* Security team leadership and development
	* Communication with stakeholders and management
	* Budget and resource management
	* Technical expertise and industry knowledge
	* Strategic security vision alignment with business
3. Provide specific feedback on the CSO's major accomplishments and areas requiring improvement or development.
4. Rate the overall organizational security posture improvement and level of trust and confidence in the CSO.
5. Provide an overall recommendation for the CSO, and finally,
6. Add any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback date** (`feedback_date`): \* The date the feedback is being provided on.
* **Feedback provider name** (`feedback_provider_name`): \* The name of the person providing the feedback.
* **CSO name being evaluated** (`cso_name`): \* The name of the Chief Security Officer being evaluated.
* **Your role in the organization** (`feedback_provider_role`): \* Your position within the organization, such as board member, executive, manager, or team member.
* **CSO tenure duration** (`tenure_duration`): \* The length of time the CSO has been in their role.
* **Effectiveness of security strategy development** (`security_strategy`): Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) the CSO's ability to develop and implement effective security strategies.
* **Incident response and crisis management** (`incident_response`): Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) the CSO's performance in managing incidents and crises.
* **Risk assessment and mitigation effectiveness** (`risk_management`): Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) the CSO's ability to assess and mitigate risks.
* **Compliance and governance implementation** (`compliance_governance`): Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) the CSO's performance in implementing compliance and governance measures.
* **Security team leadership and development** (`team_leadership`): Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) the CSO's leadership of the security team.
* **Communication with stakeholders and management** (`stakeholder_communication`): Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) the CSO's ability to communicate with stakeholders and management.
* **Budget and resource management** (`budget_management`): Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) the CSO's performance in managing the budget and resources.
* **Technical expertise and industry knowledge** (`technology_expertise`): Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) the CSO's technical expertise and industry knowledge.
* **Strategic security vision alignment with business** (`strategic_vision`): Rate from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) the CSO's strategic security vision alignment with the business.
* **Major accomplishments and achievements** (`major_accomplishments`): Provide specific feedback on the CSO's major accomplishments and achievements.
* **Areas requiring improvement or development** (`areas_for_improvement`): Provide specific feedback on areas that the CSO needs to improve or develop.
* **Overall organizational security posture improvement** (`security_posture`): Rate from Significantly improved to Declined the overall organizational security posture improvement.
* **Level of trust and confidence in CSO** (`trust_confidence`): Rate from Low to Very strong the level of trust and confidence in the CSO.
* **Overall recommendation** (`recommendation`): Provide an overall recommendation for the CSO, based on your feedback.
* **Additional comments or suggestions** (`additional_comments`): Add any additional comments or suggestions for the CSO's performance and development.

## Tips
* Be honest and constructive in your feedback, providing specific examples and suggestions whenever possible.
* Ensure that you complete the form based on your genuine experience and opinion.
* Please respect the CSO's confidentiality and do not provide any sensitive or confidential information.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to the field explanation above or consult with your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
