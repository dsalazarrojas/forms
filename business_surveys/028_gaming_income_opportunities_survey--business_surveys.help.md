# Gaming Income Opportunities Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your gaming habits and income opportunities. Your input will help us better understand the gaming industry's potential for monetization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:
1. Review each page carefully, and ensure you're comfortable with the information you provide.
2. Choose your answers based on your personal experiences and habits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the survey**: This page is just an introduction to the form and doesn't require a response from you.
* **What is your income level?**: Select the option that best describes your current income level related to gaming.
	+ Often: You earn money from gaming frequently.
	+ Sometimes: You earn money from gaming occasionally.
	+ Rarely: You rarely earn money from gaming.
* **How much do you earn from gaming?**: If you earn money from gaming, please enter the approximate amount you earn. If you don't earn money from gaming, please enter 0.
* **Why do you earn money through gaming?**: Select all the motivations that apply to why you earn money from gaming.
	+ Motivation 1: Not specified in the YAML, but potentially a specific type of income (e.g., tournaments, sponsorships, etc.)
	+ Motivation 2: Not specified in the YAML, but potentially another reason for earning money from gaming (e.g., subscriptions, donations, etc.)
	+ Motivation 3: Not specified in the YAML, but potentially yet another reason for earning money from gaming (e.g., YouTube AdSense, etc.)
* **Do you receive additional income from gaming?**: Check this option if you earn money from gaming beyond what was previously mentioned.
* **How much time do you spend playing games?**: Enter the approximate amount of time you spend playing games per week.
* **How often do you play games?**: Select how frequently you play games.
	+ Often: You play games frequently.
	+ Sometimes: You play games occasionally.
	+ Rarely: You rarely play games.
* **What gaming communities do you belong to?**: Select all the communities you're a part of, as they might be relevant to your income opportunities.
* **Do you have gaming partners?**: Check this option if you have other gamers with whom you play games.
* **How did you hear about this survey?**: Select all the methods you were introduced to the survey.
	+ Yes: You answered "yes" to the previous question.
	+ No: You answered "no" to the previous question.
	+ Other: You were introduced to the survey through another means.
* **How often do you stream games?**: Select how frequently you stream games.
	+ Often: You stream games frequently.
	+ Sometimes: You stream games occasionally.
	+ Rarely: You rarely stream games.
* **Which streaming platforms do you use?**: Select all the platforms you use for streaming.
* **Do you have a tag on your stream?**: Check this option if you have a tag on your stream.
Note: This help guide is a general guide for end-users. The specific fields and their labels might be subject to change based on your feedback and the form's actual content. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out.
