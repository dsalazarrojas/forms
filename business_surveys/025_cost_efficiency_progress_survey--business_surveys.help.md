# Cost Efficiency Progress Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cost Efficiency Progress Survey is a form used to track and record the progress of cost efficiency initiatives within a company. It helps to identify areas where the company is doing well and areas where it needs improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form is divided into several pages with multiple fields on each page.
2. Each page is labeled with a title and has fields for you to fill out.
3. You can scroll through each page to fill out the required and optional fields.
4. If you are unsure about a particular field, please refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Progress** (`cost_efficiency_progress_survey_page`, text, required/optional): This field is used to track the current progress of your cost efficiency initiative. Please select one of the provided options (High, Low, Medium) to indicate the status of your project.
* **Additional** (`cost_efficiency_progress_survey_page_2`, text, required/optional): This field is for any additional comments or information about your project that you feel is important to note.
* **Cost** (`cost_efficiency_progress_survey_page_3`, text, required/optional): This field is used to record any cost-related information about your project.
* **Progress** (`cost_efficiency_progress_survey_page_4`, text, required/optional): Similar to the first Progress field, this field is also used to track the current progress of your project.
* **Contact** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): If you need to contact someone related to your project, please fill out this field with their name and any other relevant information.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Please fill out this field with your name, which will help us identify who filled out the form.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): If you feel it's necessary, please fill out this field with your job title to provide more context about who you are.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field allows you to select multiple departments that are relevant to your project.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Please fill out this field with your work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): If you need to provide a phone number, please fill out this field.
* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, text, required/optional): Similar to the Job Title field, this field allows you to provide more context about who you are.
* **Job Title 3** (`job_title_3`, text, required/optional): Similar to Job Title 2, this field is another opportunity to provide more context about who you are.
* **Job Title 4** (`job_title_4`, text, required/optional): Same as the previous Job Title fields, use this field to provide more context.
* **Cost Efficiency** (`cost_efficiency`, number, required/optional): This field is used to record a numerical value about cost efficiency.
* **Cost Efficiency 2** (`cost_efficiency_2`, number, required/optional): Similar to the Cost Efficiency field, this field is used to record another numerical value about cost efficiency.
* **Cost Efficiency 3** (`cost_efficiency_3`, number, required/optional): As with the previous Cost Efficiency fields, this field is used to record a further numerical value about cost efficiency.
* **Cost Efficiency 4** (`cost_efficiency_4`, number, required/optional): Same as the previous Cost Efficiency fields, record a numerical value about cost efficiency.
* **Progress** (`progress`, select_one, required/optional): This field, like the first Progress field, is used to select one of the options (High, Low, Medium) to indicate the status of your project.
* **Progress 2** (`progress_2`, select_one, required/optional): Similar to the Progress field, select one of the options (High, Low, Medium) to indicate the status of your project.
* **Progress 3** (`progress_3`, select_one, required/optional): As with the Progress fields, select one of the options (High, Low, Medium) to indicate the status of your project.
* **Progress 4** (`progress_4`, select_one, required/optional): Same as the Progress fields, select one of the options (High, Low, Medium) to indicate the status of your project.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): If you need to record a date, please use the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): If you need to record a time, use the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Date 2** (`date_2`, date, required/optional): This field, like the Date field, is used to record a date in the format MM/DD/YY.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* If you are unsure about a particular field, please refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for more information.
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
