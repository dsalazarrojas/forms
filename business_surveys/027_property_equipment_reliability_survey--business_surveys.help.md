# Property Equipment Reliability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Equipment Reliability Survey is designed to collect information on the condition and maintenance status of various property equipment. This form helps users to track and evaluate the reliability of equipment, identify areas for improvement, and ensure that maintenance schedules are up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the equipment you are surveying to ensure you have the necessary information.
2. Select the correct answers for each field based on your knowledge of the equipment.
3. Fill in any necessary dates in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Property Information (1)
* **Property Information** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_1`, `text`, Optional): Describe the property or location where the equipment is installed.

### Property Equipment Information (2)
* **Property Equipment Information** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_2`, `text`, Optional): Describe the equipment in question, including its purpose and location within the property.

### Is it currently functioning normally? (3)
* **Is it currently functioning normally?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_3`, `select_one`, Required): Check whether the equipment is currently functioning as expected. Select "True" if it is, "False" otherwise.

### How would you rate the condition of the equipment? (4)
* **How would you rate the condition of the equipment?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_4`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select the condition of the equipment from the options:

    * Like New
    * Fair
    * Needs Repair

### What is the expected maintenance schedule for the equipment? (5)
* **What is the expected maintenance schedule for the equipment?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_5`, `date`, Optional): Enter the expected maintenance schedule for the equipment in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### What time is the equipment currently scheduled to be next maintained? (6)
* **What time is the equipment currently scheduled to be next maintained?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_6`, `time`, Optional): Enter the scheduled maintenance time in the format `HH:MM`.

### Is the equipment currently in use? (7)
* **Is the equipment currently in use?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_7`, `select_one`, Required): Check whether the equipment is currently in use. Select "True" if it is, "False" otherwise.

### Is there anything that would prevent you from maintaining the equipment? (8)
* **Is there anything that would prevent you from maintaining the equipment?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_8`, `text`, Optional): Describe any issues or constraints that would prevent or hinder maintenance of the equipment.

### How often do you typically maintain the equipment? (9)
* **How often do you typically maintain the equipment?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_9`, `select_one`, Optional): Select how often you maintain the equipment from the options:

    * Daily
    * Weekly
    * Monthly

### What is the equipment type? (10)
* **What is the equipment type?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_10`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select the type of equipment from the options:

    * Mechanical
    * Electrical
    * Hydraulic

### What is the age of the equipment? (11)
* **What is the age of the equipment?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_11`, `number`, Optional): Enter the age of the equipment in years.

### What is the purchase date of the equipment? (12)
* **What is the purchase date of the equipment?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_12`, `date`, Optional): Enter the purchase date of the equipment in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### Who installed the equipment? (13)
* **Who installed the equipment?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_13`, `select_one`, Optional): Select who installed the equipment from the options:

    * Property Maintenance
    * Vendor

### What is the model of the equipment? (14)
* **What is the model of the equipment?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_14`, `text`, Optional): Enter the model of the equipment.

### Is the equipment certified? (15)
* **Is the equipment certified?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_15`, `select_one`, Optional): Check whether the equipment is certified. Select "True" if it is, "False" otherwise.

### What is the serial number of the equipment? (16)
* **What is the serial number of the equipment?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_16`, `text`, Optional): Enter the serial number of the equipment.

### Is the equipment under warranty? (17)
* **Is the equipment under warranty?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_17`, `select_one`, Optional): Check whether the equipment is under warranty. Select "True" if it is, "False" otherwise.

### What is the warranty expiry date of the equipment? (18)
* **What is the warranty expiry date of the equipment?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_18`, `date`, Optional): Enter the warranty expiry date of the equipment in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### Is the equipment part of a critical system? (19)
* **Is the equipment part of a critical system?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_19`, `select_one`, Optional): Check whether the equipment is part of a critical system. Select "True" if it is, "False" otherwise.

### Has the equipment been replaced or modified? (20)
* **Has the equipment been replaced or modified?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_20`, `text`, Optional): Describe any replacements or modifications made to the equipment.

### What is the reason for replacement or modification? (21)
* **What is the reason for replacement or modification?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_21`, `text`, Optional): Explain the reason for any replacements or modifications made to the equipment.

### Is the equipment a standard piece of equipment? (22)
* **Is the equipment a standard piece of equipment?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_22`, `select_one`, Optional): Check whether the equipment is a standard piece of equipment. Select "True" if it is, "False" otherwise.

### Is the equipment part of a critical system? (23)
* **Is the equipment part of a critical system?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_23`, `select_one`, Optional): Check whether the equipment is part of a critical system. Select "True" if it is, "False" otherwise.

### Is the equipment a critical spare part? (24)
* **Is the equipment a critical spare part?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_24`, `select_one`, Optional): Check whether the equipment is a critical spare part. Select "True" if it is, "False" otherwise.

### Is the equipment a critical spare part or part of a critical system? (25)
* **Is the equipment a critical spare part or part of a critical system?** (`property_equipment_reliability_survey_25`, `select_one`, Optional): Check whether the equipment is a critical spare part or part of a critical system. Select "True" if it is, "False" otherwise.

## Tips
* Always complete this form for each piece of property equipment to ensure accurate and comprehensive data.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or maintenance team for guidance.
