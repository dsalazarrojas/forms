# Online Program Purchase Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with online programs purchased from our company. Please provide accurate and honest information to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

- To complete this form, please select the relevant answers to each question and provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Buyer Comment" field.
- Make sure to answer all required fields, marked with a star (\*), as they are essential for us to understand your feedback.
- If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact us for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Title** (`program_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the online program you purchased.
* **Program Provider** (`program_provider`, select multiple, optional): Select all providers that supplied your online program. For example, 'Yes' for all providers that provided your program.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you made the purchase. The date format should be in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" or "YYYY/MM/DD" for easier understanding, but any valid date format is accepted.
* **Purchase Time** (`purchase_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you made the purchase. Please format the time as "HH:MM AM/PM" or "HH:MM" for easier understanding.
* **Purchase Channel** (`purchase_channel`, select one, required): Choose how you made the purchase:
	+ online: Online purchase from our website or mobile application.
	+ offline: Offline purchase, such as in-store or by phone.
	+ social: Purchased through social media or other online platforms.
* **Buyer Email** (`buyer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you have one or if you'd like to be contacted for feedback or questions.
* **Buyer Phone** (`buyer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for feedback or questions.
* **Program Rating** (`program_rating`, number, optional): Rate the online program you purchased, from 1 (least satisfied) to 5 (most satisfied).
* **Program Value** (`program_value`, text, optional): Describe the value you gained from the online program.
* **Buyer Comment** (`buyer_comment`, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback about your experience with the online program.
* **Suggestion for Improvement** (`suggestion_for_improvement`, text, optional): If you have any ideas on how we can improve our services, please share them here.
* **Buyer Satisfaction** (`buyer_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the online program, from 1 (least satisfied) to 5 (most satisfied).

## Tips

- Please take a few minutes to provide as much detail as possible to help us improve our services.
- If you need help or clarification about any field, please contact us.
- The more information you provide, the better we can serve you and other customers in the future.
