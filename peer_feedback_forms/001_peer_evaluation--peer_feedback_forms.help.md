# peer_evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The peer evaluation form is a tool for users to assess and provide feedback to their peers. This form is meant for rating and commenting on the performance, behavior, and work quality of peers within the same group or team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the "Form Group" field to identify which group or team the peer belongs to.
2. In the "Rating" field, rate your peer's performance on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
3. In the "Comment" field, provide a brief comment on your peer's performance.
4. In the "Feedback" field, provide more in-depth feedback on areas of improvement.
5. In the "Peer Rating" field, rate your peer's performance on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
6. In the "Peer Comment" field, provide a brief comment on your peer's performance.
7. In the "Date" field, select the date you provided feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Group** (`form_group`, text, required: false): This field allows you to identify which group or team the peer belongs to. Please enter the name of the group or team.
- **Rating** (`rating`, number, required: false): This field is used to rate your peer's performance. Please rate on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
- **Comment** (`comment`, text, required: false): This field is for providing a brief comment on your peer's performance. Please keep your comment concise and professional.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): This field is for providing more in-depth feedback on areas of improvement. Please be specific and constructive with your feedback.
- **Peer Rating** (`peer_rating`, number, required: false): This field is used to rate your peer's performance. Please rate on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
- **Peer Comment** (`peer_comment`, text, required: false): This field is for providing a brief comment on your peer's performance. Please keep your comment concise and professional.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): This field is used to select the date you provided feedback. Please select the date you completed the form.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you're filling out the form for the correct peer and group.
- Please provide honest and constructive feedback.
- Please keep your comments and feedback concise and professional.
- Please note that this form should only be filled out once for each peer.
