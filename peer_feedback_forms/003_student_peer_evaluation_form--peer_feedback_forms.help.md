# Student Peer Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about their peers' performance, behavior, and overall attitude in academic settings. The feedback is intended to be used for peer growth and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your own student name in the `Student Name` field.
2. Select the evaluation time period you were observing your peer's performance for.
3. Evaluate your peer's performance using the `Rating Scale` field, selecting one of the provided options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Unresponsive.
4. Choose any relevant skills or attributes from the `Student Contribution` field, selecting multiple options as needed.
5. Select an overall evaluation for your peer using the `Overall Evaluation` field, choosing one of the provided options: Satisfactory, Needs Improvement, or Needs Significant Improvement.
6. Provide any additional feedback or comments in the `Feedback` field.
7. Choose how often you observed your peer's attendance using the `Student Attendance` field, selecting one of the provided options: Attends all sessions, Attends some sessions, Misses some sessions, or Rarely attends.
8. Select the skills or attributes you observed your peer's demeanor using the `Student Demeanor` field, choosing multiple options as needed.
9. Evaluate your peer's task completion rate using the `Student Work` field, selecting one of the provided options: Completes all tasks, Completes most tasks, Completes some tasks, or Rarely completes tasks.
10. (Optional) Select how you observed your peer's communication skills using the `Student Communication` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Please enter your own name to complete this form.
* **Evaluation Time**: Select the time period you were evaluating your peer's performance for (e.g., semester, quarter, or month).
* **Rating Scale**: Evaluate your peer's performance using the provided rating scale, selecting one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Unresponsive.
* **Student Contribution**: Choose any relevant skills or attributes that you observed your peer contributing to (e.g., Communication, Teamwork, Time Management, Leadership, Problem Solving, Adaptability, Creativity, Organization).
* **Overall Evaluation**: Select one of the provided options: Satisfactory, Needs Improvement, or Needs Significant Improvement.
* **Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about your peer's performance.
* **Student Attendance**: Choose how often you observed your peer's attendance using the provided options: Attends all sessions, Attends some sessions, Misses some sessions, or Rarely attends.
* **Student Demeanor**: Select any observed skills or attributes of your peer's demeanor (e.g., Punctual, Respectful, Positive attitude, Helpful, Cooperative, Independent, Flexible).
* **Student Work**: Evaluate your peer's task completion rate using the provided options: Completes all tasks, Completes most tasks, Completes some tasks, or Rarely completes tasks.
* **Student Communication**: (Optional) Select how you observed your peer's communication skills using the provided options (e.g., Good communication, Fair communication, Poor communication).

## Tips
* Be honest and constructive in your feedback, as this form is intended to help your peers grow and improve.
* Make sure to provide feedback for all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about the correct answer for a field, please ask your peer or instructor for guidance.
* Remember that this form is not intended to criticize or harm your peer's self-esteem.
