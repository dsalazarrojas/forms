# Submission Quality Peer Reviewer Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Submission Quality Peer Reviewer Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from peers on the quality of a submitted work. The form helps to gather constructive comments and ratings on various aspects of the submission, providing a comprehensive evaluation of its strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form with the required and optional fields. Each field is explained below.
*   Ensure that you are providing constructive and specific feedback to help the submission author improve their work.
*   Review your comments and ratings carefully before submitting the form to ensure they accurately reflect your evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Submission Quality Peer Reviewer Feedback Form**: This field is a text box where you can provide a general comment about the submission.
*   **Submission Title**: This field is for the title of the submitted work.
*   **Submission Author**: This field is for the author's name or name of the submitting team.
*   **Submission Year**: This field is for the year the submission was made.
*   **Submission Abstract**: This field is for a brief summary of the submission.
*   **Submission Quality**: This field is for a general comment about the submission's quality.
*   **Reviewer Feedback**: This field is for any additional feedback or comments you have about the submission.
*   **Recommended for Publication**: This field is for you to select 'Yes' or 'No' if you recommend the submission for publication. If you select 'Yes', you will also need to fill out the following fields for a more specific evaluation:
    *   **Submission Year Quality Peers Reviewer Feedback**: This field is for a comment on the submission's year-specific quality.
    *   **Submission Year Quality Peers Recommendation**: This field is for a comment on your recommendation for the submission for publication in the current year.
    *   **Submission Year Peers Rating**: This field is for a rating of the submission's quality on a scale of 1-10.
    *   **Submission Year Peers Quality Scale**: This field is for a comment on the submission's quality.
    *   **Submission Year Peers Recommendation**: This field is for a comment on your overall recommendation.
    *   **Submission Year Peers Comment**: This field is for any additional comments.
*   **Submission Quality Peers Rating**: This field is for a rating of the submission's quality on a scale of 1-10.
*   **Submission Quality Peers Quality Scale**: This field is for a comment on the submission's quality.
*   **Submission Quality Peers Recommendation**: This field is for a comment on your overall recommendation.
*   **Submission Quality Peers Comment**: This field is for any additional comments.
*   **Submission Quality Peers Rating Scale**: This field is for a rating of the submission's quality on a scale of 1-10.
*   **Submission Year Peers Quality Scale**: This field is for a comment on the submission's quality.
*   **Submission Year Peers Recommendation Scale**: This field is for a comment on your overall recommendation.
*   **Submission Year Peers Comment**: This field is for any additional comments.
*   **Submission Peers Rating**: This field is for a rating of the submission's quality on a scale of 1-10.
*   **Submission Peers Quality**: This field is for a comment on the submission's quality.
*   **Submission Peers Comment**: This field is for any additional comments.

Note to self: The form has a lot of fields! Be sure to focus on the most important ones when providing feedback.
