# Presentation Peer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect peer feedback on a presentation, providing an opportunity for observers to share their thoughts and suggestions on the content, delivery, and time management of the presentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a rating for the presentation using the rating scale provided (5-1).
2. Provide comments on the presentation's content, delivery, and time management.
3. Offer suggestions for improvement.
4. Share your overall feedback on the presentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Presenter ID (Presenter ID, number, optional)**: Enter the ID number of the presenter.
* **Rating (Rating, number, optional)**: Choose a number from 1 to 5 to rate the presentation.
* **Comments (Comments, text, optional)**: Share your thoughts about the presentation's content.
* **Suggestions (Suggestions, text, optional)**: Provide suggestions for improvement.
* **Feedback (Feedback, text, optional)**: Share your overall feedback on the presentation.
* **Rating Scale (Rating Scale, select_one, optional)**: Select a rating from 1 to 5 for the presentation.
* **Comments On Content (Comments On Content, text, optional)**: Share your thoughts about the presentation's content.
* **Comments On Delivery (Comments On Delivery, text, optional)**: Share your feedback on the presenter's delivery.
* **Comments On Participation (Comments On Participation, text, optional)**: Share your thoughts about the presenter's participation in the presentation.
* **Comments On Time Management (Comments On Time Management, text, optional)**: Share your feedback on the presenter's time management during the presentation.
