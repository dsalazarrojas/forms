# Presentation Feedback Template - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback on presentations given by presenters. It helps presenters improve their skills and provide a better experience for their audience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form thoroughly and honestly to provide constructive feedback.
2. Use the "Presenter Details" field to provide information about the presenter.
3. Rate each section of the presentation using the rating scale provided.
4. Provide specific comments and suggestions for improvement in the "Comments" and "Suggestions" fields.
5. Use the "Grading Rubric" fields to provide more detailed feedback on specific aspects of the presentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Presentation Feedback (1)**: A text box where you can provide your overall impression of the presentation.
  - Enter a brief description of your feedback on the presentation's content, structure, and overall impact.
  - Be as specific as possible and provide concrete examples.

- **Presenter Details (2)**: A field to provide information about the presenter.
  - Enter the name and title (if applicable) of the presenter.
  - Use this field to provide any other relevant details about the presenter, such as their affiliation or contact information.

- **Content Knowledge (3)**: A rating field to assess the presenter's knowledge of the subject matter.
  - Rate the presenter's knowledge on a scale of 0 to 10, where 0 is "no knowledge" and 10 is "excellent knowledge".

- **Organization (4)**: A rating field to assess the presenter's organization and structure of the presentation.
  - Rate the presenter's ability to organize and structure their content on a scale of 0 to 10.

- **Presentation Delivery (5)**: A rating field to assess the presenter's delivery of the presentation.
  - Rate the presenter's verbal and non-verbal communication skills on a scale of 0 to 10.

- **Engagement (6)**: A rating field to assess the presenter's ability to engage the audience.
  - Rate the presenter's ability to keep the audience interested and engaged on a scale of 0 to 10.

- **Overall Rating (7)**: A rating field to provide an overall assessment of the presentation.
  - Rate the presentation on a scale of 0 to 10, based on your overall impression.

- **Comments (8)**: A text box for providing specific comments on the presentation.
  - Enter any specific points you want to make about the presentation, such as areas of improvement or strengths.

- **Suggestions (9)**: A text box for providing suggestions for improvement.
  - Enter any suggestions you have for the presenter to improve their skills, such as additional resources or training.

- **Presenter Comments (10)**: A text box for the presenter to provide their own feedback on the feedback process.
  - Enter any thoughts or reactions they have to the feedback provided.

- **Feedback From (11)**: A field to identify who provided the feedback.
  - Enter your name and title (if applicable) to provide accountability and transparency.
