# Team Captain Leadership Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on a team captain's leadership style and skills from their team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully before starting to complete it.
2. Choose the leadership style that best describes the team captain's behavior.
3. Select the skills that you believe the team captain possesses.
4. Choose your level of agreement or disagreement with the team captain's leadership.
5. Add any additional comments you may have about the team captain's leadership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Captain Leadership Feedback Survey** (<page1>, <title>, not required): This page is the introduction page of the form.
* **leadership_style** (<page2, leadership_style, not required): Please select one or more of the options that best describe the team captain's behavior. Options include:
	+ Effective Communicator
	+ Strong Decider
	+ Positive Energy
* **Team Leadership Skills** (<page2, team_leadership_skills, not required): Please select one or more of the skills that you believe the team captain possesses. Options include:
	+ Strategic
	+ Organizational
	+ Problem-Solving
	+ Communication
	+ Motivation
	+ Emotional-Intelligence
	+ Time Management
* **Team Leadership Feedback** (<page2, team_leadership_feedback, not required): Please choose one or more of the options that best describe your level of agreement or disagreement with the team captain's leadership.
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Neither Agree Nor Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
* **Team Leadership Survey Comments** (<page3, team_leadership_survey_comments, not required): Please add any additional comments you may have about the team captain's leadership.

## Tips
- Please be honest and constructive in your feedback.
- Choose the options that best describe your opinion of the team captain's leadership.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your team lead or manager.
- Remember to fill out feedback forms for all team captains under your leadership to get a comprehensive picture of their leadership style and skills.
