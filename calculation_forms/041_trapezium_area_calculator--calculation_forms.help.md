# Trapezium Area Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trapezium Area Calculator is a tool to calculate the area of a trapezium. It takes in the lengths of the two parallel sides (A and B) and the height (h1) of the trapezium, and outputs the calculated area.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the length (A) of the shorter parallel side of the trapezium.
2. Enter the length (B) of the longer parallel side of the trapezium.
3. Enter the height (h1) of the trapezium.
4. Click the "Calculate" button to generate the result.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **A** (number, required): The length of the shorter parallel side of the trapezium.
* **B** (number, required): The length of the longer parallel side of the trapezium.
* **Height** (number, required): The height of the trapezium.
* **Result** (number, required): The calculated area of the trapezium.

## Tips
* Ensure you have accurately input the correct dimensions for the trapezium.
* Double-check the input values for A, B, and h1 before calculating the result.
* If the result is not as expected, re-check the input values and recalculate.
