# Triangle Area Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Triangle Area Calculator is a simple form used to calculate the area of a triangle given its base and height.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the base of the triangle in the "Base" field.
2. Enter the height of the triangle in the "Height" field.
3. The form will automatically calculate and display the area of the triangle in the "Area" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Base (Number)
* **Base** (`base`, `number`, required): Enter the length of the base of the triangle, which is the length of the side or segment that lies on the base of the triangle.

### Height (Number)
* **Height** (`height`, `number`, required): Enter the height of the triangle, which is the perpendicular distance from the base to the opposite vertex.

### Area (Result)
* **Area** (`area`, `number`, displayed only): This field will automatically display the calculated area of the triangle based on the input values.
