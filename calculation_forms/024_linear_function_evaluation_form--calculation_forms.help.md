# Linear Function Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Linear Function Evaluation Form is designed to assess students' understanding of linear functions. This form is intended for teachers or evaluators to evaluate students' knowledge and provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for each student individually. 
2. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Enter the student's email address in the "Student Email" field.
4. Select the grade level of the student in the "Student Grade Level" field.
5. Enter the subject the student is being evaluated on in the "Subject" field.
6. Evaluate the student's understanding of the linear function by providing feedback in the "Comments" field for the student and "Comments" field for the teacher.
7. Enter the student's score out of 100 in the "Score" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's name as it appears on their record or as they prefer to be called.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the student's email address.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, the teacher or evaluator.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, `number`, required: false): Select the grade level of the student from 1-12.
* **Subject** (`student_subject`, `text`, required: false): Enter the subject the student is being evaluated on.
* **Function** (`student_function`, `text`, required: false): This field is intended to capture the student's response to a specific linear function question.
* **Score** (`student_score`, `number`, required: false): Enter the student's score out of 100.
* **Comments** (`student_comments`, `note`, required: false): Enter feedback for the student on their understanding of the linear function.
* **Comments** (`teacher_comments`, `note`, required: false): Enter your feedback to the student on their understanding of the linear function.
* **Email** (`teacher_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Student Score** (`student_score`, `number`, required: false): Enter the student's score out of 100. 

Note: You must be logged in as a teacher or evaluator to access this form.
