# Base 2 Logarithm Of E (LOG2E) Calculation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to calculate the base-2 logarithm of E (LOG2E) for a given input value. The calculation is a mathematical operation that represents the power of 2 to which E (Euler's number) is raised. This form is useful for scientific and engineering applications where the base-2 logarithm of E is required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter the number to be processed in the "Input Value (x)" field.
3. Select the desired decimal precision in the "Required Decimal Precision" field.
4. Select the desired output format from the options provided in the "Desired Output Format" field.
5. Enter the date and time when the calculation was performed in the "Timestamp" field (optional).
6. Enter your department or affiliation in the "Department" field (optional).
7. Check the "Would you like the results emailed to you?" checkbox if you want to receive the results via email.
8. Enter feedback on the tool's accuracy in the "Feedback on tool accuracy" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`calculator_user_name`, `text`, required): Your name for logging purposes.
- **Input Value (x)** (`input_value_x`, `number`, required): The number to process.
- **Required Decimal Precision** (`precision_required`, `number`, required): The number of decimal places to display in the result.
- **LOG2E Constant Reference** (`constant_log2e_value`, `number`, optional): A reference value for the base-2 logarithm of E (approximately 1.442695).
- **Desired Output Format** (`output_format`, `select_one`, required): The format of the result, which can be:
  * **Scientific Notation**: The base-2 logarithm of E in scientific notation (e.g., 1.442695d+0).
  * **Fixed Decimal**: The base-2 logarithm of E as a fixed decimal (e.g., 1.44269500).
  * **Fraction**: The base-2 logarithm of E as a fraction (e.g., 144269350/100000).
- **Timestamp** (`timestamp`, `date`, required): The date and time when the calculation was performed.
- **Department** (`user_department`, `text`, optional): The user's department or affiliation (if any).
- **Would you like the results emailed to you?** (`export_results`, `select_one`, optional): A checkbox to request email notification of the results.
- **Feedback on tool accuracy** (`feedback_text`, `text`, optional): A field to provide feedback on the tool's accuracy.
