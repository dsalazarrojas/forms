# Future Value Calculation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Future Value Calculation Form is a tool for calculating the future value of an investment based on various input factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the investment amount in the "Investment Amount" field.
2. Select the compounding period in the "Years" field.
3. Enter the monthly interest rate in the "Monthly Interest Rate" field.
4. Choose the compounding frequency in the "Compounding Frequency" field.
5. Enter the inflation rate in the "Inflation Rate" field (if applicable).
6. Enter the investment period in the "Investment Period" field.
7. Click the "Calculate" button to generate the result.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Investment Amount (investment_amount)
* A field for entering the initial investment amount.

### Years (years)
* A field to select the number of years the investment will be held for.
* Options: 1 year, 5 years, 10 years

### Monthly Interest Rate (monthly_interest_rate)
* A field to enter the monthly interest rate as a decimal value.
* Example: 0.05 for 5%

### Compounding Frequency (compounding_frequency)
* A field to select how frequently interest is compounded.
* Options: Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly

### Inflation Rate (inflation_rate)
* A field to enter the inflation rate as a decimal value.
* Example: 0.03 for 3%

### Investment Period (investment_period)
* A field to enter the investment period in time format (e.g., "5 years 4 months").

### Result (result)
* A field to display the calculated future value of the investment.

### Result 2 (result_2)
* A field to display additional information related to the calculated result.
