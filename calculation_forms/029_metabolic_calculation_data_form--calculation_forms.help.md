# Metabolic Calculation Data Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about a client's physical condition, behavior, and lifestyle for metabolic calculation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the client's details, including their first and last name, date of birth, height, weight, blood pressure, and blood glucose levels.
2. List any medication allergies the client may have.
3. Mention any medical conditions the client may have.
4. Specify the client's exercise frequency, intensity, type, and duration.
5. Provide information about the client's water intake, daily calories, sleep duration, wake and bedtime, and any additional exercise habits.
6. Fill in any other relevant details the client may have provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header**: This is the title of the form, which is not editable.
* **Client Details**: This is where you can fill in the client's first and last name.
* **Date of Birth**: This is where you can enter the client's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Height (cm)**: Enter the client's height in centimeters.
* **Weight (kg)**: Enter the client's weight in kilograms.
* **Blood Pressure (mmHg)**: Enter the client's blood pressure in millimeters of mercury.
* **Blood Glucose (mmol/L)**: Enter the client's blood glucose levels in millimoles per liter.
* **Medication Allergies**: List any medication allergies the client may have.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions the client may have.
* **Client Goals**: Specify the client's goals or any health objectives they may have.
* **Exercise Frequency**: Choose the client's exercise frequency (e.g., 1-2 times per week, 3-4 times per week, etc.).
* **Exercise Minutes Per Session**: Choose the client's exercise duration (e.g., less than 30 minutes, 30-60 minutes, etc.).
* **Water Intake (L)**: Enter the client's water intake in liters.
* **Daily Calories**: Enter the client's daily calories.
* **Sleep Duration (hours)**: Enter the client's sleep duration in hours.
* **Wake Time**: Enter the client's wake time in 24-hour format.
* **Bed Time**: Enter the client's bedtime in 24-hour format.
* **Exercise Type**: Choose the client's exercise type (e.g., cardiovascular exercise, strength training, etc.).
* **Exercise Intensity**: Choose the client's exercise intensity (e.g., low, medium, high, etc.).
* **Exercise Frequency 2**: Choose the client's additional exercise frequency (e.g., 1-2 times per week, 3-4 times per week, etc.).
* **Additional Exercise**: Choose the client's additional exercise frequency (e.g., 1-2 times per week, 3-4 times per week, etc.).
* **Client Goals 2**: Specify the client's additional goals or health objectives.

## Tips
Please ensure you fill in all fields accurately and thoroughly for better results. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant personnel for assistance.
