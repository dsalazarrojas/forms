# retirement_calculator_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The retirement_calculator_form is designed to help users plan for their retirement by estimating the total savings needed for a comfortable retirement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form to begin.
2. Enter your current retirement age in the "retirement_age" field.
3. Enter the amount you can save monthly in the "monthly_savings" field.
4. Enter the number of years you plan to retire in the "years_to_retire" field.
5. Click on the "Calculate" button to receive an estimate of your total savings requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Retirement Age**: This is the current age of the user who wants to plan for their retirement. Please enter the current age to get accurate results.
  * Type: Number
  * Required: No
  * Hint: Enter your current age
- **Monthly Savings**: This is the amount the user can save each month for retirement. Please enter a numerical value to get accurate results.
  * Type: Number
  * Required: No
  * Hint: Enter the monthly savings
- **Years to Retire**: This is the number of years the user plans to retire in. Please enter a numerical value to get accurate results.
  * Type: Number
  * Required: No
  * Hint: Enter the years to retire
