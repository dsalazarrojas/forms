# Mortgage Calculation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to provide an estimate of monthly mortgage payments based on the provided loan amount, term, and interest rate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **loan amount**, which is the total cost of the property you are seeking to purchase or finance.
2. Choose the **loan term**, which is the length of time you plan to take to repay the loan, usually in years.
3. Enter the **interest rate**, which is the annual rate at which you expect to pay interest on your loan.
4. Enter the **payment date**, if you have a specific date for your first payment. Otherwise, you can leave this field blank.
4. Once you have filled in all the fields, click **Submit** to calculate and display your estimated monthly payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **loan amount** (`loan_amount`, `number`, required/optional: required): The total cost of the property you are seeking to purchase or finance.
* **loan term** (`loan_term`, `number`, required/optional: required): The length of time you plan to take to repay the loan, usually in years.
* **interest rate** (`interest_rate`, `number`, required/optional: required): The annual rate at which you expect to pay interest on your loan.
* **monthly payment** (`monthly_payment`, `number`, required/optional: calculated): The estimated amount you will pay each month towards the loan. This field is calculated automatically based on your inputs.
* **payment date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required/optional: optional): The date of your first loan payment. If you leave this field blank, it will be set to today's date by default.
