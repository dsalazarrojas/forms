# Calorie Calculation Photo Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for users to submit photos of their meals to estimate the calorie content.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in your contact information**: Enter your full name and email address to ensure we can follow up with you about your submission and provide any results.
2. **Submit a photo**: Take a clear photo of your meal and upload it to the form.
3. **Describe your meal**: Provide a brief description of what the photo shows, including portion sizes.
4. **Optional details**: If you know the calorie value, you can provide an estimated value. Additionally, you can list the main components of the meal and note any dietary restrictions.
5. **Consent to use photo**: Check the box to confirm that you consent to us using the photo for analysis and coaching.
6. **Add any additional notes**: If you have any further context or details about your meal, you can add them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Use your email address to receive follow-up and results from us.
* **Meal Date** (`meal_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the meal was consumed.
* **Meal Time** (`meal_time`, time, optional): If you know the time of the meal, enter it here.
* **Photo Description** (`meal_photo_description`, text, required): Briefly describe what the photo shows, including portion sizes.
* **Estimated Calories** (`estimated_calories`, number, optional): If you know the calorie value, enter it here.
* **Meal Components** (`meal_components`, text, optional): List the main components of the meal, such as protein, carbs, and fats.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): Note any dietary restrictions or allergies you have.
* **Consent to Use Photo for Analysis** (`consent_photo_use`, select_one, required): Check the box to confirm that you consent to us using the photo for analysis and coaching.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional context or details about your meal.
