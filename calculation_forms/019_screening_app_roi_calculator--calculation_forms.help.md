# Screening App ROI Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Screening App ROI Calculator is a tool designed to calculate the Return on Investment (ROI) of a screening application. This guide will walk you through the process of completing the form to accurately estimate the ROI of your screening app.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction, which provides a brief overview of the calculator's purpose and functionality.
2. Fill in the estimated time savings and cost savings fields with the numbers that reflect your expected benefits from implementing the screening app.
3. Enter the implementation cost, which is the initial investment required to deploy the screening app.
4. Specify the timeframe for the ROI calculation, which represents the period over which the benefits are expected to be realized.
5. Finally, calculate and view your ROI result, which will be displayed as a percentage value.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Introduction, Text, Optional): This field provides a brief overview of the calculator's purpose and functionality. Please read this section carefully to ensure you understand the context of the calculator.
* **Time Savings** (Time Savings, Number, Optional): Enter the estimated time savings that the screening app is expected to deliver. This can be expressed in hours, days, or weeks, depending on the expected benefits.
* **Cost Savings** (Cost Savings, Number, Optional): Enter the estimated cost savings that the screening app is expected to deliver.
* **Implementation Cost** (Implementation Cost, Number, Optional): Enter the initial investment required to deploy the screening app.
* **ROI Timeline** (ROI Timeline, Time, Optional): Specify the timeframe for the ROI calculation, which represents the period over which the benefits are expected to be realized.
* **ROI** (ROI, Number, Optional): The calculator will display the calculated ROI as a percentage value, indicating the return on investment of the screening app.
