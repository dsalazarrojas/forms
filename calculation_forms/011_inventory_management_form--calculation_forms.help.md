# Inventory Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Inventory Management Form is used to update or manage inventory data for products or items within a company's inventory system. This form helps warehouse staff, inventory managers, or similar personnel to quickly and efficiently track and query inventory levels, monitor stock movements, and generate reports.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select one or more products from the "Select Multiple" field to manage.
2.  Enter a product ID for the specific product or item.
3.  Enter the product's name in the "Product Name" field.
4.  Specify the quantity of the product in the "Quantity" field.
5.  Choose a location from the "Location ID" field to identify where the product is stored.
6.  Enter the location's name in the "Location Name" field.
7.  Select a category from the "Category ID" field to categorize the product.
8.  Enter the category's name in the "Category Name" field.
9.  Choose the product's status as "Active" or "Inactive" from the "Inventory Status" field.
10.  Enter comments or descriptions about the product in the "Notes" field.
11.  Enter an email address for notifications or contact purposes in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Select Multiple**: Choose one or more products from the list to manage.
*   **Product ID**: Enter a unique numeric identifier for the product or item.
*   **Product Name**: Enter the product's name for easy reference.
*   **Quantity**: Enter the number of units of the product in stock.
*   **Location ID**: Enter the location ID where the product is stored.
*   **Location Name**: Enter the location's name for easy reference.
*   **Category ID**: Enter the category ID for the product's classification.
*   **Category Name**: Enter the category's name for easy reference.
*   **Inventory Status**: Select "Active" for available products or "Inactive" for products not available.
*   **Notes**: Enter any comments or descriptions about the product.
*   **Email**: Enter an email address for notifications or contact purposes.

## Tips

*   Ensure you have the necessary permissions to access and update the inventory data.
*   Review the product list and select only the products you want to manage.
*   Enter accurate and up-to-date information for each product.
*   Use the "Notes" field to add any relevant comments or descriptions about the product.
*   Use the "Email" field for notifications or contact purposes.
