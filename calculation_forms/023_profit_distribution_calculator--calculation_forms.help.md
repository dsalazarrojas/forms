# Profit Distribution Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Profit Distribution Calculator is a tool designed to help businesses with multiple partners calculate the profit distribution among them. This guide will walk you through the form's purpose and usage.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select "Business Partnership" Options**: Choose whether the business is a partnership or not.
2. Enter **Total Profit**: Input the total profit made by the business.
3. Select **Business Partners** options: Indicate if the business has partners or not.
4. Choose **Profit Distribution** options: Select whether profit distribution is applicable or not.
5. Enter **Allocated Profit**, **Business Allocation**, **Profit**, **Total Allocation**, and **Profit Percent** fields: Enter the actual values as per your business requirements.
6. Finally, review and adjust the **Allocated Percent**, **Calculated Profit Percent**, and **Business Allocation Percent** fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Partnership (1)**: Select whether the business is a partnership or not. This will help determine the distribution of profit among partners.
* **Total Profit (2)**: Enter the total profit made by the business. This will be the base for profit distribution calculations.
* **Total (3)**: Enter the total profit made by the business. This field is redundant and not recommended for filling. Please use the **Total Profit (2)** field instead.
* **Percentage Split (4)**: Enter the percentage split among partners. This field is not required but can be useful in some cases.
* **Business Partners (5)**: Indicate if the business has partners or not. This will help determine the number of partners to be included in profit distribution.
* **Profit Distribution (6)**: This field is not applicable in this form. It's intended to be a note for future development.
* **Calculated Profit (7)**: This field is automatically populated based on the input values. Review and adjust as needed.
* **Profit Allocation (8)**: Select whether profit allocation is applicable or not. This will determine the distribution of profit among partners.
* **Allocated Profit (9)**: Enter the profit allocated to each partner. This field is automatically populated based on the input values.
* **Business Allocation (10)**: Enter the business allocation. This field is automatically populated based on the input values.
* **Profit (11)**: Enter the profit made by the business. This field is not required and can be removed.
* **Total Allocation (12)**: Enter the total profit allocation. This field is automatically populated based on the input values.
* **Allocation Percent (13)**: This field is not applicable in this form. It's intended to be a note for future development.
* **Calculated Profit Percent (14)**: This field is not applicable in this form. It's intended to be a note for future development.
* **Business Allocation Percent (15)**: This field is not applicable in this form. It's intended to be a note for future development.
* **Partner Profit (16)**: This field is not applicable in this form. It's intended to be a note for future development.
