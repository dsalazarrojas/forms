**Purpose**
The Solar Savings Calculator form is designed to calculate potential savings for solar energy systems. This form helps users estimate savings by providing necessary information about their location, solar panels, and other relevant factors.

**How To Complete This Form**

To use the Solar Savings Calculator, follow these steps:

* Enter your address in the "Address" field. This will help us understand your location and provide more accurate calculations.
* Select your state from the "State" dropdown list.
* Enter your zip code in the "Zip Code" field. Please use the 5-digit format (e.g., 12345).
* Select your electricity rate from the "Electricity Rate" field. This will help us estimate your current energy consumption.
* Choose the type of roof you have from the "Roof Type" dropdown list.
* Select your solar panel type from the "Solar Panels" dropdown list.
* Choose when your solar system was installed from the "Installation Year" field. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
* Select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or In-Person) in the "Contact Method" field.
* Enter your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* If you are representing a company, enter your company's name in the "Company Name" field (optional).
* Enter your company's address in the "Company Address" field (optional).
* Select your company's state from the "Company State" dropdown list.
* Enter your company's zip code in the "Company Zip" field. Please use the 5-digit format (e.g., 12345).
* Enter your company's city in the "Company City" field (optional).
* Enter your company's phone number in the "Company Phone" field (optional).
* Enter your company's email address in the "Company Email" field (optional).
* Choose the type of property (Residential or Commercial) you are inquiring about in the "Lead Type" field.
* Choose whether the property is existing or new in the "Property Type" field.
* If you are representing a company, select your company's size in the "Company Size" field (optional).
* Choose the status of the lead (Qualified or Not Qualified) in the "Lead Status" field.
