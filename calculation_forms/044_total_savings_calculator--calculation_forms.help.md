# Total Savings Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Total Savings Calculator is a tool designed to calculate the total savings over a specified period of time for different types of savings options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the number of **Years** you are willing to save for.
2. Enter the **Monthly Value** you are willing to invest each month.
3. Choose the type of **GIC (Guaranteed Investment Certificate)** you are interested in (Fixed Income, Guaranteed Investment Certificate, High-Interest, Low-Interest, Prime, or Variable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Years** (`years`, required): Enter the number of years you are willing to save for. This is a required field, as the calculation is dependent on the length of time.
* **Monthly Value** (`monthly_value`, required): Enter the amount you are willing to invest each month. This is a required field, as it determines the total amount invested.
* **GIC Type** (`gic_type`, required): Choose the type of GIC that best suits your investment goals. This selection will affect the calculation of your total savings.
