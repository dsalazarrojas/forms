# Hourly Rate Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hourly Rate Calculator is designed to help users calculate their hourly rate based on various factors such as currency, weekly hours worked, and billing goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the currency you work in from the options provided.
2. Choose the range of your current hourly rate from the options.
3. Enter the number of hours you work per week.
4. Enter the number of hours you bill to your clients per week.
5. The form will calculate your hourly rate for different currencies and billing scenarios.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What Is Your Current Hourly Rate?** (`hourly_rate_calculator_form`, Select One, Not Required): This field asks you to select your current hourly rate from the provided options.
* **Which Rate Range Do You Work In?** (`rate_range`, Select Multiple, Not Required): Choose the range of your current hourly rate from the options.
* **Which Currency Do You Work In?** (`hourly_rate_currency`, Select One, Not Required): Select the currency you work in from the options.
* **How Many Hours Do You Work Per Week?** (`weekly_hours_worked`, Number, Not Required): Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **How Many Hours Do You Bill To Your Clients Per Week?** (`weekly_hours_billed`, Number, Not Required): Enter the number of hours you bill to your clients per week.
* **Calculated Hourly Rate** (`calculated_hourly_rate`, Number, Not Required): This field displays the calculated hourly rate for the selected currency and billing scenario.
* **What Is Your Hourly Rate Currency?** (`rate_currency`, Select One, Not Required): Select the currency of your hourly rate from the options.
* **Total Earned Last Year** (`total_earned`, Number, Not Required): This field displays the total earned last year.
* **Weekly Hours Worked Earned Last Year** (`weekly_hours_worked_earned`, Number, Not Required): This field displays the number of hours worked and earned last year.
* **Weekly Hours Billed Earned Last Year** (`weekly_hours_billed_earned`, Number, Not Required): This field displays the number of hours billed and earned last year.
* **Calculated Hourly Rate Earned** (`calculated_hourly_rate_earned`, Number, Not Required): This field displays the calculated hourly rate for the selected currency and billing scenario.
* **Weekly Earned Last Year** (`weekly_earned`, Select One, Not Required): Select the currency for which you earned last year from the options.
* **Weekly Currency Earned Last Year** (`weekly_currency_earned`, Select One, Not Required): Select the currency in which you earned last year from the options.
* **Weekly Rate Earned Last Year** (`weekly_rate_earned`, Number, Not Required): This field displays the rate earned last year.
* **Calculated Hourly Rate Earned Last Year** (`calculated_hourly_rate_earned`, Number, Not Required): This field displays the calculated hourly rate for the selected currency and billing scenario.
* **Weekly Billed Goals** (`weekly_billed_goals`, Number, Not Required): Enter the billing goals for the week.
* **Billed Earned Goals** (`billed_earned_goals`, Number, Not Required): Enter the earned goals for the week.
* **Calculated Hourly Rate Goals** (`calculated_hourly_rate_goals`, Number, Not Required): This field displays the calculated hourly rate for the selected goals and scenario.
* **Weekly Earned Earned Goals** (`weekly_earned_goals`, Number, Not Required): This field displays the earned goals for the week.
* **Calculated Hourly Rate Earned Goals** (`calculated_hourly_rate_earned_goals`, Number, Not Required): This field displays the calculated hourly rate for the selected goals and scenario.
* **Weekly Billed Earned Goals** (`weekly_billed_earned_goals`, Number, Not Required): This field displays the earned goals for the week.
* **Calculated Hourly Rate Billed Goals** (`calculated_hourly_rate_billed_goals`, Number, Not Required): This field displays the calculated hourly rate for the selected goals and scenario.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are accurately filled to get the most accurate results.
* Use the provided currency options to select the correct currency of your work and earnings.
* If you're not sure about any of the fields, please refer back to this help guide for clarification.
