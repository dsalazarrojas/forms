# Risk Assessment And Income Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals or organizations assess their risk level and calculate their associated risk score based on various factors. It's intended to be a comprehensive tool for understanding and managing risk.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your age in the "Age" field.
2. Enter your income in the "Income" field.
3. Identify your risk factors by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Risk Factors" field.
4. Choose whether to calculate or not by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Calculate" field.
5. View your calculated risk score, assessment, and band by filling out the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age** (`age`, `text`, required): Please enter your age to provide a baseline for risk assessment.
* **Income** (`income`, `number`, required): Enter your annual income to consider financial factors in the risk assessment.
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" if you experience any of the risk factors. This will be used to calculate your risk score.
* **Calculate** (`calculate`, `select_one`, optional): Choose "Yes" to trigger the calculation of your risk score and assessment.
* **Risk Score** (`risk_score`, `number`, optional): This is the calculated risk score based on your input.
* **Calculated Risk Score** (`calculated_risk_score`, `number`, optional): This field is not used, as the actual calculation is performed elsewhere.
* **Risk Score Range** (`risk_score_range`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is not used, as the risk score band is calculated elsewhere.
* **Calculated Risk Score Range** (`calculated_risk_score_range`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is not used, as the risk score band is calculated elsewhere.
* **Risk Remarks** (`risk_remarks`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" if you have any risk-related remarks or comments.
* **Calculated Risk Remarks** (`calculated_risk_remarks`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is not used, as the risk remarks are calculated elsewhere.
* **Risk Band** (`risk_band`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" if you fall into a specific risk band.
* **Calculated Risk Band** (`calculated_risk_band`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is not used, as the risk band is calculated elsewhere.
* **Calculated Risk Band Range** (`calculated_risk_band_range`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is not used, as the risk band range is calculated elsewhere.
* **Risk Assessment Band Description** (`risk_assessment_band_description`, `text`, optional): This field provides a description of the risk assessment band you fall into.
* **Calculated Risk Assessment Band Description** (`calculated_risk_assessment_band_description`, `text`, optional): This field is not used, as the risk assessment band description is calculated elsewhere.
* **Calculated Risk Assessment** (`calculated_risk_assessment`, `text`, optional): This field provides the calculated risk assessment based on your input.
* **Risk Assessment Band** (`risk_assessment_band`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" if you fall into a specific risk assessment band.
* **Calculated Risk Assessment Band** (`calculated_risk_assessment_band`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is not used, as the risk assessment band is calculated elsewhere.
* **Calculated Risk Assessment Range** (`calculated_risk_assessment_range`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is not used, as the risk assessment range is calculated elsewhere.
