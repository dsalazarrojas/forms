# Calculate Square Root Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Calculate Square Root Form is a tool designed to compute the square root of a given number, helping students and educators in educational settings. It is suitable for educational purposes, such as homework help or test preparation, and can be used for personal interest projects. If you have other uses in mind, consider filling out the 'Purpose' field to help us better understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **Your Name**, if you want to identify yourself or your student ID.
2. Optionally provide your **Email Address** if you want to receive the calculation results or future updates about this form.
3. Input the **Calculation Date** as today's date, which helps us track usage statistics.
4. Enter the **Number** you want to calculate the square root of. Ensure it's a valid numerical input.
5. The square root will be automatically calculated and displayed in the **Square Root Calculation** field.
6. If needed, select the **Decimal Precision** for the result, choosing from 2, 4, 6, or 8 decimal places or the full form.
7. Decide whether to **Show Calculation Steps** for educational value or keep it hidden.
8. Choose the **Purpose** of the calculation, such as Homework, Test preparation, Work project, Personal interest, or Other.
9. Select your **Grade Level**, which can be Elementary, Middle school, High school, College, Graduate, Professional, or Other.
10. If applicable, choose the related **Subject**, such as Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Calculus, Statistics, Physics, Engineering, or Other.
11. If you'd like, choose whether to **Email Results**, and we'll send the calculation results to your email.
12. If you'd like, choose **Need Additional Calculations**, and we'll know you want to explore more calculations.
13. Finally, you can leave **Feedback** if you have any suggestions or comments about this tool.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (student_name, text, optional): Enter your name or student ID.
* **Email Address** (email_address, email, optional): Provide your email address if you'd like to receive the calculation results or updates.
* **Calculation Date** (calculation_date, date, optional): Enter today's date for tracking purposes.
* **Number** (number_input, number, required): Enter the number you'd like to calculate the square root of.
* **Square Root Calculation** (calculation_note, note, optional): This displays the calculated square root.
* **Decimal Precision** (precision, select_one, optional): Select the decimal places for the result.
* **Show Calculation Steps** (show_steps, select_one, optional): Choose whether to display the calculation steps.
* **Purpose** (purpose, select_one, optional): Choose the purpose of your calculation.
* **Grade Level** (grade_level, select_one, optional): Select your current grade or level.
* **Subject** (subject, select_one, optional): Choose the related subject.
* **Email Results** (email_results, select_one, optional): Choose whether to receive the calculation results by email.
* **Need Additional Calculations** (additional_calculations, select_one, optional): Choose whether you need more calculations.
* **Feedback** (feedback, text, optional): Leave feedback for us about this tool.
