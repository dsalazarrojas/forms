# Round Decimals Upward - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to round up decimal numbers for calculations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "first input" field with the initial decimal number you want to round up.
2. In the "number to round" field, enter the number of decimal places to round up to. For example, if you want to round up to the nearest whole number, enter 0.
3. Click "Submit" to get the rounded result in the "output" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Input (number)** (`first_input`, **number**, true): Enter the initial decimal number you want to round up.
* **Number to Round (number)** (`input_number_to_round`, **number**, true): Enter the number of decimal places to round up to.
* **Output (number)** (`output`, **number**, false): This field will display the result of the rounding operation.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct initial decimal number and the number of decimal places to round up to to get the expected results.
* If you are unsure about the rounding operation, test it with small numbers to understand the behavior of the form.
