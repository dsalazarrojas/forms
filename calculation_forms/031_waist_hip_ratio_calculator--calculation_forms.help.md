# Waist Hip Ratio Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for calculating the waist-hip ratio, a measure of body fat distribution. It determines health risks based on your waist and hip circumferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Input your measurements in inches or cm for both your Waist Circumference and Hip Circumference.
2. Press the "Calculate" button to determine your health risk.
3. If you wish to recalculate or try again, press "Calculate Again".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Waist Circumference** (`waist_hip_circumference_1`, number, required): Enter the circumference of your natural waistline in inches or cm. This is the narrowest point on your body between your lower edge of the ribcage and the top of your hip bone.
* **Hip Circumference** (`waist_hip_circumference_2`, number, required): Enter the circumference of your hips at your widest point, usually just above the buttocks. This measurement is taken at the widest point of the hips.
* **Calculate** (`calculate_button_1`, select_one, required): Press "Yes" to calculate your health risk.
* **Health Risk** (`risk_label_1`, select_one, required): This field will display your calculated health risk as "Low", "Medium" or "High".
* **Calculate Again** (`calculate_button_2`, select_one, required): Press "Yes" to recalculate your health risk.
* **Calculate Again** (`calculate_button_2`, select_one, required): Press "No" to discard your current measurements and start over.
