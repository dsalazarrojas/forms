# Body Mass Index Calculator - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to calculate the Body Mass Index (BMI) of users based on their height and weight.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your first and last name.
2. Provide your email address (optional).
3. Enter your date of birth.
4. Select your gender.
5. Choose your height unit preference (Feet and Inches or Centimeters).
6. Enter your height in the preferred unit (either in feet and inches or centimeters).
7. Choose your weight unit preference (Pounds or Kilograms).
8. Enter your weight in the preferred unit (either in pounds or kilograms).
9. Select your age group.
10. Choose your activity level.
11. Select your fitness goals.
12. Check if you have any health conditions (optional).
13. Leave your email address or other contact information for results (optional).
14. Understand what BMI means by selecting an option.
15. Indicate if you are interested in consulting with a professional.
16. Acknowledge that BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnostic tool.
17. Choose how you would like to receive your results.
18. Add any additional notes or comments (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, optional): Provide your email address to receive results.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender.
* **Height Unit Preference** (`height_unit`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you prefer to enter height in Feet and Inches or Centimeters.
* **Height in Feet** (`height_feet`, `select_one`, optional): If you selected Feet and Inches, select your height in feet.
* **Height in Inches** (`height_inches`, `select_one`, optional): If you selected Feet and Inches, enter the inches portion of your height.
* **Height in Centimeters** (`height_cm`, `number`, optional): If you selected Centimeters, enter your height in cm.
* **Weight Unit Preference** (`weight_unit`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you prefer to enter weight in Pounds or Kilograms.
* **Weight in Pounds** (`weight_lbs`, `number`, optional): If you selected Pounds, enter your weight in lbs.
* **Weight in Kilograms** (`weight_kg`, `number`, optional): If you selected Kilograms, enter your weight in kg.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, `select_one`, required): Select your age group.
* **Activity Level** (`activity_level`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your activity level.
* **Fitness Goals** (`fitness_goals`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your fitness goals.
* **Health Conditions** (`health_conditions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check if you have any health conditions.
* **Your BMI Result** (`calculated_bmi`, `number`, required): The calculated BMI value.
* **BMI Category** (`bmi_category`, `select_one`, optional): The BMI category (Underweight, Normal Weight, Overweight, Obese).
* **Healthy Weight Range** (`healthy_weight_range`, `text`, optional): The healthy weight range for your height.
* **BMI Understanding** (`bmi_understanding`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate how well you understand what BMI means.
* **Consultation Interest** (`consultation_interest`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you are interested in consulting with a professional.
* **Disclaimer Acknowledgment** (`disclaimer_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge that BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnostic tool.
* **Results Delivery** (`results_delivery`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you would like to receive your results (On Screen, Email, Both).
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Leave any additional comments or questions.
