# Investment Return Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Investment Return Calculator form is designed to estimate the expected rate of return on investment based on various factors such as investment amount, time period, rate of return, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of investment you are interested in from the "Investment Type" field.
2.  Choose your risk tolerance from the "Risk Tolerance" field.
3.  Select the time period for the investment from the "Time Period" field.
4.  Enter the amount of the investment in the "Investment Amount" field.
5.  Choose the compounding frequency from the "Compounding Frequency" field.
6.  Enter the expected annual interest rate for the investment in the "Expected Interest Rate" field.
7.  Choose how often interest is compounded in the account from the "Compound Frequency" field.
8.  Click on the "Calculate" button to get the estimated result.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Investment Type** (`investment_type`, required/optional): Select the type of investment you have, such as cash, stocks, bonds, cryptocurrency, or other.
    *   This field helps the calculator understand the investment type, which may affect the expected return.
*   **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, required/optional): Choose your risk tolerance, which is the level of risk you are willing to take on the investment.
    *   This field helps the calculator understand how much risk you are willing to take, which may affect the expected return.
*   **Time Period** (`time_period`, required/optional): Select the time period for the investment, such as a year, quarter, half year, or month.
    *   This field determines the time frame for the calculation.
*   **Investment Amount** (`investment_amount`, required/optional): Enter the amount of money invested.
    *   This field is the basis for the calculation.
*   **Compounding Frequency** (`compounding_frequency`, required/optional): Choose how often interest is compounded in the account, such as monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, or annually.
    *   This field determines the compounding frequency.
*   **Expected Interest Rate** (`expected_interest_rate`, required/optional): Enter the expected annual interest rate for the investment.
    *   This field is the expected interest rate for the calculation.
*   **Compound Frequency** (`compound_frequency`, required/optional): Choose how often interest is compounded in the account, such as annually, monthly, quarterly, or semi-annually.
    *   This field determines the compounding frequency.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the investment.
    *   This field is for any additional information about the investment.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
    *   This field is for contact information in case of any further questions or clarification.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
    *   This field is for contact information in case of any further questions or clarification.
*   **Investment Goal** (`investment_goal`, required/optional): Choose the investment goal, such as short term, medium term, or long term.
    *   This field helps the calculator understand the investment goal, which may affect the expected return.
*   **Investment Type Description** (`investment_type_description`, required/optional): Enter a description of the investment type.
    *   This field provides more information about the investment type.
*   **Result** (`result`, required/optional): Choose the expected result of the investment, such as high, low, or medium.
    *   This field indicates the expected outcome of the investment.
*   **Result Description** (`result_description`, required/optional): Enter a description of the expected result.
    *   This field provides more information about the expected result.
