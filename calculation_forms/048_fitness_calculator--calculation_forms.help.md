# Fitness Calculator - Help Guide

## Purpose

The fitness calculator is designed to provide you with insights into your physical fitness and help you set realistic goals. This form collects your data to calculate your fitness-related metrics, such as calories burnt and distance, which can help you track your progress and stay healthy.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on "User Information" and enter your height in centimeters, weight in kilograms, age, exercise level, and other physical activities (if any) on the first page.
2. Review and ensure that you have entered all required fields correctly.
3. Click on "Submit" to process the data and calculate your fitness-related metrics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (Height in Centimeters)**: Enter your height in centimeters to help us understand your physical proportions.
* **User Information (Weight in Kilograms)**: Enter your weight in kilograms to help us understand your body mass.
* **User Information (Age)**: Enter your age to help us calculate your age-related metrics.
* **User Information (Exercise Level)**: Choose your current exercise level to help us understand your physical fitness level.
* **User Information (Distance)**: Enter the distance you have covered in a specific activity (e.g., running, cycling) to help us calculate your energy expenditure.
* **User Information (Calories Burnt)**: This field is not required, but if you know the calories burnt during your activity, you can enter it here to help us provide more accurate results.
* **User Information (Workout Duration)**: Enter the duration of your workout to help us calculate your energy expenditure.
* **User Information (Max Heart Rate)**: Enter your max heart rate to help us understand your cardiovascular fitness level.
* **User Information (Calories Burnt 1)**: This field is for you to enter the calories burnt for a specific activity, and it's required for an accurate calculation.
* **User Information (Time)**: Enter the time spent on an activity to help us calculate your energy expenditure.
* **User Information (Push Ups)**: Enter the number of push-ups you have done to help us assess your upper body strength.
* **User Information (Sit Ups)**: Enter the number of sit-ups you have done to help us assess your lower body strength.
* **User Information (Pull Ups)**: Enter the number of pull-ups you have done to help us assess your upper body strength.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you enter accurate information to get the most out of this calculator.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for help.
* You can use the calculated results to set realistic goals and track your progress.
* This form is for general fitness assessments only; consult a healthcare professional for personalized advice.
