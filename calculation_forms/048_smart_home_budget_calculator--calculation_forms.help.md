# Smart Home Budget Calculator - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you determine your smart home budget based on your project details. It is a simple and intuitive tool that guides you through a step-by-step process to input your project information and calculate your budget.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the project details on the "Project Details" page.
2. Select your budget preferences on the "Budget Preferences" page.
3. Enter your project location on the "Project Location" page.
4. Choose your project duration on the "Project Duration" page.
5. Select your expected start date on the "Expected Start Date" page.
6. Review your answers and calculate your smart home budget.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (project_details, text, optional):
	+ Enter a brief description of your smart home project.
* **Budget Preferences** (budget_preferences, text, optional):
	+ Select your budget preferences, such as "High", "Medium", or "Low".
* **Project Location** (project_location, text, optional):
	+ Enter your project location (e.g., city, state, country).
* **Project Duration** (project_duration, time, optional):
	+ Choose the duration of your project in hours, days, weeks, months, or years.
* **Expected Start Date** (expected_start_date, date, optional):
	+ Select the date when you expect to start your project.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields to get an accurate budget calculation.
* Double-check your project duration and expected start date to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
