# parking cost estimator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The parking cost estimator form is designed to calculate the total parking fee based on the user's input of parking duration and rates. It is intended for users to input their parking duration and choose whether they want to pay a daily or hourly rate to estimate their parking fee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your parking duration in hours in the "Parking Duration" field.
2. Choose your parking rate type from the "Parking Rate Type" field. You can select "Daily Rate" or "Hourly Rate" to calculate your parking fee.
3. If you selected "Other" in the "Parking Rate Type" field, you can enter your other parking rate type in the "Other Parking Rate Type" field.
4. Enter your daily or hourly parking rate in the "Daily Parking Rate" or "Hourly Parking Rate" field, respectively.
5. Click the "Submit" button to calculate and view your estimated parking fee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parking Fee** (1, number, optional): This field will display your estimated parking fee based on the input values.
* **Parking Duration** (4, number, optional): Enter the length of time you parked in hours.
* **Daily Parking Rate** (2, number, optional): Enter the daily parking rate.
* **Hourly Parking Rate** (3, number, optional): Enter the hourly parking rate.
* **Parking Fee (Calculated)** (5, select_multiple, optional): This field will be populated based on your input values.
* **Parking Fee Total** (6, number, optional): This field will display the total estimated parking fee.
* **Parking Rate Type** (7, select_one, optional): Choose whether you want to pay a daily or hourly rate.
* **Other Parking Rate Type** (8, text, optional): Enter a custom parking rate type if you selected "Other" in the "Parking Rate Type" field.
* **Submit** (9, text, optional): Click this button to calculate and view your estimated parking fee.
