# Calculate Age - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Calculate Age" form is designed to help users calculate their age based on their date of birth. This form is useful for personal records, employment verification, insurance applications, medical records, and other purposes where age verification is required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the field "Full Name".
2. Select your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field. Please enter the date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
3. In the "Purpose of Age Calculation" field, choose the reason for calculating your age.
4. In the "Age Verification Required" field, indicate if this is for official verification or personal use only.
5. In the "Include Months and Days" field, choose how you want your age to be calculated (Years only, Years and months, or Years, months, and days).
6. Select your timezone from the options provided.
7. If you want the results emailed to you, select "Yes" in the "Email Results" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field for sending results.
9. Finally, type any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Comments" field, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (Full Name)**: Enter your full name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (Date of Birth)**: Enter your birth date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY) to calculate your age accurately.
* **Age Calculation (Calculation Note)**: This field is not required, but it will display a note about how your age will be calculated.
* **Purpose of Age Calculation (Purpose of Age Calculation)**: Select the reason for calculating your age from the provided options.
* **Age Verification Required (Age Verification Required)**: Indicate if this calculation is for official verification or personal use only.
* **Include Months and Days (Include Months and Days)**: Choose how you want your age to be calculated: Years only, Years and months, or Years, months, and days.
* **Timezone (Timezone)**: Select your current timezone to ensure accurate calculation.
* **Email Results (Email Results)**: Select "Yes" if you want the results emailed to you.
* **Email Address (Email Address)**: Enter your email address where results will be sent.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments)**: Type any additional comments or notes if needed.
