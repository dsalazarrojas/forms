# Calculate Your Caloric Need Orf - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help you determine your personalized caloric needs based on various factors such as age, height, weight, activity level, and fitness goals. The form is divided into several sections that ask for your demographic information, physical characteristics, lifestyle habits, and dietary preferences. The results will provide you with a tailored caloric intake recommendation to support your fitness goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your full name, email address, and select your calculation date.
2.  Choose your gender, age, and height unit.
3.  Enter your height in feet and inches or centimeters, depending on your selected unit.
4.  Choose your weight unit and enter your current weight.
5.  Select your goal weight, if applicable.
6.  Indicate your typical daily activity level.
7.  Choose your primary fitness goal.
8.  Note any medical conditions that may affect your metabolism.
9.  Select your preferred caloric adjustment for your goal.
10.  Choose your preferred macro split (carbohydrates, protein, fat) and dietary restrictions.
11.  Indicate how many meals you prefer to have per day.
12.  Choose your daily water intake.
13.  Select your average sleep hours per night.
14.  Choose how you would like to receive your results.
15.  Provide any additional fitness goals or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address for results and recommendations delivery.
*   **Calculation Date** (`calculation_date`, `date`, required): Select the date for your calculation.
*   **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Choose your gender (Male or Female).
*   **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): Enter your age in years.
*   **Height Unit** (`height_unit`, `select_one`, required): Choose the unit for your height (Feet and inches or Centimeters).
*   **Height in Feet** (`height_feet`, `number`, optional): Enter your height in feet, if using the feet and inches unit.
*   **Height in Inches** (`height_inches`, `number`, optional): Enter your height in inches, if using the feet and inches unit.
*   **Height in Centimeters** (`height_cm`, `number`, optional): Enter your height in centimeters, if using the centimeter unit.
*   **Weight Unit** (`weight_unit`, `select_one`, required): Choose the unit for your weight (Pounds or Kilograms).
*   **Current Weight** (`current_weight`, `number`, required): Enter your current weight.
*   **Goal Weight** (`goal_weight`, `number`, optional): Enter your target weight, if applicable.
*   **Activity Level** (`activity_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your typical daily activity level (Sedentary, Lightly active, Moderately active, Very active, Extremely active).
*   **Fitness Goal** (`fitness_goal`, `select_one`, required): Choose your primary fitness goal (Lose weight, Maintain weight, Gain muscle, Improve athletic performance, General health).
*   **BMR Calculation** (`bmr_calculation_note`, `note`, optional): Note any factors affecting Basal Metabolic Rate calculation.
*   **TDEE Calculation** (`tdee_calculation_note`, `note`, optional): Note any factors affecting Total Daily Energy Expenditure calculation.
*   **Caloric Adjustment** (`caloric_deficit_surplus`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred caloric adjustment (Aggressive deficit, Moderate deficit, Maintenance, Small surplus, Large surplus).
*   **Macro Split Preference** (`macro_split_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred macro split (Balanced, High protein, Low carb, Moderate carb, No preference).
*   **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate any dietary restrictions you have (None, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Keto-friendly, Paleo-friendly, Other).
*   **Meals Per Day** (`meals_per_day`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how many meals you prefer to have per day (2 meals, 3 meals, 4 meals, 5-6 small meals, Intermittent fasting).
*   **Daily Water Intake** (`water_intake`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your daily water intake (Less than 4 cups, 4-6 cups, 6-8 cups, 8+ cups, Not sure).
*   **Average Sleep Hours** (`sleep_hours`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your average sleep hours per night (Less than 5 hours, 5-6 hours, 6-7 hours, 7-8 hours, 8+ hours).
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): Enter any medical conditions affecting your metabolism.
*   **Results Delivery** (`results_delivery`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you would like to receive your results (Display on screen, Email detailed report, Both).
*   **Additional Goals or Questions** (`additional_goals`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional fitness goals or questions you may have.

Please note that some fields are not required, and their answers are optional.
