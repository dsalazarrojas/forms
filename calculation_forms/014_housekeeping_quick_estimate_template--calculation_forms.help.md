# Housekeeping Quick Estimate Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a quick estimate of housekeeping services for a client. It includes fields for client name, property address, services requested, estimated duration, estimated price, site visit requirement, and contact phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Enter the property address in the "Property Address" field.
3. Select the services requested from the options provided in the "Services Requested" field.
4. Enter the estimated duration of the services in the "Estimated Duration" field.
5. Enter the estimated price of the services in the "Estimated Price" field.
6. Indicate whether a site visit is required or not in the "Site Visit Required" field.
7. Enter the contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the client.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the property where the services will be provided.
* **Services Requested** (`services_requested`, select_one, required: false): Select the services you want to provide from the options Landscaping, Pool Cleaning, or Lawn Maintenance.
* **Estimated Duration** (`estimated_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the estimated duration of the services in hours.
* **Estimated Price** (`estimated_price`, number, required: false): Enter the estimated price of the services.
* **Site Visit Required** (`site_visit_required`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether a site visit is required or not.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact phone number of the client or your contact information.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate information to generate an accurate estimate.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or refer to the original request from the client.
