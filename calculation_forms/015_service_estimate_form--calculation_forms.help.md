# Service Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Estimate Form is used to collect information from users to create a service estimate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Service Estimate Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **estimate_details** field with any additional comments or notes about the service you are requesting an estimate for.
2. Enter the **total_cost** field with the estimated cost of the service.
3. In the **service_details** field, provide more details about the service you are requesting an estimate for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **estimate_details** (Text, Optional):
	+ A field for entering any additional comments or notes about the service you are requesting an estimate for.
* **total_cost** (Number, Optional):
	+ A field for entering the estimated cost of the service.
* **service_details** (Text, Optional):
	+ A field for providing more details about the service you are requesting an estimate for. 

## Tips
- Be sure to fill in all relevant fields to provide the best possible service estimate.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the relevant personnel for assistance.
- Double-check the information provided before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
