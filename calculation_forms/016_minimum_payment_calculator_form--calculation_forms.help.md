# Minimum Payment Calculator Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to estimate the minimum payment for a given account, providing a quick and easy way to calculate key details such as minimum payment, interest charge, and total payment.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the first section with your account information (Account Name, Account Number, Account Balance).
2. Enter the interest rate and minimum payment details (Minimum Payment, Interest Rate, Minimum Interest Charge).
3. Select your payment frequency (Payment Frequency) and provide the next payment date (Next Payment).
4. Add any additional comments or contact information (Comments, Email, Phone).
5. Click the "Submit" button to calculate and view your estimated minimum payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Account Name** (text): Enter the name of your account.
* **Account Number** (number): Enter the number of your account.
* **Account Balance** (number): Enter the current balance of your account.
* **Minimum Payment** (number): Enter the minimum payment for your account.
* **Interest Rate** (number): Enter the interest rate for your account.
* **Minimum Interest Charge** (number): This field will be calculated automatically based on the minimum payment and interest rate.
* **Minimum Payment Date** (date): This field will be calculated automatically based on the minimum payment.
* **Total Interest Paid** (number): This field will be calculated automatically based on the minimum payment and interest rate.
* **Total Payment** (number): This field will be calculated automatically based on the minimum payment and interest rate.
* **Remaining Balance** (number): This field will be calculated automatically based on the minimum payment and account balance.
* **Next Minimum Payment** (number): This field will be calculated automatically based on the minimum payment and account balance.
* **Account Status** (text): Enter the current status of your account.
* **Comments** (note): Add any additional comments about your account.
* **Payment Frequency** (select one): Select whether you make payments regularly (Yes) or not (No).
* **Frequency** (text): Enter the frequency of your payments (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).
* **Next Payment** (date): Enter the next payment date.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Submit** (text): Click to calculate and view your estimated minimum payment.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to get accurate results.
* Use the calculated minimum payment and interest charge as a guideline.
* Review and update your account information regularly to ensure accuracy.
