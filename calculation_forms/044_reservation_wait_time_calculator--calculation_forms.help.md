# Reservation Wait Time Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reservation Wait Time Calculator is a tool used to estimate the wait time for guests at a restaurant based on their arrival time, the number of seats available, and the arrival rate of guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Guest Arrival Time, which is the time at which the guest arrived at the restaurant.
2. Enter the Wait Time, which is the number of minutes the guest is willing to wait for a seat to become available.
3. Enter the Seats Available, which is the number of seats available at the restaurant.
4. Enter the Arrival Rate, which is the number of Guests per Minute arriving at the restaurant.
5. Press the "Calculate" button to estimate the Guest Wait Time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Arrival Time** (`guest_arrival_time`, number, not required):
    Enter the time at which the guest arrived at the restaurant. This can be any time of day or night.
* **Wait Time** (`wait_time`, number, not required):
    Enter the number of minutes the guest is willing to wait for a seat to become available.
* **Seats Available** (`seats_available`, number, not required):
    Enter the total number of seats available at the restaurant.
* **Arrival Rate** (`seats_arrival_rate`, number, not required):
    Enter the number of guests arriving per minute.
* **Guest Wait Time** (`guest_wait_time`, number, calculated):
    This field displays the estimated wait time calculated based on the input.

## Tips
* Please enter accurate values for guest arrival time and wait time to get an accurate estimate.
* If the arrival rate is high, the wait time will be shorter.
* If the number of seats available is low, the wait time will be longer.
* This calculator provides an estimate, not an exact calculation, and may not account for all factors that affect wait times.
