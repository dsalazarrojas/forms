# return_on_investment_roi_calculation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "return_on_investment_roi_calculation_form" is designed to help you calculate the return on investment (ROI) for your projects. This form collects information about your project details, investment calculations, performance indicators, and comments to provide a clear picture of your project's financial performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your project details in the "Project Details" field.
2. Provide investment calculations in the "Investment Calculations" field.
3. Enter performance indicators related to your project in the "Performance Indicators" field.
4. Add any comments or additional information about your project in the "Comments" and "Additional Comments" fields.
5. Click "Submit" to calculate and view your project's ROI calculation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your project, including its name, location, and any relevant background information.
* **Investment Calculations** (`investment_calculations`, text, optional): Enter the total investment amount, expected return, and any other relevant financial data related to your project.
* **Performance Indicators** (`performance_indicators`, text, optional): Enter the metrics that measure your project's performance, such as profit margin, return on equity, or any other relevant financial ratios.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or context related to your project, such as challenges, successes, or lessons learned.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information not covered in the previous fields, such as project status, timeline, or future plans.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate ROI calculation.
* Review and update your project's information regularly to track its performance and progress.
* Use the calculated ROI to make informed decisions and adjust your project strategy accordingly.
