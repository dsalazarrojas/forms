# Get Logarithm (log) Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Get Logarithm (log) Form is a tool to calculate the logarithm of a given base and value.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of logarithm you want to calculate. You can choose from Common, Natural, or Decimal logarithm.
2. Enter the base of the logarithm.
3. Enter the value of the logarithm.
4. Choose a time of calculation, if desired.
5. Enter your answer to the question and whether it is correct or not.
6. Provide a reason for your answer.
7. Enter your email, phone number, and name (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Base of the logarithm** (`base`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the base of the logarithm you want to calculate. This is the number that the logarithm will be calculated on.
* **Type of logarithm base** (`logarithm_base`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose from Common, Natural, or Decimal logarithm.
* **Result of the logarithm** (`result`, `number`, required/optional): This field will display the result of the calculation.
* **Value of the logarithm** (`logarithm_value`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the value to be calculated.
* **Time of calculation** (`time_of_calculation`, `date`, required/optional): Select the time of calculation, if desired.
* **Answer to the question** (`answer`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your answer to the question.
* **Correct answer to the question** (`correct_answer`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if your answer is correct, 'No' otherwise.
* **Reason for your answer** (`reason`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a reason for your answer.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name.
