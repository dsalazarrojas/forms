# Pixel Density Calculator PPI Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pixel Density Calculator PPI Calculator is a tool used to calculate the pixel density of a screen, given its width, height, and diagonal size. This calculation is useful for understanding how closely packed the pixels are on a display, which affects the visual quality of images and text.

## How To Complete This Form
To use the Pixel Density Calculator PPI Calculator, follow these steps:

1. Enter the width of your screen in pixels.
2. Enter the height of your screen in pixels.
3. Enter the diagonal size of your screen in inches.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **width_resolution** (width_resolution, number, optional): Enter the width of your screen in pixels.
* **height_resolution** (height_resolution, number, optional): Enter the height of your screen in pixels.
* **diagonal_size_inches** (diagonal_size_inches, number, optional): Enter the diagonal size of your screen in inches.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct values for your screen's width, height, and diagonal size to get an accurate pixel density calculation.
* The pixel density is calculated based on the provided values and can provide valuable information about the display's visual quality.
