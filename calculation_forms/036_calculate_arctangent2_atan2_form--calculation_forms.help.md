# Calculate Arctangent2 (atan2) Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Calculate Arctangent2 (atan2) Form is designed to calculate the arctangent2 of two given coordinates and provide the result in a specific unit and with a chosen decimal precision. The form is intended for users who need to perform this calculation and want to customize the result with their preferred unit and precision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`user_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter your name for reference.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, Optional): Enter your email address for sending results.
* **Calculation Date** (`calculation_date`, `date`, Optional): Select the date for reference.
* **Y Coordinate** (`y_coordinate`, `number`, Required): Enter the y value for the arctangent2 calculation.
* **X Coordinate** (`x_coordinate`, `number`, Required): Enter the x value for the arctangent2 calculation.
* **Calculation Note** (`calculation_note`, `note`, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments for the calculation.
* **Angle Unit** (`angle_unit`, `select_one`, Required): Choose the unit for the result, either Radians or Degrees.
* **Decimal Precision** (`precision`, `select_one`, Optional): Choose the decimal precision for the result, with options 2, 4, 6, or 8 decimal places.
* **Purpose of Calculation** (`purpose`, `select_one`, Optional): Specify the purpose of the calculation, with options Mathematics homework, Engineering, Physics, Programming, Research, or Other.
* **Display Quadrant Information** (`quadrant_info`, `select_one`, Optional): Choose to display quadrant information, either True or False.
* **Email Results** (`email_results`, `select_one`, Optional): Choose to send the results to your email address, either True or False.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
