# Number Rounding Calculator - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help users round numbers according to various methods and precisions. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the `Input Number` field with the number you want to round.
2. Select the desired `Rounding Method` from the options. Options include:
	* `nearest` to round to the nearest whole number.
	* `down` to round down to the nearest whole number.
	* `up` to round up to the nearest whole number.
	* `half` to round to the nearest half number.
	* `half even` to round to the nearest half number, even if it's closer to two halves.
3. Choose the `Rounding Precision` from the options. Options include `none` (no rounding), 1 to 10 decimal places.
4. Provide a description of the chosen rounding method (optional).
5. If you have a second number to round, fill in the `Input Number 2` field.
6. Review the `Result` field to see the rounded number(s).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Input Number** (`input_number`, number, required): Enter the number you want to round.
* **Rounding Method** (`rounding_method`, select_one, optional): Choose the method to use for rounding.
* **Rounding Method Description** (`rounding_method_description`, text, optional): Describe the chosen rounding method for context.
* **Input Number 2** (`input_number_2`, number, optional): Enter a second number to round (if needed).
* **Result** (`result`, note, not required): The result of the rounding process will be displayed here.
