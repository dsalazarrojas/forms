# Find Tangent - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to calculate the tangent of a right-angled triangle. You will be asked to provide two angles of the triangle and the tangent of the resulting angle will be calculated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Opposite Angle" field and enter the value of the opposite side of the triangle.
2. Click on the "Adjacent Angle" field and enter the value of the adjacent side of the triangle.
3. Click on the "Tangent Angle" field and press "Calculate" to get the tangent of the resulting angle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Opposite Angle** (opposite_angle, number, required): Enter the value of the opposite side of the triangle.
* **Adjacent Angle** (adjacent_angle, number, required): Enter the value of the adjacent side of the triangle.
* **Tangent Angle** (tangent_angle, number, required): This field will display the calculated tangent of the resulting angle after you press "Calculate".
