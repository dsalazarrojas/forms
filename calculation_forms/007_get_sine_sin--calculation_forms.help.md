# Get Sine (sin) - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Get Sine (sin) form is used to calculate the sine of a given number. This form is designed to provide a quick and easy way to perform this calculation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a number in the "input-number" field. This is the value you want to calculate the sine of.
2. Choose the calculation method from the "calculation-method" dropdown menu. This will apply the chosen method to the input number.
3. Click the "Calculate" button to perform the calculation and display the result in the "result" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### input-number (number, required: false)
 Enter a number to calculate its sine.
### calculation-method (select_one, required: false)
Choose the calculation method to apply to the input number. Options include sin, cos, tan, abs, round, sqrt, pow, log, max, and min.
### result (text, required: false)
This field displays the result of the calculation.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter a valid number in the input-number field for accurate results.
- Choose the correct calculation method to get the desired result.
- The result field will display the calculated value.
