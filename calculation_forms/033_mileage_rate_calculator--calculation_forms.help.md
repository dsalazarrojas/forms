# Mileage Rate Calculator - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mileage Rate Calculator is a tool used to calculate mileage rates for vehicles. This form is designed to gather information from users to provide an accurate estimate of mileage rates based on the vehicle's base information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Base Info" field to enter the vehicle's base information.
2. Enter the necessary details in the "Base Info 1" and "Base Info 2" fields as prompted.
3. Click "Submit" to calculate the mileage rate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Base Info** (`Base Info`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's base information, such as its make and model. This field is optional but recommended for more accurate calculations.
* **Base Info 1** (`Base Info 1`, text, optional): Enter the base information (e.g., mileage capacity, engine capacity, or other relevant details). This field is optional but helps refine the mileage rate calculation.
* **Base Info 2** (`Base Info 2`, text, optional): Enter additional base information (e.g., vehicle class, fuel type, or other relevant details). This field is optional but provides further context for the mileage rate calculation.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information entered is accurate and relevant to the vehicle's characteristics.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can refer back to this help guide or consult the vehicle's owner's manual.
