# Fuel Cost Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fuel Cost Calculator is a form used to estimate fuel costs based on the distance traveled, fuel efficiency, and current fuel price. It helps users to get an idea of how much fuel will cost for a specific journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Fuel Cost Calculator, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Distance Traveled** field.
2. Enter the **Fuel Efficiency** field, representing miles per gallon (mpg).
3. Enter the **Current Fuel Price** field, in dollars per gallon (dollars/gallon).
4. The **Fuel Cost Estimate** field will automatically populate with the estimated fuel cost for the journey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Distance Traveled**: Enter the total distance you plan to travel.
* **Fuel Efficiency**: Enter your vehicle's fuel efficiency in miles per gallon (mpg).
* **Current Fuel Price**: Enter the current price of fuel in dollars per gallon.
* **Fuel Cost Estimate**: This field is automatically populated with the estimated fuel cost for the journey.

## Tips
- Ensure that the **Distance Traveled** field is accurate, as it directly affects the fuel cost estimate.
- The more fuel-efficient your vehicle, the lower the fuel cost estimate will be.
- If the current fuel price is not up-to-date, it is best to use the current market price for a more accurate estimate.
