# Gold Price Estimation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gold Price Estimation Form is designed to collect data required to estimate the price of gold. Please fill out this form to provide accurate information about your gold item to get an estimated price.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Introduction** field with a brief description of the gold item.
2. Enter the **Carat Weight** of the gold item in the corresponding field.
3. Select **Price Influence** to indicate whether the gold price is influenced by other factors.
4. Choose the **Gold Type** to select the type of gold.
5. For **Purity**, select all applicable purity types.
6. Select the **Weight Unit** to choose the unit of weight.
7. Enter the **Re-Carat Weight** if applicable (optional).
8. Enter the **Price** of the gold item (optional).
9. Fill out the **Customer Name** field with the name of the customer.
10. Enter the **Email** and **Phone** fields with the customer's contact information (optional).
11. Select the **Date and Time** of the gold price estimation (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief description of the gold item, e.g., "14K gold ring".
* **Carat Weight**: Enter the weight of the gold item, e.g., 10 carats.
* **Price Influence**: Select "Yes" if the gold price is influenced by other factors such as market trends or customer demand.
* **Gold Type**: Select the type of gold, e.g., "White Gold".
* **Purity**: Select all applicable purity types, e.g., "99.9% purity".
* **Weight Unit**: Select the unit of weight, e.g., "Grams".
* **Re-Carat Weight**: Enter the re-weight of the gold item (optional).
* **Price**: Enter the price of the gold item (optional).
* **Customer Name**: Fill out the name of the customer.
* **Email**: Enter the customer's email address (optional).
* **Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number (optional).
* **Date and Time**: Select the date and time of the gold price estimation (optional).

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately to get a successful price estimation.
* If you need help with the price estimation process, contact our customer support team.
