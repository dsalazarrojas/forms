# Inheritance Tax Calculator Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inheritance Tax Calculator Form is used to calculate tax rates and collect information about estates, beneficiaries, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide estate details on the first page (Estate Details) to help us understand the scope of the estate.
2. Enter the value of the estate on the second page (Estate Value) to aid in tax calculations.
3. Indicate if the beneficiaries will receive any incentives on the third page (Beneficiaries).
4. Specify the relationship between the executor and the estate on the fourth page (Relationship).
5. Choose the tax rate for the estate on the fifth page (Tax Rate).
6. List any executor incentives on the sixth page (Executor Incentives).
7. List any other incentives on the seventh page (Other Incentives).
8. Optionally, you can provide additional comments or information on the eighth page (Tax Calculation).
9. Add any relevant notes or comments on the ninth page (Notes).
10. Finally, confirm the form submission information on the tenth page (Form Submitted By).

* **Estate Details (text)** (`Estate Details`, optional): Enter a brief description of the estate to provide context for the calculation.
* **Estate Value (number)** (`Estate Value`, optional): Enter the value of the estate to aid in tax calculations.
* **Beneficiaries (select multiple)** (`Beneficiaries`, optional): Check if the beneficiaries will receive any incentives.

* **Executor Incentives (select multiple)** (`Executor Incentives`, optional): List any incentives the executor will receive.
* **Other Incentives (select multiple)** (`Other Incentives`, optional): List any other incentives related to the estate.
