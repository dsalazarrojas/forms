# Summer Intern Timesheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Intern Timesheet form is used by project managers to track and record the work activities and time spent by interns during their projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form title to identify the form's purpose or reference number.
2. Enter the start and finish times of your workday to track your working hours.
3. Record your lunch break time if you took one.
4. Enter the total time spent on tasks and activities.
5. Briefly describe your activities and goals.
6. List any tasks you completed during your workday.
7. Select the project you were working on from the dropdown list.
8. Review and submit the form to complete the record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description or reference number for this timesheet.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the time you started your workday.
* **Finish Time** (`finish_time`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the time you finished your workday.
* **Lunch Break** (`lunch_break`, `text`, required/optional): Record the time you took a lunch break, if any.
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the total time spent on tasks and activities.
* **Activities** (`activities`, `text`, required/optional): Briefly describe your activities and goals for the day.
* **Tasks** (`tasks`, `text`, required/optional): List any tasks you completed during your workday.
* **Project** (`project`, `select_one`, required/true): Select the project you were working on from the dropdown list.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required/optional): Review and submit the form to complete the record.
* **Project Goals** (`project_goals`, `text`, required/optional): Leave this field blank; it's not currently used.

## Tips
* Make sure to accurately track your start and finish times to ensure accurate time tracking.
* Be specific when describing your activities and tasks.
* Only select the project you actually worked on for accurate project tracking.
