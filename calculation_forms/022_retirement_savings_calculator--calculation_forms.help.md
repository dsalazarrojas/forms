# Retirement Savings Calculator - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Retirement Savings Calculator is a tool designed to help you estimate your retirement savings needs and goals. It takes into account various factors such as monthly savings amount, interest rate, retirement age, and income source to provide an accurate calculation of your retirement savings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with your information:
   - Monthly Savings Amount: Enter the amount you want to save each month.
   - Interest Rate (optional): Enter the interest rate you expect to earn on your savings.
   - Retirement Age: Enter your expected retirement age.
   - Income Source (optional): Choose the source of your income (Rent, Salary, Investments, or Other).
   - Monthly Income (optional): Enter your monthly income.
   - Retirement Income Source 2 (optional): Choose the source of your retirement income (Rent, Salary, Investments, or Other).
   - Inflation Rate 2 (optional): Enter the inflation rate for the period after retirement.
   - Years in Retirement 2 (optional): Enter the number of years you expect to be in retirement.
