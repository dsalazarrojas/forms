# Calories Diet Plan Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Calories Diet Plan Form is designed to collect information from clients to create a personalized diet plan that meets their unique needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's profile information, including their name, email, and assigned coach.
2. Choose the client's dietary lifestyle, including any restrictions they may have.
3. Select the client's preferred meal frequency and hydration goals.
4. Set the client's target daily calorie intake, protein, carbohydrate, and fat percentages.
5. Choose the client's preferred weigh-in day.
6. Add any known food allergies or disliked foods.
7. Specify any recommended supplements.
8. Set the start date for the diet plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assigned Health Coach**: Enter the name of the client's assigned health coach.
* **Client Full Name**: Enter the client's full name.
* **Client Email**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Target Daily Calorie Intake**: Enter the client's target daily calorie intake in kcal per day.
* **Protein Target %**: Enter the client's target protein percentage of total calories.
* **Carbohydrates Target %**: Enter the client's target carbohydrate percentage of total calories.
* **Fats Target %**: Enter the client's target fat percentage of total calories.
* **Preferred Number of Meals per Day**: Select the client's preferred meal frequency from the options: 2 (Intermittent Fasting), 3 (Standard), 4 (Small meals + snack), 5 (High frequency), 6 (Bodybuilding style).
* **Dietary Lifestyle - Restriction**: Select the client's dietary lifestyle from the options: No Restrictions, Vegetarian, Vegan, Paleo, Keto / Low Carb, Pescatarian, Gluten-Free.
* **Known Food Allergies**: List all ingredients the client is allergic to.
* **Disliked Foods**: List all ingredients the client dislikes or avoids.
* **Recommended Supplements**: Specify any supplements recommended for the client.
* **Daily Hydration Goal (Liters)**: Enter the client's daily hydration goal in liters.
* **Weekly Check-in Day**: Select the client's preferred weigh-in day from the options: Monday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday.
* **Plan Implementation Date**: Enter the start date of the diet plan.
* **Coach Additional Instructions**: Provide any additional instructions or tips for the client's coach.

By following these steps and understanding the purpose and explanation of each field, clients and coaches can effectively use the Calories Diet Plan Form to create a personalized and effective diet plan.
