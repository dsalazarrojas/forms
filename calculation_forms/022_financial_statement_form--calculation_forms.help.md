# Financial Statement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Statement Form is used to provide financial information for reports. This form helps users submit their reports with relevant details, including report type, date, period, category, amount, and currency.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the type of report you want to submit.
2. Select the date of the report.
3. Choose the period of the report.
4. Select the category of the report.
5. Enter the details of the report.
6. Enter the amount of the report.
7. Select the currency of the report.
8. Add any additional notes or comments for the report.
9. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Type**: Select the type of report you want to submit. You can choose between 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Report Date**: Enter the date of the report. This should be in the format of a date (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Report Period**: Select the period of the report. You can choose between 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Report Category**: Select the category of the report. You can choose between 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Report Details**: Enter any additional details about the report.
* **Report Amount**: Enter the amount of the report. This should be a numerical value.
* **Report Currency**: Select the currency of the report. You can choose between 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Report Currency2**: This field is not used in the form. You can ignore it.
* **Report Notes**: Enter any additional comments or explanations for the report.
* **Submit**: Click this button to complete and submit the form.
