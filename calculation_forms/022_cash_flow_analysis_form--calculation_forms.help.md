# Cash Flow Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cash Flow Analysis Form is a tool used to analyze the cash inflows and outflows of a business over a specific period of time. It helps businesses track and calculate their revenue, expenses, loan proceeds, and debt payments to identify areas of improvement and make informed financial decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the analysis period (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually) for which you want to perform the analysis.
2. Enter the analysis date or period for which you want to perform the analysis.
3. Fill in the cash inflow fields (Operating Cash Inflows, Sales or Service Revenue, Accounts Receivable Collected, Other Operating Income, Financing Cash Inflows, Loan Proceeds, Equity Investment or Owner Contribution) by providing numbers or amounts.
4. Fill in the cash outflow fields (Operating Cash Outflows, Cost of Goods Sold, Operating Expenses, Payroll and Wages, Capital Expenditures, Debt Payments, Tax Payments) by providing numbers or amounts.
5. Review the calculated Total Cash Inflows and Net Cash Flow fields to see the summary of your analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Analysis Date**: Enter the date or period for which you want to perform the analysis.
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business or organization.
* **Reporting Period**: Select the analysis period (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually) for which you want to perform the analysis.
* **Period From Date**: Enter the start date of the analysis period.
* **Period To Date**: Enter the end date of the analysis period.
* **Operating Cash Inflows**: Enter the amount of operating cash inflows.
* **Sales or Service Revenue**: Enter the amount of sales or service revenue.
* **Accounts Receivable Collected**: Enter the amount of accounts receivable collected.
* **Other Operating Income**: Enter any other operating income that is not included in the other fields.
* **Financing Cash Inflows**: Enter the amount of financing cash inflows.
* **Loan Proceeds**: Enter the amount of loan proceeds.
* **Equity Investment or Owner Contribution**: Enter the amount of equity investment or owner contribution.
* **Operating Cash Outflows**: Enter the amount of operating cash outflows.
* **Cost of Goods Sold**: Enter the cost of goods sold.
* **Operating Expenses**: Enter the amount of operating expenses.
* **Payroll and Wages**: Enter the amount of payroll and wages.
* **Capital Expenditures**: Enter the amount of capital expenditures.
* **Debt Payments**: Enter the amount of debt payments.
* **Tax Payments**: Enter the amount of tax payments.

Note: The Total Cash Inflows and Net Cash Flow fields are auto-calculated based on the values you input in the other fields.
