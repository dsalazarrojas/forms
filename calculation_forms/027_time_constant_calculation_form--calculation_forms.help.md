# Time Constant Calculation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Time Constant Calculation Form is a tool used to calculate the time constant of a circuit. This form is designed to assist users in determining the time constant based on various circuit types and user-inputted values.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of circuit you are working with by choosing from the options provided under "Circuit Type".
2. Enter the frequency of your circuit (if applicable) in the "Frequency" field.
3. Input the resistance in ohms of your circuit in the "Resistance" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Circuit Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of circuit you are working with. This will help us understand the specifics of the calculation. Choose from:
	+ Capacitive
	+ Inductive
	+ Resistive
	+ Radiative
- **Frequency** (number, optional): Enter the frequency of your circuit, if applicable. This field is optional and only required for certain types of circuits.
- **Resistance (Ohms)** (number, optional): Input the resistance in ohms of your circuit. This field is also optional and only required for certain types of circuits.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct circuit type to ensure accurate results.
* If you are unsure about the type of circuit or the required fields, consult the applicable formulas or seek further assistance.
* Double-check your inputs for accuracy, as incorrect values may affect the calculation results.
