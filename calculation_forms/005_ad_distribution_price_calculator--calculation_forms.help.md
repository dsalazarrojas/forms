# Ad Distribution Price Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to estimate the price of ad distribution services for businesses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the required business information in the "Business Name", "Contact Person", "Email Address", and "Phone Number" fields.
2. Select the type of advertisement from the "Type of Advertisement" dropdown menu.
3. Choose the size of the advertisement from the "Advertisement Size" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the number of units to be distributed in the "Number of Units to Distribute" field.
5. Select the distribution area from the "Distribution Area" dropdown menu.
6. Choose the color printing and paper stock quality options from the respective dropdown menus.
7. Select the distribution method from the "Distribution Method" dropdown menu.
8. Choose the turnaround time from the "Desired Turnaround Time" dropdown menu.
9. If applicable, select any special finishing options from the "Special Finishing Options" dropdown menu.
10. Enter your estimated budget range in the "Estimated Budget" field.
11. If applicable, enter the desired campaign start date in the "Desired Campaign Start Date" field.
12. If applicable, provide information on your target demographic in the "Target Demographic" field.
13. Answer the question about whether this is a promotional campaign or not.
14. Answer the question about whether you have used the service before or not.
15. Finally, add any additional requests or comments in the "Special requests or additional information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter your business name as it appears on your company's letterhead.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for the campaign.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Type of Advertisement** (`ad_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of advertisement you need (e.g., Flyers and Brochures, Digital Ads, etc.).
* **Advertisement Size** (`ad_size`, select_one, required): Choose the size of the advertisement (e.g., Small (A5), Medium (A4), etc.).
* **Number of Units to Distribute** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of units to be distributed.
* **Distribution Area** (`distribution_area`, select_one, required): Choose the distribution area (e.g., Local - Single City, Regional - Multiple Cities, etc.).
* **Color Printing** (`color_printing`, select_one, required): Choose the color printing option (e.g., Black and White, Full Color, etc.).
* **Paper Stock Quality** (`paper_stock`, select_one, required): Choose the paper stock quality (e.g., Standard (20 lb), Premium (28 lb), etc.).
* **Distribution Method** (`distribution_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose the distribution method (e.g., Direct Mail, Hand Distribution, etc.).
* **Desired Turnaround Time** (`turnaround_time`, select_one, required): Choose the turnaround time (e.g., Express - 3 days, Standard - 5-7 days, etc.).
* **Special Finishing Options** (`special_finishing`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any special finishing options (e.g., Lamination, Die Cutting, etc.).
* **Estimated Budget** (`budget_estimate`, select_one, optional): Enter your estimated budget range.
* **Desired Campaign Start Date** (`campaign_start_date`, date, required): Enter the desired campaign start date.
* **Target Demographic** (`target_demographic`, text, optional): Enter information about the target demographic (e.g., age range, interests, location).
* **Is this a promotional campaign for a special event or promotion?** (`current_promotion`, select_one, optional): Answer whether this campaign is for a special event or promotion.
* **Have you used our distribution services before?** (`previous_campaigns`, select_one, optional): Answer whether you have used the service before.
* **Special requests or additional information** (`additional_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or additional information.
