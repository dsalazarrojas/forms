# Healthy Weight Calculator 3 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthy Weight Calculator 3 is a tool for quickly determining a person's weight status in a healthy, underweight, overweight, or obesity category based on their height, weight, and age.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your height in one of the available units (meter, centimeter, or inch).
2. Enter your weight in one of the available units (kilogram, pound, or gram).
3. Enter your age.
4. Select the unit of measurement for your height and weight.
5. Click on the "Calculate" button to get your weight category.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input**: a text field for entering additional information not specified. Enter any relevant details that might affect your weight category, such as activity level or body composition.
- **Height**: Enter your height in the chosen unit of measurement.
- **Weight**: Enter your weight in the chosen unit of measurement.
- **Age**: Enter your age.
- **Height Unit**: Choose the unit of measurement for your height (meter, centimeter, or inch).
- **Weight Unit**: Choose the unit of measurement for your weight (kilogram, pound, or gram).
- **Weight Category**: Select your weight status in one of the following categories: healthy, underweight, overweight, obesity, or morbidly obese.
- **Result**: This field will display your calculated weight category.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to get a reliable result.
- If you are unsure about your height or weight unit, use the unit most commonly used in your country or region.
- If you have any underlying health condition that might affect your weight category, please consult a healthcare professional for a more accurate assessment.
