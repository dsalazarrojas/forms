# Base 10 Logarithm Of E (LOG10E) Calculation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form calculates the base-10 logarithm of the mathematical constant e, which is a fundamental constant in mathematics and physics. The form is designed to provide a user-friendly interface for users to input a number and get the base-10 logarithm result.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the "Your Name" and "Email Address" fields, respectively.
2. Input the number for which you want to calculate the base-10 logarithm in the "Input Number" field.
3. Select the purpose of your calculation from the "Purpose of this calculation" dropdown list.
4. Optionally, you can provide additional comments or context in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`user_name`, text, required): This field is for your name, which will be stored for identification purposes.
* **Email Address** (`user_email`, email, required): This field is for your email address, which will be stored for contact and notification purposes.
* **Input Number** (`input_number`, number, required): Enter the number for which you want to calculate the base-10 logarithm.
* **Purpose of this calculation** (`purpose_of_calculation`, select_one, optional): Select the purpose of your calculation from the dropdown list. Options are: Academic Research, Professional Engineering, Statistical Analysis, and Personal Interest.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or context about your calculation.
* **Input Number** (`log10e_constant_used`, number, optional): This field displays the value of the constant e used in the calculation, approximately 0.43429.
* **Date of Calculation** (`calculation_date`, date, required): Enter the date and time of your calculation, which is required for record-keeping purposes.
* **Constant Used (Log10 of e)** (`log10e_constant_used`, number, optional): This field displays the base-10 logarithm of the constant e, calculated based on the input number.
* **This form calculates the Base-10 logarithm based on the entered value.** (`calculation_method_note`, note, required): This is a note that explains the calculation method used in this form. 

## Tips
