# Rental Yield Calculation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Yield Calculation Form is designed to collect information from users to calculate rental yield, a key metric in the rental property management industry. This form will help you determine the profitability of your rental property by gathering data on its income and expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Input section with your personal details.
2. Provide property details in the Property Details section.
3. Enter income and expense data in the Income and Expense section.
4. Review your calculation result in the Calculation Result section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input (Required)**: This section is where you enter your personal details.
* **Property Details (Optional)**: Enter your property's details such as its type, location, and size.
* **Income and Expense (Optional)**: Enter your property's income and expense data, including rent, utilities, and other relevant costs.
* **Calculation Result (Required)**: Review your calculated rental yield result here.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information in the User Input and Property Details sections to get an accurate calculation result.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to your property's financial records or consult with a real estate professional.
* Double-check your calculation result for any errors or discrepancies.
