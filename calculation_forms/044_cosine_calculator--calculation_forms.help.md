# Cosine Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to input the adjacent angle and the hypotenuse of a right-angled triangle, then calculate its cosine using the Cosine formula. It is designed to provide a quick and convenient way to perform these calculations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the adjacent angle in the adjacent angle field. This is the angle between the adjacent side and the hypotenuse of the triangle.
2. Fill in the hypotenuse in the hypotenuse field. This is the longest side of the triangle opposite the right angle.
3. Click the "Calculate" button to perform the calculation and display the result.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Adjacent angle** (`adjacent_angle`, `number`, required): The angle between the adjacent side and the hypotenuse of the triangle. Please enter this value in degrees.
* **Hypotenuse** (`hypotenuse`, `number`, required): The longest side of the triangle opposite the right angle. Please enter this value in degrees.
* **Cosine** (`cosine_angle`, `number`, required): This is the calculated cosine of the adjacent angle. The result will be displayed after clicking the "Calculate" button.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the values in degrees, as this is what the calculator expects.
* If you need to calculate the cosine of an angle, you can use this form to quickly get your answer.
* If you need to double-check or verify your results, you can re-enter the values and click the "Calculate" button again.
