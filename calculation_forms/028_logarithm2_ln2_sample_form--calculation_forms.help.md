# Logarithm2 (LN2) Sample Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Logarithm2 (LN2) Sample Form is used to calculate the natural logarithm of 2, a fundamental concept in mathematics. This form is designed for educational purposes, allowing users to input a number and see the result of the natural logarithm of 2.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a number in the "Number to Log" field. This is the value for which you want to calculate the natural logarithm of 2.
2. Click "Calculate" to submit the form and see the result in the "Log Result" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Number to Log** (number, required): Enter the number for which you want to calculate the natural logarithm of 2. The result will be displayed in the "Log Result" field.
* **Log Result** (number, optional): This field displays the result of the natural logarithm of 2, calculated from the input in the "Number to Log" field.
