# tenancy_clean_form_home_enterprises - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for businesses that offer home cleaning services to collect client information and schedule cleanings efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as **required** in the field descriptions below).
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (tenant_info)**: A field for the client's name and other identifying information. This is an optional field.
* **Property Address (property_details)**: Enter the address of the property where the cleaning service will be performed.
* **Cleaning Services (cleaning_details)**: Select the specific cleaning services you require, such as general cleaning or specialized services like window cleaning.
* **Availability (availability)**: Choose the available timeslot for the cleaning service from the options 9am-5pm, 8am-12pm, or 1pm-7pm.
* **Start Date (start_date)**: Select the date when you want the cleaning service to begin.
* **End Date (end_date)**: Select the date when you want the cleaning service to end.
* **Message to us (client_message)**: Enter any additional comments or messages for the cleaning staff.
* **Contact Information (contact_info)**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your cleaning service.

## Tips
- Please double-check that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you provide accurate information for each field to avoid any issues with the cleaning service.
- If you have any special requests or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us through the "Message to us" field.
