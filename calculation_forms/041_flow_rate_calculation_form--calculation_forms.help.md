# Flow Rate Calculation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to calculate the flow rate in a piping system. To calculate the flow rate, you need to provide the required parameters, including piping size, fluid density, flow rate, pressure drop, and pipe length.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate page (if multiple pages are available).
2. Fill in the necessary information for each field, making sure to provide accurate data for each field.
3. Submit the form to get the calculated flow rate result.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Piping Size (Page 1)
* **Piping size**: A measure of the pipe's diameter or diameter (`<number>`): This field determines the size of the pipe being used in the piping system.

### Fluid Density (Page 1)
* **Fluid density** (Fluid density): A measure of the fluid's mass per unit volume (`<number>`): This field determines the density of the fluid being transported through the pipe.

### Flow Rate (Page 1)
* **Flow rate**: The volume of fluid flowing through the pipe per unit time (`<number>`): This field is the result of the calculation, and you should enter the expected flow rate.

### Pressure Drop (Page 1)
* **Pressure drop**: The difference in pressure between the inlet and outlet of the pipe (`<number>`): This field represents the pressure loss or gain in the pipe due to friction, etc.

### Pipe Length (Page 1)
* **Pipe length**: The length of the pipe (`<number>`): This field determines the distance the fluid travels through the pipe.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your input data for accuracy.
* Ensure that the units for each field are consistent with the expected results.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with a qualified expert or seek additional guidance.
