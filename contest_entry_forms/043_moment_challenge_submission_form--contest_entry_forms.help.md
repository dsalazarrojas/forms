# Moment Challenge Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Moment Challenge Submission Form is a form used to collect participant information and entry details for the Moment Challenge contest.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Participant Details**: Fill out the participant details section by providing your name, email, phone number, address, and other relevant information.
2. **Entry Details**: Enter the details of your entry, including the title, description, date, time, participant photo, and video.
3. **Consent**: Confirm your consent for the contest terms and conditions by providing the required information.
