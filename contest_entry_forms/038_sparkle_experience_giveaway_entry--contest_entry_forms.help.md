# Sparkle Experience Giveaway Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to enter the "Sparkle Experience Giveaway" contest. Fill out this form to participate in the contest and, optionally, refer other people to join as well.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: "Email", "Terms and Conditions", and "Giveaway".
2. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Check the "Terms and Conditions" field to confirm you agree to the contest rules.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Giveaway" field to indicate your participation status.
5. Optionally, fill out any other fields to provide additional information about your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Fill out your email address to participate in the contest. Please ensure the address is valid and up-to-date.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms-conditions`, `select_multiple`, required): Check "Yes" to confirm you agree to the contest rules.
* **Giveaway** (`giveaway`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to participate in the giveaway.
* **Entry Date** (`entry-date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you entered the contest.
* **Entry Time** (`entry-time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you entered the contest.
* **Entry Duration** (`duration`, `number`, required): Enter the duration of your participation in the contest.
* **Entry Type** (`type`, `text`, required): Enter the type of entry or participation you are submitting.
* **Status** (`status`, `text`, required): Enter the status of your entry.
* **Referral** (`referral`, `text`, required): If you referred anyone, enter their name or email address.
* **Referred By** (`referred-by`, `text`, required): If you were referred, enter the person's name or email address who referred you.
* **Given** (`given`, `text`, required): Enter any relevant information about what you were given or received.
* **Referral Code** (`referral-code`, `text`, required): If you have a referral code, enter it here.
* **Referral URL** (`referral-url`, `text`, required): If you have a referral URL, enter it here.
* **Giveaway Status** (`status`, `text`, required): Enter the status of your participation in the giveaway.
* **Given By** (`given-by`, `text`, required): Enter any information about who gave you the entry or participation opportunity.
* **Verified** (`verified`, `text`, required): Check if you have been verified as a participant.
* **Last Seen** (`last-seen`, `date`, required): Enter the last time you participated in or interacted with the contest.
* **Last Seen Time** (`last-seen-time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the last time you interacted with the contest.
* **Last Seen Duration** (`last-seen-duration`, `number`, required): Enter the duration since your last interaction with the contest.
* **Given By Again** (`given-by-again`, `text`, required): Enter any information about who gave you the entry or participation opportunity again.
* **Given By Verified** (`given-by-verified`, `text`, required): Check if you have been verified as a participant again.
* **Referral Count** (`referral-count`, `number`, required): Enter the number of people you have referred.
* **Given By Referrals** (`given-by-referrals`, `text`, required): Enter any information about the people you have referred.
