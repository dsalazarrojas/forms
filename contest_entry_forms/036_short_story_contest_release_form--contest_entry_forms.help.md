# Short Story Contest Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants in a short story contest. It is used to gather details about the participant's entry, their permission to participate, and contact information for the school or guardian.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Ensure you have obtained the required permissions from the participant and parent/guardian before submitting the form.
3. Verify that the participant's age group is accurate.
4. Fill in the contact information for the school or guardian.
5. Enter the entry details and status.
6. Confirm the participant's name, email, and contact information.
7. Enter the participant's address and zip code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (*): Enter the participant's full name.
* **Participant Age Group**: Select the accurate age group of the participant.
* **Participant Age Group Options:**
	+ Under 12: The participant is under 12 years old.
	+ 13-15: The participant is 13 to 15 years old.
	+ 16-18: The participant is 16 to 18 years old.
	+ 19-21: The participant is 19 to 21 years old.
	+ 22-25: The participant is 22 to 25 years old.
	+ 26 or Older: The participant is 26 years old or older.
* **Participant Consent**: Select 'Yes' if the participant consents to participate in the contest.
* **Parent/Guardian Consent**: Select 'Yes' for both Mother or Father.
* **Participant Contact Info**: Enter the participant's contact information.
* **School Info**: Enter the school's contact information.
* **Entry Status**: Select the status of the entry.
	+ Incomplete: The entry is not complete.
	+ Complete: The entry is complete.
	+ In Progress: The entry is in progress.
* **Participant Address**: Enter the participant's address.
* **Participant Zip Code**: Enter the participant's zip code.
* **Entry Date**: Enter the date of entry.
* **Entry Time**: Enter the time of entry.

Please note that this form is used to gather important information, and all fields must be filled in accurately and completely. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the contest organizers for clarification.
