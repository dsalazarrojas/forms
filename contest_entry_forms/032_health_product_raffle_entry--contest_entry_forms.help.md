# Health Product Raffle Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to enter a raffle for a health product. It requires customers to provide their demographic details, entry method, and agreement with terms and privacy policies.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter your zip code in the Zip Code field.
6. Enter your birth date in the Birth Date field. Please note that this field is mandatory.
7. Select your preferred product from the Product field.
8. Enter your entry method in the Entry Method field. This could be through online, app, in-store, or other means.
9. Indicate how you would like to be contacted in the How did you enter? field.
10. Agree to the terms and conditions by selecting Agree in the Agree Terms field.
11. Agree to the privacy policy by selecting Agree in the Agree Privacy field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you use to receive communications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number that we may use to contact you.
* **Zip Code** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code or postal code as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required): Enter your birth date as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Product** (`product`, select_one, required): Select the health product you would like to enter the raffle for.
* **Entry Method** (`how_did_you_enter`, select_multiple, required): Select the method through which you entered this raffle.
* **How did you hear?** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, required): Indicate how you heard about this raffle.
* **How Prophets** (`how_prophets`, select_one, required): Indicate how you heard about this raffle (this field seems to be a duplicate of `how_did_you_hear`).
* **Entry Time** (`entry_time`, time, required): Enter the time you entered this raffle.
* **Email Opt-in** (`email_opt_in`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you agree to receive email communications from us.
* **Agree Terms** (`agree_terms`, select_one, required): Agree to the terms and conditions of the raffle.
* **Agree Privacy** (`agree_privacy`, select_one, required): Agree to the privacy policy of the raffle.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your age as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required): Select your country of residence as it appears on your official identification documents.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Ensure that you agree to the terms and conditions and privacy policy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
