# Music School Competition Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to enter their music school competition tournament. It collects information from students and their parents/guardians to evaluate their eligibility and participation in the tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the Student Information section, which includes details about your name, age, and contact information.
2.  If you are under 18 years old, please ensure that you have your parent/guardian's permission before proceeding.
3.  Once you have completed the Student Information section, you can fill out the Parent Information section with your parent/guardian's details.
4.  Finally, complete the Music Information section, which includes information about your music-related activities and experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Information
*   **Name** (`Student Name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Age** (`Age`, `text`, required): Enter your age.
*   **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, optional): Enter your email address, phone number, or any other way to contact you.

### Parent Information
*   **Name** (`Parent Name`, `text`, required): Enter your parent/guardian's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
*   **Relationship** (`Relationship`, `text`, optional): Describe your relationship to the student (e.g., mother, father, guardian).
*   **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, optional): Enter your parent/guardian's email address, phone number, or any other way to contact them.

### Music Information
*   **Instrument** (`Instrument`, `text`, optional): List the instruments you play or have played in the past.
*   **Experience** (`Experience`, `text`, optional): Describe your music experience or any relevant music training.
*   **Music Achievements** (`Music Achievements`, `text`, optional): List any notable music achievements or awards you have received.
