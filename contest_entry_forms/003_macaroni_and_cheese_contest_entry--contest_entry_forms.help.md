# Macaroni And Cheese Contest Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for contestants to enter a Macaroni and Cheese contest.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: This field is required. Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
*   **Last Name**: This field is required. Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.
*   **Email**: This field is required. Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
*   **Phone**: This field is required. Enter your phone number in the international format (e.g., +12345678901).
*   **Photo**: This field is optional. Select 'Yes' if you have a photo to upload.
*   **Recipe**: This field is required. Write the name of your Macaroni and Cheese recipe.
*   **Category**: This field is required. Select all categories that apply to your recipe (e.g., vegetarian, vegan, Italian).
*   **Cooking Time**: This field is required. Enter the cooking time in minutes.
*   **Ingredients**: This field is required. List all the ingredients used in your recipe.
*   **Instructions**: This field is required. Write a brief description of your recipe, including preparation and cooking instructions.
*   **Entry Date**: This field is required. Enter the date you submitted this entry.
*   **Entry Time**: This field is required. Enter the time you submitted this entry.
*   **Participant Name**: This field is required. Enter your name as it is written on your identification.
*   **Participant Email**: This field is required. Enter a valid email address for you.
*   **Participant Phone**: This field is required. Enter your phone number in the international format (e.g., +12345678901).
*   **Participant Photo**: This field is optional. Select 'Yes' if you have a photo to upload.
*   **Participant Category**: This field is required. Select all categories that apply to you (e.g., chef, food blogger, food enthusiast).
*   **Recipe Title**: This field is required. Enter the name of your Macaroni and Cheese recipe.
*   **Recipe Description**: This field is required. Enter a brief description of your recipe.
*   **Tags**: This field is required. Enter any relevant tags for your recipe (e.g., comfort food, pasta, cheese).
*   **Social Media**: This field is required. Select all social media platforms you are active on.
*   **Participant Tags**: This field is required. Enter any relevant tags for you (e.g., foodie, chef, blogger).
*   **Participant Social Media**: This field is required. Select all social media platforms you are active on.
*   **Entry Photo**: This field is optional. Select 'Yes' if you have a photo to upload.
*   **Entry Video**: This field is optional. Select 'Yes' if you have a video to upload.

### Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields.
*   Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
*   Ensure your recipe title and description accurately describe your entry.
*   Be honest about your social media presence.
*   Be honest about your participant tags and categories.
*   Be clear and concise in your instructions.
*   Ensure your cooking time is accurate.
*   Ensure you have permission to use any images or videos you upload.
*   Be respectful of other participants and their recipes.
*   Follow all rules and guidelines for the contest.
