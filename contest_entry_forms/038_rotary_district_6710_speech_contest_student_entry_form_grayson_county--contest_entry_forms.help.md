# rotary_district_6710_speech_contest_student_entry_form_grayson_county - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information from students in the Rotary District 6710 speech contest from Grayson County.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name.
2. Provide your school's name.
3. Enter your email address (formatted as `username@example.com`).
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Enter your school's name (this field should match the school's name you provided in step 2).
6. Enter your school's address.
7. Select your grade level.
8. Enter your club's name.
9. Enter the name of your school district.
10. Select whether your club has a club type (if applicable).
11. Select whether your club is interested in specific speech topics (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., ID, passport).
* **school_name**: Enter your school's name as it is officially known.
* **name**: Enter your full name (same as above).
* **email**: Enter your email address in the format `username@example.com`.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number in the format `123-456-7890`.
* **school_district**: Enter your school's district name as it is officially known.
* **school_address**: Enter your school's complete address.
* **grade_level**: Select your grade level (e.g., 6th, 7th, 8th, etc.).
* **club_name**: Enter your club's name as it is officially known.
* **district_name**: Enter your school district's name as it is officially known.
* **school_district**: Enter your school district's name as it is officially known.
* **club_type**: Select the type of club you are affiliated with (if applicable).
* **speech_topic**: Select the speech topics your club is interested in (if applicable).

## Tips
* Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form to ensure your entry is processed correctly.
* Double-check your entries for errors before submission.
