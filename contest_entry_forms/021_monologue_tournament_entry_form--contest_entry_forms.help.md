# Monologue Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monologue Tournament Entry Form is used to collect information from users about their monologue preferences and additional files they would like to include in the tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address to ensure we can contact you about your entry.
2. Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
3. Provide a short description of your monologue.
4. Select one monologue from the provided options.
5. If you have additional monologue files, select them from the list.
6. Enter a description of any additional monologue files you selected.
7. If you have other monologue files, you can upload them as a note.
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the tournament.
* **Email** (required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your entry.
* **Phone** (required): Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Description** (optional): Provide a short description of your monologue.
* **Select One Monologue** (required): Choose one of the available monologues to enter into the tournament.
* **Select Multiple Monologues** (optional): If you have additional monologues you would like to include, select them from the list.
* **Date of Monologue** (optional): Enter the date you plan to perform the monologue.
* **Time of Monologue** (optional): Enter the time you plan to perform the monologue.
* **Uploaded Monologue** (optional): If you have a monologue to upload, you can include it as a note.
* **Selected Monologue Files** (optional): If you have additional monologue files, select them from the list.
* **Selected Monologue Files Other** (optional): Enter any additional monologue files you would like to include.
* **Selected Monologue Files Other Description** (optional): Enter a description of any additional monologue files you selected.
* **Uploaded Monologue Other** (optional): If you have other monologue files, you can upload them as a note.
* **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to complete your entry.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your entry is complete.
* Use a brief description for your monologue and any additional files you include.
* Be accurate when entering your contact information to ensure we can contact you about your entry.
