# spin_to_win_participation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Spin to Win" participation form is used to collect information from participants in the contest. This form helps us track the progress and status of participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information, including your name and contact details.
2. Provide your contact number for easy communication.
3. Select the date and time you participated in the contest.
4. Indicate whether you have participated in the contest before.
5. Choose your participation status and type.
6. Enter the date and time you submitted your entry.
7. Provide any additional information about your participation.
8. Confirm your eligibility to participate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info**: Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact details for us to reach out to you.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for easy communication.
* **Participation Date**: Select the date you participated in the contest.
* **Participation Time**: Select the time you participated in the contest.
* **Participated Since**: Indicate whether you have participated in the contest before.
* **Participated Until**: Indicate whether your participation is currently active or inactive.
* **Participation Type**: Select all types of participation that apply to you (e.g., "Yes" for multiple types).
* **Entry Date**: Enter the date you submitted your entry.
* **Entry Time**: Select the time you submitted your entry.
* **Participation Reason**: Enter any reason for your participation.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the contest.
* **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information about your participation.
* **Participation Status**: Confirm your current participation status.
* **Eligibility**: Indicate your eligibility to participate in the contest.
* **Participation Status Updated**: Confirm whether your participation status has been updated.
