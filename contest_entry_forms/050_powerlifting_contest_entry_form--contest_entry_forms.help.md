# Powerlifting Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Powerlifting Contest Entry Form is designed to collect information from powerlifters who want to participate in a contest. This form is essential for verifying contestant eligibility and capturing necessary contact information for communication and emergency purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, and Email address.
3. Provide your Address, Phone number, and Emergency Contact information.
4. Review and acknowledge the First Page Rules.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title (Text):** Enter the title of the contest you are applying for.
* **First Name (Text):** Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name (Text):** Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth (Date):** Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email (Email):** Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone (Text):** Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Address (Text):** Enter your complete address for emergency contact purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Name (Text):** Enter the name of the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (Text):** Describe your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, or spouse).
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Text):** Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address (Text):** Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **First Page Rules (Text):** Review and acknowledge the rules on the first page, which includes important information about the contest.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact information].
