# social_media_contest_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about users who have entered a social media contest. It's used to track participation and engagement on different social media platforms. Please make sure you have entered the correct information before submitting the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the social media platform you participated on.
2. Fill in your name and user ID (if required).
3. Enter your email address.
4. If you participated on a social media platform, enter your handle or username.
5. If relevant, indicate which contest you entered.
6. Choose the date you participated in the contest.
7. (Optional) Select the time you participated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facebook** (page 1, social_media_platform):
	+ This field is a select-one field that asks if you participated on Facebook.
* **Name** (page 1, name):
	+ Enter your full name, as it appears on your profile.
* **User ID** (page 1, username):
	+ Enter your user ID or username, if it's required by the social media platform.
* **Email** (page 1, email):
	+ Enter your email address, as it's used for communication and registration.
* **Social Media Handle** (page 1, social_media_handle):
	+ If you participated on a social media platform, enter your handle or username.
* **Contest** (page 1, contest):
	+ If you participated in a specific contest, enter its name or code.
* **Date Entered** (page 2, date_entered):
	+ Choose the date you participated in the contest, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Entered** (page 2, time_entered):
	+ If relevant, select the time you participated in the contest, in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
