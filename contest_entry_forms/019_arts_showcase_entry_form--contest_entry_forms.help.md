# Arts Showcase Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Arts Showcase Entry Form is designed to gather essential information about an artist's artwork for evaluation and showcase purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entrant Full Name** (`entrant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in the showcase.
* **Age Category** (`age_category`, select_one, required): Select the category that best describes your age group:
	+ Youth - under 18
	+ Emerging Artist - 18 to 25
	+ Professional/Adult
	+ Senior - 65 plus
* **Title of the Artwork** (`entry_title`, text, required): Enter a unique title for your artwork that accurately describes it.
* **Medium and Technique Used** (`medium_technique`, text, required): Specify the medium and technique used to create your artwork, e.g., Watercolor on Paper, Bronze Casting.
* **Dimensions of Entry** (`dimensions_entry`, text, required): Enter the height, width, and depth of your artwork in inches (H x W x D).
* **Weight in pounds** (`weight_if_applicable`, number, optional): If your artwork is a sculpture or sculpture-like, enter its weight in pounds. (Note: This field is not required, but please enter the weight if applicable.)
* **Short Artist Statement** (`artist_statement_short`, text, required): Describe the inspiration behind your artwork in a brief statement.
* **Link to Artist Portfolio or Website** (`portfolio_link`, text, optional): Share a link to your online portfolio or website where your artwork can be viewed.
* **Has the entry fee been paid** (`fee_payment_status`, select_one, required): Select how you have paid the entry fee:
	+ Yes, via Online Portal
	+ Yes, by Check/Cash
	+ Not Yet
* **I agree to have my work displayed for the duration of the showcase** (`display_consent`, select_one, required): Select to agree or disagree to have your work displayed for the duration of the showcase:
	+ I Agree
	+ I Disagree
* **Sale Price if for Sale** (`sale_price`, text, optional): Enter the sale price of your artwork if it is for sale. If not for sale, enter NFS.

Please note that all fields marked as required must be filled out for the entry to be accepted. Ensure that you complete all required fields accurately and correctly.
