# Powerlifting Championships Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants interested in participating in the Powerlifting Championships. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To start the application process, fill out each section of this form carefully and completely. Make sure to provide clear and correct information for each field. If you have any questions or need clarification, do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data**: A brief description of your powerlifting experience and goals. This is where you can describe your background in powerlifting, your experience, and what motivates you to participate in the championships.
* **Championships**: A text field for any specific championships you are interested in (e.g., individual or team, specific weight classes, etc.).
* **Personal Info**: A text field for any additional personal information you want to share with us (e.g., your name, contact information, etc.).
* **Contact Info**: A text field for contact information (e.g., email, phone number, etc.).
* **Body Measures**: A text field for your body measurements (e.g., height, weight, etc.).
