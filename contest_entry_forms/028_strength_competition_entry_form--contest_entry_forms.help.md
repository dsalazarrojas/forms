# Strength Competition Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strength Competition Entry Form is designed to collect essential information from participants in a strength competition. The form is used to gather details about the participant and ensure accurate registration for the competition.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Enter Your Personal Details

- **First Name**: Enter your first name.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
- **Email**: Enter your valid email address, as this will be used for communication regarding the competition. (Required)
- **Weight**: Enter your weight in kg. (Required)
- **Age**: Enter your age. (Optional)
- **Height**: Enter your height in cm. (Optional)

### Step 2: Choose Your Division

- **Division**: Select the division you wish to participate in.
- **Division Description**: Enter a brief description of your division. (Required)

### Step 3: Select Competition Details

- **Competition Date**: Enter the date of the competition.
- **Competition Time**: Enter the time of the competition.
- **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool you have been assigned for the competition. (Optional)
- **Notes**: Add any additional information about yourself. (Optional)

## Tips

- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and completely.
- Double-check your details for any errors before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the competition organizers for assistance.
