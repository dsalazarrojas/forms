# dog_challenge_awards_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "dog_challenge_awards_submission" form is a submission form for a dog challenge or competition. Use this form to submit your dog's entry, providing necessary information about your dog and yourself.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the contest entry information, including the pet owner's details and the dog's characteristics.
2. Provide photos and any additional supporting documentation for the entry.
3. Choose the award category and program for your entry.
4. Specify your contact information, including email and phone number.
5. Review and submit the form once you have filled out all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contest Entry** (`contest_entry`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the contest entry or a brief description of your dog's participation in the challenge.
* **Pet Owner** (`pet_owner`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and relevant details of the pet's owner.
* **Pet Photo** (`pet_photo`, text, required/optional): Upload or enter a link to a photo of the pet.
* **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`, text, required/optional): Enter the breed of the pet.
* **Dog Name** (`dog_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the dog.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter a phone number for contact purposes.
* **Entry Details** (`entry_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the entry.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submission Photo** (`submission_photo`, text, required/optional): Upload or enter a link to a photo of the submission.
* **Award Program** (`award_program`, text, required/optional): Enter the award program associated with the entry.
* **Award Category** (`award_category`, text, required/optional): Enter the category of the award.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Trainer** (`trainer`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the trainer or handler.
* **Pet Brand** (`pet_brand`, text, required/optional): Enter the brand of the pet.
* **Entry Status** (`entry_status`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the status of the entry (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
* **Entry Comments** (`entry_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any comments or remarks about the entry.
* **Entry Photo** (`entry_photo`, text, required/optional): Upload or enter a link to a photo of the entry.
* **Entry Video** (`entry_video`, text, required/optional): Upload or enter a link to a video of the entry.
* **Entry Audio** (`entry_audio`, text, required/optional): Upload or enter a link to an audio file of the entry.
* **Entry Document** (`entry_document`, text, required/optional): Upload or enter a link to a document related to the entry.
* **Pet Age** (`pet_age`, number, required/optional): Enter the age of the pet.
* **Pet Weight** (`pet_weight`, number, required/optional): Enter the weight of the pet.
* **Pet Gender** (`pet_gender`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the gender of the pet (e.g., "Yes", "No").
