# Public Speaking Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Speaking Tournament Entry Form is a collection of fields designed to gather information from users who wish to participate in the public speaking tournament. The form is used to capture basic contact information and preferences for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill in the provided fields with your relevant information. You can skip any fields that do not apply to you. Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your entry is successfully submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Optional): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address. Please use a valid email format (e.g., `username@example.com`).
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number. You can use the international format (e.g., `123-456-7890`).
- **Time** (`time`, time, Optional): Select your preferred time for the tournament. If no specific time is selected, it will be assigned randomly.
- **Date** (`date`, date, Optional): Select the date you would like to participate in the tournament.
- **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, Optional): Choose one of the available options for your participation.
- **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose one or more of the available options for your participation.
- **Note** (`note`, note, Optional): Enter any additional information you would like to provide about your entry.
- **Category** (`category`, text, Optional): Enter your category for the event.
- **School** (`school`, text, Optional): Enter your school affiliation.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill in all required fields and review your submission before submitting it. Ensure that your email and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
