# Win A Beauty Treatment Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help us understand your preferences for a beauty treatment contest. Please fill out the form accurately and consistently to be eligible for the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your details.
2. Choose your preferred treatment type from the list.
3. Select the services you are interested in.
4. Enter the date and time you are available for the treatment.
5. Add any additional comments or details in the "Additional Comments" field.
6. Enter your email address to confirm your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entry Form** (`id: 1`, required/true): Enter your name and any other details you'd like to share.
* **Treatment Type** (`id: 2`, required/false): Choose the type of treatment you are interested in by selecting one of the options: Facial, Waxing, or Massage.
* **Services Offered** (`id: 3`, required/false): Select the services you are interested in by selecting multiple options: Facial, Waxing, Massage, Chemical Peel, or Microdermabrasion.
* **Date and Time** (`id: 4`, required/true): Choose a date and time you are available for the treatment. Please use the format: YYYY-MM-DD for date and HH:MM for time.
* **Additional Comments** (`id: 6`, required/false): Add any additional comments or details about your entry.
* **Email** (`id: 9`, required/true): Enter your email address to confirm your entry.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and consistently to be eligible for the contest.
* Make sure to choose your preferred treatment type and services offered truthfully.
* Enter a valid date and time for your treatment.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy.
* If you have any additional comments or details, please use the "Additional Comments" field to provide more information.
