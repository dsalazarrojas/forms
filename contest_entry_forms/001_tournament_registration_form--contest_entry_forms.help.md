# Tournament Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users to register for a tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Tournament Name** field with the name of the tournament you want to register for.
2. Enter the **Team Name** field with the name of your team.
3. Select the **Team Size** field to indicate the number of players on your team.
4. Enter the number of **Player Count** in the field.
5. Enter your **Email** address in the format of `example@example.com`.
6. Enter your **Phone Number** in the format of `123-456-7890`.
7. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the `MM/DD/YYYY` format.
8. Select the **Start Date** and **End Date** fields to indicate the start and end dates of the tournament.
9. Enter your **Address** in the field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tournament Name**: Enter the name of the tournament you want to register for.
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of your team.
* **Team Size**: Select the number of players on your team.
* **Player Count**: Enter the number of players on your team.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the format `example@example.com`.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the format `123-456-7890`.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the `MM/DD/YYYY` format.
* **Start Date**: Select the start date of the tournament.
* **End Date**: Select the end date of the tournament.
* **Address**: Enter your address.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in each field.
* Use the correct format for each field (e.g., `MM/DD/YYYY` for dates).
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If your team is already registered, please do not complete this form.
