# sculpture_tournament_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from artists participating in a sculpture tournament. It is intended to provide a standardized way for artists to submit their entries and for organizers to collect and review the necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information about your art piece.
2. Start with the first page, "Entry Details".
3. On the "Entry Details" page, fill in the required information:
	* **Entry Details**: Enter a brief description of your sculpture.
4. Proceed to the next page, "Artist Details".
5. On the "Artist Details" page, fill in the required information:
	* **Artist Details**: Enter your name and contact information.
6. Finally, move on to the "Artwork Details" page.
7. On the "Artwork Details" page, fill in the required information:
	* **Artwork Details**: Enter a detailed description of your sculpture, including its title, medium, size, and any relevant background information.
8. Review your completed form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entry Details** (`entry_details`, text, required/optional): A brief description of your sculpture, such as its title, a short summary, or any other relevant information.
* **Artist Details** (`artist_details`, text, required/optional): Your name and contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Artwork Details** (`artwork_details`, text, required/optional): A detailed description of your sculpture, including its title, medium, size, and any other relevant information.

## Tips
* Make sure to proofread your form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors or inaccuracies.
* Ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information about your sculpture and your contact details.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the form administrators.
* Remember to keep your answers concise and clear, and avoid uploading any attachments or images unless specifically requested.
