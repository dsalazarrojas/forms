# Leaderboard Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leaderboard Participation Form is used to collect information from participants in a leaderboard competition, allowing us to track and display their entries and details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Leaderboard Participation Form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand each field's purpose.
2. Enter your entry details in the corresponding fields, such as `entry_details`.
3. Provide your participant details, including any necessary information.
4. Confirm your participation by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the `confirm_participation` field.
5. Indicate your consent to participate by selecting 'True' or 'False' in the `participant_consent` field.
6. Choose whether you would like your entry to be displayed on the leaderboard or not.
7. Enter any additional entry details, such as `entry_details_2`, `entry_details_3`, `entry_details_4`.
8. Enter the leaderboard name and link, if applicable.
9. Enter the category of your entry, if applicable.
10. Enter your entry ID, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **entry_details** (`entry_details`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your entry.
* **participant_details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): Enter any additional participant information.
* **confirm_participation** (`confirm_participation`, select_one, required: false): Confirm your participation status by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **participant_consent** (`participant_consent`, select_one, required: false): Indicate your consent to participate by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
* **leaderboard_display** (`leaderboard_display`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether you want your entry to be displayed on the leaderboard.
* **entry_type** (`entry_type`, text, required: false): Enter the type of entry you are submitting.
* **entry_details_2** (`entry_details_2`, text, required: false): Enter additional entry details, if necessary.
* **entry_details_3** (`entry_details_3`, text, required: false): Enter more entry details, if needed.
* **entry_details_4** (`entry_details_4`, text, required: false): Enter further entry details, if applicable.
* **entry_details_5** (`entry_details_5`, text, required: false): Enter even more entry details, if necessary.
* **leaderboard_name** (`leaderboard_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the leaderboard you are participating in.
* **leaderboard_link** (`leaderboard_link`, text, required: false): Enter the link to the leaderboard, if applicable.
* **category** (`category`, text, required: false): Enter the category of your entry, if applicable.
* **entry_id** (`entry_id`, text, required: false): Enter your entry ID, if applicable.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Review your entries for completeness and accuracy.
* If you have any questions, contact the competition organizers for assistance.
