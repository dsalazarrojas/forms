# Barrel Race Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Barrel Race Entry Form" is designed to collect information from riders to register for the barrel racing competition. This form is intended for riders who wish to participate in the event.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "Rider Full Name" field with the name of the rider participating in the competition.
2. Enter the "Rider Age" in the corresponding field.
3. Input the "Horse Name" in the field labeled as such.
4. Choose the correct "Competition Division" from the provided options.
5. Provide your "Email Address" for entry confirmation and updates.
6. Enter your "Phone Number" for emergency contact purposes.
7. Enter the name of the "Emergency Contact" in case of an accident.
8. Provide the "Emergency Contact Phone" number.
9. Select the "Horse Gender" from the provided options (Stallion, Mare, or Gelding).
10. If applicable, select your "Rider Experience Level" from the options (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Professional).
11. Read and acknowledge the "Liability Waiver" by clicking on it.
12. Select "I Agree" to accept the terms and conditions and assume all risks.
13. Choose the "Date of Entry" for the competition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rider Full Name**: (`rider_full_name`, text, required)
	+ Enter the name of the rider participating in the competition.
* **Rider Age**: (`rider_age`, number, required)
	+ Enter the age of the rider.
* **Horse Name**: (`horse_name`, text, required)
	+ Input the registered name of the horse.
* **Competition Division**: (`competition_division`, select_one, required)
	+ Select the division for which you are registering (Open, Youth, Senior, Novice).
* **Email Address**: (`email_address`, email, required)
	+ Enter your email address for entry confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number**: (`phone_number`, text, required)
	+ Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required)
	+ Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an accident.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required)
	+ Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Horse Gender**: (`stallion_or_mare`, select_one, not required)
	+ Select the gender of the horse (Stallion, Mare, or Gelding). Note: This field is not mandatory.
* **Rider Experience Level**: (`experience_level`, select_one, not required)
	+ Select your experience level as a rider (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Professional). Note: This field is not mandatory.
* **Liability Waiver**: (`header_waiver`, note, not required)
	+ Read and acknowledge the liability waiver.
* **I agree to the terms and conditions and assume all risks**: (`accept_terms`, select_one, required)
	+ Select "I Agree" to confirm your understanding and acceptance of the terms and conditions.
* **Date of Entry**: (`entry_date`, date, required)
	+ Choose the date you are registering for the competition.
