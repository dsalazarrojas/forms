# Hot Sauce Contest Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hot Sauce Contest Application Form is used to collect information from contestants who want to participate in the Hot Sauce Contest. This form helps the contest organizers to gather all the necessary details to make the contest fair and efficient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter your first name in the First Name field**, which is a field that asks for your first name.
2. **Enter your last name in the Last Name field**, which is a field that asks for your last name.
3. **Enter your email address in the Email field**, which is where you should enter your email address.
4. **Enter your phone number in the Phone field**, which is a field that asks for your contact phone number.

* **First Name**: The First Name field is where you should enter your first name. This field is optional but it helps us to get a more personal contact with you.
* **Last Name**: The Last Name field is where you should enter your last name. This field is optional but it helps us to get a more personal contact with you.
* **Email**: The Email field is where you should enter your email address. This is a required field and will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: The Phone field is where you should enter your contact phone number. This is an optional field but it helps us to contact you more efficiently if needed.
