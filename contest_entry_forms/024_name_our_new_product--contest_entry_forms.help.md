# Name Our New Product - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user suggestions for a new product. The information collected will be used to help us understand what types of products our users are interested in, and to gather feedback on potential product features and naming.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a brief description of your product idea.
2. Select the category that best fits your product.
3. Optionally, select any additional categories that also apply to your product.
4. Provide a tag that best describes your product.
5. Optionally, provide a more detailed description of your product.
6. Enter the name of the product that you would like to use.
7. Select the date you would like the product to be released.
8. Enter any other comments or notes about your product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name**: Enter a brief name for your product.
* **Brand Name**: Enter the name of the brand that the product belongs to.
* **Category**: Select the primary category that your product fits into. This will help us understand the type of product you are suggesting.
* **Tag**: Select a tag that best describes your product. This will help us understand the characteristics of your product.
* **Description**: Provide a more detailed description of your product. This will help us understand the features and benefits of your product.
* **Release Date**: Select the date you would like the product to be released. This will help us plan and schedule the product launch.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your product. This will help us understand any additional information that is relevant to your product.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with any follow-up questions.
* **Submit**: Once you have completed all the fields, click submit to send your suggestions to us.
