# Gift Card Raffle Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gift Card Raffle Entry Form is a simple form designed to collect user details and gift card information for entry into a raffle. This form is intended for internal use only and is not a validation test.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Details page (Page 1).
2. Enter your gift card details on the Gift Card Details page (Page 2).
3. Provide the entry details on the Entry Details page (Page 3).
4. Click the Submit Entry button on the last page (Page 4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Details
• **User Details** (text, required): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant details you'd like to share.
• No specific guidance is provided for this field as it depends on user discretion and is for personal identification purposes.

### Gift Card Details
• **Gift Card Details** (text, required): Enter the details of the gift card you are entering into the raffle. This can include the gift card's value, brand, or any other relevant information.

### Entry Details
• **Entry Details** (text, required): Describe your entry into the raffle. Be as detailed as possible, including any relevant dates, times, or locations.

### Submit Entry
• **Submit Entry** (text, not required): This is the final step to submit your entry. Please ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting your entry.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
- Ensure you provide unique responses to increase your chances of winning.
- Contact us at form@gic.mx if you have any questions or concerns.
