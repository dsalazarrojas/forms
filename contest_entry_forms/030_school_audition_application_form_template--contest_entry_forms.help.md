# School Audition Application Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Audition Application Form is designed for students to submit their applications for school auditions. This form helps the student to provide the required information and supporting documents for the audition process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student details page (page 1), which includes basic information about the student.
2. Provide contact details on page 2.
3. Fill out the audition details page (page 3), which includes questions related to the student's background and experiences.
4. Upload supporting documents on page 4.
5. Select the submission type on page 5.
6. Review the submission reviews from 6 to 19.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Details** (page 1):
	+ **Student Details**: This field is for providing the student's personal details, such as name, contact information, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Details** (page 2):
	+ **Contact Details**: This field is for providing the student's contact information, such as email address, phone number, and other contact methods.
* **Audition Details** (page 3):
	+ **Audition Details**: This field is for providing information related to the student's background and experiences, such as their musical experience, education, and performance goals.
* **Supporting Documents** (page 4):
	+ **Supporting Documents**: This field is for uploading any supporting documents required for the audition process.
* **Submission** (page 5):
	+ **Submission**: This field is for selecting the type of submission the student wants to make, such as in-person or online.
* **Submission Review** (pages 6-19):
	+ **Submission Review**: These fields are for reviewing and providing feedback on the submission, which will be reviewed by the school's audition committee.

Please make sure to review each page carefully and provide all the necessary information and supporting documents to ensure a successful audition application.
