# Model Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Model Registration Form is a data collection tool used to gather information about individuals for registration within the organization. This form is essential for maintaining accurate and up-to-date records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Model Registration Form, follow these steps:

* Fill out all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
* Provide accurate and truthful information for each field.
* Ensure that you've filled out all necessary fields to avoid missing critical data.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, false): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, false): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Age** (Age, number, false): Enter your age in years.
* **Weight** (Weight, number, false): Enter your weight in units of weight that you are comfortable sharing.
* **Email** (Email, email, false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, false): Enter your phone number as it appears on official documents.
* **Phone Extension** (Phone Extension, text, false): Enter your phone extension if applicable.
* **Country Code** (Country Code, text, false): Enter your country code if applicable.
* **Height** (Height, number, false): Enter your height in units of height that you are comfortable sharing.
* **Weight (2)** (Weight, number, false): Enter your weight in units of weight that you are comfortable sharing.
* **Medical Condition** (Medical Condition, text, false): If you have any medical conditions, list them here.
* **Medical Notes** (Medical Notes, text, false): Provide any additional medical notes or information.

Note: Fields with "false" in the "required" field are optional.
