# Drawing Entry Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a drawing contest, including details about the contest's title, location, and rules.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields. Your first and last name will be used to contact you about the contest.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field. This will be used to contact you about the contest results.
3. Choose the Type of Drawing (Charity, Non-Profit, or Commercial) from the dropdown menu.
4. Choose the Prize Type (Cash, Non-Cash, or Other) from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter the Start Date and End Date of the contest.
6. Enter the Location where the contest is being held.
7. Enter the Time of the contest.
8. Enter any additional Note about the contest.
9. Confirm that your email address is valid by confirming the Email Confirmed field.
10. Enter the Entry Date and Entry Time of the contest.
11. Enter a description of the Drawing in the Drawing Description field.
12. Enter a description of the prize in the Prize Description field.
13. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for Winner Announced, Entry Closed, and Drawing Opened.
14. Fill in any redundant fields such as First Name S, Last Name S, Entry Date S, Entry Time S, Entry Closed S, Drawing Opened S, etc. These are optional fields and can be filled if the contest is repeated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to be contacted about the contest.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by phone.
* **Type of Drawing** (`drawing_type`, select multiple, required): Choose the type of drawing (Charity, Non-Profit, or Commercial).
* **Prize Type** (`prize_type`, select one, required): Choose the prize type (Cash, Non-Cash, or Other).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the contest.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the contest.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the contest.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the contest.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the contest.
* **Email Confirmed** (`email_confirmed`, email, optional): Confirm that your email address is valid.
* **Entry Date** (`entry_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the entry submission.
* **Entry Time** (`entry_time`, time, required): Enter the time of entry submission.
* **Drawing Description** (`drawing_description`, text, required): Enter a description of the drawing.
* **Prize Description** (`prize_description`, text, required): Enter a description of the prize.
* **Winner Announced** (`winner_announced`, select one, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for whether the winner has been announced.
* **Entry Closed** (`entry_closed`, select one, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for whether the entry is closed.
* **Drawing Opened** (`drawing_opened`, select one, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for whether the drawing is open.
* **First Name S** (`first_name_s`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name S** (`last_name_s`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Entry Date S** (`entry_date_s`, date, optional): Enter the date of the entry submission.
* **Entry Time S** (`entry_time_s`, time, optional): Enter the time of entry submission.
* **Entry Closed S** (`entry_closed_s`, select one, optional): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for whether the entry is closed.
* **Drawing Opened S** (`drawing_opened_s`, select one, optional): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for whether the drawing is open.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields marked with **Required**.
* If a field has options, choose the correct option from the dropdown menu.
* Double-check that your email address is valid before submitting the form.
