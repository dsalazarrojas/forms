# Marketing Contest Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for users to share their experiences, thoughts, or ideas related to marketing, which will be collected for the purpose of the marketing contest. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Scroll through the form and review each field to understand what information is being requested.
2. Provide your answer in the required fields.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
4. Submit the form to share your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Inputs** (`text`, required/optional: false): Enter any information you feel is relevant to share about yourself and your experience related to marketing.
- **Submission Details** (`text`, required/optional: false): Share more details about your submission.
- **Entry Form** (`text`, required/optional: false): Enter your marketing-related thoughts or ideas.

## Tips
- Make sure to be concise and clear in your answers.
- Proofread your submission before submitting it.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
