# harvest_basket_contest_entry - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from participants of a harvest basket contest, event, or activity. It is intended to gather essential details about each entry, including category, description, contact information, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Harvest Basket Contest Entry**: Enter a brief title for your entry.
2. **Category**: Select the category where your entry fits (e.g., fairs, markets, schools, or local businesses).
3. **Description**: Provide a detailed description of your entry.
4. **Email**: Enter your email address.
5. **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
6. **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person or organization associated with your entry.
7. **Photo URLs**: If you have any photos related to your entry, provide their URLs.
8. **Contact Label**: Enter a label for the contact person or organization.
9. **Contact E**, **Contact P**, **Contact Ad**, **Contact T**: Enter the email address, phone number, address, title, and other relevant contact information.
10. **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your entry.
11. **Submission**: Provide a brief summary or description of your submission.
12. **Submission Date**: Enter the date and time of your submission.
13. **Submission Time**: Enter the time of your submission.
14. **Submission Note**: Add any additional information about your submission.
15. **Title**: Enter a title for the contact person or organization.
16. **Submission By**: Choose whether your submission was made by the "assigned tool" or "other".
