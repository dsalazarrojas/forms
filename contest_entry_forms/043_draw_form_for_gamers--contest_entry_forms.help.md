# Draw Form For Gamers - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Draw Form For Gamers is a contest entry form designed for users to submit their entries and participate in a drawing or art contest. This form collects user information, entry details, and confirmation data to streamline the entry process and ensure that users have the necessary information to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Email** in the text field provided.
3. (Optional) Enter your **Twitch Channel** in the text field.
4. (Optional) Enter your **Discord User** in the text field.
5. Enter your **Entry Message** in the text field.
6. Select your **Entry Choice** from the dropdown menu.
7. Select your **Entry Time** from the date picker or enter it manually.
8. Select your **Entry Date** from the date picker or enter it manually.
9. (Optional) Select your **Entry Type** from the dropdown menu.
10. (Optional) Add a **Description** of your entry.
11. Click the **Confirm Entry** button to confirm your participation.
12. If necessary, select a **Confirm Date** and **Confirm Time** for your confirmation.
13. Enter the **Assigned Tool** name.
14. (Optional) Enter the **Form ID** and **Form Version**.
15. (Optional) Enter the **Title** and **Category**.
16. (Optional) Select an **Output File** (e.g., for file sharing).
17. Enter the **Description** of the form.
18. Click the "Submit" button to submit your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your username for the contest.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Twitch Channel** (`twitch_channel`, text, optional): Enter your Twitch channel name (if applicable).
* **Discord User** (`discord_user`, text, optional): Enter your Discord user name (if applicable).
* **Entry Message** (`entry_message`, text, required): Enter a message about your entry.
* **Entry Choice** (`entry_choice`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more options for your entry choice.
* **Entry Time** (`entry_time`, date, required): Select the time you want to enter the contest or choose a date.
* **Entry Date** (`entry_date`, date, required): Select the date you want to enter the contest.
* **Entry Type** (`entry_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of entry you are submitting.
* **Entry Status** (`entry_status`, note, optional): Add any comments or status updates for your entry.
* **Confirm Entry** (`confirm_entry`, select_one, required): Confirm your entry.
* **Confirm Date** (`confirm_date`, date, required): Select the date you confirm your entry.
* **Confirm Time** (`confirm_time`, time, required): Select the time you confirm your entry.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Enter the tool assigned to you for the contest.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): Enter a unique ID for the form.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, text, optional): Enter the version of the form.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a description of your entry.
* **Title** (`title`, text, optional): Enter a title for your entry.
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Enter a category for your entry.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): Select an output file for your entry.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter a tool assigned to you for the contest.
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for the form.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, text, optional): Enter a category for the form.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a successful entry submission.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the contest organizers for assistance.
* Double-check your entry details before submitting to avoid errors.
