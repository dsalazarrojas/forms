# Talent Show Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Show Registration Form is designed to collect information from contestants who want to participate in the talent show. This form is used to gather essential details that will help us understand who the contestants are, what their talent is, and when they want to perform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the first name field with the name as it is written on your identification or government-issued ID.
* Enter your last name as it appears on your identification or government-issued ID.
* Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* Enter your phone number so we can reach you for any last-minute updates.
* Select a date and time when you want to perform your talent.
* Choose the category that best describes your talent.
* Write a short bio about your talent and why you want to perform.
* Review and submit your form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name (<name>: First Name)
*   *First Name*: This field collects the first name of the contestant.

### Last name (<name>: Last Name)
*   *Last name*: This field collects the last name of the contestant.

### Email (<name>: Email)
*   *Email*: This field collects a valid email address for contact purposes.

### Phone (<name>: Phone)
*   *Phone*: This field is meant for collecting phone numbers for contact and updates.

### Date (<name>: Date)
*   *Date*: This field is intended for selecting a date when the contestant wants to perform.

### Time (<name>: Time)
*   *Time*: This field is used to select a time slot for the performance.

### Talent (<name>: Talent)
*   *Talent*: This field is meant for describing the type of talent the contestant has.

### Category (<name>: Category)
*   *Category*: This field is meant for selecting a category that best describes the talent.

### Bio (<name>: Bio)
*   *Bio*: This field is meant for writing a short description about the talent and why the contestant wants to perform.

### Submit (<name>: Submit)
*   *Submit*: This field is used to submit the completed form for review.
