# Win A Survival Kit Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants of the "Win A Survival Kit" contest.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields, which include your first name, last name, email address, and phone number (although the latter is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
 * **First Name**: This is the field where you should enter your first name (or names) as it is written on your identification documents, such as your passport or driver's license.
### Last Name
 * **Last Name**: This is the field where you should enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents, such as your passport or driver's license.
### Email
 * **Email**: This is the email address that will be used to contact you in the event of your participation in the contest. Please enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
### Phone
 * **Phone**: This is your contact phone number where we can reach you. If you have a business phone, please enter that; otherwise, enter your personal mobile number.
### Address
 * **Address**: Please enter your home or work address where you reside or work. This will help us contact you if you win the contest.
### City
 * **City**: Enter the name of the city where you reside or work.
### State
 * **State**: Enter the state or region where you reside or work.
### Zip
 * **Zip**: Enter your postal zip code.
### Country
 * **Country**: Enter the country where you reside or work.
 

## Tips
- Make sure to enter valid email and phone numbers to ensure we can contact you in case of a win.
- Use the format of your identification documents for your name and address entries.
- Ensure your zip code is in the correct format for your country.
