# Homebrew Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homebrew Tournament Entry Form is a form for participants to submit their entry for a specific competition. This form collects essential information from participants to facilitate the competition process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant name and brewer name.
2. Provide a brief description of your entry materials.
3. Enter the competition name and date.
4. Add your contact email and phone number.
5. Include any participant and brewer notes.
6. Select the category, entry type, and entry status.
7. List any additional entries.
8. If necessary, provide additional materials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Please enter your name as it appears on your registration.
* **Brewer Name** (`brewer_name`): Enter the name of the person brewing your entry.
* **Entry Materials** (`entry_materials`): Describe the materials used in your entry, including ingredients and equipment.
* **Competition Name** (`competition_name`): Enter the name of the competition you are submitting your entry to.
* **Entry Date** (`entry_date`): Enter the date you submitted your entry.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`): Include any additional information about yourself as a participant.
* **Brewer Notes** (`brewer_notes`): Include any additional information about your brewer.
* **Competition Date** (`competition_date`): Enter the date of the competition you are participating in.
* **Entry Time** (`entry_time`): Enter the time of your entry.
* **Category** (`category`): Select a category for your entry from the available options.
* **Entry Type** (`entry_type`): Select the type of entry from the available options.
* **Entry Status** (`entry_status`): Select the status of your entry from the available options.
* **Additional Entries** (`additional_entries`): List any additional entries you are participating in.
* **Additional Materials** (`entry_materials_2`): Describe any additional materials used in your entry, if necessary.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your entries for any mistakes or inaccuracies.
* Ensure that you are not entering duplicate submissions.
