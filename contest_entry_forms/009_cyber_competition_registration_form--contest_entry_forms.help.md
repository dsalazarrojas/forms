# cyber_competition_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cyber_competition_registration_form is designed for registering participants in a cyber competition. This form collects personal and team information for registration purposes. It is a required form for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out all the required fields to ensure successful submission.
2.  Enter your contact information for the submitters to communicate with you.
3.  Be accurate in filling out the team member's information for accurate evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a participant.
*   **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name if applicable.
*   **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to start your project.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to finish your project if applicable.
*   **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your project if applicable.
*   **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your project if applicable.
*   **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, optional): Enter the number of team members.
*   **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter your team name.
*   **Team Description** (`team_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your team.
*   **Participant Role** (`participant_role`, select_one, required): Choose your role in the team (Team Lead, Team Member, Team Coach, or Other).
*   **Participant Title** (`participant_title`, text, optional): Enter your title as a participant.
*   **Participant Experience** (`participant_experience`, number, optional): Enter your experience level in the field.
*   **Participant Qualifications** (`participant_qualifications`, text, optional): Enter your qualifications relevant to the competition.
*   **Team Experience** (`team_experience`, number, optional): Enter your team's experience level in the field.
*   **Team Qualifications** (`team_qualifications`, text, optional): Enter your team's qualifications relevant to the competition.
*   **Team Technology Stack** (`team_technology_stack`, text, required): Enter your team's technology stack.
*   **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
*   **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
*   **Submitter Phone Number** (`submitter_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
*   **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
*   **Submitter Organization** (`submitter_organization`, text, optional): Enter the organization of the person submitting the form.
*   **Submitter Title** (`submitter_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the person submitting the form.
*   **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments for the submission.
