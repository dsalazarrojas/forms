# 90 Day Equipment Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you record and manage the 90-day inspection of equipment. It gathers information about the equipment's condition, location, and maintenance status. Complete this form to ensure accurate and timely records of your inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Enter the equipment's unique identifier (Equipment ID / asset tag) to help track and identify the equipment.
2. Select the type of equipment (e.g., Forklift Model X, Tractor 2000) to provide context.
3. Specify the location where the equipment is currently situated (e.g., Building, lot or vehicle assignment).
4. Enter the date of the inspection.
5. Enter the name of the person performing the inspection.
6. If applicable, provide the operating hours or mileage of the equipment at the time of inspection.
7. Assess the overall visual condition of the equipment.
8. Select all relevant safety checks that were passed during the inspection.
9. Indicate whether maintenance is required or not.
10. If maintenance is required, provide a description of the recommended actions or repairs.
11. (Optional) Schedule the next inspection date based on the 90-day cycle.
12. Provide any additional notes or observations from the inspector.
13. (Required) Sign your name as the electronic signature of the inspector.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Equipment ID / asset tag**: Enter the equipment's unique identifier (e.g., manufacturer tag, internal asset number) to track and identify the equipment.
- **Equipment type / model**: Provide a description of the equipment's type or model (e.g., Forklift Model X, Tractor 2000) for reference.
- **Location or site**: Specify the location where the equipment is currently situated (e.g., Building, lot or vehicle assignment).
- **Inspection date**: Enter the date on which the inspection was conducted.
- **Inspector name**: Enter the full name of the person performing the inspection.
- **Operating hours or mileage**: If applicable, provide the operating hours or mileage of the equipment at the time of inspection (e.g., 100 hours, 5000 miles).
- **Visual condition assessment**: Select the overall visual condition of the equipment (Good, Fair, Poor).
- **Safety checks passed**: Select all relevant safety checks that were passed during the inspection (Brakes, Lights and signals, Tires and wheels, Fluid leaks, Steering and suspension, Emergency equipment).
- **Maintenance required**: Indicate whether maintenance is required or not (True, Yes - minor, Yes - major).
- **Recommended actions / repairs**: If maintenance is required, describe the recommended actions or repairs (e.g., change oil, replace tire).
- **Next scheduled inspection date**: Enter the suggested date for the next inspection based on the 90-day cycle.
- **Inspector notes and observations**: Provide any additional notes or observations from the inspector (e.g., safety concerns, nonconformances, or immediate hazards).
- **Inspector signature**: Enter your name to serve as the electronic signature of the inspector.
