# Vote For Courageous Women - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect nominations for women who have shown remarkable courage and resilience in their personal or professional lives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by selecting the Nomination Form page.
2.  Complete the Nomination Form page by filling out the provided fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Nomination Form
* **Nomination Story (`nomination_story`, text, optional):** Tell us about the woman you are nominating and the story behind their courage and resilience.
* **Nominator Name (`nominator_name`, text, optional):** Enter the name of the woman being nominated.
* **Nominator Email (`nominator_email`, email, optional):** Enter the email address of the person nominating the woman.
* **Nominator Phone (`nominator_phone`, text, optional):** Enter the phone number of the person nominating the woman.
* **Nominator Organization (`nominator_organization`, text, optional):** Enter the organization or affiliation of the person nominating the woman.
* **Nomination Date (`nomination_date`, date, optional):** Enter the date of the nomination (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Nomination Time (`nomination_time`, time, optional):** Enter the time of the nomination (in 24-hour format).

### Nomination Time
* **Nomination Time (`nomination_time`, time, optional):** Enter the time of the nomination (in 24-hour format).

### Tips

*   Please ensure that the nomination is accurate and complete before submitting.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask an administrator for help.
*   If you have already made a nomination, do not submit the same nomination again.
