# Wearable Technology Giveaway Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wearable Technology Giveaway Entry form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in a wearable technology contest. This form gathers contact and demographic data, as well as interests and preferences, to determine eligibility for the contest.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First name** in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your **Email address** for contact purposes.
4. Enter your **phone number** for contact purposes.
5. Enter your **address** for shipping and delivery purposes.
6. Choose your **City** for shipping and delivery purposes.
7. Select your **State** for shipping and delivery purposes.
8. Select your **Country** for shipping and delivery purposes.
9. Check the box to **Consent to share your contact information with our partners**, allowing us to share your contact information with our partners.
10. Select how you **Consent to be contacted**, choosing one or more methods (phone or email).
11. Review and agree to the **Terms and conditions** of the contest.
12. Check if you agree to the **Privacy policy**.
13. Enter your **Date of birth**, if applicable.
14. Select your **Time of interest** in participating in the contest, if applicable.
15. Choose your **Favorite color**, selecting one or more options.
16. Select your **Favorite animal**, selecting one or more options.
17. Choose your **Favorite food**, selecting one or more options.
18. Select your **Favorite place**, selecting one or more options.
19. Choose your **Favorite hobby**, selecting one or more options.

23. Enter your **Favorite singer**, if you have one.
