# Holiday Video Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Video Submission Form is a form created to collect videos, images, audio, and documents for a specific holiday-themed project. This form is designed to gather information and media from users to enhance the holiday experience. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. For fields "Video", "Image", "Audio", and "Document", select the corresponding file from your device.
3. If you need to submit multiple files, you can upload additional videos, images, audios, and documents by clicking the "Upload" buttons next to each field.
4. Once you have completed and uploaded all required files, click the "Submit" button to send your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **UserInfo**: Please enter a few words about yourself, such as your name, nickname, or handle.
  • A brief description of who you are.
- **Video Info**: Enter a title and a description of your holiday video.
  • A title and description of your holiday-themed video.
- **Video**: Upload your holiday video to share with the community.
  • A video file from your device.
- **Image**: Upload an image related to your holiday video.
  • An image file from your device.
- **Audio**: Upload an audio file related to your holiday video.
  • An audio file from your device.
- **Document**: Upload a document related to your holiday video.
  • A document file from your device.
- **Upload Video2**: Upload another holiday video (if necessary).
  • A video file from your device.
- **Upload Image2**: Upload another image related to your holiday video.
  • An image file from your device.
- **Upload Audio2**: Upload another audio file related to your holiday video.
  • An audio file from your device.
- **Upload Document2**: Upload another document related to your holiday video.
  • A document file from your device.
- **Submit**: Click this button to send your submission.
  • Send your completed and uploaded files to the system.
