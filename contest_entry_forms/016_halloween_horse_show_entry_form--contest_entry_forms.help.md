# Halloween Horse Show Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Halloween Horse Show. Please complete it accurately to ensure your entry is accepted.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- If you are a rider, enter your own information.
- If you are entering on behalf of another rider, ensure you have their permission to do so.
- Review and edit your entry carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event you are registering for.
* **Horse Name** (`horse_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the horse you will be riding.
* **Breed** (`breed`, select_one, optional): Select the breed of your horse (e.g. American Warmblood, Friesian, etc.).
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter the age of your horse.
* **Entry Date** (`entry_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to enter the show.
* **Entry Time** (`entry_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you plan to enter the show.
* **Rider Name** (`rider_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the rider (if entering on behalf of another).
* **Rider's Phone** (`rider_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the rider.
* **Rider's Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the rider.
* **Division** (`division`, select_one, optional): Select the division you are entering (e.g. Hunter, Jumper, etc.).
* **Class** (`class`, select_multiple, optional): Select the class you are entering (e.g. Junior, Senior, etc.).
* **Show Type** (`show_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of show you are entering (e.g. Dressage, Hunter, etc.).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information or notes you feel are relevant.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Enter a signature (e.g. rider's, representative's, etc.). 
*   **Division** and **Class** selections are mutually exclusive (you can only choose one per entry)
*   Please use a date in YYYY-MM-DD format for **Entry Date**
