# Pie Judging Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect ratings and notes for a series of pies in a competition setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the title of the pie entry.
2. Enter the name of the competitor submitting the pie.
3. Rate each of the pies using the provided rating fields.
4. Add any additional notes about each pie.
5. Select "Yes" to submit your ratings and notes, or "No" to indicate you did not view or rate each pie.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **entry_title** (text, required: false): The title of the pie entry. If you are a competitor, this would be the title of your pie.
* **report** (text, required: false): The name of the competitor submitting the pie. Please enter the name of the person or group who submitted the pie.
* **pie_1_rating** (number, required: false): Rate the first pie on a scale of 1-6.
* **pie_2_rating** (number, required: false): Rate the second pie on a scale of 1-6.
* **pie_3_rating** (number, required: false): Rate the third pie on a scale of 1-6.
* **pie_4_rating** (number, required: false): Rate the fourth pie on a scale of 1-6.
* **pie_5_rating** (number, required: false): Rate the fifth pie on a scale of 1-6.
* **pie_6_rating** (number, required: false): Rate the sixth pie on a scale of 1-6.
* **submit** (select one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you have filled out this form accurately and truthfully. Select "No" if you did not actually view or rate each pie.
* **notes** (note, required: false): Add any additional notes about each pie. This can be anything from "the crust was a bit too thick" to "this pie is a game-changer".
