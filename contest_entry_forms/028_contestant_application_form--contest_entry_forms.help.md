# Contestant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for contestants who wish to participate in the contest. It is a required form to be filled out by all contestants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information (Section 1)
2. Enter your contact details (Section 2)
3. Measure and enter your height (Section 3)
4. Describe your talent/ performance (Section 4)
5. Enter your background and motivation (Section 5)
6. List any previous contest titles or awards (Section 6)
7. Enter your reason for participating in the contest (Section 7)
8. If you are under 18, enter your guardian information (Section 8)
9. Enter your application fee reference number (Section 9)
10. Select the date and time you submitted your application (Section 10)
11. Accept the terms and conditions of the contest (Section 11)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Section 1: Applicant Personal Information
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Gender** (`gender_identification`, select_one, required): Select your gender (Female, Male, Non-binary, or Prefer not to say).

### Section 2: Contact Details
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Primary Phone Number** (`primary_phone`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number.

### Section 3: Measurements and Details
* **Height (cm)** (`height_cm`, number, required): Enter your height in centimeters.

### Section 4: Talent/Performance Description
* **Talent/Performance Description** (`talent_performance_desc`, text, required): Describe your talent or performance (e.g., Singing, Dance, Speech).

### Section 5: Experience and Motivation
* **Previous Contest Titles or Awards** (`previous_titles_awards`, text, optional): If you have any major achievements, list them here (Max 3).
* **Why Participate in this Contest** (`why_entering_contest`, text, required): Enter your reason for participating in this contest.

### Section 6: Guardian Information (If Under 18)
* **Parent/Guardian Full Name** (`guardian_name`, text, optional): If you are under 18, enter your parent or guardian's full name.

### Section 7: Application
* **Application Fee Reference Number** (`application_fee_reference`, text, required): Enter your application fee reference number.
* **Date of Application** (`application_date`, date, required): Select the date and time you submitted your application.

### Section 8: Terms and Conditions
* **I Agree to all Contest Rules and Regulations** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required): Select "I Agree" to accept the terms and conditions.
