# Flotilla Derby Awards Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flotilla Derby Awards Submission form is a tool for nominating boats for the Flotilla Derby Awards. To submit a boat, please fill out the form with accurate and complete information. Only one submission per boat is accepted.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Nominator Name field with the name of the person submitting the boat.
2. Enter the Nominator Email field with the email address of the person submitting the boat.
3. Enter the Nominator Phone field with the phone number of the person submitting the boat.
4. Select the Boat Type from the dropdown menu that best matches the boat's type (Catamaran, Sailboat, Yacht).
5. Enter the Submission Date field with the date of submission.
6. Enter the Submission Time field with the time of submission.
7. In the Nominator Comments field, provide additional comments or information about the boat.
8. In the Additional Nominator Comments field, provide any additional comments or information about the nomination.
9. In the Judge Comments field, provide comments or feedback from the judge.
10. Enter the Judge Email field with the email address of the judge.
11. Enter the Judge Phone field with the phone number of the judge.
12. In the Additional Organizer Comments field, provide comments or feedback from the organizer.
13. Enter the Submission Date and Time field with the date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nominator Name**: Enter the name of the person submitting the boat.
* **Nominator Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the boat.
* **Nominator Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the boat.
* **Boat Type**: Select the type of boat that best matches the boat you are submitting (Catamaran, Sailboat, Yacht).
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Nominator Comments**: Provide additional comments or information about the boat.
* **Additional Nominator Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about the nomination.
* **Judge Comments**: Provide comments or feedback from the judge.
* **Judge Email**: Enter the email address of the judge.
* **Judge Phone**: Enter the phone number of the judge.
* **Additional Organizer Comments**: Provide comments or feedback from the organizer.
* **Submission Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of submission.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have only submitted once for each boat.
* Please enter accurate and complete information for all fields.
* If you need help with any of the fields, please contact the Flotilla Derby Awards team.
* Double-check your submission before submitting it for review.
