# Gaming Leaderboard Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit scores and rankings for games on a leaderboard.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your username in the "username" field.
2. Enter your player name in the "player_name" field.
3. Enter a tag for your player in the "player_tag" field.
4. Select the type of game you are submitting for in the "game_type" field.
5. Enter the version of the game you are submitting for in the "game_version" field (if necessary).
6. Choose the platform(s) you played on in the "platform" field.
7. Enter your score in the "score" field.
8. Enter the maximum score possible for the game in the "score_out_of" field.
9. Select your score rank in the "score_rank" field.
10. Enter the date and time of your score submission in the "score_date" and "score_time" fields.
11. Enter any additional information about your score submission in the "score_platform" field (if necessary).
12. Select your difficulty level in the "score_difficulty" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **username** (Text, Required): Enter your username in this field.
* **player_name** (Text, Required): Enter your player name in this field.
* **player_tag** (Text, Required): Enter a tag for your player in this field.
* **player_tag_type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of player tag you are using, or enter a custom tag type if not listed.
* **player_tag_type_other** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about your player tag type if not listed.
* **game_type** (Select One, Required): Select the type of game you are submitting for.
* **game_version** (Number, Optional): Enter the version of the game you are submitting for (if necessary).
* **game_version_other** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about the game version if not listed.
* **platform** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the platform(s) you played on.
* **platform_other** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about the platform you played on if not listed.
* **score** (Number, Required): Enter your score in this field.
* **score_out_of** (Number, Required): Enter the maximum score possible for the game.
* **score_out_of_other** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about your score if not listed.
* **score_rank** (Select One, Required): Select your score rank.
* **score_rank_other** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about your score rank if not listed.
* **score_date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of your score submission.
* **score_time** (Time, Required): Enter the time of your score submission.
* **score_platform** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about the platform you played on if not listed.
* **score_ranking** (Number, Optional): Enter your ranking in the game (if necessary).
* **score_ranking_other** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about your ranking if not listed.
* **score_difficulty** (Select One, Required): Select your difficulty level.
* **score_difficulty_other** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about your difficulty level if not listed.
* **submit** (Note, Required): Click the "submit" button to submit your score and ranking.
