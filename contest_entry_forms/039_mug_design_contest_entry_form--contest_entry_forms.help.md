# Mug Design Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mug Design Contest Entry Form is a form for students, teachers, or school administrators to submit their entries for the mug design contest. Please note that all required fields must be completed to submit a valid entry.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the personal information section:
	* First Name (required): Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or documents.
	* Last Name (required): Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or documents.
	* Email (required): Provide a valid email address for contact purposes.
2. Provide your artwork:
	* Artwork (Note): This field is for submitting your design concept. Please attach a clear and concise description of your design.
3. Select your design type:
	* Design Type (Select One): Choose the type of design you're submitting (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
4. Specify your design details:
	* Size (Number): If applicable, specify the size of your design in inches.
	* Color (Select One): If applicable, select the color of your design.
	* Material (Select Multiple): If applicable, select the materials you plan to use for your design.
5. Provide teacher information (if applicable):
	* Teacher (Text): If you're a student, enter your teacher's name.
	* Teacher Email (Email): If you're a student, enter your teacher's email address.
	* Teacher Phone (Text): If you're a student, enter your teacher's phone number.
	* Teacher School (Text): If you're a student, enter your teacher's school.
	* Teacher Department (Text): If you're a student, enter your teacher's department.
	* Teacher Grade (Text): If you're a student, enter your teacher's grade.
	* Teacher Position (Text): If you're a student, enter your teacher's position.
6. Provide school information (if applicable):
	* School (Select One): If you're a student or teacher, select your school or university.
	* School Phone (Text): If you're a student or teacher, enter your school's phone number.
	* School Email (Email): If you're a student or teacher, enter your school's email address.
	* School Address (Text): If you're a student or teacher, enter your school's address.

## Tips

* Make sure to review your entry carefully before submitting.
* All required fields must be completed to submit a valid entry.
* If you're a student, ensure that your teacher's information is accurate.
* If you're a teacher, ensure that your school's information is accurate.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for assistance.
