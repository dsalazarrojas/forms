# Artist Call Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for artists to submit their artwork for consideration in a contest or competition. The form collects essential information about the artist, their artwork, and other relevant details. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accurate and complete submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address, which are required fields.
2. Provide your Phone Number and Website or Portfolio URL, if applicable.
3. Select your Primary Art Medium from the options provided.
4. Enter your Years of Professional Practice, if relevant.
5. Fill out the Form Education or Training section if you have formal education or training in art.
6. Enter the title and description of your Primary Artwork, along with its dimensions or duration.
7. Upload a high-quality image of your artwork.
8. List your Previous Exhibitions or Show History, if applicable.
9. Enter any Awards or Recognition you have received.
10. Write a 100-300 word Artist Statement explaining your work and inspiration.
11. Choose whether rights to use images for promotion will be granted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
- **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
- **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
- **Website or Portfolio URL** (Text, Optional): If you have a website or portfolio, enter the URL for reference.
- **Primary Art Medium** (Select One, Required): Choose the primary medium or technique you use for your art (e.g., Painting, Sculpture, etc.).
- **Years of Professional Practice** (Number, Optional): If applicable, enter the number of years you have been practicing art professionally.
- **Formal Education or Training** (Text, Optional): If you have formal education or training in art, describe it here.
- **Primary Artwork Title** (Text, Required): Enter the title of the artwork you are submitting.
- **Artwork Description** (Text, Required): Briefly describe your artwork, including concept, process, and inspiration.
- **Artwork Dimensions or Duration** (Text, Optional): If applicable, enter dimensions or duration of the artwork.
- **Artwork Image Upload** (Text, Required): Upload a high-quality image of your artwork for review.
- **Previous Exhibitions or Show History** (Text, Optional): If applicable, list your previous exhibitions or shows where your work has been displayed.
- **Awards or Recognition Received** (Text, Optional): If applicable, list any awards or recognition you have received for your work.
- **Artist Statement** (Text, Required): Write a 100-300 word statement explaining your work and inspiration.
- **Submission or Entry Fee Amount** (Number, Optional): If applicable, enter the submission or entry fee amount.
- **Rights to Use Images for Promotion** (Select One, Required): Choose whether rights to use images of your artwork will be granted for promotional purposes.
