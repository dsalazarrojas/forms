# mini-golf-hole-design-submission - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for submitting and sharing ideas and designs for a mini golf hole submission. It can be used to showcase your creation and share it with others for feedback and review. The form is used to gather information about your name, contact information, and details about the submission such as course ideas, descriptions, and images/videos.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name in the "Your Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Describe your course ideas in the "Course Ideas" field.
5. Upload sketches related to your submission in the "Sketches" field.
6. Write a detailed description of your submission in the "Description" field.
7. Specify any rules for the mini golf hole in the "Mini Golf Rules" field.
8. Choose a category for your submission from the "Category" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your name to be associated with your submission.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Course Ideas**: Describe your course ideas for the mini golf hole.
* **Sketches**: Upload sketches related to your submission.
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of your submission.
* **Mini Golf Rules**: Specify any rules or instructions for the mini golf hole.
* **Category**: Choose a category for your submission from the options Theme, Water Park, Theme Park, or Other.
* **Submission Date/Time**: This is where your submission date and time will be recorded. You can enter it manually.
* **Submission File**: Upload a file related to your submission.
* **Submission Attachment**: Upload an attachment related to your submission.
* **Submission Image**: Upload an image related to your submission.
* **Submission Video**: Upload a video related to your submission.
* **Submission Audio**: Upload an audio file related to your submission.
* **3D Model**: Upload a 3D model related to your submission.
* **Submission File 2**: Upload another file related to your submission.
* **Submission Audio 2**: Upload another audio file related to your submission.
* **Submission Video 2**: Upload another video related to your submission.
* **Submission Image 2**: Upload another image related to your submission.
* **Submission Description**: Provide another description of your submission.

## Tips

* Be as descriptive and creative as possible when filling out the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out to avoid any submission issues.
* You can upload multiple files and images to showcase your submission.
* Review and edit your submission before submitting it.
* If you need help or have questions, contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
