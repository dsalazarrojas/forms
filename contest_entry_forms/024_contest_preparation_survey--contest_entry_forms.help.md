# Contest Preparation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contest Preparation Survey is a form designed to gather information from participants before the event. This information will be used to tailor the contest experience to your needs, including logistics, catering, and technical support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Make sure to select all options that apply to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Participant Check-in --**: This section is just an introduction to let us know you have started filling out the form.
* **Participant Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it should appear on your contest badge. This is a required field to help us address you correctly.
* **Which contest are you preparing for**: Please select the specific contest you are participating in, such as "Regional Science Fair" or "Annual Hackathon". This will help us tailor the experience to your needs.
* **-- Preparation Status --**: This is a note section, and you can add any comments or notes about your preparation status.
* **Current Readiness Level**: Please select your current level of preparation, from "Just started" to "Fully prepared". This will help us understand how much support you may need.
* **Specific Equipment Needed on Site**: Please select all the equipment you need to bring to the contest, such as "Power Outlet/Extension Cord" or "Presentation Screen/Monitor".
* **-- Logistics --**: This is a note section, and you can add any comments or notes about your logistics.
* **Planned Arrival Date**: Please enter the date you plan to arrive at the contest site. This is a required field to ensure we can plan for your arrival.
* **Dietary Restrictions (for catering)**: Please select all the dietary restrictions you have, such as "Vegetarian", "Vegan", or "Gluten-Free".
* **-- Questions and Support --**: This is a note section, and you can add any comments or questions you have.
* **What is your biggest challenge in preparing**: Please describe any challenges you're facing in preparing for the contest.
* **Would you like a 15-minute session with a mentor**: Select "True" if you'd like to review your progress with a mentor before the event.
* **Any other needs or concerns**: Feel free to add any other needs or concerns you may have.
* **Date Survey Completed**: This is a required field to track when you completed the survey.

Note: The date and time format for the Planned Arrival Date and Date Survey Completed fields are MM/DD/YYYY, where MM is the month (01-12), DD is the day of the month (01-31), and YYYY is the year (e.g., 02/16/2023).
