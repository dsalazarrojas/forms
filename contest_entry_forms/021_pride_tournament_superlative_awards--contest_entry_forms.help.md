# Pride Tournament Superlative Awards - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Pride Tournament Superlative Awards is a recognition program that celebrates outstanding individuals in the Pride community who have made significant contributions to the community. This guide will walk you through the steps to fill out the nomination form.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2.  Ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
3.  Nominate someone who has been recognized for their achievements in the Pride community.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Pride Nominee** (`pride_tournament_nominator`, Text, **Required**): Enter the name of the nominee.
    Enter the name of the individual being nominated.
*   **Pride Nominee Name** (`pride_nominator_name`, Text, **Required**): Enter the name of the nominee.
    Enter the full name of the individual being nominated.
*   **Pride Nominee Category** (`pride_nominator_category`, Select One, **Optional**): Select the nominee's category of achievement.
    Select the category that best fits the nominee's accomplishments. Options include:
    *   Pride Champion
    *   Pride Ally
    *   Pride Activist
*   **Pride Nominee Story** (`pride_nominator_story`, Text, **Required**): Share a brief story of the nominee's achievements.
    Share a concise narrative of the nominee's accomplishments, highlighting their achievements and contributions to the Pride community.
*   **Pride Nominee Contact** (`pride_nominator_contact`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the contact information of the nominee or their representative.
    Enter the contact information of the individual or their representative for follow-up and communication purposes.
*   **Pride Nominee Email** (`pride_nominator_email`, Email, **Optional**): Enter the email of the nominee or their representative.
    Enter the email of the individual or their representative for communication and follow-up purposes.
*   **Pride Nominee Date of Birth** (`pride_nominator_date_of_birth`, Date, **Optional**): Enter the date of birth of the nominee.
    Enter the date of birth of the individual being nominated.
*   **Pride Nominee Tournament Name** (`pride_nominator_tournament_name`, Text, **Required**): Enter the name of the tournament or event the nominee participated in.
    Enter the name of the tournament or event the individual participated in.
*   **Pride Nominee Tournament Year** (`pride_nominator_tournament_year`, Number, **Required**): Enter the year of the tournament or event the nominee participated in.
    Enter the year the individual participated in the tournament or event.
*   **Pride Nominee Category** (`pride_nominator_tournament_category`, Select Multiple, **Optional**): Select the category of the tournament or event the nominee participated in.
    Select the category that best fits the tournament or event the individual participated in. Options include:
    *   Sports
    *   Community
    *   Entertainment
*   **Pride Nominee Image** (`pride_nominator_image`, Text, **Optional**): Upload an image of the nominee.
    Upload a photo of the individual being nominated.
*   **Pride Nominee Achievements** (`pride_nominator_achievements`, Text, **Required**): Share the nominee's achievements and contributions to the Pride community.
    Share a detailed account of the nominee's accomplishments and contributions to the Pride community.
*   **Pride Nominee Video** (`pride_nominator_video`, Text, **Optional**): Upload a video showcasing the nominee's achievements.
    Upload a video showcasing the individual's achievements and contributions to the Pride community.
*   **Pride Nominee Social Media** (`pride_nominator_social_media`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the social media handle of the nominee.
    Enter the social media handle of the individual for follow-up and communication purposes.
*   **Pride Nominee Website** (`pride_nominator_website`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the website of the nominee or their representative.
    Enter the website of the individual or their representative for follow-up and communication purposes.
*   **Pride Nominee Facebook Page** (`pride_nominator_facebook_page`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the Facebook page of the nominee or their representative.
    Enter the Facebook page of the individual or their representative for follow-up and communication purposes.
*   **Pride Nominee Twitter Handle** (`pride_nominator_twitter_handle`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the Twitter handle of the nominee or their representative.
    Enter the Twitter handle of the individual or their representative for follow-up and communication purposes.
*   **Pride Nominee Instagram Handle** (`pride_nominator_instagram_handle`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the Instagram handle of the nominee or their representative.
    Enter the Instagram handle of the individual or their representative for follow-up and communication purposes.
*   **Pride Nominee LinkedIn Link** (`pride_nominator_linkedin_link`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the LinkedIn link of the nominee or their representative.
    Enter the LinkedIn link of the individual or their representative for follow-up and communication purposes.
*   **Pride Nominee YouTube Link** (`pride_nominator_youtube_link`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the YouTube link of the nominee or their representative.
    Enter the YouTube link of the individual or their representative for follow-up and communication purposes.
*   **Pride Nominee Tournament Link** (`pride_nominator_tournament_link`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the tournament link of the nominee or their representative.
    Enter the tournament link of the individual or their representative for follow-up and communication purposes.
*   **Pride Nominee Additional Notes** (`pride_nominator_additional_notes`, Text, **Optional**): Enter any additional notes about the nominee.
    Enter any additional information about the individual being nominated.
*   **Pride Nominee Agrees to be Featured** (`pride_nominator_agree`, Select One, **Optional**): Indicate whether the nominee agrees to be featured.
    Select whether the individual agrees to be featured in the Pride Tournament Superlative Awards.
*   **Pride Nominee Organizer** (`pride_nominator_organizer`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the contact information of the nominee's representative.
    Enter the contact information of the individual or their representative for follow-up and communication purposes.
*   **Pride Nominee Agrees to Share** (`pride_nominator_agree_to_share`, Select One, **Optional**): Indicate whether the nominee agrees to share their information on social media.
    Select whether the individual agrees to share their information on social media.

## Tips

*   Always ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*   If unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact the Pride Tournament Superlative Awards team.
*   We appreciate your contribution to recognizing outstanding individuals in the Pride community!
