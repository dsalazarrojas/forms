# Animal Exhibition Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for animal exhibitors to enter their animals into an upcoming exhibition or show.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read this guide carefully before starting the form.
2. Fill in your exhibitor details:
   - Exhibitor Full Name: Type your name as it should appear on the exhibition's records.
   - Email Address: Enter your contact email address.
   - Phone Number: Enter your contact phone number.
3. Enter your animal's details:
   - Exhibition or Show Name: Type the name of the exhibition or show.
   - Exhibition Date: Choose the date of the event.
   - Animal's Name: Type the name of your animal.
   - Species: Select your animal's type (e.g., Dog, Cat, Horse, etc.).
   - Breed: Type the specific breed of your animal.
   - Sex: Select Male or Female.
   - Age: Enter your animal's age in years and months.
   - Color and Markings: Describe your animal's color and any distinctive markings.
   - Registration or Pedigree Number: If your animal is registered, enter the number.
4. Health and Certification:
   - Health Certification: Check the box if you have a current health certificate.
   - Do You Have a Current Health Certificate: Select Yes - attached or Yes - will provide.
   - Vaccination Status: Select Yes - all current, Some due soon, or False.
   - Veterinarian Name: Enter your animal's primary veterinarian's name.
   - Veterinarian Phone: Enter your veterinarian's phone number.
   - Special Requirements: If your animal has any special requirements, type them here.
5. Additional Information:
   - How Would You Describe Your Animal's Temperament: Select Calm and gentle, Friendly and outgoing, Energetic, Reserved, Protective, or Other.
   - Your Experience Level with This Animal: Select Professional handler, Experienced exhibitor, Some experience, or First-time exhibitor.
6. Payment and Agreement:
   - Entry Fee Payment: Select Paid with entry, Will pay at event, or Invoice requested.
   - Do You Require Stabling: Select Yes - overnight, Yes - day use, or False.
   - Would You Like Your Animal in the Catalog: Select Yes - photo attached, Yes - use existing photo, or False.
   - I Agree to the Exhibition Terms and Conditions: Select Yes - I agree.
7. Final Check:
   - Type your full name as it should appear on the exhibition's records.
   - Date: Enter today's date.
