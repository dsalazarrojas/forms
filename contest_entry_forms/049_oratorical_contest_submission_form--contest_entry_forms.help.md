# Oratorical Contest Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Oratorical Contest Submission Form is a form designed to collect information about participants and their speech submissions for an oratorical contest. To complete this form, you will need to provide personal and speech details, as well as contact information and event details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide personal details about the participant.
2. Enter the speech title and text.
3. Upload any materials required for the speech.
4. Enter the participant's contact information, including email, phone number, and address.
5. Enter school details and the contact person's name.
6. Enter event details, including date, time, venue, city, state, and country.
7. Select the submission status and provide any submission comments.

* **Participant details** (Participant details, text, optional): Enter your name, school, and any other details about yourself.
* **Participant name** (Participant name, text, required): Enter your name as it should appear on your certificate.
* **Participant school** (Participant school, text, required): Enter the name of your school.
* **Participant grade** (Participant grade, select_one, optional): Indicate if you are a student in elementary, middle, or high school.
* **Speech details** (Speech details, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your speech, such as its length or topic.
* **Speech title** (Speech title, text, required): Enter a brief title for your speech.
* **Speech text** (Speech text, text, required): Enter the full text of your speech.
* **Materials** (Materials, text, required): Upload any additional materials, such as images or documents, related to your speech.
* **Upload materials** (Upload materials, text, required): Upload your materials after typing their description in the previous field.
* **Participant email** (Participant email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Participant phone** (Participant phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant address** (Participant address, text, required): Enter your home address.
* **School details** (School details, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your school.
* **School contact person** (School contact person, text, required): Enter the name of the person to contact at your school.
* **School address** (School address, text, required): Enter the address of your school.
* **Event details** (Event details, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the event.
* **Event date** (Event date, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event time** (Event time, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event venue** (Event venue, text, required): Enter the venue of the event.
* **Event city** (Event city, text, required): Enter the city of the event.
* **Event state** (Event state, text, required): Enter the state of the event.
* **Event country** (Event country, text, required): Enter the country of the event.
