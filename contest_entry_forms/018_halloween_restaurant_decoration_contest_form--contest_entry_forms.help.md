# Halloween Restaurant Decoration Contest Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from restaurants participating in a Halloween-themed decoration contest. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to increase your chances of winning.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by entering your restaurant's basic information, including its name, description, and address.
2.  Provide your contact email and phone number so we can reach out to you with any questions or updates.
3.  Describe your Halloween decoration in a short paragraph.
4.  Select the type of decoration you have created.
5.  Choose an image that best represents your decoration.
6.  Confirm the submission date and time.
7.  Specify the submission status.
8.  Indicate who submitted the entry (you or a representative).
9.  Select the reason for submission.
10. Enter the restaurant owner's name and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Enter your restaurant information** (`halloween_restaurant_decoration_contest_form`):
    *   Enter your restaurant's name.
*   **Restaurant Name** (`restaurant_name`):
    *   Enter your restaurant's name.
*   **Restaurant Description** (`restaurant_description`):
    *   Enter a short description of your restaurant.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`):
    *   Enter your restaurant's email address.
*   **Event Details** (`event_details`):
    *   Enter any relevant event details (optional).
*   **Decoration Type** (`decoration_type`):
    *   Select one of the available decoration types (e.g., "Pumpkin Carving," "Spiders," or "Ghostly Theme").
*   **Decoration Description** (`decoration_description`):
    *   Enter a brief description of your decoration.
*   **Decoration Image** (`decoration_image`):
    *   Select one or more images that best represent your decoration.
*   **Submission Date** (`submission_date`):
    *   Enter the date you are submitting your decoration.
*   **Submission Time** (`submission_time`):
    *   Enter the time you are submitting your decoration.
*   **Submission Status** (`submission_status`):
    *   Select one of the available submission statuses (e.g., "Approved," "Rejected," or "In Progress").
*   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`):
    *   Indicate who submitted the entry (e.g., "Restaurant Owner," "Manager," or "Employee").
*   **Submitted For** (`submitted_for`):
    *   Select the reason for submission (e.g., "Halloween Contest," "Themed Event," or "Regular Decorations").
*   **Restaurant Owner** (`restaurant_owner`):
    *   Enter the name of the restaurant owner.
*   **Restaurant Phone** (`restaurant_phone`):
    *   Enter your restaurant's phone number.
*   **Restaurant Address** (`restaurant_address`):
    *   Enter your restaurant's address.
