# Training Giveaway Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect personal and course information from participants in a training program. This information is used for statistical analysis and to select winners for a training giveaway.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address to receive updates and notifications.
3. Enter your Phone number for communication purposes.
4. Enter your Date of Birth (optional).
5. Enter the name of the Course you are interested in.
6. If you have completed a similar course in the past, you can indicate whether you give back to the community or not by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the Consent field.
7. Once you have completed all the necessary fields, click the submit button to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth (optional).
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the course you are interested in.
* **Give Back** (`give_back`, `text`, optional): If you have completed a similar course in the past, indicate whether you give back to the community or not by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate that you give back to the community or 'No' otherwise.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that your entry is valid.
* If you are unable to access the form, please contact your administrator for assistance.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.

Note: The system checks for existing entries with the same email address. If you have already submitted a form with the same email address, you will not be able to submit this form again.
