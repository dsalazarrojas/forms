# Art Show People's Choice Ballot - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow you to vote for your favorite artwork in the art show and provide optional information to help us improve the show.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your favorite artwork from the list by selecting it from the "Select Your Favorite Artwork" field.
2. If you'd like, provide your full name and email address for verification and newsletter sign-up purposes.
3. Optionally, tell us why you chose your favorite piece by typing your reason for the vote in the "Why did you choose this piece" field.
4. Choose how you heard about the art show from the list in the "How did you hear about this show" field (optional).
5. Decide if you'd like to sign up for the art gallery newsletter by selecting "True" or "False" from the "Sign me up for the art gallery newsletter" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Voter Full Name** (`voter_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Voter Email Address** (`voter_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address we can use to verify your vote and for newsletter sign-up purposes.
* **Select Your Favorite Artwork** (`artwork_choice`, select_one, required): Choose one of the artworks listed from the options.
* **Why did you choose this piece** (`reason_for_vote`, text, optional): Share your reasons for choosing your favorite artwork. This helps us understand your thoughts and preferences.
* **How did you hear about this show** (`discovery_source`, select_one, optional): Choose how you heard about the art show from the options.
* **Sign me up for the art gallery newsletter** (`newsletter_opt_in`, select_one, optional): Select "True" to sign up for our art gallery newsletter and stay updated on upcoming events.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (Voter Full Name, Voter Email Address, and Select Your Favorite Artwork) to submit your vote.
* If you've already signed up for our newsletter, you can skip this step.
* Enter a valid email address to ensure we can verify your vote and sign you up for our newsletter.
* Be honest in your answer to "Why did you choose this piece" to help us understand your preferences.
