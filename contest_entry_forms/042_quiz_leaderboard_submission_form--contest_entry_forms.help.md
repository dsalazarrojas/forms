# Quiz Leaderboard Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by administrators or organizers to collect scores, names, and other details about participants who have achieved a high ranking in a quiz.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a participant's score and details, please follow the step-by-step guide:

1. Enter the **Participant**'s information, including their name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter the participant's **Name** in the corresponding field.
3. Enter the participant's **Score** achieved in the quiz.
4. Enter the participant's **Ranking** in the quiz.
5. Add any additional **Participant Details** about the participant.
6. Enter the participant's **Email** address for communication.
7. Enter the participant's **Phone** number for further contact.
8. Enter the **Event ID** of the quiz event where the participant participated.
9. Enter the **Quiz ID** of the quiz where the participant scored.

  • **Type:** Text input field
  • **Description:** Enter the name of the participant to be included in the leaderboard.

  • **Label:** Name
  • **Type:** Text input field
  • **Description:** Enter the name of the participant who achieved the score.
* **Score**:
  • **Label:** Score
  • **Type:** Number input field
  • **Description:** Enter the score achieved by the participant in the quiz.
* **Ranking**:
  • **Label:** Ranking
  • **Type:** Number input field
  • **Description:** Enter the ranking achieved by the participant in the quiz.
* **Participant Details**:
  • **Label:** Participant Details
  • **Type:** Text input field
  • **Description:** Enter any additional details about the participant, if applicable.
* **Participant Email**:
  • **Label:** Email
  • **Type:** Email input field

* **Participant Phone**:
  • **Label:** Phone
  • **Type:** Text input field

* **Event ID**:
  • **Label:** Event ID
  • **Type:** Text input field

* **Quiz ID**:
  • **Label:** Quiz ID
