# Music Competition Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Competition Application Form is a tool for artists and bands to submit their applications for a music competition. The form gathers relevant information about the artist, their music, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Artist Information section with your personal details.
2. Provide contact information for the artist or band representative.
3. Enter the music details, including title, subtitle, composer, genre, and year of creation.
4. Select the music type and style.
5. Choose the competition category and format.
6. Select whether the competition type is applicable.
7. Choose the application status.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes.
9. Fill in the submission date and competition name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Information (text)**: Enter your artist name, name of band, or orchestra name.
* **Contact Details (text)**: Provide your first and last name, email address, phone number, and address.
* **Contact E-mail (email)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Music Title (text)**: Enter the title of the music piece you are submitting.
* **Music Subtitle (text)**: Enter a subtitle for the music piece.
* **Music Composer (text)**: Enter the composer of the music piece.
* **Music Genre (text)**: Enter the genre of the music.
* **Music Year (number)**: Enter the year the music was created.
* **Prophets Name (text)**: Enter the prophet's name (this field is currently unused and may be removed in the future).
* **Music Format (select_multiple)**: Select the formats in which your music is available (e.g., mp3, wav, flac).
* **Competition Name (text)**: Enter the name of the competition you are applying for.
* **Submission Date (date)**: Enter the date you are submitting your music.
* **Additional Comments (note)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your application.
