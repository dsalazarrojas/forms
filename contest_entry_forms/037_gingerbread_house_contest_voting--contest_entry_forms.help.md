# gingerbread_house_contest_voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gingerbread house contest voting form is designed to collect ratings and comments from users to evaluate the quality of the gingerbread house.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Navigate to the gingerbread house contest voting form.
2. Follow the instructions below for each field to complete the form accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vote** (page 3): Select "yes" or "no" to cast your vote.
* **Comment** (page 2): Write your comments about the gingerbread house.
* **Email** (page 4): Enter your email address if you wish to be notified of the voting results.
* **Phone** (page 5): Enter your phone number if you wish to receive additional information.
* **Rating 2, 3, 4, 5** (pages 6-9): Rate the gingerbread house using the corresponding rating field.
* **Comments** (page 10): Write any additional comments about the gingerbread house.
* **Verified Vote** (page 11): Select "yes" or "no" to indicate whether your vote is verified or not.

## Tips
* Please make sure to complete the form accurately and honestly to ensure the integrity of the voting results.
* You can skip any optional fields if you do not have the necessary information.
* Please submit your vote by clicking the "Submit" button.
