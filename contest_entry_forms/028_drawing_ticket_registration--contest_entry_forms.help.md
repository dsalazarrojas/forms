# Drawing Ticket Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering for a contest or drawing. Please fill out the form with your personal and relevant information to participate in the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out each field with the required and optional information as prompted.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Drawing Type** (`drawing_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of drawing you are registering for (Raffle, Giveaway, or Other).
* **Prize ID** (`prize_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of the prize you are interested in.
* **Drawing Date** (`drawing_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the drawing.
* **Entry Date** (`entry_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are entering the drawing.
* **Entry Time** (`entry_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are entering the drawing.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Category ID** (`category_id`, text, optional): Enter the category ID (this field may be for internal use).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter any assigned tool or equipment (this field may be for internal use).
* **Category ID** is repeated in the YAML, so I removed it to avoid confusion.
Please make sure to review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness. 

Note: The fields marked as optional can be left blank if not applicable. 

**Tips**
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
