# Scan To Win Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is the entry form for the "Scan To Win" contest. Users can fill out this form to participate in the contest by scanning a specific promotion code.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out all the required fields, indicated by a red asterisk (*).
- Enter your email address in the format of `username@example.com`.
- Enter your first and last name exactly as they appear on your identification or official documents.
- Enter your phone number in the format of `123-456-7890`.
- Enter your promotion code, which can be found on the packaging or product packaging.
- If you're having trouble finding your promotion code, you can contact the contest administrators for help.
- If you want to leave a message for the organizers, you can type any additional information in the `User Message` field.
- Once you've filled out all the fields, submit the form to enter the contest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Email (email, optional):**
 Enter your email address to be contacted if you're selected as a winner.
* **First Name (text, optional):**
 Enter your first name, as it appears on your identification or official documents.
* **Last Name (text, optional):**
 Enter your last name, as it appears on your identification or official documents.
* **Phone Number (text, optional):**
 Enter your phone number in the format of `123-456-7890`.
* **Promotion Code (text, optional):**
 Enter the promotion code found on the packaging or product packaging.
* **Promotion Code Confirmation (text, optional):**
 Verify your promotion code is correct to ensure your entry is valid.
* **User Message (text, optional):**
 Leave any additional information you'd like to share with the contest organizers in this field.
