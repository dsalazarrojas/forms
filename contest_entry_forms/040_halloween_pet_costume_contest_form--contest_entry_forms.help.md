# Halloween Pet Costume Contest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your pet's Halloween costume and you as the submitter. Please fill out this form to submit your pet's entry into the Halloween costume contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information about your pet.
2. Make sure to submit a valid photo of your pet in its costume.
3. Review and confirm all the entered information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Halloween Pet Costume Form**: This is the title of the form and the entry point for your pet's submission.
* **Pet Info**: Enter a brief description of your pet, including its name, type, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Pet Costume**: Describe your pet's costume, including its name and a brief description.
* **Submitter Info**: Enter your name and any additional details about you, the submitter.
* **Submitter Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth, if applicable.
* **Pet Height**: Enter your pet's height, if applicable.
* **Pet Weight**: Enter your pet's weight, if applicable.
* **Pet Costume Description**: Describe your pet's costume in more detail.
* **Photo URL**: Enter the URL of a valid photo of your pet in its costume.
* **Costume Category**: Enter the category of your pet's costume.
* **Costume Color**: Enter the color of your pet's costume.
* **Submitter Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your submission.
* **Pet Costume Name**: Enter the name of your pet's costume.
* **Photo ID**: Not required, but if you have a unique ID for your photo, enter it here.

Please submit your entry and good luck in the contest!
