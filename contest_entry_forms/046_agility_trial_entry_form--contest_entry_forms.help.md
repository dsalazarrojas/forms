# Agility Trial Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agility Trial Entry Form is designed for dog owners to provide necessary information about themselves, their dogs, and their trial preferences. This information is crucial for efficient trial organization and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. For fields with multiple options (like dog breeds or trial classes), select the relevant options carefully.
3. Make sure to review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Full Name** (`owner_full_name`, text, required): Enter your complete name as it appears on official documents.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for trial updates and notifications.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Owner Address** (`owner_address`, text, required): Enter your complete address for trial communication and map purposes.
* **Dog Name** (`dog_name`, text, required): Enter your dog's name as it appears on their registration documents.
* **Dog Breed** (`dog_breed`, text, required): Select your dog's breed to help us understand their capabilities.
* **Dog Age** (`dog_age`, number, required): Enter your dog's age in years to ensure they're eligible for the trial.
* **Dog Gender** (`dog_gender`, select_one, required): Select your dog's gender from the options provided.
* **Dog Weight** (`dog_weight`, number, required): Enter your dog's weight in pounds to determine their class eligibility.
* **Dog Height at Withers** (`dog_height_at_withers`, number, optional): Enter your dog's height at withers in inches (if applicable).
* **Rabies Vaccination Date** (`rabies_vaccination_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the last rabies vaccination for your dog.
* **DHPP Vaccination Date** (`dhpp_vaccination_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the last DHPP vaccination for your dog.
* **Trial Name** (`trial_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the agility trial you wish to participate in.
* **Trial Date** (`trial_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the trial.
* **Trial Location** (`trial_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the trial.
* **Entry Classes Requested** (`entry_classes_requested`, select_multiple, required): Select the classes you wish to enter, considering your dog's abilities and the trial rules.
* **Dog's Agility Experience** (`dog_agility_experience`, select_one, required): Select your dog's level of agility experience.
* **Special Accommodations** (`special_accommodations`, text, optional): If your dog requires any special accommodations, enter them here.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact for your dog.
* **Waiver Agreement** (`waiver_agreement`, select_one, required): Select whether you agree to the liability waiver for the trial.

Please review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any mistakes or omissions. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
