# Dance Festival Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Festival Entry Form is a form designed to collect information from dance schools and their contacts to facilitate the organization of the dance festival. This form is essential for gathering necessary details about the dance schools, including their contact information and dance school specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your title to represent your dance school. 
2. Provide the parent's or guardian's information in the "Parent Information" field.
3. Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address. This will be used as the primary contact point for the festival organizers.
4. Enter your dance school's contact information, including name, phone number, and email address. This will be used to communicate with your school.
5. If applicable, enter the details about the school's dance program, such as school name. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information (Parent-Information)** (`parent_information`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the parent or guardian responsible for your school.
* **Contact Information (Contact-Phone)** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and phone number. This will be used as the primary contact point for the festival organizers.
* **Contact Information (Contact-Email)** (`contact_email`, `type`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address. This will be used for communication with your school.
* **Name of Dance School (Name-of-Dance-School)** (`name_of_dance_school`, `text`, required: false): Enter your school name, if applicable.
* **Dance School Information (Dance-School-Phone)** (`dance_school_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your school's phone number. If your school has multiple locations, enter the main contact number.
* **Dance School Information (Dance-School-Email)** (`dance_school_email`, `type`, `email`, required: false): Enter your school's email address. This will be used for communication with your school.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date contact information to ensure that festival organizers can reach you efficiently.
* If your school has multiple locations, enter the main contact information.
* Double-check your entries before submitting to prevent errors and duplicates.
