# Cultural Society Costume Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Society Costume Tournament Entry Form is a submission form for users to participate in the Cultural Society Costume Tournament. This form is used to collect essential information about your costume entry, including your name, the name and description of your costume, event details, and other relevant information. Your completed form will be reviewed and considered for selection by the Cultural Society team.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure a successful submission, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Contestant Name**, the name of the person submitting the entry.
2. Enter the **Costume Name**, a brief description of your costume.
3. (Optional) Enter a **Photo URL** if you have one; otherwise, you can skip this field.
4. Enter the **Event Name**, the name of the event your costume will be featured in.
5. Enter the **Event Date**, the date your costume will be featured in the event.
6. Select the **Event Time**, the time your costume will be featured in the event.
7. Provide a brief **Description** of your costume.
8. Choose a **Category** for your costume.
9. Enter the **Photo Credit**, the name(s) of the photographer(s) who took the photo.
10. Enter a brief **Photo Description**, a description of the photo.
11. Enter your **Submitter's Name**, the name of the person submitting the entry.
12. Enter your **Submitter's Email**, the email address of the person submitting the entry.
13. Confirm your **Submitter's Email** to ensure accuracy.
14. (Optional) Enter your **Submitter's Phone**, the phone number of the person submitting the entry.
15. Confirm your **Submitter's Phone** to ensure accuracy.
16. Select the **Event Type**, the type of event your costume will be featured in.
17. Enter the **Event Organizer**, the name of the event organizer.
18. Enter the **Event Location**, the location of the event.
19. (Optional) Enter the **Entry ID**, a unique ID for your entry; otherwise, you can skip this field.
20. (Optional) Enter the **Entry URL**, a URL to a webpage with more information about your entry; otherwise, you can skip this field.
21. (Optional) Enter a brief **Entry Description**, a description of your entry.
22. (Optional) Enter the **Entry Image URL**, a URL to an image of your entry; otherwise, you can skip this field.
23. (Optional) Enter a brief **Entry Image Description**, a description of the image.
24. Sign with your **Submitter's Signature**.
25. Select the **Submission Date**, the date you submit your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contestant Name** (`contestant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the entry.
* **Costume Name** (`costume_name`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your costume.
* **Photo URL** (`photo_url`, text, optional): (Optional) Enter a URL to a photo of your costume if you have one.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event your costume will be featured in.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date your costume will be featured in the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Select the time your costume will be featured in the event.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your costume.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required): Choose a category for your costume.
* **Photo Credit** (`photo_credit`, text, required): Enter the name(s) of the photographer(s) who took the photo.
* **Photo Description** (`photo_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the photo.
* **Submitter's Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the entry.
* **Submitter's Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person submitting the entry.
* **Confirm Submitter's Email** (`submitter_email_confirmation`, email, required): Confirm the email address of the person submitting the entry.
* **Submitter's Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): (Optional) Enter the phone number of the person submitting the entry.
* **Confirm Submitter's Phone** (`submitter_phone_confirmation`, text, required): Confirm the phone number of the person submitting the entry.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, text, required): Select the type of event your costume will be featured in.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, required): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Entry ID** (`entry_id`, text, optional): (Optional) Enter a unique ID for your entry.
* **Entry URL** (`entry_url`, text, optional): (Optional) Enter a URL to a webpage with more information about your entry.
* **Entry Description** (`entry_description`, text, optional): (Optional) Enter a brief description of your entry.
* **Entry Image URL** (`entry_image_url`, text, optional): (Optional) Enter a URL to an image of your entry.
* **Entry Image Description** (`entry_image_description`, text, optional): (Optional) Enter a brief description of the image.
* **Submitter's Signature** (`submitter_signature`, text, required): Sign with your name.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Select the date you submit your entry.
