# Poultry Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Poultry Entry Form is used to record the sale of poultry products. It captures essential details about the sale, including the date, buying and selling prices, quantity sold, customer contact information, and any additional comments or notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the sale in the "Date of Sale" field.
2. Enter the buying price of the poultry product(s) sold.
3. Enter the quantity of poultry product(s) sold.
4. Enter the selling price of the poultry product(s) sold.
5. Enter the customer's name.
6. Enter the customer's contact number.
7. Enter the customer's email address.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes about the sale in the "Comments" and "Notes" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Sale (Date of Sale, Date, required: false)**: Enter the date when the sale was conducted.
* **Buying Price (Buying Price, Number, required: false)**: Enter the price the customer paid for the poultry product(s).
* **Quantity Bought (Quantity Bought, Number, required: false)**: Enter the amount of poultry product(s) sold.
* **Selling Price (Selling Price, Number, required: false)**: Enter the price you sold the poultry product(s) for.
* **Customer Name (Customer Name, Text, required: false)**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Contact Number (Customer Contact Number, Text, required: false)**: Enter the customer's contact number.
* **Customer Email (Customer Email, Email, required: false)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Comments (Comments, Note, required: false)**: Enter any additional comments about the sale.
* **Notes (Notes, Note, required: false)**: Enter any additional notes about the sale.

## Tips
* Double-check the entered data for accuracy.
* Ensure that the form is filled out for genuine sales transactions only.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
