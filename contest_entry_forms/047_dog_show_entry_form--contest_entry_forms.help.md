# Dog Show Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Dog Show Entry Form" is used by dog owners to register their dog for a dog show.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Show Information" page with details about your dog's participation in the show.
2. Enter the date of the dog show on the "Show Date" page.
3. Select the time slot for your dog's show on the "Show Time" page.
4. Provide the location of the dog show on the "Show Location" page.
5. Enter your name and any additional information about yourself on the "Owner Information" page.
6. Enter your name on the "Name" page.
7. Enter your email address on the "Email" page.
8. Enter your phone number on the "Phone" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Show Information** (`dog_show_entry_form`, text, required: false): This page is the first page of the form where you will provide basic information about your dog's participation in the show.
* **Show Date** (`show_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the dog show. Please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Show Time** (`show_time`, time, required: false): Select the time slot for your dog's show from the available options. Please note that the time may be in 24-hour format.
* **Show Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the dog show.
* **Owner Information** (`owner_info`, text, required: false): This is the second page of the form where you will provide more information about yourself as the owner or handler of the dog.
* **Name** (`owner_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the owner or handler of the dog.
* **Email** (`owner_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with any updates or information about the dog show.
* **Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you easily.
