# Vineyard Wine Tasting Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for a Vineyard Wine Tasting Tournament. The purpose of the form is to gather data about the wines that have been entered into the tournament, including their details, tasting notes, and ratings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the entry form with the necessary details about the wine, including its name, type, and any other relevant information.
2. Enter the winery's information, including their name and address.
3. Provide details about the entry, including the entry date and status.
4. Take notes about your tasting experience and rate the wine accordingly.
5. Add any comments or additional information you would like to provide.
6. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
7. If applicable, select the wine tool that was used to evaluate the wine.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Entry Form** (`entry_form`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the wine entry.
* **Wine Info** (`wine_info`, text, required: false): Enter the name and other details of the wine.
* **Winery Info** (`winery_info`, text, required: false): Enter the name and address of the winery.
* **Entry Details** (`entry_details`, text, required: false): Enter the date and status of the entry.
* **Wine Tasting** (`wine_tasting`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of wine tasting (Champagne, White, Red, Rosé, Other).
* **Wine Tasting Notes** (`wine_tasting_notes`, note, required: false): Enter your notes about the wine's taste, smell, and overall experience.
* **Wine Rating** (`wine_rating`, number, required: false): Rate the wine from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Wine Price** (`wine_price`, number, required: false): Enter the price of the wine.
* **Winery Name** (`winery_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the winery.
* **Winery Address** (`winery_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the winery.
* **Entry Date** (`entry_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the entry.
* **Entry Status** (`entry_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the entry (Not Judged, Not Qualified, Qualified, Winning).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the entry.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): Enter the tool used to taste the wine.
* **User ID** (`user_id`, text, required: false): Enter your user ID.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
