# Stallion Incentive Bonus Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stallion Incentive Bonus Entry Form is used to collect information for a stallion incentive bonus program. It is designed to provide stallion owners and breeders with the opportunity to claim bonuses for specific incentives and events related to their stallions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review each field carefully and only provide information relevant to the specific questions asked.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stallion Incentive Bonus Entry Form (\*):** 
    * This is the main field for entering the stallion's name or identifier.
* **Breeding Stallion Name (\*):** 
    * Enter the name of the stallion you are entering for the incentive bonus program.
* **Breeding Year (\*):** 
    * Enter the year the stallion was bred.
* **Stallion Breed (\*):** 
    * Select all breeds that apply to the stallion.
* **Stallion Breed (\*):** 
    * Select whether or not the stallion is of a specific breed.
* **Stallion Incentive (\*):** 
    * Enter the date of the incentive event or program.
* **Stallion Bonuses (\*):** 
    * Enter the bonus amount for the incentive.
* **Breeding Stallion Incentive Bonuses (\*):** 
    * Select all incentives and bonuses that apply to the stallion.
* **Breeding Date (\*):** 
    * Enter the date of the breeding event.
* **Bonus Type (\*):** 
    * Select all bonus types that apply to the stallion.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the program administrators for assistance.
