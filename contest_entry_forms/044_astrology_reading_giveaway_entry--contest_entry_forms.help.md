Astrology Reading Giveaway Entry - Help Guide
================

## Purpose
The Astrology Reading Giveaway Entry form is designed for participants to enter a giveaway contest to win an astrology reading. To enter, please provide the required information, including date of entry, personal details, and preferences. This form helps us understand your interest in astrology and your eligibility for the prize.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the entry date and time in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
2.  Provide your first name, last name, and email address.
3.  Enter your date of birth and birth city.
4.  Choose your sun sign (zodiac sign).
5.  Enter your mailing address, city, state/province, and ZIP/postal code.
6.  Select how you heard about this giveaway.
7.  Choose which social media platforms you follow.
8.  Decide if you want to subscribe to our newsletter.
9.  Rate your interest in astrology.
10.  Select the type of reading you would like to receive.
11.  Tell us why you would like to receive this reading.
12.  Choose your level of experience with astrology.
13.  If applicable, enter any additional referral entry codes.
14.  Confirm that you agree to the official giveaway rules.
15.  Verify that you are 18 years or older.
16.  Optionally, add any additional comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Entry Date** (`entry_date`, date, required): Enter the date and time you are submitting your entry.
*   **First Name** (`participant_first_name`, text, required): Type your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`participant_last_name`, text, required): Type your last name.
*   **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*   **Birth City** (`birth_city`, text, required): Type the city where you were born.
*   **Sun Sign (Zodiac)** (`sun_sign`, select one, required): Choose your zodiac sign from the options listed.
*   **Mailing Address** (`address`, text, required): Type your street address.
*   **City** (`city`, text, required): Type the city of your residence.
*   **State/Province** (`state_province`, text, required): Type your state or province.
*   **ZIP/Postal Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Type your postal code.
*   **Country** (`country`, text, required): Type the country of your residence.
*   **How Did You Hear About This Giveaway** (`giveaway_promo_code`, select one, required): Choose how you heard about this giveaway from the options listed.
*   **Following on Social Media** (`social_media_follow`, select multiple, required): Select any applicable social media platforms you follow.
*   **Subscribe to Newsletter** (`newsletter_subscription`, select one, required): Decide if you want to subscribe to our newsletter.
*   **Interest in Astrology** (`astrology_interest`, select one, required): Rate your interest level in astrology from the options listed.
*   **Preferred Type of Reading** (`reading_type_preference`, select one, required): Select the type of reading you would like to receive.
*   **Why Would You Like This Reading** (`why_want_reading`, text, required): Type the reason why you would like to receive this reading.
*   **Experience with Astrology** (`astrology_experience`, select one, required): Choose your level of experience with astrology from the options listed.
*   **Referral Entry Codes (if applicable)** (`referral_codes`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any additional referral entry codes.
*   **I Agree to the Official Giveaway Rules** (`terms_and_conditions`, select one, required): Confirm that you agree to the official giveaway rules.
*   **I am 18 years or older** (`age_verification`, select one, required): Verify that you are 18 years or older.
*   **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or questions.
*   **Entry Submission Date** (`entry_completion_date`, date, required): The date your entry was submitted.
