# Culinary Bake Off Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Culinary Bake Off Tournament Entry Form is designed to collect information from users who want to participate in the Culinary Bake Off Tournament. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that your entry is eligible for the competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your **Name** in the required field. This is a text input field where you can enter your name.
* Enter your **Email** in the required field. This is the email address you'll use to receive updates and notifications about the tournament.
* If you want to be contacted via phone, enter your **Phone** number in the optional field.
* Select your **Category** from the drop-down list of options (Cake, Cookie, Pie, or Other).
* Enter the **Name of Entry** for your dish, which should be a descriptive title.
* Provide a brief **Description of Entry**, explaining your dish.
* If you wish to disclose any **Dietary Restrictions**, enter them in this field.
* Choose the **Baking Time** category from the drop-down list (Under 1 hour, 1-3 hours, 3-6 hours, or More than 6 hours).
* Optionally, list the **Baking Equipment**, **Baking Surface**, **Baking Temperature**, and **Baking Ingredients** used for your entry.
* Enter the **Baking Method** used to create your entry.
* Upload a photo of your entry as a **Entry Image**.
* Optionally, you can upload a video of your baking process as a **Entry Video**.
* Read and agree to the **Terms and Conditions** before submitting your entry.
* Click the **Submit** button to submit your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Required, Text): Enter your name as you would like to be known in the competition.
* **Email** (Required, Email): Enter your contact email address for updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (Optional, Text): If you want to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number here.
* **Category** (Required, Select One): Select the category for your entry from the drop-down list (Cake, Cookie, Pie, or Other).
* **Name of Entry** (Required, Text): Enter a descriptive title for your entry.
* **Description of Entry** (Optional, Text): Briefly describe your entry to help the judges understand your creation.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (Optional, Text): If your entry has any dietary restrictions, list them here.
* **Baking Time** (Optional, Select One): Select the baking time category from the drop-down list (Under 1 hour, 1-3 hours, 3-6 hours, or More than 6 hours).
* **Baking Equipment** (Optional, Text): List the baking equipment used to create your entry.
* **Baking Surface** (Optional, Text): Specify the baking surface used for your entry.
* **Baking Temperature** (Optional, Text): List the baking temperature used for your entry.
* **Baking Ingredients** (Optional, Text): List the baking ingredients used for your entry.
* **Baking Method** (Optional, Text): Explain the baking method used to create your entry.
* **Entry Image** (Required, Text): Upload a photo of your entry.
* **Entry Video** (Optional, Text): Upload a video of your baking process.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Required, Note): Read and agree to the terms and conditions before submitting your entry.
* **Submit** (Required, Text): Click this button to submit your entry.
