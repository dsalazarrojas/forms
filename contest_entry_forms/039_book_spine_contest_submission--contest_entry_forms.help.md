The Book Spine Contest Submission form is designed for users to submit their book spine creation entries for a contest. This form collects personal and entry details, including contact information, entry description, and photo uploads.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
2.  Select your age group.
3.  Choose your contest category.
4.  Enter your entry title and description.
5.  Explain how your entry fits the theme.
6.  Specify the number of books used in your creation.
7.  Indicate where you obtained the books.
8.  Upload photos of your entry.
9.  Provide additional information on your creation time and team members (if applicable).
10.  Confirm your previous participation in contests.
11.  Specify how you heard about the contest.
12.  Agree to display your photo and share on social media (with or without credit).
13.  Agree to the contest rules.
14.  Submit the form with the current date.
