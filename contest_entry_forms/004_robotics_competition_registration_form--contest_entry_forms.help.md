# Robotics Competition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for the robotics competition. Please fill out the form with your team's and robot's details to participate in the competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields with your personal details.
2. Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
3. Enter your team name, address, city, and state for logistical information.
4. Provide your robot's name and type for identification.
5. Enter your robot's description for a brief overview.
6. (Optional) Select the start and end dates and times for the competition, if applicable.
7. Review and submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your personal first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your personal last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Team Name** (text, required): Enter your team's name.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter your team's address.
* **City** (text, required): Enter your team's city.
* **State** (text, required): Enter your team's state.
* **Zip** (text, required): Enter your team's zip code.
* **Start Date** (date, optional): If your robot has a start date, please enter it. This field is not required.
* **Start Time** (time, optional): If your robot has a start time, please enter it. This field is not required.
* **End Date** (date, optional): If your robot has an end date, please enter it. This field is not required.
* **End Time** (time, optional): If your robot has an end time, please enter it. This field is not required.
* **Robot Name** (text, required): Enter your robot's name.
* **Robot Type** (text, required): Enter your robot's type.
* **Description** (text, required): Enter a brief description of your robot.
* **Tips**

* Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy, as this will be the primary contact method for competition updates and notifications.
* If your robot has a specific start or end time, please ensure you enter it correctly to avoid conflicts with other participants.
* Be descriptive but concise with your robot's description to provide a clear understanding of your entry.

Note: This form is not affiliated with any form platforms, and the data collected will be used for the purpose of the robotics competition only. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
