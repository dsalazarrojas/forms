# Storytelling-Tournament-Entry-Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from participants to facilitate their entry in a storytelling contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the Storytelling Form (page 1) with your story in the designated text field.
2. Provide your Participant Details (page 2) in the text field.
3. Upload any relevant files or attachments in the Uploads section (page 3).
4. Click the Submit button to submit your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Storytelling Form (page 1)**:
	+ This is the main section of the form where you will enter your story.
	+ Please write your story in the text field provided.
	+ This section is not required, so you may skip it if you do not have a story.
* **Participant Details (page 2)**:
	+ This section is where you will provide your contact information and any other details relevant to your entry.
	+ Please fill out this section completely.
	+ This section is also not required, but it is recommended to ensure that we can contact you about your entry.
* **Uploads (page 3)**:
	+ This section is where you can upload any files that support your story, such as images or videos.
	+ Please only upload files that are relevant to your story.
	+ If you do not have any files to upload, you may skip this section.
* **Submit (page 4)**:
	+ This is the final step to submit your entry.
	+ Please click the Submit button to complete and submit your entry.
