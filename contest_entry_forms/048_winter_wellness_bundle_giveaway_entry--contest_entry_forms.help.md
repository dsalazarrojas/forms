# Winter Wellness Bundle Giveaway Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect entries for the "Winter Wellness Bundle Giveaway" contest. To avoid duplication, please ensure that each entry is unique.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the contest prize or recognition materials.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter a valid email address to which we will contact you if you win the contest. This will be used for prize notification and other contest-related communications.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a unique email address, as duplicate entries will not be accepted.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
