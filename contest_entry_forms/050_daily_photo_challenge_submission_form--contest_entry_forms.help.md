# Daily Photo Challenge Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and process photo submissions for the daily photo challenge. It's a simple form that gathers essential information about the photo and the submitter's details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, providing accurate information for each field.
2. Make sure to submit a new photo for each challenge day.
3. Double-check your submission before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Entry Details** (entry_details, text, optional): Briefly describe the photo you're submitting, including its title and a short description.
* **Photo URL** (photo_url, text, optional): Provide a direct link to the photo, which will be displayed in the challenge gallery.
* **Entry Text** (entry_text, text, optional): Add any additional text or context about the photo, such as the location where it was taken or any interesting stories related to the image.
* **Entry Date** (entry_date, date, optional): Select the date the photo was taken or the date you'd like it to be featured in the challenge gallery.
* **Entry Time** (entry_time, time, optional): Choose the time the photo was taken or the time you'd like it to be featured in the challenge gallery.
* **Notes** (entry_notes, text, optional): Add any additional information or comments about the photo.
* **Entry Email** (entry_email, email, optional): Provide your email address so we can contact you if there's an issue with your submission or if we need more information.

## Tips

* Make sure to proofread your submission carefully before clicking the "Submit" button.
* Use a clear and concise format for your entry details, photo URL, and entry text.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the challenge organizers at form@gic.mx.
