# Sweepstakes Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sweepstakes Entry Form is a tool to collect information from participants who wish to enter a sweepstakes contest. To complete the form, users will need to fill out their contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the field labeled "First name".
2. Enter your last name in the field labeled "Last name".
3. Enter your email address in the field labeled "Email".
4. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "Phone".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed in the sweepstakes entry list.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed in the sweepstakes entry list.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address that we will use to contact you if you are selected as a winner.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, which we will use to contact you if you are selected as a winner. Please note that we do not use this field for any other purpose and will not share it with third parties.

## Tips
Please make sure to enter a valid email address and phone number that you own or have access to. This will help us contact you if you are selected as a winner. You can only enter this sweepstakes once per person. If you have any issues or concerns about this form, please contact us.
