# Online Contest Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Contest Application Form is designed to collect information from users who wish to participate in an online contest. It helps us to assess your eligibility and preferences for the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the **User Information** section.
2. Provide your contact information in the **Contact Information** section.
3. Enter your email address correctly in the **Email** field.
4. Fill in your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Select the type of contest you are interested in from the **Contest Type** dropdown menu.
6. If applicable, choose the contest date and time in the **Contest Date** and **Contest Time** fields.
7. Add any additional contest-related notes in the **Contest Note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (<name>: <type>, required):** This section is for your personal details. Fill in your name, surname, and any other required information.
* **Contact Information (<name>: <type>, required):** This section is for your contact details. Please enter your address, city, state, and other relevant contact information.
* **Email (<name>: <type>, required):** This is your email address where we will contact you if you are selected.
* **Phone (<name>: <type>, required):** This is your phone number where we will contact you if you are selected.
* **Contest Type (<name>: <type>, required):** Select the type of contest you are interested in from the dropdown menu.
* **Contest Date (<name>: <type>, required):** If the contest is date-sensitive, choose the date you are interested in.
* **Contest Time (<name>: <type>, required):** If the contest is time-sensitive, choose the time you are interested in.
* **Contest Note (<name>: <type>, required):** This is an optional field for any additional notes about your contest entry.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled correctly to avoid any errors in the submission process.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your entry carefully and inform you about the outcome of the contest.
