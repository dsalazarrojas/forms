2. **Page 2: Superhero Feature Details**
	* **Power Description** (text): Briefly describe your superhero's unique abilities or powers.
3. **Attachments (optional)**
	* **Artwork** (text): Upload a image or a description of your superhero's appearance or symbol.
	* **Supporting Materials** (text): Upload any relevant materials such as character profiles, concept art, or storylines that support your superhero.
4. **Story Ideas (optional)**
	* **Story Tweak** (text): Suggest a story tweak or plot twist for your superhero.
