# Ballet Event Raffle Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Ballet Event Raffle Entry** form is used to register participants for a contest associated with a ballet event, ensuring that entrants meet the eligibility requirements and agree to receive promotional emails. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Full name** in the text field provided.
2. Provide your **Email address** for winner notification and promotional emails.
3. If you have a **Phone number**, you can enter it as an optional contact method.
4. Select your **Age group** from the dropdown menu to confirm eligibility for age-restricted prizes.
5. If you have a **Ticket number**, enter it to associate your entry with a purchased ticket.
6. Choose whether to **subscribe to event updates and offers** by selecting the 'True' option.
7. Confirm that you meet the **eligibility requirements** by selecting the 'I confirm I meet eligibility requirements' option.
8. Enter your **Consent agreement** by typing your full name, acknowledging that you understand the raffle rules and privacy policy.
9. Select a **Submission date** for your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name**: Enter your full first and last name as it should appear for the prize drawing.
* **Email address**: This will be used to notify you if you win and for promotional emails. 
* **Phone number**: Enter an optional phone number for alternate contact.
* **Age group**: Select your age group to confirm you're eligible for age-restricted prizes.
* **Ticket number**: If you've purchased a ticket, enter it to associate with your entry.
* **Subscribe to event updates and offers**: Choose 'True' to receive promotional emails.
* **Eligibility confirmation**: Confirm that you meet the eligibility requirements by selecting the 'I confirm I meet eligibility requirements' option.
* **Consent agreement**: Type your full name to acknowledge that you understand the raffle rules and privacy policy.
* **Submission date**: Select the date of your entry submission.

## Tips
- Double-check that you've entered your contact information accurately to ensure you receive notifications.
- Make sure to confirm your eligibility to avoid disqualified entries.
- Understand that by entering the contest, you're agreeing to receive promotional emails.
- Enter your consent agreement accurately to avoid any issues with your entry.
