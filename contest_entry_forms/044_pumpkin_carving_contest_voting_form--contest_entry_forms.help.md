# Pumpkin Carving Contest Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect entries, judging scores, and votes from individuals participating in a pumpkin carving contest. It allows contestants to submit their creations, judges to evaluate and score them, and finally, contestants to cast their votes for their favorite entry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Entry Form (Page 1)**: If you are a contestant, fill out this page to provide your entry information. 
   * **Entry Form (page 1)** (`entry_form`, text, not required): Fill in your name, email, and a brief description of your pumpkin carving design.

2. **Judge Form (Page 2)**: If you are a judge, fill out this page to evaluate and score the entries.
   * **Judge Form (page 2)** (`judge_form`, text, not required): Fill in the entry number and provide a score for the entry's creativity, technique, and overall impression.

3. **Voting Form (Page 3)**: Once all entries are evaluated, fill out this page to cast your vote for your favorite entry.
   * **Voting Form (page 3)** (`voting_form`, text, not required): Select your top choice for the best design.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entry Form (page 1)** (`entry_form`, text, not required): 
  - Fill in your name to identify yourself as a contestant.
  - Enter your email for contact purposes.
  - Provide a brief description of your pumpkin carving design to help judges understand its significance.
* **Judge Form (page 2)** (`judge_form`, text, not required): 
  - Identify the entry number for accurate scoring.
  - Evaluate the entry's creativity on a scale (though not provided, use any scale that suits you).
  - Assess the entry's technique on a scale (similar to above, use any scale you prefer).
  - Give an overall impression of the entry (this could be any scale or criteria you prefer).
* **Voting Form (page 3)** (`voting_form`, text, not required): 
  - Select your top choice for the best design based on your preferences and criteria.
  - You can select only one entry as your top choice.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
- If you are a judge, be fair and unbiased in your scoring and comments.
- If you are a contestant, do not vote for your own entry.
- The scores and votes are used to determine the winner of the contest, so please be sincere in your evaluations.
