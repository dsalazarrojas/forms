# Art Gallery Painting Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Art Gallery Painting Tournament Entry Form is designed to collect information from artists who wish to participate in the competition. This form should only be filled out by artists who have reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions of the competition.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Artist name** in the **Artist name** field.
2. Enter your **Contact email** in the **Contact email** field.
3. Enter the **Title of the artwork** in the **Artwork title** field.
4. Describe the **Medium** used in your artwork.
5. Provide the **Dimensions** of your artwork.
6. Confirm that you have **Paid the entry fee**.
7. Upload an **Image filename** of your artwork.
8. Write a **Short artist statement** about your artwork (optional).
9. Confirm that you **Agree to the competition terms**.
10. Add any **Additional notes** you'd like to share about your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Artist name (1)**: Enter your full name as it should be displayed in the competition.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes

- **Contact email (2)**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted regarding the competition.
  - Type: Email
  - Required: Yes

- **Artwork title (3)**: Enter a title for your artwork that is no more than 50 characters long.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes

- **Medium (4)**: Describe the materials used in your artwork.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes

- **Dimensions (5)**: Enter the width and height of your artwork.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes

- **Paid entry fee (6)**: Confirm that you have paid the entry fee for the competition.
  - Type: Select One
  - Required: Yes

- **Image filename (7)**: Enter the filename of the uploaded image.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes

- **Artist statement (8)**: Provide a short statement about your artwork, up to 500 characters.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No

- **Agree to competition terms (9)**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the competition terms.
  - Type: Select One
  - Required: Yes

- **Additional notes (10)**: Add any other information you'd like to share about your entry.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
