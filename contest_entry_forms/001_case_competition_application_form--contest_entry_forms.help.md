# Case Competition Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Case Competition Application Form is a tool to gather information from teams interested in participating in a case competition. The form is divided into several sections, each with specific fields to be filled out by the team members. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, teams should follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields in the "Team Information" section, which includes the team name, university or institution name, and team leader's full name, email address, and phone number.
2. In the "Team Member" section, list the full names of the other team members.
3. Move on to the "Application Details" section, where teams should describe their motivation and interest in the case competition, as well as provide a link to a folder containing their resumes.
4. Finally, select the preferred competition track and provide information on how they heard about the competition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`team_name`, required): This is the name of the team that will be participating in the case competition.
* **University or Institution Name** (`university_name`, required): This is the name of the university or institution that the team members are from.
* **Team Leader (Member 1)** (`leader_full_name`, required): This is the full name of the team leader.
* **Email Address** (`leader_email`, required): This is the email address of the team leader.
* **Phone Number** (`leader_phone`, required): This is the phone number of the team leader.
* **Major or Field of Study** (`leader_major`, required): This is the major or field of study of the team leader.
* **Member 2 Full Name** (`member_2_name`, required): This is the full name of the second team member.
* **Member 3 Full Name** (`member_3_name`, required): This is the full name of the third team member.
* **Member 4 Full Name** (`member_4_name`, optional): This is the full name of the fourth team member, which is optional.
* **Has your team participated in case competitions before** (`previous_experience`, required): This is a question to determine if the team has participated in case competitions before.
* **Why is your team interested in this specific case competition** (`interest_reason`, required): This is a field for the team to describe their motivation for participating in the competition.
* **Preferred Competition Track** (`preferred_track`, required): This is the area of focus for the team's case competition.
* **Link to Team Folder with Resumes** (`resume_link`, required): This is a field to provide a link to a Google Drive or Dropbox folder containing the team's resumes.
* **How did you hear about this competition** (`hearing_about_us`, optional): This is a field for the team to describe how they heard about the competition.
* **Other Team Members** (`other_members`, optional): This is a field for the team to list other team members and their majors. 

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully, as any incorrect information may disqualify the team from participating in the case competition.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the competition organizers.
* Make sure to provide a link to a Google Drive or Dropbox folder for the resumes, as this will be used for evaluation. 
* Make sure to select the correct competition track based on the team's area of interest.
