# Virtual Halloween Cosplay Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Halloween Cosplay Contest Entry Form is designed for users to submit their entries for a virtual Halloween cosplay contest. The form collects essential information about the entrant and their submission, including personal details, cosplay information, and submission preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled in correctly, especially the required fields (marked with *).
3. Review your entry before submitting to ensure it's correct and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, *required*): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, *required*): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, *required*): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, *required*): Provide a brief description of your cosplay submission.
* **Cosplay Name** (`cosplay_name`, `text`, *required*): Enter the name of your cosplay submission.
* **Character** (`character`, `text`, *required*): Enter the name of the character you're cosplaying.
* **Designer Name** (`designer_name`, `text`, *required*): Enter the name of the designer or artist who created your cosplay.
* **Image URL** (`image_url`, `text`, *required*): Enter the URL of an image of your cosplay submission.
* **Entry Date** (`entry_date`, `date`, *optional*): Enter the date when you completed your cosplay (if applicable).
* **Entry Time** (`entry_time`, `time`, *optional*): Enter the time when you completed your cosplay (if applicable).
* **Category** (`category`, `text`, *required*): Choose a category for your cosplay submission (e.g., Best Costumes, Best Makeup, etc.).
* **Theme** (`theme`, `text`, *required*): Choose a theme for your cosplay submission (e.g., Horror, Fantasy, etc.).
* **Tag** (`tag`, `text`, *required*): Enter tags for your cosplay submission, separated by commas.
* **Entry Type** (`entry_type`, `text`, *required*): Choose the type of entry (e.g., Individual, Group, etc.).
* **Is Artist** (`is_artist`, `select_one`, *required*): Choose whether you are an artist or not.
* **Is Merch** (`is_merch`, `select_one`, *required*): Choose whether your submission is merchandise or not.
* **Is Free To Use** (`is_free_to_use`, `select_one`, *optional*): Choose whether your submission is free to use or not.
* **Is Free For All** (`is_free_for_all`, `select_one`, *required*): Choose whether your submission is free for all or not.
* **Is Free On Web** (`is_free_on_web`, `select_one`, *optional*): Choose whether your submission is free on the web or not.
* **Is Free On Etsy** (`is_free_on_etsy`, `select_one`, *optional*): Choose whether your submission is free on Etsy or not.
* **Free Text** (`free_text`, `text`, *optional*): Enter any free text about your submission.
* **Price** (`price`, `number`, *required*): Enter the price of your submission, if applicable.
* **Tags** (`tags`, `select_multiple`, *required*): Enter tags for your submission, separated by commas.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- Review your entry before submitting to ensure it's correct and complete.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact us for assistance.
