# Halloween Costume Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users who want to participate in the Halloween costume contest. Please note that each field has been carefully crafted to help you provide the necessary details for your entry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by uploading a clear picture of your costume in the **Halloween Costume Picture** field.
2. In the **Description** field, provide a brief description of your costume, including any relevant details that showcase its creativity and originality.
3. Enter your **Name** in the corresponding field, as it will be used to identify you in the contest.
4. Provide a valid **Email** address to ensure that we can contact you in case of any updates or notifications.
5. Select the **Category** that best describes your costume, such as Fantasy, Horror, or Superhero.
6. If applicable, select your **Sub Category** from the options provided.
7. For the **Select Multiple** fields, choose one or multiple options that fit your entry. 
8. In the **Date of Birth** and **Time of Birth** fields, enter the birthdate and time of when you were born.
9. If you have any **Additional Comments**, provide any relevant details that might help our judges evaluate your entry.
10. For the remaining **Select One** and **Select Multiple** fields, choose one or multiple options that fit your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Halloween Costume Picture**: Upload a clear picture of your Halloween costume. A minimum of 1024 x 768 pixels and 300 DPI is recommended for optimal viewing.
* **Description**: Briefly describe your costume, highlighting its creativity and originality.
* **Name**: Provide your name as it will be displayed in the contest.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive updates and notifications about the contest.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact number, if you wish to be contacted.
* **Category**: Choose the category that best represents your costume, such as Fantasy, Horror, or Superhero.
* **Sub Category**: If applicable, choose the sub-category that fits your costume.
* **Select Multiple**: Choose one or multiple options that best describe your entry. 
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your birthdate in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Birth**: Enter your birth time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information that might help our judges evaluate your entry.
* **Select One**: Choose one option that best describes your entry.
* **Select Multiple 2**: Choose one or multiple options that best describe your entry.
* **Select Multiple 3**: Choose one or multiple options that best describe your entry.
