# Win A Getaway Contest Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from contest participants to register them for a contest with multiple prizes or benefits. Participants can provide their contact information and select their preferred options.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's pages and understand the fields you need to fill out.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Get Away Entry Form" if applicable.
3. Enter your full name in the "Title" field.
4. Enter your valid phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Enter a valid email address in the "Contact Email" field.
6. Choose the date of your entry in the "Entry Date" field.
7. Choose the time of your entry in the "Entry Time" field.
8. Provide an optional note in the "Note" field.
9. Select one of the choices below for "Your Choice", "Your Choice 2", "Your Choice 3", "Your Choice 4", and "Your Choice 6" fields.
10. Review your selections and submit the form.
