# Future Cities Project Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for teams to submit their project ideas, innovations, and concepts to the Future Cities Project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Provide accurate and complete information in each field.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure you haven't missed any required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission Details** (`submission_details`, text, required: false): This field is for providing a short description of your submission.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your team.
* **Team Description** (`team_description`, text, required: false): Describe your team in a few words.
* **Team Member 1** (`team_member_1`, text, required: false): Enter the name of Team Member 1.
* **Team Member 2** (`team_member_2`, text, required: false): Enter the name of Team Member 2.
* **Team Member 3** (`team_member_3`, text, required: false): Enter the name of Team Member 3.
* **Team Member 4** (`team_member_4`, text, required: false): Enter the name of Team Member 4.
* **Team Member 5** (`team_member_5`, text, required: false): Enter the name of Team Member 5.
* **Supporting Files** (`supporting_files`, text, required: false): Upload any supporting files related to your submission.
* **File 1** (`file1`, text, required: false): This field is meant to upload a file, but no further information is provided. Please use the "Supporting Files" field instead.
* **File 2** (`file2`, text, required: false): This field is also meant to upload a file, but no further information is provided. Please use the "Supporting Files" field instead.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false): This field is for providing the date of submission. However, it's not a date picker, so please ensure you enter a valid date in the correct format.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required: false): This field is for providing the time of submission. Like the "Submission Date" field, it's not a time picker, so please ensure you enter a valid time in the correct format.
* **Team Leader E-mail** (`team_leader_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of your team leader.
* **Team Leader Phone** (`team_leader_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your team leader.
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): This field is for specifying the city related to your submission.
* **Innovation** (`innovation`, text, required: false): Enter a name for your innovation.
* **Innovation Description** (`innovation_description`, text, required: false): Describe your innovation briefly.
* **Innovation Category** (`innovation_category`, text, required: false): Specify the category of your innovation.
* **Innovation Tags** (`innovation_tags`, text, required: false): Add relevant tags for your innovation.
* **University** (`university`, text, required: false): Mention the university related to your submission.
* **Hackathon** (`hackathon`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the hackathon related to your submission.
* **Innovation Program** (`innovation_program`, text, required: false): Specify the innovation program related to your submission.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose one or more of the provided options for the submission status.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, note, required: false): Add any comments for your submission.

## Tips
* Please fill this form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth submission process.
* If you're unsure about any field, please review the provided explanations above.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact the project administrators.
