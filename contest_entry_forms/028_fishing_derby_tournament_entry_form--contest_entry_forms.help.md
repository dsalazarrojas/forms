# Fishing Derby Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fishing Derby Tournament Entry Form is used to collect information about participants in a fishing derby tournament. This form helps track and record the participant's details, species caught, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant information in the "Participant Information" section. This includes your name and any other relevant details you'd like to provide.
2. Describe the species you caught and the time you entered the water to catch it in the "Species caught and Entry Time" section.
3. Enter the name of the fishing derby you're participating in in the "Derby Name" section.
4. Provide your participant's contact information in the "Participant Contact" and "Participant Contact 2" sections. This could include phone numbers, email addresses, or other ways to reach you in case we need more information.
5. If you're fishing on behalf of someone else, you can provide their species information in the "Species" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information (id: 1)**: Enter your name and any other relevant details you'd like to provide.
* **Species caught and Entry Time (id: 2)**: Describe the species you caught and the time you entered the water to catch it. Please include the date and any other relevant details.
* **Derby Name (id: 3)**: Enter the name of the fishing derby you're participating in.
* **Participant Contact (id: 4)**: Provide your participant's contact information, such as a phone number or email address. This will help us get in touch with you if we need more information.
* **Participant Contact 2 (id: 5)**: If you have a secondary contact person, provide their information here.
* **Participant Species (id: 6)**: If you're fishing on behalf of someone else, enter their species information here.
* **Species (id: 7)**: Describe the species you caught and any other relevant details about it.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete entry information.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your participation in the fishing derby and look forward to seeing your catch!
