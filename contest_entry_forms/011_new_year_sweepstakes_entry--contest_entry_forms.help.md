# new_year_sweepstakes_entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_year_sweepstakes_entry form is used to collect information from participants who are interested in entering a new year's sweepstakes promotion. This form is designed to gather contact and personal information from participants, as well as their preferences and agreements for the sweepstakes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select how you would like to enter the sweepstakes (First, Last, or Both).
2. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email_address" field.
5. Enter your phone number (optional).
6. Enter your birthday in the "birthday" field.
7. If you have a specific date of interest, you can enter it in the "anniversary" field.
8. Check the box to indicate that you are interested in participating in the sweepstakes (sweepstakes_pledge).
9. If you are interested in participating in other sweepstakes, you can enter additional information in the "other_sweepstakes_pledge" field.
10. Check the box to agree to the terms and conditions of the sweepstakes.
11. Click the "submit" button to complete your entry.
