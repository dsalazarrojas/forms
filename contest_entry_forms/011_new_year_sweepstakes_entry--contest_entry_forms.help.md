# new_year_sweepstakes_entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_year_sweepstakes_entry form is used to collect information from participants who are interested in entering a new year's sweepstakes promotion. This form is designed to gather contact and personal information from participants, as well as their preferences and agreements for the sweepstakes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select how you would like to enter the sweepstakes (First, Last, or Both).
2. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email_address" field.
5. Enter your phone number (optional).
6. Enter your birthday in the "birthday" field.
7. If you have a specific date of interest, you can enter it in the "anniversary" field.
8. Check the box to indicate that you are interested in participating in the sweepstakes (sweepstakes_pledge).
9. If you are interested in participating in other sweepstakes, you can enter additional information in the "other_sweepstakes_pledge" field.
10. Check the box to agree to the terms and conditions of the sweepstakes.
11. Click the "submit" button to complete your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **entry_type** (`entry_type`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to enter the sweepstakes (First, Last, or Both).
* **first_name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **email_address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **birthday** (`birthday`, date, required): Enter your birthday.
* **anniversary** (`anniversary`, date, optional): If you have a specific date of interest, enter it here (optional).
* **sweepstakes_pledge** (`sweepstakes_pledge`, select_multiple, required): Check the box to indicate that you are interested in participating in the sweepstakes.
* **other_sweepstakes_pledge** (`other_sweepstakes_pledge`, text, optional): If you are interested in participating in other sweepstakes, enter additional information here (optional).
* **terms_and_conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_multiple, required): Check the box to agree to the terms and conditions of the sweepstakes.
* **submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click the "submit" button to complete your entry.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Check your email address and phone number for errors before submitting.
