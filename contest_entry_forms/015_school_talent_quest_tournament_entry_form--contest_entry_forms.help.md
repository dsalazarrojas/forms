# School Talent Quest Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The School Talent Quest Tournament Entry Form is designed to gather information from students participating in the tournament. This form is essential for tracking and recording student details, which will help in various administrative and analytical tasks.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully fill out the required fields, marked with a red asterisk (*), and provide accurate information for each field.
2. Review and understand the hints provided for each field to ensure you fill it out correctly.
3. Use the field-by-field explanations below for more detailed help with specific fields.
