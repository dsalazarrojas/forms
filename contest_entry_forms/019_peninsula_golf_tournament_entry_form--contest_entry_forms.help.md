# Peninsula Golf Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Peninsula Golf Tournament Entry Form is designed to collect demographic data and tournament-specific information from each participant. The information collected will help us to better understand our participants' backgrounds and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Click on each field and enter your information
* For each field, enter the required information in the corresponding format (e.g., text, date, number, etc.)
* Make sure to select the correct options for select_one and select_multiple fields (e.g., Course A, Course B, HC1, etc.)
* Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tournament Name** (`tournament_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the tournament you are participating in. This field is not required.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it should be displayed on your tournament materials. This field is not required.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you. This field is not required.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach you. This field is not required.
* **Participant Gender** (`participant_gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender from the provided options: Male, Female, or Other.
* **HCCs** (`participant_hc`, select_multiple, optional): Select any applicable Handicaps from the provided options: HC1, HC2, or HC3.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, optional): Enter your age. This field is not required.
* **Golf Course** (`golf_course`, select_one, optional): Select the course you will be playing on from the provided options: Course A, Course B, or Course C.
* **Handicap Index** (`handicap_index`, number, optional): Enter your handicap index.
* **Handicap HCP** (`handicap_hcp`, number, optional): Enter your handicap HCP.
* **Entry Date** (`entry_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your entry.
* **Entry Time** (`entry_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your entry.
* **Entry Fee** (`entry_fee`, number, optional): Enter the entry fee you paid.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your entry. This field is not required.
