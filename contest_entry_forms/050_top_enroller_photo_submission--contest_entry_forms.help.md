# top_enroller_photo_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by participants to submit a photo as part of a top enroller program. The form collects the participant's details and their photo submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please ensure you have a good quality photo that meets the submission requirements. Follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant details in the "Participant Details" page.
2. Upload your photo in the "Participant Photo" page.
3. Write a brief caption for your photo in the "Photo Caption" page.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`Participant Details`, `text`, required/optional): This section is where you provide your basic information, such as your name, email, and any other relevant details that you'd like to share with the program organizers.

### Participant Photo
* **Participant Photo** (`Participant Photo`, `text`, required/optional): Upload a good quality photo of yourself that is relevant to the top enroller program. Please ensure the photo is clear and meets the submission requirements.

### Photo Caption
* **Photo Caption** (`Photo Caption`, `text`, required/optional): Write a brief description of your photo in this field. This will be displayed alongside your photo, so keep it concise and clear.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, required/optional): Once you have filled out all the required fields, click this button to submit your form. Your photo submission will be reviewed and acknowledged by the program organizers.

## Tips
* Ensure that your photo is of good quality and relevant to the top enroller program.
* Please follow the submission requirements carefully to avoid any issues with your form submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
