# Creative Writing Competition Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Writing Competition Entry form is used to collect information from students who wish to participate in a creative writing competition. The form is used to gather essential details about the student's entry, including their name, email, and submission information. By filling out this form, students can submit their creative work for review and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your **User Name**, which should match the name of the student submitting the entry.
2. Enter your **email**, which will be used to contact you with updates and instructions.
3. Provide a title for your **Entry Title**.
4. Choose a **genre** for your entry, such as Fantasy, Romance, or Science Fiction.
5. Type out your **Story** in the text box provided.
6. Select a **Category** for your entry, which will help us understand its genre better.
7. If you have additional files to submit, provide their **Upload URL** or upload them directly to the form.
8. Fill out your **Submitter Name**, which should match the name of the person submitting the entry.
9. Enter the **Submitter Email**, which will be used for communication.
10. Select the permissions for your work, such as Parent, Teacher, Guardian, or School.
11. If you have any additional notes, you can type them in the **Note** field.
12. Select the **Upload Type** of your file, such as PDF, Image, or Video.
13. Agree to the **Terms of Use**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Your real name as a student.
* **email**: Your email address for communication.
* **Entry Title**: A brief title for your entry.
* **genre**: The genre of your entry, such as Fantasy or Romance.
* **Story**: Type out your creative work here.
* **Category**: A specific category for your entry, like Fiction or Non-Fiction.
* **Upload File**: If you have additional files to upload, provide their URLs or upload them directly.
* **Upload URL**: The URL of the file you're uploading.
* **Submitter Name**: The name of the person submitting the entry, which should match the User Name.
* **Submitter Email**: The email address of the person submitting the entry.
* **Submitter Permission**: The permissions granted for your work.
* **Note**: Any additional notes about your submission.
* **Upload Type**: The type of file you're uploading, such as PDF or Image.
* **Upload URL**: The URL of the uploaded file.
* **Agreed**: Your agreement to the Terms of Use.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* Ensure the permissions align with your school's policies.
* Be clear about your Upload Type and URL if you're uploading files.
