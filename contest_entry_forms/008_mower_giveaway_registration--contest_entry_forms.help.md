# Mower Giveaway Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect registration information for a contest where users can enter to win a free mower. The form is used to gather personal details, contact information, and preferences for the contest.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the required fields with your personal details: **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Street Address**, **City**, **Postal Code**, **Phone Number**, and **Email**. Ensure that your email address is accurate as it will be used for contact and confirmation purposes.
2. Confirm your email address by entering it again in the **Confirm Email** field.
3. Confirm that you have read and understood the rules in the **Rules Confirmation** field.
4. Select your preferred contact method in the **Preferred Contact Method** field.
5. Choose which contest you would like to enter in the **Which Contest Would You Like to Enter?** field.
6. Choose the type of mower you would like to win in the **What Type of Mower Would You Like to Win?** field.
7. If desired, select a brand of mower you would like in the **Which Brand of Mower Do You Like?** field.
8. Enter your age in the **Age** field.
9. Select your gender in the **Gender** field.
10. Enter your country in the **Country** field.
11. Select your province in the **Province** field.
12. Enter your state in the **State** field.
13. Enter your zip code in the **Zip Code** field.
14. Select your region in the **Region** field.
15. If desired, enter your preferred language in the **Preferred Language** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Street Address**: Enter your street address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **Postal Code**: Enter your postal code.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address by re-entering it.
* **Rules Confirmation**: Confirm that you have read and understood the rules.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Mail).
* **Which Contest Would You Like to Enter?**: Select which contest you would like to enter (Mower Giveaway, Lawn Care Giveaway, Outdoor Equipment Giveaway).
* **What Type of Mower Would You Like to Win?**: Select the type of mower you would like to win (Gas Mower, Electric Mower, Cordless Mower).
* **Which Brand of Mower Do You Like?**: If desired, select a brand of mower you would like.
* **Age**: Enter your age.
* **Gender**: Select your gender (Male, Female, Other).
* **Country**: Enter your country.
* **Province**: Select your province.
* **State**: Select your state.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code.
* **Region**: Select your region.
* **Preferred Language**: If desired, select your preferred language.
