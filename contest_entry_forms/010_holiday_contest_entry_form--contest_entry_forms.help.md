# Holiday Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Contest Entry Form is designed for users to submit their entries for a holiday contest. This form allows contestants to provide their contact information and upload supporting materials for the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely, including all required and optional fields.
* Upload any required or optional files as specified in the form.
* Review and submit your entry before finalizing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Holiday Contest Entry Form** (`holiday_contest_entry_form_page_1`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your contest entry.
* **Submission Upload** (`submission_upload`, text, optional): Upload your submission file, such as a short story, poem, or artwork.
* **Photo Upload** (`photo_upload`, text, optional): Upload a photo related to your contest entry.
* **Document Upload** (`document_upload`, text, optional): Upload any supporting documents, such as receipts or certificates.
* **Contestant Name** (`contestant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it should be displayed in the contest.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting.
* Only upload files that are relevant to your contest entry.
* Use clear and concise descriptions for your uploaded files.
* Contest entries are not guaranteed to be displayed or considered for the contest, and submission does not imply guarantee of participation.
