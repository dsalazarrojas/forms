# Sworn Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sworn Declaration Form is a document that requires users to provide personal and contact information. This form is intended to collect details about the user's identity and declaration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the personal details:
	* Enter your first name on page 1.
	* Enter your last name on page 1.
	* Enter your date of birth on page 1.
	* Enter your place of residence on page 1.
	* Enter your email address on page 2.
	* Enter your phone number on page 2.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. First Name
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name, which is typically your given name. This information will help us identify you.

### 2. Last Name
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name, which is typically your surname or family name. This information will help us verify your identity.

### 3. Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This information will help us confirm your age and identity.

### 4. Place of Residence
* **Place of Residence** (text, optional): Enter your current place of residence, including street address or post office box. This information will help us understand your location.

### 5. Email
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter a valid email address that you have access to. We will use this to contact you if necessary.

### 6. Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number, including area code and extension if applicable. This information will help us contact you in case of emergencies.

### 7. Sworn Declaration
* **Sworn Declaration** (text, optional): Enter a brief statement declaring your understanding of the purpose and terms of this form. This is a required field for official purposes.

### 8. Declaration Date
* **Declaration Date** (date, optional): Enter the date you sign this declaration. This information will help us track changes and updates to your declaration.

Note: The form is divided into two pages. Each page has its own set of fields, so please ensure you complete each page before submitting the form.
