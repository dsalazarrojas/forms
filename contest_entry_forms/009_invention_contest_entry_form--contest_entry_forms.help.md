# Invention Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Invention Contest Entry Form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to participate in an invention contest. This form will help the contest organizers gather the necessary details to evaluate and review the entries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Personal Details section with your name, address, and other relevant personal information.
2. Enter your contact information in the Contact Info section, including your email address and phone number.
3. Describe your invention in the Invention Description section, including its name, features, and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, required/optional): This section collects your personal details, including your name, address, and other relevant information.
  * Please fill out this section with accurate and up-to-date information.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): This section collects your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
  * Enter your active and reachable contact information to ensure we can get in touch with you.
- **Invention Description** (`invention_description`, text, required/optional): This section is where you describe your invention in detail.
  * Explain your invention's name, features, and any other relevant details.
