# Guess The Value Challenge - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guess The Value Challenge form is designed to collect user guesses for a specific numeric value. Users are prompted to guess a number and provide a brief explanation for their choice, as well as their contact email and phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the "Guess A Number" page, enter your numeric guess for the challenge.
2. Provide a brief explanation for why you chose the number on the "Why Did You Choose That Number" page.
3. Enter your contact email address on the "Contact Email" page.
4. Enter your phone number on the "Contact Number" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guess A Number** (`numeric_guess`, Number, Required): Enter a numeric value that you think is correct for the challenge.
* **Why Did You Choose That Number** (`guess_reason`, Text, Optional): Briefly explain why you chose the numeric value you entered. This helps us understand your thought process.
* **Contact Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact information.

## Tips

* Use a valid numeric value for your guess, as incorrect or non-numeric entries will not be accepted.
* Keep your explanation brief and clear, as it helps us understand your thought process.
* If you're not comfortable sharing your contact information, you can leave the "Contact Number" field blank, but you must provide a valid email address. 

Note: This form is not intended to be completed multiple times by the same user. If you're unsure about the form's purpose or have any questions, please contact us for clarification.
