# Water Photography Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from photographers about their water photography submissions. This includes details about the photographer, their entry, and any uploaded files.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Entry Details" page with accurate and truthful information about your entry.
2. On the "Name" page, enter your full name.
3. On the "Email" page, enter your email address.
4. On the "Date of Birth" page, enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
5. On the "Address" page, enter your address.
6. On the "Contact Number" page, enter your contact number.
7. On the "File Upload" page, upload any relevant files related to your entry.
8. On the "Photo Gallery" page, enter the name of the photo gallery where your entry will be featured.
9. On the "Category" page, select the category that best describes your entry.
10. On the "Photo Description" page, enter a brief description of your entry.
11. On the "Tags" page, select any relevant tags for your entry.
12. On the "Upload Files" page, select any additional files to be uploaded.
13. On the "Photo Details" page, enter any additional details about your entry.
14. On the "Reviewers" page, select the reviewer who will be evaluating your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entry Details**: Enter a brief description of your entry.
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number.
* **File Upload**: Upload any relevant files related to your entry.
* **Photo Gallery**: Enter the name of the photo gallery where your entry will be featured.
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes your entry.
* **Photo Description**: Enter a brief description of your entry.
* **Tags**: Select any relevant tags for your entry.
* **Upload Files**: Select any additional files to be uploaded.
* **Photo Details**: Enter any additional details about your entry.
* **Reviewers**: Select the reviewer who will be evaluating your entry.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* Double-check your entries for any errors before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to reach out to the organizers for clarification.
* This form should only be filled out once for each submission and should not be submitted multiple times for the same entry.
