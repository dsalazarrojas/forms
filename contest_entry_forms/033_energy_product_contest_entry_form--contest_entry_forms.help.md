# Energy Product Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Product Contest Entry Form is a submission form for teams to enter the contest with their innovative energy-related projects, products, or ideas.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3. Provide a clear and concise response for each field, using the space provided.
4. Ensure the images, videos, and links are uploaded and linked correctly.
5. Double-check all fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_information`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for entering your team's basic information. Please fill in the required details, such as your team's name, contact email, and contact phone number.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your energy product, including its features, benefits, and how it addresses the energy sector.
* **Innovation Story** (`innovation_story`, `text`, required/optional): Tell the story behind your energy product, explaining its innovative aspects and how it contributes to the energy sector.
* **Energy Product Category** (`energy_product_category`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one category that best describes your energy product: Solar, Energy Storage, or Energy Efficiency.
* **Energy Product Type** (`energy_product_type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all applicable types that describe your energy product: Solar Panel, Energy Storage System, or Energy Efficiency System.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, `text`, required/optional): List the names and roles of all team members.
* **Image** (`image`, `text`, required/optional): Upload an image of your energy product.
* **Video** (`video`, `text`, required/optional): Upload a video showcasing your energy product.
* **Demo Link** (`demo_link`, `text`, required/optional): Share a link to a demo or a presentation of your energy product.
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the URL of your team's website, if applicable.
* **Innovation Story Details** (`innovation_story_details`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional details about your innovation story.
* **Product URL** (`product_url`, `text`, required/optional): Share the URL of your product, if available.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your team's name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the contact email of the team or individual representing the team.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact phone number of the team or individual representing the team.
* **Team Address** (`team_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the team's physical address.
* **Team Description** (`team_description`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your team and its members.
* **Team Member 1** (`team_member_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and role of the first team member.
* **Team Member 2** (`team_member_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and role of the second team member.
* **Team Member 3** (`team_member_3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and role of the third team member.
* **Team Member 4** (`team_member_4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and role of the fourth team member.
* **Team Member 5** (`team_member_5`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and role of the fifth team member.
* **Team Member 6** (`team_member_6`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and role of the sixth team member.
* **Team Member 7** (`team_member_7`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and role of the seventh team member.
* **Team Member 8** (`team_member_8`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and role of the eighth team member.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled accurately and completely.
* Double-check the form before submitting.
* Review the contest rules and guidelines for any additional requirements.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact the contest organizers.
