# Fall Pumpkin Carving Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fall Pumpkin Carving Contest Entry Form is a submission form for individuals interested in participating in the Fall Pumpkin Carving Contest. This form is used to collect the necessary information to display and evaluate entries.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, click on each field and type in the required information for each section.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your entry is submitted correctly.
3. Double-check that your email address is formatted correctly before submitting your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on our website and in the contest display.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached in case of contest updates or questions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter a phone number where you can be contacted in case of additional contest information.
* **Pumpkin Image Url** (`pumpkin_image_url`, text, required: false): Provide a URL to a publicly accessible image of your pumpkin carving. Please ensure it is a valid URL.
* **Entry Description** (`entry_description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your pumpkin carving. This can be a sentence or a short paragraph.
* **Entry Title** (`entry_title`, text, required: false): Enter a title for your pumpkin carving entry.

## Tips

* Make sure all fields are filled out before submitting your entry.
* Double-check that your email address and phone number are entered correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the contest organizers for assistance.
