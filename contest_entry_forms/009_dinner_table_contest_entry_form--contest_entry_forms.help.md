# Dinner Table Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dinner Table Contest Entry Form is a submission form for the dinner table contest. This form collects information about each entry, including contact information, culinary details, and entry type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: entry_details, contact_info, and entry_type.
2. Optionally, provide additional information about your entry, such as culinary_info, dietary_restrictions, and other_entry_type.
3. If you have a chef or team behind your entry, provide their information in the chef_info and team_info fields.
4. Upload an entry image or file to showcase your entry.
5. Upload a video to showcase your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Entry Details** (`entry_details`, text, required): This field is the main description of your entry. Please provide a brief description of your dish or creation.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): This field is where you provide your contact information, such as your name and email address.
* **Culinary Info** (`culinary_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide additional information about your dish or creation, such as ingredients or cooking method.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): This field is where you can specify any dietary restrictions or allergies related to your entry.
* **Entry Type** (`entry_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options: Appetizer, Main Course, Dessert, or Beverage. If your entry doesn't fit into one of these categories, you can specify "other" in the other_entry_type field.
* **Other Entry Type** (`other_entry_type`, text, optional): This field is for you to specify if your entry doesn't fit into the above categories.
* **Chef Info** (`chef_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide information about the chef or person behind your entry.
* **Team Info** (`team_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide information about the team behind your entry.
* **Entry Image** (`entry_image`, text, optional): Upload an image of your entry to showcase it.
* **Entry File** (`entry_file`, text, optional): Upload a file related to your entry, such as a recipe or ingredient list.
* **Entry Video** (`entry_video`, text, optional): Upload a video related to your entry, such as a cooking demonstration.
