# Book Club Trivia Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from teams participating in the book club trivia tournament. It is intended to help the tournament organizers understand team dynamics, strengths, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields, which include:
	* Team Name
	* Team Captain Full Name
	* Team Captain Email
	* Team Captain Phone
	* Team Size
	* Registration Fee Payment
	* I Have Read and Accept the Tournament Rules
	* Photo and Video Release
	* Entry Submission Date
2. If any of the fields are not applicable, please provide a corresponding explanation.
3. Review and submit the form once all required fields are filled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name**: Enter a creative and unique name for your team.
* **Team Captain Full Name**: Enter the full name of the team captain.
* **Team Captain Email**: Enter the email address of the team captain.
* **Team Captain Phone**: Enter the phone number of the team captain.
* **Team Size**: Select the number of members in your team (2-6 members).
* **Number of Team Members 2-5 Name**: Enter the full name of team members 2-5, if applicable.
* **Team Category**: Select the type of team (Individual Entry, Book Club Team, Library Staff Team, Community Team, Corporate Team, Student Team).
* **Tournament Date**: Select the preferred tournament date.
* **Team Trivia Experience Level**: Select your team's experience level (First Time, Some Experience, Moderate Experience, Very Experienced, Trivia Champions).
* **Favorite Book Genres**: Select up to 5 genres your team is familiar with.
* **Dietary Requirements**: If you have any dietary needs for provided refreshments, select the corresponding options.
* **Registration Fee Payment**: Select how you plan to pay the registration fee (Paid Online, Pay at Event, Fee Waiver Requested).
* **I Have Read and Accept the Tournament Rules**: Select "Yes I accept" to confirm you understand and will follow the rules.
* **Photo and Video Release**: Select "Yes granted" to give permission to use photos and videos for promotional purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the full name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Special Accommodations Needed**: If you have any special requests or accessibility needs, enter them here.
* **How Did You Hear About This Tournament**: Select how you found out about the event (Social Media, Library Website, Friend or Family, Email Newsletter, Flyer or Poster, Previous Participant).
* **Entry Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Team T-Shirt Sizes**: Enter the shirt sizes for each team member.
* **Dietary Requirements**: If you have any dietary needs for provided refreshments, select the corresponding options.
* **Registration Fee Payment**: Select how you plan to pay the registration fee (Paid Online, Pay at Event, Fee Waiver Requested).
