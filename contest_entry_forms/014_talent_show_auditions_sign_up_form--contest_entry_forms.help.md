# Talent Show Auditions Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who want to sign up for a talent show. It gathers essential details about the performer, their act, and contact information for follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields as per your preference.
2. Enter your details accurately to avoid any issues with processing your application.
3. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Performer Name** (`performer_name`, Text, Required: False)
* Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. **Parent Contact Name** (`contact_name`, Text, Required: False)
* Provide your parent or guardian's name if you are a minor. If not applicable, enter your own name.

### 3. **Parent Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, Required: False)
* Enter your parent or guardian's phone number for further communication.

### 4. **Performer Age** (`performer_age`, Number, Required: False)
* Enter your age as of the current date.

### 5. **Performer Address** (`performer_address`, Text, Required: False)
* Enter your home address.

### 6. **Performer City** (`performer_city`, Text, Required: False)
* Enter your city of residence.

### 7. **Performer State** (`performer_state`, Text, Required: False)
* Enter your state of residence.

### 8. **Performer Zip** (`performer_zip`, Number, Required: False)
* Enter your 5-digit zip code.

### 9. **Parent Email** (`contact_email`, Email, Required: False)
* Enter your parent or guardian's email address for further communication.

### 10. **Type of Act** (`act_type`, Select One, Required: False)
* Choose the category that best describes your talent, e.g., Singer, Dancer, Musician, etc.

### 11. **Additional Notes** (`contact_note`, Note, Required: False)
* Enter any further details or information about your performance or needs.

### 12. **Parent Contact Ext** (`contact_phone_ext`, Text, Required: False)
* Enter any additional information about your parent or guardian's phone number.

### 13. **Parent Contact Email Ext** (`contact_email_ext`, Email, Required: False)
* Enter any additional information about your parent or guardian's email address.

### 14. **Additional Act Notes** (`act_note`, Note, Required: False)
* Enter any additional details about your performance or requirements.

### 15. **Submit Form** (`form_submit`, Text, Required: False)
* Once you've filled out all required and optional fields, click submit to complete your registration.

### 16. **Contact Info** (`contact`, Select One, Required: False)
* Choose the category of contact information that best suits your needs.

### 17. **Performer Name 2** (`performer_name_2`, Select One, Required: False)
* Select your preferred performer's name from the available options.

### 18. **Contact Phone 2** (`contact_phone_2`, Text, Required: False)
* Enter your parent or guardian's phone number if different from the primary contact.

### 19. **Parent Contact Email 2** (`contact_email_2`, Email, Required: False)
* Enter your parent or guardian's email address if different from the primary contact.

### 20. **Act Type 2** (`act_type_2`, Select One, Required: False)
* Choose the category that best describes your talent, e.g., Singer, Dancer, Musician, etc.

### 21. **Contact Note 2** (`contact_note_2`, Note, Required: False)
* Enter any further details or information about your second performer's needs.

### 22. **Performer Address 2** (`performer_address_2`, Text, Required: False)
* Enter the second performer's home address.

### 23. **Parent Contact Phone Ext 2** (`contact_phone_ext_2`, Text, Required: False)
* Enter any additional information about your parent or guardian's phone number.

### 24. **Contact Email Ext 2** (`contact_email_ext_2`, Email, Required: False)
* Enter any additional information about your parent or guardian's email address.

### 25. **Act Note 2** (`act_note_2`, Note, Required: False)
* Enter any additional details about your second performance or requirements.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
