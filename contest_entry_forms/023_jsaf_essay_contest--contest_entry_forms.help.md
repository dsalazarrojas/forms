# jsaf_essay_contest - Help Guide
## Purpose
The jsaf_essay_contest form is a collection of fields designed to gather information about essay contest entries. This form may be used to collect demographic and essay-related information from contest participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields as accurately as possible. You will be asked to provide basic contact information and details about your essay entry. Please ensure that you fill in all fields correctly, as this will help ensure that your entry is processed correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Essay** (`entry_form`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your essay entry.
* **Title** (`essay_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter a title for your essay entry.
* **Author** (`author`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name or pseudonym.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you submitted your essay.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of submission.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the provided options ('Yes' or 'No').
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose one or more of the provided options ('Yes' or 'No').
* **Decimal** (`decimal`, `number`, required: false): Enter a numerical value (e.g., 1.5 for 1 and 1/2).
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes you'd like to make about your entry.
