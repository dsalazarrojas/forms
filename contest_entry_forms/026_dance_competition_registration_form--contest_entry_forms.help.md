# Dance Competition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for dancers to register for a dance competition. It will gather information about the dancer, their performance details, contact information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the correct options for the level of competition, category, style, and other relevant fields.
3. Make sure to enter the correct performance time and date.
4. Choose the correct payment method.
5. Sign the form with the dancer's signature.
6. Provide the necessary emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dancer Information**: This section is for you to enter your information as a dancer. Make sure to enter your correct name.
* **Performance Information**: This section is for you to enter your performance details, including performance time and date.
* **Competition Details**: This section is for you to select the level of competition you are registering for.
* **Contact Information**: This section is for you to enter your contact information, including your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact**: This section is for you to enter the contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Dance Routine Information**: This section is for you to enter your dance routine details.
* **Level of Competition**: This section is for you to select the level of competition you are registering for.
* **Competition Format**: This section is for you to select the competition format you are registering for.
* **Category**: This section is for you to select the category you are registering for.
* **Style**: This section is for you to select the style of dance you are performing.
* **Age Group**: This section is for you to select the age group you are registering for.
* **Gender**: This section is for you to select your gender.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: This section is for you to select your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Competition Level 2**: This section is for you to select the level of competition you are registering for.
* **Performance Time**: This section is for you to enter the performance time.
* **Performance Date**: This section is for you to enter the performance date.
* **Fee**: This section is for you to select the fee you are paying for the competition.
* **Payment Method**: This section is for you to select the payment method you are using.
* **Registration Number**: This section is for you to enter the registration number.
* **Dancer Signature**: This section is for you to sign the form with your signature.
* **Name**: This section is for you to enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Phone Number**: This section is for you to enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 2**: This section is for you to select the relationship with the emergency contact person 2.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number 2**: This section is for you to enter the phone number of the emergency contact 2.
* **Note**: This section is for you to enter any additional notes about the emergency contact.
