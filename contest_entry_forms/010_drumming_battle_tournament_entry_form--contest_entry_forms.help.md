# Drumming Battle Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for solo and group entries for drumming battles or jam sessions. It collects information about the event type, entry type, and instrument, as well as contact and social media details for promotional purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the event type: Choose between "Drumming Battle", "Jam Session", or "Battle Royale".
2. Select the entry type: Choose between "Solo" or "Group".
3. Select your instrument(s): Choose one or more instruments you will be playing, or type "Other" if your instrument is not listed.
4. Enter your contact details: Provide your name, email, and phone number.
5. Add optional media: You can attach an image or video to your entry.
6. Review and agree to the rules: Ensure you have read and understood the rules by selecting "Yes" for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Type** (Required, Select One):
	+ Choose the type of event you are entering: "Drumming Battle", "Jam Session", or "Battle Royale".
* **Entry Type** (Required, Select One):
	+ Choose between "Solo" or "Group" to indicate whether you are entering alone or with others.
* **Instrument(s)** (Required, Select Multiple):
	+ Select one or more instruments you will be playing, or type "Other" if your instrument is not listed.
* **Group Name** (Optional, Text):
	+ If you are entering as a group, provide your group name.
* **Group Size** (Optional, Number):
	+ If you are entering as a group, provide the number of people in your group.
* **Team Name** (Optional, Text):
	+ If you are entering as a group, provide your team name.
* **Contact Name** (Optional, Text):
	+ Provide the name of the person to contact for more information.
* **Contact Email** (Optional, Email):
	+ Provide the email address of the person to contact for more information.
* **Contact Phone** (Optional, Text):
	+ Provide the phone number of the person to contact for more information.
* **Social Media** (Optional, Text):
	+ Provide a link to your social media profile.
* **Website** (Optional, Text):
	+ Provide a link to your website.
* **Image** (Optional, Text):
	+ Attach an image for your entry.
* **Video** (Optional, Text):
	+ Attach a video for your entry.
* **Video Url** (Optional, Text):
	+ Provide a URL to your video.
* **Video Embed** (Optional, Text):
	+ Embed a video for your entry.
* **Rules Acknowledged** (Optional, Select Multiple):
	+ Select "Yes" for each field to indicate you have read and understood the rules.
* **Rules Agreed** (Optional, Select Multiple):
	+ Select "Yes" for each field to indicate you have agreed to the rules.
