# Needlecraft Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Needlecraft Tournament Entry Form is designed to collect information from participants and entries for the tournament. This form helps us gather essential details about the participants and their entries, which will be used to evaluate and manage the tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the required fields: `tournament_name`, `participant_name`, `photo_url`, `entry_type`, `participant_age`, `participant_gender`, `participant_email`, `entry_date`, `entry_time`, `entry_location`, and `participant_agreement`.
* Fill out the optional fields: `entry_description`, `participant_phone`, `entry_media`, `entry_media_type`, `participant_notes`, `entry_notes`, `entry_agreement`, `entry_terms`, `participant_terms`, `participant_signature`, and `entry_signature`.
* Ensure that you fill out all required fields to submit the form correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the tournament name** (`tournament_name`, text, required): This field is where you enter the name of the tournament. Please fill out the name of the tournament you are participating in.
* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): This field is where you enter your name as the participant. Please fill out your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Photo URL** (`photo_url`, text, required): This field is where you enter a URL of a photo of yourself. Please ensure the photo is in a valid format.
* **Entry type** (`entry_type`, select_one, required): This field is where you select the type of entry you are submitting. Please choose one of the options provided.
* **Participant age** (`participant_age`, number, required): This field is where you enter your age. Please enter your age as a number.
* **Participant gender** (`participant_gender`, select_one, required): This field is where you select your gender. Please choose one of the options provided.
* **Participant email** (`participant_email`, email, required): This field is where you enter your email address. Please ensure it is a valid email format.
* **Entry date** (`entry_date`, date, required): This field is where you select the date of the entry. Please choose a valid date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Entry time** (`entry_time`, time, required): This field is where you select the time of the entry. Please choose a valid time in the format HH:MM.
* **Entry location** (`entry_location`, text, required): This field is where you enter the location of the entry. Please fill out the location where you are submitting from.
* **Participant phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your phone number. If you have one, please fill it out for contact purposes.
* **Entry media** (`entry_media`, text, optional): This field is where you enter any media you wish to share with us. If you have some, please attach it.
* **Entry media type** (`entry_media_type`, select_one, optional): This field is where you select the type of media you are sharing. If you are sharing media, please choose one of the options provided.
* **Participant email 2** (`participant_email_2`, email, optional): This field is where you enter your email address. If you have an email, please fill it out for contact purposes.
* **Entry status** (`entry_status`, select_one, optional): This field is where you select the status of your entry. Please choose one of the options provided.
* **Participant notes** (`participant_notes`, text, optional): This field is where you enter any notes about yourself. If you have some, please share them with us.
* **Entry notes** (`entry_notes`, text, optional): This field is where you enter any notes about your entry. If you have some, please share them with us.
* **Participant agreement** (`participant_agreement`, select_one, required): This field is where you select your agreement. Please choose one of the options provided.
* **Entry agreement** (`entry_agreement`, select_one, optional): This field is where you select your agreement. If you have an agreement, please choose one of the options provided.
* **Entry terms** (`entry_terms`, select_one, optional): This field is where you select your terms. If you have terms, please choose one of the options provided.
* **Participant terms** (`participant_terms`, select_one, optional): This field is where you select your terms. If you have terms, please choose one of the options provided.
* **Participant signature** (`participant_signature`, text, optional): This field is where you enter a signature. If you have a physical signature, please attach a scanned image.
* **Entry signature** (`entry_signature`, text, optional): This field is where you enter a signature. If you have a physical signature, please attach a scanned image.
* **Submission date** (`submission_date`, date, required): This field is where you select the date of submission. Please choose a valid date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

# Tips
* Ensure that you fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
