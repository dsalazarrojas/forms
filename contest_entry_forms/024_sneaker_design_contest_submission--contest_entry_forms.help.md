# Sneaker Design Contest Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit sneaker design entries for contest purposes. To complete the form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct options from the "Contest Form" field. This field allows you to choose one or multiple options, depending on your preference.
2. Fill in your contestant details in the "Contestant Details" field.
3. Write a description of your design in the "Design Description" field. Please be as detailed as possible to help the contest organizers understand your design.
4. Upload the corresponding artwork files in the "Artwork Files" field. Please ensure that the files are correctly named and organized.
5. In the "Submission Type" field, select the correct submission type.
6. Enter your submission date and time in the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" fields, respectively.
7. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Leave any additional notes in the "Note" field.
10. Confirm your email address in the "Email Confirmation" field, if required.
11. Confirm your phone number in the "Phone Confirmation" field, if required.
12. Enter your email and phone number in the "Email Phone" field, if required.
13. Select the correct submitter from the "Submitter" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contest Form**: A field that asks you to select one or multiple options for the contest entry. Please choose the correct option that suits your needs.
* **Contestant Details**: A field where you will be entering your personal details such as name and other information.
* **Design Description**: A field where you will be entering a detailed description of your design.
* **Artwork Files**: A field where you will be uploading the corresponding artwork files for your design.
* **File Description**: A field where you will be describing the uploaded files.
* **Design Description 2**: Another field where you will be entering a description of your design, if necessary.
* **Submission Type**: A field that asks you to select the type of submission, such as "Online Submission" or "Offline Submission".
* **Submission Date**: A field where you will be entering the date of your submission.
* **Submission Time**: A field where you will be entering the time of your submission.
* **Email**: A field where you will be entering your email address.
* **Phone**: A field where you will be entering your phone number.
* **Note**: A field where you can leave any additional notes about your submission.
* **Email Confirmation**: A field where you will be confirming your email address, if required.
* **Phone Confirmation**: A field where you will be confirming your phone number, if required.
* **Email Phone**: A field where you will be entering your email and phone number, if required.
* **Submitter**: A field that asks you to select who submitted the entry, such as "Designer" or "Artist".
