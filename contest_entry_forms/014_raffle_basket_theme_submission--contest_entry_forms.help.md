# raffle_basket_theme_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The raffle basket theme submission form is designed to collect theme ideas for raffle baskets. The form will help you propose a theme that fits your style and interests. By filling out this form, you will be able to submit a unique theme proposal with a title, description, estimated value, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the title of your proposed raffle basket theme.
2. Provide a detailed description of your theme proposal.
3. Estimate the value of your theme proposal.
4. Choose the raffle items that will be included in your theme.
5. Select the themes that will be used for your raffle basket.
6. Optionally, provide the image of your raffle basket theme.
7. Choose the theme color that best represents your proposal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief and creative title for your raffle basket theme proposal.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required/true): Provide a detailed description of your theme proposal. This will help the judges evaluate your idea.
* **Estimated Value** (`estimated_value`, number, required/true): Estimate the value of your theme proposal. This will help the judges understand the complexity and value of your idea.
* **Raffle Items** (`raffle_items`, select_multiple, required/true): Choose the items that will be included in your theme proposal. Select all relevant items that fit your theme.
* **Raffle Basket Image** (`raffle_basket_image`, text, required/false): If you have an image for your theme proposal, you can upload it here. This is optional.
* **Raffle Basket Themes** (`raffle_basket_themes`, select_multiple, required/false): Select the themes that will be used for your raffle basket proposal. This will help the judges understand the context of your theme.
* **Theme Description** (`theme_description`, text, required/false): Provide a brief description of the selected theme. This will help the judges understand the theme better.
* **Theme Color** (`theme_color`, select_one, required/false): Choose the color that best represents your theme proposal. This will help the judges visualize your idea.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide a unique title and description for your theme proposal.
* Estimate the value of your theme proposal accurately.
* Choose relevant raffle items and themes for your proposal.
* Ensure that the theme description is brief and clear.
* Select a theme color that best represents your idea.
* Submit only one unique theme proposal per form submission.
