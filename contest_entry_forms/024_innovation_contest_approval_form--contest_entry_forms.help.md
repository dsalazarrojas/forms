The form "Innovation Contest Approval Form" is designed to collect input for the Innovation Contest, which allows users to submit and manage their innovations. This form serves as a tool for the innovation management team to approve or reject submitted innovations based on their quality and relevance. It's essential to fill out the form correctly to ensure that your innovation is properly assessed. Please note that this form is not a substitute for the actual contest submission process, and you must have a valid account and access rights to access it.

# innovation_contest_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Innovation Contest Approval Form is a tool for managing innovations submitted to the contest. This form helps evaluate and approve or reject submitted innovations based on their quality and relevance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each field carefully, making sure to fill out the required information accurately.
2.  Select the correct options for each field according to your innovation's details.
3.  Fill out any additional comments or information in the Comments field if needed.
4.  Once you have filled out all the required information, review your submission carefully before submitting it for approval.
5.  Click the "Submit" button to send your submission for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Submission Form (id: 1)** (`text`, required: false): 
    This field is for providing a brief overview or summary of your innovation. 
*   **Prophets (id: 2)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): 
    Select any relevant prophets or individuals that have contributed to the innovation. 
*   **Date and Time (id: 3)** (`date`, required: false): 
    Provide the date and time when the innovation was submitted or when it was expected to be completed. 
*   **Innovation Description (id: 4)** (`text`, required: false): 
    This is the main description of your innovation, describing its concept, features, and goals. 
*   **Team Members (id: 5)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): 
    List any team members who contributed to the innovation, including their roles and responsibilities. 
*   **Submission Details (id: 6)** (`text`, required: false): 
    Provide any additional details about the submission, such as files or links to relevant resources. 
*   **Innovation Status (id: 7)** (`select_one`, required: false): 
    Select the current status of your innovation, such as "Draft," "In Progress," or "Completed." 
*   **Comments (id: 8)** (`text`, required: false): 
    Use this field to add any comments or feedback you'd like to provide about your innovation. 
*   **Approval Details (id: 9)** (`text`, required: false): 
    This field is for providing additional information about the approval or rejection process. 
*   **Innovation Contest Approve/Reject (id: 10/11)** (`number`, required: false): 
    Enter a numerical value to indicate whether to approve or reject the innovation. 
    -  Approve: enter 1
    -  Reject: enter 2 or 3
    Note: This field is only for the approval team members.
