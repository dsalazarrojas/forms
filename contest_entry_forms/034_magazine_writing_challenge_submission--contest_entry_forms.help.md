# Magazine Writing Challenge Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from authors submitting their manuscripts to the magazine writing challenge. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out your submission form with all required fields.
2. Provide a valid email address to ensure that your submission can be verified and communicated with.
3. Enter your manuscript details, including title, type, and length.
4. Enter your author bio and relevant experience.
5. Attach your manuscript file for review.
6. Set your submission date and determine if this entry requires payment.
7. Optionally, enter a description for your manuscript file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission Form ID** (`form_id`, `number`, required): This is a unique identifier for your submission. 
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Manuscript** (`manuscript`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your manuscript.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Author Bio** (`author_bio`, `text`, required): Enter a brief bio about yourself as an author.
* **Manuscript Type** (`manuscript_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of your manuscript from the options: Novel, Short Story, Essay, Poem, or Other.
* **Manuscript Length** (`manuscript_length`, `select_one`, required): Select the length of your manuscript from the options: Less than 1000 words, 1000-2000 words, 2001-5000 words, 5001-10000 words, or Greater than 10000 words.
* **Author Experience** (`author_experience`, `select_multiple`, required): Check all relevant experience you have as an author. Select from: Published Author, Published Illustrator/Artist, Self-Published Author, Published Poet, Published Playwright/Screenwriter.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are submitting your manuscript.
* **Manuscript File** (`manuscript_file`, `text`, required): Attach your manuscript file for review.
* **Manuscript File Description** (`manuscript_file_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of your attached manuscript file, if desired.
* **Entry Fees** (`entry_fees`, `number`, optional): Enter any entry fees you are willing to pay for submission. If not applicable, leave blank.
* **Paid Entry** (`is_paid`, `select_one`, optional): Check if this entry requires payment.
