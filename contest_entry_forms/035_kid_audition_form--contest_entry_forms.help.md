# Kid Audition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kid Audition Form is used to collect information from parents/guardians of children participating in a contest or competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the parent/guardian's information on Page 1: Contestant Info.
	* This page requires the parent/guardian's basic information, such as name, address, and contact details.
2. Provide the child's contact information on Page 2: Contact Information.
	* This page should include the child's name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
3. Answer the questions about the child's performance, skills, and preferences on Page 3: Performance Information.
	* This page asks about the child's experience and abilities in the relevant field.
4. Upload required documents and files on Page 4: Upload Documents, Upload Videos, Upload Audio, and Upload Additional Files.
	* This page allows you to upload any supporting documents or files that are required for the child's participation.
5. Schedule the auditions on Page 5: Schedule Auditions.
	* This page allows you to choose a date and time for the child's audition.
6. Confirm your submission on Page 6: Preferences.
	* This page is used to confirm that you have reviewed and submitted the form accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent info** (Page 1):
	* This field is used to collect the parent/guardian's basic information.
- **Contact Information** (Page 2):
	* This field is used to collect the child's contact details.
- **Performance information** (Page 3):
	* This field is used to answer questions about the child's performance and experience in the relevant field.
- **Contestant Skills** (Page 3):
	* This field is used to answer questions about the child's skills and abilities.
- **Upload documents** (Page 4):
	* This field is used to upload any required documents that are needed for the child's participation.
- **Upload videos** (Page 4):
	* This field is used to upload any videos that are needed for the child's participation.
- **Upload audio** (Page 4):
	* This field is used to upload any audio files that are needed for the child's participation.
- **Upload additional files** (Page 4):
	* This field is used to upload any additional files that are needed for the child's participation.
- **Schedule auditions** (Page 5):
	* This field is used to choose a date and time for the child's audition.
- **Preferences** (Page 6):
	* This field is used to confirm that you have reviewed and submitted the form accurately and completely.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Review the form before submitting it to ensure that all necessary information is provided.
- Upload any required documents and files to support the child's participation.
- Schedule the auditions at a date and time that is convenient for both the child and the organizers.
- Confirm your submission to ensure that the form is accurate and complete.
