# Beer Tasting Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beer Tasting Tournament Entry Form is designed to collect necessary information from participants who want to enter a beer tasting tournament. This form helps the organizers ensure that participants meet the age requirement and understand the event's rules and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number in the respective fields.
2. Confirm that you are 21 years or older by selecting the correct option in the Age Verification field.
3. Enter your Date of Birth to verify your age.
4. Select the category you're entering (Amateur Taster, Homebrewer, etc.).
5. Indicate your level of beer knowledge (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, etc.).
6. Choose the rounds you want to participate in.
7. Select your preferred beer styles.
8. List any dietary restrictions or allergies you may have.
9. Choose how you'll get to the event (Driving, Rideshare, etc.).
10. Select your preferred event date.
11. If you have an emergency contact, enter their name and phone number.
12. Confirm that you're willing to participate in the event and acknowledge the risks associated with alcohol consumption.
13. Indicate whether you've paid the entry fee or not.
14. If you've heard about the event through social media, a friend or family member, or any other means, select the correct option.
15. Finally, add any additional comments or information you'd like to share with the organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address**: Provide your primary email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the number you can be reached at in case of emergencies.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth to verify your age.
* **Age Verification**: Confirm that you are 21 years or older.
* **Tournament Category**: Select the category you're entering (Amateur Taster, Homebrewer, etc.).
* **Beer Experience Level**: Indicate your level of beer knowledge (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, etc.).
* **Tasting Rounds**: Choose the rounds you want to participate in.
* **Beer Style Preferences**: Select your preferred beer styles (IPA, Pale Ale, Stout, etc.).
* **Dietary Restrictions**: List any allergies or restrictions you have.
* **Transportation Method**: Choose how you'll get to the event (Driving, Rideshare, etc.).
* **Event Date Preference**: Select your preferred event date.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Liability Waiver**: Confirm that you're willing to participate and understand the event's rules.
* **Alcohol Consumption Acknowledgment**: Acknowledge that you understand the risks associated with alcohol consumption.
* **Photography Consent**: Confirm that you're willing to be photographed.
* **Entry Fee Paid**: Indicate whether you've paid the entry fee or not.
* **How Did You Hear About This Event**: Select how you heard about the event (Social Media, Friend, etc.).
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional information you'd like to share with the organizers.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure your participation in the event.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the organizers.
* The event organizers may contact you with further instructions or clarification on any of the provided information.
* Please note that the event has rules and regulations regarding age, liability, and photography.
