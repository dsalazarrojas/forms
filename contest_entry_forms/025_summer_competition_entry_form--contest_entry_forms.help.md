# Summer Competition Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users who want to participate in the Summer Competition. To be eligible, users must select at least one event to participate in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select at least one event to participate in by clicking on the "Event Choice" fields.
2. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
3. Provide your performance name in the "Performance Name" field.
4. Select your age group (if applicable).
5. Provide your consent to participate by selecting "Consent" from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Fill in your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Select your participation status for each event by clicking on the respective "Event" fields.
9. Review the terms and conditions, and if you agree, click on the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Choice**: Select at least one event to participate in by choosing from the available options "Yes" and "No". This field is multiple-choice, meaning you can select more than one option.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it should be displayed in the competition.
* **Performance Name**: Enter your performance name (if you have one).
* **Age**: Enter your age group (if applicable). Please note that this field is not required.
* **Consent**: Select "Consent" to indicate that you agree to participate in the competition.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address. This field requires a valid email format.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Event 1**: Select your participation status for the first event by choosing from the options "Yes" and "No".
* **Event 2**: Select your participation status for the second event by choosing from the options "Yes" and "No".
* **Event 3**: Select your participation status for the third event by choosing from the options "Yes" and "No".
* **Event 4**: Select your participation status for the fourth event by choosing from the options "Yes" and "No".
* **Terms and Conditions**: Review the terms and conditions of the competition before submitting your entry. If you agree, click on the "Submit" button.
* **Submit**: Click on this button to submit your entry once you have filled in all the required fields and agreed to the terms and conditions.
