# win_a_dinner_for_two_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "win_a_dinner_for_two_entry_form" is a contest entry form designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in a dinner-related competition. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form's sections and understand what kind of information is being collected.
2. Select the correct entry option (True, False, or Maybe) for each question.
3. Provide your name in the format required for a standard contact form.
4. Enter a valid email address that you or your party has access to.
5. Enter your phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Contest** (`entry_form`, select_multiple, required: false): Select how you'd like to enter the contest. Please note that this is a required field but can be left empty only if you have already entered the contest before or are sure that it's not a contest.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name or the name of your party in the required format for a contact form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address that you or your party has access to.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if applicable.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct entry option for the contest.
* Double-check your name, email, and phone number for correct formatting and accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your local support team.
* This form collects information for the purpose of entering a contest; do not submit this form if you're not interested in participating in the contest.
