# halloween decorating contest form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and register entries for the Halloween decorating contest. Fill it out carefully to provide your entry details.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, start by entering your entry details in the first field, including your name, email address, and phone number. This information will help us keep you updated with contest updates and rules.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entry Details**: This field is the first step in registering your Halloween decoration entry. Please provide your first name, last name, email address, and phone number.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as you want it to be displayed in the contest.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as you want it to be displayed in the contest.
* **Email**: Enter the email address where you want to receive contest updates and rules.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you if needed.
* **House Number**: Enter the number of the house where your Halloween decoration is located. This information is only needed if you're decorating a private residence.
* **Address Street**: Enter the street address of your Halloween decoration.
* **Address City**: Enter the city where your Halloween decoration is located.
* **Address State**: Enter the state where your Halloween decoration is located.
* **Address Zip**: Enter the zip code of your Halloween decoration location.
* **Entry Type**: Choose the type of entry your decoration is for example, front door, back door, window, or yard.
* **Entry Description**: Provide a brief description of your decoration. This can be as simple as "a spooky graveyard" or "a giant pumpkin" and will be displayed on our contest page.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form carefully as all the fields are not required.
* If you're unsure about a field, please ask our contest team for clarification.
