# popcorn_prize_collection_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about a prize for a contest. It captures details about the prize, including category, value, type, shipping details, and delivery information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the category of the prize from the dropdown list.
2. Enter your name and email address to be contacted about the prize.
3. Provide details about the prize, including its value and type.
4. Choose the delivery method for the prize.
5. Select the date and time for delivery of the prize.
6. Enter the address where the prize will be delivered.
7. Indicate the status of the prize (e.g., unclaimed, claimed, lost).
8. Confirm if you are a winner of the prize.
9. Select the collection date and time for the prize.
10. Finally, enter the name of the person who collected the prize.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (Select One, required):
    * This field allows you to select the category of the prize.
* **Name** (Text, required):
    * Enter your name to be associated with the prize.
* **Email** (Email, required):
    * Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Prize Details** (Text, required):
    * Provide a brief description of the prize.
* **Prize Value** (Number, required):
    * Enter the monetary value of the prize.
* **Prize Type** (Select One, required):
    * Select the type of prize (cash, gift card, other).
* **Shipping Details** (Text, optional):
    * Provide any additional shipping details, such as special instructions.
* **Shipping Cost** (Number, optional):
    * Enter the shipping cost of the prize.
* **Delivery Method** (Select One, required):
    * Choose how the prize will be delivered (pickup, delivery, other).
* **Delivery Date** (Date, required):
    * Enter the date the prize will be delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (Time, required):
    * Enter the time the prize will be delivered.
* **Delivery Address** (Text, required):
    * Enter the address where the prize will be delivered.
* **Prize Status** (Select One, required):
    * Indicate the status of the prize (unclaimed, claimed, lost).
* **Winner Confirmation** (Select One, required):
    * Confirm if you are a winner of the prize.
* **Comments** (Text, optional):
    * Provide any additional comments about the prize.
* **Notes** (Text, optional):
    * Provide any additional notes about the prize.
* **Winner Confirmation** (Select One, required):
    * Confirm if you are a winner of the prize.
* **Collection Date** (Date, required):
    * Enter the date the prize was collected.
* **Collection Time** (Time, required):
    * Enter the time the prize was collected.
* **Collected By** (Text, required):
    * Enter the name of the person who collected the prize.
