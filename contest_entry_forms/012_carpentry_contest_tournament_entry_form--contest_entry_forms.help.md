# Carpentry Contest Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Carpentry Contest Tournament Entry Form" is a form designed to collect information from contestants for a carpentry competition. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your entry is properly registered and considered for the competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the competition rules and guidelines carefully to ensure you understand the requirements.
2.  Fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
3.  Enter your contest information, including title, description, dimensions, and weight.
4.  Select the types of wood used and techniques applied in your project.
5.  Indicate your experience level, professional status, and entry category.
6.  Agree to the judging, display, and media coverage conditions.
7.  Confirm your entry fee payment status and delivery method.
8.  Provide emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Carpentry Contest Tournament Entry**: This field is a note field to describe the form purpose and is not editable.
*  **Entry Number**: This field is not editable and is assigned by the competition organizers.
*  **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submit your entry for the competition (MM/DD/YYYY format).
*  **Contestant Information**: This field is a note field to describe the form purpose and is not editable.
*  **Full Name**: Enter your full legal name.
*  **Email Address**: Enter your email address for contest communications.
*  **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number.
*  **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format for age category purposes.
*  **Street Address**: Enter your complete street address.
*  **City**: Enter the name of the city you reside in.
*  **State or Province**: Enter the state or province you reside in.
*  **ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter your postal code.
*  **Carpentry Experience Level**: Select your experience level in carpentry from the provided options:
    + Beginner - less than 1 year
    + Intermediate - 1 to 3 years
    + Advanced - 3 to 10 years
    + Professional - over 10 years
    + Master craftsman
*  **Professional Status**: Select your current professional status:
    + Hobbyist
    + Student
    + Apprentice
    + Journeyman
    + Master carpenter
    + Contractor
    + Other
*  **Contest Category**: Select the category for your entry from the provided options:
    + Furniture - tables or desks
    + Furniture - chairs or seating
    + Furniture - cabinets or storage
    + Decorative woodwork
    + Functional items
    + Artistic sculpture
    + Restoration project
    + Open category
*  **Project Information**: This field is a note field to describe your project and is not editable.
*  **Project Title**: Enter the name of your project.
*  **Project Description**: Enter a detailed description of your project.
*  **Types of Wood Used**: Select all types of wood used for your project from the provided options:
    + Oak
    + Maple
    + Cherry
    + Walnut
    + Pine
    + Mahogany
    + Teak
    + Ash
    + Birch
    + Exotic woods
    + Reclaimed wood
    + Other
*  **Primary Techniques Used**: Select the primary techniques used for your project from the provided options:
    + Hand joinery
    + Machine joinery
    + Carving
    + Turning
    + Veneering
    + Inlay work
    + Steam bending
    + Lamination
    + Other
*  **Time to Complete Project**: Select the total hours invested in your project:
    + Less than 10 hours
    + 10 to 25 hours
    + 25 to 50 hours
    + 50 to 100 hours
    + Over 100 hours
*  **Project Dimensions**: Enter the project dimensions in inches (Height x Width x Depth).
*  **Approximate Weight**: Enter the approximate weight of your project in pounds.
*  **Special Display Requirements**: Select any special requirements for display from the provided options:
    + None
    + Electrical power
    + Special lighting
    + Elevated platform
    + Climate control
    + Security case
    + Other
*  **Have You Entered Carpentry Competitions Before**: Select your competition history:
    + Yes - won awards
    + Yes - no awards
    + No - first competition
*  **Photos of Project**: Select your photo submission status:
    + Yes - attached
    + Yes - will provide
    + False
*  **Video of Project or Process**: Select your video submission status:
    + Yes - attached
    + Yes - will provide
    + False
*  **I Consent to Judging Evaluation**: Agree to the judging condition:
    + Yes - I agree
    + No - I do not agree
*  **I Consent to Public Display**: Agree to public display:
    + Yes - for full event duration
    + Yes - for judging only
    + No - private entry
*  **I Consent to Media Coverage**: Agree to media coverage:
    + Yes - full consent
    + Yes - with attribution
    + No - no media
*  **Entry Fee Payment Status**: Confirm your entry fee payment status:
    + Paid - receipt attached
    + Will pay at drop-off
    + Fee waiver requested
    + Sponsor covered
*  **How Will You Deliver Your Entry**: Select your delivery method:
    + Self delivery
    + Courier service
    + Need assistance
*  **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter your emergency contact name.
*  **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter your emergency contact phone number.
*  **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about your entry.
