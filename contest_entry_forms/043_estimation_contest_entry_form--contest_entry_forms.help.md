**Estimation Contest Entry Form - Help Guide**

### Purpose
The Estimation Contest Entry Form is intended for users to submit their estimated values for a contest. The form helps gather participant information for the contest.

### How To Complete This Form
To submit an entry, follow these steps:

1. **Complete all required fields** (if any) before submitting. This form is not currently requiring any fields.
2. Fill in the requested information for:
	* **Enter your name**: Type your full name in this field.
	* **Enter email address**: Type your valid email address.
	* **Enter your contact number**: Type your phone number.
	* **Leave a message**: Type any additional message you want to share with the contest organizers.
3. **Click the "Submit" button** to send your entry.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter your name**: A text field to input your name.
* **Enter email address**: A field to input your email address. Please use a valid email format, e.g., `username@example.com`.
* **Enter your contact number**: A text field to input your phone number.
* **Estimated value**: Not currently used in this form, but is a number field for future use.
* **Estimate date**: Not currently used in this form, but is a date field for future use.
* **Leave a message**: A text field for any additional comments or messages you want to leave with your entry.
* **Submit**: The button to send your entry.

### Tips
* Please fill in your information accurately and honestly.
* Make sure to enter a valid email address and phone number.
* If you have any issues or questions about the form, contact the contest organizers.
