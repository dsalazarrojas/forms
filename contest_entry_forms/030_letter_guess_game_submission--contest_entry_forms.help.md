# Letter Guess Game Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants in the Letter Guess Game contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Participant Details
	* Contest Entry
	* Confirm Entry
	* Confirm Participant
	* Contact Info
	* Contact Method
	* Submission ID
	* Submission URL
	* Category
	* Tag
	* Created At
	* Updated At
	* Deleted At
	* Submitted At
	* Confirmed At
	* Confirmed By
2. For the optional fields:
	* Feedback
	* Message
	* Tags
	* Confirmed Message
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (participant_details, text, required):
	+ Provide your name and any other identifying information.
* **Contest Entry** (entry_details, text, required):
	+ Describe your submission in a few sentences.
* **Feedback** (feedback, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional comments or feedback about the submission.
* **Confirm Entry** (confirm_entry, select_one, required):
	+ Confirm that your submission is complete and accurate.
* **Confirm Participant** (confirm_participant, select_one, required):
	+ Confirm that you are the participant who submitted this entry.
* **Contact Info** (contact_info, text, required):
	+ Enter your name and contact information, such as phone number or email.
* **Contact Method** (contact_method, select_one, required):
	+ Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone or email).
* **Submission ID** (submission_id, text, optional):
	+ If provided, enter the submission ID from the contest platform.
* **Submission URL** (submission_url, text, optional):
	+ If provided, enter the URL where your submission can be found.
* **Category** (category, text, optional):
	+ Enter the category of your submission.
* **Tag** (tag, text, optional):
	+ Enter a tag for your submission.
* **Tags** (tags, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional tags for your submission.
* **Created At** (created_at, date, required):
	+ This is the date and time your entry was created.
* **Updated At** (updated_at, date, required):
	+ This is the date and time your entry was last updated.
* **Deleted At** (deleted_at, date, required):
	+ This is the date and time your entry was deleted.
* **Submitted At** (submitted_at, date, required):
	+ This is the date and time your entry was submitted.
* **Confirmed At** (confirmed_at, date, required):
	+ This is the date and time your entry was confirmed.
* **Confirmed By** (confirmed_by, text, optional):
	+ Enter the username of the user who confirmed your entry.
* **Confirmed By Hint** (confirmed_by_hint, text, required):
	+ Enter the confirmed-by user's username.
* **Updated By** (updated_by, text, optional):
	+ Enter the username of the user who updated your entry.
* **Updated By Hint** (updated_by_hint, text, required):
	+ Enter the updated-by user's username.

## Tips
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled in.
* Double-check that your submission meets all the necessary criteria.
* If you need help or have questions, contact us at form@gic.mx.
