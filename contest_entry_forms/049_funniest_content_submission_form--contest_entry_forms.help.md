# Funniest Content Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Funniest Content Submission Form" is a tool for users to submit their funniest content, such as jokes, stories, or media, for potential publication on a public platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your content.
2. Select the type of content you are submitting (Joke, Story, or Media).
3. Rate your submission using the provided rating scales.
4. Provide additional comments or reasons for your submission.
5. Agree that the content is suitable for public viewing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Story** (required)
This is the field where you will enter your story. Please type in your story and submit it.

### 2. **Joke** (optional)
This is the field where you can enter your joke. If you have a joke, please type it in. If not, you can leave this field blank.

### 3. **Media** (optional)
If you have a funny media, such as a video or image, please select "Yes" to indicate that it's a media submission. You can then provide additional details about the media in the "Media" field.

### 4. **Submission Form** (required)
This is the field where you will enter the title of your submission. Please enter a short title that describes your joke, story, or media.

### 5. **Email** (optional)
This is the field where you can enter your email address. Please enter your email address if you have one.

### 6. **Phone** (optional)
This is the field where you can enter your phone number. Please enter your phone number if you have one.

### 7. **Name** (required)
This is the field where you will enter your name. Please enter your name to be credited as the submitter.

### 8. **User Location** (optional)
This is the field where you can enter your location. Please enter your location if you want to be associated with your submission.

### 9. **Joke Rating** (required)
This is the scale where you will rate how funny your joke is. Please rate your joke on a scale of 1-10.

### 10. **Story Rating** (required)
This is the scale where you will rate how fun your story is. Please rate your story on a scale of 1-10.

### 11. **Media Rating** (required)
This is the scale where you will rate how fun your media is. Please rate your media on a scale of 1-10.

### 12. **Joke Rating Comment** (optional)
This is the field where you can provide additional comments about what makes your joke funny.

### 13. **Story Rating Comment** (optional)
This is the field where you can provide additional comments about what makes your story fun.

### 14. **Media Rating Comment** (optional)
This is the field where you can provide additional comments about what makes your media fun.

### 15. **Submission Comment** (optional)
This is the field where you can provide any additional comments about your submission.

### 16. **Agree** (required)
This is the field where you agree that the content is suitable for public viewing. Please select "True" to confirm.

### 17. **Submission Agree Comment** (optional)
This is the field where you can provide additional comments or reasons for not agreeing that the content is suitable for public viewing.

Tips:

* Ensure that the content submitted is not a duplicate.
* Be honest and accurate in your submissions.
* Follow the submission guidelines and rating scales carefully.
* Use the provided fields to provide as much detail as possible about your submission.
