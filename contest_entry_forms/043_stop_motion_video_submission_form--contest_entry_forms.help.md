# Stop Motion Video Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help artists submit their stop motion video creations for review and potential inclusion in festivals, schools, or contests. By filling out this form, artists can provide essential information about their work and increase their chances of getting noticed by relevant entities.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Title** of your stop motion video in the text field at the top of the page.
2. Describe your artwork in the **Description** field, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Enter your **Artist Name** as you would like it to be listed in case your work is selected.
4. Ensure you enter a valid **Email** address where you can be contacted for follow-up questions or opportunities.
5. Select the **Category** that best represents the theme or genre of your artwork (Festivals, Schools, or Contests).
6. Upload your **Video File** to complete the submission.
7. If you have any supporting files, such as images or documents, you can upload them in **File 1**, **File 2**, and **File 3**.
8. Write a brief **Review** of your artwork, explaining what makes it unique and why you think it's worth considering.
9. Click the **Submit** button to send your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title**: A brief, descriptive title for your stop motion video.
* **Description**: A detailed explanation of your artwork, including its theme, style, and any notable features.
* **Artist Name**: Your name as the creator of the artwork.
* **Email**: A valid email address where you can be contacted for follow-up questions or opportunities.
* **Category**: A selection of the type of event or organization that best represents your artwork (Festivals, Schools, or Contests).
* **Upload Video**: A field where you can upload your stop motion video file.
* **File 1**, **File 2**, **File 3**: Optional fields for uploading supporting files, such as images or documents.
* **Review**: A brief statement explaining why your artwork is unique and worth considering.
* **Submit**: The button to send your completed form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your submission is complete.
* Use a valid email address to receive updates and notifications.
* Keep your **Description** concise but detailed, as this will help reviewers understand your artwork.
* Choose the correct **Category** to help your artwork reach the right audience.
* Make sure to upload a high-quality video file.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
