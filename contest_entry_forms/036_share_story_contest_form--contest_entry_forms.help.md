# Share Story Contest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Share Story Contest Form is designed to collect unique stories from participants for a contest. This form helps in maintaining the quality and uniqueness of entries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Story Title** (`story_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your story.
* **Participants Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you'd like it to be associated with your entry.
* **Story** (`story`, text, optional): Enter the actual story you'd like to share.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more categories that best fit your story.
* **Tags** (`tags`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more tags that apply to your entry.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other information you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
