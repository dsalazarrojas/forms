# Canine Competition Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Canine Competition Entry Form is a collection of fields designed to gather information about a dog's entry into a competition. It includes details about the dog's owner, dog's details, competition class, and other relevant information. Completing this form accurately and thoroughly ensures a smooth competition process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the owner's first name, last name, and email address on the first page.
2. Provide the dog's name, call name, breed, and date of birth on the second page.
3. Specify the dog's color and markings, competition class, and division or level on the third page.
4. Complete the handler's name, age, and relationship to the owner on the fourth page.
5. Provide the veterinarian's contact information and dog's vaccination status on the fifth page.
6. Specify whether the dog has any special needs or requirements on the sixth page.
7. Enter the emergency contact person's name and phone number on the seventh page.
8. Accept the liability waiver and choose a payment method for the entry fee on the eighth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner First Name** (`owner_first_name`, text, required): Enter the owner's given name.
* **Owner Last Name** (`owner_last_name`, text, required): Enter the owner's family name.
* **Owner Email Address** (`owner_email`, email, required): Enter the owner's contact email.
* **Owner Phone Number** (`owner_phone`, text, required): Enter the owner's contact number.
* **Street Address** (`owner_address`, text, required): Enter the dog's home address.
* **City** (`owner_city`, text, required): Enter the city of the dog's home address.
* **State or Province** (`owner_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province of the dog's home address.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`owner_zip`, text, required): Enter the postal code of the dog's home address.
* **Dog Name** (`dog_name`, text, required): Enter the dog's registered name.
* **Dog Call Name** (`dog_call_name`, text, required): Enter the dog's everyday name.
* **Dog Breed** (`dog_breed`, text, required): Enter the dog's breed as registered.
* **Dog Gender** (`dog_gender`, select_one, required): Select the dog's gender (Male or Female).
* **Dog Date of Birth** (`dog_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the dog's birth date.
* **Dog Color and Markings** (`dog_color_markings`, text, required): Enter the dog's coat description.
* **Kennel Club Registration Number** (`registration_number`, text, optional): Enter the dog's registration number if applicable.
* **Microchip Number** (`microchip_number`, text, optional): Enter the dog's microchip number if applicable.
* **Competition Class** (`competition_class`, select_one, required): Select the competition class (e.g. Conformation - breed standard).
* **Division or Level** (`division`, select_one, optional): Select the division or level of competition.
* **Previous Competition Wins** (`previous_wins`, text, optional): Enter any previous competition titles or awards.
* **Handler Name** (`handler_name`, text, required): Enter the handler's name who will be handling the dog.
* **Handler Age** (`handler_age`, number, optional): Enter the handler's age.
* **Handler Relationship to Owner** (`handler_relationship`, select_one, required): Select the handler's relationship to the owner (e.g. Owner).
* **Veterinarian Name and Phone** (`veterinary_contact`, text, required): Enter the veterinarian's contact information.
* **Dog's Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_one, required): Select the dog's current vaccination status (e.g. Yes - all current).
* **Health Certificate Attached** (`health_certificate`, select_one, required): Select whether a health certificate is attached or will be brought to the event.
* **Dog's Temperament Assessment** (`temperament_assessment`, select_one, required): Select the dog's behavior description (e.g. Excellent - very social).
* **Special Needs or Requirements** (`special_needs`, text, optional): Enter any special accommodations the dog may need.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact person's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Liability Waiver Accepted** (`liability_waiver`, select_one, required): Accept the liability waiver.
* **Entry Fee Payment Method** (`entry_fee_payment`, select_one, required): Select the payment method for the entry fee.
* **Entry Fee Amount** (`entry_fee_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount paid for the entry fee.
* **Photo Permission for Event Catalog** (`catalog_photo_permission`, select_one, optional): Select whether permission is granted for the use of photos in the event catalog.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional information.
* **Entry Submission Date** (`entry_date`, date, required): Enter the date the entry is submitted.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
* Double-check the owner's and handler's details for accuracy.
* Provide clear and concise information about the dog's details and needs.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all fields are filled in correctly.
