# Registration Form For Miss & Mr Mbhashe 2019 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from individuals for the Miss & Mr Mbhashe 2019 competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your surname in the "surname" field.
3. Provide your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
4. Input your email address in the "email" field.
5. Enter your birth date in the "birth_date" field in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
6. Select your age from the dropdown options in the "age" field.
7. Choose your title from the dropdown options in the "title" field.
8. Select one or more categories from the dropdown options in the "category" field.
9. Provide any additional notes in the "note" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Surname** (`surname`, text, required): Enter your surname.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Provide your contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Input a valid email address.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required): Enter your birth date in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Select your age from the dropdown options.
* **Title** (`title`, select_one, required): Choose your title from the dropdown options.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more categories from the dropdown options.
* **Note 1** (`note_input`, note, required): Provide any additional notes.
* **Note 2** (`note_input_2`, note, required): Provide any additional notes.

Note: This form requires all fields to be filled correctly to ensure accurate submission.
