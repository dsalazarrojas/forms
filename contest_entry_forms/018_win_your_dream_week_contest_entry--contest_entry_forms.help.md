# Win Your Dream Week Contest Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Win Your Dream Week Contest Entry" form is designed to collect personal details from users who wish to participate in the contest. This form will help us understand who you are and how to contact you if you win.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name.
2. Provide your Last Name.
3. Enter your valid Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Enter your Date of Birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your personal first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your personal last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`birth_date`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY). 

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for better communication and validation purposes.
- Ensure your email and phone number are valid and active.
- Review your entry carefully before submission for errors or missing information.
- If you have any issues or questions while filling out this form, please contact our support team.
- This form is intended for contest entry purposes only and will not be shared with third parties.
