# Photo Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Contest Entry Form is a simple, easy-to-use form designed to collect information from users who want to submit their entries for the photo contest. This form is intended to be completed by users who have a photo they would like to submit for judging.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Description** field with a brief description of your photo.
2. Upload your **Upload** by clicking on the upload button and selecting your photo from your computer or device.
3. Add any **Additional Comments** if you have any other information you would like to share about your entry.
4. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** numbers, if you have them, so we can contact you with updates on your entry.
5. Add any **Additional Info** about your entry, such as any relevant information about your photo.
6. Double-check that all fields are filled in correctly and submit your entry.

## Tips
- Make sure to upload a photo that is in the correct format and size.
- Keep your description and additional comments brief and concise.
- Use your email and phone number to ensure you receive updates on your entry.
- Review all fields carefully before submitting your entry to ensure everything is correct.
