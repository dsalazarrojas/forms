# short_story_contest_voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
The short_story_contest_voting form is designed to collect user feedback and ratings for a short story contest. It allows users to vote and rate submissions, providing an easy way for contest participants to engage with each other and help determine the winning story.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form completely, including all required fields (none are currently marked as required).
2. For fields with options, select one or multiple options that apply to you.
3. Enter your email address and phone number (if you wish to provide them).
4. Share your feedback and comments in the "Feedback" and "Reader Comments" fields.
5. Click the "Submit Vote" button to confirm your vote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vote For Story**: This field allows you to select one story to vote for.
* **Vote Rating**: Rate the selected story from 1 to 100.
* **Feedback**: Share any feedback or comments about the stories you've voted for.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted with contest updates or results.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted for any reason related to the contest (optional).
* **Reader Comments**: Share any comments or feedback about the stories you've voted for.
* **Has Reader Voted**: Select all options that apply to you, indicating that you have voted for this story.
* **Submit Vote**: Confirm your vote by selecting one option from the list. This does not affect the contest outcome, it's just for tracking purposes.
