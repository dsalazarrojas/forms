# Football Pick'em Contest Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit an entry for a Football Pick'em Contest. It collects information from both the participant and the contest organizer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields for the participant, including Week, Participant Name, Participant Email, Form Submit Date, Form Submit Time, Contest Name, Contest ID, Contest Start Date, and Contest End Date.
2. Fill in the required fields for the organizer, including Organizer Name, Organizer Email, and Rule 1, Rule 2, and Rule 3.
3. If the Game ID field is present, select one of the options (Yes or No) to indicate whether the participant is participating in the game.
4. The other fields, such as Team Points, Confidence, Entry Status, Participant Phone, Participant Address, Submission IP, Submission User Agent, and Submission Referrer, are not required and can be left blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Week** (`week`, number, required): Enter the week of the contest you are submitting for.
* **Game ID** (`game_id`, select_one, optional): Select Yes or No to indicate whether you are participating in the game.
* **Team Points** (`team_points`, number, required): Enter the points you have chosen for your team.
* **Confidence** (`confidence`, number, required): Enter a number to indicate your confidence level (e.g., 1-10).
* **Entry Status** (`entry_status`, select_one, required): Select Active or Inactive to indicate your entry status.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if available).
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, optional): Enter your address (if available).
* **Form Submit Date** (`form_submit_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Form Submit Time** (`form_submit_time`, time, required): Enter the time you are submitting this form.
* **Submission IP** (`submission_ip`, text, optional): Enter the IP address of the device you are using to submit this form.
* **Submission User Agent** (`submission_user_agent`, text, optional): Enter the user agent of the device you are using to submit this form.
* **Submission Referrer** (`submission_referrer`, text, optional): Enter the referrer of the device you are using to submit this form.
* **Organizer Name** (`organizer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contest organizer.
* **Organizer Email** (`organizer_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contest organizer.
* **Organizer Phone** (`organizer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contest organizer (if available).
* **Organizer Address** (`organizer_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the contest organizer (if available).
* **Contest Name** (`contest_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contest.
* **Contest ID** (`contest_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the contest.
* **Contest Start Date** (`contest_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the contest.
* **Contest End Date** (`contest_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the contest.
* **Rule 1** (`rule_1`, text, required): Enter any rules or guidelines for the contest.
* **Rule 2** (`rule_2`, text, required): Enter any additional rules or guidelines for the contest.
* **Rule 3** (`rule_3`, text, required): Enter any further rules or guidelines for the contest.
