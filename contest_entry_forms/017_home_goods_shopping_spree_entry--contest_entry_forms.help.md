# home_goods_shopping_spree_entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **home_goods_shopping_spree_entry** form is used to collect entry information for a contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review the form's purpose and each field's purpose to ensure you understand what information is being collected.
2. Fill in the **Entry Details** page with any relevant information about your entry.
3. Provide your **Name**, **Address**, and **Contact Number** to help us contact you if you are selected as a winner.
4. Select your **Entry Date** and confirm your **Eligibility** for the contest.
5. Confirm your **Consent** to participate in the contest.
6. Select your **Type of Entry** and **Entry Status**.
7. Enter any other required information, such as **Store Information**, **Winner Contact**, and **Form Version**, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Entry Details (1)**: This page provides an opportunity to enter any relevant details about your entry.
* **Name (2)**: Please enter your name as it should be displayed on any contest-related communications.
* **Address (3)**: Enter your address to help us contact you if you are selected as a winner.
* **Entry Date (4)**: Select the date you are entering the contest.
* **Contact Number (5)**: Enter your contact number to receive any contest-related notifications.
* **Consent (6)**: Confirm that you have read and understand the contest rules and conditions. You must select one of the options provided.
* **Email (7)**: Enter your email address to receive any contest-related communications.
* **Store Information (8)**: Enter any relevant store information, if applicable.
* **Confirm Eligibility (9)**: Confirm that you are eligible to participate in the contest. You must select all applicable options.
* **Winner Contact (10)**: Select the contact information for the winner, if applicable.
* **Confirm Consent (11)**: Confirm that you have read and understand the contest rules and conditions.
* **Type of Entry (12)**: Select the type of entry you are submitting.
* **Entry Status (13)**: Select the status of your entry.
* **Entry ID (14)**: Enter your entry ID, if provided.
* **Winner ID (15)**: Enter the winner ID, if provided.
* **Winner Name (16)**: Enter the winner's name, if applicable.
* **Winner Email (17)**: Enter the winner's email address, if applicable.
* **Winner Address (18)**: Enter the winner's address, if applicable.
* **Winner Phone (19)**: Enter the winner's phone number, if applicable.
* **Total Points (20)**: Enter the total points earned, if applicable.
* **Points Earned (21)**: Enter the points earned, if applicable.
* **Total Entries (22)**: Enter the total number of entries, if applicable.
* **Form Type (23)**: Select the form type, if applicable.
* **Form Version (24)**: Enter the form version, if applicable.
* **Submission Date (25)**: Select the submission date, if applicable.

## Tips

* Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure you are providing accurate and complete information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the contest administrators for clarification.
* Please note that some fields may be required or optional, depending on the contest requirements.
