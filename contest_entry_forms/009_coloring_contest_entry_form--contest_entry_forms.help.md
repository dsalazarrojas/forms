# Coloring Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coloring Contest Entry Form is a submission form for participants to enter our coloring contest. It collects information about the participant, their artwork, and their agreement to the contest rules.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the participant information section with your full name, age, and date of birth.
2. Choose your age category based on your age.
3. Select the coloring template used for your artwork.
4. Choose the medium used to color your artwork.
5. Estimate the time spent on your artwork.
6. Upload a clear photo or scan of your colored artwork.
7. Optionally, provide an artist statement and inspiration for your artwork.
8. Check if you have participated in our contests before.
9. Confirm that your artwork is original.
10. Agree to allow us to display your artwork and accept the contest rules.
11. Optionally, provide suggestions for future contests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Participant Age** (Required): Enter your age to determine your age category.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your date of birth for age category verification.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (Optional): If you are under 18, please enter your parent or guardian's name.
* **Contact Email** (Required): Enter a valid email address for contest updates and results.
* **Contact Phone** (Required): Enter a valid phone number for winner notification.
* **Entry Details**: Briefly describe your artwork and its significance.
* **Entry Title** (Required): Give your artwork a creative title.
* **Age Category** (Required): Choose your age category based on your age.
* **Coloring Template Used** (Required): Select the coloring template used for your artwork.
* **Coloring Medium Used** (Required): Choose the medium used to color your artwork.
* **Time Spent** (Optional): Estimate the time spent on your artwork.
* **Upload Your Artwork** (Required): Upload a clear photo or scan of your colored artwork.
* **Artist Statement** (Optional): Briefly describe your creative choices and inspirations.
* **What Inspired Your Color Choices** (Optional): Tell us about your inspiration.
* **Have You Entered Before** (Optional): Check if you have participated in our contests before.
* **Original Work Declaration** (Required): Confirm that your artwork is original.
* **Publicity Rights** (Required): Agree to allow us to display your artwork.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Required): Agree to accept the contest rules.
* **How Can We Improve** (Optional): Provide suggestions for future contests.
