# Graphic Design Entry Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for users to submit their graphic design entries for review, showcasing their creative work to potential clients or evaluators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "entry_details" field with a brief description of your graphic design entry.
2. Upload an "image" of your graphic design.
3. Choose a "category" (Agency, School, or Event Organizer) that best describes your entry.
4. Select relevant "tags" (Design, Creative, Innovation) to help evaluators quickly understand your entry's focus.
5. Upload "additional files" related to your graphic design entry, such as design files or documents.
6. Add any "comments" you'd like to include with your submission.
7. Enter your contact "email" and "phone" information so that evaluators can get in touch with you.
8. Optionally, add the "date" and "time" of when your design was completed or submitted.
9. Click the "submit" button to complete and submit your entry.
10. If you need to cancel your entry, click the "cancel" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **entry_details (Graphic Design Entry Upload Form 1)**: Enter a brief description of your graphic design entry.
* **image (Graphic Design Entry Upload Form 2)**: Upload an image of your graphic design.
* **description (Graphic Design Entry Upload Form 3)**: Add any additional details about your graphic design entry.
* **category (Graphic Design Entry Upload Form 4)**: Select from Agency, School, or Event Organizer to help categorize your entry.
* **tags (Graphic Design Entry Upload Form 5)**: Choose relevant tags such as Design, Creative, or Innovation to describe your entry.
* **upload_design_file (Graphic Design Entry Upload Form 6)**: Upload any additional design files related to your entry.
* **upload_additional_files (Graphic Design Entry Upload Form 7)**: Upload any other files related to your graphic design entry.
* **comments (Graphic Design Entry Upload Form 8)**: Add any comments or notes about your submission.
* **email (Graphic Design Entry Upload Form 9)**: Enter your email address so that evaluators can contact you.
* **phone (Graphic Design Entry Upload Form 10)**: Enter your phone number so that evaluators can reach out to you.
* **date (Graphic Design Entry Upload Form 11)**: Enter the date when your design was completed or submitted.
* **time (Graphic Design Entry Upload Form 12)**: Enter the time when your design was completed or submitted.
* **submit (Graphic Design Entry Upload Form 13)**: Click to complete and submit your entry.
* **cancel (Graphic Design Entry Upload Form 14)**: Click to cancel your entry and start over.
