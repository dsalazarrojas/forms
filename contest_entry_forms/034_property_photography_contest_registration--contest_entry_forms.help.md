# Property Photography Contest Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to register for a property photography contest. It is a required form that captures essential information about the participant and their entry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Info section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. In the Photo Details section, provide a brief description of your photo.
3. In the Contest Agreement section, select your level of participation in previous property photography contests.
4. In the Property Info section, provide the address and details of the property you are submitting.
5. In the Team Info section, provide the names and contact information of your team members.
6. In the Entry Details section, provide a brief description of your entry.
7. If you have a team, select the type of participant that best describes your team in the Participant Agreement section.
8. Finally, submit your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`name`, `email`, `phone number`, required): Please fill out your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Photo Details** (`description`, required): Please provide a brief description of your photo.
* **Contest Agreement** (`select one`, required): Select how many times you have participated in a property photography contest before.
* **Property Info** (`address`, `description`, required): Please provide the address and details of the property you are submitting.
* **Team Info** (`names and contact information`, required): Please provide the names and contact information of your team members.
* **Entry Details** (`description`, required): Please provide a brief description of your entry.
* **Participant Agreement** (`text`, not required): Please provide any additional information you wish to share with the contest organizers.
* **Submit Entry** (`submit`, not required): Please select at least one type of participant that best describes your team.
