# Playwriting Contest Submission Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Playwriting Contest Submission Request Form is a form designed to gather information from users who wish to submit their playwriting entries for consideration in a contest.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for your playwriting submission.
2. Provide a detailed description of your playwriting entry.
3. Enter the date and time of your submission.
4. Select the category of your submission (e.g., drama, comedy, etc.).
5. Indicate whether your submission is tagged with any specific tags (e.g., experimental, new play, etc.).
6. Enter any additional details about your submission.
7. Enter your email address so we can contact you about your submission.
8. Enter your phone number for easy contact.
9. Enter your name and other contact information for us to reach out to you.
10. Enter your address, city, state, and zip code for our records.
11. Enter the name of your organization (if any).
12. Describe your role in the organization.
13. Specify your relationship to the organization (if any).
14. Choose your preferred contact method (e.g., email, phone, etc.).
15. Enter any additional contact information for us to reach out to you.
16. Enter your contact email address.
17. Enter your contact phone number.
18. Enter your contact address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title (form_title)** (`text`, required): Enter a brief title for your playwriting submission.
* **Playwriting Contest Entry (playwriting_contest_entry)** (`text`, required): Provide a detailed description of your playwriting entry.
* **Submission Date (submission_date)** (`date`, required): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Submission Time (submission_time)** (`time`, required): Enter the time of your submission.
* **Script (script)** (`text`, required): Attach a script of your playwriting entry.
* **Playwriting Contest Category (playwriting_contest_category)** (`select_one`, required): Select the category of your submission (e.g., drama, comedy, etc.).
* **Playwriting Contest Tag (playwriting_contest_tag)** (`select_multiple`, required): Indicate whether your submission is tagged with any specific tags (e.g., experimental, new play, etc.).
* **Submission (playwriting_contest_submission)** (`text`, required): Enter any additional details about your submission.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your submission.
* **Phone (phone)** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Name (name)** (`text`, required): Enter your name and other contact information for us to reach out to you.
* **Address (address)** (`text`, required): Enter your address, city, state, and zip code for our records.
* **Organization (organization)** (`text`, required): Enter the name of your organization (if any).
* **Role in Organization (role_in_organization)** (`text`, required): Describe your role in the organization.
* **Relationship (relationship)** (`text`, required): Specify your relationship to the organization (if any).
* **Contact Method (contact_method)** (`text`, required): Choose your preferred contact method (e.g., email, phone, etc.).
* **Contact Information (contact_info)** (`text`, required): Enter any additional contact information for us to reach out to you.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)** (`email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)** (`text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Address (contact_address)** (`text`, required): Enter your contact address.

## Tips

* Ensure you provide accurate and detailed information to increase the chances of your submission being considered.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents to submit with your entry, you can attach them as a file.
* If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your submission and look forward to reviewing it.
