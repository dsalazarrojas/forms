# Team List Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Team List Submission Form is used to gather information about team members, including players and team officials. This form is designed to help teams submit their lists of players and officials for approval and processing.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the team.
2. List the names of the players on your team.
3. Assign a jersey number to each player.
4. List the names of the team officials.
5. Assign a jersey number to each team official.
6. Specify the position of each team official.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Team List** (`team_list`, text, required): Enter the title of the team.
  This field is the main entry field for the team name.
- **Players** (`players`, text, optional): List the names of the players on your team.
  Enter the names of all the players on your team, one name per line.
- **Players Name** (`players_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of a player.
  This field is to be used in conjunction with the players field above.
- **Jersey Number** (`jersey_number`, number, optional): Enter the jersey number of a player.
  This field is to be used in conjunction with the players field above.
- **Players Jersey Number** (`players_jersey_number`, number, optional): Enter the jersey number of a player.
  This field is to be used in conjunction with the players field above.
- **Team Officials** (`team_officials`, text, optional): List the names of the team officials.
  Enter the names of all the team officials, one name per line.
- **Team Officials Name** (`team_officials_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of a team official.
  This field is to be used in conjunction with the team officials field above.
- **Team Officials Jersey Number** (`team_officials_jersey_number`, number, optional): Enter the jersey number of a team official.
  This field is to be used in conjunction with the team officials field above.
- **Team Officials Position** (`team_officials_position`, text, optional): Specify the position of a team official.
  This field is to be used in conjunction with the team officials field above.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* Use the fields in conjunction with each other as specified above.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with a team representative or the form designer.
