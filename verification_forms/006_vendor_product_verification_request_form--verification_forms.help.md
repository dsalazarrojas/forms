# Vendor Product Verification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Product Verification Request Form is a crucial document that helps suppliers provide detailed information about their products for verification. This form is used to gather accurate and up-to-date product details, enabling efficient and secure verification processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Supplier Information section with your company's contact details and other relevant information.
2. Provide comprehensive General Information about the product, including its description, features, and any other notable details.
3. Upload product images to showcase the product's features and appearance.
4. Enter Shipping Details, such as the shipping address and any specific shipping instructions.
5. Enter Contact Information, including the contact person's name, phone number, and email address.
6. Add any additional Notes to provide further context or details about the product.
7. Review the form carefully before submitting it for verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Information** (Required): Enter your company's name, address, phone number, and other relevant contact details.
* **General Info** (Optional): Provide a detailed description of the product, its features, and any other notable details.
* **Product Info** (Optional): Enter more detailed information about the product, such as its dimensions, materials, and certifications.
* **Product Images** (Optional): Upload images of the product to showcase its features and appearance.
* **Shipping Details** (Optional): Enter the shipping address and any specific shipping instructions.
* **Contact Info** (Required): Enter the contact person's name, phone number, and email address.
* **Notes** (Optional): Add any additional notes or details about the product.
* **Supplier Documentation** (Optional): Upload any relevant documentation, such as certificates or manuals.
* **Product Details** (Optional): Enter more detailed information about the product, such as its dimensions, materials, and certifications.
* **Shipping Report** (Optional): Enter any shipping reports or tracking information.
* **Notes 1-7** (Optional): Add any additional notes or details about the product.
* **Shipping Address** (Optional): Enter the shipping address of the product.
* **Contact Person** (Required): Enter the contact person's name and other relevant details.
* **Contact Info** (Required): Re-enter the contact person's contact information (phone and email).
* **Phone** (Required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Email** (Required): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Website** (Required): Enter the contact person's website (if applicable).
* **Notes 3-6** (Optional): Add any additional notes or details about the product.
