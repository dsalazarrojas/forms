# Customer Refund Verification Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to assist customers in understanding the refund request form, which is used to verify and validate the details of their refund request. The form aims to gather accurate and essential information from customers to process their refund efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form fields carefully and make sure you have all the required information ready.
2.  Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring all fields are completed.
3.  Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
4.  Submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Purchase Details** (`purchase_details`, text, required/optional): Please enter your purchase details, including the date of purchase, item description, and purchase order number. This information helps us to identify your purchase accurately.
*  **Refund Request** (`refund_request`, text, required/optional): Describe the reason for your refund request. Be as detailed as possible, including any relevant documentation or proof.
*  **Documentation** (`documentation`, text, required/optional): Attach any relevant documentation or proof supporting your refund request, such as receipts, invoices, or communication with our team.
*  **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): Please provide your contact information, including your name, address, and phone number. This is crucial for us to contact you regarding your refund status.
