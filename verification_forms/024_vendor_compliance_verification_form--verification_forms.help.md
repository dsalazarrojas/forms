# Vendor Compliance Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify vendor information for compliance purposes. Please fill out the form to ensure that the vendor's data is up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the vendor from the list of options.
* Enter the vendor's address.
* Enter the vendor's phone number.
* Enter the vendor's email address.
* Add any additional notes about the vendor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor** (`vendor`, `select_one`, required): Select the vendor from the list of available options. This field is required for compliance verification.
* **Vendor Address** (`vendor_address`, `text`, required): Enter the vendor's address for accurate contact information.
* **Vendor Phone** (`vendor_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the vendor's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Vendor Email** (`vendor_email`, `email`, required): Enter the vendor's email address for contact purposes.
* **Vendor Notes** (`vendor_notes`, `note`, required): Add any additional notes or comments about the vendor. This field is not limited in length.
