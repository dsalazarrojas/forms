# Birth Certificate Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to verify a birth certificate for official purposes. It is a requirement for various government agencies, institutions, and organizations to confirm the accuracy of a birth certificate.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please ensure you have all required information and documents before starting the form.
2.  Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, following the instructions for each field.
3.  Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **--  Birth Certificate Verification --** (`section_intro`): This is an introductory section that explains the purpose of the form.
* **Submit this form to verify a birth certificate** (`intro_text`): Provide a brief explanation of why you need to verify the birth certificate.
* **--  Applicant Information --** (`section_applicant`): This section collects information about the person requesting the verification.
	+ **Applicant First Name** (`applicant_first_name`): Enter your first name.
	+ **Applicant Last Name** (`applicant_last_name`): Enter your last name.
	+ **Applicant Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
	+ **Applicant Email Address** (`applicant_email`): Enter your contact email address.
	+ **Your Relationship to Certificate Holder** (`applicant_relationship`): Select how you are related to the certificate holder.
* **--  Certificate Information --** (`section_certificate_info`): This section collects information about the birth certificate.
	+ **Certificate Holder First Name** (`cert_holder_first_name`): Enter the first name of the person on the certificate.
	+ **Certificate Holder Middle Name** (`cert_holder_middle_name`): Enter the middle name of the person on the certificate (if applicable).
	+ **Certificate Holder Last Name** (`cert_holder_last_name`): Enter the last name of the person on the certificate.
	+ **Date of Birth** (`cert_holder_dob`): Enter the birth date of the person on the certificate.
	+ **Mother's Full Name** (`mother_name`): Enter the mother's full name as it appears on the certificate.
	+ **Father's Full Name** (`father_name`): Enter the father's full name (if on the certificate).
	+ **City of Birth** (`birth_city`): Enter the city where the child was born.
	+ **State of Birth** (`birth_state`): Enter the state where the child was born.
* **--  Certificate Details --** (`section_cert_details`): This section provides additional information about the certificate.
	+ **Certificate Issue Date** (`cert_issue_date`): Enter the date when the certificate was issued.
	+ **Certificate Number** (`cert_number`): Enter the certificate number (if known).
	+ **Certificate File Number** (`cert_file_number`): Enter the certificate file number (if known).
	+ **Certificate Type** (`cert_type`): Select the type of certificate.
* **--  Purpose of Verification --** (`section_purpose`): This section explains why you need verification.
	+ **Purpose of Verification** (`verification_purpose`): Select the reason for verification.
	+ **Detailed Purpose** (`purpose_description`): Explain the need for verification.
* **--  Identity Verification --** (`section_identity_verification`): This section verifies your identity.
	+ **Type of ID Presented** (`id_type`): Select the type of ID you have.
	+ **ID Number** (`id_number`): Enter the ID number.
	+ **ID Issue Date** (`id_issue_date`): Enter the date when the ID was issued.
	+ **ID Expiration Date** (`id_expiration_date`): Enter the date when the ID expires.
* **--  Verification Request Details --** (`section_request`): This section requests additional information for verification.
	+ **Type of Verification Requested** (`verification_type`): Select the type of verification you need.
	+ **Certified Copy of Certificate Needed** (`certified_copy_needed`): Select if you need a certified copy of the certificate.
	+ **Number of Copies** (`number_of_copies`): Enter the number of certified copies you need (if applicable).
* **--  Authorization and Consent --** (`section_authorization`): This section confirms your authorization and consent.
	+ **I certify the information is accurate** (`information_accuracy`): Confirm that the information is accurate.
	+ **I am legally authorized to request verification** (`legal_authorization`): Confirm that you are authorized to request verification.
	+ **I acknowledge this request under penalty of perjury** (`signature_acknowledgment`): Confirm that you acknowledge the request under penalty of perjury.

## Tips

* Ensure all information is accurate and complete before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact our support team.
