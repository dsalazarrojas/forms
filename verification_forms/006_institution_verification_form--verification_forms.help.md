# Institution Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Institution Verification Form is a tool used to collect information from institutions, organizations, or individuals to verify their identity and credentials. This form is designed to gather relevant details about the institution, its representatives, and their contact information. By filling out this form, you will be able to verify the authenticity of the institution and ensure accurate representation of their data in our system.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **User Name**: This is the name of the person filling out the form. It is not a required field, but it helps us associate your response with your identity.
2.  Enter your **User Email**: This is your email address associated with your institution. This field is required as it helps us contact you for any further information or verification process.
3.  Enter your **Institution Name**: This field is also not required, but it helps us verify the name of your institution and associate it with your response. If your institution has a specific name or acronym, please ensure to enter it correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **user_name**: This field is a text box where you can enter your name. It's an optional field, but it helps us identify you.
*   **user_email**: This field is a required email address associated with your institution. Please enter a valid email that we can use to contact you if needed.
*   **institution_name**: This field is a text box where you can enter your institution's name. It's not required, but it helps us verify the name of your institution.

## Tips

*   Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth and efficient verification process.
*   Ensure that your email address is valid and active, so we can contact you for any further information or clarification.
*   If you're having trouble filling out the form or need assistance, do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
