# Vendor Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Verification Form is used to collect and verify a vendor's information, ensuring that all necessary details are accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with the vendor's information.
2. Ensure that you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
4. Submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name (vendor-name)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the vendor's name as it appears on their official documents or business cards.
* **Contact Person (contact-person)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the contact person's name and title (e.g., John Doe, CEO).
* **Phone Number (phone-number)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the vendor's phone number for easy contact.
* **Email Address (email-address)** (`email`, required/optional): Enter the vendor's email address for communication.
* **Vendor Type (vendor-type)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of vendor (e.g., wholesaler, distributor, other).
* **Tax ID Number (tax-id-number)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the vendor's tax ID number (e.g., VAT number).
* **Address (address)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the vendor's business address for shipping and invoicing purposes.
* **Country (country)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the vendor's country of operation (e.g., USA, UK).
* **State (state)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select the state where the vendor operates (e.g., California, New York).
* **City (city)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the vendor's city of operation (e.g., Los Angeles, New York City).

## Tips

* Ensure that you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with the vendor or seek clarification.
