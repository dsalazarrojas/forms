# Trust Service Provider Identity Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Trust Service Provider Identity Verification Form" is used to verify the identity of Trust Service Providers (TSPs) that are registered on our platform. This form is a crucial part of our trust service provider verification process, and it helps us ensure that only legitimate and reliable TSPs are listed on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the TSP you are verifying.
2. Make sure to fill in all the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Review and verify the information you have entered to ensure accuracy.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. verify_identity**: This field is not used in this form and can be ignored.
* **2. First Name**: Enter the first name of the TSP you are verifying.
* **3. Last Name**: Enter the last name of the TSP you are verifying.
* **4. Email**: Enter the email address of the TSP you are verifying. Please use a valid email format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **5. Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the TSP you are verifying.
* **6. Address**: Enter the address of the TSP you are verifying.
* **7. Service Provider ID**: Enter the ID of the TSP you are verifying.
* **8. Verification Request ID**: Enter the ID of the verification request for this TSP.
* **9. Review Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the TSP's verification process.
* **10. Service Provider Name**: Enter the name of the TSP you are verifying.
* **11. Service Provider Type**: Select the type of TSP you are verifying from the options "Bank", "Government", or "Other".
* **12. Service Provider Logo**: Enter the URL or file path of the TSP's logo, if applicable.
* **13. Service Provider License**: Enter the TSP's license number or any other relevant information.
* **14. Service Provider Cert**: Enter the TSP's certification number or any other relevant information.
* **15. Identity Verification Type**: Select the type of verification process used for this TSP from the options "Manual Check", "Automatic Check", or "Other".
* **16. Review Status**: Select the status of the TSP's verification from the options "In Progress", "Approved", or "Rejected".
* **17. Date of Verification**: Enter the date on which the TSP's verification was completed.
* **18. Time of Verification**: Enter the time on which the TSP's verification was completed.
* **19. Review Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the TSP's verification process.
* **20. Verification Request Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the verification request.
* **21. Trust Service Provider Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the TSP's service provider information.
* **22. Verification Status**: Select the final status of the TSP's verification from the options "Pending", "Verified", or "Rejected".

Note: Make sure to review all the fields carefully and ensure that you are entering accurate information to avoid any errors or delays in the verification process.
