# Employment Eligibility Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from employers to verify the employment eligibility of their employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the employee's name in the **Employee Name** field.
2.  Provide the employee's social security number in the **Employee SSN** field.
3.  Enter the name of the employer in the **Employer Name** field.
4.  Provide the address of the employer in the **Employer Address** field.
5.  Enter the date the employee started working in the **Employee Start Date** field.
6.  Enter the date the employee ended their work in the **Employee End Date** field, if applicable.
7.  Enter the employee's job title in the **Job Title** field.
8.  Provide the reason for termination or departure from the company in the **Reason for Termination** field, if applicable.
9.  Enter the last day the employee worked in the **Last Day of Work** field, if applicable.
10. Enter a reason for departure, if the employee is no longer employed, in the **Reason for Departure** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee as it appears on their government-issued ID.
* **Employee SSN**: Enter the social security number of the employee.
* **Employer Name**: Enter the name of the employer.
* **Employer Address**: Enter the address of the employer.
* **Employee Start Date**: Enter the date the employee started working.
* **Employee End Date**: Enter the date the employee ended their work, if applicable.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the employee.
* **Reason for Termination**: Enter the reason for termination, if applicable.
* **Last Day of Work**: Enter the last day the employee worked, if applicable.
* **Reason for Departure**: Enter the reason for departure, if the employee is no longer employed.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
*   Use the correct format for date fields (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact your HR or payroll department for assistance.
