# phone_number_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The phone_number_verification_form is used to verify the user's phone number. It is essential to complete this form correctly to avoid any potential issues with account verification or other related processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. Please ensure that the number is accurate and matches the one on your account.
2. Click on the "Verify" dropdown menu to select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that the phone number is correct.
3. If you selected "No" in step 2, please enter the correct phone number in the "Confirm Phone Number" field.
4. Click on the "Verify Again" dropdown menu to select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that the phone number is correct after re-entry.
5. Once you have completed these steps, click the "Submit" button to finalize the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number. Please ensure that the number is accurate and matches the one on your account. (Required)
* **Verify**: Select "Yes" to confirm that your phone number is correct or "No" if you need to re-enter your phone number. (Optional)
* **Confirm Phone Number**: If you selected "No" in the "Verify" field, enter your correct phone number here. (Optional)
* **Verify Again**: Select "Yes" to confirm that your phone number is correct after re-entry. (Optional)

## Tips
* Double-check that your phone number is accurate before completing this form.
* If you are unsure about your phone number, please contact our support team for assistance.
* Completing this form will help ensure that your account is up-to-date and secure.
