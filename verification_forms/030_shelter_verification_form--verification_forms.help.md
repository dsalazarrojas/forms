# shelter_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The shelter_verification_form is designed to collect information for verification purposes, likely for shelters or social services. It is used to gather details about an individual's shelter status, which may include shelter name, location, and other personal details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the shelter name in the "shelter_name" field.
2. Provide the location in the "location" field.
3. Enter the date of admission in the "date_of_admission" field.
4. Enter the contact number in the "contact_number" field.
5. Indicate if there is a social worker involved in the "social_worker" field.
6. Enter the case number in the "case_number" field.
7. Provide the emergency contact in the "emergency_contact" field.
8. Enter the date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field.
9. Indicate the individual's gender in the "gender" field.
10. Select the individual's racial background in the "race" field.
11. Select the individual's ethnic background in the "ethnicity" field.
12. Indicate the individual's disability status in the "disability_status" field.
13. Indicate the individual's language proficiency in the "language" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **shelter_name** (shelter_name, text, required: false): This field requires the name of the shelter where the individual is currently residing.
- **location** (location, text, required: false): Enter the current location of the shelter.
- **date_of_admission** (date_of_admission, date, required: false): Enter the date the individual was admitted to the shelter.
- **contact_number** (contact_number, text, required: false): Enter the contact number for the shelter or individual.
- **social_worker** (social_worker, select_one, required: false): Indicate if there is a social worker involved with the individual.
- **case_number** (case_number, number, required: false): Enter the case number assigned to the individual.
- **emergency_contact** (emergency_contact, text, required: false): Provide the emergency contact information.
- **date_of_birth** (date_of_birth, date, required: false): Enter the individual's date of birth.
- **gender** (gender, select_one, required: false): Select the individual's gender.
- **race** (race, select_multiple, required: false): Select the individual's racial background.
- **ethnicity** (ethnicity, select_multiple, required: false): Select the individual's ethnic background.
- **disability_status** (disability_status, select_one, required: false): Indicate the individual's disability status.
- **language** (language, select_one, required: false): Indicate the individual's language proficiency.

## Tips
- Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- Double-check the fields for any required or optional information.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the relevant policies or guidelines.
- This form is for internal use only; ensure you have the necessary permissions and access to complete this form.
