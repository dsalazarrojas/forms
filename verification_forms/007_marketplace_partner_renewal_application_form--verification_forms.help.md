# Marketplace Partner Renewal Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help marketplace partners apply for renewal of their partnership. It ensures that the partner's information is up-to-date and accurate for the renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please fill out the required fields as accurately and completely as possible.
*   Make sure to review the field-by-field explanations below to ensure you understand each field.
*   If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact the marketplace partner support team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, `text`, **Required**): This field is required to enter your official partner name as it appears in the marketplace. Please enter your name exactly as it is registered with us.
*   **Partner Type** (`partner_type`, `select_one`, **Required**): Please select the type of partner that best represents you or your business. This field will help us categorize your application correctly.
*   **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the date when your partnership is due for renewal. This will ensure that we process your application in time for the renewal.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your contact number where we can reach you in case we need more information about your application.
*   **Email** (`email`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your email address where we can reach you with any updates about your application status.
*   **Partner Address** (`partner_address`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your business address for official correspondence.
*   **Partner Website** (`partner_website`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your business website for further information about your company.
*   **Renewal Status** (`renewal_status`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the status of your renewal application from the options: Approved, Pending, or Rejected.
*   **Status Update** (`status_update`, `text`, **Optional**): If you want to include any updates or comments about your application status, this is the field to do so.
*   **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the date you submitted this application.
*   **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, `text`, **Optional**): If the reviewer has any comments about your application, they will be listed here.
*   **Reviewer Signature** (`reviewer_signature`, `text`, **Required**): Please sign and date this field to confirm that you have reviewed the application.
*   **Reviewer Notes** (`reviewer_notes`, `note`, **Optional**): This is the field for the reviewer to include any additional comments or notes about your application.
*   **Partner Signature** (`partner_signature`, `text`, **Optional**): Please sign and date this field to confirm that you have reviewed the application.
*   **Partner Notes** (`partner_notes`, `note`, **Optional**): This is the field for the partner to include any additional comments or notes about their application.

Please review the form carefully and fill out all the required fields to ensure a smooth and efficient partner renewal process.
