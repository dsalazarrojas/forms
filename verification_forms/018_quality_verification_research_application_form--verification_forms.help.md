# Quality Verification Research Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quality Verification Research Application Form is used to gather information about participant involvement in research activities and projects. This form helps verify the quality of research conducted by participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the "university" field with your university affiliation. This is an essential field that helps us verify your research involvement.
2.  Next, answer the question about your project proposals. Select "Yes" if you have any project proposals, and "No" otherwise.
3.  If you have participant details, fill out the "participant details" field. This field is optional but helps us understand more about your involvement in research.
4.  Lastly, if you have a participant phone number, fill out the "participant phone number" field. This field is also optional but aids in communication verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **university** (`university`, text, required): Enter your university affiliation to help us verify your research involvement.
* **project proposals** (`project_proposals`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if you have any project proposals and "No" otherwise.
* **participant details** (`participant_details`, number, optional): Provide more details about your participant involvement in research (e.g., project ID, date, etc.). You can enter any numeric value you think is necessary.
* **participant phone number** (`participant_phone_number`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it to facilitate communication verification.
