This form is used to request an internship verification letter from the university or institution that a student intern has attended. The purpose of this form is to provide a formal verification of the intern's status during their internship program.

Before starting the form, ensure that you are authorized to request an internship verification letter for the specific student. Also, make sure that you are not duplicating the request for the same student.

Please note that some of the fields might be self-explanatory, but to avoid any confusion, a detailed explanation of each field is provided below.

## Purpose
This form is used to request an internship verification letter from a student's university or institution. It is used to provide a formal verification of the student's status during their internship program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information such as the school name, email address, and phone number of the student's institution.
2. Enter the student's ID number, employee name, and employee title.
3. Provide the student's supervisor's name, title, and contact information.
4. Enter the student's name (if different from employee name).
5. Enter the date of hire and date of departure (if applicable).
6. Enter the number of hours worked during the internship.
7. Upload the supervisor's signature.
8. Confirm that the supervisor is willing to provide a verification letter.
9. Provide the school's address, city, and state.
10. Review and submit the form.
