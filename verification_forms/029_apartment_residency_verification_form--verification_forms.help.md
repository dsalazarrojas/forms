# Apartment Residency Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Apartment Residency Verification Form is used to verify the residency status and details of tenants or residents in an apartment or rented property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the verification date.
2. Enter the name of the tenant or resident.
3. Enter the date of birth of the tenant or resident.
4. Enter the property address.
5. Enter the unit or apartment number.
6. Select the current occupancy status.
7. If no longer occupying, enter the occupancy end date.
8. Enter the name of the landlord or property manager.
9. Enter the landlord or property manager contact information.
10. Select the type of property.
11. Select if a signed lease agreement exists.
12. Select the rent payment status.
13. Enter the monthly rent amount.
14. Select if there have been lease violations or complaints.
15. If violations exist, enter details.
16. Select if a notice to vacate has been received.
17. Select the status of the security deposit.
18. Select the purpose of this verification.
19. Enter the name of the party requesting verification.
20. Enter the contact information of the party requesting verification.
21. Confirm if the tenant is authorized for verification.
22. Enter the name of the person providing verification.
23. Enter the title of the person providing verification.
24. Enter the date of verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Verification date** (`verification_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are verifying the residency status.
* **Tenant or resident name** (`tenant_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the tenant or resident.
* **Tenant date of birth** (`tenant_dob`, `date`, required): Enter the date of birth of the tenant or resident.
* **Property address** (`property_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the property where the tenant or resident resides.
* **Unit or apartment number** (`unit_number`, `text`, not required): Enter the unit or apartment number where the tenant or resident lives.
* **Current occupancy status** (`occupancy_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current occupancy status from the options provided.
* **If no longer occupying, occupancy end date** (`occupancy_end_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date the tenant or resident is no longer occupying the property.
* **Landlord or property manager name** (`landlord_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the landlord or property manager.
* **Landlord contact information** (`landlord_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the contact information of the landlord or property manager.
* **Type of property** (`property_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of property from the options provided.
* **Signed lease agreement exists** (`lease_agreement_exists`, `select_one`, required): Select if a signed lease agreement exists.
* **Rent payment status** (`rent_payment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the rent payment status from the options provided.
* **Monthly rent amount** (`monthly_rent_amount`, `number`, not required): Enter the monthly rent amount in dollars.
* **Any lease violations or complaints** (`violations`, `select_one`, required): Select if there have been lease violations or complaints.
* **Details of violations if any** (`violation_details`, `text`, not required): Enter the details of lease violations if any.
* **Notice to vacate received** (`notice_to_vacate`, `select_one`, not required): Select if a notice to vacate has been received.
* **Security deposit status** (`security_deposit_status`, `select_one`, not required): Select the status of the security deposit from the options provided.
* **Purpose of this verification** (`verification_purpose`, `select_one`, required): Select the purpose of this verification from the options provided.
* **Name of party requesting verification** (`requesting_party`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the party requesting verification.
* **Requesting party contact information** (`requesting_party_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the contact information of the party requesting verification.
* **Tenant authorized this verification** (`authorization_to_verify`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if the tenant is authorized for verification.
* **Person providing verification** (`verifying_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person providing verification.
* **Title of verifying person** (`verifying_person_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the person providing verification.
* **Verification date signed** (`verification_date_signed`, `date`, required): Enter the date of verification.

Tips

* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure or have questions about any field, please consult the guidelines or seek help from the relevant parties.
