# Member Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Member Verification Form is a tool used to collect personal information from members for general verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Select your member status in the "Select Option 1" field.
7. Select your additional options in the "Select Option 2" field.
8. Fill in your address in the "Address" field.
9. Provide any additional notes in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required: false): Please fill in your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required: false): Please fill in your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., "example@example.com").
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false): Please enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required: false): Please provide your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Member Since** (`Member Since`, time, required: false): Please select your member status.
* **Select Option 1** (`Select Option 1`, select_one, required: false): Please select one of the options provided.
* **Select Option 2** (`Select Option 2`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select multiple options provided.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required: false): Please fill in your address.
* **Note** (`Note`, note, required: false): Please provide any additional notes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields for accurate submission.
* Double-check your information for spelling and formatting errors.
* If you have any issues, please contact our support team for assistance.
