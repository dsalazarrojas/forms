# medical_history_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for verifying patient medical history information, including medical conditions, medication use, and allergies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

* Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and required fields.
* Fill out each field accurately and completely, using the available options for medical conditions, medication use, and allergies.
* Ensure that you complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: Enter your name and any other identifying information.
* **Medical Conditions**: Select all medical conditions that apply to you from the options provided. Use multiple selections if necessary.
* **Medication Use**: Select one option that best describes your current or recent medication use.
* **Medical History**: Provide a brief description of your medical history, including any relevant details.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities you have, including the substances or substances you are allergic to.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for assistance.
* Please ensure that you complete all required fields to avoid submission errors.
* If you need to make changes to your submission, contact the relevant authority for assistance.
