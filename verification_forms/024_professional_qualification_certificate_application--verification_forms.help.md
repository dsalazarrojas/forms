# Professional Qualification Certificate Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Professional Qualification Certificate Application form is a necessary document for those seeking to verify their professional credentials.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your personal details such as **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Date of Birth**, and **Contact Number**. These are your basic contact details that help us identify you properly.
2. Next, provide your **Email** address to facilitate communication.
3. Enter your **Professional Qualification**, which is the specific skill or trade you are applying for.
4. Enter your **Issuing Organization**, which is the institution or body that granted you the qualification.
5. Enter the **Issuing Date** when you received the qualification.
6. Enter your **Certificate Number**, if applicable.
7. If you have already obtained the qualification, **Upload Certificate** to support your application.
8. Add any **Review Comments** you may have, if any.
9. Select the **Review Status** of your application (Approved, Pending, or Rejected).
10. Enter the **Verification Date** when your qualification was verified.
11. Enter the **Verification Time** when your qualification was verified.
12. Sign your application with your **Signature**.
13. Provide your **Employer Name**, **Employer Address**, **Employer City**, **Employer State**, and **Employer Zip** details.
14. Enter the **Association Name**, **Association Address**, **Association City**, and **Association State** details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required: false): Enter a brief title for your application.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your primary contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your professional email address.
* **Professional Qualification** (`professional_qualification`, text, required: false): Describe your professional qualification or certification.
* **Issuing Organization** (`issuing_organization`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the institution or body that granted you the qualification.
* **Issuing Date** (`issuing_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when you received the qualification.
* **Certificate Number** (`certificate_number`, text, required: false): Enter the number of your qualification certificate, if applicable.
* **Upload Certificate** (`upload_certificate`, text, required: false): If you have already obtained the qualification, upload a digital copy of the certificate.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments or feedback you may have about your application.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of your application (Approved, Pending, or Rejected).
* **Verification Date** (`verification_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when your qualification was verified.
* **Verification Time** (`verification_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time when your qualification was verified.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): Sign your application with your name.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your employer's name.
* **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, text, required: false): Enter your employer's address.
* **Employer City** (`employer_city`, text, required: false): Enter your employer's city.
* **Employer State** (`employer_state`, text, required: false): Enter your employer's state.
* **Employer Zip** (`employer_zip`, text, required: false): Enter your employer's zip code.
* **Association Name** (`association_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the association or institution that issued your qualification.
* **Association Address** (`association_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the association or institution that issued your qualification.
* **Association City** (`association_city`, text, required: false): Enter the city of the association or institution that issued your qualification.
* **Association State** (`association_state`, text, required: false): Enter the state of the association or institution that issued your qualification.
