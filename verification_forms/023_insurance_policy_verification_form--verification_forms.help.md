# insurance_policy_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "insurance_policy_verification_form" is a form used to collect and verify information related to an insurance policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Policy ID number.
2. Enter the policy holder's first and last name.
3. Enter the policy holder's date of birth.
4. Enter the policy number.
5. Enter the name of the insurance provider.
6. Select the start date of the policy.
7. Select the end date of the policy.
8. Choose the policy type (Term Life, Whole Life, or Variable Life).
9. Choose the policy status (Active, Inactive, or Voided).
10. Enter the date the policy became active or inactive.
11. Enter the policy holder's date of birth again (for validation purposes).
12. Enter the policy holder's phone number.
13. Enter the policy holder's email address.
14. Add any additional notes regarding the policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policy ID** (`policy_id`, number, required): Enter the unique identifier for the policy.
* **Policy Holder Name** (`policy_holder_name`, text, required): First and last name of the policy holder.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): The policy holder's date of birth.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, required): The policy's unique number.
* **Insurance Provider Name** (`insurance_provider_name`, text, required): The name of the insurance provider.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): The policy's start date.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): The policy's end date.
* **Policy Type** (`policy_type`, select_one, required): Choose the policy type (Term Life, Whole Life, or Variable Life).
* **Policy Status** (`policy_status`, select_one, required): Choose the policy status (Active, Inactive, or Voided).
* **Policy Status Date** (`policy_status_date`, date, required): The date the policy became active or inactive.
* **Policy Holder's Date of Birth** (`policy_holder_dob`, date, required): The policy holder's date of birth for validation purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): The policy holder's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): The policy holder's email address.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes regarding the policy.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in as accurately as possible to avoid delays in processing.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult the relevant department or seek clarification before proceeding.
- This form should be used for the specified purpose only; if in doubt, please contact the relevant department before using it for other tasks.
