# employment_verification_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employment verification release form is used to collect information about a job applicant's employment history for verification purposes. This form should only be filled out by the applicant or an authorized representative and submitted to the employer for verification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information. All fields are optional, but please fill out as much as you can to ensure the employer can verify your employment history accurately.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it to the employer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`): A text field for the applicant to provide a brief description of their employment history.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`): A text field for the applicant to provide the name of their current or past employer(s).
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): A text field for the applicant to provide their employee ID or number (if applicable).
* **Date of Hire** (`date_of_hire`): A date field for the applicant to provide the date they started working for the employer.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): A text field for the applicant to provide their job title or position held with the employer.
* **Work Phone** (`work_phone`): A text field for the applicant to provide their work phone number.
* **Work Email** (`work_email`): An email field for the applicant to provide their work email address.
* **Supervisor** (`supervisor`): A text field for the applicant to provide the name of their supervisor or manager.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`): A text field for the applicant to provide the reason for requesting the employment verification.
* **Verification Date** (`verification_date`): A date field for the applicant to provide the date they would like the employer to verify their employment history.
* **Signed** (`signed`): A text field for the applicant or their representative to sign the form, indicating they have provided accurate information.
