# signature_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for signature verification, ensuring the legitimacy and authenticity of a signature on a document.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Form Title to describe the document or signature being verified.
2. Provide the name of the Institution or organization associated with the signature.
3. Enter the exact signature to be verified.
4. Choose the Verification Method used to verify the signature (e.g. in-person, notarization, etc.).
5. Record the Date and Time the signature was verified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Title** (1, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the document or signature being verified.
- **Institution** (2, text, required/optional): Provide the name of the Institution or organization associated with the signature.
- **Signature** (3, text, required/optional): Enter the exact signature to be verified.
- **Verification Method** (4, text, required/optional): Choose the method used to verify the signature (e.g. in-person, notarization, etc.).
- **Date Verified** (5, date, required/optional): Record the Date the signature was verified in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Time Verified** (6, time, required/optional): Record the Time the signature was verified in 12-hour format (e.g. 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
