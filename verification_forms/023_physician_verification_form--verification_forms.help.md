# Physician Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and verify personal and professional information from physicians for regulatory or administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form with your personal details, such as your first name, last name, address, phone number, and email.
2. Provide your medical specialty, the medical school you attended, and your license number.
3. Ensure that all the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your current address, including street address and any apartment or suite number.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you currently reside.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, optional): Select the state where you currently reside from the list of available options.
* **Zip** (`zip`, number, optional): Enter your current zip code.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your professional email address.
* **Specialty** (`specialty`, text, optional): Enter your medical specialty, if applicable.
* **Medical School** (`medical_school`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medical school you attended.
* **License Number** (`license_number`, text, optional): Enter your medical license number.
* **Medical School** (`medical_school`, text, optional): This field should only be filled in if you're a medical professional.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
* This form is for verification purposes only and is not intended for medical certification or credentialing.
