# Driving License Renewal Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driving License Renewal Verification Form is a form used to verify the status of a driver's license. This form is intended to be completed by drivers who are eligible for a license renewal and need to provide information about their existing license and vehicle registration. The form helps to ensure that the driver's license and vehicle information is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Date of Birth.
3. Provide your License Number and License Type.
4. Select the correct License Expiration Date.
5. Enter your Email address.
6. Fill in your Phone number.
7. Choose the current License Status.
8. Provide a reason for renewal (if applicable).
9. If you are renewing a vehicle license, fill in the Vehicle Year, Vehicle Make, Vehicle Model, Vehicle License Number, Vehicle Plate Number, and Vehicle Plate State. If you are renewing a boat or other type of vehicle, select "Other" for the Vehicle Make and Vehicle Model.
10. Choose the current License Renewal Status.
11. Select the current Verification Status.
12. If renewing a vehicle license, fill in the Renewal Date.
13. Fill in the License Renewal Fee.
14. Choose the current Renewal Status.
15. Choose the current License Renewal Status for the vehicle license.
16. Choose the current Verification Status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your license.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your license.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **License Number** (`license_number`, text, optional): Enter your driver's license number as it appears on your license.
* **License Type** (`license_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of license you hold (Driver license, Motorcycle license, State ID, or Other).
* **License Expiration Date** (`license_expiration_date`, date, optional): Select the date your license is set to expire.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **License Status** (`license_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the current status of your license (Active, Inactive, Expired, or Pending).
* **Reason for Renewal** (`reason_for_renewal`, text, optional): Enter the reason for your license renewal (if applicable).
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): If renewing a vehicle license, enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, select_multiple, optional): If renewing a vehicle license, choose the make of the vehicle (Toyota, Honda, Ford, Chevrolet, or Other).
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, select_multiple, optional): If renewing a vehicle license, choose the model of the vehicle (Accord, Civic, Mustang, Camero, or Other).
* **Vehicle License Number** (`vehicle_license_number`, text, optional): If renewing a vehicle license, enter the vehicle license number.
* **Vehicle Plate Number** (`vehicle_plate_number`, text, optional): If renewing a vehicle license, enter the vehicle plate number.
* **Vehicle Plate State** (`vehicle_plate_state`, select_multiple, optional): If renewing a vehicle license, choose the state where the vehicle is registered (California, Florida, New York, Texas, or Other).
* **Vehicle Registration Number** (`vehicle_registration_number`, text, optional): If renewing a vehicle license, enter the vehicle registration number.
* **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, optional): If renewing a vehicle license, enter the date of renewal.
* **License Renewal Date** (`license_renewal_date`, date, optional): Enter the date your license was last renewed.
* **License Renewal Status** (`license_renewal_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the current status of your license renewal (Active, Inactive, Pending, or Expired).
* **Verification Status** (`verification_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the current status of your verification (Verified, Unverified, Pending, or Rejected).
* **License Renewal Fee** (`license_renewal_fee`, number, optional): Enter the fee for renewing your license.
* **Renewal Status** (`renewal_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the current status of your license renewal (Approved, Rejected, Pending, or Inactive).
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, select_multiple, optional): If renewing a vehicle license, choose the make of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, select_multiple, optional): If renewing a vehicle license, choose the model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle License Number** (`vehicle_license_number`, text, optional): If renewing a vehicle license, enter the vehicle license number.
* **License Renewal Status** (`license_renewal_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the current status of your license renewal.
* **Verification Status** (`verification_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the current status of your verification.

Note: This form is for internal use only and is not intended to be used for external purposes.
