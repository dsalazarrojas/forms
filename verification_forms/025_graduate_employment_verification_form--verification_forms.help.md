# Graduate Employment Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Graduate Employment Verification Form is a tool used to verify the employment details of recent graduates. This form helps us to ensure that our graduate employees are employed in the correct job titles, roles, and other relevant details. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to help us verify your employment status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, paying attention to required fields.
2. Select the correct job title from the options provided.
3. Enter your company name and address as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
4. Choose your employment status as it is currently.
5. Enter the date you were hired, as it appears on your employment contract or offer letter.
6. Enter the date you graduated, as it appears on your diploma or transcript.
7. Enter the duration of your employment, in the format MM:HH (e.g., 01:30).
8. Select your job type as it is described in your job posting or job description.
9. If your job type is not listed, please select "Yes" from the "Job Type Other" field.
10. Select your job level as it is described in your job posting or job description.
11. If your job level is not listed, please select "High" from the "Job Level Other" field.
12. Select your industry as it is described in your job posting or job description.
13. Select your company size as it is described in your job posting or job description.
14. Enter the name of the department you work in, as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
15. Select your job function as it is described in your job posting or job description.
16. If your job function is not listed, please select "Yes" from the "Job Function Other" field.
17. Enter the location of your job, as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
18. If your job type is not listed, please select "Yes" from the "Job Type Other 2" field.
19. Select your employment outcome as it is described in your job posting or job description.
20. If your employment outcome is not listed, please select "Yes" from the "Employment Outcome Other" field.
21. Select your work status as it is described in your job posting or job description.
22. If your work status is not listed, please select "Active" from the "Work Status Other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the title of your job, as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company, as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): Enter the address of your company, as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Choose your current employment status.
* **Date Hired** (`date_hired`, date, required): Enter the date you were hired, as it appears on your employment contract or offer letter.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, required): Enter the date you graduated, as it appears on your diploma or transcript.
* **Employment Duration** (`employment_duration`, time, required): Enter the duration of your employment, in the format MM:HH (e.g., 01:30).
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required): Select your job type as it is described in your job posting or job description.
* **Job Type Other** (`job_type_other`, select_multiple, required): If your job type is not listed, select "Yes" from this field.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_one, required): Select your job level as it is described in your job posting or job description.
* **Job Level Other** (`job_level_other`, select_multiple, required): If your job level is not listed, select "High" from this field.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select your industry as it is described in your job posting or job description.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Select your company size as it is described in your job posting or job description.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the name of the department you work in, as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Supervisors** (`supervisors`, select_multiple, required): Select your supervisors as they are described in your job posting or job description.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, required): Select your job function as it is described in your job posting or job description.
* **Job Function Other** (`job_function_other`, select_multiple, required): If your job function is not listed, select "Yes" from this field.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, required): Enter the location of your job, as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Job Type Other 2** (`job_type_other_2`, select_multiple, required): If your job type is not listed, select "Yes" from this field.
* **Employment Outcome** (`employment_outcome`, select_one, required): Select your employment outcome as it is described in your job posting or job description.
* **Job Level Other 2** (`job_level_other_2`, select_multiple, required): If your job level is not listed, select "High" from this field.
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, select_one, required): Select your work status as it is described in your job posting or job description.
* **Employment Outcome Other** (`employment_outcome_other`, select_multiple, required): If your employment outcome is not listed, select "Yes" from this field.
