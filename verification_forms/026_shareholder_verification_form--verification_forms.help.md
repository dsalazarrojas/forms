# shareholder_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The **shareholder_verification_form** is used to collect and verify information about a shareholder. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and honestly to ensure the process runs smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Shareholder Information** page with the relevant details about the shareholder.
2. Provide **Company Details** about the shareholder's company.
3. Enter **Identification** information about the shareholder.
4. Verify the shareholder's **Information** by filling out the **Verification** fields.
5. Specify the **Verification Method** used for verification.
6. Fill out the **Date of Birth**, **Place of Birth**, and **Citizenship** fields with the relevant details.
7. Enter the shareholder's **Address** and **Contact** information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shareholder Information** (`shareholder_info`, text, required): This field is used to collect the shareholder's name and any other identifying information.
* **Company Details** (`company_info`, text, required): Enter the company's name and any other relevant details.
* **Identification** (`identification`, text, required): This field is used to collect any identification numbers or IDs associated with the shareholder.
* **Verification** (`verification`, text, required): Verify the information you've collected by filling out this field.
* **Verification Method** (`verification_method`, text, required): Specify the method used for verification, such as a photo ID or passport.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the shareholder's date of birth.
* **Place of Birth** (`place_of_birth`, text, required): Enter the place where the shareholder was born.
* **Citizenship** (`citizenship`, text, required): Specify the shareholder's citizenship.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the shareholder's address.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, required): Enter the shareholder's contact information, such as phone number or email.

Please note that all fields marked as "required" must be filled out accurately and honestly. If you're unsure about any field, please refer to your internal guidelines or seek clarification from the relevant authorities.
