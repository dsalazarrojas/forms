# Finance Analyst Education Employment Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your finance analyst education and employment history for verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employer's name in the "Employer Name" field.
2. Provide your job title in the "Job Title" field.
3. Select your current employment status in the "Employment Status" field.
4. Enter the dates of your employment in the "Employment Dates" field.
5. Select your education level from the list in the "Education Level" field.
6. Enter the name of your educational institution in the "Institution Name" field.
7. Enter the name of your degree in the "Degree Name" field.
8. Enter the field of study you pursued in the "Field of Study" field.
9. Enter your graduation date in the "Graduation Date" field.
10. Enter your GPA in the "GPA" field.
11. Enter the date you started your employment in the "Employment Start Date" field.
12. Enter the date you ended your employment in the "Employment End Date" field.
13. Select your current employment status in the "Employment Current Status" field.
14. Enter the reason for your leave in the "Reason for Leave" field.
15. Select the employment verification status in the "Employment Verification Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employer Name (Employer Name)
This field is where you enter your current or former employer's name.

* **Job Title (Job Title)**: Enter the job title you held at your previous employer.
* **Employment Status (Employment Status)**: Select the status of your employment from the list. (e.g., Active, Inactive, Terminated, etc.)
* **Employment Dates (Employment Dates)**: Enter the start and end dates of your employment.
* **Education Level (Education Level)**: Select the level of your highest degree earned from the list. (e.g., High School Diploma, Bachelor's Degree, etc.)
* **Institution Name (Institution Name)**: Enter the name of the institution where you earned your degree.
* **Degree Name (Degree Name)**: Enter the name of your degree, if applicable.
* **Field of Study (Field of Study)**: Enter the field of study you pursued.
* **Graduation Date (Graduation Date)**: Enter the date you graduated.
* **GPA (GPA)**: Enter your grade point average.
* **Employment Start Date (Employment Start Date)**: Enter the date you started your employment.
* **Employment End Date (Employment End Date)**: Enter the date you ended your employment.
* **Employment Current Status (Employment Current Status)**: Select your current employment status from the list. (e.g., Active, Inactive, Terminated, etc.)
* **Reason for Leave (Reason for Leave)**: Enter the reason for your departure from employment.
* **Employment Verification Status (Employment Verification Status)**: Select the status of your employment verification from the list. (e.g., Pending, Approved, Denied, etc.)

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth verification process.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to the relevant authorities for clarification.
* This form is designed to be non-duplicative, so please only enter information that has not been previously collected.
