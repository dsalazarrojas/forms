# Employment Verification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for employment verification purposes, such as verifying an employee's work history or employment status.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, start by reviewing the form's fields and selecting the relevant sections that apply to your situation.
2.  Fill out the requested information in each section, making sure to provide as much detail as possible.
3.  Once you've completed all required fields, review your answers for accuracy and completeness.
4.  If you're unsure about any field or have questions, contact your HR department or the person who requested the form for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Information** (`request_information`, `text`, required: false): This section is used to ask for general information about the employment verification request.
* **Employer Details** (`employer_details`, `text`, required: false): In this section, please provide details about your employer or the organization you're representing, such as their name, address, and contact information.
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, `text`, required: false): This section is for providing information about the employee whose employment status or work history you're trying to verify, such as their name, job title, and date of hire.
* **Authorized Organizations** (`authorized_organizations`, `text`, required: false): If you're working with other organizations that have authorized you to request employment verification, list their names and contact information.
* **Verification Request** (`verification_request`, `text`, required: false): Use this section to specify the reasons for the employment verification request, such as for loan applications or government benefits.
* **Employee Confirmation** (`employee_confirmation`, `text`, required: false): In this section, ask the employee to confirm their employment status or work history by signing and dating their acknowledgement.
* **Employer Confirmation** (`employer_confirmation`, `text`, required: false): The employer or authorized representative should confirm the employee's employment status or work history by signing and dating their acknowledgement.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `text`, required: false): If you need to request additional information or clarification, use this section to ask the employee or employer to follow up with you.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, required: false): Use this section to provide any other information that's relevant to the employment verification request.

Note: All fields are optional and may be skipped depending on the specific requirements of the employment verification request.
