# Verification Of Deposit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Verification of Deposit Form is used to collect information from tenants to verify deposits or payments made to landlords or property owners. This form ensures that both the tenant and landlord have provided accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Information about the tenant** section:
	* Provide accurate information about the tenant, including their name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter the **Information about the landlord** section:
	* Provide accurate information about the landlord, including their name and any other relevant details.
3. Enter the **Signature sections**:
	* Sign your name as the tenant in the **The tenant's signature** field.
	* Sign your name as the landlord in the **The landlord's signature** field.
4. Enter the **Date of signature**:
	* Date of the signature of both the tenant and the landlord.
5. Enter the **Rent details** (if applicable):
	* Provide any relevant details about the rent, such as the amount or payment method.
6. Enter the **Amount paid**:
	* Enter the amount paid by the tenant to the landlord.
7. Select the **Payment method**:
	* Choose how the payment was made (e.g., Bank Draft, Check, Money Order, Other).
8. Enter **Notes** (if applicable):
	* Provide any additional comments or notes about the payment.
9. Select the **Verification date**:
	* Choose whether this is a one-time payment or a recurring payment.
10. Select the **Verification status**:
	* Choose whether the payment has been verified or not.
11. Select the **Verification method**:
	* Choose whether verification was done or not.

* **Information about the tenant**:
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes

	+ Type: Text
