# Drone Operator Identity Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify the identity and credentials of a drone pilot. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure that your information is up to date and meets the required standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and ensure that you have all the necessary information to complete it.
2. Fill out all required fields (marked as `required`).
3. Choose the correct options for select fields, and select multiple options as necessary.
4. Ensure that the date and time fields are formatted correctly (yyyy-mm-dd for dates and hh:mm:ss for times).
5. Attach any required photos or documents as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required): Enter your full name and any other identifying information.
* **Drone License** (`drone_license`, text, required): Enter your drone pilot license number or ID.
* **Drone Experience** (`drone_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been piloting drones.
* **Drone License Type** (`drone_license_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of license you hold (Private, Commercial, or Remote).
* **Drone License Issuing Authority** (`drone_license_issuing_authority`, select_multiple, required): Choose the authority that issued your license (FAA, ICAO, EASA).
* **Drone License Number** (`drone_license_number`, text, required): Enter your license number or ID.
* **Drone Certification** (`drone_certification`, text, required): Enter any relevant certification or training information.
* **Drone Pilot Experience** (`drone_pilot_experience`, text, required): Enter any additional details about your piloting experience.
* **Drone Certification Level** (`drone_certification_level`, select_one, required): Choose the level of your certification (Basic, Advanced, Expert).
* **Drone Certification Type** (`drone_certification_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of certification you hold (Recreational, Commercial, Agricultural).
* **Drone Equipment Used** (`drone_equipment_used`, select_one, required): Choose the equipment you use for piloting (Camera, Sensors, Mapping System).
* **Drone Operation Frequency** (`drone_operation_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Choose how often you pilot drones (Regularly, Occasionally, Rarely).
* **Drone License Status** (`drone_license_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of your license (Active, Expired, Suspended).
* **Drone Pilot License Holder** (`drone_pilot_license_holder`, select_one, required): Choose whether you hold a pilot license (`True` or `False`).
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your name and contact details (phone and email).
* **Pilot Signature** (`drone_pilot_signature`, text, required): Sign the form with your signature.
* **Pilot Signature Date** (`drone_pilot_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Pilot Signature Time** (`drone_pilot_signature_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your signature.
* **Pilot Signature Place** (`drone_pilot_signature_place`, text, required): Enter the location where you signed the form.
* **Photo ID - Front** (`photo_id_front`, text, required): Attach a photo ID front page.
* **Photo ID - Back** (`photo_id_back`, text, required): Attach a photo ID back page.
* **Drone License Photo** (`drone_license_photo`, text, required): Attach your drone license photo.
* **Drone License Number Photo** (`drone_license_number_photo`, text, required): Attach your drone license number photo.
* **Pilot Information** (`drone_pilot_info`, text, required): Enter any additional information about yourself as a pilot.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your verification.
