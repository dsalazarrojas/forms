# Insurance Verification Daily Report - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to verify and update insurance information on a daily basis, ensuring accuracy and preventing duplication.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in all required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2.  Make sure to enter information in the correct format and format.
3.  Check for any optional fields that may be required in certain situations.
4.  Review and update any existing information as necessary.

*   **Insurance Company**: Enter the name of the insurance company. (Text, Required)
*   **Date Of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the insured. (Date, Required)
*   **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the insured. (Text, Optional)
*   **Address**: Enter the address of the insured. (Text, Optional)
*   **Insureds Name**: Enter the name of the insured. (Text, Required)
*   **Insureds Date Of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the insured. (Date, Required)
*   **User Input 4**: Enter any additional information about the insured. (Text, Required)
*   **Date Of Policy**: Enter the start date of the policy. (Date, Required)
*   **Prophets**: Select whether the insurance policy has prophets. (Select Multiple, Optional)
*   **Goals**: Select whether the insured has goals. (Select Multiple, Optional)
*   **Insurance Type**: Select the type of insurance. (Select One, Required)
*   **Insureds Address**: Enter the address of the insured. (Text, Required)
*   **Insureds Zip**: Enter the zip code of the insured. (Text, Required)
*   **Insurance Policy Number**: Enter the policy number. (Text, Required)
*   **Date Received**: Enter the date the information was received. (Date, Required)
*   **Date Updated**: Enter the last update date. (Date, Optional)
*   **Insureds Phone**: Enter the phone number of the insured. (Text, Optional)
*   **Insurance Agent**: Enter the insurance agent's name. (Text, Required)
*   **Date Renewal**: Enter the renewal date. (Date, Required)
