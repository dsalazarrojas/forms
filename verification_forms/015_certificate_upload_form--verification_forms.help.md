# Certificate Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Certificate Upload Form is designed to collect information and upload certificates for verification and validation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in all required fields, including upload date, uploader name, email, phone number, organization name, certificate category, certificate name, issuing organization, issue date, and certificate file upload.
2. Choose the correct format and size of the uploaded file.
3. Specify the purpose of the upload and the status of the certificate.
4. Confirm that you authorize the verification of the certificate and that it is authentic and accurate.
5. Add any additional notes about the certificate if necessary.
6. Finally, sign and complete the form by typing your full name as signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload Date** (upload_date): Date of certificate upload. Please enter a date in the format `yyyy-mm-dd`.
* **Uploader Name** (uploader_name): Full name of person uploading certificate.
* **Email Address** (uploader_email): Email for confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (uploader_phone): Contact phone number. (Optional)
* **Organization Name** (organization_name): Name of your organization.
* **Department** (department): Your department or division. (Optional)
* **Certificate Category** (certificate_category): Type of certificate being uploaded. Please choose from the following options:
	+ Professional certification
	+ Educational degree
	+ Training completion
	+ License
	+ Accreditation
	+ Award or recognition
	+ Other
* **Certificate Name** (certificate_name): Official name of the certificate.
* **Issuing Organization** (issuing_organization): Organization that issued the certificate.
* **Issue Date** (issue_date): Date the certificate was issued.
* **Expiration Date** (expiration_date): Date the certificate expires if applicable. (Optional)
* **Certificate Number** (certificate_number): Reference or identification number. (Optional)
* **Credential Level** (credential_level): Level or tier of the credential. Please choose from the following options:
	+ Entry level
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
	+ Expert
	+ Master level
	+ Not applicable
* **Certificate File Upload** (certificate_file): Upload PDF or image of certificate.
* **File Format** (file_format): Format of uploaded file. Please choose from the following options:
	+ PDF
	+ JPEG
	+ PNG
	+ TIFF
	+ Other
* **File Size** (file_size): Approximate file size.
* **Supporting Documents** (supporting_documents): Upload any additional supporting documents. (Optional)
* **Verification Status** (verification_status): Has this certificate been verified. Please choose from the following options:
	+ Pending verification
	+ Self-attested
	+ Previously verified
	+ Third-party verified
* **Purpose of Upload** (purpose_of_upload): Why are you uploading this certificate. Please choose from the following options:
	+ Employment verification
	+ Promotion consideration
	+ Compliance requirement
	+ Client requirement
	+ Personal records
	+ Other
* **Authorization Statement** (authorization_statement): I authorize the verification of this certificate. Please choose from the following options:
	+ Yes - I authorize
	+ No - I do not authorize
* **Accuracy Confirmation** (accuracy_confirmation): I confirm that this certificate is authentic and accurate. Please choose from the following options:
	+ Yes - I confirm
	+ No - I do not confirm
* **Additional Notes** (additional_notes): Any additional information about this certificate.
* **Uploader Signature** (uploader_signature): Type your full name as signature.
