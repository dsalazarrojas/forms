# Education Credentials Verification Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Credentials Verification Employment Form is designed to collect and verify an individual's education credentials for employment purposes. This form helps employers to confirm an individual's educational background and ensure that they are providing accurate information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions carefully and ensure that you understand the purpose and requirements of the form.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information about your education background, including your education provider and level of education.
3. Select the correct option for employer verification (e.g., in-person, over the phone, via email, or via mail).
4. Provide contact information for the educational institution that verified your education credentials.
5. Sign the consent form, confirming that the provided information is accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Education Credentials Verification**: This field is used to provide a brief description of your education background. Please enter a concise and accurate statement about your education provider and level of education.
- **Candidate Consent**: This field is a selection of options that confirm your understanding and agreement to verify your education credentials. Please select all options that apply.
- **Employer Confirmation**: This field is used to provide confirmation from your employer or organization that they have verified your education credentials. This is optional but recommended.
- **Education Provider Verification**: Select the correct option for the type of education provider you attended (e.g., high school, university).
- **Education Details**: This field is used to provide additional information about your education, such as your education provider's contact information.
- **Education Institution**: Provide the name and contact information of the educational institution that verified your education credentials.
- **Date of Graduation**: If applicable, provide the date you graduated from your educational institution.
- **Employer Verification**: Select the correct option for how your employer would like to verify your education credentials (e.g., in-person, over the phone, via email, or via mail).
- **Contact Information**: Provide the contact information of your educational institution, including their address, phone number, and email.

## Tips

- Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- Use a standard format for dates of graduation (e.g., month/day/year).
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your employer or educational institution for guidance.
