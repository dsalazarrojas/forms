# Application Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Application Verification Form is a tool for verifying the information provided by applicants for employment purposes. It is used to gather details about the applicant's work experience, education, and other relevant background information. This form is essential for employers to make informed decisions about candidates.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring all fields are completed.
2. Enter the verification date, which is the date you received the request to verify the applicant.
3. Provide the applicant's name as it appears on their identification documents.
4. Enter the applicant's email address for communication purposes.
5. Enter the applicant's phone number for contact.
6. Specify the position for which the applicant is being considered.
7. Choose the type of verification, which indicates what aspect of the applicant's background you are confirming (e.g., education, work experience, or skills).
8. Enter your name as the verifier.
9. Specify your job title or position.
10. Enter the name of your organization or institution.
11. Enter your professional email address.
12. Enter your contact phone number.
13. Describe the relationship between you and the applicant.
14. Choose how long you have known the applicant.
15. If applicable, enter the name of the educational institution, degree earned, graduation date, GPA or honors received, employment dates, job title held, and job responsibilities.
16. Select any skills you can verify from the provided list.
17. Rate the applicant's performance, attendance, and eligibility for rehire.
18. Provide any additional comments or details about the applicant.
19. Confirm that the information you have provided is accurate.
20. Sign your name as the verifier.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Verification Date** (`verification_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you received the verification request.
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the applicant as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Applicant Email** (`applicant_email`, `email`, required): Enter the applicant's email address for communication purposes.
* **Applicant Phone** (`applicant_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the applicant's contact phone number.
* **Position Applied** (`position_applied`, `text`, required): Specify the position for which the applicant is being considered.
* **Verification Type** (`verification_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose what aspect of the applicant's background you are confirming (e.g., education, work experience, or skills).
* **Verifier Name** (`verifier_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the verifier.
* **Verifier Title** (`verifier_title`, `text`, required): Specify your job title or position.
* **Verifier Organization** (`verifier_organization`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your organization or institution.
* **Verifier Email** (`verifier_email`, `email`, required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Verifier Phone** (`verifier_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Relationship to Applicant** (`relationship_to_applicant`, `select_one`, required): Describe your relationship to the applicant (e.g., direct supervisor, professor, or colleague).
* **Relationship Duration** (`relationship_duration`, `select_one`, required): Choose how long you have known the applicant.
* **Education Institution** (`education_institution`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the educational institution.
* **Degree Obtained** (`degree_obtained`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the degree or certification earned.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the date of graduation or completion.
* **GPA or Honors** (`gpa_or_honors`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the GPA or honors received.
* **Employment Dates** (`employment_dates`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the start and end dates of employment.
* **Job Title Held** (`job_title_held`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the position title held by the applicant.
* **Job Responsibilities** (`job_responsibilities`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the main responsibilities of the applicant.
* **Skills Verified** (`skills_verified`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any skills you can verify from the provided list.
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the applicant's performance.
* **Attendance Record** (`attendance_record`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the applicant's attendance.
* **Eligibility for Rehire** (`eligibility_for_rehire`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you would rehire or recommend the applicant.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any other verification details.
* **Verification Accuracy** (`verification_accuracy`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the information you have provided is accurate.
* **Verifier Signature** (`verifier_signature`, `text`, required): Type your full name as the signature.
