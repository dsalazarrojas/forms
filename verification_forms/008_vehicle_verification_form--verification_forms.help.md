# vehicle_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle verification form is designed to collect information about a vehicle for verification purposes. This form is used to gather essential details about a vehicle, including its registration, make, model year, status, type, mileage, insurance status, last inspection date, last mileage reading, and last servicing date.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "Vehicle Registration" field and enter the vehicle's registration information.
2. Choose the vehicle's make from the provided options in the "Make" field.
3. Enter the vehicle's model year in the "Model Year" field.
4. Select the vehicle's status from the options provided in the "Vehicle Status" field.
5. Select the vehicle's type from the options provided in the "Vehicle Type" field.
6. Enter the vehicle's mileage in the "Vehicle Mileage" field.
7. Select the vehicle's insurance status from the options provided in the "Vehicle Insurance Status" field.
8. Enter the vehicle's last inspection date in the "Vehicle Last Inspection Date" field.
9. Enter the vehicle's last mileage reading in the "Vehicle Last Mileage Reading" field.
10. Enter the vehicle's last servicing date in the "Vehicle Last Servicing Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Registration** (`vehicle_registration`, text, required): This field is used to enter the vehicle's registration information.
* **Make** (`make`, select_one, required): Choose the vehicle's make from the provided options.
* **Model Year** (`model_year`, number, required): Enter the vehicle's model year.
* **Vehicle Status** (`vehicle_status`, select_one, required): Select the vehicle's status from the provided options.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the vehicle's type from the provided options.
* **Vehicle Mileage** (`vehicle_mileage`, number, required): Enter the vehicle's mileage.
* **Vehicle Insurance Status** (`vehicle_insurance_status`, select_one, required): Select the vehicle's insurance status.
* **Vehicle Last Inspection Date** (`vehicle_last_inspection_date`, date, required): Enter the vehicle's last inspection date.
* **Vehicle Last Mileage Reading** (`vehicle_last_mileage_reading`, text, required): Enter the vehicle's last mileage reading.
* **Vehicle Last Servicing Date** (`vehicle_last_servicing_date`, date, required): Enter the vehicle's last servicing date.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* Review the form fields carefully to ensure that you have completed all required fields.
