# vehicle_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle verification form is designed to collect information about a vehicle for verification purposes. This form is used to gather essential details about a vehicle, including its registration, make, model year, status, type, mileage, insurance status, last inspection date, last mileage reading, and last servicing date.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "Vehicle Registration" field and enter the vehicle's registration information.
2. Choose the vehicle's make from the provided options in the "Make" field.
3. Enter the vehicle's model year in the "Model Year" field.
4. Select the vehicle's status from the options provided in the "Vehicle Status" field.
5. Select the vehicle's type from the options provided in the "Vehicle Type" field.
6. Enter the vehicle's mileage in the "Vehicle Mileage" field.
7. Select the vehicle's insurance status from the options provided in the "Vehicle Insurance Status" field.
8. Enter the vehicle's last inspection date in the "Vehicle Last Inspection Date" field.
9. Enter the vehicle's last mileage reading in the "Vehicle Last Mileage Reading" field.
10. Enter the vehicle's last servicing date in the "Vehicle Last Servicing Date" field.
