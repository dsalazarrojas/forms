# Education Verification Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Education Verification Form Template is used to gather accurate information about an individual's educational background. It is essential to provide truthful and up-to-date information to ensure the accuracy of verification processes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled in completely before submitting the form.
3. Review and revise as needed before finalizing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **School Attended** (`school_attended`, text, optional): List the name of the school you attended.
* **Degree Completed** (`degree_completed`, select_one, required): Select the type of degree you have completed:
	+ Associate
	+ Bachelor
	+ Master
	+ Doctoral
* **Field of Study** (`field_of_study`, text, required): Enter the specific field of study you pursued.
* **Education Field** (`education_field`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant fields of education:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, required): Enter the date you completed your degree.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your current contact phone number.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, required): Enter your current or previous employer's name.
* **Employee Position** (`employee_position`, select_one, required): Select your current or previous employee position:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Employment Duration** (`employment_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of your employment.
* **Verification Date** (`verification_date`, date, required): Enter the date this form was verified.
* **Verified By** (`verified_by`, select_multiple, required): Select who verified this information:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or explanations for this verification.
