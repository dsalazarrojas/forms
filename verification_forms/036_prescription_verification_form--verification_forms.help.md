# Prescription Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to verify a patient's prescription details. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the verification process is completed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals before filling out this form.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (patient_info)** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your name, address, and other relevant details about yourself as a patient.
* **Patient Name (patient_name)** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or medical records.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)** (3, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Number (contact_number)** (4, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number or other contact information.
* **Address (address)** (5, text, required/optional): Enter your address where you can be reached.
* **Prescription ID (presciption_id)** (6, text, required/optional): Enter the ID number of your prescription.

## Tips
* Double-check your information to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any information, contact your healthcare provider or medical professional for guidance.
* If you are experiencing technical difficulties with the form, contact our support team for assistance.
