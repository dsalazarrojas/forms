# Training Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user information for training verification. It is used to track the completion of training sessions, including the trainer's name, the date the training was completed, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields. These fields are mandatory and should be filled out as they appear on your official documents.
2. Enter your **Email** address. This should be your official email address that you use for work or training sessions.
3. Enter the **Date Completed**, which is the date you completed the training session. Use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Check the box next to **Training Completed** if the training has been completed. If not completed, the box will remain unchecked.
5. In the **Notes** field, enter any additional comments or feedback about the training session. This is an optional field.
6. For **Trained By**, select all trainers who have trained you. This field is optional.
7. Enter the **Date Trained**, if applicable. If you were trained on a specific date, enter that date in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
8. For **Verified By**, select all individuals who have verified your training. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required):
	+ This field is for entering your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required):
	+ This field is for entering your last name.
* **Email** (email, required):
	+ This field is for entering your official email address.
* **Date Completed** (date, required):
	+ Enter the date you completed the training session in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Training Completed** (select_one, optional):
	+ Check this box if the training has been completed.
* **Notes** (note, optional):
	+ Enter any additional comments or feedback about the training session.
* **Trained By** (select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all trainers who have trained you.
* **Date Trained** (date, optional):
	+ If applicable, enter the date you were trained in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Verified By** (select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all individuals who have verified your training.
