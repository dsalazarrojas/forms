# Fresh Product Verification Scheme Application - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used for the Fresh Product Verification Scheme (FPVS) application. The FPVS is a process to verify the authenticity and safety of fresh products. It involves collecting and evaluating various data points from suppliers to ensure that their products meet the required standards.

Before starting the application, please note that this form is not intended for duplicate submissions. If you have previously submitted this form for the same product, you should not submit it again. If you are unsure whether you have previously submitted this form, please contact our support team for assistance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct product category.
2. Enter the Product ID, Product Name, and Supplier ID of your fresh product.
3. Choose the Supplier Name and Supplier Email for the supplier of your product.
4. Provide the Supplier Phone number, if available.
5. Upload any additional documentation relevant to your product (e.g., certification documents).
6. Select the Submission Date for the form.
7. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product ID (Number)**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to your product by the supplier or manufacturer.
* **Product Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the fresh product as it appears on the packaging or labeling.
* **Product Category (Select One)**: Choose the category from the options provided (Fresh Fruits, Fresh Vegetables, or Processed Food) that best fits your product's type.
* **Supplier ID (Number)**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to your supplier or supplier's ID number.
* **Supplier Name (Text)**: Enter the name of your supplier or company name.
* **Supplier Email (Email)**: Enter the email address of your supplier or contact person.
* **Supplier Phone (Text)**: Enter the phone number of your supplier or contact person.
* **Product Documentation (Text)**: Upload any additional documentation or certification related to your product, such as certifications or compliance records.
* **Submission Date (Date)**: Choose the date when you are submitting this form.
