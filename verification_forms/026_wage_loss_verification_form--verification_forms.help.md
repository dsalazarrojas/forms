# wage_loss_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify wage loss due to injury or illness. It's an essential tool for gathering information about an employee's wage loss and ensuring accurate reports.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2. Enter the employee's **Injured Date**, which is the date of the incident.
3. Provide the **Work Location** where the employee was working at the time of the incident.
4. Enter the **Employer Name**, which is the name of the employee's supervisor or manager.
5. Enter the **Employer Phone**, which is a contact number for the employer.
6. Enter the **Employer Email**, which is a contact email for the employer.
7. If applicable, enter the **Wages Lost**, which is the total wages lost due to the incident.
8. Enter the **Wages Per Week**, which is the weekly wage rate of the employee.
9. Enter the number of **Weeks Missed** due to the incident.
10. Enter the **Total Wages Lost**.
11. Select the appropriate **Wage Rate** for the incident, which can be either **Good** or **Fair**.
12. If necessary, enter an **Other Reason** for the wage loss.
13. Provide any additional **Comments** about the incident.
14. Indicate if the form was **Submitted By** the employee or someone else.
15. Enter the date the form was **Submitted On**.
16. Indicate if the form was **Verified By** the supervisor or manager.
17. Enter the date the form was **Verified On**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Injured Date**: The date of the incident that caused the wage loss.
  - **Injured Date** (date): Enter the date of the incident that caused the wage loss.
- **Work Location**: The location where the employee was working at the time of the incident.
  - **Work Location** (text): Enter the location where the employee was working.
- **Employer Name**: The name of the employee's supervisor or manager.
  - **Employer Name** (text): Enter the name of the employee's supervisor or manager.
- **Employer Phone**: The contact number for the employer.
  - **Employer Phone** (text): Enter the contact number for the employer.
- **Employer Email**: The contact email for the employer.
  - **Employer Email** (email): Enter the contact email for the employer.
- **Wages Lost**: The total wages lost due to the incident (if applicable).
  - **Wages Lost** (number): Enter the total wages lost due to the incident.
- **Wages Per Week**: The weekly wage rate of the employee.
  - **Wages Per Week** (number): Enter the weekly wage rate of the employee.
- **Weeks Missed**: The number of weeks missed due to the incident.
  - **Weeks Missed** (number): Enter the number of weeks missed due to the incident.
- **Total Wages Lost**: The total wages lost due to the incident.
  - **Total Wages Lost** (number): Enter the total wages lost due to the incident.
- **Wage Rate**: Select the appropriate wage rate for the incident (Good or Fair).
  - **Wage Rate** (select multiple): Select the appropriate wage rate for the incident.
- **Other Reason**: If necessary, enter an other reason for the wage loss.
  - **Other Reason** (text): If necessary, enter an other reason for the wage loss.
- **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the incident.
  - **Comments** (note): Provide any additional comments about the incident.
- **Submitted By**: Indicate if the form was submitted by the employee or someone else.
  - **Submitted By** (select one): Indicate if the form was submitted by the employee or someone else.
- **Submitted On**: The date the form was submitted.
  - **Submitted On** (date): Enter the date the form was submitted.
- **Verified By**: Indicate if the form was verified by the supervisor or manager.
  - **Verified By** (select one): Indicate if the form was verified by the supervisor or manager.
- **Verified On**: The date the form was verified.
  - **Verified On** (date): Enter the date the form was verified.
