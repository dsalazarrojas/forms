# Driver Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Verification Form is used to collect information about your driver's license status, vehicle information, and personal details. This form is designed to help administrators or authorities verify your identity and check your eligibility for certain services or programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you have all the required information.
2. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the "License" field if you have a valid driver's license.
3. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter your Social Security Number in the "Social Security Number" field.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Enter your Vehicle License Plate number in the "Vehicle License Plate" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **License** (`Yes', 'No'`): Select 'Yes' if you have a valid driver's license. Select 'No' if you do not have a valid driver's license.
* **Address** (`text`): Enter your current address.
* **Social Security Number** (`text`): Enter your Social Security Number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date`): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Vehicle License Plate** (`text`): Enter your vehicle's license plate number.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy to avoid any delays or errors in the verification process.
