# Car Insurance Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to verify information about a car insurance policy. It is used to gather necessary details to confirm the authenticity of an insurance policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the client information section with your full name and email address.
2. Enter your phone number as the primary contact.
3. Select the insurance provider name and enter the insurance policy number.
4. Choose the policy's effective and expiration dates.
5. Describe your vehicle make and model, including the year of manufacture and 17-character Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
6. Indicate whether the coverage is active or pending.
7. Confirm that you have read and understand the insurance verification terms.
8. Type your name for insurance verification confirmation.
9. Provide any additional comments or requests related to the verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Full Name** (Client Full Name, text, required): Enter your full name, including your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your email address for verification status updates.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Insurance Provider Name** (Insurance Provider Name, text, required): Enter the name of your insurance provider (e.g., GEICO, State Farm, Allstate).
* **Insurance Policy Number** (Insurance Policy Number, text, required): Enter your policy ID number.
* **Policy Effective Date** (Policy Effective Date, date, required): Select the date when the policy started.
* **Policy Expiration Date** (Policy Expiration Date, date, required): Select the date when the policy expires.
* **Vehicle Make and Model** (Vehicle Make and Model, text, required): Describe your vehicle make and model.
* **Vehicle Year** (Vehicle Year, number, required): Enter the year of manufacture for your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)** (Vehicle Identification Number (VIN), text, required): Enter the 17-character VIN code for your vehicle.
* **Is the coverage active?** (Is the coverage active?, select_one, required): Choose 'True' if the coverage is active, 'False' if not, or 'Pending' for uncertain status.
* **Insurance Verification Acknowledgement** (Insurance Verification Acknowledgement, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and understand the insurance verification terms.
* **Type your name for insurance verification confirmation** (Type your name for insurance verification confirmation, text, required): Type your name for confirmation.
* **Any additional comments or requests?** (Any additional comments or requests?, text, optional): Provide any additional details or requests related to the verification.
