# Parental Verification For Services - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information from a parent or guardian regarding their child's participation in services and to obtain their consent for the services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the school or service that the child is participating in.
2. Enter the guardian's name.
3. Select the relationship between the guardian and the child (e.g., Mother, Father, Guardian 1, etc.).
4. Enter the contact number of the guardian.
5. Enter the email of the guardian.
6. Enter the address of the guardian.
7. Select the consent date.
8. Select the status of the verification process (Approved, Declined, Pending).
9. Enter any additional comments from the guardian or school admin.
10. Enter the school admin's comments.
11. Select whether the guardian or school admin has signed the form.
12. Enter the consent date and time.
13. Select whether the school admin has signed the form.
14. Enter any additional notes from the guardian or school admin.
15. Enter the verification date.
16. Select the status of the verification process (Approved, Declined, Pending).
17. Enter any additional notes from the school admin.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Input** (`1`, `number`, required): Enter the ID number of the school or service that the child is participating in.
* **Guardian Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the guardian.
* **Guardian Relationship** (`3`, `select_one`, required): Select the relationship between the guardian and the child (e.g., Mother, Father, Guardian 1, etc.).
* **Contact Number** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number of the guardian.
* **E-mail** (`5`, `email`, required): Enter the email of the guardian.
* **Address** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the guardian.
* **Consent Date** (`7`, `date`, required): Select the date of the consent.
* **School Admin** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of school admin who will be handling the verification (School, Clinic, Youth Program).
* **Consent Status** (`9`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the consent (Approved, Declined, Pending).
* **Additional Comments** (`10`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments from the guardian or school admin.
* **School Admin Comments** (`11`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments from the school admin.
* **Guardian Signature** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether the guardian has signed the form.
* **School Admin Signature** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether the school admin has signed the form.
* **Consent Date and Time** (`14`, `date`, required): Enter the consent date and time.
* **School Signature** (`15`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether the school admin has signed the form.
* **Consent Status Note** (`16`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes from the guardian or school admin.
* **School Admin Note** (`17`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes from the school admin.
* **Verification Date** (`18`, `date`, required): Enter the verification date.
* **School Verification Status** (`19`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the verification process (Approved, Declined, Pending).
* **Consent Date and Time Note** (`20`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes from the guardian or school admin.
* **School Admin Status Note** (`21`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes from the school admin.
* **Guardian Name** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the guardian (if necessary).
* **School Admin Name** (`23`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the school admin (if necessary).
* **Verification Status** (`24`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the verification process (Approved, Declined, Pending).
* **Verification Note** (`25`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes from the guardian or school admin.
