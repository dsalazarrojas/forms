# Asset Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Asset Verification Form is designed for claimants to provide detailed information about the asset they are submitting a claim for. The form will help ensure that the insurance company has accurate and complete information to assess the claim.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the required fields marked as "Required" in the form.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Use the options provided in the select fields to select the most relevant option.
4. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the insurance company for guidance.
5. Once you have completed the form, review it carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Insurance Company Name** (`insurance_company`, text, required): Please enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, required): Enter the policy number associated with your insurance policy.
* **Verification Date** (`verification_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you verified the asset.
* **Claimant First Name** (`claimant_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Claimant Last Name** (`claimant_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`claimant_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`claimant_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Claim Number** (`claim_number`, text, required): Enter the insurance claim reference number.
* **Asset Description** (`asset_description`, text, required): Enter a complete description of the asset.
* **Asset Category** (`asset_category`, select_one, required): Select the type of asset (Jewelry, Electronics, Art and Collectibles, Furniture, Vehicles, Home Equipment, Sports Equipment, Other).
* **Brand or Manufacturer** (`brand_manufacturer`, text, optional): Enter the brand or manufacturer of the asset (if known).
* **Model or Serial Number** (`model_number`, text, optional): Enter the model or serial number of the asset (if known).
* **Year of Purchase** (`year_of_purchase`, number, required): Enter the year the asset was purchased.
* **Original Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, number, required): Enter the amount paid for the asset originally.
* **Current Condition** (`current_condition`, select_one, required): Select the current condition of the asset (Like New, Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Damaged).
* **Current Estimated Value** (`current_estimated_value`, number, required): Enter the estimated value of the asset now.
* **Where Is Asset Located?** (`where_asset_located`, text, required): Enter where the asset is currently located (e.g., home, office, storage).
* **Maintenance and Care** (`maintenance_condition`, select_one, required): Select how the asset has been maintained (Excellent Maintenance, Good Maintenance, Fair Maintenance, Poor Maintenance, No Maintenance).
* **Any Damage or Loss History?** (`asset_damage_history`, text, optional): If you have a history of damage or repairs, enter it here.
* **Photos of Asset Attached?** (`photos_attached`, select_one, required): Select whether photos of the asset are attached.
* **Supporting Documentation Provided** (`documentation_provided`, text, optional): If you have supporting documentation (e.g., receipt, appraisal), enter it here.
* **Professional Appraisal Available?** (`appraisal_document`, select_one, required): Select whether a professional appraisal is available.
* **I verify that the information above is accurate and complete** (`accuracy_verification`, select_one, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
* **Claimant Signature** (`claimant_signature`, text, required): Sign your name as the claimant.
* **Form Submission Date** (`form_submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date the form is completed.

Note: This guide assumes that the reader is familiar with the basics of insurance claims and assets. If you are not familiar, please seek guidance from the insurance company or consult an expert.
