# Product Origin Disclosure Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Origin Disclosure Registration Form is designed to help product manufacturers and suppliers provide accurate and detailed information about their products' origin. This form is used to register a product's origin and obtain a unique registration ID, which can be used for various purposes such as labeling, packaging, and marketing materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of your product.
2. Enter your product's name.
3. Provide a brief description of your product in 100 words.
4. Upload an image of your product.
5. Choose any applicable certifications for your product.
6. Provide any additional information about your product.
7. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Product Origin (id: 1)
* **Product Origin** (note): This field is a note field where you can provide a brief description of your product's origin.

### Product Category (id: 2)
* **Product Category** (select_one): Select the category of your product from the list below:
	+ Food
	+ Non-Food
	+ Pet Food
	+ Beauty and Personal Care
	+ Pharmaceutical
	+ Cosmetic
	+ Baby Care

### Product Name (id: 3)
* **Product Name** (text): Enter the name of your product.

### Country of Origin (id: 4)
* **Country of Origin** (select_multiple): Select all the countries where your product is made or manufactured:
	+ USA
	+ Canada
	+ Mexico
	+ China
	+ Japan
	+ South Korea
	+ United Kingdom
	+ Australia
	+ Germany
	+ France

### Product Description (id: 5)
* **Product Description** (note): Provide a brief description of your product in 100 words.

### Product Image (id: 6)
* **Product Image** (text): Upload an image of your product.

### Origin Certifications (id: 7)
* **Origin Certifications** (select_multiple): Select all the applicable certifications for your product:
	+ GMO-Free
	+ Halal
	+ Kosher
	+ Non-Gmo

### Additional Info (id: 8)
* **Additional Info** (text): Provide any additional information about your product.

### Submit (id: 9)
* **Submit** (text): Click this button to complete and submit your form.

## Tips
* Please ensure you are filling out the correct product information to avoid duplication and inaccuracies.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
