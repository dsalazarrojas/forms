# treatment_site_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from medical organizations for the purpose of verifying treatment sites. It helps to gather accurate data about patients and their treatment visits.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by entering the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Provide the patient's date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.
3. Enter the name of the treatment site in the "Treatment Site Name" field.
4. Enter the name of the medical organization in the "Medical Organization" field.
5. Enter the patient's record ID in the "Patient Record ID" field.
6. Enter the date of the patient's visit in the "Date Of Visit" field.
7. Enter the time of the patient's visit in the "Time Of Visit" field.
8. Enter the contact person's name in the "Contact Person" field.
9. Enter the contact person's title in the "Contact Person Title" field.
10. Enter the contact person's email in the "Contact Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their record.
* **Date Of Birth** (`Date Of Birth`, date, required/optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Treatment Site Name** (`Treatment Site Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the treatment site where the patient received treatment.
* **Medical Organization** (`Medical Organization`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the medical organization where the patient received treatment.
* **Patient Record ID** (`Patient Record ID`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's record ID, if available.
* **Date Of Visit** (`Date Of Visit`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the patient's visit.
* **Time Of Visit** (`Time Of Visit`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the patient's visit.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the patient's treatment.
* **Contact Person Title** (`Contact Person Title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the contact person responsible for the patient's treatment.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email of the contact person responsible for the patient's treatment.
