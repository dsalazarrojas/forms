# Dental Insurance Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Insurance Verification Form is used to collect and verify information about a patient's insurance policy, ensuring accurate coverage and eligibility for dental services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

*   Fill in the required fields with the patient's information.
*   Ensure you have the patient's consent to access their insurance information and that you are authorized to collect and verify this data.
*   Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Patient Name**
*   The Patient Name field is where you enter the patient's name.

### **Insurance Provider**
*   The Insurance Provider field is for entering the name of the insurance company or plan.

### **Group Plan**
*   The Group Plan field is for entering the name of the plan or group the patient is covered by.

### **Policy Number**
*   The Policy Number field is for entering the patient's policy number.

### **Member Name**
*   The Member Name field is for entering the name of the policy holder or member.

### **Member Birthday**
*   The Member Birthday field is for entering the birthday of the policy holder or member.

### **Phone**
*   The Phone field is for entering the contact number of the policy holder or member.

### **Email**
*   The Email field is for entering the email address of the policy holder or member.

### **Address**
*   The Address field is for entering the address of the policy holder or member.

### **Insurance ID**
*   The Insurance ID field is for entering any additional or specific identification number associated with the policy.

### **Tips**

*   Ensure you have the patient's consent before filling out this form.
*   Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you're unsure about any of the fields, consult with a supervisor or colleague to ensure you're collecting the correct information.
