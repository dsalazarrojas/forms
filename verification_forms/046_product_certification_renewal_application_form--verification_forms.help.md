# Product Certification Renewal Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information necessary for product certification renewal. This form is used by product manufacturers to renew their product certifications, ensuring that their products continue to meet the required standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about the product and the submitter.
2. Ensure that the product's certification status is current, and provide a valid reason for renewal if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Id** (`product_id`, text, required): Please enter the unique identifier for your product.
* **Certification Date** (`cert_date`, date, optional): If the product's certification is not current, please enter the date when it was last certified.
* **Certificate Number** (`cert_number`, text, required): Enter the number assigned to the product's certification.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product being certified.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company that manufactured the product.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person for the product.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the company that manufactured the product.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the physical address of the company that manufactured the product.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where the company is located.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if the product is manufactured in the United States.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the company's location.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if the product is manufactured in the United States.
* **Certificate Type** (`cert_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of certification for the product (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the product's certification will expire.
* **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the product's certification will be renewed.
* **Reason for Renewal** (`reason`, select_one, optional): Provide a valid reason for renewing the product's certification.
* **Product Category** (`product_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the product (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Certification Level** (`certification_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of certification for the product (e.g., "High" or "Low").
* **Status** (`cert_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the product's certification (e.g., "Active" or "Inactive").
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or remarks about the product's certification.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the renewal application.
* **Submitter E-mail** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person submitting the renewal application.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the renewal application.
