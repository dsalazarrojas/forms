# Existence Evidence Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Existence Evidence Assessment" form is designed to collect and verify evidence of an entity's existence. This form helps to gather essential information about the entity, including its name, type of evidence, quality of the evidence, rating, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter Entity Name**: Enter the name of the entity you are submitting evidence for. This field is required.
2. **Select Evidence Type**: Choose the type of evidence you are submitting, such as Photo, Video, Document, or Audio. This field is required.
3. **Rate Evidence Quality**: Rate the quality of the evidence on a scale of 1-4, with 1 being Poor and 4 being Excellent. This field is optional.
4. **Evidence Rating**: Select the rating for the evidence from the options provided, such as Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent. This field is optional.
5. **Date Collected**: Enter the date when you collected the evidence. This field is optional.
6. **Collected By**: Enter the name of the person who collected the evidence. This field is optional.
7. **Note**: Enter any additional notes about the evidence. This field is optional.
8. **Email**: Enter the contact email of the person who collected the evidence. This field is optional.
9. **Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the person who collected the evidence. This field is optional.
10. **Entity Description**: Enter a brief description of the entity. This field is optional.
11. **Entity Status**: Select the status of the entity, such as Active, Inactive, or Not Active. This field is optional.
12. **Form Submitted**: Confirm that the form has been submitted by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'. This field is optional.
13. **Time Collected**: Enter the time when you collected the evidence. This field is optional.
14. **Location**: Enter the location where the evidence was collected. This field is optional.
15. **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form. This field is optional.
16. **Submitted By Email**: Enter the email of the person who submitted the form. This field is optional.
17. **Submitted By Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form. This field is optional.
