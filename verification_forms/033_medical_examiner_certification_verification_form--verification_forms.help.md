# Medical Examiner Certification Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Examiner Certification Verification Form is used for verifying the certification of medical examiners. This form is a crucial document that helps ensure the credibility and legitimacy of medical examiners' certifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User Name**: This is the name of the person filling out the form.
2. Enter the **Medical Examiner Name**: This is the name of the medical examiner whose certification is being verified.
3. Enter the **Certification Number**: This is the number assigned to the medical examiner's certification.
4. Enter the **Date of Certification**: This is the date when the medical examiner was certified.
5. Select the **Verification Method**: Choose "Yes" if you are certain about the certification, or "No" if you need to verify further.
6. Select the **Verification Status**: Choose "Active" if the certification is current, or "Inactive" if it is not.
7. Enter any **Comments**: If you need to add additional information about the certification, use this field.
8. Enter the **Email Address** of the medical examiner (if applicable).
9. Enter the **Phone Number** of the medical examiner (if applicable).
10. Enter the **Medical Institution** where the medical examiner is affiliated (if applicable).
11. Enter the **Medical License Number** of the medical examiner (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (Text, Optional):
	+ This is the name of the person filling out the form.
* **Medical Examiner Name** (Text, Optional):
	+ This is the name of the medical examiner whose certification is being verified.
* **Certification Number** (Text, Optional):
	+ This is the number assigned to the medical examiner's certification.
* **Date of Certification** (Date, Optional):
	+ This is the date when the medical examiner was certified.
* **Verification Method** (Select One, Optional):
	+ Choose "Yes" if you are certain about the certification, or "No" if you need to verify further.
* **Verification Status** (Select One, Optional):
	+ Choose "Active" if the certification is current, or "Inactive" if it is not.
* **Comments** (Note, Optional):
	+ Add any additional information about the certification if needed.
* **Email Address** (Email, Optional):
	+ Enter the email address of the medical examiner (if applicable).
* **Phone Number** (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter the phone number of the medical examiner (if applicable).
* **Medical Institution** (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter the medical institution where the medical examiner is affiliated (if applicable).
* **Medical License Number** (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter the medical license number of the medical examiner (if applicable).
