# Land Title Verification Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Land Title Verification Program Registration Form is designed to collect information from property owners or applicants to verify the ownership of properties. This form helps the program to verify the accuracy of property ownership, ensuring that the records are up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the General Information page with your basic details, such as your name and contact information.
2. Provide the Applicant Details page with the required information about the property owner or applicant.
3. Enter the Contact Information page with the preferred contact method.
4. On the Property Verification page, provide information about the property you want to verify, including whether it's located within the county or not.
5. Finally, select the Property Type from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (Page 1)** (`text`, required): Fill in your name and contact information to get in touch with you for any updates.
* **Applicant Details (Page 2)** (`text`, required): Provide the details of the property owner or applicant, including their name and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Information (Page 3)** (`text`, optional): Enter your preferred email address or phone number for the program to contact you.
* **Property Verification (Page 4)** (`text`, optional): Describe the property you want to verify, including any distinguishing features or locations.
* **Property Type (Page 5)** (`select_one`, optional): Select the type of property you want to verify, such as residential or commercial.
* **Property Location (Page 5)** (`note`, optional): Note any specific location details, such as the county or city, where the property is located.
* **County (Page 5)** (`number`, optional): Enter the county number for the location of the property.
* **City (Page 6)** (`text`, optional): Enter the city where the property is located.
* **Zip Code (Page 7)** (`text`, optional): Enter the zip code of the property.
* **State (Page 8)** (`text`, optional): Enter the state where the property is located.
* **Contact Preferred Email (Page 9)** (`email`, optional): Enter your preferred email address for the program to contact you.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately to avoid any errors or delays in the verification process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us through the contact information provided above.
* The program will verify your property information as per the provided details and may contact you for any further clarification or updates.
* This form is intended for one-time submissions. If you have any additional properties to verify, please complete the form again with the relevant information.
