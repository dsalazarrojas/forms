# Reference Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a reference from the reference institution on behalf of the student. To avoid duplication of work, please only submit one reference request per student. Please ensure that you have obtained the required clearances from the reference institution before submitting this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Select the relevant sections
Fill in the relevant sections as required. For example, if you are requesting a reference from a university, fill in the university name, address, city, state, and country.

### Step 2: Enter the required information
Enter the required information for each section, making sure to be as accurate as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reference Order Request Form** (`reference_order_request_form`): This is the title of the form.
* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`): Enter your delivery preferences for the reference request, if any.
* **Clear Authorization** (`clear_authorization`): Enter the clearance information from the reference institution.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`): Enter the delivery address of the reference institution.
* **Delivery City** (`delivery_city`): Enter the city of the delivery address.
* **Delivery State** (`delivery_state`): Enter the state of the delivery address.
* **Delivery Country** (`delivery_country`): Enter the country of the delivery address.
* **Delivery Phone** (`delivery_phone`): Enter the phone number of the delivery address.
* **Delivery Email** (`delivery_email`): Enter the email of the delivery address.
* **Delivery Notes** (`delivery_notes`): Enter any additional delivery notes, if required.
* **Reference Institution** (`reference_institution`): Enter the name of the reference institution.
* **Reference Department** (`reference_department`): Enter the name of the department of the reference institution.
* **Reference Title** (`reference_title`): Enter the title of the reference person.
* **Clear Institution** (`clear_institution`): Enter the name of the institution for clearances.
* **Clear Department** (`clear_department`): Enter the name of the department for clearances.
* **Clear Title** (`clear_title`): Enter the title of the clearances person.
* **University Name** (`university_name`): Enter the name of the university.
* **University Address** (`university_address`): Enter the address of the university.
* **University City** (`university_city`): Enter the city of the university.
* **University State** (`university_state`): Enter the state of the university.
* **University Country** (`university_country`): Enter the country of the university.
* **Committee** (`committee`): Enter the name of the committee.
* **Research Center** (`research_center`): Enter the name of the research center.
* **Other** (`other`): Enter any other information, if required.
* **Other Comments** (`other_comments`): Enter any other comments, if required.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled in correctly and accurately. If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant institution or person before submitting the form.
