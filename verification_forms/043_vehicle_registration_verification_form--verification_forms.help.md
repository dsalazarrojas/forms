# Vehicle Registration Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to verify information related to vehicle registration. It collects information about a vehicle's registration status, owner details, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the vehicle's registration number.
2. Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
3. Select the current status of the vehicle's plate.
4. Enter the owner's name.
5. Enter the owner's contact information (phone number and email).
6. Select the registration status of the vehicle.
7. Enter the registration date.
8. Enter the vehicle's registration number.
9. Enter the vehicle's make, model, and year.
10. Enter the vehicle's color.
11. Select the type of vehicle.
12. Enter the plate expiration date.
13. Select the status of the vehicle's plate.
14. Provide the owner's signature.
15. Add any additional notes or comments (optional).
16. Select the verification result.
