# Marriage Certificate Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Marriage Certificate Verification Form is used to verify the authenticity of a marriage certificate. This form is designed to ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand the fields and their requirements.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Enter the correct Marriage Certificate Number to ensure verification.
4. Choose the correct Verification Status from the options provided.
5. Add any additional remarks or comments if necessary.
6. Click the "Submit Verification Form" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, select_multiple, not required): Choose whether you are verifying a marriage certificate for official records or legal proceedings.
* **Marriage Certificate Number** (`marriage_certificate_number`, text, required): Enter the unique identifier of the marriage certificate.
* **Verification Date** (`verification_date`, date, required): Enter the date of verification.
* **Verification Officer** (`verification_officer`, text, not required): Enter the name and title of the person verifying the marriage certificate.
* **Verification Status** (`verification_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the marriage certificate (Active or Inactive).
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, note, not required): Add any additional comments or remarks about the verification process.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments or observations about the verification process.
* **Submit Verification Form** (`submit_verification_form`, text, not required): This button submits the form for verification.

## Tips

* Ensure you have the correct authorization and information before filling out this form.
* Double-check the information you enter to avoid any errors or delays.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or contact the designated authority for assistance.
