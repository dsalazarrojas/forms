# # University Clinical Field Experience Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Clinical Field Experience Verification Form is designed to gather information from students about their clinical field experience. This form is a crucial part of verifying a student's clinical field experience in a university setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name as the student in the **student_name** field.
2. Enter your supervisor's name in the **supervisor_name** field.
3. Provide your supervisor's title in the **supervisor_title** field.
4. Specify the name of the field you experienced in the **field_name** field.
5. Enter the name of the university where you gained this experience in the **university_name** field.
6. Identify the clinical site where you gained this experience in the **clinical_site** field.
7. Enter the start date of your experience in the **start_date** field.
8. Enter the end date of your experience in the **end_date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_name** (Text, Optional): This field is used to capture your name as the student.
* **supervisor_name** (Text, Optional): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **supervisor_title** (Text, Optional): Enter your supervisor's title or position.
* **field_name** (Text, Optional): Specify the name of the field you experienced in the university setting.
* **university_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the university where you gained this experience.
* **clinical_site** (Text, Optional): Identify the clinical site where you gained this experience.
* **start_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the start date of your clinical experience.
* **end_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the end date of your clinical experience.

## Tips
* Double-check your details to ensure accuracy.
