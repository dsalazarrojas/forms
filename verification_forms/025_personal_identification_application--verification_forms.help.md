# Personal Identification Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Identification Application form is used for personal identification purposes. This form collects information about the applicant's personal details, including their name, contact information, and identification details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
5. Type in your Phone Number in the Phone Number field.
6. Enter your Address in the Address field.
7. Enter your Passport ID in the Passport ID field.
8. Select your Country of Birth from the list of available options in the Country of Birth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (First Name, text, optional):** Enter your given first name.
* **Last Name (Last Name, text, optional):** Enter your given last name.
* **Email (Email, email, optional):** Enter your valid email address.
* **Date of Birth (Date of Birth, date, optional):** Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number, text, optional):** Enter your phone number.
* **Address (Address, text, optional):** Enter your current address.
* **Passport ID (Passport ID, text, optional):** Enter your passport ID number.
* **Country of Birth (Country of Birth, select_one, optional):** Select your country of birth from the list of available options.
