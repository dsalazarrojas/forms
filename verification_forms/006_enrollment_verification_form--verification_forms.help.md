# Enrollment Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to verify the enrollment status of a student in an institution. It collects information about the student's name, date of enrollment, student ID, name of the institution, date of completion, degree title, degree concentration, degree status, student status, student contact method, institution contact method, institution address, institution phone, institution email, and signatures from both the student and the institution. This form is intended for use by institutions to verify the enrollment status of their students.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name.
2. Enter the date of enrollment.
3. Enter the student's ID.
4. Enter the name of the institution.
5. Enter the date of completion.
6. Enter the degree title.
7. Enter the degree concentration.
8. Select the current degree status.
9. Select the student's status.
10. Choose how you would like to be contacted by the student.
11. Choose how you would like to be contacted by the institution.
12. Enter the institution's address.
13. Enter the institution's phone number.
14. Enter the institution's email address.
15. If applicable, enter the institution's fax number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (1)**: Enter the student's full name as it appears on their records.
* **Date of Enrollment (2)**: Enter the date the student enrolled in the institution.
* **Student ID (3)**: Enter the student's ID number as it appears on their records.
* **Name of Institution (4)**: Enter the name of the institution where the student is enrolled.
* **Date of Completion (5)**: Enter the date the student completed their degree or program.
* **Degree Title (6)**: Enter the title of the degree or program the student is enrolled in.
* **Degree Concentration (7)**: Enter the concentration or major of the degree or program the student is enrolled in.
* **Degree Status (8)**: Select the current status of the student's degree or program (Active, Completed, In Progress, Not Started).
* **Student Status (9)**: Select the current status of the student (Active, Active - Leave of Absence, Deceased, Withdrawn, Not Enrolled).
* **Student Contact Method (10)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted by the student (Phone, Email, Postal Mail).
* **Institution Contact Method (11)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted by the institution (Phone, Email, Postal Mail).
* **Institution Address (12)**: Enter the address of the institution.
* **Institution Phone (13)**: Enter the phone number of the institution.
* **Institution Email (14)**: Enter the email address of the institution.
* **Institution Fax (15)**: If applicable, enter the fax number of the institution.
* **Student Signature (16)**: If required, have the student sign the form to authenticate their identity. (Note: This field is not required and should only be used when necessary.)
* **Institution Signature (17)**: If required, have the institution sign the form to authenticate their identity. (Note: This field is not required and should only be used when necessary.)

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* If you are unable to verify the student's enrollment status, contact the institution or student directly.
* If you need to update any of the fields, please submit a new form and mark it as an update.
* This form is for verification purposes only and should not be used for any other purpose.
