# vehicle_information_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for collecting vehicle information, including year, make, color, type, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing each field carefully and making sure you have all the necessary information about the vehicle.
2. Enter the vehicle's year in the "Vehicle Year" field.
3. Enter the vehicle's make in the "Vehicle Make" field.
4. Enter the vehicle's color in the "Vehicle Color" field.
5. Select the vehicle type from the options provided in the "Vehicle Type" field.
6. Enter the vehicle's registration number in the "Vehicle Registration Number" field.
7. Enter the vehicle's engine size in the "Vehicle Engine Size" field.
8. Enter the vehicle's mileage in the "Vehicle Mileage" field.
9. Select the vehicle's insurance provider from the options provided in the "Vehicle Insurance Provider" field.
10. Select the vehicle's loans provider from the options provided in the "Vehicle Loans Provider" field.
11. Select the vehicle's registration status from the options provided in the "Vehicle Registration Status" field.
12. Choose the type of report from the options provided in the "Report Type" field.
13. Enter the date of the report in the "Report Date" field.
14. Enter the time of the report in the "Report Time" field.
15. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Vehicle Year** (number, required): Enter the year of the vehicle.
- **Vehicle Make** (text, optional): Enter the make of the vehicle.
- **Vehicle Model** (text, optional): Enter the model of the vehicle.
- **Vehicle Color** (text, optional): Enter the color of the vehicle.
- **Vehicle Type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of vehicle (SUV, Truck, Van, Car).
- **Vehicle Registration Number** (text, optional): Enter the vehicle's registration number.
- **Vehicle Engine Size** (number, optional): Enter the engine size of the vehicle.
- **Vehicle Mileage** (number, optional): Enter the mileage of the vehicle.
- **Vehicle Insurance Provider** (select_multiple, optional): Select the provider of the vehicle's insurance (State Farm, Geico, Progressive, None).
- **Vehicle Loans Provider** (select_multiple, optional): Select the provider of the vehicle's loans (Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Chase, None).
- **Vehicle Registration Status** (select_one, optional): Select the status of the vehicle's registration (Registered, Unregistered, Pending).
- **Report Type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of report (ประก, Insurance Report, Registration Report, Loan Report).
- **Report Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the report.
- **Report Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the report.
- **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the vehicle.
