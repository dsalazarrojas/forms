# Dependent Details Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for collecting details about a dependent in a benefits program or insurance claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Fill out all the required fields (fields marked as `required: true`) with accurate and complete information about the dependent.
- For fields with options (select, select_multiple, or date), choose the correct option from the dropdown list or enter the date in the correct format (e.g., `YYYY-MM-DD`).
- If a field has a hint or placeholder text, use that as a guide to ensure you fill out the field correctly.
- Review and verify all information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Dependent Name
* **dependent_name** (`dependent_name`, text, optional): Enter the dependent's full name as it appears on their government-issued ID or passport.

### Date of Birth
* **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the dependent's date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### Relationship Status
* **relationship_status** (`relationship_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the dependent's relationship with you or the claimant. This field allows you to choose one or multiple status options.

### Dependent Document
* **dependent_document** (`dependent_document`, note, optional): This field is for any relevant government-issued ID or document issued to the dependent.

### Dependent Address
* **dependent_address** (`dependent_address`, text, optional): Enter the dependent's address as it appears on their government-issued ID or passport.

### Dependent Phone
* **dependent_phone** (`dependent_phone`, text, optional): Enter the dependent's phone number as it appears on their government-issued ID or passport.

### Dependent Email
* **dependent_email** (`dependent_email`, email, optional): Enter the dependent's email address as it appears on their government-issued ID or passport.

### Employer Document
* **employer_document** (`employer_document`, note, optional): This field is for any relevant government-issued ID or document issued by the dependent's employer.

### Employer Name
* **employer_name** (`employer_name`, text, optional): Enter the employer's name as it appears on their government-issued ID or passport.

### Employer Address
* **employer_address** (`employer_address`, text, optional): Enter the employer's address as it appears on their government-issued ID or passport.

### Employer Phone
* **employer_phone** (`employer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the employer's phone number as it appears on their government-issued ID or passport.

### Employer Email
* **employer_email** (`employer_email`, text, optional): Enter the employer's email address as it appears on their government-issued ID or passport.

### Employee Number
* **employee_number** (`employee_number`, text, optional): Enter the dependent's employee ID or number issued by their employer.

### Manager Name
* **manager_name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the manager's name as it appears on their government-issued ID or passport.

### Manager Title
* **manager_title** (`manager_title`, text, optional): Enter the manager's title as it appears on their government-issued ID or passport.

### Manager Phone
* **manager_phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter the manager's phone number as it appears on their government-issued ID or passport.

### Manager Email
* **manager_email** (`manager_email`, text, optional): Enter the manager's email address as it appears on their government-issued ID or passport.

### Dependent ID Number
* **dependent_id_number** (`dependent_id_number`, text, optional): Enter the dependent's government-issued ID number.

### Dependent ID Number Issue Date
* **dependent_id_number_issue_date** (`dependent_id_number_issue_date`, date, optional): Enter the issue date of the dependent's government-issued ID number.

### Dependent ID Number Expiration Date
* **dependent_id_number_expiration_date** (`dependent_id_number_expiration_date`, date, optional): Enter the expiration date of the dependent's government-issued ID number.

### Dependent ID Number Renewal Date
* **dependent_id_number_renewal_date** (`dependent_id_number_renewal_date`, date, optional): Enter the renewal date of the dependent's government-issued ID number.

### Dependent ID Number Status
* **dependent_id_number_status** (`dependent_id_number_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the dependent's government-issued ID number.

### Dependent Signature
* **dependent_signature** (`dependent_signature`, text, optional): Enter the dependent's signature.

### Employer Signature
* **employer_signature** (`employer_signature`, text, optional): Enter the employer's signature.

### Manager Signature
* **manager_signature** (`manager_signature`, text, optional): Enter the manager's signature.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any information or field, please consult the relevant department or authority for guidance.
* If you need to make changes to a dependent's information, please contact the relevant department or authority for assistance.
