# Rehabilitation Insurance Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify patient information and services rendered by a clinic, allowing for accurate insurance claims and reimbursement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read each section before starting to fill out the form.
- Enter information in each section as accurately and completely as possible.
- Do not leave any blank fields without a valid reason. If a field does not apply to your situation, please mark it as "N/A" or "Not Applicable".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Clinic Info** (`clinic_info`, text, Optional): This section is for the clinic's contact information. Please enter the name and address of the clinic where the services were rendered.
* **Insurance Details** (`insurance_details`, text, Optional): Here, you can enter the insurance company's name and policy number to help with insurance verification.
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, Optional): Please provide the patient's name and any other relevant information to assist with identification.
* **Services Rendered** (`services_rendered`, number, Optional): Indicate the number of services provided during this visit.
* **Dates** (`dates`, date, Optional): Enter the date(s) of service rendered.
* **Therapist Info** (`therapist_info`, text, Optional): Enter the therapist's name and any other relevant information.
* **Billing Info** (`billing_info`, text, Optional): This section is for billing contact information, if applicable.
* **Insurance Verification** (`insurance_verification`, text, Optional): Enter any comments or information about insurance verification, if necessary.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure correct insurance verification.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact the clinic staff for guidance.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to reach out to the clinic staff.
