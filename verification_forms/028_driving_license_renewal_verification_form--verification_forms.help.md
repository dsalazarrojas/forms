# Driving License Renewal Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify the information of a person attempting to renew their driver's license.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the applicant's information in the **applicant_info** field.
2. Enter the driver's license number in the **license_number** field.
3. Enter the expiration date of the license in the **expiration_date** field.
4. Select the **renewal_eligibility** option that applies to the applicant from the provided list.
5. Enter the applicant's name in the **applicant_name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **applicant_info** (`text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the applicant, such as their reason for license renewal.
- **license_number** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the driver's license number issued to the applicant.
- **expiration_date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the license expiration, in the format MM/DD/YY.
- **renewal_eligibility** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select the option that indicates the applicant's eligibility for license renewal.
  * Option 1
  * True
  * False
- **applicant_name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the applicant's name. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date to avoid any issues with the license renewal process.
- If you are unsure of any information, contact the relevant authorities before submitting the form.
