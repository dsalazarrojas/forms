# Global Signatory Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Global Signatory Form" is a tool used for approval processes involving multiple stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by carefully filling in the required fields, such as your name, email, phone number, and location.
2. Fill in the approval status and date/time of approval.
3. If applicable, add any additional notes or messages for approval.
4. If you are an approver, fill in your name, email, phone number, and location.
5. If you are a signatory, fill in your name, email, phone number, and location.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **User Location** (`user_location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your location.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **User Note** (`user_note`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional information.
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the approver.
* **Approver Location** (`approver_location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location of the approver.
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the approver.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the approver.
* **User Consent** (`user_consent`, `number`, required/optional): Check the box to confirm your consent.
* **Signatory Signature** (`signatory_signature`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Upload your signature image or PDF.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of approval.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of approval.
* **Approval Time** (`approval_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of approval.
* **Approval Message** (`approval_message`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any approval message.
* **Approval Date 2** (`approval_date2`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of approval 2.
* **Approval Time 2** (`approval_time2`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of approval 2.
* **Approval Message 2** (`approval_message`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any approval message 2.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, `text`, required/optional): Upload any file.
* **Note 2** (`note2`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional information.
