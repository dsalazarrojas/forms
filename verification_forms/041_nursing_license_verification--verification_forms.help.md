# Nursing License Verification - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to verify the nursing license of a user, ensuring that the user has the necessary credentials to practice their profession.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User ID in the first field. This is a unique identifier assigned to you by your licensing authority.
2. Select the country where your nursing license was issued. This will help us verify your credentials in the relevant jurisdiction.
3. Indicate the current status of your nursing license. This can be Active, Inactive, or Expired.
4. Choose the method by which you would like to receive verification of your license. You can select Phone, Email, or In-person.
5. Select the result of the verification process. This can be Verified, Not Verified, or Pending.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Id** (`user_id`, number, required: false): Enter your unique User ID assigned by your licensing authority.
* **Country** (`license_country`, select_one, required: false): Select the country where your nursing license was issued.
* **License Status** (`license_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the current status of your nursing license (Active, Inactive, or Expired).
* **Verification Method** (`verification_method`, select_one, required: false): Select the method you would like to receive verification of your license (Phone, Email, or In-person).
* **Verification Result** (`verification_result`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the result of the verification process (Verified, Not Verified, or Pending).
