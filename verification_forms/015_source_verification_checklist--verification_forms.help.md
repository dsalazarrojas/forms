# Source Verification Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for verifying various aspects of a candidate's background and employment history. It helps ensure that the candidate's provided information is accurate and trustworthy. This form is intended for use in situations where a thorough background check and employment verification is required, such as when a candidate is being considered for a job or a promotion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

* Start by filling out each field on the form according to your information.
* Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by *).
* Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* Submit the form when you have completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Background Check** (`background_check`, text, *optional*):
	+ Please provide a detailed description of any background checks performed on the candidate, including any issues or concerns found.
* **Social Media Check** (`social_media_check`, select_multiple, *optional*):
	+ Indicate if you have checked the candidate's social media profiles to verify their identity and employment history. You can select "Yes" for each platform checked (e.g., LinkedIn, Facebook, Twitter, etc.).
* **Reference Check** (`reference_check`, note, *optional*):
	+ Provide any notes about the candidate's references, such as their contact information or any notable comments they made about the candidate.
* **Education Verification** (`education_verification`, select_one, *optional*):
	+ Indicate if you have verified the candidate's education history, such as their degrees and institutions attended.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, select_multiple, *optional*):
	+ List the candidate's previous work experience, including job titles, companies, and dates of employment.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, text, *optional*):
	+ Describe the candidate's current employment status (e.g., full-time, part-time, self-employed, unemployed).
* **Job Application** (`job_application`, select_one, *optional*):
	+ Indicate if the candidate is currently applying for a job with our company.
* **Skills and Certifications** (`skills_and_certifications`, select_multiple, *optional*):
	+ List any relevant skills or certifications the candidate holds.
* **Source of Employment** (`source_of_employment`, select_one, *optional*):
	+ Indicate if the candidate's employment was sourced from a staffing agency or other external source.
* **Employment Agreement** (`employment_agreement`, select_multiple, *optional*):
	+ Indicate if the candidate has an employment agreement with our company.
* **Verification Date** (`verification_date`, date, *optional*):
	+ Enter the date you performed the verification.
* **Verification Time** (`verification_time`, time, *optional*):
	+ Enter the time you spent verifying the candidate's information.

Note: This form is intended to be a general guidance and you may need to adapt it according to your specific use case.
