# Driver’s License Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver’s License Verification Form is used to collect and verify driver's license information from individuals for various internal purposes such as insurance verification or employment verification.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your driver license number in the "Driver License Number" field.
2. Enter your issue date in the "Issue Date" field.
3. Enter your expiration date in the "Expiration Date" field.
4. Select the type of license you have from the "License Type" field.
5. Choose the country of issue from the "Country of Issue" field.
6. Fill in your photo front side in the "Photo Front Side" field.
7. Fill in your photo back side in the "Photo Back Side" field.
8. Enter your signature in the "Signature" field.
9. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
10. Enter your city in the "City" field.
11. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
12. Select your state of residence from the "State" field.
13. Click "Submit" to send your information for verification.
