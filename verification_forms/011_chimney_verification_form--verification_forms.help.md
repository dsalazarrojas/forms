# Chimney Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Chimney Verification Form is used to verify the safety and functionality of a chimney system. This form is completed by a certified inspector or verifier who has inspected the chimney and has verified its components and systems to ensure they are safe and in good working order.

To complete this form, you will need to provide information about the chimney's components, systems, and overall status. This form is used to document the results of the verification process and provide recommendations for any repairs or maintenance that may be required.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form sections and ensure you have all the necessary information required to complete it.
2. Enter the verification date and time in the "Date of Verification" field.
3. Enter the verifier's name and company information in the "Verifier Name", "Company Name", and "License or Certification Number" fields.
4. Enter the property owner's information in the "Homeowner Name", "Homeowner Email", and "Homeowner Phone" fields.
5. Select the type of chimney from the options provided in the "Chimney Type" field.
6. Enter the number of flues for the chimney system.
7. Select the primary fuel type used by the chimney system.
8. Check the boxes for any issues found during the verification process, including "Flue Liner Verified Safe", "Chimney Cap Present and Secure", "Flashing Intact and Sealed", "Masonry Structurally Sound", "Damper Operational", "Proper Clearances Verified", "Proper Draft Confirmed", "Carbon Monoxide Test Passed", and "System Status".
9. Select the overall status of the chimney system from the options provided in the "System Status" field.
10. Check the boxes for any restrictions or limitations that may affect the use of the chimney system.
11. Select any repairs required before use from the options provided in the "Repairs Required Before Use" field.
12. Enter any additional recommendations for improvement or maintenance in the "Additional Recommendations" field.
13. Enter the recommended next inspection date.
14. Enter a verification statement, indicating that you have inspected and verified the chimney system.
15. Sign the "Verifier Signature" field with your digital signature.
16. Sign the "Homeowner Acknowledgment" field with the property owner's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Verification Information** (`verification_information`, `note`, optional): This section is for general information about the verification process.
*   **Date of Verification** (`verification_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date and time of the verification process.
*   **Verifier Name** (`verifier_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the verifier.
*   **Company Name** (`verifier_company`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
*   **License or Certification Number** (`verifier_license`, `text`, required): Enter your license or certification number.
*   **Homeowner Information** (`homeowner_information`, `note`, optional): This section is for general information about the property owner.
*   **Homeowner Name** (`homeowner_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the property owner.
*   **Property Address** (`property_address`, `text`, required): Enter the full street address of the property.
*   **Homeowner Phone** (`homeowner_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the property owner.
*   **Homeowner Email** (`homeowner_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the property owner.
*   **Chimney Details** (`chimney_details`, `note`, optional): This section is for general information about the chimney system.
*   **Chimney Type** (`chimney_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of chimney from the options provided.
*   **Number of Flues** (`number_of_flues`, `number`, required): Enter the number of flues for the chimney system.
*   **Primary Fuel Type** (`fuel_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary fuel type used by the chimney system.
*   **Date of Last Chimney Cleaning** (`last_cleaning_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the last chimney cleaning, if known.
*   **Safety Verification Checklist** (`safety_verification`, `note`, optional): This section is for marking any safety issues or concerns during the verification process.
*   **Flue Liner Verified Safe** (`flue_verified`, `select_one`, required): Check if the flue liner is safe.
*   **Chimney Cap Present and Secure** (`cap_verified`, `select_one`, required): Check if the chimney cap is present and secure.
*   **Flashing Intact and Sealed** (`flashing_verified`, `select_one`, required): Check if the flashing is intact and sealed.
*   **Masonry Structurally Sound** (`masonry_verified`, `select_one`, required): Check if the masonry is structurally sound.
*   **Damper Operational** (`damper_verified`, `select_one`, required): Check if the damper is operational.
*   **Proper Clearances Verified** (`clearance_verified`, `select_one`, required): Check if the clearances are proper.
*   **Proper Draft Confirmed** (`draft_verified`, `select_one`, required): Check if the proper draft is confirmed.
*   **Carbon Monoxide Test Passed** (`carbon_monoxide_check`, `select_one`, required): Check if the carbon monoxide test has passed.
*   **System Status** (`system_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the overall status of the chimney system.
*   **Is the Chimney Safe for Immediate Use** (`safe_for_use`, `select_one`, required): Check if the chimney is safe for immediate use.
*   **Any Usage Restrictions** (`restrictions_noted`, `text`, optional): Describe any limitations or restrictions that may affect the use of the chimney system.
*   **Repairs Required Before Use** (`repairs_required`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any repairs required before use.
*   **Additional Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional recommendations for improvement or maintenance.
*   **Recommended Next Inspection** (`next_inspection_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the recommended next inspection date.
*   **Verification Statement** (`verification_statement`, `note`, required): Enter a statement indicating that you have inspected and verified the chimney system.
*   **Verifier Signature** (`verifier_signature`, `text`, required): Sign with your digital signature.
*   **Homeowner Acknowledgment** (`homeowner_signature`, `text`, required): Sign with the property owner's signature.
