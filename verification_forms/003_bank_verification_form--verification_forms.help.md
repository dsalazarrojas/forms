# Bank Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify banking information for security, identity, or other verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select the type of entity (individual or business) and provide your full name as it appears on your banking documents.
2. Enter your email address on file.
3. Enter your verified contact phone number.
4. Enter the name of your financial institution (bank).
5. Enter the last 4 digits of your account number.
6. Enter the ABA routing number for your account.
7. Select the type of bank account you have (checking, savings, etc.).
8. Select the current status of your account (active, dormant, etc.).
9. Enter the name of the primary account holder.
10. Enter the date when your account was opened.
11. Select the method of account verification you prefer (micro-deposits, etc.).
12. Select the documents you are providing for verification.
13. (Optional) Enter the amount of the first micro-deposit for verification.
14. (Optional) Enter the amount of the second micro-deposit for verification.
15. (Optional) Select a date for verification.
16. (Optional) Enter any additional information about the verification request.
17. Confirm that you authorize this verification request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Entity Type (1)**: Select "Individual" if you are a personal account holder, or "Business" if you are a company or organization.
* **Full Name or Business Name (2)**: Enter your name as it appears on your banking documents.
* **Email Address (3)**: Enter your email address on file with your bank.
* **Phone Number (4)**: Enter your verified contact phone number.
* **Bank Name (5)**: Enter the name of your financial institution (bank).
* **Account Number (6)**: Enter the last 4 digits of your account number.
* **Routing Number (7)**: Enter the ABA routing number for your account.
* **Account Type (8)**: Select the type of bank account you have (checking, savings, etc.).
* **Account Status (9)**: Select the current status of your account (active, dormant, etc.).
* **Account Holder Name (10)**: Enter the name of the primary account holder.
* **Account Opening Date (11)**: Enter the date when your account was opened.
* **Verification Method (12)**: Select the method of account verification you prefer (micro-deposits, etc.).
* **Supporting Documents (13)**: Select the documents you are providing for verification (bank statement, etc.).
* **First Micro-Deposit Amount (14)**: (Optional) Enter the amount of the first micro-deposit for verification.
* **Second Micro-Deposit Amount (15)**: (Optional) Enter the amount of the second micro-deposit for verification.
* **Verification Date (16)**: (Optional) Select a date for verification.
* **Purpose of Verification (17)**: (Optional) Enter any additional information about the verification request.
* **Additional Notes (18)**: (Optional) Enter any additional information about this verification request.
* **I Authorize This Verification Request (19)**: Confirm that you authorize this verification request.
