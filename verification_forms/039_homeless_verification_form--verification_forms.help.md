# Homeless Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to verify the status of an individual as homeless or not, and to collect information about their last night's accommodation and address, if applicable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answer for "Homeless Status" question.
2. Enter your last night's address in the "Address" field.
3. Provide a brief description of your last night's accommodation in the "Last Night's Accommodation" field.
4. Enter the address of your last night's accommodation in the "Last Night's Address" field.
5. Estimate how long you have been homeless in the "How Long Been Homeless" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Homeless Status
 Homeless (required)
- Select "Homeless" if you are currently experiencing homelessness.
Not Homeless (optional)
- Select "Not Homeless" if you are not currently experiencing homelessness.

### Address
Address (optional)
- Enter your last night's address, if different from your current address.

### Last Night's Accommodation
Last Night's Accommodation (optional)
- Describe your last night's accommodation, such as a shelter, hostel, or friend's house.

### Last Night's Address
Last Night's Address (optional)
- Enter the address of your last night's accommodation.

### How Long Been Homeless
How Long Been Homeless (optional)
- Estimate how long you have been homeless.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you are not sure about any of the questions, please ask for help or clarification.
* Please note that this form is for verification purposes only and is not a substitute for a thorough assessment or diagnosis by a qualified professional.
