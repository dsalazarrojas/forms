# Volunteer Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Volunteer Verification Form is used to collect information about volunteers, including their personal details, language skills, and other relevant information. This form helps us verify the identity of our volunteers and ensure that they have the necessary qualifications and skills to participate in our programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **Volunteer First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Volunteer Last Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Volunteer Email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Volunteer Phone** field.
5. Select the correct **Type** of volunteer: Individual, Organization, or Other.
6. Select your **Date of Birth** from the calendar picker.
7. For **mm/dd**, select the correct month and day of your birth.
8. For **yy**, select your birth year.
9. Enter your **Occupation** in the text field.
10. Indicate your language skills by selecting the languages you speak from the **Languages Spoken** field.
11. If you have additional languages to report, please select "Other" and enter them in the corresponding text field.
12. Review your submission to ensure all fields are complete and accurate.
