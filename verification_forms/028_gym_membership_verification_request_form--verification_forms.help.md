# Gym Membership Verification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Membership Verification Request Form is designed to be used by current members to request verification of their membership status and to upload any necessary documents to support their request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the requested information. Please make sure to fill out all required fields and upload any necessary documents to support your request. You can navigate through the pages of the form using the navigation buttons at the top of the page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Details** (`member_details`): This field is where you can enter your member name, ID, and other relevant details.
* **Member Documents** (`member_documents`): In this field, you can upload any supporting documents for your membership verification request. Please make sure to upload the correct documents as specified in the instructions.
* **Consent** (`consent`): This field is for you to confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of your membership.
* **Member Contact** (`member_contact`): Enter your contact information, such as your phone number and email address, so we can get in touch with you regarding your membership verification request.
* **Agreement** (`member_agreement`): Please select the correct agreement option to confirm your understanding of your membership terms and conditions.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Once you have completed all the required fields, click this button to submit your verification request.
* **Member ID** (`member_id`): Enter your member ID number to verify your membership status.
* **Name** (`member_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your membership documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`member_dob`): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Address** (`member_address`): Enter your current address to verify your membership status.
* **Phone** (`member_phone`): Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you regarding your membership verification request.
* **Email** (`member_email`): Enter your email address to receive updates on your membership verification status.
* **Document ID** (`member_document_id`): Enter the ID of the document you are uploading to support your membership verification request.
* **Document Name** (`member_document_name`): Enter the name of the document you are uploading.
* **Document Date** (`member_document_date`): Enter the date the document was issued or updated.
* **Document Details** (`member_document_details`): Provide a brief description of the document you are uploading.
* **Document Uploaded** (`member_document_uploaded`): Once you have uploaded your document, enter 'Yes' to confirm that it has been uploaded.

Note: This help guide is for informational purposes only and is subject to change without notice. For more information, please contact the gym management.
