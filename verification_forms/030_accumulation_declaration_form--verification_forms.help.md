# Accumulation Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare the accumulation of assets. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the declarant information section.
2. Provide the declarant's name, organization name if applicable, contact email, and contact phone number.
3. Specify the time period covered by the accumulation.
4. Describe the assets accumulated during the period.
5. Estimate the total value of the accumulated assets.
6. List or describe any supporting documents attached.
7. Confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
8. Sign and date the declaration.
9. Add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Declarant name** (`declarant_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person making the declaration.
* **Organization name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): If the declaration is being made on behalf of an organization, enter the name of the organization.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address used for contact and correspondence.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number for verification purposes.
* **Declaration period** (`declaration_period`, text, required): Specify the time period covered by the accumulation.
* **Assets description** (`assets_description`, text, required): Describe the assets accumulated during the period.
* **Total value** (`total_value`, number, required): Estimate the total value of the accumulated assets.
* **Supporting documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): List or describe any supporting documents attached.
* **Declaration statement** (`declaration_statement`, select_one, required): Confirm that the information provided is true and accurate.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Type your full name as an electronic signature.
* **Date signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date of the declaration.
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
* This form should not be filled out for the same declarant multiple times within the same time period.
* Attach any supporting documents as required. 

Note: The date format for `date_signed` field is expected to be in YYYY-MM-DD format.
