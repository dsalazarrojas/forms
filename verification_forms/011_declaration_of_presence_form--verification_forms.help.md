# Declaration Of Presence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Declaration Of Presence Form is designed to capture essential information about an individual's or entity's presence, allowing for accurate verification and record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information section with your name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
2. Select the correct options for each of the selection fields (Select One and Select Multiple).
3. Enter the date and time of your presence, if applicable.
4. Add any additional notes or comments, if necessary.
5. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (general_info)**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Name (name)**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be recorded.
* **Other Name (name_other)**: Enter any other name associated with you, such as a nickname or alias.
* **Contact Info (contact_info)**: Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date (date)**: Select the date of your presence.
* **Time (time)**: Select the time of your presence.
* **Select One 1 (select_one_1)**: Choose one of the available options.
* **Select One 2 (select_one_2)**: Choose one of the available options.
* **Select Multiple 1 (select_multiple_1)**: Select all applicable options.
* **Select Multiple 2 (select_multiple_2)**: Select all applicable options.
* **Note (note)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your presence.
* **Select One 3 (select_one_3)**: Choose one of the available options.
* **Date Time (date_time)**: Enter the date and time of your presence, if not already selected.
* **Time (time)**: Select the time of your presence, if not already selected.
* **Select Multiple 4 (select_multiple_4)**: Select all applicable options.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
* If you need to make any changes, please do not resubmit the entire form.
