# RBT/BCBA Information Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RBT/BCBA Information Submission form is designed to collect and verify information about individuals or entities related to RBT and BCBA certifications, licenses, or other credentials.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct category for your submission (BCBA, RBT, or Other).
2. Enter your Bcbo Rbt Id if applicable.
3. Fill out the Name field with your official name.
4. Enter your physical address.
5. Type your city of residence.
6. Select your state (if applicable).
7. Enter your zip code.
8. Provide your email address.
9. Enter your phone number.
10. Enter your fax number (if applicable).
11. Select your certification date.
12. Select your expiration date.
13. Select your renewal date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Category`, select_one, optional):
	+ Please select the category of your submission (BCBA, RBT, or Other).
* **Bcbo Rbt Id** (`Bcbo Rbt Id`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your Bcbo Rbt Id if applicable.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, optional):
	+ Fill out your official name.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your physical address.
* **City** (`City`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your city of residence.
* **State** (`State`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select your state (if applicable).
* **Zip** (`Zip`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your zip code.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional):
	+ Provide your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Fax Number** (`Fax Number`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your fax number (if applicable).
* **Certification Date** (`Certification Date`, date, optional):
	+ Select your certification date.
* **Expiration Date** (`Expiration Date`, date, optional):
	+ Select your expiration date.
* **Renewal Date** (`Renewal Date`, date, optional):
	+ Select your renewal date.
