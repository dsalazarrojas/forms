# scientific_claim_verification_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scientific_claim_verification_submission_form is designed to collect information about a scientific claim, its status, and any relevant feedback from reviewers. This form helps facilitate a structured verification process for scientific claims, ensuring that all required information is provided and reviewed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your user information in the **User Info** field.
2.  Enter details about the **Claim Details** in the designated field.
3.  Upload any relevant **Evidence** related to the claim.
4.  Indicate the current **Claim Status** (Verified, Unverified, Not Reviewed, or On Hold).
5.  Provide any **Reviewer Feedback** for the claim.
6.  Add any **Additional Comments** about the claim.
7.  Enter the **Date of Review** when the claim was reviewed.
8.  Specify the **Time of Review** when the claim was reviewed.
9.  Enter the **Reviewer Name** who reviewed the claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Info** (User Info, text, required: false): Enter your name and contact information to help us contact you if needed.
*   **Claim Details** (Claim Details, text, required: false): Provide a clear and concise description of the claim you are submitting.
*   **Evidence** (Evidence, text, required: false): Attach any supporting documents or data related to the claim.
*   **Claim Status** (Claim Status, select_one, required: false): Choose the current status of the claim (Verified, Unverified, Not Reviewed, or On Hold).
*   **Reviewer Feedback** (Reviewer Feedback, text, required: false): Provide any feedback or comments from the reviewer about the claim.
*   **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, required: false): Add any additional information or comments about the claim.
*   **Date of Review** (Date of Review, date, required: false): Enter the date when the claim was reviewed.
*   **Time of Review** (Time of Review, time, required: false): Enter the time when the claim was reviewed.
*   **Reviewer Name** (Reviewer Name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the reviewer who examined the claim.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you have all the required information before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, you can contact us for clarification.
*   Please be concise and clear in your responses to facilitate the verification process.
