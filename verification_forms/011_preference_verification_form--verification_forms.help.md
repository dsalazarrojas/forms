# Preference Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preference Verification Form is designed to gather information about your preferences and communication settings. It helps us understand how you want to be contacted and how you want your data to be handled.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and answer each question truthfully.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. If you are unsure about any of the questions, ask for clarification before submitting the form.
4. Make sure to fill out the form only once, as duplicate submissions may be rejected.
