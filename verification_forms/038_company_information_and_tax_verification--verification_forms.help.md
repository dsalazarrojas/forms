# Company Information And Tax Verification - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a company's legal and tax details, including its name, registration number, tax ID, and business activities. This information is required for various official purposes, such as tax compliance, government records, and regulatory needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review each section carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information required.
2.  Fill out each field accurately and completely.
3.  Double-check your answers for errors and inconsistencies.
4.  If a field is optional, ensure you have filled it out or indicated that it's not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Legal Name** (`Company Legal Name`, text, required): The full, official name of your company, as it appears on your business registration documents.
* **Trade Name/DBA** (`Trade Name/DBA`, text, optional): If your company uses a trade name or doing business as (DBA) name different from its legal name, enter it here.
* **Company Registration Number** (`Company Registration Number`, text, required): Your company's official registration ID.
* **Date of Incorporation** (`Date of Incorporation`, date, required): The date your company was formed and officially registered.
* **Jurisdiction of Incorporation** (`Jurisdiction of Incorporation`, text, required): The state or country where your company is registered.
* **Company Type** (`Company Type`, select_one, required): Select the type of company you are (Corporation (C-Corp), Corporation (S-Corp), Limited Liability Company (LLC), Partnership, Sole Proprietorship, Nonprofit, Government Entity, Other).
* **Principal Business Address** (`Principal Business Address`, text, required): Enter the address of your primary business location (street, city, state, zip).
* **Mailing Address** (`Mailing Address`, text, optional): If your company has a different mailing address, enter it here.
* **Registered Agent Name** (`Registered Agent Name`, text, optional): If your company has a registered agent, enter the full name.
* **Registered Agent Address** (`Registered Agent Address`, text, optional): If your company has a registered agent, enter the address.
* **-- Tax Information --** (`-- Tax Information --`, note, required): This section is for tax identification information.
* **Tax ID / EIN** (`Tax ID / EIN`, text, required): Your company's federal tax ID number or Employer Identification Number (EIN).
* **Type of Tax ID** (`Type of Tax ID`, select_one, required): Select the type of tax ID you have (Federal EIN, State Tax ID, VAT Number, GST Number, Other).
* **State Tax ID Number** (`State Tax ID Number`, text, optional): If your company has a state tax ID number, enter it here.
* **Sales Tax Permit Number** (`Sales Tax Permit Number`, text, optional): If your company has a sales tax permit number, enter it here.
* **Tax Classification** (`Tax Classification`, select_one, required): Select how your company is taxed (C Corporation, S Corporation, Partnership, Disregarded Entity, Nonprofit (501c3), Other).
* **Fiscal Year End** (`Fiscal Year End`, text, required): The month and day of your company's fiscal year end.
* **Is the company in good standing?** (`Is the company in good standing?`, select_one, required): Select the status of your company's registration (Yes, in good standing, Yes, with minor exceptions, No, issues exist, Not sure).
* **If no, please explain compliance issues** (`If no, please explain compliance issues`, text, optional): If you selected "No, issues exist", provide details about the compliance issues.
* **Is there pending litigation?** (`Is there pending litigation?`, select_one, required): Select the status of any pending litigation (False, Yes, as plaintiff, Yes, as defendant, Yes, both).
* **Has the company ever filed for bankruptcy?** (`Has the company ever filed for bankruptcy?`, select_one, required): Select the status of any bankruptcy filings (False, Yes, Chapter 7, Yes, Chapter 11, Yes, Chapter 13, Yes, other).
* **I certify that the information provided is accurate** (`I certify that the information provided is accurate`, select_one, required): Select the accuracy of the provided information (Yes, I Certify, No, I Cannot Certify).
* **I have authority to submit this verification** (`I have authority to submit this verification`, select_one, required): Select your authority to submit this verification (Yes, I Have Authority, No, I Do Not Have Authority).
* **Authorized Signatory Name** (`Authorized Signatory Name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the authorized signatory.
* **Authorized Signatory Title** (`Authorized Signatory Title`, text, required): Enter the title of the authorized signatory.
* **Authorized Signature** (`Authorized Signature`, text, required): Enter the electronic signature of the authorized signatory.
* **Verification Date** (`Verification Date`, date, required): Enter the date of verification.

## Tips
* Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Double-check your answers for errors and inconsistencies.
* If a field is optional, ensure you have filled it out or indicated that it's not applicable.
