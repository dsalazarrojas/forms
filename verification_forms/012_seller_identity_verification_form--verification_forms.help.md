# Seller Identity Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Seller Identity Verification Form is designed to collect and verify the identity of a seller, including their name, contact information, and supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on "Seller Details" to access the first field.
2. Fill in the necessary fields, providing accurate information about the seller.
3. If required, upload supporting documents in the "Supporting Documents" section.
4. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seller Details**: This section contains the basic details about the seller, such as their name, contact information, and other general information.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the seller as it appears on their official identification documents.
* **Email**: Provide the seller's email address for further communication.
* **Phone**: Enter the seller's phone number, if applicable.
* **Supporting Documents**: Attach relevant documents that support the seller's identity, such as ID cards or business registration certificates.
* **Address**: Enter the seller's address for verification purposes.
* **Tip**: Ensure all fields are accurate and complete before submission, as this form is meant for verification purposes only.
