# Foreign Beneficial Owner Certification - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on foreign beneficial owners, including their identification, background, and relationships with the entity being verified. It's essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the verification process is completed successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information and documentation before starting the form.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review and proofread your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Country of Citizenship** (`country_of_citizenship`, text, Optional): Enter the country where you were born or hold citizenship.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `DD/MM/YYYY`.
* **Passport Number** (`passport_number`, text, Optional): Enter your passport number or ID number, if applicable.
* **Passport Expire Date** (`passport_exp_date`, date, Optional): Enter the expiration date of your passport or ID document, in the format `DD/MM/YYYY`.
* **Beneficial Owner** (`beneficial_owner`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the beneficial owner, if applicable.
* **Beneficial Owner Title** (`beneficial_owner_title`, text, Optional): Enter the title or position of the beneficial owner, if applicable.
