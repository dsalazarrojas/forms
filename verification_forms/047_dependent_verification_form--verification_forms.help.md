# Dependent Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a dependent for benefits verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
- Ensure that the form is submitted correctly, as incorrect submissions may lead to processing delays or errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **dependent_details** (`dependent_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description or any additional information about the dependent.
* **dependent_documents** (`dependent_documents`, note, optional): Enter any relevant documents or supporting information about the dependent (e.g., ID, employment verification).
* **dependent_relationship_since** (`dependent_relationship_since`, number, required): Enter the number of years the dependent has been in this relationship.
* **dependent_relationship_status** (`dependent_relationship_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the dependent's relationship (e.g., Single, Married, Other).
* **dependent_relationship_type** (`dependent_relationship_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of the dependent's relationship (e.g., Son, Daughter, Other).
* **dependent_age** (`dependent_age`, number, required): Enter the dependent's age.
* **dependent_gender** (`dependent_gender`, select_one, required): Select the dependent's gender (e.g., Male, Female).
* **dependent_income_verification** (`dependent_income_verification`, select_multiple, required): Select the dependent's employment status (e.g., Employed, Unemployed, Self-Employed).
* **dependent_proof_of_income** (`dependent_proof_of_income`, date, optional): Enter the date of the dependent's proof of income (if applicable).
* **dependent_proof_of_employment** (`dependent_proof_of_employment`, time, optional): Enter the time of the dependent's proof of employment (if applicable).
* **dependent_proof_of_identity** (`dependent_proof_of_identity`, note, optional): Enter any supporting information about the dependent's proof of identity (if applicable).
* **dependent_relationship_begin_date** (`dependent_relationship_begin_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the relationship began.
* **dependent_last_name** (`dependent_last_name`, text, optional): Enter the dependent's last name.
* **dependent_first_name** (`dependent_first_name`, text, optional): Enter the dependent's first name.
* **dependent_middle_initials** (`dependent_middle_initials`, text, optional): Enter the dependent's middle initials.
* **dependent_last_four_digits** (`dependent_last_four_digits`, text, optional): Enter the last four digits of the dependent's ID number (if applicable).
* **dependent_ssn** (`dependent_ssn`, text, optional): Enter the dependent's social security number (if applicable).
* **dependent_dob** (`dependent_dob`, date, optional): Enter the dependent's date of birth.
* **dependent_relationship_status_begin_date** (`dependent_relationship_status_begin_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the relationship status began.
* **dependent_employment_status** (`dependent_employment_status`, select_one, required): Select the dependent's employment status (e.g., Employed, Unemployed).
* **dependent_proof_of_employment_last_four_digits** (`dependent_proof_of_employment_last_four_digits`, text, optional): Enter the last four digits of the proof of employment document (if applicable).
* **dependent_proof_of_employment_date** (`dependent_proof_of_employment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the proof of employment document (if applicable).
* **dependent_proof_of_id** (`dependent_proof_of_id`, text, optional): Enter the dependent's proof of ID document (if applicable).
* **dependent_relationship_begin_date_month_year** (`dependent_relationship_begin_date_month_year`, number, optional): Enter the month and year the relationship began.
* **dependent_relationship_begin_date_day** (`dependent_relationship_begin_date_day`, number, optional): Enter the day the relationship began.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields before submission to ensure accuracy.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
- Ensure that the form is submitted correctly, as incorrect submissions may lead to processing delays or errors.
