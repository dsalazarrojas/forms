# Citizen ID Document Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect verification information for citizen ID documents. It is designed to ensure the authenticity and validity of the uploaded documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the personal information section with your full name, email address, phone number, and date of birth.
2. Select the type of identification document you are uploading.
3. Provide the identification number, issuing country, and issue and expiration dates of the document.
4. Upload a clear photo or scan of the front side of the document.
5. If applicable, upload a clear photo or scan of the back side of the document.
6. Provide a selfie with the ID document, if required.
7. Assess the quality of the uploaded document.
8. Select the purpose of the verification.
9. Declare that the document is authentic and unaltered.
10. Consent to the processing of your personal information.
11. Understand and agree to data retention per privacy policy.
12. Authorize verification with legal authorities if needed.
13. Provide alternative contact information, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID document.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Type of Identification Document**: Select the type of ID document you are uploading (e.g., Passport, National ID card, etc.).
* **Identification Number**: Enter the ID number or any other unique identifier on your document.
* **Issuing Country**: Enter the country that issued your ID document.
* **Document Issue Date**: Enter the date the document was issued.
* **Document Expiration Date**: Enter the date the document expires.
* **Upload Front Side of Document**: Upload a clear photo or scan of the front side of your ID document.
* **Upload Back Side of Document**: If applicable, upload a clear photo or scan of the back side of your ID document.
* **Selfie with ID Document**: Upload a photo of yourself holding the ID document.
* **Document Quality Certification**: Assess the quality of the uploaded document (clear and legible, or has issues).
* **Purpose of Verification**: Select the purpose of the verification (e.g., Account verification, Employment verification, etc.).
* **Document Authenticity**: Declare that the document is authentic and unaltered.
* **Consent to Processing**: Consent to the processing of your personal information.
* **Data Retention**: Understand and agree to data retention per privacy policy.
* **Authorize Verification with Legal Authorities**: Authorize verification with legal authorities if needed.
* **Alternative Contact Information**: If applicable, provide alternative contact information.
