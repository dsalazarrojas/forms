# Employment Verification Affidavit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify an employee's employment details for employment purposes such as background checks or verification of employment history.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required fields.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date employment information.
3. Sign the form with a pen or digital equivalent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (Verification Affidavit First Name)** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's first name as it appears on their ID or government-issued document.
* **Last Name (Verification Affidavit Last Name)** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's last name as it appears on their ID or government-issued document.
* **Email (Verification Affidavit Email)** (Email, Optional): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Phone (Verification Affidavit Phone)** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Company (Verification Affidavit Company)** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the company the employee works for or worked for.
* **Job Title (Verification Affidavit Job Title)** (Text, Optional): Enter the employee's job title as it appears on their ID or government-issued document.
* **Start Date (Verification Affidavit Start Date)** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the employee started working for the company.
* **End Date (Verification Affidavit End Date)** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the employee ended their working relationship with the company.
* **Reason (Verification Affidavit Reason)** (Text, Optional): Enter the reason why the employee left the company.
* **Employer Signature (Verification Affidavit Employer Signature)** (Note, Required): Sign this section with a pen or digital equivalent to confirm the information provided.
* **Employer Name (Verification Affidavit Employer Name)** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the employee's current or former employer.
* **Employer Title (Verification Affidavit Employer Title)** (Text, Optional): Enter the title of the employee's current or former supervisor.
* **Employer Phone (Verification Affidavit Employer Phone)** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the employee's current or former supervisor.
* **Employer Email (Verification Affidavit Employer Email)** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the employee's current or former supervisor.
* **Employer Address (Verification Affidavit Employer Address)** (Text, Optional): Enter the address of the employee's current or former employer.
* **Employee Signature (Verification Affidavit Employee Signature)** (Note, Required): Sign this section with a pen or digital equivalent to confirm the information provided.
* **Employee Name (Verification Affidavit Employee Name)** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the employee to be verified.
* **Employee Title (Verification Affidavit Employee Title)** (Text, Optional): Enter the title of the employee to be verified.
* **Employee Phone (Verification Affidavit Employee Phone)** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the employee to be verified.
* **Employee Email (Verification Affidavit Employee Email)** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the employee to be verified.
* **Employee Address (Verification Affidavit Employee Address)** (Text, Optional): Enter the address of the employee to be verified.
* **Date (Verification Affidavit Date)** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the verification is being made.
* **Issuer Signature (Verification Affidavit Issuer Signature)** (Note, Required): Sign this section with a pen or digital equivalent to confirm the information provided.
* **Issuer Name (Verification Affidavit Issuer Name)** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person verifying the employee's employment details.
