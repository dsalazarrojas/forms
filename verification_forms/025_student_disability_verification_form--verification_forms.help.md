# Student Disability Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a student's disability for the purpose of verification and providing support and accommodations.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out your first name and last name in the fields provided.
- Enter your Student ID number.
- Provide your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- Enter the date of the verification in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- Select one or more types of disabilities that apply to you from the options provided.
- Describe your disability in detail in the text field.
- Choose the method of verification that works best for you, whether it's in person, over video conference, or by mail.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Your first name.
  (Required: False)
- **Last Name**: Your last name.
  (Required: False)
- **Student ID**: Your student ID number.
  (Required: False)
- **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  (Required: False)
- **Date of Disability Verification**: The date when you were verified as having a disability.
  (Required: False)
- **Disability**: Select one or more disabilities that apply to you from the options provided.
  (Required: False)
  - **Hearing**
  - **Visual**
  - **Physical**
  - **Mental**
  - **Learning Disability**
  - **Other** (Please select this option if your disability is not listed.)
- **Disability Description**: Describe your disability in detail.
  (Required: False)
  Please provide as much information as you can to help with accommodations and support.
- **Verification Method**: Select the method of verification that works best for you.
  (Required: False)
  - **In person**
  - **Over video conference**
  - **By mail**
