# Curriculum Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Curriculum Verification Form is a tool designed to collect information from students and schools for verification purposes. This form helps ensure that the curriculum information is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Follow the instructions on each page to fill out the form accurately.
2.  Enter your student information in the Student Information page.
3.  Enter your school information in the School Information page.
4.  Provide verification information in the Verification Information page.
5.  Review your form carefully before clicking the Submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`student_info`, text, not required): This page requires information about the student such as name, ID, and other relevant details. Please enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* **School Information** (`school_info`, text, not required): This page requires information about the school such as name, ID, and other relevant details. Please enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Verification Information** (`verification_info`, text, not required): This page requires information about the verification process such as who verified and when. Please enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, not required): This is the submit button. Please review your form carefully before clicking this button to avoid errors.
