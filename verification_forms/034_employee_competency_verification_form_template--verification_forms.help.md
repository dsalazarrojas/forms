# Employee Competency Verification Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify the competency of employees in various aspects, including job vacancy details, candidate information, competency requirements, relevant experience, certifications, references, and the upload of supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Job Vacancy Details** section with information about the job opening, including the job title, description, and location.
2. Provide **Candidate Details** such as their name, contact information, and any other relevant personal details.
3. For each **Competency Requirement**, select all that apply to the candidate.
4. In the **Relevant Experience** section, choose all the experiences that match the job requirements.
5. In the **Relevant Certifications** section, select all the certifications that match the job requirements.
6. If applicable, select **References** that can vouch for the candidate's competency.
7. Indicate whether you need to **Upload Documents**, and if so, attach the necessary files.
8. Finally, click the **Submit** button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Job Vacancy Details (required)
* **Job Vacancy Details**: Enter the job title, description, and location of the job opening being filled by the candidate.

### Candidate Details (required)
* **Candidate Details**: Enter the candidate's name, contact information, and any other relevant personal details.

### Competency Requirements (required)
* **Competency Requirements**: Select all the competency requirements that apply to the candidate, such as:
	+ 'Yes' for requirements that the candidate meets
	+ 'No' for requirements that the candidate does not meet

### Relevant Experience (required)
* **Relevant Experience**: Select all the experiences that match the job requirements, such as:
	+ 'Yes' for experiences that apply
	+ 'No' for experiences that do not apply

### Relevant Certifications (required)
* **Relevant Certifications**: Select all the certifications that match the job requirements, such as:
	+ 'Yes' for certifications that apply
	+ 'No' for certifications that do not apply

### References (optional)
* **References**: Select all the references that can vouch for the candidate's competency, such as:
	+ 'Yes' for references that apply
	+ 'No' for references that do not apply

### Upload Documents (optional)
* **Upload Documents**: Indicate whether you need to upload supporting documents, and if so, attach the necessary files.

### Submit (optional)
* **Submit**: Click the button to submit the form and send the verification request to the relevant parties.
