# Regulatory Certification Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Regulatory Certification Tracking Form is designed to capture and track the certification status of users. This form aims to collect essential information about the user, their certification, and the verification process for efficient tracking and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that each field is filled out correctly, as indicated by the field label.
3. Review and verify the information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (User Information, text, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your details, such as name, contact info, etc. Please ensure you enter accurate and complete information so we can efficiently track your certification status.
* **Certification Information** (Certification Information, text, required/optional): Enter details about your certification, including relevant documents, dates, and any additional information that will help us track your progress.
* **Renewal Information** (Renewal Information, text, required/optional): Fill in any information related to the renewal of your certification, such as expiration dates, or any other relevant details.
* **Verification Information** (Verification Information, text, required/optional): Provide any information that helps with the verification process, like proof of completion of any certification requirements.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, required/optional): This is a submission button, which you need to click after filling in all the required fields.
* **User Notes** (User Notes, note, required/optional): This is a field for any extra information that you may wish to include or add a note that you want to remember or communicate later, like any comments or additional comments that are worth knowing.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you submit all required fields to ensure a complete and accurate tracking of your certification status.
* You can add any additional comments or information in the user notes field that will help us track your certification status.
* If you have any issues or need help, contact us at form@gic.mx.
