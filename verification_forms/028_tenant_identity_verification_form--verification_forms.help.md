# tenant_identity_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to verify a tenant's identity, which is crucial for various applications such as renting, leasing, or other property-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the first page with your **Tenant Name** (Field 1), entering your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
2. Next, fill out **Email** (Field 2) and **Phone** (Field 3) with your contact information. 
3. Move on to **Date of Birth** (Field 4), providing your date of birth in the required format (month/day/year).
4. For **Nationality** (Field 5), select your country of origin from the available options (American, British, Australian, Canadian).
5. If you are not one of the listed nationalities, use **Other (please specify)** (Field 6) to provide your nationality.
6. In **ID Type** (Field 7), select the type of identification document you possess (Driver License, Passport, ID Card, Other).
7. Enter your **ID Number** (Field 8) and select the relevant **Nationality Verified** option for your ID (True, False).
8. Complete **Address** (Field 9) with your full address, including unit number, building, street, city, state, and zip.
9. Enter your unit number in **Unit Number** (Field 10) if applicable.
10. Provide the **Building** (Field 11) name.
11. Continue with **Street** (Field 12), and enter your street address.
12. Enter your **City** (Field 13) and **State** (Field 14) as it appears on your ID.
13. Enter your zip code in **Zip** (Field 15).
14. Confirm if your **Nationality** is verified (Field 16), select True or False. 
15. Select from whom you were **Verified By** (Field 17) from the available options (Self, Friend, Family, Landlord). If it's not on the list, provide the details in **Verified By (please specify)** (Field 18).
16. Provide the **Date Reported** (Field 19), the date you reported this information.
17. Enter your details in **Reported By** (Field 20).
18. Explain the **Reason For Verification** (Field 21) in the available space.
19. Enter the **Verification Fee** (Field 22) amount.
20. Select the **Currency** of the verification fee (Field 23).
21. If the currency is not listed, enter the details in **Verification Fee (other)** (Field 24).
22. Enter any **Additional Comments** (Field 25) you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Name (Field 1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email (Field 2)**: Enter your contact email for verification purposes.
* **Phone (Field 3)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth (Field 4)**: Enter your date of birth in the required format (month/day/year).
* **Nationality (Field 5)**: Select your country of origin.
* **Other (please specify) (Field 6)**: Enter your nationality if not listed.
* **ID Type (Field 7)**: Select the type of ID you possess.
* **ID Number (Field 8)**: Enter your ID number.
* **Nationality Verified (Field 9)**: Confirm your nationality is verified.
* **Address (Field 10)**: Enter your full address, including unit number, building, street, city, state, and zip.
* **Unit Number (Field 11)**: Enter your unit number.
* **Building (Field 12)**: Enter your building name.
* **Street (Field 13)**: Enter your street address.
* **City (Field 14)**: Enter your city.
* **State (Field 15)**: Enter your state.
* **Zip (Field 16)**: Enter your zip code.
* **Is Nationality Verified (Field 17)**: Confirm if your nationality is verified.
* **Verified By (Field 18)**: Select or enter from whom you were verified by.
* **Date Reported (Field 19)**: Enter the date you reported this information.
* **Reported By (Field 20)**: Enter your name.
* **Reason For Verification (Field 21)**: Explain the reason for verification.
* **Verification Fee (Field 22)**: Enter the verification fee amount.
* **Currency (Field 23)**: Select the currency of the fee.
* **Verification Fee (other) (Field 24)**: Enter the currency if not listed.
* **Additional Comments (Field 25)**: Enter any additional comments.
