# Healthcare Credential Verification Employment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Healthcare Credential Verification Employment Form is used to verify the employment status of healthcare professionals. It is used to ensure that healthcare workers have been properly authorized and are employed by the relevant healthcare institutions. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the applicant's details in the first field, 'applicant_details'.
2. Enter the verification authorization code for the applicant in the 'verification_authorization' field.
3. Enter the submission code to complete the form.
4. Enter the applicant's name in the 'applicant_name' field.
5. Enter the applicant's email address in the 'email' field.
6. Enter the applicant's phone number in the 'phone_number' field.
7. Enter the date of the verification in the 'verification_date' field.
8. Enter the time of the verification in the 'verification_time' field.
9. Enter any additional notes about the verification in the 'verification_notes' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **applicant_details** (`applicant_details`, text, required): Enter the details of the applicant, including their name, title, and any other relevant information.
- **verification_authorization** (`verification_authorization`, text, required): Enter the verification authorization code provided by the applicant's employer or HR department.
- **submit** (`submit`, text, required): Enter the submission code to complete the form.
- **applicant_name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the applicant as it appears on their official documents.
- **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the applicant.
- **phone_number** (`phone_number`, number, required): Enter the phone number of the applicant.
- **verification_date** (`verification_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the verification.
- **verification_time** (`verification_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the verification.
- **verification_notes** (`verification_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the verification.
