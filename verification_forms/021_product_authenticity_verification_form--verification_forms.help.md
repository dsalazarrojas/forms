# productAuthenticityVerificationForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The productAuthenticityVerificationForm is a tool used to verify the authenticity of a product, ensuring that it meets the required standards and specifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the product code, product name, and purchase date as accurately as possible.
2. Provide the serial number of the product, if applicable.
3. Specify the country of origin for the product.
4. Enter the date of authenticity verification.
5. Choose the authenticity verification status from the dropdown menu (Verified, Not Verified, or Pending).
6. Add any additional notes or comments for further clarification or reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Product Code** (`product_code`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the code assigned to the product.
- **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the product.
- **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of purchase, using the mm/dd/yyyy format.
- **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the serial number of the product, if applicable.
- **Country of Origin** (`country_of_origin`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the country where the product was manufactured.
- **Authenticity Verification Date** (`authenticity_verification_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the authenticity verification process.
- **Authenticity Verification Status** (`authenticity_verification_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the authenticity verification status from the dropdown menu (Verified, Not Verified, or Pending).
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional information or comments for further clarification or reference.

## Tips
- Please provide as much detail as possible to facilitate the verification process.
- If you're unsure about any information, please clarify or specify the uncertainty.
- Ensure that the information provided matches the product's original specifications or certifications.
