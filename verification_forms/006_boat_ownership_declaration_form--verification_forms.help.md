# Boat Ownership Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Boat Ownership Declaration Form. This form is used to declare ownership of a boat and provide important details about the boat, its owner, and the owner's relationship with the boat.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the required information for each field.
3. Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Boat Owner**: Enter the name of the boat owner in this field.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the boat owner.
* **Driver License or ID Number**: Enter the driver license or ID number of the boat owner.
* **Residential Address**: Enter the residential address of the boat owner.
* **City - State - Zip Code**: Enter the city, state, and zip code of the boat owner's residence.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the boat owner.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the boat owner.
* **Boat Name**: Enter the name of the boat (optional).
* **Hull Number**: Enter the unique identification number of the boat.
* **Government Registration Number**: Enter the government registration number of the boat.
* **Boat Manufacturer**: Enter the manufacturer of the boat.
* **Boat Model**: Enter the model of the boat.
* **Year of Manufacture**: Enter the year the boat was manufactured.
* **Boat Length in Feet**: Enter the length of the boat in feet (optional).
* **Type of Boat**: Select the type of boat from the options provided.
* **Primary Moorage or Dock Location**: Enter the primary location where the boat is moored or docked.
* **Ownership Percentage**: If the boat is jointly owned, enter the percentage of ownership.
* **Joint Owner Name if Applicable**: Enter the name of any joint owners.
* **Is the boat mortgaged**: Select the status of the boat's financing.
* **Lien Holder Name if Applicable**: Enter the name of the lien holder (if applicable).
* **Insurance Carrier**: Enter the name of the insurance carrier (if applicable).
* **Insurance Policy Number**: Enter the insurance policy number (if applicable).
* **Date Ownership Commenced**: Enter the date you acquired the boat.
* **Declaration Statement**: Confirm that the information provided is true and accurate.

By completing this form, you are making a declaration about your boat ownership, and it is essential to ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your local authorities for assistance.
