# identity_verification_discharge_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "identity_verification_discharge_form" is a form used for verifying identity information. It's designed to collect personal details and verification methods for identity discharge purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out each field with your relevant information.
- Ensure that the date of birth is in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
- Enter your phone number, email address, and verification method accurately.
- Provide your signature (if required by the verification method).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Information Field

* **Personal Info**: (`personal_info`, `text`, required: false) Enter your personal information as requested.

### Name Field

* **Name**: (`name`, `text`, required: false) Enter your full name.

### Date of Birth Field

* **Date of Birth**: (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false) Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).

### Address Field

* **Address**: (`address`, `text`, required: false) Enter your address for verification purposes.

### Phone Number Field

* **Phone Number**: (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false) Enter your phone number. Please make sure to include the international prefix (+XX) if applicable.

### Email Address Field

* **Email**: (`email`, `email`, required: false) Enter your email address for verification purposes.

### Verification Information Field

* **Verification Info**: (`verification_info`, `text`, required: false) Enter any additional verification information as requested.

### Verification Method Field

* **Verification Method**: (`verification_method`, `select_one`, required: false) Select one of the verification methods provided:
	+ ID Card
	+ Passport
	+ Driver License

### Signature Field

* **Signature**: (`signature`, `text`, required: false) If required by the verification method, please enter your signature.
