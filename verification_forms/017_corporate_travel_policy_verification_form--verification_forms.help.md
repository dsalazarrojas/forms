# corporate_travel_policy_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate travel policy verification form is used to verify that employees have read, understood, and accepted the company's travel policy. This form is an essential part of the onboarding process for new employees, ensuring they are aware of their responsibilities and expectations when it comes to company travels.

## How To Complete This Form

### Complete each field as instructed:

1. **Company Travel Policy**: Enter a brief description of the company's travel policy.
2. **Travel Policies**: List the specific travel policies that you have reviewed, such as "Compliance and Responsibility" or "Acceptance of Travel Policy".
3. **Date of Acknowledgement**: Enter the date you signed the travel policy acknowledgement.
4. **Travel Policies Reviewed**: Indicate whether you have reviewed the travel policies or not. Options: "True" or "False".
5. **Understanding of Travel Policy**: Provide a brief explanation of your understanding of the travel policy.
6. **Conduct of Travel Policy**: Describe how you conduct yourself according to the travel policy.
7. **Reviewed By**: If applicable, enter the name of the person who reviewed the travel policies.
8. **Comments**: If you have any comments or feedback about the travel policy, enter them here.
9. **Employee Signature**: Sign and date the form to indicate your understanding and acceptance of the travel policy.
10. **Manager Signature**: Sign and date the form to indicate their understanding and acceptance of the travel policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Travel Policy** (`company_travel_policy`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the company's travel policy.
* **Travel Policies** (`travel_policies`, text, required): List the specific travel policies that you have reviewed, such as "Compliance and Responsibility" or "Acceptance of Travel Policy".
* **Employee Acknowledgement** (`employee_acknowledgement`, select_one, required): Select "Compliance and Responsibility" or "Acceptance of Travel Policy" to indicate your understanding and acceptance of the travel policy.
* **Date of Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement_date`, date, required): Enter the date you signed the travel policy acknowledgement.
* **Travel Policies Reviewed** (`travel_policies_reviewed`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you have reviewed the travel policies or not. Options: "True" or "False".
* **Understanding of Travel Policy** (`understanding`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation of your understanding of the travel policy.
* **Conduct of Travel Policy** (`conduct`, text, required): Describe how you conduct yourself according to the travel policy.
* **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the person who reviewed the travel policies.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any comments or feedback about the travel policy, enter them here.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form to indicate your understanding and acceptance of the travel policy.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form to indicate their understanding and acceptance of the travel policy.
