# ticket_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ticket_verification_form is designed to gather information related to ticket events, specifically to verify event planners, tickets, and event participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the event planner's name in the "Event Planner" field.
2. Type in the ticket details in the "Ticket" field.
3. List the event participants in the "Event Participants" field.
4. Optionally, add any additional notes about the ticket verification in the "Ticket Verification Form Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Planner** (Event Planner, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person or organization in charge of the event.
* **Ticket** (Ticket, text, required/optional): Type in the ticket details, including any relevant numbers or codes.
* **Event Participants** (Event Participants, text, required/optional): List the names and contact information of people participating in the event.
* **Ticket Verification Form Notes** (Ticket Verification Form Notes, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the ticket verification process, such as any issues or challenges encountered.
