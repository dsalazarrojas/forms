# Address Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for resident address verification purposes. It collects the required information to verify your current place of residence for various reasons such as account management or identity verification. 

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately to the best of your ability.
2.  Please ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Resident Full Name** (`Resident Full Name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*  **Last 4 Digits of SSN / National ID** (`Last 4 Digits of SSN / National ID`, `text`, required): Enter the last four digits of your social security number or national ID for verification purposes.
*  **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*  **Street Address** (`Street Address`, `text`, required): Enter the street address you currently reside at.
*  **City** (`City`, `text`, required): Enter the city of your current residence.
*  **State / Province** (`State / Province`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province of your current residence.
*  **Zip / Postal Code** (`Zip / Postal Code`, `text`, required): Enter the zip or postal code of your current residence.
*  **Date you moved into this address** (`Date you moved into this address`, `date`, required): Enter the date you moved into your current residence.
*  **Do you own or rent this property?** (`Do you own or rent this property?`, `select_one`, required): Select how you own or rent your property.
	+ Options: Own, Rent, Live with Family / Friend, Other
*  **Which document will you provide for verification?** (`Which document will you provide for verification?`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of document you will provide for verification.
	+ Options: Valid Driver's License, Signed Lease Agreement, Utility Bill (Water / Electric), Property Tax Statement
*  **Contact Phone Number** (`Contact Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
*  **I authorize the verification of this address information** (`I authorize the verification of this address information`, `select_one`, required): Select that you authorize the verification of your address information.
	+ Options: I Authorize, Other
*  **Verification Request Date** (`Verification Request Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are requesting the verification of your address.

## Tips

*  Please ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*  The verification process may take some time, and you will be contacted with further instructions once the verification is complete.
*  If you are experiencing any issues or have concerns, please contact us at your earliest convenience.
