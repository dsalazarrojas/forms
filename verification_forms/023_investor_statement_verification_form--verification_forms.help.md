# Investor Statement Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investor Statement Verification Form is used to gather and verify the financial information of an investor for internal financial record-keeping and compliance purposes. This form is a crucial document for maintaining accurate and up-to-date records of investor's financial activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the financial institution where the investor's account is held.
2. Enter the name of the investor.
3. Enter the amount invested.
4. Select a date when the investment was made or is valid from.
5. Write a brief statement explaining the investment and the investor's intentions.
6. Select the verification result of the investor statement from the options provided.
7. Add any additional notes or comments regarding the statement if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Institution** (`financial_institution`, text, optional): Enter the name of the financial institution where the investor's account is held. This field is not required but it helps to identify the investor's account location.
* **Investor Name** (`investor_name`, text, optional): Enter the full name of the investor.
* **Investment Amount** (`investment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount invested in the financial product or service.
* **Statement Date** (`statement_date`, date, optional): Select the date when the investment was made or is valid.
* **Investor Statement** (`investor_statement`, note, optional): Write a brief statement explaining the investment and the investor's intentions. This is a text field for additional information.
* **Verification Result** (`verification_result`, select_one, optional): Select the verification result of the investor statement from the available options: verified, not verified, or pending. This field helps to determine the status of the investor's statement.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments regarding the statement if necessary. This field is for internal use only.

## Tips
- Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- Verify the information with the investor before submitting the form.
- Review and update the form regularly to reflect any changes in the investor's information.
- Consult with the financial institution's representative for any clarification if needed.
