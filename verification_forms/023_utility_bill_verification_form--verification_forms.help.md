# Utility Bill Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Utility Bill Verification Form" is used to verify utility bills for customers, ensuring accuracy and correctness of the billing information. This form is essential for verifying and confirming the details of utility bills to prevent errors or discrepancies. By using this form, customers can accurately provide their billing information, reducing the likelihood of incorrect charges or missed payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the "Utility Bill Verification Form" title.
2. Provide your customer's name.
3. Enter your customer's email address.
4. Enter your customer's phone number.
5. Enter your customer's billing address.
6. Select the verification status of the bill (Active or Inactive).
7. Enter the verification result (a brief description of the bill verification process).
8. Choose whether the utility is "Yes" or "No" for the utility type.
9. Select the utility provider (if applicable).
10. Enter the date of verification.
11. Enter the time of verification.
12. Add any additional verification notes (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Utility Bill Verification Form** (1. **form_title**): Enter the title of the utility bill.
* **Customer Name** (2. **customer_name**): Enter your customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (3. **customer_email**): Enter your customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (4. **customer_phone**): Enter your customer's phone number.
* **Billing Address** (5. **billing_address**): Enter your customer's billing address.
* **Verification Status** (6. **verification_status**): Select "Active" if the bill is active, or "Inactive" if it is not.
* **Verification Result** (7. **verification_result**): Enter a brief description of the bill verification process.
* **Utility Type** (8. **utility_type**): Choose "Yes" if the utility is active, or "No" if it is not.
* **Utility Provider** (9. **utility_provider**): Select the utility provider (if applicable).
* **Verification Date** (10. **verification_date**): Enter the date of verification.
* **Verification Time** (11. **verification_time**): Enter the time of verification.
* **Verification Note** (12. **verification_note**): Add any additional notes about the verification process (if necessary).

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review each field carefully to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact your customer support team for assistance.
