# Employment Verification Form For Child Care - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Verification Form For Child Care is used to gather essential details about a child care provider's employment status, including their company, job title, start and end dates of employment, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before filling it out.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about your employment status.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **employment_verification_form_for_child_care_form_fields** (`employment_verification_form_for_child_care_page_1`, text, required/optional): This is the main section of the form where you'll provide your employment information.
  - **Employment Information** (`section_1`, text, required/optional): Describe your current employment status.
  - **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your employer.
  - **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title or position.
  - **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select your current employment status from the options provided (Active or Inactive).
  - **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your employment.
  - **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of your employment.
  - **Section 2** (`section_2`, text, required/optional): Describe any additional information about your employment status.
  - **Applicant Information** (`applicant_information`, text, required/optional): Provide any other relevant information about yourself.
  - **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address.
  - **Section 3** (`section_3`, text, required/optional): Describe any additional information about your employment status.
  - **Verification Information** (`verification_information`, text, required/optional): Provide any other relevant information about your verification status.
  - **Verified By** (`verified_by`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and title of the person verifying your employment status.

Please note that while the form is self-explanatory, if you are still unsure or have any questions, please consult with the relevant authorities or the form's creator for further assistance.
