# Cultural Festival Performance Slot Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by event organizers to request a performance spot during a cultural festival. It allows them to provide information about themselves, their performance, and their technical needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your event name and contact information (contact_email and contact_phone) in the Event Organizer and Contact Email/Phone fields, respectively.
2. Enter the name of your performance in the Performance Name field.
3. Select your preferred time slot for the performance.
4. Enter the duration of your performance in the Performance Duration field.
5. Choose the performance formats you will be using (e.g. dance, music, etc.) in the Performance Format field.
6. Describe any technical needs you have for your performance in the Technical Needs field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer**: Enter your name and contact information.
	+ This field is required and must be filled out.
* **Performance Name**: Enter the name of your performance.
	+ This field is required and must be filled out.
* **Preferred Time Slot**: Select your preferred time slot for the performance.
	+ This field is required and must be filled out.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you if we need to discuss your application.
	+ This field is required and must be filled out.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you if we need to discuss your application.
	+ This field is required and must be filled out.
* **Performance Duration**: Enter the length of time your performance will take.
	+ This field is required and must be filled out.
* **Performance Format**: Select all performance formats that will be used in your performance.
	+ This field is required and must be filled out.
* **Technical Needs**: Describe any technical needs you have for your performance.
	+ This field is required and must be filled out.
