# Exhibition Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for exhibition organizers to book a booth at an event. The form collects relevant information about the exhibition, including name, dates, booth type, and contact details, to facilitate the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the exhibition in the `exhibitionName` field.
2. Choose the booth type that suits your needs in the `boothType` field.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the exhibition in the `startDate` and `endDate` fields, respectively.
4. Provide your company name in the `company` field.
5. Enter the representative's name and contact information, including email and phone, in the `representative` fields.
6. Enter any additional comments or notes in the `comments` field.
7. Confirm your booth booking in the `confirmBooking` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibition Name**: Enter the name of the exhibition you want to book a booth for.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the exhibition.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the exhibition.
* **Booth Type**: Choose from the available booth types (Booth 1, Booth 2, or Booth 3).
* **Company**: Enter your company's name.
* **Representative**: Enter the representative's name and contact information.
* **Email**: Enter the representative's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the representative's phone number.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the booking.
* **Confirm Booking**: Confirm your booth booking by selecting "True" or "False" from the options.
