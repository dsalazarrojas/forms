# Artist Dressing Trailer Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from artists and bands to book a trailer for an event or tour.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information and event details.
2. Make sure to choose the correct trailer size and amenities required for your event or tour.
3. Enter the correct event date and location.
4. Provide a valid billing address.
5. Ensure that you have uploaded an insurance certificate if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist or Band Name** (`artist_name`): Enter the name of your artist or band name.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`): Enter the name of the contact person, e.g., manager, tour coordinator, etc.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Event or Tour Name** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event or tour.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event or tour in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Event Location or City** (`event_location`): Enter the location or city where the event or tour will take place.
* **Venue Name** (`venue_name`): Enter the name of the venue where the event or tour will take place.
* **Trailer Setup Date and Time** (`setup_date`): Enter the date and time when the trailer needs to be set up.
* **Trailer Size Required** (`trailer_size`): Select the required trailer size for your event or tour from the list.
* **Number of Rooms or Sections** (`number_of_rooms`): Enter the number of rooms or sections required for the trailer.
* **Expected Number of Personnel** (`personnel_count`): Enter the expected number of personnel that will be staying in the trailer.
* **Amenities Required** (`amenities_needed`): Select the amenities required for the trailer, e.g., restrooms, shower, kitchen, etc.
* **Parking Access Requirements** (`parking_access`): Enter any special parking access requirements.
* **Power and Utilities Required** (`power_utilities`): Select the utilities required for the trailer, e.g., full electrical, water, sewer, internet, etc.
* **Special Requests or Customizations** (`special_requests`): Enter any special requests or customizations required for the trailer.
* **Estimated Return Date** (`return_date`): Enter the estimated return date of the trailer.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`): Enter the billing address for the trailer rental.
* **Insurance Certificate Provided** (`insurance_certificate`): Select if an insurance certificate has been provided.
