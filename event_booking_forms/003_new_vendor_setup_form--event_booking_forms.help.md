# new_vendor_setup_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for setting up a new vendor for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Go through each page, filling in the required fields.
2. Review each page before proceeding to the next.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled in before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Information (Vendor Information)** (`title`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the vendor's name and basic information.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)** (`contact_info`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the vendor's contact information, including email and phone number.
* **Booth Information (Booth Information)** (`booth_info`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter booth-related details such as booth size and type.
* **Event Information (Event Information)** (`event_info`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter event-specific information such as event dates and location.
* **Payment Terms (Payment Terms)** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter payment terms and conditions for the event.
* **Terms and Conditions (Terms and Conditions)** (`terms_and_conditions`, `text`, required):
	+ Read and review the event terms and conditions.
* **Agreement (Agreement)** (`agreement`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select one of the two options to agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Vendor Agreement (Vendor Agreement)** (`vendor_agreement`, `text`, required):
	+ Confirm the vendor's agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Agreement (Agreement)** (`agreement`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select one of the two options to agree to the terms and conditions.

**Tips**

* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submission.
* Review each page carefully before proceeding to the next.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* This form is a requirement for every new vendor setup.
* Review the event terms and conditions carefully before agreeing.
* Confirm your vendor agreement at the final step.
