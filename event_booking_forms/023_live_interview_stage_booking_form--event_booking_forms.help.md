## Live Interview Stage Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to book a live interview stage for a speaker or performer. To ensure accurate and efficient booking, please fill out the form with the required information. 

## How To Complete This Form
To book a live interview stage for a speaker or performer, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field. 
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field. 
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. 
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field. 
5. Choose the date and time for the interview in the "Interview Date" and "Interview Time" fields.
6. Select the available date for the booking in the "Available Date" field.
7. Choose the start and end times for the booking in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields.
8. Enter the duration of the interview in the "Interview Duration" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name. 
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name. 
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address. 
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number. 
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, date, required: false): Choose the date for the interview. 
* **Interview Time** (`interview_time`, time, required: false): Choose the time for the interview. 
* **Available Date** (`available_date`, date, required: false): Select the available date for the booking. 
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required: false): Choose the start time for the booking. 
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required: false): Choose the end time for the booking. 
* **Interview Duration** (`interview_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of the interview in minutes.
