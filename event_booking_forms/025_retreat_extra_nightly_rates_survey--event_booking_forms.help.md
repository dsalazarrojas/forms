# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect event and booking information for retreats, such as preferences and needs of guests.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your retreat title and details.
2. Select the start date of your retreat.
3. Choose the length of your stay.
4. If you have extra nights, specify how many and at what frequency.
5. Enter your preferred nightly rate.
6. Choose your pricing model.
7. Select the desired amenities you would like to have during your retreat.
8. Select any desired services you would like to have during your retreat.
9. Choose how you would like to receive your pricing breakdown.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Retreat Extra Nightly Rates Survey** (`1`, `text`, optional)
This field is a text input where you can enter any additional information about your retreat's nightly rates.

### 2. **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional)
Enter the start date of your retreat.

### 3. **Stay Length** (`stay_length`, `number`, optional)
Enter the length of your stay.

### 4. **Extra Nights** (`extra_nights`, `number`, optional)
If you have extra nights, enter how many.

### 5. **Extra Nights Frequency** (`extra_nights_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Choose the frequency of your extra nights.

### 6. **Preferred Nightly Rate** (`preferred_nightly_rate`, `number`, optional)
Enter your preferred nightly rate.

### 7. **Pricing models** (`pricing_model`, `select_one`, optional)
Choose your pricing model.

### 8. **Desired Amenities** (`desired_amenities`, `note`, optional)
Enter any desired amenities.

### 9. **Desired services** (`desired_services`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select any desired services you would like to have during your retreat.

### 10. **Pricing Breakdown** (`pricing_breakdown`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Choose how you would like to receive your pricing breakdown.
