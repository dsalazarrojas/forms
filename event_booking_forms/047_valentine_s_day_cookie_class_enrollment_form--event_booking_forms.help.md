# valentines_day_cookie_class_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to enroll in a Valentine's Day cookie class, a fun baking event where participants will learn how to bake delicious cookies for loved ones.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your personal details in the Participant section.
2. Select your allergies in the Allergies field.
3. Choose your acceptance of the Terms of Use in the Accepts field.
4. Select the Terms of Agreement you wish to accept in the Terms field.
5. Fill out your address and contact information in the Address and Phone fields.
6. Finally, review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the participant of this event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes. 
* **Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, date, required): Enter your date of birth to ensure age verification.
* **Gender** (`participant_gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender for participant tracking purposes.
* **Age** (`participant_age`, number, optional): Enter your age for participant tracking purposes.
* **Allergies** (`participant_allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies you have, so bakers can take necessary precautions.
* **Baker Name** (`baker_name`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more bakers from the provided list.
* **Baker Phone** (`baker_phone`, text, optional): Enter the baker's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Additional Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes for the bakers.
* **Baker Notes** (`baker_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes for the participant.
* **Accepts** (`participant_accepts`, select_one, required): Accept or decline the terms of use.
* **Baker Accepts** (`baker_accepts`, select_one, required): Accept or decline the terms of use.
* **Terms** (`participant_terms`, select_multiple, required): Select the terms you wish to accept.
* **Baker Terms** (`baker_terms`, select_multiple, required): Select the terms you wish to accept.
* **Address** (`participant_address`, select_multiple, required): Select your address for delivery purposes.
* **Baker Address** (`baker_address`, select_multiple, required): Select the baker's address for delivery purposes.
* **City** (`participant_city`, text, required): Enter your city for delivery purposes.
* **Baker City** (`baker_city`, text, required): Enter the baker's city for delivery purposes.
* **State** (`participant_state`, text, required): Enter your state for delivery purposes.
* **Baker State** (`baker_state`, text, required): Enter the baker's state for delivery purposes.
* **Zip** (`participant_zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code for delivery purposes.
* **Baker Zip** (`baker_zip`, text, required): Enter the baker's zip code for delivery purposes.
* **Country** (`participant_country`, text, required): Enter your country for delivery purposes.
* **Baker Country** (`baker_country`, text, required): Enter the baker's country for delivery purposes.
