# Special Visitor Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Special Visitor Request Form is designed to collect information about special visitors who require specific arrangements for events or meetings within our organization.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Gather all necessary information about the special visitor, including their name, organization, and contact details.
*   Determine the type of visit, if any, and provide a brief description of the event or meeting.
*   Enter the date and number of people attending the event.
*   Add any additional comments if necessary.
*   Confirm that the visit is not already scheduled or approved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (text, optional): Please enter the special visitor's first name.
*   **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter the special visitor's last name.
*   **Organization** (text, optional): Enter the name of the organization the special visitor is associated with.
*   **Phone Number** (number, optional): Enter the special visitor's phone number.
*   **Email** (email, optional): Enter the special visitor's email address.
*   **Type of Visit** (select_one, optional): Select whether the visit is for 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Description of Proposed Event** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of the event or meeting.
*   **Event Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the event or meeting.
*   **Number of People Attending** (number, optional): Enter the number of people attending the event.
*   **Additional Comments** (text, optional): Add any additional comments about the visit.
*   **End Date** (date, optional): Enter the end date of the event or meeting.

Note: Ensure all fields are completed as accurately and thoroughly as possible to facilitate smooth execution of the special visit. If unsure about any information, contact the relevant team.
