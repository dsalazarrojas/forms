# Ticket Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ticket Request Form is used to gather information for event ticket requests. It is designed to collect relevant details from the user to process and manage ticket requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information such as the ticket type, event date and time, and event name.
2. Provide a brief description of the ticket request.
3. Enter the product name, product image, product URL, and product price.
4. Enter the ticket price and quantity.
5. Choose the ticket type from the available options (Event, General, VIP, or Other).
6. Select the event date and time.
7. Enter any additional contact information if necessary.
8. Add any notes or additional comments about the ticket request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ticket Type** (`ticket_type`): Choose the type of ticket you are requesting (Event, General, VIP, or Other).
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name as it should be printed on the ticket.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address for communication and confirmation purposes.
* **Description** (`description`): Provide a brief description of the ticket request.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`): Enter the product image related to the ticket request.
* **Product URL** (`product_url`): Enter the URL associated with the product.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`): Enter the price of the product.
* **Ticket Price** (`ticket_price`): Enter the price of the ticket.
* **Ticket Quantity** (`ticket_quantity`): Enter the number of tickets you are requesting.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Choose the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Choose the time of the event.
* **Organizer** (`organizer`): Enter any contact information for the event organizer.
* **Contact No** (`contact_no`): Enter the contact phone number for the event organizer.
* **Location** (`location`): Note any specific location information.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or notes about the ticket request.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`): Enter the location of the event.
