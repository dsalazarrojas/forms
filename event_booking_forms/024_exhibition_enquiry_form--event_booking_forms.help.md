# Exhibition Enquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect visitor contact information and event booking inquiries for the exhibition.
It is intended for visitors to submit their interest in attending or booking an event, or to provide feedback on the exhibition. Please note that this form will not be used for duplicate submissions if you have already submitted an inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, indicated by an asterisk (\*). This form does not require any information to be filled, so you can submit it as is.

* **exhibition-enquiry-form-1** (`number`, required: false): Please select the page number for your event. This will help us understand which specific event you are inquiring about.
* **exhibition-enquiry-form-2** (`text`, required: false): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on our records.
* **exhibition-enquiry-form-3** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address to ensure that we can contact you about your inquiry. Your email address will not be shared with anyone, we will only use it to contact you about your event booking.
* **exhibition-enquiry-form-4** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you about your event booking. Your phone number will not be shared with anyone, we will only use it to contact you about your event.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your contact information is accurate so that we can effectively contact you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, or if you would like to book an event, contact us directly.
* We will do our best to reply to all inquiries within 3 business days, but please allow up to 7 business days for a response.
