# Artist Collaboration Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Collaboration Session Booking Form is designed to help artists book sessions for collaboration. It gathers necessary information from artists seeking collaboration, ensuring a smooth and productive session.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, ensure you have the form open in a new tab or window.
2.  Fill out the form with the required information, marked as "required" in the form.
3.  For optional fields, you can choose to fill them out or leave them blank, depending on your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Primary Artist Name** (`primary_artist_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the primary artist for the collaboration.
*   **Collaborating Artist Name** (`collaborating_artist`, text, optional): If you are collaborating with another artist, enter their name here.
*   **Email Address** (`artist_email`, email, required): Provide your email address for communication purposes.
*   **Phone Number** (`artist_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for quick contact and scheduling.
*   **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of session you are booking, such as Recording Session, Writing Session, Rehearsal, Mentoring, Brainstorming, or Performance.
*   **Preferred Session Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Select a date for the session, keeping in mind that we will do our best to accommodate your preferred date.
*   **Preferred Session Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required): Choose a time for the session, considering your schedule and availability.
*   **Estimated Session Duration - Hours** (`session_duration`, number, required): Indicate the length of the session in hours.
*   **Session Location or Venue** (`session_location`, text, required): Specify where the session will take place, whether it's online, in-person, or a studio.
*   **Online Platform (if applicable)** (`online_platform`, text, optional): If the session is online, choose the platform you will use, such as Zoom or Google Meet.
*   **Collaboration Goals and Objectives** (`collaboration_goals`, text, required): Describe what you hope to achieve during the collaboration.
*   **Required Equipment or Resources** (`required_equipment`, text, optional): List any specific equipment or resources you'll need for the session.
*   **Music Genre or Art Style** (`music_genre`, text, optional): Specify the genre or style of music you're working with.
*   **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, optional): Indicate your experience level, from Beginner to Professional.
*   **Session Compensation or Fees** (`session_compensation`, number, optional): Enter the fee you are willing to pay or receive for the session.
*   **Intellectual Property Agreement** (`intellectual_property`, select_one, optional): Choose how you'd like to handle intellectual property rights, such as Equal Rights, Lead Artist Rights, or To Be Determined.
*   **References or Previous Collaborations** (`references`, text, optional): Share any relevant references or collaborations you've done before.
*   **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Add any extra information that will help us facilitate the session.
