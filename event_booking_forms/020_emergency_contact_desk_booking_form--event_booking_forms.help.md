# Emergency Contact Desk Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Contact Desk Booking Form is a simple form that allows customers to book a meeting with a staff member at the organization's emergency contact desk. This form collects basic information about the meeting purpose, contact details, and schedule to ensure that the staff member is prepared for the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with the correct information:
   - Book a meeting: Enter the reason for booking the meeting.
   - Contact details: Enter your email address.
   - Location: Enter the location for the meeting.
   - Emergency type: Select the type of emergency (Emergency, Urgent, or Routine).
   - Schedule start time: Choose the start time for the meeting.
   - Schedule start date: Choose the start date for the meeting.
   - Schedule end time: Choose the end time for the meeting.
   - Schedule end date: Choose the end date for the meeting.
   - Contact person: Enter the name of the person you would like to contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Book a meeting
* **Book a meeting** (`Book a meeting`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the reason for booking the meeting. Please provide a brief description of the purpose of the meeting.
### Contact details
* **Contact details** (`Contact details`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you to confirm the meeting.
### Location
* **Location** (`Location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location for the meeting, such as a department or office name.
### Emergency type
* **Emergency type** (`Emergency type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of emergency (Emergency, Urgent, or Routine).
### Schedule start time
* **Schedule start time** (`Schedule start time`, `time`, required/optional): Choose the start time for the meeting in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
### Schedule start date
* **Schedule start date** (`Schedule start date`, `date`, required/optional): Choose the start date for the meeting in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
### Schedule end time
* **Schedule end time** (`Schedule end time`, `time`, required/optional): Choose the end time for the meeting in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
### Schedule end date
* **Schedule end date** (`Schedule end date`, `date`, required/optional): Choose the end date for the meeting in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
### Contact person
* **Contact person** (`Contact person`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person you would like to contact.
## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that the staff member is prepared for the meeting.
* If you have any prior meetings or bookings with us, please refer to those instead of filling this form again.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [contact email].
