# Training Venue Expression Of Interest - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential clients about their interest in booking a training venue. It helps us understand their needs and preferences, which we will use to provide a tailored and suitable venue for their events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the required information about the venue, including its type, capacity, address, and features.
2. Choose the training types and durations that best fit your needs.
3. Enter your contact details, including the contact person, email, phone number, and website (if applicable).
4. Submit the form accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Venue Type** (`Venue Type`, required/true): This field allows you to specify the size of the venue you are looking for. Please select "small", "medium", or "large" from the options provided.
* **Venue Capacity** (`Venue Capacity`, required/False): This field asks for the maximum number of people that can fit in the venue. Please provide the number.
* **Address** (`Address`, required/true): This field requires the address of the venue, including the address lines and postal code.
* **City** (`City`, required/False): The city where the venue is located.
* **State** (`State`, required/False): The state where the venue is located.
* **Postal Code** (`Postal Code`, required/False): The postal code of the venue.
* **Latitude** (`Latitude`, required/False): The latitude of the venue's location.
* **Longitude** (`Longitude`, required/False): The longitude of the venue's location.
* **Venue Features** (`Venue Features`, required/False): This field allows you to select the features of the venue that you are looking for, such as a sound system, projector screen, whiteboard, or wifi.
* **Venue Equipment** (`Venue Equipment`, required/False): This field allows you to select the equipment you need, such as a projector, sound system, or microphone.
* **Capacity** (`Capacity`, required/False): The maximum number of people that can attend your event.
* **Training Types** (`Training Types`, required/true): This field allows you to select the type of training you need, such as a classroom, workshop, or seminar.
* **Training Durations** (`Training Durations`, required/true): This field asks you to select the duration of your training event, such as short or long.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, required/False): The name of the person to contact about your event.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, required/False): The email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, required/False): The phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Website** (`Contact Website`, required/False): The website of the contact person or organization.
* **Contact Social Media** (`Contact Social Media`, required/False): The social media handle of the contact person or organization.
