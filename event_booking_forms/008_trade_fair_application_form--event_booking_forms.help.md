# Trade Fair Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Trade Fair Application Form is designed for businesses to apply for a trade fair. It collects information about the business, products, and contact details to facilitate the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your business name and type in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide detailed information about your products in the "Product Details" field.
3. Write a description of your products in the "Product Description" field.
4. Enter your contact information, including your email, phone number, and address.
5. Select your payment terms (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (text, required): Enter your business name as you would like it to appear on our trade fair promotional materials.
* **Business Type** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the category that best describes your business type. This will help us understand your business model.
* **Business Owner Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the business owner.
* **Product Details** (text, required/optional): Provide detailed information about your products, such as features, benefits, and any unique selling points.
* **Product Description** (text, required/optional): Write a brief description of your products.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your business phone number.
* **Contact Person** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Address** (text, required/optional): Enter your business address.
* **City** (text, required/optional): Enter the city of your business location.
* **Country** (text, required/optional): Enter the country of your business location.
* **Zip Code** (text, required/optional): Enter the zip code of your business location.
* **Fax** (text, required/optional): Enter your business fax number.
* **Website** (text, required/optional): Enter your business website.
* **Application Notes** (text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about your application.
* **Payment Terms** (select_one, required/optional): Choose how you would like to pay for your application fee.
