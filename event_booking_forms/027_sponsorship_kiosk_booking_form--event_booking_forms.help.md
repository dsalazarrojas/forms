# Sponsorship Kiosk Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsorship Kiosk Booking Form is used to book a sponsorship kiosk for an event. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your booking is successfully processed.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please start by filling out the Organization Details page. This will require you to enter some basic information about your organization.
- After submitting your organization details, you will be directed to the preferred Kiosk Location page where you will select the location you would like to book.
- Next, you will be directed to the Booking Date and Time page where you can select your preferred date and time.
- On the Agreement page, you will be asked to agree to the terms and conditions of the kiosk booking.
- Finally, on the Submission page, you can enter any additional comments or information related to your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Details**: Enter your organization's name and contact information. This will help us verify your organization and ensure that your booking is correctly attributed to you.
* **Preferred Kiosk Location**: Select the location you would like to book the kiosk. Please note that each location has its own unique features and availability.
* **Booking Date and Time**: Select a date and time that works best for your organization. Please keep in mind that the kiosk is subject to availability at the selected time.
* **Agreement**: Please select "Agree" to indicate that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the kiosk booking.
* **Terms and Conditions**: If you have any additional comments or concerns about the kiosk booking, please enter them here.
* **Submission**: Enter any additional information related to your booking. This will help us understand your specific needs and preferences.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions before submitting your booking.
- Make sure to select the correct preferred kiosk location that fits your needs.
- If you have any issues or concerns with the form, please contact our support team.
