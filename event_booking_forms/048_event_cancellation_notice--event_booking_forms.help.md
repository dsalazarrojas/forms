# Event Cancellation Notice - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Cancellation Notice form is used to document the cancellation of an event and provide a clear reason for the cancellation. This information will be used to communicate with stakeholders and ensure that all parties are informed about the cancellation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for the event cancellation notice.
2. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the event planners were involved in the cancellation decision.
3. Provide a reason for the cancellation.
4. Indicate whether stakeholders should be notified about the cancellation.
5. Enter the date of the cancelled event.
6. Enter the scheduled time of the cancelled event.
7. Add any additional details about the cancellation.
8. (Optional) Provide a note with any additional information or documentation.
9. Enter the email address of the contact person.
10. Enter the contact phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Cancellation Title** (`event_cancellation`, text, Required): Enter a brief title for the event cancellation notice.
* **Event Planners** (`prophets`, select_multiple, Optional): Select 'Yes' if the event planners were involved in the cancellation decision.
* **Reason for Cancellation** (`event_cancellation_reason`, text, Required): Provide a clear and concise reason for the cancellation.
* **Notify Stakeholders** (`notify_stakeholders`, select_multiple, Optional): Select 'Yes' if stakeholders should be notified about the cancellation.
* **Date of Cancellation** (`prophets`, date, Required): Enter the date of the cancelled event.
* **Scheduled Time** (`scheduled_time`, time, Required): Enter the scheduled time of the cancelled event.
* **Cancellation Details** (`cancellation_details`, text, Required): Add any additional details about the cancellation.
* **Documentation** (`documentation`, note, Optional): (Optional) Provide a note with any additional information or documentation.
* **Contact Email Address** (`email_address`, email, Required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_number`, text, Required): Enter the contact phone number.
