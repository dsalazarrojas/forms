# Barnhouse Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Barnhouse Signup Form is designed for individuals who wish to join the Barnhouse community. It collects information necessary for communication, membership, and event planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow the step-by-step instructions:

1. Scroll through each section of the form, reading the explanations and options carefully.
2. Fill in the required fields, indicated by the asterisk (\*) symbol.
3. Select the relevant options for each field.
4. Review your selections before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (*): This field is used to collect your full name for our records.
	+ Please enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (*): This field is used for email communications from the Barnhouse.
	+ Enter a valid email address to stay updated on events and news.
* **Mobile Number** (Optional): This field is for SMS alerts from the Barnhouse.
	+ Enter your mobile number for occasional event reminders.
* **Mobile Number**: If you have an existing relationship with the Barnhouse, you may have already received a welcome message or event updates.
* **Areas of Interest**: Select all the areas that interest you.
	+ Pick at least one option to indicate your interest in Barnhouse events and activities.
* **Volunteer Interest**: Indicate your interest in volunteering at the farm.
	+ Choose "Yes" to participate in volunteer activities.
* **Membership Type**: Select your preferred membership tier.
	+ Choose from four options: Free Community Member, Friend of the Farm, Farm Family, or Barnhouse Patron.
* **Communication Frequency**: Choose how often you'd like to hear from us.
	+ Select from: Weekly Highlights, Monthly Newsletter, or Only Major Event Alerts.
* **Newsletter Format**: Choose your preferred format for receiving newsletters.
	+ Select between HTML with images or plain text format.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code to help us plan local events.
	+ This information is optional but helps us tailor events to your area.
* **Referral Source**: Tell us how you heard about the Barnhouse.
	+ Choose from: A Friend/Family, Social Media, Local Newspaper, Drove by the property, or Online Search.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any feedback or suggestions you may have.
	+ This is an opportunity to share your thoughts on how we can improve our services.
* **Privacy and Consent**: Read and agree to our privacy and communication policies.
	+ Review the policies and choose "I Agree" to complete the sign-up process.
* **Signup Date**: This is the date you sign up for the Barnhouse community.
	+ This field is automatically generated and cannot be changed.
