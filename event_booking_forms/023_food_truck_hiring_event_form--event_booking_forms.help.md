# Food Truck Hiring Event Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for food truck vendors to apply for or participate in events hosted by event organizers. It collects information about the applicant, event details, food truck information, and event terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information about yourself as the applicant.
2. Provide details about the event you are applying for or participating in.
3. Enter information about your food truck, including its license number and any special requests.
4. Confirm your agreement to the event terms and conditions.
5. Ensure you understand and agree to the payment terms.
6. Confirm that you have read and understand the event terms and conditions.
7. Fill out the payment method and terms for your food truck.
8. Sign and date the form to confirm your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, required): Fill in your name and contact information.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): Enter the event name, date, and time.
* **Food Truck Information** (`food_truck_info`, text, required): Enter your food truck's name and license number.
* **Event Organizer Information** (`event_organizer_info`, text, required): Enter the event organizer's name and contact information.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the event location.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Select the time of the event.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Food Truck License** (`food_truck_license`, text, required): Enter your food truck's license number.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, text, required): Enter any special requests you have for the event.
* **Event Terms** (`event_terms`, note, required): Read and confirm that you understand the event terms and conditions.
* **Applicant Signature** (`applicant_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm your participation.
* **Event Organizer Signature** (`event_organizer_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm the event organizer's participation.
* **Event Agreement** (`event_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the event terms and conditions.
* **Event Organizer Agreement** (`event_organizer_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the event terms and conditions.
* **Event Terms Acknowledgement** (`event_terms_acknowledgement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and understand the event terms and conditions.
* **Event Agreed** (`event_agreed`, select_multiple, required): Confirm that you have agreed to the event terms and conditions.
* **Event Organizer Agreed** (`event_organizer_agreed`, select_multiple, required): Confirm that you have agreed to the event terms and conditions.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method for your food truck.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand and agree to the payment terms.
* **Food Truck Payment Method** (`food_truck_payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method for your food truck.
* **Food Truck Payment Terms** (`food_truck_payment_terms`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand and agree to the payment terms for your food truck.
* **Food Truck Terms** (`food_truck_terms`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and understand the food truck terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Read and understand all the event and payment terms and conditions.
* Sign and date the form to confirm your participation and agreement.
