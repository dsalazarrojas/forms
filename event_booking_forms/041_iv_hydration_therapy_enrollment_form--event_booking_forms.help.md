# IV Hydration Therapy Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IV Hydration Therapy Enrollment Form is designed to gather information from patients or their representatives regarding their medical history, treatment details, and other relevant data. This form will help medical professionals and caregivers to assess the patient's condition, identify potential risks, and develop a personalized treatment plan.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure that you understand the purpose and the questions being asked.
2.  Fill in the required information accurately and completely.
3.  Provide as much detail as possible for each question.
4.  Review your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: This section is used to capture the patient's basic information, such as their name, contact details, and emergency contact information. Please fill in the following:
	+ **Patient Name**: The patient's full name as it appears on their government-issued ID.
	+ **Date of Birth**: The patient's date of birth.
	+ **Phone Number**: The patient's phone number.
	+ **Email**: The patient's email address.
	+ **Emergency Contact Name**: The name of the emergency contact person.
	+ **Relationship to Patient**: The relationship of the emergency contact person to the patient.
	+ **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: The emergency contact person's phone number.
	+ **Emergency Contact Email**: The emergency contact person's email address.
* **Treatment Details**: This section is used to collect information about the patient's treatment details. Please fill in the following:
	+ **Medical Condition**: A brief description of the patient's medical condition.
	+ **Diagnosis Date**: The date of the patient's diagnosis.
	+ **Current Medications**: A list of the patient's current medications.
* **Medical History**: This section is used to collect the patient's medical history. Please fill in the following:
	+ **Previous Surgeries**: A list of the patient's previous surgeries.
	+ **Allergies**: A list of the patient's allergies.
* **Treatment Administration**: This section is used to collect information about the patient's treatment administration. Please fill in the following:
	+ **Treatment Start Date**: The start date of the patient's treatment.
	+ **Treatment End Date**: The end date of the patient's treatment.
* **Medication Allergies**: This section is used to collect information about the patient's medication allergies. Please fill in the following:
	+ **Medication Allergies**: A list of the patient's medication allergies.
* **Laboratory Test Results**: This section is used to collect the patient's laboratory test results. Please fill in the following:
	+ **Test Date**: The date of the patient's laboratory test.
	+ **Test Results**: The results of the patient's laboratory test.
* **Vital Signs**: This section is used to collect the patient's vital signs. Please fill in the following:
	+ **Temperature**: The patient's body temperature in degrees Celsius.
	+ **Blood Pressure**: The patient's blood pressure in millimeters of mercury (mmHg).
	+ **Heart Rate**: The patient's heart rate in beats per minute.
	+ **Respiratory Rate**: The patient's respiratory rate in breaths per minute.
	+ **Oxygen Saturation**: The patient's oxygen saturation level.
* **Medication Administration Plan**: This section is used to collect information about the patient's medication administration plan. Please fill in the following:
	+ **Medication Administration Plan**: A description of the patient's medication administration plan.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any question, please consult with a medical professional.
* This form is intended for medical use only and should not be used for other purposes.
