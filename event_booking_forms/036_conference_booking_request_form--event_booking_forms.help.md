# Conference Booking Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Conference Booking Request Form is a template used to collect necessary details for conference booking requests. It is used for submitting new conference event requests, ensuring that all relevant information is provided for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and title in the "Requester name" field.
2. Enter the name of your organization or department for the "Organization name" field.
3. Specify the title of your conference event in the "Event name" field.
4. Provide the start and end dates of your conference event in the "Event dates" field.
5. Estimate the expected number of participants for "Expected attendance".
6. Specify any catering requirements or dietary restrictions in the "Catering requirements" field.
7. Choose the preferred room setup for your conference event (Theater, Classroom, Banquet, or Boardroom) in the "Room setup preference" field.
8. If necessary, mention any audio-visual requirements such as projectors, microphones, or recording needs in the "Audio visual requirements" field.
9. Provide billing contact information for "Billing contact information" if required.
10. Select the current approval status of your request (Pending, Approved, or Rejected) in the "Approval status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name (Requester name, text, required):** Enter your name and title to associate your identity with the conference booking request.
* **Organization name (Organization name, text, optional):** Type in your organization's name or department to identify the hosting entity.
* **Event name (Event name, text, required):** Provide a clear title for your conference event to be recognized and organized accordingly.
* **Event dates (Event dates, text, required):** Include both the start and end dates of your conference event for proper scheduling.
* **Expected attendance (Expected attendance, number, required):** Estimate the number of participants expected to attend your conference event.
* **Catering requirements (Catering requirements, text, optional):** Specify any meal or dietary needs for your conference event attendees.
* **Room setup preference (Room setup preference, select_one, required):** Choose a suitable room setup for your conference event (Theater, Classroom, Banquet, or Boardroom).
* **Audio visual requirements (Audio visual requirements, text, optional):** Mention any necessary audio-visual equipment for your conference event.
* **Billing contact information (Billing contact information, text, optional):** Provide contact details for billing and payment purposes.
* **Approval status (Approval status, select_one, optional):** Mark the current status of your conference event request (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
