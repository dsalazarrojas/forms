# Finger Food Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Finger Food Event Registration" form is designed to gather information from attendees who are RSVPing for an event. This form helps event organizers plan and prepare for the attendees' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide the event details (e.g., Event Details and Event Details 1-2) as required.
2. Indicate your RSVP status (RSVP) to let the organizers know if you can attend the event.
3. Enter your name to help identify you as an attendee.
4. Provide your email address so the organizers can contact you.
5. Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
6. Choose your dietary preferences (Vegetarian, Vegan, or Meat-eater) from the given options.
7. If you have any dietary restrictions, please enter them in the corresponding text fields (Dietary Restrictions 1-4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`event_details`): Enter the name of the event you are RSVPing for.
* **Event Details 1** (`event_details_1`): Provide any additional information about the event (e.g., location, date, time).
* **Event Details 2** (`event_details_2`): Enter any further details about the event (e.g., event description, agenda).
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`): Choose "True" to confirm your attendance or "False" if you are unable to attend.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name as you would like to be referred to for the event.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter a valid email address so the organizers can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
* **Dietary Preferences** (`dietary_preferences`): Select your dietary preferences from the given options (Vegetarian, Vegan, or Meat-eater).
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`): Enter any specific dietary restrictions or requests you have.
* **Dietary Restrictions 1-4** (`dietary_restrictions_1-4`): Enter any additional dietary restrictions or requests if needed.

## Tips
* Please ensure your RSVP status is accurate to help the organizers plan for the event.
* If you have specific dietary restrictions, please provide as much detail as possible to ensure the organizers can accommodate your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the organizers directly.
