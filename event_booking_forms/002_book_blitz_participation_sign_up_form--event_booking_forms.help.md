# Book Blitz Participation Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Book Blitz Participation Sign-Up Form is designed to collect essential information from participants about their interest in attending the Book Blitz event. This form helps us understand who is attending, their role, and their preferences for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you've filled out all required fields.
2. Enter your first and last name in the **Full Name** field.
3. Provide your email address for us to send event details and updates.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it for urgent event communications.
5. If you represent an organization, enter its name and your role/title within it.
6. Select how you'd like to participate in the event (Attendee, Speaker, Vendor, Sponsor, Volunteer, or Media).
7. Choose your preferred event date.
8. If you're bringing a group, specify the number of people attending.
9. If you need a vendor booth, select 'Yes' and choose your preferred booth size.
10. Mention any dietary requirements for event catering.
11. Indicate if you need accommodation assistance.
12. Select the sessions you plan to attend.
13. Choose how you'd like to receive marketing updates.
14. Enter any special requests or comments.
15. Select your preferred payment method.
16. If you need to provide emergency contact information, enter their full name and phone number.
17. Finally, confirm agreement with the event terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (participant_name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (email_address, email, required): The email address we'll use to send event details and updates.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, optional): Enter your phone number for urgent event communications.
* **Organization or Company** (organization_name, text, optional): If you represent an organization, enter its name and your role/title within it.
* **Your Role or Title** (role_title, text, optional): Describe your role or title within the organization.
* **Type of Participation** (participation_type, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to participate (Attendee, Speaker, etc.).
* **Preferred Event Date** (event_date_preference, select_one, required): Select your preferred event date.
* **Number of Attendees** (number_of_attendees, number, required): Enter the number of people attending from your group.
* **Do you need a vendor booth?** (booth_required, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you need a vendor booth.
* **Preferred Booth Size** (booth_size, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred booth size (Small, Medium, Large, or Extra Large).
* **Dietary Requirements** (dietary_requirements, select_multiple, optional): Select any special dietary needs for event catering (Vegetarian, Vegan, etc.).
* **Do you need accommodation assistance?** (accommodation_needed, select_one, required): Indicate if you need accommodation assistance.
* **Sessions of Interest** (session_interests, select_multiple, optional): Select the sessions you plan to attend.
* **Receive marketing updates** (marketing_opt_in, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to receive marketing updates (Yes please, No thanks).
* **Special Requests or Comments** (special_requests, text, optional): Enter any additional information we should know about you.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (payment_method, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (emergency_contact_name, text, optional): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (emergency_contact_phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **I agree to the event terms and conditions** (terms_agreement, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement with the event terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Be accurate and honest when filling out the form to ensure we can tailor the event to your needs.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask us for clarification.
* We'll contact you with further instructions and details about the event after reviewing your form submission.
