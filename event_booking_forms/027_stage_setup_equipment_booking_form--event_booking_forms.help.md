# Stage Setup Equipment Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stage Setup Equipment Booking Form is used to book and schedule the use of stage setup equipment for a specific period. This form is essential for ensuring that the equipment is available when needed and for tracking usage and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by a (*) symbol.
2. For each field, refer to the field description below for more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. *Booking Details* (`stage_setup_equipment_booking_form_1`, text, optional)
 Enter a brief description of your booking request.

### 2. *Select Equipment* (`stage_setup_equipment_booking_form_2`, select_multiple, optional)
 Choose the equipment you wish to book by selecting all the required options.

### 3. *Start Date* (`stage_setup_equipment_booking_form_3`, date, optional)
 Enter the start date of your booking period.

### 4. *End Date* (`stage_setup_equipment_booking_form_4`, date, optional)
 Enter the end date of your booking period.

### 5. *Start Time* (`stage_setup_equipment_booking_form_5`, time, optional)
 Enter the start time of your booking period.

### 6. *End Time* (`stage_setup_equipment_booking_form_6`, time, optional)
 Enter the end time of your booking period.

### 7. *Booking Type* (`stage_setup_equipment_booking_form_7`, select_one, optional)
 Choose whether you are booking the equipment for a 'Yes' or 'No' purpose.

### 8. *Number of Users* (`stage_setup_equipment_booking_form_8`, number, optional)
 Enter the expected number of users for your booking.

### 9. *User Names* (`stage_setup_equipment_booking_form_9`, text, optional)
 Enter the names of users booked for the equipment.

## Tips
- Be accurate when selecting the equipment and booking dates to avoid conflicts or overbooking.
- Make sure to provide all necessary details to ensure a smooth booking process.
