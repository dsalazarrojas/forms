# exhibition_wi_fi_access_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from exhibitors for Wi-Fi access booking at exhibitions. The form ensures that all necessary details are captured to facilitate smooth Wi-Fi access provision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Exhibitor Details section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. In the Device Details section, specify the type and number of devices you will be bringing to the exhibition.
3. Read and agree to the Usage Agreement to understand the terms and conditions of Wi-Fi access.
4. Finally, fill in the Wi-Fi Access Booking section with your required Wi-Fi access dates and duration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibitor Details** (`exhibitor_details`, `text`, required: false): This section collects your contact information and any other relevant details that we need to know about you as an exhibitor.
* **Device Details** (`device_details`, `text`, required: false): Specify the type and number of devices you will be bringing to the exhibition.
* **Usage Agreement** (`usage_agreement`, `text`, required: false): This is a crucial section where you read and agree to the usage agreement to ensure you understand the terms and conditions of Wi-Fi access.
* **Wi-Fi Access Booking** (`wi_fi_access_booking`, `text`, required: false): Provide your required Wi-Fi access dates and duration in this section.
