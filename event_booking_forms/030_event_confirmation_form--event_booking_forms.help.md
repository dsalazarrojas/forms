# event_confirmation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **event_confirmation_form** is a simple form designed to collect event information from users. This form helps you gather essential details about events, such as date, time, and name.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the **event date** field and enter the date of the event.
2. Next, select the **event time** field and enter the time of the event.
3. Lastly, enter the **event name** in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event. For example, 2024-02-15 or 2024-02-15 08:00.
* **event time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event. For example, 08:00 or 14:00.
* **event name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event. Be as specific as possible for better reference.

## Tips
- Please ensure you have entered the correct date and time to avoid any confusion.
- If you need help or have questions, feel free to ask our support team for assistance.
- You can save your progress and come back later to complete the form if needed.
- If you need to make any changes, simply edit and resubmit the form.
