# Beauty Service Event Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Beauty Service Event Agreement Form" is a document that helps ensure a successful service event between the client and the service provider.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form carefully and accurately, making sure to include all required fields.
2.  If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the client for whom the service event is being held.
*   **Date of Event** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the service event.
*   **Type of Event** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of event from the dropdown list: Wedding, Photoshoot, Fashion Show, Corporate Event, Private Party, or Other.
*   **Event Location Address** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the address of the event location.
*   **Service Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the time at which the service will start.
*   **Required Completion Time** (`completion_time`, time, required): Enter the time by which the service should be completed.
*   **Number of Hair Services** (`hair_services_count`, number, required): Enter the number of hair services provided.
*   **Number of Makeup Services** (`makeup_services_count`, number, required): Enter the number of makeup services provided.
*   **Specific Service Details or Requirements** (`specific_service_notes`, text, optional): If there are any specific services or requirements that need to be noted, enter them here.
*   **Total Quoted Amount** (`total_quoted_amount`, number, required): Enter the total amount quoted for the services provided.
*   **Deposit Amount Paid** (`deposit_paid`, number, required): Enter the deposit amount paid.
*   **Remaining Balance Due** (`remaining_balance`, number, required): Enter the remaining balance due.
*   **Balance Due Date** (`balance_due_date`, date, required): Enter the date by which the balance is due.
*   **I acknowledge and agree to any applicable travel fees** (`travel_fee_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Choose I Agree.
*   **I have read and agree to the event cancellation terms** (`cancellation_terms_event`, select_one, required): Choose I Agree.
*   **Authorized Client Name for Signature** (`client_signature_name`, text, required): Enter the authorized client's name for signature.
*   **Date Signed** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of signature.

Note: Make sure to sign and date the form after you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
