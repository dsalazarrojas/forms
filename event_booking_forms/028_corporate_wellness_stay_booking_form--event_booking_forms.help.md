# Corporate Wellness Stay Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help corporate employees book wellness events and manage related payments. By providing the required information, you will be able to book your spot in a wellness event and make payment arrangements.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form fields and provide the required information.
2. Ensure you have provided all the necessary details to process your booking and payment.
3. Review and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page**: This page is the initial page where you can provide the employee info required for the booking process.
* **Employee Info**: Please enter your employee information, such as name, title, or any other relevant details. This information will help the wellness team identify who is booking the event.
* **Contact**: In this field, please enter a contact method for communication purposes. This can be a phone number, email, or any other contact method.
* **Event Booking Info**: Provide a brief description about the event you wish to book, including the reason for your attendance.
* **Event Booking Details**: Enter a detailed description of the event you wish to book, including the type of wellness activities and amenities you expect.
* **Event Booking Dates**: Specify the dates you are available for the event.
* **Event Booking Payment**: Provide your payment information, including the payment method and amount.
* **Event Booking Total**: This field will display the total cost of the event.
* **Form Submitted**: This field will be auto-completed once you have submitted the form.
## Tips

* Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have provided all the necessary information to process your booking and payment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the wellness team for assistance.
