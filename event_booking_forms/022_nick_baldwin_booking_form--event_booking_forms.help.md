# nick_baldwin_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `nick_baldwin_booking_form` is a form used to collect event booking information from users. It is designed to be simple and easy to use while capturing relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: A text field where you can enter your first name.
* **Last name**: A text field where you can enter your last name.
* **Email**: An email field where you can enter your email address.
* **Phone**: A text field where you can enter your phone number.
* **Message**: A text field where you can enter any additional comments or details.
* **Services**: A multiple select field where you can choose from `panel discussion`, `musical performance`, and `event speaker` options.
* **Assigned tool**: A single select field where you can choose from `chatjimmy` or `Other` options.
* **Prophets**: A single select field where you can choose from `prophets` or `Other` options.
* **Event**: A single select field where you can choose from `event` or `Other` options.
* **Service**: A single select field where you can choose from `service` or `Other` options.
* **Description**: A text field where you can enter a brief description of the event.
* **Description 2**: Another text field where you can enter additional details about the event.
* **Event**: This field is not clearly labeled, but it seems to be related to an event or category.
* **Service**: This field is also not clearly labeled, but it seems to be related to a service or category.
