# Cocktail Menu Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Cocktail Menu Selection" form is designed to help event planners collect guest information and preferences for an event. This guide will walk you through each section of the form and provide explanations for each field.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name and email address in the "Guest Information" section.
2. Enter the name and date of the event in the "Event Details" section.
3. Choose your primary and secondary cocktail preferences in the "Drink Selection" section.
4. Select your flavor profile, sweetness level, and alcohol strength preferences in the "Flavor Profile" section.
5. Choose a non-alcoholic option if applicable.
6. Select any dietary restrictions in the "Dietary Considerations" section.
7. List any food allergies or special requests in the "Additional Comments" section.
8. Enter the number of servings you would like in the "Quantities" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name**: Enter your full name in this field. This is a required field.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address. This is a required field.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event where you will be attending. This is a required field.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event. This is a required field.
* **Primary Cocktail Choice**: Select your first preference for a cocktail. You can choose one of the following options: Margarita, Mojito, Old Fashioned, Cosmopolitan, Martini, Daiquiri, Sangria, or Other.
* **Secondary Cocktail Choice**: Select your second preference for a cocktail. You can choose one of the following options: Margarita, Mojito, Old Fashioned, Cosmopolitan, Martini, Daiquiri, Sangria, or Other.
* **Preferred Spirit Type**: Select the type of spirit you prefer. You can choose one of the following options: Vodka, Gin, Rum, Tequila, Whiskey, Brandy, or No preference.
* **Flavor Profile**: Select all the flavor profiles you prefer. You can choose from the following options: Fruity, Sweet, Sour, Herbal, Spicy, Smoky, Tropical.
* **Sweetness Level Preference**: Select your preferred level of sweetness. You can choose one of the following options: Very sweet, Sweet, Balanced, Dry, Very dry.
* **Alcohol Strength Preference**: Select your preferred level of alcohol strength. You can choose one of the following options: Light, Moderate, Strong, No preference.
* **Non-Alcoholic Drink Selection**: If you prefer a non-alcoholic option, select one of the following: Mocktail version of choice, Sparkling water, Juice blend, Soda, Coffee drink, Tea, Other.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select any dietary restrictions you have. You can choose from the following options: Gluten-free, Vegan, Dairy-free, Nut-free, Sugar-free, Alcohol-free, None.
* **Allergies**: List any ingredient allergies you have.
* **Number of Servings**: Enter the number of servings you would like.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests or customizations you may have.
