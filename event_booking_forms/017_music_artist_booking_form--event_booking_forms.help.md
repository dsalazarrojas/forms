# Music Artist Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for booking music artists for events. It will be reviewed by event staff to process your request and ensure a smooth event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the event you're booking the music artist for in the **event_booking** field.
2. Enter the name of the music artist in the **music_artist** field.
3. Select the date of the event in the **event_date** field. Please use the format mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Choose the start time of the event in the **start_time** field. Please use the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
5. Choose the end time of the event in the **end_time** field. Please use the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
6. Provide any promotional information (e.g., social media links, posters) that may be useful for promoting the event in the **promotional_info** field.
7. Include any relevant artist information (e.g., bio, equipment requirements) in the **artist_info** field. This is a free-text field, so feel free to write as much or as little as you need.
8. Enter the contact number of the person or team managing the event in the **contact_number** field.
9. Enter the email of the person or team managing the event in the **email** field.
10. Enter any applicable artist fee in the **artist_fee** field. This should be a monetary amount.
11. If applicable, enter any additional information about the artist fee in the **artist_fee_2** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_booking** (text, event_booking, required): Enter the name of the event you're booking the music artist for.
* **music_artist** (text, music_artist, required): Enter the name of the music artist.
* **event_date** (date, event_date, required): Select the date of the event in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **start_time** (time, start_time, required): Choose the start time of the event in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **end_time** (time, end_time, required): Choose the end time of the event in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **promotional_info** (text, promotional_info, required): Provide any promotional information about the event, such as social media links or poster images.
* **artist_info** (note, artist_info, required): Include any relevant information about the music artist, such as their bio or equipment requirements.
* **contact_number** (text, contact_number, required): Enter the contact number of the person or team managing the event.
* **email** (email, email, required): Enter the email of the person or team managing the event.
* **artist_fee** (number, artist_fee, required): Enter any applicable artist fee as a monetary amount.
* **artist_fee_2** (text, artist_fee_2, required): Enter any additional information about the artist fee.
