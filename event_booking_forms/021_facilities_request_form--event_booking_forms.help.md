# Facilities Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for event bookings in our facilities. The form is used to request space and resources for your event, and to provide contact details and event specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter the event name and date.
3. Specify the start and end times for the event.
4. Indicate the number of people, chairs, and tables you will need.
5. Choose the type of request link for your event (Tech Arts or Other).
6. Provide a brief description of the event and any special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* **Contact Name**: Enter your full name to ensure we can contact you about your event.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can communicate with you about your event.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number, including the country code, so we can reach you.
### Contact Name
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name, which will be displayed on our event records.
### Contact Email
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your event.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, including the country code, so we can reach you.
### Event Information
* **Event Name**: Enter a brief name for your event.
* **Event Start Date**: Enter the date you would like the event to start.
* **Event Start Time**: Enter the time you would like the event to start.
* **Event End Date**: Enter the date you would like the event to end.
* **Event End Time**: Enter the time you would like the event to end.
### Capacity
* **Number of People**: Enter the total number of people you expect to attend your event.
* **Number of Chairs**: Enter the number of chairs you will need.
* **Number of Tables**: Enter the number of tables you will need.
### Request Details
* **External Form Link**: Choose the type of request link for your event:
	+ **Tech Arts Request Form**: If you are requesting a tech setup, select this option.
	+ **Other**: If you are requesting something else, select this option.
* **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of your event.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests you may have for your event.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your contact information and event details before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests, please include them in the "Special Requests" field.
* If you are requesting a tech setup, please select "Tech Arts Request Form" for the external link.
