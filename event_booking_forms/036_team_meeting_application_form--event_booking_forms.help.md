# Team Meeting Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to gather information for team meeting applications and bookings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the link provided to access the form.
2. Fill in the required fields as prompted, ensuring accuracy and relevance.
3. Select at least one meeting time that suits you best.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Meeting Application Form**: This is a heading to identify the form's purpose.
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed for the meeting.
* **Applicant ID** (`applicant_id`, number, not required): Enter a unique identifier for yourself. This can be your Employee ID or a departmental ID.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, not required): Select the date you would like to hold your meeting. If a specific date is not provided, a default date may be assigned.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, not required): Enter your email address for communication purposes. This will help the meeting organizer contact you.
* **Select Meeting Times** (`options_select_multiple_0`, select_multiple, required): Select at least one meeting time that suits you best from the provided options: 09:00, 09:30, 10:00, 10:30, 11:00, 11:30, 12:00, 12:30, 13:00, 13:30, 14:00, 14:30, 15:00, 15:30, 16:00, 16:30, 17:00, 17:30, 18:00, 18:30, 19:00, 19:30, 20:00, 20:30, 21:00, 21:30, 22:00, 22:30.
