# Innovation Leadership Stay Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to book a stay at an innovation leadership program. Please fill out the form with your preferences and needs for the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for the question "Event Booking Form" to indicate whether you have any upcoming events during the stay.
2. Enter your guest details in the "Guest Details" field. Please provide your name, email, and any other relevant information about your guests.
3. If you have any special requirements, please provide them in the "Special Requirements" field. This could be anything from dietary restrictions to mobility needs.
4. If there is a specific program organizer you are looking to work with, please provide their name in the "Program Organizer" field.
5. Select a date for your booking in the "Booking Date" field.
6. Select a time for your booking in the "Booking Time" field.
7. If you have any additional special requirements, please provide them in the "Special Requirements 2", "Special Requirements 3", and "Special Requirements 4" fields.
8. Please review all fields carefully before submitting the form to ensure you have provided all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Booking Form** (`Event Booking Form`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field asks whether you have any upcoming events during your stay. Please select "Yes" if you do have events and "No" if you do not.
* **Guest Details** (`Guest Details`, text, required/optional): This field is where you can provide any additional information about your guests.
* **Special Requirements** (`Special Requirements`, text, required/optional): This field is for any special requirements or needs you may have during your stay.
* **Program Organizer** (`Program Organizer`, text, required/optional): If you have a specific program organizer you would like to work with, please provide their name here.
* **Booking Date** (`Booking Date`, date, required/optional): Select a date for your booking.
* **Booking Time** (`Booking Time`, time, required/optional): Select a time for your booking.
* **Special Requirements 2** (`Special Requirements 2`, text, required/optional): This field is for any additional special requirements you may have.
* **Special Requirements 3** (`Special Requirements 3`, text, required/optional): This field is for any additional special requirements you may have.
* **Special Requirements 4** (`Special Requirements 4`, text, required/optional): This field is for any additional special requirements you may have.
