# Student Housing Allocation Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book student housing accommodations. It is essential to fill in the form accurately to ensure a smooth booking process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your first name.
2. Fill in your last name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Fill in your contact number.
5. Select your gender.
6. Choose your preferred accommodation type.
7. Choose your preferred room type.
8. Fill in your preferred accommodation location.
9. Fill in your student ID.
10. Fill in your student number.
11. Select the accommodation start date and time.
12. Select the accommodation end date and time.
13. Select your booking status.
14. Add any additional comments.
15. Fill in the date you made the accommodation request.
16. Fill in the reason for your accommodation request.
17. Fill in any special requests.
18. Fill in the accommodation status update.
19. Fill in any other accommodation requests.
20. Click on the submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Fill in your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Fill in your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Fill in your contact number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Select your date of birth.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred accommodation type.
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred room type.
* **Preferred Accommodation** (`preferred_accommodation`, text, optional): Fill in your preferred accommodation.
* **Preferred Location** (`preferred_location`, text, optional): Fill in your preferred location.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): Fill in your student ID.
* **Student Number** (`student_number`, number, optional): Fill in your student number.
* **Accommodation Start Date** (`accommodation_start_date`, date, optional): Select the date you want to start your accommodation.
* **Accommodation Start Time** (`accommodation_start_time`, time, optional): Select the time you want to start your accommodation.
* **Accommodation End Date** (`accommodation_end_date`, date, optional): Select the date you want to end your accommodation.
* **Accommodation End Time** (`accommodation_end_time`, time, optional): Select the time you want to end your accommodation.
* **Booking Status** (`booking_status`, select_one, optional): Select your booking status.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments.
* **Accommodation Request Date** (`accommodation_request_date`, date, optional): Fill in the date you made the accommodation request.
* **Accommodation Request Reason** (`accommodation_request_reason`, text, optional): Fill in the reason for your accommodation request.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Fill in any special requests.
* **Accommodation Status Update** (`accommodation_status_update`, text, optional): Fill in the accommodation status update.
* **Accommodation Type Request** (`accommodation_type_request`, text, optional): Fill in any other accommodation requests.
* **Other Accommodation Request** (`other_accommodation_request`, text, optional): Fill in any other accommodation requests.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to complete the form.

Note: All fields marked as optional are not required to be filled in, but it is recommended to fill in as much information as possible to ensure accurate accommodation booking.
