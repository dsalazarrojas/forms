# Stargazing Experience Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides a step-by-step explanation of the Stargazing Experience Registration form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the participant information section, including the participant's name, age range, gender, email, phone number, and agreement to the event policies.
2.  Provide details about the event, such as the title, start and end dates, time, location, and description.
3.  Enter observer information, including their name, email, and phone number.
4.  List any equipment and policies for the stargazing event.
5.  Enter any additional notes for the participant and observer.
6.  Provide session details, such as session time and date.
7.  Enter the number of participants and the event category.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant.
*   **Event Title**: Enter the title of the stargazing event.
*   **Start Date**: Enter the date of the stargazing event.
*   **End Date**: Enter the end date of the stargazing event.
*   **Event Time**: Enter the time of the stargazing event.
*   **Observer Name**: Enter the name of the observer.
*   **Observer Email**: Enter the email of the observer.
*   **Observer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the observer.
*   **Equipment Details**: List any equipment and policies for the stargazing event.
*   **Policies and Rules**: List any policies and rules for the stargazing event.
*   **Event Description**: Enter a description of the stargazing event.
*   **Event Location**: Enter the location of the stargazing event.
*   **Session Details**: List session details, such as session time and date.
*   **Number of Participants**: Enter the number of participants.
*   **Age Range**: Enter the age range of the participants.
*   **Gender**: Select the gender of the participant.
*   **Participant Notes**: Enter any additional notes for the participant.
*   **Observer Notes**: Enter any additional notes for the observer.
*   **Session Start Time**: Enter the start time of the session.
*   **Session End Time**: Enter the end time of the session.
*   **Session Date**: Enter the date of the session.
*   **Participant Email**: Enter the email of the participant.
*   **Participant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant.
*   **Participant Agreement**: Select whether the participant agrees to the stargazing experience policies.
*   **Event Category**: Enter the category of the event.
