# Cultural Event Photography Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Event Photography Booking Form is a tool used to gather information from clients about their upcoming cultural event. It helps us understand their specific needs and preferences, so we can provide the best possible experience for them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and title for reference.
2. Describe the event details, including the event name and a brief explanation of the event.
3. Select the event date in the calendar.
4. Choose the event start and end times.
5. Enter your contact phone number so we can reach you.
6. Add any photo requirements or specific shots you would like us to take.
7. Share any additional comments or concerns.
8. Enter your email address for us to contact you.
9. Leave any additional comments for us.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <i class="fa fa-circle"></i> **Client Name**
* This field is where you can enter your name for our reference.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"></i> **Event Details**
* Please describe the event in a few sentences, including the name, location, and a brief explanation of what to expect.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"></i> **Event Date**
* Use the calendar to select the date of the event you are booking us for.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"></i> **Event Time**
* Choose the start and end times for the event.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"></i> **Photographer Details**
* Please let us know who you would like to be your photographer, or if we should assign one.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"></i> **Event Location**
* Enter the exact address or location of the event.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"></i> **Client Phone**
* Enter your contact phone number so we can reach you for any updates or questions.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"></i> **Photo Requirements**
* If you have any specific photo requirements or poses you would like us to take, let us know here.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"></i> **Additional Comments**
* Leave any additional comments or concerns you may have, such as special requests or instructions for your photographers.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"></i> **Email**
* Enter the email address where we should send any updates or information to you.

### <i class="fa fa-circle"></i> **Client Notes**
* If you would like to leave any additional notes for your photographers or any other staff member, this is the field for that.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields for us to properly prepare for your event.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
