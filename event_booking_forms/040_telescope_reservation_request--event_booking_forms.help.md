# Telescope Reservation Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is used to request telescope reservations for specific dates and times.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Observatory Name** where you want to make your observation.
2. Select the **Start Date** and **End Date** for your observation. Ensure that you have enough time for your observation and that the observatory's resources are available.
3. Enter your **Observer Name** as it will be used to identify you and your observation.
4. Choose your **Reservation Time** for your observation.
5. Indicate your **Equipment Preferences** by selecting which equipment you need or do not need during your observation.
6. Select whether you have a **Telescope Schedule** that conflicts with your observation.
7. Provide **Reservation Details** about your observation, such as any special requests or requirements.
8. Enter your **School Name** (if applicable) for any educational institution related to your observation.
9. Enter your **Contact Email** and **Contact Phone** so that we can reach you to confirm your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Observatory Name**: The name of the observatory where you want to make your observation.
* **Start Date**: The date you want to start your observation.
* **End Date**: The date you want to end your observation.
* **Observer Name**: The name of the person making the observation.
* **Reservation Time**: The time slot you want for your observation.
* **Equipment Preferences**: Select the equipment you need or do not need for your observation.
	+ Select 'Yes' for equipment you need, and 'No' for equipment you do not need.
* **Telescope Schedule**: Check if you have any conflicts with the telescope schedule.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have a conflict, and 'No' if you do not.
* **Reservation Details**: Provide any special requests or requirements for your observation.
* **School Name**: The name of the school or educational institution related to your observation (if applicable).
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you to confirm your reservation.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you to confirm your reservation.
* **Assigned Telescope**: Select which telescope you want to use for your observation.
	+ Select 'Yes' for the telescope you want to use, and 'No' for any other telescope.
