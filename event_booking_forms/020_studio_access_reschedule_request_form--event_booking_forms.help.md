# Studio Access Reschedule Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to reschedule a studio booking. Please submit a request to reschedule your booking if you need to change your scheduled time or date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Booking Request** (`new_booking_request`): Enter a brief description of your reason for rescheduling.
* **New Date** (`new_date`): Enter the new date you would like to schedule your booking.
* **New Time** (`new_time`): Enter the new time you would like to schedule your booking.
* **New Duration** (`new_duration`): Enter the new duration for your booking.
* **Reschedule Reason** (`reschedule_reason`): Enter a brief description of why you need to reschedule your booking.

## Tips
* Be clear and concise when filling out the form.
* Ensure your new booking details are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact our support team.
