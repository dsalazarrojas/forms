# Group Cultural Exchange Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Group Cultural Exchange Booking Form is a tool for users to provide information about themselves and their travel preferences in order to participate in a group cultural exchange program.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form with the required information for each field.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of yourself, including your name, age, and any other relevant information.
  - This field is not required, but providing some personal details can help us get to know you better.
- **Participant Preferences** (`participant_preferences`, `text`, required: false): Please let us know about your interests, hobbies, or any specific requirements you have for the trip.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us tailor the experience to your needs.
- **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any additional information about the trip you are interested in, such as the date and location.
  - This field is optional, but it helps us get a better understanding of your preferences.
- **Trip Preferences** (`trip_preferences`, `text`, required: false): Please specify any special requirements you have for the trip, such as accommodations or transportation needs.
  - This field is not required, but it allows us to provide the best possible experience.
- **Participant Travel Details** (`participant_travel_details`, `text`, required: false): Please provide information about your travel plans, including arrival and departure dates, flight information, and other relevant details.
  - This field is not required, but it helps us coordinate travel arrangements.
- **Accommodation Details** (`accommodation_details`, `text`, required: false): Please provide information about your accommodation preferences, such as room type and location.
  - This field is not required, but it helps us plan for your stay.
