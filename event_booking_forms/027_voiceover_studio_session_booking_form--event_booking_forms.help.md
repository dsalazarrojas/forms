# Voiceover Studio Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a voiceover studio session. Please fill out the form to provide your details for the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, start by entering your first name, last name, and email address in the first section. Then, select the session date and time, choose the type of session and voice level required, and specify if the session type is yes or no. Next, select the actor you are booking for and the studio where the session will take place. Finally, confirm your email address to ensure it is correct, and enter any additional notes or booking time if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address. Please use the same email address that you will use for communication about this session.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`, text, required/optional): Confirm the email address you provided above.
* **Studio Phone** (`studio_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the voiceover studio.
* **Actor Phone** (`actor_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the actor's contact person.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date for the session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time for the session.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the duration of the session in minutes.
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the session.
* **Voice Type** (`voice_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of voiceover required (Yes or No).
* **Voice Level** (`voice_level`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the level of voice required (High or Low).
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select if the session is yes or no.
* **Session Type (Other)** (`session_type_other`, text, required/optional): If the session is not a standard session, enter the type of session.
* **Studio** (`studio`, select_one, required/optional): Select the studio where the session will take place.
* **Actor** (`actor`, select_one, required/optional): Select the actor for whom the session is booked.
* **Booking Time** (`booking_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the booking time of the session.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments for the session.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required/optional): Select the person responsible for the session.
* **Assigned Time** (`assigned_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time assigned for the session.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the current status of the session (Active or Inactive).
* **Status (Other)** (`status_other`, text, required/optional): If the status is not one of the standard status, enter the status of the session.
* **Studio Email** (`studio_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email of the studio contact person.
* **Actor Email** (`actor_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email of the actor contact person.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, required/optional): This is a system generated field and will be automatically populated by the system.

## Tips
* Please double-check the session date and time for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the studio for assistance.
* The form will be reviewed and responded to by the studio staff after submission.
