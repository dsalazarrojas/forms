# Prescreen Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prescreen Form is designed to collect critical information from users about a specific event or occurrence.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are submitting this form for the correct purpose.
2. Review the form carefully and answer all required fields.
3. Fill in the form accurately and completely.
4. If you have any doubts, contact the relevant team or support for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prescreen Form Name** (`prescreen_form_name`, text, **Required**): Please enter a brief name or description of the event or occurrence.
* **Screening Info** (`screening_info`, note, **Required**): Enter any relevant details about the event or occurrence.
* **Phone** (`prescreen_form_phone`, text, **Required**): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`prescreen_form_email`, email, **Required**): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Event** (`prescreen_form_event`, select_one, **Required**): Select 'Yes' if you are attending or involved in the event, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Additional Info** (`prescreen_form_additional_info`, text, **Required**): Provide any additional information that may be relevant to the event or occurrence.
* **Event Date** (`prescreen_form_event_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date of the event or occurrence.

## Tips
* Be accurate and concise when filling in your answers.
* Double-check the form for completeness before submission.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the relevant team or support for clarification.
