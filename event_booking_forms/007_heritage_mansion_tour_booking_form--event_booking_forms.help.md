# Heritage Mansion Tour Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a tour for the heritage mansion. It will collect visitor details and preferences, and confirm or reject requested tour dates and times.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the visitor details section with your name and contact information.
2. Select a preferred tour date.
3. Choose the number of guests in your group.
4. If you have any special requests, such as dietary restrictions or mobility needs, describe them in the "Special Requests" field.
5. Select your preferred language for the tour.
6. Confirm your booking by selecting "True" in the "Booking Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor Details** (Text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Preferred Tour Date** (Date, required): Choose a date for the tour that is not already booked.
* **Guest Count** (Number, required): Enter the number of people in your group.
* **Special Requests** (Note, optional): Describe any special requests or needs you have for the tour.
* **Preferred Language** (Select One, required): Choose the language you would like the tour to be conducted in.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (Email, required): Enter your contact email address.
