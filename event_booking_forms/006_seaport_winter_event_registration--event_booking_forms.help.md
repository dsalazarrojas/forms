# Seaport Winter Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seaport Winter Event Registration form is designed to collect information from attendees for the Seaport Winter event. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure your spot and dietary needs are recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Attendee Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Email** (if you'd like to receive any updates about the event).
3. Select your **Preferred Food** from the options provided (Vegan, Omnivore, Pescatarian).
4. Write down any **Special Requests** you might have in the text field.
5. Provide your **Date Of Birth** if you'd like to participate in age-specific activities.
6. Enter your **Time Of Arrival** if you're planning to arrive at the event at a specific time.
7. Add any **Notes** you'd like to share with the event organizers.
8. Enter your **Phone** number if you'd like to be contacted for any reason.
9. Provide your **Address** so we can ensure accurate catering and attendance tracking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name**: Please enter your real name as it is written on your identification.
* **Email**: If you'd like to receive any updates about the event, enter your email address here.
* **Preferred Food**: Select one of the three options: Vegan, Omnivore, or Pescatarian, to ensure you receive the right food at the right time.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any specific requests, such as dietary restrictions or special accessibility needs, write them down here.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth if you'd like to participate in age-specific activities.
* **Time Of Arrival**: If you're planning to arrive at the event at a specific time, enter it here.
* **Notes**: Share any additional information you'd like to share with the event organizers.
* **Phone**: If you'd like to be contacted for any reason, enter your phone number here.
* **Address**: Enter your full address so we can ensure accurate catering and attendance tracking.

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling out the form to avoid any confusion.
* Make sure to select the right food option to ensure you receive the right food.
* If you have any special requests, please write them down clearly.
* If you'd like to participate in age-specific activities, provide your date of birth.
* If you'd like to be contacted for any reason, enter your phone number and address accurately.
