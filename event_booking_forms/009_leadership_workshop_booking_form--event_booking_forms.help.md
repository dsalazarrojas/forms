# Leadership Workshop Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Workshop Booking Form is used to collect information about workshops and their participants. This form helps workshop organizers gather all necessary details for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the workshop details in the `workshop_details` field.
2. Provide details about the attendees, including names and contact information, in the `attendee_details` field.
3. Confirm the workshop details in the `confirm_details` field.
4. Add any participant requests or notes in the `participant_requests` field (optional).
5. Indicate if you are attending the workshop (`registration` field).
6. Provide your email address in the `email` field.
7. Enter your phone number in the `phone_number` field (optional).
8. Select the date of the workshop in the `event_date` field.
9. Choose the time of the workshop in the `event_time` field.
10. Enter the number of participants in the `participant_count` field (optional).
11. Specify the workshop location in the `workshop_location` field.
12. Choose the type of participant (Participant, Team, Individual) in the `participant_type` field.
13. Enter the name of the participant in the `participant_name` field.
14. Provide a description of the workshop in the `event_description` field.
15. Enter the participant's email address in the `participant_email` field.
16. Enter the participant's phone number in the `participant_phone` field.
17. Select the participant's attendance status (Confirmed, Pending, Cancelled) in the `participant_attendance` field.
18. Add any participant notes or comments in the `participant_notes` field (optional).
19. Enter the trainer's name in the `trainer` field.
20. Enter the size of the team in the `team_size` field (optional).
21. Select the participant's status (Confirmed, Pending, Cancelled) in the `participant_status` field.
22. Enter any additional status notes or comments in the `participant_status_notes` field (optional).
23. Select the date of the participant's status update in the `participant_status_date` field (optional).
24. Choose the time of the participant's status update in the `participant_status_time` field (optional).
25. Enter any additional status comments or notes in the `participant_status_comment` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **workshop_details**: A text field where you can enter the workshop details.
* **attendee_details**: A text field where you can provide details about the attendees.
* **confirm_details**: A text field where you can confirm the workshop details.
* **participant_requests**: A text field where you can add any participant requests or notes (optional).
* **registration**: A select-one field where you can indicate if you are attending the workshop.
* **email**: An email field where you can enter your email address.
* **phone_number**: A text field where you can enter your phone number (optional).
* **event_date**: A date field where you can select the date of the workshop.
* **event_time**: A time field where you can select the time of the workshop.
* **participant_count**: A number field where you can enter the number of participants (optional).
* **workshop_location**: A text field where you can specify the workshop location.
* **participant_type**: A select-one field where you can choose the type of participant (Participant, Team, Individual).
* **participant_name**: A text field where you can enter the name of the participant.
* **event_description**: A text field where you can provide a description of the workshop.
* **participant_email**: An email field where you can enter the participant's email address.
* **participant_phone**: A text field where you can enter the participant's phone number.
* **participant_attendance**: A select-multiple field where you can select the participant's attendance status (Confirmed, Pending, Cancelled).
* **participant_notes**: A text field where you can add any participant notes or comments (optional).
* **trainer**: A text field where you can enter the trainer's name.
* **team_size**: A number field where you can enter the size of the team (optional).
* **participant_status**: A select-one field where you can select the participant's status (Confirmed, Pending, Cancelled).
* **participant_status_notes**: A text field where you can add any participant status notes or comments (optional).
* **participant_status_date**: A date field where you can select the date of the participant's status update (optional).
* **participant_status_time**: A time field where you can select the time of the participant's status update (optional).
* **participant_status_comment**: A text field where you can enter any additional status comments or notes (optional).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately to avoid any issues with the workshop booking.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is correct.
* If you have any doubts or issues with the form, contact the workshop organizers for help.
