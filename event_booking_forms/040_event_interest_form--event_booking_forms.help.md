# Event Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Interest Form is a tool used to collect information about your event interests and preferences. It helps us understand what types of events you enjoy, how often you attend them, and when and where you prefer them to take place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the types of events that you are interested in (page 1).
2. Choose how often you attend events (page 2).
3. Indicate your preferred time of the year for events (page 3).
4. If you have any thoughts or comments about prophets, feel free to share them (note field).
5. Select your preferred day of the week for events (page 5).
6. Enter the number of days you can attend events (page 6).
7. Enter a specific time slot you can attend events (page 7).
8. Select a date you can attend events (page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What types of events do you like?** (`event_interest_form_1`, select_multiple, not required):
This field gives you the opportunity to select all the types of events that you are interested in, such as music concerts, sporting events, or festivals.
* **How often do you attend events?** (`event_interest_form_2`, select_one, not required):
Choose how often you attend events, such as daily, weekly, monthly, or rarely.
* **What time of the year do you prefer?** (`event_interest_form_3`, select_one, not required):
Select your preferred time of the year for events, such as summer, winter, or any time.
* **Prophets** (`event_interest_form_4`, note, not required):
If you have any thoughts or comments about prophets, feel free to share them.
* **What day of the week do you prefer?** (`event_interest_form_5`, select_multiple, not required):
Select all the days of the week you prefer for events, such as Monday, Tuesday, or any day.
* **Event 1** (`event_interest_form_6`, number, not required):
Enter the number of days you can attend events.
* **Event 2** (`event_interest_form_7`, number, not required):
Enter another number related to event availability.
* **Time** (`event_interest_form_8`, time, not required):
Enter a specific time slot you can attend events.
* **Date** (`event_interest_form_9`, date, not required):
Select a date you can attend events.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all fields to get the most accurate and personalized results.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to ask.
* Remember, the data collected will be used to tailor event suggestions and recommendations for you.
