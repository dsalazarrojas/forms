# safari_lodge_experience_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help guests book an experience at a safari lodge. It requires guests to provide their details and preferences for their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required information for each field as prompted.
2.  Select "Yes" or "No" for activities you wish to participate in during your stay.
3.  Enter your email address for communication purposes.
4.  Enter your phone number for easy contact.
5.  Add any additional notes about your stay.
6.  Confirm your booking details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **safari_lodge_experience_booking_form** (`title`, text, required): Enter the title of the form to confirm the purpose of your booking.
* **guest_details** (`guest_details`, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **dates** (`dates`, text, required): Enter the dates of your stay for accurate planning.
* **activities** (`activities`, select_multiple, required): Select activities you wish to participate in during your stay. Choose "Yes" or "No" for each activity.
* **payment_details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter your payment details for processing.
* **confirmation** (`confirmation`, text, optional): Confirm your booking details for accuracy.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for easy communication.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your stay.
* **submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click the "Submit" button to complete your booking.
* **safari_booking_details** (`safari_booking_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your booking.
* **safari_lodge_address** (`safari_lodge_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the safari lodge you are staying at.
