# Showreel Screening Slot Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Showreel Screening Slot Booking Form is used to book a showreel screening slot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your input in the User Input field.
2. Select your preferred screening slot from the Screening Slot field.
3. Choose a booking date for your screening slot in the Booking Date field.
4. Enter your contact email address in the Contact Email field.
5. Enter your contact phone number in the Contact Phone field.
6. Provide any additional screening notes in the Screening Notes field.
7. Confirm your booking in the User Confirmation field.
8. Optionally, provide screening comments in the User Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (id: 1, required): Enter your input for the screening.
* **Screening Slot** (id: 2, required): Select the screening slot you want to book.
  - Options:
    - 09:00
    - 600
    - 660
* **Booking Date** (id: 3, required): Choose a date for your screening slot booking.
* **Contact Email** (id: 4, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (id: 5, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Screening Notes** (id: 6, optional): Provide any additional notes for the screening.
* **User Confirmation** (id: 7, optional): Confirm your booking.
* **Assigned Tool** (id: 8, optional): Select a tool for the screening (if required).
* **Created By** (id: 9, optional): Display the name of the creator of this form.
* **Updated By** (id: 10, optional): Display the name of the last updater of this form.
* **Created At** (id: 11, optional): Display the date and time this form was created.
* **Updated At** (id: 12, optional): Display the date and time this form was last updated.
* **Assigned Date** (id: 13, optional): Display the assigned date for the screening.
* **Assigned Time** (id: 14, optional): Display the assigned time for the screening.
* **User Comments** (id: 15, optional): Provide any additional comments for the screening.
* **Created** (id: 16, optional): Display the name of the creator of this form.
* **Updated** (id: 17, optional): Display the name of the last updater of this form.
* **Created By 2** (id: 18, optional): Display the name of the creator of this form (if different from the first creator).
* **Updated By 2** (id: 19, optional): Display the name of the last updater of this form (if different from the last updater).
* **Updated At 2** (id: 20, optional): Display the date and time this form was last updated (if different from the last update).
* **Assigned Tool 2** (id: 21, optional): Display the assigned tool for the screening (if different from the first assigned tool).
* **Form Id** (id: 22, optional): Display the unique identifier of this form.
* **User Id** (id: 23, optional): Display the user ID of the creator of this form.
* **Updated User Id** (id: 24, optional): Display the user ID of the last updater of this form.
* **Submitted By** (id: 25, optional): Display the name of the user who submitted this form.

## Tips

* Make sure to select a valid screening slot.
* Double-check your contact email and phone number.
* Be concise in your screening notes.
* Confirm your booking to avoid any confusion.
* Provide clear and detailed comments if necessary.
* Use this form for internal use only.
