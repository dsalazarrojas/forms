# Stage Crew Training Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for booking and managing Stage Crew Training sessions. It helps in recording the required details such as booking date, time, trainer name, email, and client information for effective management of training activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Stage Crew Training Booking** field with the name of the training activity.
2. In the **Booking Date** field, select the date for the training activity.
3. In the **Booking Time** field, choose the time slot for the training activity.
4. Choose the **Trainer Name** from the dropdown list of available trainers.
5. Enter the **Trainer Email** in the format of a valid email address.
6. Add any additional **Booking Notes** for the training activity.
7. Enter the **Trainer Notes** for the training activity.
8. Fill in the **Client Name** with the name of the client.
9. Enter the **Client Email** in the format of a valid email address.
10. Add the **Client Phone** number for the client.
11. Choose the **Form Status** as **Draft** (if the form is not yet submitted) or **Submitted** (if the form is submitted) or **Completed** (if the training activity is completed).
12. Select the **Submitter** from the dropdown list of available submitters.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Stage Crew Training Booking (1)
* **Stage Crew Training Booking** (`stage_crew_training_booking_form`, `text`, optional): Enter a name for the training activity.

### Booking Date (2)
* **Booking Date** (`stage_crew_training_booking_form_booking_date`, `date`, optional): Select a date for the training activity.

### Booking Time (3)
* **Booking Time** (`stage_crew_training_booking_form_booking_time`, `time`, optional): Choose a time slot for the training activity.

### Trainer Name (4)
* **Trainer Name** (`stage_crew_training_booking_form_trainer_name`, `select_one`, optional): Choose a trainer from the dropdown list.

### Trainer Email (5)
* **Trainer Email** (`stage_crew_training_booking_form_trainer_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the trainer.

### Booking Notes (6)
* **Booking Notes** (`stage_crew_training_booking_form_booking_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes for the training activity.

### Trainer Notes (7)
* **Trainer Notes** (`stage_crew_training_booking_form_trainer_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any notes for the trainer.

### Client Name (8)
* **Client Name** (`stage_crew_training_booking_form_client_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the client.

### Client Email (9)
* **Client Email** (`stage_crew_training_booking_form_client_email`, `text`, optional): Enter the email address of the client.

### Client Phone (10)
* **Client Phone** (`stage_crew_training_booking_form_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the client.

### Form Status (11)
* **Form Status** (`stage_crew_training_booking_form_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the status of the form as Draft, Submitted, or Completed.

### Submitter (12)
* **Submitter** (`stage_crew_training_booking_form_submitter`, `select_one`, optional): Choose who submitted the form from the dropdown list.
