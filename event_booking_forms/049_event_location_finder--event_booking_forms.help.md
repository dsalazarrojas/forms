# Event Location Finder - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Location Finder form is designed to help you discover the perfect location for your event based on your specific needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To find the ideal location for your event, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of your event in the **Event Date** field.
2. Estimate your event budget in the **Budget** field.
3. Specify the number of guests attending your event in the **Guest Count** field.
4. Provide any additional details about your event in the **Event Details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your event in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, required: false): Please estimate your event budget to help us suggest suitable locations based on your financial constraints.
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, required: false): Specify the expected number of attendees to help us find a location that can accommodate them comfortably.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, note, required: false): Provide any special requirements or notes about your event, such as food and beverage preferences or unique decorations.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to help us suggest more accurate location options.
- If you have any further questions or need help with event planning, don't hesitate to contact us.
- We'll do our best to find the perfect location for your event based on your submitted information.
