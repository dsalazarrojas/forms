# Event Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Event Request Form is designed to collect information about non-recurring events. This form will help you gather the necessary details about the event, allowing you to process the request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of event you are requesting (e.g., Conference, Seminar, Fair, or Charity Event).
2.  Enter the name of the event.
3.  Provide a brief description of the event.
4.  Enter the date of the event.
5.  Choose the time of the event.
6.  Enter the venue where the event will take place.
7.  Enter the location of the event.
8.  Provide the name of the contact person responsible for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Event Type**
(`event_request_form_event_type`, select_one, required: false)
This field is used to classify the type of event you are requesting. Select one of the options provided (e.g., Conference, Seminar, Fair, or Charity Event).

### 2. **Name**
(`event_request_form_name`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of your event. This will be used to identify the event and communicate with you about it.

### 3. **Description**
(`event_request_form_description`, text, required: false)
Provide a brief description of your event. This will help us understand the purpose and details of your request.

### 4. **Date**
(`event_request_form_date`, date, required: false)
Enter the date of your event. This will help us schedule and plan accordingly.

### 5. **Time**
(`event_request_form_time`, time, required: false)
Choose the time of your event.

### 6. **Venue**
(`event_request_form_venue`, text, required: false)
Enter the venue where your event will take place. This could be a physical location, a website, or any other relevant details.

### 7. **Location**
(`event_request_form_location`, text, required: false)
Enter the location of your event. This could be a physical address, a room number, or any other specific details.

### 8. **Contact Person**
(`event_request_form_contact_person`, text, required: false)
Provide the name of the contact person responsible for the event. This will help us communicate with you about the status and details of your request.
