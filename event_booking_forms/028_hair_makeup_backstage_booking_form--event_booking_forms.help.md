# Hair & Makeup Backstage Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help event organizers book services for hair and makeup professionals for events such as weddings, photoshoots, fashion shows, and TV shows.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of event from the dropdown menu to indicate what type of service is required.
2. Fill out the client information section with the relevant details about the client, including any necessary contact information.
3. Enter any relevant hair information, such as specific services or styles required.
4. Enter any relevant makeup information, such as specific services or looks required.
5. Provide contact information for the backstage team, including their names, phone numbers, and email addresses.
6. Review and submit the form to finalize the booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Information** (`Event Type`, select_one, required): Select the type of event from the dropdown menu to indicate what type of service is required.
* **Client Information** (`Client Information`, text, optional): Enter any details about the client, including their name, contact information, and any specific needs or requirements.
* **Hair Information** (`Hair Information`, text, optional): Enter any relevant hair information, such as specific services or styles required.
* **Makeup Information** (`Makeup Information`, text, optional): Enter any relevant makeup information, such as specific services or looks required.
* **Backstage Contact** (`Backstage Contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for the backstage team, including their names, phone numbers, and email addresses.
* **Submission** (`Submission`, note, optional): This is a free-text field for any additional comments or information about the booking.
