# Music Tour Planning Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to plan and book music tours and events. It gathers necessary information for event organization and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have the necessary authority to access and fill out this form.
2.  Start by filling out the **Tour Dates** field with the dates you would like to hold the event.
3.  Enter the **Tour Locations** where the event will take place.
4.  Provide any **Tour Requirements** in the text field, such as specific equipment or setup needs.
5.  Enter **Event Details** about the event, including any relevant information about the artist or performer.
6.  If applicable, provide **Manager Info**, **Agency Info**, **Artist Info**, and **Tour Agent** details as required.
7.  Decide whether the **Booking Agency** is involved and select the corresponding option.
8.  Choose whether the **Tour Manager** is involved and select the corresponding option.
9.  If applicable, select the **Tour Agent** who will be working with the tour.
10.  Enter the **Event Date** and **Event Time** for the tour.
11.  Specify the **Event Duration**, including any relevant details.
12.  Enter any additional details about the **Artist** performing.
13.  Finally, select the **Event Type** of the tour, such as a concert or festival.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Tour Dates** (Date): Enter the dates when the event is scheduled to take place.
*   **Tour Locations** (Text): Enter the locations where the event will be held.
*   **Tour Requirements** (Text): Specify any specific requirements or needs for the event.
*   **Event Details** (Text): Enter any relevant information about the event, including artist details.
*   **Manager Info** (Text): If applicable, enter the manager's name or contact information.
*   **Agency Info** (Text): If applicable, enter the agency's name or contact information.
*   **Artist Info** (Text): Enter any relevant information about the artist or performer.
*   **Booking Agency** (Select One: Yes/No): Select whether the booking agency is involved.
*   **Tour Manager** (Select Multiple: Yes/No): Choose whether the tour manager is involved.
*   **Tour Agent** (Select Multiple: Yes/No): Choose whether the tour agent is involved.
*   **Event Date** (Date): Enter the date of the event.
*   **Event Time** (Time): Enter the time of the event.
*   **Event Duration** (Text): Enter the duration of the event, including any details.
*   **Artist Details** (Text): Enter any additional details about the artist.
*   **Tour Name** (Text): Enter the name of the tour.
*   **Event Type** (Select One: Yes/No): Select the type of event, such as a concert or festival.

## Tips

*   Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
*   If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide or contact the relevant parties.
