# Seaside Creative Studio Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seaside Creative Studio Booking Form is intended to gather information about your booking request for a session at the Seaside Creative Studio. This form is used to provide us with the necessary details to accommodate your needs and ensure that we have the required resources available during your session. By filling out this form, you will be able to reserve a time slot that suits you best and our team will be able to prepare for your session accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Scroll down the form and fill in each field with the requested information. Each field is marked as required or optional, please ensure that you fill in all required fields.
2.  Please ensure that the date and time fields are in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY or HH:MM).
3.  If you're unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seaside Creative Studio** (`studio_details`, text, optional): This field is a brief description of our studio. If you have any specific requests or requirements, you can write them here.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): This field is used to schedule your session. Please use the DD/MM/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/2023).
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): In this field, you can provide a detailed description of your project, including any specific requirements or equipment needed.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_one, optional): Please select "Yes" if your project involves creating something that we can offer services for, or "No" otherwise.
* **Project Start Time** (`project_start_time`, time, optional): If your project involves specific timing, please provide it here (e.g., 09:00 AM).
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, optional): This field is used to determine how long your session will last.
* **Number of People** (`number_of_people`, number, optional): Please indicate how many people will be participating in your session.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is used to contact you with further instructions or reminders.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted via phone, please provide your number here.
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, optional): This field is used to schedule your reserved session. If this date is not available, please let us know and we'll find an alternative.
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, time, optional): This field is used to schedule your reserved session.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, optional): Please indicate the budget you're willing to spend on your project.
