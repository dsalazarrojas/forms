# Product Sampling Booth Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from event organizers about their product sampling booth preferences. Please fill out the form to ensure that your event goes smoothly and that your product sampling booth is well-prepared.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, start by filling out the required fields (marked as "required") with accurate and up-to-date information.
- Make sure to provide clear and concise answers for each field.
- Review the form before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer**: <code>event_organizer</code> (`text`, required) - Please enter the name of the event.
* **Company**: <code>company</code> (`text`, required) - Enter the name of the company associated with the event.
* **Product**: <code>product</code> (`text`, required) - Enter the name of the product being sampled.
* **Dates**: <code>dates</code> (`select_multiple`, required) - Select the dates you would like the product sampling booth to be available.
* **Time**: <code>time</code> (`time`, required) - Enter the time you would like the product sampling booth to be available.
* **Location**: <code>location</code> (`text`, required) - Enter the location where the product sampling booth will be set up.
* **Requirements**: <code>requirements</code> (`text`, optional) - If there are any specific requirements or requests for the product sampling booth, please specify them here.
* **Preferred Dates**: <code>preferred_dates</code> (`select_one`, required) - Select your preferred dates for the product sampling booth.
