# Mountain Yoga Chalet Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mountain Yoga Chalet Booking Form is used to collect information from guests for their stay at the chalet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields ( Guest Name, Checkin, Checkout, Arrival Date, Booking Status, and Arrival Time).
2. Select the room type and booking type from the respective dropdown menus.
3. Enter your email address and phone number in the respective fields.
4. If you have any special requests, enter them in the "Special Requests" field.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text): Please enter your full name.
* **Checkin** (`checkin`, date): Select the date you plan to check-in at the chalet.
* **Checkout** (`checkout`, date): Select the date you plan to check-out from the chalet.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date): Enter the date you plan to arrive at the chalet.
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, select_multiple): Select the type of room you prefer (e.g., "Yes" for a standard room, "No" for an upgrade).
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, number): Enter the number of the room you prefer.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time): Select the time you plan to arrive at the chalet.
* **Booking Type** (`booking_type`, select_multiple): Select the type of booking (e.g., "Yes" for a standard booking, "No" for an extra-ordinary booking).
* **Booking Status** (`booking_status`, select_one): Select the status of your booking (e.g., "Active" for an active booking, "Inactive" for an inactive booking).
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, note): Enter any special requests or preferences you have.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Booking Status** (`booking_status`, select_one): Select "Active" if you have already booked a room at the chalet, or "Inactive" if you are booking for a future date.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time): Select the time you plan to arrive at the chalet.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests, please be specific and clear about your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the chalet management or staff.
