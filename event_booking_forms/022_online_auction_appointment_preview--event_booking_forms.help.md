# online_auction_appointment_preview - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for scheduling a preview appointment for an online auction. It is used to gather information from the public to facilitate communication and organization of the auction process. The form is divided into several sections that require the completion of specific fields, which are explained in the following instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Select the **Schedule a preview** option on the first page to schedule a preview appointment.
- On the **Contact details** page, enter the required information, including your **Contact person**, **Email**, and **Phone**.
- On the **Auction category interests** page, select one or more categories that interest you.
- On the **Platform preferences** page, select your platform preferences.
- On the **Message** page, add any additional comments or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule a preview**: (`1`, `time`, required: false): Select a specific time slot for the preview appointment.
* **Contact details**: (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Auction category interests**: (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more categories that interest you. This will help us understand your specific needs.
* **Platform preferences**: (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your platform preferences, whether it's for buying or selling.
* **Contact person**: (`5`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person you would like to contact.
* **Email**: (`6`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**: (`7`, `text`, required: false): Enter a valid phone number.
* **Message**: (`8`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or requests you have.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we receive your message.
- Double-check your contact information to ensure accuracy.
- Be as specific as possible when selecting your auction category interests and platform preferences.
- If you have any special requests or comments, include them in the message field.
