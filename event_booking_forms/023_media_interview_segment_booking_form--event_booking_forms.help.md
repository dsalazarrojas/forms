# Media Interview Segment Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book media interviews with guests. Fill out this form to book a segment on a show.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Guest Information" page with your contact details.
2. Select the topic of your segment.
3. Choose a date and time for your booking.
4. Enter your segment length.
5. Select your preferred time slots.
6. Enter any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" section.
7. Choose your PR contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Guest Information
* **Guest Name, Guest Information (text), required**: Enter your name as the guest.
* **Topic, Select Topic, required**: Select the topic of your segment.
* **Email, Email, required**: Enter your email address. e.g., john.doe@example.com
* **Phone, Phone, required**: Enter your phone number. e.g., 123-456-7890
* **Contact Details, Contact Details (text), optional**: Enter any additional contact details.

### Segment Booking
* **Date of Booking, Date of Booking, required**: Choose the date for your segment. 
* **Time of Booking, Time of Booking, required**: Choose the time for your segment.
* **Segment Length, Segment Length, required**: Choose the length of your segment.

### Preferred Time Slots
* **Preferred Time Slots, Preferred Time Slots, required**: Choose your preferred time slots for the booking. Select "Morning", "Afternoon", or "Evening".

### PR Contact
* **PR Contact, PR Contact, required**: Choose your PR contact. Select "Jimmy" or "Bob".
