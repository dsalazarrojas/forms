# Backstage Label Printing Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Backstage Label Printing Booking Form is designed to collect information for custom printed labels used in events and conferences. It is used by event organizers to specify the type, size, and content of the labels, as well as delivery and production details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in your event name and date.
2.  Specify the label type and size required for your event.
3.  Enter the quantity of labels needed.
4.  Select the color scheme and content for your labels.
5.  Provide any specific colors needed or logo requirements.
6.  Choose the delivery method and setup service needed.
7.  Indicate if this is a rush order.
8.  Enter any additional special instructions or requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester's Name (Full Name)** (`requester_name`, text, required): Please fill in your full name to help us identify you and your event.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to receive order confirmation and updates.
* **Contact Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact number for any additional contact or questions.
* **Company or Organization** (`company_organization`, text, required): Provide the name of your company or organization.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Specify the date of your event.
* **Event Location** (`venue_name`, text, required): Enter the location of your event.
* **Type of Labels Needed** (`label_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of labels you need (e.g., Name badges, Access passes, Equipment labels, Cable labels, Custom labels).
* **Label Size** (`label_size`, select_one, required): Select the size of the label (e.g., Small, 2x1 inches, Medium, 3x2 inches, Large, 4x3 inches).
* **Quantity Needed** (`quantity_needed`, number, required): Enter the total number of labels needed.
* **Color Preference** (`color_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred color scheme (e.g., White with black text, Color printed, Specific color required, No preference).
* **Specific Colors Required** (`specific_colors`, text, optional): Enter any specific colors needed if "Specific color required" is chosen.
* **Label Content** (`label_content`, text, required): Enter the text or graphics you want to be printed on the label.
* **Logo Included** (`logo_included`, select_one, required): Check if you have a logo to be included on the label.
* **Barcode or QR Code Needed** (`barcode_needed`, select_one, required): Choose if you need a scannable code on your labels.
* **Lamination Needed** (`laminate_needed`, select_one, required): Indicate if your labels need a protective coating.
* **Adhesive Type** (`adhesive_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of adhesive you need (e.g., Standard permanent, Removable, Heavy duty, Lanyard clip, Pin back).
* **Is This a Rush Order** (`rush_order`, select_one, required): Check if you need expedited service.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you want to receive your labels (e.g., Pickup at venue, Ship to address, Will call).
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): Enter your delivery address if shipping is chosen.
* **Setup Service Needed** (`setup_service`, select_one, required): Indicate if you need labels applied or distributed.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any additional requirements or instructions for your labels.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Select your budget category to help estimate production costs (e.g., Under 100, 100 to 250, 250 to 500, 500 to 1000, Over 1000).
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the date of this label printing request.

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or need clarification on a field, please do not hesitate to contact us.
