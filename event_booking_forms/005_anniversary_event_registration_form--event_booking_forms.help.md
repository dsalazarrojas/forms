# Anniversary Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from attendees for the anniversary event.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the event schedule and select the events you would like to attend.
2.  Fill out the form with the required information, including your full name, email address, contact number, and graduation or joining year (if applicable).
3.  Indicate the number of guests you will be bringing and provide their names (if applicable).
4.  Select your dietary restrictions and accommodation requirements.
5.  Indicate if you require accommodation and select your preferred hotel or dormitory (if applicable).
6.  Specify how you will be arriving at the event.
7.  Complete the form by indicating any special assistance you require.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Attendee Full Name (Required)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or business card.
*   **Graduation or Joining Year (Optional)**: If you are a graduate of the institution, provide your graduation year. If not, provide your joining year.
*   **Email Address (Required)**: Enter your official email address.
*   **Contact Number (Required)**: Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
*   **Number of Guests (Required)**: Indicate the number of guests you will be bringing.
*   **Names of Guests (Optional)**: If you are bringing guests, provide their names separated by commas.
*   **Dietary Restrictions**: Select all the dietary restrictions that apply to you (e.g. Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, etc.).
*   **Do you require accommodation? (Required)**: Indicate if you require accommodation for the event.
*   **Preferred Hotel or Dormitory (Optional)**: Select your preferred hotel or dormitory (if you require accommodation).
*   **How will you arrive? (Optional)**: Select the mode of transportation you will be using to arrive at the event.
*   **Specific Events Attending (Required)**: Select all the events you plan to attend.
*   **Do you require any special assistance? (Optional)**: If you require any special assistance (e.g. wheelchair accessibility), provide a brief description.

## Tips

*   Please review the event schedule and sessions carefully before selecting your events.
*   Ensure that you have provided accurate and up-to-date information.
*   If you require accommodation, please ensure that you select your preferred hotel or dormitory.
*   If you have any special assistance needs, please provide a brief description to ensure that we can provide the necessary support.
