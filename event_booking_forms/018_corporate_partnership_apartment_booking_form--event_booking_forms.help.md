# Corporate Partnership Apartment Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from corporate partners for partnership events or activities. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth processing of your partnership event or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Review the form carefully and make sure you're filling out the correct information.
2.  Fill out the "Partner Details" section with your company's or partnership's details.
3.  Select the appropriate event details that apply to your partnership event or activity.
4.  Provide your room preferences, if any.
5.  Select your arrival and departure dates and times.
6.  Enter the number of guests attending the event or activity.
7.  Choose your preferred room type.
8.  Add any additional details, if necessary.
9.  Provide your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
