# Eco Apartment Stay Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking an eco apartment stay. Please provide your booking details and the information about the accommodation you want to book.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the booking details such as arrival and departure dates.
2. Provide your guest details including name, email, and phone number.
3. Choose the type of accommodation (Apartment, Room, or House).
4. Select the number of guests.
5. Choose the arrival and departure times.
6. Select the accommodation from the provided options.
7. Add any notes about the booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **booking_details** (`text`, `text`, required/optional): Please fill in the booking details such as arrival and departure dates.
* **arrival_date** (`date`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the arrival date of your stay.
* **departure_date** (`date`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the departure date of your stay.
* **guest_details** (`text`, `text`, required/optional): Fill in your guest details including any additional information.
* **policy_agreement** (`select_multiple`, `text`, required/optional): Select the policy agreements you accept.
* **guest_details_name** (`text`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your guest's name.
* **guest_details_email** (`email`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your guest's email address.
* **guest_details_phone** (`text`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your guest's phone number.
* **accommodation_type** (`select_one`, `text`, required/optional): Choose the type of accommodation (Apartment, Room, or House).
* **number_of_guests** (`number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the number of guests.
* **arrival_time** (`time`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the arrival time.
* **Departure Time** (`time`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the departure time.
* **accommodation_id** (`select_one`, `text`, required/optional): Select the accommodation ID.
* **accommodation_name** (`text`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the accommodation name.
* **accommodation_address** (`text`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the accommodation address.
* **accommodation_city** (`text`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the accommodation city.
* **accommodation_state** (`text`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the accommodation state.
* **accommodation_postal_code** (`text`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the accommodation post code.
* **accommodation_country** (`text`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the accommodation country.
* **accommodation_notes** (`text`, `text`, required/optional): Add any notes about the accommodation.
* **policy** (`text`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any policy information.
* **policy2** (`text`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any policy2 information.
* **policy3** (`text`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any policy3 information.
* **policy4** (`text`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any policy4 information.
* **booking_notes** (`text`, `text`, required/optional): Add any booking notes.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the information you provide before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We'll review your booking request and get back to you soon.
