# Client Event Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Event Booking Form is designed to gather information about your event, allowing us to provide the best possible service for your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Be thorough when providing details about your event.
3. Select all applicable options for your preferences and needs.
4. Review your information carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number.
* **Company Name**: If you are booking on behalf of a company, enter your business name.
* **Company Address**: If applicable, enter your business address.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event you are interested in booking (e.g., Corporate Meeting, Conference, etc.).
* **Event Name**: Enter the official name of your event.
* **Preferred Event Date**: Enter your first choice date for the event.
* **Alternative Dates**: If you have other dates in mind, enter them here.
* **Event Start Time**: Enter the time your event will begin.
* **Event End Time**: Enter the time your event will end.
* **Setup Time Needed**: Select how much time is needed for setup before the event starts.
* **Expected Number of Guests**: Enter an approximate guest count.
* **Guest Count Range**: Select the estimated range of guests.
* **Venue Preference**: Choose a venue type (indoor, outdoor, or virtual).
* **Preferred Room Setup**: Select the seating arrangement you prefer.
* **Is Catering Needed?**: Select if you need food and beverage service.
* **Type of Catering**: If applicable, select the type of catering you need.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select any dietary restrictions you need to accommodate.
* **Audio Visual Equipment Needed**: Select any audio visual equipment you need.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any specific requirements or requests you have.
* **Decor or Theme Preferences**: Enter any decor or theme preferences.
* **Budget Range**: Select your estimated budget for the event.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?**: Select how you became aware of our services.
* **Booking Status**: Select your current status (e.g., ready to book, considering options, etc.).
* **Deposit Understanding**: Confirm that you understand a deposit is required to secure booking.
* **Terms and Conditions Agreement**: Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Booking Request Date**: Enter the date you would like to book your event.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by the client, and the fields marked as "required" are essential for us to provide accurate services. Please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately to ensure a smooth booking process.
