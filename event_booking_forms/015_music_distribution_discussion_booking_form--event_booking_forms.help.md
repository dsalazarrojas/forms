# music_distribution_discussion_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for music distribution discussion booking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. **Music Distribution Overview**: This is the first page where you can provide a brief overview of the music distribution discussion.
2. **Artist Information**: This page is required and you must provide information about the artist(s) involved.
3. **Music Details**: This page is also required and you can enter details about the music being distributed.
4. **Distribution Channels**: Select the channels where you want to distribute your music (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, or Other).
5. **Scheduling**: Enter the schedule for music distribution.
6. **Payment Information**: Provide payment details for music distribution.
7. **Notes**: This is an optional page where you can add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Music Distribution Overview (Page 1)** (`text`, optional): A brief overview of the music distribution discussion.
* **Artist Information (Page 2)** (`text`, required): Information about the artist(s) involved.
* **Music Details (Page 3)** (`text`, required): Details about the music being distributed.
* **Distribution Channels (Page 4)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the channels where you want to distribute your music (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, or Other).
* **Scheduling (Page 5)** (`text`, required): The schedule for music distribution.
* **Payment Information (Page 6)** (`text`, required): Payment details for music distribution.
* **Notes (Page 7)** (`note`, optional): Additional notes or comments about the music distribution discussion.
* **Other (Distribution Channels) (Page 4)** (`text`, optional): If you select "Other" in Distribution Channels, you can specify the other channel(s) here.

Please note that you must fill out all required fields to complete the form.
