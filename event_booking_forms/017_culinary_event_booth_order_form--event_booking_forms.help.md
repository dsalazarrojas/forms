# Culinary Event Booth Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Culinary Event Booth Order Form is a tool used by vendors to place orders for culinary events. This form helps vendors provide their name, contact information, and order details to the event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Vendor Name.
2. Enter your Email and Phone number.
3. Select your desired Payment Method (Stripe, Credit Card, or PayPal).
4. If you have selected 'Payment Method Other', please specify the method you'd like to use.
5. Choose the items you'd like to order (up to 5 items).
6. Select your desired Booth Type (10 ft x 10 ft, 10 ft x 5 ft, or Other).
7. You can provide additional information about your booth in the 'Booth Details' field.
8. Enter the date you'd like to order for and the date of the event.
9. Enter the total Payment Amount, or if you have selected 'Payment Method Other', please provide the amount.
10. Finally, add any additional notes you'd like to include with your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name (Vendor Name)**: Please enter your name and the name of your company.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)**: Select the payment method you'd like to use (Stripe, Credit Card, or PayPal).
* **Payment Method Other (Payment Method Other)**: If you selected 'Payment Method Other', please specify the method you'd like to use.
* **Order Items (Order Items)**: Choose up to 5 items you'd like to order.
* **Order Items Other (Order Items Other)**: If the items you'd like to order are not listed, please specify them here.
* **Booth Type (Booth Type)**: Select the size of booth you'd like to rent.
* **Booth Details (Booth Details)**: Provide any additional information about your booth.
* **Order Date (Order Date)**: Enter the date you'd like to order for.
* **Event Date (Event Date)**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Payment Amount (Payment Amount)**: Enter the total payment amount, or if you have selected 'Payment Method Other', please provide the amount.
* **Payment Amount Other (Payment Amount Other)**: If you selected 'Payment Method Other', please provide the amount here.
* **Total Price (Total Price)**: This field will be calculated based on your selections.
* **Notes (Notes)**: Add any additional information about your order.

**Tips**: Make sure to double-check your selections and information before submitting your order. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
