> # Group Enquiry Single Destination - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from users for a group enquiry single destination event. This form is meant for users who are looking to book a group event or travel package to a specific location.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form fields as instructed below.
* Make sure to provide all required information to avoid errors.
* Review your submission before submitting it to ensure everything is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Destination
* **Destination** (`destination`, `text`, required: false): Enter the destination of the group enquiry single destination event.

### Travel Type
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select multiple travel types for the group event, such as Flights, Rail, Apartments, Hotels, or Hostels.

### Start Date
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the group event.

### End Date
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the group event.

### Start Time

### Enquiry Type

### Special Instruction
* **Special Instruction** (`special_instruction`, `note`, required: false): Enter any special instructions for the group event.

### Email

### Phone
