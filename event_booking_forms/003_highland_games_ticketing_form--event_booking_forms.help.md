# Highland Games Ticketing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Highland Games Ticketing Form is designed to collect information from users to facilitate the ticketing process for the event. This includes gathering essential details for ticket allocation and distribution. Providing accurate and complete information will ensure a smooth and successful ticketing experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in your First Name (Field 1)
- Enter your Last Name (Field 2)
- Provide a valid Email Address (Field 3)
- If necessary, enter your Phone Number (Field 4)
- Select the type of Ticket you require (Field 5)
- Specify the number of Tickets you need (Field 6)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: The First Name field is required and is used to collect your name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your personal documentation.
* **Last Name**: The Last Name field is required and is used to collect your surname. Please enter your last name as it appears on your personal documentation.
* **Email**: The Email field is required and is used to collect your email address. Please enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you with ticket details.
* **Phone**: The Phone field is optional and is used to collect your phone number. If you have any issues or concerns, we may use this number to contact you.
* **Ticket Type**: The Ticket Type field is required and is used to select the type of ticket you need. Please choose from the options provided:
	+ Adult
	+ Child
	+ Family
	+ Student
* **Ticket Quantity**: The Ticket Quantity field is optional and is used to specify the number of tickets you need. Please enter the number of tickets you require, taking into account your selected Ticket Type.
