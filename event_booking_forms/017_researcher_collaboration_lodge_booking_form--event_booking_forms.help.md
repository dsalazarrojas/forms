# Researcher Collaboration Lodge Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Researcher Collaboration Lodge Booking Form is used to book a meeting room for researchers to collaborate on a project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "reservation_details" field with your reservation information.
2. Select a contact person from the "reservationist" dropdown list.
3. Enter your contact details in the "contact_details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Details** (text): Enter your project details, including date, time, and duration of the booking.
* **Contact Person** (select_one):
	+ Choose the contact person responsible for this collaboration.
* **Contact Details** (text): Provide your contact information, including name and email.
* **Submit** (text): Click to send your booking request.
