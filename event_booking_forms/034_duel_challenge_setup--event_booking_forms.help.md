# Duel Challenge Setup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to set up a duel challenge, collecting information for event management and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields for Participant 1 and Participant 2, including their details, name, tag, rank, and agreement terms.
* Select the type of duel (Singles, Doubles, or Team Battle).
* Set the duel time and time zone.
* Specify the duel duration.
* Define the duel rules.
* Review and agree to the terms of the duel, including agreement to terms and conditions, duel rules, and comments.
* Confirm your agreement to participate in the duel.
* Enter any comments or notes about the duel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Duel Challenge Setup Form** (`duel_challenge_setup_form`, `text`, required): This field is the main title of the form.
* **Duel Type** (`duel_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of duel (Singles, Doubles, or Team Battle).
* **Participant 1 Details** (`participant1_details`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of Participant 1.
* **Participant 1 Name** (`participant1_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of Participant 1.
* **Participant 1 Tag** (`participant1_tag`, `text`, required): Enter a tag or nickname for Participant 1.
* **Participant 1 Rank** (`participant1_rank`, `text`, required): Enter the rank of Participant 1.
* **Participant 1 Agreement** (`participant1_agreement`, `note`, required): Review and agree to the terms of Participant 1's participation.
* **Participant 2 Details** (`participant2_details`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of Participant 2.
* **Participant 2 Name** (`participant2_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of Participant 2.
* **Participant 2 Tag** (`participant2_tag`, `text`, required): Enter a tag or nickname for Participant 2.
* **Participant 2 Rank** (`participant2_rank`, `text`, required): Enter the rank of Participant 2.
* **Participant 2 Agreement** (`participant2_agreement`, `note`, required): Review and agree to the terms of Participant 2's participation.
* **Duel Time** (`duel_time`, `date`, required): Set the time for the duel.
* **Duel Time Zone** (`duel_time_zone`, `text`, required): Enter the time zone for the duel.
* **Duel Duration** (`duel_duration`, `number`, required): Specify the duration of the duel.
* **Duel Rules** (`duel_rules`, `text`, required): Define the rules for the duel.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `note`, required): Review and agree to the terms of the duel.
* **Duel Agreement** (`duel_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required): Select agreements related to the duel, including terms and conditions, duel rules, and comments.
* **Comments** (`duel_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any comments or notes about the duel.
* **Comments Agreement** (`duel_comments_agreement`, `note`, required): Review and agree to the terms of the comments.
* **Agreement to Duel** (`duel_agreement_agreement`, `note`, required): Confirm your agreement to participate in the duel.
* **Agreement to Duel Agreement** (`duel_agreement_agreement_agreement`, `note`, required): Confirm your agreement to the terms of the duel agreement.
* **Chatjimmy** (`chatjimmy`, `text`, required): Enter information for Chatjimmy, a specific field likely used for internal purposes.
* **Chatjimmy Agreement** (`chatjimmy_agreement`, `note`, required): Review and agree to the terms related to Chatjimmy.
* **Chatjimmy Agreement Agreement** (`chatjimmy_agreement_agreement`, `note`, required): Confirm your agreement to the terms of Chatjimmy agreement.
