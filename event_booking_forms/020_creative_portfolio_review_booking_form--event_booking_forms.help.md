# creative_portfolio_review_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The creative_portfolio_review_booking_form is a form designed to capture information for a review session of a creative portfolio. It is meant to be completed by a reviewer to provide feedback on a portfolio submission. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the review session date in the format of MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2024) in the "review_session" field.
2. If applicable, provide the link to the portfolio in the "portfolio_link" field.
3. Enter the time you plan to spend on the review in the "review_time" field. 
4. Add any additional contact details in the "contact_details" field. 
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field, which will be used to contact you after the review. 
6. If you have a phone number, add it in the "phone" field.
7. Click the "submit" button to submit your review request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **review_session (1)** (Date): The date of the review session.
* **portfolio_link (2)** (Text): A link to the portfolio you are reviewing (optional).
* **review_time (3)** (Time): The amount of time you plan to spend reviewing the portfolio (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour).
* **contact_details (4)** (Note): Any additional contact information you'd like to provide.
* **email (5)** (Email): Your email address for follow-up communication after the review.
* **phone (6)** (Text): Your phone number (optional).
* **submit (7)** (Text): Click to submit your review request.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Reviewers are encouraged to provide specific feedback and suggestions for improvement. 
* If you have any questions or need clarification on the review session, don't hesitate to contact the administrator.
