# event_venue_communication_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The event_venue_communication_form is used to collect event details and contact information from the user, allowing us to efficiently communicate with the venue and event organisers regarding event bookings and organisation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the event details section with all the necessary information about your event.
2. Provide your contact details in the contact information section.
3. Let us know who will be the organiser of the event by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the Organising Team section.
4. Enter any additional venue details if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: Enter a brief description of your event, including date, time, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Organising Team**: This field is used to indicate if you or someone else will be the organiser of the event. Select "Yes" if you are the primary contact person, or "No" if someone else is.
* **Venue Details**: Enter any additional details about the venue where the event will be held, such as the address or any specific requirements.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill in all the required fields to avoid any issues with your booking.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, feel free to reach out to us.
