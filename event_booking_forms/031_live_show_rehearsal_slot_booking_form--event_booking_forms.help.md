# Live Show Rehearsal Slot Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Live Show Rehearsal Slot Booking Form is designed to assist production teams in booking a specific time slot for live shows. This form provides a convenient way to schedule rehearsals and associated details.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the available time slot for the rehearsal from the options provided.
2. Enter the start date of the rehearsal.
3. Specify the duration of the rehearsal.
4. Provide the booking time, which is the time slot chosen in step 1.
5. Enter the production name.
6. Enter the venue details.
7. Enter the event name and event date if applicable.
8. Provide the production contact information.
9. Enter the booking status and notes if necessary.
10. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Live Show Rehearsal Slot Booking** (`slot_booking_form`): Select the time slot for the rehearsal from the available options.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the start date of the rehearsal.
* **Duration** (`duration`): Specify the duration of the rehearsal in minutes.
* **Booking Time** (`booking_time`): Confirm the selected time slot for the rehearsal.
* **Production Name** (`production_name`): Enter the name of the production team or company.
* **Venue** (`venue`): Enter the venue details for the rehearsal.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event or show.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event or show, if applicable.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Enter the time of the event or show, if applicable.
* **Production Contact** (`production_contact`): Enter the contact information of the production team or company.
* **Production Email** (`production_email`): Enter the email address of the production team or company.
* **Production Phone** (`production_phone`): Enter the phone number of the production team or company.
* **Booking Status** (`booking_status`): Select the current status of the booking (Pending, Accepted, or Rejected).
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or notes about the booking.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Click to submit the form and complete the booking.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct time slot for the rehearsal from the available options.
* Ensure that the start date and duration are accurate.
* Enter all required information for the production team or company.
* Double-check the event details before submitting the form.
* If there are any changes or updates to the booking, use the notes section to provide additional context.
