# product_demo_counter_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect product demonstration and contact preferences from customers or potential customers to facilitate the demonstration process and follow-up on customer interests.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant fields and provide the required information. Please ensure that you fill out all the fields marked as "required" to help us understand your product demonstration needs.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of the product you are interested in demonstrating. This will help us understand your product requirements and preferences.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, text, required): Please let us know your preferred contact method (e.g., phone, email, or in-person) and any specific times or dates you are available for the demonstration.
* **Demo Preferences** (`demo_preferences`, text, required): Describe your product demonstration needs, such as any specific features or functionalities you would like to see or any questions you have about the product.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible in your responses to help us better understand your product demonstration needs.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please let us know and we will do our best to accommodate them.
* We will follow up with you to confirm your demonstration details and preferences.
