# Playdate Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Playdate Reservation Form is a simple form designed to collect information from parents for booking a playdate for their children. This form is meant to be completed by parents to provide their child's playdate details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill in the "Playdate Info" section with the details about your child's playdate.
* In the "Parent Info" section, provide your contact details so we can reach out to you.
* If your child has any allergies, please list them in the "Allergies" section.
* Select the options for "Consent" to give permission for the playdate to proceed.
* Choose the "Start Time" and "End Time" for the playdate.
* Select the "Date" for the playdate.
* Add any additional notes or information in the "Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Playdate Info** (`playdate_info`, text, optional):
	+ This is the main section for providing information about your child's playdate.
* **Parent Info** (`parent_info`, text, optional):
	+ Please provide your contact details so we can reach out to you.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional):
	+ List any allergies your child may have so we can take necessary precautions.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select multiple, optional):
	+ Give permission for the playdate to proceed. Select one or more options to indicate your consent.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional):
	+ Choose the start time for the playdate.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional):
	+ Choose the end time for the playdate.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional):
	+ Select the date for the playdate.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional):
	+ Add any additional information or notes about the playdate.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when filling in the form.
* Make sure to check the "Consent" options to ensure you are giving proper permission for the playdate.
* If you need to provide any additional information, use the "Notes" section to do so.
* If you have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact us at <contact email>.
