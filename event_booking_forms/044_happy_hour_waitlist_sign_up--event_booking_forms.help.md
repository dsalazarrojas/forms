# Happy Hour Waitlist Sign-up - Help Guide

## Purpose
The happy-hr-waitlist-sign-up form is a tool for customers to join the Happy Hour waitlist. It is used to gather essential details from customers who wish to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Happy Hour details in the first field.
2. Provide your customer name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. (Optional) Enter your phone number.
5. Indicate the number of people in your party.
6. Select a date and time for the desired Happy Hour.
7. Choose your preferred table size (if applicable).
8. Identify your position on the waitlist.
9. Add any additional notes (if required).
10. Confirm your registration status.
11. Select any additional confirmations or options as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Happy Hour Details** (required, text): Enter the details about the Happy Hour event you are interested in, e.g., date and time.
* **Customer Name** (required, text): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (required, email): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (optional, text): Enter your phone number (if you would like to be contacted).
* **Number in People** (required, number): Indicate the number of people in your party.
* **Date and Time** (required, date): Select the date and time you would like to attend the Happy Hour.
* **Table Size** (optional, text): Choose the number of seats you require at the Happy Hour.
* **Waitlist Position** (required, select_one): Select your position on the waitlist:
  • First in line
  • Second in line
  • Third in line
* **Notes** (optional, note): Add any additional information about your Happy Hour reservation.
* **Confirmation** (optional, select_one): Confirm your registration status:
  • True
  • False
* **Confirmation 2** (optional, select_multiple): Select any additional options or confirmations:
  • True
  • False
* **Confirmation 3** (optional, select_multiple): Select any additional options or confirmations:
  • True
  • False

## Tips
Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in each field to ensure smooth processing of your Happy Hour waitlist registration. Only fill out fields that are relevant to your situation and needs.
