# Balloon Flights Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Balloon Flights Booking Form is designed to gather information for booking a successful and safe balloon flight. Please follow this guide to complete the form accurately and ensure a smooth experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your lead passenger's name, contact email, and contact phone number.
2. Select a preferred date and time for the flight.
3. Indicate the number of passengers, including yourself.
4. Provide the combined weight of all passengers in kilograms.
5. Enter the pickup or meeting location for the flight.
6. If necessary, specify any special requirements or mobility needs.
7. Enter the name and phone number of the emergency contact person.
8. Choose the payment method for your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Lead passenger full name** (`lead_passenger_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the lead passenger. This is the primary contact person for the booking.
- **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address you would like to receive booking confirmations and tickets.
- **Contact phone number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number you would like to be reached in case of any issues or questions.
- **Preferred flight date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Select a date for the flight from the available options.
- **Preferred flight time** (`preferred_time`, time, required): Choose the time slot you prefer for the flight. Morning flights are ideal for best wind conditions.
- **Number of passengers** (`number_of_passengers`, number, required): Enter the total number of passengers, including yourself.
- **Total combined weight kg** (`total_weight_kg`, number, required): Provide the combined weight of all passengers in kilograms for safety planning purposes.
- **Pickup or meeting location** (`pickup_location`, text, required): Enter a landmark or address for the meeting point.
- **Special requirements or mobility notes** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any special requirements or mobility needs, please specify them here.
- **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name and phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
- **Payment method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to pay for your booking: Credit card, Bank transfer, or On-site cash.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely to ensure a smooth booking process.
