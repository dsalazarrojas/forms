# Stage Props Workshop Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stage Props Workshop Booking Form is designed to collect essential information from participants for a Stage Props Workshop. This information includes contact details, booking and workshop dates and times, and any additional information relevant to the booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields (marked as `required: true`) to ensure that your booking is processed correctly.
2. Enter your participant name, email, contact information, address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
3. Select the correct options for "Select One" and "Select Multiple" fields as per your preference.
4. Enter the booking date and time, workshop date and time, and number of guests.
5. Add any additional information in the "Additional Information" field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Participant Information
* **Name**: Enter your participant name here. This is a required field to identify you for the booking.
* **Email**: Enter your participant email here. This field is required to contact you for further communication.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your participant contact information here. Although not required, it is recommended for us to reach out to you with any updates.
* **Address**: Enter your participant address here. This field is optional but recommended for further communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your participant phone number here. This field is optional but recommended for us to contact you.

### 2. Booking Information
* **Booking Date**: Enter the date of the booking here. This field is required for scheduling purposes.
* **Booking Time**: Enter the time of the booking here. This field is required for scheduling purposes.
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of guests attending the workshop here. This field is required for logistical purposes.
* **Workshop Date**: Enter the date of the workshop here. This field is required for scheduling purposes.
* **Workshop Time**: Enter the time of the workshop here. This field is required for scheduling purposes.

### 3. Additional Information
* **Additional Information**: Enter any additional information here that may be relevant to your booking. This field is not required but recommended for us to better understand your needs.
