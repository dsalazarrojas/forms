# Forest Eco Retreat Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Forest Eco Retreat Booking Form is designed to gather information from users for booking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your name and contact information in the "Guest Name" field.
3. Select the check-in and check-out dates in the "Check In" and "Check Out" fields respectively.
4. Choose the number of guests in the "Number of Guests" field.
5. Select the accommodation type from the dropdown menu in the "Accommodation Type" field.
6. If you have any special requests, enter them in the "Special Requests" field.
7. Choose the booking status as "Booked" or "Not Booked" in the "Booking Status" field.
8. If you have any comments about your booking, enter them in the "Booking Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Check In** (`check_in_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you plan to check-in at the retreat.
* **Check Out** (`check_out_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you plan to check-out from the retreat.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_guests`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of guests that will be staying with you.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of accommodation you require, such as Private Room, Shared Room, or Camping.
* **Accommodation Details** (`accommodation_details`, text, required/optional): If you have any specific requests for your accommodation, enter them here.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required/optional): Enter any special requests you may have, such as dietary requirements or equipment needs.
* **Booking Status** (`booking_status`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the status of your booking as "Booked" or "Not Booked".
* **Booking Comments** (`booking_comments`, text, required/optional): If you have any comments about your booking, enter them here.
