# gaming_tournament_stream_booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book and manage gaming tournament streams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stream Booking Info** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the stream booking.
* **Stream Type** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the types of streams that this booking is for (e.g. "Stream 1", "Stream 2", "Stream 3", "Stream 4", "Stream 5").
* **Stream Date** (`3`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the stream in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Stream Time** (`4`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the stream in hh:mm format.
* **Stream Duration** (`5`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the length of the stream in minutes.
* **Stream URL** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the URL of the stream if available.
* **Stream Notes** (`7`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the stream.
* **Stream Booking Status** (`8`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of the stream booking ("Not Started", "Started", "Finished").
* **Game Name** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the game being streamed.
* **Game Version** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the version of the game being streamed.
* **Team Name** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the team participating in the stream.
* **Team Size** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the size of the team participating in the stream.
* **Streamer Name** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the streamer.
* **Streamer Phone** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the streamer's phone number.
* **Streamer Email** (`15`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the streamer's email address.
* **Streamer Platform** (`16`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the platform used by the streamer (e.g. Twitch, YouTube, etc.).
* **Streamer URL** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the streamer's URL.
* **Streamer Social Media** (`18`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the streamer's social media handle.
* **Streamer Language** (`19`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the language spoken by the streamer ("English", "French", "Spanish", "Other").
* **Streamer Timezone** (`20`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the time zone of the streamer.
* **Streamer Country** (`21`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the country of the streamer.
* **Streamer Region** (`22`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the region of the streamer.
* **Streamer City** (`23`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the city of the streamer.
* **Streamer Zip** (`24`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the zip code of the streamer.
* **Streamer Agreement** (`25`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or terms that the streamer may need to agree to.
