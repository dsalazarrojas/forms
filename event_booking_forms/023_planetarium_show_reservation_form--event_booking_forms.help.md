The Planetarium Show Reservation Form is designed to help event organizers and attendees book and manage events at the Planetarium. This form is used to collect information from visitors about their event preferences and provide a way for event staff to manage and organize the events efficiently.

The form has the following pages:

*   **Event**: This field allows you to select the specific event you would like to book.
*   **Date**: Choose a date for your event.
*   **Time**: Choose a time for your event.
*   **Name**: Enter your name as the person booking the event.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for communication.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
*   **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of guests that will be attending your event.
*   **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or requests for your event.

# Planetarium Show Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Planetarium Show Reservation Form is designed to make it easy for you to book and manage events at the Planetarium.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the event you would like to book from the "Event" field.
2.  Choose a date for your event in the "Date" field.
3.  Choose a time for your event in the "Time" field.
4.  Enter your name, email address, and phone number for your contact information in the "Name", "Email", and "Phone" fields respectively.
5.  Enter the number of guests that will be attending your event in the "Number of Guests" field.
6.  Enter any additional comments or requests in the "Comments" field.
7.  Once you have completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to complete your booking.
