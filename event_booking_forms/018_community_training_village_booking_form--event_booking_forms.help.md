# Community Training Village Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Training Village Booking Form is a tool used to request space booking for events and training sessions in the village.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully review each field to ensure you are providing accurate and complete information.
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- If you are booking on behalf of a group, please enter your organization's name.
- Enter your contact email for booking confirmations.
- Choose a specific date and time for your booking.
- Provide accurate start and end times for your booking.
- If applicable, indicate the number of attendees expected.
- Select the equipment needed for your booking.
- If necessary, add any special instructions for facility setup or access.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester full name**: This field is a text input where you should enter your name as the person booking the space.
* **Organization name**: If you are booking on behalf of a group, enter your organization's name here.
* **Contact email**: Enter your email address so we can send booking confirmations and updates to you.
* **Booking date**: Choose a specific date for your booking.
* **Start time**: Enter the start time of your booking.
* **End time**: Enter the end time of your booking.
* **Expected number of attendees**: If you know how many people will be attending, please enter that number. This helps us with room assignment.
* **Equipment needed**: Select the equipment you require for your booking. You can choose from a list of options.
* **Special instructions**: If you need any special instructions for facility setup or access, please provide them here.

## Tips
- Be accurate and specific when filling out the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the facilities team for assistance.
- Make sure to review your form carefully before submitting it.
