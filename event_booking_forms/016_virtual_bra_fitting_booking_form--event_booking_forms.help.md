# Virtual Bra Fitting Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Bra Fitting Booking Form is designed to allow customers to schedule an online appointment for a bra fitting. Fill out this form to reserve a time slot for a virtual consultation with our bra fitting expert.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all required information ready.
2.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date details.
3.  Review and submit the form once you have filled out all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed during the appointment.
*  **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and confirmation on your appointment schedule.
*  **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Select the date and time you would like to schedule your virtual bra fitting appointment. Please use the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
