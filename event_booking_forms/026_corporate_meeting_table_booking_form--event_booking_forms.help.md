# corporate_meeting_table_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Corporate Meeting Table Booking Form" is a form designed to collect information about corporate meetings, allowing staff members and guests to request and book a meeting table within the corporate environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the meeting topic or subject matter (Meeting Topic).
2. Choose a seating preference for the meeting (Seating Preference).
3. Select the number of attendees (Attendees Count).
4. List the attendees (Attendees).
5. Specify any special requests or requirements for the meeting (Special Requests).
6. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Details** (meeting_details, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the meeting topic or subject matter.
* **Attendees** (attendees, number, required: true): Select the number of people attending the meeting.
* **Seating Preference** (seating_preference, select_one, required: false): Choose the seating arrangement for the meeting:
	+ Table: Traditional meeting table with chairs.
	+ Sofa: Meeting area with a sofa.
	+ Conference: Conference room with a large table and chairs.
	+ Roundtable: Circular meeting table with chairs.
* **Number of Attendees** (attendees_count, number, required: true): Enter the exact number of people attending the meeting.
* **Meeting Topic** (meeting_topic, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the meeting topic or subject matter.
* **Room Requirements** (room_requirements, text, required: false): If there are any special room requirements, enter them in this field.
* **Special Requests** (special_requests, text, required: false): Enter any special requests or requirements for the meeting, such as equipment needs or dietary restrictions.

## Tips
Ensure that you enter accurate and relevant information to help staff prepare for the meeting accordingly.
