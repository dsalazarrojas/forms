# Cultural Event Vendor Booth Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a vendor booth for a cultural event. It collects essential information from vendors, such as their contact details, booth requirements, and submission deadlines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in your name and email address** (Page 1, Name and Email fields): Enter your name and email address to provide a way for event organizers to contact you.
2. **Select your booth type** (Page 1, Booth Type field): Choose the type of booth you are booking (Booth, Food, Beverage, or Other).
3. **Choose your booth size** (Page 1, Booth Size field): Select the size of your booth (Small, Medium, or Large).
4. **Add any requirements** (Page 1, Requirements field): If you have any specific requirements for your booth, such as electrical or internet access, please describe them in this field.
5. **Submit your booking** (Page 1, Submission field): Enter the date and time you expect to submit your materials or setup for the event.
6. **Select your setup time** (Page 1, Time field): Choose the time you plan to set up your booth on the event day.
7. **Add any notes** (Page 1, Notes field): If you have any additional information or requests, use this field to provide them to event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address for event organizers to contact you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for event organizers to reach you.
* **Booth Type** (`Booth Type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of booth you are booking (Booth, Food, Beverage, or Other).
* **Booth Size** (`Booth Size`, select_one, required): Select the size of your booth (Small, Medium, or Large).
* **Requirements** (`Requirements`, text, required): Describe any specific requirements you have for your booth.
* **Submission** (`Submission`, date, required): Enter the date and time you expect to submit your materials or setup for the event.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required): Choose the time you plan to set up your booth on the event day.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, required): Add any additional information or requests you have for event organizers.
