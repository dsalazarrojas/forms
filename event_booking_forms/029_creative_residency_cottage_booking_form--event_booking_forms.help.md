# Creative Residency Cottage Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by individuals or teams looking to book a creative residency cottage for a stay. It is intended to collect the necessary information to facilitate the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
2. Ensure that the start and end dates for the stay are correct and realistic.
3. Provide a brief description of the project, including its name and type.
4. Enter the guest's contact information, including their name, email, and phone number.
5. Enter the cottage's contact information, including its name, address, description, phone number, and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booking Request**: Enter a brief description of why you are booking the cottage.
* **Stay Start Date**: Enter the date you plan to start your stay at the cottage (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Stay End Date**: Enter the date you plan to end your stay at the cottage (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Guest Name**: Enter the name of the guest booking the cottage.
* **Guest Email**: Enter the email of the guest booking the cottage.
* **Guest Phone**: Enter the phone number of the guest booking the cottage.
* **Guest Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments from the guest.
* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project you will be working on during your stay.
* **Project Description**: Enter a brief description of the project.
* **Project Type**: Select the type of project you will be working on (Solo Project, Collaborative Project, or Residency).
* **Project Location**: Enter the location where the project will take place.
* **Cottage Name**: Enter the name of the cottage you are booking.
* **Cottage Address**: Enter the address of the cottage.
* **Cottage Description**: Enter a brief description of the cottage.
* **Cottage Phone**: Enter the phone number of the cottage.
* **Cottage Email**: Enter the email of the cottage.
* **Cottage Website**: Enter the website of the cottage.
* **Cottage Image**: Enter an image of the cottage (optional).
* **Stay Cost**: Enter the cost of the stay in the selected currency.
* **Stay Currency**: Select the currency of the stay (USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, JPY).
* **Stay Length**: Enter the length of the stay in days.
* **Cancellation Policy**: Enter any cancellation policy or notes.
* **Cancellation Fee**: Enter any cancellation fee associated with the stay.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method for the stay (Bank Transfer, PayPal, Credit Card).
