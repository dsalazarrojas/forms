# Conference Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This Conference Reservation Form is designed to collect essential details from conference attendees for the purpose of planning their event stay, including their contact information, preferences for room type and meeting room size, and requirements such as meal plans and audio-visual equipment. The information collected in this form will help event planners to effectively organize the event and provide a comfortable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your Full Name**: Fill out your full legal name in the "Full Name" field.
2. **Enter your Email Address**: Provide your email address to ensure you receive updates and information about your event stay.
3. **Choose your Check-in and Check-out Dates**: Select the dates you plan to arrive and depart from the hotel or conference center.
4. **Select your Room Type Preference**: Choose the type of room that suits your needs, such as Single Room, Double Room, or Executive Suite.
5. **Enter the Number of Guests**: Specify the total number of people that will be attending the event.
6. **Select Meeting Room Size Requirement**: Choose the size of the meeting room you need, considering the number of attendees.
7. **Choose Audio-Visual Equipment**: Select all the equipment you require for your event from the provided options.
8. **Choose Meal Plan Selection**: Select the meal plan that suits your preferences, such as Breakfast Only, Half Board, or Full Board.
9. **Specify Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: If you have any specific dietary restrictions or allergies, please list them in the provided text box.
10. **Add Special Requests or Additional Information**: If you have any special requests or need to provide additional information, please specify it in the provided text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (2, `guest_full_name`): Enter your full legal name.
* **Email Address** (3, `email_address`): Provide your email address to receive updates and information about your event stay.
* **Check-in Date** (4, `check_in_date`): Select the date you plan to arrive at the conference center or hotel.
* **Check-out Date** (5, `check_out_date`): Select the date you plan to depart from the conference center or hotel.
* **Room Type Preference** (6, `room_type`): Choose the type of room that suits your needs, such as Single Room, Double Room, or Executive Suite.
* **Number of Guests** (7, `number_of_guests`): Specify the total number of people that will be attending the event.
* **Meeting Room Size Requirement** (9, `meeting_room_size`): Choose the size of the meeting room you need, considering the number of attendees.
* **Audio-Visual Equipment Needed** (10, `av_equipment_needed`): Select all the equipment you require for your event from the provided options.
* **Meal Plan Selection** (12, `meal_plan`): Select the meal plan that suits your preferences, such as Breakfast Only, Half Board, or Full Board.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (13, `dietary_restrictions`): If you have any specific dietary restrictions or allergies, please list them in the provided text box.
* **Special Requests or Additional Information** (14, `special_requests`): If you have any special requests or need to provide additional information, please specify it in the provided text box.
