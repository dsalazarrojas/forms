# Floral Wall Display Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Floral Wall Display Booking Form is used to gather information about an event, including its name, terms and conditions, floral wall reservations, event notes, contact information, and confirmation of terms and conditions. This information will be used to process and confirm the booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct options for the "Event Details" section.
2. Confirm your agreement to the "Terms and Conditions" by selecting "Yes".
3. Choose your floral wall reservations by selecting the options in the "Floral Wall Reservations" section.
4. Provide any additional notes about the event in the "Event Notes" section.
5. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
6. Confirm your agreement to the "Terms and Conditions" again by selecting "Yes" in the "Confirm Terms and Conditions" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Details**: This section allows you to enter the name and details of the event. Please enter the name of the event and provide any necessary information to help us understand your requirements. 
- **Terms and Conditions**: Please review the terms and conditions of the booking process and select "Yes" if you agree to them. This will help us understand your commitment to the event.
- **Floral Wall Reservations**: Here, you can select which floral wall displays you would like to reserve for the event. Please choose the options that apply to your event.
- **Event Notes**: If you have any additional comments or notes about the event, please enter them here. This will help us better understand your needs and preferences.
- **Contact Information**: Please enter your contact information, so we can reach out to you with any questions or updates about the event.
- **Confirm Terms and Conditions**: Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions again. Please select "Yes" to proceed with the booking.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions before submitting the form.
- Double-check that you have selected all necessary options for the floral wall reservations.
- Enter accurate contact information to ensure that we can reach out to you in a timely manner.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
