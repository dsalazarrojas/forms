# Luxury Glamping Tent Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Luxury Glamping Tent Booking Form is used to gather information from guests to book a luxury glamping experience. The form helps the site prepare for the guest's stay and ensures they have a personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure your booking details are correct.
- Please note that all fields marked as required must be filled out.
- If you have any special requests, please use the "Special Requests" field to let us know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you for any updates or information about your stay.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of any emergencies or questions.
* **Checkin**: Select the date you want to check in to the glamping site.
* **Checkout**: Select the date you want to check out from the glamping site.
* **Glamping Site**: Choose the type of accommodation you would like to stay in (Tent, RV, Cabin, House, or Yurt).
* **Glamping Site Type**: Choose one or more of the glamping site types available (Tent, RV, Cabin, House, or Yurt).
* **Guest Count**: Enter the number of guests that will be staying with you.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you would like to pay for your booking (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests, such as extra bedding or equipment, please let us know here. 

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled out for your booking to be processed.
