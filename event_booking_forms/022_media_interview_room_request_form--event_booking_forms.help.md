# Media Interview Room Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a media interview room for events such as video interviews, photo shoots, or filming.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have a valid reason for requesting a media interview room.
3. Review and confirm that you are not duplicating an existing request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Event Date** (`event_date`, date)
The Event Date is the date you are planning to use the media interview room. Select the date from the calendar picker.

### 2. **Event Time** (`event_time`, time)
The Event Time is the start time of your event. Select the time from the options provided.

### 3. **Event Duration** (`event_duration`, text)
The Event Duration is the length of time you plan to use the media interview room. Enter the duration in hours and minutes (e.g., 1 hour, 2 hours, etc.).

### 4. **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one)
Select the type of event you are hosting. This will help us understand the requirements for your event.

### 5. **Event Name** (`event_name`, text)
Enter the name of your event. This will help us identify your event on our calendar.

### 6. **Room Type** (`room_type`, select_multiple)
Select the type of room you need for your event. Choose one or multiple options as needed.

### 7. **Room Number** (`room_number`, number)
Enter the room number of the media interview room you are reserving. 

### 8. **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text)
Enter your name as the contact person for this event. This will ensure that we can reach out to you with any updates or changes.

### 9. **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email)
Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding this event.

### 10. **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text)
Enter your phone number as the contact person for this event.

### 11. **Event Description** (`event_description`, text)
Provide a brief description of your event, including any specific needs or requirements.

### 12. **Notes** (`notes`, note)
Add any additional notes or comments regarding your event.
