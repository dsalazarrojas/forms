# Early Booking Reservation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Early Booking Reservation Request Form is designed to help you book a room for a specific date range. This form allows you to submit your personal contact information, room preferences, and any special requests you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow the instructions below:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you for updates and notifications.
* **Checkin Date** (`checkin_date`, date, required): Select the date you wish to check-in for your stay.
* **Checkout Date** (`checkout_date`, date, required): Select the date you wish to check-out for your stay.
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of room you prefer (Single, Double, Suite).
* **Adults** (`adults`, number, required): Enter the number of adults staying with you.
* **Children** (`children`, number, optional): If there are children in your party, please enter the number of children.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or requirements, please let us know here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number so we can reach you for updates or to confirm your request.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required): Please provide any additional information or notes about your request.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your request is processed efficiently and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
