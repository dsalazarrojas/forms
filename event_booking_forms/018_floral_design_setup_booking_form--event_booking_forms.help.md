# Floral Design Setup Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an upcoming event that requires floral design setup. The form includes fields for event details, design preferences, contact information, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing the event details, including the event name, date, and time. Please note that the date and time are optional, but they are required if you are planning to book the event.
2. Next, select the design preferences, including the color and style of the floral design. If you do not select a preference, we will contact you for further information.
3. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number. This information is required to ensure we can communicate with you effectively.
4. Finally, review the form submission details and submit the form to complete your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details (Required)**: Please provide a brief description of the event, including any specific details that are relevant to the floral design setup.
* **Event Date (Optional)**: If you know the date of the event, please provide it in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Event Time (Optional)**: If you know the time of the event, please provide it in 12-hour format (e.g., 02:30 PM).
* **Event Location (Required)**: Provide the location of the event where the floral design setup will take place.
* **Event Notes (Required)**: Provide any additional notes or details about the event that may be helpful for the floral design setup.
* **Design Color**: Select the color scheme you prefer for the floral design. You can choose from Red, Blue, or Green.
* **Design Style**: Select the style of the floral design you prefer. You can choose from Modern, Classical, or Rustic.
* **Client Name (Required)**: Please provide your name as the client.
* **Client Email (Required)**: Provide your email address where we can contact you.
* **Client Phone (Required)**: Provide your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Client Notes (Optional)**: Provide any additional notes or details about your preferences or requirements.
* **Form Submission (Optional)**: This field is not required and is for internal use only.
* **Form Submission Date (Optional)**: This field is not required and is for internal use only.
* **Form Submission Time (Optional)**: This field is not required and is for internal use only.
* **Form Submission Location (Optional)**: This field is not required and is for internal use only.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to ensure we can fulfill your requirements effectively.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, feel free to contact us.
* We will contact you to confirm your booking and provide further instructions once you submit the form.
