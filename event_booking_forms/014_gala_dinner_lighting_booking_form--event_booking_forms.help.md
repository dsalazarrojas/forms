# Gala Dinner Lighting Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Gala Dinner Lighting Booking Form" is designed for clients to provide necessary information for a successful event lighting setup. This form will help the lighting team understand the event's specifics, allowing them to prepare and set up the lighting to your liking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Client Details** (`client_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name or company name.
    This field is where you should enter your name or your company's name as the client for the event.
2.  **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your event.
    In this field, you should enter a short description of your event, such as its purpose and the type of event it is (e.g., wedding, conference, etc.).
3.  **Venue Address** (`venue_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the venue address.
    Here, you should enter the physical address of the venue where the event will take place.
4.  **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of the event.
    Choose the date of your event from the calendar provided. This is crucial for the lighting team to ensure they are aware of the event date.
5.  **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the event start and end time.
    In this field, select the start and end times of your event. This will help the lighting team prepare for the duration of the event.
6.  **Lighting Packages** (`lighting_packages`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the lighting packages you require for the event.
    Choose the lighting packages you need for your event. Each option may have specific features or equipment included.
7.  **Package 1 Details** (`package_1_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter package 1 details, if applicable.
    If you selected Package 1, enter the specific details related to that package, such as any customization requests.
8.  **Package 2 Details** (`package_2_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter package 2 details, if applicable.
    If you selected Package 2, enter the specific details related to that package, such as any customization requests.
9.  **Terms Agreement** (`terms_agreement`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how you would like to agree to the terms.
    Choose how you would like to agree to the terms and conditions provided. This ensures you are aware of and agree to the terms of the lighting services.
10.  **Terms Agreement Terms** (`terms_agreement_terms`, `text`, required/optional): Enter terms agreement terms, if applicable.
    If you selected how you would like to agree to the terms, enter the specific terms you are agreeing to.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* Double-check that you have selected the correct lighting packages and entered their details correctly.
* Review the terms and conditions before agreeing to them.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, do not hesitate to contact the form creators or the lighting team.
