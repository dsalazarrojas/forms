# digital_detox_mountain_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a mountain accommodation in a Digital Detox program. It is essential to provide accurate information to ensure the correct booking status and accommodation availability.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please follow these steps:
    1. Select the desired booking status (Pending, Confirmed, or Cancelled).
    2. Enter your name and email address to contact you regarding the booking.
    3. Provide your phone number in case we need to contact you.
    4. Choose the start and end dates of your stay.
    5. Select the accommodation type (Mountain Hut, Treehouse, or Shared Accommodations).
    6. Enter any additional comments or notes about your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you regarding your booking.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you want to start your stay.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you want to end your stay.
* **Accommodation** (`accommodation`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your accommodation (e.g., Mountain Hut, Treehouse, etc.).
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of accommodation you want to book (Mountain Hut, Treehouse, or Shared Accommodations).
* **Booking Status** (`booking_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the current status of your booking (Pending, Confirmed, or Cancelled).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional information about your booking.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or requests about your booking.

## Tips
- Double-check the availability of the accommodation before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you have entered accurate contact information.
- For any concerns or queries, please contact the Digital Detox program administrators.
