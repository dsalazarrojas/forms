# Banquet Event Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather event details for seamless planning and organization.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the event date and time using the date and time pickers.
2. Enter the event organizer's name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Select the type of event and provide a brief description in the event description field.
4. Choose the expected number of guests and preferred menu option.
5. Specify any dietary accommodations.
6. Select the beverage service and room setup requirements.
7. If necessary, choose additional audio/visual equipment or entertainment needs.
8. Provide any additional decorative or special request requirements.
9. Enter the estimated budget for the event.
10. Choose the payment terms and confirm the deposit amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date**: When is your event scheduled to take place?
	+ Enter the date of your event using the date picker.
* **Event Start Time**: What time would you like the event to start?
	+ Choose the start time using the time picker.
* **Event Organizer Name**: What is your name?
	+ Enter your full name.
* **Email Address**: How can we contact you?
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: What is your phone number?
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Organization or Company**: If applicable, what is your organization's name?
	+ Enter your organization's name.
* **Event Type**: What type of event is this?
	+ Choose from: Corporate Dinner, Wedding Reception, Birthday Party, Graduation Celebration, Holiday Party, Conference Banquet, Award Ceremony, Fund Raiser, Other
* **Event Description**: Briefly describe the nature of your event.
	+ Enter a concise description of your event.
* **Expected Number of Guests**: How many guests are attending?
	+ Enter the total number of attendees expected.
* **Menu Option**: What type of menu would you prefer?
	+ Choose from: Premium Menu A, Premium Menu B, Standard Menu, Buffet Style, Custom Menu, Request Menu Planning
* **Dietary Accommodations**: Do you have any dietary requirements?
	+ Enter any dietary accommodations.
* **Beverage Service**: What beverages do you need?
	+ Choose from: Non-Alcoholic Only, Beer and Wine, Full Bar, Soft Drinks Only, Water and Juice Only
* **Room Setup Preference**: How would you like the room arranged?
	+ Choose from: Theater Style, Classroom Style, Banquet Style, Cocktail Reception, U-Shape, Hollow Square, Custom Layout
* **Preferred Room or Space**: Do you have a preferred room or space?
	+ Enter your preferred room or space.
* **Audio/Visual Equipment Needed**: What equipment do you require?
	+ Choose from: Projector and Screen, Sound System, Microphone, Lighting, Video Display, DJ Service, None
* **Entertainment**: Do you want entertainment?
	+ Choose from: DJ, Live Band, Comedian, Magician, Other Professional, No Entertainment
* **Entertainment Details**: If applicable, provide additional details about the entertainment.
	+ Enter any specific entertainment details.
* **Floral or Decorative Arrangements**: Do you need any decorative arrangements?
	+ Enter any floral or decorative needs.
* **Special Requests**: Do you have any special requests?
	+ Enter any additional requirements.
* **Estimated Budget**: What is your estimated total budget for the event?
	+ Enter the total budget.
* **Payment Terms**: How will payment be arranged?
	+ Choose from: Full Payment at Booking, 50% Deposit, Payment Plan, Payment at Event
* **Deposit Amount**: What is the deposit amount?
	+ Enter the deposit amount.
* **Payment Terms**: How will payment be arranged? 
	+ Confirm the payment terms.

Note: Some fields may be optional, but it's essential to fill out all required fields for accurate event planning.
