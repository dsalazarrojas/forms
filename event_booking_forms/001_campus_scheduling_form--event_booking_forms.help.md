# Campus Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Campus Scheduling Form is designed for campus organizations to schedule events, requesting space and amenities as needed. By filling out this form, you will be able to specify event details, requirements, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  **Organization Information**: Enter the name of your campus organization and select the contact person who will be coordinating the event.
2.  **Event Details**: Provide a brief description of the event, including the title, date, start and end times, and the number of attendees expected.
3.  **Event Requirements**: Specify any necessary Audio-Visual equipment, whether catering is needed, and preferred seating arrangements.
4.  **Policy Agreement**: Check the box to agree to follow campus scheduling policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Organization Name**: Enter the full name of your campus organization.
*   **Contact Person**: Name of the person coordinating the event.
*   **Email Address**: Primary contact email for the event.
*   **Phone Number**: Primary phone number of the contact person.
*   **Event Title**: A short title or description for the event.
*   **Event Description**: Briefly describe the purpose of the event.
*   **Event Date**: Select the date for your event.
*   **Preferred Start Time**: Choose your preferred start time for the event.
*   **Preferred End Time**: Choose your preferred end time for the event.
*   **Location Preference**: Select from the following options: Lecture Hall, Campus Center, Student Lounge, Athletic Field, or Auditorium.
*   **Expected Attendance**: Estimate the number of participants attending your event.
*   **Audio-Visual Equipment**: Select all that apply, including Projector, Microphone, Sound System, Wi-Fi Access, or None.
*   **Catering Required**: Check if you need catering for your event. (Note: Select 'True' for yes, 'False' for no).
*   **Furniture Arrangement**: Select from the following options: Classroom, Theatre, U-Shape, Banquet, or Standing Only.
*   **Special Requests**: Add any other specific needs for the event.
*   **Agreement**: Check the box to agree to follow campus scheduling policies.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
*   Review the field explanations to ensure you understand each field's purpose.
*   If you are unsure about any field, reach out to the event coordinator for clarification.
