# BACO Event Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the BACO Event Booking form. The form is designed to collect information about attendees for the BACO event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Attendee name** in the "Attendee name" field. This should be your full name as it appears on your badge.
2. Enter your **Organization** name and affiliation in the "Organization" field. This should be your current or past employer.
3. Enter your **Email address** for confirmation and ticketing purposes in the "Email address" field.
4. Select your preferred **Ticket type** from the options provided. This will determine your access to certain parts of the event.
5. If applicable, list any **Dietary requirements** you have in the "Dietary requirements" field.
6. If you require special accommodations, list your **Accessibility needs** in the "Accessibility needs" field.
7. Choose the **Sessions of interest** from the list of provided options in the "Sessions of interest" field. You can select multiple sessions.
8. If you are planning to arrive before the event, enter your **Arrival date** in the "Arrival date" field.
9. For emergency purposes, provide your **Emergency contact** name and phone number in the "Emergency contact" field.
10. Finally, if you have any **Special requests**, enter them in the "Special requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee name** (`attendee_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your badge.
* **Organization** (`organization`, Text, Optional): Enter your affiliation or employer.
* **Email address** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address for confirmation and ticketing purposes.
* **Ticket type** (`ticket_type`, Select One, Required): Select the type of ticket that suits your needs.
	+ Options: Full conference, Single day, Student, Virtual
* **Dietary requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, Text, Optional): If you have any food restrictions, list them here.
* **Accessibility needs** (`accessibility_needs`, Text, Optional): If you require special accommodations, list them here.
* **Sessions of interest** (`sessions_interested`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the sessions that interest you the most.
	+ Options: Clinical, Research, Training, Industry
* **Arrival date** (`arrival_date`, Date, Optional): For accommodation planning purposes, enter your expected arrival date.
* **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, Text, Optional): Provide the name and phone number of a contact in case of an emergency.
* **Special requests** (`special_requests`, Text, Optional): Enter any other requests you may have.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you need any assistance or have questions, feel free to ask.
- This form is designed to be user-friendly, so don't hesitate to ask if you get stuck!
