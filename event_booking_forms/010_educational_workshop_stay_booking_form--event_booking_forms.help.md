# Educational Workshop Stay Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to book educational workshops, including providing details about the workshop, dates and time, participant count, and accommodation preferences. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure smooth communication and successful workshop organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the Workshop Name field with the name of the workshop you are booking.
2. Enter the Organiser Name of the person or team responsible for the workshop.
3. Select the Dates and Time for the workshop.
4. Choose the Participant Count for the workshop.
5. Select the Accommodation Type that suits your needs.
6. Provide any additional Accommodation Request in the text field.
7. Select any additional Participant Information you would like to share (such as participant name, age, and contact info).
8. Enter your Email address for communication purposes.
9. Enter your Phone number for easy contact.
10. If necessary, leave a Participant Message or Message for the organizers.
11. Select any relevant Payment Information (method, date, and status).
12. Enter your Submitter Email address.
13. Enter your Submitter Name.
14. Enter your Submitter Phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Workshop Name (<label> `workshop_name`)
This field requires the name of the workshop being booked.

*   **Required**: No
*   **Type**: text
*   **Description**: Enter the name of the workshop.

### Organiser Name (<label> `organiser_name`)
This field requires the name of the person or team responsible for the workshop.

*   **Required**: No
*   **Type**: text
*   **Description**: Enter the name of the person or team responsible for the workshop.

### Dates (<label> `dates`)
This field requires the dates of the workshop.

*   **Required**: No
*   **Type**: date
*   **Description**: Enter the dates of the workshop.

### Time (<label> `time`)
This field requires the time of the workshop.

*   **Required**: No
*   **Type**: time
*   **Description**: Enter the time of the workshop.

### Participant Count (<label> `participant_count`)
This field requires the number of participants expected.

*   **Required**: No
*   **Type**: number
*   **Description**: Enter the expected participant count.

### Accommodation Type (<label> `accommodation_type`)
This field requires the choice of accommodation type.

*   **Required**: No
*   **Type**: select_one
*   **Description**: Choose from: Hotel, Onsite

### Accommodation Request (<label> `accommodation_request`)
This field allows for any additional accommodation requests.

*   **Required**: No
*   **Type**: text
*   **Description**: Enter any additional accommodation requests.

### Participant Info (<label> `participant_info`)
This field allows selection of additional participant information.

*   **Required**: No
*   **Type**: select_multiple
*   **Description**: Choose from: Participant Name, Participant Age, Participant Contact Info

### Email (<label> `email`)
This field requires the email address of the participant or submitter.

*   **Required**: No
*   **Type**: email
*   **Description**: Enter your email address.

### Phone (<label> `phone`)
This field requires the phone number of the participant or submitter.

*   **Required**: No
*   **Type**: text
*   **Description**: Enter your phone number.

### Participant Message (<label> `participant_message`)
This field allows for any additional message for the participant.

*   **Required**: No
*   **Type**: text
*   **Description**: Enter any additional message for the participant.

### Payment Info (<label> `payment_info`)
This field allows selection of payment information.

*   **Required**: No
*   **Type**: select_multiple
*   **Description**: Choose from: Payment Method, Payment Date, Payment Status

### Submitter Email (<label> `submitter_email`)
This field requires the email address of the submitter.

*   **Required**: No
*   **Type**: email
*   **Description**: Enter your email address.

### Submitter Name (<label> `submitter_name`)
This field requires the name of the submitter.

*   **Required**: No
*   **Type**: text
*   **Description**: Enter your name.

### Submitter Phone (<label> `submitter_phone`)
This field requires the phone number of the submitter.

*   **Required**: No
*   **Type**: text
*   **Description**: Enter your phone number.
