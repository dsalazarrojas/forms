# Business Strategy Offsite Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for booking a business strategy offsite for your organization. Please fill out the form with your company's details and preferences to help us tailor the offsite to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your company's information: 
   - **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
   - **Organizer Name**: Enter the name of the person organizing the offsite.
   - **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person coordinating the offsite.
   - **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the person coordinating the offsite.

2. Select your preferred dates for the offsite:
   - **Preferred Dates**: Enter your preferred start and end dates for the offsite.
   - **Offsite Start Date**: Enter the first day of the offsite.
   - **Offsite End Date**: Enter the last day of the offsite.

3. Plan your offsite details: 
   - **Duration (Days)**: Enter the total number of days you plan to have the offsite (optional).
   - **Expected Attendees**: Enter the number of people attending the offsite.
   - **Offsite Objectives**: Enter the main goals of the offsite.

4. Choose your offsite preferences:
   - **Location Preference**: Select your preferred location or region.
   - **Accommodation Needed**: Select whether you need hotel rooms, partial accommodation, or no accommodation at all.
   - **Meals Included**: Choose which meals you would like to be provided (optional).

5. Additional details:
   - **Venue Requirements**: Enter any specific requirements for the venue (e.g. projector, screen, etc.).
   - **Team Building Activities**: Select whether you would like to have planned activities, self-organized activities, or no activities at all.
   - **Technical Requirements**: Choose any additional technical requirements you need (e.g. conference room, projector, etc.).
   - **Special Requirements or Notes**: Enter any special requests you may have for the offsite.
   - **Budget Range**: Enter an approximate total budget for the offsite.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and detailed information to help us understand your company's needs and preferences.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review and respond to your submission promptly to discuss further details.
