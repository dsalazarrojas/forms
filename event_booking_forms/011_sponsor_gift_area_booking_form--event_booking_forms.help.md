# Sponsor Gift Area Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect booking information for a sponsor's gift area at an event. The form helps the sponsor to provide details about their gift and the recipient in a clear and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the sponsor information (if applicable)
* Enter the sponsor's name
* Enter the sponsor's email
* Enter the sponsor phone number
* Select the booking date
* Enter the gift amount
* Enter a description of the gift
* Enter the name of the gift recipient
* Enter a message for the gift
* Submit the form

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsor Information** (`main_page_1`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the sponsor information from the provided options or choose "No Sponsor" if not applicable.
* **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Email** (`sponsor_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the sponsor's email address.
* **Sponsor Phone** (`sponsor_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the sponsor's phone number.
* **Booking Date** (`booking_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of the event the gift is booked for.
* **Gift Amount** (`gift_amount`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount of the gift.
* **Gift Description** (`gift_description`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a description of the gift.
* **Gift Recipient** (`gift_recipient`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the gift recipient.
* **Gift Message** (`gift_message`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a message for the gift.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submit`, `text`, required/optional): Submit the form to confirm the booking.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out correctly to avoid any confusion or errors during the booking process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the event organizers.
