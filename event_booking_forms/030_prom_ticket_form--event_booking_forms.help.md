# Prom Ticket Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Prom Ticket Form is a form designed to collect student information and ticket details for prom events. This form is intended to streamline the ticket booking process, ensuring accurate and complete information is captured at the time of booking.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields as instructed below.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out (indicated by a *).
3. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, *): Enter the name of the student booking the ticket.
* **Email** (Email, email, *): Enter the student's email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, *): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Ticket Number** (Ticket Number, number, *): Enter the unique ticket number assigned to this booking.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Terms and Conditions, note): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the event. This is a non-editable text field and not a field to be filled out.
* **Unique ID** (Unique ID, text, *): Enter a unique identifier for this booking. This is used for internal tracking purposes.

## Tips

* Be sure to double-check your entries for accuracy to avoid any errors or issues during the booking process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
