# Animation Editing Workstation Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animation Editing Workstation Booking Form is used to book and configure an editing workstation for a specific project. Users are required to select a workstation, preferred booking date and time, and software requirements to ensure that the desired equipment and tools are available for use. This form helps the team manage resource allocation and minimize conflicts.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **User Full Name** (required): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID badge.
2. Select your **Department or Team Name** (required): Choose the team you belong to from the list of options provided.
3. Select the desired **Workstation ID** (required) from the list of available workstations:
	* WS-01 - High-end Rendering
	* WS-02 - 2D Animation Focus
	* WS-03 - 3D Modeling Focus
	* WS-04 - Compositing Station
	* WS-05 - General Purpose
4. Choose the **Preferred Booking Date** (required) for your workstation booking.
5. Select your desired **Start Time** (required) for the workstation booking.
6. Select your desired **End Time** (required) for the workstation booking.
7. If necessary, choose the software and tools you need pre-loaded on your workstation. You can select:
	* Adobe Creative Cloud
	* Autodesk Maya
	* Toon Boom Harmony
	* Maxon Cinema 4D
	* Foundry Nuke
8. Enter the **Project Name** for internal resource tracking (required).
9. If you need technical support during your workstation setup, please select **Yes**.
10. Check the **Agreement to Clean Workstation After Use** checkbox, indicating your agreement to leave the workstation tidy for the next user.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID badge.
* **Department or Team Name**: Select the department or team you belong to from the list of options.
* **Workstation ID**: Choose the workstation that best suits your needs from the list provided.
* **Preferred Booking Date**: Select the date you prefer to book the workstation.
* **Start Time**: Select the start time for your workstation booking.
* **End Time**: Select the end time for your workstation booking.
* **Software Requirements**: If necessary, select the software and tools you need pre-loaded on your workstation.
* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project you are working on for internal resource tracking.
* **Do you require technical support during setup?**: If you need help with setting up your workstation, select **Yes**.
* **Agreement to Clean Workstation After Use**: Check this box to indicate your agreement to leave the workstation tidy for the next user.

## Tips

* Be accurate when selecting your workstation ID, as this determines the equipment and software available to you.
* If you need help with technical support, be prepared to provide detailed information about your project.
* Remember to check the workstation before leaving to ensure it is tidy for the next user.
