# employment_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for candidates to provide detailed information about themselves, their previous employment, and the job they're applying for. It helps the hiring team assess the candidate's qualifications, experience, and fit for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the **User Information** section, which includes your name, contact details, and any other relevant personal information.
2. In the **Job Information** section, provide details about the job you're applying for, including job title, job type, and job location.
3. The **Contact Information** section is optional, but it's a good opportunity to provide any additional contact details if you have them.
4. If applicable, upload your **Resume** to give the hiring team a better understanding of your qualifications and experience.
5. In the **Employment History** section, list your previous employment experiences, including job title, company, job status, and duration.
6. The **Education** section is a chance to showcase your educational background and any relevant coursework or certifications.
7. In the **Job References** section, provide details about previous work references, including their name, contact information, and job title.
8. The **Job Offer** section is for candidates who have already received a job offer. Please provide details about the job, including job title, job type, and job status.
9. The **Other Information** section is for any additional information that's not covered in other sections.
10. In the **Skills** section, list any relevant skills you have, including software proficiency, languages spoken, etc.
11. If applicable, provide details about your **Language** proficiency, including languages spoken and level of proficiency.
12. The **Salary History** section is for providing details about your previous salaries and job compensation.
13. In the **Certification** section, list any relevant certifications or licenses you hold.
14. The **Experience** section is for providing a brief description of your previous work experience.
15. In the **Job Title** section, provide the title of the job you're applying for.
16. The **Job Type** section is for describing the type of job you're applying for, including full-time, part-time, contract, etc.
17. The **Company** section is for providing details about the company you're applying for.
18. In the **Job Description** section, provide a brief description of the job you're applying for.
19. The **Job Status** section is for providing details about the job's current status, including "available" or "not available".
20. The **Job Location** section is for providing details about the job location, including city, state, etc.
21. In the **Job Start Date** section, provide the date you'd start work.
22. The **Job End Date** section is for providing the date you'd end work, if applicable.
23. The **Job Hours** section is for providing details about the job's hours, including regular hours and overtime hours.
24. In the **Job Wage** section, provide details about the job's wage, including hourly or annual wage.
25. The **Job Notes** section is for any additional comments or notes about the job.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers for accuracy.
* Be concise and clear in your explanations.
* Use bullet points or short paragraphs for longer answers.
* Proofread your answers for spelling and grammar errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask the hiring team for clarification.
