# Cleaning Services Employment Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from potential employees to assess their suitability for various positions within the company's cleaning services. The information collected will be used to review their qualifications, experience, and background.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Provide accurate and truthful information.
3. Do not leave any fields blank.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the hiring manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`3`, `text`, required): Your last name.
* **Email Address** (`4`, `email`, required): Your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`5`, `text`, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Street Address** (`6`, `text`, required): Your home address.
* **City** (`7`, `text`, required): The city or town where you live.
* **State or Province** (`8`, `text`, required): The state or province where you live.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`9`, `text`, required): Your postal code.
* **Date of Birth** (`10`, `date`, required): Your date of birth.
* **Position Applied For** (`12`, `select_one`, required): Select the position you are interested in applying for.
* **Availability** (`13`, `select_one`, required): Select your availability to work.
* **Available Start Date** (`14`, `date`, required): The date you can begin working.
* **Cleaning Experience Level** (`16`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of cleaning experience.
* **Most Recent Employer** (`17`, `text`, optional): Name of your most recent employer.
* **Most Recent Job Title** (`18`, `text`, optional): Title of your most recent job.
* **Most Recent Dates** (`19`, `text`, optional): Dates of employment with your most recent employer.
* **Previous Employer** (`20`, `text`, optional): Name of your previous employer.
* **Previous Job Title** (`21`, `text`, optional): Title of your previous job.
* **Previous Dates** (`22`, `text`, optional): Dates of employment with your previous employer.
* **Reasons for Leaving** (`23`, `text`, optional): Brief explanation for leaving your previous position.
* **Cleaning Skills** (`25`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the cleaning skills you have.
* **Equipment Experience** (`26`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the equipment you have experience with.
* **Chemical Safety Certified** (`27`, `select_one`, required): Select if you have been trained in safe chemical use.
* **Reliable Transportation** (`28`, `select_one`, required): Select if you have a reliable way to get to work.
* **Valid Driver License** (`29`, `select_one`, required): Select if you have a valid driver license.
* **Able to Lift 50 Pounds** (`30`, `select_one`, required): Select if you can lift up to 50 pounds.
* **Highest Education Level** (`32`, `select_one`, required): Select your highest level of education.
* **Relevant Certifications** (`33`, `text`, optional): List any cleaning or safety certifications you have.
* **Languages Spoken** (`34`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the languages you speak.
* **Reference 1 Name** (`36`, `text`, required): Name of your first reference.
* **Reference 1 Relationship** (`37`, `text`, required): Relationship with your first reference.
* **Reference 1 Phone** (`38`, `text`, required): Phone number of your first reference.
* **Reference 2 Name** (`39`, `text`, required): Name of your second reference.
* **Reference 2 Relationship** (`40`, `text`, required): Relationship with your second reference.
* **Reference 2 Phone** (`41`, `text`, required): Phone number of your second reference.
* **Have You Ever Been Convicted of a Crime** (`43`, `select_one`, required): Select if you have been convicted of a crime.
* **Have You Ever Been Convicted Explanation** (`44`, `text`, optional): Brief explanation for being convicted of a crime.
* **Are You Legally Eligible to Work** (`45`, `select_one`, required): Select if you are legally eligible to work.
* **How Did You Hear About This Position** (`46`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you found this job opening.
* **Why Do You Want to Work Here** (`47`, `text`, optional): Brief statement on why you want to work here.
* **Additional Comments** (`48`, `text`, optional): Anything else you would like to add.
* **Applicant Signature** (`49`, `text`, required): Your electronic signature.
* **Application Date** (`50`, `date`, required): Date of application.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
* Do not leave any fields blank.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the hiring manager.
* This form is part of the hiring process, and the information collected will be used to review your qualifications and suitability for the position.
