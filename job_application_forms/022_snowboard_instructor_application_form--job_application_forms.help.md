# Snowboard Instructor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in working as a snowboard instructor. It will be reviewed by our team to assess your qualifications and determine if you're a good fit for our program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out all required fields**. Required fields are marked with an asterisk \*.
2. **Provide accurate and honest information**. Make sure to double-check your answers for spelling and grammar errors.
3. **Submit your application** once you've completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact`): Please provide your full name and any other contact information you'd like to share with us.
* **Home Address** (`address`): Enter your home address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Snowboard Experience** (`snowboard_experience`): Choose how many years of experience you have as a snowboarder:
	+ Snowboard 3+ years: You have more than 3 years of experience snowboarding.
	+ Snowboard 2-5 years: You have between 2 and 5 years of experience snowboarding.
	+ Snowboard < 2 years: You have less than 2 years of experience snowboarding.
* **Instructor Credential** (`instructor_credential`): Check all credentials relevant to you:
	+ First Aid Certification: If you have First Aid Certification.
	+ Ski Cert: If you have a Ski Cert.
	+ Instructor Cert: If you have an Instructor Cert.
	+ Option 4: Please select this if you have another type of credential not listed here.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`): Choose the days you are available to work:
	+ Monday: You are available to work on Monday.
	+ Tuesday: You are available to work on Tuesday.
	+ Wednesday: You are available to work on Wednesday.
	+ Thursday: You are available to work on Thursday.
	+ Friday: You are available to work on Friday.
	+ Saturday: You are available to work on Saturday.
	+ Sunday: You are available to work on Sunday.
* **Available Hours** (`available_hours`): Enter the time you are available to work each day. (Format: "10:00-16:00")
* **Bio** (`bio`): Write a brief introduction about yourself.
* **Resume** (`upload_resume`): Upload your resume for us to review.
* **Profile Picture** (`upload_photo`): Upload a profile picture for us to see.
