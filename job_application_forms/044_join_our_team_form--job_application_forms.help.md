# Join Our Team Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in joining a team or applying for a job. It serves as an initial screening process for employers to filter potential candidates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details, including your contact information and any required fields.
2. Choose your desired job position from the list.
3. Select one or multiple locations where you are willing to work.
4. If applicable, upload your resume and cover letter.
5. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Position**: (<label> `Job Position`, `<type>` `select_one`, required/optional): Select the job position you are interested in applying for. This field is required.
	+ Please choose one of the available job positions from the dropdown menu.
* **Contact Information**: (<label> `Contact Information`, `<type>` `text`, optional): Please provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number, if applicable.
* **Resume**: (<label> `Resume`, `<type>` `text`, optional): Upload your resume to showcase your work experience and skills.
* **Cover Letter**: (<label> `Cover Letter`, `<type>` `text`, optional): Write a brief cover letter introducing yourself and explaining why you are a good fit for the job position.
* **Job Location**: (<label> `Job Location`, `<type>` `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or multiple locations where you are willing to work.
	+ Please select one or multiple locations from the list.
* **Job Start Date**: (<label> `Job Start Date`, `<type>` `date`, optional): If you know your availability, you can select a start date; otherwise, leave it blank.
* **Job Salary**: (<label> `Job Salary`, `<type>` `text`, optional): If you have a specific salary in mind, you can enter it; otherwise, leave it blank.
