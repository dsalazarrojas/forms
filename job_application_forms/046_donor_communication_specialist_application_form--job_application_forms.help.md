# Donor Communication Specialist Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Donor Communication Specialist Application Form is designed for individuals interested in applying for a Donor Communication Specialist position. This form helps us to gather essential information from candidates to assess their suitability for the position, allowing us to efficiently match their skills and preferences with our organizational needs.

Please note that this form is not intended for internal employees or individuals already familiar with our organization's processes. If you are an employee and have not been invited to complete this form, kindly disregard it.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly to the best of your abilities.
2. Select all applicable options for each field that accurately reflect your information.
3. Ensure you complete all required fields as indicated by the red asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### What is your current job title?
* **Current Job Title**: Please select one of the provided options that best describes your current position.
	+ Current Job Title: This is your current job title, such as "Marketing Manager" or "Senior Software Developer."
	+ Previous Job Title: This is your previous job title if you're not currently employed or if it differs from your current title.
	+ Other Job Title: If your job title doesn't match any of the provided options, please select this.

### Please enter your name.
* **Name**: Provide your full name as it would be printed on official documents.
	+ Ensure you include your first name and last name.

### What is your email address?
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
	+ This is the email address we'll use for official communications.

### Do you have a resume or portfolio that showcases your experience and skills?
* **Resume or Portfolio**: If you have one, please provide a link or attachment to your resume or portfolio.
	+ This will help us assess your qualifications and experience.

### What is your availability for the position?
* **Availability**: Select all applicable days of the week when you are available to work.
	+ Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, or Sunday.

### Do you have any specific job preferences?
* **Job Preferences**: If you have any specific job preferences, such as flexible work hours or remote work, please describe them.
	+ Please keep this brief and concise.

### What is your expected start date and end date of availability for the position?
* **Availability Date**: Enter your expected start and end dates for the position in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
	+ Please ensure it's in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### What is your name as it appears on official documents?
* **Name on Official Documents**: Provide your full name as it appears on official documents, such as your passport or ID.
	+ Ensure it matches your name in the "Please enter your name" field.

### Do you have any specific job title preferences?
* **Job Title Preferences**: Select 'Yes' if you have specific job title preferences.
	+ If you prefer a specific job title, please describe it in the provided space.

### What is your availability for a specific time period?
* **Availability Period**: Enter your availability for a specific time period in the format mm/dd/yyyy to mm/dd/yyyy.
	+ This will help us assess your long-term availability.

Please note that this form is designed to be completed in one session. If you need to take a break or come back later, your progress will be saved.
