# Hostess Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register event details with the help of a Hostess.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter a brief title for your event in the Hostess Registration Form field.
2. Provide the title of your event in the Event field.
3. Enter the start date of your event in the Start Date field.
4. Enter the end date of your event in the End Date field.
5. Enter your name in the Hostess Name field.
6. Enter your contact number in the Contact Number field.
7. Enter your email address in the Email field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hostess Registration Form** (text, required): This is the title of the form that you are about to complete.
* **Event** (text, optional): Enter the title of your event, e.g., "Birthday Party" or "Wedding Reception".
* **Start Date** (date, optional): Enter the start date of your event, e.g., "2023-02-01".
* **End Date** (date, optional): Enter the end date of your event, e.g., "2023-02-03".
* **Hostess Name** (text, optional): Enter your name, e.g., "John Doe".
* **Contact Number** (text, optional): Enter your contact number, e.g., "1234567890".
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address, e.g., "john.doe@example.com".

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
- Double-check the dates entered in the Start Date and End Date fields.
- Use the correct format for your email address in the Email field.
