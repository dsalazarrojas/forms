# Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interview Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information from job applicants. It gathers details about the applicant, their motivation, and suitability for a job position.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section of the form as it appears.
2. Each section corresponds to a specific part of your application.
3. Fill out required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
4. If a field is marked as optional, you can choose whether or not to fill it out.
5. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, `text`, optional): 
  Please enter a brief description of yourself and your qualifications for the position you are applying for.

- **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, `text`, optional): 
  Provide a personal statement, including your background and experience.

- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, optional): 
  Enter your contact information, such as your address and phone number, so we can get in touch with you.

- **Motivation** (`motivation`, `number`, optional): 
  This field is not currently being used.

- **Suitability** (`suitability`, `number`, optional): 
  This field is not currently being used.

- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): 
  Enter your email address for communication purposes.

- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): 
  Enter your phone number for easy contact.

- **Job History** (`job_history`, `text`, optional): 
  Provide any relevant work experience you have.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the contact person listed below.
- This form is for job applicants only; do not fill it out if you are not applying for a job.
