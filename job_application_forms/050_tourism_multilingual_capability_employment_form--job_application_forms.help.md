# Tourism Multilingual Capability Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from job applicants with multilingual capabilities for tourism jobs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **user name** in the field "user name".
2. Provide your **email address** in the field "email".
3. Choose your **job title** from the drop-down menu, selecting "Marketing Manager", "Sales Manager", "Customer Support", or "Other".
4. Select the languages you are proficient in, from the drop-down menu with "English", "Spanish", "French", "German", "Chinese", and "Arabic" options.
5. Describe your **work experience** in the field "work experience".
6. Choose the **notice period** you require, from the drop-down menu with "1 month", "3 months", and "6 months" options.
7. Enter your **contact number** in the field "contact number".
8. Upload your **resume** in the field "resume".
9. Write a **cover letter** in the field "cover letter".
10. Provide **references** in the field "references".
11. Enter the **job posting URL** in the field "job posting url".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_one, optional): Choose the job title that best describes your position.
* **Language** (`language`, select_multiple, optional): Select the languages you are proficient in. This will be used to assess your multilingual capabilities.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your work experience in the tourism industry.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, select_one, optional): Select the amount of notice period you require if hired.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number or other contact details.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): Upload your resume to support your application.
* **Cover Letter** (`cover_letter`, text, optional): Write a letter explaining why you are suitable for the job.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): Provide contact details of your professional references.
* **Job Posting URL** (`job_posting_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the job posting you are applying for.
