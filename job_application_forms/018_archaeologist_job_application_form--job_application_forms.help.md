# Archaeologist Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Archaeologist Job Application Form is designed to collect information from candidates interested in working as archaeologists. The form helps us to assess your qualifications and fit for the position.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill in all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
- If you have any relevant certificates or credentials, list them in the "Certifications" field.
- If you have any experience with GIS software, documentation software, or other technical skills, select your proficiency level accordingly.
- If you are available for field seasons, select "True".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your active email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, `text`, required): Enter your full mailing address.
* **Willing to Relocate** (`willing_to_relocate`, `select_one`, required): Select "True" if you are willing to relocate, "False" if you are not, or "Maybe" if you are unsure.
* **Highest Degree Earned** (`highest_degree`, `select_one`, required): Select the highest degree you have earned, which can be a Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD.
* **Field of Study** (`degree_field`, `text`, required): Enter the field of study related to your highest degree.
* **University or Institution Name** (`university_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the university or institution where you earned your degree.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year you graduated.
* **Professional Certifications** (`certifications`, `text`, optional): List any professional certifications you have obtained.
* **Years of Archaeological Experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have worked in the field of archaeology.
* **Primary Specializations** (`primary_specializations`, `select_multiple`, required): Select up to three areas of primary specialization, which can be historical archaeology, prehistoric archaeology, bioarchaeology, geoarchaeology, marine archaeology, industrial archaeology, or laboratory analysis.
* **Types of Field Experience** (`field_experience_types`, `select_multiple`, required): Select up to three types of field experience, which can be excavation, survey, cultural resource management, monitoring, or sampling.
* **Geographic Areas of Expertise** (`geographic_expertise`, `text`, optional): Enter any geographic areas where you have expertise.
* **GIS Software Proficiency** (`gis_proficiency`, `select_one`, optional): Select your level of proficiency in GIS software, which can be expert, proficient, basic, or no experience.
* **Experience with Documentation Software** (`documentation_software`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you have experience with documentation software.
* **Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are available to start work.
* **Available for Field Seasons** (`field_season_available`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are available for field seasons.
