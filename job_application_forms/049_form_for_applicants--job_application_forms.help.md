# Form For Applicants - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for applicants to submit their application for a job opening. Please use this form to provide accurate and complete information about your qualifications, experience, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and select the relevant options as needed. Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email** (email, required): Enter your valid email address.
- **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by the hiring team.
- **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Job Title** (text, optional): Enter the title of the job you are applying for.
- **Work Experience** (text, optional): Describe your relevant work experience.
- **Skills** (select multiple, optional): Select all the skills that you possess from the list.
  - Software Development
  - Data Science
  - Machine Learning
  - Web Development
  - Artificial Intelligence
  - Data Analysis
  - Data Engineering
- **Resume** (text, optional): Upload a copy of your resume or CV.
- **Job Reference** (text, optional): Enter a job reference if you have one.
- **Job Status** (select one, optional): Select the status of the job you are applying for.
  - Active
  - Inactive
  - Closed
- **Job Application Status** (select one, optional): Select the status of your application.
  - Approved
  - Rejected
  - Pending
- **Job Location** (text, optional): Enter the location where you are willing to work.
- **Job Type** (text, optional): Enter the type of job you are applying for (e.g., full-time, part-time, internship).
- **Job Level** (text, optional): Enter the level of the job you are applying for (e.g., entry-level, senior-level).
- **Job Category** (text, optional): Enter the category of the job you are applying for (e.g., software development, data science).
- **Job Employment Type** (text, optional): Enter the employment type (e.g., contract, full-time, part-time).
- **Job Description** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of the job you are applying for.
- **Job Salary** (number, optional): Enter your expected salary range.
- **Job Posting ID** (number, optional): Enter the ID of the job posting.
- **Job Posting Date** (date, optional): Enter the date the job was posted.
- **Job Posting Time** (time, optional): Enter the time the job was posted.
- **Job Close Date** (date, optional): Enter the date the job will close.
- **Job Close Time** (time, optional): Enter the time the job will close.

Please note that all fields are optional unless marked as required, and you should only fill in the information that you are comfortable sharing.
