# Babysitter Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a standard application for individuals interested in working as babysitters. It gathers information about the applicant's contact details, availability, and relevant skills to help clients make informed decisions when selecting a suitable candidate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the following steps:

1. Provide your name, age, and contact information in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your availability days and preferred working times.
3. Enter your requested hourly rate.
4. Indicate your transportation method and smoking status.
5. Describe your comfort level with working in a home with pets.
6. Provide a summary of your previous experience as a babysitter.
7. List at least two references (name and contact information).
8. If applicable, mention any additional skills you possess.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Your Age**: Enter your age in numerical format (e.g., 25).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in international format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address that you check regularly.
* **Which days are you typically available?**: Select all the days of the week you are available to work as a babysitter.
* **Preferred Working Times**: Choose the times of the day you are available to work (Mornings, Afternoons, Evenings, Overnights).
* **Requested Hourly Rate ($)**: Enter your expected hourly rate for babysitting services.
* **Do you have your own reliable transportation?**: Choose how you usually travel to work (Yes, I have a car, Yes, public transport, No, I need a ride).
* **Do you smoke?**: Select if you smoke or not (True, False).
* **Are you comfortable working in a home with pets?**: Choose how you feel about working with pets (Yes, I love pets, Only certain pets, False).
* **Summary of Previous Experience**: Describe the ages of children you've cared for and your experiences as a babysitter.
* **References (Name and Contact Info)**: Provide at least two references with their name and contact information.
* **Any additional skills?**: Mention any additional skills you possess that could be beneficial for the job (e.g., Tutoring, Cooking, First Aid).

By following this help guide, you will be able to complete the application form accurately and effectively, increasing your chances of being considered as a suitable candidate for childcare services.
