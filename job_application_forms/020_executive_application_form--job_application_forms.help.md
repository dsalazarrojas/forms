# Executive Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from job applicants about their job applications. It includes fields for job title, resume, work experience, education, job description, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your resume and job title.
2. Provide a brief description of the job you're applying for.
3. Select your relevant work experience and education.
4. Answer questions about your job status, job type, job level, job location, job salary, job employment type, job tenure, job shift, job hours, job vacancy status, and contact information.
5. Submit the form once you've filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Application Form** (`job_application_form`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the job you're applying for.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required): Upload your resume in a text format.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title if applicable.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Describe your work experience in brief.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): List your educational qualifications.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the job you're applying for.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the job you're applying for (Active or Inactive).
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of job you're applying for (Yes or No).
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_one, optional): Select if you're applying for a job in a specific category (Yes or No).
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select the level of the job you're applying for (High or Low).
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the job you're applying for.
* **Job Salary** (`job_salary`, number, optional): Enter your expected salary or the salary range for the job.
* **Job Employment Type** (`job_employment_type`, select_one, optional): Select the employment type of the job you're applying for (Yes or No).
* **Job Tenure** (`job_tenure`, number, optional): Enter your job tenure in years.
* **Job Shift** (`job_shift`, select_one, optional): Select the shift of the job you're applying for (Yes or No).
* **Job Hours** (`job_hours`, select_one, optional): Select the hours of the job you're applying for (Yes or No).
* **Job Vacancy Status** (`job_vacancy_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the job vacancy (Active or Inactive).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Job Status Update** (`job_status_update`, date, optional): Enter the date of the job status update.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Check for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities.
