# Interview Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interview Request Form is a simple form used by applicants to request an interview with a hiring manager or recruiter for potential job openings at the company. This form gathers essential information about the applicant, such as their name, contact details, and a link to their resume.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the First Name field with your given name.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide a valid Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Choose one or more relevant options from the Resume field that best describe your experience and qualifications.
6. If applicable, specify your Job Title in the Job Title field.
7. Enter your current or previous Company name in the Company field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name (text, required: false)**: Enter your given name.
- **Last Name (text, required: false)**: Enter your surname.
- **Email (email, required: false)**: A valid Email address where you can be contacted.
- **Phone (text, required: false)**: Your contact phone number.
- **Resume (select_multiple, required: true)**: Choose one or more of the provided options that best describe your experience and qualifications.
- **Job Title (text, required: false)**: If applicable, specify your Job Title.
- **Company (text, required: false)**: Enter your current or previous Company name.
