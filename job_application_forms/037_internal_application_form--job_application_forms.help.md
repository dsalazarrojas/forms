# Internal Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for internal employees to submit their job applications for various positions within the organization. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to avoid any potential issues with the application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields (marked as required).
2. Make sure to select only one option for each field that asks for multiple options.
3. For the resume field, you can attach a note with relevant details about yourself.
4. Double-check your work before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Name
 * **Applicant Name** (text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.

### Email
 * **Email** (email, required): Enter your official company email address.

### Job Title
 * **Job Title** (text, required): Enter the job title you're applying for.

### Work Experience
 * **Work Experience** (select_multiple, required): Select all relevant work experiences that align with the job position.

### Education Level
 * **Education Level** (select_one, required): Choose your highest level of education attained.

### Contact Number
 * **Contact Number** (text, required): Enter your contact number for communication purposes.

### Resume (Optional)
 * **Resume** (note, optional): Attach a note with relevant details about yourself.

### Job Application Form
 * **Job Application Form** (text, required): Enter a brief description of why you're a suitable fit for the job position.

### Job Category
 * **Job Category** (select_multiple, required): Select all relevant job categories that align with your application.

### Work Status
 * **Work Status** (select_one, required): Choose your current work status within the organization.

## Tips
* Double-check your work before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you need help or have any questions, please contact your HR department.
