# Senior Management Position Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals interested in applying for a senior management position within our organization. The purpose of this form is to gather relevant information about your qualifications, experience, and background to assess your suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the field-by-field explanation section.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Upload your resume in the "Resume" field.
5. Enter your email address in the format "name@example.com" for the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Enter your current job title in the "Job Title" field.
8. Provide a note describing your relevant work experience in the "Experience" field.
9. Enter any relevant education or credentials in the "Education" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name here.
* **Resume** (`Resume`, `text`, required: false): Attach your resume in PDF or Word format to this field. This will be reviewed as part of your application.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address in the format "name@example.com".
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your current job title.
* **Experience** (`Experience`, `note`, required: false): Provide a brief note describing your relevant work experience.
* **Education** (`Education`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant education or credentials.

## Tips
* Make sure to upload a clear and concise resume to demonstrate your qualifications.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* Be specific when describing your experience and education to increase your chances of being considered for the position.
* Ensure that your resume and other submitted documents are in the correct format.
