# Site Reliability Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Site Reliability Manager Application Form is a form designed for applicants to submit their application for the Site Reliability Manager position. This form collects information about the applicant, including their personal details, professional experience, and work status. The form is intended for internal use within the company and is used to evaluate applicants for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details accurately and honestly.
2. Please provide your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and Address.
3. Enter your Job Start Date, Job End Date (if applicable), and Experience (number of years).
4. Select your Availability (time) and Work Authority (Self, Department, Team, Company, Others).
5. List your Skills (Yes/No) and References (Manager 1, Manager 2, Manager 3).
6. Select your Job Title, Department, Job Type (Full-time, Part-time, Intern), Job Category, and Job Description.
7. Choose your Work Experience (1-3 years, 3-6 years, More than 6 years).
8. Enter your Education, Certification, Job Location, Location (City), State, and Country.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you started working in your previous role.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you ended your previous role (if applicable).
* **Experience** (`experience`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have worked in your previous role.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `time`, required): Select your availability for work hours.
* **Skills** (`skills`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the skills you possess.
* **References** (`references`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the references who can vouch for your work.
* **Work Authority** (`work_authority`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the level of authority you have at work.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter your department.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `text`, optional): Enter your job type (Full-time, Part-time, Intern).
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, `text`, optional): Enter your job category.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your job.
* **Work** (`work_experience`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of work experience.
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, required): Enter your education level.
* **Certification** (`certification`, `text`, required): Enter any certifications you hold.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of your job.
* **Location** (`job_location_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city of your job location.
* **State** (`job_location_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state of your job location.
* **Country** (`job_location_country`, `text`, required): Enter the country of your job location.
