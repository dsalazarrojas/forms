# Tech Data Analyst Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Data Analyst Job Application Form is a collection of fields that require you to provide necessary information about your job application. This form is designed to help our team review your application efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant resume type from the options provided.
2. Upload your resume, cover letter, and portfolio (if applicable).
3. Provide your contact information (email, phone number, and address).
4. Enter your job title, job description, job type, salary range, and work location.
5. Specify your availability and work status.
6. Indicate your work experience, education, and skills.
7. Select your preferred languages and job URLs (if applicable).
8. Finally, review your application carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resume: CV/Resume** (`1`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This is the type of resume you are submitting. Please select one of the options provided.
* **Resume** (`2`, `text`, required): Upload your resume here.
* **Cover Letter** (`3`, `text`, required): Upload your cover letter here.
* **Portfolio** (`4`, `text`, optional): If you have a portfolio, please upload it here.
* **References** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Do you have references? If yes, please select this option.
* **Availability** (`6`, `date`, optional): If you have a specific availability date, please enter it here.
* **Submit** (`7`, `text`, optional): This is a placeholder field and not required.
* **Email** (`8`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **Contact Name** (`10`, `text`, required): Enter your name here.
* **Contact Number** (`11`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number here.
* **Contact Address** (`12`, `text`, optional): If you want to provide an address, please enter it here.
* **Contact Email** (`13`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address here.
* **Job Title** (`14`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Description** (`15`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Type** (`16`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of job you are applying for (Full-Time, Part-Time, or Intern).
* **Salary Range** (`17`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the salary range you are expecting.
* **Work Location** (`18`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the location where you would like to work.
* **Work Status** (`19`, `select_one`, required): Select your current work status (Available, Part-time/Contract, or Retired).
* **Work Experience** (`20`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your level of work experience.
* **Education** (`21`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your highest level of education.
* **Skills** (`22`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have relevant skills, select them here.
* **Languages** (`23`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you are proficient in languages other than English, select them here.
* **Availability Date** (`24`, `date`, optional): If you have a specific availability date, please enter it here.
* **Job URL** (`25`, `text`, optional): If you have a job URL, please enter it here.

## Tips
Please make sure to complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
