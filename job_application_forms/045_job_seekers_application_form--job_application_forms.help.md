# Job Seekers Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Job Seekers Application Form is a tool designed to collect detailed information from job seekers about their background, experience, and skills. This form will help you to showcase your qualifications and make it easier for potential employers to assess your fit for a job.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Start with **Job Seeker Information** (page 1).
3. Fill out the required and optional fields as prompted.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Seeker Information** (<type:text>, <required: false>): This section is a brief introduction to your background and contact information.
* **Work Experience** (<type:text, <required: false>): List your previous work experiences, including job titles, companies, and dates of employment.
* **Education** (<type:text, <required: false>): Describe your educational background, including institutions attended and relevant courses taken.
* **Skills** (<type:text, <required: false>): List your relevant skills, including programming languages, tools, and software proficiency.
* **Work History** (<type:text, <required: false>): This section is for providing more detailed information about your work history, including job responsibilities and achievements.
* **No additional fields are required to be filled out.**

## Tips

* Be accurate and truthful when filling out the form.
* Ensure that your answers are up-to-date and reflect your current qualifications.
* Use clear and concise language to provide the best possible representation of your skills and experience.
* If you have any difficulties or questions, contact [form@gic.mx] for assistance.
