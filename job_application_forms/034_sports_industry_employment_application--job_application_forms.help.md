# Sports Industry Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Sports Industry Employment Application form is used to gather information from applicants for employment opportunities in the sports industry. It is a comprehensive form that requires applicants to provide their contact information, work experience, education, skills, references, and submission details.
