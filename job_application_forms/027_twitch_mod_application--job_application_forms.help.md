# Twitch Mod Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Twitch Mod Application form is a tool for individuals to submit their application to become a Twitch mod. To be eligible, applicants must not be already employed as Twitch mods or employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information in the "User Info" field.
2. Describe your previous experience with similar platforms in the "Experience" field.
3. Specify your availability in the "Availability" field.
4. Explain why you want to become a Twitch mod in the "Why Twitch" field.
5. Select your availability time frame in the "Availability Time" field.
6. Choose your primary reason for applying as a Twitch mod in the "Why Twitch" field.
7. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
8. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
10. If necessary, provide any additional information in the "Other info" field.
11. Click the "Submit" button to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info**: Enter your username, name, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Experience**: Describe your previous experience with similar platforms, such as other streaming platforms or online communities.
* **Availability**: Specify your availability, including the days and hours you are available to work as a mod.
* **Availability Time**: Select your availability time frame from the options provided.
* **Why Twitch**: Explain why you want to become a Twitch mod, including any relevant skills or experience you bring to the role.
* **Why Twitch 2**: Select your primary reason for applying as a Twitch mod from the provided options.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your physical address or other contact information.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, including the country code.
* **Other info**: Provide any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant to your application.
* **Submit**: Review and submit your application for review.
