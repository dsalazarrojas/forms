# Expert Programmer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expert Programmer Application Form is designed to gather essential information from applicants for expert programming roles. It collects details about the applicant's personal and professional background, allowing us to assess their qualifications and fit for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and documents required for the application process.
2. Fill in your personal details, including your email address and phone number.
3. Provide accurate information about your education and work history.
4. Upload your resume or provide a link to it.
5. Select the relevant options for references and work history.
6. Review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Details
* **Email** (`email_2`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone_2`, `phone`, required): Provide your phone number for further contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date_of_birth_date`, `date_of_birth_time`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format of YYYY-MM-DD and select the time of birth.
* **Address** (`address`, required): Enter your current address for further contact and communication.
* **Note** (`note`, required): Add any additional notes or comments about your application.

### Education and Work History
* **Education** (`education`, `education_2`, required): Select the highest level of education completed.
* **Work History** (`work_history`, `work_history_2`, required): Select the relevant work history options.
* **Resume Upload** (`resume_upload`, `resume_upload_2`, required): Upload your resume or provide a link to it.
* **References** (`references`, required): Provide the names and contact information of at least two professional references.

### Select and Multiple Choice
* **Select One** (`select_one`, required): Choose one relevant option for this field.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose multiple relevant options for this field.

### Additional Information
* **Date** (`date`, required): Enter a specific date in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time Decimal** (`time_decimal`, required): Enter a decimal time value.
* **Time** (`time`, required): Select a time value.
* **Date Decimal** (`date_decimal`, required): Enter a decimal date value.

Note: The fields marked as `required` are mandatory and must be filled in for the application to be considered complete.
