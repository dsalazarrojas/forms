# Barber Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants who want to work as a barber at our shop. Please fill out this form completely and accurately to increase your chances of getting hired.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your professional licensing status and license details, including your license number and expiration date.
3. Share your experience and skills as a barber, including the number of years you've worked in the industry and specialized skills you possess.
4. List your previous work experience, including the name of your most recent shop and your availability to start work.
5. Choose your preferred working days and provide your earliest start date.
6. Write a brief self-description, showcasing your style and philosophy as a barber.
7. Finally, confirm that all the information you've provided is accurate and true.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your active and valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you for further discussions.
* **Do you hold a valid Barber License?**: Please select one of the options to indicate your current licensing status.
	+ **Yes - Current**: You hold a current barber's license.
	+ **Yes - Expired**: You hold a barber's license but it has expired.
	+ **No - In training / Student**: You're currently in training or a student.
	+ **No - Cosmetology license only**: You hold a cosmetology license but not a barber's license.
* **License Number**: Enter your valid license number as issued by the relevant authority.
* **License Expiration Date**: Enter the date your license is due to expire.
* **Years of Experience as a Barber**: Enter the number of years you've worked as a barber.
* **Specialized Skills and Services**: Select all the skills you possess, such as straight razor shave, beard trimming, etc.
* **Portfolio or Instagram Link**: If you have a portfolio or Instagram account, provide the link to showcase your work.
* **Most Recent Barber Shop Name**: Enter the name of your most recent shop.
* **Preferred Working Days**: Check all the days you're available to work.
* **Earliest Start Date**: Enter the date you're available to start work.
* **Why are you a good fit for our shop?**: Write a brief description of your style and philosophy as a barber.
* **I certify that all information is true and accurate**: Select "I Agree" to confirm that the information provided is accurate.

Please note that this form is intended to be a comprehensive guide to help you complete the form accurately. However, if you have any questions or need further clarification, don't hesitate to contact us.
