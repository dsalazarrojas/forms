# Security Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Security Job Application Form is designed to gather information from job applicants interested in internal security positions. The form helps us assess your qualifications, experience, and background for these roles.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by filling out the Application Information page**, which includes your name and contact information.
2. **Select your military service experience** from the dropdown menu on the Military Service page, if applicable.
3. **Choose your education background** from the dropdown menu on the Education page, as it relates to your security experience.
4. **List your employment history**, including relevant security-related positions or roles you have held.
5. **Indicate if you have any criminal record**, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Information**: This page is where you provide your full name and contact information. Please make sure to fill this out accurately.
* **Name**: This field is used to capture your name.
* **Address**: This field is not required but helps us to contact you.
* **Contact**: This field is where you provide a phone number or email where we can reach you.
* **Military Service**: This page is where you choose any military service experience you have. Select all relevant experiences.
* **Education**: This page is where you choose your education background. Select the one that relates most to your security experience.
* **Employment History**: This page is where you list your relevant security-related work experience.
* **Criminal Record**: This field is for indicating if you have any criminal record. Please select all relevant records. 

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form to ensure a smooth and efficient application process.
* Double-check your choices before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
