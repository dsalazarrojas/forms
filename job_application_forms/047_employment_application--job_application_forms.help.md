# Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your employment status, qualifications, and preferences. Please answer accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your application is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the sections as requested. You may save and resubmit your application if needed. Ensure that all required fields are filled out to avoid delays in processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your work or home address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you reside or work.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where you reside or work.
* **Zip** (`zip`, number, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the job you are applying for.
* **Work Years** (`work_years`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have worked in your profession.
* **Expected Salary** (`expected_salary`, number, optional): Enter the salary you expect for the position applied for.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current job status:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
	+ Retired
	+ Other
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select your job type(s):
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ prophets (please note: this option seems incorrect, please choose other options)
	+ Contractors
	+ Consultants
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_multiple, optional): Select your job category(s):
	+ Sales
	+ Marketing
	+ IT
	+ HR
	+ Finance
* **Work Type** (`work_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select your work type(s):
	+ Remote
	+ On-site
	+ Hybrid
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_one, optional): Select your job level:
	+ Entry Level
	+ Mid-level
	+ Senior
* **Education** (`education`, select_multiple, optional): Select your educational background:
	+ High School
	+ Associate
	+ Bachelor

Please note that fields with `required: false` are optional and can be filled out at your discretion. If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
