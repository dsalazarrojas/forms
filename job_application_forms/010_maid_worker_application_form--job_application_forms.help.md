# Maid Worker Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals applying for maid worker positions. It includes fields for personal and professional information, work experience, and job preferences. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a successful application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  First Name: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
2.  Last Name: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
3.  Email: Enter your email address that you regularly use for communication.
4.  Phone: Enter your phone number that you can be reached at.
5.  Work Experience: Describe your previous work experience, including job titles and durations.
6.  Address: Enter your residential address.
7.  Resume: Attach a copy of your resume, if available.
8.  Job Type: Select the type of job you are applying for (Maid, Cleaner, or Cook).
9.  Job Status: Select your employment status (Active, Retired, or Inactive).
10. Job Availability: Select the days and hours you are available to work (Full time, Part time, or Occasional).
11. Job Hours: Enter the number of hours you are willing to work per week.
12. Notice Period: Enter the number of days' notice you are willing to give in case of termination.
13. Salary: Enter your expected salary range.
14. Employment Type: Select your employment type (Contract, Freelance, or Full time).
15. Employment Status: Select your current employment status (Single, Married, or Widowed).
16. Date of Birth: Enter your date of birth.
17. Job Position: Select the job position you are applying for (Janitor, Housekeeper, or Maid).
18. Job Level: Select your job level (Junior, Senior, or Manager).
19. Gender: Select your gender (Male, Female, or Other).
20. Job Status Date: Enter the date you are currently in the job status you selected.
21. Note: Enter any additional comments or notes about your application.
22. Employer Name: If currently employed, enter your current employer's name.
23. Employer Address: If currently employed, enter your current employer's address.
24. Employer Phone: If currently employed, enter your current employer's phone number.
25. Employer Email: If currently employed, enter your current employer's email address.
