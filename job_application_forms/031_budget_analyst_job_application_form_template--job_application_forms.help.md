# Budget Analyst Job Application Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from potential applicants for a Budget Analyst position.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are marked as such and are necessary for us to consider your application.
2. Be truthful and accurate when providing information about your background, experience, and qualifications.
3. For fields like "Willing to Relocate" and "Desired Employment Type," choose the option that best fits your current situation.
4. If you have experience with multiple financial software and tools, select all that apply in the "Financial Software and Tools Experience" field.
5. Provide a brief overview of your qualifications in the "Professional Summary" field.
6. List your key skills, including financial analysis, forecasting, and other relevant skills.
7. For "Previous Employers and Positions," list job titles and companies you've worked for.
8. If you have a specific salary range in mind, enter it in the "Salary Expectations" field. If not, leave this field blank.
9. Choose a date you can start work from the calendar and select it as your "Availability Start Date."
10. If you have references who can vouch for your skills and experience, provide their contact information in the "References Contact Information" field.
11. Indicate if you have attached a resume or CV in the final field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, \*: This field is required and should be filled out with your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, \*: This field is required and should be your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, \*: This field is required and should be your valid phone number.
* **Current Location** (`current_location`, `text`, \*: This field is required and should be your current location, including city and state.
* **Willing to Relocate** (`willing_to_relocate`, `select_one`, \*: This field will be used to determine if you are willing to move for the job.
* **Desired Employment Type** (`employment_type`, `select_one`, \*: This field is used to determine if you want to work full-time, part-time, or have another employment type.
* **Years of Budget Analysis Experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, \*: This field is required and should be filled out with the number of years you have experience in budget analysis.
* **Highest Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, \*: This field is used to determine your highest level of education.
* **Field of Study or Major** (`field_of_study`, `text`, \*: This field is required and should be filled out with your field of study or major.
* **Relevant Certifications** (`certifications`, `text`, optional): If you have any relevant certifications, such as a CPA or CIA, list them here.
* **Financial Software and Tools Experience** (`financial_software`, `select_multiple`, \*: This field should be used to select all relevant financial software and tools you have experience with.
* **Professional Summary** (`professional_summary`, `text`, \*: This field should be filled out with a brief overview of your qualifications.
* **Key Skills** (`key_skills`, `text`, \*: This field should be filled out with your key skills, such as financial analysis and forecasting.
* **Previous Employers and Positions** (`previous_employers`, `text`, \*: This field should be filled out with a list of your previous job titles and companies.
* **Salary Expectations** (`salary_expectations`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific salary range in mind, fill this field out.
* **Availability Start Date** (`availability`, `date`, \*: This field should be filled out with a date you can start work.
* **References Contact Information** (`references`, `text`, optional): If you have references who can vouch for your skills and experience, provide their contact information here.
* **Resume or CV Attached** (`resume_attached`, `select_one`, \*: This field is required and should indicate if you have attached a resume or CV.
