# Grocery Outlet Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grocery Outlet Application Form is used to collect information from customers who are interested in applying for job positions at the Grocery Outlet store.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out the Personal Details section with your name, address, phone number, and email address.
- Select your employment status and work status.
- Choose your job title and type.
- Choose your job category and employment type.
- Describe your job in the Job Description field.
- Select the types of jobs you are interested in (shift and schedule) and the types of work you are able to do (work type and work location).
- Upload your resume and portfolio (if applicable).
- Upload your signature (if required by the store).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title** (required): Enter a brief title summarizing your application.
- **Personal Details** (optional): Enter your full name, address, phone number, and email address.
	+ **Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on your application.
	+ **Address**: Enter your residential address.
	+ **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
	+ **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
- **Employment Details** (optional): Enter your employment status (Active or Inactive), work status (Active or Inactive), and job title.
	+ **Employment Status**: Select whether you are Active or Inactive in your current job.
	+ **Work Status**: Select whether you are Active or Inactive in your current work.
	+ **Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
- **Contact Information** (optional): Enter your contact information.
	+ **Name**: Enter your name.
	+ **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
	+ **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
- **Education Details** (optional): Enter your education background.
- **Work Experience** (optional): Enter your work experience.
- **References** (optional): Enter your references.
- **Upload Resume** (optional): Upload your resume if you have one.
- **Upload Portfolio** (optional): Upload your portfolio if you have one.
- **Upload Signature** (optional): Upload your signature if required by the store.
- **Employment Status** (select_one): Select whether you are Active or Inactive in your current job.
- **Work Status** (select_one): Select whether you are Active or Inactive in your current work.
- **Job Title** (text): Enter your current job title.
- **Job Title Type** (select_one): Select whether your job title is yes or no.
- **Job Type** (select_one): Select whether you are interested in yes or no.
- **Job Category** (select_multiple): Select the job categories you are interested in.
- **Job Description** (text): Enter a brief description of the job you are applying for.
- **Job Employment Type** (select_multiple): Select the types of employment you are interested in.
- **Job Employment Status** (select_multiple): Select the employment status you are interested in.
- **Shift** (select_multiple): Select the shifts you are available to work.
- **Schedule** (select_multiple): Select the schedules you are available to work on.
- **Work Type** (select_multiple): Select the types of work you are able to do.
- **Work Location** (select_multiple): Select the locations where you are able to work.
- **Submit** (text): Enter a submit message.
- **Submit Button** (text): Click the submit button to submit your application.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Make sure to upload any requested documents (resume and portfolio).
- Review your application before submitting.
- If you have any questions, contact the store directly.
