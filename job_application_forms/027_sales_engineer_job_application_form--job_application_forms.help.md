# Sales Engineer Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Engineer Job Application Form is designed for job applicants to submit their applications for sales engineer positions. It gathers information about the applicant's contact details, work history, education, skills, and job-specific information to help the sales team assess their qualifications and suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
3. Review the form before submitting it to ensure that you have provided all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Applicant Info**: A note field to provide any additional information about yourself.
* **Contact Info**: Select one of the available options (John Doe, Jane Doe, Jim Doe) to provide your contact information.
* **Work History**: Select one or multiple options (1-5 years, 5-10 years, 10+ years) to indicate your work history.
* **Education**: Select one of the available options (Bachelor's, Master's, PhD) to indicate your level of education.
* **Skills**: Select one or multiple options (Sales, Technical, Communication) to indicate your skills.
* **Resume**: Upload your resume or enter a link to your online resume.
* **Background Check**: Enter a background check number (if applicable).
* **Job Title**: Select one of the available options (Sales Engineer, Software Engineer, Mechanical Engineer) to indicate the job title you are applying for.
* **Job Description**: Provide a brief description of the job position you are applying for.
* **Job Location**: Select one of the available options (USA, Canada, Mexico) to indicate the location of the job.
* **Job Type**: Select one or multiple options (Full-time, Part-time, Remote) to indicate the type of job.
* **Job Status**: Select one of the available options (Active, Inactive, Onboarding) to indicate the status of the job.
* **Job Start Date**: Enter the date you would like to start the job.
* **Job End Date**: Enter the date the job would end (if applicable).
* **Contact**: Enter your email address.
* **Company**: Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Company Website**: Enter your company website (if applicable).
* **Job Salary**: Enter the salary you are expecting.
* **Job Salary Frequency**: Enter the frequency of your salary (e.g., weekly, monthly, annually).
* **Benefits**: Select one or multiple options (True, False, Not Specified) to indicate the benefits you are offering.
* **Job Status Update**: A note field to provide any additional information about the job status.
* **Job Status Update Frequency**: Enter the frequency of the job status update (if applicable).
* **Job Status Update Date**: Enter the date of the last job status update (if applicable).
* **Additional Comments**: A note field to provide any additional comments.
* **Submission Confirmation**: Select one of the available options (True, False) to confirm that you have submitted the form correctly.
