# Advertising Agency Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for applicants to apply for jobs at the advertising agency. It is required to be completed with accurate and honest information, including personal details, employment history, and education background.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and date of birth in the **Full Name** and **Date of Birth** fields.
2. Provide your current residential address in the **Address** field.
3. Enter your contact phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
4. Enter your valid email address in the **Email Address** field.
5. Select the position you are applying for in the **Position Applied For** field.
6. Choose the department you are most interested in in the **Department Interest** field.
7. Enter the date you can start working in the **Availability Start Date** field.
8. Provide information about your education background, including your highest level of education and the name of the institution where you studied.
9. Provide information about your previous employer and previous position.
10. Enter the number of years of relevant experience you have in the **Years of Experience** field.
11. If you have a portfolio, provide a link or description of it in the **Portfolio Attachments** field.
12. Finally, declare that the information provided is accurate in the **Application Declaration** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your current residential address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_applied_for`, `text`, required): Select the position you are applying for.
* **Department Interest** (`department_interest`, `select_one`, required): Select the department you are most interested in (options: Creative, Account Management, Media Planning, Strategy, Digital Marketing, Production, Other).
* **Availability Start Date** (`availability_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you can start working.
* **Highest Education Level** (`highest_education_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your highest level of education (options: High School, Associate Degree, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree, Doctorate, Other).
* **Field of Study** (`field_of_study`, `text`, required): Enter the field of study or major you completed.
* **University College** (`university_college`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the institution where you studied.
* **Previous Employer** (`previous_employer`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your most recent employer.
* **Previous Position** (`previous_position`, `text`, required): Enter your previous job title.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years of relevant experience you have.
* **Portfolio Attachments** (`portfolio_attachments`, `text`, optional): If you have a portfolio, enter a link or description of it.
* **Application Declaration** (`application_declaration`, `select_one`, required): Declare that the information provided is accurate (options: Yes, No).
