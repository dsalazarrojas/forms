# Police Jobs Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals interested in applying for police jobs. It is designed to collect essential information from potential candidates, allowing them to submit their applications and for employers or recruiters to review and evaluate their qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields as directed on each page.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info** (`1`, `text`, optional): 
Please provide your name and any other relevant personal information you would like to share with the employer or recruiter.

* **Contact Info** (`2`, `text`, optional): 
Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email address, so that the employer or recruiter can get in touch with you.

* **Job Application** (`3`, `text`, optional): 
Explain why you are interested in this police job and what makes you a strong candidate.

* **Employment History** (`4`, `text`, optional): 
List your previous work experience, including any relevant positions you have held, dates of employment, and job responsibilities.

* **Job Preferences** (`5`, `text`, optional): 
Describe your ideal job preferences, including location, work schedule, and any other requirements you have.

* **Education** (`6`, `text`, optional): 
Provide information about your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrator.
* Please note that this form is for police job applications only. If you are applying for another type of job, please use a different form.
