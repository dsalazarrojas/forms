# Prison Officer Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who are interested in applying for a job as a Prison Officer. It is used to gather information about your personal details, availability, and job preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your contact phone number and email address.
3. Enter your date of birth in the format of dd/mm/yyyy.
4. Enter your address in the format of address line 1, address line 2, address line 3, and post code.
5. Select your country of birth from the options provided.
6. Choose a job title that accurately reflects your role or position.
7. Indicate your availability to travel by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
8. Indicate your availability to work overnight shifts by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
9. Indicate your availability to work weekend shifts by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
10. Indicate your availability to work bank holidays by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
11. If you have a job reference, please enter the relevant details.
12. Briefly explain why you are interested in this job.
13. Explain how you heard about the role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, required): Enter your personal phone number that we can contact you on.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required): Enter your email address that we can contact you on.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format of dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Address Line 1** (`Address Line 1`, text, required): Enter your address line 1.
* **Address Line 2** (`Address Line 2`, text, required): Enter your address line 2.
* **Address Line 3** (`Address Line 3`, text, required): Enter your address line 3.
* **Post Code** (`Post Code`, text, required): Enter your post code.
* **Country of Birth** (`Country of Birth`, select_one, required): Select your country of birth from the options provided.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, select_one, required): Choose a job title that accurately reflects your role or position.
* **Available to Travel** (`Available to Travel`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are available to travel for work.
* **Available for Overnight Shifts** (`Available for Overnight Shifts`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are available to work overnight shifts.
* **Available for Weekend Shifts** (`Available for Weekend Shifts`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are available to work weekend shifts.
* **Available for Bank Holidays** (`Available for Bank Holidays`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are available to work bank holidays.
* **Job Reference** (`Job Reference`, text, optional): If you have a job reference, please enter the relevant details.
* **Why this job?** (`Why this job?`, text, optional): Briefly explain why you are interested in this job.
* **How did you hear about the role?** (`How did you hear about the role?`, text, optional): Explain how you heard about the role.

Note: This form is intended to be completed by the individual applying for the role, and the information provided will be used to assess their suitability for the position.
