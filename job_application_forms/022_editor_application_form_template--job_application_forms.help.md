# Editor Application Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form template is designed to collect information from applicants for editor roles. It's meant to be used as a starting point for editors to submit their applications, and it guides the applicant through a series of questions and fields to gather necessary information about their qualifications, experience, and availability. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately, providing as much information as possible.
2. Make sure to review and update any existing information, if applicable.
3. If you're unsure about any field, you can ask for clarification or guidance from the form administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Application** (1, job_application): This is the job application form where you can describe your background, experience, and qualifications for the editor role.
* **YouTube Channel** (2, youtube_channel): If you have a YouTube channel, provide the link so we can review it.
* **Editor Experience** (3, editor_experience): Please provide a number representing the years of experience you have as an editor.
* **Editor Qualifications** (4, editor qualifications): In this field, you can describe your qualifications, skills, or training relevant to the editor role.
* **Available Time** (5, available_time): If applicable, provide your available time, e.g., hours of the day or days of the week.
* **Editor Salary** (6, editor_salary): Provide your expected salary range for the role.
* **Job Type** (7, job_type): You can choose between Full-time, Part-time, or Freelance.
* **Job Location** (8, job_location): Select the locations where you're willing to work, e.g., Remote, On-site, or Hybrid.
* **Email** (9, email): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (10, phone): Provide your phone number for communication purposes.
* **YouTube Link** (11, youtube_link): If you have a YouTube channel, provide the link so we can review it.
* **Resume** (12, resume): Attach your resume to demonstrate your qualifications and experience.
* **Work Example** (13, work_example): Provide an example of your work, e.g., a link to a video or a document.
* **Portfolio** (14, portfolio): Share your portfolio or a link to it, if applicable.
* **Availability** (15, availability): If you have availability constraints, e.g., specific dates or times, please provide them.
* **Availability Time** (16, availability_time): Provide the time of your availability.
* **Availability Location** (17, availability_location): Select the locations where you're available to work.
* **Job Frequency** (18, job_frequency): You can choose between Full-time, Part-time, or Freelance.
* **Job Frequency Days** (19, job_frequency_days): Provide the number of days you're available to work per week.
* **Job Frequency Time** (20, job_frequency_time): Provide the time you're available to work per day.
* **Job Frequency Location** (21, job_frequency_location): Select the locations where you're available to work.
* **Job Frequency Notes** (22, job_frequency_notes): Provide any additional information about your job frequency.
* **Application Method** (23, application_method): Describe how you'll apply for the role.
* **Application Notes** (24, application_notes): Provide any additional information about your application.
* **Submitted By** (25, submitted_by): Enter the name of the person who submitted the application.
