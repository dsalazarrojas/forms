# Brand Ambassador Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brand Ambassador Application Form is designed to help us understand your background, experience, and motivations for being a Brand Ambassador. This form will help us get to know you better and assess your fit as a potential ambassador. Please answer the questions honestly, and don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information (Section 1: Personal Information)
2. Provide your background details (Section 2: Background Information)
3. Share your brand knowledge and experience (Section 3: Brand Knowledge)
4. Showcase your social media presence and influence (Section 4: Influence and Reach)
5. Share your experience as an ambassador (Section 5: Ambassador Experience)
6. Motivate us with your reasons for being an ambassador (Section 6: Why You Want to Be Our Ambassador)
7. Agree to our terms and conditions to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your given name, such as John or Jane.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your surname, such as Smith or Doe.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Provide your primary email address for us to get in touch with you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your mobile or landline number for us to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If you'd like to share, enter your birthdate in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **City and State** (`city_state`, text, required): Tell us where you're from, e.g., New York, NY.
* **Current Occupation** (`occupation`, text, optional): Share what you do professionally, e.g., Marketing Manager or Artist.
* **Familiarity with Our Brand** (`brand_familiarity`, select_one, required): Choose how well you know our brand:
	+ Very familiar
	+ Familiar
	+ Somewhat familiar
	+ Limited knowledge
	+ Just learning
* **Passion for Our Brand** (`brand_passion`, select_one, required): Choose how passionate you are about our brand:
	+ Extremely passionate
	+ Very passionate
	+ Passionate
	+ Interested
	+ Some interest
* **Do You Use Our Products** (`product_usage`, select_one, required): Select your level of use:
	+ Yes, regular user
	+ Yes, occasional user
	+ Have tried before
	+ No experience
	+ Willing to learn
* **Active Social Media Platforms** (`social_media_platforms`, select_multiple, required): Choose the platforms you actively use:
	+ Instagram
	+ Facebook
	+ Twitter
	+ TikTok
	+ YouTube
	+ LinkedIn
	+ Pinterest
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Total Social Media Followers** (`total_followers`, number, required): Enter your combined followers across all selected platforms.
* **Average Engagement Rate** (`engagement_rate`, text, optional): Estimate your engagement rate, e.g., 5% or 1% (please provide a percentage or estimate).
* **Previous Brand Ambassador Experience** (`previous_ambassador`, select_one, required): Choose if you've been an ambassador before:
	+ Yes, multiple times
	+ Yes, once or twice
	+ No, this is my first time
	+ Have done similar roles
* **Why You Want to Be Our Ambassador** (`motivation`, text, required): Share why you're interested in being a brand ambassador.
* **I Agree to Ambassador Terms and Conditions** (`application_consent`, select_one, required): Check to confirm you agree to our terms and conditions to submit your application.
