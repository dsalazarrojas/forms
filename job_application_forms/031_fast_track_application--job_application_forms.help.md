# Fast Track Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fast Track Application is a form designed to collect basic contact and demographic information from individuals who wish to be considered for expedited consideration in our hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name in the `First Name` field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the `Last Name` field.
3. Enter your Email in the `Email` field. Ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your Phone number in the `Phone` field.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the `Date of Birth` field. Use the date format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
6. Enter your Street address in the `Street` field.
7. Enter your City in the `City` field.
8. Enter your State in the `State` field.
9. Enter your Zip Code in the `Zip Code` field. Ensure it is a valid 5-digit number.
10. Select your Employment Status from the list of options in the `Employment Status` field.
11. Enter your Job Title in the `Job Title` field.
12. Enter your work experience in the `Work History` field, if applicable.
13. You may provide any relevant information about your substance abuse treatment history in the `Substance Abuse Treatment` field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A text field to enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: A text field to enter your last name.
* **Email**: A text field to enter a valid email address. This field is required for us to contact you.
* **Phone**: A text field to enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: A date field to enter your date of birth in `MM/DD/YYYY` format.
* **Street**: A text field to enter your street address.
* **City**: A text field to enter the city where you reside.
* **State**: A text field to enter your state of residence.
* **Zip Code**: A numeric field to enter your valid 5-digit zip code.
* **Employment Status**: A select-one field to select one of the following options:
	+ employed
	+ student
	+ unemployed
	+ retired
	+ other
* **Job Title**: A text field to enter your current job title.
* **Work History**: A text field to enter your work experience, if applicable.
* **Substance Abuse Treatment**: A text field to enter any relevant information about your substance abuse treatment history, if applicable.

## Tips
* Ensure your email address is valid and can receive messages from us.
* Enter your date of birth in the correct format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* Enter a valid 5-digit zip code.
* Select only one option for employment status.
* Be honest and concise when filling out the `Work History` and `Substance Abuse Treatment` fields, if applicable.
