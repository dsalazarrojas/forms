# Work Opportunities In Europe - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in exploring work opportunities in Europe. It's a tool to gather relevant details about candidates for potential job openings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through this guide to understand what each field represents.
2. Fill out each field according to the instructions provided.
3. Review your submission for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **europe_application (Page 1)** (`europe_application`, `text`, not required): Enter a brief description of why you're interested in work opportunities in Europe. This field is optional, but it helps us understand your motivations.
* **candidate_details (Page 2)** (`candidate_details`, `text`, not required): Provide a short bio about yourself, including any relevant experience or skills you think would be valuable in a work opportunity in Europe. This field is also optional but gives us insight into your background.
* **work_eligibility (Page 3)** (`work_eligibility`, `text`, not required): Share any information about your work eligibility status, such as work permit requirements or visa status. This helps us understand your circumstances and ensure a smooth application process.
* **job_preferences (Page 4)** (`job_preferences`, `text`, not required): Indicate your preferences for the type of work you're interested in or open to exploring. This optional field lets us know your interests and priorities for potential job matches.
