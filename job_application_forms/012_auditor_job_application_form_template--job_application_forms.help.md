# Auditor Job Application Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auditor Job Application Form Template is designed to collect application information from job seekers for auditor positions. It is divided into several sections to help us evaluate your qualifications and availability for the job.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that you complete all required fields.
3. Answer "true" or "false" for questions that allow multiple choices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Application Date
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Date you are applying

### 2. Position Title
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, required): Title of auditor position you are applying for

### 3. Job Posting Reference Number
* **Job Posting Reference Number** (`job_posting_reference`, text, not required): Job ID or posting reference (if available)

### 4. First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your first name

### 5. Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your last name

### 6. Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Your email contact

### 7. Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Your contact phone

### 8. Street Address
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, required): Your home address

### 9. City
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Your city

### 10. State or Province
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, text, required): Your state

### 11. ZIP or Postal Code
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_postal_code`, text, required): Your postal code

### 12. Professional Licenses Held
* **Professional Licenses Held** (`professional_licenses`, select_multiple, required): Select all licenses you hold

### 13. CPA License State/Country
* **CPA License State/Country** (`cpa_license_state`, text, not required): State or country where you hold the CPA license

### 14. Years of Audit Experience
* **Years of Audit Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required): Total years working in audit

### 15. Type of Audit Experience
* **Type of Audit Experience** (`experience_type`, select_multiple, required): Select areas of audit experience

### 16. Industry Experience
* **Industry Experience** (`industry_experience`, text, not required): Industries where you have worked

### 17. Current Employer Name
* **Current Employer Name** (`current_employer`, text, not required): Your current organization

### 18. Current Job Title
* **Current Job Title** (`current_job_title`, text, not required): Your current position

### 19. Highest Education Level
* **Highest Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Your highest degree

### 20. Degree Field of Study
* **Degree Field of Study** (`degree_field`, text, required): Your major or field

### 21. University or College Name
* **University or College Name** (`university_name`, text, required): School where you earned degree

### 22. Accounting Software Skills
* **Accounting Software Skills** (`software_skills`, select_multiple, required): Select software you can use

### 23. Willing to Travel
* **Will to Travel** (`willing_to_travel`, select_one, required): Travel requirements for position

### 24. Willing to Relocate
* **Will to Relocate** (`willing_to_relocate`, select_one, required): Would you relocate for position

### 25. Availability Date
* **Availability Date** (`availability_date`, date, required): When you could start

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have all necessary documents (e.g., resume, certifications, references) readily available to complete the form efficiently.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* Contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
* This form should be completed in English.
