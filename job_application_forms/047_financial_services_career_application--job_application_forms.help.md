* **First name**: Enter your First name here.
* **Last name**: Enter your Last name here.

* **Job title**: Select your current or desired job title from the dropdown menu.

* **Job type**: Select your desired job type, full-time, part-time, or freelance.
* **Employment type**: Select your desired employment type, full-time, part-time, or contract.
* **Job description**: Provide a brief description of the job you are applying for.
