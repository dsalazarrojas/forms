# Chauffeur Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chauffeur Job Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals applying for a Chauffeur position. It aims to gather details about the applicant's personal identity, driving experience, professional history, and relevant skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first name, last name, email address, phone number, date of birth, current address, city, state or province, and zip code.
2. Enter your driver's license number, license state or province, and license expiration date.
3. Provide information about your driving experience, including the total years of professional driving experience and the years of Chauffeur experience.
4. List your previous Chauffeur employers (if any) and the types of vehicles you are experienced in driving.
5. If applicable, provide details about any professional certifications or languages you are fluent in.
6. Indicate your willingness to undergo a background check.
7. Finally, enter the current date to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Current Address**: Enter your current home address.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your postal code.
* **Driver License Number**: Enter your driver license number.
* **License State or Province**: Enter the state or province where your license was issued.
* **License Expiration Date**: Enter the date your license expires in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Traffic Violations in Last 5 Years**: Select the number of traffic violations you have had in the last 5 years (1-2, 3-5, or More than 5).
* **Years of Professional Driving Experience**: Enter the total number of years you have been driving professionally.
* **Years of Chauffeur Experience**: Enter the number of years you have been working as a Chauffeur.
* **Previous Chauffeur Employers**: Enter the names of any previous Chauffeur employers.
* **Experience Driving**: Select the types of vehicles you are experienced in driving (Sedan, SUV, Limousine, Van, Truck, or Luxury Vehicles).
* **Professional Certifications or Licenses**: Enter any relevant certifications or licenses you hold (e.g., CPR, first aid, etc.).
* **Languages Spoken**: Enter any languages you are fluent in besides your native language.
* **Willing to Undergo Background Check**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your willingness to undergo a background check.
* **Application Date**: Select the date you are submitting your application.
