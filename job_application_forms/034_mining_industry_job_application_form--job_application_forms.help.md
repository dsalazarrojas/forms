# Mining Industry Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for candidates interested in working in the mining industry. It collects relevant information about the candidate to facilitate the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring that all required fields are filled.
2. If a field is marked as optional, you can choose whether or not to fill it out, depending on your preference.
3. Use the hints provided to guide you through each section.
4. Submit the form once you have filled out all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information** (`candidate_info`, text, required): This section is for you to provide basic information about yourself as a candidate. Please enter your name, email address, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): This section is for you to provide contact details that we can use to reach you. Please include your address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): If you have relevant work experience in the mining industry, please describe it in this section. Be as detailed as possible about your experience, including job titles, dates of employment, and job responsibilities.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): If you have relevant education or training in the mining industry, please describe it in this section. This can include academic degrees, certifications, or any other relevant educational experiences.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): List any relevant skills you have that align with the requirements of the mining industry. This can include programming languages, software proficiency, or other relevant skills.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current employment status. This can be Active, Inactive, or Retired.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, optional): If you are willing to work in a specific location, please enter it in this section.
* **Work Schedule** (`work_schedule`, text, optional): If you have a preferred work schedule, please enter it in this section.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the contact information for an emergency contact person. This can be a family member, friend, or other trusted individual.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities or seek clarification.
- Once you have submitted the form, you will receive confirmation of receipt.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
