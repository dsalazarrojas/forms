# City Hall Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants interested in working with City Hall. The form is designed to help the applicant provide detailed information about their qualifications and background.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions carefully and accurately.
2. Review each section thoroughly before submitting the form.
3. Make sure to sign the declaration at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Position Title You Are Applying For (2)
• **Position Title You Are Applying For**: This question asks for the job title you are applying for. Please enter the exact title of the job you are interested in.

### Department (3)
• **Department**: This question asks you to select the department in which you are interested in working. Please select one of the following options:
  • Administrative Services
  • Community Services
  • Finance
  • Planning and Development
  • Public Safety
  • Public Works
  • Human Resources
  • Information Technology
  • Parks and Recreation
  • Other (please specify in the field below)

### First Name (5)
• **First Name**: This is your first name. Please enter your first name.

### Last Name (6)
• **Last Name**: This is your last name. Please enter your last name.

### Email Address (7)
• **Email Address**: This is your email address. Please enter your email address.

### Phone Number (8)
• **Phone Number**: This is your phone number. Please enter your phone number.

### Mailing Address (9)
• **Mailing Address**: This is your mailing address. Please enter your mailing address.

### City (10)
• **City**: This is the city where you currently reside. Please enter your city.

### State or Province (11)
• **State or Province**: This is the state or province where you currently reside. Please enter your state or province.

### ZIP or Postal Code (12)
• **ZIP or Postal Code**: This is the zip code or postal code where you currently reside. Please enter your zip code.

### Highest Level of Education Completed (14)
• **Highest Level of Education Completed**: This question asks for your level of education completed. Please select one of the following options:
  • High school diploma or equivalent
  • Some college
  • Associate degree
  • Bachelor degree
  • Master degree
  • Doctoral degree
  • Professional certificate
  • Other (please specify in the field below)

### Field of Study (15)
• **Field of Study**: This question asks for the field of study you are interested in. Please enter the field of study you are interested in.

### Years of Professional Experience (18)
• **Years of Professional Experience**: This question asks for the number of years you have worked in your profession. Please enter the number of years you have worked.

### Relevant Experience for This Position (19)
• **Relevant Experience for This Position**: This question asks you to describe your background and relevant skills. Please enter a brief description of your experience and skills.

### Currently Employed (20)
• **Currently Employed**: This question asks if you are currently employed. Please select one of the following options:
  • Yes
  • No

### Key Skills and Competencies (25)
• **Key Skills and Competencies**: This question asks for your key skills and competencies. Please enter a brief description of your skills and competencies.

### Technical or Software Skills (26)
• **Technical or Software Skills**: This question asks for any technical or software skills you have. Please enter any technical or software skills you have.

### Professional Certifications or Licenses (27)
• **Professional Certifications or Licenses**: This question asks for any professional certifications or licenses you have. Please enter any certifications or licenses you have.

### Reference 1 Name (29)
• **Reference 1 Name**: This question asks for the name of your first reference. Please enter the name of your reference.

### Reference 1 Phone (30)
• **Reference 1 Phone**: This question asks for the phone number of your first reference. Please enter the phone number of your reference.

### Reference 1 Email (31)
• **Reference 1 Email**: This question asks for the email of your first reference. Please enter the email of your reference.

### Reference 1 Relationship (32)
• **Reference 1 Relationship**: This question asks for your relationship with your first reference. Please enter a brief description of your relationship with your reference.

### Consent to Background Check (34)
• **Consent to Background Check**: This question asks if you consent to a background check. Please select one of the following options:
  • I consent
  • I do not consent

### I Am Legally Eligible to Work in This Country (35)
• **I Am Legally Eligible to Work in This Country**: This question asks if you are legally eligible to work in this country. Please select one of the following options:
  • Yes
  • No

### All Information Provided is True and Accurate (36)
• **All Information Provided is True and Accurate**: This question asks you to agree that all information provided is true and accurate. Please select one of the following options:
  • I agree
  • I do not agree

## Tips
1. Please review each question carefully before submitting the form.
2. If you need help or have any questions, please don't hesitate to ask.
3. Ensure that all information is accurate and truthful.
