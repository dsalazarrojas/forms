# Research And Development Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research and Development Job Application form is used to gather information about your background, qualifications, and experience for a Research and Development role. This form is intended for end-users who are applying for Research and Development positions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary documents and information ready, such as your resume, contact information, and employment history.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, including all required and optional fields.
3. Review and submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): If applicable, please enter the job title you are applying for.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the job type that best describes your role (Software Engineer, Data Scientist, Researcher, Intern, or Other).
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required: false): Briefly describe your job and responsibilities.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, select_multiple, required: false): Select where you are willing to work (Remote, On-site, Hybrid, or Other).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your contact number for further communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address for further communication.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required: false): Attach or upload your resume for the hiring team's review.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, required: false): Enter your time of birth in 24-hour format.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, required: false): Provide any availability information you have, such as flexible work arrangements.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, text, required: false): If applicable, enter the notice period required for your current employment.
* **Notice Period Days** (`notice_period_days`, number, required: false): If applicable, enter the number of days' notice required for your current employment.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, text, required: false): If applicable, enter your current employment type.
* **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, date, required: false): If applicable, enter your employment start date.
* **Employment End Date** (`employment_end_date`, date, required: false): If applicable, enter your employment end date.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, text, required: false): If applicable, enter your current employment status.
* **Education** (`education`, text, required: false): If applicable, enter your educational background.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, required: false): If applicable, list your relevant skills.
* **Salary Range** (`salary_range`, number, required: false): If applicable, enter the salary range you are expecting.
* **Employment History** (`employment_history`, note, required: false): If applicable, attach or upload a detailed employment history.
* **References** (`references`, text, required: false): If applicable, attach or upload references.
* **Job Application Status** (`job_application_status`, select_one, required: false): If applicable, select the current job application status.
* **Job Application Date** (`job_application_date`, date, required: false): If applicable, enter the date of your job application.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, required: false): If applicable, enter the person assigned to your job application.

Note: Please ensure that you have all necessary documents and information ready before proceeding.
