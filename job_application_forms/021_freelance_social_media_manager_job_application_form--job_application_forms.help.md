# Freelance Social Media Manager Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from potential candidates applying for a freelance Social Media Manager position. It helps us understand your qualifications, experience, and contact details to assess your suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the **Personal Details** field.
2. Describe your work experience in the **Work Experience** field.
3. List your educational background in the **Education** field.
4. Upload your resume in the **Resume Upload** field.
5. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
6. Provide your phone number in the **Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (`Personal Details`, text, required/optional): Fill in your full name, address, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, text, required/optional): Describe your previous work experience, including job titles, company names, and a brief description of your roles and responsibilities.
* **Education** (`Education`, text, required/optional): List your educational background, including degrees earned, institutions attended, and relevant courses or specializations.
* **Resume Upload** (`Resume Upload`, text, required/optional): Upload your resume in PDF or JPEG format. Please ensure it is up-to-date and reflects your current experience and qualifications.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted for further information or to discuss your application.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): Provide your phone number where you can be contacted for further information or to discuss your application.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure we can assess your application properly.
* Ensure your resume is up-to-date and reflects your current experience and qualifications.
* If you have any additional documents or information that support your application, please attach them to your resume or mention them in the relevant fields.
* If you have any questions or need further information, please don't hesitate to contact us.
