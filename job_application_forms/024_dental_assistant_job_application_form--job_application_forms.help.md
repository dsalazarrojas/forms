# Dental Assistant Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Assistant Job Application Form is designed to collect information from interested applicants for the dental assistant position. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields. 
2. Provide your Email address. 
3. Enter your Street Address. 
4. If you have previously held a job title, enter it in the Job Title field. 
5. Upload your Resume in the Resume field. 
6. Indicate your interest in the job by selecting Yes or No in the Job Application field.
7. Specify the number of years you have work experience in the Job Experience field. 
8. Enter your available start date in the Available Start field. 
9. Enter your available end date and time in the Available End field. 
10. Finally, provide any additional message to the organization in the Message field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address here. Please note that this will be the primary contact point for the organization.
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your street address here. Please use the format "street number, street name, city, state, zip".
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current or previous job title here.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required): Upload your Resume here. 
* **Job Application** (`job_application`, select_one, required): Please select Yes if you are interested in this job position.
* **Job Experience** (`experience`, select_one, required): Select the number of years you have worked experience in the field.
* **Available Start** (`available_start`, date, required): Enter the date you can start working here.
* **Available End** (`available_end`, time, required): Enter the time you are available to start working here.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Provide any additional message or information here.
