# Simple Employee Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Employee Application form is used to collect information from applicants for job openings. This form is meant to be completed by applicants to provide their relevant personal and professional details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting the form.
2. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** fields with your personal details.
3. Provide your **Job Title**, **Job Description**, and **Contact Address** to describe your job and contact information.
4. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
5. Choose the correct **Job Application Date** and enter your work phone number in the **Work Phone** field.
6. (Optional) If you have an **Emergency Contact Name**, **Emergency Contact Number**, and **Emergency Contact Relationship**, please provide this information.
7. Choose your **Employment Status**, **Job Type**, and **Job Level** from the respective dropdown menus.
8. Select your area of expertise from the **Job Category** and choose your desired **Job Salary Range**.
9. Enter your job salary in the **Job Salary** field.
10. Choose your job hours from the **Job Hours** dropdown menu.
11. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (example: example@domain.com).
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of your job.
* **Contact Address**: Enter your contact address.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Job Application Date**: Enter the date you applied for the job in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Work Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: (Optional) Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: (Optional) Enter the number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: (Optional) Choose the relationship between you and your emergency contact (Parent, Spouse, Child).
* **Employment Status**: Choose your employment status (Full-time, Part-time, Contract).
* **Job Type**: Choose your job type (Full-time, Part-time, Contract).
* **Job Level**: Choose your job level (Entry, Mid-Level, Senior).
* **Job Category**: Choose your area of expertise (Software Development, Data Science, Marketing).
* **Job Salary Range**: Choose your desired job salary range (50-100, 100-200, 200-500).
* **Job Salary**: Enter your job salary.
* **Job Hours**: Choose your job hours (24/7, 32/5, 32/6).
