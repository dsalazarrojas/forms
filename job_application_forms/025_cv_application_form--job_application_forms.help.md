# CV Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CV Application Form is intended for potential employees to submit their applications for a specific job position. The form collects information about the candidate's personal details, job specifics, education and experience, and eligibility. This form is required to be completed accurately and completely to ensure that the hiring team has all the necessary information to review your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter the city and country of your current residence.
3. Select the specific job title you are applying for.
4. Choose the department you are interested in joining.
5. Enter the earliest possible start date you can start work.
6. Provide information about your education and experience, including your highest level of education and the number of years of relevant experience you have.
7. If applicable, provide a link to your LinkedIn profile or portfolio.
8. Write a brief professional summary of your skills and background.
9. Indicate if you are eligible to work in the country where the job is located.
10. Answer truthfully about your sponsorship requirements.
11. Finally, select today's date to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Required): Please enter your full name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (Required): Please enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Please enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **City and Country of Residence** (Required): Enter your current city and country of residence.
* **Specific Job Title** (Required): Select the job title you are applying for from the provided options.
* **Department of Interest** (Select One, Required): Choose the department you are interested in joining from the provided options.
* **Earliest Possible Start Date** (Required): Enter the date you can start work as soon as possible.
* **Highest Level of Education** (Required): Select your highest level of education from the provided options.
* **Years of Relevant Experience** (Required): Enter the number of years you have relevant experience.
* **LinkedIn or Portfolio URL** (Optional): If you have a LinkedIn profile or portfolio, provide the link.
* **Professional Summary** (Required): Write a brief overview of your skills and background.
* **Are you legally authorized to work in this country?** (Required): Select True if you are eligible to work in the country where the job is located.
* **Will you now or in the future require sponsorship?** (Required): Select True if you will now or in the future require sponsorship.
* **Today's Date** (Required): Select today's date to complete the form.

Note: The form requires a valid date for the "Today's Date" field.
