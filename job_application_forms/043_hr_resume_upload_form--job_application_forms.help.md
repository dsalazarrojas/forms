# HR Resume Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Resume Upload Form is designed for job applicants to upload their resume and provide required information for the job application process. This form collects relevant details for the HR department to evaluate and manage job applications efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Make sure you are logged in as a valid job applicant or HR personnel.
- Review the form carefully to understand each field's purpose.
- Fill in the required information accurately and completely.
- If you encounter any issues, contact HR support for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resume Upload**: Upload your resume in the designated format (e.g., PDF, DOCX, or DOC). Ensure the file size is within the recommended limits (e.g., 2MB).
* **Contact Information**: Provide your contact details, including your email and phone number. This will enable the HR department to get in touch with you regarding your application.
* **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job you are applying for.
* **Work Experience**: Briefly describe your relevant work experience.
* **Education**: Mention your educational background, including any relevant degrees or certifications.
* **Skills**: Select the skills you possess from the provided list. If you have other skills not listed, please describe them in the 'Job applicant note' field.
* **Resume Attachment**: Upload your resume if you have a preferred attachment format (e.g., PDF, DOCX, or DOC).
* **Resume Link**: Provide the URL of your hosted resume if you have a hosted resume.
* **Job Application Status**: Indicate the current status of your job application.
* **Job Application Date**: Select the date you applied for the job.
* **Job Applicant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your resume.
* **Job Applicant Email**: Enter your work or personal email.
* **Job Applicant Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Job Applicant Address**: Enter your work or residential address.
* **Job Requisition ID**: Enter the ID of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Department**: Enter the department you are applying for.
* **Job Location**: Enter the location of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Position**: Enter the position you are applying for.
* **Job Salary**: Enter the salary you are expecting or have been offered.
* **Job Type**: Enter the type of job you are applying for (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Contract).
* **Job Status**: Indicate the status of the job you are applying for (e.g., New, Active, Closed, On Hold).
* **Resume Size**: Enter the size of your resume in bytes or MB.
* **Job Applicant Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Job Application Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the job application process.
* **Job Application Date**: Select the date of your job application.

Please fill in the form accurately and completely, and don't hesitate to contact HR support if you need any assistance.
