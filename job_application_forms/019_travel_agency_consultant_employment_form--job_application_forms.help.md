# Travel Agency Consultant Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential travel agency consultants to assess their suitability for job openings at our agency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the category of your job experience (Travel Agency, Job Agency, Event Company, or Other).
2. Indicate whether you have a job title (Yes or No).
3. Choose your job type (Consultant, Agent, Manager, or Intern).
4. Provide a brief description of your job.
5. Enter your experience years.
6. Indicate your availability (Yes or No).
7. Enter your availability dates and notes (if needed).
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your contact number.
10. Upload a file with a resume.
11. Enter your portfolio link (if available).
12. Provide reference information:
    * Enter the name of your reference.
    * Enter your relationship with the reference (optional).
    * Enter the reference's phone number.
    * Enter the reference's email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_one, required: false): Choose the category of the agency you have experience with.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether you have a job title.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required: false): Select your role (Consultant, Agent, Manager, or Intern).
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required: false): Describe your job responsibilities.
* **Experience Years** (`experience_years`, number, required: false): Enter your experience years.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether you are available for work.
* **Availability Notes** (`availablenotes`, note, required: false): Enter notes about your availability.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contactnumber`, text, required: false): Enter your contact number.
* **Resume File** (`resumefile`, text, required: false): Upload a file with your resume.
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, text, required: false): Enter your portfolio link.
* **Reference Name** (`referencename`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your reference.
* **Reference Relationship** (`referencereference`, text, required: false): Enter your relationship with the reference.
* **Reference Phone** (`referencephone`, text, required: false): Enter the reference's phone number.
* **Reference Email** (`referenceemail`, email, required: false): Enter the reference's email address.
* **Note** (`travelagencyconsultantnote`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about your qualifications.
