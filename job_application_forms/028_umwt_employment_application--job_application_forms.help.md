# umwt_employment_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The umwt_employment_application form is a job application form used to collect information from candidates for a job position.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address on pages 1 and 2, respectively.
2. Enter your Phone number, if applicable.
3. Provide your Date of Birth, if applicable.
4. Enter your Address, if applicable.
5. Enter your Experience (Years) of work, if applicable.
6. Select your relevant Skills and Programming languages from the respective dropdowns.
7. Enter your Available Date and Available Time, if applicable.
8. Add any additional Notes, if necessary.
9. Enter your References, if applicable.
10. Enter your Job Type, Job Title, Job Category, Job Location, Work Hours, and Job Description, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your primary email address that you use to communicate with us.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth if required for the application.
* **Address**: Enter your residential or business address, if applicable.
* **Experience (Years)**: Enter the number of years you have worked in your industry or job role.
* **Skills**: Select relevant skills you possess from the dropdown options.
* **Languages**: Select the programming languages you are proficient in from the dropdown options.
* **Available Date**: Enter the date you are available to start the new role, if applicable.
* **Available Time**: Enter your available work hours, if applicable.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information that you think is relevant to your application.
* **References**: Enter any professional references that you would like to have considered.
* **Job Type**: Enter the type of job you are applying for, if applicable.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Job Category**: Enter the category of the job you are applying for, if applicable.
* **Job Location**: Enter the location where you would like to work, if applicable.
* **Work Hours**: Enter the work hours you are comfortable with, if applicable.
* **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of your job experience and qualifications.

## Tips

* Ensure you fill in accurate and truthful information for a successful application.
* Be specific when providing job preferences and qualifications.
* Attach any supporting documents if required.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
