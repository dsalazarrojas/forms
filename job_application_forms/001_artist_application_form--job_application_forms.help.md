# Artist Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Application Form is used to collect necessary information from artists applying for various programs or residencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately and efficiently, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full legal name in the **Full Name** field.
2.  Provide your email address in the **Email Address** field. This will be used for communication.
3.  Enter your phone number, including the country code, in the **Phone Number** field.
4.  Select your primary art form from the options provided in the **Primary Art Form** field.
5.  Enter the number of years you have been practicing professionally in the **Years of Professional Experience** field.
6.  Write a brief description of your artistic vision and practice in the **Artist Statement** field.
7.  If desired, provide a link to your online portfolio in the **Portfolio Website URL** field.
8.  If desired, provide a link to your social media handle in the **Social Media Handle** field.
9.  List any notable exhibitions or galleries where your work has been shown in the **Exhibition History** field.
10. Upload 3-5 images of your recent artwork in the **Artwork Samples** field.
11.  If applicable, provide a link to a video showcasing your work in the **Demo Reel or Video Link** field.
12.  Enter the date you can begin the program or residency in the **Availability Start Date** field.
13.  Provide at least 2 professional references, including contact information in the **References Count** field.
14.  If needed, specify any special requirements or accommodations in the **Special Requirements or Accommodations** field.
15.  Confirm that all information provided is accurate by selecting **Yes** in the **I Agree to Terms and Conditions** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Full Name** (text, required): Enter your full legal name.
*   **Email Address** (email, required): We will use this to contact you.
*   **Phone Number** (text, required): Include country code.
*   **Primary Art Form** (select_one, required): Select your main artistic discipline.
*   **Years of Professional Experience** (number, required): How long have you been practicing professionally.
*   **Artist Statement** (text, required): Brief description of your artistic vision and practice.
*   **Portfolio Website URL** (text, optional): Link to your online portfolio or website.
*   **Social Media Handle** (text, optional): Instagram or other relevant platform.
*   **Exhibition History** (text, required): List notable exhibitions or galleries where your work has been shown.
*   **Artwork Samples** (text, required): Upload 3-5 images of your recent work.
*   **Demo Reel or Video Link** (text, optional): Optional link to video content.
*   **Availability Start Date** (date, required): When can you begin the program or residency.
*   **References Count** (number, required): Provide at least 2 references.
*   **Special Requirements or Accommodations** (text, optional): Any equipment or space needs for your work.
*   **I Agree to Terms and Conditions** (select_one, required): By checking this, you confirm all information is accurate.

## Tips
When uploading artwork samples, please ensure they are in a suitable format and size to be viewed correctly. Also, please proofread your application carefully to ensure all required fields are completed and accurate information is provided. 

Please note that providing all required fields is mandatory and failure to do so may lead to disqualification.

Note: This is a general guide and the requirements may vary based on the specific context or opportunity you are applying for. Please contact the organization for more information if needed.
