# Pharmaceutical Industry Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals in the pharmaceutical industry seeking employment opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
2. In the "Experience" section, describe your relevant work experience in the pharmaceutical industry.
3. In the "Education" section, provide your relevant education background.
4. In the "Skills" section, select any relevant skills you have that apply to the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Contact Information
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for your name, phone number, and email address.
### 2. Experience
* **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your previous work experience in the pharmaceutical industry, including job titles and dates of employment.
### 3. Education
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your relevant education background, including degree(s) earned and institution(s) attended.
### 4. Skills
* **Skills** (`skills`, `select_multiple`, required/optional):
	+ **Yes**: Select this option if you have the skill(s) listed.
	+ **No**: Select this option if you do not have the skill(s) listed.
