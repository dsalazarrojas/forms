# Yoga Instructor Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential applicants who are interested in becoming a yoga instructor. It will help us understand your background, qualifications, and experience in teaching yoga.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your relevant skills from the "Skills" field.
6. Enter your license information in the "License" field.
7. Enter your certification number in the "Certification Number" field.
8. Select your teaching exams in the "Teaching Exams" field.
9. Enter your emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" fields.
10. Upload your resume in the "Resume" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`text`, `optional`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`text`, `required`): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `required`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`text`, `optional`): Enter your phone number.
* **Experience** (`text`, `optional`): Enter a brief description of your yoga experience.
* **Availability** (`text`, `optional`): Enter your availability for teaching hours.
* **Education** (`text`, `optional`): Enter your educational background related to yoga.
* **Skills** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Select your relevant skills, such as Yoga, Meditation, Breathwork, Anatomy.
* **Certifications** (`text`, `optional`): Enter any relevant certifications you have.
* **Emergency Contact** (`text`, `optional`): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`text`, `optional`): Enter your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Phone** (`text`, `optional`): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Email** (`email`, `optional`): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Note** (`text`, `optional`): Enter any additional note about your emergency contact.
* **Resume** (`text`, `optional`): Upload your resume.
* **License** (`text`, `required`): Enter your yoga teacher license information.
* **Certification Number** (`text`, `optional`): Enter your certification number.
* **Expiration Date** (`date`, `optional`): Enter the expiration date of your certification.
* **Practice Hours** (`number`, `optional`): Enter the number of practice hours you have.
* **Teaching Exams** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Select your teaching exams, such as Certified, Registered.
* **References** (`text`, `optional`): Enter your references.
* **Reference 1** (`text`, `optional`): Enter the name of your first reference.
* **Reference 2** (`text`, `optional`): Enter the name of your second reference.
* **Reference 3** (`text`, `optional`): Enter the name of your third reference.
* **Reference 4** (`text`, `optional`): Enter the name of your fourth reference.

Please note that you must provide your first and last name, email, phone number, and license information as required fields.
