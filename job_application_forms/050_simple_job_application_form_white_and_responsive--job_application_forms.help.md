# Simple Job Application Form White And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and professional information from job applicants. Please fill out the form with the required information to help us evaluate your application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter a valid email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number.
- **Position** (`position`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the job position you are applying for.
- **Experience** (`experience`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  Please select all the relevant experience you have from the options below:
  - Yes
  - No
- **Education** (`education`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Please select your level of education from the options below:
  - High School
  - College
  - Graduate
- **Skills** (`skills`, `text`, required: false): Please enter any relevant skills you have.
- **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Click to submit your application.

## Tips
- Make sure to proofread your application before submitting.
- Only submit one application per job position.
- We appreciate your interest in our company and thank you for your application.
