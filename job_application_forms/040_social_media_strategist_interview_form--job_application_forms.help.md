# Social Media Strategist Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide us with information about your background, experience, and qualifications for the Social Media Strategist position.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the first two fields.
2. Upload your resume if applicable.
3. Enter your LinkedIn URL if you have one.
4. Describe your experience in the field of social media.
5. List your relevant skills.
6. Provide a brief overview of your previous job experiences.
7. Choose the reasons that best answer our questions in the "Why us" section.
8. Provide a brief description of your education.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): If you have a resume, please upload it here.
* **LinkedIn URL** (`linkedin_url`, text, optional): If you have a LinkedIn profile, please enter the URL here.
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Please describe your experience in the field of social media.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): Please list your relevant skills.
* **Previous Jobs** (`previous_jobs`, text, optional): Please provide a brief overview of your previous job experiences.
* **Why us** (`why_us`, select_one, required): Please choose the reasons that best answer our questions in this section.
	+ **What motivated you to apply for this role?**: This is a question about your interest in the position and the company.
	+ **How do you see yourself growing professionally within our organization?**: This is a question about your career goals and aspirations within the company.
	+ **What do you hope to accomplish in the first 6 months?**: This is a question about your short-term goals and expectations.
	+ **How will your skills and experience make you a good fit for the role?**: This is a question about your qualifications and how you meet the job requirements.
	+ **How long have you been in the field?**: This is a question about your level of experience in the field.
	+ **Are you available to start immediately?**: This is a question about your availability to start working.
	+ **Have you worked in the field of social media before?**: This is a question about your previous experience in the field.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your education.
