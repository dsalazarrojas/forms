# Guardian Hiring Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guardian Hiring Application form is designed to collect information from applicants seeking employment opportunities with the Guardian organization. This form is used to assess applicant qualifications, experience, and availability to ensure that we find the best fit for our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Applicant Details** page with your personal information, including your name, email, and contact number.
2. Select your relevant **Experience** level from the provided options (0-1 year, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, 10+ years).
3. Choose your **Availability** date for our review process.
4. If applicable, provide **References** with their name, title, and contact information.
5. If applicable, provide **References Details** explaining your relationship with the references.
6. List your relevant **Skills** (e.g., programming languages, softwares, etc.).
7. Select your highest **Education Level** from the provided options (Bachelor, Associate, Master, PhD, Diploma).
8. If you have other education levels, select all that apply from the **Other Education Level** options.
9. Choose the institution where you completed your highest **Education** from the provided options.
10. Select your current **Employment Status** from the options (Employer A, Employer B, Employer C).
11. Choose your relevant **Work Experience** level from the provided options (0-1 year, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, 10+ years).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details** (Applicant Details, Text, Required/Optional): Please fill out your personal details, including your name, email, and contact number.
* **Experience** (Experience, Select Multiple, Required): Select all your relevant experience levels to indicate your level of experience.
* **Availability** (Availability, Date, Required/Optional): Choose a date that you are available to start work.
* **References** (References, Text, Required/Optional): If you have references, please provide their name, title, and contact information.
* **References Details** (References Details, Text, Required/Optional): Explain your relationship with the references provided.
* **Skills** (Skills, Note, Required/Optional): List all your relevant skills, such as programming languages, softwares, etc.
* **Education** (Education, Text, Required/Optional): Provide information about your highest education level.
* **Level of Education** (Education Level, Select One, Required/Optional): Select your highest education level from the provided options.
* **Other Education Level** (Other Education Level, Select Multiple, Required/Optional): If you have other education levels, select all that apply.
* **Institution** (Institution, Select One, Required/Optional): Choose the institution where you completed your highest education.
* **Employment Status** (Employment Status, Select One, Required/Optional): Select your current employment status from the provided options.
* **Work Experience** (Work Experience, Select Multiple, Required/Optional): Choose all your relevant work experience levels to indicate your level of experience.

**Tips:**

* Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask our support team for assistance.
* Please note that your application will be reviewed and processed according to our hiring process.
