# Digital Recruitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential candidates for a job position. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to increase your chances of being considered for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the Main Info section, which includes general information about yourself.
- Then, provide your contact information in the Contact Info section.
- Next, fill out the Education section with your educational background.
- Provide your work history in the Work History section.
- Fill out the Job Application section with any relevant job application information.
- Upload your resume in the Resume section.
- Select your skills, certifications, languages, and references in their respective sections.
- Finally, provide your availability, experience, and other relevant details in the Availability, Experience, and Education Years, Skills Years, and Work Experience sections.
