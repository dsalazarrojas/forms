# Pizza Restaurant Employment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals interested in working at the pizza restaurant. The form asks questions about the applicant's contact information, employment history, education, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill out the "Job Application Info" field with a brief statement about why you are interested in working at the pizza restaurant.
3. Select the positions you are applying for in the "Position Applying For" field.
4. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
5. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure this is a valid email address.
7. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
8. Provide a brief description of your employment history in the "Employment History" field.
9. Select whether you have a degree in the "Education" field.
10. Select whether you are available to work in the "Availability" field.
11. Enter your phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Application Info** (`job_application_info`, text, not required): A brief statement about why you are interested in working at the pizza restaurant.
* **Position Applying For** (`position_applying_for`, select_multiple, not required): Select the positions you are interested in applying for.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, not required): Enter your address.
* **Employment History** (`employment_history`, text, not required): Provide a brief description of your work history.
* **Education** (`education`, select_multiple, not required): Select whether you have a degree.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, not required): Select whether you are available to work.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Please ensure you answer all relevant questions.
* Be honest and accurate in your application.
* Only submit this form if you are genuinely interested in working at the pizza restaurant.
* Do not submit duplicate applications.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the HR department.
