# Corporate Manager Employment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Corporate Manager Employment Form is a comprehensive template for collecting employment-related information from job applicants for corporate manager positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your job title and select the correct option from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your employment status (e.g., full-time, part-time, contract, etc.).
3. Select the type of job you are applying for (e.g., full-time, part-time, temporary, etc.).
4. Enter the employment start date.
5. Select the employment terms (e.g., 6 months, 12 months, etc.).
6. Enter the form information submitted by you, including your name and any other required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `select_one`, required): Please select the job title that best describes the position you are applying for from the dropdown menu. Examples include CEO, COO, CTO, CIO, CSO, and Other.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, optional): Please enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Employment History** (`employment_history`, `text`, optional): Please enter a brief description of your previous work experience.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, `text`, optional): Please enter a brief description of your education background.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, optional): Please enter a brief description of your work experience.
* **References** (`references`, `text`, optional): Please list any professional references.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, optional): Please enter a brief description of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, `text`, optional): Please list any job requirements.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, `text`, optional): Please enter the name of your reporting manager.
* **Reporting Team** (`reporting_team`, `text`, optional): Please enter the name of the team you would be reporting to.
* **Manager Observations** (`manager_observations`, `text`, optional): Please enter any observations from your manager.
* **Performance Measures** (`performance_measures`, `text`, optional): Please enter any performance measures for the job.
* **Performance Goals** (`performance_goals`, `text`, optional): Please enter any performance goals.
* **Compensation and Benefits** (`compensation_and_benefits`, `text`, optional): Please enter any compensation and benefits information.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Please select your employment status from the dropdown menu.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required): Please select the type of job you are applying for from the dropdown menu.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, required): Please select the status of your job from the dropdown menu.
* **Employment Terms** (`employment_terms`, `select_one`, required): Please select the employment terms from the dropdown menu.
* **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date you would start working.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, `text`, optional): Please enter the number of hours you would work.
* **Annual Salary** (`annual_salary`, `number`, required): Please enter your annual salary.
* **Monthly Salary** (`monthly_salary`, `number`, optional): Please enter your monthly salary.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, `text`, required): Please enter your name and any other required information.

Note: The form fields marked as "required" must be completed to ensure that your application is properly submitted.
