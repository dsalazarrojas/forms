# Training Instructor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Instructor Application Form is a form designed to collect information from individuals interested in becoming training instructors at our organization. This form is used to assess the qualifications, availability, and suitability of potential instructors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your first name in the **First name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last name** field.
3. Provide your email address in the **Email** field. Please ensure it is a valid email address that you check frequently.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Enter your address in the **Address** field.
6. Describe your physical capabilities in the **Physical capabilities** field. This includes any mobility issues or physical needs that may impact your ability to teach.
7. List any health conditions that may affect your ability to teach in the **Health conditions** field.
8. Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person in the **Emergency contact person** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last name**: Your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: A valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address**: Your current address where we can send you information and updates.
* **Physical capabilities**: Describe any physical limitations or needs that may impact your ability to teach, such as wheelchair accessibility or sight impairments.
* **Health conditions**: List any health conditions that may affect your ability to teach, such as chronic illnesses or medication requirements.
* **Emergency contact person**: The name and contact information of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency or if we need to contact you.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when filling out this form to help us get a clear understanding of your qualifications and needs.
* Ensure the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [insert contact information].
