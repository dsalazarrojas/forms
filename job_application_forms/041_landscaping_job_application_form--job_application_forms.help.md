# Landscaping Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landscaping Job Application Form is designed to collect necessary information from applicants who are interested in joining your team. It helps to gather their contact details, employment history, and other relevant qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Contact Details** section with your full name, email address, and phone number. This is the first step in the application process.
2. Enter your **Available Start Date**, which is the date you are available to start work. Make sure to select a valid date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. In the **Previous Employer** field, provide a brief description of your previous job experience, including the company name, job title, and duration of employment.
4. Select the **Job Title** you are applying for from the available options.
5. Enter the **Job Duration** in years, which is the length of time you have worked in your previous role.
6. Answer the **Reason for Leaving** question with either 'Yes' or 'No', depending on whether you are interested in leaving your current job.
7. Answer the **Will you have time to work** question with either 'Yes' or 'No', depending on your availability.
8. Answer the **Will you be able to relocate** question with either 'Yes' or 'No', depending on your willingness to relocate for the job.
9. Answer the **Do you have a license to work** question with either 'Yes' or 'No', depending on your qualifications.
10. Finally, upload your resume in the **Resume** field, which is the last required step in the application process.
