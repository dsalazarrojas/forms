# Research Scientist Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for users who are interested in applying for a Research Scientist position. By filling out this form, you will be able to submit your application and provide us with the necessary information to consider your candidacy.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your contact information: This is the first step in the application process. Please enter your name, address, and phone number in the respective fields.
2. Enter your contact email address: This is the email address where you would like to be contacted regarding your application.
3. Enter your contact phone number: This is the phone number where you can be reached regarding your application.
4. Describe your work experience: Please provide a brief description of your work experience, highlighting relevant skills and qualifications.
5. List your educational background: Please provide information about your academic background, including any relevant degrees or certifications.
6. Upload your resume: Please upload a copy of your resume, which will be reviewed as part of your application.
7. Provide additional information: If you have any additional information that you think is relevant to your application, please include it in the text box provided.
8. List your references: Please provide at least two professional references, including their name, title, and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info**: This field is where you will enter your contact information, including your name, address, and phone number.
* **Contact Address**: This field is where you will enter your contact address.
* **Contact Email**: This field is where you will enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone**: This field is where you will enter your contact phone number.
* **Work Experience**: This field is where you will describe your relevant work experience, including relevant skills and qualifications.
* **Education**: This field is where you will provide information about your academic background, including any relevant degrees or certifications.
* **Resume**: This field is where you will upload a copy of your resume, which will be reviewed as part of your application.
* **Additional Info**: This field is where you will provide any additional information that you think is relevant to your application.
* **References**: This field is where you will list at least two professional references, including their name, title, and contact information.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information in each field.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
