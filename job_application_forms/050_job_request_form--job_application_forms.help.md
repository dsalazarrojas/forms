# Job Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from job applicants, providing a structured process for submitting job requests. By filling out this form, applicants can efficiently and accurately provide their details, making it easier for reviewers to assess their qualifications and suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective text fields. These fields are not mandatory, but filling them in will help us get a better understanding of your identity.
2. Enter your **Email** address. This is not a required field, but it helps us contact you for any further information or follow-up.
3. If you have a **Reference Number**, please provide it to help us identify you.
4. Write a brief description of the **Job Details**, including the job title, industry, and any other relevant information.
5. Attach any **Related Documents** that support your application, such as resumes or portfolios.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Reference Number** (Text, Optional): If you have a reference number, enter it here.
* **Job Details** (Text, Optional): Write a brief description of the job you are applying for, including the title, industry, and any other relevant information.
* **Related Documents** (Text, Optional): Attach any relevant documents, such as resumes or portfolios, to support your application.

## Tips

* Ensure your email address is accurate and up-to-date to receive any further communication about your application.
* If you have any supporting documents, please attach them to the form to increase your chances of being considered.
* Make sure to fill in all the required information, even if it's optional. This helps us get a better understanding of your application.
