# Virtual Assistant Contract Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Assistant Contract Template is a form designed to help you create a comprehensive contract for your virtual assistant services. This form captures essential details to ensure a clear and mutually beneficial agreement between you and your virtual assistant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: `assistant_name`, `assistant_email`, and `assistant_phone`.
2. Enter the `contract_length` in months.
3. Select the `contract_start_date` and `contract_end_date` using the date picker.
4. Enter the `job_title` of your virtual assistant.
5. Click the "Submit" button to save your contract template.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your virtual assistant.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of your virtual assistant.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required): Enter the email address of your virtual assistant.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your virtual assistant.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of your virtual assistant. This field is optional but can help with identification purposes.
* **Contract Length** (`contract_length`, number, required): Enter the contract length in months.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, date, required): Select the contract start date using the date picker.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, date, optional): Select the contract end date using the date picker.
* **Contract Info** (`contract_info`, text, optional): Enter any other essential details related to the contract.
