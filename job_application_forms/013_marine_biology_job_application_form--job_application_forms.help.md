# Marine Biology Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a job application form for marine biology positions. It is intended for individuals who are interested in working in the field of marine biology and would like to be considered for available positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" field. This is where you can enter your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Describe your education and background in the "Education" field. This should include any relevant coursework, certifications, or degrees you have earned.
3. Share your work experience in the "Work Experience" field. This should include any relevant experience you have in marine biology, including volunteer work, internships, or previous employment.
4. Upload your resume in the "Resume Upload" field. This is a requirement for most applications and should include a copy of your current resume.
5. List your professional skills and areas of expertise in the "Skills" field. This can include languages, programming skills, or other relevant abilities.
6. Provide information about your references in the "References" field. This should include the names of 1-3 people who can vouch for your abilities and experience.
7. Finally, submit your application by clicking the "Submit" button.
