# Administrative Assistant Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a job application form designed for candidates to apply for the Administrative Assistant position. It gathers information about the applicant's personal details, work history, education, and professional references.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** in the respective fields. Please make sure to use your legal name and a valid email address and phone number where you can be reached.
2. Select the position you are applying for in the **Position Applied For** field.
3. Provide a brief summary of your **Employment History**, highlighting your relevant roles and dates.
4. Enter your **Education**, including degrees, certifications, and institutions attended.
5. Enter the details of your **References**, including their names and contact information.
6. Confirm that a **Resume** has been attached with your application.
7. If applicable, add a brief **Cover Letter** explaining why you are interested in the position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your best contact number for communication.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_applied_for`, `text`, required): Select the position you are applying for from the available options.
* **Employment History** (`employment_history`, `text`, required): Provide a brief summary of your relevant past roles and dates.
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, required): Enter your degrees, certifications, and institutions attended.
* **References** (`references`, `text`, required): Enter the details of your professional references, including their names and contact information.
* **Resume Attached** (`resume_attached`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that a resume has been attached with your application.
* **Cover Letter** (`cover_letter`, `text`, optional): If applicable, add a brief statement of interest in the position.
