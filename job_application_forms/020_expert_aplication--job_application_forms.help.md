# expert_aplication - Help Guide

## Purpose

This is a job application form designed to collect information from experts in various fields. The form is intended to help us understand your qualifications, experience, and availability for a potential role. Please answer the questions accurately and thoroughly to demonstrate your expertise and suitability for the position.

<Thinking>

This form is not intended for internal use within the organization. It is designed for external experts to apply for a potential role. If you are an internal candidate, please do not fill out this form. If you have already submitted an application, please do not fill out this form again.

</Thinking>

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2. Ensure you have attached your CV or resume as required.
3. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (Job Title, Text, Required/Optional): This field is for you to enter the title of the job you are applying for.
* **Name** (Name, Text, Required/Optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, Email, Required/Optional): This field is for you to enter your professional email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, Text, Required/Optional): Please enter your work phone number where you can be reached.
* **Description** (Description, Text, Required/Optional): Briefly describe yourself and your qualifications for the position.
* **Expertise Areas** (Expertise Areas, Select Multiple, Required/Optional): Select all the expertise areas that you are qualified in.
* **CV Attachment** (CV Attachment, Text, Required/Optional): Attach your CV or resume to support your application.
* **Motivation Statement** (Motivation Statement, Text, Required/Optional): Briefly explain why you are applying for this position and what motivates you.
* **Availability** (Availability, Select One, Required/Optional): Indicate your availability for the position, e.g. Monday to Friday, Every day, or Weekends only.
* **Schedule** (Schedule, Time, Required/Optional): If you have a specific schedule in mind, please enter it.
* **Experience Years** (Experience Years, Number, Required/Optional): Enter the number of years you have experience in the field.
* **Experience Months** (Experience Months, Number, Required/Optional): Enter the number of months you have experience in the field.
* **Additional Info** (Additional Info, Text, Required/Optional): If there is any additional information you would like to provide, please enter it.
* **Job Description** (Job Description, Text, Required/Optional): Briefly describe the job you are applying for.
* **Job Requirements** (Job Requirements, Text, Required/Optional): Please list the requirements for the job you are applying for.
* **Job Requirements 2** (Job Requirements 2, Text, Required/Optional): If there are any additional requirements for the job, please list them.

## Tips

* Ensure you answer all the fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Attach a professional CV or resume to support your application.
* Proofread your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
