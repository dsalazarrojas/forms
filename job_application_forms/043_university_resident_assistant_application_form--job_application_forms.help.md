## University Resident Assistant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The University Resident Assistant Application Form is a document used to gather information from applicants interested in becoming a Resident Assistant for the University. It is intended to be completed by individuals who wish to apply for this position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill out your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, and **Phone Number** in the provided fields.
2. Ensure you have completed all required fields to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (first_name)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents or government-issued ID.
* **Last Name (last_name)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents or government-issued ID.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your official email address where you can be contacted about your application.
* **Phone Number (phone)**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted about your application.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* Complete the form in a quiet, distraction-free area, free from errors.
* If you encounter any issues while filling out the form, contact your local support representative for assistance.
