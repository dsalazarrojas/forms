# Senior Hybrid Hospitality Operations Manager Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Senior Hybrid Hospitality Operations Manager Application Form is designed to help us evaluate your eligibility and qualifications for the Senior Hybrid Hospitality Operations Manager position. Please complete this form to provide us with essential information about your background, experience, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Provide Contact Information
1. **First Name** (`first_name`, text, false): Enter your first name.
2. **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, false): Enter your last name.
3. **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, false): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.

### Step 2: Share Your Contact Information and Professional Background
1. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, false): Enter your phone number.
2. **Resume Upload** (`resume_upload`, text, false): Attach your resume to help us get a better understanding of your experience and qualifications.
3. **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, false): If applicable, indicate your current or previous job title.
4. **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, false): Briefly describe your relevant work experience.
5. **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, false): Describe your educational background related to hospitality operations.

### Step 3: Showcase Your Career History and Networking
1. **Previous Workplaces** (`previous_workplaces`, text, false): List your previous workplaces, including company names and employment dates.
2. **Job References** (`job_references`, text, false): Provide names and contact information for job references who can vouch for your skills and performance.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
