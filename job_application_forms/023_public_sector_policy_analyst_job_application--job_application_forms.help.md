# Public Sector Policy Analyst Job Application - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to apply for a Public Sector Policy Analyst position. It captures relevant details about the applicant, including contact information, job application details, and supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the form with accurate and truthful information.
2. Ensure that you complete all required fields.
3. Attach supporting documents such as resumes and additional materials as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info**: Provide your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Job Application**: Describe your interest in the Public Sector Policy Analyst position and your relevant experience.
* **Resume Upload**: Attach your resume or CV in PDF or Word format.
* **Experience**: Share your relevant work experience.
* **Education**: List your educational background and any relevant courses or training.
* **Job Requirements**: Describe the job requirements that you are looking for.
* **Certifications**: List any relevant certifications you hold.
* **References**: Provide names and contact information for two professional references.
* **Job Offer**: Describe the expected job offer or salary range.
* **Upload Additional Docs**: Attach any additional documents or materials that support your application.
* **Job Application Form**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have about the position.
* **Upload CV**: Attach your CV or resume in PDF or Word format.

## Tips

* Ensure that you attach all required documents and fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
