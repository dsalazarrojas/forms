# Sales Executive Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect application information from individuals interested in sales executive roles within our organization. It will be reviewed by our hiring team to evaluate your qualifications and suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Review your application carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text): Enter the title of the job you are applying for.
- **Job Description** (`job_description`, text): Briefly describe the job role you are applying for.
- **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_one): Select the category that best fits the job role from the options listed (Sales, Marketing, Management, Operations).
- **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_multiple): Select all applicable job types from the options listed (Full Time, Part Time, Freelance, Intern).
- **Location** (`location`, select_multiple): Select all applicable locations from the options listed (Remote, New York, Chicago, Los Angeles).
- **Experience** (`experience`, select_multiple): Select the number of years of experience you have in the field from the options listed (1, 2, 3).
- **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple): Select all applicable skills from the options listed (Sales, Communication, Leadership, Team Management).
- **Resume** (`resume`, text): Attach a copy of your resume, preferably in PDF format.
- **Cover Letter** (`cover_letter`, text): Attach a copy of your cover letter, preferably in PDF format.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text): Enter your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
- **Job References** (`job_references`, text): Enter the names and contact information of your professional references.
- **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, select_multiple): Select the number of days' notice you are willing to provide if you were to leave the company (1, 2, 3).
- **Salary** (`salary`, number): Enter your expected salary for the role.

Note: All fields are required except for Resume, Cover Letter, and Job References, which are optional.
