# pharmaceutical_research_scientist_job_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pharmaceutical Research Scientist Job Application form is designed to collect your personal and professional details for the position of a Pharmaceutical Research Scientist. It is a crucial step in the hiring process, and providing accurate and complete information will help us assess your qualifications and suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure that your application is processed correctly, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the first field, "Name." This is the primary contact information for us to identify you with.
2. Input your email address in the "Email" field. We will use this to communicate with you regarding your application and potential next steps.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field. This can be your primary or secondary contact method.
4. In the "Degree" field, please specify your relevant academic qualifications, such as BSc, MSc, or Ph.D.
5. For "University," enter the name of the institution where you obtained your degree.
6. In "Company," specify the name of your current or previous employer.
7. Enter your job title(s) from your relevant work experience in the "Job Title" field. If you've had multiple roles, you can input multiple job titles.
8. Enter your dates of employment in the "Job Dates" field.
9. For "Name 1" through "Name 2," input the names of your colleagues or team members with whom you've worked closely, if applicable.
10. For "Job Title 1" through "Job Title 2," enter the job titles of the colleagues mentioned.
11. For "Contact Info 1" and "Contact Info 2," provide the contact information (email or phone number) of the colleagues mentioned.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: This is your personal name for contact purposes.
* **Email**: Your primary email address for communication.
* **Phone**: Your primary or secondary contact number.
* **Degree**: Specify your highest academic achievement related to the position.
* **University**: Where you obtained your degree.
* **Company**: Where you've worked, or where you're currently employed.
* **Job Title**: Your role's title in your work experience.
* **Job Dates**: The dates you've been in your job.
* **Name 1/2**: Names of colleagues or team members you've worked with.
* **Job Title 1/2**: Job titles of the colleagues mentioned.
* **Contact Info 1/2**: Contact information (email or phone number) of the colleagues mentioned.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled with accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you have multiple job titles or work experiences, you can input multiple "Job Title" fields.
- Please be prepared to provide further information or clarifications if requested.
- This form is for internal use only; do not share your information with others without permission.
