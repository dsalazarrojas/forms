# Church Worship Team Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to submit their application to join the church worship team. It collects information about the applicant's name, contact details, musical experience, availability, and any other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Applicant name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field. This will be used for communications.
3. If you have a preferred phone number, enter it in the "Phone number" field. This is optional.
4. Select your musical role or instrument from the dropdown list in the "Musical role or instrument" field.
5. If you have experience in years, enter it in the "Years of experience" field. This is optional.
6. In the "Availability for rehearsals and services" field, specify the days and times you are available.
7. If you have previous experience with worship groups, provide a brief history of your experience in the "Previous worship groups experience" field. This is optional.
8. If you have musical references, provide their names and contact details in the "Musical references" field. This is optional.
9. If you have any health or accessibility considerations, please provide information in the "Health or accessibility considerations" field. This is optional.
10. Finally, read and sign the "Acknowledgement note" at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name**: Your full name as it should appear in our records.
* **Email address**: The email address we will use for communications with you.
* **Phone number**: If you have a preferred phone number, please enter it here. This is optional.
* **Musical role or instrument**: Select your musical role or instrument from the dropdown list. Examples include Vocal, Guitar, Keys, Drums, and Other.
* **Years of experience**: If you have experience in years, enter it here. This is optional.
* **Availability for rehearsals and services**: Specify the days and times you are available for rehearsals and services.
* **Previous worship groups experience**: If you have previous experience with worship groups, provide a brief history of your experience.
* **Musical references**: If you have musical references, provide their names and contact details. This is optional.
* **Health or accessibility considerations**: If you have any health or accessibility considerations, please provide information to assist with planning.
* **Acknowledgement note**: Read and sign this note to confirm your application.
