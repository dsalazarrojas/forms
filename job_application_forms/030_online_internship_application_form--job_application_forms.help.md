# Online Internship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for applicants to provide their personal details, educational background, contact information, and other relevant details to be considered for the internship program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including name, email, and password.
2. Provide information about your educational background, including your degree and university name.
3. Enter your contact information, including phone number and address.
4. Select your skills and experience relevant to the internship.
5. Choose your availability for the internship, including start and end dates.
6. Add any job references or work samples.
7. Upload any additional documents or files required for the internship.
8. Review and submit your application.
