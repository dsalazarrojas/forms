# promoter_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The promoter application form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in promotion roles. This form will help us understand your qualifications, goals, and strategies for promotion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field carefully, providing accurate and detailed information about yourself and your promotion goals.
2. Make sure to check the required fields, as they are marked as required.
3. Use the available options for select fields (e.g., promotion channels, promotion methods) to select all relevant choices.
4. If a field is optional, you may skip it or add any additional information if it's not listed in the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (promoter_name, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, email, required: false): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required: false): Enter your work or personal phone number.
* **Company Name** (company_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of your current or previous company.
* **Job Title** (job_title, text, required: false): Enter your job title or position.
* **Role** (role, text, required: false): Briefly describe your role in the company or industry.
* **Job Description** (job_description, text, required: false): Provide a concise summary of your job responsibilities.
* **Experience** (experience, text, required: false): List your relevant work experience in the promotion industry.
* **Promotion Goals** (promotion_goals, text, required: false): Define your short-term and long-term promotion goals.
* **Event Type** (event_type, text, required: false): Describe the type of events you have experience with or would like to promote.
* **Target Audience** (target_audience, text, required: false): Identify your target audience for promotions.
* **Promotion Channels** (promotion_channels, select_multiple, required: false): Select all relevant channels for promotion (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, LinkedIn, Email, Print, Online).
* **Promotion Methods** (promotion_methods, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all relevant methods for promotion (e.g., Online Advertising, Social Media, Email Marketing, Print Advertising, Event Marketing, Referral, Other).
* **Why Promoting** (why_promoting, text, required: false): Explain why you want to promote events or products.

Remember to submit this form once you have completed all fields.
