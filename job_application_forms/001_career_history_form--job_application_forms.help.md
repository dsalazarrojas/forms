# Career History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Career History Form" is designed to gather comprehensive information about your work history. This includes your personal details, job titles, employment dates, and reasons for leaving previous roles. This form helps assess your experience and qualifications for various purposes such as hiring, promotions, or educational programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand what information is required.
2. Fill in your personal details in the "Personal Information" section.
3. Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
4. Provide a valid contact email address.
5. Enter your phone number, including the country code if applicable.
6. List your current or most recent job title and employer.
7. Enter your employment start and end dates, if known.
8. Provide a summary of your previous employers, listing the most recent three.
9. List your key responsibilities in recent roles.
10. Explain the reason for leaving your most recent role.
11. List your professional references with name, relationship, and contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email address**: Enter a valid contact email address that can be used for communication.
* **Phone number**: Include your country code if your phone number requires it.
* **Current or most recent position**: Enter your job title and employer in the format "Job Title - Employer".
* **Employment start date**: Enter the approximate month and year you started your most recent job. If you are currently employed, leave this field blank.
* **Employment end date**: Enter the approximate month and year you left your most recent job, if applicable. If currently employed, leave this field blank.
* **Previous employers**: List your most recent three employers with their corresponding job titles and dates of employment.
* **Key responsibilities**: Provide brief bullet points summarizing your key responsibilities in recent roles.
* **Reason for leaving**: Explain concisely why you left your most recent role.
* **Professional references**: List up to two professional references with their name, relationship to you, and contact details.
