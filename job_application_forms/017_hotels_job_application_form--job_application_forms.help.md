# hotels_job_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for job applicants to provide their contact and work-related information for job openings in the hotel industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Name", "Email", "Phone Number", "Street", "City", and "Country" fields with your contact information.
2. In the "Desired Position" field, select the job title that you are interested in applying for.
3. Enter your available start date in the "Available Start Date" field. If you are available to start immediately, you can select the current date.
4. Attach any relevant documents or references to support your application in the "Resume" and "Attachments" fields.
5. In the "Job Title", "Company Name", and "Department" fields, provide your current or most recent job information.
6. Indicate your current job status in the "Job Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your professional email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Street** (Text, Optional): Enter the street address of your residence or work location.
* **Postal Code** (Text, Optional): Enter the postal code of your residence or work location.
* **City** (Text, Optional): Enter the city of your residence or work location.
* **Country** (Text, Optional): Enter the country of your residence or work location.
* **Desired Position** (Text, Optional): Select the job title that you are applying for.
* **Available Start Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you are available to start working.
* **Resume** (Text, Optional): Attach a copy of your resume or CV.
* **Attachments** (Note, Optional): Attach any relevant documents or references that support your application.
* **Job Title** (Text, Optional): Enter your current or most recent job title.
* **Company Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your current or most recent company.
* **Department** (Text, Optional): Enter the department you are working in at your current or most recent company.
* **Job Status** (Text, Optional): Indicate your current job status (e.g. "Employed", "Unemployed", "Intern", etc.).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* You can attach multiple files to the "Resume" and "Attachments" fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can contact the hotel management or HR department for clarification.
* This form is for job applicants to provide their contact and work-related information for job openings in the hotel industry.
