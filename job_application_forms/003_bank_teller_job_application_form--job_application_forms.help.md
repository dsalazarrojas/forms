# Bank Teller Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bank Teller Job Application Form is designed to gather information from applicants for bank teller positions. This form collects personal details, professional qualifications, and other relevant data that will be used to assess your candidacy for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a complete application, please ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (2): Enter your legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (3): Provide a current email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (4): Enter your phone number, including the country code if applicable.
* **Residential Address** (5): Enter your street address, city, state, and postal code.
* **Position Applied For** (6): Specify the role you are applying for.
* **Desired Salary** (7): Enter your desired annual salary in local currency.
* **Available Start Date** (8): Choose a date when you could begin employment.
* **Highest Education Level** (9): Select your highest completed educational qualification.
	+ High school diploma: High school diploma or equivalent.
	+ Associate degree: 2-year associate degree or associate of arts.
	+ Bachelor degree: Bachelor degree or equivalent.
	+ Master degree: Master degree or equivalent.
	+ Doctorate: Doctoral degree.
* **Previous Experience** (10): Briefly describe your relevant work experience.
* **Professional References** (11): List two professional references with their names and contact information.
* **Resume or CV Link** (12): Provide a link to your online resume or portfolio.
* **Authorization to Verify Information** (13): Please confirm that you give permission for us to conduct background checks where permitted by law. 

## Tips
* Be as accurate and complete as possible when filling out this form to ensure a thorough review of your application.
* Double-check your details before submitting.
* Ensure your email address and phone number are correct and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions, please contact us for assistance.
