# Education Freelance Business Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Freelance Business Application form is designed to collect essential information about your educational qualifications, work experience, and availability to work as a freelance professional. This form will help us match you with potential clients or projects that fit your skills and expertise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Applicant Name" field.
2. Choose your highest level of education from the "Education Level" dropdown menu.
3. If applicable, enter your relevant job experience in years.
4. Select your preferred work type (Freelance, Part-time, or Full-time).
5. Choose your availability to work (no options provided, just enter your availability).
6. Select a date for your preferred work schedule (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
7. Enter your location.
8. Check if you have a job description that you'd like to share with us (yes or no).
9. Enter your contact number for us to reach out to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required: false): Select your highest level of education achieved. This could be High School, Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD.
* **Job Experience** (`job_experience`, number, required: false): If applicable, enter the number of years you have been working in your field. This is optional but helps us understand your level of expertise.
* **Work Type** (`work_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you would like to work as a freelancer (Freelance, Part-time, or Full-time).
* **Availability** (`availability`, time, required: false): Enter your availability to work in the format "HH:MM - HH:MM".
* **Work Schedule** (`work_schedule`, date, required: false): Select a specific date for your work schedule.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter your location, so we can best match you with potential clients or projects near you.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, select_multiple, required: false): Check if you have a job description that you'd like to share with us (yes or no).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your contact number, so we can reach out to you for further information or to discuss potential projects.
