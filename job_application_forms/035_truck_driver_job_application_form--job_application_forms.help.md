# Truck Driver Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us get to know you better and determine your suitability for a truck driving job. Please provide accurate and honest information about yourself.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields marked with * (asterisk).
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name in their respective fields.
3. Enter your Email address as it appears on your official email.
4. Input your Phone number for contact purposes.
5. Choose your address from the provided options.
6. Select your current employment status.
7. Choose your work experience for the position.
8. Indicate if you hold a valid driver's license.
9. Enter your available date to start work.
10. Input your expected end date.
11. Enter the number of hours you are willing to work per week.
12. Choose your experience with vehicles.
13. Choose your experience with cargo.
14. Enter the number of years of experience you have.
15. Indicate if you are available to work.
16. Choose your education level.
17. Input your previous employer's name (if applicable).
18. Enter your job title (if applicable).
19. Choose your work schedule.
20. Choose your company's vacation policy.
21. Choose if you are eligible for benefits.
22. Input your emergency contact's name.
23. Input your emergency contact's relationship to you.
24. Input your emergency contact's phone number.
25. Input your emergency contact's email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (*): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (*): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (*): Enter your official email address.
- **Phone** (*): Enter your phone number.
- **Address** (*): Choose your address from the provided options.
- **Employment Status** (*): Choose your current employment status.
- **Work Experience** (*): Choose your relevant work experience.
- **Driver License** (*): Indicate if you hold a valid driver's license.
- **Available Date** (*): Enter your available date to start work.
- **End Date** (optional): Enter your expected end date of work.
- **Hours** (*): Enter the number of hours you are willing to work per week.
- **Vehicle Experience** (optional): Choose your experience with vehicles.
- **Cargo Experience** (optional): Choose your experience with cargo.
- **Experience Year** (*): Enter the number of years of experience you have.
- **Availability** (*): Indicate if you are available to work.
- **Education** (optional): Choose your education level.
- **Previous Employer** (optional): Enter your previous employer's name (if applicable).
- **Job Title** (optional): Enter your job title (if applicable).
- **Work Schedule** (optional): Choose your work schedule.
- **Vacation Policy** (optional): Choose your company's vacation policy.
- **Benefits** (optional): Choose if you are eligible for benefits.
- **Emergency Contact** (optional): Input your emergency contact's name.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship** (optional): Input your emergency contact's relationship to you.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (optional): Input your emergency contact's phone number.
- **Emergency Contact Email** (optional): Input your emergency contact's email.
