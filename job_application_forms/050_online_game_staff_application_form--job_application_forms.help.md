# Online Game Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for online game staff applicants to submit their applications and provide information about their background and qualifications. The form is not a formal job application, but rather a way to express interest in a position and showcase their experience and fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your in-game name, which is your username or nickname used within the game.
2. Choose the position you are applying for, such as Guild Master or Member.
3. Share a brief description of your background and experience related to the game. This can include your gaming history, achievements, or any relevant skills or qualifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **In-game Name (`game_ign`, text, not required)**: Please enter your in-game name, which is your username or nickname used within the game. This will help us identify you as a game user.
- **Position Applying For (`position`, select_one, not required)**: Choose the position you are interested in applying for. Please select either Guild Master or Member from the dropdown menu.
- **A bit of background about the game (`game_background`, text, not required)**: Share a brief description of your background and experience related to the game. This can include your gaming history, achievements, or any relevant skills or qualifications you have acquired while playing the game.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to fill out as much information as you feel necessary.
- If you have any supporting materials, such as a resume or portfolio, please attach them separately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
