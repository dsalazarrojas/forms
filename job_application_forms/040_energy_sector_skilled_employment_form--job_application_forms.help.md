# energy_sector_skilled_employment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals seeking employment in the energy sector, specifically for skilled positions. It will be used by hiring managers to review and evaluate applications.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. For multiple-select fields, select all applicable options that apply to you.
3. Ensure the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required/true): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required/true): Enter your work or personal phone number.
* **Work Experience** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your relevant work experience, including job titles, companies, and dates of employment.
* **Resume** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Attach or paste your resume or a link to it.
* **Job Applications** (`7`, `select_one`, required/true): Select "Yes" if you are currently or have previously applied for this job position.
* **Location** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your current location or the location where you are willing to work.
* **Job Title** (`9`, `text`, required/true): Enter your current or most recent job title.
* **Skills** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you possess the skills listed in the options (e.g., "Yes, No").
* **Education** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your educational background, including degrees and institutions.
* **Previous Employer** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your previous employer's name and job title.
* **Position** (`13`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you are currently holding a position.
* **Department** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the department where you would be working.
* **Supervisor** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Manager** (`16`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you have a manager.
* **Years of Experience** (`17`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your years of experience in the field.
* **Salary Requirements** (`18`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your salary requirements.
* **Work Status** (`19`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select "Active" if you are actively working, "Inactive" if you are not.
* **Job Type** (`20`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select "Yes" if the job type listed applies to you (e.g., full-time, part-time, contract).
* **Job Sector** (`21`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the job sector where you would be working.
* **Job Level** (`22`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job level (e.g., entry-level, management).
* **Job Description** (`23`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the job you are applying for.
* **Job Industry** (`24`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the job industry.
* **Job Location** (`25`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location where the job is based.
