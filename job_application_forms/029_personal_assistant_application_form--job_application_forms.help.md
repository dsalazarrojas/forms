# Personal Assistant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to apply for personal assistant roles within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your personal details in the first page, which includes your first name, last name, email address, and availability.
2. On the second page, provide your job title, job description, work experience, education, and skills.
3. In the last page, submit your resume, portfolio, and references if applicable.
4. Finally, verify your job ID, resume URL, and any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Please provide your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Please provide your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): This field is for your professional email address. If you don't have a work email, you can leave this blank.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Please provide your phone number as it appears on your identification.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, date, required): Please select a date and time when you are available to start work.
* **Availability Date** (`Availability Date`, date, required): This field is to confirm the date you selected in the previous field.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, required): Please provide the title of the job you are applying for, as it appears on the job description.
* **Job Description** (`Job Description`, text, required): Please describe the job you are applying for, as it appears on the job description.
* **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, text, required): Please describe your previous work experience, highlighting your relevant skills and qualifications.
* **Education** (`Education`, text, required): Please list your educational background, including degrees and institutions.
* **Skills** (`Skills`, text, required): Please list your relevant skills and qualifications.
* **Resume** (`Resume`, text, optional): Please attach a copy of your resume, or provide a URL to view it online.
* **Portfolio** (`Portfolio`, text, optional): Please attach a copy of your portfolio, or provide a URL to view it online.
* **References** (`References`, text, optional): Please list your professional references, including name, email, and phone number.
* **References Hint** (`References Hint`, text, optional): This field is not used for the application process.
* **References Url** (`References Url`, text, optional): Please provide a URL to your references if they are publicly available.
* **References Note** (`References Note`, text, optional): Please provide any additional notes about your references.
* **References Email** (`References Email`, email, optional): Please provide a valid email address of your reference.
* **References Phone** (`References Phone`, text, optional): Please provide a valid phone number of your reference.
* **Job ID** (`Job ID`, text, optional): This field is not used for the application process.
* **Job ID Hint** (`Job ID Hint`, text, optional): This field is not used for the application process.
* **Job ID Url** (`Job ID Url`, text, optional): This field is not used for the application process.
* **Job ID Note** (`Job ID Note`, text, optional): This field is not used for the application process.
* **Resume Url** (`Resume Url`, text, optional): Please provide a URL to your resume if it is hosted online.
* **Resume Note** (`Resume Note`, text, optional): Please provide any additional notes about your resume.
* **Job ID** (`Job ID`, text, optional): This field is not used for the application process.
* **Job ID Url** (`Job ID Url`, text, optional): This field is not used for the application process.
* **Job ID Note** (`Job ID Note`, text, optional): This field is not used for the application process.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have a clear and concise resume and portfolio ready to be submitted.
* If you have any questions or need help with the application process, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your interest in joining our team and look forward to reviewing your application.
