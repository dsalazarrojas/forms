# Party Stylist Job Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Party Stylist Job Registration Form is a tool for potential candidates to provide their information for evaluation and consideration for the Party Stylist position. Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to increase your chances of being considered for the job.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, making sure to provide your name and experience in the Party Stylist Name and Experience fields respectively.
2. Provide your education background in the Education field, if applicable.
3. Enter your work experience in the Work Experience field, if applicable.
4. Select your aptitude test score and date from the Aptitude Test Score and Aptitude Test Date fields respectively.
5. Indicate your specialization in the Specialization field.
6. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
7. Provide your email address in the Email field.
8. Upload your aptitude test prophets in the Aptitude Test Prophets field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Party Stylist Name** (`party_stylist_job_registration_form_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Experience** (`party_stylist_job_registration_form_experience`, text, required): Describe your experience as a party stylist, including any relevant training or certifications you hold.
* **Education** (`party_stylist_job_registration_form_education`, text, optional): If you have any formal education related to party styling, please provide details.
* **Work Experience** (`party_stylist_job_registration_form_work_experience`, text, optional): If you have any relevant work experience, please provide details.
* **Aptitude Test Score** (`party_stylist_job_registration_form_aptitude_test_score`, number, required): Enter your aptitude test score.
* **Specialization** (`party_stylist_job_registration_form_specialization`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you have experience with any of the specialized skills required for the job.
* **Phone** (`party_stylist_job_registration_form_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`party_stylist_job_registration_form_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Aptitude Test Date** (`party_stylist_job_registration_form_aptitude_test_date`, date, required): Enter the date you took the aptitude test.
* **Aptitude Test Prophets** (`party_stylist_job_registration_form_aptitude_test_prophets`, text, optional): If you have any additional information or evidence related to your aptitude test, please provide it.
