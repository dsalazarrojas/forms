# Instructor Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Instructor Interest Form" is a tool used to gather information from instructors about their background, experience, and interest in a particular program or initiative. This form helps program administrators and decision-makers review and manage instructor applications efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field for contact purposes.
3. Provide your phone number for easy communication.
4. Choose your type of experience (instructor type) from the provided options.
5. Input your years of experience.
6. Select your education level.
7. Describe the areas of interest you have for the program.
8. Choose the available start date for your involvement.
9. Input your preferred start date for your involvement.
10. Provide a statement explaining your interest in the program.
11. If you have military experience, select the branch you served in and the number of years served.
12. Rate your military experience (this may be a subjective assessment).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (text, required): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed.
- **Last name** (text, required): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed.
- **Email** (text, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone** (text, optional): Provide your phone number for easy communication.
- **Instructor type** (select one, optional): Choose your type of experience (Military/Civilian).
- **Years experience** (number, optional): Input the number of years you have been in this type of experience.
- **Education level** (select multiple, optional): Select all applicable education levels (High School, Associate's, Bachelor's, Master's, Doctorate).
- **Areas of interest** (text, optional): Describe the specific areas of interest you have for the program.
- **Available start date** (date, optional): Choose the earliest date you can start your involvement.
- **Preferred start date** (date, optional): Input the date you prefer to start your involvement.
- **Instructor interest statement** (note, optional): Provide a statement explaining your interest in the program.
- **Military service** (select one, optional): If you have military experience, select the branch you served in (Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Air Force, Coast Guard).
- **Military branch** (select multiple, optional): If you have military experience, select all branches you served in (Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Air Force, Coast Guard).
- **Military years served** (number, optional): If you have military experience, input the number of years served.
- **Military rate** (number, optional): Rate your military experience (this may be a subjective assessment).
- **Military education** (text, optional): If you have military experience, describe your military education.
