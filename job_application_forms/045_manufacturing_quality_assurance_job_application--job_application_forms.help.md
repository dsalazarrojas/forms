# Manufacturing Quality Assurance Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a job application for a manufacturing quality assurance position. It is designed to collect information from candidates who have been referred to a job posting for this specific role. The goal of this form is to assess the suitability of each candidate for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the candidate information section with your name, phone number, and email address. This is a required field.
2. If you have experience in the manufacturing industry, answer "Yes" to the job information section if the job you are applying for is one you are qualified for.
3. Attach your resume in the resume section. This is a required field. Please upload a valid PDF or Word document.
4. Enter your relevant skills and experience in the experience section. This is a required field.
5. If you have professional references, answer "Yes" to the references section.
6. Finally, submit your application by selecting a date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Information** (`candidate_info`, text, required): This is the section where you will enter your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Job Information** (`job_info`, select_multiple, required): This is where you indicate if you have experience in the manufacturing industry and if the job you are applying for is one you are qualified for. Select "Yes" if you are a good fit.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required): This is where you will attach your resume in a PDF or Word document format.
* **Experience** (`experience`, number, required): This is the section where you will enter your relevant experience in years. For example, if you have 5 years of experience, enter 5.
* **References** (`references`, select_multiple, required): This is where you will indicate if you have professional references.
* **Submit** (`submit`, date, required): This is where you submit your application. Please select a date to confirm your application.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Ensure that your resume is in a valid PDF or Word document format.
* Be honest and accurate when filling out your experience and skills.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert contact information].
