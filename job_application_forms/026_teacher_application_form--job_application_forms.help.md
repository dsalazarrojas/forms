# Teacher Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Teacher Application Form" is designed for teachers to apply for a teaching position. This form helps the school or institution gather information about the applicant's education background, work experience, teaching experience, job preferences, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Provide detailed answers to all the questions.
3. Ensure that you have attached your resume or uploaded it through the designated upload option.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure that they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Please enter your name, email address, and phone number to ensure that we can contact you regarding your application.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, optional): Share your educational qualifications, degrees, and relevant certifications.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Describe your previous work experience, including job titles, duration, and relevant job responsibilities.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): List at least two professional references with their name, job title, and contact information.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Provide details about your educational background, including institutions attended and relevant courses taken.
* **Work History** (`work_history`, text, optional): Share your work history, including job titles, duration, and relevant job responsibilities.
* **Professional Credentials** (`professional_credentials`, text, optional): Share your professional certifications, licenses, or other relevant credentials.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, text, optional): Specify the type of job you are interested in and any requirements you have, such as specific subjects or grade levels.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Job Preferences** (`job_preferences`, text, optional): Share your preferences for the job, such as working hours, salary range, or location.
* **Work Schedule** (`work_schedule`, text, optional): Specify your work schedule, including availability and preferred working hours.
* **Teaching Experience** (`teaching_experience`, text, optional): Share your teaching experience, including grade levels and subjects taught.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your ideal job and what you can bring to the role.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): List your relevant skills, such as language proficiency or software skills.
* **Teaching Certifications** (`teaching_certifications`, text, optional): Share any teaching certifications or licenses you hold.
* **Language Abilities** (`language_abilities`, text, optional): Specify your language abilities, including languages spoken and written.
* **Work Environment** (`work_environment`, text, optional): Describe your preferred work environment, including classroom size, student age range, or other relevant factors.
* **References List** (`references_list`, text, optional): List all your references, including their name, job title, and contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): Provide the contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Provide the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide your home address.
* **Email** (`email`, text, optional): Provide your email address.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): Attach or upload your resume.
* **License** (`license`, text, optional): Share any license or certification you hold.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* Proofread your form before submitting it to ensure that it is error-free.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
