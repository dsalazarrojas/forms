# teaching_assistant_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a general application form for individuals interested in teaching positions. It is designed to collect relevant information to help us understand your background, qualifications, and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Contact Information" section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Share your relevant work experience in the "Work Experience" field.
3. Describe your educational background in the "Education Background" field.
4. List your teaching interests in the "Teaching Interests" field.
5. Provide any additional skills you have in the "Skills" field.
6. Specify your availability for teaching in the "Availability" field.
7. List any references that can vouch for your teaching abilities in the "References" field.
8. If you have any documents that support your application, such as a resume, please upload it in the "Resume Upload" field.
9. Finally, submit your application by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information (1)**:
	+ This is where you provide your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Work Experience (2)**:
	+ Please share your relevant work experience, including any relevant positions or roles you've held.
* **Education Background (3)**:
	+ Describe your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **Teaching Interests (4)**:
	+ List any topics or subjects you are interested in teaching.
* **Skills (5-10)**:
	+ Please list any additional skills you have that are relevant to teaching, such as language proficiency or certifications.
* **Availability (6)**:
	+ Specify your availability for teaching, including your preferred schedule and location.
* **References (7)**:
	+ Provide any references that can vouch for your teaching abilities.
* **Resume Upload (8)**:
	+ If you have any supporting documents, such as a resume or CV, please upload it here.
* **Submit (9)**:
	+ Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Tips
Please make sure to provide accurate and complete information in each field. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact us for clarification. We are here to help.
