# Bus Driver Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals interested in applying for a bus driver position. It collects the necessary information to verify the candidate's qualifications, licensing, and background.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your contact information in the **Contact Information** section.
2. Fill in your full name, street address, primary phone number, and email address.
3. Select your driver's license type and class in the **Licensing and Qualifications** section.
4. Fill in your CDL number and select the endorsements held.
5. Enter your years of professional bus driving experience.
6. Provide information about your most recent employer and previous accidents in the **Employment and Driving History** section.
7. Declare your clean driving record and authorize a full background and driving record check.
8. Optionally, explain why you are interested in the bus driver position in the **Motivation** section.
9. Confirm your authorization for a full background and driving record check.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your driver's license.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your street address, including city, state, and zip code.
* **Primary Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Driver License Type/Class**: Select your highest level of licensing from the options provided.
* **CDL Number**: Enter your CDL number, if applicable.
* **Endorsements Held**: Select all endorsements you hold, such as Passenger, School Bus, Air Brakes, etc.
* **Years of Professional Bus Driving Experience**: Enter the number of years you have been professionally driving a bus.
* **Most Recent Employer**: Enter the name of your most recent employer.
* **Major Traffic Accidents in Last 3 Years**: Select the number of incidents you have been involved in, if any.
* **I Declare my driving record is currently clean**: Select "I Declare" to confirm your clean driving record.
* **I Authorize a full background and driving record check**: Select "Authorized" to confirm your authorization for a full background and driving record check.
* **Why are you interested in this position**: Optionally, enter a brief statement explaining why you are interested in the bus driver position.
