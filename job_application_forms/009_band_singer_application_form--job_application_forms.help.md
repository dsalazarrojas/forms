# Band Singer Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your singing skills, experience, and suitability for joining the band. Please provide accurate and honest answers to help us assess your application.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your personal details such as your name and email address.
2.  Select your vocal range and preferred musical styles.
3.  Choose your level of proficiency in sight-reading and singing backup harmonies.
4.  Provide information about your performance experience, including the number of years you have been performing professionally.
5.  Share any notable bands or projects you have been part of.
6.  Provide links to audio or video recordings of your singing, if possible.
7.  Indicate whether you own your own microphone and in-ear monitors (IEMs).
8.  Confirm your ability to travel internationally, if required.
9.  Finally, write a brief motivation statement explaining why you want to join the band.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): This is your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, email, required): This is your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): This is your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Vocal Range** (`vocal_range`, select_one, required): Choose the category that best describes your vocal range (Soprano, Mezzo-Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Baritone, Bass).
* **Preferred Musical Styles** (`musical_styles`, select_multiple, required): Select all the musical styles you are familiar with (Pop / Top 40, Rock / Alternative, Jazz / Standards, R&B / Soul, Country, Classical / Opera, Musical Theater).
* **Can you sight-read music?** (`sight_reading`, select_one, required): Choose your level of proficiency in sight-reading (Yes - Proficiently, Yes - With some preparation, No - I learn by ear).
* **Ability to sing backup harmonies?** (`harmony_skills`, select_one, required): Choose your level of ability in singing backup harmonies (Expert - Can arrange and sing harmonies, Intermediate - Can follow a harmony line, Beginner - Prefer lead vocals only).
* **Years of Professional Performing Experience** (`years_performing`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been performing professionally.
* **Notable Previous Bands or Projects** (`previous_bands`, text, optional): List any notable bands or projects you have been part of.
* **Audio Demo Link** (`audio_link`, text, required): Provide a link to a recording of you singing on SoundCloud or Dropbox.
* **Performance Video Link** (`video_link`, text, optional): Provide a link to a live performance video on YouTube or Instagram.
* **Do you own your own microphone and IEMs?** (`gear_ownership`, select_one, required): Choose whether you own a full vocal rig, partially or rely on venue/band gear.
* **Travel Readiness** (`travel_readiness`, select_one, required): Confirm your ability to travel internationally (True, False, In Progress).
* **Why do you want to join this band?** (`additional_info`, text, required): Write a brief motivation statement explaining why you want to join the band.

Note: This form is intended for serious applicants only. By filling out this form, you confirm that you have reviewed the band's requirements and expectations and are committed to joining the band as a serious applicant.
