# Academic Librarian Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Librarian Job Application Form is designed to collect information from candidates who are applying for the Academic Librarian position. Completing this form will help the hiring team assess your qualifications, experience, and fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are eligible for the Academic Librarian position.
2. Fill in your full name and email address.
3. Enter your phone number and current employer (if applicable).
4. Describe your relevant qualifications and certifications.
5. Summarize your relevant experience.
6. Provide references (name and contact details).
7. Paste or write a short summary of your cover letter.
8. List or link to examples of your work (portfolio or publications).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Full name (required)
* **Full name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your records.

### Email address (required)
* **Email address**: Provide your contact email address where we can reach you for application updates.

### Phone number
* **Phone number**: Enter your best phone number where we can contact you.

### Current employer (optional)
* **Current employer**: If you are currently employed, enter the name of your institution or organization.

### Qualifications and certifications (required)
* **Qualifications and certifications**: Describe your relevant degrees, certifications, or other credentials.

### Summary of relevant experience (required)
* **Summary of relevant experience**: Briefly describe your experience in a library setting, highlighting your skills and achievements.

### References (optional)
* **References**: List the names and contact details of your professional references.

### Cover letter summary (optional)
* **Cover letter summary**: Paste or write a short summary of your cover letter, highlighting your interest and qualifications for the position.

### Portfolio or publications (optional)
* **Portfolio or publications**: List or link to examples of your work, such as research papers, publications, or projects.
