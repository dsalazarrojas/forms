# Preschool Teacher Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Teacher Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants interested in working as preschool teachers. The form helps us evaluate your suitability for the position and assess your qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, address, email, and phone number.
2. Select your education background and teaching certification.
3. Provide information about your work experience and job preferences.
4. Complete the job-specific fields, including job title, job description, job location, and job duration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Address** (3, text, required): Enter your residential address.
- **Email** (4, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
- **Phone** (5, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Date of Birth** (6, date, optional): If applicable, enter your date of birth.
- **Experience** (7, number, required): Enter the number of years of work experience you have as a teacher.
- **Education Background** (8, select one, required): Select your highest level of education completed.
- **Taching Certification** (9, select multiple, required): Indicate if you have any teaching certification.
- **Work Experience** (10, select multiple, required): Indicate if you have any work experience in the field.
- **Job Title** (11, text, required): Enter the title of the job you are applying for (e.g., Preschool Teacher).
- **Job Description** (12, text, required): Describe the job you are applying for in a few sentences.
- **Job Location** (13, text, required): Enter the location where you would like to work as a preschool teacher.
- **Salary Range** (14, number, optional): Enter the salary range you are expecting (if applicable).
- **Job Type** (15, select multiple, required): Select the job type (Full Time or Part Time).
- **Work Hours** (16, number, required): Enter the number of hours you are willing to work as a preschool teacher.
- **Available Days** (17, number, required): Enter the number of days you are available to work as a preschool teacher.
- **Job Start Date** (18, date, required): Enter the start date of the job you are applying for.
- **Job End Date** (19, date, required): Enter the end date of the job you are applying for.
- **Job Duration** (20, select multiple, required): Select the duration of the job (Full Time, Part Time, or Temporary).
- **Job Status** (21, select multiple, required): Select the status of the job (Active or Inactive).
- **Description** (22, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the job you are applying for.
- **Required Documents** (23, select multiple, required): Indicate which documents you have attached with your application (Resume, ID Card, or Passport).
- **Application Status** (24, select multiple, required): Select the current status of your application (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
- **Submitted On** (25, date, required): Enter the date you submitted your application.

Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, and do not hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
