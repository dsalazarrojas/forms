# Educational Teaching Experience Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect educational teaching experience and employment information from applicants. It is intended for internal HR use only and should not be shared externally.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the fields as prompted on each page.
2. Make sure to select "Teaching Experience" and "Education" fields accurately, as they are required.
3. You can upload relevant documents and attachments if requested.
4. Enter any additional information you believe will help us understand your application better.
5. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information** (Page 1): This field is the first page of the form. Here, you can enter your name and any other identifying information.
* **Teaching Experience** (Page 2, Multiple Select): This field allows you to select from a list of options related to teaching experience. Please choose the options that best describe your experience.
* **Education** (Page 2, Single Select): This field asks you to select your level of education. Choose the option that best matches your educational background.
* **Uploading Documents** (Page 3): If you have any relevant documents that support your application, you can upload them here. This is not a required field, but it may be helpful for our review.
* **Uploading Attachments** (Page 3): This field is similar to "Uploading Documents", but it is used for attaching supporting files not related to documents. Again, this is not required.
* **Additional Information** (Page 3, Note): If there's anything else you'd like to share about yourself that we should know, enter it here. This field is optional.
* **Submit** (Page 3): This is the final step. Please review and submit the form once you have completed all other fields.
* **Uploading Resume** (Page 3): This field is for attaching your resume. If you have one, you can upload it here. It's not required, but it's an opportunity to highlight your qualifications and experience.
