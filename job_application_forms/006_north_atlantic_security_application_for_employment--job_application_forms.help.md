# north-atlantic-security-application-for-employment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for job applicants to provide their application information for North Atlantic Security.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Application** (1, text, required: false): Fill in your job application details.
* **Contact Info** (2, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Security Clearance** (3, number, required: false): Enter your security clearance level, if applicable.
* **Social Security** (4, number, required: false): Enter your social security number (or equivalent).
* **Date of Birth** (5, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
* **Emergency Contact** (6, text, required: false): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Work Experience** (7, select_one, required: false): Select whether you have work experience.
* **Education** (8, select_multiple, required: false): Select your level of education.
* **Skills** (9, select_multiple, required: false): Select your relevant skills.
* **Languages** (10, text, required: false): Enter languages you can speak.
* **Submit Button** (11, text, required: false): This is the submit button, do not enter anything here.
* **Email** (12, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (13, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (14, date, required: false): Enter your availability or desired start date.
* **Time** (15, time, required: false): Enter your available time for work.
* **Select One** (16, select_one, required: false): Select one option from the list.
* **Select Multiple** (17, select_multiple, required: false): Select multiple options from the list.
* **Note** (18, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes.
* **Notes** (19, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes.
* **Text Input** (20, text, required: false): Enter any additional text input.
* **Integer Input** (21, number, required: false): Enter an integer value.
* **Decimal Input** (22, number, required: false): Enter a decimal value.
* **Select One 2** (23, select_one, required: false): Select one option from the list.
* **Select Multiple 2** (24, select_multiple, required: false): Select multiple options from the list.
* **Date of Application** (25, date, required: false): Enter the date you are applying for the job.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are accurately filled in before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the hiring manager or HR representative for clarification.
* Make sure to keep your answers concise and within the required length.
