# Application Form DSJ Mardan - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect personal and professional information from applicants for job openings, which will be used to assess the suitability of the applicant for the position applied for.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the first page, which contains the following fields:
	* **Applicant name**: Enter your full name as per your official documents.
	* **National identity number**: Enter your local identity card number.
	* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth.
2. Proceed to the second page, which contains the following fields:
	* **Position applied for**: Select the vacancy title you are applying for.
	* **Education**: Enter your highest qualification.
3. Proceed to the third page, which contains the following fields:
	* **Domicile**: Enter your district and province.
	* **Contact number**: Enter your mobile phone number.
	* **Documents uploaded**: List any supporting documents you have uploaded.
4. Proceed to the fourth page, which contains the following field:
	* **Declaration**: Declare the truthfulness of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant name** (Page 1, field 1): Enter your full name as per your official documents.
* **National identity number** (Page 1, field 2): Enter your local identity card number.
* **Date of birth** (Page 1, field 3): Enter your date of birth.
* **Position applied for** (Page 2, field 4): Select the vacancy title you are applying for.
* **Education** (Page 2, field 5): Enter your highest qualification.
* **Domicile** (Page 3, field 7): Enter your district and province.
* **Contact number** (Page 3, field 8): Enter your mobile phone number.
* **Declaration** (Page 4, field 10): Declare the truthfulness of the information provided.
* **Relevant experience** (Page 2, field 6, optional): Enter a brief summary of your work history.
* **Documents uploaded** (Page 3, field 9, optional): List any supporting documents you have uploaded.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
* If you have any difficulty filling out this form, please contact the relevant authorities.
