# Virtual Job Fair Employer Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for employers to register their company and provide essential information for the virtual job fair event. The form will help the organizers and participants understand your company's profile and job opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out each field carefully and accurately, as the information will be used to create your company's profile for the virtual job fair.
- Ensure that you have all necessary attachments, such as company logos, job descriptions, and event details, ready before starting the registration process.
- If you need to make changes or updates to your registration, please contact the event organizers directly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employers Details**: Enter your company's name, description, and any other relevant details.
  • Type: text
  • Required: no
- **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for your company's participation.
  • Type: text
  • Required: no
- **Email**: Enter your company's email address for communication purposes.
  • Type: email
  • Required: no
- **Phone**: Enter your company's phone number for contact and communication purposes.
  • Type: text
  • Required: no
- **Company Name**: Enter your company's name as it will be displayed on the event platform.
  • Type: text
  • Required: no
- **Job Title**: Enter a brief description of the job title and position available.
  • Type: text
  • Required: no
- **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of the job responsibilities and requirements.
  • Type: text
  • Required: no
- **Company Logo**: Upload your company's logo for visual representation on the event platform.
  • Type: text
  • Required: no
- **Event Date**: Enter the date of the virtual event (if already decided).
  • Type: date
  • Required: no
- **Event Time**: Enter the start and end time of the virtual event (if already decided).
  • Type: time
  • Required: no
- **Event Duration**: Enter the duration of the virtual event (if already decided).
  • Type: number
  • Required: no
- **Booth Number**: Enter the booth number for your company's virtual booth (if already assigned).
  • Type: text
  • Required: no
- **Booth Details**: Enter any additional details about your company's virtual booth.
  • Type: text
  • Required: no
- **Registration Details**: Enter any additional registration details or requirements.
  • Type: text
  • Required: no
- **Registration Fee**: Enter the registration fee for your company's participation (if already decided).
  • Type: number
  • Required: no
- **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment for the registration fee.
  • Type: select_one
  • Required: no
- **Payment Status**: Indicate the payment status of your registration fee.
  • Type: select_one
  • Required: no
- **Job Requirements**: Enter any additional job requirements or qualifications.
  • Type: text
  • Required: no
- **Company Address**: Enter your company's physical address for contact and communication purposes.
  • Type: text
  • Required: no
- **Event Details**: Enter any additional event details or requirements.
  • Type: text
  • Required: no
- **Event Link**: Enter the link to any external event-related resources (if applicable).
  • Type: text
  • Required: no
- **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
  • Type: text
  • Required: no
- **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about your company or job opportunities.
  • Type: text
  • Required: no
- **Confirmation**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the event terms and conditions.
  • Type: select_one
  • Required: no
- **Signature**: Enter your digital signature as proof of your agreement.
  • Type: text
  • Required: no

## Tips
- Please double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers directly.
- Ensure that you have all necessary attachments and documents ready before submitting the form.
