# Transportation Fleet Manager Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Transportation Fleet Manager job application form. Please complete this form to submit your application for the Transportation Fleet Manager position. The form is divided into sections that require specific information about your personal details, job preferences, and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your personal details:
   - First Name: Enter your first name as per your identification documents.
   - Last Name: Enter your last name as per your identification documents.
   - Email: Enter your valid email address.
   - Phone: Enter your contact phone number.
   - Resume: You can attach or link to your resume, if applicable.
2. Select your job preferences:
   - Job Title: Select the job title you are applying for.
   - Job Type: Choose between Full-time, Part-time, or Contract.
   - Job Level: Choose between Senior, Manager, or Supervisor.
3. Experience and skills:
   - Experience: Select your level of experience (Senior, Mid-level, or Entry).
   - Skills: Select the relevant skills you possess (Time Management, Fleet Management, or Communication).
4. Education:
   - Education: Select your highest level of education completed (High School, College, or University).
5. Additional details:
   - Language: Select the language(s) you are proficient in (English, Spanish, or French).
   - Salary: Enter your expected salary.
6. Employment status:
   - Employment Status: Select your current employment status (Full-time, Part-time, or Freelance).
   - Job Status: Select your current job status (Active or Inactive).
7. Application and employment details:
   - Date Available: Enter the date you are available to start working.
   - Time Available: Enter your available working hours.
   - Notice Period: If applicable, enter the notice period you are required to give.
   - Termination Reason: If applicable, provide the reason for your previous employment termination.
   - Termination Date: If applicable, enter the termination date.
   - Notice Period in Days: If applicable, enter the number of days you are required to give notice.
   - Job Application Status: Select the status of your application (Under Review, Approved, or Rejected).
   - Comments: If applicable, provide any additional comments about your application.
8. Finally, sign and submit your application:
   - Signature: Please sign your application with your full name and date.
