# Application For Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for applicants to submit their information and preferences for job positions available at our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields marked as required.
* Enter your details in the corresponding sections.
* If a section has a list of options, select the relevant one.
* Upload your resume if you wish to submit a supporting document.
* Save your progress and come back later if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (full legal name, text, required): Enter your complete name as per your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (email, text, required): Provide your main contact email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (phone, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number for any follow-up discussions.
* **Position Applying For** (position, text, required): Select the job position you're interested in.
* **Employment Type** (employment_type, select_one, required): Choose between Full-time, Part-time, or Temporary positions.
* **Preferred Training Date** (preferred_training_date, date, optional): Select a date for a scheduled training session (if applicable).
* **Availability Window** (availability_window, select_one, optional): Choose a time of day for the training session (if applicable).
* **Resume Filename** (resume_filename, text, optional): Indicate the filename of the uploaded resume (if uploaded).
* **Cover Letter Summary** (cover_letter, text, optional): Provide a brief summary or key points for your application.
* **Additional Requests** (additional_requests, text, optional): Include any special requests or notes about your application.

By completing this form, you'll be able to express your interest in a job at our organization and provide essential details for our review and consideration.
