# Application Form For Tech Website - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential contributors to a tech website. It is designed to be a simple and user-friendly way for applicants to express their interest in contributing and provide relevant details about themselves.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the required fields.
2. Select the role you are interested in contributing as.
3. Provide a brief description of your key skills relevant to the role.
4. If applicable, share a sample submission link that demonstrates your work.
5. Indicate your weekly availability.
6. Share a link to your GitHub or portfolio, if available.
7. Write a motivation statement explaining why you want to contribute.
8. Provide optional references, if applicable.
9. Confirm your agreement to the contributor terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant name** (`applicant_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Position interest** (`position_interest`, Select one, Required): Select the role you are interested in contributing as (e.g., Writer, Developer, Designer, Moderator, or Other).
* **Skills summary** (`skills_summary`, Text, Required): Provide a brief description of your key skills relevant to the role.
* **Sample submission link** (`sample_submission`, Text, Optional): If you have a sample submission (e.g., article, video, or code), share the link to demonstrate your work.
* **Availability** (`availability`, Text, Optional): Indicate your weekly availability, including the number of hours you can dedicate to contributing.
* **GitHub or portfolio link** (`github_or_portfolio`, Text, Optional): Share a link to your GitHub or portfolio, if you have one.
* **Motivation statement** (`motivation`, Text, Required): Write a brief explanation of why you want to contribute to the tech website.
* **References** (`references`, Text, Optional): Provide any relevant references, if applicable.
* **Consent to terms** (`consent`, Select one, Required): Confirm that you agree to the contributor terms by selecting "I agree".
