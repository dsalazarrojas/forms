# Candidate Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Candidate Application Form is a standardized document used to collect information from candidates for job openings. This form helps to streamline the application process and ensures that all necessary information is gathered accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Full Name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Provide your primary contact email address for recruitment purposes.
3. Enter your primary phone number.
4. (Optional) Enter your current physical address if you're a local candidate.
5. List your LinkedIn profile URL (if applicable).
6. Select the position you're applying for from the provided options.
7. Enter your total years of professional experience.
8. Choose your highest level of education.
9. Select your current employment status.
10. Enter your expected annual salary (optional).
11. List your key technical skills and certifications.
12. Explain why you're interested in this role.
13. Specify when you can start immediately or after a notice period.
14. Indicate how you heard about this opportunity.
15. Choose how you're providing your resume (included with the submission, sent via email, or will provide later).
16. Certify that the information provided is true.
17. Select the date you're applying for the position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required): Your primary contact email for recruitment purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Physical Address** (`Physical Address`, text, optional): Enter your current residential address (optional).
* **LinkedIn Profile URL** (`LinkedIn Profile URL`, text, optional): If you have a LinkedIn profile, provide the URL.
* **Position Applied For** (`Position Applied For`, text, required): Select the exact job title you're applying for.
* **Years of Professional Experience** (`Years of Professional Experience`, number, required): Enter your total years of professional experience.
* **Highest Level of Education** (`Highest Level of Education`, select_one, required): Select your highest level of education.
* **Current Employment Status** (`Current Employment Status`, select_one, required): Choose your current employment status.
* **Expected Salary** (`Expected Salary`, number, optional): Enter your expected annual salary (optional).
* **Key Technical Skills** (`Key Technical Skills`, text, required): List relevant software, tools, or professional certifications.
* **Why are you interested in this role?** (`Why are you interested in this role?`, text, required): Explain what attracts you to our company.
* **Notice Period** (`Notice Period`, select_one, required): Specify when you can start immediately or after a notice period.
* **Referral Source** (`Referral Source`, select_one, required): Indicate how you heard about this opportunity.
* **Resume Link or Upload Status** (`Resume Link or Upload Status`, select_one, required): Choose how you're providing your resume.
* **I certify the information is true** (`I certify the information is true`, select_one, required): Certify that the information provided is true.
* **Date of Application** (`Date of Application`, date, required): Select the date you're submitting your application.
