# Academic Mathematics Professor Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect applications for an Academic Mathematics Professor position. Please ensure that you have a strong background in mathematics and meet the eligibility criteria before applying.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Complete the form in the following order: Applicant Information, Contact Information, Current Affiliation, Areas of Expertise, Teaching Experience, Research Summary, CV or Resume Link, and References.
2. Enter your details accurately and truthfully in each field.
3. Review and proofread your application before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official records.
* **Contact email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your institutional or primary email address.
* **Contact phone number** (`phone`, text, required): Include your country code when entering your contact phone number.
* **Current institution or employer** (`current_affiliation`, text, required): Indicate your current department and institution.
* **Areas of expertise** (`area_of_expertise`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more of the following areas of mathematics: Algebra, Analysis, Geometry, Applied mathematics, or Statistics.
* **Years of teaching experience** (`teaching_experience_years`, number, required): Enter a whole number of years you have spent teaching.
* **Brief research summary** (`research_summary`, text, optional): Write a 200-word or less summary of your key research contributions.
* **CV or resume link** (`cv_or_resume_link`, text, required): Share a link to your hosted CV or resume or upload a reference.
* **References and contact details** (`references`, text, required): Provide two academic references, including their name, position, and contact information.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have reviewed the form carefully before completing it.
* Enter all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* Proofread your application before submitting.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the hiring team for assistance.
