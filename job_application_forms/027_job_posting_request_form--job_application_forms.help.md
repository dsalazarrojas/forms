# Job Posting Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to request a job posting from a job requester. It allows job requesters to provide information about the job title, description, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure that you have all the necessary information about the job posting before starting the form.
2. Select the correct job position from the dropdown menu.
3. Fill in the job title, job description, job requirements, and other necessary details in the text fields.
4. Select whether you agree to share the job posting information with our team.
5. Confirm that you agree to our terms and conditions.
6. Select what information you are willing to share with our team.
7. Finally, review all the information you've provided and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Position** (`job-position`, select_one, required): Choose the correct job position from the dropdown menu.
* **Job Title** (`job-title`, text, required): Enter the title of the job posting.
* **Job Posting URL** (`job-posting-url`, text, required): Enter the URL of the job posting.
* **Job Requestor** (`job-requestor`, text, required): Enter the name of the person requesting the job posting.
* **Job Requester Email** (`job-requester-email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person requesting the job posting.
* **Job Requester Phone** (`job-requestor-phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person requesting the job posting.
* **Agree to Posting** (`job-agree-to-posting`, select_one, required): Agree to share the job posting information with our team. This will allow us to process your request.
* **Agree to Share Data** (`job-agree-to-share-data`, select_one, required): Agree to share data about your job posting with our team.
* **Agree to Terms** (`job-agree-to-terms`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Job Terms** (`job-terms`, text, required): Enter any special terms related to the job posting.
* **Agree to Share Comments** (`job-agree-to-share-comments`, select_one, required): Agree to share comments about the job posting with our team.
* **Agree to Share What** (`job-agree-to-share-what`, select_one, required): Select what information you are willing to share with our team.
* **Agree to Share with What** (`job-agree-to-share-with`, select_one, required): Select with whom you are willing to share the information.
* **Agree to Share with What** (`job-agree-to-share-with-what`, select_one, required): Select the specific details you are willing to share.
