# Nanny Availability Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Nanny Availability Form is used to record and manage nannies' work availability, including start date, job title, available days, start and end time, and other relevant details. This form helps us to better understand each nanny's schedule and preferences, enabling us to find the best match for our clients' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Fill in your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Choose your **Job Title** by selecting the option that best represents your role as a nanny.
4. Enter your **Availability Start Date** in the required format (DD/MM/YYYY or MM/DD/YYYY, depending on your location).
5. Enter your start time in the **Start Time** field using the 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 or 08:00 PM for 8 PM).
6. Enter your end time in the **End Time** field using the 24-hour format (e.g., 12:00 or 12:00 PM for 12 PM).
7. Choose your **Available Days** by selecting all the days you are available to work (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, etc.).
8. Choose your **Available Hours** by selecting all the hours you are available to work (e.g., 09:00-12:00, 13:00-16:00, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Job Title**: Select the option that best describes your role as a nanny (e.g., "Nanny", "Au Pair", etc.).
* **Availability Start Date**: Enter the date you are available to start working.
* **Start Time**: Enter the time you are available to start working in the 24-hour format.
* **End Time**: Enter the time you are available to finish working in the 24-hour format.
* **Available Days**: Select all the days you are available to work. For example, if you are available Monday to Thursday, select "Yes" for Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday.
* **Available Hours**: Select all the hours you are available to work. For example, if you are available from 09:00 to 12:00, select "Yes" for 09:00-12:00, but not 13:00-16:00, etc.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
