# Police Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals applying to join the police force. It is intended to gather details about the candidate's background, experience, and qualifications, as well as their physical characteristics and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields carefully and accurately.
2.  Make sure to answer all questions, even if they seem redundant or unnecessary.
3.  If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Police Certification** (`Police Certification`, `select_one`, `true`): Please select one of the options to indicate your police certification or training level.
*   **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, `text`, `false`): List your previous work experience, including job roles, job duration, and relevant skills or qualifications.
*   **Education Background** (`Education Background`, `text`, `false`): Provide your education background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
*   **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, `false`): Fill in your contact information, including phone numbers and email addresses.
*   **Emergency Contacts** (`Emergency Contacts`, `text`, `false`): List your emergency contacts, including names, phone numbers, and relationships.
*   **References** (`References`, `text`, `false`): List your references, including names, positions, and contact information.
*   **Background Check** (`Background Check`, `text`, `false`): Please do not fill this field, as it is only for internal use by the police department.
*   **Review and Agreement** (`Review and Agreement`, `select_one`, `true`): Please select the option to agree to the terms and conditions of the police department.
*   **Candidate Details** (`Candidate Details`, `text`, `false`): Fill in your personal details, including name, address, and date of birth.
*   **Police Officer** (`Police Officer`, `select_one`, `false`): Please do not fill this field, as it is only for internal use by the police department.
*   **Police Sergeant** (`Police Sergeant`, `select_one`, `false`): Please do not fill this field, as it is only for internal use by the police department.
*   **Police Chief** (`Police Chief`, `select_one`, `false`): Please do not fill this field, as it is only for internal use by the police department.
