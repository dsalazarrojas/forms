# Camp Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Staff Application Form is designed to collect information from potential camp staff members to support the hiring process. This includes details about the applicant's personal contact information, work experience, and availability, as well as their consent to perform background checks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Applicant Full Name, which will be used for employment records.
2. Enter your Email Address, which will be used for recruitment correspondence.
3. Provide your Primary Phone Number for contact.
4. Select the Position Applied For from the desired role or duties.
5. Enter your Availability Dates or range of dates you are available to work.
6. If applicable, provide a Summary of your Prior Experience, including any relevant work or education experience.
7. If applicable, provide Names and contact details for References.
8. If applicable, list any Certifications or Qualifications, such as First aid or Lifeguard credentials.
9. Confirm your Consent for Background Check by selecting 'True'.
10. Finally, provide any Additional Comments or information for the hiring team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter the email address you want us to use for recruitment correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your primary phone number for contact purposes.
* **Position Applied For** (Text, Required): Choose the role or duties you are applying for.
* **Availability Dates** (Text, Required): Enter the dates or range of dates you are available to work.
* **Prior Experience** (Text, Optional): Provide a summary of your relevant work or education experience.
* **References** (Text, Optional): Enter the names and contact details for your references.
* **Certifications** (Text, Optional): List any certifications or qualifications you hold, such as First aid or Lifeguard credentials.
* **Consent for Background Check** (Select One, Required): Select 'True' to confirm your consent for a background check.
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Optional): Provide any other information for the hiring team.
