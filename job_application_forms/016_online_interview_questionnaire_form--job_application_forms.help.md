# online_interview_questionnaire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals who have applied for a job position through an online interview.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Select your current Job Title from the "Job Title Options" list.
7. Select your Level of experience in the "Years of Experience" field.
8. Choose your availability options in the "Availability Options" field.
9. Provide a brief notice of interview in the "Notice of Interview" field.
10. Upload your Resume in the "Resume" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address in this field. This field is optional but recommended for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Job Title** (select_one, required): Select your current job title from the list of options provided.
* **Job Title Options** (select_multiple, optional): Select any additional job titles that you feel are relevant to your experience.
* **Years of Experience** (number, required): Enter the number of years you have experience in your current or relevant field.
* **Availability** (time, required): Choose your availability options from the list provided.
* **Availability Options** (select_one, required): Select your preferred availability option.
* **Notice of Interview** (note, required): Provide a brief notice of interview in this field.
* **Resume** (text, required): Upload your resume in a supported file format.
