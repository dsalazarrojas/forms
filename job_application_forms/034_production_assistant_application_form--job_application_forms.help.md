# Production Assistant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Production Assistant Application Form is a comprehensive form designed to gather detailed information from applicants for production assistant roles in various industries such as film, television, digital media, and live events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently:

1.  Review the form and its sections to understand the required fields and their corresponding explanations.
2.  Provide clear and concise answers for each section.
3.  Fill out the form in the following sequence: General Information, Contact Info, Resume Uploads, Education and Certifications, Work Experience, Skills and Qualifications, Production Assistant, Work Shift, Available Days, Salary Request, Available Hours, Notice Period, Available Periods, Available Periods 2, and Days Off.
4.  Double-check your submission before hitting submit to ensure you've filled out all necessary fields correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **General Information** (`1`, text, optional): This field is for providing a brief introduction to yourself and your background.
*   **Contact Info** (`2`, text, optional): Enter your contact details such as email address and phone number.
*   **Resume Uploads** (`3`, number, optional): Please upload your resume in a format that we can easily read. We recommend using a PDF or word document.
*   **Education and Certifications** (`4`, text, optional): Describe your educational background and any relevant certifications you hold.
*   **Work Experience** (`5`, number, optional): Share your relevant work experience in production assistant roles or related fields.
*   **Skills and Qualifications** (`6`, text, optional): List any relevant skills or qualifications that you have acquired during your education or work experience.
*   **Production Assistant** (`7`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the available options for the type of production assistant role you're interested in: Film and video production assistant, Television production assistant, Digital media production assistant, Live event production assistant, or Other.
*   **Work Shift** (`8`, select_one, optional): Indicate your preferred work shift, whether it's Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night, or Rotating Shift.
*   **Available Days** (`9`, select_one, optional): Specify the days you're available to work. Select one or multiple options from Monday to Sunday.
*   **Salary Request** (`10`, number, optional): Enter your expected salary range for this role.
*   **Available Hours** (`11`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you can commit to working per week.
*   **Notice Period** (`12`, number, optional): Specify the number of weeks/months you require notice for giving your notice to your current employer.
*   **Available Periods** (`13`, number, optional): Enter the number of weeks/months you can commit to working in a production assistant role.
*   **Available Periods 2** (`14`, number, optional): Enter the number of weeks/months you can commit to working in a production assistant role for this period.
*   **Days Off** (`15`, number, optional): Indicate the number of days you need off for any reason.
*   **Available Periods 3** (`16`, number, optional): This field is intended for entering your available periods in the third instance, but as per the form, it's not used.

Note: This form is intended to gather information about the applicants. It's recommended that you answer these fields in clear and concise manner, ensuring that all sections are filled out accurately and completely. If there are any discrepancies or questions, feel free to contact the hiring team.
