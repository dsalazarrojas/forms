# Biochemist Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from applicants for a biochemist position. It includes personal contact information, academic background, professional experience, and technical skills relevant to the field of biochemistry. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to be considered for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal contact information, including First Name, Last Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
2. Provide your overall GPA and the name of the university where you earned your highest degree.
3. If applicable, describe your professional experience and the name of your current or most recent employer.
4. Select all the lab techniques you have experience with from the Technical Skills field.
5. Write a brief summary of your research interests (max 500 characters).
6. Finally, enter your available start date for the position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Highest Degree Earned**: Select the highest degree you have earned (e.g., Bachelor of Science, Master of Science, Ph.D., Post-Doctoral).
* **University Name**: Enter the name of the university where you earned your highest degree.
* **Overall GPA**: Enter your overall GPA on a 4.0 scale (e.g., 3.8).
* **Years of Experience in Biochemistry**: Enter the number of years you have worked in the field of biochemistry.
* **Current or Most Recent Employer**: Enter the name of your current or most recent employer.
* **Technical Skills - Lab Techniques**: Select all the lab techniques you have experience with (e.g., PCR, Chromatography, Mass Spectrometry, Cell Culture, Protein Purification).
* **Brief Summary of Research Interests**: Write a brief summary of your research interests (max 500 characters).
* **Available Start Date**: Enter the date you are available to start the position.
