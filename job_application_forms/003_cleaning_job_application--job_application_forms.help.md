# Cleaning JOB APPLICATION - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect job application information from potential candidates who are interested in working as cleaning professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out all required fields (marked as **required**).
* Enter your full name and contact email in the corresponding fields.
* Select your preferred position applied for from the dropdown menu.
* Enter your available start date and confirm that you can begin work on that date.
* Select all the days you are available to work.
* If you have cleaning experience, enter the number of years you have been working in the field.
* Confirm that you are able to lift 25 pounds.
* If you have references, provide their names and contact numbers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your best email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your primary phone number for us to reach out to you.
* **Current address** (`address`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your current city and state or region.
* **Position applied for** (`position_applied`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the type of position you are applying for (e.g., Residential cleaner, Commercial cleaner).
* **Available start date** (`available_start_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date you are available to begin work.
* **Preferred working days** (`available_days`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select all the days you are available to work.
* **Years of cleaning experience** (`prior_experience_years`, `number`, **required**): If you have cleaning experience, enter the number of years you have been working in the field.
* **Able to lift 25 pounds** (`able_to_lift`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm that you are able to lift 25 pounds.
* **References** (`references`, `text`, **optional**): Provide names and contact numbers for two references.
