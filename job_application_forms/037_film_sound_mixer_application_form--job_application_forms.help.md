# Film Sound Mixer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for candidates to apply for a film sound mixer position. It requires providing personal contact information and uploading a resume and portfolio to demonstrate experience and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first and last name accurately.
2. Enter your email address in a format that follows [example@domain.com](mailto:example@domain.com).
3. Enter your phone number in the format of +1-123-456-7890 or 123-456-7890.
4. Enter the number of years you have experience as a film sound mixer.
5. If applicable, upload your resume and portfolio to demonstrate your experience and qualifications.
6. Make sure to submit this form only once.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required)
* This field is used to collect your first name, which will be used to address you in communication related to your application.

### **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required)
* This field is used to collect your last name, which will be used to address you in communication related to your application.

### **Email** (`email`, email, required)
* This field requires a valid email address. Please use a format like [example@domain.com](mailto:example@domain.com). We will contact you using this email address.

### **Phone** (`phone`, text, required)
* This field is used to collect your phone number, which will be used for further communication if needed.

### **Years Experience** (`years_experience`, number, required)
* This field is used to collect the number of years you have worked as a film sound mixer.

### **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, text, optional)
* If applicable, upload a link or file to your portfolio that demonstrates your experience and qualifications.

### **Resume** (`resume`, note, optional)
* If applicable, upload a link or file to your resume that demonstrates your experience and qualifications.

### **Tips**
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use a clear and concise format for your resume and portfolio.
* Only submit this form once.
