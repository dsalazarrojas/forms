# job_candidate_written_skills_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The job candidate written skills assessment form is designed to evaluate a job candidate's written skills and communication abilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each question and its purpose.
2. Answer all required fields truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask for clarification or assistance.
