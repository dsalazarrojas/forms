# Covid 19 Pandemic Assistance Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Covid 19 Pandemic Assistance Application Form is designed to gather information from individuals who are seeking assistance related to the pandemic. This form is for individuals who are currently experiencing or have experienced difficulties due to the pandemic and are seeking help.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please follow these steps:
    - Fill in your First Name.
    - Enter your Last Name.
    - Enter your Email Address.
    - Enter your Phone Number.
    - Enter your Address.
    - Enter your Date of Birth.
    - Select your Employment Status.
    - Select your Employment Type.
    - Enter your Work Hours.
    - Enter your Job Title.
    - Enter your Job Description.
    - Enter your Work Experience (if applicable).
    - Enter any additional information about your emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your employment status.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your employment type.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, `number`, required): Enter your work hours.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `note`, required): Enter your job description.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `date`, optional): Enter your work experience (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact first name.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact last name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (`emergency_contact_relation`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact relation.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact address.
* **Job Reference Number** (`job_reference_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the job reference number.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select your job status.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your job type.
* **Work Hours Per Week** (`work_hours_per_week`, `number`, optional): Enter your work hours per week.
* **Job Title Other** (`job_title_other`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title (if different from previous field).
* **Job Description Other** (`job_description_other`, `text`, optional): Enter your job description (if different from previous field).
* **Work Experience Other** (`work_experience_other`, `text`, optional): Enter your work experience (if different from previous fields).
* **Emergency Contact Note** (`emergency_contact_note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional emergency contact information.
