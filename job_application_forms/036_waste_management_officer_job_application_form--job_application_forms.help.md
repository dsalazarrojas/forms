# Waste Management Officer Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from potential applicants for the Waste Management Officer position.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field, following the standard email format (e.g., [yourname@example.com](mailto:yourname@example.com).
3. Provide your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Describe your relevant Experience in the Experience field.
5. Select your relevant Skills from the dropdown menu.
6. Describe your Education in the Education field.
7. Check the certifications you possess from the dropdown menu in the Certifications field.
8. Upload your Resume in the Resume field, in a suitable format (e.g., PDF, Word, or text file).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Please enter a valid email address that can be used for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Please enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, optional): Please describe your relevant work experience, including job titles, companies, and dates of employment.
* **Skills** (`skills`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the skills that you have, such as Data analysis, Data collection, etc.
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, optional): Please describe your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check all the certifications you possess, such as OSHA, OSHA 30-hour, etc.
* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, optional): Upload your Resume in a suitable format (e.g., PDF, Word, or text file).

## Tips

* Please provide detailed and accurate information for each field to increase your chances of being considered for the position.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents, please upload them along with your Resume.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* All fields are optional, but providing detailed and accurate information will improve the chances of being considered for the position.
