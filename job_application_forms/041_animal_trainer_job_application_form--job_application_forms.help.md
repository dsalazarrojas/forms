# Animal Trainer Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your personal details, experience, and qualifications for the Animal Trainer position. It will help us assess your fit for the role and guide you through the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
3. Use the provided instructions for each field if needed.
4. Submit your completed form to initiate the application process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_name`, text, \*): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, \*): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, \*): Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, \*): Enter your current street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, \*): Enter the name of your city or town.
* **State or Province** (`state`, text, \*): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, text, \*): Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_applied`, select\_one, \*): Select the position you are applying for from the list provided.
* **Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, date, \*): Enter the date you are available to start work.
* **Desired Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select\_one, \*): Select your desired employment type from the list provided.
* **Salary Expectations** (`salary_expectations`, text, optional): Enter your expected salary or hourly rate.
* **Years of Animal Training Experience** (`years_experience`, number, \*): Enter the number of years you have worked with animals.
* **Which Species Have You Worked With** (`species_experience`, select\_multiple, \*): Select all the species you have experience working with.
* **Training Methods You Use** (`training_methods`, select\_multiple, \*): Select all the training methods you use.
* **Professional Certifications** (`certifications`, text, \*): List any professional certifications relevant to your work.
* **Education Background** (`education`, text, \*): Describe your educational background, including degrees and institutions.
* **Relevant Work History** (`work_history`, text, \*): Describe your last three work positions, including dates.
* **Areas of Specialization** (`specializations`, select\_multiple, \*): Select all the areas of specialization you have experience with.
* **Problem Behaviors You Can Address** (`problem_behaviors`, select\_multiple, \*): Select all the problem behaviors you can address.
* **Group Training Experience** (`group_training`, select\_one, \*): Indicate your level of experience with group training classes.
* **Can You Meet Physical Requirements?** (`physical_requirements`, select\_one, \*): Indicate your ability to meet the physical requirements of the job.
* **Schedule Flexibility** (`schedule_flexibility`, select\_one, \*): Select your level of schedule flexibility.
* **Weekend Availability** (`weekend_availability`, select\_one, \*): Indicate your availability on weekends.
* **Professional References** (`references`, text, \*): Provide three references with their contact information.
* **Why Are You Interested in This Position?** (`why_interested`, text, \*): Share your motivation for applying for this position.
* **Describe Your Training Philosophy** (`philosophy_statement`, text, \*): Write a brief statement about your training philosophy.
* **Are You Willing to Complete a Background Check?** (`background_check`, select\_one, \*): Indicate your willingness to complete a background check.
* **Have You Completed a Drug Screening?** (`drug_screening`, select\_one, optional): Indicate if you have completed a drug screening. 
* **Do You Have a Portfolio or Videos of Your Work?** (`portfolio_available`, select\_one, optional): Indicate if you have a portfolio or videos of your work.
* **Portfolio or Video Link** (`portfolio_link`, text, optional): If you have a portfolio or videos, provide the link.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your application.
* **I Certify All Information Is Accurate** (`certification_accuracy`, select\_one, \*): Indicate that you certify all information is accurate.
* **Electronic Signature** (`applicant_signature`, text, \*): Type your full name as an electronic signature.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, \*): Enter the date you submitted your application.

Note: Fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are required.
