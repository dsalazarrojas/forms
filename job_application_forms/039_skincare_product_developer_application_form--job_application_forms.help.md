# Skincare Product Developer Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Skincare Product Developer Application Form is designed for candidates to apply for a position as a Skincare Product Developer. This form will help us assess your qualifications and suitability for the role. Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email in the required fields.
2. Fill out the Job Title field with your current or desired job title.
3. Enter your Contact Number (if applicable) and your relevant Experience in the corresponding fields.
4. Choose your available date and time for an interview or meeting.
5. Select your relevant Skills from the provided options.
6. Upload your Resume, Portfolio, and any relevant References.
7. If not applicable, please leave the other fields empty.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Job Title** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Contact Number** (`5`, `text`, optional): Enter a valid phone number (if you have one).
* **Experience** (`6`, `text`, required): Describe your relevant experience in the field of skincare product development.
* **Education** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant education or certifications.
* **Availability** (`8`, `date`, required): Choose a date and time when you are available for an interview or meeting.
* **Skills** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more skills that you possess from the provided options.
* **Resume** (`10`, `text`, optional): Upload your resume or a brief summary of your qualifications.
* **Portfolio** (`11`, `text`, optional): Upload your portfolio or a link to your website.
* **References** (`12`, `text`, optional): Provide the names and contacts of your references.
* **Job Description** (`13`, `note`, optional): Briefly describe the job you are applying for.
* **Work Experience** (`14`, `date`, optional): Enter your relevant work experience.
* **Available Date** (`15`, `date`, optional): Enter your available date (if different from the availability date).
* **Salary** (`16`, `number`, optional): Enter your expected salary (if applicable).
* **Job Type** (`17`, `select_one`, required): Choose your job type from the provided options.
* **Job Type Other** (`18`, `text`, optional): Enter any other job type you possess.
* **Notice Period** (`19`, `select_one`, required): Choose your notice period from the provided options.
* **Notice Period Other** (`20`, `text`, optional): Enter any other notice period you possess.
* **Job Start Date** (`21`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you would like to start your new job.
* **Job End Date** (`22`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you would like to end your current job.
* **Time Zone** (`23`, `time`, optional): Enter your time zone (if applicable).
* **Time Zone** (`24`, `text`, optional): Enter any other time zone you possess.
* **Submit** (`25`, `text`, optional): This is a placeholder field, please do not fill it out.

Please note that some fields are optional, but please fill out all required fields to ensure a complete application. If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
