# Food Quality Control Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from job applicants for food quality control positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure you have a strong internet connection to avoid losing your progress.
2. Review each section carefully and fill out the fields as prompted.
3. For each field, provide accurate and relevant information.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Application Form** (`job_application_form`, text, required/optional): This section is the initial job application form where you can provide your job title, job description, and job location.
* **Employment Application Form** (`job_application_form_2`, text, required/optional): This section is another job application form where you can provide additional information about your employment status and job title.
* **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`, text, required/optional): This section is where you can provide your candidate details, such as your name, contact information, and work experience.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): This section is where you can provide your contact details, including your phone number and email address.
* **Employment Details** (`employment_details`, text, required/optional): This section is where you can provide information about your employment history and job preferences.
* **Job Preferences** (`job_preferences`, text, required/optional): This section is where you can specify your job preferences, such as job status and job type.
* **Submit and Save** (`submit_and_save`, select_one, required/optional): This section is where you can confirm that your application is complete and accurate. Please select "Yes" to submit and save your application.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): This section is where you can specify your job status, either active or inactive.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_one, required/optional): This section is where you can specify your job title.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required/optional): This section is where you can provide a brief description of your job.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required/optional): This section is where you can specify the company you are applying to.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required/optional): This section is where you can provide your work experience.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, required/optional): This section is where you can provide your education background.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, required/optional): This section is where you can specify the job location.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, required/optional): This section is where you can specify the job type.
* **Job Status 2** (`job_status_2`, select_multiple, required/optional): This section is similar to job status, where you can specify your job status as active or inactive.
* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, select_one, required/optional): This section is similar to job title, where you can specify your job title.
* **Job Description 2** (`job_description_2`, text, required/optional): This section is similar to job description, where you can provide a brief description of your job.
* **Company 2** (`company_2`, text, required/optional): This section is similar to company, where you can specify the company you are applying to.
* **Work Experience 2** (`work_experience_2`, text, required/optional): This section is similar to work experience, where you can provide your work experience.
* **Education Background 2** (`education_background_2`, text, required/optional): This section is similar to education background, where you can provide your education background.
