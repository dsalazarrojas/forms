# Entertainment Event Planner Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for candidates applying for an Entertainment Event Planner position. It collects information about your background, experience, and qualifications to help us evaluate your suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
2. Upload your resume, portfolio, and any relevant licenses or certificates as attachments.
3. Double-check your submissions for spelling and formatting errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Application** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): This is a free-text field where you can introduce yourself and express your interest in the position.
* **Contact Info** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Event Planning Experience** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Share your experience in event planning, including relevant roles, responsibilities, and achievements.
* **Education** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your academic background and any relevant courses or degrees you've completed.
* **Skills** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): List your relevant skills, including technical, creative, or administrative skills you think are essential for the role.
* **Availability** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Indicate your availability for the role, including your start date, end date, or any flexible work arrangements.
* **Work Experience** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Share your previous work experience relevant to the role, including job titles, company names, and achievements.
* **Personal Interest** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your personal interests or hobbies that may be relevant to the role or company culture.
* **Upload Resume** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Attach a copy of your resume to support your application.
* **Upload Portfolio** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Share your portfolio, which can include relevant work examples, images, or videos that showcase your experience.
* **Upload License** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): If applicable, attach any relevant licenses or certifications that demonstrate your expertise.
