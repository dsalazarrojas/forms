# Loan Processor Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loan Processor Job Application Form is designed to collect information about loan processors who want to apply for a position at your institution. This form is a crucial part of the hiring process and helps us get to know you better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the required fields.
2. Enter your valid Email address and Phone Number.
3. If applicable, provide your Date Of Birth and Address.
4. Check the Employment Type field to confirm your employment status.
5. Select your Employment Start Date and Employment End Date if applicable.
6. Describe your Work Experience in the text field.
7. Confirm your Education level.
8. Enter your current Job Title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number with the area code (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Date Of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter your permanent address where you reside.
* **Employment Type** (select_multiple, optional): Choose your current employment status:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Employment Start Date** (date, optional): Enter your employment start date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employment End Date** (date, optional): Enter your employment end date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Work Experience** (text, optional): Describe your previous work experience, including job title, company, and duration.
* **Education** (select_multiple, optional): Confirm your education level:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Job Title** (text, optional): Enter your current job title.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* Double-check your entered information for accuracy.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us for assistance.
