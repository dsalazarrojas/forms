# Communications Strategy And Operations Officer Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from applicants for the Communications Strategy And Operations Officer position. It is designed to help our team assess your qualifications, experience, and fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what each field requires.
2. Gather all necessary information and attachments before proceeding.
3. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
4. Select relevant options for the select fields.
5. Attach any required documents, such as your resume and cover letter.
6. Review your application for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Information** (`application_information`, `note`, required: false): 
This field is a placeholder for any additional information about your application. Please do not fill this out unless explicitly required.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, `date`, required: true): 
Please enter the date you submitted this application.
* **Position ID** (`position_id`, `text`, required: false): 
If you have a job reference number, please enter it here. If not, you can leave it blank.
* **Personal Information** (`personal_information`, `note`, required: false): 
This field is a placeholder for your contact details. Please fill in your full legal name, address, and other contact information in the subsequent fields.
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required: true): 
Please enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: true): 
Enter your professional email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: true): 
Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: true): 
Enter your city and state or province where you reside or work.
* **LinkedIn Profile** (`linkedin_profile`, `text`, required: false): 
Please enter the URL to your professional LinkedIn profile.
* **Portfolio Website** (`portfolio_website`, `text`, required: false): 
Please enter the URL to your portfolio website if you have one.
* **Education** (`education`, `note`, required: false): 
Please provide a brief description of your educational background.
* **Highest Degree** (`highest_degree`, `select_one`, required: true): 
Select the highest level of education you have completed.
* **Field of Study** (`field_of_study`, `text`, required: true): 
Enter your major or field of study.
* **University Name** (`university_name`, `text`, required: true): 
Enter the name of your university or institution.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, `number`, required: false): 
If you have graduated from university, enter the year of graduation.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `note`, required: false): 
This field is a placeholder for your professional experience. Please describe your experience in the subsequent field.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, `select_one`, required: true): 
Select the total years of relevant experience you have.
* **Current Employer** (`current_employer`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter the name of your current or last employer.
* **Current Title** (`current_title`, `text`, required: true): 
Enter your current or last job title.
* **Relevant Skills** (`relevant_skills`, `select_multiple`, required: true): 
Select all relevant skills you possess that are mentioned in the job description.
* **Key Accomplishments** (`key_accomplishments`, `text`, required: true): 
Describe your key accomplishments and achievements in the field of communications strategy and operations.
* **Why Interested** (`why_interested`, `text`, required: true): 
Explain why you are interested in this position and the company.
* **Salary Expectations** (`salary_expectations`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter your expected salary range in USD. If you are open to negotiations, please indicate so.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_one`, required: true): 
Select when you can start work.
* **Work Authorization** (`work_authorization`, `select_one`, required: true): 
Select your work authorization status.
* **References Available** (`references_available`, `select_one`, required: true): 
Indicate if you have professional references available.
* **Resume Attached** (`resume_attached`, `select_one`, required: true): 
Indicate if you have attached your resume.
* **Cover Letter Attached** (`cover_letter_attached`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Indicate if you have attached a cover letter.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): 
If you have any other information to share, please include it here.
* **Application Certification** (`application_certification`, `select_one`, required: true): 
Certify that all information provided is accurate.
