# Editor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Editor Application Form is a tool designed for potential editors to submit their applications for open editor roles within our company. This form is used to collect essential information from applicants to evaluate their qualifications and suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the instructions to understand what each field is asking for.
2.  Fill in the required fields with accurate information, as each field is marked as required or optional.
3.  Make sure to submit your application once you have completed all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, required): This is the main application form where you can provide a brief introduction about yourself, including your background, experience, and why you are interested in the editor role.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): In this section, please provide your name, email address, and phone number so that our team can contact you for further information.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required): Attach your resume as a PDF or Word document, which should include your work experience, qualifications, and relevant skills.
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, text, required): Share your portfolio by attaching relevant work samples or links to your online portfolio website, which demonstrates your writing and editing skills.
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, required): In this section, please provide a brief description of your work experience related to writing, editing, or content creation.
* **Education** (`education`, text, required): List your academic qualifications and relevant coursework, including the institution name and dates of attendance.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required): Describe your relevant work experience, including job titles, company names, and durations.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select multiple, optional): Select the skills you have, from the list of options provided, that are relevant to the editor role.
* **Language Proficiency** (`language_proficiency`, text, optional): If you have proficiency in languages other than the default language, please mention it here.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, optional): Indicate your availability to start work, including the date you can start and any other relevant information.

Note: Ensure that each field is filled in accurately and truthfully to increase the chances of your application being considered.
