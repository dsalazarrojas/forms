# Energy Industry Analyst Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us find suitable candidates for the Energy Industry Analyst role. The Energy Industry Analyst Job Application form gathers necessary information from applicants to assess their qualifications, skills, and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Please fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2.  Select your relevant experience and skills from the dropdown menus.
3.  Upload your resume, if applicable.
4.  Provide any additional information you'd like to share.
5.  Click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <span class="required">Experience</span>
*   Experience: Please describe your previous work experience related to the energy industry. This could include internships, part-time jobs, or full-time positions. (\*) 

### <span class="required">Skills</span>
*   Skills: Select the skills you have acquired that align with the job requirements. This could include Data Analysis, Energy Management, Financial Analysis, or Data Visualization. (\*) 

### <span class="required">Resume Upload</span>
*   Resume Upload: Attach a copy of your resume, if applicable. This will help us get a better understanding of your work history and experience. (\*)  

### <span class="required">Additional Information</span>
*   Additional Information: If there's anything else you'd like to share with us, please include it here. 

### <span class="required">Submit</span>
*   Submit: Review and submit your application. This is the final step to complete your application. (\*) 

Note: Please fill out all required fields before submitting your application. If you have any doubts or questions, don't hesitate to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
