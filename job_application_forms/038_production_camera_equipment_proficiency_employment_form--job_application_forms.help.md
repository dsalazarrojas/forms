# Production Camera Equipment Proficiency Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Production Camera Equipment Proficiency Employment Form is designed to collect personal and professional information from job applicants to assess their suitability for a position that requires proficiency in camera equipment operation. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, ensure you have all the necessary contact information readily available.
2. Fill in your personal details, including name, email, phone number, and address.
3. Select your years of experience in using camera equipment.
4. Choose your proficiency level in camera equipment operation (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced).
5. Indicate your proficiency in specific camera equipment types (Camera 1, Camera 2, Camera 3).
6. Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
7. Describe your work experience and any relevant references.
8. Confirm whether you are willing to work on-location or not.
9. Complete your personal details (First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Height, Weight, Eye Colour, Hair Colour, Ethnicity, and Gender).
10. Finally, add any additional notes about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: Provide your name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it should be spelled.
* **Email**: Enter your work email address or any other contact email.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number to enable communication for work purposes.
* **Years of Experience**: Specify the number of years you have been using camera equipment.
* **Proficiency Level**: Select your level of proficiency in camera equipment operation (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Equipment Proficiency**: Indicate which camera equipment types you are proficient in.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Work Experience**: Describe your previous work experience with camera equipment.
* **References**: Provide references for your work experience (if applicable).
* **On-Location Work**: Confirm whether you are willing to work on-location or not.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Address**: Enter your home address.
* **City**: Enter your city of residence.
* **State**: Enter your state or region.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip or post code.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Height**: Enter your height in centimeters or feet/inches.
* **Weight**: Enter your weight in kilograms or pounds.
* **Eye Colour**: Enter the color of your eyes (e.g., Blue, Brown, Green).
* **Hair Colour**: Enter the color of your hair (e.g., Black, Blonde, Brown).
* **Ethnicity**: Enter your ethnicity (e.g., Asian, Caucasian, African).
* **Gender**: Select your gender (Male, Female, Other).
* **Notes**: Add any additional information you'd like to share about your application.
