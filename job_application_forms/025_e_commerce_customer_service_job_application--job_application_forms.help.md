# e_commerce_customer_service_job_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from candidates applying for customer service roles in e-commerce. It helps employers or hiring managers to quickly assess and evaluate candidate applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Job Title** field with your current or desired job title.
2. Describe your **Job Description** in the space provided.
3. Select the current **Job Status** of your job (Active or Inactive).
4. Choose your **Salary Range** by selecting one of the provided options.
5. Choose your **Education Level** by selecting one or multiple options from the list.
6. Enter your **Work Experience** in the space provided.
7. Select your **Availability** date if you are ready to start immediately.
8. Enter your **Notice Period** if you are currently employed and not immediately available.
9. Fill in your **Contact Info** details.
10. Enter your **Email** address.
11. Upload your **Resume** file.
12. Upload your **Cover Letter** file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title**: Enter your current or desired job title, e.g., Customer Service Representative, Customer Support Specialist, etc.
* **Job Description**: Provide a brief description of your job responsibilities and duties.
* **Job Status**: Select Active if you are currently employed or available for hire; select Inactive if you are not available due to a job change or career break.
* **Salary Range**: Choose the salary range that best reflects your current or desired salary.
* **Education Level**: Select one or multiple options that match your educational attainment (High School, Bachelor's, or Master's Degree).
* **Work Experience**: Enter a brief description of your work experience, including job titles, company names, and relevant job duties.
* **Availability**: Select a date if you are immediately available to start work.
* **Notice Period**: Enter the number of days or weeks you require to give notice if you are currently employed and not immediately available.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your name, phone number, and physical address.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Upload Resume**: Attach your resume file to your application.
* **Upload Cover Letter**: Attach your cover letter file.

## Tips
* Make sure to upload relevant and up-to-date versions of your resume and cover letter.
* Be concise and clear in your job description and work experience.
* Use standard email address formats when entering your email.
* Use a file format compatible with our system for resume and cover letter uploads.
