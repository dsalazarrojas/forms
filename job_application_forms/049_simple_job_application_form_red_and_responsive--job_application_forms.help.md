# simple_job_application_form_red_and_responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose

This simple job application form is designed to collect necessary information from job applicants. The form is divided into several pages, each with a specific set of fields to gather relevant details about the applicant. The form requires some fields to be filled out to ensure that the applicant's information is complete and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the form.
2. Fill out the fields on each page, following the instructions provided for each field.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Position**: Select the job you are applying for from the given options.
* **Job Position** (Select Multiple): Select all the job positions you are interested in applying for.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **File**: Enter a reference or a relevant file (e.g., PDF, DOCX).
* **Resume**: Upload your resume in a supported format (e.g., PDF, DOCX, TXT).
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Job Title**: Enter a relevant job title or profession.
* **Location**: Enter your location or the location where you are applying for the job.
* **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of your job experience or relevant job description.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the given options.
* **Job Type**: Select the type of job you are applying for (e.g., Full-time, Part-time).
* **Skills**: Select all the skills you possess from the given options.
* **Work Experience**: Enter the date of your most recent work experience (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Education**: Select whether you have completed education or not.
* **Highest Degree**: Select whether you have completed the highest degree or not.
* **Highest Grade Point Average (GPA)**: Enter your highest GPA if applicable.
