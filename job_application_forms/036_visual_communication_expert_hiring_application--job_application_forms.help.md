# visual_communication_expert_hiring_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for job applicants to provide their contact details, portfolio, and other relevant information as part of the hiring process for a visual communication expert position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details on page 1.
2. Select your portfolio platforms on page 2.
3. Choose your skills on page 3.
4. Add any additional portfolio links on page 4.
5. (Optional) Add any work experience and portfolio links.
6. Upload your resume and references.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (1, `contact_details`, `text`, not required): 
Please fill in your name, phone number, email address, and other relevant contact information.

### Select Portfolio Platforms
* **What is your portfolio** (2, `portfolio`, `select_multiple`, not required): 
Select the platforms where you host your portfolio, such as your website, Behance, Dribbble, or Instagram.

### Skills
* **Skills** (5, `skills`, `select_multiple`, required): 
Choose the skills you have, such as Visual Design, UI/UX Design, Web Design, Graphic Design, or Illlustration.

### Portfolio Links
* **Portfolio Links** (6, `portfolio_links`, `select_multiple`, required): 
Select the platforms where you host your portfolio, such as your website, Behance, Dribbble, or Instagram.

### Work Experience (Optional)
* **Work Experience** (3, `work_experience`, `note`, not required): 
This is a field for you to describe your work experience. You can add any relevant details about your professional experience.

### Education Background (Optional)
* **Education Background** (4, `education_background`, `text`, not required): 
Please add any relevant information about your education background.

### Portfolio Work (Optional)
* **Portfolio Work** (7, `portfolio_work`, `time`, not required): 
This field is for you to add the time spent on your portfolio work.

### Resume
* **Resume** (8, `resume`, `text`, not required): 
Upload your resume, if you have one.

### References
* **References** (9, `references`, `text`, not required): 
Add any relevant references for this application.
