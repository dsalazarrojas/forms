# New Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Job Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants for a job opening. Please fill out the form accurately to provide your contact and personal details, work experience, and job preferences to help us determine if you're a suitable candidate for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form in sections, following the guidelines below.
- Ensure all required fields are completed.
- If a field is marked as optional, you can leave it blank if you don't have the information.
- Review your submission before submitting it to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title or role you're applying for.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address associated with your job application.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): Upload your resume or CV in PDF or Word format.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current job status (Active or Inactive).
* **Job Status Options** (`job_status_options`, select_multiple, optional): Select the job statuses that apply to you (Active, Inactive, or other options).
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, date, optional): Enter your work experience in years and months.
* **Expected Start Date** (`expected_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the expected start date for the job.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of job you're applying for.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your work experience.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_one, required): Select the job category that best describes the job you're applying for (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Job Category Options** (`job_category_options`, select_multiple, required): Select the job category options that apply to you (Yes or No).
* **Job Type Options** (`job_type_options`, select_multiple, optional): Select the job type options that apply to you (Yes or No).
* **Job Location Options** (`job_location_options`, select_multiple, optional): Select the job location options that apply to you (Yes or No).
* **Job Status Options 2** (`job_status_options_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select the job status options that apply to you (Active or Inactive).
* **Work Experience 2** (`work_experience_2`, time, optional): Enter your work experience in hours and minutes.
* **Expected Start Date 2** (`expected_start_date_2`, time, optional): Enter the expected start time for the job.
* **Job Type 2** (`job_type_2`, text, optional): Enter the type of job you're applying for.
* **Work Location 2** (`work_location_2`, text, optional): Enter the location of your work experience.
* **Job Category 2** (`job_category_2`, select_one, optional): Select the job category that best describes the job you're applying for (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Job Category Options 2** (`job_category_options_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select the job category options that apply to you (Yes or No).
* **Job Type Options 2** (`job_type_options_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select the job type options that apply to you (Yes or No).
* **Job Location Options 2** (`job_location_options_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select the job location options that apply to you (Yes or No).

Tips:
- Double-check your submission before sending it in.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, contact us.
- Ensure all required fields are completed to increase your chances of being considered for the position.
