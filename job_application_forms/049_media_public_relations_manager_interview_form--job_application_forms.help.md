# Media Public Relations Manager Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Media Public Relations Manager Interview Form is designed to collect information from candidates applying for PR positions. This form helps us understand the candidate's background, experience, and qualifications to evaluate their fit for the position.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the Job Application Form with your basic details.
2.  Provide written responses to any questions asked during the interview.
3.  Complete the PR Candidate Details form with your personal information.
4.  Select your job title, level, and function from the relevant options.
5.  Choose your job location (on-site or remote).
6.  Enter your years of service and service date (if applicable).
7.  Select your job status (active or inactive).
8.  Select your job status for this specific position (active, inactive, or on leave).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Application Form** (`job_application_form`, text, optional): This section is for providing a brief introduction about yourself.
* **Written Interview Responses** (`written_interview_responses`, text, optional): This section is for providing answers to any questions asked during the interview.
* **PR Candidate Details** (`pr_candidate_details`, text, optional): This section is for sharing personal details about yourself.
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, optional): This field is to enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is for entering your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is for entering your phone number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): This field is for entering the title of the position you're applying for.
* **Job Title Description** (`job_title_description`, text, optional): This field is for entering a brief description of the job title.
* **Job Title Level** (`job_title_level`, select_one, optional): This field is for selecting your job title level (Senior, Junior, or Lead).
* **Job Title Level 2** (`job_title_level_2`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for selecting multiple job title levels (Senior, Lead, or Assistant).
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, select_one, optional): This field is for selecting your job function (Media Relations, Public Relations, or Communications).
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, select_one, optional): This field is for selecting your job location (on-site or remote).
* **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, number, optional): This field is for entering the number of years you've worked in the industry.
* **Years Of Service 2** (`years_of_service_2`, date, optional): This field is for entering the date of when you started your service.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): This field is for entering the start date of your service.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): This field is for entering the end date of your service.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): This field is for selecting your job status (Active or Inactive).
* **Job Status 2** (`job_status_2`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for selecting multiple job status (Active, Inactive, or On Leave).
* **Job Status 3** (`job_status_3`, select_one, optional): This field is for selecting your job status (Active or Other).
