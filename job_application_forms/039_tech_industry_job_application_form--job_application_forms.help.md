# Tech Industry Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from applicants for job openings in the tech industry. Please fill out this form to submit your application. Ensure you answer accurately, as this information will be reviewed for eligibility and consideration for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

* Fill in the required fields, denoted by `**Required**`
* Select your technical skills and experience from the dropdown menus
* Make sure to enter valid email and date formats (e.g. mm/dd/yy)
* If you have attachments or links to share, please include them in the "Comments" field

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address. This will be used to contact you regarding your application.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number
* **Technical Skills**: Select all relevant technical skills you have, such as JavaScript, HTML/CSS, Python, or C++
* **Experience**: Indicate how much experience you have in the tech industry, such as "Less than 1 year" or "More than 1 year" (Note: "Not sure" is an option if you're unsure)
* **Resume**: Attach or paste a link to your resume or CV
* **Portfolio**: Attach or paste a link to your portfolio or relevant work samples
* **Additional Notes**: Share any additional information about your application or qualifications
* **Date Available**: Indicate when you can start working, using the mm/dd/yy format
* **Start Date**: Indicate the start date of your previous job or internship, using the mm/dd/yy format
* **End Date**: Indicate the end date of your previous job or internship, using the mm/dd/yy format
* **Time Available**: Indicate the amount of time you have available to work per week, using the time format (e.g. 20 hours/week)
* **Salary Expectations**: Indicate your expected salary, using a numerical value
* **Availability**: Indicate whether you are available to work, selecting "Available" or "Not Available"
* **Comments**: Attach any additional comments or files related to your application

## Tips

* Make sure to proofread your application before submitting to ensure accuracy
* Be honest and accurate in your application
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us
* This form will be reviewed and processed by our team
