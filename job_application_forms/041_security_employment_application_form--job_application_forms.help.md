## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, Text, optional): Your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, optional): The email address you want us to contact you with if we need more information or have questions about your application.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, optional): Your contact phone number where we can reach you.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, optional): Briefly describe the job title you are applying for.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, Text, optional): Briefly describe your job and its responsibilities.
* **Experience** (`experience`, Select Multiple, optional): Select the number of years of experience you have in your field (Less than 1 year, 1-5 years, 5+ years).
* **Education** (`education`, Select One, optional): Select the highest level of education you have completed (High School Diploma, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree, Other).
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, Text, optional): Briefly describe your work experience relevant to the job you are applying for.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, Select One, optional): Select your current employment status (Unemployed, Employed, Retired, Other).
