# South Jersey Babysitter Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The South Jersey Babysitter Application Form is used to collect information from potential applicants for babysitting positions in South Jersey. The form is designed to gather essential details about the applicant, including their availability, experience, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Name" field.
2. Select your available days of work in the "Availability" field.
3. Describe your previous work experience in the "Experience" field.
4. List your degrees earned in the "Education" field (optional).
5. Check all applicable special skills you possess in the "Special Skills" field.
6. List your certifications (if any) in the "Certifications" field (optional).
7. Enter the earliest date you are available to start in the "Earliest Start Date" field.
8. Enter the latest date you are available to work in the "Latest End Date" field.
9. Enter emergency contact information, including name, relationship, phone number (optional), and email (optional).
10. Describe your relationship to the emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Name
* **Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.

### 2. Availability
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, required): Select all the days you are available to work.

### 3. Experience
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, required): Describe your previous work experience as a babysitter or a similar role.

### 4. Education (optional)
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): List your degrees earned (e.g., High School, College, etc.).

### 5. Special Skills
* **Special Skills** (`special_skills`, select_multiple, required): Check all the special skills you possess, such as CPR Certification, First Aid Certification, or Swimming Lessons.

### 6. Certifications (optional)
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, text, optional): List any certifications you have, such as CPR or First Aid.

### 7. Earliest Start Date
* **Earliest Start Date** (`earliest_start_date`, date, required): Enter the earliest date you are available to start.

### 8. Latest End Date
* **Latest End Date** (`latest_end_date`, date, required): Enter the latest date you are available to work.

### 9. Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.

### 10. Emergency Contact Relationship
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship to the emergency contact person.

### 11. Emergency Contact Phone (optional)
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact phone number.

### 12. Emergency Contact Email (optional)
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the emergency contact email.

### 13. Emergency Contact Relationship Other (optional)
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other** (`emergency_contact_relationship_other`, text, optional): Describe your relationship to the emergency contact person (if not listed above).

### 14. Emergency Contact Relationship Other Phone (optional)
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other Phone** (`emergency_contact_relationship_other_phone`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact phone number (if not listed above).

### 15. Emergency Contact Relationship Other Email (optional)
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other Email** (`emergency_contact_relationship_other_email`, email, optional): Enter the emergency contact email (if not listed above).

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Ensure that you are applying for a position that suits your qualifications and experience.
