# Software QA Tester Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic information from individuals interested in exploring opportunities for employment as Software QA Testers. By filling out this form, you will be providing us with a starting point for further evaluation and discussion about potential job opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

* Read the form carefully and ensure you have a clear understanding of each field.
* Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If a field does not apply to you, simply select "N/A" or do not fill it out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required: false): This is the field to enter your full name, including first and last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): This field is for your contact email address. Please ensure it is accurate and active.
* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, required: false): Uploading a resume is optional, but recommended. If you have a relevant resume, please attach it to your application.
* **Relevant Skills** (`relevant_skills`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all relevant skills you possess that apply to the role of a Software QA Tester.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if you have work experience as a Software QA Tester. Please select "Yes" if you have prior experience.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: false): This field is for entering your current location. If you are open to relocation, simply indicate that.
* **Education** (`education`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate if you have a degree or relevant education in a field related to software testing. Select "Yes" if you have completed this step.
* **Salary Expectations** (`salary`, `number`, required: false): This field is not required, but please estimate your current salary expectations or what you are open to. If you select "N/A", we will discuss this topic with you during further evaluation.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select your availability for work and any other relevant factors that may impact your employment.

# Tips
* Answer honestly and thoroughly, as this information will be used to initiate further discussions and potential opportunities.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us directly.
* Completing this form is just the first step in the employment process. We will be in touch with you after reviewing your submission.
