# Tanker Driver Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for job applicants to submit their application for a tanker driver position. It collects required and optional information to assess their qualifications and fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the first two fields.
2. Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
3. Fill in your Phone Number for easy contact.
4. If you have a driver's license, enter its number.
5. Provide your Date of Birth for age verification.
6. If you have experience as a tanker driver, specify it.
7. Select when you can start working.
8. Choose the days you are available to work.
9. If you have a job title, enter it. If not, specify it in the "Job Title Other" field.
10. Fill in any additional work experience you have.
11. Choose the type of tanker you have experience with.
12. Enter the size of the tanker you have experience with.
13. If you have a tanker driver's license, provide its type and details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Driver License Number** (`driver_license_number`, text, required for those with a license): If you have a driver's license, enter its number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth for age verification.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, optional): If you have experience as a tanker driver, specify it.
* **Available to Start** (`available_to_start`, select_one, optional): Choose when you can start working.
* **Available to Work** (`available_to_work`, select_multiple, optional): Choose which days you are available to work.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): If you have a job title, enter it. If not, specify it in the "Job Title Other" field.
* **Job Title Other** (`job_title_other`, text, optional): If you have a job title that is not in the list, specify it here.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Fill in any additional work experience you have.
* **Tanker Type** (`tanker_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of tanker you have experience with.
* **Tanker Size** (`tanker_size`, text, optional): Enter the size of the tanker you have experience with.
* **Tanker Driver License Type** (`tanker_driver_license_type`, text, optional): If you have a tanker driver's license, specify its type.
* **Tanker Driver License Number** (`tanker_driver_license_number`, text, optional): If you have a tanker driver's license, enter its number.
* **Tanker Driver License State** (`tanker_driver_license_state`, text, optional): If you have a tanker driver's license, specify its state.
* **Tanker Driver License Issue Date** (`tanker_driver_license_issue_date`, date, optional): If you have a tanker driver's license, enter its issue date.
* **Tanker Driver License Expiration Date** (`tanker_driver_license_expiration_date`, date, optional): If you have a tanker driver's license, enter its expiration date.
* **Tanker Driver License Type** (`tanker_driver_license_cdl_type`, select_one, optional): If you have a tanker driver's license, specify its type.
* **Tanker Driver License Restrictions** (`tanker_driver_license_restrictions`, text, optional): If you have a tanker driver's license, specify any restrictions.
* **Tanker Driver License Suspension** (`tanker_driver_license_suspension`, text, optional): If you have a tanker driver's license, specify any suspension.
* **Tanker Vehicle License State** (`tanker_vehicle_license_state`, text, optional): If you have a tanker vehicle's license, specify its state.
* **Tanker Vehicle License Number** (`tanker_vehicle_license_number`, text, optional): If you have a tanker vehicle's license, enter its number.
* **Tanker Vehicle License Type** (`tanker_vehicle_license_type`, text, optional): If you have a tanker vehicle's license, specify its type.

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling in your details to ensure a smooth application process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
* We appreciate your interest in the tanker driver position and look forward to reviewing your application!
