# Medical Advisor Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from candidates interested in applying for a job as a Medical Advisor.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review all the fields provided and ensure you have all the required information before submitting.
2. Fill in your **Contact Information**, including any relevant details that may help us contact you.
3. Provide your **Medical Background**, including any relevant medical training or experience you may have.
4. If applicable, select your **Medical License** if you hold one.
5. Choose your **Work Experience** in the provided options to reflect your relevant work experience.
6. Select your **Medical Profession** from the provided options.
7. Select your **Medical Specialty** from the provided options.
8. If applicable, list your **Education**, including any relevant diplomas or certifications.
9. Provide **References** by selecting up to three individuals who can vouch for your medical skills or experience.
10. If applicable, upload any relevant **Uploaded Documents** that support your application.
11. Finally, provide **Additional Comments** if you have any further information you'd like to share about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`text`, optional): Enter your contact details, including name, email, and phone number.

### Medical Background
* **Medical Background** (`text`, optional): Describe your medical training, experience, or background.

### Medical License
* **Medical License** (`text`, optional): If applicable, enter your medical license number or details.

### Work Experience
* **Work Experience** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select up to three relevant work experiences that demonstrate your medical skills.

### Medical Profession
* **Medical Profession** (`select_one`, optional): Select your medical profession from the provided options.

### Medical Specialty
* **Medical Specialty** (`select_one`, optional): Select your medical specialty from the provided options.

### Education
* **Education** (`text`, optional): List any relevant diplomas or certifications you've achieved.

### References
* **References** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select up to three individuals who can vouch for your medical skills or experience.

### Uploaded Documents
* **Uploaded Documents** (`note`, optional): Upload any relevant documents that support your application.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, optional): Provide any further information about your application if you'd like.
