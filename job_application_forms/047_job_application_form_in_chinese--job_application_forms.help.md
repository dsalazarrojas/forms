# Job Application Form In Chinese - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Job Application Form In Chinese" is designed to collect information from individuals applying for a job in a Chinese-speaking market. The form is intended to be completed by job applicants to provide essential details about themselves, including contact information, employment history, and language proficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This will be used for communication and notification purposes.
4. Enter your **Job Title** or desired position in the "Job Title" field.
5. Enter the name of the **Company** you are applying to, or the company you are currently working for.
6. Attach your **Resume** to this form, or provide a link to your online resume.
7. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the "Date of Birth" field.
8. Enter your **Address** where you can be contacted.
9. Enter your **Contact Number** for phone or messaging.
10. Select your **Language** proficiency from the dropdown list.
11. Leave a **Note** if you have any additional information you'd like to share.
12. Enter an **Email 2** (if applicable) and **Contact Number 2** (if applicable) for alternative contact information.
