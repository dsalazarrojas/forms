# Online Staff Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Staff Registration Form is designed to collect essential information about new staff members, including contact details, job title, years of experience, skills, and work history. This form helps our team quickly and efficiently onboard new staff members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information section with your details.
2. Select your contact number and email address.
3. Upload your resume.
4. Choose your job title from the provided options.
5. Enter the number of years you have been working.
6. Select your relevant skills from the provided options.
7. Provide a brief description of your work history.
8. Once you have filled in all the required fields, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (Text, Optional): This is where you can enter your name, address, and other contact details.
* **Contact Number** (Number, Optional): Please enter a valid contact number for us to reach you.
* **Resume** (Text, Optional): Attach your resume to support your registration.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address to enable us to contact you.
* **Job Title** (Select One, Required): Choose the job title that best describes your role.
	+ Options: Account Manager, Data Scientist, Software Engineer.
* **Years of Experience** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of years you have been working.
* **Skills** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the skills that best describe your abilities.
	+ Options: Communication, Teamwork, Time Management.
* **Work History** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of your previous work experience.
* **Job Description** (Text, Optional): This field is reserved for future use, currently not required.
