# Main Construction Bhd Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Main Construction Bhd Application Form is designed to collect personal and professional information from job applicants. This form will be used to assess applicant's eligibility, skills, and background. Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) with your personal details.
2. Select your employment status from the drop-down menu.
3. If you have any previous work experience, please describe it in the "Work Experience" field.
4. Enter your emergency contact's details if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
  
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
  
- **Email**: Enter your work email address.
  
- **Phone**: Enter your work phone number (optional).
  
- **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
  
- **Time of Birth**: Enter your time of birth (optional).
  
- **Note**: Add any additional information you think is relevant to your application.
  
- **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status from the drop-down menu:
  - Active
  - Inactive
  - Retired
- **Address**: Enter your current address.
  
- **Address Line 2**: Enter any additional line for your address.
  
- **City**: Enter the city of your address.
  
- **State**: Select your state of residence from the drop-down menu:
  - CA
  - NY
  - TX
  - AZ
- **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
  
- **Skills**: Select any relevant skills you have from the drop-down menu:
  - Carpentry
  - Electrical
  - Plumbing
  - HVAC
- **Work Experience**: Describe any previous work experience.
  
- **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
  
- **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
  
- **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter your emergency contact's phone number (optional).
  
- **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter your emergency contact's email address (optional).
  
- **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter your emergency contact's address (optional).
  
- **Emergency Contact City**: Enter the city of your emergency contact's address.
  
- **Emergency Contact State**: Select your emergency contact's state of residence from the drop-down menu:
  - CA
  - NY
  - TX
  - AZ
- **Emergency Contact Zip**: Enter your emergency contact's zip code (optional).
  
- **Emergency Contact Note**: Add any additional information about your emergency contact.
  
- **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form (optional).
