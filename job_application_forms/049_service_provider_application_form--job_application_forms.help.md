# Service Provider Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Provider Application Form is used to collect information from service providers to help us understand their needs and provide them with relevant support.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Enter Your First Name

1. Fill out the "First Name" field with your given name.
2. This field is not required to be completed in order to proceed with the rest of the form.

### Step 2: Enter Your Last Name

1. Fill out the "Last Name" field with your surname.
2. This field is not required to be completed in order to proceed with the rest of the form.

### Step 3: Enter Your Email

1. Enter your business or personal email address that we can contact you on.
2. Make sure to enter a valid email address in order to be able to receive further communication from us.
3. This field is not required to be completed in order to proceed with the rest of the form.

### Step 4: Enter Your Phone Number

1. Fill out the "Phone Number" field with your contact phone number.
2. This field is not required to be completed in order to proceed with the rest of the form.

### Step 5: Enter Your Job Title

1. Fill out the "Job Title" field with your professional title.
2. This field is not required to be completed in order to proceed with the rest of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Your given name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): A contact email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): A contact phone number that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Your professional title that describes your role within your organization.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any issues or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
