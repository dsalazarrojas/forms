# Summer Internship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Internship Application Form is designed for candidates to submit their application for the summer internship program. This form is used to collect relevant information from candidates, such as their name, contact information, availability, and work experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Upload your resume in the "resume_upload" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter the start date of your summer internship in the "start_date" field.
6. Enter the expected end date of your summer internship in the "end_date" field.
7. Enter the number of hours you are available to work per week in the "available_hours" field.
8. Enter the number of hours you are available to work in total in the "available_hours_2" field.
9. Enter your job title in the "job_title" field.
10. Describe your previous work experience in the "work_experience" field.
11. Describe your educational background in the "education" field.
12. Select the days of the week you are available to work in the "available_weekdays" field.
13. Enter your available time in the "available_time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Upload Resume** (`resume_upload`, text, required/optional): Upload your resume in this field. Please make sure the file is in PDF or Docx format.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address in this field.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your summer internship in this field.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the expected end date of your summer internship in this field.
* **Available Hours** (`available_hours`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of hours you are available to work per week in this field.
* **Available Hours 2** (`available_hours_2`, number, required/optional): Enter the total number of hours you are available to work in this field.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title in this field.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required/optional): Describe your previous work experience in this field.
* **Education** (`education`, text, required/optional): Describe your educational background in this field.
* **Available Weekdays** (`available_weekdays`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the days of the week you are available to work in this field.
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, time, required/optional): Enter your available time in this field.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* Be honest about your availability and work experience.
* Double-check your resume upload format to ensure it is in PDF or Docx.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at [insert contact information].
