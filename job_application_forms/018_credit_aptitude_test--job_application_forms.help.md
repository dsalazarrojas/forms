# credit_aptitude_test - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Credit Aptitude Test" form is a tool used to assess an individual's credit aptitude and credit score for financial evaluation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide your Date of Birth.
4. Enter your Email address.
5. Enter your Phone number.
6. Enter your credit score.
7. Describe your Credit Aptitude.
8. Select your Credit Aptitude level (High, Medium, or Low).
9. Select your credit score level (High, Medium, or Low).
10. Add any additional Note (optional).
11. Select your Time Zone.
12. Select your Category.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, number, required): Enter your actual credit score.
* **Credit Aptitude** (`credit_aptitude`, text, required): Describe your credit history and habits.
* **Select Credit Aptitude** (`select_credit_aptitude_score`, select_multiple, required): Choose the level of your credit aptitude (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Select Credit Score** (`select_credit_score`, select_one, required): Choose the level of your credit score (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional information that you would like to be considered.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, time, optional): Select your time zone.
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Enter your category of selection.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill in all required fields accurately.
* If you experience any issues while filling the form, contact your supervisor or support team.
* Please note that this form is for assessment purposes only and the results may be used for internal evaluation.
