# Career Transition Program Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Career Transition Program Application form is designed to collect essential information about your career aspirations, goals, and background to support the Career Transition Program. This program aims to help individuals in career transition or those looking to change or enhance their professional trajectory. By providing accurate and comprehensive information, you'll be able to create a strong application that showcases your motivation, background, and goals for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by carefully reading each section, ensuring you understand what information is required and what type of format is expected (e.g., text, number, or select_one).
2. Fill out your personal details in sections **Program Name**, **Full Name**, **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, **Date of Birth** (if required), **Address**, **City**, **State or Province**, and **Postal Code**.
3. If employed, provide your **Current Employment Status**, **Current Job Title**, and **Current Industry**.
4. Share your **Years of Professional Experience**, **Highest Level of Education**, and **Field of Study or Major**.
5. Explain your **Transition Reason**, **Target Career or Field**, and **Target Position or Job Title**, if applicable.
6. Outline your **Motivation Statement**, highlighting why you want this career change.
7. List your **Key Skills and Strengths**.
8. Identify **Skill Gaps You Want to Address**.
9. State your **Goals for This Program**.
10. Attach your **Resume** in PDF, Word, or text format.
11. Optionally, provide a **Cover Letter or Personal Statement**.
12. Choose your **Financial Situation** for the transition (e.g., Employer sponsorship, Personal savings, etc.).
13. Indicate your available **Time Commitment** to the program in hours per week.
14. Select your preferred **Program Format** (e.g., Full-time in-person, Part-time online).
15. Provide a realistic **Preferred Program Start Date** (if applicable).
16. Specify your desired **Timeline for Program Completion**.
17. List any **Previous Training or Certifications** you've had.
18. Indicate your **Transportation** situation if the program is in-person.
19. If applicable, describe any **Accessibility or Accommodation Needs**.
20. Provide information about your **Professional References** (if required).
21. Finally, add any **Additional Information** that might be helpful for the program committee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Program Name**: Name of the career transition program.
- **Full Name**: Your complete name, first and last.
- **Email Address**: Your professional email address.
- **Phone Number**: Your work or personal phone number (with area code).
- **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth (if requested).
- **Address**: Your complete street address.
- **City**: The city of your residence.
- **State or Province**: Your state or province of residence.
- **Postal Code**: Your zip code.
- **Current Employment Status**: Your current employment status (e.g., Employed full-time, Unemployed).
- **Current Job Title**: If employed, your job title.
- **Current Industry**: If employed, your current industry.
- **Years of Professional Experience**: Number of years you've been working professionally.
- **Highest Level of Education**: Your highest degree achieved (e.g., High School Diploma, Bachelor's Degree).
- **Field of Study or Major**: Your major subject in school.
- **Transition Reason**: Explain why you're seeking career transition.
- **Target Career or Field**: The field you're aiming to transition into.
- **Target Position or Job Title**: The job you're aiming to transition into (if known).
- **Motivation Statement**: A statement explaining why you want to pursue this career change.
- **Key Skills and Strengths**: A list of your key skills and professional strengths.
- **Skill Gaps You Want to Address**: Areas you need to develop or improve upon.
- **Goals for This Program**: Your aspirations and objectives for the program.
- **Resume**: Upload your resume in PDF, Word, or text format.
- **Cover Letter or Personal Statement**: A personal statement explaining your background and goals.
- **Financial Situation**: How you plan to fund your transition (e.g., Employer sponsorship, Personal savings).
- **Time Commitment**: Your available time per week for the program.
- **Preferred Program Format**: How you prefer to learn (e.g., Full-time in-person, Part-time online).
- **Preferred Program Start Date**: When you can begin the program (if applicable).
- **Timeline for Program Completion**: When you aim to complete the program.
- **Previous Training or Certifications**: Any relevant training or certifications you've had.
- **Transportation**: If the program is in-person, do you have reliable transportation?
- **Accessibility or Accommodation Needs**: If you have any special requirements.
- **Professional References**: Names and contact information of 2 references.
- **Additional Information**: Any further details or context about your application.

By following this guide, you will be able to fill out the Career Transition Program Application form accurately and effectively, ensuring that your application stands out and meets the program's requirements.
