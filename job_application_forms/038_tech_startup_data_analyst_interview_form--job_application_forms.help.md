# tech_startup_data_analyst_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals applying for a Data Analyst/Scientist position. It helps us understand your background, experience, and preferences to determine the best fit for our team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information and documents at hand.
2. Answer each question truthfully and comprehensively, as this will increase the likelihood of a positive review.
3. Use the provided options to select the most accurate answer.
4. If a field is not required, feel free to skip it or fill it in if it's relevant to your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (2, Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (3, Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Email** (4, Email, Required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (5, Text, Required): Enter your mobile phone number.
* **What role are you applying for?** (6, Select One, Required): Choose the role for which you are applying, either Data Analyst or Data Scientist.
* **What is your availability?** (7, Select Multiple, Required): Select the types of availability that apply to you, such as Full-Time, Part-Time, or Contracted.
* **Resume** (8, Note, Optional): Attach or describe your resume.
* **Resume link** (9, Text, Optional): If you have a link to your resume, enter it here.
* **How does your background in Data Science/Analysis support this role?** (10, Text, Optional): Describe how your experience and skills align with the role.
* **Why Data Science?** (11, Select One, Optional): Select the reason you are interested in Data Science, such as For the Love of it, For the Challenge, etc.
* **How many people would you like to manage?** (12, Number, Optional): Select the number of people you would like to manage as part of your team.
* **What kind of team dynamic are you looking for?** (13, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the team dynamic that suits you, such as Team of Experts, Team of Generalists, etc.
* **What is your expected salary range?** (14, Number, Optional): Enter your expected salary range.
* **Work Experience** (15, Select Multiple, Optional): Select your level of work experience in Data Science/Analysis, such as Less than 1 year, 1-5 years, etc.
* **Data Science/Analysis Experience** (16, Select Multiple, Optional): Select your level of experience in Data Science/Analysis, such as Less than 1 year, 1-5 years, etc.
* **Work Environment** (17, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your preferred work environment, such as On-site, Remote, Hybrid.
* **Education** (18, Select Multiple, Optional): Select your educational background, such as Bachelor's, Master's, etc.
* **Certifications** (19, Select Multiple, Optional): Select any relevant certifications you hold.
* **Programming Skills** (20, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the programming languages you are proficient in.
* **Tools** (21, Select Multiple, Optional): Select any data tools you have experience with.
* **Data Platform** (22, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your experience with data platforms, such as Hadoop, Spark, etc.
* **Data Visualization** (23, Select Multiple, Optional): Select any data visualization tools you have experience with.
* **Data Storage** (24, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose your experience with data storage options, such as AWS, Azure, etc.
* **Submission** (25, Note, Optional): Attach or describe any additional information you would like to submit with your application.
