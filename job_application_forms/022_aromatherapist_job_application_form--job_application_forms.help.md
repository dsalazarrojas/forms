# Aromatherapist Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your personal and professional background, skills, and experience as an aromatherapist. It will help us assess your qualifications for the position you are applying for.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in a quiet and comfortable environment, where you can focus without distractions.
2. Answer each question carefully and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Be specific when describing your experience, skills, and qualifications.
4. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it should be spelled and formatted. This is the name that will be used for our records.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Please enter your professional email address that we can use to contact you. Make sure it is correct and up-to-date.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Please enter your phone number that we can use to contact you. This can be your work or mobile phone number.
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, text, required): Please enter your current address, including your city, state, and ZIP code. This is used to verify your identity and location.
* **City, State, ZIP Code** (`city_state_zip`, text, required): Please enter your city, state, and ZIP code as per your current address. This helps us verify your location.
* **Position Type** (`position_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of position you are applying for, such as Full-time Aromatherapist, Part-time Aromatherapist, etc.
* **Preferred Position Location** (`position_location`, text, required): Please enter the location where you would like to work. This can be a specific city or state.
* **Available Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Please select the start date you are available to begin working. This is important for scheduling purposes.
* **Aromatherapy Certification** (`aromatherapy_certification`, select_one, required): Please select your level of aromatherapy certification, such as Certified Aromatherapist, Registered Aromatherapist, etc.
* **Certification Details** (`certification_details`, text, optional): If you have any additional certification details, such as the issuing organization or license number, please enter them here.
* **Highest Level of Education** (`highest_education`, select_one, required): Please select your highest level of education, such as High school diploma, Some college, etc.
* **Additional Training or Certifications** (`additional_training`, text, optional): If you have any additional training or certifications, such as massage therapy or herbalism, please enter them here.
* **Years of Aromatherapy Experience** (`years_aromatherapy`, number, required): Please enter the number of years you have been practicing aromatherapy.
* **Previous Aromatherapy Work Experience** (`previous_employment`, text, required): Please enter your previous work experience as an aromatherapist.
* **Areas of Specialization** (`specializations`, select_multiple, optional): If you have specialized areas, such as relaxation therapy, stress management, etc., please select them here.
* **Type of Clients You Have Worked With** (`client_experience`, select_multiple, optional): If you have worked with different types of clients, such as corporate or athletic clients, please select them here.
* **Key Professional Skills** (`key_skills`, text, required): Please enter your key professional skills, such as client consultation or custom blending.
* **Essential Oil Knowledge Level** (`essential_oil_knowledge`, select_one, required): Please select your level of essential oil knowledge, such as Beginner, Intermediate, etc.
* **Customer Service Experience** (`customer_service`, select_one, required): Please select your level of customer service experience, such as Limited, Moderate, etc.
* **Why Are You Interested in This Position?** (`why_aromatherapy`, text, required): Please enter a brief statement explaining why you are interested in this position.
* **Career Goals in Aromatherapy** (`career_goals`, text, optional): If you have any career goals in aromatherapy, please enter them here.
* **Additional Information or Qualifications** (`additional_info`, text, optional): If you have any additional information or qualifications that you would like to share, please enter them here.
