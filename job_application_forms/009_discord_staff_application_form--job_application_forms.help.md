# discord_staff_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The discord_staff_application_form is a tool used to collect information from potential new staff members. It helps to gather contact details and other relevant personal details that can be used in the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling in your email address, a required field that will allow us to contact you with updates about your application.
2. Next, fill in any additional fields you feel are relevant for your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`discord_staff_application_form_1`, Text, required): This is the title of the application form. Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on our team.
* **Email** (`discord_staff_application_form_1_fields_1`, Email, required): This field is used for us to contact you with updates about your application. Please enter your email address correctly, including your domain name (e.g., example@domain.com).
* **Additional Field 1** (`discord_staff_application_form_1_fields_2`, Number, optional): Please fill in any additional details about yourself, such as your experience or skills.
* **Additional Field 2** (`discord_staff_application_form_1_fields_3`, Number, optional): This field is for any further details or qualifications you would like to share with us. 

## Tips
- Please make sure to proofread your form before submitting it to ensure it is complete and accurate.
- If you are having trouble filling out the form, please contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
