# Project Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Manager Application Form is a form designed for users to apply for the position of Project Manager. It is a multi-page form that collects relevant information from applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in each page, making sure to provide accurate and concise responses.
2. For select_multiple and select_one fields, choose only one or multiple options that apply to your situation.
3. For text fields, type in your relevant information.
4. Ensure that you are answering questions accurately and honestly.
5. Review and submit the form once you have filled out all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Manager Application** (`project_manager_application_form`, Select Multiple, Not Required): This is the main page of the form. Answer 'Yes' if you are applying for the position of Project Manager.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, Not Required): Enter your current or previous job titles you have held.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, Text, Not Required): Provide a brief description of your job responsibilities and achievements in your previous or current job.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, Select One, Not Required): Choose your education level (High or Low).
* **Resume** (`resume`, Text, Not Required): Attach a resume that includes your relevant work experience and qualifications.
* **Cover Letter** (`cover_letter`, Text, Not Required): Attach a cover letter that explains why you are a good fit for the Project Manager position.
* **Job References** (`job_references`, Text, Not Required): List your previous or current employers who can vouch for your work experience and qualifications.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, Text, Not Required): Enter your contact information so that we can reach out to you for further communication.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, Select One, Not Required): Choose your current employment status (Active or Inactive).
* **Notice Periods** (`notice_periods`, Select Multiple, Not Required): Choose how many notice periods you are willing to provide if you were to leave the position.
* **Employment History** (`employment_history`, Text, Not Required): Provide a brief description of your employment history, including the job titles and dates of employment.
