# Data Scientist Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect your information as a Data Scientist job applicant. Please provide accurate and honest details about your qualifications and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*) after the field label.
2. Use the hints provided for guidance on each field.
3. If a field type is "text", please enter your information in the text box.
4. If a field type is "select_one" or "select_multiple", choose one or multiple options from the dropdown list.
5. If a field type is "date", enter your date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
6. If a field type is "number", enter a numerical value.
7. If a field type is "email", enter a valid email address.
8. If a field type is "time", enter a time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
9. Click "Submit" to complete and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Resume** (`resume`, `note`, optional): Attach or paste a link to your resume.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): If you have a specific job title, enter it here.
* **Current Company** (`current_company`, `text`, optional): If you are currently employed, enter your current company name.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your work experience from the options provided.
* **Highest Education** (`highest_education`, `select_one`, optional): Select your highest education level from the options provided.
* **Relevant Experience** (`relevant_experience`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of years of relevant experience you have.
* **Skills** (`skills`, `text`, optional): List your relevant skills for the Data Scientist role.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `text`, optional): Enter your availability for the role (e.g., full-time, part-time, freelance).
* **Availability Date** (`availability_date`, `date`, optional): If you have a preferred start date, enter it in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Time Availability** (`time_availability`, `time`, optional): Enter your preferred start time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Availability Location** (`availability_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where you are available to work.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `text`, optional): If you are interested in a specific job type (e.g., full-time, part-time, contract), enter it here.
* **Salary Range** (`salary_range`, `number`, optional): Enter your expected salary range.
* **Expected Salary** (`expected_salary`, `number`, optional): Enter your expected salary for the role.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, `date`, optional): If you have a preferred start date, enter it in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, `date`, optional): If you have a preferred end date, enter it in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the job status you are interested in (e.g., full-time, part-time, contract, internship).
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select your employment type (e.g., employee, freelancer, independent).
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or multiple job categories relevant to the Data Scientist role.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, `select_one`, optional): Select your job level (e.g., junior, mid-level, senior, executive).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, optional): Enter your name for identification purposes.

## Tips

* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and honestly.
* You can attach or paste a link to your resume for ease of review.
* Double-check your information before submitting your application.
* You can contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
