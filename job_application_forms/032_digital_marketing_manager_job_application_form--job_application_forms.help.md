# Digital Marketing Manager Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us assess your suitability for the Digital Marketing Manager position. It will help us understand your background, experience, and qualifications in digital marketing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Please be honest and truthful in your answers.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, leave it blank or contact us for clarification.
4. Review and verify your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Application Form** (`job_application_form`, text, required: false): This field is a general description of your application. Please provide a brief introduction about yourself and your motivations for applying for the Digital Marketing Manager position.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_one, required: false): Please select the title that best describes your current job or role. This will help us understand your current level of experience and expertise in digital marketing.
	+ Options: Yes, No (Please note that this field is optional and you can select 'No' if you are not currently working)
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required: false): This field is for you to describe your job description or responsibilities in your current or previous role. Please provide specific examples or tasks you have performed in digital marketing.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, text, required: false): This field is for you to describe your qualifications, skills, or experience in digital marketing. Please provide specific examples or certifications that make you a suitable candidate for the position.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible in your answers, providing specific examples or experiences.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Please submit your form only if you are seriously interested in the Digital Marketing Manager position.
* We will review your application and contact you if we need further information or require an interview.
