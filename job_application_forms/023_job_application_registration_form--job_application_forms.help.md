# Job Application Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Application Registration Form is designed to facilitate the job application process by providing a structured way for job applicants to submit their applications and for the hiring team to track and manage the application status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Attach any necessary documents, such as your resume and additional files.
3. Choose the status of your application (Active or Inactive).
4. Set the interview schedule and time.
5. Provide any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Job Application Registration Form (1)**: This is the first page of the form. Please fill in your name and any other relevant information.
- **Job Application Details (2)**: Provide a brief description of the job position you are applying for and any relevant experience or qualifications you have.
- **Job Application Contact Information (3)**: Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
- **Job Application Resume (4)**: Attach your resume, which should be in PDF format.
- **Job Additional Files (5)**: Attach any other relevant documents or files that support your application.
- **Job Application Status (6)**: Choose the status of your application (Active or Inactive).
- **Job Application Status Note (7)**: Enter any notes or comments regarding your application status.
- **Job Application Interview Schedule (8)**: Choose the date and time of your interview.
- **Job Application Interview Time (9)**: Enter the duration of your interview.
- **Job Application Interviewer (10)**: Enter the name and title of the interviewer.
- **Job Application Notes (11)**: Enter any additional notes or comments regarding your application.
- **Job Application Follow up (12)**: Enter the date for follow up on your application status.
- **Job Application Decision Date (13)**: Enter the date of the hiring decision.
- **Job Application Rejection Reason (14)**: Enter the reason for rejection (if applicable).
- **Job Application Decision Note (15)**: Enter any notes or comments regarding the hiring decision.
- **Job Application Accepted By (16)**: Enter the name and title of the person who accepted your application.
- **Job Application Rejection Reason (17)**: Enter the reason for rejection (if applicable).
- **Job Application Follow up Date (18)**: Enter the date for follow up on your application status.
- **Job Application Status Update (19)**: Choose the status update (Active or Inactive).
