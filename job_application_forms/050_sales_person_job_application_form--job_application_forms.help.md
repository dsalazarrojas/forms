# Sales Person Job Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from job applicants for sales roles within the company. It collects personal details, work experience, and specific job-related information to help evaluate candidates.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**: Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
2. **Enter your personal details**: Provide your first name, last name, email, phone number, and education in the designated fields.
3. **Select your relevant work experience**: Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for your job experience, sales manager experience, available start date, job status, skills, work experience, and references.
4. **Enter job specifics**: Provide job title, job type, job category, job description, salary, available end date, and job status.
5. **Upload required documents**: Attach your resume and portfolio (if applicable).
6. **Review your form**: Before submitting, review your form for accuracy and completeness.

* **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years you have in sales experience.
* **Available Start Date**: Enter the date you are available to start work.
* **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job you are applying for.

* **Job Category**: Enter the category of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Description**: Write a brief description of the job you are applying for.
* **Salary**: Enter your expected salary or the one mentioned in the job posting.
