# Software Development Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Software Development Manager Application Form is designed for external candidates applying for a software development manager position. The form collects relevant information about the candidate's background, skills, and availability to determine their suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields, marked as *Required*.
2.  Choose relevant options for each select field.
3.  Upload a resume or CV if requested.
4.  Ensure that the form is filled out accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Job Title
*   **Job Title** (`Software Development Manager`, `Other`): Choose the job title that best describes the position you are applying for. If none of the options match, select "Other" and provide a brief description in the field below.

### Job Type
*   **Job Type** (`Full-time`, `Part-time`): Select the type of job you are applying for. If you are unsure, select "Full-time" or "Part-time" based on the job description.

### Job Level
*   **Job Level** (`Junior`, `Senior`): Select the level of experience you have for the position. If you are unsure, choose the level that best describes your experience.

### Job Category
*   **Job Category** (`Software Development`, `Other`): Select the category of the job you are applying for. If none of the options match, select "Other" and provide a brief description in the field below.

### Job Sector
*   **Job Sector** (`Technology`, `Other`): Select the sector of the job you are applying for. If you are unsure, choose the sector that best describes your work experience.

### Job Location
*   **Job Location** (`On-site`, `Remote`): Select the location of the job you are applying for. If you are unsure, choose the location that best describes your work arrangement.

### Job Status
*   **Job Status** (`Active`, `Inactive`): Select the status of the job you are applying for. If the job status is not listed, select "Active" or "Inactive" based on the job description.

### Job Description
*   **Job Description**: Briefly describe the job you are applying for, including any specific requirements or qualifications.

### Application Date
*   **Application Date**: Enter the date you are applying for the position.

### Application Time
*   **Application Time**: Enter the time you are available to start working.

### Application Status
*   **Application Status** (`Approved`, `Rejected`): Select the status of your application.

### Job Application ID
*   **Job Application ID**: Enter the ID of the job application you are submitting.

### Candidate Name
*   **Candidate Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Candidate Email
*   **Candidate Email**: Enter your email address.

### Candidate Phone
*   **Candidate Phone**: Enter your phone number.

### Assigned Tool
*   **Assigned Tool**: Enter any tools or software you are familiar with or have experience using.
 
Remember, this form is your chance to showcase your suitability for the software development manager position. Fill it out accurately and completely, and do not hesitate to ask if you have any questions or concerns.
