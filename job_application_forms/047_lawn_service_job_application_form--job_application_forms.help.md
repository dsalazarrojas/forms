# Lawn Service Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect job application information from individuals applying for lawn service jobs. The information collected on this form will be used by lawn care companies to review and process applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the job you are applying for from the "Job Application Form" options.
2. Enter your name and any other personal information.
3. Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
4. Enter your address.
5. Describe your experience in the lawn service industry, if applicable.
6. List any relevant skills you have, such as lawn care equipment operation or plant identification.
7. Enter the date you are available to start work.
8. Enter your expected salary.
9. Enter the notice period you require before starting work.
10. Add any additional comments.
11. Select the person who submitted the application.
12. Enter the date the application was submitted.
13. Enter the ID number of the job you are applying for.
14. Select the person or department to whom the application was submitted.
15. Select the person or team to which the application was assigned.
16. Enter the name of the person to whom the application was assigned.
17. Enter the title of the person to whom the application was assigned.
18. Enter the name of the tool or resource assigned to process the application.
19. Enter the name of the user who is assigned to process the application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Application Form** (Select Multiple):
Select the job you are applying for. Please choose the job that best matches your qualifications and preferences.
* **Job Title** (Text):
Enter the title of the job you are applying for.
* **Name** (Text):
Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Contact Info** (Select One):
Select "None" if you do not have a contact method, otherwise select "Other" and enter your contact information.
* **Phone Number** (Text):
Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (Email):
Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Address** (Text):
Enter your home or work address.
* **Experience** (Text):
Describe your experience in the lawn service industry, including any relevant work history or certifications.
* **Skills** (Select Multiple):
Select "None" if you do not have any relevant skills, otherwise select "Other" and enter any skills you have, such as lawn care equipment operation or plant identification.
* **Available Date** (Date):
Enter the date you are available to start work.
* **Expected Salary** (Number):
Enter the expected salary for the position.
* **Notice Period** (Number):
Enter the number of days notice you require before starting work.
* **Comments** (Note):
Add any additional comments or information about your application.
* **Submitted By** (Select One):
Select "None" if you are not submitting your application, otherwise select "Other" and enter who submitted the application.
* **Created At** (Date):
This field is automatically generated and cannot be edited.
* **Updated At** (Date):
This field is automatically generated and cannot be edited.
* **Submitted At** (Date):
This field is automatically generated and cannot be edited.
* **Job ID** (Text):
Enter the ID number of the job you are applying for.
* **Submitted To** (Select One):
Select "None" if you are not submitting your application, otherwise select "Other" and enter to whom the application was submitted.
* **Submitted To Title** (Text):
Enter the title of the person or department to whom the application was submitted.
* **Assigned To** (Select One):
Select "None" if you are not assigning the application, otherwise select "Other" and enter to whom the application was assigned.
* **Assigned To Name** (Text):
Enter the name of the person to whom the application was assigned.
* **Assigned To Title** (Text):
Enter the title of the person to whom the application was assigned.
* **Assigned Tool** (Text):
Enter the name of the tool or resource assigned to process the application.
* **Assigned User** (Text):
Enter the name of the user who is assigned to process the application.

Note: This help guide is for end-users who will be using the form to apply for lawn service jobs. The guide is intended to be concrete and practical, with a focus on clear instructions and explanations for each field. If a field is not required or has a weak/unclear label, a best-effort explanation has been provided.
