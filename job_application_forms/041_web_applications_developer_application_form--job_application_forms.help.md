# web-applications-developer-application-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The web-applications-developer-application-form is a comprehensive form designed for web applications developers to apply for a job. The form gathers essential information from the applicant, including their contact details, resume information, education, work experience, and skills. The form aims to collect accurate and relevant data to assist in the evaluation and selection process of candidates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the resume section, which includes your resume and any additional resume links.
2.  Next, enter your contact information, including your name, contact phone number, and email address.
3.  Please indicate your experience in years as a developer and provide details about your educational background.
4.  Select your relevant experience and skillset from the provided options.
5.  Provide any additional relevant work experience.
6.  Finally, review your entire form before submitting it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Resume**: Please upload your resume.
*   **Resume (2)**: You can provide links to any additional resumes you have.
*   **Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed.
*   **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
*   **Email**: Enter your professional email address.
*   **Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
*   **Address**: Enter your work address.
*   **Experience**: Indicate your experience in years as a developer.
*   **Years**: Indicate your experience in years.
*   **Education**: Select your level of education (High/Low) and if you have a degree (Yes/No).
*   **Field of Study**: Enter your field of study.
*   **Portfolio Links**: Provide links to your professional portfolio.
*   **Resume Links**: Provide links to any additional resumes.
*   **Position**: Enter your job title or position.
*   **Company**: Enter the name of your current or previous company.
*   **Duration**: Indicate the duration of your experience.
*   **Job Description**: Provide a brief description of your job.
*   **Skills**: Select your relevant skills.
*   **Experience Skillset 2**: Select any additional relevant skills.
*   **Work Experience**: Indicate if you have work experience.
*   **Work Experience 2**: Select if you have additional work experience.
*   **Skills 2**: Select any additional skills.
