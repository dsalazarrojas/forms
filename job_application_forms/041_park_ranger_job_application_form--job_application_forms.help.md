# Park Ranger Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for job applicants applying for a Park Ranger position. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the contact details section with your name, email address, and phone number. This information will be used to contact you regarding your application.
2.  In the employment history section, please provide a brief description of your past work experience, highlighting any relevant work experience or qualifications that would make you a strong candidate for this position. 
3.  In the education background section, please provide details about your academic qualifications, including degrees earned and institutions attended. 
4.  In the skills and certifications section, please list any relevant certifications or special skills you have acquired.
5.  In the work experience section, please provide detailed descriptions of your past work experience, including job titles, dates of employment, and achievements. 
6.  In the personal references section, please provide the names and contact information of at least two professional references. 
7.  In the job application section, please upload your resume and provide a brief description of your interest in the position. 
8.  In the portfolio section, please upload any relevant work or project samples that demonstrate your skills and experience.
9.  In the uploaded files section, please upload any required documents, such as your license or ID card. 
10.  In the park ranger special skills section, please select any special skills you have acquired that are relevant to the position. 
11.  In the conservation area of interest section, please select the areas of conservation you are interested in, such as wildlife conservation or habitat protection. 
12.  In the work with people section, please select how you would work with people, whether directly or indirectly. 
13.  In the languages section, please select any languages you can speak or read. 
14.  In the references section, please provide a brief description of your references. 
15.  In the upload references section, please upload any relevant references or certifications. 
16.  In the upload license section, please upload any required licenses or certifications. 
17.  In the upload ID card section, please upload your ID card. 
18.  In the upload other documents section, please upload any other relevant documents. 
19.  In the upload signature section, please upload your signature.
