# Fashion Model Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for fashion models who are interested in working with us. It allows us to gather necessary information from applicants to review and invite those who are selected to an audition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in your information in the corresponding fields. Please note that you can upload images from Google Drive, if necessary. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to increase your chances of being selected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Your email address, which we will use for communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required): Your date of birth.
* **Height** (`height`, number, required): Your height in inches.
* **Measurements** (`measurements`, text, required): Your body measurements (e.g., waist, bust, hip).
* **Experience** (`experience`, select_multiple, required): How many years of modeling experience do you have? Please select all that apply.
  * 0-1 year
  * 1-2 years
  * 2-5 years
  * 5-10 years
  * More than 10 years
* **Available** (`available`, select_one, required): How often are you available to work? Please select one:
  * Always
  * Some days
  * Rarely
  * Never
* **Portfolio Links** (`portfolio_links`, text, required): Provide links to your modeling portfolio or social media profiles.
* **Portfolio Images** (`portfolio_images`, select_multiple, required): How would you like to share your portfolio images?
  * None
  * Upload own images
  * Upload from URL
  * Upload from Google Drive
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required): Attach your resume or upload it as a PDF.
* **References** (`references`, text, required): Provide the names and contact information of your three references.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): The name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, select_one, required): How are you related to your emergency contact?
  * Mother
  * Father
  * Spouse
  * Partner
  * Other
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): The phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Address** (`emergency_address`, text, required): The address of your emergency contact.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Your current address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): The city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): The state where you reside.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Your zip code.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`how_did_you_hear_about_us`, text, required): How did you hear about our agency?
* **What type of modeling experience?** (`what_type_of_modeling_experience`, text, required): Briefly describe your modeling experience and style (e.g., fashion, commercial, runway).
* **What type of modeling?** (`what_type_of_modeling`, select_multiple, required): What type of modeling do you specialize in?
  * Fashion
  * Commercial
  * Editorial
  * Fitness
  * Runway
* **Modeling License** (`modeling_license`, text, optional): If you have a modeling license, please attach it as a PDF.

Tips: Please ensure that you have the necessary modeling experience and licenses before applying. This form is for modeling purposes only and should not be used for other opportunities.
