# Manufacturing Worker Employment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Manufacturing Worker Employment Form collects information from manufacturing workers about their employment status and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out each field according to the following explanations.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3.  Review your input for errors or inconsistencies before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <manufacturing_worker_employment_form>
This is the main section of the form where you will enter your employment details.

*   **Job Title:** Select all the job titles that apply to you from the list provided.
    (Multiple selections possible)
### <shift_preferences>
Enter your shift preferences, such as your availability and preferred shifts.
*   **Shift Preferences:** Enter your preferred shifts, considering your work schedule and availability.
    (Text)
### <education>
Enter your educational background and qualifications relevant to the manufacturing industry.
*   **Education:** Enter your educational background and relevant qualifications.
    (Text)
### <emergency_contact>
Enter the contact information for your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact:** Enter your emergency contact's name and contact information.
    (Text)
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship:** Enter your relationship with your emergency contact.
    (Text)
*   **Emergency Contact Phone:** Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
    (Text)
*   **Emergency Contact Email:** Enter your emergency contact's email address.
    (Text)

### <availability>
Enter your availability for work, including your preferred work schedule.
*   **Availability:** Enter your availability for work, including your preferred work schedule.
    (Text)

### <employment_status>
Select your current employment status.
*   **Employment Status:** Select your current employment status: Active or Inactive.
    (Single selection possible)

### <work_history>
Enter your work history, including relevant job experience.
*   **Work History:** Enter your work history, including relevant job experience.
    (Text)

### <contact_info>
Enter your contact information.
*   **Contact Info:** Enter your contact information for work purposes.
    (Text)

### <work_experience>
Enter your relevant work experience, including job titles and dates.
*   **Work Experience:** Enter your relevant work experience, including job titles and dates.
    (Text)
