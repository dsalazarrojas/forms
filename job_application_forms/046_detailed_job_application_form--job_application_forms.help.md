# Detailed Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from job applicants for a comprehensive job application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information.
2. Ensure that the email address is in the correct format (e.g., [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com)).
3. Enter your phone number in the format of a standard 10-digit number (e.g., 123-456-7890).
4. Enter your job title and job type as they appear on your resume or as you have described them in your resume.
5. Enter your years of experience, if applicable.
6. Select your available start date and end date if you have a specific availability.
7. Attach your resume and cover letter as text documents.
8. Provide a job reference, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Application Form** (`job_application_form`, text, not required): This field is a text input where you can provide any additional information about your application.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, not required): Enter your 10-digit phone number in the format 123-456-7890.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, not required): Enter your current or desired job title as it appears on your resume.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, not required): Enter the type of job or position you are applying for.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, not required): Enter the number of years you have worked in your field, if applicable.
* **Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, date, not required): Select your available start date.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, not required): Select your end date.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, not required): Attach your resume as a text document.
* **Cover Letter** (`cover_letter`, text, not required): Attach your cover letter as a text document.
* **Job Reference** (`job_reference`, text, not required): Enter a job reference, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and honestly.
* Proofread your application form before submitting to avoid errors and ensure a smooth evaluation process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the hiring manager for clarification.
* The information collected will be used for internal evaluation purposes only.
