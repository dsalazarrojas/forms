# Outsourcing Job Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for job applicants to submit their application for outsourcing job opportunities. It collects personal and professional information, job details, and other relevant data to help the employer evaluate your qualifications for the position.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal information, including your name, contact info, and other relevant details in the **Personal Info** section.
2. Provide your job application information, such as job type, category, and location, in the **Job App Info** section.
3. Share your work experience and education background in the **Work Exp** and **Education** sections.
4. List your skills and upload your resume in the **Skills** and **Resume** sections.
5. Attach any relevant job application form attachments, such as certificates or references, in the **Job App Form Attachments** section.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about your application in the **Job App Form Comments** section.
7. Indicate the status of your job application in the **Job Status** section.
8. Specify the type of job you are applying for and the category and subcategory it belongs to in the **Job Type**, **Job Category**, and **Job Subcategory** sections.
9. Provide the name and ID of the company you are applying to in the **Company** and **Job Opening ID** sections.
10. Specify the job location and salary range in the **Job Location** and **Job Salary** sections.
11. Share your job experience and employment type in the **Job Experience** and **Job Employment Type** sections.
12. Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number, in the **Contact Info** and **Email** and **Phone** sections.
13. List any job references or recommendations in the **Job References** section.
14. Finally, click the **Submit** button to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info**: Enter your personal details, such as your name, contact info, and other relevant information.
* **Job App Info**: Provide information about the job you are applying for, including job type, category, and location.
* **Work Exp**: Share your work experience relevant to the job application.
* **Education**: List your educational background.
* **Skills**: List your relevant skills and qualifications.
* **Resume**: Attach your resume or CV.
* **Upload Resume**: You can upload a resume or CV if you don't want to manually type it in.
* **Job App Form Attachments**: Attach any relevant job application form attachments, such as certificates or references.
* **Job App Form Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Job Status**: Indicate the status of your job application.
* **Job Type**: Specify the type of job you are applying for.
* **Job Category**: Specify the category of the job.
* **Job Subcategory**: Specify the subcategory of the job.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company you are applying to.
* **Job Opening**: Enter the job opening title and ID.
* **Job Location**: Specify the location of the job.
* **Job Salary**: Specify the salary range for the job.
* **Job Experience**: Share your job experience.
* **Job Employment Type**: Specify your employment type (e.g. full-time, part-time, contractor).
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Job References**: List any job references or recommendations.
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to complete your application.
