# Youth Ministry Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential candidates for youth ministry positions within our organization. It helps us understand your qualifications, experience, and availability to serve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Attach your resume and cover letter if applicable.
4. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Please provide your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Please provide your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Please provide a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Please provide your contact phone number.
* **Job Application Position** (text, required): Please describe the specific position you are applying for.
* **Resume Attachment** (text, required): Please attach your resume in PDF or Word format.
* **Cover Letter** (text, required): Please attach a cover letter explaining why you are a good fit for the position.
* **References** (text, required): Please provide a list of professional references.
* **Work Experience** (text, required): Please describe your relevant work experience.
* **Education** (text, required): Please list your educational background.
* **Availability** (text, required): Please describe your availability to start work.
* **Salary Range** (number, optional): Please provide your expected salary range.
* **Available Start Date** (date, optional): Please provide your expected start date.
* **Available End Date** (date, optional): Please provide your expected end date.
* **Assigned Tool** (select_one, optional): Please select the tool you are familiar with.
* **Submitted By** (select_one, optional): Please select yes or no if you were submitted by a referral.
* **Submitted Date** (date, optional): Please provide the date you submitted your application.

Note: The fields marked as required must be filled out in order to submit the form. Optional fields are not mandatory but can be filled out for additional information.
