# Veterinary Nurse Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a job application for a Veterinary Nurse position. It requires you to provide information about your personal details, education, experience, and qualifications to be considered for this role.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Contact Number and Address in the required fields.
3. Briefly describe your Experience, Qualifications, and Availability in the respective fields.
4. If applicable, provide information about your Education and Skills.
5. Select the languages you are proficient in from the options provided.
6. Choose the type of job you are applying for (Part-time, Full-time, or Volunteer).
7. Provide details about your Job Location and Job Status.
8. Enter your Expected Salary Range.
9. Describe your Work Schedule.
10. If required, select the Job Category and Job Type (Permanent, Contract, or Temporary).
10. Finally, click the Submit Button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Contact Number** (Text, required): Enter a contact number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (Text, required): Enter your current address.
* **Experience** (Text, required): Briefly describe your previous experience in the field of veterinary nursing.
* **Qualifications** (Text, required): List any relevant qualifications you have, such as certifications or diplomas.
* **Availability** (Text, required): Describe your work availability, including days of work and any specific hours you can work.
* **Education** (Text, optional): If applicable, provide details about your educational background.
* **Skills** (Text, optional): List any relevant skills you possess.
* **Work Experience** (Text, optional): Briefly describe any relevant work experience.
* **References** (Text, optional): Provide any relevant references or professional connections.
* **Emergency Contact** (Text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of a person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Certification** (Text, optional): If you have any medical certification, list it here.
* **Languages** (Select multiple, optional): Select the languages you are proficient in, from English, French, and Spanish.
* **Job Title** (Text, required): Enter the title of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Description** (Text, required): Briefly describe the job description of the position you are applying for.
* **Job Type** (Select one, required): Choose the type of job you are applying for, from Part-time, Full-time, or Volunteer.
* **Job Location** (Text, required): Enter the location of the job.
* **Expected Salary Range** (Number, required): Enter your expected salary range for the job.
* **Work Schedule** (Text, required): Describe your work schedule, including days of work and any specific hours you can work.
* **Job Category** (Text, optional): If required, enter the category of the job.
* **Job Status** (Text, optional): If required, enter the current status of the job.
* **Job Type** (Select one, optional): If required, choose the type of job, from Permanent, Contract, or Temporary.
* **Submit Button** (Note, required): Click this button to submit your application.
