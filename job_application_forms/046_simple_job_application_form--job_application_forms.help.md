# Simple Job Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Simple Job Application Form is a tool used to collect contact information and background details from job applicants. This form helps applicants provide their credentials, experience, and availability for review by hiring personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your contact information (page 1).
2.  Provide your name and any other relevant contact details.
3.  Fill out your email address and other contact information.
4.  Enter your phone number for easy contact.
5.  Enter your current address for contact and background checks.
6.  Share your educational background (degree, institution, etc.).
7.  Provide reference information for at least 2 contacts (name, title, organization, contact info).
8.  Describe your relevant experience in the field.
9.  List your qualifications and skills relevant to the job.
10.  Specify your available start date (if available).
11.  Choose your available end date (if applicable).
12.  Add any additional notes about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
*   **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required: false): This field is for your contact details, including your name, email address, and phone number. Please fill in your name and any other contact information you think is relevant.
### Name
*   **name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name.
### Email Address
*   **email address** (`email address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email address for communication and contact purposes.
### Phone Number
*   **phone number** (`phone number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
### Address
*   **address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your current address for contact and background checks.
### Educational Background
*   **educational background** (`educational background`, `text`, required: false): Share your educational background, including degree, institution, and relevant courses or certifications.
### Reference Info 1
*   **reference info 1** (`reference info 1`, `text`, required: false): Provide reference information for at least one contact, including name, title, organization, and contact info.
### Reference Info 2
*   **reference info 2** (`reference info 2`, `text`, required: false): Provide another reference for at least one more contact, with details similar to Reference Info 1.
### Experience
*   **experience** (`experience`, `text`, required: false): Describe your relevant experience in the field, highlighting your achievements and qualifications.
### Qualifications
*   **qualifications** (`qualifications`, `text`, required: false): List your relevant qualifications and skills that match the job requirements.
### Available Start Date
*   **available start date** (`available start date`, `date`, required: false): Specify the date you are available to start the job. If not applicable, leave blank.
### Available End Date
*   **available end date** (`available end date`, `date`, required: false): If you have a specific end date to your contract or engagement, enter it. If not applicable, leave blank.
### Note
*   **note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about your application, such as specific job requirements or your motivation.

## Tips
- Double-check for spelling and grammar errors before submitting the form.
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date contact information.
- Be specific when describing your experience and qualifications.
- Attach any relevant documents or certificates if requested.
- Proofread your application for clarity and coherence.

Please note that this form is a simplified example. You may need to add or modify fields based on your specific job requirements and company policies.
