# Computer Animator Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from applicants for a computer animator position. It is essential to complete this form accurately and carefully to increase your chances of being considered for the position.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the personal information section, which includes your full name, email address, phone number, and portfolio link.
2.  In the professional experience section, provide details about your years of experience in computer animation, your primary animation software tools proficiency, and your primary animation specialty.
3.  If you have relevant skills and education, select them from the provided options.
4.  Provide your earliest available start date and any additional comments or information you think is necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Information

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your work or personal email address. Please ensure it is in the format example@example.com.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Portfolio Link** (`portfolio_url`, `text`, required): Share a link to your online portfolio or animation reel.

### Professional Experience

* **Years of Experience in Computer Animation** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, required): Enter your years of experience in computer animation.
* **Primary Animation Software Tools** (`primary_software_tools`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the animation software tools you are proficient in, such as Autodesk Maya, Blender, or Cinema 4D.
* **Primary Animation Specialty** (`animation_specialty`, `select_one`, required): Choose your strongest area of animation, such as character animation or visual effects.
* **Have you worked in a Professional Studio before?** (`past_studio_experience`, `select_one`, required): Answer 'True' if you have worked in a professional studio before, or 'False' if not.
* **Highest Level of Education** (`degree_attained`, `select_one`, required): Select your highest level of education, such as High School Diploma or Master Degree.
* **Earliest Available Start Date** (`earliest_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the earliest date you are available to start work.
* **Additional Comments or Information** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information you think is relevant to your application.

Note: Please ensure you fill out all required fields to increase your chances of being considered for the position.
