# Secondary School Teaching Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secondary School Teaching Job Application Form is designed to collect information from secondary school teachers applying for a teaching position at the school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the school's job description and requirements to understand what the school is looking for.
2. Fill out the form honestly and accurately, providing all relevant information.
3. Ensure that you have attached all required documents and qualifications as specified in the school's job description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form**: This is the main form where you can provide a brief summary of your application.
* **Personal Details**: Please select one of the options (Primary Teaching Qualification, Post Primary Teaching Qualification, Option 3, or No Qualifications) that best describes your teaching qualification.
* **Teaching Experience**: Select all relevant teaching experiences from the options (Years of Teaching Experience, Teaching Area of Specialization, Teaching Certificates, Teaching Awards).
* **Education Background**: Provide a brief summary of your education background.
* **Work History**: Provide a brief summary of your work history.
* **Education**: Please note: this field is currently empty. It seems to be an error in the schema. Please fill this in as a text input.
* **Job Title**: Provide a brief summary of the job title you are applying for.
* **Job Description**: Please note: this field seems to be an error in the schema. Please fill this in as a text input.
* **School Details**: Provide the name of the school you are applying to.
* **Contact Details**: Provide your contact information (name, email, phone number).
* **Application Status**: Note: This field seems to be an informational field for the school's internal use. Please ignore it.
* **Uploaded Documents**: Note: This field is not intended for the applicant to upload documents. This field is for the school's internal use. Please ignore it.

Please note that I have assumed the fields with no options or hint are intended to be text inputs.
