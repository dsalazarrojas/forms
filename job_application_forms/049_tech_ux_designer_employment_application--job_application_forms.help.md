# tech_ux_designer_employment_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a tech UX designer employment application. It's crucial to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your application is reviewed properly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Contact Info** field with your email address or phone number.
2. Describe your **Work Experience** in the **Work History** field, selecting the relevant companies, job titles, locations, and duration of employment.
3. If you have any additional education or qualifications, provide details in the **Education** field.
4. Upload your **Resume** in the **Uploaded File** field.
5. Finally, click on the **Submit** button to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Contact Info**
* **Contact Info (Email/Phone)** (Email/Phone, Optional): Provide your email address or phone number so we can contact you for further instructions.

### **Work History**
* **Work Experience (Company 1, Job Title 1, Location 1, Duration 1)** (Optional, Multi-select): Describe your previous work experience, highlighting your relevant skills and qualifications.

### **Education**
* **Education** (None, Optional): If you have any additional education or qualifications, provide details here.

### **Resume**
* **Resume** (Text, Optional): Upload your resume as a PDF or other supported file format.

### **Submit**
* **Submit** (Text, Optional): Click on this button to send your application. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to proofread your application before submitting it.
* Be honest and accurate while filling out the form.
* Attach all necessary supporting documents, if required.
