# Swim Instructor Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Swim Instructor Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in becoming swim instructors at our organization. This form is used to gather necessary details for evaluating applicants' qualifications and experiences related to swimming instruction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information, including your name and any preferred contact method (phone number and/or email address).
2. Provide a brief description of your swimming background, including any relevant swimming experiences or achievements.
3. Answer the certification question: Do you have swim certifications?
	* Select "Yes" if you have swim certifications.
	* Select "No" if you do not have swim certifications.
4. Describe your swim education and training, including any degrees or certifications related to swimming or swimming instruction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Please enter your name and any preferred contact method (phone number and/or email address).
* **Swimming Background** (`swim_background`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your swimming background, including any relevant swimming experiences or achievements.
* **Swim Certifications** (`swim_certifications`, select_one, required: false): Answer the certification question: Do you have swim certifications? 
	+ Select "Yes" if you have swim certifications.
	+ Select "No" if you do not have swim certifications.
* **Swim Education** (`swim_education`, text, required: false): Describe your swim education and training, including any degrees or certifications related to swimming or swimming instruction.

## Tips
Please ensure that you provide accurate and honest information in this form. This information will be used to evaluate your qualifications and experiences for swim instructor positions.
