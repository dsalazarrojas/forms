# Casting Fill Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Casting Fill Out Form is used to gather information about actors and actresses for casting purposes in a production. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary documents and materials ready.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, paying attention to any required fields.
3. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number, including the area code.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Height** (`height`, text, required): Enter your height in feet and inches (e.g., 5'10").
* **Eye Color** (`eye_color`, select_one, required): Select your natural eye color from the list.
* **Hair Color** (`hair_color`, select_one, required): Select your natural hair color from the list.
* **Distinguishing Features** (`distinguishing_features`, text, optional): List any distinguishing features such as tattoos, scars, or birthmarks.
* **Years of Acting Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been acting.
* **Previous Roles** (`previous_roles`, text, optional): List any previous films, TV shows, or theater productions you have been in.
* **Training Background** (`training_background`, text, optional): Enter any acting classes, workshops, or certifications you have completed.
* **Union Affiliation** (`union_affiliation`, select_one, optional): Select your union affiliation from the list.
* **Character Name Being Cast** (`character_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the character you are interested in being cast as.
* **Are you interested in this role?** (`interested_in_role`, select_one, required): Select your level of interest in the role from the list.
* **Scheduling Availability** (`scheduling_availability`, text, optional): Enter your availability for filming dates and shooting schedule.
* **Headshot Photo** (`headshot_file`, text, required): Upload a professional headshot photo.
* **Acting Resume** (`resume_file`, text, required): Upload your acting resume, including training and experience.
* **Agent/Manager Name** (`agent_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your agent or manager.
* **Agent/Manager Contact Information** (`agent_contact`, text, optional): Enter your agent's or manager's phone number and email.
* **I agree to the casting terms and conditions** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' to agree to the casting terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
* Upload only the requested files and documents.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the contact person indicated in the form or production team. 

Please note that this form is intended to be used for casting purposes only.
