# Hospitality Language Proficiency Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on a candidate's language proficiency, availability, and work preferences for a hospitality job.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the field marked "Name".
2. Select your language proficiency level for each of the languages listed (English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Chinese).
3. Enter your available start and end dates in the "Available to start" and "Available to finish" fields, respectively.
4. Select your available work hours in the "Available to work" field.
5. Enter your proficiency level in each of the languages listed (English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Chinese), with "0" indicating no proficiency and "1" indicating proficiency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`form_field_1`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Language Proficiency** (`form_field_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all languages you are proficient in, e.g., English, French, Spanish, etc.
* **Available to start** (`form_field_3`, `date`, required): Enter the start date you are available to begin work, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Available to finish** (`form_field_4`, `date`, required): Enter the end date you are available to finish work, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Available to work** (`form_field_5`, `time`, required): Select your available work hours, e.g., full-time, part-time, flexible, etc.
* **English** (`form_field_6`, `number`, required): Enter your proficiency level in English, with "0" indicating no proficiency and "1" indicating proficiency.
* **French** (`form_field_7`, `number`, required): Enter your proficiency level in French, with "0" indicating no proficiency and "1" indicating proficiency.
* **Spanish** (`form_field_8`, `number`, required): Enter your proficiency level in Spanish, with "0" indicating no proficiency and "1" indicating proficiency.
* **German** (`form_field_9`, `number`, required): Enter your proficiency level in German, with "0" indicating no proficiency and "1" indicating proficiency.
* **Italian** (`form_field_10`, `number`, required): Enter your proficiency level in Italian, with "0" indicating no proficiency and "1" indicating proficiency.
* **Chinese** (`form_field_11`, `number`, required): Enter your proficiency level in Chinese, with "0" indicating no proficiency and "1" indicating proficiency.
* **Available to work** (`form_field_12`, `text`, required): Enter any other languages you are available to work.
* **Available to travel** (`form_field_13`, `text`, required): Enter any places you are available to travel.
* **Availability for interview** (`form_field_14`, `text`, required): Enter any availability for interview periods.
* **Availability for training** (`form_field_15`, `text`, required): Enter any availability for training periods.
* **Availability for shift** (`form_field_16`, `text`, required): Enter any other availability for shift periods.
* **Notice period** (`form_field_17`, `text`, required): Enter any notice period you are aware of.
* **Relocation period** (`form_field_18`, `text`, required): Enter any relocation period you are aware of.
* **Holiday period** (`form_field_19`, `text`, required): Enter any holiday period you are aware of.
* **Other** (`form_field_20`, `text`, optional): If you have any other availability or preferences not listed above, enter them here.
* **Available for shifts** (`form_field_21`, `text`, optional): If you have any other availability for shifts, enter them here.

## Tips
*Please fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly. The information collected on this form will be used to help determine your language proficiency and work preferences for a hospitality job.
