# Employee Reference Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Reference Request Form is used to request references from a company's former employees for the purpose of background checks for new job applications. This helps to verify the accuracy of the information provided by the employee in their job application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the requested information:

*   **Employee Name**: The name of the employee you want to request a reference for.
*   **Job Title**: The job title of the employee during their employment.
*   **Company Name**: The name of the company where the employee was employed.
*   **Supervisor Name**: The name of the employee's supervisor during their employment.
*   **Employment Start Date**: The date the employee started working at the company.
*   **Employment End Date**: The date the employee ended working at the company.
*   **Employment Length**: A brief description of the employee's employment duration.
*   **Reason for Leaving**: The reason for the employee's departure from the company.
*   **References**: A list of yes or no answers indicating if the employee has any references.
*   **Contact Info**: Any additional contact information for the reference.
*   **Email**: The email of the reference (if available).
