# Chemist Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Chemist Job Application Form is a collection of fields that helps us get to know you better as a potential candidate for a chemist position. This form is designed to gather information about your background, qualifications, and experience in chemistry. By providing accurate and honest answers, you will help us evaluate your suitability for the position and make the hiring process smoother and more efficient.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer all required questions.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and honest information in all fields.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or assistance from the hiring team.
4. Please sign the applicant signature field at the end of the form, certifying that all information is true and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. First Name (<name>): 
* **First Name** (text, Required): Please enter your first name.

### 2. Last Name (<name>): 
* **Last Name** (text, Required): Please enter your last name.

### 3. Email Address 
* **Email Address** (email, Required): Please enter your email address.

### 4. Phone Number 
* **Phone Number** (text, Required): Please enter your contact phone number.

### 5. Address 
* **Address** (text, Required): Please enter your full address.

### 6. LinkedIn Profile 
* **LinkedIn Profile** (text, Optional): Please enter the URL to your LinkedIn profile if applicable.

### 7. Position Applied For 
* **Position Applied For** (select_one, Required): Please select the chemist position you are applying for.

### 8. Available Start Date 
* **Available Start Date** (date, Required): Please enter the date you can start work.

### 9. Salary Expectations 
* **Salary Expectations** (text, Optional): Please enter your expected annual salary.

### 10. Highest Degree

### 11. Field of Study

### 12. University Name

### 13. Graduation Year 
* **Graduation Year** (number, Required): Please enter the year you graduated.

### 14. GPA 
* **GPA** (number, Optional): Please enter your grade point average if applicable.

### 15. Chemistry Specializations

### 16. Years of Experience

* **Current Employer** (text, Optional): Please enter the name of your current employer.

### 18. Current Position

### 19. Previous Experience

### 20. Laboratory Skills

### 21. Instrumentation Experience
