# Beauty Salon Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beauty Salon Job Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in applying for a job at a beauty salon. This form helps salon management or human resources staff to evaluate applicants' qualifications and suitability for available positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** in the respective fields.
2. Select the **Position Applied For** that you are interested in, such as **Stylist** or **Receptionist**.
3. Choose your desired **Employment Type**, either **Full-Time**, **Part-Time**, or **Contract / Booth Rental**.
4. Enter your available **Available Start Date** and select your **Weekly Availability** by choosing all relevant days of the week.
5. If applicable, provide information about your **Years of Professional Experience**, **Professional License Number**, and **Last Employer Name**.
6. If you have any, provide your **Portfolio or Social Media Link** and **Relevant Certifications**.
7. Finally, provide your **Professional References** with their name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Home Address**: Enter your home address.

### Position and Availability
* **Position Applied For**: Select the specific position you are applying for, such as **Stylist** or **Receptionist**.
* **Desired Employment Type**: Choose the type of employment you are looking for, either **Full-Time**, **Part-Time**, or **Contract / Booth Rental**.
* **Available Start Date**: Enter your available start date for the position.
* **Days Available to Work**: Select all the days of the week you are available to work.

### Experience and Education
* **Years of Professional Experience**: Enter the number of years you have been working professionally in the industry.
* **Professional License Number**: Enter your professional license number if applicable.
* **Last Employer Name**: Enter the name of your previous employer.
* **Reason for Leaving**: Enter the reason for leaving your previous employer.
* **Portfolio or Social Media Link**: Provide a link to your portfolio or social media to showcase your work.
* **Relevant Certifications**: List any relevant certifications or special techniques you have.
* **Professional References**: Provide the name and contact information of your professional references.
