# Office Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for job applicants to submit their applications for office roles. To apply, please fill out the form completely and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
- To begin, fill out each field with the required information. Each field is explained in more detail below.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
- Submit the form once all fields have been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (First Name, text, optional): 
  Please enter your first name in this field.
- **Last Name** (Last Name, text, optional): 
  Please enter your last name in this field.
- **Email** (Email, email, optional): 
  Enter a valid email address associated with you.
- **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): 
  Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, optional): 
  Enter your date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Address** (Address, text, optional): 
  Please enter your current address.
- **Job Title** (Job Title, text, optional): 
  If you have a specific job title you are applying for, enter it here.
- **Job Description** (Job Description, text, optional): 
  Briefly describe the job you are applying for.
- **Available Dates** (Available Dates, date, optional): 
  Enter the dates you are available to work.
- **Available Times** (Available Times, time, optional): 
  Enter the times you are available to work.
- **Job Status** (Job Status, select_one, optional): 
  Select the current status of your job (Active, Inactive, On Leave).
- **Job Type** (Job Type, select_multiple, optional): 
  Select all applicable job types (Full-time, Part-time, Freelance).
- **Department** (Department, text, optional): 
  If you have a specific department you are applying for, enter it here.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled out.
- Double-check for spelling and grammar errors before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues with the form, please contact your HR department for assistance.
