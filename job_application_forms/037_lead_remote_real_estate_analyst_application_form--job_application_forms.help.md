# Lead Remote Real Estate Analyst Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for applicants who want to apply for the Lead Remote Real Estate Analyst position. It helps us understand your background, skills, and availability to match you with the best opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required") with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. For the "job_status" field, select the option that best describes your current or desired job status.
3. For the "skills" field, select all the skills that you have experience with and are relevant to the job.
4. For the "available_months" field, select all the months you are available to work.
5. For the "work_from_home" field, select your preferred work arrangement.
6. Enter a brief description of your experience, job title, and work experience in the respective fields.
7. Finally, review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name (Text)**: Enter your first name.
* **last_name (Text)**: Enter your last name.
* **email (Email)**: Enter your email address.
* **phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **experience (Number)**: Enter the number of years you have relevant experience.
* **available_days (Number)**: Enter the number of days you are available to work per week.
* **available_hours (Number)**: Enter the number of hours you are available to work per week.
* **work_hours (Time)**: Enter the time you are available to work per day.
* **job_status (Select One)**: Select "Analyst", "Associate", "Manager", or "Other" to describe your current or desired job status.
* **skills (Select Multiple)**: Select all the skills you have experience with, including "Analytic", "Communication", "Leadership", "Problem Solving", "Time Management", or "Other".
* **available_months (Select Multiple)**: Select all the months you are available to work.
* **work_from_home (Select Multiple)**: Select "True", "False", or "Partial" to describe your preferred work arrangement.
* **job_title (Text)**: Enter a brief description of your job title.
* **job_description (Text)**: Enter a brief description of your job experience.
* **work_experience (Text)**: Enter a brief description of your work experience.
* **work_hours_per_week (Number)**: Enter the number of hours you are available to work per week.
* **work_hours_per_month (Number)**: Enter the number of hours you are available to work per month.
* **work_days_per_week (Number)**: Enter the number of days you are available to work per week.
* **work_days_per_month (Number)**: Enter the number of days you are available to work per month.
* **salary (Number)**: Enter your salary range in number format.
