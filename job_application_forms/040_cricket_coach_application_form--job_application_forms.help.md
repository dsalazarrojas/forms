# Cricket Coach Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cricket Coach Application Form is a form designed for individuals interested in applying for a cricket coach position within our organization. This form allows applicants to provide essential information about themselves, their contact details, coaching experience, and any relevant certifications. It is essential to ensure that the information provided in this form is accurate, complete, and true to the best of the applicant's ability to increase the chances of a successful application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the form sections one by one, starting with **applicant_info**.
2. Ensure you answer all required questions (marked with *) and provide information that is accurate and true.
3. For optional sections, you can choose to provide information or leave them blank.
4. Double-check your answers for spelling and grammar errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Applicant Info** (1) 
* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief introduction about yourself, stating your name and position or interest in coaching.

### **Contact Info** (2)
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Provide your contact information, including your address and any other contact details you wish to share with us.

### **Coaching Experience** (3)
* **Coaching Experience** (`coaching_experience`, text, required/optional): Describe your past coaching experience, including any relevant training, certifications, or achievements.

### **Cricket Achievements** (4)
* **Cricket Achievements** (`cricket_achievements`, note, required/optional): Use this section to highlight any notable cricket-related achievements, such as awards, recognition, or notable events.

### **Referees Certifications** (5)
* **Referees Certifications** (`referees_certifications`, select_multiple, required): Select any relevant cricket referees' certifications you hold, such as Cricket Umpire Certification or Cricket Referee Certification.

### **First Name** (6)
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.

### **Last Name** (7)
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.

### **Email** (8)
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for communication.

### **Contact Phone** (9)
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for easy communication.

## Tips
* Be sure to proofread your answers before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any attachments or additional information you want to share, please attach them separately and include them with your application.
