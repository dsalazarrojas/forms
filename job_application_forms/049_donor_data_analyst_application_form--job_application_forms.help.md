# Donor Data Analyst Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Data Analyst Application Form is a tool for potential candidates interested in joining the donor data analyst team. It is designed to collect information from applicants about their personal and professional backgrounds, including their name, contact information, job experience, and uploaded resume. The form helps our team evaluate each applicant's qualifications and determine the best fit for our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first-name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last-name" field.
3. Enter your job title in the "job-title" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
6. Describe your relevant work experience in the "work-experience" field.
7. Upload your resume in the "resume-upload" field.
8. (Optional) You may also be asked to provide additional job titles to be considered for specific roles within our organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (first-name)** (`first_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your first name for our reference.
* **Last Name (last-name)** (`last_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your last name for our reference.
* **Job Title (job-title)** (`job_title`, Text, Optional): Enter your current or previous job title(s) for us to better understand your background.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone (phone)** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for us to reach you.
* **Work Experience (work-experience)** (`work_experience`, Text, Optional): Describe your relevant work experience that aligns with our organization's mission and goals.
* **Resume Upload (resume-upload)** (`resume_upload`, Text, Optional): Upload your resume for our team to review.
* **Job Title 2 (job-title-2)** (`job_title_2`, Text, Optional): (Optional) Enter any additional job title(s) you'd like to be considered for specific roles within our organization.
* **Job Title 3 (job-title-3)** (`job_title_3`, Text, Optional): (Optional) Enter any additional job title(s) you'd like to be considered for specific roles within our organization.
* **Job Title 4 (job-title-4)** (`job_title_4`, Text, Optional): (Optional) Enter any additional job title(s) you'd like to be considered for specific roles within our organization.

## Tips
