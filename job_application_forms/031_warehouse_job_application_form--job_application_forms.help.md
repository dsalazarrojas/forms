# Warehouse Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Job Application Form is a tool for applicants to submit their interest and information for available warehouse positions within our company. This form gathers essential details to help us assess your fit for our warehouse roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the respective text fields.
2.  Provide your available Date and Time in the respective date and time fields.
3.  If applicable, share any additional Notes or comments.
4.  Attach your Resume to support your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your given name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Date Available** (date, required/optional): Enter the date you are available to start working, if applicable.
* **Time Available** (time, required/optional): Enter the time you are available to start working, if applicable.
* **Note** (note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes you'd like to share about your application.
* **Job Title** (text, required/optional): Enter the title of the job you are applying for, if known.
* **Job Description** (text, required/optional): Briefly describe why you're a good fit for the job.
* **Job Location** (text, required/optional): Enter the location you'd be willing to work, if applicable.
* **Resume** (text, required/optional): Attach your resume to support your application.
