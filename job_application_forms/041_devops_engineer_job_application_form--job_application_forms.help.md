# DevOps Engineer Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for DevOps engineers to submit their job applications.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Upload a resume in the designated "Resume" field.
4. Review your application before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (id: 1)** (`text`, required: false)
  This field is used to enter your first name. Please enter the name 
  your parents gave you, or the name you prefer to be called.

* **Last Name (id: 2)** (`text`, required: false)
  This field is used to enter your last name. Please enter the name 
  your parents gave you, or the name you prefer to be called.

* **Email (id: 3)** (`email`, required: false)
  This field is used to enter your email address. Please ensure that 
  it is accurate and up-to-date, as this will be the primary way we 
  contact you.

* **Resume (id: 4)** (`text`, required: false)
  This field is used to upload your resume. Please attach a PDF or 
  Word document of your resume.

* **Job Title (id: 5)** (`text`, required: false)
  This field is used to enter the job title you are applying for. 
  Please be specific and accurate.

* **Company (id: 6)** (`text`, required: false)
  This field is used to enter the company you currently work for. 
  Please ensure that it is accurate.

* **Job Description (id: 7)** (`text`, required: false)
  This field is used to describe the job you are applying for. 
  Please be specific and detailed.

* **Years of Experience (id: 8)** (`number`, required: false)
  This field is used to enter your experience in years. Please enter a 
  number of years you have worked in the field.

* **Job Location (id: 9)** (`text`, required: false)
  This field is used to enter the location of the job you are applying 
  for. Please be specific and accurate.

* **Job Start Date (id: 10)** (`date`, required: false)
  This field is used to enter the date you plan to start the job. 
  Please use the correct date format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* Double check your email address for accuracy.
* Attach a valid resume in PDF or Word format.
* Be specific and detailed in your job description and job location.
