# Coach Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to apply for a coaching position. It collects personal details, coaching experience, qualifications, and other relevant information to assess their suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details (Section 1) with your legal first and last name.
2. Enter your phone number and email address for contact purposes.
3. Provide your home address for communication and scheduling reasons.
4. Choose the sport or discipline you're applying to coach.
5. Indicate the total years of coaching experience.
6. List any relevant certifications or licenses.
7. Describe your approach to coaching athletes.
8. Provide name and contact info for two professional references.
9. Indicate your consent to a background check.
10. Select your available start date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal first and last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number that can be used for communication and scheduling purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address that we can use for contact and communication purposes.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Enter your full street address, city, and zip code for communication and scheduling purposes.
* **Sport or Discipline** (`sport_discipline`, text, required): Select the sport or discipline you're applying to coach (e.g., soccer, basketball, swimming).
* **Years of Coaching Experience** (`years_experience`, number, required): Indicate the total years of coaching experience as a head or assistant coach.
* **Relevant Certifications** (`certifications`, text, required): List any relevant coaching licenses or safety certifications you hold.
* **Coaching Philosophy** (`coaching_philosophy`, text, required): Briefly describe your approach to coaching athletes.
* **Professional References** (`references`, text, required): Provide name and contact info for two professional references.
* **Background Check Consent** (`background_consent`, select_one, required): Indicate your consent to undergo a background check.
* **Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, date, required): Select your available start date for the coaching position.
