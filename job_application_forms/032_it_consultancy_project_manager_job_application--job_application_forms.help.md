# IT Consultancy Project Manager Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a job application for an IT Consultancy Project Manager position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page of the form, starting with the first page.
2. Please enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure that your Email address is valid and can receive messages from us.
5. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter your Job Title in the "Job Title" field.
7. Enter your Company name in the "Company" field.
8. If you have relevant experience, please enter it in the "Experience" field.
9. Select your available notice period from the "Notice Period" dropdown menu.
10. Choose your current job status from the "Job Status" dropdown menu.
11. Choose your job type from the "Job Type" dropdown menu.
12. Choose your job category from the "Job Category" dropdown menu.
13. Choose your job location from the "Job Location" dropdown menu.
14. If you have a resume or portfolio, please attach it in the "Resume" and "Portfolio" fields respectively.
