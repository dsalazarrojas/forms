# Social Media Onboarding Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect social media information from employees to facilitate internal collaboration, networking, and company-wide initiatives. Please provide the following details to create your onboarding profile.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in all required fields (marked as "Required").
2. Select multiple social media channels for your company profile from the list (page 8).
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses to provide a comprehensive representation of your profile.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (page 1)
* **First Name**: A text field to input your first name for your onboarding profile.
* Example: John Doe

### Last Name (page 2)
* **Last Name**: A text field to input your last name for your onboarding profile.
* Example: Doe

### Email (page 3)
* **Email**: A text field for your work email address.
* Example: [johndoe@example.com](mailto:johndoe@example.com)

### Phone (page 4)
* **Phone**: A text field to input your work phone number.
* Example: 123-456-7890

### Date of Hire (page 5)
* **Date of Hire**: A date field to input your date of hire for the company.
* Example: 2022-01-01

### Job Title (page 6)
* **Job Title**: A text field to input your job title.
* Example: Software Engineer

### Manager Name (page 7)
* **Manager Name**: A text field to input your manager's name.
* Example: Jane Doe

### Social Media Channels (page 8)
* **Social Media Channels**: A select multiple field to choose your social media channels to be associated with your profile.
* Example: Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn

### Social Media Experience (page 9)
* **Social Media Experience**: A text field to provide a brief description of your social media experience.
* Example: 3+ years of experience in social media marketing

### LinkedIn (page 10)
* **LinkedIn**: A text field to input your LinkedIn profile URL or a brief description of your profile.
* Example: linkedin.com/in/johndoe

Please submit this form to complete your onboarding profile and support your company's internal social media initiatives.
