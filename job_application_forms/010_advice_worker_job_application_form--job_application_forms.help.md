# Advice Worker Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advice Worker Job Application Form is designed to collect relevant information from potential job applicants for Advice Worker positions. This form gathers data on personal details, position applied for, availability, experience, and qualifications. It is crucial to provide accurate and complete information to increase the chances of being considered for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** in the relevant fields.
2. Select the **Position Applied For** that you are interested in (Junior Advice Worker, Senior Advice Worker, Case Manager, or Volunteer Advisor).
3. Enter your **Availability** to start, using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
4. In the **Relevant Qualifications** field, list any relevant degrees or certifications (e.g., Law, Social Work).
5. Choose your **Years of Relevant Experience** from the provided options (None, Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5+ years).
6. Select the **Areas of Expertise** where you can provide advice (Housing, Debt & Money Management, Employment Rights, Immigration, Family Law, Welfare Benefits).
7. Enter your **Most Recent Employer** and provide the **Reference Contact Info** (Name and email of a professional reference).
8. If applicable, write a **Cover Letter / Personal Statement** explaining why you are a good fit for the position.
9. Finally, provide a **Link to Resume / CV**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_applied_for`, `select_one`, required): Select the position you are interested in applying for (Junior Advice Worker, Senior Advice Worker, Case Manager, or Volunteer Advisor).
* **Date Available to Start** (`availability`, `date`, required): Enter your availability to start in the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Relevant Qualifications** (`qualifications`, `text`, required): List any relevant degrees or certifications.
* **Years of Relevant Experience** (`years_experience`, `select_one`, required): Choose your level of experience from the provided options.
* **Areas of Expertise** (`areas_of_expertise`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the areas where you can provide advice.
* **Most Recent Employer** (`previous_employment`, `text`, required): Enter your most recent employer's name.
* **Reference Contact Info** (`reference_contact`, `text`, required): Provide the name and email of a professional reference.
* **Cover Letter / Personal Statement** (`cover_letter`, `text`, optional): Write a brief statement explaining why you are a good fit for the position.
* **Link to Resume / CV** (`resume_upload_link`, `text`, required): Provide a URL to your resume or CV.
