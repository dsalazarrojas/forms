# Creative Talent Recruitment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Creative Talent Recruitment Application form is designed to gather detailed information from applicants about their job application. This information will help the hiring team assess their qualifications and fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form sections in order, starting from the first section "Job Application Form".
2. For each section, provide accurate and clear information about yourself.
3. Make sure to upload any required documents, such as resume, cover letter, and portfolio.
4. Review your form before submission to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Job Application Form** (`job_application_form`, text, optional): This section is where you can provide a brief introduction about your job application, including your job title and a short description of why you're a good fit for the role.
 
- **Candidate Information** (`candidate_information`, text, optional): Provide your name, email address, and phone number. This information will be used to contact you about your application.
 
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your social media profiles or other relevant contact information to help the hiring team get in touch with you.
 
- **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): List your previous work experience, including the company name, job title, and duration of employment.
 
- **Educational Background** (`educational_background`, text, optional): Share your educational background, including the institution name, degree, and graduation date.
 
- **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): Enumerate your relevant skills, such as programming languages, tools, or software you're proficient in.
 
- **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, text, optional): Share a link or upload your portfolio to showcase your work.
 
- **Job References** (`job_references`, text, optional): List your professional references, including name, job title, and contact information.
 
- **Upload Resume** (`upload_resume`, text, optional): Upload your resume to provide more detailed information about your work experience and qualifications.
 
- **Upload Cover Letter** (`upload_cover_letter`, text, optional): Upload a cover letter to express your interest in the role and why you're a good fit.
 
- **Upload Portfolio** (`upload_portfolio`, text, optional): Upload your portfolio to showcase your work.
 
- **Submit** (`submit_form`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your application.

## Tips

* Double-check your form for accuracy before submission.
* Make sure to upload required documents, such as resume and cover letter.
* Provide clear and concise information in each section.
* Review your form before submitting to ensure everything is correct.
