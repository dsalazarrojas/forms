# Refugee Childcare Worker Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in working with refugees and their families as caregivers. It gathers information about your background, language skills, and availability to work with refugees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select your language proficiency for speaking, reading, and writing from the given options.
3. Choose your available languages and dates for work.
4. Indicate your available time for work.
5. If you're ready to submit your application, select the "Submit" option. If you're not ready, you can cancel and come back later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Information** (`application_information`): A text field where you can briefly introduce yourself and explain why you're interested in working with refugees and their families.
* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`): A text field where you can provide more information about yourself, such as your education and work experience.
* **Availability** (`availability`): Select the languages you're proficient in, such as English, Spanish, Arabic, French, Mandarin, or Other.
* **How Much Time** (`availability_2`): Enter the amount of time you're available to work with refugees and their families.
* **Experience** (`experience`): A text field where you can describe your experience working with diverse populations or in relevant fields.
* **Languages Spoken** (`languages_spoken`): Select the languages you can speak, such as English, French, Spanish, Arabic, Mandarin, or Other.
* **Languages Read** (`languages_read`): Select the languages you can read, such as English, French, Spanish, Arabic, Mandarin, or Other.
* **Languages Written** (`languages_written`): Select the languages you can write, such as English, French, Spanish, Arabic, Mandarin, or Other.
* **Available Dates** (`available_dates`): Enter your available dates for work, using the date format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Available Time** (`available_time`): Enter your available time for work, using 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Consent** (`consent`): Please note that your application is not binding, and you may cancel or withdraw it at any time.
* **Submit Application** (`submit_application`): Select "Submit" to send your application, or "Cancel" to exit the form.
