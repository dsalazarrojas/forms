# Online Application Form For Qualified Drivers - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for qualified drivers who want to apply for various positions related to driving or driving-related activities. Please make sure you have the required qualifications and experience before completing this form.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, fill in each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible. Make sure to provide clear and concise answers to each question.
2.  Start by filling in your driver information, including your education level and driving experience.
3.  Select your employment history, including your start and end dates, and your employment status.
4.  Provide your driving license information.
5.  Fill in any other relevant information requested in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Driver Information**: This section is where you will fill in your basic driver information. This includes your:
    *   **Driver Education** (`driver_education`): Please select the highest level of education you have completed. Options include:
        +   High School Diploma
        +   Some College
        +   Bachelor's
        +   Master's
        +   Doctoral
    *   **Driver Training** (`driver_training`): Please provide a brief description of your relevant training or courses related to driving.
    *   **Driver Experience** (`driver_experience`): Please provide a description of your driving experience, including any relevant certifications or qualifications.
    *   **Reference** (`driver_reference`): Please provide a contact information for a professional reference.
*   **Driver Employment History**:
    *   **Employment History** (`driver_employment_history`): Please provide a brief description of your employment history as a driver.
    *   **How Long Were You Employed** (`driver_employment_history_length`): If applicable, please select the length of your employment.
    *   **Start Date** (`driver_employment_history_start_date`): If applicable, please select the start date of your employment.
    *   **End Date** (`driver_employment_history_end_date`): If applicable, please select the end date of your employment.
    *   **Employment Status** (`driver_employment_history_employment_status`): Please select your current employment status. Options include:
        +   Active
        +   Inactive
        +   Retired
*   **Driving License**:
    *   **Driving License** (`driver_license`): Please select the status of your driving license. Options include:
        +   True
        +   False

## Tips

*   Please make sure to answer each question accurately and thoroughly.
*   If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
*   Do not provide any false or misleading information.
*   This form is for qualified drivers who want to apply for various positions related to driving or driving-related activities. Please make sure you have the required qualifications and experience before completing this form.
