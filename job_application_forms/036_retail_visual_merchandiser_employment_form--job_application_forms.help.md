# Retail Visual Merchandiser Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for retail visual merchandisers to apply for employment opportunities. It collects information about your contact details and work experience to help us understand your background and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each section. You can do this by typing in the corresponding fields or selecting from the provided options. Once you have filled out all the required information, click the submit button to send your application to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, required): This field is where you can enter your initial application message. Please provide a brief introduction about your qualifications and interest in the retail visual merchandiser position.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): This field allows you to provide your contact information, such as name, email, and phone number. This will help us reach out to you regarding your application.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe your previous work experience related to retail visual merchandiser roles. Please provide a concise summary of your relevant experience.

## Tips
* Be as concise as possible when filling out the form, as we will review your application based on the information provided.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents, you can attach them separately and provide the relevant file link in this form.
* Please proofread your application before submitting it to ensure it is free of errors and accurately reflects your qualifications.
