# Dentist Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us evaluate your qualifications and experience for a position in the dental field. Please fill out this form with accurate and relevant information to help us get to know you better.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:
- Fill out the form in English, the official language of our organization.
- Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
- Make sure to save your progress if you are not completing the form in one sitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Basic Info (text)**: This field is for basic contact information. Please enter your name and other basic details as requested.
- **Last Name (text)**: This field is for your last name. Please make sure to enter your correct last name.
- **Email (email)**: This field is for your professional email address. Please use an email address that you use exclusively for work purposes.
- **Experience (number)**: This field is for your number of years of experience in the dental field. If you don't have any experience, you can enter 0.
- **Education (select_multiple)**: Please select all relevant fields of study related to dentistry, such as Dental, English, Leadership, and Team Management.
- **Skills (select_multiple)**: Choose the skills you possess from the given options, such as Dental, English, Leadership, and Team Management.
- **Certifications (select_multiple)**: If you have any dental certifications, please select them from the given options, such as ADA and CE.
- **Medical History (note)**: This field is for any medical conditions or history that you would like to share with us. Please write a brief description of any conditions that may be relevant to your work in the dental field.
- **Emergency Contact (select_multiple)**: Please select the emergency contact information that we should have in case of an emergency.
- **Upload Files (time)**: If you have any files that you would like to upload to support your application, please do so here.
- **Upload Documents (select_one)**: You can upload one document that best represents your qualifications for the position, such as a CV or ID.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your form before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
- You can save your progress and come back to the form at a later time if you need to.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
