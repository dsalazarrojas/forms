# media_work_sample_submission_employment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from job applicants for a media work sample submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the following steps:

1. Fill in the "New Job Application" section with your job application details.
2. Upload your work samples in the "Uploaded Work Samples" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Job Application** (`title`, `text`, not required):
	+ Please fill in this section with your job application details. This field is not mandatory.
* **Job Application Form** (`title`, `text`, not required):
	+ This field is not further described. You may want to provide a brief description of your job application.
* **Uploaded Work Samples** (`title`, `text`, not required):
	+ Upload your work samples in this field. Please ensure they meet the required file formats and sizes, if specified.
	
## Tips
* Please ensure you are submitting your work samples for the correct job application.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid any issues.
* This form is not suitable for all types of job applications, so you might need to explore other templates for more complex use cases.
