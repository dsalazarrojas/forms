# Fancy Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your contact and professional details for the job application process. Please fill out the form accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Attach your resume or provide a link to your resume.
3. Select your desired position and level of experience.
4. If you want to submit your application, select "Submit". If you want to preview your application before submitting, select "Preview".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification documents. Please enter your name in the format "First Name Last Name".
* **Email**: Your email address where you can be reached for further communication. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
* **Resume**: Attach your resume as a document or provide a link to it. This is an opportunity to showcase your professional experience and skills.
* **Address**: Enter your home or work address where we can contact you. This is not a required field, but it helps us to contact you more efficiently.
* **Phone**: Your phone number where you can be reached. This is not a required field, but it helps us to contact you more efficiently.
* **Position**: Select your desired position (Intern, Full time, Part time) that matches your skills and experience.
* **Salary**: Enter your expected salary range. Please be realistic and take into account the market rate for your position.
* **Skills**: Enter your relevant skills and experience. Please be specific and concise.
* **Experience**: Select your level of experience (1-2 years, 3-5 years, 6-10 years) that matches your skills and qualifications.
* **Submit**: Select this option to submit your application. If you want to preview your application before submitting, select "Preview".

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Ensure your email address and phone number are up-to-date and valid.
* Attach a professional-looking resume that highlights your skills and experience.
* Be honest about your experience and skills, and select the options that match your qualifications.
* Review your application carefully before submitting to ensure it is accurate and complete.
