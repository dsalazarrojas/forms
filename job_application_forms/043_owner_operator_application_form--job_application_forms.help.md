# Owner Operator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Owner Operator Application Form is designed to collect basic information from applicants for an owner operator position. The form aims to gather essential details about the applicant's personal and vehicle-related information to assess their eligibility and suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out your First Name and Last Name accurately, as they will be used for reference purposes.
2. Enter your Date of Birth in the format MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY.
3. Provide your Social Security Number (SSN) to verify your identity.
4. Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you for further information.
5. Enter a valid email address to receive updates and communication from us.
6. Fill out your address, which is essential for us to contact you or send information.
7. If you have a driver's license, please enter the number for our records.
8. If you own a vehicle, please fill out the Vehicle Make and Model for our reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your given name, as you would like it to appear on our records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your surname, as you would like it to appear on our records.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your birthdate in the format MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY.
* **SSN** (`ssn`, text, required): Provide your Social Security Number to verify your identity.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number to enable us to contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and communication from us.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address, which is necessary for us to contact you or send information.
* **Driver License Number** (`driver_license_number`, text, required): If you have a driver's license, enter the number for our records.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): If you own a vehicle, enter the make for our reference.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): If you own a vehicle, enter the model for our reference.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth evaluation process.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us using the provided email address.
* If you own a vehicle, please ensure that the Vehicle Make and Model fields are filled out correctly.
* This form is for your benefit, and we encourage you to provide clear and accurate information to help us assess your application.
