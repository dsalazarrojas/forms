# Beautician Job Application Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from applicants for a beautician position in our salon. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address.
2. Enter your Phone Number and Address, including your City and State.
3. Select your preferred Position Applying For and Availability.
4. Check if you have a Cosmetology License and specify its status.
5. If applicable, enter your License Number and License State.
6. Choose your Years of Experience.
7. Provide information about your Previous Salon or Employer.
8. Select all that apply for Your Specialties.
9. Check if you have a Portfolio and provide its Link if you have it.
10. Enter the reasons why you want to work in this salon.
11. Enter your Available Start Date.
12. Indicate if you can provide References.
13. Check if you attached a Resume.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is spelled.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is spelled.
* **Email Address**: Enter your active email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your active phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Address**: Enter your full address including house number and apartment number (if applicable).
* **City**: Enter the name of the city you live in.
* **State**: Enter the two-letter state code of the state you live in.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter the ZIP code of your postal address.
* **Position Applying For**: Select the position you are interested in applying for from the available options.
* **Availability**: Select your preferred availability, whether it is Full-Time, Part-Time, Weekends Only, or Flexible.
* **Do You Have a Cosmetology License**: Check yes if you have an active license and specify its status.
* **License Number**: Enter your license number if you have it.
* **License State**: Enter the state your license was issued in.
* **Years of Experience**: Choose your experience level from the given options.
* **Previous Salon or Employer**: Enter the name and address of your most recent employer or salon.
* **Your Specialties**: Select all the specialties you are proficient in.
* **Do You Have a Portfolio**: Check yes if you have a portfolio.
* **Portfolio Link**: Enter the URL of your portfolio if you have it.
* **Why Do You Want to Work Here**: Enter the reasons for your interest in this salon.
* **Available Start Date**: Enter the date you can start working with us.
* **References Available**: Check yes if you can provide references.
* **Resume Attached**: Check yes if you attached a resume.
