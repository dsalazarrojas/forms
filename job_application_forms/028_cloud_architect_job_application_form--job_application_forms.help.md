# Cloud Architect Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cloud Architect Job Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals applying for the Cloud Architect position. This form helps the hiring team assess the applicant's qualifications and fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your basic information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your work experience, including your current job title and years of experience in cloud roles.
3. List your relevant technical skills, such as programming languages and infrastructure tools.
4. Specify your areas of expertise, such as cloud security and infrastructure design.
5. Add any additional information or comments you'd like to share with the hiring team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your resume.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Provide your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Years of Cloud Architecture Experience** (`years_experience`, number, required): Enter the total years you have spent in cloud roles.
* **Total Years of IT Experience** (`total_it_experience`, number, required): Enter the total years you have spent in IT roles.
* **Current Job Title** (`current_role`, text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Cloud Platforms Experience** (`cloud_platforms`, select_multiple, required): Select the cloud platforms you have experience with (e.g., Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, etc.).
* **AWS Certifications** (`aws_certifications`, select_multiple, optional): Select any AWS certifications you hold.
* **Azure Certifications** (`azure_certifications`, select_multiple, optional): Select any Azure certifications you hold.
* **GCP Certifications** (`gcp_certifications`, select_multiple, optional): Select any GCP certifications you hold.
* **Programming Languages** (`programming_languages`, select_multiple, optional): Select any programming languages you are proficient with.
* **Infrastructure and DevOps Tools** (`infrastructure_tools`, select_multiple, optional): Select any infrastructure and DevOps tools you have experience with.
* **Areas of Specialization** (`expertise_areas`, select_multiple, required): Select areas where you specialize, such as cloud security or infrastructure design.
* **Highest Degree Earned** (`highest_degree`, select_one, required): Select the highest degree earned (e.g., high school diploma, bachelor's degree, etc.).
* **Field of Study** (`education_field`, text, optional): Enter the field of study for your highest degree.
* **Additional Information or Comments** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or comments you'd like to share with the hiring team.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the hiring team.
* This form is not meant to be a comprehensive evaluation, but it will help the hiring team assess your qualifications and fit for the role.
