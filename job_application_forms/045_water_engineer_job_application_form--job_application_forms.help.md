# Water Engineer Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Engineer Job Application Form is designed to collect information from job applicants for water engineering positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Job Title**, choosing from the predefined options or select "Other" if your job title doesn't fit into one of the options.
2. Provide your **Work Experience**, describing your relevant work experience in the field of water engineering.
3. Enter your **Education**, including any relevant degrees or certifications you have.
4. Enter your **Contact Information**, including your contact number and email.
5. Upload your **Resume**, if you have one available.
6. Describe your **Work History**, including any relevant work history you have.
7. Choose your **Job Preferences**, including any specific preferences you have for the job.
8. Enter your **Available Start Date** and **Available End Date**, if applicable.
9. Choose your **Required Skills**, if applicable.
10. Choose your **Work Schedule**, if applicable.
11. Choose your **Salary Range**, if applicable.
12. Choose your **Job Type**, if applicable.
13. Choose your **Job Category**, if applicable.
14. Choose your **Job Level**, if applicable.
15. Choose your **Work Experience Level**, if applicable.
16. Enter your **Job Status Update**, if applicable.
17. Enter your **Contact Name**, if applicable.
18. Enter your **Assigned User**, if applicable.
