# football_recruiting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The football_recruiting_form is designed to collect information from football players who are interested in being recruited by our team. The form is used to gather contact information, athletic background, and other relevant details about the player. Please note that this form is not a full employment application, but rather a way for us to learn more about potential recruits and their interests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section of the form, providing as much information as possible.
2. Select the correct options for each field, based on your experience and qualifications.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **football_recruiting_form_page_1**: This is the first page of the form, where you can provide a brief introduction about yourself and your interest in being recruited.
* **contact_information**: On this page, please select whether you have a contact person (e.g., parent, coach, or agent) who can be reached for further information. If yes, provide their name and contact details.
* **high_school_info**: Indicate whether you have any high school football experience. If yes, provide details about your position, achievements, and any notable games or tournaments played.
* **college_info**: Answer whether you have any college football experience. If yes, provide details about your position, achievements, and any notable games or tournaments played.
* **football_recruiting_experience**: Check if you have any football recruiting experience. If yes, describe your role in the recruiting process, and any notable successes or achievements.
* **athletic_stats**: Indicate your athletic statistics, such as height, weight, and any other relevant physical attributes.
* **references**: List any sports-related references, such as coaches, trainers, or other players who can vouch for your abilities.
* **football_recruiting_form_page_2**: This page is intended for additional information about yourself, but it appears to be a duplicate of a previous page.
* **additional_info**: Provide any additional information that you think would be relevant to your recruitment, such as skills, interests, or accomplishments.
* **signature**: This field is not explicitly used in the current form structure, but it might be used in a future version for authentication or signature purposes.
* **football_recruiting_form_page_3** to **football_recruiting_form_page_16**: These pages appear to be duplicates of previous pages, but they might be intended for additional sections or fields in future versions of the form.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form, as it will help us better understand your background and qualifications.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to ask, and we will do our best to address them.
* Remember that this form is not a full employment application, but rather a way for us to learn more about potential recruits and their interests.
