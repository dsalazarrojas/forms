# Marketing Lead Generation Specialist Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a simple application form designed to collect basic contact information and skills relevant to a Marketing Lead Generation Specialist position within our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Application Information" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "lead_email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "lead_phone" field.
4. Describe your relevant experience in the "lead_experience" field.
5. Enter your level of education completed in the "lead_education" field.
6. Select your relevant education level (e.g., High School, Bachelor's, Master's, etc.) in the "lead_education_level" field.
7. Specify your availability for a lead generation role in the "lead_availability" field.
8. Choose your relevant skills from the "lead_skills" dropdown list.
9. Select the date you are available to start in the "lead_availability_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Information**: A field to enter a short description of yourself.
* **lead_name**: Please enter your name.
* **lead_email**: Your email address will be used to communicate with you.
* **lead_phone**: Enter a phone number we can use to contact you.
* **lead_experience**: Describe your relevant marketing experience, including any experience with lead generation or similar roles.
* **lead_education**: Specify your highest level of education completed.
* **lead_education_level**: Select the level of education you have completed (e.g., High School, Bachelor's, Master's, etc.).
* **lead_availability**: Specify your availability for a full-time or part-time lead generation role.
* **lead_skills**: Choose from a list of relevant skills, including Marketing Lead Generation, Lead Research, Lead Follow-up, Lead Conversion, and Lead Qualification.
* **lead_availability_date**: Select the date you are available to start in the role.
