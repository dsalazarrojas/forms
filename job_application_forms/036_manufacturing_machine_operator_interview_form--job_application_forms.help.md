# Manufacturing Machine Operator Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for candidates applying to the Manufacturing Machine Operator role. It is used to gather essential details about the candidate's background, availability, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form with your details to help us better understand your qualifications and schedule your interview.
2. Ensure you complete all the required fields to avoid any issues with your application.
3. If you have any questions or need help, do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name (Candidate Name)** (`text`, `required: false`):
	+ This field is used to capture your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ Please enter your name as it is written.
* **Job Title (Job Title)** (`text`, `required: false`):
	+ This field is for the title of your current or previous job.
	+ If you don't have a specific job title, you can enter "Not Applicable" if applicable.
* **Contact E-mail (Contact E-mail)** (`email`, `required: false`):
	+ This is the email address we will use to contact you for further communication.
	+ Please ensure it is a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone)** (`text`, `required: false`):
	+ This field is for your contact phone number.
	+ Please enter a number that we can use to contact you for further communication.
* **Employment Status (Employment Status)** (`select_one`, `required: false`):
	+ Choose your current employment status from the provided options (Employed, Unemployed, Retired, Disabled).
* **Years of Service (Years of Service)** (`number`, `required: false`):
	+ Enter the number of years you have been working as a Manufacturing Machine Operator or in a related field.
* **Availability (Availability)** (`select_multiple`, `required: false`):
	+ Select the days you are available to work from the provided options (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Education Level (Education Level)** (`select_multiple`, `required: false`):
	+ Choose your education level from the provided options (High School, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree, Doctoral Degree).
* **Relevant Courses (Relevant Courses)** (`text`, `required: false`):
	+ List any relevant courses or training that can help you excel in the role.
* **Employment History (Employment History)** (`text`, `required: false`):
	+ Briefly describe your previous work experience, especially in Manufacturing Machine Operator roles.
* **Relevant Work Experience (Relevant Work Experience)** (`text`, `required: false`):
	+ Describe any relevant work experience that demonstrates your ability to perform as a Manufacturing Machine Operator.

## Tips

* Be accurate and honest when providing your details.
* If you have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Completing this form will help us to better understand your application and schedule an interview at your preferred time.
