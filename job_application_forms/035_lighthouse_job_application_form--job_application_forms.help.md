# Lighthouse Job Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lighthouse Job Application Form is designed to collect relevant information from potential candidates for Lighthouse positions. This form will be reviewed by Lighthouse staff to determine the best candidate for the role.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out all the required fields. If a field is marked as "required", it's essential to fill it out to ensure your application can be properly evaluated.
2.  Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and relevant to your application.
3.  Be as specific as possible when providing information, especially for fields like "work experience" and "location."

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Applicant Information (Page 1)** 
    (`title`, `text`, `false`: not required)
    This is the first page of the form where you will provide your basic information.
    Please enter your name and any other details as specified on the page.
*   **Contact Information**
    (`contact_info`, `text`, `false`: not required)
    Fill in your contact information, including email address and phone number.
    This will help Lighthouse staff get in touch with you about your application.
*   **Job Title** 
    (`job_title`, `select_one`, `false`: not required)
    Choose the job title you are applying for from the provided list. If you're interested in multiple positions, feel free to list them all.
*   **Work Experience**
    (`experience`, `text`, `false`: not required)
    Describe your relevant work experience, including companies, positions, and job duties.
    Be specific and concise in your answer.
*   **Education**
    (`education`, `text`, `false`: not required)
    If relevant to the job, please specify your educational background.
*   **Availability**
    (`availability`, `text`, `false`: not required)
    Indicate your availability to work, including your preferred schedule or any time constraints.
*   **Location**
    (`location`, `select_multiple`, `false`: not required)
    Select all the locations where you would be available to work.
*   **Skills**
    (`skills`, `select_multiple`, `false`: not required)
    Indicate your skills, selecting all the relevant ones for the job.
*   **Resume**
    (`resume`, `text`, `false`: not required)
    Attach or paste your resume or CV to give a detailed overview of your qualifications.
*   **Notes**
    (`notes`, `note`, `false`: not required)
    If you'd like to add any additional information not covered in other fields, this is the place.
*   **Email**
    (`email`, `email`, `false`: not required)
    Enter your email address for easy contact.
*   **Phone**
    (`phone`, `text`, `false`: not required)
    Fill in your phone number for quick communication.
*   **Job Status**
    (`job_status`, `select_one`, `false`: not required)
    Indicate your current job status, e.g., Full-time, Part-time, or Freelance.
*   **Salary Range** 
    (`salary_range`, `number`, `false`: not required)
    If relevant, indicate your expected salary range.
