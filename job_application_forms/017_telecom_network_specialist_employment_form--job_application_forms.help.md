# Telecom Network Specialist Employment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from candidates for job openings, particularly for Telecom Network Specialist positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Candidate Name** in the designated field.
2. Select the **Job Title** that you're applying for, from the provided options.
3. If the **Job Title** you're applying for is not listed, enter it in the **Other Job Title (if not listed above)** field.
4. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** contact information.
5. Upload your **Resume (upload)**.
6. Provide details on **Job References**, **Experience**, **Education**, **Skills**, and **Certifications** by selecting the relevant options.
7. Specify your **Availability**, **Notice Period**, and **Job Location**.
8. Select the **Job Type**, **Job Status**, **Job Category**, and **Job Category 2** options as relevant.
9. Fill in the **Job Description**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (text): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Job Title** (text): Select the job title for which you're applying.
* **Job Title 2** (select_one): Select if the job title you're applying for is not listed in the previous field.
* **Other Job Title (if not listed above)** (text): Enter the job title if it's not listed in the previous field.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Resume (upload)** (text): Upload your resume in a format that can be read by our system.
* **Job References** (note): Provide details on your job references, if applicable.
* **Experience** (select_multiple): Select the relevant experience you have for the job.
* **Education** (select_multiple): Select the level of education you have for the job.
* **Skills** (select_multiple): Select the skills relevant to the job.
* **Certifications** (select_multiple): Select the relevant certifications you hold.
* **Availability** (date): Specify your start and end dates of availability for the job.
* **Notice Period** (time): Specify your notice period for the job.
* **Job Location** (text): Enter the location where you can work.
* **Job Type** (select_one): Select the job type that fits your needs.
* **Job Status** (select_one): Select the status of your job (Active or Inactive).
* **Job Status Other** (text): Enter the status of your job if not listed above.
* **Job Type 2** (select_one): Select the job type 2 that fits your needs.
* **Job Type Other** (text): Enter the job type 2 if not listed above.
* **Job Category** (select_multiple): Select the job category that fits your needs.
* **Job Category 2** (select_one): Select the job category 2 that fits your needs.
* **Job Category Other** (text): Enter the job category 2 if not listed above.
* **Job Description** (text): Enter a brief description of your job and how it relates to the position you're applying for.
