# Basic Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Basic Job Application Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect personal, address, position, education, experience, skills, and qualification information from job applicants. This form helps employers and hiring managers to evaluate and filter applicants efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1.  Complete the personal information section by providing your first name, last name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and available start date.
2.  Provide your street address, city, state, and ZIP/Postal code in the address information section.
3.  In the position information section, specify the job position you are applying for, your available start date, and the type of employment you are seeking.
4.  In the education section, provide information about your highest level of education, school or university attended, field of study or major, and graduation date.
5.  In the experience section, describe your years of relevant experience and list your previous employers (last 3).
6.  In the skills and qualifications section, provide information about your key skills, professional certifications or licenses, and software or technical skills.
7.  Upload your resume or CV and cover letter in the document upload section (if required).
8.  Finally, confirm your willingness to undergo background checks and certify that the information provided is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is spelled on your identification documents.
*   **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is spelled on your identification documents.
*   **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
*   **Phone number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
*   **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   **Street address** (`street_address`, text, required): Enter your street address where you can be contacted.
*   **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
*   **State/Province** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state or province where you reside.
*   **ZIP/Postal code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
*   **Position applied for** (`position_applied`, text, required): Enter the job position you are applying for.
*   **Available start date** (`available_start_date`, date, required): Enter your available start date in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   **Type of employment seeking** (`employment_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of employment you are seeking (Full-Time, Part-Time, Temporary, Internship, Seasonal).
*   **Highest level of education** (`highest_education`, select_one, required): Choose your highest level of education (High School, Some College, Associate Degree, Bachelor Degree, Master Degree, Ph.D., Other).
*   **School/University attended** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your school or university.
*   **Field of study or major** (`field_of_study`, text, optional): Enter your field of study or major.
*   **Graduation date** (`graduation_date`, date, optional): Enter your graduation date in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   **Years of relevant experience** (`years_experience`, number, required): Enter your years of relevant experience.
*   **List previous employers (last 3)** (`previous_employers`, text, optional): List your previous three employers.
*   **Key skills relevant to position** (`key_skills`, text, required): List your key skills relevant to the position applied for.
*   **Professional certifications or licenses** (`certifications`, text, optional): List any professional certifications or licenses you hold.
*   **Software or technical skills** (`software_skills`, text, optional): List your software or technical skills.
*   **Resume or CV file** (`resume_cv`, text, required): Upload your resume or CV file.
*   **Cover letter** (`cover_letter`, text, optional): Upload a cover letter (if required).
*   **Will to undergo background checks** (`willing_background_check`, select_one, required): Choose whether you are willing to undergo a background check.
*   **Certify all information is accurate and complete** (`application_agreement`, select_one, required): Choose whether you agree or disagree that the provided information is accurate and complete.
