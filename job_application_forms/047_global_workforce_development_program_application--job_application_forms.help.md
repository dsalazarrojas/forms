# Global Workforce Development Program Application - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect necessary information for participant profiling and program administration. Please follow the instructions to complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on each field to fill in your details accurately. Make sure to double-check your information before submission.
2. For fields with specific format requirements (e.g. Participant Phone, Program Start Date), ensure you enter the data in the specified format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Participant Name
Participant Name is required to be filled out accurately to ensure participant identification.

*   **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it should be spelled correctly.

### 2. Participant Email
Participant Email is a crucial part of the participant's profile and should be accurate and up-to-date.

*   **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address exactly as it is registered on other systems (e.g. your email address on your university, social media, etc.).

### 3. Participant Phone
The participant phone number should be filled out in an international format.

*   **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number in the format: `+1-234-567-8901` or `123 456 7890`.

### 4. Participant Address
Participant Address should be accurate to ensure smooth correspondence.

*   **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address for correspondence and other purposes.

### 5. Participant Career Goal
Describe your career aspirations.

*   **Participant Career Goal** (`career_goal`, `text`, optional): Describe what career goal you would like to achieve.

### 6. Program Type
Please indicate the type of program you are enrolling in.

*   **Program Type** (`program_type`, `text`, optional): Select the type of program you are applying for.

### 7. Program Start Date
Enter the date of the program start.

*   **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### 8. Training Provider
If applicable, specify the training provider.

*   **Training Provider** (`training_provider`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the training provider.

### 9. Eligibility Criteria
If applicable, specify your eligibility criteria.

*   **Eligibility Criteria** (`eligibility_criteria`, `text`, optional): Enter the eligibility criteria for the program.

### 10. Program Length
Enter the length of the program in months.

*   **Program Length** (`program_length`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of the program in months.

### 11. Participant Status
Specify your participant status.

*   **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, `text`, optional): Enter your current status in the program.

### 12. Participant Notes
Provide any additional notes about the participant.

*   **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any notes about the participant.

### 13. Program Status
Indicate the program status.

*   **Program Status** (`program_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the program as either `Pending`, `Approved`, or `Rejected`.

### 14. Form Submitted By
Specify who submitted this form.

*   **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, `select_one`, required): Select who submitted this form as either `User 1`, `User 2`, or `User 3`.
