# Payment Processing Engineer Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect application information for a Payment Processing Engineer position. It is a crucial step in the application process for potential candidates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out your Personal Details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2.  Provide your Contact Details, such as your address and any other relevant contact information.
3.  List your Education, highlighting any relevant courses or training received.
4.  Describe your Work Experience, including any relevant positions or projects.
5.  List your relevant Skills, including experience with payment processing systems.
6.  Provide References, including names and contact information of professionals who can vouch for your skills and experience.
7.  Review your form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed.
8.  Click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Personal Details** (`payment_processing_engineer_job_application_form`, `text`, required):
    This section includes essential information such as your name, email, and phone number. Please ensure you use your real name and email address for proper contact.
*   **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required):
    Provide your address, and any other relevant contact information for further communication.
*   **Education** (`education`, `text`, optional):
    This is where you can highlight any relevant courses or training you've received.
*   **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, optional):
    Describe any relevant positions or projects you've worked on, especially those involving payment processing.
*   **Skills** (`payment_processing_engineer_job_application_form_skills`, `text`, required):
    List your relevant skills, including experience with payment processing systems.
*   **References** (`references`, `text`, optional):
    Provide names and contact information of professionals who can vouch for your skills and experience.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required):
    Once all necessary fields are completed, click this button to submit your application.
