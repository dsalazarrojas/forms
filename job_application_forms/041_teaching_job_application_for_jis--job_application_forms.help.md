# Teaching Job Application For JIS - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect your personal details and other relevant information to process your application for a teaching position at Japan International School (JIS).

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, follow these steps:
	1. Fill out your name and contact information in the first few fields.
	2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
	3. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
	4. Choose your marital status from the dropdown menu.
	5. If you have any medical conditions, enter them in the "Medical Conditions" field.
	6. Provide a personal message in the "Personal Message" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address that will be used for communication.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number that we can reach you at.
* **Position Applied**: Enter the position you are applying for or the one you are interested in.
* **Marital Status**:
	+ Choose from: Married, Single, Widowed, Divorced
* **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions that we should know about, enter them in this field.
* **Personal Message**: If you would like to include any additional information about yourself, enter it in this field.

## Tips

* Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us at JIS.
