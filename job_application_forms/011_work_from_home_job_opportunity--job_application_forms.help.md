# work_from_home_job_opportunity - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect job opportunity information from job seekers to facilitate a successful work-from-home job application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Double-check that all required fields are filled out before submission.
3. Ensure that the information provided matches the job requirements and employer's expectations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the job title you are applying for.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the job you are applying for, including your qualifications and experience.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required): Select the category that best matches your job type (e.g., Job Application Forms, Employment Forms).
* **Job URL** (`job_url`, text, required/optional): Enter the URL of the job posting or company website.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_one, required): Select the payment frequency you are expecting (Weekly or Monthly).
* **Payment Rate** (`payment_rate`, number, required): Enter the amount you are expecting per payment period (e.g., $XX.XX per week).
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, text, required): List the job requirements and qualifications you have (e.g., skills, experience).
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address for communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Contact URL** (`contact_url`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional contact information or website URL.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required/optional): Add any additional terms or conditions of the job offer (e.g., confidentiality agreements).
* **Apply Button Label** (`apply_button_label`, text, required/optional): Customize the text displayed on the apply button (e.g., "Apply Now").
* **Apply Button Text** (`apply_button_text`, text, required/optional): Enter the text displayed on the apply button.
* **Apply Button URL** (`apply_button_url`, text, required/optional): Enter the URL associated with the apply button.
* **Apply Button Link Text** (`apply_button_link_text`, text, required/optional): Customize the text displayed on the apply button link (e.g., "Learn More").
* **Survey URL** (`survey_url`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional survey information or URL.
* **Apply URL** (`apply_url`, text, required): Enter the URL where candidates can submit their application.
* **Apply Email** (`apply_email`, text, required/optional): Enter your email address for candidates to contact you.
* **Apply Phone** (`apply_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for candidates to contact you.
* **Apply Address** (`apply_address`, text, required): Enter your address for shipping or meeting purposes.
* **Apply Description** (`apply_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the job opportunity and how to apply.
* **Disclaimer** (`disclaimer`, text, required/optional): Add any additional disclaimers or terms and conditions.
* **Disclaimer URL** (`disclaimer_url`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional disclaimers or terms and conditions URL.
* **Disclaimer Text** (`disclaimer_text`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional disclaimers or terms and conditions text.
