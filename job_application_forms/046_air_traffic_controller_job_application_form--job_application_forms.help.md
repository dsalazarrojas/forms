# Air Traffic Controller Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Air Traffic Controller Job Application Form is a form used to collect information from applicants interested in applying for an ATC position. This form will help the hiring team assess your qualifications and suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form with your personal information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your citizenship status from the options provided.
3. Provide details about your ATC experience, including the type of facilities you have worked at.
4. Enter your CTO/ATC Certificate Number.
5. Confirm your current medical clearance class.
6. Indicate your highest level of education completed.
7. If you are willing to relocate, select the relevant option.
8. Confirm your availability to work shifts, including 24/7 operations.
9. Provide contact information for your references.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your official email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for communication.
* **Citizenship** (`citizenship`, `select_one`, required): Select your current citizenship status to determine eligibility for the position.
* **Years of ATC Experience** (`atc_experience`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have worked as an ATC.
* **Previous Facility Type** (`facility_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the types of facilities you have worked at as an ATC.
* **CTO / ATC Certificate Number** (`license_number`, `text`, required): Enter your current CTO/ATC Certificate Number.
* **Medical Clearance Class** (`medical_clearance`, `select_one`, required): Select your current medical clearance class to ensure you meet the necessary qualifications.
* **Education Level** (`education`, `select_one`, required): Select your highest level of education completed.
* **Will to Relocate** (`relocation`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your willingness to relocate for the position.
* **Shift Work Availability** (`shift_work`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your availability to work shifts, including 24/7 operations.
* **References** (`references`, `text`, optional): Provide the name and contact information for your professional references. 

Note: The form will guide the applicant through each section, and each field will have a brief description of what is expected from the applicant. Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to increase your chances of being considered for the position.
