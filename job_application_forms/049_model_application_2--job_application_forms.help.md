# Model Application 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a job application for a model position. It collects information about the applicant, including their name, age, birth date, model bio, email, address, phone number, category of expertise, assigned tool, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your name and age in the first two fields.
2. Enter your birth date in the "Birth Date" field.
3. In the "Model Bio" field, describe a brief introduction about yourself, including your experience and qualifications.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Provide your address in the "Address" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Select your category of expertise from the "Category" field. Choose one or multiple options that best describe your experience and skills.
8. Select the tool assigned to you from the "Assigned Tool" field. If you don't see your tool listed, you can choose "other" and enter your tool in the "Assigned Tool Other" field.
9. In the "Description" field, add any additional information about your application or project experience.
10. If required, select the "Output File" option that corresponds to your project submission. You can choose from the listed options or select "other" and enter your file name in the "Output File Other" field.
11. Finally, if required, select the "Output File Uploads" option that corresponds to your project submission. You can choose from the listed options or select "other" and enter your file name in the "Output File Uploads Other" field.

* **Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Age** (number, required/optional): Enter your age.
* **Birth Date** (date, required/optional): Enter your birth date.
* **Model Bio** (text, required/optional): Describe a brief introduction about yourself, including your experience and qualifications.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (text, required/optional): Provide your address.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Category** (select multiple, required/optional): Select one or multiple categories that best describe your experience and skills.
* **Assigned Tool** (select one, required/optional): Select the tool assigned to you or choose "other" and enter your tool in the "Assigned Tool Other" field.
* **Assigned Tool Other** (text, required/optional): Enter your tool name if you selected "other" in the "Assigned Tool" field.
* **Description** (note, required/optional): Add any additional information about your application or project experience.
* **Output File** (select one, required/optional): Select the "Output File" option that corresponds to your project submission. You can choose from the listed options or select "other" and enter your file name in the "Output File Other" field.
* **Output File Other** (text, required/optional): Enter your file name if you selected "other" in the "Output File" field.
* **Output File Uploads** (select multiple, required/optional): Select the "Output File Uploads" option that corresponds to your project submission. You can choose from the listed options or select "other" and enter your file name in the "Output File Uploads Other" field.
