# Financial Advisor Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your application for the Financial Advisor position at our company. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your application is considered for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through each section of the form and ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
2. Enter your contact details, including your email address and phone number, in the appropriate fields.
3. Provide detailed information about your employment history, including your job title, employment type, and employment status.
4. List your relevant financial skills and certifications.
5. Upload your resume for our review.
6. Click the "Submit" button to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Application Form (1)**: This is the main section of the form where you can enter a brief summary of your application.
* **Applicant Info (2)**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Details (3)**: Enter your email address and phone number to ensure we can contact you about your application.
* **Employment History (4)**: Describe your previous work experience, including job title, employment type, and employment status.
* **Employment History Item (5)**: If you have multiple employment history items, list them here.
* **Education Background (6)**: Describe your educational background, including degrees and institutions attended.
* **Work Experience (7)**: Describe your work experience, including job title, employment type, and employment status.
* **Financial Skills (8)**: List your relevant financial skills and experience.
* **Financial Certifications (9)**: List any relevant financial certifications you hold.
* **Upload Resume (10)**: Attach a copy of your resume for our review.
* **Submit (11)**: This is the button to send your application.
* **Email (13)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your application.
* **Email Label (14)**: Enter a label for your email address.
* **Phone (15)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your application.
* **Phone Label (16)**: Enter a label for your phone number.
* **Address (17)**: Enter your address so we can contact you about your application.
* **Address Label (18)**: Enter a label for your address.
* **Job Title (19)**: Enter your current or most recent job title.
* **Job Title Label (20)**: Enter a label for your job title.
* **Employment Type (21)**: Enter your employment type (e.g. full-time, part-time, freelance).
* **Employment Type Label (22)**: Enter a label for your employment type.
* **Employment Status (23)**: Enter your current employment status (e.g. employed, unemployed).
* **Employment Status Label (24)**: Enter a label for your employment status.
* **Job Type (25)**: Enter your job type (e.g. financial advisor, analyst).
* **Job Type Label (26)**: Enter a label for your job type.
* **Employment Status Details (27)**: Enter any additional details about your employment status.
* **Employment Status Details Label (28)**: Enter a label for your employment status details.
* **Employment History Years (29)**: Enter the number of years you have been employed in your current or most recent role.
* **Employment History Years Label (30)**: Enter a label for your employment history years.
