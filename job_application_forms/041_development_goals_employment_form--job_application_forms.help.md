# Development Goals Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Development Goals Employment Form is used to collect job application details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, select the job type that best suits your needs. Choose from the following options: *Automation* or *Manual*.
2. Then, select the status of the job. You can either choose *Active* or *Inactive*.
3. Next, provide a brief description of the job position. This will help match you with the right opportunities.
4. Choose the location where you would like to work. Enter the city, state, or region you prefer.
5. Indicate whether the job title you are applying for is *Yes* or *No*.
6. Indicate the level of the job you are interested in. Choose from *High* or *Low*.
7. Select the types of jobs you are interested in applying for. Choose from the options provided for *Job type 1*, *Job type 2*, and *Job type 3*.
8. Describe your professional and personal goals. In the *Professional goals* section, explain what your career aspirations are. In the *Personal goals* section, mention any personal objectives you have.
9. Provide your expected salary range. This will help match you with opportunities within your budget.
10. Indicate whether you are looking for a job in a specific category. Choose from *Yes* or *No*.
11. Indicate the industry you would like to work in. Select from *Yes* or *No*.
12. Indicate the sector you prefer. Choose from *Yes* or *No*.
13. Choose the job function you are interested in. Select from *Yes* or *No*.
14. Indicate your current employment status. Select from *Active* or *Inactive*.
15. Indicate the type of employment you are looking for. Choose from *Yes* or *No*.
16. Provide the date you would like to start employment. Choose a date from the calendar.
17. If you have already worked, indicate the date you started and ended employment.
18. Provide a description of your current education level.
19. Describe your work experience. This will help match you with opportunities that value your skills and experience.
20. List your key skills. This will help match you with opportunities that require your skills.

* **Job level**: Choose the level of the job you are interested in, selecting from *High* or *Low*.
* **Job type 1**, **Job type 2**, **Job type 3**: Select the types of jobs you are interested in applying for.
* **Professional goals**: Describe your career aspirations.
* **Personal goals**: Explain any personal objectives you have.
* **Salary range**: Enter your expected salary range.
* **Job category**: Indicate whether you are looking for a job in a specific category, choosing from *Yes* or *No*.
* **Job industry**: Indicate the industry you would like to work in, selecting from *Yes* or *No*.
* **Job sector**: Indicate the sector you prefer, choosing from *Yes* or *No*.
* **Job function**: Choose the job function you are interested in, selecting from *Yes* or *No*.
* **Employment status**: Indicate your current employment status, selecting from *Active* or *Inactive*.
* **Employment type**: Indicate the type of employment you are looking for, choosing from *Yes* or *No*.
* **Employment date**: Choose the date you would like to start employment.

* **Education**: Describe your current education level.
