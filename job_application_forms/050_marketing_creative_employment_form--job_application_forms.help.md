# Marketing Creative Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Creative Employment Form is used to collect information about marketing creative employment applications. It's designed to gather data about potential or current employees, including their contact information, employment status, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the respective fields.
2. Enter your contact number and portfolio URL if applicable.
3. Provide your resume URL if you have one.
4. Answer questions about your job position, job type, availability, employment status, and contract length.
5. Choose the correct job status and employment type.
6. Enter your employment status, contract length, and notice period.
7. Provide your salary and location information.
8. Select the department and job level you're applying for or currently working in.
9. Enter the size of your team.
10. Enter the name of the company you're applying from or currently working for.
11. Answer questions about whether you submitted from a certain location or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **marketing_creative_employment_form_data**: Enter a brief description of your marketing creative employment application.
* **applicant_name**: Enter your full name.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **contact_number**: Enter your contact number.
* **portfolio_url**: Enter the URL of your portfolio, if you have one.
* **resume_url**: Enter the URL of your resume, if you have one.
* **job_position**: Choose whether you're applying for or currently in a job position.
* **job_type**: Choose the type of job you're applying for or currently in.
* **availability**: Enter your availability date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **employment_status**: Choose your employment status (Active or Inactive).
* **contract_length**: Enter the length of your contract, for example, 3 months.
* **notice_period**: Enter the notice period, for example, 2 weeks.
* **salary**: Enter your salary information.
* **location**: Enter your location information.
* **job_status**: Choose your job status (Active or Inactive).
* **created_at**: This field is not editable and displays the date the form was created.
* **updated_at**: This field is not editable and displays the date the form was updated.
* **submitted_at**: This field is not editable and displays the date the form was submitted.
* **submitted_by**: This field is not editable and displays the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **employment_type**: Choose whether you're applying for or currently in full-time or part-time employment.
* **department**: Enter the name of the department you're applying for or currently working in.
* **job_level**: Choose the level of the job you're applying for or currently in (High or Low).
* **team_size**: Enter the size of your team.
* **company**: Enter the name of the company you're applying from or currently working for.
* **submitted_from**: Choose whether you submitted from the company or not.
