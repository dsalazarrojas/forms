# Farmer Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farmer Job Application Form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in farm or agricultural job positions. The form is used by employers to find suitable candidates for their job openings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "Applicant Name" and "Contact Email" fields.
2. Provide a detailed description of your work experience in the "Job Title" and "Job Description" fields.
3. Select your relevant job type (Farmer, Farmhand, Rancher, etc.) and job category (Crops, Livestock, Poultry, etc.).
4. Enter your available start and end dates, days, and hours per week.
5. Specify your salary expectations.
6. Provide your job location and any additional job references.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name**: Enter your full name as it should be listed on your job application.
* **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Job Title**: Describe the job or position you are applying for (e.g., "Farmhand", "Rancher", etc.).
* **Job Description**: Provide a detailed description of your work experience and relevant skills.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Work Experience**: Enter your available start and end dates for work, including any gaps in employment.
* **Job Start Date**: Enter the start date of your previous or current job.
* **Job End Date**: Enter the end date of your previous or current job.
* **Available Start Date**: Enter the date when you can start working.
* **Available End Date**: Leave this field blank if you are available full-time, or enter the date when your availability ends.
* **Available Days per Week**: Enter how many days per week you are available to work.
* **Available Hours per Week**: Enter how many hours per week you are available to work.
* **Salary**: Enter your expected salary for the job.
* **Job Location**: Enter the location where you are available to work (e.g., farm, ranch, etc.).
* **Job Type**: Select the type of job you are applying for (Farmer, Farmhand, Rancher, etc.).
* **Job Category**: Select the category of job you are applying for (Crops, Livestock, Poultry, etc.).
* **Job Duties**: Describe the specific duties you are responsible for in your previous or current job.
* **Education**: Select your highest level of education completed (High School, College, University, etc.).
* **Languages Spoken**: Select the languages you can speak (e.g., English, Spanish, French, etc.).
* **Languages Additional**: If you speak more languages not listed, enter them here.
* **Work Authorization**: Enter your work authorization date (if any).
* **Job References**: Enter the names and contact information of your previous or current job references.
* **Job References Additional**: If you have more job references not listed, enter them here.
* **Job Agreement**: Enter any specific job agreements or conditions you have.

Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your application is considered for the job.
