# Truck Farmer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Truck Farmer Application Form is designed to collect information from truck farmers who wish to participate in a program or initiative. This form is used to gather relevant details about the applicant, their truck, and their availability for uploads.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*) after the field label.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information in all fields.
3. If you have any experience, enter the number of years of experience you have.
4. Select the options for "Available For Uploads" and "Available For Downloads" that apply to you.
5. For "Vehicle Year" and "Vehicle Make", select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
6. You can upload any additional documents, videos, or audio files that you feel are relevant to your application.
7. Add any notes or comments you would like to share with the team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Truck Farmers Name**: The name of the truck farmer applying for the program or initiative. (`truck_farmers_name`, `text`, `required: false`)
*   **Vehicle Name**: The name of the vehicle the truck farmer is using. (`vehicle_name`, `text`, `required: false`)
*   **Truck Description**: A brief description of the truck, including its make, model, and any other relevant details. (`truck_description`, `text`, `required: false`)
*   **Upload Photo**: You can upload a photo of yourself or your truck to help us get to know you better. (`upload_photo`, `text`, `required: false`)
*   **Photo Caption**: A brief caption for the photo you uploaded. (`photo_caption`, `text`, `required: false`)
*   **Email**: The email address you can be reached at. (`email`, `email`, `required: false`)
*   **Phone**: Your phone number where you can be reached. (`phone`, `text`, `required: false`)
*   **Location**: The location where you are based. (`location`, `text`, `required: false`)
*   **Experience**: The number of years of experience you have as a truck farmer. (`experience`, `number`, `required: false`)
*   **Available For Uploads**: Select all the types of uploads you are available for, such as documents, videos, or audio files. (`available_for_uploads`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`)
*   **Available For Downloads**: Select all the types of downloads you are available for, such as photos or videos. (`available_for_downloads`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`)
*   **Vehicle Year**: The year of your vehicle, select from the dropdown menu. (`vehicle_year`, `select_one`, `required: true`)
*   **Vehicle Make**: The make of your vehicle, select from the dropdown menu. (`vehicle_make`, `select_one`, `required: true`)
*   **Vehicle Model**: The model of your vehicle. (`vehicle_model`, `text`, `required: false`)
*   **Upload Documents**: You can upload any additional documents relevant to your application. (`upload_documents`, `text`, `required: false`)
*   **Upload Videos**: You can upload any additional videos relevant to your application. (`upload_videos`, `text`, `required: false`)
*   **Upload Audio**: You can upload any additional audio files relevant to your application. (`upload_audio`, `text`, `required: false`)
*   **Upload Other**: You can upload any other files relevant to your application. (`upload_other`, `text`, `required: false`)
*   **Notes**: Any additional comments or notes you would like to share with the team. (`notes`, `text`, `required: false`)
