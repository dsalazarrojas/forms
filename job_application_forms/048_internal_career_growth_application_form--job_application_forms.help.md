# Internal Career Growth Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internal Career Growth Application Form is a tool for internal employees to express their interest in career growth opportunities within the company. This form helps to identify areas where employees would like to develop their skills and take on new challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are eligible to fill it out.
2. Fill out each page with your details and thoughts.
3. Make sure to answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
4. Submit your application to the designated authority.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Employee Details
* **Employee ID**: This is your employee ID number assigned to you by the company.
* **Job Title**: This is your current job title within the company.
* **Department**: This is the department where you currently work.
* **Job Level**: This is your current job level within the company.
* **Years of Service**: This is the number of years you have been working with the company.

### Page 2: Motivation
* **Why do you want to grow your career within the company?**: Write a brief explanation of why you are interested in career growth within the company.

### Page 3: Skills
* **Do you have a particular skill you would like to develop?**: Choose "Yes" if you have a specific skill you'd like to develop, and "No" otherwise.
* **Do you have a particular area of interest for skill development?**: If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, list a specific area of interest.

### Page 4: Resume
* **Attach your current resume**: This is the place to attach your current resume, which will be reviewed as part of your application.

### Page 5: Employee Motivations
* **What motivates you to grow your career?**: Write a brief explanation of what motivates you to grow your career within the company.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting your application.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, do not hesitate to ask for help.
* Submit your application to the designated authority for review.
