# summer_internship_application_form_ceos_of_tomorrow - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in a summer internship position. It is intended for individuals to provide their details and qualifications for review by the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out each page of the form carefully.
- Ensure all required fields are completed.
- Provide honest and accurate information in each field.
- Double-check your responses before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Program Information** (`text`, optional): Share some details about the internship program you are interested in.
- **Contact Details** (`text`, optional): Provide your contact information, including your full name, email address, and any other relevant contact details.
- **Guardian Information** (`text`, optional): If you are under 18, please provide the contact information of a guardian or parent.
- **Availability** (`text`, optional): Share your availability for the internship, including the dates and times you are available.
- **Quick Quiz** (`text`, optional): This is a short question and answer section for you to demonstrate your knowledge and skills. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the organization.
- The form will be reviewed and processed upon submission.
