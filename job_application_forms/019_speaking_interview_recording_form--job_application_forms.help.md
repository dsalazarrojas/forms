# Speaking Interview Recording Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speaking Interview Recording Form is designed to record and document speaking interviews with candidates for potential job positions. This form helps in gathering crucial information, ensuring that all key points are addressed and noted during the interview process.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety.
2. Ensure that you have the necessary information and files prior to filling out the form.
3. Review the form carefully to ensure all required and optional fields are filled out accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Interview Questions** (`interview_questions`, text, optional): This field is for taking notes on the questions asked during the interview. You can enter any questions you asked or any other relevant information.
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the candidate you interviewed.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title or position the candidate is applying for.
* **Date Recorded** (`date_recorded`, date, optional): Enter the date when the interview was recorded.
* **Time Recorded** (`time_recorded`, time, optional): Enter the time when the interview was recorded.
* **Duration In Minutes** (`duration_in_minutes`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the interview in minutes.
* **Recording File** (`recording_file`, select_one, optional): Select the file for the recording from the provided options. If no file is selected, this field will be marked as incomplete.
* **Recording Notes** (`recording_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes on the recording.
* **Interviewer Name** (`interviewer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the interviewer.
* **Recorded By** (`recorded_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who recorded the interview.
* **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who reviewed the interview.
* **Review Notes** (`review_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional review notes on the interview.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the candidate.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the candidate.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, text, optional): This field is currently not used but is a placeholder for future file upload functionality. Do not fill it out.
