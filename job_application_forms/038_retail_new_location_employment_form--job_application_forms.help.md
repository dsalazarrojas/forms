# Retail New Location Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail New Location Employment Form is a comprehensive form designed to gather information about you and your employment preferences for a new retail location. This form is used to assess your qualifications, availability, and experience to match you with the best possible job opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, making sure to provide your current contact information and employment details.
2. Answer each question truthfully, as your responses will be used to determine your eligibility for employment with our company.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Please enter a valid email address that you check frequently, as we will use this to communicate with you.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Please enter a phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Available to start** (time, required): Please select a specific time when you are available to start work.
* **Availability** (text, optional): If you have any specific availability constraints or preferences, please provide them here.
* **Availability Date** (date, optional): If you have a specific date when you are available to start work, please provide it here.
* **Employment Status** (select_one, required): Please select your current employment status from the options provided.
* **Job Title** (text, required): Please enter the job title you are applying for.
* **Work Experience** (text, required): Please provide a brief description of your relevant work experience.
* **Availability Period** (select_multiple, required): Please select the periods you are available to work from the options provided.
* **Available Days** (select_multiple, required): Please select the days you are available to work from the options provided.
* **Days off** (text, optional): If you have any days off that you would like to take, please provide them here.
* **Emergency Contact info** (text, optional): If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name and relationship to you.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (text, optional): Please describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text, optional): Please provide the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (email, optional): Please provide the email of your emergency contact.
* **References** (text, optional): If you have any professional references, please provide their names and contact information.
* **Referee 1** (text, optional): Please provide the name of your first referee.
* **Contact Number 1** (text, optional): Please provide the phone number of your first referee.
* **Contact Email 1** (email, optional): Please provide the email of your first referee.
* **Contact Name 2** (text, optional): Please provide the name of your second referee.
* **Contact Number 2** (text, optional): Please provide the phone number of your second referee.
* **Contact Email 2** (email, optional): Please provide the email of your second referee.
* **Job Title 2** (text, optional): Please provide the job title of your referee 2.

## Tips
* Please make sure to attach any required documents or materials with your application.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.

Note that all fields are required unless otherwise specified. Please ensure that you have completed all the necessary information before submitting your application.
