# Scratch Studio Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from applicants about their studio name, contact information, experience, and availability to help the studio manager select the best candidate for the position.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Provide a valid email address for communication.
3. Attach a resume that highlights your relevant experience and skills.
4. Write a cover letter that explains your interest in the position and why you're a good fit for the studio.
5. Answer the questions about your availability and contact information.
6. Select the relevant experience and skills that match the studio requirements.
7. Explain why you're interested in the Scratch Studio Manager position and why you're a good fit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Studio Name** (`studio_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the studio you're applying for.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required): Attach a resume that highlights your relevant experience and skills.
* **Cover Letter** (`cover_letter`, text, required): Write a cover letter explaining your interest in the position and why you're a good fit for the studio.
* **Job Reference** (`job_reference`, select_multiple, required): Check if you have a job reference that we can contact.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, required): Enter your availability for the position, including start and end dates.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Provide your contact information, including phone number and address.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): List any relevant skills you have that match the studio requirements.
* **Previous Studio Experience** (`previous_studio_experience`, text, optional): Explain any relevant experience you have with other studios.
* **Why Scratch?** (`why_scratch`, text, optional): Explain why you're interested in Scratch and why you're a good fit.
* **Why Studio Manager?** (`why_studio_manager`, text, optional): Explain why you're interested in the studio manager position and why you're a good fit.

## Tips

* Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness.
* Attach relevant documents, such as your resume and cover letter.
* Be specific when providing information about your experience and skills.
* Explain why you're interested in the Scratch Studio Manager position and why you're a good fit.
