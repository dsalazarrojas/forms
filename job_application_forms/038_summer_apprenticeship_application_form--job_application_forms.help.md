# Summer Apprenticeship Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals applying for a summer apprenticeship position. The collected information will be used to determine eligibility and suitability for the program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Determine which options are available to you by selecting from the dropdown menu on page 1. This will help us understand your availability for the apprenticeship.
2. Enter your first name in the "first name" field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your last name in the "last name" field. This is a required field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "date of birth" field. This is a required field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone number" field. This is an optional field.
6. Enter your email address in the "email" field. This is an optional field.
7. Enter your job title in the "job title" field. This is an optional field.
8. Enter a brief description of your work experience in the "work experience" field. This is an optional field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Availability** (Page 1, Select Multiple): Select the options that best match your availability for the summer apprenticeship program.
* **First Name** (Field 1, Text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Field 2, Text): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (Field 3, Date): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number** (Field 4, Text): If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Email** (Field 5, Email): If you have an email address, enter it here.
* **Job Title** (Field 6, Text): If you have a job title, enter it here.
* **Work Experience** (Field 7, Text): Enter a brief description of your work experience, if applicable.

## Tips

* Please answer all required fields (first name, last name, date of birth).
* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure a smooth review process.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact us at [support email/phone number].
