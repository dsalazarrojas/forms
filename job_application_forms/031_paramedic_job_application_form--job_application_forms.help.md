# Paramedic Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paramedic Job Application Form is designed to collect information about your experience, qualifications, and availability for potential positions within our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your first and last name, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide the first name, last name, and relationship of your emergency contact.
3. Select your level of medical certifications and experience.
4. Enter your job title and job description.
5. Enter your availability and available time.
6. Enter your address, city, state, and zip code.
7. Choose your medical group affiliation (e.g., American Heart Association, American Red Cross).
8. Enter any additional certifications or licenses you hold.
9. Select your submit option to finish the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (id: 1, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (id: 2, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (id: 3, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (id: 4, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (id: 5, `text`, required: false): Enter the first name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (id: 6, `text`, required: false): Enter the last name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (id: 7, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the relationship of your emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Friend, Spouse, Child, Other).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (id: 8, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Certifications** (id: 9, `text`, required: false): Enter any medical certifications you hold.
* **Years of Experience** (id: 10, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of years you have been practicing as a paramedic.
* **Job Title** (id: 11, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Job Description** (id: 12, `note`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your job.
* **Availability** (id: 13, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you are available to start working.
* **Available Time** (id: 14, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you are available to work.
* **Address** (id: 15, `text`, required: false): Enter your address.
* **City** (id: 16, `text`, required: false): Enter your city.
* **State** (id: 17, `text`, required: false): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (id: 18, `text`, required: false): Enter your zip code.
* **Medical Group** (id: 19, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your medical group affiliation (e.g., American Heart Association, American Red Cross, Other).
* **Certification Name** (id: 20, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of any additional certifications you hold.
* **Certification Date** (id: 21, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you obtained any additional certifications.
* **License Number** (id: 22, `text`, required: false): Enter your license number.
* **License State** (id: 23, `text`, required: false): Enter the state your license was issued in.
* **Expiration Date** (id: 24, `date`, required: false): Enter the date your license expires.
* **Submit** (id: 25, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Submit" to complete the form.
