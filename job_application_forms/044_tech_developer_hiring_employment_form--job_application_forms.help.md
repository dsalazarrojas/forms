# Tech Developer Hiring Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Tech Developer Hiring Employment Form is designed to gather information from tech developers who are interested in working with us. This form is used to collect contact and employment details, as well as your resume.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Make sure your resume is up-to-date and relevant to the position you're applying for.
3. Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
4. Once you've completed the form, submit it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Address**: Enter your current address for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Resume Upload**: If you have an uploaded resume, please provide it for our review.
* **Resume Link**: If you don't have an uploaded resume, you can provide a link to your online resume.

## Tips

* Make sure to check your form submission for errors before submitting.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you're having trouble with the form, contact us at [your contact info].
