# Flight Attendant Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Flight Attendant Job Application Form is used for applicants to apply for a flight attendant position. It helps us assess your qualifications and experience for the role. Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly to provide us with the necessary information to review your application.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form by following the step-by-step instructions below.
2.  Enter your name, email, phone number, and date of birth in the corresponding fields.
3.  Select your relevant experience and availability from the drop-down menus.
4.  List your skills and relevant documents in the provided text fields.
5.  Provide two professional references with their contact information.
6.  Review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name (1)**: Please enter your full name as you would like it to appear on your uniform and any company materials.
*   **Email (2)**: Enter your professional email address, where you would like to receive communications from us.
*   **Phone (3)**: Enter your work or contact phone number.
*   **Date of Birth (4)**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
*   **Address (5)**: Enter your permanent address.
*   **Experience (6)**: Select all relevant experience you have from the drop-down menu:
    *   **High School Diploma**: High school diploma or equivalent.
    *   **College Diploma**: College diploma or degree.
    *   **Flight Attendant Experience**: Relevant flight attendant experience.
*   **Availability (7)**: Choose how often you are available to work:
    *   **Full-Time**: Available to work full-time.
    *   **Part-Time**: Available to work part-time.
    *   **Other**: Select other if you are not available full-time or part-time.
*   **Skills (8)**: List any relevant skills you have, such as first aid, language proficiency, etc.
*   **Documents (9)**: Select which documents you have:
    *   **ID**: Government-issued ID.
    *   **Passport**: A valid passport.
    *   **Driver's License**: A valid driver's license.
*   **References (10)**: Provide two professional references with their name, job title, and contact information.

Note: This form is not a substitute for an in-person interview. If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to contact us.
