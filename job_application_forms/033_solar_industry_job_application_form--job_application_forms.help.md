# Solar Industry Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to walk you through the Solar Industry Job Application Form, which is used to collect information from individuals interested in job opportunities in the solar industry. 

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Type**: This field allows you to select the type of contact that best describes your relationship with the solar industry (Primary, Secondary, or Other).
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title in the solar industry.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company you are currently working with or have previously worked with.
* **Job Duration**: Specify the length of your job duration in the solar industry.
* **Job Description**: Write a brief description of your job responsibilities and experiences.
* **Institution**: Enter the name of the institution where you studied.
* **Field of Study**: Mention the field of study you are familiar with.
* **Graduate Year**: Enter your graduation year.
* **Education Level**: Select your highest level of education attained (High School Diploma, Bachelor, Master, or Doctorate).
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Address**: Enter your home address.
* **Nationality**: Select your nationality (American, British, or Other).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender**: Select one or multiple gender identities that apply to you.
* **Currently Employed**: Indicate whether you are currently employed or looking for employment.
* **Employment Type**: Select the type of employment you are looking for (Full Time, Part Time, or Freelance).
* **Job Status**: Indicate your job status and select one or multiple job types that apply to you.
* **Resume**: Upload a resume that highlights your qualifications and experiences.
* **Cover Letter**: Upload a cover letter that explains your interest in the solar industry and the job opportunities available.
* **References**: Provide references that can speak to your work experience and qualifications.
* **Portfolio**: Add any relevant portfolio or work samples that showcase your expertise.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to us for clarification.
* The more information you provide, the better we can understand your qualifications and fit for potential job openings.
