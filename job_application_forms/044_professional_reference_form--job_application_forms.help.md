# Professional Reference Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Professional Reference Form is designed to collect information about a job applicant's professional references. This form is intended to gather detailed contact information and feedback from individuals who can vouch for the applicant's work experience and skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Ensure you are filling out the form for a valid job applicant.
2. Enter the job applicant's ID number (Job Application ID) on page 1.
3. Fill out the applicant's name, email, phone number, professional reference, and any additional feedback on pages 2-6.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Applicant ID** (`job_applicant_id`, number, Required): Enter the job applicant's unique ID number.
* **Name** (`name`, text, Optional): Enter the job applicant's full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Required): Enter the job applicant's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter the job applicant's phone number.
* **Professional Reference** (`professional_reference`, text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the professional reference's relationship with the job applicant.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the job applicant.
* **Job Application** (`job_application`, text, Optional): Enter any additional information about the job application.
