# Apply For A Temp Position Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Apply For A Temp Position Form is designed to gather information from applicants for temporary positions. The form is divided into several sections, including Applicant Information, Position Information, Work Experience, and References. The sections are designed to gather relevant information about the applicant's contact details, preferred temporary duration, and relevant skills and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
3. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
4. **Current Address**: Enter your current address.
5. **Available to start immediately?**: Select "True" if you are available to start immediately, or select "No - specify date" if you need a specific start date.
6. **Position Type Applying For**: Select the type of position you are applying for from the options provided (Administrative, Sales, Customer Service, Warehouse, Production, or Other).
7. **Preferred Temporary Duration**: Select your preferred temporary duration from the options provided (1-3 weeks, 1 month, 2-3 months, 3-6 months, or Open to discussion).
8. **Years of Work Experience**: Enter the number of years of work experience you have.
9. **Relevant Skills**: Enter the relevant skills you possess for the position.
10. **Computer Skills**: Select the computer skills you have from the options provided (Microsoft Word, Microsoft Excel, Outlook, Data entry, or Other).
11. **Languages Spoken**: Enter the languages you speak.
12. **Certifications and Licenses**: Enter any relevant certifications or licenses you hold.
13. **Reference Name**: Enter the name of your reference.
14. **Reference Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of your reference.
