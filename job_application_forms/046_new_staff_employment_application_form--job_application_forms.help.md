## new_staff_employment_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employment information from new staff applicants, ensuring a seamless hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the **Job Title**: Choose the job title that you are applying for by selecting from the available options (**Manager**, **Assistant Manager**, **Director**).
2.  Choose your **Work Experience**: Select your level of work experience from the available options (**1-3 years**, **4-6 years**, **7-9 years**, **10+ years**).
3.  Select your **Job Type**: Choose whether you are applying for a **Full-time**, **Part-time**, or **Contract** position.
4.  Provide a brief **Job Description**: Describe your role and responsibilities in a concise manner.
5.  Specify your **Salary Expectation**: Clearly state your expected salary.
6.  Choose your **Employment Status**: Select whether you are **Active**, **Inactive**, or **Retired**.
7.  Enter your **Contact Number**: Provide your contact phone number.
8.  Enter your **Contact Email**: Enter your professional email address.
9.  Enter your **Address**: Provide your address for communication purposes.
10. Select your **Job Location**: Choose where you are willing to work (**Remote**, **Office**).
11. Enter your **Date of Birth**: Select your date of birth in the required format.
12. Enter your **Date of Hire**: If applicable, select the date you are available to start working.
13. Attach your **Resume**: Attach your resume or CV as proof of your qualifications.
14. Attach your **Employment Document**: Attach any relevant employment documents, such as a letter of recommendation or a contract.
15. Choose your **Employment Agreement**: Select whether you have accepted or declined the employment offer.
16. Enter your **Job Reference**: Provide any relevant job references or information.
17. Select your **Education Level**: Choose your highest level of education completed (**Bachelor**, **Master**, **PhD**).
18. Select your **Employment Type**: Choose whether you are applying for a **Full-time**, **Part-time**, or **Contract** position.
19. Enter your **Work Hours**: If applicable, select your work hours.
20. Enter your **Start Workday**: If applicable, select your start workday.
21. Enter your **End Workday**: If applicable, select your end workday.
22. Enter your **Break Duration**: If applicable, specify your break duration.
23. Select your **Job Type**: Choose the type of job you are applying for (**Sales**, **Service**, **Management**).
24. Enter your **Submit Form**: Review and submit the form.
