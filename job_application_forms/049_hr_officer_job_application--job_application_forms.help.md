# HR OfficercareerApplication - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "HR OfficercareerApplication" form is designed to collect basic information from job applicants to facilitate the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin with "Basic Information" and provide your name, email, and phone number.
2. On the "Contact Information" page, enter your address and any other relevant contact details.
3. The "Qualification Information" page is where you can provide any relevant education, skills, or qualifications.
4. "Work Experience" is where you can list your previous work history, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.
5. "Education" should include details about your educational background, including institutions and degrees earned.
6. Finally, "References" is where you can provide contact information for professional references.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Basic Information
* **Name**: Your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email**: Your email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact number where you can be reached for further information.

### Contact Information
* **Address**: Your current address or the address you'd like to be reached at.
* **Any other contact details**: Any other information you feel is relevant or important for us to know.

### Qualification Information
* **Qualification details**: Any relevant education, skills, or qualifications you'd like to highlight.

### Work Experience
* **Job Title**: Your previous job title, role or position.
* **Company Name**: The company where you worked.
* **Dates of Employment**: Start and end dates of your employment.

### Education
* **Institution Name**: The name of the institution where you earned your degree or qualifications.
* **Degree(s) Earned**: A list of degrees or qualifications earned.

### References
* **Reference Name**: The name of your professional reference.
* **Contact Information**: A valid email or phone number for your reference.

## Tips
- Please ensure you provide accurate and honest information.
- Be concise and clear in your answers.
- Do not leave any blank fields as they are marked as required.
- If a field is not relevant to you, do not hesitate to mention it so we can review your application correctly.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
