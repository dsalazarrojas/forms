# Apply For A Position Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you apply for a job position. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information about yourself, and you will be able to submit your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the application process.
2. Select the position title you are applying for.
3. Fill out each field with accurate and complete information.
4. Review your application before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Date of application submission.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Legal first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Legal last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Primary email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Contact phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Full mailing address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): City of residence.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): State or province.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Postal or zip code.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, required): Specific position you are applying for.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Department of interest.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_one, required): Type of employment sought (Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract, Temporary, Internship, Freelance).
* **Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, date, required): When can you start working.
* **Desired Salary** (`desired_salary`, text, optional): Expected salary or hourly rate.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Highest level of education (High School, Associate Degree, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree, Doctorate, Other).
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Name of school or university.
* **Major** (`major`, text, optional): Field of study or major.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, number, optional): Year of graduation.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, select_one, required): Total years of relevant experience (No Experience, Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, More than 10 years).
* **Current Employer** (`current_employer`, text, optional): Current or most recent employer.
* **Current Position** (`current_position`, text, optional): Current or most recent job title.
* **Key Skills** (`key_skills`, select_multiple, required): Select your key skills (Communication, Leadership, Problem Solving, Teamwork, Time Management, Technical Skills, Customer Service, Project Management).
* **Why Interested** (`why_interested`, text, required): Why are you interested in this position.
* **Strengths** (`strengths`, text, required): What are your greatest strengths.
* **Weaknesses** (`weaknesses`, text, optional): Areas you are working to improve.
* **Work Authorization** (`work_authorization`, select_one, required): Are you authorized to work in this country (True, False, Will Require Sponsorship).
* **Background Check** (`background_check`, select_one, required): Willing to undergo background check (True, False).
* **References Available** (`references_available`, select_one, required): Can you provide professional references (True, False).
* **How Heard** (`how_heard`, select_one, optional): How did you hear about this position (Company Website, Job Board, Social Media, Employee Referral, Career Fair, Newspaper, Other).
* **Referral Name** (`referral_name`, text, optional): If referred by employee - their name.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other information to share.
* **Certify Accuracy** (`certify_accuracy`, select_one, required): I certify all information is accurate (True, False).
