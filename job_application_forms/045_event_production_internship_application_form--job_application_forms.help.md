# Event Production Internship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Production Internship Application Form is a comprehensive application form designed for candidates to apply for an Event Production internship position within the company. This form will help us assess your qualifications, availability, and experience to determine if you're a good fit for the internship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal details (First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone).
2. Provide your date of birth.
3. Share your experience in years.
4. Choose your available start date for the internship.
5. Select your preferred work hours.
6. Choose your available days of the week for work.
7. Select your availability type (Flexible, Part-time, Full-time).
8. Select your notice period.
9. Choose your expected salary range.
10. Select the unit of your notice period (Hour, Half Day, Day).
11. Provide any additional availability notes.
12. If applicable, provide emergency contact information.
13. Select your relevant skills.
14. Select your language proficiency.
15. Indicate if you have any references.
16. Select your availability period (Short-term, Long-term).
17. Select your past work experience type (Intern, Freelance, Part-time).
18. If applicable, provide your previous work experience start and end dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, First Name, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text, Last Name, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, Email, required): Enter your email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (text, Phone, required): Enter your phone number that you can be contacted on.
* **Date of Birth** (date, Date of Birth, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Experience Years** (number, Experience Years, required): Enter the number of years you have been working in the industry.
* **Available Start Date** (date, Available Start Date, required): Select the date you're available to start the internship.
* **Available End Date** (date, Available End Date, required): Select the date you're available to end the internship.
* **Work Hours** (select_one, Work Hours, required): Choose your preferred work hours.
* **Available Days per Week** (select_multiple, Available Days per Week, required): Choose the days of the week you're available to work.
* **Availability** (select_one, Availability, required): Select if you're available to work Flexibly, Part-time or Full-time.
* **Notice Period** (number, Notice Period, required): Select your expected notice period.
* **Salary Range** (select_one, Salary Range, required): Choose your expected salary range.
* **Notice Period Unit** (select_one, Notice Period Unit, required): Choose the unit of your notice period (Hour, Half Day, Day).
* **Availability Notes** (note, Availability Notes, optional): Add any additional availability notes.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text, Emergency Contact Name, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text, Emergency Contact Phone, optional): If applicable, enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (select_one, Emergency Contact Relationship, optional): If applicable, select the relationship with your emergency contact (Spouse, Family Member, Friend, Coworker).
* **Relevant Skills** (select_multiple, Relevant Skills, required): Select your relevant skills to the position (Photography, Writing, Graphic Design, Social Media Management, Event Planning, Video Production, Music Production, Audio Production).
* **Languages** (select_multiple, Languages, optional): Select your language proficiency.
* **References** (select_multiple, References, optional): Indicate if you have any references.
* **Availability Period** (select_one, Availability Period, required): Select your availability period (Short-term, Long-term).
* **Work Experience** (select_one, Work Experience, required): Select your past work experience type (Intern, Freelance, Part-time).
* **Work Experience Start Date** (date, Work Experience Start Date, optional): If applicable, enter your previous work experience start date.
* **Work Experience End Date** (date, Work Experience End Date, optional): If applicable, enter your previous work experience end date.

## Tips
* Please answer all the required fields.
* Make sure to proofread your application before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
