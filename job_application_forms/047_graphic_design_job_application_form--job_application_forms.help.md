# Graphic Design Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graphic Design Job Application Form is a tool used to gather information from individuals who are applying for graphic design positions. It is designed to help the human resources department or hiring managers evaluate the candidates' background, skills, and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what each field is asking for.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Applicant Info** (applicant_info, text, optional)
This field is where you can provide any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant to your application.

### **University Information** (university_info, select_one, optional)
This field is used to determine if you have a degree in graphic design or a related field. Please select 'Yes' if you have a degree, 'No' if you do not.

### **Design Skills** (design_skills, select_multiple, optional)
In this field, please indicate which design skills you possess. You can select all skills that apply to you. Please note that having multiple skills is not a requirement, but it is beneficial to showcase a wide range of skills.

* **Yes**: You have a strong proficiency in this skill.
* **No**: You do not have this skill.

### **Attachments** (attachments, select_multiple, optional)
This field is used to determine if you are willing to provide attachments to support your application. Please select 'Yes' if you are willing, 'No' if you are not.

### **Portfolio Samples** (portfolio_samples, select_multiple, optional)
This field is used to determine if you are willing to share your portfolio samples. Please select 'Yes' if you are willing, 'No' if you are not.

### **Links** (links, select_multiple, optional)
This field is used to determine if you are willing to share links to your online presence. Please select 'Yes' if you are willing, 'No' if you are not.
