# sozo_ministry_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from applicants for a ministry program at Sozo.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out your name, father's name, son's name, and relationship with each of them in the "Applicant Details" section.
2. In the "Relationship with Father", "Relationship with Son", and "Relationship with Holy Spirit" sections, select the appropriate option that best describes your relationship.
3. In the "Medical History" section, select the option that best describes your medical history.
4. If required, provide details about your medical history in the "Medical History Detail" section.
5. In the "Medical Certifications" section, select all relevant certifications you hold.
6. In the "Spiritual History" section, select the option that best describes your spiritual background.
7. In the "Spiritual Certifications" section, select all relevant certifications you hold.
8. In the "Family History" section, select the option that best describes your family background.
9. In the "Family Certifications" section, select all relevant certifications you hold.
10. In the "Education History" section, select the option that best describes your education background.
11. In the "Education Certifications" section, select all relevant certifications you hold.
12. In the "Work History" section, select the option that best describes your work background.
13. In the "Work Certifications" section, select all relevant certifications you hold.
14. In the "Financial History" section, select the option that best describes your financial background.
15. In the "Financial Certifications" section, select all relevant certifications you hold.
16. In the "Personal Reference" section, select the option that best describes your personal reference.
17. In the "Personal Certifications" section, select all relevant certifications you hold.
18. In the "Emergency Contacts" section, select all relevant contacts.
19. In the "References" section, select all relevant references.
20. If required, upload relevant attachments in the "Upload Attachments" section.
21. In the "Terms and Conditions" section, select the option that you have read and agreed to the terms.
22. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your application.
