# Senior Outreach Specialist Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Outreach Specialist Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants who are interested in applying for the Senior Outreach Specialist position. This form is used to gather necessary details for the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, first name, last name, and email address.
2. Select your job title, work type (full-time, part-time, or freelance), and relevant work experience.
3. Choose the skills you possess (programming, writing, design, etc.).
4. Select your level of education (bachelor, master, or doctorate).
5. Upload your portfolio and resume.
6. Provide references (optional).
7. Agree with the terms of the application.
8. Sign your application with your signature.
9. Select the date and time of submission.
10. Click the "Submit" button to complete the application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Apply as (Page 1)
* **Apply as** (`applicant_info`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.

### First name (Page 1)
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.

### Last name (Page 1)
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.

### Email (Page 1)
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address associated with you.

### Job Title (Page 2)
* **Job title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title or the job you are applying for.

### Work history (Years) (Page 2)
* **Work history (Years)** (`work_history`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have worked in your job title.

### Type of work (Page 3)
* **Type of work** (`work_type`, select_one, required): Select your work type, either full-time, part-time, or freelance.

### Relevant work experience (Page 3)
* **Relevant work experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Describe your relevant work experience. 

### Skills (Page 4)
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, required): Select the skills you possess, such as programming, writing, design, etc.

### Education (Page 4)
* **Education** (`education`, select_multiple, required): Select your level of education, such as bachelor, master, or doctorate.

### Portfolio (Page 5)
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, note, required): Upload your portfolio or a link to it. 

### Resume (Page 5)
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required): Upload your resume or a link to it.

### References (Page 6)
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): Provide a list of professional references.

### I agree with the terms (Page 6)
* **I agree with the terms** (`agree_terms`, select_one, required): Select that you agree with the application terms.

### Applicant's Signature (Page 7)
* **Applicant's Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign your application with your physical or digital signature.

### Date (Page 7)
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.

### Time (Page 7)
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of submission.

### Submit (Page 7)
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click the "Submit" button to complete the application.
