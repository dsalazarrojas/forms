# CV Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect detailed information from applicants for a job opening, including their name, contact details, job application details, educational background, work experience, and additional relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, fill in your full name as it appears on your documents.
2. Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
3. If available, provide your mobile number for faster communication.
4. Select the job title you are applying for.
5. Summarize your educational background, including the highest qualification and institution.
6. Provide the number of years you have relevant work experience.
7. Optionally, provide a link to your portfolio or LinkedIn link.
8. If needed, provide additional documents references, such as a CV cover letter or references.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on documents.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for application correspondence.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your mobile phone number for faster communication (if available).
* **Position applied for** (`position_applied`, text, required): Select the job title you are applying for.
* **Education summary** (`education_summary`, text, required): Summarize your highest qualification and institution.
* **Years of relevant experience** (`work_experience_years`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have relevant work experience.
* **Portfolio or LinkedIn link** (`portfolio_link`, text, optional): Optionally, provide a link to your portfolio or LinkedIn profile.
* **Additional documents reference** (`additional_documents`, text, optional): If needed, provide additional documents references, such as a CV cover letter or references.
