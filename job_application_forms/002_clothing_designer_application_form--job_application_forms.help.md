# Clothing Designer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clothing Designer Application Form is a comprehensive application form designed to collect essential information about your background, skills, and experience in the fashion industry. The form is intended for candidates interested in working as a clothing designer and will help the employer assess your eligibility, skills, and fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Fill Out the Personal Information Section

* **Full Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter the best number to reach you.
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, `text`, required): Enter your full mailing address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.

### Step 2: Select Your Professional Information

* **Position Applied For** (`position_applied`, `select_one`, required): Select the role you are interested in, such as Junior Designer, Senior Designer, Lead Designer, etc.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, `select_one`, required): Select your total years of experience in fashion design.
* **Highest Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your highest completed education, such as High School Diploma, Associate Degree, Bachelor Degree, etc.
* **Design School or Institution** (`design_school`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the fashion school or university you attended.
* **Area of Specialization** (`specialization`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your primary design focus, such as Womenswear, Menswear, Childrenswear, etc.
* **Design Software Proficiency** (`software_skills`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all software you are proficient in, such as Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop, etc.

### Step 3: Provide Additional Information

* **Portfolio URL** (`portfolio_url`, `text`, required): Enter the link to your online portfolio or website.
* **Previous Fashion Employers** (`previous_employers`, `text`, optional): List any previous fashion employers.
* **Notable Design Projects** (`notable_projects`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your most proud work.
* **Design Influences** (`design_influences`, `text`, optional): Name anyone or anything that inspires your designs.

### Step 4: Availability and Availability

* **Available Start Date** (`availability_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you can start working.
* **Salary Expectation** (`salary_expectation`, `select_one`, optional): Select your expected annual salary range.
* **Work Authorization Status** (`work_authorization`, `select_one`, required): Select your work authorization status, such as Citizen, Permanent Resident, Work Visa Required, etc.
* **References Available** (`references_available`, `select_one`, required): Select if you can provide professional references.
* **Cover Letter** (`cover_letter`, `text`, required): Enter a brief statement about why you want this position.
* **Resume Upload** (`resume_upload`, `text`, required): Upload your current resume.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter anything else you would like to share with us.
