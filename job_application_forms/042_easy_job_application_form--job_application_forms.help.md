# easy_job_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "easy_job_application_form" is a generic form designed to collect various information from job applicants in different contexts such as HR, recruitment, or internal processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Upload your Resume in the "Resume" field.
5. Select your Job Status from the "Job Status" dropdown.
6. Specify your Desired Position in the "Desired Position" field.
7. Enter your available Start Date in the "Available Start Date" field.
8. Provide your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
10. Add any Notes in the "Note" field.
11. Specify the tool assigned to you in the "Assigned Tool" field.
12. Select a Category from the list.
13. Enter a brief Description of your application.
14. If you need to upload other documents, click on the "Other Documents" field and follow the upload process.
15. For "Other Documents Type", select the format of the uploaded documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): Upload your resume.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current job status.
	+ Options: Active, Passive, Unemployed, Retired, Student, Military
* **Desired Position** (`desired_position`, text, optional): Specify your desired position.
* **Available Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter your available start date.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any notes you'd like to include with your application.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Specify the tool assigned to you.
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Select a category for your application.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your application.
* **Other Documents** (`other_documents`, text, optional): Upload other documents relevant to your application.
* **Other Documents Type** (`other_documents_type`, select_one, optional): Select the format of the uploaded documents.
	+ Options: PDF, DOCX, DOCM, JPEG, PNG, BMP, TIFF, CSV, TXT, RTF, MP3, MP4, WAV, MPG, AVI, FLV
* **Assigned Tool 2** (`assigned_tool_2`, text, optional): Specify the tool assigned to you.
* **Category 2** (`category_2`, text, optional): Select a category for your application.
* **Description 2** (`description_2`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your application.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): Upload an output file related to your application.
* **Form Ids** (`form_ids`, number, optional): Enter form IDs.
* **Form Ids 2** (`form_ids_2`, number, optional): Enter form IDs.
* **Other Documents Description** (`other_documents_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the uploaded documents.
* **Other Documents File** (`other_documents_file`, text, optional): Upload other documents related to your application.
* **Other Documents Type** (`other_documents_type`, select_one, optional): Select the format of the uploaded documents.
	+ Options: PDF, DOCX, DOCM, JPEG, PNG, BMP, TIFF, CSV, TXT, RTF, MP3, MP4, WAV, MPG, AVI, FLV

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, even if they seem optional.
* If you're unsure about any field, try to use the hint or contact the person who assigned this form for help.
* Remember to keep your uploaded files in the correct format for better compatibility.
