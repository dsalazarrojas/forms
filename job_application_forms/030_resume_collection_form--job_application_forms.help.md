# Resume Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resume Collection Form is designed to collect information from job applicants for hiring purposes. It's meant to be filled out by individuals applying for a job, and the data collected will be used to assess their qualifications and fit for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **contact information** section (id: 2) with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide a brief description of your **employment history** (id: 3), including your previous work experience and the companies you've worked for.
3. Describe your **education background** (id: 4), including your degree(s), institution(s), and relevant coursework or training.
4. List your **work experience** (id: 5), including your job title, company name, and duration of employment.
5. Specify your **job preferences** (id: 6), including your desired job role, industry, or work environment.
6. Upload your **resume** (id: 7), if applicable.
7. Add any **notes** (id: 8) you'd like to include, such as additional information about your qualifications or experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Info** (id: 2, text, required false): Fill out your name, email address, and phone number.
- **Employment History** (id: 3, text, required false): Describe your previous work experience, including company names and job titles.
- **Education Background** (id: 4, text, required false): Mention your degree(s), institution(s), and relevant coursework or training.
- **Work Experience** (id: 5, text, required false): List your job title, company name, and duration of employment.
- **Job Preferences** (id: 6, text, required false): Specify your desired job role, industry, or work environment.
- **Resume Upload** (id: 7, text, required false): Upload your resume, if applicable.
- **Notes** (id: 8, text, required false): Add any additional information about your qualifications or experience.

## Tips
* Ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Be specific and concise when describing your employment history, education background, work experience, and job preferences.
* Upload your resume as a PDF or Word document, if applicable.
* Use the space for notes to provide any additional details that may help us assess your qualifications.
