# Municipality Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Municipality Job Application Form is designed to collect necessary information from job applicants to help them get considered for a position at the municipality. Please fill out this form to increase your chances of being considered for a role that matches your qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your name and any other identifying information about yourself in the **Applicant Information** field.
2. Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number, in the **Contact Information** field.
3. Describe your education background, including relevant courses or degrees, in the **Education Background** field.
4. Provide a summary of your professional background, including previous work experience, in the **Professional Background** field.
5. Share your relevant work experience in the **Work Experience** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Information
* **Name** (text, not required): Enter your name as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Applicant Information** (text, not required): This is a free-form field where you can provide any other information you feel is relevant to your application.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (text, not required): Enter your email and phone number so we can reach out to you with updates on your application.

### Education Background
* **Education Background** (text, not required): Describe your education background, including relevant courses or degrees that align with the job requirements.

### Professional Background
* **Professional Background** (text, not required): Summarize your professional experience, including relevant job titles, responsibilities, or achievements.

### Work Experience
* **Work Experience** (text, not required): Share your relevant work experience that aligns with the job requirements.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you only apply to roles that match your qualifications to avoid wasting time for both you and the hiring team.
* Review the job description and requirements carefully before filling out the form.
* Be honest and accurate when providing information about yourself.
* Proofread your application before submitting it to avoid errors.
