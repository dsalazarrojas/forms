# invigilator_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The invigilator_application_form is a comprehensive form designed to collect information from individuals applying to join the Invigilator team. The form covers various fields that help us assess your suitability for potential opportunities within our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details in the Personal Details section.
2. Enter your address in the Address field.
3. Enter your contact number in the Contact Number field.
4. Enter your email address in the Email field.
5. List your qualifications or relevant experience in the Qualifications field.
6. Select a date for your availability in the Availability field.
7. Describe your experience in the Experience field.
8. Choose your availability period in the Availability Period field.
9. Enter any relevant skills you have in the Skills field.
10. Provide the names of referees or references who can vouch for your skills.
11. Add any additional notes or comments about yourself in the Additional Notes field.
12. Select the Terms and Conditions you have read and understood.
13. Choose how you wish to submit the form: Submit or Do Not Submit.
14. If you selected "Do Not Submit," you will not be able to proceed with the application.
15. Enter the date and time of your application in the Date of Application and Time of Application fields.
16. Specify the mode of application you used (e.g., online, email, phone).
17. Describe how you applied (e.g., online form, email, phone).
18. Choose your preferred contact method (e.g., email, phone, other).
19. If you chose "other," provide a description of your preferred contact method.
20. Enter the name of your referee or reference.
21. Enter the contact information of your referee or reference.
22. Describe your relationship with your referee or reference.
23. Enter the email address of your referee or reference.
24. Enter the phone number of your referee or reference.
25. Add any additional referees or references you have.
