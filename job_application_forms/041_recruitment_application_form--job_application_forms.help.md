# recruitment-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The recruitment-application-form is a comprehensive tool designed to collect detailed information about job applicants. It helps hiring managers and recruiters to evaluate candidates' qualifications and fit for a specific role. By completing this form, candidates can showcase their skills, experience, and background, increasing their chances of landing an interview or getting hired.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Start with the first section, "job application details," which asks for general information about the job posting, such as the job title, position, and location.
2. Move on to "candidate info," where you will be asked to provide your personal details, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
3. The next section, "job posting details," is where you will provide more information about the job posting, such as the job description and requirements.
4. "Skills" is where you list your relevant skills and qualifications.
5. "Work experience" is where you describe your work history, including job titles, company names, and durations.
6. "Education background" is where you provide information about your educational qualifications and achievements.
7. Finally, "references" is where you can list any professional references you have.


## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Application Details** (`job_application_details`, text, required: false): This field is where you provide general information about the job posting you are applying for.
* **Candidate Info** (`candidate_info`, text, required: false): Please enter your full name, email address, and phone number.
* **Job Posting Details** (`job_posting_details`, text, required: false): Describe the job posting you are applying for, including the job title, position, and location.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, required: false): List your relevant skills and qualifications for the job posting.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of your work history, including job titles, company names, and durations.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, text, required: false): Share information about your educational qualifications and achievements.
* **References** (`references`, text, required: false): List any professional references you have, including their name, title, and contact information.

## Tips
* Be accurate and truthful when filling out the form.
* Use clear and concise language to answer each question.
* Do not leave any fields blank if you have information to provide.
* Proofread your form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the form creators.
