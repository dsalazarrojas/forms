# Manufacturing Skilled Worker Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for potential candidates applying for skilled worker positions in the manufacturing industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.
2. For date fields (e.g., Work Experience, Work Education, Available Dates), use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. For select_one and select_multiple fields (e.g., Job Type, Skills, Shift Type), choose the option that best matches your situation.
4. Make sure to save your progress as you go and review your information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address. This will be used for communication and account creation.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Resume**: Attach a copy of your resume to help us understand your background and qualifications.
* **Work Experience**: Enter the date you started working in your current (or most recent) position. This should be the date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current or most recent job title.
* **Skills**: Select all the relevant skills you possess from the available options (Accounting, Engineering, Programming).
* **Work Education**: Enter the date you completed your education. This should be the date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Available Dates**: Enter the dates you are available to start work. This should be the dates in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Job Type**: Select the type of job you are looking for (Full-time, Part-time).
* **Job Status**: Select your current job status (Active, Inactive).
* **Department**: Enter the department you are interested in or currently working in.
* **Location**: Enter the location where you are willing to work or currently residing.
* **Shift Type**: Select the type of shift you are comfortable with (Morning, Afternoon, Night).
* **Job Type Details**: Enter any additional information about the type of job you are looking for.
