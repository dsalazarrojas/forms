# Sample Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants for job openings. It is a general-purpose form that can be used for a wide range of job applications. Please use this form to provide your contact information and other relevant details about yourself and your qualifications for the job you are applying for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information on pages 4 and 7.
2. Upload your resume and cover letter on pages 2 and 3, respectively.
3. Enter a brief description of the job you are applying for on page 9.
4. Provide a description of your work experience on page 10.
5. Enter any additional information about your education on page 11.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (Page 4)**: Please enter your full name as it should appear on your application.
* **Resume Upload (Page 2)**: Attach your resume in a PDF or Word document format to showcase your work experience and qualifications.
* **Cover Letter Upload (Page 3)**: Attach a cover letter in a PDF or Word document format that introduces yourself and explains why you are a good fit for the job.
* **Email (Page 6)**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your application.
* **Contact Number (Page 7)**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you about your application.
* **Address (Page 8)**: Enter your physical address so that we can mail any necessary materials.
* **Job Description (Page 9)**: Enter a brief description of the job you are applying for, so that we can verify your qualifications.
* **Work Experience (Page 10)**: Describe your previous work experience related to the job you are applying for.
* **Education (Page 11)**: Describe any additional information about your education that is relevant to the job.
