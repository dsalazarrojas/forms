# Transport Associate Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transport Associate Application Form is a comprehensive application form designed to help candidates apply for transport associate positions. The form gathers necessary information about your qualifications, work history, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your job title (page 1, field 1) to indicate your area of expertise or the position you are applying for.
2. Provide a brief description of your work experience (page 1, field 2) relevant to the transport associate role.
3. Describe your work history (page 1, field 3) in the transport industry, highlighting your accomplishments and job responsibilities.
4. Select your transport license class (page 1, field 4) that aligns with your qualifications and experience.
5. Enter your email address (page 2, field 6) to ensure we can contact you about your application.
6. Enter your phone number (page 2, field 7) to facilitate communication about your application.
7. Indicate your preferred location for work (page 2, field 8) to help us determine potential work opportunities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (page 1, field 1, `text`):
  Enter your current or intended job title in the transport associate role you are applying for. This helps us understand your area of expertise and experience.
* **Work Experience** (page 1, field 2, `text`):
  Describe your previous work experience in the transport associate field, highlighting your job responsibilities and achievements. This section should be concise but detailed enough to demonstrate your qualifications for the role.
* **Work History** (page 1, field 3, `text`):
  Provide an overview of your work history, including any relevant experience in the transport industry. Focus on your job responsibilities, skills, and accomplishments.
* **Transport License** (page 1, field 4, `select_multiple`):
  Select the class of transport license that aligns with your qualifications and experience. This helps us assess your safety and regulatory compliance.
* **Email** (page 2, field 6, `email`):
  Enter a valid email address to receive updates and notifications about your application.
* **Phone Number** (page 2, field 7, `text`):
  Enter a valid phone number to facilitate communication about your application and potential interviews.
* **Location** (page 2, field 8, `text`):
  Indicate your preferred location for work to help us determine potential work opportunities.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Ensure your email address and phone number are up-to-date and valid.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant department or supervisor for assistance.
