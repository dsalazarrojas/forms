# Media Copywriter Job Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Media Copywriter Job Application Form is designed for candidates interested in applying for a Media Copywriter position. This form collects various pieces of information from applicants to help hiring managers or recruiters evaluate their qualifications and suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your basic information in the Applicant Info field.
2. Provide your contact details in the Contact Info field.
3. Share a link to your portfolio in the Portfolio URL field.
4. Attach your resume in the Resume field.
5. Describe your portfolio work in the Portfolio Work field.
6. Select the type of work you've done in the Type of Work field.
7. Describe your work experience in the Work Experience field.
8. Select the length of your work experience in the Length of Experience field.
9. Describe your education background in the Education Background field.
10. Enter the name of your institution in the Institution field.
11. Select the degree you've earned in the Degree Earned field.
12. Select the type of portfolio review you'd like to receive in the Portfolio Review field.
13. Finally, click the Submit button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`): Enter your full name and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Enter your contact details, including phone number and email address.
* **Portfolio URL** (`portfolio_url`): Share a link to your portfolio or online presence.
* **Resume** (`resume`): Attach your resume as a PDF or text file.
* **Portfolio Work** (`portfolio_work`): Describe the types of projects you've worked on.
* **Type of Work** (`portfolio_work_type`): Select the type of work you've done (e.g., content writing, copywriting, editing).
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`): Describe your work experience, including job titles and responsibilities.
* **Length of Experience** (`work_experience_length`): Select the length of your work experience (e.g., 3-5 years, 5-10 years, more than 10 years).
* **Education Background** (`education_background`): Describe your education background, including institutions and degrees earned.
* **Institution** (`education_institution`): Enter the name of your institution.
* **Degree Earned** (`education_degree`): Select the degree you've earned (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, Doctoral).
* **Portfolio Review** (`portfolio_review`): Select the type of portfolio review you'd like to receive (e.g., Option 1, Option 2).
* **Submit** (`submit`): Click this button to submit your application.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all questions accurately and completely.
* Attach a valid PDF or text file as your resume.
* Keep your portfolio URL up to date and active.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Select the correct options for Type of Work, Length of Experience, and Degree Earned.
* Choose the correct options for Portfolio Review.
* Proofread your application carefully before submitting it.
