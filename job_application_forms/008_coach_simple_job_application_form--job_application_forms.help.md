# Coach Simple Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals interested in coaching positions, allowing them to apply and express their interest in being part of the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information: Enter your First Name, Last Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
2. Share your coaching interest: Describe your preferences for coaching, including the sport you wish to coach.
3. Select your availability: Choose the days and times you are available to coach.
4. Share your experience: Enter the number of years you have coached this sport.
5. Upload your resume (optional): If applicable, provide a link to your resume.
6. Sell yourself: Write a brief summary of why you would be a valuable addition to the team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for further contact and communication.
* **Desired Sport** (`desired_sport`, `text`, required): Select the sport you are interested in coaching.
* **Preferred Age Group** (`preferred_age_group`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the age groups you are interested in coaching.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the days and times you are available to coach.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have coached this sport.
* **Upload Resume Link** (`resume_link`, `text`, optional): If you have a resume, provide a link to it.
* **Why should we hire you?** (`pitch`, `text`, required): In a brief summary, explain why you would be a valuable addition to the team.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a complete application.
* If you have a resume, please provide a link to it, as it will help us to get a better understanding of your qualifications.
* Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly to give us a complete understanding of your application.
