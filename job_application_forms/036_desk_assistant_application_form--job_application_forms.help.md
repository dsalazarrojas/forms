# Desk Assistant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants for a Desk Assistant position. It has multiple pages with various fields that need to be filled out carefully.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Personal Details page with your contact information and any other required personal details.
2. On the Work Experience page, provide a detailed account of your previous work experience, including job titles, duration, and a brief description of your role.
3. On the Job Qualifications page, specify your relevant skills, qualifications, and certifications.
4. On the Education Background page, provide information about your educational background, including institutions attended and degrees earned.
5. If applicable, provide references on the References page, including their contact information and a brief description of your work relationship.
6. On the Upload Documents page, upload any required documents, such as a cover letter or a resume.
7. Finally, on the Submission page, submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details (required)**: This field is used to collect your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
* **Work Experience**: This field is used to collect information about your previous work experience, including job titles, duration, and a brief description of your role.
* **Job Qualifications**: This field is used to collect information about your relevant skills, qualifications, and certifications.
* **Education Background**: This field is used to collect information about your educational background, including institutions attended and degrees earned.
* **References**: This field is used to collect information about your references, including their contact information and a brief description of your work relationship.
* **Upload Documents**: This field is used to upload any required documents, such as a cover letter or a resume.
* **Upload Resume**: This field is used to upload your resume, which is a required document for this application.
* **Job Application**: This field is used to enter any additional information about your job application.
* **Submission**: This field is used to submit your application for review.

## Tips

* Ensure you have all required documents ready before uploading them.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting your application.
