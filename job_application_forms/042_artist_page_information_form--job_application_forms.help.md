# Artist Page Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and display essential information about an artist's profile, allowing them to promote themselves and their work to potential clients, managers, or promoters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full legal name in the **Full Legal Name** field.
2. Enter your professional name or stage name in the **Artist or Professional Name** field.
3. Provide your primary contact email in the **Email Address** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
5. Select your **Primary Artistic Discipline** from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter a 150-300 word **Professional Biography** that describes your work and experience.
7. If applicable, enter your **Years of Professional Experience**.
8. Provide links to your **Official Website**, **Social Media Handles**, **Streaming or Music Platform Links**, and **Press Kit or Media Materials**.
9. Upload a **Professional Headshot Photo** and **Action or Performance Photos**.
10. Enter your **Notable Venues or Events**, **Awards, Honors, or Recognition**, and **Media Mentions or Publications**.
11. If you have representation, enter your **Booking Agent or Manager Contact** information.
12. Select your **Current Availability for Booking** from the dropdown menu.
13. Finally, enter any **Additional Information** you'd like to share with potential clients or promoters.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Artist or Professional Name** (`artist_name`, `text`, required): Enter your professional name or stage name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Primary Artistic Discipline** (`primary_discipline`, `select_one`, required): Select your main area of artistic work.
* **Professional Biography** (`professional_biography`, `text`, required): Enter a 150-300 word biography that describes your work and experience.
* **Years of Professional Experience** (`years_professional`, `number`, optional): If applicable, enter your years of professional experience.
* **Official Website** (`official_website`, `text`, optional): Provide a link to your main website or portfolio.
* **Social Media Handles** (`social_media`, `text`, optional): Enter your social media handles (e.g., Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, etc.).
* **Streaming or Music Platform Links** (`streaming_profiles`, `text`, optional): Enter links to your streaming or music platform profiles (e.g., Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, etc.).
* **Press Kit or Media Materials** (`press_materials`, `text`, optional): Enter links to your press materials.
* **Professional Headshot Photo** (`headshot_photo`, `text`, optional): Upload a high-resolution promotional photo.
* **Action or Performance Photos** (`action_photos`, `text`, optional): Upload photos of you performing.
* **Notable Venues or Events** (`notable_venues`, `text`, optional): Enter where you've performed or exhibited.
* **Awards, Honors, or Recognition** (`awards_honors`, `text`, optional): Enter any notable achievements or accolades.
* **Media Mentions or Publications** (`media_mentions`, `text`, optional): Enter press coverage or featured mentions.
* **Booking Agent or Manager Contact** (`booking_agent`, `text`, optional): If you have representation, enter your contact information.
* **Current Availability for Booking** (`availability_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your current availability for new opportunities.
* **Additional Information for Artist Page** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any other details for promotion.
