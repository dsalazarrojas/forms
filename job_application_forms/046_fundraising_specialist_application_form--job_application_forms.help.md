# Fundraising Specialist Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect application information from potential candidates for a fundraising specialist position. The form will be used to evaluate candidates' backgrounds, experiences, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure you have completed all required fields.
3. Use the "Experience" field to select your relevant experience in fundraising, event planning, or grant writing.
4. Upload your resume in the "Resumes" field.
5. Select your relevant skills in the "Skills" field.
6. Write a statement of interest in the "Statement of Interest" field, explaining why you're interested in this position.
7. Provide your availability in the format MM/DD/YYYY (month/day/year) in the "Availability" field.
8. If required, provide references and education information in the "References" and "Education" fields.
9. Finally, review and sign the "Agreement" field to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Information
• **Personal Information** (`Personal Information`, `text`, required/optional): Please fill out this form carefully.
### Contact Information
• **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact information for follow-up and communication purposes.
### Experience
• **Experience** (`Experience`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your relevant experience in fundraising, event planning, or grant writing.
### Resumes
• **Resumes** (`Resumes`, `note`, required): Upload your resume to showcase your qualifications.
### Skills
• **Skills** (`Skills`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your relevant skills in fundraising, event planning, or grant writing.
### Statement of Interest
• **Statement of Interest** (`Statement of Interest`, `text`, required): Write a statement explaining why you're interested in this position.
### Availability
• **Availability** (`Availability`, `date`, required): Provide your availability in the format MM/DD/YYYY (month/day/year).
### References
• **References** (`References`, `text`, required/optional): If required, provide 2-3 references with their name, job title, organization, and contact information.
### Education
• **Education** (`Education`, `text`, required/optional): If required, provide your education information, including institution, degree, and date graduated.
### Certifications
• **Certifications** (`Certifications`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select any relevant certifications, such as Certified Fundraising Professional or Certified Nonprofit Professional.
### Agreement
• **Agreement** (`Agreement`, `text`, required): Review and sign this agreement to confirm your application.

## Tips
1. Please ensure you have completed all required fields before submitting the form.
2. Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
3. Proofread your form carefully before submitting it.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form creator or the organization administering the form.
