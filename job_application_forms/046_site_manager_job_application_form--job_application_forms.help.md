# site_manager_job_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a job application form designed to collect information from individuals interested in applying for a site manager position within the company. It aims to gather relevant details from applicants, including job application details, contact information, and educational background.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your job application details in the **Job Application Details** field on the first page.
2. Enter your contact information in the **contact_info** field on the second page.
3. Share your educational background and training in the **education_and_training** field on the third page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Job Application Details** (`job_application_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can provide a brief description of your job application, including your experience, skills, and qualifications.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, phone number, and email address in this field so we can get in touch with you regarding your application.
- **Education and Training** (`education_and_training`, `text`, required: false): In this field, please describe your educational background, certifications, or relevant training that aligns with the site manager position.
