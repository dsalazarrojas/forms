# Locksmith Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects contact information, personal details, and supporting documents from potential locksmith applicants for the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your contact information on the first page.
2. Provide personal details on the second page.
3. Submit your application by clicking on the "Submit" button.
4. If you have any supporting documents, you can upload them on the last page.
5. Review and confirm your application on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (1)**: Enter your name, address, phone number, and email address.
* **Personal Info (2)**: Provide your current work experience, qualifications, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Locksmith Details (3)**: Describe your experience and skills as a locksmith.
* **Experience (4)**: Share your locksmith-related work experience and achievements.
* **Education (5)**: List any locksmith-related education or training you have received.
* **Skills (6)**: List your technical skills, such as proficiency in specific tools or software.
* **References (7)**: Provide information about your professional references.
* **Emergency Contact (8)**: Enter the contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Upload Resume (9)**: Upload your resume or CV.
* **Upload License (10)**: Upload a copy of your locksmith license.
* **Upload ID Card (11)**: Upload a copy of your ID card.
* **Upload Signature (12)**: Upload a scanned copy of your signature.
* **Upload Other Docs (13)**: Upload any other relevant documents.
* **Submit (14)**: Click this button to submit your application.
* **Upload License Front (14)**: Upload a front copy of your license.
* **Upload License Back (15)**: Upload a back copy of your license.
* **Upload License Card Front (16)**: Upload a front copy of your license card.
* **Upload License Card Back (17)**: Upload a back copy of your license card.
* **Submit 2 (18)**: This button is a duplicate of the "Submit" button.
* **Confirm (19)**: Review and confirm your application details.
* **Review 2 (20)**: This button is not used.
* **Confirm 3 (21)**: Confirm your application details again.
* **Confirm 4 (22)**: This button is not used.
* **Upload License Front (23)**: This field is a duplicate of "Upload License Front".
* **Upload License Back (24)**: This field is a duplicate of "Upload License Back".
* **Upload License Card Front (25)**: This field is a duplicate of "Upload License Card Front".
* **Upload License Card Back (26)**: This field is a duplicate of "Upload License Card Back".
