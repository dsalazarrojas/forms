# Video Editor Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Video Editor Job Application Form is designed to collect your personal and professional contact information and details for the Video Editor position. This form helps our HR team to review and contact potential candidates for further evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details and upload your resume.
2. Enter your job title and portfolio URL to showcase your professional experience and skills.
3. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and contact number.
4. Submit the form to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents or resume. 
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents or resume.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current or desired job title in the field of video editing.
* **Resume**: Upload a copy of your resume to showcase your professional experience and skills.
* **Portfolio URL**: Enter a URL to your online portfolio or website that demonstrates your video editing work.
* **Contact Number**: Enter a valid contact number that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Address**: Enter your current address for our records.

## Tips
* Make sure to upload a clear and recent copy of your resume.
* Use a URL that is publicly accessible to your portfolio.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Ensure that you haven't applied for this position before, as it may be flagged as a duplicate application in our system.
