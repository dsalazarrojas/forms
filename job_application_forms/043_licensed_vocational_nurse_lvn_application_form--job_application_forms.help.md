# Licensed Vocational Nurse (LVN) Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from potential job applicants for a Licensed Vocational Nurse (LVN) position. Please provide accurate and truthful information to ensure a smooth and efficient application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your medical experience and education background.
3. Specify your skills and certifications relevant to the LVN role.
4. Provide your work experience and job preferences.
5. Indicate your availability to start work and availability to work in terms of schedule.
6. Choose your preferred salary range.
7. List your emergency contact and language skills.
8. Repeat the same steps for the second section of the form, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name.
- **Email** (2, email, required): Enter your work email address.
- **Phone** (3, text, required): Enter your work phone number.
- **Medical Experience** (4, select_multiple, required): Choose your relevant medical experience.
- **Education** (5, select_one, required): Select your relevant education background.
- **Skills** (6, note, required): List your relevant skills.
- **Certifications** (7, select_multiple, optional): Choose any additional certifications you hold.
- **Work Experience** (9, select_one, required): Select your relevant work experience.
- **Job Preferred** (10, select_one, required): Select your preferred job location.
- **Available To Work** (11, date, required): Choose your availability to start work.
- **Available To Start** (12, date, required): Choose your availability to start work, considering any notice period required.
- **Salary Range** (13, select_one, required): Choose your preferred salary range.
- **Availability** (14, select_multiple, optional): Choose your availability for shifts and work schedule.
- **Emergency Contact** (15, select_multiple, optional): List your emergency contact information.
- **Languages** (16, select_multiple, optional): List your language skills.
- **References** (17, select_multiple, optional): List your professional references.
- **Certifications 2** (18, select_one, optional): Select any additional certifications you hold.
- **Skills 2** (19, note, optional): List any additional skills you possess.
- **Work Experience 2** (20, select_one, optional): Select any additional work experience.
- **Job Preferred 2** (21, select_one, optional): Select your second preferred job location.
- **Available To Work 2** (22, date, optional): Choose your second availability to start work.
- **Available To Start 2** (23, date, optional): Choose your second availability to start work, considering any notice period required.
- **Salary Range 2** (24, select_one, optional): Choose your second preferred salary range.
- **Availability 2** (25, select_multiple, optional): Choose your second availability for shifts and work schedule.
- **Emergency Contact 2** (26, select_multiple, optional): List your second emergency contact information.
- **Languages 2** (27, select_multiple, optional): List your second language skills.

Note: The optional fields are not required to be filled unless specified otherwise.
