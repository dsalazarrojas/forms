# Legal Corporate Law Associate Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Corporate Law Associate Job Application form is used for internal job applications within the corporate law department. It serves as a tool for employees to apply for available positions within the company, track the status of their submissions, and facilitate the review process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Job Application Form with all required information.
2. Select the job position and status of the job that you are applying for.
3. Upload your resume and cover letter.
4. Choose whether you want to submit your application.
5. If you choose to submit, select the submission type and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Job Application Form** (`job_application_form`, text, required): Enter your job application form with a brief description of your qualifications and experience.
- **Job Position** (`job_position`, select_one, required): Select the job position you are applying for. 
- **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the job you are applying for.
- **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the job that you are applying for.
- **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of job you are applying for. 
- **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of the job. 
- **Resume Upload** (`resume_upload`, text, required): Upload your resume. 
  Please note that the resume should be in a format that can be easily readable by the reviewer.
- **Resume** (`resume_file`, text, required): Enter the title of your uploaded resume.
- **Cover Letter Upload** (`cover_letter_upload`, text, required): Upload your cover letter. 
  Please note that the cover letter should be in a format that can be easily readable by the reviewer.
- **Cover Letter** (`cover_letter`, text, required): Enter the title of your uploaded cover letter.
- **Submission** (`submission`, select_one, required): Select whether you want to submit your application. 
  Please note that only submitted applications will be reviewed.
- **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_multiple, required): Select whether you want the application to be submitted by you.
- **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your submission.
- **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your submission.
- **Reviewer** (`reviewer`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the reviewer for your application.
- **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the review process.
- **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments for the reviewer.
- **Submission Notes** (`submission_notes`, text, required): Enter any notes for the submission.
- **Submission Date 2** (`submission_date_2`, date, required): Enter the date of your submission again for review purposes.
- **Submission Time 2** (`submission_time_2`, time, required): Enter the time of your submission again for review purposes.
- **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the submission.
- **Submission Type** (`submission_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of submission.
- **Submitted By 2** (`submitted_by_2`, select_multiple, required): Select whether you want the application to be submitted by you again for review purposes.
- **Submission Notes 2** (`submission_notes_2`, text, required): Enter any notes for the submission.
- **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the reviewer for your application.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Ensure that your resume and cover letter are in formats that can be easily readable by the reviewer.
* Be clear and concise in your submissions.
* Follow up with the reviewer to confirm that your application has been submitted and reviewed.
