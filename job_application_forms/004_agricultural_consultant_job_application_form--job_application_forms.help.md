# Agricultural Consultant Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a job application for an agricultural consultant position. It collects information about your background, education, experience, and preferences to help us assess your suitability for the role. Please ensure that you are applying for a real job opportunity and not duplicate applications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, email address, phone number, and current location.
2. Select your highest education level and provide information about your field of study.
3. Share your relevant work experience and areas of expertise in agriculture.
4. Provide information about your previous employer and any relevant certifications you hold.
5. Specify your work preferences, including your availability start date and willingness to relocate.
6. Upload your resume and a cover letter to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Fill in your full legal name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your professional email address. This will be used for communication and will not be shared with other parties.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
* **Current Location** (`current_location`, text, required): Provide your current city and state or province.
* **Highest Education Level** (`highest_education`, select_one, required): Select your highest level of education completed, such as High School Diploma, Associate's Degree, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree, or PhD.
* **Field of Study** (`field_of_study`, text, required): Enter your major or area of specialization.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have worked in agricultural consulting.
* **Areas of Expertise** (`areas_of_expertise`, select_multiple, required): Choose all relevant areas of expertise, such as Crop Science, Animal Husbandry, Agribusiness Management, Sustainable Farming, Soil Science, Precision Agriculture, or Organic Farming.
* **Previous Employer** (`previous_employer`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your most recent employer.
* **Relevant Certifications** (`relevant_certifications`, text, optional): List any professional certifications you hold.
* **Availability Start Date** (`availability_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are available to start work.
* **Willing to Relocate** (`willing_to_relocate`, select_one, required): Select whether you are open to moving for this role, True, False, or Negotiable.
* **Resume** (`resume_note`, note, optional): Upload a copy of your resume, or send it to hr@agriconsult.com.
* **Cover Letter** (`cover_letter`, text, optional): Write a brief explanation of why you are a good fit for this role.
