# Job Posting Subscription Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Job Posting Subscription Requisition Form is a form used to collect information about job postings from a job requisitioner. This form helps to identify and document the details of a job posting, making it easier to manage and track job posting subscriptions. 

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

- Click on the form's "Submit" button when you are ready to fill out the form.
- Select your answer to "Job Posting Subscription" from the dropdown menu. (e.g., "Yes" or "No".)
- Enter the job title, location, category, and description in the text boxes provided.
- Choose the job status, level, type, and posting subscription type from the dropdown menus.
- Enter the manager's name, email, and phone number in the text boxes.
- Select the job posting subscription start and end dates from the date boxes.
- Choose whether to add notes, attachments, comments, or job posting requisitioner details.
- Finally, click on the "Submit" button to save your changes.
