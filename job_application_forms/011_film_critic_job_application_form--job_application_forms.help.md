# Film Critic Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a way for you to apply for a Film Critic job position. Fill out this form to submit your application and be considered for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your job position you're applying for in the "Job Position" field.
6. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
7. Upload or attach your resume in the "Resume" field. You can upload a file or paste a link to a cloud storage service.
8. Select your work experience from the "Work Experience" field. Choose one or more options that describe your experience.
9. Select your availability to start from the "Availability" field. Choose one of the available options.
10. If available to start immediately, enter today's date in the "Availability Date" field.
11. If available to start immediately, enter your start time in the "Availability Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Position** (text, required): Enter the job position you're applying for.
* **Job Title** (text, required): Enter the job title you're applying for.
* **Resume** (note, required): Upload or attach your resume. You can upload a file or paste a link to a cloud storage service.
* **Work Experience** (select-multiple, optional): Briefly describe your work experience. Choose one or more options that describe your experience.
	+ **Senior Editor**: Select if you have experience as a senior editor.
	+ **Editor**: Select if you have experience as an editor.
	+ **Assistant Editor**: Select if you have experience as an assistant editor.
* **Availability** (select-one, optional): Select your availability to start. Choose one of the available options.
	+ **Available to start immediately**
	+ **Available to start in two weeks**
	+ **Available to start in one month**
	+ **Available to start in three months**
	+ **Available to start in six months**
* **Availability Date** (date, optional): If available to start immediately, enter today's date.
* **Availability Time** (time, optional): If available to start immediately, enter your start time.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* If you have any issues uploading your resume, try using a different file format or browser.
* If you're available to start immediately, enter today's date and time.
