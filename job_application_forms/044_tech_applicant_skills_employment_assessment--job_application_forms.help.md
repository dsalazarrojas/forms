# Tech Applicant Skills Employment Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from tech applicants about their employment status and skills. The information provided will be used by hiring managers or recruiters to assess the applicants' suitability for tech jobs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your contact information:
	* **Enter Applicant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
	* **Contact Email**: Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your mobile or landline number where you can be reached.
2. Select your employment status:
	* **Employment Status**: Choose one of the options (Employed, Unemployed, Part-time, or Freelance) that best describes your current employment status.
3. Select your employment type:
	* **Employment Type**: Choose one of the options (Full-time, Contract, Temporary, or Other) that best describes your employment type.
4. Describe your work experience:
	* **Work Experience**: Briefly describe your relevant work experience in the tech industry.
5. Select your job type:
	* **Job Type**: Choose one of the options (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Internship, or Other) that best describes the type of job you are looking for.
6. Select your available time:
	* **Available For**: Choose one of the options (Immediate, In 1-3 months, In 6-12 months) that best describes when you are available to start working.
7. Select your education:
	* **Education**: Choose one of the options ('Yes' or 'No') that best describes whether you have relevant education for the job.
8. Enter your job location:
	* **Job Location**: Enter the city or region where you are willing to work.
9. Enter your job industry:
	* **Job Industry**: Enter the sector or industry where you have experience.
10. Enter your job function:
	* **Job Function**: Enter the specific job function or role you are interested in.
11. Select your work schedule:
	* **Work Schedule**: Choose one of the options (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Weekends, Flexible, or Variable) that best describes your availability.
