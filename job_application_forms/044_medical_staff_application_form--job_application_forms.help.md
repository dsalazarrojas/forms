# Medical Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive information from individuals applying to work in the medical field. It helps to ensure that all applicants have the necessary credentials and qualifications for their role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information and documents ready for submission.
2. Complete each field accurately and truthfully, providing relevant details about your background, skills, and qualifications.
3. For fields with options, select the correct response from the available choices.
4. Attach any required documents, such as medical licenses or certifications, as specified in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Information** (`Main Information`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of yourself, including your name and any other identifying information.
* **Medical Staff** (`Medical Staff`, `select_one`, required: false): Check if you are currently or have been a medical staff member. Choose 'Yes' if you have worked in the medical field before, 'No' otherwise.
* **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check if you have worked in the medical field before. Select all relevant experiences, such as hospitals, clinics, or private practices.
* **Education** (`Education`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm if you have completed any medical education or training. Select 'Yes' if you have a medical background, 'No' otherwise.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Medical Specialties** (`Medical Specialties`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check if you are proficient in any medical specialties, such as pediatrics, surgery, or cardiology. Select all relevant specialties.
* **Language** (`Language`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Confirm your language proficiency, including languages spoken or read fluently.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, required: false): Provide information about your emergency contact, including their name, relationship, and contact details.
* **Work History** (`Work History`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your work history, including dates of employment and job titles.
* **Medical License** (`Medical License`, `text`, required: false): Attach your medical license, if applicable.
* **Certification** (`Certification`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm if you hold any medical certifications, such as ACLS or PALS. Choose 'Yes' if you have certifications, 'No' otherwise.
* **Personal Statement** (`Personal Statement`, `text`, required: false): Write a personal statement, introducing yourself and highlighting your relevant qualifications and experience.
* **Medical Staff Application Form Attachments** (`Medical Staff Application Form Attachments`, `text`, required: false): Upload any supporting attachments, such as references or medical transcripts, as required.
