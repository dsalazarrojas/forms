# Summer Activities Staff Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Summer Activities Staff Application Form is a tool used by staff members to apply for summer activities positions. It collects relevant information about your experience, skills, and qualifications to help us match you with suitable roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select a suitable category that matches your area of expertise (e.g., Activity Leader, Counselor, Food Service, Medical Staff).
2. Enter your job title or the title you would like to be known as on our team.
3. Provide your years of experience in the relevant field.
4. Describe your educational background.
5. Select one or more skills that you possess, such as Leadership, Communication, Teamwork, and Time Management.
6. Specify your availability for the summer season (e.g., dates, hours, or specific time slots).
7. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
8. Provide your email address for communication and follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes your role or area of expertise. This will help us match you with suitable summer activities positions.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current or desired job title. This will help us understand your level of authority and responsibility.
* **Experience**: Enter your years of experience in the relevant field. This will help us assess your level of expertise.
* **Education**: Briefly describe your educational background, including degrees, certifications, or relevant courses completed.
* **Skills**: Select one or more skills that you possess, such as Leadership, Communication, Teamwork, and Time Management. These skills will help us understand your capabilities.
* **Availability**: Specify your availability for the summer season. This can include dates, hours, or specific time slots.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes. This will enable us to reach out to you for further information or to discuss potential opportunities.
* **Email**: Provide your email address for communication and follow-up. This will help us keep you informed about summer activities positions and opportunities.
