# Hiring Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Hiring Inquiry Form" is a form used to gather information from candidates or potential applicants about their qualifications, experience, and interest in a job position at the company.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, providing accurate and relevant information.
2. Check the required fields before submitting the form.
3. Use the "Job Information" page to provide more specific job details if applicable.
4. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, `text`, required): Provide your basic contact information, such as name, address, and contact details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Provide more contact details, including phone number and email.
* **Job Information** (`job_info`, `text`, not required): Provide more specific job details if applicable.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your current employment status from the dropdown list.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Fill in the job title you are applying for.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, not required): Provide more details about your work experience related to the job.
* **Education Information** (`education_info`, `text`, not required): Provide more details about your educational background.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, `text`, required): Fill in the location of the job.
* **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, `date`, not required): Fill in the date you started your current employment, if applicable.
* **Employment End Date** (`employment_end_date`, `date`, not required): Fill in the date you ended your current employment, if applicable.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your current job.
* **Other Job Title** (`job_title_other`, `text`, not required): Provide more details about your job title.
* **Other Job Title 2** (`job_title_other_2`, `text`, not required): Provide more details about your second job title.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, not required): Provide more details about your job description.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, `text`, required): Fill in the job requirements of the position you are applying for.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of job you are applying for.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, `text`, not required): Fill in the category of the job.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of the job.
* **Job Opening Type** (`job_opening_type`, `select_one`, not required): Select the type of job opening.
* **Job Opening Status** (`job_opening_status`, `select_one`, not required): Select the status of the job opening.
* **Job Opening Type** (`job_opening_type_2`, `text`, not required): Fill in the type of job opening.
* **Job Opening Description** (`job_opening_description`, `text`, not required): Fill in the description of the job opening.
* **Job Opening Note** (`job_opening_note`, `text`, not required): Fill in any notes about the job opening.
* **Submission Note** (`submission_note`, `text`, not required): Fill in any submission notes.
