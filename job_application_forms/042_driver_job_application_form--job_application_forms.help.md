# Driver Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals who want to apply for a driver job. It's intended to assess and filter applicants for a position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. If you have a vehicle, select "Yes" in the "vehicle_type" field.
5. If you have a vehicle, enter the make in the "vehicle_make" field.
6. Enter the year of your vehicle in the "vehicle_year" field.
7. If you have a valid driver's license, select "Yes" in the "license_type" field.
8. Enter the number of years of experience you have as a driver in the "years_of_experience" field.
9. Provide a description of your driving experience in the "driving_experience_description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name**: A required field to enter your name.
* **email**: A required field to enter your email address.
* **phone**: A required field to provide your phone number.
* **vehicle_type**: A field to indicate if you have a vehicle. Select "Yes" if you own a vehicle.
* **vehicle_make**: A required field to enter the make of your vehicle if you have one.
* **vehicle_year**: A required field to enter the year of your vehicle if you have one.
* **license_type**: A field to indicate if you have a valid driver's license. Select "Yes" if you have a valid license.
* **years_of_experience**: A required field to enter the number of years of experience you have as a driver.
* **driving_experience_description**: A required field to provide a description of your driving experience.
