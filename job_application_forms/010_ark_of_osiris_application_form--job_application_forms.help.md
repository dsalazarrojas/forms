# Ark Of Osiris Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ark Of Osiris Application Form is a comprehensive form designed to help us assess your gaming profile, experience, and team commitment. It's a crucial step in the application process for joining our team. Please take the time to carefully complete this form to ensure we get a complete understanding of your background and motivations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and provide detailed information for each field.
2. Ensure you answer all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Enter your responses in the format requested for each field (e.g., select from options, type a specific number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Section 1: Gamer Profile
* **Real Name** (\*: text): Enter your real name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Gaming Username** (\*: text): Enter your in-game username, used in all games and platforms.
* **Email Address** (\*: email): Enter a valid email address for communication and updates.
* **Discord Username** (optional, text): Enter your Discord username for communication and team coordination.
* **Age** (\*: number): Enter your age to ensure we comply with EULA requirements.
* **Country** (\*: text): Enter the country of your residence.

### Section 2: Gaming Experience
* **How many years playing Ark of Osiris** (\*: select_one): Select the duration of your gaming experience with Ark of Osiris.
* **Current Character Level** (\*: number): Enter your current character level in the game.
* **Approximate Hours Played per Week** (\*: number): Estimate the number of hours you spend playing the game per week.
* **Preferred Gaming Role** (\*: select_one): Choose your preferred gaming role (e.g., Warrior, Mage, Ranger).
* **Previous Guilds or Team Affiliations** (optional, text): List any teams or guilds you have been part of.
* **Position or Rank** (optional, text): Specify your position or rank in previous teams or guilds.
* **Years of Team-Based Gaming** (\*: select_one): Select the duration of your team-based gaming experience.
* **PvP Combat Experience** (\*: select_one): Choose your level of PvP combat experience (Extensive, Moderate, Some, Limited, None).
* **Team Experience Duration** (optional, text): Specify the length of time you have been gaming with a team.

### Section 3: Team Commitment
* **Expected Weekly Playtime Availability** (\*: select_one): Choose the number of hours you are willing and able to play per week.
* **Raid Attendance** (\*: select_one): Confirm your commitment to attending scheduled raids and events.
* **Comfort with Voice Communication** (\*: select_one): Select your comfort level with voice communication (headset and microphone, listening only, prefer text only, False).
* **Team Commitment** (optional, text): Specify your willingness to commit to a team environment.

### Section 4: Skills and Motivation
* **Key Gaming Strengths** (\*: text): List your key gaming strengths or skills.
* **Motivation for Joining the Team** (\*: text): Explain why you want to join our team.
* **Additional Information or Comments** (optional, text): Share any additional information or thoughts.
