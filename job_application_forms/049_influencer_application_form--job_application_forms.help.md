# Influencer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Influencer Application Form is a tool for individual social media influencers to submit their application to be considered for collaboration with our brand. This form helps us to gather information about your online presence and experience in order to assess your suitability for potential partnerships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following fields with accurate and up-to-date information:

1. Enter your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Enter your email address associated with your online presence (e.g. the one you use for your social media profiles).
4. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
5. Provide your website or portfolio URL (if applicable).
6. Select the category related to your expertise (e.g. beauty, fashion, gaming, etc.).
7. Describe your experience as an influencer.
8. Mention your location.
9. Write a brief bio about yourself and your online presence.
10. Upload your profile picture.
11. Provide the URL of your profile on social media platforms (if applicable).
12. Enter your social media username (if applicable).
13. Enter your social media password (if applicable).
14. Select the social media platform you are most active on.
15. Enter the number of followers you have on your social media platform.
16. Enter your engagement rate (e.g. likes, comments, etc.) on your social media platform.
17. Enter the number of posts you have on your social media platform.
18. Enter your followers rate (e.g. growth rate, etc.).
19. Enter your engagement rate (e.g. engagement rate, etc.).
20. Enter your posts rate (e.g. posting frequency, etc.).
21. Provide the URL of your profile picture.
22. Provide the URL of a video (if applicable).
23. Upload any additional files (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your real name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter the email address associated with your online presence (e.g. the one you use for your social media profiles).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Website**: Enter the URL of your personal website or portfolio (if applicable).
* **Category**: Select the category related to your expertise (e.g. beauty, fashion, gaming, etc.).
* **Experience**: Describe your experience as an influencer, including the platforms you've worked with and your role.
* **Location**: Enter your city or region.
* **Bio**: Write a brief description of yourself and your online presence, including your niche and expertise.
* **Profile Picture**: Upload a photo of yourself or your logo.
* **Profile URL**: Enter the URL of your social media profile (if applicable).
* **Social Media Username**: Enter your social media username (if applicable).
* **Social Media Password**: Enter your social media password (if applicable).
* **Social Media Platform**: Select the social media platform you are most active on (e.g. Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, etc.).
* **Followers**: Enter the number of followers you have on your social media platform.
* **Engagement**: Enter your engagement rate (e.g. likes, comments, etc.) on your social media platform.
* **Posts**: Enter the number of posts you have on your social media platform.
* **Followers Rate**: Enter your followers growth rate (e.g. 10% growth in the past month).
* **Engagement Rate**: Enter your engagement rate (e.g. engagement rate, etc.).
* **Posts Rate**: Enter your posting frequency (e.g. 5 posts per week).
* **Profile Picture URL**: Enter the URL of your profile picture.
* **Video URL**: Enter the URL of a video showcasing your content (if applicable).
* **File**: Upload any additional files required for your application (e.g. a portfolio of your work).
