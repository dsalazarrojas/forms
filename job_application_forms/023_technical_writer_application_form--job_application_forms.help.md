# Technical Writer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for applying to a technical writing position within the company. Please make sure you are eligible to apply and that you are not submitting a duplicate application. To check if your application has been received, please contact the relevant department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the field labeled "First Name". This field is not required.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the field labeled "Last Name". This field is not required.
3. Enter your **Job Title** in the field labeled "Job Title". This field is not required.
4. Enter your **Email** in the field labeled "Email". This field is required to be a valid email address.
5. Enter your **Phone** in the field labeled "Phone". This field is not required.
6. Attach a copy of your **Resume** in the field labeled "Resume". This field is required to be in a format that can be uploaded.
7. Enter your **Skills** in the field labeled "Skills". This field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title or position you are applying for.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required): Attach a copy of your resume in a format that can be uploaded.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): List your relevant skills and experience in this field.
