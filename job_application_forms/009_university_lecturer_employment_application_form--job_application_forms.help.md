# University Lecturer Employment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Lecturer Employment Application Form is designed to collect necessary information from candidates applying for lecturer positions at our institution. This form helps us assess each candidate's qualifications, experience, and fit for the role. By providing accurate and detailed responses, candidates can help us understand their potential contributions to our academic community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and concise information.
2. Choose relevant options for the Department and Job Position fields, as they are crucial for our review process.
3. Attach a resume with relevant details about your academic experience and qualifications.
4. Write a clear Motivation Statement that explains why you are a strong candidate for the position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy (e.g., 12/12/1990).
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication and contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter a contact phone number where we can reach you.
* **Department**: Select one or multiple departments that you have experience in or are interested in, from the following options:
	+ Academic
	+ Engineering
	+ Science
	+ Arts
* **Job Position**: Select one or multiple job positions that match your qualifications and experience, from the following options:
	+ Lecturer
	+ Adjunct Lecturer
	+ Assistant Professor
	+ Associate Professor
* **Resume**: Attach a document containing relevant details about your academic experience, qualifications, and achievements.
* **Motivation Statement**: Write a clear and concise statement explaining why you are a strong candidate for the position, highlighting your relevant skills, experience, and fit for the role.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Ensure your resume is up-to-date and relevant to your application.
* Proofread your Motivation Statement carefully to showcase your writing skills.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [your institution's contact information].
