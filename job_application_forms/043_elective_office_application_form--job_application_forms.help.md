# Elective Office Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elective Office Application Form is used to capture information from applicants for elective office positions within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Electve Office Application** (1)
	* This field is the first page of the form and requires no additional information.
2. **Contact Person** (2)
	* Enter the name and contact details of the person filling the form.
3. **Address** (3)
	* Enter your address in the format "Street, City, Country".
4. **Email** (4)
	* Enter your email address in the format "example@example.com".
5. **Phone Number** (5)
	* Enter your phone number in the format "01234 567890".
6. **Elective Office** (6)
	* Select "Yes" if you are applying for an elective office position, "No" otherwise.
7. **Elective Office Title** (7)
	* Enter the title of the elective office position you are applying for.
8. **Party Name** (8)
	* Enter the name of the party or organization you are representing (if applicable).
9. **Party ID** (9)
	* Enter the ID of the party or organization you are representing (if applicable).
10. **Position** (10)
	* Enter your position within the party or organization (if applicable).
11. **Resume** (11)
	* Upload your resume in PDF format.
12-15 **Elective Office Application Forms 2-5** (12-15)
	* These fields are for additional comments or information. You can enter up to 4 more lines of text.
16 **Elective Office Application Form 6** (16)
	* This field is currently not in use.
17-25 **Elective Office Application Forms 7-15** (17-25)
	* These fields are currently not in use. Please disregard.

## Tips

* Ensure your answers are accurate and complete.
* Use clear and concise language in your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the HR department.
