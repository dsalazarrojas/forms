# Tech Industry Developer Job Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for job applicants to submit their applications for tech industry development roles. Please ensure that this is the correct form for your intended application purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "personal-info" field.
2. Provide your contact information in the "contact-info" field.
3. Upload your resume in the "resume-upload" field.
4. Describe your work experience in the "work-experience" field.
5. Indicate your technical skills and proficiency levels in the "tech-skills" field.
6. Select your education level in the "education" field.
7. Specify the languages you are proficient in in the "languages-spoken" field.
8. Choose your desired job role in the "desired-role" field.
9. Select your availability date and time in the "availability" field.
10. Specify your preferred work arrangements in the "preferred-work" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info (personal-info)**: Enter your full name, email address, and phone number.
* **Contact Info (contact-info)**: Enter your full name, email address, and phone number.
* **Resume Upload (resume-upload)**: Attach your resume file in PDF or Word format.
* **Work Experience (work-experience)**: Describe your previous work experience, including job title, company name, and duration of employment.
* **Tech Skills (tech-skills)**: Select the technical skills you possess, such as programming languages, frameworks, and tools.
* **Education (education)**: Select your highest level of education completed.
* **Languages Spoken (languages-spoken)**: Specify the languages you are proficient in.
* **Desired Role (desired-role)**: Choose your desired job role in the tech industry.
* **Availability (availability)**: Select a date and time when you are available to start work.
* **Preferred Work (preferred-work)**: Specify your preferred work arrangement, such as full-time or part-time.

## Tips
* Ensure that your resume is in PDF or Word format and is no more than 2 pages in length.
* Be as specific as possible when describing your work experience and skills.
* Use standard date and time formats when filling in the "availability" field.
* Proofread your application carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
