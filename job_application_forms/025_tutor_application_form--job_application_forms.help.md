# Tutor Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from potential tutors about their qualifications, experience, and availability for offering tutoring services. Please fill out this form to help us get to know you better and consider your application for tutoring opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your application information in the "Application Form" section (Page 1).
2. Provide your student information in the "Student Info" section (Page 2).
3. Add your contact information in the "Contact Info" section (Page 3).
4. Specify your education level (e.g., High School Diploma, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree, or Doctoral Degree) in the "Education Level" section (Page 4).
5. Share your relevant experience (number of hours or years) in the "Experience" section (Page 5).
6. Choose your preferred teaching method (e.g., In-person, Online, or Both) in the "Teaching Method" section (Page 6).
7. Indicate your availability for tutoring sessions in the "Availability" section (Page 7).
8. Rate your availability (number of sessions you can commit to per week) in the "Rate" section (Page 8).
9. Enter your contact information (name, email, and/or phone number) in the "Contact" section (Page 9).
10. Leave any additional notes or comments in the "Note" section (Page 10).
11. Enter your email address in the "Email" section (Page 11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Application Form (Page 1)**: A text field for entering your application information.
- **Student Info (Page 2)**: A text field for providing student information, such as name, age, or other relevant details.
- **Contact Info (Page 3)**: A text field for entering your contact information (name, email, and/or phone number).
- **Education Level (Page 4)**: Select one of the provided options (High School Diploma, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree, or Doctoral Degree) to indicate your highest level of education.
- **Experience (Page 5)**: Enter the number of hours or years of experience you have in teaching or tutoring.
- **Teaching Method (Page 6)**: Choose one of the provided options (In-person, Online, or Both) for your preferred teaching method.
- **Availability (Page 7)**: Enter your availability for tutoring sessions in a specific format (e.g., "Monday to Friday, 3-5 PM").
- **Rate (Page 8)**: Enter the number of tutoring sessions you can commit to per week.
- **Contact (Page 9)**: Enter your name, email, and/or phone number for contact purposes.
- **Note (Page 10)**: Leave any additional notes or comments about your application.
- **Email (Page 11)**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
- **Submit (Page 12)**: Click the submit button to complete and submit your application.
