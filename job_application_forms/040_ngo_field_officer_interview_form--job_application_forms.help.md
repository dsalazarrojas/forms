# NGO Field Officer Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect basic information from candidates applying for the NGO Field Officer position. The goal is to gather relevant details to assess the candidate's qualifications and suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
2. Be honest and provide truthful information about your qualifications and experience.
3. If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Candidate Information**
**Label: Candidate Information**
**Type: Text**
**Required: No**
This field is where you provide a brief introduction about yourself, including your name and any relevant information you'd like to share with the hiring team.

### 2. **Contact Information**
**Label: Contact Information**
**Type: Text**
**Required: No**
In this field, please enter your contact details, including your email address and phone number. This will ensure that the hiring team can get in touch with you for further discussions.

### 3. **Work Experience**
**Label: Work Experience**
**Type: Text**
**Required: No**
 Describe your previous work experience, including the job title, duration, and a brief description of your responsibilities and achievements.

### 4. **Education Background**
**Label: Education Background**
**Type: Text**
**Required: No**
Provide a brief description of your educational background, including degrees earned, institutions attended, and any relevant courses or certifications.

### 5. **Work Authorization**
**Label: Work Authorization**
**Type: Text**
**Required: No**
If you have any required work authorization or clearance, please provide details in this field. This includes any permits, licenses, or approvals necessary for your work as a Field Officer.

## Tips
- Be as concise as possible while providing accurate information.
- Proofread your application carefully to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the hiring team for assistance.
