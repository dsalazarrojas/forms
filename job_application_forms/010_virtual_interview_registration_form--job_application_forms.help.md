# Virtual Interview Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for a virtual interview. Please complete it accurately and honestly.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Enter your phone number, if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
3. Provide your address.
4. Select your availability for the virtual interview.
5. Choose your preferred contact method (email, phone, mail, or WhatsApp).
6. Answer how you heard about us.
7. Provide any additional information about yourself, such as your job title, resume, skills, languages, and employment status.
8. If applicable, provide details about your previous job title, company, salary, and employment status.
9. Select your current and job type.
10. Finally, provide any additional information in the message field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
* **Address**: Enter your address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Availability**: Select the days of the week you are available for the virtual interview.
* **Preferred contact method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, mail, or WhatsApp).
* **How did you hear about us?**: Answer how you came to know about our company.
* **Message**: Provide any additional information about yourself that is not covered in the previous fields.
* **Job Sought**: Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Job Sought (other)**: If your job title is not listed, provide the alternative job title.
* **Education**: Enter your educational background (e.g., bachelor's degree, master's degree).
* **Work Experience**: Enter any relevant work experience.
* **Skills**: Enter your relevant skills and qualifications.
* **Languages**: Enter the languages you speak fluently.
* **Previous Job Title**: Enter your previous job title.
* **Previous Company**: Enter the company you previously worked for.
* **Previous Salary**: Enter your previous salary.
* **Current Salary**: Enter your current salary.
* **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status.
* **Job Type**: Select the type of job you are looking for (full-time, part-time, freelance, volunteer, intern, or contract).
* **Message**: Enter any additional information about yourself that is not covered in the previous fields.
