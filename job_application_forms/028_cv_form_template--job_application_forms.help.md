### CV Form Template - Help Guide

### Purpose
This form template is designed to collect detailed and standardized information about a candidate's professional background and qualifications. It is intended to be used as a supplement to a resume, providing a more comprehensive view of a candidate's experience, skills, and achievements.

### How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the basic professional information in the first section.
2.  Move on to the recent work experience section, listing the most recent position first. For each position, provide the company name, job title, and dates of employment.
3.  Continue with the educational background section, detailing your degree or qualification, university or school name, and year of graduation.
4.  In the skills section, list your technical and soft skills, separating them clearly to highlight your areas of expertise.
5.  Select your soft skills from the given options or add any other relevant skills not listed.
6.  Indicate if you have references available upon request.
7.  Finally, add any additional information you believe is important for the employer to know.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: This field is for your full name, as it appears on official documents and communication.
* **Professional Email Address**: This field is for your professional email address used for work or professional correspondence.
* **Telephone Number**: This field is for your work-related telephone number.
* **Most Recent Company**: This field is for the name of your current or most recent employer.
* **Job Title**: This field is for the title of your current or most recent position.
* **Dates of Employment**: Provide the start and end dates of your employment, including any current or ongoing employment.
* **Key Responsibilities and Achievements**: Summarize your key responsibilities and achievements in the role, using bullet points if necessary.
* **Degree or Qualification**: This field is for your degree or qualification, e.g., Bachelor's or Master's degree.
* **University or School Name**: This field is for the name of the institution where you received your degree or qualification.
* **Year of Graduation**: This field is for the year you graduated.
* **Technical Skills**: This field is for listing your technical skills, e.g., programming languages, software proficiency.
* **Soft Skills**: Select from the given options or add your own relevant soft skills, e.g., communication, leadership.
* **Are References Available Upon Request**: Indicate whether you have references available upon request.
* **Additional Information**: This field is for any additional relevant information you believe is important for the employer to know, e.g., awards, publications, or hobbies.
