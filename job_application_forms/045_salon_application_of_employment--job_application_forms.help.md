# Salon Application Of Employment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Salon Application Of Employment" form is designed to collect contact information and job-related details from applicants interested in working at a salon or spa.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields, which include:

* Your contact information (name, email, phone number, and address)
* Job-related details (job title, job type, and desired salary)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info (Optional):**
	+ <label> (Name):* (text): Enter your name as you want to be addressed.
	+ <label> (Email):* (text): Enter your valid email address.
	+ <label> (Phone): (text): Enter your phone number.
	+ <label> (Address): (text): Enter your mailing address.
* **Job Info (Required)**:
	+ <label> (Job Title):* (text): Enter the job title you are applying for (e.g. "Hair Stylist", "Esthetician").
	+ <label> (Job Type): (text): Select the type of job you are interested in (e.g. "Full-time", "Part-time", "Internship").
	+ <label> (Desired Salary):* (text): Enter your desired salary range.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any additional information or attachments you would like to include with your application, you can add them in the space provided.
* Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to send your application.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact the salon or spa where you are applying.
