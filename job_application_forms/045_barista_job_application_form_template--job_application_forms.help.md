# 045_barista_job_application_form_template--job_application_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Barista Job Application Form is a template designed to collect information from potential candidates for barista positions in a coffee shop. This form gathers essential details about the applicant's contact information, experience, and qualifications for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number in the **Personal Information** section.
2. Indicate your experience as a barista in the **Coffee and Hospitality Experience** section. Select the correct duration of your experience from the options provided.
3. Choose the equipment you are comfortable using in the **Equipment Knowledge** section. Select all that apply.
4. Describe what "great customer service" means to you in the **Hospitality Philosophy** section.
5. State your favorite coffee drink and why you enjoy it in the **Favorite Coffee** section.
6. Select your desired work hours in the **Availability and Commitment** section.
7. Indicate your availability for early morning shifts and weekend work in the **Availability and Commitment** section.
8. Choose your earliest potential start date for the role in the **Final Details** section.
9. Indicate if you have a current Food Handler's Permit and how you heard about the opening in the **Final Details** section.
10. Sign the **Signature Acknowledgment** section to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Provide your active email address where we can reach you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Current Address** (`address`, text, required): Type your current address for our records.
* **Have you worked as a barista before?** (`previous_barista_exp`, select_one, required): Select the duration of your experience as a barista from the options provided.
* **Equipment you are comfortable using** (`equipment_knowledge`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the equipment you are comfortable using in a barista role.
* **What does "great customer service" mean to you in a coffee shop?** (`hospitality_philosophy`, text, required): Explain what "great customer service" means to you in the context of a coffee shop.
* **What is your favorite coffee drink and why?** (`favorite_coffee`, text, required): State your favorite coffee drink and explain why you enjoy it.
* **How many hours per week are you looking for?** (`desired_hours`, select_one, required): Select your desired work hours from the options provided.
* **Are you available for early morning shifts?** (`early_bird_check`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are available for early morning shifts.
* **Weekend Availability** (`weekend_availability`, select_one, required): Choose your weekend work availability.
* **Earliest potential start date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the earliest date you could start in the role.
* **Do you have a current Food Handler's Permit?** (`food_handler_permit`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have a current Food Handler's Permit.
* **How did you hear about this opening?** (`application_source`, select_one, required): Select how you heard about the opening.
* **I certify that the information provided is accurate** (`signature_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Confirm that the information provided is accurate by selecting "I Agree".
