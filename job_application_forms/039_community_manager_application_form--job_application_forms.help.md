# Community Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Manager Application Form is a comprehensive tool used to gather information from potential candidates interested in the Community Manager role. By filling out this form, you will be considered for the position and will have the opportunity to showcase your skills and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full legal name in the "Full Legal Name" field.
2.  Provide your primary contact email in the "Primary Contact Email" field.
3.  Enter your primary contact phone number in the "Primary Contact Phone" field.
4.  Share your LinkedIn profile URL in the "LinkedIn Profile URL" field (optional).
5.  Indicate the total years of professional community management experience in the "Total Years of Professional Community Management Experience" field.
6.  Select all relevant platforms you have managed at a professional level in the "Core Platform Expertise" field.
7.  Briefly describe your approach to conflict resolution in the "Briefly Describe Your Approach to Conflict Resolution" field.
8.  Explain why you are interested in the Community Manager role in the "Why Are You Interested in This Community Manager Role?" field.
9.  Indicate your experience in managing community or project budgets in the "Do You Have Experience Managing Community or Project Budgets?" field.
10. Select all days you are available for a virtual interview in the "General Weekly Availability for a Virtual Interview" field.
11.  Enter your annual salary expectations in the "Annual Salary Expectations (USD)" field.
12.  Share a link to your resume or online portfolio in the "Link to Resume or Online Portfolio" field.
13.  Indicate if you give permission to contact your previous community references in the "May We Contact Your Previous Community References?" field.
14.  Finally, enter the date of your application submission in the "Date of Application Submission" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Full Legal Name** (Required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on government records.
*  **Primary Contact Email** (Required): This is your primary contact email that our team can use to reach you.
*  **Primary Contact Phone** (Required): Enter your primary phone number that our team can use to contact you.
*  **LinkedIn Profile URL** (Optional): Share a link to your LinkedIn profile to showcase your professional experience.
*  **Total Years of Professional Community Management Experience** (Required): Indicate the total years of experience you have in managing communities.
*  **Core Platform Expertise** (Required): Select all platforms you have managed at a professional level.
*  **Briefly Describe Your Approach to Conflict Resolution** (Required): Describe how you handle conflicts or disputes with community members.
*  **Why Are You Interested in This Community Manager Role?** (Required): Explain your motivation for applying for this role.
*  **Do You Have Experience Managing Community or Project Budgets?** (Required): Indicate your experience in managing budgets for communities or projects.
*  **General Weekly Availability for a Virtual Interview** (Required): Select all days you are available for a virtual interview.
*  **Annual Salary Expectations (USD)** (Required): Enter your expected salary in USD.
*  **Link to Resume or Online Portfolio** (Required): Share a link to your resume or online portfolio showcasing your work.
*  **May We Contact Your Previous Community References?** (Required): Indicate if you give permission for us to contact your previous references.
*  **Date of Application Submission** (Required): Enter the date you submit your application.
