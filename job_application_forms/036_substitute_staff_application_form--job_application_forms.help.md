# Substitute Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Substitute Staff Application Form is a tool used by internal staff members to submit their applications for substitute teaching positions within the school district. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field, including your name and contact details.
2. Select the job application from the dropdown menu in the "Job Application" field.
3. Enter the date of your first day of work in the "Work Experience" field.
4. Select the type of education you have completed from the dropdown menu in the "Education" field.
5. Click on the "Submit" field to submit your application.
6. Upload your resume in the "Upload Resume" field.
7. Agree to the terms and conditions in the "Agreement" field by selecting "I have read and agreed to the terms and conditions".
8. Upload any supporting documents required for your application in the "Upload Supporting Documents" field.
9. Confirm your agreement to the second set of terms and conditions in the "Agreement 2" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (Required): Enter your name and contact details, including your email address and phone number.
* **Job Application** (Required): Select the job application you are applying for. This is a crucial field as it helps the school district administration to route your application to the relevant area.
* **Work Experience** (Required): Enter the date of your first day of work. This is necessary to ensure that the school district administration has an accurate record of your employment history.
* **Education** (Required): Select the type of education you have completed. This will help the school district administration to evaluate your qualifications for the position.
* **Upload Resume** (Required): Please upload your resume to provide more information about your qualifications and experience.
* **Agreement** (Required): Select "I have read and agreed to the terms and conditions" to confirm that you have read and understood the agreement.
* **Upload Supporting Documents** (Required): Upload any supporting documents required for your application, such as references or certificates.
* **Agreement 2** (Optional): This is a second confirmation of your agreement to the terms and conditions.
