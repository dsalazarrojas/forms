# Environmental Sector Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Sector Job Application Form is designed to collect job application information from candidates in the Environmental Sector industry. It helps employers find suitable candidates for their open positions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with your personal details, including name, email, and other relevant information.
2. Provide your work experience, education, and skills that align with the job posting.
3. Add any references or recommendations that can support your application.
4. Enter the job-related information, including job title, type, location, and posting date.
5. Submit your application by filling in all required fields and clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info** (`1`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter your name, email, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Work Experience** (`2`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Describe your previous work experience in the Environmental Sector industry.
* **Education** (`3`, `text`, required: false):
	+ List your educational background and qualifications relevant to the job posting.
* **Skills** (`4`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Select the skills you possess that align with the job posting requirements.
* **References** (`5`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Provide any references or recommendations that can support your application.
* **Email** (`7`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Job Title** (`8`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the job title of the position you are applying for.
* **Job Type** (`9`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Specify the type of job you are applying for (e.g., full-time, part-time, contract).
* **Location** (`10`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the location where you would like to work.
* **Phone** (`11`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Job Description** (`12`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Briefly describe the job you are applying for.
* **Start Date** (`13`, `date`, required: false):
	+ Enter the start date of the job you are applying for.
* **End Date** (`14`, `date`, required: false):
	+ Enter the end date of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Status** (`15`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Specify the current job status you are applying for (e.g., available, employed).
* **Job Sector** (`16`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the job sector you are applying for (e.g., environmental conservation, sustainability).
* **Job Category** (`17`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Select the job category you are applying for (e.g., operations, management).
* **Job Level** (`18`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the job level you are applying for (e.g., junior, senior).
* **Job Posting** (`19`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the job posting number or ID.
* **Job Location** (`20`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the location of the job posting.
* **Company Name** (`21`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of the company posting the job.
* **Contact Person** (`22`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the contact person's name and email.
* **Job URL** (`23`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the URL of the job posting.
* **Job ID** (`24`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the unique ID of the job posting.
* **Job Notes** (`25`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Add any additional notes or comments about the job posting.
