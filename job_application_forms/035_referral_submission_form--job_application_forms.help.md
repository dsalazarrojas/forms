# Referral Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit referrals for job applicants. It helps the hiring team to get more information about the applicant and their job search experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields with the relevant information about the job applicant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, optional): Please fill out the name of the job applicant.
* **Email** (email, optional): Please enter the job applicant's email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Please enter the job applicant's phone number.
* **Address** (text, optional): Please enter the job applicant's address.
* **City** (text, optional): Please enter the city of the job applicant's location.
* **State** (select_one, optional): Please select the state where the job applicant is located. For example, 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Zip** (number, optional): Please enter the job applicant's zip code.
* **Job Title** (text, optional): Please enter the job title of the job applicant.
* **Referral Date** (date, optional): Please enter the date of the referral.
* **Referred By** (select_multiple, optional): Please select who referred the job applicant. For example, 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Submitted By** (select_one, optional): Please select who submitted the referral. For example, 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Please enter any additional comments or notes about the job applicant.
* **Submitted Date** (date, optional): Please enter the date the referral was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (time, optional): Please enter the time the referral was submitted.
* **Referred** (text, optional): Please enter a description of the job applicant's referral source.
* **Referred Phone** (text, optional): Please enter the referred person's phone number.
* **Referred Email** (email, optional): Please enter the referred person's email address.
* **Submitted By Phone** (text, optional): Please enter the submitted person's phone number.
* **Submitted By Email** (email, optional): Please enter the submitted person's email address.
* **Status** (select_one, optional): Please select the status of the referral. For example, 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
