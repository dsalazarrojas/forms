# Physiotherapist Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from applicants for a physiotherapist position. Please ensure you have all required qualifications and experience before applying.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the contact information section (page 1) with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide your experience in the field of physiotherapy in the experience section (page 2).
3. Select your educational background in the education section (page 3).
4. List your certifications (e.g. Certified, Licensed, Registered) in the certifications section (page 4).
5. Attach your resume and upload it to the resume section (page 5).
6. Indicate your current license status (Active or Inactive) in the license section (page 6).
7. Select your availability for work hours in the availability section (page 7).
8. Choose your relevant skills (e.g. Manual, Sports, Orthopedic) in the skills section (page 8).
9. Enter your language proficiency in the language section (page 9).
10. Provide a brief description of your work experience in the work experience section (page 10).
11. Explain why you are interested in a physiotherapist position in the why physio section (page 11).
12. Describe your preferred work environment in the work environment section (page 12).
13. Enter your desired salary in the salary section (page 13).
14. Indicate your work hours in the work hours section (page 14).
15. Provide the contact information of an emergency contact person in the emergency contact section (page 15).
16. Add any other relevant information in the other info section (page 16).
17. List your professional references in the references section (page 17).
18. Indicate the availability of your references in the references 2 section (page 18).
19. Add any other references in the other references section (page 19).
20. Select the documents you have uploaded (Resume, Cover Letter, References) in the uploaded documents section (page 20).
21. Select the documents you have uploaded (Resume, Cover Letter) in the uploaded documents 2 section (page 21).
22. Select the documents you have uploaded (References, Other) in the uploaded documents 3 section (page 22).
23. Select the documents you have uploaded (ID and Proof of Education, ID and Proof of Licensure) in the uploaded documents 4 section (page 23).
24. Select the documents you have uploaded (ID and Proof of Licensure, Other) in the uploaded documents 5 section (page 24).
25. Select the documents you have uploaded (Yes, No) in the uploaded documents 6 section (page 25).
