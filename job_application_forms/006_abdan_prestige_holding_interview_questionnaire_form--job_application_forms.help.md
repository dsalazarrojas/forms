# Abdan Prestige Holding Interview Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Abdan Prestige Holding Interview Questionnaire Form is designed to collect essential information from job applicants, allowing us to assess their qualifications, experience, and fit for available positions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability, providing accurate and truthful information.
2. Ensure that you complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. If you are unsure about any question or field, please provide a brief explanation or request clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`candidate_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email address** (`candidate_email`, `email`, required): Provide your current or most recent email address.
* **Phone number** (`candidate_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your current or most recent phone number.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the standard YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Nationality** (`nationality`, `text`, required): Describe your nationality.
* **Highest level of education attained** (`highest_education_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the highest level of education you have achieved:
	+ High School Diploma
	+ Diploma or Certificate
	+ Bachelor's Degree
	+ Master's Degree
	+ Doctorate
	+ Other
* **Field of study or major** (`field_of_study`, `text`, required): Specify your field of study or major.
* **Name of educational institution** (`institution_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your educational institution.
* **Total years of relevant work experience** (`years_of_experience`, `select_one`, required): Choose the range that best describes your relevant work experience:
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1 to 3 years
	+ 3 to 5 years
	+ 5 to 10 years
	+ More than 10 years
* **Current or most recent employer** (`current_employer`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your current or most recent employer.
* **Current or most recent job title** (`current_job_title`, `text`, required): Describe your current or most recent job title.
* **Reason for leaving current or most recent position** (`reason_for_leaving`, `text`, optional): Provide the reason for leaving your current or most recent position (if applicable).
* **Position you are applying for** (`position_applied_for`, `text`, required): Specify the position you are applying for.
* **Expected monthly salary** (`expected_salary`, `number`, optional): Enter the expected monthly salary in your local currency (if applicable).
* **Earliest available start date** (`available_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the earliest date you are available to start.
* **Why are you interested in working at Abdan Prestige Holding** (`why_interested`, `text`, required): Describe your interest in working at Abdan Prestige Holding.
* **What are your key strengths relevant to this role** (`key_strengths`, `text`, required): List your key strengths relevant to this role.
* **Are you willing to relocate if required** (`willing_to_relocate`, `select_one`, required): Choose your willingness to relocate:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Open to discussion
* **Any additional information you would like to share** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information that may be helpful for your application.
