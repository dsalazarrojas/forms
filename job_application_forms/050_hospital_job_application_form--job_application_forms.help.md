# Hospital Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals applying for jobs within the hospital setting. It's used for internal purposes, such as hiring and recruitment processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Ensure that you answer all questions completely and concisely.
3. If you are unsure about any of the information, please ask your HR representative for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
**First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Please enter your full first name.

### Last Name
**Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Please enter your full last name.

### Date of Birth
**Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `text`, **required**): Please enter your date of birth in the standard date format (DD/MM/YY).

### Email
**Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Please enter your valid email address.

### Phone Number
**Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, **required**): Please enter your phone number in the format (123) 456-7890.

### Address
**Address** (`address`, `text`, **required**): Please enter your physical address.

### Education Background
**Education Background** (`education_background`, `text`, **required**): Please enter your educational background, including degrees, qualifications, and institutions.

### Previous Work Experience
**Previous Work Experience** (`previous_work_experience`, `text`, **required**): Please provide a concise description of your previous work experience, including job titles, employers, and dates.

### Personal Statement
**Personal Statement** (`personal_statement`, `text`, **required**): Please write a brief personal statement, describing why you are suitable for the job, and what qualities you possess that make you an ideal candidate.

### Job Title
**Job Title** (`job_title`, `select_one`, **required**): Please select one of the following job titles: Doctor, Nurse, Manager, or Admin.

### Hospital Location
**Hospital Location** (`hospital_location`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Please select all applicable hospital locations: Boston, New York, Los Angeles, or Chicago.
