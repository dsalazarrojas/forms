## Stage Management Position Acceptance - Help Guide
## 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. **Confirm the Stage Management Position**: Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm or reject the stage management position.
2. **Select Start Date** (if applicable): If you are accepting the position, choose the date you are willing to start.
3. **Agree to Stage Management Position Terms**: Type in your agreement to the terms of the stage management position.
4. **Specify Role Expectations**: Describe your understanding of your role and responsibilities in this position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **confirm_stage_management_position**: Confirm whether you accept or reject the stage management position. This field is required as it is a crucial step in the acceptance process.
* **start_date**: If you are accepting the position, select a start date for your work. This field is optional, so you can leave it blank if you are not sure about the start date yet.
* **agree_to_stage_management_position**: Specify which option best describes your agreement to the stage management position. Please note that this field is optional, but it's essential to understand the terms of your agreement.
* **agree_to_stage_management_position_agreement_terms**: Type in your agreement to the terms of the stage management position. This field is required as it ensures you understand and accept the terms of your agreement.
* **role_expectations**: Describe your understanding of your role and responsibilities in this position. This field is required to ensure you are clear on your expectations and obligations.
