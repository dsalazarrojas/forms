# Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Application Form is designed to collect essential information from job applicants for staff positions within the organization. Please provide accurate and complete information to help us assess your qualifications and fit for the job.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2.  Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3.  Provide your valid email address in the **Email** field. Please note that we will use this email address to communicate with you about your application.
4.  Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5.  Specify the **Job Position** you are applying for.
6.  Briefly describe your **Work Experience** relevant to the job position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name (text):** Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name (text):** Enter your last name.
*   **Email (email):** Provide your valid email address that we will use to communicate with you about your application.
*   **Phone (text):** Enter your phone number.
*   **Job Position (text):** Specify the job position you are interested in applying for.
*   **Work Experience (text):** Briefly describe your relevant work experience that aligns with the job position. 

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled in completely and accurately before submitting the form.
