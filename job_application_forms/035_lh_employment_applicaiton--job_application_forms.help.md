# LH Employment Applicaiton - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LH Employment Application is a form designed to collect information from job applicants, including their employment history, educational background, and descriptions of their internships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Sign the social covenant (page 1)
2. Complete the internship application (page 2)
3. Upload your documents (page 3)
4. Provide your employment history (page 4)
5. Share your educational background (page 5)
6. Describe your internship experience (page 6)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sign Social Covenant** (page 1, text): This field is where you sign a social covenant, which is a statement that you will abide by the company's code of conduct and rules.
* **Complete the Internship Application** (page 2, text): This field is where you provide detailed information about your internship experience, including any relevant job titles, dates, and responsibilities.
* **Upload your documents** (page 3, text): In this field, you can upload any relevant documents that support your application, such as transcripts, diplomas, or proof of employment.
* **Employment History** (page 4, text): This field is where you list your previous work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.
* **Educational Background** (page 5, text): This field is where you provide information about your educational background, including degrees earned, institutions attended, and relevant courses or training programs.
* **Internship Description** (page 6, text): This field is where you describe your internship experience, including the company or organization you interned with, your job title, and any notable accomplishments or achievements.
