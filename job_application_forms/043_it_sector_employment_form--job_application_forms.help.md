# IT Sector Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employment information from applicants in the IT sector. It's essential to complete this form accurately and truthfully to ensure a successful application process.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, start by providing your contact information in the first section.
*   Next, provide your email address, which is required to verify your identity.
*   Then, confirm your phone number and upload your resume for us to review.
*   Please indicate your work experience, education, and skills in the following sections.
*   Specify your job type, job title, job place, and employment status in the next sections.
*   Finally, enter your employment date, employment duration, available hours, salary, job description, and job requirements in the last sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact (1)** (`contact`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact information for us to get in touch with you.
*   **Email (2)** (`email`, `email`, required/true): Enter your email address to verify your identity.
*   **Phone (3)** (`phone`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose 'Yes' to confirm your phone number.
*   **Resume (4)** (`resume`, `note`, required/true): Upload your resume for us to review.
*   **Work Experience (5)** (`work_experience`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check 'Yes' if you have work experience.
*   **Education (6)** (`education`, `select_one`, required/true): Select 'Yes' if you have education in the relevant field.
*   **Skills (7)** (`skills`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your relevant skills.
*   **Job Type (8)** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose 'Yes' if this is your job type.
*   **Job Title (9)** (`job_title`, `text`, required/true): Enter your job title.
*   **Work Place (10)** (`work_place`, `text`, required/true): Enter your workplace.
*   **Employment Status (11)** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose 'Active' if you are currently employed.
*   **Job Status (12)** (`job_status`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose 'Active' if you are currently working.
*   **Employment Date (13)** (`employment_date`, `date`, required/true): Enter the date of your last employment.
*   **Employment Duration (14)** (`employment_duration`, `text`, required/true): Enter how long you have been employed.
*   **Available Hours (15)** (`available_hours`, `time`, required/true): Enter the number of hours you have available to work.
*   **Salary (16)** (`salary`, `number`, required/true): Enter your annual salary.
*   **Job Description (17)** (`job_description`, `text`, required/true): Enter your job description.
*   **Job Requirements (18)** (`job_requirements`, `text`, required/true): Enter any special job requirements.
*   **Work Schedule (19)** (`work_schedule`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose 'Yes' if you can work a regular schedule.
*   **Work Location (20)** (`work_location`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose 'Yes' if you are willing to travel for work.
*   **Language (21)** (`language`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose 'Yes' if you speak the language required for the job.
*   **Education Level (22)** (`education_level`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose 'High' for a higher education level.
*   **Education Degree (23)** (`education_degree`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose 'Yes' if you have a degree in the relevant field.
*   **Employment Status 2 (24)** (`employment_status_2`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose 'Active' if you are currently employed.
*   **Available Days (25)** (`available_days`, `text`, required/true): Enter the number of days you have available to work.

## Tips

*   Please fill out all required fields to ensure a complete application.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
