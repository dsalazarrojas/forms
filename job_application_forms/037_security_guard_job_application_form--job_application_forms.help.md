# Security Guard Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a job application form for potential security guards. It is used to collect personal details, work preferences, and relevant experience for evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") with accurate information.
2. For optional fields, provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your application.
3. Review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your current address as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Select the job title you are applying for. Options: Security Guard, Surveillance Officer.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select any of the job types that you are interested in. Options: Security Guard, Surveillance Officer.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, required): Enter the date and time you are available to start working.
* **Work Schedule** (`work_schedule`, time, required): Enter your preferred work schedule. Please use the format: day of the week, start time, end time (e.g., Monday, 8:00 AM, 5:00 PM).
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your relevant experience.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your educational background.
* **Skills** (`skills`, note, optional): List any relevant skills you have that align with the job requirements.
* **Referees** (`referees`, select_multiple, optional): Select up to two referees who can vouch for your character. Options: Primary Referee, Secondary Referee.
* **Upload Document** (`upload_document`, note, optional): Upload any relevant documents that support your application.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Select "Submit" to complete and submit your application.
* **Assigned Form** (`assigned_form`, select_one, optional): Select "chatjimmy" if you are assigned to a specific form. Options: chatjimmy, Other.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_by`, text, optional): If you are assigned to a specific form, enter the name of the form assigned to you.
* **Date Assigned** (`date_assigned`, date, optional): Enter the date you were assigned to a specific form.
