# SOPW Officer & Committee Member Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SOPW Officer & Committee Member Agreement is a form used to collect information from System of Personalized Medicine (SOPW) officers and committee members. This form aims to gather essential data to facilitate effective communication, collaboration, and decision-making within the SOPW team. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Officer Name in the first field.
2. Provide your Title, which is typically your position within the SOPW team.
3. If required, select a valid date for the Agreement Date. This date will be used to track when you accepted or updated your agreement.
4. If required, enter your Organization Name to help identify the organization you represent.
5. Add your contact information, which can include your email, phone number, or any other contact method you prefer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Officer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Title**: Enter your position or title within the SOPW team. This is usually a specific role or function within the team.
* **Date**: If you need to agree to the terms on a specific date, enter that date here. Otherwise, you can leave this field blank.
* **Organization Name**: If you are representing an organization, enter its name here.
* **Contact**: Enter your preferred contact information, such as email, phone number, or any other method you prefer.

## Tips
- Please ensure your contact information is accurate and up-to-date so that the SOPW team can reach you effectively.
- Review the agreement terms before signing to ensure you understand your responsibilities and obligations. 

Please note that this form is primarily designed for SOPW officers and committee members. If you are not an officer or committee member, please do not fill out this form. The fields marked as "required" are essential and must be filled out accurately for the form to be valid.
