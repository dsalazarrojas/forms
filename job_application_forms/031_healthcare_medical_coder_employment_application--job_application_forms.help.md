# healthcare_medical_coder_employment_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The healthcare medical coder employment application form is designed to collect information from applicants interested in applying for healthcare medical coder positions. This form is used to gather contact information, employment history, education background, medical license information, professional references, and additional information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete all required fields with accurate information.
2.  Ensure all fields are filled in correctly before submitting the form.
3.  Double-check the form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form Data** (`Form Data`, text, required: false): This is the main content area of the form where you can provide any additional information about yourself.
*   **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, required: false): Please enter your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
*   **Employment History** (`Employment History`, text, required: false): Describe your previous work experience in the healthcare industry, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.
*   **Education Background** (`Education Background`, text, required: false): List your medical degree and any relevant coursework or training.
*   **Medical License Info** (`Medical License Info`, text, required: false): If you hold a medical license, provide the state where it was issued.
*   **Medical Certifications** (`Medical Certifications`, text, required: false): List any relevant medical certifications you hold.
*   **Professional References** (`Professional References`, text, required: false): Provide the names and contact information of professional references.
*   **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, text, required: false): Please provide any additional information that you feel is relevant to your application.
*   **Submit** (`Submit`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" to submit your application.
*   **Medical Degree** (`Medical Degree`, select_multiple, required: false): Check "Yes" if you have a medical degree.
*   **Medical Field** (`Medical Field`, select_multiple, required: false): Check "Yes" if you have experience in the medical field.
*   **Medical Specialties** (`Medical Specialties`, select_multiple, required: false): Check "Yes" if you specialize in a particular area of healthcare.
*   **Medical License State** (`Medical License State`, select_one, required: false): Check "Yes" if you hold a medical license in the state specified.
*   **Medical Licenses** (`Medical Licenses`, select_multiple, required: false): Check "Yes" if you hold multiple medical licenses.
*   **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required: false): Please enter your date of birth in the correct format.
*   **Date of Employment** (`Date of Employment`, date, required: false): If you have any employment history, enter the date you were last employed.
*   **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for communication.
*   **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
*   **Address** (`Address`, text, required: false): Enter your address.
*   **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, select_multiple, required: false): Check "Yes" if you have relevant work experience.
*   **Medical School** (`Medical School`, text, required: false): If applicable, please enter the name of your medical school.
*   **Work Experience Years** (`Work Experience Years`, number, required: false): Enter the number of years you have relevant work experience.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you answer all required fields accurately and truthfully.
*   If you have any doubts or concerns about a particular field, please don't hesitate to contact us.
*   Double-check your application before submitting it.
