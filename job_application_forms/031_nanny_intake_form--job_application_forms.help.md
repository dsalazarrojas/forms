# Nanny Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from potential nannies to help families find the right fit for their childcare needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the required fields marked with a *.
2. Enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
3. If you're unsure about any field, contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, *): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, *): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, *): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Employment Status** (`Employment Status`, `select_one`, *): Select your current employment status from the options provided.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `text`, *): Enter your job title or a brief description of your previous work experience.
* **Years of Experience** (`Years of Experience`, `number`, *): Enter the number of years you have worked as a nanny or in a related field.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, `select_multiple`, ): Select your available days of the week for work.
* **Available Dates** (`Available Dates`, `select_multiple`, ): Select the specific dates you are available to work.
* **Available Time** (`Available Time`, `time`, *): Enter your available work hours (e.g., 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM).
* **Job Types** (`Job Types`, `select_multiple`, ): Select the types of jobs you are willing to take.
* **Salary Range** (`Salary Range`, `number`, ): Enter your expected salary range per hour.
* **Job Location** (`Job Location`, `text`, *): Enter the specific location you are willing to work.
* **Notice Periods** (`Notice Periods`, `select_one`, ): Select your required notice period for work.
* **Notice Flexible** (`Notice Flexible`, `select_one`, ): Select your flexible notice period for work.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, ): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`Emergency Phone`, `text`, ): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **User Input 1-10** (`User Input 1-10`, `text`, ): Enter any additional information you'd like to share about yourself.
