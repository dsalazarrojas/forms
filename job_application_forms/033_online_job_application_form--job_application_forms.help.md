# Online Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Online Job Application Form" is a digital platform designed for individuals to submit their applications for job openings. This form captures essential information to help employers and hiring teams assess applicants' qualifications and suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, ensure you've identified the specific job opening for which you wish to apply. If you've already submitted an application for the same job, please do not resubmit.
2.  Fill out the form with your accurate and honest information.
3.  Provide the required fields for each section as specified by the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required/false): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required/false): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/false): Enter your email address where the employer can contact you.
*   **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required/false): Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
*   **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `text`, required/false): Specify the job title you're applying for.
*   **Experience Years** (`Experience Years`, `number`, required/false): Enter the number of years of experience you have in your profession.
*   **Job Type** (`Job Type`, `select_one`, required/false): Select one of the options: Full Time, Part Time, or Remote, depending on the job type you're applying for.
*   **Resume** (`Resume`, `text`, required/false): Attach a PDF or document containing your resume.
*   **Note** (`Note`, `note`, required/false): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding your application.
*   **Job Location** (`Job Location`, `text`, required/false): Specify the location where you are willing to work.

## Tips

*   Please ensure all fields are filled with accurate and honest information.
*   Be prepared to attach a resume or other supporting documents as required.
*   If you have any difficulties or questions, contact the hiring team for assistance.
