# Retail Sales Assistant Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Sales Assistant Interview Form is designed to gather information from potential candidates for retail sales positions. This form will help assess a candidate's basic details, availability, and suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
- Ensure you have provided all required fields (indicated by a asterisk *).
- Review and verify your details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in the application process and in any potential future communications.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted regarding your application or any other related matters.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Indicate the specific job title or position you are applying for (e.g., Sales Assistant, Sales Associate).
- **Available Shifts** (`availability`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the days of the week you are available to work (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, etc.). This will help us schedule you for interviews and potential work hours.
- **Work Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Briefly describe your previous work experience, especially any relevant sales experience.
- **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, time, optional): Please provide your available start date and end date if known. If unsure, you can leave it blank.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easier contact and communication.
- **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Mark if you are available to start immediately or if you have other commitments that affect your availability.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments you feel are relevant to your application.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the hiring team for assistance.
- This form is intended to be a helpful guide; however, if you have any questions or need further clarification, please ask.

Please note that this form is designed to assist the candidate in providing the necessary information for the application process. The fields are intended to help the hiring team assess the candidate's suitability for the role. Please answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible. Thank you for your time and consideration.
