# Technician Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for technicians or individuals interested in applying for a job position. It gathers essential information about you, helping us assess your suitability for the role. Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure a successful application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field.
3. Provide your **Work Email** in the third field. Make sure it's a valid email address you regularly use.
4. Enter your **Work Phone** in the fourth field.
5. Upload or paste a link to your **Resume** in the fifth field.
6. Indicate your **Availability** for the job in the sixth field:
	* Select 'Yes' if you are available to work.
	* Select 'No' if you are not available.
7. Choose your **Available Hours** in the seventh field:
	* Select 'Yes' for the hours you are available to work.
	* Select 'No' for the hours you are not available.
8. Enter your **Job Title** in the eighth field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`technician_application`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it is commonly known.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it is commonly known.
* **Work Email** (`work_email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address you regularly use.
* **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your work-related phone number.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required: false): Upload or paste a link to a document with your resume.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you are available to work.
* **Available Hours** (`available_hours`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the times when you are available to work.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title, if applicable or relevant to your application.
