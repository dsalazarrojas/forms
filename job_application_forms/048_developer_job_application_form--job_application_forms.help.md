# developer_job_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The developer job application form is a tool used to collect information from developers applying for a job within the organization. It is essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure that your application is considered complete and processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Resume Input" field with a brief description of your educational background, work experience, and relevant skills. This is a required field.
2. Select up to three ways you would like to be contacted from the "Contact Information" dropdown menu. This field is optional.
3. Choose the programming languages, frameworks, and other skills that you are proficient in from the "Skills" dropdown menu. This field is optional.
4. Describe your most notable projects in the "Projects" field. This is a required field and should be filled out in a note format.
5. Provide details about your education in the "Education" field. This field is optional.
6. Describe your work experience in the "Experience" field. This field is optional.
7. Upload your resume as a document in the "Resume Attachment" field. This field is optional.
8. Write a brief description of your skills in the "Skills Description" field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Resume Input (Required)
* **Resume Input (text)**: A brief description of your educational background, work experience, and relevant skills.

### Contact Information (Optional)
* **Contact Information (select_multiple)**: Choose up to three ways you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, Address).

### Skills (Optional)
* **Skills (select_multiple)**: Choose the programming languages, frameworks, and other skills that you are proficient in.

### Projects (Required)
* **Projects (note)**: Describe your most notable projects in a note format.

### Education (Optional)
* **Education (text)**: Provide details about your educational background, including institution and degrees earned.

### Experience (Optional)
* **Experience (text)**: Describe your work experience, including job titles, company names, and relevant achievements.

### Resume Attachment (Optional)
* **Resume Attachment (text)**: Upload your resume as a document in PDF or other supported formats.

### Skills Description (Optional)
* **Skills Description (text)**: Write a brief description of your skills and how they relate to the job requirements.
 
## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when describing your resume, skills, and experience.
- Proofread your application carefully before submitting to ensure it is complete and accurate.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
