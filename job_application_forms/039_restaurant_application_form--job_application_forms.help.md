# Restaurant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Application Form is designed to collect information from potential employees for job application purposes within the restaurant industry. This form helps us gather relevant details about your work experience, availability, and qualifications to assess your suitability for positions within our restaurant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required information for "Restaurant Information", "Contact Info", and "Resume Upload".
2.  Select your available "Job Positions" and "Hours Worked" as per the options provided.
3.  Provide your work experience in the "Experience" field.
4.  Add any relevant "Special Skills" you have.
5.  Ensure to agree to our "Terms and Conditions" by checking the corresponding boxes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Restaurant Information** (text, required): Fill in the name of the restaurant you are applying to, if any.
* **Contact Info** (text, required): Fill in your name, address, and contact information to allow us to get in touch with you.
* **Availability** (date, required): Select your availability for a job interview or start date.
* **Hours Worked** (number, required): Select how many hours you are willing and able to work per week.
* **Job Positions** (select multiple, required): Choose one or more of the job positions you are interested in applying for.
* **Experience** (text, optional): Add any work experience you have that may be relevant to the position.
* **Education** (text, optional): Add any relevant education that may be beneficial for the position.
* **Special Skills** (text, optional): List any special skills you have that could be an asset for the role.
* **Resume Upload** (text, optional): Upload your resume for us to review.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Provide your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can communicate with you.
* **Agree to Terms** (text, required): Check the box to agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Date** (date, optional): Select a date you are available for a job interview.
* **Time** (time, optional): Select a time you are available for a job interview.
* **Address** (text, optional): Provide your address for us to send any relevant documents or information.
* **City** (text, optional): Enter the city you reside in.
* **State** (text, optional): Enter the state you reside in.
* **Zip** (text, optional): Enter your zip code for us to send any relevant documents or information.
* **Country** (text, optional): Enter your country of residence.
* **Agree To All** (text, optional): Check the box to agree to all terms and conditions.
* **Agreed Terms** (text, optional): This is a redundant field, please ignore.
* **Agree Tos** (text, optional): This field seems to be a repeat of "Agree To All", please ignore.
* **Agree Tos All** (text, optional): This field is a repeat of "Agree To All", please ignore.
* **Terms and Conditions** (text, optional): This is a placeholder, please read and agree to our actual terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill out all required fields.
- Check the job positions you are interested in applying for to ensure we get a correct understanding of your qualifications.
- Be honest and accurate in your answers to ensure a smooth evaluation process.
- Upload your resume to ensure we can review it for your qualifications.
- Ensure you provide a valid email address for us to contact you.
- Agree to our terms and conditions to proceed with the application process.
