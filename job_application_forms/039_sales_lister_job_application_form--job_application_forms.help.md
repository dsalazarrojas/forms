# Sales Lister Job Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sales Lister Job Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants for the sales position at our company. This form helps our HR department assess your eligibility and suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form accurately and honestly, making sure to provide all required information.
2. Ensure that your email address is correct, as this will be the primary contact method for further communication.
3. Enter your contact number to ensure we can reach you for any additional information or next steps.
4. Choose the correct job title that matches your role or position.
5. Select the correct education level that aligns with your qualifications.
6. Enter your years of experience in sales, if applicable.
7. Complete any other optional fields that you feel are relevant to your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, *): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, *): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, *): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, ): Enter your physical address, if you wish to provide it.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `select_one`, *): Choose the job title that best describes your role or position. (e.g., Sales Representative, Sales Manager)
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, *): Enter the number of years you have experience in sales, if applicable.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, ): Choose your highest level of education completed. (e.g., High School Diploma, Bachelor's Degree, Postgraduate Degree)

## Tips

* Please double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact our HR department for assistance.
* We appreciate your honesty and completeness in filling out this form.
