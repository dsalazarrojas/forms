# Supermarket Stock Clerk Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential supermarket stock clerks, including contact details, job preferences, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details, including your name and contact number.
2. Select your availability for work.
3. Choose your job preferences, such as job title and job title 2.
4. Upload your resume if applicable.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Application** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for a brief job application summary.
* **Contact Details** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your name and contact number.
* **Availability** (`3`, `text`, required): Select your availability for work.
* **Qualifications** (`4`, `text`, optional): Describe your qualifications and experiences.
* **Job Preferences** (`5`, `text`, optional): State your job preferences, such as job title and job title 2.
* **Upload Resume** (`6`, `text`, optional): Upload your resume if you have one.
* **Job Title** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Select your desired job title, such as Manager or Stock Clerk.
* **Contact Number** (`9`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more contact numbers.
* **Email** (`10`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Job Title 2** (`11`, `select_one`, optional): Select another job title.
* **Contact Number 2** (`12`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more contact numbers 2.
* **Email 2** (`13`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address 2.
* **Job Application Form** (`14`, `select_one`, required): Select the job application form 2.
* **Date of Birth** (`16`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' or 'False'.
* **Gender** (`17`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender.
* **Age** (`18`, `number`, optional): Enter your age.
* **Salary** (`19`, `select_one`, optional): Select your salary preference.
* **Employment Status** (`20`, `select_one`, optional): Select your employment status.
* **Employment Status 2** (`21`, `select_one`, optional): Select your employment status 2.
* **Availability 2** (`22`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' or 'False'.
* **Qualifications 2** (`23`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'True' or 'False'.
* **Qualifications 3** (`24`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'True' or 'False'.
* **Uploaded Files** (`25`, `text`, optional): This field is not used in this form.

Note: Some fields may not be applicable based on the form configuration.
