## Chef Job Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Chef Job Application Form is designed to collect essential information from applicants who are interested in working as chefs. This form gathers personal details, professional background, experience, and logistical requirements to help the hiring team evaluate applicants for available positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling in the personal information section, including your full name, email address, phone number, and current address.
2. Select the position you are applying for from the options provided.
3. Provide your years of professional culinary experience.
4. Specify your primary cuisine specialization(s).
5. Enter your culinary education and certifications, if applicable.
6. List any food safety certifications you hold.
7. Enter your earliest available start date and expected salary/wage.
8. Confirm your work eligibility status.
9. Provide information about your professional references.
10. (Optional) Share a link to your professional portfolio or LinkedIn profile.
11. Certify that all information provided is accurate.
12. Finally, submit the application and date it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Full Name
• **Full Name** (`applicant_full_name_chef`, text, required): Please enter your full name.

### 2. Email Address
• **Email Address** (`applicant_email_chef`, email, required): Enter your valid email address to which we can send correspondence.

### 3. Phone Number
• **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone_chef`, text, required): Please enter your phone number for us to contact you.

### 4. Current Address
• **Current Address** (`applicant_residential_address`, text, required): Provide your current address for our records.

### 5. Position Applied For
• **Position Applied For** (`target_chef_position`, select_one, required): Select the position you are interested in applying for.

### 6. Years of Professional Culinary Experience
• **Years of Professional Culinary Experience** (`total_culinary_experience_years`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have spent as a professional chef.

### 7. Primary Cuisine Specialization
• **Primary Cuisine Specialization** (`primary_cuisine_specialization`, select_multiple, required): Choose your primary cuisine specialization(s), e.g., French, Italian, etc.

### 8. Culinary Education / Degree
• **Culinary Education / Degree** (`culinary_education_credentials`, text, required): List your culinary school and degree, if applicable.

### 9. Food Safety Certifications
• **Food Safety Certifications** (`food_safety_certifications`, text, required): Enter any food safety certifications you hold, e.g., ServSafe Manager.

### 10. Earliest Available Start Date
• **Earliest Available Start Date** (`earliest_start_date_avail`, date, required): Please select your earliest available start date.

### 11. Expected Salary / Wage
• **Expected Salary / Wage** (`expected_salary_annual_hourly`, text, required): Enter your expected salary/wage, taking into account your experience and position applied for.

### 12. Legally Authorized to Work
• **Legally Authorized to Work** (`work_eligibility_status`, select_one, required): Confirm your work eligibility status.

### 13. Professional References
• **Professional References** (`professional_references_summary`, text, required): Please provide the name, title, and contact information of your professional references.

### 14. Professional Portfolio / LinkedIn
• **Professional Portfolio / LinkedIn** (`culinary_portfolio_url`, text, optional): Share a link to your professional portfolio or LinkedIn profile for us to review.

### 15. Accurate Information
• **I Certify** (`application_certification_statement`, select_one, required): Confirm that all information provided is accurate.

### 16. Submission Date
