# Ambassador Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is for individuals to apply to be an ambassador for the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, ensure you have the necessary information and are eligible to be an ambassador. Fill out the required fields and answer the questions accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (Full name)**: Enter your legal name in the format First Name Last Name.
* **Email address (Email address)**: Enter your primary email address for communication.
* **Phone number (Phone number)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Why are you applying to be an ambassador (Why are you applying to be an ambassador)**: Briefly explain your interest in being an ambassador.
* **Relevant experience (Relevant experience)**: List any relevant experience or past roles that make you a good fit for the role.
* **Availability (Availability)**: Specify your days and times you are available.
* **References (References)**: Provide names and contact information for your references.
* **Consent to background checks (Consent to background checks)**: Select "yes" to consent to background checks if required.

## Tips

* Ensure you are eligible to be an ambassador before applying.
* Be accurate and honest when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the organization.
