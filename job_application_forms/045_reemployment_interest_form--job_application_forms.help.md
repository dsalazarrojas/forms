# reemployment_interest_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Reemployment Interest Form" is a tool for employees to express their interest in being reemployed by the company after a certain period of absence. It's a chance for them to share their availability, preferred roles, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Job Title** (or previous job title) in the corresponding field.
2.  Select your **Availability** on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is "not available" and 10 is "fully available".
3.  Choose your **Role Preferred** from the available options (Software Engineer, Data Scientist, or Other).
4.  Enter your **Available Dates** in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5.  Select your **Preferred Dates** for reemployment (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, etc.).
6.  Enter your **Contact Number** to ensure we can reach you.
7.  Enter your **Email** address for further communication.
8.  Write a brief **Message** explaining why you're interested in reemployment.
9.  Select your preferred **Department** (Sales, Marketing, Product, or Other).
10. Choose any additional **Teams** you're interested in joining (Team A, Team B, Team C).
11. Enter your **Location** where you'd like to work.
12. Select your **Job Status** (Full time, Part time, Contract, or Other).
13. Choose your **Job Type** (Full time, Part time, Contract, or Other).
14. Enter your preferred **Job Category**.
15. Enter your **Job ID** (if applicable).
16. Select your desired **Job Level** (Entry Level, Mid Level, Senior Level, Manager Level).
17. Enter the **Notice Period** for your expected notice.
18. Enter the number of **Prophets** (if applicable).
19. Select your **Job Status 2** (Full time, Part time, Contract, or Other).
20. Enter the number of **Available Hours Per Week**.
21. Enter your expected **Earnings**.
22. Enter your desired **Salary**.
23. Enter your **Job Title 2** (if applicable).
24. Write a brief **Job Description** explaining why you're interested in this role.
25. Enter your **Job ID 2** (if applicable).

*   **Department**: Choose your preferred department from the options provided.
