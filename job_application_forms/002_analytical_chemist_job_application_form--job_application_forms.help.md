# Analytical Chemist Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from analytical chemists interested in employment or promotion opportunities in our organization. By providing detailed personal and professional information, you will be able to showcase your qualifications and increase your chances of being considered for a position.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your educational background, including your highest degree earned, field of study, university or institution name, and year of graduation.
3. Outline your professional experience, including years of experience in analytical chemistry, current or most recent employer, and job title.
4. Select the analytical techniques and software you are proficient in.
5. If applicable, indicate any regulatory experience you have.
6. If you have any additional information you would like to share, such as professional certifications, publications or research, or expected salary range, please provide this in the designated fields.
7. Finally, select the earliest date you are available to start and indicate if you are willing to relocate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full legal name as you would like to be known in the organization.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address that we can use for correspondence.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact number for us to reach out to you.
* **Current Address**: Enter your current residential address.
* **Highest Degree Earned**: Select your highest level of education (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, Doctoral Degree (PhD), Postdoctoral).
* **Field of Study**: Enter your major or field of specialization.
* **University or Institution Name**: Enter the name of the institution where you earned your degree.
* **Year of Graduation**: Enter the year you graduated.
* **Years of Experience in Analytical Chemistry**: Enter your total years of experience in analytical chemistry.
* **Current or Most Recent Employer**: Enter the name of your current or most recent employer.
* **Current or Most Recent Job Title**: Enter your current or most recent position.
* **Analytical Techniques Proficiency**: Select all analytical techniques you are proficient in (e.g., HPLC, GC-MS, LC-MS, NMR Spectroscopy, UV-Vis Spectroscopy, FTIR Spectroscopy, Atomic Absorption Spectroscopy, ICP-MS).
* **Software Proficiency**: Select all software you are proficient in (e.g., ChemStation, MassHunter, Empower, Chromeleon, LabSolutions, Python, R).
* **Regulatory Experience**: Select all regulatory frameworks you have experience with (e.g., FDA, GMP, GLP, ISO 17025, EPA, ICH Guidelines).
* **Professional Certifications**: If you have any relevant professional certifications, list them here.
* **Publications or Research**: If you have any publications or research, briefly describe them here.
* **Expected Salary Range**: If you have any expected salary range, enter it here.
* **Available Start Date**: Select the earliest date you are available to start.
* **Willing to Relocate**: Indicate if you are willing to relocate for this position.
* **Cover Letter or Additional Comments**: Enter any additional information you would like to share about yourself.
