# Public Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals who are applying for a job within our organization. It helps us to gather necessary information from applicants in a structured and organized manner, allowing us to evaluate their suitability for potential positions. The form is designed to be completed by the applicant, providing us with the details needed to review their qualifications and assess their fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the designated fields.
2. Enter your phone number and job title (if applicable).
3. Upload your resume to provide us with more information about your background and qualifications.
4. Answer the following questions with a "Yes" or "No" for:
	* Job Title: Are you looking for a job with a title?
	* Job Description: Do you have any specific job descriptions in mind (e.g., manager, developer, etc.)?
	* Location: Are you available to work at our location?
	* Education: What levels of education have you achieved (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, etc.)? (Select all that apply)
	* Skills: What skills do you possess that would be relevant to the position (e.g., programming, communication, etc.)? (Select all that apply)
	* Availability: Are you available to work at our organization?
	* Work Schedule: What time of day do you prefer to work?
5. Enter your salary and experience expectations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (Required):** Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents or business cards.
* **Last Name (Required):** Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents or business cards.
* **Email (Required):** Enter your work or personal email address.
* **Phone (Optional):** Enter a phone number where we can contact you for further discussion or scheduling.
* **Resume (Optional):** Upload your resume as a PDF or DOCX document.
* **Job Title (Optional):** Answer "Yes" if you are looking for a job with a specific title. You can also type in the title you are looking for.
* **Job Description (Optional):** Answer "Yes" if you have a specific job description in mind. If you have any further information, you can type it in.
* **Job Type (Optional):** Answer "Yes" if you are looking for a full-time or part-time job. If you have any further specifications, please type them in.
* **Location (Optional):** Answer "Yes" if you are available to work at our location. If you have any specific location preferences, please type them in.
* **Salary (Optional):** Enter your expected salary range or type in any salary requirements you have.
* **Experience (Optional):** Enter the number of years you have gained experience relevant to the position.
* **Education (Optional):** Select all the levels of education you have achieved (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, Doctorate, etc.).
* **Skills (Optional):** Select all the skills you possess that would be relevant to the position (e.g., programming, communication, etc.).
* **Availability (Optional):** Answer "Yes" if you are available to work at our organization.
* **Work Schedule (Optional):** Select the time of day you prefer to work (e.g., morning, afternoon, etc.).
