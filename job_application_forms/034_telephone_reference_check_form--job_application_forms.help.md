# telephone_reference_check_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather contact information for a supervisor or previous employer as part of a job application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. If you've had previous work experience, fill in your previous job title in the "job_title" field.
6. If you're providing information about your current or previous job, enter the start date of your employment in the "job_start_date" field.
7. If you're providing information about your current or previous job, enter the end date of your employment in the "job_end_date" field.
8. Fill in the first name of your supervisor or previous employer in the "supervisor_first_name" field.
9. Fill in the last name of your supervisor or previous employer in the "supervisor_last_name" field.
10. Enter the phone number of your supervisor or previous employer in the "supervisor_phone_number" field.
11. Enter the email address of your supervisor or previous employer in the "supervisor_email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **first_name** (text, required): Your first name.
- **last_name** (text, required): Your last name.
- **phone_number** (text, required): Your phone number.
- **email** (email, required): Your email address.
- **job_title** (text, optional): If you've had previous work experience, enter your job title.
- **job_start_date** (date, optional): If you're providing information about your current or previous job, enter the start date of your employment.
- **job_end_date** (date, optional): If you're providing information about your current or previous job, enter the end date of your employment.
- **supervisor_first_name** (text, required): First name of your supervisor or previous employer.
- **supervisor_last_name** (text, required): Last name of your supervisor or previous employer.
- **supervisor_phone_number** (text, required): Phone number of your supervisor or previous employer.
- **supervisor_email** (email, required): Email address of your supervisor or previous employer.
- **supervisor_job_title** (text, optional): Job title of your supervisor or previous employer.
- **supervisor_job_start_date** (date, optional): Start date of your supervisor or previous employer.
- **supervisor_job_end_date** (date, optional): End date of your supervisor or previous employer.
