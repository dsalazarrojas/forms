# Purpose
The Cashier Job Application Form is used to collect information from applicants for cashier positions. This form helps us understand your qualifications, experience, and availability, which will be used to evaluate your suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your full name, date of birth, phone number, email address, and current address.
2. Select the position you are applying for (Cashier, Head Cashier, Lead Cashier, Shift Supervisor, or Other).
3. Choose your preferred hours of work (Full-time, Part-time, Morning Shift, Afternoon Shift, Evening Shift, or Flexible).
4. Enter your available start date.
5. Provide information about your previous employment history, including any previous experience as a cashier or in a retail setting.
6. Select your level of experience as a cashier (Yes, extensive, Yes, some, or No experience).
7. If applicable, list your previous employers and provide a brief reason for leaving.
8. Self-assess your skills in cash handling and accuracy, computer and POS system skills, customer service experience, communication and interpersonal skills, and transportation.
9. Confirm that you have reliable transportation.
10. Agree to a background check (if applicable).
11. Add any additional information or comments you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (id: 2): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (id: 3): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Phone Number** (id: 4): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email Address** (id: 5): Enter your email address.
* **Current Address** (id: 6): Enter your current full street address.
* **City, State, Zip** (id: 7): Enter your city, state, and zip code.
* **Position Applied For** (id: 9): Select the position you are applying for.
* **Preferred Hours** (id: 10): Choose your preferred hours of work.
* **Available Start Date** (id: 11): Enter your available start date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Do you have cashier experience?** (id: 13): Select your level of experience as a cashier.
* **Years of Cashier or Retail Experience** (id: 14): Enter the number of years you have worked as a cashier or in a retail setting.
* **List Previous Employers** (id: 15): List your previous employers and provide a brief reason for leaving.
* **Reason for Leaving** (id: 16): Enter a brief reason for leaving your previous job.
* **Cash Handling and Accuracy** (id: 18): Self-assess your cash handling and accuracy skills (Excellent, Good, Fair, Limited, or No Experience).
* **Computer and POS System Skills** (id: 19): Self-assess your computer and POS system skills (Advanced, Intermediate, Basic, or No Experience).
* **Customer Service Experience** (id: 20): Self-assess your customer service experience (Extensive, Moderate, Limited, or No Experience).
* **Communication and Interpersonal Skills** (id: 21): Self-assess your communication and interpersonal skills (Excellent, Good, Fair, Limited, or No Experience).
* **Do you have reliable transportation?** (id: 23): Confirm that you have reliable transportation.
* **Background Check Consent** (id: 24): Agree to a background check (if applicable).
* **Additional Information or Comments** (id: 25): Add any additional information or comments you would like to share.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* We appreciate your honesty and transparency throughout the application process.
