# Chief Technology Officer Job Application Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us assess your qualifications for the Chief Technology Officer position.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Be honest and accurate in your answers.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`applicant_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`applicant_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **LinkedIn profile URL** (`linkedin_profile`, text, optional): If you have a LinkedIn profile, please provide the URL.
* **GitHub or portfolio profile** (`github_profile`, text, optional): If you have a GitHub or portfolio profile, please provide the URL.
* **Current or most recent title** (`current_title`, text, required): Enter your current or most recent title.
* **Current or most recent organization** (`current_organization`, text, required): Enter the name of your current or most recent organization.
* **Years of technology leadership experience** (`years_tech_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have spent leading technology teams.
* **Years as CTO or VP Engineering or equivalent** (`years_cto_or_vp`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have spent in a CTO or VP Engineering role.
* **Highest education level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Select your highest education level (e.g., Bachelor's degree, Master's degree, Ph.D., etc.).
* **Primary field of study** (`field_of_study`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your primary field of study.
* **Primary programming languages proficiency** (`programming_languages`, select_multiple, required): Select all programming languages you are proficient in.
* **Technology stack expertise** (`technology_stack`, text, required): Describe your technology stack expertise.
* **Cloud platform experience** (`cloud_platforms`, select_multiple, required): Select all cloud platforms you have experience with.
* **Largest engineering team led** (`team_size_led`, select_one, required): Select the size of the largest engineering team you have led.
* **Experience scaling technology infrastructure** (`scaling_experience`, text, required): Describe your experience scaling technology infrastructure.
* **Software architecture and design patterns expertise** (`architecture_expertise`, select_one, required): Rate your expertise in software architecture and design patterns.
* **Security and compliance knowledge** (`security_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate your knowledge of security and compliance.
* **Agile and development methodology experience** (`agile_methodology`, select_one, required): Select your experience with agile and development methodologies.
* **Startup vs enterprise experience** (`startup_vs_enterprise`, select_multiple, required): Select your experience with startups and enterprises.
* **Key technology achievements and accomplishments** (`key_achievements`, text, required): Describe your key technology achievements and accomplishments.
* **Your vision for the company's technology direction** (`vision_statement`, text, required): Describe your vision for the company's technology direction.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask us.
* Please proofread your submission before submitting it.
