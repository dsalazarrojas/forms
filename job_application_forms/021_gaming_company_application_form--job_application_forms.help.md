# Gaming Company Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from candidates applying for roles within the Gaming Company. It's essential to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the form, start by providing your personal details:

- **First Name**: Enter your first name.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
- **Email**: Enter your email address. Please make sure to use a valid email address that you frequently check.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number, if applicable.
- **Job Title**: Enter the job title you're applying for.

Next, provide details about your work experience and qualifications:

- **Job Type**: Select one or more of the available job types that best match your skills and experience.
- **Resume**: Attach your resume or CV to provide more details about your background and qualifications.
- **Job Position Description**: Briefly describe the job position you're applying for.
- **Work Experience (Years)**: If applicable, enter the number of years you've worked in your field.
- **Work Experience (Months)**: If applicable, enter the number of months you've worked in your field.
- **Work Experience Description**: Describe your previous work experience, highlighting relevant roles, responsibilities, and achievements.
- **Work Experience Job**: If you have a specific job title, enter it here.
- **Work Experience Company**: If you've worked for a company, enter the name here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address. Please use a valid email address that you frequently check.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): If applicable, enter your phone number.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, required): Enter the job title you're applying for.
* **Job Type** (Job Type, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the available job types that best match your skills and experience. Options: Software Engineer, Game Designer, Artist, Writer, Producer.
* **Education Level** (Education Level, select_one, required): Select your highest level of education completed. Options: High School, Bachelor, Master, Doctorate, Other.
* **Education Name** (Education Name, text, required): Enter the name of your educational institution.
* **Submission Date** (Submission Date, date, required): Not required for submission, but will be used internally to track application submissions.
* **References** (References, text, required): List at least one professional reference.
* **Reference Name** (Reference Name, text, required): Enter the name of your reference.
* **Reference Relationship** (Reference Relationship, text, required): Describe your relationship with the reference (e.g., supervisor, colleague, mentor).

**Tips:**

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to avoid any delays in the review process.
* Attach a valid resume or CV as a supporting document.
* Be specific when describing your previous work experience and education.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to support for assistance.
