# ประกิน Internship Applicant Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from internship applicants, allowing them to register their interest and provide necessary details for the internship opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name, email address, and any other required fields as prompted.
2. Attach relevant documents such as resumes, cover letters, and work experience attachments as required.
3. Choose your job title, job description, and preferred location from the provided options.
4. Select your available start date, available end date, and preferred communication method.
5. Specify your salary requirements and available workload.
6. Indicate your work experience, education background, and skills.
7. Provide motivation for your application and contact information.
8. Choose your preferred social media platform for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`Applicant Name`, text, required): Fill in your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Resume** (`Resume`, text, required): Attach a copy of your resume in PDF or Word format.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, required): Select the job title that best describes your role or desired role.
* **Job Description** (`Job Description`, text, required): Briefly describe your job responsibilities and achievements.
* **Job Category** (`Job Category`, select_one, required): Choose the category that best suits your job description (Marketing, Sales, IT, Finance).
* **Available Start Date** (`Available Start Date`, date, required): Select the date you are available to start the internship.
* **Available End Date** (`Available End Date`, date, required): Choose the date you expect to end the internship.
* **Preferred Location** (`Preferred Location`, select_one, required): Select the location where you would like to work (USA, Canada, UK, Australia).
* **Salary Requirements** (`Salary Requirements`, text, required): State your expected salary range or requirements.
* **Available Workload** (`Available Workload`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your available workload (Yes, No).
* **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, select_multiple, required): Specify your work experience (Yes, No).
* **Education Background** (`Education Background`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your education background (Yes, No).
* **Skills** (`Skills`, text, required): List any relevant skills you have.
* **Motivation** (`Motivation`, text, required): Provide a brief statement on why you are interested in the internship.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Preferred Communication** (`Preferred Communication`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred communication method (English, French, Spanish).
* **Social Media** (`Social Media`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your presence on social media platforms (Yes, No).
* **Job References** (`Job References`, text, required): Provide information about your job references.
* **Portfolio** (`Portfolio`, text, required): Attach your portfolio or relevant work samples.
* **Resume Attachment** (`Resume Attachment`, text, required): Attach your resume in PDF or Word format.
* **Cover Letter** (`Cover Letter`, text, required): Attach a cover letter explaining your interest in the internship.
* **Skills Attachment** (`Skills Attachment`, text, required): Attach a document listing your skills.
* **Available Workload Attachment** (`Available Workload Attachment`, text, required): Attach a document outlining your available workload.
* **Work Experience Attachment** (`Work Experience Attachment`, text, required): Attach a document detailing your work experience.
