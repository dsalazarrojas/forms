# Hiring Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form template is used to collect information for job postings and applications. It is used to gather details about job openings, including job categories, levels, and posting types, as well as information about the job applicant, such as employment status and job title. The form is designed to be completed by job applicants, HR personnel, or other authorized users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant fields for the job posting or application.
2. Enter the required information for each field, such as the job category, level, and posting type.
3. Provide accurate and detailed information about the job applicant, including employment status and job title.
4. Confirm the job application form by signing and dating it.
5. Attach any additional documents required for the application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Type** (`Job type1`, `Job type2`, `Job type3`): Select the type of job you are applying for or posting.
* **Job Category** (`Category1`, `Category2`, `Category3`): Select the category of the job.
* **Job Level** (`Level1`, `Level2`, `Level3`): Select the level of the job.
* **Job Posting Type** (`Posting type1`, `Posting type2`, `Posting type3`): Select the type of job posting.
* **Salary** (`number`): Enter the salary for the job.
* **Employment Type** (`Type1`, `Type2`, `Type3`): Select the type of employment.
* **Employment Status** (`Status 1`, `Status 2`, `Status 3`): Select the employment status.
* **Job Opening** (`Option 1`, `Option 2`, `Option 3`): Select the job opening options.
* **Job Opening 2** (`date`): Enter the date of the job opening.
* **Candidate Information** (`text`): Enter the candidate's information.
* **Work History** (`text`): Enter the candidate's work history.
* **Education** (`text`): Enter the candidate's education background.
* **Skills** (`text`): Enter the candidate's skills.
* **Experience** (`text`): Enter the candidate's experience.
* **Referees** (`text`): Enter the candidate's referees.
* **Contact Information** (`text`): Enter the candidate's contact information.
* **Job Applicant** (`text`): Enter the candidate's name.
* **Job Application Form Confirmation** (`text`): Confirm that you have read and understand the job application form.
* **Additional Documents** (`text`): Attach any additional documents required for the application.
