# Full Time Employment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect personal and professional details from applicants for full-time employment positions within the organization. This form will help us get to know you better and understand your qualifications for the position you are applying for.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To get started, simply follow the instructions below for each section.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to the hiring team for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (required): Please enter your professional email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (optional): Please enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (optional): Please enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Resume** (optional): Please attach a copy of your resume as a note field. This can be a PDF or a Word document.
* **Employment Status** (optional): Please select your current employment status from the options provided:
	+ Employed
	+ Self-Employed
	+ Unemployed
* **Job Title** (optional): Please enter the title of your current or desired job position.
* **Years of Experience** (optional): Please enter the number of years you have worked in your field.
* **Education** (optional): Please select all applicable education levels from the options provided:
	+ High School Diploma
	+ College Degree
	+ Master's Degree
	+ Doctoral Degree
* **Job Preferences** (optional): Please attach a note field with any job preferences you may have. This can be a list of desired job functions, work hours, or work environment.
* **Work Experience** (optional): Please select all relevant work experience from the options provided:
	+ Customer Service
	+ Sales
	+ Marketing
	+ IT
* **Languages** (optional): Please select all languages you are proficient in from the options provided:
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ French
	+ Mandarin Chinese
* **Availability** (optional): Please attach a note field with any availability information you may have. This can be a list of days and hours you are available to work.
* **Job Title 1** (optional): Please enter the title of your first job position.
* **Job Type** (optional): Please enter the type of job you are applying for (e.g. full-time, part-time).
* **Job Status** (optional): Please select the status of your job application from the options provided:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
* **Job Category** (optional): Please select all applicable job categories from the options provided:
	+ Software Development
	+ Sales
	+ Customer Service
	+ Marketing

Please note that all fields marked as required are necessary for the application process. If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to the hiring team for clarification.
