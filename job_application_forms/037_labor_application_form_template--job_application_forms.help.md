# labor_application_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a template for job applicants to provide their information during the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "Applicant Info" section with your name, email address, and any other relevant contact details.
2.  Choose the relevant work experience from the dropdown menu in the "Work Experience" section.
3.  Select your relevant skills from the dropdown menu in the "Skills" section.
4.  Choose your educational background from the dropdown menu in the "Education Background" section.
5.  In the "Personal References" section, provide the names and contact details of at least two professional references.
6.  Select your desired job title from the dropdown menu in the "Job Title" section.
7.  In the "Job Description" section, provide a brief overview of the job you are applying for.
8.  In the "Availability" section, select your available work hours.
9.  In the "Resume File" section, upload your resume or CV.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Info** (`Applicant Info`, `text`, required/optional): This is the first section where you can input your name, email address, and any other contact details.
* **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your relevant work experience from the dropdown menu.
* **Skills** (`Skills`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your relevant skills from the dropdown menu.
* **Education Background** (`Education Background`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your educational background from the dropdown menu.
* **Personal References** (`Personal References`, `text`, optional): Provide the names and contact details of at least two professional references.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `select_one`, optional): Select your desired job title from the dropdown menu.
* **Job Description** (`Job Description`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the job you are applying for.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, `time`, optional): Select your available work hours.
* **Resume File** (`Resume File`, `text`, optional): Upload your resume or CV.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your work experience, skills, and education background to ensure accuracy.
* Be concise in your job description.
* Use a professional tone throughout the form.
* Upload a clear and concise resume or CV.
