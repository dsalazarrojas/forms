# Office Assistant Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential office assistant applicants. This form is designed to gather relevant details about the applicant, their contact information, job details, and educational background.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details in the **Applicant Info** section (Page 1, Field 1).
2.  Provide your contact information in the **Contact Info** section (Page 1, Field 2).
3.  Describe your job details in the **Job Details** section (Page 1, Field 3).
4.  Share your educational background in the **Education** section (Page 1, Field 4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Applicant Info (Page 1, Field 1)**
	* **Applicant Info**: Enter your personal details such as name, address, and any other relevant information.
### **Contact Info (Page 1, Field 2)**
	* **Contact Info**: Provide your email address and phone number to get in touch with you.
### **Job Details (Page 1, Field 3)**
	* **Job Details**: Describe your job experience, job title, or any relevant job-specific skills.
### **Education (Page 1, Field 4)**
	* **Education**: Share your educational background, qualifications, or relevant courses taken.
