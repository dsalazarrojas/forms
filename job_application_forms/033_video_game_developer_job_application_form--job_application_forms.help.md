# Video Game Developer Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Video Game Developer Job Application Form is designed to gather information from applicants for a position as a video game developer. This form helps us to evaluate your qualifications and suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details such as First Name, Last Name, Contact Email, and Contact Phone.
2. Upload your Resume to showcase your experience and skills.
3. Provide a link to your Portfolio to demonstrate your work.
4. Choose your Job Title and Job Reference (if applicable).
5. Select your Salary Range, Job Type, and Available Date.
6. Specify your Notice Period.
7. Choose your Skills, Job Location, and Languages Spoken.
8. Select your Education Level and Work Experience.
9. Describe your Availability and Job Description.
10. Provide a brief explanation of Why Video Games and Why you are Available for the position.
11. Specify your Contract Type and Job Status.
12. Add any additional Notes (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address that we can contact you on.
* **Contact Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number that we can contact you on.
* **Resume** (5, text, required): Upload your resume to show us your experience and skills.
* **Portfolio Link** (6, text, optional): Provide a link to your portfolio to showcase your work.
* **Job Title** (7, text, required): Enter your job title as it appears on your resume or LinkedIn profile.
* **Job Reference** (8, text, optional): If you have a job reference, provide their name and contact information.
* **Salary Range** (9, select_one, optional): Choose your expected salary range.
* **Job Type** (10, select_one, required): Choose between Full-time, Part-time, Contract, or Freelance.
* **Available Date** (11, date, required): Choose a specific date when you are available to start working.
* **Notice Period** (12, number, required): Specify the number of days' notice you require before starting.
* **Skills** (13, select_multiple, required): Choose your relevant skills such as Programming languages, Web development, Game development, etc.
* **Job Location** (14, text, required): Enter the location where you are available to work.
* **Languages** (15, select_multiple, optional): Choose the languages you speak or read.
* **Education** (16, select_one, required): Choose your highest level of education.
* **Work Experience** (17, number, required): Specify your number of years of work experience.
* **Availability** (18, text, required): Describe your availability and any constraints.
* **Job Description** (19, text, required): Briefly describe your job responsibilities and duties.
* **Why Video Games** (20, text, required): Explain why you are interested in video games.
* **Available For** (21, text, required): Briefly describe why you are available for the position.
* **Contract Type** (23, select_one, optional): Choose between Fixed-term, Rolling contract, or Hourly.
* **Job Status** (24, select_one, required): Choose between Active, Inactive, or On hold.
* **Notes** (25, note, optional): Add any additional information you think is relevant to your application.
