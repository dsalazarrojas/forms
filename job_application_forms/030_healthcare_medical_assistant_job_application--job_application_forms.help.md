# Healthcare Medical Assistant Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in applying for a healthcare medical assistant position. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your application is considered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal details, including your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and Address.
2. Provide your employment history, including your Job Title, Work Place, Employment Start, and Employment End dates.
3. Share your educational background, including your Degree, School, Certificate Type, and Certifying Organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for further communication.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.

### Address
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your current address where you can receive mail and other communications.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your previous job title in the medical field.

### Work Place
* **Work Place** (`work_place`, text, optional): Enter the name of your current or previous workplace.

### Employment Start
* **Employment Start** (`employment_start`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the start date of your employment in the medical field.

### Employment End
* **Employment End** (`employment_end`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the end date of your employment in the medical field.

### Degree
* **Degree** (`degree`, text, optional): Enter the degree you have earned in the medical field.

### School
* **School** (`school`, text, optional): Enter the name of your school where you earned your degree.

### Certificate Type
* **Certificate Type** (`certificate_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of certificate you hold in the medical field.

### Certifying Organization
* **Certifying Organization** (`certifying_organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization that certified you in the medical field.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your contact information, including your email and phone number.
* Be clear and concise in your answers, especially when describing your work and education history.
* If you have any supporting documents, such as certificates or degrees, please attach them to your application.
* Proofread your application before submitting to ensure it is free of errors.
