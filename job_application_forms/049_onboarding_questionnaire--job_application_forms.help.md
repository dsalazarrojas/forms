# onboarding_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The onboarding_questionnaire form is designed to gather information about new employees or job applicants. It helps to streamline the onboarding process and categorize jobs for classification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field (Page 1).
2. Enter your email in the "email" field (Page 1).
3. Indicate your job title and other job-related details as required.
4. Fill out the job-related fields (hiring date, job time, job status, manager's name, job description, department name, job type, job category, job level, job location, supervisor's name, team name, and job family) according to your job information.
5. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **job_title** (`job_title`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more job titles that apply to you.
* **hire_date** (`hire_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you started your job.
* **hire_time** (`hire_time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time you started your job.
* **job_status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate if you are an active or inactive employee.
* **manager_name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **job_description** (`job_description`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your job.
* **department_name** (`department_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your department.
* **job_type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate if this job is yes or no.
* **job_category** (`job_category`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select if this job is yes or no.
* **job_level** (`job_level`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the level of your job, either high or low.
* **job_location** (`job_location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location of your job.
* **supervisor_name** (`supervisor_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **team_name** (`team_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your team.
* **job_family** (`job_family`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate if this job has a family or not.
