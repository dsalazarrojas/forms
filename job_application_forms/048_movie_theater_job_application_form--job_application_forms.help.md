# Movie Theater Job Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants for job openings at a movie theater. It helps us to get to know you better and understand your qualifications for the position you're applying for.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your applicant information, including your first and last name.
2. Select the job title and description that best matches the position you're applying for.
3. Choose your available work dates using the date picker for Job Availability.
4. Upload your resume, if applicable.
5. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Job Title**: Choose the job title that best matches the position you're applying for.
* **Job Description**: Provide a brief description of the job you're applying for.
* **Job Availability**: Select the date you're available to start work using the date picker.
* **Skills**: Select the skills that you think are relevant to the position you're applying for.
* **Resume Upload**: Upload your resume, if you have one.
* **References**: Provide names and contact information for your professional references.
* **Salary Range**: If you have a salary range in mind, enter it here.
* **Emergency Contact**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their name and contact information.
