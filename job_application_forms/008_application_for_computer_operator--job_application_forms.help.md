# Application For Computer Operator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Application For Computer Operator form is used to collect information from potential candidates who are interested in applying for the Computer Operator position. This form helps us to assess your qualifications, experience, and availability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields for contact information, including your application date, name, email, phone number, address, city, state, and zip code.
2. Select the specific position you are applying for from the "Position Applied" dropdown menu.
3. Choose your availability for different shifts from the "Available Shift" dropdown menu.
4. Indicate when you can start working, by selecting a date.
5. Fill out your education level and field of study, and if applicable.
6. Select the number of years of experience you have as a Computer Operator.
7. Choose the operating systems and technical skills you are familiar with.
8. List any relevant certifications you hold.
9. If applicable, fill out the fields for previous employment information.
10. Indicate if you are willing to undergo a background check and if you are authorized to work in this country.
11. Select if you have any additional qualifications or skills.
12. Indicate if you have professional references available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): The date when you are submitting your application.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your legal last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Your primary email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Your full mailing address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): The city of your residence.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): The state or province of your residence.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Your postal or zip code.
* **Position Applied** (`position_applied`, select_one, required): Select the specific position you are applying for.
* **Available Shift** (`available_shift`, select_one, required): Choose the shifts you are available to work.
* **Earliest Start Date** (`earliest_start_date`, date, required): The date when you can start working.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Your highest level of education completed.
* **Field of Study** (`field_of_study`, text, optional): Your major or field of study.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, select_one, required): The number of years of Computer Operator experience you have.
* **Operating Systems** (`operating_systems`, select_multiple, required): Select the operating systems you are familiar with.
* **Technical Skills** (`technical_skills`, select_multiple, required): Select your technical skills.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, text, optional): List any relevant certifications you hold.
* **Previous Employer** (`previous_employer`, text, optional): The name of your most recent employer.
* **Previous Job Title** (`previous_job_title`, text, optional): Your job title in your previous position.
* **Employment Duration** (`employment_duration`, text, optional): How long you worked in your previous position.
* **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, text, optional): Why you left your previous position.
* **Supervisor Contact** (`supervisor_contact`, text, optional): Your previous supervisor's name and contact information.
* **Willing Background Check** (`willing_background_check`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are willing to undergo a background check.
* **Authorized to Work** (`authorized_to_work`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are authorized to work in this country.
* **Additional Qualifications** (`additional_qualifications`, text, optional): Any other qualifications or skills you have.
* **References Available** (`references_available`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have professional references available.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the hiring team.
