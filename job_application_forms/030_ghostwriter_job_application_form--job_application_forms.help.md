# Ghostwriter Job Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Ghostwriter Job Application Form is designed for individuals to submit their application for a job posting. This form will collect necessary information to evaluate a candidate's suitability for the position. Please note that this form is used for internal purposes only and is not intended for public access.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your Name**: This is the field for your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Enter your Email**: This is your professional email address where you can be reached.
3. **Enter your Phone**: This is the phone number where you can be reached.
4. **Enter your Address**: This is your current address.
5. **Enter your Job Title**: This is the job title you are applying for.
6. **Enter your Company (current or previous)**: If you are currently employed, please enter your company name. If you are a freelancer or have previously worked in this field, provide the relevant company name.
7. **Resume (Upload your resume in PDF format)**: Please attach a PDF version of your resume for easy reference.
8. **Portfolio (Provide a link or attachment)**: If applicable, share a link to your online portfolio or attach relevant work samples.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: The field for your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Your professional email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: The phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address**: Your current address.
* **Job Title**: The job title you are applying for.
* **Company (current or previous)**: If you are currently employed, please enter your company name. If you are a freelancer or have previously worked in this field, provide the relevant company name.
* **Resume**: Attach a PDF version of your resume for easy reference.
* **Portfolio**: If applicable, share a link to your online portfolio or attach relevant work samples.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your application is complete.
* Use a clear and concise format when answering the "Company" field.
* If you have a professional online presence, such as a website or blog, consider sharing a link to it in the "Portfolio" field.
* Ensure your resume is in PDF format for easy reading.
