# Fintech Software Engineer Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Fintech Software Engineer Job Application form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please start by filling in the General Information section, which includes any necessary details about yourself that are not covered in the other sections.
2. In the Contact Info section, provide your email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
3. The Work Experience section should be used to describe your relevant work experience, including your job title, company, and a brief summary of your responsibilities and achievements.
4. In the Education section, list your educational background, including your degrees earned and institutions attended.
5. The Skills section is where you should list any relevant skills you have that align with the job requirements.
6. Certifications should be listed in the Certifications section, including any relevant certification names and issuing organizations.
7. Finally, you can attach your resume in the Resume Attachment section to provide more detailed information about your background.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (Page 1)**: This section is for any other general information about yourself that doesn't fit into other categories.
* **Contact Info (Page 2)**: Provide your email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Work Experience (Page 3)**: Describe your previous work experience, including job title, company, and a brief summary of your responsibilities and achievements.
* **Education (Page 4)**: List your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **Skills (Page 5)**: List any relevant skills you have that match the job requirements.
* **Certifications (Page 6)**: List any relevant certifications you have earned.
* **Resume (Page 7)**: Attach your resume to provide more detailed information about your background.
* **Resume Attachment (Page 8)**: Attach your resume in a file format that can be easily opened.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your application is processed correctly.
* Make sure to check the required fields marked as required.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer back to this guide for clarification.
* Attach your resume as a PDF or Word document for easier reading and review.
