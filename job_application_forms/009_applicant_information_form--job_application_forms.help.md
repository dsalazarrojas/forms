# Applicant Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Applicant Information Form is a mandatory form that requires applicants to provide their personal and professional details in order to be considered for the job opening.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
2. In the "Professional Details" section, enter your current job title, total years of professional experience, and highest degree earned.
3. Select your primary skill area from the provided options.
4. If applicable, provide your LinkedIn profile URL, portfolio URL, and information about how you heard about the position.
5. Enter your earliest possible start date.
6. If you have a referral, provide their name and how you heard about the position.
7. Finally, select your desired annual salary range.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number that can be used to contact you.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your mailing address where you can be reached.
* **Current Job Title**: Enter your current job title or position.
* **Total Years Experience**: Enter the number of years you have gained since your highest degree earned.
* **Highest Degree Earned**: Select the highest degree earned from the provided options.
* **Primary Skill Area**: Select the area where you have the most experience and interest.
* **Resume Upload Information**: Note: Please ensure your resume is in PDF or DOCX format and is up to 2MB in size.
* **LinkedIn Profile URL**: Enter your LinkedIn profile URL.
* **Portfolio or Website URL**: Enter your portfolio or website URL.
* **How did you hear about this position?**: Select how you heard about the position from the provided options.
* **Referral Name (If applicable)**: If you have a referral, enter their name.
* **Earliest Possible Start Date**: Enter the date you can start working.
* **Desired Annual Salary Range**: Select the desired salary range from the provided options.
