# Facebook Ad Marketer Recruitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us find the right candidate for our Facebook Ad Marketer position. It collects information about your job experience, preferences, and availability to match you with the best possible job opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Select Your Job Category
- **Job Category (Job Category)**: Please select the category that best describes the job you are applying for, such as "Part-time", "Full-time", "Freelance", and so on.

### Step 2: Provide Job Description
- **Job Description (Job Description)**: Briefly describe the job you are applying for, including your previous job experience and relevant skills.

### Step 3: Enter Budget Information
- **Budget (Budget)**: If you have a specific budget in mind, please enter it here.

### Step 4: Enter Job Details
- **Job Title (Job Title)**: Enter the job title you are applying for.
- **Job Type (Job Type)**: Select whether the job is "Part-time", "Full-time", or "Freelance".
- **Job Location (Job Location)**: Enter the location where you are available to work.
- **Job Start Date (Job Start Date)**: If applicable, enter the date when you can start working on the job.
- **Job End Date (Job End Date)**: If applicable, enter the date when you will finish working on the job.
- **Job Status (Job Status)**: Select the status of your job, such as "Active", "Archived", or "Cancelled".

### Step 5: Enter Ad Information
- **Ad Budget (Ad Budget)**: If applicable, enter the budget for the ad you are working on.
- **Ad Frequency (Ad Frequency)**: Select how frequently you will be advertising, such as "One Day", "Two Days", or "Multiple Days".
- **Ad Type (Ad Type)**: Select the type of ad you will be creating, such as "Display Ad", "Video Ad", or "Carousel Ad".
- **Ad Target Audience (Ad Target Audience)**: Select who you are targeting with your ad, such as "Men", "Women", or "Both".
- **Ad Target Location (Ad Target Location)**: Select the location you are targeting with your ad, such as "United States", "Canada", or "Worldwide".
- **Ad Budget Frequency (Ad Budget Frequency)**: If applicable, enter how often you will be advertising.
- **Ad Budget Period (Ad Budget Period)**: If applicable, enter the period for which you are advertising.
- **Ad Target Audience Age (Ad Target Audience Age)**: Select the age range you are targeting with your ad, such as "18-24", "25-34", "35-44", "45-54", or "55+".
- **Ad Budget Currency (Ad Budget Currency)**: Select the currency for the ad budget, such as "USD", "EUR", or "Other".
- **Ad Budget Amount (Ad Budget Amount)**: If applicable, enter the amount for the ad budget.
- **Ad Duration (Ad Duration)**: Select how long your ad will be running, such as "One Day", "Two Days", or "Multiple Days".
- **Ad Target Location (Ad Target Location)**: Select the location you are targeting with your ad, such as "United States", "Canada", "Worldwide".

### Step 6: Enter Additional Information
- **Contact Email (Contact Email)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
- **Contact Phone (Contact Phone)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **Company Name (Company Name)**: Enter the name of your company, if applicable.
- **Assigned Tool (Assigned Tool)**: If applicable, enter the tool assigned to you for the job.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are providing accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a successful application process.
- If you have any questions, feel free to contact us.
- Thank you for your application!
