# Zoologist Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to submit their application for a zoologist position. A zoologist is a professional who studies the behavior, physiology, and classification of animals. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the candidate's background, experience, and qualifications for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what information is required.
2. Fill in the fields with your relevant information.
3. Provide accurate and truthful responses to each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Info (Page 1)
* **General Info** (`general_info`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can provide a brief description of yourself, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.

### Contact Details (Page 2)
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): In this field, please enter your contact information, including your email address, phone number, and any other contact method you prefer.

### Education (Page 3)
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Describe your educational background, including any degrees, diplomas, or certifications related to zoology or a related field.

### Work Experience (Page 4)
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): List your previous work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.

### Skills (Page 5)
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): In this field, list any relevant skills you have, such as proficiency in languages, software, or tools used in zoology.

### References (Page 6)
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): Provide the names and contact information of two or three professional references who can vouch for your qualifications and experience.

### Upload Resume (Page 7)
* **Upload Resume** (`upload_resume`, text, optional): Please upload your resume as a PDF or Word document. This will help us assess your qualifications more efficiently.

### Submit (Page 8)
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Once you have filled in all the fields, click the "Submit" button to send your application to us.
