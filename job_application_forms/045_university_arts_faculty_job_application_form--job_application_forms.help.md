# University Arts Faculty Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Arts Faculty Job Application Form is designed to collect necessary information from applicants for faculty positions at the University of the Arts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form submission fields**: Enter your name, contact information, and other relevant details in the Form Submission fields (fields 1-5).
2. **Provide education background**: In the Education Background field (field 3), describe your educational qualifications and experience.
3. **Select your job role**: In the Work Experience field (field 7), choose your job title and relevant work experience.
4. **Choose your job type**: In the Job Type field (field 10), select the type of job you are applying for (Faculty, Non-Faculty, or Both).
5. **Enter available date and time**: If applicable, enter your available date and time in the Available Date and Available Time fields (fields 12 and 13).
6. **Upload documents**: If required, upload relevant documents in the Upload Documents field (field 6).
7. **Submit the form**: Click the Submit Button (field 7) to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Form Submission**: Enter your name and other contact information to identify yourself as an applicant.
* **2. Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, including email addresses and phone numbers.
* **3. Candidate Info**: Enter any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant to your application.
* **4. Education Background**: Describe your educational qualifications and experience.
* **5. Job Application**: Enter a brief description of the job you are applying for.
* **6. Upload Documents**: Upload any relevant documents required for the application (e.g., resume, references, etc.).
* **7. Work Experience**: Select your job title and describe your relevant work experience.
* **8. Job Type**: Choose the type of job you are applying for (Faculty, Non-Faculty, or Both).
* **9. Available Date**: Enter your available date for an interview or other relevant date.
* **10. Available Time**: Enter your available time for an interview or other relevant time.
* **11. Available Time Note**: Enter any additional information about your available time.
* **12. Job Application Date**: Enter the date you applied for the job.
* **13. Note**: Enter any additional information about your application.
* **14. Email 2**: Enter an additional email address (e.g., for a second email account).
* **15. Phone 2**: Enter an additional phone number.
* **16. Job Title 2**: Enter an additional job title.
* **17. Job Type 2**: Enter an additional job type.
* **18. Job Description**: Enter a brief description of the job you are applying for.
* **19. Upload Files**: Upload additional files related to your application.
* **20. Submit Button 2**: This is a duplicate submit button.
