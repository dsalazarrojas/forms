# Burger Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Burger Order Form is a simple form designed to collect customer information for ordering burgers. It collects contact details, order preferences, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer information section with your name and email address.
2. Select your preferred order type (e.g. Dine-In, Takeout, Delivery, Curbside Pickup).
3. Choose your burger type (e.g. Classic Beef Burger, Cheeseburger, etc.).
4. Enter the quantity of burgers you would like to order.
5. Select your cooking preference (e.g. Rare, Medium Rare, Medium, Medium Well, Well Done).
6. Choose your desired toppings (e.g. lettuce, tomato, onion, etc.).
7. Select your condiments (e.g. ketchup, mustard, etc.).
8. Choose your side dish (e.g. French Fries, Onion Rings, etc.).
9. Select your drink (e.g. Soda, Water, Milkshake, etc.).
10. Enter any special instructions or allergies.
11. If you have chosen delivery, enter your delivery address and preferred delivery time.
12. Select your payment method (e.g. Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.).
13. Enter any promotional code if you have one.
14. Review your total amount before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for order updates.
* **Order Type**: Select how you would like to receive your order (e.g. Dine-In, Takeout, Delivery, Curbside Pickup).
* **Burger Selection**: Choose your desired burger type (e.g. Classic Beef Burger, Cheeseburger, etc.).
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of burgers you would like to order.
* **Cooking Preference**: Select how you would like your burger cooked (e.g. Rare, Medium Rare, Medium, Medium Well, Well Done).
* **Toppings**: Select your desired toppings (e.g. lettuce, tomato, onion, etc.).
* **Condiments**: Select your desired condiments (e.g. ketchup, mustard, etc.).
* **Sides**: Choose your desired side dish (e.g. French Fries, Onion Rings, etc.).
* **Drinks**: Select your desired drink (e.g. Soda, Water, Milkshake, etc.).
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any special instructions or allergies.
* **Delivery Information**: If you have chosen delivery, enter your delivery address.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter your full delivery address.
* **Preferred Delivery Time**: Enter your preferred delivery time.
* **Payment Method**: Select your payment method (e.g. Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.).
* **Promo Code**: Enter any promotional code if you have one.
* **Total Amount**: This field will be populated with the order total after submitting the form.

Note: All fields marked as "note" are informational fields and will not be populated automatically.
