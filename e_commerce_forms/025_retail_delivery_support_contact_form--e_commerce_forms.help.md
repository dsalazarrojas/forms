# retail_delivery_support_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The retail delivery support contact form is designed to help customers seek assistance with their delivery experiences. This form is used to report delivery issues, request schedule changes, or ask general delivery-related questions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of support you require from the available options.
2. Provide the order reference number or other relevant details about your delivery.
3. Specify the delivery location and date.
4. If relevant, provide evidence or supporting information about your delivery issue.
5. Clearly describe your support request.
6. Choose your preferred method of contact (phone, email, or chat) and provide the corresponding contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Retail Delivery Support** (`retail_delivery_support`, select_multiple): Select one or more reasons for your support request, such as "Yes, I'm experiencing an issue with my delivery" or "No, I have a general question about my delivery."
* **Order Reference** (`order_reference`, text): Enter the order reference number or any other relevant details about your delivery.
* **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`, time): Specify the location of your delivery, such as your address or the specific store where you received your delivery.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date): Enter the date of your delivery, if known. This will help our support team understand your delivery timeline.
* **Evidence** (`evidence`, text): If you have any supporting documents or images related to your delivery issue, please upload or attach them here.
* **Support Request** (`support_request`, text): Clearly describe your support request, including any specific issues or concerns you're experiencing.
* **Retail Delivery Support Contact Method** (`retail_delivery_support_contact_method`, select_one): Select how you'd like to be contacted by our support team, such as phone, email, or chat.
* **Retail Delivery Support Phone Number** (`retail_delivery_support_phone_number`, text): Enter your phone number if you've chosen to be contacted by phone.
* **Retail Delivery Support Email** (`retail_delivery_support_email`, email): Enter your email address if you've chosen to be contacted by email.

## Tips
* Please make sure to complete all required fields to ensure our support team can assist you efficiently.
* If you have any attachments or evidence, please ensure they're attached or uploaded correctly.
* Our support team will respond to your request as soon as possible, typically within 24-48 hours.
