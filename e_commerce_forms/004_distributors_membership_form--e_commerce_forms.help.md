# Distributors Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create or update a distributor's membership record. It is intended for use by the distributor or their authorized representative.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting the form, please make sure you have all the necessary information ready. The process is straightforward, and you can complete it in a few steps.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is spelled.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is spelled.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your company email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your company phone number.
- **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name as it is spelled.
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your company address.
- **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where your company is located.
- **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter your company's zip code.
- **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Select the country where your company is located. Please choose from the options: **United States**, **Canada**, or **Mexico**.
- **Agree** (`agree`, select multiple, optional): Please select the options you agree to from the list. You must select at least one option.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information or note about your account.
- **Assignee Name** (`assignee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the assignee, if applicable.
- **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): Enter the name of the assigned user, if applicable.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the name of the assigned tool, if applicable.
- **Created By** (`created_by`, text, optional): This field is automatically populated and should not be edited.
