# Fitness Equipment New Release Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for a new release of fitness equipment, allowing them to place a pre-order for the product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form by providing your personal details, such as your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and shipping address.
2. Choose your preferred payment method and any additional payment details required.
3. Select your delivery preferences, including whether you want to have the product delivered to your home or pick it up from a store.
4. Provide any additional retailer details, such as the store name, address, and type.
5. Enter the product details, including the quantity, price, and total price.
6. Add any product description and comments from both the customer and retailer.
7. Review and submit the form to place your pre-order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header** (Form Header, Note, Not required): This section provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **First name** (First name, Text, Not required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last name** (Last name, Text, Not required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Not required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for updates on your order.
* **Phone** (Phone, Text, Not required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for updates on your order.
* **Order Date** (Order Date, Date, Not required): Enter the date you would like to place the order.
* **Order Time** (Order Time, Time, Not required): Enter the time you would like to place the order.
* **Shipping Address** (Shipping Address, Text, Not required): Enter the address where the product will be shipped to.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, Select One, Not required): Choose from the available payment methods (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Stripe).
* **Payment Details** (Payment Details, Note, Not required): Enter any additional payment details required for the order.
* **Delivery Preferences** (Delivery Preferences, Select One, Not required): Choose from the available delivery options (Home delivery, Store pickup, In-store pickup).
* **Retailer Details** (Retailer Details, Text, Not required): Enter any additional details about the retailer.
* **Store Name** (Store Name, Text, Not required): Enter the name of the store where the product will be available for pickup.
* **Store Address** (Store Address, Text, Not required): Enter the address of the store where the product will be available for pickup.
* **Gym Name** (Gym Name, Text, Not required): Enter the name of the gym where the product will be available for pickup.
* **Gym Address** (Gym Address, Text, Not required): Enter the address of the gym where the product will be available for pickup.
* **Store Type** (Store Type, Select One, Not required): Choose from the available store types (Online store, Offline store, Both).
* **Retailer Type** (Retailer Type, Select One, Not required): Choose from the available retailer types (Gym, Retailer, Online store).
* **Quantity** (Quantity, Number, Not required): Enter the quantity of the product you would like to order.
* **Price** (Price, Number, Not required): Enter the price of the product.
* **Total Price** (Total Price, Number, Not required): Enter the total price of the order.
* **Product Description** (Product Description, Text, Not required): Enter a description of the product.
* **Retailer Comment** (Retailer Comment, Text, Not required): Enter comments from the retailer.
* **Customer Comment** (Customer Comment, Text, Not required): Enter comments from the customer.
* **Form Footer** (Form Footer, Note, Not required): This section provides a final note about the form and its submission.
