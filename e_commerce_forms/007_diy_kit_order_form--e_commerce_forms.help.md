# DIY Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DIY Kit Order Form is designed to collect customer information and order details for the purchase of a DIY kit. To use this form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required fields with the requested information. You can navigate through the pages of the form using the navigation controls.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`Order Details`, `text`, required/optional: false): Please enter a brief description of your order, including any specific details or instructions you'd like to include.
* **Customer Information** (`Customer Information`, `text`, required/optional: false): This field is for your personal information, including your name and contact details. Please fill it in accurately to ensure smooth communication and order fulfillment.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your address to receive your DIY kit. This will help us to ship your order to the correct location.
* **name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents. This will help us to verify your identity and process your order efficiently.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
