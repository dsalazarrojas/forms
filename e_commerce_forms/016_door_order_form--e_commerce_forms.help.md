# door_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The door_order_form is used to collect information for door orders in an e-commerce or order management system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Enter Your Name" field.
2. Enter your address in the "Enter Your Address" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (format: 555-1234).
4. Enter your email in the "Email" field.
5. Select your time of day in the "Time of Day" field (24-hour format: 14:00).
6. Choose one option from the "Select One" field.
7. Select multiple options from the "Select Multiple" field.
8. Enter your order preference in the "Order Preference" field.
9. Enter any additional notes about the order in the "Note" field.
10. Enter the date of the order in the "Date of Order" field (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
11. Enter your customer ID in the "Customer ID" field.
12. Select the person assigned to the order in the "Assigned To" field.
13. Enter the total cost of the order in the "Order Total" field.
14. Enter the time of the order in the "Time of Order" field.
15. Select your payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
16. Enter any additional notes about the order in the "Order Notes" field.
17. Select the status of the order in the "Order Status" field.
18. Enter any additional customer notes in the "Customer Notes" field.
19. Select the tool assigned to the order in the "Assigned Tool" field.
20. Enter the type of order in the "Order Type" field.
21. Enter any contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
22. Enter any additional order notes in the "More Order Notes" fields (Fields 23-25).
