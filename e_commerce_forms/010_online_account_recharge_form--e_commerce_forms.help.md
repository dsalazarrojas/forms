# Online Account Recharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online account recharge form is designed for customers to easily top up their account balances. This form helps us to quickly and securely process recharges for your account.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the customer name.
- Enter the customer's email address.
- Specify the amount to be recharged.
- Select a payment method (Credit/Debit Card or PayPal).
- Enter the required information for your chosen payment method (card number, expiration date, and CVC code for card payments or PayPal information for PayPal payments).
- Add any customer notes or comments for our reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name**: Your full name as it appears on your account.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
  - Hint: Please enter your name as it is registered on your account.
  - **Customer Email**: The email address associated with your account.
  - Type: Email
  - Required: No
  - Hint: Use the email you registered with us.
  - **Recharge Amount**: The amount to top up your account balance.
  - Type: Number
  - Required: No
  - Hint: Enter the exact amount you want to recharge.
  - **Payment Method**: Choose between Credit/Debit Card or PayPal for the payment.
  - Type: Select One
  - Required: No
  - Hint: Please select between the two available options.
  - **Card Number**: Enter the card number for card payments.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No (for card payments only)
  - Hint: Enter the 16-digit card number.
  - **CVV/CVV Code**: Enter the CVV/CVC/CVV2 for card payments.
  - Type: Note
  - Required: No (for card payments only)
  - Hint: Enter the security code on the back of your card.
  - **Expiration Date**: For card payments, enter the expiration date of your card.
  - Type: Date
  - Required: No (for card payments only)
  - Hint: Enter the expiration date in MM/DD format (e.g., 12/2024).
  - **Expiration Time**: For card payments, enter the expiration time of your card.
  - Type: Time
  - Required: No (for card payments only)
  - Hint: Enter the expiration time in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
  - **Customer Notes**: Add any notes or comments about your recharge.
  - Type: Note
  - Required: No
  - Hint: Enter any comments or notes about your recharge for our reference.
