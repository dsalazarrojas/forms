# we_quality_u_can_trust_asking_for_donation - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to allow users to submit a donation request or proposal to us. The form is used to collect information about the request, including the title, category, description, and form IDs. This information is used to review and process the request in a secure and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the title of your donation request in the **we_quality_u_can_trust_asking_for_donation** field. This field is used to provide a brief title for your request.
2.  Select the category of your donation request from the **e_commerce_forms** dropdown list. This field is used to indicate the type of request you are submitting.
3.  Enter a brief description of your donation request in the **hello_we_are_accepting_we_quality_u_can_trust_asking_for_donation** field. This field is used to provide additional information about your request.
4.  If you are providing form IDs, enter them in the **form_ids** field. This field is used to provide the IDs of the forms associated with your request.
5.  Enter any additional information about your donation request in the **we_quality_u_can_trust_asking_for_donation** field. This field is used to provide further details about your request.
6.  Enter any additional information about your donation request in the **e_commerce_forms** field. This field is used to provide additional details about your request.
7.  Enter a brief description of your donation request in the **we_are_accepting_we_quality_u_can_trust_asking_for_donation** field. This field is used to provide a summary of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **we_quality_u_can_trust_asking_for_donation** (`form_title`, text, required/optional: false): Enter the title of your donation request.
*   **e_commerce_forms** (`form_category`, select_multiple, required/optional: false): Select the category of your donation request from the dropdown list.
*   **hello_we_are_accepting_we_quality_u_can_trust_asking_for_donation** (`form_description`, text, required/optional: false): Enter a brief description of your donation request.
*   **form_ids** (`form_form_ids`, number, required/optional: false): If you are providing form IDs, enter them in this field.
*   **we_quality_u_can_trust_asking_for_donation** (`form_title`, text, required/optional: false): Enter any additional information about your donation request.
*   **e_commerce_forms** (`form_category`, text, required/optional: false): Enter any additional information about your donation request.
*   **we_are_accepting_we_quality_u_can_trust_asking_for_donation** (`form_description`, text, required/optional: false): Enter a brief description of your donation request.
