# Non Fungible Tokens Marketplace Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to onboard artists into the Non Fungible Tokens (NFT) marketplace. It collects information about the artist, their artwork, and their experience with NFTs.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the artist details section.
2. Provide your name, email address, and website (if applicable).
3. Share your social media handles and any other relevant online presence.
4. Add a brief description of your portfolio and a description of your art style.
5. Upload images of your artwork and art style.
6. Write a statement about your artistic vision and goals.
7. Specify your contact information.
8. Check the "Terms of Use" box to confirm your understanding of the marketplace's rules.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artist Details**: Enter your artist name, email address, and website (if you have one). This information helps us get in touch with you and share updates about the marketplace.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you'd like to receive updates and notifications about the marketplace.
* **Website**: Provide a link to your website, if you have one. This helps us learn more about your online presence.
* **Social Media**: Share your social media handles (e.g., Instagram, Twitter, Facebook) so we can find you and promote your work.
* **Portfolio**: Briefly describe your portfolio, including the types of art you create.
* **Art Style**: Describe your art style, including any notable techniques or mediums you use.
* **Artwork Images**: Upload images of your artwork to showcase your creativity.
* **Art Style Images**: Upload images that represent your art style.
* **Artist Statement**: Write a statement about your artistic vision and goals.
* **Contact Information**: Provide any additional contact details you'd like to share with us.
* **Terms of Use**: Check the box to confirm that you've read and agree to the terms of use for the marketplace.
