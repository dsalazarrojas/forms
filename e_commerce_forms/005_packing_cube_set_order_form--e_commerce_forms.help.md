# Packing Cube Set Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers when they place an order for a packing cube set. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure timely and correct order fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Select the type of order you're placing from the "order_type" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the quantity of the packing cube set you're ordering.
6. Select the payment method you'd like to use from the "payment_method" dropdown menu.
7. Enter your shipping address in the "shipping_address" field.
8. Enter the city of your shipping address in the "shipping_city" field.
9. Enter your shipping zip code in the "shipping_zip" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (first_name, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name** (last_name, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email** (email, email, required: false): Enter the email address you'd like to use for order updates and communication.
- **Order Type** (order_type, select_one, required: false): Select the type of order you're placing, such as "Standard" or "Express".
- **Quantity** (quantity, number, required: false): Enter the number of packing cube sets you're ordering.
- **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_multiple, required: false): Select the payment method you'd like to use, such as "Credit Card" or "PayPal".
- **Shipping Address** (shipping_address, text, required: false): Enter your shipping address in the format "Street Address, City, State, Zip".
- **Shipping City** (shipping_city, text, required: false): Enter the city of your shipping address.
- **Shipping Zip** (shipping_zip, text, required: false): Enter your shipping zip code.
- **Payment Method** (payment, time, required: false): This field is currently not used in the provided form structure and seems to be a duplicate of "payment_method" field.

**Tips**

- Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy to ensure you receive order updates and communication.
- Be sure to enter your shipping address correctly to avoid delays in order fulfillment.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact our customer support team for assistance.
