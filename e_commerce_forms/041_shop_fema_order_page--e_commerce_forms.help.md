# shop-fema-order-page - Help Guide
## Purpose
The shop-fema-order-page form is used to collect information from customers about their orders, including shipping details, items, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information in the "customer" field.
2. Select the shipping option from the "Shipping" field.
3. Indicate whether you want to order items or not using the "items" field.
4. Enter the quantity and price of your order in the "Quantity" and "Price" fields.
5. Enter the total cost of your order in the "Total" field.
6. Add any additional notes about your order in the "Notes" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Select your address option from the "Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer** (text): Enter your name as the customer.
* **Shipping** (number): Select the shipping option for your order.
* **Items** (select multiple): Check the box if you want to order items.
* **Quantity** (number): Enter the quantity of the item you want to order.
* **Price** (number): Enter the price of the item you want to order.
* **Total** (number): Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Notes** (note): Add any additional notes about your order.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (select one): Select the address option for your order.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information to facilitate the order process.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact the support team for assistance.
- This form is designed to be completed once per order.
- If you need to place multiple orders, submit each order separately.
