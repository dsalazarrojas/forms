# Amazon PPC Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about Amazon PPC campaigns, providing a clear overview of a campaign's objectives, budget, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Company name** field with the brand or seller name.
2. Provide the **Account manager** name and contact information for the account.
3. Enter the **Monthly budget** in USD for the campaign.
4. Select the main **Campaign goals**, choosing one or more from the available options (Brand awareness, Sales, Traffic, Leads).
5. Enter the **Target products** (SKUs or ASINs) for the campaign.
6. Choose the preferred **Bid strategy** (Manual, Automatic, Dynamic) for the campaign.
7. Enter the email address for **Reporting email**, where campaign reports will be sent.
8. Finally, provide any additional **Notes** for the campaign.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Company name** (text, required)
Enter the brand or seller name associated with this campaign.

### **Account manager** (text, optional)
Provide the name and contact information for the account manager who will be responsible for this campaign.

### **Monthly budget** (number, required)
Enter the total monthly budget for the campaign in USD.

### **Campaign goals** (select_multiple, required)
Select one or more main objectives for the campaign (Brand awareness, Sales, Traffic, Leads).

### **Target products** (text, required)
Enter the SKUs or ASINs for the products targeted by this campaign.

### **Preferred bid strategy** (select_one, optional)
Choose the preferred bidding approach for the campaign (Manual, Automatic, Dynamic).

### **Reporting email** (email, required)
Enter the email address where campaign reports will be sent.

### **Additional notes** (text, optional)
Provide any additional information for the PPC team to reference when working on the campaign.
