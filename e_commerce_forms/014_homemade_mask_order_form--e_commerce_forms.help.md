# homemade-mask-order-form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The homemade-mask-order-form is a form used to collect information from customers for order management and fulfillment. It gathers details like customer contact information, order date, time, shipping address, order message, and other relevant order details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **customer name**, which is your full name that will be used for communication and order management.
2.  Provide your **phone number**, which will be used for contact purposes.
3.  Enter the **order date** and **order time** to track the order timeline.
4.  Input your **shipping address**, which is the location where your order will be delivered.
5.  Add a brief **order message**, which can be any note or instruction for the order.
6.  Select your **payment method** from the options provided. This is how you will be paying for the order.
7.  Choose your **shipping method** from the options provided. This is how the order will be delivered.
8.  Enter the **order total**, which is the total cost of your order.
9.  Select the **order status** from the options provided. This is an update on the order's current state.
10. Enter your **customer notes**, if any additional information or comments are required.
11.  Input your **order ID**, if provided by the system.
12.  Enter your **customer title**, if relevant to the order.
13.  Select the **order type** from the options provided. This could be custom, standard, or premium.
14.  Enter the **quantity** of the order, if applicable.
15.  Enter the **price** of the order, if required.
16.  Input the **product name**, if relevant to the order.
17.  Enter the **order date added**, if tracking order creation date is needed.
18.  Enter your **customer name**, to provide a record of the customer's name.
19.  Enter the **shipping cost**, if this is a part of the order.
20.  Enter the **tax rate**, if applicable.
21.  Enter the **total cost**, which is the final cost after tax and shipping.
