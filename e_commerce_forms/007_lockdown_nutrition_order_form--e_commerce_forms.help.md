# Lockdown Nutrition Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a simple order form for customers to purchase various nutrition-related products or services from the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Select the products you would like to order from the list in the "Select Products" field.
* Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
* If you have any special requests or needs, please provide them in the "Order Info" field.
* Click submit to send the form to the customer service team for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`title`, `customer_info`, **required: false**): Please provide your name and any other relevant information about yourself, such as your address and phone number.
* **Contact Details** (`title`, `contact_details`, **required: false**): This is where you can provide your contact information, such as your email address and phone number.
* **Select Products** (`title`, `products`, **required: false**): Choose the products you would like to order by selecting one or more from the list. 
* **Order Info** (`title`, `order_info`, **required: false**): If you have any special requests or needs, please provide them in this field.
* **Payment Details** (`title`, `payment_details`, **required: false**): Please provide any necessary payment information, such as your credit card number or other payment methods.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your order before submitting it to ensure that it is accurate and complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the customer service team for assistance.
* We strive to process orders as quickly and efficiently as possible.
