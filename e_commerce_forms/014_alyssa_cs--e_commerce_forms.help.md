# Alyssa Cs - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alyssa Cs form is a user-facing form used to collect information about account settings and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields: User name, Email address, and select a Typical order frequency.
2. Optionally, provide your Display name, Agree to receive marketing emails, and enter your Phone number.
3. Answer the two select_one questions with either 'True' or 'False' and/or select 'Weekly', 'Monthly', or 'Rarely' in the Typical order frequency field.
4. Enter your Favorite product and any Optional comments in the Feedback for the team field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User name (User name)
* **User name** (`user_name`, `text`, required): Enter your display name for the account.

### Email address (Email address)
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address for account notifications.

### Typical order frequency (Typical order frequency)
* **Typical order frequency** (`order_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose your typical order frequency from the options: Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely.

### Favorite product (Favorite product)
* **Favorite product** (`favorite_product`, `text`, optional): Enter your favorite product of interest.

### Agree to receive marketing emails (Marketing opt in)
* **Agree to receive marketing emails** (`marketing_opt_in`, `select_one`, optional): Agree to receive marketing emails by selecting 'True'.

### Phone number (Phone number)
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your optional contact phone number.

### Feedback for the team (Feedback for the team)
* **Feedback for the team** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Enter any optional comments for the team.

### Agree to receive marketing emails (Marketing opt in)
* **Agree to receive marketing emails** (`marketing_opt_in`, `select_one`, optional): This question is asking you to agree to receive marketing emails by selecting 'True'. This can be useful for the team to know your preferences on receiving marketing materials.
