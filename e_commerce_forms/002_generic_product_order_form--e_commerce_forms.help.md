# Generic Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The generic product order form is designed to collect essential information from customers who want to place an order for a product. This form helps to ensure that the order is processed correctly and that the customer's details are collected accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Phone Number, and Email Address in the respective fields.
2. Select the required payment method from the options provided.
3. If required, select one or multiple options from the 'Select One' or 'Select Multiple' fields.
4. Enter the shipping address, city, state, and zip code for delivery.
5. Specify the total items and quantity you want to order.
6. Enter the product name and price.
7. Provide a brief description of the product you're ordering.
8. Review all the information you've entered to ensure it's correct before submitting the form.
