The Customer Checkout And Shipping Form is a tool to gather essential information from customers for e-commerce transactions.

To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Fill out your customer information on page 1 (Customer Information).

* **Customer Information** (Customer Information, text, required): Enter your personal details such as name, email, and phone number.

* **Shipping Method** (Shipping Method, select_one, optional): Choose between First Choice, Second Choice, or Third Choice for shipping.
* **Shipping Details** (Shipping Details, text, optional): Enter any special instructions or details about your shipping preferences.
