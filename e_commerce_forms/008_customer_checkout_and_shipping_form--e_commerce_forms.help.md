# Customer Checkout And Shipping Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer Checkout And Shipping Form is a tool to gather essential information from customers for e-commerce transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Fill out your customer information on page 1 (Customer Information).
* Enter your shipping address on page 2 (Shipping Address).
* Select your preferred shipping method from page 3 (Shipping Method).
* Provide any additional shipping details on page 4 (Shipping Details).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (Customer Information, text, required): Enter your personal details such as name, email, and phone number.
* **Shipping Address** (Shipping Address, text, required): Enter your address where you'd like the items to be shipped to.
* **Shipping Method** (Shipping Method, select_one, optional): Choose between First Choice, Second Choice, or Third Choice for shipping.
* **Shipping Details** (Shipping Details, text, optional): Enter any special instructions or details about your shipping preferences.

## Tips
* Double-check your contact information and address for accuracy.
* Choose a shipping method that suits your needs.
* If you have special shipping requests, enter them in the Shipping Details field.
