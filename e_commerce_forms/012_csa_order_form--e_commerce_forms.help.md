# CSA Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CSA Order Form is designed for customers to place orders for Community Supported Agriculture (CSA) shares. This form collects contact information, share selection, payment details, and other preferences to enable smooth order processing and efficient communication with the farm management team.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by providing your contact information, including your Full Name, Phone Number, and Email Address. These are essential details that help the farm connect with you efficiently.
2.  Select your preferred CSA Share Type from the provided options, such as Full Share - Family Size, Half Share - Small Household, or other add-ons like Egg Share or Bread Share. This selection determines the type and quantity of produce you will receive.
3.  Indicate the Number of Shares you wish to order. This will affect the total cost of your order.
4.  Choose your Preferred Pickup Location, such as The Farm Stand, Downtown Farmers Market, or East Side Hub. This ensures that your produce is delivered or made available at the correct location.
5.  Select your Preferred Payment Plan, which can be Full Payment upfront, 50-50 Split, or Monthly Installments. This influences the payment schedule and terms.
6.  If you were referred to the farm by a current member, provide their name to receive the referral bonus.
7.  Check if you are interested in volunteering at the farm by selecting 'True' or 'False'. This informs the farm management about your potential for involvement.
8.  Optionally, provide Special Instructions or Dietary Preferences to ensure that the farm staff can accommodate your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Full Name** (Required): Provide your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*  **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your primary phone number for efficient communication.
*  **Email Address** (Required): Enter a valid email address for regular updates and notifications.
*  **Mailing Address** (Required): Enter your current mailing address for share delivery or pick-up details.
*  **Select Your Share Type** (Required): Choose from available share options, such as Full Share - Family Size or Half Share - Small Household.
*  **Number of Shares** (Required): Enter the quantity of shares you wish to purchase, which affects the total cost.
*  **Preferred Pickup Location** (Required): Select from available locations, such as The Farm Stand or Downtown Farmers Market.
*  **Preferred Payment Plan** (Required): Choose from available payment plans, such as Full Payment upfront or 50-50 Split.
*  **Were you referred by a current member** (Optional): If yes, provide the name of the referring member.
*  **Are you interested in volunteering at the farm** (Optional): Select 'True' if you're interested in volunteering, 'False' otherwise.
*  **Special Instructions or Dietary Preferences** (Optional): Enter any specific instructions or dietary preferences to help the farm staff tailor your order.
