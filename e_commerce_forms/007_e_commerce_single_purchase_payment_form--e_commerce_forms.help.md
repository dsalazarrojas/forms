# e_commerce_single_purchase_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to provide information for a single purchase transaction in an e-commerce setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form (form_header).
2. Provide your customer details (customer_details).
3. Fill in the order details (order_details).
4. Enter your address (address).
5. Choose your payment method (payment).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_header** (Text): Enter a brief description of the purchase transaction.
* **customer_details** (Text): Provide your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
* **order_details** (Text): Enter the product(s) name, quantity, and any other order information.
* **address** (Text): Enter your shipping address for the purchase.
* **payment** (Text): Describe the payment method you would like to use for the purchase.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields to avoid errors.
- Double-check your order details for accuracy.
- Confirm your payment method before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact the support team.
