# Package Delivery Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Package Delivery Check Out Form is designed to collect information about package delivery details, including delivery date, shipping details, recipient contact information, and delivery status. This form may be used by logistics or customer service teams to track and manage package deliveries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the delivery date for the package in the **delivery_date** field.
2. Provide the shipping details in the **shipping_details** field.
3. Enter the recipient's first name and last name in the **recipient_first_name** and **recipient_last_name** fields, respectively.
4. Enter the recipient's phone number in the **recipient_phone** field.
5. Enter the recipient's email address in the **recipient_email** field.
6. Select the delivery time in the **delivery_time** field.
7. Choose the current delivery status from the dropdown menu in the **delivery_status** field.
8. (Optional) Enter any additional notes about the delivery in the **notes** field.
9. (Optional) Select the payment terms that apply to the delivery in the **payment_terms_accepted** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **delivery_date**: Enter the date the package was delivered or is scheduled to be delivered.
* **shipping_details**: Provide any relevant shipping details, such as carrier information or tracking numbers.
* **recipient_first_name**: Enter the recipient's first name.
* **recipient_last_name**: Enter the recipient's last name.
* **recipient_phone**: Enter the recipient's phone number.
* **recipient_email**: Enter the recipient's email address.
* **delivery_time**: Choose the time of day when the package was delivered or is scheduled to be delivered.
* **delivery_status**: Select the current status of the delivery from the dropdown menu:
	+ **Not Started**: The delivery process has not begun.
	+ **In Progress**: The delivery is underway.
	+ **Completed**: The delivery has been completed.
* **notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the delivery (optional).
* **payment_terms_accepted**: Select which payment terms apply to the delivery from the dropdown menu:
	+ **Terms and Conditions**: Standard payment terms apply.
	+ **Shipping and Handling**: Special payment terms apply for shipping and handling.
	+ **Payment Terms**: Other payment terms apply.

## Tips
* Double-check the delivery date and status to ensure accurate tracking.
* Make sure to enter the recipient's contact information correctly.
* Review the payment terms carefully to ensure accurate billing.
