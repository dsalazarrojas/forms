# Dad Sweatshirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dad Sweatshirt Order Form is used to collect information about orders, including order details, shipping details, payment method, and other relevant information. This form is intended for internal use by the customer service team to process and manage orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the order details in the first field.
2. Select the shipping details in the second field.
3. Choose the payment method in the third field.
4. Select the shirt color in the fourth field.
5. Choose the shirt size in the fifth field.
6. Enter any order comments in the sixth field.
7. Enter the shipping address in the seventh field.
8. Enter any payment details in the eighth field.
9. Enter the order total in the ninth field.
10. Enter the shipping cost in the tenth field.
11. Choose the order status in the eleventh field.
12. Enter any additional payment details in the twelfth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the order.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, required): Enter the shipping address and any other relevant shipping details.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method used for the order (credit card or bank transfer).
* **Shirt Color** (`t-shirt_color`, select_one, required): Select the color of the shirt.
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, select_one, required): Select the size of the shirt.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments about the order.

* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required): Enter any additional payment details.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required): Enter the total cost of the order.
