# Hoodie Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers to process their hoodie orders. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that their order is fulfilled correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Hoodie Order Form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer details, including your name and any other relevant information.
2. Fill out the Order section with the details of the order you want to place.
3. Choose a payment method for your order.
4. Enter your contact details, including your address and any other contact information.
5. Confirm your order and payment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details**: Enter your name and any other relevant information.
* **Order**: Enter the details of the order you want to place, including the type of hoodie you want to order.
* **Payment Method**: Choose a payment method from the available options (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Contact Details**: Enter your address and any other contact information.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the shipping address where the hoodie will be delivered.
* **Payment Info**: Enter your payment information, including the amount you are paying.
* **Confirm Order**: Confirm that the order details are accurate.
* **Payment Confirmation**: Confirm that the payment details are accurate.
* **Customer Confirmation**: Confirm that you are the customer placing this order.
* **Order Details**: Enter any additional information about the order, such as special requests or instructions.

Note: This form is for the purpose of ordering a hoodie only. Please do not use this form for any other purpose.
