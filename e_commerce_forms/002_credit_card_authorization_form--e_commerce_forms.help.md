# credit_card_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The credit card authorization form is designed for verifying credit card information, ensuring accurate and secure financial transactions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your **Customer Name** in the "Customer Name" field. This is your personal name as it appears on your credit card.
2.  Provide your **Card Number** in the "Card Number" field. This is the 16-digit number on the front of your credit card.
3.  Enter your **Expiration Date** in the "Expiration Date" field. The format should be MM/YY.
4.  Enter your **Cvv** in the "Cvv" field. This is the 3-digit number on the back of your credit card.
5.  Enter your **Customer Email** in the "Customer Email" field. This is your email associated with your credit card account.
6.  Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field. This is the phone number associated with your credit card account.
7.  Enter your **Address** in the "Address" field. This is your billing address.
8.  Enter your **Zip Code** in the "Zip Code" field. This is your postal/zip code.
9.  Select your **Card Type** in the "Card Type" field. Choose from the available options (Visa, Mastercard, Amex).
10. Enter your **Cvc** in the "Cvc" field. This is the 3-digit number on the back of your credit card.
11. Enter your **Customer IP** in the "Customer IP" field. This is your current IP address (this field is not required but might be used to track user information).
12. Select your **Card Brand** in the "Card Brand" field. Choose from the available options (Visa, Discover, Amex, Mastercard, JCB, Diners).
13. Enter your **Customer City** in the "Customer City" field. This is your city of residence.
14. Enter your **Card Country** in the "Card Country" field. This is your country of card issue.
15. Enter your **Customer Country** in the "Customer Country" field. This is your country of residence.
16. Enter your **Card State** in the "Card State" field. This is your state of card issue.
17. Enter your **Card Zip** in the "Card Zip" field. This is your zip code of card issue.
18. Enter your **Card City** in the "Card City" field. This is your card city of issue.
19. Enter your **Card State** in the "Card State" field. This is your card state of issue.
20. Enter your **Customer City** in the "Customer City" field. This is your city of residence.
21. Enter your **Customer Country** in the "Customer Country" field. This is your country of residence.
22. Enter your **Customer Zip** in the "Customer Zip" field. This is your zip code of residence.
23. Enter your **Customer State** in the "Customer State" field. This is your state of residence.
