# custom_patches_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "custom_patches_order_form" is a form designed to collect information for custom patches orders from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer details in the "Customer Details" section.
2. Provide your valid email address in the "Customer Email" section.
3. If applicable, upload your logo image in the "Logo Image" section.
4. Enter your design specifications in the "Design Details" section.
5. Enter your customer phone number in the "Customer Phone" section.
6. Enter your order details in the "Order Details" section, including any relevant information.
7. Add any order notes in the "Order Notes" section, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Details
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, not required): Enter your customer name and any other relevant information.

### Customer Email
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your order.

### Logo Image
* **Logo Image** (`logo_image`, text, not required): If you have a logo image, you can upload it here to include in your order.

### Design Details
* **Design Details** (`design_details`, text, not required): Enter any design specifications for your custom patch, such as size, color, etc.

### Customer Phone
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, not required): Enter your customer phone number, if applicable.

### Order Details
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required): Enter any relevant information about your order, such as quantity, material, etc.

### Order Notes
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, not required): Add any additional notes about your order, such as special instructions or requests.

## Tips
* Ensure that your email address is valid and up-to-date.
* If uploading a logo image, ensure it is in the correct format.
* Be clear and concise when entering your design details to avoid misunderstandings.
* Enter any relevant information in the "Order Details" section to ensure accurate processing of your order.
* Use the "Order Notes" section to add any special requests or instructions for your order.
