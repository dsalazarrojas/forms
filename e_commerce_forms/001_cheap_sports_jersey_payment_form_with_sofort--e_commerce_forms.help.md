# Cheap Sports Jersey Payment Form With Sofort - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the purchase of cheap sports jerseys with the Sofort payment method. It is intended for customers who wish to pay with Sofort, an instant card payment service. The form collects contact and shipping information, jersey selection, size, and payment details to facilitate the ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This will be used for order confirmation and delivery updates.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This will be used for delivery and order updates.
4. Select your preferred sports jersey from the "Select Jersey" dropdown menu.
5. Choose your preferred jersey size from the "Jersey Size" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the number of jerseys you wish to purchase in the "Quantity" field.
7. If desired, enter a custom name or number on the jersey in the "Custom Name on Jersey" and "Custom Number on Jersey" fields.
8. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
9. Enter your city in the "City" field.
10. Enter your postal code (ZIP or postal code) in the "Postal Code" field.
11. Select your country of residence from the "Country" dropdown menu.
12. Select the "Sofort" payment method to complete the purchase.
13. Enter your card number for Sofort payment in the "Card Number" field.
14. Enter the expiry date of your card in the "Expiry Date" field in the format MM/YY.
15. Enter the 3-digit security code on the back of your card in the "CVV" field.
16. Optionally, enter any special instructions or notes for your order in the "Order Notes" field.
17. Check the box indicating that you accept the terms and conditions to complete the form.
