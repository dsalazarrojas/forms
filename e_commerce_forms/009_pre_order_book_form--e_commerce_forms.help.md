# Pre Order Book Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect pre-order information for a book from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in your **Customer Name** in the `Customer Name` field.
- Enter your **Email** address in the `Email` field.
- Choose how many **Quantity** of books you would like to pre-order.
- Provide your **Contact Number** for us to reach you.
- Select the preferred **Book** from the dropdown menu.
- If you have a specific **Book** not listed, type it in the `Other Book` field.
- Optionally, add any **Order Comments** about your pre-order.
- Finally, click **Submit** to send us your pre-order details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where we can contact you about your order.
* **Quantity**: Choose how many books you would like to pre-order, from 1-10.
* **Contact Number**: Provide your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Book Preferred**: Select the book you would like to pre-order from the list.
* **Other Book**: If your preferred book is not on the list, type it in here.
* **Order Comments**: Add any additional comments or instructions about your order.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send your pre-order details to us.
