# Custom Candle Quote Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers for custom candle quote requests. It's meant to be filled out by customers who want to inquire about custom candle orders, including details about the type of candle they want, the fragrance, color, and other specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address to contact you for further communication.
2. If you have a phone number, please enter it for easier contact and follow-up.
3. Select the type of product you're interested in from the list provided.
4. Choose the fragrance you prefer.
5. If you have any specific color requests, enter the details in the "Color Request" field.
6. Enter the quantity of candles you're interested in purchasing.
7. Enter the unit price you're willing to pay per candle (this will help us provide you with an accurate quote).
8. Finally, click submit to send us your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Custom Candle Quote Request Form (1)** (`form_data`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your custom candle request. This field is optional, but if you have any specific details or requests, please enter them here.
* **Customer Name (2)** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your name so we can contact you about your custom candle quote.
* **Customer Email (3)** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further communication about your custom candle quote.
* **Phone Number (4)** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it for easier contact and follow-up.
* **Product Type (5)** (`product_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of candle you're interested in from the list provided.
* **Fragrance (6)** (`fragrance`, text, optional): Choose the fragrance you prefer for your custom candle.
* **Color Request (7)** (`color_request`, text, optional): If you have any specific color requests, enter the details here.
* **Quantity (8)** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of candles you're interested in purchasing.
* **Unit Price (9)** (`unit_price`, number, optional): Enter the unit price you're willing to pay per candle.
* **Total Cost (10)** (`total_cost`, number, optional): This field is automatically calculated based on your previous input, but you can also enter this value if you know it.
