# Full Reset Coaching Client Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Full Reset Coaching Client Form is a tool used to collect information from coaching clients who are interested in restarting their coaching journey or seeking guidance on how to overcome specific challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each field explanation below to ensure you understand what is required.
2. Fill out each field according to your needs and preferences.
3. Make sure to submit the form completely and correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address you check most frequently.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your current address.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are available for coaching sessions.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information you would like to share with your coach.
* **What Kind Of Coaching Are You Interested In** (`coaching_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of coaching you are interested in.

* **How Often Would You Like To Meet** (`coaching_frequencies`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred coaching frequency.

* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to start coaching sessions.
