# Personalized Notebook Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personalized Notebook Order Form is used to collect information from customers for a customized order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Customer Info field with your name, address, and other contact details.
2. Enter your Customer Email address if required.
3. Select your preferred Shipping Method:
	* Credit Card
	* PayPal
	* Bank Transfer
4. Add any Customer Notes if necessary.
5. Enter the Order Total and Shipping Amount for your order.
6. Choose the color and cover type for your notebook.
7. Select your preferred Payment Method:
	* Credit Card
	* PayPal
	* Bank Transfer
8. Fill in Customer Info 2 and Order Details if required.
9. Select your Country and City for shipping purposes.
10. Review your order details before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (Text): Enter your name, address, and other contact details.
* **Customer Email** (Email): Enter your email address if required.
* **Shipping Method** (Select One): Choose your preferred shipping method:
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Bank Transfer
* **Customer Notes** (Text): Add any additional comments or notes for your order.
* **Order Total** (Number): Enter the total amount for your order.
* **Shipping Amount** (Number): Enter the shipping amount for your order.
* **Color** (Select One): Choose the color for your notebook:
	+ Black
	+ Red
	+ Blue
	+ Green
* **Cover Type** (Select One): Choose the cover type for your notebook:
	+ Plain
	+ Printed
	+ Leather
	+ Cloth
* **Payment Method** (Select One): Choose your preferred payment method:
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Bank Transfer
* **Customer Info 2** (Text): Fill in additional customer information if required.
* **Order Details** (Text): Enter any additional order details if required.
* **Country** (Select One): Select your country for shipping purposes:
	+ Australia
	+ New Zealand
	+ USA
	+ Canada
* **City** (Select One): Select your city for shipping purposes:
	+ Sydney
	+ Auckland
	+ New York
	+ Toronto

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled correctly.
* Review your order details before submitting.
* If you have any issues with your order, contact the support team for assistance.
