# scented_candle_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scented_candle_order_form is designed to collect customer information and order details for a scented candle purchase. Fill out the form with your relevant details to ensure accurate processing of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Navigate through the form's pages using the navigation buttons.
2. Fill out the required and optional fields with the requested information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your order or leave this field empty if not applicable.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required: false): Describe the order you're placing, including the type and quantity of scented candles.
* **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, text, required: false): Provide any special shipping instructions or notes, if applicable.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication and order updates.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for any further communication or order updates.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, note, required: false): Provide a note or description of your shipping address; you can enter this field as a text area with no specific formatting restrictions.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill out all required fields correctly to avoid any issues with processing your order.
* Double-check your contact information to ensure we can reach you for any further updates or inquiries.
