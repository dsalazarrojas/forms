## Purpose
The premier_designs_style_quiz is a form designed to gather information about a design project's aesthetic and functional aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form questions carefully and answer them based on your genuine opinions and perceptions of the design.
2. For numerical questions, provide the most accurate numerical value that represents your opinion.
3. Make sure to complete all questions on each page before proceeding to the next.
4. When you have finished, submit the form to provide your feedback.
