# School T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate ordering school T-shirts. It helps you gather details about your order, payment information, and any additional comments you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the order details page with your name, school name, and T-shirt design you want to order.
2. Proceed to the payment details page and enter your payment method and amount.
3. In the order summary page, provide a brief overview of your order.
4. Finally, add any additional information you'd like to include in your order in the additional information page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`Order Details`, `text`, required: false): On the first page, enter your name, school name, and T-shirt design you're interested in purchasing.
* **Payment Details** (`Payment Details`, `text`, required: false): On the second page, enter your payment method and amount.
* **Order Summary** (`Order Summary`, `text`, required: false): On the third page, write a brief summary of your order, including your chosen T-shirt design and quantity.
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, `text`, required: false): On the fourth page, add any additional comments or instructions you'd like to include in your order.
