> ### E Commerce Quality Control Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The E Commerce Quality Control Application Form is used to gather information about an e-commerce product's quality control process. The form helps to ensure that products meet the required quality standards and are suitable for shipment. This form is a crucial tool for quality assurance and control in the e-commerce industry.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the product you wish to inspect and enter its unique Product ID.
2. Enter the customer's information, including their name and contact details.
3. Mark the inspection result as passed or failed.
4. Check the Compliance Status to confirm if the product meets the quality standards.
5. Enter the product's description, weight, and dimensions in the Product Information field.
6. Select the shipping method (e.g., ground, air, express).
7. Choose the payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer).
8. Indicate the review status of the product (e.g., satisfactory or not).
9. Confirm if the product is certified or not.
10. Select the current product status (e.g., ready for shipment, in production).
11. Check the Compliance Status 2 to confirm if the product meets the quality standards.
12. Select the payment status (e.g., paid, pending).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product ID** (`1`, `number`, required): Enter the unique ID of the product you wish to inspect.
* **Customer Information** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's name and contact information, such as their address, phone number, and email.
* **Inspection Result** (`3`, `select_one`, required): Mark the inspection result as passed or failed.
* **Compliance Status** (`4`, `checkbox`, required): Check the box if the product meets the quality standards.
* **Product Information** (`5`, `text`, required): Enter the product's description, weight, and dimensions.
* **Shipping Method** (`6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the shipping method (e.g., ground, air, express).
* **Payment Method** (`7`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer).
* **Review Status** (`8`, `radio`, optional): Indicate the review status of the product (e.g., satisfactory or not).
* **Certification Status** (`9`, `radio`, optional): Confirm if the product is certified or not.
* **Product Status** (`10`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the current product status (e.g., ready for shipment, in production).
* **Compliance Status 2** (`11`, `checkbox`, required): Check the box if the product meets the quality standards.
* **Payment Status** (`12`, `select_one`, optional): Select the payment status (e.g., paid, pending).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly to ensure accurate quality control results.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your quality assurance team for guidance.
* This form is designed to be used for quality control purposes only; please do not use it for other purposes.
