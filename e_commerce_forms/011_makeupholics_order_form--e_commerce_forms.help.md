## Makeupholics Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to place orders, including order details, customer contact information, and order status updates. This guide will walk you through how to complete the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Order Details section with any relevant information about your order.
2. Enter your Customer Name and Customer Email in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your Order Total, which is the total cost of your order.
4. Select your preferred Payment Method from the dropdown menu.
5. Update the Order Status field according to your order's current status.
6. Add any Customer Comments or Order Notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details**: Enter a brief description of your order.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as the customer placing the order.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address to receive order updates and notifications.
* **Order Total**: Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you will be paying for the order. Options are Card, Cash, or Transfer.
* **Order Status**: Update the status of your order to Placed, Delivered, or Cancelled, depending on your order's progress.
* **Customer Comments**: Add any comments or instructions you have for the customer service team.
* **Order Notes**: Add any additional information about your order that is not included in the Order Details section.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your Order Total and Payment Method to ensure accuracy.
* Keep your Customer Comments and Order Notes clear and concise to help our team process your order efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer service team for assistance.
