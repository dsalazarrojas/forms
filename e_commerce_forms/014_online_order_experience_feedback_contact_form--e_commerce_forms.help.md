# online_order_experience_feedback_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The online_order_experience_feedback_contact_form is used to capture feedback and concerns related to online orders. This form is designed to allow customers to provide their experiences, concerns, and suggestions about their orders. The data collected will be reviewed and addressed by our team to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have your order details ready, including the Order ID.
2. Fill in the required information, such as your Email, Name, and Phone number.
3. Select the relevant Status of your order from the drop-down list.
4. Provide your feedback in the note section.
5. Optionally, enter your Order Date and Address if applicable.
6. Click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Order ID (ID 1)
* Order ID (`number`, required: false): Enter your order number as it appears on your order confirmation email or receipt.

### Email (ID 2)
* Email (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.

### Name (ID 3)
* Name (`text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Date (ID 4)
* Date (`date`, required: false): Enter the date of your order if applicable.

### Status (ID 5)
* Status (`select_one`, required: false): Select the status of your order:
	+ Cancelled: Your order has been cancelled.
	+ Delayed: Your order is delayed beyond the expected delivery time.
	+ On-time: Your order was received within the expected time frame.
	+ Late: Your order was received later than expected.

### Feedback (ID 6)
* Feedback (`note`, required: false): Share your thoughts, concerns, or suggestions about your order.

### Phone (ID 7)
* Phone (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

### Address (ID 8)
* Address (`text`, required: false): Enter your address if you want us to contact you at your location.

## Tips
- Please ensure you have all necessary information before submitting the form.
- If you have any attachments or supporting documents, you can email them to us separately.
- Our team will review your feedback and address your concerns as soon as possible.
