# Ticket Marketplace Customer Support Contact Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to contact the ticket marketplace's customer support team with any issues or questions they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the type of inquiry you have by choosing from the "Inquiry Type" dropdown menu.
2. Enter a brief description of your issue in the "Inquiry Subject" field.
3. Provide more details about your issue in the "Inquiry Detail" field.
4. If you have a customer ID or account number, enter it in the "Customer ID" field.
5. Let us know how quickly you need a response from us by selecting an option from the "Best Time for Response" dropdown menu.
6. If you would like the support team to follow up with you, select "Yes" in the "Follow-up Information" field.
7. Finally, provide your contact information (email or phone number) so we can get back to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inquiry Type**: This dropdown menu allows you to select the type of issue you are experiencing. Please choose from "Technical Support" or "General Inquiries" to help the support team assist you more efficiently.
* **Inquiry Subject**: Enter a brief description of your issue in this field. This will help the support team understand the scope of your problem.
* **Inquiry Detail**: Provide more details about your issue in this field. This will help the support team better understand your question or problem.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can get back to you. Please use a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you would prefer to be contacted by phone instead of email.
* **Best Time for Response**: Let us know how quickly you need a response from us. Please choose from "As soon as possible", "Within a few hours", "Later today", or "Later tomorrow".
* **Follow-up Information**: If you would like the support team to follow up with you after your issue has been addressed, please select "Yes" in this field.
* **Customer ID**: If you have a customer ID or account number, enter it so we can easily identify your account.
* **Technical Support**: This field is not used in this form, however it is a label from the original YAML input schema. Please disregard this field. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked with *) to ensure that your issue is addressed efficiently.
* If you have any attachments or images related to your issue, please email them to us separately.
* If you have any further questions or concerns after receiving our response, don't hesitate to contact us back.
* Our support team is available to assist you during [business hours]. If you have an urgent issue, please contact us outside of business hours and we will do our best to assist you as soon as possible.
