# mobile_device_sales_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from mobile device sellers about their sales submissions. It's meant for use in an e-commerce context where mobile devices are bought and sold.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your seller information in the "seller_details" field on page 1.
2. Provide the necessary specifications of the mobile device being sold in the "device_specs" field on page 1.
3. If applicable, upload photos of the mobile device for better representation in the "photos" field on page 2 (note: this field is not required).
4. Click the "Submit" button on page 2 to finalize your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Seller Information (required)
* Seller Details (seller_details)
	+ This field collects your name and contact information as the seller.
	+ Please ensure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
### Device Specs (required)
* This field collects the specifications of the mobile device being sold, such as its condition, storage capacity, and any other relevant details.
	+ Please provide as much detail as possible for buyers to make informed decisions.
### Photos (optional)
* This field allows you to upload photos of the mobile device for better representation and buyer satisfaction.
	+ If you have any visual aids, please upload them for the buyer's benefit.
### Submit (required)
* This button finalizes your submission and makes the mobile device available for purchase.
	+ Please ensure all required fields have been filled before clicking this button.
### Seller Additional Info (optional)
* This field is for any additional information you'd like to share with the buyer.
	+ Use it to mention any unique features or concerns about the mobile device.
## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth buying process.
- Ensure you're filling out the form only once for each mobile device being sold.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to this guide for clarification.
