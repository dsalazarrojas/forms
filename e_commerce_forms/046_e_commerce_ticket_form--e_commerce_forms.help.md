# e_commerce_ticket_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e_commerce_ticket_form is designed to collect information about an order from an e-commerce platform. This form will help us process and resolve any issues related to orders in a quick and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
2. Ensure that you have all the necessary information about the order before submitting it.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order ID* (Order ID, number, required):** Enter the unique identifier for the order. If you're not sure what this is, try looking for a confirmation email or contacting the customer to get the correct information.
* **Customer Name (Customer Name, text, optional):** Enter the name of the customer associated with the order. This field is optional, but including it can help us provide better customer service.
* **Order Date (Order Date, date, optional):** Enter the date the order was placed. This field is optional, but it can help us track order history.
* **Order Total (Order Total, number, optional):** Enter the total cost of the order. This field is optional, but it can help us process refunds or other financial transactions.
* **Customer Email (Customer Email, email, optional):** Enter the email address associated with the customer. This field is optional, but including it can help us contact the customer directly.
* **Customer Phone (Customer Phone, text, optional):** Enter the phone number associated with the customer. This field is optional, but including it can help us contact the customer directly.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid delays in processing the issue.
- If you're unsure about any information, try to verify it with the customer or the sales team before submitting the form.
- If you're experiencing technical difficulties while filling out the form, please contact the help desk for assistance.
