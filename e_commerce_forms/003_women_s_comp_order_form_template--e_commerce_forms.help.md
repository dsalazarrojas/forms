# Women's Comp Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Women's Comp Order Form Template is designed to collect information from customers for orders related to women's products or services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the "Customer Info" section, providing your contact details.
2. In the "Order Details" section, describe the order, including any relevant details.
3. In the "Shipping Info" section, provide your shipping address.
4. In the "Billing Info" section, enter the billing address.
5. In the "Order Confirmation" section, confirm your order details.
6. Select the desired shipping method in the "Shipping Method" section.
7. In the "Payment Method" section, choose how you'd like to make your payment.
8. Enter any relevant payment information in the "Payment Info" section.
9. If required, enter the order total, customer notes, special instructions, and assigned tool information.
10. In the "Customer Signature" section, sign your name to confirm your order.
11. If necessary, enter your phone number, email, company name, and customer notes.
12. Finally, review your submission carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name and contact details.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your order.
* **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, text, required: false): Enter your shipping address.
* **Billing Info** (`billing_info`, text, required: false): Enter your billing address.
* **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, text, required: false): Confirm your order details.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you'd like to receive your order.
	+ Select one of the available options: Ground, 2-day, 3-day, or Express.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you'd like to make your payment.
	+ Select one of the available options: Check or Online Payment.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required: false): Enter any payment-related information.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required: false): Enter the total value of your order.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, required: false): Enter any special instructions for the merchant.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required: false): Enter the ID of this form (if applicable).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false): Select the tool assigned for this order.
	+ Select one of the available tools: Tool 1, Tool 2, or Tool 3.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, required: false): Sign your name to confirm your order.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Company** (`customer_company`, text, required: false): Enter your company name.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional customer notes or comments.
