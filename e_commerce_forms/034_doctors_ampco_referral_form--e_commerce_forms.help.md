# Doctors&Co Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from patients or clients for referral to a medical doctor. Please fill in the form with the required details for a smooth and efficient referral process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the patient/client's first name in the "Patient/Client First Name" field.
2. Enter the patient/client's last name in the "Patient/Client Last Name" field.
3. Enter the patient's/client's contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Enter the patient's/client's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter the date of the visit/appointment in the "Date of Visit/Appointment" field.
6. Enter the time of the visit/appointment in the "Time of Visit/Appointment" field.
7. Provide a note for the doctor in the "Note for the Doctor" field.
8. Select all relevant conditions or symptoms in the "Conditions or Symptoms" field.
9. Select the referring doctor in the "Referring Doctor" field.
10. Enter the patient's/client's age in years in the "Age (in years)" field.
11. Enter the number of visits the patient/client requires in the "Number of Visits" field.
12. Select all available time slots for the visit/appointment in the "Available Time Slots" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient/Client First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the patient/client's first name.
* **Patient/Client Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the patient/client's last name.
* **Contact Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the patient's/client's contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's/client's phone number.
* **Date of Visit/Appointment** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the visit/appointment.
* **Time of Visit/Appointment** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the visit/appointment.
* **Note for the Doctor** (`note`, `text`, required): Provide a note for the doctor to review before the visit.
* **Conditions or Symptoms** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all relevant conditions or symptoms from the available options.
* **Referring Doctor** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required): Select the referring doctor from the available options.
* **Age (in years)** (`decimal`, `number`, required): Enter the patient's/client's age in years.
* **Number of Visits** (`integer`, `number`, required): Enter the number of visits the patient/client requires.
* **Available Time Slots** (`select_multiple_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all available time slots for the visit/appointment.
