# Promo Code Validation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather your feedback on your experience with promo codes. Your input will help us improve the performance and usability of promo codes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Select your user experience with the promo code from the list.
2. Choose the time you redeemed the promo code.
3. Select the category of the product involved in the promo code.
4. Choose the reason you couldn't redeem the promo code.
5. Fill in any additional information you'd like to provide about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Experience** (`user_experience`): This field asks you to rate your experience with the promo code. Select the option that best describes how you felt about the promo code.
* **Redeem Time** (`redeem_time`): This field asks for the time you redeemed the promo code. Enter the date in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Redeem Time** (`redeem_time_2`): This field is a duplicate of the previous one for consistency and data collection purposes. You can select any time you want, but please be aware it's optional.
* **Product Category** (`product_category`): This field asks you to select the category of the product you tried to redeem the promo code for. Choose the option that best fits your product.
* **Issue** (`issue`): This field asks you to choose the reason why you couldn't redeem the promo code. Select the option that best describes your experience.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when describing your experience.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the space provided.
* If you're having trouble with a specific promo code, please let us know and we'll do our best to assist you.
