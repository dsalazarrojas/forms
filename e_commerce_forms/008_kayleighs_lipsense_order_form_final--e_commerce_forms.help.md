# Kayleighs Lipsense Order Form FINAL - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture customer order information for efficient order processing and fulfillment. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2.  Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email (2)**: Enter your active and valid email address. This will be used for order updates and notifications.
* **Phone (3)**: Enter your phone number in the format of XXX-XXXX, e.g., 123-456-7890.
* **Product Name (4)**: Enter the exact name of the product you are ordering.
* **Description (5)**: Provide any additional details about your order, such as product preferences or special requests.
* **Order Date (6)**: Select the date you wish your order to be fulfilled.

## Tips
*   Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   Ensure all required fields are completed to avoid delays in order processing.
