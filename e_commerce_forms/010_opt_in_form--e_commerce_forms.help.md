# Opt In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Opt In Form is designed for customers to express their interest in receiving marketing materials, updates, and other communications from the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First name** in the first_name field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the last_name field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the email field. Please use a valid email address that you check regularly.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the phone field. This can be any type of phone number you have, but please be aware that it may not be used for further communication.
5. If applicable, enter your **Company** name in the company field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name if you are affiliated with one.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter information accurately to avoid any issues with data storage and retrieval.
* Use a valid email address to receive important updates and communications.
* If you change your mind or have any questions, contact us using the information provided.
