# Return Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by customers to request authorization for returning a product they purchased.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information (customer name, address, phone number, and email) in the customer_info section.
2. Enter the details of the product you wish to return, including its name, in the product_info section.
3. Specify the reason for your return in the reason_for_return section. You can select one of the provided options or type in a different reason.
4. Attach any relevant documents or proof of purchase, if needed, in the attachment section.
5. Enter any additional comments or questions you may have in the customer_comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required): Enter your name, address, and contact details so we can get back to you with updates on your return request.
* **Product Information** (`product_info`, text, required): Describe the product you wish to return, including its name.
* **Reason for Return** (`reason_for_return`, select_one, required): Choose the reason for your return from the provided options or type in a different reason.
* **Attachment Files** (`attachment_files`, text, optional): Attach any relevant documents or proof of purchase, if needed.
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, number, required): Enter the order ID number for the product you are returning.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the order for the product you are returning.
* **Attachment** (`attachment`, text, optional): Attach any additional documents or images related to your return request.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the customer.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required): Enter your address for the return delivery.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Additional Reason for Return** (`reason_for_return_2`, text, optional): If the reason for your return is not already listed, enter an additional reason.
 
**Tips:** Please fill out the form accurately and consistently to ensure that your return request is handled correctly and efficiently.
