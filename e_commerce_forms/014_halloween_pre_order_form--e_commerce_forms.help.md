# Halloween Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for a Halloween-themed pre-order. It includes fields for customer information, shipping details, product preferences, and special instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked as such in the form.
2. Fill out the customer name field (1) with the customer's name.
3. Enter the customer's shipping address in the address field (2).
4. Enter the customer's email address in the email field (3).
5. Fill out the product quantity field (4) with the desired quantity of the product.
6. Enter the product price field (5) with the price of the product.
7. Select the product type in the select field (6) from the available options: T-Shirt, Tote Bag, or Mug.
8. Choose the order date (7) and time (8) for the pre-order.
9. If necessary, add special instructions (9) for the order.
10. Finally, enter the customer's phone number (10) if it is provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, not required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, not required): Enter the customer's shipping address.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, not required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Product Quantity** (`product_quantity`, number, not required): Enter the desired quantity of the product.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, not required): Enter the price of the product.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, select, not required): Select from the available options: T-Shirt, Tote Bag, or Mug.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, not required): Choose the date for the pre-order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, not required): Choose the time for the pre-order.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, note, not required): Enter any special instructions for the order.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, not required): Enter the customer's phone number.
