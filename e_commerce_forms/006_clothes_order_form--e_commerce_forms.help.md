# Clothes Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture customer information and preferences for an online clothes order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select your preferred delivery address.
6. Enter the type of clothing item you wish to purchase.
7. Choose the color preference for your order.
8. Select the size of the clothing item.
9. Enter the chest, length, and sleeve measurements for fitted items.
10. Enter the quantity of items to order.
11. If you have any material preferences, select one of the options.
12. If you have any special requests or notes, enter them in the "Special Requests or Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, \*): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, \*): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, \*): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
- **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, \*): Enter your street address for delivery purposes.
- **City** (`city`, `text`, \*): Enter the city where you would like to receive your order.
- **State - Province** (`state_province`, `text`, \*): Enter the state or province where you reside.
- **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, `text`, \*): Enter your postal code for delivery purposes.
- **Country** (`country`, `text`, \*): Enter the country where you reside.
- **Type of Clothing** (`clothing_type`, `select_one`, \*): Select the type of clothing item you wish to purchase.
- **Color Preference** (`color_preference`, `text`, \*): Select the color preference for your order.
- **Size** (`size`, `select_one`, \*): Select the size of the clothing item.
- **Chest Measurement - Inches** (`chest_measurement`, `number`, \*): Enter the chest measurement for fitted items.
- **Length Measurement - Inches** (`length_measurement`, `number`, \*): Enter the length measurement for fitted items.
- **Sleeve Length - Inches** (`sleeve_length`, `number`, \*): Enter the sleeve length for fitted items.
- **Waist Measurement - Inches** (`waist_measurement`, `number`, \*): Enter the waist measurement for fitted items.
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, \*): Enter the number of items to order.
- **Material Preference** (`material_preference`, `select_one`, \*): Select the material preference for your order.
- **Preferred Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, \*): Select the preferred delivery date.
- **Special Requests or Notes** (`special_requests`, `text`, \*): Enter any special requests or notes for your order.
