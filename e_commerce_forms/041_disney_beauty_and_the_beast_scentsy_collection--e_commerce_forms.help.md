# Disney Beauty and the Beast Scentsy Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information for the Disney Beauty and the Beast Scentsy Collection. Please fill out the form with your details to place an order for one of the scented products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Fill out all required fields (marked as required) with your personal information.
* Select the desired product from the options provided.
* Enter the quantity you would like to order.
* If you have any additional comments or requests, use the "Message" field to share them with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User First Name** (`user_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **User Last Name** (`user_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Address 1** (`address_1`, text, required): Enter your house number and street address.
* **Address 2** (`address_2`, text, required): Enter your apartment or suite number, if applicable.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you are located.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state or province where you are located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your postal code or zip code.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Product** (`product`, select_one, required): Select one of the available Disney Scentsy Collection products: Beauty and the Beast, Enchanted Castle, or Mrs. Potts & Chip.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of products you would like to order.
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, use this field to share them with us.
