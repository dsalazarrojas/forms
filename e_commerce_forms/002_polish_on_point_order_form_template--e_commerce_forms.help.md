# Polish On Point Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended to collect information for processing customer orders. It is designed to be completed by customers or sales representatives for in-store or online purchases.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the **Order Details** section.
    - Select the appropriate options for your order.
    - If you are not sure what to fill in this field, leave it blank or contact the sales representative for guidance.
2.  Next, enter your **Customer Information** in the **Customer Info** section.
    - Enter your name and any other information as required or requested.
    - This information helps the sales team to contact you and process your order.
3.  Fill out the **Shipping Address** section to ensure correct delivery.
    - Include your shipping address, including the city and zip code.
    - This ensures that your order is delivered to the correct location.
4.  In the **Items To Order** section, select the items you wish to purchase.
    - Check or enter the items you want to order.
    - This information helps the sales team understand what you need.
5.  Choose your **Payment Type** in the **Payment Type** section.
    - Select the payment method that best suits your needs.
    - Options include 'Cash', 'Credit Card', and 'PayPal'.
    - This choice determines how you will pay for your order.
6.  Enter the **Payment Amount** for your order.
    - Input the total cost of your purchase.
    - This information helps the sales team process your payment accurately.
7.  Optionally, you can leave a **Customer Message** for any special requests or comments.
    - Use this field to communicate any specific requests you have.
    - This helps the sales team address any issues promptly.
8.  In the **Order Notes** field, you can include any additional information or comments.
    - This is a place for any notes or comments you want to make about your order.
    - It's a space for you to provide context for the sales team.
9.  Finally, ensure your **Email** and **Phone** are up-to-date for contact purposes.
    - Enter your contact email address and phone number.
    - This helps the sales team contact you with any order updates or issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   *Order Details* (`order_details`, text, optional): This field is intended for you to enter any information about your order.
    *   *Customer Info* (`customer_info`, text, optional): This section is for you to enter your name and any other contact details.
    *   *Shipping Address* (`shipping_address`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your shipping address so that your order can be delivered correctly.
    *   *Shipping City* (`shipping_city`, text, optional): Enter your city for the correct delivery of your order.
    *   *Shipping Zip* (`shipping_zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code for correct delivery of your order.
    *   *Items To Order* (`items_to_order`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the items you wish to purchase from the available options.
    *   *Payment Type* (`payment_type`, select_one, optional): Select the best method of payment for you.
    *   *Payment Amount* (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total cost of your order.
    *   *Customer Message* (`customer_message`, text, optional): If you have any special requests, use this field.
    *   *Order Notes* (`order_notes`, note, optional): You can add any additional notes or comments here.
    *   *E-mail* (`email`, email, optional): Enter your up-to-date email address.
    *   *Phone* (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your current phone number.

## Tips

- Always enter accurate information for smooth order processing.
- If you have any questions, contact the sales representative for assistance.
- Make sure to check your order carefully before submitting it to ensure that it is correct.
- If you need to make changes, contact the sales team promptly.
