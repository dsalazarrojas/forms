# Standard Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Standard Delivery Form is intended to capture customer delivery information for standard delivery purposes, including delivery address, date, time, contact information, and delivery instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
2. Select the delivery date in the "Delivery Date" field.
3. Choose the delivery time in the "Delivery Time" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone Number" field.
6. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
7. If you have any special delivery instructions, enter them in the "Delivery Instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Address**: Enter your delivery address to ensure that your delivery arrives at the correct location.
* **Delivery Date**: Select the date on which you would like to receive your delivery.
* **Delivery Time**: Choose the time on which you would like to receive your delivery.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with any updates or information about your delivery.
* **Customer Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any issues or updates about your delivery.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Delivery Instructions**: If you have any special instructions, such as "Please leave at the back door" or "Please call before delivering", enter them here.

## Tips
* Be accurate when entering your delivery address to ensure that your delivery arrives at the correct location.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your delivery, feel free to contact us using the email or phone number you provided.
* Please note that this form is for standard delivery purposes only, and additional information may be required for special delivery instructions or situations.
