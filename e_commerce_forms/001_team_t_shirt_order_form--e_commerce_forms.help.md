# Team T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect team order information, including team and user details, for t-shirt orders.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the team's name in the "Team Name" field.
2. Enter the team's email in the "Team Email" field.
3. Enter the team's phone number in the "Team Phone" field.
4. Select the team's shirt size from the "Team Shirt Size" dropdown menu.
5. Select the team's desired color from the "Team Color" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the quantity of shirts ordered in the "Team Quantity" field.
7. Enter the team's note in the "Team Note" field (optional).
8. Enter your name in the "User Name" field.
9. Enter your email in the "User Email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "User Phone" field.
11. Enter your note in the "User Note" field (optional).
12. Enter the quantity of shirts you are ordering in the "User Quantity" field.
13. Select your payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
14. Select the payment gateway from the "Payment Gateway" dropdown menu.
15. Enter the order date in the "Order Date" field (optional).
16. Enter the order time in the "Order Time" field (optional).
17. Enter any additional order notes in the "Order Note" field (optional).
18. Enter the team's discount (optional).
19. Enter your discount (optional).
20. Enter the total amount for the order in the "Total Amount" field.
21. Enter the team's address in the "Team Address" field.
22. Enter the team's city in the "Team City" field (optional).
23. Enter the team's state in the "Team State" field.
24. Enter the team's zip code in the "Team Zip" field.
25. Enter the team's country in the "Team Country" field.
26. Enter the team's currency in the "Team Currency" field.
27. Enter any additional team order notes in the "Team Order Notes" field (optional).
28. Select the team's shipping method from the "Team Shipping Method" dropdown menu.
29. Select the team's status from the "Team Status" dropdown menu (optional).
30. Enter the team's shipping cost in the "Team Shipping Cost" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the team that is placing the order.
* **Team Email** (2, email, required): Enter the email of the team that is placing the order.
* **Team Phone** (3, text, required): Enter the phone number of the team that is placing the order.
* **Team Shirt Size** (4, select_one, required): Select the size of the shirt that the team is ordering.
* **Team Color** (5, select_multiple, required): Select the color of the shirt that the team is ordering.
* **Team Quantity** (6, number, required): Enter the quantity of shirts that the team is ordering.
* **Team Note** (7, note, required): Enter any additional notes for the team order.
* **User Name** (8, text, required): Enter your name.
* **User Email** (9, email, required): Enter your email.
* **User Phone** (10, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **User Note** (11, note, optional): Enter any additional notes for your order.
* **User Quantity** (12, number, required): Enter the quantity of shirts you are ordering.
* **Payment Method** (13, text, required): Select the payment method that you will use (e.g. credit card).
* **Payment Gateway** (14, select_multiple, required): Select the payment gateway that you will use (e.g. Stripe, PayPal).
* **Order Date** (15, date, optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (16, time, optional): Enter the time of the order.
* **Order Note** (17, note, optional): Enter any additional notes for the order.
* **Team Discount** (18, number, optional): Enter the team's discount (if applicable).
* **User Discount** (19, number, optional): Enter your discount (if applicable).
* **Total Amount** (20, number, required): Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Team Address** (21, text, required): Enter the team's address.
* **Team City** (22, text, optional): Enter the team's city.
* **Team State** (23, text, required): Enter the team's state.
* **Team Zip** (24, text, required): Enter the team's zip code.
* **Team Country** (25, text, required): Enter the team's country.
* **Team Currency** (26, text, required): Enter the team's currency.
* **Team Order Notes** (27, note, optional): Enter any additional team order notes.
* **Team Shipping Method** (28, select_multiple, required): Select the team's shipping method (e.g. Ground, Air).
* **Team Status** (29, select_one, optional): Select the team's status (e.g. New, In Transit, Shipped).
* **Team Shipping Cost** (30, number, required): Enter the team's shipping cost.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your team's and user's details before submitting the form.
* Review the team's and user's notes for any additional information.
* Select the correct payment method and payment gateway.
* Enter the correct order date and time (if applicable).
* Verify the team's and user's details before submitting the form.
