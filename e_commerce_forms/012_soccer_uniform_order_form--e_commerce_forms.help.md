# Soccer Uniform Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soccer Uniform Order Form is a simple form designed to collect information required for order fulfillment and payment processing. It is intended for customers who want to place orders for soccer uniforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Select the correct Jersey Size for the uniform.
3. Enter your Team Name.
4. Enter your Team Number.
5. Choose an Order Date for the uniform.
6. Select the payment method you wish to use.
7. Enter your Email address.
8. Enter your Phone number.
9. Enter your Address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (first_name, text, optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (last_name, text, optional): Enter your last name.
- **Jersey Size** (jersey_size, select_one, optional): Choose the size of the jersey you want to order.
- **Team Name** (team_name, text, optional): Enter your team name.
- **Team Number** (team_number, number, optional): Enter your team number.
- **Order Date** (order_date, date, optional): Choose the date you want the uniform to be ordered for.
- **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_one, optional): Select the method you want to use for payment (Square or PayPal).
- **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Address** (address, text, optional): Enter your address.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your order details before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or issues, contact your team management for assistance.
- Ensure you have entered correct payment information to avoid any processing issues.
