# Product Customization Prototype Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to order customized products and provide order details to facilitate the order process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the product information section by selecting the product you want to customize and providing any additional details required.
2. Choose your customization options from the dropdown menu to select the specific options you want for your product.
3. Enter your shipping information, including any delivery dates and times you have specified.
4. Select your customer information, including your order type and any other relevant details.
5. Provide any additional order notes or comments if needed.
6. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
7. Select the order date and time you want to place the order.
8. Enter any order comments or notes if needed.
9. Finally, submit the form to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Information**: Enter the product you want to customize and any additional details required.
* **Customization Options**: Choose from the dropdown menu to select the specific options you want for your product.
* **Product Description**: Enter a brief description of the product you want to customize.
* **Shipping Information**: Select your shipping details, including any delivery dates and times you have specified.
* **Customer Information**: Select your order type and any other relevant details.
* **Order Notes**: Enter any additional order notes or comments if needed.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Order Date**: Select the order date and time you want to place the order.
* **Order Comments**: Enter any additional order comments or notes if needed.
* **Form Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the order form itself.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool assigned to work on your order.
* **Assigned User**: Enter the user assigned to work on your order.
* **Form Version**: Enter the version of the form you are using.
