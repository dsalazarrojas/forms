# Tool Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers when they place orders for tools. Fill out the form with the required fields and submit it to place your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields with your details as requested.
2. Choose your preferred order type (new or existing).
3. Select the tool you would like to order (chatjimmy or other).
4. Enter your shipping details and any additional order notes.
5. Enter your contact information (email and phone number).
6. Review your submission before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the customer placing the order.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping details, including any relevant information about your delivery address.
* **Damaged Tool Number** (`damaged_tool_number`, text, required/optional): If the tool is damaged, enter the number of the tool to help us identify it.
* **Preferred Order Type** (`preferred_order_type`, select_one, required): Choose between "New" and "Existing" to indicate whether this is a new or existing order.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or instructions about your order.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Choose between "chatjimmy" and "other" to select the tool you would like to order.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Click to submit your order after filling out all the required fields.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your order is processed efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, contact the support team for assistance.
* Double-check your submission before clicking the "Submit" button to avoid errors.
