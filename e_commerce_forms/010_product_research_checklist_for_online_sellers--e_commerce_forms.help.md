# Product Research Checklist For Online Sellers - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help online sellers research and prepare products for their store.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, start by selecting the relevant category for your product from the options provided. Then, fill out the product name, description, and target audience based on your product and market analysis. 

Next, select the pricing strategy that best fits your product and business goals. 

After that, evaluate the competition by selecting how strong or weak it is. You can also tag your product with relevant tags to help with search and organization. 

Finally, fill out any additional product attributes such as price, image, and video links. 

Note that some fields are optional and might not be necessary for every product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category Name** (`category_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the main category of your product, such as Electronics or Fashion.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, required/optional): Identify the target audience for your product, such as customers who are interested in home decor or electronics.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required/optional): Write a brief description of your product, including its key features and benefits.
* **Pricing Strategy** (`pricing_strategy`, select_one, required/optional): Select a pricing strategy that best fits your product, such as Low, Medium, or High.
* **Competition Analysis** (`competition_analysis`, select_multiple, required/optional): Evaluate how strong or weak the competition is for your product.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your product, such as a brand name or product line name.
* **Product Tags** (`product_tags`, select_multiple, required/optional): Tag your product with relevant tags to help with search and organization, such as Electronics or Home Goods.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, required/optional): Enter the price of your product.
* **Product Image** (`product_image`, text, required/optional): Enter the URL or link to an image of your product.
* **Product Video** (`product_video`, text, required/optional): Enter the URL or link to a video of your product.
* **Product Audio** (`product_audio`, text, required/optional): Enter the URL or link to an audio of your product.
* **Product File** (`product_file`, text, required/optional): Enter the URL or link to a file related to your product.
* **Customer Reviews** (`customer_reviews`, text, required/optional): Enter any customer reviews for your product.
* **Product Rating** (`product_rating`, number, required/optional): Enter the rating of your product.
* **Product Ranking** (`product_ranking`, number, required/optional): Enter the ranking of your product.
* **Product Sales** (`product_sales`, number, required/optional): Enter the sales of your product.
* **Product Revenue** (`product_revenue`, number, required/optional): Enter the revenue of your product.
* **Product Margin** (`product_margin`, number, required/optional): Enter the margin of your product.
* **Product Weight** (`product_weight`, number, required/optional): Enter the weight of your product.
* **Product Dimension** (`product_dimension`, number, required/optional): Enter the dimension of your product.
* **Product Color** (`product_color`, text, required/optional): Enter the color of your product.
* **Product Size** (`product_size`, text, required/optional): Enter the size of your product.
* **Product Weight Unit** (`product_weight_unit`, select_one, required/optional): Select the unit of weight for your product.
* **Product Dimension Unit** (`product_dimension_unit`, select_one, required/optional): Select the unit of dimension for your product.
