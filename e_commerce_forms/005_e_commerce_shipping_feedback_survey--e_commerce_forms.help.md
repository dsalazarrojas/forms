# E Commerce Shipping Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Commerce Shipping Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from customers about their shipping experience. This feedback will help us identify areas for improvement and refine our shipping processes to enhance customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Select the shipping carrier that was used to deliver your package.
2. Indicate the delivery status (if it was on time, late, or unknown).
3. If you have a specific delivery time, please enter it in the format "MM/DD/YY".
4. Provide a brief note about your delivery experience, if any.
5. Check the communication channels used by the shipping carrier (phone, email, text message, etc.).
6. Describe the condition of your package (in good condition, damaged, or missing parts).
7. Select the type of package you received (box, envelope, or other).
8. If you're comfortable sharing, enter the value of your package.
9. Rate your shipping speed experience (fast, slow, or unknown).
10. Assess your overall experience with the shipping service (excellent, good, fair, or poor).
11. Evaluate the quality of communication from the shipping carrier (excellent, good, fair, or poor).
12. Rate the quality of customer support from the shipping carrier (excellent, good, fair, or poor).
13. Check how clear the return policy was (clear, unclear, or unknown).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipping Carrier** (shipping_carrier, select_one): Select the carrier that delivered your package (USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL, or Other).
* **Delivery Status** (delivery_status, select_one): Indicate the delivery status (On Time, Late, or Unknown).
* **Delivery Time** (delivery_time, date): If you have a specific delivery time, enter it in the format "MM/DD/YY".
* **Delivery Note** (delivery_note, text): Provide a brief note about your delivery experience, if any.
* **Communication Channel** (communication_channel, select_multiple): Check the communication channels used by the shipping carrier (Phone, Email, Text Message, etc.).
* **Package Condition** (package_condition, select_one): Describe the condition of your package (In Good Condition, Damaged, Missing Parts).
* **Package Type** (package_type, select_one): Select the type of package you received (Box, Envelope, or Other).
* **Package Value** (package_value, number): If you're comfortable sharing, enter the value of your package.
* **Shipping Speed** (shipping_speed, select_one): Rate your shipping speed experience (Fast, Slow, or Unknown).
* **Overall Experience** (overall_experience, select_one): Assess your overall experience with the shipping service (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Communication Quality** (communication_quality, select_one): Evaluate the quality of communication from the shipping carrier (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Customer Support** (customer_support, select_one): Rate the quality of customer support from the shipping carrier (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Return Policy** (return_policy, select_one): Check how clear the return policy was (Clear, Unclear, or Unknown).

## Tips
- Please answer all questions truthfully to provide valuable feedback.
- Select only one option from each field, even if none of the options apply.
- If you have any additional comments or feedback, feel free to add them in the "Delivery Note" field.
- Your input will help us improve our shipping services and better serve you.
