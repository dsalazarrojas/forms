# Beer Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beer Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for their beer orders, ensuring accurate and efficient processing of their requests. This guide will walk you through each field of the form, explaining its purpose and required information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field (ID 1).
2. Provide your primary contact email address in the "Email Address" field (ID 2).
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (ID 3).
4. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field (ID 4).
5. Confirm that you are 21 years or older in the "Age Verification" field (ID 6).
6. Select the type of order (ID 7).
7. Choose the beers you'd like to order in the "Beer Selection" field (ID 8).
8. Specify the number of cases or packs in the "Quantity" field (ID 9).
9. Choose your preferred package size in the "Package Size" field (ID 10).
10. Select the method for delivery (ID 11).
11. If applicable, choose a preferred delivery date (ID 12).
12. Select your preferred shipping method (ID 13).
13. Choose your payment method (ID 14).
14. If applicable, choose your subscription frequency (ID 15).
15. If applicable, indicate if the order is for a special occasion (ID 16).
16. Enter a gift message if the order is a gift (ID 17).
17. Enter any promo codes you might have (ID 18).
18. Enter any special instructions for the order (ID 19).
19. Estimate the total cost of the order (ID 20).
20. Accept the terms and conditions (ID 21).
21. Acknowledge the risks associated with alcohol consumption (ID 22).
22. Enter the date of the order (ID 23).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (ID 1)**: Enter your legal full name.
* **Email Address (ID 2)**: Provide your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number (ID 3)**: Enter your best phone number to reach you.
* **Shipping Address (ID 4)**: Enter your full street address.
* **Date of Birth (ID 5)**: Enter your date of birth, as you must be 21 years or older to purchase beer.
* **Age Verification (ID 6)**: Confirm that you are 21 years or older.
* **Order Type (ID 7)**: Select the type of order (individual, subscription, wholesale, event or catering, gift, etc.).
* **Beer Selection (ID 8)**: Choose the beers you'd like to order.
* **Quantity (ID 9)**: Enter the number of cases or packs you'd like to order.
* **Package Size (ID 10)**: Select your preferred package size.
* **Delivery Method (ID 11)**: Choose how you'd like to receive your order.
* **Preferred Delivery Date (ID 12)**: If applicable, select a preferred delivery date.
* **Shipping Method (ID 13)**: Select your preferred shipping speed.
* **Payment Method (ID 14)**: Choose how you'd like to pay for the order.
* **Subscription Frequency (ID 15)**: If subscribing, select how often you'd like to receive your order.
* **Special Occasion (ID 16)**: If the order is for a special occasion, select the type of occasion.
* **Gift Message (ID 17)**: Enter a message to include with the gift (if applicable).
* **Promotional Code (ID 18)**: Enter any applicable promo codes.
* **Order Notes (ID 19)**: Enter any special instructions for the order.
* **Estimated Total (ID 20)**: Estimate the total cost of the order.
* **Terms and Conditions (ID 21)**: Accept the terms and conditions of the form.
* **Alcohol Warning Acknowledgment (ID 22)**: Acknowledge the risks associated with alcohol consumption.
* **Order Date (ID 23)**: Enter the date of the order.

## Tips

* Please double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer support team.
* Ensure that you have entered all required fields to avoid delays in processing your order.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask our support team for assistance.
