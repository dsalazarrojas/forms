# Fashion And Hip Hop Marketplace Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion And Hip Hop Marketplace Registration form is designed to collect essential information from vendors who want to become part of the marketplace.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as "required" and ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
2. Select your vendor category from the "What type of vendor are you?" field.
3. Provide your business contact information, including email, password, vendor name, and business name.
4. Enter your address, city, country, and ZIP code.
5. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that you use regularly.
* **Password** (`password`, text, required/optional): Enter a secure password that you will use to access your account.
* **What type of vendor are you?** (`vendor_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the following categories:
	+ Fashion
	+ Hip Hop
	+ Other
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your business or personal brand.
* **Business Name** (`vendor_business_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your business name (if applicable).
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your business or physical address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city where your business or location is situated.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required/optional): Enter the country where your business or location is situated.
* **ZIP** (`zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the ZIP code of your business or location.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
