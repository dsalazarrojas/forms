# Soccer Team T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to place orders for soccer team t shirts and game jerseys. The form collects customer information and payment method details, making it easier for customers to place orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
 1. Enter your name and email address in the fields provided.
  2. Select the name of your team and the type of order you want to place (t shirt or game jersey).
  3. Choose the size of the order and quantity.
  4. Select the method of payment (credit card or bank transfer).
  5. Optionally, add any additional notes about the order.
  6. Confirm your email address to confirm the order details.
  7. Submit your order to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (1): Enter your name to be associated with the order.
* **Email** (2): Enter your email address for order confirmations and communication.
* **Team Name** (3): Select the name of your team to place the order.
* **Player Number** (4): Enter your player number to confirm the order.
* **Order Type** (5): Select between "team t shirt" and "game jersey" to place the correct type of order.
* **Size** (6): Choose the size of the order, selecting from medium, large, and x-large.
* **Quantity** (7): Enter the number of items to order.
* **Payment Method** (8): Select between "credit card" and "bank transfer" to choose the method of payment.
* **Note** (9): Optionally, add any additional notes about the order (e.g., special requests).
* **Email Confirmation** (10): Enter your email address again to confirm your email address.
* **Confirm Order** (11): Enter a confirmation of your order details (e.g., "I confirm this order is correct").
* **Confirm Email** (12): Enter your email address again to confirm your email address.

## Tips
* Double-check the order details before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* Use a valid email address for communication and order confirmations.
* If you have any issues with the order process, contact our support team.
* Orders are subject to availability and processing times; contact us if you have any questions.
