# custom_simple_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **custom_simple_order_form** is designed to collect information from customers about their orders. This form is intended for use in e-commerce environments where customers need to provide details about their orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form layout and understand the sections provided.
2. Fill in the relevant information for each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **customer_info** (Customer Information)
* **customer_info** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): 
  This field is where you can enter your name, email, or any other relevant customer information.

### 2. **product_info** (Product Information)
* **product_info** (`product_info`, text, required: false): 
  Enter the product details, such as product name or description, that you would like to order.

### 3. **order_info** (Order Information)
* **order_info** (`order_info`, text, required: false): 
  Provide any additional information about your order, such as special requests or comments.

## Tips
- Fill in the fields as accurately and completely as possible to ensure that the order is processed correctly.
- If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact form@gic.mx.
