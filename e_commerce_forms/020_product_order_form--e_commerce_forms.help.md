# Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by customers to place orders for products. Please fill it out carefully to ensure your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click "Submit" when you are ready to place an order.
2. Fill in your email address, if required.
3. Enter your phone number, if required.
4. Enter your name, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email (customer_email)**: Enter the email address associated with the order. This will help us contact you with updates and information about your order.
* **Phone (customer_phone)**: Enter the phone number associated with the order, if required. This will be used to contact you in case we need more information or have questions about your order.
* **Name (customer_name)**: Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the order receipt, if required. Please use the format "Name Surname" or "First Name Last Name". 
* **Product Order Form (product_order_form)**: This is the main section of the form where the customer can enter their order details. This field is not required.
