# E Commerce Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Commerce Claim Form is designed to collect information from customers who are making claims about their purchases on our e-commerce platform. This form helps to verify the customer's claim and process their request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your answer to "Order Info" from the provided options.
2. Enter the date of your order using the calendar or date picker.
3. Enter the purchase price of the product(s) you are making a claim about.
4. Choose the relevant option for "Product Info" from the provided options.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Order ID" depending on whether you have an order ID or not.
6. Enter the price of the product you are making a claim about.
7. Provide a brief description of your purchase details in the "Purchase Details" field.
8. Enter your email address if you want us to contact you about your claim.
9. Provide your phone number so we can contact you.
10. Leave a comment about your claim or issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Order Info
* **Order Info**: (Optional) Yes/No - Indicate if the order was made on our platform.
* **Order Info** (`Yes`, `No`, required: `false`): Select "Yes" if you made a purchase on our platform. This will help us verify your claim.

### Order Date
* **Order Date**: (Optional) - Enter the date of your order.
* **Order Date** (`date`, required: `false`): Enter the date when you made the purchase.

### Purchase Price
* **Purchase Price**: (Optional) - Enter the price you paid for the product(s).
* **Purchase Price** (`number`, required: `false`): Enter the price of the product you are making a claim about.

### Product Info
* **Product Info**: (Optional) - Choose the relevant option from the list.
* **Product Info** (`Yes,No`, required: `false`): Select "Yes" if the product you are making a claim about is related to the issue.

### Order ID
* **Order ID**: (Optional) - Select "Yes" if you have an order ID.
* **Order ID** (`Yes,No`, required: `false`): Select "Yes" if you have a valid order ID.

### Product Price
* **Product Price**: (Optional) - Enter the price of the product.
* **Product Price** (`number`, required: `false`): Enter the price of the product you are making a claim about.

### Purchase Details
* **Purchase Details**: (Optional) - Provide a brief description of your purchase details.
* **Purchase Details** (`note`, required: `false`): Provide a brief description of your purchase details.

### Customer Email
* **Customer Email**: (Optional) - Enter your email address.
* **Customer Email** (`email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address for us to contact you about your claim.

### Customer Phone
* **Customer Phone**: (Optional) - Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Phone** (`text`, required: `false`): Provide your phone number so we can contact you.

### Customer Comments
* **Customer Comments**: (Optional) - Leave a comment about your claim or issue.
* **Customer Comments** (`text`, required: `false`): Leave a comment about your claim or issue.

## Tips

* Please fill in the form accurately and carefully.
* Avoid submitting duplicate claims.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
