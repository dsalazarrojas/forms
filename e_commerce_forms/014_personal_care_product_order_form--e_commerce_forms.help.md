# Personal Care Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to provide their personal care product order details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the product you are interested in from the "Choose a product" dropdown menu.
2. If necessary, enter the quantity of the product you would like to purchase in the "Quantity" field.
3. Enter the price of the product in the "Price" field, if applicable.
4. Input your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Input your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Add any additional notes you have in the "Additional Notes" field.
7. Choose your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
8. Input the date of payment in the "Payment Date" field, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Choose a product** (`product`, select_one, required/optional): Select the product you are interested in purchasing.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required/optional): Specify the number of products you would like to purchase.
* **Price** (`price`, number, required/optional): Input the price of the product, if applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Input your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Input your phone number.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional information you want to share.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required/optional): Input the date of the payment, if required.
