## Wholesale Purchase Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wholesale Purchase Order Form is a form designed for wholesale customers to place orders for bulk quantities of items. This form allows customers to provide their contact and customer details, select the items they wish to purchase, and specify the quantity and additional information for each item.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
2. Provide your customer details in the "Customer Details" field.
3. Select the items you wish to purchase from the "Item Details" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the item number for each selected item in the "Item Number" field.
5. Specify the quantity for each item in the number field.
6. Add any additional information in the "Additional Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Enter your name, email, phone number, or any other relevant contact information.
### Customer Details
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required: false): Provide your company name, address, and any other relevant customer details.
### Item Details
* **Item Details** (`item_details`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the items you wish to purchase from the dropdown menu.
### Item Number
* **Item Number** (`item_number`, number, required: false): Enter the item number for each selected item.
### Quantity
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Specify the quantity for each item.
### Additional Info
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or information related to your order.
