# new_customer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_customer_registration_form is used to register a new customer by collecting necessary information such as name, contact details, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your first name in the `First Name` field.
* Enter your last name in the `Last Name` field.
* Provide your email address in the `Email` field.
* Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field (optional).
* Enter your address in the `Address` field (optional).
* Enter your city in the `City` field (optional).
* Enter your postal code in the `Postal Code` field (optional).
* Select your country of residence from the `Country` field (optional).
* Create a password in the `Password` field.
* Confirm your password in the `Confirm Password` field.
* Choose your role from the `Role` field (Customer, Staff, or Owner) (optional).
* Select your department from the `Department` field (Sales, Marketing, or Support) (optional).
* Enter your date of birth in the `Date of Birth` field (optional).
* Enter the date and time of registration in the `Date of Registration` and `Time of Registration` fields (optional).
* Add any additional note in the `Note` field (optional).
* If applicable, select your assigned tool from the `Assigned Tool` field.
* Select your category from the `Category` field (E-commerce, Finance, or Human Resources) (optional).
* Enter your form ID in the `Form ID` field (optional).
* If you want to download the output file, click on the `Output File` field.
* If you have been assigned a tool, enter its ID in the `Assigned Tool ID` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Text)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Address (Text)**: Enter your address (optional).
* **City (Text)**: Enter your city (optional).
* **Postal Code (Text)**: Enter your postal code (optional).
* **Country (Text)**: Select your country of residence (optional).
* **Password (Text)**: Create a password for your account.
* **Confirm Password (Text)**: Confirm your password.
* **Role (Select One)**: Choose your role (Customer, Staff, or Owner) (optional).
* **Department (Select Multiple)**: Select your department (Sales, Marketing, or Support) (optional).
* **Date of Birth (Date)**: Enter your date of birth (optional).
* **Date of Registration (Date)**: Enter the date of registration (optional).
* **Time of Registration (Time)**: Enter the time of registration (optional).
* **Note (Note)**: Add any additional note (optional).
* **Assigned Tool (Text)**: If applicable, select your assigned tool.
* **Category (Select One)**: Select your category (E-commerce, Finance, or Human Resources) (optional).
* **Form ID (Number)**: Enter your form ID (optional).
* **Output File (Text)**: If you want to download the output file, click on this field.
* **Assigned Tool ID (Text)**: If you have been assigned a tool, enter its ID.
