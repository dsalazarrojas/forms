# Vegetables Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Vegetables Order Form" is a tool used by customers to place orders for vegetables. It collects essential information to process and fulfill the order accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **customer_info** page with your details (page 1).
2. Provide your **delivery_info** (page 2), including any additional instructions.
3. On the **products** page (page 3), select the vegetables you want to purchase and choose their quantities on the **product_quantities** page (page 4).
4. Optionally, add any **comments** on the last page (page 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_info** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): Please fill in your name and contact information to help us process your order accurately.
* **delivery_info** (`delivery_info`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional delivery instructions or special requests you may have.
* **products** (`products`, select_multiple, required): Select the vegetables you would like to purchase. You can choose from the options provided.
* **product_quantities** (`product_quantities`, text, required): Enter the quantities for each selected vegetable.
* **comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, please add them here.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your order.
- Select the correct quantities for each vegetable to avoid errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
