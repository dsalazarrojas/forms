## Healthy And Affordable Food Product Order Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to order food products from a restaurant at affordable prices. It gathers information about the customer's order preferences and requirements for delivery.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the restaurant name and any other relevant details.
2. Select the product you would like to order (Product 1, Product 2, or Product 3).
3. Specify the quantity of the product you would like to order.
4. Select a delivery date for your order.
5. Choose a delivery time for your order.
6. Enter your contact email.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. If you have any additional information to share with the restaurant, please use the "Additional Information" field.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order From Restaurant**: Enter the name of the restaurant from which you would like to place your order.
* **Product Orders**: Select the product you would like to order (Product 1, Product 2, or Product 3).
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of units of the product you would like to order.
* **Delivery Date**: Select the date on which you would like to receive your order.
* **Delivery Time**: Choose the time slot for your order delivery.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with order updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Additional Information**: If you have any additional information to share with the restaurant, such as special requests or instructions, please enter it here.

### Tips

* Please make sure to provide accurate information for a smooth order fulfillment process.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your order and look forward to serving you soon!
