# Market Seller Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for market sellers to apply to become certified as a seller on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Personal Details" section.
2. Indicate whether you have a list of products or not.
3. Provide details about your products, including:
	* Product name
	* Product description
	* Price
	* Quantity
	* Tags (key words or phrases that describe your product)
4. Choose your payment method (yes/no).
5. Provide your business details, including:
	* Store address
	* Contact person
	* Contact details
	* Operating hours
	* Payment details
	* Return policy
	* Cancellation policy
	* Terms and conditions
	* Licensing agreement
	* Certification certificate
	* Certification number
	* Certification date
	* Certification expiration
6. Indicate the status of your certification (active/inactive or not required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details** (`Personal Details`, text, required): Enter your personal details such as name, address, and contact information.
* **Products** (`Products`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you have a list of products or not.
* **Product Name** (`Product Name`, text, required): Enter the name of your product.
* **Product Description** (`Product Description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your product.
* **Product Price** (`Product Price`, number, required): Enter the price of your product.
* **Product Quantity** (`Product Quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of your product.
* **Product Tags** (`Product Tags`, text, required): Enter key words or phrases that describe your product.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, required): Choose the payment method you will use for your transactions.
* **Payment Terms** (`Payment Terms`, text, required): Enter your payment terms and conditions.
* **Shipping Terms** (`Shipping Terms`, text, required): Enter your shipping terms and conditions.
* **Market Seller Terms** (`Market Seller Terms`, text, required): Enter your market seller terms and conditions.
* **Store Address** (`Store Address`, text, required): Enter your store address.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, text, required): Enter your contact person's name and title.
* **Store Contact Details** (`Store Contact Details`, text, required): Enter your store contact details.
* **Operating Hours** (`Operating Hours`, time, required): Enter your store's operating hours.
* **Payment Details** (`Payment Details`, text, required): Enter your payment details.
* **Return Policy** (`Return Policy`, text, required): Enter your return policy.
* **Cancellation Policy** (`Cancellation Policy`, text, required): Enter your cancellation policy.
* **Terms And Conditions** (`Terms And Conditions`, text, required): Enter your terms and conditions.
* **Licensing Agreement** (`Licensing Agreement`, text, required): Enter your licensing agreement.
* **Certification Certificate** (`Certification Certificate`, text, required): Enter your certification certificate.
* **Certification Number** (`Certification Number`, text, required): Enter your certification number.
* **Certification Date** (`Certification Date`, date, required): Enter your certification date.
* **Certification Expiration** (`Certification Expiration`, date, required): Enter your certification expiration date.
* **Certification Status** (`Certification Status`, select_multiple, not required): Indicate the status of your certification (active/inactive).
