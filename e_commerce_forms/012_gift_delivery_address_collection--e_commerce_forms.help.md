# Gift Delivery Address Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gift Delivery Address Collection form is a tool to gather information for gift delivery to recipients. This form helps to ensure that gifts are delivered to the correct address and that the recipient knows who is sending the gift and why.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the recipient's name in the **recipient_name** field.
2. Enter the recipient's email address in the **email** field.
3. Select a delivery date using the **delivery_date** field.
4. Specify the delivery time using the **delivery_time** field.
5. Enter your name in the **sender_name** field.
6. Enter your email address in the **sender_email** field.
7. Enter your phone number in the **sender_phone** field.
8. Include a message for the recipient in the **gift_message** field (optional).
9. Enter the recipient's address in the **recipient_address** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **recipient_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the person receiving the gift.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the person receiving the gift.
* **delivery_date** (date, optional): Select the date to deliver the gift (if no date is selected, the gift will be delivered on the current date).
* **delivery_time** (time, optional): Select the time to deliver the gift.
* **sender_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person sending the gift.
* **sender_email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the person sending the gift.
* **sender_phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person sending the gift.
* **gift_message** (text, optional): Enter a message to be included with the gift (optional).
* **recipient_address** (text, optional): Enter the address of the person receiving the gift.
