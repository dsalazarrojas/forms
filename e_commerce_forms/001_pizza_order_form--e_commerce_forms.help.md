# Pizza Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal details and order information from users to facilitate delivery or pickup of a pizza. It will guide users through a series of steps to provide all necessary information.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  In the **Form Data** section, please provide as much detail as you need to describe your order and any special requests.
2.  Enter your name in the **Name** field to help the restaurant staff identify you.
3.  Provide your **Address** where the order should be delivered or for pickup.
4.  Enter your **Phone** number so we can contact you to confirm the order.
5.  If you have an **Email**, please provide it as a contact point.
6.  For **Date** and **Time**, select the day you would like to receive your order.
7.  Enter any **Notes** you have about your order in this field.
8.  Indicate if the **Payment** method is **Yes** or **No**.
9.  If you are paying with a **Payment Gateway**, select **Yes** or **No**.
10. Select the **Payment Method** used to pay for the order.
11. Enter the **Total Cost** of the order, this field is optional and will be populated with the order total.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Form Data** (Text): A field to add any additional information about your order.
*   **Name** (Text): Enter your name to help the restaurant identify you.
*   **Address** (Text): Enter your address for delivery or pickup.
*   **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for contact.
*   **Email** (Email): Enter your email address as a contact point.
*   **Date** (Date): Select the date you would like to receive your order.
*   **Time** (Time): Select the time you would like to receive your order.
*   **Notes** (Text): Add any additional notes about your order.
*   **Payment** (Select One): Indicate if you are paying for the order.
*   **Payment Gateway** (Select One): Indicate if you are using a payment gateway.
*   **Payment Method** (Select One): Select the payment method used.
*   **Total Cost** (Number): Enter the total cost of the order, this field will be populated with the order total.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide accurate information to help us get your order right.
*   If you have any special requests, please include them in the **Form Data** field.
*   Please confirm your order details before submitting the form.
