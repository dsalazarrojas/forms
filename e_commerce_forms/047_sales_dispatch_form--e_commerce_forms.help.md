# Sales Dispatch Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sales Dispatch Form is an e-commerce form used for collecting sales and order information from customers or sales teams.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the sale number field with the corresponding sales order number.
2. Provide the dispatch date for the sale.
3. (Optional) Enter the product name if relevant.
4. Enter the order number.
5. Enter the customer name.
6. Enter the customer email, which will be used to contact the customer regarding the sale.
7. (Optional) Enter the customer phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sale** (`1`, `number`, required): Enter the sales order number.
* **Dispatch Date** (`2`, `date`, required): Enter the date the sale will be dispatched.
* **Product Name** (`3`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the product being sold, if applicable.
* **Order Number** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter the order number associated with the sale.
* **Customer Name** (`5`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`6`, `email`, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
