# Nail Sample Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Nail Sample Form" is designed for customers to provide their feedback and suggestions on our nail polish product samples.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select one or more nail samples you have received from us in the "Choose Nail Sample" field.
2.  If you have a specific order number, please provide it in the "Order" field. This is optional and should only be filled in if you have a valid reason to do so.
3.  Enter your contact email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Provide your contact phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Choose Nail Sample** (`nail_sample_form_input_1`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose one or more nail samples you have received from us. Select as many options as apply.
*   **Order** (`nail_sample_form_input_2`, `text`, required: false): This field is optional and only needed if you have a valid reason to do so. Please only fill this in if you have a specific order number.
*   **Email** (`nail_sample_form_input_3`, `email`, required: false): Enter your contact email address.
*   **Phone** (`nail_sample_form_input_4`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Date** (`nail_sample_form_input_5`, `date`, required: false): This field will be used to track the date the customer received the nail samples.
