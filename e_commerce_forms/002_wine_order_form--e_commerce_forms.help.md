# Wine Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Wine Order Form is a digital interface designed for customers to submit their orders for wine. By completing this form, customers will be able to select wine options, provide contact details, and submit their notes or requests. This guide will help ensure that you use the form correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all the required fields to ensure your order is successfully submitted.
2. Enter your personal details (First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and Address) in the corresponding fields.
3. Select the type of wine you wish to order from the Wine dropdown menu.
4. Specify the quantity of wine you would like to order.
5. Choose one or multiple options from the Select One or Select Multiple dropdown menus as applicable.
6. Add any additional notes or messages to your order if needed.
7. Enter the date and time for the order (if relevant).
8. Repeat the same process for any additional wine orders (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your valid email address to receive order confirmations and updates.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number to receive call or text confirmations.
* **Address** (Text): Enter your address to facilitate delivery or pick-up.
* **Date** (Date): Enter the date of the order if it's a specific date or the desired delivery date.
* **Wine** (Select One): Select the type of wine you wish to order from the available options (Chardonnay, Merlot, or Pinot).
* **Quantity** (Number): Specify the quantity of wine you would like to order.
* **Select One** (Select One): Choose one option from the dropdown menu as applicable.
* **Select Multiple** (Select Multiple): Select multiple options from the dropdown menu as applicable.
* **Note** (Note): Add any additional notes or messages to your order.
* **Time** (Time): Enter the time of the order if it's a specific time.
* **Note 1** (Note): Enter any additional notes or messages for the order.
* **Phone 1** (Text): Enter any additional phone numbers for the order.
* **Email 1** (Email): Enter any additional email addresses for the order.
* **Address 1** (Text): Enter any additional addresses for the order.
* **Date 1** (Date): Enter the date for the additional order if it's a specific date.
* **Wine 1** (Select One): Select the type of wine for the additional order from the available options.
* **Additional orders can be added as needed** by repeating the same steps for each wine order. 

## Tips

* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* Make sure to select the correct wine type and quantity for each order.
* Use the Select Multiple field for orders with multiple options.
* Add any additional notes or messages to ensure your order is processed correctly.

This help guide is designed to support end-users in completing the Wine Order Form efficiently and effectively. If you have any further questions or need additional support, please don't hesitate to contact us.
