# E Commerce Subscription Box Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users sign up for an e-commerce subscription box. It is intended for users who want to purchase a subscription to a specific e-commerce platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Information section with your name, email address, and other required information.
2. Choose your plan selection from the available options.
3. Provide subscription details, such as the length of your subscription and any other relevant information.
4. Select your payment method.
5. Add any notes or comments you'd like to include with your application.
6. Read and sign the agreement to confirm your understanding of the terms.
7. Click the "Submit" button to submit your application.
8. Review your application and make any necessary corrections before clicking the "Confirm" button.
9. If you need to cancel your application, click the "Cancel" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required): Fill in your name, email address, and other required information to identify yourself as a user.
* **Plan Selection** (`plan_selection`, text, required): Choose your preferred plan from the available options to determine the terms of your subscription.
* **Subscription Details** (`subscription_details`, text, required): Provide details about your subscription, such as the length of your subscription and any other relevant information.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, text, required): Select your preferred payment method to complete the transaction.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any notes or comments you'd like to include with your application.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, note, required): Read and sign the agreement to confirm your understanding of the terms.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign your name to confirm your application.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your application.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, text, optional): Review and confirm your application before proceeding.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, optional): If you need to cancel your application, click this button.
