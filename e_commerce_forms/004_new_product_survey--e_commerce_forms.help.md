# New Product Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers about a new product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form by entering your honest feedback in the text fields.
2. Select your rating by choosing from the available options in the rating field.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by the * symbol.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description or name of the product you are providing feedback on.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a detailed description of the product you are providing feedback on.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the available rating options to indicate your level of satisfaction with the product.
* **Form 1** (`form_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your feedback about the product.
* **Form 2** (`form_2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your feedback about the product.
* **Form 3** (`form_3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your feedback about the product.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your feedback.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Choose a rating that accurately reflects your level of satisfaction with the product.
