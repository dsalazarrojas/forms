# CFAC Fundraiser - Help Guide

## Purpose
The CFAC Fundraiser form is designed to collect donations for the Children & Family Academy of Canada (CFAC). This form allows donors to contribute to the organization's mission to support children, families, and communities through various programs and services. By completing this form, you can choose how you want to donate, including the amount, frequency, and method of payment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the CFAC Fundraiser form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by reading the introductory note, which explains the purpose and impact of your donation.
2.  Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These include:
    *   **Donor Full Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be listed.
    *   **Email Address**: Enter the email address to which the donation receipt will be sent.
    *   **Donation Amount**: Select a suggested amount or choose "Other Amount" to enter your own amount.
    *   **Direct my donation to**: Select the area of focus for your donation.
    *   **Donation Frequency**: Choose whether your donation is a one-time gift, monthly recurring, or annual gift.
    *   **Date**: Enter the date of your donation.
    *   **Preferred Payment Method**: Select how you want to pay for your donation.
    *   **Mailing Address**: Enter your mailing address for tax records.
    *   **How did you hear about this fundraiser?**: Optional field to share how you discovered this opportunity.
3.  For non-required fields, you can choose to leave them blank or fill them in if desired:
    *   **Other Amount (USD)**: If you select "Other Amount" above, use this field to enter the amount.
    *   **This gift is in honor/memory of**: Leave blank if not applicable.
    *   **Notification Email for Dedications**: Leave blank if not applicable.
    *   **I wish to remain anonymous**: Choose this option if you prefer not to be listed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   *   **Donor Full Name** (Donor Full Name, Text, \*): Enter your full name as you would like it to be listed.
    *   **Email Address** (Email Address, Email, \*): This is where the donation receipt will be sent.
    *   *   **Donation Amount** (Donation Amount Selection, Select One, \*): Choose a suggested amount or enter your own amount.
    *   *   **Other Amount (USD)** (Other Amount, Number, ): If you select "Other Amount" above, use this field to enter the amount.
    *   *   **Direct my donation to** (Fund Designation, Select One, \*): Select the area of focus for your donation.
    *   *   **Donation Frequency** (Donation Frequency, Select One, \*): Choose whether your donation is a one-time gift, monthly recurring, or annual gift.
    *   *   **This gift is in honor/memory of** (Dedication Info, Text, ): This is for donations made in honor or memory of someone.
    *   *   **Notification Email for Dedication** (Notification Email, Email, ): We will notify them of your thoughtful gift (amount will be hidden).
    *   *   **Preferred Payment Method** (Payment Method, Select One, \*): Select how you want to pay for your donation.
    *   *   **Employer Match Check** (Employer Match Check, Select One, \*): If your employer matches donations, choose this option.
    *   *   **I wish to remain anonymous** (Anonymous Donor Request, Select One, \*): Choose this option if you prefer not to be listed.
    *   *   **Mailing Address** (Mailing Address, Text, \*): Enter your mailing address for tax records.
    *   *   **How did you hear about this fundraiser?** (Fundraiser Referral Source, Select One, ): Optional field to share how you discovered this opportunity.
    *   *   **Date** (Donation Submission Date, Date, \*): Enter the date of your donation.

## Tips

*   Please ensure you complete all required fields.
*   For non-required fields, only fill in the information you want to share.
*   If you select "Other Amount" for your donation, enter the amount in the "Other Amount (USD)" field.
*   If you choose to be anonymous, we will not list your name in our annual report.
*   If you are unsure about any field, you can always contact us for assistance.
