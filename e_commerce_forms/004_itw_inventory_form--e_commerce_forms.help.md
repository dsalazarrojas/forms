# ITW Inventory Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The ITW Inventory Form is a tool for staff members to update or add new product information in the ecommerce application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (if any) and select from options where applicable.
2. Enter a product title in the **Product Title** field. This is a brief title of the product.
3. Enter the **Price** of the product if you know it.
4. Enter the **Quantity** of the product in stock.
5. Provide a **Description** of the product.
6. Select the **Stock Status** of the product: either "in stock" or "out of stock".
7. Enter the name of the **Supplier** if applicable.
8. Select the **Categories** of the product from the available options.
9. Add any **Tags** related to the product.
10. Optionally, add an **Image** of the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Title** (`product_title`, text, required): Enter the product's name or title.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the product if you know it.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the product in stock.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the product.
* **Stock Status** (`stock_status`, select_one, optional): Select "in stock" or "out of stock" for the product's availability.
* **Supplier** (`supplier`, text, optional): Enter the name of the supplier if applicable.
* **Categories** (`categories`, select_multiple, optional): Select the categories that best fit the product.
* **Tags** (`tags`, text, optional): Add any relevant tags for the product.
* **Image** (`image`, note, optional): Optionally, add an image of the product.

## Tips
* Be accurate with product information to ensure up-to-date and consistent inventory tracking.
* If you're unsure about any field, try to fill it in as best as you can, or leave it blank and ask for help when needed.
* The form is designed for ease and clarity, so fill it out at your own pace and don't hesitate to ask if you have questions.
