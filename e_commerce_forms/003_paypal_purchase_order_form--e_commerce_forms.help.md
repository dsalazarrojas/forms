# PayPal Purchase Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The PayPal Purchase Order Form is designed for online transactions or e-commerce platforms, allowing users to purchase multiple products with ease. This form collects necessary information for a smooth and secure payment process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the required fields and their purpose.
2. Select the products you wish to purchase and enter their respective prices and quantities.
3. Provide your shipping address, city, and zip code to ensure accurate delivery.
4. Enter your PayPal email address for payment verification.
5. Choose the currency for the transaction.
6. Review and submit the form to complete the payment process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Information**: This section is for selecting and entering details about the products you wish to purchase.
	+ **Product 1 Price**: Enter the price of the product you wish to purchase.
	+ **Product 1 Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the product you wish to purchase.
* **Shipping Information**: Enter your shipping address, city, and zip code for accurate delivery.
	+ **Shipping Address 1**: Enter your shipping address.
	+ **Shipping Address 2**: Enter any additional shipping information.
	+ **City**: Enter the city for shipping.
	+ **State**: Enter the state for shipping.
	+ **Zip**: Enter your zip code for shipping.
* **Payment Settings**: Set up your payment settings for the transaction.
	+ **PayPal Email**: Enter your PayPal email address for payment verification.
	+ **Currency**: Choose the currency for the transaction (e.g., USD, EUR, CNY).
	+ **PayPal Total**: Enter the total cost of the transaction.
	+ **PayPal Shipping Cost**: Enter any shipping costs associated with the transaction.
	+ **PayPal Handling Cost**: Enter any handling costs associated with the transaction.
	+ **PayPal Tax**: Enter any tax associated with the transaction.
	+ **PayPal Discount**: Enter any discount applied to the transaction.
	+ **PayPal Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the transaction.
* **Submit**: Click submit to complete the payment process.
* **PayPal Submit**: Confirm your payment submission.
* **PayPal Failure**: This field is likely used to track payment failures or issues. Please do not fill in this field.
