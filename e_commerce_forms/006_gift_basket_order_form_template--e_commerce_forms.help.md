# Gift Basket Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gift Basket Order Form Template is designed to help users create a gift basket order template for a gift-giving occasion. It allows users to select one or multiple products, provide gift details, contact information, payment information, and delivery details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the products you want to include in your gift basket on the "Select Product" page.
2. On the "Gift Details" page, choose whether the gift is a gift card or not.
3. On the "Contact Info" page, provide your contact information.
4. On the "Payment Info" page, enter your payment details.
5. On the "Delivery" page, choose the delivery option and enter any additional delivery details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Select Product
* **Select Product**: This field allows you to select one or multiple products to include in your gift basket.
  * You can select up to 8 options.
  * Each option represents a different product.
  * Make sure to select the products you want to include in your gift basket.

### Gift Details
* **Gift Details**: Choose whether the gift is a gift card or not.
  * You can choose between 'Yes' or 'No'.
  * This option will determine whether the gift is a physical item or a digital gift card.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information.
  * This field is for your convenience and will be used to contact you regarding your order.
  * You can enter any relevant contact information, such as your email address or phone number.

### Payment Info
* **Payment Info**: Enter your payment information.
  * This field is where you enter your payment details, such as credit card information or bank account details.
  * Make sure to enter accurate payment information to avoid any issues with your order.

### Gift Card
* **Gift Card**: This field is not currently used in the form, but you can enter any additional gift card details if needed.
  * This field is optional and only appears if you selected 'Yes' on the Gift Details page.
  * You can enter any additional details about the gift card, such as the card value or card type.

### Delivery
* **Delivery**: Choose the delivery option for your gift.
  * You can choose between up to 3 options.
  * Each option represents a different delivery method.
  * Make sure to choose the delivery option that best suits your needs.

### Delivery Details
* **Delivery Details**: Enter any additional delivery details.
  * This field is for your convenience and will be used to provide additional delivery information.
  * You can enter any relevant delivery details, such as delivery address or delivery instructions.
