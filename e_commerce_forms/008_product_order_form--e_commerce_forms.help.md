# Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Order Form is a tool for customers to provide their order details to place orders. This form collects customer, shipping, and payment information, which is then submitted to the server for processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Enter your product details in the "order form" field.
2.  Fill in your customer information in the "customer info" field.
3.  Provide your shipping information in the "shipping info" field.
4.  Leave any additional feedback in the "review" field.
5.  Select your preferred shipping method in the "shipping method" dropdown.
6.  Enter your payment information in the "payment info" field.
7.  Add any additional notes about your order in the "order note" field.
8.  Enter your email address in the "email" field.
9.  Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **order form (order form, text, optional)**: Enter a brief description of the product or service you are trying to purchase.
*   **customer info (customer info, text, optional)**: Enter your name, company name, or any other relevant information about the customer.
*   **shipping info (shipping info, text, optional)**: Enter your address, city, state, and zip code for shipping purposes.
*   **review (review, text, optional)**: Leave any feedback or comments about your order.
*   **shipping method (shipping method, select_one, optional)**: Choose the shipping method that suits your needs. This could be "Express", "Standard", or "Free Shipping".
*   **payment info (payment info, text, optional)**: Enter your payment details such as card number, expiration date, and security code.
*   **order note (order note, note, optional)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
*   **email (email, email, optional)**: Enter your email address for order updates and notifications.
*   **phone (phone, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number for any further communication.

## Tips

*   Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure smooth order processing.
*   Check for any typos or errors before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or issues with your order, feel free to contact us.
