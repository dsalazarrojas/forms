# e_commerce_partner_training_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from e-commerce partners who want to participate in training sessions. It captures basic contact information and preferences for training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your Participant Name in the Participant Name field.
2. Enter your Company Name in the Company Name field.
3. Enter your Email in the Email field. Please ensure the email address is valid.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the Phone Number field.
5. Select a Date for the training sessions in the Date field.
6. Select a Time for the training sessions in the Time field.
7. Choose your preferred Training Sessions from the dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (text, required: false)
Enter your name as it is to be displayed in our records and communication. Please keep it concise and professional.
* **Company Name** (text, required: false)
Enter the name of your company as it will help us understand your affiliation and organization. Keep it consistent with your company's branding.
* **Email** (email, required: false)
Enter your valid email address where we can reach you for any updates and training materials.
* **Phone Number** (text, required: false)
Enter your phone number where we can contact you for any urgent matters.
* **Date** (date, required: false)
Select a date for the training sessions. Please choose a date that suits your schedule and preference.
* **Time** (time, required: false)
Select a time for the training sessions. Please ensure the time you select aligns with our available training schedules.
* **Training Sessions** (select_multiple, required: false)
Choose the Training Sessions you would like to attend from the dropdown list.
