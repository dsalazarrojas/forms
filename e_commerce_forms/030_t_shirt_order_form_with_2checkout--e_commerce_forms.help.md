# T Shirt Order Form With 2Checkout - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to place a t-shirt order with 2Checkout. Please follow the steps below to complete your order.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the size of t-shirt you would like.
2. Choose the color of t-shirt you prefer.
3. Enter your desired design for your t-shirt.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number (optional).
6. Enter your first name.
7. Enter your last name.
8. Enter your address.
9. Enter your city.
10. Enter your state.
11. Enter your zip code.
12. Select your country (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What size of t-shirt would you like?** (`tshirt_size`, select_one, **required**)
  This field allows you to select the size of t-shirt you prefer.
* **What color of t-shirt would you like?** (`tshirt_color`, select_one, **required**)
  This field allows you to choose the color of t-shirt you prefer.
* **What design do you want on your t-shirt?** (`tshirt_design`, text, **required**)
  This field allows you to enter the desired design for your t-shirt.
* **Your email address** (`email`, email, **required**)
  Enter your valid email address.
* **Your phone number** (`phone`, text, **optional**)
  Enter your phone number (if you would like to be contacted).
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, **required**)
  Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, **required**)
  Enter your last name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, **required**)
  Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, **required**)
  Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, **required**)
  Enter your state.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, **required**)
  Enter your zip code.
* **Country** (`country`, text, **optional**)
  Enter your country of residence.
