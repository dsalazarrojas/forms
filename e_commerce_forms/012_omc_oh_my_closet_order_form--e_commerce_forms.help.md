# OmC! (Oh My Closet!) ORDER FORM - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect shipping information from users to place an order with OmC! (Oh My Closet!). 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (none are required in this form).
2. Provide your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
3. Enter your city in the "City" field.
4. Enter your state in the "State" field.
5. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. If you have any special requests or notes, use the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address, including your street address or PO box.
* **City**: Enter the city where you would like to have your order shipped to.
* **State**: Enter the state where you would like to have your order shipped to.
* **Zip**: Enter your 5- or 6-digit zip code.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, including any area code or country code.
* **Note**: If you have any special requests or notes, enter them here. You can include any additional information to help us understand your order better.
