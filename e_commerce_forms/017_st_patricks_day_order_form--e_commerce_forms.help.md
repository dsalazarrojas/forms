**St. Patricks Day Order Form - Help Guide**
=====================================

## Purpose
The St. Patricks Day Order Form is used to collect information for orders placed on this holiday. It is designed to gather customer information, order details, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Customer Information** section with your details.
2. Provide order details, such as the **Order Number**, **Total Price**, **Subtotal**, **Tax Rate**, and **Discount**.
3. Choose your **Payment Method** from the available options.
4. If you are choosing **Mail** as your delivery option, enter your **Delivery Address**.
5. If you are choosing **Credit Card** as your payment method, enter your **Email** and **Phone Number**.
6. Add any additional **Customer Comments** you'd like to include with your order.
7. Review all the information you've entered to ensure it is accurate.
8. Click submit to complete your order.

* **St. Patricks Day Form** (`st_patricks_day_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your order.
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant customer details.
