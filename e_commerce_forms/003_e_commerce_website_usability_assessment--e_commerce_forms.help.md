# E Commerce Website Usability Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to help assess the usability of an e-commerce website, ensuring that it meets the necessary standards for a smooth and efficient user experience. It evaluates the performance of different pages and elements across various devices, allowing for data-driven decisions to be made for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the pages you want to assess, choosing from "Site Experience Across Devices", "Navigation Menu", "Search Box", "Contact Us Form", or any other relevant page.
2. For the "Navigation Menu" page, choose the type of menu you think would be most beneficial for the website.
3. For the "Contact Us Form" page, choose the options that best fit your assessment.
4. Complete each field according to your evaluation, using the available options or providing a custom response if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Experience Across Devices (Mobile Devices, Desktop Computers, Tablets)**: Select the devices you want to assess the site experience on. This page is used to evaluate how well the website performs on different devices.
* **Navigation Menu (Main Menu, Submenu)**: Choose the type of menu that you think would be most beneficial for the website. This page is used to assess the effectiveness of the navigation menu.
* **Search Box (Search)**: Enter your evaluation of the search box, noting its performance and any issues you've encountered.
* **Search Box Happiness (Note)**: Use this field to note any issues or comments you have regarding the search box.
* **Search Box Suggestions (Autocomplete, Instant Search)**: Select the type of search suggestions you would like to see on the search box.
* **Contact Us Form (Form on Every Page, Form on Footer)**: Choose the location of the contact form that you think would be most beneficial for the website.
* **Contact Us Form Fields (Text, Number, Date)**: Select the type of field that you think would be most beneficial for the contact form.
* **Contact Us Form Fields 1-17**: Each of these fields are specific fields within the contact form. Select the option that best fits your assessment or provide a custom response if necessary.
* **Tips**
* Be as specific as possible when providing your assessment.
* Use the available options for fields where possible.
* If a field does not have an available option, please provide a custom response in the comments section.
* Take your time and be thorough when completing this form to ensure that your assessment is accurate and detailed.
