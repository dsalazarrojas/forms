# Final Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Final Payment Form is used to collect information from customers for a final payment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are filling out all required fields.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information.
3. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Final Payment Form 1** (`title`, `text`, required: false): This is the title of the form. You can enter the title of the final payment.
* **Final Payment** (`label`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the option for this field as it represents the nature of the final payment.
* **Amount** (`label`, `number`, required: true): Enter the amount of the final payment.
* **Tax Rate** (`label`, `number`, required: false): Enter the tax rate for the final payment.
* **Total** (`label`, `number`, required: true): This field is automatically calculated based on the amount and tax rate.
* **Payment Method** (`label`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the payment method for the final payment.
* **Notes** (`label`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments for the final payment.
* **Confirm Payment** (`label`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the option to confirm the payment.
* **Email** (`label`, `email`, required: true): Enter the email of the customer.
* **Phone** (`label`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Confirm Email** (`label`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the option to confirm the email.
* **Phone** (`label`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the option to confirm the phone.
* **Confirm Payment Method 2** (`label`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the option to confirm the payment method 2.
* **Payment Method 2** (`label`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the option for payment method 2.
* **Confirm Payment Method 3** (`label`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the option to confirm the payment method 3.
* **Payment Method 3** (`label`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the option for payment method 3.
* **Date** (`label`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Time** (`label`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the payment.
* **Confirm Time** (`label`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the option to confirm the time.
* **Address** (`label`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the customer.
* **Confirm Address** (`label`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the option to confirm the address.
* **Confirm Payment** (`label`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the option to confirm the payment.
* **Notes** (`label`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments for the payment.

## Tips
* Ensure you are using this form correctly to avoid any errors or confusion.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* This form should only be used for final payments.
