# Packing Cubes Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Packing Cubes Order Form is used to gather customer information and preferences for the packing cubes order process. This form helps us understand your needs and requirements for your packing cubes order, ensuring that we can provide you with the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the required information.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for your order.
3. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Customer Note" field if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, optional): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Order Cubes** (`id: 2`, `type: select_multiple`, optional): Select how many cubes you would like to order:
	+ 'Yes' to order cubes
	+ 'No' to not order cubes
* **Shipping Information** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, optional): Enter your shipping address for the order.
* **Customer Email** (`id: 4`, `type: email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Note** (`id: 6`, `type: note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We will review your order form submission and will be in touch with you soon.
