# Gadget Purchase Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gadget Purchase Payment Form is designed for customers to complete their purchase information for a gadget purchase. To use this form, simply follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields to complete your personal details.
2. Provide the payment information as required.
3. Review and confirm your payment details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address where the gadget will be delivered.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates on your purchase.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Amount Paid** (`amount_paid`, number, optional): Enter the exact amount paid for the gadget.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method you would like to use, such as Visa or Mastercard.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your payment.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Review and confirm your payment details before submitting the form.
