# Customer Registration Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer Registration Form Template is used to collect customer information for the creation of a new customer account on your site.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your Last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "email" field. Please ensure that you enter a valid email address.
4. (Optional) Enter your Company name in the "Company" field.
5. Enter your address on "Address 1" (e.g., Apartment or Street name).
6. Enter your address on "Address 2" (e.g., Apartment or Street number).
7. Enter your ZIP code in the "ZIP" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field (optional).
9. Enter your Password in the "Password" field. Please ensure that your password is at least 8 characters long.
10. Confirm your Password in the "Confirm Password" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`text`, required): Enter your first name as it is known to others.
* **Last name** (`text`, required): Enter your last name as it is known to others.
* **email** (`email`, required): Enter a valid email address that you can be reached at.
* **Company** (`text`, optional): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Address 1** (`text`, optional): Enter your address (e.g., Apartment or Street name).
* **Address 2** (`text`, optional): Enter your address (e.g., Apartment or Street number).
* **ZIP** (`number`, required): Enter your zip code.
* **phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Password** (`text`, required): Enter a password at least 8 characters long to ensure security.
* **Confirm Password** (`text`, required): Re-enter your password to confirm it is correct.

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure that your email and password are correct and valid.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us.
