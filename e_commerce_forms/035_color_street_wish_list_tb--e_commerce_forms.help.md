# Color Street Wish List TB! - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow customers to submit a wish list and order or add items from Color Street. The form is designed to collect essential information such as name, email, shipping address, payment method, and delivery option.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields at the top of the form, including your name, email, and shipping address.
2. In the "Wish List Items" section, enter the sets you would like to order or add to your wish list.
3. In the "Payment and Logistics" section, select your preferred payment method and delivery option.
4. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can include them in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (tb_name, text, required): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
- **Email** (tb_email, email, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
- **Shipping Address** (tb_address, text, required): Enter your shipping address where you would like the items to be delivered.
- **Wish List Items** (tb_sets_list, text, required): List the sets you would like to order or add to your wish list.
- **Total Sets** (tb_total_count, number, required): Enter the total number of sets you would like to order.
- **Is this for a Pre-Order?** (tb_is_preorder, select_one, required): Select 'True' if this is for a pre-order, 'False' if not.
- **How will you pay?** (tb_payment_type, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method (Venmo, PayPal, Facebook Pay).
- **Payment Handle** (tb_payment_handle, text, required): Enter your payment handle associated with the selected payment method.
- **Delivery Option** (tb_shipping_type, select_one, required): Select your preferred delivery option (Shipping ($4.00), TB Pick-up (Free)).
- **Hostess Name** (tb_hostess, text, optional): If this is for a hostess order, enter the hostess's name.
- **Additional Notes** (tb_notes, text, optional): Any additional notes or comments you would like to include.
