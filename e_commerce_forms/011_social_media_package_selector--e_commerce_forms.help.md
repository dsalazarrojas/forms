# Social Media Package Selector - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Package Selector form is designed to help you choose the best-fit social media package for your e-commerce business needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of social media platform you want to use from the options provided.
2. Choose the plan that suits your business needs from the options provided.
3. Enter your client's name, email, and phone number if necessary.
4. Provide a brief description of your goals and objectives for using the social media package.
5. Select your preferred payment method and confirm your payment amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Package Type**
`Package Type` (`package_type`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select the type of social media platform you want to use for your business, such as Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, or LinkedIn.

### 2. **Package Plan**
`Package Plan` (`package_plan`, `select_one`, required: false)
Choose the plan that suits your business needs from the options: Basic, Standard, or Premium.

### 3. **Client Name**
`Client Name` (`client_name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your client's name for reference.

### 4. **Email**
`Email` (`email`, `email`, required: false)
Enter your or your client's email address.

### 5. **Phone**
`Phone` (`phone`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your or your client's phone number.

### 6. **Goals**
`Goals` (`goals`, `text`, required: false)
Provide a brief description of your goals and objectives for using the social media package.

### 7. **Payment**
`Payment` (`payment`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select your preferred payment method from the options: Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.

### 8. **Confirm Payment**
`Confirm Payment` (`confirm_payment`, `select_one`, required: false)
Confirm your selected payment method.

### 9. **Payment Amount**
`Payment Amount` (`payment_amount`, `number`, required: false)
Enter the payment amount for your chosen package.

## Tips
- Be accurate when selecting your package type and plan to ensure you receive the correct recommendations.
- Ensure you provide your client's information accurately for efficient communication and service delivery.
- Confirm your payment method and amount to avoid misunderstandings. 

Note: The payment fields are designed to be completed after selecting the package type and plan. 

This guide will help you understand the Social Media Package Selector form and its fields, ensuring a smooth and successful completion process.
