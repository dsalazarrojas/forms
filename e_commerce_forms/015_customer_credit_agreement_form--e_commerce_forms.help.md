# Customer Credit Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Credit Agreement Form is a document used to document customer credit agreements, outlining terms and conditions, including credit amount and dates of agreement. It is intended to help businesses establish and manage customer credits in a clear and consistent manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to understand the fields and their purpose.
* Select the correct customer type (Individual, Business, or Other) in the `customer_info` field.
* Enter the credit amount in the `credit_amount` field, following the format of dollars and cents (e.g., 100.00).
* Choose the relevant credit terms from the `credit_terms` field.
* Select the date of the agreement in the `agreement_date` field.
* Sign the form with your signature in the `customer_signature` field.
* Add any additional notes or comments in the `customer_notes` field.
* Indicate the current credit status (Open, Closed, or Pending) in the `credit_status` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer info (customer_info)**: This field helps determine the type of customer you are entering information for (Individual, Business, or Other).
* **Credit Amount (credit_amount)**: Enter the credit amount in dollars and cents.
* **Credit Terms (credit_terms)**: Check the applicable credit terms to ensure you understand the customer's credit agreement.
* **Agreement Date (agreement_date)**: Select the date when the credit agreement was made.
* **Customer Signature (customer_signature)**: Sign the form to confirm your agreement with the customer.
* **Customer Notes (customer_notes)**: Add any additional comments or notes related to the customer's credit.
* **Credit Status (credit_status)**: Indicate the current status of the credit (Open, Closed, or Pending).
* **Form Footer (form_footer)**: This field is for additional comments or notes from the sales team or account manager.

## Tips
* Ensure you accurately fill out all required fields to ensure accurate documentation of the customer credit agreement.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are completed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to your sales or account management team for support.
