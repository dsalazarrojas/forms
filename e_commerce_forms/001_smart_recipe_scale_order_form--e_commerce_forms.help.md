# smart_recipe_scale_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for customers to enter their contact and order details when placing an order for a smart recipe scale.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information.
2. Enter your shipping details.
3. Enter your payment information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact number.
* **Shipping Details** (`shipping_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address and any additional shipping instructions.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your payment method and any additional payment instructions.
 
## Tips
- Double-check your contact, shipping, and payment information for accuracy.
- Make sure to provide clear and complete information in each field to ensure smooth order processing.
