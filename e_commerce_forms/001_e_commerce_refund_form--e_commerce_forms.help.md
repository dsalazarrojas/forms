# E Commerce Refund Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The E Commerce Refund Form is a necessary document for handling return and refund requests in e-commerce transactions. It helps customers submit their refund requests and provides essential information for processing and managing the refund process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with all required fields.
2.  Provide your order information, including order number, customer name, and order date.
3.  Describe the reason for your refund request.
4.  Enter the refund amount and date.
5.  Specify the refund status.
6.  Provide shipping information for both the original order and return items.
7.  Submit the form for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Refund Reason** (`refund_reason`, text): Provide a clear and concise explanation of why you are requesting a refund. This will help us understand your situation and process your request efficiently.
*   **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, number): Enter the exact amount you are requesting as a refund for the order.
*   **Refund Date** (`refund_date`, date): Select the date when the refund was received or should be processed.
*   **Refund Status** (`refund_status`, select_one): Choose the status of the refund request (Active or Inactive).
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note): Provide any additional comments or information to support your refund request.
*   **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
*   **Order Number** (`order_number`, text): Enter the number of the order for which you are requesting a refund.
*   **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text): Enter your name as it appears on your order.
*   **Order Date** (`order_date`, date): Select the date of the order for which you are requesting a refund.
*   **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one): Choose the status of the order (Active or Inactive).
*   **Return Reason** (`return_reason`, text): Provide a clear explanation of why you are returning the order.
*   **Return Notes** (`return_notes`, text): Provide any additional information to support your return request.
*   **Return Address** (`return_address`, text): Enter the address where the return item will be sent.
*   **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text): Enter the city where the order was shipped.
*   **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, text): Enter the state where the order was shipped.
*   **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`, text): Enter the zip code where the order was shipped.
*   **Shipping Country** (`shipping_country`, text): Enter the country where the order was shipped.

This form helps us process and manage your refund requests accurately and efficiently. Please fill it out carefully to avoid any delays in processing your request.
