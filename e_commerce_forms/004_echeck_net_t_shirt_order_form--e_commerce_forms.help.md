# echeck_net_t_shirt_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The echeck_net_t_shirt_order_form is designed to collect customer information and preferences for an e-check net t-shirt order. Fill out this form to let us know if you're interested in purchasing an e-check net t-shirt, your size preference, payment options, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Select the "Echeck Net" option that best suits your needs from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose your preferred t-shirt size from the options provided.
4. Select your preferred payment option from the options provided.
5. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
6. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to use a valid email address that you check regularly.
8. Enter your street address in the "Street" field.
9. Choose your state from the dropdown menu.
10. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
11. Click the "Submit" button when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Echeck Net** (Echeck Net, select_one, required/optional): Choose 'Yes' to purchase an e-check net t-shirt.
* **t_shirt_size** (t_shirt_size, select_one, required/optional): Select the size of the t-shirt you prefer.
* **Payment Option** (Payment Option, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the payment option that best suits your needs.
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Street** (Street, text, required/optional): Enter your street address.
* **State** (State, select_one, required/optional): Choose your state from the dropdown menu.
* **Zip** (Zip, text, required/optional): Enter your zip code.
 
## Tips
* Ensure you fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Double-check your email address for correctness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us through the provided email address.
