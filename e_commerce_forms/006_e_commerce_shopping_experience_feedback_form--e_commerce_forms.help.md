# Ecommerce Shopping Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Ecommerce Shopping Experience Feedback Form is a tool to gather feedback from customers on their recent ecommerce purchase. It consists of four main fields, each focused on different aspects of their shopping experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, simply click on the "Start" button to begin.
2. Answer each of the rating questions truthfully to the best of your ability.
3. Provide any additional feedback you may have in the "Feedback Form" field.
4. Once you've completed the form, click on the "Done" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rate The Product** (`rating_product`, number, required: false): Rate the product you purchased, using a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Rate The Shipping** (`rating_shipping`, number, required: false): Rate the shipping experience, using a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Rate The Payment** (`rating_payment`, number, required: false): Rate the payment process, using a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Feedback Form** (`feedback_form`, note, required: false): Provide any additional feedback you may have about your shopping experience. This is an open-ended field for you to express your thoughts and suggestions.
* **Start** (`form_start_screen`, text, required: false): Begin the form by clicking on this button.
* **Done** (`form_end_screen`, text, required: false): Submit your feedback by clicking on this button.
