# Blank T Shirt Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for customers to order custom T-shirts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details on the first page, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your shipping address on the first page.
3. On the second page, choose your T-shirt style, size, and quantity.
4. Select the material type and design option for your T-shirt.
5. Choose the print location for your design.
6. If you have any special requests or notes, provide them on this page.
7. Select your preferred delivery timeframe.
8. Enter the estimated total price for your order (if known).
9. Choose your payment method.
10. Enter the order date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to contact you about your order.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter your shipping address to ensure accurate delivery.
* **T-Shirt Color** (`shirt_color`, select_one, required): Choose the color of your T-shirt from the available options.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, select_one, required): Choose the size of your T-shirt from the available options.
* **Quantity** (`shirt_quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of T-shirts you want to order.
* **Preferred Material** (`material_type`, select_one, required): Choose the material type for your T-shirt from the available options.
* **Design Option** (`design_option`, select_one, required): Choose the design option for your T-shirt from the available options.
* **Print Location** (`print_location`, select_one, optional): Choose the location to print your design on your T-shirt.
* **Special Requests or Notes** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or notes for your order.
* **Preferred Delivery Timeframe** (`delivery_timeframe`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred delivery timeframe from the available options.
* **Estimated Total Price** (`total_price`, number, optional): Enter the estimated total price for your order (if known).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your payment method from the available options.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your order.

Note: Please ensure you fill in accurate and complete information to ensure proper order fulfillment. If you have any questions or need assistance, contact your representative.
