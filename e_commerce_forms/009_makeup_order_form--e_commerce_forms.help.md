# Makeup Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Makeup Order Form is designed to collect information for online orders of makeup products from customers. This form helps us understand the customer's preferences and details for processing their orders efficiently. It is recommended to fill out this form carefully, ensuring that all required fields are completed.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name** in the "customer-name" field.
2. Provide your **Shipping Address** in the "shipping-address" field.
3. Select your preferred **Payment Method** from the options provided.
4. Choose the **Products** you wish to order from the list of options.
5. Enter the quantity of each **Order Product** you wish to purchase.
6. Enter the price for each **Order Product**.
7. Add any additional **Order Comments** if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (customer-name)**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on your order receipt.
* **Shipping Address (shipping-address)**: Enter your address where you would like your order to be shipped.
* **Payment Method (payment-method)**: Select your payment method for this order. Please note that this field is required.
* **Order Products (order-products)**: Select the products you would like to purchase. This field is a multiple-select field, so you can choose more than one product.
*   - For each selected product:
    *   **Order Quantity (order-quantity)**: Enter the quantity of the product you wish to purchase.
    *   **Order Price (order-price)**: Enter the price for the product you are ordering, if different from the listed price.
*   - **Order Comments (order-comments)**: Enter any additional comments or instructions for your order if necessary.
