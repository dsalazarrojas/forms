# Baby Registry Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents-to-be to create a personalized baby registry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the names of the parents-to-be.
2. Choose the expected due date.
3. Select if the baby's gender is a boy, a girl, or if you're waiting to find out.
4. Select a nursery theme or color palette.
5. Choose the clothing sizes needed for the baby.
6. Select the diapering preferences (diapering option: disposable, cloth, or both).
7. List the top 5 most needed items.
8. Enter the shipping address for the gifts.
9. Select the registry visibility (public or private).
10. Add any other registry links (e.g., Amazon, Target).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Names of Parents-to-be** (`parent_names`, `text`, required): Please enter the names of the parents-to-be.
* **Expected Due Date** (`expected_due_date`, `date`, required): Enter the expected due date of the baby.
* **Is the baby's gender a surprise?** (`baby_gender_info`, `select_one`, required): Choose the baby's gender from the options: Boy, Girl, or We're waiting to find out!
* **Nursery Theme or Color Palette** (`nursery_theme`, `text`, optional): Select a theme or color palette for the nursery (e.g., Woodland, Stars, Pastels).
* **Clothing Sizes Needed** (`clothing_sizes`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the clothing sizes needed for the baby (Newborn, 0-3 Months, 3-6 Months, 6-9 Months, 9-12 Months, 12 Months +).
* **Diapering Preferences** (`diaper_preferences`, `select_one`, optional): Select the diapering preference (Disposable, Cloth, Open to both).
* **Top 5 Most Needed Items** (`essential_items_list`, `text`, required): List the top 5 most needed items.
* **Shipping Address for Gifts** (`shipping_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the shipping address for the gifts.
* **Registry Visibility** (`registry_public_visibility`, `select_one`, required): Select the registry visibility (Public, Private).
* **Other Registry Links** (`additional_registry_links`, `text`, optional): Add any other registry links (e.g., Amazon, Target).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that friends and family can easily find the perfect gifts for the new baby.
