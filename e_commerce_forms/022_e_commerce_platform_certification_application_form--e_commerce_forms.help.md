# e_commerce_platform_certification_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to apply for certification of your e-commerce platform. It is essential to provide accurate and detailed information about your platform to ensure a smooth certification process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as "true" in the form.
2. For the select fields (e.g., Platform Type), choose the most accurate option from the list.
3. For the text fields, provide a brief but detailed description of your platform.
4. Ensure that the email address you provide is valid and active.
5. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Platform Name (<platform_name> - Required)
Enter the name of your e-commerce platform.
### Platform Url (<platform_url> - Optional)
Enter the URL of your platform.
### Platform Type (<platform_type> - Optional)
Choose the type of your platform:
* B2B: Business-to-Business
* B2C: Business-to-Consumer
* C2C: Consumer-to-Consumer
### Platform Logo (<platform_logo> - Optional)
Enter a brief description of your platform's logo.
### Business Name (<business_name> - Required)
Enter the name of your business/organization.
### Address (<address> - Required)
Enter your business/organization's address.
### Contact Name (<contact_name> - Required)
Enter the name of the person to contact.
### Email (<email> - Required)
Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
### Phone (<phone> - Required)
Enter the contact phone number.
### Date of Birth (<date_of_birth> - Required)
Enter the date of birth of the person to contact.
### Certification Level (<certification_level> - Optional)
Choose the level of certification:
* Level 1
* Level 2
* Level 3
### Platform Info (<platform_info> - Optional)
Provide a brief description of your platform.
### Certification Status (<certification_status> - Required)
Indicate the current status of your certification application.
### Certification Notes (<certification_notes> - Optional)
Add any additional comments or notes about your certification.
### Additional Info (<additional_info> - Optional)
Add any additional information about your platform.

## Tips
* Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Double-check your answers for spelling and formatting errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the platform administrators.
* The certification process may take some time, please be patient and follow up on your application status.
* If you are re-submitting an updated version of your application, please make sure to note the changes made.
