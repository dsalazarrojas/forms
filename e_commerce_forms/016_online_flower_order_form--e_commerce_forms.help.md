# online_flower_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order flowers online, providing detailed information about the order, including flower type, color, and other specifications.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of flower you would like to order.
2. Choose the color of the flower(s).
3. Enter the name of the bouquet or arrangement you would like to create.
4. Specify the quantity of flowers you would like to order.
5. Enter the date you would like to have the flowers delivered.
6. Select the time you would like to have the flowers delivered.
7. Enter your name.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your phone number.
10. If you have any additional message or note, you can enter it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Flower Type**: The type of flower you would like to order. For example, roses, lilies, or sunflowers.
* **Flower Color**: The color you would like the flowers to be. Please select one of the options provided.
* **Bunch name**: Enter the name of the bouquet or arrangement you would like to create.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of flowers you would like to order.
* **Delivery date**: Enter the date you would like to have the flowers delivered.
* **Delivery time**: Select the time you would like the flowers to be delivered.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.
* **Message**: If you have any special requests or messages for the delivery person, you can enter it here.
* **Note**: Any other information you would like to add about your order.
