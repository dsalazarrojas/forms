# shop-contact-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a contact form for customers to provide their contact information and messages to the shop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter your message in the Message field.
6. Click on the "Submit" button to send your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to collect the customer's first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to collect the customer's last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): This field is used to collect the customer's email address. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to collect the customer's phone number.
* **Message** (`Message`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to collect the customer's message to the shop. Enter a clear and concise message.
