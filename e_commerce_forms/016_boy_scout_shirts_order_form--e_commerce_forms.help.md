# Boy Scout Shirts Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boy Scout Shirts Order Form is a tool for Boy Scout leaders to place orders for custom shirts for their troop. This form is used to collect relevant information about the troop, order details, and shipping information to ensure accurate processing and delivery of the ordered shirts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the troop number to identify your scouting troop.
2. Enter the name and contact information of the troop leader.
3. Choose the order date and requested delivery date for the shirts.
4. Select the shirt size, color, and quantity for each scout.
5. Choose whether to add embroidery to the shirt and select the text to be embroidered.
6. Enter the shipping address and choose the shipping method for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Troop Number** (`troop_number`, text, required): Enter the unique identification number of your scouting troop.
* **Troop Leader Name** (`troop_leader_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person for the troop.
* **Troop Leader Email** (`leader_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the troop leader for order communication.
* **Troop Leader Phone** (`leader_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the troop leader.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the date the order is being placed.
* **Requested Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Enter the date the shirts are needed by.
* **Scout Name** (`scout_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the scout placing the order.
* **Scout Rank** (`scout_rank`, select_one, required): Select the current rank level of the scout from the options provided.
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, select_one, required): Choose the correct size for the shirt from the available options.
* **Shirt Color** (`shirt_color`, select_one, required): Select the preferred color for the shirt.
* **Quantity of Shirts** (`shirt_quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of shirts ordered.
* **Shirt Embroidery Requested** (`embroidery_requested`, select_one, optional): Choose whether to add embroidery to the shirt.
* **Emroidery Text** (`embroidery_text`, text, optional): Enter the text to be embroidered on the shirt, if applicable.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter the full address for delivery of the order.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, required): Choose the shipping method, whether standard ground, expedited, overnight, or local pickup.
* **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, select_one, required): Check to confirm that the order is complete and accurate.
