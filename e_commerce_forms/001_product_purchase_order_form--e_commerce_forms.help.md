# product_purchase_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a new product purchase order. Fill out the form with the required information to place a new order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the product ID of the product being ordered.
3. Enter the customer's ID.
4. Select the order date and time.
5. Enter the product name (if required).
6. Enter the purchase price.
7. Enter the order quantity.
8. Provide the customer's address.
9. Enter the customer's email address.
10. Enter the customer's phone number.
11. Choose the order status (e.g., Pending, Processing, Shipped, Cancelled).
12. Select the payment method used (e.g., Credit Card, Bank Transfer, PayPal).
13. Add any additional notes about the order.
14. Choose the person responsible for this order (assigned to).
15. Select who submitted this order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **product_id**: Enter the ID of the product being ordered.
* **customer_id**: Enter the ID of the customer placing the order.
* **order_date**: Select the date when the order is being placed.
* **order_time**: Select the time when the order is being placed.
* **product_name**: Enter the name of the product being ordered (if required).
* **purchase_price**: Enter the price of the product.
* **order_quantity**: Enter the quantity of the product being ordered.
* **customer_address**: Enter the customer's address.
* **customer_email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **customer_phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **order_status**: Select the current status of the order (e.g., Pending, Processing, Shipped, Cancelled).
* **payment_method**: Select the payment method used (e.g., Credit Card, Bank Transfer, PayPal).
* **notes**: Add any additional notes about the order.
* **assigned_to**: Select the person responsible for this order.
* **submitted_by**: Select who submitted this order.
