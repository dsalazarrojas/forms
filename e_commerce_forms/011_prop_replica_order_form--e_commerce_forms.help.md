# Prop Replica Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information for replica orders, including shipping details, customer information, and order items. It is intended for use by authorized personnel in the replica department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the shipping address from the options provided.
2. Enter the shipping phone number.
3. Enter the shipping zip code.
4. Indicate whether or not the order includes order items.
5. Indicate whether or not payment is accepted.
6. Enter the total cost of the order.
7. Indicate whether or not customer information is included.
8. Select the shipping method.
9. Enter the shipping quantity.
10. Enter the shipping weight.
11. Enter the customer's name.
12. Enter the customer's email address.
13. Enter any additional notes for the customer.
14. Enter the form owner's name.
15. Enter the date and time the form was created.
16. Enter the date and time the form was last updated.
17. Enter the version of the form.
18. Select the category of the form.
19. Enter a unique identifier for the form.
20. Enter the user who created the form.
21. Enter the title of the form.
22. Enter a brief description of the form.
23. Enter any customer notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, optional): The main page of the form, where you can enter any additional information related to the replica order.
* **Shipping Address** (`id: 2`, `type: select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether or not shipping address is included in the order.
* **Shipping Phone** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, optional): Enter the phone number for the shipping address.
* **Shipping Zip** (`id: 4`, `type: number`, optional): Enter the zip code for the shipping address.
* **Order Items** (`id: 5`, `type: select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether or not order items are included in the order.
* **Payment Method** (`id: 6`, `type: select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether or not payment is accepted.
* **Total Cost** (`id: 7`, `type: number`, optional): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Customer Info** (`id: 8`, `type: select_multiple`, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether or not customer information is included.
* **Shipping Method** (`id: 9`, `type: select_one`, optional): Select the shipping method (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Shipping Quantity** (`id: 10`, `type: number`, optional): Enter the quantity of items being shipped.
* **Shipping Weight** (`id: 11`, `type: number`, optional): Enter the weight of the shipment.
* **Customer Name** (`id: 12`, `type: text`, optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`id: 13`, `type: email`, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Note** (`id: 14`, `type: note`, optional): Enter any additional notes for the customer.
* **Form Owner** (`id: 15`, `type: text`, optional): Enter the name of the form owner.
* **Created At** (`id: 16`, `type: date`, optional): Automatically generated timestamp for when the form was created.
* **Updated At** (`id: 17`, `type: date`, optional): Automatically generated timestamp for when the form was last updated.
* **Form Version** (`id: 18`, `type: number`, optional): Enter the version of the form.
* **Form Category** (`id: 19`, `type: text`, optional): Enter the category of the form.
* **Form Id** (`id: 20`, `type: number`, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the form.
* **User Created By** (`id: 21`, `type: text`, optional): Enter the user who created the form.
* **Form Title** (`id: 22`, `type: text`, optional): Enter a title for the form.
* **Form Description** (`id: 23`, `type: text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the form.
* **Customer Note** (`id: 24`, `type: text`, optional): Enter any additional notes from the customer.
* **Form Owner Email** (`id: 25`, `type: email`, optional): Enter the email address of the form owner.
