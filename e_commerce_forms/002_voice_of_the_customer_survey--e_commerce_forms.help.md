# Voice Of The Customer Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers to help us better understand their experiences and preferences. The information collected will be used to improve our services and tailor them to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `Customer Name` field.
2. Provide your email address in the `E-mail` field.
3. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
4. Enter your company name in the `Company` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (`customer_name`, text, optional)**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **E-mail (`email`, email, optional)**: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone (`phone`, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Company (`company`, text, optional)**: Enter the name of your company or organization.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure we can contact you effectively.
* If you have any feedback or comments, please provide them in the space provided.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [support email/phone number].
