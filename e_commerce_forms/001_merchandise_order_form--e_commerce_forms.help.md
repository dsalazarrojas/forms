# Merchandise Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create and manage merchandise orders. It collects information from customers and stores for merchandise orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the **Customer Info** section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter the **Merchandise Info** section with the item name, description, and image URL.
3. In the **Shipping Info** section, input your shipping name, address, city, state, zip, and country.
4. In the **Payment Info** section, select the order status (**Confirmed**, **Pending**, or **Cancelled**).
5. In the **Order Details** section, input the quantity, unit price, total price, date, time, and note (if necessary).
6. Finally, submit the form to complete the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Info
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name to help us identify you as the customer.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further contact and communication.
### Merchandise Info
* **Merchandise Name**: Enter the name of the merchandise you are ordering.
* **Merchandise Description**: Briefly describe the merchandise you are ordering.
* **Merchandise Image URL**: Enter the URL of the merchandise image.
### Shipping Info
* **Shipping Name**: Enter the name of the person shipping the merchandise.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the shipping address where the merchandise will be delivered.
* **Shipping City**: Enter the city where the shipping address is located.
* **Shipping State**: Enter the state where the shipping address is located.
* **Shipping Zip**: Enter the zip code of the shipping address.
* **Shipping Country**: Enter the country where the shipping address is located.
### Order Status
* **Order Confirmed, Order Pending, or Order Cancelled**: Select the status of the order.
### Order Details
* **Quantity**: Enter the amount of merchandise you are ordering.
* **Unit Price**: Enter the price of each item.
* **Total Price**: Enter the total price of the order.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the order.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the order.
* **Note**: Enter any additional information about the order.
### Payment Info
* This section is not applicable as it is not required in the provided form structure.

## Tips
* Double-check all the information before submitting the form.
* Make sure to enter correct information to avoid any issues with order processing.
* If you have any questions or need assistance with your order, contact us at [your email/phone number].
