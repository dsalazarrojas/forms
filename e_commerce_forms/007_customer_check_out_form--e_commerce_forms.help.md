# Customer Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Check Out Form is designed to collect customer information and payment details for online transactions. This form is meant to facilitate secure and accurate processing of customer orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer name in the `customer_name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `customer_email` field.
3. Enter your order number in the `order_number` field.
4. Enter the total value of your order in the `total_value` field.
5. Select your preferred payment method from the `select_payment_method` dropdown menu.
6. If you selected Credit Card as your payment method, fill in the `credit_card_details` field with your credit card information.
7. Enter the expiration date of your credit card in the `expiration_date` field.
8. Enter the amount you would like to pay in the `payment_amount` field.
9. Enter your billing address in the `billing_address` field.
10. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
11. Enter the name of the shipping recipient in the `shipping_name` field.
12. Enter the shipping address in the `shipping_address` field.
13. Enter the shipping city in the `shipping_city` field.
14. Enter the shipping country in the `shipping_country` field.
15. Enter the shipping postal code in the `shipping_postal_code` field.
16. If you want to add a note, you can enter it in the `customer_note`, `customer_note_2`, or `customer_note_3` field.
17. If you want to display terms and conditions, you can select them from the `terms_and_conditions`, `terms_and_conditions_2`, or `terms_and_conditions_3` field.
18. Finally, confirm your payment terms by entering them in the `payment_terms`, `payment_terms_2`, `payment_terms_3`, or `payment_terms_4` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, number, required): Enter the number of your order.
* **Total Value** (`total_value`, number, required): Enter the total value of your order.
* **Select Payment Method** (`select_payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose between Credit Card and PayPal as your payment method.
* **Credit Card Details** (`credit_card_details`, select_multiple, required): Enter your credit card information, including Card Type, Card Number, and Security Code.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, required): Enter the expiration date of your credit card.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you want to pay.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, required): Enter your billing address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Shipping Name** (`shipping_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the shipping recipient.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter the shipping address.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, required): Enter the city of the shipping address.
* **Shipping Country** (`shipping_country`, text, required): Enter the country of the shipping address.
* **Shipping Postal Code** (`shipping_postal_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code of the shipping address.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, text, optional): Enter a note for the customer.
* **Customer Note 2** (`customer_note_2`, text, optional): Enter another note for the customer.
* **Customer Note 3** (`customer_note_3`, text, optional): Enter a third note for the customer.
* **Terms and Conditions 1** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, optional): Review and accept the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions 2** (`terms_and_conditions_2`, note, optional): Review and accept additional terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions 3** (`terms_and_conditions_3`, note, optional): Review and accept further terms and conditions.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Enter payment terms.
* **Payment Terms 2** (`payment_terms_2`, text, optional): Enter more payment terms.
* **Payment Terms 3** (`payment_terms_3`, text, optional): Enter further payment terms.
* **Payment Terms 4** (`payment_terms_4`, text, optional): Enter additional payment terms.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth processing of your order.
* Be cautious when entering sensitive information, such as credit card details.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully to ensure you understand your payment obligations.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the customer service team.
