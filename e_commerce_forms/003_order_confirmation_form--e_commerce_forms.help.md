# Order Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Order Confirmation Form is a form used to confirm customer orders and shipping information. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best describe your order and shipping preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation_form`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can enter a brief description of your order. If you have any special requests or notes about your order, you can enter them here.
- **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, select_multiple, optional): This is a multiple-choice field where you can select whether or not you want to ship to your address. If you select "Yes", we will ship to your address. If you select "No", we will not ship to your address.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can enter any contact information you want us to have, such as your phone number or email address.
- **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can enter your shipping address.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can enter your contact number.
- **Confirm Order** (`confirm_order`, select_one, optional): This is a multiple-choice field where you can select whether or not you want to confirm your order. If you select "Yes", we will confirm your order.
- **Confirm Shipping** (`confirm_shipping`, select_multiple, optional): This is a multiple-choice field where you can select whether or not you want to ship your order. If you select "Yes", we will ship your order.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your order is processed correctly. Also, please select "Yes" or "No" for the confirm and shipping fields as these fields are crucial for us to process your order.
