# Shopping List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shopping List Form is a tool to record and manage shopping list items. It is intended for individuals to document their purchases, helping them to keep track of their shopping history and identify patterns. This form captures essential information such as item details, store names, and associated dates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the shopping_list_form page to start filling out the form.
2. Enter the item name in the **item_name** field. This is the name of the item you purchased.
3. Enter the price of the item in the **item_price** field.
4. Enter the quantity of the item in the **item_quantity** field.
5. Select the store from which you purchased the item using the **store_type** dropdown menu.
6. Optionally, enter any additional notes about the purchase in the **notes** field.
7. If you would like to contact you about this purchase, provide your email address in the **email** field.
8. If you would like to be contacted about this purchase, provide your phone number in the **phone** field.
9. Click on the **purchase_date** field to select the date of the purchase from a calendar.
10. Once you have filled out all the required and optional fields, click on the "Submit" button to save your shopping list entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **item_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the item you purchased.
* **item_price** (number, optional): Enter the price of the item.
* **item_quantity** (number, optional): Enter the quantity of the item.
* **store_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the store where you purchased the item.
* **purchase_date** (date, optional): Select the date of the purchase from the calendar.
* **store_type** (select_one, optional): Select the store from which you purchased the item.
* **notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the purchase.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address to be contacted about this purchase.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number to be contacted about this purchase.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete shopping list entry.
* Use the calendar to select the correct date of the purchase.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can always ask a form administrator for assistance.
* This form is designed to be used for personal, non-commercial purposes.
* If you have any further questions or need additional help, please don't hesitate to contact us.
