# Screen Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Screen Printing Order Form is used to gather customer and order information for screen printing services. This form is designed to ensure accurate and timely production of screen printed orders.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field. This will help us get your order started.
2. Enter your Email in the "Email" field. This will help us contact you for any order updates or questions.
3. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field. This will help us contact you quickly and efficiently.
4. Provide the Product Name you would like to order in the "Product Name" field. This is the name of the item to be printed.
5. Add any additional Order Details in the "Order Details" field. This is a good place to include any specific instructions or requests for your order.
6. Select your preferred Payment Method from the "Payment Method" dropdown. Options are "Cash" and "Credit/Debit Card".
7. Enter your Shipping Address in the "Shipping Address" field. This is where you'd like the order delivered to.
8. Enter your Billing Address in the "Billing Address" field. This is the address where the payment will be processed.
9. Enter the Total Amount due in the "Total Amount" field.
10. If this is a time-sensitive order, select the Date and Time in the "Date and Time" field.
11. You can add any additional notes in the "Notes" field. This is a good place to include any other relevant information.
12. If you have a specific tool assigned to your order, enter it in the "Assigned Tool" field.
13. Finally, confirm the Form ID in the "Form ID" field. This will help us track your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`user_input`, text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number here.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product to be printed here.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required): Add any additional details about your order here.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method here. Options are "Cash" and "Credit/Debit Card".
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter the address where you'd like the order delivered to.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, required): Enter the address where the payment will be processed.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, number, required): Enter the total amount due for your order.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, date, required): Select the date and time for your order if necessary.
* **Notes** (`note`, text, required): Add any additional notes about your order.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): If a specific tool is assigned to your order, enter it here.
* **Form ID** (`form_ids`, text, required): Confirm the Form ID for your order.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required): Confirm the category of your order.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Add a brief description of your order.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, required): Confirm the output file format for your order.
* **Sync Apps** (`sync_apps`, select_multiple, required): Select any apps to sync with your order. Options are "Google Sheets" and "Dropbox Paper".
* **Accept Payments** (`accept_payments`, text, required): Confirm if payments are accepted for this order.
* **Output Format** (`output_format`, text, required): Confirm the output format for your order.
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Confirm the title of this form.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, text, required): Confirm the category of this form.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, required): Add a brief description of this form.
* **Assigned Tool Name** (`assigned_tool_name`, text, required): Confirm the assigned tool name.
* **Assigned Tool ID** (`assigned_tool_id`, text, required): Confirm the assigned tool ID.

Please ensure to fill in all required fields and double-check your information before submitting this form. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
