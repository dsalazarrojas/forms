# food_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The food_order_form is designed to collect information for customers to place a food order. It is intended for customers to provide their details and any additional information for their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are a customer and have a valid reason for submitting this form.
2. Fill out all required fields correctly.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Food order customer name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Order type** (`order_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you want to receive your order: Delivery or Pickup.
* **Delivery address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your delivery address.
* **Order date** (`order_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date for your order.
* **Order time** (`order_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time for your order.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Order message** (`order_message`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional information for your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Use a valid email address and phone number.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant support team.
