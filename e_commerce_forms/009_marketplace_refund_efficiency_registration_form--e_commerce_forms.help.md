# Marketplace Refund Efficiency Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register information about a refund related to a purchase made through a marketplace. This will help increase the efficiency of the refund process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the first page, "Order Information", and fill in the details of your purchase, including any relevant order number.
2. On the second page, "Refund Reason", provide a clear and concise explanation for your refund request.
3. In the "Product Condition" field, select the current state of the product you are requesting a refund for.
4. Optionally, you can attach a product photo to provide further evidence of the product's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Information (Order Information, text, Optional)**: Enter the details of your purchase, including any relevant order number.
* **Refund Reason (Refund Reason, text, Optional)**: Explain the reason for your refund request in a clear and concise manner.
* **Product Condition (Product Condition, select_one, Optional)**: Select the current state of the product you are requesting a refund for.
	+ New: The product is in its original packaging and has never been used.
	+ Used: The product has been used but is still in good condition.
	+ Open Box: The product comes in an opened box or package.
	+ Opened Box: The product has been opened and is no longer in its original packaging.
	+ Damaged: The product has been damaged during shipping or handling.
	+ Other: Select this option if the product condition does not fit any of the above categories.
* **Product Photo (Product Photo, note, Optional)**: Attach a photo of the product to provide further evidence of its condition.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and honest information in all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the marketplace support for guidance.
* Do not register duplicate entries for the same order.
* Attach a clear and legible photo if possible.
