# State Football Championship T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to order custom T-shirts for a state football championship game.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your customer information in the **Customer Information** section.
2. Select the details of your T-shirt in the **Shirt Details** section, including size, color, and type.
3. Provide your shipping and payment information in the **Ship To** and **Payment Information** sections.
4. Enter the shipping cost and total cost of your order in the **Shipping Cost** and **Total Cost** sections.
5. Optionally, add any **Order Notes** you'd like to provide.
6. Enter your **Email** address so we can contact you about your order.
7. Enter your **Phone** number so we can contact you about your order.
8. Enter your **Address** so we can ship your T-shirt to you.
9. Enter the **Order Date** you want to receive your T-shirt.
10. Select the **Shirt Size** and **Shirt Color** of your T-shirt.
11. Choose the **T-Shirt Type** you prefer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (customer_info)**: Enter your name, address, and contact information to help us get in touch with you.
* **Shirt Details (shirt_details)**: Select the details of the T-shirt you want, including size, color, and type.
* **Ship To (shipping)**: Enter the address where you want to receive your T-shirt.
* **Payment Information (payment)**: Enter your payment method and information so we can complete your order.
* **Shipping Cost (shipping_cost)**: Enter the shipping cost of your order.
* **Total Cost (total_cost)**: Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Order Notes (order_notes)**: Add any additional information about your order if you'd like.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your order.
* **Address (address)**: Enter your shipping address so we can deliver your T-shirt.
* **Order Date (order_date)**: Enter the date you want to receive your T-shirt.
* **Shirt Size (shirt_size)**: Select the size of your T-shirt (Large, Medium, Small, X-Large).
* **Shirt Color (shirt_color)**: Select the color of your T-shirt (White, Black, Red, Blue).
* **T-Shirt Type (t_shirt_type)**: Select the type of T-shirt you prefer (Short Sleeve, Long Sleeve, Hoodie).
