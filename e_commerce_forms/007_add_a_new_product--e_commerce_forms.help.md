# Add A New Product - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to add a new product to the e-commerce system. It collects relevant information for product listing and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked as such in the form.
2. Select the correct category, shipping class, tax status, and product status from the respective dropdown menus.
3. Upload up to 5 product images as a comma-separated list in the Product Images field.
4. Ensure that all numerical fields (e.g., Price, Inventory Quantity) are filled with accurate values.
5. Review and adjust fields as needed, ensuring that all necessary information is provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (Product Name, text, required): Enter the official name of the product.
* **SKU (Stock Keeping Unit)** (SKU, text, required): Enter the unique identifier for inventory tracking.
* **Category** (Category, select_one, required): Select the primary category for the product from the dropdown menu.
* **Brand** (Brand, text, optional): Enter the manufacturer or brand name of the product.
* **Product Description** (Product Description, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the product.
* **Price** ($, number, required): Enter the regular selling price of the product.
* **Sale Price** (Sale Price, number, optional): Enter the discounted price if applicable.
* **Currency** (Currency, select_one, required): Select the currency code for pricing from the dropdown menu.
* **Inventory Quantity** (Inventory Quantity, number, required): Enter the number of units currently in stock.
* **Weight** (Weight (lbs), number, optional): Enter the product weight in pounds.
* **Length** (Length (inches), number, optional): Enter the product length in inches.
* **Width** (Width (inches), number, optional): Enter the product width in inches.
* **Height** (Height (inches), number, optional): Enter the product height in inches.
* **Product Images** (Product Images, text, optional): Upload up to 5 product images as a comma-separated list.
* **Tags** (Tags, text, optional): Enter comma-separated keywords for searchability.
* **Available Colors** (Available Colors, text, optional): List available colors separated by commas.
* **Available Sizes** (Available Sizes, text, optional): List available sizes separated by commas.
* **Shipping Class** (Shipping Class, select_one, required): Select the shipping class for the product from the dropdown menu.
* **Tax Status** (Tax Status, select_one, required): Select whether the product is taxable or not.
* **Product Status** (Product Status, select_one, required): Select the current status of the product from the dropdown menu.
* **Featured Product** (Featured Product, select_one, optional): Select whether the product should be featured on the homepage.
* **Supplier Name** (Supplier Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the product supplier.
* **Supplier Contact** (Supplier Contact, text, optional): Enter the contact information for the supplier.
