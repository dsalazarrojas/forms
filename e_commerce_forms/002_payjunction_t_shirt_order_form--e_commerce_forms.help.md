# PayJunction T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect order details from customers for t-shirts. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth and successful order experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the customer information section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter your shipping information, including your address, city, state, and zip code.
3. Provide payment details, such as the payment method and any required payment information.
4. Enter order specifics, including the shirt type, size, and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Customer Info (customer_info)** (`text`, `text`, optional)
This field is where you enter your name, email address, and phone number. Please ensure your email address is correct, as we will use it to communicate with you about your order.

### **Shipping Info (shipping_info)** (`text`, `text`, optional)
This field is where you enter your shipping address, city, state, and zip code. Please double-check your address for accuracy to avoid delays in shipping.

### **Payment Info (payment_info)** (`text`, `text`, optional)
This field is where you enter payment details, including the payment method and any required payment information. Please ensure you have the necessary funds for payment.

### **Order Info (order_info)** (`text`, `text`, optional)
This field is where you enter order specifics, including the t-shirt type, size, and any other relevant details. Please be as detailed as possible to ensure we can fulfill your order correctly.

## Tips
- Double-check your email address for accuracy.
- Ensure your shipping address is correct to avoid delays.
- Provide clear and concise payment information.
- Enter order details accurately to ensure correct fulfillment.
