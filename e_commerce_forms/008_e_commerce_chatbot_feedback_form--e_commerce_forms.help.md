# E Commerce Chatbot Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our E-commerce chatbot. Your input will help us improve the chatbot's performance and provide a better experience for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your **Customer Name** in the first field. This will help us identify you and ensure that your feedback is addressed correctly.
2. Provide your **Order ID** in the second field. This information will help us locate your order and resolve any issues related to it.
3. Rate your experience with the chatbot in the third field. This will help us understand the severity of the issue.
4. Describe your experience and any issues you've encountered with the chatbot in the fourth field.
5. If you're reporting an issue, select the type of issue in the fifth field. You can choose between "Technical", "Content", and "Design".
6. Describe the issue in detail in the sixth field. This will help us understand the root cause of the problem.
7. Let us know what you'd like us to do about the issue in the seventh field. You can choose to improve the chatbot, fix the issue immediately, or contact customer support.
8. Update the resolution status in the eighth field once the issue is resolved.
9. If the issue is resolved, enter the date and time it was resolved.
10. Provide any additional comments about the resolution.
11. Enter your **Customer Message** in the eleventh field. This is your opportunity to describe your experience and any suggestions you may have.
12. Rate your experience with the resolution in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Customer Name 
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. Order ID 
- **Order ID** (`order_id`, number, required): Enter your order number, if available.

### 3. Rating 
- **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your experience with the chatbot from 1-5.

### 4. How would you rate your experience? 
- **How would you rate your experience?** (`feedback`, text, required): Describe your experience and any issues you've encountered.

### 5. Issue Type 
- **Issue Type** (`issue_type`, select_one, required): Choose between "Technical", "Content", and "Design" to describe the type of issue you're experiencing.

### 6. Description 
- **Description** (`issue_description_long`, note, required): Describe the issue in detail.

### 7. Resolution 
- **Resolution** (`expected_resolution`, select_multiple, required): Choose what you'd like us to do about the issue. You can choose to improve the chatbot, fix the issue immediately, or contact customer support.

### 8. Resolution Status 
- **Resolution Status** (`resolution_status`, select_one, required): Update the resolution status once the issue is resolved.

### 9. Resolution Date 
- **Resolution Date** (`resolution_date`, date, required): Enter the date and time the issue was resolved.

### 10. Resolution Time 
- **Resolution Time** (`resolution_time`, time, required): Enter the time the issue was resolved.

### 11. Resolution Comment 
- **Resolution Comment** (`resolution_comment`, text, required): Provide any additional comments about the resolution.

### 12. Customer Message 
- **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, text, required): Enter your customer message and any suggestions you may have.

### 13. How would you rate this issue? 
- **How would you rate this issue?** (`feedback_rating`, number, required): Rate your experience with the resolution from 1-5.

### 14. Resolution Message 
- **Resolution Message** (`resolution_message`, text, required): Enter any additional comments about the resolution.
