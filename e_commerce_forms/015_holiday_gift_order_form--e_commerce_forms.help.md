# Holiday Gift Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a holiday gift order. It captures details about the gift, recipient, shipping method, shipping address, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Gift Details field if you wish to provide additional information about the gift.
2. Enter the Recipient's name and contact information in the Recipient field.
3. Select the desired Shipping Method from the options provided (First Class, Second Class, or Local Pickup).
4. If you wish, enter the shipping address in the Shipping Address field.
5. If you wish, provide billing information in the Billing Details field.
6. Enter payment information in the Payment Info field, if applicable.
7. Upload or enter an image of the gift in the Gift Image field.
8. Provide a description of the gift image in the Gift Image Description field, if applicable.
9. Enter the quantity of the gift in the Quantity field, if applicable.
10. Enter the price of the gift in the Price field, if applicable.
11. If you wish, add a message for the gift in the Gift Message field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gift Details**: This field is not required and provides a space for you to enter any additional information about the gift you are ordering.
* **Recipient**: This field is not required and is used to enter the name and contact information of the person receiving the gift.
* **Shipping Method**: This field is required and allows you to select the shipping method for the gift. Options include First Class, Second Class, and Local Pickup.
* **Shipping Address**: This field is not required and is used to enter the shipping address for the gift.
* **Billing Details**: This field is not required and is used to enter billing information for the gift.
* **Payment Info**: This field is not required and is used to enter payment information.
* **Gift Image**: This field is not required and is used to upload or enter an image of the gift.
* **Gift Image Description**: This field is not required and is used to enter a description of the gift image.
* **Gift Image**: This field is not required and seems to be redundant with Gift Image field.
* **Quantity**: This field is not required and is used to enter the quantity of the gift.
* **Price**: This field is not required and is used to enter the price of the gift.
* **Gift Message**: This field is not required and is used to add a message for the gift.
* **Shipping Address**: This field is not required and seems to be intended to be redundant with Gift Details field.

## Tips

* Ensure that you select a shipping method for the gift, as this is the only required field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact customer support for clarification.
* Ensure that you review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors.
