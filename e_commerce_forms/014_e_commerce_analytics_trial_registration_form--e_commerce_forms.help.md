# E Commerce Analytics Trial Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register customers interested in e-commerce analytics trials. It collects basic information to facilitate communication and further discussion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering your name in the **Customer Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Customer Email** field.
3. Provide your phone number in the **Customer Phone** field.
4. Select your location from the **Location** drop-down menu.
5. Choose the industries that relate to your business from the **Industry** multi-select menu.
6. If you have any additional comments or questions, type them in the **Comments** field.
7. Finally, enter any additional information in the **Form Footer** field (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (1): Enter a brief title for your registration.
* **Customer Name** (2): Please enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Customer Email** (3): Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Customer Phone** (4): Enter your phone number, including area code and any extension.
* **Location** (5): Select your country, location, or region where your business is based.
* **Industry** (6): Choose the industries that relate to your business (e.g., Retail, Finance, Healthcare, etc.).
* **Comments** (7): Add any additional comments or questions you have about e-commerce analytics trials.
* **Form Footer** (8): Enter any additional information or instructions, if necessary.
