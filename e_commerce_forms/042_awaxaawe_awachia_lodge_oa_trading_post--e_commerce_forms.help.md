# Awaxaawe Awachia Lodge OA Trading Post - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Awaxaawe Awachia Lodge OA Trading Post form is designed to collect information from customers for their orders, including their contact and lodge information, patch and insignia orders, apparel and gear orders, fulfillment and payment preferences, and other order details. This form ensures that all required information is collected for efficient order processing and fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Customer Full Name** field with the customer's full name as it appears on their identification.
2. Enter the customer's **Email Address** in the format of `email@example.com`.
3. Provide the customer's **Phone Number** in the format of `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
4. Enter the customer's **Lodge Member ID**, a unique identifier for membership verification.
5. Select the **Patch Type** from the list:
	* Lodge Flap (Standard)
	* CSP (Council Shoulder Patch)
	* Event Patch (Limited Edition)
	* No Patch Order
6. If a patch is ordered, specify the **Quantity of Patches**.
7. Select the **Apparel Selection** from the list:
	* T-shirt (Lodge Logo)
	* Hoodie (Embroidered)
	* Cap/Hat (One Size)
	* No Apparel Order
8. If apparel is ordered, select the **Apparel Size** from the list:
	* Small (S)
	* Medium (M)
	* Large (L)
	* Extra Large (XL)
	* Double Extra Large (XXL)
9. Select the **Accessory Selection** from the list:
	* Coffee Mug (Ceramic)
	* Vinyl Sticker (Pack)
	* Collector Pin
	* Challenge Coin
	* No Accessory Order
10. Choose the **Fulfillment Preference** from the list:
	* Pickup at Lodge Meeting
	* Pickup at Scout Office
	* Standard Shipping (to address)
11. If shipping is selected, provide the **Shipping Address** in the format of `Street Address, City, State, ZIP Code`.
12. Enter the **Order Total Amount** in USD.
13. Select the **Preferred Payment Method** from the list:
	* Credit/Debit Card
	* PayPal Account
	* Personal Check (to Lodge)
14. Finally, select **I agree to all Lodge Trading Post Policies** to confirm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name**: Enter the customer's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter the customer's email address in the format of `email@example.com`.
* **Phone Number**: Provide the customer's phone number in the format of `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
* **Lodge Member ID**: Enter the customer's unique Lodge Member ID for membership verification.
* **Patch Type Selection**: Select the primary patch being ordered from the list.
* **Quantity of Patches**: If a patch is ordered, specify the quantity.
* **Apparel Selection**: Select the clothing item being ordered from the list.
* **Apparel Size**: If apparel is ordered, select the size from the list.
* **Accessory Selection**: Select the accessory item being ordered from the list.
* **Fulfillment Preference**: Choose how the order will be fulfilled from the list.
* **Shipping Address**: If shipping is selected, provide the full address in the format of `Street Address, City, State, ZIP Code`.
* **Order Total Amount**: Enter the total amount of the order in USD.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Select the payment method from the list.
* **I agree to all Lodge Trading Post Policies**: Confirm agreement to all policies.
