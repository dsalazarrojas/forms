# Profile Builder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create or update user profiles for e-commerce purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have completed all required fields before proceeding.
2. Fill in the required fields first:
	* `What's your name?`
	* `Are you a team member?` (Yes/No)
	* `What is your email address?`
	* `Is this a public profile?` (Yes/No)
	* `Are you a team member?` (Yes/No)
	* `Is checkbox 1` checked (Yes/No)
3. The remaining fields are optional and can be filled in if you wish.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What's your name?**: A field to input your full name.
* **Are you a team member?**: A select one field asking whether you are a team member or not.
* **What is your email address?**: A field to input your email address for contact purposes.
* **Is this a public profile?**: A select one field asking whether your profile is public or not.
* **Are you a team member?** (again): A select one field asking whether you are a team member or not.
* **Is checkbox 1 checked?**: A checkbox field asking whether you choose Yes or No.
* **What is your favorite color?** (profile_field_1): A field to input your favorite color.
* **What is your age?** (profile_field_2): A field to input your age.
* **What is your height?** (profile_field_3): A field to input your height in inches.
* **What is your date of birth?** (profile_field_4): A field to input your date of birth.
* **What is your favorite time?** (profile_field_5): A field to input your favorite time.
* **What is your email?** (profile_field_6): A field to input your email address.
* **What is your phone number?** (profile_field_7): A field to input your phone number.
* **What is your favorite hobby?** (profile_field_8): A field to input your favorite hobby.
* **What is your favorite food?** (profile_field_9): A field to input your favorite food.
* **What is your favorite animal?** (profile_field_10): A field to input your favorite animal.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out in sections, with required fields first. Ensure to complete all required fields before proceeding to the next section.
