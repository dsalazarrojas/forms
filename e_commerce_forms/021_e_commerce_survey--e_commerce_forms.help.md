# E Commerce Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Commerce Survey is a collection of questions designed to gather information from customers about their online shopping habits, preferences, and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **first name** and **last name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **email address** as it appears on your email account.
3. Provide your **phone number** if you wish to be contacted by our team.
4. Enter your **company** (if applicable).
5. Select your **country** of residence.
6. Choose the **states** where you have purchased from or are interested in purchasing from.
7. Enter your **zip code**.
8. Indicate your **purchase frequency** (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
9. Choose the **channel** through which you usually make online purchases (e.g., online, mobile, physical store).
10. Select the **products you have bought** and **products you would like to buy** (e.g., electronics, clothing, home goods).
11. Provide any **comments** about your shopping experience.
12. Rate your **satisfaction level** with online shopping (1-5).
13. Enter the **time and date** of your last purchase.
14. If you did not make a purchase in the last period, choose the **reasons why** (e.g., price, quality, availability).
15. If you made a purchase, choose the **reasons for buying** (e.g., price, quality, recommendation).
16. Rate your **satisfaction level** with online shopping (1-5).
17. Enter any **additional comments** about your satisfaction.
18. Select any **products you have recommended** to others.
19. Select any **product categories** you would recommend to others.
20. Choose any **vendors** you have recommended to others.
21. Enter any additional **notes** about your shopping experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first name**: A field to enter your first name. This is optional but recommended for a more personalized experience.
* **last name**: A field to enter your last name. This is required for identification purposes.
* **email**: A field to enter the email address associated with your email account. This is required for contact purposes.
* **phone**: A field to enter your phone number. This is optional for follow-up contact.
* **company**: A field to enter your company name (if applicable).
* **country**: A field to select your country of residence. This helps us understand your market preferences.
* **state**: A field to select the state where you have purchased from or are interested in purchasing from. This helps us understand your region-specific preferences.
* **zip code**: A field to enter your zip code. This helps us understand your market demographics.
* **age**: A field to select your age. This is optional but helps us understand your demographics.
* **purchase frequency**: A field to select how often you make online purchases. This helps us understand your shopping habits.
* **purchase channel**: A field to select how you usually make online purchases (e.g., online, mobile, physical store). This helps us understand your shopping channels.
* **products bought**: A field to select products you have bought. This helps us understand your buying preferences.
* **products wanted**: A field to select products you would like to buy. This helps us understand your interests.
* **comments**: A field to enter any comments about your shopping experience.
* **rating**: A field to rate your satisfaction level with online shopping (1-5). This helps us understand your satisfaction.
* **date of last purchase**: A field to enter the date and time of your last purchase. This helps us understand your shopping history.
* **time of last purchase**: A field to enter the time of your last purchase. This helps us understand your shopping timing.
* **reasons for not buying**: A field to select reasons for not making a purchase (e.g., price, quality, availability). This helps us understand your purchase decisions.
* **reasons for buying**: A field to select reasons for making a purchase (e.g., price, quality, recommendation). This helps us understand your buying motivations.
* **satisfaction level**: A field to rate your satisfaction level with online shopping (1-5). This helps us understand your satisfaction.
* **satisfaction comments**: A field to enter any comments about your satisfaction. This helps us understand your satisfaction.
* **recommended products**: A field to select products you have recommended to others. This helps us understand your product endorsements.
* **recommended product categories**: A field to select product categories you would recommend to others. This helps us understand your product preferences.
* **recommended vendors**: A field to select vendors you have recommended to others. This helps us understand your vendor preferences.
* **notes**: A field to enter any additional comments about your shopping experience. This is optional but helps us understand your feedback.
