# Holiday Gift Box Preorder Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to facilitate the pre-ordering of holiday gift boxes. Users are required to select a gift box option and provide basic information for the order. Additional fields are optional and can be filled in if necessary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select a gift box from the options provided.
2. Optionally, provide your name and a brief description for the gift box.
3. Optionally, enter the price and quantity of the gift box.
4. Optionally, enter the customer's email and phone number for communication purposes.
5. Optionally, provide shipping details for the gift box.
6. Optionally, enter the shipping and handling costs for the gift box.
7. Optionally, enter the tax rates for the shipping and handling costs.
8. Optionally, calculate the subtotal and total costs of the gift box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gift Box Options (Gift Box A, Gift Box B, Gift Box C)**: Select one of the three gift box options.
* **Gift Box Name**: Enter a name for your gift box, if desired.
* **Price**: Enter the price of your gift box, if not provided.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of gift boxes you would like to order, if not provided.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your customer's email address for communication purposes, if desired.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your customer's phone number for communication purposes, if desired.
* **Shipping Details**: Enter the shipping details for your gift box, if desired.
* **Gift Box Shipping Cost**: Enter the shipping cost of your gift box, if not provided.
* **Gift Box Handling Cost**: Enter the handling cost of your gift box, if not provided.
* **Gift Box Shipping Cost Tax**: Enter the tax rate for the shipping cost of your gift box, if not provided.
* **Gift Box Handling Cost Tax**: Enter the tax rate for the handling cost of your gift box, if not provided.
* **Gift Box Total Cost**: Enter the total cost of your gift box, if not provided.
* **Gift Box Discount**: Enter a discount for your gift box, if desired.
* **Gift Box Subtotal**: Enter the subtotal for your gift box, if not provided.
* **Gift Box Tax**: Enter the tax rate for your gift box, if not provided.
* **Gift Box Total**: Enter the total price of your gift box, if not provided.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have all necessary information before submitting the form.
* Double-check your selections and calculations to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
