# Online Marketplace Negative Feedback Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report negative issues or concerns that you have encountered while using an online marketplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer name and email address in the respective fields.
2. Describe the issue you experienced in the "Issue Description" field.
3. Select the relevant marketplace and agent from the drop-down menus.
4. Choose the type and priority level of the issue you are reporting.
5. Provide any additional details or notes that may help the assigned agent to follow up on the issue.
6. Submit the form to send the feedback to the online marketplace administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or profile.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address associated with your online marketplace account.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, `text`, required/optional): Clearly describe the issue you experienced, including any details that may help identify the problem.
* **Marketplace Name** (`marketplace_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the online marketplace where you encountered the issue.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to be contacted back by the marketplace administrator to follow up on the issue.
* **Assigned Agent** (`assigned_agent`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the agent who will be assigned to follow up on your issue.
* **Issue Type** (`issue_type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose all the types of issues you experienced, such as "Product Quality", "Ordering", "Payment", "Delivery", or "Returns".
* **Priority Level** (`priority_level`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the priority level of the issue, such as "High", "Medium", or "Low".
* **Issue Status** (`issue_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the status of the issue, such as "Open", "Closed", or "Pending".
* **Follow-up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional details or notes that may help the assigned agent to follow up on the issue.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional message or feedback you would like to provide to the marketplace administrator.
* **Marketplace Notes** (`marketplace_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or feedback you would like to provide to the marketplace administrator.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any actions taken by the marketplace administrator to resolve the issue.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional information that may help to resolve the issue.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when describing the issue.
* Use the correct marketplace name and agent assigned to follow up on your issue.
* Choose the correct issue type and priority level to help the administrator to understand the severity of the issue.
* Use the "Follow-up Notes" field to provide any additional details that may help to resolve the issue.
* Use the "Customer Message" field to provide any additional feedback or comments that may be helpful to the marketplace administrator.
* Use the "Marketplace Notes" field to provide any additional feedback or comments that may be helpful to the marketplace administrator.
* Use the "Action Taken" field to document any actions taken by the marketplace administrator to resolve the issue.
* Use the "Additional Info" field to provide any additional information that may be helpful to resolve the issue.
