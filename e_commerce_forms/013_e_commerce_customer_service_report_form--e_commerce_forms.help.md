# e_commerce_customer_service_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The e_commerce_customer_service_report_form is designed to report customer service interactions and provide valuable information for the customer service team to resolve issues efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, click on the "Start" button or navigate to the corresponding page.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as required) and any additional information you think is relevant to the situation.
3. Once you've filled in all the required fields, click on the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order ID (1)** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the order ID number associated with the issue or interaction. This helps the customer service team locate the specific order in their system.
* **Customer Name (2)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their order or correspondence. This helps the customer service team recognize the customer and their issue.
* **Order Date (3)** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date the order was placed or the date of the interaction. This helps the customer service team understand the timeline of events.
* **Order Status (4)** (`select_one`, required): Select the current status of the order from the dropdown menu:
	+ Pending: The order is still pending and hasn't been processed.
	+ Shipped: The order has been shipped or delivered.
	+ Cancelled: The order has been cancelled.
	+ Refunded: The order has been refunded.
	+ Held for verification: The order is being held for verification or further review.
* **Customer Issue (5)** (`text`, required/optional): Describe the issue or interaction with the customer, including any specific problems or concerns they've raised.
* **Customer Comments (6)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback from the customer, which can help the customer service team understand their perspective.
* **Order Notes (7)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any internal notes or information about the order that's relevant to the situation.
* **Follow-up Notes (8)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any follow-up actions or steps taken by the customer service team, including any further actions or next steps.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to help the customer service team understand the situation.
* Use the dropdown menu for Order Status to ensure the correct status is selected.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the customer's correspondence or interaction for clarification.
* Double-check the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
