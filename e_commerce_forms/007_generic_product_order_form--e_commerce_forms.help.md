# Generic Product Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Generic Product Order Form is a form designed to collect information for placing an order with our company. This form gathers essential details about the order, customer, and shipping information. It is intended for customers, clients, or internal staff to submit their orders for products or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Generic Product Order Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Order Details" section with a brief description of the order.
2. Provide your "Customer Info" in the respective fields, including your email and phone number.
3. Select your preferred "Payment Method" from the available options.
4. Enter your shipping details in the "Shipping Details" section.
5. Provide the "Product Name" of the item you wish to order.
6. Enter the "Product Price" and "Product Quantity" of the item.
7. Choose the "Order Date" for the delivery.
8. Optionally, provide any additional details in the "Order Notes" field (though this field is not currently visible in the form).
9. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`id: 1`): Enter a brief description of the order, such as the type of product or service being ordered.
* **Customer Info** (`id: 2`): Fill in your contact information, including email and phone number, so our staff can efficiently reach you.
* **Payment Method** (`id: 3`): Select the payment method you would like to use for the order, e.g., Visa, Mastercard, or PayPal.
* **Shipping Details** (`id: 4`): Provide your shipping address details for the delivery of the product.
* **Product Name** (`id: 5`): Enter the name of the product or service you wish to order.
* **Product Price** (`id: 6`): Enter the price of the product or service you wish to order.
* **Product Quantity** (`id: 7`): Enter the quantity of the product or service you wish to order.
* **Order Date** (`id: 8`): Choose the date for the delivery of the product or service.
* **Customer Email** (`id: 9`): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Customer Phone** (`id: 10`): Enter your phone number for any urgent communication.
* **Shipping Street** (`id: 11`): Enter the street address where you would like the product delivered.
* **Shipping City** (`id: 12`): Enter the city where you would like the product delivered.
* **Shipping Province** (`id: 13`): Enter the province where you would like the product delivered.
* **Shipping Postal** (`id: 14`): Enter your postal code for the delivery address.

## Tips

* Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us for assistance.
* We may contact you via the email or phone number you provided to confirm your order details before processing the order.
