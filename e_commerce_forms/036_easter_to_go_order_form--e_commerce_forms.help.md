# easter_to_go_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 'easter_to_go_order_form' is for customers to place an Easter-themed order for pickup or delivery. Please provide your information and order details to place your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (none are marked as required, so fill out all fields to ensure accurate order processing).
2. Enter your customer information (Customer ID, Email, Phone number).
3. Choose an order date and time for your order.
4. Submit your completed form to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer ID (2)**: Your unique identifier for our records.
* **Customer Email (3)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your order.
* **Customer Phone (6)**: Your phone number for any last-minute questions or order updates.
* **Order Date (4)**: Select the date you'd like to pick up or receive your order.
* **Order Time (5)**: Choose the time you'd like to pick up or receive your order.

## Tips
* Double-check your order details before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
* If you have any special requests or questions, contact us at your chosen email or phone number.
* Our team will review and process your order promptly.
