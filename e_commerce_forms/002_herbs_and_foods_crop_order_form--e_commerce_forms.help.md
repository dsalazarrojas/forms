# Herbs And Foods Crop Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect order details, customer information, payment details, and other relevant information related to herb and food crops from customers. It is intended to be a one-stop-shop for farmers, farm owners, or crop sellers to collect all necessary information from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Order Details" page with the specific details of your order, including the type and quantity of crops you are interested in purchasing.
2. Provide your "Customer Details" on the following page, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant information.
3. Enter your "Payment Info" on the next page, including your payment method and any additional payment instructions.
4. Complete the "Shipping Details" page with information about where you'd like your crops to be delivered.
5. Provide any additional "Order Notes" or "Customer Notes" if necessary.
6. Review and confirm your "Order Summary" on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details (page 1)**: This page is used to collect specific details about your order, such as the type and quantity of crops you are interested in purchasing.
* **Customer Details (page 2)**: This page is used to collect your contact information and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Payment Info (page 3)**: This page is used to enter your payment method and any additional payment instructions.
* **Shipping Details (page 4)**: This page is used to provide information about where you'd like your crops to be delivered.
* **Order Notes (page 5)**: This page is used to provide any additional notes or instructions about your order.
* **Customer Notes (page 6)**: This page is used to provide any additional notes or instructions from your side.
* **Payment Terms (page 7)**: This page is used to provide any payment terms or conditions that apply to your order.
* **Order Terms (page 8)**: This page is used to provide any terms or conditions that apply to your order.
* **Additional Info (page 9)**: This page is used to provide any additional information about your order.
* **Delivery Schedule (page 10)**: This page is used to provide information about when you'd like your crops to be delivered.
* **Order Status (page 11)**: This page is used to track the status of your order.
* **Order Date (page 12)**: This page is used to select the date you'd like to place your order.

Note: All form fields are optional unless marked as "required". However, please ensure that all necessary information is provided to avoid any delay or issues with your order.
