# E Commerce Checkout Process Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather feedback on the e-commerce checkout process to identify areas for improvement and enhance customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you understand each section.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
3. Provide accurate information for the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. customer_info
 * **customer_info** (`text`, optional): This section is for providing general information about your purchase. Please feel free to share any details that might help us understand your experience.

### 2. order_details
 * **order_details** (`text`, optional): Describe the product(s) you purchased or attempted to purchase. Be as specific as possible about the product and its features.

### 3. shipping_details
 * **shipping_details** (`text`, optional): Share details about how you shipped or received your order, including any shipping methods used.

### 4. payment_info
 * **payment_info** (`text`, optional): Explain how you made the payment for your order, including payment methods used.

### 5. order_status
 * **order_status** (`text`, optional): Report the status of your order, such as whether it was delivered or cancelled.

### 6. feedback
 * **feedback** (`text`, optional): Share any feedback about your experience, including suggestions for improvement.

### 7. additional_info
 * **additional_info** (`text`, optional): If you have any additional information or comments about your experience, please share them here.

### 8. comments
 * **comments** (`text`, optional): Leave any comments or suggestions about the checkout process.

### 9. email
 * **email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address to receive a follow-up survey or any updates about your order.

## Tips
* Please keep your responses clear and concise.
* If you need help or have questions, contact us at form@gic.mx.
* We appreciate your participation and feedback, which will help us improve the e-commerce checkout process.
