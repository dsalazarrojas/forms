# Free Shipping Eligibility Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Free Shipping Eligibility Request Form is designed to help us determine if an order is eligible for free shipping. Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to help us review your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in all the required fields.
*   Make sure to fill out all fields correctly and accurately.
*   Double-check your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **FirstName** (`First Name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
*   **LastName** (`Last Name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
*   **ContactMethod** (`Contact Method`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you would like us to contact you regarding your order.
*   **EmailAddress** (`Email Address`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
*   **ShippingAddress** (`Shipping Address`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address.
*   **FreeShipping** (`Free Shipping`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if this order is eligible for free shipping.
*   **Reason** (`Reason`, text, optional): Enter a brief explanation for your free shipping request.
*   **AdditionalComments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your order.
*   **DateOfLastPurchase** (`Date Of Last Purchase`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last purchase.
*   **TimeOfLastPurchase** (`Time Of Last Purchase`, time, optional): Enter the time of your last purchase.
*   **CustomerID** (`Customer ID`, number, optional): Enter your customer ID.
*   **OrderID** (`Order ID`, number, optional): Enter your order ID.
*   **TotalOrders** (`Total Orders`, number, optional): Enter the total number of orders you have placed.
*   **TotalPurchases** (`Total Purchases`, number, optional): Enter the total value of your purchases.
*   **PurchaseValue** (`Purchase Value`, number, optional): Enter the value of this order.
*   **CustomerService** (`Customer Service`, text, optional): Enter any information about your customer service experience.
*   **CustomerServiceType** (`Customer Service Type`, text, optional): Enter the type of customer service you have received.
*   **CustomerServiceLevel** (`Customer Service Level`, text, optional): Enter the level of customer service you have received.
*   **CustomerType** (`Customer Type`, text, optional): Enter your customer type.
*   **CustomerTypeOther** (`Customer Type Other`, text, optional): Enter any other customer type information.
*   **CustomerTypeOtherValue** (`Customer Type Other Value`, text, optional): Enter any other customer type value information.
*   **OrderValue** (`Order Value`, number, optional): Enter the value of this order.
*   **PurchaseValueOver** (`Purchase Value Over`, number, optional): Enter the purchase value over a certain amount.
*   **PurchaseValueUnder** (`Purchase Value Under`, number, optional): Enter the purchase value under a certain amount.
*   **PurchaseValueEqual** (`Purchase Value Equal`, number, optional): Enter the purchase value equal to a certain amount.
*   **PurchaseValueBetween** (`Purchase Value Between`, number, optional): Enter the purchase value between two certain amounts.
