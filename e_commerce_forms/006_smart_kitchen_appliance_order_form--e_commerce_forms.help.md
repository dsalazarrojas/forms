# smart_kitchen_appliance_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for smart kitchen appliances. It allows users to enter their order details, select the items they want to order, specify the quantity, and enter their shipping and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your order details in the "Order Details" field.
2. Select the items you want to order from the "Items" dropdown list.
3. Specify the quantity of each item in the "Quantity" field.
4. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
5. Enter your contact information in the "Shipping Contact" field.
6. Enter any order comments in the "Order Comments" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Select your payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown list.
10. Confirm your order by selecting "Yes" in the "Confirm Order" field.
11. Confirm your phone number by selecting "Yes" in the "Confirm Phone" field.
12. Confirm your email address by selecting "Yes" in the "Confirm Email" field.
13. Accept the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes" in the "Terms and Conditions" field.
14. Click "Submit" to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your order.
* **Items** (`items`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the items you want to order from the dropdown list.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required/optional): Enter the quantity of each item you want to order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of your order.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address.
* **Shipping Contact** (`shipping_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the shipping contact.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any comments or notes about your order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select the payment method you want to use.
* **Confirm Order** (`confirm_order`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm your order by selecting "Yes".
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, select_multiple, required/optional): Confirm your phone number by selecting "Yes".
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, select_multiple, required/optional): Confirm your email address by selecting "Yes".
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms`, select_multiple, required/optional): Accept the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes".
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, select_one, required/optional): Click "Yes" to submit your order.
* **Cancel** (`cancel_button`, select_one, required/optional): Click "No" to cancel your order.
* **Save Order** (`save_order`, select_one, required/optional): Click "Yes" to save your order.
* **View Order** (`view_order`, select_one, required/optional): View your order.
* **Delete Order** (`delete_order`, select_one, required/optional): Delete your order.
* **Update Order** (`update_order`, select_one, required/optional): Update your order.
* **Confirm Update** (`confirm_update`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm the update of your order.
