# Lockdown Food Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lockdown Food Order Form is designed for users to order food items from various categories. The form has multiple pages, each with a list of available items for selection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select an item from the menu categories, such as "Burgers", "Pizzas", or "Salads". You can choose one or multiple items from the same category.
2. Enter the quantity of the selected item(s).
3. If necessary, add a comment for your order.
4. Select a date for your order.
5. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
6. Review your order and submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Menu Item 1**: Select an item from the "Burgers" category.
* **Quantity 1**: Enter the quantity of the selected burger.
* **Menu Item 2**: Select an item from the "Pizzas" category.
* **Quantity 2**: Enter the quantity of the selected pizza.
* **Order Date**: Select a date for your order.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Order Comment**: Add any additional comments for your order.
* **Burger**: Select a burger option.
* **Pizza**: Select a pizza option.
* **Salads**: Select salad options (multiple selection allowed).
* **Sandwiches**: Select a sandwich option.
* **Drinks**: Select a drink option.
* **Desserts**: Select a dessert option.
* **Order Note**: Add any additional comments for your order.
* **Sushi**: Select a sushi option.
* **Total Price**: This field will be populated automatically after selecting items and quantities.
* **Submit**: Review and submit your order.
* **Submit Comment**: This field is not necessary; the "Submit" field is where you should review and finalize your order.
* **Confirm**: This field is not necessary; the "Submit" field is where you should confirm your order.
* **Order Status**: This field is not intended for user interaction; it's for internal use only.
