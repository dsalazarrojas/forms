# Womens Fashion Order Form Paypal Invoicing - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Womens Fashion Order Form Paypal Invoicing is intended to facilitate ordering and invoicing for women's fashion items using the PayPal payment gateway.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your First Name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your Email address in the field labeled "Email".
4. Enter your Phone Number in the field labeled "Phone Number".
5. Enter your Shipping Address in the field labeled "Shipping Address".
6. Select the desired Products from the options listed in the field labeled "Product".
7. Enter the quantity of the selected Product(s) in the field labeled "Quantity".
8. Enter the Price of the selected Product(s) in the field labeled "Price".
9. If you have any additional Order Notes, you may enter them in the field labeled "Order Notes".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (text, required):** Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (text, required):** Enter your last name.
* **Email (email, required):** Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number (text, required):** Enter your phone number.
* **Shipping Address (text, required):** Enter your shipping address.
* **Product (select multiple, required):** Select one or more products from the listed options.
* **Quantity (number, required):** Enter the quantity of the selected product(s).
* **Price (number, required):** Enter the price of the selected product(s).
* **Order Notes (note, optional):** Enter any additional notes or instructions for your order, if needed.
