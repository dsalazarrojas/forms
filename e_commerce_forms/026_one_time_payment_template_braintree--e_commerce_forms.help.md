# one_time_payment_template_braintree - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential customer information for a one-time payment template using the Braintree payment gateway.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the first text field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second text field.
3. Provide your **Email** in the email field, ensuring it follows the proper email format (e.g., `username@example.com`).
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the last text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification or personal records.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification or personal records.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that you use to receive notifications or updates. Ensure it follows the standard email format (e.g., `username@example.com`).
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number, including the country code if necessary. This field is optional but recommended for communication purposes.

## Tips
* Double-check your input for errors or inaccuracies before submitting the form.
* Ensure your email address is valid and corresponds to your actual contact information.
* If you encounter any issues or have questions, contact `form@gic.mx` for assistance.
