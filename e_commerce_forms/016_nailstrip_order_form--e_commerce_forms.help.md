# Nailstrip Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nailstrip Order Form is a crucial tool for customers to place their orders for nailstrip products or services. By providing the necessary information, customers can ensure that their orders are processed efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name.
2. Fill in your last name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Enter your address.
6. Choose the order date for your nailstrip order.
7. Select the time for your order.
8. Choose your desired product (yes or no).
9. Specify the quantity of the product you want to order.
10. Provide any additional note for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name)** (`1`, text, required: false): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Last Name)** (`2`, text, required: false): Please enter your last name.
* **Email (Email)** (`3`, email, required: false): Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you regarding your order.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`4`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number. This will be used to contact you if there are any issues with your order.
* **Address (Address)** (`5`, text, required: false): Enter your address. This will be used to ship your order to you.
* **Order Date (Order Date)** (`6`, date, required: false): Choose the date for your nailstrip order.
* **Order Time (Order Time)** (`7`, time, required: false): Choose the time for your nailstrip order.
* **Product (Product)** (`8`, select_one, required: false): Choose your desired product (yes or no).
* **Quantity (Quantity)** (`9`, number, required: false): Specify the quantity of the product you want to order.
* **Note (Note)** (`10`, note, required: false): Provide any additional note for your order.
* **Video Content (Video Content)** (`11`, text, required: false): Not applicable for this form.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any issues with your order, please contact us at the email address you provided.
