# Easy Order System - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Easy Order System form is a customizable, user-friendly form designed to collect information about orders and delivery preferences from customers. It's ideal for businesses and organizations that need to gather data from individuals or groups with a specific request for goods or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To use the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Navigate through the three pages: Order Details, Customer Details, and Delivery Preferences.
2.  On each page, fill in the required and optional fields as instructed.
3.  Review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
4.  Once you've completed all fields, click the submit button to send the form data to the relevant party.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Details
*   **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, Optional)
    Enter a description of the order you would like to place, including any specific details or comments.
### Customer Details
*   **Customer Name** (`customer_details`, `text`, Optional): Enter your name or the name of the person or entity placing the order.
*   **Customer Email** (`customer_details`, `text`, Optional)
    Enter your email address or the email address of the person or entity placing the order.
*   **Customer Phone Number** (`customer_details`, `text`, Optional)
    Enter your phone number or the phone number of the person or entity placing the order.
### Delivery Preferences
*   **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, `text`, Optional)
    Specify any special delivery instructions or requests you may have.
 
## Tips
- Be sure to fill out all required fields for accurate data collection.
- If unsure about any field, you can always ask for help or clarification.
- Once submitted, review your entries for accuracy and completeness.
