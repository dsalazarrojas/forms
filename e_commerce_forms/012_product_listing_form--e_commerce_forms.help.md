# product_listing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The product listing form is a tool used to create or update product listings in the e-commerce system. It is essential to ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and consistently to maintain data quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the product name in the "Product Name" field. This should be a concise and descriptive name for the product.
2.  Enter the product price in the "Price" field. This should be the current price of the product in the currency of your choice.
3.  Provide the URL of the product image in the "Image URL" field. This should point to a valid image URL on your website or storage service.
4.  Select the category for the product from the "Category" dropdown list. This will help with filtering and sorting products in the system.
5.  Enter a brief description of the product in the "Description" field.
6.  Enter the shipping cost of the product in the "Shipping Cost" field.
7.  Select the product tags relevant to the product from the "Product Tags" dropdown list. Multiple tags can be selected.
8.  Select the product status from the "Product Status" dropdown list. This will determine the product's availability in the system.
9.  Enter the product ID if you are updating an existing product listing. This will link the new listing to the existing product.
10.  Enter the customer name and email in the respective fields if you are creating a new product listing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Product Name** (`Product Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product in a concise and descriptive manner.
*   **Price** (`Price`, number, required): Enter the current price of the product.
*   **Image URL** (`Image URL`, text, required): Provide the URL of the product image.
*   **Category** (`Category`, select_one, required): Select the category for the product.
*   **Description** (`Description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the product.
*   **Shipping Cost** (`Shipping Cost`, number, optional): Enter the shipping cost of the product.
*   **Product Tags** (`Product Tags`, select_multiple, optional): Select the tags relevant to the product.
*   **Product Status** (`Product Status`, select_one, required): Select the product status.
*   **Product ID** (`Product ID`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the product if updating an existing listing.
*   **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer if creating a new product listing.
*   **Customer Email** (`Customer Email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the customer if creating a new product listing.

## Tips
When filling out this form, ensure that all fields that are marked as required are completed accurately and consistently. If you are updating an existing product listing, ensure that the product ID is entered correctly. If you are creating a new product listing, ensure that the customer name and email are accurate and valid.
