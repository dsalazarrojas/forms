# A.L.P Services Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to apply for A.L.P Services, which provide advisory, implementation, managed services, training, and audit services to various industries.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section of the form, starting from the first field, "Applicant Information".
2. Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
3. Enter your company or organization name.
4. Provide a valid business email address for contact purposes.
5. Enter your phone number, including the country code if you are outside the US.
6. Select the primary service type you are applying for from the provided options.
7. Describe the services you are requesting in a few sentences, including scope, objectives, and any constraints.
8. If applicable, select your preferred project start date.
9. Enter your estimated budget for the services, in USD.
10. If applicable, select the desired contract term in months.
11. Choose up to 5 industry sectors that apply to your project.
12. If applicable, select the level of previous experience you have with similar providers.
13. Select "Yes" to indicate you agree to the initial terms and contact.
14. Enter any additional notes or special requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Company/Organization Name** (Required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Contact Email** (Required): Enter a valid business email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number, including the country code if outside the US.
* **Requested Service Type** (Required): Select the primary service type you are applying for.
	+ **Advisory**: For general consulting and advice.
	+ **Implementation**: For the actual implementation of services.
	+ **Managed Services**: For ongoing management and maintenance services.
	+ **Training**: For educational and training services.
	+ **Audit**: For auditing and assessment services.
* **Describe the Service Needs** (Required): Provide a brief description of the services you are requesting, including scope, objectives, and any constraints.
* **Preferred Project Start Date** (Optional): Select the preferred start date for the project, if applicable.
* **Estimated Budget (USD)** (Optional): Enter your estimated budget for the services, in USD.
* **Desired Contract Term (months)** (Optional): Select the desired contract term in months.
* **Industry Sectors Applicable** (Optional): Choose up to 5 industry sectors that apply to your project.
	+ **Finance**: Financial services and banking.
	+ **Healthcare**: Healthcare services and organizations.
	+ **Retail**: Retail and consumer services.
	+ **Manufacturing**: Manufacturing and production services.
	+ **Technology**: Technology and software services.
	+ **Education**: Educational institutions and services.
* **Previous Experience with Similar Providers** (Optional): Select the level of previous experience you have with similar providers.
	+ **None**: No experience.
	+ **Limited**: Some experience.
	+ **Moderate**: Moderate experience.
	+ **Extensive**: Extensive experience.
* **Agrees to Initial Terms and Contact** (Required): Select "Yes" to indicate you agree to the initial terms and contact.
* **Additional Notes or Special Requirements** (Optional): Enter any other details or special requirements for the project.
