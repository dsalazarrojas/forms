# Team Brilliance Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Brilliance Template is designed to collect information for a team project related to e-commerce. Please provide accurate and relevant details to ensure the data submitted is useful for the project's goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, but you don't need to answer every question. The fields marked as "required" are essential for the template's functionality.
2. For fields with multiple options, select the relevant answer or answers that apply to your situation.
3. Enter your information in the text field and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Line as of July including Summer Splash**: This field is a text box where you can enter a description or details about the full line, as it relates to your e-commerce project. (Required)
* **E-Commerce Forms**: Choose all the E-Commerce Forms that apply to your project: Select one or more of the options provided. (Required)
* **Category**: Select the Category that best describes your project: (Required)

## Tips
- Double-check your submissions to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
- If you have any issues or concerns, reach out to the project team for support.
