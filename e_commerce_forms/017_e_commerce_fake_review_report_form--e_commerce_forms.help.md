# E-Commerce Fake Review Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E-Commerce Fake Review Report Form is used to report fake or suspicious reviews on an e-commerce platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Product Id" field and enter the ID of the product being reviewed.
2. Enter the "Product Name" field with the name of the product being reviewed.
3. Choose the "Marketplace" where the review was posted.
4. Select the "Review Date" when the review was posted.
5. Rate the review as "Good", "Fair", or "Other" in the "Rating" field.
6. Enter the "Reviewer Name" who posted the review.
7. Enter a detailed description of the review in the "Review Text" field.
8. Choose the "Review Status" from the options provided.
9. (Optional) Enter a reason for reporting the review in the "Reason" field.
10. Select "Marketplace Admin", "Reviewer", or "Other" as the "Reported By" person.
11. Enter any additional "Notes" about the review.
12. Enter the "Reported Date" when the review was reported.
13. Check if the "Product Category" is a "Yes" or "No" for the review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Id**: Enter the ID of the product being reviewed.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product being reviewed.
* **Marketplace**: Choose the marketplace where the review was posted.
* **Review Date**: Select the date when the review was posted.
* **Rating**: Choose the rating for the review as "Good", "Fair", or "Other".
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of the reviewer who posted the review.
* **Review Text**: Enter a detailed description of the review.
* **Review Status**: Choose the status of the review as "Verified Reviewer", "Suspicious Review", "Spam Review", or "Other".
* **Reason**: (Optional) Enter a reason for reporting the review.
* **Reported By**: Select who reported the review, either "Marketplace Admin", "Reviewer", or "Other".
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the review.
* **Reported Date**: Enter the date when the review was reported.
* **Product Category**: Check if the product category is a "Yes" or "No" for the review.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the information provided to ensure accuracy.
* If unsure about any field, refer to the help guide or contact platform support.
