# Deli Order Form For In Store Pickup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to place orders for in-store pickup at a deli. It helps you gather information about the customer's order, including their preferred category, sandwich selection, quantity, salad choice, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your title or name in the "Deli Order Form" field.
2. Select your preferred category from the "Category" dropdown menu. This will help us understand your order preferences.
3. If you've selected "Yes" to a category, enter your description in the "Description" field.
4. Select your preferred sandwich options in the "Sandwiches" field (multiple selection is allowed).
5. Enter the quantity you'd like to order in the "Quantity" field.
6. Select your preferred salad options in the "Salad" field (multiple selection is allowed).
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field for contact purposes.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field for further contact.
9. Finally, enter a brief description of your order in the "Description" field if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Deli Order Form** (title, text, required/optional): Enter your title or name here.
* **Category** (category, select one, required/optional): Select the category of food you're interested in ordering. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
* **Description** (description, text, required/optional): If you've selected "Yes" to a category, enter a brief description of your order.
* **Form Id** (form_id, text, required/optional): Enter a unique identifier for your order, if needed.
* **Sandwiches** (sandwiches, select multiple, required/optional): Select the type of sandwich you'd like to order. You can choose multiple options: Grilled Chicken, Turkey, Veggie, Tuna, or Ham.
* **Quantity** (quantity, number, required/optional): Enter the number of sandwiches you'd like to order.
* **Salad** (salad, select multiple, required/optional): Select the type of salad you'd like to accompany your order. You can choose multiple options: Garden, Caesar, Greek, or Spinach.
* **Email** (email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for further contact and order tracking.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact and order update.
* **Description** (description, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your order if needed.
