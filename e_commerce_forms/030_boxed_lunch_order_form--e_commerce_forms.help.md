# Boxed Lunch Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Boxed Lunch Order Form is designed to gather information from customers for their orders. This form helps the business to understand the customer's preferences, special requirements, and other essential details to provide an excellent service.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, fill out all the required fields marked as **required**. These fields are essential for the business to process your order correctly.
2.  Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address** to allow the business to contact you.
3.  Choose how you want to receive your order: **Delivery** or **Pickup** from one of the listed locations.
4.  Select the date and time you would like to receive your order.
5.  Choose the number of **Lunches** you require.
6.  Select the **Lunch Package** that suits your needs.
7.  Pick your **Sandwich Selection** and **Side Selection** from the options provided.
8.  Choose your **Dessert Selection** and **Drink Selection**.
9.  If you have any **Dietary Restrictions**, list them in the provided options.
10. If you have any **Special Instructions**, enter them in this field.
11. Select the **Condiments Needed** and **Utensils Needed** for your order.
12. If you require extra **Napkins**, select the option accordingly.
13. For **Business Orders**, enter your **Company Name**.
14. Choose your **Payment Method**.
15. If you have a **Promo Code**, enter it.
16. The system will automatically calculate the **Total Amount** based on your selections.
17. Finally, enter any **Order Notes** you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as per your identification.
*  **Last Name**: Enter your last name as per your identification.
*  **Email Address**: Enter your email address for order confirmation.
*  **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact number.
*  **Delivery or Pickup**: Choose how you want to receive your order.
*  **Pickup Location**: Choose where you want to pickup your order from (if delivery).
*  **Delivery Address**: Enter your delivery address (if delivery).
*  **Order Date**: Enter the date you want to receive your order.
*  **Pickup or Delivery Date**: Enter the date you want to receive your order.
*  **Pickup or Delivery Time**: Enter the time you want to receive your order.
*  **Number of Lunches**: Enter the number of lunches you require.
*  **Lunch Package**: Choose the type of lunch package you require.
*  **Sandwich Selection**: Choose your preferred sandwich.
*  **Side Selection**: Choose your preferred side.
*  **Dessert Selection**: Choose your preferred dessert.
*  **Drink Selection**: Choose your preferred drink.
*  **Dietary Restrictions**: List any dietary restrictions you have.
*  **Special Instructions**: Enter any special requests you may have.
*  **Condiments Needed**: Select the condiments you need.
*  **Utensils Needed**: Indicate if you need utensils.
*  **Napkins Needed**: Indicate if you need extra napkins.
*  **Occasion**: Indicate the occasion of the order (if business).
*  **Company Name**: Enter your company name (if business order).
*  **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method.
*  **Promo Code**: Enter any promo code you have.
*  **Total Amount**: The system calculates this automatically.
*  **Order Notes**: Enter any additional information you may have.

## Tips

*   Always ensure that you have filled out all required fields correctly.
*   If you are unsure about any field, contact the business support team for clarification.
*   Review your order carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
