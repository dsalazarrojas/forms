# Offers And Trades - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Offers And Trades" form is a tool used to create, manage, and analyze offers and trades. It is a structured way to input and manage data about offers and trades.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant fields to fill out, as indicated by the required fields.
2. Fill out each field with the corresponding information.
3. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Offers Form** (`offer_form`, text, optional): This field is used to enter a brief description of the offer.
* **Trades Form** (`trade_form`, text, optional): This field is used to enter a brief description of the trade.
* **Product** (`product`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the product being offered or traded.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): This field is used to enter the price of the product being offered or traded.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): This field is used to enter the quantity of the product being offered or traded.
* **Trade Terms** (`trade_terms`, text, optional): This field is used to enter any terms and conditions related to the trade.
* **Shipping** (`shipping`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the shipping method for the trade.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, number, optional): This field is used to enter the shipping cost of the trade.
* **Contact** (`contact`, email, optional): This field is used to enter the contact information for the trade.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the phone number for the trade.
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): This field is used to enter any additional notes or comments about the trade.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required): This field is used to select the category of the trade (e.g., Item A, Item B, etc.).
* **Subcategory** (`subcategory`, select_multiple, required): This field is used to select the subcategory of the trade (e.g., Item D, Item E, etc.).
* **Item Description** (`item_description`, text, optional): This field is used to enter a detailed description of the item being offered or traded.
* **Item Name** (`item_name`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the item being offered or traded.
* **Item Price** (`item_price`, number, optional): This field is used to enter the price of the item being offered or traded.
* **Item Quantity** (`item_quantity`, number, optional): This field is used to enter the quantity of the item being offered or traded.
* **Trade Notes** (`trade_notes`, note, optional): This field is used to enter any notes or comments about the trade.
* **Trade Agreement** (`trade_agreement`, select_one, required): This field is used to select the trade agreement (e.g., Agreement One, Agreement Two, etc.).
* **Offer Terms** (`offer_terms`, text, optional): This field is used to enter any terms and conditions related to the offer.
* **Trade Type** (`trade_type`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the type of trade (e.g., trade, offer, etc.).
* **Trade Status** (`trade_status`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the status of the trade (e.g., Active, Inactive, etc.).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Use clear and concise language when entering information.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact your administrator.
