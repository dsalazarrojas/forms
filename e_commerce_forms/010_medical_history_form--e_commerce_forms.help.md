# medical_history_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient medical history and other relevant information from patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's contact information in the `contact_info` field.
2. List any allergies the patient has in the `allergies` field.
3. Provide a brief medical history in the `medical_history` field.
4. List any medications the patient is currently taking in the `medication` field.
5. Provide information about any surgeries the patient has had in the `surgery_history` field.
6. List any family medical history in the `family_history` field.
7. Report any medical conditions the patient has in the `medical_conditions` field.
8. Provide information about any medical test results in the `medical_test_results` field.
9. For fields labeled `medical_history_2` to `medical_history_15`, provide any additional medical history information.
