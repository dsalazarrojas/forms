# Online Plant Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from customers for online plant orders, including order details, shipping address, payment information, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, click on the "Submit" button to open the form.
2. Fill in the required fields with the necessary information.
3. For fields that are not required, you can provide optional information if you have it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Details (1)
* **Order Details**: This is the main section where you can enter your order details such as product name, quantity, and any other information about your order.
*   Enter the required details about your order.

### Shipping Address (2)
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the address where you want your order to be shipped to.

### Payment Gateway (3)
* **Payment Gateway**: Select the payment method you want to use to complete your purchase (Stripe, PayPal, or Authorize.net).
*   This selection will determine how your payment will be processed.

### Order Notes (4)
* **Order Notes**: Enter any additional comments or instructions about your order, such as special requests or any other information.

### Payment Details (5)
* **Payment Details**: Select the payment details you want to provide (Card number, Card expiration, Card type, or CVV/Security Code).
*   This information will be used to process your payment.

### Shipping Details (6)
* **Shipping Details**: Select the shipping details you want to provide (First name, Last name, Email, Phone, Address, City, State, Zip, or Country).
*   This information will help us get your order to you safely and efficiently.

### Email (7)
* **Email**: Enter your email address to allow us to contact you about your order.
*   This will also allow you to access your account and track your order.

### Phone (8)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to enable communication about your order.
*   This will allow us to quickly contact you with updates and information about your order.
