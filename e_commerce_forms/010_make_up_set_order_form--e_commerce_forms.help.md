# Make Up Set Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Make Up Set Order Form is designed to collect customer information and order details for a fictional or creative item. This form helps you create a new order by specifying the customer's email address, selecting a product, choosing a payment method, and providing shipping details. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
2. Select the product you want to order in the "Choose Product" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the product price in the "Product Price" field.
4. Choose a payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the order total in the "Order Total" field.
6. Provide the shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
7. Enter the shipping city in the "Shipping City" field.
8. Enter the shipping state in the "Shipping State" field.
9. Enter the shipping zip code in the "Shipping Zip" field.
10. Enter the billing name and email address in the "Billing Name" and "Billing Email" fields, respectively.
11. Add any additional order notes in the "Order Notes" field.
12. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Email** (customer_email, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Choose Product** (product_choice, select_one, optional): Select the product you want to order from the dropdown menu.
* **Product Price** (product_price, number, optional): Enter the price of the product.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_one, optional): Choose the payment method for the order from the dropdown menu.
* **Order Total** (order_total, number, optional): Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Shipping Address** (shipping_address, text, optional): Enter the shipping address.
* **Shipping City** (shipping_city, text, optional): Enter the shipping city.
* **Shipping State** (shipping_state, text, optional): Enter the shipping state.
* **Shipping Zip** (shipping_zip, text, optional): Enter the shipping zip code.
* **Billing Name** (billing_name, text, optional): Enter the billing name.
* **Billing Email** (billing_email, email, optional): Enter the billing email address.
* **Order Notes** (order_notes, note, optional): Add any additional order notes.
* **Submit** (submit, text, optional): Click to submit the order.
