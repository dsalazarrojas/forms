# e_commerce_data_science_partnership_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e_commerce_data_science_partnership_application_form is a document used to collect information from partners interested in collaborating with your organization for e-commerce data science projects. This form helps to gather essential details about the partner, their product, and the partnership request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Choose the correct options for each field according to your circumstances.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Please provide your name and contact information, including email and phone number.
* **Partnership Details** (`partnership_details`, text, required: false): Describe your partnership proposal, including the project idea, goals, and expectations.
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your product or service.
* **Data Science Partners** (`data_science_partners`, select_one, required: false): Choose one or more of our data science partner(s) you are interested in partnering with.
* **Data Science Partnership Form Submission** (`data_science_partnership_form_submission`, note, required: false): This field is used for any additional comments or information about your partnership proposal.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please provide your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required: false): The date and time the form was submitted.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, required: false): The time the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose who submitted the form.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, required: false): Please provide your name and title.
* **Form Comments** (`form_comments`, text, required: false): Additional comments or information about your submission.
* **Form Attachment** (`form_attachment`, text, required: false): An optional attachment or document to support your partnership proposal.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required: false): The submitter's email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required: false): The submitter's phone number.
* **Submitter Address** (`submitter_address`, text, required: false): The submitter's address.

Please make sure to fill out all the necessary fields and provide accurate information to ensure a smooth and effective partnership process.
