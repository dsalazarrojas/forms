# Contact Form For Ecommerce Sites - Help Guide

## Purpose
The contact form is designed to collect information from customers regarding any issues or concerns they may have with their order, product, or experience with the site. This form will help our support team to provide efficient and effective assistance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form by providing the required information in each field.
2. Ensure that you fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. Use the "Attach photos or files via email" field if you need to upload larger attachments.
4. Click submit to send the form to our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer name (Customer name)** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name associated with the order.
* **Order number (Order number)** (`order_number`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the order reference for your order.
* **Email address (Email address)** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address used for your order.
* **Phone number (Phone number)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for urgent issues.
* **Reason for contact (Reason for contact)** (`reason_for_contact`, select_one, required): Select the reason for your message from the provided options.
* **Product affected (Product affected)** (`product_affected`, text, optional): Enter the item name or SKU if the issue relates to a specific product.
* **Description of issue (Description of issue)** (`description_of_issue`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the issue you're experiencing.
* **Preferred resolution (Preferred resolution)** (`preferred_resolution`, select_one, optional): Choose the desired outcome from the provided options.
* **Shipping address (Shipping address)** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): If the issue relates to delivery, enter your shipping address.
* **Best time to contact (Best time to contact)** (`best_time_to_contact`, time, optional): Select a suitable time window for follow-up.
* **Attach photos or files via email (Attach photos or files via email)** (`additional_files_note`, note, optional): Use this field to attach larger files via email.
* **Additional comments (Additional comments)** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional information you'd like to share with us.

Please fill out the form with the required information and provide as much detail as possible to help our support team resolve your issue efficiently.
