# custom_t_shirt_order_form_template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the creation of custom t-shirt orders. It gathers essential information from customers, including their preferred t-shirt color, size, and order details, as well as their contact information and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the desired color of the t-shirt from the "Shirt Color" dropdown menu.
2. Choose one or multiple sizes for the order from the "Shirt Size" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
4. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
5. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
6. Provide additional order details in the "Order Details" text field.
7. Select the preferred shipping method from the "Shipping Method" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the total price of the order in the "Total Price" field.
9. Optionally, provide a customer note in the "Customer Note" text field.
10. Confirm the order by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Order Confirmation" dropdown menu.
11. Select whether the form submission is "Accepted" or "Declined" in the "Form Submission" dropdown menu.
12. If required, provide a customer signature in the "Customer Signature" text field.
13. Select the preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
14. Finally, select the order status from the "Order Status" dropdown menu, ensuring it is set to "Pending", "Shipped", or "Delivered".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Custom T Shirt**: This is the main field where you select the design for your custom t-shirt.
* **Shirt Color**: Choose the color for your custom t-shirt from the available options: red, blue, green, or yellow.
* **Shirt Size**: Select one or multiple sizes for your custom t-shirt from the available options: small, medium, or large.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name who is placing the order.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address where they can be reached for updates or inquiries.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number for further communication.
* **Order Details**: Add any additional information about the order, such as specific requests or instructions.
* **Shipping Method**: Select how the order will be shipped, either through "Shipping" or "Local Pickup".
* **Total Price**: Enter the total price of the order, including any applicable taxes or fees.
* **Customer Note**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the customer or order.
* **Order Confirmation**: Confirm the order by selecting "True" or "False", indicating whether the order has been placed successfully.
* **Form Submission**: Select "Accepted" or "Declined" to indicate whether the form submission was successful.
* **Customer Signature**: If required, provide a signature from the customer to confirm their agreement with the order.
* **Payment Method**: Select the preferred payment method, either "Credit Card" or "Cash".
* **Order Status**: Set the order status to "Pending", "Shipped", or "Delivered" to reflect its current state.
