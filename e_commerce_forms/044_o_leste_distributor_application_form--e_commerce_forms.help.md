# O.leste_Distributor_Application_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to apply as a distributor for a company or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what is required and what is optional.
2.  Fill out the form accurately and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3.  Be aware that some fields may have specific options to choose from.
4.  Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form (Text):** This field is a free-text area where you can provide any additional information about your application.
* **Address (Text):** Enter your address.
* **Email (Text):** Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Text):** Enter your phone number.
* **Note (Text):** This field is a free-text area where you can provide any additional comments or information about your application.
* **Distributor Info (Text):** Enter any additional information about your distributor experience or qualifications.
* **English (Text):** If you are proficient in English, mark "Yes" or "No".
* **Other Languages (Select one):** Select "Yes" or "No" if you are proficient in languages other than English.
* **Experience (Number):** Enter the number of years of experience you have in your field.
* **English Experience (Number):** Enter the number of years of experience you have in English.
* **Other Experience (Number):** Enter the number of years of experience you have in other areas.
* **Education (Select multiple):** Select "Yes" or "No" if you have education in languages other than English.
* **English Education (Select multiple):** Select "Yes" or "No" if you have education in English.
* **Other Education (Select multiple):** Select "Yes" or "No" if you have education in languages other than English.
* **Certifications (Select multiple):** Select "Yes" or "No" if you have certifications in languages other than English.
* **English Certifications (Select multiple):** Select "Yes" or "No" if you have certifications in English.
* **Other Certifications (Select multiple):** Select "Yes" or "No" if you have certifications in languages other than English.
* **Languages (Select multiple):** Select "Yes" or "No" if you are proficient in languages other than English.
* **English Languages (Select multiple):** Select "Yes" or "No" if you are proficient in English.
* **Other Languages (Select multiple):** Select "Yes" or "No" if you are proficient in languages other than English.
* **References (Note):** Enter any references or testimonials about your work or experience.
* **English References (Note):** Enter any references or testimonials about your English-related work or experience.
* **Other References (Note):** Enter any references or testimonials about your experience in languages other than English.
* **Date (Date):** Enter the date you are applying or have completed the form.
* **Time (Time):** Enter the time you are applying or have completed the form.
