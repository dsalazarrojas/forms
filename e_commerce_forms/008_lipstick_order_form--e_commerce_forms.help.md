## Purpose
The Lipstick Order Form is used to collect information from customers for lipstick orders. This includes shipping details, payment methods, and customer information to ensure a smooth order process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form. In this case, the Subtotal field is the only required field.
2. Fill out the remaining fields as prompted, including Lipstick Order Form, Shipping, Product, Quantity, Color, Size, Email, Phone, Note, Confirm Shipping, Confirm Pickup, Payment Method, Confirm Payment, and Customer Information.
3. Select your preferred payment method and confirm the payment status.
4. Ensure that the total price and other calculated fields are filled out correctly, as they may be automatically populated based on the previous entries.
