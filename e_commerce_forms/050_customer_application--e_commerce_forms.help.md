# Customer Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for the purpose of registering them in our system. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information in this form to ensure that we can process your application correctly and provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that you have a valid email address and a working phone number.
3. Enter your address, date of birth, last name, city, state, and country correctly.
4. Review your application carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`First Name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents, such as your passport or driver's license.
- **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for updates and communications.
- **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your working phone number to ensure that we can contact you if needed.
- **Address** (`Address`, text, optional): Enter your full address for delivery and communication purposes.
- **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
- **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **City** (`City`, text, optional): Enter the city where you reside or work.
- **State** (`State`, text, optional): Enter the state where you reside or work.
- **Zip** (`Zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code to ensure correct delivery and communication.
- **Country** (`Country`, text, optional): Enter your country of residence or nationality.
