# Solar Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Solar Quiz form is designed to collect user opinions on various solar-related topics.
## How To Complete This Form
1. The form is divided into three sections, each with a different type of question (text, select_one, and select_multiple).
2. For the text section, simply type your answer to the question in the text box.
3. For the select_one section, choose one of the two available options by selecting the corresponding radio buttons.
4. For the select_multiple section, choose multiple options from the available choices by selecting the corresponding checkboxes.
5. Review your answers before submission to ensure you've completed all sections accurately.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form 1 (text)**: Type a short answer to the question in this text box.
* **Form 2 (select_one)**: Choose one of the two available options for this question.
* **Form 3 (select_multiple)**: Select all applicable options from the available choices.
## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Review each section carefully to ensure you've answered all questions accurately.
* If you're unsure about any question, try to answer based on your best judgment.
