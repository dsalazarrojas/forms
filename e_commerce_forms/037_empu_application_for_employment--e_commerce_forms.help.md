# empu_application_for_employment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is the employment application form for individuals seeking employment. Fill out this form to provide basic details about your current or desired job title, category, type, description, and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Fill out the job title field with your current or desired job title.
* Fill out the job category field with your job category.
* Select a yes or no option for the job type field indicating if the job is full-time or part-time.
* Fill out the job description field with a brief description of your job.
* Fill out the job requirements field with any specific requirements for your job.
* Upload your resume to the resume field for our reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, required/optional): Fill out your job title with a brief and clear title of your job, e.g., "Software Engineer".
* **Job Category** (`Job Category`, text, required/optional): Fill out your job category with a brief description of your job category, e.g., "Software Development".
* **Job Type** (`Job Type`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if your job is full-time and "No" if it's part-time.
* **Job Description** (`Job Description`, text, required/optional): Fill out a brief description of your job, e.g., "Development of software applications".
* **Job Requirements** (`Job Requirements`, text, required/optional): Fill out any specific requirements for your job, e.g., "3+ years of experience in Java programming".
* **Resume** (`Resume`, text, required/optional): Upload a copy of your resume for our reference, this should be in a standard pdf or docx format.
