# upspring_resource_collaborative_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The upspring_resource_collaborative_order_form is designed to collect information from customers for collaborative orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the required fields on each page, marked with *.
2. Fill out the form section by section, starting from the Family Information Form.
3. Review and ensure that all required fields are complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Information Form** (`family_info_form`, text, *): This form gathers family information, such as family members' names and ages.
* **Contact Information Form** (`contact_info_form`, text, *): Provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Order Form** (`order_form`, text, *): Enter the order details, such as order date, time, and items ordered.
* **Delivery Information Form** (`delivery_info_form`, text, *): Provide delivery details, such as delivery address and date.
* **Payment Method Form** (`payment_method_form`, text, *): Select your payment method, such as credit card or bank transfer.
* **Item Details Form** (`item_details_form`, text, *): Enter item details, such as item name and quantity.
* **Item List Form** (`item_list_form`, text, *): List the items you are ordering, along with their respective quantities.
* **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, text, *): Confirm your submission by clicking the submit button.
* **Confirm Form** (`confirm_form`, text, *): Review and confirm your order details.
* **Review Form** (`review_form`, text, *): Enter any additional comments or reviews about your order (optional).
* **Shipping Form** (`shipping_form`, text, *): Provide shipping details, such as shipping method and address (optional).
* **Order Review Form** (`order_review_form`, text, *): Review your order status (optional).
* **Payment Information Form** (`payment_info_form`, text, *): Provide payment information, such as payment method and amount.
* **Order Status Form** (`order_status_form`, text, *): Check the status of your order.
* **Shipping Status Form** (`shipping_status_form`, text, *): Check the status of your shipping.
* **Refund Status Form** (`refund_status_form`, text, *): Check the status of your refund (optional).
* **Cancel Order Form** (`cancel_order_form`, text, *): Cancel your order if necessary.
* **Return Order Form** (`return_order_form`, text, *): Return your order if necessary.
* **Payment Method Form** (`payment_method_form`, text, *): Select your payment method (optional).
* **Item Price Form** (`item_price_form`, text, *): Enter the item price (optional).
* **Order Date Form** (`order_date_form`, text, *): Enter the order date (optional).
* **Order Time Form** (`order_time_form`, text, *): Enter the order time (optional).
* **Shipping Method Form** (`shipping_method_form`, text, *): Select your shipping method (optional).
* **Shipping Address Form** (`shipping_address_form`, text, *): Enter your shipping address (optional).
* **Payment Terms Form** (`payment_terms_form`, text, *): Review the payment terms and conditions (optional).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed.
* Double-check your information before submission.
* For any optional fields, feel free to provide additional information if needed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
