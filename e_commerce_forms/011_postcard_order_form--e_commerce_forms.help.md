# postcard_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows customers to place an order for a postcard by selecting the product, specifying the quantity, and making a payment method choice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the postcard product you would like to purchase from the list of available options.
2. Specify the quantity of the postcard you would like to buy.
3. Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product** (Select One, `select_product`, required: false): Select the type of postcard you would like to purchase from the available options (Apple, Banana, Orange).
* **Quantity** (Number, `quantity`, required: false): Type the number of postcards you would like to buy. Please use a whole number only.
* **Make Payment** (Select One, `make_payment`, required: false): Choose how you would like to make the payment for your postcard order. Please select one of the options: Cash, Cheque, or Credit Card.
* **Email** (Email, `email`, required: false): If you would like to receive a confirmation email about your order, please provide your email address here.
* **Phone** (Text, `phone_number`, required: false): If you would like to receive a phone call or text about your order, please provide your phone number here.
