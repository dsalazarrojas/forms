# Healthy Juice Order Form with Paysafe - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to order healthy juices with a secure payment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Healthy Juice Order" field, this is the main body of the order form. Here, enter your choice of healthy juice.
2. Click on the "Email" field and enter the customer's email address. 
3. In the "Notes" field, provide any additional comments or instructions for the customer's order. 
4. In the "Phone" field, enter the customer's phone number. 
5. Click on the "Date" field and select the date when the customer would like to receive their order.
