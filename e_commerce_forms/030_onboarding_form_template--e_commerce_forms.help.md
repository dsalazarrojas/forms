# Onboarding Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Onboarding Form Template is a standardized form used to collect essential information from new users, ensuring accuracy and consistency across all input fields. This form is designed to gather relevant details for efficient onboarding processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Type your valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter a password in the "Password" field.
5. Confirm your password in the "Confirm password" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. For "Select one", choose one option from the available choices.
8. Select multiple options for "Select multiple" field.
9. If applicable, provide a date in the "Date" field.
10. Enter a time in the "Time" field.
11. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
12. Confirm the provided note in the "Confirm note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Password** (`password`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your desired password.
* **Confirm password** (`confirm_password`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm your password to ensure accuracy.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Select one** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one option from the available choices.
* **Select multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select multiple options from the available choices.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): If applicable, enter a date.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter a time if applicable.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Confirm note** (`confirm_note`, `note`, required/optional): Confirm the provided note.
