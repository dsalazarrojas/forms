# Research Deposit Fee Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Deposit Fee Agreement form is a crucial document used in the research process to collect essential details about the research deposit, ensuring a smooth and transparent interaction between customers and travel agencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, such as travel agent name, deposit fee, deposit date, deposit amount, contact phone, and deposit status.
2. Select the appropriate deposit status and research deposit agreement status from the provided options.
3. Enter any additional notes about the research deposit, if necessary.
4. Provide travel agency contact information, including name, address, email, and phone number.
5. Review and submit the form to complete the deposit fee agreement process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **travel_agent_name** (Text): Enter the name of the travel agent handling your deposit.
* **deposit_fee** (Number): Specify the deposit fee for the research.
* **deposit_date** (Date): Enter the date of the deposit.
* **deposit_amount** (Number): Enter the amount of the deposit.
* **deposit_status** (Select Multiple): Choose the status of your deposit (e.g., paid, pending, refunded).
* **total_deposit** (Number): This field is optional and not required; you can fill it in if necessary.
* **research_deposit_notes** (Note): Enter any additional notes about your research deposit.
* **review_page** (Select One): Choose the review page for your deposit agreement (e.g., page 1, page 2).
* **research_deposit_status** (Select Multiple): Select the status of your research deposit (e.g., approved, pending, rejected).
* **research_deposit_agreement** (Note): Enter any notes about your research deposit agreement.
* **travel_agency_name** (Text): Enter the name of the travel agency handling your deposit.
* **contact_phone** (Text): Enter the contact phone number of the travel agency.
* **contact_email** (Email): Enter the email address of the travel agency.
* **contact_address** (Text): Enter the address of the travel agency.
* **research_deposit_agreement_date** (Date): Enter the date of the research deposit agreement.
* **research_deposit_agreement_time** (Time): Enter the time of the research deposit agreement.
* **research_deposit_agreement_status** (Select Multiple): Choose the status of your research deposit agreement (e.g., approved, pending, rejected).
* **research_deposit_agreement_notes** (Note): Enter any additional notes about your research deposit agreement.
* **total_deposit_fee** (Number): This field is optional and not required; you can fill it in if necessary.
* **travel_agency_address** (Text): Enter the address of the travel agency.
* **travel_agency_contact_name** (Text): Enter the name of the contact person at the travel agency.
* **travel_agency_contact_phone** (Text): Enter the contact phone number of the travel agency.
* **travel_agency_email** (Email): Enter the email address of the travel agency.
