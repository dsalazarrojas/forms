# E Commerce App Integration Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help us understand your e-commerce app integration request. Please fill it out completely and accurately to facilitate a smooth integration process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3.  Review the form before submitting it to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Integration Request** (`integration_request`, note, not required): This is the main description of your e-commerce app integration request. Provide a brief explanation of what you are looking for.
*   **App Name** (`app_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of your e-commerce app.
*   **App URL** (`app_url`, text, not required): Enter the URL of your e-commerce app.
*   **Use Case** (`use_case`, text, not required): Describe how you plan to use our platform for your e-commerce app.
*   **Documentation URL** (`documentation_url`, text, not required): If you have documentation related to your e-commerce app, please share it with us.
*   **SaaS Team** (`saas_team`, select multiple, required): Select one or more members of our SaaS team to work with.
*   **Partner Info** (`partner_info`, text, not required): Provide any additional information about your integration request.
*   **Integration Method** (`integration_method`, select one, required): Choose the method of integration you prefer (API, Webhook, or SDK).
*   **API Key** (`api_key`, text, not required): If you are choosing the API integration method, you will need to provide your API key.
*   **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, email, not required): Provide your email address so we can contact you about your integration request.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): If you have any additional notes or comments, please share them with us.

## Tips

*   Please fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
*   Be as detailed as possible in your explanations to help us understand your integration request.
*   We will review your form submission to determine if the integration request is valid and to guide the integration process.
*   We will contact you using the email address you provided if we need more information or clarification about your integration request.
