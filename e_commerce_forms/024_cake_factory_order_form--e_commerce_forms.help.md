This form is designed to gather information from customers to place an order for a custom cake at a bakery. The form is divided into various sections, each collecting different details about the order, such as customer information, cake preferences, and delivery details. To complete this form, customers will need to provide their name, email address, phone number, order date, pickup date, and preferred pickup time. They will also need to choose the cake flavor, size, frosting type, and frosting flavor. Additionally, they can select the number of layers, filling choice, design style, and special decorations. For delivery options, customers can choose to pick up their cake, have it delivered locally, or have it delivered to an event with setup. They will also need to select a payment method and can provide additional notes about their order.

## Cake Factory Order Form - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose the date you would like to order your cake and the date you would like to pick it up.
3. Select your preferred cake flavor, size, and frosting type.
4. Choose the number of layers and filling you would like in your cake.
5. Select a design style for your cake.
6. If you have any special requests, such as writing a message on the cake or requesting special decorations, please include them in the "Additional Notes" field.
7. Choose your preferred delivery option and provide any additional information needed.
8. Select your payment method.
9. If you are ordering a custom cake, please indicate if you have paid a deposit.
10. Finally, provide any additional notes or comments about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name for order purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for order questions.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Choose the date you would like to order your cake.
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, required): Choose the date you would like to pick up your cake.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, optional): If you have a preferred pickup time, please enter it here.
* **Cake Flavor** (`cake_flavor`, select_one, required): Choose the flavor of cake you would like to order.
* **Cake Size** (`cake_size`, select_one, required): Choose the size of cake you would like to order.
* **Frosting Type** (`frosting_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of frosting you would like on your cake.
* **Frosting Flavor** (`frosting_flavor`, select_one, required): Choose the flavor of frosting you would like on your cake.
* **Number of Layers** (`number_of_layers`, select_one, optional): If you would like more than one layer, please select the number of layers you would like.
* **Filling Choice** (`filling_choice`, select_one, optional): If you would like a filling between your cake layers, please select one from the options.
* **Design Style** (`design_style`, select_one, required): Choose the style of decoration you would like on your cake.
* **Cake Message** (`cake_message`, text, optional): If you would like to write a message on your cake, please enter it here.
* **Color Scheme** (`color_scheme`, text, optional): If you have a preferred color scheme for your cake, please enter it here.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, select_one, required): If you have any dietary requirements, such as gluten-free or dairy-free, please select one from the options.
* **Special Decorations** (`special_decorations`, text, optional): If you have any special decoration requests, please describe them here.
* **Occasion** (`occasion`, select_one, required): Choose the occasion for which you are ordering the cake.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of cakes you would like to order.
* **Delivery Option** (`delivery_option`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to receive your cake.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): If you would like to have your cake delivered, please enter your delivery address here.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to pay for your cake.
* **Deposit Paid** (`deposit_paid`, select_one, optional): If you have paid a deposit for your custom cake, please select one from the options.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions about your order, please enter them here.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in completely and accurately.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the "Additional Notes" field.
* If you are unsure about any of the options or have questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
