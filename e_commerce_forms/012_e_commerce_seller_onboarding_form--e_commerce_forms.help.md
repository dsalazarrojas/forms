# E Commerce Seller Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about you as an e-commerce seller to help you set up your account and start selling on our platform. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth and efficient onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields accurately and completely, providing all required and optional information.
2. Make sure to enter your correct and up-to-date contact details, including email and phone number.
3. Provide a detailed list of your products and their descriptions.
4. Set your payment terms and time zone accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's official name as it appears on your business cards or marketing materials.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your company's physical address where you receive packages and correspondence.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your company's zip or postal code as per your address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that is checked frequently for important updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your company's phone number where you can be reached for inquiries and communication.
* **Product List** (`product_list`, text, required): List all the products you want to sell on the platform, separated by commas or line breaks.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of each product, including its features and benefits.
* **Shipping Cost** (`shipping_cost`, number, required): Enter the average shipping cost for each order.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_one, required): Choose how you want to handle payments, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Other Payment Terms** (`payment_terms_other`, text, optional): If you have any other payment terms, enter them here.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, time, required): Select your time zone to ensure smooth communication and coordination.
* **Seller Information** (`seller_info`, text, required): Enter a short introduction about your company and yourself as a seller.
* **Name** (`seller_info_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a seller and representative of your company.
* **Email** (`seller_info_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that is checked frequently for important updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`seller_info_phone`, text, required): Enter your personal phone number where you can be reached for inquiries and communication.

Note: Some fields are optional, but it's best to fill them out to ensure a complete and thorough onboarding process.
