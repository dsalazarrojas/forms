# Holiday Collection Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Collection Order Form is designed to allow customers to place orders for a holiday collection. This form collects necessary information about the customer's order, shipping details, and order notes to ensure accurate and timely fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your holiday collection order details in the **order_details** field. This field is where you can enter any specific information about your order.
2. Enter your shipping address in the **shipping_address**, **shipping_city**, **shipping_state**, and **shipping_postal_code** fields. This will help us to accurately calculate shipping costs and ensure timely delivery.
3. Select your country of residence in the **shipping_country** field.
4. If you have any additional notes about your order, you can enter them in the **order_notes** field.
5. Enter your **customer_name** to associate this order with your account.
6. Enter your **email** address to receive updates and information about your order.
7. Finally, enter your **phone** number in case we need to contact you regarding your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Holiday Collection Order Form** (`holiday_collection_order_form`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter any specific information about your holiday collection order.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Enter any specific information about your order, such as gift notes or special instructions.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your address where you would like the holiday collection to be delivered.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you would like the shipment to be delivered.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, text, optional): Enter the state where you would like the shipment to be delivered.
* **Shipping Postal Code** (`shipping_postal_code`, text, optional): Enter the postal/zip code where you would like the shipment to be delivered.
* **Shipping Country** (`shipping_country`, text, optional): Select the country where you would like the shipment to be delivered.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about your order.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name to associate this order with your account.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and information about your order.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you regarding your order.
