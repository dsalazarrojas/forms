# Seasonal Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information for seasonal product orders, allowing customers to select products, provide order details, and input shipping information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one or more products from the product selection dropdown menu.
2. Enter the customer name in the text field.
3. Enter the customer email address in the email field.
4. Enter the customer phone number in the text field.
5. Add any additional order comments in the text field.
6. Enter the shipping address in the text field.
7. Enter the city in the text field.
8. Enter the state in the text field.
9. Enter the zip code in the text field.
10. Choose the order date in the date field.
11. Choose the order time in the time field.
12. Once all fields have been filled out, click the submit button to submit the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Product (product_selection)**: Select one or more products to be ordered. This field allows you to choose the products you wish to order from the available options.
* **Quantity (quantity)**: Enter the quantity of the product(s) selected.
* **Customer Name (customer_name)**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Email (customer_email)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone (customer_phone)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Order Comments (order_comments)**: Add any additional comments about the order.
* **Shipping Address (shipping_address)**: Enter the address where the order will be shipped.
* **City (shipping_city)**: Enter the city where the order will be shipped.
* **State (shipping_state)**: Enter the state where the order will be shipped.
* **Zip (shipping_zip)**: Enter the zip code where the order will be shipped.
* **Order Date (order_date)**: Choose the date for the order.
* **Order Time (order_time)**: Choose the time for the order.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all relevant products before submitting the form.
* Double-check all entered information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to contact our customer support team.
