# Custom Dessert Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Custom Dessert Order Form is designed to capture information from customers for custom cake orders. This form collects personal contact information and details about the order, including cake type, design, and color preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select a date for your order in the "Order Date" field.
6. Select a time for your order in the "Order Time" field.
7. Enter any additional details about your order in the "Order Details" field.
8. Choose whether you would like to have a cake ("Cake Type") and specify its design ("Cake Design") and color ("Cake Color").
9. Enter any custom text for your cake, if desired, in the "Custom Text" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Select a date for your order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Select a time for your order.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, note, optional): Enter any additional details about your order.
* **Cake Type** (`cake_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether you would like to have a cake.
* **Cake Design** (`cake_design`, text, optional): Specify the design of your cake.
* **Cake Color** (`cake_color`, select_one, optional): Choose the color of your cake.
* **Custom Text** (`custom_text`, text, optional): Enter any custom text for your cake, if desired.

## Tips
- Double-check your email address for accuracy, as this is a required field.
- Be sure to choose a specific date and time for your order to ensure accurate processing.
- If you have any special requests or questions, feel free to include them in the "Order Details" field.
- If you would like to have a cake, select the "Cake Type" options to confirm.
- If you have a specific design in mind, specify it in the "Cake Design" field.
