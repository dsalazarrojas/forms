# Liquor Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Liquor Order Form is a crucial tool for sales and customer service teams to collect necessary information from customers before fulfilling their liquor orders. This form aims to provide accurate and efficient communication with customers, ensuring timely and correct order fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the type of liquor you would like to order from the options provided.
2. Enter the desired quantity of the selected liquor.
3. Choose a date and time for your order.
4. Enter your name and contact details for efficient communication.
5. Add any additional notes for the sales team to understand your requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Liquor Type** (`liquor_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of liquor you would like to order, such as Vodka, Whiskey, Rum, Gin, or Tequila.

- **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the number of bottles or units of the selected liquor you would like to order.

- **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required: false): Choose the date you would like to have your order fulfilled.

- **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required: false): Select the time of day you would like your order to be fulfilled.

- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name to be used for communication purposes.

- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for efficient communication with our team.

- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for direct contact.

- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Provide any additional information or special requests for your order.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately for efficient order fulfillment.
* Be as specific as possible when selecting the type of liquor and order details for accurate processing.
* If you have any special requests or instructions, do not forget to add them in the "Notes" field.
