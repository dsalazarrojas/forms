# Diamond Jewelry Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diamond Jewelry Order Form is used by sales representatives to collect and store information related to diamond jewelry sales and orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill in this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your **Shipping Address** in the "Shipping Address" field.
3. Provide your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field. Please use a valid email format (e.g., name@domain.com).
5. Select your preferred **Payment Method** from the options provided in the "Payment Method" field.
6. Enter any additional **Payment Details** in the "Payment Details" field.
7. If required for the sale, fill in your **Date of Birth** in the "Date of Birth" field.
8. Enter the **Time of Visit** for scheduling purposes in the "Time of Visit" field.
9. Add any relevant **Notes** for the sales representative or order coordinator in the "Notes" field.
10. Select the sales representative to **Assign To** from the options provided in the "Assigned To" field.
11. Review and ensure your **Form ID** is correct, as this is a tracking number for the order.
12. Finally, review the form to ensure all fields are filled in correctly before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the customer's shipping address.
* **Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method for the order (Credit Card, Cash, or PayPal).
* **Payment Details**: Enter any additional payment specifics or notes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the customer's date of birth (if required for age verification or customer profile creation).
* **Time of Visit**: Enter the time of visit for scheduling purposes.
* **Notes**: Add any relevant notes for the sales representative or order coordinator.
* **Assigned To**: Select the sales representative to assign the order to.
* **Form ID**: Review and ensure the correct form ID is selected.
* **Created At**: This field is automatically populated and shows the timestamp when the form was created.
