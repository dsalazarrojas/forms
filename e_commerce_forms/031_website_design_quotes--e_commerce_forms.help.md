# website_design_quotes - Help Guide
## Purpose
The website_design_quotes form is used to collect contact information and design quote requests from website visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Write a brief description of your design quote request in the "Quote" field.
4. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
5. If you have a company, please enter its name in the "Company" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: A text field where you can enter your full name.
* **Phone**: A text field where you can enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Quote**: A text field where you can write a brief description of your design quote request.
* **Email**: A field where you can enter your email address.
* **Company**: A text field where you can enter the name of your company if applicable.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate contact information will help us respond to your design quote request more efficiently.
