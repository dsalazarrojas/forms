# Beauty Supplies Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for an online order of beauty supplies, including various product categories and order details. The form is intended for both personal and business orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your business name and professional license number if applicable.
3. Select the primary category of products you are ordering, such as hair care, styling, nail care, or tools and equipment.
4. Choose the specific products needed within each category.
5. Select the shipping method and payment method for your order.
6. Enter the purchase order number if paying by purchase order.
7. Provide special instructions for shipping, if needed.
8. Confirm your terms and conditions acceptance.
9. Acknowledge the return policy.
10. Enter the estimated order total and the date of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your legal name or business name as it appears on your identification or business license. (<name>, <type>: text, required)
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email address. (<name>, <type>: email, required)
* **Phone Number**: Enter the best number to reach you. (<name>, <type>: text, required)
* **Business or Salon Name**: Enter your business or salon name if applicable. (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
* **Professional License Number**: Enter your cosmetology or business license number if applicable. (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your full street address for delivery. (<name>, <type>: text, required)
* **Billing Address**: Enter your billing address if different from shipping. (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
* **Order Category**: Choose the primary category of products you are ordering. (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
* **Hair Care Items**: Select the hair care products needed. (<name>, <type>: select_multiple, optional)
* **Styling Products**: Select the styling products needed. (<name>, <type>: select_multiple, optional)
* **Hair Color Products**: Select the hair color products needed. (<name>, <type>: select_multiple, optional)
* **Nail Care Products**: Select the nail care products needed. (<name>, <type>: select_multiple, optional)
* **Tools and Equipment**: Select the tools and equipment needed. (<name>, <type>: select_multiple, optional)
* **Salon Furniture**: Select the salon furniture needed. (<name>, <type>: select_multiple, optional)
* **Sanitation Supplies**: Select the sanitation supplies needed. (<name>, <type>: select_multiple, optional)
* **Mixed Order**: Select the mixed order options. (<name>, <type>: select_multiple, optional)
* **Quantity Discount Tier**: Select the discount tier based on the order total. (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
* **Shipping Method**: Select the shipping speed. (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
* **Payment Method**: Select how you will pay. (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
* **Purchase Order Number**: Enter your purchase order number if paying by PO. (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any special delivery instructions. (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
* **Order Notes**: Enter any additional information about your order. (<name>, <type>: text, optional)
* **Estimated Order Total**: Enter the approximate total before shipping. (<name>, <type>: number, required)
* **Terms and Conditions Acceptance**: Confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions. (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
* **Return Policy Acknowledgment**: Confirm your understanding of the return policy. (<name>, <type>: select_one, required)
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of your order. (<name>, <type>: date, required)

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate contact information to ensure smooth communication and delivery.
- Double-check your product selections to avoid errors.
- Review your order total and shipping method before confirming.
- Read and agree to the terms and conditions to ensure understanding of policies and procedures.
- Familiarize yourself with the return policy to avoid confusion.
