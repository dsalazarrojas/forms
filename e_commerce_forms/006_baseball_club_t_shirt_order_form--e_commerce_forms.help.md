# Baseball Club T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information for a custom baseball team order. It is intended for teams to provide their team information, design preferences, and order details. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form to start the order process.
2. Enter your team name in the "Team name" field.
3. Enter the contact person's name in the "Contact person name" field.
4. Enter the contact person's email address in the "Contact email" field.
5. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Contact phone number" field.
6. Select the desired shirt color in the "Shirt color preference" field.
7. Choose the design type in the "Design type" field.
8. If you have a logo or design file, select "Yes - File Attached" or "Yes - Will Provide Later".
9. Enter any additional design notes or specifications in the "Additional design notes or specifications" field.
10. Select the total number of shirts needed in the "Total number of shirts needed" field.
11. Provide a size breakdown for each shirt size in the "Size breakdown" field.
12. Choose the shirt style preference in the "Shirt style preference" field.
13. If desired, add player names or numbers on the back in the "Do you want player names/numbers on back?" field.
14. Choose the preferred printing method in the "Preferred printing method" field.
15. Enter the requested delivery date in the "Requested delivery date" field.
16. Enter the delivery address in the "Delivery address" field.
17. Choose an approximate budget range in the "Approximate budget per shirt" field.
18. Finally, confirm the order information in the "I confirm this order information is accurate" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team name** (Team name, Text, Required): Enter your team's name as it appears in official records.
* **Contact person name** (Contact person name, Text, Required): Enter the name of the person who will be coordinating the order.
* **Contact email** (Contact email, Email, Required): Enter the email address of the person who will be coordinating the order.
* **Contact phone number** (Contact phone number, Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the person who will be coordinating the order.
* **Shirt color preference** (Shirt color preference, Select One, Required): Choose the desired color for the shirts.
* **Design type** (Design type, Select One, Required): Select the type of design to be printed on the shirts.
* **Do you have a logo or design file?** (Do you have a logo or design file?, Select One, Required): If you have a logo or design file, select the corresponding option.
* **Additional design notes or specifications** (Additional design notes or specifications, Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes or specifications for the design.
* **Total number of shirts needed** (Total number of shirts needed, Number, Required): Enter the total number of shirts needed.
* **Size breakdown** (Size breakdown, Text, Required): Provide a breakdown of the desired shirt sizes (e.g., 2XL, 3XL, etc.).
* **Shirt style preference** (Shirt style preference, Select One, Optional): Select the desired shirt style.
* **Do you want player names/numbers on back?** (Do you want player names/numbers on back?, Select One, Optional): If desired, select this option.
* **Preferred printing method** (Preferred printing method, Select One, Optional): Choose the preferred printing method.
* **Requested delivery date** (Requested delivery date, Date, Required): Enter the date you would like to receive the order.
* **Delivery address** (Delivery address, Text, Required): Enter the delivery address for the order.
* **Approximate budget per shirt** (Approximate budget per shirt, Select One, Optional): Choose an approximate budget range for the order.
* **I confirm this order information is accurate** (I confirm this order information is accurate, Select One, Required): Confirm the order information before submitting the form.
