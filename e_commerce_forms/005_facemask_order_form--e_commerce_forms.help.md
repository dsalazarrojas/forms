# Facemask Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facemask Order Form is designed to help customers place an order for facemasks online. It is used to collect relevant information required for processing the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your contact information in the first field, including your name and any other relevant contact details.
2. Provide delivery details, such as the date and time you would like the facemask to be delivered.
3. Add any payment instructions in the third field.
4. Select your preferred payment method from the drop-down menu (Credit, Debit, PayPal, or Other).
5. Enter the total amount of the order in the next field.
6. Add any additional payment instructions, if necessary.
7. Enter any customer comments you would like to add to the order.
8. Select the method of delivery (Pickup, Delivery, or Shipping).
9. Confirm the payment status of the order (Paid, Unpaid, or Pending).
10. Submit the form to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to provide your contact details, such as your name and email address, so we can get in touch with you to confirm your order.
- **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, required/optional): Here, you can provide the date and time you would like to receive your order.
- **Payment Instructions** (`payment_instructions`, text, required/optional): This field is for any special payment instructions or details you may need to provide for your order.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to pay for your order: Credit, Debit, PayPal, or Other.
- **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, number, required): Enter the total amount for the order, including tax and shipping costs.
- **Payment Instructions 2** (`payment_instructions`, text, required/optional): If you need to add any additional payment information, you can enter it in this field.
- **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, text, required/optional): If you have any comments or requests about your order, you can add them here.
- **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_multiple, required): Select how you would like to receive your order: Pickup, Delivery, or Shipping.
- **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Confirm the payment status of your order: Paid, Unpaid, or Pending.
