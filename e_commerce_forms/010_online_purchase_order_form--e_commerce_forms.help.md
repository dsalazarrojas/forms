# online_purchase_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for online purchases, including customer details and order status.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "customer-name" field.
2. Provide the customer's email address in the "email-address" field.
3. Select the order date from the calendar or manually enter it in the "order-date" field.
4. Choose the order time from the dropdown menu or manually enter it in the "order-time" field.
5. For "product-name", select the relevant options from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter the quantity of the product(s) in the "quantity" field.
7. Enter the price of the order in the "price" field.
8. Add any additional notes about the order in the "order-notes" field.
9. Select the shipping address from the dropdown menu in the "shipping-address" field.
10. Enter the shipping city in the "shipping-city" field.
11. Select the shipping state from the dropdown menu in the "shipping-state" field.
12. Enter the shipping zip code in the "shipping-zip" field.
13. Select the shipping country from the dropdown menu in the "shipping-country" field.
14. Choose the payment method from the dropdown menu in the "payment-method" field.
15. Confirm the order in the "confirm" field.
16. Enter the customer's email address in the "customer-email" field.
17. Enter the customer's phone number in the "customer-phone" field.
18. Enter the customer's zip code in the "customer-zip" field.
19. Select the customer address from the dropdown menu in the "customer-address" field.
20. Select the order status from the dropdown menu in the "order-status" field.
21. Enter the customer's message in the "customer-message" field.
22. Select whether the submission is "Yes" or "No" in the "form-submission" field.
23. Select the submission status from the dropdown menu in the "submission-status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer-name (Text, Required)**: Enter the customer's name.
* **email-address (Email, Required)**: Provide the customer's email address.
* **order-date (Date, Required)**: Select or enter the order date.
* **order-time (Time, Required)**: Choose or enter the order time.
* **product-name (Select Multiple, Required)**: Select the relevant options for the product name.
* **quantity (Number, Not Required)**: Enter the quantity of the product.
* **price (Number, Required)**: Enter the price of the order.
* **order-notes (Text, Not Required)**: Add any additional notes about the order.
* **shipping-address (Select One, Not Required)**: Select the shipping address from the dropdown menu.
* **shipping-city (Text, Not Required)**: Enter the shipping city.
* **shipping-state (Select One, Not Required)**: Select the shipping state.
* **shipping-zip (Text, Not Required)**: Enter the shipping zip code.
* **shipping-country (Select One, Not Required)**: Select the shipping country from the dropdown menu.
* **payment-method (Select One, Required)**: Choose the payment method from the dropdown menu.
* **confirm (Select One, Required)**: Confirm the order.
* **customer-email (Email, Not Required)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **customer-phone (Text, Not Required)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **customer-zip (Text, Not Required)**: Enter the customer's zip code.
* **customer-address (Select One, Not Required)**: Select the customer address from the dropdown menu.
* **order-status (Select One, Required)**: Select the order status from the dropdown menu.
* **order-notes (Text, Not Required)**: Enter the customer's message.
* **customer-message (Text, Not Required)**: Select whether the submission is "Yes" or "No".
* **form-submission (Select Multiple, Not Required)**: Select the submission status from the dropdown menu.
