# Cross Marketplace Listing Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for listing products on a cross-marketplace platform. It requires you to provide detailed information about your product, including its category, name, description, image, price, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of your product from the list of options.
2. Enter the name and description of your product in the respective fields.
3. Upload an image of the product.
4. Enter the price of the product.
5. Select the status of the product from the three options.
6. Input the URL of your product's page.
7. (Optional) Provide a label for the URL.
8. (Optional) Enter additional information about the URL.
9. (Optional) Provide an alternative text for the product's image.
10. (Optional) Link to a video of the product.
11. (Optional) Enter the physical dimensions of the product (weight, width, height).
12. (Optional) Select the unit of measurement for the product's weight.
13. (Optional) Enter notes about the product's status.
