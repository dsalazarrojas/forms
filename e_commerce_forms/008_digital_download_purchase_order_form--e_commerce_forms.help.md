# digital_download_purchase_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The digital download purchase order form is used to collect information from customers to complete a purchase order for a digital product. This form is essential for ensuring accurate and secure payment processing, as well as providing the correct product delivery details to our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Customer Information**: Fill in the form with your details, including your name and contact information. This information will be used for communication and order processing.
2. **Select Your Product**: Choose the digital product you wish to purchase from the options provided. Please select only one product to avoid any confusion.
3. **Provide Shipping Details**: Enter your shipping address and any other relevant information for delivery.
4. **Confirm and Complete Order**: Double-check all the details and click the confirmation button to submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**: <label>Customer Information</label> (id: 1, **customer_info**, required: false, type: text)
	+ Enter your name and contact information to help us process your order efficiently.
* **Select Product**: <label>Select Product</label> (id: 2, **product_selection**, required: true, type: select_one)
	+ Choose the digital product you want to purchase from the available options.
* **Shipping Details**: <label>Shipping Details</label> (id: 3, **shipping_details**, required: false, type: text)
	+ Enter your shipping address and any other relevant information to ensure timely delivery.
* **Payment Information**: <label>Payment Information</label> (id: 4, **payment_info**, required: false, type: text)
	+ Enter your payment details to complete the order.
* **Confirm**: <label>Confirm</label> (id: 5, **confirmation**, required: false, type: text)
	+ Double-check all the details and submit your order to confirm your purchase.

## Tips

* Double-check your product selection to avoid any issues with delivery or payment processing.
* Ensure that you enter accurate and complete contact information to receive updates on your order status.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our support team.
