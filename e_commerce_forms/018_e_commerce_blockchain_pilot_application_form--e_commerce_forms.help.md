# E Commerce Blockchain Pilot Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Commerce Blockchain Pilot Application Form is designed to gather information from businesses and individuals interested in participating in the e-commerce blockchain pilot program. This form helps us understand their needs, products, and goals to better support their integration into the pilot program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*) in the label. 
2. Provide accurate and truthful information in all fields. 
3. Make sure to save your progress frequently to avoid losing any entered data.
4. Once you have filled out all the required fields, submit the form to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your official company email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, not required): Briefly describe your company and your interest in the e-commerce blockchain pilot program.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, not required): Select the category that best describes your company (e-commerce, finance, health, technology).
* **Subcategory** (`subcategory`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all subcategories that apply to your company (e-commerce, finance, health, technology).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date you are applying for the pilot program.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, not required): Enter the time you are applying for the pilot program.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Company URL** (`company_url`, `text`, not required): Enter the URL of your company's website.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, not required): Enter your company's location.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, not required): Enter the name and title of the contact person for your company.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, not required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Application Reason** (`application_reason`, `text`, not required): Briefly describe why you are applying for the e-commerce blockchain pilot program.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of your business.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of your product or service.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, `text`, not required): Briefly describe your product or service.
* **Application Status** (`application_status`, `select_one`, not required): Select the status of your application (open, in progress, pending, closed).
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, `text`, not required): Add any comments or feedback about the review process.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, `select_one`, not required): Select the status of your review (incomplete, complete, in progress).
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the review.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, `time`, not required): Enter the time of the review.
* **Review Notes** (`review_note`, `note`, not required): Add any notes or comments about the review.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the person or team assigned to review your application. 

Note that all fields marked as `required` in the form are not actually required in the help guide, because the original YAML does not require them to be filled.
