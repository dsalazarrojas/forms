# Online Retail Platform Usability Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Retail Platform Usability Evaluation Form is designed to assess the usability and user experience of our online retail platform. We want to hear from you about your experience using our platform, including your impressions of the product information page, search function, checkout process, and overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each field according to your experience on our platform. You can select all that apply from the multiple select fields.
2. If you have any comments or feedback, please provide them in the "Comments" field.
3. If you're willing, you can optionally provide your email address, phone number, and website URL for follow-up contact.
4. Click the "Submit" button to send your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Online Retail Platform** (Text): Briefly describe your experience using our online retail platform. What did you like or dislike about it?
* **2. Product Information** (Select Multiple: Good, Bad, Not Sure): Rate the quality of the product information provided on our platform. Select all that apply.
* **3. Product Page** (Select One: Good, Bad, Not Sure): Rate the quality of the product information page on our platform.
* **4. Search Experience** (Text): How easy was it to find what you needed on our platform? Describe your experience.
* **5. Checkout Process** (Number): How easy was the checkout process? Rate on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is very difficult and 10 is very easy.
* **6. Product Information Page** (Note): If you have any thoughts about the product information page, please describe them here.
* **7. Contact Us** (Email): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, provide your email address here. (Optional)
* **8. Phone Number** (Text): If you'd like to be contacted via phone, provide your phone number here. (Optional)
* **9. Website URL** (Text): If you'd like to be contacted via website, provide your website URL here. (Optional)
* **10. Rating** (Number): Rate your overall experience on our platform on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is very bad and 5 is very good.
* **11. Comments** (Note): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them here.
* **12. Form Submission** (Date): Your form submission date will be automatically recorded.
* **13. Form Completion Time** (Time): The time it took you to complete this form will be automatically recorded.
