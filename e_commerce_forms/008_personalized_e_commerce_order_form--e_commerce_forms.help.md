# Personalized E-commerce Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather customer information, order details, payment methods, and product specifics for an e-commerce order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Customer Information** section (Page 1) with your details as required.
2. Proceed to **Order Details** (Page 2) and provide as much information as possible about your order.
3. On **Payment Method** (Page 3), select your preferred payment method from the available options.
4. Finally, in **Product Information** (Page 4), enter any product-specific details that might be required for processing your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (Page 1)**:
  • **customer_info** (Text): Enter your name, contact number, and other relevant details that you would like to share with our team.
* **Order Details (Page 2)**:
  • **order_details** (Text): Provide as much information as possible about your order, including product name, quantity, and any specific requests or instructions.
* **Payment Method (Page 3)**:
  • **payment_method** (Select One): Choose from **Credit card**, **PayPal**, or **Bank transfer** as your preferred payment method.
* **Product Information (Page 4)**:
  • **product_info** (Text): Enter any product-specific details that might be required for processing your order, such as product name, size, or color.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill out each field completely and accurately for smooth order processing.
- If you have any specific requests or questions, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
- Our team will be in touch with you to confirm your order details and complete the payment process.
