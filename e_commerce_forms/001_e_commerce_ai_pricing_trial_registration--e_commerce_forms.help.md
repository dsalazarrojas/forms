# E Commerce AI Pricing Trial Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from merchants interested in trying AI-generated pricing for their e-commerce business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for the "Trial Signup Form" section.
2. Provide details about your merchant business in the "Merchant Info" section.
3. Answer the questions in the "Review Form" section.
4. Enter information about your merchant's pricing and trial details in the "Merchant Pricing" and "Merchant Trial Details" sections.
5. Fill out the "SaaS Info" and "Review Details" sections with relevant information.
6. Update your trial status in the "Trial Status" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trial Signup Form**: Enter your email address, name, and other details to sign up for the trial.
* **Merchant Info**: Provide a brief description of your e-commerce business, including your business name, website, and type (e.g., B2B, B2C, etc.).
* **Review Form**: Answer questions about your business, such as what products/services you offer, your target audience, and your business goals.
* **Merchant Pricing**: Enter information about your current pricing strategy, including your pricing model and any discounts or promotions you have in place.
* **Merchant Trial Details**: Describe the specific features or services you are interested in testing with AI-generated pricing.
* **SaaS Info**: Provide details about your Software as a Service (SaaS) product, including its main features and benefits.
* **Review Details**: Share any specific review or rating you received from customers or partners.
* **Trial Status**: Update your trial status, such as whether you have completed the trial or if you are waiting for further instructions.

# Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields for a smooth and efficient trial experience.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing information about your business and pricing strategy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
