# Adult Product Same Day Delivery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who wish to place an order for adult products and receive them on the same day. It is designed to gather necessary customer information and order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Enter your Full Name**: Type your full legal name in the "Full Name" field.
2. **Enter your Email Address**: Provide a valid email address for order confirmation.
3. **Enter your Phone Number**: Provide your contact phone number for delivery updates.
4. **Enter Delivery Address**: Complete the address where you would like the order to be delivered.
5. **Enter Apartment/Suite Number (if applicable)**: If you have an apartment or suite, please enter the number.
6. **Choose Your Product Selection**: Select the adult products you wish to order from the options provided.
7. **Specify Quantity Requested**: Enter the number of units for each selected product.
8. **Choose Preferred Delivery Time**: Select when you would like to receive your order.
9. **Choose Payment Method**: Select how you would like to pay for your order.
10. **Verify Your Age**: Confirm that you are 18 years or older.
11. **Enter Special Requests (if any)**: If you have any additional requests, type them in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (Customer Full Name)** (`customer_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Email Address (Email Address)** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Provide a valid email address for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Provide your contact phone number for delivery updates.
* **Delivery Address (Delivery Address)** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required): Complete the address where you would like the order to be delivered.
* **Apartment/Suite Number (Apartment/Suite)** (`apartment_suite`, `text`, optional): If you have an apartment or suite, please enter the number.
* **Delivery Instructions (Delivery Instructions)** (`delivery_instructions`, `text`, optional): Special instructions for delivery driver.
* **Product Selection (Product Selection)** (`product_selection`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the adult products you wish to order from the options provided.
* **Quantity Requested (Quantity Requested)** (`quantity_requested`, `number`, required): Enter the number of units for each selected product.
* **Preferred Delivery Time (Preferred Delivery Time)** (`preferred_delivery_time`, `select_one`, required): Select when you would like to receive your order.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to pay for your order.
* **Age Verification (Age Verification)** (`age_verification`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you are 18 years or older.
* **Special Requests (Special Requests)** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional requests, type them in this field.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter all required fields to ensure accurate processing of your order.
* If you have any special requests, please make sure to include them in the "Special Requests" field.
* If you select multiple products, make sure to enter the correct quantity for each product.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
