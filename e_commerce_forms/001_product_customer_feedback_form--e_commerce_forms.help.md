# product_customer_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Customer Feedback Form is a means for customers to share their experiences, provide product ratings, and contact information with us. This form is optional and serves to gather valuable feedback to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information.
2. Please ensure that you fill out all required fields (if applicable) to submit the form correctly.
3. You can access the form through the e-commerce module.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Feedback** (`product_customer_feedback`, Text, Required: False): This is the main area where you can provide detailed feedback about your experience with the product.
* **Rate Product** (`rate_product`, Number, Required: False): Please provide a rating for the product, using a numerical scale (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
* **Product Comments** (`product_comments`, Text, Required: False): If you have any comments or thoughts about your experience, please share them here.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, Required: False): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for future correspondence.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone_number`, Text, Required: False): Enter a valid phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Recommend This Product?** (`product_recommendation`, Select One, Required: False):
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Submit** (`submit`, Text, Required: False): Once you have filled out all required fields, click this button to submit your feedback.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, Text, Required: False): If you're providing feedback for a specific product, please enter its name here.
* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, Date, Required: False): Enter the date of your visit or purchase.
* **Feedback Time** (`feedback_time`, Time, Required: False): Enter the time of your visit or purchase.

## Tips
* Please be honest and detailed in your feedback to help us improve our services.
* Ensure that you fill out all required fields to submit the form correctly.
* If you need help or have any concerns, please contact us for assistance.
