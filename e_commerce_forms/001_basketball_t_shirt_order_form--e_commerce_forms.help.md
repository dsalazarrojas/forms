# Basketball T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Basketball T Shirt Order Form is a tool for customers to order custom basketball T-shirts. It gathers necessary information for order fulfillment, including customer details, shipping information, and payment options. By following this guide, users can complete the form quickly and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill in Your Order Information**: Complete the fields with your name, last name, and email address in the "Order Information" section.
2. **Enter Shipping Details**: Fill in your shipping address, city, and state in the "Shipping Address" field. Include your ZIP code for accurate delivery.
3. **Choose Your T-Shirt Details**: Select your preferred T-shirt size and color from the dropdown menus.
4. **Add Any Custom Touches**: If you'd like to add a team name or number to your T-shirt, provide the information in the "Team Name" field. You can also choose to add a player number or not.
5. **Specify Your Payment Method**: Select how you'd like to pay for your order from the payment method dropdown menu.
6. **Confirm Your Order**: Check that your shipping and payment information is accurate, and confirm you've read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
7. **Submit Your Order**: Review your order and click submit to place your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name to help us get your order right.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name to complete your order information.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address associated with your order.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for any follow-up questions or updates.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your address where you'd like to receive your order.
* **City**: Enter the city where you'd like to receive your order.
* **State/Province**: Enter the state or province where you'd like to receive your order.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter the ZIP code where you'd like to receive your order.
* **T-Shirt Size**: Select your preferred T-shirt size from the list.
* **Color**: Select your preferred T-shirt color from the list.
* **Team Name**: If you'd like to add a team name to your T-shirt, enter it here.
* **Add Player Number**: Choose whether you'd like to add a player number to your T-shirt.
* **Player Number**: If you've chosen to add a player number, enter the number here.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you'd like to pay for your order from the available options.
* **Billing Address Same as Shipping**: Choose whether your billing and shipping addresses are the same.
* **I Agree to the Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you've read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any special requests or instructions for your order.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your order details before submitting for accurate fulfillment.
* If you have any special requests, include them in the "Special Instructions" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us through the provided contact information.
