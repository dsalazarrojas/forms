# Shipping Discount Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shipping Discount Registration Form is designed to collect information about your business and its shipping details, allowing you to register for shipping discounts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your business information.
2. Select your business type (Wholesale, Retail, or Other).
3. Enter your contact number.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your website (if applicable).
6. Enter your address.
7. Provide additional information about your shipping details.
8. Enter your shipping discount (a numerical value).
9. Enter the discount percentage (a numerical value).
10. Enter the discount amount (a numerical value).
11. Decide whether to have follow-up communications with our company.
12. Enter any additional information you'd like to provide.
13. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Information** (1): Enter a brief description of your business.
* **Business Type** (2): Choose the type of business you are (Wholesale, Retail, or Other).
* **Contact Number** (3): Enter the number that customers can contact you at.
* **Email** (4): Enter your business's email address.
* **Website** (5): Enter your website URL if you have one.
* **Address** (6): Enter your business's physical address.
* **Shipping Details** (7): Enter any additional information about your shipping details.
* **Shipping Discount** (8): Enter the numerical value of your shipping discount.
* **Discount Percentage** (9): Enter the percentage of your shipping discount.
* **Discount Amount** (10): Enter the amount of your shipping discount.
* **Follow-up** (11): Decide whether to have follow-up communications with our company (True or False).
* **Additional Information** (12): Enter any additional information you'd like to provide.
* **Submit** (13): Once you've filled out all the required fields, click "Submit" to register your business.
