# Online Coffee Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place an order for coffee online. It collects contact information, delivery details, payment method, and order preferences to fulfill the order efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To place an order, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the customer details section, providing your name and any other relevant contact information.
2.  Select your preferred type of coffee from the dropdown menu.
3.  Enter your contact details, including your phone number and email address.
4.  Choose a delivery date and time that suits your schedule.
5.  Select a payment method that is convenient for you.
6.  Choose your order size from the available options (large, medium, small).
7.  If you have any extra comments or requests, type them in the "note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Details (customer_details)
• **customer_details** (text field): Enter your name, email address, and phone number to help us contact you regarding your order.

### Preferred Coffee (preferred_coffee)
• **preferred_coffee** (select_one field): Choose your preferred type of coffee (Coffee Latte, Coffee Cappuccino, or Coffee Espresso).

### Contact Details (contact_details)
• **contact_details** (text field): Enter your address or any other contact information if you have it.

### Contact Name (contact_name)
• **contact_name** (text field): Enter your name if you want to be contacted under a different name.

### Contact Email (contact_email)
• **contact_email** (email field): Enter your email address so we can reach you about your order.

### Contact Phone (contact_phone)
• **contact_phone** (text field): Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you efficiently.

### Delivery Date and Time (delivery_date_time)
• **delivery_date_time** (date and time field): Choose a delivery date and time that suits your schedule.

### Delivery Time (delivery_time)
• **delivery_time** (time field): Select a specific time for your delivery.

### Note (note)
• **note** (text field): Enter any extra comments or requests you may have.

### Confirm (confirm)
• **confirm** (text field): This field is optional and can be used to confirm your order details. (Note: This field can be omitted if not needed.)

### Payment Method (payment_method)
• **payment_method** (select_one field): Choose your preferred payment method (Credit card, Cash, or Online transfer).

### Order (order)
• **order** (select_multiple field): Choose your order size (large, medium, small).
