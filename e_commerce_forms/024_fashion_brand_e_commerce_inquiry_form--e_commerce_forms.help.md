# Fashion Brand E Commerce Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to inquire about fashion brand products and services, and submit style requests for potential future releases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the relevant fields.
2. Answer any customer questions that are relevant to your inquiry.
3. Specify your style requests for future products or services.
4. Choose whether you have any contact details that you would like to share with the brand.
5. Optionally, specify a date and time for when you would like to be contacted.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about your inquiry.
7. Click the submit button to send your inquiry to the fashion brand.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where the brand can contact you.
* **Customer Questions** (`customer_questions`, `text`, optional): Answer any questions that are relevant to your inquiry.
* **Customer Style Requests** (`customer_style_requests`, `text`, optional): Specify any style requests you have for future products or services.
* **Customer Style Requests 2-16** (`customer_style_requests2-16`, `text`, optional): More style requests for future products or services.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you have any contact details you would like to share with the brand.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Optionally specify a date for when you would like to be contacted.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Optionally specify a time for when you would like to be contacted.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your inquiry.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you have a phone number you would like to share with the brand.
