# Nail Stripes Invoice Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create an invoice for a nail stripes order. It captures essential details such as order number, customer name, stripe name, order date, and stripe price for the invoice generation process. 

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the `Order Number` field, which is the reference number associated with the order.
2. Enter the `Customer Name` of the person or entity making the order.
3. Enter the `Stripe Name` of the product being ordered.
4. Enter the `Order Date` in the format MM/DD/YYYY when the order was placed.
5. Enter the `Stripe Price` of the product, as a numeric value.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Number** (`Order Number`, number, required/optional: false): Enter the reference number for the order.
* **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, text, required/optional: false): Enter the name of the person or entity making the order.
* **Stripe Name** (`Stripe Name`, text, required/optional: false): Enter the name of the product being ordered.
* **Order Date** (`Order Date`, date, required/optional: false): Enter the date when the order was placed in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Stripe Price** (`Stripe Price`, number, required/optional: false): Enter the price of the product as a numeric value.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information for a correct invoice generation.
* Double-check the fields before submitting the form to avoid mistakes.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your local support team for assistance.
