# Detox Drinks Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Detox Drinks Order Form is designed for customers to provide their order information, including customer details, order specifics, billing address, order notes, payment information, and terms and conditions. This form helps streamline the ordering process, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and accurately recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required information before starting.
2. Fill in the customer information section with your details.
3. Provide order details, such as products or services ordered.
4. Enter your billing address.
5. Add any additional order notes or comments.
6. Confirm your payment information.
7. Agree to the terms and conditions.
8. Review the order summary for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant details.
* **Order Details**: Describe the products or services you wish to order.
* **Billing Address**: Enter your address where you want the order to be delivered.
* **Order Notes**: Share any additional information or comments about your order.
* **Payment Info**: Enter your payment method and details (e.g., credit card number, expiration date).
* **Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the order.
* **Confirmation**: Review the form for completeness and accuracy; this is a sample form for testing purposes.
* **Order Summary**: Review the summary of your order before submitting.

Note: This form is for sample purposes only. Please ensure to fill in all the required fields carefully and accurately. If you're using this form for actual orders, please adjust it according to your needs and requirements.
