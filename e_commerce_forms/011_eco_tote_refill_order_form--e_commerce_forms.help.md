# Eco Tote Refill Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for eco-friendly tote bag refill orders. It is used to gather customer details, product selections, and delivery details to facilitate the order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your **Customer Name** (Field 1) with your personal name or company name.
2. Enter your **Email** (Field 2) address if you'd like to receive updates on your order status.
3. Enter your **Phone** (Field 3) number for contact purposes.
4. Select one or more **Product Selections** (Field 4) from the available options.
5. Provide **Delivery Details** (Field 5) for our team to know where to deliver your order.
6. Read and agree to the **Customer Agreement** (Field 6) terms and conditions.
7. Finally, sign your **Customer Signature** (Field 7) to confirm your order.
8. Our delivery team will need to sign the **Delivery Signature** (Field 8) upon delivery to confirm receipt of your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (Field 1, Text): Enter your personal name or company name.
* **Email** (Field 2, Email): Enter your email address to receive order updates.
* **Phone** (Field 3, Text): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Product Selections** (Field 4, Select Multiple): Select one or more products for your refill order.
* **Delivery Details** (Field 5, Note): Describe your delivery location and any special instructions.
* **Customer Agreement** (Field 6, Text): Read and agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Customer Signature** (Field 7, Text): Sign to confirm your order.
* **Delivery Signature** (Field 8, Text): Our delivery team will sign upon delivery to confirm receipt.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and clearly.
* If you have any special requests or concerns, please use the **Delivery Details** field to communicate them to us.
* If you need further assistance, contact our customer service team for help.

Note: This form is designed to be user-friendly, and our team is available to assist you with any questions or concerns you may have.
