# hemp_extraction_customer_questions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who want to have their hemp extracted. It collects information about the customer's hemp crop, contact details, the type of hemp, quantity, price, and delivery address.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (there are no required fields, so this step is actually optional)
2. Answer each question in a clear and concise manner

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hemp Crop Information (crop_info)**: Enter a brief description of the hemp crop you would like to have extracted.
* **Contact (contact_info)**: Enter your name, email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **What type of hemp (hemp_type)**: Select the type of hemp you have. You can choose from Hemp, Cannabis, CBD, or CBG.
* **Quantity (quantity)**: Enter the amount of hemp you would like to have extracted (optional).
* **Price (price)**: Enter the price you are willing to pay for the extraction (optional).
* **Delivery Address (delivery_address)**: Enter the address where you would like your hemp extract to be delivered.

## Tips
- Be accurate and thorough in your responses to ensure that we can properly process your extraction request.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
