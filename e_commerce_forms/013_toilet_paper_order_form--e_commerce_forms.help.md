# toilet_paper_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information and order details for an e-commerce platform. It's intended to be completed by customers to place orders for a product, potentially toilet paper, through an online channel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information on the first page, which is optional.
2. Enter your order details on the second page, which is also optional.
3. If you have a payment method, enter it on the third page, which is also optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`User Information`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any identifying details you'd like to provide about yourself, such as your name and contact information.
* **Order** (`Order`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the specifics of the order, including product details and any special requests or instructions.
* **Payment** (`Payment`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your payment method details, such as credit card information or other payment methods accepted by the platform.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the platform's support team for help.
- Review your order details carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
