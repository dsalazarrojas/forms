# july_lin_facebook_shopping - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to place orders on your ecommerce site using July Lin Facebook Shopping. Fill out the form to create a new order, providing essential shipping details and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Click on the 'Submit Order' button to start filling out the form.
2. On the first page, you'll see the 'Order Form' section. This is where you'll enter your order details.
3. In the 'Order Form' section, fill in the 'Order Form' field with the required order information.
4. On the second page, enter your 'shipping_address' in the corresponding field.
5. On the second page, enter your 'phone_number' in the corresponding field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form (Order Form, text, required):** Enter the details of the order you'd like to place.
* **shipping_address (shipping_address, text, required):** Enter your shipping address to ensure accurate delivery of your order.
* **phone_number (phone_number, text, required):** Enter your phone number to contact you regarding the order.

## Tips
Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure smooth order processing. If you have any questions or issues, please contact our customer support team.
