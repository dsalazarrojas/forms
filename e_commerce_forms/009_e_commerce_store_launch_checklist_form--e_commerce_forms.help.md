# E Commerce Store Launch Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The E Commerce Store Launch Checklist Form is a comprehensive tool used to collect and track information about your e-commerce store's setup and readiness for launch. This guide will walk you through each field and help you fill out the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what information is required.
2. Fill out the form completely, making sure to include all necessary details.
3. Double-check your responses to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **E commerce store launch** (`1`): A brief description of your e-commerce store launch.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **store info** (`2`): A numerical value representing store information. Please enter a number.
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: No
* **payment details** (`3`): Select the payment method you'll use for your store:
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Paypal, Stripe, Bank transfer
* **shipping details** (`4`): Choose one or more shipping options:
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Options: Free, Flat rate, Weight based
* **store settings** (`5`): Select the platform you're using for your store:
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Shopify, Woo Commerce, Shopify +
* **product info** (`6`): Choose the types of products you'll sell:
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Options: Physical, Digital, Both
* **launch plan** (`7`): Enter the date and time you plan to launch your store.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: No
* **store launch** (`8`): Confirm your store's launch status:
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **store owner** (`9`): Enter your name or the name of the store owner.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **store address** (`10`): Enter your store's address.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No

## Tips
- Double-check your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult with your team or the form creator.
- This form is for informational purposes only and will not directly impact store functionality.
