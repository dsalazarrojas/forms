# yonnired_brand_products - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides an overview of the yonnired_brand_products form, including its purpose, how to complete it, and explanations for each field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name or description of the products being sent or received in the "products" field.
2.  Enter the price of the products in the "price" field.
3.  Provide a brief description of the products or context of the order in the "description" field.
4.  Record the total costs associated with sending the products in the "send_total" field and the total costs associated with receiving the products in the "receive_total" field.
5.  Enter any instructions related to sending the products in the "send_instructions" field and any instructions related to receiving the products in the "receive_instructions" field.
6.  Add any additional details or comments about the order in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **products**: Enter the name or description of the products being sent or received.
*   **price**: Enter the price of the products.
*   **description**: Provide a brief description of the products or context of the order.
*   **send_total**: Record the total costs associated with sending the products.
*   **receive_total**: Record the total costs associated with receiving the products.
*   **send_instructions**: Enter any instructions related to sending the products.
*   **receive_instructions**: Enter any instructions related to receiving the products.
*   **notes**: Add any additional details or comments about the order.
