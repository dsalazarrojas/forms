# GoCardless Magazine Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The GoCardless Magazine Subscription Form is designed to collect your information and payment details for a smooth and secure subscription process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review your submission before submitting the form to ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please fill out your first name in this text field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Please fill out your last name in this text field.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address in the format of `user@example.com`.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Fill out your address in the format of `Street Address, City, State, Zip`.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required: false): Enter your city name.
* **State** (`state`, `select_one`, required: true): Select your state of residence from the list.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, required: false): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select your credit card type from the list.
* **Expiry** (`expiry_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter your card expiry date in the format `MM/YY` or `MM/YY`.
* **CVV2** (`cvv2`, `select_one`, required: true): Select your CVV2 from the list.
* **CVC** (`cvc`, `number`, required: false): Enter your card's CVC in the format `###`.
* **Amount** (`amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount in USD.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select your subscription frequency from the list.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: true): Select your payment method from the list.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, `select_one`, required: true): Select your payment type from the list.
* **Subscription Date** (`subscription_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter your subscription date.
* **Next Payment Date** (`next_payment_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter your next payment date.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required: true): Select your payment status from the list.
* **Subscription Status** (`subscription_status`, `select_one`, required: true): Select your subscription status from the list.
* **Card Brand** (`card_brand`, `select_one`, required: true): Select your card brand from the list.
* **Card Type** (`card_type`, `select_one`, required: true): Select your card type from the list.
* **Bank Account Number** (`bank_account_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your bank account number.
* **Bank Routing Number** (`bank_routing_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your bank routing number.
* **Bank Branch** (`bank_branch`, `text`, required: false): Enter your bank branch.
* **Bank IBAN** (`bank_iban`, `text`, required: false): Enter your bank IBAN.
