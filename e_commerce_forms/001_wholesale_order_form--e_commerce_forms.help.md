# Wholesale Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help businesses or organizations receive and process wholesale orders from their customers. It gathers necessary details about the customer and their product selections to expedite the order fulfillment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's details in the "Customer Details" section, including any relevant contact information.
2. In the "Product Catalog" field, list the products you wish to order.
3. Provide a brief description of your order in the "Order Summary" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details (customer_details)**: This section is for you to provide your company's name, address, contact information (phone number and email), and any other relevant details. Please ensure all required fields are completed, even though this section is not marked as required; providing accurate information ensures efficient order processing.
* **Product Catalog (product_catalog)**: This field is where you list the products you wish to order from our wholesale catalog. Please separate each product by comma, and include the product name and quantity you wish to order. If you need to order multiple products, simply list them in this field.
* **Order Summary (order_summary)**: Briefly describe the purpose and any special instructions or requirements for your order. This field helps us ensure accurate order processing and fulfillment.

## Tips

- Ensure all fields are filled accurately and completely to prevent delays or errors in order processing.
- If you need to place multiple orders, you can fill out this form multiple times.
- If you have any questions or concerns about placing an order, contact us at form@gic.mx.
