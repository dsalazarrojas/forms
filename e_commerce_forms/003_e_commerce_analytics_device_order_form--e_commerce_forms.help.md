# e_commerce_analytics_device_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data for e-commerce analytics and device ordering purposes. It is intended to gather information about device orders, including shipping and payment details. The form is used to track and analyze data for business insights and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the device being ordered in the "device_order" field.
2. Select the shipping method for the device in the "shipping_details" field (optional).
3. Enter any additional payment details in the "payment_details" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **device_order**: Enter the title of the device you are ordering, such as "iPhone 13 Pro" or "Samsung Galaxy S21".
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: yes

* **shipping_details**: Select the shipping method you prefer for your device:
	+ Type: select multiple
	+ Required: no
	+ Options: Shipping, Delivery, Pickup

* **payment_details**: Enter any additional payment details or instructions, such as "Pay with credit card" or "Pay with PayPal".
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no

## Tips

* Please enter accurate and relevant information in the "device_order" field to ensure proper tracking of device orders.
* If you have any special shipping requests or instructions, please include them in the "payment_details" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
