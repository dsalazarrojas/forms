# e_commerce_service_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e_commerce_service_registration_form is a tool for customers to provide their details for a smooth and secure registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Customer Information** page with your basic details.
2. Proceed to the **Shipping Address** page to provide your shipping information.
3. Move on to the **Payment Information** page to specify your payment method.
4. Select your preferred **Service Type** from the available options.
5. Add your **E-mail** address for communication purposes.
6. Enter your **Phone Number** for future contact.
7. Provide your **Date of birth** to confirm your identity.
8. Specify your preferred **Time** for any scheduled services.
9. Add any additional **Note** to ensure a personalized experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (Customer Information, text, Optional)**: Please fill out this page with your basic details, including your name and any other identifying information you'd like to share.
* **Shipping Address (Shipping Address, text, Optional)**: Enter your shipping address to ensure our services are delivered correctly.
* **Payment Information (Payment Information, text, Optional)**: Please specify your payment method to secure your order.
* **Service Type (Service Type, select_one, Optional)**: Select one of our available service types to get started.
* **E-mail (E-mail, email, Optional)**: Enter a valid email address for future communications.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number, text, Optional)**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Date of birth (Date of birth, date, Optional)**: Provide your date of birth to confirm your identity.
* **Time (Time, time, Optional)**: Specify your preferred time for any scheduled services.
* **Note (Note, note, Optional)**: Add any additional note to ensure a personalized experience.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid submission errors.
- Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
- Select your preferred service type carefully, as it may affect your service experience.
