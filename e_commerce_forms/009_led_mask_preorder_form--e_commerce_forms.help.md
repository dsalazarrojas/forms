# LED Mask Preorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LED Mask Preorder Form is designed to collect customer information and preferences for a preorder. This form helps us better understand your needs and provide a more personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer details, including your name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Select your preferred shipping details, including date and time for delivery.
3. Choose your order type (Standard or Deluxe) and provide any special instructions or review details if needed.
4. Confirm your review agreement and provide any further details if required.
5. Confirm your customer agreement and provide any further details if required.
6. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
7. Add any additional notes or comments if you have any.

* **Customer Details (26)** (`Name`, text): Enter your name to associate with your preorder.
* **Email (15)** (`Email`, email): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your preorder.
* **Phone (16)** (`Phone`, text): Provide your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Address (17)** (`Address`, text): Enter your delivery address to ensure smooth and safe delivery.
* **Postcode (18)** (`Postcode`, text): Enter your postcode to ensure accurate shipping.
* **Date (19)** (`Date`, date): Choose a date for delivery.
* **Time (20)** (`Time`, time): Choose a time for delivery.
