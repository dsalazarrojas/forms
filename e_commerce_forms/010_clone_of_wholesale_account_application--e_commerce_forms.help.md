# Clone Of Wholesale Account Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clone Of Wholesale Account Application form is designed to create a copy of an existing wholesale account, typically for when the original account is no longer active or needs to be duplicated for a new entity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields:
	* Business name
	* Contact person
	* Business email
	* Business address
	* Preferred payment terms
2. If applicable, provide additional information:
	* Business phone
	* Resale certificate availability
	* Expected monthly volume
	* Additional comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Business name
* **Business name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the legal trading name of the business.

### Contact person
* **Contact person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person responsible for account management.

### Business email
* **Business email** (`business_email`, email, required): Enter the email address used for business communication and invoicing.

### Business address
* **Business address** (`business_address`, text, required): Enter the street address, city, state, and postal code of the business location.

### Resale certificate
* **Resale certificate available** (`resale_certificate`, select one, optional): Indicate if a resale certificate is available and can be provided.

### Expected monthly volume
* **Expected monthly volume** (`expected_monthly_volume`, number, optional): Enter an estimated order value or units.

### Preferred payment terms
* **Preferred payment terms** (`payment_terms`, select one, required): Choose the preferred payment arrangement for the account, such as Net 30 or Prepaid.

### Additional comments
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other information for the wholesale team to consider.

## Tips
* Ensure you have authorization to fill out this form to prevent mis-authorization or duplication of accounts.
* Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the wholesale team for assistance.
