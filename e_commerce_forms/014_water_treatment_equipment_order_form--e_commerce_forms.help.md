# water_treatment_equipment_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "water_treatment_equipment_order_form" is used to collect customer information and order details for water treatment equipment. This form helps us to process your order efficiently and ensure timely delivery of the ordered products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Customer Email** in the format of a valid email address.
3. Select your preferred **Product Type** from the options listed.
4. Enter the **Quantity** of the product you wish to order.
5. Choose a **Order Date** for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (customer_name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Customer Email (customer_email)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached regarding your order.
* **Product Name (product_name)**: Enter the name of the product you wish to order. This will help us to identify the correct product for delivery.
* **Product Type (product_type)**: Select the type of water treatment equipment you require, such as "Aquastar-100" or "AquaPro-2000".
* **Quantity (quantity)**: Enter the number of products you wish to order.
* **Order Date (order_date)**: Choose the date you would like to take delivery of your order.
