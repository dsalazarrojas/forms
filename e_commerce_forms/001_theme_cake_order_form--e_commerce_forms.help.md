# Theme Cake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Theme Cake Order Form" is designed to collect information and input for a cake order, ensuring that customers provide essential details for their desired cake.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the desired "Cake Design" option from the available choices.
3. Choose a payment method from the "Payment Method" options.
4. Review and confirm the "Total Cost" before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info**: Enter your name, email, and phone number to help us contact you about your order.
	+ This field is not required but recommended for smooth communication.
* **Theme Info**: Provide a brief description of your desired cake theme.
* **Cake Design**: Choose from the following options:
	+ **Plain**: A simple, unadorned cake design.
	+ **Custom**: A custom-designed cake based on your preferences.
	+ **Red Velvet**: A red velvet cake with a unique twist.
	+ **White Fondant**: A cake covered in white fondant.
* **Order Date**: Select the date you would like to receive your cake order.
* **Delivery Time**: Select a delivery time that suits your schedule.
* **Payment Method**: Choose from the following options:
	+ **Pay Pal**: Pay through PayPal.
	+ **Pay Cash**: Pay in person or via other accepted payment methods.
* **Total Cost**: Review and confirm the total cost of your order before submitting the form.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information or special requests for your order.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Submit**: Review and submit your order details.
* **Cancel**: Cancel your order if needed.
