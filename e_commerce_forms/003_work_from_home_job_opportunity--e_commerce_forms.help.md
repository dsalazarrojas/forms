# work-from-home-job-opportunity - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about work-from-home job opportunities. It's meant to be a one-time submission per job opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a brief title for the job opportunity in the "Work From Home Job Opportunity" field.
2. Select the category that best describes the job opportunity.
3. Type a brief description of the job opportunity in the "Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Work From Home Job Opportunity** (form_title, text, optional): Enter a clear and concise title for the job opportunity, e.g., "Remote Software Engineer Position" or "Marketing Assistant Needed".
* **Category** (category, select_one, optional): Choose the category that best describes the job opportunity, such as "E-commerce" or "Other".
* **Description** (description, text, optional): Type a brief summary of the job opportunity, including details about the position, requirements, and any relevant information. This could include a job description, qualifications, or contact details.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing details about the job opportunity.
* Make sure to submit only one instance of each job opportunity.
* Review and proofread your submission before sending for accuracy and clarity.
