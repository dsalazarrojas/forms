# Product Dimensions Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Dimensions Information Form is used to collect accurate product dimensions and information to provide customers with the correct details about products listed in your store. This form helps ensure that product listings are accurate and complete, making it easier for customers to make informed purchasing decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Product Title** of the product being listed.
2. Provide the **Product Width**, **Product Height**, and **Product Depth** of the product using numeric values.
3. Enter the **Product Weight** of the product using a numeric value.
4. Enter the **Product Length** of the product using a numeric value.
5. Select the unit of **Unit of Measurement** that applies to your product (e.g., inches, cm, etc.).
6. Provide a brief **Product Description** of the product (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Title** (`Product Title`, text, optional): Enter the title or name of the product being listed. This field should clearly and concisely describe the product.
* **Product Width** (`Product Width`, number, required): Enter the width of the product in the unit of measurement selected in the next field.
* **Product Height** (`Product Height`, number, required): Enter the height of the product in the unit of measurement selected in the next field.
* **Product Depth** (`Product Depth`, number, required): Enter the depth of the product in the unit of measurement selected in the next field.
* **Product Weight** (`Product Weight`, number, required): Enter the weight of the product in the unit of measurement selected in the next field.
* **Product Length** (`Product Length`, number, required): Enter the length of the product in the unit of measurement selected in the next field.
* **Unit of Measurement** (`Unit of Measurement`, select_one, required): Select the unit of measurement that applies to your product (e.g., inches, cm, etc.).
* **Product Description** (`Product Description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the product being listed.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all numeric fields for accuracy, as incorrect dimensions can cause issues for customers and shipping.
* Select the unit of measurement that applies to your product to ensure consistent and accurate information.
* Keep the product description concise and clear to help customers understand the product better.
