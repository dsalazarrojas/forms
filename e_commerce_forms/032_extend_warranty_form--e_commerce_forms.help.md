# Extend Warranty Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Extend Warranty Form is used to collect information from customers to extend their warranty on products they have purchased.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the product you would like to extend the warranty for.
2. Enter the product ID associated with the product.
3. Enter your name as the customer requesting the warranty extension.
4. Enter your email address for warranty updates and notifications.
5. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
6. Add any additional notes about your request.
7. Select the start date for the extended warranty.
8. Select the expiration date for the extended warranty.
9. Choose how you would like to pay for the extended warranty.
10. Enter the payment amount for the extended warranty.
11. Confirm if you would like to extend the warranty.
12. Choose the type of warranty extension you are requesting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product you would like to extend the warranty for.
- **Product ID** (`product_id`, number, optional): Enter the product ID associated with the product.
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the customer requesting the warranty extension.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for warranty updates and notifications.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your request.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Select the start date for the extended warranty.
- **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, optional): Select the expiration date for the extended warranty.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to pay for the extended warranty.
- **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the payment amount for the extended warranty.
- **Extend Warranty** (`extend_warranty`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm if you would like to extend the warranty.
- **Warranty Type** (`warranty_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of warranty extension you are requesting.
