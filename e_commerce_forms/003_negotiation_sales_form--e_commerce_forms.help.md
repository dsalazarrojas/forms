This help guide will cover the "Negotiation Sales Form", which is designed to facilitate sales negotiations between customers and sales representatives. Its purpose is to ensure accurate and complete data collection for smooth and efficient sales processes.

# Negotiation Sales Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Negotiation Sales Form is used to document and track sales negotiations, ensuring that all relevant details are captured and communicated effectively between customers and sales representatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the product being negotiated.
2. Enter the terms of the sale, including any special conditions or agreements.
3. Choose the transaction type (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Other).
4. Add any additional notes or comments about the sale.
5. Provide the customer's email and phone number (if applicable).
6. Mark the sale as finalized (if complete).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **negotiation sales form product** (Text Field): Enter the product or service being negotiated. This field is required.
* **negotiation sales form terms** (Text Field): Enter the terms of the sale, including any special conditions or agreements. This field is optional.
* **negotion sales form date** (Date Field): Select the date of the sale or negotiation. This field is optional.
* **negotion sales form time** (Time Field): Select the time of the sale or negotiation. This field is optional.
* **negotion sales form price** (Number Field): Enter the price of the sale. This field is optional.
* **negotion sales form terms finalization** (Text Field): Enter any final terms of the sale. This field is optional.
* **negotion sales form transaction type** (Select Field): Choose the type of transaction (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Other). This field is optional.
* **negotion sales form notes** (Note Field): Add any additional notes or comments about the sale. This field is optional.
* **negotion sales form email** (Email Field): Enter the customer's email. This field is optional.
* **negotion sales form phone** (Text Field): Enter the customer's phone number. This field is optional.
* **negotion sales form finalized** (Text Field): Mark the sale as finalized. This field is optional.
