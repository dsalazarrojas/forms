# food_and_beverage_order_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for food and beverages.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: name, order_date, payment_method, quantity, and item.
2. Select your preferred payment method from the available options (Payment, Cash, or Credit Card).
3. Choose the items you'd like to order from the available options (Apple, Banana, Burger, Coffee, or Donut).
4. Enter any comments or additional information in the comments field (optional).
5. Review your order before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **order_date** (required): Enter the date you'd like to pick up or receive your order.
* **delivery_time** (optional): Enter the time you'd like to receive your order.
* **contact_details** (optional): Enter your contact information for any additional details.
* **payment_method** (required): Select your preferred payment method (Payment, Cash, or Credit Card).
* **quantity** (required): Enter the quantity of the item(s) you'd like to order.
* **item** (required): Select the item(s) you'd like to order (Apple, Banana, Burger, Coffee, or Donut).
* **comments** (optional): Enter any comments or additional information about your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your order is complete and accurate.
* If you have any special requests or instructions, use the comments field to include them with your order.
* Review your order carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors or mistakes.
