# Healthy Life Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in joining the Healthy Life Program. The data collected will help us better understand your needs and preferences, which will inform the development of our program's offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "Healthy Food", "Healthy Drinks", or "Healthy Supplements" options for the Healthy Living Products field, based on your current interests.
2. In the "Previous Experiences" field, please describe how you found out about this healthy living product, e.g., social media, online search, or a friend's recommendation.
3. Choose one of the "Main Motivation" options that best represents why you're interested in the program.
4. Fill in any additional information that supports your application.
4. Finally, submit the completed form to proceed with your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Healthy Living Products
- **Healthy Living Products** (`Healthy Food`, `Healthy Drinks`, `Healthy Supplements`, required):
  This field allows you to indicate which type of healthy living product you are most interested in. You can select one or multiple options, as they are all mutually exclusive.

### Previous Experiences
- **Previous Experiences** (text, required):
  This field is where you can describe how you found out about this healthy living product. Please provide as much detail as possible, such as social media, online search, or a friend's recommendation.

### Main Motivation
- **Main Motivation** (`Improve overall health and wellbeing`, `Weight Loss`, `Better nutrition`, required):
  This field asks you to choose the primary reason why you're interested in the Healthy Life Program. Please select the option that best reflects your goals and objectives.

### 
- **Main Form** (text, optional):
  This field is where you can provide any additional information that you feel is relevant to your application. Please use this space to share any details or thoughts that will help us better understand your needs.

## Tips
* Please fill out each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* You can select multiple options for "Healthy Living Products" and "Main Motivation" fields.
* Do not leave any required fields blank; you will need to submit the form with all required fields filled out.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us at `form@gic.mx`.
