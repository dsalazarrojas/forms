# Buyers Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from buyers about their purchasing habits and preferences to help sellers tailor their products or services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Contact Information** section with your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. In the **Product Preferences** section, select your primary category of interest.
3. In the **Product Features** section, select all the features that are most important to you.
4. In the **Purchasing Habits** section, select how often you purchase items in your category of interest.
5. In the **Shipping and Delivery** section, select your preferred shipping method and provide any special delivery instructions if needed.
6. Finally, indicate your preference for receiving personalized email offers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Contact Information
* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, required): Fill out your full name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Enter your email address to contact you about offers and promotions.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required): Enter your phone number for further communication if needed.

### Product Preferences
* **Primary Category of Interest** (`product_category`, required): Select the primary category that interests you, such as Electronics, Apparel, etc.

### Product Features
* **Features Important to You** (`feature_priorities`, required): Select all the features that are most important to you, such as Price, Quality/Durability, etc.

### Purchasing Habits
* **Purchasing Frequency** (`purchase_frequency`, required): Select how often you purchase items in your category of interest, such as Weekly, Monthly, etc.

### Shipping and Delivery
* **Preferred Shipping Method** (`preferred_shipping_method`, required): Select your preferred shipping method, such as Standard (5-7 days), Expedited (2-3 days), etc.
* **Special Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, optional): Provide any special delivery instructions, such as "leave at back door".

### Promotional Consent
* **Receive Promotional Offers** (`promotional_consent`, required): Indicate your preference for receiving personalized email offers.
