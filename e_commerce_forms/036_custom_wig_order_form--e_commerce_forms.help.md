# Custom Wig Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Wig Order Form is designed to collect information from customers to create a custom wig that meets their requirements and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please fill out all required fields and answer all questions accurately.
- Review each field carefully before submitting the form to ensure that your information is correct.
- Make sure to include your confirmation and payment information to ensure a smooth transaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info**: This field is where you can provide your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details you'd like to share with us.
* **Contact Info**: This field is required and should include your email address and phone number so we can contact you with order updates and inquiries.
* **Hair Type**: Please select "Yes" if you're interested in getting a custom-made wig, and "No" otherwise.
* **Wig Type**: Please select "Yes" if you're looking for a custom-made wig, and "No" otherwise.
* **Hair Length**: This field is for you to provide the length of hair you're looking for in your custom-made wig.
* **Hair Color**: This field is for you to provide your preferred hair color.
* **Head Size**: Please note your head size for a proper fit.
* **Hair Texture**: Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your hair texture preference.
* **Hair Style**: Please select "Yes" if you have a specific hair style in mind.
* **Hair Length 2** (optional): If you have a second hair length preference, please provide it here.
* **Hair Color 2** (optional): If you have a second hair color preference, please provide it here.
* **Head Size 2** (optional): If you have a second head size preference, please provide it here.
* **Hair Texture 2** (optional): Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your second hair texture preference.
* **Payment Info**: This field is for you to provide any payment information, including payment method and any relevant details.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests or requirements for your custom-made wig, please provide them here.
* **Notes**: This is a field for any additional comments or instructions you'd like to share with us.
* **Shipping Info**: This field is for you to provide shipping information, including address and any other relevant details.
* **Order Date**: Please provide the date you'd like to receive your custom-made wig.
* **Confirmation**: Please select "Yes" to confirm that you've read and understood the order details.
* **Confirmation 2**: Please select "Yes" to confirm that you're aware of any custom-made wig terms and conditions.
* **Confirmation 3**: Please select "Yes" to confirm that you're aware of any custom-made wig cancellation policies.
* **Confirmation 4**: This field is optional and should be selected only if you're aware of any specific custom-made wig cancellation policies.
* **Confirmation 5**: This field is optional and should be selected only if you're aware of any specific custom-made wig terms and conditions.
* **Confirmation 6**: This field is optional and should be selected only if you're aware of any specific custom-made wig policies.
* **Confirmation 7**: This field is optional and should be selected only if you're aware of any specific custom-made wig requirements.
* **Confirmation 8**: This field is optional and should be selected only if you're aware of any specific custom-made wig specifications.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
- We'll review and update your order information promptly upon submission.
