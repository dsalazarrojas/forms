# Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Order Form is a simple tool to collect customer information and order details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
6. Select the order items you wish to purchase from the "Order Items" dropdown menu.
7. If applicable, enter the total amount of the order in the "Total" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name here.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name here.
- **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address.
- **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address here.
- **Order Items** (`order_items`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the order items you wish to purchase from the options provided.
- **Total** (`total`, number, required/optional): If applicable, enter the total amount of the order. 

Note: The "Total" field is not required, but it is expected that if you are entering a total amount, it will be used for processing.
