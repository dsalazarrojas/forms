# festival_registration_form_with_eway - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and process online payments for festival attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an *asterisk* in their labels.
2. Select the options for "Attendee Details", "Select One", and "Select Multiple" fields according to your preferences.
3. Enter your contact information, including Email, Phone, and Notes.
4. Choose a date and time for the festival.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Festival Name**: Enter the name of the festival you are registering for.
* **Attendee Details**: Select the details of the attendee (True/False). This field is optional.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date**: Choose the date of the festival.
* **Time**: Choose the time of the festival.
* **Notes**: Leave any additional notes or comments about your registration.
* **Select One**: Select one option from the list. This field is optional.
* **Select Multiple**: Select multiple options from the list. This field is optional.
* **Decimal**: Enter a decimal value. This field is optional.
* **Integer**: Enter an integer value. This field is optional.
* **Notes 2**: Leave any additional notes or comments about your registration. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you have any issues with online payment processing, contact our support team.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
