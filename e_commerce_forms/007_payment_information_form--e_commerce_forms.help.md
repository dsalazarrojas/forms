# payment_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential payment information from customers. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide details about the payment, including any relevant information.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your shipping address.
4. Enter your mobile number.
5. Enter your Facebook URL (if applicable).
6. Select the payment mode.
7. If applicable, select a date for a scheduled payment.
8. Enter any additional notes about the payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Details** (`Payment Details`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant details about the payment.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address for delivery purposes.
* **Customer Mobile Number** (`customer_mobile`, text, required/optional): Enter your mobile number for contact purposes.
* **Facebook URL** (`facebook_url`, text, required/optional): If you have a Facebook account, enter your URL for verification purposes.
* **Payment Mode** (`payment_mode`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the available payment modes.
* **Customer Date** (`customer_date`, date, required/optional): If you have a scheduled payment, select a date for the payment.
* **Scheduled Payment** (`scheduled_payment`, date, required/optional): Enter a date for a scheduled payment (if applicable).
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the payment.
