# Basketball Uniform Order Form With Skrill - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help customers place an order for basketball uniforms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill in the contact information section**: Enter your organization or team name, contact person's name, email address, and phone number in the required fields.
2. **Enter delivery address details**: Provide your street address, city, state or province, postal code, and country in the required fields.
3. **Specify uniform details**: Choose the colors of the jerseys and shorts, select the quantity of each size, and decide if you want custom printing of your team name or logo.
4. **Select your payment method**: Choose Skrill as the payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization/Team Name**: Enter your organization or team name.
* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Street Address**: Enter your street address for delivery.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State/Province**: Enter your state or province.
* **Postal Code**: Enter your postal code.
* **Country**: Enter your country.
* **Small Jersey Quantity**: Enter the number of small jerseys needed.
* **Medium Jersey Quantity**: Enter the number of medium jerseys needed.
* **Large Jersey Quantity**: Enter the number of large jerseys needed.
* **X-Large Jersey Quantity**: Enter the number of X-large jerseys needed.
* **Jersey Color**: Select the color of the jerseys.
* **Shorts Color**: Select the color of the shorts.
* **Custom Team Name/Logo**: Choose to have your team name or logo printed on the uniform.
* **Team Name for Printing**: Enter your team name for printing.
* **Select Payment Method**: Choose Skrill as the payment method.
