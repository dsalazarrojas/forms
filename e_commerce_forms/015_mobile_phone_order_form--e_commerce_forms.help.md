# Mobile Phone Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential details for processing a mobile phone order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your preferred phone brand from the options provided in the "Phone Selection" field.
6. If you're paying with a credit card, enter your card number in the "Card Number" field.
7. Enter the CVV (Credit Card Verification Value) in the "CVV" field.
8. If you're paying with a credit card, enter the expiration date in the "Exp. Date" field.
9. (Optional) Select your payment method from the options provided in the "Payment Method" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Phone Selection** (select multiple, required): Select your preferred phone brand from the options provided:
	+ iPhone
	+ Samsung
	+ Google
	+ Other
* **Payment Method** (select one, optional): Select your payment method from the options provided:
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Bank Transfer
* **Card Number** (text, required): Enter your credit card number if paying with a credit card.
* **CVV** (text, required): Enter the CVV (Credit Card Verification Value) from your credit card.
* **Exp. Date** (date, required): Enter the expiration date of your credit card if paying with a credit card.
* **Payment Method** (select one, optional): This field is optional and allows you to choose your payment method.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you're paying with a credit card, ensure that your card number, CVV, and expiration date are entered correctly.
* If you're using a payment method other than credit card, ensure that you have provided the required payment information.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
