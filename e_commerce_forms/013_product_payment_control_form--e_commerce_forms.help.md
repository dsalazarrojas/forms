# Product Payment Control Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to control product payments, including setting up product pricing, payment methods, and capturing customer information. It appears to be used for products sold through an e-commerce system.

## How To Complete This Form

- To start, review the available options for each field to understand the choices.
- Fill out the product payment control form fields, providing accurate and relevant information for each product.
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure proper processing of the product payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **payment_info (payment_info, text, not required)**: Enter information related to the product's payment details, such as payment terms or conditions.
- **price (product_price, number, not required)**: Enter the product's price, ensuring it is a numerical value.
- **description (product_description, text, not required)**: Add a brief description of the product, providing context for the payment control form.
- **image (product_image, text, not required)**: Upload or enter an image related to the product, which could be a photo or another visual representation.
- **customer_info (customer_info, text, not required)**: Enter any relevant customer information, such as their notes or comments about the product.
- **customer_email (customer_email, email, not required)**: Enter the customer's email address, which may be used for communication or notifications.
- **customer_phone (customer_phone, text, not required)**: Enter the customer's phone number, which could be useful for contact or follow-up.
- **payment_method (payment_method, select_one, not required)**: Choose whether the product payment method is 'Yes' or 'No', indicating if payment is accepted or not.
- **payment_method_other (payment_method_other, select_multiple, not required)**: Choose additional payment methods, also as 'Yes' or 'No', for other payment options.
- **payment_method** and **payment_method_other** should be used to control the payment method for the product.
- **customer_email** and **customer_phone** should be used to capture the customer's contact information.
- **payment_info**, **price**, **description**, **image**, and **customer_info** should be used to provide context and details about the product.
- **product_price** should be a numerical value.
- **image** should be a URL or a file upload link.
- Make sure to review each field carefully and understand its purpose and relevance before filling out the form.
- If unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.

## Tips

- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Use this form to manage product payments effectively and efficiently.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your administrator or technical support.
