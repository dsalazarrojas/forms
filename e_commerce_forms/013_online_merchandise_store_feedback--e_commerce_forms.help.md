# Online Merchandise Store Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about their experience with the online merchandise store. This feedback will help the store's administrators to understand customer needs and preferences, and make informed decisions to improve the store.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please enter your product name in the "Product Name" field. This field is required and should contain the name of the product you are providing feedback on.
2. Choose a rating from 1 to 5 in the "Rating" field. This will help us understand how satisfied you are with the product.
3. In the "Comments" field, please provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the product. This is an optional field.
4. Choose a rating from 1 to 5 in the "Rate This Product" field. This rating is specific to the product you are providing feedback on.
5. If you have any further comments or feedback, please enter them in the "Comments" field. This is an optional field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Product Name (form_header_1)** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the product that you are providing feedback on.
- **Rating (form_header_2)** (`select_one`, required): Choose a rating from 1 to 5 to express your satisfaction with the product.
- **Comments (form_header_2)** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the product.
- **Rate This Product (product_rating)** (`select_one`, required): Choose a rating from 1 to 5 for the product, as a separate rating for this specific product.
- **Comments (form_header_3)** (`note`, optional): Enter any further comments or feedback about your overall experience with the store.
