# Store Front Banner Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Store Front Banner Order Form is designed to help you place an order for a storefront banner. This form collects information about your order details, allowing us to process your request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a brief description of your banner order in the "Banner Order Form" field.
2. Enter any specific artwork required for your order in the "Artwork" field.
3. Indicate the quantity of banners you wish to order in the "Quantity" field.
4. Enter the price for your order in the "Price" field.
5. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field so we can contact you about your order.
7. If necessary, provide your phone number in the "Phone" field for us to reach out to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Banner Order Form**: A text field where you can provide a brief description of your banner order.
* **Order Details**: Another text field for any additional information about your banner order.
* **Text**: A text field to input any specific text for your banner order.
* **Artwork**: A text field to input any required artwork for your banner order.
* **Quantity**: A numerical field to input the number of banners you wish to order.
* **Price**: A numerical field to input the price for your order.
* **Payment Method**: A dropdown menu to select one of the available payment methods (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Email**: An email field to input your email address so we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone**: A text field to input your phone number for us to reach out to you.
