# T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to process orders for custom t-shirts. The customer can choose their t-shirt size, payment method, and shipping details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your shipping address in the Shipping Address field.
2. Select your shipping country from the dropdown menu.
3. Select your shipping state from the dropdown menu.
4. Choose your t-shirt size from the dropdown menu.
5. Select your payment method from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter your order total (if applicable).
7. Enter your customer name.
8. Enter your customer email address.
9. Enter your customer phone number.
10. Enter any additional customer message (if desired).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address in this field. This is the address where you want your custom t-shirt to be delivered.
* **Shipping Country** (`shipping_country`, select_multiple, optional): Select your shipping country from the dropdown menu. Please choose the country where your shipping address is located.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, select_one, optional): Select your shipping state from the dropdown menu. This is the state or region where your shipping address is located.
* **T-shirt Size** (`tshirt_size`, select_one, optional): Choose the size of the t-shirt you want to order from the dropdown menu.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method you want to use to make the purchase. This can be credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, optional): Enter the total cost of your order. This is optional but recommended for accurate processing.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your customer name as you would like it to be on the order receipt.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your customer email address. This will be used for communication and order updates.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your customer phone number. This will be used for communication and order updates.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, text, optional): Enter any additional message you would like to include with your order.
