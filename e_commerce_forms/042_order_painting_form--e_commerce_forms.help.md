# Order Painting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Order Painting Form is a digital form used to place orders for paintings. This form helps customers provide necessary information about their order and allows them to upload relevant files if required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Order Painting Form, follow these steps:

1. Select the size of the painting from the options provided.
2. Choose the color of the painting from the available options.
3. If applicable, upload a picture of the painting.
4. Enter the term of the painting in the text field.
5. Enter any additional notes about the order in the order notes field.
6. Provide a valid email address of the customer for communication purposes.
7. Enter the customer's phone number (if applicable).
8. Select the method of payment.
9. Review and agree to the order terms.
10. Enter the customer's name and address to complete the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Painting Size (painting_size)**: Select the size of the painting you want to order.
* **Color (color)**: Choose the color of the painting from the available options.
* **Painting Term (painting_term)**: Enter the term or title of the painting.
* **Upload Painting Picture (painting_upload)**: Upload a picture of the painting if required or if you want to provide more information.
* **Video (video)**: Enter a link to a video if relevant to the order.
* **Order Notes (order_notes)**: Enter any additional notes or instructions about the order.
* **Customer Email (customer_email)**: Enter a valid email address of the customer.
* **Customer Phone (customer_phone)**: Enter the customer's phone number (if applicable).
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Select the method of payment from the available options.
* **Order Terms (order_terms)**: Review and agree to the terms of the order.
* **Customer Name (customer_name)**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Address (customer_address)**: Enter the customer's address.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information to avoid any confusion or delays in processing your order.
* Double-check the order terms and agree to them before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions about the form, contact your sales representative for assistance.
* Ensure that the painting size and color options are suitable for your needs.
