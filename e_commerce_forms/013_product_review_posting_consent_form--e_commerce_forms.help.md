# product_review_posting_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about user consent and review details for a product. It ensures that users understand and agree to the terms of posting a review about a product. This form is intended to be a simple and straightforward way to capture the necessary information without overwhelming the user.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
2.  Fill out the "Product Review Posting Consent" field with your consent status.
3.  Provide more details about your review in the "Review Details" field.
4.  Select "Agree" if you understand and agree to the terms of posting your review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Product Review Posting Consent** (`consent_form`, text, required):
    This field is where you will confirm your agreement to share your product review. Please indicate your consent status by writing "true" if you agree to the terms.
*   **Review Details** (`review_form`, note, required):
    In this field, please provide more information about your review, such as its purpose, relevance, and any other important details you'd like to share.
*   **Agree** (`agree`, select_one, true, required):
    Please select "Agree" if you are willing to share your review according to the set rules, "False" if you are not, or "Decline" if you have second thoughts about it.

## Tips
- Please be clear and concise in your answers.
- If you need more space or details to be filled, don't hesitate to ask for support.
