# Streetwear Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information for a streetwear order, including customer details, order items, payment method, and order status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information section, including name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the order items and payment method from the provided options.
3. Enter the total amount of the order.
4. Choose the order status (e.g., New, Pending, Processing, etc.).
5. If necessary, enter any additional notes or comments about the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data** (Text, Optional):
This field is not a actual field in the form.
* **Customer Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Order Items** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the items to be included in the order.
* **Total Amount** (Number, Optional): Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Required): Choose the payment method (Card, Cash, Transfer, or PayPal).
* **Order Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Customer Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments from the customer.
* **Order Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the order.
* **Store Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the store's address.
* **Store Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the store's name.
* **Store Address 2** (Text, Optional): Enter the store's second address (if applicable).
* **Store City** (Text, Optional): Enter the store's city.
* **Order Status** (Select One, Required): Choose the order status (e.g., New, Pending, Processing, etc.).
* **Form ID** (Number, Optional): Enter a unique ID for the form.
* **Submitted By** (Select One, Required): Choose who submitted the order (Jimmy, John, or Jane).
* **Order Total** (Number, Optional): Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Itemized Amount** (Number, Optional): Enter the amount for each itemized item.
* **Tax Amount** (Number, Optional): Enter the tax amount for the order.
* **Shipping Cost** (Number, Optional): Enter the shipping cost for the order.
* **Itemized Subtotal** (Number, Optional): Enter the subtotal for each itemized item.
* **Itemized Tax** (Number, Optional): Enter the tax amount for each itemized item.
* **Itemized Total** (Number, Optional): Enter the total amount for each itemized item.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that the form is complete and accurate.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, contact the relevant support team.
