# Online Subscription Sample Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Subscription Sample Order Form is designed to collect subscription-related information from users. It helps the user select their preferred subscription plan, enter subscription details, and provide contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your preferred subscription plan from the list provided.
2. Enter a brief description of your plan, if needed.
3. Choose the number of subscriptions you want to order.
4. Select a start date for your subscription.
5. Select an end date for your subscription.
6. Choose your preferred payment method.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your phone number.
9. Provide any other relevant details, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subscription Plan** (`select_one`, **Subscription Plan 1**, **2**, **3**): Select the type of subscription plan that suits your needs from the available options.
* **Plan Description** (`text`, **Plan Description**): If necessary, enter a brief description of your plan to help us understand your needs better.
* **Number of Subscriptions** (`number`, **Number of Subscriptions**): Enter the number of subscriptions you want to order.
* **Subscription Start Date** (`date`, **Subscription Start Date**): Select the start date of your subscription.
* **Subscription End Date** (`date`, **Subscription End Date**): Select the end date of your subscription.
* **Subscription Type** (`select_multiple`, **Subscription Type**): Choose all that apply regarding the subscription type (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Payment Method** (`select_one`, **Payment Method**): Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Draft).
* **Email** (`email`, **Email**): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`text`, **Phone**): Enter your phone number.
* **Additional Details** (`text`, **Additional Details**): Provide any other relevant information about your subscription.
