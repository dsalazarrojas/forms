# e_learning_program_payment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment information for an e-learning program. Users must provide the payment amount, course code, and payment method. The form also captures student contact information and payment status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the payment amount in the designated field.
2. Select the course code from the options provided.
3. Choose the payment method (e.g., Bank Transfer, Check).
4. Optionally, input your student name, email, and phone number if required.
5. Select the payment status (Paid, Partially Paid, Unpaid).
6. Click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount to be paid for the e-learning program.
* **Student Name**: If required, input your name as a student.
* **Course Code**: Select the code for the course you are enrolling in.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay (e.g., Bank Transfer, Check).
* **Student Email**: If required, input your email address.
* **Student Phone**: If required, input your phone number.
* **Transaction Date**: If required, input the date of the transaction.
* **Payment Status**: Select the status of your payment (Paid, Partially Paid, Unpaid).

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
- Double-check your input for accuracy before submission.
- If you have issues with payment, contact our support team for assistance.
