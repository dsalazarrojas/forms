# Summer T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a Summer T Shirt order from customers. It is intended to be a simple and straightforward form for customers to provide their order details, shipping information, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the first page, "Order Details".
2. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed.
3. Click "Next" to proceed to the next page.
4. On the second page, "Shipping Information", enter your shipping details.
5. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details (Order Details, text, required)**: Enter your Summer T Shirt order details, such as the type of t-shirt and any additional information you'd like to include with your order.
* **Shipping Information (Shipping Information, text, required)**: Enter your shipping address and any other relevant information to ensure timely delivery of your order.
* **Payment Information (Payment Information, text, required)**: Enter your payment details, such as your credit card number and expiration date. Please ensure you have the necessary payment information to complete your order.

## Tips
* Please double-check your order details for accuracy before proceeding.
* Make sure to enter your shipping information correctly to avoid delays in delivery.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
