# Online Boutique Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect and process online payments and customer information for an e-commerce business. It's used to capture details such as payment method, total price, tax rate, order status, and customer contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "Payment Method" from the dropdown list to choose how the payment will be made (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
2. Enter the "Total Price" of the order.
3. Enter the "Tax Rate" (if applicable).
4. Select the "Payment Status" of the order (Paid, Pending, or Refunded).
5. Enter the customer's "Customer Name".
6. Enter the customer's "Customer Email".
7. Enter the customer's "Customer Phone" (optional).
8. Enter the customer's "Customer Address" (optional).
9. Select the "Order Date" of the order.
10. Select the "Order Time" of the order (optional).
11. Select the "Payment Gateway" used for the payment (Stripe, PayPal, or Authorize.net).
12. Enter any additional "Order Notes" or "Order Comments" (optional).
13. Enter the customer's "Customer Name 2" (not actually used in the original form, but included to provide consistency).
14. Enter the customer's "Customer Email 2" (not actually used in the original form, but included to provide consistency).
15. Enter the customer's "Customer Phone 2" (not actually used in the original form, but included to provide consistency).
16. Enter the customer's "Customer Address 2" (not actually used in the original form, but included to provide consistency).
17. Select the "Payment Method 2" (not actually used in the original form, but included to provide consistency).
18. Enter any additional "Order Notes 2" or "Order Comments 2" (not actually used in the original form, but included to provide consistency).
19. Enter the customer's "Customer Name 3" (not actually used in the original form, but included to provide consistency).
20. Select the "Order Status" of the order (Paid, Pending, or Refunded).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Online** (`online_boutique_payment_form`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how the payment will be made (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Total Price** (`total_price`, number, required/optional): Enter the total price of the order.
* **Tax Rate** (`tax_rate`, number, required/optional): Enter the tax rate (if applicable).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Select the payment status of the order (Paid, Pending, or Refunded).
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number (if applicable).
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter the customer's address (if applicable).
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Select the date of the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the order (if applicable).
* **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`, select_one, required): Select the payment gateway used (Stripe, PayPal, or Authorize.net).
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional order notes or comments.
* **Customer Name 2** (`customer_name_2`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name (not actually used in the original form).
* **Customer Email 2** (`customer_email_2`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address (not actually used in the original form).
* **Customer Phone 2** (`customer_phone_2`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number (not actually used in the original form).
* **Customer Address 2** (`customer_address_2`, text, optional): Enter the customer's address (not actually used in the original form).
* **Payment Method 2** (`payment_method_2`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method (not actually used in the original form).
* **Order Notes 2** (`order_notes_2`, note, optional): Enter any additional order notes or comments (not actually used in the original form).
* **Customer Name 3** (`customer_name_3`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name (not actually used in the original form).
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, required): Select the order status (Paid, Pending, or Refunded).
