# Limited Edition Drop Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place orders for limited edition products. It collects information about the order, payment details, shipping information, and order confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your order information on page 1.
2. Provide your payment details on page 2.
3. Enter your shipping information on page 3.
4. Confirm your order details on page 4.
5. View your payment and shipping status on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Information (order_information)** (`text`, required): Enter your order information, including any relevant details about your purchase.
* **Payment Details (payment_details)** (`text`, optional): If you have any specific payment instructions or questions, enter them here.
* **Shipping Details (shipping_details)** (`text`, optional): If you have any specific shipping instructions or questions, enter them here.
* **Order Confirmation (order_confirmation)** (`text`, optional): Enter your confirmation of the order details, including any specific details you want to ensure are correct.
* **Payment Status (payment_status)** (`text`, optional): Enter your payment status, including any payment confirmation or questions.
* **Shipping Status (shipping_status)** (`text`, optional): Enter your shipping status, including any shipping confirmation or questions.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out each section accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
- We will review and confirm your order as soon as possible.
- We will keep you updated on your payment and shipping status.
