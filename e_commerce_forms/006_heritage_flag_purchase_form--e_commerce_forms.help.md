# heritage_flag_purchase_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The heritage flag purchase form is a simplified purchase process for a heritage flag, a product that is likely to be a novelty item or a collectible. The form guides users through the required information collection for a successful transaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your order is processed correctly.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what is required of you.
3. Do not proceed to the next field until you have finished the current one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info (`customer_info`, text, optional)**: Enter any relevant information about yourself that may help with your order, such as a reference number or a special instruction.
* **Shipping Details (`shipping_details`, text, required)**: Provide your shipping address, including your name, street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Payment Details (`payment_details`, text, required)**: Enter your payment information, including the type of payment method you will use (e.g., credit card or bank transfer).
* **Confirm Order (`confirm_order`, text, optional)**: Confirm that you have reviewed and accepted the terms of the sale.
* **Contact Info (`customer_info_2`, text, required)**: Enter your contact information, including your name and email address or phone number.
* **Shipping Address (`shipping_details_2`, text, optional)**: Enter your shipping address, including your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Payment Method (`payment_method`, select_one, required)**: Choose from the following payment methods: Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Paypal.
* **Shipping Method (`shipping_method`, select_one, required)**: Choose from the following shipping methods: Standard Shipping or Rush Shipping.
* **Confirm Order 2 (`confirm_order_2`, text, optional)**: Confirm that you have reviewed and accepted the updated terms of the sale.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have entered accurate and complete information in each field to avoid any issues with your order.
* Review each field carefully before proceeding to the next one.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us for assistance.
