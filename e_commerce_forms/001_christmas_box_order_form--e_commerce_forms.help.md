# Christmas Box Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool to collect order information for Christmas gift boxes. It helps customers provide personal details, choose a preferred gift box type, select a payment option, and specify delivery preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4.  Choose your preferred Christmas box type in the "Select Christmas Box Type" field.
5.  Specify the quantity of boxes you would like to order in the "Quantity" field.
6.  Optionally, include a personal message for the gift recipient in the "Gift Message" field.
7.  Enter your full delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
8.  Select your preferred delivery city in the "City" field.
9.  Enter your postal code in the "Postal Code" field.
10.  Choose your preferred delivery date in the "Preferred Delivery Date" field.
11.  Optionally, provide special delivery instructions, such as gate codes or delivery location, in the "Special Delivery Instructions" field.
12.  Select your preferred payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
13.  Decide if you would like to add gift wrapping to your order in the "Add Gift Wrapping" field.
14.  Choose whether to subscribe to our newsletter in the "Subscribe to Newsletter" field.
15.  Finally, let us know how you heard about us in the "How Did You Hear About Us" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Full Name** (Required)
Enter your full name here.

### 2. **Email Address** (Required)
Enter your email address here. We will send order confirmation and other updates here.

### 3. **Phone Number** (Required)
Enter your phone number here. This is for delivery coordination.

### 4. **Select Christmas Box Type** (Required)
Choose your preferred Christmas box type from the options available.

### 5. **Quantity** (Required)
Specify the number of boxes you would like to order.

### 6. **Gift Message** (Optional)
Provide a personal message for the gift recipient, if desired.

### 7. **Delivery Address** (Required)
Enter your full delivery address here.

### 8. **City** (Required)
Select your preferred delivery city.

### 9. **Postal Code** (Required)
Enter your postal code here.

### 10. **Preferred Delivery Date** (Required)
Choose your preferred delivery date.

### 11. **Special Delivery Instructions** (Optional)
Provide any special delivery instructions, such as gate codes or delivery location.

### 12. **Add Gift Wrapping** (Required)
Decide if you would like to add gift wrapping to your order.

### 13. **Subscribe to Newsletter** (Optional)
Choose whether you would like to subscribe to our newsletter for holiday updates and promotions.

### 14. **How Did You Hear About Us** (Optional)
Let us know how you heard about us, such as social media or friend referral.

This form is designed to collect the necessary information to process your order successfully, while also allowing for some flexibility and personalization to enhance the gift-giving experience.
