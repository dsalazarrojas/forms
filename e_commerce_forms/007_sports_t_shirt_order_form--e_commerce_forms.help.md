# Sports T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers order sports T-shirts with ease.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Order Information** section with your order details, such as the type of T-shirt you want to order.
2. Provide your **Shipping Information** in the next section, including your address and contact details.
3. In the **Order Items** section, list the specific T-shirts you want to order, including the quantity and any special requests.
4. If applicable, fill in the **Team Information** section with relevant details about your team or group.
5. Enter your **Billing Information** in the final section, including your name, address, and payment method.
