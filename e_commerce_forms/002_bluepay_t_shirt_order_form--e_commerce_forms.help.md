# Bluepay T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bluepay T Shirt Order Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect customer contact and order information, shirt details, and payment preferences for the processing of custom T-shirt orders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the contact information fields: Full Name, Company Name, and Email Address.
2. Provide your phone number and delivery address to ensure timely delivery.
3. Select the desired T-Shirt color(s) from the options provided.
4. Choose your T-Shirt size(s) from the available options. If multiple sizes are required, you can select each size individually.
5. For each size selected, enter the desired quantity in the respective quantity field. If no quantity is needed for a particular size, leave the field blank.
6. If you require custom printing on the shirts, select "Yes, custom print" and provide a description of the design.
7. Select your preferred delivery date, keeping in mind that this is a required field.
8. Add any special instructions for this order, such as delivery instructions or any specific requirements.
9. Finally, choose your preferred payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Company Name** (text, required): If you are ordering on behalf of a company, enter the company name here.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your primary email address for communication and order confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Provide your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Delivery Address** (text, required): Enter your street address for order shipment.
* **T-Shirt Color** (select_one, required): Select the color of your desired T-shirt from the available options.
* **T-Shirt Sizes** (select_multiple, required): Choose all the T-shirt sizes you require. You can select multiple sizes if needed.
* **Quantity - XS** (number, optional): Enter the quantity of extra small shirts you require. If not needed, leave blank.
* **Quantity - Small** (number, optional): Enter the quantity of small shirts you require. If not needed, leave blank.
* **Quantity - Medium** (number, optional): Enter the quantity of medium shirts you require. If not needed, leave blank.
* **Quantity - Large** (number, optional): Enter the quantity of large shirts you require. If not needed, leave blank.
* **Quantity - XL** (number, optional): Enter the quantity of XL shirts you require. If not needed, leave blank.
* **Quantity - XXL** (number, optional): Enter the quantity of XXL shirts you require. If not needed, leave blank.
* **Custom Print Design** (select_one, required): If you require custom printing on the shirts, select "Yes, custom print" and provide a description of the design. Otherwise, select "No, plain shirts".
* **Design Description** (text, optional): Describe your custom print design, if applicable. Leave blank if not needed.
* **Required Delivery Date** (date, required): Select the required delivery date for your order.
* **Special Instructions** (text, optional): Provide any additional instructions or specific requirements for this order.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method from the available options.
