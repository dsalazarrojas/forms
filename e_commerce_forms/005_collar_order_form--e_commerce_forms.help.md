# Collar Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide customers in completing their order for custom collars. It collects essential information to facilitate the processing and delivery of their order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name**, **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, and select your preferred **Collar Type**.
2. Choose the **Material** and **Color Selection** of your collar.
3. Enter the **Collar Size** in inches.
4. Select your preferred **Collar Fit**.
5. Enter the **Quantity** of collars to order.
6. If you want to add special features, select from the **Special Features** options.
7. If you selected monogramming, provide the **Monogram Details** in the text field.
8. Enter your **Shipping Information**, including **Shipping Address**, **City**, **State - Province**, **Postal Code**, and **Country**.
9. Choose your preferred **Shipping Method**.
10. Select your preferred **Payment Method**.
11. If you have any **Special Instructions**, you can add them here.
12. Finally, confirm that you agree to the **Terms and Conditions**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name to identify you in the order.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for order confirmation and future communications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for delivery and communication purposes.
* **Collar Type**: Choose the style you want to order.
* **Material**: Select the material of your collar.
* **Color Selection**: Choose the color of your collar.
* **Collar Size**: Enter the neck circumference measurement in inches.
* **Collar Fit**: Select the fit of your collar.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of collars to order.
* **Special Features**: Select any additional options you want for your collar.
* **Monogram Details**: If you selected monogramming, enter the initial or custom text here.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your full delivery address.
* **City**: Enter the city of your shipping address.
* **State - Province**: Enter the state or province of your shipping address.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of your shipping address.
* **Country**: Enter the country of your shipping address.
* **Shipping Method**: Choose your preferred shipping method.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method.
* **Special Instructions**: Add any additional requests or instructions for your order.
* **I agree to the terms and conditions**: Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.
