# Skincare Line Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skincare Line Pre Order Form is designed to capture the necessary information for customers to pre-order a skincare line product. This form will guide you through the ordering process, ensuring that we have accurate information to process your order efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the second field. This is where we will send updates and confirmation about your order.
3. Select the date you would like to **Order On** in the third field.
4. Choose the **Product** you would like to order from the drop-down menu in the fourth field.
5. Select the quantity of the product you would like to order in the fifth field.
6. If you have any **Note** or additional comments, you can type them in the sixth field.
7. Please confirm that the **Customer Note** is correct or provide any additional comments you'd like to add in the seventh field.
8. Enter your **Phone Number** in the eighth field.
9. If you have any additional **Billing Notes**, you can type them in the ninth field.
10. If you'd like to select any **Billing Terms**, choose one from the drop-down menu in the tenth field.
11. Select the date you would like to **Ship On** in the eleventh field.
12. If you'd like to select any **Shipping Report** options, choose one from the drop-down menu in the twelfth field.
13. If you have any additional **Shipping Notes**, you can type them in the thirteenth field.
14. Confirm that the **Payment Terms** are correct or choose one from the drop-down menu in the fourteenth field.

* **Billing Country** (Optional, Text): Enter your billing country.
* **Billing Phone** (Optional, Text): Enter your billing phone number.
