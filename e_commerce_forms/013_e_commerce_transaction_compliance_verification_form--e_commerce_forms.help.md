# E Commerce Transaction Compliance Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify the compliance of an e-commerce transaction. It helps to ensure that the transaction follows certain rules or standards, and is an internal auditing tool.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the transaction details in the text field on page 1.
2. Provide information about the customer in the text field on page 2.
3. If applicable, enter the order details in the text field on page 3.
4. Enter the shipping details in the text field on page 4.
5. Enter the payment information in the text field on page 5.
6. If you have any additional comments or notes about the transaction, enter them in the text field on page 6.
7. Finally, if you need to contact the customer, enter their email address in the email field on page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Transaction Details (Page 1)** (`transaction_details`, text, required): Enter the details of the transaction, such as the transaction ID, date, or any other relevant information.
* **Customer Info (Page 2)** (`customer_info`, text, required): Enter information about the customer, including their name, address, or any other relevant details.
* **Order Details (Page 3)** (`order_details`, text, optional): If applicable, enter information about the order, such as the order ID or any other relevant details.
* **Shipping Details (Page 4)** (`shipping_details`, text, optional): Enter information about the shipping, including the shipping address or any other relevant details.
* **Payment Info (Page 5)** (`payment_info`, text, required): Enter information about the payment, including the payment method or any other relevant details.
* **Notes (Page 6)** (`note`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or notes about the transaction, enter them here.
* **Email (Page 7)** (`email`, email, optional): If you need to contact the customer, enter their email address here.
* **Phone (Page 8)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number here (if required).
