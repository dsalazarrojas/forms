# SK50D Feedback Program - Help Guide
## Purpose

The SK50D Feedback Program is a one-page survey designed to collect feedback from clients and partners about our products or services. This form is used to gauge customer satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. You can only submit this form once per individual.
3. Do not resubmit feedback for the same individual.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address. This will be used for contact purposes.
* **Contact Info** (`contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional contact information you'd like to provide.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, enter the name of your company.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, enter the name of your department.
* **Product** (`product`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, enter the name of the product we discussed or interacted about.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with our product or service, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Provide any comments or feedback about our products or services.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the name of the person you interacted with (e.g. John, Jane, Jim).
