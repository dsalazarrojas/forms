# Game T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game T Shirt Order Form is used to collect information for a game-themed T-shirt order. It is a multi-page form that covers order details, payment information, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Order Information" page and enter the required information.
2. Click on the "Payment Information" page and select the payment method and enter the amount.
3. Click on the "Shipping Information" page and enter the shipping details.
4. On the "T-Shirt Options" page, select the required options.
5. On the "Special Instructions" page, enter any special instructions or comments.
6. On the "Email" and "Phone" pages, enter your contact information.
7. On the "First Name", "Last Name", "Address", and "Zip" pages, enter your personal information.
8. On the "Payment Method" page, select the payment method you would like to use.
9. On the "Amount" page, enter the amount you would like to pay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Information** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter the order details, such as the game title and quantity.
* **Payment Information** (`2`, `text`, required): Select the payment method and enter the amount.
* **Shipping Information** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter the shipping details, such as the address and zip code.
* **T-Shirt Options** (`4`, `text`, required): Select the options for the T-shirt, such as size and color.
* **Special Instructions** (`5`, `note`, optional): Enter any special instructions or comments.
* **Email** (`6`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **First Name** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Address** (`10`, `text`, optional): Enter your address.
* **Zip** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Payment Method** (`12`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method, such as credit card or PayPal.
* **Amount** (`13`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount you would like to pay.
