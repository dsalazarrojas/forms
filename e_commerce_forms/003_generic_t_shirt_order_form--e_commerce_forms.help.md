# Generic T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a generic t-shirt order. It is used for both new and replacement orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the size of the t-shirt you would like to order.
2. Enter your name and contact information.
3. Choose the type of order (new or replacement).
4. Select the color of the t-shirt.
5. Select the design(s) for the t-shirt.
6. Enter the type of payment you would like to use.
7. If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **T Shirt Size** (t_shirt_size): Select the size of the t-shirt you would like to order (S, M, L, XL).
* **Customer Name** (customer_name): Enter your name.
* **Customer Email** (customer_email): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (customer_phone): Enter your phone number.
* **Order Type** (order_type): Choose between "New" and "Replacement".
* **Customer Message** (customer_message): If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them in the space provided.
* **Payment Type** (payment_type): Choose between "PayPal" and "Stripe".
* **Customer Note** (customer_note): If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them in the space provided.
* **T Shirt Color** (t_shirt_color): Select the color of the t-shirt you would like to order (Blue, Red, White).
* **T Shirt Design** (t_shirt_design): Select the design(s) for the t-shirt you would like to order.
* **Customer Address** (customer_address): Enter your address.
* **Customer City** (customer_city): Enter your city.
* **Customer Zip** (customer_zip): Enter your zip code.
* **Customer State** (customer_state): Enter your state or province.
* **Customer Country** (customer_country): Enter your country.
* **Customer Postal Code** (customer_postal_code): Enter your postal code.
* **Customer Card Type** (customer_card_type): Choose between "Visa" and "Mastercard".
* **Customer Card Number** (customer_card_number): Enter your credit card number.
* **Customer Expiration Date** (customer_expiration_date): Enter the expiration date of your credit card.
* **Customer Card CVV** (customer_card_cvv): Enter your CVV (card verification value).
* **Customer Card Code** (customer_card_code): Enter your card code.
* **Customer Card Address 1** (customer_card_address1): Enter your address (line 1).
* **Customer Card Address 2** (customer_card_address2): Enter your address (line 2).
* **Customer Card Note** (customer_card_note): If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them in the space provided.
* **Form Submission** (form_submission): Please enter your comments or notes about the form submission.
