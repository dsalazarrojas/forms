# Color Street Spring - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect order information for the "Color Street Spring" product catalog. It gathers customer information, product selections, shipping, and payment details to process orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Customer Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** fields to provide your contact information.
2. Select the product sets you are interested in from the respective sections:
	* **Spring Collection** for pastel floral designs
	* **Garden Party** for vibrant garden colors
	* **Easter Special** for Easter-themed designs
	* **Pastel Dreams** for soft pastel collections
	* **Floral Fantasy** for beautiful floral patterns
	* **Classic Red** for time-tested red shades
	* **Nude Collection** for natural tones
	* **Glitter Bomb** for sparkly glitter designs
3. For shipping and payment details:
	* Enter your **Shipping Address** in the text field
	* Select your preferred **Payment Method** from the drop-down menu
	* If you have a promo code, enter it in the **Promo Code** field (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (Required): Enter your full name for order processing.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address for order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your contact phone number for delivery issues.
* **Spring Collection** (Select One): Select the pastel floral design set(s) you are interested in (Not Interested, 1 Set, 2 Sets, 3 Sets, 4 or More Sets).
* **Garden Party** (Select One): Select the vibrant garden color set(s) you are interested in (Not Interested, 1 Set, 2 Sets, 3 Sets, 4 or More Sets).
* **Easter Special** (Select One): Select the Easter-themed design set(s) you are interested in (Not Interested, 1 Set, 2 Sets, 3 Sets, 4 or More Sets).
* **Pastel Dreams** (Select One): Select the soft pastel collection set(s) you are interested in (Not Interested, 1 Set, 2 Sets, 3 Sets, 4 or More Sets).
* **Floral Fantasy** (Select One): Select the beautiful floral pattern set(s) you are interested in (Not Interested, 1 Set, 2 Sets, 3 Sets, 4 or More Sets).
* **Classic Red** (Select One): Select the time-tested red shade set(s) you are interested in (Not Interested, 1 Set, 2 Sets, 3 Sets, 4 or More Sets).
* **Nude Collection** (Select One): Select the natural tone set(s) you are interested in (Not Interested, 1 Set, 2 Sets, 3 Sets, 4 or More Sets).
* **Glitter Bomb** (Select One): Select the sparkly glitter design set(s) you are interested in (Not Interested, 1 Set, 2 Sets, 3 Sets, 4 or More Sets).
* **Shipping Address** (Required): Enter your complete delivery address.
* **Payment Method** (Required): Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay).
* **Promo Code** (Optional): Enter any discount code you may have.
* **Special Instructions** (Optional): Provide any special delivery instructions or notes.
