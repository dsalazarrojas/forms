# Universal Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Universal Order Form is designed to collect information for orders placed with our company. This form helps us to understand customer details, order specifics, and payment information. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure we receive the correct information for your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk (*)).
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name for identification purposes.
3. Provide your Email address and Phone number for communication.
4. Enter your Address for shipping and delivery purposes.
5. Provide Order Details, such as the items ordered and any special requests.
6. Select your preferred Payment Method (Card or PayPal).
7. Enter the Order Total and any additional Order Comments.
8. Select the Order Status (New, In Progress, or Complete).
9. If desired, add additional Customer Notes.
10. Review all fields to ensure accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your shipping and delivery address.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional order details or special requests.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred payment method (Card or PayPal).
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, optional): Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the payment status of your order (Paid or Pending).
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY format).
* **Time Of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, `time`, optional): Enter your time of birth (HH:MM AM/PM format).
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes for the order.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, `email`, optional): Enter an alternate email address (if different from the primary email).
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments for the order.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional customer comments or notes.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the current status of your order (New, In Progress, or Complete).
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the customer (if different from you).
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, `number`, optional): Enter the order number for the order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the order (MM/DD/YYYY format).
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the order (HH:MM AM/PM format).
* **Order Total 2** (`order_total_2`, `number`, optional): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Payment Method 2** (`payment_method_2`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred payment method (Card or PayPal).
* **Payment Status 2** (`payment_status_2`, `select_one`, optional): Select the payment status of your order (Paid or Pending).
* **Date Of Birth 2** (`date_of_birth_2`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY format).
* **Note 2** (`note_2`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes for the order.
