# Takeaway Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Takeaway Order Form is designed to be completed by customers who want to place an order for a takeaway service. Please use this form only for this specific purpose and follow the instructions provided to ensure your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select your preferred delivery time in the Delivery Time field.
6. Choose your preferred delivery option in the Pickup field.
7. Select the menu items you would like to order from the Menu field.
8. If you have any special requests, enter them in the Special Request field.
9. Confirm your email address in the Confirm Email field.
10. Select your preferred payment gateway in the Payment Gateway field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (3): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for updates on your order.
* **Phone** (4): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for updates on your order.
* **Delivery Time** (6): Select a delivery time that works for you from the available options.
* **Pickup** (7): Choose how you would like to receive your takeaway order:
	+ Pickup: You will pick up your order from our location.
	+ Delivery: We will deliver your order.
	+ Self Collect: You will come to pick up your order at a designated time.
* **Menu** (8): Select the items you would like to order from the available menu options.
* **Special Request** (9): If you have any special requests or instructions for your order, enter them here.
* **Confirm Email** (10): Confirm that the email address you entered is accurate.
* **Payment Gateway** (11): Choose the payment method you would like to use for your order:
	+ PayPal: You will be directed to the PayPal website to complete the payment.
	+ Bank Transfer: We will provide instructions on how to complete the bank transfer.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information for each field, as this will help us process your order correctly.
- If you have any questions or issues with your order, contact us directly.
