# E Commerce Website Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on an e-commerce website's user experience, design, and overall quality. The data collected will be used to identify areas of improvement and optimize the website for better performance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the website's **URL** in the **Website URL** field. This is a required field.
2. Enter the website's **name** in the **Website Name** field. This is also a required field.
3. If you have contact information for the website's owner or administrator, enter it in the **Contact Email** field. This field is optional.
4. If you have a contact number for the website's owner or administrator, enter it in the **Contact Phone** field. This field is optional.
5. Evaluate the website's **design** by selecting one of the following options:
	* Very Poor
	* Poor
	* Fair
	* Good
	* Excellent
6. Evaluate the website's **navigation** by selecting one of the following options:
	* Very Difficult
	* Somewhat Difficult
	* Not Difficult at All
7. Evaluate the website's **content** by selecting one of the following options:
	* Very Confusing
	* Somewhat Confusing
	* Not Confusing at All
8. Rate the website's **overall experience** on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
9. If you have any additional comments or feedback, enter them in the **Comments** field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Website URL**: The website's URL is the address of the website you are evaluating. Enter the exact address, including the protocol (http:// or https://), in the field.
* **Website Name**: The website's name is the title of the website. Enter the exact name as it appears on the website's home page or title bar.
* **Contact Email**: The website's contact email is an optional field for entering an email address that corresponds to the website's owner or administrator.
* **Contact Phone**: The website's contact phone number is an optional field for entering a phone number that corresponds to the website's owner or administrator.
* **Website Design**: Evaluate the website's design by selecting one of the following options:
	* Very Poor: The website's design is not visually appealing and may be confusing to users.
	* Poor: The website's design is average and may need improvement.
	* Fair: The website's design is average but not exceptional.
	* Good: The website's design is good but not excellent.
	* Excellent: The website's design is exceptional and appealing.
* **Website Navigation**: Evaluate the website's navigation by selecting one of the following options:
	* Very Difficult: The website's navigation is confusing and difficult to navigate.
	* Somewhat Difficult: The website's navigation is slightly challenging but manageable.
	* Not Difficult at All: The website's navigation is clear and easy to follow.
* **Website Content**: Evaluate the website's content by selecting one of the following options:
	* Very Confusing: The website's content is difficult to understand and may be overwhelming.
	* Somewhat Confusing: The website's content is slightly confusing but generally clear.
	* Not Confusing at All: The website's content is clear and easy to understand.
* **Overall Experience**: Rate the website's overall experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the website in this field. This field is optional.
