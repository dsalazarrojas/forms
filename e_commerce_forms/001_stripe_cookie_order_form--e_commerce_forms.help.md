# Stripe Cookie Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stripe Cookie Order Form is a simple order form designed to collect details about cookie orders, making it easier for customers to place their orders and for the business to process and fulfill them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Order Details" section with the required information.
2. Choose the "Cookie Type" you would like to order.
3. Enter the "Quantity" of cookies you would like to order.
4. Enter the "Unit Price" of the cookies.
5. Calculate the "Subtotal" based on the quantity and unit price.
6. Enter the "Tax" (if applicable).
7. Calculate the "Total" (if applicable).
8. Choose the "Payment Method" you would like to use (e.g. online or in-person).
9. Enter the "Customer Name", "Email", "Phone", "Street", "Street Number", "City", "State", and "Zip" for shipping and contact information.
10. Enter any "Notes" about your order (e.g. special requests or instructions).
11. Enter the "Stripe Publishable Token", "Stripe Publishable Secret", "Stripe Webhook Secret", and "Stripe Payment Method" (if applicable).
12. Choose the "Stripe Payment Gateway" (if applicable).
13. Finally, enter the "Stripe API Keys" (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Order Details**: Enter the details about the order, such as the type of cookie and any specific requests.
* **Cookie Type**: Choose the type of cookie you would like to order (e.g. chocolate chip, oatmeal raisin, etc.).
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of cookies you would like to order.
* **Unit Price**: Enter the price per unit of cookies.
* **Subtotal**: Calculate the total price based on the quantity and unit price.
* **Tax**: Enter the tax amount (if applicable).
* **Total**: Calculate the total price including tax (if applicable).
* **Payment Method**: Choose whether you would like to use an online or in-person payment method (if applicable).
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name for shipping and contact purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact and shipping purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Street**: Enter your street address for shipping purposes.
* **Street Number**: Enter your street number for shipping purposes.
* **City**: Enter the city for shipping purposes.
* **State**: Enter the state for shipping purposes.
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code for shipping purposes.
* **Country**: Enter the country for shipping purposes.
* **Notes**: Enter any special requests or instructions for your order.
* **Stripe Publishable Token**: Enter the Stripe publishable token for payment processing.
* **Stripe Publishable Secret**: Enter the Stripe publishable secret for payment processing.
* **Stripe Webhook Secret**: Enter the Stripe webhook secret for payment processing.
* **Stripe Payment Method**: Choose whether you would like to use online or in-person payment (if applicable).
* **Stripe Payment Gateway**: Choose the Stripe payment gateway (if applicable).
* **Stripe API Keys**: Enter the Stripe API keys for payment processing.
* **Payment Method**: This field is not actually required; it seems to be a duplicate of "Stripe Payment Method". Remove this field or make it optional.
