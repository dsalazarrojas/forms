# Fall 2020 Color Street - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fall 2020 Color Street form is a pre-ordering form designed to collect information from customers for products. It appears to be used for customers to select products, choose colors and sizes, specify quantity, and provide contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the product form you want to pre-order (Product form for pre order).
2. Fill in the required fields:
   * Select a color (`Select a color` field on page 1).
   * Select a size (`Select a size` field on page 1).
   * Enter the quantity you want to pre-order (`Quantity` field on page 1).
   * Enter the price you are willing to pay (`Price` field on page 1).
   * Enter the date and time you want the product delivered (`Date` and `Time` fields on page 1).
   * Enter any additional notes about your pre-order (`Note` field on page 1).

3. Optionally, you can fill in any additional fields:
   * Select more colors (`Select a size` field on page 2, this field appears to be a select_multiple field).
   * Select more sizes (`Select a size` field on page 2).
   * Enter your email address (`Email` field on page 2).
   * Enter your phone number (`Phone` field on page 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Select a color (Select a color, select_one)**: Choose the color you prefer for your pre-order from the available options.
- **Select a size (Select a size, select_multiple)**: Select all the sizes you prefer for your pre-order.
- **Quantity (Quantity, number)**: Enter the number of items you want to pre-order.
- **Price (Price, number)**: Enter the price you are willing to pay for your pre-order.
- **Date (Date, date)**: Enter the date you want the product delivered.
- **Time (Time, time)**: Enter the time you want the product delivered.
- **Note (Note, note)**: Enter any additional notes about your pre-order.
- **Email (Email, email)**: Enter your email address.
- **Phone (Phone, text)**: Enter your phone number.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your pre-order is processed accurately.
- Review all fields carefully before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact our support team.
