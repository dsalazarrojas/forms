# Pantry Food Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place a food order from the Pantry Food management system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the "Enter name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Enter email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Enter phone" field.
4. Enter your address in the "Enter address" field.
5. Select the food items you would like to order from the dropdown menu labeled "Select items".
6. Choose the quantity you would like to order for each selected item.
7. Select the date you would like to pick up or have your order delivered on the "Select date" field.
8. Choose a time for the order to be fulfilled on the "Select time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Enter name
* **Enter name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
### 2. Enter email
* **Enter email**: Enter your email address as used by your email provider.
### 3. Enter phone
* **Enter phone**: Enter your phone number as used by your phone service provider.
### 4. Enter address
* **Enter address**: Enter your current physical address for delivery or pickup purposes.
### 5. Select items
* **Select items**: Choose the food items you would like to order from the dropdown menu. Please select all the items you would like to order.
### 6. Select quantity
* **Select quantity**: Choose the quantity you would like to order for each selected item.
### 7. Select date
* **Select date**: Choose a date for your order to be fulfilled. This could be the date for delivery or pickup.
### 8. Select time
* **Select time**: Choose a time for your order to be fulfilled. This could be the time for delivery or pickup.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have selected the correct items and quantities to avoid any errors.
* Double-check the date and time to ensure they align with your needs.
* If you have any special requests or questions, please contact our support team for assistance.
