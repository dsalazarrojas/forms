# E Commerce Shipping Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Commerce Shipping Communication Form is designed to gather information from customers about their orders, helping you communicate with them effectively and resolve any issues efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information, including name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter shipping details, such as the order number, shipping date, and shipping method.
3. Update the order status to ensure we are aware of any changes or updates.
4. Enter the shipping address, including street, city, state, and zip code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (Page 1, Field 1)** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Please enter your name, email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Shipping Details (Page 1, Field 2)** (`shipping_details`, text, optional): Provide the order number, shipping date, and shipping method for the order you're contacting us about.
* **Order Status (Page 1, Field 3)** (`order_status`, text, optional): Update the order status to inform us of any changes or updates.
* **Shipping Address (Page 2, Field 1)** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter the shipping address, including street, city, state, and zip code.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any issues or concerns about your order, please include a detailed description.
* If you need assistance, contact us using the provided contact information.
* You can modify this form as per your business needs, but please give credit to the original creator.
