# Stationery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stationery Order Form is a form used to place an order for stationery items such as paper, cards, or other office supplies. This form is designed to capture the necessary information for the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of your stationery order in the **Form Submission** field.
2. Select your preferred **Customer Details** from the options provided.
3. Choose the type of paper you would like to order using the **Paper Type** field.
4. Select the quantity and color of paper you require using the **Quantity** and **Paper Color** fields respectively.
5. Select the size of paper you need from the options provided in the **Paper Size** field.
6. Choose the weight of paper you would like to order from the options provided in the **Paper Weight** field.
7. If you have any special instructions or requests, please provide them in the **Special Instructions** field.
8. Enter the total number of pages you need using the **Total Pages** field.
9. Sign the form by adding your digital signature in the **Form Signature** field.
10. Select a time for the form submission using the **Form Submission Time** field.
11. Enter the date of form submission using the **Form Submission Date** field.
12. Enter your email address in the **Email Address** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Submission (1)**: A text field where you can briefly describe your stationery order.
* **Customer Details (2)**: Select the details of the customer who is placing the order.
* **Paper Type (3)**: Choose the type of paper you would like to order.
* **Quantity (4)**: Enter the quantity of paper you need.
* **Paper Color (5)**: Select the color of paper you would like to order.
* **Paper Size (6)**: Choose the size of paper you need.
* **Paper Weight (7)**: Select the weight of paper you would like to order.
* **Special Instructions (8)**: Enter any special instructions or requests you have.
* **Total Pages (9)**: Enter the total number of pages you need.
* **Form Signature (10)**: Add your digital signature to confirm the order.
* **Form Submission Time (11)**: Select a time for the form submission.
* **Form Submission Date (12)**: Enter the date of form submission.
* **Email Address (13)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
