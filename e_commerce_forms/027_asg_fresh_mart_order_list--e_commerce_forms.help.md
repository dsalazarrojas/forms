# Asg Fresh Mart Order List - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Asg Fresh Mart Order List form is used to collect information from customers about their orders for seafood products from Asg Fresh Mart. The form helps the customer to accurately provide their details and preferences for the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Full Name**, which is required to process your order.
2. Provide your **Delivery Address**, which is also required to ensure accurate delivery of your order.
3. Enter your **Contact Phone Number**, which is required for us to contact you if needed.
4. Select your preferred **Seafood Category**, which will help us to suggest suitable products.
5. Select the specific **Products** you wish to order, which is also required for order processing.
6. Enter the **Quantity in Kilograms**, which is required for accurate billing.
7. If needed, provide **Special Cleaning or Prep Instructions** for your order.
8. Choose your **Preferred Delivery Time**, which is required for timely delivery of your order.
9. If you have an estimate of the order cost, provide your **Estimated Budget**.
10. Select your preferred **Payment Mode**, which is required for payment processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Full Name (1)** (`customer_full_name_fm`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Delivery Address (2)** (`delivery_address_fm`, text, required): Enter your delivery address to ensure accurate delivery of your order.
* **Contact Phone Number (3)** (`contact_phone_fm`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Seafood Category (4)** (`seafood_category_fm`, select_one, required): Choose from Fresh Fish, Crustaceans - Crab, Lobster, Shrimp, Mollusks - Clams, Oysters, Mussels, Squid and Octopus, or Processed/Frozen Seafood.
* **Specific Product Selection (5)** (`product_selection_fm`, select_multiple, required): Select the items you wish to order.
* **Quantity in Kilograms (6)** (`order_quantity_kg`, number, required): Enter the quantity in kilograms for accurate billing.
* **Special Cleaning or Prep Instructions (7)** (`cleaning_instructions_fm`, text, optional): Provide any special instructions for your order.
* **Preferred Delivery Time (8)** (`delivery_time_fm`, select_one, required): Choose from Morning - 8 AM to 12 PM, Afternoon - 12 PM to 5 PM, or Evening - 5 PM to 8 PM.
* **Estimated Budget (9)** (`estimated_budget_fm`, number, optional): Enter a maximum value in your local currency.
* **Preferred Payment Mode (10)** (`payment_mode_fm`, select_one, required): Choose from Cash on Delivery, Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Mobile Wallet.
