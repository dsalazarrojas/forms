# nail_stripe_invoice_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request an invoice for a nail stripe service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Stripe Name field with the name of the customer as it appears on their account in Stripe.
2. Enter the customer's name in the Customer Name field.
3. Select the desired stripe color from the Stripe Color field.
4. Choose the design style from the Stripe Design field.
5. Enter the price of the order in the Price field.
6. Select the quantity of the order from the Quantity field.
7. Enter the date for the order in the Date field.
8. Select the time of the order from the Time field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stripe Name (text, not required)**: Enter the Stripe Name of the customer as it appears on their account.
* **Customer Name (text, not required)**: Enter the name of the customer who is placing the order.
* **Stripe Color (select_one, not required)**: Select the color of the stripe from the options Black, White, or Gray.
* **Stripe Design (select_one, not required)**: Select the design style for the stripe from the options Nail Stripe, Nail Art, or Nail Art with Color.
* **Price (number, not required)**: Enter the price of the order.
* **Quantity (select_multiple, not required)**: Select the number of orders to process from the options 1, 2, or 3.
* **Date (date, not required)**: Enter the date of the order.
* **Time (time, not required)**: Select the time of the order.
