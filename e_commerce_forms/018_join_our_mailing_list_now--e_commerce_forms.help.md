# join_our_mailing_list_now - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects contact information from users who want to join our mailing list to stay up-to-date on our company's latest news, promotions, and products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked with *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First_name, text, *): Enter your first name for our mailing list.
* **Last Name** (Last_name, text, *): Enter your last name for our mailing list.
* **Email** (Email, email, *): Enter a valid email address where you'd like to receive updates from us.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, ): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Company** (Company, text, ): Enter your company name (optional).
* **Industry** (Industry, text, ): Enter your industry (optional).
* **Job Title** (Job_title, text, ): Enter your job title (optional).
* **Job Title 1**, **Job Title 2**, ..., **Job Title 18**: Enter your job titles (optional, one per field).

Note: The fields marked with \* are required to be filled out. The fields without \* are optional.
