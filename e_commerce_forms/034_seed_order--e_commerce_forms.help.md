# seed_order - Help Guide
## Purpose
The seed_order form is designed to collect and track information related to seed sales, purchases, and orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the seed_order form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the user information section (id: 1) with your details, if required.
2. Select the payment method (id: 2) from the available options.
3. Enter the quantity of seeds ordered (id: 3) or select the seed type.
4. Provide the date and time (id: 4, 5) of the order, if necessary.
5. Input the total amount (id: 6) associated with the order.
6. Add a note to the order (id: 7).
7. Input your email address (id: 8) for communication.
8. Enter your phone number (id: 9) for contact purposes.
9. Enter the shipping address (id: 10), city (id: 11), and state (id: 12) details.
10. Select the shipping country (id: 14) from the available options.
11. Choose the order status (id: 15) from the available options.
12. Determine the order type (id: 16) as seed sale or purchase.
13. Input product-related information (id: 17-25), which includes:
	* Product info (id: 17)
	* Product price (id: 18)
	* Product image (id: 19)
	* Seed info (id: 20)
	* Seed quantity (id: 21)
	* Seed price (id: 22)
	* Seed description (id: 23)
	* Seed image (id: 24)
	* Seed type (id: 25)
