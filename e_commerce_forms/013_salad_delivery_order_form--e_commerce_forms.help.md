# Salad Delivery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Salad Delivery Order Form is designed to help users place orders for salad deliveries. Please fill out this form with the required and optional fields to provide accurate information for your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the salad you would like to order.
2. Select the type of salad (Mixed, Garden, or Vegan).
3. If you would like to receive instructions with your salad, select "with instructions" from the dropdown menu.
4. Choose a delivery date for your order.
5. Choose a delivery time for your order.
6. Enter your contact phone number.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your address for delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salad Name** (`base_salad_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the salad you would like to order.
* **Salad Type** (`base_salad_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of salad (Mixed, Garden, or Vegan).
* **Salad Price** (`base_salad_price`, number, optional): You can enter a price for your salad, but this is not required.
* **Salad Instructions** (`base_salad_instructions`, select_multiple, optional): If you would like to receive instructions with your salad, select "with instructions" from the dropdown menu.
* **Delivery Date** (`base_salad_delivery_date`, date, optional): Choose a delivery date for your order.
* **Delivery Time** (`base_salad_delivery_time`, time, optional): Choose a delivery time for your order.
* **Phone** (`base_salad_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`base_salad_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact.
* **Address** (`base_salad_address`, text, optional): Enter your address for delivery.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields to avoid delays in processing your order.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at your email address provided above.
