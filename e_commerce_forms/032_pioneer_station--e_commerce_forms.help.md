# Pioneer Station - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pioneer Station form is designed to collect user information in a simple text-based format.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Pioneer Station form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill out the 3 text fields with your information.
3. For the select_one field, choose either "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **home-page-label** (`home_page`, text, optional): A text field for entering information related to the "home page".
* **form-field-1-label** (`form_field_1`, text, optional): A text field for entering information related to "form-field-1-label".
* **form-field-2-label** (`form_field_2`, text, optional): A text field for entering information related to "form-field-2-label".
* **form-field-3-label** (`form_field_3`, select_one, optional):
	+ "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown options. This field is used for selecting one of the two options.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information in the text fields.
* Choose the correct option in the select_one field to ensure your response is captured correctly.
