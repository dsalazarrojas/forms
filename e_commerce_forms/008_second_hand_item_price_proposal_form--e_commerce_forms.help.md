# second-hand-item-price-proposal-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used for price proposals on second-hand items. It is meant for sellers to provide contact information, details about the second-hand item, and offer information, such as pricing. This form is part of a larger ecommerce application and is used to streamline the process of buying and selling second-hand items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on "Contact Information" and fill in your full name, email, and phone number.
2.  Provide details about the second-hand item in the "Used Item Details" field.
3.  In the "Offer Information" field, select "Yes" if you are making an offer on the item, or "No" if you are not.
4.  Enter the proposed price of the item in the "Item Price" field.
5.  Optionally, include a proposal message in the "Proposal Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Contact Information** (required: false): Please fill in your full name, email, and phone number. This information will be used to contact you for further communication.
*  **Used Item Details** (required: true): Please provide a detailed description of the second-hand item, including its condition, brand, and any other relevant information that will help the seller understand the item's value.
*  **Offer Information** (required: false): Select "Yes" if you are making an offer on the item, or "No" if you are not.
*  **Item Price** (required: true): Enter the proposed price of the item.
*  **Proposal Message** (required: false): This is an optional field for you to include any additional message with your proposal.
