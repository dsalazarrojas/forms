# Online Shopping Form Euphoria - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Shopping Form Euphoria is designed for customers to input information related to their online purchases. This form can be used by businesses to collect contact details, shipping addresses, product information, and payment options from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Contact Information** (Page 1) with your relevant details.
2. Enter your **Shipping Address** (Page 1) where you would like your purchase to be delivered.
3. Provide **Product Information** (Page 1) about the items you would like to purchase or the product(s) you are interested in.
4. Select your preferred **Delivery Information** (Page 1) option from the available choices.
5. Choose your preferred **Payment Information** (Page 1) option from the available choices.
6. Add any **Note** (Page 1) if you have any additional comments or remarks.
7. If needed, provide **Quantity** and **Size** details in the corresponding **Quantity Note** and **Size Note** sections (Page 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information (Page 1)
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required:false): Enter your name, email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you about your order.

### Shipping Address (Page 1)
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required:false): Enter your address where you would like your purchase to be delivered.

### Product Information (Page 1)
* **Product Information** (`product_info`, `text`, required:false): Provide details about the products you would like to purchase or are interested in.

### Delivery Information (Page 1)
* **Delivery Information** (`delivery_info`, `select_one`, required:false): Select one of the available delivery options to suit your needs.

### Payment Information (Page 1)
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, `select_one`, required:false): Choose your preferred payment option.

### Note (Page 1)
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required:false): Add any additional comments or remarks about your order.

### Quantity (Page 2)
* **Quantity** (`quantity_note`, `note`, required:false): If needed, provide details about the quantity of the product you would like to purchase.

### Size (Page 2)
* **Size** (`size_note`, `note`, required:false): If needed, provide details about the size of the product you would like to purchase.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask the form administrator for assistance.
* Make sure to proofread your input for any errors before submitting the form.
