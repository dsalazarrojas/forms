# Short Sleeve T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect customer information for a short sleeve t-shirt order. This includes customer details, product details, payment method, and shipping information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer details, such as name, address, and contact information.
2. Select the payment method you wish to use.
3. Enter the email address and phone number associated with your account.
4. Enter any additional customer notes or comments.
5. Calculate and enter the total cost of your order.
6. Enter the payment amount for your order.
7. Enter your name, address, and other contact information to complete the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details (customer_details)**: Enter your name, address, and other contact information to identify yourself as the customer.
* **Product Details (product_details)**: Enter a description of the product you wish to order.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Select the payment method you wish to use, such as Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Email (customer_email)**: Enter the email address associated with your account or the email address you wish to use for communication.
* **Phone (customer_phone)**: Enter your phone number for communication and order updates.
* **Note (customer_note)**: Enter any additional customer notes or comments about your order.
* **Total (total)**: Calculate and enter the total cost of your order.
* **Payment Amount (payment_amount)**: Enter the payment amount for your order.
* **Name (customer_name)**: Enter your name to complete your customer details.
* **Address (customer_address)**: Enter your address to complete your customer details.
* **City (customer_city)**: Enter the city where you would like to receive your order.
* **State (customer_state)**: Enter the state or region where you would like to receive your order.
* **Zip (customer_zip)**: Enter your zip or postal code to complete your shipping details.
* **Country (customer_country)**: Enter the country where you would like to receive your order.
