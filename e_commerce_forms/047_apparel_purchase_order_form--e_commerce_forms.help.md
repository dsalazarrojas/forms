# Apparel Purchase Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Apparel Purchase Order Form is designed to collect information required for apparel sales. This includes buyer details, order specifications, payment terms, and delivery instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Provide your billing and shipping addresses, making sure to include all relevant details.
3. Specify the items you want to order, including sizes, colors, and quantities.
4. Choose your preferred payment terms.
5. Add any delivery instructions or special handling requirements.
6. Confirm your contact phone number for delivery queries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Buyer Information --
* **Buyer name* (`Buyer name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your name or company name.
### Contact email* (`Contact email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email address for order confirmations and invoices.
### Purchase order number (`Purchase order number`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the internal reference number for this purchase order if you have one.
### Billing address* (`Billing address`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full billing address.
### Shipping address* (`Shipping address`, `text`, `required`): Enter the full delivery address, if different from the billing address.
### Items ordered and specifications* (`Items ordered and specifications`, `text`, `required`): List the items you want to order, including sizes, colors, and quantities.
### Payment terms* (`Payment terms`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose one of the following options: Net 30, Prepaid, Net 60, or Other.
### Delivery instructions (`Delivery instructions`, `text`, `optional`): Provide any loading dock times or special handling requirements.
### Contact phone (`Contact phone`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your phone number for delivery queries.

## Tips
- Please double-check your contact phone number to ensure you receive delivery updates.
- Use precise item descriptions and specifications to avoid order discrepancies.
- Confirm your payment terms with your sales representative before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact our sales team.
