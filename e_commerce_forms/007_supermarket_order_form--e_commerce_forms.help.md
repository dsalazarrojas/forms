# Supermarket Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The supermarket order form is used to collect customer and order information for both in-store and online orders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Customer Details" page to input the customer's name, email, and phone number.
2. On the "Product List" page, select the products you wish to purchase and choose between "In-Store" and "Online" options.
3. On the "Payment Info" page, select the payment method (cash, credit card, or PayPal) and provide any additional payment information.
4. On the "Delivery Info" page, select the delivery method (in-store pickup, delivery, or shipping) and input the delivery address, zip code, city, state, and contact phone number.
5. In the "Order Notes" field, add any additional notes about your order.
6. Finally, review the "Order Summary" for a final check before submitting the form.
