# Supermarket Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The supermarket order form is used to collect customer and order information for both in-store and online orders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Customer Details" page to input the customer's name, email, and phone number.
2. On the "Product List" page, select the products you wish to purchase and choose between "In-Store" and "Online" options.
3. On the "Payment Info" page, select the payment method (cash, credit card, or PayPal) and provide any additional payment information.
4. On the "Delivery Info" page, select the delivery method (in-store pickup, delivery, or shipping) and input the delivery address, zip code, city, state, and contact phone number.
5. In the "Order Notes" field, add any additional notes about your order.
6. Finally, review the "Order Summary" for a final check before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Details**: Customer Details are collected on this page, including customer's name, email, and phone number.
  - **Customer Name:** Enter your name as it is written on your ID or passport.
  - **Customer Email:** Enter a valid email address to allow us to contact you regarding your order.
  - **Customer Phone:** Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any delivery or payment issues.
- **Products:** Select the products you want to purchase from the available options.
  - Select between "In-Store" and "Online" options based on your purchase preference.
  - Select multiple products if you are purchasing more than one item.
- **Payment Info:** Select your payment method and provide any additional payment information.
  - Select "Cash" for in-store payments.
  - Select "Credit Card" for credit card payments.
  - Select "PayPal" for online payments.
  - Provide any additional payment information required for your selected method.
- **Delivery Info:** Select your delivery method and input the delivery address.
  - Select "In-Store Pickup" for in-store pickup.
  - Select "Delivery" for delivery to your address.
  - Select "Shipping" for shipping to your address.
  - Select "Self Pickup" for self-delivery at a specified location.
  - Input delivery address, zip code, city, state, and contact phone number.
- **Order Notes:** Add any additional notes about your order.
  - Provide any special requests or instructions for your order.
- **Customer Email:** Enter a valid email address to allow us to contact you regarding your order.
- **Customer Phone:** Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any delivery or payment issues.
- **Customer Note:** Add any additional notes for the store staff.
  - Provide any relevant information about your order that might be helpful for our staff.
- **Order Date:** Enter the date of your order.
- **Order Time:** Enter the time of your order.
- **Product Select:** Select your product from the available options.
  - Select one product from the options.
- **Products:** Select multiple products from the available options.
  - Select all the products you want to purchase.
- **Payment Method:** Select how you will pay for your order.
  - Select "Cash" for in-store payments.
  - Select "Credit Card" for credit card payments.
  - Select "PayPal" for online payments.
- **Payment Info:** Provide any additional payment information required for your selected method.
  - Provide any necessary payment information for your chosen payment method.
- **Delivery Method:** Select how you will receive your order.
  - Select "In-Store Pickup" for in-store pickup.
  - Select "Delivery" for delivery to your address.
  - Select "Shipping" for shipping to your address.
  - Select "Self Pickup" for self-delivery at a specified location.
- **Delivery Address:** Input your delivery address.
  - Enter your complete address including address line 1 and 2, city, state, and zip code.
- **Zip Code:** Input your zip code.
  - Enter your zip code for delivery purposes.
- **City:** Enter your city.
  - Enter the city where your delivery address is located.
- **State:** Enter your state.
  - Enter the state where your delivery address is located.
- **Contact Phone:** Input your contact phone number.
  - Enter the phone number we can contact you at regarding delivery issues.
- **Delivery Note:** Add any additional delivery notes.
  - Provide any special requests or instructions for delivery.
- **Total Price:** This is an auto-filled field that shows the total price of the order.
  - This field is automatically calculated based on your selected products and payment method.
- **Discount:** This is an auto-filled field that shows the applied discount.
  - This field is automatically calculated based on your selected products and payment method.
- **Tax:** This is an auto-filled field that shows the applied tax.
  - This field is automatically calculated based on your selected products and payment method.
- **Order Notes 2:** Add any additional notes about your order.
  - Provide any special requests or instructions for your order.

## Tips
1. Make sure to select the correct delivery method and address for your order.
2. Double-check the total price and payment information before submitting the form.
3. Ensure to enter accurate contact information so we can contact you about your order.
4. Read and understand each field's purpose before submitting the form.
5. Review the "Order Summary" before submitting the form to ensure all information is accurate.
6. Take note of any additional delivery notes or instructions for your order.
