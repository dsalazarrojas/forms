# Manual Delivery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manual Delivery Order Form is used to collect customer information, delivery details, and payment information for manual delivery orders. It is intended to be used when a customer places a new order or updates an existing one.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's information in the "Customer Information" section, including their name, address, city, state, and zip code.
2. Provide the delivery details, including the delivery method and status.
3. Enter the payment information, including the payment method and any relevant notes.
4. Add any additional order items in the "Order Items" section.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments about the delivery.
6. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting it.
