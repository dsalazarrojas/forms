# e_commerce_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e_commerce_referral_form is a form used to collect information from customers about their referral sources and preferences. It helps in maintaining accurate and consistent data.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have a clear understanding of the form purpose and relevance to your referral information.
- Fill out the form accurately and consistently for each customer referral.
- Review each field for completeness and correctness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **referral_source** (select_multiple, required: false): Select the correct options that describe the source where the customer found us.
	+ Choose one or more of the provided options that best describe where the customer found your company or product.
* **customer_name** (text, required: false): Enter the customer's name as they prefer to be addressed.
	+ Provide the customer's name as it is written on their identification or as they prefer to be called.
* **customer_email** (email, required: false): Enter the customer's email address.
	+ Provide the customer's email address where they can be reached.
* **referral_type** (select_one, required: false): Confirm if the customer is referring or has been referred by another customer.
	+ Choose 'Yes' if the customer is referring someone, 'No' if the customer was referred by someone else.
* **referred_by** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the customer who referred this customer (if applicable).
	+ If the customer was referred by another customer, provide their name as it is written on their identification.
* **referral_reason** (text, required: false): Briefly describe the reason for the referral.
	+ Explain the reason why the customer referred this company or product to someone else.
* **customer_message** (text, required: false): Add any additional information about the customer's referral.
	+ Provide any other details that might be helpful for our team to know.
* **customer_referral_code** (text, required: false): Enter the customer's referral code (if any).
	+ If the customer has a referral code, provide it here.
* **reward** (text, required: false): Describe the reward or incentive the customer received for their referral.
	+ Briefly explain the reward or incentive that motivated the customer to refer someone.
* **customer_referral_message** (text, required: false): Add any additional information about the customer's referral.
	+ Provide any other details that might be helpful for our team to know.

## Tips
- Double-check the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
- Review each field to ensure it aligns with your referral information.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact our support team for clarification.
