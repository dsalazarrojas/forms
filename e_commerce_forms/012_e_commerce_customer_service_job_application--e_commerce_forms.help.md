# E Commerce Customer Service Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your background and experience, so we can discuss potential job opportunities with you. Please answer the questions honestly, and don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, simply fill out the form with your contact information.
2. Next, tell us about your work experience in the field.
3. Answer questions about your education and relevant skills.
4. Let us know about your language abilities and job preferences.
5. Finally, provide your available work dates and current work status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Info**: Please fill out your name, email address, and phone number so we can get in touch with you.
- **Experience**: Describe your past work experience, highlighting any relevant skills or achievements you have.
- **Education**: Select "Yes" if you have completed a degree or certification relevant to the customer service field, or "No" otherwise.
- **Skills**: Choose all the relevant skills you have from the list, such as communication, problem-solving, or teamwork.
- **Language Abilities**: Select all the languages you are fluent in, so we can gauge your ability to interact with our customers in their preferred languages.
- **Job Preferences**: Choose the types of customer service roles or industries you are interested in or have experience with.
- **Available Dates**: Select the dates and times you are available to work, so we can schedule an interview or further evaluation.
- **Work Status**: Select your current work status, whether that's "Active" (currently employed), "Inactive" (not currently employed), or another option if it doesn't fit.
