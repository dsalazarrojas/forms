# Link Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place new orders or update existing orders on our e-commerce platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information in the `User Info` field.
2. Provide your shipping information in the `Shipping Info` field.
3. Select your preferred payment method from the `Payment Method` dropdown.
4. Enter any additional order details in the `Order Details` field.
5. Review the `Order Total` field for the total cost of your order.
6. Update the `Order Status` field to reflect the current status of your order.
7. Enter the date and time you placed the order in the `Order Date` and `Order Time` fields respectively.
8. Enter your phone number in the `Order Phone` field.
9. Enter your email address in the `Email Address` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info (user_info)**:
	+ A field for entering your basic information.
	+ Please provide your name and contact details here.
* **Shipping Info (shipping_info)**:
	+ A field for entering your shipping address details.
	+ Please provide your shipping address here.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**:
	+ A dropdown list of payment options, including Card Number, PayPal, and Bank Transfer.
	+ Select your preferred payment method.
* **Order Details (order_details)**:
	+ A field for entering any additional information related to your order.
	+ Please provide any relevant details about your order here.
* **Order Total (order_total)**:
	+ A field displaying the total cost of your order.
	+ This field is readonly and cannot be edited.
* **Order Status (order_status)**:
	+ A dropdown list of order status options, including Pending, Shipped, and Cancelled.
	+ Update the status of your order here.
* **Order Date (order_date)**:
	+ A field for entering the date you placed the order.
	+ Please provide the date here.
* **Order Time (order_time)**:
	+ A field for entering the time you placed the order.
	+ Please provide the time here.
* **Order Phone (order_phone)**:
	+ A field for entering your phone number.
	+ Please provide your phone number here.
* **Email Address (email_address)**:
	+ A field for entering your email address.
	+ Please provide your email address here.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you are updating an existing order, ensure you are using the correct order ID or details.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
