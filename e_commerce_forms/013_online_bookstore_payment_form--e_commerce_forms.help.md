# Online Bookstore Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture relevant information from customers for online book purchases. It will be reviewed and processed by bookstore staff to confirm order details and customer information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out this form with the required and optional information to the best of your ability. Please ensure you fill out all required fields marked as 'Required'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **form_info**: A note field for any additional information regarding the form, if needed.
- **customer_info**: Please select 'Yes' or 'No' depending on whether you're a new or existing customer.
- **delivery_info**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if delivery is desired or not.
- **payment_info**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you're paying or not.
- **order_items**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each item ordered. Please be accurate as the system will process this information.
- **total_price**: Fill out the total price of the order. Please ensure accuracy as this will also be used for order processing.
- **payment_method**: Although this field is non-essential, it's still here for your reference. It's likely to be used for internal purposes.
- **payment_status**: Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' for the payment status. Although it's not marked as required, it's still a crucial piece of information for order processing.
- **order_note**: A field for any additional comments or messages about the order. Please keep it concise and clear for customer service.
- **customer_phone**: A text field for the customer's phone number. This information will be kept confidential and used for internal purposes only.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully, as it will be used for order processing and customer service.
- If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the form instructions or contact customer service for assistance.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact customer service at your earliest convenience.
