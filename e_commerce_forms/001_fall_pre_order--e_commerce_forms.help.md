# Fall Preorder - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to place a preorder for a specific product during the fall season.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the product you would like to preorder from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the quantity of the product you would like to preorder.
3. Choose how you would like to be contacted.
4. If you are interested in other products, select the option from the checkbox.
5. Enter your email address to receive updates and confirm your preorder details.
6. Add any additional comments or questions you may have.
7. Check if you would like to receive updates on your preorder.
8. Check if you would like to add other products to your preorder.
9. Confirm your email address to ensure accurate communication.
10. Add any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the product being preordered?**
    * `product_preorder`: Select the product you would like to preorder from the dropdown menu.
* **How many would you like to preorder?**
    * `quantity`: Enter the quantity of the product you would like to preorder.
* **How would you like to be contacted?**
    * `contact_method`: Choose how you would like to be contacted.
* **Are you interested in other products?**
    * `other_products`: Check if you are interested in other products.
* **What is your email address?**
    * `email`: Enter your email address to receive updates and confirm your preorder details.
* **Do you have any other comments or questions?**
    * `comments`: Add any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Would you like to receive updates on this preorder?**
    * `updates`: Check if you would like to receive updates on your preorder.
* **Would you like to add any other products to your preorder?**
    * `add_other_products`: Check if you would like to add other products to your preorder.
* **Confirm your email address**
    * `email_confirmation`: Confirm your email address to ensure accurate communication.
* **Do you have any additional comments?**
    * `additional_comments`: Add any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Would you like to confirm you want to receive updates?**
    * `confirm_updates`: Check if you would like to confirm updates on your preorder.
* **Add any other products you would like to preorder?**
    * `additional_products`: Add any other products you would like to preorder. 

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid any issues with your preorder.
* Double-check your email address to ensure accurate communication.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to our customer support team.
* This form is for the fall season, please ensure you are preordering the correct product and quantity. 

Note: This help guide assumes that the form will be filled out by customers who are interested in placing a preorder for the fall season. Please review carefully to ensure all required information is provided. 

This help guide is intended to be a general guide and may not cover every possible scenario. If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to our customer support team. 

Please review this guide carefully before filling out the form.

The form has the following fields:
* **product_preorder**: The product being preordered.
* **quantity**: The quantity of the product being preordered.
* **contact_method**: The contact method chosen by the user.
* **other_products**: Whether the user is interested in other products.
* **email**: The email address of the user.
* **comments**: Additional comments or questions from the user.
* **updates**: Whether the user would like to receive updates on the preorder.
* **add_other_products**: Whether the user would like to add other products to the preorder.
* **email_confirmation**: The user's confirmed email address.
* **additional_comments**: Additional comments or questions from the user.
* **confirm_updates**: Whether the user would like to confirm updates on the preorder.
