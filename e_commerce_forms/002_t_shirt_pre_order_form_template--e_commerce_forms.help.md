# T Shirt Pre Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The T Shirt Pre Order Form Template is designed to collect information from customers who want to pre-order a T shirt. This form gathers necessary details for successful order fulfillment and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely with your personal and order details.
2. Ensure you fill out all mandatory fields (*required*).
3. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, *required*): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on your T shirt.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, *required*): Enter your valid email address for order confirmations and updates.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, *required*): Enter your phone number for order updates and delivery confirmations.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, *optional*): Enter your shipping address to ensure accurate delivery.
* **T Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, select_one, *optional*): Select the size of T shirt you prefer.
* **T Shirt Color** (`t_shirt_color`, select_one, *optional*): Select the color of T shirt you prefer.
* **Shirt Quantity** (`shirt_quantity`, number, *required*): Enter the number of T shirts you would like to order.
* **T Shirt Design** (`t_shirt_design`, select_multiple, *optional*): Select any additional designs you would like on your T shirt.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, *optional*): Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, note, *optional*): Enter any special instructions for your order.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, *optional*): Leave a note for the order team.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, *optional*): Leave a note for the customer.
* **T Shirt Options** (`tshirt_size_options`, select_multiple, *optional*): Select any additional options for your T shirt.
* **Shirt Type** (`shirt_type`, select_multiple, *optional*): Select any additional types for your T shirt.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, *optional*): Enter your shipping address.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, *optional*): Enter your shipping city.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, text, *optional*): Enter your shipping state.
* **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`, text, *optional*): Enter your shipping zip code.
* **T Shirt Price** (`tshirt_price`, number, *optional*): Enter the price you are willing to pay for your order.
* **Customer Order Comments** (`customer_order_comments`, text, *optional*): Leave a comment for the order team.
* **Order Comment** (`order_comment`, text, *optional*): Leave a comment for the order team.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, text, *optional*): Leave a message for the customer.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, *optional*): Select the shipping method you prefer.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, *optional*): Select the payment method you prefer.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, *optional*): Assign the order to a specific team member.
