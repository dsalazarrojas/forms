# E Commerce Agreement Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Commerce Agreement Assessment form is used to collect information from customers regarding their e-commerce agreements, ensuring a clear understanding of the terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Customer Name field with the full name of the customer.
2. Select the appropriate agreement type (yes or no) in the Agreement Type field.
3. Enter the date of the agreement in the Agreement Date field.
4. Enter the length of the agreement term in the Agreement Term field.
5. Fill in the Customer Address field with the customer's address.
6. Enter the customer's phone number in the Customer Phone field.
7. Enter the customer's email address in the Customer Email field.
8. Fill in the Agreement Terms field with any additional terms or conditions.
9. Enter any customer messages or comments in the Customer Message field.
10. Mark the agreement as signed by checking the Signed field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (Customer Name)**: A required field where the full name of the customer is entered.
* **Agreement Type (agreement type)**: Select "yes" to indicate that the customer agrees to the terms and conditions, or "no" otherwise.
* **Agreement Date (Agreement Date)**: A date field where the customer enters the date of the agreement.
* **Agreement Term (Agreement Term)**: A numerical field where the customer enters the length of the agreement term.
* **Customer Address (Customer Address)**: A field where the customer enters their full address.
* **Customer Phone (Customer Phone)**: A field where the customer enters their phone number.
* **Customer Email (Customer Email)**: A field where the customer enters their email address.
* **Agreement Terms (Agreement Terms)**: A field where the customer can enter any additional terms or conditions.
* **Customer Message (Customer Message)**: A field where the customer can enter any comments or messages.
* **Signed (agreement_signed)**: A checkbox field where the customer marks the agreement as signed.
* **Agreement Expires (agreement_expires)**: A date field where the customer enters the date the agreement expires.
* **Customer Note (customer_note)**: A field where the customer can enter any additional notes or comments.

# Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to the customer to ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.
* Use the customer information to update the agreement terms and conditions as needed.
* Keep the customer's information confidential and secure.
