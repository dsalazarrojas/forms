# Purchase A Digital Greeting Card - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for creating a digital greeting card that will be sent to the recipient's email address.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the required fields.
2. Enter the recipient's name and email address in the required fields.
3. Choose the desired card design from the options provided.
4. Enter a message for the recipient in the "Message" field.
5. Select the type of card you want to send (ECARD, PRINTCARD, or PHYSICALCARD).
6. You may also be able to select additional options depending on your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it will be displayed on the digital greeting card.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Recipient's Name** (`recipient_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person you are sending the digital greeting card to.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required): Enter a personalized message for the recipient.
* **Card Design** (`card_design`, select_one, required): Choose the design of the digital greeting card.
* **Recipient's Email** (`recipient_email`, email, required): Enter the recipient's email address.
* **Card Price** (`card_price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the digital greeting card.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Select the date you want the digital greeting card to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Select the time you want the digital greeting card to be delivered.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments for the delivery team.
* **Form Submitted From** (`form_submitted_from`, select_multiple, optional): Select where this form was submitted from (Desktop, Mobile, or Tablet).
* **Form Submitted At** (`form_submitted_at`, date, optional): The date and time this form was submitted.
* **Card Type** (`card_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of card to send (ECARD, PRINTCARD, or PHYSICALCARD).
* **Recipient's Address** (`recipient_address`, text, optional): Enter the recipient's address (if applicable).
* **Recipient's Phone** (`recipient_phone`, text, optional): Enter the recipient's phone number (if applicable).
* **Sender's Address** (`sender_address`, text, optional): Enter your address (if applicable).
* **Sender's Phone** (`sender_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, number, optional): Enter the order ID (if applicable).
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the order (PENDING, DELIVERED, or CANCELLED).
* **Custom Fields** (`custom_fields`, text, optional): Add any additional custom fields.
* **Custom Fields 2** (`custom_fields_2`, text, optional): Add any additional custom fields.
* **Custom Fields 3** (`custom_fields_3`, text, optional): Add any additional custom fields.
* **Custom Fields 4** (`custom_fields_4`, text, optional): Add any additional custom fields.
* **Custom Fields 5** (`custom_fields_5`, text, optional): Add any additional custom fields.
* **Custom Fields 6** (`custom_fields_6`, text, optional): Add any additional custom fields.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the recipient's email address is entered correctly to avoid any delivery issues.
* Please select the correct card type and design to ensure that the recipient receives the correct type of card.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
