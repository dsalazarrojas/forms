# E Commerce Transaction Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Commerce Transaction Consent Form is a necessary step for customers to provide their consent for e-commerce transactions. This form helps us understand their preferences and requirements for future interactions related to their transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the email address associated with your account.
2. Enter the date of the transaction.
3. Enter the name of the product(s) involved in the transaction.
4. Enter the price of the product(s) involved in the transaction.
5. Enter the total amount spent on the transaction.
6. Provide your shipping address for any relevant delivery purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email**: Enter your registered email address, as this will be used for any further communication.
	+ Type: `email` (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`)
	+ Required: No
* **Transaction Date**: Enter the date on which the transaction took place.
	+ Type: `date` (e.g., `2022-09-15`)
	+ Required: No
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product(s) involved in the transaction.
	+ Type: `text` (e.g., "Product A")
	+ Required: No
* **Product Price**: Enter the price of the product(s) involved in the transaction.
	+ Type: `number` (e.g., `12.99`)
	+ Required: No
* **Transaction Amount**: Enter the total amount spent on the transaction.
	+ Type: `number` (e.g., `120.00`)
	+ Required: No
* **Shipping Address**: Provide your shipping address for any relevant delivery purposes.
	+ Type: `text` (e.g., "123 Main St.")
	+ Required: No

## Tips
- Please fill out the form only once, as this will be recorded for your future interactions.
- Double-check your information before submitting to ensure accuracy.
