# Apparel Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you place an order for apparel items. Please fill out the form with the required information to ensure a smooth order process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Full Name** field with your personal details.
2. Enter your **Shipping Address** including street, apartment, and suite numbers.
3. Enter your **City** where the order will be shipped to.
4. Enter your **ZIP / Postal Code** for shipping purposes.
5. Provide your **Phone Number** for contact and order updates.
6. Select the item you would like to order from the **Select Item from Collection** dropdown menu.
7. Choose the color of the item from the **Color Choice** dropdown menu.
8. Select the size of the item from the **Select Size** dropdown menu.
9. Enter the quantity of the item you would like to order.
10. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** from the dropdown menu.
11. If you would like to add gift wrapping to your order, select **Yes** from the **Add Gift Wrapping?** dropdown menu.
12. Finally, read and agree to the **I agree to the return and exchange policy** by selecting the options that apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Full Name**
* **Customer Name** (text): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### **Shipping Address**
* **Shipping Address** (text): Enter your street address, apartment number, and suite number where the order will be shipped to.

### **City**
* **City** (text): Enter the city where the order will be shipped to.

### **ZIP / Postal Code**
* **ZIP / Postal Code** (text): Enter the zip code or postal code where the order will be shipped to.

### **Phone Number**
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your contact phone number for order updates.

### **Select Item from Collection**
* **Select Item from Collection** (select_one): Choose the item you would like to order from the list provided.

### **Color Choice**
* **Color Choice** (select_one): Choose the color of the item you selected from the dropdown menu.

### **Select Size**
* **Select Size** (select_one): Choose the size of the item you selected from the dropdown menu.

### **Quantity**
* **Quantity** (number): Enter the quantity of the item you would like to order.

### **Preferred Payment Method**
* **Preferred Payment Method** (select_one): Choose your preferred payment method from the dropdown menu.

### **Add Gift Wrapping?**
* **Add Gift Wrapping?** (select_one): Select **Yes** if you would like to add gift wrapping to your order.

### **I agree to the return and exchange policy**
* **I agree to the return and exchange policy** (select_multiple): Read and select the options that apply to you, such as I agree or Other.
