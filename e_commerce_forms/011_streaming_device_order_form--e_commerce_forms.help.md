# Streaming Device Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for customers to order streaming devices. It will guide you through the required information for processing your order.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Order Form** section with any relevant details or comments about your order.
2. Provide your shipping information in the **Shipping** section.
3. Enter your **Payment Info** for processing the order.
4. Enter your **Shipping Address** where you want the streaming device delivered.
5. Select your **Shipping Country** from the options.
6. Enter your **Order Details** if applicable.
7. Enter your **Shipping City** name.
8. Enter your **Shipping Street** name and number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Form** (<type:text>): Provide any specific comments or details about your order.
* **Shipping** (<type:text>): This is for general shipping information.
* **Payment Info** (<type:text>): Enter your payment method or any payment-related details.
* **Shipping Address** (<type:text>): Enter your address for shipping.
* **Shipping Country** (<type:text>): Select your country where you want the product shipped.
* **Order Details** (<type:text>): Add any specific details about your order if required.
* **Shipping City** (<type:text>): Enter the city name for your shipping address.
* **Shipping Street** (<type:text>): Enter the street and number for your shipping address.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields correctly to avoid delays or issues with your order.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you need assistance or have questions, you can contact our support team for help.
