# Men’s Grooming Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place orders for men's grooming services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide order details in the designated text field.
2. Enter customer information, such as name and any relevant details.
3. Choose a delivery option (Delivery or Pickup).
4. Select a payment option (Cash, Card, or Other).
5. Enter product information, such as product name and description.
6. Enter the quantity of the product being ordered.
7. Calculate and enter the order total.
8. Choose a date for the order.
