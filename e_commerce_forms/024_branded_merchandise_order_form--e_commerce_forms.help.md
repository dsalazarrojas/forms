# Branded Merchandise Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for custom branded merchandise orders. It captures essential details such as customer contact information, order specifications, and payment methods to facilitate the ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Customer Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** to ensure we can contact you for order confirmation and delivery purposes.
2. If applicable, provide your **Company Name** for the order.
3. Enter your **Shipping Address Line 1**, **City**, **State or Province**, and **ZIP or Postal Code** to ensure accurate delivery.
4. Select the **Country** where the order should be delivered.
5. Choose the desired **Product Category** from the list to narrow down the available products.
6. Select the specific **Selected Products** you want to order by choosing multiple items from the list.
7. Input the **Total Quantity** of items ordered.
8. Specify the **Size Breakdown** if any apparel products are ordered.
9. Provide any additional **Customization Notes** if needed.
10. Enter the **Required Delivery Date** when you need the order to be fulfilled.
11. Select the **Payment Method** used for the order, e.g., Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Purchase Order.
12. Add any **Special Instructions** for the order or delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Full Name** (1): Your legal name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (2): A working email address for order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (3): A phone number for delivery contact and order inquiries.
* **Company Name** (4): Your company or organization name (if applicable).
* **Shipping Address Line 1** (5): Your street address for delivery.
* **Shipping Address Line 2** (6): Apartment, suite, or unit number for delivery.
* **City** (7): The city for the delivery address.
* **State or Province** (8): The state or province for the delivery address.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (9): The ZIP code or postal code for the delivery address.
* **Country** (10): The country for the delivery address.
* **Product Category** (11): Select a category to narrow down available products.
* **Selected Products** (12): Choose specific products from the category.
* **Total Quantity** (13): The total number of items ordered.
* **Size Breakdown** (14): Specify sizes for apparel products if needed.
* **Color Preference** (15): Specify preferred colors for products if needed.
* **Logo Placement** (16): Where on the product you'd like the logo to be placed.
* **Customization Notes** (17): Any special requests for customization.
* **Required Delivery Date** (18): The date by which you need the order to be fulfilled.
* **Payment Method** (19): The payment method used for the order.
* **Special Instructions** (20): Any delivery or order notes.
* **Terms and Conditions** (21): Accept our terms and conditions to complete the order.

## Tips
- Review all fields carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Contact us if you have any questions or need clarification on any field.
