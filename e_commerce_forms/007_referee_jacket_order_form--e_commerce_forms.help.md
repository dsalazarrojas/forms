# Referee Jacket Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to order a referee jacket. It collects basic contact information, order details, and shipping information. Please complete this form to place your order for a referee jacket.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your order number in the "Order Number" field (if applicable).
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown list.
7. Enter the date of your order in the "Date" field.
8. Enter the time of your order in the "Time" field.
9. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
10. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
11. Enter the city of your shipping address in the "Shipping City" field.
12. Enter the state of your shipping address in the "Shipping State" field.
13. Enter the zip code of your shipping address in the "Shipping Zip" field.
14. Enter the country of your shipping address in the "Shipping Country" field.
15. Enter the total cost of your order in the "Total" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name, such as "John" or "Jane".
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname, such as "Doe" or "Smith".
* **Order Number**: If you have an order number, enter it here. This helps us track your order.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you want to pay for your order: Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Date**: Enter the date you'd like to receive your order.
* **Time**: Enter the time you'd like to receive your order.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address, including street, number, and postal code.
* **Shipping City**: Enter the city of your shipping address.
* **Shipping State**: Enter the state of your shipping address.
* **Shipping Zip**: Enter the zip code of your shipping address.
* **Shipping Country**: Enter the country of your shipping address.
* **Total**: Enter the total cost of your order.
