# Sample Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sample Request Form is a simple and easy-to-use form that allows customers to submit a request for a product or service from our e-commerce website. This form is designed to gather essential information from customers to help our customer service team address their inquiries efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of product or service you are interested in by choosing from the options in the "form_type" field.
2. In the "product_name" field, please provide the name of the product or service you are inquiring about.
3. In the "product_description" field, provide a brief description of your request.
4. If you have an example of what you are looking for, please provide it in the "example_request" field.
5. If you prefer to contact us via phone, please enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
6. Finally, please enter your contact information in the "contact_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_type** (Select One): Choose the type of product or service you are interested in. You can select from "Product" or "Service".
* **product_name** (Text): Enter the name of the product or service you are inquiring about.
* **product_description** (Text): Provide a brief description of your request.
* **example_request** (Text): If you have an example of what you are looking for, please provide it here.
* **phone** (Text): If you prefer to contact us via phone, enter your phone number here.
* **contact_info** (Text): Enter your contact information so we can get back to you.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that our customer service team can address your inquiry efficiently.
* If you have any additional information or comments, please include it in the "example_request" field.
* Our customer service team will review your request and respond to you as soon as possible.
