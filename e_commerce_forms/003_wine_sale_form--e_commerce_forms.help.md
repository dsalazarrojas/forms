# Wine Sale Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and data for wine sales.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Customer Information section with your personal details.
2. Provide information about the order you'd like to place in the Order Details section.
3. Select the items you'd like to order in the Order Items section.
4. Choose your payment option in the Payment Information section.
5. Confirm the event details in the Event Details section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`Customer Information`, `text`, required: `false`): Fill out your personal details, such as your name and contact information.
* **Order Details** (`Order Details`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide a brief description of the order you'd like to place.
* **Order Items** (`Order Items`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Choose the wine items you'd like to order. The options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Payment Information** (`Payment Information`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose your payment option. The options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Event Details** (`Event Details`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Confirm if the order is related to an event. The options are 'Yes' and 'No'.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid any issues with your order.
- Double-check your choices in the Order Items and Payment Information sections to ensure accuracy.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact your sales representative for assistance.
