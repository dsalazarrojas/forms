# Financial Aid Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report student financial aid status, which includes the type of aid, amount received, start and end dates, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your student name and other identifying details in fields 3 and 4.
2. Select the type of financial aid you are receiving in field 5.
3. Enter the amount of financial aid you are receiving in field 7.
4. Provide the start and end dates for the financial aid in fields 8 and 9.
5. Update your enrollment status in field 10.
6. If necessary, add additional notes about your enrollment status in field 11.
7. Finally, select the source of the financial aid in field 12.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full student name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Financial Aid Type** (`financial_aid_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of financial aid you are receiving. Options: Financial Aid, Scholarship, Grant, Loan.
* **Financial Aid Amount** (`financial_aid_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of financial aid you are receiving.
* **Financial Aid Start Date** (`financial_aid_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the financial aid.
* **Financial Aid End Date** (`financial_aid_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the financial aid.
* **Student Enrollment Status** (`student_enrollment_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current enrollment status. Options: Pending, Active, Inactive, Denied.
* **Student Enrollment Status Note** (`student_enrollment_status_note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your enrollment status.
* **Student Enrollment Source** (`student_enrollment_source`, select_one, optional): Select the source of the financial aid. Options: Self Reported, Institutional, External Agency, State/Province Government.
