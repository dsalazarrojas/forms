# gymnastics_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gymnastics enrollment form is used to collect information from students enrolling in a gymnastics program offered by an organization or institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** details, which are all required fields.
2. Enter your **Phone** number for contact purposes.
3. Select your preferred **Class Time** from the dropdown list. This will help the organization determine the best time slot for your gymnastics class.
4. Choose your **Experience Level** from the options provided, indicating your current level of expertise in gymnastics.
5. If applicable, provide **Notes** about your previous experience or any special requests you have.
6. You may be assigned a **Assigned Tool**, but this is optional.
7. Select the **Category** of the class you're enrolling in (though this is not required).
8. You can provide an **Output File** (though this is not required).
9. If you have a **Form ID**, you can enter it (though this is not required).
10. Provide a brief **Description** of your background or goals (though this is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A required field to collect your first name.
* **Last Name**: A required field to collect your last name.
* **Email**: A required field to collect your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: A required field to collect your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Birth Date**: A required field to collect your birth date, which will be used to determine your age and suitability for the class.
* **Class Time**: A required field to select your preferred class time. Please choose from Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, or Saturday.
* **Experience**: A required field to select your current level of experience in gymnastics.
* **Notes**: An optional field to provide any additional information about your previous experience or special requests.
* **Assigned Tool**: An optional field to collect any tool or equipment you may be using or have access to.
* **Category**: An optional field to select the category of the class you're enrolling in (though not required).
* **Output File**: An optional field to collect an output file (though not required).
* **Form ID**: An optional field to enter a form ID (though not required).
* **Description**: An optional field to provide a brief background or goals (though not required).
