# School Counseling Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Counseling Intake Form is a document designed to gather information about a student's background, concerns, needs, and goals to provide personalized support in a school setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Provide detailed information about your background, concerns, needs, and goals.
3. Be as specific as possible when describing your concerns, needs, and goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Background** (`student_background`, text, optional): This section is where you can provide information about your personal background, such as your name, age, grade level, or any other relevant details.
* **Student Contact Info** (`student_contact_info`, text, optional): Please list your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Parent Contact Info** (`parent_contact_info`, text, optional): If you are a minor, please list your parent or guardian's contact information, including their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Student Concerns** (`student_concerns`, text, optional): Describe any concerns or issues you are currently facing, such as academic difficulties, personal struggles, or emotional challenges.
* **Student Needs** (`student_needs`, text, optional): Specify what support or accommodations you may need, such as extra time to complete assignments, tutoring, or counseling sessions.
* **Student Goals** (`student_goals`, text, optional): Share your short-term and long-term goals, such as improving grades, developing social skills, or increasing self-confidence.
* **Academic Background** (`academic_background`, text, optional): Describe your academic history, including previous schools attended, courses taken, or academic achievements.
* **Education History** (`education_history`, text, optional): Outline your education history, including any previous education-related achievements or challenges.
* **Health History** (`health_history`, text, optional): Provide information about your health-related concerns or needs, such as medical conditions, medication, or chronic health issues.
* **Additional Notes** (`academic_background`, text, optional): Add any additional information that may help the school counseling team understand your situation better.
 
## Tips

* Please be honest and forthcoming when completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any question, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
* The information you provide will help the school counseling team create a supportive and inclusive environment for you.
* If you have any questions or concerns after completing this form, please don't hesitate to reach out to the school's counseling department for assistance.
