# Student Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Enrollment Form is designed to collect information about a new student's details and enrollment schedule. It is used to enroll a new student in an educational institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "New Student" page with the student's name.
2. Move to the "Student Details" page and provide any additional information about the student.
3. Enter the parents' names on the "Parents" page.
4. Select the fee type on the "Fee Type" page.
5. Schedule the enrollment on the "Student Enrollment Date" and "Student Enrollment Time" pages.
6. Enter the student's email and phone number on the "Student Email" and "Student Phone" pages, respectively.
7. Add any additional comments or notes on the "Student Note" page.
8. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Student** (text, required/optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Student Details** (text, required/optional): Provide any additional information about the student.
* **Parents** (text, required/optional): Enter the parents' names.
* **Guardian Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the guardian's name (this field is likely meant to be for the parent who is also the guardian).
* **Fee Type** (select_one, required/optional): Select the type of fee you want to apply for the student.
* **Student Enrollment Date** (date, required/optional): Schedule the enrollment date.
* **Student Enrollment Time** (time, required/optional): Schedule the enrollment time.
* **Student Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Student Note** (note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the student.
* **Submit** (text, required/optional): Click to complete the form.
