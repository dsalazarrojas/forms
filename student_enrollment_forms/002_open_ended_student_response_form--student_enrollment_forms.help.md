# Open Ended Student Response Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect student feedback and information about their experiences and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your answers to the open-ended questions on this form.
   - What is your favorite hobby? 
   - What is the most interesting thing about your favorite hobby?
   - Tell about a time when you had to overcome a challenge?

2. Enter your personal contact information:
   - Enter your name
   - Enter your email address
   - Enter your phone number

3. Provide your school and program information:
   - Select your school from the list
   - Select your program

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`page_1`, `text`, required: false): This is the first page of the form and serves as an introduction to the other questions.
* **What is your favorite hobby?** (`question_1`, `text`, required: false): Please answer this question to the best of your ability.
* **What is the most interesting thing about your favorite hobby?** (`question_2`, `text`, required: false): Describe the most interesting aspect of your hobby.
* **Tell about a time when you had to overcome a challenge?** (`question_3`, `text`, required: false): Share a story about a time when you overcame a challenge.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your grade level.
* **Student School** (`student_school`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your school.
* **Student Program** (`student_program`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your program.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
- Make sure to enter accurate contact information.
- If you are unsure about a question, feel free to ask for clarification.
