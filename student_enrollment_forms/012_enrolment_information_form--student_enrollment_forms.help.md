# Enrolment Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enrolment Information Form is designed to collect essential demographic and personal data from students or individuals enrolling in an educational institution. This form is intended to be filled out by students or enrollers to provide accurate information for enrollment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Fill out the First Name field with your first name.
2.  Enter your Last Name in the corresponding field.
3.  Select your Date of Birth by choosing the correct month, day, and year in the Date of Birth field.
4.  Enter your email address in the Email field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, false): Fill out this field with your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, false): Enter your last name in this field.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, false): Select your date of birth from the calendar.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, false): Enter a valid email address in this field.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all fields accurately and completely for a smooth enrollment process.
*   If you have any issues with the form, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
