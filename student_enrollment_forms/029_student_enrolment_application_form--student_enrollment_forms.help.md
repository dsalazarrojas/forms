# Student Enrolment Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect student information for enrollment, which can be used to create or update records in a database or system.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name, which is your name as it appears on your records or identification documents.
2. Enter your Last Name, which is your surname as it appears on your records or identification documents.
3. Enter your Date of Birth, using the format YYYY-MM-DD.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" for School Name, indicating whether you are currently enrolled in the school or not.
5. Enter your Parent Name, which is the name of your parent or guardian.
6. Enter your Email, which is your contact email address.
7. Enter your Contact Phone, which is your phone number that can be used to contact you.
8. Enter your Student Number, which is a unique identifier assigned to you by the school or system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text, required/optional)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your records or identification documents.
* **Last Name (text, required/optional)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your records or identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (date, required/optional)**: Enter your date of birth using the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **School Name (select_one, required/optional)**: Select "Yes" to indicate you are currently enrolled in the school, or "No" if you are not.
* **Parent Name (text, required/optional)**: Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Email (email, required/optional)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone (text, required/optional)**: Enter your phone number that can be used to contact you.
* **Student Number (number, required/optional)**: Enter a unique identifier assigned to you by the school or system.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you enter is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the school administration for clarification.
* Make sure to enter your Student Number if it is assigned to you by the school or system.
