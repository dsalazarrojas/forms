# Student Data Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Data Form is designed to collect demographic information from students, ensuring that we have accurate and up-to-date information about each student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the **Student Name** field.
2. Enter the student's mother's name in the **Mothers Name** field.
3. Enter the student's father's name in the **Fathers Name** field.
4. Enter the student's home phone number in the **Home Phone** field.
5. Enter the student's cell phone number in the **Cell Phone** field.
6. Enter the student's work phone number in the **Work Phone** field.
7. Enter the student's home address in the **Home Address** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name as it appears on their records.
* **Mothers Name** (`mother_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's mother's full name.
* **Fathers Name** (`father_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's father's full name.
* **Home Phone** (`home_phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's home phone number.
* **Cell Phone** (`cell_phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's cell phone number.
* **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's work phone number.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, optional): Enter the student's home address.
