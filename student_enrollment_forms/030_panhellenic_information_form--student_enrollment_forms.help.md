# Panhellenic Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Panhellenic Information Form is designed to collect personal and contact information from students for enrollment or registration purposes. It is intended for use in educational or administrative settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is a required field.
2. Provide your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This field is not required, but it is recommended for accurate identification.
3. Enter your valid **Email** in the "Email" field. This is a required field for communication purposes.
4. Note: This is a duplicate field, please ignore it.
5. Provide your **Home Address** in the "Home Address" field. This field is required for address verification.
6. Double-check your information to ensure accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First name`, `text`, required): Enter your personal name.
* **Last Name** (`Last name`, `text`, optional): Enter your family name.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Email** (Duplicate, `note`, not required): This field is a duplicate and not required.
* **Home Address** (`Home Address`, `text`, required): Enter your home address for address verification.

## Tips
* Ensure that your first name is accurately spelled and formatted correctly.
* Enter a valid email address that is actively checked by you.
* Be accurate when providing your home address, including house number and postal code.
* This form does not support multiple email addresses or home addresses, if you have more than one, please contact the relevant authorities for further assistance.
