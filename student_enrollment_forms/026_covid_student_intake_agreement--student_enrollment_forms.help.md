# COVID Student Intake Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects critical information about your student's COVID-19 status and agreements for school policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's information, including their name, ID number (if applicable), and date of birth.
2. Select the student's grade level.
3. Provide your name and contact information as the parent/guardian.
4. Answer questions about the student's COVID-19 vaccination status and history.
5. Agree to participate in daily health screening and report any symptoms immediately.
6. Agree to isolate if the student develops symptoms.
7. Agree to participate in contact tracing if exposed to COVID-19.
8. Confirm understanding of the mask policy.
9. Provide an electronic signature as the parent/guardian.
10. Date and time of agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Fill in the student's name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the student's ID number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the student's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Select the student's current grade level from the provided options (Kindergarten, Elementary, Middle School, High School, College, Other).
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Email Address** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Parent Phone Number** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_one, required): Select the student's COVID-19 vaccination status (Fully vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, Unvaccinated, Unknown).
* **Date of Last Vaccination** (`last_vaccination_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date of the last COVID-19 vaccination.
* **Prior COVID-19 Infection** (`prior_covid`, select_one, optional): Check if the student has had a prior COVID-19 infection (True, False, Unknown). If applicable, enter the date of the infection in the next field.
* **Date of Prior COVID-19 Infection** (`prior_covid_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the prior COVID-19 infection, if applicable.
* **Agreeing to Daily Health Screening** (`daily_health_screening`, select_one, required): Agree to participate in daily health screening (Yes, I agree, No, I do not agree).
* **Agree to Report Any Symptoms Immediately** (`symptom_reporting`, select_one, required): Agree to report any symptoms immediately (Yes, I agree, No, I do not agree).
* **Agree to Isolate If Sick** (`isolation_agreement`, select_one, required): Agree to isolate if the student develops symptoms (Yes, I agree, No, I do not agree).
* **Agree to Participate in Contact Tracing** (`contact_tracing`, select_one, optional): Agree to participate in contact tracing if exposed to COVID-19 (Yes, I agree, No, I do not agree).
* **Understand Mask Policy** (`mask_policy`, select_one, required): Confirm understanding of the school's mask policy (Yes, I understand, No, I do not understand).
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required): Type your full name as an electronic signature.
* **Date of Agreement** (`agreement_date`, date, required): Date and time of agreement.

## Tips

* Please be sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the student's safety and well-being.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the school administration.
