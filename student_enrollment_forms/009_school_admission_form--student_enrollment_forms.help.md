# School Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather essential information from students who are applying to the school. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth admission process.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting, review the form to understand the required sections and fields. You will need to fill out each field in the following order:

1. **Student Details**: Enter your name, email, and phone number to provide a means of contact.
2. **Student Address**: Provide your current address.
3. **Guardian Details**: If you have a guardian, provide their details.
4. **Student Documents**: If required, upload relevant student documents.
5. **Student Notes**: Any additional information about the student.
6. **School Details**: Enter information about your current school level, type, and year.
7. **Student Status**: Choose your current status (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
8. **Student Grade**: Select your current grade.
9. **Student Course**: Enter your current course or subject.
10. **School Type**: Choose the type of school you are currently attending.
11. **Student Level**: Select your current level of education.
12. **School Level**: Select the level of education in your current school.
13. **School Year**: Enter the current year of education.
14. **Student Gender**: Select your gender.
15. **Student DOB**: Enter your date of birth.
16. **Student Age**: Enter your age.
17. **Student Nationality**: Enter your nationality.
18. **Student Language**: Enter your native language.
19. **Student Ethnicity**: Enter your ethnicity.
20. **Student Additional Notes**: Any additional information about the student.
