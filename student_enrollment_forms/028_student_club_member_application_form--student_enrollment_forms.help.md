# Student Club Member Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Club Member Application Form is used to collect information from students who wish to become members of the club. It's a one-page form with several fields that will help the club's administration team assess your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your contact information, such as your email address and phone number.
3. You may also add any additional pages or comments if you have any special requests or additional information to share.
4. If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name and contact information.
5. Finally, select your current student status from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): This field is where you will provide your name and any other relevant personal details. Please ensure that your information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Contact Information** (`label`, `text`, required/optional): This field is where you will provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number. Please ensure that you have provided a valid email address and phone number that can be used to contact you.
* **Additional Pages** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): This field is where you can add any additional pages or comments. If you have any special requests or additional information to share, please use this field.
* **Emergency Contact** (`label`, `text`, required/optional): This field is where you will provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact. Please ensure that you have provided a valid contact information for your emergency contact.
* **Student Status** (`label`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is where you will select your current student status from the options provided. Please select the option that best describes your current status.
