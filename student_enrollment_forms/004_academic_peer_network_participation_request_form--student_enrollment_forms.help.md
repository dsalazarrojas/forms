# Academic Peer Network Participation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students to request participation in the Academic Peer Network, a peer network for academic support and collaboration. By filling out this form, you will be able to join a network that can help you achieve your academic goals and provide support for your academic success.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, including your first name, last name, student ID number, current academic program or major, year of study, preferred peer network, participation goals, availability, and preferred meeting format.
2. Select your preferred peer network from the list of available options.
3. If you have previously participated in an academic peer network or study group, select "True".
4. If you require any special accommodations, describe them in the text box.
5. Finally, select "Yes - I agree" to confirm your agreement to actively participate and abide by the peer network guidelines.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your student ID or institutional records.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your student ID or institutional records.
* **Student email address**: Use your institutional email address to receive updates and communications from the peer network.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Student ID number**: Enter your student ID number as it appears on your institutional records.
* **Current academic program or major**: Select your current academic program or major from the options provided.
* **Year of study**: Select your current year of study from the options provided.
* **Preferred peer network**: Select the peer network you are interested in joining from the options provided.
* **What are your goals for participating in the peer network**: Briefly describe what you hope to achieve through participating in the peer network.
* **When are you typically available for peer network meetings**: Select all the times you are available to meet.
* **Preferred meeting format**: Select your preferred meeting format from the options provided.
* **Have you previously participated in any academic peer network or study group**: Select "True" if you have previously participated in an academic peer network or study group.
* **Do you require any special accommodations**: If you require any special accommodations, describe them in the text box.
* **I agree to actively participate and abide by the peer network guidelines**: Select "Yes - I agree" to confirm your agreement.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure your participation request is processed successfully.
* Be specific and clear in your participation goals and availability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the peer network administrator for support.
