# Student Empowerment Initiative Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Empowerment Initiative Support Request Form is a tool for students to request support for one or more of the three available initiatives. The form is designed to gather essential information about the student's request, ensuring that their needs are properly assessed and addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct category for the Student Empowerment Initiative Support Request Form.
2. Enter your student ID number.
3. Provide your name.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Select the initiative for which you are requesting support.
6. Describe the program details you are applying for in the "Program Details" field.
7. Provide your phone number.
8. Choose the type of support you require (e.g., "Request for initiative 1 support").
9. Specify the duration of support you need.
10. Add any comments or additional information about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Enrollment Forms Category** (`student_enrollment_forms_category`):
	+ This field categorizes the support request based on the initiative you are seeking support for.
	+ Please select "Yes" to indicate that you are requesting support for one of the initiatives.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`):
	+ This is your unique student ID number.
	+ Enter your student ID to ensure that your request is attributed to you.
* **Student Name** (`name`):
	+ This is your name as it appears on your student record.
	+ Enter your name as it is listed on your student ID or in your university records.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`):
	+ This is the email address associated with your student account.
	+ Enter your email address to ensure that you can receive updates and communication about your support request.
* **Support Initiative** (`support_initiative`):
	+ Select the initiative for which you are requesting support.
	+ Please choose one of the three initiatives: "Initiative 1", "Initiative 2", or "Initiative 3".
* **Program Details** (`program_details`):
	+ Describe the specific program or initiative you are applying for.
	+ This will help us understand the context of your support request.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`):
	+ This is your phone number.
	+ Enter your phone number to ensure that we can contact you about your support request.
* **Support Request Type** (`support_request_type`):
	+ Choose the type of support you require (e.g., "Request for initiative 1 support").
	+ Select one or more options to indicate the specific support you need.
* **Requested Support Duration** (`requested_support_duration`):
	+ Specify the duration of support you require.
	+ Please enter a time value to indicate the length of time you need support for.
* **Comments** (`comments`):
	+ Add any additional information or context about your support request.
	+ This is an open-ended field for you to provide any necessary details about your request.
