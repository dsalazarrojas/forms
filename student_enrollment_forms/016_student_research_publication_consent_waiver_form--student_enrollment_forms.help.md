# Student Research Publication Consent Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain student consent for their research work to be published. Please fill out the form to provide your consent and allow your research work to be published.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. Most fields are optional, but please ensure that you select at least one option for each relevant field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consent** (`Select Multiple`): Check the boxes to indicate your preference for publication.
* **Research Work** (`Text`): Describe the research work you are giving permission for.
* **Institutional Name** (`Text`): Enter the name of the institution where the research was conducted.
* **Publication Medium** (`Select One`): Select the medium in which you would like your research to be published (e.g. Journal, Conference, Book).
* **Publication Date** (`Date`): Enter the date of publication.
* **Research Description** (`Text`): Provide a brief description of your research work.
* **Student Name** (`Text`): Enter your name as the student researcher.
* **Publication Location** (`Text`): Enter the location of the publication (e.g. online, print, etc.).
* **Publication Title** (`Text`): Enter the title of the publication.
* **Contact Person** (`Text`): Enter the contact person's name and title.
* **Contact Email** (`Email`): Enter the contact email for the publication.
* **Contact Phone** (`Text`): Enter the contact phone number for the publication.
* **Research Group** (`Text`): Enter the name of the research group.
* **Publication URL** (`Text`): Enter the URL of the publication.
* **Research PII** (`Text`): Check if your research work contains personally identifiable information.
* **Publication PII** (`Text`): Check if the publication contains personally identifiable information.
* **Student Researcher** (`Select One`): Indicate if you are the student researcher.
* **Institutional Review** (`Select One`): Check if the publication has been reviewed by the institution.
* **Reviewer** (`Select One`): Check if you have reviewed the publication.
* **Publication Date Range** (`Date`): Enter the range of dates for the publication.
