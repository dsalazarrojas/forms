> # Student Housing Allocation Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Housing Allocation Booking Form is a means to express interest in a specific accommodation for a student. It is not a comprehensive accommodation selection process but rather a way to provide necessary information to be considered for accommodation allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Student Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Student Phone" field.
4. Describe your accommodation request in the "Accommodation Request" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`<student_name>`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Student Email** (`<student_email>`, email, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your university account.
* **Student Phone** (`<student_phone>`, text, required): Enter a working phone number where you can be reached.
* **Accommodation Request** (`<accommodation_request>`, text, required): Clearly describe the type of accommodation you are looking for. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that your contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
* Be as specific as possible when describing your accommodation request.
