# Boarding School Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains the Boarding School Admission Form, which is designed to collect essential information from students applying to the boarding school. The form aims to identify the student, gather their academic background, and obtain contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
Please review the following steps to complete the form successfully:

1. Enter your full legal name in the "Student name" field.
2. Enter your date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy) in the "Date of birth" field.
3. Type the grade you are applying for in the "Grade applying for" field.
4. Enter the name and contact information of your parent or guardian in the "Parent or guardian name" field.
5. Provide your emergency contact phone number in the "Emergency contact phone" field.
6. If applicable, list any chronic conditions or allergies in the "Medical conditions" field.
7. If you have attended another school before, enter the name and location of the school in the "Previous school attended" field (optional).
8. Write a short essay expressing your thoughts, goals, and motivations for joining the boarding school in the "Personal statement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student name**: A text field to enter your full legal name.
  * Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
  * This field is required for identification purposes.
- **Date of birth**: A date field to enter your birth date.
  * Format: mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 01/01/1999)
  * This field is required to determine your age.
- **Grade applying for**: A number field to select the grade you are applying for.
  * Choose the grade level you are currently in or the grade level you will be attending if you are transferring.
  * This field is required to determine your academic background.
- **Parent or guardian name**: A text field for the name and contact information of your parent or guardian.
  * Enter the name and contact details of your primary guardian.
  * This field is required for emergency contact purposes.
- **Emergency contact phone**: A text field for your emergency contact phone number.
  * Enter the phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
  * This field is required.
- **Medical conditions**: A text field for any chronic conditions or allergies.
  * If you have any medical conditions, please list them here (e.g., diabetes, peanut allergy).
  * This field is optional.
- **Previous school attended**: A text field for the name and location of the school you previously attended.
  * If you have attended another school, enter the name and location of that school.
  * This field is optional.
- **Personal statement**: A text field for a short essay expressing your thoughts, goals, and motivations.
  * Write a brief essay (max 500 words) explaining why you want to join the boarding school.
  * This field is required.
