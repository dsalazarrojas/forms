# Student Policy Advisor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students about their policy advisor application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your student's name in the `Student Name` field.
2. Check the `Program Interest` field for the programs your student is interested in.
3. Enter your student's email address in the `Email` field.
4. Enter your student's ID number in the `Student ID` field.
5. Enter your student's phone number in the `Phone` field.
6. Select your student's status as `Active` or `Inactive` in the `Student Status` field.
7. Select the date of graduation in the `Graduation Date` field.
8. Select the time of graduation in the `Graduation Time` field.
9. Enter your student's major in the `Major` field.
10. Select your student's level as `High` or `Low` in the `Student Level` field.
11. Check the `Student Type` field for the types that apply to your student.
12. If your student has any other type, enter it in the `Other Student Type` field.
13. Enter your student's faculty in the `Faculty` field.
14. Enter your student's department in the `Department` field.
15. Enter your student's group in the `Student Group` field.
16. Enter the name of your student's advisor in the `Advisor Name` field.
17. Enter your advisor's email address in the `Advisor Email` field.
18. Enter your advisor's phone number in the `Advisor Phone` field.
19. Select whether the advisor has the title of `Yes` or `No` in the `Advisor Title` field.
20. Enter the advisor's department in the `Advisor Department` field.
21. Add any additional notes in the `Notes` field.
22. Enter your name as the submitter in the `Submitter` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your student's name.
* **Program Interest** (`program_interest`, select_multiple, required): Check all programs your student is interested in.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your student's email address.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter your student's ID number.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your student's phone number.
* **Student Status** (`student_status`, select_one, required): Select whether your student is `Active` or `Inactive`.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, required): Select the date of graduation.
* **Graduation Time** (`graduation_time`, time, required): Select the time of graduation.
* **Major** (`major`, text, required): Enter your student's major.
* **Student Level** (`student_level`, select_one, required): Select your student's level as `High` or `Low`.
* **Student Type** (`student_type`, select_multiple, required): Check all types that apply to your student.
* **Other Student Type** (`student_type_other`, text, required): If your student has any other type, enter it here.
* **Faculty** (`faculty`, text, required): Enter your student's faculty.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your student's department.
* **Student Group** (`student_group`, text, required): Enter your student's group.
* **Advisor Name** (`advisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your student's advisor.
* **Advisor Email** (`advisor_email`, email, required): Enter your advisor's email address.
* **Advisor Phone** (`advisor_phone`, text, required): Enter your advisor's phone number.
* **Advisor Title** (`advisor_title`, select_multiple, required): Select whether your advisor has the title of `Yes` or `No`.
* **Advisor Department** (`advisor_department`, text, required): Enter your advisor's department.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes about your student.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, required): Enter your name as the submitter.
