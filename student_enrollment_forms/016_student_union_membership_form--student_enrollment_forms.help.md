# Student Union Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from students to determine their eligibility for membership in the Student Union. It helps track membership status and facilitates communication with the student union.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have your student ID number ready.
2. Fill in your name and contact information (email and phone number).
3. Select your membership type (Full-time or Part-time).
4. Choose your department (Academic, Research, or Other).
5. Choose your faculty (Faculty of Arts and Sciences, Faculty of Engineering and Architecture, or Other).
6. Enter your membership date and time (if applicable).
7. Indicate if you are currently a member of the Student Union (Yes or No) and provide the reason for your decision.
8. If you are a member, list your interests.
9. If you have an emergency contact, provide their name, phone number, and relationship to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID**: Enter your unique student ID number to identify yourself as a student.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your student record.
* **Email**: Enter your official university email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Membership Type**: Select whether you are a Full-time or Part-time student.
* **Department**: Choose your department of study (Academic, Research, or Other).
* **Faculty**: Select your faculty of affiliation (Faculty of Arts and Sciences, Faculty of Engineering and Architecture, or Other).
* **Membership Date**: If applicable, enter the date you joined the Student Union.
* **Membership Time**: If applicable, enter the time you joined the Student Union.
* **Student Union**: Indicate if you are currently a member of the Student Union (Yes or No).
* **Reason for Joining Student Union**: Provide a brief explanation for why you joined the Student Union.
* **Student Union Interests**: If you are a member, list your interests related to the Student Union.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their phone number.
* **Relationship**: If you have an emergency contact, indicate their relationship to you (Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, or Other).

## Tips
* Ensure you are filling in accurate and up-to-date information to ensure your membership status is correctly recorded.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask your student union representative for guidance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your student union office.
* Please do not submit this form multiple times, as this can cause confusion and may affect your membership status.
