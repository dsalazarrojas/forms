# Student Enrollment Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for students or parents to provide required information and approval for student enrollment in a particular program or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form with the required information, and make sure to select options correctly for fields with dropdown or radio options.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, optional): Enter the student's first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, optional): Enter the student's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, optional): Enter the student's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter the student's contact phone number.
* **Grade Level** (`Grade Level`, select_one, optional): Select the student's grade level (High School, Middle School, or College).
* **Parent Name** (`Parent Name`, text, optional): Enter the student's parent's name if required.
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Student ID** (`Student ID`, number, optional): Enter the student's ID (if applicable).
