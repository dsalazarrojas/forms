# Gap Year Program Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gap Year Program Admission Form is designed to collect contact information, academic background, and program interest from applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your contact information.
2. Indicate your interest in the program.
3. Provide details about your academic background.
4. Submit the form by selecting "Yes" under the "Submit Form" field.
5. Enter your name, title, department, job title, organization, phone number, and email address under the "Submitter Information" section.
6. Review and acknowledge the agreements listed under "Submitter Agreement" and "Submitter Agreement 2".
7. Select "Yes" under "Submitter Agreement 3" if applicable.
8. Provide any additional comments or notes under "Submitter Comments".
9. Submit your application by selecting "Yes" under "Submitter Agreement 4", "Submitter Agreement 5", "Submitter Agreement 6", "Submitter Agreement 7" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Page 1)** (`contact_information`, text, required: false)
  * Please provide your name, address, and any other relevant contact details. This will help us get in touch with you about your application status.
* **Program Interest (Page 1)** (`program_interest`, select_multiple, required: true)
  * Indicate your interest in the program by selecting "Yes" if you are interested in participating.
* **Academic Background (Page 1)** (`academic_background`, text, required: false)
  * Briefly describe your academic background, including any relevant coursework, research experience, or academic achievements.
* **Education Background (Page 1)** (`education_background`, text, required: false)
  * Provide details about your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **Personal Statement (Page 2)** (`personal_statement`, text, required: true)
  * Write a brief statement outlining your goals, motivations, and expectations from the Gap Year Program.
* **References (Page 2)** (`references`, text, required: false)
  * List any relevant references or recommendations from academic or professional contacts.
* **Submit Form (Page 2)** (`submit_form`, select_one, required: false)
  * Select "Yes" to confirm your submission of this form.
* **Submitter Information (Page 3)** (`submitter_name`, text, required: false)
  * Enter your name, title, department, job title, organization, phone number, and email address.
* **Submitter Agreement (Page 3)** (`submitter_agreement`, select_one, required: false)
  * Review and acknowledge the agreements by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Submitter Agreement 2 (Page 4)** (`submitter_agreement2`, select_one, required: false)
  * Review and acknowledge additional agreements by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Submitter Agreement 3 (Page 4)** (`submitter_agreement3`, select_one, required: false)
  * If applicable, select "Yes" to acknowledge agreement.
* **Submitter Agreement 4 (Page 5)** (`submitter_agreement4`, select_one, required: false)
  * Select "Yes" to confirm submission and agreement with the program.
* **Submitter Agreement 5 (Page 5)** (`submitter_agreement5`, select_one, required: false)
  * Select "Yes" to acknowledge terms and conditions.
* **Submitter Agreement 6 (Page 5)** (`submitter_agreement6`, select_one, required: false)
  * Select "Yes" to confirm understanding of policies and procedures.
* **Submitter Agreement 7 (Page 5)** (`submitter_agreement7`, select_one, required: false)
  * Select "Yes" to confirm submission and acknowledgment of agreements.
* **Submitter Comments (Page 6)** (`submitter_comments`, text, required: false)
  * Provide any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Submitter Notes (Page 6)** (`submitter_notes`, text, required: false)
  * Add any additional notes or details about your application.
* **Submitter Agreement 7 (Page 7)** (`submitter_agreement7`, select_one, required: false)
  * Select "Yes" to confirm submission and acknowledgment of agreements.
* **Submit (Page 7)** (`submit_form`, select_one, required: false)
  * Select "Yes" to submit your application.
