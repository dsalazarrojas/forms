# Community Tutoring Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from students and their guardians or parents for the Community Tutoring program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Student full name**: Enter the student's full name.
2. **Guardian full name (optional)**: If the student is a minor, please enter the guardian or parent's full name.
3. **Contact email**: Enter the student's contact email address. This will be used for schedules and updates.
4. **Grade level**: Select the student's current grade level from the options provided.
5. **Subjects needing support**: Select all the subjects the student needs help with.
6. **Preferred tutoring schedule**: Enter the days and times you are available for tutoring.
7. **Learning goals**: Briefly describe the student's learning goals.
8. **Accommodations needed**: Describe any special learning needs or accommodations the student may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student full name**: This is the student's full name, including first and last name.
* **Guardian full name**: If the student is a minor, this field is used to collect the guardian or parent's full name. (Optional)
* **Contact email**: This is the student's contact email address. It will be used for schedules and updates.
* **Grade level**: Select the student's current grade level from the provided options.
* **Subjects needing support**: Select all subjects the student needs help with.
* **Preferred tutoring schedule**: Enter the days and times you are available for tutoring.
* **Learning goals**: Briefly describe the student's learning goals.
* **Accommodations needed**: Describe any special learning needs or accommodations the student may have.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the student is aware of the form's content and purpose before proceeding.
* This form is not intended to be filled out by anyone other than the student or their guardian/parent.
* Be as specific as possible when describing the student's learning goals and accommodations.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask a program staff member for guidance.
