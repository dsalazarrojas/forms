# Academic Learning Support Hub Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Learning Support Hub Access Request Form is used to gather information from students and staff members to provide access to various support services. This form helps the institution understand individual needs and preferences, ensuring that support is tailored to meet each user's requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To access the Academic Learning Support Hub, follow these steps:
1. Enter your full name as the person requesting access.
2. Provide your institutional email address for communication.
3. Enter your student or staff ID number.
4. Select your current role or status at the institution.
5. Choose the academic program or department you are affiliated with.
6. Select all the support services you need access to (e.g., tutoring, writing center, math lab).
7. Indicate how often you would like to access support services.
8. Specify when you are typically available for support sessions.
9. Describe any specific academic challenges you are facing.
10. If you have used support services before, indicate your previous experience.
11. If you have any special accommodation needs, enter a description.
12. Choose your preferred format for receiving support (in-person, online, etc.).
13. State what you hope to achieve through these services.
14. Consent to have your usage data collected for service improvement.
15. Add any additional comments about your support needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`): Enter your full name as the person requesting access.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`): Provide your institutional email address for communication.
* **Student/Staff ID** (`applicant_id`): Enter your official ID number.
* **Role/Status** (`applicant_role`): Select your current role or status at the institution (e.g., undergraduate student, faculty member).
* **Academic Program/Department** (`academic_program`): Choose your current program or department affiliation.
* **Support Services Needed** (`support_services_needed`): Select all support services you need access to (e.g., tutoring, writing center, math lab).
* **Specific Academic Area** (`specific_academic_area`): Describe any specific academic challenges you are facing.
* **Preferred Support Frequency** (`support_frequency`): Indicate how often you would like to access support services (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, as needed).
* **Availability Schedule** (`availability_schedule`): Specify when you are typically available for support sessions.
* **Learning Challenges** (`learning_challenges`): Describe any specific academic challenges you are facing.
* **Previous Support Experience** (`previous_support_experience`): Indicate if you have used support services before.
* **Accommodation Requirements** (`accommodation_requirements`): Enter any special accommodation needs you have.
* **Preferred Support Format** (`preferred_support_format`): Choose your preferred format for receiving support (e.g., in-person, online).
* **Expected Outcome** (`expected_outcome`): State what you hope to achieve through these services.
* **Consent to Data Use** (`consent_to_data_use`): Consent to having your usage data collected for service improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Add any other information about your support needs.
