# Student Wellbeing Research Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Wellbeing Research Network Registration Form is a crucial document that gathers essential information about students, including their contact details, student enrolment details, and student wellbeing details. This form is essential for the registration process, and accurate completion is vital for smooth participation in the wellbeing research network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting the form.
2. Fill out the **Main Form** section with a brief description of why you are enrolling in the Student Wellbeing Research Network.
3. In the **Contact Details** section, enter your contact information, including your **Email** and **Phone**.
4. In the **Student Enrolment Details** section, provide your details, such as student ID, year of study, and other relevant information.
5. In the **Student Wellbeing Details** section, answer questions related to your wellbeing, such as your Date of Birth and Time of Submission.
6. In the **Note** section, add any additional comments or notes related to your registration.
7. Finally, review the form for accuracy and click the **Submit** button to complete the registration process.

* **Main Form** (`text`, not required): A brief description of why you are enrolling in the Student Wellbeing Research Network.
* **Wellbeing Research Network Registration Form** (`text`, not required): This field seems to be a duplicate of the **Main Form** field and can be left blank.
* **Contact Details** (`text`, not required): Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
	+ **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address in the format of `example@domain.com`.
	+ **Phone** (`text`, not required): Enter your phone number in the format of `123-456-7890`.
* **Student Enrolment Details** (`text`, not required): Provide your student details, such as student ID, year of study, and other relevant information.
* **Student Wellbeing Details** (`text`, not required): Answer questions related to your wellbeing, such as Date of Birth and Time of Submission.
	+ **Date of Birth** (`date`, not required): Enter your date of birth in the format of `YYYY-MM-DD`.
	+ **Time of Submission** (`time`, not required): Enter the time of your submission in the format of `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Note** (`note`, not required): Add any additional comments or notes related to your registration.
* **Submit** (`text`, not required): Click this button to complete the registration process.
