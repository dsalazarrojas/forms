# Student Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students, which can be useful for tracking student progress, attendance, and other related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your Student ID if required.
2. Fill in your First and Last Name.
3. Provide your Date of Birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Select your current Grade Level from the provided options.
5. Enter your Email and Phone Number (if applicable).
6. Add any Additional Comments or Additional Info you'd like to provide about yourself.
7. Enter your Student Name and Teacher Name (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID (ID number, required/optional)**: Please enter your unique Student ID if required.
* **First Name (Text, optional)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (Text, optional)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (Date, optional)**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Grade Level (Select One, required)**: Choose your current grade level from the provided options.
* **Email (Email, optional)**: Enter your email address, if applicable.
* **Phone Number (Text, optional)**: Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **Additional Comments (Note, optional)**: Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share.
* **Additional Info (Note, optional)**: Add any additional information you'd like to provide.
* **Student Name (Text, optional)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Teacher Name (Text, optional)**: Enter your teacher's name if you're a student with a teacher assigned to you.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your teacher or school administration.
* This form is for collecting general student information, not for sensitive or confidential data.
