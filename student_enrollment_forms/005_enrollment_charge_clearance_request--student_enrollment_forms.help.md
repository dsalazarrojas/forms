# Enrollment Charge Clearance Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enrollment Charge Clearance Request form is used to request clearance for enrollment charges related to student enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the student whose clearance is requested.
2. Select the reason for clearance from the provided options.
3. Enter the date and time of clearance.
4. Choose the submitted by and cleared by fields from the provided options.
5. Set the status of the request to either "Pending", "Approved", or "Rejected".
6. Add any comments related to the clearance request.
7. Set the submit and updated fields with the current date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_id** (`student_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Student's Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter the full name of the student.
* **email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for the student.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`, number, required/optional): Enter a unique ID number for the student.
* **Course ID** (`course_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the ID number for the course.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the course.
* **Reason for Clearance** (`reason_for_clearance`, text, required/optional): Choose a reason for the clearance request from the provided options.
* **Date of Clearance** (`date_of_clearance`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of clearance.
* **Time of Clearance** (`time_of_clearance`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of clearance.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required/optional): Choose who submitted the request, either "Student", "Faculty", or "Staff".
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the request was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the request was submitted.
* **Cleared By** (`cleared_by`, select_one, required/optional): Choose who cleared the request, either "Student", "Faculty", or "Staff".
* **Cleared Date** (`cleared_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the request was cleared.
* **Cleared Time** (`cleared_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the request was cleared.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the status of the request, either "Pending", "Approved", or "Rejected".
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information related to the request.
* **Cleared** (`cleared`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information related to the clearance.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any comments related to the clearance request.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the request was submitted.
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the request was submitted.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, select_one, required/optional): Choose who updated the request, either "Student", "Faculty", or "Staff".
* **Updated Date** (`updated_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the request was updated.
* **Updated Time** (`updated_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the request was updated.

Please note that all fields are labeled with their respective names and types, and are explained in detail above.
