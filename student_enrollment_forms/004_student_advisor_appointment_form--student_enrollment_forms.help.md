# Student Advisor Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help students schedule meetings with advisors for various reasons, such as academic guidance, course selection, or personal issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the advisor's name in the "Advisor Name" field.
3. Select a date for the meeting in the "Date" field.
4. Choose the start time for the meeting in the "Start Time" field.
5. Choose the end time for the meeting in the "End Time" field.
6. If desired, provide a meeting duration in the "Meeting Duration" field.
7. Enter the purpose of the meeting in the "Meeting Purpose" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (text, <required>):** Enter your student name.
* **Advisor Name (text, <required>):** Enter the name of the advisor you want to meet with.
* **Date (date, <required>):** Select a date for the meeting.
* **Start Time (time, <required>):** Choose the start time for the meeting.
* **End Time (time, <required>):** Choose the end time for the meeting.
* **Meeting Duration (text, <optional>):** If desired, provide the expected duration of the meeting.
* **Meeting Purpose (text, <optional>):** Enter the reason for the meeting.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have not already scheduled an appointment with the same advisor for the same reason.
* If you need to make changes to your appointment, please contact the advisor directly or use the institution's appointment system to update your appointment.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact <form@gic.mx>.
