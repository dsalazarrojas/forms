# Erasmus Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Erasmus Application Form is designed to collect information from students who are interested in participating in the Erasmus program, a European Union-funded exchange program that allows students to study or work in another EU country. This form is used to gather essential details about the student, including their personal and contact information, as well as their preferred program name.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is the name you would like to be referred to by in the program.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone" field. This will help us contact you for any further information.
5. Select your preferred **Program Name** from the list provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This is the first name that you would like to be referred to by in the program.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This is the last name that you would like to be referred to by in the program.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This is the email address that we will use to contact you for any further information or updates about the program.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`, text, optional): This is your phone number that we will use to contact you for any further information or updates about the program.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): Please select the name of the program that you are interested in applying for.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any issues or concerns while completing the form, please contact us at [support email/phone number].
* Once you have submitted the form, please allow a few days for us to review and process your application.
* You will receive a notification once your application has been processed and a decision has been made.
