# Student Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Form is used for registering student information in the student enrollment process. This form is intended for parents or guardians to provide details about their child or ward.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by providing the requested information for your child or ward.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding.
3. Double-check that you are entering accurate and up-to-date information.
4. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Student First Name, text, optional): Enter your child's first name.
* **Last Name** (Student Last Name, text, optional): Enter your child's last name.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your child's email (if applicable).
* **Phone** (phone number, optional): Enter your child's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your child's date of birth.
* **Gender** (select one: M for Male, F for Female, O for Other, optional): Select your child's gender.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter your child's address.
* **Parent Phone** (text, optional): Enter your child's contact information (if applicable).
* **Grade Level** (number, optional): Enter your child's grade level (if applicable).
* **Start Date** (date, optional): Enter the date your child started in the school (if applicable).
* **End Date** (date, optional): Enter the date your child ended in the school (if applicable).
* **Emergency Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Phone** (text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the person for emergencies.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, you may consult with the school administration or your child's school representative.
* If you are providing contact information, ensure it is correct and up-to-date.
