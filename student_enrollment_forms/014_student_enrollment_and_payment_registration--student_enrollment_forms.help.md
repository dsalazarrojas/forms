# Student Enrollment And Payment Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Student Enrollment And Payment Registration" form is designed to help students provide their necessary details and payment information for enrollment purposes. This form is required to be completed accurately and correctly to avoid any processing issues or duplication of work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Start by filling in the basic details of the student, including their name, phone number, and email address.
2. Ensure you provide your correct student name and enrollment date in the respective fields.
3. Choose a payment method among Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or Cash for processing your payment.
4. Fill in your address accurately, if required.
5. Provide your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).
6. Confirm your enrollment date in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Provide your contact phone number for future communication.
* **Student Enrollment Details** (`student_enrollment_details`, text, optional): Briefly describe your enrollment purpose, if necessary.
* **Tuition Payment Info** (`tuition_payment_info`, text, optional): If applicable, provide any specific payment instructions or references.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select multiple, optional): Choose one or more from Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or Cash for payment processing.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): If required, provide your residential or mailing address.
* **Student DOB** (`student_dob`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YY.
* **Enrollment Date** (`student_enrollment_date`, date, optional): Provide the date you plan to enroll in school.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill in your details accurately to avoid errors and duplication of work.
- Review each field carefully before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.
- If you need help or have questions, contact your administrator or support team for assistance.
