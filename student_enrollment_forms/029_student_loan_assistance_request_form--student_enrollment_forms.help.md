# Student Loan Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Loan Assistance Request Form is designed to provide students with the necessary information and tools to request assistance with their student loans. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide detailed information about your loan needs, including your current status as a student, loan amount, repayment plan, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each field explanation below to ensure you understand what information each field requires.
2. Fill out each field completely and accurately, according to your needs and circumstances.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Current Student Status** (`student_loan_status`, select_one, optional)
* This field asks about your current student status, such as whether you are a full-time or part-time student. This information helps us understand your loan eligibility.

### 2. **Loan Type** (`loan_type`, select_one, optional)
* Select the type of loan you are applying for, such as Federal, Private, or In-Loan Default.

### 3. **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, optional)
* Enter the amount of loan you are requesting assistance with. If you are unsure, please provide as much detail as you can.

### 4. **Repayment Plan** (`repayment_plan`, select_one, optional)
* Choose your repayment plan from the following options:
	+ Standard Repayment
	+ Income-Driven Repayment
	+ Pay-As-You-Go

### 5. **Loan Forgiveness Options** (`loan_forgiveness`, select_one, optional)
* Select which loan forgiveness options you are interested in, such as:
	+ Public Service Loan Forgiveness
	+ Deferment
	+ Forbearance

### 6. **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional)
* Please provide your contact information, including your name and contact email address.

### 7. **Student Loan Assistance** (`student_loan_assistance`, text, optional)
* Provide any additional information about your student loan assistance needs or concerns.

### 8. **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional)
* This field is a free-text area for you to provide any additional comments or details.

### 9. **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
* Enter your email address for further communication.

### 10. **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
* Enter your phone number for further communication.

### 11. **Notes** (no label, type: note, optional)
* This field is intended for any additional comments or details. Please feel free to use it to provide any extra information about your request.
