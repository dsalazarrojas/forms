# Work Study Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students for work study programs, including application date, student ID, name, contact email, phone number, address, program name, start and end dates, and program status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all the necessary information before starting to fill it out.
2. Fill out the "Application Form" field with a clear and concise description of your application.
3. Enter your "Application Date" in the required date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
4. Provide your "Student ID" number.
5. Enter your "Student Name" in the text field.
6. Enter your "Contact Email" in the email format (jane.doe@example.com).
7. Enter your "Student Phone" number in the text field.
8. Enter your "Student Address" in the text field.
9. Enter your "Program Name" in the text field.
10. Enter your "Program Start Date" and "Program End Date" in the date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
11. Select your "Program Status" from the dropdown menu (Active or Inactive).
12. If your program is inactive, enter a brief explanation for the "Reason for Inactivation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, optional): A brief description of your application.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you're submitting your application in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Your unique student ID number.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your student ID.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address in the email format (jane.doe@example.com).
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in the text field.
* **Student Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address in the text field.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the program you're applying for.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the program in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the program in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Program Status** (`program_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your program from the dropdown menu (Active or Inactive).
* **Reason for Inactivation** (`reason_for_inactivation`, text, optional): If your program is inactive, enter a brief explanation for the reason.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid any potential discrepancies or delays.
* If you're unsure about any field, please refer to this guide for clarification.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities for assistance.
