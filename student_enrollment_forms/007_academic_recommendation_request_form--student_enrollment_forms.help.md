# Academic Recommendation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from students to request a letter of recommendation from a teacher or mentor for various academic purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out all the required fields with accurate and detailed information about yourself and your request.
- Make sure to check your relationship with the recommender and the purpose of the recommendation before submitting the form.
- Provide specific examples and details whenever possible, especially for highlighting accomplishments.
- Review and edit your form carefully before submission.
- If you are unsure about any information or fields, please ask for help or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Student First Name**: The first name of the student requesting the recommendation.
  *Type: Text*
  *Required: Yes*
  *Example: John*

- **Student Last Name**: The last name of the student requesting the recommendation.
  *Type: Text*
  *Required: Yes*
  *Example: Doe*

- **Student Email Address**: The student's email address. This will be used to send the form to the recommender.
  *Type: Email*
  *Required: Yes*
  *Example: [student@email.com](mailto:student@email.com)*

- **Student Phone Number**: The student's phone number. This is optional but will be used for communication.
  *Type: Text*
  *Required: No*

- **Student ID Number**: The student's ID number.
  *Type: Text*
  *Required: Yes*
  *Example: 123456*

- **Current Academic Program or Major**: The current academic program or major the student is in.
  *Type: Text*
  *Required: Yes*
  *Example: Computer Science*

- **Expected Graduation Date**: The expected graduation date.
  *Type: Date*
  *Required: Yes*
  *Format: MM/DD/YYYY*

- **Name of the person you are requesting a recommendation from**: The name of the teacher or mentor requesting the letter of recommendation from.
  *Type: Text*
  *Required: Yes*
  *Example: Professor Smith*

- **Recommender Email Address**: The recommender's email address. This will be used for communication.
  *Type: Email*
  *Required: Yes*
  *Example: [recommender@email.com](mailto:recommender@email.com)*

- **Your relationship with the recommender**: The relationship you have with the recommender. Choose from: Professor - current course, Professor - previous course, Academic advisor, Research supervisor, Department head, Mentor.
  *Type: Select One*
  *Required: Yes*

- **What is the recommendation for**: The purpose of the recommendation. Choose from: Graduate school application, Scholarship application, Job application, Internship application, Transfer application, Other.
  *Type: Select One*
  *Required: Yes*

- **Name of institution or employer the recommendation is being sent to**: The name of the institution or employer the letter of recommendation is being sent to.
  *Type: Text*
  *Required: Yes*
  *Example: University of XYZ*

- **Deadline for the recommendation**: The deadline for the recommendation.
  *Type: Date*
  *Required: Yes*
  *Format: MM/DD/YYYY*

- **List courses you took with this recommender**: List the courses you took with this recommender, including the semester.
  *Type: Text*
  *Required: Yes*
  *Example: CS101 - Fall 2019, CS202 - Spring 2020*

- **Key accomplishments or skills you would like the recommender to highlight**: Key accomplishments or skills you would like the recommender to highlight.
  *Type: Text*
  *Required: No*
  *Example: Leadership skills, programming skills*

- **Do you waive your right to access this recommendation**: Choose Yes or No.
  *Type: Select One*
  *Required: Yes*
  *Example: Yes*

- **Any additional notes for the recommender**: Additional notes for the recommender.
  *Type: Text*
  *Required: No*
