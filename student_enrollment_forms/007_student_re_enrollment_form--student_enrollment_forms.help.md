# Student Re Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the re-enrollment process of students in an educational institution. It gathers necessary information from students and parents to evaluate and process their re-enrollment applications.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have all required documents and information before proceeding.
- Review each field carefully to provide accurate information.
- Fill in the required fields with the correct information.
- Verify the form for any errors or discrepancies before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Re Enrollment Form (#21)**: A unique identifier for the enrollment form, which is required to be filled.
* **Enrollment Form Date (#22)**: The date the enrollment form is submitted.
* **Enrollment Form Time (#23)**: The time the enrollment form is submitted.
* **Student Last Name (#2)**: Enter the student's last name.
* **First Name (#3)**: Enter the student's first name.
* **Date of Birth (#4)**: Enter the student's date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Date of Re-Enrollment (#5)**: Enter the date of the student's re-enrollment.
* **Student ID Number (#6)**: Enter the student's ID number (if applicable).
* **School Name (#7)**: Enter the name of the school where the student is enrolled.
* **Grade Level (#8)**: Select the student's grade level from the options provided (High School, Middle School, Elementary School).
* **Student Re-Enrollment Status (#9)**: Select the status of the student's re-enrollment application (Pending, Accepted, Denied).
* **Student Re-Enrollment Reason (#10)**: Provide a brief explanation for the student's re-enrollment application.
* **Student Re-Enrollment Date (#11)**: Enter the date of the student's re-enrollment (if applicable).
* **Student Re-Enrollment Time (#12)**: Enter the time of the student's re-enrollment (if applicable).
* **Enrollment Officer (#13)**: Enter the name of the enrollment officer who processed the student's re-enrollment application.
* **Parent Name (#14)**: Enter the name of the student's parent or guardian.
* **Parent Phone (#15)**: Enter the student's parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Parent Email (#16)**: Enter the student's parent or guardian's email address.
* **Student Notes (#17)**: Enter any relevant notes about the student's re-enrollment application.
* **Student Signature (#18)**: Have the student sign the re-enrollment form.
* **School Signature (#19)**: Have the school representative sign the re-enrollment form.
* **Student Agreement (#20)**: Have the student agree to the terms and conditions of the re-enrollment application.
* **Student Re-Enrollment Form ID (#21)**: A unique identifier for the enrollment form, which is required to be filled.
