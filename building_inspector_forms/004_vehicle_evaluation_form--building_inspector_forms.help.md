# Vehicle Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Evaluation Form is used by building inspectors to collect and assess information about vehicles during their inspections.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have selected the correct vehicle type.
2. Enter the make of the vehicle being inspected.
3. Record the year of the vehicle (e.g., 2005, 2000, etc.).
4. Select the current condition of the vehicle (e.g., New, Used, Refurbished).
5. Enter the mileage of the vehicle (in miles).
6. Enter the price of the vehicle (in dollars).
7. Add any comments or observations about the vehicle inspection.
8. Select the assigned inspector who performed the evaluation.
9. Enter the contact email of the person submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Type** (Vehicle Type, select_one, required: false): Select the type of vehicle being inspected (e.g., Vehicle, Motorcycle, Caravan).
* **Make** (Make, text, required: false): Enter the make of the vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Ford, Honda).
* **Year** (Year, number, required: false): Record the year of the vehicle (e.g., 2005, 2000, etc.).
* **Condition** (Condition, select_multiple, required: false): Select the current condition of the vehicle (e.g., New, Used, Refurbished).
* **Mileage** (Mileage, number, required: false): Enter the mileage of the vehicle (in miles).
* **Price** (Price, number, required: false): Enter the price of the vehicle (in dollars).
* **Comments** (Comments, note, required: false): Add any comments or observations about the vehicle inspection.
* **Assigned Inspector** (Assigned Inspector, select_one, required: false): Select the assigned inspector who performed the evaluation.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required: false): Enter the contact email of the person submitting the form.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If unsure about any field, ask for clarification or guidance from the relevant authorities.
* Double-check all fields for errors or inconsistencies before submitting the form.
* Be aware of any organizational policies or guidelines for data handling and storage.
