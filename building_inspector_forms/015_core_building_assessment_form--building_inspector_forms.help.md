# Core Building Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help building inspectors and auditors assess the safety and compliance features of a building. It is used to gather information on the presence or absence of various safety features, such as exit signs, fire suppression systems, and sprinkler systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, based on your inspection or assessment of the building.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. For single-answer questions, select only one option.
4. Make sure to answer all questions, even if you are unsure or do not have information on a particular feature.
