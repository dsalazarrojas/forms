# Road Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data from road inspections, including its surface, grade, alignment, and condition. The goal is to provide a standard assessment of the road's features.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the date of the inspection in the "inspection_date" field.
2. Take notes on the conditions you observe during the inspection in the "inspector_notes" field.
3. Evaluate the condition of the road's surface using the "road_condition" field, selecting all applicable options (e.g., "good", "fair", "poor").
4. Choose the type of road surface in the "road_surface" field (e.g., "asphalt", "concrete", "dirt", "gravel", "sand").
5. Measure and enter the road's width in the "road_width" field.
6. Measure and enter the road's length in the "road_length" field.
7. Evaluate the road's elevation in the "road_elevation" field.
8. Choose the road's alignment type in the "road_alignment" field, selecting all applicable options (e.g., "straight", "curved", "circular", "zig zag").
9. Choose the road's grade in the "road_grade" field (e.g., "A", "B", "C", "D").
10. Evaluate the condition of the road's surface using the "road_surface_condition" field, selecting all applicable options (e.g., "good", "fair", "poor").
11. Evaluate the road's surface texture using the "road_surface_texture" field, selecting all applicable options (e.g., "smooth", "rough", "bumpy").
12. Choose the road's alignment type in the "road_alignment_type" field, selecting all applicable options (e.g., "straight", "curved", "circular", "zig zag").
13. Evaluate the road's grade condition using the "road_grade_condition" field, selecting all applicable options (e.g., "good", "fair", "poor").
14. Choose the road's gradient in the "road_gradient" field, selecting all applicable options (e.g., "flat", "slight", "moderate", "steep").
