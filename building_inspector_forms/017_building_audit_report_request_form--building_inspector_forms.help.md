# Building Audit Report Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a building audit report for a specific building. The audit report will assess the building's condition, identify areas for improvement, and provide recommendations for maintenance and safety upgrades.

## How To Complete This Form

- Read through each section carefully to ensure you have the necessary information.
- Fill in your name and contact information in the "Requester" section.
- Provide details about the building, including its name, address, and type.
- Specify the type and focus areas of the audit.
- Define the objectives and goals of the audit.
- Choose the preferred date and time for the audit.
- Select the report format and any additional services required.
- Confirm your contact preferences and any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the person requesting the audit.
* **Requester title/position** (`requester_title`, text, required): Enter your title or position.
* **Email address** (`requester_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone number** (`requester_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Organization or company** (`organization`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization or company.
* **Building name** (`building_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the building being audited.
* **Building address** (`building_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the building being audited.
* **Building type** (`building_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of building from the options provided.
* **Year of construction** (`construction_date`, number, required): Enter the year the building was constructed.
* **Total square footage** (`square_footage`, number, required): Enter the total square footage of the building.
* **Number of floors** (`number_of_floors`, number, required): Enter the number of floors in the building.
* **Audit scope** (`audit_scope`, note, required): This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Type of audit requested** (`audit_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of audit required from the options provided.
* **Focus areas for the audit** (`audit_focus_areas`, select_multiple, required): Choose the focus areas for the audit from the options provided.
* **Audit objectives and goals** (`audit_objectives`, text, required): Define the specific objectives and goals of the audit.
* **Specific areas of concern** (`specific_concerns`, text, optional): Enter any specific areas of concern.
* **Scheduling** (`scheduling`, note, required): This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Preferred audit date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Choose the preferred date for the audit.
* **Flexibility with date** (`date_flexibility`, select_one, required): Choose the level of flexibility with the date.
* **Expected audit duration** (`audit_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the expected duration of the audit.
* **Building access available for audit** (`building_access`, select_one, required): Choose how access to the building will be arranged.
* **Reports and deliverables** (`reports_needed`, note, required): This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Report format** (`report_format`, select_multiple, required): Choose the preferred report format from the options provided.
* **When is report needed?** (`deliverable_timeline`, select_one, required): Choose when the report is needed.
* **Additional services or consultations** (`additional_services`, text, optional): Enter any additional services or consultations required.
* **Budget or cost expectations** (`budget_considerations`, text, optional): Enter any budget or cost expectations.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, note, required): This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Preferred contact method** (`contact_preference`, select_one, required): Choose the preferred contact method.
* **Additional notes or special requests** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or special requests.
