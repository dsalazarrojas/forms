# Mobile Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a property during a mobile inspection. The form is used to gather data about the property's address, type, characteristics, safety features, electrical, heating and cooling, plumbing, waste disposal, electrical outlets, water and sewage meters, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.
2. Make sure to review and update all fields carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction**: This is a text field where you can enter any introduction or comments about the inspection. (text, required: false)
* **property_address**: Enter the address of the property being inspected. (text, required: false)
* **property_type**: Select the type of property from the options provided. (select_one, required: false)
* **building_characteristics**: Describe the characteristics of the building, such as its age, size, and condition. (text, required: false)
* **building_characteristics_details**: Provide more details about the building's characteristics, such as its features and amenities. (text, required: false)
* **fire_safety**: Select any fire safety features present in the building. (select_multiple, required: false)
* **electrical_system**: Choose the type of electrical system in the building. (select_one, required: false)
* **heating_cooling_system**: Select the type of heating and cooling system in the building. (select_one, required: false)
* **plumbing_system**: Choose the type of plumbing system in the building. (select_one, required: false)
* **waste_disposal**: Select the type of waste disposal system in the building. (select_one, required: false)
* **electrical_outlet**: Choose the type of electrical outlet in the building. (select_one, required: false)
* **electrical_outlet_details**: Enter any additional details about the electrical outlet. (text, required: false)
* **water_meter**: Select the type of water meter in the building. (select_one, required: false)
* **water_meter_details**: Enter any additional details about the water meter. (text, required: false)
* **sewage_meter**: Select the type of sewage meter in the building. (select_one, required: false)
* **sewage_meter_details**: Enter any additional details about the sewage meter. (text, required: false)
* **comments**: Enter any additional comments about the inspection. (text, required: false)
* **signature**: Enter your signature as the inspector. (text, required: false)
* **inspector_signature**: Enter your name as the inspector. (text, required: false)
* **inspector_name**: Enter your name as the inspector. (text, required: false)
* **inspector_date**: Enter the date of the inspection. (date, required: false)
* **inspector_time**: Enter the time of the inspection. (time, required: false)
* **inspector_notes**: Enter any additional notes about the inspection. (text, required: false)
* **inspector_company**: Enter the name of your company as the inspector. (text, required: false)
* **inspector_location**: Enter the location of the inspection. (text, required: false)

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact your supervisor or HR department.
