# Facility Safety Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help facility managers or inspectors evaluate the safety conditions of a facility and identify areas that require attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
* **Building Address** (`building_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the building being inspected.
* **Fire Extinguishers** (`fire_extinguishers`, select_one, optional): Are fire extinguishers present in the facility?
* **Emergency Exit Signs** (`emergency_exit_signs`, select_one, optional): Are emergency exit signs in place in the facility?
* **Fire Drills** (`fire_drills`, select_multiple, optional): Has a recent fire drill been taken in the facility?
* **Emergency Equipment** (`emergency_equipment`, select_one, optional): Is emergency equipment present in the facility?
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): Is emergency contact information available in the facility?
* **Fire Supervision** (`fire_supervision`, select_one, optional): Is fire supervision present in the facility?
* **Fire Extinguisher Inspection Dates** (`fire_extinguisher_inspection_dates`, date, optional): When were fire extinguishers last inspected?
* **Fire Safety Plan** (`fire_safety_plan`, select_one, optional): Has a fire safety plan been developed for the facility?
* **Fire Drill Scheduling** (`fire_drill_scheduling`, select_one, optional): Is fire drill scheduled for the facility?
* **Fire Inspection Certifications** (`fire_inspection_certifications`, select_one, optional): Are fire inspectors certified to perform inspections?
* **Fire Extinguisher Charges** (`fire_extinguisher_charges`, select_one, optional): Are fire extinguishers charged and ready for use?
* **Emergency Exit Signs Description** (`emergency_exit_signs_description`, text, optional): Provide a description of emergency exit signs in the facility.
* **Fire Safety Plan Description** (`fire_safety_plan_description`, text, optional): Provide a description of the fire safety plan in the facility.
* **Emergency Equipment Description** (`emergency_equipment_description`, text, optional): Provide a description of emergency equipment in the facility.
* **Fire Extinguishers Inspection Certificate** (`fire_extinguisher_inspection_certificate`, text, optional): Provide any fire extinguisher inspection certificate.
* **Fire Supervision Description** (`fire_supervision_description`, text, optional): Provide a description of fire supervision in the facility.

Note: The presence of certain fields is optional and may not apply in all situations. The descriptions provided are intended to clarify the purpose and context of each field.
