# Framing Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Framing Inspection Checklist is a comprehensive guide for building inspectors to ensure thorough and accurate inspections of buildings. The form is designed to gather all necessary information about the building's structure and functionality, facilitating efficient and precise documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the building's blueprints or plans to familiarize yourself with its layout and features.
2. Begin at page 1 and work your way through each section, carefully reading and completing each field as needed.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, but do not hesitate to add additional information if necessary.
4. Use clear and concise notes to record your observations and findings.
5. Move on to the next page when finished with the current one, using the navigation buttons to access the next section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Below is a list of each page with a brief description of the field labels:

* **Building Inspector Forms Page 1**
	+ **Inspector's Name**: Enter your name and title/position.
	+ **Inspector's Date**: Record the date of the inspection.
	+ **Building Address**: Enter the building's address.
* **Building Inspector Forms Page 2**
	+ **Exterior**: Describe the building's exterior condition, including any damage or defects.
	+ **Roofing**: Report any issues with the roofing material or structure.
* **Building Inspector Forms Page 3**
	+ **Walls**: Inspect and report on the condition of walls, including any cracks, water damage, or other issues.
	+ **Windows**: Check the condition and operation of windows.
* **Building Inspector Forms Page 4**
	+ **Doors**: Report on the condition and functionality of doors.
	+ **Hardware**: Inspect and record the state of door hardware.
* **Building Inspector Forms Page 5**
	+ **Flooring**: Describe the type and condition of flooring material.
	+ **Carpeting**: Report on the condition and age of carpeting.
* **Building Inspector Forms Page 6**
	+ **Ceiling**: Inspect and report on the condition of ceiling tiles or material.
	+ **Lighting**: Check the condition and functionality of lighting fixtures.
* **Building Inspector Forms Page 7**
	+ **Electrical**: Inspect and report on electrical outlets, switches, and wiring.
* **Building Inspector Forms Page 8**
	+ **Plumbing**: Report on water and gas pipes, fixtures, and connections.
* **Building Inspector Forms Page 9**
	+ **HVAC**: Inspect and report on the heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems.

## Tips
- Always refer to the building's blueprints or plans for specific information about the building's layout and features.
- Be thorough and meticulous in your inspections, using all pages as a guide.
- Take clear and concise notes to ensure accurate documentation.
- Verify the accuracy of the information provided by the building inspector.
- Use the navigation buttons to move between pages efficiently.
- In case of any uncertainty or question, consult with a supervisor or authority on site.
