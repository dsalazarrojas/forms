# Building Inspector Forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from building inspectors to assess the compliance and safety of a building. It guides the inspectors through various sections to evaluate the building's compliance with regulations, standards, and procedures, as well as its environmental impact and operational efficiency. The form also helps inspectors identify hazards and assess risks, and prepares for audits and continuous improvement. The goal is to ensure the building meets the necessary safety and regulatory standards.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start with the "Introduction" page, which provides an overview of the form's purpose and guidelines.
2. Move on to the "Compliance Check" section, where you will select the areas of compliance you are evaluating.
3. Next, complete the "Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment" section, where you choose the types of hazards you have identified and assessed.
4. Continue with the "Safety Measures" section, where you select the safety measures that are being implemented.
5. Then, evaluate the "Environmental Impact" of the building, choosing from the available options.
6. In the "Operational Efficiency" section, select the areas of operational efficiency that you are evaluating.
7. Complete the "Continuous Improvement" section, where you choose the type of action you will take for continuous improvement.
8. Move on to the "Audit Preparation" section, where you select the frequency, schedule, and checklist for the audit.
9. Finally, complete the "Review and Evaluation" section, where you evaluate and review the results.
10. Finish with the "Action Planning" section, where you select the corrective and preventative actions to take.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `text`, optional): This is the first page of the form, providing an overview of the form's purpose and guidelines.
* **Compliance Check** (`Compliance Check`, `select_one`, optional): Select one or more of the options to indicate areas of compliance you are evaluating.
	+ Compliance with regulations
	+ Compliance with standards
	+ Compliance with procedures
	+ Compliance with policies
* **Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment** (`Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the types of hazards you have identified and assessed.
	+ Hazards in equipment
	+ Hazards in environment
	+ Hazards in procedures
	+ Hazards in work processes
* **Safety Measures** (`Safety Measures`, `select_one`, optional): Select the safety measures being implemented.
	+ Safety signs and labels
	+ Safety equipment
	+ Safety training
* **Environmental Impact** (`Environmental Impact`, `select_multiple`, optional): Evaluate the environmental impact of the building.
	+ Waste management
	+ Energy efficiency
	+ Water management
* **Operational Efficiency** (`Operational Efficiency`, `select_one`, optional): Select the areas of operational efficiency.
	+ Work flow management
	+ Performance monitoring
	+ Quality management
* **Continuous Improvement** (`Continuous Improvement`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of action for continuous improvement.
	+ Corrective action
	+ Preventative action
	+ Root cause analysis
* **Audit Preparation** (`Audit Preparation`, `select_one`, optional): Select the frequency, schedule, and checklist for the audit.
	+ Audit frequency
	+ Audit schedule
	+ Audit checklist
* **Review and Evaluation** (`Review and Evaluation`, `select_one`, optional): Evaluate and review the results.
	+ Review and evaluation
	+ Review and feedback
	+ Evaluation and assessment
* **Action Planning** (`Action Planning`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the corrective and preventative actions to take.
	+ Corrective action
	+ Preventative action

## Tips
- Make sure to answer each question carefully, as it will help in creating a comprehensive report for the building's compliance and safety evaluation.
- You can skip any section that does not apply to your building or situation.
- Review and double-check your answers before submitting the form.
- Use the information collected from this form to improve the building's safety and regulatory compliance.
