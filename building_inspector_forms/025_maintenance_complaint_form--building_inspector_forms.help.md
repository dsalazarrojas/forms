# Maintenance Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Maintenance Complaint Form" is used to document and report maintenance complaints from residents. This form collects essential information to help address and resolve the issue effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the "Maintenance Complaint Form" accurately, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date the complaint was reported in the "Date Reported" field (page 2).
2. Provide a detailed description of the problem in the "Description of Problem" field (page 2).
3. Specify the location where the issue is occurring in the "Location of Problem" field (page 2).
4. Choose the priority level of the complaint by selecting from the "Priority" options (page 3).
5. Select the personnel to assign the complaint to from the "Assigned to" options (page 3).
6. Add any additional comments or observations about the issue in the "Comments" field (page 4).
7. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field (page 4).
8. Provide a phone number in the "Phone" field (page 4).
9. Attach any relevant documents or photos to the issue in the "Attachment" field (page 4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Building Inspections Complaints** (page 1): 
    Enter a description of the maintenance complaint, e.g., "Leaky faucet in the kitchen."
*   **Date Reported** (page 2):
    Enter the date the complaint was reported, in a date format (DD/MM/YYYY).
*   **Description of Problem** (page 2):
    Provide a detailed description of the issue, including any relevant details or circumstances.
*   **Location of Problem** (page 2):
    Specify the exact location where the issue is occurring, e.g., "Apartment 101, 3rd Floor."
*   **Priority** (page 3):
    Choose a priority level:
        - **High**: Critical issue that requires immediate attention.
        - **Medium**: Important issue that requires timely attention.
        - **Low**: Minor issue that can be addressed at a later time.
*   **Assigned to** (page 3):
    Select the personnel or team member to handle the issue from the options provided.
*   **Comments** (page 4):
    Add any additional comments or observations about the issue.
*   **Email** (page 4):
    Enter a valid email address to enable quick and efficient communication.
*   **Phone** (page 4):
    Provide a phone number to facilitate direct communication.
*   **Attachment** (page 4):
    Attach any relevant documents or photos to support the complaint.
