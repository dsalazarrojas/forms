# Furniture Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Furniture Inspection Form is used for documenting the inspection and assessment of furniture in a building or home.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

### Step 1: Provide Home Address (optional)
Enter the home address where the inspection was conducted. If this field is not applicable, leave it blank.

### Step 2: Describe the Furniture
Provide a brief description of the furniture item being inspected.

### Step 3: Assess the Condition
Select the condition of the furniture using the following scale:
* Excellent: The furniture is in excellent condition, with no significant wear or damage.
* Fair: The furniture has some minor wear or damage, but is still usable.
* Poor: The furniture is in poor condition, with significant wear or damage.

### Step 4: Enter Price (optional)
Enter the price of the inspected item, if applicable.

### Step 5: Provide Seller's Name and Contact Information (optional)
Enter the name of the seller or owner of the item, and any relevant contact information.

### Step 6: Enter Date and Time of Inspection
Enter the date and time of the inspection.

### Step 7: Enter Inspector's Name
Enter the name of the inspector conducting the assessment.

### Step 8: Add Notes (optional)
Add any additional notes from the inspector, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Address**: Enter the home address where the inspection was conducted.
* **Furniture Description**: Provide a brief description of the furniture item being inspected.
* **Condition (Excellent, Fair, or Poor)**: Assess the condition of the furniture using the provided scale.
* **Price**: Enter the price of the inspected item, if applicable.
* **Seller's Name**: Enter the name of the seller or owner of the item.
* **Contact Info**: Provide any relevant contact information for the seller or owner.
* **Date Inspected**: Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Time Inspected**: Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Inspector's Name**: Enter the name of the inspector conducting the assessment.
* **Inspector's Notes**: Add any additional notes from the inspector, if necessary.
