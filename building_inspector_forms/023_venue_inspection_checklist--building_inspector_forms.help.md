# Venue Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information on the safety and compliance of a venue with the required safety and emergency response features.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*) below.
2. For fields that allow multiple selections, choose as many options as apply.
3. Be accurate and detailed when filling in the comments and notes sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizers** (`event_organizer`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the event organizer or person responsible for the event.
* **Venue Details** (`venue_details`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the venue, including its address and any notable features.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Time Inspected** (`time_inspected`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Inspected By** (`inspected_by`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the building inspector.
* **Venue Address** (`venue_address`, `text`, required): Enter the physical address of the venue.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person to contact for emergencies.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the person to contact for emergencies (e.g., Security Manager).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number of the person to contact for emergencies.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Venue Capacity** (`venue_capacity`, `number`, optional): Enter the maximum capacity of the venue.
* **Fire Safety Features** (`fire_safety_features`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all fire safety features present in the venue, including Fire Extinguishers, Sprinkler System, and Fire Alarm System.
* **Electrical Features** (`electrical_features`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all electrical features present in the venue, including Generators, Power Outlets, and Electrical Panel.
* **Plumbing Features** (`plumbing_features`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all plumbing features present in the venue, including Sinks, Water Heater, and Plumbing Fixtures.
* **Security Features** (`security_features`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all security features present, including Security Cameras, Locks, and Alarm System.
* **Exit Features** (`exit_features`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all exit features present, including Fire Exits, Emergency Exit, and Safety Signs.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, `text`, required): Enter the emergency contact information, including name, title, and contact number.
* **Emergency Equipment** (`emergency_equipment`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all emergency equipment present, including Fire Extinguishers, Fire Alarm, and Emergency Kit.
* **First Aid Kit** (`firstaid_kit`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all items in the first aid kit, including Gloves, First Aid Kit, and Bandages.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Contact Title** (`emergency_contact_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the emergency contact person.
* **Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number of the emergency contact person.
* **Venue Inspection Comments** (`venue_inspection_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Inspectors Comments** (`inspector_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any comments or notes from the inspector.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
