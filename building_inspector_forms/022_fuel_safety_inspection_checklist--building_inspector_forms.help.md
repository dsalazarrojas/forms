# Fuel Safety Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fuel Safety Inspection Checklist is used to guide inspectors through a comprehensive fuel safety inspection to identify and address potential hazards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and ensure it's relevant to the inspection being performed.
2. Fill out each field as instructed, making sure to answer all required questions.
3. Document any hazards identified during the inspection.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tanker Inspection** (`tanker_inspection`): Describe the overall condition of the tanker, including its cleanliness, integrity, and any visible defects.
* **Driver Inspection** (`driver_inspection`): Report on the driver's qualifications, experience, and any concerns or issues with their performance.
* **Emergency Response Plan** (`emergency_response`): Outline the procedures to be followed in the event of an emergency, including evacuation routes and equipment availability.
* **Fuel Tank Inspection** (`fuel_tank_inspection`): Describe the condition of the fuel tank, including any signs of wear, damage, or leaks.
* **Hazards Identified** (`hazards_identified`): List any hazards or safety concerns found during the inspection, including any areas that require immediate attention.
	+ Select all applicable options to document multiple hazards.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`): Describe any actions taken to address hazards or safety concerns identified during the inspection.
* **Inspector Signature** (`inspector_signature`): Sign and date the form to confirm completion and acceptance of the inspection's findings.

## Tips
- Be sure to review and complete each section carefully to ensure an accurate and thorough inspection report.
- Document all hazards and safety concerns to help prevent accidents and ensure a safe working environment.
- Keep the form in a safe and secure location, as it contains sensitive information.
