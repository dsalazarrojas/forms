# Roof Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Roof Inspection Form is a tool for building inspectors to document the condition and findings of a roof inspection. It helps to gather information about the roof's condition, including the type and condition, and provides a space for recording recommended actions and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the title of the form and your name and license number as the inspector.
2. Enter the property address where the inspection was conducted.
3. Select the type of roof being inspected.
4. Assess the condition of the roof by selecting from Minor, Moderate, or Major.
5. Enter the date and time of the inspection.
6. Record any inspection comments and findings in the dedicated space.
7. Provide recommended actions and comments for any observed issues.
8. Sign and date the form as the inspector.
9. Have the client sign and date the form as well.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Roof Inspection Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Inspector Name**: Please enter your name as the building inspector.
* **Inspector License Number**: Enter your license number as the building inspector.
* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property being inspected.
* **Roof Type**: Select the type of roof from the list: Asphalt, Clay, Composite, Metal, Shingle, Slate, Tile, or Wood.
* **Roof Condition**: Assess the condition of the roof by selecting from Minor, Moderate, or Major.
* **Date of Inspection**: Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Inspection**: Enter the time of the inspection in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Inspection Comments**: Record any comments or findings from the inspection.
* **Inspection Findings**: Record any findings or issues observed during the inspection.
* **Recommended Actions**: Suggest any necessary actions or repairs for the observed issues.
* **Inspector Signature**: Sign your name as the building inspector.
* **Date Signed**: Date the form as the building inspector.
* **Client Signature**: Sign your name as the client.
* **Date Signed**: Date the form as the client.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* Use the provided space for recording comments and findings.
* Sign and date the form as required.
