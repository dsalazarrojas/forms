# Care Facility Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate a care facility's quality and compliance with standards and regulations in various aspects, including its building structure, sanitation, fire safety, and resident care.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form's sections and questions carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2.  Fill in the required information accurately and thoroughly.
3.  Use the provided options to answer each question, or provide detailed explanations in free-text areas.
4.  Ensure you have all necessary documents and data before proceeding.
5.  Review the form's sections and questions again to ensure you've addressed all aspects of the care facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Facility Name** (`1`): Enter the name of the care facility being evaluated.
    *   Please use the name as it appears on the facility's documents and records.
*   **Facility Address** (`2`): Enter the street address of the care facility.
    *   Please ensure accuracy to help identify the facility correctly.
*   **Facility License Number** (`3`): Enter the facility's state license or regulatory ID number.
    *   This is a required field for regulatory compliance and records.
*   **Date of Evaluation** (`4`): Enter the date of the evaluation (or assessment).
    *   This date will be used as a reference for the evaluation report.
*   **Inspector or Evaluator Name** (`5`): Enter your full name and title as the evaluator.
    *   This ensures accurate attribution and accountability.
*   **Evaluator Title** (`6`): Enter your title or position as the evaluator.
    *   This helps to establish your authority and expertise.
*   **Type of Evaluation** (`7`): Select the type of evaluation being conducted.
    *   This helps to determine the scope and focus of the assessment.
*   **Building Structure and Safety** (`8`): Rate the general condition of the building's structure.
    *   Choose one of the provided options based on your observation.
*   **Specific Structural Issues** (`9`): Describe any specific structural concerns or issues found.
    *   Provide detailed explanations for any noted issues.
*   **ADA Accessibility Compliance** (`10`): Rate the facility's compliance with ADA accessibility standards.
    *   Choose one of the provided options based on your observation.
*   **Fire Safety and Emergency Systems** (`11`): Rate the condition of fire exits, alarms, and evacuation routes.
    *   Choose one of the provided options based on your observation.
*   **Fire Extinguishers and Emergency Equipment** (`12`): Report the status of fire extinguishers and emergency equipment.
    *   Choose one of the provided options based on your observation.
*   **Sanitation and Cleanliness** (`13`): Rate the overall cleanliness of common and resident areas.
    *   Choose one of the provided options based on your observation.
*   **Specific Sanitation Issues** (`14`): Describe any specific sanitation concerns or issues found.
    *   Provide detailed explanations for any noted issues.
*   **Resident Rooms and Living Spaces** (`15`): Rate the condition and adequacy of resident rooms.
    *   Choose one of the provided options based on your observation.
*   **Kitchen and Food Service** (`16`): Rate the condition and safety of kitchen facilities and food preparation.
    *   Choose one of the provided options based on your observation.
*   **Staffing Levels and Qualifications** (`17`): Rate the adequacy of staffing levels and qualifications.
    *   Choose one of the provided options based on your observation.
*   **Quality of Resident Care** (`18`): Rate the quality of care provided to residents.
    *   Choose one of the provided options based on your observation.
*   **Activity Programs** (`19`): Rate the availability and quality of recreational and therapeutic programs.
    *   Choose one of the provided options based on your observation.
*   **Medication Storage and Management** (`20`): Rate the condition and security of medication storage and management.
    *   Choose one of the provided options based on your observation.
*   **Infection Control Practices** (`21`): Rate the rigor and consistency of infection control practices.
    *   Choose one of the provided options based on your observation.
*   **Equipment and Facilities Maintenance** (`22`): Rate the condition and maintenance of equipment and facilities.
    *   Choose one of the provided options based on your observation.
*   **Code Violations** (`23`): Describe any code violations or regulatory concerns found.
    *   Provide detailed explanations for any noted issues.
*   **Required Corrective Actions** (`24`): Describe any corrective actions that must be taken.
    *   Provide detailed explanations for any required actions.
*   **Compliance Timeline** (`25`): Report the deadline for corrective actions to be taken.
    *   Provide a specific date or timeframe for the actions to be completed.
*   **Overall Facility Rating** (`26`): Rate the overall quality of the facility.
    *   Choose one of the provided options based on your overall assessment.
*   **Recommendations for Improvement** (`27`): Suggest any additional suggestions for facility improvement.
    *   Provide detailed explanations for any recommendations.

## Tips

*   This form is designed to guide you through the evaluation process.
*   Ensure you have all the required information and documents before proceeding.
*   Choose answers based on your thorough observation and assessment.
*   Provide detailed explanations when necessary to support your answers.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out.
