# Septic Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Septic Inspection Form is designed to collect information about a property's septic system, including its status, size, and inspection details. This form is used to document septic inspections for regulatory compliance and property maintenance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields as indicated by the red asterisk (\*). This form has 5 required fields.
2. Enter your contact information in the `contact_information` field.
3. Provide the property address in the `property_address` field.
4. Select the property type from the options (Single, Multi, or Commercial).
5. Choose the current status of the septic system from the options (Active, Inactive, or Not Installed).
6. Select the size of the septic system from the options (Less than 1000 gpd, 1000-2000 gpd, 2000-3000 gpd, or 3000 gpd or more).
7. Describe the condition of the septic tank in the `tank_condition` field, selecting from the options (Good, Fair, Poor, or Not Inspected).
8. Enter the date of the inspection in the `inspection_date` field.
9. Fill in the name of the inspector in the `inspector_name` field.
10. Sign in as the inspector with your name and certification number in the `inspector_name` and `inspector_certification_number` fields, respectively.
11. Select the frequency of inspections (Monthly, Bi-Weekly, Quarterly, Semi-Annual, or Annual) in the `inspection_frequency` field.
12. Add any additional comments about the septic inspection in the `inspection_notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_information** (Text, Optional): Enter your contact information for communication purposes.
* **property_address** (Text, Required): Enter the property address where the septic system is located.
* **property_type** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of property (Single, Multi, or Commercial).
* **septic_system_status** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the current status of the septic system (Active, Inactive, or Not Installed).
* **system_size** (Select One, Required): Choose the size of the septic system (Less than 1000 gpd, 1000-2000 gpd, 2000-3000 gpd, or 3000 gpd or more).
* **tank_condition** (Select Multiple, Required): Describe the condition of the septic tank (Good, Fair, Poor, or Not Inspected).
* **inspection_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **inspector_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the inspector.
* **inspector_signature** (Text, Optional): Sign with your inspector signature.
* **septic_inspection_comments** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments about the septic inspection.
* **property_owner_signature** (Text, Optional): Sign with the property owner's signature.
* **septic_inspection_notes** (Text, Optional): Add any additional notes about the septic inspection.
* **property_owner_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the property owner's name.
* **property_owner_address** (Text, Optional): Enter the property owner's address.
* **certification_number** (Text, Optional): Enter your certification number.
* **inspector_certification_number** (Text, Optional): Enter your inspector certification number.
* **inspector_license_number** (Text, Optional): Enter your inspector license number.
* **inspection_frequency** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the frequency of inspections (Monthly, Bi-Weekly, Quarterly, Semi-Annual, or Annual).
* **notes** (Text, Optional): Add any additional notes about the septic inspection.
