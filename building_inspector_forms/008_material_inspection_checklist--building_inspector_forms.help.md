# Material Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the results of a material inspection during a building inspection. It is designed to help inspectors evaluate the condition of materials and make notes on any issues found.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the inspector in the "Inspector Name" field.
2. Enter the date of the inspection in the "Inspection Date" field.
3. Describe the materials inspected in the "Materials Description" field.
4. For each material inspected, select the condition from the list of options in the "Materials Condition" fields (e.g. "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", "Poor").
5. Enter any additional comments about the materials inspected in the "Inspector Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Materials Received**: A text field for a brief description of the materials received during the inspection.
	+ This field is required.
* **Inspector Name**: A field to enter the name of the inspector completing the form.
	+ This field is required.
* **Inspection Date**: A field to enter the date of the inspection.
	+ This field is required.
* **Time Inspected**: A field to enter the time of the inspection.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Materials Description**: A field for a brief description of the materials inspected.
	+ This field is required.
* **Inspector Comments**: A field for any additional comments about the materials inspected.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Materials Condition 1-10**: Fields for selecting the condition of each material inspected. The options are "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", and "Poor".
	+ These fields are not required.
*   **Materials Condition 1**: A field to select the condition of the first material inspected.
*   **Materials Condition 2**: A field to select the condition of the second material inspected.
*   **Materials Condition 3**: A field to select the condition of the third material inspected.
*   **Materials Condition 4**: A field to select the condition of the fourth material inspected.
*   **Materials Condition 5**: A field to select the condition of the fifth material inspected.
*   **Materials Condition 6**: A field to select the condition of the sixth material inspected.
*   **Materials Condition 7**: A field to select the condition of the seventh material inspected.
*   **Materials Condition 8**: A field to select the condition of the eighth material inspected.
*   **Materials Condition 9**: A field to select the condition of the ninth material inspected.
*   **Materials Condition 10**: A field to select the condition of the tenth material inspected.
## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the materials inspected.
* Use the condition options to accurately reflect the condition of each material.
* If a material is not applicable to the inspection, leave the corresponding "Materials Condition" field blank.
