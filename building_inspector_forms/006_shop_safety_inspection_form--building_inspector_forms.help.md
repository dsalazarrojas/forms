# shop_safety_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to inspect shops for safety hazards and ensure compliance with safety guidelines. It's crucial to fill it out carefully, as it helps identify potential risks and promotes a safe environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the shop's safety guidelines and procedures.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
3. Answer each question truthfully, providing specific examples or details when requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`general_info`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the shop, including its name, address, and contact information.
* **Shop Location** (`shop_location`, text, optional): Enter the shop's physical address, including the room or area where the safety inspection is being conducted.
* **Hazard Areas** (`hazard_areas`, text, optional): List the areas in the shop where potential hazards have been identified.
* **Hazardous Items** (`hazards`, text, optional): List any hazardous materials or substances found in the shop.
* **Fire Extinguishers** (`fire_extinguishers`, text, optional): Describe the available fire extinguishers, including their type and location.
* **First Aid Kits** (`first_aid_kits`, number, optional): Report the number of first aid kits available in the shop.
* **Fire Suppression Systems** (`fire_suppression_systems`, text, optional): Describe the fire suppression systems in place, including their type and functionality.
* **Spill Containment** (`spill_containment`, text, optional): Confirm whether the spill containment system is functioning correctly.
* **Fire Extinguisher Type** (`fire_extinguisher_type`, text, optional): Identify the type of fire extinguishers used in the shop.
* **First Aid Kit Location** (`first_aid_kit_location`, text, optional): Specify the location of first aid kits within the shop.
* **Spill Containment Type** (`spill_containment_type`, text, optional): Describe the type of spill containment system used.
* **Electrical Equipment** (`electrical_equipment`, text, optional): List any electrical equipment, including its type and location.
* **Electrical Equipment Type** (`electrical_equipment_type`, text, optional): Describe the type of electrical equipment found in the shop.
* **Electrical Equipment Location** (`electrical_equipment_location`, text, optional): Specify the location of electrical equipment within the shop.

## Tips
* Be thorough and honest when answering each question.
* Provide specific examples or details when requested.
* If you're unsure about any aspect of the form, consult the shop's safety guidelines or procedures.
* Ensure the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to promote a safe environment for shop visitors and employees.
