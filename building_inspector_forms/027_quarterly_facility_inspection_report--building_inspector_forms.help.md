# quarterly Facility Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The quarterly Facility Inspection Report form is designed to document the condition and status of a facility. It is used by building inspectors to collect information on a facility's safety and regulatory compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the facility being inspected in the **Facility Name** field.
2. Provide the address of the facility in the **Facility Address** field.
3. Enter the date of the last inspection in the **Date Last Inspected** field.
4. Enter your inspector ID in the **Inspector ID** field.
5. Select the type of facility from the options provided in the **Facility Type** field.
6. Enter any safety comments about the facility in the **Safety Comments** field.
7. Describe the current condition of the facility in the **Facility Condition** field.
8. Enter any additional notes about the facility in the **Notes** field.
9. Enter any comments about the facility's status in the **Facility Status Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Name**: The name of the facility being inspected.
* **Facility Address**: The physical address of the facility.
* **Date Last Inspected**: The date of the last inspection.
* **Inspector ID**: The ID of the inspector completing the form.
* **Facility Type**: The type of facility (e.g. office building, residential complex, etc.).
* **Facility Status**: This field is currently not required, but if it were, it would be used to document the facility's status.
* **Safety Comments**: Any safety-related comments or concerns about the facility.
* **Facility Condition**: A description of the facility's overall condition.
* **Notes**: Additional notes or comments about the facility.
* **Facility Status Comments**: Comments about the facility's status, if applicable.

Note: The facility status field is currently not required. In the future, this field may become a required field.
