# Building Occupancy Audit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Building Occupancy Audit Registration form is designed to collect necessary information from facility managers to facilitate a comprehensive occupancy audit. This audit is a critical assessment of a building's compliance with safety and security regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the registration date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
2. Enter the facility's name and address in the respective fields.
3. Select the type of facility from the options provided.
4. Provide the total square footage of the building (if applicable).
5. Enter the facility manager's name, email, and phone number.
6. Choose a preferred audit date and duration.
7. Select the focus areas for the audit (at least one is required).
8. Provide the approximate number of people currently occupying the building.
9. If necessary, describe any recent changes to the building's structure.
10. Confirm if there have been any previous occupancy audits conducted.
11. Enter the date of the last audit.
12. Mention any known compliance issues.
13. Confirm if there have been any recent modifications to the building.
14. Describe the details of any recent modifications.
15. Provide arrangements for inspector access and security.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration Date
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date you are registering for the audit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Facility Name
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of your facility.

### Facility Address
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, `text`, `required`): Enter the complete address of your facility.

### Building Type
* **Building Type** (`building_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the type of facility from the options provided.

### Total Square Footage
* **Total Square Footage** (`building_sqft`, `number`, `not required`): If applicable, enter the total square footage of the building.

### Number of Floors
* **Number of Floors** (`num_floors`, `number`, `not required`): Enter the number of stories in the building.

### Facility Manager Name
* **Facility Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the primary contact person.

### Facility Manager Email
* **Facility Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, `required`): Enter the email of the primary contact person.

### Facility Manager Phone
* **Facility Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.

### Preferred Audit Date
* **Preferred Audit Date** (`preferred_audit_date`, `date`, `required`): Choose a date for the audit to take place.

### Expected Audit Duration
* **Expected Audit Duration** (`audit_duration`, `select_one`, `not required`): Select the expected duration of the audit.

### Focus Areas for Audit
* **Focus Areas for Audit** (`audit_focus_areas`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select at least one area of focus for the audit.

### Current Building Occupancy
* **Current Building Occupancy** (`current_occupancy`, `number`, `not required`): Enter an approximate number of people occupying the building.

### Recent Occupancy Changes
* **Recent Occupancy Changes** (`occupancy_changes_recent`, `text`, `not required`): Describe any recent changes in occupancy.

### Previous Occupancy Audits Conducted
* **Previous Occupancy Audits Conducted** (`previous_audits`, `select_one`, `not required`): Confirm if there have been previous occupancy audits conducted.

### Date of Last Audit
* **Date of Last Audit** (`prior_audit_date`, `date`, `not required`): Enter the date of the last audit.

### Known Compliance Issues
* **Known Compliance Issues** (`compliance_issues`, `text`, `not required`): Mention any known compliance issues.

### Recent Building Modifications
* **Recent Building Modifications** (`recent_modifications`, `select_one`, `not required`): Confirm if there have been any recent modifications to the building.

### Details of Modifications
* **Details of Modifications** (`modification_details`, `text`, `not required`): Describe the details of any recent modifications.

### Key Access and Security Arrangements
* **Key Access and Security Arrangements** (`facility_access`, `text`, `not required`): Describe the arrangements for inspector access and security.

Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to prevent delays in the audit process. If you have any doubts or questions, contact our support team for assistance.
