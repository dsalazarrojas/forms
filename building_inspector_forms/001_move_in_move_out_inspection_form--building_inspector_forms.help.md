# Move In Move Out Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist building inspectors in conducting a comprehensive inspection of a property during the move-in and move-out process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the property's systems and features thoroughly before starting the inspection.
2. Answer each question based on your observations and findings.
3. Be thorough and accurate in your responses.
4. Use the "Add Comment" field to provide any additional information or supporting documentation.
5. Save the form regularly to avoid losing your work.
