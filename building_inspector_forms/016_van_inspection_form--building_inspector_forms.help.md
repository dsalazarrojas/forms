# Van Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Van Inspection Form is a comprehensive guide for inspecting and documenting a van's condition, features, and details. It is likely used for maintenance, repair, or certification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and tools before starting the inspection.
2. Read each field's label and description carefully.
3. Fill in the required fields according to the information you've gathered during the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Van Inspection Form** (`van_inspection_form_title`): A brief title to identify the van and inspection.
*   **Make Year** (`van_inspection_form_make_year`): Enter the van's make and year.
*   **Van Model** (`van_inspection_form_model`): Enter the van's model (optional, but recommended for accurate identification).
*   **Engine Size** (`van_inspection_form_engine_size`): Measure and record the engine's size (e.g., liters or cubic inches).
*   **Engine Type** (`van_inspection_form_engine_type`): Select the van's engine type (Inline-4, V6, V8).
*   **Transmission** (`van_inspection_form_transmission`): Choose the van's transmission type (Automatic, Manual).
*   **Body Type** (`van_inspection_form_body_type`): Choose the van's body type (Box, Panel, Van).
*   **Condition** (`van_inspection_form_condition`): Describe the van's overall condition (e.g., excellent, fair, poor).
*   **Safety Features** (`van_inspection_form_safety_features`): List the van's safety features (e.g., airbags, anti-lock brakes).
*   **Comments** (`van_inspection_form_comments`): Add any additional comments or notes about the van's condition or features.
*   **Signature** (`van_inspection_form_signature`): Sign the form to confirm the inspection was done.
*   **Inspector Signature** (`van_inspection_form_inspector_signature`): Sign the form as the inspector.
*   **Completed On** (`van_inspection_form_completed_on`): Date the form was completed.
*   **Assigned To** (`van_inspection_form_assigned_to`): If applicable, enter the name of the person assigned to the van (optional).
*   **Inspection Date** (`van_inspection_form_inspection_date`): Date the van was inspected.
