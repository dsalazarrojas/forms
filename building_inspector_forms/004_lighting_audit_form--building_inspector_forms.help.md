# Lighting Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lighting Audit Form is a tool used to collect and record data on the lighting characteristics and power requirements of a site during an initial inspection.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **General Info** field with any relevant information about the site, such as its name and address.
2. Enter the **Location** of the site, which may be a room, building, or area within a facility.
3. In the **Equipment Info** field, list any relevant information about the lighting equipment used at the site.
4. In the **Characteristics** field, describe any distinctive or notable characteristics of the lighting fixtures or installation.
5. In the **Power Requirements** field, enter the power requirements of the lighting system, using the format "Watts" or "VA" (Volts Amps).
6. Choose the **Quality Assessments** option from the dropdown menu that best describes the overall quality of the lighting:
	* Lumen: measures the total amount of light emitted by a source
	* Lux: measures the illumination level in a given area
	* WATTS: measures the power consumption in terms of Watts
	* KWH: measures the electrical energy consumption in terms of kilowatt-hours
7. In the **Other Characteristics** field, note any other notable or unique characteristics of the lighting that are not captured in the other fields.
8. Finally, add any comments or notes in the **Comments** field to provide additional context or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info**: Enter a brief description of the site being inspected.
* **Location**: Enter the address or location of the site being inspected.
* **Equipment Info**: List any relevant information about the lighting equipment, including type, model, and quantity.
* **Characteristics**: Describe any notable or unique characteristics of the lighting fixtures or installation.
* **Power Requirements**: Enter the power requirements of the lighting system, using the format "Watts" or "VA" (Volts Amps).
* **Quality Assessments**: Choose from one of the four options to describe the overall quality of the lighting:
	+ Lumen: measures the total amount of light emitted by a source
	+ Lux: measures the illumination level in a given area
	+ WATTS: measures the power consumption in terms of Watts
	+ KWH: measures the electrical energy consumption in terms of kilowatt-hours
* **Other Characteristics**: Enter any additional information that does not fit in the other fields.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or notes to provide context or additional information.
