# Elevator Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record information related to the inspection of elevators, including the status, date, and other relevant details. It is designed for building inspectors to provide accurate and comprehensive records of elevator inspections.

## How To Complete This Form
### Prerequisites

- Make sure you have the necessary information about the elevator before starting the form.

### Completing the Form

1. Select the correct status of the elevator in question from the options provided. This could be a new inspection, a re-inspection, or other options as per your judgment.
2. Enter the date of the inspection, using the date format as specified in the field.
3. If you are the inspector, enter your name and any other relevant details.
4. Provide the number of the elevator being inspected.
5. Select the status of the elevator after the inspection from the options provided.
6. If necessary, choose additional options related to the inspection from the options provided.
7. Enter any comments or notes regarding the inspection.
8. If necessary, assign this inspection report to someone else.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **elevator_inspection_form**:
    - This is the title of the form.
* **elevator_status**: 
    - The current status of the elevator.
* **inspection_date**: 
    - The date on which the inspection was performed.
* **inspector_name**:
    - The name of the inspector who conducted the inspection.
* **elevator_number**:
    - The specific number of the elevator being inspected.
* **elevator_status_2**: 
    - An additional status of the elevator.
* **elevator_type**: 
    - The type of elevator inspected.
* **inspection_completed**:
    - Indicate which of the inspection was completed.
* **comments**:
    - Any notes or comments made during or after the inspection.
* **assigned_to**:
    - Who the inspection report has been assigned to (if anyone).

## Tips
- Make sure to enter all the required information to ensure accurate records.
- Review the options carefully to select the correct status and other details.
- Enter comments or notes in the "comments" field for future reference.
- Ensure the form is completed in a way that allows for easy tracking and follow-up actions.
