# Green Building Certification Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Green Building Certification Inspection Form is used by building inspectors to collect and verify information about a building's characteristics, sustainability features, and energy efficiency status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information section with the building's details.
2. Provide the building location and type.
3. Indicate the building's status (new, existing, or in progress).
4. List the building's characteristics, such as insulation, solar panels, and rainwater harvesting system.
5. Select the sustainability features, such as green roof, green wall, and greenhouse.
6. Determine the certification status (certified, provisional, or denied).
7. Add any comments about the building.
8. Enter the building's energy consumption details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (Page 1, Field 1):
	* **General Information** (`title`, `text`, required: false)
	* This field is the building's name, address, or a brief description.
* **Building Location** (Page 1, Field 2):
	* **Building Location** (`building_location`, `text`, required: false)
	* Enter the building's location, including street address or coordinates.
* **Building Type** (Page 1, Field 3):
	* **Building Type** (`building_type`, `select_one`, required: false)
	* Select from the options: Single-Family, Multi-Family, or Commercial.
* **Building Status** (Page 1, Field 4):
	* **Building Status** (`building_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	* Select from the options: New Construction, Existing, or In Progress.
* **Building Characteristics** (Page 1, Field 5):
	* **Building Characteristics** (`building_characteristics`, `text`, required: false)
	* List the building's characteristics, such as insulation, solar panels, or rainwater harvesting system.
* **Energy Efficiency Features** (Page 1, Field 6):
	* **Energy Efficiency Features** (`energy_efficiency_features`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	* Select from the options: Insulation, Solar Panels, or Rainwater Harvesting System.
* **Sustainability Features** (Page 1, Field 7):
	* **Sustainability Features** (`sustainability_features`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	* Select from the options: Green Roof, Green Wall, or Greenhouse.
* **Certification Status** (Page 1, Field 8):
	* **Certification Status** (`certification_status`, `select_one`, required: false)
	* Select from the options: Certified, Provisional, or Denied.
* **Comments** (Page 1, Field 9):
	* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false)
	* Add any additional comments about the building.
* **Energy Consumption** (Page 2, Field 10):
	* **Energy Consumption** (`energy_consumption`, `number`, required: false)
	* Enter the building's energy consumption details.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are filled accurately, as missing information may cause errors in the certification process. If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the building's documentation or consult with the building owner/manager.
