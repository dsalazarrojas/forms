The purpose of this form is to conduct an energy audit on a building, which is a thorough analysis of the building's energy usage, efficiency, and potential areas for improvement. This audit helps identify areas where energy conservation can be achieved, which can lead to cost savings and a reduced environmental impact. The form is designed to gather information about the building's site name, administration, status, equipment, facility concerns, safety concerns, comments, energy consumption, energy conservation measures, energy efficiency status, and a walk-through completion status. This information is crucial for building administrators, energy auditors, and facility managers to make informed decisions about improving the building's energy efficiency. This form is meant to be completed by authorized personnel, and it's essential to fill it out thoroughly and accurately to ensure the accuracy of the data collected.

## Purpose
The Energy Audit Checklist form is a comprehensive tool used to assess the energy efficiency of a building. It helps identify areas where energy can be saved and provides valuable information for making informed decisions about energy conservation measures.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the **Site Name** field with the name of the building being audited.
2.  In the **Admin** field, enter the name of the person responsible for the audit.
3.  In the **Building Status** field, describe the current state of the building (e.g., occupied, vacant, under construction, etc.).
4.  Select all the **Equipment Checklist** options that apply to the building, such as High-Performance Motors, High-Performance Pumps, and High-Performance Fans.
5.  In the **Facility Concerns** field, list any facility-related issues that need attention.
6.  In the **Safety Concerns** field, list any safety-related issues that need attention.
7.  In the **Comments** field, provide any additional comments or observations about the building.
8.  Enter the **Energy Consumption** value (in units of kWh or MMBtu) that represents the building's current energy consumption.
9.  Select the **Energy Conservation Measures** that are applicable to the building, such as Lighting, HVAC, and Water.
10. In the **Energy Efficiency Status** field, select the status of the building's energy efficiency (High, Medium, Low).
11. Check the **Walk Through Completed** field if the walk-through inspection has been completed.
12. In the **Walk Through Comments** field, provide any comments or observations about the walk-through inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Name**: Enter the name of the building being audited.
* **Admin**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the audit.
* **Building Status**: Describe the current state of the building.
* **Equipment Checklist**: Select all applicable equipment options that need attention.
* **Facility Concerns**: List any facility-related issues that need attention.
* **Safety Concerns**: List any safety-related issues that need attention.
* **Comments**: Provide additional comments or observations about the building.
* **Energy Consumption**: Enter the building's current energy consumption value.
* **Energy Conservation Measures**: Select all applicable energy conservation measures that are in place.
* **Energy Efficiency Status**: Select the status of the building's energy efficiency.
* **Walk Through Completed**: Check if the walk-through inspection has been completed.
* **Walk Through Comments**: Provide any comments or observations about the walk-through inspection.

## Tips
