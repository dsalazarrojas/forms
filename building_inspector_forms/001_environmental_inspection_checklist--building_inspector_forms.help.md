# Environmental Inspection Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Environmental Inspection Checklist is a form used to gather information on the condition of a facility's environmental systems, including plumbing, electrical, and HVAC systems. This form helps identify potential hazards and ensures compliance with regulatory standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each field according to your inspection findings.
2. Be thorough and accurate in your responses.
3. Provide clear and concise descriptions of any issues or concerns.
4. Use the provided fields to report any observations or evidence of environmental system malfunctions or deficiencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name** (1, text, Optional): Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
* **Building Age** (2, number, Optional): Record the age of the building or facility, in years.
* **Electrical Systems** (3, text, Optional): Describe the condition of the electrical systems, including any concerns or issues.
* **Plumbing Systems** (4, text, Optional): Describe the condition of the plumbing systems, including any concerns or issues.
* **Fire Safety Systems** (5, text, Optional): Describe the condition of the fire safety systems, including any concerns or issues.
* **Water Supply Systems** (6, text, Optional): Describe the condition of the water supply systems, including any concerns or issues.
* **Heating and Cooling Systems** (7, text, Optional): Describe the condition of the heating and cooling systems, including any concerns or issues.
* **Structural Systems** (8, text, Optional): Describe the condition of the structural systems, including any concerns or issues.
* **Electrical Systems Equipment** (9, text, Optional): Describe the condition of the electrical systems equipment, including any concerns or issues.
* **Safety Procedures** (10, text, Optional): Describe the safety procedures in place, including any concerns or issues.
* **Emergency Procedures** (11, text, Optional): Describe the emergency procedures in place, including any concerns or issues.
* **Facility Condition** (12, text, Optional): Describe the overall condition of the facility, including any concerns or issues.

Note: This form is not a substitute for a comprehensive building inspection or assessment. The information collected on this form is for informational purposes only and should be used to identify potential areas for further investigation and action.
