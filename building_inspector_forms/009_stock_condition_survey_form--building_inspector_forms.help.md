# stock_condition_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stock Condition Survey Form is used to inspect and record the condition of stock, products, or goods during various stages of storage, transportation, or handling. This form helps ensure that the stock is properly inspected for any damages, defects, or other conditions that may affect its quality or value.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of inspection you are performing (General Inspections, Inspect the inventory, Inspect the stockroom, or Inspect the warehouse).
2. Choose the specific conditions you want to check for (Condition Check List, Additional Condition Checks).
3. Provide a brief description of any damage found (Damage Description).
4. Rate the severity of the damage (Damage Grade).
5. Add any additional information about the damage (Damage Grade Note).
6. Select any additional conditions you want to check for (Additional Condition Checks 1, Additional Condition Checks 2).
7. Indicate where the stock was located (Stock Location).
8. Enter the date of purchase (Date of Purchase).
9. Enter the quantity of stock inspected (Quantity).
10. Enter the name of the inspector (Inspector Name).
11. Add any comments about the inspection (Comments).
12. Select the category and subcategory of the stock condition survey form (Stock Condition Survey Form Category, Stock Condition Survey Form Subcategory).
13. Enter the description of the stock condition survey form (Stock Condition Survey Form Description).
14. Enter the form ID and version (Form ID, Form Version).
15. Select the person or group assigned to the form (Assigned To).
16. Enter the date assigned (Assigned Date).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Inspections** (`1`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of inspection you are performing. Options include Inspect the inventory, Inspect the stockroom, and Inspect the warehouse.
* **Condition Check List** (`2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the specific conditions you want to check for. Options include Check for water damage, Check for fire damage, Check for mold growth, Check for pest infestation, and Check for other.
* **Damage Description** (`3`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of any damage found.
* **Damage Grade** (`4`, `number`, optional): Rate the severity of the damage on a scale of 1-10.
* **Damage Grade Note** (`5`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information about the damage.
* **Additional Condition Checks 1** (`6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any additional conditions you want to check for. Options include Check for stains, Check for tears, and Check for other.
* **Additional Condition Checks 2** (`7`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any additional conditions you want to check for. Options include Check for rust, Check for scratches, Check for dents, and Check for discoloration.
* **Stock Location** (`8`, `text`, optional): Indicate where the stock was located.
* **Date of Purchase** (`9`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the stock was purchased.
* **Quantity** (`10`, `number`, optional): Enter the quantity of stock inspected.
* **Inspector Name** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the inspector.
* **Comments** (`12`, `text`, optional): Add any comments about the inspection.
* **Stock Condition Survey Form Category** (`13`, `text`, optional): Select the category of the stock condition survey form.
* **Stock Condition Survey Form Subcategory** (`14`, `text`, optional): Select the subcategory of the stock condition survey form.
* **Stock Condition Survey Form Description** (`15`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the stock condition survey form.
* **Form ID** (`16`, `text`, optional): Enter the form ID.
* **Form Version** (`17`, `text`, optional): Enter the form version.
* **Assigned To** (`18`, `text`, optional): Select the person or group assigned to the form.
* **Assigned Date** (`19`, `date`, optional): Enter the date assigned.
* **Stock Condition Survey Form Category** (`20`, `text`, optional): Select the category of the stock condition survey form.
* **Stock Condition Survey Form Subcategory** (`21`, `text`, optional): Select the subcategory of the stock condition survey form.
* **Stock Condition Survey Form Description** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the stock condition survey form.
