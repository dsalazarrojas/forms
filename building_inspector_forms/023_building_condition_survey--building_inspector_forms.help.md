# Building Condition Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Building Condition Survey form is designed to collect data on the condition of a building, focusing on various aspects such as its structure, systems, and safety features.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing the form and ensure you have all the necessary credentials and permissions to complete it.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
3. For each field, follow the instructions provided in the Field-by-Field Explanation section for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey date** (`id: 2`, `label: Survey date`, `type: date`, `required: true`): Enter the date when the survey was conducted.
* **Inspector name** (`id: 3`, `label: Inspector name`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the name of the person conducting the survey.
* **Inspector credentials** (`id: 4`, `label: Inspector credentials or license`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the relevant credentials or licenses of the inspector.
* **Property name** (`id: 6`, `label: Property name`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the name of the property being surveyed.
* **Property address** (`id: 7`, `label: Property address`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the address of the property being surveyed.
* **Property type** (`id: 8`, `label: Property type`, `type: select_one`, `options: Single family home, Multi-family residential, Office building, Commercial, Industrial, Mixed-use, Historical, Other`, `required: true`): Select the type of property being surveyed.
* **Year constructed** (`id: 9`, `label: Year constructed`, `type: number`, `required: true`): Enter the year the property was built.
* **Year of last major renovation** (`id: 10`, `label: Year of last major renovation`, `type: number`, `required: false`): Enter the year of the last major renovation, if applicable.
* **Total square footage** (`id: 11`, `label: Total square footage`, `type: number`, `required: true`): Enter the total square footage of the property.
* **Roof condition and integrity** (`id: 13`, `label: Roof condition and integrity`, `type: select_one`, `options: Good - Recently replaced, Fair - Serviceable, Poor - Nearing end of life, Bad - Leaks or major damage, Critical - Immediate replacement needed`, `required: true`): Select the condition of the roof.
* **Roof material type** (`id: 14`, `label: Roof material type`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the type of roof material used.
* **Roof access and safety** (`id: 15`, `label: Roof access and safety`, `type: select_one`, `options: Safe access present, Limited access, No safe access`, `required: true`): Select the condition of roof access and safety.
* **Foundation condition** (`id: 16`, `label: Foundation condition`, `type: select_one`, `options: Excellent - No visible issues, Good - Minor cracks acceptable, Fair - Moderate foundation issues, Poor - Significant settling or damage, Critical - Structural danger`, `required: true`): Select the condition of the foundation.
* **Exterior wall condition** (`id: 17`, `label: Exterior wall condition`, `type: select_one`, `options: Good - Well maintained, Fair - Normal wear, Poor - Deterioration visible, Critical - Dangerous conditions`, `required: true`): Select the condition of exterior walls.
* **Primary exterior material** (`id: 18`, `label: Primary exterior material`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the type of material used for the exterior.
* **Windows and doors condition** (`id: 19`, `label: Windows and doors condition`, `type: select_one`, `options: Good - Functional and sealed, Fair - Some issues, Poor - Multiple problems, Critical - Immediate repairs needed`, `required: true`): Select the condition of windows and doors.
* **Interior wall and finishes condition** (`id: 21`, `label: Interior walls and finishes`, `type: select_one`, `options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical`, `required: true`): Select the condition of interior walls and finishes.
* **Flooring condition throughout** (`id: 22`, `label: Flooring condition throughout`, `type: select_one`, `options: Excellent - Recently installed, Good - Well maintained, Fair - Functional, Poor - Worn or damaged, Critical - Safety hazard`, `required: true`): Select the condition of flooring.
* **Interior ceiling condition** (`id: 23`, `label: Interior ceiling condition`, `type: select_one`, `options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical`, `required: true`): Select the condition of the interior ceiling.
* **Stairs and railings safety** (`id: 24`, `label: Stairs and railings`, `type: select_one`, `options: Safe and compliant, Safe with minor issues, Requires improvements, Unsafe`, `required: true`): Select the safety of stairs and railings.
* **Electrical system condition** (`id: 26`, `label: Electrical system condition`, `type: select_one`, `options: Modern and safe, Adequate and safe, Aging but functional, Needs upgrades, Unsafe - requires immediate attention`, `required: true`): Select the condition of the electrical system.
* **Electrical panel condition** (`id: 27`, `label: Electrical panel condition`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the condition of the electrical panel.
* **Plumbing system condition** (`id: 28`, `label: Plumbing system condition`, `type: select_one`, `options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Critical`, `required: true`): Select the condition of the plumbing system.
* **Water quality and pressure** (`id: 29`, `label: Water quality and pressure`, `type: select_one`, `options: Good, Acceptable, Poor, Unknown`, `required: true`): Select the water quality and pressure.
* **HVAC system condition** (`id: 30`, `label: HVAC system condition`, `type: select_one`, `options: Recently serviced, Good condition, Fair - needs service, Poor - multiple issues, Not functional`, `required: true`): Select the condition of the heating and cooling system.
* **Heating and cooling adequacy** (`id: 31`, `label: Heating and cooling adequacy`, `type: select_one`, `options: Excellent coverage, Good coverage, Adequate coverage, Inadequate`, `required: true`): Select the adequacy of heating and cooling.
* **Fire safety features** (`id: 33`, `label: Fire safety features present`, `type: select_multiple`, `options: Fire extinguishers, Smoke detectors, Fire alarm system, Emergency lighting, Evacuation plan posted, Sprinkler system, None`, `required: true`): Select the fire safety features present.
* **Code compliance** (`id: 34`, `label: Apparent code compliance`, `type: select_one`, `options: Compliant, Mostly compliant, Minor violations, Multiple violations, Unknown`, `required: true`): Select the apparent code compliance.
* **Estimated deferred maintenance cost** (`id: 36`, `label: Estimated deferred maintenance cost`, `type: number`, `required: false`): Enter the estimated cost of deferred maintenance.
* **Priority repairs and recommendations** (`id: 37`, `label: Priority repairs and recommendations`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter any priority repairs and recommendations.

## Tips

* Be accurate and thorough in completing the form.
* Use the options provided to make informed selections.
* If unsure about a particular condition, select the option that best reflects your assessment.
* Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed before submitting.

Please refer to this guide for guidance on completing the form accurately and effectively.
