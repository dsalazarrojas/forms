# Restaurant Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Inspection Checklist is a comprehensive guide for health and safety inspections of restaurants. It helps health inspectors and regulatory bodies assess the compliance of a restaurant with local regulations and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you cover all aspects of the inspection.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately based on your observations.
3. If you're unsure or have any doubts, use the space provided for explanations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Restaurant Name** (1, text, required)
This field is for the name of the restaurant being inspected.
### **Address** (2, text, required)
This field is for the address of the restaurant.
### **Inspector Information** (3, text, required)
This field is for the inspector's name and badge/ID number.
### **Comments** (4, text, optional)
This field is for any comments or observations related to the inspection.
### **Recommendations** (5, text, optional)
This field is for any recommendations for improvement or further actions.

### Page 1: Restaurant Premises
- **Entrance and Exit** (1.1, text, required)
This field is for the condition of the entrance and exit areas of the restaurant.
- **Waiting Area** (1.2, text, required)
This field is for the condition of the waiting area of the restaurant.
### Page 2: Kitchen and Storage
- **Kitchen Area** (2.1, text, required)
This field is for the condition of the kitchen area of the restaurant.
- **Storage Areas** (2.2, text, required)
This field is for the condition of the storage areas of the restaurant.
### Page 3: Food Handling and Preparation
- **Food Handling** (3.1, text, required)
This field is for the condition of food handling practices in the restaurant.
- **Food Preparation** (3.2, text, required)
This field is for the condition of food preparation practices in the restaurant.
### Page 4: Hygiene and Sanitation
- **Washrooms** (4.1, text, required)
This field is for the condition of the washrooms of the restaurant.
- **Waste Management** (4.2, text, required)
This field is for the condition of waste management practices in the restaurant.
### Page 5: Fire Safety and Security
- **Fire Safety** (5.1, text, required)
This field is for the condition of fire safety measures in the restaurant.
- **Security Measures** (5.2, text, required)
This field is for the condition of security measures in the restaurant.
