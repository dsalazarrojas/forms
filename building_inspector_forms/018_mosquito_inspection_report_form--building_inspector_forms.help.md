# Mosquito Inspection Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mosquito Inspection Report Form is used to gather data about mosquito infestations in properties. This data will help building inspectors or pest control professionals understand the extent of the infestation and develop effective mosquito control measures.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Property Address** of the property being inspected.
2. Enter your **Inspector Name**.
3. Select the **Mosquito Species** found during the inspection.
4. Enter the **Mosquito Count** of the species found.
5. Enter the **Date Inspected**.
6. Enter the **Time Inspected**.
7. (Optional) Add any **Inspector Notes** regarding the inspection.
8. (Optional) Enter the **Assigned Tool** used during the inspection.
9. Enter the **Property Type**, e.g., residential, commercial, etc.
10. (Optional) Enter any **Mosquito Control Measures** taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property being inspected.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter your name and contact information.
* **Mosquito Species**: Select the species of mosquito found during the inspection from the available options.
* **Mosquito Count**: Enter the number of mosquitoes found.
* **Date Inspected**: Enter the date the inspection took place.
* **Time Inspected**: Enter the time the inspection took place.
* **Inspector Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes from the inspector.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool assigned to the inspector for the inspection (if applicable).
* **Property Type**: Enter the type of property being inspected, e.g., residential, commercial, etc.
* **Mosquito Control Measures**: Enter any mosquito control measures taken during the inspection (if applicable).
