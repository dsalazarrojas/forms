# Trash Violation Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Trash Violation Report Form" is a tool used to report incidents or infractions related to trash or waste management. This form is designed to collect information about trash violations, and its completion may be mandatory or voluntary depending on the situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the "Trash Violation Report Form", follow these steps:

1.  Determine whether a trash violation has occurred.
2.  Answer the "Trash Violation Report" question with "Yes" or "No" to confirm or deny the presence of a violation.
3.  Fill out the "Neighbor" field with the name and contact information of a neighbor or relevant contact person (if applicable).
4.  Enter the date and time of the incident using the "Date" and "Time" fields.
5.  Provide a detailed description of the violation in the "Description" field.
6.  If necessary, specify the location of the incident in the "Location" field.
7.  If you have additional comments or remarks, you can include them in the "Inspector Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Trash Violation Report (yes/no)**: Answer this question with "Yes" if you have observed or confirmed a trash violation. If no violation is present, choose "No".
*   **Neighbor**: Enter the name and contact information of a neighbor or relevant contact person who may be affected by the violation.
*   **Date**: Record the date of the incident when the trash violation was observed or occurred.
*   **Time**: Enter the time of the incident using 24-hour format (HH:MM).
*   **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the trash violation, including details about the location, size, and type of waste or trash.
*   **Location**: If you have specific information about the location of the incident, enter it here. This could include the address, street name, or landmark.
*   **Inspector Comments**: Include any additional comments or remarks you have about the incident or the situation.
