# Asbestos Inspection Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Asbestos Inspection Report Form is used to document the results of asbestos inspections conducted on properties. It helps inspectors gather information about the properties, including inspector details, inspection dates, property information, and asbestos findings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as a certified inspector in the "Inspector Name" field.
2. Provide your inspector license number in the "Inspector License Number" field.
3. Enter the date of the inspection in the "Date of Inspection" field.
4. Fill in the full property address in the "Property Address" field.
5. Choose the type of property inspected from the "Property Type" options (e.g., Residential Single Family, Commercial Office).
6. Enter the approximate year the property was built in the "Year Property Was Built" field.
7. Enter the total building square footage in the "Building Square Footage" field.
8. Select all areas inspected from the "Areas Inspected" options (e.g., Basement, Attic, Garage).
9. Indicate if asbestos is suspected based on the visual inspection in the "Is Asbestos Suspected" field.
10. Describe the condition of suspected materials in the "Condition of Suspected Materials" field.
11. Enter the total number of samples collected in the "Number of Samples Collected" field.
12. Describe where each sample was taken from in the "Sample Collection Locations" field.
13. Enter the name of the laboratory where samples will be analyzed in the "Testing Laboratory Name" field.
14. Enter the expected date of lab results in the "Expected Results Date" field.
15. Indicate if the property poses immediate health risk in the "Is There Immediate Danger" field.
16. Summarize your recommendations for follow-up actions in the "Inspector Recommendations" field.
17. Indicate if a follow-up inspection is required in the "Is Follow-up Inspection Required" field.
18. Enter the number of photos attached to support your findings in the "Number of Photos Attached" field (optional).
19. Sign your name as the inspector in the "Inspector Signature" field.
20. Confirm the report status as "Complete" if all fields are filled out accurately, "Pending Lab Results" if lab results are still pending, or "Incomplete" if the report is not complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector Name**: Enter your full name as a certified inspector.
* **Inspector License Number**: Provide your inspector license number for verification purposes.
* **Date of Inspection**: Enter the date when the inspection was conducted.
* **Property Address**: Enter the full address of the inspected property.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property inspected (e.g., Residential Single Family, Commercial Office).
* **Year Property Was Built**: Enter the approximate year the property was built.
* **Building Square Footage**: Enter the total area of the property.
* **Areas Inspected**: Select all areas that were inspected (e.g., Basement, Attic, Garage).
* **Is Asbestos Suspected**: Indicate if asbestos is suspected based on the visual inspection.
* **Condition of Suspected Materials**: Describe the state of suspected materials.
* **Number of Samples Collected**: Enter the total number of samples collected.
* **Sample Collection Locations**: Describe where each sample was taken from.
* **Testing Laboratory Name**: Enter the name of the laboratory where samples will be analyzed.
* **Expected Results Date**: Enter the expected date of lab results.
* **Is There Immediate Danger**: Indicate if the property poses immediate health risk.
* **Inspector Recommendations**: Summarize your recommendations for follow-up actions.
* **Is Follow-up Inspection Required**: Indicate if a follow-up inspection is required.
* **Number of Photos Attached**: Enter the number of photos attached to support your findings (optional).
* **Inspector Signature**: Type your full name as a signature.
* **Report Status**: Confirm the report status as "Complete" if all fields are filled out accurately, "Pending Lab Results" if lab results are still pending, or "Incomplete" if the report is not complete.
