## Site Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report on site inspections, ensuring that buildings and properties are in compliance with safety and regulatory standards.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, select the "Site Inspection Form" page and fill out the fields as necessary.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the inspector who conducted the site inspection.
* **Inspector Email** (`inspector_email`, email, Optional): Enter the email address of the inspector who conducted the site inspection.
* **Inspector Phone** (`inspector_phone`, text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the inspector who conducted the site inspection.
* **Inspector License** (`inspector_license`, text, Optional): Enter the license number of the inspector who conducted the site inspection.
* **Date Inspected** (`date_inspected`, date, Optional): Enter the date of the site inspection.
## Tips
