# offsite_accommodation_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The offsite accommodation inspection form is designed to collect information for offsite accommodations inspected.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the "Building Details" field to enter any necessary information about the building or accommodation being inspected.
2. Answer the "Property Manager" question with 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the property manager was present during the inspection.
3. Answer the "Property Inspector" question with 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the property inspector was present during the inspection.
4. Enter the "Date Inspected" using the date format.
5. Enter the "Time Inspected" using the time format.
6. In the "Accommodation Details" field, enter any additional information about the accommodation, such as condition or issues found.
7. Finally, type any comments or additional information in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Building Details** (`building_details`, text, Optional): Enter any necessary information about the building or accommodation being inspected.
* **Property Manager** (`property_manager`, select_one, Optional): Answer with 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the property manager was present during the inspection.
* **Property Inspector** (`property_inspector`, select_one, Optional): Answer with 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the property inspector was present during the inspection.
* **Date Inspected** (`date_inspected`, date, Optional): Enter the date of the inspection in the correct format.
* **Time Inspected** (`time_inspected`, time, Optional): Enter the time of the inspection in the correct format.
* **Accommodation Details** (`accommodation_details`, text, Optional): Enter any additional information about the accommodation, such as condition or issues found.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, Optional): Type any comments or additional information in the free text field.
