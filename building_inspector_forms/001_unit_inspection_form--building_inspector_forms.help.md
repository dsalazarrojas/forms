# Unit Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Unit Inspection Form is designed to gather information about the condition of a unit, including its address, appliances, and safety equipment. This form is used by property managers, building inspectors, or maintenance personnel to ensure the unit's safety and compliance.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Start by filling out the address section**: Enter the address of the unit being inspected.
2.  Enter the **move-in date**, if applicable.
3.  In the **tenant information** section, provide the tenant's name, email, and phone number.
4.  For **contact method**, select the preferred method of communication (email, phone, or in-person).
5.  Enter any **comments** or notes about the inspection.
6.  In the **safety equipment** section, check the status of the following:
    *   **Smoke detectors**
    *   **Fire extinguishers**
    *   **Fire alarms**
    *   **Sprinkler system**
    *   **Emergency exits**
7.  For each **appliances**, select the status (working, not working, or broken).
8.  In the **contact note** section, add any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
