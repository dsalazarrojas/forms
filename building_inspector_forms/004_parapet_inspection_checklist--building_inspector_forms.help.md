# Parapet Inspection Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Parapet Inspection Checklist form is used to document the condition and status of parapets. This form helps building inspectors gather and record essential information about a parapet's general information, condition, photo location, photo comment, findings, and notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the parapet's general information, including any relevant details.
2. Describe the parapet's condition using the provided field.
3. Indicate where the photo was taken, using the photo location field.
4. Provide a comment about the photo, if applicable.
5. Record the date when the findings were observed or recorded.
6. Add any additional notes about the parapet's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (1, text, required/optional): Enter the parapet's identification number, address, or any other relevant details.
* **Parapet Condition** (2, number, required/optional): Describe the parapet's current condition using a number scale (e.g., 1-10) where 1 is very poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Where was the photo taken?** (3, text, required/optional): Specify the location where the photo was taken to provide context.
* **Photo Comment** (4, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the photo.
* **When were the findings observed or recorded?** (5, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the findings were observed or recorded.
* **Notes** (6, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or observations about the parapet's condition.
