# Chimney Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a chimney's condition and structure, used for regulatory, insurance, or maintenance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Inspector Information** section with your name and license number.
2. Enter the **Inspection Date** when you performed the verification check.
3. If required, fill out the **Property Details** section with the property address, city, state, and zip code.
4. Enter the **Property Owner** details if available.
5. Select the **Chimney Specifications** based on your observation.
6. Check the **Chimney Condition** based on your findings, including **Flue Condition**, **Chimney Cap Condition**, and **Structural Integrity**.
7. Fill out the **Verification Result** and **Verification Status** sections based on your assessment.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector Information**
	+ **Inspector Name**: Your name, as it appears on your license.
	+ **Inspector License**: Your license number, as issued by a relevant authority.
* **Inspection Date**: Date when you performed the verification check.
* **Property Details**
	+ **Property Address**: Address of the property with the chimney.
	+ **City**: City where the property is located.
	+ **State**: State where the property is located.
	+ **Zip Code**: Postal zip code of the property.
* **Property Owner**
	+ **Property Owner Name**: Name of the property owner.
	+ **Owner Phone Number**: Phone number of the property owner.
	+ **Owner Email**: Email of the property owner (if available).
* **Chimney Specifications**
	+ **Chimney Construction Type**: Type of construction used for the chimney (e.g., masonry brick, metal frame, etc.).
* **Chimney Condition**
	+ **Structural Integrity**: Assessment of the chimney's overall structural integrity.
	+ **Flue Condition**: Condition of the flue pipe.
	+ **Chimney Cap Condition**: Condition of the chimney cap.
* **Verification Result**
	+ **Verification Status**: Final assessment of the chimney's status (approved, conditional, failed, or pending).
* **Verification Status**
	+ **Verification Status**: Status of the chimney based on your findings (approved, conditional, failed, or pending).
