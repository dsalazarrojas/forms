# Workplace Safety Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help building administrators or safety officers inspect and document the safety status of the workplace in various aspects, including general hazards, fire protection, and emergency procedures. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the checklist and ensure you have the necessary knowledge and experience to complete it accurately.
2. Answer each question with "Yes" or "No" based on your observations and findings during the inspection.
3. Enter any relevant comments or notes in the text fields if necessary.
4. Submit the completed form after reviewing all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Hazards** (`general_hazards`, select_multiple, required): Check if there are any general hazards present in the workplace that could pose a risk to employees, such as uneven flooring, exposed wiring, or sharp edges. 
* **Electrical Hazards** (`electrical_hazards`, select_one, required): Check if electrical hazards such as exposed wiring or frayed cords are present. If yes, note the location and severity of the issue. 
* **Fire Hazards** (`fire_hazards`, select_multiple, required): Check if fire hazards such as open flames, hot surfaces, or fire extinguishers are present. 
* **Fire Protection Equipment** (`fire_protection_equipment`, select_multiple, required): Check if fire protection equipment such as fire alarms, sprinklers, or smoke detectors are present and functioning properly. 
* **Fire Extinguishing Equipment** (`fire_extinguishing_equipment`, select_multiple, required): Check if fire extinguishing equipment such as fire extinguishers, fire hoses, or standpipes are present, easily accessible, and in good condition. 
* **Emergency Exit Routes** (`emergency_exit_routes`, select_multiple, required): Check if emergency exit routes are clearly marked and easily accessible. 
* **Emergency Exit Lights** (`emergency_exit_lights`, select_multiple, required): Check if emergency exit lights are present and functioning properly. 
* **Fire Drills** (`fire_drills`, select_one, required): Check if fire drills have been conducted in the last month. 
* **First Aids** (`first_aids`, select_one, required): Check if first aid kits are readily available and easily accessible in the workplace. 
* **Fire Extinguishers** (`fire_extinguishers`, select_multiple, required): Check if fire extinguishers are present, easily accessible, and in good condition. 
* **Fire Supervisors** (`fire_supervisors`, select_multiple, required): Check if fire supervisors are designated and easily accessible. 
* **Emergency Contact Numbers** (`emergency_contact_numbers`, select_multiple, required): Check if emergency contact numbers such as the fire department's phone number are posted and easily accessible. 
* **Fire Safety Procedures** (`fire_safety_procedures`, text, required): Enter any fire safety procedures that are in place, such as evacuation plans or emergency contact procedures. 
* **Electrical Equipment** (`electrical_equipment`, select_one, required): Check if electrical equipment is properly maintained and in good condition. 
* **Fire Sprinkler Systems** (`fire_sprinkler_systems`, select_multiple, required): Check if fire sprinkler systems are present and functioning properly. 
* **Fire Safety Plan** (`fire_safety_plan`, text, required): Enter any fire safety plan in place, such as emergency contact procedures. 
* **Fire Extinguishers Location** (`fire_extinguishers_location`, text, required): Enter the location of fire extinguishers, including their proximity to exit routes. 
* **Fire Extinguishers Condition** (`fire_extinguishers_condition`, select_multiple, required): Check if fire extinguishers are easily accessible, in good condition, and properly charged. 
* **Fire Safety Inspection** (`fire_safety_inspection`, select_one, required): Check if a fire safety inspection has been conducted in the last month. 
* **Fire Equipment Maintenance** (`fire_equipment_maintenance`, select_multiple, required): Check if fire equipment is properly maintained and in good condition. 
* **Fire Equipment Testing** (`fire_equipment_testing`, select_multiple, required): Check if fire equipment is regularly tested and maintained. 
* **Fire Equipment Inspection** (`fire_equipment_inspection`, select_multiple, required): Check if fire equipment is inspected regularly. 
* **Fire Equipment Service** (`fire_equipment_service`, select_multiple, required): Check if fire equipment is properly serviced and in good condition. 
* **Fire Equipment Location** (`fire_equipment_location`, text, required): Enter the location of fire equipment, including fire extinguishers, fire alarms, and sprinklers. 

## Tips
- Always complete the form in conjunction with a thorough physical inspection of the workplace.
- Ensure that all answers are accurate and complete.
- Review and update the form regularly to ensure safety procedures are up-to-date and followed.
- Submit the completed form to designated authorities for review and action.
