# Crane Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crane Inspection Checklist is a comprehensive form for inspecting cranes to identify potential safety hazards and ensure compliance with safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have access to the crane's logbook and review it for any previous inspection results or concerns.
* Identify the crane type, condition, and equipment.
* Determine if the crane is currently in use or has been used in the recent past.
* If the crane is in use, ensure that all necessary inspections have been completed.
* Complete the form by answering each question honestly and accurately.
* Document any concerns or issues identified during the inspection in the comments field.
* Review and sign the form to confirm that the inspection has been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Crane Inspection Form** (`crane_section`, text, optional): This is a space to document any notes or comments about the crane inspection form.
* **Crane Type** (`crane_type`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if the crane is a critical lift crane or 'No' if it is not.
* **Crane Condition** (`crane_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Select all conditions that apply to the crane's current state.
	+ 'Yes' if the crane is new and has never been used.
	+ 'No' if the crane is old or has been used extensively.
* **Location** (`crane_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the crane is currently in use or has been used.
* **Crane Equipment** (`crane_equipment`, text, optional): Describe the crane's equipment in detail.
* **Operator Name** (`operator_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the crane's operator.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or concerns about the crane.
* **Safety Standards** (`safety_standards`, text, optional): Enter any applicable safety standards or regulations related to the crane. 

Note: The following fields are not required and can be skipped if not applicable:

* Crane Type
* Crane Condition
* Location
* Crane Equipment
* Operator Name
* Inspection Date
* Inspection Time
* Comments
* Safety Standards 

Please note that some fields may not be applicable depending on the crane's specific use and location.
