# Inspection Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inspection Tracking Form is designed for tracking and documenting inspection activities. This form is used to gather information about the inspection process, including category, description, date, time, and any notes taken during the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Category** field with the appropriate category (e.g., Building Inspector Forms).
2.  Enter a clear and concise description of the inspection process or findings in the **Description** field.
3.  Enter the date of the inspection in the **Date** field in the required format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
4.  Enter the time of the inspection in the **Time** field in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
5.  Record any notes taken during the inspection in the **Note** field.
6.  Enter the email address of the contact person or inspector in the **Email** field.
7.  Enter the phone number of the contact person or inspector in the **Phone** field.
8.  Select from the list of assigned tools used during the inspection in the **Assigned Tool** field.
9.  Choose the user who conducted the inspection from the list in the **Assigned User** field.
10. Record the date on which the inspection was assigned to the user in the **Assigned Date** field.
11. Record the time at which the inspection was assigned to the user in the **Assigned Time** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Category**: This field is used to determine which type of form this inspection tracking form is being used for. Select the appropriate category (e.g., Building Inspector Forms).
*   **Description**: This field is a text input field for describing the inspection process or findings. Enter a clear and concise description of the inspection.
*   **Date**: This field is for recording the date of the inspection. Enter the date in the required format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Time**: This field is for recording the time of the inspection. Enter the time in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
*   **Note**: This field is for recording any notes taken during the inspection. This field is typically used for additional comments or observations.
*   **Email**: This field is for recording the email address of the contact person or inspector.
*   **Phone**: This field is for recording the phone number of the contact person or inspector.
*   **Assigned Tool**: This field is for selecting from a list of assigned tools used during the inspection. Choose the tools that were used.
*   **Assigned User**: This field is for selecting from a list of assigned users who conducted the inspection. Choose the user who conducted the inspection.
*   **Assigned Date**: This field is for recording the date on which the inspection was assigned to the user.
*   **Assigned Time**: This field is for recording the time at which the inspection was assigned to the user.

## Tips

*   Always enter the required fields to ensure accurate reporting and tracking.
*   Select the correct category for the form to ensure proper categorization.
*   Enter the correct date and time to ensure accurate record-keeping.
*   Enter clear and concise descriptions and notes to ensure accurate inspection reporting.
*   Verify the contact person's email and phone number to ensure they can be reached.
*   Verify the assigned tools and users to ensure accurate tracking.
