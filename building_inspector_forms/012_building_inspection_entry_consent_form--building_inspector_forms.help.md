# Building Inspection Entry Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from tenants before conducting a building inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your full name as the tenant.
2.  Enter your contact email address.
3.  Enter your contact phone number.
4.  Choose a proposed inspection date.
5.  Select a proposed inspection time.
6.  Choose the purpose of the inspection from the available options.
7.  Select the estimated duration of the inspection.
8.  If not using the scheduled inspector, enter the name of who will conduct the inspection.
9.  Read through the consent acknowledgment and confirm your understanding.
10.  Choose whether you consent to the inspection.
11.  If rescheduling the inspection, choose a preferred alternative time.
12.  If necessary, list any concerns or restrictions for the inspector.
13.  Finally, sign the form with the date of consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Tenant Name (Required)**: Enter your full name as the tenant.
*   **Email Address (Required)**: Enter your contact email address.
*   **Phone Number (Required)**: Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Proposed Inspection Date (Required)**: Choose a date for the inspection.
*   **Proposed Inspection Time (Required)**: Choose a time for the inspection.
*   **Purpose of Inspection (Required)**: Select the reason for the inspection from the available options.
*   **Estimated Duration (Optional)**: Choose how long the inspection will take.
*   **Inspector or Contractor Name (Optional)**: If not using the scheduled inspector, enter the name of who will conduct the inspection.
*   **Consent Acknowledgment**: Read through the inspection details and confirm your understanding.
*   **Consent**: Confirm whether you consent to the inspection.
*   **If Rescheduling, Preferred Time (Optional)**: If rescheduling the inspection, choose a preferred time.
*   **Special Access Instructions (Optional)**: If necessary, list any concerns or restrictions for the inspector.
*   **Any Concerns or Restrictions (Optional)**: If necessary, list any concerns or restrictions for the inspector.
*   **Date of Consent (Required)**: Sign the form with the date of consent.
*   **I understand my rights regarding this inspection (Required)**: Confirm your understanding of your rights.
