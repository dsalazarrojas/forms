# Hospital Daily Statistics Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Daily Statistics Report Form is used to collect and display daily statistics for hospital operations. It helps track important metrics such as patient activity, staff availability, and facility utilization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the relevant information for each category from the options provided. For example, choose whether there are staff present or not, or the type of admission.
2. Enter numerical values for metrics such as total patients, doctors, and nurses.
3. Select specific details for staff attendance, admission type, ward capacity, and bed occupation.
4. Enter time of admission and discharge, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Daily Activity (1, Text)
* Daily Activity: Enter a brief description of the day's events, such as notable medical emergencies or notable patient counts.

### Total Patients (2, Number)
* Total Patients: Enter the total number of patients admitted to the hospital for the day.

### Total Patients Category 1 (3, Number)
* Total Patients Category 1: Enter the number of patients admitted in Category 1.

### Total Doctors (4, Number)
* Total Doctors: Enter the total number of doctors available to work on the day.

### Total Doctors On Call (5, Number)
* Total Doctors On Call: Enter the number of doctors on call for the day.

### Total Doctors On Call Category 1 (6, Number)
* Total Doctors On Call Category 1: Enter the number of doctors on call in Category 1.

### Total Nurses (7, Number)
* Total Nurses: Enter the total number of nurses available to work on the day.

### Total Nurses On Call (8, Number)
* Total Nurses On Call: Enter the number of nurses on call for the day.

### Total Operating Rooms (9, Number)
* Total Operating Rooms: Enter the number of operating rooms used on the day.

### Total Available Beds (10, Number)
* Total Available Beds: Enter the total number of available beds in the hospital.

### Total Utilization (11, Number)
* Total Utilization: Enter the total utilization of hospital services.

### Total Utilization Category 1 (12, Number)
* Total Utilization Category 1: Enter the total utilization of Category 1.

### Total Doctors Available (13, Number)
* Total Doctors Available: Enter the total number of available doctors on the day.

### Total Doctors Unavailable (14, Number)
* Total Doctors Unavailable: Enter the number of doctors unavailable on the day.

### Total Nurses Available (15, Number)
* Total Nurses Available: Enter the total number of available nurses on the day.

### Total Nurses Unavailable (16, Number)
* Total Nurses Unavailable: Enter the number of nurses unavailable on the day.

### Staff Attendance (17, Select One)
* Staff Attendance: Choose whether staff are present or not.

### Admission Type (18, Select One)
* Admission Type: Select the type of admission, such as emergency or elective.

### Ward Capacity (19, Select Multiple)
* Ward Capacity: Choose how the wards are utilized, such as full, partly, or over capacity.

### Bed Occupation (20, Select Multiple)
* Bed Occupation: Select the bed occupation status, such as empty or occupied.

### Discharge Status (21, Select One)
* Discharge Status: Select the discharge status, such as admitted or discharged.

### Reason For Admission (22, Select One)
* Reason For Admission: Select the reason for admission, such as medical emergency or non-medical.

### Time Of Discharge (23, Time)
* Time Of Discharge: Enter the time of discharge, if applicable.

### Time Of Admission (24, Time)
* Time Of Admission: Enter the time of admission, if applicable.

### Date Of Discharge (25, Date)
* Date Of Discharge: Enter the date of discharge, if applicable.

Note: The fields with id 23, 24, and 25 are for optional input and can be skipped if not applicable.
