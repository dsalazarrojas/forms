# Measles Exposure Monitoring Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from individuals who may have been exposed to measles. The form is intended to be completed by people who have been in contact with someone who has been diagnosed with measles or have recently traveled to an area with an outbreak.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions carefully and honestly to provide accurate information.
2. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals to collect and store the data before proceeding.
3. If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (2)**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have been in contact with someone who has been diagnosed with measles or have recently traveled to an area with an outbreak.
* **Recent Travel (19)**: Select "Yes" if you have recently traveled to a country where there is a measles outbreak, otherwise select "No".
* **Exposure Date (14)**: Provide the date of your exposure to measles, if applicable.
* **Exposure Location (8)**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you were exposed to measles in a certain location.
* **Exposure Risk (16)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are at risk of exposure to measles.
* **Exposure Type (17)**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate the type of exposure you had to measles.
* **Recent Vaccination (24)**: Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of your recent vaccination.
* **Recent Symptoms (23)**: Describe any recent symptoms you have experienced.
* **Submit (25)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm that you have completed the survey.

Note: Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals to collect and store the data before proceeding. If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
