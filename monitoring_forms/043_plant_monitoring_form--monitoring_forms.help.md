# Plant Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plant Monitoring Form is used to track and manage information about plants in a monitoring system. It's designed to gather relevant data about plants, including their status, dates, heights, notes, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the options for "plant_monitoring_form_1" to indicate the plant's status.
2. Enter the date the plant was planted using the "plant_date_planted" field.
3. Record the plant's height using the "plant_height" field.
4. Add any additional notes about the plant in the "plant_notes" field.
5. If you have a contact email address for the plant, enter it in the "plant_email" field.
6. If you have a contact phone number for the plant, enter it in the "plant_phone" field.
7. For "plant_monitoring_form_2", "plant_monitoring_form_3", "plant_monitoring_form_4", and "plant_monitoring_form_5", select one of the available options. These fields are used for tracking specific monitoring data.
8. For "plant_monitoring_form_6", select multiple options from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **plant_monitoring_form_1**: A select-one field to choose the plant's status (Yes/No).
* **plant_date_planted**: A date field to record the date the plant was planted.
* **plant_height**: A number field to record the plant's height.
* **plant_notes**: A note field to add any additional notes about the plant.
* **plant_email**: An email field to enter a contact email address for the plant.
* **plant_phone**: A text field to enter a contact phone number for the plant.
* **plant_monitoring_form_2**: A select-multiple field to choose from a list of options.
* **plant_monitoring_form_3**: A select-one field to choose from a list of options.
* **plant_monitoring_form_4**: A note field to add any additional notes about the plant.
* **plant_monitoring_form_5**: A time field to record the plant's monitoring time.
* **plant_monitoring_form_6**: A select-multiple field to choose from a list of options.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact your administrator.
