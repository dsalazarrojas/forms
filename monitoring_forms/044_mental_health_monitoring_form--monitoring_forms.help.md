# mental_health_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health monitoring form is a tool used to track and monitor mental health status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer honestly.
2. Fill out the form for your own mental health monitoring.
3. Do not fill out the form for multiple individuals at once.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Monitoring Form
* **Monitoring Form** (`Monitoring Form`, text, optional): This field is a free text area where you can describe your current mental state.
### Page 1: Monitoring Form
* **Mood Level** (`mood_level`, number, optional): This field is used to rate your current mood level from 1 to 10, where 1 is very low and 10 is very high.
### Page 1: Monitoring Form
* **Stress Level** (`stress_level`, number, optional): This field is used to rate your current stress level from 1 to 10, where 1 is very low and 10 is very high.
### Page 1: Monitoring Form
* **Mental Clarity** (`mental_clarity`, number, optional): This field is used to rate your current mental clarity from 1 to 10, where 1 is very low and 10 is very high.
### Page 1: Monitoring Form
* **Anxiety Level** (`anxiety_level`, number, optional): This field is used to rate your current anxiety level from 1 to 10, where 1 is very high and 10 is very low.
### Page 1: Monitoring Form
* **Energy Level** (`energy_level`, number, optional): This field is used to rate your current energy level from 1 to 10, where 1 is very low and 10 is very high.
### Page 1: Monitoring Form
* **Focus** (`focus`, text, optional): This field is a free text area where you can describe how focused you are currently.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your healthcare provider.
* This form is for personal use only, and should not be shared with others without permission.
