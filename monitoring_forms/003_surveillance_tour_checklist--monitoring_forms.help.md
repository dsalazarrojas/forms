# Surveillance Tour Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a quick and easy checklist for surveillance teams to document their observations and incidents during their tours.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the "start of shift" page and enter a brief description of the start of your shift. Then, move to the "patrol details" page and enter a description of your patrol route or any notable events during your tour. Next, move to the "incidents" page and document any incidents you encountered. Finally, move to the "checkpoints" page and note any checkpoints you visited during your tour. Each page has space for a text description, so simply type in as much or as little information as you deem necessary for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Start Of Shift
* **Start Of Shift** (`start_page`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the start of your shift, including any notable events or instructions from your supervisor.

### Patrol Details
* **Patrol Details** (`patrol_details`, text, required: false): Describe your patrol route or any notable events that occurred during your tour. Be as detailed as possible, but keep it concise.

### Incidents
* **Incidents** (`incidents`, text, required: false): Document any incidents you encountered during your tour, including their location, description, and any actions taken.

### Checkpoints
* **Checkpoints** (`checkpoints`, text, required: false): Note any checkpoints you visited during your tour, including their location and any notable events or observations.
