# Internship Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to monitor and track intern progress, collect feedback, and record performance metrics for internships. It is a tool for supervisors, managers, or HR representatives to evaluate and improve the internship experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each progress check question in the text fields provided.
2. Provide feedback on your experience in the feedback fields.
3. Select relevant performance metrics that apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Progress Check 1**: Briefly describe your progress on the internship's initial objectives.
* **Progress Check 2**: Report your progress on the internship's intermediate goals.
* **Progress Check 3**: Update your progress on the internship's advanced objectives.
* **Progress Check 4**: Describe any challenges or obstacles you faced and how you overcame them.
* **Progress Check 5**: Summarize your progress on the internship's final objectives.
* **Feedback 1**: Share your thoughts on any positive experiences during the internship.
* **Feedback 2**: Share your thoughts on any areas that need improvement.
* **Feedback 3**: Suggest any changes or suggestions for future internships.
* **Performance Metrics**: Select the performance metrics that best describe your experience (Performance Metrics 1, Performance Metrics 2).
