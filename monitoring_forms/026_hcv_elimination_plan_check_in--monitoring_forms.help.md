# HCV Elimination Plan Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HCV Elimination Plan Check In form is used to collect information on the progress of the HCV elimination plan.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your progress in the **Progress** field.
2. Enter the date of your last progress reporting in the **Date Last Progress Reporting** field.
3. Enter the next date you plan to report progress in the **Next Reporting Date** field.
4. Enter the location where you last reported progress in the **Last Progress Reporting Location** field.
5. Check the box next to **Monitoring Team Name** if your team is responsible for monitoring this project.
6. Check the box next to **Assigned Team Members** to indicate if team members have been assigned to the task.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes about the progress in the **Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Progress** (`hcv_progress`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the progress made towards eliminating hazardous chemicals.
* **Date Last Progress Reporting** (`date_last_progress_reporting`, date, optional): Enter the date you last reported progress on the HCV elimination plan.
* **Next Reporting Date** (`next_reporting_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to report progress next.
* **Next Progress Reporting** (`next_progress_reporting`, text, optional): Enter any specific details about the next progress reporting activity.
* **Last Progress Reporting Location** (`last_progress_reporting_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you last reported progress.
* **Monitoring Team Name** (`monitoring_team_name`, text, optional): Check this box if your team is responsible for monitoring this project.
* **Assigned Team Members** (`assigned_team_members`, select_multiple, optional): Check the box next to each team member assigned to the task.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the progress made.
