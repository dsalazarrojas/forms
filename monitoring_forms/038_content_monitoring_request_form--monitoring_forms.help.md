# Content Monitoring Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for monitoring specific content on various platforms. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that your request is processed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your contact information: This includes your full name, email address, and phone number (optional). Please ensure that the email address is correct, as it will be used for communication and report delivery.
2. Select the content type and scope: Choose the type of content to be monitored (e.g., social media posts, blog articles, etc.) and the scope of the monitoring (e.g., single platform, multiple platforms, etc.).
3. Specify the platforms to monitor: Select the specific platforms where you want to monitor the content (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, etc.).
4. Enter specific URLs (if applicable): If there are specific URLs you want to monitor, enter them in the corresponding field.
5. Define keywords or phrases: Enter the keywords or phrases you want to track.
6. Determine alert settings: Choose the alert threshold and frequency of report generation.
7. Specify report recipients: Enter the email addresses of those who should receive monitoring reports.
8. Set up competitor monitoring (if applicable): Check if you want to monitor competitors and list their names.
9. Define specific concerns: If there are specific issues you want to watch for, enter them in the corresponding field.
10. Enter additional requirements: If there are any other monitoring requirements, enter them in the corresponding field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name**: <i class="fa fa-user"></i> Your full name, used for communication and reporting. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Requester Email**: <i class="fa fa-envelope"></i> Your email address, used for communication and reporting. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Requester Phone**: <i class="fa fa-phone"></i> Your contact number, optional but recommended. (<em>Optional</em>)
* **Requester Department**: <i class="fa fa-building"></i> Your department, selected from the provided options. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Request Date**: <i class="fa fa-calendar"></i> The date when this monitoring request is being made. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Content Type to Monitor**: <i class="fa fa-file-text"></i> The type of content to be monitored (e.g., social media posts, blog articles, etc.). (<em>Required</em>)
* **Monitoring Scope**: <i class="fa fa-lock"></i> The scope of the monitoring (e.g., single platform, multiple platforms, etc.). (<em>Required</em>)
* **Platforms to Monitor**: <i class="fa fa-globe"></i> The specific platforms to monitor (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, etc.). (<em>Required</em>)
* **Content URLs**: <i class="fa fa-link"></i> Specific URLs to monitor, if applicable. (<em>Optional</em>)
* **Keywords to Track**: <i class="fa fa-tag"></i> The keywords or phrases to track. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Brand Mentions**: <i class="fa fa-shares"></i> Whether to track brand mentions, and if so, whether to track all mentions, negative mentions only, or positive mentions only. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Competitor Monitoring**: <i class="fa fa-users"></i> Whether to monitor competitors, and if so, who they are. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Competitor Names**: <i class="fa fa-user"></i> List the names of competitors to monitor. (<em>Optional</em>)
* **Monitoring Purpose**: <i class="fa fa-question-circle"></i> The reason for this monitoring request. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Urgency Level**: <i class="fa fa-exclamation-triangle"></i> The level of urgency for this request (e.g., low, medium, high, critical). (<em>Required</em>)
* **Monitoring Start Date**: <i class="fa fa-calendar-check-o"></i> The date when monitoring should start. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Monitoring End Date**: <i class="fa fa-calendar-times-o"></i> The date when monitoring should end (if applicable). (<em>Optional</em>)
* **Monitoring Frequency**: <i class="fa fa-clock"></i> The frequency of report generation (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.). (<em>Required</em>)
* **Report Recipients**: <i class="fa fa-envelope"></i> The email addresses of those who should receive monitoring reports. (<em>Required</em>)
* **Alert Threshold**: <i class="fa fa-bell"></i> The threshold for sending immediate alerts (e.g., any negative mention, multiple negative mentions, etc.). (<em>Required</em>)
* **Specific Concerns**: <i class="fa fa-exclamation-circle"></i> Specific issues to watch for. (<em>Optional</em>)
* **Additional Requirements**: <i class="fa fa-plus-circle"></i> Any other monitoring requirements. (<em>Optional</em>)

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to avoid delays in processing your request.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation above or contact the support team for clarification.
* This form is designed to help us process your request efficiently. Please be as detailed as possible in your answers to help us understand your needs accurately.
* If you have any specific requirements or concerns, please enter them in the "Additional Requirements" field.
* Please note that some fields are required, and failure to complete them may delay the processing of your request.
