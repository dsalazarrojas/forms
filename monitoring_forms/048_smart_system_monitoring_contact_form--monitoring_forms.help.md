# Smart System Monitoring Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart System Monitoring Contact Form is designed for users to provide feedback, comments, or concerns about the Smart System Monitoring system. This form will help us gather your input to improve the system's performance and features.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Contact" page with a detailed description of your issue or concern.
2. On the "Attachments" page, select any relevant attachments that support your submission, such as screenshots or error logs.
3. On the "Description" page, provide a brief explanation of your attachment.
4. On the "Attachment" page, upload any additional documents or files that support your submission.
5. Click "Submit" to send your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact (page2)**:
	+ This is the main description field where you can provide detailed information about your issue or concern.
* **Attachments (page3)**:
	+ Select any relevant attachments that support your submission, such as screenshots or error logs.
* **Description (page4)**:
	+ Provide a brief explanation of the attachment you uploaded.
* **Attachment (page5)**:
	+ Upload any additional documents or files that support your submission.
* **Page6**, **Page7**, **Page8**, **Page9**, **Page10**:
	+ These fields are reserved for future use and should be left blank. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields, which are indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field labels.
* Do not submit duplicate forms, as this may cause confusion or redundancy in the system's analytics.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us at <form@gic.mx>.
