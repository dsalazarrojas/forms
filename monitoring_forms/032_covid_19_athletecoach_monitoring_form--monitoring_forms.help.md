# COVID 19 AthleteCoach Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help track and monitor the health and safety of athletes and coaches during the COVID-19 pandemic. It collects information on personal details, health status, and potential risks related to COVID-19. This data helps in ensuring a safe and healthy environment for all individuals involved in sports activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
2. Select your role as an athlete, coach, or support staff.
3. Enter your sport or activity.
4. Provide your team or club name (if applicable).
5. Enter your email address for communication purposes.
6. Enter your phone number for best contact.
7. Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
8. Enter the date of today for monitoring purposes.
9. Provide your current body temperature in Fahrenheit.
10. Select how you're feeling today.
11. Choose any COVID-19 symptoms you may be experiencing.
12. Select whether you have known COVID-19 exposure in the past 14 days.
13. If you've had a recent COVID-19 test, select the result and enter the date of the test.
14. Indicate your vaccination status.
15. Choose whether you're cleared for activity.
16. If you have any activity restrictions, provide details.
17. Select whether you'll be participating in contact sports.
18. Indicate whether a mask is required during activity.
19. Select whether you're traveling for competition.
20. If any household members are symptomatic, indicate this.
21. Select whether you work outside home and indicate the risk level.
22. Provide the name of your health insurance provider (if applicable).
23. Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
24. (Optional) List any relevant medical conditions that may affect your participation.
25. (Optional) List any current medications.
26. (Optional) Add any notes from your trainer or coach.
27. (Optional) Enter the name of the healthcare provider who cleared you for activity.
28. Sign the form with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification. 
* **Role**: Select your role as athlete, coach, or support staff.
*   **Sport**: Enter your sport or activity.
* **Team Name**: Enter your team or club name if you're part of one.
* **Email Address**: Provide your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for best contact.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Monitoring Date**: Enter the date of today.
* **Current Temperature**: Enter your current body temperature in Fahrenheit.
* **Feeling Well**: Select how you're feeling today.
* **COVID-19 Symptoms**: Choose any symptoms you may be experiencing.
* **Known Exposure**: Select whether you have known COVID-19 exposure in the past 14 days.
* **Recent Test**: If you've had a recent COVID-19 test, select the result and enter the date.
* **Vaccination Status**: Indicate your COVID-19 vaccination status.
* **Cleared for Activity**: Choose whether you're cleared for activity.
* **Activity Restrictions**: If you have any activity restrictions, provide details.
* **Contact Sports**: Select whether you'll be participating in contact sports.
* **Mask Required**: Indicate whether a mask is required during activity.
* **Travel for Competition**: Select whether you're traveling for competition.
* **Household Members Symptomatic**: Select whether any household members are symptomatic.
* **Work Outside Home**: Select whether you work outside home and indicate the risk level.
* **Health Insurance Provider**: (Optional) Provide the name of your health insurance provider.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions**: (Optional) List any relevant medical conditions that may affect your participation.
* **Medications**: (Optional) List any current medications.
* **Trainer or Coach Notes**: (Optional) Add any notes from your trainer or coach.
* **Clearing Provider**: (Optional) Enter the name of the healthcare provider who cleared you for activity.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign the form with your full name.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: (Optional) If you're under 18, have your parent or guardian sign.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed the form.
