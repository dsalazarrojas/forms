# healthcare_professional_effectiveness_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to monitor and evaluate the effectiveness of a medical professional in their work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Medical Professional name in the field labeled "Medical Professional".
2. Select the relevant performance indicators that are being monitored in the field labeled "Performance Indicators".
3. Choose the frequency at which the medical professional's performance is being monitored in the field labeled "Monitoring Frequency".
4. Select the frequency at which feedback is given to the medical professional in the field labeled "Feedback Frequency".
5. Describe any performance improvement actions taken or planned in the field labeled "Performance Improvement Actions".
6. Describe the action plan for improvement in the field labeled "Action Plan".
7. Indicate whether the action plan has been reviewed in the field labeled "Action Plan Reviewed".
8. Enter the next planned review date for the action plan in the field labeled "Next Action Plan Review".
9. Select the frequency at which feedback is given to the medical professional in the field labeled "Feedback Frequency 2" (this field is an alternate option for feedback frequency).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Professional**: Enter the name of the medical professional being monitored.
* **Monitoring Form**: This field is not intended for user input.
* **Performance Indicators**: Select up to three performance indicators being monitored for the medical professional. Choose from the provided options: indicator1, indicator2, or indicator3.
* **Feedback**: Provide any additional information or comments about the medical professional's performance.
* **Monitoring Frequency**: Select the frequency at which the medical professional's performance is being monitored. Choose from: daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Performance Improvement Actions**: Describe any actions taken or planned to improve the medical professional's performance.
* **Action Plan**: Describe the specific actions to be taken to improve performance.
* **Action Plan Reviewed**: Indicate if the action plan has been reviewed. Choose from: reviewed or not reviewed.
* **Next Action Plan Review**: Enter the date for the next planned review of the action plan.
* **Feedback Frequency**: Select the frequency at which feedback is given to the medical professional. Choose from: daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Feedback Frequency 2**: This is an alternative option for feedback frequency, identical to Feedback Frequency.
* **Performance Improvement Areas**: Provide any additional information or comments about areas for improvement for the medical professional.
