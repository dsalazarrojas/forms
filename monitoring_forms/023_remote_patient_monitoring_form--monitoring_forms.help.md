# Remote Patient Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Patient Monitoring Form is used to collect information about a patient's health status, monitor type, and monitoring schedule. It is essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure the best care and treatment for the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
2. **Address**: Provide the patient's current address.
3. **Monitor Type**: Choose the type of monitoring that the patient requires (e.g., ECG, Blood Pressure, Blood Oxygen).
4. **Patient Contact**: Enter the patient's contact information for follow-up purposes.
5. **Practitioner Name**: Enter your name as the healthcare practitioner.
6. **Practitioner Contact**: Enter your contact information for further communication.
7. **Monitor Frequency**: Specify the frequency of monitoring (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
8. **Monitor Start**: Set the date and time when the monitoring should begin.
9. **Monitor Stop**: Set the date and time when the monitoring should stop.
10. **Monitor Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the patient's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (Text, Required)**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Address (Text, Optional)**: Provide the patient's current address. This field is optional, but it helps the healthcare team to contact the patient if needed.
* **Monitor Type (Select One, Required)**: Choose the type of monitoring that the patient requires from the available options (ECG, Blood Pressure, Blood Oxygen).
* **Patient Contact (Text, Required)**: Enter the patient's contact information for follow-up purposes. This includes their phone number, email, or any other contact method.
* **Practitioner Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your name as the healthcare practitioner.
* **Practitioner Contact (Text, Required)**: Enter your contact information for further communication with the patient or other healthcare team members.
* **Monitor Frequency (Time, Required)**: Specify the frequency of monitoring using the time format (e.g., 08:00, 12:00).
* **Monitor Start (Date, Required)**: Set the date and time when the monitoring should begin.
* **Monitor Stop (Date, Required)**: Set the date and time when the monitoring should stop.
* **Monitor Notes (Text, Optional)**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the patient's condition. This field is optional, but it helps to provide a more comprehensive understanding of the patient's health status.
