# Daycare Video Monitoring Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This is a Daycare Video Monitoring Consent Form, designed for parents or guardians to provide their consent and information for their child's video monitoring while in a daycare setting. The form aims to ensure that you, as a parent or guardian, are aware of and agree to the video monitoring process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read this guide carefully before starting the form.
2. Ensure you understand the purpose and requirements of the form.
3. Select the correct answer for each field, following the explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Permission** (`Daycare Video Monitoring Consent Form`, Select Multiple, **Yes/No**): Choose whether you give permission for your child to be monitored or not.
* **Parent Information** (`Daycare Video Monitoring Consent Form Parent Info`, Text, **Optional**): Provide your contact information, such as your name, address, and phone number, so we can reach you if needed.
* **Video Monitoring** (`Daycare Video Monitoring Consent Form Video Monitoring`, Select One, **Optional**): Choose how you would like to receive video monitoring:
	+ Option 1: Always
	+ Option 2: Only during designated hours
	+ Option 3: Never
* **Consent** (`Daycare Video Monitoring Consent Form`, Note, **Note**: This section is for you to provide a personal note about your consent.)
* **Date and Time** (`Daycare Video Monitoring Consent Form Date and Time`, Date, **Optional**): Select the date and time when you would like the monitoring to start or end.
* **Uploads** (`Daycare Video Monitoring Consent Form Uploads`, Number, **Optional**): Choose how many times you would like to receive uploads per day.
* **Signature** (`Daycare Video Monitoring Consent Form Signature`, Text, **Optional**): Sign the form to confirm your consent.
* **Agreement** (`Daycare Video Monitoring Consent Form Agreement`, Select One, **Optional**): Choose which option best represents your understanding:
	+ Option 1: I have read and understand the daycare's monitoring policy.
	+ Option 2: I have not read and do not understand the daycare's monitoring policy.
	+ Option 3: I would like more information about the policy.

Please note that all fields are optional, but please make sure to fill in all the required fields (marked as **required**) to ensure your consent is valid.
