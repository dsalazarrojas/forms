# Long Term Health Monitoring Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture participant consent for long term health monitoring. 
It is used in a clinical setting where participants and researchers need to agree 
on monitoring parameters.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, first read the instructions and each field carefully. 
Please answer all required fields accurately and honestly. If you are unsure 
about a field, please ask the researcher or healthcare provider for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information (<id: 1>)
Participant information includes your basic details. Please fill out this form 
with your name, date of birth, and contact information.

### Monitoring Type (<id: 2>)
Please select the monitoring type that best describes your health status. This 
can be ongoing, occasional, or as scheduled.

### Monitoring Frequency (<id: 3>)
Please select how often your health will be monitored. This can be once, 
daily, weekly, or monthly.

### Health Conditions (<id: 4>)
Please list all your health conditions that require monitoring.

### Medical History (<id: 5>)
Please provide a brief medical history, including any allergies, medications, or 
previous medical conditions that may impact your health monitoring.

### Consent Date (<id: 6>)
Please enter the date you are giving consent for the monitoring.

### Start Date (<id: 7>)
Please enter the date you would like the monitoring to start.

### End Date (<id: 8>)
Please enter the date you would like the monitoring to end.

### Monitoring Location (<id: 9>)
Please enter the location where you will be monitored.

### Emergency Contact (<id: 10>)
Please enter the name and contact information for your emergency contact.

### Emergency Contact Phone (<id: 11>)
Please enter the phone number for your emergency contact.

### Emergency Contact Email (<id: 12>)
Please enter the email address for your emergency contact.

### Consent Notes (<id: 13>)
Please include any additional consent notes you would like to add.

### Participant Notes (<id: 14>)
Please include any additional participant notes you would like to add.

### Researcher Notes (<id: 15>)
Please include any additional researcher notes you would like to add.

### Researcher Consent (<id: 16>)
Please indicate if the researcher has given consent for the monitoring.

### Participant Consent (<id: 17>)
Please indicate if you, the participant, have given consent for the 
monitoring.

### Participant Signature (<id: 18>)
Please sign this form to confirm your consent.

### Researcher Signature (<id: 19>)
Please sign this form to confirm your consent as the researcher.

### Reviewed By (<id: 20>)
Please enter the name of the person who reviewed this form.

### Review Date (<id: 21>)
Please enter the date this form was reviewed.

### Reviewer (<id: 22>)
Please enter the name of the reviewer.

### Review Status (<id: 23>)
Please enter the review status of this form.

### Review Comments (<id: 24>)
Please enter any review comments.

### Assigned Tool (<id: 25>)
Please enter the tool assigned for the monitoring.

Note: This help guide is a basic template and may need to be reviewed and 
updated according to your organization's specific policies and procedures.
