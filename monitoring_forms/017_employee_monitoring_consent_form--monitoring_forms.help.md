# employee_monitoring_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather consent from employees to participate in the monitoring activities. It's a crucial step in maintaining transparency and trust within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Your Consent** (`employee_monitoring_consent`, text, required): Please type your consent statement in the text area.
2. **Select Activities to Monitor** (`monitoring_activities`, select_one, required): Choose from the list of available monitoring activities.
3. **Frequency of Monitoring** (`monitoring_frequency`, number, required): Enter a number that represents how often you would like to be monitored (e.g., 1 for once a week, 2 for twice a week, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Your Consent**: This field is where you will type your consent statement to participate in the monitoring activities. Please provide a clear and concise statement of your consent.
* **Select Activities to Monitor**: This field allows you to choose which monitoring activities you are willing to participate in. Select only the activities that apply to you.
* **Frequency of Monitoring**: This field is where you will indicate how often you would like to be monitored. Please enter a number that reflects your preference.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise consent statement in the "Enter Your Consent" field.
* Select only the monitoring activities that you are willing to participate in.
* Be accurate when entering the frequency of monitoring to ensure that you are monitored as per your preference.
