# Indoor Air Quality Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Indoor Air Quality Program Registration Form is a tool used to gather information from facilities about their air quality status and needs. By completing this form, facilities can provide valuable data to help monitor and improve their indoor air quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
2. Enter your facility's details, contact information, and service needs.
3. Select your facility type from the options provided.
4. Provide your facility's address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Details** (text, required: false): This field is used to enter your facility's name and description. Please provide a brief overview of your facility, including its purpose and any relevant details.
* **Contact Details** (text, required: false): This field is used to enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number. Please ensure that the contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Service Needs** (text, required: false): This field is used to specify your facility's service needs related to indoor air quality. Please provide a detailed description of the services you require, such as maintenance, monitoring, or education.
* **Facility Type** (text, required: false): This field is used to select your facility type from the provided options. Please choose the option that best describes your facility's characteristics.
* **Facility Address** (text, required: false): This field is used to enter your facility's address. Please provide a complete and accurate address, including the street address, city, state, and zip code.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your facility's management or maintenance team for assistance.
* This form is meant to be completed by facility administrators, owners, or designated representatives.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
