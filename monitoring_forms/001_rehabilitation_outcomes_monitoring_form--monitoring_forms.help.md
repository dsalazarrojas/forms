# rehabilitation_outcomes_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rehabilitation_outcomes_monitoring_form is used for monitoring patient progress and outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
* Enter the patient's medical record number in the "Medical Record Number" field.
* Enter the patient's ID number in the "Patient ID" field.
* Enter the patient's PROPHET score in the "PROPHET Score" field.
* Enter the date of admission in the "Date of Admission" field.
* Enter the date of discharge in the "Date of Discharge" field.
* Enter the patient's outcomes in the "Patient Outcomes" field.
* Enter the follow-up date in the "Follow-up Date" field.
* Enter the follow-up frequency in the "Follow-up Frequency" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, number, required): Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, text, required): Enter the patient's ID number.
* **PROPHET Score** (`prophets_score`, number, required): Enter the patient's PROPHET score.
* **Date of Admission** (`date_of_admission`, date, required): Enter the date the patient was admitted.
* **Date of Discharge** (`date_of_discharge`, date, required): Enter the date the patient was discharged.
* **Patient Outcomes** (`patient_outcomes`, text, required): Enter a brief summary of the patient's outcomes.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the follow-up visit.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, number, required): Enter the frequency of follow-up visits.
