# Smart Waste Bin Monitoring Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Waste Bin Monitoring Consent Form is used to collect demographic information, contact details, and consent status from participants for waste bin monitoring activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the Participant Details section with your name, address, and any other details as required.
2.  Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email.
3.  Provide your date and time of birth.
4.  Check one of the options in the Participant Consent 1 section to indicate your consent status.
5.  Check one or more of the options in the Participant Consent 2 section to specify your consent status.
6.  Enter any notes or comments about your participation in the Participant Notes section.
7.  Enter the contact information of the person who can be contacted, including their phone number and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: This section is for providing your demographic information. Please fill in your name, address, and any other details as required.
* **Participant Address**: Enter your address in this field.
* **Participant Consent**: This section is for indicating your consent status for waste bin monitoring. Please select one of the options to indicate your consent status.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number in this field.
* **E-mail**: Enter your email address in this field.
* **Participant Consent 1**: Check one of the options in this section to specify your consent status.
* **Participant Consent 2**: Check one or more of the options in this section to specify your consent status.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Birth**: Enter your time of birth in the format HH:MM.
* **Participant Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about your participation in this section.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person who can be contacted in this field.
* **Contact E-mail**: Enter the email address of the person who can be contacted in this field.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who can be contacted in this field.
* **Contact Address**: Enter the address of the person who can be contacted in this field.
