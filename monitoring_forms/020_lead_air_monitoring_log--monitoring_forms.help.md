# Lead Air Monitoring Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lead Air Monitoring Log is a form used for tracking and documenting lead levels in the air. It helps maintain accurate and consistent data, providing a clear picture of the air quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Lead Air Monitoring Log form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Form Data**, which serves as a brief description of the monitoring event.
2. Enter the **Title** of the form, which should reflect the specific job site where the monitoring took place.
3. Choose the **Sampling Date** for the event.
4. Enter the **Sampling Time** for the event.
5. Provide the **Sampling Location** where the monitoring occurred.
6. Enter the **Lead Level Result** value.
7. Specify the units of measurement for the **Lead Level Result** in the **Lead Level Result Units** field.
8. Enter the actual **Lead Level Result Value**.
9. Finally, enter the name of the **Sampler Name**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Air Monitoring Log Form Data**:
  - Label: Lead Air Monitoring Log Form Data
  - Type: Text
  - Description: A brief description of the monitoring event, providing context for the data.
* **Title**:
  - Label: Title
  - Type: Text
  - Description: Enter the title of the form as used on the job site for reference.
* **Sampling Date**:
  - Label: Sampling Date
  - Type: Date
  - Description: Select the date when the sampling took place.
* **Sampling Time**:
  - Label: Sampling Time
  - Type: Time
  - Description: Enter the time of the sampling event.
* **Sampling Location**:
  - Label: Sampling Location
  - Type: Text
  - Description: Enter the location where the sampling occurred.
* **Lead Level Result**:
  - Label: Lead Level Result
  - Type: Number
  - Description: Enter the numerical value of the lead level result.
* **Lead Level Result Units**:
  - Label: Lead Level Result Units
  - Type: Text
  - Description: Specify the units of measurement for the lead level result.
* **Lead Level Result Value**:
  - Label: Lead Level Result Value
  - Type: Number
  - Description: Enter the actual numerical value of the lead level result.
* **Sampler Name**:
  - Label: Sampler Name
  - Type: Text
  - Description: Enter the name of the person performing the sampling.

## Tips
- Ensure that each entry is unique and not a duplicate.
- If unsure about any field, refer to the form's purpose and context.
- Always enter accurate and clear information for better data reliability and future reference.
