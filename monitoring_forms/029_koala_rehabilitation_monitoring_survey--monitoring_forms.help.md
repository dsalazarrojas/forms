# Koala Rehabilitation Monitoring Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Koala Rehabilitation Monitoring Survey is designed to track the progress of koalas in a rehabilitation program. This form collects information on the health status, behavior, and treatments of the koalas, as well as the start and end dates of their rehabilitation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
2. Select the relevant options for the health status and treatments fields.
3. Enter dates and notes as required.
4. Review and submit the form to save your progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Koala Observation** (`koala_observation`, text, optional):
    The observation field is used to record any notable observations made during the survey.
* **Koala Health Status** (`koala_health_status`, select_one, required):
    Select the current health status of the koala. Options are: Healthy, Sick, Dead.
* **Koala Behavior** (`koala_behavior`, text, optional):
    The behavior field is used to record any notable behaviors observed during the survey.
* **Koala Treatments** (`koala_treatments`, select_multiple, required):
    Select all treatments the koala is currently receiving. Options are: Medication, Rest, Diet.
* **Koala Notes** (`koala_notes`, text, optional):
    The notes field is used to record any additional information about the koala.
* **Rehabilitation Start Date** (`rehabilitation_start_date`, date, optional):
    Enter the date when the koala's rehabilitation began.
* **Rehabilitation End Date** (`rehabilitation_end_date`, date, optional):
    Enter the date when the koala's rehabilitation ended.
* **Rehabilitation Days** (`rehabilitation_days`, number, optional):
    Enter the number of days the koala spent in rehabilitation.
* **Rehabilitation Notes** (`rehabilitation_notes`, text, optional):
    The notes field is used to record any additional information about the koala's rehabilitation.
* **Observation Notes** (`observation_notes`, text, optional):
    The observation notes field is used to record any additional information about the observation.
