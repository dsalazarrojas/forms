# team_leader_feedback_form_generic_monitoring - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow team leaders to provide feedback on their team members' performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the "Team Leader - Manager" or "Team Leader - Team Member" option under the "Feedback" field to indicate the type of feedback you are providing.
2.  Fill in the employee ID, name, and other relevant details for the team member you are providing feedback on.
3.  Provide feedback on the team member's performance, including any comments or suggestions you may have.
4.  Select the department and team the team member belongs to.
5.  Optionally, provide additional information such as the team member's email, phone number, and team lead's details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Info**: This section is for capturing basic information about the team member and the feedback being provided.
    *   **Employee ID**: Enter the team member's ID for easy reference and tracking.
    *   **Employee Name**: Enter the team member's name for clarity and identification.
*   **Team Member Details**:
    *   **Team Leader ID**: Enter the team lead's ID for reference and tracking.
    *   **Team Member Name**: Enter the team member's name for clarity and identification.
    *   **Email**: Enter the team member's email for communication and reference.
    *   **Phone**: Enter the team member's phone number for communication and reference.
*   **Feedback**: Select the type of feedback you are providing: Team Leader - Manager or Team Leader - Team Member.
*   **Score**: Enter a score from 1-5 to indicate the team member's performance.
*   **Date**: Enter the date of the feedback being provided.
*   **Time**: Enter the time of the feedback being provided.
*   **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
*   **Email Confirmation**: Enter the team lead's email for confirmation and reference.
*   **Team Lead ID Confirmation**: Enter the team lead's ID for reference and tracking.
*   **Team Member ID**: Enter the team member's ID for easy reference and tracking.
*   **Team Member Name**: Enter the team member's name for clarity and identification.
*   **Manager ID**: Enter the manager's ID for reference and tracking.
*   **Manager Name**: Enter the manager's name for clarity and identification.
*   **Form Type**: Select the type of form you are filling out.
*   **Department**: Enter the department the team member belongs to.
*   **Team**: Enter the team the team member belongs to.
*   **Employee Count**: Enter the number of employees in the team.
*   **Team Lead ID**: Enter the team lead's ID for reference and tracking.
*   **Team Lead Name**: Enter the team lead's name for clarity and identification.
*   **Team Lead Email**: Enter the team lead's email for communication and reference.
*   **Team Lead Phone**: Enter the team lead's phone number for communication and reference.

## Tips

*   Be concise and clear in your feedback to help the team member understand and improve.
*   Focus on specific behaviors or actions that can be improved, rather than general traits or qualities.
*   Use this form as a tool for growth and development, not as a means to criticize or punish.
*   Ensure that you are providing feedback on performance, not personal opinions or biases.
