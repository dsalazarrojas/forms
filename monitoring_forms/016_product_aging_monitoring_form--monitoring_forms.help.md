# Product Aging Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on product aging monitoring, specifically for products nearing or beyond their expected shelf life. It is intended for use in industries such as manufacturing, inventory management, or logistics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide information about the merchandiser, including their details. This is an optional field, but it helps identify the person responsible for the product.
2. Add product information, including any relevant details about the product.
3. Enter any remarks or comments about the product.
4. If applicable, add a signature from the submitter to confirm they have completed and reviewed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Merchandiser Details** (merchandiser_details): This field collects information about the person or entity responsible for the product. This is optional.
* **Product Info** (product_info): Enter any details about the product, such as its name, type, or quantity. This field helps identify the specific product being monitored.
* **Remarks** (remarks): Add any additional comments or remarks about the product. This field is intended for any extra information about the product's condition or status.
* **Submitter's Signature** (submitter_signature): If the submitter needs to verify that they have completed and reviewed the form, they can add their signature here. This field is optional but recommended for official purposes.
