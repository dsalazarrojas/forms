# Access Control Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document access control events and observations for security tracking and compliance. It is an essential tool for security officers to monitor and report incidents, equipment malfunctions, and other issues that affect access control systems during their shifts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields as follows:
   - **Security Officer/Monitor Name**: Enter your name as the security officer or monitor who is submitting this report.
   - **Badge Number/ID**: Enter your badge number or identification number as assigned by your organization.
   - **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for follow-up questions or actions.
   - **Date of Monitoring**: Select the date when the access control monitoring occurred.
   - **Shift Start Time**: Select the start time of your shift when you began monitoring the access control systems.
   - **Shift End Time**: Select the end time of your shift when you completed monitoring the access control systems.
   - **Location or Zone Monitored**: Describe the specific location or zone that was being monitored for access control.
   - **Access Points Checked**: Select all the access points you checked during your shift (e.g., Main Entrance, Emergency Exits, etc.).
   - **Overall System Status**: Report the overall status of access control systems during your shift (e.g., Fully Operational, Partially Operational, etc.).
   - **Unauthorized Access Attempts**: Indicate if there were any unauthorized access attempts during your shift, and if so, describe the attempts.
   - **Number of Security Incidents**: Report the total number of security incidents that occurred during your shift.
   - **Incident Summary**: Briefly describe any security incidents that occurred during your shift.
   - **Total Visitors Processed**: Count the total number of visitors you processed during your shift.
   - **Equipment Malfunctions**: Indicate if there were any equipment malfunctions (e.g., card readers, cameras, etc.).
   - **Follow-up Required**: Indicate if any follow-up actions are required after your shift.
   - **Follow-up Details**: Describe the follow-up actions required.
   - **General Observations**: Provide any additional general observations or notes about your shift.
