# daily_health_monitoring_sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The daily health monitoring sheet is used to track the progress of employees and their health conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Name.
2. Report your health conditions in the health_conditions field.
3. Provide a Progress Report in the progress_report field.
4. Enter your Employee ID.
5. Select the Date Monitored from the calendar.
6. Enter the Time Monitored.
7. Choose your Monitoring Frequency from the options provided.
8. (Optional) Add any additional comments in the comments field.
9. Enter your Email Notification address.
10. (Optional) Add your Phone Number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID card.
  * Type: text
  * Required: yes
  * Hint: Please enter your name accurately.

- **Health Conditions**: Report any health conditions you are experiencing.
  * Type: text
  * Required: yes
  * Hint: Be as detailed as possible.

- **Progress Report**: Provide a report of your progress, including any changes or updates.
  * Type: text
  * Required: yes
  * Hint: Be concise but thorough.

- **Employee ID**: Enter your ID number as found on your ID card.
  * Type: number
  * Required: yes
  * Hint: Enter your ID accurately.

- **Date Monitored**: Select the date you are being monitored from the calendar.
  * Type: date
  * Required: yes
  * Hint: Select the correct date.

- **Time Monitored**: Enter the time you were monitored.
  * Type: time
  * Required: yes
  * Hint: Enter the correct time.

- **Monitoring Frequency**: Choose the frequency of monitoring that suits your needs.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: yes
  * Hint: Select Daily, Weekly, or Bi-Weekly.

- **Comments**: Add any additional comments or information you want to share.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
  * Hint: This is optional and can be used for further clarification.

- **Email Notification**: Enter your email address for notification purposes.
  * Type: email
  * Required: yes
  * Hint: Ensure your email is up-to-date and accurate.

- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
  * Hint: This is optional and can be used for additional contact.
