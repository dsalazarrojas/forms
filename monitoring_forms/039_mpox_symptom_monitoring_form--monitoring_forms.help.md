# Mpox Symptom Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and track symptoms and experiences of patients with Mpox, allowing healthcare professionals to make informed decisions about treatment and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the page that corresponds to the section of the form you are filling out (e.g., Symptoms, Skin Issues, etc.)
2. Read each field's label and explanation below to understand what information is being collected
3. Enter or select the relevant information for each field accurately and completely

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Symptoms (`mpox_symptom_monitoring_form_page_1`)

* **Symptoms** (`mpox_symptom_monitoring_form_page_1`, text, required): Enter any symptoms you are experiencing, including any new or ongoing symptoms you have noticed recently

### 2. Skin Issues (`mpox_rash_symptoms`)

* **Skin issues** (`mpox_rash_symptoms`, select_multiple, required): Select all the skin issues that you are experiencing, including:
	+ Redness
	+ Swelling
	+ Pain
	+ Blister
	+ Lesion
	+ Rash
	+ Lesions
	+ Pustules
	+ Blisters

### 3. Rash Characteristics (`mpox_rash_characteristics`)

* **Rash characteristics** (`mpox_rash_characteristics`, text, required): Describe any notable characteristics of the rash or skin issues you are experiencing, such as color, size, shape, etc.

### 4. Fever (`mpox_fever`)

* **Fever** (`mpox_fever`, number, required): If you have a fever, report the number of degrees (e.g., 102.4 degrees Fahrenheit)

### 5. Headache (`mpox_headache`)

* **Headache** (`mpox_headache`, text, optional): If you are experiencing a headache, describe it

### 6. Muscle Aches (`mpox_muscle_aches`)

* **Muscle aches** (`mpox_muscle_aches`, text, optional): If you are experiencing muscle aches, describe them

### 7. Swollen Lymph Nodes (`mpox_swollen_lymph_nodes`)

* **Swollen lymph nodes** (`mpox_swollen_lymph_nodes`, text, optional): If you are experiencing swollen lymph nodes, describe them

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing your symptoms and experiences
* Enter accurate and complete information to help healthcare professionals make informed decisions about your care
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact your healthcare provider for guidance
