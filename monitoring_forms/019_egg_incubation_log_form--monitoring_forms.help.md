# Egg Incubation Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record data related to egg incubation, allowing users to track batches of eggs and their hatching status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the status of the batch of eggs:
    *   Choose "Yes" if the batch is still incubating
    *   Choose "No" if the batch has completed incubation or hatched
2.  Enter the date when the incubation process started:
    *   This date should be in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD)
3.  Enter the date when the batch is expected to hatch or has completed incubation:
    *   This date should be in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD)
4.  Record the number of eggs in the batch:
    *   Enter the total number of eggs in the batch
5.  Record the number of eggs that hatched:
    *   Enter the number of eggs that successfully hatched
6.  Record the number of eggs that died:
    *   Enter the number of eggs that died during incubation
7.  Record the temperature during incubation:
    *   Enter the temperature in degrees Celsius
8.  Record any additional notes:
    *   Enter any relevant comments or observations
