# Temperature Monitoring Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Temperature Monitoring Certification Registration Form is a user-facing form designed to collect demographic information from participants in the temperature monitoring certification program. This form is used to register participants' details, ensuring accurate and up-to-date data for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please use a valid email format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
6. Enter the time of birth in the "Time of Birth" field in the format `HH:MM AM/PM` or `HH:MM 24hr`.
7. Select your participant type from the options provided in the "Participant Type" field.
8. If applicable, enter the ID of the course you're participating in in the "Course ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
### Last Name
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
### Email
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive important updates and communications from the program.
### Phone
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy` (e.g., 01/01/1990).
### Time of Birth
* **Time of Birth**: Enter your time of birth in the format `HH:MM AM/PM` or `HH:MM 24hr` (e.g., 08:00 AM).
### Participant Type
* **Participant Type**: Select the type that best describes you:
	+ **Individual**: This option is for personal registrations.
	+ **Organization**: This option is for representatives of organizations or groups.
	+ **Other**: This option is for any other type not listed above.
### Course ID
* **Course ID**: If you're participating in a specific course, enter the provided course ID.

## Tips
* Please ensure you've read and understood the program's terms and conditions before proceeding.
* Double-check your input values for accuracy before submitting the form.
* This form should only be submitted once to avoid duplication and ensure accurate participant data.
