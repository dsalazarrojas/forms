# equal_opportunity_monitoring_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for equal opportunity monitoring, gathering information to identify potential biases or discriminatory practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee ID of the person being monitored.
2. Provide the name of the employee.
3. Select the job title of the employee.
4. Choose the department the employee belongs to.
5. Enter the date and time of the monitoring.
6. Enter the email address of the submitter.
7. Enter the phone number of the submitter.
8. Provide comments from the submitter.
9. Enter the name of the submitter.
10. Enter the email address of the submitter.
11. Enter the phone number of the submitter.
12. Enter the department of the submitter.
13. Enter the name of the submitter's supervisor.
14. Enter the job title of the submitter.
15. Enter the diversity group of the submitter (e.g., women, men, etc.).
16. Select the ethnicity of the submitter (e.g., Asian, White, etc.).

18. Select the disability of the submitter (e.g., hearing, sight, etc.).
19. Enter comments from the submitter.
20. Enter the name of the submitter.
