# Occupational Vibration Exposure Monitoring Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and monitor occupational vibration exposure in various industries. It helps in tracking and maintaining records of employee exposure to hazardous vibrations, which is vital for ensuring their safety and health.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the option "Yes" or "No" in the "Job Details" field to indicate whether the job involves vibration exposure.
2. Enter the employee's name and other required information, such as job number, start and end dates, and job duration.
3. Choose the correct monitoring location from the provided options.
4. Select the type of monitoring frequency that best describes the exposure monitoring (e.g., Daily, Weekly, etc.).
5. Provide the job site ID, which is a unique identifier for the job location.
6. Add any relevant job notes and employee notes for additional context.
7. Optionally, add comments from the safety officer and internal review notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Details** (`Yes/No`): This field indicates whether the job involves vibration exposure. Select "Yes" if the job involves exposure to vibrations, "No" otherwise.
* **Employee Name** (`text`): Enter the employee's name who is performing the job.
* **Job Number** (`number`): Enter a unique identifier for the job, which helps track and reference the job record.
* **Job Start Date** (`date`): Record the start date of the job.
* **Job End Date** (`date`): Record the end date of the job.
* **Job Duration** (`number`): Enter the duration of the job.
* **Vibration Level** (`select_multiple`): Choose the level of vibration exposure, which can be Low, Medium, or High.
* **Monitoring Location** (`text`): Select the location where the vibration exposure monitoring was conducted.
* **Monitoring Frequency** (`select_one`): Choose the frequency of monitoring, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Job Site ID** (`text`): Enter a unique identifier for the job site.
* **Job Notes** (`text`): Add any additional notes about the job.
* **Employee Notes** (`text`): Enter any notes from the employee about their exposure.
* **Safety Officer Comments** (`text`): Add any comments from the safety officer about the job.
* **Internal Review Notes** (`text`): Add any internal review notes about the job.
