# Off Site Equipment Condition Monitoring Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Off Site Equipment Condition Monitoring Request form is used to collect information about the condition of off-site equipment for monitoring and maintenance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Enter your information in the following fields:
	- Requestor
	- Site Equipment ID
	- Date
	- Site Access Notes (any additional information about site access)
	- Scheduling Details (any scheduling requirements)
	- Maintenance Team (who will be performing the maintenance)
	- Requestor Name (your name)
	- Requestor Email (your email address)
	- Maintenance Team Contact (the contact information of the maintenance team)
	- Maintenance Team Phone (the phone number of the maintenance team)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor** (<type:text>): Enter your name and information as the person requesting the monitoring or maintenance.
* **Site Equipment ID** (<type:text>): Enter the ID of the off-site equipment being monitored.
* **Date** (<type:date>): Enter the date on which the monitoring or maintenance is scheduled to take place.
* **Site Access Notes** (<type:text>): Add any notes about accessing the site where the equipment is located.
* **Scheduling Details** (<type:text>): Provide any necessary scheduling details, such as time slots or specific tasks.
* **Maintenance Team** (<type:text>): Enter the name of the team responsible for performing the maintenance.
* **Requestor Name** (<type:text>): Enter your name as the person requesting the monitoring or maintenance.
* **Requestor Email** (<type:email>): Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates.
* **Maintenance Team Contact** (<type:text>): Enter the contact information of the maintenance team, including their name and phone number.
* **Maintenance Team Phone** (<type:text>): Enter the phone number of the maintenance team.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have authorization to complete this form before submitting it.
* Enter accurate and complete information for each field to ensure that the monitoring or maintenance process can be completed efficiently and effectively.
