# Hydraulic System Monitoring Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hydraulic System Monitoring Log Form is used to track and record the status of hydraulic systems on a daily or weekly basis. This form helps in maintaining accurate records and providing a centralized platform for team members to report on the system's state and any changes made.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you are a team member authorized to submit data on the hydraulic system's status.
2. Select the correct hydraulic system from the options provided.
3. Choose your team's response to the questions asked, being truthful and accurate in your answers.
4. Fill out the form completely, providing as much detail as possible.
5. Verify your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **hydraulic_system_monitoring_log_form** (`hydraulic_system_monitoring_log_form`, text, required/optional): This is the main field where you will enter the hydraulic system's monitoring log. Be as detailed as possible, describing the system's status and any changes made.
* **team_id** (`team_id`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select all teams that have access to the hydraulic system.
* **hydraulic_unit_id** (`hydraulic_unit_id`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the hydraulic unit being monitored.
* **hydraulic_unit_location** (`hydraulic_unit_location`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the location of the hydraulic unit.
* **date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the monitoring log.
* **time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the monitoring log.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about the hydraulic system's status.
* **submitter_id** (`submitter_id`, select_one, required/optional): Please select who submitted this form.
* **team_member_id** (`team_member_id`, select_one, required/optional): Choose who was present during the monitoring.
* **equipment_status_id** (`equipment_status_id`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the equipment being monitored (Active or Inactive).
* **hydraulic_system_status_id** (`hydraulic_system_status_id`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the hydraulic system (Active or Inactive).
* **hydraulic_system_notes** (`hydraulic_system_notes`, note, required/optional): Provide any notes or comments about the hydraulic system's status.
* **submit** (`submit`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm that you have completed and reviewed the form for accuracy.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the team leader or the system administrator.
