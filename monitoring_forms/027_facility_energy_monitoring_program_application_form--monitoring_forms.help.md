# Facility Energy Monitoring Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from facilities participating in the energy monitoring program. It helps gather details about their energy usage and program enrollment status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Use the provided options for fields like "Energy Monitoring Program" and "Enrollment Status".
3. If you have any additional comments or notes, use the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form (text)** (`application_form`, optional): This field is for any additional comments or information about your application.
* **Facility Details (text)** (`facility_details`, optional): In this field, please provide details about your facility, such as its name and address.
* **Energy Usage Details (text)** (`energy_usage_details`, optional): Describe your energy usage patterns and any unique aspects of your facility's energy consumption.
* **Contact Info (text)** (`contact_info`, optional): Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Energy Monitoring Program (select)** (`energy_monitoring_program`, optional, Yes/No): Indicate whether your facility is participating in the energy monitoring program.
* **Enrollment Status (select)** (`enrollment_status`, optional, Active/Inactive): Choose your facility's current enrollment status in the program.
* **Review Status (select)** (`review_status`, optional, Active/Inactive): Select the current review status of your facility's application.
* **Notes (text)** (`notes`, optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments or explanations about your facility's energy monitoring program.
