# Daily Agricultural Data Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Agricultural Data Collection form is designed to gather essential information from farmers or data collectors about their daily activities and conditions. This form helps in monitoring and tracking progress, identifying trends, and making informed decisions for improved agricultural practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct category or section for each question.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields with accurate and relevant information.
3. Review and edit your entries before submitting the form.
4. Save and submit the form for further processing and analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Farm Details
* **Farm Name**: Enter the name of your farm or agricultural operation.
* **Farm Location**: Enter the location of your farm, including the address or coordinates.
* (Required/Optional) This field is not directly mentioned in the yaml as a required field, it's a text field, but it's a good idea to include it in the form as it is a crucial piece of information)

### Crop Info
* **Crop Info**: Enter a description of the crop you are working with, including type, variety, and any other relevant details.

### Observations
* **Observations**: Record any notable events, issues, or observations from your daily activities.

### Weather Conditions
* **Weather Conditions**: Report the current weather conditions, such as sunny, cloudy, rain, or other.

### Farm Equipment
* **Farm Equipment**: Select all the equipment used on your farm, such as Tractor, Hoe, or Till.

### Labor Force
* **Labor Force**: Report the number of people working on your farm or in your agricultural operation.

### Work Hours
* **Work Hours**: Record the total hours worked on your farm or in your agricultural operation.

### Notes
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your daily activities.

### Email
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address for further communication or support.

### Phone
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for further communication or support.

### Date
* **Date**: Select the date of your data collection, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Time
* **Time**: Select the time of your data collection, in the format 12:00 (HH:MM).

### Weather
* **Weather**: Select the current weather condition, such as Sunny, Cloudy, or Rain.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest when filling out this form.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* This form is designed to be a quick and easy way to collect your daily data.
