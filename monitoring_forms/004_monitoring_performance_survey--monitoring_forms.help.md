# Monitoring Performance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Monitoring Performance Survey" form is designed to collect feedback and performance data from employees. This data will help monitor and improve performance in the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review each page of the form and ensure you have filled out all required fields. Some fields are optional.
2.  Fill in your responses to the performance survey questions.
3.  Select the corresponding answers based on your experience and knowledge.
4.  Ensure you have filled out the feedback field to provide detailed comments.
5.  Click the "Submit Survey" button to submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Performance Survey** (`performance_survey`, `text`, optional)
Fill in your honest answer to the performance survey question. This field is used to gather your thoughts and opinions on the performance.

### 2. **Employee Rating** (`employee_rating`, `number`, optional)
Rate your satisfaction with your work experience. Please use the provided numerical rating scale to select your answer.

### 3. **Performance Period** (`performance_period`, `text`, optional)
Indicate the period of performance you are evaluating. This can be a specific project, quarter, or year.

### 4. **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, `select_one`, optional)
Choose your rating from the provided options: 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5.

### 5. **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional)
Provide detailed comments and feedback on your performance experience.

### 6. **Performance Area** (`performance_area`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all applicable areas where you experienced performance. You can choose one or multiple options: Yes or No.

### 7. **Performance Indicators** (`performance_indicators`, `text`, optional)
Fill in any additional performance indicators that contributed to your performance evaluation.

### 8. **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional)
Provide further comments or explanations on your performance experience.

### 9. **Performance Rationale** (`performance_rationale`, `text`, optional)
Add any additional rationale that influenced your performance evaluation.

### 10. **Submit Survey** (`submit_survey`, `select_one`, optional)
Confirm that you have completed the survey by selecting "Yes" to submit your form.
