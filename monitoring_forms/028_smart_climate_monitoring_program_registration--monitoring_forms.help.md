# Smart Climate Monitoring Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Climate Monitoring Program Registration form is used to collect information about climate monitoring programs. It's intended for internal use, likely for tracking and managing climate monitoring projects within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the climate monitoring program.
2. Provide the location of the program.
3. State the objective of the program.
4. Indicate whether the program has specific equipment types.
5. Select the preferred method of communication for the program.
6. Enter the contact name for the program.
7. Enter the email address of the contact person.
8. Enter the phone number of the contact person.
9. Enter the number of equipment used in the program.
10. Provide any additional details about the equipment used in the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Program Name** (Program Name, text, required)
The program name is the title of the climate monitoring program. Enter the name as it's commonly known or used.

### 2. **Location** (location, text, required)
Provide the location of the climate monitoring program.

### 3. **Objective** (objective, text, required)
State the objective of the climate monitoring program. This is the main goal or purpose of the program.

### 4. **Equipment Type** (Equipment Type, select_multiple, optional)
Select all the types of equipment used in the program. The options are:
* 'Yes'
* 'No'

### 5. **Communication Preference** (Communication Preference, select_multiple, optional)
Select the preferred method of communication for the program. The options are:
* 'Yes'
* 'No'

### 6. **Contact Name** (Contact Name, text, required)
Enter the name of the contact person for the program.

### 7. **Email** (Email, email, required)
Enter the email address of the contact person.

### 8. **Phone** (Phone, text, required)
Enter the phone number of the contact person.

### 9. **Equipment Count** (Equipment Count, number, optional)
Enter the number of equipment used in the program.

### 10. **Equipment Details** (Equipment Details, text, optional)
Provide any additional details about the equipment used in the program.

## Tips
* Ensure that the program name is clear and concise.
* Be accurate when providing the location and objective of the program.
* Select the correct equipment types and communication preferences.
* Enter the contact person's details correctly.
* Be specific when providing equipment details.
