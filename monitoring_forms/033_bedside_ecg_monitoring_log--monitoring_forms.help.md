# Bedside ECG Monitoring Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and document the results of a patient's ECG monitoring, which includes information about the patient's heart rate, cardiac rhythm, and any symptoms or changes observed during the monitoring period.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the patient's basic information, including their full name, medical record number (MRN), and room number.
2. Choose the clinical unit where the patient is being monitored (e.g., ICU, CCU, telemetry, Med-Surg, ER, or Recovery).
3. Record the date and time the ECG was performed.
4. Enter the heart rate (in beats per minute) and any observed cardiac rhythm changes (e.g., sinus bradycardia, tachycardia, atrial fibrillation).
5. Select any ST segment changes (e.g., elevation, depression, T-wave inversion).
6. List any symptoms the patient reported during the monitoring period (e.g., chest pain, shortness of breath).
7. Indicate whether the physician or provider was notified of any significant rhythm changes.
8. Enter your name and any additional clinical observations or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification badge or medical record.
* **Medical Record Number (MRN)**: Enter the patient's unique MRN number as it appears on their medical record.
* **Room and Bed Number**: Enter the patient's room and bed number where they are being monitored.
* **Clinical Unit**: Choose the clinical unit where the patient is being monitored (e.g., ICU, CCU, telemetry, Med-Surg, ER, Recovery).
* **Date and Time of Recording**: Enter the date and time the ECG was performed using the format MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Time of Recording**: Enter the time of the recording only, using the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Heart Rate (BPM)**: Enter the patient's heart rate in beats per minute (BPM).
* **Observed Cardiac Rhythm**: Select the observed cardiac rhythm from the list (e.g., normal sinus rhythm, sinus bradycardia, tachycardia).
* **PR Interval (ms)**: Enter the PR interval in milliseconds (optional).
* **QRS Duration (ms)**: Enter the QRS duration in milliseconds (optional).
* **QT Interval (ms)**: Enter the QT interval in milliseconds (optional).
* **ST Segment Changes Observed**: Select any ST segment changes observed during the recording (e.g., elevation, depression, T-wave inversion).
* **Patient Symptoms During Recording**: Select all symptoms the patient reported during the monitoring period (e.g., chest pain, shortness of breath).
* **Was the Physician or Provider Notified?**: Indicate whether the physician or provider was notified of any significant rhythm changes (yes or no).
* **Name of Staff Member Completing Log**: Enter your name as the staff member completing the log.
* **Additional Clinical Observations**: Enter any additional clinical observations or comments about the patient's condition.
