# Employee Internet Usage Monitoring Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to monitor and track employee internet usage activity.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form for each employee's internet usage monitoring log.
2. Enter the user ID of the employee whose internet usage is being monitored.
3. Enter the date of the internet usage.
4. Enter the start and end time of the internet usage.
5. Enter the website(s) visited during the internet usage.
6. Enter the time spent on the internet.
7. Enter any relevant description of the internet activity.
8. Enter the user's IP address.
9. Enter the device used during the internet usage.
10. Enter the browser used during the internet usage.
11. Enter the department of the employee whose internet usage is being monitored.
12. Enter the type of user (e.g., employee, admin, etc.).
13. Enter any supervisor's notes for the internet usage monitoring.
14. Enter the approver's name.
15. Select the approval status of the internet usage.
16. Enter the reason for non-approval (if applicable).
17. Enter any review notes for the internet usage monitoring.
18. Enter the name of the person who reviewed the internet usage.
19. Enter the time of the review.
20. Enter whether the review is completed.
21. Enter whether the internet usage is approved.
22. Enter the name of the person who created the form.
23. Enter the name of the person who last updated the form.
24. Enter the date when the form was created.
25. Enter the date when the form was last updated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User ID**: Enter the ID of the employee whose internet usage is being monitored.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the internet usage.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the internet usage.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the internet usage.
* **Websites Visited**: Enter the website(s) visited during the internet usage.
* **Time Spent**: Enter the time spent on the internet.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the internet activity.
* **User IP**: Enter the IP address of the user.
* **Device Used**: Enter the device used during the internet usage.
* **Browser Used**: Enter the browser used during the internet usage.
* **Department**: Enter the department of the employee whose internet usage is being monitored.
* **User Type**: Enter the type of user (e.g., employee, admin, etc.).
* **Supervisor Notes**: Enter any supervisor's notes for the internet usage monitoring.
* **Approver**: Enter the name of the approver.
* **Approval Status**: Select the approval status of the internet usage.
* **Reason for Non-Approval**: Enter the reason for non-approval (if applicable).
* **Review Notes**: Enter any review notes for the internet usage monitoring.
* **Reviewed By**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed the internet usage.
* **Review Time**: Enter the time of the review.
* **Reviewed**: Enter whether the review is completed.
* **Approved**: Enter whether the internet usage is approved.
* **Created By**: Enter the name of the person who created the form.
* **Updated By**: Enter the name of the person who last updated the form.
* **Created At**: Enter the date when the form was created.
* **Updated At**: Enter the date when the form was last updated.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* Ensure that all fields are filled out before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
* This form is designed to maintain a transparent and fair work environment.
