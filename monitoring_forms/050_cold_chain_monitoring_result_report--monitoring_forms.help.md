# Cold Chain Monitoring Result Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cold Chain Monitoring Result Report is used to collect data on the monitoring results of cold chain equipment to ensure that products are stored under optimal conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Report" button to start the form.
2.  Fill in the "Report Date" and "Report Time" fields with the current date and time.
3.  Enter your name in the "Reporting Person Name" field.
4.  Enter the "Facility Name" and "Facility Location" of the cold storage room or facility.
5.  Select the correct "Facility Type" from the dropdown list.
6.  Enter the "Target Temperature Range" for the products stored within the facility.
7.  Enter the current temperature in the "Current Temperature" field.
8.  Select the "Temperature Status" to indicate whether the temperature is within range, above range, below range, or critical deviation.
9.  Enter the "Minimum Temperature (last 24 hours)" and "Maximum Temperature (last 24 hours)" recorded.
10. Select the "Alarm Status" to indicate whether the alarm is normal, high temperature alarm, low temperature alarm, sensor malfunction, or multiple alarms.
11. If the alarm was triggered, enter the "When was alarm activated" date and time.
12. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Has alarm been resolved."
13. Enter the "Corrective Action Taken" if any.
14. Select the "Effectiveness of Corrective Action" to indicate whether the action was very effective, effective, partially effective, not effective, or not applicable.
15. Select the "Equipment Status" to indicate whether the equipment is in normal operation, has minor issues, major issues, or is a failure.
16. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Maintenance Needed."
17. If maintenance is needed, enter the "Maintenance Details."
18. Enter any "Product Integrity Assessment" in the "Product Integrity Assessment" field.
19. Enter any "General Observations" or other relevant notes in the "General Observations" field.
20. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Follow-up Action Needed."
21. If follow-up action is needed, enter the "Follow-up Details."
22. Enter the "Scheduled Follow-up Date" if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Report Date**: The date on which the report is being submitted.
*   **Report Time**: The time on which the report is being submitted.
*   **Reporting Person Name**: The name of the person submitting the report.
*   **Facility Name**: The name of the cold storage room or facility.
*   **Facility Location**: The geographic location or address of the cold storage room or facility.
*   **Facility Type**: The type of facility where the cold chain equipment is located. 
     *   Select from: Cold Room, Refrigerated Warehouse, Transport Vehicle, Portable Unit, Other.
*   **Target Temperature Range**: The specified temperature range for the products stored within the facility.
*   **Current Temperature**: The current temperature within the facility.
*   **Temperature Status**: 
     *   Select from: Within range, Above range, Below range, Critical deviation.
*   **Minimum Temperature (last 24 hours)**: The lowest temperature recorded over the last 24 hours.
*   **Maximum Temperature (last 24 hours)**: The highest temperature recorded over the last 24 hours.
*   **Alarm Status**: 
     *   Select from: Normal, High temperature alarm, Low temperature alarm, Sensor malfunction, Multiple alarms.
*   **When was alarm activated**: The date and time when the alarm was triggered.
*   **Has alarm been resolved**: 
     *   Select from: Yes, No, In progress.
*   **Corrective Action Taken**: If a corrective action was taken, describe the action in this field.
*   **Effectiveness of Corrective Action**: 
     *   Select from: Very effective, Effective, Partially effective, Not effective, Not applicable.
*   **Equipment Status**: 
     *   Select from: Normal operation, Minor issues, Major issues, Equipment failure.
*   **Maintenance Needed**: 
     *   Select from: Yes, No.
*   **Maintenance Details**: Describe the maintenance required.
*   **Product Integrity Assessment**: Enter any notes on the condition of the stored products.
*   **General Observations**: Enter any other relevant notes.
*   **Follow-up Action Needed**: 
     *   Select from: Yes, No.
*   **Follow-up Details**: Enter any details on the follow-up action needed.
*   **Scheduled Follow-up Date**: The date and time for the scheduled follow-up.
*   **Follow-up Required**: 
     *   Select from: Yes, No.
