# Referral Code Submission For Credit Monitoring Service - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referral Code Submission For Credit Monitoring Service form is designed to allow you to submit a referral code for our credit monitoring service. This service helps you monitor your credit report and receive alerts about any changes or updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and any other required information.
2. Enter your Referrer's Email and Phone (if applicable).
3. Agree to our Credit Monitoring Agreement and Terms and Conditions.
4. Provide your Address, City, and Region (if applicable).
5. Enter your Postal Code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Referrer Email** (`referrer_email`, email, optional): If you were referred by someone, please enter their email address. (Leave blank if not applicable)
* **Referrer Phone** (`referrer_phone`, text, optional): If you were referred by someone, please enter their phone number. (Leave blank if not applicable)
* **Credit Monitoring Agreement** (`credit_monitoring_agreement`, select_one, required): Please select "Yes" to agree to our credit monitoring agreement.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_one, required): Please select "Yes" to agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Please enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Please enter your city name.
* **Region** (`region`, text, required): Please enter your region name.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, optional): Please enter your postal code (zip code).

Note: This form is designed to be completed by one person for their own benefit. If you are submitting on behalf of someone else, please ensure that you have their permission to do so.
