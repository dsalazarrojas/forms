# Risk Mitigation Monitoring And Management Plan Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to create a structured plan to identify, assess, and mitigate risks associated with your project. This form guides you through a series of questions to ensure that you have considered all aspects of risk management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the project details section to ensure you have a clear understanding of the project's objectives, timelines, and stakeholders.
2. List the risk controls currently implemented for the project.
3. Describe the risk assessment process and identify any risks that were identified.
4. Develop a mitigation strategy to address the identified risks.
5. Describe how ongoing monitoring will be conducted.
6. Identify the risk owner and ensure they understand their role and responsibilities.
7. Assign a risk rating (if applicable).
8. Describe the actions to be taken to mitigate the risk.
9. Schedule regular reviews of the risk management plan.
10. Document the review status and any changes made to the plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details**: This section is used to document the project's details, such as objectives, timelines, and stakeholders. Please ensure that you have a clear understanding of the project's scope and goals.
* **Risk Controls**: List the risk controls that are currently implemented for the project. These can include policies, procedures, and practices that mitigate or minimize risks.
* **Risk Assessment**: Describe the process used to assess the risks, including any tools or methodologies employed. If a risk assessment was conducted, please describe the results and identify any risks that were identified.
* **Mitigation Strategy**: Develop a mitigation strategy to address the identified risks. This can include actions, procedures, or practices that will be implemented to mitigate the risk.
* **Ongoing Monitoring**: Describe how ongoing monitoring will be conducted. This can include regular check-ins, progress reports, or other mechanisms to track the risk.
* **Risk Owner**: Identify the risk owner and ensure they understand their role and responsibilities in risk management.
* **Risk Rating**: Assign a risk rating (if applicable) to help prioritize the risk mitigation efforts.
* **Mitigation Actions**: Describe the actions to be taken to mitigate the risk.
* **Review Frequency**: Schedule regular reviews of the risk management plan to ensure it remains up-to-date.
* **Review Status**: Document the review status of the risk management plan, including any changes made or updates to the plan.
* **Departmental Review**: Identify any departmental reviews or approvals that will be conducted.
* **Risk Acceptance**: Describe the risk acceptance process and any risks that were accepted.
* **Risk Classification**: Assign a risk classification (if applicable) to help prioritize the risk mitigation efforts.
* **Risk Level**: Assign a risk level (if applicable) to help prioritize the risk mitigation efforts.
* **Review Date**: Record the review date of the risk management plan.
* **Review Time**: Record the review time of the risk management plan.
* **Review Notes**: Record any notes from the review.
* **Risk Comments**: Provide any additional comments or observations about the risk.
* **Project Status**: Document the project's status, including any changes or updates.
* **Departmental Status**: Document the departmental status, including any changes or updates.
* **Team Status**: Document the team status, including any changes or updates.
* **Risk Category**: Identify the risk category (if applicable).
* **Risk Description**: Provide a description of the risk.
* **Risk Impact**: Describe the potential impact of the risk.
* **Risk Frequency**: Describe the potential frequency of the risk.

## Tips
- Review the form thoroughly to ensure you have considered all aspects of risk management.
- Use clear and concise language when answering the questions.
- Ensure that you have a risk owner and that they understand their role and responsibilities.
- Schedule regular reviews of the risk management plan to ensure it remains up-to-date.
