# Sickness Monitoring Invitation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an employee's sickness case, including type, symptoms, medical history, contact info, and other relevant details. The data collected will be used to monitor and track sickness cases within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name and other required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. Select the correct sickness type from the available options.
3. Enter any symptoms the employee is experiencing.
4. Fill in the medical history, contact info, and any other relevant details.
5. If the employee has been to the doctor, indicate if they require medical attention.
6. Enter the date the employee reported the sickness and the date they returned to work.
7. Select the employee's status and the reason they returned to work.
8. Indicate the severity of the sickness.
9. Enter any treatment the employee has undergone.
10. Fill in the sickness duration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the employee's name as it appears on their personnel file.
* **Sickness Type** (`sickness_type`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the type of sickness the employee is experiencing from the available options (Common Cold, Flu, Allergy, Fever).
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any symptoms the employee is experiencing.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `note`, required: `false`): Enter any relevant medical history or information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any relevant contact information.
* **Medical Attention** (`medical_attention`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Check if the employee requires medical attention.
* **Date Reported** (`date_reported`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date the employee reported the sickness.
* **Reported By** (`reported_by`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the person reporting the sickness.
* **Medical Certificate** (`medical_certificate`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any medical certificate or documentation.
* **Sickness Description** (`sickness_description`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter a detailed description of the sickness.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the employee's status (Active, Inactive, Retired).
* **Date Returned To Work** (`date_returned_to_work`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date the employee returned to work.
* **Returned To Work Status** (`returned_to_work_status`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Check if the employee has returned to work.
* **Sickness Severity** (`sickness_severity`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select the severity of the sickness (Mild, Moderate, Severe).
* **Treatment** (`treatment`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any treatment the employee has undergone.
* **Sickness Duration** (`sickness_duration`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the duration of the sickness.

Note: Some fields may appear on different pages depending on the user's selections or choices in the form. Please review each field carefully before proceeding.
