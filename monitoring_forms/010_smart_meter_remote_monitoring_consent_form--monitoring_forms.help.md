# Smart Meter Remote Monitoring Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Meter Remote Monitoring Consent Form is a document that allows you to obtain consent from customers for remote monitoring of their smart meters. This form is a part of our commitment to transparency and accountability in our data collection practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information and sign at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Please provide your name and contact information to help us contact you with any questions or concerns related to the monitoring process.
* **Monitoring Settings** (`monitoring_settings`, text, optional): If you have any specific settings you'd like to apply to the monitoring process, please enter them here. This can include any particular days or times you'd like to be excluded from monitoring.
* **Monitoring Schedule** (`monitoring_schedule`, text, optional): If you'd like to request specific monitoring schedules or frequencies, please enter them here.
* **Smart Meter Info** (`smart_meter_info`, text, optional): This field is to provide any additional information about your smart meter, if needed.
* **Monitoring Frequency** (`monitoring_frequency`, number, optional): You can enter a specific frequency for the monitoring process, if you'd like. Please note that this is not required and will default to our standard schedule.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, optional): Please sign your name to confirm your consent for remote monitoring.
* **Customer Signature Date** (`customer_signature_date`, date, optional): Please date and sign the form to confirm your consent for remote monitoring.
* **Customer Signature Time** (`customer_signature_time`, time, optional): Please enter the time of your signature for record-keeping purposes.
