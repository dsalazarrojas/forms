# equipment_maintenance_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The equipment maintenance monitoring form is designed to track and record information about equipment maintenance activities. This form will help you monitor and keep track of your equipment's maintenance schedules and performance metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the general information page (page 1) and provide a brief description of the equipment being monitored.
2. On the second page, enter information about the equipment, such as its name or ID.
3. On the third page, record any maintenance schedules relevant to the equipment.
4. On the fourth page, document any performance metrics related to the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (General Info, Text, Optional)**: This field is for providing a brief description of the equipment being monitored. Enter a short text that summarizes the key details about the equipment.
* **Equipment Information (Equipment Info, Text, Optional)**: This field is for entering any information about the equipment, such as its name or ID. This will help you keep track of the specific equipment being monitored.
* **Maintenance Schedules (Maintenance Schedules, Text, Optional)**: In this field, record any maintenance schedules that apply to the equipment. This can include dates, frequencies, or other relevant details.
* **Performance Metrics (Performance Metrics, Text, Optional)**: Document any performance metrics related to the equipment, such as efficiency, productivity, or other relevant metrics. This will help you track the equipment's performance over time.
