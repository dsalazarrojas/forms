# Fishery Pond Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fishery Pond Inspection Form is used by fishery pond inspectors to record and report on their inspections of fishery ponds. The form is used to track various metrics and observations such as water level, water temperature, water pH, and the health of fish in the pond.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the inspection in the `date_of_inspection` field. This is the date on which you performed the inspection.
2. Enter the location of the pond in the `pond_location` field. This is the location of the pond, such as the name or address.
3. Enter your name and title/position in the `inspector_name` field. This will help identify who performed the inspection.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments about the pond or inspection in the `notes` field. This field is optional, but can be useful for additional information.
5. Enter the water level reading from the pond in the `water_level` field. This is a required field as it's critical for water quality and management.
6. Enter the water temperature reading from the pond in the `water_temp` field. This is also a required field as it's critical for water quality and management.
7. Enter the water pH reading from the pond in the `water_ph` field. This is a required field as it's critical for water quality and management.
8. Answer the question about the presence of fish species in the `fish_species` field. If fish species are present, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.
9. Enter the average fin length of the fish in the `fin_length` field. This is a required field as it's critical for assessing fish health.
10. Answer the question about the health of the fish in the `fin_health` field. If fish health is good, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.
11. Enter any recommendations for the pond in the `recommendations` field. This field is optional, but can be useful for suggestions for pond management.
12. Enter any additional information or observations in the `additional_info` field. This field is optional, but can be useful for additional comments or notes.
13. Upload any supporting images of the pond or fish in the `images` field. This field is optional, but can be useful for visual evidence or documentation.
14. Upload any supporting files related to the pond or inspection in the `files` field. This field is optional, but can be useful for additional documentation.
15. Upload any videos of the pond or fish in the `videos` field. This field is optional, but can be useful for visual evidence or documentation.
16. Enter any additional comments or notes in the `comments` field. This field is optional, but can be useful for additional information.
17. Answer the question about whether follow-up is necessary in the `follow-up` field. If follow-up is necessary, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.
18. Enter the name and title/position of the person who completed the inspection in the `completed_by` field. This field is a required field as it's critical for tracking who completed the inspection.
19. Enter the date on which the form was submitted in the `date_submitted` field. This field is a required field as it's critical for tracking when the inspection was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### date_of_inspection (Date)
The date on which the inspection was performed.

### pond_location (Text)
The location of the pond, such as the name or address.

### inspector_name (Text)
The name and title/position of the inspector.

### notes (Note)
Any additional comments or notes about the pond or inspection.

### water_level (Number)
The reading from the water level gauge or probe.

### water_temp (Number)
The temperature reading of the water in degrees Celsius or Fahrenheit.

### water_ph (Number)
The pH reading of the water.

### fish_species (Select One)
If fish species are present, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.

### fin_length (Number)
The average fin length of the fish.

### fin_health (Select One)
If fish health is good, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.

### notes (Note)
Any additional comments or notes about the pond or inspection.

### pond_condition (Select Multiple)
Select any applicable conditions of the pond, such as 'Yes' for good conditions, 'No' for poor conditions.

### recommendations (Text)
Any suggestions or recommendations for the pond's management.

### additional_info (Note)
Any additional information or comments about the pond or inspection.

### images (Text)
Any supporting images of the pond or fish.

### files (Text)
Any supporting files related to the pond or inspection.

### videos (Text)
Any supporting videos of the pond or fish.

### comments (Text)
Any additional comments or notes about the pond or inspection.

### follow_up (Select Multiple)
If follow-up is necessary, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.

### completed_by (Text)
The name and title/position of the person who completed the inspection.

### date_submitted (Date)
The date on which the form was submitted.
