# Noise Exposure Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Noise Exposure Monitoring Form is used to collect information about an employee's exposure to noise in the workplace. This form is essential for monitoring and managing noise levels to prevent potential health risks and ensuring a safe working environment. By filling out this form, you will help your organization maintain a healthy and safe work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the **Employee Name** field. This will help us identify you as the employee who has been exposed to noise.
2. Enter the **Date of Exposure** when you were exposed to noise in the form's format (mm/dd/yyyy).
3. Fill in the **Noise Exposure Level**, indicating the level of noise you were exposed to, on a scale from 1 to 10.
4. Enter the duration of your exposure to noise, measured in minutes.
5. Indicate the **Noise Frequency** of the noise you were exposed to, from 1 (low) to 10 (high).
6. If applicable, enter the **Observer Name** and **Observer Organization** to help us track who observed the noise exposure.
7. Enter the **Observation Point** where the noise exposure occurred.
8. In the **Noise Levels** field, select all levels to which you were exposed (e.g., LPA, LEP, LET).
9. Add any additional **Comments** about your experience, which will help us understand the situation better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (text): Enter your name as an employee.
* **Date of Exposure** (date): Enter the date of the noise exposure in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Noise Exposure Level** (number): Indicate the level of noise you were exposed to, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Noise Duration** (number): Enter the duration of your noise exposure, measured in minutes.
* **Noise Frequency** (number): Indicate the frequency of the noise you were exposed to, from 1 (low) to 10 (high).
* **Observer Name** (text): Enter the name of the person who observed the noise exposure.
* **Observer Organization** (text): Enter the name of the organization that observed the noise exposure.
* **Observation Point** (text): Enter the location where the noise exposure occurred.
* **Noise Levels** (select multiple): Select all levels to which you were exposed, (e.g., LPA, LEP, LET).
* **Comments** (note): Provide any additional information about your experience.

**Tips:**

* Always fill out the form as soon as possible after your exposure to noise.
* Use the **Noise Levels** field to select all the levels you experienced.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
