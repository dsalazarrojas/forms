# signal_quality_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Signal Quality Assessment Form is a tool used by field technicians to report on the signal quality at a specific location. This form gathers critical information that will help us identify areas of improvement and optimize signal delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Location" page and enter the name of the location where you performed the signal quality assessment.
2.  On the "Signal Strength" page, enter the signal strength you observed, using whole numbers (e.g., 5 or 0).
3.  On the "Noise Level" page, describe the type of noise you encountered (e.g., static, interference, etc.).
4.  On the "Issues" page, select all the issues you observed, such as poor reception, dropped calls, etc.
5.  On the "Time of Day" page, enter the time when you performed the assessment.
6.  On the "Technician Name" page, enter your name as the person completing the form.
7.  On the "Location Coordinates" page, enter the GPS coordinates of the location where you performed the assessment.
8.  On the "Notes" page, include any additional comments or observations.
9.  If you have any contact information (e.g., email or phone number), please enter it on the "Email" and "Phone" pages.
10.  Finally, submit the form to complete the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Location**:
  • **Location** (text, required):
    This field is for entering the name of the location where you performed the signal quality assessment.
*  **Signal Strength**:
  • **Signal Strength** (number, required):
    Enter the signal strength you observed, using whole numbers (e.g., 5 or 0). A higher number indicates stronger signal, while a lower number indicates weaker signal.
*  **Noise Level**:
  • **Noise Level** (text, required):
    Describe the type of noise you encountered during the assessment, such as static, interference, or other issues.
*  **Issues**:
  • **Issues** (select multiple, required):
    Select all issues you observed, such as poor reception, dropped calls, etc.
*  **Time of Day**:
  • **Time of Day** (time, required):
    Enter the time when you performed the assessment.
*  **Technician Name**:
  • **Technician Name** (text, required):
    Enter your name as the person completing the form.
*  **Location Coordinates**:
  • **Location Coordinates** (text, required):
    Enter the GPS coordinates of the location where you performed the assessment.
*  **Notes**:
  • **Notes** (note, optional):
    Include any additional comments or observations about the assessment.
*  **Email**:
  • **Email** (email, optional):
    Enter your contact email address.
*  **Phone**:
  • **Phone** (text, optional):
    Enter your contact phone number.
*  **Issues**:
  • **Issues** (select multiple, required):
    Select all issues you observed during the assessment.
