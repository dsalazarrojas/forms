# Insider Risk Mitigation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insider Risk Mitigation Assessment form is used to assess and mitigate potential insider risks in the organization. It helps gather information about incidents or threats, their severity, and recommended controls or actions to take.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer them based on your knowledge of the incidents or threats.
2. Select the correct options for each question, taking into account the severity and impact of the incident or threat.
3. Fill out all required fields, denoted by `*`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident History** (`incident_history`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to select all incidents that have occurred in the past that are related to the current threat or risk. Select all applicable options from the dropdown list.
* **Threat Controls** (`threat_controls`, text, optional): This field is used to describe any controls or measures that are currently in place to mitigate the threat or risk. Provide a brief description of the controls.
* **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, number, optional): This field is used to rate the risk level of the threat or risk, with higher numbers indicating greater severity.
* **Incident Response** (`incident_response`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the response plan or actions taken to address the threat or risk. Provide a brief description of the response plan.
* **Root Cause** (`root_cause`, text, optional): This field is used to identify the root cause of the threat or risk. Provide a clear and concise explanation of the root cause.
* **Recommended Controls** (`recommended_controls`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select whether recommended controls or measures are in place to mitigate the threat or risk. Select the correct option from the dropdown list.
* **Improvement Plan** (`improvement_plan`, text, optional): This field is used to describe any plans or actions to improve control or mitigate the threat or risk. Provide a brief description of the plan.
* **Risk Rating** (`risk_rating`, number, optional): This field is used to rate the risk level of the threat or risk, with higher numbers indicating greater severity.
* **Risk Comment** (`risk_comment`, text, optional): This field is used to provide any additional comments or information about the threat or risk. Provide a clear and concise explanation.
* **Threat Level** (`threat_level`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the current threat level of the threat or risk. Provide a brief description of the threat level.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out only relevant fields and select only applicable options.
* Be honest and accurate when providing information in the form.
* Use clear and concise language when describing controls, plans, or comments.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
