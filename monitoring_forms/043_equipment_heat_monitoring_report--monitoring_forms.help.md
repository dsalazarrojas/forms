# equipment_heat_monitoring_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The equipment heat monitoring report form is used to collect data on equipment temperature readings, issues, and maintenance activities. This form is used to identify potential problems and schedule maintenance for equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct asset ID from the asset dropdown menu.
2. Enter the temperature readings for the equipment, if applicable.
3. Identify any issues with the equipment and select all that apply.
4. Provide evidence of any issues encountered.
5. Add any comments or notes about the equipment and its condition.
6. Enter the date and time of the temperature check.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information** (Text): This field is for any general information about the equipment and the temperature check. Enter any relevant details that may be helpful for maintenance personnel.

### Asset
* **Asset** (Select One): Choose the asset ID of the equipment being monitored.
  - Choose the correct asset ID from the dropdown menu.

### Temperature Readings
* **Temperature Readings** (Number): Enter the temperature reading for the equipment, if applicable.
  - This field is for recording the current temperature reading of the equipment.

### Issue
* **Issue** (Select Multiple): Identify any issues with the equipment and select all that apply.
  - Choose all issues that apply from the dropdown menu.

### Issue Evidence
* **Issue Evidence** (Text): Provide evidence of any issues encountered, such as photos or descriptions.
  - This field is for providing supporting evidence of any issues selected.

### Comments
* **Comments** (Text): Add any additional comments or notes about the equipment and its condition.
  - This field is for providing any additional information about the equipment.

### Date
* **Date** (Date): Enter the date of the temperature check.
  - This field is for recording the date of the temperature check.

### Time
* **Time** (Time): Enter the time of the temperature check.
  - This field is for recording the time of the temperature check.
