# Lead Conversion Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to track the conversion status of leads.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the relevant status for each lead conversion category.
2.  Choose the correct source(s) for the lead.
3.  Enter the priority level of the lead.
4.  Provide any additional notes about the lead.
5.  Review and confirm your answers before submitting the form.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **lead_conversion** (`lead_conversion`, select_one, required: false): This field asks whether a lead has been converted or not. Choose 'Yes' if the lead has been converted and 'No' if it has not.
- **lead_status** (`lead_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the statuses that apply to the lead. Typically, this includes Active and Inactive status.
- **lead_date** (`lead_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the lead conversion occurred, if applicable.
- **lead_time** (`lead_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time the lead conversion occurred, if applicable.
- **lead_notes** (`lead_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any relevant notes about the lead conversion.
- **lead_email** (`lead_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the individual who converted the lead.
- **lead_phone** (`lead_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the individual who converted the lead.
- **assigned_to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required: false): Indicate if the lead was assigned to someone or not.
- **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false): Indicate if a specific tool was used for the lead conversion.
- **lead_conversion_id** (`lead_conversion_id`, number, required: false): Enter the unique ID of the lead conversion.
- **lead_source** (`lead_source`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the sources that contributed to the lead conversion.
- **lead_priority** (`lead_priority`, number, required: false): Enter the level of priority for the lead.
## Tips
*   Be consistent with your answers and use the same format for each lead.
*   Make sure to review the lead's history in the system before filling out the form to ensure accuracy.
*   If you're unsure about any of the questions, please consult with the relevant team members for confirmation.
*   Use clear and concise language when filling out the form to ensure readability.
