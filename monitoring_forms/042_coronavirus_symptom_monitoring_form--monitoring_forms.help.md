# Coronavirus Symptom Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coronavirus Symptom Monitoring Form is designed to collect information about recent or ongoing symptoms related to the coronavirus. It helps track and record symptoms over time, making it easier to identify patterns and provide insights for medical professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Select one of the options for each symptom, if you have experienced or are experiencing it.
* If you have not experienced or are not experiencing a symptom, select "None" from the dropdown list.
* Complete all pages as instructed.
* Submit the form after you have completed all pages.
