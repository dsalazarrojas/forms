# Diabetes Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare providers monitor the health status of patients with diabetes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above. Enter the required information for each section and submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Blood Sugar Level**: Select your current blood sugar level from the dropdown list. Options are Normal, High, and Low.
* **Blood Pressure**: Enter your blood pressure as a single value (e.g. 120/80).
* **Weight**: Enter your current weight.
* **Medication Notes**: Describe any medication notes you have taken as a result of your recent doctor's appointment.
* **Diet**: Describe your diet for the past week.
* **Exercise**: Describe your exercise routine for the past week.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about your condition.
* **Medication Reminders**: Enter any medication reminders you need help with.

## Tips
* Please complete this form once a week or as advised by your healthcare provider.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your healthcare provider.
* Be honest and accurate when completing this form to ensure your healthcare provider has the best information to provide you with optimal care.
