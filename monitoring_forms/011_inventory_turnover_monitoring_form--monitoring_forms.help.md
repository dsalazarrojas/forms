# Inventory Turnover Monitoring Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Inventory Turnover Monitoring Form is used to track and monitor the inventory levels and turnover rates of specific items. This form is used to collect data on current and previous inventory levels, turnover status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, select a page from the list below.
2. Fill out each field with relevant information.
3. Review the fields and ensure you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inventory Form Data** (`inventory_form_data`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the inventory form data.
* **Inventory Details** (`inventory_details`, text, not required): Enter a description of the inventory details.
* **Turnover Status** (`turnover_status`, text, not required): Enter the turnover status of the inventory item.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, not required): Enter any additional information about the inventory item.
* **Dates and Times** (`dates_and_times`, text, not required): Enter the dates and times relevant to the inventory item.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Enter contact information for the inventory item.
* **Inventory Item Description** (`inventory_item_description_1`, text, not required): Enter a description of the inventory item.
* **Current Inventory Level** (`current_inventory_level_1`, text, not required): Enter the current inventory level of the item.
* **Previous Inventory Level** (`previous_inventory_level_1`, text, not required): Enter the previous inventory level of the item.
* **Inventory Turnover Rate** (`inventory_turnover_rate_1`, text, not required): Enter the inventory turnover rate of the item.
* **Inventory Average Cost** (`inventory_average_cost_1`, text, not required): Enter the average cost of the item.
* **Period** (`period_1`, text, not required): Enter the period relevant to the inventory item.
* **Turnover Status 1** (`turnover_status_1`, text, not required): Enter the turnover status of the item.
* **Email** (`email_1`, text, not required): Enter a contact email for the inventory item.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_1`, text, not required): Enter a contact phone number for the inventory item.
* **Note** (`note_1`, text, not required): Enter a note about the inventory item.
* **Email Phone Note** (`email_phone_note_1`, text, not required): Enter a note with email and phone information about the inventory item.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, text, not required): Enter a second contact email for the inventory item.
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`, text, not required): Enter a second contact phone number for the inventory item.
* **Note 2** (`note_2`, text, not required): Enter a second note about the inventory item.

## Tips

* Be sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the inventory management team.
