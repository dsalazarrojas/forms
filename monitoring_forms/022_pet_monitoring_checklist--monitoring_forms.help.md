# Pet Monitoring Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pet Monitoring Checklist is used to collect information about pets that require monitoring. This information helps pet owners and caregivers track the pet's health, feeding, and care status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form for each pet that requires monitoring.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
3. Use the provided options for select fields to choose the best response.
4. Enter any relevant notes or comments in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the pet being monitored.
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of pet (dog, cat, or other).
* **Pet Health** (`pet_health`, select_multiple, required): Choose the current health status of the pet (good, okay, or bad).
* **Feeding Status** (`feeding_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the feeding status of the pet (yes or no).
* **Water Status** (`water_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the water status of the pet (yes or no).
* **Temperature** (`temperature`, number, required): Enter the current temperature of the pet.
* **Medication Administered** (`medication_administered`, text, required): Enter any medication administered to the pet.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, required): Enter the follow-up date for the pet's status.
* **Notes** (`note`, note, required): Enter any relevant notes or comments about the pet.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the pet owner or caregiver.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the pet owner or caregiver.
* **Assigned Contact** (`assigned_contact`, select_one, required): Select the assigned contact person (John Doe or Jane Doe).
* **Assigned Contact Email** (`assigned_contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the assigned contact person.
* **Assigned Contact Phone** (`assigned_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the assigned contact person.
* **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, select_multiple, required): Choose any additional notes or comments (good or bad).
* **Follow-up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, text, required): Enter any follow-up notes or comments.
* **Follow-up Phone** (`follow_up_phone`, text, required): Enter the follow-up phone number.
* **Follow-up Email** (`follow_up_email`, email, required): Enter the follow-up email address.
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_one, required): Select the follow-up status (done or not done).
* **Follow-up Notes 3** (`follow_up_notes`, text, required): Enter any follow-up notes or comments.
* **Notes 3** (`notes_3`, select_multiple, required): Choose any additional notes or comments (good or bad).
* **Notes 4** (`notes_4`, select_multiple, required): Choose any additional notes or comments (good or bad).
* **Follow-up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, text, required): Enter the follow-up contact information.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately. If you are unsure about any field, please ask your veterinarian or caregiver for guidance.
