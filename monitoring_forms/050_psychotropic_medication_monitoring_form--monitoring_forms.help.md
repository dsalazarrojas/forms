# Psychotropic Medication Monitoring Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Psychotropic Medication Monitoring Form is designed to track and monitor the usage of psychotropic medications. This form helps healthcare professionals and patients keep track of medication names, dosages, administration frequencies, start and stop dates, and any side effects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Medication Name** in the text field. Please make sure to enter the correct name of the medication you are taking.
2. Enter the **Medication Dose** in the text field. This is where you can specify the dosage information for the medication.
3. Select the **Administration Frequency** from the dropdown list: **DAILY**, **WEEKLY**, or **MONTHLY**. This will help determine how often the medication is taken.
4. Enter the **Start Date** of the medication in date format. This is when you first started taking the medication.
5. Enter the **Stop Date** (if applicable) in date format. If you have stopped taking the medication, provide the date it was discontinued.
6. If you have stopped taking the medication, enter the **Reason for Stopping** in the text field. Explain why you have discontinued the medication.
7. Select the **Monitoring Status** from the dropdown list: **ACTIVE** or **INACTIVE**. This indicates whether the medication is currently being monitored.
8. Enter any **Notes** regarding the medication in the text field. This is a space for additional comments or information.
9. If the **Monitoring Status** has changed, enter the **Reason for Monitoring Status Change** in the text field. Explain why the monitoring status has changed.
10. If the medication's **Administration Frequency** has changed, enter the **Reason for Change** in the text field. Explain why the administration frequency has changed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medication Name**: Enter the name of the medication you are taking.
* **Medication Dose**: Enter the dosage information for the medication.
* **Administration Frequency**: Select how often you take the medication. This can be **DAILY**, **WEEKLY**, or **MONTHLY**.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you first started taking the medication.
* **Stop Date**: Enter the date you stopped taking the medication (if applicable).
* **Reason for Stopping**: Explain why you stopped taking the medication.
* **Monitoring Status**: Select whether the medication is currently being monitored (**ACTIVE** or **INACTIVE**).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about the medication.
* **Reason for Monitoring Status Change**: Explain why the monitoring status has changed.
* **Medication Side Effects**: Describe any known side effects of the medication.
* **Monitoring Status Change**: Enter the date when the monitoring status changed (if applicable).
* **Medication Frequency Change**: Enter any changes to the medication's administration frequency (if applicable).
* **Reason for Change**: Explain why the administration frequency has changed (if applicable).

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
