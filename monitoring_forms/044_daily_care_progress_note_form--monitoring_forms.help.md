# Daily Care Progress Note Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Care Progress Note Form is designed to collect and record daily care activities and progress for patients. This form helps care providers document important information, track changes, and ensure consistency in care delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the client's name** in the `client_name` field. This field is optional but recommended for identifying the client.
2. **Select the visit date** in the `visit_date` field. This is a date field where you can select a date for the care activity.
3. **Enter the visit time** in the `visit_time` field. This field allows you to record the time of the care activity.
4. **Choose the client's status** in the `client_status` field. This field helps track the client's condition or status during the care activity.
5. **Add any relevant notes** in the `notes` field. This is an open-ended field for recording any important observations or comments about the care activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **client_name**: The client's name, which helps identify the individual being cared for. (optional)
* **visit_date**: The date of the care activity. (required)
* **visit_time**: The time of the care activity. (required)
* **client_status**: A field to track the client's condition or status during the care activity. (optional)
* **notes**: A space to record any important observations or comments about the care activity. (required)

## Tips
* Be as concise as possible when filling out the form.
* Ensure accuracy when selecting dates and times.
* Use the client's name field to identify the individual being cared for.
* Use the client's status field to provide a clear and concise description of their condition.
* Keep the notes field for important comments or observations.
