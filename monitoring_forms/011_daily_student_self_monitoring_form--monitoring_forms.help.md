# Daily Student Self Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help students track and report their daily progress. It's meant to be filled out by students on a daily basis, and the data collected will be used to monitor and support their academic or personal development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. If you have a Last Name, enter it in the "Last Name" field (optional).
3. Select the date you're completing the form for in the "Date" field.
4. Enter the time you're completing the form for in the "Time" field.
5. Select the subject or topic you're working on or related to in the "Subject" field (optional).
6. Choose how you're doing in relation to your progress in the "Progress" field, such as "on track," "off track," or "unknown."
7. Enter any additional notes or comments about your progress in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): If you have a last name, enter it here. Otherwise, leave this field blank.

### Date
* **Date** (Date, Optional): Select the date you're completing this form for.

### Time
* **Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time you're completing this form.

### Subject
* **Subject** (Text, Optional): Select the subject or topic you're working on or related to.

### Progress
* **Progress** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose how you're doing in relation to your progress:
	+ **On track**: You're meeting your goals and expectations.
	+ **Off track**: You're falling behind or having challenges.
	+ **Unknown**: You're unsure about your progress.

### Notes
* **Notes** (Note, Required): Enter any additional comments or observations about your progress. This is an opportunity to provide more context or information that might be helpful for your monitors or supporters.
