# NICU Monitoring Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This NICU Monitoring Checklist form helps caregivers and medical professionals track the vital signs, status, and medical interventions of neonatal patients in the NICU. It covers essential parameters such as critical care plans, vital signs, respiratory status, cardiovascular status, neurological status, and laboratory tests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the patient's critical care plan and ensure you have a clear understanding of their treatment needs.
2. Enter the patient's current vital signs, including their oxygen saturation levels, respiratory rate, and any other relevant information.
3. Record the patient's respiratory status, including oxygen saturation levels, respiratory rate, and a brief description of their condition.
4. Document the patient's cardiovascular status, including any medication administered and relevant laboratory test results.
5. Enter the patient's neurological status, including any relevant findings or concerns.
6. Record any laboratory tests performed, selecting the relevant options from the available choices.
7. Document the patient's current medical imaging studies, including any recent X-rays or EKGs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Critical Care Plan** (`critical_care_plan`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the patient's critical care plan, including any specific goals, interventions, or treatments.
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, text, required): Record the patient's current vital signs, including temperature, blood pressure, pulse, and respirations.
* **Respiratory Status** (`respiratory_status`, select_multiple, required):
	+ **Oxygen Saturation**: Record the patient's current oxygen saturation levels.
	+ **Respiratory Rate**: Enter the patient's current respiratory rate.
	+ **Respiratory Status Description**: Provide a brief description of the patient's respiratory status.
* **Cardiovascular Status** (`cardiovascular_status`, text, required): Document the patient's cardiovascular status, including any relevant laboratory test results or medication administered.
* **Neurological Status** (`neurological_status`, text, required): Enter a description of the patient's neurological status, including any relevant findings or concerns.
* **Medication Administered** (`medication_administered`, text, optional): Record any medications administered to the patient.
* **Laboratory Tests** (`laboratory_tests`, select_one, optional):
	+ **Blood Gas Analysis**: Select this option if the patient has undergone blood gas analysis.
	+ **Chest Xray**: Select this option if the patient has undergone a chest X-ray.
	+ **Electrocardiogram (EKG)**: Select this option if the patient has undergone an EKG.
	+ **Other (specify)**: Select this option if the patient has undergone other laboratory tests, and enter a brief description.
* **Imaging Studies** (`imaging_studies`, text, required): Document any recent imaging studies, including X-rays or EKGs.

## Tips
Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field. If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your medical team or supervisor. This form is designed to be completed by caregivers and medical professionals with expertise in neonatal care. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or the form administrator.
