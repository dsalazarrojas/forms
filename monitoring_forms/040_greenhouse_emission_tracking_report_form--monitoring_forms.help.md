# Greenhouse Emission Tracking Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Greenhouse Emission Tracking Report Form is designed to monitor and report on greenhouse gas emissions and related reduction actions. It provides a standardized format for collecting and recording data on emissions levels, reduction targets, and supporting reports.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form instructions and ensure you have the necessary information and permissions to accurately fill out the form.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, based on your knowledge and available data.
3. Complete each field as thoroughly and accurately as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Greenhouse Gas Emissions** (`greenhousegas_emission`):
	+ Select the relevant options to indicate whether or not you have greenhouse gas emissions.
* **Reduction Actions** (`reductionactions`):
	+ Provide a brief description of the actions taken to reduce greenhouse gas emissions.
* **Reports** (`report`):
	+ Select the relevant options to indicate whether supporting reports are available for internal reporting.
* **Year** (`year`):
	+ Enter the year for which you are reporting on greenhouse gas emissions.
* **Greenhouse Gas Reduction** (`greenhousegasreduction`):
	+ Enter the amount of greenhouse gas reduction achieved.
* **Reduction Targets** (`reductiontargets`):
	+ Enter the reduction target for greenhouse gas emissions.
* **Baseline** (`baseline`):
	+ Enter the baseline for greenhouse gas emissions.
* **Reduction Percent** (`reductionpercent`):
	+ Enter the percentage of reduction achieved.
* **Report Date** (`reportdate`):
	+ Enter the date on which the report was completed.
* **Reporting Period** (`reportingperiod`):
	+ Select the reporting period for which the data applies.
* **Reduction Actions Description** (`reductionactionsdescription`):
	+ Provide a detailed description of the reduction actions taken.
* **Reduction Targets Present** (`reductiontargetspresent`):
	+ Select the relevant option to indicate whether reduction targets are available.
