# Neonatal Monitoring Market Research Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect market research data for neonatal monitoring products. This data will help to better understand the usage and satisfaction of healthcare professionals with neonatal monitoring devices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each page and question carefully.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to complete all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
4. If you have any questions or need clarification on a particular question, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`<page 1>`, `<page 1>`, not required): This is the first page of the form where you will be introduced to the purpose and instructions of the form.

### Device Usage
* **Device Usage** (`<page 2>`, `text`, not required): This question asks about your experience with neonatal monitoring devices. Please provide a brief description of your experience, including any specific devices you have used and how often you use them.

### Satisfaction
* **Satisfaction** (`<page 3>`, `select multiple`, not required): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the devices you have used. Please select all the options that apply to your experience.

### Buying Cycles
* **Buying Cycles** (`<page 4>`, `select one`, not required): This question asks about your typical process when purchasing neonatal monitoring devices. Please select the option that best describes your purchasing process.

### Adoption Barriers
* **Adoption Barriers** (`<page 5>`, `text`, not required): This question asks about any challenges or obstacles you have faced when adopting new neonatal monitoring devices. Please provide a brief description of any barriers you have encountered.

### Device Satisfaction
* **Device Satisfaction** (`<page 6>`, `number`, not required): This question asks about your level of satisfaction with the devices you have used. Please rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the least satisfied and 10 being the most satisfied.

### Usage Frequency
* **Usage Frequency** (`<page 7>`, `time`, not required): This question asks about how often you use neonatal monitoring devices. Please select the option that best describes your typical usage frequency.

### Device Purchasing Power
* **Device Purchasing Power** (`<page 8>`, `number`, not required): This question asks about your perception of the purchasing power of neonatal monitoring devices. Please rate your perception on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the least powerful and 10 being the most powerful.

### Purchasing Cycle Length
* **Purchasing Cycle Length** (`<page 9>`, `number`, not required): This question asks about the length of time you typically spend purchasing new neonatal monitoring devices. Please rate your typical cycle length on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the shortest cycle and 10 being the longest cycle.

Please note that this form is for market research purposes only and the information collected will be kept confidential. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
