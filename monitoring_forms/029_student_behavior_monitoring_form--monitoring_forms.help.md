# Student Behavior Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Behavior Monitoring Form is a tool used by teachers or school staff to document and monitor student behavior, incidents, and concerns that have arisen during the school day or at other times. This form is designed to help identify areas where students may need additional support or guidance, ensuring that issues are addressed promptly and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
* Enter the Student ID in the field provided.
* Enter the Student Name associated with the Student ID.
* Fill in the Behavior Notes section with details of the observed behavior or incident.
* Select the appropriate Behavior Risk level (High, Medium, or Low) for the observed behavior.
* If applicable, describe the Action Taken or Parent Concerns/Teacher Concerns/Other Concerns.
* Add any additional Next Steps or notes.
* Save and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, Number, Required): A unique identifier for the student associated with this report. 
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, Text, Optional): The name of the student associated with this report.
* **Teacher ID** (`teacher_id`, Number, Optional): The identifier of the teacher or staff member submitting this report.
* **Behavior Notes** (`behavior_notes`, Text, Optional): A detailed description of the observed behavior or incident.
* **Date/Time Observed** (`date_time_observed`, Date, Optional): The date and time when the behavior was observed or incident occurred.
* **Behavior Risk** (`behavior_risk`, Select One, Optional): A measure of the risk level of the observed behavior (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, Text, Optional): A description of any action taken or planned to address the behavior or incident.
* **Parent Concerns** (`parent_concerns`, Text, Optional): Any concerns or questions raised by parents or caregivers related to the behavior or incident.
* **Teacher Concerns** (`teacher_concerns`, Text, Optional): Any concerns or questions raised by teachers or school staff related to the behavior or incident.
* **Other Concerns** (`other_concerns`, Text, Optional): Any other concerns or questions related to the behavior or incident.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, Text, Optional): Additional steps or notes for further action or follow-up.
* **Date Last Updated** (`date_last_updated`, Time, Optional): The date and time the form was last updated.
* **Date Last Updated By** (`date_last_updated_by`, Text, Optional): The name of the last person to update the form. 

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the Behavior Notes section.
* Select the correct Behavior Risk level based on the observed behavior or incident.
* Be mindful of sensitive information and ensure all entries are made with respect and care for the students involved.
* If unsure about any field, refer to school policy or seek guidance from a supervisor.
