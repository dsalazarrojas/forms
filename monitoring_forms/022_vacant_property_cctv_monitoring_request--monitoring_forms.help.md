# vacant_property_cctv_monitoring_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for CCTV monitoring on a vacant property. It collects information about the property, authorization, monitoring, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide property details in the first field.
2. Enter authorization details in the second field.
3. Enter monitoring details in the third field.
4. Enter submission details in the fourth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address and other relevant details of the vacant property.
* **Authorization Details** (`authorization_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the details of the person or entity authorized to request CCTV monitoring for the property.
* **Monitoring Details** (`monitoring_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the duration and frequency of the desired CCTV monitoring.
* **Submission Details** (`submission_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information or comments about the submission of the request.
