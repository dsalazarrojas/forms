# Observation And Media Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Observation and Media Log form is used to collect and document data on observations and media associated with a specific location, providing a record of events and media captured during monitoring activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant observation page to fill out.
	* Choose the page that corresponds to the observation you want to document.
2. Provide a brief description of the observation on the **Observation Page**.
	* Type in a short summary of the observation, including any relevant details.
3. Select the type of media involved on the **Media Page**.
	* Choose the option that best describes the type of media captured during the observation.
4. Upload media files on the **Media Upload Page**.
	* Use the provided upload fields to add media files associated with the observation.
5. Enter the details of the observation on the **Observation Details Page**.
	* Fill out the fields for observation date, time, latitude, longitude, and any other relevant details.
6. Choose whether the media was uploaded by an observer on the **Media Type** field.
	* Select 'Yes' if the media was uploaded by the observer, or 'No' otherwise.
7. Add the media URL on the **Media URL** field.
	* If the media was not uploaded, add the URL where it can be found.
8. On the **Media Upload** field, use the upload field to add media files.
9. Choose whether the media was uploaded by the observer on the **Observer** field.
	* Select 'Yes' if the media was uploaded by the observer, or 'No' otherwise.
10. Select the date and time of submission on the **Submission Date** and **Submission Time** fields.
	* Enter the date and time of when the media was submitted.
11. Fill out the **Latitude** and **Longitude** fields with the location of the observation.
12. Add any additional comments on the **Comment** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Observation Page**: A page for describing the observation.
	+ Type a short summary of the observation, including any relevant details.
* **Media Page**: A page for selecting the type of media involved.
	+ Choose the option that best describes the type of media captured during the observation.
* **Media Upload Page**: A page for uploading media files.
	+ Use the provided upload fields to add media files associated with the observation.
* **Media Type**: A field to indicate whether the media was uploaded by the observer.
	+ Select 'Yes' if the media was uploaded by the observer, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Media URL**: A field to add a URL of the media.
	+ If the media was not uploaded, add the URL where it can be found.
* **Media Upload**: A field to add media files.
	+ Use the provided upload field to add media files associated with the observation.
* **Observer**: A field to indicate whether the media was uploaded by the observer.
	+ Select 'Yes' if the media was uploaded by the observer, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Observation Date**: A field for the date of the observation.
	+ Enter the date of the observation.
* **Observation Time**: A field for the time of the observation.
	+ Enter the time of the observation.
* **Submission Date**: A field for the date of submission.
	+ Enter the date the media was submitted.
* **Submission Time**: A field for the time of submission.
	+ Enter the time the media was submitted.
* **Latitude**: A field for the latitude of the observation location.
	+ Enter the latitude of the observation location.
* **Longitude**: A field for the longitude of the observation location.
	+ Enter the longitude of the observation location.
* **Comment**: A field for adding any additional comments.
	+ Type in any additional comments or notes related to the observation.
