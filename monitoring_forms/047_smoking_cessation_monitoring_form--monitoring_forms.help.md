# Smoking Cessation Monitoring Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Smoking Cessation Monitoring Form is designed to collect information about a smoker's habits, quit dates, coping strategies, triggers, and medication use. This form will help track the smoker's progress and provide a baseline for follow-up evaluations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the smoker's information in the "Smoker Information" field.
2. Select all the current habits the smoker is experiencing in the "Current Habits" field.
3. Enter the date the smoker quit smoking in the "Quit Date" field.
4. Explain the reason for quitting in the "Quit Reason" field.
5. Describe the strategies the smoker is using to cope with cravings and other challenges in the "Coping Strategies" field.
6. Select all the triggers that cause the smoker to crave or smoke in the "Triggers" field.
7. Select the medication the smoker is using to aid in their quit attempt in the "Medication Use" field.
8. Schedule a follow-up appointment with the smoker in the "Follow-up" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Smoker Information (smoker_info)**: Enter the smoker's name, age, and any other relevant information.
* **Current Habits (habits)**: Select all the current habits the smoker is experiencing, such as cigarettes, cigars, and joints.
* **Quit Date (quit_date)**: Enter the date the smoker quit smoking.
* **Quit Reason (quit_reason)**: Explain why the smoker quit, such as health concerns or family pressure.
* **Coping Strategies (coping_strategies)**: Describe the strategies the smoker is using to cope with cravings and other challenges, such as meditation or exercise.
* **Triggers (triggers)**: Select all the triggers that cause the smoker to crave or smoke, such as social situations or certain foods.
* **Medication Use (medication_use)**: Select the medication the smoker is using to aid in their quit attempt, such as Chantix or no medication.
* **Follow-up (follow_up)**: Schedule a follow-up appointment with the smoker to check on their progress.
