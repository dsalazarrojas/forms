# Noise Monitoring Device Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect consent from users to monitor their Noise Monitoring Devices, providing essential information and consent for the monitoring process. The form captures details such as name, phone number, email, location, device model, device serial number, and additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the required fields**: Provide your name, phone number, email, location, device model, device serial number, and date and time of consent.
2. **Select relevant options**: Choose your consent status for each relevant option, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.
3. **Add any additional notes**: If you have any additional information or comments, use the 'Additional Notes' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent** (`consent`, `text`, **required**): Provide your consent for the noise monitoring device by typing 'Yes' in this field.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your name as it should be displayed on the monitoring report.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, **optional**): Provide your location for the monitoring report.
* **Device Model** (`device_model`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the model of your noise monitoring device.
* **Device Serial Number** (`device_serial_number`, `text`, **required**): Enter the serial number of your noise monitoring device.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `note`, **optional**): Add any additional comments or information about your device.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose 'Yes' if you have selected one device model.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Choose 'Yes' if you have selected multiple device models.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date of your consent.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, **required**): Enter the time of your consent.
* **Noise Level** (`noise_level`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the noise level of your device (if applicable).
* **Device Status** (`device_status`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose whether your device is Active or Inactive.
* **Device Model Select One** (`device_model_select_one`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose whether you have selected one device model.
* **Device Model Select Multiple** (`device_model_select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Choose whether you have selected multiple device models.
* **Device Type** (`device_type`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose whether your device is Active or Inactive.
* **Device Status 2** (`device_status_2`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose whether your device is Active or Inactive.
* **Select Multiple 2** (`select_multiple_2`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Choose whether you have selected multiple devices.
* **Select One 2** (`select_one_2`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose whether you have selected one device.
* **Date 2** (`date_2`, `date`, **optional**): Enter the date of your second device (if applicable).
* **Time 2** (`time_2`, `time`, **optional**): Enter the time of your second device (if applicable).
* **Decimal 2** (`decimal_2`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the decimal level of your second device (if applicable).
* **Minimalist 2** (`minimalist_2`, `note`, **optional**): Add any additional comments or information about your second device.
