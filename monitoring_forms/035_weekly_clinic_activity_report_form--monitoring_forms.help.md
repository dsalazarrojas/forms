# Weekly Clinic Activity Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Clinic Activity Report Form is used to record and report the activities and performances of a clinic over a week. This form is required to be filled out by clinic staff at the end of each week and is used to track and analyze clinic activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Make sure you have permission to fill out this form. This form should only be completed by authorized clinic staff members.
2. Ensure you have access to a computer or mobile device with a stable internet connection.
3. Select the correct staff present for the week (e.g., Manager, Doctor, Nurse).
4. Enter the correct start and end times of the clinic.
5. Enter the number of patients seen and consultations conducted during the week.
6. Provide your email address and phone number for communication with the manager.
7. Enter any comments or notes about the week's activities.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Present** (`Staff Present`, `select_multiple`, **required**):
  This field is used to select the type of staff present at the clinic during the week. Select all that apply from the available options.
* **Week Start Date** (`Week Start Date`, `date`, **required**):
  Enter the date when the week started. This will help in determining which week's activities are being reported.
* **Patients Seen** (`Patients Seen`, `number`, **required**):
  Enter the number of patients seen during the week.
* **Consultations Conducted** (`Consultations Conducted`, `number`, **required**):
  Enter the number of consultations conducted during the week.
* **Clinic Start Time** (`Clinic Start Time`, `time`, **required**):
  Enter the time when the clinic started for the week.
* **Clinic End Time** (`Clinic End Time`, `time`, **required**):
  Enter the time when the clinic ended for the week.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, **optional**):
  Use this field for any additional comments or notes about the week's activities.
* **Email for Manager** (`Email for Manager`, `email`, **required**):
  Enter your email address for the manager to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, **required**):
  Enter your phone number for the manager to contact you.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, **optional**):
  Use this field for any additional comments or observations about the week's activities.
* **Staff Comments** (`Staff Comments`, `text`, **optional**):
  Use this field for any additional comments or observations from the staff about the week's activities.
* **Email for Manager** and **Phone Number** fields are for the manager's contact information. Please ensure they are accurate to facilitate communication.
