# Rehabilitation Center Daily Activity Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is designed to document daily activities, interactions, and progress of residents in a rehabilitation center. It helps staff members track and record important information about each resident's day, allowing for timely and accurate reporting and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have access to the form and understand its purpose.
2. Choose the correct session date.
3. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information about the resident's daily activities and progress.
4. Add any relevant notes or observations about the resident's behavior, interactions, or progress.
5. Confirm the next steps and actions to be taken regarding the resident's care.
