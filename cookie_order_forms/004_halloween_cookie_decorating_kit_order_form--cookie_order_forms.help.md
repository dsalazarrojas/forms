# Halloween Cookie Decorating Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help customers order Halloween-themed cookie decorating kits. The form is used to collect order details, shipping information, and other relevant information to process the order correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your shipping address in the "Shipping Address" field.
2. Select the Halloween kit ingredients you would like to purchase in the "Halloween Kit Ingredients" field.
3. Choose the Halloween decorations you would like to include in your kit in the "Halloween Decorations" field.
4. Enter your preferred shipping date in the "Shipping Date" field.
5. Enter your preferred shipping time in the "Shipping Time" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
8. Add any additional notes about your order in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Form** (`holiday_form`, text, optional): This is the main order form where you can enter any details about your order.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address so that we can deliver your order correctly.
* **Halloween Kit Ingredients** (`holiday_form_ingredients`, select multiple, optional): Choose the ingredients you would like to purchase for your kit.
	+ Options: Decorating Kit, Cookie Sticks, Fondant
* **Halloween Decorations** (`holiday_form_decorations`, select one, optional): Choose one of the Halloween decorations you would like to include in your kit.
	+ Options: Bats, Ghosts, Pumpkins
* **Shipping Date** (`shipping_date`, date, optional): Enter your preferred shipping date for your order.
* **Shipping Time** (`shipping_time`, time, optional): Enter your preferred shipping time for your order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you about your order.
* **Notes** (`holiday_form_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your order.
