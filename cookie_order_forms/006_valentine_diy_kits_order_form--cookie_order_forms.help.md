# valentine_diy_kits_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Valentine's Day DIY kits order form is designed to collect customer information and order details for Valentine's Day DIY kits.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer information section with your contact details and preferences.
2. Provide the gift details, including the type of kit and any customization requests.
3. Enter your shipping information, including the delivery address and any special instructions.
4. Confirm your payment information to ensure successful order processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your name, email address, and phone number so we can contact you about your order.
- **Gift Details** (`gift_details`, `text`, optional): Describe the type of Valentine's Day DIY kit you're interested in, including any customization requests you have.
- **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your shipping address and any special instructions for delivery.
- **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your payment method and details to complete your order.
- **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, `text`, optional): Confirm your order details to ensure accuracy.

## Tips

- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Use a valid email address and phone number so we can contact you about your order.
- Make sure to include all required information for successful order processing.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
