# Color Street Order Form - Get Polished With Tami - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Color Street Order Form is designed to collect necessary information from customers to place an order with the Get Polished With Tami brand. This form helps us understand your preferences and requirements for a seamless shopping experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure a smooth and accurate order processing, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details, including your name and email address.
2. Enter your shipping address.
3. Select your preferred Glitter sets and Solid sets.
4. Indicate if you are taking advantage of the Buy 3 Get 1 Free deal.
5. Choose your payment method and enter your payment handle.
6. Select your shipping method.
7. Provide any special notes or referrals (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Section 1: Your Information
* **Your Name** (1, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Your Email** (2, email, required): Enter your email address for communication and order updates.
* **Your Shipping Address** (4, text, required): Provide your shipping address for efficient delivery.

### Section 2: Product Preferences
* **Which Glitter sets would you like?** (6, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more Glitter sets from the available options.
* **Which Solid sets would you like?** (7, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more Solid sets from the available options.

### Section 3: Order Details
* **Are you taking advantage of the Buy 3 Get 1 Free deal?** (8, select_one, required): Indicate if you are taking advantage of the offer.
* **Who referred you to Tami?** (13, text, optional): If someone referred you, please provide their name or reference number.

### Section 4: Payment and Shipping
* **How do you want to pay?** (10, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method from the available options.
* **Payment Handle** (11, text, required): Enter your payment handle for secure processing.
* **Shipping Choice** (12, select_one, required): Select your preferred shipping method.

### Tips
* Double-check your information and preferences before submitting the form.
* If you have any special notes or questions, please include them in the "Special Notes for Tami" field.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact our support team for assistance.
