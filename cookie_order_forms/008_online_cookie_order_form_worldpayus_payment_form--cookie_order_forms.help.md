# Online Cookie Order Form WorldPayUS Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Cookie Order Form is designed for customers to place orders for custom cookies. It guides the customer through a step-by-step process to complete their order.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Cookie Order Form" page with your cookie selection and quantity.
2. Move on to the "Payment Form" page and fill out the required fields.
3. Select your payment method and amount for each payment method.
4. Review and submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cookie Order Form** (`cookie_order_form`, text, required): Select the type of cookie you want to order from the list of available options.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of cookies you want to order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with order updates.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with order updates.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date you want to pick up your order.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select the time you want to pick up your order.
* **Payment Method 1** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your payment method (PayPal, Credit Card, or Cash).
* **Amount 1** (`amount`, number, required): Enter the amount to pay for this payment method.
* **Payment Method 2** (`payment_method_2`, select_one, required): Select your second payment method (PayPal, Credit Card, or Cash).
* **Amount 2** (`amount_2`, number, required): Enter the amount to pay for this payment method.
* **Payment Method 3** (`payment_method_3`, select_one, required): Select your third payment method (PayPal, Credit Card, or Cash).
* **Amount 3** (`amount_3`, number, required): Enter the amount to pay for this payment method.
* **Payment Method 4** (`payment_method_4`, select_one, required): Select your fourth payment method (PayPal, Credit Card, or Cash).
* **Amount 4** (`amount_4`, number, required): Enter the amount to pay for this payment method.
* **Payment Method 5** (`payment_method_5`, select_one, required): Select your fifth payment method (PayPal, Credit Card, or Cash).
* **Amount 5** (`amount_5`, number, required): Enter the amount to pay for this payment method.
* **Payment Method 6** (`payment_method_6`, select_one, required): Select your sixth payment method (PayPal, Credit Card, or Cash).
* **Amount 6** (`amount_6`, number, required): Enter the amount to pay for this payment method.
* **Payment Method 7** (`payment_method_7`, select_one, required): Select your seventh payment method (PayPal, Credit Card, or Cash).
* **Amount 7** (`amount_7`, number, required): Enter the amount to pay for this payment method.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Review and submit your order.
