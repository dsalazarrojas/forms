# Pumpkin Cheesecake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers interested in placing an order for pumpkin cheesecakes. Please fill out the form with your details to ensure we can process your order correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. Make sure to provide accurate details to avoid any issues with your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed on your order.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed on your order.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and notifications about your order.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, `number`, required: false): If you have a specific order ID, please enter it here. This will help us associate your order with your name.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like to receive your order on.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you would like to receive your order on.
* **Number Of Pumpkin Cheesecakes** (`number_of_pumpkins`, `number`, required: false): Please specify the correct number of pumpkin cheesecakes you would like to order.
* **Type of Pumpkin** (`type_of_pumpkin`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of pumpkin you would like to have on your cheesecake. Options are:
	+ Pumpkin
	+ Squash
	+ Gourd
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `note`, required: false): If you have any special requests or notes about your order, please enter them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address for correct spelling and formatting.
* If you have any issues with your order, please contact us via the provided email.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please enter them in the "Special Instructions" field.
