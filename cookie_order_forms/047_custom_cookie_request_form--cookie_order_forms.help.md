# Custom Cookie Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers request custom cookies for their special occasions or treats. Please fill in your cookie preferences and any special requests you may have, and we'll do our best to accommodate them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the type of cookie you would like to request, such as chocolate chip or sugar cookies.
2. Choose the size of the cookies you would like, including small, medium, large, or extra large.
3. If you have any special requests, such as custom messages or flavors, enter them in the "Special Requests" field.
4. Select the delivery date and time for your cookies, if you have a specific request.
5. Finally, add any additional special instructions or notes to help us understand your cookie preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cookie Type** (`cookie_type`, select_one): Choose the type of cookie you would like to request, such as chocolate chip or sugar cookies.
* **Cookie Size** (`cookie_size`, select_multiple): Select the size of the cookies you would like, including small, medium, large, or extra large.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text): If you have any special requests, such as custom messages or flavors, enter them here.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date): Select the date you would like to receive your cookies.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time): Select the time you would like to receive your cookies.
* **Cookie Message** (`cookie_message`, text): Add any personal message or message you would like to include with your cookies.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, note): Add any additional special instructions or notes to help us understand your cookie preferences.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, as indicated by the required status next to each field.
* If you have any specific dietary restrictions or allergies, please let us know in the "Special Requests" field.
* If you would like to cancel or change your order, please contact us directly.
