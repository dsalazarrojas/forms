# Girl Scout Cookie - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Girl Scout Cookie form is used to collect order details from customers. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to process orders efficiently and generate accurate receipts or confirmations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name in the **First name** field.
2. Enter your Last name in the **Last name** field.
3. Provide your address in the **Address 1** field, which is the main address line.
4. Add any additional address information in the **Address 2** field, if needed.
5. Enter your City in the **City** field.
6. Enter your State in the **State** field.
7. Enter your Zip code in the **Zip** field.
8. If applicable, select a date for your order in the **Order date** field.
9. If applicable, select a time for your order in the **Order time** field.
10. Specify the number of cookies you would like to order in the **Cookies** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is spelled.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is spelled.
* **Address 1** (`address1`, text, required/optional): Enter your main address line, including house or apartment number if applicable.
* **Address 2** (`address2`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional address information, such as a suite or apartment number.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the city where you reside or your delivery location.
* **State** (`state`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the state where you reside or your delivery location.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required/optional): Enter your zip code, as required for shipping and delivery purposes.
* **Order date** (`order_date`, date, required/optional): If you have a specific date for your order, select it here. This is not required for immediate orders.
* **Order time** (`order_time`, time, required/optional): If you have a specific time for your order, select it here. This is not required for immediate orders.
* **Cookies** (`cookies`, number, required/optional): Specify the number of cookies you would like to order.
