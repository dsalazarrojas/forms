# Father's Day Cookie Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather order information for Father's Day cookies. Use this guide to ensure you complete the form correctly and provide accurate order details.

## How To Complete This Form
* To start, select the type of cookie you would like to order. Choose from Circle, Heart, Square, or Star.
* Select the size of the cookie you would like, such as Small, Medium, or Large.
* Enter a message to include with your order, if desired.
* Choose the date you would like to receive your cookies.
* Select the time you would like to receive your cookies.
* Enter your name and phone number so we can contact you.
* Add any additional comments or information about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cookie Shape** (`cookie_shape`, select_one, required: false): Choose the shape of the cookie you would like to order.
* **Cookie Size** (`cookie_size`, select_one, required: false): Select the size of the cookie you would like.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required: false): Enter a message to include with your order, if desired.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false): Choose the date you would like to receive your cookies.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required: false): Select the time you would like to receive your cookies.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name so we can contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or information about your order.
