# R_and_D_Cooking_Corner - Help Guide
## Purpose
The R_and_D_Cooking_Corner form is designed to collect information about user interactions with the cookie corner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "First page" section, which is optional.
2. Select one of the options for "Category" to indicate whether you have interacted with the cookie corner.
3. Enter your "First name" and "Last name" in the text fields.
4. Enter your "Email" in the format of a standard email address.
5. Enter your "Phone" number in the format of a standard phone number.
6. Select a "Date" for the interaction.
7. Select a "Time" for the interaction.
8. Enter any additional "Note" about your interaction.
9. Finally, select whether you have placed a "Cookie Orders".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First page** (`r_and_d_cooking_corner_first_page`, optional): This is the first page of the form, which is optional.
* **Category** (Category, optional): Select whether you have interacted with the cookie corner.
	+ "Yes" or "No" options are available.
* **First name** (`First name`, optional): Enter your first name in this text field.
* **Last name** (`Last name`, optional): Enter your last name in this text field.
* **Email** (`Email`, optional): Enter your email address in this format: `user@example.com`.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, optional): Enter your phone number in the standard format.
* **Date** (`Date`, optional): Select a date for the interaction.
* **Time** (`Time`, optional): Select a time for the interaction.
* **Note** (`Note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your interaction.
* **Cookie Orders** (`Cookie Orders`, optional): Select whether you have placed a cookie order.
	+ "Yes" or "No" options are available.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for assistance.
* This form is not intended for any other purpose than gathering information about user interactions with the cookie corner.
