# 2022 ABC Baker Girl Scout Cookie Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 2022 ABC Baker Girl Scout Cookie Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for their cookie orders. This form will help us process your order accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information: Enter your full name, email address, and phone number to help us contact you about your order.
2. Select your cookie preferences: Choose the packs of cookies you'd like to order and the delivery method you prefer.
3. Choose your payment method: Select how you'd like to pay for your order.
4. Add any special requests or comments: If you have any special instructions or comments, this is the place to add them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`customer_full_name`, text, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email address** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can send you order confirmation.
* **Phone number** (`customer_phone`, phone, optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you about your order.
* **Troop number** (`troop_number`, text, optional): If you know the troop number of the Girl Scout selling to you, please enter it.
* **Samoa cookies (packs)** (`cookie_samoa`, integer, optional): Select the number of Samoa cookies you'd like to order.
* **Thin Mints (packs)** (`cookie_thin_mints`, integer, optional): Select the number of Thin Mints you'd like to order.
* **Peanut Butter Patties (packs)** (`cookie_peanut_butter_patrol`, integer, optional): Select the number of Peanut Butter Patties you'd like to order.
* **Trefoils (packs)** (`cookie_trefoils`, integer, optional): Select the number of Trefoils you'd like to order.
* **Daisy cookies (packs)** (`cookie_daisies`, integer, optional): Select the number of Daisy cookies you'd like to order.
* **Lemonades (packs)** (`cookie_lemonades`, integer, optional): Select the number of Lemonades you'd like to order.
* **Chocolate Chip cookies (packs)** (`cookie_chocolate_chip`, integer, optional): Select the number of Chocolate Chip cookies you'd like to order.
* **Sugar Cookies (packs)** (`cookie_sugar_cookies`, integer, optional): Select the number of Sugar Cookies you'd like to order.
* **Favorites (packs)** (`cookie_favorites`, integer, optional): Select the number of Favorites cookies you'd like to order.
* **Preferred delivery method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to receive your cookies: Standard delivery, Express delivery, Pickup from school, Pickup from community center, or Curbside pickup.
* **Delivery address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): If choosing delivery option, enter your full address.
* **Preferred delivery date** (`delivery_date_preference`, date, optional): Enter the date you'd like to receive your cookies.
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to pay for your order: Cash, Check, Credit card, Digital payment (Venmo/PayPal), Money order.
* **Special delivery instructions** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Add any special instructions for delivery or pickup.
* **Additional comments** (`order_comments`, text, optional): Add any questions, feedback, or other notes.
* **Order date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you're placing your order.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter all required fields to ensure we can process your order accurately.
* If you have any special requests, please add them in the "Special delivery instructions" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
