# halloween_candy_gram_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for a Halloween-themed event. It will ask for your contact information, candy preferences, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the contact information section**: Please enter your first name, last name, email, phone number, and address in the corresponding fields.
2. **Choose your candy options**: Select your preferred candies from the available options (yes, no, other).
3. **Select your payment type**: Choose how you would like to pay for your order.
4. **Add any additional notes**: If you have any special requests or comments, please type them in the note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Address**: Enter your home or office address where we will ship your order.
* **City**: Enter the city where you would like your order to be shipped.
* **State**: Select the state where you would like your order to be shipped.
* **Candy 1**: Choose whether you prefer candy 1 (Yes, No, or Other).
* **Candy 2**: Choose whether you prefer candy 2 (Yes, No, or Other).
* **Date**: If you have a specific date you would like your order to be shipped, enter it here.
* **Time**: If you have a specific time you would like your order to be shipped, enter it here.
* **Note**: If you have any special requests or comments, please type them in this field.
* **Payment Type**: Choose how you would like to pay for your order (Cash, Credit, or Bank).
* **Field 8**: Not used in this form, please disregard.
* **Candy 2**: Not used in this form, please disregard.
* **Payment Type (Field 8)**: This field is not used to confirm that the payment type is required, please enter 'Credit'.
