# personal_graduation_planning_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "personal_graduation_planning_form" is used for tracking personal graduation planning information. This form captures details such as graduation date, expected major, graduation location, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the graduation date by entering the date on which you plan to graduate.
2. Select your expected major from the list of available options.
3. Choose the location where you plan to graduate from the list of available options.
4. Select the type of graduation you plan to have from the list of available options.
5. Choose your graduation status from the list of available options.
6. If desired, enter any additional comments or information about your graduation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **graduation_date** (id: 2, type: date): Enter the date on which you plan to graduate.
* **expected_major** (id: 3, type: select_multiple): Select your expected major from the list of available options.
* **graduation_location** (id: 4, type: select_multiple): Choose the location where you plan to graduate from the list of available options.
* **graduation_type** (id: 5, type: select_one): Select the type of graduation you plan to have from the list of available options.
* **graduation_status** (id: 6, type: select_one): Choose your graduation status from the list of available options.
* **graduation_comment** (id: 7, type: note): Enter any additional comments or information about your graduation.
* **graduation_phone** (id: 8, type: text): Enter your contact phone number.
* **graduation_email** (id: 9, type: email): Enter your email address.
* **graduation_address** (id: 10, type: text): Enter your address.
* **graduation_postal_code** (id: 11, type: text): Enter your postal code.
* **graduation_city** (id: 12, type: text): Enter your city.
* **graduation_state** (id: 13, type: text): Enter your state.
* **graduation_country** (id: 14, type: text): Enter your country.
* **graduation_postal_code2** (id: 15, type: text): Enter your postal code (second instance).
* **graduation_note** (id: 16, type: note): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **graduation_additional_info** (id: 17, type: note): Enter any additional information about your graduation.
