# Greenhouse Emission Tracking Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Greenhouse Emission Tracking Report Form is designed to collect data on greenhouse gas emissions, reduction actions, and supporting reports for environmental or scientific research. This form helps organizations, researchers, or individuals track and analyze environmental impact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Greenhouse Emission Input" page and provide information on the emissions data you are tracking.
2. Move to the "Reduction Actions" page and describe the actions taken to reduce emissions.
3. On the "Supporting Reports" page, upload any relevant reports or documents that support your data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Greenhouse Emission Input** (`Greenhouse Emission Input`, text, optional): Enter information on the emissions data you are tracking, such as carbon dioxide, methane, or other greenhouse gases. Be as specific as possible with your input, including units and timestamps.
* **Reduction Actions** (`Reduction Actions`, text, optional): Describe the actions taken to reduce greenhouse gas emissions, including strategies, initiatives, or projects implemented. Be sure to include details on effectiveness and impact.
* **Supporting Reports** (`Supporting Reports`, text, optional): Upload or paste any relevant reports, studies, or documents that support your emissions data and reduction actions. This could include images, videos, or other media.
