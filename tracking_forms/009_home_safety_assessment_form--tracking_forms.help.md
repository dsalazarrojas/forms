# Home Safety Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Safety Assessment Form is a tool designed to collect information about the safety features and conditions of a home. This form is used to gather data on various aspects of a home's safety, including electrical, plumbing, and HVAC systems, as well as other critical features such as fire suppression and emergency exits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and select the options that accurately reflect the safety features of your home.
2. For each field, select the options that apply to your home.
3. Use the text fields to provide additional information if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Floor** (`first_floor`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks if there is a first floor in your home. Select "Yes" if your home has a first floor, and "No" if it does not.
* **Electrical** (`electrical`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks about the electrical safety features in your home. Select "Yes" if your home has electrical outlets, circuit breakers, or other electrical systems, and "No" if it does not.
* **Plumbing** (`plumbing`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks about the plumbing safety features in your home. Select "Yes" if your home has water supply lines, toilets, sinks, or other plumbing systems, and "No" if it does not.
* **HVAC** (`hvac`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks about the heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) systems in your home. Select "Yes" if your home has heating or cooling systems, and "No" if it does not.
* **Foundation** (`foundation`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional information about your home's foundation.
* **Roof** (`roof`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional information about your home's roof.
* **Exterior** (`exterior`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional information about your home's exterior.
* **Interior** (`interior`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional information about your home's interior.
* **Fire Suppression** (`fire_suppression`, select_one, required: false): This field asks about the presence of a fire suppression system in your home. Select "Yes" if you have a fire suppression system, and "No" if you do not.
* **Emergency Exits** (`emergency_exits`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks about the number and condition of emergency exits in your home. Select "Yes" if you have multiple emergency exits, and "No" if you do not.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can always contact your local authorities or a professional for guidance.
* Take your time to carefully review each field and provide accurate information about your home's safety features.
* If you need to add additional information, use the text fields to provide more details.
