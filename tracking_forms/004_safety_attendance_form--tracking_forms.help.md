# Safety Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Attendance Form is designed to track and record attendance at safety meetings. The form is intended to be completed by authorized personnel to ensure accurate and efficient tracking of meeting attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the date of the meeting in the "Meeting Date" field.
2. Enter the time of the meeting in the "Meeting Time" field.
3. Enter the attendee's name and other relevant information in the "Attendee" field.
4. Indicate if the attendee attended the meeting by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Attended" field.
5. Enter any comments or notes about the meeting in the "Comments" field.
6. Enter the attendee's email address in the "Email" field.
7. Enter the attendee's phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Enter any comments or notes from the meeting manager in the "Manager Comments" field.
9. Enter the manager's name in the "Manager" field.
10. Enter the manager's email address in the "Manager Email" field.
11. Enter the manager's phone number in the "Manager Phone" field.
12. Enter the department where the meeting took place in the "Department" field.
13. Enter the location where the meeting took place in the "Location" field.
14. Enter the meeting location details in the "Meeting Location" field.
15. Enter any additional notes about the meeting in the "Notes" field.
16. Select the type of safety meeting (e.g., "Weekly", "Monthly", etc.) in the "Safety Meeting Type" field.
17. Select the frequency of the meeting (e.g., "Daily", "Weekly", etc.) in the "Meeting Frequency" field.
18. Select the status of the meeting (e.g., "Active", "Inactive", etc.) in the "Meeting Status" field.
19. Enter the duration of the meeting in the "Meeting Duration" field.
20. Enter the end date of the meeting in the "Meeting Date End" field.
21. Enter the start time of the meeting in the "Meeting Start Time" field.
22. Enter the end time of the meeting in the "Meeting End Time" field.
23. Select the status of the meeting (e.g., "Active", "Completed", etc.) in the "Meeting Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Date**: Enter the date of the meeting, in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Meeting Time**: Enter the time of the meeting in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Attendee**: Enter the name of the attendee.
* **Attended**: Select "True" if the attendee attended the meeting, and "False" otherwise.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the meeting.
* **Email**: Enter the attendee's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the attendee's phone number.
* **Manager Comments**: Enter any comments or notes from the meeting manager.
* **Manager**: Enter the manager's name.
* **Manager Email**: Enter the manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter the manager's phone number.
* **Department**: Enter the department where the meeting took place.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the meeting took place.
* **Meeting Location**: Enter the details of the meeting location.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the meeting.
* **Safety Meeting Type**: Select the type of safety meeting (e.g., "Weekly", "Monthly", etc.).
* **Meeting Frequency**: Select the frequency of the meeting (e.g., "Daily", "Weekly", etc.).
* **Meeting Status**: Select the status of the meeting (e.g., "Active", "Inactive", etc.).
* **Meeting Duration**: Enter the duration of the meeting.
* **Meeting Date End**: Enter the end date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Start Time**: Enter the start time of the meeting.
* **Meeting End Time**: Enter the end time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Status**: Select the status of the meeting (e.g., "Active", "Completed", etc.).
