# Wrist Stretching Exercise Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wrist Stretching Exercise Log is a form used to record and track information about wrist stretching exercises. This form helps users document their progress and provides a record of their workouts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the date you completed the exercise.
- Select the duration of the exercise.
- Select the number of sets and repeats you completed for each exercise.
- Choose the muscles you stretched during the exercise.
- Add any additional notes about the exercise.
- Optionally, enter your phone number or email for contact purposes.
- Select who assigned you the exercise, whether it be a trainer or yourself.
- (Optional) Enter the session ID and Session Date if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **date** (`date`, Required: false): The date you completed the exercise. Please enter the date in the correct format.
* **duration** (`number`, Required: false): Select the duration of the exercise.
* **Sets Completed** (`select_one`, Required: true): Choose the number of sets you completed. You can select 1, 2, or 3 sets.
* **Repeats Completed** (`select_multiple`, Required: false): Choose the repeats you completed. You can select 1, 2, or 3 repeats.
* **Muscle Stretched** (`select_multiple`, Required: true): Choose the muscles you stretched during the exercise. Select Flexor Carpi Ulnaris, Extensor Pollicis, Extensor Carpi Radialis, Pronator Teres, or Flexor Carpi Radialis.
* **notes** (`note`, Required: false): Add any additional notes about the exercise.
* **phone** (`text`, Required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **email** (`email`, Required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Assigned** (`select_one`, Required: true): Choose who assigned you the exercise, whether it be a Trainer or yourself.
* **Session ID** (`number`, Required: false): (Optional) Enter the session ID if applicable.
* **Session Date** (`date`, Required: false): (Optional) Enter the session date if applicable.
