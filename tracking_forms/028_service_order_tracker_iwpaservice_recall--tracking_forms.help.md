# service_order_tracker_iwpaservice_recall - Help Guide
## Purpose
The service order tracker is a form used to capture and track information related to services provided to customers. It is designed to facilitate collaboration and data consistency among team members.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant "Service Order" field to begin entering information for the specific service you are tracking.
2. Select the service type to indicate the type of service provided.
3. Enter the service date and time.
4. Add any additional service location information.
5. Enter the contact details of the person providing the service (service contact).
6. Enter the phone number of the person providing the service (service phone).
7. Enter the customer's name.
8. Enter the customer's email address.
9. Enter the customer's phone number.
10. Enter the customer's address.
11. Add any additional notes related to the service provided.
12. Enter the tracking number for the service order.
13. Select the person assigned to handle the service order.
14. Enter any additional notes for the assigned person.
15. Select the status of the service order (e.g., completed, ongoing, etc.).
16. Enter any additional notes for the status.
17. Enter any additional notes for the service order.
18. Enter any additional notes for the tracking number.
19. Enter any additional notes for the assigned person.
20. Enter any additional notes for the status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Time**: (<id: 4, name: service_time, type: time, required: false>): Enter the time of the service provided.
