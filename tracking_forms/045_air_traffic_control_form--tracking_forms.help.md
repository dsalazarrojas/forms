# Air Traffic Control Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about air traffic control operations, including controller details, shift information, and aircraft data. This form is used to record and track various aspects of air traffic control, such as clearances, handovers, and incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your controller name and ID.
2. Enter the shift ID and sector/position of the controller.
3. Enter the date of the operation.
4. Enter the aircraft callsign and type.
5. Enter the flight level/altitude and heading.
6. Select the clearance issued to the aircraft, such as "Cleared for Takeoff" or "Cleared to Land".
7. Enter the handover time in UTC.
8. Provide any notable notes or incidents, such as go-arounds or deviations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Controller Name** (Controller Name): Enter the name of the controller.
  This field is used to identify the controller who is responsible for the operation.
  (Required)

- **Shift ID** (Shift ID): Enter the ID of the shift, e.g., "Morning-A".
  This field is used to identify the shift the controller is working on.
  (Required)

- **Sector / Position** (Sector/Position): Enter the sector or position of the controller, e.g., "Tower" or "Ground".
  This field is used to identify the specific area of operation.
  (Required)

- **Date** (Date): Enter the date of the operation in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
  This field is used to record the date of the operation.
  (Required)

- **Aircraft Callsign** (Aircraft Callsign): Enter the callsign of the aircraft, e.g., "UAL123".
  This field is used to identify the aircraft.
  (Required)

- **Aircraft Type** (Aircraft Type): Enter the type of the aircraft, e.g., "B737".
  This field is used to identify the type of aircraft.
  (Required)

- **Flight Level / Altitude** (Flight Level/Altitude): Enter the flight level or altitude of the aircraft, e.g., "FL350" or "3000ft".
  This field is used to record the flight level or altitude of the aircraft.
  (Required)

- **Heading** (Heading): Enter the magnetic heading of the aircraft, 0-360 degrees.
  This field is used to record the magnetic heading of the aircraft.
  (Optional)

- **Squawk Code** (Squawk Code): Enter the transponder code of the aircraft, e.g., "4521".
  This field is used to record the transponder code of the aircraft.
  (Optional)

- **Clearance** (Clearance): Select the clearance issued to the aircraft, e.g., "Cleared for Takeoff" or "Cleared to Land".
  This field is used to record the clearance issued to the aircraft.
  (Required)

- **Handover Time** (Handover Time): Enter the handover time in UTC.
  This field is used to record the handover time.
  (Required)

- **Notes / Incidents** (Notes/Incidents): Enter any notable notes or incidents, such as go-arounds or deviations.
  This field is used to record any notable notes or incidents.
  (Optional)
