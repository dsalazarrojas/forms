# Mpox Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Investigation Form is designed to track and document information about mpox cases, including patient demographics, case confirmation status, contact tracing information, and investigation status. This form helps healthcare professionals and contact tracers to accurately and efficiently track mpox cases.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure that all required fields are completed.
2. Enter the patient's Mpox Case Identifier in the "Mpox Case Identifier" field.
3. Enter the date and time of the patient's symptoms onset in the "Date of Onset" field.
4. Enter the patient's age in the "Patient Age" field.
5. Select all relevant contact tracing options in the "Contact Trace Form" field.
6. Enter the patient's travel history in the "Travel History" field.
7. Select the case confirmation status in the "Case Confirmation Status" field.
8. Select the contact tracing form status in the "Contact Trace Form Status" field.
9. Select the laboratory test results in the "Laboratory Test Form" field.
10. Enter the date and time of case confirmation in the "Case Confirmation Date" and "Case Confirmation Time" fields.
11. Enter the date and time of contact tracing form completion in the "Contact Trace Form Completion Date" and "Contact Trace Form Completion Time" fields.
12. Enter the patient's current location and address in the "Patient Location" and "Patient Address" fields.
13. List all household contacts in the "Household Contacts" field.
14. Enter the number of household contacts in the "Household Contacts Count" field.
15. List all community contacts in the "Community Contacts" field.
16. Enter the number of community contacts in the "Community Contacts Count" field.
17. Enter the country of the patient's first travel in the last 30 days in the "Travel History 1" field.
18. List all travel prophets in the "Travel Prophets" field.
19. Enter the country of the patient's second travel in the last 30 days in the "Travel History 2" field.
20. List all travel prophets in the "Travel Prophets 2" field.
21. List all patient symptoms in the "Patient Symptoms" field.
22. Enter the number of patient symptoms in the "Patient Symptoms Affected" field.
23. Select the investigation status in the "Investigation Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mpox Case Identifier** (1, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the mpox case.
* **Date of Onset** (2, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the patient's symptoms onset.
* **Patient Age** (3, number, optional): Enter the patient's age.
* **Contact Trace Form** (4, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant contact tracing options, including:
	+ Case confirmed in the household
	+ Case confirmed in the community
	+ Case confirmed in a healthcare setting
* **Travel History** (5, text, optional): Enter the country of the patient's travel, if applicable.
* **Case Confirmation Status** (6, select_one, optional): Select one of the following:
	+ Confirmed
	+ Likely Confirmed
	+ Likely Not Confirmed
* **Contact Trace Form Status** (7, select_one, optional): Select one of the following:
	+ Completed
	+ Not Completed
* **Laboratory Test Form** (8, select_one, optional): Select one of the following:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Pending Results
* **Case Confirmation Date** (9, date, optional): Enter the date of case confirmation.
* **Case Confirmation Time** (10, time, optional): Enter the time of case confirmation.
* **Contact Trace Form Completion Date** (11, date, optional): Enter the date of contact tracing form completion.
* **Contact Trace Form Completion Time** (12, time, optional): Enter the time of contact tracing form completion.
* **Patient Location** (13, text, optional): Enter the patient's current location.
* **Patient Address** (14, text, optional): Enter the patient's current or recent address.
* **Household Contacts** (15, text, optional): List all household contacts.
* **Household Contacts Count** (16, number, optional): Enter the number of household contacts.
* **Community Contacts** (17, text, optional): List all community contacts.
* **Community Contacts Count** (18, number, optional): Enter the number of community contacts.
* **Travel History 1** (19, text, optional): Enter the country of the patient's first travel in the last 30 days.
* **Travel Prophets** (20, text, optional): List all travel prophets.
* **Travel History 2** (21, text, optional): Enter the country of the patient's second travel in the last 30 days.
* **Travel Prophets 2** (22, text, optional): List all travel prophets.
* **Patient Symptoms** (23, text, optional): List all patient symptoms.
* **Patient Symptoms Affected** (24, number, optional): Enter the number of patient symptoms.
* **Investigation Status** (25, select_one, optional): Select one of the following:
	+ Completed
	+ Not Completed
