# Verses Read Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Verses Read Tracking Form is designed to record and track the reader's progress in reading verses. This form helps keep a record of the date verses are read and the number of verses completed. Users can enter their user ID and date to track progress, and provide a reflection or note for each verse read.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user ID, if required to do so.
2. Select or enter the date you finished reading the verses.
3. Enter the number of verses you have read.
4. Provide a brief reflection or note about your reading experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User ID**: A unique identifier for your account. This is optional but recommended for tracking purposes.
* **Date**: The date you finished reading the verses. Use the calendar tool to select the correct date.
* **Verses**: The number of verses you have read. Please enter the correct number.
* **Reflection**: A brief note or reflection about your reading experience. Please keep it concise and to the point.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information for tracking purposes.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact your administrator.
* This form is for personal use only, and all data will be kept confidential.
