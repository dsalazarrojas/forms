# Material Movement Audit Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about material movements within the organization. It helps to track the transfer of materials between different warehouses and factories.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the transfer date and time when the material transfer occurred.
2. Provide the reason for the material transfer.
3. Choose the warehouse and factory that were involved in the transfer.
4. Select the material being transferred.
5. Check if the material was transferred by 'Yes' or 'No'.
6. Check if the material was transferred to 'Yes' or 'No'.
7. Choose any reason codes that apply to the transfer.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about the transfer.
9. Enter the email address of the person responsible for the transfer.
10. Add any other comments about the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transfer Date** (`transfer_date`): The date when the material transfer occurred.
* **Transfer Time** (`transfer_time`): The time of the material transfer.
* **Reason** (`reason`): The reason for the material transfer. Please provide a brief description of the reason for the transfer.
* **Warehouse** (`warehouse_id`): The warehouse where the material was transferred from. Enter the ID number of the warehouse.
* **Factory** (`factory_id`): The factory where the material was transferred to. Enter the ID number of the factory.
* **Material** (`material_id`): The material being transferred. Enter the ID number of the material.
* **Transferred By** (`transferred_by`): Check if the material was transferred by 'Yes' or 'No'. This field indicates whether the transfer was carried out by the person responsible for the transfer.
* **Transferred To** (`transferred_to`): Check if the material was transferred to 'Yes' or 'No'. This field indicates whether the material was transferred to the destination specified.
* **Reason Code** (`reason_code`): Choose any reason codes that apply to the material transfer.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or notes about the material transfer.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the transfer.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the transfer.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments about the material transfer.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or department head.
