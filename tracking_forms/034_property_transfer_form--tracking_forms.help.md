# Property Transfer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Transfer Form is used to record the details of a property transfer between divisions and departments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields for the transferee (the division or department receiving the property) and transferor (the division or department sending the property).
2. Select the correct options for the transferee and transferor divisions and departments.
3. Choose the correct date and time of the transfer.
4. Fill in the asset details, including asset ID, serial number, model number, condition, and description.
5. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Transfer Details**: This is the header section of the form where you can add any additional information about the transfer.
* **Transferee Division**: Select the division that is receiving the property.
* **Transferee Department**: Select the department that is receiving the property.
* **Transferee Location**: Select the locations that the property will be used at.
* **Transferee Date**: Enter the date of the transfer.
* **Transferee Time**: Enter the time of the transfer.
* **Transferee Reason**: Provide a brief explanation for the transfer.
* **Transferee Asset ID**: Select the asset being transferred.
* **Transferee Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the asset being transferred.
* **Transferee Model Number**: Enter the model number of the asset being transferred.
* **Transferee Condition**: Describe the condition of the asset being transferred.
* **Transferee Description**: Add a brief description of the asset being transferred.
* **Transferee Status**: Select the status of the asset after the transfer (Active or Inactive).
* **Transferor Division**: Select the division that is sending the property.
* **Transferor Department**: Select the department that is sending the property.
* **Transferor Location**: Select the locations that the property will no longer be used at.
* **Transferor Date**: Enter the date of the transfer from the transferor.
* **Transferor Time**: Enter the time of the transfer from the transferor.
* **Transferor Reason**: Provide a brief explanation for the transfer from the transferor.
* **Transferor Asset ID**: Select the asset being transferred.
* **Transferor Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the asset being transferred.
* **Transferor Model Number**: Enter the model number of the asset being transferred.
* **Transferor Condition**: Describe the condition of the asset being transferred.
* **Transferor Description**: Add a brief description of the asset being transferred.
* **Transferor Status**: Select the status of the asset after the transfer (Active or Inactive).
* **Transferor Comments**: Add any additional comments about the transfer.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the transfer.
