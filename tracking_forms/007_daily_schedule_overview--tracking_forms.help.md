# daily_schedule_overview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about daily schedules or meetings. It helps users track and record details about meetings, including start and end times, participants, and other relevant notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the start time of the scheduled meeting or event.
2. Enter the end time of the scheduled meeting or event.
3. Provide the meeting's priority level or status.
4. Add any additional notes about the meeting.
5. Enter the name of the meeting.
6. Provide the meeting location.
7. Enter the meeting duration.
8. Indicate whether the meeting has participants.
9. Enter the names of the participants, if applicable.
10. Enter the start and end times of the meeting again for confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Start Time** (`schedule_start_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the start time of the scheduled meeting or event.
* **Schedule End Time** (`schedule_end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the end time of the scheduled meeting or event.
* **Schedule Prio** (`schedule_prio`, `text`, optional): Provide the meeting's priority level or status.
* **Schedule Notes** (`schedule_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about the meeting.
* **Meeting Name** (`meeting_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, `text`, optional): Provide the meeting location.
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, `number`, optional): Enter the meeting duration.
* **Meeting Participants** (`meeting_participants`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether the meeting has participants.
* **Meeting Participants Label** (`meeting_participants_label`, `text`, optional): Enter the names of the participants, if applicable.
* **Meeting Start Time** (`meeting_start_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the start time of the meeting again for confirmation.
* **Meeting End Time** (`meeting_end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the end time of the meeting again for confirmation.
* **Meeting Notes** (`meeting_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about the meeting.

## Tips
When filling out this form, be sure to provide as much detail as possible, especially regarding meeting locations and participant names. This will ensure accurate record-keeping and help with planning and coordination.
