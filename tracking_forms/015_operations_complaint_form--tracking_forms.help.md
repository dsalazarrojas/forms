# operations_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "operations_complaint_form" is a tool for tracking and managing operational complaints or issues within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To effectively use this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the operation or activity that the complaint is related to from the "Operation" dropdown menu.
2. Choose the category that best describes the complaint.
3. Enter the date the complaint occurred in the "Complaint Date" field.
4. Enter the time the complaint occurred in the "Complaint Time" field.
5. Provide a detailed description of the issue in the "Description" field.
6. Specify whether a resolution was reached ("Yes" or "No") in the "Resolution" field.
7. Identify the party responsible for the complaint ("Yes" or "No") in the "Responsible Party" field.
8. Select the status of the complaint ("Active" or "Inactive") in the "Status" field.
9. Add any additional comments or information in the "Comments" field.
10. If applicable, enter the date the issue was resolved in the "Resolution Date" field.
11. Specify if follow-up actions are required ("Yes" or "No") in the "Followup" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Operation
* **Operation** (`Operation`, `text`, required/optional): Select the operation or activity that the complaint is related to. This helps in organizing and categorizing complaints for better management.
### Category
* **Category** (`Category`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the category that best describes the nature of the complaint. This helps in understanding the scope and urgency of the issue.
### Complaint Date
* **Complaint Date** (`Complaint Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date the complaint occurred or was noticed.
### Complaint Time
* **Complaint Time** (`Complaint Time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time the complaint occurred or was noticed.
### Description
* **Description** (`Description`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the issue or problem raised.
### Resolution
* **Resolution** (`Resolution`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check if a resolution was achieved or if the issue remains unsolved.
### Responsible Party
* **Responsible Party** (`Responsible Party`, `select_one`, required/optional): Identify the party (or person) responsible for the complaint.
### Status
* **Status** (`Status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Specify the current status of the complaint (Active or Inactive).
### Comments
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional information or comments related to the complaint.
### Resolution Date
* **Resolution Date** (`Resolution Date`, `date`, required/optional): If applicable, enter the date the issue was resolved or resolved.
### Followup
* **Followup** (`Followup`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check if follow-up actions are required for the complaint.
