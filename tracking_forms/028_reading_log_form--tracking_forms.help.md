# Reading Log Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Reading Log Form" is a tool used to track and record information about an individual's reading habits and experiences. This form is designed to be completed by students, educators, or individuals looking to monitor their reading progress and comprehension.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Reading Log Form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the student's name in the **student_name** field.
2.  Record the time spent reading in the **time_spent_reading** field.
3.  Select the reading level completed from the options provided in the **reading_level** field.
4.  Rate your comprehension by entering a number in the **comprehension** field.
5.  Provide a brief description of your reading habits in the **reading_habits** field.
6.  Add any additional notes or comments in the **notes** field.
7.  Enter your email address in the **email_address** field (optional).
8.  Enter your phone number in the **phone_number** field (optional).
9.  Select the date of submission in the **submission_date** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's name.
* **time_spent_reading** (`time_spent_reading`, number, optional): Record the time spent reading.
* **reading_level** (`reading_level`, select_one, optional): Select the reading level completed from 1st to 12th grade.
* **comprehension** (`comprehension`, number, optional): Rate your comprehension of the material.
* **reading_habits** (`reading_habits`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your reading habits.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **email_address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted).
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted).
* **submission_date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Select the date of submission.
