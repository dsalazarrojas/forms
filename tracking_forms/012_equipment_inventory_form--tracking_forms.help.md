# equipment_inventory_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to track and manage equipment inventory for companies and organizations. It helps in maintaining a record of purchased, acquired, and last seen dates of equipment items, along with any additional notes or comments. This form is suitable for companies, organizations, and individuals who need to keep track of their equipment inventory for inventory management, asset management, or other similar purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by clicking on the "Company" field and enter the name of the company owning the equipment.**
2. **In the "Distributor" field, enter the name of the supplier or distributor from whom the equipment was purchased.**
3. **For "Item Description", describe the item being tracked, including its model number, serial number, or any other relevant details.**
4. **In the "Date Acquired" field, enter the date the equipment was acquired or purchased.**
5. **In the "Last Seen" field, enter the date the equipment was last seen or used.**
6. **In the "Notes" field, add any additional comments or notes about the equipment, such as its condition or any issues it may have.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company (text)**: The name of the company or organization owning the equipment. This is a required field for identifying the owner of the equipment.
* **Distributor (text)**: The name of the supplier or distributor from whom the equipment was purchased. This helps in tracking the origin of the equipment.
* **Item Description (text)**: A brief description of the equipment, including its model number, serial number, or any other relevant details.
* **Date Acquired (date)**: The date the equipment was acquired or purchased. This helps in tracking when the equipment was brought into the inventory.
* **Last Seen (date)**: The date the equipment was last seen or used. This helps in monitoring the equipment's usage and availability.
* **Notes (text)**: Any additional comments or notes about the equipment, such as its condition, any issues it may have, or its location.
