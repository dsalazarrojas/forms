# Geodetic Mark Recovery Report - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about geodetic mark recovery.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the geodetic mark's status in the `geodetic_mark_status` field.
2. Enter the control point ID in the `geodetic_control_point_id` field.
3. Select a date for the recovery in the `geodetic_recovery_date` field.
4. Provide any additional recovery notes in the `geodetic_recovery_note` field (optional).
5. Upload a recovery photo in the `geodetic_recovery_photo` field (optional).
6. Enter the surveyor's name in the `geodetic_surveyor_name` field.
7. Enter the surveyor's organization in the `geodetic_surveyor_organization` field.
8. Enter the surveyor's email in the `geodetic_surveyor_email` field (optional).
9. Enter the surveyor's phone number in the `geodetic_surveyor_phone` field (optional).
10. Enter the surveyor's comments in the `geodetic_surveyor_comments` field (optional).
11. Select the type of control point in the `geodetic_control_point_type` field (select multiple options).
12. Select the current status of the mark in the `geodetic_status` field (select one option).
13. Provide a note about the status in the `geodetic_status_note` field.
14. Enter your signature in the `geodetic_surveyor_signature` field.
15. Enter the date of surveying in the `geodetic_surveyor_date` field.
16. Select the hours of surveying in the `geodetic_surveyor_hours` field.
17. Enter any additional comments in the `geodetic_surveyor_comments` field (optional).
18. Upload a recovery photo in the `geodetic_recovery_photo_upload` field (optional).
19. Select the date of the status in the `geodetic_status_date` field.
20. Enter the surveyor's email (secondary) in the `geodetic_surveyor_email_2` field (optional).
21. Enter the surveyor's phone number (secondary) in the `geodetic_surveyor_phone_2` field (optional).
22. Enter the surveyor's comments (secondary) in the `geodetic_surveyor_comments_2` field (optional).
23. Enter the person or organization that surveyed by in the `geodetic_surveyor_surveyed_by` field.
24. Enter the name of the person or organization that surveyed by in the `geodetic_surveyor_surveyed_by_name` field (optional).
25. Enter the phone number of the person or organization that surveyed by in the `geodetic_surveyor_surveyed_by_phone` field (optional).
