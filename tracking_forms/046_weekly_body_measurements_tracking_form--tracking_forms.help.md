# weekly_body_measurements_tracking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "weekly_body_measurements_tracking_form" is a form used by coaches or trainers to track the weekly body measurements of their clients. It's essential to fill out the form accurately and consistently to monitor clients' progress over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Week Start Date" field and choose the date that corresponds to the start of the week for which you are recording measurements.
2. Select the measurement areas you wish to record (Chest, Waist, Hip, Neck, or Shoulder) in the "Measurements" field.
3. Enter the measurement values for each selected area in the "Measurement Values" field.
4. Provide any comments or notes about the client in the "Comments" field.
5. If needed, add additional comments or feedback from the coach in the "Coach Comments" field.
6. Enter the client's weight in the "Weight" field.
7. Enter the client's height in the "Height" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Week Start Date** (Week Start Date, date, required): Choose the date that corresponds to the start of the week for which you are recording measurements.
  * This field is used to specify the start date of the week for which you are tracking measurements.
  * Make sure to choose the correct date to ensure accurate tracking.
- **Measurements** (Measurements, select_multiple, false): Select the areas of the body you wish to measure (Chest, Waist, Hip, Neck, or Shoulder).
  * This field allows you to select multiple areas to measure to track progress.
  * Select all areas that apply for a comprehensive measurement.
- **Measurement Values** (Measurement Values, number, false): Enter the measurement values for each selected area.
  * This field is used to record the actual measurement values for each selected area.
  * Enter the values accurately and consistently for reliable progress tracking.
- **Comments** (Comments, text, false): Provide any comments or notes about the client.
  * This field is for any additional information or feedback about the client.
  * Keep comments concise and relevant for future reference.
- **Coach Comments** (Coach Comments, text, false): Add any additional comments or feedback from the coach.
  * This field is for the coach's thoughts or observations about the client.
  * Keep comments concise and relevant for future reference.
- **Weight** (Weight, number, false): Enter the client's weight.
  * This field is used to record the client's weight.
  * Enter the weight accurately for reliable progress tracking.
- **Height** (Height, number, false): Enter the client's height.
  * This field is used to record the client's height.
  * Enter the height accurately for reliable progress tracking.
