# Daily Routine Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Routine Checklist Form is designed to help you track your daily routines, tasks, and schedules in a structured and organized way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form daily, ideally at the same time each day.
* Select your morning routine activities from the list of options provided.
* Choose your afternoon activity from the options provided.
* Select your evening routine activities from the list of options provided.
* Enter any additional notes or comments about your day.
* Enter your wake-up time (in hours).
* Enter the duration of your sleep (in hours).
* Enter the date of your activities.
* Enter your email (optional).
* Select your work schedule for the day (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* Select any tasks you need to complete from the lists provided.
* Select any morning routines you need to complete from the lists provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Morning Routine**: This is a list of activities you should perform in the morning, such as Wake up, Shower, Get dressed, Have breakfast, and Exercise. Select all the activities that apply to you.
* **Afternoon Activity**: This is the activity you are performing during the afternoon. Choose one of the options provided, such as Work, Study, or Relax.
* **Evening Routine**: This is a list of activities you should perform in the evening, such as Have dinner, Watch movie, Read, and Practice yoga. Select all the activities that apply to you.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your day, such as accomplishments, challenges, or anything else you'd like to record.
* **Wake Up Time**: Enter the time you woke up (in hours).
* **Sleep Duration**: Enter the duration of your sleep (in hours).
* **Date**: Enter the date of your activities.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (optional).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Work Schedule**: This is the time of day when you are working. Choose one of the options provided, such as Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Task 1-10**: These are lists of tasks you need to complete on a daily basis. Select all the tasks that apply to you from each list.
* **Morning Routine 1-3**: These are lists of morning routines you need to complete on a daily basis. Select all the routines that apply to you from each list.
