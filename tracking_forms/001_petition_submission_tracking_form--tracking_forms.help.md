# Petition Submission Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Petition Submission Tracking Form is used to track the progress and status of petitions submitted for review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **New Petition** section with the details of the petition being submitted.
2. Select the **Intake Date** of the petition.
3. Enter the **Email** of the person submitting the petition.
4. Provide your **Petitioner Name**.
5. Select the current **Status** of the petition from the options provided.
6. Add any additional **Notes** about the petition, if necessary.
7. Select the **Review Date** of the petition.
8. Identify the person who will **Review** the petition.
9. Enter the **Phone** number of the person submitting the petition.
10. Review and update the form as necessary, taking note of when it was **Updated At** and when it was **Created At**.
11. Enter the form **ID** to help us track the form internally.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Petition** (`Petition Submission Tracking Form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the petition being submitted.
* **Intake Date** (`Intake Date`, date, optional): Select the date when the petition was submitted.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person submitting the petition.
* **Petitioner Name** (`Petitioner Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or entity submitting the petition.
* **Status** (`Status`, select_one, true): Choose the current status of the petition from the provided options.
	+ Option 1: Submitted
	+ Option 2: In Review
	+ Option 3: Approved
	+ Option 4: Denied
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments about the petition.
* **Review Date** (`Review Date`, date, optional): Select the date when the petition was reviewed.
* **Review By** (`Reviewed By`, text, optional): Identify the person who reviewed the petition.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the petition.
* **Updated At** (`Updated At`, date, optional): Select the date and time when the form was last updated.
* **Created At** (`Created At`, date, optional): Select the date and time when the form was created.
* **Form ID** (`Form ID`, text, optional): Enter the unique identifier for the form, which helps us track the form internally.
