# Daily Routine Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Daily Routine Checklist Form" is designed to track daily routines and activities that contribute to overall health and wellness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

- Start by selecting the correct response for each field. For most fields, select either "Yes" or "No" as the answer will be one of these two options. For fields like "Daily Mileage Log" and "Daily Mileage Time", enter the corresponding information as requested.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked as such in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Daily Routine Checklist
* **Daily Routine Checklist** (`routine_checklist`, `note`, required/optional): A general note section for users to describe any daily activities not covered in the other fields.

### 2. Have You Had Breakfast
* **Have You Had Breakfast** (`breakfast`, `select_one`, required): Check "Yes" if you had breakfast, "No" otherwise.

### 3. Take Medication
* **Take Medication** (`medication`, `select_one`, required): Check "Yes" if you took medication, "No" otherwise.

### 4. Exercise
* **Exercise** (`exercise`, `select_one`, required): Check "Yes" if you exercised, "No" otherwise.

### 5. Drink Water
* **Drink Water** (`water`, `select_one`, required): Check "Yes" if you drank water, "No" otherwise.

### 6. Social Media
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, `select_one`, required): Check "Yes" if you used social media, "No" otherwise.

### 7. Work
* **Work** (`work`, `select_one`, required): Check "Yes" if you worked, "No" otherwise.

### 8. Sleep
* **Sleep** (`sleep`, `select_one`, required): Check "Yes" if you slept well, "No" otherwise.

### 9. Daily Mileage Log
* **Daily Mileage Log** (`daily_mileage_log`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all modes of transportation used today, such as Vehicle, Flight, Train.

### 10. Daily Mileage Distance
* **Daily Mileage Distance** (`daily_mileage_distance`, `number`, optional): Enter the total distance traveled.

### 11. Daily Mileage Time
* **Daily Mileage Time** (`daily_mileage_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the total travel time.

### 12. Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): A general note section for users to provide any additional information.

**Tips:**

- Be honest and accurate when reporting your daily routines.
- Fill out all required fields, as they are marked as such.
- Make sure to save your work after completing the form.

Note: This help guide assumes the user is aware of the form's purpose and has access to the form.
