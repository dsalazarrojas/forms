# stock_condition_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The stock condition survey form is used to track the condition and status of stock items. This form is meant to be filled out by users who need to report on the status of items in stock, particularly those that are not currently in use or are in a pending status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the stock item from the list.
2. Fill in the stock item details, such as condition, status, and description.
3. Upload any relevant images or notes for further reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stock Item (`stock_item`, text):** Enter the name of the stock item.
* **Stock Item Condition (`stock_item_condition`, select_one):** Choose the current condition of the stock item from the options provided: "new", "used", or "refurbished".
* **Purchase Date (`purchase_date`, date):** Enter the date the stock item was purchased, if applicable.
* **Purchase Time (`purchase_time`, time):** Enter the time the stock item was purchased, if applicable.
* **Stock Condition Comments (`stock_condition_comments`, note):** Enter any additional comments about the stock item's condition.
* **Assigned To (`assigned_to`, select_one):** Choose the person or team responsible for the stock item.
* **Stock Item Status (`stock_item_status`, select_multiple):** Select the status of the stock item from the options provided: "active", "inactive", or "pending".
* **Stock Item Description (`stock_item_description`, text):** Enter a brief description of the stock item.
* **Stock Item Image (`stock_item_image`, text):** Enter any relevant images or file references for the stock item.
* **Stock Item Location (`stock_item_location`, text):** Enter the location of the stock item.
* **Stock Item Notes (`stock_item_notes`, note):** Enter any additional notes or comments about the stock item.

## Tips
* Make sure to be accurate and thorough when filling out this form.
* If an item's status is pending, make sure to clearly explain the reason in the stock condition comments field.
* If an item's condition is not new, used, or refurbished, please select "other" as the condition and provide further explanation in the stock condition comments field.
