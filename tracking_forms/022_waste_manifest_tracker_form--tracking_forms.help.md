# waste_manifest_tracker_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The waste manifest tracker form is used to log and manage information about waste shipments, including shipment details, tracking numbers, and status updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the required fields and the purpose of each section.
2. Enter the relevant information for each field, starting with the Shipment ID, which is used to identify a unique shipment.
3. Provide the Tracking Number, which is a unique code assigned to the shipment for tracking purposes.
4. Enter the Shipment Date, which is the date the shipment was made.
5. Enter the Shipment Weight, which is the weight of the shipment.
6. Select the Shipment Type from the options provided, indicating whether the shipment contains hazardous or non-hazardous materials.
7. Add any additional Shipment Notes for reference or further information.
8. Enter the Driver ID, Driver Name, and Driver Phone number for the person responsible for the shipment.
9. Update the Shipment Status and provide a reason for the status change in the Shipment Status Reason field.
10. Finally, enter the Shipper ID and Shipper Name for the company or organization shipping the materials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shipment ID (1)**: A unique identifier for the shipment.
* **Tracking Number (2)**: A unique code assigned to the shipment for tracking purposes.
* **Shipment Date (3)**: The date the shipment was made.
* **Shipment Weight (4)**: The weight of the shipment.
* **Shipment Type (5)**: Select from options to indicate whether the shipment contains hazardous or non-hazardous materials.
* **Shipment Notes (6)**: Additional information or notes about the shipment.
* **Driver ID (7)**: The identifier for the person responsible for the shipment.
* **Driver Name (8)**: The name of the person responsible for the shipment.
* **Driver Phone (9)**: The phone number of the person responsible for the shipment.
* **Shipment Status (10)**: Select from options to indicate the current status of the shipment.
* **Shipment Status Reason (11)**: The reason for the shipment status change.
* **Shipper ID (12)**: The identifier for the company or organization shipping the materials.
* **Shipper Name (13)**: The name of the company or organization shipping the materials.

## Tips
* Ensure accurate and up-to-date information is entered for each field to maintain accurate records.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors or duplication.
* If unsure about any field, refer to the provided instructions or seek clarification from the relevant personnel.
