# daily_charge_summary_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The daily charge summary form is used to record and track charges made on a specific date. This form is a key component in the billing process and helps to maintain an accurate record of charges for easy reference and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Select the "daily_charge_form" page to begin.
3. Fill in the "transaction_details" field with a brief description of the charge.
4. Enter the "sales_tax_rate" as a percentage value.
5. Calculate the "subtotal" by multiplying the total amount with the sales tax rate.
6. Select the "tax_liable_party" to determine who is liable for the sales tax.
7. Choose the tax exemptions applicable for the charge.
8. Enter your "email", "phone_number", and "date" and "time" for contact and timestamping information.
9. Select the "time_zone" for the location of the charge.
10. Optionally, add "comments" for any additional information regarding the charge.
11. You can attach files as "attachments" for further reference.
12. Repeat the steps for any additional sales tax rates and tax exemptions as needed.

* **customer_details**: This field is not currently used. Please disregard for now.

* **subtotal**: The total amount of the charge before tax.
* **tax_liable_party**: Select who is liable for the sales tax.
* **tax_exemptions**: Choose the tax exemptions applicable for the charge.
* **notes**: Additional comments regarding the charge.
* **email**: The contact email address for the charge.
* **phone_number**: The contact phone number for the charge.

* **time_zone**: The time zone for the location of the charge.
