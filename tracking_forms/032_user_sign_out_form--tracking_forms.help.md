# User Sign Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for user sign-out purposes in a work-related setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the reason for your sign-out from the options provided in the "Sign-out for" field.
2. Enter the reason for your sign-out in the "reason_sign_out" field.
3. Confirm your sign-out status by selecting "True" or "False" in the "confirmation_sign_out" field.
4. Enter the date and time of your sign-out in the "date_sign_out" and "time_sign_out" fields, respectively.
5. Enter your user ID in the "user_id" field.
6. Enter the time worked before signing out in the "time_worked" field.
7. Select your current status in the "user_status" field.
8. Enter any comments about your sign-out in the "comments_sign_out" field.
9. If required, confirm your signature by filling out the fields in the "confirm_signature" section.
10. Enter your name in the "user_name" field.
11. Enter the time you signed in in the "sign_in_time" field.
12. Enter the time you signed out in the "sign_out_time" field.
13. Enter the reason for signing out in the "reason_signed_out" field.
14. Enter your confirmed sign-out status in the "confirmed_by" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sign-out for** (`user_sign_out_form`, select_multiple): Select one or more reasons for your sign-out.
* **reason_sign_out** (`reason_sign_out`, text): Enter the reason for your sign-out.
* **confirmation_sign_out** (`confirmation_sign_out`, select_one): Confirm your sign-out status.
* **date_sign_out** (`date_sign_out`, date): Enter the date of your sign-out.
* **time_sign_out** (`time_sign_out`, time): Enter the time of your sign-out.
* **user_id** (`user_id`, text): Enter your user ID.
* **time_worked** (`time_worked`, time): Enter the time worked before signing out.
* **user_status** (`user_status`, select_one): Select your current status.
* **comments_sign_out** (`comments_sign_out`, text): Enter any comments about your sign-out.
* **confirm_signature** (`confirm_signature`, note): If required, confirm your signature by filling out the fields in this section.
* **confirm_signature_date** (`confirm_signature_date`, date): Enter the date of your confirmation.
* **confirm_signature_time** (`confirm_signature_time`, time): Enter the time of your confirmation.
* **confirm_signature_status** (`confirm_signature_status`, select_one): Select the status of your confirmation.
* **confirm_signature_comment** (`confirm_signature_comment`, text): Enter any comments about your confirmation.
* **confirm_signature_user** (`confirm_signature_user`, text): Enter the user who confirmed your signature.
* **sign_out_by** (`sign_out_by`, text): Enter the person who signed you out.
* **confirm_signature_by** (`confirm_signature_by`, text): Enter the person who confirmed your signature.
* **time_signed_out** (`time_signed_out`, time): Enter the time you were signed out.
* **reason_signed_out** (`reason_signed_out`, text): Enter the reason you were signed out.
* **user_name** (`user_name`, text): Enter your name.
* **sign_in_time** (`sign_in_time`, time): Enter the time you signed in.
* **sign_out_time** (`sign_out_time`, time): Enter the time you signed out.
* **reason_signed_out** (`reason_signed_out`, text): Enter the reason you signed out.
* **confirmed_by** (`confirmed_by`, text): Enter the person who confirmed your sign-out status.
* **time_worked** (`time_worked`, time): Enter the time worked before signing out.
* **user_status** (`user_status`, select_one): Select your current status.
