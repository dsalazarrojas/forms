# Cholesterol Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cholesterol Screening Form is designed to collect patient information for cholesterol screening purposes, including medical history, lifestyle factors, and test results. This form helps healthcare providers gather essential data to assess the patient's risk factors for heart disease and provide personalized recommendations for maintaining healthy cholesterol levels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the patient's **Full Name** in the designated field. Please ensure it is spelled correctly.
2. Enter the patient's **Date of Birth** in the date format (mm/dd/yy or dd/mm/yy).
3. Select the patient's **Age** in the designated field.
4. Select the patient's **Gender** from the provided options (Male, Female, Other, or Prefer not to say).
5. Enter the patient's **Patient ID Number** (optional).
6. Fill in the patient's **Phone Number** and **Email Address**.
7. Provide the patient's **Primary Care Provider Name** (optional).
8. Select the **Screening Date** for the cholesterol test.
9. Indicate the patient's **Fasting Status**:
	* Yes, fasted 9+ hours
	* Yes, fasted less than 9 hours
	* No, not fasting
10. If applicable, enter the last **Meal or Beverage Time**.
11. Enter the patient's **Total Cholesterol (mg/dL)**, **LDL Cholesterol (mg/dL)**, **HDL Cholesterol (mg/dL)**, and **Triglycerides (mg/dL)** values from the cholesterol test results.
12. Select the patient's **Family History of Heart Disease**:
	* Yes
	* No
	* Unsure
13. List any **Current Cholesterol Medications** the patient is taking (optional).
14. Select the patient's **Primary Dietary Pattern**:
	* Mediterranean diet
	* Low-fat diet
	* High protein diet
	* Standard American diet
	* Plant-based diet
	* Other
15. If applicable, select the patient's **Exercise Frequency** in weeks per year (optional).

**Tips**

* Please answer each question carefully, as the accuracy of the patient's data is crucial for the healthcare provider's assessment.
* If you are unsure about any question, please consult the patient or the healthcare provider for guidance.
* Ensure all required fields are filled in before proceeding to the next section.
* If you need to add or remove pages, you can do so by clicking the corresponding buttons.
