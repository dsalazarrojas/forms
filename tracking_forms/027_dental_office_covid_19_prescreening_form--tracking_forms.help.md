# Dental Office COVID 19 Prescreening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients about their COVID-19 status before their scheduled appointments in a dental office setting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure a safe and healthy environment for all patients and staff.
2. Please answer all questions honestly, as they are required fields unless otherwise specified as optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Please enter your name and other relevant details as prompted.
- **Have a fever** (`have_fever`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if you have a fever, or "No" if you do not.
- **Have a cough** (`have_cough`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if you have a cough, or "No" if you do not.
- **Have shortness of breath** (`have_shortness_of_breath`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if you have shortness of breath, or "No" if you do not.
- **Have been vaccinated** (`have_been_vaccinated`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if you have been vaccinated, or "No" if you have not.
- **Vaccine Date** (`vaccine_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you received your COVID-19 vaccination, if applicable.
- **Other Information** (`other_info`, note, optional): Please provide any additional information you believe is relevant to your health status.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Please submit this form to complete the pre-screening process.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate in your responses to ensure the health and safety of yourself and others.
- If you are unsure or have questions about any of the fields, please contact the dental office staff for assistance.
