# Weekly Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data for weekly reports in a school setting, monitoring student and staff performance, engagement, and overall progress over the course of a week.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by selecting the Week Start Date for the period you are reporting on.
- Answer all the questions according to your observations and experiences during the week, using the corresponding scales for numeric fields.
- Fill in any additional comments or suggestions in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Week Start Date** (`week_start_date`, `date`, optional): Select the start date of the week you are reporting on.
* **Notes** (`session_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any relevant notes about the session or week.
* **Student Improvement** (`student_improvement`, `number`, optional): Rate student improvement based on your observations, using the scale 1-5.
* **Homework Completed** (`homework_completed`, `select_one`, optional): Check if students completed their assigned homework.
* **Activities Planned** (`activities_planned`, `number`, optional): Record the number of activities planned for the week.
* **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, `number`, optional): Rate student attendance based on your observations, using the scale 1-5.
* **Student Engagement** (`student_engagement`, `number`, optional): Rate student engagement based on your observations, using the scale 1-5.
* **Student Progress** (`student_progress`, `number`, optional): Rate student progress based on your observations, using the scale 1-5.
* **Student Interest** (`student_interest`, `number`, optional): Rate student interest based on your observations, using the scale 1-5.
* **Student Motivation** (`student_motivation`, `number`, optional): Rate student motivation based on your observations, using the scale 1-5.
* **Student Behavior** (`student_behavior`, `number`, optional): Rate student behavior based on your observations, using the scale 1-5.
* **Staff Attendance** (`staff_attendance`, `number`, optional): Rate staff attendance based on your observations, using the scale 1-5.
* **Staff Engagement** (`staff_engagement`, `number`, optional): Rate staff engagement based on your observations, using the scale 1-5.
* **Staff Progress** (`staff_progress`, `number`, optional): Rate staff progress based on your observations, using the scale 1-5.
* **Staff Interest** (`staff_interest`, `number`, optional): Rate staff interest based on your observations, using the scale 1-5.
* **Staff Motivation** (`staff_motivation`, `number`, optional): Rate staff motivation based on your observations, using the scale 1-5.
* **Staff Behavior** (`staff_behavior`, `number`, optional): Rate staff behavior based on your observations, using the scale 1-5.
* **Student Goals** (`student_goals`, `text`, optional): Add any comments about student goals or objectives.
* **Teacher Notes** (`teacher_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any notes from the teacher about the week.
* **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any comments from the student about their experience.
* **Staff Comments** (`staff_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any comments from staff members about their experience.
* **Staff Suggestions** (`staff_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Add any suggestions from staff members.
* **Teacher Suggestions** (`teacher_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Add any suggestions from the teacher.
* **Other Activities** (`other_activities`, `text`, optional): Add any other activities that took place during the week.
* **Overall Comments** (`overall_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any final comments or feedback.

## Tips
* Be thorough and honest in your reporting to ensure that the data collected is accurate and informative.
* Use the provided scales consistently and fairly when rating numeric fields.
* Take advantage of the text fields to provide more detailed and contextual information.
