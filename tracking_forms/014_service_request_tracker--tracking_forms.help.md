# Service Request Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Request Tracker form is used to track and manage service requests from customers or users. It helps you to keep track of service requests, prioritize them, and assign them to specific individuals or departments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by an asterisk *):
	* Enter a brief description of the service request in the **Service Request Description** field.
2. Select the priority level of the service request in the **Priority** field.
3. Choose the category of the service request from the **Category** field.
4. Enter the date the service request was made in the **Request Date** field.
5. Enter the status of the service request in the **Request Status** field.
6. Select the person assigned to handle the service request in the **Assignee** field.
7. Select the person who submitted the service request in the **Submitter** field.
8. Choose the type of service request from the **Service Request Type** field.
9. Enter the location where the service request was made in the **Location** field.
10. Enter the name of the contact person in the **Contact Name** field.
11. Enter the email of the contact person in the **Contact Email** field.
12. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the **Contact Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Request Tracker**: This is the form title and is for reference only.
* **Priority**: Select the priority level of the service request. This field is optional, but selecting 'Yes' indicates that the service request is high priority.
* **Category**: This field allows you to categorize the service request. Select the relevant category from the dropdown options.
* **Request Date**: Enter the date the service request was made. This field is a date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Service Request Description**: Enter a brief description of the service request.
* **Request Status**: This field displays the current status of the service request. Select 'Active' if the service request is active, or 'Inactive' if it is not.
* **Assignee**: Select the person assigned to handle the service request. This field is optional, but selecting 'Yes' indicates that the service request is assigned to a specific person.
* **Submitter**: Select the person who submitted the service request. This field is optional, but selecting 'Yes' indicates that the service request was submitted by a specific person.
* **Service Request Type**: This field allows you to categorize the type of service request. Select the relevant type from the dropdown options.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the service request was made.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate tracking and management of service requests.
* Review and update the form regularly to track the status and progress of service requests.
* Use the form for internal use only. Do not share with external users.
