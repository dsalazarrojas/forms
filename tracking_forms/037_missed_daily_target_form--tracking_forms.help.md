# Missed Daily Target Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track and report missed daily targets. It helps users to keep track of dates and times when they missed their targets.
## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, fill in the following fields:
	* Fill in the date when you missed your target.
	* Fill in the time when you missed your target.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Missed Daily Target (daily_target)**: This field is for entering the text description of the missed target.
* **Target Date (target_date)**: This field is for selecting the date when you missed your target.
* **Target Time (target_time)**: This field is for entering the time when you missed your target.
## Tips
* Only submit one missed target per form.
* Be accurate when filling in the date and time fields.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact form@gic.mx.
