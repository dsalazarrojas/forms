# Outbound Contact Tracing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record information about individuals who have come into contact with someone who has a medical condition or symptoms. This form helps track and document this information for contact tracing and potential outbreak prevention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the name of the individual who has come into contact with someone who has a medical condition or symptoms.
2. Enter the phone number of the individual, if available.
3. Enter the email address of the individual, if available.
4. Describe the address where the individual was in contact with the person with a medical condition or symptoms.
5. List any medical conditions or symptoms the individual may have.
6. Provide a brief description of what happened.
7. Specify who is assigned to handle the information.

## Tips

* Please complete this form as accurately and thoroughly as possible to ensure the information is up-to-date and reliable.
* If you are unsure about any information or have concerns about a medical condition, please contact a healthcare professional.
