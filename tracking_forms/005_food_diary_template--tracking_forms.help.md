# Food Diary Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Diary Template form is designed to help you track your daily food intake for monitoring your eating habits and dietary patterns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Food Diary Template" field with a brief description of your form, if desired.
2. Select the date of consumption in the "Date" field.
3. Enter the food items consumed in the "Food Items" field.
4. Add any additional notes about your day in the "Notes" field.
5. Finally, select the time of consumption in the "Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Food Diary Template** (`food_diary_template`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your form, if desired.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date of consumption.
* **Food Items** (`food_items`, text, optional): Enter the food items consumed on that date.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about your day.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time of consumption.
