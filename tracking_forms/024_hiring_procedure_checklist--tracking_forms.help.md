# Hiring Procedure Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hiring Procedure Checklist is a form used to track the hiring procedure for a new employee. It ensures that all necessary steps are completed before onboarding a new hire, verifying that the hiring process is followed consistently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Posting (text)**: Enter a brief description of the job posting, including the job title, job classification, and any other relevant details.
* **Candidate Sourcing (select one)**: Choose either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the candidate was sourced from internal or external sources.
* **Interview Schedule (date)**: Select the date and time of the interview.
* **Offer Creation (text)**: Enter the details of the offer letter, including the job title, salary, and other relevant information.
* **New Hire Onboarding (text)**: Enter the date and time of the new hire's onboarding process.
* **Training Programs (select multiple)**: Choose one or more options indicating if the new hire will participate in training programs.
* **Job Description (text)**: Enter a brief description of the job role, including key responsibilities and requirements.
* **Job Title (text)**: Enter the official job title of the new hire.
* **Job Classification (select one)**: Choose either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the job is classified as exempt or non-exempt.
