**Warranty Tracking Dashboard - Help Guide**

## Purpose

The **Warranty Tracking Dashboard** is a tool used to track and manage warranty claims and customer interactions. This form is designed to help organizations efficiently manage their warranty programs by collecting and storing relevant customer and product information. 

It's intended for use by customer service teams and warranty administrators to streamline their workflow and improve customer satisfaction. 

Please make sure you're viewing the correct form and that your browser allows pop-ups/ JavaScript before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete the form, start by filling in the **Customer Information** page.
2.  On the **Customer Information** page, provide the customer's contact information and any relevant details about the customer.
3.  Move on to the **Product Details** page, where you can enter information specific to the product, such as model number, purchase date, and serial number.
4.  On the **Warranty Information** page, provide any relevant warranty details, such as warranty start date, end date, and terms of coverage.
5.  In the **Claims** page, document any claims made by the customer, including dates, descriptions, and supporting evidence.
6.  Update the **Warranty Status** page to reflect the current status of the warranty claim.
7.  Add any relevant customer service interactions on the **Customer Service** page.
8.  Record any terms or conditions associated with the warranty on the **Warranty Terms** page.
9.  Leave any additional comments about the interaction or claim on the **Additional Comments** page.
10. Finally, enter the customer's email address on the **Customer Email** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Information (Customer Information)**: Enter the customer's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
*   **Product Details (Product Details)**: Enter product model number, purchase date, and serial number for the product in question.
*   **Warranty Information (Warranty Information)**: Enter warranty start date, end date, and terms of coverage for the warranty.
*   **Claims (Claims)**: Document any claims made by the customer, including dates, descriptions, and supporting evidence.
*   **Warranty Status (Warranty Status)**: Update to reflect the current status of the warranty claim.
*   **Customer Service (Customer Service)**: Add any relevant customer service interactions.
*   **Warranty Terms (Warranty Terms)**: Record any terms or conditions associated with the warranty.
*   **Additional Comments (Additional Comments)**: Leave any additional comments about the interaction or claim.
*   **Customer Email (Customer Email)**: Enter the customer's email address.
