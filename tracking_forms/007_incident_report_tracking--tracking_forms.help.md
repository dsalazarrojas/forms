# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to track and manage incidents in a organized manner. It can be used for both internal and external reporting of incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the incident is being tracked.
2. Enter a brief description of the incident in the "incident description" field.
3. Select the status of the incident (active, inactive, or resolved).
4. Enter the incident date and time.
5. Enter the location where the incident occurred.
6. Choose a category and subcategory for the incident.
7. Select the severity and priority of the incident.
8. Enter the user who reported the incident.
9. Enter the email of the user who reported the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <label> (Yes/No) (Required/Optional)
* **incident_report_tracking_form (Yes/No)** (`incident_report_tracking_form`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is used to indicate whether the incident is being tracked.

* **incident_report (incident_report, text, optional)**: Enter a brief description of the incident.

* **date (incident_date, date, optional)**: Enter the date of the incident.

* **time (incident_time, time, optional)**: Enter the time of the incident.

* **location (location, text, optional)**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.

* **incident_status (incident_status, select_one, optional)**: Select the status of the incident (active, inactive, or resolved).

* **incident_type (incident_type, text, optional)**: Enter the type of incident.

* **incident_reported_by (incident_reported_by, text, optional)**: Enter the name of the user who reported the incident.

* **incident_reported_to (incident_reported_to, text, optional)**: Enter the name of the user or team to whom the incident was reported.

* **assigned_to (assigned_to, text, optional)**: Enter the name of the user assigned to handle the incident.

* **incident_category (incident_category, text, optional)**: Enter the category of the incident.

* **incident_subcategory (incident_subcategory, text, optional)**: Enter the subcategory of the incident.

* **incident_severity (incident_severity, text, optional)**: Enter the severity of the incident.

* **incident_priority (incident_priority, text, optional)**: Enter the priority of the incident.

* **incident_reported_by_user (incident_reported_by_user, text, optional)**: Enter the user who reported the incident.
