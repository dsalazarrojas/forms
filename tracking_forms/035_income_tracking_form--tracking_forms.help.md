# income_tracking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The income_tracking_form is used to track income payments made to team members. This form helps keep a record of payment dates, amounts, frequencies, and other relevant details for income payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Payment Date for the income payment. This date should be the date the payment was actually made.
2. Enter the Payment Amount, the total amount of the payment.
3. Select the Payment Frequency to indicate how often the payment is made (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly).
4. Enter the name of the team member receiving the payment.
5. Enter the type of payment (e.g., salary, bonus, commission).
6. Select the payment status (e.g., Pending, Received, Rejected).
7. Select the Reconciliation Frequency to indicate how often the payment is reconciled (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
8. Add any additional Notes about the payment.
9. Enter the name of the client (if applicable).
10. Enter the name of the company (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Date**: The date on which the payment was made.
* **payment_amount**: The total amount of the payment.
*   **payment_frequency**: The frequency of the payment (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly).
*   **team_member**: The name of the team member receiving the payment.
*   **payment_type**: The type of payment (e.g., salary, bonus, commission).
*   **payment_status**: The status of the payment (e.g., Pending, Received, Rejected).
*   **reconciliation_frequency**: The frequency of reconciliation (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
*   **notes**: Additional information about the payment.
*   **client_name**: The name of the client (if applicable).
*   **company_name**: The name of the company (if applicable).
