# online_client_tracking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and manage client interactions and information for business use. It should only be filled out for clients that the company has interacted with.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the client details, including any relevant information about the client.
2. Indicate the client's current status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
3. Enter any notes or comments about the client in the client notes field.
4. Enter the client's email address.
5. Enter the client's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the client, including their name, company, and any other relevant information.
* **Tracking Interactions** (`tracking_interactions`, text, required): Describe the interactions you have had with the client, including any meetings, calls, or correspondence.
* **Client Status** (`client_status`, select_one, required): Select the client's current status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the client.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter the client's phone number.
