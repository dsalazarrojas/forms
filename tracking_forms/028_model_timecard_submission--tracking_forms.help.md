# Model Timecard Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Model Timecard Submission form is designed to record your work hours and activities for tracking and reporting purposes. It helps in accurately recording your time spent on various projects and models.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the **Work Dates** field and choose the dates you worked on from the calendar provided or enter them manually.
2. Enter the number of **Work Hours** you logged for the selected dates.
3. Choose the **Job Details** for which you worked by selecting from the available options (Production, Agency, Model). This will help in categorizing your work hours accurately.
4. Add any additional **Notes** you may have regarding your work, such as comments or explanations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Work Dates**: This field is where you specify the dates you worked on. Please enter the dates in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Work Hours**: This field is for entering the total number of hours you worked on the selected dates.
* **Job Details**: This field is for selecting the specific job or project you worked on. Select the option that best describes your job details (Production, Agency, Model).
* **Notes**: This field is for any additional comments or explanations about your work. This can be used to provide context or reference materials for your work hours.

## Tips
- Ensure that you accurately record your work hours to maintain accurate tracking and reporting.
- If you need to log multiple work dates, you can use the "Add Another Date" feature to add more dates as needed.
- Make sure to select the correct job details to ensure that your work hours are properly categorized.
