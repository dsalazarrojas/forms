# Surveillance Tour Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Surveillance Tour Checklist is a form designed to track the details of a surveillance tour, which is a critical component of security and risk management protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Patrol Details**: This section is for recording information about the patrol, including the Patrol ID, Date, and Time.
	* Patrol ID: Enter the ID of the patrol you are tracking.
	* Date: Enter the date of the patrol.
	* Time: Enter the time of the patrol.
2. **Incidents**: This section is for recording incidents that occurred during the patrol.
	* Incident 1: Check 'Yes' if an incident occurred at Checkpoint 1.
	* Incident 2: Check 'Yes' if an incident occurred at Checkpoint 2.
	* Incident 3: Check 'Yes' if an incident occurred at Checkpoint 3.
3. **Checkpoints**: This section is for recording the status of each checkpoint.
	* Checkpoint 1: Check 'Yes' if the checkpoint was successfully completed.
	* Checkpoint 2: Check 'Yes' if the checkpoint was successfully completed.
	* Checkpoint 3: Check 'Yes' if the checkpoint was successfully completed.
4. **Comments**: This section is for additional comments about the patrol.
	* Comments: Enter any additional comments about the patrol.
5. **Contact Information**: This section is for recording contact information.
	* Phone: Enter the phone number of the individual or organization in contact.
	* Email: Enter the email address of the individual or organization in contact.
	* Address: Enter the address of the individual or organization in contact.
	* City: Enter the city of the individual or organization in contact.
	* State: Enter the state of the individual or organization in contact.
	* Zip: Enter the zip code of the individual or organization in contact.
	* Location: Enter the location of the individual or organization in contact.
6. **Latitude and Longitude**: This section is for recording latitude and longitude coordinates.
	* Latitude: Check 'Yes' if the latitude was accurately recorded.
	* Longitude: Check 'Yes' if the longitude was accurately recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patrol ID**: A unique identifier for the patrol.
* **Date**: The date of the patrol.
* **Time**: The time of the patrol.
* **Checkpoints 1-3**: Check 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the status of each checkpoint.
* **Incidents 1-3**: Check 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if an incident occurred at each checkpoint.
* **Comments**: Additional comments about the patrol.
* **Phone**: The phone number of the individual or organization in contact.
* **Email**: The email address of the individual or organization in contact.
* **Address**: The address of the individual or organization in contact.
* **City**: The city of the individual or organization in contact.
* **State**: The state of the individual or organization in contact.
* **Zip**: The zip code of the individual or organization in contact.
* **Location**: The location of the individual or organization in contact.
* **Latitude**: Check 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the latitude was accurately recorded.
* **Longitude**: Check 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the longitude was accurately recorded.
