# counter_inquiry_log_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The counter_inquiry_log_form is designed to track and manage customer inquiries, allowing for efficient organization and follow-up on requests, inquiries, and complaints. This form ensures that each inquiry is thoroughly reviewed and addressed in a timely and effective manner, preventing duplication and ensuring resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Customer Request** field with a brief description of the customer's inquiry.
2. Select the **Inquiry Type** from the options provided (Request, Inquiry, Question, or Complaint).
3. Select the **Assigned User** from the options provided (Jim, Jane, or John).
4. Choose the **Resolution Status** from the options provided (Open, Closed, or In Progress).
5. Optionally, add additional details in the **Request Description**, **Time Submitted**, **Next Steps**, **Customer Response**, and **Comments** fields as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Request
• Customer Request: Enter a brief description of the customer's inquiry, providing essential information for review and action.

### Inquiry Type
• Inquiry Type: Select the type of inquiry (Request, Inquiry, Question, or Complaint) to help classify and prioritize the inquiry.

### Request Description
• Request Description: Add more details about the customer's request, if necessary, to facilitate thorough understanding and resolution.

### Date Submitted
• Date Submitted: Enter the date the inquiry was submitted by the customer.

### Time Submitted
• Time Submitted: Enter the time the inquiry was submitted by the customer.

### Assigned User
• Assigned User: Select the user assigned to handle the inquiry to ensure timely and effective resolution.

### Action Taken
• Action Taken: Record any actions taken to address the customer's inquiry, providing a clear audit trail.

### Next Steps
• Next Steps: Document any next steps or follow-up actions required for resolution or further investigation.

### Customer Response
• Customer Response: Record any response provided by the customer regarding the inquiry or resolution.

### Resolution Status
• Resolution Status: Select the status of the inquiry (Open, Closed, or In Progress) for real-time tracking and evaluation.

### Comments
• Comments: Add any additional comments or notes about the inquiry or resolution.
