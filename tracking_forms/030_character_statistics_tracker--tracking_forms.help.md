# Character Statistics Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Character Statistics Tracker is a form designed to record and monitor character statistics during sessions or adventures. It allows users to keep track of their character's progress, experiences gained, health, resources collected, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form's purpose and the specific session or adventure you are tracking.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3. Enter the character's name and session number in the respective fields.
4. Record any experience gained, health points, resources collected, and status effects in the provided fields.
5. Add any additional notes about the session.
6. Enter your name and initials as the person recording the statistics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Character Name** (`character_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the character being tracked.
- **Session Number** (`session_number`, number, optional): Enter the index or date of the session.
- **Experience Gained** (`experience_gained`, number, optional): Enter the experience gained in this session.
- **Health Current** (`health_current`, number, optional): Enter the current health points of the character.
- **Resources Collected** (`resources_collected`, text, optional): Enter any resources collected during the session, such as loot or currency.
- **Milestones** (`milestones`, text, optional): Record any achieved milestones or level ups.
- **Status Effects** (`status_effects`, text, optional): Enter any temporary conditions impacting the character.
- **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the session.
- **Recorded By** (`recorded_by`, text, optional): Enter your name and initials as the person recording the statistics.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled in.
- Use this form for each session or adventure to maintain consistency and accuracy in tracking character statistics.
