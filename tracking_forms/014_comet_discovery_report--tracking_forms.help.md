# Comet Discovery Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Comet Discovery Report form is used to document and track the discovery of comets. This form helps to gather crucial information about the comet, including its designation, location, and characteristics. It's a vital tool for astronomers and researchers to record and analyze comet data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), carefully and accurately.
2. Provide the observer's name, email address, and affiliation.
3. Enter the date and time of the discovery.
4. Select the correct observation location (e.g., observatory or private location).
5. Choose the correct telescope and camera equipment used.
6. Specify the sky and weather conditions during the observation.
7. If applicable, upload images of the comet.
8. Indicate if follow-up verification is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Observer Name (1)**: *Required* Name of the person who discovered the comet.
  Enter the name of the person who made the discovery.
- **Email Address (2)**: *Required* Contact email for follow-up.
  Enter the email address of the person who made the discovery.
- **Affiliation (3)**: *Required* Observatory, institution, or independent researcher.
  Select the correct affiliation of the person who made the discovery.
- **Discovery Date (4)**: *Required* Date when the comet was first observed.
  Enter the date of the initial observation, in the format MM/DD/YY or YYYY/MM/DD.
- **Discovery Time (5)**: *Required* Time of initial observation.
  Enter the time of the initial observation in the format HH:MM.
- **-- Observation Location -- (6)**: Not required, but a note section to enter additional information.
  Enter any additional details about the location where the comet was observed.
- **Observatory Name (7)**: *Required* Name of the observatory or location.
  Enter the name of the observatory or location where the observation was made.
- **Latitude (8)**: *Required* Observation site latitude.
  Enter the latitude of the observation location in decimal degrees (-90 to +90).
- **Longitude (9)**: *Required* Observation site longitude.
  Enter the longitude of the observation location in decimal degrees (-180 to +180).
- **Elevation (10)**: Not required, but can be useful for atmospheric corrections.
  Enter the elevation of the observation location in meters.
- **Provisional Designation (12)**: Not required, but can be useful for future reference.
  Enter any provisional designation assigned to the comet.
- **Apparent Magnitude (13)**: *Required* Observed brightness.
  Enter the apparent brightness of the comet.
- **Coma Size (14)**: Not required, but can be useful for comet shape analysis.
  Enter the size of the comet's coma in arcminutes.
- **Tail Length (15)**: Not required, but can be useful for tail analysis.
  Enter the length of the comet's tail in degrees.
- **Tail Type (16)**: *Required* Type of comet tail observed.
  Select the type of tail observed (Ion, Dust, Both, or None Visible).
- **-- Observation Equipment -- (17)**: Not required, but a note section to enter additional information.
  Enter any additional details about the equipment used.
- **Telescope Type (18)**: *Required* Type of telescope used.
  Select the type of telescope used (Refractor, Reflector, Catadioptric, Radio Telescope, Naked Eye, or Binoculars).
- **Telescope Aperture (19)**: Not required, but can be useful for telescope characterization.
  Enter the aperture size of the telescope in millimeters.
- **Magnification (20)**: Not required, but can be useful for image analysis.
  Enter the magnification power used.
- **Camera Equipment (21)**: Not required, but can be useful for image analysis.
  Enter the camera or imaging device used.
- **Filter Used (22)**: Not required, but can be useful for image analysis.
  Enter any filters applied during the observation.
- **-- Images and Documentation -- (23)**: Not required, but a note section to enter additional information.
  Enter any additional details about images taken.
- **Were Images Captured (24)**: *Required* Did you photograph the comet.
  Select whether images were captured (Yes Single Image, Yes Multiple Images, or No Images).
- **Upload Images (25)**: Not required, but can be useful for image analysis.
  If images were captured, upload the image files here.
- **-- Additional Observations -- (26)**: Not required, but a note section to enter additional information.
  Enter any additional details about the observation.
- **Weather Conditions (27)**: *Required* Sky and weather during observation.
  Select the correct weather conditions (Clear, Partly Cloudy, Hazy, Poor Seeing, or Excellent Seeing).
- **Moon Phase (28)**: *Required* Moon phase during observation.
  Select the moon phase during the observation (New, Waxing Crescent, First Quarter, Waxing Gibbous, Full, Waning Gibbous, Last Quarter, or Waning Crescent).
- **Additional Notes (29)**: Not required, but can be useful for future reference.
  Enter any additional comments or observations.
- **Follow-Up Required (30)**: *Required* Does this discovery need verification.
  Select whether follow-up verification is required (Yes Please Verify, No Confirmation Needed, or Unsure).
