# fantasy_quarterback_waiver_pickup_tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fantasy_quarterback_waiver_pickup_tracker form is used to record waiver requests for fantasy football teams. It captures essential information about the requesting team, the player, and the status of the waiver request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Team ID** for the team submitting the waiver request.
2. Enter the **Player Name** of the player for whom the waiver request is being made.
3. Select the **Pickup Date** when the player's waiver is expected to be processed.
4. Indicate whether the **Waiver Requester** is the person submitting the request.
5. Enter the **Email** and **Phone** contact information for the Waiver Requester.
6. Add any additional **Notes** regarding the waiver request.
7. Choose the **Waiver Priority** level for the request (High, Medium, or Low).
8. Enter the **Waiver Requesting Player** name (if different from the Waiver Requester).
9. Indicate the **Waiver Granted** status for the request (True or False).
10. Select the **Waiver Request Status** (Granted, Denied, or Pending).
11. Finally, enter the **Form Completion Date** when the form was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team ID (id: 1)**: Enter the unique identifier for your fantasy football team.
* **Player Name (id: 2)**: Enter the name of the player for whom the waiver request is being made.
* **Pickup Date (id: 3)**: Enter the date when the waiver for the player is expected to be processed.
* **Waiver Requester (id: 4)**: Indicate who is making the waiver request by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Email (id: 5)**: Enter the email address of the Waiver Requester.
* **Phone (id: 6)**: Enter the phone number of the Waiver Requester.
* **Notes (id: 7)**: Add any additional comments or details about the waiver request.
* **Waiver Priority (id: 8)**: Choose the priority level for the waiver request (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Waiver Requesting Player (id: 9)**: Enter the name of the player making the waiver request (if different from the Waiver Requester).
* **Waiver Granted (id: 10)**: Indicate whether the waiver has been granted (True or False).
* **Waiver Request Status (id: 11)**: Select the status of the waiver request (Granted, Denied, or Pending).
* **Form Completion Date (id: 12)**: Enter the date when the form was completed.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If a waiver request has already been granted, denied, or is pending, update the relevant fields accordingly.
* If you have any questions or concerns about a waiver request, contact your team's management or administration for assistance.
