# Alarm Session Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to upload information about a specific alarm session, providing detailed data for incident tracking and response.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Session ID**, a unique identifier for this alarm session.
2.  Enter the **Alarm System ID**, the ID of the alarm system that triggered the alarm.
3.  Enter the **Location Name** and **Location Address** to specify the location where the alarm was triggered.
4.  Choose the **Alarm Date** and **Alarm Time** when the alarm was triggered.
5.  Select the **Alarm Type**, which can be one of the following options:
	*   Intrusion/Break-in
	*   Fire/Smoke
	*   Carbon Monoxide
	*   Panic/Duress
	*   Glass Break
	*   Motion Detected
	*   Door/Window Open
	*   System Tamper
	*   Low Battery
	*   Communication Loss
6.  Enter the **Alarm Zone** or sensor that triggered the alarm.
7.  Choose the **Alarm Priority**, which indicates the severity level of the alarm:
	*   Critical
	*   High
	*   Medium
	*   Low
	*   Informational
8.  Select the **Alarm Status**, which indicates the current status of this alarm:
	*   New
	*   Acknowledged
	*   In Progress
	*   Resolved
	*   False Alarm
9.  Indicate whether the **Response Team** was notified or not.
10. If applicable, enter the **Response Time** when the response team arrived.
11. Provide a detailed **Incident Description** of the alarm event.
12. Enter any **Actions Taken** in response to the alarm.
13. Upload any supporting **Evidence Files** or images related to the alarm.
14. Indicate whether there is **Video Footage** available or not.
15. Enter your **Name** as the person uploading this session.
16. Select the date of this **Upload**.
17. Enter any **Additional Notes** regarding the alarm session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Session ID**: Unique identifier for this alarm session.
*   **Alarm System ID**: ID of the alarm system that triggered the alarm.
*   **Location Name**: Name of the protected location.
*   **Location Address**: Full address of the alarm location.
*   **Alarm Date**: Date when the alarm was triggered.
*   **Alarm Time**: Time when the alarm was triggered.
*   **Alarm Type**: Type of alarm triggered.
*   **Alarm Zone**: Zone or sensor that triggered the alarm.
*   **Alarm Priority**: Severity level of the alarm.
*   **Alarm Status**: Current status of this alarm.
*   **Triggered By**: What or who triggered the alarm.
*   **Response Team Notified**: Was the response team contacted?
*   **Response Time**: Time when response team arrived.
*   **Incident Description**: Detailed description of the alarm event.
*   **Actions Taken**: What actions were taken in response to the alarm.
*   **Evidence Files**: Upload any supporting documents or images.
*   **Video Footage Available**: Is there video evidence?
*   **Uploaded By**: Name of person uploading this session.
*   **Upload Date**: Date of this upload.
*   **Additional Notes**: Any other relevant information.

Note: Please ensure you fill out all mandatory fields before submitting the form.
