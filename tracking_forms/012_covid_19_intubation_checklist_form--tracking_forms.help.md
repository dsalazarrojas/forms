# COVID 19 Intubation Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Intubation Checklist Form is a standardized document designed to guide healthcare professionals through the intubation process, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and recorded accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Collect the necessary patient information in the "Patient Information" section.
2. Complete the pre-intubation assessment by providing the patient's vital signs in the "Pre-Intubation Assessment" section.
3. Document the intubation procedure, including the route of intubation and tube size, in the "Intubation Procedure" section.
4. Monitor the patient's vital signs post-intubation in the "Post-Intubation Monitoring" section.
5. Record any complications that may have occurred during the intubation process.
6. Add any additional clinical notes in the "Clinical Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (2): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Medical Record Number** (3): Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Age** (4): Enter the patient's age.
* **Date of Intubation** (5): Enter the date the patient was intubated.
* **Time of Intubation** (6): Enter the time the patient was intubated.
* **Oxygen Saturation - Pre-Intubation** (8): Record the patient's oxygen saturation level before intubation.
* **Heart Rate - Pre-Intubation** (9): Record the patient's heart rate before intubation.
* **Blood Pressure - Pre-Intubation** (10): Record the patient's blood pressure before intubation.
* **Respiratory Rate - Pre-Intubation** (11): Record the patient's respiratory rate before intubation.
* **Route of Intubation** (13): Select the route of intubation (oral, nasal, surgical, or other).
* **Tube Size (mm)** (14): Record the size of the tube used for intubation.
* **Tube Depth at Teeth (cm)** (15): Record the depth of the tube at the patient's teeth.
* **Tube Placement Confirmed By** (16): Select the method used to confirm tube placement (chest X-ray, capnography, auscultation, visualization, or ultrasound).
* **Oxygen Saturation - Post-Intubation** (18): Record the patient's oxygen saturation level after intubation.
* **Ventilator Mode** (19): Select the ventilator mode used (AC/VC, AC/PC, SIMV, PSV, or other).
* **Sedation Administered** (20): Select whether sedation was administered (true or false).
* **Any Immediate Complications** (21): Record any complications that may have occurred during intubation.
* **Provider Name** (24): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who performed the intubation.
* **Date** (25): Enter the date the intubation was performed.
* **Additional Clinical Notes** (23): Record any additional clinical notes regarding the patient's care.
