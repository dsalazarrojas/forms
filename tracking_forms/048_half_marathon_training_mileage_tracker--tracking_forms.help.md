# Half Marathon Training Mileage Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Half Marathon Training Mileage Tracker form is a tool for athletes to record and track their training progress during half-marathon preparation. It helps to monitor and analyze performance metrics such as distance, pace, and dates.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please fill in the required fields with your training data.
2. Ensure that you are providing accurate and up-to-date information.
3. You may save your progress and come back later to edit or add new entries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name to save your progress under your own identity.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes if desired.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Select the date you started your training session.
* **Start Distance** (`start_distance`, number, optional): Record the initial distance covered during your session.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you finished your session.
* **End Distance** (`end_distance`, number, optional): Record the total distance covered during your session.
* **Pace** (`pace`, number, optional): Note your average pace per mile or kilometer. 
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Write any additional notes about your session you would like to remember.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, text, optional): If you are working with a coach, enter their name to save their contact information.
* **Coach Email** (`coach_email`, email, optional): If applicable, enter your coach's email address.
* **Club Name** (`club_name`, text, optional): If you are part of a club, record the name for future reference.
* **Club Email** (`club_email`, email, optional): Enter your club's email address if they have one.

## Tips

* Ensure you keep your records accurate and up-to-date to track your progress effectively.
* Review and update your form entries as often as necessary to monitor your improvements.
* You may come back to this form multiple times to record new entries and track your progress over time.
