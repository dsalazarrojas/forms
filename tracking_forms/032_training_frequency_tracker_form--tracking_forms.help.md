# Training Frequency Tracker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Frequency Tracker Form is designed to track the frequency of training and exercise progress of participants. It collects information on the number of times an individual trains, logs exercises, and records their overall progress in the training process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant Name**, **Contact Number**, and **Email Address** in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the **Exercise Log** options that best describe your recent exercise activities.
3. Enter the specific **Workout Log** of your recent training activities.
4. Enter the frequency of your **Training Frequency** by selecting a date.
5. Select multiple options for **Participant Progress** that apply to your current status.
6. Enter any additional comments in **Progress Tracker**.
7. Finally, confirm your **Frequency Input** by entering a numerical value.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name, as this will be used to identify you within the tracking system.

### Exercise Log
* **Exercise Log** (`exercise_log`, `select_one`, required): Choose one option that best describes your exercise activities.

### Workout Log
* **Workout Log** (`workout_log`, `text`, required): Describe your recent training activities, including specific exercises and routines performed.

### Frequency Input
* **Frequency Input** (`frequency_input`, `number`, required): Enter the number of times you train per week.

### Progress Tracker
* **Progress Tracker** (`progress_tracker`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple options that describe your progress status. This field is used to track your growth within the training process.

### Participant Progress
* **Participant Progress** (`participant_progress`, `text`, required): Describe your current progress in a specific training activity or phase.

### Training Frequency
* **Training Frequency** (`training_frequency`, `date`, required): Select a date to indicate when you last trained. This is used to track consistency in your training schedule.

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number for communication and follow-up purposes.

### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication and follow-up purposes.

## Tips
- Be accurate and consistent when filling out the form to ensure accurate tracking and progress.
- Review and update your information regularly to reflect changes in your training frequency and progress.
- Use this form as a tool to monitor and evaluate your progress, and make adjustments as needed.
