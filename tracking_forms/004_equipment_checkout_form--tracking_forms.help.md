# Equipment Checkout Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for tracking equipment checkouts. It helps to record important information about the equipment being checked out, including the company name, technician name, start and end dates, status, description, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Provide a valid Company Name**. Enter the name of the company that owns the equipment being checked out.
2. **Enter the Technician Name**. Select the name of the technician responsible for checking out the equipment.
3. **Select Start Date**. Choose the date when the equipment was checked out.
4. **Select End Date**. Choose the date when the equipment is expected to be returned.
5. **Choose Status**. Select the status of the equipment: Active or Inactive.
6. **Description of the equipment**. Provide a brief description of the equipment being checked out.
7. **Enter Form ID**. Enter a unique identifier for the form.
8. **Form was created on**. This field is automatically populated with the current date and time, but you can edit it if needed.
9. **Form was updated on**. This field is automatically populated with the current date and time, but you can edit it if needed.
10. **Enter User ID**. Enter your User ID for internal tracking purposes.
11. **Provide a detailed description of the form**. Describe the purpose and context of the form in detail.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company that owns the equipment.
* **Technician Name** (`technician_name`, text, required): Select the name of the technician responsible for checking out the equipment.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Choose the date when the equipment was checked out.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Choose the date when the equipment is expected to be returned.
* **Status** (`equipment_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the equipment: Active or Inactive.
* **Description** (`equipment_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the equipment being checked out.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required): Enter a unique identifier for the form.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, required): This field is automatically populated with the current date and time, but you can edit it if needed.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, required): This field is automatically populated with the current date and time, but you can edit it if needed.
* **User ID** (`user_id`, text, required): Enter your User ID for internal tracking purposes.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, required): Describe the purpose and context of the form in detail.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate tracking.
* Double-check your dates to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or support team for clarification.
