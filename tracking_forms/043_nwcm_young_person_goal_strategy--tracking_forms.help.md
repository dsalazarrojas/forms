# NWCM Young Person Goal Strategy - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NWCM Young Person Goal Strategy form is used to collect information about young individuals within the New World Community Model (NWCM). This information helps identify and support their specific goals and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Gather all required information and permissions before starting.
3. Fill in the form with the correct and accurate information for the young person.
4. Verify your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the young person's first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: true): Enter the young person's last name.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the young person's date of birth.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the young person's current address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the young person's contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the young person's email address.

## Tips

* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* Use the correct format for the "Date of birth" field (e.g., DD/MM/YYYY).
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions and information before completing the form.
* If unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities or support team for guidance.
