# performance_appraisal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "performance_appraisal_form" is designed for use in performance appraisals to evaluate the performance of employees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select the relevant performance period from the "Performance Period" dropdown menu.
3. Rate the employee's overall performance using the "Overall Performance Rating" dropdown menu.
4. Provide feedback and comments regarding the employee's performance in the "Performance Comments" text field.
5. Identify areas where the employee has shown improvement in the "Development Areas" dropdown menu.
6. Identify areas that require improvement or development in the "Improvement Areas" dropdown menu.
7. Describe any action plans or steps that will be taken to address areas of improvement in the "Action Plans" text field.
8. Determine if further follow up is required or not using the "Follow Up" dropdown menu.
9. (Optional) Sign and date the form as the employee and reviewer to confirm completion and agreement with the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Name (`Employee Name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.

### Performance Period (`Performance Period`, select_one, required)
Select the relevant performance period being assessed (e.g. "Yes" for the current period).

### Overall Performance Rating (`Overall Performance Rating`, select_multiple, required)
Rate the employee's overall performance using the following options: "Good", "Fair".

### Performance Comments (`Performance Comments`, text, required)
Provide feedback and comments regarding the employee's performance.

### Development Areas (`Development Areas`, select_multiple, required)
Identify areas where the employee has shown improvement or development.

### Improvement Areas (`Improvement Areas`, select_multiple, required)
Identify areas that require improvement or development.

### Action Plans (`Action Plans`, text, required)
Describe any action plans or steps that will be taken to address areas of improvement.

### Follow Up (`Follow Up`, select_one, required)
Determine if further follow up is required or not (e.g. "Yes" for further action).

### Employee Signature (`Employee Signature`, text, not required)
Optional: sign and date the form as the employee to confirm completion.

### Reviewer Signature (`Reviewer Signature`, text, not required)
Optional: sign and date the form as the reviewer to confirm agreement with the evaluation.
