# Car Park Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Park Record Form is designed to record and track vehicle parking information at a parking lot. This form helps to ensure accurate and efficient management of parking records for vehicles. It captures essential details about the vehicle, parking location, and parking duration to provide quick and accurate information for parking management.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start by providing basic information about the vehicle, including its make and model, license plate number, and color.
2.  Enter the vehicle owner's full name and phone number as the primary contact for the parking record.
3.  Choose the parking location, including the exact spot where the vehicle is parked.
4.  Enter the date and time of the vehicle's parking entry and exit.
5.  If known, estimate the duration of parking in hours.
6.  Select the parking fee status as per the established parking policies (Paid, Unpaid, Waived, or Monthly Pass).
7.  Confirm or deny the vehicle's parking record entry.
8.  Enter your name for parking record confirmation.
9.  Provide any additional comments or requests regarding the parking experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Make and Model** (`vehicle_make_model_park`): Enter the make and model of the vehicle being parked.
* **Vehicle License Plate Number** (`vehicle_license_plate`): To identify the vehicle, please enter the exact license plate number.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color_park`): Enter the primary color of the vehicle for easy identification.
* **Vehicle Owner Full Name** (`vehicle_owner_full_name`): Enter your full name as the vehicle owner or authorized driver.
* **Vehicle Owner Phone Number** (`vehicle_owner_phone`): Enter the primary contact phone number for the vehicle owner.
* **Vehicle Owner Email Address** (`vehicle_owner_email`): Provide the vehicle owner's email address for parking record confirmation (optional).
* **Parking Location Name** (`parking_location_name`): Identify the parking location by entering the exact spot name (e.g., "North Lot, Level 2, Spot 45").
* **Parking Entry Date** (`parking_entry_date`): Enter the date when the vehicle entered the lot.
* **Parking Entry Time** (`parking_entry_time`): Enter the time of the vehicle's entry into the lot.
* **Parking Exit Date** (`parking_exit_date`): If the vehicle exited the parking lot, enter the date. Otherwise, leave blank.
* **Parking Exit Time** (`parking_exit_time`): If the vehicle exited the parking lot, enter the time. Otherwise, leave blank.
* **Estimated Parking Duration (Hours)** (`parking_duration_estimate`): If known, estimate the parking duration in hours.
* **Parking Fee Status** (`parking_fee_status`): Select the payment status according to the established parking policies (Paid, Unpaid, Waived, or Monthly Pass).
* **Confirmation of Parking Record Entry** (`parking_record_acknowledgement`): Confirm or deny the vehicle parking record entry.
* **Parking Record Confirmation Name** (`signature_confirmation_park`): Enter your name to confirm the parking record entry.
* **Additional Comments or Requests** (`additional_parking_comments`): Provide any additional comments or requests regarding the parking experience.

By following this guide, users can complete the form accurately and efficiently, ensuring that parking records are up-to-date and reliable.
