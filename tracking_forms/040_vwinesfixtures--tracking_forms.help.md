# Vwinesfixtures - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vwinesfixtures form is designed to collect information about wines and fixtures. This form helps you track and organize data about home and away teams, their scores, dates, and times for upcoming or past events. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant pages to fill in the necessary information about the wines and fixtures.
2. Fill in the Home Score field with the score of the home team.
3. Fill in the Away Score field with the score of the away team.
4. Fill in the Home Team field with the name of the home team.
5. Fill in the Away Team field with the name of the away team.
6. Fill in the Date field with the date of the event.
7. Fill in the Time field with the time of the event.
8. If necessary, add notes about the event in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Score** (`home_score`, number, required: false): Fill in the score of the home team.
* **Away Score** (`away_score`, number, required: false): Fill in the score of the away team.
* **Home Team** (`home_team_name`, text, required: false): Fill in the name of the home team.
* **Away Team** (`away_team_name`, text, required: false): Fill in the name of the away team.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Fill in the date of the event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Fill in the time of the event.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about the event. 

## Tips
* Please fill in accurate and up-to-date information about the wines and fixtures.
* You can add any number of pages to collect more data about the wines and fixtures.
* Remember to save your changes to avoid losing your work.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your administrator for assistance.
