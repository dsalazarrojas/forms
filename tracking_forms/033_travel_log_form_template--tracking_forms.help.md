# Travel Log Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record travel-related activities and events, providing a structured way to document trips, including travel dates, destinations, modes of transport, and associated expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your travel details in the designated fields:
	* **Travel Details**: Enter a brief description of your travel.
	* **Destination**: Enter the destination of your travel.
	* **Travel Start Date**: Enter the date of your travel start.
	* **Travel Start Time**: Enter the time of your travel start.
	* **Travel End Date**: Enter the date of your travel end.
	* **Travel End Time**: Enter the time of your travel end.
	* **Travel Duration**: Enter the duration of your travel in hours.
	* **Mode of Transport**: Choose the mode of transport used for your travel.
2. Enter relevant information about your travel:
	* **Travel Reason**: Enter the reason for your travel.
	* **Travel Budget**: Enter the total budget for your travel.
	* **Travel Expenses**: Enter any expenses related to your travel.
	* **Travel Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your travel.
3. Assign users who are associated with this travel:
	* **Assigned Users**: Choose users who are assigned to this travel.
4. Update the travel status:
	* **Travel Status**: Choose the status of your travel (approved or pending).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Details**: Enter a brief description of your travel.
* **Destination**: Enter the destination of your travel. This will help you keep track of where you traveled to.
* **Travel Start Date**: Enter the date when you started your travel. This field is used to calculate the duration of your travel.
* **Travel Start Time**: Enter the time when you started your travel.
* **Travel End Date**: Enter the date when you ended your travel.
* **Travel End Time**: Enter the time when you ended your travel.
* **Travel Duration**: Enter the duration of your travel in hours.
* **Mode of Transport**: Choose the mode of transport used for your travel (e.g., car, bus, plane, train, bike, other).
* **Travel Reason**: Enter the reason for your travel. This will help you keep track of the purpose of your travel.
* **Travel Budget**: Enter the total budget for your travel.
* **Travel Expenses**: Enter any expenses related to your travel (e.g., food, accommodations, etc.).
* **Travel Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your travel.
* **Assigned Users**: Choose users who are assigned to this travel. This will help you keep track of who else is responsible for your travel.
* **Travel Status**: Choose the status of your travel (approved or pending).
* **Email**: Enter your email address (optional).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in all fields.
* Use the mode of transport options (e.g., car, bus, plane, train, bike, other) to choose the most relevant option for your travel.
* If you have any expenses related to your travel, enter them in the travel expenses field.
* Use the assigned users field to choose users who are associated with your travel.
* Update the travel status regularly to keep track of the progress of your travel.
