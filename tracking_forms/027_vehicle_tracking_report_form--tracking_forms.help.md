# Vehicle Tracking Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Tracking Report Form is used to collect information about vehicle trips, including trip dates, destinations, driver and vehicle details, and associated costs. This form helps the organization to track and record trip data for administrative purposes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the trip date in the "Trip Date" field (field 1). This is the date of the trip you are reporting.
2. Enter the destination of the trip in the "Destination" field (field 2).
3. Enter the name of the driver in the "Driver Name" field (field 3).
4. Enter the driver's phone number in the "Driver Phone" field (field 4).
5. Enter the make of the vehicle used for the trip in the "Vehicle Make" field (field 5).
6. Enter the model of the vehicle used for the trip in the "Vehicle Model" field (field 6).
7. Enter the trip cost in the "Trip Cost" field (field 7). This can be a numeric value, such as a dollar amount.
8. Enter the driver's license number in the "Driver License" field (field 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Trip Date (1)**: Date of the trip.
	+ This is the date on which the trip was taken.
- **Destination (2)**: Place or location the trip was taken to.
	+ Enter the location or city of the trip destination.
- **Driver Name (3)**: Name of the driver of the vehicle.
	+ Enter the name of the person who was driving the vehicle.
- **Driver Phone (4)**: Phone number of the driver.
	+ Enter the phone number of the person who was driving the vehicle.
- **Vehicle Make (5)**: Make of the vehicle used for the trip.
	+ Enter the make or brand of the vehicle used for the trip.
- **Vehicle Model (6)**: Model of the vehicle used for the trip.
	+ Enter the specific model of the vehicle used for the trip.
- **Trip Cost (7)**: Cost associated with the trip.
	+ Enter the cost of the trip, including any expenses or expenditures.
- **Driver License (8)**: License number of the driver.
	+ Enter the license number of the person who was driving the vehicle.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information for all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or the form administrator for guidance.
* This form is designed to be completed by individuals with access to the vehicle tracking system. If you do not have access, please contact your supervisor or the form administrator for assistance.
