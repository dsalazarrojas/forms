# Daily Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Submission Form is a form designed to collect information from users, allowing you to track daily submissions. This form is meant for tracking and logging data for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information and submit it. The form is divided into the following sections:

1. **Form Data**: This is the main section where you can provide any additional information you want to be tracked.
2. **Date**: Enter the date of the submission.
3. **Submitter**: Provide the name of the person or entity submitting the information.
4. **Submission**: Describe the nature of the submission (e.g., report, request, etc.).
5. **Email**: Enter the email address of the submitter or a contact email address associated with the submission.
6. **Website**: Enter a website related to the submission (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (form_data, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional information you want to track.
* **Date** (date, optional):
	+ Select the date of the submission.
* **Submitter** (submitter, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the person or entity submitting the information.
* **Submission** (submission, text, optional):
	+ Describe the nature of the submission (e.g., report, request, etc.).
* **Email** (email, email, optional):
	+ Enter the email address of the submitter or a contact email address associated with the submission.
* **Website** (website, text, optional):
	+ Enter a website related to the submission (if applicable).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check the date and email fields for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, don't hesitate to reach out to form@gic.mx.
