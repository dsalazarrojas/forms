# Expense Statement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Statement form is used by employees to accurately report their expenses, which is essential for financial planning and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Expense Statement" button to open the form.
2. Select the date of the expense from the calendar.
3. Choose a valid expense category from the options.
4. Describe the expense in detail.
5. Enter the expense amount.
6. Enter your employee ID for submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Expense Category** (`Expense Category`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select one of the pre-defined categories to classify the expense accurately.
* **Expense Date** (`Expense Date`, `date`, **Optional**): Enter the date of the expense. This will be used for accurate financial planning.
* **Expense Description** (`Expense Description`, `text`, **Optional**): Describe the expense in as much detail as possible. This field is helpful for further analysis.
* **Expense Amount** (`Expense Amount`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the total expense amount. This is a crucial field for financial reporting.
* **Expense Submitted By** (`Expense Submitted By`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter your employee ID to identify the expense submitter. 

## Tips
* Double-check that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Review the form for any errors before submission.
* Use the same format for date entry (MM/DD/YY).
* Use the provided expense categories for classification.
* Keep in mind that this form is used for financial planning, so accuracy is essential.
