# .report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track student attendance, progress, and engagement in virtual learning environments. It helps us gather data to inform decisions on course optimization, resource allocation, and student support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's weekly virtual log, including all relevant activities and progress.
2. Report the number of students enrolled in the virtual platform.
3. Record the time spent on the virtual platform, as well as other activities.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments about the student's progress.
5. Provide your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Weekly Virtual Log**: Enter a brief summary of the student's progress and activities in the virtual platform for the week.
* **Teaching Activities**: Describe any teaching activities or initiatives taken during the week, such as lectures, discussions, or workshops.
* **Time Spent on Virtual Platform**: Record the total hours spent on the virtual platform by the student.
* **Students Enrolled**: Enter the number of students currently enrolled in the virtual platform.
* **Time Spent on Non-Virtual Platforms**: Report the time spent on other learning platforms or activities.
* **Time Spent on Other Activities**: Record the time spent on non-platform related activities.
* **Total Hours**: Calculate the total hours spent by the student across all platforms.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the student's progress or needs.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Chat with Jimmy**: Enter any relevant details about your interaction or conversation with Jimmy.

## Tips
* Ensure that you complete the form accurately and consistently for each student.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the support team.
