# Diagnostic Angiography Event Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to log and track events related to diagnostic angiography procedures, including patient information, procedure details, image quality, and review status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter patient information in the `patient_info` field.
2. Enter details about the angiography procedure in the `angiography_info` field.
3. Enter the image ID, image quality, and cath lab ID in the `image_id`, `image_quality`, and `cath_lab_id` fields, respectively.
4. Select the procedure status from the options provided in the `procedure_status` field.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments in the `notes` field.
6. Review and update the form as needed, including selecting a review status and entering comments or approval/rejection comments.
7. Enter the reviewer's name and role in the `reviewer_name` and `reviewer_role` fields, respectively.
8. Enter the review date and time in the `review_date` and `review_time` fields, respectively.
9. Select the submitter's role from the options provided in the `submitter_role` field.
10. Enter the submission date and time in the `submitted_date` and `submitted_time` fields, respectively.
11. Update the form as needed, including selecting the updated role and entering the updated date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_info**: Enter the patient's identifying information, including name, date of birth, and medical record number.
* **angiography_info**: Enter details about the angiography procedure, including the procedure code and any relevant notes.
* **image_id**: Enter the ID number of the angiography image.
* **image_quality**: Enter the quality of the angiography image, using a scale of 1-5.
* **cath_lab_id**: Enter the ID number of the cath lab where the procedure was performed.
* **procedure_status**: Select the status of the procedure from the options provided: "Procedure Not Started", "Procedure Complete", or "Procedure Incomplete".
* **notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the procedure.
* **review_status**: Select the review status from the options provided: "Not Reviewed", "Approved", or "Rejected".
* **review_comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the review.
* **reviewer_name**: Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **reviewer_role**: Select the role of the reviewer from the options provided: "Radiologist", "Radiologist Resident", or "Cath Lab Manager".
* **review_date**: Enter the date the review was completed.
* **review_time**: Enter the time the review was completed.
* **submitter**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **submitter_role**: Select the role of the submitter from the options provided: "Physican", "Radiologist", or "Cath Lab Manager".
* **submitted_date**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **submitted_time**: Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **updated_by**: Enter the name of the person who last updated the form.
* **updated_date**: Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **updated_time**: Enter the time the form was last updated.
* **updated_role**: Select the role of the person who last updated the form from the options provided: "Admin" or "User".
* **comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the procedure.
