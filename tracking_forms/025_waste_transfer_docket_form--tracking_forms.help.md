# Waste Transfer Docket Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waste Transfer Docket Form is used to document and track waste transfer operations between generators and receivers, ensuring accurate and efficient recording of transfer details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Generator ID, which identifies the source of the waste.
2. Enter the Transfer Date, when the waste was transferred.
3. Enter the Transporter ID, who handled the transfer.
4. Record the Quantity Transferred, the amount of waste moved.
5. Describe the Waste Characteristics, any notable details about the waste.
6. Enter the Receiver ID, who received the waste.
7. Add any additional Notes, comments, or observations about the transfer.
8. Select whether to send an Email Notification to the involved parties.
9. Enter the Email Address or Phone Number of the involved parties for further contact.
10. Confirm submission by selecting the "Confirm Submit Form" option.
11. Ensure that the Email Address and Phone Number fields match the selected option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Waste Transfer Docket Form** (`name: waste_transfer_docket_form`): This is the title of the form.
* **Generator ID** (`name: generator_id`): Enter the ID of the source of the waste.
* **Transfer Date** (`name: transfer_date`): Enter the date when the waste was transferred.
* **Transporter ID** (`name: transporter_id`): Enter the ID of the person or organization handling the transfer.
* **Quantity Transferred** (`name: quantity_transferred`): Enter the amount of waste moved.
* **Waste Characteristics** (`name: waste_characteristics`): Describe any notable details about the waste.
* **Receiver ID** (`name: receiver_id`): Enter the ID of the person or organization receiving the waste.
* **Notes** (`name: notes`): Add any additional comments or observations about the transfer.
* **Email Notification** (`name: email_notification`): Select whether to send an email notification.
* **Phone Number** (`name: phone_number`): Enter the phone number of the involved parties.
* **Submit** (`name: submit`): Select how to proceed with the submission: Submit Form, Decline Form, or Save Draft.
* **Email Address** (`name: email_address`): Enter the email address of the involved parties.
* **Confirm Submit** (`name: confirm_submit`): Confirm that the submission is accurate.
* **Confirm Email Address** (`name: confirm_email_address`): Confirm the email address entered.
* **Confirm Phone Number** (`name: confirm_phone`): Confirm the phone number entered.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
- Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
- Use clear and concise language when describing waste characteristics and notes.
- Verify that email and phone numbers are accurate and up-to-date.
