# Commercial Invoice Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Commercial Invoice Form is used to document the terms and conditions of international shipments between two parties. It ensures accurate tracking and payment for goods exchanged.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Invoice Number**: Enter a unique identifier for the invoice.
2.  **Invoice Date**: Enter the date of the invoice.
3.  **Exporting Company Information**:
    *   **Exporting Company Name**: Enter the name of the company or person exporting the goods.
    *   **Exporting Address**: Enter the complete address of the exporting company or person.
    *   **Exporting Contact**: Enter the contact person's name.
    *   **Exporting Phone**: Enter the contact number.
    *   **Exporting Email**: Enter the exporting company's or person's email address.
    *   **Exporting Tax ID**: Enter the tax identification number of the exporting company or person (optional).
4.  **Importing Company Information**:
    *   **Importing Company Name**: Enter the name of the company or person importing the goods.
    *   **Importing Address**: Enter the complete address of the importing company or person.
    *   **Importing Contact**: Enter the contact person's name.
    *   **Importing Phone**: Enter the contact number.
    *   **Importing Email**: Enter the importing company's or person's email address.
    *   **Importing Tax ID**: Enter the tax identification number of the importing company or person (optional).
5.  **Shipment Details**:
    *   **Country of Origin**: Enter the country where the goods were made or produced.
    *   **Country of Destination**: Enter the country where the goods will be delivered.
    *   **Port of Loading**: Enter the port where the goods will be loaded.
    *   **Port of Discharge**: Enter the port where the goods will be discharged.
    *   **Shipping Method**: Select how the goods will be shipped (e.g., Ocean Freight, Air Freight, etc.).
    *   **Terms of Sale**: Select the terms of the sale (e.g., EXW, FOB, CIF, etc.).
6.  **Shipment Items**:
    *   Enter details for each item shipped, including:
        *   **Item ID**: Enter the item's identifier.
        *   **Item Description**: Enter a description of the item.
        *   **Item Quantity**: Enter the number of units.
        *   **Item Weight**: Enter the weight of the item in kilograms.
        *   **Item Price**: Enter the price per unit of the item.
        *   **Item Total**: Calculate the total cost of the item by multiplying the quantity and price.
7.  **Invoice Totals**:
    *   **Total Weight**: Enter the total weight of all items.
    *   **Subtotal**: Calculate the sum of all items.
    *   **Shipping Cost**: Enter the shipping costs.
    *   **Insurance Cost**: Enter any insurance costs.
    *   **Other Charges**: Enter any additional fees.
    *   **Total Amount**: Calculate the total amount due by multiplying the subtotal and total weight.
8.  **Payment Information**:
    *   **Payment Terms**: Select when payment is due.
    *   **Payment Method**: Select how payment will be made (e.g., Wire Transfer, Letter of Credit, etc.).
    *   **Bank Name**: Enter the name of the bank for wire transfer payments.
    *   **Bank Account Number**: Enter the account number of the bank.
9.  **Certification**:
    *   **Exporting Signature**: Enter the authorized signature of the exporting company or person.
    *   **Signature Date**: Enter the date of the signature.
    *   **Additional Comments**: Enter any other information about the shipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Invoice Number**: A unique identifier for the invoice.
*   **Invoice Date**: The date of the invoice.
*   **Exporting Company Information**: The exporting company or person's details.
*   **Importing Company Information**: The importing company or person's details.
*   **Shipment Details**: Details about the shipment, including country of origin, country of destination, port of loading, port of discharge, shipping method, and terms of sale.
*   **Shipment Items**: Details for each item shipped, including item ID, item description, item quantity, item weight, item price, and item total.
*   **Invoice Totals**: The total weight of all items, subtotal, shipping cost, insurance cost, other charges, and total amount due.
*   **Payment Information**: Details about payment terms, payment method, bank name, and bank account number.
*   **Certification**: The authorized signature and date of the exporting company or person.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   Use the correct format for dates (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   For payment terms, select the correct due date for payment.
*   For payment method, select how the payment will be made.
*   Verify that all fields are completed before submission. 

Please review and adjust the form according to your company's or business requirements and local regulations.
