# details_barbershop - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to track and manage customer interactions at a barbershop, including appointments, screenings, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form title to describe the purpose or reason for the visit.
2. Enter the customer's name in the Customer Name field.
3. Enter the date of the visit in the Date of Visit field.
4. Select the Screening Status of the customer from the drop-down menu.
5. Enter the customer's email address in the Customer Email field.
6. Enter the Barber ID of the assigned barber.
7. Select the Screening Status of the assigned barber from the drop-down menu.
8. Enter any additional Comments about the customer or visit in the Comments field.
9. Enter the customer's phone number in the Customer Phone field.
10. Enter the Assigned Tool used during the visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): A brief description of the purpose or reason for the visit.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the customer.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required): Enter the date of the visit.
* **Screening Status** (`screening_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the customer's screening (Active or Inactive).
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Barber ID** (`barber_id`, number, required): Enter the ID number of the assigned barber.
* **Barber Screening Status** (`barber_screening_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the barber's screening (Active or Inactive).
* **Comments** (`barber_comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments about the customer or visit.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Enter the tool used during the visit.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the customer's email address for accuracy.
* Select the correct screening status for the customer and barber.
* Enter any additional comments to help with customer tracking and management.
