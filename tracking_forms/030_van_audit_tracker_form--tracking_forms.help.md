# Van Audit Tracker Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Van Audit Tracker Form is a form designed to collect and track details about vans. It is intended to store information such as van models, registration dates, inspection dates, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Basic Information
- Fill in the basic information about the van, including the "Van" (name of the van), "Vehicle Model", and "Van Color". These fields are optional, so you can skip them if you don't have the necessary information.

### Step 2: Tracking Dates
- Enter the "Van Registration Date" to record the date the van was registered.
- Enter the "Van Inspection Date" to record the date the van was last inspected.

### Step 3: Driver Information
- Enter the "Van Driver Name" and their contact information, including their "Van Driver Phone" and "Van Driver Email".
- Fill in the "Van Driver Address" if required.

### Step 4: Additional Details
- Select the current "Van Odometer Reading".
- Enter any "Audit Results" or "Van Status" information.
- Add any additional "Notes" about the van.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Van** (`van_audit_vehicle`, text, optional): Enter the name of the van.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the model of the van from the provided options (e.g., Ford F-350, Ford F-250, Chevrolet 3500).
* **Van Color** (`van_color`, select_one, optional): Choose the color of the van from the provided options (e.g., Black, White, Grey).
* **Van Year** (`van_year`, number, optional): Enter the year of the van.
* **Van Registration Date** (`van_registration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the van was registered.
* **Van Inspection Date** (`van_inspection_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the van was last inspected.
* **Van Status** (`van_status`, text, optional): Enter any status updates about the van.
* **Audit Results** (`audit_results`, text, optional): Enter any audit results or findings.
* **Van Driver Name** (`van_driver_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the van driver.
* **Van Driver Phone** (`van_driver_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the van driver.
* **Van Driver Email** (`van_driver_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the van driver.
* **Van Driver Address** (`van_driver_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the van driver.
* **Van Odometer Reading** (`van_odometer_reading`, number, optional): Enter the current odometer reading of the van.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the van.

## Tips
- Please fill in as much information as you have available.
- If unsure about a field, use your best judgment to estimate or provide a placeholder.
- You can save your changes and come back to this form later to add or update information.
