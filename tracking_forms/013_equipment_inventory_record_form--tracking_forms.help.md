# equipment_inventory_record_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The equipment_inventory_record_form is used to create or edit a record for an equipment item in the company's inventory. This form captures key information about the equipment, including asset details, purchase and maintenance history, and warranty information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the company you are representing by entering the company name in the "Company" field.
2. Choose the type of equipment from the "Asset Type" field, which will help categorize the equipment in the inventory.
3. Enter the asset number assigned to the equipment in the "Asset Number" field.
4. Provide the model number of the equipment in the "Model Number" field.
5. Enter the date the equipment was purchased in the "Purchase Date" field.
6. Enter the purchase price of the equipment in the "Purchase Price" field.
7. Specify the location where the equipment is currently situated in the "Location" field.
8. Assess the condition of the equipment using the "Condition" field, which should be one of "Excellent", "Fair", or "Poor".
9. Indicate whether maintenance is required or has been completed on the equipment in the "Maintenance Status" field.
10. Enter the date of the last maintenance performed on the equipment in the "Last Maintenance Date" field, if applicable.
11. Provide any additional notes or comments about the equipment in the "Notes" field.
12. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the equipment in the "Contact Name" field.
13. Provide the contact phone number and email of the contact person in the "Contact Phone" and "Contact Email" fields.
14. Enter any relevant equipment tags or keywords in the "Equipment Tag" field.
15. Indicate whether the equipment is currently active or inactive in the "Equipment Status" field.
16. Specify the location where the equipment is currently situated in the "Equipment Location" field.
17. Enter the serial number of the equipment in the "Serial Number" field.
18. Enter the start date of the warranty period in the "Warranty Start Date" field.
19. Enter the end date of the warranty period in the "Warranty End Date" field.
20. Indicate whether the equipment has a warranty in the "Warranty Type" field.
21. Enter the name of the warranty provider in the "Warranty Provider" field.
22. Specify the status of the warranty in the "Warranty Status" field.
23. Choose the category of the asset from the "Asset Category" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company**: Enter the name of the company you are representing.
* **Asset Type**: Choose the type of equipment from the list. Examples: Computer Equipment, Furniture, Office Supplies.
* **Asset Number**: Enter the asset number assigned to the equipment.
* **Model Number**: Enter the model number of the equipment.
* **Purchase Date**: Enter the date the equipment was purchased.
* **Purchase Price**: Enter the price the equipment was purchased for.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the equipment is currently situated.
* **Condition**: Assess the condition of the equipment using the options: Excellent, Fair, Poor.
* **Maintenance Status**: Indicate whether maintenance is required or has been completed on the equipment.
* **Last Maintenance Date**: Enter the date of the last maintenance performed on the equipment, if applicable.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the equipment.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the equipment.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email of the contact person.
* **Equipment Tag**: Enter any relevant equipment tags or keywords.
* **Equipment Status**: Indicate whether the equipment is currently active or inactive.
* **Equipment Location**: Specify the location where the equipment is currently situated.
* **Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the equipment.
* **Warranty Start Date**: Enter the start date of the warranty period.
* **Warranty End Date**: Enter the end date of the warranty period.
* **Warranty Type**: Indicate whether the equipment has a warranty.
* **Warranty Provider**: Enter the name of the warranty provider.
* **Warranty Status**: Specify the status of the warranty.
* **Asset Category**: Choose the category of the asset from the list. Examples: IT and Technology, Furniture, Office Supplies.
