# Plasma Donation Payment Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plasma Donation Payment Tracker is a form designed to record and track payments received for plasma donations. This form is used to document the collection site, donor information, payment details, and administrator contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the collection site where the plasma was collected.
2. Enter the donor's ID number.
3. Select the payment date and enter the amount received.
4. Choose the payment method used.
5. (Optional) Re-enter the collection site information if it was not populated automatically.
6. Enter the administrator's ID number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Collection Site** (`collection_site`, text, not required): Enter the location where the plasma was collected.
* **Donor ID** (`donor_id`, number, not required): Enter the unique identifier for the donor.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, not required): Enter the date when the payment was made.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, not required): Choose from the available payment options.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, not required): Enter the amount received as payment.
* **Collection Site** (`collection_site`, text, not required): Re-enter the collection site information if not populated automatically.
* **Administrator ID** (`administrator_id`, number, not required): Enter the identifier for the administrator who handled the payment.
