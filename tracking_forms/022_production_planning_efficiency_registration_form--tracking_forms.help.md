# Production Planning Efficiency Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to track and record production planning data for efficient production runs. It captures essential information for monitoring production performance and optimizing production processes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Choose the correct options for each field, as they appear in the dropdown menus.
3. Enter your answers accurately and completely in the text fields.
4. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): A free-text field for any additional information or comments you'd like to provide about your production run.
* **Production Date** (`production_date`, date, optional): The date when your production run took place.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): The start time of your production run.
* **Finish Time** (`finish_time`, time, optional): The finish time of your production run.
* **Work Center** (`work_center`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you used any of the following:
	+ Yes (used)
	+ No (not used)
* **Job Number** (`job_number`, text, optional): The unique identifier for your production run.
* **Job Name** (`job_name`, text, optional): A brief description of your production run.
* **Task ID** (`task_id`, number, optional): A unique identifier for your production task.
* **Work Order** (`work_order`, text, optional): The work order number for your production run.
* **Production Type** (`production_type`, select_one, optional): Indicate if your production run was:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Production Status** (`production_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate the status of your production run:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **Equipment Used** (`equipment_used`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if any of the following equipment were used:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Shift** (`shift`, number, optional): The shift number for your production run.
* **Work Center ID** (`work_center_id`, number, optional): A unique identifier for your work center.
* **Production Hours** (`production_hours`, number, optional): The total hours spent on your production run.
* **Production Hours Actual** (`production_hours_actual`, number, optional): The actual hours spent on your production run.
* **Production Cost** (`production_cost`, number, optional): The total cost of your production run.
* **Labor Cost** (`labor_cost`, number, optional): The labor cost of your production run.
* **Material Cost** (`material_cost`, number, optional): The material cost of your production run.
* **Machine Breakdown** (`machine_breakdown`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if any of the following machine breakdowns occurred:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Any additional comments or notes about your production run.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address for contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number for contact.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): The name of your production manager.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): The email address of your production manager.

Note: Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*). If a field is not marked as required, it is optional.
