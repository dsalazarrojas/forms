# Contact Archive Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Archive Log is a centralized database for recording contacts with various individuals and organizations. It helps in maintaining a structured record of communication and actions taken, ensuring that no important details are missed and follow-up tasks are assigned correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of contact from the "Contact Type" dropdown menu: Choose the correct category for the contact, such as Customer, Vendor/Partner, Internal Employee, Potential Lead, or Other.
2. Enter the contact's name and primary email address: Provide the full name and email address of the person or organization contacted.
3. Select the communication date and time: Choose the date and time of the contact.
4. Select the communication channel: Choose how the contact was made, such as Phone Call, Email, Video Meeting, In-person Meeting, or LinkedIn/Social Media.
5. Write a brief summary of the communication: Provide a short overview of the discussion.
6. Set the follow-up status: Mark if follow-up is required or not.
7. Set the follow-up action item and due date: If follow-up is required, specify the next action and the deadline for completion.
8. Assign a team member to follow-up: Select the name of the team member responsible for the follow-up action.
9. Set the status of the contact entry: Choose the current status, such as Open, In Progress, Completed, or Archived.
10. Add any additional notes: Provide any other relevant information about the contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the full name of the person or organization contacted.
* **Company/Organization** (`contact_company`): Enter the name of the company or organization.
* **Contact Type** (`contact_type`): Select the type of contact (Customer, Vendor/Partner, Internal Employee, Potential Lead, or Other).
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the primary email address of the contact.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Enter the best phone number for the contact.
* **Communication Date** (`communication_date`): Choose the date of the contact.
* **Communication Time** (`communication_time`): Choose the time of the contact.
* **Communication Channel** (`communication_channel`): Select the method of communication (Phone Call, Email, Video Meeting, In-person Meeting, LinkedIn/Social Media, or Chat/Instant Message).
* **Summary of Communication** (`summary_of_communication`): Write a brief overview of the discussion.
* **Follow-up Required?** (`follow_up_required`): Mark if follow-up is required.
* **Follow-up Action Item** (`follow_up_action_item`): If follow-up is required, specify the next action.
* **Follow-up Due Date** (`follow_up_due_date`): Set the deadline for the follow-up action.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Select the name of the team member responsible for the follow-up action.
* **Status** (`status`): Choose the current status of the contact entry (Open, In Progress, Completed, or Archived).
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): Provide any other relevant information about the contact.
* **Log Entry Date** (`log_entry_date`): The date this record was created.
