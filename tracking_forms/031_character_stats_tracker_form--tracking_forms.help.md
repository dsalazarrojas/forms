# Character Stats Tracker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track changes to character attributes, such as strength, agility, and intelligence, for a given character.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the character's unique identifier in the "character id" field.
2. Enter the name of the attribute that changed in the "attribute name" field.
3. Enter the old value of the attribute in the "old value" field. This should be a numerical value, such as 10.
4. Enter the new value of the attribute in the "new value" field. This should be a numerical value, such as 11.
5. Describe the reason for the change in the "reason for change" field.
6. Enter the date when the new value took effect in the "effective date" field.
7. Enter the person who recorded the change in the "recorded by" field.
8. Enter any additional session notes in the "session notes" field (optional).
9. Confirm the accuracy of the entry by selecting "Confirmed" or "Unconfirmed" from the "confirmation" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **character id (required)**: Enter the unique identifier for the character.
* **attribute name (required)**: Enter the name of the attribute that changed, such as "strength" or "agility".
* **old value (optional)**: Enter the previous value of the attribute, if applicable.
* **new value (required)**: Enter the updated value of the attribute.
* **reason for change (optional)**: Describe the reason for the change, such as "gained from level up" or "lost from injury".
* **effective date (optional)**: Enter the date when the new value took effect.
* **recorded by (required)**: Enter the person who recorded the change.
* **session notes (optional)**: Enter any additional session notes or context for the change.
* **confirmation (required)**: Confirm the accuracy of the entry by selecting "Confirmed" or "Unconfirmed" from the dropdown menu.
