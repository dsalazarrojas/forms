# moving_expense_details_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about moving expenses related to a specific move. It's intended to gather data on specific dates and costs associated with both the move-in and move-out periods, including rent and other expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start with the moving expense details page.
2. Input dates and costs associated with your move-in and move-out periods. Ensure accuracy when inputting dates in the `move_in_date` and `move_out_date` fields.
3. Input costs associated with the move, including `rent`, `utilities`, and other expenses. Be sure to include move-in and move-out expenses separately.
4. Indicate whether utilities and rent are included in the move-out period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **moving_expense_details** (1): Briefly describe the details of your move.
* **move_in_date** (2): Enter the date when you moved in.
* **move_out_date** (3): Enter the date when you moved out.
* **rent** (4): Input the total rent cost for the move-in and move-out periods separately.
* **utilities** (5): Input any utility costs associated with the move-in and move-out periods.
* **other_expenses** (6): Input any other expenses you incurred during the move.
* **move_in_expenses** (7): Input the total move-in expenses.
* **move_out_expenses** (8): Input the total move-out expenses.
* **utilities_included** (9): Check 'Yes' if utilities are included in the move-out period. If not, select 'No'.
* **rent_included** (10): Check 'Yes' if rent is included in the move-out period. If not, select 'No'.

## Tips
- Double-check your input dates for accuracy.
- Be precise when inputting costs to avoid errors.
- If you're unsure about any field, please reach out for assistance.
