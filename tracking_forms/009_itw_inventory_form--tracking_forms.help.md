# itw_inventory_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to create and manage records for various items within the inventory. You can add new items, update existing ones, or search for specific items by filling out this form.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out all fields**:
   - Ensure all fields with a `*` next to the field label are filled out as they are required.
   - If an `email` field is present, ensure you enter a valid email address (e.g., `example@email.com`).
   - When entering a date or time field, use the format `YYYY-MM-DD` or `HH:MM:SS`, respectively.
   - For select fields, choose one of the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Item Name** (`item_name`, text, *): 
  Please enter the name of the item being tracked.
* **Item Type** (`item_type`, select_one, *): 
  Choose the type of item (e.g., type1, type2, type3).
* **Item Quantity** (`item_quantity`, number, *): 
  Enter the quantity of the item.
* **Item Price** (`item_price`, number, *): 
  Enter the price of the item.
* **Item Location** (`item_location`, text, ): 
  Enter the location of the item.
* **Item Note** (`item_note`, note, ): 
  Enter any additional notes about the item.
* **Item Email** (`item_email`, email, ): 
  Enter the email address associated with the item.
* **Item Phone** (`item_phone`, text, ): 
  Enter the phone number associated with the item.
* **Item Date** (`item_date`, date, ): 
  Enter the date the item was added or modified.
* **Item Time** (`item_time`, time, ): 
  Enter the time the item was added or modified.

## Tips

* Ensure you fill out all required fields (*).
* Double-check the accuracy of your input before submitting the form.
* Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies before finalizing your entry.
