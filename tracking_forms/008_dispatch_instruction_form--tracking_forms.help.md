# Dispatch Instruction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Dispatch Instruction Form is designed to gather essential details from customers for the efficient handling of their delivery requests. It ensures that our team can provide accurate and timely service by capturing relevant information about the customer, their contact details, and specific delivery requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the customer type from the "Customer" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
3. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
4. Enter the customer's address in the "Customer Address" field.
5. Provide any additional notes in the "Customer Notes" field.
6. Optionally, add a customer signature in the "Customer Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dispatch Instruction Form Details** (`dispatch_instruction_form_details`, note, required: false): This field is a free-form text area where you can enter any critical information about the delivery instruction.
* **Customer** (`customer_details`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of customer from the dropdown menu to categorize the customer's information.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's phone number for easy communication.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter the customer's email address to ensure we can contact them directly.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's address for accurate delivery and logistics handling.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, note, required: false): Provide any additional details about the customer or delivery requirements.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, required: false): Optionally, add a customer signature for verification purposes.

## Tips

- Always fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible to ensure we can provide the best service.
- If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, contact your supervisor or support team for assistance.
- Remember that this form is intended for one-time submissions, so avoid duplication or unnecessary submissions.
