# Comp Tracking - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Comp Tracking form is used for recording and tracking changes to an employee's compensation and performance. This form helps maintain accurate and up-to-date information about these changes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the Employee Identification Number of the employee being tracked.
2.  Enter the Effective Date of Change when the new compensation takes effect.
3.  Enter the Current Annual Salary before adjustments.
4.  Select the Type of Adjustment (e.g., Merit Increase, Market Adjustment, etc.).
5.  Enter the Adjustment Percentage (if applicable).
6.  Enter the New Annual Salary after the adjustment.
7.  Select the Latest Performance Rating (e.g., 1 - Unsatisfactory, 5 - Outstanding).
8.  Enter the Full name of the Approving Manager.
9.  Select the current status of the tracking record (e.g., Pending Review, Approved, etc.).
10.  Optionally, provide additional Justification Notes for the compensation adjustment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Identification Number** (`employee_id`, `text`, required): Enter the unique ID for the employee being tracked.
*   **Effective Date of Change** (`track_date`, `date`, required): Date when the new compensation takes effect.
*   **Current Annual Salary** (`current_annual_salary`, `number`, required): Existing base salary before adjustments.
*   **Type of Adjustment** (`adjustment_type`, `select_one`, required): Reason for the compensation change.
*   **Adjustment Percentage** (`adjustment_percentage`, `number`, required): Percentage increase (e.g., 3.5).
*   **New Annual Salary** (`new_annual_salary`, `number`, required): Total base salary after adjustment.
*   **Latest Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, `select_one`, required): Most recent annual review score.
*   **Approving Manager Name** (`manager_approval_name`, `text`, required): Full name of the manager authorizing this change.
*   **HR Review Status** (`hr_review_status`, `select_one`, required): Current status of the tracking record (e.g., Pending Review, Approved, etc.).
*   **Justification Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide additional context for the compensation adjustment.
*   **Approving Manager Name** (`manager_approval_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the manager authorizing this change.

## Tips

*   Make sure to enter the correct Employee Identification Number and Approving Manager Name to ensure accuracy and authorization.
*   Double-check the Adjustment Percentage and New Annual Salary calculations to ensure consistency.
*   Select the most recent Performance Rating for the employee.
*   Verify the current status of the tracking record with the HR team.
*   Keep in mind that Justification Notes are optional, but recommended for future reference.
