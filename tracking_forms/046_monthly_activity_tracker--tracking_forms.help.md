# Monthly Activity Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monthly Activity Tracker form is a tool for users to monitor and record their activities, goals, and progress towards those goals across 12 months of the year. It is used to assess and adjust goals, track progress, and reflect on outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the month you want to track (January to December).
2. Choose one or more activities (e.g., Task, Meeting, Event, Phone Call, Note, Other, Call, Project) for the selected month.
3. Describe your main goal for the month.
4. Write a reflection (optional).
5. Write a review of progress towards your goal.
6. Choose the outcome of your progress (On track, At risk, Behind, Complete).
7. Add additional comments for review (optional).
8. Enter additional comments for outcome (optional).
9. Select whether the review was done by a team member (True or False).
10. Enter the name of the team member who reviewed you (optional).
