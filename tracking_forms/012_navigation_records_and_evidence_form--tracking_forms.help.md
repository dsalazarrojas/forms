# Navigation Records And Evidence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Navigation Records And Evidence Form is used to collect information related to navigation incidents or events that occurred during a navigation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the relevant information, and then click the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Navigation Records**: Enter a brief description of the navigation record.
* **Navigation Events**: Enter details of events that occurred during navigation, such as events or observations made by staff members.
* **Navigation Incidents**: Describe incidents that occurred during navigation, such as near-misses or actual incidents.
* **Navigation Observations**: Record any observations made during navigation, such as weather conditions or notable events.
* **Supporting Evidence**: Attach any supporting evidence, such as photos or videos, to support the navigation record.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the navigation took place.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the navigation event.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the navigation event.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the navigation record.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact information.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit the form and save your changes.
