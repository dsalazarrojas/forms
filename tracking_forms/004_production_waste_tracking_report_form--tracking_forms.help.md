# Production Waste Tracking Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data on production waste generated during various stages of the manufacturing process. The collected information helps identify areas where improvements can be made to reduce waste.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill out the form for each instance of production waste that occurs during production.
*   Be as detailed as possible when reporting the waste, including the quantity and weight of waste generated.
*   Check all relevant boxes for the type and reason of the waste.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Production Waste**: This field is for describing the type of waste generated during production. Enter as much detail as possible about the waste, including quantity and weight.
*   **Date and Time**: Please enter the date and time the waste was generated, as this will help track waste over time.
*   **Production Area**: This field asks you to confirm if the production line where the waste was generated was a known or unknown issue. Select all that apply.
*   **Production Line**: Indicate if the waste was generated on a specific production line or if it was unknown.
*   **Waste Type**: Similar to the Production Waste field, use this to describe the type of waste generated. Be as detailed as possible.
*   **Quantity of Waste**: Enter the total quantity of waste generated during production. This field is optional but helps track waste levels over time.
*   **Weight of Waste**: If possible, provide the weight of the waste generated. This can help in understanding the scale of the waste.
*   **Reason for Waste**: Check all boxes for reasons that apply to why the waste was generated.
*   **Notes**: Use this field to add any additional context or details that might be helpful for the production team to understand the waste.
