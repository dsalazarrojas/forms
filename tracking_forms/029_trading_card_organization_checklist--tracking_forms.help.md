# Trading Card Organization Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help users track and organize their trading card collections. It gathers information on the condition and storage of individual cards, allowing users to easily access and manage their collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, such as "storage_method" and "collection_type".
2. Choose the relevant conditions and storage capacities from the drop-down menus.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "notes" field.
4. Review and submit the form to save your data.
