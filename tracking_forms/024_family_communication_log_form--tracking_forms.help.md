# Family Communication Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Communication Log Form is a tool used to document and track important conversations, decisions, and interactions within the family unit. This information is valuable to healthcare professionals to understand family dynamics and provide personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant page for the specific type of conversation or interaction you want to record. Currently, there are four pages to choose from:
	* Family Conversations
	* Family Decisions
	* Follow-Ups
	* Household Members

2. Answer the questions or select the options relevant to your interaction. Some fields may be optional, so please only select what applies to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Family Conversations (<id: 1>)
* **Family Conversations** (`family_communication_log_form_1`, text, required/optional): This is a text field where you can record any important conversations or discussions that took place within the family. Please provide as much detail as possible, including dates and times of conversations.

### Family Decisions (<id: 2>)
* **Family Decisions** (`family_decision_form_1`, select_one, required/optional): Please indicate whether any significant family decisions were made during this interaction, such as changes in medication, appointment schedules, or other important choices. Select one of the provided options: 'Yes' or 'No'.

### Follow-Ups (<id: 3>)
* **Follow-Ups** (`follow_ups_form_1`, text, required/optional): If you need to schedule or discuss follow-ups, such as appointment reminders, medication refills, or other ongoing care, use this text field. Include any relevant details, like dates and times.

### Caregiver Details (<id: 4>)
* **Caregiver Details** (`caregiver_details_form_1`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate which caregivers were involved in this interaction. Select all that apply: 'Yes' for each caregiver who participated in the conversation.

### Household Members (<id: 5>)
* **Household Members** (`household_form_1`, select_multiple, required/optional): Identify all household members who were present during the interaction. Select all that apply: 'Yes' for each household member who was present.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and specific as possible when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any question or option, please ask your healthcare professional for clarification.
* Remember to save your progress and come back to the form later if needed.
