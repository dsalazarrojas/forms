# regulatory_certification_tracking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The form is designed to track and verify the certification status of an individual or team, ensuring accurate and up-to-date information is maintained.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First Name**: Enter the individual's first name.
2. **Last Name**: Enter the individual's last name.
3. **Certification Status**: Select the current status of the certification (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
4. **Renewal Date**: Enter the date for the certification renewal.
5. **Certification Details**: Enter a brief description of the certification.
6. **Tracking ID**: Enter a unique tracking ID for the certification.
7. **Tracking URL**: Enter a URL for tracking the certification (optional).
8. **Verification Date**: Enter the date for the certification verification.
9. **Verification Method**: Select the method used for verification (Email, Phone, In-person).
10. **Verification Notes**: Enter any additional notes for the verification process.
11. **Certification Type**: Select the type of certification (Type 1, Type 2, Type 3).
12. **Team Name**: Enter the name of the team associated with the certification (optional).
13. **Team Location**: Enter the location of the team associated with the certification (optional).
14. **Certification URL**: Enter a URL for the certification (optional).
15. **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the certification.
16. **Email**: Enter the email address of the individual or team (optional).
17. **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the individual or team (optional).
18. **Verification Email**: Enter the email address used for verification (optional).
19. **Verification Phone**: Enter the phone number used for verification (optional).
20. **Team Size**: Enter the size of the team associated with the certification (optional).
21. **Certification Date**: Enter the date of the certification.
22. **Renewal Status**: Select the status of the certification renewal (Active, Inactive, Unknown).
23. **Status Update Date**: Enter the date for the status update.
24. **Verification Method (Other)**: Enter any other verification method used.
25. **Form Submitted By**: Select the person who submitted the form (Jimmy, Jane).
