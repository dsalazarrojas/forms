# Location Detection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the locations of teams. It is meant to be filled out by team members or administrators to keep track of team locations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each of the fields as follows:

* Select the team that is relevant to the location detection.
* Input the name of the location where the team is present.
* Enter the date of the location detection.
* Enter the time of the location detection.
* If necessary, enter the start and end times of the location detection.
* Input the name of the team leader who conducted the location detection.
* Enter any additional notes about the location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **team_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the team that is present at the location.
* **location_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the location where the team is present.
* **date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the location detection.
* **time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the location detection.
* **start_time** (time, optional): If necessary, enter the start time of the location detection.
* **end_time** (time, optional): If necessary, enter the end time of the location detection.
* **team_leader** (text, optional): Enter the name of the team leader who conducted the location detection.
* **location_notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the location.
* **team_notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the team.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all necessary fields, especially date and time, for accurate record keeping.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to your team's standard operating procedures or consult with a team leader.
* This form can be used for both daily and periodic location detection, depending on your team's needs.
