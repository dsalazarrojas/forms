# Blood Draw Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Blood Draw Log Form is used to track blood draw details for patients in a medical setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Blood Draw Log Form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's full name in the **Patient Name** field.
2.  Enter the patient's medical record number in the **Patient ID** field.
3.  Enter the patient's birth date in the **Date of Birth** field.
4.  Enter the date when the blood draw was performed in the **Draw Date** field.
5.  Enter the time when the blood draw was performed in the **Draw Time** field.
6.  Enter the name of the phlebotomist who performed the blood draw in the **Phlebotomist Name** field.
7.  Enter the ID number of the phlebotomist who performed the blood draw in the **Phlebotomist ID** field.
8.  Select the location where the blood draw was performed from the **Draw Location** dropdown menu.
9.  Select the vein or site where the blood was drawn from in the **Draw Site** dropdown menu.
10. Enter the number of tubes collected during the blood draw in the **Number of Tubes** field.
11. Select all the types of tubes used during the blood draw from the **Tube Types** dropdown menu.
12. Enter the approximate total volume of blood collected during the draw in the **Total Volume** field.
13. Select the method used to draw the blood from the **Draw Method** dropdown menu.
14. Select the size of the needle used for the blood draw from the **Needle Gauge** dropdown menu.
15. Enter the number of attempts made to collect the blood in the **Attempts Required** field.
16. Select any complications or issues that occurred during the draw from the **Complications** dropdown menu.
17. Enter a description of any complications that occurred during the draw in the **Complication Notes** field.
18. Select the patient's tolerance level during the blood draw from the **Patient Tolerance** dropdown menu.
19. Select all the lab departments that the blood samples were sent to from the **Samples Sent To** dropdown menu.
20. Select the urgency level of the blood draw from the **Priority Level** dropdown menu.
21. If any special handling requirements exist, enter them in the **Special Handling Required** field.
22. Enter any additional information about the blood draw in the **Additional Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Patient Name**: The patient's full name.
*   **Patient ID**: The patient's medical record number.
*   **Date of Birth**: The patient's birth date.
*   **Draw Date**: The date when the blood draw was performed.
*   **Draw Time**: The time when the blood draw was performed.
*   **Phlebotomist Name**: The name of the phlebotomist who performed the blood draw.
*   **Phlebotomist ID**: The ID number of the phlebotomist who performed the blood draw.
*   **Draw Location**: The location where the blood draw was performed.
*   **Draw Site**: The vein or site where the blood was drawn from.
*   **Number of Tubes**: The number of tubes collected during the blood draw.
*   **Tube Types**: The list of types of tubes used during the blood draw.
*   **Total Volume**: The approximate total volume of blood collected during the draw.
*   **Draw Method**: The method used to draw the blood (e.g., syringe, vacutainer, etc.).
*   **Needle Gauge**: The size of the needle used for the blood draw.
*   **Attempts Required**: The number of attempts made to collect the blood.
*   **Complications**: Any complications or issues that occurred during the draw.
*   **Complication Notes**: A description of any complications that occurred during the draw.
*   **Patient Tolerance**: The patient's tolerance level during the blood draw.
*   **Samples Sent To**: The list of lab departments that the blood samples were sent to.
*   **Priority Level**: The urgency level of the blood draw (e.g., routine, ASAP, etc.).
*   **Special Handling Required**: Any special handling requirements for the blood draw.
*   **Additional Notes**: Any additional information about the blood draw.

## Tips
*   Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
*   Double-check the accuracy of all information entered before submitting the form.
*   If any complications or issues occurred during the draw, be sure to document them in the **Complication Notes** field.
*   If any special handling requirements exist, be sure to enter them in the **Special Handling Required** field.
*   Enter any additional information about the blood draw in the **Additional Notes** field.
