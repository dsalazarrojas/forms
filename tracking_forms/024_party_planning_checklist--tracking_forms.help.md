# Party Planning Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is a tool for party planners and hosts to gather important information for events. It is used to collect details that will help with the organization and planning of a party.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the title of the party.
2. Enter any invitations that have been sent.
3. Check the guest list to indicate if guests have responded positively to the invitations.
4. Select a date for the party.
5. Choose a time for the party.
6. Enter the location of the party.
7. Enter the number of food items you plan to have.
8. Enter any special drinks or beverages you plan to serve.
9. Choose if you plan to have decorations.
10. Enter any music details.
11. Enter any games or activities you plan to have.
12. Enter any favors you plan to give to guests.
13. Enter any notes or additional information.
14. Enter the email address of the host or planner.

* **invitations** (text, optional): This field is used to keep track of any invitations that have been sent to guests.
* **guest_list** (select multiple, required): This field is used to indicate if guests have responded positively to invitations.
	+ Select 'Yes' for guests who have responded positively, and 'No' for those who have not.
* **date** (date, optional): This field is used to select a date for the party.
* **time** (time, optional): This field is used to select a time for the party.

* **food** (number, optional): This field is used to enter the number of food items you plan to have.

* **music** (text, optional): This field is used to enter any music details.
* **games** (text, optional): This field is used to enter any games or activities you plan to have.
* **favors** (text, optional): This field is used to enter any favors you plan to give to guests.
