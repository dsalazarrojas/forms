# Spray Batch Production Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the production process of spray batches. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that all relevant information is recorded for quality control and production tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*). 
2. Enter the batch number in the "Batch Number" field.
3. Select the correct "Spray Type" from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the production date and shift start and end times in their respective fields.
5. Enter the batch size and lot size.
6. Enter the machine, operator, supervisor, and shift ID numbers.
7. Fill out the "Quality Check Complete" field with either "Yes," "No," or "Not Applicable."

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Batch Number** (`batch_number`, number, *): Enter the batch number for this production batch.
* **Batch Production** (`spray_batch_production_log`, text, *): Enter a brief description of the batch production.
* **Batch Type** (`spray_type`, select_one, *): Select the type of spray batch being produced (Aerosol, Spray, Foam, or Other).
* **Date Produced** (`date_produced`, date, *): Enter the production date.
* **Batch Weight** (`batch_weight`, number, *): Enter the weight of the batch.
* **Machine ID** (`machine_id`, number, *): Enter the machine ID used for production.
* **Operator ID** (`operator_id`, number, *): Enter the operator ID in charge of production.
* **Supervisor ID** (`supervisor_id`, number, *): Enter the supervisor ID responsible for supervision.
* **Shift ID** (`shift_id`, number, *): Enter the shift ID.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, ): Enter any additional notes about this batch production.
* **Batch Size** (`batch_size`, number, *): Enter the batch size.
* **Lot Size** (`lot_size`, number, *): Enter the lot size.
* **Batch ID** (`batch_id`, number, *): Enter the batch ID.
* **Production Date** (`production_date`, date, *): Enter the production date.
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, time, *): Enter the shift start time.
* **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time`, time, *): Enter the shift end time.
* **Shift Duration** (`shift_duration`, number, *): Enter the shift duration.
* **Total Hours Worked** (`total_hours_worked`, number, *): Enter the total hours worked.
* **Operator Name** (`operator_name`, text, *): Enter the operator's name.
* **Operator Email** (`operator_email`, email, *): Enter the operator's email address.
* **Operator Phone** (`operator_phone`, text, *): Enter the operator's phone number.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, *): Enter the supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, *): Enter the supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, *): Enter the supervisor's phone number.
* **Quality Check Complete** (`quality_check_complete`, select_one, *): Select whether quality check is complete or not.
