# Expense Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request reimbursement for expenses. It is used to provide details about the expense, including the date it was incurred, the amount spent, and the purpose of the expense.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the expense in the `expense_date` field.
2. Fill in the amount spent on the expense in the `amount` field.
3. Provide a brief description of the purpose of the expense in the `purpose` field.
4. Choose the status of the request from the dropdown menu next to `Status`.
5. If applicable, choose the person assigned to handle the request from the dropdown menu next to `Assigned to`.
6. Enter any comments or additional information about the request in the `Comments` field.
7. If necessary, select the tool or resource used for the expense from the dropdown menu next to `Assigned Tool`.
8. Enter the email address of the person responsible for the expense in the `Email` field.
9. Provide the phone number of the person responsible for the expense in the `Phone` field.
10. Leave the `Date Created` field blank, as it is automatically populated by the system.
11. Leave the `Date Updated` field blank, as it is automatically populated by the system.
12. Leave the `Created by` and `Updated by` fields blank, as they are automatically populated by the system.
13. Leave the `Deleted by` field blank, as it is not applicable for this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Expense Request** (`expense_request_form`): Enter the title of the expense request.
* **Expense Date** (`expense_date`): Enter the date of the expense in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Amount** (`amount`): Enter the total amount spent on the expense.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`): Briefly describe the purpose of the expense.
* **Status** (`Status`): Choose from the dropdown menu to indicate the current status of the request.
	+ Approved: The request has been approved.
	+ Denied: The request has been denied.
	+ Pending: The request is awaiting approval.
* **Assigned to** (`Assigned to`): Choose from the dropdown menu to select the person assigned to handle the request.
	+ John
	+ Jane
	+ Jim
* **Comments** (`Comments`): Provide any additional comments or information about the request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`): If the expense requires a specific tool, select it from the dropdown menu.
	+ Other
* **Email** (`Email`): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the expense.
* **Phone** (`Phone`): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the expense.
* **Date Created** (`Date Created`): This field is automatically populated by the system, do not fill it out.
* **Date Updated** (`Date Updated`): This field is automatically populated by the system, do not fill it out.
* **Created by** (`Created by`): This field is automatically populated by the system, do not fill it out.
* **Updated by** (`Updated by`): This field is automatically populated by the system, do not fill it out.
* **Deleted by** (`Deleted by`): This field is not applicable for this form, do not fill it out.
