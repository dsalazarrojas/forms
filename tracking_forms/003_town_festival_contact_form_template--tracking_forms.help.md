# Town Festival Contact Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Town Festival Contact Form Template is designed to allow attendees, organizers, and stakeholders to provide feedback, inquiries, and reports related to the town festival. It is intended to facilitate communication and gather valuable information for the festival organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Enter your name, email, and phone number in the respective fields.
3. Select the "What's on your mind?" option that best describes your reason for contacting the festival organizers.
4. Enter your message in the text area provided.
5. If you want to report a lost and found item, select the corresponding option.
6. Check the "Submit" button to send your message to the festival organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Us**: *name (text, optional)* - Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email**: *email (email, required)* - Enter your valid email address so that the festival organizers can get in touch with you.
* **Phone**: *phone (text, optional)* - Enter your phone number if you want the festival organizers to call you back.
* **Message**: *message (text, optional)* - Enter a detailed description of your message or inquiry.
* **What's on your mind?**: *attendee_type (select_one, optional)* - Select the reason for your contact, such as:
	+ General Inquiry
	+ Feedback
	+ Lost and Found
	+ Volunteer
* **Submitted At**: *form_submitted_at (date, optional)* - This field is automatically filled out when you submit the form and is not meant to be edited by the user.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your message gets sent to the festival organizers.
* Be as detailed as possible when entering your message to help the festival organizers understand your inquiry.
* If you have any attachments or files to attach, please upload them separately and mention them in your message.
* The festival organizers may contact you back through this email address. Please keep an eye on your inbox for any response.
