# Dog Training Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Dog Training Log" form is used to record details about dog training sessions. Trainers use this form to track information such as the dog's name, the session date and time, the location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the dog being trained.
2. Enter the name of the trainer conducting the session.
3. Select the date of the training session.
4. Select the start and end times of the session.
5. Enter the duration of the session.
6. Select the location where the session took place.
7. Choose the type of training method used during the session.
8. Enter any notes about the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dog Name**: Enter the name of the dog being trained.
* **Trainer Name**: Enter the name of the trainer conducting the session.
* **Session Date**: Select the date of the training session.
* **Start Time**: Select the start time of the session.
* **End Time**: Select the end time of the session.
* **Session Duration**: Enter the duration of the session in hours or minutes.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the training session took place.
* **Training Method**: Select the type of training method used during the session (e.g., Basic Obedience, Recall, Trick Training, or Advanced Trick Training).
* **Session Notes**: Enter any notes about the session.
* **Dog Breed**: Enter the breed of the dog being trained.
* **Trainer Level**: Select the level of the trainer (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).
* **Training Area**: Enter the area where the session took place.
* **Session Notes**: Enter any follow-up notes about the session.
* **Training Notes**: Enter any notes about the training process or progress.
* **Follow Up Notes**: Enter any follow-up notes about the session or the dog's training progress.

## Tips
* Please enter accurate information for each field to ensure consistent tracking.
* Use clear and concise notes to provide a clear record of the session.
* If a session was cancelled or rescheduled, update the form accordingly.
