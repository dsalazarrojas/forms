# Asset Inventory Information Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Asset Inventory Information Record Form is used to create a comprehensive record of assets within the organization, including their details, condition, and maintenance schedules. This form helps ensure accurate tracking, management, and maintenance of assets for the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1.  Fill in the "Record Date" field with the date you are recording the asset information.
2.  In the "Recorded By" field, enter your name or the name of the person who recorded the information.
3.  Enter the "Location" where the asset is currently situated.
4.  Enter the "Asset ID" which is a unique identifier for the asset.
5.  Enter the "Asset Name" that describes the asset in detail.
6.  Select the "Asset Category" from the provided options to classify the type of asset.
7.  If required, select a specific "Asset Subcategory" to further classify the asset.
8.  Enter the "Manufacturer" of the asset, if known.
9.  Enter the "Model Number" of the asset, if known.
10. Enter the "Serial Number" of the asset, if known.
11. Enter the "Purchase Date" of the asset.
12. Enter the "Purchase Price" paid for the asset.
13. Enter the "Current Estimated Value" of the asset.
14. Select the "Depreciation Method" used for the asset.
15. Enter the "Useful Life in Years" for the asset.
16. Select the current "Asset Condition" of the asset.
17. Enter the number of "Quantity" of the assets.
18. Select the "Unit of Measure" for the asset.
19. Enter the "Assigned To" department or person using the asset.
20. Enter the "Department" responsible for the asset.
21. Enter the name of the "Custodian" or person responsible for the asset.
22. Select the "Maintenance Schedule" for the asset.
23. If applicable, enter the "Last Maintenance Date" for the asset.
24. If applicable, enter the "Next Maintenance Due" date for the asset.
25. Select the "Warranty Status" of the asset.
26. If applicable, enter the "Warranty Expiry Date" for the asset.
27. Select whether the asset is "Insured" or not.
28. If applicable, enter the "Insurance Policy Number".
29. Enter any "Additional Notes" regarding the asset.
30. Finally, select if a "Photo Attached" is included with the asset record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Record Date (1)**: Date of recording the asset information.
* **Recorded By (2)**: Name of person recording the asset information.
* **Location (3)**: Where the asset is currently located.
* **Asset ID (4)**: A unique identifier for the asset.
* **Asset Name (5)**: Descriptive name for the asset.
* **Asset Category (6)**: Type of asset (e.g., Electronics, Furniture).
* **Asset Subcategory (7)**: Further classification of the asset type (optional).
* **Manufacturer (8)**: Brand or maker of the asset.
* **Model Number (9)**: Model identifier for the asset.
* **Serial Number (10)**: Unique serial number for the asset.
* **Purchase Date (11)**: Date the asset was acquired.
* **Purchase Price (12)**: Original cost of the asset.
* **Current Estimated Value (13)**: Present worth of the asset.
* **Depreciation Method (14)**: Method used for depreciating the asset (optional).
* **Useful Life in Years (15)**: Expected lifespan of the asset.
* **Asset Condition (16)**: Current state of the asset.
* **Quantity (17)**: Number of items of the asset.
* **Unit of Measure (18)**: How the asset is counted.
* **Assigned To (19)**: Department or person using the asset.
* **Department (20)**: Department responsible for the asset.
* **Custodian (21)**: Person responsible for the asset.
* **Maintenance Schedule (22)**: Maintenance schedule for the asset.
* **Last Maintenance Date (23)**: Date of last maintenance.
* **Next Maintenance Due (24)**: Date of next scheduled maintenance.
* **Warranty Status (25)**: Status of warranty coverage.
* **Warranty Expiry Date (26)**: Date when warranty expires.
* **Is Asset Insured (27)**: Whether the asset is insured or not.
* **Insurance Policy Number (28)**: Policy identifier for the asset.
* **Additional Notes (29)**: Other information regarding the asset.
* **Photo Attached (30)**: If a photo is attached to the asset record.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly for each asset.
- If unsure about any field, please contact your asset manager or supervisor.
- If you are unsure whether an asset is insured or not, please check with the finance or accounting department for verification.
- If you are unsure about any of the asset conditions, please verify with the relevant departments or personnel involved in the asset's usage or maintenance.
- If a photo is attached, please ensure it is relevant and up-to-date.
