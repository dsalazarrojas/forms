# Departure Time Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Departure Time Survey is a form designed to collect information on departure and arrival times for a trip. It aims to gather accurate and comprehensive data in a user-friendly way, allowing users to input their details without requiring a login or account creation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Departure Time Survey, follow these steps:

* Start by selecting the departure date from the calendar view or entering it manually.
* Choose the departure location, method, and time as needed.
* Enter the arrival location and method used.
* Provide any additional travel method details, if applicable.
* Enter the arrival time, if different from the departure time.
* Add any notes about your trip, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Departure Date**: This field is used to select or enter the date of departure. Please choose the correct date or enter it in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Departure Location**: Enter the location from which you are departing. This could be an airport, station, bus stop, or any other starting point.
* **Travel Method**: Select the method used for travel, which could be Air, Train, Bus, Car, Bike, or Walk.
* **Departure Time**: Enter the time of departure, in 12-hour format (e.g., 08:00 AM or 14:00 PM).
* **Arrival Location**: Enter the location you arrive at. This could be an airport, station, or any other destination.
* **Travel Method Details**: If your travel method requires additional information, enter it here (e.g., seat number for a flight or bus number for a bus ride).
* **Arrival Time**: Enter the time you arrive, in 12-hour format (e.g., 10:00 AM or 18:00 PM).
* **Notes**: Add any additional information or comments about your trip that you think will be useful.

## Tips
* Be as precise as possible when entering dates, times, and locations.
* Use the correct format for dates and times to ensure accurate processing.
* If you are unsure about any information, ask for help or clarification before submitting your form.
