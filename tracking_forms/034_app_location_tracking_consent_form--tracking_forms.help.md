# App Location Tracking Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from users for tracking their location data. It helps us understand their preferences and ensure compliance with data protection regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your:
	* Full Name
	* User ID or Email
	* App Name
2. Provide the date you consent to location tracking
3. Select your consent to GPS tracking while the app is in use
4. Choose whether you consent to background location tracking
5. Confirm your understanding of revoking this permission
6. Agree to the data retention policy (30 days)
7. Agree to the privacy policy

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **User ID or Email**: Enter your unique User ID or Email associated with your account.
* **App Name**: Enter the name of the app being tracked.
* **Date of Consent**: Enter the date you consent to location tracking in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **-- Purpose of Tracking --**: This field is a note where you can provide more details on why you're tracking your location, but it's not required.
* **I consent to GPS tracking while the app is in use**: Choose "Yes, I consent" to allow GPS tracking.
* **I consent to background location tracking**: Choose "Yes, I consent" to allow background location tracking, which allows features to work even when the app is closed.
* **I understand I can revoke this permission at any time in app settings**: Confirm your understanding that you can revoke this permission in app settings.
* **I agree to the data retention policy (30 days)**: Choose "I agree" to agree to the data retention policy.
* **I have read and agree to the Privacy Policy**: Choose "I agree" to agree to the Privacy Policy.
* **Digital Signature (Type Name)**: Enter your digital signature, typically your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your selections and understand the implications of your choices.
* Review the data retention policy and privacy policy before making your selections.
