# RFP Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RFP Intake Form is a tool used to gather basic information about a Request for Proposal from a customer. This form is meant to be a starting point for assessing the customer's needs and providing a quote or bid.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields carefully and fill in the required information.
2. Provide accurate and detailed responses to each field.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### RFP Form Data (`rfp_form_data`)
* **RFP Form Data** (Text, Optional): This field is used to enter any additional information about the RFP that is not covered in the other fields. Please provide a brief description of the RFP and any relevant details.

### Bid/No-Bid (`rfp_bid_status`)
* **Bid/No-Bid** (Select One, Optional): This field is used to indicate whether or not the customer is seeking a bid from us. Please select either "Bid" or "No-Bid" accordingly.

### General Review (`general_review`)
* **General Review** (Text, Optional): This field is used to enter any general comments or observations about the RFP. Please provide any relevant insights or notes about the customer's needs.

### General Comments (`general_comments`)
* **General Comments** (Text, Optional): This field is used to enter any general comments or feedback about the RFP. Please provide any additional information that may be helpful for our team to review.
