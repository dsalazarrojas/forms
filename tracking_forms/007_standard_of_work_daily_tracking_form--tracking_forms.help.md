# Standard Of Work Daily Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track your daily activities and provide a record of your progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with your relevant information. Start with the top section, "Daily Tracking Form", which asks for your tracking operations. Choose one operation that best describes your daily activities. Then, proceed to the next section, "Progress Updates", where you can provide a brief update on your current progress. 

After that, move on to "Daily Tasks", where you select all the relevant tasks that you have completed or are currently working on. 

Finally, in the last section, "Productivity Measurements", you can input your productivity measurements, if applicable. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tracking Operations**: This field tracks your daily operations. Choose one that best describes your daily activities. 
* **Progress Updates**: This field is for providing a brief update on your current progress. 
* **Daily Tasks**: Select all relevant tasks that you have completed or are currently working on. 
* **Productivity Measurements**: This field is for inputting your productivity measurements, if applicable. 
* **Daily Notes**: This field is for any additional notes you may have. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out this form for the correct date to avoid duplication of effort. 
* If you have already completed this form for the current date, do not fill it out again. 
* Be as specific as possible when filling out each field to ensure that your data is accurate. 
* If you are unsure of what to enter in a particular field, refer to the available options or your supervisor for guidance. 
* This form is meant to be used on a daily basis, so try to fill it out each day to keep track of your progress.
