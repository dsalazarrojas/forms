# Honey Harvest Data Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Honey Harvest Data Collection Form is a tool for collecting data on honey harvests from beehives or apiaries. The form aims to capture detailed information about the beekeeper, the apiary, and the honey yield.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the Beekeeper Information section with details about the person or organization responsible for the beehive or apiary.
2. Move on to the Harvest Info section and provide information about the specific honey harvest, including the date and any other relevant details.
3. Next, fill in the Honey Info section with details about the harvested honey, such as its quality and quantity.
4. The Apiary Details section is where you can enter information about the location and conditions of the beehive or apiary.
5. If the apiary has a specific yield, enter it in the Honey Yield section.
6. In the Apiary Location section, enter the address and geographical coordinates of the apiary.
7. Optionally, provide notes and images or videos about the apiary, and select the appropriate units for the apiary altitude and honey yield.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Beekeeper Information**: Enter the name and contact details of the person responsible for the beehive or apiary.
- **Harvest Info**: Provide the date and any other relevant details about the honey harvest.
- **Honey Info**: Enter details about the quality and quantity of the harvested honey.
- **Apiary Details**: Enter information about the location, conditions, and management practices of the apiary.
- **Honey Yield**: Enter the quantity of honey harvested, if applicable.
- **Apiary Location**: Enter the address and geographical coordinates of the apiary.
- **Apiary Notes**: Provide any additional notes about the apiary.
- **Apiary Image**: Upload an image of the apiary, if available.
- **Apiary Video**: Provide a video of the apiary, if available.
- **Apiary Audio**: If available, provide an audio recording of the apiary.
- **Apiary Document**: Upload a document about the apiary, if applicable.
- **Honey Yield Units**: Select the unit of measurement for the honey yield (e.g., kg, liters, pounds).
- **Apiary Altitude Units**: Select the unit of measurement for the apiary altitude (e.g., meters, feet).
- **Apiary Temperature**: Enter the temperature of the apiary, if available.
- **Apiary Humidity**: Enter the humidity of the apiary, if available.
- **Apiary Wind**: Enter the wind speed at the apiary, if available.
- **Apiary Sunlight**: Enter the amount of sunlight the apiary receives, if available.
- **Apiary Note**: Enter any additional notes about the apiary.
- **Apiary Image**: Upload an additional image of the apiary, if available.
- **Apiary Video**: Provide another video of the apiary, if available.
- **Apiary Audio**: If available, provide an additional audio recording of the apiary.
- **Apiary Document**: Upload another document about the apiary, if applicable.
