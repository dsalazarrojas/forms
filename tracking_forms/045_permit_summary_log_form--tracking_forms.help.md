# Permit Summary Log Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Permit Summary Log Form is used to track and document the status of permits. This form is designed to collect information on permit details, status, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by carefully reading the guide to understand the purpose and requirements of the form.
2.  Select the correct permit status from the dropdown menu.
3.  Enter the correct dates for the key dates, date received, date reviewed, date approved, date denied, and date closed.
4.  Enter the permit number, if available.
5.  Enter any status comments, reviewer comments, approver comments, created by, modified by, assigned to, created at, modified at, and date last updated.
6.  Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Permit Status** (Select One, Required):
    *   Select the current status of the permit (Open, Closed, or Pending) to help track its progress.
*   **Key Dates** (Date, Required):
    *   Enter the dates for the key events in the permit process, like key dates or milestones.
*   **Date Received** (Date, Optional):
    *   Enter the date the permit was received, if applicable.
*   **Date Reviewed** (Date, Required):
    *   Enter the date the permit was reviewed.
*   **Date Approved** (Date, Optional):
    *   Enter the date the permit was approved, if applicable.
*   **Date Denied** (Date, Optional):
    *   Enter the date the permit was denied, if applicable.
*   **Permit Number** (Text, Optional):
    *   Enter the permit number, if available.
*   **Status Comments** (Text, Optional):
    *   Enter any comments related to the permit status.
*   **Reviewer Comments** (Text, Optional):
    *   Enter any comments from the reviewer.
*   **Approver Comments** (Text, Optional):
    *   Enter any comments from the approver.
*   **Created By** (Text, Optional):
    *   Enter the name of the person who created the permit.
*   **Modified By** (Text, Optional):
    *   Enter the name of the person who last modified the permit.
*   **Assigned To** (Text, Optional):
    *   Enter the name of the person assigned to handle the permit.
*   **Created At** (Date, Optional):
    *   Enter the date the permit was created.
*   **Modified At** (Date, Optional):
    *   Enter the date the permit was last modified.
*   **Date Last Updated** (Date, Optional):
    *   Enter the date the permit was last updated.
*   **Date Deleted** (Date, Optional):
    *   Enter the date the permit was deleted, if applicable.
*   **Permitted Area** (Text, Optional):
    *   Enter any details about the permitted area.
*   **Permit Description** (Text, Optional):
    *   Enter a brief description of the permit.
*   **Permit Status Comments** (Text, Optional):
    *   Enter any comments on the permit status.
*   **Reviewer** (Text, Optional):
    *   Enter the name of the reviewer.
*   **Approver** (Text, Optional):
    *   Enter the name of the approver.
*   **Assigned Tool** (Text, Optional):
    *   Enter the assigned tool or equipment.
*   **Date Closed** (Date, Optional):
    *   Enter the date the permit was closed, if applicable.
