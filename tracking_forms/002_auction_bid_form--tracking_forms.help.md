# Auction Bid Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auction Bid Form is used to submit bids on auctioned items. It collects important information from the bidder, such as their name, email, phone number, item number, bid amount, and time of bid submission. This information is used to process the bid and handle delivery or payment if the bid is successful.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Auction Bid Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and thorough information.
2. Provide your full name and email address.
3. Enter the item number for the auctioned item.
4. Enter the bid amount in your currency.
5. Select your preferred payment method.
6. Enter the time of bid submission.
7. Enter any special delivery or handling instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Bidder name** (required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Bidder email** (required): Enter the email address to be used for bid confirmation and receipt purposes.
* **Bidder phone** (optional): Enter your contact phone number for delivery or payment purposes.
* **Item number** (required): Enter the unique identifier for the auctioned item.
* **Item description** (optional): Enter a brief description of the auctioned item.
* **Bid amount** (required): Enter the whole number amount you are willing to bid.
* **Time of bid** (required): Enter the local time of bid submission.
* **Preferred payment method** (optional): Select the method you would like to use for payment if your bid is successful.
* **Special delivery or handling instructions** (optional): Enter any special delivery or handling instructions for the auctioned item.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the bid amount for correctness.
* Enter your email address and phone number correctly for bid confirmation and delivery purposes.
* Select the correct payment method for your convenience.
