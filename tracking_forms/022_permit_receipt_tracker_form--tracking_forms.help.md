# Permit Receipt Tracker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and track receipt information from businesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review the form for any errors or missing fields.
3. Submit the form once you have entered all necessary details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Permit Receipt Tracker Form Page 1** (`permit_receipt_tracker_form_page_1`, text, required/optional): This is the introductory page where the form name and description are provided.
* **Business Information** (`business_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the business name, address, and contact information.
* **Receipt Date** (`receipt_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the permit receipt was received or is due.
* **Receipt Type** (`receipt_type`, select one, required/optional): Select the type of receipt (e.g., permit, license, certification).
* **Tracking Status** (`tracking_status`, select one, required/optional): Choose the tracking status of the receipt (e.g., pending, received, expired).
* **Tracking Notes** (`tracking_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the receipt.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the business phone number for contact purposes.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the business email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person for the business.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Receipt Number** (`receipt_number`, number, required/optional): Enter the permit receipt number.
* **Receipt Date/Time** (`receipt_date_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time when the permit receipt was received or is due.
* **Tracking Notes 1-12** (`tracking_notes_1-12`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the receipt (repeat fields for multiple notes).

## Tips
* Review each field carefully and ensure accuracy before submitting the form.
* Enter any relevant business information and tracking status for effective tracking.
* Use tracking notes to capture any notable events or issues related to the receipt.
* Submit the form once you have entered all necessary details to avoid errors.
