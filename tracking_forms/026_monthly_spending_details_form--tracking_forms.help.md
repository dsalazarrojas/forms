# monthly_spending_details_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and report monthly spending details, including income earned, income spent, total expenses spent, and other relevant metrics.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by entering your income earned on Page 1, followed by any additional notes.
2. On Page 2, enter your total income earned and the total amount of expenses earned.
3. On Page 3, enter your income spent, add any notes, and finally enter the total amount of expenses spent.
4. On Page 4, enter your total expenses spent, add any notes, and confirm your income spent.
5. On the final page, enter the date you submit this form and your total income and expenses amounts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Income Earned** (`income_earned`, text, required): Enter your income earned for the month.
* **Total Earned** (`total_earned`, number, required): Enter the total income earned for the month.
* **Notes for Income Earned** (`income_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about your income earned.
* **What is your total monthly income?** (`total_income_earned`, number, optional): Enter your total monthly income earned.
* **Total Expenses Earned** (`total_expenses_earned`, number, required): Enter the total expenses earned for the month.
* **Add any notes about your total income earned.** (`notes_for_total_income_earned`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about your total income earned.
* **Income Spent** (`income_spent`, text, required): Enter your income spent for the month.
* **What is your total monthly income spent?** (`total_income_spent`, number, required): Enter the total income spent for the month.
* **Add any notes about your income spent.** (`notes_for_income_spent`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about your income spent.
* **Total Expenses Spent** (`total_expenses_spent`, text, required): Enter the total expenses spent for the month.
* **What is your total monthly expenses spent?** (`total_expenses_spent_amount`, number, required): Enter the total expenses spent for the month.
* **Add any notes about your expenses spent.** (`notes_for_total_expenses_spent`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about your expenses spent.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required): Enter the date you submit this form.
* **What is your monthly income spent?** (`income_spent_amount`, number, required): Enter your total income spent for the month.
* **What is your total monthly income earned?** (`total_income_earned_amount`, number, required): Enter your total income earned for the month.
* **What is your total monthly expenses earned?** (`total_expenses_earned_amount`, number, required): Enter your total expenses earned for the month.
