# Resident Amenity Access Tracking Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resident Amenity Access Tracking Consent Form is designed to collect information from residents about their preferences and consents for access to amenities and emergency contacts. This form helps in tracking and recording residents' consents and preferences, ensuring that their rights and wishes are respected while providing them with the necessary information for smooth and efficient service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
2. Provide your name and contact information (page 2).
3. Choose your emergency contact (page 4).
4. Enter your relationship with the emergency contact (page 5).
5. Indicate whether you want to receive emergency contact information (page 6).
6. Choose what you consent to (page 7).
7. Select whether you want to be contacted (page 8).
8. Choose what you want to share with management (page 9).
9. Indicate whether you want to be contacted for other reasons (page 10).
10. Enter your emergency contact's name and contact information (pages 11-13).
11. Enter your relationship with the emergency contact (page 14).
12. Provide any consent notes (page 15).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (Page 1): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **What is your contact number?** (Page 2): Enter your contact phone number.
* **What is your email address?** (Page 3): Enter your email address.
* **Emergency Contact?** (Page 4): Choose a family member or friend who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship with Emergency Contact** (Page 5): Enter your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., spouse, friend, parent).
* **Emergency Contact Information?** (Page 6): Choose whether you want to receive emergency contact information.
* **What do you consent to?** (Page 7): Select what you are willing to be contacted for (e.g., events, meetings, etc.).
* **Do you want to be contacted?** (Page 8): Choose whether you want to be contacted by management or staff.
* **What do you want to share with management?** (Page 9): Select what you want to share with management (e.g., your availability, schedule, etc.).
* **Do you want to be contacted for other reasons?** (Page 10): Choose whether you want to be contacted for reasons other than emergencies or events.
* **Emergency Contact's Name** (Page 11): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact's Number** (Page 12): Enter the contact number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact's Email** (Page 13): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact's Relationship** (Page 14): Enter your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Consent Notes** (Page 15): Provide any additional notes or comments regarding your consent.
