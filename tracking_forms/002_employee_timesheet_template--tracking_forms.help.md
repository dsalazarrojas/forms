# Employee Timesheet Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Timesheet Template is a form used to track employee work hours, work dates, and other relevant information for the week. It collects data for the employee, manager, and supervisor review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, make sure you have access to this form and are in a suitable environment (e.g., office, home, or on-site).
2. Select the correct week's data using the date field.
3. Fill in the required fields (marked with an asterisk \*):
	* Employee Name
	* Employee Number
	* Manager Name
	* Manager Number
	* Department
	* Review Date
	* Status (optional)
	* Signature
4. Select your job title from the list:
	* Account Manager
	* Marketing Manager
	* Sales Manager
	* HR Generalist
	* Intern
	* Data Analyst
	* Software Developer
	* Quality Analyst
	* Customer Support
	* Finance Manager
5. Choose your team (optional):
	* Sales
	* Development
	* Marketing
	* Accounting
	* IT
	* Customer Support
	* Finance
6. Choose your supervisor (optional):
	* Account Manager
	* Marketing Manager
	* Sales Manager
	* Finance Manager
	* IT Manager
	* HR Manager
	* Operations Manager
	* Quality Manager
	* Customer Support Manager
7. Enter your hours worked for the week:
8. Enter your break hours (optional):
9. Add any additional notes or comments:
10. Review and submit your form once you've completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (*): Enter the name of the employee filling out this form.
* **Employee Number** (*): Enter the employee number associated with this account.
* **Manager Name** (*): Enter the name of the manager responsible for this employee.
* **Manager Number** (*): Enter the manager's number associated with this account.
* **Department** (*): Enter the department where the work was performed.
* **Team** (optional): Select one or multiple teams that the employee is a part of.
* **Supervisor** (optional): Select the supervisor for this employee, if applicable.
* **Job Title** (*): Select the job title of the employee.
* **Work Date** (*): Enter the date when work was performed.
* **Start Time** (*): Enter the start time of work.
* **End Time** (*): Enter the end time of work.
* **Status** (optional): Select the current status of the timesheet (Approved, Rejected, In Progress, or Pending).
* **Comments** (optional): Add any comments about the week's work.
* **Review Date** (*): Enter the review date for this timesheet.
* **Review Time** (*): Enter the review time.
* **Hours Worked** (*): Enter hours worked for the week.
* **Break Hours** (optional): Enter any break time during the week.
* **Notes** (optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Signature** (*): Enter your digital signature for validation.
* **Employee Status** (optional): Select the current status of the employee (Active, Inactive, On Leave, or On Vacation).
