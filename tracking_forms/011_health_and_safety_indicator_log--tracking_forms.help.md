# Health And Safety Indicator Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health And Safety Indicator Log is used to track and record incidents that occur within the organization's health and safety department. This form helps to document incidents, their types, locations, and status, ensuring that all relevant information is gathered and recorded for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, click on each tab or page to navigate through the different sections.
*   Fill out the required information, which is marked with an asterisk \*\*.
*   Enter or select the information in the respective fields as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Details (<id: 1>):** Enter a brief description of the incident.
    \* This field should include any necessary details to help understand the incident.
*   **Incident Type (<id: 2>):** Select one of the following options:
    \* 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the type of incident.
*   **Location (<id: 3>):** Select one or multiple locations where the incident occurred.
    \* Choose from the options provided.
*   **Date (<id: 4>):** Enter the date of the incident.
    \* This should be in a format that can be easily understood, such as MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY.
*   **Time (<id: 5>):** Enter the time of the incident.
    \* Use the 24-hour format, such as HH:MM.
*   **Description (<id: 6>):** Enter a detailed description of the incident.
    \* This can include any relevant information about the incident.
*   **Incident Status (<id: 7>):** Select one of the following options:
    \* Active or Inactive to indicate the status of the incident.
*   **Assigned To (<id: 8>):** Select one or multiple individuals assigned to follow up on the incident.
    \* Choose from the options provided.
*   **Follow Up (<id: 9>):** Enter a message for follow up on the incident.
    \* This can include any relevant information about the follow up action.
*   **Follow Up Date (<id: 10>):** Enter the date for follow up on the incident.
    \* This should be in the same format as the date of the incident.
*   **Follow Up Time (<id: 11>):** Enter the time for follow up on the incident.
    \* Use the 24-hour format, such as HH:MM.
