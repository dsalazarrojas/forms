# Daily Call Log Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Call Log Form is designed to track and document the progress of customer calls. It helps to gather relevant information about each call, including contact details, call outcomes, next steps, and follow-up tasks assigned to team members. This form is intended for daily use, allowing team members to keep a record of their interactions with customers and track progress towards specific objectives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "First Page" tab.
2. Enter the contact information of the person you interacted with (optional).
3. Fill in the notes section with a brief description of the call.
4. Choose the date and time of the call.
5. Select the duration of the call.
6. Select the outcome(s) of the call from the options provided.
7. Indicate the next steps to be taken.
8. Choose the team member assigned to follow-up on the call.
9. Select the follow-up status.
10. Enter the follow-up date and time.
11. Add any additional follow-up notes.
12. Finally, select the review status of the call.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (id: 1):
	+ A text field for entering additional information (optional).
* **Contact Info** (id: 2):
	+ Enter the contact information of the person you interacted with (optional).
* **Notes** (id: 3):
	+ A text field for filling in a brief description of the call.
* **Date** (id: 4):
	+ Enter the date of the call (format: MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time** (id: 5):
	+ Enter the time of the call (format: HH:MM).
* **Call Duration** (id: 6):
	+ Enter the duration of the call in minutes.
* **Call Outcomes** (id: 7):
	+ Select one or multiple outcomes from the following options: Success, Failed, Missed.
* **Next Steps** (id: 8):
	+ Select one or multiple next steps from the following options: Follow up with customer, Follow up with team, Other.
* **Assigned User** (id: 9):
	+ Select the team member assigned to follow-up on the call from the list: John, Jane, Jim.
* **Follow-up Status** (id: 10):
	+ Select the follow-up status from the following options: Not Set, In Progress, Completed.
* **Follow-up Date** (id: 11):
	+ Enter the follow-up date (format: MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Follow-up Time** (id: 12):
	+ Enter the follow-up time (format: HH:MM).
* **Follow-up Notes** (id: 13):
	+ A text field for adding additional follow-up notes.
* **Review Status** (id: 14):
	+ Select the review status from the following options: Pending, Approved, Rejected.
