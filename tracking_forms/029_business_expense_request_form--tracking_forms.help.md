# Business Expense Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you submit a business expense request, allowing you to request reimbursement for expenses related to work activities. Please complete this form to provide detailed information about your request, including the type of expense, estimated cost, and justification for the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information:
	* Enter your name and email address.
	* Enter your department and manager's name.
2. Select the type of expense and provide a detailed description:
	* Choose the type of expense (e.g., Travel - Airfare, Meals and Entertainment, etc.).
	* Enter a brief description of the expense.
3. Enter expense-specific details:
	* Enter the estimated cost of the expense.
	* Select the urgency level of the expense (Routine, Standard, Urgent, Critical).
	* If applicable, enter project code and project name.
4. Confirm approval information:
	* Indicate whether the expense is budgeted or not.
5. Add any additional comments, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Department** (Text, Required): Enter your department.
* **Manager Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of your direct manager.
* **Request Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the expense request.
* **Type of Expense** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of expense (e.g., Travel - Airfare, Meals and Entertainment, etc.).
* **Detailed Description** (Text, Required): Provide a detailed description of the expense.
* **Estimated Amount** (Number, Required): Enter the estimated cost of the expense.
* **Business Purpose** (Text, Required): Explain why this expense is necessary.
* **Expected Business Benefit** (Text, Required): Describe how this expense will benefit the company.
* **Alternative Options Considered** (Text, Optional): If applicable, enter any alternative options considered.
* **Urgency Level** (Select One, Required): Select the urgency level of the expense (Routine, Standard, Urgent, Critical).
* **Project Code** (Text, Optional): If applicable, enter the project code.
* **Project Name** (Text, Optional): If applicable, enter the project name.
* **Client Name** (Text, Optional): If applicable, enter the client name.
* **Is this budgeted?** (Select One, Required): Indicate whether the expense is budgeted or not.
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Optional): Add any other information about the expense request.

Note: Fields with a `Required: true` label are mandatory and must be completed for the form to be submitted successfully.
