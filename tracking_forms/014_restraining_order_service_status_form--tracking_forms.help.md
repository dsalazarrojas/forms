# Restraining Order Service Status Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and update the status of a restraining order service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the current service status from the options provided.
2. Enter the date when the service began.
3. Enter the date when the service ended (if applicable).
4. Provide a brief explanation for the reason why the service status is not complete.
5. Add any additional comments regarding the service status.
6. Indicate the name of the legal team handling the case.
7. Mention the name of the administrative team handling the case.
8. Select the tool assigned to handle the case.
9. Confirm who submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Active** (Select One): Choose "Active" to indicate that the service is currently active and being actively worked on.
* **Inactive** (Select One): Choose "Inactive" to indicate that the service is no longer active.
* **date_service_began** (Date): Enter the date when the service began.
* **date_service_ended** (Date): Enter the date when the service ended (if applicable).
* **service_status** (Select One): Choose "Complete" to indicate that the service is complete, "In Progress" to indicate that it's ongoing, or "Not Started" to indicate that it's not yet begun.
* **service_status_reason** (Text): Provide a brief explanation for the reason why the service status is not complete. This field is optional.
* **service_status_comment** (Text): Add any additional comments regarding the service status. This field is optional.
* **legal_team_name** (Text): Enter the name of the legal team handling the case.
* **administrative_team_name** (Text): Enter the name of the administrative team handling the case.
* **assigned_tool** (Select One): Choose the tool assigned to handle the case, such as "chatjimmy" or "other".
* **submitted_by** (Text): Confirm who submitted the form.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Use clear and concise language when providing additional comments or explanations.
* Double-check dates for accuracy.
* Verify the correct assignment of tools and teams handling the case.
