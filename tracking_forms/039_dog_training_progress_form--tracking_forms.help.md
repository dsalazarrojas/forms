# Dog Training Progress Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Dog Training Progress Form" is designed to track the progress of dog training sessions. It collects information on the current status of the dog's training, schedule, details about the dog, and owner's information. This form helps to efficiently monitor and record the progress of each dog's training sessions, ensuring that trainers can keep track of every session and provide effective feedback to dog owners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Current Status" field to indicate the current stage of the training session.
2. Choose the "Training Schedule" field to select the date and time of the upcoming or past training session.
3. In the "Dog Details" field, provide detailed information about the dog, such as its breed, age, and any relevant health conditions.
4. Enter the "Owner Details" field to provide contact information and relevant details about the dog's owner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Current Status** (text, required: false): This field allows you to select the current status of the dog's training session. Please choose one of the following options or type a custom status (e.g., "Basic Obedience", "Advanced Training", etc.).
* **Training Schedule** (date, required: false): Select the date and time of the upcoming or past training session. This will help you keep track of your scheduled appointments.
* **Dog Details** (text, required: false): Provide detailed information about the dog, including its breed, age, and any health conditions that may impact its training.
* **Owner Details** (text, required: false): Enter the owner's contact information and any relevant details about their schedule and availability.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, even if they're not marked as "required".
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the form administrator for assistance.
* Save your progress regularly to avoid data loss.
* If you need to modify any information, please use the edit feature to update the form.
