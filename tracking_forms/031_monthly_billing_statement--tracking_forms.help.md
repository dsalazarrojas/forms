# Monthly Billing Statement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their monthly billing status, which can be used for internal accounting or reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your relevant information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, `text`, required: false): This field is meant to capture the customer's name, address, and other identifying information.
* **Billing Information** (`billing_info`, `text`, required: false): In this field, please enter any relevant billing details, such as invoice numbers, due dates, or amounts owed.
* **Summary** (`summary`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief summary of the billing situation, such as any outstanding issues or concerns.

## Tips

* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns about your billing, be sure to provide a clear and concise summary in the Summary field.
* Contact us if you need assistance or clarification on any field or process.
