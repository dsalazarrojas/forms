# Employee Instructional Hours Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to track instructional hours for employees within an organization. It captures information about the employee, the instructional hours they have worked, and the details of their submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter the employee's last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter the employee's job title in the "job title" field.
4. Enter the employee's ID in the "employee id" field.
5. Enter the total instructional hours worked by the employee in the "instructional hours" field.
6. Select the date when the instructional hours were worked in the "instructional hours date" field.
7. Enter the start time of the instructional hours in the "instructional hours start time" field.
8. Enter the end time of the instructional hours in the "instructional hours end time" field.
9. Enter the break time in the "instructional hours break time" field.
10. Enter the break time length in the "instructional hours break time length" field.
11. Enter any additional notes about the instructional hours in the "instructional hours notes" field.
12. Enter the employee's email address in the "email" field.
13. Enter the employee's phone number in the "phone" field.
14. Select the person who submitted this form from the "submitted by" field.
15. Enter the email address of the person who submitted this form in the "submitted by email" field.
16. Enter the phone number of the person who submitted this form in the "submitted by phone" field.
17. Select the date when this form was submitted in the "submitted by date" field.
18. Enter the start time of the submission in the "submitted by start time" field.
19. Enter the end time of the submission in the "submitted by end time" field.
20. Enter the break time in the "submitted by break time" field.
21. Enter the break time length in the "submitted by break time length" field.
22. Enter any additional notes about the submission in the "submitted by notes" field.
