# Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory Form is used to gather information about items in inventory. It allows users to select items, enter product names, quantities, and locations to track their status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select Items**
	* On the "Select Items" page, choose the items you want to track from the list provided.
2. **Enter Product Name**
	* In the "Product Name" field, enter the name of the selected item.
3. **Enter Quantity**
	* In the "Quantity" field, enter the number of items.
4. **Enter Location**
	* In the "Location" field, enter the location where the item is stored.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Items**: Select all items that you want to track by choosing the relevant options from the list. If you want to track no items, select "No".
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the selected item to provide more information about it.
* **Quantity**: Enter the total quantity of the selected item(s).
* **Location**: Enter the location where the selected item(s) is/are stored.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all relevant items to ensure accurate tracking.
* Double-check the information entered to ensure accuracy.
* If you are not sure about an item or location, please contact the relevant department for clarification.
