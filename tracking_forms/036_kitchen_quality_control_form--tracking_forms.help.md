# kitchen_quality_control_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The kitchen quality control form is used to assess the kitchen environment, staff, and food handling practices to ensure a safe and healthy food handling environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the date of your visit in the "date_of_visit" field.
2. In the "restaurant_name" field, type the name of the restaurant being visited.
3. In the "kitchen_manager_name" field, type the name of the kitchen manager or responsible staff member.
4. Evaluate the food standard as either "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" in the "food_standard_measured" field.
5. Answer "Yes" or "No" for the following questions:
   - "kitchen_area_inspected"
   - "issues_found"
   - "kitchen_staff_training"
   - "food_handling_safety_certification"
   - "food_temperature_check"
   - "refigerated_storage"
   - "food_labels_and_date_stamps"
   - "food_handling_safety_practices"
   - "waste_disposal"
   - "cleanliness_standards"
   - "proper_hand_washing"
   - "food_storage"
   - "food_temperature_checklist"
   - "kitchen_hygiene"
6. Answer "Yes" or "No" for the following questions:
   - "pest_control_measures"
7. Provide a brief action plan to address any issues found in the "action_taken" field.
8. In the "follow_up_action" field, describe the follow-up action to be taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **kitchen_quality_control_form_page_1_label** (`kitchen_quality_control_form_page_1_label`, text, required): This is the first page of the form.
* **date_of_visit** (date, optional): Enter the date of your visit.
* **restaurant_name** (text, required): Type the name of the restaurant being visited.
* **kitchen_manager_name** (text, required): Type the name of the kitchen manager or responsible staff member.
* **food_standard_measured** (select_one, required): Evaluate the food standard as either "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **comments** (text, optional): Provide any additional comments about your visit.
* **kitchen_area_inspected** (text, optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" if the kitchen area was inspected.
* **issues_found** (select_multiple, required): Answer "Yes" or "No" for each issue found.
* **action_taken** (text, required): Provide a brief action plan to address any issues found.
* **follow_up_action** (text, optional): Describe the follow-up action to be taken.
* **next_visit** (date, optional): Enter the date of the next visit.
* **kitchen_staff_training** (select_multiple, required): Answer "Yes" or "No" if the kitchen staff was trained.
* **food_handling_safety_certification** (select_multiple, optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" if the food handling safety certification is present.
* **food_temperature_check** (select_multiple, required): Answer "Yes" or "No" for each temperature check.
* **refigerated_storage** (select_multiple, required): Answer "Yes" or "No" if the refrigerator storage is adequate.
* **food_labels_and_date_stamps** (select_multiple, optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" if food labels and date stamps are present.
* **food_handling_safety_practices** (select_multiple, required): Answer "Yes" or "No" for each food handling safety practice.
* **pest_control_measures** (select_multiple, optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" if pest control measures are in place.
* **waste_disposal** (select_multiple, required): Answer "Yes" or "No" if waste disposal is proper.
* **cleanliness_standards** (select_multiple, required): Answer "Yes" or "No" for each cleanliness standard.
* **proper_hand_washing** (select_multiple, optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" if proper hand washing is observed.
* **food_storage** (select_multiple, optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" if food storage is proper.
* **food_temperature_checklist** (select_multiple, optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" if food temperature checklist is present.
* **kitchen_hygiene** (select_multiple, optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" if kitchen hygiene is maintained.
* **follow_up_action** (text, optional): Describe the follow-up action to be taken.
* **next_visit** (date, optional): Enter the date of the next visit.
