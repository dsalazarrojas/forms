# Personal Finance Organizer - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you organize your personal finance by collecting information on your income, expenses, savings, and other financial habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each of the 9 pages, one by one.
2. Each page has a text field where you can enter your information.
3. Be thorough and accurate when providing your information.
4. Use the space provided on each page to write your thoughts, notes, or any additional information that may be relevant to your financial situation.
