# Van Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Van Safety Checklist is a form designed to record and track the safety checks on a van, ensuring that all critical systems and components are properly functioning. This form helps identify potential issues and schedule necessary maintenance or repairs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Van Safety Checklist, follow these steps:

1. Review the van and identify any safety concerns or issues.
2. Answer each question in the corresponding field. Select the correct option or enter a numerical value, if applicable.
3. Ensure you complete all fields, even if it seems unnecessary.
4. Save or submit the form to record the safety checks and track progress.
