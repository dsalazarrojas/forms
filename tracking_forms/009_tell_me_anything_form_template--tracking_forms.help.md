# Tell Me Anything Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tell Me Anything Form Template is designed to collect various types of information from users in a flexible and organized manner. This form is suitable for use cases where users want to share their thoughts, ideas, or concerns without having to navigate through a complex set of options or fields.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User Message** in the text field, and share your thoughts or ideas.
2. Enter your **Name** in the text field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the text field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** (if applicable).
5. Select the type of message you want to share from the **Message Type** dropdown menu.
6. Select the category of your message from the multiple choice options.
7. Enter the date and time you are sending the message.
8. Select the **Location** from the dropdown menu (if applicable).
9. Optionally, select **Submit** to finalize your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Message** (`user_message`, text, not required): Enter your message, thoughts, or ideas in this text field.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Enter your name in this text field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address in this text field.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number in this text field (if you have one).
* **Message Type** (`message_type`, select_one, not required): Select the type of message you want to share (option, question, concern, or other).
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the categories that apply to your message.
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Enter the date of your message.
* **Time** (`time`, time, not required): Enter the time of your message.
* **Location** (`location`, select_one, not required): Select the location associated with your message.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, not required): Select this option to submit your message and complete the form.
