# Medical Center Online Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients about their medical history, contact details, and other relevant information to ensure that the medical team has all necessary information before their appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the page you wish to complete by clicking on the corresponding button.
2. Fill out the fields as required, using the options provided for select fields.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, as indicated by the required label.
4. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for assistance from the medical staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information**: Please fill out this field with your basic information, such as your name, address, and any other identifying details.

### Medical History
* **Medical History**: Please select all the medical conditions that apply to you from the options provided. For example, if you have hypertension and diabetes, select both options.

### Patient Assessments
* **Patient Assessments**: Select the medical condition that best describes your current assessment. If none of the options apply, please select "Other (Specify)" and provide a brief explanation in the field below.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information**: Please fill out this field with your email address. This will be used as the primary contact point for further communication.

### Medical Contact
* **Medical Contact**: Select the type of medical professional that typically handles your medical care. Please note that this field is optional and will be used for internal purposes only.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact**: Please provide the name and phone number of a contact person who can be reached in case of an emergency.

### Emergency Contact Information
* **Emergency Contact Information**: Please provide any additional information about your emergency contact, such as their relationship to you.

### Insurance Information
* **Insurance Information**: Please fill out this field with any relevant insurance information, such as your policy number and provider name.

### Patient Notes
* **Patient Notes**: This field is for you to provide any additional information about your medical history or concerns. Please keep this field concise and clear.

### Medical Notes
* **Medical Notes**: This field is for you to provide any additional information about your medical history or concerns. Please keep this field concise and clear.

### Medical Contact Information
* **Medical Contact Information**: Please provide any additional information about your medical contact, such as their practice name and address.

### Emergency Information
* **Emergency Information**: Please provide any additional information about your emergency contact, such as their medical conditions or allergies. 

Tips:
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any medical conditions that are not listed in the select field, please select "Other (Specify)" and provide a brief explanation.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for assistance from the medical staff.
