# Early Access List Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Early Access List Template is a form used to track and document customer interactions, providing a structured approach to gathering essential information and customer insights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's information, including name and contact details.
2. Specify the contact method (email or phone number) and the early access date and time.
3. Provide any additional notes or comments related to the customer.
4. Indicate the customer's interest in the product or service.
5. Leave any customer comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information**: Enter the customer's name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the customer's email address where they can be reached.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number where they can be reached.
* **Early Access Date**: Specify the date when the customer has early access to the product or service.
* **Early Access Time**: Specify the time when the customer has early access to the product or service.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or details that may be relevant to the customer's interaction.
* **Customer Interest**: Select whether the customer is Interested, Not Interested, or Undecided in the product or service.
* **Customer Comments**: Leave any feedback or comments from the customer.
* **Company**: Enter the customer's company name or affiliation.
* **Customer Comments 2**: Leave any additional comments or feedback from the customer.

## Tips
* Always ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* Double-check for any existing records or systems that may contain similar information.
* Use this form in conjunction with the Early Access process to streamline customer interactions.
