# Event Expense Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about employee expenses related to company events. This information will be used for financial management and compliance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you have provided all necessary information.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID badge.
* **Expense Date** (`expense_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event related to the expense.
* **Expense Description** (`expense_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the nature of the expense, including any relevant context or details.
* **Expense Amount** (`expense_amount`, number, optional): Enter the exact amount of the expense.
* **Submit Request** (`submit_button`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to submit your request, or "No" if you are not ready to submit it at this time.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, optional): Enter your company email address as it appears on your email account.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, optional): Enter your company phone number as it appears on your employee ID badge.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have checked the company's expense policies and procedures before submitting your request.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the finance department for guidance.
* Please submit only one request per event-related expense. If you have multiple expenses related to the same event, please submit one form with all relevant information.
