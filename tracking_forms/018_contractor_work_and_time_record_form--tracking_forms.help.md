# Contractor Work and Time Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Contractor Work and Time Record Form is a tool to record and track work hours, progress, and milestones for each project. It helps to maintain accurate and standardized records for both contractors and project managers. This form is to be completed by contractors after every working day, and is a mandatory part of their workflow.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the contractor information:
	* Enter your name and ID number in the first two fields.
	* Provide your email address for contact purposes.
	* Enter your phone number so that you can be reached for work-related matters.
2. Enter project details:
	* Provide the project name and identification code.
	* Enter the project location.
	* Identify your on-site supervisor.
3. Log your work:
	* Fill in the date of work.
	* Record the start and end times of your work.
	* Report any breaks taken and their duration.
	* Calculate and record the total hours worked.
	* If you worked overtime, report the hours worked beyond the standard workday.
4. Track progress:
	* Estimate the percentage of the task completed.
	* Mention any milestones achieved.
	* Describe any delays or issues encountered.
5. Safety:
	* List the safety equipment used.
	* Report any safety incidents.
6. Verify your work:
	* Identify the person verifying your work.
	* Date of verification.
7. Sign and date:
	* Sign the form to confirm your work was done.
	* Date when you signed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contractor Full Name**: Enter your legal name as recorded in your contract or personnel records.
* **Contractor ID Number**: Enter your identification or badge number.
* **Email Address**: Provide your email address for work-related contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for work-related calls.
* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project you are working on.
* **Project Code**: If applicable, enter the project identification code.
* **Project Location**: Enter the address or site of work.
* **Site Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your on-site supervisor.
* **Work Date**: Enter the date of this work record.
* **Start Time**: Record the time work began.
* **End Time**: Record the time work ended.
* **Break Duration**: Report the total break time taken.
* **Total Hours**: Calculate and enter the net hours worked.
* **Overtime Hours**: If worked beyond the standard workday, report these hours.
* **Work Category**: Choose the category of work performed.
* **Task Description**: Describe the detailed work done.
* **Specific Work Area**: If applicable, mention the location within the project site where work was done.
* **Materials Used**: List the materials consumed during work.
* **Equipment Used**: If applicable, list the equipment or machinery used.
* **Progress Made Today**: Estimate the percentage of task completed.
* **Milestones Reached**: Mention any milestones achieved.
* **Delays or Issues Encountered**: Describe any problems encountered.
* **Weather Conditions**: If outdoor work, report the weather conditions.
* **Safety Equipment Used**: List the PPE worn during work.
* **Safety Incidents**: Report any accidents or near-misses.
* **Verified By**: Identify the person verifying your work.
* **Verification Date**: Date of verification.
* **Contractor Signature Date**: Date when you signed the form.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any other relevant information.
* **Progress Percentage**: Choose an estimated percentage of task completed.
* **Safety Harness**: Choose safety equipment used.
* **High-Visibility Vest**: Choose safety equipment used.
* **Gloves**: Choose safety equipment used.
* **Hearing Protection**: Choose safety equipment used.
* **Respirator**: Choose safety equipment used.
* **Hard Hat**: Choose safety equipment used.
* **Steel-Toe Boots**: Choose safety equipment used.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If unsure about any field, ask your project manager or supervisor for clarification.
* Be honest and accurate when reporting work hours, progress, and safety incidents.
* Use this form as a tool to improve your work efficiency and quality.
