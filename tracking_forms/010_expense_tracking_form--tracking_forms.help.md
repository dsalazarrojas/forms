# expense_tracking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The expense tracking form is a tool for employees to report their expenses, helping management keep track of company spendings and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the transaction information in the "transaction_info" field.
2. Select the date of the transaction using the calendar icon.
3. Enter the amount of the transaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **transaction_info** (`transaction_info`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the transaction, such as what you purchased or what it was for.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of the transaction from the calendar icon.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the total amount of the transaction.
