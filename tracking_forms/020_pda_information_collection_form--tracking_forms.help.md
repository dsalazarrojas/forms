# PDA Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your experience with a PDA (Personal Digital Assistant), helping us understand your device usage, preferences, and any issues encountered while using the device. Your feedback is essential in shaping our future product development and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the provided questions and ensure you have the necessary information before starting.
2.  Fill out each page of the form carefully, following the guidelines provided for each field.
3.  Ensure that you complete all required fields.
4.  Optional fields can be left blank if not applicable.
5.  Double-check your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **page_1** (`page_1`, `note`, required: `false`): This is an introductory page that serves as a note-taking section for you to record any general comments or observations about your PDA usage. You can use this space for any relevant information that doesn't fit into other fields.
- **page_2** (`page_2`, `time`, required: `false`): This field is used to record the amount of time you used the PDA for a particular activity. Please enter the total time spent using the device for that activity.
- **page_3** (`page_3`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose one of the options below to describe how often you use your PDA:
    • Option 1: Daily
    • Option 2: Several times a week
    • Option 3: Rarely
- **page_4** (`page_4`, `number`, required: `false`): This field is meant for recording the number of times you have used the PDA for a particular task or activity. For example, if you have used the PDA to track your blood glucose levels three times this week, enter "3".
- **page_5** (`page_5`, `date`, required: `false`): This field is for recording the date on which you last used the PDA for a particular activity. Please enter the date in a standard date format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
- **page_6** (`page_6`, `number`, required: `false`): Similar to field page_4, this is for recording the number of times you have used the PDA for another task or activity. For example, if you have used the PDA to track your blood pressure five times this month, enter "5".
- **page_7** (`page_7`, `text`, required: `false`): This text field is for any additional, more detailed information about your PDA experience. Feel free to include any comments, concerns, or feedback that you think would be helpful for us to consider when improving the PDA.

## Tips
Remember to submit this form only if you have used a PDA and are providing feedback on your experience. Double-check your responses for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for support.
