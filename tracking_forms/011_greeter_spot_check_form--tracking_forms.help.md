## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a Greeter Spot Check, which is used to assess the quality of service provided by a representative (or "Greeter") during a customer interaction. The purpose of this form is to track key details about the interaction and help identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Greeter's name in the "Greeter Name" field. This is the name of the representative who interacted with the customer.
2. In the "greeting" field, enter a brief description of how the Greeter greeted the customer.
3. Record the time of the Spot Check interaction in the "Spot Check Time" field. This will help track the duration and timing of the interaction.
4. Enter a brief summary of the customer's interaction in the "Customer Interaction" field.
5. Enter any additional feedback or comments about the interaction in the "Feedback" field.
6. Optionally, provide additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
7. If applicable, assign a score (on a scale of 1-10) to the interaction in the "Score" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Greeter Name** (`greeter_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the representative who interacted with the customer.
* **greeting** (`greeting`, text, optional):
	+ Enter a brief description of how the Greeter greeted the customer.
* **Spot Check Time** (`spot_check_time`, time, optional):
	+ Record the time of the Spot Check interaction.
* **Customer Interaction** (`customer_interaction`, text, optional):
	+ Enter a brief summary of the customer's interaction with the representative.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional feedback or comments about the interaction.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional comments or context about the interaction.
* **Score** (`score`, number, optional):
	+ Assign a score (on a scale of 1-10) to the interaction, indicating its quality.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any information, it's okay to ask for clarification.
* Remember that this form is for tracking and improvement purposes only, so be honest and thorough in your responses!
