# Subscription Tracker Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for tracking and analyzing subscription-related data. It includes various fields for capturing information about services, renewal dates, and statuses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the relevant fields as follows:
* Select the "Service Type" field to indicate the type of service you are tracking.
* Enter the "Service Name" to identify the specific service.
* Enter the "Frequency" to indicate how often the service is renewed.
* Input the "Renewal Date" for the renewal date of the service.
* Enter the "Subscription Date" to track when the subscription began.
* Select the "Renewal Status" to indicate the current status of the service.
* Select the "Subscription Status" to indicate the status of the service.
* Enter any relevant "Renewal Notes" or "Subscription Notes" for additional information.
* Input the "Renewal Date Remarks" for any remarks about the renewal date.
* Enter the "Renewal Frequency" and "Subscription Frequency" for how often the service is renewed or subscribed.
* Input the "Renewal Spendings" and "Subscription Spendings" for any financial information.
* Enter any additional "Notes" for any other comments.
* Enter the "Contact" email address if you need to reach out to the subscriber.
* Enter the "Phone" number if you need to contact the subscriber.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Type**: This field is used to indicate the type of service being tracked. (Text field)
* **Service Name**: Enter the name of the service being tracked. (Text field)
* **Frequency**: Enter how often the service is renewed or subscribed. (Text field)
* **Renewal Date**: Input the date of the renewal. (Date field)
* **Subscription Date**: Enter the date the subscription began. (Date field)
* **Renewal Status**: Select the current status of the service. (Select_one field with options "Active", "Inactive", "Canceled")
* **Subscription Status**: Select the status of the service. (Select_one field with options "Active", "Inactive", "Pending")
* **Renewal Notes**: Enter any notes about the renewal. (Note field)
* **Subscription Notes**: Enter any notes about the subscription. (Note field)
* **Renewal Date Remarks**: Input any remarks about the renewal date. (Time field)
* **Renewal Frequency**: Enter how often the service is renewed. (Text field)
* **Subscription Frequency**: Enter how often the service is subscribed. (Text field)
* **Renewal Spendings**: Input any financial information about the renewal. (Number field)
* **Subscription Spendings**: Input any financial information about the subscription. (Number field)
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments. (Note field)
* **Contact**: Enter the email address of the subscriber. (Email field)
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the subscriber. (Text field)
