# Unreturned Item Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Unreturned Item Report form is designed to collect information about items that have not been returned to the office or facility. It helps administrators or asset managers track the status of borrowed items, including the reason for return, borrower details, and follow-up activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Office Name** (required) to identify the office or facility.
2. Select the **School Department** (required) where the item was borrowed from.
3. Choose the **Item Type** (required) of the item being reported (e.g., Equipment, Materials, Library, or Other).
4. Enter a brief description of the **Item Description** (required).
5. Enter the **Borrower Name** (required), who borrowed the item.
6. Enter the **Borrower Email** (required), associated with the borrower.
7. Enter the date the item was **Borrowed Date** (required).
8. Enter the date the item was **Due Date** (required).
9. Enter the date the item was **Returned Date** (required).
10. Select the reason for return, choose from **Reason for Return** (required) options: Not Available, Not Used, or Other.
11. Enter the **Asset Number** (required) of the item.
12. Enter the **Location** (required) where the item is currently located.
13. Add any additional comments in the **Comments** field (optional).
14. Select the **Follow Up Status** (required): Completed, Not Contacted, or Follow Up Needed.
15. If selected, enter the date and time of the **Follow Up Date** (required).
16. Select the method of **Contact Method** (required): Email, Phone, or In Person.
17. Enter the number associated with the contact method, **Contact Number** (optional).
18. Add any follow-up comments in the **Follow Up Comments** field (optional).
19. Enter the **Asset Condition** (required) of the item.
20. Enter your name as the **Borrower Signature** (optional), for verification purposes.
21. Enter the **Office Email** (optional), if applicable.
22. Enter the person or department responsible for the **Follow Up By** (optional).
23. Select the current **Asset Status** (required): Lost, Found, or In Transit.
24. Enter any additional notes regarding the asset's condition in the **Asset Condition Notes** field (optional).
25. Add any follow-up comments in the **Follow Up Comments** field (optional).
