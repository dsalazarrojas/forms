# Monthly Drug Inventory Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to track the status of drug items on a monthly basis. It is designed to help keep track of inventory items and their current status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill in the Inventory Date** (`inventory_date`, date): Enter the date for which you are currently tracking the inventory (e.g., the current month).
2. **Choose the Location** (`location`, text): Select the location where the item is currently at or will be stored.
3. **Select Items to Track** (`inventory_items`, select_multiple): Choose from the list which items you want to track (e.g., select "Yes" for items you want to track and "No" for items you don't).
4. **Provide Drug Description** (`drug_description`, text): Enter a brief description of the drug item.
5. **Enter Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text): Enter the serial number of the drug item.
6. **Enter Condition** (`condition`, text): Enter the current condition of the drug item.
7. **Enter Quantity** (`quantity`, number): Enter the current quantity of the drug item.
8. **Enter Value** (`value`, number): Enter the value or cost of the drug item.
9. **Enter Remarks** (`remarks`, text): Enter any additional information or comments regarding the item.
10. **Set Status** (`status`, select_one): Select the status of the item from the options "Active", "Inactive", or "Lost".
11. **Assign To** (`assigned_tool`, select_one): Select the person responsible for the item (e.g., "chatjimmy" or "Other").

* **Inventory Date** (`inventory_date`, date): Date for which you are currently tracking the inventory.
