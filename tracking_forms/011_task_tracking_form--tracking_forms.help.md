# Task Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Task Tracking Form is a tool used to monitor and manage tasks. It helps users input and manage various task details such as task status, due date, description, assignee, priority, progress, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant task status from the "Task Status" field.
2. Enter the due date of the task if it is applicable.
3. Provide a detailed description of the task in the "Task Description" field.
4. If the task is assigned to a team, select the team from the "Assigned To" field.
5. Choose the priority level of the task from the "Priority" field.
6. Input the current progress of the task from the "Task Progress" field.
7. Add any notes related to the task in the "Task Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Task Status** (`task_status`): Choose the current status of the task, such as Label 1, Label 2, Label 3, Label 4, or Label 5.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`): Enter the date by which the task is due. This field is not required.
* **Task Description** (`task_description`): Enter a detailed description of the task.
* **Assignee** (`assignee`): Select who the task is assigned to, such as Team A, Team B, or Team C.
* **Priority** (`priority`): Choose the priority level of the task, such as Low, Medium, or High.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`): This field is not used in this form. 
* **Task Notes** (`task_notes`): Add any additional notes related to the task.
* **Task Priority** (`task_priority`): Select the priority level of the task, such as Low, Medium, or High.
* **Task Progress** (`task_progress`): Choose the current progress of the task, such as Not Started, In Progress, or Completed.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Select who the task is assigned to, such as Team A, Team B, or Team C.
* **Created at** (`created_at`): This field displays the date and time the task was created.
* **Updated at** (`updated_at`): Select the date and time the task was last updated, such as Yesterday, Today, or Never.

Note: The form fields and their labels are self-explanatory, but the explanations above provide a more detailed understanding of what each field is used for.
