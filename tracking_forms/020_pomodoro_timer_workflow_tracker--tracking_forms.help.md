# Pomodoro Timer Workflow Tracker - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pomodoro Timer Workflow Tracker is a form designed to track and analyze individual sessions of work. It helps users understand their workflow and productivity during each session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form for each session as you complete it.
2. Enter the Session start time to record the beginning of your work session.
3. Enter the Session duration to record the length of your work session.
4. Enter the Work done today to record the amount of work completed during the session.
5. Enter the Focused time spent to record the amount of time spent on focused work.
6. Enter the Break time spent to record the amount of time spent on breaks.
7. Enter the Total time spent to record the total time spent during the session.
8. Choose the Session frequency to select the type of session (Pomodoro, Short session, or Long session).
9. Enter the Session goal to set a goal for what you want to accomplish during the session.
10. Add any Comments about your session.
11. Choose whether to submit Form submissions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session start time**:
	+ A time field where you can input the start time of your work session.
* **Session duration**:
	+ An integer field where you can input the length of your session in minutes.
* **Work done today**:
	+ An integer field where you can input the amount of work completed during the session.
* **Focused time spent**:
	+ An integer field where you can input the amount of time spent on focused work.
* **Break time spent**:
	+ An integer field where you can input the amount of time spent on breaks.
* **Total time spent**:
	+ An integer field where you can input the total time spent during the session.
* **Session frequency**:
	+ A select field where you can choose the type of session (Pomodoro, Short session, or Long session).
* **Session goal**:
	+ An integer field where you can input a goal for what you want to accomplish during the session.
* **Comments**:
	+ A free-text field where you can add any comments about your session.
* **Form submissions**:
	+ A select field where you can choose whether or not you want to submit this form (yes or no).
