# Key Issuance Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Key Issuance Log form is used to record and track the issuance and return of keys. This form ensures that each key is only recorded once and provides a clear and concise record of the key issuance and return process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Issued By" field with the name of the person or department that issued the key.
2. Enter the date the key was issued in the "Date Issued" field.
3. Enter the key number in the "Key Number" field.
4. Enter the location where the key was issued in the "Location" field.
5. Enter the name of the person who received the key in the "Issued To" field.
6. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the key in the "Contact Person" field.
7. If the key has been returned, enter the date it was returned in the "Date Returned" field.
8. Fill in any additional comments or remarks in the "Remarks" field.
9. Select the current status of the key in the "Status" field (e.g., "Returned", "Lost", or "Pending").
10. If necessary, provide a brief explanation for the key's status in the "Reason for Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Issued By** (`form_issued_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or department that issued the key.
* **Date Issued** (`date_issued`, date, optional): Enter the date the key was issued.
* **Key Number** (`key_number`, text, optional): Enter the key number.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the key was issued.
* **Issued To** (`issued_to`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who received the key.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the key.
* **Date Returned** (`date_returned`, date, optional): If the key has been returned, enter the date it was returned.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, note, optional): Fill in any additional comments or remarks about the key.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the key (e.g., "Returned", "Lost", or "Pending").
* **Reason for Status** (`reason_for_status`, text, optional): If necessary, provide a brief explanation for the key's status.
