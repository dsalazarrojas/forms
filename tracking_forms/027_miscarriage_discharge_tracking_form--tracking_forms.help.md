# Miscellaneous Discharge Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track miscellaneous discharges, which can include any information related to a patient's or individual's medical treatment, care, or condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form fields below and ensure that you have all necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
3. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): This field is for entering general information about the discharge, such as the date and time of discharge or any other relevant details.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, text, optional): This field is for entering any medical information related to the discharge, such as the diagnosis or treatment plan.
* **Medical Team** (`medical_team`, text, optional): This field is for listing the medical team members involved in the patient's care, such as doctors or nurses.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, text, optional): This field is for scheduling any follow-up appointments or care that may be required after the discharge.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are using the correct information in each field to accurately and completely track the patient's discharge.
* If a field is unclear or not applicable, you can skip it or contact your supervisor or healthcare provider for further guidance.
* Please save and keep a record of the completed form for future reference.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [support email: form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
