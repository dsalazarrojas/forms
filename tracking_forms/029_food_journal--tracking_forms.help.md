# Food Journal - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Food Journal form is designed to help you track your meal options and related information. This guide will walk you through each field to ensure you fill out the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page of the form carefully, as each page contains a specific set of fields for recording your meal information.
2. Select one option for each meal option field (Select Meal Option 1, Select Meal Option 2, etc.) based on your actual meal choices.
3. Enter a date if applicable, using the date field provided.
4. Take advantage of the note fields to provide additional context or comments about your meal choices.
5. If you have an email or phone number where you can be reached for follow-up, please include it in the respective fields.
