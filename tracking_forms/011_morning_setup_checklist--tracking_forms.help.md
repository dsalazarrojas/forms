# morning-setup-checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their morning setups. The purpose of this form is to gather data on various aspects of morning setups, including tasks, readiness, issues, and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, select the "Morning Tasks" field and choose the tasks that apply to your morning setup.
2. In the "Morning Readiness" field, select whether you are ready or not ready for the day.
3. In the "Morning Issues" field, provide any issues or concerns you have for the day.
4. In the "Morning Goals" field, enter your goals or objectives for the morning.
5. In the "Morning Notes" field, provide any additional notes or comments about your morning setup.
6. In the "Morning Time" field, enter the time you woke up or the time you started your morning routine.
7. In the "Morning Date" field, enter the date of the event or occurrence you are tracking.
8. In the "Morning Email" field, enter your email address (if applicable).
9. In the "Morning Phone" field, enter your phone number (if applicable).
10. In the "Morning Comments" field, enter any additional comments or feedback about your morning setup.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Morning Tasks**: Select all the tasks you completed or plan to complete during your morning setup.
* **Morning Readiness**: Choose whether you are ready or not ready for the day.
* **Morning Issues**: Enter any issues or concerns you have for the day.
* **Morning Goals**: Enter your goals or objectives for the morning.
* **Morning Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your morning setup.
* **Morning Time**: Enter the time you woke up or the time you started your morning routine.
* **Morning Date**: Enter the date of the event or occurrence you are tracking.
* **Morning Email**: Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Morning Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Morning Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your morning setup.
