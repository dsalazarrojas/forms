# Food Storage Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Storage Inventory Form is used to track food storage inventory. It helps users to keep accurate records of food items, their amounts, and categories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct **food-item** from the drop-down menu.
2. Enter the **amount-on-hand** to record the current quantity of the selected food item.
3. Enter the **amount-needed** to record the desired quantity of the selected food item.
4. Add any relevant **notes** regarding the food item.
5. If applicable, specify the **purchased** date and time of the last purchase.
6. Select the **category** of the form from the drop-down menu (Tracking Forms, Food Forms, or Admin Forms).
7. Enter any additional **description** of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Storage Inventory Form** (Page 1):
	+ **food-item** (Select One): Select the food item from the drop-down menu. This field is not required.
		- Options: Food A, Food B, Food C
	+ **amount-on-hand** (Text): Enter the current quantity of the selected food item. This field is not required.
	+ **amount-needed** (Number): Enter the desired quantity of the selected food item. This field is not required.
	+ **purchases** (Text): Enter any relevant information about the last purchase. This field is not required.
	+ **notes** (Note): Enter any additional notes regarding the food item. This field is not required.
	+ **assigned-tool** (Text): Enter any specific tool information for the food item. This field is not required.
* **Category** (Page 2):
	+ **category** (Select Multiple): Select the category of the form from the drop-down menu (Tracking Forms, Food Forms, or Admin Forms). This field is not required.
* **Additional Information** (Page 3):
	+ **description** (Text): Enter any additional description of the form. This field is not required.

Note: Please make sure to fill out the form according to your specific needs and requirements.
