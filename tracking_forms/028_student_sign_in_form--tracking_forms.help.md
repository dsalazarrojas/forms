# Student Sign In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Sign In Form is a tool for students to sign in and record their attendance, providing essential information to the educational institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information accurately and completely.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID (1)**: A unique identifier assigned to each student.
* **Name (2)**: The student's full name.
* **Date (3)**: The date of attendance, in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time (4)**: The time of attendance, in the format 12:00 AM/PM.
* **School ID (5)**: A unique identifier assigned to each school or institution.
* **Teacher ID (6)**: A unique identifier assigned to each teacher.
* **Attendance (7)**: Indicate whether the student is present or absent.
* **Email (8)**: The student's email address.
* **Phone (9)**: The student's phone number.
* **Notes (10)**: Optional field for additional comments or remarks.
* **Assigned Tool (11)**: The tool assigned to the student for a specific activity or lesson.
* **Form ID (12)**: A unique identifier assigned to this form for tracking purposes.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Verify that the correct format is used for date and time fields.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the relevant authority or support team.
