# Truck Loan Payment Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Truck Loan Payment Tracker form is designed to collect and track information about loan payments for trucks, including loan amounts, payment frequencies, payment dates, borrower details, loan terms, payment statuses, and notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the loan amount in the "Loan Amount" field.
2. Select the payment frequency from the "Payment Frequency" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the payment date in the "Payment Date" field.
4. Enter the borrower's name in the "Borrower Name" field.
5. Enter the borrower's address in the "Borrowers Address" field.
6. Select the loan term from the "Loan Term" dropdown menu.
7. Select the payment status from the "Payment Status" dropdown menu.
8. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter the current loan balance in the "Loan Balance" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount of the loan.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Select the frequency of payments (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Borrower Name** (`borrower_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the borrower.
* **Borrowers Address** (`borrower_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the borrower.
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the term of the loan (6 months, 12 months, 24 months).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the loan (Active, Overdue, Paid).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Loan Balance** (`loan_balance`, `number`, optional): Enter the current loan balance.
