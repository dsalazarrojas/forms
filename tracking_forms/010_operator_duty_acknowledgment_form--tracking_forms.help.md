# Operator Duty Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Operator Duty Acknowledgment Form is a form used to document an operator's duty status and details of their assignment. This form helps ensure that operators confirm and acknowledge their duties, allowing for accurate tracking and communication within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your operator details in the "Operator Details" field.
2. Provide the timing of your duty in the "Duty Timing" field.
3. Enter any relevant assignment notes in the "Assignment Notes" field.
4. Sign and date your acknowledgment in the "Signed Acknowledgment" field.
5. Add your signature to the "Operator Signature" field.
6. Optional fields can be filled out as needed:
	* "Assigned Tool Type" if you were assigned a specific tool during your duty
	* "Shift Start Time" and "Shift End Time" if applicable
	* "Notes" for any additional comments
	* "Assigned Team" if you were part of a specific team
	* "Operator Role" if it's relevant to your duty
	* "Assigned Location" if it's relevant to your duty
	* "Duty Status" for a brief status update
	* "Shift Duration" if you need to report the length of your shift

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Operator Details** (text, required):
	+ Enter your name and other relevant details as per your operator profile.
* **Duty Timing** (text, required):
	+ Enter the start and end times of your duty, if applicable.
* **Assignment Notes** (text, required):
	+ Provide any notes or comments related to your assignment.
* **Signed Acknowledgment** (text, required):
	+ Type in the date and time you are acknowledging your duty.
* **Operator Signature** (text, required):
	+ Sign your name as the operator acknowledging your duty.
* **Assigned Tool Type** (text, optional):
	+ If you were assigned a specific tool during your duty, enter it here.
* **Shift Start Time** (date, optional):
	+ Enter the start time of your shift, if applicable.
* **Shift End Time** (date, optional):
	+ Enter the end time of your shift, if applicable.
* **Notes** (note, optional):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about your duty.
* **Assigned Team** (text, optional):
	+ If you were part of a specific team during your duty, enter it here.
* **Operator Role** (text, optional):
	+ Enter your role or position during your duty, if applicable.
* **Assigned Location** (text, optional):
	+ If it's relevant to your duty, enter the location where you were assigned.
* **Duty Status** (text, optional):
	+ Briefly update your duty status.
* **Shift Duration** (number, optional):
	+ Report the length of your shift if needed.

Note: The operator is responsible for filling out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the correct tracking and documentation of their duties.
