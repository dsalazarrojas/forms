# Running Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Running Log Form is used to record and track individual workout sessions, allowing users to monitor their progress over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct date for the workout session.
2. Enter the distance completed during the workout.
3. Enter the pace achieved during the workout.
4. Enter the time taken to complete the workout.
5. Estimate the calories burned during the workout.
6. Select the type of workout (e.g., jogging, running, biking, etc.).
7. Add any relevant notes about the workout.
8. Select the fitness apps connected to track the workout (e.g., Google Fit, Strava, Apple Health, etc.).
9. If you have multiple workouts, repeat steps 1-8 for each session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the workout session.
* **Distance** (`distance`, optional): Enter the distance completed during the workout.
* **Pace** (`pace`, optional): Enter the pace achieved during the workout.
* **Time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time taken to complete the workout.
* **Calories Burned** (`calories_burned`, optional): Estimate the calories burned during the workout.
* **Workout Type** (`workout_type`, required): Select the type of workout (e.g., jogging, running, biking, etc.).
* **Notes** (`notes`, optional): Add any relevant notes about the workout.
* **Apps Connected** (`apps_connected`, required): Select the fitness apps connected to track the workout (e.g., Google Fit, Strava, Apple Health, etc.).
* **Date 2** (`date_2`, optional): Enter the date of a repeatable workout session.
* **Distance 2** (`distance_2`, optional): Enter the distance completed during the repeatable workout session.
* **Pace 2** (`pace_2`, optional): Enter the pace achieved during the repeatable workout session.
* **Calories Burned 2** (`calories_burned_2`, optional): Estimate the calories burned during the repeatable workout session.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form for each workout session separately.
* Select the correct workout type to accurately track your progress.
* Enter accurate information for distance, pace, time, and calories burned to ensure accurate tracking.
* Use the repeatable fields for multiple workout sessions.
