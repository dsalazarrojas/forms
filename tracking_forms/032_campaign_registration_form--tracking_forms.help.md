# Campaign Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about potential participants for a specific campaign. The data collected will help the campaign organizers to understand the interest and availability of potential team members, allowing them to make informed decisions about team assignments and communication strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your basic information, including your participant's first name, last name, email, phone number, and date of birth.
2. Select the campaign you are interested in joining.
3. Choose your role in the campaign and the team you want to be assigned to.
4. Indicate your availability for the campaign, including your work schedule and any limitations.
5. List your relevant skills and expertise that can be beneficial to the campaign.
6. If applicable, describe your previous campaign experience and what you can bring to the campaign.
7. Choose the training or support you need to effectively participate in the campaign.
8. Select your preferred method of communication for future updates and interactions.
9. Finally, confirm your registration status and any additional comments you'd like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Participant First Name (1)** (Required) - Enter your participant first name.
- **Participant Last Name (2)** (Required) - Enter your participant last name.
- **Email Address (3)** (Required) - Enter your contact email address.
- **Phone Number (4)** (Required) - Enter your contact phone number.
- **Date of Birth (5)** (Required) - Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Street Address (6)** (Required) - Enter your street address.
- **City (7)** (Required) - Enter your city or town.
- **State (8)** (Required) - Enter your state or province.
- **ZIP Code (9)** (Required) - Enter your postal code.
- **Campaign Name (10)** (Required) - Select the campaign you are interested in joining.
- **Campaign Type (11)** (Required) - Select the type of campaign you are interested in joining.
- **Registration Date (12)** (Required) - Enter the date you are registering for the campaign.
- **Your Role in Campaign (13)** (Required) - Select your role in the campaign.
- **Team Assignment (14)** (Optional) - Select the team you want to be assigned to. If not assigned, select "Unassigned".
- **Campaign Start Date (15)** (Required) - Enter the start date of the campaign.
- **Campaign End Date (16)** (Optional) - Enter the end date of the campaign. If the campaign is ongoing, leave this field blank.
- **Your Availability (17)** (Required) - Select all the days and times you are available to work on the campaign.
- **Skills and Expertise (18)** (Required) - Select all the skills and expertise you have that can be beneficial to the campaign.
- **Previous Campaign Experience (19)** (Required) - Select whether you have previous campaign experience.
- **Describe Your Experience (20)** (Optional) - If applicable, describe your previous campaign experience.
- **Training Needed (21)** (Required) - Select the training or support you need to effectively participate in the campaign.
- **Preferred Communication Method (22)** (Required) - Select your preferred method of communication.
- **Tracking ID (23)** (Optional) - Enter an auto-generated or manual tracking ID.
- **Registration Status (24)** (Required) - Confirm your registration status.
- **Additional Comments (25)** (Optional) - Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to provide.
