# students_cumulative_record_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The students_cumulative_record_form is designed to collect and track students' grades across multiple levels or semesters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the Student Name field.
2. Provide the student's ID number, if applicable.
3. Record each student's grades for the relevant levels or semesters, from 1 to 10.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the student's name.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required: false): Enter the student's ID number, if applicable.
* **Grade 1** (`student_grade1`, number, required: false): Record the student's grade for level 1.
* **Grade 2** (`student_grade2`, number, required: false): Record the student's grade for level 2.
* **Grade 3** (`student_grade3`, number, required: false): Record the student's grade for level 3.
* **Grade 4** (`student_grade4`, number, required: false): Record the student's grade for level 4.
* **Grade 5** (`student_grade5`, number, required: false): Record the student's grade for level 5.
* **Grade 6** (`student_grade6`, number, required: false): Record the student's grade for level 6.
* **Grade 7** (`student_grade7`, number, required: false): Record the student's grade for level 7.
* **Grade 8** (`student_grade8`, number, required: false): Record the student's grade for level 8.
* **Grade 9** (`student_grade9`, number, required: false): Record the student's grade for level 9.
* **Grade 10** (`student_grade10`, number, required: false): Record the student's grade for level 10.

## Tips
* Only record grades that are not already being tracked through other means.
* Double-check the student's name and ID to ensure accuracy.
* Use this form to supplement or correct records, rather than create new ones.
