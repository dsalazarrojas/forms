# Employee Data Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Data Record Form is used to store and update employee information for internal tracking and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's **first name** in the `employee_first_name` field.
2. Enter the employee's **last name** in the `employee_last_name` field.
3. Select the employee's **job title** from the drop-down menu.
4. Choose one or more **job roles** that the employee holds.
5. Enter the employee's **department**.
6. Enter the employee's **date of birth** in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
7. Enter the employee's **manager's name**.
8. Enter the employee's **manager's ID**.
9. Choose the employee's **employee status** from the drop-down menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`employee_first_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's first name. Please keep it concise and accurate.
* **Last Name** (`employee_last_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's last name. Please keep it concise and accurate.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_one, optional): Select the employee's job title. Choose from the available options to describe the employee's role and responsibilities.
* **Job Role** (`job_role`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more job roles that the employee holds. Select from the available options to describe the employee's responsibilities and duties.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the employee's department or team. This helps to categorize and filter employees for internal purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the employee's date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`. This helps to track employee demographics and age-related benefits.
* **Manager's Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee's manager. This helps to track reporting relationships and communication channels.
* **Manager's ID** (`manager_id`, number, optional): Enter the employee's manager's ID. This helps to track reporting relationships and access control.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the employee's current status. Select from the available options to describe the employee's employment status, such as Active, Inactive, or Resigned.
