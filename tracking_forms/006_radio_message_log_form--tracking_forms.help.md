# Radio Message Log Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Radio Message Log Form is used to record and track two-way communications between dispatch teams and radio operators. This form helps to keep a record of received and sent messages, allowing for efficient communication and response management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant options for each field as required.
2. Fill in the details for each field as prompted.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Two-way Communications**
**Select Multiple**: Choose all that apply.
* This field allows you to select multiple response options.

### 2. **Dispatch Team**
* Please enter the name of the dispatch team involved in the two-way communication.

### 3. **Radio Operator**
* Please enter the name or call sign of the radio operator.

### 4. **Date Received**
* Enter the date of when the radio message was received.

### 5. **Time Received**
* Enter the time of when the radio message was received.

### 6. **Message Received**
* Please enter the content of the received message.

### 7. **Response Given**
* Please enter the response or action taken.

### 8. **Response Time**
* Enter the time it took to respond to the radio message.

### 9. **Message Given**
* Please enter the content of the message sent in response.

### 10. **Radio Operator Name**
* Please enter the name or call sign of the radio operator who sent the message.

### 11. **Radio Message Type**
* Choose the type of radio message:
	+ **Voice**: Voice messages.
	+ **Text**: Text messages.
	+ **Emergency**: Emergency messages.
	+ **Other**: Other types of messages.

### 12. **Location**
* Choose the location where the radio message was received:
	+ **Dispatch Center**: Dispatch center.
	+ **Mobile**: Mobile or in-field.
	+ **Remote Site**: Remote site.
	+ **Other**: Other locations.

### 13. **Priority**
* Choose the priority level of the radio message:
	+ **Low**: Low priority message.
	+ **Medium**: Medium priority message.
	+ **High**: High priority message.

Tips:
* Ensure accurate and complete information is entered for each field to maintain accurate records.
* Review and update the form regularly to reflect changes in communication status.
* Use the form to track and analyze communication patterns and trends.
* If you are unsure about any field or process, please contact your supervisor or support team for assistance.
