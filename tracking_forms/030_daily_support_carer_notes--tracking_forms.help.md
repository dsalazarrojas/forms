# Daily Support Carer Notes - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Support Carer Notes form is used to record details of a client's condition and interactions with support care staff. This information helps inform other staff members and ensures that accurate records are kept up to date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the relevant information:
* Make sure to enter the client's details as per their unique ID number.
* Select the correct submit option to indicate whether this report is being submitted.
* Enter the email address of the staff member submitting the report.
* Enter the phone number of the staff member submitting the report.
* Ensure all other fields are completed accurately as relevant to the client's situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details (id: 2)** (`client_details`, `text`, required): This field is used to enter any relevant details about the client, such as their unique ID number.
* **Observation Date (id: 3)** (`observation_date`, `date`, required): This field is used to record the date of the client's observation.
* **Observation Time (id: 4)** (`observation_time`, `time`, optional): This field is used to record the time of the client's observation.
* **Support Carer Name (id: 5)** (`support_carer_name`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the support care staff member who made the observation.
* **Support Carer ID (id: 6)** (`support_carer_id`, `number`, required): This field is used to enter the unique ID number of the support care staff member who made the observation.
* **Observation Location (id: 7)** (`observation_location`, `text`, optional): This field is used to record the location of the client's observation.
* **Observations (id: 8)** (`observations`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter any observations or notes about the client's condition.
* **Notes (id: 9)** (`notes`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter any additional notes or comments about the client's situation.
* **Additional Notes (id: 10)** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter any further additional notes or comments about the client's situation.
* **Submit to (id: 11)** (`submit_to`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' to submit the report, or 'No' if it's not ready for submission.
* **Submit to Email (id: 12)** (`submit_to_email`, `email`, required): This field is used to enter the email address of the staff member submitting the report.
* **Submit to Phone (id: 13)** (`submit_to_phone`, `text`, required): This field is used to enter the phone number of the staff member submitting the report.
