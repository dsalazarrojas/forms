# Display Scheme Compliance Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Display Scheme Compliance Tracker form is a tool to track and monitor the compliance status of a display scheme, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and recorded for review and follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Site Information section with the relevant details about the site.
2. Select the current compliance status from the dropdown list in the Compliance Status field.
3. Add any additional notes or comments in the Notes field.
4. Enter the names and roles of the operational and audit teams involved in the scheme.
5. If applicable, provide evidence for review in the Evidence field.
6. Schedule follow-up actions and dates for the scheme, including the Follow-up Date and Follow-up Time fields.
7. Confirm whether the scheme has been completed or not by selecting Yes or No in the Completed field.
8. Update the Review Status field to reflect the current review status of the scheme.
9. Enter the dates and review notes for completed schemes in the Review Date, Review Time, Review Notes, and Completed Date fields.
10. Add any relevant follow-up notes and details in the Follow-up field.
11. Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the follow-up actions in the Follow-up Person, Follow-up Email, and Follow-up Phone fields.
12. Select the current Facility Status from the dropdown list in the Facility Status field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Information** (`id: 1`): Enter the relevant details about the site, such as the location, address, and contact information.
* **Compliance Status** (`id: 2`): Select the current compliance status of the scheme, whether Active or Inactive.
* **Notes** (`id: 3`): Add any additional comments or notes about the scheme.
* **Facility** (`id: 4`): Enter the name of the facility related to the scheme.
* **Operational Team** (`id: 5`): Enter the names and roles of the operational team members involved in the scheme.
* **Audit Team** (`id: 6`): Enter the names and roles of the audit team members involved in the scheme.
* **Evidence** (`id: 7`): If applicable, provide evidence for review, such as images, documents, or other relevant files.
* **Follow-up** (`id: 8`): Add any additional notes or details about the follow-up actions and timelines.
* **Facility Notes** (`id: 9`): Enter any notes or comments related to the facility.
* **Operational Notes** (`id: 10`): Enter any notes or comments related to the operational aspects of the scheme.
* **Audit Notes** (`id: 11`): Enter any notes or comments related to the audit aspects of the scheme.
* **Next Steps** (`id: 12`): Enter any upcoming actions or steps related to the scheme.
* **Follow-up Date** (`id: 13`): Schedule the follow-up date for the scheme.
* **Follow-up Time** (`id: 14`): Schedule the follow-up time for the scheme.
* **Completed** (`id: 15`): Confirm whether the scheme has been completed or not.
* **Review Status** (`id: 16`): Update the review status of the scheme.
* **Completed Date** (`id: 17`): Enter the date when the scheme was completed.
* **Review Date** (`id: 18`): Enter the date of the review.
* **Review Time** (`id: 19`): Enter the review time for the scheme.
* **Review Notes** (`id: 20`): Enter any review notes about the scheme.
* **Reviewer** (`id: 21`): Enter the name and role of the reviewer.
* **Follow-up Person** (`id: 22`): Enter the name and role of the person responsible for the follow-up actions.
* **Follow-up Email** (`id: 23`): Enter the email address of the follow-up person.
* **Follow-up Phone** (`id: 24`): Enter the phone number of the follow-up person.
* **Facility Status** (`id: 25`): Select the current status of the facility related to the scheme.

## Tips
* Make sure to regularly review and update the compliance status of the scheme.
* Ensure that all necessary information is collected and recorded for review and follow-up actions.
* Use the notes fields to capture any additional comments or details about the scheme.
* Use the evidence field to store relevant files and data for review.
* Use the follow-up fields to schedule and track follow-up actions and timelines.
