# Health Insurance Claim Payment Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Insurance Claim Payment Tracker form is designed to provide an overview of health insurance claims payments, including claimant and patient information, payment status, and reimbursement details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the claim, including the claim ID.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Submit the form to track and update claim payments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the claimant associated with this claim.
* **Claimant Email** (`claimant_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the claimant associated with this claim.
* **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the claimant associated with this claim.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the patient associated with this claim.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, text, optional): Enter the email address of the patient associated with this claim.
* **Claim ID** (`claim_id`, text, optional): Enter the unique ID of the claim.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the claim:
	+ Processing
	+ Paid
	+ Denied
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current payment status:
	+ Not Started
	+ In Progress
	+ Completed
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, text, optional): Select the type of payment.
* **Document Update Status** (`document_update_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of document updates:
	+ Not Required
	+ Required
	+ Completed
* **Reimbursement Fund** (`reimbursement_fund`, text, optional): Enter the reimbursement fund associated with this claim.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the payment.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are accurately filled out to prevent errors in payment tracking.
* Use the Claim ID to track payments for a single claim.
* Update fields as necessary to reflect changing claim and payment status.
* Use the Document Update Status to track document updates and required updates for accurate payment processing.
* Review payment amount for accuracy.
* Submit the form for accurate and up-to-date payment tracking.
