# Field Leaderboard Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document field information for various stages of cultivation.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:
* Fill out each field carefully and accurately, ensuring that your responses are true and up-to-date.
* Ensure that you have permission to access the field data before submitting this form.
* Only submit this form once for each field, to avoid duplication of effort.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farm Location** (`farm_location`, Text, Optional): Enter the location of the farm, for reference and tracking purposes.
* **Farm Name** (`farm_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the farm, for identification and tracking purposes.
* **Field ID** (`field_id`, Number, Optional): Enter a unique identifier for the field, for internal tracking and analysis.
* **Crop Type** (`crop`, Text, Optional): Enter the type of crop being cultivated in the field, for tracking and analysis.
* **Season** (`season`, Text, Optional): Enter the season in which the field is being cultivated, for tracking and analysis.
* **Weather** (`weather`, Text, Optional): Enter any notes about the weather conditions during cultivation, for reference and tracking.
* **Temperature** (`temperature`, Text, Optional): Enter any notes about the temperature during cultivation, for reference and tracking.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, Select One, Optional): Choose the type of equipment used in the field, for tracking and analysis.
*   - Tractor
    - Plow
    - Harvester
* **Equipment Status** (`equipment_status`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the status of the equipment used in the field, for tracking and analysis.
    - Good
    - Bad
    - Unknown
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date when the cultivation started, for tracking and analysis.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date when the cultivation ended, for tracking and analysis.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time when the cultivation started, for tracking and analysis.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time when the cultivation ended, for tracking and analysis.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes about the cultivation, for reference and tracking.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address, for contact and follow-up purposes. 

Note: This form is for internal use only. Please ensure that you have permission to access and submit this form. 
Please ensure that the submitted data is accurate and up-to-date. 
Please do not share sensitive data without consent. 
Please do not submit duplicate entries for the same field.
