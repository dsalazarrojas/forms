# Win Loss Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to track the outcome of sales opportunities. It allows users to select the outcome of a deal (Won, Lost, or Pending), specify the deal size, product/service name, account name, and provide a brief description of the deal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the outcome of the deal by choosing from the options "Won", "Lost", or "Pending" in the "Outcome" field.
2. If applicable, enter the deal size in the "Deal Size" field.
3. Enter the name of the product or service related to the deal in the "Product/Service Name" field.
4. Enter the name of the account associated with the deal in the "Account Name" field.
5. Provide a brief description of the deal in the "Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Outcome (Outcome)** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the outcome of the deal from the options "Won", "Lost", or "Pending". This field determines the status of the deal.
* **Deal Size (Deal Size)** (`number`, required: false): Enter the size of the deal, if applicable. This field is optional and allows users to provide a value if the deal size is relevant to the sale.
* **Product/Service Name (Product/Service Name)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the product or service related to the deal. This field is optional and allows users to provide the name of the product or service sold.
* **Account Name (Account Name)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the account associated with the deal. This field is optional and allows users to identify the account related to the sale.
* **Description (Description)** (`text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the deal. This field is optional and allows users to add context to the sale.
