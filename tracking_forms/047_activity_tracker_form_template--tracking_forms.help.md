# Activity Tracker Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Activity Tracker Form Template is a tool designed to track the progress and details of various activities. This form is meant to be filled out for each activity, ensuring that you're keeping a record of your progress and challenges faced.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the activity in the **Activity Name** field.
2. Select the category of the activity from the **Activity Category** dropdown menu.
3. Choose the start and end dates of the activity.
4. Enter the duration of the activity in minutes.
5. Select how often this activity occurs from the **Frequency** dropdown menu.
6. Rate the intensity of the activity from the **Activity Intensity** dropdown menu.
7. Describe the progress made during the activity in the **Progress Made** field.
8. Describe any challenges faced during the activity in the **Challenges Faced** field (optional).
9. List any goals achieved during the activity in the **Goals Achieved** field (optional).
10. Outline plans for the next session in the **Next Session Plans** field (optional).
11. Finally, rate your overall satisfaction with the activity from the **Overall Satisfaction** dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tracker Owner**: Enter the name of the person responsible for this activity. This field is required for accurate tracking and reporting.
* **Activity Name**: Type the name of the activity being tracked.
* **Activity Category**: Select the type of activity being tracked from the dropdown menu:
	+ Exercise
	+ Work Task
	+ Study Session
	+ Project
	+ Meeting
	+ Personal Goal
	+ Other
* **Start Date**: Enter the date when this activity began.
* **End Date**: Enter the date when this activity will end or has ended.
* **Duration**: Type the total duration of the activity in minutes.
* **Frequency**: Select how often this activity occurs:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Twice Weekly
	+ Three Times Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ As Needed
* **Activity Intensity**: Rate the intensity of the activity from the dropdown menu:
	+ Low
	+ Moderate
	+ High
	+ Very High
* **Progress Made**: Describe the progress achieved during this activity.
* **Challenges Faced**: (Optional) Describe any challenges faced during the activity.
* **Goals Achieved**: (Optional) List any goals achieved during the activity.
* **Next Session Plans**: (Optional) Outline plans for the next session.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the activity from the dropdown menu:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied

## Tips
* Please fill out this form for each activity separately to track progress accurately.
* Ensure that you're providing clear and concise information for each field.
* Review and update this form regularly to reflect changes in your activities and progress.
