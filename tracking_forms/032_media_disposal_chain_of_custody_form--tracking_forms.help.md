# Media Disposal Chain Of Custody Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to track the media disposal process, ensuring that media items are properly handled, transported, and stored, and that all necessary information about the process is documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a brief description of the form and the category it falls under.
2. Enter the media item ID, handler's name, and the date the media item was transferred.
3. Select the method of transfer (Handover, Shipment, Pickup, or Hand Delivery).
4. Enter the signature of the handler or personnel responsible for the transfer.
5. Enter the disposal date of the media item.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Media Disposal Chain Of Custody Form** (`form_title`, text, optional): A brief description of the form and its purpose.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): The category under which this form falls, such as Tracking Forms or IT Forms.
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): A brief description of the form.
* **Assigned Tool** (`form_assigned_tool`, text, optional): The tool assigned to handle the media item.
* **Media Item ID** (`media_item_id`, text, optional): The unique identifier of the media item being disposed.
* **Handler Name** (`handler_name`, text, optional): The name of the handler or personnel responsible for the transfer.
* **Date Transferred** (`date_transferred`, date, optional): The date the media item was transferred.
* **Transferred To** (`transferred_to`, text, optional): The person, department, or organization to which the media item was transferred.
* **Method Transferred** (`method_transferred`, select_one, optional): The method used to transfer the media item, such as Handover, Shipment, Pickup, or Hand Delivery.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): The signature of the handler or personnel responsible for the transfer.
* **Disposal Date** (`disposal_date`, date, optional): The date the media item was disposed of.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields correctly to ensure accurate tracking of media disposal process.
- Use the provided options for the category, method transferred, and method of disposal fields to select the most suitable option.
- Enter dates in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy) for the date transferred and disposal date fields.
- Use a clear and legible signature for the signature field.
