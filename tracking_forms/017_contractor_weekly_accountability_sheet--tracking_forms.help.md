# Contractor Weekly Accountability Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contractor Weekly Accountability Sheet is a form used to track a contractor's work activities, hours, and progress towards goals on a weekly basis. It serves as a means of communication between the contractor and their supervisor, providing an overview of their work and any challenges or issues faced during the week.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **Contractor Full Name** in the field labeled "Contractor Full Name". This is the legal name of the contractor.
2. Enter your **Contractor ID Number** in the field labeled "Contractor ID Number". This is the identification number assigned to you.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the field labeled "Email Address". This is your primary email address used for communication.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the field labeled "Phone Number". This is your contact number.
5. For the **Week Starting Date**, select the Monday of the reporting week.
6. For the **Week Ending Date**, select the Friday or last day of the reporting week.
7. Enter the **Primary Project Name** in the field labeled "Primary Project Name". This is the main project assignment you are working on.
8. Enter the **Supervisor Name** in the field labeled "Supervisor Name". This is the name of your reporting manager.
9. For each day of the week, enter the tasks completed in the field labeled with the corresponding day (e.g., "Monday Tasks", "Tuesday Tasks", etc.). This is where you log your work activities for each day.
10. Enter the total **Hours Worked** for each day in the field labeled with the corresponding day (e.g., "Monday Hours Worked", "Tuesday Hours Worked", etc.). This is the total hours spent on tasks for each day.
11. In the **Goals Accomplished This Week** field, enter what you completed towards your goals and objectives.
12. In the **Goals for Next Week** field, enter your goals and objectives for the upcoming week.
13. In the **Challenges or Obstacles Faced** field, report any issues or challenges you faced during the week.
14. In the **Support or Resources Needed** field, indicate any support or resources you need.
15. In the **Additional Notes** section, enter any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contractor Full Name** (Required, Text): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Contractor ID Number** (Required, Text): Enter the identification number assigned to you.
*   **Email Address** (Required, Email): Enter your primary email address used for communication.
*   **Phone Number** (Required, Text): Enter your contact number.
*   **Week Starting Date** (Required, Date): Select the Monday of the reporting week.
*   **Week Ending Date** (Required, Date): Select the Friday or last day of the reporting week.
*   **Primary Project Name** (Required, Text): Enter the main project assignment you are working on.
*   **Supervisor Name** (Required, Text): Enter the name of your reporting manager.
*   **Monday Tasks** (Required, Text): Enter the tasks completed on Monday.
*   **Monday Hours Worked** (Required, Number): Enter the total hours spent on Monday.
*   **Tuesday Tasks** (Required, Text): Enter the tasks completed on Tuesday.
*   **Tuesday Hours Worked** (Required, Number): Enter the total hours spent on Tuesday.
*   **Wednesday Tasks** (Required, Text): Enter the tasks completed on Wednesday.
*   **Wednesday Hours Worked** (Required, Number): Enter the total hours spent on Wednesday.
*   **Thursday Tasks** (Required, Text): Enter the tasks completed on Thursday.
*   **Thursday Hours Worked** (Required, Number): Enter the total hours spent on Thursday.
*   **Friday Tasks** (Required, Text): Enter the tasks completed on Friday.
*   **Friday Hours Worked** (Required, Number): Enter the total hours spent on Friday.
*   **Saturday Tasks** (Optional, Text): Enter the tasks completed on Saturday if applicable.
*   **Saturday Hours Worked** (Optional, Number): Enter the total hours spent on Saturday if applicable.
*   **Sunday Tasks** (Optional, Text): Enter the tasks completed on Sunday if applicable.
*   **Sunday Hours Worked** (Optional, Number): Enter the total hours spent on Sunday if applicable.
*   **Total Hours This Week** (Required, Number): Enter the sum of all hours worked this week.
*   **Overtime Hours** (Optional, Number): Enter any overtime hours worked.
*   **Goals Accomplished This Week** (Required, Text): Enter what you completed towards your goals and objectives.
*   **Goals for Next Week** (Required, Text): Enter your goals and objectives for the upcoming week.
*   **Challenges or Obstacles Faced** (Optional, Text): Report any issues or challenges you faced during the week.
*   **Support or Resources Needed** (Optional, Text): Indicate any support or resources you need.
*   **Additional Comments** (Optional, Text): Enter any other relevant information.
*   **Submission Date** (Required, Date): Enter the date this form is submitted.
*   **Safety Incidents This Week** (Optional, Text): Report any accidents or near-misses this week.
*   **Equipment Issues or Needs** (Optional, Text): Enter any equipment problems or requests.
*   **Any Safety Incidents This Week** (Optional, Text): Report any accidents or near-misses this week.
*   **Equipment Issues or Needs** (Optional, Text): Enter any equipment problems or requests.
