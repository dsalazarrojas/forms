# Services Directory Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Services Directory Submission Form is used to collect and track information about services submitted to the directory. This guide will walk you through each field and what you need to do to complete it successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: title, contact info, services offered, email, phone, note, date submitted, time submitted, submission status, submission notes, submission date, submission time, and submitted by field.
2. Select the services you offer from the "Services Offered" dropdown menu.
3. Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information in the other fields.
4. Review and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data (1)**: This is the title of your service.
* **Contact Info (2)**: Enter your name, address, and other relevant contact details.
* **Services Offered (3)**: Select all services you offer from the dropdown menu.
* **Email (4)**: Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone (5)**: Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you.
* **Note (6)**: Add any additional notes or details about your service.
* **Date Submitted (7)**: Enter the date you submitted this service.
* **Time Submitted (8)**: Enter the time you submitted this service.
* **Services Directories (9)**: Indicate if this service is submitted to the services directory.
* **Submitted By (10)**: Enter your ID number.
* **Services Categories (11)**: Select the categories that apply to your services.
* **Assigned Tool (12)**: Indicate if a tool is assigned to this submission.
* **Assigned User (13)**: Choose the user to whom the submission is assigned.
* **Submission Status (14)**: Choose the status of this submission.
* **Submission Notes (15)**: Add any additional notes or details about this submission.
* **Submission Date (16)**: Enter the date of the submission.
* **Submission Time (17)**: Enter the time of the submission.
* **Submission ID (18)**: Enter your unique submission ID.

Note: Some fields are only visible or editable to users with necessary permissions, such as submission status and assigned fields.
