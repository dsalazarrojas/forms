# Standardized Recipe Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Standardized Recipe Form is designed to collect information from users about their recipes, including ingredients, instructions, and additional notes. This form helps to standardize the way recipes are collected, making it easier to manage and compare different recipes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out each section**: The form is divided into multiple sections, each with its own field(s) for you to fill out. Complete one section at a time, making sure to provide as much information as possible.
2. **Use the correct field type**: Each field has a specific type (e.g., text, email, phone number). Use the correct type to ensure your input is accurate and easily readable.
3. **Provide as much information as possible**: While some fields are optional, try to provide as much detail as you can in each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ingredients Form** (`ingredients_form`, text, optional): Please enter a list of ingredients used in the recipe.
* **Instructions Form** (`instructions_form`, text, optional): Describe the step-by-step instructions for preparing the recipe.
* **Notes Form** (`notes_form`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the recipe.
* **Additional Notes Form** (`additional_notes_form`, note, optional): Add any extra information that doesn't fit in the previous notes field.
* **Email Form** (`email_form`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address associated with the recipe or its creator.
* **Phone Form** (`phone_form`, text, optional): Enter a phone number for the recipe or its creator.

## Tips

* Be as detailed and thorough as possible when filling out each field.
* Use clear and concise language in your text fields.
* Double-check your email and phone number fields for accuracy.
* If you need to add more information, use the "Additional Notes Form" field.

This help guide should make it easy for users to understand the purpose and usage of the Standardized Recipe Form.
