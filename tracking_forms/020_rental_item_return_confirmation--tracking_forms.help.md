# Rental Item Return Confirmation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to confirm the return of a rental item. It's a crucial step in the rental process, ensuring that the item is properly accounted for and its condition is accurately assessed. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your confirmation in the "Return Confirmation" field (page 1).
2. Provide details about the item being returned in the "Item Details" field (page 2).
3. Enter information about the user who is returning the item in the "User Details" field (page 3).
4. Enter the date and time the item was rented (Rental Date and Time) and the date and time it's being returned (Return Date and Time) (pages 4 and 5).
5. Assess the item's condition by selecting one of the options in the "Item Condition" field (page 6).
6. Add any comments about the return in the "Comments" field (page 7).
7. Indicate whether the item was assigned to anyone for return (Assigned To) and select the relevant option (page 8).
8. Enter the form ID, which is automatically generated and displayed on page 9.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Return Confirmation
*   **Return Confirmation** (`Return Confirmation`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief confirmation of the item return.

### Item Details
*   **Item Details** (`item_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter specific details about the item being returned, such as the item's name, serial number, or any other relevant information.

### User Details
*   **User Details** (`user_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter information about the user who is returning the item, including their name, department, or any other relevant details.

### Rental Date and Time
*   **Rental Date and Time** (`rental_date_time`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date and time the item was originally rented.

### Return Date and Time
*   **Return Date and Time** (`return_date_time`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date and time the item is being returned.

### Item Condition
*   **Item Condition** (`item_condition`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the item's condition by choosing one of the options: "new", "used", "damaged", or "good".

### Comments
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the return.

### Assigned To
*   **Assigned To** (`assigned_tool`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate whether the item was assigned to anyone for return, and select the relevant option.

### Form ID
*   **Form ID** (`form_id`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the automatically generated form ID, which is displayed on the last page.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information about the item and its condition to ensure proper accounting and assessment.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to your rental agreement or contact your rental representative for guidance.
- Double-check the form ID to ensure you're entering the correct value.
