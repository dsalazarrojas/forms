# Material Delivery Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and report on material deliveries. It captures information on the delivery date, time, location, and status of the delivery. This helps in maintaining accurate records and enabling efficient tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the delivery date using the date field.
2. Enter the delivery time using the time field.
3. In the delivery location field, enter the address or a brief description of the location.
4. In the delivery address field, enter the full address of the delivery location.
5. Select the material being delivered from the material itemized dropdown list.
6. Choose the status of the delivery (Delivered, Pending, or Cancelled).
7. In the delivery notes field, enter any additional comments or notes related to the delivery.
8. Enter the contact name of the person or entity receiving the material.
9. Enter the contact phone number of the person or entity receiving the material.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the delivery.
- **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the delivery.
- **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`, text, required): Enter the address or a brief description of the location.
- **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required): Enter the full address of the delivery location.
- **Material Itemized** (`material_itemized`, select_one, optional): Select the material being delivered.
- **Delivery Status** (`delivery_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the delivery.
- **Delivery Notes** (`delivery_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes related to the delivery.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or entity receiving the material.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the person or entity receiving the material.
