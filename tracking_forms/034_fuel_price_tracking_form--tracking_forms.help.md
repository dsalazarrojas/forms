# Fuel Price Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and track data on fuel prices across different regions, countries, and locations. It helps personnel responsible for tracking and managing fuel price data to gather accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Click on the "Start Form" button to begin filling out the form.
* Enter the relevant information in each field as prompted.
* Review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Region** (`region`, text, optional): Enter the region or area where the fuel price data is applicable.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter the country where the fuel price data is applicable.
* **Fuel Type** (`fuel_type`, text, optional): Select the type of fuel being tracked (e.g. gasoline, diesel, etc.).
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the specific location where the fuel price data is applicable (e.g. city, state, etc.).
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): Enter the current price of fuel in the specified location.
* **Update Frequency** (`update_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select how often the fuel price is updated (e.g. Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
* **Fuel Price** (`fuel_price`, number, optional): Enter the current fuel price in the specified location.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to maintain the integrity of the fuel price data.
* If you are submitting data for a new location, please ensure that the location does not already exist in the database.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the form's purpose and use your best judgment to provide the most accurate information possible.
