# Impulse Resistance Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Impulse Resistance Log is a tool designed to help users track and manage impulsive behaviors that may be hindering their progress. By filling out this form, users can identify areas where they are struggling with impulsive behaviors and develop strategies to better resist them.

## How To Complete This Form

This form consists of several sections and fields that require different types of input. Please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first page of the form, which provides space to note any initial thoughts or observations.
2. Select the date of the behavior that you are tracking.
3. Enter the time when the behavior started.
4. Indicate whether or not the behavior was impulsive (yes/no).
5. Describe the impulsive behavior in the text field.
6. Select the resistance technique you used or would like to use to resist the behavior.
7. If you selected a resistance technique in the previous step, provide any additional details in this text field.
8. Enter the time it took to resist the behavior.
9. Plan your next action for managing the behavior (e.g., schedule a follow-up meeting, seek support, etc.).
10. Add any additional notes about your experience or progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (optional): This is an open-ended field where you can note any initial thoughts or observations about your impulsive behavior. 
* **Impulse Date** (`impulse_date_label`): Enter the date when the impulsive behavior occurred.
* **Impulse Start Time** (`impulse_start_time_label`): Enter the time when the impulsive behavior started.
* **Impulsive Behavior** (`impulsive_behavior_label`): Indicate whether the behavior was impulsive (yes/no).
* **Impulsive Behavior Description** (`impulsive_behavior_description_label`): Describe the behavior in detail.
* **Resistance Technique** (`resistance_tech_label`): Select a technique that you used or would like to use to resist the behavior.
* **Resistace Technique Details** (`resistance_tech_other_label`): If you selected a resistance technique, provide additional details about your experience with it.
* **Resistace Time** (`resistance_time_label`): Enter the time it took to resist the behavior.
* **Next Action Plan** (`next_action_plan_label`): Plan your next action for managing the behavior.
* **Notes** (`notes_label`): Add any additional notes about your experience or progress.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when describing your impulsive behavior.
* Choose the most relevant resistance technique from the options provided.
* Be honest and accurate when reporting your progress and experiences.
* This form is not meant to be a substitute for professional help or therapy; if you are struggling with impulsive behaviors, seek support from a mental health professional.
