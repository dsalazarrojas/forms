# Gift Progress Record Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Gift Progress Record Form is used to record and track gifts made to non-profit organizations. It helps you keep a record of donations, including the type of gift, amount, date received, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the Non Profit Organization from the list of organizations you work with.
2. Choose the type of gift being recorded (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
3. Enter the amount of the gift in the Gift Amount field.
4. Enter the date the gift was received.
5. Select the status of the gift (e.g., Active or Inactive).
6. Choose the method used to track the gift (e.g., Mail, Phone, or Email).
7. Add any additional notes about the gift.
8. If available, enter the email address of the donor.
9. If available, enter the phone number of the donor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Non Profit Organization**: Enter the name of the non-profit organization that received the gift.
* **Gift Type**: Select "Yes" to indicate that the gift was received, or "No" to indicate that it was not received.
* **Gift Amount**: Enter the monetary amount of the gift.
* **Date Received**: Enter the date the gift was received (in the standard date format).
* **Gift Value**: Enter any value associated with the gift (e.g., quantity, number).
* **Non Profit Organization ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the non-profit organization (if applicable).
* **Gift Status**: Select the status of the gift (e.g., Active or Inactive).
* **Tracking Method**: Select the method used to track the gift (e.g., Mail, Phone, or Email).
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about the gift.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the donor (if available).
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the donor (if available).
