# Inventory Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and manage the inventory of items in the warehouse. It is a comprehensive form that captures various details about each item, including its category, quantity, description, price, location, serial number, date received, date added, time received, employee ID, employee name, notes, phone number, and email address. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Inventory Management Form, follow these steps:
1. Select the category of the item from the "Category" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the quantity of the item.
3. Enter a brief description of the item.
4. Enter the price of the item.
5. Select the location of the item from the "Location" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the serial number of the item.
7. Enter the date the item was received.
8. Enter the date the item was added to the inventory.
9. Select the time the item was received.
10. Enter the employee ID of the person who received the item.
11. Enter the name of the employee who received the item.
12. Enter any additional notes about the item.
13. Enter the phone number of the person who received the item (if applicable).
14. Enter the email address of the person who received the item (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Item** (text): This field is used to enter the name of the item being managed.
* **Quantity** (number): Enter the number of items in stock.
* **Category** (select_one): Select the category of the item from the dropdown menu.
	+ Choose from: Electronics, Clothing, Furniture.
* **Description** (text): Enter a brief description of the item.
* **Price** (number): Enter the price of the item.
* **Location** (select_multiple): Select the location where the item is stored from the dropdown menu.
	+ Choose from: Warehouse, Shelf 1, Shelf 2.
* **Serial Number** (text): Enter the serial number of the item.
* **Date Received** (date): Enter the date the item was received.
* **Date Added** (date): Enter the date the item was added to the inventory.
* **Time Received** (time): Enter the time the item was received.
* **Employee ID** (number): Enter the ID of the person who received the item.
* **Employee Name** (text): Enter the name of the person who received the item.
* **Notes** (note): Enter any additional notes about the item.
* **Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the person who received the item (if applicable).
* **Email** (email): Enter the email address of the person who received the item (if applicable).
