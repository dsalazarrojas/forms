# epic_departure_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an employee's departure from an epic journey or trip. It is intended to be used for tracking purposes and should be completed by the employee or their representative. The form should only be filled out once per journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
2. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
6. Select the date of your departure using the date picker or calendar icon.
7. Select the time of your departure using the time picker or clock icon.
8. Indicate whether you will be using flight accommodation by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
9. Enter any additional notes about your departure in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **last_name** (text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your journey.
* **phone_number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **date_of_departure** (date, required): Select the date of your departure using the calendar icon.
* **time_of_departure** (time, required): Select the time of your departure using the clock icon.
* **flight_accommodation** (select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you will be using flight accommodation by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your departure here.
