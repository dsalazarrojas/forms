# end_of_work_day_report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The end_of_work_day_report form is used to capture details about an employee's daily tasks, goals, and communication. This information helps management and colleagues track progress, set priorities, and plan for the next day.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Ensure that you provide a clear and concise description of your completed tasks.
3. Enter your goals for the next day.
4. Add any comments that are relevant to the work done.
5. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
6. Enter your phone number for easy communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Completed Tasks** (`completed_tasks`, text, required): Describe all the tasks you completed today.
- **Tomorrow Goals** (`tomorrow_goals`, text, required): Enter your goals for the next day.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or notes about your work.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for easy communication.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when describing your completed tasks.
- Prioritize your goals for the next day based on urgency and importance.
- Use the comments field to capture any important details or reminders.
- Make sure your email and phone number are up-to-date and correct.
- Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure everything is complete and accurate.
