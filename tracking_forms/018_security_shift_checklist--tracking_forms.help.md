# Security Shift Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Security Shift Checklist is a form used to document the details of a security shift, including the security personnel on shift and any incidents that occurred. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the security personnel on shift. You can choose from the list of available options.
2. Provide a brief description of the shift, including any notable events or incidents.
3. Update the shift status (Open, In Progress, or Completed) as required.
4. Add any additional shift notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shift Details** (shift_details, text, not required):
This field is for you to provide a brief description of the shift, including any notable events or incidents. 
* **Security Personnel** (security_personnel, select_multiple, not required): 
List all personnel on shift, including their names and job titles. This will help us to better understand who was present during the shift and their roles.
* **Shift Changeovers** (shift_changeovers, text, not required): 
This field is for you to describe any changes made during the shift, such as shift handovers or updates.
* **Security Incident** (security_incident, text, not required): 
In this field, please describe any security incidents that occurred during the shift, such as reported issues or concerns.
* **Security Shift Notes** (security_shift_notes, text, not required): 
This field is for you to add any additional comments or notes about the shift, such as successes or areas for improvement.
* **Shift Status** (shift_status, select_one, not required): 
Choose the current status of the shift from the options: Open, In Progress, or Completed. 

## Tips
- Double-check that you've selected all the necessary security personnel on shift.
- Be specific and clear when describing incidents or notable events.
- Ensure that you update the shift status accurately, so we can track the shift's progress.
- Take notes on any incidents or areas for improvement to help us refine our procedures. 
- If you're unsure about any field, please consult your supervisor or relevant procedures.
