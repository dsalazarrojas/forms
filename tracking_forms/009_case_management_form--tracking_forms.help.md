# Case Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and organize case management information for law firms, courts, or legal organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by providing the case's title and type.
2.  Enter the case's unique identifier (Case ID Number) and the date it was filed.
3.  Select the current case status from the given options.
4.  Enter the names and contact information of all parties involved, including the plaintiff/complainant, their attorney, and the defendant's attorney.
5.  Specify the assigned judge and case manager.
6.  Set the priority level of the case and provide a brief summary of recent developments.
7.  Summarize the main legal issues and claims or damages sought.
8.  Record any important dates and deadlines, such as the next hearing date, discovery deadline, and trial date.
9.  Finally, include any additional case management notes or observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Case ID Number (2)**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to this case.
* **Case Title (3)**: Briefly describe the case's title.
* **Type of Case (4)**: Select the type of case from the given options (Civil, Criminal, Family Law, etc.).
* **Date Case Was Filed (5)**: Enter the date the case was filed in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Current Case Status (6)**: Select the current status of the case from the given options (Active, On Hold, etc.).
* **Plaintiff or Complainant Name (8)**: Enter the name of the plaintiff or complainant.
* **Plaintiff's Attorney (9)**: Enter the name and contact information of the plaintiff's attorney.
* **Defendant Name (10)**: Enter the name of the defendant.
* **Defendant's Attorney (11)**: Enter the name and contact information of the defendant's attorney.
* **Assigned Judge (12)**: Enter the name of the assigned judge.
* **Case Manager Name (14)**: Enter the name of the case manager.
* **Case Manager Contact Information (15)**: Enter the case manager's contact information.
* **Case Priority Level (16)**: Select the priority level of the case from the given options (High, Medium, Low, etc.).
* **Recent Case Developments (17)**: Provide a brief summary of recent developments in the case.
* **Main Legal Issues (19)**: Summarize the main legal issues in the case.
* **Claims or Damages Sought (20)**: Describe the claims or damages sought in the case.
* **Next Scheduled Hearing Date (22)**: Enter the date of the next scheduled hearing in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Discovery Deadline (23)**: Enter the discovery deadline in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Scheduled Trial Date (24)**: Enter the scheduled trial date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Case Management Notes (25)**: Include any additional case management notes or observations.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the format for date fields (MM/DD/YYYY).
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure accuracy and consistency in case data collection.
