# Warehouse Packing Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Packing Assessment form is a tool used to track the packing of warehouse shipments, gathering information about materials used, observations, compliance level, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling out the "Packing Procedure" field, which is the first page of the form.
2. On the next page, select the "Materials Used" option by choosing from the available options or typing in the specific materials used.
3. In the "Observations" field, provide any relevant comments or notes about the packing process.
4. Choose the "Compliance Level" by selecting from the available options.
5. Enter the date and time the packing was completed in the "Date Packed" and "Time Packed" fields.
6. Fill out the "Packed By" field with the name or ID of the person responsible for packing.
7. If applicable, enter the name of the "Packing Assistant" who helped with the packing.
8. Provide any additional details in the "Notes" field.
9. Add your supervisor's signature and contact information in the "Supervisor Signature", "Email", "Phone", and "Location" fields.
10. Enter the date and time the shipment was received in the "Date Received" and "Time Received" fields.
11. Fill out the "Received By" field with the name or ID of the person who received the shipment.
12. Enter the name of the warehouse in the "Warehouse Name" field.
13. Provide any additional details in the "Packing List" field.
14. Fill out the "Remarks" fields (Remarks 1-6) with any additional comments or notes about the packing process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Packing Procedure**: A brief description of the packing process, including any notable events or issues that occurred.
* **Materials Used**: A list of materials used during the packing process, such as boxes, packing materials, etc.
* **Observations**: Any relevant comments or notes about the packing process.
* **Compliance Level**: A measure of the compliance with established packing standards or procedures.
* **Date Packed**: The date the packing was completed.
* **Time Packed**: The time the packing was completed.
* **Packed By**: The name or ID of the person responsible for packing.
* **Packing Assistant**: The name or ID of the person who assisted with the packing.
* **Notes**: Any additional details or comments about the packing process.
* **Supervisor Signature**: A signature from the supervisor or manager responsible for the packing process.
* **Email**: The email address of the supervisor or manager.
* **Phone**: The phone number of the supervisor or manager.
* **Location**: The location where the packing was completed.
* **Date Received**: The date the shipment was received.
* **Time Received**: The time the shipment was received.
* **Received By**: The name or ID of the person who received the shipment.
* **Warehouse Name**: The name of the warehouse where the packing was completed.
* **Packing List**: Any additional details about the packing list.
* **Remarks 1-6**: Any additional comments or notes about the packing process.
