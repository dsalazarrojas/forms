The purpose of this form is to create a comprehensive checklist for a tea party, allowing users to input essential details such as guest list, food and beverages preferences, decorations, start and end times, venue, dress code, parking information, and more. This form will help the party host or organizer to track and manage all the necessary aspects of the tea party.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the desired options for each field to provide the most accurate information about your tea party.
2. Fill in the required fields (indicated by *Required*) with the necessary details.
3. Ensure that you have entered all the necessary fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tea Party Details** (`tea_party_details`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of your tea party.
* **Guest List** (`guest_list`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose who will be attending your tea party. You can select multiple options.
* **Food and Beverages** (`food_beverages`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose which food and beverages will be served at your tea party.
* **Decorations** (`decorations`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the decorations you plan to have at your tea party.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Note, Optional): Leave any additional comments or notes about your tea party.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the start time of your tea party.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the end time of your tea party.
* **Venue** (`venue`, Text, Optional): Enter the location of your tea party.
* **Dress Code** (`dress_code`, Text, Optional): Enter the dress code for your tea party.
* **Parking** (`parking`, Text, Optional): Enter information about parking arrangements for your tea party.
* **Food Preferences** (`food_preferences`, Select One, Optional): Choose the main course you plan to serve.
* **Drink Preferences** (`drink_preferences`, Select One, Optional): Choose the drink options for your tea party.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, Email, Optional): Enter the email addresses of your guests. This will help you keep track of RSVPs.
* **Host Name** (`host_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your name or the name of the person hosting the tea party.
* **Tea Party Date** (`tea_party_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of your tea party.
* **Tea Party Time** (`tea_party_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of your tea party.
* **Location** (`location`, Text, Optional): Enter the location of your tea party.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the host.
* **Food Budget** (`food_budget`, Number, Optional): Enter your budget for food and beverages.
* **Decoration Budget** (`decorations_budget`, Number, Optional): Enter your budget for decorations.
* **Note** (`note`, Note, Optional): Leave any additional comments or notes about your tea party.
* **Tea Party ID** (`tea_party_id`, Text, Optional): Enter a unique ID for your tea party.
* **Tea Party Status** (`tea_party_status`, Select One, Optional): Select the status of your tea party (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Created At** (`created_at`, Date, Optional): The date this tea party was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, Date, Optional): The date this tea party was last updated.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have selected all the necessary options for each field to get an accurate report.
* Double-check your details before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the host or organizer of the tea party.
