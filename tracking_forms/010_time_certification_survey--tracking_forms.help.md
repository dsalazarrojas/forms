# Time Certification Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Time Certification Survey is a form used by employees to provide accurate information about their work hours and certification status. It is used to track employee work hours, certification status, and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Time Certification Survey, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Name (Field 2) and Start Date (Field 3) to provide your work hours information.
2. Enter your End Date (Field 4) if applicable.
3. Check the Certification (Field 5) box if you have been certified. If not, leave it unchecked.
4. Enter the number of Hours Worked (Field 6) in numerical format.
5. Write your Feedback (Field 7) to provide any comments or feedback about your work hours.
6. Enter any additional Comments (Field 8) if necessary.
7. Finally, confirm your Submission (Field 9) by checking the box if you have completed the form accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is for your name, please enter your name as it appears on your identification or personnel records.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of your work period, if applicable.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of your work period, if applicable.
- **Certification** (`certification`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check this box if you have been certified for your work hours. 
- **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total number of hours you have worked.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Write any comments or feedback about your work hours.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments about your work hours.
- **Submission** (`submission`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm that you have completed the form accurately by checking this box.
- **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm that you have reviewed and submitted the form.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
- This form is designed for tracking work hours and certification status, if you have any questions or concerns, please contact your team leader or HR representative.
