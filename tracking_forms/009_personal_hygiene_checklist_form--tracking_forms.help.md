# personal_hygiene_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "personal_hygiene_checklist_form" is a form used to track and maintain personal hygiene habits of individuals, typically in a household or office setting. This form helps ensure that essential personal hygiene practices are being followed to maintain cleanliness, health, and overall well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all the fields on each page.
* Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* Use the available options for select fields to choose the correct answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Hygiene Checklist** (page 1): This is the main page where you can select all the personal hygiene practices you have completed on a given day.
* **Wash Your Hands** (id: 2): This field asks whether you washed your hands, selecting either "Yes" or "No" from the available options.
* **Brush Teeth** (id: 3): In this field, describe your dental hygiene routine, including any relevant details about toothbrushing, toothpaste, and flossing.
* **Shower** (id: 4): Report your bathing or showering habits, describing any soap or body wash used, shampoo and conditioner, and any other relevant details.
* **Hair Care** (id: 5): Select all the hair care practices you completed, such as washing, conditioning, and styling your hair.
* **Laundry** (id: 6): Describe your laundry routine, including whether you washed, dried, or ironed clothes and any other relevant details.
* **Clean Room** (id: 7): Record your cleaning habits for the living spaces, including any dusting, vacuuming, or other tasks.
* **Exercise** (id: 8): Report any physical activity or exercise you completed, including details about duration and intensity.
* **Sleep** (id: 9): Describe your sleep schedule, including hours slept and any sleep aids or disruptions.
* **Refill Water Bottle** (id: 10): In this field, report whether you refilled your water bottle and describe the water temperature and other relevant details.
* **Drink Water** (id: 11): Record your water intake, including amount and source (e.g., tap, filter, bottled).
* **Personal Item** (id: 12): Report any personal items you washed or cleaned, such as glasses, utensils, or other frequently used items.
* **Trash** (id: 13): Describe your trash habits, including recycling, composting, or disposal methods.
* **Recycle** (id: 14): Record any recycling practices, including materials recycled, such as paper, plastic, glass, or other materials.
* **Food Storage** (id: 15): Report your food storage habits, including storage containers, refrigeration, and any food waste management practices.
