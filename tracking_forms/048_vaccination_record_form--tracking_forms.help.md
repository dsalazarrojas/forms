# Vaccination Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vaccination Record Form is designed to collect and document various aspects of a patient's vaccination experience. This form is intended to be filled out by healthcare professionals or trained staff members to track and record vaccination details, patient information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant fields that apply to the patient's vaccination record.
2. Fill out the form accordingly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Be sure to review and update the form regularly to reflect any changes or new information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Vaccination Details
* **Vaccination Details** (`vaccination_details`, text, not required): This field is intended to capture the specific details of the vaccination, such as the type, date, and dosage administered.
### Patient Information
* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, not required): This field is meant to record the patient's personal details, including their name, age, and contact information.
### Dosage Information
* **Dosage Information** (`dosage_info`, text, not required): This field is used to collect information about the dosage of the vaccination, including any relevant medical history or contraindications.
### Adverse Events
* **Adverse Events** (`adverse_events`, text, not required): This field is used to document any adverse events or reactions that occurred as a result of the vaccination.
### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, not required): This field is intended to capture any relevant medical history or allergies that may be relevant to the vaccination.
### Allergies
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, not required): This field is used to record any allergies that the patient has.
### Medication Administered
* **Medication Administered** (`medication_administered`, text, not required): This field is used to document any medication that was administered in conjunction with the vaccination.
### Vaccine Side Effects
* **Vaccine Side Effects** (`vaccine_side_effects`, text, not required): This field is used to capture any side effects or reactions that occurred as a result of the vaccination.
### Patient Outcome
* **Patient Outcome** (`patient_outcome`, text, not required): This field is used to document the patient's outcome after receiving the vaccination.

## Tips
* Be sure to update the form regularly to reflect any changes or new information.
* Double-check for accuracy and completeness before finalizing the form.
* If you are unsure about any field or information, please consult with a healthcare professional.
