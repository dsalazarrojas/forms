# Time Off Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Time Off Request Form is a form designed to help employees submit their time off requests to HR or management for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your employee information in the `Employee Information` section.
2.  Enter the start and end dates of your time off in the `Time Off Details` section.
3.  Add any additional comments or information you feel is relevant to your time off request in the `Additional Comments` section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information (`employee`, text, required: false)**: Enter your employee ID or name, so that the request can be correctly attributed to you.
* **Time Off Details (`time_off`, text, required: false)**: Enter the start and end dates of your time off, including the date you start and end your absence from work. 
* **Additional Comments (`comments`, text, required: false)**: Use this field to add any additional information that may help HR or management process your time off request, such as your vacation destination or the reason for your absence. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have obtained approval from your supervisor before submitting this form.
* Be as specific as possible when entering your time off dates to help with scheduling and processing. 
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to HR or your supervisor for assistance. 
* If you need to make changes to your submitted form, please contact HR or your supervisor to initiate the process.
