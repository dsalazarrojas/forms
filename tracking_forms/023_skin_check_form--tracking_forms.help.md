# Skin Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Check Form is a tool used to collect and track information related to skin checks in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the provided fields.
2. Be as detailed as possible when providing information about your skin concerns, medical history, and other relevant factors.
3. Ensure that you have permission from your doctor to sign the form and provide your medical history and other sensitive information.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and agreement with the collected information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Skin Concerns** (`skin_check_form`, Text, required): Provide a brief description of your skin concerns or issues.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, Text, required): Share your medical history, including any previous skin conditions, allergies, or medical treatments.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, Text, required): List any current medications you are taking.
* **Skin Condition Risk Factors** (`skin_condition_risk_factors`, Text, required): List any risk factors that may be contributing to your skin condition, such as family history or environmental factors.
* **Skin Condition History** (`skin_condition_history`, Text, required): Describe your skin condition's history, including when it started and any previous treatments.
* **Medical Treatment** (`skin_condition_medical_treatment`, Text, required): Explain any medical treatments you have received for your skin condition.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, Text, required): Share your family medical history, including any relatives with similar skin conditions.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, Text, required): List any allergies you have, including medication allergies.
* **Medical History Family** (`medical_history_family`, Text, required): Provide your family medical history, including any relatives with medical conditions similar to yours.
* **Social History** (`social_history`, Text, required): Share any relevant social history, including lifestyle factors that may be contributing to your skin condition.
* **Skin Check Results** (`skin_check_results`, Text, required): Summarize the results of your skin check, including any findings or conclusions.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, Text, required): Outline any treatment plans discussed or recommended by your doctor.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, Text, required): Describe any next steps or follow-up appointments or actions you need to take.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, Text, required): Schedule any follow-up appointments or actions you need to take.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, Text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and agreement with the collected information.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, Text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and agreement with the collected information.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when providing information to ensure the collected data is reliable and useful for your healthcare provider.
* Ensure you have permission from your doctor to share your medical history and other sensitive information.
* Review the form before signing to ensure you understand and agree with the collected information.
* Ask your healthcare provider if you have any questions or concerns about the form or your skin condition.
