# Bar Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bar Inventory Form is used to track and record the inventory levels of various products within a bar or restaurant setting. This includes the quantities and values of different types of products, as well as information about their state and discrepancies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the date and time of the inventory count.
2. Enter the name of the staff member conducting the inventory.
3. Select the name of the manager responsible for the inventory.
4. Record the name of the bar or restaurant.
5. Enter the quantities and values of different types of products, such as spirits, beer, wine, and soft drinks.
6. Indicate whether any products were damaged or broken and provide a description.
7. Verify that all storage areas were checked.
8. Enter the total value of the inventory.
9. Indicate any discrepancies from the previous count.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inventory Date**: The date of the inventory count. This helps to track changes over time.
* **Inventory Time**: The time of the inventory count. This can help to identify when counts are taken.
* **Staff Member Name**: The name of the person conducting the inventory count.
* **Manager Name**: The name of the person responsible for overseeing the inventory.
* **Establishment Name**: The name of the bar or restaurant.
* **Liquor - Spirits Count**: The total number of bottles of spirits in stock.
* **Spirits Inventory Value**: The total value of the spirits inventory.
* **Beer - Bottles Count**: The total number of bottles of beer in stock.
* **Beer - Cans Count**: The total number of cans of beer in stock (optional).
* **Beer Inventory Value**: The total value of the beer inventory (optional).
* **Wine - Bottles Count**: The total number of bottles of wine in stock.
* **Wine Inventory Value**: The total value of the wine inventory (optional).
* **Soft Drinks - Bottles Count**: The total number of bottles of soft drinks in stock (optional).
* **Soft Drinks Value**: The total value of the soft drinks inventory (optional).
* **Draft Beer - Barrels/Kegs**: The total number of kegs of draft beer in stock (optional).
* **Draft Beer Value**: The total value of the draft beer inventory (optional).
* **Bottled Water Count**: The total number of bottles of bottled water in stock (optional).
* **Premium/Top-Shelf Liquor Count**: The total number of premium/top-shelf liquor bottles in stock (optional).
* **Damaged or Broken Items**: The number of items that are damaged or broken.
* **Description of Damaged Items**: A description of the damaged or broken items.
* **Total Inventory Value**: The grand total of all inventory values.
* **Variance from Expected**: The difference from the expected count.
* **Additional Notes**: Any additional comments or observations about the inventory count.
* **All Storage Areas Checked**: A confirmation that all storage areas were checked.
* **Physical Count Verified**: Confirmation that the count was verified against records.
* **Physical Count Verified**: A confirmation that the count was verified against records.

Note: Fields marked as "optional" can be completed or left blank if not applicable.
