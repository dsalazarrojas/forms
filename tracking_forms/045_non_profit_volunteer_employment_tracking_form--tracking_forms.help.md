# Non Profit Volunteer Employment Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about non-profit volunteers, including their roles, availability, start and end dates of employment, and total hours worked.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the volunteer's role in the "Volunteer Role" field.
2. Select their availability in the "Availability" field. Please format the date in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
3. Enter the start date of their employment in the "Volunteer Start Date" field.
4. Enter the end date of their employment in the "Volunteer End Date" field.
5. If applicable, enter the total hours worked by the volunteer in the "Volunteer Hours" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Role**: Enter a brief description of the volunteer's role, such as "Data Entry Assistant" or "Event Coordinator".
* **Availability**: This field is used to track when the volunteer is available to work. Please enter the date in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Volunteer Start Date**: Enter the date when the volunteer started working for the non-profit.
* **Volunteer End Date**: Enter the date when the volunteer ended their employment with the non-profit.
* **Volunteer Hours**: If applicable, enter the total number of hours worked by the volunteer.
