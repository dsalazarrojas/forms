# New Studio Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential details about a new studio, including its name, location, and prospect status.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter the title of your new studio in the "studio_name" field.
2. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
4. Enter the location of your studio in the "studio_location" field.
5. Select the status of your prospect in the "prospect_status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **studio_name (text, required: false):** Enter the title of your new studio.
* **first_name (text, required: false):** Enter your first name.
* **last_name (text, required: false):** Enter your last name.
* **studio_location (text, required: false):** Enter the location of your studio.
* **prospect_status (select_one, required: false):** Select the status of your prospect from the provided options: active or inactive.
* **(Note that all fields are optional unless otherwise specified.)**

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information about your studio and prospect status.
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
- You can modify this form as needed to suit your specific requirements.
