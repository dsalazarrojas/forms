# Incident Investigation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and document information about incidents that have occurred in a workplace or facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Physical injury, Property damage, Lost time, or Other).
2. Enter the location where the incident occurred.
3. Provide a brief description of the incident.
4. Enter the date and time the incident occurred.
5. Choose the current status of the incident (Open, Closed, or In progress).
6. Enter the name and contact information of the incident reporter.
7. (Optional) Enter any information about escalations or follow-up actions.
8. (Optional) Add any additional comments or notes about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one): Choose the category of the incident (e.g., Physical injury, Property damage, Lost time, or Other).
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text): Provide a brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time): Enter the time the incident occurred. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one): Choose the current status of the incident (Open, Closed, or In progress).
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, text): Enter the name and contact information of the incident reporter.
* **Incident Escalation** (`incident_escalation`, text): Enter any information about escalations or follow-up actions. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Incident Comments** (`incident_comments`, note): Add any additional comments or notes about the incident. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Incident Safety Measures** (`incident_safety_measures`, text): Enter any safety measures taken or recommended. (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Incident Followup** (`incident_followup`, text): Enter any follow-up information about the incident. (Note: This field is optional.)

## Tips
- Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information about the incident.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
- Please be thorough in your responses to ensure that all relevant information is collected and documented.
