# Recruiting Activity Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help track and record recruiting activities, such as dials made and persons interviewed. It's used to gather information on recruiting progress and metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the recruiting activity in the "recruiting_activity_report_form" field.
2. Provide the number of dials made in the "recruiting_dials" field.
3. Enter the number of persons interviewed in the "persons_interviewed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Recruiting Activity Report Form** (`recruiting_activity_report_form`, text, required)
This field is for entering the title of the recruiting activity.

### **Recruiting Dials** (`recruiting_dials`, text, required)
This field is for entering the number of dials made during the recruiting activity.

### **Persons Interviewed** (`persons_interviewed`, text, required)
This field is for entering the number of persons interviewed during the recruiting activity.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields as accurately as possible.
- If you need to add more information, you can always contact the form owner or department in charge of recruiting activities.
