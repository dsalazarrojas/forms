# Outdoor Safety Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outdoor Safety Checklist Form is designed to identify and track hazards and take corrective actions in outdoor settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Identify a hazard and answer "Hazard Found" accordingly.
2. Describe the hazard in the "Hazard Description" field.
3. Check the "Corrective Action Taken" field to indicate if an action was taken.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
5. Complete the "Form Submitted By" field with your name.
6. Enter the date and time you submitted the form in the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" fields.
7. Optionally, you can add a version number of the form in the "Form Version" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hazard Found (5)**: Check this field to indicate if a hazard was found.
* **Hazard Description (6)**: Describe the hazard in detail, including its location and severity.
* **Corrective Action Taken (7)**: Check this field to indicate if a corrective action was taken.
* **Notes (8)**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the hazard.
* **Form Submitted By (11)**: Enter your name to indicate who submitted the form.
* **Submission Date (12)**: Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Submission Time (13)**: Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Form Version (14)**: Optionally, enter the version number of the form.
