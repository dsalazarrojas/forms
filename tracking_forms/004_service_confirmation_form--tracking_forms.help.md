# Service Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Service Confirmation Form is used to track and confirm services completed by employees. This form collects information about the service, including the date and time it was completed, the employee's name, and any relevant notes. The form is essential for maintaining accurate records and ensuring that services are properly accounted for.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the service completion option** (`confirm_service_completed`): Choose the correct option to confirm the service completion from the dropdown menu.
2. **Enter employee name (employee_name)**: Type the employee's name who completed the service.
3. **Enter date of service completion (date_service_completed)**: Type the date the service was completed using the date picker.
4. **Enter time of service completion (time_service_completed)**: Type the time the service was completed using the time picker.
5. **Enter service description (description_service)**: Provide a brief description of the service completed (optional).
6. **Add any notes (notes_service)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the service completion (optional).
7. **Assign a tool (assigned_tool)**: Enter the name of the tool used to complete the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **confirm_service_completed (Select One)**: This field is used to confirm that the service has been completed. Select the correct option from the dropdown menu.
* **employee_name (Text)**: This field is used to enter the name of the employee who completed the service.
* **date_service_completed (Date)**: This field is used to enter the date the service was completed.
* **time_service_completed (Time)**: This field is used to enter the time the service was completed.
* **description_service (Text)**: This field is used to provide a brief description of the service completed (optional).
* **notes_service (Note)**: This field is used to enter any additional notes or comments about the service completion (optional).
* **assigned_tool (Text)**: This field is used to enter the name of the tool used to complete the service.

Tips:

* Make sure to select the correct option to confirm service completion.
* Double-check the accuracy of the entered information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, contact your supervisor or IT department.
