# Book Distribution Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Book Distribution Release Form is a tool used to track and verify the distribution of books to recipients. This form is designed to ensure that both the recipient and the distributor acknowledge receipt and the condition of the books.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, fill in the required fields with the requested information. Start by providing the recipient's details, including their name, email address, and phone number. If applicable, provide the name and type of the organization the recipient is associated with. Then, specify the distribution date and location. Next, list the titles of the books distributed and the quantity of books given out. Confirm the condition of the books and select the category and target age group of the recipients. Indicate the purpose of the distribution and whether the recipient acknowledges receipt. Finally, provide your signature and witness signature if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recipient Full Name** (`recipient_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person receiving the books.
* **Recipient Email** (`recipient_email`, email, required): Enter the contact email address of the recipient.
* **Recipient Phone** (`recipient_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the recipient.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization if the recipient is associated with one.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of organization the recipient is associated with.
* **Distribution Date** (`distribution_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the books were distributed.
* **Distribution Location** (`distribution_location`, text, required): Specify the location where the books were distributed.
* **Distributor Name** (`distributor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person distributing the books.
* **Titles of Books Distributed** (`book_titles`, text, required): List all the book titles distributed.
* **Total Quantity of Books** (`total_quantity`, number, required): Enter the total number of books given out.
* **Condition of Books** (`book_condition`, select_one, required): Confirm the condition of the books (e.g., New, Like New, Good, etc.).
* **Book Categories** (`book_categories`, select_multiple, optional): Select the categories of the books distributed.
* **Target Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Select the age group the books are intended for.
* **Purpose of Distribution** (`purpose_of_distribution`, select_one, required): Specify why the books are being distributed (e.g., educational program, community outreach, etc.).
* **I Acknowledge Receipt of Books** (`recipient_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes I confirm receipt' to confirm receipt of the books.
* **Books Received in Good Condition** (`books_in_good_condition`, select_one, required): Confirm if all or most books are in good condition or if there's any damage.
* **Notes on Any Damage** (`damage_notes`, text, optional): Describe any damage found on the books.
* **Intended Use of Books** (`intended_use`, text, optional): Specify how the books will be used.
* **Is Follow Up Required** (`follow_up_required`, select_one, optional): Select if follow up is required.
* **Recipient Signature** (`recipient_signature`, text, required): Sign with your full name as the recipient.
* **Distributor Signature** (`distributor_signature`, text, required): Sign with your full name as the distributor.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the witness if applicable.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, text, optional): Sign with the witness's full name if applicable.
