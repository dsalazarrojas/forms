# My Writing Log: Track Your Writing - Help Guide
## Purpose
The My Writing Log: Track Your Writing form is designed to help you monitor and track your writing sessions, providing a convenient and organized way to record and reflect on your progress, thoughts, and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To get the most out of this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of your writing session.
2. Record the start and end times of your session.
3. Note the location where you wrote.
4. Provide any additional notes or observations about your session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date of your writing session, following the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Start Time** (`start_time`, required: false): Record the start time of your session, using the format HH:MM.
- **End Time** (`end_time`, required: false): Enter the end time of your session, also in the format HH:MM.
- **Location** (`location`, required: false): Write down the location where you conducted your writing session. This can be your home, office, library, or any other place.
- **Notes** (`notes`, required: false): Take a moment to record any notes, thoughts, or reflections about your writing session. This is your chance to reflect on what worked well, what didn't, and what you'd like to improve on next time.
- **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, required: false): Choose the time of day when your writing session took place. Select one of the options: Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
- **Writing Location** (`writing_location`, required: false): Select all locations where you conducted your writing session. Choose from Home, Office, Library, Beach, or Park.

## Tips
- Be as precise as possible when entering dates and times to ensure accurate tracking.
- Use the provided fields consistently to maintain an organized and comparable log.
- Reflect on your sessions regularly to identify patterns and areas for improvement.
