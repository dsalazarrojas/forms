# Digital Marketing Campaign Planning Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you plan and track digital marketing campaigns. It collects information on your campaign's objectives, target audience, and execution details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select relevant options for each field as needed.
3. Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
