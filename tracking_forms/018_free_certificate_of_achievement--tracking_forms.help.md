# Free Certificate Of Achievement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to generate a free certificate of achievement for students. It gathers information from both the student and the school, including details about the course and the achievement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name as the user.
2. Enter the student's name.
3. Enter the teacher's name.
4. Enter the school name.
5. Enter the course name.
6. Enter the course code.
7. Select the achievement level (Bronze, Silver, or Gold).
8. Enter the date of the course completion or achievement.
9. Enter the time of the achievement.
10. Enter the certificate number.
11. Enter the achievement reason.
12. Enter the achievement details.
13. Add a teacher's signature.
14. Add a student's signature.
15. Add a school's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the user submitting the certificate.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student receiving the certificate.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the teacher issuing the certificate.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school issuing the certificate.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the course for which the student achieved.
* **Course Code** (`course_code`, text, optional): Enter the course code or reference number.
* **Course Date** (`course_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the course completion or achievement.
* **Achievement Date** (`achievement_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the achievement.
* **Achievement Time** (`achievement_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the achievement.
* **Certificate Number** (`certificate_number`, text, optional): Enter a unique certificate number.
* **Achievement Level** (`achievement_level`, select_one, optional): Select the level of achievement (Bronze, Silver, or Gold).
* **Achievement Reason** (`achievement_reason`, text, optional): Enter a brief reason for the achievement.
* **Achievement Details** (`achievement_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the achievement.
* **Teacher Signature** (`teacher_signature`, text, optional): Add a signature from the teacher.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, optional): Add a signature from the student.
* **School Signature** (`school_signature`, text, optional): Add a signature from the school.
* **School Logo** (`school_logo`, note, optional): Add a logo of the school.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, optional): Enter the school's email address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, optional): Enter the school's phone number.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): Enter the student's address.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter the school's address.
* **Course Logo** (`course_logo`, note, optional): Add a logo of the course.
* **Achievement Certificate Number** (`achievement_certificate_number`, text, optional): Enter a unique achievement certificate number.
