# Outdoor Equipment Check List - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help field teams conduct thorough outdoor equipment checks, ensuring the safety and integrity of the equipment and participants in various settings such as construction, manufacturing, or emergency response teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant information section, which includes general details about the participant.
2. Provide a list of equipment that needs to be checked, along with any relevant details.
3. Add any additional notes or comments about the equipment or participant.
4. Enter the participant's email address if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the participant, such as their name, team name, or any other relevant details.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the participant, such as their role, department, or contact information.
* **Equipment List** (`equipment_list`, text, optional): List the equipment that needs to be checked, along with any relevant details such as serial numbers, model numbers, or expiration dates.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the equipment or participant, such as maintenance schedules or issues.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter the participant's email address if required.
