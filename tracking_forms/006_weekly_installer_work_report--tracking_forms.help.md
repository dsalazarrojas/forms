# Weekly Installer Work Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Installer Work Report form is used to collect weekly progress from installers, allowing team leaders to have a consolidated view of the team's overall status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the job sites you have worked on during the week by choosing all applicable options from the "job_sites" field. You can select multiple options by clicking on the checkbox next to each option.
2. Report the total hours you have worked during the week using the "hours_worked" field.
3. Provide any additional comments or notes about your work under the "notes" field.
4. Indicate if you have completed any activities during the week by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "weekly_activities" field.
5. Report any incidents that occurred during the week by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "incidents" field.
6. Select the team you were assigned to from the "assigned_team" field.
7. Check if you worked on a specific job site during the week by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "job_site" field.
8. Report the number of weeks you have completed in the "weeks_completed" field.
9. Indicate if you are working full hours per week by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "hours_per_week" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **job_sites** (Select multiple): This field allows you to report all the job sites you have worked on during the week. Select all applicable options by clicking on the checkbox next to each option.
* **hours_worked** (Number): Enter the total hours you have worked during the week.
* **notes** (Text): Provide any additional comments or notes about your work during the week.
* **weekly_activities** (Select one): Check if you have completed any activities during the week.
* **incidents** (Select multiple): Report any incidents that occurred during the week.
* **assigned_team** (Select one): Select the team you were assigned to.
* **job_site** (Select one): Check if you worked on a specific job site during the week.
* **weeks_completed** (Number): Enter the number of weeks you have completed.
* **hours_per_week** (Select one): Check if you are working full hours per week.
