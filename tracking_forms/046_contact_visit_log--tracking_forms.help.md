# Contact Visit Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Visit Log form is used to track workplace visitors, including their contact information, visit details, and interactions with employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the visitor's visit using the calendar input field.
2. Enter the visitor's arrival and departure times.
3. Fill out the visitor's contact information, including their first and last name, email, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
4. Identify the employee being visited and their department.
5. Select the purpose of the visit.
6. Choose the expected duration of the visit.
7. Provide any additional information about the visit, such as any necessary paperwork or documents.
8. Confirm if an NDA was required and signed.
9. Check if health screening was completed.
10. Enter the name of the receptionist who checked in the visitor.
11. Add any additional notes about the visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Visit Log** (`log_header`): This is the title of the form.
- **Visit Date** (`visit_date`): Enter the date of the visitor's visit using the calendar input field.
- **Time In** (`visit_time_in`): Enter the arrival time of the visitor.
- **Visitor First Name** (`visitor_first_name`): Enter the first name of the visitor.
- **Visitor Last Name** (`visitor_last_name`): Enter the last name of the visitor.
- **Visitor Email** (`visitor_email`): Enter the visitor's email address.
- **Visitor Phone** (`visitor_phone`): Enter the visitor's phone number.
- **Visitor Company** (`visitor_company`): Enter the visitor's company or organization name (optional).
- **Host Name** (`host_name`): Enter the name of the employee being visited.
- **Host Department** (`host_department`): Select the department of the employee being visited.
- **Purpose of Visit** (`visit_purpose`): Select the purpose of the visit.
- **Expected Duration** (`expected_duration`): Select the expected duration of the visit.
- **Time Out** (`time_out`): Enter the departure time of the visitor (optional).
- **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_info`): Enter any vehicle information, such as license plate number (optional).
- **Parking Spot Number** (`parking_spot`): Enter the assigned parking spot number (optional).
- **Badge Issued** (`badge_issued`): If a visitor badge was issued, enter the badge number (optional).
- **NDA Required** (`nda_required`): Confirm if a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) was required.
- **NDA Signed** (`nda_signed`): Confirm if the NDA was signed (if required).
- **Health Screening Passed** (`health_screening`): Confirm if health screening was completed.
- **Receptionist Name** (`receptionist_name`): Enter the name of the receptionist who checked in the visitor.
- **Visit Details** (`visit_details`): Enter any additional information about the visit.
- **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): Add any other notes or comments about the visit.
