# Urine Drug Screening Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Urine Drug Screening Form Template is designed to record the results of a urine drug screening test. The form captures essential information for patient care and medical decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name.
2. Enter the date the test was received.
3. Enter the sample ID or number.
4. Indicate whether the test was performed on a panel (e.g., general, specific, or comprehensive).
5. Select the test result (e.g., positive, negative, inconclusive).
6. Enter any comments about the test result.
7. Enter the date and time the test was performed.
8. Enter the location where the test was performed.
9. Enter any notes about the test result.
10. Sign the patient's signature.
11. Sign the healthcare provider's signature.
12. Enter the date the results were reviewed.
13. Enter the name of the person who reviewed the results.
14. Sign the result reviewer's signature.
15. Enter the name of the healthcare provider who ordered the test.
16. Enter the position/title of the healthcare provider who ordered the test.
17. Sign the healthcare provider's signature.
18. Enter the date the test was ordered.
19. Enter the phone number of the healthcare provider who ordered the test.
20. Enter the email address of the healthcare provider who ordered the test.
21. Enter the laboratory's ID or name.
22. Enter the date the test was shipped to the laboratory.
23. Enter the date the test was received in the laboratory.
24. Enter the laboratory's ID or name (if different from above).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name** (required): Enter the name of the patient.
* **date_received** (required): Enter the date the test was received.
* **sample_id** (required): Enter the sample ID or number.
* **test_panel** (required): Select the type of test panel (e.g., general, specific, or comprehensive).
* **test_result** (required): Select the test result (e.g., positive, negative, or inconclusive).
* **test_result_comments** (required): Enter any comments about the test result.
* **test_date_performed** (required): Enter the date and time the test was performed.
* **test_time_performed** (required): Enter the time the test was performed.
* **test_location** (required): Enter the location where the test was performed.
* **test_result_notes** (required): Enter any additional notes about the test result.
* **patient_signature** (required): Sign the patient's signature.
* **healthcare_provider_signature** (required): Sign the healthcare provider's signature.
* **date_reviewed** (required): Enter the date the results were reviewed.
* **result_reviewed_by** (required): Enter the name of the person who reviewed the results.
* **result_reviewed_by_signature** (required): Sign the result reviewer's signature.
* **test_ordered_by** (required): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who ordered the test.
* **test_ordered_by_position** (required): Enter the position or title of the healthcare provider who ordered the test.
* **test_ordered_by_signature** (required): Sign the healthcare provider's signature.
* **date_ordered** (required): Enter the date the test was ordered.
* **test_ordered_by_phone** (required): Enter the phone number of the healthcare provider who ordered the test.
* **test_ordered_by_email** (required): Enter the email address of the healthcare provider who ordered the test.
* **lab_id** (required): Enter the laboratory's ID or name.
* **test_date_shipped** (required): Enter the date the test was shipped to the laboratory.
* **test_date_received_in_lab** (required): Enter the date the test was received in the laboratory.
* **lab_id_shipped** (required): Enter the laboratory's ID or name (if different from above).
