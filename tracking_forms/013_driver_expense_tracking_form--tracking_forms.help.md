# Driver Expense Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Expense Tracking Form is a tool to track and record expenses incurred by drivers during a trip. It helps to collect and store information about the trip, including the type of expense, location, vehicle details, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of expense you are tracking (e.g., Fuel, Mileage, Tolls).
2. Enter the date of the trip.
3. Enter the time of the trip.
4. Describe the trip in the description field.
5. Provide your driver name, vehicle number, and vehicle type.
6. Enter the location of the trip.
7. Enter the cost of the trip (fuel, mileage, tolls).
8. Select the unit of measurement for the trip distance and cost.
9. Enter any additional notes about the trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Expense Type (Expense Type)**: Select the type of expense you are tracking (e.g., Fuel, Mileage, Tolls).
* **Fuel Cost (Fuel Cost)**: Enter the cost of fuel used during the trip.
* **Mileage (Mileage)**: Enter the total distance traveled during the trip.
* **Tolls Cost (Tolls Cost)**: Enter the cost of tolls paid during the trip.
* **Date Of Trip (Date Of Trip)**: Enter the date of the trip.
* **Time Of Trip (Time Of Trip)**: Enter the time of the trip.
* **Description (Description)**: Describe the trip and provide any additional notes.
* **Driver Name (Driver Name)**: Enter your name as the driver.
* **Vehicle Number (Vehicle Number)**: Enter the vehicle number.
* **Location (Location)**: Enter the location of the trip.
* **Expense Date (Expense Date)**: Enter the date of the expense.
* **Trip Distance (Trip Distance)**: Enter the total distance traveled during the trip.
* **Vehicle Type (Vehicle Type)**: Select the type of vehicle used during the trip.
* **Fuel Type (Fuel Type)**: Select the type of fuel used during the trip.
* **Driver Location (Driver Location)**: Enter your location during the trip.
* **Trip Cost (Trip Cost)**: Enter the total cost of the trip.
* **Vehicle Condition (Vehicle Condition)**: Select the condition of the vehicle used during the trip.
* **Driver Hours (Driver Hours)**: Enter the number of hours spent driving during the trip.
* **Driver Speed (Driver Speed)**: Enter your driving speed during the trip.
* **Trip Distance Unit (Trip Distance Unit)**: Select the unit of measurement for the trip distance.
* **Trip Cost Unit (Trip Cost Unit)**: Select the unit of measurement for the trip cost.
* **Trip Expense (Trip Expense)**: Enter any additional notes about the trip.
* **Vehicle Expense (Vehicle Expense)**: Enter any additional notes about the vehicle used during the trip.
* **Submit (Submit)**: Click to submit the form.

**Tips**

* Make sure to enter accurate and detailed information about the trip.
* Select the correct unit of measurement for distance and cost.
* Enter any additional notes about the trip and vehicle used.
