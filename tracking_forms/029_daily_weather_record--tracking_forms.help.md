# Daily Weather Record - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Weather Record form is used to track and record daily weather information for a given location. This form collects data on various weather parameters such as date, temperature, wind speed, and humidity. The form is likely used for meteorological or scientific research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the date of the weather observation
* Enter the location of the weather observation
* Enter the temperature reading (if available)
* Describe the weather conditions (e.g., sunny, cloudy, rainy)
* Enter the wind speed (if available)
* Enter the humidity level (if available)
* Add any additional notes about the weather observation

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date** (`date_field`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the weather observation.
* **Location** (`location_field`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the weather observation was made.
* **Temperature** (`temperature_field`, number, required/optional): Enter the temperature reading in the appropriate units (e.g., Celsius, Fahrenheit).
* **Weather Conditions** (`weather_conditions_field`, text, required/optional): Describe the weather conditions at the time of observation (e.g., sunny, cloudy, rainy).
* **Wind** (`wind_speed_field`, number, required/optional): Enter the wind speed reading in the appropriate units (e.g., meters per second, miles per hour).
* **Humidity** (`humidity_field`, number, required/optional): Enter the humidity level reading as a percentage.
* **Notes** (`notes_field`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the weather observation.

## Tips
* Ensure that each entry is unique and represents a new day's data to avoid duplication.
* If a field is not relevant to the weather observation, you can leave it blank.
* Use the appropriate units for temperature, wind speed, and humidity readings.
