# Sober Progress Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track progress in recovery, monitoring changes in behavior, mood, and other relevant factors on a regular basis.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Start Date" field to enter the current date, marking the beginning of the tracking period.
2. Enter the number of "Days Sober" to track the length of time sober.
3. Select the "Mood" field to document your current emotional state.
4. Enter the "Challenge" field to describe any obstacles or difficulties encountered.
5. Enter the "Goal" field to set and document your short-term goals.
6. Select the "Support System" field to indicate your current support system, such as a support group or sponsor.
7. Select "Relapse" to indicate if you have relapsed, with options for "True" or "False".
8. Enter the "Next Appointment" field to schedule the next appointment with your treatment provider.
9. Select the "Next Appointment Time" field to schedule the next appointment time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sober Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you started tracking your progress.
* **Days Sober** (`days_sober`, number, required: false): Enter the number of days sober.
* **Mood** (`mood`, text, required: false): Describe your current emotional state.
* **Challenge** (`challenge`, text, required: false): Describe any obstacles or difficulties encountered.
* **Goal** (`goal`, text, required: false): Set and document your short-term goals.
* **Support System** (`support_system`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your current support system, such as "Support Groups", "Sponsor", "Therapist".
* **Relapse** (`relapse`, select_one, required: false): Indicate if you have relapsed, with options for "True" or "False".
* **Next Appointment** (`next_appointment`, date, required: false): Schedule the next appointment with your treatment provider.
* **Next Appointment Time** (`next_appointment_time`, time, required: false): Schedule the next appointment time.
