# Engineering Shift Log Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Engineering Shift Log Form Template is used for tracking the progress and activities of engineering shifts. This form is used to record the tasks completed, progress, and any issues encountered during a shift. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that the team's performance and work are properly documented and reported.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant **Category** for your shift (e.g., Category 1, Category 2, Category 3).
2. Provide a detailed **Description** of the activities and tasks completed during your shift.
3. Answer the **Next Shift** question, selecting the shift you are scheduled for or the next available shift.
4. Check the **Daily Tasks** box if you completed any daily tasks.
5. Select **TroubleShoot Issues** if you encountered any issues during your shift.
6. Check the **Communication** box if you engaged in any communication with colleagues or customers.
7. Answer the **Progress Monitor** question, entering the progress you made during your shift.
8. If applicable, provide additional information in the **Description 2** field.
9. Answer the **Daily Tasks 2** question, indicating if you completed any daily tasks on the second shift.
10. Select **TroubleShoot Issues 2** if you encountered any issues on the second shift.
11. Check the **Communication 2** box if you engaged in any communication on the second shift.
12. Enter your progress in the **Progress Monitor 2** question.
13. Answer the **Next Shift** question for the second shift, selecting the next available shift.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Category** (Select One)
* The Category field helps to classify and organize the shift logs based on specific categories.
* This field is required.

### **Description** (Text)
* This field is for providing a detailed description of the activities and tasks completed during the shift.
* This field is required.

### **Daily Tasks** (Select Multiple)
* This field is used to indicate if you completed any daily tasks during the shift.
* Answer with "Yes" or "No".

### **TroubleShoot Issues** (Select Multiple)
* This field is used to select if you encountered any issues during your shift.
* Answer with "Yes" or "No".

### **Communication** (Select Multiple)
* This field is used to indicate if you engaged in any communication with colleagues or customers during the shift.
* Answer with "Yes" or "No".

### **Progress Monitor** (Number)
* This field is used to enter the progress made during the shift.
* Answer with a numerical value.

### **Next Shift** (Select One)
* This field is used to select the next shift you are scheduled for or the next available shift.
* This field is required.

### **Description 2** (Text)
* This field is for providing a detailed description of the activities and tasks completed on the second shift.
* This field is optional.

### **Daily Tasks 2** (Select Multiple)
* This field is used to indicate if you completed any daily tasks on the second shift.
* Answer with "Yes" or "No".

### **TroubleShoot Issues 2** (Select Multiple)
* This field is used to select if you encountered any issues on the second shift.
* Answer with "Yes" or "No".

### **Communication 2** (Select Multiple)
* This field is used to indicate if you engaged in any communication on the second shift.
* Answer with "Yes" or "No".

### **Progress Monitor 2** (Number)
* This field is used to enter the progress made on the second shift.
* Answer with a numerical value.

## Tips
* Ensure that you accurately and thoroughly complete all fields to ensure proper documentation and reporting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your team lead or supervisor.
