# Rental Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Payment Form is designed to record and track rental payments made by tenants to their landlords. It is used to gather information about payment methods, dates, and other essential details for accurate and efficient tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Fill in the required information for the landlord, tenant, and payment details.
* Select a payment method from the available options (Bank transfer, Check, Cash, Credit card, or Other).
* Choose the payment date and frequency (Weekly, Biweekly, or Monthly).
* Specify the payment status (Paid, Pending, or Late).
* Enter the payment amount, if applicable.
* Add any optional notes for the landlord or tenant to communicate further details.
* Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Landlord info** (Landlord info, `text`, required: false): Enter the landlord's name and contact information.
* **Tenant info** (Tenant info, `text`, required: false): Enter the tenant's name and contact information.
* **Payment info** (Payment info, `text`, required: false): Enter the payment details, including the amount paid.
* **Payment method** (Payment method, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more payment methods (Bank transfer, Check, Cash, Credit card, or Other).
* **Payment date** (Payment date, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Rent frequency** (Rent frequency, `select_one`, required: false): Select the frequency of the payment (Weekly, Biweekly, or Monthly).
* **Payment status** (Payment status, `select_one`, required: false): Specify the status of the payment (Paid, Pending, or Late).
* **Payment amount** (Payment amount, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount paid.
* **Notes** (Notes, `note`, required: false): Add any optional notes for the landlord or tenant to communicate further details.
* **Submit** (Submit, `text`, required: false): Click to complete and submit the form.
