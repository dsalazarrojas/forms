# x_ray_testing_log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track X-Ray testing logs, allowing users to record their testing activities, equipment usage, and results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the "X Ray Testing" page.
2. Enter any relevant information about the X-Ray testing process in the "X Ray Testing" field.
3. Move on to the "Equipment Usage" page.
4. Enter any relevant information about the equipment used during the testing process in the "Equipment Usage" field.
5. Proceed to the "Results and Notes" page.
6. Select the relevant options for the test results and enter any additional notes or observations in the "Results and Notes" field.
7. On the "Image Store" page, enter any relevant information or images related to the testing process in the "Image Store" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **X Ray Testing** (`X Ray Testing`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the X-Ray testing process.
* **Equipment Usage** (`Equipment Usage`, `text`, optional): Describe the equipment used during the testing process.
* **Results and Notes** (`Results and Notes`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select from the options provided to indicate the test results and add any additional notes or observations.
* **Image Store** (`Image Store`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant information or images related to the testing process.
