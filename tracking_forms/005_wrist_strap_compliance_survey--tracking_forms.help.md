# Wrist Strap Compliance Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wrist Strap Compliance Survey is used to record employee wrist strap testing information, including details about the wrist strap used, testing area, testing method, and supervisor reviews. This form helps ensure that employees are wearing the correct wrist strap during testing, and provides a record of each employee's testing history and progress.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Enter Employee Information

* Enter your employee name: Enter your name as it appears on your official ID or personnel record.
* Enter supervisor name: Enter the name of your supervisor.

### Step 2: Record Testing Information

* Date of Testing: Enter the date when you completed the testing procedure.
* Wrist Strap Used: Select the wrist strap you used during testing (A, B, or C).
* Testing Area: Select the area where you completed the testing (1, 2, or 3). You can also enter "Other" if the testing area is not listed.
* Testing Method: Select the method used for testing (1, 2, or 3). You can also enter "Other" if the testing method is not listed.
* Testing Results: Select the results of your testing (1, 2, or 3). You can also enter "Other" if the testing results are not listed.

### Step 3: Review and Sign

* Supervisor Review: Enter your supervisor's comments about your performance during the testing procedure.
* Testing Completed: Enter the date when you completed the testing procedure.
* Employee Signature: Sign and date the form to confirm that you completed the testing procedure.
* Supervisor Signature: Sign and date the form to confirm that you were supervised during the testing procedure.
