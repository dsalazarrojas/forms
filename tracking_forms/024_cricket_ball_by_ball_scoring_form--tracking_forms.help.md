# cricket_ball_by_ball_scoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for scoring and tracking individual cricket balls and team performance. It helps record the details of each ball, including type, score, and various player roles. This form is likely used for data analysis, team comparison, and historical data storage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by entering the team A and team B names in the respective fields.
2. Select the type of ball ('cricket', 'tennis', 'rugby', etc.) and confirm if a batshan ('yes'/'no') and fielder ('yes'/'no') were involved in the ball.
3. Enter the score for the batsman and fielder.
4. Select if the ball resulted in an over ('yes'/'no') and runs ('yes'/'no').
5. Enter the number of runs and wickets for the ball.
6. Provide the date and time of the event.
7. (Optional) Enter the match name and user ID for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **team_a_name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of Team A.
* **team_b_name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of Team B.
* **ball_type** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of ball (cricket, tennis, etc.).
* **batsman** (Select Multiple, Required): Indicate if a batsman was involved in the ball ('yes' or 'no').
* **score_for_the_batsman** (Number, Required): Enter the score of the batsman.
* **fielder** (Select Multiple, Required): Indicate if a fielder was involved in the ball ('yes' or 'no').
* **score_for_the_fielder** (Number, Required): Enter the score of the fielder.
* **over** (Select One, Required): Confirm if the ball resulted in an over ('yes' or 'no').
* **ball** (Select One, Required): Confirm if the ball was played ('yes' or 'no').
* **runs** (Number, Required): Enter the number of runs scored in the ball.
* **wickets** (Number, Required): Enter the number of wickets taken in the ball.
* **date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the event.
* **time** (Time, Required): Enter the time of the event.
* **match_id** (Text, Optional): Enter the ID of the match for reference.
* **user_id** (Text, Optional): Enter the ID of the user who recorded the ball.
* **notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional notes about the ball.
* **match_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the match for reference.
* **team_a_score** (Number, Optional): Enter the score of team A.
* **team_b_score** (Number, Optional): Enter the score of team B.
* **team_a_batsman** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate if Team A's batsman was involved in the ball ('yes' or 'no').
* **team_b_batsman** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate if Team B's batsman was involved in the ball ('yes' or 'no').
* **team_a_fielder** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate if Team A's fielder was involved in the ball ('yes' or 'no').
* **team_b_fielder** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate if Team B's fielder was involved in the ball ('yes' or 'no').

## Tips

- Please ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
- For better data quality, try to fill in as much information as possible.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult with a cricket expert or refer to cricket scoring rules.
