# Bug Tracker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bug Tracker Form is a tool designed to help users report bugs, issues, or errors they encounter while interacting with our systems or applications. This form collects crucial information about the problem, allowing our technical team to analyze and resolve the issue efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a bug report, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the respective fields. These details will be used to contact you if we need more information or to notify you about the status of your report.
2. Provide your phone number (optional) if you would like to be contacted via phone.
3. Enter the date you experienced the issue.
4. Specify the system or application affected by the bug.
5. Choose the category of the bug from the available options.
6. Indicate the urgency of the issue, which will help us prioritize the bug resolution.
7. Select the browser and operating system you were using when the issue occurred.
8. Provide a brief summary of the problem.
9. Describe the issue in detail, including any relevant screenshots or log files.
10. Explain the steps you took to reproduce the issue.
11. Specify what was expected to happen and what actually happened.
12. Describe how the bug affects your business or users.
13. Attach any relevant screenshots, log files, or other supporting documents.
14. Choose your notification preference for updates on the bug status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reporter Name** (`reporter_name`, required): Enter your full name as the person reporting the bug.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted via phone.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, required): Enter the date you experienced the issue.
* **System or Application** (`system_name`, required): Specify the system or application affected by the bug.
* **Bug Category** (`bug_category`, required): Choose the category of the bug from the available options.
* **Urgency** (`urgency`, required): Indicate the urgency of the issue.
* **Browser** (`browser`, required): Select the browser you were using when the issue occurred.
* **Operating System** (`os`, required): Select the operating system you were using when the issue occurred.
* **Screen Resolution** (`screen_resolution`, optional): Enter the screen resolution you were using if known.
* **Issue Summary** (`issue_summary`, required): Enter a brief summary of the problem.
* **Issue Details** (`issue_details`, required): Describe the issue in detail.
* **Reproduction Steps** (`reproduction_steps`, required): Explain the steps you took to reproduce the issue.
* **Expected Outcome** (`expected_outcome`, required): Specify what was expected to happen.
* **Actual Outcome** (`actual_outcome`, required): Describe what actually happened.
* **Business Impact** (`impact`, required): Describe how the bug affects your business or users.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, optional): Attach any relevant screenshots, log files, or other supporting documents.
* **Notification Preference** (`notification_preference`, optional): Choose how you would like to be notified about updates on the bug status.
