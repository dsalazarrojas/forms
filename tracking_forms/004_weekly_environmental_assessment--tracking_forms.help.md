# Weekly Environmental Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to track and report on environmental conditions and performance at a site or facility.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and permissions before filling out the form.
2. Review the site's current conditions, including any notable hazards or concerns.
3. Enter the site name, location, and manager's name accurately.
4. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for hazards and sustainability metrics as applicable.
5. Fill in any additional comments or notes about the site conditions.
6. Ensure all required fields are completed and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Conditions** (`site_conditions`, text, required): Describe the current site conditions, including any notable issues or concerns.
* **Hazards** (`hazards`, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' for any hazards present at the site.
* **Sustainability Metrics** (`sustainability_metrics`, number, required): Enter a numerical score indicating the site's sustainability performance.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the site conditions.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the site manager's or team member's email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the site manager's or team member's phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the current date in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the current time.
* **Site Name** (`site_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the site or facility.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of the site.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the site manager or team lead.
* **Team** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team responsible for the site.
* **Site ID** (`site_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the site.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the site conditions.
* **Sustainability Index** (`sustainability_index`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the site's sustainability performance.
* **Condition Score** (`condition_score`, number, optional): Enter a numerical score indicating the site's condition.
* **Hazard Score** (`hazard_score`, number, optional): Enter a numerical score indicating the site's hazard level.
* **Sustainability Score** (`sustainability_score`, number, optional): Enter a numerical score indicating the site's sustainability performance.
* **Comments 2** (`comments2`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the site conditions.
* **Comments 3** (`comments3`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the site conditions.
* **Comments 4** (`comments4`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the site conditions.
* **Comments 5** (`comments5`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the site conditions.
