# daily performance report form in indonesian - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your daily performance reports in Indonesian. By submitting this form, you will be providing valuable insights about your daily activities and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the category you want to report under (e.g., Indonesian).
2. Provide a brief description of your daily performance.
3. Choose the date of the report.
4. Choose the time of the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category (Indonesian/English/Other)**: This field asks you to choose the category under which you want to report your daily performance. Select the option that best fits your needs.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your daily performance, including any notable achievements or challenges you faced.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the report. This will help track the submission date for record-keeping purposes.
* **Time**: Choose the time of submission. This will help in tracking the timing of your report.
