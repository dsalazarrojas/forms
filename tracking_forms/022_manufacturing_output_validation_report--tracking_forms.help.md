# Manufacturing Output Validation Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the manufacturing output validation results.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the batch details in the "batch_details" field.
2. Describe the inspection results in the "inspection_results" field.
3. Enter the quantity of the inspected items in the "quantities" field.
4. Provide any additional observations or notes in the "observations" field.
5. Enter the batch ID number in the "batch_id" field.
6. Enter the name of the inspector who performed the inspection in the "inspector_name" field.
7. Select the outcome of the inspection in the "inspection_status" field.
8. Provide any additional comments or remarks in the "remarks" field.
9. Select the date of the inspection in the "date_of_inspection" field.
10. Select the time of the inspection in the "time_of_inspection" field.
11. Enter the quantity of the inspected items in the "quantity" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **batch_details**: Enter the batch details, such as batch number, production date, and other relevant details.
* **inspection_results**: Briefly describe the inspection results.
* **quantities**: Enter the quantity of the inspected items.
* **observations**: Provide any additional observations or notes related to the inspection.
* **batch_id**: Enter the batch ID number.
* **inspector_name**: Enter the name of the inspector who performed the inspection.
* **inspection_status**: Select the outcome of the inspection from the options provided.
* **remarks**: Provide any additional comments or remarks related to the inspection.
* **date_of_inspection**: Select the date of the inspection.
* **time_of_inspection**: Select the time of the inspection.
* **quantity**: Enter the quantity of the inspected items.
* **inspector_name**: Enter the name of the inspector who performed the inspection.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information in the "batch_details" field.
* Use the "inspection_status" field to accurately reflect the outcome of the inspection.
* Use the "remarks" field for any additional comments or remarks related to the inspection.
* Use the "date_of_inspection" and "time_of_inspection" fields to accurately record the inspection dates and times.
* Use the "quantity" field to accurately record the quantity of inspected items.
