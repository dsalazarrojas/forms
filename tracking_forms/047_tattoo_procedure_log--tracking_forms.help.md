# Tattoo Procedure Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tattoo Procedure Log form is designed to track and document tattoo procedures, allowing users to quickly reference and manage the data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Tattoo Procedure Log" form.
2. Fill in the required fields:
	* Procedure Date: The date of the tattoo procedure.
3. Optional fields:
	* Patient Name: Enter the name of the patient who received the tattoo.
	* Tattoo Location: Enter the location on the body where the tattoo was performed.
	* Artist Name: Enter the name of the artist who performed the tattoo.
	* Procedure Description: Enter a brief description of the procedure.
	* Notes: Add any additional notes about the procedure.
4. Select the Procedure Status:
	* Active: The procedure is currently active or ongoing.
	* Inactive: The procedure is complete or inactive.
5. Select the Procedure Outcome:
	* Yes: The procedure was successful.
	* No: The procedure was not successful.
6. Select if the procedure was Assigned To:
	* Yes: The procedure was assigned to someone.
	* No: The procedure was not assigned to anyone.
7. Enter the Procedure ID (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Procedure Date (id: 1)**: Enter the date of the tattoo procedure.
* **Patient Name (id: 2)**: Enter the name of the patient who received the tattoo.
* **Tattoo Location (id: 3)**: Enter the location on the body where the tattoo was performed.
* **Artist Name (id: 4)**: Enter the name of the artist who performed the tattoo.
* **Procedure Description (id: 5)**: Enter a brief description of the procedure.
* **Notes (id: 6)**: Add any additional notes about the procedure.
* **Procedure Status (id: 7)**: Select the status of the procedure.
	+ Active: The procedure is currently active or ongoing.
	+ Inactive: The procedure is complete or inactive.
* **Procedure Outcome (id: 8)**: Select the outcome of the procedure.
	+ Yes: The procedure was successful.
	+ No: The procedure was not successful.
* **Assigned To (id: 9)**: Select if the procedure was assigned to someone.
	+ Yes: The procedure was assigned to someone.
	+ No: The procedure was not assigned to anyone.
* **Procedure ID (id: 10)**: Enter a unique identifier for the procedure (optional).
