# Call Tracker Team Point Zero 26 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for team members to log and track their interactions with clients, including calls, meetings, and other discussions. It helps the team to keep a record of all interactions, which can be useful for reporting, analysis, and performance tracking.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Team Member Name** (`team_member_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the team member who logged the call.
2. **Client Account ID** (`client_account_id`, text, required): Enter the unique identifier for the client's account.
3. **Timestamp** (`timestamp_of_call`, text, required): Enter the date and time of the interaction (e.g., "2022-03-16 14:30").
4. **Call Direction** (`call_direction`, select_one, required): Select whether the call was inbound ( incoming call from the client) or outbound (call from the team member to the client).
5. **Customer Concern Category** (`concern_category`, select_one, required): Choose the main topic of the discussion (e.g., Technical Issue - Hardware, Billing and Payments, etc.).
6. **Resolution Level** (`resolution_level`, select_one, required): Select how the issue was addressed (e.g., Tier 1 Resolution, Tier 2 Resolution, etc.).
7. **Current Call Status** (`call_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the call (e.g., Open, Waiting for Client, In Progress, etc.).
8. **Call Summary** (`call_summary`, text, required): Provide a concise but complete summary of the discussion.
9. **Client Sentiment - Mood** (`client_mood`, select_one, optional): Evaluate the client's mood after the interaction (e.g., Satisfied, Neutral, Frustrated, etc.).
10. **Escalation Required?** (`escalation_flag`, select_one, required): Select whether the call needs higher-level review or escalation.
11. **Reason for Escalation** (`escalation_reason`, text, optional): If Yes, provide a brief explanation for the escalation.
12. **Internal Team Notes** (`internal_team_notes`, text, optional): Record any internal team notes not intended for client viewing.
13. **Performance Points Earned** (`points_earned`, number, optional): Track internal performance metrics.
14. **Tracking Reference Number** (`submission_id`, text, optional): Enter an auto-generated or manual tracking reference number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Member Name**: Enter the name of the team member who logged the call.
* **Client Account ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the client's account. This helps the team to quickly identify the client and their interactions.
* **Timestamp**: Enter the date and time of the interaction. This is a required field to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
* **Call Direction**: Select whether the call was incoming or outgoing. This helps the team to understand the flow of calls and prioritize tasks accordingly.
* **Customer Concern Category**: Choose the main topic of the discussion. This helps the team to quickly identify common concerns and trends.
* **Resolution Level**: Select how the issue was addressed. This helps the team to track resolution progress and identify areas for improvement.
* **Current Call Status**: Select the current status of the call. This helps the team to prioritize tasks and track call outcomes.
* **Call Summary**: Provide a concise but complete summary of the discussion. This helps the team to quickly review call details and identify key takeaways.
* **Client Sentiment - Mood**: Evaluate the client's mood after the interaction. This provides valuable feedback for team improvement and client relationship building.
* **Escalation Required?**: Select whether the call needs higher-level review or escalation. This helps the team to identify calls that require special attention.
* **Reason for Escalation**: If the call needs escalation, provide a brief explanation. This helps the team to understand the reason for escalation and prioritize tasks accordingly.
* **Internal Team Notes**: Record any internal team notes not intended for client viewing. This helps the team to discuss and refine ideas internally.
* **Performance Points Earned**: Track internal performance metrics. This helps the team to evaluate individual and team performance.
* **Tracking Reference Number**: Enter an auto-generated or manual tracking reference number. This helps the team to quickly reference calls and track progress.
