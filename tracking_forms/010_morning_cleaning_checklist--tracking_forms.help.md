# Morning Cleaning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Morning Cleaning Checklist form is designed to track daily cleaning tasks and their respective completion status. This form helps to ensure that cleaning tasks are completed on time and that team members are held accountable for their responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the team members responsible for completing the tasks.
2. Choose the specific tasks to be completed on the given date and time.
3. Enter the date and time of completion.
4. Optionally, add any notes regarding the morning cleaning tasks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Morning Cleaning Checklist (morning_cleaning_checklist)** (`morning_cleaning_checklist`, text, required): This field is the main title of the checklist, indicating what tasks are being completed.
* **Daily Tasks (daily_tasks)** (`daily_tasks`, select_multiple, required): Select multiple tasks to be completed from the available options.
* **Morning Cleaning Checklist Notes (morning_cleaning_checklist_notes)** (`morning_cleaning_checklist_notes`, note, optional): A field for adding any relevant notes about the morning cleaning tasks.
* **Team Members (team_members)** (`team_members`, select_one, required): Choose the team member responsible for completing the selected tasks.
* **Assigned Tool (assigned_tool)** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Enter the tool or equipment used for the cleaning tasks.
* **Date (date)** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date when the tasks were completed.
* **Time (time)** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time when the tasks were completed.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in to complete the form.
* Be mindful of selecting the correct team members and tools used.
* Use the notes field for any additional information or clarification about the morning cleaning tasks.
* Enter the correct date and time of completion.
