# #SeasTheDay Beach Clean Up Data Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data on beach clean-up events. It is designed for teams to record information about their clean-up activities, including event details, litter counts, and observations. This data will help beach cleanup initiatives and organizations track their progress and improve their efforts.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Fill in the Basics
Enter the team leader's name, organization or school name (if applicable), and email address for follow-up questions.

*   **Team leader full name**: Enter the primary contact person's name.
*   **Organization or school name**: Provide the name of the company, school, or volunteer group (if applicable).
*   **Email address**: Enter the contact email address for follow-up questions.

### Step 2: Record Event Details

*   **Date of cleanup**: Enter the date of the beach cleanup event.
*   **Beach name**: Enter the official or commonly known name of the beach.
*   **Zone or sector code**: Enter the zone or sector code (e.g., Sector A, North Zone, near Pier 3).
*   **GPS latitude**: Enter the decimal degree GPS latitude (e.g., 1.3521).
*   **GPS longitude**: Enter the decimal degree GPS longitude (e.g., 103.8198).
*   **Cleanup start time**: Enter the start time of the cleanup event (counting began).
*   **Cleanup end time**: Enter the end time of the cleanup event (counting stopped).

### Step 3: Count Litter and Other Items

*   **Plastic beverage bottles & caps**: Count all sizes, including bottle caps.
*   **Plastic bags & food wrappers**: Count shopping bags, chip bags, candy wrappers.
*   **Food containers & cups**: Count takeaway containers, foam cups, lids.
*   **Straws, stirrers & cutlery**: Count plastic forks, spoons, knives, straws.
*   **Glass & metal items**: Count bottles, cans, metal fragments.
*   **Total weight collected (kg)**: Weigh all bags combined, in kilograms.

### Step 4: Additional Observations

*   **Weather conditions**: Select the current weather conditions (Sunny, Partly cloudy, Overcast, Light rain, Heavy rain).
*   **Tide level**: Select the tide level (High, Mid, Low, Incoming, Outgoing).
*   **Wildlife interactions observed**: Select the types of wildlife interactions observed (None, Possible interaction, Animal freed and documented, Deceased animal found, Nesting site disturbance).
*   **Unusual or hazardous items found**: Select all unusual or hazardous items found (Medical waste, Construction debris, Fishing nets/gear, Electronics, Industrial drums/chemicals, Oil/tar balls).
*   **Other debris description**: Enter a description of other large debris items not counted above (tires, appliances, etc.).
*   **Data quality notes**: Enter any issues with counting accuracy, weather interruptions, etc.

### Step 5: Review and Submit

*   Double-check all information for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   Review all observations and counts carefully.
*   Ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.

## Tips

*   Review all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   Take note of any unusual or hazardous items found.
*   Be as accurate as possible when counting and weighing litter items.

*   Enter a description of other large debris items not counted above in the "Other debris description" field.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be left blank.
