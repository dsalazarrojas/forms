# Trainee Progress Report Form Cellular Sales - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track the progress of trainees in the Cellular Sales department. It is used to assess their overall performance, communication skills, time management, and other essential competencies. The form helps managers and mentors evaluate trainees' strengths and weaknesses, providing valuable insights for their development and growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the trainee's overall performance from the options provided.
2.  Assess the trainee's communication skills, time management, and other key areas using the same options.
3.  Identify any areas where the trainee needs improvement and select them from the options.
4.  Indicate if the trainee received support during the training period.
5.  Provide any additional comments or feedback for the trainee.
6.  Enter the trainee's name and mentor's name for accurate tracking and reference.
7.  Enter the form ID and training progress form ID for record-keeping purposes.
8.  Select the trainee's current progress status.
9.  Enter the date and time of submission for record-keeping purposes.
10. Provide any additional comments or feedback for the trainee's progress.
11. Enter the date and time of submission for record-keeping purposes.
