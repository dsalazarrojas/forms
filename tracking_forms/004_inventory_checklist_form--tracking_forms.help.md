# Inventory Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory Checklist Form is a tool used to track and manage inventory items in the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Item Name in the text field.
2. Enter the quantity of the item.
3. Select the Category of the item from the dropdown list.
4. Choose the status of the item from the multi-select dropdown list.
5. Enter the Date Received, if applicable.
6. Enter the name of the person who received the item.
7. Add any additional Notes about the item.
8. Enter the Email contact information, if applicable.
9. Enter the phone number of the person who received the item.
10. Enter the Serial Number of the item, if applicable.
11. Enter the Location of the item, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Item Name (1)**: A text field for entering the name of the inventory item.
* **Quantity (2)**: A numeric field for entering the quantity of the item.
* **Price (3)**: A numeric field for entering the price of the item.
* **Category (4)**: A dropdown list for selecting the category of the item.
	+ Select 'Yes' for items that belong to a specific category.
	+ Select 'No' for items that do not belong to a specific category.
* **Item Status (5)**: A multi-select dropdown list for choosing the status of the item.
	+ Select 'Active' for items that are currently available and usable.
	+ Select 'Inactive' for items that are not available or not usable.
* **Date Received (6)**: A date field for entering the date the item was received.
* **Received By (7)**: A text field for entering the name of the person who received the item.
* **Notes (8)**: A text area for adding additional notes or comments about the item.
* **Email (9)**: An email field for entering the contact email of the person who received the item, if applicable.
* **Phone (10)**: A text field for entering the phone number of the person who received the item, if applicable.
* **Serial Number (11)**: A text field for entering the serial number of the item, if applicable.
* **Location (12)**: A text field for entering the location of the item, if applicable.
