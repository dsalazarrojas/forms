# Life Member Reaffirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Life Member Reaffirmation Form is used to update the status of Life Members and reaffirm their affiliation with the organization. This form should only be used by Life Members who have been previously registered with the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the field provided.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the field provided.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the format `example@email.com`.
4. Enter your **Phone Number**, without any special characters.
5. Enter your **Life Member ID**, a unique number assigned to you by the organization.
6. Select **Yes/No** to indicate if you have reaffirmed your membership with the organization.
7. Enter any **Notes** you have regarding your membership status.
8. Select a **Date** for the current date.
9. Select a **Time** for the current time.
10. Select your current **Status** from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A text field to enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: A text field to enter your last name.
* **Email**: A field to enter your email address in the format `example@email.com`.
* **Phone**: A text field to enter your phone number, without any special characters.
* **Life Member ID**: A number field to enter your unique Life Member ID assigned by the organization.
* **Reaffirmed**: A checkbox to indicate if you have reaffirmed your membership with the organization.
* **Notes**: A text area to enter any notes or comments you have regarding your membership status.
* **Date**: A date field to select the current date.
* **Time**: A time field to select the current time.
* **Status**: A select field with the options **Option 1**, **Option 2**, **Option 3**, **Option 4**, and **Option 5** to select your current status.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the organization's administration for assistance.
* This form should only be used by Life Members who have been previously registered with the organization.
