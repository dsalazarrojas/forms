# Vital Signs Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vital Signs Tracking Form is a tool for medical professionals to collect and track vital signs information from patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the patient's medical chart to ensure that the information is up-to-date and accurate.
2. Enter the patient's body temperature, pulse rate, respiration rate, blood pressure, and oxygen level.
3. Provide an update on the patient's status.
4. Add any additional notes or comments regarding the patient's condition.
5. Enter any other relevant information as prompted.

* **Body Temperature** (`body_temp`, number, optional): Record the patient's body temperature, typically measured in degrees Celsius or Fahrenheit.
