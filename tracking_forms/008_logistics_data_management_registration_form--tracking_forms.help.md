# Logistics Data Management Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register logistics data management information for tracking and managing logistics activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field. Please ensure it's a valid email address.
4. Fill out the "Phone" field with your contact phone number.
5. Enter the date of the activity in the "Date" field. Use the date format "YYYY-MM-DD".
6. Enter the time of the activity in the "Time" field. Use the time format "HH:MM:SS".
7. Provide the location of the activity in the "Location" field.
8. Enter a brief description of the activity in the "Description" field.
9. Select the "Category" of the activity from the options provided (Logistics, Operations, or Maintenance).
10. Choose the status of the activity from the options provided (In Progress, Complete, or Pending).
11. You can add additional notes about the activity in the "Notes" field. (Optional)
12. If applicable, enter a different email address in the "Email2" field.
13. If applicable, fill out the "Phone2" field with an alternative contact phone number.
14. If applicable, enter the date of a different activity in the "Date2" field. Use the date format "YYYY-MM-DD".
15. If applicable, enter the time of a different activity in the "Time2" field. Use the time format "HH:MM:SS".
16. If applicable, enter the location of a different activity in the "Location2" field.
17. If applicable, enter the description of a different activity in the "Description2" field.
18. If applicable, select the category of the different activity from the options provided (Logistics, Operations, or Maintenance).
19. If applicable, choose the status of the different activity from the options provided (In Progress, Complete, or Pending).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents or ID.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents or ID.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the activity in "YYYY-MM-DD" format.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the activity in "HH:MM:SS" format.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the activity.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the activity.
* **Category**: Select the category of the activity (Logistics, Operations, or Maintenance).
* **Status**: Choose the status of the activity (In Progress, Complete, or Pending).
* **Notes**: Additional notes about the activity. (Optional)
* **Email2**: Enter a different email address (if applicable).
* **Phone2**: Fill out an alternative contact phone number (if applicable).
* **Date2**: Enter the date of a different activity (if applicable).
* **Time2**: Enter the time of a different activity (if applicable).
* **Location2**: Enter the location of a different activity (if applicable).
* **Description2**: Enter the description of a different activity (if applicable).
* **Category2**: Select the category of the different activity (if applicable).
* **Status2**: Choose the status of the different activity (if applicable).
* **Contact**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Company**: Enter the company name (if applicable).
* **Website**: Enter the website URL (if applicable).
* **City**: Enter the city of the location (if applicable).
* **State**: Enter the state or province of the location (if applicable).
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the location (if applicable).
* **Country**: Enter the country of the location (if applicable).
