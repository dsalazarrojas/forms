# Protected Areas Infrastructure Inventory - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Protected Areas Infrastructure Inventory form is used to track the presence and conditions of infrastructure assets within protected areas, such as parks, wildlife reserves, or other conservation areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form pages and sections that apply to your specific situation.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, such as "Yes" or "No" for certain questions.
3. Provide any necessary notes or comments in the text field.
4. Ensure that you complete all required fields, indicated by a *.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **infrastructure_assets** (`infrastructure_assets`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether infrastructure assets are present or absent in the protected area.
* **location** (`location`, select_one, required: false): Choose the specific location where the infrastructure is situated, such as a park or wildlife reserve.
* **asset_conditions** (`asset_conditions`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the condition of the infrastructure assets, such as whether they are functional or not.
* **conditions** (`conditions`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of any conditions affecting the infrastructure assets, such as maintenance needs or repair requirements.
* **photos** (`photos`, note, required: false): Attach any relevant photographs or images of the infrastructure assets to provide additional context.
* **asset_conditions_note** (`asset_conditions_note`, note, required: false): Provide any additional notes or comments about the condition of the infrastructure assets.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any general notes or comments about the protected area or its infrastructure.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when completing the form to ensure accurate tracking of protected area infrastructure.
* If you are unsure about any field or option, refer back to the form's purpose and the explanation above.
* Remember to review and update the form regularly to reflect changes in the protected area's infrastructure.
