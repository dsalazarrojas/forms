# Agenda Item Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agenda Item Details Form is used to document and track agenda items for meetings or events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Meeting Name, which should be a descriptive title for the meeting or event.
2. Enter the Meeting Date, which should be the date that the meeting or event took place.
3. Enter the Meeting Time, which should be the start and end times of the meeting or event.
4. Enter the Meeting Location, which should be the location or platform where the meeting was held.
5. Enter the Meeting Chair or Facilitator, which should be the name of the person who led the meeting.
6. Enter the Agenda Item Number, which is a sequential number for the agenda item.
7. Enter the Agenda Item Title, which should be a brief title for the agenda item.
8. Enter a detailed Description of the agenda item.
9. If a Presenter is involved, enter their name.
10. Enter the Allocated Time for the agenda item.
11. In the Discussion Points section, enter any main points discussed during the agenda item.
12. If Decisions were made, record them in the Decisions Made section.
13. If Action Items were assigned, list them in the Action Items section.
14. Enter the name of the party responsible for each Action Item.
15. If Deadlines were set, enter them for the Action Items.
16. If Resources are needed, list them in the Resources Needed section.
17. Indicate if Follow-up is required, and if so, provide details.
18. Enter any Additional Notes about the agenda item.
19. Finally, enter the name of the person who recorded the details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Meeting Name (1)**: Enter the name of the meeting or event.
  This is the title of the meeting or event.
- **Meeting Date (2)**: Enter the date of the meeting.
  This is the date that the meeting or event took place.
- **Meeting Time (3)**: Enter the start and end times of the meeting.
  This is the time the meeting started and ended.
- **Meeting Location (4)**: Enter the location or platform where the meeting was held.
  This is the physical or virtual location of the meeting.
- **Meeting Chair (5)**: Enter the name of the meeting chair or facilitator.
  This is the person who led the meeting.
- **Agenda Item Number (6)**: Enter the sequential number of the agenda item.
  A number to keep track of the order of agenda items.
- **Agenda Item Title (7)**: Enter a brief title for the agenda item.
  A concise title for the agenda item.
- **Agenda Item Description (8)**: Enter a detailed description of the agenda item.
  A description of what was discussed during the agenda item.
- **Presenter Name (9)**: Enter the name of the person presenting this item.
  The person who will be presenting the agenda item.
- **Allocated Time (10)**: Enter the amount of time allocated for this agenda item.
  The amount of time allocated for the agenda item.
- **Key Discussion Points (11)**: List the main points discussed during this agenda item.
  The main points that were discussed.
- **Decisions Made (12)**: Record any decisions made regarding this agenda item.
  Any decisions made during the discussion.
- **Action Items (13)**: List any action items assigned during this discussion.
  Tasks assigned to someone to follow up on.
- **Responsible Party (14)**: Enter the name of the person responsible for each action item.
  The person responsible for completing the action items.
- **Deadline for Action Items (15)**: Enter the deadline for completing the action items.
  When the action items should be completed by.
- **Resources Needed (16)**: List any resources required to complete the action items.
  Resources needed to complete the action items.
- **Follow-up Required (17)**: Indicate if follow-up is required for this agenda item.
  If yes, indicate that follow-up is required.
- **Follow-up Details (18)**: If yes, provide details about the follow-up needed.
  Details about any follow-up required.
- **Additional Notes (19)**: Enter any other notes about this agenda item.
  Any other notes or information that should be recorded.
- **Recorder Name (20)**: Enter the name of the person recording these details.
  The person recording the details for this agenda item.
