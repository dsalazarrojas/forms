# Focus Session Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track the progress of a focus session, a period of time spent on a specific topic or task. It helps users keep accurate records of their session details, which can be useful for productivity tracking and time management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the focus session in the **Session Date** field.
2. Select the topic of the focus session in the **Session Topic** field.
3. Enter the start and end times of the focus session in the **Session Start** and **Session End** fields, respectively.
4. Enter the total time spent on the focus session in the **Total Session Time** field.
5. Choose the frequency of the focus session from the **Focus Session Frequency** field.
6. Enter the goal of the focus session in the **Focus Session Goal** field.
7. (Optional) Enter any notes about the session in the **Session Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Date (1)** (`session_date`, date): Enter the date on which the focus session took place.
* **Session Topic (2)** (`session_topic`, text): Enter a brief description of the topic you were focusing on during the session.
* **Session Start (3)** (`session_start`, time): Enter the start time of the focus session.
* **Session End (4)** (`session_end`, time): Enter the end time of the focus session.
* **Session Notes (5)** (`session_notes`, text): Enter any notes or comments about the session.
* **Focused Time (6)** (`focused_time`, number): Enter the total time spent on the focus session.
* **Break Time (7)** (`break_time`, number): Enter the total time taken for breaks during the focus session.
* **Total Session Time (8)** (`total_session_time`, number): Enter the total time spent on the focus session, including breaks.
* **Focus Session Frequency (9)** (`focus_session_frequency`, select_one): Choose the frequency of the focus session from the available options.
* **Focus Session Goal (10)** (`focus_session_goal`, text): Enter a brief description of the goal you aimed to achieve during the focus session.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and clear information in each field to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
* If you need to record multiple focus sessions on the same date, create a new form instance for each session.
* You can use this form to track your progress and identify areas for improvement.
