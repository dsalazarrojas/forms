# Quick Reference Guide Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create a quick reference guide for your organization or business. A quick reference guide is a document that provides essential information for staff or stakeholders to quickly reference during a process or task.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the title of the guide you are creating.
2.  Add any necessary instructions or guidance for the guide.
3.  Provide any instructions specifically for staff members who will be using the guide.
4.  Enter the contact information for your staff members.
5.  Add any additional notes or comments about the guide.
6.  If you used a form builder tool, please specify which one you used.
7.  Describe your submission management process for the guide.

*   **Process Guide** (`process_guide`): Enter the title of the guide you are creating. This will help identify the purpose and scope of the guide.
*   **Guide Instructions** (`guide_instructions`): Add any necessary instructions or guidance for the guide. This could include step-by-step processes, important considerations, or other relevant information.
*   **Instructions for Staff** (`instructions_for_staff`): Provide any instructions specifically for staff members who will be using the guide. This could include information on how to access the guide, how to use it, or other related guidance.
*   **Staff Contact** (`staff_contact`): Enter the contact information for your staff members who are responsible for the guide.
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Add the contact information for your organization or business.
*   **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): Add any additional notes or comments about the guide.
*   **Form Builder Used** (`form_builder_used`): If you used a form builder tool, please specify which one you used.
