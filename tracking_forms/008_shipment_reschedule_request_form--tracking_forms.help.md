# shipment_reschedule_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for rescheduling shipments. It is meant to be completed by customers or staff members who wish to change the date or time of an already scheduled shipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field according to the instructions provided for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Shipment Date** (`new_shipment_date`, date, optional): Please enter the new shipment date you would like to schedule for. If left blank, the original shipment date will remain unchanged.
* **New Shipment Time** (`new_shipment_time`, time, optional): Please enter the new shipment time you would like to schedule for. If left blank, the original shipment time will remain unchanged.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Please provide a brief explanation for the reschedule request.
* **Staff Comments** (`staff_comments`, text, optional): If you are a staff member, please provide any additional comments or context that may be helpful for processing the reschedule request.
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, text, optional): If you are a customer, please provide any additional comments or context that may be helpful for staff processing the reschedule request.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, select_one, optional): Please select who is making this reschedule request (Customer or Staff).
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address so we can contact you with updates or questions related to the reschedule request.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you with updates or questions related to the reschedule request.
* **Submitter Note** (`submitter_note`, note, optional): If you are making this request, please add any additional details or context that may be helpful for staff processing the request.
