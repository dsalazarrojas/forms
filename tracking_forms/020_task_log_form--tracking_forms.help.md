# Task Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track task completion status. It helps in keeping a record of tasks and their respective completion status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Select the option for "Yes" or "No" for "Task" field.
- If "Yes" is selected, enter the date of completion in the "Date" field if required.
- In the "Notes" field, provide any additional information about the task.
- Choose "Yes" or "No" for "Assigned User" field.
- Select the priority level ("Yes", "No") for "Priority" field.
- Choose the status of the task ("Active", "Inactive") for "Status" field.
- If "Yes" is selected, enter the date of completion in the "Completed At" field.
- Choose "Yes" or "No" for "Completed By" field.
- Select the category of the task ("Yes", "No") for "Category" field.
- Choose "Yes" or "No" for "Submitted By" field.
- If "Yes" is selected, enter the date of submission in the "Submitted At" field.
- Choose "Yes" or "No" for "Completed By" field.
- Enter the date of completion in the "Completed At" field if "Yes" is selected for "Completed By".
- Select the option "Yes" or "No" for "Task Log Form Completed At" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Task** (`text`, optional): Enter the description of the task if "Yes" is selected.
* **Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of completion if "Yes" is selected.
* **Notes** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional information about the task.
* **Assigned User** (`select_one`, optional): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate assignment status.
* **Priority** (`select_one`, optional): Select the priority level ("Yes", "No") for the task.
* **Status** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the status of the task ("Active", "Inactive").
* **Completed At** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of completion if "Yes" is selected.
* **Completed By** (`select_one`, optional): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate completion status.
* **Category** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the category of the task ("Yes", "No").
* **Submitted By** (`select_one`, optional): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate submission status.
* **Submitted At** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of submission if "Yes" is selected.
* **Completed By** (`select_one`, optional): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate completion status.
* **Completed At** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of completion.
* **Task Log Form Completed At** (`select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate completion status.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct option for each field to ensure accurate tracking of task completion status.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out, if the field is marked as required.
- Double-check the dates entered in the "Date" and "Submitted At" fields.
