# Office Supplies Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track office supplies inventory, including item numbers, names, categories, quantities, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the **Item Number** field with a unique identifier for the item.
- Enter the **Item Name** in the next field.
- Select the **Category** for the item.
- Enter the **Number of Items On Hand**, which is the current quantity of the item available.
- Enter the **Quantity to Order**, which is the amount of the item you want to order.
- Enter the **Unit Price**, which is the cost per unit of the item.
- In the **Checked By** field, enter the name of the person or department that verified the information.
- Finally, enter the **Approver** name, which is the person responsible for approving the inventory update.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Item Number** (text, required): Enter a unique identifier for the item, such as a stock number or a barcode.
- **Item Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the item, such as "Pens" or "Paperclips".
- **Category** (text, required): Select the category for the item, such as "Writing Supplies" or "Desk Accessories".
- **Number of Items On Hand** (number, required): Enter the current quantity of the item available.
- **Quantity to Order** (number, required): Enter the amount of the item you want to order.
- **Unit Price** (number, required): Enter the cost per unit of the item.
- **Checked By** (text, required): Enter the name of the person or department that verified the information.
- **Approver** (text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for approving the inventory update.
