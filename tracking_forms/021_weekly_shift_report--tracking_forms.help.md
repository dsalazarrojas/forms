# weekly_shift_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data on employee shifts for a given week.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form for the correct week, based on the "Week Ending Date" field.
2. Select either "Yes" or "No" for whether you worked during the week.
3. Enter your Employee ID and Employee Name.
4. Fill out the Shift Start and Shift End times for the week.
5. Check the "Shift Days" field to indicate which days you worked during the week.
6. Enter the number of hours you worked during the week.
7. Add any Comments related to your work hours or shift.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Week Ending Date**: Select the date at the end of the week you worked (e.g., Friday).
* **Employee ID**: Enter your unique employee ID.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Shift Start Time**: Enter the time you started your shift during the week.
* **Shift End Time**: Enter the time you ended your shift during the week.
* **Shift Days**: Check the days you worked during the week. Select all relevant days.
* **Worked Hours**: Enter the total number of hours you worked during the week.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or notes about your work hours or shift.

## Tips

* Ensure that you complete the form for the correct week.
* Only select "Yes" for days you actually worked during the week.
* Be accurate when entering your employee ID and name.
* Double-check your worked hours for correctness.
* Keep the comments field brief and to the point.
* This form is for weekly submission, so ensure it's filled out only once per week.
