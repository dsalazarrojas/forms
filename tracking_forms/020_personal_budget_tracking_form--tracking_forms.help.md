# Personal Budget Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you track your personal income and expenses over a specified period. It will help you set and monitor your income and expense goals, providing you with accurate financial insights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your monthly income under "Monthly Income".
2. Enter your monthly expenses under "Monthly Expenses".
3. Set your income goal for the month under "Income Goal".
4. Set your expense goal for the month under "Expense Goal".
5. Select whether you have made progress towards your goals under "Progress".
6. Enter the date for which you want to track your income and expenses under "Date".
7. Enter the time for which you want to track your income and expenses under "Time".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Monthly Income** (`monthly_income`, `number`, required): Enter your total monthly income from all sources, such as salary, investments, and other earnings.
* **Monthly Expenses** (`monthly_expenses`, `number`, required): Enter your total monthly expenses, including rent, utilities, groceries, and other regular expenses.
* **Monthly Savings** (`monthly_savings`, `number`, not required): This field is optional, but if you do have separate savings goals, you can enter the amount here.
* **Income Goal** (`income_goal`, `text`, required): Enter a specific and achievable income target for the month, e.g., "$5,000".
* **Expense Goal** (`expense_goal`, `text`, required): Enter a specific and achievable expense target for the month, e.g., "$2,500".
* **Progress** (`progress`, `select_one`, not required): Select "Yes" if you have made progress towards your income and expense goals, or "No" if you have not.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date for which you want to track your income and expenses. This should be the first day of the month if you are tracking for the entire month.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time for which you want to track your income and expenses. This should be the time of day for which you are tracking, e.g., "9:00 AM".
