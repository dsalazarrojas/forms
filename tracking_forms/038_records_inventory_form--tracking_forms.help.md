# Records Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Records Inventory Form is designed to collect team member and supervisor information, track inventory items, and identify team coordinators. It's likely used for team management, project tracking, or asset tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. If you're a team member, fill in your personal details.
2. Select all applicable coordinators for the team.
3. Fill in the text fields for your supervisor's details and any inventory items.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_details** (user_details, text, required/optional): This field is for you to fill in your personal details. If it's optional, you can leave it blank.
* **supervisor_details** (supervisor_details, text, required/optional): This field is for your supervisor's details. If it's optional, you can leave it blank.
* **inventory_items** (inventory_items, text, required/optional): This field is for free-form entry of any inventory items. If it's optional, you can leave it blank.
* **coordinators** (coordinators, select_multiple, required/true): Select all applicable coordinators for the team. This field is mandatory.
* **coordinator_details** (coordinator_details, text, required/optional): This field is for coordinator details. If it's optional, you can leave it blank.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all required coordinators for the team.
* Fill in your personal and supervisor details if required.
* Use the free-form "inventory_items" field for any additional inventory items.
* Review your form before submission to ensure completeness.
