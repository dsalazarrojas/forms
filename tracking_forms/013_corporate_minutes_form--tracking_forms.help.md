# Corporate Minutes Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Minutes Form is used to document and track corporate meetings. It helps in organizing and storing meeting-related information, such as meeting dates, types, attendees, locations, agendas, action items, and next meeting dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the meeting date (if required).
2. Select the meeting type (if required).
3. Enter meeting attendees.
4. Choose the meeting location (if required).
5. Enter the meeting agenda.
6. Record meeting minutes.
7. Add action items from the meeting.
8. Schedule the next meeting date (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Meeting Date (page 1)
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, optional)
	+ Enter the date the meeting took place.
### Meeting Type (page 1)
* **Meeting Type** (`meeting_type`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select the type of meeting (e.g., Regular, Emergency).
### Meeting Attendees (page 1)
* **Meeting Attendees** (`attendees`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the names of people who attended the meeting.
### Meeting Location (page 2)
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose the location where the meeting was held (e.g., Conference room, Video conference, Phone call).
### Meeting Agenda (page 2)
* **Meeting Agenda** (`meeting_agenda`, text, optional)
	+ Record the main topics discussed during the meeting.
### Meeting Minutes (page 3)
* **Meeting Minutes** (`meeting_minutes`, text, optional)
	+ Write a brief summary of the meeting's discussion and decisions.
### Action Items (page 3)
* **Action Items** (`action_items`, text, optional)
	+ List tasks assigned to attendees or actions agreed upon.
### Next Meeting Date (page 4)
* **Next Meeting Date** (`next_meeting_date`, date, optional)
	+ Enter the date of the next scheduled meeting.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use a clear and concise format when entering meeting minutes.
* Review and update the form after each meeting to maintain accurate records.
* Consider adding this form to your meeting schedule routine.
