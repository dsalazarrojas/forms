# Daily Warehouse Stock Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Warehouse Stock Assessment form is a tool used to track the current stock levels, stock received, and any stock discrepancies in the warehouse on a daily basis. It ensures the warehouse inventory is up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1.  Select the **Location** of the warehouse from the provided options (e.g., Warehouse 1, Warehouse 2, or Warehouse 3).
2.  Choose the **Stock Type** (e.g., Stock, Inventory, or Supplies) from the options.
3.  Enter the **Stock Item** (e.g., a specific product name or code).
4.  Enter the **Current Stock** level for the selected item.
5.  Enter the **Received Stock** for the selected item.
6.  Enter any **Stock Discrepancies** that occurred.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Location** (select_one): Select the specific location of the warehouse where the stock assessment is taking place.
    *   Options: Warehouse 1, Warehouse 2, Warehouse 3
*   **Stock Type** (select_multiple): Choose the type of stock being assessed.
    *   Options: Stock, Inventory, Supplies
*   **Stock Item** (text): Enter the specific item being assessed.
*   **Current Stock** (number): Enter the current stock level for the selected item.
*   **Received Stock** (number): Enter the stock received on that day.
*   **Stock Discrepancies** (text): Enter any discrepancies that occurred.

## Tips

*   Make sure to review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
*   Ensure accuracy in all fields, especially those marked as "required."
*   If there's any discrepancy, please provide a clear explanation of what caused it.
