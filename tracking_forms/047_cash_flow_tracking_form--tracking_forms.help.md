# Cash Flow Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cash Flow Tracking Form is designed to help users track and monitor all cash inflows and outflows, providing a comprehensive overview of a company's financial activities. This form allows users to record transactions, track changes in cash balance, and identify discrepancies or anomalies in the company's financial activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2. Enter the **Entry Date** in the date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
3. Enter the **Business Name** and **Bank Account Name** as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
4. Select the appropriate **Transaction Type** (inflow or outflow) and **Transaction Category** (e.g., Operating activity, Investing activity, Financing activity).
5. Enter a detailed description of the **Transaction Description**.
6. Choose the correct **Payment Method** (e.g., Cash, Check, Credit card, Debit card, Wire transfer, ACH, Online payment, Other).
7. Identify the **Counterparty Name** and its **Counterparty Type** (e.g., Customer, Vendor, Employee, Lender, Investor, Government, Other).
8. Enter the **Transaction Amount** and ensure it's accurate and up-to-date.
9. Select the correct **Subcategory** (e.g., Sales revenue, Customer payment, Interest income, Investment income, Other income, Operating expense, Payroll, Rent, Utilities, Inventory, Equipment, Loan payment, Tax payment, Owner draw, Other).
10. If applicable, enter the **Project Code**, **Department Code**, and **Tax Applicable** status.
11. Choose the correct **Transaction Type** for the **Tax Amount** (if applicable).
12. Enter the **Transaction Notes**, and if necessary, select the **Is This an Unusual Transaction** flag.
13. Choose whether a **Follow-Up Is Needed** and enter any **Follow-Up Notes**.
14. Review and verify the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Tracking ID Number**: A unique reference number for the transaction.
- **Entry Date**: The date the transaction was entered.
- **Business Information**: Company details, such as name, address, and contact information.
- **Business Name**: The name of the business or organization associated with the transaction.
- **Bank Account Name**: The name of the bank account associated with the transaction.
- **Account Number**: The last 4 digits of the bank account number (optional).
- **Transaction Information**: A detailed description of the transaction.
- **Transaction Date**: The date the transaction occurred.
- **Transaction Type**: Whether the transaction is an inflow or outflow.
- **Transaction Category**: A classification of the transaction (e.g., Operating activity, Investing activity, Financing activity).
- **Transaction Description**: A detailed description of the transaction.
- **Reference Number**: A reference number for the transaction (e.g., invoice number or receipt number).
- **Payment Method**: The method used to complete the transaction (e.g., Cash, Check, Credit card, Debit card, Wire transfer, ACH, Online payment, Other).
- **Counterparty Name**: The name of the counterparty involved in the transaction.
- **Counterparty Type**: The type of counterparty (e.g., Customer, Vendor, Employee, Lender, Investor, Government, Other).
- **Transaction Amount**: The value of the transaction.
- **Running Balance**: The balance after the transaction (optional).
- **Subcategory**: A more specific classification of the transaction (e.g., Sales revenue, Customer payment, Interest income, Investment income, Other income, Operating expense, Payroll, Rent, Utilities, Inventory, Equipment, Loan payment, Tax payment, Owner draw, Other).
- **Project Code**: A code associated with the project (if applicable).
- **Department Code**: A code associated with the department (if applicable).
- **Tax Applicable**: Whether tax is applicable to the transaction.
- **Tax Amount**: The value of the tax (if applicable).
- **Transaction Notes**: Additional details about the transaction.
- **Is This an Unusual Transaction**: A flag indicating whether the transaction is an anomaly.
- **Is Follow-Up Needed**: A flag indicating whether follow-up is required.
- **Follow-Up Notes**: Action items related to the transaction.
- **Is Transaction Approved**: A flag indicating whether the transaction is approved.
- **Approved By**: The name of the person who approved the transaction.
- **Approval Date**: The date the transaction was approved.
- **Is Transaction Reconciled**: A flag indicating whether the transaction is reconciled.
- **Reconciliation Date**: The date the transaction was reconciled.
- **Entered By**: The name of the person who entered the transaction.
- **Entry Timestamp**: The date and time the transaction was entered.
- **Periodic Summary**: A summary of transactions for the period.
- **Total Inflows for Period**: The sum of inflows for the period.
- **Total Outflows for Period**: The sum of outflows for the period.
- **Net Cash Flow for Period**: The net change in cash flow for the period.
- **Balance at Period Start**: The balance at the start of the period.
- **Balance at Period End**: The balance at the end of the period.
- **Variance Analysis**: A comparison of budgeted and actual inflows and outflows.
- **Budgeted Inflows**: The planned inflows for the period.
- **Actual Inflows**: The realized inflows for the period.
- **Inflow Variance**: The difference between budgeted and actual inflows.
- **Budgeted Outflows**: The planned outflows for the period.
- **Actual Outflows**: The realized outflows for the period.
- **Outflow Variance**: The difference between budgeted and actual outflows.
- **Explanation of Significant Variances**: A commentary on variances.
- **Flags and Alerts**: Issues to note about the transaction.
- **Is This an Unusual Transaction**: A flag indicating whether the transaction is an anomaly.
- **Is Follow-Up Needed**: A flag indicating whether follow-up is required.
