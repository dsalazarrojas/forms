# new_client_record_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for new client records, enabling efficient data collection and ensuring that all relevant details are captured accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's information in the first field.
2. Provide contact information for the client in the second field.
3. Add birth information for the client in the third field.
4. Add any additional information for the client in the fourth field.
5. Include any notes or comments related to the client in the fifth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information
* **Client Information (client_info)** (`text`, optional): Enter the client's general details, such as name, address, or any other relevant information.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information (contact_info)** (`text`, optional): Enter the client's contact details, such as phone number, email, or any other relevant contact information.

### Birth Information
* **Birth Information (birth_info)** (`text`, optional): Enter the client's birth details, such as date of birth, place of birth, or any other relevant birth information.

### Additional Information
* **Additional Information (additional_info)** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional information about the client that is not covered in the previous fields.

### Notes
* **Notes (notes)** (`text`, optional): Include any notes or comments about the client that may be useful for reference.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all relevant fields are completed before saving the form.
