# Container Drop Off Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Container Drop Off Log is a form used to track container drop-offs, providing a clear record of each drop-off event.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Navigate through the form, section by section. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of the drop-off event.
2.  Complete each section thoroughly, ensuring all required fields are filled out accurately.
3.  Enter information in the required fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Container Identification Number** (id: 2, `container_id`, required): A unique identifier for the container. This should be entered accurately to ensure the correct record is created.
*   **Container Type** (id: 3, `container_type`, required): A selection of standard container types, such as Standard (20ft), Standard (40ft), High Cube (40ft), Refrigerator (40ft), Open Top, or Other. Choose the correct type from the list.
*   **Drop-off Date** (id: 4, `drop_off_date`, required): The date of the drop-off event. Enter in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Drop-off Time** (id: 5, `drop_off_time`, required): The time of the drop-off event. Enter in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
*   **Vehicle License Plate Number** (id: 6, `vehicle_license_plate`, required): The license plate number of the vehicle used for the drop-off.
*   **Driver Full Name** (id: 7, `driver_name`, required): The full name of the driver who conducted the drop-off.
*   **Driver Phone Number** (id: 8, `driver_phone`, required): The phone number of the driver who conducted the drop-off.
*   **Description of Contents** (id: 10, `shipment_contents`, required): A detailed description of the contents being dropped off.
*   **Shipment Origin** (id: 11, `shipment_origin`, required): The origin of the shipment being dropped off.
*   **Recipient Full Name** (id: 12, `recipient_name`, required): The full name of the recipient of the shipment.
*   **Recipient Phone Number** (id: 13, `recipient_phone`, required): The phone number of the recipient of the shipment.
*   **Delivery Confirmed?** (id: 15, `delivery_confirmed`, required): A confirmation of whether the delivery was successfully completed. Select 'True' for confirmed or 'False' for not confirmed.

## Tips
- Be accurate and comprehensive in filling out each field.
- Review the form carefully before submitting.
- This form is for tracking purposes only; ensure it is used for this purpose.
