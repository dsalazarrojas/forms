# Digital Memory Log For Engineers - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Digital Memory Log For Engineers" form is designed to track and document incidents, near misses, and corrective actions within an organization, enabling the team to learn from them and improve their performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of incident (Incident Report, Near Miss, or Corrective Action).
2. Provide detailed information about the incident, including:
	* Date (Page 1: incident_date)
	* Type (Page 2: incident_type)
	* Location (Page 10: incident_location)
	* Description (Page 11: incident_description)
	* Affected parties (Page 12: incident_affected)
3. Update the status of the incident (Page 7: incident_status).
4. Specify whether the corrective action was effective (Page 9: corrective_action_effectiveness).
5. Indicate whether the incident was reported by someone and who reported it (Page 16: incident_reported_by and Page 17: incident_reporter_contact).
6. Track the progress and closure of the incident, including who updated the status and when (Page 18: incident_status_updated_by and Page 21: incident_last_update_date).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: incident_date
* **Incident Date**: Date of the incident.
### Page 2: incident_type
* **Incident Type**: Type of incident (Incident Report, Near Miss, or Corrective Action).
### Page 3: learning_points
* **Learning Points**: Notes on what was learned from the incident.
### Page 4: incident_details
* **Incident Details**: Detailed information about the incident.
### Page 5: corrective_action
* **Corrective Action**: Notes on the corrective action taken.
### Page 6: corrective_action_completed
* **Corrective Action Completed**: Whether the corrective action was completed.
### Page 7: incident_status
* **Incident Status**: Status of the incident (In Progress, Closed, or Deferred).
### Page 8: root_cause
* **Root Cause**: The underlying cause of the incident.
### Page 9: corrective_action_effectiveness
* **Effectiveness of Corrective Action**: Notes on the effectiveness of the corrective action.
### Page 10: incident_location
* **Incident Location**: Location where the incident occurred.
### Page 11: incident_description
* **Incident Description**: Detailed description of the incident.
### Page 12: incident_affected
* **Affected**: Whether the incident affected parties (True or False).
### Page 13: incident_frequency
* **Incident Frequency**: Frequency of the incident (One time or Multiple Times).
### Page 14: incident_severity
* **Incident Severity**: Severity of the incident (Low, Medium, or High).
### Page 15: incident_lesson_learned
* **Lesson Learned**: Notes on what was learned from the incident.
### Page 16: incident_reported_by
* **Reported By**: Who reported the incident.
### Page 17: incident_reporter_contact
* **Reporter Contact**: Contact information of the reporter.
### Page 18: incident_status_updated_by
* **Last Status Update By**: Who updated the status of the incident.
### Page 19: 
*   (No field description available)
### Page 20: incident_closed_by
* **Closed By**: Who closed the incident.
### Page 21: incident_last_update_date
* **Last Update Date**: Date of the last update.
### Page 22: incident_last_update_by
* **Last Update By**: Who made the last update.
### Page 23: incident_last_review_date
* **Last Review Date**: Date of the last review.
### Page 24: incident_last_review_by
* **Last Review By**: Who made the last review.
### Page 25: incident_last_reviewed
* **Last Reviewed**: Whether the incident was reviewed (True or False).
