# Microdermabrasion Treatment Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record and track microdermabrasion treatment sessions, including client information, session details, and treatment outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client who received the microdermabrasion treatment.
2. **Microdermabrasion Session ID**: Enter a unique ID for the treatment session.
3. **Client Address**: Enter the address of the client (optional).
4. **Practitioner Name**: Enter the name of the practitioner who performed the treatment (optional).
5. **Microdermabrasion Session Start Date**: Enter the start date of the treatment session.
6. **Microdermabrasion Session Duration**: Enter the duration of the treatment session (optional).
7. **Microdermabrasion Session End Date**: Enter the end date of the treatment session (optional).
8. **Microdermabrasion Session Notes**: Enter any relevant notes about the treatment session (optional).
9. **Microdermabrasion Next Session**: Enter the next session date (optional).
10. **Microdermabrasion Treatment Outcome**: Select the outcome of the treatment session using the options "Excellent", "Good", or "Fair".
11. **Microdermabrasion Side Effects**: Enter any side effects experienced by the client (optional).
12. **Microdermabrasion Session Photo**: Upload a photo of the treatment session (optional).
13. **Microdermabrasion Next Session Notes**: Enter any notes for the next session (optional).
14. **Microdermabrasion Treatment Outcome Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the treatment outcome (optional).
15. **Practitioner Signature**: Sign the form to confirm that the treatment session was performed.
16-20 are label fields for the respective fields above.

Please note that fields 16-20 are not editable and are intended for labeling the respective fields above.
