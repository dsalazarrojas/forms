# Summer Intern Timesheet - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Summer Intern Timesheet is a form designed to track summer interns' progress, work hours, and project updates during their internship period.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you're filling out this form for the correct purpose: if you're an intern, you should report your work hours, project updates, and progress. If you're a team lead or supervisor, you're responsible for monitoring and updating the intern's progress.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, including all required fields.
3. Review the form before submitting to ensure you've captured all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required):
	+ Enter the title of the project you're working on.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the start time of your workday (HH:MM format).
* **Finish Time** (`finish_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the finish time of your workday (HH:MM format).
* **Lunch Break Start** (`lunch_break_start`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the start time of your lunch break.
* **Lunch Break Finish** (`lunch_break_finish`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the finish time of your lunch break.
* **Activities** (`activities`, text, optional):
	+ List any notable activities or tasks you completed during your workday.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any specific goals or objectives you've achieved during your workday.
* **Tasks** (`tasks`, text, optional):
	+ List any tasks or responsibilities you've completed during your workday.
* **Project Duration** (`project_duration`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the duration of the project (in hours).
* **Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required):
	+ Select the current status of your project:
		+ Active: The project is ongoing.
		+ In Progress: The project is partially completed.
		+ Completed: The project is finished.
