# Occupational Vibration Exposure Monitoring Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to track and document occupational vibration exposure monitoring logs for employees who are exposed to vibrations during their work hours. Accurate reporting of vibration exposure can help prevent health issues associated with prolonged exposure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if the employee was exposed to vibrations during their work hours (`Employee` field).
2. Provide an accurate account of the job details, including the type of equipment used and any other relevant information (`Job Details` field).
3. Enter the frequency and duration of the vibration exposure (`Frequency`, `Duration` fields).
4. Record the peak levels of vibration exposure (`Peak` field).
5. Note the axis of vibration exposure (`Axis` field).
6. Record the range of vibration frequencies (`Frequency Range` field).
7. Enter the range of acceleration experienced by the employee (`Acceleration Range` field).
8. Note the range of peak levels (`Peak Range` field).
9. List any prophets or supervisors involved in monitoring the vibration exposure (`Prophets`, `Supervisors` fields).
10. Add any additional notes about the exposure (`Notes` field).
11. Select the date and time of the exposure (`Date`, `Time` fields).
12. Assign a unique ID to the form (`Form ID` field).
13. Select the user who filled out the form (`User` field).
14. Select the tool used to monitor the vibration exposure (`Assigned Tool` field).
15. Choose a category for the form (`Category` field).
16. Enter the title of the form (`Title` field).
17. Provide a description of the form (`Description` field).
18. Enter any other IDs related to the form (`Form IDs` field).
19. List any additional prophets involved in the monitoring process (`Prophets` field).
20. List any supervisors involved in the monitoring process (`Supervisors` field).
