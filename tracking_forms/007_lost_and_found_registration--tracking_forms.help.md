# Lost And Found Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a lost and found item.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter a detailed description of the lost item in the "Item Description" field.
2. If applicable, add a photo of the item in the "Item Photo" field.
3. Enter the date the item was found in the "Found Date" field.
4. Enter the location where the item was found in the "Found Location" field.
5. If the item was found and the owner's contact information is known, enter their name in the "Contact Name" field and their phone number in the "Contact Phone" field, and their email in the "Contact Email" field.
6. Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lost or Found Item** (`found_item`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the lost or found item.
* **Item Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the item, including any distinguishing features or characteristics.
* **Item Photo** (`photo`, text, optional): If possible, upload a photo of the item to help identify it.
* **Found Date** (`found_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the item was found.
* **Found Location** (`found_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the item was found.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): If the item was found and the owner's contact information is known, enter their name.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): If the item was found and the owner's contact information is known, enter their phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, optional): If the item was found and the owner's contact information is known, enter their email address.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit the form and register the lost and found item.
