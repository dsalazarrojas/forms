# golf_scorecard_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track golf scores for a round of 21 holes.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the date of your golf game.
2.  Enter the name of the golf course you played on.
3.  Record your scores for each hole, from hole 1 to 18, using the corresponding input field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Golf Game Progress**: This is a text field where you can describe your game progress. It's optional and can be used to note any notes or comments about your game.
*   **Golf Scorecard Date**: This is a date field where you can enter the date you played your game. This field is required.
*   **Golf Course Name**: This is a text field where you can enter the name of the golf course you played on. This field is optional.
*   **Golf Scorecard Hole 1-20**: These are numeric fields where you can enter your scores for each hole. Each field is required and represents the score you achieved on the corresponding hole. For example, enter 4 for a par-4 hole, 5 for a par-5 hole, etc.
    Note: Enter your scores for each hole from 1 to 20.

## Tips
*   Make sure to enter all required fields to ensure accurate scoring.
*   Review your form before submission to ensure accuracy.
*   If you're not sure about any field, refer to the golf scorecard format for guidance.
