# Athlete Training Session Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athlete Training Session Log form is used to document the details of an athlete's training session. This form helps athletes track their progress, provide qualitative feedback, and receive coaching feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as an athlete.
2. Select the date of the training session.
3. Enter the start and end times of the training session.
4. Choose the primary type of training (e.g., Strength and Weight Training, Conditioning and Cardio).
5. Rate your perceived exertion level on a scale of 1 (Very Easy) to 10 (Max Effort).
6. Enter the total volume of the session (e.g., total sets and reps, or distance covered).
7. List the key exercises or drills performed during the session.
8. Provide qualitative feedback on your overall performance during the session.
9. Describe any pain or discomfort experienced during the session.
10. Record any technical focus or key learning points from the session.
11. Set specific goals for the next session.
12. Enter the date of log submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name** (`athlete_name`, required): Enter your full name as an athlete.
* **Date of Training** (`training_date`, required): Select the date of the training session.
* **Session Start Time** (`start_time`, required): Enter the start time of the training session.
* **Session End Time** (`end_time`, required): Enter the end time of the training session.
* **Primary Type of Training** (`training_type`, required): Select the primary type of training (e.g., Strength and Weight Training, Conditioning and Cardio).
* **Rate of Perceived Exertion - RPE** (`rpe_rating`, required): Choose your perceived exertion level on a scale of 1 (Very Easy) to 10 (Max Effort).
* **Total Volume** (`training_volume`, required): Enter the total volume of the session (e.g., total sets and reps, or distance covered).
* **Key Exercises Performed** (`key_exercises`, required): List the main movements or drills performed during the session.
* **Overall Session Feel** (`overall_session_feel`, required): Choose how you felt overall during the session (e.g., Great - High energy, Poor - High fatigue or pain).
* **Pain or Discomfort Notes** (`pain_discomfort_notes`, optional): Describe any pain or discomfort experienced during the session.
* **Technical Focus or Key Learning Points** (`technical_focus_points`, optional): Record any technical focus or key learning points from the session.
* **Specific Goals for Next Session** (`next_session_goals`, optional): Set specific goals for the next session.
* **Coach Feedback or Notes** (`coach_notes`, optional): Enter any feedback or notes from the coach.
* **Date of Log Submission** (`log_submission_date`, required): Enter the date of log submission.
