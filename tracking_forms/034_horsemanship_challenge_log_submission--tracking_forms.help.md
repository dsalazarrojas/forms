# horsemanship_challenge_log_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track and document information related to horsemanship challenges or sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have access to a device with a stable internet connection.
2. Click on the form link or button to access it.
3. Scroll through the form's pages to input your data accordingly.
4. Complete each field as needed, and ensure to fill in required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Session Details
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, `text`, `required`: \*): This field is used to enter any relevant information about the session. Please provide a brief description or notes about the session.

### Rider Name
* **Rider Name** (`rider_name`, `text`, `optional`: ): This field is used to record the name of the rider participating in the challenge or session.

### Trainer Name
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, `text`, `optional`: ): This field is used to record the name of the trainer involved in the challenge or session.

### Challenge Type
* **Challenge Type** (`challenge_type`, `select_one`, `optional`: ): Select the type of challenge or session that took place (e.g., Horse Show, Clinic, Ride).

### Session Date
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, `optional`: ): Enter the date on which the challenge or session occurred.

### Session Time
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, `time`, `optional`: ): Enter the time at which the challenge or session took place.

### Media Uploader
* **Media Uploader** (`media_uploader`, `select_multiple`, `optional`: ): Select the type of media (Video, Photo, Audio) uploaded for the challenge or session.

### Reflection
* **Reflection** (`reflection`, `text`, `required`: \*): This field is used to record your reflection or thoughts about the challenge or session.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, `optional`: ): This field is used to add any additional comments or notes about the challenge or session.

### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, `optional`: ): Record any tools or equipment used during the challenge or session.

### Submitted By
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, `optional`: ): This field is used to record the name of the person submitting the form.

## Tips
- Be sure to provide accurate and complete information in each field to ensure thorough tracking and documentation of the challenge or session.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
