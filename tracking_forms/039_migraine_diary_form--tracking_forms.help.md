# Migraine Diary Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Migraine Diary Form is designed to help individuals track their migraine attacks, including the start and end date, duration, frequency, and symptoms experienced. This form will help medical professionals better understand the user's situation, allowing for more effective treatment and management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the **Start Date** and **End Date** of each migraine attack, using the date fields provided.
2. Record the **Duration** of each attack, using the provided number field.
3. Choose the **Frequency** of attacks, selecting from the available options (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
4. Select all applicable symptoms from the provided lists for **Headache**, **Sensitivity**, and **Visual Disturbance**, and any other additional symptoms you may experience.
5. Enter any **Comments** you may have about your migraine attack, if applicable.
6. Input your **Email** address for communication and follow-up.
7. Enter your **Phone** number for easy contact.
8. Select the **Assigned To** field to direct your data to a specific medical professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Date**: Enter the date when your migraine attack began.
* **End Date**: Enter the date when your migraine attack ended.
* **Duration**: Record the total duration of your migraine attack.
* **Frequency**: Choose how often you experience migraines (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Headache**: Select all symptoms you experience with your headache, such as Numbness, Sensitivity, and Visual Disturbance.
* **Sensitivity**: Select all symptoms you experience with sensitivity, such as Nausea, Vomiting, and Dizziness.
* **Visual Disturbance**: Select all symptoms you experience with visual disturbance, such as Photophobia, Phonophobia, and Aural Disturbance.
* **Other Symptoms**: Input any other symptoms you experience that are not listed, if any.
* **Comments**: Write any additional comments you have about your migraine attack, if applicable.
* **Email**: Input your email address for communication and follow-up.
* **Phone**: Input your phone number for easy contact.
* **Assigned To**: Select the medical professional you want your data to be directed to.
