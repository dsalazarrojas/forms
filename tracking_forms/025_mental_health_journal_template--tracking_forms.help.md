# Mental Health Journal Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Journal Template is a tool designed to help individuals track and monitor their mental health. This template provides a structured framework for users to record their moods, emotions, and progress over time. It's a valuable resource for anyone seeking to better understand their mental health and gain insights into their well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To make the most out of this template, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you understand what information it requires.
2. Complete each field accurately and truthfully, using the options provided or free-text fields as needed.
3. Be as detailed as possible when filling out fields with text options, such as the "feelings" field.
4. For select fields, choose the options that best describe your current state or experiences.
5. Don't hesitate to revisit previous entries to track changes and progress over time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Mood Tracker** (`mood_tracker`, number, required: false)
Record your current mood on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the worst and 5 is the best.

### 2. **Emotions Tracker** (`emotions_tracker`, text, required: false)
Describe your current emotions, including any feelings of happiness, sadness, anger, or fear.

### 3. **Progress Notes** (`progress_notes`, note, required: false)
Record any notable progress or changes you've experienced, positive or negative.

### 4. **Mood Scale** (`mood_scale`, select_one, required: false)
Choose your current mood from the available options: Yes or No.

### 5. **Sleep Tracker** (`sleep_tracker`, number, required: false)
Track your sleep patterns, including number of hours slept.

### 6. **Activity Tracker** (`activity_tracker`, number, required: false)
Record the number of hours spent on daily activities.

### 7. **Feelings** (`feelings`, text, required: false)
Describe any notable feelings or emotions you're experiencing.

### 8. **Energy Level** (`energy_level`, number, required: false)
Rate your current energy level on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is extremely low and 5 is extremely high.

### 9. **Mental Health Status** (`mental_health_status`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select any applicable mental health status from the options: Active or Inactive.

### 10. **Medication Side Effects** (`medication_side_effects`, text, required: false)
Record any side effects from medication you're taking.

### 11. **Social Contacts** (`social_contacts`, select_multiple, required: false)
Indicate the presence or absence of social contacts.

### 12. **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, select_multiple, required: false)
List any emergency contacts or contacts you've made for support.

## Tips

- Be honest and accurate when filling out this template to get the most out of it.
- Use the template regularly to track progress and changes over time.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out for support.
- Consider sharing your progress with a healthcare professional for guidance and feedback.
