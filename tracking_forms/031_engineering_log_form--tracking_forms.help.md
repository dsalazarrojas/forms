# Engineering Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Engineering Log Form is used to track progress and activities of engineers in the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant fields that apply to your activity.
2. Fill in the date and time of the activity.
3. Provide a brief note describing the activity.
4. If applicable, select the disciplines involved (civil, mechanical, electrical, or computer).
5. Enter your email and phone number for contact purposes.
6. If necessary, describe the tool(s) assigned to you for the activity.
7. Click "Submit" to save your log.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Civil Discipline
* **civil_discipline** (`civil_discipline`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if civil discipline is involved in your activity, and "No" otherwise.

### Mechanical Discipline
* **mechanical_discipline** (`mechanical_discipline`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if mechanical discipline is involved in your activity, and "No" otherwise.

### Electrical Discipline
* **electrical_discipline** (`electrical_discipline`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if electrical discipline is involved in your activity, and "No" otherwise.

### Computer Discipline
* **computer_discipline** (`computer_discipline`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if computer discipline is involved in your activity, and "No" otherwise.

### Date
* **date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the activity (YYYY-MM-DD format).

### Time
* **time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the activity (HH:MM AM/PM format).

### Note
* **note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter a brief description of the activity.

### Email
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### Phone
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Tool
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Describe the tool(s) assigned to you for the activity.
