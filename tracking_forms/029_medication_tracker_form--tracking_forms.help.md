# Medication Tracker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track patient medication intake and schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
* Fill out the form for each medication you need to track.
* Enter the prescription information on the first page.
* List the medications and their dosages on the second page.
* Complete the dosage info page to track dosage details.
* On the frequency page, select the frequency of the medication (e.g. Once, Twice, Thrice).
* Enter the last taken date and the start date on separate pages.
* Enter the end date, if applicable.
* Track dosages taken on the dosages page.
* Add any additional notes on the notes page.
* Set reminders and follow-up appointments on the reminders and follow-ups pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prescription Info** (`title`, `prescription_info`, required): Enter the name of the medication and its corresponding prescription information.
* **Medications** (`medication_list`, `medications`, required): List the medications being tracked, one per line.
* **Dosage Info** (`dosage_info`, `dosage_info`, required): Enter the dosage details for the medication.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, `frequency`, required): Select the frequency at which the medication is taken.
* **Last Taken** (`last_taken`, `last_taken`, required): Enter the date the medication was last taken.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `start_date`, required): Enter the start date of the medication regimen.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `end_date`, required): Enter the end date of the medication regimen, if applicable.
* **Dosages** (`dosages`, `dosages`, required): Track the dosages taken.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `notes`, required): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Reminders** (`reminders`, `reminders`, required): Set reminders for when to take the medication.
* **Follow Ups** (`follow_ups`, `follow_ups`, required): Schedule follow-up appointments.
* **Next** (`next`, `next`, required): Set the next appointment date.
* **Call Backs** (`call_backs`, `call_backs`, required): Schedule call backs.
* **Medication** (`medication`, `medication`, required): Enter the name of the medication being tracked.
* **Dosage** (`dosage`, `dosage`, required): Enter the dosage amount.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form for each medication type, as this will help track and manage multiple medications efficiently.
* Double-check the medication names and dosages to ensure accuracy.
* Use the form to track progress and stay on schedule.
* Review and update the form regularly to stay informed about medication intake and schedule.
