# Goods In - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to track goods that have been received into the inventory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date and time of arrival in the "date" field.
2. Enter a brief note about the goods in the "note" field.
3. Enter the email address and phone number of the person receiving the goods in the "email" and "phone" fields, respectively.
4. Select the category to which the goods belong from the options provided in the "category" field.
5. Enter a brief description of the goods in the "description" field.
6. Enter any other relevant details about the goods in the "Goods 1" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Goods In
* `goods_in_date` (date, required): Enter the date of arrival of the goods.
* `goods_in_time` (time, required): Enter the time of arrival of the goods.
* `goods_in_note` (note, required): Enter a brief note about the goods.
* `goods_in_email` (email, required): Enter the email address of the person receiving the goods.
* `goods_in_phone` (text, required): Enter the phone number of the person receiving the goods.
* `goods_in_category` (select_one, required): Select the category to which the goods belong.
* `goods_in_description` (text, required): Enter a brief description of the goods.
* `goods_in_1` (text, optional): Enter any other relevant details about the goods (e.g., quantity, weight, etc.). 

## Tips
* Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
* Check the inventory database to prevent duplicate entries.
* Keep the "Goods 1" field for any other relevant details about the goods.
