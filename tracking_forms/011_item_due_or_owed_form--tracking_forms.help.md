# Item Due Or Owed Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Item Due Or Owed Form" is a comprehensive form designed to track items that are due or owed to the user. It is used to gather detailed information about the item, including the due date, contact details, amount owed, amount paid, and status updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the item's "due date" (field 2) if it is applicable.
2. Provide contact details (field 3) to help us reach you regarding the item.
3. Enter the amount "owed" (field 5) to the user.
4. Enter the amount "paid" (field 6) to the user.
5. Update the status of the item (field 8) with one of the options: "Not Started", "In Progress", or "Done".
6. If additional contact information is required, enter it in the "contact_info" field (field 17).
7. Enter any further status updates (field 19) related to the item.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **item_due_or_owed** (field 1, text, optional): A brief description of the item that is due or owed.
* **due_date** (field 2, date, optional): The date by which the item is due.
* **contact_details** (field 3, text, optional): A space to enter contact information for the item.
* **contact_info** (field 4, select_one, optional): Select one of "Phone", "Email", or "Message" to specify how to contact you.
* **amount_owed** (field 5, number, optional): Enter the amount you owe to the user.
* **amount_paid** (field 6, number, optional): Enter the amount you have paid to the user.
* **status_update** (field 7, text, optional): Enter any additional status updates for the item.
* **status_update_1** (field 8, select_one, optional): Select one of "Not Started", "In Progress", or "Done" to update the status.
* **contact_details_1** (field 9, text, optional): More contact information for the item.
* **email** (field 10, email, optional): Enter the email address for the item.
* **phone** (field 11, text, optional): Enter the phone number for the item.
* **status_update_2** (field 12, text, optional): Another status update for the item.
* **amount_paid_2** (field 13, number, optional): Enter the amount you have paid to the user.
* **due_date_1** (field 14, date, optional): The due date for the item.
* **amount_owed_1** (field 15, number, optional): Enter the amount owed to the user.
* **amount_paid_1** (field 16, number, optional): Enter the amount paid to the user.
* **contact_info_1** (field 17, text, optional): More contact information for the item.
* **contact_info_2** (field 18, text, optional): More contact information for the item.
* **contact_info_3** (field 19, text, optional): Even more contact information for the item.
* **due_date_2** (field 20, date, optional): Another due date for the item.
* **due_date_3** (field 21, date, optional): Another due date for the item.

**Tips**: Make sure to fill out the fields accordingly, taking into account the type of field, such as date fields for dates and email fields for email addresses.
