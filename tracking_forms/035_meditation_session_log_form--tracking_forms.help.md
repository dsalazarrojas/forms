# meditation_session_log_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their meditation sessions. It helps users track and record their practice duration, focus, location, and any other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Click on the "Submit" button to initiate the form.
*   Answer the questions on the first page, which includes:
    *   "meditation_session_log_form_questions": Enter a brief description of your meditation session.
*   Proceed to the next page and answer the questions:
    *   "practice_duration": Enter the duration of your meditation session (in minutes).
    *   "session_focus": Check if you were able to focus during your session and select 'Yes' if you were able to focus, 'No' otherwise.
    *   "session_focus_other": If you were not able to focus, provide a brief explanation for the reason.
    *   "practice_type": Select all applicable types of practices you engaged in during the session.
    *   "practice_location": Enter the location where you meditated.
    *   "meditation_session_log_form_additional_comments": Add any additional comments about your session.
