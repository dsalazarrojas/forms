# Car Maintenance Log Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record important maintenance and service history for your vehicle. It ensures that you have all necessary information in one place, making it easier to identify patterns and potential issues with your vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Vehicle Identification
- Identify the vehicle being logged by providing the correct vehicle make, model, year, and Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
- Enter the correct year of manufacture for your vehicle.
- If you have the 17-character VIN, please enter it for reference.

### Step 2: Service Record
- Enter the date the service was performed.
- Record the mileage reading at the time of service.
- Select the primary service type from the options provided.
- Briefly describe the service performed.
- If any parts were replaced, list them.

### Step 3: Service Provider
- Enter the name of the service provider or shop name.
- Enter the location where the service was performed.

### Step 4: Additional Information
- If you have any additional comments or requests related to the service, please provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Car Make and Model**: Identify the make and model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of manufacture for your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)**: A 17-character code unique to your vehicle for reference.
* **Date of Service**: When was the service performed?
* **Mileage at Service**: What was the odometer reading during the service?
* **Type of Service Performed**: Select the primary service performed from the options.
* **Description of Work Done**: Briefly describe what work was done during the service.
* **Parts Replaced (If any)**: List any parts that were replaced.
* **Total Cost of Service ($)**: Enter the amount paid for parts and labor.
* **Service Provider / Shop Name**: Where was the service performed?
* **Service Provider Location**: Enter the city or address of the shop.
* **Next Service Due Date**: If applicable, enter the recommended date for the next service.
* **Next Service Due Mileage**: If applicable, enter the recommended mileage for the next service.
* **I acknowledge the maintenance log entry**: Indicate your agreement with the entry.
* **Type your name for maintenance log confirmation**: Provide your name for official confirmation.
* **Any additional comments or requests?**: Provide any further details or requests related to the service.

## Tips
- Ensure accurate and consistent data entry to maintain the effectiveness of this form.
- Review previous entries for patterns or potential issues with your vehicle.
- Use this form to stay on top of your vehicle's maintenance schedule.
