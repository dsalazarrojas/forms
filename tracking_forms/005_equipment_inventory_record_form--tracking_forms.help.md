# Equipment Inventory Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track equipment inventory details. It is essential to maintain accurate and up-to-date records for efficient management and maintenance of equipment.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Start by ensuring you have all the necessary information about the equipment.
2. Select the correct status for the equipment from the options provided (Active, Sold, or Offline).
3. Choose the asset location from the available options (Floor 1, Warehouse, or Other).
4. Enter the date of the last maintenance or calibration.
5. Specify the maintenance schedule as noted.
6. Enter the serial number of the equipment.
7. Enter the manufacturer and model number of the equipment (if applicable).
8. Enter the asset tag number.
9. Enter the purchase date of the equipment.
10. If applicable, enter the start and end dates of the warranty period.
11. Enter the vendor's information (if applicable).
12. Add any notes or comments about the equipment status.
13. Add any additional notes or comments about the equipment.
14. Upload any relevant documents or photos of the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **asset_status (Active, Sold, Offline)** (`asset_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the equipment.
* **asset_location (Floor 1, Warehouse, Other)** (`asset_location`, select_multiple, required): Choose the location where the equipment is stored.
* **last_maintenance_date (Date)** (`last_maintenance_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the last maintenance or calibration.
* **maintenance_schedule (Note)** (`maintenance_schedule`, note, required): Describe the maintenance schedule for the equipment.
* **serial_number (Text)** (`serial_number`, text, required): Enter the serial number of the equipment.
* **manufacturer (Text)** (`manufacturer`, text, optional): Enter the equipment's manufacturer.
* **model_number (Text)** (`model_number`, text, optional): Enter the equipment's model number.
* **asset_tag (Text)** (`asset_tag`, text, required): Enter the asset tag number.
* **purchase_date (Date)** (`purchase_date`, date, required): Enter the date the equipment was purchased.
* **warranty_start (Date)** (`warranty_start`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the start date of the warranty period.
* **warranty_end (Date)** (`warranty_end`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the end date of the warranty period.
* **vendor (Text)** (`vendor`, text, optional): Enter the vendor's information.
* **notes (Note)** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any notes or comments about the equipment.
* **photos (Text)** (`photos`, text, optional): Upload any relevant photos of the equipment.
* **documents (Text)** (`documents`, text, optional): Upload any relevant documents about the equipment.
* **equipment_status_comments_1 (Text)** (`equipment_status_comments_1`, text, optional): Add any comments about the equipment status.
* **equipment_status_comments_2 (Text)** (`equipment_status_comments_2`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the equipment status.
* **equipment_status_comments_3 (Text)** (`equipment_status_comments_3`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the equipment status.
* **equipment_status_comments_4 (Text)** (`equipment_status_comments_4`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the equipment status.
* **equipment_status_comments_5 (Text)** (`equipment_status_comments_5`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the equipment status.
* **created_by (Text)** (`created_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who created this form.
* **maintenance_interval (Number)** (`maintenance_interval`, number, optional): Enter the maintenance interval for the equipment.
* **last_calibration_date (Date)** (`last_calibration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last calibration.
* **equipment_status_comments_6 (Text)** (`equipment_status_comments_6`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the equipment status.

Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the equipment inventory records are up-to-date and reliable.
