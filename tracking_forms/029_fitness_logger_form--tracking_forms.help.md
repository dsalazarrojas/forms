# Fitness Logger Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Logger Form is designed to track the user's fitness data, including exercise name, date logged, notes, and other relevant information. This form allows users to log their physical activities, which can be useful for monitoring progress, setting goals, and maintaining a record of their fitness journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Fitness Logger Form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Client Name** to identify the person being logged.
2. Enter the **Date Logged**, which should be the date you completed the exercise or activity.
3. Enter the **Exercise Name** of the activity you completed.
4. Add any **Notes** about your workout, such as equipment used or observations.
5. Enter your **Email** if you want to be contacted for any reason.
6. Enter your **Phone** number, which is not required but can be useful for contacting you.
7. Select the **Category** of the exercise or activity from the list provided.
8. Enter your **Weight** (if applicable).
9. Enter your **Height** (if applicable).
10. Enter your **Age** (if applicable).
11. Enter the weight in **Weight (lbs)** (if applicable).
12. Enter the number of **Reps** you completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name (text)**: Enter the name of the person being logged.
* **Date Logged (date)**: Enter the date the exercise or activity was completed.
* **Exercise Name (text)**: Enter the name of the exercise or activity completed.
* **Notes (text)**: Add any relevant notes about the workout, such as equipment used or observations.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number, which is not required.
* **Category (select_one)**: Select the category of the exercise or activity from the provided options.
	+ Choose from:
		- Strength
		- Cardio
		- Flexibility
		- Endurance
		- Core
		- Power
		- Balance
		- Stretching
* **Weight (number)**: Enter the weight used or achieved during the exercise or activity.
* **Height (number)**: Enter the height of the person being logged.
* **Age (number)**: Enter the age of the person being logged.
* **Weight (lbs) (number)**: Enter the weight in pounds, which is not always necessary but can be useful.
* **Reps (number)**: Enter the number of reps completed during the exercise or activity.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you need help with a specific field, click on the "Hint" button for guidance.
* Double-check your inputs for accuracy before submitting the form.
