# Monthly Lead Update Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Monthly Lead Update Checklist" is used to update and track the status of leads on a monthly basis.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category of the lead from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter a detailed description of the lead.
3. Choose the current lead status.
4. Select the sales stage of the lead.
5. Enter the contact person's name and email.
6. Enter the contact person's phone number.
7. Add any additional notes about the lead.
8. Enter the lead date.
9. Enter the lead time.
10. Select "Submit" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (Category, select_one): Select the category of the lead from the dropdown menu.
* **Description** (Description, text): Enter a detailed description of the lead.
* **Lead Status** (Lead Status, text): Choose the current lead status.
* **Sales Stage** (Sales Stage, select_one): Select the sales stage of the lead from the dropdown menu.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Email** (Email, email): Enter the contact person's email.
* **Phone** (Phone, text): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Notes** (Notes, note): Add any additional notes about the lead.
* **Lead Date** (Lead Date, date): Enter the lead date.
* **Lead Time** (Lead Time, time): Enter the lead time.
* **Form ID** (Form ID, number): Not applicable for end users.
* **Form Output File** (Form Output File, text): Not applicable for end users.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, text): Not applicable for end users.
* **Form Title** (Form Title, text): Not applicable for end users.
* **Form Category** (Form Category, text): Not applicable for end users.
* **Form Description** (Form Description, text): Not applicable for end users.
* **Form Form ID** (Form Form ID, number): Not applicable for end users.
* **Submit** (Submit, text): Select "Submit" to submit the form.
