# Dispatch Checklist For Cutting Boxes - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Dispatch Checklist For Cutting Boxes" form is used to track the status of boxes being prepared for shipment. It captures essential information from both the customer and warehouse teams to ensure a smooth and efficient shipping process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is being requested.
2. Complete each section according to your role and the specific information available to you.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
4. Review and verify the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Cutting Box Dispatch**: A brief description of the box being dispatched.
  * This field should be filled out with a clear and concise description of the box being dispatched.
  * Example: "Box #123, Customer Order 456"
- **Confirm Signature 1**: 
  * Choose "Yes" if the customer signature has been confirmed.
  * Choose "No" if the customer signature is not confirmed.
- **Capture Signature 1**: 
  * Select a time to capture the customer signature.
  * This field is optional and not required if the customer signature is not confirmed.
- **Additional Notes**: 
  * Enter any additional notes or comments about the box or shipping process.
  * This field is optional and not required.
- **Dispatch Date**: 
  * Enter the date the box is being dispatched.
  * This field is optional and not required.
- **Dispatch Time**: 
  * Enter the time the box is being dispatched.
  * This field is optional and not required.
- **Customer Name**: 
  * Enter the name of the customer for whom the box is being shipped.
  * This field is optional and not required.
- **Customer Signature 1**: 
  * Choose "Yes" if the customer signature has been confirmed.
  * Choose "No" if the customer signature is not confirmed.
- **Customer Contact Info**: 
  * Enter any relevant contact information for the customer, such as phone number or email.
  * This field is optional and not required.
- **Warehouse Name**: 
  * Enter the name of the warehouse where the box is being stored or shipped from.
  * This field is optional and not required.
- **Warehouse Signature 1**: 
  * Choose "Yes" if the warehouse signature has been confirmed.
  * Choose "No" if the warehouse signature is not confirmed.
- **Warehouse Contact Info**: 
  * Enter any relevant contact information for the warehouse, such as phone number or email.
  * This field is optional and not required.
- **Box Type**: 
  * Choose "Yes" if the box is of a special type or requires special handling.
  * Choose "No" if the box is a standard box.
- **Box Size**: 
  * Enter the size of the box in the format "Length x Width x Height".
  * This field is optional and not required.
- **Box Weight**: 
  * Enter the weight of the box.
  * This field is optional and not required.
- **Box Weight Unit**: 
  * Choose the unit of measurement for the box weight, e.g., "lbs" or "kg".
  * This field is optional and not required.
- **Box Length**: 
  * Enter the length of the box.
  * This field is optional and not required.
- **Box Width**: 
  * Enter the width of the box.
  * This field is optional and not required.
- **Box Height**: 
  * Enter the height of the box.
  * This field is optional and not required.
- **Box Color**: 
  * Choose "Yes" if the box is of a special color or requires special handling.
  * Choose "No" if the box is a standard color.
- **Customer Notes**: 
  * Enter any additional notes or comments from the customer.
  * This field is optional and not required.
- **Box Condition**: 
  * Choose "Yes" if the box is in good condition.
  * Choose "No" if the box is damaged or in poor condition.
- **Box Status**: 
  * Choose the status of the box, e.g., "Active" or "Inactive".
  * This field is optional and not required.
- **Box Status Date**: 
  * Enter the date the box status was last updated.
  * This field is optional and not required.
- **Box Status Time**: 
  * Enter the time the box status was last updated.
  * This field is optional and not required.

## Tips
- Always review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
- If you are unsure about any field or value, please consult with your team or supervisor.
- The form is designed to capture critical information for both the customer and warehouse teams, so please ensure all relevant fields are filled out.
