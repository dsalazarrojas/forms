# Progress Note - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Progress Note form is used to record and track medical progress in patient visits. This form helps healthcare professionals document and monitor patient conditions, treatments, and visits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the date of the patient's visit in the `date_of_visit` field.
2. Enter the patient's name in the `patient_name` field.
3. Describe the patient's current medical condition in the `patient_condition` field.
4. Summarize the patient's medical history in the `medical_history` field.
5. Specify the current treatment or plan in the `current_treatment` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **date_of_visit** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of the patient's visit.
* **patient_name** (text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name.
* **patient_condition** (text, required/optional): Describe the patient's current medical condition.
* **medical_history** (text, required/optional): Summarize the patient's medical history.
* **current_treatment** (text, required/optional): Specify the current treatment or plan.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter the date of visit in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- If the patient has a medical condition that is not listed, you can add it under the `patient_condition` field.
- Keep the `medical_history` field concise and focused on the most important information.
- If there are any changes to the current treatment, make sure to update the `current_treatment` field accordingly.
