# Stock Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stock Inventory Form is used to track and manage the inventory of items in your stores. This form helps you to store and manage data about each item, including its description, price, quantity, and status. The form is an essential tool for retailers and inventory managers to keep their stock up-to-date and organized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the store and item for which you want to manage the inventory.
2. Fill in the required fields, such as Store Name, Store ID, Item ID, Quantity in Stock, Store Phone, Store Email, and Store Notes.
3. Optional fields can be filled in as needed to provide additional information about the item and store.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Name**: Enter the name of the store that owns the item. (Required)
* **Store ID**: Enter the unique ID of the store that owns the item. (Required)
* **Item Description**: Briefly describe the item. (Optional)
* **Purchase Price**: Enter the price of the item. (Optional)
* **Report Date**: Enter the date when the item was purchased or updated. (Optional)
* **Quantity in Stock**: Enter the current quantity of the item in stock. (Required)
* **Purchase Location**: Enter where the item was purchased from. (Required)
* **Item Status**: Check the status of the item (Active or Inactive). (Optional)
* **Store Status**: Check the status of the store (Active or Inactive). (Optional)
* **Store Category**: Select the category of the store (e.g., Electronics, Clothing, etc.). (Optional)
* **Store Address**: Enter the address of the store. (Required)
* **Item Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the item. (Optional)
* **Store Phone**: Enter the phone number of the store. (Required)
* **Item Tags**: Add tags to the item (e.g., Brand, Color, Size, etc.). (Optional)
* **Item Weight**: Enter the weight of the item. (Optional)
* **Store Email**: Enter the email of the store. (Required)
* **Item Image**: Upload an image of the item. (Optional)
* **Store Manager**: Enter the name of the store manager. (Optional)
* **Item Image URL**: Enter the URL of the item image. (Optional)
* **Store Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the store. (Required)
* **Item Image Alt**: Enter a description of the item image. (Optional)
* **Store Image**: Upload an image of the store. (Optional)
* **Store Image URL**: Enter the URL of the store image. (Optional)
* **Item Price**: Enter the price of the item. (Optional)
* **Store Location**: Enter the location of the store. (Optional)
* **Item Status 2**: Check the status of the item (Active or Inactive). (Optional)
* **Store Status 2**: Check the status of the store (Active or Inactive). (Optional)
* **Store Category 2**: Select the category of the store (e.g., Electronics, Clothing, etc.). (Optional)

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use the optional fields to provide additional information about the item and store.
* Review and update the form regularly to keep your inventory up-to-date.
