# Wedding Gifts Registry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Gifts Registry Form is designed to collect information from guests about their gift preferences and details for a wedding celebration. This form helps the hosts or organizers gather necessary data to create a gift registry, ensuring that guests know what gifts to bring or buy for the newlyweds.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Get Started" button, which will begin the form.
2. Answer each question or select an option according to your preference.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Get Started** (`get_started`, required):
	* Click this button to begin the form.
- **Guest Info** (`guest_info`, optional):
	* Select "Yes" if you are attending the wedding or bring a gift.
	* Select "No" if you won't be attending or don't want to bring a gift.
- **Gift Request** (`gift_request`, optional):
	* Write a note about what gift you plan to bring or want for the newlyweds.
- **Gift Details** (`gift_details`, optional):
	* If you've chosen to bring a gift, provide the gift value.
- **Gift Wish List** (`gift_wish_list`, optional):
	* Select "Yes" if you'd like to receive a gift.
	* Select "No" if you don't want to receive a gift.
- **Gift Value** (`gift_value`, optional):
	* Enter the value or price of the gift you plan to bring.
- **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, optional):
	* Enter the date of the wedding celebration.
- **Wedding Time** (`wedding_time`, optional):
	* Enter the time of the wedding celebration.
- **Wedding Location** (`wedding_location`, optional):
	* Enter the location where the wedding will take place.
- **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, optional):
	* Enter the total number of guests attending the wedding.
- **Gift Sender** (`gift_sender`, optional):
	* Select "Yes" if you will be sending a gift.
	* Select "No" if you won't be sending a gift.
- **Message** (`message`, optional):
	* Add a personal message to the newlyweds.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that the gift registry is accurate.
* Use the "Gift Details" field to specify the gift's value for the host's reference.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask the host or wedding organizers for clarification.
