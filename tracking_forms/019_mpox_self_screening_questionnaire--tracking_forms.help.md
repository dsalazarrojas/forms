> # Mpox Self Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Self Screening Questionnaire is a tool used to track contact history and locations related to Mpox. Completing this form helps public health officials gather information on your contacts, which is essential for tracking and managing public health outbreaks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Answer each question accurately and honestly.
3. If you're unsure about any question, ask for help or clarification.
4. Submit the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Location 1** (`contact_location_1`, required/optional): This field is used to record the location where you last had contact with someone who has been diagnosed with Mpox. Enter the exact location, such as a city, town, or village.
* **Contact History 1** (`contact_history_1`, optional): Describe the nature of your contact with the person who has been diagnosed with Mpox. This could be a social interaction, a shared meal, or any other contact that might be relevant.
* **Contact Location 2** (`contact_location_2`, optional): If you had contact with multiple people, list the second location where you had contact.
* **Contact History 2** (`contact_history_2`, optional): Describe the second contact with the person who has been diagnosed with Mpox.
* **Contact Location 3** (`contact_location_3`, required): List the location where you last had contact with someone who has been diagnosed with Mpox.
* **Contact History 3** (`contact_history_3`, optional): Describe the nature of your last contact with the person who has been diagnosed with Mpox.
* **Contact Location 4** (`contact_location_4`, optional): If you had further contact with someone who has been diagnosed with Mpox, list the location.
* **Contact History 4** (`contact_history_4`, optional): Describe the further contact with the person who has been diagnosed with Mpox.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any question, ask for help or clarification.
* Submit the form as soon as possible to help public health officials track and manage public health outbreaks.
