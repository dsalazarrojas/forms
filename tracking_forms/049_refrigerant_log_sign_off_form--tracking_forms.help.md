# Refrigerant Log Sign Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track the log of refrigerants signed off during a service call. It captures essential information about the technician, supervisor, and service details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information and permissions before completing this form.
2. Fill out the form header with your information as required.
3. Enter the date of the service call.
4. Provide contact information for the technician and supervisor involved.
5. Select the correct refrigerant type.
6. Enter the refrigerant charge amount.
7. Enter the service location details.
8. Set the service date.
9. Enter any additional comments (if applicable).
10. Verify the signature of the supervisor and sign off as required.
11. Enter your contact information for submission purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header** (1) (optional): This field is for additional information related to the form.
* **Date** (2) (required): Enter the date of the service call.
* **Technician Name** (3) (required): Enter your name as the technician performing the service.
* **Technician Phone** (4) (optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Technician Email** (5) (optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Supervisor Name** (6) (required): Enter the name of the supervisor who verified the refrigerant sign off.
* **Supervisor Phone** (7) (optional): Enter the supervisor's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Supervisor Email** (8) (optional): Enter the supervisor's email address for contact purposes.
* **Supervisor Verification** (9) (required): Verify that the supervisor has signed off the refrigerant.
* **Refrigerant Name** (10) (required): Enter the name of the refrigerant being serviced.
* **Refrigerant Type** (11) (required): Select the type of refrigerant used.
* **Refrigerant Charge** (12) (required): Enter the charge amount for the service.
* **Refrigerant Charge Amount** (13) (required): Enter the amount of refrigerant charged.
* **Service Location** (14) (required): Enter the location where the service was performed.
* **Service Date** (15) (required): Set the date of the service.
* **Comments** (16) (optional): Enter any additional comments about the service.
* **Signature Verification** (17) (required): Verify the signature of the supervisor.
* **Signed By** (18) (required): Enter your name as the person signing off the refrigerant.
* **Created By** (19) (optional): Indicate if you are creating this form.
* **Created At** (20) (optional): Indicate the date this form was created.
* **Submitted By** (21) (required): Indicate if this form has been submitted.
* **Submitted At** (22) (optional): Indicate the date this form was submitted.
* **Submitted By User Name** (23) (optional): Enter your name for submission purposes.
* **Submitted By Email** (24) (optional): Enter your email address for submission purposes.
* **Submitted By Phone** (25) (optional): Enter your phone number for submission purposes.

## Tips
1. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
2. Verify the supervisor's signature before signing off the refrigerant.
3. Enter accurate contact information for submission purposes.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it.
5. Save a copy of the form for future reference.
