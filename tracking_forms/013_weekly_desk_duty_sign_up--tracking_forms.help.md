# Weekly Desk Duty Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to sign up users for weekly desk duty. It collects contact information, preferred weeks, time slots, and other details to ensure accurate scheduling and efficient desk management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your contact details, including your email and phone number, in the "Contact Details" section.
2.  Select your preferred weeks for desk duty in the "Preferred Weeks" section.
3.  Choose your preferred time slot from the options in the "Time Slots" section.
4.  Add any additional notes about your availability or requirements in the "Notes" section.
5.  Finally, fill in the "Assigned Tool" section with the name of the tool you are assigned to use during desk duty.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): This section collects your contact information, such as your name and email. Please enter your contact email address to ensure that we can reach you with any updates or instructions.
* **Preferred Weeks** (`preferred_weeks`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the weeks you are available for desk duty. You can choose multiple weeks if you are available more than one week.
* **Time Slots** (`time_slots`, select_one, required): Choose the day of the week you prefer for your desk duty. Please select one of the available options: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, or Friday.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information you think will help us with your desk duty assignment, such as any scheduling conflicts or special requests.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Please enter the name of the tool you are assigned to use during desk duty. This will help us ensure that you have the necessary tool for your scheduled duty.
* **Output File Name** (`output_file_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the output file that will be generated as a result of your desk duty. This will help us identify the output correctly.
