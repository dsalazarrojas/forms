# Customer Package Usage Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Package Usage Log Form is a tool used to record information about a customer's package usage. This form serves as a means to gather data on customer package ID, usage date, hours, and submitter's contact information, among other details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer package ID in the "Customer Package ID" field. This is a required field and must be completed to submit the form.
2. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Select the date of package usage by clicking on the "Date" field. This will open a calendar where you can select the desired date.
4. Enter the number of hours the customer used the package in the "Usage Hours" field.
5. In the "Customer Package Notes" field, enter any additional notes or comments about the package usage.
6. Enter your name in the "Submitter Name" field. This is a required field and must be completed to submit the form.
7. Enter your email address in the "Submitter Email" field. This is a required field and must be completed to submit the form.
8. If applicable, enter your phone number in the "Submitter Phone" field.
9. In the "Submitter Notes" field, enter any additional comments or notes about submitting the form.
10. If applicable, enter your department name in the "Submitter Department" field.
11. Enter your job title in the "Submitter Job Title" field.
12. Select the customer package tracking status in the "Customer Package Tracking Status" field. This field is optional.
13. In the "Submitter Signature" field, enter your electronic signature to confirm your submission.
14. Select whether you have read and agree with the terms and conditions in the "Submitter Agreement" field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Package ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the customer package used. This is a required field.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer who used the package. This is an optional field.
* **Date**: Select the date when the package was used. This field is required and must be completed using the provided calendar.
* **Usage Hours**: Enter the total hours used by the customer.
* **Customer Package Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about package usage.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as the person submitting the form. This is a required field.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address as the person submitting the form. This is a required field.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number as the person submitting the form. This is an optional field.
* **Submitter Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about submitting the form.
* **Submitter Department**: Enter your department name as the person submitting the form. This is an optional field.
* **Submitter Job Title**: Enter your job title as the person submitting the form. This is an optional field.
* **Customer Package Tracking Status**: Select the current status of the customer package. This is an optional field.
* **Submitter Signature**: Enter your electronic signature to confirm submission. This is an optional field.
* **Submitter Agreement**: Select whether you have read and agree with the terms and conditions. This is an optional field.
