# Risk Forecasting And Mitigation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Forecasting and Mitigation Registration Form is a tool used to collect and document information about potential risks that need to be managed and mitigated. This form helps to identify, assess, and prioritize risks, which enables effective risk management and mitigation strategies to be put in place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Risk Forecasting Form (Page 1)
	* Enter a brief description of the risk.
2. Identify the Risk Factors (Page 2)
	* Type in the specific factors that contribute to the risk.
3. Specify the Mitigation Strategy (Page 3)
	* Describe the strategy to be implemented to mitigate the risk.
4. Evaluate the Risk Score and Probability (Page 4)
	* Enter a score for the risk (out of 10) and its probability (out of 10).
5. Determine the Impact (Page 5)
	* Briefly describe the potential impact of the risk on the project or organization.
6. Estimate the Mitigation Cost (Page 6)
	* Enter an estimate of the cost associated with mitigating the risk.
7. Rank the Risk (Page 7)
	* Select the risk ranking (High, Medium, or Low) based on the risk score and probability.
8. Provide a Detailed Risk Description (Page 8)
	* Enter a detailed description of the risk.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Risk Forecasting Form
* **Risk Forecasting Form (Page 1)** (`risk_forecasting_form`, text, required/optional): A brief description of the risk.
### Risk Factors
* **Risk Factors (Page 2)** (`risk_factors`, text, required/optional): The specific factors that contribute to the risk.
### Mitigation Strategy
* **Mitigation Strategy (Page 3)** (`mitigation_strategy`, text, required/optional): The strategy to be implemented to mitigate the risk.
### Risk Score
* **Risk Score (Page 4)** (`risk_score`, number, required/optional): A score for the risk (out of 10).
### Probability
* **Probability (Page 5)** (`probability`, number, required/optional): The probability of the risk (out of 10).
### Impact
* **Impact (Page 6)** (`impact`, text, required/optional): The potential impact of the risk on the project or organization.
### Mitigation Cost
* **Mitigation Cost (Page 7)** (`mitigation_cost`, number, required/optional): An estimate of the cost associated with mitigating the risk.
### Risk Ranking
* **Risk Ranking (Page 8)** (`risk_ranking`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the risk ranking (High, Medium, or Low).
### Risk Description
* **Risk Description (Page 8)** (`risk_description`, text, required/optional): A detailed description of the risk.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to provide concise and accurate information to ensure effective risk management.
* Review and update the risk information regularly to reflect changes in risk status.
* Use the risk ranking to prioritize and focus on high-risk areas first.
