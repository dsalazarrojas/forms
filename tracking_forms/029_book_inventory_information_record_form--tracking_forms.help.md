# Book Inventory Information Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by librarians to record information about their book inventory, tracking various aspects of their collection's condition, including book counts, discrepancies, and preservation needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the inventory record (`Record Date`).
2. Enter your name as the recording librarian (`Librarian Name`).
3. Select the branch where the inventory was conducted (`Library Branch`).
4. Specify the section of the collection being inventoried (`Library Section`).
5. Enter the total number of books counted (`Total Books Counted`).
6. Record any books currently checked out (`Books Currently Checked Out`).
7. Note the number of books on hold (`Books On Hold`).
8. Record any books in processing (`Books In Processing`).
9. Identify books needing repair (`Books Needing Repair`).
10. Mark any books lost or missing (`Books Lost or Missing`).
11. Count any new additions to the collection (`New Books Added This Period`).
12. Record any books weeded or removed (`Books Weeded or Removed`).
13. Specify the method used for the inventory count (`Inventory Method Used`).
14. Record the accuracy rate of the inventory (`Inventory Accuracy Rate`).
15. Indicate if any discrepancies were found (`Were Discrepancies Found`).
16. Enter notes on any discrepancies found (`Discrepancies Found Notes`).
17. Rate the overall condition of the collection (`Overall Collection Condition`).
18. Specify any preservation needs (`Preservation Needs Identified`).
19. Evaluate shelf space utilization (`Shelf Space Utilization`).
20. Make recommendations for improvement (`Recommendations`).
21. Schedule the next inventory date (`Next Scheduled Inventory Date`).
22. Mark if this record requires a supervisor review (`Supervisor Review Required`).
23. Sign with your name as the recording librarian (`Recorder Signature`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Record Date**: The date the inventory record was created.
*   **Librarian Name**: Your name as the librarian conducting the inventory.
*   **Library Branch**: The branch where the inventory took place.
*   **Library Section**: The specific section of the library being inventoried.
*   **Total Books Counted**: The overall number of books counted during the inventory.
*   **Books Currently Checked Out**: Number of books currently checked out.
*   **Books On Hold**: Number of books currently on hold.
*   **Books In Processing**: Number of books currently being processed.
*   **Books Needing Repair**: Number of books requiring maintenance.
*   **Books Lost or Missing**: Number of books that cannot be located.
*   **New Books Added This Period**: Number of new books added to the collection during this period.
*   **Books Weeded or Removed**: Number of books removed from the collection.
*   **Inventory Method Used**: The method used to conduct the inventory count.
*   **Inventory Accuracy Rate**: A measure of how accurate the inventory count was.
*   **Were Discrepancies Found**: Indicate if any discrepancies were found during the count.
*   **Discrepancies Found Notes**: Describe any discrepancies found during the count.
*   **Overall Collection Condition**: Rate the overall condition of the collection.
*   **Preservation Needs Identified**: Identify any preservation needs for the collection.
*   **Shelf Space Utilization**: Evaluate how full the shelves are.
*   **Recommendations**: Make any recommendations for improvement.
*   **Next Scheduled Inventory Date**: Schedule the next inventory date.
*   **Supervisor Review Required**: Indicate if this record requires a supervisor review.
*   **Recorder Signature**: Sign with your full name as the recording librarian.

## Tips

*   Make sure to accurately report any discrepancies found during the inventory count.
*   Be thorough in reporting preservation needs and recommendations.
*   Ensure that your inventory method and accuracy rate are correctly recorded.
*   Sign your name as the recording librarian to validate the record.
*   Schedule the next inventory date and indicate if this record requires a supervisor review.
