# Computer Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Computer Maintenance Checklist" is a form designed to record the maintenance activities performed on computers. It helps track the technician's name, computer identification, maintenance date, tasks completed, software updates applied, hardware checks, issues found, and the recommended next maintenance due date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the technician's name in the "Technician name" field, which is required.
2. Type the computer's asset tag or serial number in the "Computer identifier" field, which is also required.
3. Select the date of the maintenance activity from the "Maintenance date" field, which is required.
4. In the "Tasks performed" field, list the maintenance tasks completed during the activity.
5. If any software updates were applied, provide the details in the "Software updates applied" field. This field is optional.
6. If you've performed any hardware checks, describe them in the "Hardware checks" field. This field is optional.
7. If any issues were found during the maintenance activity, list them in the "Issues found" field. This field is optional.
8. If you've recommended a next maintenance due date, select it from the "Next maintenance due" field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Technician name** (`Technician name`, Required): Enter the name of the technician who performed the maintenance activity.
* **Computer identifier** (`Computer identifier`, Required): Enter the asset tag or serial number of the computer being maintained.
* **Maintenance date** (`Maintenance date`, Required): Select the date of the maintenance activity.
* **Tasks performed** (`Tasks performed`, Required): List the maintenance tasks completed during the activity.
* **Software updates applied** (`Software updates applied`, Optional): If any software updates were applied, provide a brief description of the updates.
* **Hardware checks** (`Hardware checks`, Optional): Describe any hardware checks performed during the maintenance activity.
* **Issues found** (`Issues found`, Optional): List any issues or defects identified during the maintenance activity.
* **Next maintenance due** (`Next maintenance due`, Optional): If a recommended next maintenance due date is available, select it.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete records of maintenance activities.
* For optional fields, provide as much detail as possible to help with future reference and maintenance planning.
* If you're unsure about any field or have questions, consult with your supervisor or the maintenance team lead.
* This form is designed to be used in conjunction with other maintenance procedures and records to ensure consistency and accuracy.
