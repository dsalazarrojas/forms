# Product Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Registration form is used to collect information about a product from the customer, including their contact details, product details, and purchase history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information section, including their name, contact number, and email address.
2. Provide details about the product, including its serial number and any relevant comments.
3. Select the correct contact number from the list of options.
4. Enter the date of the purchase, if applicable.
5. Leave any additional comments or remarks about the product.
6. Click "Submit" to send the registration information to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (customer_info)** (`Name`, `Text`, required): This section is used to collect information about the customer, including their name.
* **Product Details (product_details)** (`Name`, `Text`, required): This section is used to enter details about the product, including any relevant information that the customer wants to provide.
* **Contact Number (contact_number)** (`Name`, `Select One`, required): This section is used to select the customer's contact number from the list of options provided.
* **Email (email)** (`Name`, `Email`, required): This is the customer's email address.
* **Address (address)** (`Name`, `Text`, optional): This section is used to enter the customer's address, which is not required.
* **Purchase Date (purchase_date)** (`Name`, `Date`, optional): This section is used to enter the date of the purchase, if the customer knows it.
* **Product Serial Number (product_serial_number)** (`Name`, `Number`, required): This section is used to enter the product's serial number.
* **Comments (comments)** (`Name`, `Note`, optional): This section is used to enter any comments or remarks about the product, which is not required but may be helpful for our reference.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (customer_info, product_details, contact_number, email, product_serial_number).
* Double-check that the email address and contact number are correct.
* Provide any additional comments or remarks that might be helpful in understanding the product's history.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us directly.
