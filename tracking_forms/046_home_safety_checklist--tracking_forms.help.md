# home_safety_checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The home_safety_checklist is a form used for tracking and documenting building safety checklists. This guide will help you understand how to complete the form and ensure that you are providing the necessary information for building safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the building address in the **Building Address** field.
2.  Enter the name of the building in the **Building Name** field.
3.  Enter the name of the person responsible for the building in the **Manager Name** field.
4.  Select the type of fire extinguisher present in the building from the **Fire Extinguishers** field.
5.  Select the type of smoke alarm present in the building from the **Smoke Alarm** field.
6.  Select the type of fire alarm present in the building from the **Fire Alarms** field.
7.  Select the type of carbon monoxide detector present in the building from the **Carbon Monoxide** field.
8.  Select the types of gas leaks present in the building from the **Gas Leak** field.
9.  Select the types of leak checks present in the building from the **Leak Check** field.
10.  Select the types of leak tests present in the building from the **Leak Test** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Building Address** (1, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the building.
*   **Building Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the building.
*   **Manager Name** (3, text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for the building.
*   **Safety Inspector** (4, text, optional): Enter the name of the safety inspector, if applicable.
*   **Fire Extinguishers** (5, select_one, required): Select the type of fire extinguisher present in the building:
    \- Fire Extinguisher 1
    \- Fire Extinguisher 2
    \- Fire Extinguisher 3
*   **Smoke Alarm** (6, select_one, required): Select the type of smoke alarm present in the building:
    \- Smoke Alarm 1
    \- Smoke Alarm 2
    \- Smoke Alarm 3
*   **Fire Alarms** (7, select_one, required): Select the type of fire alarm present in the building:
    \- Fire Alarm 1
    \- Fire Alarm 2
    \- Fire Alarm 3
*   **Carbon Monoxide** (8, select_one, required): Select the type of carbon monoxide detector present in the building:
    \- Carbon Monoxide 1
    \- Carbon Monoxide 2
    \- Carbon Monoxide 3
*   **Gas Leak** (9, select_multiple, required): Select the types of gas leaks present in the building:
    \- Gas Leak 1
    \- Gas Leak 2
*   **Leak Check** (10, select_multiple, required): Select the types of leak checks present in the building:
    \- Leaks 1
    \- Leaks 2
*   **Leak Test** (11, select_multiple, required): Select the types of leak tests present in the building:
    \- Leaks 1
    \- Other

## Tips
*   Ensure that you are filling out the form for the correct purpose.
*   Double-check the fields to ensure that you have selected the correct options.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or safety inspector.
