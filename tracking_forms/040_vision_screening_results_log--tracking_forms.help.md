# Vision Screening Results Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to log results from vision screenings for patients. The goal is to track the outcome of the screening, including the date and location of the screening, patient contact information, and any additional notes. This form helps healthcare professionals gather and document the necessary information to follow up with patients and ensure their eye health is on track.

## How To Complete This Form

* Fill out all required fields: `screening_outcome`, `screening_date`
* Fill out optional fields as needed: `patient_name`, `patient_age`, `screening_location`, `screening_result`, `notes`, `contact_phone`, `contact_email`, `assigned_tool`

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Screening Outcome** (`screening_outcome`, `select_one`, required): This field indicates whether the screening was successful or not. Choose 'Yes' if the screening was completed without issues, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Screening Date** (`screening_date`, `date`, required): This field records the date the screening was performed.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the patient who underwent the screening.
* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`, `number`, optional): Enter the age of the patient.
* **Screening Location** (`screening_location`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the location where the screening was performed (e.g., 'Yes' for a specific clinic, 'No' for a different location).
* **Screening Result** (`screening_result`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all relevant screening results (e.g., 'Yes' for multiple issues found, 'No' for no issues found).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or information about the screening.
* **Contact Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Contact E-mail** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the contact email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, optional): Choose which tool was used for the screening (e.g., 'Yes' for a specific device, 'No' for other methods).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with a healthcare professional or refer to the relevant guidelines.
* Double-check your input before submitting to ensure accuracy.
