# Mailbox Usage Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mailbox Usage Log form is used to track user access to the mailbox, including the date, time, and purpose of access. This information is helpful for auditing and monitoring purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Purpose Of Access" field to indicate the reason for accessing the mailbox.
2. Enter your "User Id" and "User Name" information.
3. Choose the "Mailbox Access" options to select who accessed the mailbox.
4. Enter the "Date Of Access" and "Time Of Access" fields to record the exact time of access.
5. Enter the total "Duration Of Access" in hours and minutes.
6. Provide any additional "Comments" about your access.
7. Enter your "Email" and "Phone" information.
8. Review and submit the form.
