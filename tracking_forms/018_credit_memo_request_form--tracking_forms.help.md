# credit_memo_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The credit memo request form is used to collect and track credit memo requests for your account. 

## How To Complete This Form

- Please enter your details about the credit memo request. If this is a new request, please fill out this form completely and submit it. 
- If you need to update an existing request, you can use the form to provide additional details or modifications to the existing request. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Details** (`Form Details`, text, required/optional): 
This field allows you to enter a brief description of your credit memo request. 
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required/optional): 
Enter the date you need to process the credit memo request. 
* **Credit Memo Reason** (`Credit Memo Reason`, text, required/optional): 
Enter the reason for your credit memo request. 
* **Credit Memo Amount** (`Credit Memo Amount`, number, required/optional): 
Enter the amount of credit you are requesting. 
* **Original Order ID** (`Original Order ID`, number, required/optional): 
Enter the original order ID for the order you are requesting credit on. 
* **Credit Memo Type** (`Credit Memo Type`, select_one, required): 
Choose the type of credit memo you are requesting: 
  - Credit Memo 
  - Partial Refund 
  - Partial Credit 
* **Order Number** (`Order Number`, text, required/optional): 
Enter the order number associated with your credit memo request. 
* **Account Number** (`Account Number`, text, required/optional): 
Enter the account number associated with your credit memo request. 
* **Billing Address** (`Billing Address`, text, required/optional): 
Enter the billing address for the account associated with your credit memo request. 
* **Street Address** (`Street Address`, text, required/optional): 
Enter the street address associated with your credit memo request. 
* **Apartment/Suite Number** (`Apartment/Suite Number`, text, required/optional): 
Enter the apartment or suite number associated with your credit memo request. 
* **City** (`City`, text, required/optional): 
Enter the city associated with your credit memo request. 
* **State** (`State`, text, required/optional): 
Enter the state associated with your credit memo request. 
* **Zip/Postal Code** (`Zip/Postal Code`, text, required/optional): 
Enter the zip or postal code associated with your credit memo request. 
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): 
Enter the email associated with your credit memo request. 
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): 
Enter the phone number associated with your credit memo request. 
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, required/optional): 
Enter any additional comments about your credit memo request. 
* **Attachment** (`Attachment`, text, required/optional): 
Attach any supporting documents or files to your credit memo request. 
* **Attachments** (`Attachments`, text, required/optional): 
Attach additional supporting documents or files to your credit memo request. 
* **Attachments 2** (`Attachments 2`, text, required/optional): 
Attach more supporting documents or files to your credit memo request. 
* **Attachments 3** (`Attachments 3`, text, required/optional): 
Attach even more supporting documents or files to your credit memo request. 
* **Attachments 4** (`Attachments 4`, text, required/optional): 
Attach more supporting documents or files to your credit memo request. 
* **Attachments 5** (`Attachments 5`, text, required/optional): 
Attach even more supporting documents or files to your credit memo request. 
* **Attachments 6** (`Attachments 6`, text, required/optional): 
Attach more supporting documents or files to your credit memo request.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all relevant fields to ensure a complete credit memo request.
* If you need to attach supporting documents or files, please make sure to include them with your credit memo request.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please feel free to contact the relevant parties. 

Please review this form carefully before submitting, and ensure all necessary fields are filled out. This will help in the processing of your credit memo request.
