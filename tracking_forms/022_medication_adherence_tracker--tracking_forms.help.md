# medication_adherence_tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "medication_adherence_tracker" form is designed for healthcare professionals to monitor and analyze patient medication routines. This form helps track medication regimens, including medication taken, dose frequency, and adherence scores. The form is used to gather data for research, clinical, or administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer each question carefully and accurately.
* Select the appropriate options for each field based on the patient's actual medication routine and adherence.
* Enter relevant information, such as dosage instructions and missed or skipped doses, if applicable.
* Ensure to provide contact information for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **medication_routine** (1): Briefly describe the patient's current medication routine.
* **medication_taken** (2): Report whether the patient has taken their medication (Yes/No).
* **dose_taken** (3): Enter the number of doses taken by the patient.
* **time_taken** (4): Record the time the medication was taken.
* **side_effects** (5): Document any side effects experienced by the patient.
* **follow_up** (6): Schedule a follow-up appointment or note the next follow-up date.
* **medication_schedule** (7): Select the schedule for medication administration (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **adherence_score** (8): Enter a numerical score indicating the patient's adherence to their medication routine.
* **notes** (9): Add any additional comments or observations about the patient's medication routine.
* **contact_info** (10): Provide contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **next_follow_up** (11): Schedule the next follow-up appointment or note the date.
* **additional_comments** (12): Add any further comments or information about the patient.
* **medication_list** (13): Report whether the patient has a medication list (Yes/No).
* **dosage_instructions** (14): Document any dosage instructions or guidelines.
* **missed_doses** (15): Enter the number of missed doses.
* **skipped_doses** (16): Enter the number of doses skipped.
* **reasons_missed** (17): Document reasons for missed doses.
* **reasons_skipped** (18): Document reasons for skipped doses.
* **adherence_issues** (19): Document any adherence issues or concerns.
* **follow_up_notes** (20): Add any additional follow-up notes.
* **follow_up_frequency** (21): Enter the frequency of follow-up appointments.
* **medication_dose_frequency** (22): Report whether the medication is taken with a frequency (Yes/No).
* **dosage_frequency** (23): Report whether the medication has a frequency requirement (Yes/No).
* **dosage_dosage** (24): Report whether the medication has a dosage requirement (Yes/No).
* **dosage_dosage_unit** (25): Report the unit used for dosages (e.g., mL, g, mg).
