# Prg Uhg Weekly Meeting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record and track details about weekly meetings, including attendance, topics, outcomes, and follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are on the correct page (id: 1 - meeting_form_fields).
2. Select whether or not you attended the meeting (id: 2 - meeting_attendance).
3. Enter the meeting topic (id: 3 - meeting_topic).
4. Add any meeting notes (id: 4 - meeting_notes).
5. Enter the start time of the meeting (id: 5 - meeting_start_time).
6. Enter the finish time of the meeting (id: 6 - meeting_finish_time).
7. Select the outcome of the meeting (id: 8 - meeting_outcome).
8. Add notes about the outcome (id: 9 - meeting_outcome_notes).
9. Enter next steps (id: 10 - meeting_next_steps).
10. Select the next meeting date (id: 11 - meeting_next_meeting).
11. Enter the next meeting time (id: 12 - meeting_next_meeting_time).
12. Select whether or not to follow up on the meeting (id: 13 - meeting_follow_up).
13. Add follow-up notes (id: 14 - meeting_follow_up_notes).
14. Enter the number of attendees at the meeting (id: 15 - meeting_attendance_count).
15. Add additional meeting notes (id: 16 - meeting_next_meeting_details).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Attendance** (id: 2): Select 'Yes' if you attended the meeting, 'No' otherwise.
* **Meeting Topic** (id: 3): Enter a brief description of the meeting topic.
* **Meeting Notes** (id: 4): Add any relevant notes about the meeting.
* **Meeting Start Time** (id: 5): Enter the start time of the meeting using the date format (DD/MM/YY).
* **Meeting Finish Time** (id: 6): Enter the finish time of the meeting using the date format (DD/MM/YY).
* **Meeting Outcome** (id: 8): Select 'Yes' if the meeting was productive, 'No' otherwise.
* **Meeting Notes** (id: 9): Add any follow-up notes about the meeting outcome.
* **Next Steps** (id: 10): Enter any next steps or actions to be taken as a result of the meeting.
* **Next Meeting** (id: 11): Select the next meeting date using the date format (DD/MM/YY).
* **Follow Up** (id: 13): Select 'Yes' if follow-up is required, 'No' otherwise.
* **Follow Up Notes** (id: 14): Add any follow-up notes or actions to be taken.
* **Meeting Attendance Count** (id: 15): Enter the number of attendees at the meeting.
* **Additional Meeting Notes** (id: 16): Add any additional meeting notes.
* **Next Meeting Details** (id: 17): Enter any details about the next meeting.
* **Meeting Follow Up Date** (id: 18): Enter the follow-up date using the date format (DD/MM/YY).
* **Meeting Follow Up Time** (id: 19): Enter the follow-up time using the time format (HH:MM).
* **Meeting Outcome Details** (id: 20): Add any details about the meeting outcome.
* **Meeting Follow Up Details** (id: 21): Add any follow-up details.
* **Meeting Follow Up Time** (id: 22): Enter the follow-up time using the time format (HH:MM).
* **Meeting Attendance Count** (id: 24): Enter the number of attendees at the meeting.
* **Additional Meeting Notes** (id: 25): Add any additional meeting notes.
