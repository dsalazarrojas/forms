# Camp Supplies Preparation Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Supplies Preparation Check Form is a checklist designed to ensure that campers are properly prepared for an outdoor experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing each page of the form, which corresponds to different sections of the checklist.
2. For each section, check the boxes accordingly:
	* If the item is present and complete, select "Yes - Present".
	* If the item is missing, select "No - Missing".
	* If the item needs replacement or is in an incorrect condition, select "Needs Replacement" or "Improper".
3. Complete the "Counselor Sign-off" section with your typed name.
4. Record the date of the supplies check.
5. Review the "List of Missing or Needed Items" section to note any items that are missing or need to be replaced.
6. Submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper Full Name** (`check_camper_name`): Enter the full name of the camper.
* **Assigned Group - Cabin** (`check_group_cabin`): Enter the group or cabin number assigned to the camper.
* **Staff Member Conducting Check** (`checking_staff_member`): Enter your name as the staff member conducting the check.
* **Sleeping Bag and Pillow** (`check_sleeping_bag_bedding`): Check if the camper's sleeping bag and pillow are adequate for the current season.
* **Reusable Water Bottle** (`check_water_bottle_reusable`): Check if the camper has a reusable water bottle with a minimum of 24oz capacity.
* **Proper Footwear (Hiking - Athletic)** (`check_proper_footwear`): Check if the camper's footwear is suitable for hiking or athletic activities.
* **Sunscreen and Bug Spray** (`check_sunscreen_bug_spray`): Check if the camper has sunscreen and bug spray, labeled with their name.
* **Personal Hygiene Kit** (`check_personal_hygiene_kit`): Check if the camper has a complete personal hygiene kit, including toothbrush, toothpaste, soap, and towel.
* **Weather-Appropriate Clothing** (`check_clothing_weather`): Check if the camper has clothing suitable for the weather conditions.
* **Labeled Medications (If any)** (`check_medications_labeled`): Check if the camper has any medications, and if so, they are in their original pharmacy container with a label.
* **Overall Preparation Status** (`check_gear_status_overall`): Select the overall status of the camper's preparation.
* **List of Missing or Needed Items** (`supplies_missing_items_list`): Record any items that are missing or need to be replaced.
* **Counselor Sign-off (Typed Name)** (`counselor_sign_off_check`): Enter your name as the counselor conducting the check.
* **Date Checked** (`date_of_supplies_check`): Record the date the supplies were checked.
