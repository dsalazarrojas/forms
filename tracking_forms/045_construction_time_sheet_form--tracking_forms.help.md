# Construction Time Sheet Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Construction Time Sheet Form is used to track employee work hours and pay periods for a week-long period. It includes fields for employee and supervisor signatures, as well as hours worked, pay period start and end dates, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name, ID, and company name in the respective fields.
2. Enter the pay period start and end dates in the "Pay Period Start Date" and "Pay Period End Date" fields.
3. For each day of the pay period, enter the date and hours worked in the "Day X Date" and "Day X Hours" fields.
4. If applicable, enter any overtime hours worked for each day in the "Day X Overtime" fields.
5. Calculate the total regular hours by summing up the hours worked on each day for each day in the "Total Regular Hours" fields.
6. Calculate the total overtime hours by summing up the overtime hours worked on each day for each day in the "Total Overtime Hours" fields.
7. Enter the total hours worked, including regular and overtime hours, in the "Total Hours" field.
8. Fill in any applicable PTO, sick, holiday, or per diem hours taken in the respective fields.
9. Enter any additional information in the "Notes" field.
10. Sign and date your employee and supervisor signature fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID card. (<em>Full name of employee</em>)
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number, if applicable.
* **Pay Period Start Date**: Enter the start date of the pay period. (<em>Beginning of pay period</em>)
* **Pay Period End Date**: Enter the end date of the pay period. (<em>End of pay period</em>)
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company you are employed by. (<em>Employer name</em>)
* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project you are working on.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position. (<em>Position or trade</em>)
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Day X Date**: Enter the date for each day of the pay period. (<em>First day of period</em>)
* **Day X Hours**: Enter the hours worked on each day, including regular and overtime hours. (<em>Hours worked</em>)
* **Day X Overtime**: Enter any overtime hours worked on each day, if applicable. (<em>Overtime hours</em>)
* **Total Regular Hours**: Sum up the regular hours worked on each day. (<em>Sum of regular hours</em>)
* **Total Overtime Hours**: Sum up the overtime hours worked on each day. (<em>Sum of overtime</em>)
* **Total Hours**: Enter the total hours worked, including regular and overtime hours. (<em>Total all hours</em>)
* **Vacation Hours Used**: Enter any PTO taken.
* **Sick Hours Used**: Enter any sick leave taken.
* **Holiday Hours**: Enter any holiday pay taken.
* **Per Diem Hours**: Enter any travel time taken, if applicable.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information.
* **Employee Signature**: Sign and date your name in this field. (<em>Type full name as signature</em>)
* **Supervisor Signature**: Sign and date your name in this field. (<em>Type full name as signature</em>)
