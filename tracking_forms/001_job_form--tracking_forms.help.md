# Job Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Form is a document used to track and record job-related information, including job titles, descriptions, staff names, task titles, start and end times, staff descriptions, materials used, files uploaded, job dates, and completion dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Job Title** field with a brief title of the job.
2. Enter a **Job Description** that provides a brief overview of the job.
3. Provide the **Staff Name(s)** assigned or responsible for the job.
4. Enter a **Task Title** that gives a brief title for the task associated with the job.
5. Enter the **Start Time** and **End Time** for the task, which may be dates in a specific format.
6. Write a **Staff Description** that explains the role of the staff member(s) involved.
7. List any **Materials Used** in the job.
8. Check if **Files Uploaded** are available or not.
9. Enter the **Job Date** for the job.
10. Enter the **Completion Date** for the job.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title**: Enter a brief title for the job.
* **Job Description**: Provide a brief description of the job.
* **Staff Name**: Enter the name of the staff member(s) assigned to the job.
* **Task Title**: Enter a brief title for the task associated with the job.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time for the task, which may be a date in a specific format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **End Time**: Enter the end time for the task, which may be a date in a specific format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Staff Description**: Enter a description of the staff member's role in the job.
* **Materials Used**: Enter any materials used for the job.
* **Files Uploaded**: Check if any files were uploaded for the job.
* **Job Date**: Enter the date the job was completed or took place.
* **Completion Date**: Enter the date the job was completed.

Note: The fields marked as "required: false" are not mandatory and can be left blank if not applicable.
