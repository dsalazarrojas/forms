# Firewood Update Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Firewood Update Submission form is used to track updates of firewood inventory levels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of firewood update.
2. Enter the quantity of firewood.
3. Choose the status of the firewood update.
4. Provide the photo URL of the firewood update (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wood Type** (`firewood_type`, text, optional): 
  Enter the type of firewood update, such as type and size of wood.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): 
  Enter the exact quantity of firewood being updated.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): 
  Choose the current status of the firewood update, including Pending, Delivered, Cancelled, In progress, Shipped, or Received.
* **Photo URL** (`photo_url`, text, optional): 
  Provide the URL of a photo or image associated with the firewood update, if applicable.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the information before submitting.
- If you're unsure about any field, you can always reach out to the team for assistance.
- Please ensure you're submitting only new updates, not duplicates.
