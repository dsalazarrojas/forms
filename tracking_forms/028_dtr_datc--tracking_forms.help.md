# Dtr Datc - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of filling out the Dtr Datc form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Monitoring" field with a brief description of your performance over the month.
2. Enter the time you arrived at work in the "Time-in" field.
3. Enter the time you left work in the "Time-out" field.
4. If you were late to work, enter the reason why in the "Late" field.
5. Enter any overtime hours worked in the "Overtime" field.
6. List any time-related issues you experienced during the month in the "Time-related issues" field.
7. Select an option that best describes your performance overview from the "Get an overview of your monthly performance" field.
8. Finally, click submit to save your performance report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Monitoring** (`monitoring`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your performance over the month.
* **Time-in** (`time_in`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you arrived at work.
* **Time-out** (`time_out`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you left work.
* **Late** (`late`, `text`, optional): If you were late to work, enter the reason why.
* **Overtime** (`overtime`, `text`, optional): Enter any overtime hours worked.
* **Time-related issues** (`time_related_issues`, `select_multiple`, optional): List any time-related issues you experienced during the month, select multiple options if applicable.
* **Get an overview of your monthly performance** (`get_an_overview_of_your_monthly_performance`, `select_one`, optional): Select an option that best describes your performance overview.

## Tips

* Please be thorough when filling out the form, as this data will be used to generate a report on your monthly performance.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the form's description for guidance.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
