# Student Daily Work Hours Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for students to check in and log their daily work hours. It helps in tracking and recording their work hours accurately. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Start Time** when you began your work hours. 
2. Enter your **End Time** when you finished your work hours. 
3. Enter the **Assignment ID** associated with the work you've done. 
4. Type the **Course Name** where the work hours were logged. 
5. Enter the **Work Hours** you've completed. 
6. Add any additional **Notes** about your work hours. 
7. Click the **Submit** button to save your entry. 
8. Enter your **Student ID** to help identify your work hours. 
9. (Optional) Enter the **Form ID** to provide additional information. 
10. The **Created by** field will be automatically populated with the name of the person who submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time when you started your work hours.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time when you finished your work hours.
* **Assignment ID** (`assignment_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the ID of the assignment you worked on.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required/optional): Type the name of the course where your work hours were logged.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of work hours you completed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your work hours.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Click this button to save your entry.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required/optional): Enter your student ID to help identify your work hours.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the ID of the form (optional).
* **Created by** (`created_by`, text, required/optional): This field will be automatically populated with the name of the person who submitted the form.
