# Student Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Attendance Form is used to record student attendance on a particular date and time. This form is meant to be completed by teachers or administrators to track student presence or absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student information (Student Information page).
2. Select the date and time of the attendance (Date and Time page).
3. Enter the location where the class took place (Location page).
4. Identify the teacher conducting the class (Teacher page).
5. Provide class information (Class Info page).
6. Select the students who were present or absent (Students page).
7. Record the student's attendance status (Student Attendance page).
8. Click the "Submit" button to save the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information** (`Student Information`, text, required): Fill in the student's name, class number, or any other relevant information.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Select the date of the attendance.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required): Select the time of the attendance.
* **Location** (`Location`, text, required): Enter the location where the class took place.
* **Teacher** (`Teacher`, text, required): Enter the name of the teacher who conducted the class.
* **Class Info** (`Class Info`, text, required): Enter any relevant class information, such as the subject or topic.
* **Students** (`Students`, select_multiple, required): Select the students who were present or absent.
* **Student Attendance** (`Student Attendance`, select_one, required): Select the student's attendance status (Yes or No).
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required): Click this button to save the form.
