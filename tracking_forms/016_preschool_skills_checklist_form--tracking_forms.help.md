# Preschool Skills Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Skills Checklist Form is designed to track and record the skills of preschool children, providing a comprehensive overview of their abilities and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the age of the student from the provided options.
2. Choose the student's gender from the options.
3. Select the skills that the student has demonstrated or achieved, such as Reading skills, Writing skills, etc.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes about the student's progress.
5. Enter any assigned tool or equipment used for the form.
6. Enter a unique ID for the form.
7. Choose a category for the form, such as Tracking Forms or Reporting Forms.
8. Enter a description of the form, if necessary.
9. Enter the name of the person who assigned the form.
10. Enter the date and time the form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Age** (`student_age`): Select the age of the student from the provided options (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
- **Gender** (`gender`): Choose the student's gender from the options (male, female, other).
- **Skills Checked** (`skills_checked`): Select the skills that the student has demonstrated or achieved, such as Reading skills, Writing skills, etc.
- **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the student's progress.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Enter any assigned tool or equipment used for the form.
- **Form ID** (`form_id`): Enter a unique ID for the form.
- **Category** (`category`): Choose a category for the form, such as Tracking Forms or Reporting Forms.
- **Description** (`description`): Enter a description of the form, if necessary.
- **Assigned By** (`assigned_by`): Enter the name of the person who assigned the form.
- **Created By** (`created_by`): Not applicable in this form.
- **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
- **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`): Enter the date the form was submitted.
- **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`): Enter the time the form was submitted.
- **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the student's progress.
- **Email** (`email`): Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form.
- **Address** (`address`): Enter the address of the person who submitted the form.
- **City** (`city`): Enter the city of the person who submitted the form.
- **State** (`state`): Enter the state of the person who submitted the form.
- **Zip Code** (`zip_code`): Enter the zip code of the person who submitted the form.
- **Skills Checked 2** (`skills_checked_2`): Select the skills that the student has demonstrated or achieved, similar to `skills_checked`.
- **Form Version** (`form_version`): Enter the version of the form submitted.
- **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`): Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
- **Submitted By Name** (`submitted_by_name`): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
