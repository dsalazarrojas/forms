## Purpose
The Roommate Mediation Report form is intended to help users document and track mediation processes between residents and their roommates, providing a clear and efficient way to record and resolve conflicts.

Before proceeding, please ensure that:

1. You are authorized to complete this form.
2. You have all the necessary information to complete the form accurately.
3. The mediation process is complete and the conflict is resolved or has been escalated to a higher authority if necessary.
4. The form is filled out in a calm and objective manner.

This report should not be used to rehash or reiterate past conflicts. If you need to report a new incident or conflict, please fill out a new form.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Choose the Mediation Type

* Select the type of mediation that occurred from the provided options (Conflict, Issue, or Other).

### Step 2: Provide Mediation Details

* Enter the date of the mediation in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* Describe the mediation in detail, including any relevant context or background information.
* Identify the resolution of the mediation, selecting all applicable options (Resident Director, RHA Staff, or Other).

### Step 3: Follow-up Action

* Enter any follow-up actions or next steps that were taken or planned.
* Identify the Resident Director and RHA Staff involved in the mediation.

### Step 4: Record Resolution and Follow-up

* Enter the date the mediation was resolved (if applicable).
* Select the Resident Director and RHA Staff involved in the resolution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mediation Type (Select One)** (`mediation_type`, `Select One`, optional):
	+ Choose the type of mediation that occurred (Conflict, Issue, or Other).
* **Date of Mediation (Date)** (`date_of_mediation`, `Date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date of the mediation in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Description of Mediation (Text)** (`description_of_mediation`, `Text`, optional):
	+ Provide a detailed description of the mediation, including any context or background information.
* **Resolution of Mediation (Select Multiple)** (`resolution_of_mediation`, `Select Multiple`, optional):
	+ Select all applicable options (Resident Director, RHA Staff, or Other) to describe the resolution of the mediation.
* **Follow-up Action (Text)** (`follow_up_action`, `Text`, optional):
	+ Enter any follow-up actions or next steps that were taken or planned.
* **Resident Director (Select One)** (`resident_director`, `Select One`, optional):
	+ Identify the Resident Director involved in the mediation.
* **RHA Staff (Select Multiple)** (`rha_staff`, `Select Multiple`, optional):
	+ Identify all RHA Staff members involved in the mediation.
* **Resolution Date (Date)** (`resolution_date`, `Date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date the mediation was resolved (if applicable).

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when describing the mediation and its resolution.
* Use clear and concise language to avoid confusion.
* If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
* This form is for official documentation purposes only, so please ensure you have the necessary authority and information to complete it accurately.

Please fill out this form with care, as it will help us track and resolve conflicts efficiently, ensuring a safe and respectful living environment for all.
