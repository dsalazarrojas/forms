# Player Medical History - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record the medical history of a player, including their name, birth date, health card number, emergency contact information, and a list of medical conditions such as allergies, illnesses, and previous injuries. This information will help in providing medical care and attention to the player in case of any health-related issues.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the player's name in the "Player Name" field.
2.  Select the player's birth date in the "Birth Date" field.
3.  Enter the player's health card number in the "Health Card Number" field.
4.  Enter the name and phone number of the player's emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" and "Emergency Contact Phone Number" fields.
5.  List any allergies in the "Allergies" field.
6.  List any previous medical conditions the player has had in the "Illnesses" field.
7.  List any medications the player is currently taking in the "Meditations" field.
8.  Describe any previous injuries in the "Previous Injuries" field.
9.  Answer "Yes" or "No" for "Do you have a family history of heart disease or stroke?" in the "Do you have a family history of heart disease or stroke?" field.
10. Answer "Yes" or "No" for "Do you have all of your paired organs?" in the "Do you have all of your paired organs?" field.
11. Answer "Yes" or "No" for "Do you have asthma?" in the "Do you have asthma?" field.
12. Answer "Yes" or "No" for "Do you have a heart condition?" in the "Do you have a heart condition?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required): Enter the player's full name.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required): Select the player's birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Health Card Number** (`health_card_number`, text, required): Enter the player's health card number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the player's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the player's emergency contact.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies the player has.
* **Illnesses** (`illnesses`, text, optional): List any previous medical conditions the player has had.
* **Meditations** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications the player is currently taking.
* **Previous Injuries** (`previous_injuries`, text, optional): Describe any previous injuries the player has had.
* **Do you have a family history of heart disease or stroke?** (`family_history_heart_disease`, text, required): Answer "Yes" or "No".
* **Do you have all of your paired organs?** (`paired_organs`, text, required): Answer "Yes" or "No".
* **Do you have asthma?** (`asthma`, text, required): Answer "Yes" or "No".
* **Do you have a heart condition?** (`heart_condition`, text, required): Answer "Yes" or "No".
