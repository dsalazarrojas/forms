# Weekly Activity Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Activity Log is a form used to track and record activities that have occurred within a club or organization during a week. The form helps to keep a record of events, meetings, and other activities that have taken place, providing valuable information for future planning and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Weekly Activity Log form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the club details, including the club name and any relevant information.
2. Choose the type of activity that occurred (e.g. Activity, Event, Meeting, or Other).
3. Enter the date of the activity.
4. Enter the start and end times of the activity.
5. Provide a brief description of the activity.
6. List the participants involved in the activity.
7. List the attendees who attended the activity.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about the activity.
9. If applicable, enter the email and phone numbers of those involved in the activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **club_details** (`club_details`, text, optional): Enter the name and any other relevant details about the club or organization.
* **Activity Type** (`activity_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of activity that occurred (e.g. Activity, Event, Meeting, or Other).
* **date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the activity.
* **start_time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the activity.
* **end_time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the activity.
* **description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the activity.
* **participants** (`participants`, text, optional): List the participants involved in the activity.
* **attendees** (`attendees`, select_multiple, optional): Select the attendees who attended the activity. The options provided are examples of IDs or names of individuals.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the activity.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): If applicable, enter the email of those involved in the activity.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the phone number of those involved in the activity.
