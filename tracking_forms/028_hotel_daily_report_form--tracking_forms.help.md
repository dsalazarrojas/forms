# Hotel Daily Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from hotel staff about their daily activities and events during their shift. It helps the management to track work hours, identify situations that occurred, and understand the daily activities of employees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by selecting a date and time range for the shift you want to report on**.
2. Click on each "Add Field" button to create a new field for each situation or activity summary. 
3. In each field, enter your observations about the situation, including any relevant details about the activities you performed. 
4. If there's any other relevant information you want to add, click on "Add Field" to create another field.
