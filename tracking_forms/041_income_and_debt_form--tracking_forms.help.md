# Income And Debt Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Income And Debt Form is used to collect information about a person's income and debt status. This data will be used for financial assessment and planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to fill out all fields that are marked as "required" (indicated by a *).
3. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please provide the best answer you can given the information you have.
4. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **income_and_debt_survey** (`income_and_debt_survey`, select_multiple, required: false): This question is a multiple-choice question asking if you have any income and debt obligations.
* **annual_income** (`annual_income`, number, required: false): Enter your annual income (e.g., $50,000.00).
* **monthly_interest_rate** (`monthly_interest_rate`, number, required: false): Enter your monthly interest rate (e.g., 2.50).
* **annual_debt** (`annual_debt`, number, required: false): Enter your annual debt (e.g., $30,000.00).
* **debt_service_charge** (`debt_service_charge`, number, required: false): Enter your monthly debt service charge (e.g., 1.00).

## Tips
* Make sure to answer these questions accurately and honestly to ensure that the financial assessment and planning process is accurate.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please provide the best answer you can given the information you have.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
