# Assignment Completion Check Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to track the progress and completion of assignments in a class. It's intended to help instructors monitor and evaluate the overall quality of submissions, identify common issues, and make informed decisions about grading and due dates.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Name of Person Checking Assignments**: Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the report.
	* **Date of Check**: Enter the date you are performing the check.
	* **Course or Class Name**: Enter the name of the class or course you are checking.
	* **Assignment Name**: Enter the name or title of the assignment being checked.
	* **Original Due Date**: Enter the date the assignment was originally due.
	* **Grading Started?**: Indicate whether grading has begun or not.
	* **Need to Announce Grade Extension?**: Indicate whether the due date needs to be extended.
	* **Overall Quality of Submissions**: Select the overall quality of submissions (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Below Average, Poor).
	* **Students Requiring Follow-up**: List the names of students who require follow-up.
2. Optional fields:
	* **Time of Check**: Enter the time you performed the check.
	* **Total Students in Class**: Enter the total number of students in the class.
	* **Total Assignments Submitted**: Enter the total number of assignments submitted.
	* **On-Time Submissions**: Enter the number of submissions made before the due date.
	* **Late Submissions**: Enter the number of submissions made after the due date.
	* **Missing Assignments**: Enter the number of students who have not submitted the assignment.
	* **Completion Percentage**: Enter the percentage of students who have completed the assignment.
	* **Students with Format Issues**: Enter the number of students who submitted in an incorrect format.
	* **Incomplete Submissions**: Enter the number of incomplete submissions.
	* **File Corruption or Unreadable Files**: Enter the number of files with issues.
	* **Estimated Grading Completion Date**: Enter the date when grading is expected to be completed.
	* **Additional Notes or Comments**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
