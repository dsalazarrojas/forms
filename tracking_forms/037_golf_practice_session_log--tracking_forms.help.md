# Golf Practice Session Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for golfers to track their practice sessions, coaches/instructors to monitor student progress, and club/course managers to collect data on practice sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of your practice session in the "Golf Practice Session" field.
2. Select the type of session you had (e.g., Full Swing, Short Game, Par 3, Putting, or Other).
3. If applicable, choose the areas of focus for your practice (e.g., Grip, Stance, Flexibility, Strength, Power, Accuracy).
4. Evaluate your self-assessment of your performance by selecting from the provided options (High, Medium, Low).
5. Add any additional notes about your session in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Golf Practice Session**: A date field where you can enter the date of your practice session.
  (Type: date)
- **Session Type**: Select the type of session you had (e.g., Full Swing, Short Game, Par 3, Putting, or Other).
  (Type: select_one, Required: false)
- **Focus Area**: Select one or more areas of focus for your practice (e.g., Grip, Stance, Flexibility, Strength, Power, Accuracy).
  (Type: select_multiple, Required: false)
- **Self-Assessment**: Evaluate your performance level (High, Medium, Low).
  (Type: select_one, Required: false)
- **Notes**: Add any notes about your session, e.g., what you achieved, challenges you faced, or areas to improve.
  (Type: note, Required: false)

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when selecting the session type and areas of focus.
- Use the "Notes" field to capture any relevant details about your session.
- This form is designed to be a flexible tool, feel free to add or modify fields as needed to suit your specific needs.
