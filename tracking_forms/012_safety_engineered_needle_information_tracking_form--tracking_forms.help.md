# safety_engineered_needle_information_tracking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a structured way of tracking essential details related to safety-engineered needles. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Select the correct needle type from the options provided in the "Needle Type" field.
2. Enter the manufacturer's name and any other relevant information in the "Manufacturer" field.
3. Add any relevant handling information in the "Handling Information" field.
4. Select the safety engineering status of the needle in the "Safety Engineering Status" field.
5. Enter the storage location of the needle in the "Storage Location" field.
6. Add any special handling instructions for the needle in the "Special Handling Instructions" field.
7. Enter the expiration date of the needle in the "Expiration Date" field.
8. Enter the next due date for the needle in the "Next Due Date" field.
9. Select or enter the last updated date of the needle in the "Last Updated" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Needle Type (<label>)
* **Needle Type** (`<name>`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of needle from the options provided.

### Manufacturer (<label>)
* **Manufacturer** (`<name>`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the manufacturer of the needle.

### Handling Information (<label>)
* **Handling Information** (`<name>`, `note`, optional): Enter any relevant information about the handling of the needle.

### Safety Engineering Status (<label>)
* **Safety Engineering Status** (`<name>`, `select_one`, optional): Select the safety engineering status of the needle from the options provided.

### Storage Location (<label>)
* **Storage Location** (`<name>`, `text`, optional): Enter the storage location of the needle.

### Special Handling Instructions (<label>)
* **Special Handling Instructions** (`<name>`, `text`, optional): Add any special handling instructions for the needle.

### Expiration Date (<label>)
* **Expiration Date** (`<name>`, `date`, optional): Enter the expiration date of the needle.

### Next Due Date (<label>)
* **Next Due Date** (`<name>`, `date`, optional): Enter the next due date for the needle.

### Last Updated (<label>)
* **Last Updated** (`<name>`, `date`, optional): Select or enter the last updated date of the needle.
