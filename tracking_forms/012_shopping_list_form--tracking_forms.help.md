# Shopping List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shopping List Form is designed to gather information about items on a shopping list, including item details, purchase date and time, and store information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the **Item Name** field with a brief description of the item.
* Select the correct **Store** where the item was purchased or will be purchased.
* Enter the **Purchase Date** and **Purchase Time** of the item.
* Select whether each item is currently on the **Store List** for each relevant store.
