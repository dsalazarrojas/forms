# Smart Sensor Calibration Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and record details of smart sensor calibrations and other sensor-related maintenance activities.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with all required and optional fields as applicable.
- Select the correct sensor type from the options provided.
- Enter the sensor's serial number, model, reading date, and reading time.
- Record the sensor reading value and any relevant notes or comments.
- Provide the technician's signature and name.
- Enter the department's signature and comments (if applicable).
- Review and update the calibration status and results accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Sensor Type** (select_one):
  - Select the type of sensor being calibrated from the provided options.
- **Sensor Serial Number** (text):
  - Enter the unique serial number of the sensor.
- **Sensor Model** (text):
  - Enter the model number or name of the sensor.
- **Sensor Reading Date** (date):
  - Enter the date the sensor reading was taken.
- **Sensor Reading Time** (time):
  - Enter the time the sensor reading was taken.
- **Sensor Reading Value** (number):
  - Enter the value obtained from the sensor reading.
- **Notes** (note):
  - Record any relevant notes or comments about the calibration.
- **Email Address** (email):
  - Enter the email address of the person or department responsible for the calibration.
- **Phone Number** (text):
  - Enter the phone number of the person or department responsible for the calibration.
- **Technician Signature** (text):
  - Sign and date the calibration record.
- **Technician Name** (text):
  - Enter the name of the technician performing the calibration.
- **Department** (text):
  - Enter the department or team responsible for the calibration.
- **Equipment Number** (text):
  - Enter the unique identifier for the equipment being calibrated.
- **Equipment Type** (select_multiple):
  - Select all applicable equipment types from the provided options.
- **Calibration Result** (select_one):
  - Select the result of the calibration from the provided options.
- **Calibration Date** (date):
  - Enter the date the calibration was performed.
- **Next Calibration Date** (date):
  - Enter the date the next calibration is due.
- **Comments** (note):
  - Record any relevant comments or notes about the calibration.
- **Calibration Status** (select_multiple):
  - Select the current status of the calibration from the provided options.
- **Calibration Result Status** (select_one):
  - Select the status of the calibration result from the provided options.
- **Comments Status** (select_one):
  - Select the status of the comments from the provided options.
- **Equipment Location** (text):
  - Enter the location where the equipment is situated.
- **Technician Comments** (text):
  - Record any comments from the technician performing the calibration.
- **Department Comments** (text):
  - Record any comments from the department or team responsible for the calibration.
- **Department Signature** (text):
  - Sign and date the calibration record.
