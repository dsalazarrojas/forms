# Production Quality Scorecard Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report on the production quality of a specific product or product variant. It captures information about the inspection process, including quality criteria, photos, and ratings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant production quality scorecard report form page from the dropdown options.
2. Choose the inspection criteria (e.g., 1, 2, 3, or 4) that was used during the inspection process.
3. Provide any relevant photos taken during the inspection.
4. Choose the production shift or batch that the inspection was performed on.
5. Enter any comments or observations made during the inspection.
6. Rate the production quality on a scale of 1-5.
7. Enter a score for the production quality.
8. Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Production Quality Scorecard Report Form Page** (`1`, `note`, required/optional): Select the relevant report form page for the inspection process.
* **Production Quality Scorecard Report Form Page Inspections** (`2`, `note`, required/optional): This field is used to capture any notes or comments made during the inspection process.
* **Production Quality Scorecard Report Form Page Quality Criteria** (`3`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the inspection criteria that was used during the inspection process. Options: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.
* **Production Quality Scorecard Report Form Page Photos** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Upload any photos taken during the inspection process.
* **Production Quality Scorecard Report Form Page Shift or Batch** (`5`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the production shift or batch that the inspection was performed on. Options: Product, Shift, Batch.
* **Production Quality Scorecard Report Form Page Inspection Comments** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any comments or observations made during the inspection process.
* **Production Quality Scorecard Report Form Page Rating** (`7`, `number`, required/optional): Rate the production quality on a scale of 1-5.
* **Production Quality Scorecard Report Form Page Score** (`8`, `number`, required/optional): Enter a score for the production quality.
* **Production Quality Scorecard Report Form Page Notes** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection process.
* **Production Quality Scorecard Report Form Page Additional Comments** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or observations that may be relevant to the inspection process.
