# HR Issue Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Issue Tracking Form is a tool used to record and track HR issues within the organization. It helps in maintaining an organized and centralized record of all HR issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the contact type (Issue, Concern, Complaint, or Other) that best describes the HR issue.
2. Choose the division and problem type relevant to the issue.
3. Identify the employee(s) involved in the issue.
4. Provide a clear and concise description of the problem.
5. Specify the investigation department responsible for handling the issue.
6. Enter the submitter's name and contact information.
7. Choose the status of the issue (Open, In Progress, or Resolved).
8. Select the date and time the issue was submitted.
9. Enter additional comments and attachments (if any).
10. Assign a priority level (High, Medium, or Low) to the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee who reported the issue or is affected by the issue.
* **Contact Type**: Choose the type of contact that best describes the issue (Issue, Concern, Complaint, or Other).
* **Division**: Select the division related to the issue (e.g., Finance, HR, Operations, etc.).
* **Problem Type**: Choose the type of problem relevant to the issue (e.g., Recruiting, Hiring, Training, etc.).
* **Employee**: Select the employee(s) involved in the issue (or the employee who reported the issue).
* **Problem Description**: Provide a clear and concise description of the problem.
* **Investigation Department**: Specify the department responsible for handling the issue.
* **Submitter**: Enter the name and contact information of the person submitting the issue.
* **Submitted On**: Enter the date and time the issue was submitted.
* **Status**: Choose the status of the issue (Open, In Progress, Resolved).
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the issue.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the issue.
* **Category**: Select the category relevant to the issue (e.g., Recruiting, Hiring, Training, etc.). (Note: This field is optional.)
* **Subcategory**: Provide a more specific category for the issue (if desired).
* **Issue Date**: Enter the date the issue occurred.
* **Issue Time**: Enter the time the issue occurred (if desired).
* **Assigned To**: Select the employee(s) responsible for resolving the issue.
* **Resolution Status**: Choose the status of the resolution (Open, In Progress, Resolved).
* **Resolution Date**: Enter the date the issue was resolved (if resolved).
* **Resolution Time**: Enter the time the issue was resolved (if resolved).
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the issue.
* **Attachments**: Attach any relevant files or documents to the issue.
* **Tags**: Assign a tag to the issue to indicate its priority (High, Low, Medium).
* **Priority**: Choose the priority level of the issue (High, Medium, Low).

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing the issue.
* Avoid using jargon or technical terms that may be unfamiliar to others.
* Ensure that the issue is accurately categorized and prioritized.
* Use attachments and comments to provide more context about the issue.
* Review and update the issue as necessary to reflect its status and resolution.
