# COVID-19 AthleteCoach Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track the health status of athletes, coaches, and staff members in relation to COVID-19, helping to identify potential cases and mitigate any risks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Monitoring Date" field and choose the date you are filling out the form.
2. Choose your "Person Type" from the options: Athlete, Coach, or Staff.
3. Enter your "Full Name" and "Team Name" accurately.
4. Select your "Sport or Activity".
5. If applicable, provide your "Phone Number" and "Email Address" for contact purposes.
6. Answer the question about whether you have a fever today ("Do you have a fever today?"), and if yes, enter your body temperature.
7. Report any other symptoms, such as new or worsening cough, shortness of breath, loss of taste or smell, muscle aches, headache, sore throat, nausea or vomiting, or diarrhea.
8. Answer the question about COVID-19 exposure in the past 14 days, and select the correct option (Yes, No, or Not sure).
9. If you've been tested recently, select your COVID-19 test status (Never tested, Tested - Positive, Tested - Negative, Tested - Pending Results, or Prefer not to answer).
10. Provide your vaccination status (Fully vaccinated, Partially vaccinated, Not vaccinated, or Prefer not to answer).
11. Report your current status (Cleared to participate, Restricted participation, Quarantine - stay home, Isolate - medical supervision).
12. If restricted or isolated, estimate the date you will return to play.
13. Enter any additional notes or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Monitoring Date**: Date of health status check (required)
* **Person Type**: Athlete, Coach, or Staff (required)
* **Full Name**: Your name (required)
* **Team Name**: Your team name (required)
* **Sport or Activity**: Select your sport or activity (required)
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number (optional)
* **Email Address**: Your contact email (optional)
* **Fever Symptoms**: Do you have a fever today? (required)
	+ Yes: Enter body temperature in Fahrenheit or Celsius
	+ No: Select "No"
* **Fever Temperature**: If you have a fever, enter your body temperature (optional)
* **New or Worsening Cough**: Do you have a new or worsening cough? (required)
* **Shortness of Breath**: Do you have shortness of breath? (required)
* **Loss of Taste or Smell**: Have you lost sense of taste or smell? (required)
* **Fatigue**: Do you feel excessive fatigue or tiredness? (optional)
* **Muscle Aches**: Do you have muscle or body aches? (optional)
* **Headache**: Do you have a headache? (optional)
* **Sore Throat**: Do you have a sore throat? (optional)
* **Nausea or Vomiting**: Do you have nausea or vomiting? (optional)
* **Diarrhea**: Do you have diarrhea? (optional)
* **COVID-19 Exposure**: Have you been exposed to someone with COVID-19 in the past 14 days? (required)
* **COVID-19 Test Status**: If you've been tested recently, select your status (optional)
* **Vaccination Status**: Select your vaccination status (optional)
* **Current Status**: Report your current status (required)
* **Return to Play Date**: Estimated date to return to play (if restricted or isolated, optional)
* **Additional Notes or Concerns**: Any other health information to report (optional)

Please fill out this form accurately and truthfully, and report any changes or updates as needed. If you are unsure about any question, please select "Not sure" and report your concerns to the relevant authorities.
