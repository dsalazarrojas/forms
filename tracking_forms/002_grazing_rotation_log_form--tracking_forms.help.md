# Grazing Rotation Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Grazing Rotation Log Form is designed to gather information from users about their grazing activities and interactions with animals. This form is used to track user inputs, rotation details, and notes for grazing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **User Input** field with your name or username.
2. Provide details about the rotation in the **Rotation Details** field.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments in the **Notes** field.
4. Select your **User Confirmation** by choosing one of the provided options (Agree, Disagree, or Not Selected).
5. Choose one or more people you want to assign the rotation to from the list in the **Assigned To** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter your name or username to identify yourself as the user.
* **Rotation Details** (`rotation_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the rotation, including any relevant details such as date, time, and location.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the rotation.
* **User Confirmation** (`user_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement or disagreement with the rotation by selecting one of the provided options (Agree, Disagree, or Not Selected).
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more individuals to assign the rotation to from the list of provided options (chatjimmy, JohnDoe, JaneDoe).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields (User Confirmation and Assigned To).
* Use clear and concise language in the User Input and Rotation Details fields.
* Use the Notes field to provide additional context or information about the rotation.
* Select your User Confirmation based on your actual agreement or disagreement with the rotation.
* Be mindful of who you assign the rotation to and select all relevant individuals.
