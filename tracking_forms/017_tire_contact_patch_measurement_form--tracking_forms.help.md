# Tire Contact Patch Measurement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and document tire contact patch measurements for vehicles. It collects information about the vehicle, test location, and measurement details for each patch.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the vehicle information section with the required details.
2. Select the relevant test location where the measurement was taken.
3. Choose the correct technician who performed the measurement.
4. Record the date and time of the measurement.
5. Enter the patch measurement values for each tire.
6. Add any additional notes about the measurement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Tire Size** (`tire_size`, select_one): Select the size of the tire.
* **Test Location** (`test_location`, select_multiple): Choose where the test was performed (e.g., shop1, shop2, shop3).
* **Technician Name** (`technician_name`, text): Enter the name of the technician who performed the measurement.
* **Patch Measurement 1** (`patch_measurement_1`, number): Record the measurement value for the first patch.
* **Patch Measurement 2** (`patch_measurement_2`, number): Record the measurement value for the second patch.
* **Patch Measurement 3** (`patch_measurement_3`, number): Record the measurement value for the third patch.
* **Date Measured** (`date_measured`, date): Enter the date the measurement was taken.
* **Time Measured** (`time_measured`, time): Enter the time the measurement was taken.
* **Measured By** (`measured_by`, select_one): Select the technician who performed the measurement (e.g., chatjimmy, other technician).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text): Add any additional notes about the measurement.
