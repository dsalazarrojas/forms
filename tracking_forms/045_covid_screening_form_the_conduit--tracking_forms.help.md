# COVID Screening Form The Conduit - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect and assess information about an individual's health status, specifically for COVID-19 screening. It's designed to identify potential risks and provide recommendations for further actions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete the form accurately and honestly to ensure the screening process is effective.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please ask for assistance from the screener.
- The form is divided into several sections for easy reference:
  1. Participant Information
  2. COVID-19 Symptom Screening
  3. Exposure Assessment
  4. Test Results
  5. Screening Result and Recommendations

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (conduit_participant_name, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (conduit_email, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (conduit_phone, phone, optional): Provide your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Screening Date** (conduit_screening_date, date, required): Enter the date of this screening.
* **Organization or Department** (conduit_organization, text, optional): Enter your organization or department name if applicable.
* **Do You Have a Fever** (conduit_fever, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you have a temperature of 100.4F or higher.
* **Do You Have a Cough** (conduit_cough, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you have a new or persistent cough.
* **Difficulty Breathing** (conduit_difficulty_breathing, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you experience shortness of breath.
* **Loss of Taste or Smell** (conduit_loss_taste_smell, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you have a new loss of taste or smell.
* **Muscle or Body Aches** (conduit_muscle_pain, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you experience unusual muscle aches.
* **Headache** (conduit_headache, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you have an unusual headache.
* **Sore Throat** (conduit_sore_throat, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you have throat pain or irritation.
* **Nausea, Vomiting, or Diarrhea** (conduit_nausea_diarrhea, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you experience gastrointestinal symptoms.
* **Close Contact with Confirmed Case** (conduit_close_contact, select_one, required): Answer "Yes" if you've had close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case within 14 days or 48 hours.
* **If Exposed, Provide Contact Details** (conduit_contact_details, text, optional): Provide details about your exposure if you answered "Yes" to the previous question.
* **Have You Tested Positive for COVID-19** (conduit_tested_positive, select_one, required): Answer "Recently tested positive" if you've tested positive for COVID-19.
* **Date of Positive Test** (conduit_positive_test_date, date, optional): Enter the date of your positive test, if applicable.
* **Date of Recovery if Applicable** (conduit_recovery_date, date, optional): Enter the date of your recovery if you've previously tested positive.
* **Screening Result** (conduit_screening_result, select_one, required): Based on your responses, this field will be auto-filled with the recommended screening result.
* **Recommended Actions** (conduit_recommendations, text, optional): Read and follow the recommendations provided based on your screening result.
* **Screening Completed By** (conduit_screening_completed, text, optional): This field will be auto-filled with the name of the screener.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your responses to ensure effective screening.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please ask for assistance.
- The screening process is designed to protect you and others from potential risks.
