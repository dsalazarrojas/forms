# PCI Preparation And Outcome Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PCI Preparation And Outcome Log is a form used to document the preparation and outcome of a PCI procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the preparation steps taken in the "Preparation Steps" field.
2. Provide details about the procedure performed in the "Procedure Details" field.
3. Select the date the procedure was performed from the "Date" field.
4. Choose the time zone of the procedure from the "Time Zone" field.
5. Select the tool used for the procedure from the "Assigned Tool" field.
6. Indicate whether the form was submitted by you or not in the "Submitted By" field.
7. Add any comments about the procedure in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Preparation Steps** (`preparation_steps`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the steps taken during the preparation phase.
* **Procedure Details** (`procedure_details`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the procedure performed.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date the procedure was performed.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time the procedure was performed.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, select_one, optional): Choose the time zone of the procedure.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool used for the procedure.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the form was submitted by you.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the procedure.
