# Product Aging Monitoring Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Product Aging Monitoring Form is designed to collect data on products as they age. This form helps in tracking and monitoring the product information, merchandiser details, and signature processes associated with the products.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, ensure you have all the required information about the product and merchandiser.
2. Select the relevant details from the available options in each field.
3. Complete the "Product Information" field with the exact number.
4. For the "Signature of Submitter" and "Merchandiser Signature" fields, select the applicable option(s) from the given choices.
5. If required, enter the signature date and time using the provided date and time input fields.
6. In the "Remarks" field, provide any additional comments or observations about the product.
7. Lastly, confirm your signature in the "Signature of Submitter" and "Merchandiser Signature" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Merchandiser Details (Page 1)**: Enter a brief description of the merchandiser, including any relevant details.
* **Product Information (Page 2)**: Enter the exact number of the product you are tracking. This field is mandatory.
* **Remarks (Page 3)**: Provide any additional comments or observations about the product, if necessary.
* **Signature of Submitter (Page 4)**: Select your signature from the available options. This field is mandatory.
* **Merchandiser Signature (Page 5)**: Select the merchandiser's signature from the provided options. This field is mandatory.
* **Signature Date (Page 6)**: If required, enter the date of your signature using the date input field. This field is optional.
* **Signature Time (Page 7)**: If required, enter the time of your signature using the time input field. This field is optional.
* **Remarks Signature (Page 8)**: Enter any additional comments or observations related to your signature in this field. This field is optional.
