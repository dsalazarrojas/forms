This is the purpose of the Isolation And Absence Tracking Form: to record and track student absences or isolations due to COVID-19 or other reasons, allowing teachers and staff to keep accurate records of student attendance and potential exposure risks. This form is designed to be simple and user-friendly, making it easy for teachers and staff to quickly input relevant information while minimizing data entry burden. ## Purpose
The Isolation And Absence Tracking Form is used to record and track student absences or isolations due to COVID-19 or other reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the **student_name** field.
2. Enter the teacher's name in the **teacher_name** field.
3. Select the start time of the absence or isolation period in the **start_time** field.
4. Select the end time of the absence or isolation period in the **end_time** field.
5. Select the start date of the absence or isolation period in the **start_date** field.
6. Select the end date of the absence or isolation period in the **end_date** field.
7. Enter a brief reason for the absence or isolation in the **reason** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student who is absent or isolated.
* **teacher_name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the teacher who reported the absence or isolation.
* **start_time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Select the start time of the absence or isolation period. 
* **end_time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Select the end time of the absence or isolation period. 
* **start_date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Select the start date of the absence or isolation period. 
* **end_date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Select the end date of the absence or isolation period. 
* **reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Enter a brief reason for the absence or isolation. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the form captures all relevant information. 
* If the student's absence or isolation is ongoing, you can leave the end date field blank. 
* If you need to edit or update an existing form, you can simply modify the relevant fields as needed. 

Note: This form is designed to be adaptable to your specific institution's needs, so please modify the fields or the form as a whole as needed to suit your institution's requirements.
