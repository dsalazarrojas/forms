# Weekly Timesheet And Progress Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and report progress on a weekly basis. It is designed to capture information on daily work and working hours for two different tasks or projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and submit the form as required.
2. Fill in the daily work and working hours for two different tasks or projects.
3. Enter any comments or remarks about your progress.
4. Enter the date you submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Daily work 1** (`daily_work_1`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the work done on the first task or project for the day.
* **Working hours 1** (`working_hours_1`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of hours worked on the first task or project for the day.
* **Daily work 2** (`daily_work_2`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the work done on the second task or project for the day.
* **Working hours 2** (`working_hours_2`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of hours worked on the second task or project for the day.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or remarks about your progress.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, `text`, optional): Enter your name.
* **Submitted at** (`submitted_at`, `date`, optional): Enter the date and time you submitted the form.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or manager for clarification.
- Please be concise and clear in your answers to ensure accurate tracking and reporting of your progress.
