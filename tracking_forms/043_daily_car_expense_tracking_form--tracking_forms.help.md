# daily-car-expense-tracking-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and track daily car expenses for corporate vehicle use or personal finance management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the relevant information for the day's car usage.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out, even if optional fields are left blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Car Expense**: (`car_expense`, `number`, required: false)
	+ This field is for entering the total cost of the day's car expenses.
* **Fuel Price**: (`fuel_price`, `number`, required: false)
	+ This field is for entering the price of fuel consumed during the day.
* **Fuel Quantity**: (`fuel_quantity`, `number`, required: false)
	+ This field is for entering the amount of fuel consumed during the day.
* **Odometer Reading**: (`mileage`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for entering the odometer reading at the end of the day.
* **Mileage HA**: (`mileage_ha`, `number`, required: false)
	+ This field is for entering the mileage (in miles) traveled in the day.
* **Odometer Reading**: (`odometer_reading`, `number`, required: false)
	+ This field is for entering the odometer reading at the end of the day.
* **Date**: (`date`, `date`, required: false)
	+ This field is for selecting the date of the expense.
* **Time**: (`time`, `time`, required: false)
	+ This field is for selecting the time of the expense.
* **Location**: (`location`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for entering the location of the expense.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* You can leave optional fields blank if not applicable.
* Use the correct format for date and time fields (YYYY-MM-DD and HH:MM respectively).
