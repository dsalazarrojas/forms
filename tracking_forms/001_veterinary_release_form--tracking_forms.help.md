# Veterinary Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the release of an animal from a veterinary facility, such as a hospital or clinic. It includes fields to record the owner's details, veterinary information, and medical treatment history. The form's goal is to ensure that all necessary data is gathered and documented, facilitating better communication and collaboration among veterinary professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the owner's name in the **owner_name** field.
2. Enter the veterinarian's name in the **veterinarian_name** field.
3. List any existing medical conditions in the **medical_conditions** field.
4. Select the type of authorization required in the **authorization** field:
	* Animal Owner: indicates that the owner is authorizing the medical procedure.
	* Authorized Veterinarian: indicates that a veterinarian is authorizing the medical procedure.
	* Both: indicates that both the owner and a veterinarian are authorizing the medical procedure.
5. List any medications currently being administered to the animal in the **medications** field:
	* Select multiple options as needed.
6. Record any additional medical instructions or notes in the **medical_instructions** field.
7. Record any specific veterinary instructions in the **veterinary_instructions** field.
8. Enter the date and time of the signature in the **signature_date** and **signature_time** fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **owner_name** (Text): Enter the name of the animal's owner.
* **veterinarian_name** (Text): Enter the name of the veterinarian performing the medical procedure.
* **medical_conditions** (Text): List any pre-existing medical conditions or allergies the animal may have.
* **authorization** (Select One):
	+ Animal Owner: The owner is authorizing the medical procedure.
	+ Authorized Veterinarian: A veterinarian is authorizing the medical procedure.
	+ Both: Both the owner and a veterinarian are authorizing the medical procedure.
* **medications** (Select Multiple):
	+ None: The animal is not currently on any medication.
	+ Acetaminophen: The animal is on acetaminophen.
	+ Aspirin: The animal is on aspirin.
	+ Benadryl: The animal is on benadryl.
	+ Carprofen: The animal is on carprofen.
	+ Corticosteroids: The animal is on corticosteroids.
	+ Flumist: The animal is on flumist.
	+ Metronidazole: The animal is on metronidazole.
	+ Meloxicam: The animal is on meloxicam.
	+ Phenobarbitol: The animal is on phenobarbitol.
	+ Prednisone: The animal is on prednisone.
	+ Rimadyl: The animal is on rimadyl.
	+ Tramadol: The animal is on tramadol.
	+ Vantrox: The animal is on vantrox.
* **medical_instructions** (Text): Provide any additional medical information or notes about the animal's condition.
* **veterinary_instructions** (Text): Provide any specific instructions for the veterinarian performing the medical procedure.
* **signature_date** (Date): Enter the date of the signature.
* **signature_time** (Time): Enter the time of the signature.

## Tips
* Always ensure that you have obtained the necessary authorization before proceeding with any medical procedures.
* Verify that all required information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Keep this form as a reference for future consultations and collaborations.
