# Stress Attack Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to track stress attacks. A stress attack is a period of high stress or anxiety that can be difficult to manage. By filling out this form, you can record the details of a stress attack, including the duration and timing. This information can help healthcare professionals better understand your experience and provide more effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Stress Score. This is a measure of how high or low you're feeling. You can rate your stress level as a number from 0 to 10.
2. Enter the duration of the stress attack in hours. This will help us understand how long the episode lasted.
3. Select the start and end dates for the stress attack. This will help us track when the attack occurred.
4. Choose the start and end times for the stress attack. This will help us understand when during the day the attack occurred.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stress Score** (stress_score, number, required): Enter a number from 0 to 10 that reflects your current stress level.
* **Duration Hours** (duration_hours, number, required): Enter the number of hours the stress attack lasted.
* **Start Date** (start_date, date, required): Select the date when the stress attack started.
* **End Date** (end_date, date, required): Select the date when the stress attack ended.
* **Start Time** (start_time, time, required): Select the time when the stress attack started.
* **End Time** (end_time, time, required): Select the time when the stress attack ended. 

## Tips
* Be as accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can contact your healthcare professional for guidance.
* If you have any other stress-related concerns, feel free to add them in the "Additional Comments" field, which is not shown in this form.

Please note: This form is designed to be completed by healthcare professionals, so if you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to them directly.
