# Alcohol Withdrawal Symptom Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alcohol Withdrawal Symptom Tracker is designed to help recovery programs and counselors track the progress of patients undergoing alcohol withdrawal. This form collects data on physical and psychological symptoms, vital signs, and other relevant information to support treatment decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, select "Alcohol Withdrawal Symptom Tracker" form and click on the "Start Entry" button.
2.  Select "Withdrawal Tracking Entry" (Page 1) and enter a brief description or note about the patient's current status, if necessary.
3.  Enter the "Patient Reference ID" (ID number assigned by the recovery program or clinic) and select the "Date of Tracking" (date) and "Time of Entry" (time) fields to record the tracking details.
4.  In the "Physical Symptoms" section, rate the severity of each symptom from 0 (None) to 5 (Severe) using the scale provided.
5.  Answer the "Tremors or Shaking," "Nausea or Excessive Sweating," "Headache Intensity," "Rapid Heart Rate or Palpitations," and other "Psychological Symptoms" questions by selecting a number from 0 to 5.
6.  In the "Vital Signs" section, record any monitored vital signs, such as blood pressure.
7.  In the "Summary and Support" section, provide any additional notes for the counselor's review.
8.  Answer "Yes" or "No" to questions "Withdrawal Medication Taken?" and "Do you require immediate medical assistance?"
9.  Finally, add any "Additional Notes" for the counselor's review, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Withdrawal Tracking Entry**: A brief description or note about the patient's current status, if necessary.
*   **Patient Reference ID**: ID number assigned by the recovery program or clinic. (Required)
*   **Date of Tracking**: Date of tracking. (Required)
*   **Time of Entry**: Time of entry. (Required)
*   **Physical Symptoms**: Rate the severity of each physical symptom from 0 (None) to 5 (Severe).
	+   **Tremors or Shaking**: Rate the severity of tremors or shaking from 0 to 5.
	+   **Nausea or Excessive Sweating**: Rate the severity of nausea or excessive sweating from 0 to 5.
	+   **Headache Intensity**: Rate the severity of headache intensity from 0 to 5.
	+   **Rapid Heart Rate or Palpitations**: Rate the severity of rapid heart rate or palpitations from 0 to 5.
*   **Psychological Symptoms**: Rate the severity of each psychological symptom from 0 (None) to 5 (Severe).
	+   **Anxiety or Agitation Level**: Rate the severity of anxiety or agitation from 0 to 5.
	+   **Difficulty Sleeping (Insomnia)**: Rate the severity of difficulty sleeping from 0 to 5.
	+   **Irritability or Mood Swings**: Rate the severity of irritability or mood swings from 0 to 5.
	+   **Confusion or Disorientation**: Rate the severity of confusion or disorientation from 0 to 5.
*   **Vital Signs**: Record any monitored vital signs, such as blood pressure. (Optional)
*   **Blood Pressure**: Record blood pressure (e.g., 120/80). (Optional)
*   **Body Temperature**: Record body temperature. (Optional)
*   **Summary and Support**: Provide any additional notes for the counselor's review.
*   **Intensitiy of Cravings**: Rate the intensity of cravings from 0 to 10. (Required)
*   **Withdrawal Medication Taken?**: Answer "Yes" or "No" if prescribed. (Required)
*   **Do you require immediate medical assistance?**: Answer "Yes" or "No". (Required)
*   **Additional Notes**: Add any additional notes for the counselor's review. (Optional)
*   **Withdrawal Medication Taken?**: Answer "Yes" or "No". (Required)
*   **Do you require immediate medical assistance?**: Answer "Yes" or "No". (Required)
