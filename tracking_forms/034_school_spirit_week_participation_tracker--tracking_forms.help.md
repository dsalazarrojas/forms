# School Spirit Week Participation Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track student participation in School Spirit Week activities. It gathers information about the student's engagement in various events and allows staff to monitor and report on participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `student_name` field.
2. Select the grade level you are in from the `grade_level` dropdown menu.
3. For each activity, select either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you participated.
4. Enter any additional feedback about your experience in the `feedback` field.
5. Enter the number of spirit points you earned from the activities in the `school_spirit_points` field.
6. Enter your teacher's name or ID, if applicable, in the `teacher_id` field.
7. Choose your class and section from the `class` and `section` dropdown menus.
8. Select the day of the event from the `event` dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your school records.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, optional): Select your current grade level from the dropdown menu. For example, if you are in 8th grade, select "8".
* **Activity 1** (`activity_1`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether you participated in each activity by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Activity 2** (`activity_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether you participated in each activity by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or thoughts about your experience during School Spirit Week.
* **School Spirit Points** (`school_spirit_points`, number, optional): Enter the number of points you earned during the activities.
* **Teacher ID** (`teacher_id`, text, optional): If you have a teacher, enter their ID or name. This will help staff track your points.
* **Class** (`class`, select_one, optional): Select your class (e.g. A, B, C, or D).
* **Section** (`section`, select_one, optional): Select your section (e.g. A, B, C, or D).
* **Event** (`event`, select_one, optional): Choose the day of the event, which could be Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday.
