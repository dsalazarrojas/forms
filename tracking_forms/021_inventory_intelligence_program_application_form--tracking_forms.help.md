# Inventory Intelligence Program Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is an application for the Inventory Intelligence Program. Please fill it out with your information to help us better understand your needs and preferences for the program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please make sure you have all the necessary information ready before filling out the form.
2. Start by filling in the "Program Title" field with a brief description of the program you are applying for.
3. In the "Name" field, enter your full name.
4. In the "Email" field, enter your email address where you can be contacted.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. If applicable, enter a specific date in the "Date Field" and time in the "Time Field".
7. You may want to add some additional notes in the "Note" field.
8. Select your preferences in the "Select Multiple" field.
9. Choose one option from the list in the "Select One" field.
10. Enter a decimal number in the "Decimal Field".
11. Enter an integer number in the "Integer Field".
12. Fill in the "Required Field" as it is marked as a required field.
13. You may also want to add some additional notes in the "Note 2" field.
14. Select multiple options in the "Select Multiple 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Title** (Text): Enter a brief description of the program you are applying for.
* **Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date Field** (Date, Optional): If applicable, enter a specific date.
* **Time Field** (Time, Optional): If applicable, enter a specific time.
* **Note** (Note, Optional): Add any additional notes that you think would be helpful.
* **Select Multiple** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select your preferences from the provided options.
* **Select One** (Select One, Required): Choose one option from the list.
* **Decimal Field** (Number, Optional): Enter a decimal number.
* **Integer Field** (Number, Optional): Enter an integer number.
* **Required Field** (Text, Required): Fill in this field as it is marked as required.
* **Optional Field** (Text, Optional): You may want to add some additional information here.
* **Select Multiple 2** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select multiple options from the provided list.
* **Note 2** (Note, Optional): Add any additional notes that you think would be helpful.
