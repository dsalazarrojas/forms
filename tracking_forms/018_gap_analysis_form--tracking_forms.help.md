# Gap Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gap Analysis Form is a tool used to identify and analyze gaps in performance between current and future states or goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and answers ready.
2. Select the current and future performance dates from the date fields.
3. Choose the current and future goals for each section (current goals, future goals).
4. Select the actions taken for both current and future states.
5. Identify the benefits already achieved for current and future states.
6. For future states, select any additional goals you want to achieve.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Current Performance** (`current_performance`): Enter the current performance date, if known.
* **Future Performance** (`future_performance`): Enter the future performance date, if known.
* **Current Goals** (`current_goals`): Choose whether you currently have or do not have the goals for the current state.
* **Future Goals** (`future_goals`): Choose whether you currently have or do not have the goals for the future state.
* **Current Actions** (`current_actions`): Select the actions you are currently taking or have taken for the current state.
* **Future Actions** (`future_actions`): Select the actions you plan to take or have taken for the future state.
* **Current Benefits** (`current_benefits`): Select the benefits you have achieved or are achieving for the current state.
* **Future Benefits** (`future_benefits`): Select the benefits you expect to achieve or have achieved for the future state.
* **Current Goals 2** (`current_goals_2`): Provide a note describing any additional current goals.
* **Future Goals 2** (`future_goals_2`): Provide a note describing any additional future goals.
* **Current Actions 2** (`current_actions_2`): Select the actions you are currently taking or have taken for the current state.
* **Future Actions 2** (`future_actions_2`): Select the actions you plan to take or have taken for the future state.
* **Future Goals 3** (`future_goals_3`): Select the additional future goals for the future state.
* **Current Benefits 2** (`current_benefits_2`): Select the benefits you have achieved or are achieving for the current state.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers for both current and future states.
* Ensure you have filled out all required fields.
* Review your input carefully before submitting the form.
