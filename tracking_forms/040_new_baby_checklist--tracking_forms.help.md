# New Baby Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track and record various details about a new baby's birth and early development. It is used for monitoring and tracking the baby's progress and health.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the new baby's name and other basic details as prompted.
2. Select the baby's gender and enter the birth date and time.
3. Enter the mother's height.
4. Record the baby's birth weight and length.
5. Specify the feeding frequency.
6. Record the number of diaper changes in 24 hours.
7. Enter the number of vaccinations the baby has received.
8. Note any medical conditions the baby may have.
9. Specify the baby's birth weight unit (pounds or kilograms).
10. Specify the baby's birth length unit (inches or centimeters).
11. Record the baby's feeding time (optional).
12. Record the baby's official feeding frequency.
13. Record the baby's official diaper changes in 24 hours.
14. Record the baby's vaccination date (if applicable).
15. Specify the type of vaccines the baby has received (if applicable).
