# heart_rate_tracking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The heart rate tracking form is designed to collect data on patients' heart rates at different time points. This form will be used to monitor and track heart rates for patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the relevant information on each page of the form.
2. For each page, enter the required and optional fields according to the guidelines provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header** (Page 1, id: 1): This is the title of the form, providing an overview of what the form is about.
* **Heart Rate** (Page 1, id: 2): Enter the patient's current heart rate.
* **Resting Heart Rate** (Page 1, id: 3): Enter the patient's resting heart rate.
* **Heart Rate 1** (Page 1, id: 4): Enter the patient's heart rate at the first time point.
* **Maximum Heart Rate 1** (Page 1, id: 5): Enter the maximum heart rate of the patient at the first time point.
* **Heart Rate 2** (Page 1, id: 6): Enter the patient's heart rate at the second time point.
* **Maximum Heart Rate 2** (Page 1, id: 7): Enter the maximum heart rate of the patient at the second time point.
* **Date** (Page 1, id: 8): Enter the date on which the heart rate measurement was taken.
* **Time** (Page 1, id: 9): Enter the time at which the heart rate measurement was taken.
* **User Name** (Page 1, id: 10): Enter the name of the user taking the measurement.
* **Email** (Page 1, id: 11): Enter the email address of the user taking the measurement.
* **Phone** (Page 1, id: 12): Enter the phone number of the user taking the measurement.
* **Notes** (Page 1, id: 13): Enter any additional notes or comments regarding the measurement.
