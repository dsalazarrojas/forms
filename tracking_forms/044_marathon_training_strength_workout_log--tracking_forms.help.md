# marathon_training_strength_workout_log - Help Guide

## Purpose

The marathon training strength workout log form is used to track a user's strength training sessions for marathons.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Marathon Strength Session" page to input your training details.
2. Choose "Yes" or "No" for each exercise to indicate whether you did it during the session.
3. Enter the number of sets and reps for each exercise.
4. Provide your email address, name, coach, and athlete ID and name if applicable.
5. Select the form status (draft, published, or archived) and version.
6. Optionally, add any comments about your session.
7. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Marathon Strength Session** (`marathon_strength_session`, text, required): This field is used to input a brief description of your training session.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the training session.
* **Exercises** (`exercises`, select_multiple, required): Check "Yes" for each exercise you performed during the session.
* **Sets** (`sets`, number, optional): Enter the number of sets you performed for each exercise.
* **Reps** (`reps`, number, optional): Enter the number of reps for each exercise.
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight used for each exercise.
* **Exercises 2** (`exercises_2`, select_multiple, optional): Check "Yes" for each additional exercise you performed during the session.
* **Weight 2** (`weight_2`, number, optional): Enter the weight used for each additional exercise.
* **Sets 2** (`sets_2`, number, optional): Enter the number of sets for each additional exercise.
* **Reps 2** (`reps_2`, number, optional): Enter the number of reps for each additional exercise.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments about your session.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Coach** (`coach`, text, optional): Enter your coach's name.
* **Athlete ID** (`athlete_id`, text, optional): Enter your athlete's ID.
* **Athlete Name** (`athlete_name`, text, optional): Enter your athlete's name.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the person who submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted At** (`form_submitted_at`, date, optional): Enter the date and time when the form was submitted.
* **Form Completed At** (`form_completed_at`, date, optional): Enter the date and time when the form was completed.
* **Form Last Updated** (`form_last_updated`, date, optional): Enter the date and time when the form was last updated.
* **Form Status** (`form_status`, select_one, optional): Select the form status (draft, published, or archived).
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, select_one, optional): Select the form version (v1, v2, or v3).
* **Form Created By** (`form_created_by`, text, optional): Enter the person who created the form.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about the form.

**Remember to review the form before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed and the form is accurate.**

This guide is designed to help users understand the purpose and usage of the marathon training strength workout log form. Follow the steps and field explanations provided to ensure accurate and effective submission of the form.
