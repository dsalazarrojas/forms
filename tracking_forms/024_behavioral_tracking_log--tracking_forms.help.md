# Behavioral Tracking Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track behavioral incidents that occur in a school setting. It helps staff members to gather information and develop follow-up plans to address student behavior issues.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the student/client information (fields 2-4)
* Describe the behavior that occurred (fields 10, 11, 13, 23)
* Record the incident details (fields 7, 8, 9)
* Document the intervention response (fields 16, 17, 18)
* Specify follow-up actions (fields 21-22, 24)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student/Client Name** (2): Enter the name of the student/client.
* **Staff Member Recording** (5): Type your name as the staff member who recorded this incident.
* **Date of Incident** (7): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Time of Incident** (8): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Location of Incident** (9): Choose where the incident occurred from the options provided.
* **Description of Behavior** (10): Describe the specific behavior that occurred.
* **Type of Behavior** (11): Choose the type of behavior that occurred from the options provided.
* **Severity Level** (12): Choose the severity level of the behavior from the options provided.
* **Triggering Event or Antecedent** (13): Describe any events that may have led to the behavior.
* **Immediate Action Taken** (16): Describe the steps you took to address the behavior.
* **Type of Intervention** (17): Choose the type of intervention used from the options provided.
* **Student Response to Intervention** (18): Record the student's response to the intervention.
* **Follow-up Actions Needed** (20): Specify if follow-up actions are needed.
* **Description of Follow-up** (21): Describe any follow-up actions taken.
* **Scheduled Follow-up Date** (22): Enter the date when follow-up will occur.
* **Parents Contacted** (23): Specify if parents were contacted.
* **Date of Parent Contact** (24): Enter the date parents were contacted.
* **Additional Notes or Observations** (25): Add any additional notes or observations about the incident.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when completing this form to ensure accurate tracking and follow-up.
* Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Review and update this form regularly to ensure it remains relevant and accurate.
