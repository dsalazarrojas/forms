# music_inventory_information_record_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record music inventory information for internal management and tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the page that corresponds to the music inventory record you want to complete.
2. Fill in the fields as needed, providing accurate and relevant information.
3. Save the form to complete it multiple times for multiple music inventory records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **music_inventory_record_form** (`music_inventory_record_form`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter a brief description of the music inventory record.
* **music_store_location** (`music_store_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the music inventory record is stored.
* **music_store_owner** (`music_store_owner`, text, optional): Enter the owner of the music inventory record.
* **music_inventory_number** (`music_inventory_number`, number, optional): Enter the unique number of the music inventory record.
* **music_inventory_date_recorded** (`music_inventory_date_recorded`, date, optional): Enter the date when the music inventory record was recorded.
* **music_inventory_time_recorded** (`music_inventory_time_recorded`, time, optional): Enter the time when the music inventory record was recorded.
* **music_inventory_note** (`music_inventory_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the music inventory record.
* **music_inventory_email** (`music_inventory_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the music inventory record owner or contact.
* **music_inventory_phone** (`music_inventory_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the music inventory record owner or contact.
* **music_inventory_status** (`music_inventory_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the music inventory record (pending, in stock, or out of stock).
* **music_inventory_type** (`music_inventory_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of music inventory record (vinyl, cd, dvd, blu-ray, cassette, tape, or mp3).

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and consistently.
- If you need to report multiple music inventory records, save this form and complete it multiple times.
- If you need help or have questions, contact your internal support team.
