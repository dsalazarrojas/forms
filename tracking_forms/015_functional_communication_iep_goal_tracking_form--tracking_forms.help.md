# Functional Communication IEP Goal Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Functional Communication IEP Goal Tracking Form is used to track the progress and achievements of students towards their IEP goals, specifically in the area of functional communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's name as recorded in their official documentation.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter the student's ID number as recorded in their official documentation.
* **Communication Goal** (`communication_goal`, text, optional): Describe the communication goal the student is working towards.
* **Goal Date** (`goal_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the goal was set.
* **Target Behavior** (`target_behavior`, text, optional): Specify the target behavior or skill the student is expected to develop or improve.
* **Observation Date** (`observation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the observation or assessment.
* **Progress Notes** (`progress_notes`, text, optional): Add any progress notes or comments about the student's performance.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_one, optional): Rate the student's progress using the scale: Good, Fair.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
