# Milk Distribution Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Milk Distribution Tracker is used to track milk deliveries, allowing for accurate and efficient record-keeping and management of milk distribution activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the delivery date for the milk delivery.
2. Enter the quantity of milk being delivered.
3. Enter the name of the farm where the milk was obtained.
4. Enter the name of the cooperative involved in the milk distribution.
5. Select whether milk is being delivered ("Yes" or "No").
6. Enter the number of the vehicle used for delivery.
7. Enter the location where the milk is being delivered.
8. Enter the name of the person making the delivery.
9. Select whether the milk has been received ("Yes" or "No").
10. If milk has been received, enter the quantity received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Date (1)** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of milk delivery. This field allows you to track when milk was delivered to the customer.
* **Quantity (2)** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of milk being delivered. This field helps you keep track of the amount of milk delivered.
* **Farm Name (3)** (`farm_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the farm where the milk was obtained. This field is used to identify the source of the milk.
* **Cooperative Name (4)** (`cooperative_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the cooperative involved in the milk distribution. This field helps to identify the organization responsible for the milk delivery.
* **Milk Type (5)** (`milk_type`, select_one, optional): Select whether milk is being delivered ("Yes" or "No"). This field helps determine the type of milk being delivered.
* **Vehicle Number (6)** (`vehicle_number`, text, optional): Enter the number of the vehicle used for delivery. This field is used to track the vehicle used for milk delivery.
* **Delivery Location (7)** (`delivery_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where milk is being delivered. This field helps identify the destination of the milk delivery.
* **Delivery Person (8)** (`delivery_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person making the delivery. This field is used to track the individual responsible for the delivery.
* **Milk Received (9)** (`milk_received`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether milk has been received ("Yes" or "No"). This field helps determine the status of milk delivery and reception.
* **Quantity Received (10)** (`quantity_received`, number, optional): If milk has been received, enter the quantity received. This field is used to track the amount of milk received.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have authorization to complete this form before proceeding.
* Double-check the accuracy of each field before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your organization's milk distribution policies or seek guidance from a supervisor.
