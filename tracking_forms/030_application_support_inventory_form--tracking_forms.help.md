# Application Support Inventory Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Application Support Inventory Form is designed to collect and track application information, support details, and usage metrics to ensure efficient support and maintenance management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field carefully, ensuring accuracy and completeness of information.
2. Select the correct options from dropdown menus where applicable.
3. Enter text into text fields, adhering to any specific guidelines (e.g., date format).
4. Save and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Name** (`application_name`, text, required): Enter the exact name of the application, including any necessary prefixes or acronyms.
* **Application Acronym or Code** (`application_acronym`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the acronym or code used for the application.
* **Current Version** (`current_version`, text, required): Enter the current version of the application, including any relevant build numbers or patches.
* **Vendor or Developer Name** (`vendor_name`, text, optional): Identify the vendor or developer responsible for the application.
* **Support Level** (`support_level`, select_one, required): Choose the level of support provided for the application: Tier 1 - Basic, Tier 2 - Standard, Tier 3 - Premium, Tier 4 - Enterprise, or Tier 5 - Custom.
* **Support Priority** (`support_priority`, select_one, required): Select the priority level for support: Critical - 24/7 support, High - Business hours, Medium - Standard SLA, or Low - As available.
* **Primary Support Contact** (`support_contact`, text, required): Enter the name and email of the primary support contact.
* **Support Phone Number** (`support_phone`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the support phone number.
* **Support Portal or Ticket System URL** (`support_portal_url`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the support portal or ticket system URL.
* **Deployment Location** (`deployment_location`, select_one, required): Choose the deployment location of the application: On-premises, Cloud-hosted, Hybrid, or SaaS.
* **Number of Active Users** (`number_of_users`, number, optional): Enter the number of active users if applicable.
* **Departments/Teams Using This Application** (`department_usage`, text, required): List the departments or teams that use the application.
* **Critical Business Functions Dependent on This App** (`critical_business_functions`, text, required): Identify any critical business functions dependent on this application.
* **Date of Last Update or Patch** (`last_update_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last update or patch, if applicable.
* **Scheduled Maintenance Window** (`maintenance_schedule`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the scheduled maintenance window.
* **End of Support or Vendor Sunset Date** (`end_of_support_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of end of support or vendor sunset, if applicable.
* **Known Issues or Limitations** (`known_issues`, text, optional): List any known issues or limitations of the application.
* **Additional Support Notes** (`support_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional support notes or comments.
