# Pesticide Application Record - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pesticide Application Record form is designed to document and track pesticide applications in a systematic and detailed manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Date of Application** in the date field.
2. Provide the **Applicator**'s contact details in the text field.
3. Type in the **Location** where the pesticide was applied.
4. Specify the **Pesticide Product** used.
5. Describe the **Conditions** under which the pesticide was applied.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Date of Application** (`date_of_application`): Enter the date and time when the pesticide was applied.
- **Applicator** (`applicator`): Provide the name and contact information of the person or organization who applied the pesticide.
- **Location** (`location`): Type in the specific location where the pesticide was applied, such as a field, garden, or orchard.
- **Pesticide Product** (`pesticide_product`): Identify the name and type of the pesticide product used.
- **Conditions** (`conditions`): Describe any relevant weather conditions, soil conditions, or other environmental factors that may have affected the pesticide application, such as temperature, humidity, or soil moisture levels.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when providing dates and locations to ensure accurate tracking and referencing.
- Use clear and concise language when describing conditions to ensure easy understanding and reference.
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting to ensure a comprehensive record.
- Save the completed form for future reference and review.
