# Toolbox Talks Sign In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track information about attendees who have signed in for a toolbox talk. The purpose of this form is to collect and store attendee details for administrative and organizational purposes.

## How to Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all required and optional fields filled in accurately.
2. Review each field carefully before submission to ensure the information is correct.
3. Double-check for any errors or inconsistencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sign In Form Data** (`sign_in_form_data`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information that you would like to record about the attendee's sign in.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the attendee, as they would like to be recorded.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the attendee.
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the attendee.
* **Attendee Organization** (`attendee_organization`, text, optional): Enter the organization or department of the attendee.
* **Attendee Job Title** (`attendee_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title or position of the attendee.
* **Attendee Position** (`attendee_position`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the attendee's position or role.
* **Attendee Location** (`attendee_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the attendee.
* **Attendee Comments** (`attendee_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the attendee.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool that the attendee is attending the talk for.
* **Sign In Form Date** (`sign_in_form_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the sign-in form.
* **Sign In Form Time** (`sign_in_form_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the sign-in form.

## Tips

* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered before submitting.
* Ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.
* Use this form to record and track information accurately and consistently.
