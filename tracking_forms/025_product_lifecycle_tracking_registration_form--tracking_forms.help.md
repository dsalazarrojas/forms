# Product Lifecycle Tracking Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the status and progress of products throughout their lifecycle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "General Information" section with broad details about the product.
2. Provide specific product information in the "Product Details" section.
3. Select the relevant lifecycle stages for the product in the "Lifecycle Stages" section.
4. Record any relevant tracking numbers or codes in the "Tracking" field.
5. If applicable, provide a product portfolio or inventory management information in the "Portfolio" field.
6. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.
7. Input contact information for the product in the "Contact Information" field.
8. Select the teams assigned to the product in the "Assigned Teams" field.
