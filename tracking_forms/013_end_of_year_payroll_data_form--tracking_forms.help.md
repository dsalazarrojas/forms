# end_of_year_payroll_data_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The end_of_year_payroll_data_form is a form used to track yearly payroll data. This form helps to collect information about an employee's pay period, such as the start and end dates, pay rate, wages paid, and insurance premiums.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether the employee had a pay period for the year.
2. Enter the business name.
3. Enter the employee ID.
4. Enter the start date of the pay period.
5. Enter the end date of the pay period.
6. Enter the pay rate for the employee.
7. Enter the wages paid for the employee.
8. Enter the health insurance premiums for the employee.
9. Enter the dental insurance premiums for the employee.
10. Enter the vision insurance premiums for the employee.
11. Enter the long term care insurance premiums for the employee.
12. Enter the total wages paid for the year.
13. Select the year for which the payroll data is being reported.
14. Select whether the employee has a monthly payroll.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Yearly Payroll Data** (`end_of_year_payroll_data`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicates whether the employee had a pay period for the year.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the business where the employee worked.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the employee ID number.
* **Pay Period Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the pay period.
* **Pay Period End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the pay period.
* **Pay Rate** (`pay_rate`, `number`, required: false): Enter the pay rate for the employee.
* **Wages Paid** (`wages_paid`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total wages paid for the employee.
* **Health Insurance Premiums** (`health_insurance_premiums`, `number`, required: false): Enter the health insurance premiums paid for the employee.
* **Dental Insurance Premiums** (`dental_insurance_premiums`, `number`, required: false): Enter the dental insurance premiums paid for the employee.
* **Vision Insurance Premiums** (`vision_insurance_premiums`, `number`, required: false): Enter the vision insurance premiums paid for the employee.
* **Long Term Care Insurance Premiums** (`long_term_care_insurance_premiums`, `number`, required: false): Enter the long term care insurance premiums paid for the employee.
* **Total Wages Paid** (`total_wages_paid`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total wages paid for the year.
* **Year** (`year`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the year for which the payroll data is being reported.
* **Month** (`month`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether the employee has a monthly payroll.

Note: If any of these fields are not relevant, you may not need to fill them in, but be cautious as they are required fields.
