# Embryo Transfer Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to track the transfer of embryos in a medical setting, collecting essential information related to patient details, procedure information, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide patient details, including any relevant medical history or background information.
2. Enter procedure information, such as the type of procedure and any relevant dates or times.
3. Add any relevant notes or comments about the procedure.
4. Sign and date the form to authenticate the transfer of the embryos.
5. Make a declaration, stating that the information provided is accurate and true.
6. Enter the form version and any comments about the form itself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Details** (`patient_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant medical history or background information.
* **Procedure Information** (`procedure_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the type of procedure, dates, and any other relevant information about the embryo transfer.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the date and time of the form submission.
* **Procedure Notes** (`procedure_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Add any notes or comments about the procedure, such as the outcome or any complications.
* **Procedure Notes Details** (`procedure_notes_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any further details about the procedure, such as the location or any other relevant information.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required/optional): Sign and date the form to authenticate the transfer of the embryos.
* **Patient Declaration** (`patient_declaration`, `text`, required/optional): Make a declaration, stating that the information provided is accurate and true.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the version of the form being used.
* **Form Comments** (`form_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any comments or feedback about the form.
* **Embryo Tracking Number** (`embryo_tracking_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any embryo tracking number, if applicable.

## Tips

* Ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review the form carefully to ensure it meets your specific needs.
* Use the form for its intended purpose and context.
* If unsure about any field, consult the relevant medical professional or expert.
