# Utility Data Tracking Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track utility data collection practices, including consent for data sharing and storage, for various utility services.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read the form and understand the purpose and implications of each question.
2. Answer all relevant questions, leaving blank fields that do not apply to you.
3. Ensure that you answer the questions with the utmost honesty and accuracy.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Utility Data Tracking Form**: This field is a placeholder for the main title of the form.
* **Utility Data Collection**: Select "Yes" if you consent to the collection of your utility data, and "No" otherwise. This field is required.
* **Data Collection Frequency**: Enter the frequency of utility data collection, e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, etc.
* **Collection Date**: Enter the date when your utility data was last collected.
* **Collection Time**: Enter the time when your utility data was last collected.
* **Data Usage**: Select "Yes" if you allow the sharing of your utility data for certain purposes, such as billing or marketing, and "No" otherwise.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, including name, address, and phone number, so that the utility company can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Consent**: Select "Yes" if you give consent for the data collection, storage, and sharing of your utility data, and "No" otherwise.
* **Consent Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes regarding your consent.
* **Utility Data Type**: Select "Yes" if you allow the collection of certain types of data, such as usage, meter reading, or other, and "No" otherwise.
* **Utility Name**: Enter the name of the utility service you are using.
* **Utility Address**: Enter the address where the utility service is located or provided.
* **Utility Service Provider**: Enter the name of the company or organization providing the utility service.
* **Data Protection**: Enter any measures taken to protect your utility data, such as encryption or secure storage.
* **Consent To Share**: Select "Yes" if you give consent to share your utility data with others, and "No" otherwise.
* **Share Data With**: Select "Yes" if you allow your utility data to be shared with certain individuals or organizations, and "No" otherwise.
* **Share Method**: Select "Yes" if you allow your utility data to be shared through specific methods, such as email, phone, or in-person, and "No" otherwise.
* **Share Frequency**: Select "Yes" if you allow your utility data to be shared at specific frequencies, such as weekly or monthly, and "No" otherwise.
* **Share Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes regarding data sharing.
* **Data Retention**: Enter the period of time that your utility data is kept stored.
* **Data Retention Period**: Enter the specific time period for data retention.
* **Data Storage**: Enter the method of data storage used by the utility company.
* **Data Security**: Enter any measures taken to secure your utility data.
