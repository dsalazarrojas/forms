# weekly_client_tracking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The weekly_client_tracking_form is designed to track client updates and progress on a weekly basis. It's a quick and easy way to ensure both the client and their manager are aware of current goals, progress, and upcoming events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Weekly Report**: Provide a brief overview of your current week in the `Weekly Report` section.
2. **Progress Update**: In the `Progress Update` section, describe any progress you've made on your goals or any significant events that have occurred this week.
3. **Goals**: Select your current goals for the week in the `Goals` section.
4. **Goal Progress**: Choose the progress you've made on your current goals in the `Goal Progress` section.
5. **Next Report**: Enter the date of your next report in the `Next Report` section.
6. **Next Contact**: Enter the date and time of your next contact in the `Next Contact` section.
7. **Current Status**: Enter your current status in the `Current Status` section.
8. **Issues Update**: Enter any issues or concerns you have in the `Issues Update` section.
9. **Action Plan**: Describe the actions you and your manager will take this week in the `Action Plan` section.
10. **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments in the `Notes` section.
11. **Email**: Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
12. **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
13. **Next Follow-up**: Enter the date and time of your next follow-up in the `Next Follow-up` section.
