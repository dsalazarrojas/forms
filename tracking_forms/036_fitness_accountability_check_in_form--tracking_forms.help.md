# fitness_accountability_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track the fitness accountability of a client, helping the user to set and achieve their goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields with the relevant information about your fitness journey. Start by providing your client's name, then answer the questions about their exercise frequency, workout type, goals, workout schedule, and progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person you are tracking the fitness accountability for.
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, text, optional): Describe the frequency of the client's workouts, such as how many times a week they exercise.
* **Workout Type** (`workout_type`, text, optional): Specify the type of workouts the client is doing, such as cardio, strength, or flexibility exercises.
* **Goals And Objectives** (`goals_and_objectives`, text, optional): Outline the client's short-term and long-term fitness goals.
* **Workout Days** (`workout_days`, text, optional): Specify the number of days the client works out per week.
* **Exercise Level** (`exercise_level`, text, optional): Describe the client's current fitness level, such as beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
* **Current Progress** (`current_progress`, text, optional): Record the client's current progress towards their goals.
* **Workout Schedule** (`workout_schedule`, text, optional): Provide a detailed schedule of the client's workouts.
* **Workout Plan** (`workout_plan`, text, optional): Outline the client's workout plan, including exercises, sets, and reps.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and thoroughly to get a clear picture of the client's fitness journey.
* Review and update the form regularly to track progress and make adjustments as needed.
* Use this form to set and achieve fitness goals, and to provide accountability and support to the client.
