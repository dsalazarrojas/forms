# Precautionary Measures Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Precautionary Measures Checklist is a form used to gather information on the safety measures taken during electrical work. It helps identify potential hazards and ensures that necessary precautions are taken to prevent accidents and injuries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "Take Precautions" section by describing any precautions taken while performing electrical work.
2.  In the "Review Precautions" section, select the option that best describes the level of review conducted.
3.  In the "Lockout Tag" section, select all options that apply to the lockout tag used.
4.  In the "Electrical Lockout Precautions" and "Review Precautions Precautions" sections, you can provide additional information if needed.
5.  In the "Electrical Shock Prevention Precautions" and other similar sections, you can provide additional information if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Take Precautions** (take_precautions, text, required): Describe any precautions taken while performing electrical work. This field is crucial in identifying potential hazards and ensuring that necessary precautions are taken.
- **Review Precautions** (review_precautions, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes the level of review conducted. This field helps ensure that the work is reviewed thoroughly.
- **Lockout Tag** (electrical_lockout_tag, select_multiple, required): Select all options that apply to the lockout tag used. This field ensures that all electrical disconnects are accounted for.
- **Electrical Lockout Precautions** (electrical_lockout_precautions, text, required): Complete the lockout tag with all electrical disconnects. This field is essential for ensuring that the electrical system is safe.
- **Electrical Shock Prevention Precautions** (electrical_shock_precautions, text, required): Provide information on precautions taken to prevent electrical shock. This field is critical for preventing electrical shock.
- **Review Precautions Precautions** (review_precautions_precautions, text, required): This field is for additional information on reviewing precautions. This field is not typically used.
- **Lockout Tag Precautions** (lockout_tag_precautions, text, required): This field is for additional information on lockout tags. This field is not typically used.
- **Electrical Lockout Precautions Precautions 2** (electrical_lockout_precautions_precautions_2, text, required): This field is for additional information on electrical lockout precautions. This field is not typically used.
