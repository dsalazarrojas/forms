# Behavior Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Behavior Observation Form is used to record and report on a student's behavior during an observation. This form helps to identify areas of concern and strengths, and informs future interventions and support strategies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form in a clear and detailed manner**, taking into account all the information you observed during the behavior.
2. **Be accurate and thorough** in your responses, but keep in mind that some fields may not apply to the student's behavior.
3. **Use the options provided** for each field, selecting the one that best describes the student's behavior.
