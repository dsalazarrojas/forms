# Daily Fridge Temperature Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for logging daily temperature readings from different parts of a refrigerator. Please fill it out regularly to monitor your fridge's temperature conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the temperature readings for each section of your refrigerator. You can log as many or as few fields as you have temperature probes or monitoring points.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fridge Temperature 1** (`fridge_temperature_1`, number, optional): Enter the current temperature reading for the first temperature probe in the fridge.
* **Fridge Temperature 2** (`fridge_temperature_2`, number, optional): Enter the current temperature reading for the second temperature probe in the fridge.
* **Fridge Temperature 3** (`fridge_temperature_3`, number, optional): Enter the current temperature reading for the third temperature probe in the fridge.
* **Fridge Temperature 4** (`fridge_temperature_4`, number, optional): Enter the current temperature reading for the fourth temperature probe in the fridge.
* **Freezer Temperature 1** (`freezer_temperature_1`, number, optional): Enter the current temperature reading for the first temperature probe in the freezer.
* **Freezer Temperature 2** (`freezer_temperature_2`, number, optional): Enter the current temperature reading for the second temperature probe in the freezer.
* **Freezer Temperature 3** (`freezer_temperature_3`, number, optional): Enter the current temperature reading for the third temperature probe in the freezer.
* **Freezer Temperature 4** (`freezer_temperature_4`, number, optional): Enter the current temperature reading for the fourth temperature probe in the freezer.

## Tips
* Make sure to log temperatures regularly to track changes over time.
* If you have multiple probes, try to log temperatures at the same time each day to provide a consistent comparison.
* If you notice any unusual or concerning temperature readings, report them to your supervisor or facility management.
* You can add or remove fields as needed to accommodate your specific monitoring setup.
