# Energy Audit Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Energy Audit Checklist is a site-specific form used to track and document energy-related status. It is designed to provide site administrators with a standardized process for evaluating site conditions and identifying areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Site Name field with the current site name.
2. For the Building Status field, select the current status of the building (Active or Inactive).
3. Answer the Equipment Checklist questions by selecting all applicable options.
4. Answer the Facility Checklist questions by selecting all applicable options.
5. Identify any Safety Concerns by selecting all relevant options.
6. Add any relevant Comments for future reference.
7. Optionally, provide additional information in the Site Admin field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Name** (1, text, required): Enter the current site name.
* **Admin** (2, text, optional): Enter the current site administrator's name or contact information.
* **Building Status** (3, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the building (Active or Inactive).
* **Equipment Checklist** (4, select_multiple, optional): Identify which of the following applies to the site:
	+ HVAC system: 
	+ Lighting system: 
	+ Power generation system: 
	+ Other (please specify):
* **Facility Checklist** (5, select_multiple, optional): Identify which of the following applies to the site:
	+ Fire suppression system: 
	+ Emergency exit signs: 
	+ Safety doors: 
	+ Other (please specify):
* **Safety Concerns** (6, select_multiple, optional): Identify any safety concerns or hazards on site:
	+ Electrical issues: 
	+ Structural issues: 
	+ Chemical hazards: 
	+ Other (please specify):
* **Comments** (7, note, optional): Add any relevant comments or notes for future reference.
* **Site Admin** (8, text, optional): Enter the current site administrator's name or contact information.
