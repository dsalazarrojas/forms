# Truck Hour Meter Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Truck Hour Meter Log is a form used to track vehicle usage and performance metrics such as engine hours, routes completed, fuel consumed, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the required information:
	* **Driver Name**: Enter the name of the driver operating the vehicle.
	* **Vehicle Number**: Enter the unique identifier of the vehicle.
2. Fill in the optional fields:
	* **Engine Hours**: Record the total hours the engine has been running.
	* **Routes Completed**: Record the number of routes completed.
	* **Driver Experience**: Record the driver's experience level (e.g., 1-10 years).
	* **Fuel Consumed**: Record the amount of fuel consumed (e.g., gallons).
	* **Driver Rating**: Rate the driver's performance (Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent).
	* **Route Details**: Provide additional details about the route taken.
	* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Driver Name**: This field is required and should be filled with the name of the driver who operated the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Number**: This field is required and should be filled with the unique identifier of the vehicle.
* **Engine Hours**: This field is optional and should be filled with the total hours the engine has been running. If left blank, the default value will be 0.
* **Routes Completed**: This field is optional and should be filled with the number of routes completed. If left blank, the default value will be 0.
* **Driver Experience**: This field is optional and should be filled with the driver's experience level. The field is not currently used in the calculation of any specific metrics.
* **Fuel Consumed**: This field is optional and should be filled with the amount of fuel consumed. If left blank, the default value will be 0.
* **Driver Rating**: This field is optional and should be filled with a rating of the driver's performance (Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent).
* **Route Details**: This field is optional and should be filled with additional details about the route taken.
* **Notes**: This field is optional and should be filled with any additional comments or notes about the trip.

## Tips
* Ensure that each entry is unique and not a duplication of an existing record.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or support team for assistance.
