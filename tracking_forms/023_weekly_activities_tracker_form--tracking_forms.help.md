# weekly_activities_tracker_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track weekly activities, progress, and deadlines. It allows users to report their work and set goals for the week.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out each field as prompted:
	+ **Weekly activities**: Describe the activities you worked on this week.
	+ **Progress**: Enter a numerical value representing your progress on these activities.
	+ **Start Date**: Enter the date when you started working on these activities.
	+ **End Date**: Enter the date when you finished working on these activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Weekly activities**: This field allows you to describe what you worked on this week. Try to be concise and clear in your description.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **Progress**: Enter a numerical value representing your progress on the activities you listed. For example, if you completed 50% of a task, enter "50".
	+ Type: number
	+ Required: No
* **Start Date**: Enter the date when you started working on the activities listed.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: No
* **End Date**: Enter the date when you finished working on the activities listed.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: No

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing your activities and progress.
* Use specific dates if you have them.
* Remember that this form is for tracking progress and reporting, so be honest and accurate in your input.
* You can fill out this form as often as necessary to track your progress throughout the week.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your team or manager for assistance.
