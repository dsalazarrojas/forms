# SAS Observation And Coaching Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on observations and coaching interactions between team members or supervisors. It is meant to be used for tracking and recording observations, providing guidance, and monitoring progress in coaching and development activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Review the form header and fill in any necessary information if required.
*   Fill in the name of the individual being coached in the "sas_coach" field.
*   Select the date the observation was made in the "observed_date" field.
*   Enter the note from the observer in the "sas_note" field.
*   Describe the action item that was discussed in the "action_item" field.
*   Provide the next steps to take in the "next_steps" field.
*   Enter any additional coaching notes or comments in the "sas_coaching_notes" field.
*   Enter any notes from the coach in the "sas_coach_notes" field.
*   If applicable, select the coaching status from the dropdown menu in the "sas_coaching_status" field.
*   If not listed, enter the coaching status in the "sas_coaching_status_other" field.
*   Enter the date of the coach's next session in the "sas_coach_date" field.
*   Enter the time of the coach's next session in the "sas_coach_time" field.
*   Enter any comments from the coach in the "sas_coach_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form Header (form_header)**: This field is for displaying the form title. You can add any necessary information if required.
*   **SAS Coach (sas_coach)**: This field is for filling in the name of the individual being coached.
*   **Observed By (observed_by)**: This field is not currently used in this form.
*   **Observed Date (observed_date)**: This field is for selecting the date the observation was made.
*   **SAS Note (sas_note)**: This field is for entering the note from the observer.
*   **Action Item (action_item)**: This field is for describing the action item that was discussed.
*   **Next Steps (next_steps)**: This field is for providing the next steps to take.
*   **SAS Coaching Notes (sas_coaching_notes)**: This field is for entering any additional coaching notes or comments.
*   **SAS Coach Notes (sas_coach_notes)**: This field is for entering any notes from the coach.
*   **Observed By Title (observed_by_title)**: This field is not currently used in this form.
*   **SAS Coaching Status (sas_coaching_status)**: This field is a dropdown menu for selecting the coaching status.
*   **SAS Coaching Status Other (sas_coaching_status_other)**: This field is for entering the coaching status if not listed in the dropdown menu.
*   **SAS Coach Date (sas_coach_date)**: This field is for entering the date of the coach's next session.
*   **SAS Coach Time (sas_coach_time)**: This field is for entering the time of the coach's next session.
*   **SAS Coach Comments (sas_coach_comments)**: This field is for entering any comments from the coach.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out each field as accurately and completely as possible.
*   Review your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and consistency.
*   Use this form for tracking and recording observations and coaching interactions.
*   This form is not intended for sharing confidential information, so be mindful of sensitive data. 
*   If you have any questions or need assistance, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
