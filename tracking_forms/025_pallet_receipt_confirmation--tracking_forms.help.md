# Pallet Receipt Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pallet Receipt Confirmation form is used to track and confirm the reception of pallets. Warehouse staff should complete this form after each pallet is delivered, to ensure accurate tracking and processing of the received pallets.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Ensure that you have the correct Pallet ID, Supplier ID, and Delivery Location selected.
3. Enter the correct Date and Time of shipment and reception.
4. Check the condition of the pallet and select the correct option.
5. Record any relevant Remarks.
6. Confirm that the signature is correct and enter the date and time of the signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pallet Receipt**: This is the main section of the form, where you enter the pallet details.
* **Date**: Enter the date you received the pallet.
* **Pallet ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the pallet.
* **Supplier ID**: Choose the correct supplier for the pallet.
* **Supplier Name**: Enter the name of the supplier.
* **Delivery Location**: Choose where the pallet was delivered to.
* **Pallet Weight**: Enter the weight of the pallet.
* **Shipment Date**: Enter the date the pallet was shipped.
* **Shipment Time**: Enter the time the pallet was shipped.
* **Received Date**: Enter the date you received the pallet.
* **Received Time**: Enter the time you received the pallet.
* **Pallet Condition**: Choose the condition of the pallet.
* **Remarks**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the pallet.
* **Signed By**: Choose who signed the form.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Signature Time**: Enter the time you signed the form.

## Tips
- Always complete the form carefully and accurately.
- Ensure that you have the correct information before submitting the form.
- Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submission.
- Do not submit the form if the pallet has already been received and processed in the system.
