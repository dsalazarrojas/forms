# Experience and Purchase Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Experience and Purchase Tracker form is used to collect data on customer interactions, including their experiences and purchases. This form will help your organization analyze customer feedback and behavior to inform business decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
2. Select the customer's country of residence from the "Customer Address" dropdown list.
3. Enter the customer's city in the "City" field.
4. Select the date and time of the customer's experience in the "Experience Date" and "Experience Time" fields.
5. Indicate whether the customer made a purchase in the "Purchase" field.
6. If a purchase was made, enter the date and time of the purchase in the "Purchase Date" and "Purchase Time" fields.
7. Enter the amount of the purchase in the "Purchase Amount" field and select the currency in the "Purchase Currency" field.
8. Choose the method of the purchase in the "Purchase Method" field.
9. Rate the customer's experience in the "Customer Survey" field.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" fields.
11. Answer the yes or no questions in the "Customer Survey 1" field.
12. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Customer Notes 1" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Experience** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the customer's experience.
* **Customer Purchase** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the purchase made.
* **Customer Service** (Select Multiple, Optional): Rate the customer service received.
* **Customer Feedback** (Text, Optional): Enter any feedback or comments from the customer.
* **Is the customer a prophet?** (Number, Optional): Indicate whether the customer is a prophet or not.
* **Customer Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Address** (Select One, Required): Select the customer's country of residence.
* **City** (Text, Required): Enter the customer's city.
* **State** (Text, Optional): Enter the customer's state or province.
* **Postal Code** (Text, Optional): Enter the customer's postal code or zip code.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the customer.
* **Experience Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the customer's experience.
* **Experience Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time of the customer's experience.
* **Purchase** (Select Multiple, Required): Indicate whether a purchase was made.
* **Purchase Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the purchase (if applicable).
* **Purchase Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the purchase (if applicable).
* **Purchase Amount** (Number, Required): Enter the amount of the purchase.
* **Purchase Currency** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the currency of the purchase.
* **Purchase Method** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the method of the purchase.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the purchase.
* **Customer Survey** (Select Multiple, Required): Rate the customer's experience.
* **Customer Survey Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the customer's experience.
* **Customer Survey 1** (Select Multiple, Optional): Answer the yes or no question.
* **Customer Notes 1** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the customer.

## Tips

* Ensure that you are filling out the form for the correct customer.
* Enter accurate and clear information for each field.
* Review and edit your entries as needed before submitting the form.
