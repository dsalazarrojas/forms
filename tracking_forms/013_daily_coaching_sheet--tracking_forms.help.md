# Daily Coaching Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Coaching Sheet is designed to help coaches track and record their coaching activities, student progress, and feedback on a daily basis. It is an essential tool for maintaining accurate records and providing valuable insights for future coaching sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the number of lessons completed by students.
2. Record the number of lessons planned for the next day.
3. Enter the student's attendance percentage.
4. Update the student's progress by providing a numerical value.
5. Write any comments or feedback from the teacher.
6. Write any comments or feedback from the student.
7. Record any questions or concerns raised by the student.
8. Collect feedback from the student on their coaching experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lessons Completed**: Enter the number of lessons completed by students. This helps coaches track student progress and stay on top of their coaching activities.
* **Lessons Planned**: Record the number of lessons planned for the next day. This helps coaches stay organized and ensure students are on track with their learning goals.
* **Student Attendance**: Enter the student's attendance percentage, which helps coaches assess student engagement and participation.
* **Student Progress**: Update the student's progress by providing a numerical value, which helps coaches monitor student achievement and adjust their coaching strategies accordingly.
* **Teacher Comments**: Write any comments or feedback from the teacher, which helps coaches stay informed about student performance and adjust their coaching approach.
* **Student Comments**: Write any comments or feedback from the student, which helps coaches understand student perspectives and preferences.
* **Student Questions**: Record any questions or concerns raised by the student, which helps coaches address student needs and provide targeted support.
* **Student Feedback**: Collect feedback from the student on their coaching experience, which helps coaches evaluate their effectiveness and make improvements.
* **Coach Comments**: Write any comments or feedback from the coach, which helps maintain a record of coaching activities and facilitate future reference.
