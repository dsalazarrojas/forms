# Trip Planning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about travel planning, allowing users to provide details about their destination, travel dates, costs, and other relevant trip details. This information can be useful for tracking and planning trip-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the destination of your trip.
2. Provide the travel dates.
3. Select the travel time.
4. Choose the type of accommodation you will be staying in.
5. Select the mode(s) of travel you will be using.
6. Choose the type(s) of travel documents required.
7. Enter flight details (if applicable).
8. Enter flight number and seat number (if applicable).
9. Enter departure and arrival dates.
10. Enter travel cost and cost for travel mode, travel documents, and other costs (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Destination** (destination, text, optional): Enter the name of the destination of your trip.
- **Travel Dates** (travel_dates, date, optional): Enter the dates you plan to travel.
- **Travel Time** (travel_time, time, optional): Select the time you plan to travel.
- **Accommodation** (accommodation, select_one, optional): Choose the type of accommodation you will be staying in (e.g., hotel, inn, etc.).
- **Travel Mode** (travel_mode, select_multiple, optional): Select the mode(s) of travel you will be using (e.g., car, plane, etc.).
- **Travel Documents** (travel_documents, select_one, optional): Choose the type of travel document(s) you will be required to bring (e.g., driver's license, passport, etc.).
- **Flight Details** (flight_details, text, optional): Enter any additional flight details.
- **Flight Number** (flight_number, text, optional): Enter your flight number.
- **Seat Number** (seat_number, text, optional): Enter your seat number.
- **Departure Date** (departure_date, date, optional): Enter your departure date.
- **Arrival Date** (arrival_date, date, optional): Enter your arrival date.
- **Travel Cost** (travel_cost, number, optional): Enter the cost of your travel.
- **Travel Mode Cost** (travel_mode_cost, number, optional): Enter the cost of your travel mode.
- **Travel Docs Cost** (travel_docs_cost, number, optional): Enter the cost of your travel documents.
- **Travel Docs Type** (travel_docs_type, text, optional): Enter the type of travel documents you have.
- **Flight Status** (flight_status, select_one, optional): Choose the status of your flight (e.g., on-time, delayed, etc.).
