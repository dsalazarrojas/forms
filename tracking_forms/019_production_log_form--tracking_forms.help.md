# Production Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Production Log Form is designed to help you record and track production activities. This form is a simple tool for documenting and maintaining a record of production-related data.

## How To Complete This Form

1. This form is divided into several sections, each with specific fields that you need to fill out to track and document production activities. Please follow the fields explained below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **production_log**: (1/10) This is the main production log field where you enter the description of your production activity. You can be as brief or as detailed as needed, depending on the specifics of your production activity.
- **production_date**: (2/10) Enter the date when your production activity took place. This field helps in tracking the timeline of your production activities.
- **start_time**: (3/10) Fill out this field with the start time of your production activity.
- **end_time**: (4/10) Enter the end time of your production activity. This field is essential for tracking the duration and progress of your production activities.
- **production_location**: (5/10) Provide the location where your production activity took place. This can be a specific room, area, or site.
- **equipment_used**: (6/10) List the equipment used during your production activity. This field helps in identifying the resources and tools utilized for the production activity.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the fields accurately and completely to ensure that the data is reliable and can be used effectively.
- If a field is optional, only fill it out if it is relevant to your specific production activity.
- If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
