# Standard And Technical Work Log Templates - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Standard And Technical Work Log Templates" form is designed to help track and record work logs for standard and technical tasks. It captures various information such as date, category, and notes related to the work done.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Technical Log" field and choose between "Tracking" and "Non-Tracking" options.
2. Enter the "Log Date" field with the date of the log entry.
3. Enter the "Work Start Date" field with the start date of the work done.
4. Enter the "Work End Date" field with the end date of the work done.
5. Select the "Category" field and choose between "Tracking" and "Non-Tracking" options.
6. Optionally, enter the "Notes" field with any additional information related to the work done.
7. Enter the "Troubleshooting Log" field with any relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Troubleshooting Log** (1, text, required): This field is for free-form input and is used to record any troubleshooting information related to the work done.
* **Technical Log** (2, select_multiple, required): This field is for selecting between "Tracking" and "Non-Tracking" options. Choose the relevant option that best describes the type of work done.
* **Log Date** (3, date, required): This field is for recording the date of the log entry. Enter the date in a standard date format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Work Start Date** (4, date, required): This field is for recording the start date of the work done.
* **Work End Date** (5, date, required): This field is for recording the end date of the work done.
* **Category** (6, select_one, required): This field is for selecting between "Tracking" and "Non-Tracking" options. Choose the relevant option that best describes the category of work done.
* **Notes** (7, text, optional): This field is for free-form input and is used to record any additional information related to the work done.
