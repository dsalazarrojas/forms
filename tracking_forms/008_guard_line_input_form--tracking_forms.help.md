# Guard Line Input Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information related to guard lines, including general details, specific guard line information, and patrol information. The goal is to provide a clear and concise way to track and record important data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the General Information section with relevant details.
2. Provide specific information about the guard line in the Guard Line Info section.
3. Enter any patrol information in the Patrol Info section.
4. Record any incident-related information in the Incident Info section.
5. Add any relevant comments or log entries in the Guard Line Log section.
6. Include any additional notes in the Notes section.
7. Choose the correct status for the Location.
8. Fill in the name of the guard line if applicable.
9. If necessary, select a date and time for the event.
10. Finally, submit the form.
