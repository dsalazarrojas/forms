# Student Volunteer Hour Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Volunteer Hour Log Form is a simple tracking tool designed to record hours spent volunteering outside of work hours by students. The form gathers information on student's volunteer activities, such as hours worked, organization involved, and date of service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the student who is logging their volunteer hours.
2. Enter the name of the organization they volunteered with.
3. Enter the total number of hours spent volunteering for that organization.
4. Select the date of when the volunteer work was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_volunteer_hours**: Enter the total number of hours spent volunteering.
* **volunteer_organization**: Enter the name of the organization where the volunteer work was completed.
* **volunteer_hours**: Enter the total number of hours spent volunteering at the organization.
* **volunteer_hours_date**: Select the date when the volunteer work was completed.

## Tips
* Be thorough when recording hours to ensure accurate tracking.
* Ensure to enter the correct organization name for accurate representation.
* Select the correct date to associate with the volunteer hours.
