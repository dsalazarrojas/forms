# Wildlife Tracking Equipment Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about wildlife tracking equipment issues, maintenance needs, and other concerns that require attention from the conservation team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Submit your issue**: Fill in the "Equipment Issue Reported" field to describe the problem with your tracking equipment.
2. **Provide a detailed description**: Type in the "Issue Description" field to give additional context about the issue you're experiencing.
3. **Upload evidence**: Attach any relevant images, documents, or other files that support your issue in the "Upload Evidence" field.
4. **Select maintenance priority**: Choose the level of priority for the maintenance needed in the "Maintenance Priority" field.
5. **Check your tracking device status**: Select whether your tracking device is functional, requires maintenance, or is lost/destroyed in the "Tracking Device Status" field.
6. **Set issue priority**: Enter your assessment of the issue's priority in the "Issue Priority" field.
7. **Identify conservation team involvement**: Check if the conservation team should be involved in the issue resolution in the "Conservation Team" field.
8. **Additional comments**: Provide any additional information or comments about your issue in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Issue Reported**: This field is where you describe the problem with your tracking equipment.
* **Issue Description**: This field is for providing more context about the issue, such as symptoms or circumstances surrounding it.
* **Upload Evidence**: Attach any images, documents, or other files that support your issue.
* **Maintenance Priority**: Select the level of priority for the maintenance needed, with options being Low, Medium, and High.
* **Tracking Device Status**: Choose the status of your tracking device, which can be Functional, Maintenance Required, or Lost/Destroyed.
* **Issue Priority**: Enter your assessment of the issue's priority.
* **Conservation Team**: Check if the conservation team should be involved in resolving the issue.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any extra information or comments about your issue.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing your issue in the "Equipment Issue Reported" and "Issue Description" fields.
* Attach relevant evidence to support your issue in the "Upload Evidence" field.
* Choose the correct maintenance priority to ensure prompt and effective response from the conservation team.
* Double-check your tracking device status to ensure accurate assessment.
* If the conservation team should be involved, select "Yes" in the "Conservation Team" field.
* Use the "Additional Comments" field for any extra information or clarification that may be needed.
