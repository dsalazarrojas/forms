# Lumber Inventory Update Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lumber Inventory Update Log form is used to record updates to the lumber inventory, including new arrivals, removals, and adjustments. This form ensures accurate tracking of quantity, type, and value of lumber stock, as well as any changes to the stock balance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Yard Update" field and enter a brief description of the update.
2. Select the "Warehouse" field and choose the relevant warehouse for the update.
3. Select the "Lumber Type" field and select the type of lumber being updated.
4. Enter the "Quantity" of lumber being updated.
5. Enter the "Unit Price" of the lumber (if applicable).
6. Enter the "Total Value" of the lumber (if applicable).
7. Enter a "Reason" for the update (if applicable).
8. Enter any "Notes" related to the update.
9. Enter the "Updated By" field and select the person responsible for the update (if applicable).
10. Enter the "Assigned To" field and select the person assigned to follow up on the update (if applicable).
11. Enter the "Submitted By" field and select the person submitting the update (if applicable).
12. Enter the "Submitted At" field and select the date and time of submission.
13. Enter the "Submitted By Phone" field and enter the phone number of the person submitting the update (if applicable).
14. Enter the "Submitted By Email" field and enter the email address of the person submitting the update (if applicable).
15. Enter the "Assigned To Phone" field and enter the phone number of the person assigned to follow up (if applicable).
16. Enter the "Submitted At Phone" field and select the time of submission (if applicable).
17. Enter the "Submitted At Date" field and select the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lumber Inventory Update** (Text): Enter a brief description of the update.
* **Yard Update** (Text): Select the type of update (New Stock Arrival, Stock Removal, or Adjustment).
* **Warehouse** (Select Multiple): Select the warehouse where the lumber is stored.
* **Lumber Type** (Select One): Select the type of lumber being updated (Softwoods, Hardwoods, or Plywood).
* **Quantity** (Number): Enter the quantity of lumber being updated.
* **Unit Price** (Number): Enter the price of the lumber per unit (if applicable).
* **Total Value** (Number): Enter the total value of the lumber being updated (if applicable).
* **Reason** (Text): Enter a reason for the update (if applicable).
* **Notes** (Note): Enter any additional notes related to the update.
* **Updated By** (Email): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the update (if applicable).
* **Assigned To** (Text): Select the person assigned to follow up on the update (if applicable).
* **Submitted By** (Select Multiple): Select the person submitting the update (Operations Manager, Production Supervisor, or Inventory Clerk).
* **Submitted At** (Date): Select the date and time of submission.
* **Submitted By Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the update (if applicable).
* **Submitted By Email** (Email): Enter the email address of the person submitting the update (if applicable).
* **Assigned To Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the person assigned to follow up (if applicable).
* **Submitted At Phone** (Text): Enter the time of submission (if applicable).
* **Submitted At Date** (Date): Select the date of submission.

This guide should be easy to follow and understand, even for users unfamiliar with the form.
