# Warehouse Delivery Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Delivery Check In Form is used to collect essential information about a delivery's arrival, allowing warehouse staff to accurately record and verify delivery details.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the delivery details, including the delivery date and time.
2. Provide the driver's name and contact information (if available).
3. Enter the delivery number and shipment ID (if known).
4. Add any additional delivery notes, if necessary.
5. Indicate if the delivery is assigned to a dock (yes or no).
6. Enter the dock name and vehicle number (if applicable).
7. Record the contact person's name (if available).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Details** (delivery_details): Enter a brief description of the delivery, including any relevant information about the shipment.
* **Delivery Date Time** (delivery_date_time): Record the date and time of the delivery.
* **Driver Name** (driver_name): Enter the name of the person making the delivery.
* **Delivery Number** (delivery_number): Enter the delivery number or other identifying information for the shipment.
* **Shipment ID** (shipment_id): Enter the shipment ID or other identifying information for the shipment.
* **Delivery Notes** (delivery_notes): Add any additional notes about the delivery, if necessary.
* **Dock Assignment** (dock_assignment): Indicate if the delivery is assigned to a dock (yes or no).
* **Dock Name** (dock_name): Enter the name of the dock assigned to the delivery (if applicable).
* **Vehicle Number** (vehicle_number): Enter the vehicle number or other identifying information for the vehicle making the delivery.
* **Contact Name** (contact_name): Enter the name of the contact person for the delivery (if available).
