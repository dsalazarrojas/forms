The Narcotic Reconciliation Form is used to track the reconciliation of narcotics, including drugs received, used, and discarded. This form helps in maintaining accurate records and ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements.

2. Review the form to understand the purpose and scope of the reconciliation process.
