# Kite Damage Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kite Damage Report Form is used to document and report damages sustained by kites, allowing for thorough analysis, assessment, and resolution of the incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field completely and accurately, paying attention to the specific type of information requested.
2. For required fields, ensure that you provide the necessary information to prevent form submission failures.
3. Double-check your input before submitting the form to ensure correctness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. User Information
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact details to report the incident.

### 2. Incident Date
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Record the date of the incident, when the damage occurred.

### 3. Incident Time
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Note the time of day when the incident happened.

### 4. Equipment Type
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_one, optional): Indicate if the damaged equipment was a kite or another type of equipment.

### 5. Damage Description
* **Damage Description** (`damage_description`, text, optional): Describe the nature and extent of the damage sustained by the kite.

### 6. Damage Image
* **Damage Image** (`damage_image`, text, optional): Attach an image of the damaged area for reference and analysis.

### 7. Witness Names
* **Witness Names** (`witness_names`, select_multiple, optional): Identify any witnesses present during the incident, selecting one or more from the available options.

### 8. Witness Contacts
* **Witness Contacts** (`witness_contacts`, select_multiple, optional): Provide contact information for any witnesses, selecting one or more from the available options.

### 9. Assigned To
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate who was assigned to handle the incident, selecting one or more from the available options.

**Tips**
* Review your input carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If unsure about any field, refer to the guidelines above or consult with the relevant personnel.
* This form will help to gather necessary information for swift and effective incident response and resolution.
