# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track your daily habits and activities for health and wellness purposes. Please provide accurate and honest answers to give yourself a comprehensive picture of your health status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant fields that apply to your daily activities.
2. Enter your answers in the corresponding fields.
3. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intake Day** (`intake_day`, number, optional): Enter the day of the month when you last took food and drink. This field helps track your eating habits over time.
* **Intake Time** (`intake_time`, date, optional): Record the time when you last ate or consumed food and drinks.
* **Calories Burned** (`calories_burned`, number, optional): Report the number of calories you burned during physical activity, such as exercise or sports.
* **Water Consumed** (`water_consumed`, number, optional): Record the amount of water you consumed in ounces.
* **Exercise Duration** (`exercise_duration`, time, optional): Report the length of time spent exercising.
* **Sleep Duration** (`sleep_duration`, time, optional): Record the length of time slept.
* **Food Consumed** (`food_consumed`, text, optional): Describe the food you consumed, including any specific foods or snacks.
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, number, optional): Report how many times you exercised during the day.
* **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, select one, optional): Rate your sleep quality as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **User Notes** (`user_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your day.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool or equipment used for the assigned activity.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter the form ID for identification purposes.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, text, optional): Enter the version of the form used.
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Select the category or group you belong to.
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the form.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the form.
* **Prophet's Favorite Food** (`prophets_fav_food`, select multiple, optional): Select the foods you like, including pizza, burgers, sushi, tacos, ice cream, cake, fruit, salad, chicken, steak, fish, fries, chicken wing, eggs, sandwiches, and pizza wing, and popcorn.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will help you gain a better understanding of your health and wellness status.
