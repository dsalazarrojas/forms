# Warranty Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warranty Claim Form is designed for customers to report issues with their products. This form allows customers to submit claims, provide detailed descriptions of the problems, and track the status of their claims. It also enables customers to communicate with assigned staff members for resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you're submitting a genuine warranty claim and not duplicating an existing claim.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate information:
	* Customer Name
	* Customer Email
	* Date Purchased
	* Claim Status (if applicable)
3. Provide detailed descriptions of the issues you're experiencing with your product in the following fields:
	* Product Description
	* Claim Details
	* Customer Notes (Customer Note, Customer Note 2, and Customer Note 3)
4. Attach any relevant proof of purchase documents, such as receipts or invoices.
5. Select the correct claim type (new, repaired, or replacement product) in the Warranty Claim field.
6. Upload any supporting images or attachments that can help explain the issue.
7. Sign the form to acknowledge your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Warranty Claim** (`warranty_claim_form`): This is the main form where you submit your warranty claim.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Enter your full name as the customer who purchased the product.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter the email address associated with your purchase.
* **Date Purchased** (`date_purchased`): Enter the date you purchased the product.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`): Describe the product you're claiming issues with.
* **Proof of Purchase** (`proof_of_purchace`): Attach a receipt, invoice, or other proof of purchase document to support your claim.
* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`): Provide a detailed description of the issue you're experiencing with your product.
* **Customer Contact** (`customer_contact`): Enter your contact information for communication purposes.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`): Select the status of your claim from the options provided (Pending, Approved, Rejected, Closed).
* **Warranty Claim (2)** (`warranty_claim`): Select the type of claim you're submitting (new, repaired, or replacement product).
* **Product Image** (`product_image`): Upload images that can help explain the issue you're experiencing.
* **Attachment** (`attachment_image`): Upload any supporting documents or images.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`): Sign the form to acknowledge your claim.
* **Customer Note** (`customer_note`): Enter any additional notes or comments about your claim.
* **Customer Note 2** (`customer_note_2`): Enter any further notes or comments.
* **Customer Note 3** (`customer_note_3`): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`): Select the staff member assigned to handle your claim.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`): Enter the date when the claim was assigned to the staff member.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`): The due date for the staff member to complete the claim.
* **Completed Date** (`completed_date`): The date when the claim was completed.

Note: Some fields might not be applicable for your specific situation; fill them out accordingly.
