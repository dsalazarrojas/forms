This form is designed to collect general notes from users. It is intended for non-technical users to provide information that is not necessarily related to a specific project or task. This form is meant to be a catch-all for any additional information that doesn't fit into other more specific forms. Before filling out this form, please ensure that you do not have any other form that suits your purpose already available, and you are not duplicating information across multiple forms.

## Purpose
The General Notes Collection Form is a flexible form designed to gather additional information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter a title for the form to help identify the purpose of the notes.
*   Choose a category that best describes the nature of the notes.
*   Assign a priority level to the notes (optional).
*   Attach any relevant files to the form.
*   Enter the date and time of the notes (optional).
*   Add a personal note to provide additional context.
*   Enter your email address (optional).
*   Enter your phone number (optional).
*   Click the "Submit" button to save the form.

*   **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for the form to help identify its purpose.

*   **Priority** (`form_priority`, select_multiple, optional): Assign a priority level to the notes. This can be "Low", "Medium", or "High".
*   **Attach Files** (`form_file`, text, optional): Attach any relevant files to the form.

*   **Time** (`form_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the notes.
*   **Form Note** (`form_note`, note, optional): Add a personal note to provide additional context.
*   **Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
