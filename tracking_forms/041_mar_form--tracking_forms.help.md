# MAR Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MAR Form is used for tracking patient data for medications administered. It is designed to be a simple and concise way for medical professionals to record the necessary information about the medication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the medication administered in the "Medication Administered" field.
3. Enter the dosage of the medication in the "Dosage" field.
4. Enter the frequency of administration in the "Frequency" field.
5. Enter the route of administration (e.g. oral, intravenous) in the "Route" field.
6. Enter the time the medication was administered in the "Time" field.
7. Enter the date the medication was administered in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name, as it appears in their medical record.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No
- **Medication Administered**: Enter the name of the medication given to the patient.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No
- **Dosage**: Enter the amount of medication given to the patient.
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No
- **Frequency**: Enter how often the medication is to be administered (e.g. daily, weekly).
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No
- **Route**: Enter the way the medication was administered (e.g. oral, intravenous).
  * Type: Text field
  * Required: No
- **Time**: Enter the time the medication was administered (using a 24-hour clock format).
  * Type: Time field
  * Required: No
- **Date**: Enter the date the medication was administered (using the standard date format).
  * Type: Date field
  * Required: No

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check the information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
