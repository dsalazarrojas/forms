# Daily Food Temperature Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Food Temperature Form is designed to track the temperature of food items stored in the facility. This form helps in maintaining accurate records of food items, their storage locations, and the individuals responsible for storing and checking them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Food Item" field with the name of the food item being tracked.
2. Enter the "Start Date" and "Start Temperature" fields with the date and temperature readings of the food item at the time it was stored.
3. Record the "End Date" and "End Temperature" fields with the date and temperature readings of the food item at the time it was checked.
4. Enter the "Storage Area" field with the location where the food item is stored.
5. Fill in the "Storage Location" field with the specific location within the storage area where the food item is stored.
6. Enter the "Stored By" field with the name of the person who stored the food item.
7. Fill in the "Checked By" field with the name of the person who checked the food item.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Food Item** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the food item being tracked.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): Record the date when the food item was stored.
* **Start Temperature** (`start_temperature`, `number`, optional): Record the initial temperature reading of the food item when it was stored.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): Record the date when the food item was checked.
* **End Temperature** (`end_temperature`, `number`, optional): Record the temperature reading of the food item when it was checked.
* **Storage Area** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter the location or department where the food item is stored.
* **Storage Location** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter the specific location or shelf where the food item is stored.
* **Stored By** (`name`, `text`, optional): Record the name of the person who stored the food item.
* **Checked By** (`name`, `text`, optional): Record the name of the person who checked the food item.

**Tips**: Fill out this form accurately and completely for each food item. If the food item's temperature reading exceeds the acceptable limits, take action immediately to ensure food safety. Always verify the temperature readings with a reliable thermometer.
