# Sanitizing Disinfectant Products Needs Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sanitizing Disinfectant Products Needs Tracking Form is used to track the needs for sanitizing disinfectant products within the organization. This form is designed to monitor and report on the status of sanitizing disinfectant products.
## How To Complete This Form
1. **Form Title** (`form_title`): Enter a brief title for the submission, e.g., "Sanitizing Disinfectant Products for [Facility Location]".
2. **Category** (`category`): Select the category for the submission, either "Tracking Forms" or "Other".
3. **Description** (`description`): Provide a brief description of the need for sanitizing disinfectant products.
4. **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Choose the tool assigned to handle the request, either "chatjimmy" or "Other".
5. **Form ID** (`form_id`): Enter a unique identifier for the submission.
6. **Output File** (`output_file`): Provide an optional output file for additional information (e.g., photos, documents).
7. **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Check if this submission was made by a user or tool, either "Yes" or "No".
8. **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`): Enter the email address of the person submitting the request (if submitted by a user).
9. **Vendor ID** (`vendor_id`): Enter a unique vendor ID (if applicable).
10. **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`): Enter the date and time of submission (if applicable).
11. **Created By** (`created_by`): Check if this submission was created by a user or tool, either "Yes" or "No".
12. **Form Ids** (`form_ids`): Enter a list of form IDs (if applicable).
13. **Submitted At Date Time** (`submitted_at_date_time`): Enter the date and time of submission (if applicable).
14. **Updated At** (`updated_at`): Enter the date and time of the last update (if applicable).
15. **Updated By** (`updated_by`): Check if this submission was updated by a user or tool, either "Yes" or "No".
