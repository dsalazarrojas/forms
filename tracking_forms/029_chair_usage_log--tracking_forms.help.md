# Chair Usage Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chair Usage Log is a form designed to track the usage of chairs within an office or similar setting. It is intended to be completed by employees or authorized personnel after each use of a chair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Select the "Log Date" field to choose the date of chair usage.
2. Enter the "User Name" field with your full name.
3. Enter the "Email Address" field with your email for notifications.
4. Select your department from the "Department" field.
5. Enter the "Chair ID Number" field with the identification number of the chair used.
6. Select the location where the chair was used from the "Chair Location" field.
7. Choose the time when the chair was taken into use from the "Checkout Time" field.
8. Choose the time when the chair was returned from the "Return Time" field (if applicable).
9. Select the purpose of chair usage from the "Usage Purpose" field.
10. Indicate the condition of the chair when taken (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Damaged).
11. Indicate the condition of the chair when returned (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Damaged).
12. Select if any damage was noted during use from the "Damage Noted" field.
13. If applicable, describe any damage from the "Damage Description" field.
14. Select if the chair needs cleaning from the "Cleaning Required" field.
15. Select if the chair needs maintenance from the "Maintenance Needed" field.
16. If applicable, describe maintenance needs from the "Maintenance Details" field.
17. Select if the chair was returned to its original location from the "Returned to Original Location" field.
18. If not returned to the original location, enter the new location from the "New Location" field.
19. Select the duration of chair usage from the "Usage Duration" field.
20. Enter your name as the person completing this log from the "Logged By" field.
21. Sign the log with your full name as "Log Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Log Date
* **Log Date** (`log_date`, date, required): Enter the date of chair usage.

### User Name
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.

### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your email for notifications.

### Department
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Select your department.

### Chair ID Number
* **Chair ID Number** (`chair_id`, text, required): Enter the identification number of the chair used.

### Chair Location
* **Chair Location** (`chair_location`, select_one, required): Select where the chair was used.

### Checkout Time
* **Checkout Time** (`checkout_time`, time, required): Choose the time when the chair was taken into use.

### Return Time
* **Return Time** (`return_time`, time, required): Choose the time when the chair was returned (if applicable).

### Usage Purpose
* **Usage Purpose** (`usage_purpose`, select_one, required): Select the purpose of chair usage.

### Condition at Checkout
* **Condition at Checkout** (`condition_at_checkout`, select_one, required): Indicate the condition of the chair when taken (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Damaged).

### Condition at Return
* **Condition at Return** (`condition_at_return`, select_one, required): Indicate the condition of the chair when returned (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Damaged).

### Damage Noted
* **Damage Noted** (`damage_noted`, select_one, required): Select if any damage was noted during use.

### Damage Description
* **Damage Description** (`damage_description`, text, required): If applicable, describe any damage.

### Cleaning Required
* **Cleaning Required** (`cleaning_required`, select_one, required): Select if the chair needs cleaning.

### Maintenance Needed
* **Maintenance Needed** (`maintenance_needed`, select_one, required): Select if the chair needs maintenance.

### Maintenance Details
* **Maintenance Details** (`maintenance_details`, text, required): If applicable, describe maintenance needs.

### Returned to Original Location
* **Returned to Original Location** (`returned_to_original_location`, select_one, required): Select if the chair was returned to its original location.

### New Location
* **New Location** (`new_location`, text, required): If not returned to the original location, enter the new location.

### Usage Duration
* **Usage Duration** (`usage_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration of chair usage.

### Logged By
* **Logged By** (`logged_by`, text, required): Enter your name as the person completing this log.

### Log Signature
* **Log Signature** (`log_signature`, text, required): Sign the log with your full name.

## Tips
* Always enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that the chair usage data is reliable.
* Do not complete this form for duplicate usage.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or the facilities team for guidance.
* This form is designed to track chair usage patterns and condition for the purpose of maintenance and cleaning.
