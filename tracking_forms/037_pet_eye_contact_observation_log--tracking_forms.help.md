# Pet Eye Contact Observation Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Eye Contact Observation Log is designed to help veterinary professionals and pet owners track and document the eye health of pets. It allows users to record observations and track progress over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the session date for the observation
2. Enter the owner's name
3. Select the eye condition (e.g., Normal, Redness, Discharge, etc.)
4. Provide a brief description of the eye condition
5. Indicate the type of contact (direct or indirect)
6. Specify the area of contact
7. Rate the level of comfort during the contact
8. Choose if there was any change in the eye condition after the observation
9. Enter any additional comments or notes
10. Set a date for the next session

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Date** (`Session Date`, date): Record the date of the observation.
- **Owner Name** (`Owner Name`, text): Enter the name of the pet owner or individual responsible for the pet's care.
- **Eye Condition** (`Eye Condition`, select_one): Select the current state of the pet's eye condition, such as Normal, Redness, Discharge, etc.
- **Eye Condition Details** (`Eye Condition Details`, text): Provide a brief description of the observed eye condition.
- **Contact Type** (`Contact Type`, select_multiple): Indicate the type of contact (direct or indirect).
- **Contact Area** (`Contact Area`, text): Specify the area of contact (e.g., cornea, iris, etc.).
- **Comfort Level** (`Comfort Level`, select_one): Rate the level of comfort during the contact (e.g., Very Comfortable, Somewhat Comfortable, Neutral, etc.).
- **Eye Condition Change** (`Eye Condition Change`, select_multiple): Choose if there was any change in the eye condition after the observation (Improved, No Change, Worsened, etc.).
- **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text): Enter any additional comments or notes about the observation.
- **Next Session Date** (`Next Session Date`, date): Set a date for the next session or follow-up observation.
