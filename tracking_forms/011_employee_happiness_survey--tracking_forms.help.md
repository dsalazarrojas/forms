# Employee Happiness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Happiness Survey is designed to gather information about your feelings and experiences as an employee. This survey is used to help us understand what matters most to you and identify areas for improvement in the workplace. Please answer the questions honestly, and your responses will be kept confidential.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each section and answer the questions to the best of your ability. You may skip any questions that do not apply to you. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you generally feel satisfied with your job. This question helps us understand your overall sentiment.
* **Job Satisfaction**: Describe in a few words why you feel satisfied or not with your job. This will help us understand what matters most to you in your job.
* **Manager Satisfaction**: Describe in a few words why you feel satisfied or not with your manager. This will help us understand what qualities you look for in a manager.
* **Team Satisfaction**: Describe in a few words why you feel satisfied or not with your team. This will help us understand what you value in a team dynamic.
* **Communication**: Describe in a few words how you experience communication in the workplace. This will help us understand areas for improvement in communication.
* **Job Security**: Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you feel secure in your job. This question helps us understand your confidence in your job tenure.
* **Salary**: Please enter your current salary or a range that represents your salary. This helps us understand your compensation expectations.
* **Benefits**: Describe in a few words what benefits you value most in your job. This will help us understand your priorities.
* **How often do you feel satisfied with your job?**: Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate how often you feel satisfied with your job. This question helps us understand your job satisfaction patterns.
* **General Information 2**: Describe any additional information that may not fit in other questions. This is an open-ended question for you to express your thoughts freely.
* **Manager Information**: Describe any feedback or suggestions you have for your manager. This is an open-ended question for you to share your concerns or appreciation.
* **Team Information**: Describe any feedback or suggestions you have for your team. This is an open-ended question for you to share your concerns or appreciation.
* **Department Information**: Describe any feedback or suggestions you have for your department. This is an open-ended question for you to share your concerns or appreciation.
