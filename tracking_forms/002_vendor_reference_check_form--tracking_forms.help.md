# Vendor Reference Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a vendor for reference checks. This process is part of our business's vetting process for vendors we consider partnering with or working with. It helps us assess a vendor's contact details, company information, and references to ensure we're making informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct page (Contact Details, Company Details, or Reference) and start filling out the form.
2. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. For optional fields, provide as much information as you can.
4. Review each page carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Details Page
* **Contact Name** (`contact_details`): The full name of the contact person at the vendor.
* **Contact Title** (`contact_title`): The title of the contact person (e.g., CEO, Sales Manager, etc.).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): The phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): The email address of the contact person.

### Company Details Page
* **Company Size** (`company_size`): The size of the vendor company (Small, Medium, Large, or Enterprise).
* **Employee Count** (`employee_count`): The total number of employees at the vendor.
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`): The annual revenue of the vendor.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`): The type of company (Publicly Traded, Private, or Not For Profit).
* **Status** (`company_status`): The status of the vendor (Active, Inactive, or Terminated).

### Reference Page
* **Date of Reference** (`date_of_reference`): The date the reference was made.
* **Time of Reference** (`time_of_reference`): The time the reference was made.
* **Type of Reference** (`reference_type`): The type of reference (Positive, Negative, or Neutral).
* **Level of Reference** (`reference_level`): The level of reference (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Reference Description** (`reference_description`): A brief description of the reference.
* **Vendor Reference** (`vendor_reference`): Select relevant references (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3) to include with this form.
