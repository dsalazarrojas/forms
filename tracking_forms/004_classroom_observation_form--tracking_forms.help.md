# Classroom Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Classroom Observation Form is a tool used to gather information about the classroom environment, teaching practices, and student engagement during a lesson. This form helps observers assess the quality of instruction and provide feedback to teachers. It is intended for teachers, instructional coaches, or external evaluators to use during classroom observations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your observation.
3. Provide clear and concise answers to the open-ended questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Observer Name** (`observer_name`, Text, *): Enter the name of the person conducting the observation.
* **Observer Role** (`observer_role`, Select One): Choose your position or title, such as Administrator, Peer Teacher, Instructional Coach, or External Evaluator.
* **Teacher Observed** (`teacher_observed`, Text): Enter the name of the teacher being observed.
* **Observation Date** (`observation_date`, Date): Enter the date of the observation in MM/dd/yyyy format.
* **Observation Start Time** (`observation_start_time`, Time): Enter the time the observation began.
* **Observation End Time** (`observation_end_time`, Time): Enter the time the observation ended.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, Text): Choose the grade level of the class, such as elementary, middle school, high school, or college.
* **Subject Area** (`subject_area`, Text): Select the subject area being taught, such as math, science, language arts, or social studies.
* **Lesson Objective** (`lesson_objective`, Text): Describe the learning goal for the lesson.
* **Instructional Methods Used** (`instructional_methods`, Select Multiple): Select all the methods observed during the lesson, such as Direct Instruction/Lecture, Group Work, Individual Practice, or Technology Integration.
* **Student Participation Rate** (`student_participation_rate`, Select One): Choose the percentage of students actively participating in the lesson, from 25% to 100%.
* **Classroom Environment Quality** (`classroom_environment_quality`, Select One): Rate the physical and emotional environment, from Excellent to Poor.
* **Technology Integration** (`technology_integration`, Select One): Choose how technology was used in the lesson, from Not Used to Extensively.
* **Assessment Methods Observed** (`assessment_methods`, Select Multiple): Select all the assessment methods observed, such as Formative Questions, Exit Tickets, or Quizzes.
* **Evidence of Learning** (`evidence_of_learning`, Text): Describe what evidence you saw that students were learning.
* **Notable Strengths** (`notable_strengths`, Text): Identify the teaching practices that stood out positively.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, Text): Provide suggestions for improvement or growth.
* **Follow-Up Needed** (`follow_up_needed`, Select One): Choose whether a follow-up observation or conference is needed, from No to Yes.

Note: This form is intended to be used as a starting point for feedback and discussion, not as a formal evaluation or assessment tool.
