# Student Behavior Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Behavior Tracking Form is a tool for teachers and staff to report on student behavior, actions, and comments. This form helps to gather data for identifying areas where students may need additional support or resources.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are filling out the form for the correct student.
2. Fill in the relevant fields with the necessary information, such as general behavior, behavior during hours, teacher comments, and student comments.
3. Select the appropriate options for tracking form hours and assigned teacher.
4. Leave any fields that are not relevant or required blank.
5. Save your progress and review your submissions before finalizing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Behavior** (`general_behavior`, text, required: false):
	+ Use this field to describe a student's general behavior, such as their attitude or demeanor during class time.
* **Behavior during Hours** (`during_hours`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe a student's behavior during specific hours, such as during a particular time of day or day of the week.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, note, required: false):
	+ Enter any comments or observations made by the teacher about the student's behavior.
* **Student Comments** (`student_comments`, note, required: false):
	+ Enter any comments or observations made by the student about their own behavior.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false):
	+ Enter the student's email address for communication purposes.
* **During Certain Hours** (`during_certain_hours`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe a student's behavior during specific hours, such as during a particular time of day or day of the week.
* **Tracking Form Hours** (`tracking_form_hours`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select the hours when the student's behavior is being tracked, such as 8, 9, or 10.
* **Assigned Teacher** (`assigned_teacher`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select the teacher who is responsible for the student, such as Jim, Jane, or John.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, number, required: false):
	+ Leave this field blank, as it is not currently used.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form, especially in the teacher comments and student comments fields.
* Review your submissions carefully before finalizing the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please consult with your supervisor or contact the relevant department.
