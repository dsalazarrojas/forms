# AE Coaching Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and insights from employees after coaching sessions, aiming to improve future coaching experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and ensure you've answered every required question.
2. Enter your complete name as per your official records.
3. Provide a professional email address for communication purposes.
4. Enter your unique employee identifier (if applicable).
5. Select your department from the provided options.
6. Record the date and time of the coaching session.
7. Enter the name of your coach.
8. Indicate the session duration in minutes.
9. Choose the type of coaching session that took place.
10. Rate your satisfaction with the session.
11. Provide an overall rating of the session (1-10).
12. Indicate whether you'd like to schedule a follow-up coaching session.
13. If applicable, enter the tentative date for the next session.
14. For the experience assessment, select all aspects that applied to your coaching experience.
15. Briefly describe the main goals covered during the session.
16. Enumerate the action items agreed upon during the session.
17. Rate your confidence level in achieving your goals (1-10).
18. Provide open-ended feedback on how effective the coaching was in addressing your concerns.
19. Share any other thoughts or comments about the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your complete name as per your official records.
* **Email address**: (`email_address`, `email`, required): Provide a professional email address for communication purposes.
* **Employee ID**: (`employee_id`, `text`, optional): Enter your unique employee identifier (if applicable).
* **Department**: (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select your department from the provided options.
* **Date of coaching session**: (`coaching_session_date`, `date`, required): Record the date and time of the coaching session.
* **Coach name**: (`coach_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your coach.
* **Session duration**: (`session_duration`, `integer`, required): Indicate the session duration in minutes.
* **Type of coaching session**: (`session_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of coaching session that took place.
* **Satisfaction**: (`satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with the session.
* **Overall rating**: (`overall_rating`, `integer`, required): Provide an overall rating of the session (1-10).
* **Confidence level**: (`confidence_level`, `integer`, required): Rate your confidence level in achieving your goals (1-10).
* **Aspects applied to coaching experience**: (`experience_aspects`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all aspects that applied to your coaching experience.
* **Goals discussed**: (`goals_discussed`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the main goals covered during the session.
* **Action items agreed upon**: (`action_items_agreed`, `text`, optional): Enumerate the action items agreed upon during the session.
* **Feedback and Improvement**: (`open_feedback`, `text`, required): Provide open-ended feedback on how effective the coaching was in addressing your concerns.
* **Additional comments**: (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Share any other thoughts or comments about the session.
* **Follow-up needed**: (`follow_up_needed`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you'd like to schedule a follow-up coaching session.
* **Next session date**: (`next_session_date`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the tentative date for the next session.
