# office_first_aid_equipment_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The office first aid equipment checklist form is used to track the status of office first aid equipment and medical supplies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the current status of first aid equipment and medical supplies in the office.
2. Check each section of the form and mark the correct status for each item.
3. Fill in any necessary information and select the correct options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### office_first_aid_equipment_checklist_form_page_1_label ( office_first_aid_equipment_checklist_form_page_1_label )
This is the first page of the form, which is the first step in reviewing the status of office first aid equipment and medical supplies.

### first_aid_equipment (first aid equipment)
- **First aid equipment**: This section is used to track the status of all first aid equipment in the office.
- **Required**: This field is required because all offices are expected to have a certain level of first aid equipment available at all times.
- **Select all available equipment**: Choose all the first aid equipment that are available in the office.

### medical_supplies (medical supplies)
- **Medical supplies**: This field is used to track the quantity of medical supplies available in the office.
- **Required**: This field is required because medical supplies are essential for treating injuries and illnesses.
- **Number**: Enter the number of medical supplies available in the office.

### first_aid_equipment_2 (first aid equipment 2)
- **First aid equipment 2**: This section is used to track the status of additional first aid equipment in the office, beyond what is listed in the previous section.

### office_first_aid_equipment_3 (office first aid equipment 3)
- **Office first aid equipment 3**: This section is used to track the status of other first aid equipment in the office.

### medical_supplies_2 (medical supplies 2)
- **Medical supplies 2**: This field is used to track the quantity of medical supplies that will need to be restocked or reordered soon.
- **Date**: Enter the date by which these supplies will need to be restocked or reordered.

### first_aid_equipment_4 (first aid equipment 4)
- **First aid equipment 4**: This section is used to track whether there is a first aid kit available in the office.
- **Yes/No**: Select 'Yes' if a first aid kit is available, and 'No' otherwise.

### office_first_aid_equipment_4 (office first aid equipment 4)
- **Office first aid equipment 4**: This section is used to track whether other office first aid equipment is available.
- **Yes/No**: Select 'Yes' if other office first aid equipment is available, and 'No' otherwise.
