# student_records_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students about their records, including their name, academic performance, and other relevant details. The form will help administrators and teachers to track student progress and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with the required and optional fields as per the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the student's name as it appears on their records.
* **Grade** (`grade`, number, required: false): Enter the student's current grade level (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the student's exam.
* **Homework Date** (`homework_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the student submitted their homework.
* **Exam Results** (`exam_results`, select_one, required: false): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the student's exam results.
* **Homework Results** (`homework_results`, select_one, required: false): Choose 'Good', 'Average', or 'Below average' to indicate the student's homework results.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required: false): Enter the student's ID number as per the school's records.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate recording of student information.
* Use the correct date formats for dates fields (e.g., 'mm/dd/yyyy').
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult the student or the teacher for clarification.
