# Utility Cost And Vendor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Utility Cost And Vendor Information Form" is designed to collect information about your utility usage and vendor interactions. This form will help us better understand your needs and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Please answer all fields with the correct information**. If you are not sure about a question, try to answer with your best guess or leave it blank if you don't know.
- **Select the correct options** for questions that require a choice.
- **Enter numerical answers** for questions that ask for numbers.
- **Be honest and thorough** in your answers to provide accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What kind of utility do you use?** (`page_1`, `select_one`, required): This question is to determine the type of utility you use (e.g., electricity, gas, water, internet, or other).
- **Do you currently have a contract with your utility supplier?** (`page_2`, `select_one`, required): Check if you have a contract with your current supplier.
- **How do you typically make payments for your utility bills?** (`page_3`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the methods you use to pay your utility bills (e.g., online, in-person, by mail).
- **How much does your utility bill typically cost?** (`page_4`, `number`, required): Enter the approximate cost of your utility bill.
- **Do you agree with the prices of your current utility supplier?** (`page_5`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you agree or disagree with the prices of your current supplier.
- **Who do you contact for utility-related issues?** (`page_6`, `select_one`, required): Choose who you contact for issues related to your utility supplier.
- **What time of day do you typically pay your utility bills?** (`page_7`, `select_one`, required): Select the time of day you usually pay your bills.
- **Do you have any concerns about your utility supplier?** (`page_8`, `select_multiple`, required): Check any concerns you have about your supplier (e.g., quality of service, pricing, communication).
- **What is your current supplier's name?** (`page_9`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your current supplier.
- **How long have you been with your current supplier?** (`page_10`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years/months/weeks you've been with your supplier.
- **Do you have any other questions or comments about your utility supplier?** (`page_11`, `text`, optional): If you have any other questions or comments, feel free to type them here.
- **Do you agree with the terms and conditions of your contract with your utility supplier?** (`page_12`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you agree or disagree with the terms and conditions of your contract.
- **Do you have any other comments or feedback about your utility supplier?** (`page_13`, `text`, optional): If you have any other comments or feedback, feel free to type them here.
- **Do you have any other questions or concerns about this form?** (`page_14`, `text`, optional): If you have any questions or concerns about the form, type them here.
- **What is your preferred method of communication for utility-related issues?** (`page_15`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred method of communication (e.g., phone, email, chat).
- **What time of day do you prefer for utility-related communication?** (`page_16`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred time of day for communication.
- **Do you have any other comments or feedback about this form?** (`page_17`, `text`, optional): If you have any other comments or feedback, feel free to type them here.
- **Additional comments or feedback** (`page_18`, `text`, optional): If you have anything else you'd like to share, type it here.
