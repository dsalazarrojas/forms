## Purpose
The "Sprint Backlog Items Entry Form" is designed to collect data for sprint backlog items, which are used to track and manage project progress. This form helps users to input and store project details, sprint status, priority, start and end dates, assigned team members, comments, and attachments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the project you want to add a new sprint item for.
2. Choose the sprint for which you're submitting the item.
3. Select the priority level of the item (High, Low, or Medium).
4. Choose the status of the item (To-do, In progress, Done, or Blocked).
5. Input the start and end dates for the sprint item.
6. Assign team members to the item by selecting one or multiple users from the list.
7. Provide any additional comments for the item.
8. Attach relevant files or documents to the item.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project** (`project`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the project for which this sprint item is being submitted.
* **Sprint** (`sprint`, `text`, required/optional): Select the name of the sprint to which this item belongs.
* **Priority** (`priority`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the priority level of the item (High, Low, or Medium).
* **Status** (`status`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the status of the item (To-do, In progress, Done, or Blocked).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the start date of the sprint item.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the end date of the sprint item.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or multiple team members assigned to the item.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments about the sprint item.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, `text`, required/optional): Attach relevant files or documents to the sprint item.

## Tips

* Ensure you have the necessary permissions to edit and manage sprint items.
* Be concise when filling out the comments field to provide context and clarity.
* Use attachments to upload relevant documents and files for further reference.
* Review and update the sprint item as its status changes.
* Keep the sprint item's priority level aligned with project priorities.
