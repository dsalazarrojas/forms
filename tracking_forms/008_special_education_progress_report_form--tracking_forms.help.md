# Special Education Progress Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Education Progress Report Form is designed to track the progress of special education students, providing a structured way to collect relevant information from students, teachers, and other staff members. This form helps to ensure that everyone involved in the student's education is on the same page and can work collaboratively to support the student's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student being reported on.
* **Grade level** (`grade_level`, select one, optional): Select the current grade level of the student.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the progress report.
* **Special Instruction** (`special_instruction`, text, optional): Add any special instructions or notes for the student's teacher.
* **IEP Goals** (`iep_goals`, text, optional): Enter the Individualized Education Program (IEP) goals for the student.
* **Progress Reports** (`progress_reports`, text, optional): Add any progress reports or updates on the student's performance.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Student e-mail** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address (if applicable).
* **Teacher e-mail** (`teacher_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the student's teacher (if applicable).
* **Parent e-mail** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the student's parent (if applicable).
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the student or other staff member (if applicable).
* **Special Education Teacher** (`special_education_teacher`, text, optional): Enter the name of the special education teacher involved.
* **Special Education Assistant** (`special_education_assistant`, text, optional): Enter the name of the special education assistant involved.
* **Other Staff Member** (`other_staff_member`, text, optional): Enter the name of any other staff member involved in the student's education.

## Tips
* Make sure to keep the information up-to-date and accurate to ensure the student's progress is tracked effectively.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are filled in correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant staff member or supervisor for guidance.
