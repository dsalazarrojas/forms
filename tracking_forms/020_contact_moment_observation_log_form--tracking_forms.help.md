# Contact Moment Observation Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Moment Observation Log Form is a tool for quality assurance and coaching. It helps track observations of interactions between employees or agents, providing a way to evaluate performance and identify areas for development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the relevant page by choosing the "Interaction Observation Log" page.
2. Fill in your observer name in the "Observer Name" field.
3. Enter the name of the agent or employee being observed in the "Agent / Employee Observed" field.
4. Fill in the date of the observation in the "Date of Observation" field.
5. Choose the communication channel used for the observation in the "Communication Channel" field.
6. If applicable, fill in the duration of the observation in the "Duration (Minutes)" field.
7. Rate the professional greeting and introduction in the "Professional Greeting and Introduction" field, using the 1-5 scale.
8. Evaluate the active listening and empathy displayed during the observation using the 1-5 scale in the "Active Listening and Empathy" field.
9. Rate the accuracy and resolution effectiveness in the "Accuracy and Resolution Effectiveness" field, using the 1-5 scale.
10. Rate the professional closing and follow-up in the "Professional Closing and Follow-up" field, using the 1-5 scale.
11. List any key strengths observed during the interaction in the "Key Strengths Observed" field.
12. Identify areas for development in the "Areas for Development" field.
13. Indicate whether immediate coaching was provided or not in the "Was immediate coaching provided?" field.
14. If desired, schedule the next review date in the "Scheduled Next Review" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Interaction Observation Log**: This page allows you to select which page to use for tracking observations.
* **Observer Name**: Enter your name as the person making the observation.
* **Agent / Employee Observed**: Enter the name of the employee or agent being observed.
* **Date of Observation**: Enter the date the observation took place.
* **Communication Channel**: Choose the method of communication used for the observation (e.g. phone call, live chat, email, in-person, video meeting).
* **Duration (Minutes)**: Enter the length of the observation, if applicable.
* **Professional Greeting and Introduction**: Rate the professional greeting and introduction on a 1-5 scale.
* **Active Listening and Empathy**: Evaluate the active listening and empathy displayed during the observation on a 1-5 scale.
* **Accuracy and Resolution Effectiveness**: Rate the accuracy and resolution effectiveness on a 1-5 scale.
* **Professional Closing and Follow-up**: Rate the professional closing and follow-up on a 1-5 scale.
* **Key Strengths Observed**: List the strengths observed during the interaction.
* **Areas for Development**: Identify areas that need improvement or development.
* **Was immediate coaching provided?**: Check whether immediate coaching was provided after the observation.
* **Scheduled Next Review**: Suggest a date for the next review, if applicable.
