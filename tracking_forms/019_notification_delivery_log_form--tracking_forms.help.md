# Notification Delivery Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Notification Delivery Log Form is a tool for tracking notifications sent to recipients. It is used to record the details of sent notifications and follow-up actions taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by entering the details of the notification sent in the first section, "Notices Sent".
2. In the second section, "Recipient Email", enter the email address of the recipient who received the notification.
3. In the third section, "Notice Sent Time", enter the time the notification was sent.
4. In the fourth section, "Follow-up Date", enter the date of any follow-up action taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Notices Sent** (`page_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the details of the notification sent, including a brief description of the notification and any relevant information about the notification.
* **Recipient Email** (`page_2`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the recipient who received the notification.
* **Notice Sent Time** (`page_3`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time the notification was sent. This field accepts a 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Follow-up Date** (`page_4`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of any follow-up action taken.
