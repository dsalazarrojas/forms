# Logistics Delivery Efficiency Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Logistics Delivery Efficiency Report Form is designed to collect information about delivery services, aiming to provide insights into the effectiveness of logistics operations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the delivery route where the delivery was made.
2. Enter the driver's name who made the delivery.
3. Report the time of delivery using the numerical format provided.
4. Check if there was a delay in document delivery, and provide a brief description if applicable.
5. Indicate if the delivery was made on-time or not.
6. Specify the type of document delivered.
7. Check the status of the document (Active or Inactive).
8. Indicate the status of the delivery itself (Active or Inactive).
9. If the delivery was not made, provide a brief explanation for the reason.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **delivery_route** (text): Enter the route or location where the delivery was made.
* **driver_name** (text): Enter the name of the driver who made the delivery.
* **delivery_time** (number): Report the time of delivery using the numerical format provided.
* **document_delay** (text): Check if there was a delay in document delivery, and provide a brief description if applicable.
* **on_time_delivery** (select one): Indicate if the delivery was made on-time or not.
* **document_type** (select one): Specify the type of document delivered.
* **reason_not_delivered** (text): If the delivery was not made, provide a brief explanation for the reason.
* **document_status** (select one): Check the status of the document (Active or Inactive).
* **delivery_status** (select one): Indicate the status of the delivery itself (Active or Inactive).
