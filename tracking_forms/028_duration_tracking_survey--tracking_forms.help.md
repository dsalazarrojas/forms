# Duration Tracking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the duration of tasks and monitor work progress.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the following information:
	* Start Time: Enter the time when you began working on the task.
	* End Time: Enter the time when you finished working on the task.
	* Break Time: Enter the duration of any breaks you took during the task.
	* Total Time: Enter the total duration of the task.
	* Task Name: Provide a brief description of the task.
	* Comments: Add any additional comments or notes about the task.
	* Assigned to: Indicate whether the task was assigned to you.
	* Status: Update the status of the task as Active or Inactive.
2. Submit the form to record your work progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start Time (id: 1)**: Enter the time you started working on the task.
* **End Time (id: 2)**: Enter the time you finished working on the task.
* **Break Time (id: 3)**: Enter the duration of any breaks you took during the task.
* **Total Time (id: 4)**: Enter the total duration of the task.
* **Task Name (id: 5)**: Provide a brief description of the task you were working on.
* **Comments (id: 6)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the task.
* **Assigned to (id: 7)**: Indicate whether you were assigned to work on the task.
* **Status (id: 8)**: Update the status of the task as Active or Inactive.
* **Form ID (id: 9)**: This field is not relevant for users, it's an internal ID.
* **Created At (id: 10)**: Displays the time when the form was created.
* **Updated At (id: 11)**: Displays the last time the form was updated.
* **User ID (id: 12)**: This field is not relevant for users, it's an internal ID.
* **Assigned Tool (id: 13)**: Not relevant for users, it's an internal field.
* **Output File (id: 14)**: Not relevant for users, it's a potential output file.
* **Category (id: 15)**: Not relevant for users, it's an internal field.
* **Category ID (id: 16)**: Not relevant for users, it's an internal ID.
* **Created At 2 (id: 17)**: Displays the time when the form was created ( duplicate of id: 11).
* **Submitted By (id: 18)**: Displays the user who submitted the form.
* **Submitted At (id: 19)**: Displays the time when the form was submitted.
* **Assigned To 2 (id: 20)**: Indicate whether the task was assigned to you ( duplicate of id: 7).
* **Assigned At (id: 21)**: Displays the time when the task was assigned.
* **Updated At 2 (id: 22)**: Displays the last time the form was updated ( duplicate of id: 11).
* **Submitted At 2 (id: 23)**: Displays the time when the form was submitted ( duplicate of id: 19).
* **Form Id 2 (id: 24)**: This field is a duplicate of id: 9, not relevant for users.
* **Assigned To 2 (id: 25)**: This field is a duplicate of id: 20, not relevant for users.
