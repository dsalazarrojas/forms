# Project Expense Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Expense Reimbursement Form is a tool used to track and record project expenses, making it easier to manage and process reimbursements for approved project expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form with the required and optional fields as needed.
2.  Ensure all fields have accurate and relevant information for your expense record.
3.  Review your completed form for errors before submitting it for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Expense Date** (<label>expense_date, <type>date, required:false): Enter the date when the expense occurred or was incurred.
*   **Expense Category** (<label>expense_category, <type>text, required:false): Provide a brief description of the expense category (e.g., travel, equipment, etc.).
*   **Expense Amount** (<label>expense_amount, <type>number, required:false): Enter the exact amount of the expense.
*   **Expense Reimbursed By** (<label>expense_reimbursed_by, <type>select_one, required:false): Select the person or entity that reimbursed the expense (e.g., John or Jane).
*   **Project Name** (<label>project_name, <type>text, required:false): Enter the name of the project related to the expense.
*   **Institution** (<label>institution, <type>text, required:false): Enter the institution associated with the expense (if applicable).
*   **Notes** (<label>notes, <type>note, required:false): Provide any additional comments or details about the expense.

## Tips
*   Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely for accurate expense tracking.
*   Review the form carefully before submission for any errors or inconsistencies.
*   If you have questions or concerns about this form, contact your project manager or administration for assistance.
