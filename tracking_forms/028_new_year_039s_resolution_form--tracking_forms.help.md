# New Year's Resolution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Year's Resolution Form is a tool designed to help users set and track their New Year's resolutions. By providing a structured format, users can easily input and categorize their resolutions, including the name, description, objective, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief name for your resolution in the "Resolution Name" field.
2. Select a category for your resolution from the "Category" dropdown menu (e.g., "Tracking Forms" or "Entertainment Forms").
3. Provide a detailed description of your resolution in the "Description" field.
4. Specify the objective of your resolution (e.g., what you want to achieve).
5. Optionally, add the intention behind your resolution (e.g., why you want to achieve this goal).
6. Identify any habits you want to break or form in the "Habits" field.
7. Determine the type of resolution (e.g., "Goals", "Habits", or "Intentions").
8. Set a specific date for your resolution.
9. Choose how much time you have for your resolution, using the "Resolution Time" field.
10. Decide how frequently you want to track your progress, using the "Resolution Frequency" field (e.g., daily, weekly, or monthly).
11. Add any additional notes about your resolution in the "Resolution Notes" field.
12. If applicable, enter your email address or phone number to receive updates or reminders.
13. Select the tool assigned to you for tracking (e.g., "chatjimmy", "chatjimmy2", or "chatjimmy3").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Resolution Name (1)
* **Resolution Name** (`resolution_name`, required): Enter a brief name for your resolution. This should be a concise and descriptive title that summarizes your goal or intention.

### Category (2)
* **Category** (`category`, required): Select a category for your resolution from the dropdown menu. This will help you and others organize and track your resolutions effectively.

### Description (3)
* **Description** (`description`, required): Provide a detailed description of your resolution. This is the space to explain what you want to achieve and why.

### Objective (4)
* **Objective** (`objective`, required): Specify the objective of your resolution. What do you want to achieve with this goal or intention?

### Intention (5)
* **Intention** (`intention`, optional): Optionally, add the intention behind your resolution. This can help you stay motivated and focused on your goal.

### Habits (6)
* **Habits** (`habit`, optional): Identify any habits you want to break or form related to your resolution.

### Resolution Type (7)
* **Resolution Type** (`resolution_type`, required): Determine the type of resolution (e.g., "Goals", "Habits", or "Intentions").

### Resolution Date (8)
* **Resolution Date** (`resolution_date`, required): Set a specific date for your resolution.

### Resolution Time (9)
* **Resolution Time** (`resolution_time`, required): Choose how much time you have for your resolution.

### Resolution Frequency (10)
* **Resolution Frequency** (`resolution_frequency`, required): Decide how frequently you want to track your progress (e.g., daily, weekly, or monthly).

### Resolution Notes (11)
* **Resolution Notes** (`resolution_notes`, optional): Add any additional notes about your resolution.

### Resolution Email (12)
* **Resolution Email** (`resolution_email`, optional): If applicable, enter your email address to receive updates or reminders.

### Resolution Phone (13)
* **Resolution Phone** (`resolution_phone`, optional): If applicable, enter your phone number to receive updates or reminders.

### Assigned Tool (14)
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, required): Select the tool assigned to you for tracking (e.g., "chatjimmy", "chatjimmy2", or "chatjimmy3").

### Resolution Tags (15)
* **Resolution Tags** (`resolution_tags`, optional): Add any relevant tags to your resolution.

## Tips
* Be honest and specific when filling out this form to ensure you set effective resolutions.
* Regularly review and update your resolutions to track your progress.
* Use the assigned tool to track your progress and stay on track.
