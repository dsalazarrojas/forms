# Inventory Transfer Coordination Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory Transfer Coordination Contact Form is used to capture information about inventory transfers, including contact details and transfer dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your transfer request information, including:
	* If this transfer is a "Yes" or "No" request.
	* A brief description of the item being transferred.
2. Enter your contact details, including:
	* Your name.
	* Phone number.
	* Email address.
3. Enter the transfer date and time.
4. Specify the reason for the transfer.
5. Add any additional notes or comments.
6. If applicable, provide additional information about the transfer requester and transferer, including their contact details, organization, and notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transfer Request** (`transfer_request`):
	+ A text field where you can type in your transfer request.
* **Transfer Item** (`transfer_item`):
	+ A select one field with options "Yes" and "No".
	+ Select "Yes" if this is a transfer request.
* **Item Description** (`item_description`):
	+ A text field where you can provide a brief description of the item being transferred.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`):
	+ A select multiple field with options "Yes" and "No".
	+ Select "Yes" if this is a contact request.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`):
	+ A text field where you can enter your phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`):
	+ An email field where you can enter your email address.
* **Transfer Date** (`transfer_date`):
	+ A date field where you can select the date of the transfer.
* **Transfer Time** (`transfer_time`):
	+ A time field where you can select the time of the transfer.
* **Reason for Transfer** (`reason_for_transfer`):
	+ A text field where you can provide a brief explanation for the transfer.
* **Notes** (`notes`):
	+ A note field where you can add any additional comments or notes.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`):
	+ A text field where you can enter any additional information about the tool assigned to the transfer.
* **Transfer Requester** (`transfer_requester`):
	+ A text field where you can enter information about the transfer requester.
* **Transfer Requester Phone** (`transfer_requester_phone`):
	+ A text field where you can enter the transfer requester's phone number.
* **Transfer Requester Email** (`transfer_requester_email`):
	+ An email field where you can enter the transfer requester's email address.
* **Transfer Requester Organization** (`transfer_requester_organization`):
	+ A text field where you can enter the transfer requester's organization.
* **Transfer Requester Title** (`transfer_requester_title`):
	+ A text field where you can enter the transfer requester's title.
* **Transfer Requester Department** (`transfer_requester_department`):
	+ A text field where you can enter the transfer requester's department.
* **Transfer Requester Job Title** (`transfer_requester_job_title`):
	+ A text field where you can enter the transfer requester's job title.
* **Transfer Requester Notes** (`transfer_requester_notes`):
	+ A note field where you can add any additional comments or notes about the transfer requester.
* **Transfer Requester Comments** (`transfer_requester_comments`):
	+ A note field where you can add any additional comments or notes about the transfer requester.
* **Transferer** (`transferer`):
	+ A text field where you can enter information about the transferer.
* **Transferer Phone** (`transferer_phone`):
	+ A text field where you can enter the transferer's phone number.
* **Transferer Email** (`transferer_email`):
	+ An email field where you can enter the transferer's email address.
* **Transferer Organization** (`transferer_organization`):
	+ A text field where you can enter the transferer's organization.
* **Transferer Notes** (`transferer_notes`):
	+ A note field where you can add any additional comments or notes about the transferer.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary information and permissions to complete the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the relevant team member for guidance.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
