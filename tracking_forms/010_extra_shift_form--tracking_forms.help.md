# Extra Shift Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Extra Shift Form is a tool used to track employee willingness to work extra shifts. It is intended to be used for employees who may be available to work additional shifts, but not necessarily have already been assigned to one.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Determine if you are available to work extra shifts. Select "Yes" if you are available and "No" if you are not.
2. If you are available, select the shifts you are willing to work from the list of available shifts.
3. Provide any extra details about the shifts you are willing to work.
4. Enter your phone number and email address so we can contact you about further shifts.
5. Click "Submit Form" to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Available Shifts** (Extra Shift Form, Select Multiple, Required: No): Select the shifts you are available to work. Choose one or more from the list.
- **Extra Shifts** (Extra Shifts, Text, Required: No): If you have any preferences or requirements for the extra shifts you are available to work, enter them here.
- **Extra Shift Time** (Extra Shift Time, Time, Required: No): Enter the time you are available to work the extra shifts. 
- **Extra Shift Date** (Extra Shift Date, Date, Required: No): Select the date(s) you are available to work the extra shifts. 
- **Extra Shift Phone** (Extra Shift Phone, Text, Required: No): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about further shifts. 
- **Extra Shift Email** (Extra Shift Email, Email, Required: No): Enter your email address so we can contact you about further shifts. 
- **Extra Shift Note** (Extra Shift Note, Text, Required: No): Any additional comments or notes about the extra shifts you are available to work. 
- **Submit Form** (Submit Form, Select One, Required: No): Select "I have read and agree to sign up for this extra shift" to submit your application. 

Note: If you are not available to work extra shifts, select "I don't have any available shifts" from the "Submit Form" field.
