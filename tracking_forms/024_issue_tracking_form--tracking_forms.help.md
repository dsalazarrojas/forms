# Issue Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Issue Tracking Form is used for documenting and tracking issues in a project, including bugs, feature requests, and other issues that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of issue you are reporting, such as "bug", "feature request", or "other".
2. Describe the issue in the "description" field.
3. Choose the status of the issue to be assigned to, such as "open", "resolved", or "closed".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **report** (`prophets`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief summary of the issue.
* **issue_type** (`prophets`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the type of issue you are reporting, such as "bug" or "feature request".
* **description** (`prophets`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the issue.
* **assignee** (`prophets`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the issue to be assigned to, such as "open", "resolved", or "closed".
