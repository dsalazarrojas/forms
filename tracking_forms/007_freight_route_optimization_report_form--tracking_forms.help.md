# Freight Route Optimization Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data for freight route optimization reports. It's used to gather information about routes, their characteristics, and any issues that may be affecting them. The data collected will help optimize and improve freight routes, making them more efficient and reliable.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. In the "Route Details" field, provide a brief description of the route, including its name and any relevant details.
3. Enter the "Route Id" to identify the specific route being reported on.
4. Choose the "Route Type" from the options provided (Highway, Railway, or Waterway).
5. Enter the distance of the route in the "Distance" field.
6. Enter the transport time in the "Transport Time" field.
7. Enter the transport date in the "Transport Date" field.
8. Enter any notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
9. Select the "Assigned Team" responsible for the route from the options provided (Logistics, Operations, or Management).
10. Enter improvement recommendations for the route in the "Improvement Recommendations" field.
11. Enter any route issues in the "Route Issues" field.
12. Describe the issues affecting the route in the "Issues Description" field.
13. Choose the type of route issue from the options provided (Traffic Jam, Weather Conditions, or Road Conditions).
14. Enter the date of the route issue.
15. Enter the time of the route issue.
16. Select if the route was affected by the issue.
17. Enter the reason why the route was affected.
18. Choose the status of the route from the options provided (Active, Inactive, or Cancelled).
19. Enter the transport id.
20. Enter the driver id.
21. Choose the transport type from the options provided (Trucking, Rail, or Ship).
22. Enter the transport number.
23. Enter the name of the driver.
24. Enter the number of the vehicle.
25. Enter the date when the route was created.
26. Enter the date when the route was updated.
27. Enter the date when the route was deleted.
28. Enter the name of the user who created the route.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Route Details** (`route_details`, text, optional): A brief description of the route, including its name and any relevant details.
* **Route Id** (`route_id`, number, optional): The specific route being reported on.
* **Route Type** (`route_type`, select_one, optional): The type of route (Highway, Railway, or Waterway).
* **Distance** (`distance`, number, optional): The distance of the route.
* **Transport Time** (`transport_time`, time, optional): The transport time.
* **Transport Date** (`transport_date`, date, optional): The transport date.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Any additional comments or notes about the route.
* **Assigned Team** (`assigned_team`, select_multiple, optional): The team responsible for the route (Logistics, Operations, or Management).
* **Improvement Recommendations** (`improvement_recommendations`, text, optional): Recommendations for improving the route.
* **Route Issues** (`route_issues`, text, optional): Any issues affecting the route.
* **Issues Description** (`issues_description`, text, optional): A description of the issues affecting the route.
* **Route Issues Type** (`route_issues_type`, select_one, optional): The type of route issue (Traffic Jam, Weather Conditions, or Road Conditions).
* **Route Issues Date** (`route_issues_date`, date, optional): The date of the route issue.
* **Route Issues Time** (`route_issues_time`, time, optional): The time of the route issue.
* **Route Affected** (`route_affected`, select_multiple, optional): If the route was affected by the issue.
* **Route Affected Reason** (`route_affected_reason`, text, optional): The reason why the route was affected.
* **Route Status** (`route_status`, select_one, optional): The status of the route (Active, Inactive, or Cancelled).
* **Transport Id** (`transport_id`, number, optional): The transport id.
* **Driver Id** (`driver_id`, number, optional): The driver id.
* **Transport Type** (`transport_type`, select_multiple, optional): The type of transport (Trucking, Rail, or Ship).
* **Transport Number** (`transport_number`, text, optional): The transport number.
* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, text, optional): The name of the driver.
* **Vehicle Number** (`vehicle_number`, text, optional): The number of the vehicle.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, optional): The date when the route was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, optional): The date when the route was updated.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, date, optional): The date when the route was deleted.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, optional): The name of the user who created the route.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure accurate data collection.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can contact your team for assistance.
