# Facility Usage Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Facility Usage Form is used to track the usage of facilities within an organization. This form helps facility managers or administrators keep track of which facilities are being used, by whom, and for what purpose.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Facility" field, indicating if the requested facility is being used or not.
3. Choose the correct "Usage Type" for the purpose of the facility usage.
4. Select the "Facility User" who will be using the facility.
5. Enter the "Start Date" and "End Date" for the period of facility usage.
6. Provide a brief justification for the facility usage in the "Justification" field (optional).
7. Add any additional information in the "Additional Info" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility User** (`facility_usage_form_user`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you are the user requesting the facility, or "No" if not.
* **Facility** (`facility_usage_form_facility`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" if the facility is being used, or "No" if not.
* **Start Date** (`facility_usage_form_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the facility usage.
* **End Date** (`facility_usage_form_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the facility usage.
* **Usage Type** (`facility_usage_form_usage_type`, select_one, required): Choose the purpose of the facility usage from the available options.
* **Justification** (`facility_usage_form_justification`, text, optional): Provide a brief reason for the facility usage.
* **Additional Info** (`facility_usage_form_additional_info`, text, optional): Add any additional information about the facility usage.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Double-check the fields marked as required.
* If you're unsure about any field, reach out to the facility manager or administrator for assistance.
* This form is designed to be simple and straightforward. If you have any issues or concerns, contact the facility management team.
