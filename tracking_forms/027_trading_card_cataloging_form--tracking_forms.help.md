# Trading Card Cataloging Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about trading cards, including their cataloging details such as title, condition, rarity, and price. The form is used to track and catalog trading cards in a database.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter your address in the Address field.
6. Enter the URL of the card's photo in the Photo URL field.
7. Select the Set Name from the available options in the Set Name field.
8. Enter the Card Name in the Card Name field.
9. Select the Condition of the card from the available options in the Condition field.
10. Select the Rarity of the card from the available options in the Rarity field.
11. Enter the Card Number in the Card Number field.
12. Enter the Set Number in the Set Number field.
13. Enter the Edition in the Edition field.
14. Select the Price of the card from the available options in the Price field.
15. Select whether the card is Sold or not in the Sold field.
16. Enter the Sold Price of the card in the Sold Price field, if applicable.
17. Enter the URL of the card's main image in the Photo Main field.
18. Enter the URL of the card's back image in the Photo Back field.
19. Enter the URL of the card's front image in the Photo Front field.
20. Enter the URL of the set's image in the Set Image URL field.
21. Enter the Card Text in the Card Text field.
22. Enter any additional Notes about the card in the Notes field.
23. Enter the URL of the card's image in the Card Image URL field.
24. Enter the URL of the rarity image in the Rarity Image URL field.
25. Enter the date the card was sold in the Sold Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Photo URL** (`photo_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the card's photo.
* **Set Name** (`set_name`, select_one, optional): Select the set name of the card from the available options.
* **Card Name** (`card_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the card.
* **Condition** (`condition`, select_one, optional): Select the condition of the card from the available options (Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Rarity** (`rarity`, select_one, optional): Select the rarity of the card from the available options (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Super Rare).
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, number, optional): Enter the number of the card.
* **Set Number** (`set_number`, text, optional): Enter the set number.
* **Edition** (`edition`, text, optional): Enter the edition of the card.
* **Price** (`price`, select_one, optional): Select the price of the card from the available options (Below Market, Market, Above Market).
* **Sold** (`sold`, select_one, optional): Select whether the card is sold or not (True, False).
* **Sold Price** (`sold_price`, number, optional): Enter the sold price of the card, if applicable.
* **Photo Main** (`photo_main`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the card's main image.
* **Photo Back** (`photo_back`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the card's back image.
* **Photo Front** (`photo_front`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the card's front image.
* **Set Image URL** (`set_image_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the set's image.
* **Card Text** (`card_text`, text, optional): Enter any additional text about the card.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the card.
* **Card Image URL** (`card_image_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the card's image.
* **Rarity Image URL** (`rarity_image_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the rarity image.
* **Sold Date** (`sold_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the card was sold.
