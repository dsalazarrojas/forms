# Nutrition Diary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Nutrition Diary Form, a tool designed to help you track your daily food intake and corresponding dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Food Entries" pages, providing details about your food intake for each day. Each page has a set of text fields that you must fill out. Make sure to fill out all required fields for each page.
2. Fill out the "Date" fields for each corresponding "Food Entries" page. These fields are required to match the dates with your food intake.
3. Optionally, fill out the "Notes" fields to add any additional information about your food intake.
4. Review and submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.
