# Personal Assistant Time InOut Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Assistant Time InOut Form is used to track the arrival and departure times of employees in the office. This form helps management to monitor work hours and attendance for internal time in and out events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the time you arrived at work.
* Fill in the time you left work.
* Choose a date for the event.
* If necessary, enter the duration of your time out.
* Add any additional notes about your time in or out.
* Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* Add any notes specific to your employee, if applicable.
* Enter your employee ID for tracking purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Time In** (`time_in`, time, required): Enter the time you arrived at work.
* **Time Out** (`time_out`, time, required): Enter the time you left work.
* **Date** (`date`, date): Choose a date for the event.
* **Time Out Duration** (`time_out_duration`, number, optional): If necessary, enter the duration of your time out.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your time in or out.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Employee Notes** (`employee_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes specific to your employee, if applicable.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter your employee ID for tracking purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form for internal time in and out events only.
* If you need to track external events, use the "Events" form.
* Double-check your input for accuracy to ensure correct tracking.
