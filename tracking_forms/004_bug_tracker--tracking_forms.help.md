# Bug Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Bug Tracker form, helping you understand its purpose and how to fill it out correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Bug Tracker form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your information as the reporter.
2. Provide a brief and descriptive title for the bug.
3. Give a detailed description of the issue.
4. Describe the steps to reproduce the bug.
5. Specify the expected result and actual result of the bug.
6. Select the severity level of the bug.
7. Fill in your browser and operating system information.
8. Provide the application version.
9. Indicate whether you can reproduce the bug consistently.
10. Add any additional files or attachments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your full name** (`reporter_name`, required): Enter your full name as the reporter of the bug.
* **Email address** (`reporter_email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further information.
* **Username or user ID** (`reporter_username`, optional): Enter your username or user ID if you have one.
* **Bug title** (`bug_title`, required): Provide a brief and descriptive title of the bug.
* **Detailed bug description** (`bug_description`, required): Give a detailed explanation of the issue.
* **Steps to reproduce** (`steps_to_reproduce`, required): Describe the steps to reproduce the bug, using numbered steps.
* **Expected result** (`expected_result`, required): Specify what was expected to happen.
* **Actual result** (`actual_result`, required): Describe what actually happened.
* **Bug severity** (`severity_level`, required): Select the level of severity of the bug from the given options.
* **Browser name** (`browser_name`, required): Fill in the name of your browser.
* **Browser version** (`browser_version`, required): Enter the version of your browser.
* **Operating system** (`operating_system`, required): Select the operating system you're using.
* **Operating system version** (`os_version`, required): Enter the version of your operating system.
* **Device type** (`device_type`, required): Select the type of device you're using (Desktop, Laptop, Tablet, Smartphone, etc.).
* **Screen resolution** (`screen_resolution`, optional): Enter the resolution of your screen (e.g., 1920x1080).
* **Application version** (`app_version`, required): Enter the version of the application.
* **Date bug was first noticed** (`date_first_noticed`, optional): If you remember, indicate when you first noticed the bug.
* **Can you reproduce this bug consistently?** (`reproducibility`, required): Indicate how often you can reproduce the bug.
* **Additional files or attachments** (`attachment_note`, optional): Attach any additional files or screenshots that might help in identifying the bug.
* **Additional application details** (`application_version`, required): This field is actually a note field, so feel free to add any additional information about the application.
