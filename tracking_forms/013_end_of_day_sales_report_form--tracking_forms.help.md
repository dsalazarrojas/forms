# End Of Day Sales Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The End Of Day Sales Report Form is used to record sales activities and progress at the end of each trading day. This form helps sales teams to track their performance, identify trends, and provide accurate sales data to management or supervisors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Id field with your unique identifier.
2. Enter the Sales Date in the format `mm/dd/yyyy` or as required by your organization.
3. Enter the Sales Amount, which is the total sales for the day.
4. Select the Product Id for the products sold.
5. Enter the Product Name of the products sold.
6. Enter the Quantity Sold, which is the total number of products sold.
7. Enter the Revenue generated from sales, if applicable.
8. Select the Sales Channel from the given options (Channel A, Channel B, or Channel C).
9. Add any Notes about the sales activities.
10. Enter your Email and Phone number for contact.
11. Select the Sales Person responsible for the sales activities.
12. Enter any Sales Person Notes, if necessary.
13. Select the Sales Status (Status A, Status B, or Status C).
14. Add any Sales Status Notes, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Id (`user_id`, number, optional)
* This field is for your unique identifier assigned by your organization.
### Sales Date (`sales_date`, date, optional)
* Enter the date on which sales activities occurred.
### Sales Amount (`sales_amount`, number, optional)
* Enter the total sales amount for the day.
### Product Id (`product_id`, number, optional)
* Select the ID for the products sold.
### Product Name (`product_name`, text, optional)
* Enter the name of the products sold.
### Quantity Sold (`quantity_sold`, number, optional)
* Enter the number of products sold.
### Revenue (`revenue`, number, optional)
* Enter any revenue generated from sales.
### Sales Channel (`sales_channel`, select_one, optional)
* Select the sales channel from Channel A, Channel B, or Channel C.
### Notes (`notes`, note, optional)
* Add any notes about the sales activities.
### Email (`email`, email, optional)
* Enter your Email address.
### Phone (`phone`, text, optional)
* Enter your phone number.
### Sales Person (`sales_person`, select_multiple, optional)
* Select the sales person responsible for the sales activities.
### Sales Person Note (`sales_person_note`, note, optional)
* Enter any notes about the sales person.
### Sales Status (`sales_status`, select_one, optional)
* Select the sales status from Status A, Status B, or Status C.
### Sales Status Note (`sales_status_note`, note, optional)
* Enter any notes about the sales status.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure you haven't missed any important information.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or sales manager for clarification.
* Keep your entries organized and consistent for better reporting and tracking.
