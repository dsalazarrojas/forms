# Service Advisor Follow Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Advisor Follow Up Form is designed to gather feedback and input from customers on their recent service visit. This form is intended to be used for customers to provide their opinions and suggestions to help us improve our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the contact information of the service advisor who assisted you.
2. Choose the details about your service visit (service type, date, time, advisor, vehicle make, model, and year).
3. If you have any additional feedback you would like to share, you can do so in the text field.
4. Indicate how likely you are to recommend our service.
5. Enter any final comments or thoughts you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information** (first name, last name, email, phone, address line 1, address line 2, city, state, zip): Please select your contact information to help us get in touch with you for any follow up or further communication.
- **Service Details** (service type, service date, service time, service advisor, vehicle make, vehicle model, vehicle year): Choose the details about your service visit to help us understand your experience.
- **Additional Feedback**: If you have any comments or thoughts that you'd like to share with us, this is the place to do so.
- **How likely are you to recommend our service?** (very likely, somewhat likely, not likely): Please select how likely you are to recommend our service to others.
- **Comments**: Enter any final comments or thoughts you may have about your service visit.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed as possible when providing feedback, as it will help us better understand your experience.
- If you're unsure about any of the options, please don't hesitate to contact us for clarification.
- Your input is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time and consideration.
