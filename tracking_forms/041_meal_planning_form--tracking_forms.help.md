# Meal Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meal Planning Form is designed to help you plan and track your meals in an easy and convenient way.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "date" field with the date of the meal you are planning for.
2. In the "time" field, enter the time you plan to eat the meal.
3. In the "meal" field, type in the name of the meal you are planning to have.
4. In the "notes" field, add any additional notes or comments about the meal you are planning.
5. In the "email" field, enter your email address (optional, but recommended for tracking purposes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **date**: Enter the date you plan to have the meal.
* **time**: Enter the time you plan to eat the meal.
* **meal**: Type in the name of the meal you are planning to have.
* **notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the meal.
* **email**: Enter your email address (optional, but recommended for tracking purposes).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form for each meal you plan to have.
* You can use this form to track your meal planning for any day of the week.
* You can customize your notes field to include any relevant information about the meal, such as the recipe or cooking method.
* If you want to track your meal planning for future reference, consider saving a copy of this form for each day.
