# Doctor Note Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doctor Note Form is a tool used to document a doctor's visit, including the patient's name, the date and time of the visit, the reason for the visit, and any other relevant medical information. This form is designed to provide a simple and straightforward way for doctors to record their notes in a format that is easy to read and understand.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Doctor Note Form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill in the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
* Select the date of the visit from the "Date" field.
* Enter the time of the visit in the "Time" field.
* Provide a brief description of the reason for the visit in the "Reason" field.
* Sign your name in the "Signature" field to confirm that you are the doctor who completed this form.
* Type any additional notes or comments about the visit in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name (`doctor_note_form_patient_name`, text, **optional**): Enter the name of the patient you are seeing.
* **Date (`doctor_note_form_date`, date, **optional**): Select the date of the patient's visit.
* **Time (`doctor_note_form_time`, time, **optional**): Enter the time of the patient’s visit.
* **Doctor Name (`doctor_note_form_doctor_name`, text, **optional**): Enter your name as the doctor who completed this form.
* **Reason (`doctor_note_form_reason`, text, **optional**): Briefly describe the reason for the patient's visit.
* **Signature (`doctor_note_form_signature`, text, **optional**): Sign your name to confirm that you are the doctor who completed this form.
* **Note (`doctor_note_form_note`, text, **optional**): Enter any additional comments or notes about the patient's visit.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the patient's information is recorded correctly.
* If you have any concerns or questions about filling out this form, please don't hesitate to ask your staff or colleagues for assistance.
* Remember to sign your name as the doctor who completed this form to confirm your identity.
