# Shared Living Clock In And Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shared Living Clock In And Out Form is used to track employee clock-in and clock-out activities in a shared space.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select whether you are clocking in or out.
2. If clocking in, select "Yes" for clocking in. If clocking out, select "No".
3. Enter the date you clocked in or out.
4. Enter the time you started working on the clock-in form.
5. Enter the time you finished working on the clock-out form.
6. (Optional) Enter a reason for clocking in or out.
7. (Optional) Add any additional notes about your clock-in or clock-out activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Clock In/Out** (`clock_in_and_out_form_1`, `clock_in_and_out_form_2`, required/optional): Select "Yes" to clock in, or "No" to clock out.
* **Date** (`clock_in_and_out_form_3`, `type: date`, required/false): Enter the date you clocked in or out. Use the date format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Start Time** (`clock_in_and_out_form_5`, `type: time`, required/true): Enter the time you started working on the clock-in form.
* **End Time** (`clock_in_and_out_form_6`, `type: time`, required/true): Enter the time you finished working on the clock-out form.
* **Reason** (`clock_in_and_out_form_7`, `type: text`, required/false): Enter a brief explanation for clocking in or out, if applicable.
* **Notes** (`clock_in_and_out_form_8`, `type: note`, required/false): Add any additional notes about your clock-in or clock-out activity.
