# Practicum Log Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Practicum Log Submission Form is used to log and report practicum activities, hours spent, and relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that this is the first submission for this practicum session. If you have already submitted a log for this session, do not proceed.
2. Fill out the form carefully, providing accurate information for each field.
3. Review and double-check your submission before finalizing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Practicum Log Submission Form** (`practicum_log`, `note`, optional): This is the main title of the form. You can fill out a brief description of the session if needed.
* **Practicum Hours** (`practicum_hours`, `number`, optional): Report the total hours spent on the practicum.
* **Practicum Activities** (`practicum_activities`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate whether you engaged in activities such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Reflections** (`reflections`, `text`, optional): Provide any thoughts or observations about the practicum experience.
* **Supervisory Meetings** (`supervisory_meetings`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you attended supervisory meetings during the practicum, selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Documentation** (`documentation`, `text`, optional): Attach relevant documentation or supporting materials related to the practicum.
* **Practicum Start Date** (`practicum_start`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of the practicum session.
* **Practicum End Date** (`practicum_end`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of the practicum session.
* **Practicum Location** (`practicum_location`, `text`, optional): Report the location of the practicum.
* **Supervisors** (`practicum_supervisors`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate if the supervisor(s) attended the practicum, selecting "Yes" or "No".

## Tips
- Ensure you fill out all required fields, if any.
- Review and double-check your submission before finalizing.
- Submit only once per practicum session to prevent duplication.
