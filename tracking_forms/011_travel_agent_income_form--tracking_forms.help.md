# Travel Agent Income Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Agent Income Form is a tool for travel agents to report their income and expenses. This form helps to track and record income earned from different travel agencies, dates, and expense categories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the travel agency you worked with from the list.
* Enter the date of the income you earned.
* Enter the amount of income you earned.
* Choose the expense account to associate with the income.
* Select the category of the expense.
* Enter the amount of the expense.
* Add any additional notes or comments about the transaction.
* Enter your email address for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Agent Income Form** (`travel_agent_income_form`, text, required): This field is the title of the form, which is already pre-filled.
* **Travel Agencies** (`travel_agencies`, select_one, required): Select the travel agency you worked with to earn the income.
* **Income Date** (`income_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the income you earned.
* **Income Amount** (`income_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of income you earned.
* **Expense Account** (`expense_account`, select_one, required): Select the expense account to associate with the income.
* **Expense Category** (`expense_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the expense.
* **Expense Amount** (`expense_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the expense.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the transaction.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
