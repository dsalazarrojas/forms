# SIPOC Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SIPOC Form is designed to collect data for Supply-Chain-Of-Criticality (SIPOC) analysis. This analysis is used to identify potential bottlenecks and areas of inefficiency in the supply chain process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the "suppliers" field and enter the names of the suppliers involved in your supply chain.
2. In the "inputs" field, enter the raw materials or products received from the suppliers.
3. Describe the "process" of your supply chain in the "process" field.
4. List the "outputs" produced or delivered to customers in the "outputs" field.
5. Enter the "customers" who are recipients of the outputs.
6. Write a brief description of the "process" in the "process description" field.
7. Write a brief description of the "suppliers" in the "supplier description" field.
8. Write a brief description of the "inputs" in the "input description" field.
9. Write a brief description of the "outputs" in the "output description" field.
10. Write a brief description of the "customers" in the "customer description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Suppliers** (`suppliers`, text, required: false): Enter the names of the suppliers involved in your supply chain.
* **Inputs** (`inputs`, text, required: false): Enter the raw materials or products received from the suppliers.
* **Process** (`process`, text, required: false): Describe the process of your supply chain.
* **Outputs** (`outputs`, text, required: false): List the products or services produced or delivered to customers.
* **Customers** (`customers`, text, required: false): Enter the names of the customers who are recipients of the outputs.
* **Process description** (`process description`, text, required: false): Write a brief description of the process of your supply chain.
* **Supplier description** (`supplier description`, text, required: false): Write a brief description of the suppliers, their role in the supply chain, and any relevant details.
* **Input description** (`input description`, text, required: false): Write a brief description of the inputs, their origin, and any relevant details.
* **Output description** (`output description`, text, required: false): Write a brief description of the outputs, their characteristics, and any relevant details.
* **Customer description** (`customer description`, text, required: false): Write a brief description of the customers, their needs and expectations, and any relevant details.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out all fields to provide a complete picture of your supply chain.
* If you have any further questions or need help, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
