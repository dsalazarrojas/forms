# Wildlife Research Logistics Support Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wildlife Research Logistics Support Request form is used to submit requests for support with wildlife research projects. This form is used to gather information about the project, including its category, location, team members, and specific requirements for field support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields to the best of your ability.
2. Select the category that best describes your project (Conservation, Research, Wildlife Management, or Other).
3. List team members who will be involved in the project (e.g., John Doe, Jane Doe).
4. Provide the project location and its start and end dates.
5. Enter the email and phone number of the team lead.
6. Enter the name of the research institution.
7. Describe the project in a few sentences.
8. Select the study area (National Park, Wildlife Reserve, or Other).
9. Specify any field support requirements you need.
10. Choose the size of the field crew you will need.
11. Select any equipment you need for the field (e.g., Binoculars, Compass, Camera).
12. Describe any dietary needs for field staff.
13. Describe any water support needs for field staff.
14. Specify any first aid needs or requirements.
15. Choose the type of field shelter you will need.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name**: This is the name of your project. Please enter a brief name for the project.
* **Category**: This is the category of your project. You can select from Conservation, Research, Wildlife Management, or Other.
* **Team Members**: This is a list of team members who will be involved in the project. Please enter their names (e.g., John Doe, Jane Doe).
* **Project Location**: This is the location of the project. Enter the city, state, or region where the project will take place.
* **Project Start Date**: This is the start date of the project. Enter the date when the project will begin.
* **Project End Date**: This is the end date of the project. Enter the date when the project will end.
* **Team Lead Email**: This is the email address of the team lead. Enter the email address of the person in charge.
* **Team Lead Phone**: This is the phone number of the team lead. Enter the phone number of the person in charge.
* **Research Institution**: This is the name of the research institution. Enter the name of the institution where the research will take place.
* **Project Description**: This is a brief description of the project. Please enter a few sentences describing the project.
* **Study Area**: This is the area where the project will take place. You can select from National Park, Wildlife Reserve, or Other.
* **Field Support Requirements**: This is any specific support requirements you have for the field team (e.g., food, water, accommodations).
* **Field Crew Size**: This is the number of people you will need in the field crew.
* **Field Equipment**: This is any equipment you need for the field (e.g., Binoculars, Compass, Camera).
* **Field Diet Support**: This is any dietary needs or requirements for field staff.
* **Field Water Support**: This is any water needs or requirements for field staff.
* **Field First Aid**: This is any first aid needs or requirements.
* **Field Shelter Type**: This is any shelter type you will need for field staff. 

Note: The following fields are optional and you can leave them blank if not applicable: project_name, category, team_members, project_location, project_start_date, project_end_date, team_lead_email, team_lead_phone, research_institution, field_crew_size, field_equipment, field_diet_support, field_water_support, field_first_aid, field_shelter_type.
