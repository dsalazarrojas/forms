# New Year's Goals Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Year's Goals Form Template is a tool to help you set and track your New Year's resolutions or goals for the upcoming year.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the goal name in the first field.
2. Choose the type of goal: short-term, long-term, or financial.
3. Select the start date when you plan to start working on the goal.
4. Set the end date when you plan to complete the goal.
5. Set the target completion date if you want to track progress towards it.
6. Assign a priority level to the goal.
7. Update the progress status of the goal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **goal_name** (`goal_name`, `text`, required/optional): This is where you input the name of your New Year's goal. Be as specific and descriptive as possible.
* **goal_type** (`goal_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of goal you want to set: short-term, long-term, or financial.
* **start_date** (`start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when you plan to start working on your goal.
* **end_date** (`end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when you plan to complete your goal.
* **target_completion_date** (`target_completion_date`, `date`, required/optional): This field is optional, but if you want to set a target completion date, enter it here.
* **priority** (`priority`, `number`, required/optional): Assign a priority level to your goal, with higher numbers indicating higher priority.
* **progress_status** (`progress_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Update the progress status of your goal: not started, in progress, or done.
