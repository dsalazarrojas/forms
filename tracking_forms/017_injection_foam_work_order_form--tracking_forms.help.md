# Injection Foam Work Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Injection Foam Work Order Form is used to track and manage work orders related to injection foams. It provides a structured way to gather essential information about work orders, ensuring that all relevant details are captured and reviewed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and identify the relevant fields that apply to your work order.
2. Enter your name and email address in the `Technician` field to associate your name with the work order.
3. Enter the customer information in the `Customer Information` field.
4. Provide details about the work order, including the date and time in the `Date and Time` field.
5. Specify the quantity of the product required in the `Quantity of Product` field.
6. Select the foam type from the available options in the `Foam Type` field.
7. Enter any special instructions or comments in the `Special Instructions` field.
8. Select the relevant status of the work order from the options provided in the `Work Order Status` and `Status` fields.
9. Choose the technician assigned to the work order from the available options in the `Assigned to` field.
10. Enter the work order ID, due date, and work order number in the respective fields.
11. Enter the date completed if the work order is completed.
12. Finally, select the work order type and provide any additional comments or notes in the `Comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Work Order Request** (`form_1`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the work order.
- **Customer Information** (`form_2`, text, required: false): Enter the customer name, email, and contact details.
- **Work Order Details** (`form_3`, text, required: false): Provide additional details about the work order.
- **Date and Time** (`form_4`, date, required: false): Enter the date and time of the work order.
- **Injection Foam** (`form_5`, text, required: false): Enter the type of injection foam required.
- **Quantity of Product** (`form_6`, number, required: false): Enter the quantity of the product required.
- **Unit of Measure** (`form_7`, text, required: false): Enter the unit of measure for the product.
- **Foam Type** (`form_8`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of foam from the available options.
- **Special Instructions** (`form_9`, text, required: false): Enter any special instructions or comments.
- **Technician** (`form_10`, text, required: true): Enter your name and email address to associate with the work order.
- **Email** (`form_11`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`form_12`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
- **Address** (`form_13`, text, required: false): Enter your address.
- **City** (`form_14`, text, required: false): Enter the city.
- **State** (`form_15`, text, required: false): Enter the state.
- **Zip** (`form_16`, text, required: false): Enter the zip code.
- **Comments** (`form_17`, text, required: false): Enter any comments or notes about the work order.
- **Work Order Status** (`form_18`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the status of the work order from the available options.
- **Status** (`form_19`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the status of the work order from the available options.
- **Assigned to** (`form_20`, select_multiple, required: true): Select the technician assigned to the work order.
- **Work Order ID** (`form_21`, text, required: false): Enter the work order ID.
- **Due Date** (`form_22`, date, required: false): Enter the due date of the work order.
- **Work Order Type** (`form_23`, text, required: false): Enter the type of work order.
- **Work Order Number** (`form_24`, text, required: false): Enter the work order number.
- **Date Completed** (`form_25`, date, required: false): Enter the date completed for the work order.

Please note that each field is optional unless specified as required in the field-by-field explanation section.
