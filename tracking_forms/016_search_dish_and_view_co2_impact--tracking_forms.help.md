# search_dish_and_view_co2_impact - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track and view the CO2 impact of dishes, including their name, type, size, ingredients, cooking method, serving size, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the dish you want to track.
2. Select the type of dish (e.g., beef, pork, chicken, fish, vegetarian).
3. Enter the size of the dish.
4. Select the cooking method (e.g., steamed, raw, grilled, fried).
5. Enter the serving size.
6. List the ingredients used in the dish, including their quantities.
7. Enter the CO2 impact of the dish.
8. Select the source of the food (e.g., local, imported).
9. Select the production method of the food (e.g., mechanized, non-mechanized).
10. Select the amount of food waste generated by the dish (e.g., none, some, most).
11. Enter the date when the dish was viewed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Dish Name** (`dish_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the dish you want to track.
- **Dish Type** (`dish_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of dish (e.g., beef, pork, chicken, fish, vegetarian).
- **Dish Size** (`dish_size`, `number`, required): Enter the size of the dish.
- **Cooking Method** (`cooking_method`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the cooking method (e.g., steamed, raw, grilled, fried).
- **Serving Size** (`serving_size`, `number`, required): Enter the serving size.
- **Ingredient 1 Name** (`ingredient1_name`, `text`, required): List the first ingredient used in the dish.
- **Ingredient 1 Quantity** (`ingredient1_quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the quantity of the first ingredient used.
- **Ingredient 2 Name** (`ingredient2_name`, `text`, optional): List the second ingredient used in the dish (if applicable).
- **Ingredient 2 Quantity** (`ingredient2_quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the quantity of the second ingredient used (if applicable).
- **CO2 Impact** (`co2_impact`, `number`, required): Enter the CO2 impact of the dish.
- **Food Source** (`food_source`, `select_one`, optional): Select the source of the food (e.g., local, imported).
- **Food Production** (`food_production`, `select_one`, optional): Select the production method of the food (e.g., mechanized, non-mechanized).
- **Food Waste** (`food_waste`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the amount of food waste generated by the dish (e.g., none, some, most).
- **Dish Viewed** (`dish_viewed`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the dish was viewed.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure accurate tracking and viewing of CO2 impact.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact authorized personnel for assistance.
* This form is only accessible to authorized personnel, so please do not use it for any other purpose.
