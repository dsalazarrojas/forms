# Weekly Sales Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Sales Report Form is used by sales teams to collect and track weekly sales data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the sales date for the report.
2. Choose the sales region where the sales were made.
3. Enter the total revenue generated during the sales period.
4. Select the sales manager(s) responsible for the sales.
5. Enter any notes or comments about the sales performance.
6. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
7. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
8. If applicable, select the sales representative responsible for the sales.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sales Date** (`sales_date`, date, optional): Select the date for which you are submitting the sales report. This will help us to track sales performance over time.
* **Sales Region** (`sales_region`, select_one, required): Choose the region where the sales were made, such as North, South, East, or West.
* **Total Revenue** (`total_revenue`, number, optional): Enter the total revenue generated during the sales period.
* **Sales Manager** (`sales_manager`, select_multiple, required): Select the sales manager(s) responsible for the sales. This will help us to identify who was involved in the sales process.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes or comments about the sales performance. This can include any additional information that may be helpful for future reference.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes. This will allow us to reach out to you if we need more information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes. This will allow us to reach out to you if we need more information quickly.
* **Sales Rep** (`sales_rep`, select_one, optional): If applicable, select the sales representative responsible for the sales. This will help us to track who was involved in the sales process.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to us for clarification.
* We appreciate your timely submission of the sales report. This will help us to track sales performance accurately.
