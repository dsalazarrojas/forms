# Tool And Equipment Allocation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tool And Equipment Allocation Form is used to track and manage the allocation of tools and equipment within a project environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the "Yes" or "No" option for "First Page" to indicate whether the equipment is currently being used.
2. Choose the name of the project from the "Project Name" dropdown list to associate the equipment with the correct project.
3. Enter the name of the equipment requested in the "Equipment Requested" field.
4. Set the due date for returning the equipment in the "Due Date" field.
5. Enter the name of the project manager in the "Project Manager" field.
6. Select the location where the equipment is currently assigned in the "Location" dropdown list.
7. Enter the quantity of the equipment in the "Quantity" field.
8. Choose whether the equipment is assigned to someone in the "Assigned To" dropdown list.
9. Select the tools that are assigned to the user in the "Assigned Tool" dropdown list.
10. Choose the status of the equipment in the "Status" dropdown list.
11. Add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
12. Enter any comments or notes about the equipment in the "Comments" field.
13. Enter the ID of the project in the "Project ID" field.
14. Select the status of the equipment in the "Equipment Status" dropdown list.
15. Enter the date when the equipment was assigned in the "Assigned Date" field.
16. Enter the time when the equipment is due back in the "Due Time" field.
17. Choose whether the user is assigned to the equipment in the "Assigned User" dropdown list.
18. Select the status of the tool in the "Tool Status" dropdown list.
19. Select the status of the project in the "Project Status" dropdown list.
20. Enter the ID of the form in the "Form ID" field.
21. Enter the type of equipment in the "Equipment Type" field.
22. Enter the ID of the location in the "Location ID" field.
23. Enter the ID of the user assigned to the equipment in the "Assigned To ID" field.
24. Enter the date and time when the equipment is due back in the "Due Date Time" field.
25. Enter the type of project in the "Project Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page**: (`Yes`, `No`, not required) - This field is used to indicate whether the equipment is currently being used.
* **Project Name**: (`project a` or `project b`, not required) - Choose the name of the project that the equipment is associated with.

* **Due Date**: (`date`, not required) - Enter the date when the equipment is due back.
* **Project Manager**: (`string`, not required) - Enter the name of the project manager.
* **Location**: (`site a` or `site b`, not required) - Select the location where the equipment is currently assigned.
* **Quantity**: (`number`, not required) - Enter the quantity of the equipment.
* **Assigned To**: (`Yes` or `No`, not required) - Choose whether the equipment is assigned to someone.
* **Assigned Tool**: (`Yes` or `No`, not required) - Select the tools that are assigned to the user.
* **Status**: (`Active` or `Inactive`, not required) - Select the status of the equipment.

* **Comments**: (`string`, not required) - Enter any comments or notes about the equipment.
* **Project ID**: (`string`, not required) - Enter the ID of the project.
* **Equipment Status**: (`Active` or `Inactive`, not required) - Select the status of the equipment.

* **Assigned User**: (`Yes` or `No`, not required) - Choose whether the user is assigned to the equipment.
* **Tool Status**: (`Active` or `Inactive`, not required) - Select the status of the tool.
* **Project Status**: (`Active` or `Inactive`, not required) - Select the status of the project.
* **Form ID**: (`string`, not required) - Enter the ID of the form.
