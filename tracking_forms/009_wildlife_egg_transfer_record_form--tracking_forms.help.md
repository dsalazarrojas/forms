# Wildlife Egg Transfer Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wildlife Egg Transfer Record Form is used to document the transfer of wildlife eggs between locations. This form helps track the date, location, and species of the eggs transferred, as well as any additional notes about the transfer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Select a date for the transfer using the calendar picker.
3. Enter the location where the eggs were collected from (Location From).
4. Enter the location where the eggs were transferred to (Location To).
5. Select the species of the eggs transferred.
6. Enter the number of eggs transferred.
7. Add any additional notes about the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Transfer Date** (`transfer_date`, date, **required**: false): Enter the date on which the eggs were transferred. This date will be automatically generated if you select the "Today" option.
* **Location From** (`location_from`, text, **required**: false): The location where the eggs were collected from. This could be a specific field, park, or other location where the eggs were found.
* **Location To** (`location_to`, text, **required**: false): The location where the eggs were transferred to. This could be a specific field, park, or other location where the eggs were received.
* **Species** (`species`, text, **required**: false): The species of the eggs transferred. This will help identify the type of eggs transferred.
* **Egg Count** (`egg_count`, number, **required**: false): The number of eggs transferred. This will help track the quantity of eggs transferred.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, **required**: false): Any additional notes about the transfer, such as the condition of the eggs or any challenges encountered.

## Tips
* Make sure to be accurate when entering the transfer date, as it will affect the tracking of the transfer.
* Use a consistent naming convention for locations to ensure easy tracking.
* Be as specific as possible when describing the species and egg count.
* Use the notes field to document any important details about the transfer.
