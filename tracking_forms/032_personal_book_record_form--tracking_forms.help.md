# personal_book_record_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about personal books for tracking and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the book in the "Book Title" field.
2. Select the authors of the book from the "Authors" field.
3. Enter the publication year of the book in the "Publication Year" field.
4. Select the edition of the book from the "Edition" field.
5. Enter the name of the publisher in the "Publisher" field.
6. Enter the publication date of the book in the "Publication Date" field.
7. Add any additional notes about the book in the "Notes" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (if applicable).
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Book Title (book_title)
* **Book Title** (text, required): Enter the title of the book.

### Authors (authors)
* **Authors** (select_multiple, required): Select the authors of the book.

### Publication Year (publication_year)
* **Publication Year** (number, required): Enter the year of publication of the book.

### Edition (edition)
* **Edition** (select_one, required): Select the edition of the book.

### Publisher (publisher)
* **Publisher** (text, required): Enter the name of the publisher.

### Publication Date (publication_date)
* **Publication Date** (date, required): Enter the date of publication of the book.

### Notes (notes)
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes about the book.

### Email (email)
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address (if applicable).

### Phone (phone)
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).

### Created On (created_on)
* **Created On** (date, required): This field is used to track the date when the book record was created.
