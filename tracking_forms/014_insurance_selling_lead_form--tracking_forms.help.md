# Insurance Selling Lead Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers interested in purchasing insurance policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Customer Name, Customer Email, Customer Phone, Date of Application, Time of Application, Customer Note, Customer Reference, Policy Number, Customer State, and Customer Zip.
2. Select one of the insurance policy types and status from the drop-down menus, if applicable.
3. Enter any additional information as required or noted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: A text field used to collect the customer's full name.
* **Customer Email**: A text field used to collect the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone**: A text field used to collect the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Address**: A text field used to collect the customer's address.
* **Policy Type**: A drop-down menu with options to select the type of insurance policy.
* **Policy Status**: A drop-down menu with options to select the status of the insurance policy.
* **Insurance Agent**: A drop-down menu with a list of insurance agents.
* **Date of Application**: A date field used to collect the date the customer applied for the policy.
* **Time of Application**: A time field used to collect the time the customer applied for the policy.
* **Customer Note**: A text field used to collect any additional notes about the customer.
* **Customer Reference**: A text field used to collect any additional reference information.
* **Policy Number**: A text field used to collect the policy number.
* **Customer State**: A text field used to collect the customer's state.
* **Customer Zip**: A text field used to collect the customer's zip code.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled in for the form to be submitted. The other fields can be left blank if not applicable.
