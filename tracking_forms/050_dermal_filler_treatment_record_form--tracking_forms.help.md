# Dermal Filler Treatment Record Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record the details of a client's dermal filler treatment session, including their medical history, treatment procedure, results, follow-up instructions, and billing information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name.
2. Record the date of the appointment.
3. Provide a detailed medical history for the client.
4. Describe the treatment procedure and results.
5. Enter any follow-up instructions or appointments.
6. Add any doctor comments.
7. Enter any client comments.
8. Add any medical notes.
9. Enter billing information, including the dermal filler used, amount, cost, and payment date.
10. Enter client signature for verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Record the date of the appointment.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, text, required): Enter your signature as the doctor performing the treatment.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide a detailed medical history of the client, if applicable.
* **Treatment Procedure** (`treatment_procedure`, text, optional): Describe the treatment procedure used.
* **Results** (`results`, text, optional): Record the results of the treatment.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, text, optional): Enter any follow-up instructions or appointments.
* **Doctor Comments** (`doctor_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments from the doctor.
* **Client Comments** (`client_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments from the client.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional medical notes.
* **Billing** (`billing`, text, optional): Enter billing information, including dermal filler used, amount, cost, and payment date.
* **Dermal Filler Used** (`dermal_filler_used`, text, optional): Record the dermal filler used.
* **Dermal Filler Amount** (`dermal_filler_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of dermal filler used.
* **Total Amount Paid** (`total_amount_paid`, number, optional): Enter the total amount paid.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Record the date of payment.
* **Dermal Filler Cost** (`dermal_filler_cost`, text, optional): Record the cost of the dermal filler.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, optional): Enter the client's signature for verification.
* **Billing Provider** (`billing_provider`, text, optional): Enter the billing provider information.
* **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`, text, optional): Enter the insurance company information.
* **Insurance Claim Status** (`insurance_claim_status`, text, optional): Record the insurance claim status.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes.
* **Dermal Filler Product** (`dermal_filler_product`, text, optional): Record the dermal filler product used.
* **Dermal Filler Vial Size** (`dermal_filler_vial_size`, text, optional): Record the dermal filler vial size.
* **Dermal Filler Vial Serial Number** (`dermal_filler_vial_serial_number`, text, optional): Record the dermal filler vial serial number.
* **Dermal Filler Lot Number** (`dermal_filler_lot_number`, text, optional): Record the dermal filler lot number.
