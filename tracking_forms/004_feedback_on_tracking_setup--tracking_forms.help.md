# feedback_on_tracking_setup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the setup of tracking on websites or web pages. This form is designed to gather details about how tracking is configured and being used on a specific page or website.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Tracking Setup" field with a brief description of the tracking setup on your website or page.
2. Select the "Tracking Implementation" option to indicate how tracking is currently implemented (e.g. Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, Mixpanel).
3. Choose the "Analytics Platform" option to select the platform being used for tracking (e.g. Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, Mixpanel).
4. Enter the "Tracking Code" if applicable.
5. Select the "Setup Type" to indicate whether the tracking is single-page, multi-page, or server-side.
6. Enter the "Page Title" and "Page URL" of the page being tracked.
7. Indicate the current "Tracking Status" of the page (e.g. Enabled, Disabled).
8. Provide any "Feedback" about the tracking setup.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tracking Setup** (Tracking Setup, text, required): A brief description of the tracking setup on your website or page.
* **Tracking Implementation** (Tracking Implementation, text, required): An indication of how tracking is currently implemented (e.g. Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, Mixpanel).
* **Analytics Platform** (Analytics Platform, select_one, required): A selection of the platform being used for tracking (e.g. Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, Mixpanel).
* **Tracking Code** (Tracking Code, text, optional): The tracking code used for the analytics platform selected.
* **Setup Type** (Setup Type, select_multiple, required): A selection of whether the tracking is single-page, multi-page, or server-side.
* **Page Title** (Page Title, text, required): The title of the page being tracked.
* **Page URL** (Page URL, text, required): The URL of the page being tracked.
* **Tracking Status** (Tracking Status, select_one, required): An indication of the current tracking status (e.g. Enabled, Disabled).
* **Feedback** (Feedback, text, required): Any additional feedback or comments about the tracking setup.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the "Tracking Setup" field.
* Select the correct "Analytics Platform" and "Setup Type" that apply to your tracking setup.
* Enter the correct "Tracking Code" if applicable.
* Make sure to select "Enabled" or "Disabled" for "Tracking Status" accurately.
* Provide any additional feedback or comments about the tracking setup in the "Feedback" field.
