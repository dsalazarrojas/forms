# Meeting Plan Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Meeting Plan Checklist is a tool to help you organize and track your meetings. It allows you to select the pre-meeting and post-meeting activities to be completed, as well as take notes and keep track of the meeting itself.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of pre-meeting activity you will be doing from the options provided.
2. Choose the pre-meeting checklist items you will be completing from the available options.
3. Select the meeting type you will be having from the options provided.
4. Choose the post-meeting checklist items you will be completing from the available options.
5. Fill in any additional notes about the meeting in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Plan Checklist Form** (`meeting_plan_checklist_form`, label, required/optional): This is the main form title.
* **Pre Meeting** (`Pre Meeting`, select_one, required): Choose the pre-meeting activity you will be doing. Options are Meeting Notes, Meeting Minutes, Agenda Item 1, and Agenda Item 2.
* **Pre Meeting Checklist** (`Pre Meeting Checklist`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the pre-meeting checklist items you will be completing. Options are Check 1, Check 2, and Check 3.
* **Meeting** (`Meeting`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of meeting you will be having. Options are Meeting 1, Meeting 2, and Meeting 3.
* **Meeting Checklist** (`Meeting Checklist`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the post-meeting checklist items you will be completing. Options are Checklist 1, Checklist 2, and Checklist 3.
* **Post Meeting** (`Post Meeting`, select_one, optional): Choose the post-meeting activity you will be doing. Options are Post Meeting 1, Post Meeting 2, and Post Meeting 3.
* **Post Meeting Checklist** (`Post Meeting Checklist`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the post-meeting checklist items you will be completing. Options are Checklist 1, Checklist 2, and Checklist 3.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, text, optional): Fill in any additional notes about the meeting.
