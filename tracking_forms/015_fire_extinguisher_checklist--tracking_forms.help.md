# Fire Extinguisher Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about fire extinguishers, including their locations, conditions, types, and other relevant details. It helps with scheduled maintenance and tracking of fire extinguishers to ensure they are ready for use in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the location of the fire extinguisher, choosing from the available options (Kitchen, Bathroom, Office, etc.).
2. Select the type of fire extinguisher, which can be Water, Foam, or Dry.
3. Indicate the condition of the fire extinguisher, selecting one or more of the options (Good, Partially Extinguished, Needs Recharge).
4. Enter any special tags or notes related to the fire extinguisher in the Tag Number field.
5. If applicable, enter the model year of the fire extinguisher.
6. Enter the model name of the fire extinguisher.
7. Record the service life of the fire extinguisher, usually in years.
8. Select the assigned location where the fire extinguisher is typically kept.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments about the fire extinguisher.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fire Extinguisher Location**: Where the fire extinguisher is located (e.g. Kitchen, Bathroom, Office).
* **Fire Type**: The type of fire extinguisher, which can be Water, Foam, or Dry.
* **Fire Extinguisher Condition**: The current condition of the fire extinguisher, which can be Good, Partially Extinguished, or Needs Recharge.
* **Tag Number**: Any special tags or notes related to the fire extinguisher.
* **Fire Extinguisher Model Year**: The model year of the fire extinguisher (if applicable).
* **Fire Extinguisher Model Name**: The name of the fire extinguisher model.
* **Fire Extinguisher Service Life**: The remaining service life of the fire extinguisher in years.
* **Assigned Fire Extinguisher Location**: Where the fire extinguisher is typically kept.
* **Notes**: Additional comments or notes about the fire extinguisher.

## Tips
* Always enter accurate and up-to-date information about the fire extinguishers to ensure their effectiveness in case of an emergency.
* Make sure to check the condition of fire extinguishers regularly to prevent accidents and ensure compliance with fire protection regulations.
* If you are unsure about any information, consult with your supervisor or the fire safety officer for guidance.
