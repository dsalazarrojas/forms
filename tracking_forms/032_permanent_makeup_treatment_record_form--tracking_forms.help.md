## Permanent Makeup Treatment Record Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record information about permanent makeup treatments, including client details, treatment specifics, and billing information. 

## How To Complete This Form
This form has multiple pages. Please start by filling in the client's information on the first page, followed by treatment details and notes on the subsequent pages. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info** (`client_info`, text, not required): Enter the client's name and any relevant contact information.
* **Treatment Details** (`treatment_details`, text, not required): Describe the treatment administered, including the procedure and any notable events.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, not required): Additional notes from the technician about the treatment.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, not required): Enter the client's email address for correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, not required): Enter the client's phone number for follow-up calls or appointments.
* **Treatment Date** (`treatment_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the treatment.
* **Treatment Time** (`treatment_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the treatment.
* **Treatment Duration** (`treatment_duration`, number, not required): Enter the total duration of the treatment.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the next follow-up.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the next follow-up.
* **Follow-up Duration** (`follow_up_duration`, number, not required): Enter the total duration of the next follow-up.
* **Assigned Technician** (`assigned_technician`, select multiple, required): Select the technician who administered the treatment.
* **Notes from Technician** (`notes_from_technician`, note, not required): Enter additional notes from the technician about the treatment.
* **Billing Info** (`billing_info`, text, not required): Enter any relevant billing information or instructions.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access the client's contact information.
* Verify the accuracy of the information entered before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the client or the assigned technician for clarification.
* Please ensure that you have the necessary training and expertise to administer permanent makeup treatments.
