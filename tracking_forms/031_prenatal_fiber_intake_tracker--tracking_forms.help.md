# Prenatal Fiber Intake Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track the prenatal fiber intake of users, helping healthcare professionals monitor and analyze data to provide informed care.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the correct options for each field, filling out all required fields. You can find more details on each field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information (User Info)
• **User Information (User Info)** (`user_info`, text, required): Please enter your name.

### Fiber Consumed (Fiber Consumed)
• **Fiber Consumed (Fiber Consumed)** (`fiber_consumed`, number, required): Report the total amount of fiber consumed in a 24-hour period.

### Fiber Sources (Fiber Sources)
• **Fiber Sources (Fiber Sources)** (`fiber_sources`, select_multiple, required): Select all fiber sources from the list:
  • Beans
  • Cereal
  • Fruit
  • Veggies

### Digestive Status (Digestive Status)
• **Digestive Status (Digestive Status)** (`digestive_status`, select_one, required): Choose your digestive status:
  • Good
  • Fair
  • Poor

### Comments
• **Comments (Comments)** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your fiber intake.

### Time of Day (Time of Day)
• **Time of Day (Time of Day)** (`time_of_day`, time, optional): Report the time when you consumed the fiber.

### Date (Date)
• **Date (Date)** (`date`, date, optional): Report the date you consumed the fiber.

### Notes (Notes)
• **Notes (Notes)** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about your fiber intake.

Tips:
* Please ensure you complete all required fields for accurate data submission.
* Only select relevant and accurate fiber sources from the list.
* If unsure about any field, consult your healthcare professional.
