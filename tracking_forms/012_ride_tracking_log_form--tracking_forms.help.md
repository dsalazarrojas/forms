# Ride Tracking Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and track information about company-owned vehicle rides. Authorized personnel use this form to monitor and record details about each ride, including start and end times, vehicle and driver information, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **trip details**, including the start and end times of the ride.
2. Select the **vehicle** used for the ride from the available options.
3. Enter the **driver** responsible for the ride from the available options.
4. Record the **distance** traveled during the ride.
5. Enter the current **fuel level** of the vehicle.
6. Select the **vehicle status** at the end of the ride (e.g., Running, Stopped, Not Available).
7. Add any additional **notes** about the ride.
8. Select the **vehicles** used for the ride (if multiple vehicles were used).
9. Select the **drivers** responsible for the ride (if multiple drivers were involved).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **trip_details**: Record the start and end times of the ride in the designated text box.
* **vehicle_details**: Enter the details of the vehicle used for the ride.
* **trip_start**: Enter the start time of the ride, in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **trip_end**: Enter the end time of the ride, in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **distance**: Record the distance traveled during the ride.
* **fuel_level**: Enter the current fuel level of the vehicle.
* **driver_details**: Enter the details of the driver responsible for the ride.
* **vehicle_status**: Select the status of the vehicle at the end of the ride (e.g., Running, Stopped, Not Available).
* **notes**: Record any additional information about the ride.
* **vehicle_id**: Select the vehicles used for the ride from the available options.
* **driver_id**: Select the drivers responsible for the ride from the available options.
