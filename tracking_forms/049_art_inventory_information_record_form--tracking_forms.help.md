# Art Inventory Information Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Inventory Information Record Form is a centralized tool for tracking and managing art pieces within the organization's inventory. It ensures accurate and up-to-date records of each item's unique characteristics, condition, and ownership history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a unique **Inventory ID** that will serve as the identifier for this artwork within the inventory.
2. Enter the **Title of work**, which should be the actual title of the artwork.
3. Enter the **Artist** who created the work.
4. If applicable, enter the **Year created** when the work was created.
5. If applicable, enter the **Medium** used to create the work.
6. If applicable, enter the **Dimensions** of the artwork.
7. If applicable, enter a **Condition report** noting any issues or concerns with the work's condition.
8. Enter the **Storage or display location** where the artwork is currently stored or displayed.
9. If applicable, enter the **Provenance** of the work, including its ownership history.
10. If applicable, enter the **Insurance value** of the artwork for insurance purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Inventory ID** (1):
	* A unique identifier for the item, typically a number or code.
	* Required.
- **Title of work** (2):
	* The actual title of the artwork.
	* Required.
- **Artist** (3):
	* The name of the artist who created the work.
	* Required.
- **Year created** (4):
	* The year the artwork was created.
	* Optional.
	* Example: 1990
- **Medium** (5):
	* The materials or technique used to create the work.
	* Optional.
	* Example: oil on canvas or digital print.
- **Dimensions** (6):
	* The height, width, and depth of the artwork.
	* Optional.
	* Example: 12 x 18 inches.
- **Condition report** (7):
	* Notes about the current condition of the work, including any issues or concerns.
	* Optional.
- **Storage or display location** (8):
	* Where the work is currently stored or displayed.
	* Required.
- **Provenance** (9):
	* A brief summary of the ownership history of the work.
	* Optional.
- **Insurance value** (10):
	* The appraised value of the artwork for insurance purposes.
	* Optional.
	* Example: $1,000.00
