# Cleaning Routine Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track the cleaning routine of a facility. It is meant to be completed by cleaning staff to report their activities and experiences on a daily basis. The form is divided into several sections to help you provide detailed information about the cleaning process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Begin by providing the Facility Information: Enter the name and location of the facility, as well as the name of the person responsible for the facility's maintenance.
2.  In the Daily Tracking section, select the date and shift period of the cleaning activities.
3.  For each area cleaned, select whether it was completely or partially cleaned. If an area was not cleaned, select "False". If not applicable, select "Not Applicable".
4.  In the Specific Tasks Completed section, indicate whether floors were swept and mopped, surfaces were dusted and wiped, windows were cleaned, carpets were vacuumed, and bathrooms were sanitized.
5.  In the Observations and Issues section, describe any issues encountered during the cleaning process and mention any maintenance or repairs needed.
6.  Finally, rate the overall quality of the cleaning and provide any additional comments or observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Facility Name** (`facility_name`): Enter the name of the facility being cleaned.
*   **Facility Location** (`facility_location`): Enter the address or location identifier of the facility.
*   **Facility Manager** (`facility_manager`): Enter the name of the person responsible for the facility's maintenance.
*   **Tracking Date** (`tracking_date`): Select the date of the cleaning activities.
*   **Shift Period** (`shift_period`): Choose the shift period of the cleaning activities (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
*   **Cleaning Staff** (`cleaning_staff`): Enter the names of the staff members assigned to the cleaning activities.
*   **Shift Supervisor** (`supervisor`): Enter the name of the supervising person (if applicable).
*   **Entryway and Lobby** (`entryway_cleaned`): Select whether the entryway and lobby were completely or partially cleaned.
*   **Restrooms** (`restrooms_cleaned`): Select whether the restrooms were completely or partially cleaned.
*   **Break/Rest Room** (`break_room_cleaned`): Select whether the break/ rest room was completely or partially cleaned.
*   **Office Areas** (`offices_cleaned`): Select whether the office areas were completely or partially cleaned.
*   **Conference/Meeting Rooms** (`conference_rooms_cleaned`): Select whether the conference/meeting rooms were completely or partially cleaned.
*   **Hallways and Corridors** (`hallways_cleaned`): Select whether the hallways and corridors were completely or partially cleaned.
*   **Common Areas** (`common_areas_cleaned`): Select whether the common areas were completely or partially cleaned.
*   **Trash Emptied and Replaced** (`trash_emptied`): Select whether the trash was completely or partially emptied and replaced.
*   **Floors - Swept and Mopped** (`floors_swept_mopped`): Select whether the floors were completely or partially swept and mopped.
*   **Surfaces - Dusted and Wiped** (`surfaces_dusted`): Select whether the surfaces were completely or partially dusted and wiped.
*   **Windows - Cleaned** (`windows_cleaned`): Select whether the windows were completely or partially cleaned.
*   **Carpets - Vacuumed** (`carpets_vacumed`): Select whether the carpets were completely or partially vacuumed.
*   **Bathrooms - Sanitized** (`bathrooms_sanitized`): Select whether the bathrooms were completely or partially sanitized.
*   **Issues Encountered During Cleaning** (`issues_encountered`): Describe any issues encountered during the cleaning process.
*   **Maintenance or Repairs Needed** (`maintenance_needed`): Mention any maintenance or repairs needed for the facility.
*   **Supplies That Need Restocking** (`supplies_needed`): Mention any supplies that need restocking.
*   **Overall Cleaning Quality Rating** (`quality_rating`): Rate the overall quality of the cleaning activities (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
*   **Additional Comments** (`comments`): Provide any additional comments or observations.
