# harvest_tracking_form--tracking_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Harvest Tracking Form is used to track and record information about the harvest of crops on a farm. This form helps farm personnel to accurately and efficiently track their harvests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the farm id field, which is the unique identifier for your farm.
2. Enter the plant id of the crop being harvested.
3. If the date of harvest is known, enter it in the date harvested field.
4. Record the quantity of crop harvested in the quantity harvested field.
5. Provide any additional notes about the harvest in the notes field.
6. Indicate the status of the form by selecting from the tracking form status options.
7. Enter the farm name, address, city, state, zip, latitude, longitude, and altitude of the farm.
8. If applicable, enter the email and phone number of the farm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Farm Id** (`farm_id`, `text`, required): Enter the unique identifier for your farm.
* **Plant Id** (`plant_id`, `text`, required): Enter the id of the plant being harvested.
* **Date Harvested** (`date_harvested`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the harvest if known.
* **Quantity Harvested** (`quantity_harvested`, `number`, required): Record the quantity of crop harvested.
* **Harvested By** (`harvested_by`, `text`, required): Enter the person or entity that harvested the crop.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Provide any additional notes about the harvest.
* **Harvested On** (`harvested_on`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the harvest.
* **Harvested By Phone** (`harvested_by_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or entity that harvested the crop.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email of the farm.
* **Farm Notes** (`farm_notes`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional notes about the farm.
* **Tracking Form Status** (`tracking_form_status`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the status of the form by selecting from the options.
* **Farm Name** (`farm_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the farm.
* **Farm Address** (`farm_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the farm.
* **Farm City** (`farm_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city of the farm.
* **Farm State** (`farm_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state of the farm.
* **Farm Zip** (`farm_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the zip code of the farm.
* **Farm Phone** (`farm_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the farm.
* **Farm Email** (`farm_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email of the farm.
* **Farm Latitude** (`farm_latitude`, `text`, required): Enter the latitude of the farm.
* **Farm Longitude** (`farm_longitude`, `text`, required): Enter the longitude of the farm.
* **Farm Altitude** (`farm_altitude`, `text`, required): Enter the altitude of the farm.
* **Weather Station** (`weather_station`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the weather station.
* **Weather Data** (`weather_data`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional weather data.
* **Weather Station Latitude** (`farm_weather_station_latitude`, `text`, optional): Enter the latitude of the weather station.
* **Weather Station Longitude** (`farm_weather_station_longitude`, `text`, optional): Enter the longitude of the weather station.
