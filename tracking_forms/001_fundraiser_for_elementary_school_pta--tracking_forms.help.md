# Fundraiser For Elementary School PTA - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report donations made to the Elementary School PTA fundraiser. Fill in the form accurately to ensure that your donation is recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Form Name (Field 1)
	* Email (Field 3)
	* Amount Donated (Field 5)
	* Method Donated (Field 6)
2. Optionally, fill in the following fields:
	* Name (Field 2, not required but recommended for your convenience)
	* Phone (Field 4, not required but recommended for communication purposes)
	* Additional Info (Field 7, not required but recommended for any additional comments or details)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Name** (Form 1):
	+ A text field to input the name of the form (e.g., "Fundraiser 2023").
* **Name** (Field 2):
	+ A text field to input your name for reference and convenience.
* **Email** (Field 3):
	+ An email field to input your email address (e.g., "john.doe@example.com").
	+ This field is required.
* **Phone** (Field 4):
	+ A text field to input your phone number (e.g., "123-456-7890").
	+ Not required but recommended for communication purposes.
* **Amount Donated** (Field 5):
	+ A number field to input the amount donated in currency (e.g., "10.00").
	+ This field is required.
* **Method Donated** (Field 6):
	+ A select-one field with options to choose the method of donation (e.g., "Cash", "Card", etc.).
	+ Choose one option from the list.
* **Additional Info** (Field 7):
	+ A note field for any additional comments or details about your donation.
	+ Not required but recommended for any extra information.
* **Submit** (Field 8):
	+ A select-multiple field to select how you'd like to be contacted (e.g., "Email", "Phone").
	+ Choose one or more options from the list.

## Tips
* Fill in all required fields accurately and correctly.
* Use the "Additional Info" field if you have any extra comments or details about your donation.
* If you're unsure about any information, contact the PTA representative for assistance.
