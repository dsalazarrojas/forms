# Digital Dominance Quiz Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Dominance Quiz Form is designed to collect information for user engagement and satisfaction analysis. It helps us assess individual progress and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name and last name in the "User First Name" and "User Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Choose the category that best suits your interests or preferences from the "Quiz Category" dropdown menu.
3. Select all that apply from the "Quiz Question" multiple-choice options.
4. Provide your answer to the "Quiz Answer" question.
5. Enter your "Quiz Score" if you're tracking progress.
6. Optionally, record the time it took you to complete the quiz by selecting a time in the "Quiz Time" field.
7. Add any additional notes about your experience in the "Quiz Notes" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "User Email" field (not required, but recommended for contact purposes).
9. Enter your phone number in the "User Phone" field (not required).
10. Enter your date of birth in the "User Date of Birth" field (not required).
11. Select your local time in the "User Time" field (not required).
12. Enter your home address in the "User Address" field (not required).
13. Enter your zip code in the "User Zip" field (not required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **User Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Quiz Category** (Select One, Required): Choose the category that best relates to your interests or preferences.
* **Quiz Question** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all that apply from the available options.
* **Quiz Answer** (Text, Required): Provide a brief answer to the quiz question.
* **Quiz Score** (Number, Required): Enter your current quiz score, if you're tracking progress.
* **Quiz Time** (Time, Required): Select the time it took you to complete the quiz.
* **Quiz Notes** (Note, Required): Add any additional notes about your experience.
* **User Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **User Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted.
* **User Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth, if you'd like to provide additional information.
* **User Time** (Time, Optional): Select your local time, if you'd like to track completion time.
* **User Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your home address, if you'd like to provide additional context.
* **User Zip** (Text, Optional): Enter your zip code, if you'd like to provide additional location information.
