# personnel_emergency_response_group_training_record_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The personnel emergency response group training record form is designed to track and record the training status and details of members of the emergency response group. This form helps ensure that personnel within the group have up-to-date information about their own training status and specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing Training Info, which includes any relevant details about the training session.
2. Enter Personnel Info, which includes information about the personnel member, such as their name and title.
3. Choose the current Training Status, which indicates whether the member is currently active or inactive in the group.
4. Select the Training Type, which indicates whether the member has received the required training or not.
5. Enter the Training Location, which is the location where the training was held.
6. Enter the Training Date and Training Time, which are the date and time of the training session.
7. Enter the Personnel Name, which is the name of the member being tracked.
8. Enter the Personnel Title, which is the title of the member being tracked.
9. Enter the Personnel Department, which is the department of the member being tracked.
10. Enter the Training Hours, which is the total amount of training hours completed.
11. Enter the Training Location, which is the location where the training was held.
12. Select the Training Status Update, which indicates whether the member's status has changed.
13. Enter any additional Notes about the training session.
14. Enter the Email address of the member being tracked.
15. Enter the Phone number of the member being tracked.
16. Enter the Personnel ID, which is a unique identifier for the member being tracked.
17. Enter the name of the person who created the record.
18. Enter the time when the record was created.
19. Enter the name of the user to whom the record is assigned.
20. Enter the ID of the tool assigned to the record.
21. Enter the ID of the user assigned to the record.
22. Enter the date when the record was assigned.
23. Enter the time when the record was assigned.
