# Strength Empire Consult Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strength Empire Consult Form is a simple form designed to collect information from users who want to get in touch with the Strength Empire team or provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the contact information section with your details.
2. Answer the personal goals question to help us understand your goals and objectives.
3. Choose your contact preferences for communication.
4. Select the communication channels you are comfortable with.
5. Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted via email.
6. Provide your phone number if you wish to be contacted via phone.
7. Enter your date of birth.
8. Choose your preferred time of day for communication.
9. Add any additional notes you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Fill in your name, address, and any other contact details you are comfortable sharing.
- **Personal Goals** (`personal_goals`, text, required: false): Describe your goals and objectives, so we can better understand what you are looking for.
- **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you prefer to be contacted via phone or email.
- **Communication Channel** (`communication_channel`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the communication channels you are comfortable with, such as phone, email, or both.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted via email.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted via phone.
- **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Time Of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, required: false): Choose a time of day that suits you best for communication.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional information or comments you may have.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure your information is processed correctly.
* Use the correct format for date of birth (YYYY-MM-DD).
* Be as specific as possible when answering the personal goals question to help us understand your needs better.
* Choose the correct communication channels you are comfortable with for us to contact you.
