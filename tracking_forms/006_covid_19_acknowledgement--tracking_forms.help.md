# Covid 19 Acknowledgement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the administration of COVID-19 vaccines. It captures information about the patient, the vaccine type, and the administering medical staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and permissions to complete this form.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
3. Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Consent Form** (`1`, `Patient Consent Form`, required): This is where the patient or their representative provides consent for vaccination administration. Enter the patient's consent statement or signature.
* **Patient Info** (`2`, `Patient Info`, required): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other identifying information.
* **Vaccine Administered** (`3`, `Vaccine Administered`, required): Select "Yes" if the vaccine was administered, or "No" if it was not.
* **Vaccine Type** (`4`, `Vaccine Type`, required): Choose the type of vaccine administered, such as "Pfizer" or "Moderna".
* **Medical Practice** (`5`, `Medical Practice`, optional): Enter the medical practice name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Medical Staff** (`6`, `Medical Staff`, optional): Enter the name of the medical staff member who administered the vaccine.
* **Facility Name** (`7`, `Facility Name`, optional): Enter the name of the facility where the vaccination was administered.
* **Patient Signature** (`8`, `Patient Signature`, required): This is where the patient or their representative signs to acknowledge vaccine administration.
* **Vaccine Administered By** (`9`, `Vaccine Administered By`, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare professional who administered the vaccine.
* **Date Administered** (`10`, `Date Administered`, required): Enter the date the vaccine was administered.
* **Time Administered** (`11`, `Time Administered`, required): Enter the time the vaccine was administered.
* **Vaccine Lot Number** (`12`, `Vaccine Lot Number`, optional): Enter the vaccine lot number, if applicable.
* **Batch Number** (`13`, `Batch Number`, optional): Enter the batch number of the vaccine, if applicable.
* **Patient ID** (`14`, `Patient ID`, required): Enter the patient's ID number or unique identifier.
* **Date of Birth** (`15`, `Date of Birth`, required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Medical Practice Address** (`16`, `Medical Practice Address`, optional): Enter the address of the medical practice.
* **Medical Practice Phone** (`17`, `Medical Practice Phone`, optional): Enter the phone number of the medical practice.
* **Medical Practice Email** (`18`, `Medical Practice Email`, optional): Enter the email address of the medical practice.
* **Facility Phone** (`19`, `Facility Phone`, optional): Enter the phone number of the facility.
* **Facility Email** (`20`, `Facility Email`, optional): Enter the email address of the facility.
* **Facility Address** (`21`, `Facility Address`, optional): Enter the address of the facility.
* **Vaccine Administered By Title** (`22`, `Vaccine Administered By Title`, optional): Enter the title of the healthcare professional who administered the vaccine.
* **Vaccine Administered By First Name** (`23`, `Vaccine Administered By First Name`, optional): Enter the first name of the healthcare professional who administered the vaccine.
* **Vaccine Administered By Last Name** (`24`, `Vaccine Administered By Last Name`, optional): Enter the last name of the healthcare professional who administered the vaccine.
* **Vaccine Administered By Title Position** (`25`, `Vaccine Administered By Title Position`, optional): Enter the title position of the healthcare professional who administered the vaccine.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting.
- Ensure all required fields are filled before proceeding.
- If you need help, refer back to this guide or contact the relevant medical staff or system administrators.
