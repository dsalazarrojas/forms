# Material Return Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Material Return Form is designed to track material returns from customers, providing a centralized record of returned materials and aiding in the management of the return process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Material Return Form** (<code>material_return_form</code>): Enter a brief description of the returned material.
2. **Date Received** (<code>date_received</code>): Enter the date the material was received back.
3. **Description** (<code>description</code>): Briefly describe the returned material.
4. **Reason for Return** (<code>reason_for_return</code>): Specify the reason the material was returned.
5. **Material Returned** (<code>material_returned</code>): Enter the material that was returned.
6. **Condition of Material** (<code>condition_of_material</code>): Describe the condition of the returned material.
7. **Signature** (<code>signature</code>): Sign and date your return receipt.
8. **Customer Details** (<code>customer_details</code>): Enter your contact number, email, and signature.
9. **Contact Number** (<code>contact_number</code>): Enter your contact number.
10. **Email** (<code>email</code>): Enter your email address.
11. **Return Reference Number** (<code>return_reference_number</code>): Enter the return reference number.
12. **Material Return Status** (<code>material_return_status</code>): Select the status of the returned material (Available, In Process, Returned).
13. **Customer Signature** (<code>customer_signature</code>): Sign and date your return receipt.
14. **Company Details** (<code>company_details</code>): Enter your company's name, contact number, and address.
15. **Customer Contact Number** (<code>customer_contact_number</code>): Enter your customer's contact number.
16. **Material Type** (<code>material_type</code>): Enter the type of material returned.
17. **Customer Name** (<code>customer_name</code>): Enter your customer's name.
18. **Return Date** (<code>return_date</code>): Enter the date of the return.
19. **Return Time** (<code>return_time</code>): Enter the time of the return.
20. **Customer Address** (<code>customer_address</code>): Enter your customer's address.
21. **Reason for Returning** (<code>reason_for_returning</code>): Specify the reason the material was returned.
22. **Material Returned At** (<code>material_returned_at</code>): Enter the date the material was returned.
23. **Company Name** (<code>company_name</code>): Enter your company's name.
24. **Return Status** (<code>return_status</code>): Select the status of the returned material (Available, In Process, Returned).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
* This form is intended to be used once per material return, so please do not reuse this form for multiple returns.
