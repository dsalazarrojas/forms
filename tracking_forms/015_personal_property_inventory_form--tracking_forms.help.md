# personal_property_inventory_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and maintain an inventory of personal properties, including items and their associated information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start a new entry or edit an existing one.
2. Fill in the required fields for each item, including its name, description, value, location, and category.
3. Select the insurance policy type and enter the insurance policy number.
4. Enter the contact person's email and phone number.
5. Select the insurance company and effective date of the policy.
6. Provide any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Property Info (1)**: Enter a brief title or description for the property inventory.
* **Item 1 (2)**: Enter the name of the first item in the inventory. (Text field)
* **Item 1 (3)**: Enter a description of the first item. (Text field)
* **Item 1 (4)**: Enter the value of the first item. (Number field)
* **Item 1 Location (5)**: Enter the location of the first item. (Text field)
* **Item 2 (6)**: Enter the name of the second item in the inventory. (Text field)
* **Item 2 (7)**: Enter a description of the second item. (Text field)
* **Item 2 (8)**: Enter the value of the second item. (Number field)
* **Item 2 Location (9)**: Enter the location of the second item. (Text field)
* **Categories (4)**: Select the category of the items in the inventory. (Select One field)
* **Items 1 Item Name (5)**: Enter the name of the first item. (Text field)
* **Items 1 Item Description (6)**: Enter a description of the first item. (Text field)
* **Items 1 Item Value (7)**: Enter the value of the first item. (Number field)
* **Items 1 Item Location (8)**: Enter the location of the first item. (Text field)
* **Items 2 Item Name (9)**: Enter the name of the second item. (Text field)
* **Items 2 Item Description (10)**: Enter a description of the second item. (Text field)
* **Items 2 Item Value (11)**: Enter the value of the second item. (Number field)
* **Items 2 Item Location (12)**: Enter the location of the second item. (Text field)
* **Notes (13)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the property inventory. (Text field)
* **Insurance Policy Number (14)**: Enter the insurance policy number. (Text field)
* **Insured Person (15)**: Enter the name of the person insured. (Text field)
* **Contact Person (16)**: Enter the name of the contact person. (Text field)
* **Contact Person Email (17)**: Enter the email of the contact person. (Email field)
* **Contact Person Phone (18)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person. (Text field)
* **Insurance Policy Type (19)**: Select the type of insurance policy. (Select One field)
* **Insurance Company (20)**: Enter the name of the insurance company. (Text field)
* **Effective Date (21)**: Enter the effective date of the insurance policy. (Date field)

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information about each item in the inventory.
* Use the categories field to easily identify and filter items by category.
* Double-check the insurance policy details to ensure accuracy.
* Enter clear and concise notes or comments to facilitate communication and decision-making.
