# Healthcare Professional Time Tracking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for tracking shifts worked by healthcare professionals such as doctors and nurses. It is used to record details about their shifts, including start and end times, roles, units, activities, and staff names.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date for which you want to track your shifts.
2. Enter the start time for your shift.
3. Choose your role (e.g. Doctor, Nurse, Other).
4. Select the units where you worked (e.g. Cardiothoracic Surgery Unit, Emergency Department, Neurology).
5. Choose the activities you performed (e.g. Patient Care, Consultations, Laboratory Tests).
6. Enter the end time for your shift.
7. Select your name from the list.
8. Optionally, enter the length of your shift.
9. Choose the unit where you worked.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Shifts** (`1`, date, required: false): Select the date for which you want to track your shifts. This is the date that will be associated with your shift.
* **Shift Start Time** (`2`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of your shift in 24-hour format (e.g. 08:00).
* **Role** (`3`, select_one, required: false): Choose your role in the healthcare setting (e.g. Doctor, Nurse, Other).
* **Units** (`4`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the units where you worked (e.g. Cardiothoracic Surgery Unit, Emergency Department, Neurology).
* **Activities** (`5`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the activities you performed during your shift (e.g. Patient Care, Consultations, Laboratory Tests).
* **Shift End Time** (`6`, time, required: false): Enter the end time of your shift in 24-hour format (e.g. 17:00).
* **Staff Name** (`7`, select_one, required: false): Select your name from the list.
* **Shift Length** (`8`, number, required: false): Optionally, enter the length of your shift in hours.
* **Unit** (`9`, select_one, required: false): Choose the unit where you worked (e.g. Hosp 1, Clinic A).
