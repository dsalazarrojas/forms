# Creature Catching Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Creature Catching Log" form is designed to record and track details of a creature caught or observed. This form will help researchers, conservationists, and enthusiasts to monitor and analyze data on creature sightings, habitat, and behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "species" field to indicate whether the creature was caught or observed.
2. Provide a brief description of the encounter in the "details" field.
3. (Optional) Upload a photo of the creature in the "photo" field.
4. Record the date and time of the encounter in the "date_time" field.
5. Specify the location where the creature was caught or observed.
6. Select the "researcher" field to indicate if a researcher was present during the encounter.
7. Choose the correct species from the "species_id" field.
8. Select the correct "species_group" from the "species_group" field.
9. (Optional) Select the conservation status of the species from the "conservation_status" field.
10. Select the user's ID from the "user_id" field.
11. Choose the user's location from the "user_location" field.
12. Enter the species name in the "species_name" field.
13. Enter the common name of the species (if applicable) in the "common_name" field.
14. (Optional) Enter the species' family, genus, and common name in the "family", "genus", and "common_name" fields.
15. (Optional) Enter a description of the species in the "species_description" field.
16. (Optional) Select the species' class from the "species_class" field.
17. (Optional) Select the species' order from the "species_order" field.
18. (Optional) Enter the species' habitat and range in the "species_habitat" and "species_range" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **species** (Select One): Indicates whether the creature was caught or observed.
* **details** (Text): Provide a brief description of the encounter.
