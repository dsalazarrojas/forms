# Building Maintenance Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help track and report on the maintenance of various aspects of a building's facilities. It covers the inspection and maintenance of various systems, including the roof, gutters, doors and windows, HVAC system, electrical, and more. The form is intended for use by building maintenance personnel to ensure that all necessary maintenance and repairs are properly documented and addressed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the inspection/maintenance date in the "Inspection/Maintenance Date" field.
2.  Enter the name of the maintenance person in the "Maintenance Person Name" field.
3.  Enter the building or facility name in the "Building or Facility Name" field.
4.  Select the type of maintenance being performed in the "Type of Maintenance" field.
5.  For each of the sections on the form, select the status of the items being inspected:
   - Exterior Maintenance: Select the overall condition of the exterior of the building.
   - Roof Inspection and Maintenance: Select the condition of the roof.
   - Gutters and Downspouts: Select the condition of the gutters and downspouts.
   - Exterior Paint and Caulking: Select the condition of the exterior paint and caulking.
   - Landscaping and Grounds: Select the overall condition of the landscaping and grounds.
   - Parking Lot Condition: Select the condition of the parking lot.
   - Interior Maintenance: Enter any notes about the interior of the building.
   - Floor Cleaning and Maintenance: Select the condition of the floors.
   - Walls and Surfaces: Select the condition of the walls and surfaces.
   - Ceilings and Lighting Fixtures: Select the condition of the ceilings and lighting fixtures.
   - Doors and Windows: Select the condition of the doors and windows.
   - HVAC System: Select the condition of the HVAC system.
   - Electrical System: Enter any notes about the electrical system.
   - Plumbing: Enter any notes about the plumbing system.
   - Fire Safety: Enter any notes about the fire safety system.
   - Fire Extinguisher Inspection: Select the condition of the fire extinguishers.
   - Smoke Detector Maintenance: Select the condition of the smoke detectors.
   - Emergency Lighting: Select the condition of the emergency lighting.
   - General Maintenance: Enter any other notes about general maintenance.
   - Trash and Waste Management: Select the condition of the trash and waste management.
   - Pest Control and Treatment: Select the condition of the pest control and treatment.
   - Maintenance Summary: Enter any additional notes about maintenance and repairs.
   - Items Needing Repair or Maintenance: Enter any items needing repair or maintenance.
   - Priority Level for Next Maintenance Visit: Select the priority level for the next maintenance visit.
   - Next Scheduled Maintenance: Enter the date of the next scheduled maintenance visit.
   - Additional Maintenance Notes: Enter any additional notes about maintenance and repairs.

## Tips

- Be sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that all necessary maintenance and repairs are properly documented and addressed.
- If an item is not applicable, select "Pass - No issues" or indicate that it is not applicable in the text field.
- If an item requires further explanation or notes, use the text fields to provide additional information.
- If an item requires action, be sure to prioritize it according to the selected priority level.
