# Rewards Redemption Log Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Rewards Redemption Log Form is used to track user rewards redemptions. It is designed to help administrators record and manage rewards given to users for their participation in various programs. This form is not intended for user self-service, and its content will be reviewed and updated regularly by authorized personnel to ensure accuracy and consistency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your User Name**: This is the name of the user who received the reward.
2. **Select a Date**: Choose the date when the reward was given to the user.
3. **Enter Reward Details**: Type in the name of the reward or gift card provided to the user.
4. **Enter Redemption Points**: Record the points redeemed for the reward.
5. **Describe the Redemption**: Provide a brief description of the reward or gift card details.
6. **Enter Email (if available)**: If the user's contact information is available, enter their email address.
7. **Enter Phone Number (if available)**: If the user's contact information is available, enter their phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: The name of the user who received the reward.
* **Date**: The date when the reward was given to the user.
* **Reward**: The name of the reward or gift card provided to the user.
* **Points**: The points redeemed for the reward.
* **Description**: A brief description of the reward or gift card details.
* **Email**: The email address of the user (if available).
* **Phone**: The phone number of the user (if available).

## Tips
* Always enter accurate information to ensure correct tracking of rewards redemptions.
* Review and update this form content regularly to maintain accuracy and consistency.
* If a user's contact information is not available, leave those fields blank.
* If you are unsure about a field, do not hesitate to ask for assistance from authorized personnel.
* This form is only for tracking rewards redemptions, not for other purposes.
