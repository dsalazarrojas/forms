# Worker Timesheet And Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track employee work hours and breaks for multiple sessions. It captures start and end times for clock-in/clock-out sessions, break times, and total hours worked. Employee information, such as name and contact details, is also captured.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each page of the form for a single clock-in/clock-out session.
2. Enter the clock-in time for the session.
3. Enter the report date for the session.
4. Enter the clock-out time for the session.
5. Enter the lunch break start and end times.
6. Enter the break duration.
7. Enter the hours worked for the session.
8. Enter any additional notes about the session.
9. Enter the employee's name.
10. Enter the employee's email address.
11. Enter the employee's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Clock In** (`clock_in`, time, required): Enter the time you started working on the current session.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, required): Enter the date for the current session.
* **Clock Out** (`clock_out`, time, required): Enter the time you ended the current session.
* **Lunch Break Start** (`lunch_report_date`, time, required): Enter the start time of your lunch break.
* **Lunch Break End** (`lunch_break_start`, time, required): Enter the end time of your lunch break.
* **Break Duration** (`break_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of your lunch break in minutes.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, required): Enter the total hours worked for the current session.
* **Clock Out Time** (`clock_out_time`, time, required): Enter the time you clocked out for the current session.
* **Total Hours Worked** (`total_hours_worked`, number, required): Enter the total hours worked for the current session.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the current session.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
