# Weekly To Do List Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Weekly To Do List Entry Form is designed to help you record and manage your tasks and deadlines. It's a straightforward form that collects essential information about the tasks you plan to complete.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the **task_title** field with a brief description of the task you want to complete.
2. In the **task_description** field, provide a more detailed description of the task, including any necessary context or background information.
3. Select the **start_date** field and choose the date you intend to start working on the task.
4. Once you have filled out all the required fields, you can review your completed form to ensure that all the information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **task_title** (Text, Optional): A brief description of the task you want to complete. This field is a good place to start by jotting down the task's main objective.
* **task_description** (Text, Optional): A more detailed description of the task, including any necessary background information or context.
* **start_date** (Date, Optional): The date you plan to start working on the task. This field will help you schedule your task and ensure that you meet your deadlines.

## Tips

* Make sure to review your completed form to ensure that all the information is accurate and complete.
* Use this form regularly to track your tasks and deadlines over the course of the week.
* You can modify the form as needed to suit your specific needs and requirements.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
