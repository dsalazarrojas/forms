## Track Borrowed Items Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to track items that students/employees have borrowed from the classroom/office. This form will help you keep a record of these items, making it easier to manage and retrieve them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the **Tracking Forms** category from the drop-down list.
2. Enter a brief **Description** of the item being borrowed.
3. Enter the **Items** that have been borrowed. You can select from the available options or enter **Other** if the item is not listed.
4. Click **Submit** to submit your record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tracking Forms**: Select the category of the form you are filling out. This will help in categorizing the records.
* **Description**: A brief description of the item being borrowed. This will help in identifying the item and its purpose.
* **Items**: Choose from the list of available items or select **Other** if the item is not listed. This will help in identifying the specific item being borrowed.
* **Other**: This option should be used only if the item is not listed in the available options.
