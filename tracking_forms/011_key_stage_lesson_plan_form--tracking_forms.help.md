# key_stage_lesson_plan_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "key_stage_lesson_plan_form" is a crucial tool for tracking and recording lesson plans for key stages of a curriculum. It helps teachers gather essential information for efficient planning and execution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by navigating through the different pages.
2. Start with the "Teacher Information" page, which includes fields for:
	* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
	* **Teacher Email** (`teacher-name`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
3. Move on to the "School Information" page, which includes fields for:
	* **School Name** (`school-name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your school.
4. Then, select the **Subject Area** (`subject-area`, select_one, required: false): Choose the relevant subject area from the available options.
5. Add any additional comments or notes in the **Note** (`note`, note, required: false) field.
6. If necessary, enter a date for the lesson plan in the **Lesson Plan Date** (`lesson-plan-date`, date, required: false) field.
7. Enter the time for the lesson plan in the **Lesson Plan Time** (`lesson-plan-time`, time, required: false) field.
8. Select the lessons relevant to this key stage in the **Lessons** (`lessons`, select_multiple, required: false): Pick one or more lessons from the available options.
9. Enter the desired outcomes for this lesson plan in the **Outcomes** (`outcomes`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the learning objectives for this lesson.
10. List the necessary materials required for this lesson plan in the **Materials** (`materials`, text, required: false): Specify the materials needed for this lesson.
11. If applicable, enter an **Assessment** (`assessment`, text, required: false): Describe how you will assess student progress.
12. Add any additional comments or notes in the **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false) field.
13. Finally, you may attach relevant files or documents to this lesson plan in the **Uploads** (`uploads`, note, required: false) field.
14. If you have additional comments, you can enter them in the **Additional Comments** (`additional-comments`, text, required: false): This is an optional field for extra remarks.
