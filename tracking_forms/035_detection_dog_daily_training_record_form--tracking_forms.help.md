# Detection Dog Daily Training Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record daily training information for a detection dog, including date, time, training type, location, and notes. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the date and time of the training session.
2. Select the type of training that was conducted, such as Dog Walking, Obedience Training, etc.
3. Choose the training location and any other relevant details.
4. Enter any notes or observations about the training session.
5. Add any photos, attachments, or videos that can support the training record.
6. Finally, provide information about the dog, including its name, breed, age, weight, height, and other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the training session.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the day when the training session took place.
* **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of the training session.
* **Training Type** (`training_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of training that was conducted, such as Dog Walking, Obedience Training, etc.
* **Training Area** (`training_area`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the training location, such as Back Yard, Field, Park, etc.
* **Training Notes** (`training_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any notes or observations about the training session.
* **Trained With** (`trained_with`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose who was present during the training session, such as Owner, Trainer, etc.
* **Trainer** (`trainer`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the trainer or handler.
* **Trained By** (`trained_by`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person training the dog.
* **Owner** (`owner`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the dog's owner.
* **Dog Name** (`dog_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the detection dog.
* **Dog Breed** (`dog_breed`, text, required: false): Enter the breed of the detection dog.
* **Dog Age** (`dog_age`, number, required: false): Enter the age of the detection dog.
* **Dog Weight** (`dog_weight`, number, required: false): Enter the weight of the detection dog.
* **Dog Height** (`dog_height`, number, required: false): Enter the height of the detection dog.
* **Weather** (`weather`, text, required: false): Enter the weather conditions during the training session.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or observations about the dog's behavior or performance.
* **Photos** (`photos`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose to attach photos or links to photos taken during the training session.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose to attach files or links to attachments relevant to the training session.
* **Videos** (`videos`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose to attach videos or links to videos recorded during the training session.
* **Training Notes** (`training_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or observations about the training session.
* **Next Training Date** (`next_training_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the next training session.
* **Next Training Time** (`next_training_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the next training session.
* **Next Training Location** (`next_training_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the next training session.
* **Next Training Type** (`next_training_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of training scheduled for the next session. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out as much information as possible to maintain a comprehensive record of the dog's training progress.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
* Attach relevant photos, attachments, or videos to support the training record.
