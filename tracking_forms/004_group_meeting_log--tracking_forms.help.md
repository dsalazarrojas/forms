# Group Meeting Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Group Meeting Log form is used to document and track the status of group meetings. It helps to maintain a clear and up-to-date record of ongoing and finished items, member attendance, and any additional notes.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the meeting's details, including the date and time it took place.
2. Indicate if any items are ongoing or finished by selecting the corresponding options.
3. List any absent members who did not attend the meeting.
4. Decide if you need to add or remove any members from the meeting.
5. Add any additional notes about the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Log** (`meeting_log`, text, optional): A free-form text field where you can describe the meeting's details, such as its objectives, discussions, and outcomes.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date the meeting took place. Use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time the meeting took place. Use the format `HH:MM`.
* **Ongoing** (`ongoing_item`, select_one, optional): Select `Yes` if the meeting's items are still ongoing, `No` otherwise.
* **Finished** (`finished_item`, select_one, optional): Select `True` if the meeting's items are finished, `False` otherwise.
* **Absent Members** (`absent_member`, select_multiple, optional): Select the members who were absent from the meeting.
* **Add Member** (`add_member`, select_one, optional): Select `Yes` if you need to add a new member to the meeting, `No` otherwise.
* **Remove Member** (`remove_member`, select_one, optional): Select `Yes` if you need to remove a member from the meeting, `No` otherwise.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): A free-form text field where you can add any additional notes about the meeting.
