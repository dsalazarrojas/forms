# Master Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Master Checklist Form is a tool for tracking and monitoring various aspects of project management, including recurring workflows, project action items, and other related tasks and statuses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section of the form carefully, ensuring you understand the purpose and options for each field.
2. For each field, select the relevant option that applies to your project's status. For example, if you have recurring workflows, select 'Yes' for that field.
3. If a field is not relevant to your project, you can leave it blank.
4. Review your selections to ensure accuracy and completeness.
5. Submit the form once you have reviewed and completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recurring Workflows** (`recurring_workflows`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all the workflows that are recurring in your project, such as weekly, biweekly, or monthly updates.
* **Project Action Items** (`project_action_items`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all the actions items that are relevant to your project, such as 'Yes' for each action item.
* **Task Management** (`task_management`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the last task management update, if applicable.
* **Progress Tracking** (`progress_tracking`, select_one, required: false): Check if you are tracking progress, with options 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Priority Management** (`priority_management`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all the priorities that are relevant to your project, such as 'Yes' for each priority.
* **Task Status** (`task_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all the task statuses, such as Active or Inactive.
* **Operational Routines** (`operational_routines`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all the operational routines, such as 'Yes' for each routine.
* **Task Priorities** (`task_priorities`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all the task priorities, such as 'Yes' for each priority.
* **Task Assignments** (`task_assignments`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all the task assignments, such as 'Yes' for each assignment.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about your project.
* **Team Management** (`team_management`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all the team management settings, such as 'Yes' for each setting.
* **Progress Updates** (`progress_updates`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the last progress update, if applicable.

Note: Each field is marked as "required: false" to indicate that it's not mandatory, but it's still essential to complete it for accurate tracking and monitoring.
