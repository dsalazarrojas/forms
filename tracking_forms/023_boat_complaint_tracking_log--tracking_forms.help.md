# Boat Complaint Tracking Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boat Complaint Tracking Log is a system used to record and track boat-related complaints received by the marina. It helps staff to quickly identify and address issues, ensuring timely resolution and customer satisfaction. This guide will walk you through how to complete the form and what information is required.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information.
2. Select the appropriate options for the complaint category and severity level.
3. Describe the issue in detail in the complaint description field.
4. List any witnesses involved and provide their names.
5. Attach any relevant evidence, such as photos or videos.
6. Select the status of the complaint and provide any investigation notes.
7. Record the resolution action taken and the resolution date.
8. Mark whether the complainant is satisfied with the resolution.
9. Schedule a follow-up if additional action is needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint Date**: The date the complaint was received. This is a required field, as it helps track the timeline of the complaint.
* **Complaint Time**: The time the complaint was received. This field is optional, but helps provide more context for the issue.
* **Complainant Name**: The person filing the complaint. This is a required field, as it helps to identify the individual or organization involved.
* **Complainant Email**: The contact email of the person filing the complaint. This is a required field, as it allows staff to contact the complainant easily.
* **Complainant Phone**: The contact phone of the person filing the complaint. This is a required field, as it provides an alternative contact method.
* **Complainant Type**: The relationship of the person filing the complaint to the marina (Boat Owner, Tenant, Visitor, Staff Member, or Other). This is a required field, as it helps to identify the complainant's status.
* **Boat Name**: The name of the boat involved in the complaint. This field is optional, but can be helpful for tracking specific boats.
* **Boat Slip Number**: The slip or dock number of the boat involved. This field is optional, but can be helpful for locating the boat.
* **Complaint Category**: The type of complaint (Noise Disturbance, Property Damage, Safety Violation, Environmental Issue, Facility Issue, Staff Conduct, Billing Dispute, or Other). This is a required field, as it helps to categorize and prioritize complaints.
* **Complaint Description**: A detailed description of the issue. This is a required field, as it helps staff to understand the problem.
* **Incident Location**: The location where the incident occurred. This is a required field, as it helps to identify the scene of the complaint.
* **Severity Level**: The level of severity of the complaint (Low, Medium, High, or Critical). This is a required field, as it helps staff to prioritize the complaint.
* **Witnesses Involved**: Whether there were any witnesses to the incident. This field is optional, but can be helpful for gathering more information.
* **Witness Names**: If there were witnesses, list their names here. This field is optional, but can be helpful for investigation.
* **Evidence Provided**: Any evidence attached, such as photos or videos. This field is optional, but can be helpful for investigation.
* **Received By**: The staff member who received the complaint. This is a required field, as it helps to track who handled the complaint.
* **Assigned To**: The staff member handling the complaint. This is a required field, as it helps to track who is working on the complaint.
* **Complaint Status**: The current status of the complaint (New, Under Investigation, Pending Response, In Resolution, Resolved, or Closed). This is a required field, as it helps to track the progress of the complaint.
* **Investigation Notes**: Any notes from the investigation. This field is optional, but can be helpful for staff reference.
* **Resolution Action Taken**: A description of the action taken to resolve the complaint. This field is optional, but can be helpful for documentation.
* **Resolution Date**: The date the complaint was resolved. This field is optional, but can help to track the timeline of the complaint.
* **Complainant Satisfied**: Whether the complainant was satisfied with the resolution. This field is optional, but can be helpful for tracking customer satisfaction.
* **Follow-Up Required**: Whether additional action is needed after the complaint is resolved. This is a required field, as it helps to schedule follow-ups.
* **Follow-Up Date**: The scheduled follow-up date. This field is optional, but can be helpful for tracking follow-up actions.
* **Complaint Tracking Number**: The official tracking number for the complaint. This field is optional, but can be helpful for referencing the complaint.
