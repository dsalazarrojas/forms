# Client Journal Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to track and reflect on the client's progress, mood, and experiences over time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the **Client Name** field, which is the name of the client you are working with.
2. Record the **Journal Entry Date**, which is the date you are completing the form.
3. In the **Overall Mood Today** field, choose the best description of your current mood from the select options.
4. In the **Primary focus or goal for today** field, enter a brief description of your primary focus or goal for the day.
5. If applicable, describe the **Biggest challenge faced today** field.
6. In the **One thing I am grateful for today** field, enter something you are grateful for.
7. If applicable, update the **Progress made on long-term goals** field.
8. In the **Insights or realizations during today's reflection** field, enter any insights or realizations you had during the reflection.
9. If applicable, enter any **Topics to discuss in our next session**.
10. Finally, rate your **Current Energy Level**, from 1 (Very Low) to 5 (Very High).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (text)**: Enter the name of the client you are working with.
* **Journal Entry Date (date)**: Record the date you are completing the form.
* **Overall Mood Today (select)**: Choose the best description of your current mood from the options provided.
* **Primary focus or goal for today (text)**: Enter a brief description of your primary focus or goal for the day.
* **Biggest challenge faced today (text)**: If applicable, describe the biggest challenge faced today.
* **One thing I am grateful for today (text)**: Enter something you are grateful for today.
* **Progress made on long-term goals (select)**: If applicable, update the progress made on long-term goals from the options provided.
* **Insights or realizations during today's reflection (text)**: Enter any insights or realizations you had during the reflection.
* **Topics to discuss in our next session (text)**: If applicable, enter any specific topics to discuss in the next session.
* **Current Energy Level (select)**: Rate your current energy level from 1 (Very Low) to 5 (Very High).
