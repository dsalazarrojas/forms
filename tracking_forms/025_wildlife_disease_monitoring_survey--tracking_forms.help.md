# wildlife_disease_monitoring_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on wildlife diseases, their locations, and the organisms affected. It's intended for researchers, conservationists, and fieldworkers to track and monitor wildlife diseases.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form page by page, starting from Page 1.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as you can.
3. If a field is optional, you can skip it if you don't have the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **wildlife_disease_monitoring_survey_page_1_label**: This is the first page of the form. Please select the relevant option for your purpose.
* **wildlife_disease_monitoring_survey_form_group_1**: Choose all the options that apply to your disease monitoring survey.
* **wildlife_disease_monitoring_survey_form_group_2**: Choose the option that best describes your disease monitoring survey.
* **wildlife_disease_monitoring_survey_form_group_3**: Choose the option that best describes your disease monitoring survey.
* **wildlife_disease_monitoring_survey_form_group_4**: Choose all the options that apply to your disease monitoring survey.
* **wildlife_disease_monitoring_survey_photo**: Upload a photo of the affected organisms or the disease symptoms.
* **wildlife_disease_monitoring_survey_photo_details**: Describe the photo you've uploaded.
* **wildlife_disease_monitoring_survey_photo_date**: Enter the date of the photo.
* **wildlife_disease_monitoring_survey_photo_time**: Enter the time of the photo.
* **wildlife_disease_monitoring_survey_location**: Enter the location where you observed the disease.
* **wildlife_disease_monitoring_survey_location_details**: Choose the option that best describes the location.
* **wildlife_disease_monitoring_survey_organism**: Choose the option that best describes the organism affected.
* **wildlife_disease_monitoring_survey_organism_group**: Choose the option that best describes the organism group.
* **wildlife_disease_monitoring_survey_disease**: Choose the option that best describes the disease.
* **wildlife_disease_monitoring_survey_other_details**: Enter any additional details about the disease monitoring survey.
* **wildlife_disease_monitoring_survey_photo_upload**: Upload a photo of the disease symptoms or affected organisms.

## Tips

* Make sure to upload clear and high-quality photos of the disease symptoms or affected organisms.
* Enter as much detail as you can about the disease monitoring survey.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask for help or clarification before proceeding.

Please note that this form is for general guidance and might need to be adapted or modified to fit your specific needs. If you need to make changes or additions, you can modify the form as you see fit.
