# Kart Racing Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kart Racing Registration Form is a simple and straightforward form designed to collect information from participants for a kart racing event. It helps the event organizers to manage registrations and keep track of the participants' details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Racing Event Name** in the designated field. This is the name of the event you're participating in.
2. Enter your **Participant Name** in the next field. Please make sure to provide your full name as it should be spelled on your official documents.
3. Provide your **Email** address. This is how we'll contact you about event updates and any other relevant information.
4. Enter your **Participant Phone** number. This is optional but helps us reach out to you in case of any last-minute changes or cancellations.
5. Select your **Participant Date of Birth (DOB)**. This is a required field to ensure you're eligible to participate in the event.
6. Enter your **Kart Number**. This is the number of your kart, if you have one. If not, just leave this field blank.
7. Select your **Kart Type**. This is the type of kart you'll be using for the event. Select 'Yes' if you have a kart and it's suitable for the event, or 'No' if you don't have a kart or it's not suitable.
8. Enter your **Participant Country**. This is your country of origin or where you're currently residing.
9. Select your **Participant Gender**. This is your personal gender.
10. Choose your **Participant Age Group**. This field allows you to select which age group you're in for the event. Select from 'Junior', 'Senior', or 'Veteran'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Racing Event Name**: The name of the event you're participating in.
* **Participant Name**: Your full name as it should be spelled on official documents.
* **Email**: Your contact email address for event updates and communications.
* **Participant Phone**: Your phone number for last-minute contact.
* **Participant DOB**: Your Date of Birth to verify your eligibility to participate.
* **Kart Number**: The number of your kart, if you have one. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Kart Type**: Select 'Yes' if you have a kart that's suitable for this event, or 'No' if you don't have a kart or it's not suitable.
* **Participant Country**: Your country of origin or current residence.
* **Participant Gender**: Your personal gender.
* **Participant Age Group**: Choose your age group from 'Junior', 'Senior', or 'Veteran'.

Note: Please ensure you're providing accurate and honest information to help us manage the event smoothly. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
