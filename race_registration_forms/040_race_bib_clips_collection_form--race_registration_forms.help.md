# Race Bib Clips Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Race Bib Clips Collection Form is used to collect information about the collection and return of race bib clips. This form is designed to gather details for administrative and logistical purposes, such as tracking event-related data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the race in the "form_title" field.
2. Select the "event_id" field to indicate whether the event has a yes or no status.
3. Enter the "bib_id" field to indicate whether the bib id is present or not.
4. Enter the "return_date" field to select the date of return.
5. Enter the "return_time" field to select the time of return.
6. Provide any relevant notes in the "notes" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
9. Select "yes" or "no" in the "confirm_return" field to confirm the return.
10. Select the "confirm_return_date" field to select the date of confirmation.
11. Select the "confirm_return_time" field to select the time of confirmation.
12. Enter any additional comments or remarks in the "inventory" field.
13. Finally, select "yes" or "no" in the "submit" field to confirm submission.

* **form_title**: Enter the title of the race or event.
