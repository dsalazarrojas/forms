# Swimming Competition Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Swimming Competition Entry Form is a form designed to collect information from swimmers who are registering for a swimming competition. The form is divided into two main categories: swimmer information and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in all the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
2. If you are unsure about any field, please ask the competition organizers for clarification.
3. Make sure to double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Please enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **Swimming Event** (`swimming_event`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the swimming event you are registering for.
* **Swimmer Gender** (`swimmer_gender`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select your swimmer's gender (Male, Female, Other).
* **Height** (`swimmer_height`, number, required/optional): Please enter your swimmer's height in centimeters.
* **Weight** (`swimmer_weight`, number, required/optional): Please enter your swimmer's weight in kilograms.
* **Age** (`swimmer_age`, number, required/optional): Please enter your swimmer's age.
* **Club** (`swimmer_club`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of your swim club.
* **Coach** (`swimmer_coach`, text, required/optional): Please enter your coach's name.
* **Coach Phone** (`swimmer_coach_phone`, text, required/optional): Please enter your coach's phone number.
* **Coach Email** (`swimmer_coach_email`, email, required/optional): Please enter your coach's email address.
* **Notes** (`swimmer_notes`, note, required/optional): Please enter any additional notes or comments about your swimmer.
* **Medical Conditions** (`swimmer_medical_conditions`, note, required/optional): Please enter any medical conditions your swimmer has.
* **Medical Equipment** (`swimmer_medical_equipment`, note, required/optional): Please enter any medical equipment your swimmer uses.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, required/optional): Please enter your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required/optional): Please enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, email, required/optional): Please enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Notes** (`emergency_notes`, note, required/optional): Please enter any additional notes or comments about the emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (`emergency_medical_conditions`, note, required/optional): Please enter any medical conditions of the emergency contact.
* **Medical Equipment** (`emergency_medical_equipment`, note, required/optional): Please enter any medical equipment of the emergency contact.
* **Medical Procedures** (`emergency_medical_procedures`, note, required/optional): Please enter any medical procedures of the emergency contact.
