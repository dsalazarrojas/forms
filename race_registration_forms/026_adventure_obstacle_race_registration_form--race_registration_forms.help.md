# Adventure Obstacle Race Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information, emergency contact details, and waiver and consent information for registration for an obstacle race. The form is divided into sections for participant information, emergency contact information, and race details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the participant information section with your full name, date of birth, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide emergency contact information if necessary. This includes the name, relationship, and phone number of your emergency contact.
3. Select the race you are registering for, including the race name, date, distance, category, and fitness level.
4. Indicate your medical conditions and agree to the waiver and consent terms.
5. Confirm that you understand the registration fee and agree to pay it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your participant's date of birth.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your participant's gender from the available options.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for race communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Race Name** (`race_name`, `text`, required): Select the name of the obstacle race you're registering for.
* **Race Date** (`race_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the race event.
* **Race Distance** (`race_distance`, `select_one`, required): Select the distance option for the race.
* **Race Category** (`race_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category you are registering for.
* **Fitness Level** (`fitness_level`, `select_one`, required): Rate your current fitness level.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have any medical conditions that staff should know about.
* **Waiver and Consent** (`waiver_consent`, `select_one`, required): Agree to the waiver and consent terms.
* **Registration Fee Payment** (`registration_fee_payment`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand the registration fee and agree to pay it.
