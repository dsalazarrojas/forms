# ocean-series-competitor-registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from competitors for the Ocean Series events. It is required for every competitor to complete this form to participate in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the **Competitor Info** page, providing basic information about yourself as a competitor.
2. Proceed to the **Contact Info** page, where you can provide your contact details.
3. On the **Acceptance** page, choose whether you accept or decline the terms and conditions of the event.
4. If you wish to provide more details about yourself, you can do so on the **Participant Details** page. However, this is optional.
5. Finally, on the **Event Details** page, you can provide any additional information about the event you are participating in. This is also optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Competitor Info** (`participant_info`, text, required): Provide your name and any other relevant information about yourself as a competitor.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email.
* **Acceptance** (`waiver_acceptance`, select_one, required): Choose whether you accept or decline the terms and conditions of the event.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): Provide more details about yourself, if you wish. This can be helpful for us to know more about you.
* **Event Details** (`event_info`, text, optional): If you have any information about the event you are participating in, you can provide it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked with \*).
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult the event organizers.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the event organizers.
