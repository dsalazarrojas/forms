# Holiday Run & Cycle Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for the Holiday Run & Cycle event. It collects essential information to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Enter your details in the **Participant Details** section.
2.  Agree to the **Waiver Consent** by selecting "True".
3.  Choose your T-shirt preferences in the **T-shirt Size** and **Shirt Color** sections.
4.  Provide emergency contact information in the **Emergency Contact** section.
5.  If necessary, report any **Medical Conditions** that might affect your participation.
6.  Finally, leave any additional comments in the **Additional Comments** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Participant Details**: This is the main section for you to enter your personal details.
    *   Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details as required.
*   **Waiver Consent**: Please select "True" to agree to the waiver consent.
    *   This is a required field and is necessary for your participation.
*   **T-shirt Size**: Choose your preferred T-shirt size.
    *   Select one of the provided options: Large, X-Large, Small, Medium, or X-Small.
*   **Shirt Color**: Choose the color of your T-shirt.
    *   Select one of the provided options: Blue, Red, Green, or Yellow.
*   **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name, contact number, and relationship of your emergency contact.
    *   This is a required field for your safety and the safety of the event organizers.
*   **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions, please report them here.
    *   This field is optional but highly recommended for your safety and the event organizers.
*   **Additional Comments**: This is an open-ended section for you to add any further information you think is essential.
    *   Use this section to provide any additional details that might be important for us to know.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you fill out all required fields to avoid any issues with your registration.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us using the provided contact information.
