# Isle Of 8 Histourally 2020 Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and medical details from participants of the Isle of 8 Histourally 2020 event.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form with accurate and up-to-date information.
- Complete each field carefully, as this information will be used in case of an emergency.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the event organizers for assistance.

- **Contact Information**: This is the first page of the form. It is required to fill out this page completely.
  * **Contact Information**: This section is used to collect your contact details, which is crucial in case of an emergency.

  * **Last Name**: This field is used to collect your last name.

  * **Emergency Contact Last Name**: This field is used to collect the last name of your emergency contact.
  * **Emergency Contact Phone**: This field is used to collect the phone number of your emergency contact.
  * **Emergency Contact Email**: This field is used to collect the email address of your emergency contact.
  * **Medical Information**: This field is used to collect any pre-existing medical conditions, if applicable.
