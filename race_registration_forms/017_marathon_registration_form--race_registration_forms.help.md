# Marathon Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marathon Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants for the marathon event. It ensures that all participants are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Participant Name field with the name of the participant.
2. Enter the participant's Email address in the Participant Email field.
3. Enter the participant's Phone number in the Participant Phone field.
4. Enter the name of the marathon in the Marathon Name field.
5. Select the Event Date from the calendar.
6. Select the Participant Gender from the dropdown menu.
7. Enter the participant's Age in the Participant Age field.
8. Enter the marathon Location in the Marathon Location field.
9. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can add them in the Participant Notes field.
10. Finally, select the Participant Agreement checkbox to agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the participant.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the participant.
* **Marathon Name** (`marathon_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the marathon event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the event.
* **Participant Gender** (`participant_gender`, select_one, optional): Select the gender of the participant from the dropdown menu.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, optional): Enter the age of the participant.
* **Marathon Location** (`marathon_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the marathon event.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the participant.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, select_multiple, optional): Select the checkbox to agree to the participant agreement terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are having trouble selecting the correct participant agreement, please contact us for assistance.
* Please review the participant agreement carefully before selecting the checkbox.
* This form will only be used for actual participants of the marathon event and not for demonstration or testing purposes.
