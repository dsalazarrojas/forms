# 90 Day Action Plan Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 90 Day Action Plan Intake Form is a tool designed to help clients set and outline their goals and objectives, providing a clear framework for achieving success. This form is meant to be used as an intake tool for coaches, advisors, or other support personnel to guide clients in defining and working towards their goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely**: Answer all the required fields (marked as "true" in the form).
2. **Be specific and clear**: When answering the questions, provide detailed and concise information.
3. **Choose relevant options**: Select the most relevant option(s) for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name**: Please enter your first and last name to identify yourself.
* **Client role / title**: Enter your job title or a brief description of your role.
* **Intake date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Primary 90-day goal**: Describe your most important objective, be as specific as possible and measurable.
* **Goal category**: Choose the most relevant category for your goal.
* **Key milestones**: List key milestones with target dates, e.g., "Complete training by 01/15/2024; Launch pilot by 02/01/2024"
* **Success metrics / KPIs**: Choose the metric(s) to measure progress, e.g., Revenue/sales targets, Time saved/efficiency, etc.
* **Anticipated obstacles or risks**: Identify internal and external barriers that may hinder your progress.
* **Accountability preference**: Choose the frequency for check-ins, e.g., Weekly check-in, Biweekly check-in, etc.
* **Coach or main contact**: Enter the name and contact info of your coach or main contact.
* **Support resources requested**: Choose the support resources you need, e.g., One-on-one coaching, Group workshop, etc.
* **Client additional notes or context**: Enter any additional information or context the client wants to share.

## Tips

* This form is designed to be a starting point for your 90-day action plan.
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Review and revise your answers before submitting the form.
