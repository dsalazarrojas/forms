# Mountain Bike Race Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mountain Bike Race Registration Form is used to register riders for mountain bike races, including collecting their details, emergency contact information, waiver consent, race location, date, time, category, and other relevant details. It is essential to complete this form accurately and securely to ensure a smooth and safe participation in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Rider Details section with your personal information.
2. Select one or more events to participate in (if applicable).
3. Enter emergency contact information for at least one contact.
4. Provide waiver consent.
5. Enter the race location, date, and time.
6. Select your race category.
7. Provide rider license, insurance, and bib number information.
8. Enter any additional race details (if necessary).
9. Enter emergency contact information for any additional contacts (if necessary).
