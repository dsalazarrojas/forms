# Charity Run Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register and provide information for a charity run tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the participant information section, which includes your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and date of birth.
2. Next, select the race event name and date you are registering for.
3. Choose your race distance and participation format (individual or team entry).
4. If you are a team entry, provide your team name.
5. Select your age category and gender.
6. Choose your t-shirt size.
7. If necessary, provide any special needs or medical conditions.
8. Indicate if you will be fundraising for the race and set a personal fundraising goal.
9. Finally, confirm your entry fee and total amount due.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address (For race communications)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for race communications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for race communications.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Race Event Name**: Select the name of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event.
* **Race Distance**: Select the distance of the race you will be participating in.
* **Participation Format**: Select whether you are entering as an individual or part of a team.
* **Team Name**: If you are a team entry, enter your team name.
* **Age Category**: Select your age group.
* **Gender**: Select your gender.
* **T-Shirt Size**: Select the size of t-shirt you would like to wear.
* **Special Needs or Accommodations**: If you have any special needs or medical conditions, please describe them here.
* **Medical Conditions We Should Know About**: If you have any medical conditions, please describe them here.
* **Will You Fundraise for This Race**: Indicate whether you will be collecting pledges or donations.
* **Personal Fundraising Goal**: Set a personal fundraising goal if you will be fundraising.
* **Entry Fee**: Confirm your entry fee.
* **Optional Donation**: If you would like to make an additional contribution to the charity, enter the amount here.
* **Total Amount Due**: Confirm the total amount due, including the entry fee and any optional donation.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled out to complete the registration process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* We appreciate your participation in this charity run tournament!
