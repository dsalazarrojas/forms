# Tournament Participant Bib Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and preferences for bib printing in a tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Complete the Participant Information section by providing your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. In the Bib Printing Requirements section, specify the type of printing you require, such as color or black and white.
3. In the Printing Details section, provide any additional details about your printing requirements, such as font sizes or special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (Participant Information)**: Provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself as a participant.
* **Bib Printing Requirements (Bib Printing Requirements)**: Specify the type of printing you require for your bib, such as color or black and white.
* **Printing Details (Printing Details)**: Provide any additional details about your printing requirements, such as font sizes or special requests.
