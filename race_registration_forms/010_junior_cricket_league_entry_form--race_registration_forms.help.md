# Junior Cricket League Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Junior Cricket League Entry Form is a tool to collect essential information from teams participating in the Junior Cricket League. This form is designed to be completed in multiple pages, each focusing on a specific aspect of team details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the instructions on each page carefully. Start by selecting the correct options for each field, taking note of the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) in the form title. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, `text`, required): Enter a unique category for the team to be displayed in the league.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the team, including their goals, objectives, and any notable achievements.
* **Category 1** (`category_1`, `text`, required): Enter the first category relevant to the team.
* **Category 2** (`category_2`, `text`, required): Enter the second category relevant to the team.
* **Category 3** (`category_3`, `text`, required): Enter the third category relevant to the team.
* **Category 4** (`category_4`, `text`, required): Enter the fourth category relevant to the team.
* **Category 5** (`category_5`, `text`, required): Enter the fifth category relevant to the team.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required): Select the tool assigned to the team.
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`, `number`, required): Enter the unique form ID assigned to the team.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Review each page before proceeding to the next to avoid errors.
