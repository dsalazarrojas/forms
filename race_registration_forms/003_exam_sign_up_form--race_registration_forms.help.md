# Exam Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Exam Sign Up Form is a form designed to collect information about upcoming exams. This form is intended to be filled out by individuals who want to sign up for an exam. It is used for internal purposes within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the text field provided.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the format `your_email@example.com`.
4. Select one of the available **Exam Name** options from the dropdown menu. This will indicate if you are interested in signing up for the exam or not.
5. Choose a valid **Start Date** for the exam, following the standard date format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
6. Select a valid **End Date** for the exam, following the standard date format `mm/dd/yyyy`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your official email address where you can be reached.
* **Exam Name** (Select One): Choose if you are interested in signing up for the exam. Options: Yes / No.
* **Start Date** (Date): Choose the start date of the exam in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **End Date** (Date): Choose the end date of the exam in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double check your email address for correct formatting.
* Ensure you select a valid start and end date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the exam administrators.

Note: The exam administrators will use the information provided to finalize your exam sign-up.
