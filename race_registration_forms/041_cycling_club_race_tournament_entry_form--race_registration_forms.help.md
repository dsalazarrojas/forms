# Cycling Club Race Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for the Cycling Club Race Tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your participant email address in the "Participant Email" field.
3. Enter your participant phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
4. Select your preferred category for the tournament in the "Category" field.
5. Select the type of bike you will be using in the "Bike" field.
6. Indicate if you will be racing alone or with a team in the "Team Members" field.
7. Choose the day of the race you wish to participate in from the "Race Day" field.
8. Select your preferred start time for the race in the "Start Time" field.
9. Choose your expected finish time for the race in the "Finish Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Race Registration Form** (`race_registration_form_1`): This field is a free-text field for any additional information you want to provide about your registration.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`): Enter your personal email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Category** (`category_select_one`): Select the type of category you wish to participate in (e.g., Cycling, Mountain).
* **Bike** (`bike_select_multiple`): Select the type of bike you will be using (e.g., Mountain, Road, Hybrid).
* **Team Members** (`team_members_select_one`): Indicate if you will be racing alone or with a team (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
* **Race Day** (`race_day_select_one`): Choose the day of the race you wish to participate in (e.g., Sat, Sun, Mon, etc.).
* **Start Time** (`start_time_select_one`): Select your preferred start time for the race (e.g., 6:00-8:00, 8:00-10:00, etc.).
* **Finish Time** (`finish_time_select_one`): Choose your expected finish time for the race (e.g., 8:00-10:00, 10:00-12:00, etc.).

Note: All fields are optional except for **Participant Name** and **Participant Email**.
