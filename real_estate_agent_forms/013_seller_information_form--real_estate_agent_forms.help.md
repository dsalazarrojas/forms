# Seller Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from a seller about their real estate property, which will be used for marketing or administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. Please ensure it's a valid email address.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the type of property you are selling (Residential, Commercial, or Industrial) in the "Type" field.
6. Indicate if you are **Reason to Sell** (Yes or No) in the "Reason to Sell" field.
7. Check if you are **Best Time** (Yes or No) in the "Best Time" field.
8. Indicate if your property is **Soon to Sell** (Yes or No) in the "Soon to Sell" field.
9. Select the **Move Out Timeframe** (Yes or No) in the "Move Out Timeframe" field.
10. Enter the date you plan to **Move Out** in the "Move Out Date" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email**: A valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Your contact phone number.
* **Type**: The type of property you are selling, e.g., residential, commercial, or industrial.
* **Reason to Sell**: Indicate if you are currently selling your property (Yes or No).
* **Best Time**: Check if you are the best time to contact you (Yes or No).
* **Soon to Sell**: Indicate if you are selling your property soon (Yes or No).
* **Move Out Timeframe**: Select if you have a specific timeframe for moving out (Yes or No).
* **Move Out Date**: Enter the date you plan to move out, if applicable.
