# Rent Schedule Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Rent Schedule Form is a tool used to collect information about a renter's schedule, including their name, contact information, and lease dates. This form is used to gather relevant data for rent management and accounting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form with the required information.
2. Enter the renter's name in the "renter_name" field.
3. Provide the renter's contact information in the "renter_contact" field.
4. Enter the start date of the rent in the "rent_start_date" field.
5. Enter the end date of the rent in the "rent_end_date" field.
6. Enter the total rent amount in the "rent_amount" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rent Schedule Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **renter_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the renter.
* **renter_contact** (text, optional): Enter the contact information of the renter, including phone number and email.
* **rent_start_date** (date, optional): Enter the start date of the rent.
* **rent_end_date** (date, optional): Enter the end date of the rent.
* **rent_amount** (number, optional): Enter the total rent amount.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate data collection.
* Double-check the form for any errors before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with a supervisor or seek additional guidance.
