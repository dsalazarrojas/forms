# Real Estate Listing Boost Campaign Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for real estate agents to register for the Real Estate Listing Boost Campaign, a program designed to enhance their listings. Please fill out the form carefully, as it's intended to understand your commitment and needs for the campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the following steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer every question truthfully.
2. Fill out each section according to your real estate business needs and goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Real Estate Listing Boost Campaign Registration** (`id: 1`): This is the title of your registration.
* **Participants Commitment** (`id: 2`, optional): If applicable, enter the number of your real estate participants (agents, brokers, etc.).
* **Current Listings** (`id: 3`, multiple choices): Select the current listings you'd like to enhance.
* **Target Listings** (`id: 4`, single choice): Choose the listings you'd like to target for the campaign.
* **Agreement To Campaign Rules** (`id: 5`, single choice): Accept or decline the campaign rules, which might include terms and conditions.
* **Time-Limited Listing Incentive** (`id: 6`, date): If you're eligible for a time-limited listing incentive, enter the date you're eligible to start.
* **Listing Incentive Duration** (`id: 7`, single choice): Select the duration of the listing incentive you'd like to apply for.
* **Participant Agreement** (`id: 8`, single choice): Enter your agreement to participate in the campaign.
* **Listing Agreement Details** (`id: 9`, text): Provide any additional information or details related to your listings and campaign participation.

## Tips
* Ensure you answer every question truthfully and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the campaign administrators for clarification.
* Review the campaign rules and terms before agreeing to participate.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
