# Property Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Survey form is designed to collect information about a property, including its address, type, boundaries, and features, for use in real estate activities such as listings or market research.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the property's address in the "Property Address" field.
2. Select the property type from the "Property Type" dropdown menu.
3. Describe the property's boundaries in the "Boundaries" field.
4. List any structures on the property in the "Structures" field.
5. Mention any charges or fees related to the property in the "Charges" field.
6. Describe any guarantees or warranties for the property in the "Guarantees" field.
7. List any notable features of the property in the "Property Features" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Property Address** (text, required: false): Enter the property's address, including street number and name, city, and state.
- **Property Type** (select_one, required: false): Select the type of property, such as Land, Building, or Other.
- **Boundaries** (text, required: false): Describe the property's boundaries, such as acreage or lot size.
- **Structures** (text, required: false): List any structures on the property, including buildings, bridges, or other permanent fixtures.
- **Charges** (text, required: false): Mention any charges or fees associated with the property, such as taxes or maintenance fees.
- **Guarantees** (text, required: false): Describe any guarantees or warranties for the property, such as manufacturer warranties or builder guarantees.
- **Property Features** (text, required: false): List any notable features of the property, such as pool, view, or high-speed internet.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to provide complete and correct information about the property.
