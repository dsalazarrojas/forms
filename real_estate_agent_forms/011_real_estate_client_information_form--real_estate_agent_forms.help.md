# real_estate_client_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information from real estate agents to facilitate efficient communication and collaboration with the client. This information will be used to initiate the client relationship and to ensure that all necessary details are captured in a single session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select the type of property you are working with from the Property Type field.
6. Enter the location of the property in the Property Location field.
7. Enter the price range of the property in the Property Price Range field.
8. Choose your Preferred Language from the drop-down menu.
9. Add any Additional Comments in the Additional Comments field (optional).
10. Specify any Special Requests you may have (optional).
11. Define your relationship with the emergency contact in the Emergency Contact Relationship field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it should be displayed in our communication.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it should be displayed in our communication.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Please enter the email address you want to use for communication.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): If you have a preferred phone number, please enter it here.
* **Property Type** (`Property Type`, text, optional): Select the type of property you are working with (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.).
* **Property Location** (`Property Location`, text, required): Enter the location of the property (e.g., city, state, or country).
* **Property Price Range** (`Property Price Range`, number, required): Enter the price range of the property.
* **Preferred Language** (`Preferred Language`, select_one, optional): Choose the language you prefer to communicate with us in (e.g., English, Spanish, French, etc.).
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or information, please enter them here.
* **Special Requests** (`Special Requests`, text, optional): Specify any special requests you may have.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`Emergency Contact Relationship`, select_multiple, optional): Define your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., Primary, Secondary).

**Tips:**

* Ensure that your First Name, Last Name, and Email fields are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any special requests or comments, please use the Additional Comments field.
* If you have any special requests, please specify them in the Special Requests field.
* If you have an emergency contact, please define your relationship with them accurately.
