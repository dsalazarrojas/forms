# Estate Planning Client Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a guide to help clients provide essential information about themselves and their relationships for estate planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Estate Planning Client Questionnaire, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have the necessary information and documents available before starting to fill out the form.
* Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
* Double-check your answers to ensure that they are complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false)
* Enter your first name to help us understand your personal details.

### **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false)
* Enter your last name to help us understand your identity.

### **Street address** (`street_address`, text, required: false)
* Enter your current street address to help us locate you.

### **City** (`city`, text, required: false)
* Enter the city where you reside to help us understand your location.

### **State** (`state`, text, required: false)
* Enter the state where you currently reside to help us understand your location.

### **Zip code** (`zip_code`, number, required: false)
* Enter your current zip code to help us locate you.

### **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required: false)
* Enter your current email address to help us contact you.

### **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false)
* Enter your current phone number to help us contact you.

### **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required: false)
* Enter the name of the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.

### **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required: false)
* Enter the phone number of the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.

### **Next of kin name** (`next_of_kin_name`, text, required: false)
* Enter the name of the person you would like us to contact in the event of your incapacitation.

### **Next of kin phone** (`next_of_kin_phone`, text, required: false)
* Enter the phone number of the person you would like us to contact in the event of your incapacitation.

### **Estate manager relationship** (`estate_manager_relationship`, text, required: false)
* Describe your relationship with the estate manager.

### **Estate manager address** (`estate_manager_address`, text, required: false)
* Enter the address of the person who manages your estate.

### **Estate manager phone** (`estate_manager_phone`, text, required: false)
* Enter the phone number of the person who manages your estate.

### **Trustee relationship** (`trustee_relationship`, text, required: false)
* Describe your relationship with the trustee.

### **Trustee name** (`trusteed_name`, text, required: false)
* Enter the name of the person you have appointed as the trustee.

### **Trustee address** (`trusteed_address`, text, required: false)
* Enter the address of the person you have appointed as the trustee.

### **Trustee phone** (`trusteed_phone`, text, required: false)
* Enter the phone number of the person you have appointed as the trustee.

Please note that this form is for informational purposes only and is not a comprehensive document for estate planning. It is essential to review and discuss your estate planning needs with a qualified professional.
