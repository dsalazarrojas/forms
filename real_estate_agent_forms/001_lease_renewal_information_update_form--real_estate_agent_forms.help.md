# Lease Renewal Information Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to update lease renewal information for properties. It's used to collect updates on the lease end date, lease start date, lease length, rent frequency, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for lease renewal related questions.
2. Enter the date for lease start and end dates.
3. Enter the lease length in the required format (e.g., 12 months, 24 months, etc.).
4. Enter the rent frequency and amount for the relevant lease.
5. Select the renewal status and lease status for the relevant lease.
6. Enter the contact information for the contact person, landlord, and tenant.
7. Enter the property name and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lease Renewal** (Select Multiple, id: 1): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the lease is being renewed.
* **Lease End Date** (Date, id: 2): Enter the date when the lease will end.
* **Lease Start Date** (Date, id: 3): Enter the date when the lease will start.
* **Lease Length** (Number, id: 4): Enter the length of the lease in the required format.
* **Rent Frequency** (Select One, id: 5): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the rent is paid frequently.
* **Rent Amount** (Number, id: 6): Enter the rent amount.
* **Lease Status** (Select Multiple, id: 7): Select the status of the lease (Active or Inactive).
* **Contact Person** (Text, id: 8): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, id: 9): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (Email, id: 10): Enter the contact person's email.
* **Landlord Name** (Text, id: 11): Enter the name of the landlord.
* **Landlord Phone** (Text, id: 12): Enter the landlord's phone number.
* **Landlord Email** (Email, id: 13): Enter the landlord's email.
* **Tenant Name** (Text, id: 14): Enter the name of the tenant.
* **Tenant Phone** (Text, id: 15): Enter the tenant's phone number.
* **Tenant Email** (Email, id: 16): Enter the tenant's email.
* **Property Name** (Text, id: 17): Enter the name of the property.
* **Property Address** (Text, id: 18): Enter the address of the property.
* **Lease Number** (Text, id: 19): Enter the lease number.
* **Renewal Date** (Date, id: 20): Enter the date when the lease will be renewed.
* **Renewal Status** (Select One, id: 21): Select the status of the renewal (Active or Inactive).
* **Reason for Renewal** (Select Multiple, id: 22): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the reason for renewal.
* **Lease Status 2** (Select Multiple, id: 23): Select the status of the lease (Active or Inactive).
* **Rent Frequency 2** (Select One, id: 24): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the rent is paid frequently.
* **Rent Amount 2** (Number, id: 25): Enter the rent amount for the second lease.
