# Rental Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Review Form is a tool used to collect feedback and reviews from applicants about their rental experience. This form helps property managers or landlords understand how their properties or services are perceived by applicants, allowing them to identify areas for improvement and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name and email.
2. Enter your rental address to help us understand which property you are reviewing.
3. Share your thoughts and opinions about your rental experience in the review section.
4. If you have any comments or suggestions, please provide them in the comments field.
5. Lastly, rate your overall experience with the rental property. You can choose a number from 1 to 5 to indicate your level of satisfaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information (1)**: Enter your name and any contact information you feel comfortable sharing.
* **Rental Address (2)**: Type in the address of the rental property you are reviewing.
* **Applicant Name (3)**: Enter the name of the person who is reviewing the property.
* **Phone Number (4)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Email (5)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your review.
* **Review (6)**: Share your thoughts about your rental experience, including your satisfaction and any areas for improvement.
* **Rating (7)**: Choose a number from 1 to 5 to indicate your level of satisfaction with the rental property.
* **Comments (8)**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the rental property.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide detailed feedback about your experience.
* Keep your review concise and clear.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please let us know in the comments section.
