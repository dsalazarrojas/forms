# Landlord Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Landlord Permission Form" is a document used by landlords or property managers to capture essential information about tenants and their pets in a rental property. This form is crucial for maintaining accurate records and ensuring that all necessary details are captured.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Tenant** field with the name of the person or entity leasing the rental unit.
2. If you have information regarding the tenant's pet(s), enter it in the **Pet Info** field. This is an optional field, so feel free to skip it if not applicable.
3. For the **Pet Type**, select the correct type from the options: Cat, Dog, or Other. This is a required field.
4. Enter the total number of pets living in the property in the **Pet Count** field. This field is also required.
5. Provide the **Rental Unit** details in the provided field. This field is required and could be the unit's address, number, or any other unique identifier.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant (Required)**: Enter the name of the person or entity leasing the rental unit.
* **Pet Info (Optional)**: Provide any additional details about the tenant's pet(s).
* **Pet Type (Required)**: Choose from three options: Cat, Dog, or Other.
* **Pet Count (Required)**: Enter the total number of pets living in the property.
* **Rental Unit (Required)**: Enter the details or identifier of the rental unit, such as its address or number.
