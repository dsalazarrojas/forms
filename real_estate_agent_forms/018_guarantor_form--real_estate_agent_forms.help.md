# Guarantor Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guarantor Form is used to collect information about a guarantor's background and employment details in real estate transactions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in the following pages:
	* Guarantor Form

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guarantor Form** (`guarantor_form`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the guarantor.
* **Guarantor** (`guarantor`, text, required): Enter the name of the guarantor.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the loan.
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, time, required): Enter the loan term.
* **Guarantor Income** (`guarantor_income`, text, required): Enter the guarantor's income.
* **Loan Purpose** (`loan_purpose`, text, required): Enter the purpose of the loan.
* **Guarantor Relationship** (`guarantor_relationship`, text, required): Enter the relationship between the guarantor and the borrower.
* **Guarantor Address** (`guarantor_address`, text, required): Enter the guarantor's address.
* **Guarantor Income Source** (`guarantor_income_source`, text, required): Enter the source of the guarantor's income.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Select the guarantor's employment status.
	+ Options: Employed, Self Employed, Unemployed
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the guarantor's employment type.
	+ Options: Full-Time, Part-time, Freelance
* **Employment Income** (`employment_income`, number, required): Enter the guarantor's employment income.
* **Employment Length** (`employment_length`, select_one, required): Select the length of the guarantor's employment.
	+ Options: Less than 1 year, 1-5 years, 5-10 years, 10+ years
* **Employment Position** (`employment_position`, text, required): Enter the guarantor's employment position.
* **Employment Income Source** (`employment_income_source`, text, required): Enter the source of the guarantor's employment income.
* **Guarantor Employer** (`guarantor_employer`, text, required): Enter the guarantor's employer.
* **Guarantor Job Title** (`guarantor_job_title`, text, required): Enter the guarantor's job title.
* **Guarantor Job Description** (`guarantor_job_description`, text, required): Enter a description of the guarantor's job.
* **Guarantor Years with Employer** (`guarantor_years_with_employer`, number, required): Enter the number of years the guarantor has worked with their current employer.
* **Guarantor Annual Income** (`guarantor_annual_income`, number, required): Enter the guarantor's annual income.
* **Guarantor Annual Income Source** (`guarantor_annual_income_source`, text, required): Enter the source of the guarantor's annual income.
* **Guarantor Property Type** (`guarantor_property_type`, text, required): Enter the type of property owned by the guarantor.
* **Guarantor Property Location** (`guarantor_property_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the guarantor's property.
* **Guarantor Property Size** (`guarantor_property_size`, text, required): Enter the size of the guarantor's property.
* **Guarantor Other Financial Assets** (`guarantor_other_financial_assets`, text, required): Enter any other financial assets owned by the guarantor.

## Tips
* Please enter accurate and truthful information for the guarantor.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the guarantor or seek further guidance.
