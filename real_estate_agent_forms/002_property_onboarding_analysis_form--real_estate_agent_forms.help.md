# property-onboarding-analysis-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about a property to assist in its onboarding process. It gathers data on the property's current status, condition, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Property Information section with the property's basic details.
2. In the Property Owner section, provide the owner's identification number.
3. The Property Address section requires the property's address.
4. Select the current property status from the dropdown menu.
5. For the Property Condition section, choose the best description of the property's current state.
6. Enter the property's features and amenities in the Property Features section.
7. In the Property Notes section, add any additional comments or information about the property.
8. Provide the property owner's email address in the Property Email field.
9. Enter the property owner's phone number.
10. Input the date the property was acquired and the date it was sold.
11. If applicable, upload photos of the property in the Property Photos section.
12. If available, provide a video tour of the property.
13. Describe the property's physical condition in the Property Physical Condition section.
14. Enter the property's legal details in the Property Legal section.
15. Select the current property occupancy status.
16. In the Property Management section, provide information on the property's current management.
17. If applicable, input the results of any inspections in the Property Inspections section.
18. Enter the property's financial details in the Property Financials section.
19. Select whether or not there are tenants in the property in the Property Tenants section.
20. In the Property Rent Schedule section, enter the rent schedule details.
21. In the Property Repair Schedule section, enter any repair schedule details.
22. In the Property Maintenance Schedule section, enter any maintenance schedule details.
23. Select the status of the property's maintenance in the Property Maintenance Status section.
24. Enter any reviews or feedback about the property in the Property Reviews section.
25. In the Property Photos Count section, enter the number of photos uploaded.
26. In the Property Video Count section, enter the number of videos uploaded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Information**: Enter the property's basic details, such as its name, location, and identification number.
* **Property Owner**: Enter the owner's identification number.
* **Property Address**: Enter the property's address.
* **Status**: Select the current status of the property from the dropdown menu.
* **Condition**: Choose the best description of the property's current state.
* **Features**: Enter the property's features and amenities.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about the property.
* **Email**: Enter the property owner's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the property owner's phone number.
* **Date Acquired**: Input the date the property was acquired.
* **Date Sold**: Input the date the property was sold.
* **Photos**: Upload photos of the property.
* **Video Tour**: Provide a video tour of the property.
* **Physical Condition**: Describe the property's physical condition.
* **Legal**: Enter the property's legal details.
* **Occupancy**: Select the current property occupancy status.
* **Management**: Provide information on the property's current management.
* **Inspections**: If applicable, input the results of any inspections.
* **Financials**: Enter the property's financial details.
* **Tenants**: Select whether or not there are tenants in the property.
* **Rent Schedule**: Enter the rent schedule details.
* **Repair Schedule**: Enter any repair schedule details.
* **Maintenance Schedule**: Enter any maintenance schedule details.
* **Maintenance Status**: Select the status of the property's maintenance.
* **Reviews**: Enter any reviews or feedback about the property.
* **Photos Count**: Enter the number of photos uploaded.
* **Video Count**: Enter the number of videos uploaded.
