# COVID 19 Real Estate Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from real estate agents and their clients about their COVID-19 related safety protocols and liabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the disclosure date with the date of this form.
2. Enter your licensed real estate agent's full name in the "Real Estate Agent Full Name" field.
3. Enter your real estate agent's state license number in the "Agent License Number" field.
4. Enter your brokerage company's name in the "Brokerage Company Name" field.
5. Enter your brokerage company's address in the "Brokerage Address" field.
6. Enter your brokerage company's phone number in the "Brokerage Phone" field.
7. Enter your brokerage company's email address in the "Brokerage Email" field.
8. Enter the client's full name in the "Client Full Name" field.
9. Enter the client's phone number in the "Client Phone" field.
10. Enter the client's email address in the "Client Email" field.
11. Enter the address of the property in the "Property Address" field.
12. Select the type of property in the "Property Type" field.
13. Select the type of transaction in the "Transaction Type" field.
14. Answer "Yes I acknowledge this risk" in the "I acknowledge that in-person meetings and property showings may expose me to COVID-19" field.
15. Answer "No I am not experiencing symptoms" in the "To the best of your knowledge are you currently experiencing COVID-19 symptoms" field.
16. Select "False" in the "Have you tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 14 days" field.
17. Select "False" in the "Have you had close contact with anyone who tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 14 days" field.
18. Select "Yes I agree" in the "I agree to follow all COVID-19 safety precautions during in-person meetings" field.
19. Select all applicable safety precautions in the "Which safety precautions will you follow" field.
20. Select "Yes whenever possible" in the "Would you prefer virtual meetings and tours when available" field.
21. Select "Yes I release liability" in the "I release and hold harmless the real estate agent and brokerage from any COVID-19 related claims arising from in-person meetings" field.
22. If applicable, enter the name of the witness in the "Witness Name" field.
23. If applicable, date the "Date witnessed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Disclosure Date (id: 1)** (`Disclosure Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of this disclosure.
* **Real Estate Agent Full Name (id: 2)** (`agent_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your licensed real estate agent's full name.
* **Agent License Number (id: 3)** (`agent_license_number`, `text`, required): Enter your real estate agent's state license number.
* **Brokerage Company Name (id: 4)** (`brokerage_name`, `text`, required): Enter your brokerage company's name.
* **Brokerage Address (id: 5)** (`brokerage_address`, `text`, required): Enter your brokerage company's address.
* **Brokerage Phone (id: 6)** (`brokerage_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your brokerage company's phone number.
* **Brokerage Email (id: 7)** (`brokerage_email`, `email`, required): Enter your brokerage company's email address.
* **Client Full Name (id: 8)** (`client_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter the client's full name.
* **Client Phone (id: 9)** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Email (id: 10)** (`client_email`, `email`, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Property Address (id: 11)** (`property_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the property.
* **Property Type (id: 12)** (`property_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of property (Single family home, Condominium, Townhouse, Multi-family, Commercial, Land, Other).
* **Transaction Type (id: 13)** (`transaction_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of transaction (Purchase, Sale, Rental, Lease, Property management).
* **I acknowledge that in-person meetings and property showings may expose me to COVID-19 (id: 14)** (`covid_risk_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes I acknowledge this risk".
* **To the best of your knowledge are you currently experiencing COVID-19 symptoms (id: 15)** (`health_status_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Select "No I am not experiencing symptoms".
* **Have you tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 14 days (id: 16)** (`recent_positive_test`, `select_one`, required): Select "False".
* **Have you had close contact with anyone who tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 14 days (id: 17)** (`close_contact_exposure`, `select_one`, required): Select "False".
* **I agree to follow all COVID-19 safety precautions during in-person meetings (id: 18)** (`safety_precautions_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes I agree".
* **Which safety precautions will you follow (id: 19)** (`safety_precautions_list`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable safety precautions.
* **Would you prefer virtual meetings and tours when available (id: 20)** (`virtual_option_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes whenever possible".
* **I release and hold harmless the real estate agent and brokerage from any COVID-19 related claims arising from in-person meetings (id: 21)** (`liability_release`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes I release liability".
* **Witness Name (id: 22)** (`witness_name`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the witness.
* **Date witnessed (id: 23)** (`witness_signature_date`, `date`, optional): If applicable, date the "Date witnessed" field.
* **Date of Acknowledgment (id: 24)** (`client_signature_date`, `date`, required): Date this acknowledgment.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Take necessary safety precautions before and during in-person meetings.
