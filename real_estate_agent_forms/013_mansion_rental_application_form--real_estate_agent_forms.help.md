# Mansion Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mansion Rental Application Form is a crucial document that helps us understand your interest in renting a mansion. By filling out this form, you will be able to express your requirements and provide necessary information about your rental needs. Please note that providing accurate and detailed information increases the chances of your application being processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **form_data** (page 1, question 1). This field is not required to fill but can provide additional context about your application.
2. Fill out your **name** (page 1, question 2). Provide your full name.
3. Enter your **email** (page 1, question 3). Make sure to enter a valid email address.
4. Select a **start_date** (page 1, question 4) and **end_date** (page 1, question 5) for the desired rental period.
5. Enter your **address** (page 1, question 6). This should be the address where you will be staying during the rental period.
6. Fill out your **phone_number** (page 1, question 7). Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
7. Enter the **rent_price** (page 1, question 8). Specify the price you are willing to pay for the rental.
8. Select the **rent_status** (page 1, question 9) that applies to you:
	* Active: You are willing to pay and rent the mansion.
	* Inactive: You are not willing to pay and rent the mansion.
9. If you select **Inactive**, you may provide additional information about your **rent_status_other** (page 1, question 10).
10. Select whether you are willing to **reason_for_renting** (page 1, question 11):
	* Yes: You are willing to rent the mansion for a reason.
	* No: You are not willing to rent the mansion.
11. If you select **Yes**, you may provide additional information about your reasoning in the **renter_info** (page 1, question 12) field.
12. If you are a representative of a landlord, you may provide additional information about yourself in the **landlord_info** (page 1, question 13) field.
13. Finally, you may provide additional information or context in the **references** (page 1, question 14) field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_data**: This field is not required to fill but can provide additional context about your application.
* **name**: Your full name.
* **email**: A valid email address.
* **start_date**: The date you wish to start renting the mansion.
* **end_date**: The date you wish to end renting the mansion.
* **address**: The address where you will be staying during the rental period.
* **phone_number**: Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **rent_price**: The price you are willing to pay for the rental.
* **rent_status**: 
	+ Active: You are willing to pay and rent the mansion.
	+ Inactive: You are not willing to pay and rent the mansion.
* **rent_status_other**: If you select Inactive, you may provide additional information about your reasons.
* **reason_for_renting**: 
	+ Yes: You are willing to rent the mansion for a reason.
	+ No: You are not willing to rent the mansion.
* **renter_info**: If you select Yes, you may provide additional information about your reasoning.
* **landlord_info**: If you are a representative of a landlord, you may provide additional information about yourself.
* **references**: You may provide additional information or context about your application.

## Tips

* Ensure you fill out all required fields.
* Enter accurate and detailed information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* Review your application before submission to ensure it is complete and accurate.
