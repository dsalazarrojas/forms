## Vineyard Estate Rental Application Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
The Vineyard Estate Rental Application Form is designed to collect information from potential renters who wish to rent a property at our vineyard estate. This form helps us understand your needs and preferences for the rental period, allowing us to provide you with a tailored experience.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each field. Please note that some fields are optional, so feel free to skip them if you're not providing that information. If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rental Application** (`rental_application`, text, optional):
	+ This is the main section of the form where you can provide any additional information about your rental request.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional):
	+ Please provide your contact details so we can get in touch with you regarding your rental application.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional):
	+ If you're planning an event, describe the purpose and nature of the event.
* **Rental Period** (`rental_period`, text, optional):
	+ Specify the duration of your rental period.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any information about your payment method and schedule.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select one or multiple locations for your event.
	+ Options: Location 1, Location 2, Location 3
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of your event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time of your event.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the number of attendees for your event.
	+ Options: 10, 20, 50, 100
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the nature and purpose of your event.
* **Renter Info** (`renter_info`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose whether you're a individual or a group.
	+ Options: Individual, Group
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the address of the property you're interested in renting.
* **Property City** (`property_city`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the city of the property you're interested in renting.
* **Property State** (`property_state`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the state of the property you're interested in renting.
* **Property Zip** (`property_zip`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the zip code of the property you're interested in renting.
* **Event Notes** (`event_notes`, text, optional):
	+ Add any additional notes about your event.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the payment amount for your rental.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose your payment method.
	+ Options: Cash, Credit Card, Other
* **Payment Due Date** (`payment_due_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the payment due date.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the status of your payment.
	+ Options: Pending, Paid, Declined
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional):
	+ Add any additional notes about your rental application.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the person assigned to handle your rental application.

### Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask.
* We appreciate your honesty and accuracy when filling in the form.
* Once you've completed the form, please review it carefully before submission.
