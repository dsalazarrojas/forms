# Real Estate Contract Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Contract Release Form is used to formally release real estate contracts when a buyer or seller wishes to cancel their agreement. This form ensures that all necessary information is provided and signed by both parties involved in the contract.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the designated fields.
2. Enter your Email address if it is required.
3. Enter your Phone number if it is required.
4. Enter the Name of the Real Estate Agent who has been handling your contract.
5. Enter the Contract Date of the original contract.
6. Sign the Agent Signature field to confirm that you are the real estate agent.
7. Sign the Buyer Signature field to confirm that you are the buyer.
8. Enter the Date Signed if it is required.
9. Provide a Reason for Removation, if applicable.
10. Enter the Name of the Buyer who is canceling the contract.
11. Enter the Address of the Buyer.
12. Enter the Buyer's Phone number.
13. Enter the Buyer's Email address.
14. Sign the Buyer Signature field to confirm that you are the buyer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address if it is required. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if it is required. Please use the international format (e.g., +1-1234567890).
* **Agent Name**: Enter the name of the real estate agent who has been handling your contract.
* **Contract Date**: Enter the date of the original contract.
* **Agent Signature**: Sign this field to confirm that you are the real estate agent.
* **Buyer Name**: Enter the name of the buyer who is canceling the contract.
* **Buyer Address**: Enter the address of the buyer.
* **Buyer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the buyer.
* **Buyer Email**: Enter the contact email address of the buyer.
* **Buyer Signature**: Sign this field to confirm that you are the buyer.
* **Reason for Cancellation**: Provide a brief explanation for canceling the contract (if applicable).
* **Date Signed**: If applicable, enter the date you signed this form.
* **Reason for Removation**: If you are canceling the contract due to circumstances, enter a brief explanation.
