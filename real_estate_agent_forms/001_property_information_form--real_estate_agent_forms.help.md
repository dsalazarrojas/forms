# Property Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and record property information for real estate agents. Complete this form for properties that are currently active or under sale.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with the required information.
2. Make sure to select the correct property type (Residential, Commercial, or Industrial) and status (Active, Sold, or Leased).
3. Enter accurate date and sale price of the property.
4. Fill out the agent's name and phone number for easy contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Street Address** (property_address): Enter the property's street address.
*   **City** (property_city): Enter the city where the property is located.
*   **State** (property_state): Select the state where the property is located.
*   **Zip** (property_zip): Enter the property's zip code.
*   **Type** (property_type): Select the type of property (Residential, Commercial, or Industrial).
*   **Status** (property_status): Select the status of the property (Active, Sold, or Leased).
*   **Date of Sale** (property_date_of_sale): Enter the date the property was sold or leased.
*   **Sale Price** (property_sale_price): Enter the sale price of the property.
*   **Agent** (property_agent): Enter the agent's name.
*   **Agent Phone** (property_agent_phone): Enter the agent's phone number.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
*   Check for any errors before submitting the form.
*   If you need to make changes, contact your real estate agent or administrator for assistance.
