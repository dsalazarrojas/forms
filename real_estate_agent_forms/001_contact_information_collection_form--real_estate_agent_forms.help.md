# Contact Information Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect contact information from potential customers who are interested in buying, selling, or renting properties. This form helps real estate agents understand the customer's preferences, budget, and time frame for the property search, allowing them to provide a personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** in the respective fields. Make sure to use a valid email address for communication.
2. Select your **Preferred Contact Method** from the list provided (Email, Phone Call, Text Message, or No Preference).
3. Select your **Primary Reason for Inquiry** from the list provided (Buy a Home, Sell a Home, Rent a Property, Investment Opportunity, or General Inquiry).
4. Choose all that apply for **Preferred Property Type** (Single Family Home, Condo, Townhouse, Multi-Family, Land, or Commercial).
5. Select your **Estimated Budget Range** (Under $250k, $250k - $500k, $500k - $750k, $750k - $1M, or $1M+).
6. Choose when you are looking to **Move** (Immediately, Within 3 Months, 3-6 Months, 6-12 Months, or Just Browsing).
7. If applicable, provide any **Additional Comments** about your property search.
8. Decide if you would like to **Subscribe to our monthly real estate market updates** (Yes please or No thank you).
9. Finally, let us know how you **Heard about us** (Social Media, Google Search, Referral, Signage, or Online Advertisement).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your given name.
* **Last Name**: Your surname.
* **Email Address**: The email address you would like us to use for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact number.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: How you would like us to communicate with you (Email, Phone Call, Text Message, or No Preference).
* **Primary Reason for Inquiry**: The main reason for your property search.
* **Preferred Property Type**: The type of property you are looking for (e.g., Single Family Home, Condo, etc.).
* **Estimated Budget Range**: Your estimated budget for the property.
* **When are you looking to Move**: When you plan to move into your new property.
* **Any additional comments**: Any specific neighborhoods or features you are looking for.
* **Subscribe to our monthly real estate market updates**: Whether you would like to receive our monthly updates.
* **How did you hear about us**: How you became aware of our real estate services.

Note: All fields marked as "required" are essential for the agent to provide a personalized experience. The fields marked as "optional" are for additional information.
