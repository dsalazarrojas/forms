# Real Estate Referral Network Signup - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Real Estate Referral Network Signup form is designed for real estate agents to join our referral network. By filling out this form, agents can provide us with their contact information and specify their referral terms. This information will be used to connect them with other agents and provide them with more business opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Partner Details** section with your contact information.
2. In the **Referral Terms** section, select the terms that best describe your referral practices.
3. In the **Submission Terms** section, provide any additional details or instructions for referrals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Details** (Required)
	+ This section is where you provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and any other relevant details.
* **Referral Terms** (Required)
	+ Select all the terms that apply to your referral practices. These terms will help us understand how you operate and connect you with other agents who have similar practices.
* **Submission Terms** (Optional)
	+ This section is where you can provide any additional details or instructions for referrals. You can use this space to specify how you want to be contacted or to provide any specific requirements for submissions. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "Required").
* If you have any issues with the form, contact us at [support email].
* If you are not a real estate agent, do not submit this form.
* This form is not intended for agents to use for personal or non-business purposes.
