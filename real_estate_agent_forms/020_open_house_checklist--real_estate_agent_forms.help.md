# Open House Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open House Checklist is a form designed to collect and track information about a real estate property, specifically an open house. This form is intended for agents and stakeholders in the real estate industry to gather data about the property's details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by a asterisk (*) symbol next to the field label.
2. Enter accurate and relevant information for each field.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Address * (id: 1)
* **Address**: Enter the property's street address, including the house number and street name.

### 2. Lot Number * (id: 2)
* **Lot Number**: Enter the property's lot number or reference number.

### 3. Property Type * (id: 3)
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property (Residential, Commercial, Industrial, or Other).

### 4. Year Built * (id: 4)
* **Year Built**: Enter the year the property was built.

### 5. Square Footage * (id: 5)
* **Square Footage**: Enter the total square footage of the property.

### 6. Number of Bedrooms * (id: 6)
* **Number of Bedrooms**: Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.

### 7. Number of Bathrooms * (id: 7)
* **Number of Bathrooms**: Enter the number of bathrooms in the property.

### 8. Lot Size/Land Length * (id: 8)
* **Lot Size/Land Length**: Enter the length of the property's lot size.

### 9. Lot Size/Square Footage * (id: 9)
* **Lot Size/Square Footage**: Enter the property's lot size in square footage (optional).

### 10. Year Renovated (id: 10)
* **Year Renovated**: Enter the year the property was renovated.

### 11. Last Price (id: 11)
* **Last Price**: Enter the last known sale or rent price of the property.

### 12. Year Sold (id: 12)
* **Year Sold**: Select the year the property was sold (2020, 2021, 2022, or 2023).

### 13. Sold Price (id: 13)
* **Sold Price**: Enter the sold price of the property.

### 14. Year Rent (id: 14)
* **Year Rent**: Select the year the property is rented (2020, 2021, 2022, or 2023).

### 15. Rent Price (id: 15)
* **Rent Price**: Enter the rent price of the property.

### 16. Notes (id: 16)
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the property.

### 17. Email (id: 17)
* **Email**: Enter the contact email of the property's representative.

### 18. Phone (id: 18)
* **Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the property's representative.

### 19. Fax (id: 19)
* **Fax**: Enter the fax number of the property's representative.

### 20. Other (id: 20)
* **Other**: Select any additional options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, or Option 5).

### 21. Other Text (id: 21)
* **Other Text**: Enter any additional text for the selected option.

### 22. Date (id: 22)
* **Date**: Enter the date the form is being filled.

### 23. Time (id: 23)
* **Time**: Enter the time the form is being filled.

### 24. Date and Time (id: 24)
* **Date and Time**: Enter the date and time the form is being filled.

### 25. Notes 2 (id: 25)
* **Notes 2**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the property.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* Enter accurate and relevant information for each field.
* If a field is not relevant, leave it blank.
* If you are unsure about a field, refer to the field label for guidance.
