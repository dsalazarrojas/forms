# Property Purchase Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a property purchase, including details about the buyer, property, and transactional aspects. It is intended for internal use within a real estate agency or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Buyer Information** section with the name and contact details of the buyer.
2. Enter the **Property Details** in the next section, including any relevant property information.
3. In the **Price and Terms** section, input the purchase price and any additional terms or conditions related to the sale.
4. Select the **Contact Information** section to indicate who is responsible for the purchase (Seller, Buyer, or Agent).
5. Provide the **Property Address** where the property is located.
6. Enter the **Purchase Price** of the property.
7. Choose the **Purchase Date** for the transaction.
8. Input the **Seller Conveyance Fees** and **Buyer Conveyance Fees** associated with the sale.
9. Enter any **Other Fees** related to the transaction.
10. Choose the current **Purchase Order Status** (Draft, Completed, or Canceled).
11. Finally, add any **Comments** about the transaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Information (1)** (text, required: no): Enter the name and contact details of the individual or organization purchasing the property.
* **Property Details (2)** (text, required: no): Provide any relevant information about the property, such as its location, size, or condition.
* **Price and Terms (3)** (number, required: no): Input the total purchase price of the property, including any additional terms or conditions of the sale.
* **Contact Information (4)** (select multiple, required: no): Choose from Seller, Buyer, or Agent to indicate who is responsible for the purchase.
* **Property Address (5)** (text, required: no): Enter the exact address of the property.
* **Purchase Price (6)** (number, required: no): Enter the total purchase price of the property.
* **Purchase Date (7)** (date, required: no): Choose the date of the purchase transaction.
* **Seller Conveyance Fees (8)** (number, required: no): Input the fees associated with the seller, such as commissions or other charges.
* **Buyer Conveyance Fees (9)** (number, required: no): Input the fees associated with the buyer, such as closing costs or other expenses.
* **Other Fees (10)** (number, required: no): Enter any additional fees or charges related to the transaction.
* **Purchase Order Status (11)** (select one, required: no): Choose from Draft, Completed, or Canceled to indicate the current status of the purchase order.
* **Comments (12)** (note, required: no): Add any additional comments or notes about the transaction.

## Tips
This form should only be used for internal purposes within the real estate agency or organization. If you are a property buyer or seller, please contact the relevant authority or agent for guidance on completing the purchase process.
