# Real Estate Virtual Tour Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register for a real estate virtual tour. It is designed to collect information from the user about their real estate agent, contact information, and preferences for the virtual tour.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information, including your real estate agent's name, email, phone number, and appointment date and time.
2. Select your preferred real estate type (e.g., Single Family Home, Condo, Townhouse, or Other).
3. Confirm that you will be available at the scheduled appointment time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Real-Estate-Agent** (Required): Enter the name of your real estate agent.
* **Email** (Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (Optional): Enter your address for our records.
* **Real Estate** (Required): Select the type of real estate you are interested in (e.g., Single Family Home, Condo, Townhouse, or Other).
* **Real-Estate-Appointment** (Required): Choose a date and time for the virtual tour appointment.
* **Real-Estate-Availability** (Required): Choose a time slot that you are available for the virtual tour.
* **Message** (Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your preferences or questions.
* **Real-Estate-Submission-Confirmation** (Required): Confirm that you will submit your real estate submission for review.
* **Real-Estate-Agreement** (Required): Confirm that you agree to the terms of the virtual tour agreement.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, contact your real estate agent for guidance.
* This form is for registration purposes only and does not guarantee a virtual tour appointment.
