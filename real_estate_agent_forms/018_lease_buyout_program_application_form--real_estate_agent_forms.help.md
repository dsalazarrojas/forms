# Lease Buyout Program Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lease Buyout Program Application Form is used to provide essential information about the applicant's lease agreement and unit details. This form is part of the lease buyout program, which aims to facilitate the transition of a unit from one lessee to another. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required information in the fields below. Each section is designed to capture specific data that will aid the lease buyout process.
2. Ensure you fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *) as accurately as possible.
3. For fields with options, select the most relevant option or provide a brief description if you don't see a suitable option.
4. Once you have completed all the required fields, review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First** (`first_name`, text, not required): 
This field is for your first name.
* **Last** (`last_name`, text, not required):
This field is for your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required):
Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, number, not required):
Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Unit** (`unit_number`, text, not required):
Enter the unit number (if applicable).
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, not required):
Select the type of property from the options: Apartment, Townhouse, House.
* **Lease Term** (`lease_term`, select_multiple, not required):
Choose the term of your lease from the options: 6 months, 12 months, 18 months, 24 months.
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, not required):
Enter the date of when you moved in to the unit.
* **Move Out Date** (`move_out_date`, date, not required):
Enter the date of when you plan to move out of the unit.
* **Lease Amount** (`lease_amount`, number, not required):
Enter the original lease amount.
* **ID Number** (`id_number`, select_multiple, not required):
Select the ID number from the options: id1, id2, id3.
* **Lease Agreement** (`lease_agreement`, text, not required):
Provide a brief description of the lease agreement.
* **Lease Addendum** (`lease_addendum`, text, not required):
Enter any lease addendums or conditions.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required):
Enter your name.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, not required):
Name the contact person for the lease agreement.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, not required):
Enter your contact person's phone number.
* **Rent** (`rent`, number, not required):
Enter the original rent amount.
* **Deposit** (`deposit`, number, not required):
Enter the deposit amount.
* **Utility Bills** (`utility_bills`, number, not required):
Enter the original utility bills amount.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, note, required):
This is a legal agreement section where you must acknowledge that you have read and understood the lease buyout program terms and conditions.
* **Additional Terms** (`additional_terms`, text, not required):
Provide any additional terms or conditions that you would like to be considered in the lease buyout process.
