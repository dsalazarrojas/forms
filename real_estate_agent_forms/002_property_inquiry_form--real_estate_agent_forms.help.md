# property_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "property_inquiry_form" is a form designed to gather information from users about a property they're interested in. This information will be used by real estate agents to help them better understand the user's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "property_inquiry" page.
2. Fill out the required fields as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Property Inquiry** (property_inquiry_form_page_1)
* **property_type**: Select the type of property you're interested in (Single, Multi-unit, Townhouse).
* This field helps us understand the type of property you're looking for.
### 2. **Location** (location)
* Enter the location where you're interested in the property.
* This field will help us narrow down the search to the specific area you're interested in.
### 3. **Square Footage** (square_footage)
* Enter the square footage of the property you're looking for.
* This field helps us understand the size of the property you're interested in.
### 4. **Price Range** (price_range)
* Select the price range you're interested in (Low, Medium, High).
* This field helps us understand the budget you're comfortable with.
### 5. **Contact Info** (contact_info)
* Enter your contact information (e.g., name, email, phone number).
* This field is used by our agents to contact you with more information about properties that match your interests.
### 6. **Additional Comments** (additional_comments)
* Enter any additional comments or questions you have about the property you're interested in.
* This field is optional but helps us better understand your needs and preferences.
### 7. **Date of Interest** (date_of_interest)
* Select the date you're interested in visiting or viewing the property.
* This field is optional but helps us understand your time frame.
### 8. **Time of Interest** (time_of_interest)
* Select the time you're interested in visiting or viewing the property.
* This field is optional but helps us understand your availability.
### 9. **Email** (email)
* Enter your email address so we can contact you with more information about the property you're interested in.
* This field is optional but recommended.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Our agents will review your inquiry and get back to you with more information about the properties that match your interests.
