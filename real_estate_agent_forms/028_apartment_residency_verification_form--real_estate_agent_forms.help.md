# Apartment Residency Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify the residency status of an individual at an apartment address. It's used to gather information from both the resident and the verifier who is confirming the resident's status.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the required fields accurately and thoroughly. Start by providing your name, apartment address, unit number, residency start date, and lease expiration date if applicable. Then, select the correct rent payment standing and indicate whether utilities are included in the rent. Finally, confirm your current occupancy status and provide details about the verifier who is confirming your residency.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name of Resident**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Property Address**: Enter the exact address of your apartment, including the building number and street name.
* **Unit Number**: Enter the unit number of your apartment.
* **Date of Move-in**: Enter the date you started your residency.
* **Lease Expiration Date**: Enter the date your lease is set to expire if you are not currently on a month-to-month lease.
* **Monthly Rent Amount**: Enter the total monthly rent you pay for your apartment.
* **Rent Payment Standing**: Select the option that best describes your payment history:
	+ **Excellent - Always on time**
	+ **Good - Occasional late payments**
	+ **Poor - Significant arrears**
* **Are utilities included in the rent?**: Select the option that best describes your lease agreement:
	+ **Yes, all utilities**
	+ **Some utilities included**
	+ **No utilities included**
* **Is the individual still residing at this address?**: Select **True** if you are currently residing at this address, **False** otherwise.
* **Full Name of Verifier**: Enter the full name of the person verifying your residency.
* **Title or Role**: Enter the title or role of the person verifying your residency (e.g., Property Manager, Landlord).
* **Verifier Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the person verifying your residency.
* **Date of Verification**: Enter the date when the verifier confirmed your residency.
* **Requesting Agency/Company**: Enter the name of the agency or company requesting the verification.
* **Note**: This field is for additional information or comments from the verifier.
