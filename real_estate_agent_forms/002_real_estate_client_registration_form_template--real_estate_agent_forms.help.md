# Real Estate Client Registration Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from real estate clients, enabling agents to manage their client relationships effectively. It collects data on client demographics, contact preferences, and follow-up details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **First name** field with the client's first name.
2. Enter the client's **Last name** in the corresponding field.
3. Provide the client's **Email** address, ensuring it is a valid email format.
4. Enter the client's **Phone** number.
5. If applicable, provide the client's **Address**.
6. Select the client's **Birth date** from the calendar or enter it manually.
7. Enter the **Agent name**, which is likely the name of the representative handling the client's case.
8. Select whether the client is affiliated with an **Agency** (yes or no).
9. Choose the client's **Client type** (yes or no).
10. Select the client's **Client status** (active or inactive).
11. Indicate the client's preferred **Contact method** (yes or no) for different methods (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
12. Choose a **Follow-up method** (yes or no) if applicable.
13. Optionally, provide a **Note** regarding the client's case.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter the client's first name.
* **Last name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter the client's last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Provide a valid email address for the client.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Address** (`5`, `text`, optional): If the client's address is known, enter it here.
* **Birth date** (`6`, `date`, optional): Select the client's birth date from the calendar or enter it manually.
* **Agent name** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter the representative's name handling the client's case.
* **Agency** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the client is affiliated with an agency (yes or no).
* **Client type** (`9`, `select_one`, required): Choose the client's type (yes or no).
* **Client status** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Select the client's status (active or inactive).
* **Contact method** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate the client's preferred contact methods (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
* **Follow up** (`12`, `date`, optional): Schedule a follow-up date for the client.
* **Follow up method** (`13`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the follow-up method (yes or no).
* **Note** (`14`, `note`, optional): Add any relevant notes regarding the client's case.
