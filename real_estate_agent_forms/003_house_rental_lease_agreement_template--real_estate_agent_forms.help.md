# House Rental Lease Agreement Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to create a comprehensive lease agreement template for house rental properties. It captures essential details about the property and the tenant's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Tenant information section (Page 1).
	* **Tenant Name**: Enter the tenant's name.
	* **Email**: Enter the tenant's email address.
	* **Tenant Signature Date**: Enter the date the tenant signs the agreement.
2. Move on to the Lease terms section (Page 2).
	* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the lease.
	* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the lease.
	* **Lease Term**: Select the lease duration (1, 3, 6, 12, or 24 months).
	* **Rent Frequency**: Select the rent payment frequency (Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, or Annually).
3. Continue to the Payment details section (Page 3).
	* **Rent Amount**: Enter the rent amount.
	* **Payment Due Date**: Enter the payment due date.
	* **Payment Frequency**: Select the payment frequency (same as in the Rent Frequency field).
4. Proceed to the Property details section (Page 4).
	* **Property Address**: Enter the property address.
	* **Property City**: Enter the property city.
	* **Property Province**: Enter the property province.
5. Finally, review and fill out the Additional terms and conditions section (Page 5).
	* **Security Deposit**: Check if the security deposit is returnable.
	* **Pet**: Check if pets are allowed.
	* **Property Description**: Describe the property.
	* **Rent Frequency Description**: Describe the rent frequency.
	* **Payment Terms**: Describe any payment terms.
6. Sign and date the **Tenant Signature Date** field.
7. Sign and date the **Landlord Signature Date** field.
