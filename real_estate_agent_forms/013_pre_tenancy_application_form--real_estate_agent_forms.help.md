# Pre Tenancy Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Tenancy Application Form is a comprehensive document designed to collect essential information from individuals applying to be a tenant for a real estate property. This form helps the landlord or property manager to assess the applicant's eligibility and suitability for tenancy.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read through the form and ensure you understand each field's purpose and relevance.
- Enter your accurate and truthful contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
- Provide detailed information about your employment and work experience in the "Employment Info" field.
- Share your past rental history, including the duration and type of accommodation, in the "Rental History" field.
- Specify your rental preferences, such as the type of property or location you are looking for, in the "Rental Preferences" field.
- Submit the form once you have completed all required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info**:
	+ This field is used to collect your basic contact information, including your name, address, email, and phone number.
* **Employment Info**:
	+ Please provide detailed information about your current and past employment, including job title, work experience, and salary range.
* **Rental History**:
	+ Share your past rental experiences, including the type of accommodation, duration of tenancy, and any relevant details.
* **Rental Preferences**:
	+ Specify your preferences for the type of property or location you are looking for, such as the neighborhood, amenities, or lease term.

## Tips
- Ensure accuracy and truthfulness in your submissions.
- Complete the form in a quiet and private environment to minimize errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrator at form@gic.mx.
