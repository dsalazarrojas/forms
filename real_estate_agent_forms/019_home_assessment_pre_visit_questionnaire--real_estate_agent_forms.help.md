# Home Assessment Pre Visit Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from homeowners before an assessor visits their property. The goal is to gather essential details about the property and the contact information to facilitate a smooth and efficient assessment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the property and yourself before starting.
2. Fill in the contact information, including your name, address, and contact method.
3. Select the relevant contact method (call, email, text, or visit) and indicate the status of your contact (pending, completed, or cancelled).
4. Provide additional contact details, if necessary.
5. Determine if you need follow-up reminders and select the status of follow-up reminders (on or off).
6. Choose a follow-up reminders frequency.
7. Specify the type of property (single family home, multi-family home, condo, or duplex).
8. Select any features that apply to the property (swimming pool, fireplace, park view, or basement).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (1) (text)**: Please enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Property Address (2) (text)**: Enter the address of the property to be assessed.
* **Contact Date (3) (date)**: Enter the date you would like to schedule the assessment.
* **Contact Time (4) (time)**: Select the time of day you would like to schedule the assessment.
* **Contact Method (5) (select multiple)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (call, email, text, or visit).
* **Contact Status (6) (select one)**: Indicate the status of your contact (pending, completed, or cancelled).
* **Contact Details (7) (note)**: Add any additional information about your contact that is important for the assessor.
* **Follow-up Reminders (8) (text)**: Enter any follow-up reminders or notes related to the assessment.
* **Follow-up Reminders Status (9) (select one)**: Determine if you need follow-up reminders (on or off).
* **Follow-up Reminders Frequency (10) (number)**: Choose how frequently you would like to receive follow-up reminders.
* **Property Type (11) (select one)**: Specify the type of property (single family home, multi-family home, condo, or duplex).
* **Property Features (12) (select multiple)**: Select any features that apply to the property (swimming pool, fireplace, park view, or basement).
* **Property Size (13) (number)**: Enter the size of the property.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* The assessment process may take some time, so please be patient and we will contact you as soon as possible.
