# Property Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Inquiry Form is a text-based form used to gather information about a property inquiry. It is designed to collect contact details and property-related information from users, enabling real estate agents or property managers to respond to their inquiries efficiently.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter the property address in the "Property Address" field.
5. (Optional) Enter the property price, if relevant.
6. Click the submit button to send your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name to help the real estate agent or property manager respond to your inquiry efficiently.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to enable the real estate agent or property manager to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number to facilitate communication about your property inquiry.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property you are inquiring about.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): (Optional) Enter the price of the property, if relevant to your inquiry.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your contact information correctly to ensure the real estate agent or property manager can respond to your inquiry efficiently.
* If you're not sure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification or additional information.
* You can always contact us directly if you need assistance or have any questions.
