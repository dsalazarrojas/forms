# Prescreen Rental Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The prescreen rental questionnaire is a form used to collect crucial information about a potential tenant's background, preferences, and expectations. This information helps landlords or property managers decide whether to proceed with the rental application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the prescreen rental questionnaire, follow these steps:

* Enter your First Name in the text field.
* Enter your Last Name in the text field.
* Enter your Email address in the email field.
* Enter your Phone Number in the text field.
* Enter the Rent Amount you are willing to pay in the numeric field.
* Select the Date you are available to move-in on.
* Enter the Length of Stay you are expecting.
* Choose the Type of Rental from the dropdown options.
* Provide the Reason for Move-in in the text field.
* Choose the Rental Status from the multiple-choice options.
* Enter the Property Address of the rental property.
* Provide any additional Rental Notes about the rental opportunity.
* Select the Tenant Status from the multiple-choice options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that can be contacted for further inquiries.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number that can be used to contact you.
* **Rent Amount**: Enter the rent amount you are willing to pay for the rental property.
* **Move-in Date**: Select the date you are available to move-in to the property.
* **Length of Stay**: Enter the length of time you plan to stay in the rental property.
* **Type of Rental**: Choose the type of rental you are looking for (e.g., apartment, house, etc.).
* **Reason for Move-in**: Provide a brief explanation for your reason for moving-in.
* **Rental Status**: Select all the rental statuses that apply to your situation (e.g., single, couple, etc.).
* **Property Address**: Enter the exact address of the rental property.
* **Rental Notes**: Provide any additional information about the rental property that may be relevant to your application.
* **Tenant Status**: Select all the statuses that apply to your tenant situation (e.g., student, family, etc.).
