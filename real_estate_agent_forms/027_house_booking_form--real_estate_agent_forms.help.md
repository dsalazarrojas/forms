# House Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users about their property booking requests. The form helps real estate agents to manage and process booking requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Property Id of the property you are interested in booking.
2. Provide your Guest Name and contact information, including Email and Phone Number.
3. Choose the dates for Checkin and Checkout.
4. Select the type of property (House, Apartment, or Condo) and room type (Single, Double, or Family).
5. Specify the booking duration and any Special Requests.
6. Finally, choose a Payment Method (Cash, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Id**: Enter the property id of the property you are interested in booking. This will help the real estate agent to identify the correct property.
* **Guest Name**: Enter your name as the guest.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where the real estate agent can contact you.
* **Checkin Date**: Enter the date when you would like to check-in.
* **Checkout Date**: Enter the date when you would like to check-out.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property you are interested in (House, Apartment, or Condo).
* **Room Type**: Select the type of room you are interested in (Single, Double, or Family).
* **Booking Duration**: Enter the number of days or nights you would like to book the property.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests or requirements you have.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for the booking (Cash, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer).
