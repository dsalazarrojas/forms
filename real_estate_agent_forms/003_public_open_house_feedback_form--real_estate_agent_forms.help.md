# public_open_house_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The public_open_house_feedback_form is designed to collect feedback from visitors to public open houses. This form will help us understand their experiences, preferences, and opinions about the event and the real estate agents involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **open_house_location** field with the location of the open house you visited.
2. Provide your **contact_name** and **contact_email** to help us get back to you.
3. Answer the question **what_do_you_like** about the open house.
4. Rate your experience with **rating**.
5. Select whether you would recommend the real estate **real_estate_agent** who assisted you.
6. Provide any additional **comments** you'd like to share.
7. Fill in your **date_of_visit** and **time_of_visit** for the open house.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Open House Location** (text, required): Enter the location of the open house you visited.
* **Contact Name** (text, required): Enter your name as you would like to be contacted.
* **What Do You Like** (text, optional): Share what you liked about the open house. (e.g., "The agent was very helpful" or "The location was beautiful")
* **Rating** (select_one, required): Rate your experience with the open house on a scale of Good or Fair.
* **Prophet's Answer** (text, required): Provide a brief answer to the feedback question.
* **Contact Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (text, required): Enter a valid phone number where we can contact you.
* **Contact Other** (text, optional): If you have any other contact information, please provide it.
* **Date of Visit** (date, required): Enter the date of the open house visit.
* **Time of Visit** (time, required): Enter the time of the open house visit.
* **Comments** (text, optional): Share any additional comments about the open house.
* **Real Estate Agent** (select_multiple, required): Select whether you would recommend the real estate agent who assisted you.
* **Comments 2** (text, optional): This field is optional and can be used to add any additional comments.
