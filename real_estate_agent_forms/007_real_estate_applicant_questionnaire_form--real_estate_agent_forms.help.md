# real_estate_applicant_questionnaire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a real estate applicant questionnaire used to gather information from applicants about their home address, personal details, employment status, and rental status. The form helps to collect relevant information that can be used in the real estate application process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling out the form in sections, starting with your home address and personal details.
2. Fill in your name, contact number, and email address.
3. Provide your current income level.
4. Answer questions about your family status and the number of dependents.
5. Select your employment status and occupation.
6. Answer questions about your employer and years of experience.
7. Answer questions about your rental status, including the type of home you're looking for, the rental lease start and end dates, and the rent you're willing to pay.
8. Provide any additional information about your employment status or property type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Address**: Enter your current home address. This will help us to understand where you are currently living.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. We will use this to communicate with you about your application.
* **Income**: Enter your current income level.
* **Family Status**: Answer whether you are married, partnered, or single.
* **Number of Dependents**: Answer how many dependents you have.
* **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status (Employed, Retired, Self-Employed, Student, Unemployed).
* **Occupation**: Enter your occupation.
* **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years you have been in your current occupation.
* **Employer**: Enter your current employer's name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Years of Service**: Enter the number of years you have been working for your current employer.
* **Rental Status**: Select whether you currently rent or own a property.
* **Current Rental Address**: Enter your current rental address.
* **Rental Lease Start Date**: Enter the start date of your current rental lease.
* **Rental Lease End Date**: Enter the end date of your current rental lease.
* **Rent**: Enter the amount of rent you are willing to pay.
* **Utilities Included**: Enter the utilities included in your rental lease.
* **Square Footage**: Enter the square footage of the property you are looking for.
* **Lease Type**: Enter the type of lease you are looking for.
* **Lease Duration**: Enter the duration of the lease you are looking for.
* **Type of Home**: Select the type of home you are looking for (Single, Condo, Townhouse, Apartment, Duplex, Semi-Detached, Detached, Other).
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property you are looking for (Single Home, Condo, Townhome, Duplex, Semi-Detached, Other).
* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about your employment status or property type.

This form should take around 10-15 minutes to complete, and please fill out all fields to ensure your application is complete. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
