# Property Transfer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the details of a property transfer, ensuring that all relevant information is captured accurately and efficiently. It helps in tracking and verifying the status of properties within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the transfer details, including the transfer date and time.
2. Select the status of the transfer, which can be either Active or Inactive.
3. Provide any comments or notes about the transfer.
4. Enter the equipment ID and model number if applicable.
5. Identify the transferor, who is the person or team responsible for the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Transfer Details (1)** (`transfer_details`, text, required: false): This field is used to enter any additional details about the property transfer. It's an optional field, and you can use it to provide any extra information that's relevant to the transfer.
* **From Division (2)** (`from_division`, select_one, required: false): This field allows you to select the division that the property is being transferred from. You can choose from the options 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the property is being transferred from this division.
* **To Division (3)** (`to_division`, select_one, required: false): Similar to the 'From Division' field, this field is used to select the division that the property is being transferred to. You can choose from the options 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the property is being transferred to this division.
* **Equipment ID (4)** (`equipment_id`, number, required: false): If the property being transferred has an associated equipment ID, enter it here.
* **Equipment Model (5)** (`equipment_model`, text, required: false): This field is used to enter the model number or description of the equipment being transferred.
* **Transfer Date (6)** (`transfer_date`, date, required: false): This field is used to enter the date of the property transfer.
* **Transfer Time (7)** (`transfer_time`, time, required: false): This field is used to enter the time of the property transfer.
* **Transfer Status (8)** (`transfer_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the status of the transfer, which can be either Active or Inactive.
* **Comments (9)** (`comments`, text, required: false): This field is for entering any comments or notes about the transfer.
* **Transferor (10)** (`transferor`, text, required: false): This field is used to enter the name of the person or team responsible for the transfer.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the relevant fields accurately and completely to avoid any confusion or mistakes.
* If you are not sure about any of the field's purpose, please ask your supervisor or colleague for clarification. 
* Double-check the details before submitting the form.
