# Pre Market Listing Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Market Listing Questionnaire is a comprehensive form to gather information about a pre-market listing. This form helps real estate agents collect accurate and up-to-date data about the property, ensuring that it is correctly listed in the market.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Answer all the questions accurately and honestly.
3. Make sure to check and correct any errors before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Pre Market Listing Questionnaire**: This field is the title of the form.
- **Property Details**: This field is for providing a brief description of the property. Please include any relevant information such as property type, size, and location.
- **Condition of Property**: Check if the property is in 'Yes' or 'No' condition. If 'Yes', please provide further details in the space below.
- **Contact Info**: This field is for providing contact information of the property owner or agent. Please ensure you have the correct contact details.
- **Home Owner**: This field is for listing the home owner's name.
- **Listing Agent**: This field is for listing the listing agent's name.
- **Property Location**: This field is for providing the location of the property. Please ensure accurate spelling and formatting.
- **Property Size**: Enter the property size in square feet or acres.
- **Year Built**: Enter the year the property was built.
- **Lot Size**: Enter the lot size in square feet or acres.
- **Lot Orientation**: This field is for describing the orientation of the property. Please be as specific as possible.
- **Property Type**: Choose the type of property from the options provided (Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Agricultural).
- **Property Subtype**: This field is for providing a more specific type of property. Please ensure it's accurate.
- **Neighborhood**: This field is for listing the neighborhood or area of the property.
- **Zoning Status**: Choose the status of the property from the options provided (current, vacant, unknown).
- **Ownership Type**: Choose the type of ownership (owned, lease, rent).
- **Property Address**: This field is a note field for providing the property's address. Please ensure accurate formatting.
- **Property Neighborhood**: This field is for listing the neighborhood or area of the property.
- **Postal Code**: This field is for listing the postal code or post office code.
- **Property City**: This field is for listing the city of the property.
- **Property State**: This field is for listing the state of the property (not applicable for Canadian properties, use **property_province** instead).
- **Property Province**: Choose the province of the property (Ontario, British Columbia, Alberta).
- **Property Country**: This field is for listing the country of the property. Only applicable for international properties.
- **Property Zip**: Enter the zip code of the property.

Note: This form is for general guidance and is not a substitute for official government documentation. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities.
