# Rental Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Rental Checklist" form is a quick and easy tool for collecting and viewing basic information about rental properties. It's designed to be used by real estate agents, property managers, or individuals seeking to showcase or review rental properties' details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Tenant Info", "Property Details", "Lease Terms", and "Rent And Payments" fields with the relevant information about the rental property.
2. Make sure to fill in all the required fields (none of the fields on this form are required, but it's best to complete all fields for a comprehensive view of the property).
3. Review your entries for accuracy before submitting or saving the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Info (id: 1)**: Enter the tenant's name and contact information, if applicable.
* **Property Details (id: 2)**: Fill in the property's address, location, and any other relevant details you want to highlight about the property.
* **Lease Terms (id: 3)**: Describe the lease terms and conditions, such as the rent, length of lease, and any specific rules or regulations.
* **Rent And Payments (id: 4)**: Document the rent amount, payment schedule, and any other payment-related details.

## Tips
- Double-check the completed form for accuracy and completeness before submitting or saving the data.
- You can adjust the form layout or add more fields if needed, but the current structure should cover the essential information about a rental property.

This help guide is meant to be concise and practical, providing a clear understanding of the form's purpose and usage. If you have any further questions or need additional clarification, feel free to ask.
