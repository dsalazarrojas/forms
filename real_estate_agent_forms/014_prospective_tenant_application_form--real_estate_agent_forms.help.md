# Prospective Tenant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prospective Tenant Application Form is a collection of fields designed to gather information from prospective tenants to assess their suitability to rent a property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each section, which is marked as "required" in the form.
2. Provide accurate and truthful information for all fields.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Landlord Info (landlord_info, text, required)**: Please provide your name and contact information.
* **Tenant Info (tenant_info, text, required)**: Enter your name, address, and other relevant contact information.
* **Property Info (property_info, text, required)**: Describe the property you are interested in renting, including its address and approximate square footage.
* **Tenant History (tenant_history, text, required)**: Provide any relevant information about your rental history, such as past landlord references or rental agreements.
* **Rental Agreement (rental_agreement, text, required)**: Explain your understanding of the rental agreement, including your expectations and any special requests.
* **Move In Move Out (move_in_move_out, text, required)**: Specify the desired move-in and move-out dates for the rental period.
* **Payment Info (payment_info, text, required)**: Provide details about your payment history and financial situation.
* **Guarantor Info (guarantor_info, text, optional)**: If you have a guarantor, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Other Docs (other_docs, text, optional)**: Attach any relevant documents, such as proof of income or employment.
* **Submission (submission, text, required)**: Review and submit your application for review.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* Double-check your information for any errors before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the landlord or property manager.
