# Condo Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect comprehensive details about a condominium property, including its address, unit number, owner's contact information, financial details, and amenities. It is designed to be completed by real estate agents to gather essential data for their clients.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all necessary information about the condo property before starting the form.
2.  Enter the property address in the "Property Address" field (Field 3).
3.  Enter the unit number in the "Unit Number" field (Field 4).
4.  Enter the building name in the "Building Name" field (Field 5) if applicable.
5.  Select the number of bedrooms and bathrooms in the respective fields (Fields 6 and 7).
6.  Enter the total square footage of the property (Field 8).
7.  Select the floor level and total floors in the building (Fields 9 and 10).
8.  Enter the year the building was constructed (Field 11).
9.  Select the ownership type from the options (Field 16).
10. Enter the owner's name, email, and phone number in the respective fields (Fields 13, 14, and 15).
11. Select all applicable amenities and parking spaces (Fields 21, 22, and 23).
12. Specify any pet policy and special assessments (Fields 24 and 25).
13. Enter any recent renovations made to the property (Field 26).
14. Select the reason for filling out this form (Field 27).
15. Enter any additional comments about the property (Field 28).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Property Address (Field 2)**: Enter the full address of the condominium property.
*   **Unit Number (Field 3)**: Enter the specific unit number of the property.
*   **Building Name (Field 4)**: Enter the name of the condominium building if applicable.
*   **Number of Bedrooms (Field 6)**: Select the total number of bedrooms in the property.
*   **Number of Bathrooms (Field 7)**: Select the total number of bathrooms in the property.
*   **Square Footage (Field 8)**: Enter the total square footage of the property.
*   **Floor Level (Field 9)**: Select the floor level where the property is located.
*   **Total Floors in Building (Field 10)**: Select the total number of floors in the building.
*   **Year Built (Field 11)**: Enter the year the building was constructed.
*   **Owner Name (Field 13)**: Enter the full name of the property owner.
*   **Owner Email (Field 14)**: Enter the contact email of the property owner.
*   **Owner Phone (Field 15)**: Enter the contact phone number of the property owner.
*   **Ownership Type (Field 16)**: Select the type of ownership from the options provided.
*   **Monthly HOA Fee (Field 18)**: Enter the monthly association fee.
*   **HOA Fee Includes (Field 19)**: Select all services included in the HOA fee.
*   **Annual Property Taxes (Field 20)**: Enter the yearly property tax amount.
*   **Building Amenities (Field 21)**: Enter any additional amenities provided by the building.
*   **Amenities Available (Field 22)**: Select all applicable amenities from the options.
*   **Parking Spaces (Field 23)**: Enter the number of parking spaces provided by the building.
*   **Pet Policy (Field 24)**: Select the pet policy of the building.
*   **Special Assessments (Field 25)**: Enter any special assessments pending on the property.
*   **Recent Renovations (Field 26)**: Enter any recent renovations made to the property.
*   **Reason for Inquiry (Field 27)**: Select the reason for filling out this form.
*   **Additional Comments (Field 28)**: Enter any additional comments or information about the property.

## Tips
*   Ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to provide the most accurate information possible.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid missing any essential details.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant real estate agent or authority for assistance.
