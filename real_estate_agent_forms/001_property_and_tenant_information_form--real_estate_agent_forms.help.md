# Property And Tenant Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential details about a property and its owner or tenant. It will help the relevant parties to understand the property's characteristics, the rent amount, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, follow these steps:
    *   Fill in the property address, making sure to include the house number, street name, and any other relevant details.
    *   Enter the rent amount for the property.
    *   Select the type of property (residential, commercial, industrial, agricultural, or other).
    *   Fill in your first name, last name, email address, phone number, company name, job title, and employment start and end dates.
    *   Indicate whether you have a criminal record (true or false).
    *   Provide a brief description of any crime.
    *   If applicable, enter additional references with details (first name, last name, company, job title, phone number, and employment start and end dates).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   * **Property Address** (`property_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address where you own or rent the property. This includes house number, street name, and any other relevant details.
*   * **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the monthly or annual rent amount for the property.
*   * **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of property (residential, commercial, industrial, agricultural, or other) from the provided options.
*   * **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
*   * **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
*   * **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
*   * **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
*   * **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Enter your company name or affiliation.
*   * **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title or position.
*   * **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you started employment in your current position.
*   * **Employment End Date** (`employment_end_date`, `date`, required): If applicable, enter the date you ended employment in your current position.
*   * **Has Criminal Record** (`has_criminal_record`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have a criminal record (true or false).
*   * **Crime Description** (`crime_description`, `text`, required): If you selected true for the previous field, provide a brief description of the crime.
*   * **Reference 1** (`reference_1`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter details for Reference 1, including first name, last name, company, job title, phone number, and employment start and end dates.
