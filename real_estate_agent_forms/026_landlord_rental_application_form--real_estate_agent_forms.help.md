# Landlord Rental Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from landlords who are interested in renting their properties through our platform. We use this data to create a landlord profile and manage the rental process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the field labeled "first_name".
2.  Enter your last name in the field labeled "last_name".
3.  Enter your email address in the field labeled "email".
4.  Enter your phone number in the field labeled "phone".
5.  Enter the address of the property you wish to rent in the field labeled "property_address".
6.  Select the start date of the rent in the field labeled "rent_start_date".
7.  Select the end date of the rent in the field labeled "rent_end_date".
8.  Choose the rent period from the options "Monthly", "Weekly", "Bi-Weekly", "Quarterly" in the field labeled "rent_period".
9.  Select how often you rent the property, either "Once" or "Ongoing" in the field labeled "rent_frequency".
10.  Select the date you plan to move in in the field labeled "move_in_date".
11.  Choose whether you are able to "Yes" or "No" to the question about moving in in the field labeled "move_in_status".
12.  Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have a lease agreement in the field labeled "landlord_lease_agreement".
13.  Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have a rental agreement in the field labeled "landlord_rental_agreement".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (Text): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (Text): Enter your last name.
* **email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **property_address** (Text): Enter the address of the property you wish to rent.
* **rent_start_date** (Date): Select the start date of the rent.
* **rent_end_date** (Date): Select the end date of the rent.
* **rent_period** (Select One): Choose how often you wish to rent, from "Monthly", "Weekly", "Bi-Weekly", "Quarterly".
* **rent_frequency** (Select One): Select how often you rent the property, either "Once" or "Ongoing".
* **move_in_date** (Date): Select the date you plan to move in.
* **move_in_status** (Select One): Choose whether you are able to "Yes" or "No" to the question about moving in.
* **landlord_lease_agreement** (Select Multiple): Indicate whether you have a lease agreement, by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **landlord_rental_agreement** (Select Multiple): Indicate whether you have a rental agreement, by selecting "Yes" or "No".
