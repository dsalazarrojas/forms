# Rental Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Payment Form is used by real estate agents to collect rental payment information from tenants. It captures essential details for rent collection and reconciliation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the designated fields.
2. Enter your **Email** for contact purposes.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** for easier contact.
4. Enter the **Amount Due**, the total amount of rent you are paying.
5. Select the **Due Date**, the date by which the payment is due.
6. Choose the **Payment Type** from the options provided (Cash, Check, or Bank Transfer).
7. Select the **Payment Method** from the options provided (Online Payment, Bank Transfer, or Other).
8. Enter the **Rental Address** for the property you are paying rent for.
9. Optionally, enter any additional notes in the **Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name for identity purposes.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name for identity purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easier contact.
* **Amount**: Enter the total amount of rent you are paying.
* **Due Date**: Select the date by which the payment is due.
* **Payment Type**: Select the method through which you will be making the payment (Cash, Check, or Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment you will be using (Online Payment, Bank Transfer, or Other).
* **Rental Address**: Enter the address of the property for which you are making the payment.
* **Notes**: Optional field to enter any additional notes or comments.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out to ensure accurate and complete payment information.
* Double-check the **Due Date** to avoid late payments.
* Be accurate when selecting the **Payment Type** and **Payment Method** to ensure smooth processing of the payment.
