# Housing Cancellation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Housing Cancellation Form is used to officially cancel a housing lease between a landlord and a tenant. This form is a record of the cancellation and serves as proof of the termination of the lease agreement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately, following the instructions for each field.
2.  Make sure to include all required fields.
3.  Proofread for errors before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info (id: 1)** (`User Info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the user's name and any other relevant information.
* **Lease Info (id: 2)** (`Lease Info`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the lease, including the address and lease terms.
* **Reason for Cancellation (id: 3)** (`Reason for Cancellation`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more of the provided options to indicate the reason for the cancellation. The options provided are:
    + Option 1
    + Option 2
    + Option 3
*   **Signature (id: 4)** (`Signature`, `note`, required: false): Include a signature to authenticate the cancellation.
*   **Date (id: 5)** (`Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the cancellation.
*   **Time (id: 6)** (`Time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the cancellation.
*   **Email (id: 7)** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the tenant or representative.
*   **Phone (id: 8)** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the tenant or representative.
*   **Comments (id: 9)** (`Comments`, `text`, required: false): Include any additional comments about the cancellation.
*   **User Notes (id: 10)** (`User Notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information or notes about the cancellation.

## Tips

*   Always proofread the form before submitting it to ensure it is complete and accurate.
*   Keep the form organized and easy to read.
*   Make sure to keep a record of the cancellation for future reference.
