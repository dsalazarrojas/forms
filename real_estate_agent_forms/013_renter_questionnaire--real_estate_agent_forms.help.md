# Renter Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Renter Questionnaire is designed to collect relevant information from renters to assess their credibility and suitability as a tenant.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all that apply from the "Rental Agreement" page.
2. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
3. Choose your employment status in the "Employment" field.
4. Select your rent history in the "Rent History" field.
5. Enter your move-in date in the "Move In Date" field.
6. Enter your rent amount in the "Rent Amount" field.
7. Enter the duration of your lease in the "Lease Duration" field.
8. Enter your move-out date in the "Move Out Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rental Agreement** (`rental_agreement`, Select Multiple, required: false):
	+ This section allows you to agree or disagree with certain terms of your rental agreement. Please select all that apply.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your full name, phone number, and email address.
* **Employment** (`employment`, Select One, required: false):
	+ Choose from the options: Employed, Unemployed, Disabled, or Other.
* **Rent History** (`rent_history`, Select Multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all that apply from the options: Paid Rent on Time, Partially Paid Rent on Time, Did not pay rent.
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, Date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date you moved in.
* **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, Number, required: false):
	+ Enter the current rent amount.
* **Lease Duration** (`lease_duration`, Number, required: false):
	+ Enter the length of your current lease in months.
* **Move Out Date** (`move_out_date`, Date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date you plan to move out.
