# Blank Property Deed Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Blank Property Deed Form is used to create a document that records a transfer of property ownership between two parties. This form is a legal document that confirms the sale or transfer of a property from the grantor (seller) to the grantee (buyer).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review and understand the purpose of the form.
2. Ensure you have all necessary information and documents ready, including the grantor's and grantee's names, property address, consideration amount, and notarization details (if required).
3. Fill in the grantor's and grantee's names, property address, consideration amount, and any other required fields.
4. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Grantor name** (`grantor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the party selling or transferring the property.
* **Grantee name** (`grantee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the party buying or receiving the property.
* **Property address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the street, city, and postal code of the property being transferred.
* **Legal description** (`legal_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the property, including the lot and plan details.
* **Consideration amount** (`consideration_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount exchanged for the property in numbers.
* **Registration date** (`registration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of deed registration (if applicable).
* **Notarization details** (`notarization_details`, text, optional): Enter notary name and jurisdiction (if required).
* **Witness names** (`witness_names`, text, optional): Enter names of witnesses if required (if not applicable).

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to avoid errors.
* Ensure you have all necessary supporting documents, such as the property deed.
* This form is a legally binding document, so be sure to review it carefully before signing.
