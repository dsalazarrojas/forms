# Listing Cancellation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Listing Cancellation Form is used to cancel a listing, providing a structured process for documenting and communicating the cancellation details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the listing in the `listing_cancellation_form` field.
2. Provide a brief explanation for the cancellation reason in the `cancellation_reason` field.
3. If applicable, enter the listing ID and user ID in the respective fields.
4. Specify the reason for removal and provide any additional details in the `reason_for_removal` and `reason_for_removal_details` fields.
5. Enter the date and time of removal in the `date_removal` and `time_removal` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **listing_cancellation_form**: Enter the title of the listing you wish to cancel.
* **cancellation_reason**: Provide a brief explanation for the cancellation reason.
* **listing_id**: If you know the listing ID, enter it here (optional).
* **user_id**: If you know the user ID associated with the listing, enter it here (optional).
* **reason_for_removal**: Specify the reason for removing the listing.
* **reason_for_removal_details**: Provide any additional details related to the reason for removal.
* **date_removal**: Enter the date when the listing will be removed.
* **time_removal**: Enter the time when the listing will be removed.
* **review_removal_date**: Optional field to specify a review date after removal (enter if applicable).
* **additional_information**: Add any additional information related to the listing cancellation (optional).
