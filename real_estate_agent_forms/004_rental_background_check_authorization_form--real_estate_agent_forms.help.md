# Rental Background Check Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information from tenants and property owners to perform a rental background check. It ensures that all relevant parties are aware of and agree to the process, providing a smooth and secure experience for all parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the appropriate authorization option for the background check.
3. Review and submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Info (Page 1)** (`tenant_info`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect basic information about the tenant. Please provide your name and contact details.
* **Property Owner Info (Page 1)** (`property_owner_info`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect information about the property owner. Please provide their name and contact details.
* **Authorization (Page 1)** (`authorization`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the two options:
	+ Tenant to conduct: You are authorizing the background check to be conducted by the tenant.
	+ Property Manager to conduct: You are authorizing the background check to be conducted by the property manager.
* **Rental Agreement (Page 1)** (`rental_agreement`, text, required: false): This field is used to attach a copy of the rental agreement or provide a brief description of the rental terms.
* **Background Check Info (Page 2)** (`background_check_info`, text, required: false): This field is used to provide any additional information related to the background check.
