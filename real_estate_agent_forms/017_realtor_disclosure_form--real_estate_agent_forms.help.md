# Realtor Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Realtor Disclosure Form is used to collect information about real estate agents working within the agency. This form helps the agency keep track of agent details, including their name, contact information, and agency affiliation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as follows:
1. Enter your **first name** in the text field.
2. Enter your **last name** in the text field.
3. Enter your **email address** in the email field (ensure it follows the standard email format).
4. Enter your **phone number** in the text field.
5. Enter your **address** in the text field.
6. Enter your **real estate agency** name in the text field.
7. Enter your **agency name** in the text field.
8. Enter your **agent name** in the text field.
9. Enter your **brokerage** name in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first-name**: This is your given name.
* **last-name**: This is your surname.
* **email**: This is your professional email address.
* **phone**: This is your phone number.
* **address**: This is your office or mailing address.
* **real-estate-agency**: This is the name of the real estate agency you work with.
* **real-estate-agency-name**: This is the name of the real estate agency.
* **agent**: This is your name as an agent.
* **brokerage**: This is the name of the brokerage firm you are affiliated with.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and honestly.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the agency administration for clarification.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy before submitting the form.
