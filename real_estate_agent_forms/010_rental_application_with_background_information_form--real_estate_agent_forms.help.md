# Rental Application With Background Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for renters to submit their application and background information for a rental property. It's meant to collect necessary details for the landlord or property manager to assess the applicant's eligibility and suitability for the rental.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Rental Application** section with your desired rental information.
2. Provide your **Background Information**, including any relevant details that might affect your rental application.
3. Submit your **Applicant Information**, which includes your personal details.
4. Share your **Employment Information**, including your job type and work experience.
5. Disclose your **Rental History**, including any previous rental experiences or evictions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rental Application**: This section allows you to specify the rental property you're interested in or currently renting. Please provide the property address or the name of the property.
* **Background Information**: Share any relevant background details that might impact your rental application, such as outstanding warrants or previous disputes.
* **Applicant Information**: Fill out this section with your personal details, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant personal data.
* **Employment Information**: Provide your employment type and work experience to demonstrate your financial stability and ability to pay rent.
* **Rental History**: Share your previous rental experiences, including any evictions, lease terminations, or other relevant information.
* **Tips**: Remember to be honest and thorough when filling out this form. Incomplete or inaccurate information might lead to delays or denied applications.
