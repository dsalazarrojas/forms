# residential_rental_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The residential rental application form is used to gather information from prospective tenants for rental applications. It is used to collect demographic data and rental history information for real estate agents to review and potentially show properties to.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** (id: 1)
	* A text field where you can enter your first name.
2. Enter your **Last Name** (id: 2)
	* A text field where you can enter your last name.
3. Enter your **Email** (id: 3)
	* A field where you can enter your email address.
4. Enter your **Phone** (id: 4)
	* A field where you can enter your phone number.
5. Enter your **Address** (id: 5)
	* A text field where you can enter your current or desired address.
6. If applicable, enter your **Rental History** (id: 6)
	* A text field where you can briefly describe your rental history.
7. Enter your **Desired Move-in Date** (id: 7)
	* A field where you can select a date for your desired move-in date.
8. If applicable, enter your **Rental Agent Name** (id: 8)
	* A text field where you can enter your rental agent's name.
9. If applicable, enter your **Rental Agent Phone** (id: 9)
	* A field where you can enter your rental agent's phone number.
10. If applicable, enter your **Rental Agent Email** (id: 10)
	* A field where you can enter your rental agent's email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (id: 1): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (id: 2): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (id: 3): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (id: 4): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (id: 5): Enter your current or desired address.
* **Rental History** (id: 6): Briefly describe your rental history, if applicable.
* **Desired Move-in Date** (id: 7): Select a date for your desired move-in date.
* **Rental Agent Name** (id: 8): Enter your rental agent's name, if applicable.
* **Rental Agent Phone** (id: 9): Enter your rental agent's phone number, if applicable.
* **Rental Agent Email** (id: 10): Enter your rental agent's email address, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are accurate and truthful.
* If you are working with a rental agent, please have them fill out their information as well.
* If you have any rental history, please briefly describe it in the "Rental History" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the real estate agent or the form administrator.
