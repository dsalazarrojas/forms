# Offer To Purchase Real Estate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Offer To Purchase Real Estate Form is designed to collect essential information for real estate transactions, including property details, buyer and seller information, price, and terms of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Use the drop-down menus to select the correct options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property being sold or bought.
* **Buyer Info**: Select either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the buyer is a real estate agent or not.
* **Buyer Name**: Enter the name of the buyer.
* **Seller Info**: Select either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the seller is a real estate agent or not.
* **Seller Name**: Enter the name of the seller.
* **Date Of Purchase Offer**: Enter the date of the purchase offer in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 01/01/2022).
* **Purchase Price**: Enter the price of the purchase in numbers (e.g., 200000).
* **Purchase Price Currency**: Select the currency of the purchase price (e.g., USD, CAD, EUR, JPY).
* **Terms And Conditions**: Enter any additional terms or conditions of the purchase agreement.
* **Buyer Email**: Enter the email address of the buyer.
* **Buyer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the buyer.
* **Buyer Address**: Enter the address of the buyer.

Note: Some fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will ensure a smooth and efficient real estate transaction process.
