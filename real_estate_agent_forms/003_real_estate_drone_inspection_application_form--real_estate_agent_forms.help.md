# real_estate_drone_inspection_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect data for a real estate drone inspection application. The purpose of this form is to gather relevant information for a one-time inspection of a property using a drone. This information will be used to assess the property's condition and identify potential areas for improvement or repairs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "real_estate_drone_inspection" page from the form's page selection.
2. Fill in the "inspection_date" field with the date you plan to conduct the inspection.
3. Enter the "inspection_location" field with the address of the property to be inspected.
4. Enter the "property_owner_name" field with the name of the property owner.
5. Enter the "property_address" field with the address of the property to be inspected.
6. Enter the "inspection_schedule" field with the schedule of the inspection, if any.
7. Enter the "inspection_time" field with the start and end times of the inspection.
8. Enter the "inspection_duration" field with the estimated time the inspection will take.
9. Enter the "drone_model" field with the model of the drone you will be using for the inspection.
10. Enter the "drone_pilot" field with the name of the pilot conducting the inspection.
11. Enter the "drone_serial_number" field with the serial number of the drone.
