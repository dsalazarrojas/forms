## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **inspection_date**: 
	+ (required/optional: false) 
	+ Enter the date you plan to conduct the inspection in the "mm/dd/yyyy" format.
* **inspection_location**: 
	+ (required/optional: false) 
	+ Enter the address of the property to be inspected.
* **property_owner_name**: 
	+ (required/optional: false) 
	+ Enter the name of the property owner.
* **property_address**: 
	+ (required/optional: false) 
	+ Enter the address of the property to be inspected.
* **inspection_schedule**: 
	+ (required/optional: false) 
	+ Enter the schedule of the inspection, if any.
* **inspection_time**: 
	+ (required/optional: false) 
	+ Enter the start and end times of the inspection in the "hh:mm AM/PM" format.
* **inspection_duration**: 
	+ (required/optional: false) 
	+ Enter the estimated time the inspection will take.
* **drone_model**: 
	+ (required/optional: false) 
	+ Enter the model of the drone you will be using for the inspection.
* **drone_pilot**: 
	+ (required/optional: false) 
	+ Enter the name of the pilot conducting the inspection.
* **drone_serial_number**: 
	+ (required/optional: false) 
	+ Enter the serial number of the drone.

## Tips

* Ensure you have the necessary permissions and authorizations to inspect the property and collect data.
* Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
