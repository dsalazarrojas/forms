# Real Estate Broker Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from real estate broker applicants, including their past work experience, educational background, professional certifications, language skills, references, and employment status. This information will help evaluate their eligibility and suitability for a real estate broker's license.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read each section and ensure you understand its relevance to your application.
- Fill in all the required fields, indicated by the "required" label.
- For optional fields, provide as much relevant information as possible to strengthen your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **1. Real_Estate_Broker_Application_Form**
Label: Real Estate Broker Application Form
Type: text
Required: false
This field is the title of the application form. You can enter your real estate broker application form name here.

### **2. past_work_experience**
Label: past_work_experience
Type: text
Required: false
Please describe your past work experience in this field. This will help us understand your background and qualifications.

### **3. education_history**
Label: education_history
Type: text
Required: false
List your education history, including institutions attended and degrees earned. This will help us evaluate your academic qualifications.

### **4. real_estate_license**
Label: real_estate_license
Type: select_one
Required: false
Select your real estate license status from the options provided:
- Real Estate License
- None
- Other

### **5. years_of_experience**
Label: years_of_experience
Type: number
Required: false
Please enter the number of years you have been working as a real estate broker.

### **6. certifications**
Label: certifications
Type: select_multiple
Required: false
List your real estate-related certifications, if any:
- Broker
- Agent
- Other

### **7. languages_spoken**
Label: languages_spoken
Type: select_multiple
Required: false
Indicate your spoken languages, if applicable:
- English
- Spanish
- French
- Mandarin
- Other

### **8. references**
Label: references
Type: text
Required: false
Provide 2 professional references, including their name, job title, and contact information.

### **9. criminal_history**
Label: criminal_history
Type: select_one
Required: false
Please indicate your criminal history status:
- True
- False

### **10. employment_status**
Label: employment_status
Type: select_one
Required: false
Select your current employment status:
- Employed
- Self-Employed
- Retired
- Other

## Tips
- Ensure you complete all required fields.
- Provide accurate and truthful information.
- Attach supporting documentation, if necessary.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
