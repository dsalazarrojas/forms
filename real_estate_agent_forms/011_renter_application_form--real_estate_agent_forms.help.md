# Renter Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Renter Application Form is a document designed to collect information from individuals seeking to rent a property. This form is intended for property owners or managers to gather essential details about potential renters in order to assess their eligibility and suitability for renting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Renter Information" page to start.
2. Fill in the required information, such as your name, email, and phone number.
3. Move on to the "Contact Details" page to provide your address, city, and state.
4. Complete the "Renter Details" page with your rental history, employment status, and income information.
5. On the "Property Information" page, provide the address and details about the property you're interested in renting.
6. Review the "Lease Terms" page to understand the lease agreement conditions.
7. Finally, sign and date the "Renter Signature" and "Property Manager Signature" pages to confirm your understanding of the lease terms and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Renter Information** (Renter Information, text, required):
	+ Enter your name, email, and phone number to ensure we can contact you about your rental application.
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, required):
	+ Provide your address, city, and state so we can verify your contact information.
* **Renter Details** (Renter Details, text, required):
	+ Share your rental history, employment status, and income information to help us evaluate your rental application.
* **Property Information** (Property Information, text, required):
	+ Enter the address and details of the property you're interested in renting.
* **Lease Terms** (Lease Terms, text, required):
	+ Review the lease agreement conditions before signing.
* **Renter Signature** (Renter Signature, note, required):
	+ Sign and date this page to confirm you've read and understand the lease terms.
* **Property Manager Signature** (Property Manager Signature, note, required):
	+ Sign and date this page to confirm you've reviewed and agreed on the lease terms.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure your application is processed correctly.
- Double-check your contact and property information for accuracy.
- Review the lease terms carefully before signing.
- Ensure you have a clear understanding of the lease agreement before signing.
