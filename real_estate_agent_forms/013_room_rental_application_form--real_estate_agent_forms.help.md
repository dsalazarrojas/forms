# Room Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for landlords and renters to collaborate on the terms of a room rental agreement. It gathers necessary information to establish a mutual understanding between both parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Landlord Info**: Enter your contact information and any additional details you'd like to share with the renter.
2. **Renter Info**: Provide your personal details and any relevant information about your renter status.
3. **Rent Details**: Enter the rent amount, rent frequency, and any other terms of the rental agreement.
4. **Security Deposit**: Specify the security deposit amount and any details about its use.
5. **Lease Terms**: Describe the lease duration, rent renewal options, and any other terms of the agreement.
6. **Renter Status**: Indicate your renter status, such as whether you are a student, professional, or other.
7. **Guarantor Info**: If applicable, provide information about your guarantor or co-signer.
8. **Property Info**: Enter any relevant information about the property, such as its address and amenities.
9. **Move In Info**: Specify the move-in date and any details about the move-in process.
10. **Renter Signature**: Date and sign the form as the renter.
11. **Landlord Signature**: Date and sign the form as the landlord.
12. **Date Completed**: Enter the date the form was completed.
13. **Date Signed**: Enter the date the form was signed by the landlord.
14. **Date of Renter Signature**: Enter the date the renter signed the form.
15. **Date of Landlord Signature**: Enter the date the landlord signed the form.
