# Buyers Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your property needs and preferences to help the real estate agent provide you with a more efficient and personalized search experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
* Enter your contact email address, which will be used for communications.
* If you have a preferred contact phone number, please enter it. This can be your mobile or a landline.
* Select an approximate budget range for your property search.
* Choose the type of property you are looking for (e.g. Single Family, Condo, Townhouse, etc.).
* If you're looking for a property with a specific number of bedrooms, enter the minimum number you require.
* If you have any specific neighborhoods or areas in mind, list them in order of preference.
* Check if you have any must-have features for your property (e.g. garage, yard, etc.).
* Indicate if you have financing preapproval in place.
* Choose the expected timeframe for your purchase.
* Finally, provide any additional comments or information that might be helpful for the agent to know.
