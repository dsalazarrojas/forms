# open_house_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about our open house events. The feedback form helps us understand what they liked most about the event, their contact information, and any additional comments they may have. Please complete this form to provide us with your feedback and help us improve our future events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) after each label. These fields are necessary for us to process your feedback.
2. Enter your answer to each question in the corresponding field.
3. For the "Additional Feedback" field, you may provide as much detail as you like, but keep in mind that this is optional.
4. Once you have completed all fields, click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the most impressive feature of the open house?** (`feedback`, text, *required*): 
	+ Please describe the feature that impressed you the most during the open house event. This could be a presentation, a display, or any other aspect that caught your attention.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, email, *required*): 
	+ Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Name** (`contact_name`, text, *required*): 
	+ Please enter your name so we can address our response to you correctly.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, *required*): 
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, *required*): 
	+ Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Date of Event** (`event_date`, date, *required*): 
	+ Select the date when the open house event took place.
* **Time of Event** (`event_time`, time, *required*): 
	+ Select the time when the open house event occurred.
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, note, *optional*): 
	+ If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them here. This is a great opportunity to provide more detailed feedback and help us improve our events.
