# Real Estate Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Survey form is designed to collect information about a customer's properties, expectations, and pricing. This form will help us better understand the customer's needs and provide a more personalized and efficient service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling out the form in the order of the sections provided.
2. Ensure each field is filled out thoroughly and accurately reflects the customer's situation.
3. Review each field explanation below for more guidance on how to fill out each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (Customer Name)** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the customer's full name, as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the customer's email address, if applicable. This field is not required but is helpful for communication purposes.
* **Customer Phone (Customer Phone)** (`customer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the customer's phone number in the format of XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Customer Address (Customer Address)** (`customer_address`, `text`, required): Please enter the customer's address, including house number and street name.
* **Survey Questions (Survey Questions)** (`survey_questions`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable options from the list provided. This field is used to gauge the customer's preferences and needs.
* **Customer Expectations (Customer Expectations)** (`customer_expectations`, `text`, required): Describe the customer's expectations for the service, including any specific requirements or concerns.
* **Customer Pricing (Customer Pricing)** (`customer_pricing`, `number`, required): Enter the customer's budget or pricing expectations for the service. This field should be a numerical value.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when filling out each field.
* If unsure about a field, refer back to the section on how to complete the form or ask your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
* Remember to review each field explanation to ensure you understand its purpose before filling it out.
* If you encounter any issues or have concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us for support.
