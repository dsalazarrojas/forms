# real_estate_project_appointment_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_project_appointment_request form is designed to gather information from users who want to schedule meetings or meetings with real estate professionals or agents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required contact information on Page 1.
2. Provide your schedule information on Page 1.
3. Add any additional project details on Page 1.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### contact_info (`contact_info`, text, optional)
* **Contact Information (contact_info)**: Enter your name, email, and phone number to help us get in touch with you.

### schedule_info (`schedule_info`, text, optional)
* **Schedule Information (schedule_info)**: Please provide your available schedule for the meeting, including dates and times.

### project_info (`project_info`, text, optional)
* **Project Information (project_info)**: Share any relevant details about your real estate project, such as the type of property, location, or expected budget.

### additional_info (`additional_info`, text, optional)
* **Additional Information (additional_info)**: If you have any other details or questions related to your project, please include them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your schedule information for accuracy.
* Provide clear and concise details about your project to help us understand your needs better.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
