# Tenant Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Screening Form is designed to collect relevant information from potential tenants to screen them for eligibility to rent a property. This form is intended for residential or commercial rental applications and should only be used for this purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Tenant Screening Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Application Information" section with your name, contact information, and other relevant details about your rental application.
2. In the "Contact Information" section, provide your contact information to ensure that we can get in touch with you if needed.
3. The "Rental History" section is where you should provide any relevant information about your past rental experiences, including the address, lease duration, and any outstanding debts or issues.
4. In the "Landlord Inquiries" section, provide information about your past landlords, including their name, contact information, and any issues you had with them.
5. In the "Rental Application Details" section, provide information about the property you are interested in renting, including the address, lease duration, and any other relevant details.
6. In the "Tenant Information" section, provide any relevant information about yourself that is not already covered in the previous sections, such as any past rental history or evictions.
7. Finally, in the "Property Information" section, provide any details about the property you are interested in renting, such as its address, size, and any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Information** (`application_information`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide any information about your rental application that you think is relevant, such as your name and contact information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide your contact information, such as your phone number and email address, so that we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Rental History** (`rental_history`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide any relevant information about your past rental experiences, including the address, lease duration, and any outstanding debts or issues.
* **Landlord Inquiries** (`landlord_inquiries`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide information about your past landlords, including their name, contact information, and any issues you had with them.
* **Rental Application Details** (`rental_application_details`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide information about the property you are interested in renting, including the address, lease duration, and any other relevant details.
* **Tenant Information** (`tenant_info`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide any relevant information about yourself that is not already covered in the previous sections, such as any past rental history or evictions.
* **Property Information** (`property_info`, `text`, optional): This section is for you to provide any details about the property you are interested in renting, such as its address, size, and any other relevant information.
