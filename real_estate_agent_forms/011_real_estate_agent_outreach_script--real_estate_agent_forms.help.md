# real-estate-agent-outreach-script - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by real estate agents to create an outreach script for potential leads who have shown interest in buying or selling properties. The information collected helps agents tailor their approach and follow up with the leads effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question accurately and honestly to provide the most relevant information about your lead.
2. Choose the correct options for each field based on your preferences and needs.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info**: Select 'Yes' if you have a preferred method of contact (phone, email, or in-person), or 'No' if you don't have a preferred method. This helps the agent know how to best reach out to you.
* **Preferred Communication Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, or in-person). This way, the agent knows your preferred communication style.
* **Lead Source**: Enter the source of your interest in the property, such as a friend, online search, or agent referral.
* **Property Preferences**: Select 'Yes' for specific property features you're looking for (e.g. location, type, price range), or 'No' if none of these features matter to you.
* **Budget**: Enter the maximum budget you are willing to spend on the property.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select 'Yes' if you have a preferred method of contact (phone, email, or in-person), similar to the 'Contact Info' question.
* **Call Schedule**: Enter a date when you'd like to be called by the agent, in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Meeting Schedule**: Enter a time when you'd like to have a meeting with the agent, in the format 'HH:MM'.

## Tips
* Be specific and accurate when answering each question to help the agent tailor their approach.
* Use the 'Contact Info' field to indicate your preferred contact method, so the agent knows how to best reach out to you.
* If you have any other questions or requests, be sure to mention them in the comments section or by contacting the agent directly.
