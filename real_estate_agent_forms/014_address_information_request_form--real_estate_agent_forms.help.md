# Address Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect address information from clients on behalf of real estate agents or agencies. It is used to request specific types of address information, such as current or previous addresses, mailing addresses, property deed addresses, or tax assessor records. The form helps real estate professionals gather accurate and up-to-date information from clients to facilitate property transactions, due diligence, and other related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, real estate license number, and real estate agency or company name as the requester.
2. Provide your professional email address and phone number for communication.
3. Enter the client's name and property address.
4. Specify the type of address information needed (e.g., current address, previous addresses, etc.).
5. Choose the purpose of the request (e.g., property transaction, due diligence, etc.).
6. Select the date range for the address records needed (if applicable).
7. Confirm whether you have written consent from the client to request this information.
8. Upload any supporting documents required for the request (if applicable).
9. Indicate the urgency level for the request (e.g., standard, rush, emergency).
10. Choose your preferred contact method for communication.
11. Add any additional notes or comments about the request.
12. Select the request date.
13. Sign the form electronically to confirm the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`): Enter your full name as a real estate agent or representative.
* **Real Estate License Number** (`requester_license`): Enter your professional real estate license number.
* **Real Estate Company** (`requester_company`): Enter the name of your real estate agency or company.
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`): Enter your professional email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`requester_phone`): Enter your professional phone number for communication.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`): Enter the name of the client whose address information is requested.
* **Property Address** (`client_property_address`): Enter the address of the property in question.
* **Property City** (`client_property_city`): Enter the city of the property.
* **Property State** (`client_property_state`): Enter the state of the property.
* **Property ZIP Code** (`client_property_zip`): Enter the ZIP code of the property.
* **Type of Address Information Requested** (`requested_address_type`): Select the type of address information needed (e.g., current address, previous addresses, etc.).
* **Purpose of Request** (`request_purpose`): Choose the purpose of the request (e.g., property transaction, due diligence, etc.).
* **Date Range for Records** (`request_date_range`): Enter the date range for the address records needed (if applicable).
* **Client Consent** (`client_consent`): Confirm whether you have written consent from the client to request this information.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`): Upload any supporting documents required for the request (if applicable).
* **Request Urgency** (`urgency_level`): Indicate the urgency level for the request (e.g., standard, rush, emergency).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`): Choose your preferred contact method for communication.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): Add any additional comments or notes about the request.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`): Enter the date of submitting this request.
* **Requester Signature** (`requester_signature`): Sign the form electronically to confirm the request.
* **Supervisor Approval** (`supervisor_approval`): Confirm whether your supervisor has approved this request (if applicable).

Note: This form requires electronic signatures to confirm the request, ensuring that all parties are aware of the information requested and the purpose of the request.
