# Mortgage Payoff Statement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mortgage Payoff Statement Request Form is used to request a mortgage payoff statement for a customer's loan. This form is designed to gather necessary information to facilitate the process of obtaining the statement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter the customer's address in the "Customer Address" field.
3. Select the date of sale from the calendar or enter it manually in the "Date of Sale" field.
4. Enter the loan number, which can be found on the loan agreement or mortgage statement.
5. Select the type of property from the options provided in the "Property Type" field.
6. Enter the loan amount, which is the outstanding balance of the loan.
7. Enter the name of the lender who issued the loan.
8. Enter the name of the contact person, which can be the loan officer or representative.
9. Enter the contact number of the contact person.
10. Enter the email address of the contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (Customer Name, text, required: false)**: Enter the customer's name as it appears on their loan agreement or mortgage statement.
* **Customer Address (Customer Address, text, required: false)**: Enter the customer's address where the loan is secured.
* **Date of Sale (Date of Sale, date, required: false)**: Select the date the loan was sold or entered into.
* **Loan Number (Loan Number, text, required: false)**: Enter the unique number assigned to the loan.
* **Property Type (Property Type, select_one, required: false)**: Select the type of property associated with the loan (House, Condo, or Townhouse).
* **Loan Amount (Loan Amount, number, required: false)**: Enter the outstanding balance of the loan.
* **Lender Name (Lender Name, text, required: false)**: Enter the name of the lender who issued the loan.
* **Contact Person (Contact Person, text, required: false)**: Enter the name of the contact person for the loan.
* **Contact Number (Contact Number, text, required: false)**: Enter the contact number of the contact person.
* **Email (Email, email, required: false)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the loan payoff statement is generated correctly.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, you can contact the customer service department for assistance.
* This form is designed to be used by authorized personnel within the organization, and the customer's information should not be duplicated or submitted through other means.
