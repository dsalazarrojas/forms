# real_estate_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for real estate agents and property managers to gather and manage contact information for various properties. It is used to track properties' statuses, features, and contact methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for "Home Search", "Contact Information", "Property Types", and "Property Status" to indicate whether the property is currently for sale or rent.
2. Choose the contact methods that apply to the property, such as email, phone, or text message.
3. Fill in the location of the property.
4. Provide any additional information about the property in the "Agent Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Search**: Select "Yes" if the property is currently on the market, "No" if it is not.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the contact information for the property, such as email, phone number, or text message.
* **Property Types**: Select the types of properties that apply, such as "Yes" for "Yes" options, or "No" for "No" options.
* **Property Status**: Select the status of the property, "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Location**: Enter the location of the property.
* **Contact Method**: Select the contact methods that apply, such as "Yes" for "Yes" options, or "No" for "No" options.
* **Agent Information**: Provide any additional information about the property, such as the agent's name or contact number.
* **Property Types 1-10**: Select the specific types of properties that apply, such as single-family home, condo, or commercial building.
* **Contact Method 1-6**: Select the specific contact methods that apply, such as email, phone, or text message.

## Tips
This form is for internal use only, and the information collected will be used to manage and track properties. Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and consistently.
