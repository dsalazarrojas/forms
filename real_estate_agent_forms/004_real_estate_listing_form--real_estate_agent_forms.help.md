# Real Estate Listing Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Real Estate Listing Form is a tool for listing properties for sale or rent. It helps you collect and organize property information to share with potential clients. Use this form to quickly and accurately provide property details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**, including all fields.
2. **Choose the correct property type** from the options provided.
3. **Enter the property address** in the designated field.
4. **Provide a detailed description** of the property.
5. **Specify the property price** in the designated field.
6. **Enter the seller's name** in the designated field.
7. **Enter the seller's email** in the correct format (e.g. [sellername@example.com](mailto:sellername@example.com)).
8. **Enter the seller's phone number** in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Type**: Choose the type of property you are listing (residential, commercial, industrial).
* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property in the format "123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345".
* **Property Description**: Provide a detailed description of the property, including features and amenities.
* **Property Price**: Enter the price of the property in the designated format (e.g. $200,000).
* **Seller Name**: Enter the name of the person selling the property.
* **Seller Email**: Enter the email address of the seller, in the format "sellername@example.com".
* **Seller Phone**: Enter the phone number of the seller in the format "123-456-7890".
