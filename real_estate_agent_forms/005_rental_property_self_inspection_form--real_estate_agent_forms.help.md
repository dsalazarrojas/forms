# Rental Property Self Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the condition and status of a rental property after inspection. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the address of the property in the "Property Address" field.
2. Select the current status of the property ("Occupied", "Vacant", or "Pending") in the "Property Status" field.
3. Enter the date of the inspection in the "Date of Inspection" field, using the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
4. Enter the results of the inspection in the "Inspection Result" field.
5. Add any additional notes about the property in the "Notes" field.
6. Select the type of property being inspected ("House", "Condo", or "Apartment") in the "Property Type" field.
7. Enter the name of the contact person for the property in the "Contact Name" field.
8. Enter the email of the contact person in the "Contact Email" field.
9. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "Contact Phone" field.
10. Select the member of the inspection team who performed the inspection in the "Inspection Team Member" field.
11. Enter the location of the inspection in the "Inspection Location" field.
12. Select the date and time of the inspection in the "Inspection Date" and "Inspection Time" fields.
13. Add any additional comments about the property in the "Comments" field.
14. Enter any additional comments that were not previously mentioned in the "Additional Comments" field.
15. Enter the contact number of the contact person in the "Contact Number" field.
16. Sign and date the "Agent Signature" field.
17. Enter your initial in the "Agent Initials" field.
18. Enter the ID of the property being inspected in the "Property ID" field.
19. Enter the number of the property being inspected in the "Property Number" field.
20. Enter the street address of the property being inspected in the "Property Street" field.
21. Enter the street address of the contact person in the "Contact Street" field.
22. Enter the city of the contact person in the "Contact City" field.
23. Enter the state of the contact person in the "Contact State" field.
24. Enter the ZIP code of the contact person in the "Contact Zip" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Address**: This is the address of the rental property being inspected.
  * Enter the full address of the property, including street, city, state, and ZIP code.
  * This field is not required but recommended.
* **Property Status**: Select the current status of the property.
  * "Occupied": The property is currently occupied by a tenant.
  * "Vacant": The property is vacant.
  * "Pending": The property is pending sale or lease.
  * This field is not required but recommended.
* **Date of Inspection**: Enter the date of the inspection in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
  * This field is not required but recommended.
* **Inspection Result**: Enter the results of the inspection, including any findings or issues.
  * This field is not required but recommended.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the property.
  * This field is not required but recommended.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property being inspected.
  * "House": A single-family home.
  * "Condo": A condominium.
  * "Apartment": A multi-unit apartment building.
  * This field is not required but recommended.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
  * This field is not required but recommended.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email of the contact person.
  * This field is not required but recommended.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
  * This field is not required but recommended.
* **Inspection Team Member**: Select the member of the inspection team who performed the inspection.
  * This field is not required but recommended.
* **Inspection Location**: Enter the location of the inspection.
  * This field is not required but recommended.
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
  * This field is not required but recommended.
* **Inspection Time**: Enter the time of the inspection.
  * This field is not required but recommended.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the property.
  * This field is not required but recommended.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments that were not previously mentioned.
  * This field is not required but recommended.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the contact person.
  * This field is not required but recommended.
* **Agent Signature**: Sign and date the inspection report.
