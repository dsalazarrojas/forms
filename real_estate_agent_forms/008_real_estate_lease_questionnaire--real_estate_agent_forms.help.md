# Real Estate Lease Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a lease agreement between a landlord and a tenant. It helps gather essential details for real estate professionals to manage and track lease-related data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read the form carefully and understand the purpose.
* Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
* Make sure to sign the form at the end to acknowledge that you have completed and reviewed the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **lease_property_address** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the lease property, including the street number and apartment number (if applicable).
* **tenant_info** (`text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description about the tenant, including their contact information and any relevant details.
* **start_date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the start date of the lease in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **end_date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the end date of the lease in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **lease_term** (`text`, required/optional): Describe the terms of the lease, including any specific conditions or restrictions.
* **rent** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the monthly rent amount for the lease.
* **security_deposit** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the security deposit amount, if applicable.
* **tenant_signature** (`text`, required/optional): Sign the form to acknowledge that you have reviewed and completed the information provided.
