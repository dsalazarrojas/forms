# real_estate_agent_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_agent_feedback_form is a survey designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with a real estate agent. This feedback is used to help improve the agent's performance and provide better services to future clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please enter your name in the `agent_name` field.
2. Share your feedback about your experience with the agent in the `feedback` field.
3. If you have a contact number, please enter it in the `contact_number` field.
4. Provide the date of your feedback in the `feedback_date` field.
5. If you have a rating, select one from 1-5 in the `rating` field.
6. You can leave a message if you have any additional comments or feedback in the `message` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agent Name (1)**: Enter your name to be associated with the feedback.
* **Feedback (2)**: Provide a short description of your experience with the agent. 
* **Email Address (3)**: Enter your email address if you have one.
* **Rating (4)**: Rate the agent's performance from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Contact Number (6)**: Enter your contact number if you would like to be contacted by the agent or office.
* **Message (5)**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the agent.
* **Feedback Date (7)**: Enter the date of your feedback.
* **Feedback Time (8)**: Enter the time of your feedback.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you rate the agent, please base it on your actual experience.
* You can skip any fields you don't have or don't want to provide.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to reach out to us.
