# Uniform Residential Loan Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Uniform Residential Loan Application Form is a comprehensive questionnaire designed to gather essential details from borrowers to assess their eligibility for a loan. It covers various aspects of their personal loan information, employment, financial history, loan purpose, other assets, loan history, and additional details they may wish to provide.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Carefully read through each page and fill out the relevant sections**. Each page is labeled with a specific category to help you navigate the form.
2. **Ensure you fill out all required fields**, as indicated by the "required" label. These are crucial for the assessment process.
3. **Be honest and accurate in your responses**. Provide truthful and reliable information to ensure a smooth and effective evaluation.
4. **Use the provided text boxes to write your answers**. If you need to attach any supporting documents or extra information, you can do so in the designated spaces.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Loan Information (<name: personal_loan_info>)**: This section is where you provide general information about your loan needs, such as the loan amount and type. Please be specific about the purpose of your loan.
* **Employment Information (<name: employment_info>)**: This section is for you to share your employment details, including your job title, employer, and income. Please ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Financial Information (<name: financial_info>)**: In this section, you will be asked about your financial resources, including your income, savings, and debts. Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* **Loan Purpose (<name: loan_purpose>)**: This field is essential for us to understand the reason for your loan. Please explain the purpose of your loan in a clear and concise manner.
* **Other Assets (<name: other_assets>)**: If you have other assets you'd like to mention, such as investments or valuable possessions, this section is for you.
* **Loan History (<name: loan_history>)**: Share any relevant information about your past loan experiences or financial transactions. This will help us understand your creditworthiness.
* **Other Information (<name: other_info>)**: If there is any other relevant information you believe would be helpful for the evaluation process, please provide it here.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
- Provide supporting documents or attachments if necessary.
- Be patient and allow the evaluation process to complete.
