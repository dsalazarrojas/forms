# property_management_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The property management survey is designed to collect feedback from clients about their experience with the property management company. This form will help the company understand their strengths and weaknesses, and use this information to improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. Provide honest and detailed responses to help the company improve their services.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, but do not feel obligated to answer any question that you are not comfortable with.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Survey**: This is the introduction to the survey, please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Rate Your Community**: This question is to gauge your overall satisfaction with the community management. Please select the option that best reflects your experience.
* **Community Rating**: This question is to rate your experience with the community management.
* **Rate Your Property**: This question is to rate your experience with the property management.
* **Property Rating**: This question is to rate your experience with the property management services.
* **Rate Your Manager**: This question is to rate your experience with the property manager.
* **Manager Rating**: This question is to rate your experience with the property manager.
* **Rate Your Neighborhood**: This question is to rate your experience with the neighborhood.
* **Neighborhood Rating**: This question is to rate your experience with the neighborhood.
* **How Likely to Recommend**: This question is to ask how likely you are to recommend the property management company to others.
* **Why Didn't Recommend**: This question is to provide a brief explanation on why you didn't recommend the property management company.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your experience with the property management company.
* **Contact Preferences**: This question is to ask about your preferred method of contact (email, phone, or in person).
* **Email Address**: This field is to provide your email address for communication.
* **Contact Method**: This question is to ask about your preferred method of contact (email, phone, or in person).
* **Best Contact Method**: This question is to ask about your best method of contact (email, phone, or in person).
* **Contact Hours**: This field is to provide your preferred hours of contact.
* **Contact Frequency**: This question is to ask about your preferred frequency of contact.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: This question is to ask about your preferred method of contact (email, phone, or in person).
* **Preferred Contact Hours**: This question is to ask about your preferred hours of contact.
* **How Did You Find Us**: This question is to ask about how you found the property management company.
* **Where Did You Find Us**: This question is to ask about where you found the property management company.
* **How Were You Treated**: This question is to ask about your experience with the customer service.
* **How Did We Do**: This question is to ask about your experience with the property management company's services.
* **Next Steps**: This field is to provide any additional comments or suggestions for the property management company.
