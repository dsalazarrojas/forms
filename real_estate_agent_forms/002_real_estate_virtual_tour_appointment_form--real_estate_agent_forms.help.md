# real_estate_virtual_tour_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule virtual tours for potential clients. It helps real estate agents to manage their schedule and keep track of clients who have shown interest in a property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all potential clients from the list who have shown interest in the property.
2. Provide additional information about the client(s) in the "Client Details" field.
3. Enter details about the appointment in the "Appointment Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Potential Clients**: <label> (Potential Clients, select_multiple, required: false): This field allows you to select all potential clients from the list who have shown interest in the property. Please choose all clients who have expressed interest.
* **Client Details <label> (Client Details, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the client(s) that is relevant to the appointment.
* **Appointment Details <label> (Appointment Details, text, required: false): Enter details about the appointment, such as date, time, and duration. This will help the agent to prepare for the virtual tour.
