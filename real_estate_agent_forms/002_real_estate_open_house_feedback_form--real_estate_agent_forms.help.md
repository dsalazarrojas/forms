# real_estate_open_house_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for agents to gather feedback and information from attendees after an open house event. It helps the agency to understand the visitor's experience and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date you visited the open house event in the "Open House Visit Date" field.
2. Choose the time you visited the open house event in the "Open House Visit Time" field.
3. Select the location of the open house event from the options provided in the "Open House Location" field.
4. Choose how you would like to be contacted for future open house events in the "Contact" field. Note that this field is required.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes about the event in the "Open House Feedback" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if you would like to be contacted by the agency for future events.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, if you would like to be contacted by the agency for future events.
8. Rate your experience at the event on a scale of 1-10 in the "Open House Rating" field.
9. Provide any additional comments or feedback about the event in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Open House Visit Date (id: 1)**: Enter the date you visited the open house event.
* **Open House Visit Time (id: 2)**: Choose the time you visited the open house event.
* **Open House Location (id: 3)**: Select the location of the open house event from the options provided.
* **Contact (id: 4)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted for future open house events. This field is required.
* **Notes (id: 5)**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the event.
* **Real Estate Agent Name (id: 6)**: Enter the name of the real estate agent who led the open house event. This field is not required.
* **Open House Feedback (id: 7)**: Provide any feedback or comments about the event.
* **Email (id: 8)**: Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted by the agency for future events.
* **Phone Number (id: 9)**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by the agency for future events.
* **Open House Rating (id: 10)**: Rate your experience at the event on a scale of 1-10.
* **Comments (id: 11)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the event.
