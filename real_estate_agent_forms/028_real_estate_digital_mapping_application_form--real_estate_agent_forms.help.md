# Real Estate Digital Mapping Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for real estate agents to provide detailed information about properties they would like to list on our digital mapping application. The form is used to collect relevant data about the property, including its address, type, size, price, and location, as well as contact information and project details. The purpose of this form is to ensure that all required information is accurately captured and easily accessible for our team to process and update the property listings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the property address in the "property_address" field.
2. Select the correct property type from the "property_type" dropdown menu.
3. Enter any additional details about the property in the "property_details" field.
4. Enter the name of the contact person in the "contact_name" field.
5. Enter the email address of the contact person in the "contact_email" field.
6. (Optional) Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "contact_phone" field.
7. Enter the size of the property in the "property_size" field.
8. Enter the price of the property in the "property_price" field.
9. If applicable, upload any supporting documents in the "upload_file" field.
10. Enter a detailed description of the project in the "project_details" field.
11. Enter the location of the project in the "project_location" field.
12. Select the project status from the "project_status" dropdown menu.
13. Click the "submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **property_address**: Enter the address of the property you would like to list.
* **property_type**: Select the type of property (e.g. house, apartment, etc.).
* **property_details**: Enter any additional details about the property (e.g. amenities, features, etc.).
* **contact_name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **contact_email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **contact_phone**: (Optional) Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **property_size**: Enter the size of the property (e.g. square feet).
* **property_price**: Enter the price of the property (e.g. monetary value).
* **supporting_documents**: If applicable, upload any supporting documents for the property.
* **project_details**: Enter a detailed description of the project (e.g. construction plans, etc.).
* **project_location**: Enter the location of the project.
* **project_status**: Select the status of the project (e.g. Active, Inactive, etc.).
* **upload_file**: (Optional) Upload any supporting documents for the property.
* **submit**: Click this button to send the form and complete the application.
