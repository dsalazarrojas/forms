The goal of the "Notice Of Entry Form" is to provide a straightforward and efficient way to record essential information regarding the entry or presence of a tenant or agent in a rental property. This form serves as a tool to gather details such as the tenant/landlord names, entry date, property address, and other relevant specifics. Ensuring the information is accurate and up-to-date can facilitate smooth and organized record-keeping, communication, and decision-making for all parties involved.

To complete this form, users should follow the steps below:

1.  Ensure the form is properly filled out and accurate. While the form is not required, the provided information will be used for official purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, enter the name of the tenant in the "Tenant Name" field.
2.  Next, enter the name of the landlord in the "Landlord Name" field.
3.  Enter the date of entry in the "Entry Date" field in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
4.  Provide the address of the property in the "Address" field.
5.  If available, enter the unit number in the "Unit" field.
6.  Select the reason for the entry from the "Purpose of Entry" dropdown menu.
7.  Finally, provide the contact phone number of the person entering in the "Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Tenant Name (Tenant Name, text, required/optional):** Enter the name of the tenant.
*   **Landlord Name (Landlord Name, text, required/optional):** Enter the name of the landlord.
*   **Entry Date (Entry Date, date, required/optional):** Enter the date in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   **Address (Address, text, required/optional):** Enter the address of the property.
*   **Unit (Unit, text, required/optional):** If available, enter the unit number.
*   **Purpose of Entry (Purpose of Entry, select multiple, required/optional):** Select the reason for the entry from the provided options.
*   **Contact Phone (Contact Phone, text, required/optional):** Enter the contact phone number of the person entering.
