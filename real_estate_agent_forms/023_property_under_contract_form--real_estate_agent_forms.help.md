# Property Under Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the details of a property under contract, including its status, type, price, and buyer information. It helps real estate agents to track and manage properties that are currently under contract.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the sale status of the property from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose the type of property (residential, commercial, or other).
3. Enter the sale price of the property.
4. Fill out the buyer's name.
5. Enter the buyer's phone number.
6. Enter the buyer's email address.
7. Select the date of the sale.
8. Enter the location of the sale.
9. Fill out any additional sale terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Sale Status**: Select the current status of the property (Active, Pending, or Sold).
    * This field determines the property's status in the contract.
- **Sale Type**: Choose the type of property (Residential, Commercial, or Other).
    * This field categorizes the type of property under contract.
- **Sale Price**: Enter the price at which the property is for sale.
    * This field is used to store the sale price of the property.
- **Buyer Name**: Enter the name of the person buying the property.
    * This field stores the name of the buyer.
- **Buyer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the buyer.
    * This field stores the buyer's phone number.
- **Buyer Email**: Enter the email address of the buyer.
    * This field stores the buyer's email address.
- **Sale Date**: Select the date of the sale.
    * This field stores the date of the sale.
- **Sale Location**: Enter the location of the sale.
    * This field stores the location of the sale.
- **Sale Terms**: Enter any additional terms of the sale.
    * This field stores any additional terms or conditions of the sale.
