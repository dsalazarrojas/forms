# real_estate_marketing_strategy_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on your current marketing approach, desired marketing outcomes, and preferred marketing channels, messages, and frequency. It will help you assess your current marketing practices and preferences, and provide a starting point for understanding your marketing needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each section in the following order:

1. Introduction: Please read the introduction and familiarize yourself with the purpose of the questionnaire.
2. Current Marketing Approach: Describe your current marketing practices and strategies.
3. Desired Marketing Outcomes: Share your goals and objectives for your marketing efforts.
4. Current Social Media Usage: Report your current use of social media platforms.
5. Current Email Marketing Usage: Report your current use of email marketing.
6. Current Print Media Usage: Report your current use of print media.
7. Current Other Marketing Channels: Report your current use of other marketing channels.
8. Desired Marketing Channels: Select the marketing channels you would like to use in the future.
9. Preferred Marketing Messages: Share your preferred marketing messages.
10. Preferred Marketing Images: Share your preferred marketing images.
11. Current Client Relationships: Report your current client relationships.
12. Preferred Client Relationships: Describe your preferred client relationships.
13. Marketing Messages Frequency: Report your preferred frequency of marketing messages.
14. Email Marketing Frequency: Report your preferred frequency of email marketing messages.
15. Print Marketing Frequency: Report your preferred frequency of print marketing messages.
16. Social Media Marketing Frequency: Report your preferred frequency of social media marketing messages.
17. Other Marketing Frequency: Report your preferred frequency of other marketing messages.
18. Marketing Messages Content: Share your preferred content for marketing messages.
19. Marketing Message Style: Share your preferred style for marketing messages.
20. Preferred Language: Select the languages you prefer for marketing messages.
21. Additional Comments: Add any additional comments or notes.
22. Preferred Contact Method: Select your preferred methods of contact.
23. Preferred Contact Method 2: Select your preferred methods of contact (if different from preferred contact method).
24. Preferred Contact Method 3: Select your preferred methods of contact (if different from preferred contact method 1 and 2).
25. Notes: Add any final notes or comments.
