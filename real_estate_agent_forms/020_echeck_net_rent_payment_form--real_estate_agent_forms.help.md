# ECheck.Net Rent Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects contact and payment information for rent payments. It is used to process rent payments for properties or services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate information.
2. Ensure all fields are accurate and complete before submitting.
3. Double-check your information before finalizing your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your surname as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your current address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city of your current address.
* **ZIP** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the ZIP code of your current address.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state of your current address.
* **First Name (Authorization)** (`authorization_first_name`, text, optional): Enter the first name of the person authorizing this payment.
* **Last Name (Authorization)** (`authorization_last_name`, text, optional): Enter the last name of the person authorizing this payment.
* **Signature Date** (`authorization_sign_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of this payment authorization (e.g., 2022-01-01).

## Tips
* Ensure you have permission to access and process this form.
* Double-check your information before finalizing your submission.
* If you are an authorized agent, enter the correct authorization information.
* If you are unsure about any information, contact the property owner or manager.
