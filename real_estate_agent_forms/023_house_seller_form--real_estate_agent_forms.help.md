# house_seller_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for real estate agents to provide information about a house sale listing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the necessary listing information in the "Listing Info" section.
2. Provide details about the listing in the "Listing Details" section.
3. Update your contact information in the "Contact Info" section.
4. Add any relevant status updates in the "Status Update" section.
5. Select the type of seller and their contact method in the "Seller Info" and "Contact Method" sections.
6. Choose the listing status and location in the "Status" and "Location" sections.
7. Enter the listing and expiration dates in the "Listing Date" and "Expiration Date" sections.
8. Specify whether or not photos and documents are included with the listing.
9. Add any notes about the listing in the "Notes" section.
10. Enter the seller's email, phone number, and website in the "Email", "Phone", and "Website" sections.
11. Choose the real estate agency and real estate license type (if applicable).
12. Update the listing status and realtor name (if applicable).
13. Enter the real estate agency ID (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Listing Info** (`listing_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the listing.
* **Listing Details** (`listing_details`, text, optional): Provide more details about the listing.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Update your contact information.
* **Status Update** (`status_update`, note, optional): Add any relevant status updates about the listing.
* **Seller Info** (`seller_info`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of seller.
* **Contact Type** (`contact_type`, select_one, optional): Choose how you prefer to be contacted.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, text, optional): Enter your preferred contact method.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current listing status.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): Enter the listing price.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the listing.
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the location of the listing.
* **Listing Date** (`listing_date`, date, optional): Enter the listing date.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, optional): Enter the expiration date.
* **Photos** (`photos`, select_multiple, optional): Specify whether photos are included with the listing.
* **Documents** (`documents`, select_multiple, optional): Specify whether documents are included with the listing.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the listing.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter your website URL.
* **Realtor Name** (`realtor_name`, text, optional): Enter your realtor name.
* **Real Estate Agency** (`realestate_agency`, select_one, optional): Choose your real estate agency.
* **Real Estate License** (`realestate_license`, text, optional): Enter your real estate license number.
* **Listing Status Update** (`listing_status_update`, text, optional): Add any updates about the listing status.
* **Listing Status** (`listing_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current listing status.
* **Realtor Name** (`realtor_name`, text, optional): Enter the realtor name.
* **Real Estate Agency ID** (`realestate_agency_id`, text, optional): Enter the real estate agency ID.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have all the necessary information and permissions to fill out this form completely and accurately.
* Use the correct format for dates (MM/DD/YYYY).
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or contact the relevant party for clarification.
* If you need to provide additional information or updates, please use the "Notes" section.
* If you need to contact the seller or realtor, use the "Contact Method" and "Phone" or "Email" fields.
* If you need to update the listing status, please use the "Status Update" and "Listing Status" fields.
