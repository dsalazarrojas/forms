# Property Sentiment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Property Sentiment Survey is designed to capture the opinions and sentiments of real estate agents on the current state of the property market. This form will help the organization understand the trends, challenges, and opportunities in the market, providing valuable insights for strategic planning and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, based on your expertise and knowledge of the property market.
2. Select the most accurate options for each question from the provided choices.
3. If you have any additional comments or insights, feel free to provide them in the space provided on each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think is the current state of the property market?** (Page 1)
	+ This question is meant to gauge your overall perception of the current market situation.
	+ Please provide a brief written response in the text box.
* **How would you rate your confidence in the current property market?** (Page 2)
	+ This question asks for your level of confidence in the market's current state.
	+ Select one of the options provided, or provide a written response if you prefer.
* **What is your current property status?** (Page 3)
	+ This question inquires about your current situation or role in the property market.
	+ Choose one or more of the options provided.
* **How do you rate the current rent levels?** (Page 4)
	+ This question asks for your opinion on the current rent levels in the market.
	+ Enter a numerical value between 0 and 10, where 0 indicates very low and 10 indicates very high.
* **How do you rate the current sale prices?** (Page 5)
	+ This question inquires about your opinion on the current sale prices in the market.
	+ Enter a numerical value between 0 and 10, where 0 indicates very low and 10 indicates very high.
* **What do you think is the main reason for the changes in the property market?** (Page 6)
	+ This question asks for your analysis on the driving factors behind the market's current state.
	+ Provide a detailed written response in the space provided.
* **How do you feel about the current state of the property market?** (Page 7)
	+ This question inquires about your emotions and sentiments towards the market.
	+ Choose one or more of the options provided.
* **What are your expectations for the future of the property market?** (Page 8)
	+ This question asks for your predictions on the market's future trajectory.
	+ Enter a date in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **What do you think is the main driver for the changes in the property market?** (Page 9)
	+ This question asks for your analysis on the primary causes behind the market's current state.
	+ Enter a value representing the time period, e.g. "6 months", "1 year", etc.
* **Do you think the changes in the property market are positive or negative?** (Page 10)
	+ This question inquires about your overall view on the market's current state.
	+ Choose one or more of the options provided.

Please note that all questions are optional, and you may choose to answer some or all of them. Your input is invaluable, and your responses will help the organization gain valuable insights into the property market.
