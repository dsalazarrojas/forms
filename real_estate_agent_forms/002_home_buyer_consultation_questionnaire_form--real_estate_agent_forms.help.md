# Home Buyer Consultation Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from home buyers about their preferences and requirements for a new home. The information collected will be used to provide personalized recommendations and consultations to help them find the right property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select or enter the relevant information for each field. The form has several pages of questions, each with different types of fields (text, select_one, select_multiple, etc.). You can navigate between pages using the navigation buttons on the right.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (1)
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last Name (2)
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email (3)
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. This will be used for further communication.

### Phone (4)
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

### Preferred Language (5)
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred language for communication.

### Primary Residence (6)
* **Primary Residence** (`primary_residence`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you currently own or rent your primary residence.

### Home Type (7)
* **Home Type** (`home_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of home you are looking for (Single Family Home, Townhouse, Condo).

### Number of Beds (8)
* **Number of Beds** (`number_of_beds`, number, optional): Enter the number of bedrooms you prefer in your new home.

### Number of Bathrooms (9)
* **Number of Bathrooms** (`number_of_bathrooms`, number, optional): Enter the number of bathrooms you prefer in your new home.

### Square Feet (10)
* **Square Feet** (`square_feet`, number, optional): Enter the square footage you prefer in your new home.

### Preferred Location (11)
* **Preferred Location** (`preferred_location`, select_one, optional): Select the type of location you prefer (Rural, Urban, Suburban).

### Number of People (12)
* **Number of People** (`how_many_people`, number, optional): Enter the number of people who will be living in the home.

### Preferred Budget (13)
* **Preferred Budget** (`preferred_budget`, number, optional): Enter your preferred budget for the home.

### Preferred Price Range (14)
* **Preferred Price Range** (`preferred_price_range`, select_one, optional): Select the price range you prefer (Under 200k, 200k-400k, 400k-600k, 600k-1M, Over 1M).

### Preferred Commute (15)
* **Preferred Commute** (`preferred_commute`, select_one, optional): Select the type of commute you prefer (Short, Medium, Long).

### Primary Reason (16)
* **Primary Reason** (`primary_reason`, select_one, optional): Select the reason why you're looking for a home (Looking for a Home, Relocating, Other).

### Preferred School Districts (17)
* **Preferred School Districts** (`preferred_school_districts`, select_multiple, optional): Check the school districts you prefer.

### Preferred Commute Time (18)
* **Preferred Commute Time** (`preferred_commute_time`, select_one, optional): Select the preferred commute time (Less than 30 minutes, 30 minutes to 1 hour, 1-2 hours, More than 2 hours).

### Preferred Home Type (19)
* **Preferred Home Type** (`preferred_home_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of home you prefer (Single Family Home, Townhouse, Condo).

### Preferred Neighborhood (20)
* **Preferred Neighborhood** (`preferred_neighborhood`, select_one, optional): Check if you prefer a neighborhood with a pool or other features.

### Preferred Home Features (21)
* **Preferred Home Features** (`preferred_home_features`, select_multiple, optional): Check the features you prefer in a home (Parking, Pool, Other).

### Preferred Schools (22)
* **Preferred Schools** (`preferred_schools`, select_multiple, optional): Check the school districts you prefer.

### Preferred Community Amenities (23)
* **Preferred Community Amenities** (`preferred_community_amenities`, select_multiple, optional): Check the amenities you prefer in a community (Yes, No).

### Preferred Home Age (24)
* **Preferred Home Age** (`preferred_home_age`, select_one, optional): Select the age of the home you prefer (New, 5-10 years old, 10-20 years old, 20-30 years old).

### Preferred Neighborhood Characteristics (25)
* **Preferred Neighborhood Characteristics** (`preferred_neighborhood_characteristics`, select_multiple, optional): Check the neighborhood characteristics you prefer (Park or Playground, Shopping Mall, Other).

Please note that some fields are optional, but providing accurate and honest information will help us provide the best possible consultation and recommendations.
