# House Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The House Rental Application Form is a general-purpose form used by landlords or property managers to gather essential information from potential renters.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in your personal details in the **Applicant Information** section.
- Provide your contact information in the **Contact Information** section.
- Share your property requirements and preferences in the **Property Details** section.
- Review and sign the **Rental Agreement** section.
- Check the **Contact Request** box for how you'd like to be contacted.
- Fill in the **Rental Amount** and **Lease Term** fields if applicable.
- Select your preferred **Rental Frequency** and **Rent Frequency**.
- Choose your **Move In** and **Move Out** dates and times.
- Fill in any **Additional Comments** about your rental application.
- Choose your **Applicant Type** and **Contact Method** from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information**: Enter your name and other identifying details as per your preference.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact details for communication and follow-up purposes.
* **Property Details**: Share your requirements and preferences for the rental property.
* **Rental Agreement**: Review and sign the rental agreement form to confirm your understanding and commitment.
* **Contact Request**: Check the box for how you'd like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).
* **Rental Amount**: Enter the amount of money you're willing to pay as rent.
* **Lease Term**: Select the length of your rental agreement.
* **Rental Frequency**: Choose how often you'd like to rent.
* **Rent Frequency**: Select the frequency of your rent payments (e.g., weekly, monthly, or annually).
* **Move In Date**: Enter the date you'd like to move into the property.
* **Move In Time**: Enter the time you'd like to move into the property.
* **Move Out Date**: Enter the date you'd like to move out of the property.
* **Move Out Time**: Enter the time you'd like to move out of the property.
* **Additional Comments**: Share any additional information about your rental application.
* **Applicant Type**: Choose whether you're an individual, family, or company.
* **Contact Method**: Select the method you'd prefer to use to contact you.
* **Contact Request**: This field is not clearly defined, and it's recommended to be removed or rephrased for better user experience.
