# Home Matching Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Matching Request Form is designed to collect your personal details, property preferences, budget, and other relevant information to help us find your dream home.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Personal Details section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. In the Property Preferences section, select the features that you are looking for in your new home (e.g., Pool, Parking, View, etc.).
3. Enter your budget in the Budget section.
4. In the Schedule section, select a time of day when you are available to view properties.
5. Select your preferred contact method for follow-up communication.
6. Confirm that you have reviewed and understand the form data.
7. Finally, submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Details** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number to ensure we can contact you about your property search.
  • **Name**: Enter your full name.
  • **Email**: Enter your email address.
  • **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
- **Property Preferences** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): Select the features that you are looking for in your new home (e.g., Pool, Parking, View, etc.).
- **Budget** (`title`, `number`, required): Enter your budget for the property you are looking for.
- **Property Features** (`title`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple features from the list that you are looking for in your new home (e.g., Pool, Parking, View, etc.).
  • **Property Features**: Select all the features that you are interested in for your new home.
- **Additional Features** (`title`, `note`, required/optional): This field is not currently being used. However, you can enter any additional features or notes you would like to include in your form submission.
- **Schedule** (`title`, `select_one`, required): Select a time of day when you are available to view properties.
  • **Schedule**: Select a time of day that works best for you.
- **Follow up** (`title`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to be followed up (e.g., Yes, No).
  • **Follow up**: Select how you would like to be contacted.
- **Contact Method** (`title`, `select_multiple`, required): Select how you would like to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone, Message, etc.).
  • **Contact Method**: Select one or more methods of contact that work best for you.
- **Confirm** (`title`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have reviewed and understand the form data.
  • **Confirm**: Confirm that you have reviewed and understand the form.
- **Submission** (`title`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you are submitting the form.
  • **Submission**: Confirm that you are submitting the form.

Note: The fields are labeled as required or optional based on the provided YAML data.
