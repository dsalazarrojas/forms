# Real Estate Sales Lead Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about real estate sales leads, which can be generated through various sources such as online leads, social media, or referrals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and contact information in the first section.
2. Select the type of real estate you are working with (residential, commercial, or industrial).
3. Provide details about the property, including square footage, year built, and listing price.
4. Describe the property and your sales strategy.
5. Select the source of the lead (online lead, social media, or referral).
6. Enter the date the property was sold.
7. Enter the agent's name, phone number, email, and real estate agency information.
8. Finally, review and submit your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the property.
* **Real Estate Type** (`real_estate_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of real estate you are working with (residential, commercial, or industrial).
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, text, required: false): Enter the type of property (e.g. house, condo, etc.).
* **Square Footage** (`square_footage`, number, required: false): Enter the square footage of the property.
* **Year Built** (`year_built`, number, required: false): Enter the year the property was built.
* **Listing Price** (`listing_price`, number, required: false): Enter the listing price of the property.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Describe the property and your sales strategy.
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, select_one, required: false): Select the source of the lead (online lead, social media, or referral).
* **Date Sold** (`date_sold`, date, required: false): Enter the date the property was sold.
* **Agent Name** (`agent_name`, text, required: false): Enter the agent's name.
* **Agent Phone** (`agent_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the agent's phone number.
* **Agent Email** (`agent_email`, email, required: false): Enter the agent's email address.
* **Agent License Number** (`agent_license_number`, text, required: false): Enter the agent's license number.
* **Real Estate Agency** (`real_estate_agency`, text, required: false): Enter the real estate agency name.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required: false): Enter the zip code of the property's location.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Enter the company name.
* **Agent Phone** (`agent_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the agent's phone number.
* **Agent Email** (`agent_email`, email, required: false): Enter the agent's email address.
* **Agent License Number** (`agent_license_number`, text, required: false): Enter the agent's license number.

Note: All fields marked as required: false are optional and can be completed if relevant.
