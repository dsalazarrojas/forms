<thinking>Realtor Review Template - Help Guide

The "Realtor Review Template" is a form designed to capture feedback from clients about their interactions with real estate agents. This form is intended to collect specific, quantifiable data on the agent's performance, which can be used to provide actionable insights for realtors to improve their services.

Before starting, please ensure you're using this form for its intended purpose: to review a realtor's performance during a specific interaction or transaction. If you've reviewed the same agent multiple times, use the provided "Agent Name" field to differentiate between reviews.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
2. Provide any relevant contact information in the "Contact Information" field (optional).
3. Enter the name of the realtor you're reviewing in the "Agent Name" field.
4. Rate the agent's performance using the "Agent Rating" dropdown menu (required).
5. Describe your experiences with the agent's communication skills in the "Communication" field.
6. Share your observations about the agent's knowledge in the "Knowledge" field.
7. Evaluate the agent's responsiveness in the "Responsiveness" field.
8. Assess the agent's level of professionalism (on time, late, unresponsive, unprofessional) in the "Professionalism" field.
9. Indicate whether or not you would recommend the agent to others in the "Recommendations" field (required).
10. Provide any additional comments about your experience with the agent in the "Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (Text, required): Your name for reference and to help differentiate between reviews.
* **Contact Information** (Note, optional): Any additional contact information you'd like to share with the agency (e.g., email, phone number).
* **Agent Name** (Text, required): The name of the realtor being reviewed.
* **Agent Rating** (Select One, required): A subjective rating from 1 to 5 that represents your overall impression of the agent's performance.
* **Communication** (Text, optional): Describe the agent's communication style and effectiveness.
* **Knowledge** (Text, optional): Share your observations about the agent's knowledge and expertise.
* **Responsiveness** (Text, optional): Evaluate the agent's timeliness and responsiveness to your queries.
* **Professionalism** (Select Multiple, optional): Rate the agent's level of professionalism (on time, late, unresponsive, unprofessional).
* **Recommendations** (Select Multiple, required): Indicate whether or not you would recommend the agent to others.
* **Comments** (Text, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the agent.</thinking>
