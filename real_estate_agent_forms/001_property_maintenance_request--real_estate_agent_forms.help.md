# property_maintenance_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The property_maintenance_request form is used by property managers to track and manage maintenance requests from real estate agents. Agents can submit a request for maintenance work on a property, providing details such as the property address, issue date, repair cost, and more. The form helps property managers to prioritize and assign maintenance tasks effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the property_maintenance_request form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Property Details** field with a brief description of the property.
2. Enter the **Property Address** in the correct format (e.g., 123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345).
3. Provide the **Maintenance Request**, a clear and concise description of the issue.
4. Enter the **Issue Date**, the date when the issue was first noticed.
5. Enter the **Repair Cost**, the estimated cost of the maintenance work.
6. Select the **Assigned Tool** responsible for the maintenance work from the list of available options (chatjimmy, john, or jane).
7. Choose the **Maintenance Status**, the current status of the maintenance work (pending, in progress, or complete).
8. Enter the **Additional Comments**, any further details about the maintenance request.
9. Enter the **Email**, **Phone Number**, and **Requestor Name** for contact information.
10. Select the **Property Manager**, the person responsible for managing the property.
11. Choose the **Property Status**, the current status of the property (e.g., vacant, rented, or sold).
12. Enter the **Property Type**, the type of property (e.g., house, apartment, or commercial building).
13. If necessary, specify the **Maintenance Category**, and **Maintenance Item** to further describe the maintenance work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Details** (`property_details`): A brief description of the property.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`): Enter the full property address in the correct format (e.g., 123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345).
* **Maintenance Request** (`maintenance_request`): A clear and concise description of the issue.
* **Issue Date** (`issue_date`): The date when the issue was first noticed.
* **Repair Cost** (`repair_cost`): The estimated cost of the maintenance work.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Select the person or entity responsible for the maintenance work (chatjimmy, john, or jane).
* **Maintenance Status** (`maintenance_status`): Choose the current status of the maintenance work (pending, in progress, or complete).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any further details about the maintenance request.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the email address of the requestor.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter the phone number of the requestor.
* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`): Enter the name of the person making the request.
* **Requestor Email** (`requestor_email`): Enter the email address of the requestor.
* **Requestor Phone Number** (`requestor_phone_number`): Enter the phone number of the requestor.
* **Requestor Company** (`requestor_company`): Enter the company name or affiliation of the requestor (optional).
* **Requestor Title** (`requestor_title`): Enter the title or position of the requestor.
* **Property Manager** (`property_manager`): Select the person responsible for managing the property.
* **Property Status** (`property_status`): Choose the current status of the property (e.g., vacant, rented, or sold).
* **Property Type** (`property_type`): Enter the type of property (e.g., house, apartment, or commercial building).
* **If Other, specify** (`property_type_other`): If the property type is not in the list, enter the custom type.
* **If Other, specify** (`assigned_tool_other`): If the assigned tool is not in the list, enter the custom tool name.
* **Maintenance Category** (`maintenance_category`): Enter the category of the maintenance work.
* **Maintenance Item** (`maintenance_item`): Enter the item or task to be performed during the maintenance work.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`): Enter the date when the maintenance work will be assigned.
* **Maintenance Description** (`maintenance_description`): Enter a detailed description of the maintenance work.
* **Tips**: Please ensure the form is filled out accurately and thoroughly, as any missing or incorrect information may cause delays in processing the maintenance request.
