# Rental Application - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Rental Application form is designed to collect relevant information from potential renters, allowing landlords or real estate agents to assess their qualifications and determine the feasibility of renting them a property.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Rental Application form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by the asterisk * symbol) with your relevant information.
2. Select the appropriate options for each field.
3. If you have any additional comments, provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Enter your desired move-in date and time.
5. Enter your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rental Application Form** (`rental_application_form`): A text field where you can provide a brief description of your application.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`): Select the type of property you are interested in renting (e.g., House, Condo, Townhouse, Other).
* **Property Size** (`property_size`): Select the number of rooms you are looking for in a property (e.g., 1-2 room, 3-4 room, 5-6 room, 7-8 room).
* **Desired Location** (`desired_location`): Select the type of location you are interested in (e.g., Urban, Suburban, Rural).
* **Income Range** (`income_range`): Select your annual income range (e.g., 20000-40000, 40001-60000, 60001-80000, 80001-100000).
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`): Select your current employment status (e.g., Employed, Self-Employed, Unemployed).
* **Rental History** (`rental_history`): Select your current rental history (e.g., Renting, Owning, Both).
* **Credit Score** (`credit_score`): Enter your credit score.
* **Rental Application Status** (`rental_application_status`): Select the status of your rental application (e.g., Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments or information about your application.
* **Date of Move In** (`date_of_move_in`): Enter the date you are interested in moving in.
* **Time of Move In** (`time_of_move_in`): Enter the time you are interested in moving in.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
* We appreciate your interest in renting a property, and we look forward to reviewing your application.
