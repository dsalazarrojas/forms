# Rental History Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an applicant's rental history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Provide your current rental address in the "current_rental_address" field.
2. Describe your previous rental history in the "previous_rental_history" field.
3. Enter the start date of your previous rental in the "rental_start_date" field.
4. Enter the end date of your previous rental in the "rental_end_date" field.
5. Indicate the duration of your previous rental in the "rental_duration" field.
6. Provide any additional comments or information about your rental history in the "rental_history_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Current Rental Address** (`current_rental_address`, text, optional): Enter your current rental address. This is where you currently reside.
* **Applicant First Name** (`applicant_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Applicant Last Name** (`applicant_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Previous Rental History** (`previous_rental_history`, text, optional): Describe your previous rental history, including the address, dates, and length of stay.
* **Start Date of Previous Rental** (`rental_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your previous rental.
* **End Date of Previous Rental** (`rental_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your previous rental.
* **Rental Duration** (`rental_duration`, number, optional): Indicate the duration of your previous rental (in months).
* **Rental History Comments** (`rental_history_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your rental history.
