# Rental Application Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Application Interest Form is designed to collect information from potential renters interested in renting a property. This form helps the property owner or landlord determine whether the applicant is a suitable candidate for renting the property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective text fields.
2. Provide your **Phone** number so that the property owner or landlord can contact you for further information.
3. Enter your **Email** address to ensure that you can be contacted for updates and follow-up questions.
4. If applicable, provide the **Property ID** of the property you are interested in renting.
5. Select the **Move In Date** when you plan to move in. Note that this is not the actual move-in date, but rather the date you are interested in moving in.
6. Select the **Move In Time** when you plan to move in.
7. Type in a brief **Message** about why you are interested in renting the property.
8. Choose the **Lease Term** that suits your needs (One Month, Six Months, or Twelve Months).
9. Select the month(s) you are interested in moving in from the **Move In Month** field.
10. Select the year(s) you are interested in moving in from the **Move In Year** field.
11. Choose the type of **Property Type** that suits your needs (Apartment or House).
12. Select the **Property Size** that suits your needs (1 Bedroom, 2 Bedrooms, or 3 Bedrooms).
13. Enter your **Budget** for rent.
14. Select the reason for your **Reason for Move**, which may be Family, Work, or School.
15. Indicate whether you are **Employed** or not.
16. If employed, enter your **Employer** name.
17. Select your **Income** range.
18. Choose your **Credit Score** (600, 650, 700, 750, 800).
19. Select your **Employment Length** (Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years).
20. Indicate whether you would like to undergo a **Credit Check**.
21. Select the **Move In Status** of the property (Available, Occupied, Not Available).
22. Select the **Move Out Status** of the property (Available, Occupied, Not Available).
23. Enter your **Reference** (e.g. previous landlord or property manager).
24. Type in any additional **Message** about your application.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the property owner or landlord has a clear understanding of your needs and qualifications.
* Review the property's availability before filling out the form to ensure that it matches your search criteria.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the property owner or landlord for further information.

This help guide is meant to be a starting point for the user. If additional information is required or if the form requires any further clarification, please contact the property owner or landlord directly.
