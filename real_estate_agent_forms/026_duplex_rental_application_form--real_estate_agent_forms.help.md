# Duplex Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Duplex Rental Application Form is designed to collect information from potential tenants for a duplex rental property. It consists of five sections: Tenant Information, Contact Information, Property Details, Rental History, and Rental Agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Tenant Information section with your full name, address, and date of birth (if required).
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Information section, including your email address and phone number.
3. Describe the property you are interested in renting in the Property Details section.
4. Share your rental history in the Rental History section, including previous rental experiences and any relevant information.
5. Review and agree to the rental agreement in the Rental Agreement section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Information** (Text):
	+ A field to enter your full name and any other identifying information.
* **Contact Information** (Text):
	+ A field to provide your email address and phone number.
* **Property Details** (Text):
	+ A field to describe the property you are interested in renting, including its address and any other relevant details.
* **Rental History** (Text):
	+ A field to share your previous rental experiences, including any relevant information about your rental history.
* **Rental Agreement** (Text):
	+ A field to review and agree to the rental terms and conditions. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the landlord or property manager.
- Review the rental agreement carefully before agreeing to it. 

Note: This help guide assumes that the form fields are self-explanatory. If a field label is unclear, please contact the form author for further clarification.
