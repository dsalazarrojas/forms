# Lead Based Paint Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lead Based Paint Disclosure Form is designed to help real estate agents collect and document information about lead-based paint disclosures in residential properties. This form is a crucial tool to ensure that real estate agents are aware of the potential risks associated with lead-based paint and comply with local regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and understand the purpose and context of each field.
2. Fill in the relevant information for each field, using the space provided.
3. Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information for each field.
4. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Lead Based Paint Disclosure Form (1)
* **Lead Based Paint Disclosure Form**: This is the title of the form, which is used to identify the purpose and scope of the form.

### Lead Paint Info (2)
* **Lead Paint Info**: This field is used to enter any additional information about the lead-based paint disclosure, including the type of paint used, the area of the property affected, or any other relevant details.

### Home Address (3)
* **Home Address**: This field is used to enter the address of the residential property that is being disclosed for lead-based paint.

### Home City (4)
* **Home City**: This field is used to enter the city where the residential property is located.

### Lead Based Paint Disclosure (5)
* **Lead Based Paint Disclosure**: This field is used to enter the disclosure statement or report about the lead-based paint in the property.

### Additional Info (6)
* **Additional Info**: This field is used to enter any additional information or comments about the lead-based paint disclosure.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
* Consult local regulations and guidelines for specific requirements and best practices related to lead-based paint disclosures.
