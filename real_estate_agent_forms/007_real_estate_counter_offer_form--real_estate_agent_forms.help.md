# Real Estate Counter Offer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect counter offer or acceptance details from real estate agents, which helps in managing and tracking such interactions efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information, including any relevant details such as name, email, and phone number.
2. Describe the counter offer or acceptance, being as specific as possible.
3. Choose whether to accept, reject, or make a counter offer.
4. If applicable, mention any additional terms or conditions related to the counter offer.
5. Provide any counter details, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Contact Information)**: Please enter your name, email, and phone number to ensure we can get in touch with you regarding your counter offer.
* **Counter Offer (Counter Offer)**: Describe the counter offer you are making or accepting, being as specific as possible. This could include details about the price, terms, or conditions.
* **Counter Accept (Counter Accept)**: Choose whether you want to accept, reject, or make a counter offer. This field is required to ensure we know your response.
* **Additional Terms (Additional Terms)**: If there are any additional terms or conditions you'd like to include with your counter offer, please enter them here.
* **Counter Details (Counter Details)**: If you have any further details about your counter offer, such as specifics about the price or terms, please enter them here.

## Tips

* Please make sure to answer all required fields.
* Be as specific as possible when describing your counter offer or acceptance.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
