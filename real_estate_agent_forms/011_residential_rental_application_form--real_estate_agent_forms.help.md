# Residential Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Residential Rental Application Form is designed to collect information from potential tenants to help landlords or property managers evaluate their eligibility for a rental property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your name and any other relevant applicant information on the "Applicant Info" field.
2.  Enter your contact details, including phone number and email, on the "Contact Details" field.
3.  Describe the rental property you are interested in on the "Rental Property" field.
4.  Specify the unit you are applying for on the "Rental Unit" field.
5.  List your rental history, including previous rental properties and durations, on the "Rental History" field.
6.  Indicate your employment status and income source on the "Employment Status" field.
7.  Provide information about your income proof on the "Income Proof" field.
8.  Check the box indicating whether you have given permission for a credit check on the "Credit Check" field.
9.  Read and agree to the terms of the rental application on the "Rental Application Terms" field.
10.  Sign and date the "Rental Property Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rental Application**: This field is a text box where you can type in your rental application.
* **Applicant Info**: In this field, please provide your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact details, including phone number and email, so that the landlord or property manager can get in touch with you.
* **Rental Property**: Describe the rental property you are interested in, including its address, location, and amenities.
* **Rental Unit**: Specify the specific unit you are applying for, including its address, location, and amenities.
* **Rental History**: List your rental history, including previous rental properties, durations, and reasons for moving.
* **Employment Status**: Indicate your current employment status, whether working, student, or other.
* **Income Source**: Specify your income source, such as salary, investments, or other.
* **Income Proof**: Attach any proof of income you may have, such as pay stub or bank statement.
* **Credit Check**: Check the box if you have given permission for a credit check.
* **Rental Application Terms**: Read and agree to the terms of the rental application.
* **Rental Property Agreement**: Sign and date this field to confirm your acceptance of the rental property terms.
