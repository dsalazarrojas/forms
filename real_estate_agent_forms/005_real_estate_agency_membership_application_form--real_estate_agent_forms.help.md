# real_estate_agency_membership_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_agency_membership_application_form is used to collect information from real estate agents applying for membership with our agency. This form helps us verify your eligibility and ensures a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Information, including your name and contact details in the **User Information** page.
2. Enter your contact information and property details in the **Contact Information** page.
3. Confirm your real estate license status in the **Real Estate License** page.
4. Provide your agency information, including any additional details required.
5. Review and agree to our **Contract Terms and Conditions**.
6. Sign and date the form, indicating your agreement to our contract terms.
7. Confirm your agreement to our membership contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (1, User Information, text, required):
    Fill in your name, email address, and other relevant contact details.
* **Contact Information** (2, Contact Information, text, required):
    Enter your agency's contact details, including phone number and address.
* **Property Information** (3, Property Information, text, required):
    Provide any additional property-related details as required.
* **Real Estate License** (4, Real Estate License, select_one, required):
    Check 'True' if you hold a valid real estate license, 'False' otherwise.
* **Agency Information** (5, Agency Information, text, required):
    Enter your agency's name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Contract Terms and Conditions** (6, Contract Terms, note, optional):
    Carefully review our contract terms and conditions before proceeding.
* **Signature Date** (7, Signature Date, date, optional):
    Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Signature** (8, Signature, select_one, required):
    Check 'Yes' to confirm you've read and agreed to our contract terms.
* **Agreement** (9, Agreement, select_multiple, required):
    Check 'Yes' for each term you've read and agreed to in the contract.

## Tips
- Be accurate and truthful in your responses.
- Ensure you've read and understood all contract terms before proceeding.
- Double-check your information for any errors or discrepancies.
