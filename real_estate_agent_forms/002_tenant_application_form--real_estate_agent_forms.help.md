# Tenant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tenant Application Form is a crucial document that helps property managers evaluate potential tenants. It collects essential information about the applicant, their employment status, income, and lease requirements. A thorough and accurate submission of this form will aid in a successful application process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Page 1:**
	* **First Name**: Please enter your complete first name.
	* **Last Name**: Enter your complete last name.
	* **Email**: Enter your active email address.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the 'dd/mm/yyyy' format.
2. **Page 2:**
	* **Street**: Enter your current street address.
	* **City**: Enter your current city.
	* **State**: Enter your current state.
	* **Zip**: Enter your current zip code.
	* **Rent Requirements**: Specify your rent requirements.
3. **Page 3:**
	* **Lease Start Date**: Enter the desired lease start date.
	* **Lease End Date**: Enter the desired lease end date.
	* **Rent**: Enter the proposed rent amount.
4. **Page 4:**
	* **Property Type**: Choose 'Yes' if the property is an apartment or 'No' if it's not.
	* **Property Status**: Choose the property's current status - 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
	* **Property Location**: Choose 'Yes' if the property is located near your current residence or 'No' if not.
	* **Application Method**: Choose 'Yes' if you're applying for the property through this form or 'No' otherwise.
5. **Page 5:**
	* **Tenant Income**: Choose 'Yes' if your income is verifiable or 'No' otherwise.
	* **Move In Date**: Enter the desired move-in date.
	* **Move In Location**: Enter the location you plan to move into.
6. **Page 6:**
	* **Employment Status**: Choose 'Active' if you're actively employed or 'Inactive' if not.
	* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
	* **Employer**: Enter your current employer.
7. **Page 7:**
	* **Income Verification**: Choose 'Yes' if your income is verifiable or 'No' otherwise.
	* **Other Income Sources**: Enter any other sources of income not mentioned previously.
