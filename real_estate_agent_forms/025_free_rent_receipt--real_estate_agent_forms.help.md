# Free Rent Receipt - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and verify rental payments between landlords and tenants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the landlord's information in the first page, including their name and contact details.
2. Enter the tenant's information in the second page, including their name and contact details.
3. Select the payment method used for the rent payment in the third page.
4. Enter the amount paid for the rent in the fourth page.
5. Enter the date the rent was received in the fifth page.
6. Enter the address of the rental property in the sixth page.
7. Include both the tenant's and landlord's signatures to verify that the form is accurate and agreed upon.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Landlord info** (`landlord_info`, text, required: false): Enter the landlord's name, contact number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Tenant info** (`tenant_info`, text, required: false): Enter the tenant's name, contact number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Select the method used for the rent payment (Check, Cash, Online banking, Bank transfer, or Other).
* **Amount paid** (`amount_paid`, number, required: false): Enter the exact amount paid for the rent.
* **Date received** (`date_received`, date, required: false): Enter the date the rent was received.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the rental property.
* **Tenant signature** (`tenant_signature`, text, required: false): Include the tenant's signature to verify that the form is accurate.
* **Landlord signature** (`landlord_signature`, text, required: false): Include the landlord's signature to verify that the form is accurate and agreed upon.

## Tips

* Make sure to accurately enter the landlord and tenant information to ensure correct payments are recorded.
* Choose the correct payment method and amount to match the actual payment transaction.
* Include both signatures to verify that the form is accurate and agreed upon.
* Double-check the date of payment to ensure it matches the actual date the rent was received.
