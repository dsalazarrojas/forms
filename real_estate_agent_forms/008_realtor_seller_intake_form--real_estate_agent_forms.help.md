# Realtor Seller Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from real estate sellers for the purposes of facilitating real estate transactions and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, providing accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure you're a real estate agent filling out this form with valid business purposes.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seller Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person selling the property.
* **Seller Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the person selling the property.
* **Property Address** (text, optional): Enter the address of the property being sold.
* **Property Type** (select one, optional): Choose the type of property being sold (e.g., Single Family Home, Condo, Townhouse, etc.).
* **Property Size** (number, optional): Enter the size of the property in square feet or acres.
* **Property Status** (select one, optional): Indicate the current status of the property (e.g., Active, Pending, Sold).
* **Property Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the property.
* **Contact Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person for the property.
* **Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the contact person.
* **Realtor License** (select one, optional): Indicate whether the realtor filling out this form has a valid realtor license.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your realtor representative for guidance.
* This form is for real estate agents with valid business purposes, so please do not fill it out if you're not a real estate agent.
