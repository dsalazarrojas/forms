# House Buyers Need Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential home buyers about their preferences and requirements for a house purchase. The data gathered through this form can aid real estate agents in identifying the most suitable properties for each buyer and tailor their sales strategy to meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name, email, and phone number as per the "Buyer Name", "Buyer Email", and "Buyer Phone" fields respectively. These details will help us to contact you for further discussions.
2. Select your preferred neighborhood and location from the available options.
3. Indicate whether you have a budget for the purchase, and if so, select your budget from the provided options.
4. Select the type of home you are looking for (e.g., house, condo, etc.).
5. Indicate your family status and any preferred features you may have for your new home (e.g., pool, garden, etc.).
6. Specify any other features you have in mind that are not listed in the previous options.
7. Provide a brief description of your ideal home and the date by which you wish to complete the sale.
8. Finally, indicate the type of loan you are planning to use for the purchase.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Name** (`buyer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it should be displayed in the CRM.
* **Buyer Email** (`buyer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussions.
* **Buyer Phone** (`buyer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can reach you.
* **Preferred Neighborhood** (`preferred_neighborhood`, select_multiple, required): Select all the neighborhoods that you are interested in.
* **Budget** (`budget`, select_one, required): Select your budget for the house purchase.
* **Home Type** (`home_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of home you are looking for (e.g., house, condo, etc.).
* **Family Status** (`family_status`, select_one, required): Select your family status.
* **Home Location** (`home_location`, select_one, required): Select the location of your desired home.
* **Preferred Features** (`preferred_features`, select_multiple, required): Select all the features you have in mind for your new home.
* **Other (Specify)** (`preferred_features_other`, text, required): Describe any other features you have in mind that are not listed in the options.
* **Home Wishes** (`home_wishes`, note, required): Provide a brief description of your ideal home.
* **Closing Date** (`closing_date`, select_one, required): Select the date by which you wish to complete the sale.
* **Loan Type** (`loan_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of loan you are planning to use for the purchase.
