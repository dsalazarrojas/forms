# HOA Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HOA Disclosure Form is designed to gather essential information about a Homeowners Association (HOA). This form is used to collect details such as the address, fees, rules, services, and other relevant information about the HOA.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the HOA's title in the "HOA Disclosure Form" field. This is a required field.
2. Provide the address of the HOA in the "hoa_address" field. This field is optional.
3. Indicate whether or not the HOA charges fees by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "hoa_fees" field. This field is optional.
4. Describe the rules that govern the HOA in the "hoa_rules" field. This field is optional.
5. List any services provided by the HOA in the "hoa_services" field. This field is optional.
6. Specify any maintenance areas within the HOA in the "hoa_maintenance_areas" field. This field is optional.
7. Provide any additional information about the HOA in the "hoa_other_info" field. This field is optional.
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **HOA Disclosure Form** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a descriptive title for the HOA.
* **HOA Address** (`hoa_address`, text, optional): Provide the physical address of the HOA.
* **Fees Charged by HOA** (`hoa_fees`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the HOA charges fees or not.
* **HOA Rules** (`hoa_rules`, text, optional): Describe the rules that govern the HOA.
* **HOA Services** (`hoa_services`, text, optional): List any services provided by the HOA.
* **Maintenance Areas** (`hoa_maintenance_areas`, text, optional): Specify any maintenance areas within the HOA.
* **Other Information** (`hoa_other_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the HOA.
* **Submit** (`form_submit`, text, required): Click this button to complete the form and submit your information.
