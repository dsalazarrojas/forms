# Container Home Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and employment information from applicants for a container home rental application. It's used to assess the suitability of potential renters for container home rentals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details in **Section 1: Applicant Details**:
	* **Full Name**: Enter your complete name as it appears on your identification.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your current email address.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your current phone number.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter your birthdate in the format (mm/dd/yyyy).
	* **Current Street Address**: Enter your current street address.
	* **City**: Enter the city of your current address.
2. Provide your employment information in **Section 2: Employment Information**:
	* **Current Employer**: Enter the name of your current employer.
	* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
	* **Approximate Monthly Income**: Enter your approximate monthly income.
	* **How long have you been with this employer?**: Enter the duration of your employment.
3. (Optional) If applicable, provide your rental history in **Section 3: Rental History**:
	* **Current Landlord Name**: Enter the name of your current landlord.
	* **Current Landlord Phone Number**: Enter your current landlord's phone number.
4. Add a personal reference in **Section 4: Personal References**:
	* **Reference Full Name**: Enter the full name of your reference.
	* **Reference Phone Number**: Enter your reference's phone number.
5. Confirm your understanding and consent in **Section 5: Background Check and Consent**:
	* **I consent to a background and credit check**: Choose "True" to indicate your agreement.
	* **I have read and agree to the privacy policy**: Choose "True" to indicate your understanding.
	* **I confirm that the information provided is accurate**: Choose "I Agree".
