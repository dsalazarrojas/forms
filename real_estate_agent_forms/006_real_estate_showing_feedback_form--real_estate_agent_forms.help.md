# real_estate_showing_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from real estate agents about their experiences with showings, helping the company to improve the showing process and provide better services to clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your feedback about the showing you participated in.
2. Rate your experience using the "Rating" field.
3. Provide any comments or additional feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the form.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name as you would like it to appear on the form.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Feedback** (Text, Optional): Provide any feedback or comments about the showing.
* **Rating** (Select One, Optional): Rate your experience as Excellent, Good, or Needs Improvement.
* **Comments** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Date of Showing** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the showing you participated in.
* **Time of Showing** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the showing you participated in.
* **Showing Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the address of the property that was shown.
* **Showing Agent** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the agent or agent's company that showed the property.

## Tips
* Please only fill out this form for showings you have actually participated in.
* Please provide as much detail as possible to help us improve the showing process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
