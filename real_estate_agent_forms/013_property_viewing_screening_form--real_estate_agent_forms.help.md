# Property Viewing Screening Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential clients about their properties and viewing preferences, enabling real estate agents to screen potential clients for property viewings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide a valid Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Select your Available Viewings Days (Monday, Wednesday, or Friday).
6. Choose a Contact Person (if applicable).
7. Choose the type of Property you are interested in (Apartment, House, Land, or Other).
8. If applicable, select a Move In Date.
9. If applicable, select a View Date.
10. If applicable, select a Preferred Viewing Time.
11. Add any additional View Details.
12. Add any Agent Comments.
13. Add any Tenant Comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: This field is used to collect your First Name. Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: This field is used to collect your Last Name. Please enter your last name.
* **Email**: This field is used to collect your Email address. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**: This field is used to collect your Phone number. Please enter your phone number.
* **Available Viewings Days**: This field is used to collect your preferred days for viewings (Monday, Wednesday, or Friday). Please select your preferred days.
* **Contact Person**: This field is used to specify the person to contact (Mr, Mrs, Miss, or None). Please select your preferred contact person.
* **Property Type**: This field is used to collect the type of property you are interested in (Apartment, House, Land, or Other). Please select your preferred property type.
* **Move In Date**: This field is used to collect the date you plan to move in. Please enter your move in date, if applicable.
* **View Date**: This field is used to collect the date you would like to view the property. Please enter your view date, if applicable.
* **Preferred View Time**: This field is used to collect your preferred time for viewings (9am, 10am, 11am, 1pm, 2pm, 3pm, or 4pm). Please select your preferred time.
* **View Details**: This field is used to add any additional details about your viewing requirements. Please enter any relevant details.
* **Agent Comments**: This field is used to add any comments or notes for the agent. Please enter any comments or notes.
* **Tenant Comments**: This field is used to add any comments or notes for the tenant. Please enter any comments or notes.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill in all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your real estate agent for clarification.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact your real estate agent.
* Please note that the preferred viewing times are subject to change based on the agent's or tenant's availability.
