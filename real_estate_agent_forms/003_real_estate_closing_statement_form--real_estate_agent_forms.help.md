The purpose of this form is to collect information about a real estate transaction. It is used to document the details of a closing statement, which is a record of the payment and status of a real estate transaction. This form will be used by agents to gather the necessary information for the transaction.

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the seller information section with the seller's name, address, phone number, and email address.
2. Select the payment method and enter the payment amount.
3. Choose the payment date and status.
4. Fill in the buyer information section with their name, phone number, and email address (if applicable).

The form is divided into two sections: seller and buyer information. The first section, "Seller Information," requires the following:

* **Seller Name (<type:text>, required):** Enter the seller's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Seller Address (<type:text, required):** Enter the seller's full address.
* **Seller Phone (<type:text, required):** Enter the seller's phone number.
* **Seller Email (<type:email, required):** Enter the seller's email address.
* **Payment Amount (<type:number, required):** Enter the amount paid for the transaction.
* **Payment Method (<type:select_one, required):** Select the method of payment (Check, Cash, or Wire Transfer).
* **Payment Date (<type:date, required):** Enter the date the payment was made.
* **Payment Status (<type:select_one, required):** Select the status of the payment (Paid or Unpaid).

The second section, "Buyer Information," is optional and requires the following:

* **Buyer Name (<type:text, optional):** Enter the buyer's name if applicable.
* **Buyer Phone (<type:text, optional):** Enter the buyer's phone number if applicable.
* **Buyer Email (<type:email, optional):** Enter the buyer's email address if applicable.

Note that this form is used for documentation and record-keeping purposes. Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
