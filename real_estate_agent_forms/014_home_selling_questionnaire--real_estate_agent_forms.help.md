# Purpose of the "Home Selling Questionnaire" is to collect essential information about a home for sale, ensuring accuracy and thoroughness in the listing process. This form aims to gather comprehensive details about the property, including its address, features, condition, and other relevant information that will be useful for potential buyers and real estate agents. It is crucial to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to provide a clear and honest representation of the property.

To ensure the form is filled out correctly, please review each field carefully and follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start with a clear and correct address** of the property at the top of the form.
2. **Select the correct home features** that apply to the property, such as Basement, Fireplace, Pool, Parking, and Security System.
3. **Choose the home condition** from the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
4. **List any additional features** that are not listed in the provided options, such as a Deck, Patio, or Garage.
5. **Indicate the current condition** of any additional features listed, such as Minor, Major, or Needs Repair.
6. **Enter the buyer's information** if you have any, such as their name, contact number, and email.
7. **Enter the agent's information** if you have any, such as your name, contact number, and email.
8. **Write any other comments** about the property that might be helpful for the real estate agent or potential buyer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Address**: Enter the exact address of the property, including the street number and name, city, and state.
- **Home Features**: Select all features that apply to the property from the provided options.
- **Home Condition**: Indicate the overall condition of the property from the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
- **Additional Features**: List any additional features not listed in the provided options that apply to the property.
- **Additional Condition**: Indicate the condition of any additional features listed.
- **Buyer Information**: Enter any information about the buyer, such as their name, contact number, and email.
- **Contact Details**: Enter any contact information for the contact person.
- **Agent Information**: Enter any agent information, such as their name, contact number, and email.
- **Other Comments**: Write any additional comments or notes about the property that might be helpful for the real estate agent or potential buyer.

## Tips

- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
- If unsure about any field, it is best to leave it blank or consult with a real estate expert before completing the form.
- This form is a tool to assist in listing the property accurately, but it is the responsibility of the property owner or real estate agent to ensure all details are correct and up-to-date.
