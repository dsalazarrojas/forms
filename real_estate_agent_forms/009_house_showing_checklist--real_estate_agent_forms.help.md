# house_showing_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and report information about a property for real estate agents to facilitate internal tracking and reporting, or for sharing with other agents or potential buyers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the property status from the "Property Status" dropdown menu.
2. Choose the property type from the "Property Type" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the property's square footage in the "Square Footage" field.
4. Enter the number of bedrooms in the "Number of Bedrooms" field.
5. Enter the number of bathrooms in the "Number of Bathrooms" field.
6. Enter the number of sinks in the "Number of Bedrooms Sinks" field.
7. Enter the number of kitchens in the "Number of Kitchens" field.
8. Enter the property's lot size in the "Lot Size" field.
9. Enter the property's lot dimensions in the "Lot Dimensions" field.
10. Choose the lot orientation from the "Lot Orientation" dropdown menu.
11. Select whether or not the property has a separate address for each unit from the "Property Address 1" dropdown menu.
12. Enter the secondary address information in the "Property Address 2" field.
13. Enter the city where the property is located.
14. Choose whether or not the state is part of the property's address from the "State" dropdown menu.
15. Enter the property's zip code.
16. Choose whether or not the property was built in the current year from the "Year Built" dropdown menu.
17. Enter the property's lot number, if applicable.
18. Choose whether or not the property has a neighborhood, from the "Neighborhood" dropdown menu.
19. Enter the property's lot/lot number, if applicable.
20. Enter the property's block number, if applicable.
21. Choose the property condition from the "Property Condition" dropdown menu.
22. Enter any notes or comments about the property in the "Property Notes" field.
23. Enter a brief description of the property in the "Property Description" field.
24. Choose whether or not the property has an agent from the "Property Agent" dropdown menu.
25. Enter the property agent's email address, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Status** (1, select_one, required): Choose the current status of the property from the dropdown menu.
* **Property Type** (2, select_one, required): Select the type of property from the dropdown menu.
* **Square Footage** (3, number, required): Enter the property's total square footage.
* **Number of Bedrooms** (4, number, required): Enter the number of bedrooms in the property.
* **Number of Bathrooms** (5, number, required): Enter the number of bathrooms in the property.
* **Number of Bedrooms Sinks** (6, number, required): Enter the number of sinks in the property.
* **Number of Kitchens** (7, number, required): Enter the number of kitchens in the property.
* **Lot Size** (8, number, required): Enter the size of the property's lot.
* **Lot Dimensions** (9, text, required): Enter the dimensions of the property's lot (e.g. length, width, etc.).
* **Lot Orientation** (10, select_one, required): Choose the orientation of the property's lot from the dropdown menu.
* **Property Address 1** (11, select_multiple, required): Select whether or not the property has a separate address for each unit.
* **Property Address 2** (12, text, required): Enter the secondary address information.
* **City** (13, text, required): Enter the city where the property is located.
* **State** (14, select_one, required): Choose whether or not the state is part of the property's address.
* **Zip Code** (15, text, required): Enter the property's zip code.
* **Year Built** (16, select_one, required): Choose whether or not the property was built in the current year.
* **Lot Number** (17, text, required): Enter the property's lot number, if applicable.
* **Neighborhood** (18, select_one, required): Choose whether or not the property has a neighborhood.
* **Lot/Lot Number** (19, text, required): Enter the property's lot/lot number, if applicable.
* **Lot Block** (20, text, required): Enter the property's block number, if applicable.
* **Property Condition** (21, select_one, required): Choose the property condition from the dropdown menu.
* **Property Notes** (22, note, required): Enter any notes or comments about the property.
* **Property Description** (23, text, required): Enter a brief description of the property.
* **Property Agent** (24, select_one, required): Choose whether or not the property has an agent.
* **Property Agent E-mail** (25, email, required): Enter the property agent's email address, if applicable.
