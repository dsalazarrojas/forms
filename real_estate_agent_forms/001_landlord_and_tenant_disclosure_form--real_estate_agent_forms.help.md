# Landlord And Tenant Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document the agreement between landlords and tenants in a rental property transaction. It collects information about the property, the rent, and the security deposit, as well as other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information as the Landlord.
2. Enter the address, city, and state of the property.
3. Enter the date of the agreement between the landlord and tenant.
4. Enter the rent amount and total days of the rental term.
5. Enter the amount of the security deposit.
6. Indicate whether the security deposit has been returned.
7. Provide any additional comments or information as Landlord and Tenant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Information about the Landlord** (`1`): Enter your name and contact information as the landlord.
* **Real Estate Agent** (`2`): Enter your name and contact information as the real estate agent.
* **Real Estate Agent 2** (`3`): Enter your name and contact information as the real estate agent.
* **Tenant 1** (`5`): Enter the name of the first tenant.
* **Tenant 2** (`6`): Enter the name of the second tenant.
* **Landlord** (`7`): Enter your name as the landlord.
* **Landlord 2** (`8`): Enter an additional landlord's name.
* **Landlord Address** (`9`): Enter the address of the property.
* **City** (`10`): Enter the city of the property.
* **State** (`11`): Enter the state of the property.
* **Zip** (`12`): Enter the zip code of the property.
* **Real Estate Agent Forms 4** (`13`): Enter any additional information about the real estate agent.
* **Landlord and Tenant Agreement** (`14`): Enter the date of the agreement between the landlord and tenant.
* **Date of Agreement** (`15`): Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Date of Move-in** (`16`): Enter the date the tenant moved in.
* **Rent** (`17`): Enter the rent amount.
* **Total Days** (`18`): Enter the total number of days in the rental term.
* **Total Amount** (`19`): Enter the total amount of the rent.
* **Security Deposit** (`20`): Enter the amount of the security deposit.
* **Security Deposit Returned** (`21`): Indicate whether the security deposit has been returned.
* **Security Deposit Return** (`22`): Enter any additional information about the return of the security deposit.
* **Landlord and Tenant** (`23`): Enter any additional comments or information from the landlord and tenant.
* **Landlord And Tenant Disclosure Form 12** (`24`): Enter any additional information about the form.
* **Landlord And Tenant Disclosure Form 13** (`25`): Enter any additional information about the form.
