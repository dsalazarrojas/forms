# Smart Rental Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Smart Rental Application Form is a tool used to collect information from tenants, applicants, or guarantors for rental applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided the correct information.
3. Submit the completed form for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Application** (`tenant_application`, text, optional): This field is intended for the tenant's written application, where they describe their interest in renting the property. Please provide a brief explanation of your rental needs and preferences.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, such as name, email, and phone number, so the landlord or property manager can get in touch with you.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, optional): Describe the property you are interested in renting, including its address, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Rental History** (`rental_history`, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about your rental history, including past experiences with landlords or property managers.
* **Rental History Items** (`rental_history_items`, text, optional): If you have multiple rental histories, please list them separately in this field.
* **Landlord Info** (`landlord_info`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of landlord you are dealing with: Landlord, Property Manager, Owner, or Administrator.
* **Tenant Info** (`tenant_info`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your relationship with the tenant: Tenant, Applicant, or Guarantor.
* **Employment Info** (`employment_info`, text, optional): Provide your employment details, such as job title, company, and salary, to demonstrate your financial stability and rental eligibility.
