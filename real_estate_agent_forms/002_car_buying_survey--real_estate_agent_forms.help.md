# Car Buying Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Car Buying Survey" form is designed to collect information about car buyers' demographics, current vehicle experience, and preferences for future car purchases. The goal is to gather data that can be used for market research and to better understand car buying behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Customer Information" section, which includes questions about your age group, occupation, and household size.
2. Provide information about your current vehicle, including its make and model, age, and your level of satisfaction with it.
3. Answer questions about your future car purchase plans, including when you plan to buy a new car and why.
4. Answer questions about your preferences for a new car, including your preferred brand, dealership experience, research methods, and importance of environmental features.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required)
Select your age group from the provided options (Under 25, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55+)

### 2. **Your Current Occupation** (`occupation`, text, optional)
Please enter your current occupation.

### 3. **Number of People in your Household** (`household_size`, number, required)
Enter the total number of people in your household.

### 4. **Current Car Make and Model** (`current_car_make_model`, text, required)
Enter the make and model of your current car.

### 5. **Age of Current Vehicle** (`current_car_age`, number, required)
Enter the age of your current car in years.

### 6. **Satisfaction with Current Vehicle** (`satisfaction_with_current_car`, number, required)
Rate your satisfaction with your current car on a scale of 1 to 10.

### 7. **Future Purchase Plans** (`purchase_timeframe`, select_one, required)
Select when you plan to buy a new car (Within 3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, After 1 year, Not sure)

### 8. **Primary Reason for Buying a New Car** (`main_reason_for_new_purchase`, select_one, required)
Select your primary reason for buying a new car (Replacing old vehicle, Need more space, Seeking better fuel economy, Want newer technology, Growing family, First time buyer)

### 9. **Would you buy the same brand again?** (`brand_loyalty`, select_one, required)
Select whether you would buy the same brand again (Yes, definitely, Maybe, No, want a change, Unsure)

### 10. **Research Methods** (`research_methods`, select_multiple, required)
Select all the methods you use to research for a new car (Online reviews, Dealership visits, Recommendations from friends, Social media, Car magazines, TV Advertisements)

### 11. **Importance of Dealership Reputation** (`dealership_experience_importance`, select_one, required)
Select how important the dealership's reputation is to you (Very Important, Somewhat Important, Neutral, Not Very Important)

### 12. **Is a test drive essential?** (`test_drive_importance`, select_one, required)
Select whether a test drive is essential to you (True, False, Depends on the car)

### 13. **Importance of Environmental Features** (`environmental_consideration`, select_one, required)
Select how important eco-friendly features are to you (Crucial (Must be EV/Hybrid), Important, Nice to have, Not a priority)

### 14. **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional)
Share any other thoughts or comments about your car buying experience.
