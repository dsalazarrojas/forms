# Housing Discrimination Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This Housing Discrimination Disclosure Form is designed to help monitor and address potential housing-related discrimination. It is a tool to help identify areas where discrimination may be occurring, facilitating fair housing practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2.  Fill out the form honestly and accurately based on your status.
3.  Ensure that all required fields are completed.
