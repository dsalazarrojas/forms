# Tenant Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from tenants about their experience with a real estate agency. It will help the agency to better understand the tenant's perception and improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page of the form. Each page will have multiple questions related to different aspects of your experience with the agency.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply from the options provided.
3. For open-ended questions, provide your thoughts and comments in the text field.
4. Finally, submit the form by selecting the 'Submit' option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What's your rent** (`tenant_feedback_form_1`, Number, optional): This question asks for your current rent amount.
* **What do you like most?** (`feedback_question_1`, Select Multiple, required): Select all the amenities or services that you like most about your experience with the agency.
	+ Options: Rent and Utilities, Property Management, Communication
* **What do you don't like?** (`feedback_question_2`, Select One, required): Select one aspect that you are not satisfied with.
	+ Options: Not enough parking, No amenities, Other (please specify)
* **Any other comments?** (`tenant_feedback`, Text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about your experience.
* **Rate your satisfaction** (`tenant_rating`, Select One, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the agency.
	+ Options: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5
* **Submit** (`submit_form`, Select One, optional): Finally, submit your feedback by selecting this option.
	+ Options: Submit, Other
