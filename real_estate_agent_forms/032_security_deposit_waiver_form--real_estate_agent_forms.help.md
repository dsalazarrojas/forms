# Security Deposit Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Security Deposit Waiver Form is a document that allows tenants to waive their right to a security deposit for a rental property. This form is usually used in residential settings to offer incentives such as rent discounts or other benefits to tenants in exchange for waiving their security deposit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name as the tenant.
2. Provide the name and contact information of the landlord or property manager.
3. Specify the frequency of rent payments (e.g., weekly, biweekly, monthly).
4. Enter the amount of the security deposit you are waiving.
5. Provide the date you are waiving your security deposit.
6. Indicate the start date of your rent period.
7. Indicate the end date of your rent period.
8. Enter the address of the property you are waiving the security deposit for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **tenant** (<name>, <type> text, required: false): Enter your name as the tenant.
* **landlord** (<name>, <type> text, required: false): Provide the name and contact information of the landlord or property manager.
* **rent_frequency** (<name>, <type> text, required: false): Specify the frequency of rent payments (e.g., weekly, biweekly, monthly).
* **deposit_amount** (<name>, <type> number, required: false): Enter the amount of the security deposit you are waiving.
* **deposit_date** (<name>, <type> date, required: false): Provide the date you are waiving your security deposit.
* **rent_start_date** (<name>, <type> date, required: false): Indicate the start date of your rent period.
* **rent_end_date** (<name>, <type> date, required: false): Indicate the end date of your rent period.
* **property_address** (<name>, <type> text, required: false): Enter the address of the property you are waiving the security deposit for.

## Tips
- Make sure to carefully review the fields to ensure you are completing the form accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the landlord or property manager directly.
- This form is only for residential properties.
