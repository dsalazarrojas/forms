# real_estate_ethics_certification_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a real estate agent's ethics certification status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your license details.
2. Provide your contact information.
3. Choose whether or not you are certified.
4. Read and agree to the terms of the agreement.
5. Choose whether or not the training provider is correct.
6. If you are certified, choose "Yes" for the agreement.
7. If you are not certified, choose "No" for the agreement.
8. Choose whether or not the real estate school is correct.
9. Enter the name of the real estate provider.
10. Enter the name of the real estate school.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **License Info** (`real_estate_license`, `text`, required): This field is used to enter your real estate license number.
* **Contact** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): This field is used to enter your contact information.
* **Certification** (`certification_info`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to choose whether or not you are certified.
* **Agreement** (`agreement_info`, `note`, not required): This field is used to view the agreement terms.
* **License Details** (`license_details`, `select_multiple`, not required): This field is used to choose whether or not you have multiple licenses.
* **Real Estate License** (`real_estate_license`, `text`, not required): This field is used to enter your real estate license number.
* **Ethics Code** (`ethics_code`, `text`, not required): This field is used to enter your real estate ethics code.
* **Real Estate School** (`real_estate_school`, `text`, not required): This field is used to enter the name of your real estate school.
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`, `select_one`, not required): This field is used to choose whether or not you used a training provider.
* **Agreement** (`real_estate_agreed`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to choose whether or not you agree to the terms.
* **Real Estate Provider** (`real_estate_provider`, `text`, not required): This field is used to enter the name of your real estate provider.
* **Real Estate School** (`real_estate_school`, `text`, not required): This field is used to enter the name of your real estate school.
