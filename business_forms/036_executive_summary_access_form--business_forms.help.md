# executive_summary_access_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to aid executives in accessing and evaluating executive summaries. It gathers essential information to help make informed decisions quickly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your User Details (page 1).
2. Specify your Executive Summary Request (page 1).
3. Provide Report Details (page 1).
4. Describe your Decision Making Process (page 1).
5. Indicate the Availability of the Executive Summary (page 1).
6. Add any Additional Comments (page 1).
7. Consider the Business Impact (page 1).
8. Define your Decision Making Criteria (page 1).
9. Select your Executive Summary Access Level (page 2).
10. Decide on Access (page 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: User Details
* **User Details**: This field is for you to enter your personal details (name, role, etc.). (required: false)
* **Executive Summary Request**: In this field, describe the purpose of your executive summary request. (required: false)
* **Report Details**: Provide a brief description of the report you are requesting access to. (required: false)
* **Decision Making Process**: Explain your process for making decisions. (required: false)
* **Availability of the Executive Summary**: Specify when you expect the executive summary to be available. (required: false)
* **Additional Comments**: Add any extra information that might be relevant to your request. (required: false)
* **Business Impact**: Consider the potential impact of the executive summary on the business. (required: false)

### Page 2: 
* **Decision Making Criteria**: Define the criteria you use to make decisions. (required: false)
* **Executive Summary Availability**: Select the availability of the executive summary (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3). (required: false)
* **Access Decision**: Approve, Decline, or Hold on access to the executive summary. (required: false)

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate information gathering.
- If unsure about any field, refer to the provided hints or ask a colleague for help.
- Use clear and concise language to ensure the information is easily understandable.
