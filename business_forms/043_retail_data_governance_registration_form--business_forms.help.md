# Retail Data Governance Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for retail data governance projects within the organization. The goal is to ensure that all projects are properly registered and accounted for, aligning with the organization's data governance policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by filling out the **User Details** field, which will collect your name and other basic information.
- Move on to the **Purpose** field, where you can describe the primary objective of your project.
- In the **Retail Data Access** field, select the level of data access required for your project. This will help determine the scope of your project's data access permissions.
- Proceed to the **Project Details** field to provide a brief description of your project.
- Add your project lead's information in the **Project Lead** field.
- Enter your business unit in the **Business Unit** field to associate your project with the correct department.
- Indicate the current project status in the **Project Status** field, selecting from New, Active, or Closed.
- Write a detailed description of your project in the **Project Description** field.
- If applicable, enter the date of approval for your project in the **Approval Date** field.
- Provide the name of the person who approved your project in the **Approval Person** field.
- List any project approvals required in the **Project Approvals** field.
- Lastly, provide any additional retail data governance approvals needed in the **Retail Data Governance Approvals** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, required): This is where you can enter your basic information, including your name and other relevant details.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, required): Please describe the main objective of your project.
* **Retail Data Access** (`retail_data_access`, select_one, required): Select the level of data access needed for your project, which will help in determining the scope of data access permissions.
*   * Retail Data Access: Limited access to specific areas of the data.
*   * Full Data Access: Full access to all available data.
*   * Limited Data Access: Limited access to specific areas of the data.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your project.
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, text, required): Enter the name of the person leading your project.
* **Business Unit** (`business_unit`, text, required): Select the business unit associated with your project.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required): Indicate the current status of your project, selecting from New, Active, or Closed.
*   * New: The project is new and has not started yet.
*   * Active: The project is currently ongoing.
*   * Closed: The project has been completed.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): Write a detailed description of your project.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, required): Enter the date of approval for your project, if applicable.
* **Approval Person** (`approval_person`, text, required): Provide the name of the person who approved your project.
* **Project Approvals** (`project_approvals`, text, required): List any project approvals required.
* **Retail Data Governance Approvals** (`retail_data_governance_approvals`, text, required): Provide any additional retail data governance approvals needed.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your project details for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, please seek guidance from your supervisor or data governance team.
