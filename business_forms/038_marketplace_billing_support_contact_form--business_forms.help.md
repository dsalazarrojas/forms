# Marketplace Billing Support Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketplace Billing Support Contact Form is used to gather information from customers or clients about their issues with invoices and provide a way for them to contact the billing team for assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Describe your issue with your invoice in the text field labeled "What is your issue with your invoice?".
2. Enter your email address in the field labeled "Email".
3. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "Phone".
4. If you would like to do something with your invoice, select all that apply in the field labeled "What would you like to do?".
5. If you are available to discuss further, enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY in the field labeled "When are you available to discuss further?".
6. If you would like to be contacted at your work or home, select one option in the field labeled "Can we call you at your work or home?".
7. If there's anything else you would like to share with us, you can type it in the field labeled "Is there anything else we should know about your inquiry?".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your issue with your invoice?** (Text): Describe your issue in detail so we can assist you better.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address so we can contact you to discuss your issue.
* **Phone** (Phone): Enter your phone number so we can contact you directly.
* **What would you like to do?** (Select Multiple): Select all that apply to let us know what you would like to do with your invoice.
	+ Cancel Invoice: Cancel your invoice.
	+ Update Invoice: Update your invoice.
	+ Resend Invoice: Resend your invoice.
* **When are you available to discuss further?** (Date): Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY when you are available to discuss further.
* **Can we call you at your work or home?** (Select One): Select one option to let us know where you would like to be contacted.
	+ Work: Call you at work.
	+ Home: Call you at home.
	+ No Preference: We will not contact you.
* **Is there anything else we should know about your inquiry?** (Note): Add any additional information that you think would be helpful to help us assist you better.

## Tips
Please be as detailed as possible when describing your issue with your invoice, and make sure to include any relevant information that may be helpful to us in resolving your issue.
