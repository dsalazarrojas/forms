# Refrigeration Energy Review Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a refrigeration energy review for a specific system. The goal of this form is to gather information about the system being reviewed, its energy consumption patterns, and the goals for the energy review.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with all required and optional information.
2. Be as detailed as possible when providing information about the system being reviewed.
3. Make sure to provide accurate contact information so that the reviewer can follow up with questions and provide feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data**: A text field to provide general information about the system being reviewed.
* **Building System Info**: A text field to provide information about the building's system, including its components and their specifications.
* **Refrigeration Data**: A text field to provide information about the refrigeration system, including its type, model, and manufacturer.
* **System Description**: A text field to describe the system in detail.
* **Building Location**: A text field to provide the location of the building where the system is installed.
* **Energy Consumption Details**: A text field to provide information about the energy consumption patterns of the system.
* **System Characteristics**: A text field to provide information about the system's characteristics, such as its size and capacity.
* **Assessment Schedule**: A text field to provide information about the schedule for the energy review and assessment.
* **Contact Person**: A text field to provide the name of the contact person responsible for the system.
* **Contact Email**: An email field to provide the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone**: A text field to provide the contact person's phone number.
* **System Manufacturer**: A text field to provide the manufacturer of the system.
* **System Model**: A text field to provide the model number of the system.
* **System Type**: A text field to provide the type of system (e.g., refrigeration, air conditioning, etc.).
* **System Capacity**: A number field to provide the capacity of the system.
* **Energy Usage Patterns**: A text field to provide information about the energy usage patterns of the system.
* **Energy Usage Patterns Details**: A text field to provide more detailed information about the energy usage patterns.
* **Energy Usage Patterns Frequency**: A text field to provide the frequency of the energy usage patterns.
* **Energy Consumption Data**: A text field to provide more detailed information about the energy consumption of the system.
* **Energy Consumption Data Frequency**: A text field to provide the frequency of the energy consumption data.
* **Energy Saving Goals**: A text field to provide the goals for energy savings.
* **System Description Details**: A text field to provide more detailed information about the system.
* **System Characteristics Details**: A text field to provide more detailed information about the system's characteristics.
* **Assessment Notes**: A note field to provide any additional notes about the system and the energy review.
* **System Status**: A select multiple field to select all statuses that apply to the system.
* **Energy Rating**: A number field to provide a rating of the system's energy efficiency on a scale from 1 to 10.

Note: If you have any questions or need help filling out this form, please contact us at [your email or phone number].
