# Investor Milestone Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investor Milestone Report Form is used to collect and track milestone progress and status from investors. This form is intended to be completed by project managers or team members to provide updates on project status and obtain signoff from investors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct page from the drop-down menu, which includes First Page, Second Page, Third Page, and so on.
2. Complete each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page**: Enter a brief description of the project or milestone.
* **Second Page**: Enter any additional comments or information regarding the project.
* **Third Page**: Enter any further comments or information regarding the project.
* **Investor Comments**: Enter comments from the investor regarding the project.
* **Milestones**: Select the current status of the milestone (Yes or No).
* **Business Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information regarding the business.
* **Expected Completion Date**: Enter the expected completion date of the milestone.
* **Actual Completion Date**: Enter the actual completion date of the milestone.
* **Milestone Status**: Select the current status of the milestone (Active or Inactive).
* **Investor Signoff**: Select whether the investor has signed off on the milestone (Yes or No).
* **Investor Signoff Comment**: Enter comments from the investor regarding signoff.
* **Milestone Signoff Status**: Select the status of the milestone signoff (Active or Inactive).
* **Signoff Comment**: Enter comments regarding signoff.
* **Signoff Status**: Select the status of the signoff (Active or Inactive).
* **Investor Signoff Comment 2**: Enter comments from the investor regarding signoff 2.
* **Business Signoff Status**: Select the status of the business signoff (Active or Inactive).
* **Signoff Comments**: Enter comments regarding signoff.
* **Investor Signoff Date**: Enter the date of the investor signoff.
* **Business Signoff Date**: Enter the date of the business signoff.
* **Signoff Status 2**: Select the status of the signoff (Active or Inactive).
* **Final Signoff Date**: Enter the date of the final signoff.
* **Final Signoff Complete**: Select whether the final signoff is complete (Yes or No).
* **Final Signoff Status**: Select the status of the final signoff (Active or Inactive).
* **Final Signoff Comments**: Enter comments regarding final signoff.
* **Final Signoff Complete (2)**: Select whether the final signoff is complete (Yes or No).

Note: The date fields are expected to be in the format MM/dd/yyyy.
