# Brewery Export Documentation Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brewery Export Documentation Order Form is a crucial document for breweries to provide accurate and complete information about their export shipments. This form helps the exporter and importer to ensure that all necessary documents and information are exchanged, reducing the risk of errors and potential issues during shipping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Make sure to provide all necessary documents and information.
3. Double-check your answers to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Brewery Name** (Required): Enter the name of your brewery.
* **Brewery Address** (Required): Enter your brewery's physical location.
* **Contact Person** (Required): Enter the name of the person you would like to contact for this export documentation.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your business phone number.
* **Order Date** (Required): Enter the date of this documentation request.
* **Export Destination Country** (Required): Enter the country where the beer is being sent.
* **Importer Name** (Required): Enter the name of the receiving company.
* **Importer Address** (Required): Enter the address of the importing company.
* **Expected Shipment Date** (Required): Enter the date when the shipment is expected to depart.
* **Shipment Method** (Required): Select the method of shipment (e.g., Ocean container, Air cargo, etc.).
* **Port of Export** (Not Required): Enter the departure port or terminal.
* **Port of Entry** (Not Required): Enter the destination port or terminal.
* **Beer Products in Shipment** (Required): List the beer products being shipped and their quantities.
* **Total Shipment Volume** (Required): Enter the volume in barrels or liters.
* **Total Shipment Value** (Required): Enter the FOB or CIF value.
* **Required Documentation** (Required): Select the documents needed for this shipment.
* **Special Regulatory Requirements** (Optional): If there are any country-specific regulations, list them here.
* **Insurance Coverage** (Optional): Select if shipment will be insured.
* **Incoterms** (Required): Select the international commercial terms (e.g., FOB, CIF, etc.).
* **Timeline for Documentation** (Optional): Select when the documents are needed (e.g., Within 1 week, Within 2 weeks, etc.).
* **Additional Requirements** (Optional): Enter any special instructions or requirements for this shipment.
