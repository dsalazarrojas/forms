# Distribution And Promotion Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by businesses to create and manage distribution and promotion agreements with partners or third parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company's name in the "Business Name" field.
2. Choose the type of agreement that best describes the agreement you are creating in the "Agreement Type" field.
3. Select the date on which the agreement term begins and ends in the "Agreement Term" field.
4. Upload any supporting files or attachments related to the agreement in the "Agreement Files" field.
5. Review and submit the form to create a formal distribution and promotion agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your company's name and name that will be associated with this agreement.
* **Agreement Files** (`agreement_files`, text, required/optional): Upload any supporting files or attachments related to this agreement, such as documents or contracts.
* **Agreement Type** (`agreement_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of agreement that best describes the agreement you are creating. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Agreement Term** (`agreement_term`, date, required/optional): Select the date on which the agreement term begins and ends.
