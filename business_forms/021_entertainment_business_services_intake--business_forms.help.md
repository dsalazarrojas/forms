# Entertainment Business Services Intake - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entertainment Business Services Intake form is designed to collect information from clients and agencies regarding their entertainment business needs and services required. This form helps us understand their specific requirements, ensuring that we can provide tailored support and services to meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the available fields and ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). You can click on the field label to view available options, if applicable.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New client inquiries** (Required, Text): Provide a brief description of your new client inquiries, including any relevant details about their business or services required.
* **Event details** (Required, Text): Describe the event or services you are seeking for the client, including any specific requirements or requests.
* **Budgets** (Required, Number): Enter the budget allocated for the event or services you are seeking.
* **Contact preferences** (Required, Select Multiple): Select how you would like to be contacted: Agency, Venue, or Creative team. This will help us tailor our communication with you.
* **Event type** (Required, Select One): Choose the type of event you are seeking: Live Performance, Event, or Other (please specify in the text field).
* **Event start date** (Required, Date): Enter the start date of the event.
* **Event end date** (Required, Date): Enter the end date of the event.
* **Event start time** (Required, Time): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event end time** (Required, Time): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Location** (Required, Select One): Choose the location for the event: Studio, Theater, Arena, or Other (please specify in the text field).
* **Event description** (Required, Text): Provide a detailed description of the event or services you are seeking.
* **Client email** (Required, Email): Enter the email address of the client you are representing.
* **Client phone** (Required, Text): Enter the phone number of the client.
* **Agency email** (Required, Email): Enter the email address of your agency.
* **Agency phone** (Required, Text): Enter the phone number of your agency.

Note: Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely to ensure we can provide you with the best possible service and support.
