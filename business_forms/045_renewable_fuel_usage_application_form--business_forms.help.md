# Renewable Fuel Usage Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Renewable Fuel Usage Application Form is used by businesses and organizations to apply for the use of renewable fuel in their operations. This form collects essential information about the applicant's organization, intended use of the fuel, and other relevant details to facilitate the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your organization's name and details in the "Organization" field.
2. Describe the intended use of the renewable fuel in the "Intended Use" field.
3. Specify the expected quantity of fuel required in the "Expected Quantity" field.
4. Select the supporting documents that you are submitting with your application.
5. Choose the document type from the options provided.
6. Enter the date and time of when you would like to start using the renewable fuel. If this field is not required, you can leave it blank.
7. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
8. Enter your email address (if applicable).
9. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field (if applicable).

• **Organization** (text, required): Enter your organization's name, including the full name and any relevant details.

### Expected Quantity (Required)

### Document Type (Required)

### Time (Optional)
