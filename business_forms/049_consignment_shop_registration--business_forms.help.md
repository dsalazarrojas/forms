# Consignment Shop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The consignment shop registration form is designed to help us get to know you better and set up a partnership for your shop to list and sell items on our platform. This form is a necessary step to start listing items on our shop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide accurate information for each field to ensure a smooth and secure registration process.
2. Fill out all required fields marked as required.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.
4. You may contact our support team if you need assistance with the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date** (1, Required): Date of registration.
* **Consignor Name** (2, Required): Your full name for contact and record keeping purposes.
* **Email Address** (3, Required): A valid contact email for communication and shop updates.
* **Phone Number** (4, Required): A contact number for shop calls and messages.
* **Address** (5, Required): Your shop's mailing address for sending information and packages.
* **City** (6, Required): Your shop's city.
* **State** (7, Required): Your shop's state or province.
* **Postal Code** (8, Required): Your shop's ZIP or postal code.
* **Date of Birth** (9, Required): Date of birth, age 18 or older.
* **Are You Under 18** (10, Required): A status indicator for minors, which is required if you are under 18.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (11, Optional): If you are under 18, please provide your parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (12, Optional): Required if you are under 18, for parental consent.
* **Tax ID or SSN** (13, Required): Your tax ID or SSN for tax reporting and financial purposes.
* **Payment Preference** (14, Required): A choice of payment methods.
* **Commission Tier** (15, Required): A selection of commission levels for your shop.
* **Consignment Categories** (16, Required): Select all categories your shop offers.
* **Minimum Item Condition** (17, Required): Select a quality level for items listed on the platform.
* **Pricing Method** (18, Required): Choose how you set prices for your items.
* **Consignment Period** (19, Required): A choice of how long your items will be listed on the platform.
* **Markdown Agreement** (20, Required): A choice to agree to price reductions or not.
* **Markdown Schedule** (21, Optional): If you choose Markdown Agreement - Yes - With notification, please provide a schedule for markdowns.
* **Unsold Items Policy** (22, Required): Choose what to do with unsold items.
* **Pickup Deadline** (23, Optional): If you choose to Pick up by consignor, please provide a deadline for item pickup.
* **Item Limit** (24, Required): Select the maximum items you can list at once.
* **Quality Standards Acknowledged** (25, Required): Indicate if you understand quality requirements.
* **Prohibited Items Understood** (26, Required): Indicate if you know what items cannot be consigned.
* **Prohibited Items** (27, Required): Select all items that do not apply to your shop.
* **Insurance Coverage** (28, Required): Choose who insures items.
* **Loss or Damage Policy Understood** (29, Required): Indicate if you understand loss policy.
* **Communication Preference** (30, Required): Select all ways you prefer communication.
* **Join Newsletter** (31, Required): Choose to receive updates and promotions.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (32, Optional): A choice of how you heard about us.
* **Terms and Conditions Accepted** (33, Required): Indicate if you agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Consignor Signature** (34, Required): A signature of your full name.
* **Registration Status** (35, Required): A status indicator for administrative purposes.

Note: Please make sure to review and agree to all terms and conditions before submitting this form.
