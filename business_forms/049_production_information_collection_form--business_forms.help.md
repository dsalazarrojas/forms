# Production Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Production Information Collection Form is designed to collect production information about a project or product.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the project name in the "Project Name" field.
2. Enter the product name in the "Product Name" field.
3. Select the product type from the "Product Type" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the production date in the "Production Date" field.
5. Enter the production time in the "Production Time" field.
6. Enter the production location in the "Production Location" field.
7. Enter the production manager's name in the "Production Manager" field.
8. Add any additional information in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter phone numbers in the "Phone Numbers" field.
10. Enter email addresses in the "Email Addresses" field.
11. Select the production team members from the "Production Team" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name** (project_name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the project.
* **Product Name** (product_name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the product.
* **Product Type** (product_type, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of the product from the dropdown menu.
* **Production Date** (production_date, date, required/optional): Enter the production date of the project or product.
* **Production Time** (production_time, time, required/optional): Enter the production time.
* **Production Location** (production_location, text, required/optional): Enter the location of production.
* **Production Manager** (production_manager, text, required/optional): Enter the production manager's name.
* **Notes** (notes, note, required/optional): Add any additional information.
* **Phone Numbers** (phone_numbers, text, required/optional): Enter phone numbers related to the production.
* **Email Addresses** (email_addresses, email, required/optional): Enter email addresses related to the production team or manager.
* **Production Team** (production_team, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the production team members from the dropdown menu.
