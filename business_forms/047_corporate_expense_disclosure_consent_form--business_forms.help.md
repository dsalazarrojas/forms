# Corporate Expense Disclosure Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Expense Disclosure Consent Form is a document designed to capture employee consent for reviewing and sharing business expenses. This form ensures that employees understand and agree to the disclosure of their business expenses, promoting transparency and compliance within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for the form.
2. Select the correct category for the form.
3. Enter a description of the form (optional).
4. Read the instructions provided for guidance on capturing employee consent for reviewing and sharing business expenses.
5. Enter the employee's name.
6. Enter the employee's title.
7. Enter the department the employee belongs to.
8. Enter the manager's name.
9. Enter the manager's title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Corporate Expense Disclosure Consent Form** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for the form.
* **Business Forms** (`form_category`, text, required): Select the category for the form.
* **Corporate Expense Disclosure Consent Form** (`form_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the form (optional).
* **Use the Corporate Expense Disclosure Consent Form from Jotform to capture employee consent for reviewing and sharing business expenses** (`form_instructions`, text, required): Read and understand the instructions provided.
* **Capture employee consent for reviewing and sharing business expenses** (`form_capture_expenses`, text, required): Enter the employee's consent for reviewing and sharing business expenses.
* **Employee Name** (`form_1`, text, required): Enter the employee's name.
* **Employee Title** (`form_1`, text, required): Enter the employee's title.
* **Department** (`form_1`, text, required): Enter the employee's department.
* **Manager Name** (`form_1`, text, required): Enter the manager's name.
* **Manager Title** (`form_1`, text, required): Enter the manager's title.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter the employee's and manager's names and titles accurately to avoid any confusion.
- Ensure that the employee understands the purpose and instructions of the form before obtaining their consent.
- Store the completed form securely to maintain the confidentiality of sensitive information.
