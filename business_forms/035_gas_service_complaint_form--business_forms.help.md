# Gas Service Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for customers to report any issues they have with our gas services. Please fill out this form if you have experienced any problems with your gas supply, meter, or service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your customer information.
2. Describe your complaint in detail.
3. Select the type of complaint you are experiencing.
4. Specify the location of the complaint.
5. Enter your gas meter serial number and meter ID.
6. Enter your contact information.
7. Provide any additional customer service request.
8. (Optional) Attach any relevant documents or images to support your complaint.
9. Select the user who will be assigned to review your complaint.
10. Update the status of your complaint.
11. Select the user who updated the complaint status.
12. Enter the date and time the complaint was updated.
13. Enter the date and time the complaint was created.
14. Enter any comments regarding your complaint.
15. (Optional) Enter the timestamp when the complaint was created.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer info** (`customer_info`, required): Enter your name, address, and contact information.
* **complaint details** (`complaint_details`, required): Describe the issue you are experiencing with your gas service.
* **complaint type** (`complaint_type`, required): Select one of the following:
	+ Gas Service Interruption
	+ No Gas Flow
	+ No Gas Scent
	+ Other
* **complaint location** (`complaint_location`, required): Select one or more locations where the complaint occurred:
	+ Home
	+ Business
	+ Outside
* **gas meter serial number** (`gas_meter_serial_number`, required): Enter your gas meter's serial number.
* **gas meter ID** (`gas_meter_meter_id`, required): Enter your gas meter's ID number.
* **contact number** (`contact_number`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **contact email** (`contact_email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **contact address** (`contact_address`, required): Enter your physical address.
* **customer service request** (`customer_service_request`, required): Describe your request or question.
* **customer service request attachments** (`customer_service_request_attachments`, optional): Attach any relevant documents or images to support your complaint.
* **assigned user** (`assigned_user`, required): Select the user who will review your complaint, but only choose Jimmy if you know him personally.
* **assigned user note** (`assigned_user_note`, optional): Enter any notes for the assigned user.
* **status** (`status`, required): Select the status of your complaint:
	+ Open
	+ Resolved
	+ Pending
* **updated by** (`updated_by`, required): Select the user who updated the complaint status.
* **updated on** (`updated_on`, optional): Enter the date and time the complaint was updated.
* **created by** (`created_by`, optional): Select the user who created the complaint.
* **created on** (`created_on`, optional): Enter the date and time the complaint was created.
* **comments** (`comments`, optional): Enter any comments regarding your complaint.
* **updated on timestamp** (`updated_on_timestamp`, optional): Enter the timestamp when the complaint was updated.
* **created on timestamp** (`created_on_timestamp`, optional): Enter the timestamp when the complaint was created.
