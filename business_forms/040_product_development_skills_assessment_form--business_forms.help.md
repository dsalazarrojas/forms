# product_development_skills_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the skills and project information of individuals within the company. The form helps to gather relevant data on project development projects, including team size, team location, skills, time spent, and project status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your contact phone number in the contact phone field.
3. Enter your contact email address in the contact email field.
4. If applicable, enter your project name in the project name field.
5. Provide a brief description of your project in the project description field (if applicable).
6. Select the number of members in your team.
7. Choose the location of your team from the options provided.
8. Select your primary skills for the project, which can include:
	* Project Management
	* Product Design
	* Product Development
9. Choose secondary skills for the project (if applicable).
10. Enter the type of product you are developing, which can include:
	* Software Product
	* Hardware Product
	* Service Product
11. Provide a description of your product (if applicable).
12. Select your team lead.
13. Select the project lead.
14. Choose secondary skills for the project (if applicable).
15. Enter the time spent on the project (in hours).
16. Select your skills for the project again.
17. Enter the time spent on the project (in hours).
18. Select your project status from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Contact Phone** (3, text, required): Enter your contact phone number here.
* **Contact Email** (4, email, required): Enter your contact email address here.
* **Project Name** (5, text, optional): If you have a project, enter its name here (optional).
* **Project Description** (6, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your project here (if applicable).
* **Team Size** (7, number, optional): Select the number of members in your team.
* **Team Location** (8, select_one, required): Choose the location of your team from the options provided.
* **Skills 1** (9, select_multiple, required): Select your primary skills for the project, which can include:
	+ Project Management
	+ Product Design
	+ Product Development
* **Skills 2** (10, select_one, required): Choose your secondary skills for the project (if applicable).
* **Skills 3** (11, select_multiple, optional): Choose secondary skills for the project (if applicable).
* **Skills 4** (12, select_multiple, optional): Choose secondary skills for the project (if applicable).
* **Time Spent** (13, time, required): Enter the time spent on the project (in hours).
* **Skills 5** (14, select_one, required): Select your skills for the project again.
* **Skills 6** (15, select_multiple, optional): Choose secondary skills for the project (if applicable).
* **Product Type** (16, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of product you are developing, which can include:
	+ Software Product
	+ Hardware Product
	+ Service Product
* **Product Description** (17, text, optional): Provide a description of your product (if applicable).
* **Team Lead** (18, select_one, optional): Select your team lead.
* **Project Lead** (19, select_one, optional): Select the project lead.
* **Skills 7** (20, select_multiple, optional): Choose secondary skills for the project (if applicable).
* **Skills 8** (21, select_multiple, optional): Choose secondary skills for the project (if applicable).
* **Time** (22, time, required): Enter the time spent on the project (in hours).
* **Skills 9** (23, select_multiple, optional): Choose secondary skills for the project (if applicable).
* **Project Status** (24, select_multiple, optional): Select your project status from the options provided.
* **Project Lead Email** (25, email, optional): Enter the email of your project lead (if applicable).

This form is designed to be completed by the person who is actually responsible for the project, or by the authorized person who has been delegated by the project lead.
