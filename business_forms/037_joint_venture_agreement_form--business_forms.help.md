# Joint Venture Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture the essential details of a joint venture agreement, including the project name, partner participation, agreement terms, and other relevant clauses. By filling out this form, all parties involved can refer to it for a clear understanding of their mutual obligations and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Project Name** field with the name of the joint venture project.
2. For the **Partners** field, select the participation status of each partner.
3. Enter the **Partner Percentage** as a percentage value.
4. In the **Agreement Terms** field, describe the terms and conditions of the partnership.
5. Enter the **Term Length** in years.
6. Select the **Term Start Date** and **Term End Date** for the partnership term.
7. In the **Termination Clauses** field, describe the clauses for termination of the partnership.
8. Describe the **Payment Terms** and **Payment Schedule** for the partnership.
9. In the **Dispute Resolution** field, describe how disputes will be resolved.
10. Enter the **Governing Law** of the partnership.
11. Enter the **Jurisdiction** of the partnership.
12. List the **Signatories** of the partnership.
13. Enter the **Signature Date** and **Signed By** fields to signify agreement and signature of all signatories.
14. Enter the **Joint Venture Agreement Version** to track versions of the agreement.
15. Enter the **Last Updated** date to keep track of updates made to the agreement.
16. Enter the **Created At** and **Updated At** dates for record keeping.
17. Enter the **Created By** and **Updated By** to record the creators and updators of the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name**: <em>(Required)</em>: The name of the joint venture project.
* **Partners**: <em>(Required)</em>: Select 'Yes' if the partner is participating, 'No' if not.
* **Partner Percentage**: <em>(Required)</em>: The percentage of the partnership that this partner holds.
* **Agreement Terms**: <em>(Required)</em>: Describe the terms and conditions of the partnership.
* **Term Length**: <em>(Required)</em>: The length of the partnership term in years.
* **Term Start Date**: <em>(Required)</em>: The start date of the partnership term.
* **Term End Date**: <em>(Required)</em>: The end date of the partnership term.
* **Termination Clauses**: <em>(Required)</em>: Describe the clauses for termination of the partnership.
* **Payment Terms**: <em>(Required)</em>: Describe the payment terms and conditions.
* **Payment Schedule**: <em>(Required)</em>: Describe the payment schedule and frequency.
* **Dispute Resolution**: <em>(Required)</em>: Describe how disputes will be resolved between partners.
* **Governing Law**: <em>(Required)</em>: The law that governs the partnership.
* **Jurisdiction**: <em>(Required)</em>: The jurisdiction of the partnership.
* **Signatories**: <em>(Required)</em>: List the signatories of the partnership.
* **Signature Date**: <em>(Required)</em>: The date of signature of all signatories.
* **Signed By**: <em>(Required)</em>: The signatory who signed the agreement.
* **Signature Date**: <em>(Required)</em>: The date of signature of all signatories.
* **Signed By**: <em>(Required)</em>: The signatory who signed the agreement.
* **Joint Venture Agreement Version**: <em>(Required)</em>: Track the version of the agreement.
* **Last Updated**: <em>(Required)</em>: Record the last update date of the agreement.
* **Created At**: <em>(Required)</em>: Record the creation date of the agreement.
* **Updated At**: <em>(Required)</em>: Record the last update date of the agreement.
* **Created By**: <em>(Required)</em>: Record the creator of the agreement.
* **Updated By**: <em>(Required)</em>: Record the updator of the agreement.
