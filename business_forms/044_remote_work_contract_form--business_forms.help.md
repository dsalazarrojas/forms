# Remote Work Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information necessary for a remote work contract, which is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of remote work arrangements between a company and an employee. It will help ensure that all remote work contracts are completed consistently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following sections:

1. Company information:
	* Enter the name of the company.
	* Enter the job title of the employee.
2. Employee information:
	* Enter the name of the employee.
	* Enter the email address of the employee.
	* Enter the phone number of the employee.
3. Job details:
	* Enter the start date of the remote work arrangement.
	* Enter the end date of the remote work arrangement.
	* Enter the job title of the employee.
	* Enter the job description.
4. Work arrangement:
	* Enter the type of work arrangement (e.g. full-time, part-time, freelance).
	* Enter the work schedule.
5. Payment information:
	* Enter the pay rate of the employee.
	* Enter the pay frequency (e.g. weekly, biweekly, monthly).
6. Benefits and emergency contact:
	* Enter any benefits provided by the company.
	* Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact.
7. Company information:
	* Enter the address of the company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company name**: Please enter the name of the company.
* **Employee name**: Please enter the name of the employee.
* **Role**: Please enter the role of the employee.
* **Job title**: Please enter the job title of the employee.
* **Start date**: Please enter the start date of the remote work arrangement.
* **End date**: Please enter the end date of the remote work arrangement.
* **Work arrangement**: Please enter the type of work arrangement (e.g. full-time, part-time, freelance).
* **Work schedule**: Please enter the work schedule.
* **Pay rate**: Please enter the pay rate of the employee.
* **Pay frequency**: Please enter the pay frequency (e.g. weekly, biweekly, monthly).
* **Benefits**: Please enter any benefits provided by the company.
* **Emergency contact name**: Please enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact phone number**: Please enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact email**: Please enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact relationship**: Please enter the relationship between the employee and the emergency contact.
* **Company address**: Please enter the address of the company.
* **Manager name**: Please enter the name of the manager.
* **Manager email**: Please enter the email address of the manager.
* **Manager phone number**: Please enter the phone number of the manager.
* **Job URL**: Please enter the URL where the job description can be found.
* **Job description**: Please enter the description of the job.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your manager or HR representative for clarification.
* This form is meant to be completed once the remote work arrangement is established.
