# Lighting Failure Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and track information related to lighting failure incidents, including the description of the failure, location, date, and time of the incident, as well as any attachments and follow-up actions required. The form is intended for use by employees and reporting officers to report and track lighting failures within their designated areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Lighting Failure Description**: Provide a brief description of the lighting failure, including any relevant details.
2. **Lighting Failure Location**: Specify the location where the failure occurred.
3. **Lighting Failure Date**: Enter the date of the failure (optional).
4. **Lighting Failure Time**: Enter the time of the failure (optional).
5. **Lighting Failure Duration**: Provide the duration of the failure (optional).
6. **Lighting Failure Cause**: Specify the cause of the failure (optional).
7. **Lighting Failure Effect**: Describe the effect of the failure (optional).
8. **Assigned To**: Identify the person responsible for addressing the failure.
9. **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool or equipment used to address the failure (optional).
10. **Followup Action**: Choose the follow-up action required, such as No Action Required, Additional Analysis Required, or Repair/Replace Required.
11. **Followup Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments related to the follow-up action.
12. **Followup Deadline**: Enter the deadline for completing the follow-up action (optional).
13. **Followup Completed**: Record the date when the follow-up action was completed (optional).
14. **Followup Status**: Select the status of the follow-up action, such as Complete, In Progress, or Not Started.
15. **Followup Description**: Provide a brief description of the follow-up action (optional).
16. **Followup Comments**: Enter any additional comments related to the follow-up action (optional).
17. **Lighting Failure Priority**: Select the priority level of the failure, such as Low, Medium, or High.
18. **Reporting Officer**: Identify the person reporting the failure.
19. **Reporting Date**: Enter the date of the report (optional).
20. **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations related to the incident.
21. **Attachments**: Upload any relevant attachments, such as images or videos, to support the report.
22. **Submission Date**: Record the date when the form was submitted.
