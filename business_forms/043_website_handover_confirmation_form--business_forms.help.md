# Website Handover Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain confirmation from the client regarding the handover of a website, ensuring that both parties are on the same page and that the handover process is finalized accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each page and field purpose.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, paying attention to the required fields.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Website Details** (`page_1`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the website being handed over, its URL, and a brief description of the website.
* **Client Info** (`page_2`, `select_one`, required): Select the client's name and email from the provided options to ensure that the correct client is being addressed.
* **Transfer Details** (`page_3`, `note`, required): Describe the method and date of the website transfer, providing a clear and concise explanation.
* **Confirm Transfer** (`page_4`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm or deny the transfer, selecting the correct option to finalize the handover process.
* **Transfer Date** (`page_5`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the website transfer, using the correct date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Transfer Time** (`page_6`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the website transfer, in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Transfer Notes** (`page_7`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the transfer, if necessary.
* **Client Confirmations** (`page_8`, `select_one`, required): Check the confirmations box to indicate that the client has confirmed the transfer.
* **Confirmations** (`page_9`, `select_one`, required): Confirm or deny the final confirmations, selecting the correct option to finalize the process.
* **Finalize Confirmations** (`page_10`, `select_one`, required): Confirm or deny the finalize confirmations, selecting the correct option to complete the form.
* **Client Notes** (`page_11`, `text`, optional): Add any notes or comments from the client.
* **Page 12** (`page_12`, `select_one`, required): Confirm or deny the finalize confirmations, selecting the correct option to complete the form.

## Tips
* Always review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Use the correct format for dates and times.
* Make sure to select the correct options for each field to ensure that the form is submitted correctly.
