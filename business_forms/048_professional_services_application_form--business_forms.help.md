# professional_services_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a structured way for applicants to submit their application for professional services. Please use this form to provide accurate and detailed information about your experience, skills, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the job title and type that best match your qualifications and experience.
2. Fill in the years of experience and key achievements relevant to the selected job title and type.
3. Upload your resume in PDF or Word format.
4. Provide a brief description of your work experience.
5. List your skills and education background.
6. If you are applying for a contract, provide the contract details, including contract length, type, start and end dates, and notice period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the available job titles that best match your qualifications and experience.
* **Job Type** (`Job Type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more job types that you are interested in (e.g., Full Time, Part Time, Project Based).
* **Years of Experience** (`Years of Experience`, `number`, not required): Provide the number of years you have spent in your field.
* **Key Achievements** (`Key Achievements`, `text`, not required): Briefly describe your key achievements and accomplishments in your field.
* **Resume** (`Resume`, `text`, not required): Upload your resume in PDF or Word format.
* **Experience Details** (`Experience Details`, `note`, not required): Provide a brief description of your work experience.
* **Skills** (`Skills`, `text`, not required): List your relevant skills and qualifications.
* **Education Background** (`Education Background`, `text`, not required): Provide your educational background and any relevant certifications.
* **References** (`References`, `text`, not required): Provide contact information for professional references.
* **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, `note`, not required): Provide a brief description of your work experience.
* **Job Location** (`Job Location`, `text`, not required): List the location where you are willing to work.
* **Notice Period** (`Notice Period`, `time`, not required): Provide the notice period you require before starting or ending a contract.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, `text`, not required): Describe your availability and any restrictions on your work schedule.
* **Notice Start Date** (`Notice Start Date`, `date`, not required): If you are applying for a contract, provide the expected start date of your notice period.
* **Notice End Date** (`Notice End Date`, `date`, not required): If you are applying for a contract, provide the expected end date of your notice period.
* **Contract Length** (`Contract Length`, `number`, not required): If you are applying for a contract, provide the length of the contract.
* **Contract Type** (`Contract Type`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the available contract types (e.g., Fixed, Hourly, Project Based).
* **Contract Start Date** (`Contract Start Date`, `date`, not required): If you are applying for a contract, provide the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date** (`Contract End Date`, `date`, not required): If you are applying for a contract, provide the end date of the contract.
* **Hourly Rate** (`Hourly Rate`, `number`, not required): If you are applying for a contract, provide your hourly rate.
* **Weekly Hours** (`Weekly Hours`, `number`, not required): If you are applying for a contract, provide the number of weekly hours you are willing to work.
* **Notice Pay** (`Notice Pay`, `text`, not required): If you are applying for a contract, provide any notice pay required.
* **Payment Terms** (`Payment Terms`, `text`, not required): If you are applying for a contract, provide any payment terms you require.
