# Address Change Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to update your official address, ensuring that your information is accurate and up-to-date. Please fill out this form to notify us of any changes to your address.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
2.  Select the type of address change you are making from the dropdown menu.
3.  Fill in your new address details, including street, city, state, zip, and country.
4.  If you have updated your phone number, please enter it in the "Updated Contact Phone Number" field.
5.  Finally, confirm that the information provided is true and correct by selecting "I Solemly Declare" from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name / Legal Entity Name** (`declarant_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name or the name of your organization.
*   **Account / Member Number** (`account_id`, `text`, optional): If you have an account or member number, please enter it here. This will help us identify you for the update.
*   **Type of Address Change** (`declaration_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of address change you are submitting:
    *   Residential / Personal Move
    *   Business Headquarters Relocation
    *   Billing Address Update Only
    *   Seasonal / Temporary Change
*   **Effective Date of Change** (`effective_date`, `date`, required): When does the new address become active?
*   **Previous Address** (`old_address_header`, `note`, optional): This is a header for the next section, please fill in the details in the fields that follow.
*   **Full Previous Address** (`old_address_full`, `text`, required): Enter your previous address in the format street, city, state, zip, country.
*   **New Address** (`new_address_header`, `note`, optional): This is a header for the next section, please fill in the details in the fields that follow.
*   **New Street Address** (`new_street_address`, `text`, required): Enter your new street address.
*   **New City** (`new_city`, `text`, required): Enter your new city.
*   **New State / Province** (`new_state_province`, `text`, required): Enter your new state or province.
*   **New Zip / Postal Code** (`new_zip_postal_code`, `text`, required): Enter your new zip or postal code.
*   **New Country** (`new_country`, `text`, required): Enter your new country.
*   **Updated Contact Phone Number** (`updated_phone`, `text`, optional): If you have updated your phone number, please enter it here.
*   **I Solemnly Declare** (`legal_declaration_ack`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the information provided is true and correct.
*   **Date of Declaration** (`declaration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are submitting this declaration.
