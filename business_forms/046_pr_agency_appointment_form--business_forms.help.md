# pr_agency_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "pr_agency_appointment_form" is a form used to collect information from clients and agents for meeting appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the meeting details (Page 1).
2. Select the meeting date.
3. Choose the meeting time.
4. Enter the meeting location.
5. Enter any additional meeting notes.
6. If possible, provide preferred meeting times (up to 4 options).
7. Enter client details.
8. Provide contact information (phone number and email).
9. Confirm the meeting (True or False).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Details (Page 1)** (Page 1, Text, Required: No)
	+ Enter any additional details about the meeting.
* **meeting_date** (Page 2, Date, Required: Yes)
	+ Select the date of the meeting.
* **meeting_time** (Page 2, Time, Required: Yes)
	+ Select the time of the meeting.
* **meeting_location** (Page 2, Text, Required: Yes)
	+ Enter the location of the meeting.
* **meeting_notes** (Page 2, Text, Required: No)
	+ Enter any additional notes about the meeting.
* **preferred_time_1** (Page 2, Time, Required: No)
	+ Select an alternative meeting time if possible.
* **preferred_time_2** (Page 2, Time, Required: No)
	+ Select an alternative meeting time if possible.
* **preferred_time_3** (Page 2, Time, Required: No)
	+ Select an alternative meeting time if possible.
* **preferred_time_4** (Page 2, Time, Required: No)
	+ Select an alternative meeting time if possible.
* **client_details** (Page 2, Text, Required: Yes)
	+ Enter client details as requested.
* **contact_number** (Page 2, Text, Required: Yes)
	+ Enter the client's contact information (phone number).
* **email** (Page 2, Email, Required: Yes)
	+ Enter the client's email address.
* **confirm** (Page 2, Select One, Required: Yes)
	+ Confirm that the meeting information is correct (True or False).
