# Electrical Safety Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Electrical Safety Checklist Form is designed to help electrical system assessors identify potential hazards, evaluate electrical systems, and determine necessary repairs to ensure electrical safety.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the Electrical Systems Assessment section (Field 2) and provide a detailed evaluation of the electrical system's condition, including any observed hazards, defects, or concerns.
2. In the Safety Standards section (Field 3), list and describe the relevant safety standards that apply to the electrical system being assessed.
3. In the Necessary Repairs section (Field 4), detail any necessary repairs, replacements, or maintenance required to address the identified hazards or defects.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Electrical Systems Assessment** (`electrical_systems_assessment`, text, required/optional): Describe the condition of the electrical system, including any observed hazards, defects, or concerns.
* **Safety Standards** (`safety_standards`, text, required/optional): List and describe the relevant safety standards that apply to the electrical system being assessed.
* **Necessary Repairs** (`necessary_repairs`, text, required/optional): Detail any necessary repairs, replacements, or maintenance required to address the identified hazards or defects.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when evaluating the electrical system's condition.
* Ensure that you list all relevant safety standards applicable to the system.
* Prioritize repairs and maintenance based on the level of risk or severity of the hazards identified.
* Consult with a qualified electrician or engineer if you are unsure about any aspect of the electrical system's safety.
