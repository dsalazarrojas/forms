# legal_service_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a complaint made against a legal service provider. It helps to document the issue, category, and any supporting documents related to the complaint.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing a detailed description of the complaint in the "Complaint details" field.
2. Select the category of the complaint from the "Category" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the contact information in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
4. Specify the date and time of the occurrence of the complaint in the "Date of occurrence" and "Time of occurrence" fields.
5. Provide the address related to the complaint in the "Address" field.
6. Select the lawyer assigned to handle the complaint from the "Assigned to" dropdown menu.
7. Choose the status of the complaint from the "Complaint status" dropdown menu.
8. Indicate the priority of the complaint from the "Priority" dropdown menu.
9. Add any comments or additional information in the "Comments" field.
10. If required, attach supporting documents or files to the complaint by selecting the relevant options from the "Attachments" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint details**: A field where you can provide a detailed description of the issue you're experiencing with the legal service provider.
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes the type of complaint (Legal Issues, Business Matters, or Other (please specify)).
* **Email**: Enter the email address associated with the complaint.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number associated with the complaint.
* **Date of occurrence**: Enter the date when the issue occurred.
* **Time of occurrence**: Enter the time when the issue occurred.
* **Address**: Enter the address related to the complaint.
* **Assigned to**: Select the lawyer assigned to handle the complaint (Lawyer 1, Lawyer 2, or Lawyer 3).
* **Complaint status**: Choose the status of the complaint (Open, Closed, or On-going).
* **Priority**: Indicate the priority of the complaint (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or information related to the complaint.
* **Attachments**: Attach supporting documents or files related to the complaint by selecting the relevant options (Documents, Images, or Audio Files).
* **Attachment 1**: Attach a document or file to support the complaint (text input).
* This field and the following attachment fields (2-12) are for attaching specific supporting documents or files to the complaint.
* Each attachment field allows you to upload a text-based file with a brief description.
* Ensure that all the attachments fields are completed according to the required options for a proper complaint submission.
