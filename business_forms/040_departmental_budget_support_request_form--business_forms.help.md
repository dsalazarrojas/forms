# Departmental Budget Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a departmental budget request, providing essential details to support your department's financial needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Requestor Department in the **Requestor Department** field.
2. Provide a clear description of your request in the **Request Description** field.
3. Specify the **Requested Amount** needed for your request.
4. Enter the **Budget Start Date** and **Budget End Date** for your request.
5. (Optional) Attach supporting documents and/or attachments in the **Attach Documents** and **Attach Attachments** fields.
6. Select the team that should review your request from the **Review By** field (Finance Team or Admin Team).
7. Add any review comments in the **Review Comments** field (optional).
8. Finally, enter your name as the **Submitter**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor Department**: Enter the name of your department.
* **Request Description**: Provide a clear and concise description of the request you're making.
* **Requested Amount**: Enter the specific amount of budget support you're requesting.
* **Budget Start Date**: The date from which you require the budget support to begin.
* **Budget End Date**: The date by which you expect the budget support to end.
* **Attach Documents**: If applicable, attach any relevant documents to support your request. This can include invoices, receipts, or other proof of expenditure.
* **Attach Attachments**: Attach any additional files that may be relevant to your request. This can include images, spreadsheets, or other supporting materials.
* **Review By**: Select the team that should review your request: Finance Team or Admin Team.
* **Review Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback you'd like the reviewers to consider during the review process.
* **Submitter**: Enter your name as the person submitting the request.
