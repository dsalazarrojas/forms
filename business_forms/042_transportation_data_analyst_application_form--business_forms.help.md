# transportation_data_analyst_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help potential applicants apply for a data analyst position in our organization. It collects information about your background, skills, and experience to assess your qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your name and email address in the respective fields.
2. Enter your current or past phone number.
3. Provide information about your educational background, including the degree and major you earned.
4. If you have work experience, fill in the job title, start and end dates, and the name of the company you worked for.
5. Select your areas of expertise in data analysis, data visualization, and programming languages.
6. If you have any certifications, provide details about the name, date, and issuer of the certification.
7. Finally, attach any relevant attachments, such as a resume, cover letter, and portfolio.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name to identify yourself.
* **position_applied_for** (`position_applied_for`, `text`, required/optional): The title of the position you are applying for, likely Data Analyst.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your professional email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Your current or past phone number.
* **degree** (`degree`, `text`, required/optional): The degree you earned, e.g., Bachelor of Science in Computer Science.
* **major** (`major`, `text`, required/optional): The major you studied in college.
* **graduation_date** (`graduation_date`, `date`, required/optional): The date you graduated.
* **company** (`company`, `text`, required/optional): The name of the company you worked for.
* **job_title** (`job_title`, `text`, required/optional): The title of your job.
* **job_start_date** (`job_start_date`, `date`, required/optional): The start date of your job.
* **job_end_date** (`job_end_date`, `date`, required/optional): The end date of your job.
* **data_analysis** (`data_analysis`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more areas of data analysis you have expertise in.
* **data_visualization** (`data_visualization`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more areas of data visualization you have expertise in.
* **programming_languages** (`programming_languages`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more programming languages you have experience with.
* **certification_name** (`certification_name`, `text`, required/optional): The name of your certification.
* **certification_date** (`certification_date`, `date`, required/optional): The date of your certification.
* **certification_issuer** (`certification_issuer`, `text`, required/optional): The issuer of your certification.
* **resume** (`resume`, `text`, required/optional): Attach your resume for us to review.
* **cover_letter** (`cover_letter`, `text`, required/optional): Attach a cover letter introducing yourself.
* **application_form** (`application_form`, `text`, required/optional): Attach a completed application form for reference.
* **portfolio** (`portfolio`, `text`, required/optional): Attach a portfolio of your work.
* **submit** (`submit`, `text`, required/optional): Click to submit your application.
