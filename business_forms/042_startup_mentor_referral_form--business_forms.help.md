# Startup Mentor Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to refer a startup to a mentor who can provide guidance and support. It captures important details about the startup and the mentor, ensuring that the best match is made for both parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Organization Name** of the startup you are referring, which is the name of the company or organization that you represent.
2. Choose the **Mentorship Program** that you think would be the best fit for the startup.
3. Select the **Referral Source**, which is where you heard about the startup (e.g., "Internal Referral", "Social Media", etc.).
4. Enter the **Organization Description**, which is a brief description of the organization and its goals.
5. Enter the **Email** and **Confirm Email** of the person referring the startup.
6. Enter the **Phone** and **Confirm Phone** of the person referring the startup.
7. Enter the **Mentor's Name** and their **Position**.
8. Enter the **Bio** of the mentor, including a brief description of their experience and qualifications.
9. List the **Skills and Expertise** of the mentor, which are their areas of expertise that would be beneficial to the startup.
10. Describe the mentor's **Mentorship Experience**, including any relevant experience they have in mentoring or guiding startups.
11. Enter the **Mentor's Education**, which includes their academic background and any relevant certifications.
12. List the **Mentor's Interests**, which are areas they are passionate about and enjoy mentoring in.
13. Enter the **Mentor's Location**, which is where they are based.
14. Enter the **Mentor's Language**, which is the language they are proficient in.
15. Enter the **Availability**, which includes their work schedule and any conflicts they may have.
16. Choose the **Preferred Contact Method**, which is how the mentor would prefer to be contacted.
17. Enter the **Time Zone**, which is their time zone.
18. Enter the **Preferred Hours**, which are the time of day they are available to be contacted.
19. Enter the **Availability Time Frame**, which is a specific date and time they are available to be contacted.
20. Choose the **Preferred Days**, which are the days of the week they are available to be contacted.
21. Select the **Type of Mentorship** they are willing to provide, which can be One-to-One, Group, or Panel.
22. Finally, agree to the **Referral Agreement**, which is a contract that outlines the terms of the mentorship.

### Tips
- Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Be honest and thorough in your answers to ensure the best match is made for both the startup and the mentor.
- If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
- If you are referring multiple startups, please submit separate forms for each one.
