# Business Health Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Health Assessment Survey is a tool designed to gauge the overall health of your business unit. It assesses various aspects of your work environment, including management support, communication, operational efficiency, and employee satisfaction. This survey helps identify areas of strength and weakness, providing valuable insights to inform management decisions and drive positive change.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, please go through each section and answer the questions accordingly. Note that some sections are optional, but all required fields are indicated as such. 

1. **Section 1: Organizational Unit**
	* Enter your department or business unit in the "Department or Business Unit" field.
2. **Section 2: Management and Leadership**
	* Rate your satisfaction with management support on a scale of 1-5.
	* Rate how clearly information is shared on a scale of 1-5 (Communication Transparency Score).
3. **Section 3: Operational and Resource Health**
	* Choose how you usually feel about the tools and staff needed to do your job (Resource Adequacy for Tasks).
4. **Section 4: Overall Sentiment**
	* Describe the single biggest challenge facing your unit (Primary Operational Issue).
	* Suggest ways to improve business health (Management Improvement Recommendations).
5. **Section 5: Overall Ratings**
	* Rate your overall job satisfaction on a scale from 1 (Low) to 10 (High) (Overall Job Satisfaction Rating).
	* Choose how likely you are to recommend this company as a place to work (Willingness to Recommend Organization).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Department or Business Unit (Section 1: Organizational Unit)**:
	+ This field asks for the name of the department or business unit you belong to.
* **Operational Efficiency Rating (Section 2: Management and Leadership)**:
	+ Rate how smoothly day-to-day tasks run on a scale of 1-5.
* **Satisfaction with Management Support (Section 2: Management and Leadership)**:
	+ Rate your satisfaction with the support you receive from your immediate supervisor on a scale of 1-5.
* **Communication Transparency Score (Section 2: Management and Leadership)**:
	+ Rate how clearly information is shared on a scale of 1-5.
* **Alignment with Organizational Vision (Section 2: Management and Leadership)**:
	+ Choose how well you understand and support the company direction.
* **Resource Adequacy for Tasks (Section 3: Operational and Resource Health)**:
	+ Choose how often you feel you have the tools and staff needed to do your job.
* **Awareness of Growth and Development Opportunities (Section 3: Operational and Resource Health)**:
	+ Rate your awareness of paths for career advancement on a scale of 1-5.
* **Primary Operational Issue (Section 3: Operational and Resource Health)**:
	+ Describe the single biggest challenge facing your unit.
* **Recommendations for Management (Section 3: Operational and Resource Health)**:
	+ Suggest ways to improve business health.
* **Overall Job Satisfaction Rating (Section 4: Overall Sentiment)**:
	+ Rate your overall job satisfaction on a scale from 1 (Low) to 10 (High).
* **Willingness to Recommend Organization (Section 4: Overall Sentiment)**:
	+ Choose how likely you are to recommend this company as a place to work.
* **Assessment Date (Section 4: Overall Sentiment)**:
	+ Enter the date you completed this survey.

## Tips
Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
