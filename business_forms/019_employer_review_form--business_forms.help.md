# Employer Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employer Review Form" is used to collect feedback from employees on their performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about yourself as the employee, including your current job title and department.
2. Rate your overall performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
3. Identify areas where you have shown improvement and check 'Yes' for these areas.
4. Identify areas where you would like to see improvement and check 'Yes' for these areas.
5. Provide the date of the last review.
6. Enter your reviewer's name, job title, and email address.
7. Include any additional comments or notes from your reviewer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): Enter your current job title and department to provide context for your reviewer.
* **Staff Rating** (`staff_rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall performance on a scale of 1-5. This will help your reviewer understand your perspective on your performance.
* **Overall Performance** (`overall_performance`, number, optional): Enter the number of 1-5 that best reflects your current performance level.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, select_multiple, optional): Check 'Yes' for any areas where you have shown improvement and 'No' for areas where you would like to see improvement.
* **Development Opportunities** (`development_opportunities`, select_multiple, optional): Check 'Yes' for any areas where you would like to see growth and development opportunities.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last review.
* **Reviewer Info** (`reviewer_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes from your reviewer.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, optional): Enter your reviewer's name.
* **Reviewer Position** (`reviewer_position`, text, optional): Enter your reviewer's job title.
* **Reviewer Email** (`reviewer_email`, email, optional): Enter your reviewer's email address.
* **Reviewer Notes** (`reviewer_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes from your reviewer.
