# travel_insurance_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect and verify travel insurance information, primarily for business use.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all necessary information before filling it out.
2. Select the policy type and status as accurately as possible.
3. Enter the coverage start and end dates correctly.
4. Fill out the customer information fields with accurate details.
5. Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Policy Details** (`policy_details`, `note`, optional): This field is a note field where you can add any additional details about the policy.
- **Policy Type** (`policy_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of policy you are verifying ('Yes' or 'No').
- **Policy Status** (`policy_status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the status of the policy ('Active' or 'Inactive').
- **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the customer's information.
- **Coverage Start Date** (`coverage_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of the policy coverage.
- **Coverage End Date** (`coverage_end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of the policy coverage.
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their policy.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the customer's email address as it appears on their policy.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer's phone number as it appears on their policy.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
- If a field is optional, you can leave it blank if it's not applicable.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
