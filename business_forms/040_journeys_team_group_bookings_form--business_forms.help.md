# journeys_team_group_bookings_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a new group booking for a team within the organization. It is intended for booking team activities or events, such as meetings, workshops, or training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the team's group name.
2. Select the start date of the booking.
3. Select the end date of the booking.
4. Choose the start time of the booking.
5. Choose the end time of the booking.
6. Identify the team leader or person in charge of the group.
7. Enter the email address of the team leader or contact person.
8. Provide the phone number of the team leader or contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Group Name (team_group_name)**: A text field for the name of the team group making the booking. Enter the name of the team you represent.
* **Start Date (start_date)**: A date field for selecting the date when the activity or event will start. Use the date picker to choose the correct date.
* **End Date (end_date)**: A date field for selecting the date when the activity or event will end. Use the date picker to choose the correct date.
* **Booking Start Time (booking_start_time)**: A time field for selecting the start time of the activity or event. Use the time picker to choose the correct start time.
* **Booking End Time (end_time)**: A time field for selecting the end time of the activity or event. Use the time picker to choose the correct end time.
* **Team Leader (team_leader)**: A text field to identify the person in charge of the team making the booking.
* **Email (email)**: A field for entering the email address of the team leader or contact person.
* **Phone (phone)**: A field for entering the phone number of the team leader or contact person.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately to avoid form submission issues.
- Use the correct date and time format as provided in the date and time pickers.
- Verify your team group details and contact information before submitting the form.
