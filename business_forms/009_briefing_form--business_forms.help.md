# Briefing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Briefing Form is a tool to help you prepare for business briefings, meetings, and presentations by gathering essential information in one place. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to help you complete it efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Briefing Form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Participant Information section, which includes your full name, department, job title, and email address.
2. Select your preferred briefing category, such as "Project Update" or "Strategy Discussion."
3. Enter a title for the briefing and choose a preferred date and time.
4. Select the expected duration of the briefing and the preferred meeting format (In-Person, Video Conference, Phone Call, or Hybrid).
5. List the key agenda items to be discussed during the briefing.
6. Add any documents that need to be reviewed prior to the briefing.
7. List any specific questions that need to be addressed during the briefing.
8. Describe the expected outcomes from the briefing.
9. In the Additional Participants section, list any colleagues who need to be invited to the briefing.
10. If executive leadership needs to be present, indicate this in the Executive Participation Required field.
11. Finally, add any additional notes or comments in the Additional Notes or Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Colleague Name** (`colleague_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as a colleague participating in the briefing.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select your department from the options provided.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Briefing Category** (`briefing_category`, select_one, required): Select the category for this briefing from the options provided.
* **Briefing Title** (`briefing_title`, text, required): Enter a title for this briefing.
* **Preferred Briefing Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required): Select your preferred date for the briefing.
* **Preferred Briefing Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required): Select your preferred time for the briefing.
* **Duration Needed** (`duration_needed`, select_one, required): Select the expected duration of the briefing.
* **Meeting Format** (`meeting_format`, select_one, required): Select the preferred meeting format from the options provided.
* **Agenda Items** (`agenda_items`, text, required): List the key items to be discussed during the briefing.
* **Documents to Review** (`documents_attached`, text, optional): List any documents that should be reviewed prior to the briefing.
* **Key Questions to Address** (`key_questions`, text, optional): List any specific questions that need to be addressed during the briefing.
* **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, text, required): Describe what you hope to achieve from this briefing.
* **Additional Attendees** (`additional_attendees`, text, optional): List any additional colleagues who should be invited to the briefing.
* **Executive Participation Required** (`executive_participation`, select_one, optional): Indicate if executive leadership needs to be present.
* **Additional Notes or Comments** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant information.
