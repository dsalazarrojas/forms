# Tutor Recruiting Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tutor Recruiting Application form is designed to collect information from potential tutors who want to get hired for tutoring services. Fill out this form to apply as a tutor and get started with our tutoring service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Briefly describe your goal for seeking tutoring services in the "Goal" field.
6. Indicate your level of experience (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced) in the "Experience Level" field.
7. Select the days you are available to work in the "Available Days" field.
8. Specify the preferred time you want to work in the "Preferred Time" field.
9. Choose the preferred duration of your work in the "Preferred Duration" field (if applicable).
10. Enter any additional information in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact and scheduling purposes.
* **Goal** (`goal`, text, optional): Briefly describe your goal for seeking tutoring services.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your level of experience (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, select_multiple, required): Select the days you are available to work.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Specify the preferred time you want to work.
* **Preferred Duration** (`preferred_duration`, number, optional): Choose the preferred duration of your work (if applicable).
* **Message** (`message`, note, required): Enter any additional information about yourself and your availability.

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth application process.
- Double-check your availability and preferred time to ensure you are available for the selected days and time.
- You can upload any supporting documents (e.g., certificates, references) if required.
- Our team will review your application and get back to you with further instructions.
