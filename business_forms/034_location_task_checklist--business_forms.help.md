# location_task_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users track location-specific tasks and activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the relevant information for each field as it applies to your task or activity.
2. Use the "location_1" field to enter the first location associated with your task or activity.
3. Enter the contact person or organization responsible for the task or activity in the "contact_1" field.
4. Enter the manager's name or organization associated with the task or activity in the "manager_1" field.
5. Select the date of the task or activity using the "date_1" field.
6. Enter the time of the task or activity using the "time_1" field.
7. List any specific tasks or activities associated with the location in the "task_1", "task_2", "task_3", "task_4", and "task_5" fields.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about the task or activity in the "notes_1", "notes_2", and "notes_3" fields.
9. Enter any additional information about the location in the "location_2" field.

* **location_1** (Text, required): Enter the name of the first location associated with your task or activity.

* **time_1** (Time, required): Select the time of the task or activity.
