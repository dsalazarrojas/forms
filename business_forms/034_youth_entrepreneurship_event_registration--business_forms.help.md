# youth-entrepreneurship-event-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for a youth entrepreneurship event. It collects information on participant details, contact info, and event-related topics.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please provide your participant information in the first page.
- Proceed to provide your contact and address information.
- Fill in your payment details and event-related information.
- Provide business-related information, including business owner details.
- Answer event-specific questions and provide any relevant comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your participant name, age, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address to help us locate you at the event.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide your payment method and details.
* **Event Info** (`event_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter event-related details such as event date, time, and location.
* **Business Details** (`business_details`, `text`, required: false): Share your business details, if applicable.
* **Business Owner** (`business_owner`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business owner's name and contact details.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business address.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Email** (`business_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business email address.
* **Participant Acceptance** (`participant_acceptance`, `text`, required: false): Confirm your participation in the event.
* **Participant Contract** (`participant_contract`, `text`, required: false): Review and agree to the participant contract.
* **Participant Disagreement** (`participant_disagreement`, `text`, required: false): If you disagree with the participant contract, enter your reasons.
* **Participant Cancellation** (`participant_cancellation`, `text`, required: false): If you need to cancel your participation, enter your reason.
* **Participant Questions** (`participant_questions`, `text`, required: false): Ask any questions related to your participation.
* **Event Requirements** (`event_requirements`, `text`, required: false): Review and confirm event requirements.
* **Event Coordinators** (`event_coordinators`, `text`, required: false): Enter the event coordinators' details.
* **Event Participants** (`event_participants`, `text`, required: false): Enter the number of event participants.
* **Event Terms** (`event_terms`, `text`, required: false): Review and agree to the event terms.
* **Event Disclaimer** (`event_disclaimer`, `text`, required: false): Review and agree to the event disclaimer.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, `text`, required: false): Enter the event organizer's details.
* **Business Owner Relationship** (`business_owner_relationship`, `text`, required: false): Describe your relationship with the business owner.
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, `text`, required: false): Enter your current participant status.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any comments related to your participation.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional notes.
