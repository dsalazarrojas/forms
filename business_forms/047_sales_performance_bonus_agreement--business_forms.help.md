# Sales Performance Bonus Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and record the bonus structure and payment details for sales representatives. It captures key information about sales targets, bonus criteria, and payment methods to ensure that sales representatives understand their performance and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the sales target and bonus criteria to ensure you understand the expectations and requirements.
2. Select the correct answers for each field based on your sales performance and bonus payment details.
3. Enter your sales representative name, email, and phone number for contact information.
4. Confirm the bonus payment details, including payment method and amount.
5. Review and sign the form to confirm your understanding and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Sales Performance Bonus Agreement** (Text, Required): Enter a brief description of your sales performance and bonus structure.
- **Sales Target** (Select One, Required): Indicate whether you have met or exceeded your sales targets.
    - Select 'Yes' if you have met or exceeded your sales targets.
    - Select 'No' if you have not met your sales targets.
- **Sales Target Value** (Number, Optional): Enter the target value you achieved if you select 'Yes' for Sales Target.
- **Bonus Criteria** (Text, Optional): Describe the bonus criteria that applies to your sales performance.
- **Sales Target Achieved** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate whether you have achieved your sales targets.
    - Select 'Yes' if you have achieved your sales targets.
    - Select 'No' if you have not achieved your sales targets.
- **Sales Target Value Achieved** (Number, Optional): Enter the target value you achieved if you select 'Yes' for Sales Target Achieved.
- **Sales Target Achieved Percentage** (Number, Optional): Enter the percentage of target value achieved if you select 'Yes' for Sales Target Achieved.
- **Bonus Issued** (Select One, Optional): Indicate whether bonus has been issued.
    - Select 'Yes' if bonus has been issued.
    - Select 'No' if bonus has not been issued.
- **Bonus Percentage** (Number, Optional): Enter the bonus percentage if bonus has been issued.
- **Sales Rep Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your sales representative name.
- **Manager Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your manager's name.
- **Date of Bonus** (Date, Optional): Enter the date bonus was issued if bonus has been issued.
- **Bonus Issued Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date bonus was issued if bonus has been issued.
- **Payment Method** (Select One, Optional): Indicate the payment method used.
    - Select 'Yes' if payment method is used.
    - Select 'No' if payment method is not used.
- **Payment Amount** (Number, Optional): Enter the payment amount if payment method is used.
- **Total Bonus Earned** (Number, Optional): Enter the total bonus earned if bonus has been issued.
- **Total Bonus Payment** (Number, Optional): Enter the total bonus payment if payment method is used.
- **Sales Rep Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your sales representative email.
- **Manager Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your manager's email.
- **Sales Rep Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your sales representative phone number.
- **Manager Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your manager's phone number.
- **Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your sales performance and bonus payment.
- **Manager 2 Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your secondary manager's name (if applicable).
- **Sales Rep 2 Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the second sales representative (if applicable).
- **Sales Rep 3 Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the third sales representative (if applicable).
