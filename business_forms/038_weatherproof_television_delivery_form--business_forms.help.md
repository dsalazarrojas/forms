# Weatherproof Television Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form collects information for the delivery of weatherproof televisions, which are specialized TVs designed to function in extreme weather conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all necessary information before completing this form. Please have the delivery address, delivery date, delivery time, delivery contact name, delivery contact number, delivery instructions, and television model and serial number(s) ready.
2. Fill in the required fields marked as required in the form.
3. Be thorough and accurate when providing contact information and delivery details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address where the weatherproof television is to be delivered.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required: false): Provide the date of the delivery. If no specific date is selected, the delivery will be arranged as soon as possible.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required: false): Choose a specific time for the delivery. If no time is selected, the delivery will be arranged as soon as possible.
* **Delivery Contact Name** (`delivery_contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person to contact for delivery.
* **Delivery Contact Number** (`delivery_contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact number of the person to contact for delivery.
* **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, `text`, required: false): Provide any special delivery instructions or requests.
* **Delivery Instructions Hints** (`delivery_instructions_hints`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional information or hints for delivery.
* **Television Model** (`television_model`, `text`, required: false): Enter the model number of the weatherproof television.
* **Television Serial Number** (`television_serial_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the serial number of the weatherproof television.
* **Television Serial Number 2** (`television_serial_number_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional serial number for the weatherproof television.

## Tips

* Double-check the accuracy of all the fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure you've included all necessary information for a smooth delivery.
