**Purpose**
This form is used for registering a new money services business. The form requires providing information about the business, including its name, address, service type, and other relevant details. It also collects contact information, goals, and business registration status among other things.

**How To Complete This Form**

1.  Fill in all the fields marked as "required" to ensure your submission is complete.
2.  Enter a brief description of your money services business in the "title" field.
3.  Enter the name of your business in the "business_name" field.
4.  Provide the address of your business in the "business_address" field.
5.  Select the type of service your business offers using the "service_type" field.
6.  Enter the name of the person your customers can contact for business inquiries in the "contact_person" field.
7.  Describe your business goals in the "goals" field.
8.  Enter your business phone number in the "business_phone" field.
9.  Enter your business email address in the "business_email" field.
10. Enter your business website address in the "business_website" field.
11. Select the status of your business license using the "business_license_status" field.
12. Indicate whether your business has been registered using the "business_registration_type" field.
13. Enter the date of your business registration using the "business_registration_date" field.
14. Enter your business license number in the "business_license_number" field.
15. Enter your business tax ID in the "business_tax_id" field.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

*   **Title** (`title`, <string>, **required**): Enter a brief description of your money services business.
*   **Business Name** (`business_name`, <string>, **optional**): Enter the name of your money services business.
*   **Business Address** (`business_address`, <string>, **optional**): Enter the address of your business.
*   **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, **optional**): Select the type of service your business offers.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, <string>, **optional**): Enter the name of the person your customers can contact for business inquiries.
*   **Goals** (`goals`, <string>, **optional**): Describe your business goals.
*   **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, <string>, **optional**): Enter your business phone number.
*   **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, **optional**): Enter your business email address.
*   **Business Website** (`business_website`, <string>, **optional**): Enter your business website address.
*   **Business License Status** (`business_license_status`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select the status of your business license.
*   **Business Registration Type** (`business_registration_type`, select_one, **optional**): Indicate whether your business has been registered.
*   **Business Registration Date** (`business_registration_date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date of your business registration.
*   **Business License Number** (`business_license_number`, <string>, **optional**): Enter your business license number.
*   **Business Tax ID** (`business_tax_id`, <string>, **optional**): Enter your business tax ID.

**Tips**

*   Please ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
*   Be honest and accurate when filling out the form to avoid errors or issues with your business registration.
