# Job Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for staff members to report on job details after completion.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the job title to give a brief name to the job.
2. Enter the staff name associated with the job.
3. Provide the date when the job was completed.
4. Enter the start and end times of the job, including AM/PM if necessary.
5. Give a detailed description of the work performed during the job.
6. List the materials used during the job.
7. Select any files or documents related to the job.
8. Optionally, enter any specific tasks performed during the job and provide a brief description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter a brief name for the job.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the staff member who completed the job.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the job was completed, in the standard date format (e.g., 2024-02-15).
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the job, including AM/PM if necessary.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the job, including AM/PM if necessary.
* **Work Description** (`work_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the work performed during the job.
* **Materials Used** (`materials_used`, text, optional): List the materials used during the job.
* **Files** (`files`, select_multiple, optional): Select any files or documents related to the job.
* **Task 1** (`task1`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the first task performed during the job.
* **Task 2** (`task2`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the second task performed during the job.
* **Task 3** (`task3`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the third task performed during the job.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately.
- Double-check that all fields are filled before submitting the form.
- If additional files or attachments are required for the job, ensure they are attached or uploaded accordingly.
