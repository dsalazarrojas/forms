# Soundproofing Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for soundproofing materials. Please fill out the form with accurate information to ensure efficient processing of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Customer Name** in the field provided.
2. Choose the type of **Material** you need from the list.
3. Enter the quantity of material you require.
4. Enter the **Unit Price** of the material.
5. Select your preferred **Payment Method**.
6. Enter your **Email** address.
7. Enter your **Phone Number**.
8. Enter your **Shipping Address**.
9. Enter your **Billing Address**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name and surname.
* **Material Type** (`material_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of soundproofing material you need (e.g., Acoustic Insulation, Acoustic Sealant, etc.).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the number of soundproofing materials you require.
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, optional): Enter the price per unit of the material.
* **Total Price** (`total_price`, number, optional): This field will be populated automatically based on the unit price and quantity.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred payment method (e.g., Credit, Debit, PayPal).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter the address where the soundproofing material should be shipped.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, optional): Enter your billing address (usually the same as your shipping address, but can be different if needed).

## Tips
* Please ensure you enter accurate information to avoid any order issues.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact our support team.
