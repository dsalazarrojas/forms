# Estate Plan Client Questionnaire Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about the client's personal and financial situation, which will be used to create a tailored estate plan that meets their unique needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your basic contact information.
2. Answer the questions about yourself and your spouse (if applicable).
3. Provide information about any dependents (e.g., children, elderly parents).
4. Share your contact information and any emergency contact details.
5. List any assets you own or are responsible for.
6. Review and submit your completed form to initiate the estate planning process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, optional): Please enter your basic contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Spouse Information** (`spouse_info`, text, optional): If you are married or in a domestic partnership, please provide information about your spouse.
* **Children Information** (`children_info`, text, optional): List any dependents, such as children, grandchildren, or other family members you are responsible for.
* **Dependents Information** (`dependents_info`, text, optional): If you have dependents other than children, please provide their names and ages.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Please provide your current address for emergency contact purposes.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Designate an emergency contact person, including their name and relationship to you.
* **Assets** (`assets`, text, optional): List any assets you own or are responsible for, including real estate, investments, or other valuable items.
