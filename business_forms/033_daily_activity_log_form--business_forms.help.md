# daily_activity_log_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record and track daily activities of employees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for the form, if desired.
2. Provide a short description of your daily activities.
3. Enter the date of the activity.
4. Enter the time of the activity.
5. Describe the main activity or task you were doing during the day.
6. Add any additional comments about your activity.
7. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
8. Enter your email address (if applicable).
9. Check if the activity is related to work or business.
10. Select all non-business activities that apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (form_title, text, optional): Enter a brief title for the form to help identify the record.
* **Description** (form_description, text, optional): Provide a short description of your daily activities.
* **Date** (date, date, optional): Enter the date of the activity.
* **Time** (time, time, optional): Enter the time of the activity.
* **Activity** (activity, text, optional): Describe the main activity or task you were doing during the day.
* **Comments** (comments, note, optional): Add any additional comments about your activity.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Business Activity** (business_activity, select_one, optional): Check if the activity is related to work or business.
* **Non-Business Activities** (non_business_activity, select_multiple, optional): Select all non-business activities that apply to you.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are optional, so you can fill out only what is relevant to your activities.
* Use the "Description" field to provide context about your activities.
* Use the "Comments" field to add any additional details about your activities.
* Select all applicable non-business activities that apply to you in the "Non-Business Activities" field.
* You can always add or remove fields as needed, depending on your activities.
