# Banking Service Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Banking Service Recommendation Form is designed to help our team understand your needs and preferences regarding banking services. This form will help us tailor our services to meet your specific requirements and provide you with the best possible support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Customer Full Name**, which is the name of the person filling out the form.
2. Enter your **Email Address** and **Phone Number** in the respective fields. These will be used as contact information.
3. Provide your **Mailing Address** in the format of street, city, state, and ZIP code.
4. Select your **Customer Type**, which indicates whether you are an existing personal customer, existing business customer, new customer, or a prospect.
5. If applicable, enter your **Account Number**.
6. Select the **Current Services Used**, which are the services you are currently using from us.
7. Choose your **Financial Goals**, which are the objectives you have for your banking needs.
8. Select your **Life Stage**, which indicates your current life phase.
9. Choose your **Annual Income** from the provided options.
10. Select your **Risk Tolerance**, which indicates how much risk you are willing to take on.
11. Select the **Services Interested In**, which are the services that interest you.
12. Choose your **Preferred Contact Method**, which is how you would like us to reach you.
13. Select the **Best Time to Contact**, which is when you are most available for contact.
14. Choose your **Meeting Preference**, which is how you would like to meet with our team.
15. If applicable, select your **Preferred Branch** location.
16. Select your **Decision Timeline**, which is when you plan to make a decision.
17. Answer **How Did You Hear About Us**, which is where you heard about us.
18. If applicable, enter any **Additional Financial Needs** you have.
19. Enter any **Questions** you may have.
20. Finally, choose whether you **Consent to Contact**, which will allow us to contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your street address, city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Customer Type**: Choose whether you are an existing personal customer, existing business customer, new customer, or prospect.
* **Account Number**: If you have an account with us, please enter your account number.
* **Current Services Used**: Select all the services you are currently using from us.
* **Financial Goals**: Choose the objectives you have for your banking needs, such as saving for retirement or funding education.
* **Life Stage**: Choose your current life phase, which will help us understand your needs better.
* **Annual Income**: Choose your annual income from the provided options.
* **Risk Tolerance**: Choose your level of risk tolerance, which will help us recommend services that suit your risk level.
* **Services Interested In**: Select all the services that interest you, such as investment accounts or retirement planning.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose how you would like us to reach you, such as by email or phone call.
* **Best Time to Contact**: Choose when you are most available for contact, such as morning or afternoon.
* **Meeting Preference**: Choose how you would like to meet with our team, such as in-person or by video call.
* **Preferred Branch**: If applicable, select your preferred branch location.
* **Decision Timeline**: Choose when you plan to make a decision, such as immediately or within a month.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Choose where you heard about us, such as online search or social media.
* **Additional Financial Needs**: If you have any specific financial requirements, please enter them here.
* **Questions**: If you have any questions, please enter them here.
* **Consent to Contact**: Choose whether you consent to be contacted by us.
* **Submission Date**: This field will be automatically populated with the current date and time.

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the Banking Service Recommendation Form effectively and efficiently. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us!
