# Contact Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information from individuals or companies. The form is divided into three sections: Personal Information, Company Details, and Contact Preferences. It is used for internal purposes, such as communication and correspondence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information in the Personal Information section.
   - Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
   - Enter your Email Address for primary correspondence.
   - If applicable, enter your Phone Number for additional contact options.
2. Fill in the Company Details section if you are representing a company.
   - Enter the full name of your company.
   - Enter your Job Title or position within the company.
3. Provide your mailing address in the Mailing Address section.
   - Enter your Street Address, including any apartment or suite numbers.
   - Enter your City and State/Province.
   - Enter your ZIP/Postal Code.
   - Select your Country.
4. Choose your preferred contact method in the Contact Preferences section.
   - Select one of the options: Email, Phone, SMS/Text, or Mail.
5. Add any additional comments or information in the Additional Comments field.
6. Finally, select the current date in the Submission Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, Required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, Required): Enter your last name.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, Required): Enter your primary email address for correspondence.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Optional): Enter your mobile or landline phone number for additional contact options.
- **Company/Organization Name** (`company_name`, Optional): Enter the full name of your company or organization.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, Optional): Enter your current position or job title within the company.
- **Street Address** (`street_address`, Optional): Enter your street address, including any apartment or suite numbers.
- **City** (`city`, Optional): Enter the city of your mailing address.
- **State/Province** (`state_province`, Optional): Enter the state or province of your mailing address.
- **ZIP/Postal Code** (`postal_code`, Optional): Enter your postal code.
- **Country** (`country`, Optional): Select your country of residence.
- **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, Required): Choose one of the options: Email, Phone, SMS/Text, or Mail.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Optional): Add any additional comments or information.
- **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, Required): Select the current date.

Note: This form is used for internal purposes, such as communication and correspondence. The information collected will not be shared with any third-party entities.
