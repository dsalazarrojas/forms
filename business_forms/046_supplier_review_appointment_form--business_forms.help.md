# Supplier Review Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track the progress of reviews with suppliers, including contact information, review status, and notes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is the supplier's name?** (`supplier_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the supplier being reviewed.
- **What is the supplier's email address?** (`supplier_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the supplier.
- **What is the supplier's address?** (`supplier_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the supplier's physical address.
- **What is the supplier's job title?** (`supplier_job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the job title of the supplier.
- **What is the supplier's company name?** (`supplier_company`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the supplier's company.
- **What is the review status?** (`review_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ In Progress
	+ Completed
	+ Cancelled
- **What are the review notes?** (`review_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any relevant notes or comments about the review.
- **What is the reviewer's name?** (`reviewer_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person conducting the review.
- **What is the reviewer's phone number?** (`reviewer_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
- **What is the reviewer's job title?** (`reviewer_job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the job title of the reviewer.
- **What is the reviewer's address?** (`reviewer_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the reviewer.
- **What are the review details?** (`review_details`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional details or comments about the review.
