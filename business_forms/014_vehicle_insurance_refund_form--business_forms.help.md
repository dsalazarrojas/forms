# Vehicle Insurance Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Insurance Refund Form is designed to collect information for vehicle insurance refund purposes. It is intended to be completed by the claimant, the person submitting the claim.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out each section of the form according to the instructions provided.
- Start with the Vehicle Insurance Refund Form on page 1, which contains general information about the form.
- On page 2, select the type of vehicle you want to claim for insurance refund, if applicable.
- On page 3, select the types of supporting documents required for your claim, if applicable.
- On page 4, enter a numeric value representing the refund amount.
- On page 5, select the options that apply to your claim, if applicable.
- On page 6, select the types of supporting documents to attach, if applicable.
- On page 7, enter your address for contact and correspondence.
- On page 8, indicate whether you are available for a call, if applicable.
- On page 9, select whether you have a claim history, if applicable.
- On the final page 10, enter any additional comments, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Insurance Refund Form** (`vehicle_insurance_refund_form_first_page`):
    - This is the first page of the form, which contains general information about the form.
    * **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_insurance_refund_form_second_page`): 
    - Select the type of vehicle you are claiming for insurance refund. This will help us identify your vehicle type accurately.
*   **Claimant Information** (`vehicle_insurance_refund_form_third_page`):
    - Select the types of documents you are attaching as proof for your claim. This will help us assess your claim efficiently.
* **Refund Information** (`vehicle_insurance_refund_form_fourth_page`):
    - Enter a numeric value representing the refund amount you are claiming.
* **Additional Information** (`vehicle_insurance_refund_form_fifth_page`):
    - Select any additional options that apply to your claim, if applicable.
* **Supporting Document** (`vehicle_insurance_refund_form_sixth_page`):
    - Select the types of supporting documents you are attaching as proof for your claim.
* **Address** (`vehicle_insurance_refund_form_seventh_page`):
    - Enter your address for contact and correspondence purposes.
* **Availability for Call** (`vehicle_insurance_refund_form_eighth_page`):
    - Indicate whether you are available for a call to discuss your claim, if applicable.
* **Claim History** (`vehicle_insurance_refund_form_ninth_page`):
    - Select whether you have a claim history, if applicable.
* **Vehicle Insurance Refund Form Tenth Page** (`vehicle_insurance_refund_form_tenth_page`):
    - Enter any additional comments or information that you would like to include with your claim.

## Tips
- Be accurate and honest in filling out the form.
- Ensure that all relevant information is provided.
- Attach all required supporting documents.
- Do not hesitate to ask if you need help or clarification.
