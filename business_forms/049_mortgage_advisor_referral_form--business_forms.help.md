# Mortgage Advisor Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mortgage Advisor Referral Form is designed to gather information from mortgage advisors about their referrals, helping the organization to track and manage lead referrals effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
2. Select the options provided in the "Lead Status", "Lead Source", and "Follow Up Method" fields.
3. Choose a date for the "Referral Date" field.
4. Leave any fields that are not required for your specific situation blank, as they do not affect the form's submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information
* **Client Information** (`Client Information`, text, false): Enter the client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details about the client. This field is not required.

### Referral Details
* **Referral Details** (Referral Details, text, false): Provide a brief description of the referral, including the client's goals or needs. This field is not required.

### Additional Info
* **Additional Info** (Additional Info, text, false): Enter any additional information about the client or the referral. This field is not required.

### Lead Status
* **Lead Status** (Lead Status, select_one, required): Select the current status of the lead. Options include:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive

### Lead Source
* **Lead Source** (Lead Source, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable sources where the lead came from:
	+ Web
	+ Social
	+ Word of mouth

### Follow Up Method
* **Follow Up Method** (Follow Up Method, select_one, required): Choose the method to use for follow up:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Text

### Follow Up Schedule
* **Follow Up Schedule** (Follow Up Schedule, date, false): Enter the date for the follow up. This field is not required and only visible if you select a follow-up method (phone, email, or text).

### Follow Up Time
* **Follow Up Time** (Follow Up Time, time, false): Enter the time for the follow up. This field is not required and only visible if you select a follow-up method (phone, email, or text).

### Additional Referrals
* **Additional Referrals** (Additional Referrals, text, false): Enter any additional referrals for the same client. This field is not required.

### Referral Date
* **Referral Date** (Referral Date, date, false): Enter the date of the referral. This field is not required.
