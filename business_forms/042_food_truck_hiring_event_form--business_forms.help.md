# Food Truck Hiring Event Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Food Truck Hiring Event Form is a tool used to collect information from applicants for a specific event. It is crucial to fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter the event name and date in the respective fields.
3. Provide a valid contact number and email address for communication.
4. Describe your experience related to food trucks or events.
5. Specify the event location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name (1)**: Enter the name of the event for which you are applying.
* **Event Date (2)**: Enter the date of the event in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Event Time (3)**: Enter the time of the event in the format 12:00 AM/PM.
* **Applicant Name (4)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Number (5)**: Enter your valid phone number for communication.
* **Email (6)**: Enter your valid email address for communication.
* **Experience (7)**: Describe your experience related to food trucks or events.
* **Event Location (8)**: Enter the location of the event.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information to avoid any confusion.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Please fill out all required fields to ensure efficient processing of your application.
* This form will be reviewed and considered for event hiring.
