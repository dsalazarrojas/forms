# C Level Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you request authorization for executive-level decisions or actions. It ensures that all necessary information is gathered and presented in a clear and concise manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Authorization Date" field with the date of your request.
2. Enter the "Requesting Party Name" and "Requesting Party Title" fields with the relevant information about the party making the request.
3. Provide the "Requesting Party Email" and "Requesting Party Phone" fields with the contact information of the requesting party.
4. Select the executive level required for approval from the "C-Level Authorization Required From" dropdown list.
5. Enter the "Executive Name" field with the name of the executive who needs to sign off on the request.
6. Provide the executive's email address in the "Executive Email" field.
7. Describe the "Subject of Authorization" field with a brief summary of what needs approval.
8. Select the type of authorization needed from the "Type of Authorization Needed" dropdown list.
9. Enter the "Detailed Description" field with a full description of what needs approval.
10. If applicable, fill in the "Financial Impact" field with the dollar amount or budget impact of the decision.
11. Select the timeline for the decision from the "Timeline for Decision" dropdown list.
12. Choose whether supporting documents are attached from the "Supporting Documents Attached" dropdown list.
13. Enter the "Business Justification" field with a brief explanation of why this authorization is needed.
14. If any risks or concerns are identified, enter them in the "Identified Risks or Concerns" field.
