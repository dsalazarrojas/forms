# Boiler Room Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the results of a boiler room inspection, providing a comprehensive overview of the inspected equipment and its conditions. The form is designed to help identify potential issues and ensure the safe operation of the boiler room.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the required fields with the date and time of the inspection.
2.  Enter the details of the inspector, including name and certification number.
3.  Provide the name and location of the facility being inspected.
4.  Record the details of the boiler, including manufacturer, model, and serial number.
5.  Enter the date of installation and last inspection (if applicable).
6.  Report the overall condition of the boiler, including cleanliness, rust or corrosion, and leaks or drips.
7.  Evaluate the condition of key components, such as pressure and temperature gauges, safety valves, and fuel supply lines.
8.  Check the condition of the ventilation system and flue pipes.
9.  Verify the presence and functionality of safety equipment, such as fire extinguishers and first aid supplies.
10. Confirm the availability of maintenance records.
11. Review and confirm the condition of all controls and their functionality.
