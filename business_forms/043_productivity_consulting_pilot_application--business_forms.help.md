# Productivity Consulting Pilot Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the Productivity Consulting Pilot Application. It aims to gather essential details from organizations interested in participating in a productivity consulting pilot project. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure that your organization's needs are properly assessed and addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your organization's name and any additional information relevant to your organization.
2.  Provide the name and contact details of the person responsible for this application.
3.  Enter your organization's email address for communication purposes.
4.  Enter your primary phone number for easy contact.
5.  List the key challenges your organization is facing.
6.  Describe your goals and objectives for the productivity consulting project.
7.  Indicate your consent to share data for the project.
8.  Select the type of organization you represent.
9.  Provide more details about your goals and objectives if needed.
10. Enter your contact address for easy communication.
11.  If applicable, select the current project status and enter the project start date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): Enter your organization's name, which will be used to identify your organization in the project.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact details of the person responsible for this application.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your organization's email address for communication purposes.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your primary phone number for easy contact.
*   **Key Challenges** (`key_challenges`, text, optional): List specific challenges your organization is facing.
*   **Goals** (`goals`, text, optional): Describe your goals and objectives for the productivity consulting project.
*   **Consent to Share Data** (`consent_to_share_data`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you consent to share data for the project.
*   **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of organization you represent.
	+   Choose one or more of the following options:
		- Large Corporation
		- Small-Medium Corporation
		- Non-Profit
		- Government Agency
		- Other
*   **Goals Details** (`goals_details`, text, optional): Provide more details about your goals and objectives if needed.
*   **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter your contact address for easy communication.
*   **Project Status** (`project_status`, date, optional): If applicable, select the current project status.
*   **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the project start date.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, do not hesitate to contact us.
