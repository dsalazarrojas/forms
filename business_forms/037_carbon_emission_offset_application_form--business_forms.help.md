# Carbon Emission Offset Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help organizations and individuals calculate and report their carbon emissions, and apply for carbon offsetting to reduce their environmental impact. It gathers essential information about the applicant's details, emissions, and offsetting requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide accurate and detailed information about yourself or your organization and your carbon emissions. Follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal or company details such as your name, email address, phone number, and organization name (if applicable).
2.  Provide your estimated annual carbon emissions in metric tons CO2e.
3.  Select your primary emission sources, such as energy use, transportation, manufacturing, or other sources.
4.  Choose your preferred offset project type and geographic location for the project.
5.  Specify your offset timeline and the amount of carbon emissions you plan to offset.
6.  Choose your preferred offset percentage and certification standards.
7.  Provide information on your verification and auditing strategy.
8.  Add any additional comments or details that might be relevant to your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Your name (`applicant_name`): 
*   Your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   Required: Yes
*   Type: `text`

### 2. Email address (`applicant_email`): 
*   The email address you'd like to use for communication about your application.
*   Required: Yes
*   Type: `email`

### 3. Phone number (`applicant_phone`): 
*   The phone number you'd like to use for communication about your application.
*   Required: Yes
*   Type: `text`

### 4. Organization name (if applicable) (`organization_name`): 
*   The name of your organization, if you are a business or entity.
*   Optional: Yes
*   Type: `text`

### 5. Mailing address (`mailing_address`): 
*   The address where you can be contacted or received mail.
*   Required: Yes
*   Type: `text`

### 6. City, state, zip code (`city_state_zip`): 
*   Your location details.
*   Required: Yes
*   Type: `text`

### 7. Estimated annual carbon emissions (metric tons CO2e) (`annual_emissions`): 
*   A numerical value for your estimated annual emissions.
*   Required: Yes
*   Type: `number`

### 8. Primary emission sources (select all that apply) (`emission_sources`): 
*   Select all the sources of emissions you're concerned with.
*   Required: Yes
*   Options: Energy Use, Transportation, Manufacturing, Waste, Agriculture, Supply Chain, Travel, Commuting, Other

### 9. Emission reporting period (`emission_reporting_period`): 
*   When you are reporting your emissions, e.g., calendar year or fiscal year.
*   Required: Yes
*   Options: Calendar Year, Fiscal Year, Other Period, Not Specified

### 10. Amount of carbon emissions to offset (metric tons CO2e) (`offset_amount`): 
*   The amount of emissions you'd like to offset.
*   Required: Yes
*   Type: `number`

### 11. Percentage of emissions to offset (`offset_percentage`): 
*   What percentage of your emissions you'd like to offset.
*   Optional: Yes
*   Type: `number`

### 12. Timeline for achieving offset (`offset_timeline`): 
*   When you plan to achieve your offset goals.
*   Required: Yes
*   Options: Immediate, Within 1 Year, Within 2 Years, Within 5 Years, Custom Timeline

### 13. Preferred offset project type (`offset_project_preference`): 
*   What type of project you'd like to use for offsetting.
*   Optional: Yes
*   Options: Renewable Energy, Reforestation, Methane Capture, Energy Efficiency, Carbon Sequestration, Community Projects

### 14. Geographic preference for offset projects (`geographic_preference`): 
*   Where you'd like to have your offset project located.
*   Optional: Yes
*   Options: Local, Regional, National, International, No Preference

### 15. Certification and verification requirements (`certification_requirements`): 
*   Details about certifications and verification required.
*   Optional: Yes
*   Type: `note`

### 16. Preferred certification standards (`certification_standards`): 
*   Your preferred certification standards for the offset project.
*   Optional: Yes
*   Options: Gold Standard, VCS, CDM, CAR, ACR, ISO 14064, Other

### 17. Verification type preferred (`verification_type`): 
*   Your preferred verification type.
*   Optional: Yes
*   Options: Third-Party Verified, Self-Certified, Audit Ready, Other

### 18. Budget allocated for carbon offset (`budget_allocated`): 
*   The budget you've allocated for carbon offsetting.
*   Optional: Yes
*   Type: `number`

### 19. Implementation strategy (`implementation_strategy`): 
*   How you plan to achieve and track your emissions offset.
*   Optional: Yes
*   Type: `text`

### 20. Monitoring and reporting plan (`monitoring_plan`): 
*   Details about your plan for monitoring and reporting emissions.
*   Optional: Yes
*   Options: True, In Development, False, Will Develop Later

### 21. Additional information or comments (`additional_information`): 
*   Any additional comments or information about your application.
*   Optional: Yes
*   Type: `text`
