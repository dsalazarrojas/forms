# Daily Sales Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Sales Report form is used to collect daily sales data for reporting and analysis purposes. It is essential to fill this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the data is reliable and useful for analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the date for which you are reporting sales.
2. Enter the total sales amount for the selected date.
3. Choose the sales region from the options provided.
4. Add any additional notes or comments about the sales.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sales Date** (`sales_date`, `date`, required/optional: No): 
    This field is used to select the date for which you are reporting sales. Please select the date from the calendar provided.
* **Sales Amount** (`sales_amount`, `number`, required/optional: No): 
    Enter the total sales amount for the selected date. Please ensure that the amount is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Sales Region** (`sales_region`, `select_one`, required/optional: No): 
    Choose the sales region from the options provided. Select the region that corresponds to the area where the sales were made.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional: No): 
    Add any additional notes or comments about the sales. This field is optional but can be helpful for providing more context about the sales.
