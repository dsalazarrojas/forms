# Wig Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wig Contract Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of a wig purchase agreement between a customer and the seller. This form is used to capture essential information from the customer and ensure a clear understanding of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Customer Name**, **Customer Email**, and **Customer Phone** fields with the relevant contact information.
2. Select the **Contract Type** from the available options (Option 1 or Option 2).
3. Review the **Terms of Service** field, which is a non-editable text field.
4. Check the **Agreement Signed** field and select **True** if you agree to the terms and conditions.
5. Fill out the **Contract Date** field with the date of the contract.
6. Enter the **Customer Address** field if you'd like to provide additional contact information.
7. If the contract has been signed, enter the **Contract Signed Date** and the **Contract Signed By** field with the relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (text)**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email (email)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone (text)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Contract Type (select_one)**: Choose the type of contract you wish to sign (Option 1 or Option 2).
* **Terms of Service (note)**: Review the terms and conditions of the contract.
* **Agreement Signed (select_one)**: Check **True** if you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Contract Date (date)**: Enter the date of the contract.
* **Customer Address (text)**: Enter the customer's address, if desired.
* **Contract Signed Date (date)**: Enter the date the contract was signed, if applicable.
* **Contract Signed By (text)**: Enter the name of the person signing the contract, if applicable.
