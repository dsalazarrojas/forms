# Business Enquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from end-users about their business enquires.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields accurately and truthfully.
2. Make sure to select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
3. Provide a clear description of your enquiry in the "Detailed Description" field.
4. If you have any additional information that is relevant to your enquiry, please provide it in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (required): A required field that asks for your complete name.
* **Company Name** (required): A required field that asks for your company or organization name.
* **Job Title** (required): A required field that asks for your position.
* **Email Address** (required): A required field that asks for your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (required): A required field that asks for your contact number.
* **Enquiry Type** (required): A required field that asks for the type of enquiry you are making. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Product Information
	+ Service Inquiry
	+ Partnership Opportunity
	+ Pricing Inquiry
	+ General Question
	+ Support Request
	+ Feedback
	+ Other
* **Area of Interest** (required): A required field that asks for the specific area or product you are inquiring about.
* **Detailed Description** (required): A required field that asks for a full description of your enquiry.
* **Budget Range** (optional): An optional field that asks for your budget range if applicable.
* **Timeline for Decision** (required): A required field that asks for when you need to make a decision.
* **Industry Sector** (required): A required field that asks for your industry sector.
* **Company Size** (required): A required field that asks for the number of employees in your company.
* **Annual Revenue** (optional): An optional field that asks for your company's annual revenue range.
* **Prefered Contact Method** (required): A required field that asks for how you prefer to be contacted. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Video Call
	+ In-person Meeting
	+ No Preference
* **Best Time to Contact** (required): A required field that asks for when you prefer to be contacted. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
	+ Anytime
* **Follow-up Frequency** (optional): An optional field that asks for how often you would like us to follow up. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ As needed
* **Additional Information** (optional): An optional field that asks for any other relevant information you may have.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (optional): An optional field that asks for where you found our information.

Note: Fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled out accurately and truthfully.
