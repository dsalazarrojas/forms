# Warranty Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warranty Registration Form is used to collect customer information and product details to ensure that customers are aware of the warranty terms and conditions and to facilitate any potential warranty claims or repairs in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the customer information section, including name, company, address, phone number, and email.
2. Enter the product model and serial number.
3. Select the start date of the warranty period.
4. Choose whether the product is still under warranty or not.
5. Select the expiration date of the warranty period.
6. Enter the zip code of the customer's location.
7. Add any additional notes or comments.
8. Sign and date the form to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (1)**: This section collects the customer's name, company, address, phone number, and email.
* **Product Information (2)**: Enter the product model and serial number.
* **Warranty Information (3)**: Provide the start date of the warranty period. This date should be the date the product was received or the expected start date of the warranty period.
* **Warranty Status (4)**: Choose whether the product is under warranty or not.
* **Contact Information (5)**: Enter the customer's phone number and email.
* **Warranty Expiration (6)**: Select the expiration date of the warranty period.
* **Warranty Expiration Time (7)**: Enter the time of day for the warranty expiration.
* **Zip Code (8)**: Enter the zip code of the customer's location.
* **Notes (9)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the product.
* **Signature (10)**: Sign and date the form to confirm your registration.
* **Warranty Type (11)**: Choose the type of warranty the product has.
* **Customer Message (12)**: Add any additional comments or messages from the customer.
* **Product Image (13)**: Enter any image or description of the product being registered.
