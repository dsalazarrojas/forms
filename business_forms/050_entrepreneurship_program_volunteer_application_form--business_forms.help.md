# Entrepreneurship Program Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect your information for an Entrepreneurship Program. Your details will be used to assess your fit for the program and schedule your shifts accordingly. The program aims to provide a unique experience for volunteers interested in entrepreneurship.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field. This is the name you wish to be known as within the program.
2.  Provide your email address in the "Email" field. This is the contact information that will be used for internal communication.
3.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This will be used for further communication and scheduling.
4.  Select your Date of Birth from the calendar in the "Date of Birth" field. This will help in determining your eligibility and fitting the program requirements.
5.  Choose your Volunteer Experience in the "Volunteer Experience" field. This will help in assessing your level of volunteer experience.
6.  Select your available days in the "Available Days" field. Please choose all the days you are available to volunteer.
7.  Choose your preferred shift(s) in the "Preferred Shift" field. Select all the shifts that suit you best.
8.  Enter your available time in the "Time" field. This is crucial for scheduling purposes.
9.  Add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field. This will help in understanding your specific needs and requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as you wish to be known within the program.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: true): This is your contact email address. Ensure it is correct for internal communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Your phone number will be used for scheduling and communication.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: true): Select your date of birth from the calendar. This will be used to determine your eligibility.
* **Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your level of volunteer experience (Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, More than 2 years).
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select all the days you are available to volunteer (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Preferred Shift** (`preferred_shift`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the shifts that suit you best (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: true): Enter your available time for volunteering.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your application.

## Tips
- Be honest in your application.
- Ensure your email and phone numbers are accurate.
- Choose your available days and time carefully for scheduling purposes.
- Add any necessary comments or notes in the "Notes" field.
