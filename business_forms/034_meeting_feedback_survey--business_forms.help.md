The Meeting Feedback Survey form is designed to gather feedback from meeting participants on various aspects of the meeting, including overall satisfaction, meeting content, organization, presentation, materials, objectives, goals, outcomes, participation, duration, frequency, effectiveness, engagement, and any additional feedback. The form is intended to help meeting organizers and facilitators understand the effectiveness of the meeting and make informed improvements for future meetings.

### Meeting Feedback Survey - Help Guide

#### Purpose

The Meeting Feedback Survey form is a tool for evaluating the effectiveness of a meeting. It helps meeting organizers and facilitators understand participant satisfaction, content quality, and overall experience.

#### How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully and answer each question honestly.
2.  For numerical fields, select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
3.  For text fields, provide a brief description or answer in the provided space.
4.  For the "Meeting Frequency" field, select "Yes" if the meeting should occur more frequently, "No" if it should occur less frequently, and "No" if it's fine as is.

#### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Overall Satisfaction (1)** (Number, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the meeting on a scale of 1 to 10.
*   **Meeting Content (2)** (Number, Required): Rate the meeting content on a scale of 1 to 10.
*   **Meeting Organization (3)** (Number, Required): Rate the meeting organization on a scale of 1 to 10.
*   **Meeting Presentation (4)** (Number, Required): Rate the meeting presentation on a scale of 1 to 10.
*   **Meeting Materials (5)** (Number, Required): Rate the meeting materials on a scale of 1 to 10.
*   **Meeting Objectives (6)** (Text, Required): Provide a brief description of the meeting's objectives.
*   **Meeting Goals (7)** (Text, Required): Provide a brief description of the meeting's goals.
*   **Meeting Outcomes (8)** (Text, Required): Describe the meeting's outcomes.
*   **Meeting Participation (9)** (Text, Required): Rate the level of participation among attendees.
*   **Meeting Duration (10)** (Time, Required): Rate the ideal duration of the meeting on a scale of 1 hour to 10 hours.
*   **Meeting Frequency (11)** (Select One, Required): Select "Yes" if you think the meeting should occur more frequently, "No" if it should occur less frequently, and "No" if it's fine as is.
*   **Overall Effectiveness (12)** (Number, Required): Rate the meeting's overall effectiveness on a scale of 1 to 10.
*   **Level of Engagement (13)** (Number, Required): Rate your level of engagement with the meeting on a scale of 1 to 10.
*   **Any Additional Feedback (14)** (Text, Required): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.
*   **Meeting Feedback (15)** (Text, Required): Describe your feedback and suggestions for the meeting.

This form is designed to be completed by meeting participants. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your meeting organizer or facilitator for clarification.

Please answer this form honestly and thoroughly, as it will help the meeting organizers and facilitators improve the quality and effectiveness of future meetings.
