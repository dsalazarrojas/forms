# dynamic_partner_leads_collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about potential leads for a business opportunity. It helps us understand the needs and preferences of individuals looking for new job opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start Collection" button to begin the collection process.
2. Fill in the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. For fields with a dropdown menu, select one or multiple options that apply to you.
4. Ensure all fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required=False): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed on any communication we may have with you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required=False): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed on any communication we may have with you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required=False): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required=False): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required=False): Enter your current or desired company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required=False): Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required=False): Select your current or desired role (CEO, Manager, Sales, or Other).
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required=False): Enter your current or desired department.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `text`, required=False): Enter your job type (Full-time, Part-time, Freelance, or Other).
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, `select_one`, required=False): Select your current or desired job level (Entry Level, Mid-Level, Senior, C-Level).
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required=False): Select your current or desired employment status (Full-time, Part-time, Freelance, Intern, or Other).
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, `text`, required=False): Enter the number of years of relevant work experience.
* **Desired Job Type** (`desired_job_type`, `text`, required=False): Enter your desired job type.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, `select_multiple`, required=False): Select any of your desired job functions (Sales, Marketing, Operations, or Other).
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required=False): Enter your preferred location.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `text`, required=False): Enter your current or desired industry.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required=False): Enter a brief description of your desired job.
* **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, `text`, required=False): Enter any specific job requirements.
* **Salary Range** (`salary_range`, `text`, required=False): Enter your desired salary range.
* **Education** (`education`, `select_multiple`, required=False): Select any of your completed or desired education levels (Bachelor's, Master's, Doctorate, Some college, or Other).
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, `select_multiple`, required=False): Select any relevant certifications you have (Google Analytics, HubSpot, or Other).
* **Languages** (`languages`, `select_multiple`, required=False): Select any languages you are fluent in (English, Spanish, Mandarin, or Other).
* **Available Start Date** (`available_start_date`, `date`, required=False): Enter your available start date.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `date`, required=False): Enter your availability date.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required=False): Enter your desired end date.
* **Tips**
* Ensure that you provide accurate and thorough information in the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* We appreciate your participation in this process.
