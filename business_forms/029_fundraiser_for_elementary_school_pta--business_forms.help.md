# Fundraiser for Elementary School PTA - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fundraiser for Elementary School PTA form is used to collect information for a specific fundraiser event. This form is meant to be filled out by users to provide details about their donation, including who the donor is, their contact information, and the financial details of the donation.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the **Name** field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the **Email** field.
3. If applicable, enter your **Organization** if you are representing an organization.
4. Enter a **Description** of your donation, including any relevant details.
5. Select the **Date** of the donation.
6. Choose how you would like to **Share** your donation on social media or by email/text message.
7. Enter the **Amount** of the donation.
8. Select the **Currency** type of the donation (e.g. USD, CAD, EUR, JPY).
9. Enter your **Phone** number if you would like to be contacted.
10. Confirm your **Email** again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fundraiser Form**: This field is not applicable for this form as it's the main form title.
* **Name**: Enter your name to identify yourself as the donor.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Organization**: If you are representing an organization, enter it here.
* **Description**: A brief description of your donation.
* **Date**: The date of your donation.
* **Share**: Choose how you would like to share your donation (Facebook, Twitter, Email, Text Message).
* **Notes**: Any additional notes about your donation.
* **Amount**: The amount of your donation.
* **Currency**: The type of currency used for your donation (e.g. USD, CAD, EUR, JPY).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted.
* **Email**: Confirm your email address.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
- Ensure that your email and phone number are up to date and valid.
- Be as specific as possible with your donation description.
- Select the correct currency for your donation.
