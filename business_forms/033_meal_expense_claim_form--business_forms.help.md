# Meal Expense Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meal Expense Claim Form is used to claim reimbursement for meal expenses. To use this form, simply follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are submitting a claim for an expense that occurred within the last two weeks.
2. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
3. Enter the date of the expense in the "Date of Expense" field.
4. Provide a brief description of the expense in the "Expense Description" field.
5. Enter the amount of the expense in the "Expense Amount" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official employee ID.
* **Date of Expense**: Enter the date on which you incurred the meal expense.
* **Expense Description**: Briefly describe the meal expense, including the type of meal and any other relevant details.
* **Expense Amount**: Enter the total amount of the meal expense, including any taxes or gratuities.
* **Tips**

* Always attach a receipt or proof of purchase for your expense.
* If you are unsure about any part of the process, contact your HR representative for assistance.
