# Personnel Emergency Response Group Training Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to record and track training events for personnel emergency response groups.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **What is the training date?** (`training_date`, date): Enter the date of the training event.
    *   Select the "training_date" field.
    *   Enter the date of the training event.
*   **What is the training time?** (`training_time`, time): Enter the time of the training event.
    *   Select the "training_time" field.
    *   Enter the time of the training event.
*   **Who is the training instructor?** (`training_instructor`, text): Enter the name of the training instructor.
    *   Select the "training_instructor" field.
    *   Enter the name of the training instructor.
*   **Where is the training location?** (`training_location`, text): Enter the location of the training event.
    *   Select the "training_location" field.
    *   Enter the location of the training event.
*   **What is the training type (single selection)?** (`training_type_single`, select_one): Select one option from the list.
    *   Select the "training_type_single" field.
    *   Select one option from the list.
*   **Is the training type 'Yes' or 'No'?** (`training_type_yes_no`, select_one): Select one option from the list.
    *   Select the "training_type_yes_no" field.
    *   Select one option from the list.
*   **What are the training types?** (`training_type_multiple`, select_multiple): Select multiple options from the list.
    *   Select the "training_type_multiple" field.
    *   Select multiple options from the list.
*   **Is the training 'Yes' or 'No'?** (`training_type_yes_no_multiple`, select_multiple): Select multiple options from the list.
    *   Select the "training_type_yes_no_multiple" field.
    *   Select multiple options from the list.
*   **Duplicate Location 1** (`duplicate_location_1`, text): This field is likely a duplicate and can be ignored.
    *   Select the "duplicate_location_1" field.
    *   This field is likely a duplicate and can be ignored.
*   **What is the training location?** (`duplicate_location_2`, text): This field is likely a duplicate and can be ignored.
    *   Select the "duplicate_location_2" field.
    *   This field is likely a duplicate and can be ignored.
*   **Training Location** (`duplicate_location_3`, text): This field is likely a duplicate and can be ignored.
    *   Select the "duplicate_location_3" field.
    *   This field is likely a duplicate and can be ignored.
*   **Duplicate Location 4** (`duplicate_location_4`, text): This field is likely a duplicate and can be ignored.
    *   Select the "duplicate_location_4" field.
    *   This field is likely a duplicate and can be ignored.
