# LED Screen Energy Audit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LED Screen Energy Audit Application Form is designed to collect data for energy audits on LED screens. This form helps us understand the energy consumption and potential cost savings of using LED screens in various settings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the correct options for each field to ensure accuracy.
* Fill out the required fields with relevant information.
* Use the "Notes" field to add any additional information or comments.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Screen Size**: Select the size of your LED screen (e.g., 24 inches, 40 inches, etc.).
* **Wattage**: Enter the wattage of your LED screen.
* **Screen Type**: Choose the type of LED screen you are using (e.g., LED, OLED, etc.).
* **Location**: Select the location where the LED screen is used (e.g., office, home, etc.).
* **Date**: Enter the date you began using the LED screen.
* **Time**: Enter the time you began using the LED screen.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or information about the LED screen.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned**: Select who is assigned to work on the energy audit (e.g., your department, team lead, etc.).
* **Completed**: Select the status of the energy audit (e.g., completed, pending, etc.).
* **Energy Consumption**: Enter the energy consumption of your LED screen in watts or kWh.
* **Power Consumption**: Enter the power consumption of your LED screen in watts.
* **CO2 Emissions**: Enter the CO2 emissions of your LED screen.
* **CO2 Reduction**: Enter the CO2 reduction of your LED screen.
* **Cost Savings**: Enter the cost savings of your LED screen.
* **Payback Period**: Enter the payback period of your LED screen.
* **Return On Investment**: Enter the return on investment of your LED screen.
* **Energy Efficiency**: Enter the energy efficiency of your LED screen.
* **Energy Savings**: Enter the energy savings of your LED screen.
* **Cost Savings Dollars**: Enter the cost savings dollars of your LED screen.
* **Energy Consumption Cost**: Enter the energy consumption cost of your LED screen.
* **Carbon Credit**: Enter the carbon credit of your LED screen.
* **Cost Per KwH**: Enter the cost per KwH of your LED screen.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about the energy audit.

Please note that all fields are optional, but filling out the required fields accurately will help us provide a more accurate energy audit.
