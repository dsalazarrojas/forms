# Business Networking Event Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals and organizations make reservations for various business networking events. It collects necessary information to ensure a smooth and efficient experience for both the attendee and the event organizers. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and email address for confirmation purposes.
2. Provide your contact phone number.
3. Enter your company name and job title.
4. Select the event you wish to attend from the provided list.
5. Choose a date for the event.
6. Indicate the number of seats you need to reserve.
7. If applicable, provide the names of additional attendees.
8. Choose a meal selection and any dietary restrictions.
9. Indicate your beverage preference.
10. Specify your seating preferences, if any.
11. Identify your networking goals, which may include seeking new clients, strategic partners, or industry peers.
12. Indicate your focus areas in business.
13. If you have interest in presenting at future events, let us know.
14. If you are interested in sponsoring events, select the corresponding option.
15. Select your payment method.
16. Finally, include any special requests or additional comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservist Name**: Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed. 
* **Email Address**: Enter your email for us to contact you for event details. 
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number in case we need to reach you. 
* **Company Name**: Enter your organization's name for networking purposes. 
* **Job Title**: Indicate your role within the company. 
* **Event Name**: Select the event you wish to attend to ensure we have enough space. 
* **Event Date**: Choose the date you wish to attend. 
* **Venue Location**: If you know the location, please let us know (optional). 
* **Number of Reservations**: Indicate how many seats you need. 
* **Guest Names**: If you're not the only one attending, please list their names. 
* **Reservation Type**: Choose the type of seating you require. 
* **Meal Selection**: Select your primary meal choice. 
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any food allergies or restrictions, please indicate. 
* **Beverage Preference**: If you have a preference for drinks, let us know. 
* **Table Preferences**: If you have any seating preferences, please describe them. 
* **Networking Goals**: Identify what connections you're seeking. 
* **Business Focus Areas**: List the industries you operate in. 
* **Presentation Interest**: If you're interested in presenting, let us know. 
* **Sponsorship Interest**: If you're interested in sponsoring events, select the option. 
* **Payment Status**: Indicate your payment status to ensure accurate billing. 
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests, please include them. 
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional information you think might be helpful.
