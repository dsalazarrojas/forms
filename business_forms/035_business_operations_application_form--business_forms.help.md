# Business Operations Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants for business operations positions within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. If a field is not applicable, please mark it as N/A (Not Applicable) or leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Applicant Name** (Applicant Name, text, required)
Enter your full legal name.

### 2. **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required)
Enter your primary email address.

### 3. **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required)
Enter your contact phone number.

### 4. **Address** (Address, text, required)
Enter your full address.

### 5. **Position Applied For** (Position Applied For, text, required)
Enter the job title you are applying for.

### 6. **Department** (Department, select_one, required)
Select the department you are applying for (Operations, Administration, Finance, Human Resources, IT, Customer Service, Sales, Marketing).

### 7. **Employment Type** (Employment Type, select_one, required)
Select the type of position you are applying for (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Temporary, Internship).

### 8. **Available Start Date** (Available Start Date, date, required)
Enter the date you can start work.

### 9. **Salary Expectations** (Salary Expectations, select_one, required)
Select your expected salary range (30K to 40K, 40K to 50K, 50K to 60K, 60K to 75K, 75K to 100K, Over 100K, Negotiable).

### 10. **Highest Education Level** (Highest Education Level, select_one, required)
Select your highest completed education level (High school, Some college, Associate degree, Bachelor degree, Master degree, Doctorate).

### 11. **Field of Study** (Field of Study, text, optional)
Enter your major or area of study.

### 12. **Years of Experience** (Years of Relevant Experience, number, required)
Enter the total years in a similar role.

### 13. **Relevant Skills** (Relevant Skills, select_multiple, required)
Select all applicable skills (Project management, Data analysis, Process improvement, Team leadership, Budget management, Customer service, Software proficiency, Communication).

### 14. **Software Proficiency** (Software Proficiency, select_multiple, required)
Select all software you are proficient in (Microsoft Office, Google Workspace, Salesforce, SAP, Oracle, QuickBooks, Slack, Asana or Trello).

### 15. **Most Recent Employer** (Most Recent Employer, text, required)
Enter the company name of your current or most recent employer.

### 16. **Previous Role** (Previous Role, text, required)
Enter your job title in your most recent role.

### 17. **Reason for Leaving** (Reason for Leaving, text, optional)
Enter why you left your previous role.

### 18. **Work Authorization** (Work Authorization, select_one, required)
Select your work authorization status (Yes - citizen, Yes - permanent resident, Yes - work visa, No - need sponsorship).

### 19. **Background Check Consent** (Background Check Consent, select_one, required)
Select your consent to a background check (Yes - I consent, No - I do not consent).

### 20. **References Available** (References Available, select_one, required)
Select the number of professional references you have available (Yes - 3 professional references, Yes - 2 professional references, Yes - 1 professional reference, No - not available).

### 21. **Cover Letter** (Cover Letter, text, optional)
Enter a brief statement about why you are a good fit for the position.

### 22. **How Did You Hear** (How Did You Hear About This Position, select_one, optional)
Select how you heard about the job posting (Company website, Job board, Social media, Referral, Recruiter, Other).

### 23. **Referral Name** (Referral Name, text, optional)
Enter the name of the person who referred you.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
