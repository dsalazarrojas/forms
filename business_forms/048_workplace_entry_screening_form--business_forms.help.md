# Workplace Entry Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Entry Screening Form is a crucial tool for employees to ensure their safety and compliance with workplace policies upon re-entry or entry into the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer accurately based on your experiences and circumstances.
2. Select the relevant options for each question, making sure to consider all possible scenarios.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
4. If you're unsure about any question, please reach out to your HR representative or manager for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Workplace location** (`workplace_location`, text, required): Enter the specific location of your workplace.
* **Employee phone** (`employee_phone`, text, required): Enter your personal phone number for contact purposes.
* **Has symptoms** (`has_symptoms`, select multiple, required): Indicate if you have any COVID-19 symptoms or concerns.
* **Has traveled abroad** (`has_traveled_abroad`, select multiple, required): If you've traveled outside the country recently, select 'Yes' to indicate that you may pose a higher risk.
* **Has contacted someone with COVID-19** (`has_contacted_someone`, select multiple, required): If you've been in close contact with someone who has COVID-19, select 'Yes' to ensure we take necessary precautions.
* **Date last symptom** (`date_last_symptom`, date, required): If you're experiencing COVID-19 symptoms, enter the date when you first noticed them.
* **Date traveled abroad** (`date_traveled_abroad`, date, required): If you've traveled outside the country, enter the date of your return to minimize the risk of transmission.
* **Employee email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your work email address for internal communication.
* **Workplace phone** (`workplace_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your workplace or office.
* **Has health conditions** (`has_health_conditions`, select multiple, required): If you have any underlying medical conditions, please select 'Yes' to ensure we provide necessary accommodations.
* **Has had a positive test result** (`previous_positive_test`, select multiple, required): If you've tested positive for COVID-19, select 'Yes' to inform our safety protocols.
* **Has been vaccinated** (`has_vaccinated`, select multiple, required): If you've received a COVID-19 vaccine, select 'Yes' to help us track vaccine compliance.
* **Vaccinated location** (`vaccinated_location`, text, required): If you've received a vaccination, enter the location where you got vaccinated.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID for identification purposes.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department name for workplace tracking.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title for role identification.
* **Has underlying medical conditions** (`has_underlying_medical_conditions`, select multiple, required): If you have any underlying medical conditions, please select 'Yes' to ensure we provide necessary accommodations.
* **Previous positive test result** (`previous_positive_test_result`, select multiple, required): If you've had a previous positive test result, select 'Yes' to inform our safety protocols.
* **Has received vaccination** (`has_received_vaccination`, select multiple, required): If you've received a COVID-19 vaccination, select 'Yes' to help us track vaccine compliance.
* **Employee status** (`employee_status`, text, required): Enter your current status (e.g., 'On leave', 'Working from home', 'Remote work').
* **Date last vaccination** (`date_last_vaccination`, date, required): If you've received a vaccination, enter the date of your last vaccination dose.
* **Workplace** (`workplace`, text, required): Enter the name of your workplace or office.
* **Manager name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your supervisor or manager.
* **Manager phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of your supervisor or manager.
