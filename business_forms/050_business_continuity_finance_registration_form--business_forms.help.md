# Business Continuity Finance Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from businesses affected by disruptions to help them access financing for recovery. Please answer the questions accurately and truthfully to ensure that you receive the assistance you need.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions and explanations for each field carefully before answering.
2. Answer all required fields (marked as "required") accurately and truthfully.
3. Skip fields that are not applicable to your situation.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, `required`: This is the legal name of your business.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Please select the type of business you are registered as: Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, Corporation, S-Corporation, Non-Profit, Other.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, `required`): Please select the industry your business operates in: Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Manufacturing, Retail, Food Service, Hospitality, Professional Services, Other.
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, `select_one`, `required`): Please select how long your business has been operating: Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, More than 10 years.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, `select_one`, `required`): Please select the size of your team: 1-5, 6-10, 11-20, 21-50, 51-100, More than 100.
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, `select_one`, `required`): Please select your business's approximate annual revenue: Under $100K, $100K-$500K, $500K-$1M, $1M-$5M, $5M-$10M, More than $10M.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `note`, `required`: This field is for your reference and should not be filled out for other reasons.
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, `required`): Please enter your full name as a representative of the business.
* **Position** (`position`, `text`, `required`): Please enter your job title or position.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, `required`): Please enter your contact number.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, `required`): Please enter your business's full address.
* **Continuity Impact** (`continuity_impact`, `note`, `required`): This field is for your reference and should not be filled out for other reasons.
* **Type of Disruption** (`disruption_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Please select the type of disruption that affected your business: Natural Disaster, Pandemic, Economic Downturn, Supply Chain Disruption, Cyber Attack, Fire or Damage, Regulatory Change, Other.
* **Disruption Date** (`disruption_date`, `date`, `required`): Please select the date the disruption occurred.
* **Revenue Impact** (`revenue_impact`, `select_one`, `required`): Please select the approximate revenue lost due to the disruption: Less than 10%, 10-25%, 26-50%, 51-75%, More than 75%.
* **Current Operational Status** (`operational_status`, `select_one`, `required`): Please select the current operational status of your business: Fully Operational, Partially Operational, Temporarily Closed, Permanently Closed, Considering Closure.
* **Finance Request** (`finance_request`, `note`, `required`): This field is for your reference and should not be filled out for other reasons.
* **Funding Type** (`funding_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Please select the type of financing your business needs: Emergency Grant, Low-Interest Loan, Line of Credit, Debt Relief, Tax Deferral, Other.
* **Funding Amount Needed** (`funding_amount`, `select_one`, `required`): Please select the approximate amount of financing your business needs: Under $10,000, $10,000-$50,000, $50,000-$100,000, $100,000-$500,000, More than $1,000,000.
* **Funding Purpose** (`funding_purpose`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Please select all the ways you plan to use the funds: Payroll, Rent or Mortgage, Utilities, Inventory, Equipment, Debt Repayment, Operating Expenses, Recovery Costs, Other.
* **Existing Debt** (`existing_debt`, `select_one`, `required`): Please select if your business has any outstanding debt: No Debt, Minimal Debt, Moderate Debt, High Debt.
* **Other Assistance Received** (`other_assistance`, `text`, `required`): Please enter any other assistance you've received.
* **Recovery Plan** (`recovery_plan`, `select_one`, `required`): Please select the status of your recovery plan: Yes - Comprehensive, Yes - Basic, In Progress, No - Need Help.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, `required`): Please enter any other information that may be helpful.

## Tips

* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
