# Camera Inventory Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camera Inventory Management Form is a tool used to track and manage camera assets within the organization. It helps ensure that cameras are accurately accounted for, their condition and status are monitored, and essential information is documented for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form to open it in your browser or application.
2. Scroll through the form sections to gather all necessary information.
3. Fill out the required fields, marked as such, and provide additional details where applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Internal Asset Tag Number** (`asset_tag_number`, text, **required**)
Internal Asset Tag Number is a unique identifier given to each camera from our tracking system. Enter this number to associate the camera with its tracking record.

### 2. **Manufacturer** (`manufacturer_brand`, select_one, **required**)
Select the camera's manufacturer from the available options.

### 3. **Camera Model Name** (`model_name`, text, **required**)
Enter the specific model of the camera, including any model-specific details.

### 4. **Serial Number** (`serial_number_cam`, text, **required**)
Enter the camera's serial number, which can usually be found on the body of the device or in the provided documentation.

### 5. **Sensor Type** (`sensor_type`, select_one, **optional**)
Choose the type of sensor used in the camera from the given options.

### 6. **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date_inventory`, date, **required**)
Enter the date the camera was purchased or acquired.

### 7. **Purchase Cost (USD)** (`asset_cost`, number, **optional**)
Enter the cost at which the camera was purchased or the current value for inventory purposes.

### 8. **Current Physical Condition** (`current_condition`, select_one, **required**)
Indicate the current physical condition of the camera, taking into account any wear or issues it may have.

### 9. **Current Operational Status** (`operational_status`, select_one, **required**)
Choose how the camera is currently being used or its status within the organization, such as whether it's available for checkout, in use, or decommissioned.

### 10. **Storage Location - Bin Number** (`storage_location_bin`, text, **required**)
Specify where the camera is stored when it's not in use, such as a designated storage bin or location.

### 11. **Included Accessories with this Body** (`accessories_included`, text, **optional**)
List any accessories that come with the camera, such as batteries, a charger, or a strap.

### 12. **Current Firmware Version** (`firmware_version`, text, **optional**)
Enter the current firmware version of the camera, if applicable.

### 13. **Last Maintenance Check Date** (`last_maintenance_check`, date, **optional**)
Record the date of the last maintenance check or servicing for the camera.

### 14. **Maintenance and Service History** (`maintenance_notes_cam`, text, **optional**)
Document any maintenance or service history for the camera, including any notes on its condition and actions taken.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information entered for each field.
- If unsure about any field, refer to the provided hints or contact the relevant department for guidance.
