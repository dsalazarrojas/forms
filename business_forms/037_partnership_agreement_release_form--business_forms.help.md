# partnership_agreement_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the terms and conditions for the release of a business partner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Business Partner" field with the name of the business partner being released.
2. Enter the name of the business partner in the "Business Partner Name" field.
3. Provide the exit date of the business partner in the "Exit Date" field.
4. Select the release type from the provided options in the "Release Type" field.
5. Enter the reason for release in the "Release Reason" field.
6. Specify the responsibilities of the released business partner in the "Responsibilities" field.
7. List the rights of the released business partner in the "Rights" field.
8. Describe the obligations of the released business partner in the "Obligations" field.
9. Enter the number of notice period in the "Notice Period" field.
10. Select the units for the notice period in the "Notice Period Units" field.
11. Enter the termination date of the business partner in the "Termination Date" field.
12. Provide the reason for termination in the "Termination Reason" field.
13. Enter the start date of the notice period in the "Notice Period Start Date" field.
14. Enter the end date of the notice period in the "Notice Period End Date" field.
