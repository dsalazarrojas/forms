# Schedule Collection Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a schedule collection request from an employee in a business setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the collection request (not required but recommended for organizational purposes).
2. Select the Pickup Date for the collection.
3. Choose the Pickup Time for the collection.
4. Enter the Pickup Location where the collection is to take place.
5. Choose the Contact Method (Phone, Email, or In-person) for the collection.
6. Enter the Contact Email and/or Phone number for the collection.
7. If necessary, enter an additional Pickup Date and Pickup Time for the collection.
8. If necessary, enter an additional Pickup Location for the collection.
9. Provide any additional Comments regarding the collection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Collection Request**: This is the title of the collection request. You can enter a description of the collection request to provide more context.
* **Pickup Date**: Enter the date you would like the collection to take place. This field is a date picker that allows you to select a specific date.
* **Pickup Time**: Choose the time you would like the collection to occur. This field is a time picker that allows you to select a specific time.
* **Pickup Location**: Enter the location where the collection should take place. This could be an office, department, or other location within the building.
* **Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted regarding the collection. Options include Phone, Email, or In-person.
* **Contact Email/Phone**: Enter your contact email and/or phone number for communication regarding the collection.
* **Pickup Date 2**: If necessary, enter an additional date for the collection. This can be used for recurring collections or additional pickups.
* **Pickup Time 2**: Choose an additional time for the collection.
* **Pickup Location 2**: If necessary, enter an additional location for the collection.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information regarding the collection.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you need to enter multiple pickup dates or times, use the secondary fields (Pickup Date 2, Pickup Time 2, Pickup Location 2).
* If you have any additional comments regarding the collection, use the Comments field.
