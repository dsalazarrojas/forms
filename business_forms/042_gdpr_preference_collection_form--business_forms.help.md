# gdpr-preference-collection-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The GDPR Preference Collection Form is a mandatory form used to collect personal data from individuals, specifically to determine their preferences and consent to the collection of their personal data for marketing and other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review our Privacy Policy to understand how we collect, use, and protect your personal data.
2. Make sure you are 18 years old or older.
3. Select your preference for data collection from the options provided in the form.
4. Fill out your personal information, if applicable.
5. Review your selections and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header**: This is the header of the form. It provides a note, but no additional information is required.
* **Do you consent to the collection of your personal data**: This field is a select one field where you can choose between "I consent" and "I do not consent" to the collection of your personal data.
* **Data Type**: This field is a select multiple field where you can choose the type of data you are comfortable with being collected. You can select one or more options from the list, including "Contact", "Location", "Marketing", "Order history", and "Payment information".
* **Contact Information**: This field is a select one field where you can choose if you want to provide your contact information.
* **Address**: This field is a select multiple field where you can choose your address information, including "Street 1", "Street 2", "Country", "City", and "Zip code".
* **E-mail**: This field is an email field where you can enter your email address.
* **Phone**: This field is a text field where you can enter your phone number.
* **Data Collection**: This field is a select multiple field where you can choose how you want your data to be collected, including "Online", "Offline", and "Third Party".
* **Data Storage**: This field is a select one field where you can choose how you want your data to be stored, including "EU" and "Non EU".
* **Data Sharing**: This field is a select multiple field where you can choose how you want your data to be shared, including "Internal", "External", and "Both".
* **Data Removal**: This field is a select one field where you can choose when you want your data to be removed, including "Anytime" and "Never".
* **Data Access**: This field is a select multiple field where you can choose how you want to access your data, including "Yes" and "No".
* **Consent**: This field is a select one field where you can choose to give your consent to the collection of your personal data.
* **Form Footer**: This is the footer of the form. It provides a note, but no additional information is required.
* **Name**: This field is a text field where you can enter your name.
* **Last Name**: This field is a text field where you can enter your last name.
* **First Name**: This field is a text field where you can enter your first name.
* **Birthdate**: This field is a date field where you can enter your birthdate.
* **Birth Month**: This field is a select one field where you can choose your birth month.
* **Birth Day**: This field is a select one field where you can choose your birth day.
* **Birth Year**: This field is a text field where you can enter your birth year.
* **E-mail**: This field is an email field where you can enter your email address again.
* **Phone**: This field is a text field where you can enter your phone number again.
* **Street 1**: This field is a text field where you can enter your street address 1.
* **Street 2**: This field is a text field where you can enter your street address 2.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Review your selections before submitting the form.
* Contact our support team if you have any questions or concerns about the form.
* If you have any issues accessing or using this form, please contact our support team.
