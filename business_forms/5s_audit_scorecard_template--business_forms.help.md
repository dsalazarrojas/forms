# 8th Grade Course Request Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 8th Grade Course Request Registration Form is a crucial tool for students, parents, and school administrators to plan and coordinate the next year's courses for 8th grade students.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please carefully fill out all required fields, including student name, parent/guardian name, and parent/guardian phone number.
2. Select all the core courses required for the next academic year.
3. Choose elective courses in order of preference (ranked) from the available options.
4. If necessary, provide any special requests, such as honors placement, accelerated math, or IEP/504 accommodations.
5. Leave the 'Counselor Review Date' field blank to indicate that it will be handled by the school's counselors.
6. Finally, type your full name as an electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student full name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your first and last name exactly as it appears on school records.
* **Student ID number** (`student_id`, text, optional): Leave blank if you don't know your school-assigned ID number. If known, enter it for faster processing.
* **Parent/guardian name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, required): Enter your name, which will serve as the main contact point for scheduling and updates.
* **Parent/guardian phone** (`parent_contact_phone`, phone, required): Include your area code for efficient communication.
* **Core courses** (`requested_core_courses`, select_multiple, required): Choose all required courses for the next academic year from the available options.
* **Elective preferences** (`elective_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select up to three elective courses of preference from the available options. Note that these are ranked according to your preference.
* **Special requests** (`credit_or_special_requests`, text, optional): Provide any special course requests or requirements, such as honors placement or IEP/504 accommodations.
* **Counselor Review Date** (`counselor_review_date`, date, optional): Leave this field blank if the school's counselors will handle the review and scheduling process.
* **Parent/guardian e-signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required): Type your full name as an electronic signature, confirming the accuracy of the submitted information.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your school records or contact the school's administrators for guidance.
* Double-check that your elective preferences are ranked according to your true preferences.
* This form will be reviewed and processed promptly, and updates will be communicated to you via phone or email.
