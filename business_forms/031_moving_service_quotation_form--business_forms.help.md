# Moving Service Quotation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for a moving service quotation. It is used to gather details about the moving service requested by the customer, including address information, move type, number of movers, distance, and other relevant details. The form also collects contact information for both the customer and the moving company. Finally, it collects the terms and conditions of the move, which the customer must agree to before the moving service is provided.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the address where the move will take place. If the move is international, please enter the address of the destination country.
2. Select the type of move (Residential, Commercial, or International).
3. Enter the number of movers required for the move.
4. Enter the distance of the move.
5. Select the type of moving company (Local or Long Distance).
6. Enter the contact information for the customer, including phone number, email, and name.
7. Enter the contact information for the moving company, including phone number, email, and name.
8. Enter any additional details about the move.
9. Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
10. Enter the quote number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Address**: Enter the address where the move will take place.
* **Move Date**: Select the date of the move.
* **Move Type**: Select the type of move (Residential, Commercial, or International).
* **Number of Movers**: Enter the number of people required for the move.
* **Move Distance**: Enter the distance of the move.
* **Moving Company**: Select the type of moving company (Local or Long Distance).
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the email address of the customer.
* **Customer Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Customer Note**: Enter any additional details about the customer.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the moving company.
* **Company Email**: Enter the email address of the moving company.
* **Company Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the moving company.
* **Company Note**: Enter any additional details about the moving company.
* **Customer License**: Enter the customer's license number or ID.
* **Quote Details**: Enter any additional details about the quote.
* **Quote Value**: Enter the value of the quote.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Signature**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Quote Number**: Enter the quote number.
* **Assigned To**: Select whether the quote is assigned to someone.
