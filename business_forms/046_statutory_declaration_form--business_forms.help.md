# Statutory Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Statutory Declaration Form is used to collect personal information, typically from individuals who are required to declare their personal details for a particular purpose. The form helps to verify and confirm the identity of the individual. It is essential to fill this form accurately and completely to ensure that the information provided is correct and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Start by filling in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
* Enter your valid Email address to ensure that you can be contacted for any further communication.
* Provide your Phone number to allow for easy contact in case of any updates or inquiries.
* Fill in your current Address to help us locate you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your given name as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., passport, driver's license, etc.).
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., passport, driver's license, etc.).
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you have access to and regularly check. This will help us contact you for any further communication.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any updates or inquiries.
* **Address** (5, text, required): Enter your current residential or business address to help us locate you.
