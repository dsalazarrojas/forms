# inventory_tracking_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and manage inventory levels for subscription-based products or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, if any.
2. For fields with options, select the desired value from the list.
3. Make sure to enter accurate values for each field to ensure correct inventory tracking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stock Level** (`stock_level`, number, optional): Enter the current stock level of the product.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, text, optional): Enter the quantity of the product that you want to track.
* **Reorder Level** (`reorder_level`, number, optional): Set the reorder level for the product, indicating when the stock level is low enough to reorder.
* **Lead Time** (`lead_time`, date, optional): Enter the lead time for the product, indicating how long it takes to receive the item.
* **Ordering Schedule** (`ordering_schedule`, select_one, optional): Select how often you need items to be reordered, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Reorder Frequency** (`reorder_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency at which you need items to be reordered, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Reorder Point** (`reorder_point`, number, optional): Set the point at which you want to be notified to reorder the product.
* **Reorder Quantity** (`reorder_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity to reorder when the stock level reaches the reorder level.
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, optional): Enter the unit price of the product.
* **Reorder Level Type** (`reorder_level_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of reorder level, such as Percentage or Dollar.
* **Reorder Level Value** (`reorder_level_value`, number, optional): Enter the value of the reorder level, depending on the type selected.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate values for each field to ensure correct inventory tracking.
* Review and update your inventory tracking settings regularly to reflect changes in your business.
* Use this form to set up and maintain inventory tracking parameters for your products.
