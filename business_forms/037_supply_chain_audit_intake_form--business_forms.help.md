# Supply Chain Audit Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supply Chain Audit Intake Form is used to collect information about your business's supply chain audit history. This form helps our team to gather data about your company's previous audits, including the date, result, and frequency of the audits. This information will be used to improve our services and provide better support for your business.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company's name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Provide your contact's first name in the "Contact First Name" field.
3. Enter your contact's last name in the "Contact Last Name" field.
4. Enter your contact's email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your contact's phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Choose the date of your last audit from the calendar.
7. Select the result of your last audit (Pass or Fail).
8. Briefly describe your business in the "Briefly describe your business" field.
9. Select your industry type (Manufacturing, Retail, Technology).
10. Select your location (USA, Canada, Mexico).
11. Choose how often you report audits (Annually, Bi-Annually, Quarterly).
12. Enter the date of your last audit in mm/dd/yyyy format.
13. Select the frequency of your audit results (Every Time, Periodically, Never).
14. Enter the location of your last audit.
15. Enter your contact's address in the "Contact Address" field.
16. Enter your contact's city in the "Contact City" field.
17. Enter your contact's state in the "Contact State" field.
18. Enter your contact's zip code in the "Contact Zip" field.
19. Enter your contact's country in the "Contact Country" field.
20. Enter the name of the vendor who performed your last audit in the "Last Audit Vendor" field.
21. Enter the vendor ID of your last audit in the "Last Audit Vendor ID" field.
22. Enter the date and time of your last audit in mm/dd/yyyy hh:mm:ss format.
23. Select the frequency of your first audit (Annually, Bi-Annually, Quarterly).
24. Select the frequency of your second audit (Annually, Bi-Annually, Quarterly).
25. Select the frequency of your third audit (Annually, Bi-Annually, Quarterly).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, required): Enter your company's name as it appears on your business cards or letterhead.
* **Contact First Name** (Contact First Name, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Contact Last Name** (Contact Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Last Audit** (Date of Last Audit, date, required): Choose the date of your last audit from the calendar.
* **Last Audit Result** (Last Audit Result, select_one, required): Select Pass or Fail as the result of your last audit.
* **Briefly describe your business** (Briefly describe your business, note, required): Provide a brief description of your business.
* **Industry** (Industry, select_multiple, required): Choose the industry type of your business (Manufacturing, Retail, Technology).
* **Location** (Location, select_one, required): Select the location of your business (USA, Canada, Mexico).
* **Audit Frequency** (Audit Frequency, select_multiple, required): Choose how often you report audits (Annually, Bi-Annually, Quarterly).
* **Date of Last Audit (mm/dd/yyyy)** (Date of Last Audit, date, required): Enter the date of your last audit in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Audit Result Frequency** (Audit Result Frequency, select_one, required): Select how often your audit results are reported (Every Time, Periodically, Never).
* **Last Audit Location** (Last Audit Location, text, required): Enter the location of your last audit.
* **Contact Address** (Contact Address, text, required): Enter your contact's address.
* **Contact City** (Contact City, text, required): Enter your contact's city.
* **Contact State** (Contact State, text, required): Enter your contact's state.
* **Contact Zip** (Contact Zip, text, required): Enter your contact's zip code.
* **Contact Country** (Contact Country, text, required): Enter your contact's country.
* **Last Audit Vendor** (Last Audit Vendor, text, required): Enter the name of the vendor who performed your last audit.
* **Last Audit Vendor ID** (Last Audit Vendor ID, text, required): Enter the vendor ID of your last audit.
* **Last Audit Date and Time** (Last Audit Date and Time, date, required): Enter the date and time of your last audit in mm/dd/yyyy hh:mm:ss format.
* **Audit Frequency 1** (Audit Frequency 1, select_multiple, required): Select the frequency of your first audit (Annually, Bi-Annually, Quarterly).
* **Audit Frequency 2** (Audit Frequency 2, select_multiple, required): Select the frequency of your second audit (Annually, Bi-Annually, Quarterly).
* **Audit Frequency 3** (Audit Frequency 3, select_multiple, required): Select the frequency of your third audit (Annually, Bi-Annually, Quarterly).

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure that we have the most up-to-date information about your business.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or guidance from our team.
* Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to help us provide better support for your business.
