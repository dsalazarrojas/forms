# painting_service_lead_capture_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture lead information for a painting service company. It is intended to be completed by potential customers who are interested in obtaining painting services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your project type from the options provided in the "Project Type" field.
2. Indicate your customer relationship status in the "Customer Relationship" field.
3. Enter the location of the project in the "Project Location" field.
4. Provide a brief description of the project in the "Project Description" field.
5. Select your preferred method of contact in the "Contact Method" field.
6. Fill out any additional information you feel is relevant to your project in the "Customer Information" field.
7. Provide any additional details you would like to share in the "Lead Capture Form" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of project you are interested in, such as residential, commercial, or industrial.
* **Customer Relationship** (`customer_relationship`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you are a new or existing customer.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the project will take place.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your project.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred method of contact, such as phone, email, text, or mail.
* **Customer Information** (`customer_information`, text, optional): Fill out any additional information you feel is relevant to your project.
* **Lead Capture Form** (`lead_capture_form`, text, optional): Provide any additional details you would like to share.
