# Magazine Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer information for magazine subscription purposes. Fill out the form to provide your details and subscription preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

* Enter your customer details in the "customer_details" field.
* Select your name in the "name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
* Choose your preferred plan from the options provided in the "plan" field.
* Indicate whether you'd like to sign up for our magazine by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "sign_up" field.
* If you have any promotional code, enter it in the "promo" field.
* Select the subscription type that suits you best from the options in the "subscription_type" field.
* You may add any additional notes or comments about your subscription in the "notes" field.
* If you have any specific notes about the customer, you can add them in the "customer_notes" field.
* Finally, choose your preferred payment method and whether you'd like to pay for the subscription by answering "Yes" or "No" in the "payment_method" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_details**: Enter your personal details, such as address, etc.
* **name**: Enter the name of the person subscribing.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address to receive notifications and updates.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **plan**: Select the subscription plan that suits your needs.
* **sign_up**: Indicate your interest in subscribing to the magazine.
* **promo**: If you have a promotional code, enter it here.
* **subscription_type**: Choose the type of subscription that works for you.
* **notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your subscription.
* **customer_notes**: Add any specific notes about the customer, if needed.
* **payment_method**: Select the payment method you prefer.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out the form accurately and clearly to avoid errors.
* Ensure that your email and phone number are up-to-date and valid.
* Choose the correct subscription plan and type to suit your needs.
* If you have any issues or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
