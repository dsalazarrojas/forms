# Contract Security Classification Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about contract security classification for the purpose of managing sensitive and confidential information within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name and title in the "Surveyor Name" and "Surveyor Title" fields respectively.
2. Select the correct department from the list provided in the "Department" field.
3. Enter the date of the survey in the "Survey Date" field.
4. Provide the contract number and title in the "Contract Number" and "Contract Title" fields respectively.
5. Select the correct contract type from the list provided in the "Contract Type" field.
6. Choose the correct classification level from the list provided in the "Security Classification" field.
7. Provide a justification for the classification chosen in the "Classification Justification" field.
8. Select all sensitive information types that apply to the contract in the "Sensitive Information Types" field.
9. Select all data handling requirements that apply to the contract in the "Data Handling Requirements" field.
10. Choose the correct access restrictions from the list provided in the "Access Restrictions" field.
11. Select the required clearance level for the contract in the "Clearance Level Required" field.
12. Choose the correct storage requirements from the list provided in the "Storage Requirements" field.
13. Select all transmission methods that apply to the contract in the "Transmission Methods" field.
14. Select all marking requirements that apply to the contract in the "Marking Requirements" field.
15. Choose the correct retention period for the contract in the "Retention Period" field.
16. Select the correct destruction requirements from the list provided in the "Destruction Requirements" field.
17. Choose whether the contract can be shared internationally from the "Foreign Disclosure" field.
18. Select whether the contract is export controlled in the "Export Control" field.
19. Provide any special handling notes or requirements in the "Special Handling Notes" field (optional).
20. If applicable, select the date to reclassify the contract in the "Review Date" field.
21. Enter the name of the approving authority who approved the classification in the "Approving Authority" field.
22. Provide any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Surveyor Name**: Enter your full name as the surveyor of the contract.
* **Surveyor Title**: Enter your job title as the surveyor of the contract.
* **Department**: Choose your department from the list provided.
* **Survey Date**: Enter the date of the survey.
* **Contract Number**: Enter the contract number.
* **Contract Title**: Enter the title of the contract.
* **Contract Type**: Choose the type of contract from the list provided.
* **Security Classification**: Choose the classification level for the contract.
* **Classification Justification**: Provide a justification for the chosen classification level.
* **Sensitive Information Types**: Select all sensitive information types that apply to the contract.
* **Data Handling Requirements**: Select all data handling requirements that apply to the contract.
* **Access Restrictions**: Choose the correct access restrictions for the contract.
* **Clearance Level Required**: Select the required clearance level for the contract.
* **Storage Requirements**: Choose the correct storage requirements for the contract.
* **Transmission Methods**: Select all transmission methods that apply to the contract.
* **Marking Requirements**: Select all marking requirements that apply to the contract.
* **Retention Period**: Choose the correct retention period for the contract.
* **Destruction Requirements**: Select the correct destruction requirements for the contract.
* **Foreign Disclosure**: Choose whether the contract can be shared internationally.
* **Export Control**: Select whether the contract is export controlled.
* **Special Handling Notes**: Provide any special handling notes or requirements (optional).
* **Review Date**: If applicable, select the date to reclassify the contract.
* **Approving Authority**: Enter the name of the approving authority who approved the classification.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information (optional).
