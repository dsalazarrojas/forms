# Group Meeting Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to create and manage logs of group meetings. This includes recording important details about the meetings, such as dates, times, attendees, and tasks assigned.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "New Group Meeting Log" button to start a new log entry.
2. Enter the meeting date and time in the "Meeting Date" field.
3. Choose the meeting status (Ongoing, Upcoming, or Finished) from the "Meeting Status" dropdown menu.
4. List all members who attended the meeting in the "Attendees" field.
5. List all members who were absent from the meeting in the "Absent Members" field.
6. Provide a brief description of the meeting topic in the "Description" field.
7. Add any tasks that need to be completed in the "Task List" field.
8. If necessary, add follow-up tasks in the "Follow-up" field.
9. Add any additional notes about the meeting in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Date**: Enter the date of the meeting in the format of YYYY-MM-DD. This field is used to track when the meeting took place.
* **Meeting Time**: Enter the time of the meeting in 24-hour format (HH:MM). This field is used to track the start and end times of the meeting.
* **Meeting Status**: Choose the status of the meeting from the dropdown menu. Options include Ongoing, Upcoming, and Finished. This field is used to indicate whether the meeting is still in progress, has not yet occurred, or has been completed.
* **Attendees**: List all members who attended the meeting. Select each member from the available options to indicate their presence. This field is used to keep track of who was present at the meeting.
* **Absent Members**: List all members who were not present at the meeting. Select each member from the available options to indicate their absence. This field is used to keep track of who was not present at the meeting.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the meeting topic. This field is used to document the main purpose or subject matter of the meeting.
* **Task List**: List all tasks that need to be completed as a result of the meeting. This field is used to track tasks assigned to team members.
* **Follow-up**: Add any follow-up tasks or actions that need to be taken as a result of the meeting. This field is used to track additional tasks that need to be completed.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the meeting. This field is used to store extra information or context about the meeting.
