# Trade Mission Private Sector Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from private sector businesses that are planning or have trade missions to our organization. The purpose of this form is to collect details about the company, its type, and contact information, in order to facilitate communication and cooperation during the trade mission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your company's name in the "What is your Company Name" field.
2. Select your company's type from the options provided in the "business_type" field (e.g. Manufacturer, Exporters, Service Providers, Consultants).
3. Choose the type of mission you are undertaking from the options provided in the "mission_type" field (e.g. Market Research, Partnership, Market Entry).
4. Enter the start date and duration of your mission in the "What is the start date of the mission?" and "What is the duration of the mission?" fields respectively.
5. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact email" field.
6. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact phone number" field.
7. Enter your company's contact address in the "Contact address" field.
8. Select your company's size from the options provided in the "What is your company size?" field (e.g. Small, Medium, Large, Unknown).
9. Select your company's type from the options provided in the "What is the company type?" field (e.g. Manufacturer, Exporters, Service Providers, Consultants).
10. Enter the number of employees in your company in the "What is the number of employees?" field.
11. Select your company's annual turnover from the options provided in the "What is your annual turnover?" field (e.g. Less than 10 million, Between 10 million - 50 million, Between 50 million - 100 million, More than 100 million).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your Company Name** (`trade_mission_private_sector_form_1`, text, required: false): Enter your company's name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **business_type** (`business_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of business you represent (e.g. Manufacturer, Exporters, Service Providers, Consultants).
* **mission_type** (`mission_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of mission you are undertaking (e.g. Market Research, Partnership, Market Entry).
* **What is the start date of the mission?** (`trade_mission_private_sector_form_5`, time, required: false): Enter the start date of your mission.
* **What is the duration of the mission?** (`trade_mission_private_sector_form_4`, date, required: false): Enter the duration of your mission.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your company's contact email address.
* **Contact phone number** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your company's contact phone number.
* **Contact address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your company's contact address.
* **What is your company size?** (`trade_mission_private_sector_form_9`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your company's size (e.g. Small, Medium, Large, Unknown).
* **What is the company type?** (`trade_mission_private_sector_form_10`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your company's type (e.g. Manufacturer, Exporters, Service Providers, Consultants).
* **What is the number of employees?** (`trade_mission_private_sector_form_11`, number, required: false): Enter the number of employees in your company.
* **What is your annual turnover?** (`trade_mission_private_sector_form_12`, select_one, required: false): Select your company's annual turnover (e.g. Less than 10 million, Between 10 million - 50 million, Between 50 million - 100 million, More than 100 million).

**Tips**

* Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to help us better understand your company's needs and interests.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
* This form is for private sector businesses only. If you are a government agency or other type of organization, please do not fill out this form.
