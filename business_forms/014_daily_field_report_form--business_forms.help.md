# Daily Field Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Daily Field Report Form is used to collect information about your workday, including the work order, weather conditions, and other relevant details. This form helps us track and analyze the work performed by our field staff, ensuring that we can provide accurate reporting and optimize our operations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete the form, please follow these steps:
2. Ensure that you are filling out the form for the correct date.
3. Select the correct work order from the provided options.
4. Enter the crew size and hours worked, including hours paid.
5. Provide any comments or observations about the weather conditions and work performed.
6. Enter any additional comments or remarks.
7. Review and submit the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Date Field

* **Date Field (date)**: This field is used to select the date for which you are reporting your workday. Please select the correct date from the calendar.

### Weather Conditions

* **weather_conditions (text)**: Describe the weather conditions you encountered during your workday. This can include any notable weather events, such as rain, wind, or extreme temperatures.

### Work Order

* **work_order (select_one)**: This field is used to select the work order for which you are reporting your workday. Please select the correct work order from the provided options.

### Job Type

* **job_type (select_multiple)**: This field is used to select the type(s) of job performed during your workday. Please select all that apply from the provided options.

### Crew Size

* **crew_size (number)**: Enter the size of your crew, including any assistants or support staff.

### Hours Worked

* **hours_worked (number)**: Enter the total number of hours worked during your shift.

### Hours Paid

* **hours_paid (number)**: Enter the total number of hours paid during your shift.

### Work Order Comments

* **work_order_comments (text)**: Enter any comments or remarks about the work order.

### Weather Observations

* **weather_observations (text)**: Enter any notable observations about the weather during your workday.

### Comments

* **comments (text)**: Enter any additional comments or remarks about your workday. 

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are accurate and thorough in your reporting.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
* If you need assistance with the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
