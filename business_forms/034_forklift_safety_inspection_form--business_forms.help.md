# Forklift Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Forklift Safety Inspection Form is used to conduct regular safety inspections on forklifts to ensure they are in good working condition and comply with safety standards. The form helps to identify potential hazards and track maintenance and inspection history.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form for each forklift in your inventory.
2. Complete all required fields.
3. Provide accurate and detailed information where applicable.
4. Use the form to track maintenance and inspections over time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Address**: Enter the address where the forklift is located or last used.
* **Company Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the forklift's maintenance and inspections.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Forklift Description**: Briefly describe the forklift, including its model and any distinctive features.
* **Forklift Status**: Indicate whether the forklift is currently active or inactive.
* **Safety Hazards**: Check "Yes" if you identify any safety hazards or concerns with the forklift.
* **Safety Hazards Details**: Describe any safety hazards or concerns you identified.
* **Corrective Action**: Record any necessary corrective actions taken or recommended.
* **Inspector Sign-off**: Record the date and time you completed the inspection.
* **Last Use Date**: Record the date the forklift was last used.
* **Next Use Date**: Record the date the forklift is due for its next use.
* **Last Inspection Date**: Record the date of the last inspection.
* **Last Inspection Status**: Indicate the status of the last inspection (Active or Inactive).
* **Last Inspector**: Record the name of the person who last inspected the forklift.
* **Next Inspection Due Date**: Record the date of the next scheduled inspection.
* **Next Inspection Status**: Indicate the status of the next inspection (Active or Inactive).
* **Last Inspection Notes**: Record any notes from the last inspection.
* **Last Inspection Result**: Record the outcome of the last inspection (Yes or No).
* **Next Inspection Due Date**: Record the date of the next scheduled inspection.
* **Assigned Tool**: Record the tool used during the inspection.
* **Comments**: Leave any additional comments or notes about the forklift.
* **Last Use Time**: Record the time of the last use.
* **Last Location**: Record the location where the forklift was last used.
* **Last Maintenance Date**: Record the date of the last maintenance.
* **Last Maintenance Status**: Indicate the status of the last maintenance (Active or Inactive).

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Use the form to track and maintain a record of forklifts' safety inspections.
* Update the form regularly to reflect changes in the forklift's status or maintenance history.
* Use the form to identify and address potential safety hazards before they become a problem.
