# Data Handling Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form assesses an organization's data handling practices and policies to ensure they align with industry standards and best practices for data security, privacy, and compliance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully read each question and select the most relevant answer based on your organization's current practices.
2. Ensure you answer all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
3. Take your time, as this form is designed to ensure you understand your organization's data handling practices and identify areas for improvement.
