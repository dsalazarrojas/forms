# Business Strategy Offsite Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Strategy Offsite Booking Form is a tool used to plan and book offsite events for business strategy sessions. It helps organizers gather all the necessary information to make informed decisions about the offsite, including venue requirements, objectives, activities, and special requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Organizer Information**: Enter your name, title, and company name to provide your contact information.
2. **Offsite Details**: Enter the offsite title, start and end dates, total duration, and expected number of attendees.
3. **Venue Requirements**: Choose your preferred venue type (e.g., Hotel with Conference Center, Resort, Conference Center, etc.) and indicate whether on-site lodging is needed.
4. **Budget**: If applicable, enter the budget per person per day.
5. **Offsite Objectives**: Select the primary objectives of the offsite (e.g., Strategic Planning, Team Building, Leadership Development).
6. **Key Discussion Topics**: Enter the main subjects to be discussed during the offsite.
7. **Activities and Format**: Choose the types of activities you prefer (e.g., Formal Presentations, Workshops, Breakout Sessions, Team Activities).
8. **Special Requests**: Enter any additional requests or special requirements (e.g., accessibility needs, dietary restrictions).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organizer Name (2)**: Enter your name as the organizer.
* **Title or Position (3)**: Enter your title or position.
* **Company Name (4)**: Enter your company name.
* **Email Address (5)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (6)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Offsite Title or Theme (8)**: Enter the name of the offsite.
* **Start Date (9)**: Enter the first day of the offsite.
* **End Date (10)**: Enter the last day of the offsite.
* **Total Duration in Days (11)**: Enter the total number of days for the offsite.
* **Expected Number of Attendees (12)**: Enter the expected number of attendees.
* **Venue Type Preference (14)**: Choose your preferred venue type.
* **Location Preference (15)**: Enter any geographic preference or specific area.
* **Lodging Needed (16)**: Indicate whether on-site lodging is needed.
* **Budget Per Person Per Day (17)**: Enter the budget per person per day (if applicable).
* **Primary Objectives (19)**: Select the main focus areas of the offsite.
* **Key Discussion Topics (20)**: Enter the main subjects to be discussed during the offsite.
* **Activity Preferences (22)**: Choose the types of activities you prefer.
* **Catering Requirements (23)**: Enter any meal plans needed.
* **Technology and AV Needs (24)**: Enter any equipment required.
* **Special Accommodations Needed (26)**: Enter any accessibility or dietary needs.
* **Additional Requests or Notes (27)**: Enter any other special requirements.
