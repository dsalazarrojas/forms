# Manuscript Review Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for a peer review process in academic or professional settings. It captures essential information about the manuscript, including review terms, submission dates, and manuscript version details. The form ensures a clear understanding and agreement on the review process between the reviewer and editor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the manuscript and review process.
2. Review the manuscript version details and ensure they match the actual status.
3. Sign and date the agreement to confirm your understanding and commitment to the review process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Reviewer Name (Page 1)
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of the reviewer, responsible for evaluating the manuscript.
* **Required**: Yes

### 2. Reviewer Email (Page 1)
* **Reviewer Email**: Enter the email address of the reviewer for communication purposes.
* **Required**: Yes

### 3. Manuscript Title (Page 1)
* **Manuscript Title**: Enter the title of the manuscript being reviewed.
* **Required**: Yes

### 4. Reviewer Term Start Date (Page 1)
* **Reviewer Term Start Date**: Enter the start date of the review term for the manuscript.
* **Type**: Date
* **Required**: Yes

### 5. Reviewer Term End Date (Page 1)
* **Reviewer Term End Date**: Enter the end date of the review term for the manuscript.
* **Type**: Date
* **Required**: Yes

### 6. Reviewer Signature (Page 1)
* **Reviewer Signature**: Sign this field to confirm your understanding and commitment to the review process.
* **Required**: Yes

### 7. Reviewer Terms (Page 1)
* **Reviewer Terms**: Read and agree to the review terms.
* **Required**: Yes

### 8. Manuscript ID (Page 2)
* **Manuscript ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the manuscript.
* **Required**: Yes

### 9. Manuscript Status (Page 2)
* **Manuscript Status**: Select the status of the manuscript from the options provided.

### 15. Agreement Date (Page 3)
