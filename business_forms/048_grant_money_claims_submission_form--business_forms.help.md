# Grant Money Claims Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grant Money Claims Submission Form is used to submit claims related to grant money, allowing users to provide accurate and complete information to support their claim.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by reviewing the form's purpose and understanding what information is required.
2. Complete the form in sections, starting from Page 1.
3. On Page 1, if you have any attachments to include with your submission, check the "Attachments" field.
4. On Page 2, select "Yes" if your submission includes attachments and "No" otherwise.
5. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1** (`page_1`, text, optional): This is the first page of the form where you can provide any additional context or information about your claim.
* **Submission Details** (`page_2`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you have attachments to include with your submission, otherwise select "No".
* **Attachments** (`page_3`, note, optional): If you selected "Yes" on the previous field, you can attach files here to include with your submission.
* **Submit** (`page_4`, note, optional): Review your submission and click "Submit" to complete and submit your claim.
