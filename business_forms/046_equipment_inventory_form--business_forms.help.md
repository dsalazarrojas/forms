# equipment_inventory_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The equipment inventory form is used to gather and manage information about items in the inventory.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps listed above in the field explanations.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_header** (form_header, note, optional): This is a header section for the form.
* **item_name** (item_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the item in the inventory.
* **item_description** (item_description, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the item to help identify it accurately.
* **item_price** (item_price, number, optional): Enter the price of the item.
* **item_quantity** (item_quantity, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the item in the inventory.
* **distributor_item_list** (distributor_item_list, select_multiple, optional): Select all the distributors that supply the item. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **distributor_name** (distributor_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the distributor.
* **item_status** (item_status, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the item. Options are 'Active' and 'Inactive'.
