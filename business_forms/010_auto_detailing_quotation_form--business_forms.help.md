# Auto Detailing Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers to generate a quote for auto detailing services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, which are marked as "required". 
2. Be as accurate as possible when entering information about your vehicle and services needed.
3. If you have any specific requests or stains on your vehicle, note them in the "Specific Requests or Stains" field.
4. Select your preferred date and time for service in the "Preferred Date" and "Preferred Time" fields, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Customer Name** (Customer Name, text, required)
    Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or documents.
    
- **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required)
    Enter your email address where you would like to receive the quote and any further communications from us.
    
- **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required)
    Enter your primary contact number where we can reach you.
    
- **Vehicle Make and Model** (Vehicle Make and Model, text, required)
    Enter the make and model of your vehicle, e.g. BMW X5.
    
- **Vehicle Type** (Vehicle Type, select_one, required)
    Select the type of vehicle you have (Sedan, Coupe, SUV, Truck, Van).
    
- **Vehicle Condition** (Vehicle Condition, select_one, required)
    Select the current state of your vehicle (Good, Fair, Poor, Extremely Dirty).
    
- **Detailing Services Required** (Detailing Services Required, select_multiple, required)
    Select all the services you need from our detailing services list (Interior Detail, Exterior Detail, Full Detail, Ceramic Coating, Engine Bay Cleaning, Headlight Restoration).
    
- **Service Location** (Service Location, select_one, required)
    Choose where you would like to receive the service (At Workshop, Mobile - At My Location).
    
- **Specific Requests or Stains** (Specific Requests or Stains, text, optional)
    If you have any specific issues with your vehicle like pet hair or coffee stains, note them here.
    
- **Preferred Date** (Preferred Date, date, optional)
    Enter a date when you would like to receive the service. If no date is chosen, we will contact you to schedule.
    
- **Preferred Time** (Preferred Time, time, optional)
    Enter a time when you would like to receive the service. If no time is chosen, we will contact you to schedule.

## Tips
- Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
- If you have any questions, contact us at your selected contact information.
- We will contact you to confirm your quote and schedule the service.
