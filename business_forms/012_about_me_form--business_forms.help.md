# About Me Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The About Me Form is designed to gather information about you, an employee or student. This form helps the organization understand your basic contact details, personal background, and social and communication preferences. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to help us get to know you better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling in your **Full name**, **Email address**, and **Phone number**. Make sure to enter your full name and a valid email address that you use regularly.
2. Provide your **Date of birth**, **Gender**, and **Current address**.
3. Share your **Current occupation or job title**, **Employer or school name**, and **Hobbies and interests**.
4. Indicate your proficiency in **Languages spoken**.
5. Choose your **Preferred method of contact**, if you have one.
6. Optionally, share your **Social media handle or profile link** and a **Fun fact about yourself**.
7. Finally, share any other **Personal or professional goals** and **Other things you'd like others to know about you**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Your full name as you prefer to be called.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Your valid email address that you use regularly.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Your self-identified gender (Male, Female, Non-binary, or Prefer not to say).
* **Current address** (`address`, `text`, required): Your current address including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Current occupation or job title** (`occupation`, `text`, required): Your current occupation or job title.
* **Employer or school name** (`employer_or_school`, `text`, optional): Your current or past employer or school name, if applicable.
* **Hobbies and interests** (`hobbies_interests`, `text`, optional): A list of your main hobbies or interests.
* **Languages spoken** (`languages_spoken`, `text`, optional): A list of languages you are proficient in.
* **Preferred method of contact** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, optional): Your preferred method of contact (Email, Phone, Text message, or No preference).
* **Social media handle or profile link** (`social_media_handle`, `text`, optional): Your social media handle or profile link, if you have one.
* **Fun fact** (`fun_fact`, `text`, optional): A fun fact about yourself that you'd like to share.
* **Personal or professional goals** (`goals_or_aspirations`, `text`, optional): A statement of your personal or professional goals.
* **Anything else** (`anything_else`, `text`, optional): Any other information you'd like to share about yourself.
