# Order Delivery Cost Calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Order Delivery Cost Calculator is a form designed to calculate the estimated cost of delivery for an order. It helps businesses to plan and manage their delivery costs efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your delivery location, date, and time of delivery.
2. Enter the weight of the order.
3. Enter the distance of the delivery (in kilometers or miles).
4. Choose whether there is a fuel surcharge for the delivery.
5. Select whether the delivery is active or inactive.
6. Choose the type of delivery vehicle (if applicable).
7. Enter the estimated delivery time.
8. Enter the estimated delivery cost.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Location**: Select the location where the order will be delivered. This field is used to determine the specific conditions of the delivery.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date of the delivery. This will help in determining the correct estimated delivery cost.
* **Delivery Time**: Enter the time of the delivery. This will help in determining the correct estimated delivery cost.
* **Delivery Cost**: Enter the cost of the delivery. This will be used as the base cost for the calculation.
* **Order Weight**: Enter the weight of the order. This will help in determining the estimated delivery cost based on the order weight.
* **Delivery Distance**: Enter the distance of the delivery (in kilometers or miles). This will help in determining the estimated delivery cost based on the distance.
* **Fuel Surcharge**: Select whether there is a fuel surcharge for the delivery.
* **Delivery Type**: Select whether the delivery is active or inactive.
* **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle used for the delivery (if applicable).
* **Estimated Delivery Time**: Enter the estimated delivery time.
* **Estimated Delivery Cost**: Enter the estimated delivery cost. This is the calculated result of the delivery cost based on the provided information.
* **Delivery Status**: Select whether the delivery is active or inactive. This will be used to determine the status of the delivery.
