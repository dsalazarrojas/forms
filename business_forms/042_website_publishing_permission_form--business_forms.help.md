# website_publishing_permission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request permission to publish website content on a platform managed by the organization. You will need to provide necessary details for your request to be processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your publisher's name in the "Publisher Name" field.
2. Enter the URL of the website you want to publish in the "Website URL" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the content you want to publish in the "Content Description" field.
4. Select the type of content you want to publish from the "Content Type" dropdown menu.
5. Choose the status of your content from the "Content Status" dropdown menu.
6. Select the location where your content will be published from the "Content Location" dropdown menu.
7. If applicable, enter the name and contact information of the copyright holder in the "Copyright Holder" field.
8. Fill in the date and time of publication in the "Date Published" and "Time Published" fields.
9. Indicate whether permission is granted or not in the "Permission Granted" field.
10. Add any comments about your request in the "Comments" field.
11. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
12. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
13. If applicable, select whether the form is assigned to a specific user from the "Assigned User" dropdown menu.
14. Select whether the form is assigned to a specific tool from the "Assigned Tool" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Publisher Name (text)**: Enter the name of the user publishing the content.
* **Website URL (text)**: Enter the URL of the website you are requesting permission to publish.
* **Content Description (text)**: Provide a brief description of the content you want to publish.
* **Content Type (select_one)**: Choose the type of content you want to publish (Text, Image, Video, or Audio).
* **Content Status (select_multiple)**: Select the status of your content (Draft, Published, or Unpublished).
* **Content Location (select_multiple)**: Choose where your content will be published (Web, Mobile, or Offline).
* **Copyright Holder (text)**: Enter the name and contact information of the copyright holder if applicable.
* **Date Published (date)**: Enter the date you want to publish your content.
* **Time Published (time)**: Enter the time you want to publish your content.
* **Permission Granted (select_one)**: Indicate whether permission is granted or not.
* **Comments (note)**: Add any comments about your request.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned User (select_one)**: Select whether the form is assigned to a specific user.
* **Assigned Tool (select_one)**: Select whether the form is assigned to a specific tool.

Note: All fields are optional, so you can fill in only the necessary ones.
