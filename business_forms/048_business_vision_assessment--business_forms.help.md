# Business Vision Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the company assess the clarity and alignment of its vision, values, and mission. The form will collect feedback from respondents about their understanding, alignment, and inspiration from the company's vision, values, and mission.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your full name
2. Select your role within the organization
3. Enter your company's name
4. Provide the date of this assessment
5. Answer the questions regarding the company's vision:
	* How clear is the company's vision?
	* Do you understand the company's vision?
	* Do you align with the company's vision?
	* Is the company's vision relevant to the business?
6. Answer the questions regarding the company's values:
	* How clear are the company's values?
	* Do you understand the company's values?
	* Do you align with the company's values?
7. Answer the questions regarding the company's mission:
	* How clear is the company's mission?
8. Answer the questions regarding the company's strategic direction:
	* How clear is the company's strategic direction?
9. Answer the questions regarding the achievability of the vision:
	* Is the vision achievable?
10. Answer the questions regarding leadership's embodiment of the vision:
	* Does leadership strongly embody the vision?
11. Answer the questions regarding the organization's commitment level:
	* How committed is the organization?
12. Answer the questions regarding the vision's inspiration level:
	* Does the vision inspire you?
13. (Optional) If you have any suggested improvements for the vision, provide them in this section
14. (Optional) Any additional comments on the vision

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Your full name as the respondent.
* **Your Role in the Organization** (`respondent_role`, text, required): Your position or title within the organization.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): The name of the organization.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): The date of this assessment.
* **Clarity of Company Vision** (`vision_clarity`, select_one, required): How clear is the company's vision? Options: Extremely Clear, Very Clear, Clear, Somewhat Clear, Unclear.
* **Understanding of Vision Statement** (`vision_understanding`, select_one, required): Do you understand the vision statement? Options: Complete Understanding, Good Understanding, Adequate Understanding, Limited Understanding, No Understanding.
* **Alignment with Vision** (`vision_alignment`, select_one, required): Do you align with the company's vision? Options: Fully Aligned, Mostly Aligned, Somewhat Aligned, Not Very Aligned, Not Aligned.
* **Vision Relevance to Business** (`vision_relevance`, select_one, required): Is the company's vision relevant to the business? Options: Highly Relevant, Relevant, Adequately Relevant, Not Very Relevant, Not Relevant.
* **Clarity of Company Values** (`values_clarity`, select_one, required): How clear are the company's values? Options: Extremely Clear, Very Clear, Clear, Somewhat Clear, Unclear.
* **Understanding of Core Values** (`values_understanding`, select_one, required): Do you understand the company's values? Options: Complete Understanding, Good Understanding, Adequate Understanding, Limited Understanding, No Understanding.
* **Personal Alignment with Values** (`values_alignment`, select_one, required): Do you align with the company's values? Options: Fully Aligned, Mostly Aligned, Somewhat Aligned, Weakly Aligned, Not Aligned.
* **Clarity of Company Mission** (`mission_clarity`, select_one, required): How clear is the company's mission? Options: Extremely Clear, Very Clear, Clear, Somewhat Clear, Unclear.
* **Clarity of Strategic Direction** (`strategic_direction`, select_one, required): How clear is the company's strategic direction? Options: Very Clear, Clear, Adequately Clear, Unclear, Very Unclear.
* **Vision Achievability** (`vision_achievability`, select_one, required): Is the company's vision achievable? Options: Very Achievable, Achievable, Somewhat Achievable, Difficult to Achieve, Not Achievable.
* **Leadership Embodiment of Vision** (`leadership_embodiment`, select_one, required): Does leadership strongly embody the vision? Options: Strongly Embodies, Embodies Well, Adequately Embodies, Weakly Embodies, Does Not Embody.
* **Organizational Commitment Level** (`organizational_commitment`, select_one, required): How committed is the organization? Options: Highly Committed, Committed, Moderately Committed, Weakly Committed, Not Committed.
* **Vision Inspiration Level** (`vision_inspiration`, select_one, required): Does the vision inspire you? Options: Highly Inspiring, Inspiring, Somewhat Inspiring, Not Inspiring, Demotivating.
* **Suggested Vision Improvements** (`suggested_improvements`, text, optional): Any suggested improvements for the vision.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any additional comments on the vision.

## Tips

- Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
- Take as much time as needed to complete each question.
- If you are unsure or unclear about any question, please ask for clarification.
- The more feedback you provide, the more accurate the assessment will be.
- This form is for internal use only.
