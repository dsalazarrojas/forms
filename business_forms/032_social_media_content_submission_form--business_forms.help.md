# social media content submission form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about social media content submitted by users. It's meant to be filled out by clients or users who want to share their social media content, such as posts, photos, or videos, with your team or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. 1. **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client who submitted the content.
2. 2. **Social Media Platform**: Select the platform where the content was posted (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn).
3. 3. **Content Description**: Briefly describe the content you want to share (e.g., "New product launch" or "Company news").
4. 4. **Content Type**: Choose the type of content (e.g., video, photo, or post).
5. 5. **Content Image**: If applicable, enter the image URL or file upload information.
6. 6. **Report Date**: Select the date the content was posted.
7. 7. **Report Time**: Select the time the content was posted.
8. 8. **Report Location**: Enter the location where the content was posted.
9. 9. **Social Media Content**: Enter the content itself, such as a link or a brief description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the client who submitted the content.
* **Social Media Platform** (Select One, Required): Choose the platform where the content was posted (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn).
* **Content Description** (Text, Optional): Briefly describe the content you want to share.
* **Content Type** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the type of content (e.g., video, photo, or post).
* **Content Image** (Text, Optional): If applicable, enter the image URL or file upload information.
* **Report Date** (Date, Optional): Select the date the content was posted.
* **Report Time** (Time, Optional): Select the time the content was posted.
* **Report Location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location where the content was posted.
* **Social Media Content** (Text, Optional): Enter the content itself, such as a link or a brief description.

## Tips

* Please be accurate when filling out this form, as the information will be used to report and share the content.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your team or the client for clarification.
* Use this form to submit social media content that is work-related and relevant to your organization.
