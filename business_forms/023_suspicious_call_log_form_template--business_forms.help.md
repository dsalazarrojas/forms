# Suspicious Call Log Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Suspicious Call Log Form Template is designed to record and track suspicious calls made to a business, such as harassment, threats, or other concerning incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the `Callers Name` field with the name of the person who made the call.
* Enter the `Callers Phone` number in the `Callers Phone` field.
* Describe the call in the `Call Description` field.
* Select the level of `Suspiciousness Level` that best matches the call's severity.
* Choose the level of `Threat Level` that best describes the potential threat posed by the call.
* Enter the `Date and Time of Call` in the format YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS.
* Type the `Name of the person recording the call` in the `Recorded By` field.
* Select the outcome of the call in the `Call Outcome` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Callers Name (`caller_name`)**: Enter the name of the person who made the call.
* **Callers Phone (`caller_phone`)**: Enter the phone number of the person who made the call.
* **Call Description (`call_description`)**: Briefly describe the call, including any relevant details.
* **Suspiciousness Level (`suspiciousness_level`)**: Select the level of suspicion (Low, Medium, High) that best matches the call's severity.
* **Threat Level (`threat_level`)**: Select the level of potential threat (None, Low, Medium, High) that best describes the call.
* **Date and Time of Call (`date_and_time`)**: Enter the date and time the call was made in the format YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS.
* **Recorded By (`recorded_by`)**: Type the name of the person who recorded the call.
* **Call Outcome (`call_outcome`)**: Select the outcome of the call (Disposed, Held, Transferred, Escalated, Closed).

## Tips
* Be concise in your descriptions to ensure accurate and clear documentation of the call.
* Use clear and correct formatting for dates and times to ensure proper sorting and searching.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, even if they are not marked as required.
* If a field is not relevant to a particular call, you can leave it blank or fill it with a placeholder value.
