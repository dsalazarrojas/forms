# Real Estate Seller Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Seller Intake Form is designed to collect information from real estate sellers. This form will help us create a comprehensive database for real estate sales.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

*   Fill in the required information for each field accurately and completely.
*   Review the form for any errors before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **1. **real_estate_seller** (Text): Briefly describe the property you are trying to sell.
*   **2. **contact_details** (Text): Enter your contact information for us to reach out to you.
*   **3. **property_details** (Text): Write a detailed description of the property you are selling.
*   **4. **property_type** (Select One): Select the type of property you are selling (e.g., single family, multi-family, commercial).
*   **5. **property_status** (Select Multiple): Choose the current status of the property (e.g., active, pending, sold).
*   **6. **contact_name** (Text): Enter your name as the contact person for the property.
*   **7. **contact_email** (Email): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
*   **8. **contact_phone** (Text): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
*   **9. **property_address** (Text): Enter the address of the property.
*   **10.** **property_address_2** (Text): Enter the address line 2 (if applicable).
*   **11.** **property_city** (Select One): Select the city where the property is located.
*   **12.** **property_state** (Select One): Select the state where the property is located.
*   **13.** **property_zip** (Number): Enter the zip code of the property.
*   **14.** **property_sqft** (Number): Enter the square footage of the property.

**Tips**

*   Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
*   Use the correct property type and status to ensure accurate categorization.
*   Fill in all the required fields to avoid submission issues.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
