# workforce_performance_consulting_program_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit an application for the workforce performance consulting program. The program aims to provide guidance and support to organizations in identifying and improving their performance challenges and focus areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your organization's name in the **Organization Name** field.
2. Provide your contact name and details in the **Contact Name** field.
3. In the **Performance Challenges** field, describe the main performance challenges your organization is facing.
4. In the **Priority Focus Areas** field, identify the specific focus areas where your organization needs improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, required): Enter your organization's name as it appears on official documents and correspondence.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, required): Provide your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you about your application.
* **Performance Challenges** (`performance_challenges`, required): Describe the main performance challenges your organization is facing, such as poor employee engagement, low productivity, or high turnover rates.
* **Priority Focus Areas** (`priority_focus_areas`, required): Identify the specific areas where your organization needs improvement, such as improving communication, leadership development, or change management.

## Tips
* Be as specific and thorough as possible when describing your performance challenges and priority focus areas.
* If you need help or guidance on the application process, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will review your application and provide feedback and support to help your organization improve its performance.
