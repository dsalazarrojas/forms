# Remote Transmission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Remote Transmission Form is a versatile online form designed to collect information from end-users for a variety of purposes. It consists of multiple fields to gather various types of data, including text, selection, and file uploads. This guide will walk you through each field and its intended use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the remote transmission in the text field labeled "Remote Transmission Form".
2. Provide a brief description of the remote transmission in the text field labeled "Remote Transmission Form Description".
3. Select relevant options for the "Capture" field (if applicable).
4. Upload files (if applicable) in the "File Uploads" field.
5. Answer the CAPTCHA question by selecting an option from the dropdown menu.
6. Confirm or deny the "Capture 2" option by selecting one of the provided choices.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Select a date and/or time (if applicable) for the remote transmission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Remote Transmission Form** (1, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title for the remote transmission.
* **Remote Transmission Form Description** (2, text, required/optional): Provide a short description of the remote transmission.
* **Capture** (3, select_multiple, required/optional): Select multiple options relevant to the remote transmission. This field is intended to capture specific details about the transmission.
* **File Uploads** (4, text, required/optional): Upload files related to the remote transmission. This field supports multiple file uploads.
* **CAPTCHA** (5, select_one, required/optional): Answer the CAPTCHA question by selecting an option from the dropdown menu.
* **Capture 2** (6, select_multiple, required/optional): Confirm or deny the "Capture 2" option by selecting one of the provided choices.
* **Email** (7, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for further communication related to the remote transmission.
* **Phone** (8, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact purposes.
* **Date** (9, date, required/optional): Select a date for the remote transmission (if required).
* **Time** (10, time, required/optional): Choose a time for the remote transmission (if required).

Note: Some fields are not required for submission, but it is recommended to fill them out for a more complete transmission package.
