# Sales Team Daily Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Team Daily Report Form is a tool for sales teams to submit their daily reports, allowing sales managers to track and review the team's progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your sales team name in the "Sales Team Name" field.
2. Provide your sales manager's name in the "Sales Manager Name" field.
3. Enter your sales manager's title in the "Sales Manager Title" field.
4. Enter your sales manager's email in the "Sales Manager Email" field.
5. Check the "Region" field to indicate whether the region is active or inactive.
6. Select all applicable sales regions in the "Sales Region" field.
7. Select all applicable sales regions in the "Sales Region 2" field.
8. Enter the sales manager's region in the "Sales Manager Region" field.
9. Choose the daily report date in the "Daily Report Date" field.
10. Choose the daily report time in the "Daily Report Time" field.
11. Enter any additional notes in the "Region Note" field.
12. Enter any additional notes for the sales region in the "Sales Region Note" field.
13. Check the "Region Status" field to indicate the region's status.
14. Check the "Region Status 2" field to indicate the region's status.
15. Enter the total sales amount in the "Total Sales" field.
16. Enter the sales amount in the "Sales Amount" field.
17. Enter any additional notes in the "Daily Report Note" field.
18. Check whether the sales team submitted a daily report in the "Sales Team Daily Report" field.
19. Check the "Region Status 3" field to indicate the region's status.
20. Enter the sales total in the "Sales Total" field.
21. Enter the sales amount in the "Sales Amount 2" field.
22. Enter any additional notes in the "Sales Manager Note 2" field.
