# Wellness Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Wellness Form" is designed to collect general user information, including demographics and personal details. This form helps us understand better the user's background and needs, which is useful for various business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Wellness Form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required information ready.
2. Read each field explanation below to understand what is expected from you.
3. Fill out the form accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional)
This field is used to collect your first name.

### 2. **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional)
This field is used to collect your last name.

### 3. **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you for any further communication.

### 4. **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
Provide your phone number to enable us to reach you via call or text.

### 5. **Select One Option** (`select_one_option`, select_one, optional)
Choose either "Yes" or "No" from the options provided, indicating your preference or situation.

### 6. **Select Multiple Options** (`select_multiple_options`, select_multiple, optional)
Select one or multiple options from the list to express your preferences or needs.

### 7. **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional)
Enter your date of birth for demographic purposes.

### 8. **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional)
Select your gender from the provided options to help us better understand your identity.

### 9. **Time of Birth** (`dob_time`, time, optional)
This field is currently not clear in its purpose; however, it seems to collect the time of birth. Please enter this information accordingly.

### 10. **Notes** (`note`, note, optional)
Enter any additional comments or thoughts you have regarding your submission.

### 11. **Select One Option 2** (`select_one_option_2`, select_one, optional)
Choose either "Yes" or "No" from the provided options for this selection.

Note: If any field is marked as optional, it is not required to be filled out.
