# Incorporation Process Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help our team better understand your needs and experiences during the incorporation process. The information collected will be used to improve our services, address any challenges you face, and provide more tailored support to ensure a smooth incorporation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
2. Select the relevant options for your business type and structure.
3. Provide your contact information and additional notes if necessary.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name?**: This is a required field where you can enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business, which should match the name you have chosen for your company.
* **Business Type**: Choose the type of business that best describes your company:
	+ Legal: A sole proprietorship or partnership.
	+ Corporate: A corporation.
	+ Partnership: A partnership.
	+ LLC: A limited liability company.
	+ Non-profit: A non-profit organization.
	+ Sole Proprietorship: A business owned by one person.
	+ Other: If your business type is not listed, please specify.
* **Business Structure**: Select the structure that best describes your business:
	+ Corporation.
	+ LLC.
	+ Partnership.
	+ Sole Proprietorship.
* **Address**: Enter your business address, which should match the address on your identification documents.
* **City**: Enter the city where your business is located.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code for your business location.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your business phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your business email address.
* **Website**: Enter your business website URL (if applicable).
* **What stage are you in the incorporation process?**: Choose the stage that best describes your progress:
	+ Not Started.
	+ In Progress.
	+ Completed.
* **Which service provider are you working with?**: Select all relevant service providers:
	+ Law Firm.
	+ Corporate Service.
* **Have you faced any issues during the incorporation process?**: Choose the answer that best describes your experience:
	+ True.
	+ False.
	+ Not Sure.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **Comments (Additional)**: Provide any further comments or feedback.
* **Business License Number**: Enter your business license number (if applicable).
* **Business License Type**: Choose the type of business license:
	+ Business License.
	+ Sales Tax ID.
	+ Employee ID.
* **Business Registration Number**: Enter your business registration number (if applicable).
* **Additional Notes**: Share any additional notes or feedback about your experience.
* **Additional Notes Additional 1-4**: You can provide any additional notes or feedback for each of these fields.
