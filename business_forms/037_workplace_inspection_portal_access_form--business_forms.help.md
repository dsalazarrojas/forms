# Workplace Inspection Portal Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Inspection Portal Access Form is a simple, user-friendly form designed to gather necessary information from employees who need access to the Workplace Inspection Portal. The form is used to collect details such as inspection dates, times, and contact information to facilitate the inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct "Workplace Inspection Form" page.
2. Enter the date of your inspection request using the "Request Date" field.
3. Enter the start and end times of your inspection, if applicable, using the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields.
4. Choose the correct safety inspector(s) from the "Safety Inspector" dropdown menu, who will be conducting the inspection.
5. List any contractors involved in the inspection in the "Contractor" field.
6. Enter your organization name in the "Organization" field.
7. Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address in the "Contact Person", "Contact Number", and "Email" fields, respectively.
8. Enter a brief description of your work in the "Work Description" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workplace Inspection Form**: This field is the main section of the form where you will provide your inspection details.
	+ *Required*: No
	+ *Type*: text
* **Request Date**: Enter the date on which you would like to conduct the inspection.
	+ *Type*: date
	+ *Required*: No
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your inspection, if applicable.
	+ *Type*: time
	+ *Required*: No
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your inspection, if applicable.
	+ *Type*: time
	+ *Required*: No
* **Safety Inspector**: Choose the safety inspector(s) who will be conducting the inspection.
	+ *Type*: select_one
	+ *Required*: No
* **Contractor**: List any contractors involved in the inspection.
	+ *Type*: select_multiple
	+ *Required*: No
* **Organization**: Enter the name of your organization.
	+ *Type*: text
	+ *Required*: No
* **Contact Person**: Enter your name.
	+ *Type*: text
	+ *Required*: No
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number.
	+ *Type*: text
	+ *Required*: No
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ *Type*: email
	+ *Required*: No
* **Address**: Enter the address where the inspection will take place.
	+ *Type*: text
	+ *Required*: No
* **Work Description**: Provide a brief description of your work.
	+ *Type*: text
	+ *Required*: No

## Tips
- Ensure that you have a valid reason for requesting access to the Workplace Inspection Portal.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR department.
