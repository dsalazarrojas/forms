# Manufacturing Cost Variance Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report discrepancies or variations in manufacturing costs, allowing users to identify areas of inefficiency or over/under-spending.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form fields and ensure you are familiar with each section.
2. Answer the questions honestly and accurately to the best of your abilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **<blank>** (`manufacturing_cost_variance_report_form_page_1_label`, text): This is the first section of the form. Enter a brief description of the cost variance issue you are reporting.
- **Option 1** (`manufacturing_cost_variance_report_form_page_2_label`, select_one): Select the relevant option that best describes the type of cost variance issue you are reporting.
- **<blank>** (`manufacturing_cost_variance_report_form_page_3_label`, text): This is the second section of the form. Enter additional information about the cost variance issue.
- **Option 1, Option 2, Option 3** (`manufacturing_cost_variance_report_form_page_4_label`, select_multiple): Select all applicable options that best describe the specific cost variance issue you are reporting.
- **<blank>** (`manufacturing_cost_variance_report_form_page_13_label`, select_one): Select the option that best describes the cause of the cost variance issue.
- **<blank>** (`manufacturing_cost_variance_report_form_page_14_label`, text): This is the last section of the form. Enter any additional comments or notes about the cost variance issue.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate in your responses to ensure the most informative report.
- If you are unsure about any section, consult with your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
- This form is a tool to help identify and address cost variance issues, not to assign blame.
