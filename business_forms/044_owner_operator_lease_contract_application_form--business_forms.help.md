# owner-operator-lease-contract-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from potential lessees for the purpose of generating a lease contract. It is intended to be filled out by the lessee (the individual or organization seeking to enter into a lease agreement with a trucking company).

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Application Form** on page 1, providing any requested information about your lease application.
2. Move on to **Owner Operator Info** on page 2, where you will provide details about the owner and operator of the vehicle.
3. In **Lease Start Date** and **Lease End Date** (pages 3 and 4), enter the desired start and end dates of the lease agreement.
4. In **Truck Info** (page 5), provide any relevant information about the vehicle being leased.
5. Sign **Owner Operator Signature** (page 6) to confirm that you are the owner or operator of the vehicle.
6. Sign **Assignee Signature** (page 7) to confirm that you are the individual or organization applying for the lease.
7. Finally, review and sign the **Lease Contract** (page 8), which will contain the terms of your lease agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Application Form
* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, required: false): This is the main form where you will provide any necessary information about your lease application. Please fill out this form as thoroughly and accurately as possible.

### Owner Operator Info
* **Owner Operator Info** (`owner_operator_info`, text, required: false): Here, you will provide details about the owner and operator of the vehicle.

### Lease Start Date
* **Lease Start Date** (`lease_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the desired start date of the lease agreement. Please use the date format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Lease End Date
* **Lease End Date** (`lease_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the desired end date of the lease agreement. Please use the date format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Truck Info
* **Truck Info** (`truck_info`, text, required: false): In this field, you can provide any relevant information about the vehicle being leased, such as its make, model, and condition.

### Owner Operator Signature
* **Owner Operator Signature** (`owner_operator_signature`, text, required: false): Sign your name here to confirm that you are the owner or operator of the vehicle.

### Assignee Signature
* **Assignee Signature** (`assignee_signature`, text, required: false): Sign your name here to confirm that you are the individual or organization applying for the lease.

### Lease Contract
* **Lease Contract** (`lease_contract`, note, required: false): This is the lease agreement that will contain the terms of your lease. Please review and sign this carefully before submitting your application.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out this form.
* Make sure to sign all required fields, including the lease contract.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact form@gic.mx.
