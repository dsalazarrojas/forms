# Intercultural Competence Training Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect requests for intercultural competence training from employees, ensuring that the training needs are accurately met.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form completely and accurately.
- Make sure to submit only one request per form.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Request Form** (1): Briefly describe the type of intercultural competence training you are requesting.
* **Training Request** (2): Provide a detailed description of the training you are requesting, including any specific requirements or objectives.
* **Preferred Format** (3): Indicate whether you prefer the training to be conducted in-person, online, or a combination of both.
* **Start Date** (4): Enter the date when you would like the training to start.
* **End Date** (5): Enter the date when you would like the training to end.
* **Training Provider** (6): Provide the name of the training provider you would like to conduct the training.
* **Preferred Language** (7): Indicate whether you prefer to have the training conducted in English or Spanish.
* **Special Requests** (8): List any special requests or accommodations you require for the training, such as accessibility needs or dietary restrictions.
* **Submit Request** (9): Double-check your answers carefully before submitting the form.
