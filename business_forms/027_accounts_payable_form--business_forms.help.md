## Accounts Payable Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
The "Accounts Payable Form" is a tool used to track and manage payments to vendors or suppliers. This form collects essential information to ensure accurate and timely payments.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the **Company Name** in the first field. This is the name of the vendor or supplier you're dealing with.
2. If applicable, enter the **Purchase Order Number** for reference purposes.
3. Select the **Category** of the expense (e.g., Rent, Utilities, Supplies, Services, or Inventory).
4. Enter the **Date of Purchase** when the item was acquired or services were rendered.
5. Enter the **Amount Due**, which is the total outstanding balance to be settled with the vendor or supplier.
6. Select the **Due Date** for payment to be made to the vendor or supplier.
7. Choose the **Payment Status** of the obligation (Unpaid, Partially Paid, or Paid).
8. Provide any **Notes and Comments** about the debt for internal reference.
9. Determine if the payment is a **One-time** or **Recurring** payment.
10. Enter the **Contact Person for Billing**, which is the vendor's contact name.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the vendor or supplier.
* **Purchase Order Number** (Text, Optional): If applicable, enter the purchase order number for reference.
* **Category** (Select One, Required): Select the type of expense (Rent, Utilities, Supplies, Services, or Inventory).
* **Date of Purchase** (Date, Required): Enter the date the item was acquired or services were rendered.
* **Amount Due** (Number, Required): Enter the total outstanding balance to be settled.
* **Due Date** (Date, Required): Select the payment deadline.
* **Payment Status** (Select One, Required): Choose the current state of the obligation (Unpaid, Partially Paid, or Paid).
* **Notes and Comments** (Text, Optional): Provide any internal remarks regarding this debt.
* **Frequency** (Select One, Required): Determine if the payment is a one-time or recurring payment.
* **Contact Person for Billing** (Text, Required): Enter the vendor's contact name.

### Tips
Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure smooth payment processing. If you're unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or accounting department for clarification.
