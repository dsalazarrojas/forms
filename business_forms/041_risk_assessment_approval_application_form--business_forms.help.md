# risk_assessment_approval_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess and manage risks within your business or organization. It allows you to identify, evaluate, and prioritize risks, and to make informed decisions about their mitigation and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of risk you are assessing (Mitigation Plans, Risk Description, Severity, Risk Owner, Risk Type, or Risk Level)
2. Choose the relevant options for each of the selected risk types
3. Provide a description of the risk
4. Describe the plans for mitigating the risk
5. Select the status of the risk assessment (Pending, Approved, or Rejected)
6. Specify whether internal approval is required (True or False)
7. If required, select the date and time of assignment
8. Select the team to which the risk is assigned
9. Add any additional comments or notes about the risk

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Risk Assessment Form (Select Multiple)**: 
    This field allows you to select the type of risk you are assessing. Choose one or more of the options (Mitigation Plans, Risk Description, Severity, Risk Owner, Risk Type, or Risk Level) that best fits the risk you are addressing.
* **Risk Level (Select One)**: 
    This field allows you to select the level of risk you are assessing (Low, Medium, High). This will help to prioritize and categorize the risk.
* **Risk Owner (Select One)**: 
    This field allows you to select the person or team responsible for the risk. Choose the owner who will be responsible for mitigating or managing the risk.
* **Risk Type (Text)**: 
    This field allows you to describe the type of risk (e.g., financial, operational, or strategic).
* **Risk Description (Text)**: 
    This field allows you to provide a detailed description of the risk.
* **Mitigation Plans (Text)**: 
    This field allows you to describe the plans or measures to mitigate or reduce the risk.
* **Risk Assessment Status (Select One)**: 
    This field allows you to select the status of the risk assessment (Pending, Approved, or Rejected). Choose the status that best represents the current state of the risk.
* **Internal Approval (Select One)**: 
    This field allows you to specify whether internal approval is required or not (True or False). Check this box if internal approval is needed for the risk.
* **Assigned Date (Date)**: 
    If internal approval is required, this field allows you to select the date and time of assignment.
* **Assigned Time (Time)**: 
    If internal approval is required, this field allows you to select the time of assignment.
* **Assigned User (Email)**: 
    If internal approval is required, this field allows you to select the user to whom the risk is assigned.
* **Assigned Team (Select One)**: 
    This field allows you to select the team to which the risk is assigned.
* **Assigned Note (Note)**: 
    This field allows you to add any additional comments or notes about the risk.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have a clear understanding of the risk and the risk level before completing this form.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, even if internal approval is not required.
* Double-check your selections before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with your risk assessment team or department.
