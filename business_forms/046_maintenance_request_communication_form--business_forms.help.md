# Maintenance Request Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and communicate maintenance requests from tenants, property managers, or other stakeholders. It helps to record and track issues, facilitating efficient resolution and reducing potential conflicts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Request" option from the "Issue Type" field, if the issue you're reporting is a routine or regular problem.
2. Provide a detailed description of the issue in the "description" field.
3. Choose the priority level of the issue from the "Priority" field (High, Medium, or Low).
4. Select the current status of the issue from the "Status" field (Open, In Progress, or Closed).
5. Choose the user assigned to handle the issue from the "Assigned User" field (John, Jane, or Bob).
6. Provide additional details about the issue type if necessary in the "Issue Type Details" field.
7. Add any comments or notes about the issue in the "Comments" field.
8. Indicate the type of maintenance required for the issue (Routine, Preventative, or Emergency).
9. Specify the frequency of the maintenance (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
10. Identify the location of the issue.
11. Provide the date and time the issue was reported or observed.
12. Select who reported the issue (Tenant or Property Manager).
13. Record the date when the issue was first reported.
14. Record the due date for resolving the issue.
15. Specify who last modified the issue (Tenant or Property Manager).
16. Add any comments about the issue resolution in the "Resolution Note" field.
17. Specify the resolution method used (e.g., repair, replacement, or temporary fix).
18. Attach any relevant documents or images to the issue in the "Attachments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Issue Type (Select One)**: Choose the type of issue being reported (Request, Emergency, or Maintenance).
* **description**: Provide a detailed description of the issue.
* **Priority (Select One)**: Select the priority level of the issue (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Status (Select One)**: Indicate the current status of the issue (Open, In Progress, or Closed).
* **Assigned User (Select One)**: Choose the user assigned to handle the issue (John, Jane, or Bob).
* **Issue Type Details (Text)**: Provide additional details about the issue type if necessary.
* **Comments (Text)**: Add any comments or notes about the issue.
* **Maintenance Type (Select One)**: Indicate the type of maintenance required (Routine, Preventative, or Emergency).
* **Frequency (Select One)**: Specify the frequency of the maintenance (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Location (Text)**: Identify the location of the issue.
* **Date (Date)**: Provide the date and time the issue was reported or observed.
* **Reported By (Select One)**: Specify who reported the issue (Tenant or Property Manager).
* **Reported On (Date)**: Record the date when the issue was first reported.
* **Last Modified (Date)**: Record the date when the issue was last modified.
* **Due Date (Date)**: Record the due date for resolving the issue.
* **Last Modified By (Select One)**: Specify who last modified the issue (Tenant or Property Manager).
* **Attachments (Text)**: Attach any relevant documents or images to the issue.
* **Requester (Text)**: Indicate the person or entity requesting the maintenance.
* **Request Date (Date)**: Record the date the maintenance request was submitted.
* **Status (Select One)**: Indicate the current status of the maintenance request (Open, In Progress, or Closed).
* **Resolution Method (Text)**: Specify the resolution method used (e.g., repair, replacement, or temporary fix).
* **Resolution Note (Text)**: Provide any comments about the issue resolution.
* **Maintenance Status (Select One)**: Indicate the maintenance status (Open, In Progress, or Closed).

## Tips

* Ensure you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Select the correct options for your specific situation.
* Review the form for completeness before submitting.
* Attach any supporting documents or images to facilitate issue resolution.
* Update the form as needed to reflect changes in the issue status or resolution.
