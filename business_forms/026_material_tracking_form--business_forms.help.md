# material_tracking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The material tracking form is designed to keep track of materials used in a business setting. It helps in recording and monitoring the usage of materials, which can be useful for inventory management, cost tracking, and other related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the user's name in the **User Name** field.
2. Answer the **Material Used** question by selecting one or multiple options from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the current **Stock Level** if it's available.
4. Enter the date of material usage in the **Usage Date** field.
5. Provide a tracking number for the material if applicable.
6. Add any additional **Notes** about the material usage.
7. If the material is being tracked, enter the **Material Tracking Form** details.
8. Mark if the material was **Assigned Tool** or not.
9. Select the **Category** of the material (e.g., business forms, order forms).
10. Provide a brief **Description** of the material.
11. Enter the **Output File** if it's available.
12. Finally, select the **Category** of the material again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name (1)**: Enter your name as a reference for your entry.
* **Material Used (2)**: Check yes or no to indicate if material was used.
* **Stock Level (3)**: Enter the current stock level of the material.
* **Usage Date (4)**: Enter the date material was used.
* **Tracking Number (5)**: Enter a unique tracking number for the material.
* **Notes (6)**: Add any additional notes about material usage.
* **Material Tracking Form (7)**: Enter any material tracking form details if applicable.
* **Assigned Tool (8)**: Mark if the material was assigned to a tool.
* **Category (9)**: Select the category of the material (e.g., business forms, order forms).
* **Category (10)**: Confirm the category of the material.
* **Description (11)**: Enter a brief description of the material.
* **Output File (12)**: Enter the output file name if it's available.
* **Category (12)**: Confirm the category of the material again.
