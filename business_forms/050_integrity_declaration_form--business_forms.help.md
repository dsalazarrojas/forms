# Integrity Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Integrity Declaration Form is designed to collect information about a company's, its subsidiaries, and people with a significant share of ownership or control, and their involvement in certain activities. This form is essential for businesses to declare their integrity and commitment to ethical practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately, providing all required information.
2. Make sure to declare all relevant information about your company, its subsidiaries, and individuals with a significant share of ownership or control.
3. Provide detailed explanations for each statement, ensuring that your declarations are clear and concise.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
