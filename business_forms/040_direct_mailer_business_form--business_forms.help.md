# Direct Mailer Business Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for businesses to provide their information for direct mailer services. It is used to gather essential details from businesses for the direct mailer service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the company information page with your business details.
* Move on to the mail details page and fill out the required information.
* Proceed to the shipping address page and enter your business's shipping address.
* Finally, complete the additional information page with any extra details you would like to include.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Information (Company Name, Type, Address, Contact Number, Contact Email)** (required: false): Enter your business's name, type, address, contact number, and contact email on this page. This information will help the direct mailer service identify your business.
* **Mail Details (Mail Type, Mail Date, Mail Content)** (required: false): Enter the type of mail you would like to send, the date you would like to mail, and any additional content you would like to include in the mail.
* **Shipping Address (Street, City, State, Postal Code)** (required: false): Enter your business's shipping address on this page. This will help the direct mailer service know where to send the mail.
* **Additional Information (Any extra details you'd like to include)** (required: false): Enter any extra details you would like to provide to the direct mailer service. This can be any information that is relevant to your business.
* **Contact Number** (required: false): Enter a contact number for your business. This can be an emergency contact number or a number for further communication.
* **Contact Email** (required: false): Enter a contact email for your business. This can be used for further communication and updates from the direct mailer service.
