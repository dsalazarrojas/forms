# Consortium Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consortium Management Form is used to collect information about your organization's membership in the consortium, including its role, membership start date, areas of expertise, and resource contribution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information about your organization's participation in the consortium.
2. Fill in the Organization Name field with your organization's full legal name.
3. Select the correct Organization Type from the provided options.
4. Enter the full name of your primary contact person in the Contact Person Name field.
5. Enter the email address of your primary contact person in the Contact Email field.
6. Enter your primary contact person's phone number with country code in the Contact Phone Number field.
7. Select your organization's role in the consortium from the provided options in the Role in Consortium field.
8. Enter the date your organization joined the consortium in the Membership Start Date field.
9. Select all areas where your organization contributes from the provided options in the Area of Expertise field.
10. If applicable, select the resource(s) your organization contributes in the Resource Contribution Type field (this field is optional).
11. Select the expected level of participation in consortium activities from the provided options in the Participation Level field.
12. If applicable, select your organization's meeting availability from the provided options in the Meeting Availability field (this field is optional).
13. If applicable, describe specific projects or initiatives of interest in the Project Interests field (this field is optional).
14. Select whether your organization agrees to comply with consortium policies in the Compliance Agreement field.
15. Select whether your organization consents to share data within the consortium in the Data Sharing Consent field.
16. If necessary, enter any additional comments or special requirements in the Additional Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (1): Enter the full legal name of your organization.
* **Organization Type** (2): Select the type of organization you represent (Academic Institution, Research Institute, Government Agency, Non-Profit Organization, Private Company, or International Organization).
* **Contact Person Name** (3): Full name of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Email** (4): Email address for official communications.
* **Contact Phone Number** (5): Primary phone number with country code.
* **Role in Consortium** (6): Select your organization's role in the consortium (Lead Organization, Core Partner, Associate Partner, Advisory Member, or Observer).
* **Membership Start Date** (7): Date when your organization joined the consortium.
* **Area of Expertise** (8): Select all areas where your organization contributes (Research and Development, Project Management, Data Analysis, Policy Development, Training and Education, Technical Support, Funding and Finance, or Communications).
* **Resource Contribution Type** (9): If applicable, select the resource(s) your organization contributes (Financial Support, Personnel Time, Equipment and Facilities, Data and Information, Technical Expertise, or Network Access).
* **Participation Level** (10): Expected level of participation in consortium activities (Full Participation, Partial Participation, Occasional Participation, or Remote Participation Only).
* **Meeting Availability** (11): Select your organization's meeting availability (Weekday Mornings, Weekday Afternoons, Weekday Evenings, Weekends, or Flexible Schedule).
* **Project Interests** (12): If applicable, describe specific projects or initiatives of interest.
* **Compliance Agreement** (13): Select whether your organization agrees to comply with consortium policies.
* **Data Sharing Consent** (14): Select whether your organization consents to share data within the consortium.
* **Additional Comments** (15): If necessary, enter any additional comments or special requirements.
