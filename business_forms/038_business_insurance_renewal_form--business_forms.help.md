**Business Insurance Renewal Form - Help Guide**

### Purpose

This form is designed to gather essential business details and information for insurance renewal purposes. It serves as a crucial document for updating your business insurance coverage and ensuring continuity of coverage.

### How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in your policy number and name of your insured business.
2.  Select your desired renewal term (6, 12, or 24 months).
3.  Report any changes to your business activities, operations, or assets.
4.  Provide accurate counts of total employees and assets valuation.
5.  Indicate if you have filed any claims during the current policy term.
6.  Confirm your understanding of the proposed renewal terms by signing this document.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policy Number for Renewal** (`policy_number_for_renewal`, text, required): Type the exact policy number for your current insurance coverage to enable seamless renewal.
* **Insured Business Name** (`renewal_business_name_official`, text, required): Enter the official name of your insured business as per your business registration documents.
* **Desired Renewal Term** (`renewal_period_selection_term`, select_one, required): Select the desired length of your insurance coverage term, which can be 6, 12, or 24 months.
* **Changes to Primary Business Activity** (`changes_to_business_activity_renewal`, select_one, required): Report any changes to your primary business activities, such as adding new services or products, by selecting one of the provided options.
* **Updated Total Assets Valuation (USD)** (`total_assets_valuation_update_amount`, number, required): Enter the current value of your business assets and equipment, including property and other valuables, in US dollars.
* **Current Total Employee Count** (`current_employee_count_renewal_update`, number, required): Record the total number of full-time and part-time employees in your business.
* **Recent Safety or Risk Improvements** (`recent_safety_improvements_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe any recent safety or risk improvements implemented in your business.
* **Claims Filed in Current Policy Term** (`claims_filed_current_term_selection`, select_one, required): Indicate the number of claims filed during the current policy term.
* **Authorized Representative Signature** (`renewal_auth_signature_name`, text, required): Type your full name as the authorized representative to authorize the renewal.
* **Date Signed** (`renewal_form_date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date of signing this document to confirm the renewal terms.

**Note:**

This form requires accurate and complete information for efficient insurance renewal processing. Please ensure all fields are filled correctly before submitting this form to avoid delays or rejections.
