# Competitor Analysis Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Competitor Analysis Report Form is designed to help businesses gain insights into their competitors' strategies, product launches, pricing, and marketing campaigns. This form will guide you through collecting and organizing data to create a comprehensive report that highlights your competitors' strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title and subject for your report, including a brief description.
2. Provide your name as the report author.
3. List all competitors included in the analysis.
4. Enter the start and end dates of the observation period.
5. Provide a description of recent product or feature launches from competitors.
6. Describe any observed pricing or packaging shifts from competitors.
7. Identify the most effective marketing campaigns from competitors.
8. Assess the current threat level of competitors' actions.
9. Identify opportunities for your business based on competitors' weaknesses.
10. Summarize the report in a few key takeaways and actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Title / Subject** (`report_title_subject`):
	+ Enter a title and brief description of the report, including its subject (e.g., "Q1 Competitor Landscape Analysis").
* **Prepared By** (`analyst_name_report`): 
	+ Enter your name as the report author.
* **Competitors Included in Analysis** (`competitors_included_in_report`): 
	+ List all companies or competitors included in this analysis.
* **Analysis Start Date** (`report_period_start`): 
	+ Enter the start date of the observation period.
* **Analysis End Date** (`report_period_end`): 
	+ Enter the end date of the observation period.
* **Recent Product or Feature Launches** (`recent_product_launches_obs`): 
	+ Provide a description of any new offerings from competitors.
* **Observed Pricing or Packaging Shifts** (`pricing_strategy_shifts`): 
	+ Describe any observed changes in pricing or packaging from competitors.
* **Marketing Campaign Observation** (`marketing_campaign_effectiveness`): 
	+ Identify the most effective marketing campaigns from competitors.
* **Current Threat Level** (`threat_assessment_rating`): 
	+ Assess the current threat level based on competitors' actions.
	+ Choose from options: High (Critical action needed), Moderate (Monitor closely), Low (No immediate action).
* **Identified Opportunities for Us** (`identified_opportunities_report`): 
	+ Describe where your business can gain a competitive advantage based on competitors' weaknesses.
* **Key Takeaways and Actions** (`key_takeaways_summary`): 
	+ Summarize the report in a few key takeaways and proposed actions for leadership.
* **Date of Report** (`report_submission_date_final`): 
	+ Enter the date the report was finalized.

This guide will help you effectively use the Competitor Analysis Report Form to gather and organize data on competitors' strategies, products, pricing, and marketing campaigns.
