# Change Coordinator Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from end-users on their experience with a change coordinator. It is meant to be completed by individuals who have interacted with the change coordinator and have valuable insights on their performance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date when you provided feedback.
2. Enter your full name.
3. Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
4. Choose your department.
5. Enter the name of the change initiative you were part of.
6. Enter the name of the change coordinator you are providing feedback on.
7. Select the duration of the change initiative.
8. Rate the change coordinator's communication skills, organizational abilities, and problem-solving skills.
9. Rate the change coordinator's overall effectiveness.
10. Indicate if you would work with this coordinator again.
11. Enter the strengths you perceived in the change coordinator.
12. Enter areas where the change coordinator could improve.
13. Provide any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you provided feedback, which is essential for tracking and referencing your comments.
* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Enter your full name, which is necessary for addressing your feedback.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes, which can be helpful for the change coordinator or organization.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Choose your department, which helps categorize your feedback and identify trends.
* **Change Initiative Name** (`change_initiative_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the change project you were part of, which is crucial for referencing specific initiatives.
* **Change Coordinator Name** (`coordinator_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the change coordinator you are providing feedback on, which ensures accurate targeting of feedback.
* **Project Duration** (`project_duration`, select_one, required): Choose how long the change initiative lasted, which helps in evaluating the coordinator's performance over time.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the change coordinator's communication skills, which is a critical aspect of their performance.
* **Organization Skills** (`organization_skills`, select_one, required): Rate the change coordinator's organizational abilities, which is essential for their effectiveness.
* **Stakeholder Engagement** (`stakeholder_engagement`, select_one, required): Rate the change coordinator's ability to engage stakeholders, which is vital for their success.
* **Problem Solving Ability** (`problem_solving`, select_one, required): Rate the change coordinator's problem-solving skills, which is a key aspect of their performance.
* **Responsiveness** (`responsiveness`, select_one, required): Rate how responsive the change coordinator was, which is a critical factor in their performance.
* **Change Management Knowledge** (`change_management_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate the change coordinator's expertise in change management, which is a key skill.
* **Support Provided** (`support_provided`, select_one, required): Rate how well the change coordinator supported you, which is essential for their performance.
* **Training Quality** (`training_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of training provided by the change coordinator, which is a critical aspect of their performance.
* **Resistance Management** (`resistance_management`, select_one, required): Rate how well the change coordinator handled resistance, which is a challenging aspect of their performance.
* **Overall Effectiveness** (`overall_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the change coordinator's overall effectiveness, which is a summary of their performance.
* **Would Work Again** (`would_work_again`, select_one, required): Indicate if you would work with this change coordinator again, which is a measure of their success.
* **Coordinator Strengths** (`coordinator_strengths`, text, optional): Enter the strengths you perceived in the change coordinator, which helps in identifying areas of excellence.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): Enter areas where the change coordinator could improve, which is valuable for growth and development.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have, which can be helpful for the organization.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate in your feedback.
* Provide specific examples and reasons for your ratings.
* Keep your comments clear and concise.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please address them in the "Areas for Improvement" field.
* If you have any additional feedback, please provide it in the "Additional Comments" field.
