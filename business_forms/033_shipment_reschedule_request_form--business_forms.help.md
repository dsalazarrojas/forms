# Shipment Reschedule Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shipment Reschedule Request Form is used to reschedule a shipment that has not yet been collected or delivered. This form allows you to adjust the delivery schedule of a shipment by selecting a new reschedule option and providing relevant customer information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Determine if you want to reschedule, cancel, or delay the shipment.
2.  Select the new shipment type (Reschedule, Cancel, or Delay) from the "shipment_type" field.
3.  Enter the date for the new shipment schedule in the "shipment_date" field.
4.  Enter the time for the new shipment schedule in the "shipment_time" field.
5.  Enter the customer's email address in the "customer_email" field.
6.  Enter the customer's phone number in the "customer_phone" field.
7.  Enter any additional comments or notes from the customer in the "customer_note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **shipment_type**: Choose the reason for rescheduling the shipment (Reschedule, Cancel, or Delay).
* **shipment_date**: Enter the date for the new shipment schedule (Format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **shipment_time**: Enter the time for the new shipment schedule (Format: HH:MM).
* **customer_email**: Enter the customer's email address for communication.
* **customer_phone**: Enter the customer's phone number for communication.
* **customer_note**: Enter any additional comments or notes from the customer.

## Tips
* Make sure to update the customer's contact information if it has changed.
* Double-check the new shipment schedule to ensure it is accurate.
* Ensure that the customer is notified and can respond to the new schedule.
* If the shipment is already in transit, it may not be possible to reschedule and this form may not be used.
