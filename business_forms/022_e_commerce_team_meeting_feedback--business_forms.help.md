# E Commerce Team Meeting Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from team members about their experiences with team meetings. This feedback helps the team and leadership identify areas of improvement and make necessary adjustments to ensure productive and effective meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Rate the meeting on the provided scale for:
	* Meeting Rating
	* Agenda Effectiveness
	* Collaboration
	* Meeting Outcome
	* Overall Rating
2. Indicate whether the following were present or not during the meeting:
	* Action Items
	* Meeting Topics
	* Facilities
3. Provide any comments or feedback you have about the meeting:
	* Overall Comments
	* Meeting Notes
4. If necessary, add any additional comments or suggestions:
	* Overall Comments
5. Enter any additional information:
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Team Name
	* Department
	* Job Title
	* Meeting Date
	* Meeting Start Time
	* Meeting End Time
	* Meeting Duration
	* Total Time Spent
	* Meeting Location

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Meeting Rating**: Rate the meeting on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
- **Agenda Effectiveness**: Rate how effectively the meeting's agenda was executed.
- **Collaboration**: Rate how well team members collaborated during the meeting.
- **Meeting Outcome**: Rate the outcome of the meeting.
- **Overall Rating**: Rate the overall experience of the meeting.
- **Action Items**: Check if any action items were assigned.
- **Meeting Topics**: Select the topics discussed during the meeting.
- **Facilities**: Check if any facilities were provided.
- **Overall Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the meeting.
- **Meeting Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the meeting.
- **Email**: Enter your email address.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
- **Team Name**: Enter your team name.
- **Department**: Select your department.
- **Job Title**: Select your job title.
- **Meeting Date**: Enter the date of the meeting.
- **Meeting Start Time**: Enter the start time of the meeting.
- **Meeting End Time**: Enter the end time of the meeting.
- **Meeting Duration**: Enter the total duration of the meeting.
- **Total Time Spent**: Enter the total time you spent in the meeting.
- **Meeting Location**: Enter the location of the meeting.

## Tips
- Please answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the comments section.
- If you need assistance with anything, feel free to reach out to the meeting organizer or leadership.
