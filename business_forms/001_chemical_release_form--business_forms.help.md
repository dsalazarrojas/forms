# Chemical Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about chemical releases that have occurred at a facility or business site, allowing the safety team to respond promptly and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as the reporter of the release.
2. Provide your business email address so that the safety team can contact you for follow-up questions or information.
3. Specify the name of the facility or business where the release occurred.
4. Enter the common or trade name of the chemical released.
5. Estimate the quantity released in liters or kilograms.
6. Report the date and time of the release, if known.
7. Describe the immediate actions taken to contain, evacuate, or mitigate the release.
8. Identify the affected area within the facility or surrounding environment.
9. If applicable, list the safety contacts notified about the release.
10. Provide any additional comments or relevant details about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reporter Name (1)**: Enter your full name as the person reporting the release.
* **Reporter Contact Email (2)**: Provide your business email address so the safety team can contact you for follow-up questions.
* **Facility Name (3)**: Type the name of the business or site where the release occurred.
* **Chemical Name (4)**: Enter the common or trade name of the chemical released.
* **Quantity Released (5)**: Estimate the amount released in liters or kilograms.
* **Release Date (6)**: Enter the date of the release.
* **Release Time (7)**: If known, report the time of the release.
* **Immediate Actions Taken (8)**: Describe the containment, evacuation, or mitigation measures taken.
* **Affected Area (9)**: Specify the location within the facility or surrounding area.
* **Safety Contacts Notified (10)**: List the safety contacts or departments notified about the release.
* **Additional Comments (11)**: Provide any additional details or context about the incident.
