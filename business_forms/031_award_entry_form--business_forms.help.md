# Award Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Award Entry Form is designed to collect information for an award entry submission. It is used to gather the necessary details for the award committee to evaluate your entry. The form is typically used by business organizations to submit their entries for recognition of their achievements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name of the Recipient or Entry** (`recipient_name`, `text`, required): Enter the official name of the recipient or entry.
* **Primary Contact Email** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter the primary contact email of the recipient or entry.
* **Primary Contact Phone** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter the primary contact phone number of the recipient or entry.
* **Official Award Name** (`award_name_entry`, `text`, required): Enter the official name of the award being entered.
* **Entry Category** (`entry_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the most appropriate category for the entry, such as Small Business Award, Large Corporation Award, etc.
* **Business or Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the business or organization being entered.
* **Year of Entry** (`entry_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year of the entry, which is the current award cycle.
* **Brief Description of Achievement** (`achievement_summary_short`, `text`, required): Provide a 1-2 sentence summary of the achievement.
* **Detailed Entry Narrative** (`achievement_narrative_long`, `text`, required): Provide a full explanation of why you deserve the award.
* **Link to Supporting Evidence** (`evidence_link`, `text`, optional): Enter a URL to supporting documents, videos, or portfolios.
* **Total Number of Employees** (`employee_count`, `number`, optional): Enter the total number of employees in the organization.
* **Annual Revenue Range** (`revenue_range`, `select_one`, optional): Select the annual revenue range of the organization.
* **Agreement to Publicity Rights** (`publicity_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you agree to the terms of the publicity rights.
* **Declaration of Accuracy** (`declaration_accuracy`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you declare the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Digital Signature** (`entry_signature`, `text`, required): Enter your full name to sign the entry.

Please note that the **Year of Entry** field only includes the current award cycle, and the **Total Number of Employees** and **Annual Revenue Range** fields are optional for internal classification purposes only. Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it, as the required fields must be completed accurately and truthfully.
