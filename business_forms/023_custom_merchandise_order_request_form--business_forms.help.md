# Custom Merchandise Order Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from businesses to fulfill their custom merchandise orders. Please use this form to provide details about your order, including business and shipping information, order specifics, and payment details. This will help us process your request accurately and promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your business information, including your company name and contact details.
2. Provide shipping information, including the address and contact details for where the order should be shipped to.
3. Specify the order details, including the type and quantity of merchandise needed.
4. Add any custom specifications for the merchandise, such as color or design preferences.
5. Enter payment information, including the payment method and amount due.
6. Review your order confirmation details, ensuring everything is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Custom Merchandise Request** (`custom_merch_request`, text, optional)
Enter a brief description of your custom merchandise order, including the type of product, quantity, and any special requests.

### **Business Information** (`business_info`, text, optional)
Enter your company's name and contact details, including your name, title, and contact number.

### **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, text, optional)
Enter the address where you would like the order to be shipped to, including the name, street address, city, state, and zip code.

### **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional)
Specify the type and quantity of merchandise needed, including any special requests or customizations.

### **Custom Merchandise Specifications** (`custom_merch_specs`, text, optional)
Provide any custom specifications for the merchandise, such as color, design, or material preferences.

### **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, optional)
Enter payment details, including the payment method and amount due.

### **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, text, optional)
Review your order details to ensure everything is accurate before submitting your request.

## Tips
- Please double-check your order details for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, feel free to contact us.
- We will contact you with further instructions and updates on your order status.
