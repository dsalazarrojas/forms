# fishing_industry_licensing_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect and verify information for the fishing industry licensing application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first text field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second text field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the third field, in the format **example@domain.com**.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the fourth text field, in the format **E.555.123.4567**.
5. Select your **Industry** from the dropdown list. This will help us determine which type of license you are applying for. The options are:
	* Fish Farming
	* Fishing Guides
	* Seafood Processor
6. Enter your **Business Name** in the sixth text field. This is a required field.
7. Select your **License Type** from the dropdown list. This will help us determine which type of license you are applying for. The options are:
	* Commercial Fishing
	* Recreational Fishing
	* Aquaculture
	* Shellfish
	* Commercial Food Processing
	* Aquatic Farm
	* Other
8. (Optional) If you have any additional **Notes** about your application, you can enter them in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format **example@domain.com**.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format **E.555.123.4567**.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select your industry type from the dropdown list to help us determine which type of license you are applying for.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter your business name as it appears on your business registration.
* **License Type** (`license_type`, select_multiple, required): Select your license type from the dropdown list to help us determine which type of license you are applying for.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional information about your application, you can enter it here.
