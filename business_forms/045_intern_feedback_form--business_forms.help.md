# intern_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The intern_feedback_form is a standardized form for collecting feedback on interns' performance during their work placement. This feedback is vital for the intern to evaluate their performance and grow professionally, while the supervisor evaluates the intern's capabilities and provides valuable insights. The organization also benefits from this feedback to refine its internship programs and policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the supervisor's name and email address in the "supervisor name" and "email" fields, respectively.
2. Enter the intern's name in the "name" field.
3. Provide a detailed feedback on the intern's performance in the "feedback" field.
4. Rate the intern's quality of work in the "rating" field using a number (1-10).
5. Offer suggestions for improvement in the "suggestions" field.
6. Select the submission date and time in the "submission date" and "submission time" fields.
7. Assess the intern's performance using the "intern performance" field with three options: "performing well," "performing average," or "performing poorly."
8. Evaluate the intern's communication skills, work ethic, and teamwork using the "communication skills," "work ethic," and "teamwork" fields with three options each.
9. Enter any additional feedback or comments from the supervisor in the "supervisor feedback" field.
10. Confirm whether there is additional feedback or comments in the "additional feedback" field.
11. Select the submission status from the "submission status" field with two options: "draft" and "submitted."
12. Indicate who submitted the feedback in the "submitted by" field with two options: "intern" or "supervisor."
13. Confirm whether the intern has an internship agreement in the "has internship agreement" field with two options: "True" or "False."
14. Confirm whether the submission is submitted by an intern in the "submitted by" field with two options: "intern" or "supervisor."

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **supervisor name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the supervisor who is providing the feedback.
* **email** (2, email, required): Enter the email address of the supervisor who is providing the feedback.
* **name** (3, text, required): Enter the name of the intern who is receiving the feedback.
* **feedback** (4, text, required): Provide a detailed feedback on the intern's performance during their work placement.
* **rating** (5, number, required): Rate the intern's quality of work using a number from 1 to 10.
* **suggestions** (6, text, required): Offer suggestions for the intern's improvement.
* **submission date** (7, date, required): Select the submission date of the feedback.
* **submission time** (8, time, required): Select the submission time of the feedback.
* **intern performance** (9, select_one, required): Assess the intern's performance using three options: "performing well," "performing average," or "performing poorly."
* **communication skills** (10, select_multiple, required): Evaluate the intern's communication skills using three options: "excellent," "average," or "below average."
* **work ethic** (11, select_multiple, required): Evaluate the intern's work ethic using three options: "excellent," "average," or "below average."
* **teamwork** (12, select_multiple, required): Evaluate the intern's teamwork using three options: "excellent," "average," or "below average."
* **supervisor feedback** (13, text, required): Enter any additional feedback or comments from the supervisor.
* **additional feedback** (14, select_one, required): Confirm whether there is additional feedback or comments in the "supervisor feedback" field.
* **submission comments** (15, text, required): Enter any comments or additional information about the submission.
* **submitted by** (16, select_one, required): Indicate who submitted the feedback using two options: "intern" or "supervisor."
* **has internship agreement** (17, select_one, required): Confirm whether the intern has an internship agreement using two options: "True" or "False."
* **submission status** (18, select_multiple, required): Select the submission status using two options: "draft" and "submitted."
* **Submission Date 2** (19, date, optional): Select the submission date of the feedback if required.
* **Submission Time 2** (20, time, optional): Select the submission time of the feedback if required.
