# Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conference Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from attendees for a seamless conference experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Company Name in the respective fields.
2. Select your Industry from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose your Registration Category from the dropdown menu.
4. Select all the days you plan to attend from the dropdown menu.
5. Select any sessions that interest you from the dropdown menu.
6. Choose your meal selection from the dropdown menu.
7. If you have any special dietary requirements, type them in the text field.
8. Indicate if you need hotel booking, and select your preferred room type.
9. Select if you need transportation and choose the type of transfer required.
10. Choose your networking event preference.
11. Indicate if you need a certificate of attendance.
12. Specify any accessibility requirements you have.
13. Select if you want to receive marketing updates.
14. Choose your payment method.
15. If you have a promotional code, type it in the text field.
15. Finally, agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your family name.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company Name** (5, text, required): Enter your organization's name.
* **Job Title** (6, text, required): Enter your position at your organization.
* **Industry** (7, select_one, required): Select your industry from the list.
* **Registration Category** (8, select_one, required): Choose your registration category from the list.
* **Days Attending** (9, select_multiple, required): Select all the days you plan to attend.
* **Session Preferences** (10, select_multiple, required): Select any sessions that interest you.
* **Meal Selection** (11, select_one, required): Choose your meal selection from the list.
* **Special Dietary Requirements** (12, text, false): Specify any dietary requirements you have.
* **Hotel Booking Required** (13, select_one, required): Indicate if you need hotel booking.
* **Hotel Preference** (14, select_one, false): Select your preferred room type.
* **Transportation Needed** (15, select_one, required): Select if you need transportation.
* **Networking Events** (16, select_one, required): Choose your networking event preference.
* **Certificate of Attendance** (17, select_one, required): Indicate if you need a certificate.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (18, text, false): Specify any accessibility requirements you have.
* **Marketing Opt-in** (19, select_one, required): Select if you want to receive marketing updates.
* **Payment Method** (20, select_one, required): Choose your payment method.
* **Promotional Code** (21, text, false): If you have a promotional code, enter it here.
* **I Accept Terms and Conditions** (22, select_one, required): Agree to the terms and conditions.
