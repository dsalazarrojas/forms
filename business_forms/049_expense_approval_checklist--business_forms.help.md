# Expense Approval Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Approval Checklist is a form used to review and approve expenses submitted by employees. It helps to ensure that all expenses are properly accounted for and approved by authorized personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the expense date on which the expense was incurred.
2. Enter the amount of the expense.
3. Select the expense category (e.g., business trip, office supplies, etc.).
4. Provide a brief description of the expense.
5. Indicate whether the expense was submitted by an employee (yes/no).
6. Select the individual or department responsible for approving this expense (yes/no).
7. Choose the current status of the expense (Active/Inactive).
8. (Optional) Enter any additional comments about the expense.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Expense Date (Page 1)
* **Expense Date** (`expense_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which the expense was incurred.

### Expense Amount (Page 2)
* **Expense Amount** (`expense_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the total amount of the expense.

### Expense Category (Page 3)
* **Expense Category** (`expense_category`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the following options to categorize the expense.
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Expense Description (Page 4)
* **Expense Description** (`expense_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the expense.

### Expense Submitted By (Page 5)
* **Expense Submitted By** (`expense_submitted_by`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether the expense was submitted by an employee (yes/no).

### Expense Approver (Page 6)
* **Expense Approver** (`expense_approver`, select_one, required/optional): Select the individual or department responsible for approving this expense (yes/no).

### Expense Status (Page 7)
* **Expense Status** (`expense_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the current status of the expense.
	+ Active
	+ Inactive

### Expense Comment (Page 8)
* **Expense Comment** (`expense_comment`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments about the expense.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please refer to your company's expense policies or consult with your supervisor.
