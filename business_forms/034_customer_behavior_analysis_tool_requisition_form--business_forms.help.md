# customer_behavior_analysis_tool_requisition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Behavior Analysis Tool requisition form is used to collect information from customers to create a custom analysis report for their business.

## How To Complete This Form
- To start, fill in the form by following these steps:
    - Enter your name and email in the required fields.
    - Provide a brief description of why you are interested in using the Customer Behavior Analysis Tool.
    - Enter the name of the tool you would like to use.
- Click on the "Submit" button to send the form to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **customer_behavior_form**: A text field where you can describe your business and the specific issues you would like to analyze.
- **tool_name**: Enter the name of the tool you are interested in using.
- **description**: Provide a brief description of why you would like to use the tool.
- **customer_name**: Enter your name, as we will use it to address our report to you.
- **customer_email**: Enter your valid email address, as we will use it to send you the analysis report.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information in the form to ensure that we can create an effective analysis report.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
