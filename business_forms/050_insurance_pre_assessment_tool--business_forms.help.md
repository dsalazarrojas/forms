# insurance_pre_assessment_tool - Help Guide
## Purpose
The insurance_pre_assessment_tool is a form designed to help users assess their insurance needs and provide relevant information to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and fill out all required fields.
2. For multiple-choice fields, select the relevant option that best describes your situation.
3. For date fields, enter dates in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. For text fields, enter your information accurately and clearly.
5. For note fields, enter any additional information that may be helpful for the insurance provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input (page 1)
- **User Input** (`User Input`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all that apply regarding your insurance needs.

  * Yes: You need some form of insurance.
  * No: You do not need any insurance.

### Insurance Type (page 1)
- **Insurance Type** (`Insurance Type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of insurance you are interested in, such as health, life, or property insurance.

### Date of Birth (page 1)
- **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Age (page 1)
- **Age** (`Age`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your age in years.

### Contact Info (page 1)
- **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact information, such as phone number or address.

### Email (page 1)
- **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.

### Note (page 1)
- **Note** (`Note`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional information that may be helpful for the insurance provider.

### Insurance Provider (page 1)
- **Insurance Provider** (`Insurance Provider`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the insurance provider you are interested in, such as Blue Cross or Red Shield.

### Insurance Deductible (page 1)
- **Insurance Deductible** (`Insurance Deductible`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the deductible amount for your insurance policy.

### Insurance Coverage (page 1)
- **Insurance Coverage** (`Insurance Coverage`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all that apply regarding your insurance coverage needs.

  * Yes: You need some form of insurance coverage.
  * No: You do not need any insurance coverage.

### Coverage Type (page 1)

### Premium (page 1)

### End Date (page 2)
