# Pet Product Recall Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to support pet product recall requests, providing essential information about a pet product recall.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out all required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2.  Select the correct options from drop-down menus as needed.
3.  Double-check your submission for completeness and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Pet Brand** (`pet_brand`): Enter the brand of the pet product involved in the recall.
*   **Product Type** (`product_type`): Choose the type of pet product, such as food, treat, or toy.
*   **Product Recall Date** (`product_date`): Select the date when the product was recalled.
*   **Product ID** (`product_id`): If available, provide the unique ID number for the recalled product.
*   **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your name or the name of the person responsible for the recall.
*   **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`): Provide your email address or the email address of the person responsible for the recall.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the person responsible for the recall.
*   **Product Description** (`product_description`): Briefly describe the product in question.
*   **Product Image** (`product_image`): If available, upload an image of the recalled product.
*   **Recall Details** (`recall_details`): Provide a detailed description of the reasons for the recall.
*   **Veterinarian Name** (`vet_name`): If applicable, enter the name of the veterinarian involved.
*   **Veterinarian Clinic** (`vet_clinic`): Enter the name of the clinic where the recall was discovered.
*   **Pet Type** (`pet_type`): Choose the type of pet affected by the recall, such as cat or dog.
*   **Pet Age** (`pet_age`): Enter the age of the pet affected by the recall.
*   **Recall Date** (`recall_date`): Select the date when the recall was discovered.
*   **Submitter** (`submitter`): Enter your name or the name of the person submitting the recall.
*   **Submitter Organization** (`submitter_organization`): Enter your organization or the name of the submitting organization.
*   **Submitter Position** (`submitter_position`): Enter your position or the position of the submitting person.
*   **Submitter Contact E-mail** (`submitter_contact_email`): Enter your email address or the email address of the submitting person.
*   **Submitter Contact Phone** (`submitter_contact_phone`): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the submitting person.
*   **Submitter Note** (`submitter_note`): Add any additional comments or information from the submitter.
*   **Product Note** (`product_note`): Enter any additional comments or information about the product.
*   **Veterinarian Note** (`vet_note`): Add any comments or information from the veterinarian involved.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Add any further comments or information.
*   **Review Status** (`review_status`): Select the current status of the review, such as Not Reviewed, Under Review, Approved, Rejected, or Closed.
