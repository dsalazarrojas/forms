# Investigation Findings Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the outcome of an investigation, including the findings, actions taken, and subsequent reviews. It helps to track the progress and status of investigations, ensuring that all relevant details are captured and communicated effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Investigation Outcome**: Select the outcome of the investigation, which should be either 'Yes', 'No', or leave this field blank if not applicable.
2. **Attach Evidence**: Provide any supporting evidence or documentation relevant to the investigation.
3. **Actions Taken**: Describe the actions taken during the investigation, including any steps taken to address the issue.
4. **Actions Outcome**: Select the outcome of the actions taken, which should be either 'Yes', 'No', or leave this field blank if not applicable.
5. **Risk Level**: Enter a numerical value to indicate the level of risk associated with the investigation.
6. **Risk Description**: Provide a brief description of the risk identified during the investigation.
7. **Root Cause**: Identify the root cause of the issue investigated.
8. **Recommendation**: Provide any recommendations for future actions or improvements.
9. **Investigator**: Enter the name of the investigator who conducted the investigation.
10. **Investigation Date**: Select the date the investigation was conducted.
11. **Investigator Email**: Enter the email address of the investigator.
12. **Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of the reviewer who reviewed the investigation.
13. **Review Date**: Select the date the review was conducted.
14. **Review Status**: Select the status of the review, which can be either 'Approved', 'Disapproved', or leave this field blank if not applicable.
15. **Review Comments**: Provide any comments from the reviewer.
16. **Approval Note**: Enter any approval notes or comments.
17. **Approval Date**: Select the date the approval was given.
18. **Approver**: Enter the name of the approver who approved the investigation.
19. **Investigator Phone**: Enter the phone number of the investigator.
20. **Risk Level Description**: Provide a brief description of the risk level.
21. **Actions Outcome Description**: Provide a description of the actions taken and their outcome.
22. **Risk Level Rating**: Enter a numerical rating to further describe the risk level.
23. **Review Status Description**: Provide a description of the review status.
24. **Investigation Status**: Select the status of the investigation, which can be either 'Complete', 'In Progress', or 'In Progress - Completed'.
25. **Investigator Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the investigator.

### Tips
* Be sure to provide accurate and clear information in each field to ensure the investigation findings are well-documented and communicated effectively.
* Review all fields carefully to ensure completeness and accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the relevant section in this guide for guidance.
