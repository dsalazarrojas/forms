# Expense Report Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Expense Report Verification Form is a tool for verifying the accuracy and completeness of an expense report submitted by an employee. This form helps approvers confirm or reject the report and provide comments for further action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the employee's ID number in the field "Employee ID".
* Enter the date the report was submitted in the field "Date Submitted".
* Select the verification status from the dropdown menu next to "Verification Status":
	+ "Verified" if the report is accurate and complete.
	+ "Not Verified" if the report contains errors or missing information.
	+ "Needs Review" if the report requires further review or clarification.
* Enter the employee's name in the field "Employee Name".
* Enter the claim amount in the field "Claim Amount".
* Enter any comments or feedback in the field "Verification Comments".
* Enter the name of the person submitting the report in the field "Submitter".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (1): Enter the employee's ID number as listed in our database.
* **Date Submitted** (2): Enter the date the employee submitted the report.
* **Verification Status** (3): Select one of the three options to indicate the report's status.
* **Employee Name** (4): Enter the full name of the employee who submitted the report.
* **Claim Amount** (5): Enter the amount claimed by the employee.
* **Verification Comments** (6): Enter any comments or feedback for the employee.
* **Submitter** (7): Enter the name of the person submitting this report (usually the approver).
