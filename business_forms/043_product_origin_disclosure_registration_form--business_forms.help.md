# Product Origin Disclosure Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Origin Disclosure Registration Form is a mandatory form that businesses must fill out to register their products and disclose their origin. This form collects information about the product's name, contact person, business details, and origin, including country, state, zip code, city, and date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and truthful information about your product and business.
2. Ensure that you select the correct country of origin and state of origin for your product.
3. Enter the zip code and city of origin for your product.
4. Select the type of product origin (e.g., Organic, Non-GMO, or Other).
5. Enter the certification number and date of certification for your product.
6. Provide the name and address of the business that is producing or selling the product.
7. Enter the business's email and phone number.
8. Select the type of business (e.g., Corporation, Partnership, or Other).
9. Indicate the current certification status of the product (e.g., Active, Expired, or Pending).
10. Enter the expiration date of the certification.
11. Finally, select the "Save and Submit" option to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product being registered.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the product.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person or business.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person or business.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the business.
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of the business producing or selling the product.
* **Product Origin Country**: Select the country where the product was manufactured or produced.
* **Product Origin State**: Select the state where the product was manufactured or produced.
* **Product Origin Zip**: Enter the zip code where the product was manufactured or produced.
* **Product Origin City**: Enter the city where the product was manufactured or produced.
* **Product Origin Date**: Enter the date when the product was manufactured or produced.
* **Product Origin Time**: Enter the time when the product was manufactured or produced.
* **Product Description**: Enter a brief description of the product.
* **Certification Number**: Enter the number of the certification for the product.
* **Certifying Body**: Enter the name of the entity that certified the product.
* **Certification Date**: Enter the date of the certification for the product.
* **Business Email**: Enter the email address of the business.
* **Business Phone**: Enter the phone number of the business.
* **Business Address**: Enter the address of the business.
* **Product Origin Type**: Select the type of product origin (e.g., Organic, Non-GMO, or Other).
* **Country of Origin**: Enter the country of origin for the product.
* **Business Type**: Select the type of business (e.g., Corporation, Partnership, or Other).
* **Certification Status**: Indicate the current certification status of the product (e.g., Active, Expired, or Pending).
* **Expiration Date**: Enter the date of the certification expiration.
* **Submit**: Select "Save and Submit" to complete the form.
