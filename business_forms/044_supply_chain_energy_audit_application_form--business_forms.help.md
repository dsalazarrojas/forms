# Supply Chain Energy Audit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supply Chain Energy Audit Application Form is designed to gather information from business units within the company about their energy consumption patterns, audit locations, and energy types.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are filling out this form for a legitimate reason and have necessary approvals.
2. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). These include `Audit Location`, `Audit Dates`, `Energy Types`, `Email`, and `Phone`.
3. Select the correct `Audit Location` from the dropdown menu.
4. Choose all relevant `Audit Dates` and `Energy Types`.
5. Enter a valid `Email` address.
6. Enter a valid `Phone` number.
7. Optionally, add any additional `Notes` about your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Info (Page 1)
- **User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional): This field is intended for the business unit to provide a brief description of who is filling out the form.

### Contact Info (Page 1)
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This field is meant for the business unit to provide contact details of the person filling out the form.

### Audit Location (Page 2)
- **Audit Location** (`audit_location`, select_one, required\*): This field allows you to select the physical location where the audit will take place. Please choose one of the listed locations: New York, Chicago, or Los Angeles.

### Audit Dates (Page 2)
- **Audit Dates** (`audit_dates`, select_multiple, required\*): This field asks you to select multiple dates for the audit. Choose all dates that apply: Yes or No.

### Energy Types (Page 3)
- **Energy Types** (`energy_types`, select_multiple, required\*): This field asks you to select multiple energy types. Choose all that apply: Electricity, Gas, or Water.

### Notes (Page 3)
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): This is a text area for you to add any additional information about your submission.

### Email (Page 4)
- **Email** (`email`, email, required\*): This field is where you enter a valid email address associated with your submission.

### Phone (Page 4)
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required\*): This field is where you enter a valid phone number associated with your submission.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately, and the form is submitted with the correct information for the intended purpose.
