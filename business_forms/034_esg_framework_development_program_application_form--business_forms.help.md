# ESG Framework Development Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants for the ESG Framework Development Program. Please fill it out with your relevant details to support the review process.
## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, fill out the required fields: Your First and Last Name, Email, and Company Name. This information is essential for us to contact and verify your application.
2. Enter your phone number in the Phone field, but note that this field is not required.
3. If you have a company address, provide it in the Address 1 field. This is optional.
4. For international addresses, you can provide the Address 2 field for additional details.
5. Enter your postal code (zip or postal code) in the Postal Code field, but note that this field is not required.
6. For the Select one option field, choose between 'True' and 'False' to answer the question.
7. In the Select multiple options field, select multiple options from the available choices (True, False). You can choose multiple options by holding the shift key while selecting.
8. If applicable, provide a Date of availability or a specific date you would like to be contacted.
9. Enter your preferred Contact Time in the Contact Time field.
10. Finally, provide any additional Note or comments you would like to share with the review committee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (form_1, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **What is your last name?** (form_2, text, required): Enter your family name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (form_3, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (form_4, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any additional contact purposes.
* **Company Name** (form_5, text, required): Enter your company name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Address 1** (form_6, text, optional): Enter your company address for international applications or additional details.
* **Address 2** (form_7, text, optional): Provide any additional address details for international applications.
* **Postal Code** (form_8, text, optional): Enter your postal code (zip or postal code) for address validation.
* **Select one option** (form_9, select_one, required): Choose between 'True' or 'False' for this option.
* **Select multiple options** (form_10, select_multiple, required): Select multiple options between 'True' and 'False' for multiple answers.
* **Date** (form_11, date, optional): Provide a specific date if you have a date availability restriction or a specific date you'd like to be contacted.
* **Contact Time** (form_12, time, optional): Enter your preferred contact time.
* **Note** (form_13, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or explanations you'd like to share with the review committee.
