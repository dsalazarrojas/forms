> # Courier Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to process reimbursements for couriers related to a particular business process. It will be used to track and manage courier's expenses for time spent, gas cost, additional purchases, and other associated costs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Time Spent field with the total time you spent on the business process.
2. Enter the Gas Cost field with the total cost of gas incurred during the process.
3. If applicable, select the Additional Purchases field to indicate any other expenses you incurred during the process.
4. Enter the Total Reimbursement field with the total reimbursement amount you are requesting.
5. In the Notes field, provide any additional information you would like to include.
6. Enter your Email field with your email address for the company to contact you.
7. If you have an Optional phone number, please enter it in the Phone field.
8. In the Assigned Tool field, select the tool assigned to you by the business process.
9. Click submit to submit your reimbursement request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, number, required/optional): Enter the total time you spent on the business process.
* **Gas Cost** (`gas_cost`, number, required/optional): Enter the total cost of gas you incurred during the process.
* **Additional Purchases** (`additional_purchases`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any additional purchases or expenses you made during the process. Options include Parking, Tolls, and Food.
* **Total Reimbursement** (`total_reimbursement`, number, required/optional): Enter the total reimbursement amount you are requesting.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional information you would like to include.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for the company to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your optional phone number for the company to contact you.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required/optional): Select the tool assigned to you by the business process. Options include chatjimmy and admin jimmy.
