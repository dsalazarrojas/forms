# Treasury Compliance Monitoring Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Compliance Monitoring Registration Form is designed to assist entities in registering for Treasury Compliance Monitoring services. The form collects essential information about the entity, including their type, contact person, and contact email. This information is used to ensure that regulatory requirements are met and to facilitate effective monitoring activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Treasury Compliance Monitoring Registration Form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a legitimate reason for completing the form. This form is intended for entities that have a genuine need for the information collected.
2. Select the entity type from the dropdown menu that accurately represents your entity's type.
3. Enter your company name and the name of the contact person responsible for the registration.
4. Enter your contact email address and phone number.
5. Provide a brief description of your entity and the purpose of your registration.
6. Choose the regulatory body and address that applies to your entity.
7. Specify the frequency of your monitoring activities.
8. Select the regulatory compliance area that applies to your entity.
9. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Entity Type
* **Entity Type** (`entity_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of entity you are registering for (e.g., corporation, individual, partnership).

### Company Name
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your company.

### Contact Person
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person responsible for the registration.

### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person.

### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.

### Entity Description
* **Entity Description** (`entity_description`, `note`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your entity and the purpose of your registration.

### Date
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date for which you are registering for.

### Time
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time for which you are registering for.

### Regulatory Body
* **Regulatory Body** (`regulatory_body`, `text`, required: false): Select the regulatory body that applies to your entity.

### Monitoring Frequency
* **Monitoring Frequency** (`monitoring_frequency`, `text`, required: false): Specify the frequency of your monitoring activities.

### Regulatory Compliance Area
* **Regulatory Compliance Area** (`regulatory_compliance_area`, `text`, required: false): Select the regulatory compliance area that applies to your entity.

### Regulatory Body Address
* **Regulatory Body Address** (`regulatory_body_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the regulatory body.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Review and submit the form for review and approval.

### Second Submit
* **Second Submit** (`submit_2`, `text`, required: false): This field is not used in the current form and can be ignored.

Note: Some fields are not required for the form to be submitted, but they are essential for the purpose of the form.
