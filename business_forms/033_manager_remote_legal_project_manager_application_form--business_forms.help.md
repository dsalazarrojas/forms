# Manager Remote Legal Project Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from potential applicants for a Manager Remote Legal Project Manager position. The form aims to gather relevant data to assess their qualifications, experience, and availability. Please note that this form is not intended to be a comprehensive evaluation tool, but rather a starting point for further discussion and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your email address in the email field. Ensure it is a valid email address that you regularly check.
3. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
4. Describe your work experience in the work experience field.
5. Provide details about your education in the education field.
6. Enter your job title and a brief description of it in the job title and job title description fields, respectively.
7. Select your available work schedule (Monday to Friday) in the work schedule field.
8. Choose your desired work schedule (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Full Day, or Part Time) in the desired schedule field.
9. Select the type of job you are looking to take on (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Freelance, or Intern) in the job type field.
10. Indicate your availability (Yes or No) in the availability field.
11. List any languages you are proficient in in the languages field.
12. Mention any relevant skills you have in the skills field.
13. Provide references or any notable work in the references field.
14. Share your LinkedIn profile link (if applicable) in the linkedin field.
15. Share your GitHub profile link (if applicable) in the github field.
16. Attach a resume in the resume field (if applicable).
17. Share your portfolio (if applicable) in the portfolio field.
18. Specify your location (city, state, or country) in the location field.
19. Set a notice period for your employment contract in the notice period field.
20. Choose your current employment status (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Freelance, or Intern) in the employment status field.
21. Enter the date you are available to start work in the job start date field.
22. Enter the date you would like to end your current job in the job end date field.
23. If applicable, submit a reference who will vouch for you in the submitted by field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Please fill in your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Please fill in your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please provide a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required: false): Describe your previous work experience.
* **Education** (`education`, text, required: false): Provide details about your educational background.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Job Title Description** (`job_title_description`, text, required: false): Describe your job title in a few words.
* **Work Schedule** (`work_schedule`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the days of the week you are available to work (Monday to Friday).
* **Desired Schedule** (`desired_schedule`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your preferred schedule (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Full Day, or Part Time).
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of job you are looking for (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Freelance, or Intern).
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether you are available to take on a new role (Yes or No).
* **Languages** (`languages`, text, required: false): List languages you are proficient in.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, required: false): Mention your relevant skills.
* **References** (`references`, text, required: false): Provide references or notable work.
* **LinkedIn** (`linkedin`, text, required: false): Share your LinkedIn profile link.
* **GitHub** (`github`, text, required: false): Share your GitHub profile link.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required: false): Attach a resume.
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, text, required: false): Share your portfolio.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Specify your location.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, date, required: false): Set a notice period for your employment contract.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your current employment status (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Freelance, or Intern).
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you are available to start work.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to end your current job.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required: false): If applicable, provide a reference who will vouch for you.

Please note that this form is not a substitute for a thorough background check or evaluation process.
