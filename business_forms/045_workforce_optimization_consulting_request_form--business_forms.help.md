# Workforce Optimization Consulting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for workforce optimization consulting services. It is meant to be filled out by employees who have a project that requires specific guidance and support from the consulting team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the space provided, providing as much detail as possible.
2. If you have already filled out this form for the same project, do not submit another request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Inquiry** (`project_inquiry`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your project, including its objectives, goals, and any relevant background information.
* **Workforce Challenges** (`workforce_challenges`, note, optional): Describe any specific workforce challenges you are facing or anticipate facing during the project, such as staffing shortfalls, skill gaps, or cultural difficulties.
* **Project Schedule** (`project_schedule`, date, optional): If applicable, provide the expected start and end dates of your project.
* **Consulting Request** (`consulting_request`, select one, optional): Check 'Yes' if you would like to receive workforce optimization consulting services for your project. This includes guidance on project planning, resource allocation, and other support areas as needed.
