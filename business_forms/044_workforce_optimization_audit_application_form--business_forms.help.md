# Workforce Optimization Audit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Optimization Audit Application Form is a data collection tool used to gather information from employees to inform decisions regarding work schedule optimization and efficiency.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the instructions and purpose of the form to understand its significance.
2. Fill out each field according to the provided explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page**: This is the introductory page where you can provide any additional comments or remarks that are relevant to your audit request.
* **Audit Request**: Describe the reasons why you are requesting the audit. This can be a brief statement of your needs or concerns regarding your work schedule.
* **Key Details**: Please enter your employee ID or any other relevant identification number associated with your role.
* **Audit Dates**: Select the dates you would like to schedule the audit. This will help us to plan and coordinate the audit more efficiently.
* **Schedule Preferences**: Choose your preferred schedule for the audit. You can select from the following options: Monday to Friday, Monday to Friday 9-5, or Flexible.
* **Organization**: If you are requesting the audit on behalf of your organization, please enter your organization's name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position within the organization.
* **Role**: Briefly describe your role within the organization.
* **Team Leader**: If you have a team leader who needs to be informed about the audit, please enter their name.

## Tips
* Ensure you are providing accurate and relevant information in each field.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact your HR department for assistance.
* Please note that the information collected through this form will be treated confidentially and used to inform future decisions regarding work schedule optimization.
