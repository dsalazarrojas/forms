# Customer Experience Transformation Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Experience Transformation Contact Form is designed to gather feedback and information from customers about their transformation process. This form helps us understand your needs and preferences, allowing us to improve our services and products.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the first field, if you have one.
2. Enter your last name in the second field.
3. Provide your email address in the third field. Make sure it's a valid email address.
4. Enter your company name in the fourth field, if you have one.
5. Enter your phone number in the fifth field. This can be an office or personal number.
6. Describe the subject of your feedback or request in the sixth field.
7. In the seventh field, select all the goals you want to achieve through our transformation services. Please select as many or as few as needed.
8. Choose your preferred contact method in the eighth field. You can choose to be contacted via Email, Phone, or Mail.
9. If desired, select a date and time for our transformation process. Please note that these options will be displayed as text, so please format them according to MM/DD/YY and HH:MM AM/PM.
10. Finally, select a time slot for our transformation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (Text, optional): Please enter your first name, if you have one.
* **last_name** (Text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **email** (Email, required): Provide a valid email address so we can contact you.
* **company** (Text, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter the company name.
* **phone** (Text, optional): Enter your phone number. This can be an office or personal number.
* **subject** (Text, optional): Describe the subject of your feedback or request.
* **goals** (Multiple Select, optional): Select all the goals you want to achieve through our transformation services. Please select as many or as few as needed.
* **preferred_contact** (Select One, optional): Choose your preferred contact method: Email, Phone, or Mail.
* **date** (Date, optional): Select a date for our transformation process. Please format the date as MM/DD/YY.
* **time** (Time, optional): Select a time slot for our transformation process. Please format the time as HH:MM AM/PM. 

## Tips
- Make sure to provide a valid email address to ensure we can contact you.
- Select all relevant goals to help us tailor our services to your needs.
- Choose a contact method that suits your preferences.
- Be specific with your subject and goals to help us understand your feedback or request accurately.
