# Asset Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Asset Survey form is designed to collect data on the current state of assets within a specific location or facility. This information will help asset managers and administrators make informed decisions about asset allocation, maintenance, and disposal.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the correct **Survey Date** (1) and enter the date of the survey.
2. Enter the **Surveyor Name** (2) of the person conducting the survey.
3. Enter the **Surveyor ID** (3) number of the person conducting the survey.
4. Enter the **Location Name** (4) of the facility or site being surveyed.
5. Enter the full **Location Address** (5) of the facility or site.
6. Select the **Department Surveyed** (6) department or area being surveyed.
7. Choose the **Survey Type** (7) that best describes the purpose of the survey.
8. Select the **Asset Category Surveyed** (8) that best matches the type of assets being surveyed.
9. Enter the **Total Assets Found** (9) number of assets identified during the survey.
10. Enter the number of **Assets In Use** (10) at the location.
11. Enter the number of **Assets Idle or Unused** (11) at the location.
12. Enter the number of **Assets Damaged** (12) at the location.
13. Enter the number of **Assets Missing** (13) at the location.
14. Enter the **Utilization Rate Percentage** (14) of the assets at the location.
15. Enter the number of **Assets in Excellent Condition** (15) at the location.
16. Enter the number of **Assets in Good Condition** (16) at the location.
17. Enter the number of **Assets in Fair Condition** (17) at the location.
18. Enter the number of **Assets in Poor Condition** (18) at the location.
19. Enter the number of **Assets Needing Maintenance** (19) at the location.
20. Enter the number of **Assets Recommended for Replacement** (20) at the location.
21. Enter the number of **Assets Recommended for Disposal** (21) at the location.
22. Indicate if there are any **Security Concerns** (22) at the location, and if so, select 'Yes'.
23. If there are **Security Concerns**, enter the **Security Details** (23) in the provided text field.
24. Enter any **Survey Recommendations** (24) for the location or assets.
25. If a follow-up visit is required, select **Yes** (25) and enter the **Follow-up Date** (26).
26. Enter the **Site Contact Name** (27) of the person responsible for the location.
27. Enter the **Site Contact Signature** (28) of the person responsible for the location.
28. Enter your **Surveyor Signature** (29) to confirm completion of the survey.
29. Select **Yes** (30) to confirm that the survey is complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Date (1)**: Date of the survey. Enter the date you conducted the survey.
* **Surveyor Name (2)**: Name of the person conducting the survey.
* **Surveyor ID (3)**: Employee number or ID of the person conducting the survey.
* **Location Name (4)**: Name of the facility or site being surveyed.
* **Location Address (5)**: Full address of the facility or site.
* **Department Surveyed (6)**: Select the department or area being surveyed.
* **Survey Type (7)**: Choose the type of survey that best describes the purpose of the survey.
* **Asset Category Surveyed (8)**: Select the type of assets being surveyed.
* **Total Assets Found (9)**: Number of assets identified during the survey.
* **Assets In Use (10)**: Number of assets currently utilized.
* **Assets Idle or Unused (11)**: Number of assets not in use.
* **Assets Damaged (12)**: Number of assets that need repair.
* **Assets Missing (13)**: Number of assets that cannot be located.
* **Utilization Rate Percentage (14)**: Percentage of assets in use.
* **Assets in Excellent Condition (15)**: Number of assets in excellent condition.
* **Assets in Good Condition (16)**: Number of assets in good condition.
* **Assets in Fair Condition (17)**: Number of assets in fair condition.
* **Assets in Poor Condition (18)**: Number of assets in poor condition.
* **Assets Needing Maintenance (19)**: Number of assets that need maintenance.
* **Assets Recommended for Replacement (20)**: Number of assets recommended for replacement.
* **Assets Recommended for Disposal (21)**: Number of assets recommended for disposal.
* **Security Concerns (22)**: Indicate if there are any security concerns at the location.
* **Security Details (23)**: Enter any security concerns or details.
* **Survey Recommendations (24)**: Enter any recommended actions or suggestions.
* **Is Follow-up Required (25)**: Indicate if a follow-up visit is required.
* **Follow-up Date (26)**: Enter the date for the follow-up visit, if required.
* **Site Contact Name (27)**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the location.
* **Site Contact Signature (28)**: Enter the signature of the person responsible for the location.
* **Surveyor Signature (29)**: Enter your signature to confirm completion of the survey.
* **Is Survey Complete (30)**: Confirm that the survey is complete.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* If unsure about any field, consult with a team member for clarification.
* Do not duplicate surveys for the same location or facility.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Review and proofread the form carefully before submitting it.
