# Inbound Drop Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inbound Drop Record Form is used to document and track inbound deliveries, ensuring accurate records and efficient delivery management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the delivery before starting.
2. Select the relevant options for each field as required.
3. Fill in the fields accurately and completely.
4. Review the form before submission to prevent errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the delivery.
* **Delivery Slip Number** (`delivery_slip_number`, text, required: false): Record the slip number associated with the delivery.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the customer who received the delivery.
* **Customer Contact Number** (`customer_contact_number`, text, required: false): Record the contact number of the customer.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant details about the delivery, such as the contents or any notable issues.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address where the delivery was received.
* **Delivery Notes** (`delivery_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the delivery.
* **Delivery Slip Attachment** (`delivery_slip_attachment`, text, required: false): Attach any related documents or information.
* **Delivery Status** (`delivery_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the current status of the delivery from the options (Active, Inactive).
* **Delivery Status Label** (`delivery_status_label`, text, required: false): Provide a descriptive label for the selected delivery status.
* **Warehouse Name** (`warehouse_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the warehouse where the delivery was received.
* **Warehouse Name Label** (`warehouse_name_label`, text, required: false): Provide a descriptive label for the warehouse name.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, required: false): Record the signature of the customer.
* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the driver who made the delivery.

Note: This form is designed to be flexible and accommodating, allowing you to add or modify fields as needed.
