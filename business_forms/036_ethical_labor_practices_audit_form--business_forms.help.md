# Ethical Labor Practices Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Ethical Labor Practices Audit Form is designed to collect information about your organization's labor practices, policies, and procedures. This form helps us identify areas of improvement and ensure that your organization is meeting its ethical and legal obligations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your organization's name**: Fill out the organization name field to identify the company or organization being audited.
2. **Enter your contact information**: Provide your name, email, and phone number so we can contact you for any further questions or clarifications.
3. **Describe your labor policies**: In this field, please provide a brief description of your organization's labor policies, including any relevant procedures or guidelines.
4. **Describe your labor procedures**: Describe the procedures your organization follows to address labor-related issues.
5. **List any relevant labor laws**: Provide a list of any relevant labor laws or regulations your organization is subject to.
6. **Grievance procedures**: Describe your organization's procedures for handling grievances or complaints related to labor practices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name**: <organization_name> (text, required)
	+ This field is required to identify the company or organization being audited.
* **Contact Person**: <contact_person> (text, optional)
	+ This field is optional but provides a way to get in touch with the person filling out the form.
* **Contact Email**: <contact_email> (email, optional)
	+ This field is optional but provides a way for us to contact the person filling out the form.
* **Contact Phone**: <contact_phone> (text, optional)
	+ This field is optional but provides a way for us to contact the person filling out the form.
* **Labor Policies**: <labor_policies> (text, optional)
	+ This field is optional and should describe your organization's policies related to labor practices.
* **Training Procedures**: <training_procedures> (text, optional)
	+ This field is optional and should describe the procedures your organization follows for training employees on labor-related issues.
* **Labor Laws**: <labor_laws> (text, optional)
	+ This field is optional and should list any relevant labor laws or regulations your organization is subject to.
* **Grievance**: <grievance> (text, optional)
	+ This field is optional and should describe your organization's procedures for handling grievances or complaints related to labor practices.

## Tips

* Please be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact us for clarification.
* This form is a confidential document and will be used for internal purposes only.
