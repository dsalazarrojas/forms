# New Client Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a new client record. Fill out the form with the client's personal details to store their information for future reference and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with the required and optional information provided.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the client's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the client's last name.
* **Email**: Enter the client's valid email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the client's phone number.

## Tips
- Double-check that the information provided is accurate.
- Use the email address to send important updates and notifications.
- Phone number can be used for scheduling appointments or sending reminders.
