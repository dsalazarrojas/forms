# patent_application_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patent Application Authorization Form is used to authorize the representation of a patent application. This form is essential for the inventor, agent, law firm, or university to formally acknowledge and confirm their involvement in the patent application process. It helps to establish a clear understanding of their role and responsibilities in the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Representative Name** field, which should match the name of the person authorizing the application.
2. Provide a valid email address in the **Contact Email** field, which will be used for communication purposes.
3. Optionally, enter a valid phone number in the **Contact Phone** field, which will help us contact you for additional information or clarification.
4. Select your role as **Representative Type** (Inventor, Agent, Law Firm, University, or Other) to indicate your relationship with the patent application.
5. (Optional) Select a date and time for the authorization in the **Authorization Date** and **Authorization Time** fields, respectively.
6. Provide any additional notes or comments in the **Authorization Note** field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Representative Name (Required)** (`representative_name`, text): Enter the name of the person authorizing the patent application.
* **Contact Email (Required)** (`contact_email`, email): Enter a valid email address to enable communication and update notifications.
* **Contact Phone (Optional)** (`contact_phone`, text): Enter a valid phone number for additional contact purposes.
* **Representative Type (Required)** (`representative_type`, select_one): Select one of the options (Inventor, Agent, Law Firm, University, or Other) to indicate your role in the patent application.
* **Authorization Date (Optional)** (`authorization_date`, date): If applicable, enter the date of the authorization.
* **Authorization Time (Optional)** (`authorization_time`, time): If applicable, enter the time of the authorization.
* **Authorization Note (Optional)** (`authorization_note`, note): Provide any additional comments or notes related to the authorization.
