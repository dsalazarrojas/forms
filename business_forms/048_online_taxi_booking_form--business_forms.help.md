# Online Taxi Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers book a taxi online by providing their address, pickup time, trip preferences, and other relevant information.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your address in the "address" field.
2. Select your pickup time from the "Pickup time" field.
3. Choose your trip preferences from the "Select trip" field.
4. Select your fare preference from the "Select fare" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the "note" field.
8. Confirm your request by typing "yes" or "confirm" in the "Confirm" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **address**: Enter your pickup address.
* **Pickup time**: Select your desired pickup time.
* **Select trip**: Choose from available trip options (e.g., Trip 1, Trip 2, or Trip 3).
* **Select fare**: Select your preferred fare option (e.g., Fare 1, Fare 2, or Fare 3).
* **email**: Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for verification.
* **note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your ride.
* **Confirm**: Type "yes" or "confirm" to confirm your request.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to enter your address and contact information accurately to ensure efficient service.
* Select your trip and fare preferences carefully to ensure you get the best option for your needs.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to avoid errors.
