# Recycled Packaging Certification Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from businesses interested in obtaining recycled packaging certification. The purpose of this form is to collect relevant details about the business and their packaging products, which will be reviewed for certification eligibility. This form is intended for businesses that have developed new or existing packaging products that meet the requirements for recyclable materials, design, and manufacturing processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Begin by filling out the **Main Page** (Page 1) with your business information. This section includes general details about your company and contact information.
2.  Proceed to the **Certification Information** (Page 2) and provide details about the certification you are applying for, such as the type of certification you are seeking and any relevant documentation.
3.  Next, move to the **Certification Details** (Page 3) and describe the packaging product you are seeking certification for, including its material composition, design specifications, and manufacturing processes.
4.  Finally, provide any **Additional Information** (Page 4) that may be relevant to your application, such as any special considerations or supporting documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Main Page** (Page 1):
    *   **Company Name** (text, required): Enter your company's name as it appears on your business documents.
    *   **Contact Person's Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for the application.
    *   **Contact Email** (text, required): Enter your company's official email address.
    *   **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your company's official phone number.
*   **Certification Information** (Page 2):
    *   **Certification Type** (text, required): Select the type of certification you are applying for (e.g., recyclable, biodegradable, etc.).
    *   **Certification Level** (text, required): Indicate the level of certification you are seeking (e.g., basic, premium, etc.).
*   **Certification Details** (Page 3):
    *   **Product Description** (text, required): Describe the packaging product you are seeking certification for, including its intended use and any unique features.
    *   **Material Composition** (text, required): Specify the materials used to manufacture the packaging product, including any recycled content.
    *   **Design Specifications** (text, required): Describe the packaging product's design, including its size, shape, and color scheme.
    *   **Manufacturing Process** (text, required): Explain the manufacturing process used to produce the packaging product, including any relevant production details.
*   **Additional Information** (Page 4):
    *   **Special Considerations** (text, optional): If applicable, provide any special considerations or requirements related to your application.
    *   **Supporting Documents** (file upload, optional): Upload any relevant documents or certifications that support your application.
