# # Proof Approval - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Proof Approval" form is used to collect and review information for proofing and approval processes within an organization. This form helps to ensure that all necessary details are included and approved before finalizing any proof or artwork.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information, including the Artwork Title, Client Name, and Artist Name. These fields are all optional, but they are included to provide more context about the proof.
2. If applicable, select the Proof Date and Proof Time to record the dates and times associated with the proofing process.
3. Add any Comments, if necessary, to provide additional context or explanations.
4. Finally, enter your name in the Approved By field to indicate your approval of the proof.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artwork Title (Artwork Title)** (`artwork_title`, optional): Enter the title of the artwork or proof being approved.
* **Client Name (Client Name)** (`client_name`, optional): Enter the name of the client associated with the proof or artwork.
* **Artist Name (Artist Name)** (`artist_name`, optional): Enter the name of the artist associated with the proof or artwork.
* **Proof Date (Proof Date)** (`proof_date`, optional): If applicable, select the date associated with the proofing process.
* **Proof Time (Proof Time)** (`proof_time`, optional): If applicable, select the time associated with the proofing process.
* **Approved By (Approved By)** (`approved_by`, optional): Enter your name to indicate your approval of the proof.
* **Comments (Comments)** (`comments`, optional): Add any additional context or explanations related to the proof.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Double-check the entered information before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before approving or finalizing the proof.
