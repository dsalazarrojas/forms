# Contact A Manufacturing Technology Advisor - Help Guide
## Purpose

The goal of this form is to gather information about your company's technology needs and preferences so that we can connect you with a suitable manufacturing technology advisor who can help you achieve your goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your contact information to get in touch with us.
2. Describe your company's technology needs and challenges in the 'Technology Inquiry Details' field.
3. Answer all required fields to ensure we have a clear understanding of your needs.
4. Choose your preferred contact method in the 'Preferred Contact Method' field.
5. Provide any additional comments or information that you think is relevant to your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Technology Inquiry Details** (`inquiry_details`, Note, Optional): This is the initial description of your company's technology needs and challenges. Use this field to tell us about any specific problems you're experiencing or technologies you're interested in exploring.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your last name.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your company's name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, Required): Please enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Required): Please enter your direct contact number.
* **Job Role** (`job_role`, Select One, Required): Please select your job role from the list provided.
* **Manufacturing Sector** (`manufacturing_sector`, Select One, Required): Please select your company's manufacturing sector from the list provided.
* **Technology Areas of Interest** (`technology_interest`, Select Multiple, Required): Please select the technologies that interest you.
* **Current Technology Adoption Level** (`current_technology_level`, Select One, Required): Please select how you would describe your current technology adoption level.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, Select One, Required): Please select the type of project you're embarking on.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, Select One, Optional): If applicable, please select your estimated budget range.
* **Implementation Timeline** (`timeline`, Select One, Required): Please select your expected timeframe for implementation.
* **Project Team Size** (`team_size`, Select One, Optional): If applicable, please select the number of people involved in your project.
* **Pain Points** (`pain_points`, Select Multiple, Required): Please select the challenges you're currently facing.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, Text, Required): Please describe your technology needs in more detail.
* **Success Criteria** (`success_criteria`, Text, Optional): If applicable, please define what success means for this project.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`, Select One, Required): Please select how you would like to be contacted.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Please provide any other relevant information about your project.
