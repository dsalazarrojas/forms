# Governance Risk Audit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Governance Risk Audit Application Form is designed to collect information from users about their governance risk assessments. The form is meant to be used for risk analysis and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the 'User Information' field with your details.
2. Provide 'Submission Details' about your submission.
3. If necessary, include 'Risk Assessment' of your submission.
4. Add 'Submission Information' about your submission.
5. Provide any 'Additional Information' that is relevant to your submission.
6. Upload any relevant attachments using the 'Upload Attachments' field.
7. Add any comments you have about your submission in the 'Comments' field.
8. Finally, select the 'Review Status' of your submission from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information
* **User Information** (`user_input`, text, required): Fill in your name and contact details.

### Submission Details
* **Submission Details** (`submission_details`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your submission.

### Risk Assessment
* **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, text, optional): If you want to provide a risk assessment for your submission, use this field.

### Submission Information
* **Submission Information** (`submission_info`, text, required): Add any additional information about your submission.

### Additional Information
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): If you have any other information that might be helpful, use this field.

### Upload Attachments
* **Upload Attachments** (`attachment_upload`, text, optional): You can upload any relevant documents or files.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`user_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments about your submission.

### Submission Comments
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments about the review process.

### Review Status
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of your submission (Open, Replied, Rejected, Done, In Progress, Cancelled).
