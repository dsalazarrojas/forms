# App Development Application Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This questionnaire is designed to gather information about your app development project. It will help us understand your concept, team, and expectations, and guide you through the development process on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about your project.
2. Be honest and clear in your answers, as they will help us evaluate your application and provide guidance.
3. Review and proofread your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date** (1): Enter the date of your application.
* **Applicant or Company Name** (2): Enter your name or your company's name.
* **Contact Email Address** (3): Enter your email address where we can reach you.
* **Contact Phone Number** (4): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Type of Organization** (5): Select the type of organization you represent.
	+ Individual developer
	+ Startup
	+ Small business
	+ Medium company
	+ Large corporation
	+ Non-profit
	+ Educational institution
	+ Other
* **App Concept or Idea** (6): Describe your app concept or idea briefly.
* **What Problem Does Your App Solve?** (7): Describe the problem your app solves.
* **Estimated Target Market Size** (8): Estimate the number of potential users.
* **Current Development Stage** (9): Select your current development stage.
	+ Concept only
	+ Early prototype
	+ Working prototype
	+ Beta version
	+ Ready for launch
	+ Already launched
* **Expected Development Timeline** (10): Enter the expected development timeline in months.
* **Development Budget Range** (11): Enter the development budget range in dollars.
* **Current Team Size** (12): Enter the number of people in your team.
* **Team Development Experience** (13): Enter the average years of experience of your team members.
* **Key Technical Skills** (14): List the key technical skills of your team.
* **Funding Status** (15): Select your funding status.
	+ Fully funded
	+ Partially funded
	+ Seeking funding
	+ Self-funded
	+ Other
* **Expected Revenue Model** (16): Select your expected revenue model.
	+ Freemium with in-app purchases
	+ Subscription based
	+ One-time purchase
	+ Ad-supported
	+ Enterprise licensing
	+ Other
* **Have You Analyzed Competitors?** (17): Select how you've analyzed competitors.
	+ Yes, detailed analysis
	+ Yes, basic analysis
	+ Somewhat
	+ False
* **Your Competitive Advantage** (18): Describe what makes your app unique.
* **Marketing and Launch Strategy** (19): Describe your marketing and launch strategy.
* **Additional Information** (20): Provide any additional information about your project.
