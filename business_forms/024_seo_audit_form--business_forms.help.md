# seo_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SEO audit form is used to collect information about a website's search engine ranking, search volume, and online presence. This form is designed to help users input their website's details and get insights on how to improve their website's SEO.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `User Input` field with any additional information about your website that is not already included in the form.
2. Enter the `Website URL` of your website that you want to audit.
3. Select the relevant search engine from the `Search Engine` dropdown:
	* Google
	* Bing
	* Yahoo
4. Enter the search term that you want to focus on in the `Search Term` field.
5. Select the desired search results in the `Search Results` field.
6. Input the date range for your website's data in the `Date Range` field.
7. Enter the time of day when you want to analyze your website's data in the `Time of Day` field.
8. Add any additional `Notes` about your website or audit.
9. Optionally, provide your `Email` and `Phone` contact information for follow-up contact.
10. Choose the person(s) you would like to be assigned to your audit from the `Assigned` dropdown:
	* Chatjimmy
	* Other

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: Enter any additional information about your website that is not already included in the form. (text, optional)
* **Website URL**: Enter the URL of your website that you want to audit. (text)
* **Search Engine**: Select the search engine to focus on for your audit: Google, Bing, or Yahoo. (select one)
* **Search Term**: Enter the term to focus on in your website's search results. (text)
* **Search Results**: Select all the relevant search results for your website. (select multiple)
* **Date Range**: Input the date range for your website's data. (date)
* **Time of Day**: Enter the time of day when you want to analyze your website's data. (time)
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your website or audit. (note)
* **Email**: Enter your email contact information for follow-up contact. (email, optional)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for follow-up contact. (text, optional)
* **Assigned**: Choose who you would like to be assigned to your audit: Chatjimmy or Other. (select multiple)
