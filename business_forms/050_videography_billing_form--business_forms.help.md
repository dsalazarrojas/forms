# videography_billing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The videography_billing_form is used to gather information for videography services billing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client name, project name, client email, and client phone numbers in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter the total cost of the videography service provided.
3. Select the payment option(s) that apply to the service.
4. Choose the payment method for the service.
5. Set the billing status as "Completed", "Not Started", or "In Progress".
6. Provide any additional information related to the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`videography_client_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the client for whom the videography service was provided.
* **Project Name** (`videography_project_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the project for which the videography service was provided.
* **Client Email** (`videography_client_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the client.
* **Client Phone** (`videography_client_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Billing Date** (`videography_billing_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the billing for the videography service.
* **Total Cost** (`videography_total_cost`, number, required/optional): Enter the total cost of the videography service provided.
* **Payment Option** (`videography_payment_option`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the payment options that apply to the service. 
* **Payment Method** (`videography_payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the payment method for the service.
* **Billing Status** (`videography_billing_status`, select_one, required/optional): Set the billing status as "Completed", "Not Started", or "In Progress".
* **Additional Info** (`videography_additional_info`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional information related to the service.
