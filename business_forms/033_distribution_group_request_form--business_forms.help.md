# Distribution Group Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distribution Group Request Form is used to collect information from team leads or team members to request the creation of a new distribution group. This form will help the distribution team understand the needs and requirements of the team, ensuring that the new distribution group is created efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name and Last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your Department from the dropdown list.
3. Enter your Date of birth.
4. Select the size of your team from the dropdown list.
5. Select the Product that you are working with.
6. Enter the Distribution location where your team will be working from.
7. Enter the email address and phone number of your Team lead.
8. Optionally, you can enter any additional information you feel is necessary to support your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (First name, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Last name** (Last name, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Date of birth** (Date of birth, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Department** (Department, select_one, required): Select the department that you belong to. This will help us understand your team's needs and create an effective distribution group.
* **Team size** (Team size, select_multiple, required): Choose the size of your team from the options provided. This will help us determine the number of distributions needed for your team.
* **Product** (Product, select_one, required): Select the product that your team is working with. This will help us understand your team's specific needs and requirements.
* **Distribution location** (Distribution location, text, optional): Enter the location where your team will be distributing the product. This will help us ensure that the distribution process is done efficiently and effectively.
* **Team lead email** (Team lead email, email, optional): Enter the email address of your Team lead. This will help us contact them with further instructions and updates.
* **Team lead phone** (Team lead phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your Team lead. This will help us contact them in case of any urgent matters.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID, number, optional): Enter your Employee ID, if applicable.
* **Manager name** (Manager name, text, optional): Enter your manager's name, if applicable.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (Department, Team size, Product) to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form is for internal use only. Please do not share it with anyone outside of the company.
