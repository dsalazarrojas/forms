# LLC Extension Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LLC Extension Request Form is used to request an extension for a Limited Liability Company (LLC) that is past due for various reasons. This form helps to gather information about the LLC's current status and the reason for the extension request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Make sure to attach any supporting documents as required.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all necessary information is included.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Details** (`company_details`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your company, including its name and address.
* **Company Details 2** (`company_details_2`, text, optional): This field is for any additional details about your company that you would like to mention.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, optional): If your request date is different from the current date, please specify the actual date you would like to request an extension.
* **Request Deadline** (`request_deadline`, number, optional): If your request deadline is different from the standard deadline, please specify it here.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): Attach any relevant documents that support your extension request.
* **Authorization** (`authorization`, select_multiple, optional): If your company requires authorization from someone else, please select 'Yes' to indicate this.
* **Administrator Name** (`administrator_name`, text, optional): If your company has an administrator who can be contacted, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Administrator Email** (`administrator_email`, email, optional): Enter the administrator's email address.
* **Follow Up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose the frequency of follow-ups you would like to receive (1 week, 2 weeks, or 3 weeks).
* **Follow Up Frequency 2** (`follow_up_frequency_2`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information about follow-up frequency.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about your extension request.
* **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, text, optional): This field is for any additional notes or information you would like to provide.
* **Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): If you would like to schedule follow-ups, please specify the date.
* **Follow Up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the follow-up.
* **Follow Up Method** (`follow_up_method`, text, optional): Choose the method of follow-up you would like to receive (e.g., email, phone, or in-person).
* **Next Follow Up Deadline** (`next_follow_up_deadline`, number, optional): If you would like to schedule a next follow-up, please specify the deadline.
* **Next Follow Up Frequency** (`next_follow_up_frequency`, text, optional): Choose the frequency of the next follow-up (1 week, 2 weeks, or 3 weeks).
* **Next Follow Up Date** (`next_follow_up_date`, date, optional): If you would like to schedule the next follow-up, please specify the date.
* **Next Follow Up Time** (`next_follow_up_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the next follow-up.
* **Follow Up Method 2** (`follow_up_method_2`, text, optional): Choose the method of the next follow-up you would like to receive.
* **Follow Up Deadline** (`follow_up_deadline`, number, optional): If you would like to schedule a follow-up deadline, please specify it.
* **Notes 3** (`notes_3`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or information you would like to provide.
* **Follow Up Deadline 2** (`follow_up_deadline_2`, number, optional): If you would like to schedule a next follow-up deadline, please specify it.
* **Follow Up Frequency 3** (`follow_up_frequency_3`, text, optional): Choose the frequency of the next follow-up you would like to receive.
* **Follow Up Method 3** (`follow_up_method_3`, text, optional): Choose the method of the next follow-up you would like to receive.
