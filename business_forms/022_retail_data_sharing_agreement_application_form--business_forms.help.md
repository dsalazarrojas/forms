# Retail Data Sharing Agreement Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Data Sharing Agreement Application Form is used to submit an application for data sharing between retail partners and the company. It ensures that both parties understand their responsibilities and the terms of the agreement before data sharing takes place.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to check the correct options for "Data Types" and "Data Sharing Purposes" that apply to your retail partner's needs.
3. Enter your preferred contact information (Retail Partner, Contact Person, Email, and Phone).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retail Partner Information** (`retail_partner`):
	+ A field to provide your retail partner company's name and information.
* **Data Types** (`data_types`):
	+ A field to select the type of data you want to share (Product Information, Order History, Customer Data).
* **Data Sharing Purposes** (`data_sharing_purposes`):
	+ A field to select the purpose of data sharing (Business Development, Marketing Research, Product Development).
* **Data Sharing Agreement** (`data_sharing_agreement`):
	+ A field to provide a brief description of the data sharing agreement.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`):
	+ A field to provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for data sharing.
* **Email** (`email`):
	+ A field to provide the contact email of the person responsible for data sharing.
* **Phone** (`phone`):
	+ A field to provide the contact phone number of the person responsible for data sharing.
* **Consent** (`consent`):
	+ A field to confirm that you consent to data sharing.
* **Notes** (`notes`):
	+ A field to provide any additional notes or comments about the data sharing agreement.
* **Submit**:
	+ A button to submit the form after completing all the required fields. 

Note: Please fill out all required fields to ensure that your application is complete and accurate. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact the relevant department.
