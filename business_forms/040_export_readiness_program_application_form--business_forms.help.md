# Export Readiness Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Export Readiness Program Application Form is designed to help businesses assess their export readiness and compliance with regulations and guidelines for exporting products to other countries.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2. For each question, choose the relevant option that best describes your situation.
3. Provide detailed answers where necessary, such as descriptions or dates.
4. If you have any additional comments or information, please use the "Additional Comments" section at the end of the form.
