# Green Supplier Evaluation Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Green Supplier Evaluation Application Form is designed to collect information about your company's sustainability efforts and green practices. This data is used to evaluate your company's commitment to environmental responsibility and assess its eligibility for partnership opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
2. Fill out the form in the order specified, starting with the first page.
3. Enter your contact details, including contact person, title, company, and contact email, phone number, address, and website.
4. Select any relevant certifications and documents that demonstrate your company's commitment to sustainability.
5. Provide any additional notes about your company's sustainability practices.
6. Double-check your responses for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (<contact_email>):
	+ This field is optional but recommended.
	+ Enter your company's primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (<contact_phone>):
	+ This field is optional but recommended.
	+ Enter your company's primary phone number.
* **Notes** (<notes>):
	+ This field is optional.
	+ Enter any additional comments or information about your company's sustainability practices.
* **Contact Person** (<contact_person>):
	+ This field is optional but recommended.
	+ Enter the name of the person who will be in charge of sustainability efforts.
* **Company** (<company>):
	+ This field is optional but recommended.
	+ Enter your company name.
* **Contact Person Title** (<contact_person_title>):
	+ This field is optional but recommended.
	+ Enter the title or position of the contact person.
* **Website** (<website>):
	+ This field is optional but recommended.
	+ Enter your company's website URL.
* **Contact Address** (<contact_address>):
	+ This field is optional but recommended.
	+ Enter your company's physical address.
* **Sustainability Practices** (<sustainability_practices>):
	+ This field is optional.
	+ Select any relevant practices from the list, such as Green Supply Chain Management, Life Cycle Assessment, and Product Take-Back.
* **Certifications** (<certifications>):
	+ This field is optional.
	+ Select any relevant certifications from the list, such as IATF, ISO 14001, and ISO 14024.
* **Documents** (<documents>):
	+ This field is optional.
	+ Select any relevant documents, such as Document 1, Document 2, and Document 3.
* **Green Household** (<green_household>):
	+ This field is optional.
	+ Enter the number of employees or staff in your company.
* **Website** (<website>):
	+ This field is optional but not used as it's redundant with the previous "Website" field.
	+ Please ignore this field.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to us for clarification.
* You can submit any additional supporting documents or information separately.
* This form is designed to be completed by the supplier or company representative, not by a third party.
