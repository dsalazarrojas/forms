# vendor_management_effectiveness_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The <code>vendor_management_effectiveness_survey</code> form is designed to help evaluate the effectiveness of a vendor in managing their business. This form is used to collect data on various aspects of vendor performance, such as rating and feedback, to help identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the <code>Vendor Rating</code> field and choose one of the options: <code>Good</code> or <code>Fair</code>.
2. Enter the <code>Vendor Name</code> in the corresponding field.
3. Fill in the <code>Contact Person</code> field with the name of the contact person for the vendor.
4. Enter the <code>Vendor Address</code> in the corresponding field.
5. Enter the <code>Email Address</code> of the vendor in the corresponding field.
6. Fill in the <code>Purchase Order Date</code> and <code>Purchase Order Time</code> fields with the dates and times associated with the purchase order.
7. Enter the <code>Purchase Order Note</code> in the corresponding field.
8. Select the <code>Total Value</code> field and enter the total value of the purchase order.
9. Select the <code>Payment Terms</code> field and choose one or more of the options: <code>Yes</code> or <code>No</code>.
10. Select the <code>Communication Frequency</code> field and choose one of the options: <code>Yes</code> or <code>No</code>.
11. Select the <code>Performance Metrics</code> field and choose one or more of the options: <code>Yes</code> or <code>No</code>.
12. Enter the <code>Vendor Performance Rating</code> in the corresponding field.
13. Fill in the <code>Vendor Comment</code> field with any additional comments.
14. Select the <code>Rating Scale</code> field and choose one or more of the options: <code>Good</code> or <code>Fair</code>.
15. Select the <code>Overall Rating</code> field and choose one or more of the options: <code>Good</code> or <code>Fair</code>.
16. Fill in the <code>Rating Comment</code> field with any additional comments.
17. Select the <code>Purchase Order Status</code> field and choose one of the options: <code>Active</code> or <code>Inactive</code>.
18. Select the <code>Vendor Status</code> field and choose one of the options: <code>Active</code> or <code>Inactive</code>.
19. Select the <code>Rating Scale Reported</code> field and choose one of the options: <code>Good</code> or <code>Fair</code>.
20. Select the <code>Vendor Rating Reported</code> field and choose one of the options: <code>Good</code> or <code>Fair</code>.
21. Enter the <code>Purchase Order Date Reported</code> in the corresponding field.
22. Enter the <code>Purchase Order Time Reported</code> in the corresponding field.
23. Select the <code>Overall Rating Reported</code> field and choose one or more of the options: <code>Good</code> or <code>Fair</code>.
24. Fill in the <code>Rating Comment Reported</code> field with any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Rating** (`Vendor Rating`, `select_one`, not required): Select the rating of the vendor, which is the overall impression of the vendor's performance.
* **Vendor Name** (`Vendor Name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the vendor being evaluated.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the contact person for the vendor.
* **Vendor Address** (`Vendor Address`, `text`, not required): Enter the address of the vendor.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, not required): Enter the email address of the vendor.
* **Purchase Order Date** (`Purchase Order Date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date associated with the purchase order.
* **Purchase Order Time** (`Purchase Order Time`, `time`, not required): Enter the time associated with the purchase order.
* **Purchase Order Note** (`Purchase Order Note`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional notes about the purchase order.
* **Total Value** (`Total Value`, `number`, not required): Enter the total value of the purchase order.
* **Payment Terms** (`Payment Terms`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or more of the options to indicate if payment terms are accepted or not.
* **Communication Frequency** (`Communication Frequency`, `select_one`, not required): Select the frequency of communication with the vendor.
* **Performance Metrics** (`Performance Metrics`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or more of the options to indicate if performance metrics are met or not.
* **Vendor Performance Rating** (`Vendor Performance Rating`, `number`, not required): Enter the performance rating of the vendor.
* **Vendor Comment** (`Vendor Comment`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional comments about the vendor.
* **Rating Scale** (`Rating Scale`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or more of the options to indicate if the rating scale is used or not.
* **Overall Rating** (`Overall Rating`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or more of the options to indicate if the overall rating is good or fair.
* **Rating Comment** (`Rating Comment`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional comments about the rating.
* **Purchase Order Status** (`Purchase Order Status`, `select_one`, not required): Select the status of the purchase order.
* **Vendor Status** (`Vendor Status`, `select_one`, not required): Select the status of the vendor.
* **Rating Scale Reported** (`Rating Scale Reported`, `select_one`, not required): Select the rating scale reported.
* **Vendor Rating Reported** (`Vendor Rating Reported`, `select_one`, not required): Select the vendor rating reported.
* **Purchase Order Date Reported** (`Purchase Order Date Reported`, `date`, not required): Enter the date associated with the purchase order.
* **Purchase Order Time Reported** (`Purchase Order Time Reported`, `time`, not required): Enter the time associated with the purchase order.
* **Overall Rating Reported** (`Overall Rating Reported`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or more of the options to indicate if the overall rating is reported or not.
* **Rating Comment Reported** (`Rating Comment Reported`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional comments about the rating.
