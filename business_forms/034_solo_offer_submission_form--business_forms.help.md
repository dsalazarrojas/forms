# solo_offer_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit offers and proposals for services you are providing as a solo user.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form's fields with relevant information about your service.
2. Ensure you fill out all required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3. Review and submit your completed form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit (form_submit)** (`form_submit`, text, required/optional): 
  This field is used to submit your completed form. Enter a brief message, if desired.
* **Offer Details (offer_details)** (`offer_details`, note, required/optional): 
  Briefly describe your proposal, including relevant experience and qualifications.
* **Service Description (service_description)** (`service_description`, text, required/optional): 
  Enter a detailed description of the service you are offering.
* **Service Price (service_price)** (`service_price`, number, required/optional): 
  Enter the price for the service you are offering.
* **Service Time (service_time)** (`service_time`, time, required/optional): 
  Enter the amount of time required to complete the service.
* **Available Dates (available_dates)** (`available_dates`, select_one, required/optional): 
  Select one of the available dates to indicate when you are available to complete the service.
* **Service Location (service_location)** (`service_location`, select_multiple, required/optional): 
  Choose from one or more locations where you can provide the service.
  Options: 
  - Home
  - Office
  - Remote
  - Client Site
