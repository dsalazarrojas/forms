# Zero Emission Delivery Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Zero Emission Delivery Program Application Form is designed to help delivery services apply for participation in the program. The program aims to reduce carbon footprint and promote environmentally friendly transportation methods. All delivery services are encouraged to apply to contribute to this initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out all required fields marked as required (e.g., Email, Phone Number, ZIP).
*   Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
*   Review and submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Applicant Contact Details (<em>Applicant Contact Details</em>, <em>text</em>, required/optional)**: Please enter your company's contact name and address to help the program team get in touch with you for further information or clarifications.
*   **Fleet Information (<em>Fleet Information</em>, <em>text</em>, required/optional)**: Describe your company's fleet, including the types of vehicles you use, and how many vehicles you have in operation. This will help the program team assess your eligibility for the program.
*   **Motivations (<em>Motivations</em>, <em>text</em>, required/optional)**: Explain why you want to participate in the Zero Emission Delivery Program and how it aligns with your company's goals and objectives.
*   **Consent (<em>Consent</em>, <em>text</em>, required/optional)**: Confirm that you agree to the program's terms and conditions and understand the benefits and expectations.
*   **Email (<em>Email</em>, <em>email</em>, required/optional)**: Enter your company's email address to facilitate communication between your company and the program team.
*   **Phone Number (<em>Phone Number</em>, <em>number</em>, required/optional)**: Provide your company's phone number for quick contact and communication purposes.
*   **Address (<em>Address</em>, <em>text</em>, required/optional)**: Enter your company's address to help us get in touch with you for further information or any clarifications.
*   **City (<em>City</em>, <em>text</em>, required/optional)**: Enter your city name for better location tracking and identification.
*   **ZIP (<em>ZIP</em>, <em>number</em>, required/optional)**: Enter your zip code for accurate delivery and communication purposes.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
*   Review your application before submitting to ensure everything is correct.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the program team.
