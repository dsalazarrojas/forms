# r_and_r_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of the "r_and_r_form" is to collect information for employee recognition and reward programs. It captures details about employee awards, including nomination and recognition dates, award types, and recognition status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields (marked as *required*) and provide any additional information as needed for each field.
2. Review each field's description for specific guidance on what information is expected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (employee_name)**: Enter the name of the employee being recognized or nominated.
* **Award Date (award_date)**: Select the date when the award was received or will be received. (Required)
* **Award Type (award_type)**: Choose the type of award being recognized, such as Team Lead, Manager, or President's Award. (Required)
* **Award Justification (award_justification)**: Provide a brief reason or explanation for the award. (Optional)
* **Employee ID (employee_id)**: Enter the unique identification number of the employee being recognized. (Required)
* **Manager Name (manager_name)**: Enter the name of the manager of the employee being recognized. (Optional)
* **Employee Title (employee_title)**: Enter the job title of the employee being recognized. (Optional)
* **Manager Email (manager_email)**: Enter the email address of the manager of the employee being recognized. (Optional)
* **Employee Department (employee_department)**: Enter the department of the employee being recognized. (Optional)
* **Award Value (award_value)**: Enter the monetary value of the award, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Nomination Date (nomination_date)**: Select the date when the nomination was submitted. (Required)
* **Nomination Status (nomination_status)**: Choose the status of the nomination, such as Pending, Approved, or Denied. (Required)
* **Manager Comments (manager_comments)**: Enter any additional comments from the manager. (Optional)
* **Employee Comments (employee_comments)**: Enter any additional comments from the employee. (Optional)
* **Award Recipient (award_recipient)**: Select the type of recipient for the award, such as Employee, Team, or Department. (Required)
* **Recognition Date (recognition_date)**: Select the date when the recognition occurred. (Optional)
* **Recognition Type (recognition_type)**: Choose the type of recognition, such as Monetary or Non-Monetary. (Required)
* **Recognition Value (recognition_value)**: Enter the value of the recognition. (Optional)
* **Recognition Status (recognition_status)**: Choose the status of the recognition, such as Not Yet Recognized or Already Recognized. (Optional)
* **Form Submitted By (form_submitted_by)**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form. (Optional)
* **Form Submitted By Email (form_submitted_by_email)**: Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form. (Optional)
* **Form Completed By (form_completed_by)**: Enter the name of the person who completed the form. (Optional)
* **Form Completed By Email (form_completed_by_email)**: Enter the email address of the person who completed the form. (Optional)
* **Date Completed (date_completed)**: Select the date when the form was completed. (Required)
* **Form Version (form_version)**: Enter the version of the form being used. (Optional)
