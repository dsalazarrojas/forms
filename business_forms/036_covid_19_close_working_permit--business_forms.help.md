# Covid 19 Close Working Permit - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid 19 Close Working Permit form is designed to document the closure of a business's workspace due to the COVID-19 pandemic. This form helps business owners and administrators keep track of essential information, ensuring that all relevant details are recorded and documented accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide **Contact Information**: Enter the business's contact details in the **Contact Information** field. This includes the business's address, phone number, and email address.
2.  Enter **Business Details**: In the **Business Details** field, provide a brief description of the business and its current status.
3.  Specify the **Business Owner**: Enter the name of the business owner or manager in the **Business Owner** field.
4.  Enter **Business Phone**: Provide the phone number of the business.
5.  Add **Business Email**: Enter the email address of the business.
6.  Record **Close Measuring Method**: Select the method used to measure the business's closure in the **Close Measuring Method** field. This can be any of the options provided.
7.  Specify the **Close Measuring Device**: Choose the device or tool used to measure the business's closure in the **Close Measuring Device** field. Select all applicable options.
8.  Measure **Distance**: Enter the distance of the business's closure in the **Distance** field.
9.  Record **Time**: Enter the time of the business's closure in the **Time** field.
10.  Update **Close Working Status**: Select the current status of the business's closure in the **Close Working Status** field.
11.  Add **Comments**: Enter any additional comments related to the business's closure in the **Comments** field.
12.  Assign to **Person**: Choose the person responsible for updating this form in the **Assigned To** field.
13.  Identify **Created By**: Enter the name of the person who created this form in the **Created By** field.
14.  Update by **Last Updated By**: Enter the name of the person who last updated this form in the **Last Updated By** field.
15.  Record **Updated At**: Select the date of the last update made to this form in the **Updated At** field.
16.  Record **Created At**: Select the date this form was first created in the **Created At** field.
17.  Close Measuring Device 1-4: Select all applicable options for the devices used to measure the business's closure.
