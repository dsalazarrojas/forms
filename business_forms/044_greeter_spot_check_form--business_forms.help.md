# Greeter Spot Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Greeter Spot Check Form is designed to collect information from greeters about their spot checks, which are informal meetings or interactions with guests in a facility, such as a hotel or resort. The form is used to gather data on whether guests have been greeted correctly, efficiently, and in a friendly manner. This information helps the facility to evaluate the effectiveness of their greeter services and make improvements as needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answers to the spot check items on the second page of the form.
2. Enter any comments or feedback you have about the spot check interaction in the comments section on the third page.
3. Enter any additional information you think would be helpful for the facility to know in the comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Greeter Information** (`Greeter Information`, text, optional): Please provide some background information about yourself, such as your name and position/title.
- **Spot Check Items** (`Spot Check Items`, select_one, optional): On this page, select whether the spot check items have been successfully checked or not. The options are:
	- **Yes**: The spot check items were successful and met the expected standards.
	- **No**: The spot check items were not successful and did not meet the expected standards.
- **Comments** (`Comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or feedback you have about the spot check interaction.
- **greeter_spot_check_form_4_page** (`greeter_spot_check_form_4_page`, email, optional): This is an email address field, but it seems to be incorrectly labeled. Please enter your email address if you wish to have the form submitter notified of your response. 

Note: This field may not actually be relevant to the form's purpose and might not be necessary. It would be better to remove it or change its label to match the form's intended purpose. 

# Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and truthful answers to the spot check items.
- If you are unsure about any of the spot check items, feel free to ask the facility staff for clarification.
- Remember, the information collected on this form will help the facility improve their greeter services, so please be as detailed and helpful as possible in your feedback.
