# Business Continuity Advisory Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from business organizations about their business continuity management (BCM) plans and needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions in the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Fill in the name of your business.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select your industry from the provided options.
* **Organization Size** (`organization_size`, select_one, required): Choose your organization size.
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, select_one, optional): Indicate your approximate annual revenue.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, note, optional): Provide your contact information including full name, position, email address, and phone number.
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Fill in your full name.
* **Position** (`position`, text, required): Fill in your position.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Fill in your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Fill in your phone number.
* **Continuity Status** (`continuity_status`, note, optional): Answer the question about whether you have a continuity plan in place and its current status.
* **Do You Have a Continuity Plan** (`current_plan`, select_one, required): Specify if you have a continuity plan and its status.
* **When Was Plan Last Reviewed** (`last_review`, date, optional): Specify when was your plan last reviewed.
* **Known Plan Gaps** (`plan_gaps`, text, optional): Identify any known gaps in your continuity plan.
* **Advisory Needs** (`advisory_needs`, note, optional): Indicate what help you need for your continuity planning.
* **Primary Need** (`primary_need`, select_one, required): Select your primary need for seeking advisory services.
* **Specific Challenges** (`specific_challenges`, text, required): Describe any specific challenges you are facing.
* **Top Risk Concerns** (`risk_concerns`, select_multiple, required): Identify your top risk concerns.
* **Regulatory Requirements** (`regulatory_requirements`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have any regulatory requirements.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, select_one, required): Choose when you need assistance.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Select your expected investment range for advisory services.
* **Key Stakeholders** (`stakeholders`, text, optional): Provide the names and contact information of key stakeholders.
* **Previous Disruption Incidents** (`previous_incidents`, text, optional): Share information about any previous disruption incidents.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information.
