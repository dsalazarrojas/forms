# Advertising Network Agreement Renewal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather necessary information for the renewal of an existing advertising network agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Renewal Type" that best describes your current situation.
2. Enter the "Agreement Number" found on your current contract.
3. Fill in your "Business Name" and the "Authorized Representative Name".
4. Enter the "Business Email" and "Billing Address" that will be used for correspondence and billing.
5. Provide feedback on your "Satisfaction with Current Period" and select the "Key Metrics Achieved" that met your expectations.
6. Enter the "Comments or Concerns" about the current agreement and the next term.
7. Select the "Renewal Term Length" for the new agreement.
8. Enter the "Date of Submission" for the completed form.
9. Check the "Authorization" box to confirm that you are authorizing the renewal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agreement Number** (Required, Text): Enter the ID from your current contract with the advertising network.
* **Renewal Type** (Required, Select One): Select the best option that describes your current situation.
	+ **Standard Renewal (Same Terms)**: No modifications to the current agreement.
	+ **Renal with Modifications**: Changes to the current agreement terms.
	+ **Upgrade Package**: Upgrade to a higher agreement plan.
	+ **Downgrade Package**: Downgrade to a lower agreement plan.
* **Business Name** (Required, Text): Enter the name of your business.
* **Authorized Representative Name** (Required, Text): Enter the name of the representative authorized to sign the agreement.
* **Business Email** (Required, Email): Enter the email used for business communication with the advertising network.
* **Billing Address** (Required, Text): Enter the address used for billing purposes.
* **Satisfaction with Current Period** (Optional, Select One): Rate your satisfaction with the current advertising period.
	+ **Very Satisfied**: Satisfied with the current performance.
	+ **Satisfied**: Generally satisfied with the current performance.
	+ **Neutral**: No significant change in performance.
	+ **Dissatisfied**: Generally dissatisfied with the current performance.
	+ **Very Dissatisfied**: Very dissatisfied with the current performance.
* **Key Metrics Achieved** (Optional, Select Multiple): Select the metrics that met your expectations during the current agreement.
	+ **Click-Through Rate (CTR)**: Number of clicks on the advertisements.
	+ **Conversion Rate**: Number of conversions from the advertisements.
	+ **Impression Volume**: Number of times the advertisements were displayed.
	+ **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)**: Cost per conversion.
	+ **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)**: Revenue generated per ad spend.
* **Comments or Concerns** (Optional, Text): Share any feedback or concerns about the current agreement and the next term.
* **Renewal Term Length** (Required, Select One): Select the duration of the new agreement.
	+ **3 Months**: Renewal for 3 months.
	+ **6 Months**: Renewal for 6 months.
	+ **12 Months**: Renewal for 12 months.
	+ **24 Months**: Renewal for 24 months.
* **Date of Submission** (Required, Date): Enter the date of the completed form.
* **Authorization** (Required, Select One): Confirm that you are authorizing the renewal.
	+ **I Authorize**: Confirmation of agreement renewal.
