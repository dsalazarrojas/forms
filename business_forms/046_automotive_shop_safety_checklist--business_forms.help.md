# Automotive Shop Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Automotive Shop Safety Checklist is designed to help assess the safety compliance of an automotive shop, ensuring a safe working environment for employees and customers.

## How To Complete This Form

This form is divided into multiple sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of safety. Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. **Section 1: Inspection Details**
	* Date of inspection: Enter the date of the inspection in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`
	* Time of inspection: Enter the time of the inspection in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`
	* Inspector name: Enter the name of the inspector who performed the inspection
	* Shop Location: Enter the physical location of the shop
2. **Section 2: General Safety Areas**
	* Emergency Exits Clear and Marked: Check if the emergency exits are clear and marked
	* Fire Extinguishers Present and Accessible: Check if fire extinguishers are present and accessible
	* First Aid Kit Stocked and Accessible: Check if first aid kits are stocked and accessible
3. **Section 3: Floor and Walkway Safety**
	* Shop Floor Clean and Non-Slippery: Check if the shop floor is clean and has a non-slip surface
	* Walkways Clear of Obstacles: Check if walkways are clear of obstacles
	* Spill Cleanup Materials Available: Check if spill cleanup materials are available
4. **Section 4: Equipment Safety**
	* All Equipment in Safe Condition: Check if all equipment is in safe condition
	* Safety Guards on All Equipment: Check if safety guards are installed on all equipment
	* Vehicle Lifts Inspected and Safe: Check if vehicle lifts are inspected and safe
5. **Section 5: Personal Protective Equipment**
	* PPE Available and Accessible: Check if PPE is available and accessible
	* Eye Wash Station Available and Functional: Check if the eye wash station is available and functional
6. **Section 6: Hazardous Materials**
	* Hazardous Materials Properly Stored: Check if hazardous materials are properly stored
	* All Hazardous Materials Properly Labeled: Check if hazardous materials are properly labeled
7. **Section 7: Overall Assessment**
	* Overall Shop Safety Rating: Rate the shop's overall safety level

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date**: The date when the shop was inspected, ensuring that the shop is up-to-date with its safety compliance.
* **Inspection Time**: The time when the shop was inspected, which helps in tracking the progress of safety checks.
* **Inspector Name**: The name of the person who performed the inspection, which helps in identifying the person responsible for the inspection.
* **Shop Location**: The physical address of the shop, which helps in tracking the shop's safety compliance in different locations.
* **Emergency Exits Clear and Marked**: Check if emergency exits are clear and marked, ensuring a quick and safe exit in case of an emergency.
* **Fire Extinguisher Present and Accessible**: Check if fire extinguishers are available and accessible, helping to prevent fires and ensure safe exit routes.
* **First Aid Kit Stocked and Accessible**: Check if first aid kits are stocked and accessible, providing immediate medical attention in case of an accident.
* **Shop Floor Clean and Non-Slippery**: Check if the shop floor is clean and has a non-slip surface, reducing the risk of slips and falls.
* **Walkways Clear of Obstacles**: Check if walkways are clear of obstacles, ensuring a safe and obstacle-free walkway.
* **Spill Cleanup Materials Available**: Check if spill cleanup materials are available, helping to prevent slips and falls caused by spills.
* **All Equipment in Safe Condition**: Check if all equipment is in safe condition, ensuring that it does not pose a risk to employees and customers.
* **Safety Guards on All Equipment**: Check if safety guards are installed on all equipment, reducing the risk of accidents caused by equipment malfunctions.
* **Vehicle Lifts Inspected and Safe**: Check if vehicle lifts are inspected and safe, reducing the risk of accidents caused by defective lifts.
* **PPE Available and Accessible**: Check if PPE is available and accessible, ensuring that employees have the necessary equipment to perform their tasks safely.
* **Eye Wash Station Available and Functional**: Check if the eye wash station is available and functional, providing a safe and clean area for employees to clean their eyes.
* **Hazardous Materials Properly Stored**: Check if hazardous materials are properly stored, reducing the risk of accidents caused by unsecured materials.
* **All Hazardous Materials Properly Labeled**: Check if hazardous materials are properly labeled, ensuring that employees know the risks associated with each material.
* **Overall Shop Safety Rating**: Rate the shop's overall safety level, providing a clear picture of the shop's safety compliance.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete the form carefully and accurately, as it will help in identifying potential safety hazards and providing a clear picture of the shop's safety level.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form, ensuring that you have answered all questions truthfully.
* Use the overall safety rating to identify areas that need improvement and work towards rectifying them.
