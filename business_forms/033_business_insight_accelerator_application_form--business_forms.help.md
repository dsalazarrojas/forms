# Business Insight Accelerator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Insight Accelerator Application Form is designed to collect essential information from founders and businesses interested in joining an accelerator program. The form is used to assess and evaluate applications for the program, ensuring that the most suitable candidates are selected for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as **required** in the form.
2. Provide accurate and honest information about your business and goals.
3. Ensure that you have committed to the required time commitment for the accelerator program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Founder Name (id: 2)**: Enter the name of the founder(s) of the business. This is a required field, and it's essential to provide the correct name as it will be used for communication and program materials.
* **Current Business Stage (id: 3)**: Select the current stage of your business, which helps the accelerator team understand your business's development level. Choose from the options: Pre-revenue / Ideation, MVP / Beta Testing, Early Revenue (traction established), or Growth / Scaling.
* **Core Business Problem (id: 5)**: Describe the primary pain point your business solves. This is a required field, and it's crucial to provide a clear and concise explanation of your business's purpose.
* **Most Significant Growth Metric (id: 6)**: Choose the area where you've seen the most traction in the last 6 months. This helps the accelerator team understand your business's growth and progress.
* **Reason for Applying to Accelerator (id: 7)**: Explain why you are applying to the accelerator program now, and why this specific program is suitable for your business. This is a required field, and it's essential to provide a clear and compelling reason.
* **Goals for Accelerator Participation (id: 8)**: Select the goals you hope to achieve during the program. Choose from Mentor Access, Fundraising / Pitch Coaching, Market Validation, Product / Tech Refinement, or Networking / Partnerships.
* **Current Revenue Model (id: 9)**: Describe how your business generates income. This is a required field, and it's essential to provide a clear and accurate explanation.
* **Current Team Size (id: 10)**: Select the current size of your team, including founders and full-time staff. Choose from Founder only (1), Small team (2-5), Growing team (6-15), or Established team (16+).
* **Time Commitment (id: 12)**: Confirm that you can commit 10-15 hours per week to the accelerator program. This is a required field, and it's essential to ensure that you can dedicate the necessary time for the program.
* **Pitch Deck or Website URL (id: 13)**: Provide a link to your pitch deck or website, which demonstrates your business's online presence.
* **Application Date (id: 14)**: Enter the date you are submitting your application. This is a required field and helps the accelerator team track submissions.

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the Business Insight Accelerator Application Form successfully and increase your chances of getting accepted into the program.
