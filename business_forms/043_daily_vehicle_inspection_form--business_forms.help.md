# Daily Vehicle Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Vehicle Inspection Form is used to document and track vehicle inspections. It is used to record and store information about a vehicle's condition, registration status, and maintenance schedule.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the User Name field with your name and initials.
2.  Enter the Vehicle Make and Model fields with the corresponding information about the vehicle being inspected.
3.  Record the Year and Mileage fields with the accurate information about the vehicle's year and current mileage.
4.  Describe the current condition of the vehicle's Exterior and Interior in the respective fields.
5.  Choose the correct status from the Vehicle Registration Status and Maintenance Status fields.
6.  Add any additional comments or notes in the Notes field.
7.  Fill in the Email and Phone fields with your contact information.
8.  Record the Date and Time you completed the form in the Date and Time field.
9.  Enter the Location where the vehicle inspection took place.
10. Record the name of the user assigned to inspect the vehicle in the Assigned User field.
11. Record the name of the person who submitted the form in the Submitted By field.
12. Record the date and time the form was submitted in the Submission Date and Submission Time fields.
13. Record the current status of the submission in the Submission Status field.
14. Add any additional comments or notes in the Notes 2 field.
15. Record the date and time the submission was made in the Submission Date 2 and Submission Time 2 fields.
16. Enter the date the submission was made in the Submission Date 3 field.
17. Record the time the submission was made in the Submitted Time 3 field.
18. Add any additional comments or notes in the Notes 3 field.
