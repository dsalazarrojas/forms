# Financial Obligation Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Obligation Discharge Form is used to document the discharging of financial obligations between businesses. This form helps to ensure that all relevant details are captured, and the process is completed accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have access to the necessary permissions to complete it.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, based on your organization's financial obligations.
3. Enter the release date and time, if applicable.
4. Enter the name of the financial institution involved.
5. Review your selections and submitted data for accuracy before saving the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **business_forms** (Select Multiple): Choose whether the financial obligation includes any business forms (e.g., contracts, agreements, or invoices). Select all that apply.
* **legal_forms** (Select Multiple): Indicate which legal forms (e.g., court documents, agreements, or contracts) are involved in the financial obligation.
* **release_date** (Date): Enter the date on which the financial obligation was discharged or will be discharged.
* **release_time** (Time): Enter the time of day when the financial obligation was discharged or will be discharged.
* **financial_institution_name** (Text): Enter the name of the financial institution or organization involved in the financial obligation.

## Tips
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions to access and complete this form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you have any doubts or questions, consult the relevant authorities or contact your organization's financial department.
* Save the form after reviewing and verifying all details.
