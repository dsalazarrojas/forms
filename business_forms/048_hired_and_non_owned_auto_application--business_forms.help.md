# Hired And Non Owned Auto Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hired And Non Owned Auto Application form is used to collect information about company vehicles that are owned or hired by the company, and non-owned vehicles that have coverage. This form helps ensure that all relevant details about vehicle usage, coverage, and prior insurance are accurately and consistently recorded.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the vehicle type: Check the box next to "Yes" if the vehicle is owned or hired by the company, or "No" if it is a non-owned vehicle with coverage.
2. Determine prior coverage: Check "Yes" if the vehicle has prior insurance coverage, "No" otherwise.
3. Enter coverage start and end dates: If the vehicle has prior coverage, enter the start and end dates of that coverage.
4. Enter vehicle value and mileage: Enter the vehicle's value and annual mileage.
5. Indicate vehicle usage frequency: Check "Yes" if the vehicle is used frequently, "No" otherwise.
6. Enter vehicle details: Enter the vehicle's make, model, year, and any other relevant details.
7. Enter prior insurer information: If the vehicle had prior coverage, enter the name, phone number, and email of the prior insurer, and describe your relationship with them.
8. Enter policy and coverage deductible details: Enter the deductible amounts and currencies, and indicate whether they apply to the policy or coverage.
9. Indicate vehicle usage and insurance status: Enter any additional information about the vehicle's usage and insurance status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **vehicle_use** (Select multiple):
	+ This field indicates whether the vehicle is owned or hired by the company (Yes/No).
* **prior_coverage** (Select one):
	+ This field indicates whether the vehicle has prior insurance coverage (Yes/No).
* **coverage_start_date** (Date):
	+ Enter the start date of the vehicle's prior insurance coverage.
* **coverage_end_date** (Date):
	+ Enter the end date of the vehicle's prior insurance coverage.
* **vehicle_value** (Number):
	+ Enter the vehicle's value.
* **annual_mileage** (Number):
	+ Enter the vehicle's annual mileage.
* **vehicle_use_frequency** (Select one):
	+ Indicate whether the vehicle is used frequently (Yes/No).
* **vehicle_make** (Text):
	+ Enter the vehicle's make.
* **vehicle_model** (Text):
	+ Enter the vehicle's model.
* **vehicle_year** (Number):
	+ Enter the vehicle's year.
* **prior_insurer** (Text):
	+ Enter the name of the prior insurer.
* **prior_insurer_phone** (Text):
	+ Enter the phone number of the prior insurer.
* **prior_insurer_email** (Email):
	+ Enter the email of the prior insurer.
* **prior_insurer_relationship** (Text):
	+ Describe your relationship with the prior insurer.
* **policy_deductible** (Number):
	+ Enter the policy deductible amount.
* **policy_deductible_currency** (Text):
	+ Enter the currency of the policy deductible.
* **policy_deductible_frequency** (Select one):
	+ Indicate whether the policy deductible applies (Yes/No).
* **coverage_deductible** (Number):
	+ Enter the coverage deductible amount.
* **coverage_deductible_currency** (Text):
	+ Enter the currency of the coverage deductible.
* **coverage_deductible_frequency** (Select one):
	+ Indicate whether the coverage deductible applies (Yes/No).
* **vehicle_insured_by** (Text):
	+ Enter who insures the vehicle (e.g. the company, an individual).
* **vehicle_insured_for** (Text):
	+ Enter what the vehicle is insured for (e.g. business use, personal use).
* **vehicle_usage** (Text):
	+ Enter any additional information about the vehicle's usage.
* **vehicle_use_other** (Text):
	+ Enter any other information about the vehicle's usage.
* **vehicle_notes** (Text):
	+ Enter any additional notes about the vehicle.
