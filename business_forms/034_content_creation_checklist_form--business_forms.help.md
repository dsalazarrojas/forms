# Content Creation Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to track the progress and completion of content creation projects. It ensures that each project's stages are met and verified by its respective stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Enter the project title and ID in their respective fields.
2.  Confirm the completion status of each production stage and quality check.
3.  Select the completion status for each stage, using the "True" or "False" options.
4.  Enter the supervisor's full name and ID for sign-off.
5.  Finally, confirm the completion status of the checklist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Content Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the content project.
* **Content Project Identification Number** (`project_id`, text, required): Enter the unique ID of the content project.
* **Ideation and Research Completed?** (`ideation_research_completed`, select_one, required): Confirm if the ideation and research stage is completed.
* **Drafting and Outlining Completed?** (`drafting_outlining_completed`, select_one, required): Confirm if the drafting and outlining stage is completed.
* **Content Creation Completed?** (`content_creation_completed`, select_one, required): Confirm if the content creation stage is completed.
* **Review and Editing Completed?** (`review_editing_completed`, select_one, required): Confirm if the review and editing stage is completed.
* **Final Approval Completed?** (`final_approval_completed`, select_one, required): Confirm if the final approval stage is completed.
* **Alignment with Marketing Strategies?** (`alignment_marketing_strategies`, select_one, required): Confirm if the content is aligned with marketing strategies.
* **SEO and Readability Checked?** (`seo_readability_checked`, select_one, required): Confirm if the content's SEO and readability have been checked.
* **Brand Voice Consistency?** (`brand_voice_consistency`, select_one, required): Confirm if the content maintains brand voice consistency.
* **Supervisor Full Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the supervisor.
* **Supervisor Identification Number** (`supervisor_id`, text, required): Enter the ID of the supervisor.
* **Checklist Completed?** (`checklist_completed`, select_one, required): Confirm if the content creation checklist is completed.
