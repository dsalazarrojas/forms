<thinking>Business Quote Template - Help Guide

The purpose of this form is to create a business quote for a client, capturing essential information such as quote number, date, client details, work description, pricing, and total cost. This template is used to generate a comprehensive quote that includes all the relevant details for the client and internal stakeholders.

This help guide will walk you through how to complete the form step-by-step.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in the required fields:
    *   `Quote Number`: A unique identifier for this quote, which is essential for reference and tracking purposes.
    *   `Quote Date`: The date the quote is issued, which helps in understanding the timeline and timeline of the project or service.
    *   `Valid Until Date`: The date when the quote will expire or be valid for.
    *   `Client Name`: The name of the client or individual involved.
    *   `Client Email`: The email address of the client for communication purposes.
    *   `Client Phone Number`: The phone number of the client for contact and communication.
    *   `Client Address`: The full business address of the client.
    *   `Description of Work or Services`: A brief description of the services or work being quoted.
    *   `Expected Commencement Date`: The date when the work is expected to start.
    *   `Expected Completion Date`: The date when the work is expected to be finished.
    *   `Subtotal`: The sum of all line items before discount and tax.
    *   `Tax Rate Percentage`: The applicable tax percentage for the work or services.
    *   `Tax Amount`: The calculated tax amount.
    *   `Final Quote Amount`: The total cost of the quote after all adjustments.
    *   `Payment Terms`: The payment schedule or conditions.
2.  Add work items as applicable, including descriptions, quantities, and unit prices. This is a flexible section for listing additional items related to the work or services.
3.  Enter any applicable discount percentage, discount amount, and calculate the total cost after discount.
4.  Review and adjust the form as needed to ensure all necessary information is included and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Quote Number** (`quote_number`, `text`, required): A unique identifier for this quote, which is essential for reference and tracking purposes.
*   **Quote Date** (`quote_date`, `date`, required): The date the quote is issued, which helps in understanding the timeline and timeline of the project or service.
*   **Valid Until Date** (`valid_until_date`, `date`, required): The date when the quote will expire or be valid for.
*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): The name of the client or individual involved.
*   **Contact Person** (`client_contact`, `text`, optional): The name of the decision maker.
*   **Email Address** (`client_email`, `email`, required): The email address of the client for communication purposes.
*   **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): The phone number of the client for contact and communication.
*   **Client Address** (`client_address`, `text`, optional): The full business address of the client.
*   **Description of Work or Services** (`work_description`, `text`, required): A brief description of the services or work being quoted.
*   **Expected Commencement Date** (`commencement_date`, `date`, required): The date when the work is expected to start.
*   **Expected Completion Date** (`completion_date`, `date`, required): The date when the work is expected to be finished.
*   **Subtotal** (`subtotal`, `number`, required): The sum of all line items before discount and tax.
*   **Discount Percentage** (`discount_percentage`, `number`, optional): The optional discount percentage.
*   **Discount Amount** (`discount_amount`, `number`, optional): The dollar amount of discount.
*   **Tax Rate Percentage** (`tax_rate`, `number`, required): The applicable tax percentage for the work or services.
*   **Tax Amount** (`tax_amount`, `number`, required): The calculated tax amount.
*   **Final Quote Amount** (`total_cost`, `number`, required): The total cost of the quote after all adjustments.
*   **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, optional): The payment schedule or conditions.
