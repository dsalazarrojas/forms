# Sustainability Certification Audit Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from businesses on their sustainability practices, including their energy usage, waste management, and emissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions in each field to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to provide the required information for each field.
3. Review your responses before submission to ensure accuracy.

* **Date** (`7`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the audit.
* **Time** (`8`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the audit (optional but recommended).
* **Notes** (`9`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about the audit (e.g., comments, concerns, etc.).
* **Certification** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if you have obtained sustainability certification and 'No' otherwise.
* **Audit Type** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Specify the type of audit (e.g., energy usage, emissions, etc.).
* **Certification Type** (`12`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of sustainability certification your company has obtained (e.g., ISO 14001, etc.).
* **Audit Location** (`13`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the audit took place.
