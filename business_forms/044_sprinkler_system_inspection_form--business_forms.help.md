# Sprinkler System Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help inspectors evaluate the status and condition of a sprinkler system. It's used to gather information about the system's performance, functionality, and maintenance history. This form will help you identify any issues and provide recommendations for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions thoroughly and honestly.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each field.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **System Status (Text)**: Describe the overall status of the sprinkler system, including any issues or concerns you've observed.
* **Sprinkler System Description** (Text): Provide a brief description of the system's configuration, including its purpose, components, and installation date.
* **System Condition** (Select One): Choose the current condition of the system from the provided options (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Water Quality** (Number): Enter the current water quality reading (e.g., pH level, TDS, etc.).
* **Flow Rate** (Number): Measure and enter the current flow rate of the system (e.g., liters per minute).
* **Pump Operation** (Select Multiple): Select all the pumps that are currently operational (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Inspection Date** (Date): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name** (Text): Enter your name and/or contact information for follow-up questions or further evaluation.
 
## Tips
* Take your time to ensure the form is completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If a field is not applicable, mark it as N/A or provide a brief explanation.
* If you need more information or clarification on a field, don't hesitate to ask.
