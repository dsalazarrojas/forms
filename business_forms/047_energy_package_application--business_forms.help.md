# Energy Package Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Package Application form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in submitting an application for energy services. This form will help our team understand your energy needs and preferences, ensuring that we can provide you with the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide your contact information, including your name and email address.
3. If you need to upload any supporting documents, please use the "Document Upload" field.
4. Indicate your consent to receive energy services by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Consent" field.
5. Leave any additional comments or questions in the "Form Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data**: Enter your general information about the energy services you are interested in.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Document Upload**: If you need to upload any supporting documents, please do so here.
* **Consent**: Select "Yes" if you are willing to receive energy services and "No" if you are not.
* **Form Comments**: Leave any additional comments or questions you have about your energy needs.
