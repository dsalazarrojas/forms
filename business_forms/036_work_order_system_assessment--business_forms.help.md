This form is designed to assess the user's satisfaction with the Work Order System. It is meant for end-users to provide their feedback and improvement suggestions about the current platform. It is not a form for general support or issue reporting, so users without departmental knowledge might feel confused. It is also not designed for non-end-users, so it's not intended for people outside of the organization.

Please keep this in mind before proceeding.

# Work Order System Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and improvement ideas from end-users about the Work Order System.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the department you usually contact in the Work Order System.
2. Provide detailed feedback about your experience with the Work Order System.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the Work Order System.
4. If you have any issues with the platform, describe them in detail.
5. Share any improvement ideas you have.
6. Add any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department you usually contact in the Work Order System, such as Maintenance, Facilities, or IT.
* **Feedback form** (`feedback_form`, text, required): Provide detailed feedback about your experience with the Work Order System. This is your chance to tell us what you like or dislike about the platform.
* **How do you feel about this work order platform?** (`satisfaction_rating`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the Work Order System on a scale of 1-10.
* **What department do you usually contact?** (`satisfaction_department`, select_one, required): This is the department you usually reach out to when you have a work order issue or question.
* **What issues do you have with this work order platform?** (`issue_description`, text, required): Describe any issues or problems you have encountered with the Work Order System in detail.
* **Do you have any improvement ideas?** (`improvement_ideas`, text, required): Share any ideas you have to improve the Work Order System.
* **Any additional comments?** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
