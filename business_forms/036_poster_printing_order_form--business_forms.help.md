# Poster Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Poster Printing Order Form is used to create a new poster printing order. Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your required information, making sure to choose the correct options for your poster size, design, and shipping details.
2. Select your preferred payment method.
3. Add any additional order notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info (Customer Info)**: Please enter your name and contact information so we can reach out to you with any questions or updates about your order.
* **Poster Size (Poster Size)**: Choose the size of the poster you would like to print, from options like Large, Small, or Medium.
* **Quantity (Quantity)**: Enter the quantity of posters you would like to print.
* **Poster Design (Poster Design)**: Select one or multiple design options for your poster, such as Color, Pattern, or Image.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)**: Choose how you would like to pay for your order, such as Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Cash.
* **Shipping Address (Shipping Address)**: Enter your shipping address so we can send your printed posters to you.
* **Shipping Country (Shipping Country)**: Choose the country where you would like your order shipped to, from options like USA, Canada, Mexico, or Other Country.
* **Shipping City (Shipping City)**: Enter the city where you would like your order shipped to.
* **Shipping Zip (Shipping Zip)**: Enter your shipping zip code.
* **Order Notes (Order Notes)**: Add any additional notes or instructions about your order, such as special requests or deadlines.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your order, please contact us for assistance.
* If you need to change any part of your order, please let us know as soon as possible so we can make the necessary adjustments.
