# Overcharge Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Overcharge Complaint Form is a tool for customers to report instances of overcharging in their interactions with a company or service provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **customer_name** field.
2. Provide your email address in the **email** field.
3. Enter the date when you noticed the overcharging incident in the **overcharge_date** field.
4. Specify the amount you were overcharged for in the **amount_overcharged** field.
5. Briefly describe the incident in the **description** field.
6. If you have any supporting documents or evidence, you can attach them to the form in the **attachment** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_name**: Your name, as you would like to be known.
* **email**: Your email address for communication purposes.
* **overcharge_date**: The date when you noticed you were overcharged.
* **amount_overcharged**: The exact amount you were overcharged for.
* **description**: A clear and concise description of the incident, including any details that might be relevant to the situation.
* **attachment**: Any supporting documents or evidence related to the overcharging incident, such as receipts, invoices, or screenshots.
