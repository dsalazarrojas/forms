# pest_control_service_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to process the checkout process for a pest control service.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure efficient processing and billing of the service.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before proceeding to the next step.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (Page 1)

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): This is the first name of the customer.

### Last Name (Page 1)

* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): This is the last name of the customer.

### Email (Page 1)

* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): This is the email address of the customer.

### Phone (Page 1)

* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): This is the phone number of the customer.

### Service Date (Page 1)

* **Service Date** (`service_date`, `date`, required): This is the date on which the service was performed.

### Service Time (Page 1)

* **Service Time** (`service_time`, `time`, required): This is the time at which the service was performed.

### Payment Method (Page 1)

* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): This is the method used by the customer to make the payment (Credit, Debit, or Cash).

### Service Provider (Page 1)

* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, `text`, optional): This is the name of the service provider, if applicable.

### Notes (Page 2)

* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): This is an optional field for additional notes.

### Total Charge (Page 2)

* **Total Charge** (`total_charge`, `number`, required): This is the total charge for the service.

### Payment Amount (Page 2)

* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required): This is the amount paid by the customer.

### Balance (Page 2)

* **Balance** (`balance`, `number`, required): This is the remaining balance after the payment.

### Payment Status (Page 2)

* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required): This is the status of the payment, whether paid or partial.

### Customer Signature (Page 2)

* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, `select_multiple`, required): This is a selection of the customer's signature.

### Service Provider Signature (Page 3)

* **Service Provider Signature** (`service_provider_signature`, `select_multiple`, required): This is a selection of the service provider's signature.

## Tips

* Please double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If any required fields are left blank, the form will not proceed to the next step.
