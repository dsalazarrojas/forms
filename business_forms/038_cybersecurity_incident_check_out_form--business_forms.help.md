# cybersecurity_incident_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cybersecurity Incident Check-Out Form is used to report, track, and resolve incidents that affect the organization's IT infrastructure, systems, or data. This form provides a structured approach to documenting and investigating incidents, helping to identify and address issues efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on the "Submit" button to start the incident reporting process.
2.  Fill out the form sections below, providing as much detail as possible.
3.  Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
4.  Review and edit your report as needed before submitting it for review and resolution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the incident, including its impact and any relevant details.
* **Impact Level** (`impact_level`, select_one, required/optional): Select the severity level of the incident from the list: High, Medium, Low, Critical.
* **Affected Assets** (`affected_assets`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the assets that were affected by the incident from the list: Server, Database, Network.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of incident from the list: Phishing, Ransomware, Malware, Data Breach.
* **Incident Classification** (`incident_classification`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the classification of the incident from the list: Malware, Phishing, Ransomware, Data Breach, Insider, Outside, Unspecified.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including what happened, when, and where.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Severity** (`incident_severity`, select_one, required/optional): Select the severity level of the incident from the list: Low, Medium, High, Critical.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the incident from the list: Resolved, In Progress, New.
* **Incident Notes** (`incident_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes related to the incident.
* **Date Reported** (`incident_date_reported`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the incident was reported.
* **Time Reported** (`incident_time_reported`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the incident was reported.
* **Last Updated** (`incident_last_updated`, time, required/optional): Enter the last time the incident was updated.
* **Reported By** (`incident_reported_by`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Affected Users** (`incident_affected_users`, text, required/optional): Enter the number of users affected by the incident.
* **Affected Users Count** (`incident_affected_users_count`, number, required/optional): Enter the count of users affected by the incident.
* **Affected Groups** (`incident_affected_groups`, text, required/optional): Enter the groups affected by the incident.
* **Affected Groups Count** (`incident_affected_groups_count`, number, required/optional): Enter the count of groups affected by the incident.
* **Reporter Notes** (`incident_reporter_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes from the reporter.
* **Reviewer Notes** (`incident_reviewer_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes from the reviewer.
* **Reviewer** (`incident_reviewer`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Time** (`incident_reviewer_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the reviewer spent reviewing the incident.
* **Closed Time** (`incident_closed_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the incident was closed.

Remember, it's essential to fill out as much information as possible to ensure efficient tracking and resolution of the incident. If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to your IT team for guidance.
