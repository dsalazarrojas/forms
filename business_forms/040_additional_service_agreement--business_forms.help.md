# Additional Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Additional Service Agreement" form is used to add new services to an existing membership plan for a client.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the services you would like to add from the options provided in the "Available Services (Gym/Wellness)" section.
2. Choose the requested start date for the new services in the "Requested Start Date for New Services" field.
3. Confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions in the "Terms and Conditions" section.
4. Acknowledge the 30-day notice requirement for service cancellation in the "I acknowledge the 30-day notice requirement for service cancellation" field.
5. Agree to the liability waiver for the selected services in the "I agree to the liability waiver for the selected services" field.
6. Choose whether to use the current payment method on file or provide new payment details in the "Charge the payment method currently on file?" field.
7. Add any special requests or comments in the "Special Requests or Comments" field (optional).
8. Confirm the date of the agreement in the "Date of Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Full Name** (Required): Enter the full name of the client.
* **Member / Account ID** (Required): Enter the client's member or account ID number.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Contact Phone Number** (Required): Enter the client's contact phone number.
* **Available Services (Gym/Wellness)** (Required): Select the additional services you would like to add to your current plan.
	+ Personal Training Package (10 sessions)
	+ Nutritional Consultation / Meal Planning
	+ Group Fitness Unlimited Pass
	+ Spa and Sauna Access
	+ Locker Rental (Annual)
	+ Towel Service
* **Requested Start Date for New Services** (Required): Choose the start date for the new services.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Optional): Review and confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions.
* **I Understand and Agree** (Required): Confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions.
* **I Acknowledge** (Required): Acknowledge the 30-day notice requirement for service cancellation.
* **I Agree** (Required): Agree to the liability waiver for the selected services.
* **Yes - Use current method** (Required): Choose whether to use the current payment method on file or provide new payment details.
* **Special Requests or Comments** (Optional): Add any special requests or comments.
* **Date of Agreement** (Required): Confirm the date of the agreement.
* **I Certify and Sign** (Required): Confirm that you are the account holder or authorized representative.
