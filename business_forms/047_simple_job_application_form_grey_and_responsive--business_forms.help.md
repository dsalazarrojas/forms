# Simple Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for job applicants to submit their applications to a business. The purpose of this form is to collect basic information from applicants, including their contact information, work experience, education, skills, qualifications, references, and resume.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form on the main page, which includes contact information.
2. Scroll down to the "Work Experience" section and enter your relevant work experience.
3. Move on to the "Education" section to provide information about your educational background.
4. In the "Skills" section, list your relevant skills and experience.
5. The "Qualifications" section is for any relevant qualifications or certifications you hold.
6. In the "References" section, provide the names and contact information of your professional references.
7. Finally, upload your resume in the "Upload Resume" section.
8. Once you have completed all the sections, click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, optional)
This field is for entering your name, email address, and phone number. Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.

### **Work Experience** (Work Experience, text, optional)
Describe your previous work experience, highlighting relevant roles, industries, or skills you've used.

### **Education** (Education, text, optional)
List your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.

### **Skills** (Skills, text, optional)
Enter your relevant skills, such as programming languages, tools, or software proficiency.

### **Qualifications** (Qualifications, text, optional)
List any relevant certifications, licenses, or special qualifications you hold.

### **References** (References, text, optional)
Enter the names and contact information of your professional references, such as former managers or colleagues.

### **Resume** (Upload Resume, text, optional)
Attach a copy of your resume in PDF or Word format.

### **Submit** (Submit, text, optional)
Click this button to submit your application.
