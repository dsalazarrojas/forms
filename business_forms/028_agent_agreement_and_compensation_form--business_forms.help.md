# Agent Agreement And Compensation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a formal agreement and compensation arrangement with an agent, outlining their role, responsibilities, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the agent's full name, as per the "Agent Full Name" field.
2.  Enter the name of the agency or company, as per the "Agency or Company Name" field.
3.  Specify the effective date of the agreement, as per the "Effective Date of Agreement" field.
4.  Describe the agent's role and responsibilities in the "Detailed Description of Duties" field.
5.  Select the designated service territory or market segment for the agent's services, as per the "Designated Service Territory" field.
6.  Enter the base commission rate as a percentage, as per the "Base Commission Rate - %" field.
7.  Select the compensation frequency, such as monthly, quarterly, or bi-weekly, as per the "Compensation Frequency - Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-weekly" field.
8.  If applicable, set a minimum performance threshold to maintain the agent's status, as per the "Minimum Performance Threshold - if any" field.
9.  Describe any additional bonus or incentive terms, as per the "Additional Bonus or Incentive Terms" field.
10.  Review and agree to the terms and conditions by checking the "By submitting this form, I agree to the terms and conditions outlined above" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Agent Full Name** (`agent_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the agent.
*   **Agency or Company Name** (`agency_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the agency or company.
*   **Effective Date of Agreement** (`effective_date`, `date`, required): Specify the date on which the agreement becomes effective.
*   **Designated Service Territory** (`service_territory`, `text`, required): Describe the geographic area or market segment where the agent will provide services.
*   **Detailed Description of Duties** (`duties_description`, `text`, required): Provide a comprehensive description of the agent's responsibilities.
*   **Base Commission Rate - %** (`commission_rate`, `number`, required): Enter the percentage of sales or revenue the agent will receive as commission.
*   **Compensation Frequency - Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-weekly** (`payment_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select the frequency at which the agent will be compensated.
*   **Minimum Performance Threshold - if any** (`performance_threshold`, `number`, optional): Set a threshold for the agent to maintain their status.
*   **Additional Bonus or Incentive Terms** (`incentive_terms`, `text`, optional): Describe any performance-based rewards or bonus terms for the agent.
*   **By submitting this form, I agree to the terms and conditions outlined above** (`agreement_statement`, `note`, required): Review and agree to the terms and conditions outlined in the form.
