# research_lab_partnership_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about potential research partnership opportunities. It allows users to provide details about themselves and their contact information for multiple potential partners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information in the first section.
2. Provide information about the first potential partner, including their name, email, organization, and best time to contact.
3. Repeat steps 1-2 for each additional potential partner, filling in the relevant fields as necessary.
4. Add any additional comments or information in the text fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Your name for internal reference purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Research Area** (`research_area`, text, optional): The area of research you are interested in partnering on.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): The name of your organization or institution.
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): Any additional comments or information you would like to share.
* **Name of Contact** (`name_of_contact`, text, optional): The name of the contact person for the potential partner.
* **Email of Contact** (`email_of_contact`, email, optional): The email address of the contact person for the potential partner.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the contact person for the potential partner.
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time_to_contact`, time, optional): The best time of day to contact the potential partner.
* **Research Area 2** (`research_area_2`, text, optional): The area of research for the second potential partner.
* **Organization 2** (`organization_2`, text, optional): The name of the organization or institution for the second potential partner.
* **Message 2** (`message_2`, note, optional): Any additional comments or information for the second potential partner.
* **Name of Contact 2** (`name_of_contact_2`, text, optional): The name of the contact person for the second potential partner.
* **Email of Contact 2** (`email_of_contact_2`, email, optional): The email address of the contact person for the second potential partner.
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`, text, optional): The phone number of the contact person for the second potential partner.
* **Best Time to Contact 2** (`best_time_to_contact_2`, time, optional): The best time of day to contact the second potential partner.
* **Research Area 3** (`research_area_3`, text, optional): The area of research for the third potential partner.
* **Organization 3** (`organization_3`, text, optional): The name of the organization or institution for the third potential partner.
* **Message 3** (`message_3`, note, optional): Any additional comments or information for the third potential partner.
* **Name of Contact 3** (`name_of_contact_3`, text, optional): The name of the contact person for the third potential partner.
* **Email of Contact 3** (`email_of_contact_3`, email, optional): The email address of the contact person for the third potential partner.
* **Phone 3** (`phone_3`, text, optional): The phone number of the contact person for the third potential partner.
* **Best Time to Contact 3** (`best_time_to_contact_3`, time, optional): The best time of day to contact the third potential partner.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): The date for which the partnership is being inquired about.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): The time of day for which the partnership is being inquired about.
