# taylor_attili_photography - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Taylor Attili Photography form is a comprehensive contract form designed to capture the details of a wedding photography service. This form is intended to outline the terms and conditions of the service, including the photographer's and client's information, services offered, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Photographer's Name" field with your full name.
2. Enter the "Client Name" field with your client's name.
3. Select the "Wedding Date" field with the date of the wedding.
4. Choose the "Package Selected" field with the chosen package (Basic, Deluxe, or Platinum).
5. Select the services you wish to offer from the "Services Selected" field (Portrait, Engagement, or Wedding).
6. Enter your "Contact Number" field with a valid phone number.
7. Enter your "Email" field with a valid email address.
8. Sign the "Signature" field with your handwritten signature.
9. Review and sign the "Review and Sign" field with your acceptance or decline of the contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographer's Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as the photographer.
* **Client Name** (text, optional): Enter your client's name.
* **Wedding Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the wedding.
* **Ceremony Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the ceremony.
* **Ceremony Location** (text, optional): Enter the location of the ceremony.
* **Reception Location** (text, optional): Enter the location of the reception.
* **Package Selected** (select_one, optional): Select the chosen package (Basic, Deluxe, or Platinum).
* **Services Selected** (select_multiple, optional): Select the services you wish to offer (Portrait, Engagement, or Wedding).
* **Contact Number** (text, optional): Enter a valid phone number.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Signature** (note, optional): Sign with your handwritten signature.
* **Review** (text, optional): Enter a review or comments.
* **Review Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the review.
* **Reviewer's Signature** (text, optional): Sign with your handwritten signature.
* **Payment Terms** (text, optional): Enter payment terms or conditions.
* **Payment Terms Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of payment terms.
* **Payment Terms Amount** (number, optional): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Terms Method** (select_one, optional): Select the payment method (Check, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Terms Terms** (text, optional): Enter any additional payment terms.
* **Client's Signature** (text, optional): Sign with your handwritten signature.
* **Photographer's Signature** (text, optional): Sign with your handwritten signature.
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter the current date.
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter the current time.
* **Contract Review** (select_one, optional): Select "Accept" or "Decline" to review and sign the contract.
* **Contract Review Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the contract review.
