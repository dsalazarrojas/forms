# marketResearchIntakeForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a market research project. It's essential to complete it accurately to ensure that the project starts on the right foot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your customer's name in the "customerName" field.
2.  Enter your customer's phone number in the "phoneNumber" field.
3.  Provide your customer's email address in the "email" field.
4.  Describe the research question you want to investigate in the "researchQuestion" field.
5.  Add any relevant comments or observations about the project in the "comments" field.
6.  Choose whether a researcher has been assigned to the project ("assignedResearcher") or select multiple options if it's a joint assignment.
7.  Select the date of the research project from the calendar ("dateOfResearch").
8.  Choose the time of the research project from the time picker ("timeOfResearch").
9.  Enter the research location, e.g., city, state, or region ("researchLocation").
10. Describe the research objective, what you aim to achieve ("researchObjective").
11. Explain the research method, e.g., survey, interview, or focus group ("researchMethod").
12. Add any additional research notes ("researchNotes").
13. Enter the customer's contact information, e.g., phone number, email, or address ("customerContact").
14. Enter the customer's address if applicable ("customerAddress").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Customer Name** (`customerName`, text): Enter your customer's name.
*  **Phone Number** (`phoneNumber`, text): Enter your customer's phone number.
*  **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your customer's email address.
*  **Research Question** (`researchQuestion`, text): Describe the research question you want to investigate.
*  **Comments** (`comments`, text): Add any comments or observations about the project.
*  **Assigned Researcher** (`assignedResearcher`, select_one): Choose whether a researcher has been assigned to the project.
*  **Assigned Researcher Options** (`assignedResearcherOptions`, select_multiple): Select multiple options if it's a joint assignment.
*  **Date Of Research** (`dateOfResearch`, date): Select the date of the research project from the calendar.
*  **Time Of Research** (`timeOfResearch`, time): Choose the time of the research project from the time picker.
*  **Research Location** (`researchLocation`, text): Enter the research location, e.g., city, state, or region.
*  **Research Objective** (`researchObjective`, text): Describe the research objective, what you aim to achieve.
*  **Research Method** (`researchMethod`, text): Explain the research method, e.g., survey, interview, or focus group.
*  **Research Notes** (`researchNotes`, text): Add any additional research notes.
*  **Customer Contact** (`customerContact`, text): Enter the customer's contact information, e.g., phone number, email, or address.
*  **Customer Address** (`customerAddress`, text): Enter the customer's address if applicable.

## Tips
*  Please fill in the form carefully and thoroughly to avoid errors and ensure accurate data collection.
*  If you're unsure about any field, refer back to the form's purpose and instructions.
*  Double-check your selections in "Assigned Researcher" and "Assigned Researcher Options" fields to ensure accuracy.
*  Use the "Research Location" field to specify the location where the research will take place.
