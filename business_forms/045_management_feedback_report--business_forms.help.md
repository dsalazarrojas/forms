# Management Feedback Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Management Feedback Report is a form used by managers to provide feedback on their direct reports or team members. It helps to identify areas of concern and improvement, and to establish a clear understanding of the manager's perspective on their team members' performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Management Feedback Report, follow these steps:

1. Select the report date for the feedback.
2. Enter the manager's name and title.
3. Answer the questions about the manager, providing any relevant information or comments.
4. Rate the staff motivation level on a scale of 1-10.
5. Identify areas of concern for the team member.
6. Provide any additional comments or feedback.
7. Rate the next steps needed to address the areas of concern.
8. Rate if staff comments were provided.
9. Identify areas for improvement for the team member.
10. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, required: false): Select the date for which the feedback is being provided. This will help to track feedback over time.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the manager providing the feedback.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the manager providing the feedback.
* **Questions about manager** (`questions_about_manager`, note, required: false): This field is for any additional comments or questions the manager wants to ask about the team member.
* **Staff Motivation** (`staff_motivation`, number, required: false): Rate the staff motivation level on a scale of 1-10.
* **Areas of Concern** (`areas_of_concern`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any areas of concern for the team member, such as performance, attendance, or communication.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback for the team member.
* **Follow up** (`follow_up`, date, required: false): Select a date for any follow-up actions or meetings.
* **Manager Behavior** (`manager_behavior`, text, required: false): Describe the manager's behavior or actions taken regarding the team member.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, select_one, required: false): Select the next steps to be taken, such as training, coaching, or performance improvement plans.
* **Staff Comments** (`staff_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any comments from the team member in response to the feedback.
* **Areas of Improvement** (`areas_of_improvement`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any areas for improvement for the team member, such as communication, teamwork, or leadership skills.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct report date to avoid duplication of effort.
- Be specific and concrete when providing feedback or comments.
- Use the areas of concern and areas of improvement sections to identify specific areas that need attention.
- Ensure that the next steps are clearly defined and achievable.
- Review and update the form as needed to reflect changes in the team member's performance or progress.
