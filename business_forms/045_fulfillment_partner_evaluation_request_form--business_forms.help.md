# Fulfillment Partner Evaluation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fulfillment Partner Evaluation Request Form is a tool used to gather information from our fulfillment partners about their services, capabilities, and performance. This form is essential for us to assess and improve our partnerships with them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please ensure that you have a valid contact email address and phone number.
2.  Select one service that you offer from the list provided (Service A, Service B, or Service C).
3.  Specify your services in the text field.
4.  Choose the capabilities that your partner offers (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
5.  Provide any additional comments about your services and performance.
6.  Rate your overall performance on a date that reflects your current capabilities and service offerings.
7.  Review your submission to ensure that you have provided all the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: Please provide your first name in this text field.
*   **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this text field.
    *   **Note**: This field is optional, but please fill in your last name if you have one.
*   **Contact Email**: Enter the email address we can use to contact you.
*   **Contact Phone**: Provide your contact phone number.
*   **Partner Name**: If you are representing a company, enter the name of your company.
*   **Services**: Choose one service that you offer from the list provided (Service A, Service B, or Service C). Please be specific about the services you provide.
*   **Services Details**: Specify the details of your services in this text field.
*   **Capabilities**: Choose the capabilities that your partner offers (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3). Be specific about what you can do.
*   **Evaluation Comments**: If you want to add any additional comments or information, please provide them in this text field.
*   **Evaluation Rating**: Rate your overall performance on a date that reflects your current capabilities and service offerings.

**Tips**

*   Please ensure that all fields are completed before submitting.
*   Review your submission to ensure that it is accurate and complete.
*   If you have any issues or concerns, contact us so we can assist you.
