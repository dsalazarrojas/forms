# Tax Classification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Classification Form is used to classify and categorize a vendor's tax status, type, rate, and other related information. This form helps to ensure accurate and efficient tax reporting and classification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions and options provided for each field.
2. Select the correct answers based on the vendor's current status, type, and rate.
3. Complete each field according to your findings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Status**: Select the current status of the vendor, which can be either Active or Inactive.
* **Classification Type**: Choose whether the vendor is a Yes or No classification type.
* **Tax Type**: Select whether the vendor's tax type is Yes or No.
* **Tax Rate**: Select the tax rate for the vendor, which can be either Good or Fair.
* **Other Tax Rate**: If the vendor has an other tax rate, select Good or Fair.
* **Vendor Tax Status**: Select the current tax status of the vendor, which can be either Active or Inactive.
* **Contractor Status**: Choose whether the vendor is a contractor, which can be either Active or Inactive.
* **Tax Classification**: Select whether the vendor's tax classification is Yes or No.
* **Other Tax Status**: If the vendor has an other tax status, select Active or Inactive.
* **Business Type**: Choose multiple business types for the vendor, which can be either Yes or No.
* **Business Address**: Enter the vendor's business address.
* **Business Phone**: Enter the vendor's business phone number.
* **Business Email**: Enter the vendor's business email address.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have accurate and up-to-date information about the vendor before completing the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the vendor's previous tax classification or contact the vendor directly.
* Double-check your answers to ensure they accurately reflect the vendor's current status and type.
