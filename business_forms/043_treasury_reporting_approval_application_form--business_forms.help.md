# Treasury Reporting Approval Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Reporting Approval Application Form is a validation and approval process for treasury reports submitted by employees or departments. It allows managers and administrators to review and approve or reject reports based on certain criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
This form consists of multiple pages. Please complete each page carefully, as each field is designed to ensure accurate and efficient approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **report_details** (select_multiple, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the report is complete and accurate.
    *   If 'Yes', indicate that the report is complete and accurate.
    *   If 'No', explain why the report is not complete or accurate.
*   **supporting_documents** (text, optional): Attach any supporting documents that are relevant to the report, such as receipts, invoices, or other evidence.
*   **submission_details** (text, optional): Provide any additional details or context about the report, such as the purpose of the report or the expected outcome.
*   **first_page** (text, optional): This field is used to capture any initial comments or feedback about the report.

## Tips
- Ensure you have all required documents and information before submitting this form.
- Please submit one form per report.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your manager or the financial department for guidance.
- Please proofread your form carefully before submission to avoid any errors.
