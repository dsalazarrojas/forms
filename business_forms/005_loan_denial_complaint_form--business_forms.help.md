# Loan Denial Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loan Denial Complaint Form is used to file a complaint when a loan application is denied. This form helps us understand the reason for the denial and gather additional details to aid in revising our decision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your loan application ID to help us identify your case.
2. Describe the reason for the loan denial, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Enter your name, which will be used to contact you regarding your complaint.
4. Provide your email address to ensure we can reach you regarding your complaint.
5. Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted via phone.
6. Finally, type your message, explaining your complaint in as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **loan_denial_complaint_form**: A text field where you can describe your complaint in detail.
* **loan_application_id**: Enter the ID number assigned to your loan application to help us identify your case.
* **reason_for_denial**: Explain the reason why your loan application was denied.
* **name**: This is your name, which we'll use to contact you about your complaint.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to reach you regarding your complaint.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number, if you'd like us to contact you via phone.
* **message**: Write a detailed message explaining your complaint and any relevant details.

## Tips
Please ensure that you've filled out all required fields accurately and truthfully. If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
