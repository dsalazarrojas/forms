# Train Freight Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Train Freight Inspection Form is used to gather information about the train freight being inspected. This form is used by train operators to report on the condition of the freight, including its train operator information, train car details, and safety standards compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the first page, which asks for information about the freight train. This page is optional and can be skipped if not necessary.
2. Proceed to the second page, which requires you to provide your train operator information. This includes selecting the correct train operator details from the available options.
3. On the third page, you will be asked to select the train car details. Please choose all applicable options from the list.
4. On the fourth page, select the applicable safety standards compliance from the list. This is a required field, so make sure to choose one of the options.
5. Finally, on the fifth page, you can add any additional notes or comments about the freight train inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Train Operator Information** (`train_operator_information`, `number`, required): This field is used to select the train operator information. Please choose the correct train operator details from the available options.
* **Train Car Details** (`train_car_details`, `select_multiple`, required): This field allows you to select multiple train car details. Please choose all applicable options from the list.
* **Safety Standards** (`safety_standards`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to select the applicable safety standards compliance. Please choose one of the options from the list.
* **Notes and Comments** (`notes_and_comments`, `note`, optional): This field is used to add any additional notes or comments about the freight train inspection.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with the relevant personnel or the form designer.
- The form is designed to be completed by train operators. If you are not a train operator, please do not complete this form.
