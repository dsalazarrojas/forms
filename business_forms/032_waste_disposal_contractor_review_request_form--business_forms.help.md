# Waste Disposal Contractor Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Waste Disposal Contractor Review Request Form" is used to collect feedback and reviews for waste disposal contractors. This form helps facility managers evaluate and assess the service quality and performance of these contractors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the provided options and select the correct answers for each field.
2. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
3. Provide detailed review notes in the "Review Notes" field.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Teams (\*)**: Choose "Yes" if the waste disposal contractor's service was provided by a facility team. Choose "No" otherwise.
* **Facility Name (\*)**: Enter the name of the facility where the waste disposal service was provided.
* **Contractor (\*)**: Choose "Yes" if a contractor was involved in the waste disposal service. Choose "No" otherwise.
* **Service Quality (\*)**: Rate the quality of service provided by the contractor. Choose between "Good" and "Poor".
* **Performance over time (\*)**: Select the date of the performance review. This field is not currently used in the form, but it might be in the future. You can leave it empty.
* **Rating (\*)**: Enter a numerical rating of the service provided by the contractor.
* **Review Notes (\*)**: Provide detailed notes about your experience with the contractor. This field is meant for any additional comments or feedback you may have.
