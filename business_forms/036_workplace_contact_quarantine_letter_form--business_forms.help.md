# Workplace Contact Quarantine Letter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Contact Quarantine Letter Form is a document used to inform employees who have been quarantined due to potential exposure to COVID-19. This form provides a template for gathering essential information to create a comprehensive quarantine letter that can be used to communicate with the employee and the employer.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in the employee's name, job title, and contact information.
- Enter the date of exposure.
- Describe the symptoms experienced by the employee.
- Specify the risk of exposure.
- Provide the employer's name and address.
- Enter the date the letter was sent.
- Provide the contact person's name, phone number, and email.
- Obtain the employee's signature to confirm their understanding of the quarantine procedures.
- Obtain the employer's signature to confirm their understanding of the quarantine procedures.
- Enter the date the employee is expected to return to work.
- Provide a message to the contact person.
- If necessary, enter additional contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (1): Enter the name of the employee who has been quarantined.
* **Job Title** (2): Enter the job title of the employee (optional).
* **Contact Information** (3): Enter the employee's contact information, such as phone number and email.
* **Work Location** (4): Enter the work location of the employee.
* **Exposure Date** (5): Enter the date the employee was exposed to COVID-19.
* **Symptoms** (6): Describe any symptoms experienced by the employee (optional).
* **Exposure Risk** (7): Specify the risk of exposure (text field).
* **Quarantine Procedures** (8): Enter any quarantine procedures to be followed by the employee (optional).
* **Employer Name** (9): Enter the name of the employer.
* **Employer Address** (10): Enter the address of the employer (optional).
* **Date Letter Sent** (11): Enter the date the quarantine letter was sent (optional).
* **Contact Person** (12): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Phone Number** (13): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email** (14): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Employee Signature** (15): Obtain the employee's signature to confirm their understanding of the quarantine procedures.
* **Employer Signature** (16): Obtain the employer's signature to confirm their understanding of the quarantine procedures.
* **Date Quarantine End** (17): Enter the date the employee is expected to return to work (optional).
* **Contact Message** (18): Provide a message to the contact person (optional).
* **Date Employee Last Presented** (19): Enter the last date the employee was presented with the quarantine letter (optional).
* **Last Date Presented** (20): Enter the last date the quarantine letter was presented to the employee (optional).
* **Last Employee Presented** (21): Enter the date the employee last understood the quarantine procedures (optional).
* **Last Updated** (22): Enter the date the contact information was last updated (optional).
* **Next Update** (23): Enter the date the quarantine procedures are next to be reviewed or updated (optional).
* **Employer Signature 2** (24): Obtain the employer's signature on a second page (optional).
* **Signature Line** (25): A text field for any further comments or signature (optional).

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Review the form for any additional information that may be required for the employee and employer.
- Keep the employee and employer informed throughout the quarantine process.
- Update the form regularly to reflect any changes in quarantine procedures or contact information.
