# Janitorial Services Performance Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the performance of janitorial services provided by a company. It is intended to be completed by the person who received the services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide a description of the evaluation in the Evaluation field.
3. Rate your experience with the janitorial services provided.
4. Select one category that best describes your experience.
5. Choose the janitorial services you received from the provided options.
6. Enter any additional comments about your experience.
7. Enter any additional comments about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number. Please enter your name in this field as it will help the company to contact you for follow-up questions.
* **Evaluation** (`Evaluation`, text, required/optional): This field is a description of the evaluation of the janitorial services you received.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required/optional): This field is not clearly labeled, so it is assumed that it is a placeholder for your name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): This field is used to collect your email address.
* **Phone number** (`Phone number`, number, required/optional): This field is used to collect your phone number.
* **Date of visit** (`Date of visit`, date, required/optional): This field is used to collect the date of the visit when you received the janitorial services.
* **Time of visit** (`Time of visit`, time, required/optional): This field is used to collect the time of the visit when you received the janitorial services.
* **Rating** (`Rating`, number, required/optional): This field is used to rate your experience with the janitorial services on a scale.
* **Categories** (`Categories`, select_one, required/optional): This field is used to select one category that best describes your experience.
* **Janitorial services** (`Janitorial services`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is used to select the janitorial services you received.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, required/optional): This field is used to enter any comments about your experience with the janitorial services.
* **Additional comments** (`Additional comments`, note, required/optional): This field is used to enter any additional comments about your experience.

## Tips

* Please fill out this form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you have any issues or problems with the form, please contact the company's support team.
* This form is designed to be completed online, so ensure that you have a stable internet connection.
* If you are filling out this form multiple times, please do not enter duplicate information.
