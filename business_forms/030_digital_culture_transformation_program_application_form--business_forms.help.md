# Digital Culture Transformation Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Culture Transformation Program Application Form is a form designed to collect information from applicants who are interested in undergoing digital transformation within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details, including your first name, last name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the job function questions.
3.  Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the department questions.
4.  Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the job classification questions.
5.  Select the job category that best describes your job.
6.  Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' for the job status.
7.  Select multiple job families and job functions that best describe your role.
8.  Ensure that you have filled all the required fields and review your answers carefully before submission.
