# Company Payment Card Renewal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a payment card renewal for a company employee. It is essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and authorization before submitting the form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Make sure you have manager's approval before submitting the form, as indicated by field 5.
4. Submit the form for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee name** (`employee_name`, text, required):
	+ This field is for the employee's full name as it appears on the payment card.
* **Card last 4 digits** (`card_number_last4`, text, required):
	+ Enter the last 4 digits of the payment card number for identification purposes.
* **Reason for renewal** (`reason_for_renewal`, select_one, required):
	+ Select the reason for renewing the payment card from the list:
		- Expired
		- Damaged
		- Lost
		- Change of employee
* **Requested card limit** (`requested_limit`, number, optional):
	+ If you wish to request a change in card limit, enter the new proposed limit. Otherwise, leave blank.
* **Manager approval name** (`manager_approval`, text, required):
	+ Enter your manager's name to approve the request.
