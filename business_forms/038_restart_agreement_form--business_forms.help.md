# Restart Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Restart Agreement Form is used to document and verify team member agreements on restarts. It's a crucial tool for team leaders or managers to gather information on team member restarts, including reasons for restarts, restart dates, and follow-up dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure all required fields are filled out by the team leader or manager.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, paying attention to the "Select at least one" hint for fields with multiple options.
3. Enter the restart date and follow-up date accurately.
4. Provide a clear reason for the restart.
5. Sign the form to confirm agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Restart Agreement Form** (`restart_agreement_form`): This is the title of the form.
* **Select Team** (`team_members`): This field allows you to select which team members are affected by the restart. You can choose multiple team members by selecting each one individually. Note that this field is optional.
* **Select Approvals** (`approval_status`): This field allows you to select at least one approval status, which indicates whether the team member is active or inactive. You can select multiple approvals by selecting each one individually.
* **Restart Date** (`restart_date`): Enter the date when the team member's restart occurred.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`): Enter the date for the follow-up on the team member's restart.
* **Team Leader** (`team_lead`): This field is used to specify the team leader's name. It's optional, but it's useful for tracking purposes.
* **Reason for Restart** (`restart_reason`): Enter a clear reason for the team member's restart.
* **Company** (`company_name`): This field is used to specify the company name. It's optional, but it's useful for tracking purposes.
* **Manager** (`manager_name`): This field is used to specify the manager's name. It's optional, but it's useful for tracking purposes.
* **Signature** (`restart_agreement_form_signature`): This field is used to sign the form, confirming agreement on the restart.
* **Comments** (`restart_agreement_form_comments`): This field is used for additional comments on the restart.
* **Approval Status** (`restart_agreement_form_approval_status_2`): This field allows you to select at least one approval status, which indicates whether the team member is active or inactive. You can select multiple approvals by selecting each one individually.
