## Purpose
The Restart Agreement Form is used to document and verify team member agreements on restarts. It's a crucial tool for team leaders or managers to gather information on team member restarts, including reasons for restarts, restart dates, and follow-up dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure all required fields are filled out by the team leader or manager.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, paying attention to the "Select at least one" hint for fields with multiple options.
3. Enter the restart date and follow-up date accurately.
4. Provide a clear reason for the restart.
5. Sign the form to confirm agreement.
