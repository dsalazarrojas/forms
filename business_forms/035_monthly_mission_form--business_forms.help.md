# monthly_mission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The monthly mission form is used to capture the monthly mission and objectives of a project or task. This information is essential for tracking progress, identifying areas of improvement, and making informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief and concise mission statement in the **Mission Statement** field.
2. Select all the applicable objectives for your project or task from the **Objectives** dropdown menu.
3. Choose the current status of your key results from the **Key Results** dropdown menu.
4. Choose the current status of your action items from the **Action Items** dropdown menu.
5. Enter the progress percentage of your project or task in the **Progress** field.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about your project or task in the **Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mission Statement** (`title`, `text`, required): This field is used to enter a brief and concise statement of your project or task's mission. Please make sure to keep it short and to the point.
* **Objectives** (`objectives`, `select_multiple`, required): This field allows you to select all the applicable objectives for your project or task. Please choose the options that best describe your objectives.
* **Key Results** (`key_results`, `select_multiple`, required): This field shows the current status of your key results. Please select the option that best describes your current status.
* **Action Items** (`action_items`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field shows the current status of your action items. Please select the option that best describes your current status.
* **Progress** (`progress`, `number`, required): This field is used to enter the progress percentage of your project or task. Please enter a whole number between 0 and 100.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): This field is used to add any additional notes or comments about your project or task.
