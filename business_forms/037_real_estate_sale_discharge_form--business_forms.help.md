# Real Estate Sale Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Sale Discharge Form is used to record the details of a real estate sale, including sale details, seller and buyer information, property address, mortgage details, and discharge information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the sale details, including the date of the sale, in the "Sale Details" section.
2. Fill in the seller and buyer information in the "Seller Info" and "Buyer Info" sections, respectively.
3. Enter the property address in the "Property Address" section.
4. Provide the mortgage details, if applicable, in the "Mortgage Info" section.
5. Enter the discharge information, if applicable, in the "Discharge Info" section.
6. Sign the form to confirm that you are the individual responsible for discharging the property.
7. Enter the date of the sale in the "Date Sold" section.
8. Enter the sale price of the property in the "Sale Price" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sale Details** (`sale_details`, text, optional): This section is for entering any additional details about the sale, such as any special conditions or notes.
* **Seller Info** (`seller_info`, text, optional): This field is for entering the information about the seller, such as their name, contact details, and any relevant notes.
* **Buyer Info** (`buyer_info`, text, optional): This field is for entering the information about the buyer, such as their name, contact details, and any relevant notes.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): This field is for entering the address of the property that was sold.
* **Mortgage Info** (`mortgage_info`, text, optional): This field is for entering any mortgage details, such as the mortgage amount, interest rate, and repayment terms.
* **Discharge Info** (`discharge_info`, text, optional): This field is for entering any discharge information, such as the discharge date and method.
* **Sign** (`signature`, text, optional): This field is for signing and dating the form to confirm that you are the individual responsible for discharging the property.
* **Date Sold** (`date_sold`, date, optional): This field is for entering the date on which the property was sold.
* **Sale Price** (`sale_price`, number, optional): This field is for entering the sale price of the property.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and consistently.
* This form is not intended for multiple sales, so each sale should be recorded on a separate form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the real estate agent or attorney handling the sale.
