# Service Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Evaluation Form is designed to collect user feedback and evaluations on a specific service, helping us to improve the quality and customer satisfaction of our programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Read each field description carefully to understand what information is required.
- Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
- Use the available options for select fields to choose the most suitable answer.
- Leave notes or comments in the "additional_comments" field for any additional information.
- Submit your evaluation and suggestions once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name, so we can address your evaluation personally.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required/optional): If you are filling this form on behalf of your company, please enter your company's name.
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the course or service you evaluated.
* **Telephone** (`telephone`, text, required/optional): Enter your telephone number, in case we need to contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address, so we can contact you for any further questions.
* **Facilitator Name** (`facilitator_name`, text, required/optional): If there was a facilitator or instructor for the course, enter their name.
* **Knowledge of Subject Matter** (`knowledge_of_subject_matter`, number, required/optional): Please rate your level of knowledge on the subject matter on a scale from 1-5.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required/optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving the program.
* **Recommend** (`recommend`, select_one, required/optional): Answer the question "Would you recommend this course to your colleagues?" with the options "I would", "False", or "I do not know".
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required/optional): Leave any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service on a scale from 1-5.
