# Keys And Rugs Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about keys and rugs in the inventory. It's essential to provide accurate and complete details to ensure accurate tracking and management of these items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct category for the item (if applicable).
2. Enter the number of keys or rugs you have.
3. Provide a brief description of the item.
4. Enter the quantity of the item.
5. Enter the price of the item.
6. Enter the weight of the item (if applicable).
7. Enter the dimensions of the item (if applicable).
8. Enter the size of the item (if applicable).
9. Indicate the condition of the item (if applicable).
10. Select the material of the item (if applicable).
11. Indicate the location of the item (if applicable).
12. Enter any relevant tags or notes about the item.
13. Enter the phone number of the item's owner or contact (if applicable).
14. Enter the email of the item's owner or contact (if applicable).
15. Enter the address of the item's owner or contact (if applicable).
16. Select the status of the item (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the correct category for the item from the available options. This will help sort and filter the inventory.
* **Keys** (`keys`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total number of keys or rugs you have.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the item, including any relevant details about its material, color, or other distinctive features.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required: false): Enter the quantity of the item.
* **Price** (`price`, `number`, required: false): Enter the price of the item.
* **Weight** (`weight`, `number`, required: false): Enter the weight of the item (if applicable).
* **Dimensions** (`dimensions`, `text`, required: false): Enter the dimensions of the item (if applicable).
* **Size** (`size`, `text`, required: false): Enter the size of the item (if applicable).
* **Condition** (`condition`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the condition of the item. Choose 'Yes' for items in good condition, and 'No' for items that are damaged or in need of repair.
* **Color** (`color`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the color(s) of the item (if applicable).
* **Material** (`material`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the material(s) of the item (if applicable).
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the item (if applicable).
* **Tags** (`tags`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant tags or notes about the item.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the item's owner or contact (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email of the item's owner or contact (if applicable).
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the item's owner or contact (if applicable).
* **Status** (`status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the status of the item. Choose 'Active' for items that are currently in use, and 'Inactive' for items that are no longer in use.
* **Image URL** (`image_url`, `text`, required: false): Enter the URL of an image related to the item (if applicable).
* **Image Path** (`image_path`, `text`, required: false): Enter the path to an image file related to the item (if applicable).
