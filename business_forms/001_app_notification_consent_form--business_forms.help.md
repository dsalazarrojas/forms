# App Notification Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect users' notification preferences, enabling them to customize how they receive notifications from the application. It's essential that users provide their information accurately to ensure they receive the right types of notifications at the right time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your full name in the **Full Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email Address** field. This will be used to send you notifications.
3. If you'd like to receive SMS notifications, enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
4. Choose which types of notifications you'd like to receive by selecting all applicable options in the **Which types of notifications would you like to receive?** field.
5. Decide if you want to allow push notifications on your mobile device by selecting **Yes, enable push notifications** or **No, disable push notifications** in the **Allow push notifications on mobile devices?** field.
6. Choose how often you'd like to receive notifications by selecting one of the options in the **How often would you like to receive notifications?** field.
7. Select your preferred notification time by choosing one of the options in the **Preferred notification time** field.
8. Decide on your preferred communication channel by selecting one of the options in the **What is your preferred communication channel?** field.
9. Consent to data analysis by selecting **I consent to data analysis**.
10. Accept notifications for specific purposes by selecting all applicable options in the **Accept notifications for the following purposes?** field.
11. Finally, acknowledge that you have read and agreed to the notification policy by selecting **I agree**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (Required)**: Please enter your complete name, as it will be used to address notifications to you.
* **Email Address (Required)**: Enter your email address, which will be used to send you notifications.
* **Phone Number (Optional)**: If you'd like to receive SMS notifications, enter your phone number here.
* **Which types of notifications would you like to receive? (Required)**: Select all types of notifications you'd like to receive, such as product updates or promotions.
* **Allow push notifications on mobile devices? (Required)**: Decide if you want to receive push notifications on your mobile device.
* **How often would you like to receive notifications? (Required)**: Choose how often you'd like to receive notifications, such as daily or weekly.
* **Preferred notification time (Optional)**: Select your preferred time for receiving notifications.
* **What is your preferred communication channel? (Required)**: Choose your preferred method of communication, such as email or SMS.
* **May we use your data to improve notification relevance? (Required)**: Consent to data analysis to send more targeted notifications.
* **Accept notifications for the following purposes? (Required)**: Select all purposes for which you'd like to receive notifications, such as service improvements or payment confirmations.
* **I acknowledge that I have read and agreed to the notification policy (Required)**: Confirm that you've read and agreed to the notification policy.
* **Additional preferences or comments (Optional)**: If you have any special requests or additional preferences, enter them here.
