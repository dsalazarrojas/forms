# mrr_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The mrr_evaluation_form is a critical tool for evaluating the financial performance of a business or organization. It helps in understanding the revenue streams, growth rate, and other key metrics that contribute to the overall MRR.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the relevant period for the evaluation, specifying the month and year for which you want to evaluate the MRR.
2. Indicate whether the organization has revenue streams during the specified period.
3. Enter the total revenue for the specified period.
4. Calculate the churn rate, which is the percentage of lost customers over the specified period.
5. Evaluate the MRR growth, which is the rate at which the revenue has increased or decreased over the specified period.
6. Provide next steps that will be taken for improving the MRR, based on the insights gained from the evaluation.
7. Identify growth drivers, such as marketing initiatives, product development, or pricing strategies, that contribute to the MRR growth.
8. If required, enter relevant action items, such as specific actions or tasks that need to be performed to improve the MRR.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Period** (1): Enter the relevant period for which you want to evaluate the MRR. This will help in calculating the churn rate and MRR growth.
* **Month** (2): Select the month for which you want to evaluate the MRR. This will help in understanding the revenue streams and growth rate for that specific month.
* **Year** (3): Enter the year for which you want to evaluate the MRR. This will help in understanding the revenue streams and growth rate for that specific year.
* **Revenue Streams** (4): Indicate whether the organization has revenue streams during the specified period. Select 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
* **Total Revenue** (5): Enter the total revenue for the specified period. This will help in calculating the churn rate and MRR growth.
* **Churn Rate** (6): Calculate the percentage of lost customers over the specified period. This will help in understanding the revenue loss and areas for improvement.
* **MRR Growth** (7): Evaluate the rate at which the revenue has increased or decreased over the specified period. This will help in understanding the growth trend and areas for improvement.
* **Next Steps** (8): Provide next steps that will be taken for improving the MRR, based on the insights gained from the evaluation.
* **Growth Drivers** (9): Identify growth drivers that contribute to the MRR growth. Select 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
* **Action Items** (10): Enter relevant action items, such as specific actions or tasks that need to be performed to improve the MRR (this field is optional).
