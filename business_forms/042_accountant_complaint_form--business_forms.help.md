# Accountant Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record and track complaints against accountants or firms providing bookkeeping services. It helps in documenting the details of the complaint, the type of service provided, and the desired resolution.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please provide the following information:
    *   Fill in the client's full name and email address in the **Client Full Name** and **Email Address** fields, respectively.
    *   Enter the client's phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
    *   If applicable, enter the client's company name in the **Company Name (if applicable)** field.
    *   Enter the name of the accountant or firm being complained about in the **Name of Accountant or Firm Being Complained About** field.
    *   Select the **Type of Service Provided** from the dropdown menu.
    *   Choose the **Category of Complaint** from the dropdown menu. Examples include Inaccurate work or errors, Missed deadlines, Overcharging or billing dispute, Poor communication, Breach of confidentiality, Unprofessional behavior, and Negligence.
    *   Enter the date the issue occurred in the **Date the Issue Occurred** field using the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
    *   Describe the issue in detail in the **Detailed Description of the Complaint** field.
    *   Specify if the issue has caused any financial loss by selecting **Yes - Significant financial loss**, **Yes - Minor financial loss**, or **No financial loss** from the dropdown menu.
    *   If the issue has caused a financial loss, estimate the amount of loss in the **Estimated Financial Loss (if applicable)** field.
    *   Indicate if you have previously raised this issue with the accountant by selecting **Yes, and it was not resolved**, **Yes, and it was partially resolved**, or **False** from the dropdown menu.
    *   Select the desired resolution from the dropdown menu, which includes Full refund, Partial refund, Corrective action on the work, Formal apology, Disciplinary action, or Other.
    *   If you have any additional comments or information, enter them in the **Additional Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Full Name (Required)**: The full name of the client submitting the complaint.
*   **Email Address (Required)**: The email address of the client submitting the complaint.
*   **Phone Number (Required)**: The phone number of the client submitting the complaint.
*   **Company Name (if applicable)**: The name of the company of the client submitting the complaint.
*   **Name of Accountant or Firm Being Complained About (Required)**: The name of the accountant or firm being complained about.
*   **Type of Service Provided (Required)**: The type of service provided by the accountant or firm being complained about. Examples include Tax preparation, Bookkeeping, Audit, Payroll services, Financial consulting, Business advisory, and Other.
*   **Category of Complaint (Required)**: The category of complaint, which can be Inaccurate work or errors, Missed deadlines, Overcharging or billing dispute, Poor communication, Breach of confidentiality, Unprofessional behavior, and Negligence.
*   **Date the Issue Occurred (Required)**: The date the issue occurred in MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **Detailed Description of the Complaint (Required)**: A detailed description of the issue, including specific examples and dates.
*   **Has This Issue Caused Financial Loss (Required)**: Indicate if the issue has caused any financial loss.
*   **Estimated Financial Loss (if applicable)**: The amount of financial loss caused by the issue, if any.
*   **Have You Previously Raised This Issue with the Accountant (Required)**: Indicate if you have previously raised this issue with the accountant.
*   **Desired Resolution (Required)**: The desired resolution of the complaint, which can be Full refund, Partial refund, Corrective action on the work, Formal apology, Disciplinary action, or Other.
*   **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or information that you have regarding the complaint.
