# financial_kpi_dashboard_setup_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your organization's financial KPI dashboard setup.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page of the form, one at a time.
2. On each page, you will find a series of fields with labels and hints.
3. Enter the required information in each field, and select options as needed.
4. Review each page to ensure you have completed all required fields.
