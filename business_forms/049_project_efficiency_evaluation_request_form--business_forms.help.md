# Project Efficiency Evaluation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Efficiency Evaluation Request Form is designed to collect and document the progress and status of a project. By using this form, project teams can assess their project's efficiency, identify areas for improvement, and make necessary adjustments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the project's details, such as its name, date, and time.
2. Choose the method of evaluation that best suits your project's needs.
3. Select the project's status from the available options.
4. Provide any additional comments or notes about the project.
5. Indicate who requested the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Project Efficiency Form 
* **Project Efficiency Form** (`project_efficiency_form`, text, optional): This field is intended to capture the project's current efficiency level. Enter a brief description of the project's efficiency, such as "High", "Medium", "Low", or any other relevant information.

### Project Details 
* **prophets Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): This field is meant to provide additional details about the project. Enter any relevant information about the project, such as its purpose, goals, or objectives.

### Evaluation Date 
* **prophets date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): This field is for selecting the date of the evaluation. Choose a date from the calendar or enter a specific date in the required format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).

### Evaluation Time 
* **prophets time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional): This field is for selecting the time of the evaluation. Choose a time from the available options or enter a custom time in the required format (e.g., HH:MM).

### Evaluation Method 
* **prophets method** (`evaluation_method`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to choose the method of evaluation that best suits your project's needs. Select one of the provided options, such as "Manual", "Automated", or "Hybrid".

### Project Status 
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select the current status of the project. Choose one or more of the provided options, such as "Active", "Paused", "Completed", or "Cancelled".

### Evaluation Comments 
* **prophets Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, note, optional): This field is for providing additional comments or notes about the project. Enter any relevant information that would be helpful for the evaluation.

### Evaluation Requester 
* **Evaluation Requester** (`evaluation_requester`, text, optional): This field is intended to identify who requested the evaluation. Enter the name or title of the person requesting the evaluation.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate data collection.
* Use clear and concise language when filling in the form.
* Review and update the form as needed to align with your project's specific requirements.
