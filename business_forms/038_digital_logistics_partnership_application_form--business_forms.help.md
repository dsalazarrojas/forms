# Digital Logistics Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help us assess your suitability for a digital logistics partnership with our company. It will help us understand your services, experience, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form completely and accurately. Make sure to enter your name, services, regions, experience, documents (if any), contact information, email, and phone number.
2. Select all the services you wish to offer.
3. Enter the regions you have experience in.
4. Enter your experience in the selected regions.
5. Attach any relevant documents or files.
6. Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Form Data (1)** (<type:text>)

* This is the main body of the form where you can enter any additional information you wish to share about yourself or your company.
* Please be as brief as possible, but include all relevant details.

### 2. **Services (2)** (<type:select_multiple>)

* Select all the services you wish to offer in a digital logistics partnership. Our company provides services such as Amazon, Google Cloud, and Azure. You can also choose "Other" if your service is not listed.

### 3. **Regions (3)** (<type:text>)

* Enter the regions you have experience in. For example, if you have experience in Asia, Europe, and North America, list them out.

### 4. **Experience (4)** (<type:text>)

* Enter a brief description of your experience in the selected regions. This will help us understand your capabilities and how they apply to our partnership.

### 5. **Documents (5)** (<type:text>)

* If you have any relevant documents or files that support your application, attach them here. This could be certificates, testimonials, or any other supporting materials.

### 6. **Contact Information (6)** (<type:text>)

* This is where you enter your contact information. Please include your name (or company name), your email, and your phone number.

### 7. **Email (7)** (<type:email>)

* Enter your email address. We will use this to contact you about your application and next steps.

### 8. **Phone (8)** (<type:text>)

* Enter your phone number. We will use this to contact you in case we need more information or to schedule a follow-up meeting.
