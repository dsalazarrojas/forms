# Phone Service Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Phone Service Complaint Form is used to report phone service-related issues to the service provider. This form helps the provider identify problems and take necessary steps to resolve them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the fields as accurately as possible.
* Select the phone service provider from the list if they are listed.
* Enter the date when the issue started.
* Provide a detailed description of the problem.
* Enter the phone number used when the issue occurred.
* Enter your name and contact information if you wish to be contacted.
* Choose the resolution status from the provided options.
* Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Problem** (`service_problem`, text, optional): Briefly describe the issue you experienced with your phone service.
* **Phone Service Provider** (`phone_service_provider`, select_multiple, required): Choose the phone service provider that you are experiencing the issue with.
* **Date Issue Started** (`date_issue_started`, date, required): Enter the date when you first noticed the problem.
* **Detailed Complaint** (`detailed_complaint`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the issue and how it affected you.
* **Phone Number Used** (`phone_number_used`, text, required): Enter the phone number associated with the issue.
* **Name Complainant** (`name_complainant`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information if you wish to be contacted.
* **Name Service Provider** (`name_service_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the service provider's representative who helped or will help resolve the issue.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number if you wish to be contacted.
* **Name Escalation Escalated To** (`name_escalation_escalated_to`, text, optional): Enter the name of the representative who escalated or will escalate the issue.
* **Name Of Resolution** (`name_of_resolution`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who resolved the issue.
* **Date Resolved** (`date_resolved`, date, optional): Enter the date the issue was resolved.
* **Resolution Status** (`resolution_status`, select_one, required): Choose from the options to indicate the current resolution status of the issue.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the issue.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information if you wish to be contacted.
