# Financial Services Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Services Intake Form is designed to collect essential details from users for financial services. Please fill out this form with accurate and complete information to expedite the process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and documents required for the form before starting.
2. Enter your First Name in the field labeled "First Name".
3. Enter your Last Name in the field labeled "Last Name".
4. Provide your Email address in the field labeled "Email".
5. Enter your Phone number in the field labeled "Phone".
6. Enter your Date of Birth in the field labeled "Date of Birth".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text, Optional)**: Please enter your given name as it appears on your ID or documentation.
* **Last Name (Text, Optional)**: Please enter your surname as it appears on your ID or documentation.
* **Email (Email, Optional)**: Enter a valid email address to help us contact you for further information.
* **Phone (Text, Optional)**: Provide your phone number to allow for efficient communication.
* **Date of Birth (Date, Optional)**: Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).

## Tips
* Double-check that all fields are completed accurately before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have entered the correct format for the "Date of Birth" field.
* If you are unsure about any field or require clarification, contact our financial services team for assistance.
