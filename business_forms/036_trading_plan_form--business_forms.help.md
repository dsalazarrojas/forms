# trading_plan_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help traders or financial professionals document and plan their trading activities. It's meant to assist in managing or optimizing investment strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields, if any.
2. For fields with options, select the relevant choice that best describes your situation or strategy.
3. Enter the required numerical values as accurate as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trading Plan Form** (`title`, text, required): This field is the form title. Please enter a descriptive name for your trading plan.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Trading Behavior** (`trading_behavior`, text, optional): Describe the trading behavior or strategy you're using or planning.
* **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, number, optional): Rate your risk tolerance on a scale, with higher values indicating higher risk.
* **Profit Goal** (`profit_goal`, number, optional): Enter your target profit or return on investment.
* **Investment Strategy** (`investment_strategy`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the provided options to select your investment strategy.
	+ Select one: Choose this option for a specific, well-defined strategy.
	+ Other: Choose this option if you're using a custom or unconventional strategy.
* **Asset Allocation** (`asset_allocation`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or both of the provided options for asset allocation.
	+ Yes: Choose this option to indicate that you're allocating assets to this category.
	+ No: Choose this option to indicate that you're not allocating assets to this category.
* **Risk Free Rate** (`risk_free_rate`, number, optional): Enter the risk-free rate of your investment or strategy.
