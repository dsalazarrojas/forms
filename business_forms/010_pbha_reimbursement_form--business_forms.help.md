# PBHA Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to gather information about a claim that has been filed, ensuring that the claimant's details are accurately recorded and processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the claimant's information, including name, contact details, and date of birth.
2.  Select the claim type and status.
3.  Enter the claim amount and date.
4.  Provide business information, if applicable.
5.  Add any additional notes or comments about the claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Claimant Information** (1, text, required/optional): This section captures the claimant's name, address, phone number, email, and other contact details.
- **Claim Type** (2, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of claim that is being filed.
- **Claim Description** (3, text, required/optional): Briefly describe the nature of the claim.
- **Claim Amount** (4, number, required/optional): Enter the total amount of the claim.
- **Claim Date** (5, date, required/optional): Specify the date the claim was filed.
- **Claim Status** (6, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the claim (Active or Inactive).
- **Business Information** (8, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter the claimant's business information.
- **Claimant Address** (7, text, required/optional): Enter the claimant's residential or business address.
- **Claimant Phone** (9, text, required/optional): Enter the claimant's phone number.
- **Claimant Email** (10, email, required/optional): Enter the claimant's email address.
- **Claimant Representative** (11, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter the name of the claimant's representative.
- **Claimant Signature** (12, note, required/optional): This is an optional field for the claimant's signature, if required.
- **Claimant Date of Birth** (13, date, required/optional): Enter the claimant's date of birth.
- **Claimant Place of Birth** (14, text, required/optional): Enter the claimant's place of birth.
- **Claimant SSN** (15, text, required/optional): Enter the claimant's social security number.
- **Claimant Date of Birth** (16, date, required/optional): Enter the claimant's date of birth.
- **Claimant Date of Birth Year** (17, number, required/optional): Enter the claimant's year of birth.
- **Claimant Date of Birth Month** (18, number, required/optional): Enter the claimant's month of birth.
- **Claimant Date of Birth Day** (19, number, required/optional): Enter the claimant's day of birth.
- **Claimant Date of Birth AM/PM** (20, select_one, required/optional): Select AM or PM for the claimant's time of birth.
- **Claimant Date of Birth Time** (21, time, required/optional): Enter the claimant's time of birth.
- **Claimant Date of Birth Time Zone** (22, text, required/optional): Enter the claimant's time zone (if applicable).
- **Claimant Relationship** (23, text, required/optional): Enter the claimant's relationship status.
- **Claimant Notes** (24, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the claim.
- **Business Name** (25, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the business related to the claim (if applicable).

Note: Fields marked as "required/optional" indicate that they are not necessarily required but are important for a complete claim submission.
