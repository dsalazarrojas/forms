  # Employee Benefit Enrollment Form - Help Guide
  ## Purpose
  This form is used to enroll employees in various employee benefits, such as medical, dental, and vision insurance. It collects information about the employee's name, ID, benefit type, insurance provider, and other relevant details.

  ## How To Complete This Form
  To complete this form, follow these steps:

  1. Fill in your employee name and ID on pages 1 and 2, respectively.
  2. Select the benefit type you want to enroll in on page 3.
  3. Choose your insurance provider on page 4.
  4. Enter the enrollment date and benefit start date on pages 5 and 6, respectively.
  5. Select the benefit status and reason for termination on pages 7 and 8, respectively.
  6. Enter the termination date and reason on pages 9 and 10, respectively.
  7. Add any additional notes or comments on pages 11 and 12.
  8. Enter your email, phone number, and employee group on pages 13, 14, and 15, respectively.
  9. Enter your manager's name, email, and phone number on pages 16, 17, and 18, respectively.
  10. Enter your manager's title and notes on pages 19 and 20, respectively.
  11. Enter your supervisor's name, title, phone number, and email on pages 21, 22, 23, and 24, respectively.
  12. Enter your supervisor's notes on page 25.

  ## Field-by-Field Explanation
  - **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your employee name.
  - **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your employee ID number.
  - **Benefit Type** (`benefit_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of benefit you want to enroll in (e.g., Medical, Dental, Vision).
  - **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, select_multiple, required): Select the insurance provider for your benefit (e.g., Aetna, Blue Cross, Cigna).
  - **Enrollment Date** (`enrollment_date`, date, required): Enter the date you want to enroll in the benefit.
  - **Benefit Start Date** (`benefit_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you want the benefit to start.
  - **Benefit End Date** (`benefit_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you want the benefit to end.
  - **Benefit Status** (`benefit_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the status of your benefit (e.g., Active, Inactive).
  - **Reason for Termination** (`reason_for_termination`, text, required): Enter the reason why you want to terminate the benefit.
  - **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, date, required): Enter the date you want to terminate the benefit.
  - **Termination Reason** (`termination_reason`, text, required): Enter a brief reason for terminating the benefit.
  - **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes or comments about your benefit enrollment.
  - **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
  - **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
  - **Employee Group** (`employee_group`, select_multiple, required): Select your employee group (e.g., Sales, Marketing, Customer Support).
  - **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your manager's name.
  - **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter your manager's email address.
  - **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required): Enter your manager's phone number.
  - **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, text, required): Enter your manager's title.
  - **Manager Notes** (`manager_notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes or comments from your manager.
  - **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter your supervisor's name.
  - **Supervisor Title** (`supervisor_title`, text, required): Enter your supervisor's title.
  - **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, required): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
  - **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, required): Enter your supervisor's email address.
  - **Supervisor Notes** (`supervisor_notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes or comments from your supervisor.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative.
* This form is for employee benefit enrollment purposes only, and any other use is not permitted.
