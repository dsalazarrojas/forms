# Group Chat Meeting Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants after a group chat meeting. The feedback will help the facilitators and organizers of the meeting to evaluate the effectiveness of the meeting and make improvements for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your feedback and observations about the meeting.
2. Provide your participant name and email address so that the facilitators can contact you if needed.
3. If applicable, share your phone number so that the facilitators can call you for further clarification or discussion.
4. Enter the date and time of the meeting for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Meeting Feedback** (`group_chat_meeting_feedback_form_1`, text, optional): Describe your overall thoughts and impressions about the meeting. 
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Please type in your full name, as it will help us get in touch with you if needed. 
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you if there's any follow-up questions or need to reach you. 
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it so that we can reach you more easily. 
- **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, optional): If you're able to, please provide the date of the meeting for reference. 
- **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, optional): If you know the exact time of the meeting, please provide it for reference. 

## Tips
- Please be as specific and detailed as possible in your feedback.
- If you have any suggestions or recommendations for future meeting improvements, please don't hesitate to share them.
- If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to reach out to us after submitting this form.
