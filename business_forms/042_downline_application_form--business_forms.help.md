# downline_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The downline application form is a tool used to gather information from individuals or organizations interested in becoming downliners, or those who sell products or services directly under the company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) and are crucial for processing your application.
2. Enter your first name in the First Name field. This will be used for identification purposes.
3. Enter your last name in the Last Name field. This will be used for identification purposes.
4. Enter your email address in the Email field. This will be used for communication and correspondence.
5. Enter your phone number in the Phone field. This will be used for communication and contact purposes.
6. Select one of the available options in the Business Type field: Service, Product, or Other. This will categorize your business under the company.
7. Enter a brief description of your business in the Business Details field. This will help the company understand your business better.
8. Enter your business contact address in the Contact Address field. This will be used for communication and correspondence.
9. Enter your business name in the Business Name field. This will be used for identification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Text, *): Enter your first name here.
- **Last Name** (Text, *): Enter your last name here.
- **Email** (Email, *): Enter your valid email address for communication.
- **Phone** (Text, *): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Business Type** (Select One, *): Choose a category for your business: Service, Product, or Other.
- **Business Details** (Text, *): Enter a brief description of your business.
- **Contact Address** (Text, *): Enter your business contact address.
- **Business Name** (Text, *): Enter your business name for identification purposes.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled out to avoid delays in processing your application.
- Please ensure that the email address you provide is valid and active.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the company for assistance.

Please note that the asterisk (*) indicates a required field. 

This help guide is designed to be easy to follow and understand, and it should be easy to complete the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the company.
