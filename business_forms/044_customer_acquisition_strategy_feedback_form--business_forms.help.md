# customer_acquisition_strategy_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer acquisition strategy feedback form is designed to gather feedback from end-users on the current customer acquisition strategy. This form helps to identify areas of improvement and provide valuable insights for future strategy development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions according to your feedback.
2. Select the options that best describe your thoughts on each section.
3. Complete all fields as required or if you have an opinion on the topic.
4. Once you have filled out all fields, click submit to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **marketing_strategies** (`customer_acquisition_strategy_form_1`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all marketing strategies that you feel have been effective or not effective for customer acquisition.
* **feedback** (`customer_acquisition_strategy_form_2`, note, required/optional): Provide any comments or feedback you have on the customer acquisition strategy. This field is not required but will be read by our team to gain a deeper understanding of your thoughts.
* **customer_engagement** (`customer_acquisition_strategy_form_3`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your level of engagement with the customer acquisition strategy.
* **team_feedback** (`customer_acquisition_strategy_form_4`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all team members that you have worked with on the customer acquisition strategy. This will help us understand who was involved in the strategy development process.
* **marketing_budget** (`customer_acquisition_strategy_form_5`, number, required/optional): Estimate the marketing budget allocated for customer acquisition.
* **customer_acquisition_channels** (`customer_acquisition_strategy_form_6`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all customer acquisition channels that have been used.
* **marketing_messages** (`customer_acquisition_strategy_form_7`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your level of satisfaction with the marketing messages used.
* **customer_acquisition_goals** (`customer_acquisition_strategy_form_8`, note, required/optional): Describe the goals and objectives of the customer acquisition strategy.
* **customer_acquisition_tactics** (`customer_acquisition_strategy_form_9`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all customer acquisition tactics that have been used.
* **customer_acquisition_results** (`customer_acquisition_strategy_form_10`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your level of success with the customer acquisition strategy.
