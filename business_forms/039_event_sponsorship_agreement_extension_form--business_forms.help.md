# event_sponsorship_agreement_extension_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to extend an existing event sponsorship agreement. It's a crucial document that requires specific details from sponsors to ensure the agreement is up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the event information and select the correct options from the dropdown list.
2. Provide details about the sponsor, selecting all that apply from the options.
3. Enter the agreement date and time (if applicable).
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and commitment.
5. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Info**: Select the relevant event information from the dropdown list to ensure the correct agreement details are captured.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: true
* **Sponsor Info**: Select all sponsor details that apply from the options to ensure the correct agreement details are captured.
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: true
* **Agreement Date**: Enter the date and time when the agreement was made or extended (if applicable).
  * Type: date/time
  * Required: false
* **Agreement Date**: Enter the date when the agreement was extended or updated (if applicable).
  * Type: date
  * Required: false
* **Signature**: This field is for the sponsor to sign and date the form to confirm their understanding and commitment.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
* **Sponsor Name**: Enter the name of the sponsor who is agreeing to the terms.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event that the sponsorship is for.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

## Tips
* Ensure you have the relevant sponsorship agreement details before starting the form.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are completed.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant department for clarification.
