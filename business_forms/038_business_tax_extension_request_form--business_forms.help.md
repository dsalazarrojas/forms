# Business Tax Extension Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an extension for a tax return that is due soon. It collects information about the business, including its name, tax ID or EIN, and contact details. The form also asks for the type of tax return, original due date, and requested extension date. This information will help the tax authority to assess and process the request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your business name and tax ID or EIN in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the type of tax return that needs an extension from the drop-down menu.
3. Enter the original due date of the tax return.
4. Choose the requested extension date from the calendar.
5. Provide your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
6. If applicable, enter the name and contact details of your accountant or tax preparer.
7. Estimate the tax liability and indicate if you plan to pay on time or not.
8. Provide any additional information that supports your extension request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: Enter your business's legal name.
* **Business Tax ID or EIN**: Enter your tax identification number or Employer Identification Number.
* **Type of Tax Return**: Select the type of tax return that needs an extension from the drop-down menu.
* **Original Due Date**: Enter the date the tax return was originally due.
* **Requested Extension Date**: Choose the new date you are requesting for the tax return.
* **Business Owner or Representative Name**: Enter the name of the person filling out the form.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Accountant or Tax Preparer Name**: Enter the name of your accountant or tax preparer (if applicable).
* **Accountant Email**: Enter the email of your accountant or tax preparer (if applicable).
* **Accountant Phone**: Enter the phone number of your accountant or tax preparer (if applicable).
* **Reason for Extension Request**: Explain why you are requesting an extension.
* **Extension Reason Category**: Select the primary category for your reason for extension from the drop-down menu.
* **Estimated Tax Liability**: Estimate the amount of tax you owe or will owe.
* **Will You Pay on Time**: Indicate if you plan to pay the estimated tax by the original due date.
* **Previous Extensions Received**: If you have received previous extensions, list them.
* **Supporting Documents Attached**: List any documents attached to support your extension request.
* **Request Submission Date**: This field is automatically filled with today's date, but you can modify it if necessary.

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled out to submit the form.
