# Content Calendar Planning Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees plan and record their absences for content creation and planning purposes. It gathers essential information about the employee's absence, including start and end dates, reason, and handover plan.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure your absence is properly recorded and communicated to your team.
- Enter your full name and employee identification number for identity verification.
- Specify the start and end dates of your absence, ensuring you provide an exact start and end date.
- Choose a valid reason for your absence from the provided options (Vacation, Medical, Personal, or Other).
- Provide a detailed handover plan to ensure a seamless transition of work during your absence.
- Enter an emergency contact person and their phone number for communication during your absence.
- Confirm that you have completed the form by selecting 'True' at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Member Full Name** (`employee_name`): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employee Identification Number** (`employee_id`): Enter your unique employee identification number for verification purposes.
* **Start Date of Absence** (`start_date_of_absence`): Provide the exact date when your absence will start.
* **End Date of Absence** (`end_date_of_absence`): Enter the date when your absence will end.
* **Reason for Absence** (`reason_for_absence`): Select a valid reason for your absence from the provided options (Vacation, Medical, Personal, or Other).
* **Description of the Handover Plan** (`handover_details`): Describe the plan for a smooth handover, including tasks, deadlines, and critical information.
* **Emergency Contact Person during Absence** (`contact_person_during_absence`): Enter the name and contact information of the person to contact during your absence.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone_during_absence`): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Absence Form Completed** (`absence_form_completed`): Confirm that you have completed the form by selecting 'True'.
