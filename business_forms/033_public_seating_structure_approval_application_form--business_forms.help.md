# Public Seating Structure Approval Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain approval on the public seating structure from relevant stakeholders in the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your user information in the User Information section.
2. Provide details about the public seating structure in the Seating Structure Information section.
3. Enter the approval date and time in the Approval Date and Approval Time sections.
4. Select the approver(s) responsible for the approval from the Approvers section.
5. Add any comments if necessary in the Comments section.
6. Choose the approval terms and conditions from the Approval Terms section.
7. Select the current approval status from the Approval Status section.
8. Add any additional information if required in the Additional Information section.
9. Enter the details of who submitted the form in the Submitted By section.
10. Enter the date and time the form was submitted in the Submitted On and Submitted At sections.
11. Enter the location where the form was submitted from in the Submitted From section.
12. Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form in the Submitted By Email section.
13. Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form in the Submitted From Phone section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (text, required): Enter your name and other relevant personal details.
* **Seating Structure Information** (text, required): Describe the public seating structure you are seeking approval for.
* **Approval Date** (date, required): Enter the date on which you would like to receive approval.
* **Approval Time** (time, required): Enter the time on which you would like to receive approval.
* **Approvers** (select multiple, required): Select all applicable approvers who need to approve the seating structure.
* **Comments** (text, optional): Add any comments or observations related to the seating structure.
* **Approval Terms** (select one, required): Choose the terms and conditions under which you are seeking approval.
* **Approval Status** (select one, required): Choose the current status of the approval process.
* **Additional Information** (note, optional): Add any additional details that may be relevant to the approval process.
* **Submitted By** (select one, required): Select who submitted the form.
* **Submitted On** (date, optional): Enter the date on which the form was submitted.
* **Submitted At** (time, optional): Enter the time on which the form was submitted.
* **Submitted From** (text, optional): Enter the location where the form was submitted from.
* **Submitted By Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted From Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form.
