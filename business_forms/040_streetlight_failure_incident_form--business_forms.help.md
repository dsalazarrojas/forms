# Streetlight Failure Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Streetlight Failure Incident Form is used to report incidents of streetlights that are not functioning correctly. This form gathers information necessary for the maintenance team to identify the failure and take corrective action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a short title of the incident in the "Streetlight Failure Incident Form" field. This should be a brief description of what happened.
2. Enter the location of the streetlight that failed in the "streetlight_location" field.
3. Provide a description of what happened in the "Description" field.
4. Enter the date of the failure in the "Date of Failure" field. This date should be in a format that can be easily read by the system, such as MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Indicate whether or not there was an incident by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Type of Incident" field. This field is used to help the maintenance team prioritize their work.
6. Enter any additional details about the incident in the "Incident Details" field.
7. If possible, upload a photo of the streetlight failure in the "Photo of Failure" field. This can help the maintenance team understand the nature of the failure.
8. Enter the Municipality ID, which is the unique identifier for the municipality where the streetlight is located.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Streetlight Failure Incident Form** (text, required): Enter a short title of the incident.
* **streetlight_location** (text, required): Enter the location of the streetlight that failed.
* **Description** (text, required): Provide a description of what happened.
* **Date of Failure** (date, required): Enter the date of the failure in a format that can be easily read by the system, such as MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Type of Incident** (select_one, required): Indicate whether or not there was an incident by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Incident Details** (note, optional): Enter any additional details about the incident.
* **Photo of Failure** (text, optional): If possible, upload a photo of the streetlight failure.
* **Municipality ID** (text, optional): Enter the unique identifier for the municipality where the streetlight is located.
