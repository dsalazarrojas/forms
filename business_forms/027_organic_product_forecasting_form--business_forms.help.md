# Organic Product Forecasting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to forecast the production and distribution of organic products. It captures information on products, quantities, prices, and other relevant details to help farmers and distributors plan and manage their organic products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of organic product you are forecasting.
2. Select the distribution channels and production locations where the product will be sold.
3. Enter the quantity and unit price of the product.
4. Select the forecast date for the product.
5. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Organic Product Forecasting Form

* **Organic Product Forecasting Form** (`organic_product_forecasting_form_1`, text, optional): This is the main title of the form, which should match the name of the product being forecasted.
* **Select Products** (`organic_product_forecasting_form_2`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of organic product you are forecasting, such as Apple or Banana.
* **Quantity** (`organic_product_forecasting_form_3`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the product you are forecasting.
* **Unit Price** (`organic_product_forecasting_form_4`, number, optional): Enter the unit price of the product.
* **Forecast Date** (`organic_product_forecasting_form_5`, date, optional): Select the forecast date for the product.
* **Distribution Channel** (`organic_product_forecasting_form_6`, select_multiple, optional): Select the distribution channels where the product will be sold, such as Store 1 or Store 3.
* **Production Location** (`organic_product_forecasting_form_7`, select_one, optional): Select the production location of the product, such as Farm 1.
* **Notes** (`organic_product_forecasting_form_8`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the product.

## Tips
* Please make sure to select only the relevant information for your product.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information you input.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact your supervisor or the IT department.
* This form is designed to be user-friendly, but if you experience any issues, please report them to us so we can improve it.
