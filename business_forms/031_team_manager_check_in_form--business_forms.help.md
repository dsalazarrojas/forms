# Team Manager Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from team managers about their team's activities, status, and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the team's name and enter any relevant information.
2. Choose the team's alignment: Team, Manager, Both, or Neither.
3. Select the current status of the team's updates: In Progress, Complete, or Not Started.
4. Provide any feedback or comments about the team's progress.
5. List the team members assigned to the task.
6. List the team members involved in the task.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team you are checking in.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the team's activity.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the team's activity.
* **Team Update** (`team_update`, text, optional): Enter any updates or changes about the team's progress.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any feedback or comments about the team's progress.
* **Team Alignment** (`team_alignment`, select_one, required): Select the team's alignment: Team, Manager, Both, or Neither.
* **Team Update Status** (`team_update_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the current status of the team's updates: In Progress, Complete, or Not Started.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team members assigned to the task.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, text, optional): List the team members involved in the task.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct team's alignment and status to accurately reflect the team's progress.
* Provide clear and concise feedback to help the team move forward.
* List all team members involved in the task to ensure everyone is on the same page.
* Complete all required fields to avoid submitting an incomplete form.
