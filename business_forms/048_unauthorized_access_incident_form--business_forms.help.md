# Unauthorized Access Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Unauthorized Access Incident Form is designed to report incidents of unauthorized access to company assets, systems, or data. This form helps to gather information about the incident, including the date, time, location, and details of what happened. By completing this form, you will help our security team identify and address potential security threats.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
    1.  Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
    2.  Enter the date and time of the incident in the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" fields.
    3.  Provide the location where the incident occurred in the "Incident Location" field.
    4.  Describe what happened in as much detail as possible in the "Incident Description" field.
    5.  Add any additional details about the incident in the "Incident Details" field.
    6.  Submit the form to report the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the incident in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
    *   For example: `2022-07-25`
*   **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the incident in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
    *   For example: `14:30:00`
*   **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
    *   For example: "Server Room, 3rd floor"
*   **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required: false): Describe what happened in as much detail as possible.
    *   For example: "An unauthorized user accessed our database and deleted sensitive data."
*   **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, required: false): Add any additional information about the incident, such as the IP address of the perpetrator.
    *   For example: "The incident occurred on IP address 192.0.2.1"

## Tips

*   Make sure to report all incidents, no matter how small they may seem.
*   Provide as much detail as possible when filling out the form.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the security team for assistance.
