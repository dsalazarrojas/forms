# New Client Goal Setting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help new clients set up their goals in alignment with the company's objectives. It will help you understand the client's objectives, progress tracking preferences, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by entering the client's name and contact information (if applicable).
2. Describe the client's objective, which is a brief statement of what they aim to achieve.
3. Select whether the goal is short-term or long-term.
4. Choose a date for the expected completion of the goal.
5. Select the methods you will use to track the client's progress (e.g., phone, email, chat, or meeting).
6. (Optional) Enter a detailed description of the goal, if desired.
7. Confirm whether the goal is aligned with the company's objectives.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the client's name**: Enter the client's name as they would like to be referred to.
* **What is the client's contact information**: Enter any relevant contact information for the client, such as email or phone number.
* **What is the client's objective**: Describe the client's goal in a brief statement.
* **Is this a long-term or short-term goal**: Choose whether the goal is expected to be completed within a short or long period.
* **What is the expected completion date**: Select a date for when you anticipate the goal to be completed.
* **How will you track progress**: Choose the methods you'll use to monitor the client's progress (e.g., phone, email, chat, or meeting).
* **What is the client's goal description (Optional)**: Enter a detailed description of the goal, if desired.
* **Is this goal aligned with company objectives**: Confirm whether the goal is aligned with the company's objectives.
