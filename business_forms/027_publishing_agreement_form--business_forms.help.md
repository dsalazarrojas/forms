# Publishing Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a publishing agreement between the publisher and the author. It includes fields for author information, publisher information, agreement terms, electronic and print pricing, publishing date, and signatures from both the publisher and the author.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are filling out a new publishing agreement. If you are revising or updating an existing agreement, please use the relevant update form.
2. Fill out the author information fields (fields 2-3) with the required details.
3. Fill out the publisher information fields (fields 4-5) with the required details.
4. Enter the agreement terms (field 6) as per the terms of the agreement.
5. Enter the electronic and print pricing (fields 7-8) as per the agreed-upon prices.
6. Select a publishing date (field 9) for the agreement.
7. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header** (`1`, `note`, required: `false`): This field serves as a header for the form and provides an introduction to the form's purpose.
* **Author Information** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name and contact information of the author.
* **Publisher Information** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name and contact information of the publisher.
* **Agreement Terms** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the terms of the agreement as per the agreed-upon terms.
* **Electronic Pricing** (`5`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the price of the electronic version of the work.
* **Print Pricing** (`6`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the price of the print version of the work.
* **Publishing Date** (`7`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date on which the work will be published.
* **Publisher Signature** (`8`, `text`, required: `false`): Sign and date this field to confirm the publisher's agreement.
* **Author Signature** (`9`, `text`, required: `false`): Sign and date this field to confirm the author's agreement.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the form for any errors or omissions before submitting.
* Make sure to save a copy of the form for future reference.
