# Employee of the Month Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employee of the Month" nomination form is designed to collect nominations for the Employee of the Month award. This award is meant to recognize and appreciate employees who demonstrate exceptional performance, teamwork, and dedication to their job.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Nomination Form" section with any additional details you'd like to provide about the nomination.
2. Enter the name of the employee being nominated in the "Employee Name" field.
3. Enter the job title of the employee being nominated in the "Job Title" field.
4. Select the date of the nomination in the "Nomination Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Nomination Form
* **Nomination Form** (`nomination_form`, text, optional): This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional information about the nomination. You can type in as much or as little as you'd like.

### Employee Name
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Here, you'll enter the name of the employee being nominated. Make sure to spell it correctly.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the employee being nominated. This could be their official job title, such as "Software Engineer" or "Marketing Manager".

### Nomination Date
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, optional): Select the date you're nominating the employee for the award. This will help track the nominations throughout the year.
