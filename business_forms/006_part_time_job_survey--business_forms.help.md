# Part Time Job Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from employees or job seekers about their part-time job preferences, schedules, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Choose your preferred days of work from the list provided.
2. Select your preferred work hours.
3. Choose your preferred pay rate (if applicable).
4. Indicate your preferred location of work (e.g., home, office, warehouse, remote).
5. Choose the type of work you're interested in (e.g., part-time, freelance, etc.).
6. Select any job types that interest you (e.g., temporary, seasonal, internship).
7. Indicate the status of your job search (e.g., open to new opportunities, currently employed, etc.).
8. Enter any additional comments or notes about your work preferences.

* **work_schedule**: This field allows you to select the days of the week you are available to work. Please choose from the list provided.

* **notes**: This field is for you to enter any additional comments or notes about your work preferences.
