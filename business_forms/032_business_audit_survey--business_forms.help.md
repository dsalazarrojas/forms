# Business Audit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your business to help us understand your company's situation, goals, and challenges. Please answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions on each page, as indicated by the required/optional label.
2. For select_one fields, choose the most accurate answer from the provided options.
3. For select_multiple fields, select all relevant options.
4. For text fields, enter a short and concise description.
5. For note fields, write a brief explanation or description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (Company Name): Enter the legal name of your business.
* **Industry** (Industry): Select your industry sector, e.g., Technology, Healthcare, Finance, etc.
* **Company Size** (Company Size): Choose the number of employees in your company.
* **Annual Revenue** (Annual Revenue): Select your company's approximate annual revenue.
* **Years in Business** (Years in Business): Choose how long your business has been operating.
* **Full Name** (Full Name): Enter your full name.
* **Position** (Position): Enter your title or position in the company.
* **Email Address** (Email Address): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Financial Health** (Financial Health): Select your current financial status.
* **Current Profitability** (Current Profitability): Choose how profitable your company is currently.
* **Cash Flow Status** (Cash Flow Status): Select your current cash flow situation.
* **Debt Level** (Debt Level): Choose your current debt level.
* **Operational Efficiency** (Operational Efficiency): Select how efficient your operations are.
* **Growth Status** (Growth Status): Choose your current growth trajectory.
* **Challenges** (Current Challenges): If you have any challenges, list them in the note field.
* **Main Challenges** (Main Challenges): Select all relevant challenges facing your business.
* **Biggest Pain Point** (Biggest Pain Point): Enter the main issue that keeps you up at night.
* **Audit Goals** (Audit Goals): If you have any goals for the audit, list them in the note field.
* **Primary Goal** (Primary Goal): Choose the main objective for the audit.
* **Previous Audits** (Previous Audits): Select how many audits your business has had.
* **Timeline for Engagement** (Timeline for Engagement): Choose when you want to start the engagement.
* **Budget Range** (Budget Range): Select the expected investment range.
* **Decision Timeline** (Decision Timeline): Choose when you will decide on the engagement.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments): If you have any other information to share, enter it here.

Note:
* Please answer all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
* We will review your answers and follow up with you shortly.
