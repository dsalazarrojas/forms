# HR Client Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Client Registration Form is a tool for HR clients to provide necessary information about their business to the HR department. This form helps the HR department get to know you better and understand your needs, enabling us to provide you with the best possible services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide a title for your business in the **HR Client Registration Form** field.
2. Select a **Category** that best describes your business (e.g., Business Forms, Other Forms).
3. Enter a brief **Description** of your business.
4. Select the **Key Areas** in which we can assist you (e.g., Talent Acquisition & Contract Staffing, HR Outsourcing).
5. Enter any relevant **Team Experience** in the text box.
6. Enter any relevant **Domains** (e.g., industries, specialties) related to your business.
7. Enter any relevant **Edge** (e.g., unique selling points, USPs) of your business.
8. If you wish to **Partner with** us, enter your preferred method of contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **HR Client Registration Form** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a title for your business that will be used to identify it in our records.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Select the category that best describes your business (e.g., Business Forms, Other Forms).
* **Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your business.
* **Key Areas** (`key_areas`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the areas where we can assist you (e.g., Talent Acquisition & Contract Staffing, HR Outsourcing).
* **Team Experience** (`team_experience`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant experience of your team.
* **Domains** (`domains`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant domains (e.g., industries, specialties) related to your business.
* **Edge** (`edge`, text, required/optional): Enter any unique selling points (USPs) or competitive advantages of your business.
* **Partner with** (`partner_with`, text, required/optional): If you wish to partner with us, enter your preferred method of contact.
* **Success** (`success`, note, required/optional): This field is not intended for user input; it's a placeholder for any internal note from our side.
