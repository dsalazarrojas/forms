# Global Licensing Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Licensing Application Form is a crucial document required for organizations seeking to obtain a license to operate in a specific region or jurisdiction. This form ensures that your application is complete, accurate, and meets the necessary requirements for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the `first_page` field, providing a brief description of your organization and its purpose.
2. In the `application_details` field, provide a detailed explanation of your application, including any relevant documentation or evidence.
3. In the `contact_details` field, enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
4. In the `address` field, provide your organization's address.
5. In the `email` field, enter your email address.
6. In the `phone` field, enter your phone number.
7. In the `license_type` field, select the type of license you are applying for from the provided options.
8. In the `applicant_confirmation` field, confirm your submission by selecting the correct option.
9. In the `region` field, select the region or jurisdiction for which you are applying for.
10. In the `document_uploader` field, upload any required documents or supporting evidence.
11. In the `document` field, attach any additional documents or files.
12. In the `signature` field, provide your digital signature or approval.
13. In the `notes` field, enter any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_page** (text, required/optional): This field is the first page of your application. Please provide a brief description of your organization and its purpose.
* **application_details** (text, required/optional): This field requires a detailed explanation of your application, including any relevant documentation or evidence.
* **contact_details** (text, required/optional): Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **address** (text, required/optional): Provide your organization's address.
* **email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **license_type** (select multiple, required): Select the type of license you are applying for from the provided options.
* **applicant_confirmation** (select one, required): Confirm your submission by selecting the correct option.
* **region** (select one, required): Select the region or jurisdiction for which you are applying for.
* **document_uploader** (text, required/optional): Upload any required documents or supporting evidence.
* **document** (text, required/optional): Attach any additional documents or files.
* **signature** (text, required/optional): Provide your digital signature or approval.
* **notes** (text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.

## Tips
- Ensure that you complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check your submission for any errors or inaccuracies before confirming.
- Be prepared to upload supporting documents or evidence as needed.
- Review our internal documentation or contact our support team if you have any doubts or concerns.
