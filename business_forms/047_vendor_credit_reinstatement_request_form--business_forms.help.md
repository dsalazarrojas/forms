# Vendor Credit Reinstatement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Credit Reinstatement Request Form is a tool to help customers request reinstatement of their vendor credit. This form is used to provide detailed information required for our team to review and process the request. Please note that this form is only for genuine requests and not for duplicate or fraudulent purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your contact information in the "Contact Name", "Contact Phone", and "Contact Email" fields.
2.  Select the "Request Date" when you would like to request the credit reinstatement.
3.  Choose the relevant "Product Details" that are related to the credit request.
4.  Enter the amount you are requesting for the "Vendor Credit Amount".
5.  Choose the "Payment Method" through which you would like to receive the credit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Vendor Credit Request** (`vendor_credit_request_form`, text, optional): Please describe the reason for your request to reinstate your vendor credit. This field should be a brief explanation of your request.
*   **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the contact person for this request.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can easily contact you.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, optional): Enter your work email address so we can easily respond to you.
*   **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to request the credit reinstatement.
*   **Product Details** (`product_details`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the products related to the credit request. This will help us process your request efficiently.
*   **Vendor Credit Amount** (`vendor_credit_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you are requesting for the credit reinstatement.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose the payment method through which you would like to receive the credit.
