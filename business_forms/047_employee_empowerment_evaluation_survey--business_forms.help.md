# Employee Empowerment Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate team leads and their teams, gathering essential data on their level of empowerment, communication style, job satisfaction, and areas for improvement. The information collected will help us identify opportunities to enhance our work environment and foster positive change.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your response for each question by choosing from the available options.
2. Enter your answers in the corresponding text fields.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Lead**: This is the first question, and you must select whether your team lead is empowered or not.
* **Employee Name**: This field requires you to enter your name as an employee.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title as it appears on your personnel card.
* **Department**: Enter the name of the department you belong to.
* **Team Size**: If applicable, enter the number of people in your team.
* **Empowerment Level**: Select whether your team lead is empowered or not.
* **Communication Style**: Choose how often your team lead communicates with you.
* **Feedback Frequency**: Enter how often you receive feedback from your team lead.
* **Communication Channel**: Select the channels your team lead uses to communicate with you.
* **Job Satisfaction**: Choose how satisfied you are with your job.
* **Open Communication**: Select whether your team lead maintains an open communication style.
* **Feedback Channel**: Choose the channels you use to give feedback to your team lead.
* **Suggestion for Improvement**: Enter any suggestions you have for improving the work environment.
* **Action Item**: Enter any action items or tasks you would like your team lead to take to improve the work environment.
* **Follow-up Question**: This is a free-text field for any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Date**: Enter the current date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

Note: Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
