# Free Liquor Inventory Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Free Liquor Inventory Form is used to track and manage the inventory of free liquor items in the store.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Liquor Brand" field with the brand of the liquor item.
2. Enter the "Size" of the liquor item in the "Size" field.
3. Enter the "Weight" of the liquor item in the "Weight" field.
4. Enter the current "Quantity" of the liquor item in stock in the "Quantity" field.
5. Set the "Reorder Quantity" for the liquor item in the "Reorder Quantity" field.
6. Enter the "Unit Price" of the liquor item in the "Unit Price" field.
7. Calculate the "Total Value" of the liquor item by multiplying the "Quantity" and "Unit Price" fields and entering it in the "Total Value" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Liquor Brand**: Enter the brand of the liquor item.
* **Size**: Enter the size of the liquor bottle or package.
* **Weight**: Enter the weight of the liquor item.
* **Quantity**: Enter the current quantity of the liquor item in stock.
* **Reorder Quantity**: Enter the quantity that should be reordered.
* **Unit Price**: Enter the price of a single unit of the liquor item.
* **Total Value**: Calculate the total value of the liquor item by multiplying the "Quantity" and "Unit Price" fields.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure accurate inventory tracking.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with a supervisor or inventory specialist.
* This form is for inventory tracking purposes only and is not for sales or ordering purposes.
