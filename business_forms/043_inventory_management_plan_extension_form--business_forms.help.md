# Inventory Management Plan Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you extend and manage your inventory. It will guide you in making the right decisions for your business form's management.

## How To Complete This Form
To get the most out of this form, follow these simple steps:

1.  Review the form carefully, ensuring you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2.  Choose whether to request an extension for your inventory management plan, and select all the appropriate options for your business.
3.  Provide accurate dates for when your inventory management plan will start and end.
4.  Enter a name and description for your business form if necessary.
5.  Finally, select the category of your business form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Extension** (`request_extension`, Select Multiple, Not required): Choose whether to request an extension for your inventory management plan. 
* **Inventory Items** (`inventory_items`, Select One, Not required): Indicate if you need to include or exclude items from your inventory management.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, Date, Not required): Enter the date when your inventory management plan will start. Please format the date according to your system's preferred date format.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, Date, Not required): Enter the date when your inventory management plan will end. Please format the date according to your system's preferred date format.
* **Business Form Name** (`business_form_name`, Text, Not required): Enter a name for your business form if necessary.
* **Business Form Description** (`business_form_description`, Text, Not required): Provide a description for your business form if necessary.
* **Business Form Category** (`business_form_category`, Select One, Required): Select the category for your business form. This is an essential field to categorize your business form for easier management.
* **Business Form Tags** (`business_form_tags`, Select Multiple, Not required): Choose any additional tags for your business form to make it easier to find and manage.
