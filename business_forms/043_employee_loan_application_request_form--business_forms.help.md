# Employee Loan Application Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Loan Application Request Form is used to collect information from employees who wish to request a loan from the company. This form is used to gather details such as loan amount, loan term, start and end dates, loan frequency, and other relevant information required for loan processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
2. Review the "loan_frequency" field to select the frequency of the loan (daily, monthly, quarterly, or yearly).
3. Enter the loan amount and term in the respective fields.
4. Enter the start and end dates of the loan in the respective fields.
5. If the loan has been approved, select "approved" from the "manager_approval_status" field.
6. If the loan has been rejected, select "rejected" from the "manager_approval_status" field.
7. If the loan is still pending approval, select "pending" from the "manager_approval_status" field.
8. If the loan is rejected, please enter a reason for rejection in the "manager_reason_for_rejection" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or HR records.
* **Loan Amount**: Enter the total amount of money you are requesting as a loan.
* **Loan Term**: Enter the term of the loan in months or years.
* **Loan Start Date**: Enter the start date of the loan.
* **Loan End Date**: Enter the end date of the loan.
* **Loan Frequency**: Select the frequency of the loan (daily, monthly, quarterly, or yearly).
* **Loan Frequency (daily/monthly/quarterly/yearly)**: Enter the number of days, months, quarters, or years that the loan frequency selected.
* **Employee Manager Name**: Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager Approval Status**: Select the status of the loan (approved, rejected, or pending).
* **Manager Reason for Rejection**: If the loan is rejected, enter the reason for rejection.
* **Department**: Enter your department as it appears in your employee ID or HR records.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your manager.
* **Employee Department**: Enter your department as it appears in your employee ID or HR records.
* **Employee Job Title**: Enter your job title as it appears in your employee ID or HR records.
* **Manager Department**: Enter your manager's department as it appears in their ID or HR records.
* **Manager Job Title**: Enter your manager's job title as it appears in their ID or HR records.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name or initials to indicate who submitted the loan request.
