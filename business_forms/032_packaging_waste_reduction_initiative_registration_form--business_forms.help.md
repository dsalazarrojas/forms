# Packaging Waste Reduction Initiative Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from organizations and individuals participating in the Packaging Waste Reduction Initiative. It collects essential details to register participants, including their contact information, organization details, and representative information. The data collected will be used to support the initiative's efforts in reducing packaging waste and promoting sustainable practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that you have the authority to register your organization and any representative you are listing.
3. Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or official ID.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter your organization's name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached during business hours.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached during business hours.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter a physical address if you have one (if you're an individual, this can be your home address).
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you are registering for the initiative in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Type Of Participation** (`type_of_participation`, select one, optional): Indicate whether you are participating in the initiative ('Yes' or 'No').
* **Participant Representative Name** (`participant_representative_name`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization, enter the name of the representative person.
* **Participant Representative Title** (`participant_representative_title`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the title of the representative person.
* **Participant Representative Email** (`participant_representative_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the representative person.
* **Participant Representative Phone** (`participant_representative_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the representative person.
* **Participant Representative Address** (`participant_representative_address`, text, optional): If the representative person has a physical address, enter it here.
* **Participant Representative Date** (`participant_representative_date`, date, optional): If the representative person has a date, enter it in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Participant Representative Type** (`participant_representative_type`, select one, optional): Indicate whether the representative person is participating in the initiative ('Yes' or 'No').
* **Submitter Organization** (`submitter_organization`, text, optional): If you are submitting on behalf of someone else, enter their organization name.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): If you are submitting on behalf of someone else, enter their email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): If you are submitting on behalf of someone else, enter their phone number.
* **Submitter Address** (`submitter_address`, text, optional): If you are submitting on behalf of someone else, enter their physical address.

This guide is intended to help you complete the form accurately and efficiently. If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
