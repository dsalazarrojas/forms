# Safety Data Sheet Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to verify the accuracy and completeness of safety data sheets for materials and substances used in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the provided fields to ensure you understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill in the form with the required information for each field.
3. Check the required fields for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, optional): This field is intended to capture any general information about the safety data sheet, such as the date and version number.
* **Safety Data Sheet** (`safety_data_sheet`, text, optional): Enter a brief description or reference number of the safety data sheet being audited.
* **Chemical Name** (`chemical_name`, text, optional): Fill in the chemical name of the material or substance being audited.
* **Chemical Formula or Molecular Structure** (`chemical_formula_or_molecular_structure`, text, optional): This field is for entering the chemical formula or molecular structure of the substance.
* **Physical State** (`physical_state`, text, optional): Describe the physical state of the substance, such as solid, liquid, or gas.
* **Hazard Identification** (`hazard_identification_chemical_name`, text, optional): Identify any known hazards associated with the chemical name.
* **Health Hazards** (`health_hazards`, text, optional): List any health hazards related to the substance.
* **Safety Precautions** (`safety_precautions`, text, optional): Describe the safety precautions that should be taken when handling the substance.
* **Special Hazards** (`special_hazards`, text, optional): Enter any special hazards associated with the substance.
* **Emergency Procedure** (`emergency_procedure`, text, optional): Outline the emergency procedures in place for handling spills or incidents involving the substance.
* **Storage** (`storage`, text, optional): Provide information on the storage requirements for the substance.
* **Spill/Incident Response** (`spill_incident_response`, text, optional): Describe the response procedures in case of a spill or incident involving the substance.

Please note that this form is designed to be flexible and adaptable to different use cases. The fields provided are not exhaustive, and you may need to add or modify fields to suit your specific needs.
