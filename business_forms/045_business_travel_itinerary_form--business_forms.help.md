# Business Travel Itinerary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Travel Itinerary Form is used to collect essential information from employees regarding their business travel plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out all the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your personal details such as your employee name, employee ID, department, and manager's name.
3. Provide information about your trip, including the trip purpose, start and end dates, primary destination, and departure city.
4. Specify your flight details, such as the flight departure and return dates, times, airlines, and numbers.
5. Enter your hotel details, including the name, address, phone number, check-in and check-out dates.
6. Indicate any emergency contacts, including name and phone number.
7. Mention any special requirements or needs you may have during the trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID.
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): If you have an employee ID, please enter it.
- **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Specify your department for organizational purposes.
- **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your direct manager's name.
- **Trip Purpose** (`trip_purpose`, text, required): Briefly describe the purpose of your trip.
- **Trip Start Date** (`trip_start_date`, date, required): Enter your departure date.
- **Trip End Date** (`trip_end_date`, date, required): Enter your return date.
- **Primary Destination** (`primary_destination`, text, required): Specify the main location or city of your trip.
- **Departure City** (`departure_city`, text, required): Enter the city where you're leaving from.
- **Flight Departure Date** (`flight_departure_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your outgoing flight.
- **Flight Departure Time** (`flight_departure_time`, time, optional): If you have a specific departure time, enter it.
- **Airline Name** (`airline_name`, text, optional): If you're flying with a specific airline, mention it.
- **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, text, optional): If you have a flight number, enter it.
- **Return Flight Date** (`flight_return_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your return flight.
- **Return Flight Time** (`flight_return_time`, time, optional): If you have a specific return time, enter it.
- **Return Flight Number** (`flight_return_number`, text, optional): If you have a return flight number, mention it.
- **Hotel Name** (`hotel_name`, text, required): Specify the name of your hotel accommodation.
- **Hotel Address** (`hotel_address`, text, optional): If you have the complete hotel address, enter it.
- **Hotel Phone Number** (`hotel_phone`, text, optional): If you have the hotel's contact number, mention it.
- **Check-in Date** (`check_in_date`, date, required): Enter your check-in date at the hotel.
- **Check-out Date** (`check_out_date`, date, required): Enter your check-out date from the hotel.
- **Daily Activities and Schedule** (`daily_activities`, text, optional): Briefly describe your planned activities and schedule during the trip.
- **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
- **Special Requirements or Needs** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any special needs or requirements, mention them.
- **Itinerary Submission Date** (`itinerary_submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submit this form to your manager.
