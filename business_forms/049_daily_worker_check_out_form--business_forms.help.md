# Daily Worker Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Worker Check Out Form is used to record the daily hours worked and other relevant details for a worker.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields, one at a time, following the instructions in the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, Number, Optional): Report your total hours worked on this day. Please note that this field is optional, so you can leave it blank if you are not reporting hours.
* **Check Out Time** (`check_out_time`, Time, Optional): Record the time you left work on this day. Please use the correct format for the time (e.g., 12:00 AM) and ensure it accurately reflects when you finished your tasks for the day.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief explanation for why you needed to leave work on this day. Be as detailed as possible, and make sure to specify the reason for your leave (e.g., medical emergency, appointment, etc.).
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your work email address. This will help your supervisor contact you if they need to discuss anything related to this form.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your work phone number. This will allow your supervisor to reach you quickly if needed.
* **Note** (`note`, Note, Optional): Use this field to add any additional comments or details that you think would be helpful for your supervisor to know. 

Note: The fields with a label "Optional" can be left blank unless you are specifically required to fill them out by your supervisor or the company's policy. 

## Tips
* Always be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions, please contact your supervisor for guidance.
* Make sure to submit the form before leaving work for the day.
