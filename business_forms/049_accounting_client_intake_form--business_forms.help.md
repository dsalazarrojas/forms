# Accounting Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients who are seeking accounting services. It helps us understand your business needs, including your industry, annual revenue, services required, and other relevant details. The information gathered will be used to tailor our services to meet your specific needs and ensure a smooth accounting experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Client Information**: Fill out the "Full Name or Business Name" field with the name of the client.
2. **Contact Person**: If the client is a business, enter the primary contact person's name in the "Primary Contact Person" field. Leave this field blank if it's an individual.
3. **Email Address**: Enter the client's email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. **Phone Number**: Enter the client's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. **Mailing Address**: Enter the client's mailing address in the "Mailing Address" field.
6. **Business Information**: Select the type of business entity from the dropdown list in the "Business Entity Type" field.
7. **Industry**: Enter the client's industry in the "Industry" field.
8. **Years in Business**: Enter the number of years the client has been in business in the "Years in Business" field. This is optional.
9. **EIN (Optional)**: If the client has an Employer Identification Number (EIN), enter it in the "EIN" field. This is optional.
10. **Number of Employees**: Enter the number of employees the client has in the "Number of Employees" field. This is optional.
11. **Annual Revenue**: Select the approximate annual revenue range from the dropdown list in the "Approximate Annual Revenue" field.
12. **Fiscal Year End**: Select the month when the client's fiscal year ends from the dropdown list in the "Fiscal Year End Month" field.
13. **Services Needed**: Select all the services the client requires from the dropdown list in the "Services Needed" field.
14. **Accounting Software**: Select the current accounting software the client is using from the dropdown list in the "Current Accounting Software" field.
15. **Previous Accountant**: Indicate if the client currently has an accountant or bookkeeper by selecting "True", "False", or "Previously had one" from the dropdown list in the "Do You Currently Have an Accountant or Bookkeeper" field.
16. **Reason for Seeking New Services**: If the client has a reason for seeking new accounting services, enter it in the "Reason for Seeking New Accounting Services" field. This is optional.
17. **Outstanding Tax Issues**: Indicate if the client has any outstanding tax issues or IRS notices by selecting "True" or "False" from the dropdown list in the "Are There Any Outstanding Tax Issues or IRS Notices" field.
18. **Preferred Communication Method**: Select the client's preferred communication method from the dropdown list in the "Preferred Communication Method" field.
19. **Additional Information**: If the client has any additional information or questions, enter them in the "Additional Information or Questions" field.
