# Startup Pitch Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Startup Pitch Evaluation Form" is designed to assess the feasibility and potential of a startup idea by evaluating its key aspects, including the pitch, business model, competitive advantage, financials, management team, and more. This form helps evaluators provide feedback to the startup teams, helping them refine their ideas and increase their chances of success.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by reading the "Startup Pitch" section, which allows you to describe your startup's idea, its goals, and why it has potential for success.
2.  In the "Business Plan" section, provide an overview of your business model, including your target market, competition landscape, and revenue model.
3.  Describe your startup's unique selling point in the "Uniqueness Of Your Product" section.
4.  Outline your business model, including how you plan to generate revenue and manage your finances.
5.  Introduce your management team, highlighting their skills and experience relevant to your startup.
6.  Discuss the market trends that influence your startup's success.
7.  Explain your competitive advantage over other businesses in the same market.
8.  Describe your future plans and how they align with your business model.
9.  Write a brief statement on why your startup is worth investing in, in the "Why Our Startup" section.
10.  Evaluate your startup's feasibility with the "Evaluation" section, where you answer "Yes" or "No" based on the provided options.
11.  In the "Start Up Pitch" section, you can attach any additional information or documents that support your startup idea.
12.  Use the "Final Remarks" section to add any final comments or questions.
13.  In the "Overall Evaluation" section, provide a summary of your startup's potential.
14.  If requested, rate your startup using the "Rating" scale provided.
15.  Finally, recommend potential partners or investors based on your startup's strengths, and provide any additional remarks in the "Final Remark" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pitch Evaluation Form**

  * **Start Up Pitch** (`id: 1`, type: text, optional): A brief summary of your startup idea, highlighting its potential for success.
  * **Business Plan** (`id: 2`, type: text, optional): A detailed overview of your business model, including target market, revenue model, and financials.
  * **Start Up Pitch** (`id: 3`, type: text, optional): A more in-depth description of your startup, its goals, and why you believe it has potential for success.
  * **Target Market** (`id: 4`, type: text, optional): Describe your target audience, including their demographics, needs, and preferences.
  * **Competition Landscape** (`id: 5`, type: text, optional): Outline the competitive companies in your market and how your startup differs from them.
  * **Uniqueness Of Your Product** (`id: 6`, type: text, optional): Highlight what makes your product unique and how it addresses the needs of your target market.
  * **Business Model** (`id: 7`, type: text, optional): Describe how your startup will generate revenue and manage its finances.
  * **Financials** (`id: 8`, type: text, optional): Provide an overview of your financial projections and how you plan to manage your finances.
  * **Management Team** (`id: 9`, type: text, optional): Introduce your management team, highlighting their relevant skills and experience.
  * **Revenue Model** (`id: 10`, type: text, optional): Outline your revenue streams and how you plan to generate income.
  * **Market Trends** (`id: 11`, type: text, optional): Discuss the market trends that influence your startup's success.
  * **Competitive Advantage** (`id: 12`, type: text, optional): Explain how your startup has a competitive edge over other businesses in the same market.
  * **Future Plans** (`id: 13`, type: text, optional): Describe your future plans, including product development, market expansion, and team growth.
  * **Why Our Startup** (`id: 14`, type: text, optional): Write a brief statement on why your startup is worth investing in.
  * **Evaluation** (`id: 15`, type: select_one, optional): Evaluate your startup's feasibility with "Yes" or "No" based on the provided options.
  * **Start Up Pitch** (`id: 16`, type: note, optional): Attach any additional information or documents that support your startup idea.
  * **Final Remarks** (`id: 17`, type: text, optional): Add any final comments or questions.
  * **Overall Evaluation** (`id: 18`, type: text, optional): Summarize your startup's potential based on the provided information.
  * **Rating** (`id: 19`, type: number, optional): Rate your startup using the provided scale.
  * **Recommendation** (`id: 20`, type: select_multiple, optional): Recommend potential partners or investors based on your startup's strengths.
  * **Final Remark** (`id: 21`, type: text, optional): Provide any additional remarks or questions.
  * **Rating Scale** (`id: 22`, type: text, optional): Rate your startup's overall potential using the provided scale.
  * **Rating Score** (`id: 23`, type: number, optional): Provide a numerical rating for your startup's overall potential.
  * **Evaluation Score** (`id: 24`, type: number, optional): Provide an evaluation score for your startup.
  * **Final Evaluation** (`id: 25`, type: text, optional): Summarize your final evaluation of your startup.
