# PTA Debit Card Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The PTA Debit Card Request Form is used to request a debit card for a PTA member to make purchases on behalf of the school or PTA. This form helps the PTA track and manage its expenses, ensuring transparency and accountability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, including your name, email, phone number, and role in the PTA.
2. Select the category for your request (PTA, school parent organization, or other).
3. Provide a brief description of your request, including the name of the card holder and the purpose of the purchase.
4. Specify the usage of the debit card, if any additional information is required, please provide it in the "Additional Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Pta Debit Card Request Form** (`pta_debit_card_request_form_data`, text, **required**): This is the description of your request. Please provide a brief description of why you need a debit card and how you will use it.
- **Pta Debit Card Request Form Category** (`pta_debit_card_request_form_category`, select_one, **optional**): Select the category for your request (PTA, school parent organization, or other). If you are unsure of the category, please choose "other".
- **Pta Debit Card Request Form Name** (`pta_debit_card_request_form_name`, text, **optional**): Please enter your name as the card holder, if this is not you, please include the name of the person who will be using the card.
- **Pta Debit Card Request Form Email** (`pta_debit_card_request_form_email`, email, **optional**): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Pta Debit Card Request Form Phone** (`pta_debit_card_request_form_phone`, text, **optional**): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Pta Debit Card Request Form Role** (`pta_debit_card_request_form_role`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select one or more roles you hold in the PTA (Treasurer, President, or other). If you are unsure, choose "other".
- **Pta Debit Card Request Form Usage** (`pta_debit_card_request_form_usage`, text, **optional**): If there is any specific usage of the card, please specify it here.
- **Pta Debit Card Request Form Additional Info** (`pta_debit_card_request_form_additional_info`, text, **optional**): If you have any additional information to provide, please enter it here.

## Tips
- Please make sure to provide clear and accurate information to avoid any confusion.
- If you are unsure of any field, please ask your PTA administrator or contact the PTA leadership for assistance.
