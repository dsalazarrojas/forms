# Courier Enquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a courier service request from a client. It is intended to be used by customers who want to send packages to different destinations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client details section with your information, including your name, contact phone number, and contact email.
2. In the package details section, provide information about the package you want to send, including its type, weight, and dimensions.
3. Specify the delivery address and date in the delivery details section.
4. Ensure you select the correct package type from the options provided.
5. Fill in your client phone number and email in the client phone and email sections.
6. Finally, provide any additional comments or notes about your request in the client note section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details**
	+ `client_details`: This section is used to collect your information as the client. Please enter your name, contact phone number, and contact email.
* **Package Details**
	+ `package_details`: This section is used to provide information about the package you want to send. Enter a brief description of the package.
* **Delivery Details**
	+ `delivery_details`: This is the section where you specify the delivery address and date.
* **Delivery Address**
	+ `delivery_address`: Enter the address where the package should be delivered.
* **Package Type**
	+ `package_type`: Select the type of package you want to send from the options provided.
* **Delivery Date**
	+ `delivery_date`: Enter the date when you want the package to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time**
	+ `delivery_time`: Select the time of day when you want the package to be delivered.
* **Client Phone**
	+ `client_phone`: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Client Email**
	+ `client_email`: Enter your contact email.
* **Package Weight**
	+ `package_weight`: Enter the weight of the package.
* **Package Length**
	+ `package_length`: Enter the length of the package.
* **Package Width**
	+ `package_width`: Enter the width of the package.
* **Package Height**
	+ `package_height`: Enter the height of the package.
* **Client Note**
	+ `client_note`: Provide any additional comments or notes about your request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our support team.
* If you have submitted a similar request in the past 30 days, you will be prevented from submitting this form again.
