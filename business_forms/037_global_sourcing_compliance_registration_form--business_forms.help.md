# Global Sourcing Compliance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Sourcing Compliance Registration Form is a document used to gather information from suppliers regarding their compliance status with global sourcing regulations. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that all required information is provided and that your supplier is aware of their compliance obligations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about your supplier before starting.
2. Review each field carefully and fill in the required information.
3. Be thorough and accurate when providing your contact information and supplier details.
4. Select the correct compliance program your supplier is part of (if applicable).
5. Indicate the type of vendor your supplier represents (if applicable).
6. Type a note about your submission (if desired).
7. Enter your email address (if applicable).
8. Enter your phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information about your supplier that you want to provide. You can enter as much or as little text as you need.
* **Supplier Information** (`supplier_info`, text, optional): This field is for any additional details about your supplier. You can enter as much or as little text as you need.
* **Contact Information** (`contact`, text, optional): This field is for your contact information. Please enter your name or title, company name, and address.
* **Compliance Program** (`compliance_program`, select, optional): This field is for selecting which compliance program your supplier is part of. Please choose one of the provided options.
* **Vendor Type** (`vendor_type`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for indicating which type of vendor your supplier represents. Please choose one or more of the provided options.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, note, optional): This field is for providing a note about your submission. You can type as much or as little text as you need.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is for entering your email address. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is for entering your phone number. Please enter a valid phone number.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your compliance officer or supplier representative for guidance.
* This form is for internal use only, please do not share it with anyone outside the company.
