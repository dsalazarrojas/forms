# gdpr_contact_us_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The GDPR Contact Us form is designed to collect and manage personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation. It is intended for customers, partners, and internal staff to submit requests or inquiries related to GDPR.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Provide your full name and a valid email address in the "Full Name" and "Email" fields, respectively.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if applicable.
4. In the "Message" field, describe your GDPR-related inquiry or request.
5. If you have a specific date or time constraint, enter it in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
6. If you need to select one of the provided options, choose from the "Select" dropdown menu. For example, if you are asking about data protection, choose "Yes" to indicate that you are looking for data protection advice.
7. If you need to select multiple options, use the "Select 2" dropdown menu to choose from the available options. For example, if you are asking about data protection and data privacy, choose both "Yes" options.
8. Add any additional comments or information in the "Note" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`name`, `text`, required): This field is where you enter your full name, as it will be displayed on our records and communication with you.
* **Email** (`email`, required): This is your valid email address that we will use to respond to your inquiry.
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here so we can contact you if needed.
* **Message** (`text`, required): Describe your GDPR-related inquiry or request in this field.
* **Date** (`date`, optional): If you have a specific date constraint or deadline for your inquiry, enter it here.
* **Time** (`time`, optional): If you have a specific time constraint or deadline for your inquiry, enter it here.
* **Select** (`select_one`, optional): Choose "Yes" if you are looking for advice or information about data protection, "No" if you are not, or "Other" if your request is not related to data protection.
* **Select 2** (`select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or both of the available options if you have multiple inquiries or requests. This field is not for "Other" options, use the "Select" field for those.
* **Note** (`note`, optional): Add any additional comments or information related to your GDPR inquiry.
* **Email 2** (`email`, optional): This is a secondary email address that we can use to contact you, if desired.
* **Phone 2** (`text`, optional): This is a secondary phone number that we can use to contact you, if desired.
* **Other Selects** (`select_multiple`, optional): This field is not applicable as it is not used in the form.
