# Annual Report Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Report Access Request Form is designed to capture user information and request access to annual reports. It will guide you in providing the required details for a successful request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information.
2. Specify the type of requester you are (e.g., shareholder, employee, etc.).
3. Choose the report year and format you require.
4. Specify the purpose of accessing the report and how you intend to use it.
5. Confirm your agreement to maintain confidentiality and data protection.
6. Choose your preferred delivery method.
7. Mark if your request is urgent.
8. Add any additional comments or requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name**: Provide your first and last name to help us verify your identity.
	+ Example: John Doe
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email address.
	+ Format: john.doe@example.com
* **Phone Number** (optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
	+ Format: (123) 456-7890
* **Company or Organization Name** (optional): Enter the name of the organization you represent.
	+ Example: ABC Corporation
* **Position or Title** (optional): Enter your position or title within the organization.
	+ Example: CEO
* **Type of Requester**: Choose the category that best describes you:
	+ Shareholder
	+ Employee
	+ Customer
	+ Investor
	+ Analyst
	+ Media
	+ Academic
	+ General Public
	+ Other
* **Relationship to Organization**: Describe your connection to the organization.
	+ Example: I am a shareholder
* **Which year report do you need**: Choose the year of the report you need:
	+ Current year
	+ Previous year
	+ 2-3 years back
	+ Multiple years
	+ All available reports
* **Preferred Report Format**: Choose your desired format for the report:
	+ PDF
	+ Print copy
	+ Online access
	+ Email delivery
	+ Any format acceptable
* **Specific Sections or Information Needed** (optional): If you need only certain parts of the report, specify them here.
	+ Example: I only need the financial section
* **Purpose of Accessing the Report**: Describe why you need the report:
	+ Investment research
	+ Business analysis
	+ Academic research
	+ Regulatory compliance
	+ Media inquiry
	+ General information
	+ Due diligence
	+ Other
* **Intended Use of Report**: Describe how you will use the information:
	+ Example: For investment research
* **Are you requesting a public or confidential report** (required): Choose your preference:
	+ Public - can be shared
	+ Confidential - for internal use only
	+ No preference
* **Do you need authorization from a manager** (optional): Check if you require authorization:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Unsure
* **Manager or authorized person name** (if applicable): Enter the name of the person authorizing your request:
	+ Example: John Smith
* **Will you sign an NDA if required** (required): Agree to sign a non-disclosure agreement:
	+ Yes, I agree
	+ No
	+ Need to review
* **I commit to maintaining confidentiality** (required): Confirm your commitment to confidentiality:
	+ I agree
	+ I do not agree
* **I consent to data protection and privacy terms** (required): Agree to data protection terms:
	+ I agree
	+ I do not agree
* **Preferred delivery method**: Choose how you want to receive the report:
	+ Email
	+ Download link
	+ Physical mail
	+ Portal access
	+ No preference
* **Is this an urgent request** (optional): Mark if you need the report quickly:
	+ Yes - urgent
	+ No - standard timeline acceptable
* **Additional comments or requirements** (optional): Add any further information or requirements:
	+ Example: Please expedite the report as quickly as possible.
