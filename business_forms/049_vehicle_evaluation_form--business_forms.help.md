# vehicle_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect detailed information about a vehicle, including its characteristics and condition.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of vehicle you are evaluating (Sedan, SUV, Truck).
2. Enter the engine size of the vehicle (in cubic centimeters or liters).
3. Choose the transmission type (Manual, Automatic, Continuously Variable Transmission).
4. Select the drive type (Front Wheel Drive, Rear Wheel Drive, All Wheel Drive).
5. Choose the fuel type (Gasoline, Diesel, Hybrid).
6. Enter the mileage of the vehicle (in kilometers or miles).
7. Enter the price of the vehicle (in currency of your choice).
8. Enter the year of the vehicle.
9. Select the condition of the vehicle (New, Used).
10. Enter the make and model of the vehicle.
11. Select any relevant features of the vehicle (Low mileage, High performance, Comfortable, Low maintenance).
12. Select any safety features of the vehicle (Airbag system, Anti-lock braking system, Electronic stability system).
13. Choose the warranty period (1 year or less, 2-3 years, 4-5 years).
14. Enter any notes about the vehicle.
15. Enter your email address.
16. Enter your phone number.
17. Enter your address.
18. Upload an image of the vehicle.
19. Enter the date the vehicle was purchased.
20. Enter the location where the vehicle was purchased.
21. Enter the owner's name.
22. Enter the vehicle's number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Vehicle Type
* **Vehicle Type** (Sedan, SUV, Truck): This field asks you to select the type of vehicle you are evaluating. Choose the correct type from the available options.

### Engine Size
* **Engine Size** (number): Enter the engine size of the vehicle in cubic centimeters or liters. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank.

### Transmission
* **Transmission** (Manual, Automatic, Continuously Variable Transmission): Select the type of transmission used in the vehicle. Choose from the available options.

### Drive Type
* **Drive Type** (Front Wheel Drive, Rear Wheel Drive, All Wheel Drive): Select the drive type of the vehicle. Choose from the available options.

### Fuel Type
* **Fuel Type** (Gasoline, Diesel, Hybrid): Choose the type of fuel used in the vehicle. Select from the available options.

### Mileage
* **Mileage** (number): Enter the mileage of the vehicle. This is the total mileage of the vehicle.

### Price
* **Price** (number): Enter the price of the vehicle in the currency of your choice.

### Year
* **Year** (number): Enter the year of the vehicle.

### Vehicle Condition
* **Vehicle Condition** (New, Used): Select the condition of the vehicle (New or Used). If the vehicle is new, select New. If it's used, select Used.

### Make/Model
* **Make/Model** (text): Enter the make and model of the vehicle. For example, "Toyota Camry".

### Description
* **Description** (Select Multiple): Choose any relevant features of the vehicle from the available options. Select all that apply.

### Features
* **Features** (Select Multiple): Choose any relevant safety features of the vehicle from the available options. Select all that apply.

### Color
* **Color** (Select One): Select the color of the vehicle from the available options.

### Safety Features
* **Safety Features** (Select Multiple): Choose any safety features of the vehicle from the available options. Select all that apply.

### Warranty
* **Warranty** (Select Multiple): Choose the warranty period from the available options.

### Notes
* **Notes** (text): Enter any notes about the vehicle.

### Email
* **Email** (text): Enter your email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.

### Address
* **Address** (text): Enter your address.

### Vehicle Image
* **Vehicle Image** (text): Upload an image of the vehicle.

### Date Purchased
* **Date Purchased** (date): Enter the date the vehicle was purchased.

### Purchased At
* **Purchased At** (time): Enter the location where the vehicle was purchased.

### Owner
* **Owner** (text): Enter the owner's name.

### Vehicle Number
* **Vehicle Number** (text): Enter the vehicle's number (if applicable).

### Comments
* **Comments** (text): Enter any comments about the vehicle.
