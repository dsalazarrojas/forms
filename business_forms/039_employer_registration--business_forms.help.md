# employer-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employer-registration form is designed to collect essential information about the employer and their job positions. This information will be used to create an employer profile and facilitate communication between the employer and their employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the company details, including company name, description, URL, industry, and type.
2. Provide contact information, including email, phone number, and username.
3. Fill in the job position details, including title, description, benefits, and salary.
4. Optional: Fill in any additional fields that apply to your company and job position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employer Registration** (1, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Email** (2, email, required): Enter your company's email address.
* **Company Name** (3, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Name** (4, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (5, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (6, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Company URL** (7, text, required): Enter the URL of your company.
* **Company Description** (8, text, required): Briefly describe your company.
* **Company Industry** (9, select multiple, required): Select any industries that apply to your company.
* **Company Type** (10, select one, required): Select the type of company you are.
* **Company Size** (11, select one, required): Select the size of your company.
* **Company Owner Name** (12, text, required): Enter the name of the company owner.
* **Company Owner Role** (13, text, required): Enter the role of the company owner.
* **Company Location** (14, select one, required): Select the location of your company.
* **Company Website** (15, text, required): Enter the URL of your company website.
* **Contact Username** (16, text, required): Enter a unique username for your contact person.
* **Job Position Title** (17, text, required): Enter the title of the job position.
* **Job Position Description** (18, text, required): Briefly describe the job position.
* **Job Position Benefits** (19, select multiple, required): Select any benefits that apply to the job position.
* **Employee Number** (20, text, required): Enter the number of employees you have.
* **Employee 2** (21, text, optional): Enter any additional employee details.
* **Industry Type 1** (22, select one, optional): Select any industry types that apply to your company.
* **Industry Type 2** (23, select one, optional): Select any additional industry types that apply to your company.
* **Job Position Benefits** (24, select multiple, optional): Select any additional benefits that apply to the job position.
* **Job Position Salary** (25, number, optional): Enter the salary range of the job position.

## Tips

* Double-check your company and job position details to ensure accuracy.
* Use a unique contact username and email address.
* Provide as much detail as possible to ensure successful registration.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the contact person or HR department.
* Make sure to complete all required fields to avoid delays in the registration process.
