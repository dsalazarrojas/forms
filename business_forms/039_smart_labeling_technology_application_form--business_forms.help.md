# Smart Labeling Technology Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Smart Labeling Technology Application Form is used to gather information for the Smart Labeling Technology application. This form is designed to collect relevant details about the application, including organizational information, submission status, review status, and reviewer information. Please note that the form is not a standard business application form; it is specifically tailored for a custom business application.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the application form with the required and optional fields, as marked.
2. Ensure that you have all necessary information before submitting the form.
3. Review and update any fields as needed before final submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Application Form (id: 1)
* **Application Form (Application Form)**: This field is the main application form.
* **organization (id: 2)**: Please enter the name of your organization.
* **Name (id: 3)**: Enter your full name.
* **Email (id: 4)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (id: 5)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address (id: 6)**: Enter your address.
* **Notes (id: 7)**: Enter any additional notes about your application.

### Submission Information (id: 8-12)
* **Categories (id: 8)**: Select all categories that apply to your submission.
* **Submission Type (id: 9)**: Select the type of submission you are making.
* **Submission Status (id: 10)**: Select the status of your submission.
* **Submission Date (id: 11)**: Select the date of your submission.
* **Submission Time (id: 12)**: Select the time of your submission.
* **Submission Notes (id: 13)**: Enter any additional notes about your submission.

### Review Information (id: 14-18)
* **Review Status (id: 14)**: Select the status of your review.
* **Review Date (id: 15)**: Select the date of your review.
* **Review Time (id: 16)**: Select the time of your review.
* **Review Notes (id: 17)**: Enter any additional notes about your review.
* **Review Comments (id: 18)**: Enter any comments about your review.

### Reviewer Information (id: 19-24)
* **Reviewer Name (id: 19)**: Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Department (id: 20)**: Enter the department of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Title (id: 21)**: Enter the title of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email (id: 22)**: Enter the email of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Phone (id: 23)**: Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Notes (id: 24)**: Enter any additional notes about the reviewer.

### Form Information (id: 25)
* **Form ID (id: 25)**: Enter the ID of the form.
