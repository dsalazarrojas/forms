# swap-item-tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "swap-item-tracker" form is designed for tracking and managing the status and details of items being swapped or exchanged within a business setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, fill out the fields as needed for each item. Please ensure that you have the necessary information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name** (`user_info_form`, text, optional): Enter the name of the user submitting the form.
*   **Status** (`exchange_status_form`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the item being swapped or exchanged from the dropdown menu.
*   **Select Status** (`exchange_status_form_options`, text, optional): Provide additional details or description of the item's status.
*   **Photo 1** (`photo`, note, optional): Upload or reference a photo related to the item being swapped or exchanged.
*   **Photo 2** (`photo_alt`, note, optional): Upload or reference another photo related to the item being swapped or exchanged.
*   **Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the item.
*   **Swap Date** (`swap_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the swap in mm/dd/yyyy format.
*   **Exchange Date** (`exchange_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the exchange or swap in mm/dd/yyyy format.

## Tips
*   Ensure that you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
*   If a field is not required, it can be left blank.
*   Use the dropdown menu to select the status of the item accurately.
*   Use clear and concise language when entering notes or comments.
*   Enter dates in the mm/dd/yyyy format for consistency.
