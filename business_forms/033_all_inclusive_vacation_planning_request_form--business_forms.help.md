# All Inclusive Vacation Planning Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients about their vacation preferences and requirements to create an all-inclusive quote or plan. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your needs are met.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. Please make sure to check the "Required" fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (3, Email, Required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (4, Text, Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (5, Select One, Required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone call, Text message, Any method).
* **Destination Preference** (6, Select One, Required): Select where you would like to go (Caribbean, Mexico, Hawaii, Europe, Asia, South Pacific, No preference).
* **Preferred Travel Dates** (7, Text, Required): Enter your ideal travel dates.
* **Date Flexibility** (8, Select One, Required): Choose how flexible you are with your travel dates (Very flexible, Somewhat flexible, Fixed dates).
* **Trip Duration** (9, Select One, Required): Select the length of your vacation (3-5 days, 6-7 days, 8-10 days, 11-14 days, 15+ days).
* **Number of Adults** (10, Number, Required): Enter the number of adults traveling with you.
* **Number of Children** (11, Number, Optional): Enter the number of children traveling with you.
* **Children Ages** (12, Text, Optional): List the ages of the children traveling with you.
* **Accommodation Type** (13, Select One, Required): Choose your preferred accommodation type (Standard room, Ocean view room, Junior suite, Master suite, Private villa, Overwater bungalow).
* **Budget Per Person** (14, Select One, Required): Select your budget per person (Under $1500, $1500-$2500, $2501-$4000, $4001-$6000, $6001-$10000, No budget limit).
* **Most Important Inclusions** (15, Select Multiple, Required): Choose your top three inclusions (Unlimited dining, Premium beverages, Water sports, Spa access, Golf, Kids club, Entertainment, Airport transfers, Excursions).
* **Dining Preferences** (16, Select Multiple, Optional): Select any dietary restrictions or preferences (No restrictions, Vegetarian options, Vegan options, Gluten-free, Kosher, Halal, Allergy-friendly).
* **Special Occasion** (17, Select One, Optional): Check if you are celebrating a special occasion (None, Honeymoon, Anniversary, Birthday, Graduation, Retirement, Family reunion, Other).
* **Flight Requirements** (18, Select One, Required): Choose your flight requirements (Include flights, Book separately, Have flights already).
* **Departure City** (19, Text, Required): Enter the city where you will be departing from.
* **Travel Insurance** (20, Select One, Required): Check if you want travel insurance (Yes, include, No, decline, Need more information).
* **Accessibility Requirements** (21, Text, Optional): Enter any special accessibility requirements.
* **Room Preferences** (22, Text, Optional): Enter any specific room preferences.
* **Activities Interest** (23, Text, Optional): Enter any activities or interests you enjoy.
* **Previous All-Inclusive Experiences** (24, Text, Optional): Enter any previous all-inclusive experiences you have had.
* **How Did You Find Us?** (25, Select One, Optional): Check how you found out about us (Online search, Social media, Referral, Previous client, Advertisement, Travel show, Other).
* **Additional Comments** (26, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Availability for Consultation** (27, Text, Optional): Enter any availability for consultation or further discussion.

Please review the form carefully before submitting it. If you have any questions or need further assistance, feel free to ask!
