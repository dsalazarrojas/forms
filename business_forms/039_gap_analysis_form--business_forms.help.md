# Gap Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gap Analysis Form is a tool to help you identify and document performance gaps between your current and future performance goals. This form will help you assess areas where you need to improve and focus your efforts on closing the gaps.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by filling out the current performance section** on Page 1 of the form. Here, you'll provide information about your current performance status.
2. **Move on to Page 2** and enter your future performance goals.
3. **On Page 3** of the form, calculate the performance gap by providing the difference between your future and current performance.
4. **Submit the form** to complete the exercise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Current Performance** (`current_performance`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your current performance status.
* **Future Performance** (`future_performance`, text, optional): Describe your future performance goals or expectations.
* **Performance Gap** (`performance_gap`, text, optional): Calculate the performance gap by subtracting your current performance from your future performance.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide honest and realistic assessments of your current and future performance.
* The performance gap field should reflect the difference between your future and current performance goals.
