# IT Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Support Request Form is designed to collect information from users regarding IT support requests, allowing IT staff to track and manage requests efficiently. This form is used to report issues, ask for help, or provide feedback on IT-related matters.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including all the required fields.
2. Choose the correct category and priority for your request.
3. Provide a clear and concise description of the issue.
4. Select the assigned user or tool for your request.
5. Set the correct status and priority.
6. Submit the form to send it to the IT staff for review and action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Category** (`it_category`): Choose the category that best describes the type of request you're making (e.g., Help Desk, Hardware, Software, Network).
* **Request Subject** (`it_request_subject`): Enter a brief description of the issue or request.
* **Request Description** (`it_request_description`): Provide a clear and concise description of the issue or request.
* **Assigned To** (`it_assigned_to`): Select the user who will be responsible for handling your request (e.g., chatjimmy, supportuser).
* **Priority** (`it_priority`): Choose the priority level of your request (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
* **Status** (`it_status`): Set the status of your request (e.g., Open, Closed).
* **Request Date** (`it_request_date`): This field will be automatically populated with the current date.
* **Request Time** (`it_request_time`): This field will be automatically populated with the current time.
* **Note** (`it_note`): Add any additional information or notes about your request.
* **Email** (`it_email`): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`it_phone`): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Assigned Tool** (`it_assigned_tool`): Select the tool or application used to handle your request (e.g., chatjimmy, supportuser).
* **Comment** (`it_comment`): Add any additional comments or details about your request.
* **Category** (`it_category_id`): Choose the category for your request (e.g., Business Forms, IT Forms).
* **File** (`it_file`): Attach any relevant files or attachments to support your request.
* **Description** (`it_description`): Enter a brief description of the attached file.
* **Keywords** (`it_keywords`): Enter any relevant keywords or tags to help identify your request.
* **Tags** (`it_tags`): Enter any additional tags or keywords to help identify your request.
* **Created By** (`it_created_by`): This field will be automatically populated with the creator's name.
* **Created On** (`it_created_on`): This field will be automatically populated with the current date.
* **Updated By** (`it_updated_by`): Choose the user who last updated your request (e.g., Jim Bob, Jane Doe).
* **Updated On** (`it_updated_on`): This field will be automatically populated with the last update date.
* **Deleted By** (`it_deleted_by`): Choose the user who deleted your request (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Deleted On** (`it_deleted_on`): This field will be automatically populated with the deletion date.
* **Deleted At** (`it_deleted_at`): This field will be automatically populated with the deletion date.
* **Version** (`it_version`): Enter the version number of your request.
* **Created At** (`it_created_at`): This field will be automatically populated with the creation date.
* **Updated At** (`it_updated_at`): This field will be automatically populated with the last update date.

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the IT Support Request Form accurately and efficiently, ensuring that your requests are handled by the IT staff in a timely and effective manner.
