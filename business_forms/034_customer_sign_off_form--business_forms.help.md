# Customer Sign Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer Sign Off Form is a document that verifies the completion of a service or agreement between a service provider and a customer. It is used to confirm that the customer has received and acknowledged the service, and that the service provider has provided the agreed-upon service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "customer name" field. This is the name of the person signing this document.
2. Enter your email address in the "customer email" field. This will be used as a point of contact for any further communication.
3. Enter the name of the service provider who delivered the service.
4. Select the date you signed the document in the "date signed" field.
5. Select the time you signed the document in the "time signed" field.
6. Provide a brief summary of the terms and conditions of the agreement in the "terms and conditions" field. Please note that this field has a fixed size limit.
7. Provide your signature to confirm that you have received and understood the service.
8. The service provider will provide their signature to confirm that they have provided the agreed-upon service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer name** (customer_name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Customer email** (customer_email, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you can be reached at for any further communication.
* **Service provider** (service_provider, text, required): Enter the name of the person or company that delivered the service.
* **Date signed** (date_signed, date, required): Select the date that you signed this document.
* **Time signed** (time_signed, time, required): Select the time that you signed this document.
* **Terms and conditions** (terms_and_conditions, note, required): Provide a brief summary of the agreement. Please keep it concise and within the size limit.
* **Customer signature** (customer_signature, note, required): Draw or print your signature to confirm that you have received and understood the service.
* **Service provider signature** (service_provider_signature, note, required): The service provider will sign this field to confirm that they have provided the agreed-upon service.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* The service provider will review and verify the information provided before finalizing the agreement.
* Keep the "terms and conditions" field brief and concise.
* Ensure that your signature is legible and clear.
