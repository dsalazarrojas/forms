# Wholesale Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Wholesale Order Form is a critical document that allows customers to place and confirm wholesale orders with our company. This form is used to collect essential information from customers, including their contact details, order specifics, and product selections. By filling out this form, customers can ensure that their wholesale orders are accurately processed and delivered on time.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer information section, including your email address and phone number.
2. Enter the date and time of the order.
3. Select the products you wish to order.
4. Confirm your order details, including name, address, and order date and time.
5. Review and confirm all entered information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`customer_email`, `text`, required): Enter your email address to receive order confirmations and updates.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required): Enter your shipping address to ensure accurate order delivery.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, required): Describe the specific details of your order.
* **Select Products** (`select_products`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the products you wish to order.
* **Confirm Name** (`confirm_name`, `text`, required): Confirm your name as it appears on your order.
* **Confirm Address** (`confirm_address`, `text`, required): Confirm your shipping address.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, `text`, required): Confirm your email address.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, `text`, required): Confirm your phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **Confirm Order Details** (`confirm_order_details`, `text`, required): Confirm the specific details of your order.
* **Confirm Order Date** (`confirm_order_date`, `date`, optional): Confirm the date of your order.
* **Confirm Order Time** (`confirm_order_time`, `time`, optional): Confirm the time of your order.
* **Confirm Products** (`confirm_products`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm the products you selected to order.

Note: Please review all fields carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
