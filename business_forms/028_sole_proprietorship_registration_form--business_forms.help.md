# Sole Proprietorship Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a business for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with accurate information about the business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (1): Enter the name of the business.
* **Owner Name** (2): Enter the name of the owner of the business.
* **Address** (3): Enter the address of the business.
* **City** (4): Enter the city of the business.
* **State** (5): Enter the state of the business.
* **Zip** (6): Enter the zip code of the business.
* **Country** (7): Enter the country of the business.
* **Phone** (8): Enter the phone number of the business.
* **Email** (9): Enter the email address of the business. (Note: This field is for the business's email address, not the owner's personal email.)
* **Registration Date** (10): Enter the date of business registration.
* **Business Type** (11): Choose the type of business from the list:
	+ Sole Proprietorship
	+ Corporation
	+ Partnership
	+ LLC
	+ Other
* **Business Purpose** (12): Enter a brief description of the business purpose.
* **Tax ID** (13): Enter the Tax ID number of the business.
* **Tax Residence** (14): Enter the tax residence of the business.
* **Registered Business** (15): Indicate if the business is registered:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Registration Status** (16): Choose the registration status of the business from the list:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
	+ Pending
* **Business Address** (17): Enter the address of the business.
* **Business City** (18): Enter the city of the business.
* **Business State** (19): Enter the state of the business.
* **Business Zip** (20): Enter the zip code of the business.
* **Business Country** (21): Enter the country of the business.
* **Tax Filing Status** (22): Indicate if the business is taxed:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Number of Employees** (23): Enter the number of employees in the business.
* **Business Ownership** (24): Enter a brief description of the business ownership.
* **Submission Date** (25): Enter the date of submission of the form.

Note: All fields are required unless otherwise specified.
