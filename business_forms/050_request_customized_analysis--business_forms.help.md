# Request Customized Analysis - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from project submitters to request a customized analysis for their project. The form is used to gather project requirements, submitter details, and other relevant information for the analysis process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Enter your project requirements, project name, project type, and project deadline.
3. Select the project manager responsible for the project.
4. Provide a brief description of the project.
5. Enter your submitter information, including email, phone number, and company (if applicable).
6. Sign and date the form with your signature and acknowledgement of the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Customized Analysis** (Page 1, text): This is the main description of your project and analysis requirements.
* **Project Requirements** (Page 1, text): Enter a brief description of your project requirements.
* **Project Name** (Page 1, text): Enter the name of your project.
* **Project Description** (Page 1, text): Provide a brief description of your project.
* **Project Type** (Page 1, select_one): Choose the type of analysis report you need (e.g. Analysis Report, Status Report, Progress Report, Meeting Summary).
* **Project Deadline** (Page 1, date): Enter the deadline for the analysis report.
* **Project Manager** (Page 1, select_multiple): Select the project manager responsible for the project.
* **Analysis Requirements** (Page 1, text): Enter any additional analysis requirements not covered in the previous fields.
* **Submitter Comments** (Page 1, text): Enter any comments or notes you'd like to include with your submission.
* **Submitter Information** (Page 1, note): This is for any additional information or clarification you'd like to include with your submission.
* **Submitter Email** (Page 1, email): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (Page 1, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Address** (Page 1, text): Enter your address (if applicable).
* **Submitter Company** (Page 1, text): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Submitter Job Title** (Page 1, text): Enter your job title.
* **Submitter Department** (Page 1, text): Enter your department (if applicable).
* **Submitter Designation** (Page 1, text): Enter your designation (if applicable).
* **Submitter Designation Level** (Page 1, number): Enter your designation level (if applicable).
* **Submitter Designation Date** (Page 1, date): Enter the date of your designation (if applicable).
* **Submitter Designation Date Range** (Page 1, date): Enter the range of dates for your designation (if applicable).
* **Submitter Designation Location** (Page 1, text): Enter the location of your designation (if applicable).
* **Submitter Additional Info** (Page 1, text): Enter any additional information you'd like to include with your submission.
* **Submitter Signature** (Page 1, time): Sign and date your submission.
* **Submitter Agreement** (Page 1, text): Confirm that you have read and agree with the terms and conditions.
* **Submitter Acknowledgement** (Page 1, text): Confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.

I hope this help guide is clear and concise. If you have any further questions or clarification, please let me know!
