# Web Hosting Contract Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Web Hosting Contract Form is a tool used to collect customer information and service requirements for hosting purposes. This form helps the customer and the service provider (host) understand each other's expectations and requirements, ensuring a smooth hosting experience.

## How To Complete This Form

Before you start filling out the form, make sure you have all the necessary information ready. This includes your personal and contact details, your hosting preferences, and the service requirements you need for your hosting needs. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any issues or misunderstandings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required):
  Please fill in your personal details, including your name, address, email, and phone number. This information will be used to contact you and manage your hosting account.

* **Hosting Preferences** (`hosting_preferences`, select multiple, optional):
  If you have any hosting preferences or requirements, please select them from the options provided. These can include preferences for server location, backup frequency, and security features.

* **Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`, note, required):
  This section is reserved for any contract terms or agreements that we need to know about. Please enter any relevant information that is not already covered in the other fields.

* **Service Start Date** (`service_start_date`, date, required):
  Please enter the start date you would like your hosting service to begin.

* **Service End Date** (`service_end_date`, date, required):
  Please enter the end date you would like your hosting service to end.

* **Service Preferences** (`service_preferences`, select multiple, optional):
  If you have any preferences or requirements for your service, please select them from the options provided. These can include preferences for support, updates, and security features.

* **Billing Details** (`billing_details`, text, required):
  Please enter your billing information, including your billing address, payment method, and any other relevant details.

* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required):
  Please enter your contact information, including your phone number and any other relevant details. This will help us contact you in case of any issues or questions.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure that all fields are filled out according to your requirements.
- Review the form before submitting it to ensure that everything is correct.
- If you're unsure about any field or requirement, contact your sales representative for clarification.
