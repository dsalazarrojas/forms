# Dream Girl Ambassador Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential Dream Girl ambassadors.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. For each field, please follow the explanations below for guidance.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the program you are applying for.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): Please enter a brief description of your experience and qualifications.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): Please enter your location.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, `select_one`, optional): Please select your social media presence (Yes or No).
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, `date`, optional): Please enter the date of the agreement.
* **Agreement Time** (`agreement_time`, `time`, optional): Please enter the time of the agreement.
* **Agreement Duration** (`agreement_duration`, `text`, optional): Please enter the duration of the agreement.
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, optional): Please enter your role.
* **Agreement Amount** (`agreement_amount`, `number`, optional): Please enter the amount of the agreement.
* **Agreement Status** (`agreement_status`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the status of the agreement (Yes or No).
* **Agreement End Date** (`agreement_end_date`, `date`, optional): Please enter the end date of the agreement.
* **Agreement End Time** (`agreement_end_time`, `time`, optional): Please enter the end time of the agreement.
* **Agreement Notes** (`agreement_notes`, `note`, optional): Please enter any agreement notes.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, `text`, optional): Please enter any agreement terms.
* **Agreement Reference** (`agreement_reference`, `text`, optional): Please enter any agreement reference.
* **Agreement Signed** (`agreement_signed`, `select_one`, optional): Please select whether the agreement is signed (Yes or No).
* **Agreement Start Date** (`agreement_start_date`, `date`, optional): Please enter the start date of the agreement.
* **Agreement Start Time** (`agreement_start_time`, `time`, optional): Please enter the start time of the agreement.
* **Agreement Signed By** (`agreement_signed_by`, `text`, optional): Please enter your signed by title.
* **Agreement Signed At** (`agreement_signed_at`, `time`, optional): Please enter the signed time of the agreement.
* **Agreement Signed By Role** (`agreement_signed_by_role`, `text`, optional): Please enter your signed by role.
* **Agreement Signed By Title** (`agreement_signed_by_title`, `text`, optional): Please enter your signed by title.
* **Agreement Signed At Title** (`agreement_signed_at_title`, `text`, optional): Please enter your signed at title.
