# Scent Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scent Order Form is designed to collect customer information and order details for efficient processing and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your company name in the Company field.
4. Enter your email address in the Email field.
5. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
6. Select the type of scent you would like to order from the Scent Type field.
7. Enter the quantity of scent you would like to order.
8. Provide any additional notes in the Note field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Company** (text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address in the format of [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com).
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Product** (text, required): Enter the type of product you would like to order.
* **Quantity** (number, required): Enter the quantity of the product you would like to order.
* **Scent Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of scent you would like to order from the options provided.
* **Note** (note, optional): Provide any additional information or comments about your order.
