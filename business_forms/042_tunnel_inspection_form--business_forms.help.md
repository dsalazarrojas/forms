# Tunnel Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tunnel Inspection Form is a tool used to collect information on the condition of a tunnel during an inspection. It helps maintenance personnel assess the tunnel's structural integrity, identify safety concerns, and determine maintenance needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the fields and their purpose.
2. Complete all required fields (indicated by the "Required" label).
3. Provide accurate and relevant information in each field.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Tunnel Condition Assessment** (`tunnel_condition_assessment`, `text`, Optional): Provide a brief description of the tunnel's overall condition.
- **Structural Integrity** (`structural_integrity`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the current structural integrity of the tunnel (e.g., "Option 1: Good", "Option 2: Fair", "Option 3: Poor", etc.).
- **Safety Concerns** (`safety_concerns`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select any safety concerns or hazards present in the tunnel.
- **Maintenance Needs** (`maintenance_needs`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the maintenance needs of the tunnel (e.g., "Option 1: Routine Maintenance", "Option 2: Minor Repair", etc.).
- **Inspection Comments** (`inspection_comments`, `note`, Optional): Provide any additional comments or observations about the inspection.
- **Assigned Inspector** (`assigned_inspector`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the inspector assigned to conduct the inspection.
- **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, `date`, Optional): Enter the date of the inspection.
- **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, `time`, Optional): Enter the time of the inspection.
- **Tunnel ID** (`tunnel_id`, `text`, Optional): Enter the tunnel's identification number.
- **Tunnel Location** (`tunnel_location`, `text`, Optional): Enter the tunnel's location.
- **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter your name as the inspector conducting the assessment.
