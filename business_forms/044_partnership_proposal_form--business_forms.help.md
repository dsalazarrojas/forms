# Partnership Proposal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Partnership Proposal Form is used to gather information about potential partners and their business plans. This information will be used to review and assess the suitability of potential partnerships for our company.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the details of Party A, including their legal name and contact information.
2. Enter the details of Party B, including their legal name and contact information.
3. Answer the questions about the business plan, such as whether it exists and its current status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Party A** (`party_a`): A short description of the partner (e.g., company name, individual name)
  - This is the name of Party A. Please enter the name of the organization or individual.

- **Party A Legal Name** (`party_a_legal_name`): The legal name of the organization or individual
  - This is the formal name of Party A as it appears on their documentation or registration.

- **Party A Address** (`party_a_address`): The address of Party A
  - Enter the street address of Party A.

- **Party A Phone** (`party_a_phone`): The phone number of Party A
  - Enter the phone number of Party A, in the format of 10 digits.

- **Party A Email** (`party_a_email`): The email address of Party A
  - Enter the email address of Party A.

- **Party B** (`party_b`): A short description of the partner (e.g., company name, individual name)
  - This is the name of Party B. Please enter the name of the organization or individual.

- **Party B Legal Name** (`party_b_legal_name`): The legal name of the organization or individual
  - This is the formal name of Party B as it appears on their documentation or registration.

- **Party B Address** (`party_b_address`): The address of Party B
  - Enter the street address of Party B.

- **Party B Phone** (`party_b_phone`): The phone number of Party B
  - Enter the phone number of Party B, in the format of 10 digits.

- **Party B Email** (`party_b_email`): The email address of Party B
  - Enter the email address of Party B.

- **Business Plan** (`business_plan`): Whether a business plan exists
  - Check "Yes" if a business plan exists, or "No" otherwise.

- **Business Plan Details** (`business_plans_details`): Additional information about the business plan
  - If a business plan exists, provide additional details about it.

- **Business Plan Duration** (`business_plans_duration`): The duration of the business plan
  - Check "Yes" if the business plan is valid, or "No" otherwise.

- **Business Plan Status** (`business_plans_status`): The current status of the business plan
  - Check "Active" if the business plan is active, or "Inactive" otherwise.
