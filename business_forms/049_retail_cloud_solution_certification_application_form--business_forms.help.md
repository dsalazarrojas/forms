# Retail Cloud Solution Certification Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is for retail business customers to apply for certification of their cloud solutions. It helps us understand your solution, assess its compatibility, and facilitate a smooth certification process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Provide your contact information to help us get in touch with you and ensure we have a clear communication channel.
2. Describe your cloud solution, including details about its features and functionalities.
3. Attach supporting documentation to help us evaluate your solution.
4. Provide the dates and times when you submitted your solution for certification.
5. Specify who submitted your solution (you or someone from your team).
6. Choose the category, status, and type of your solution.
7. Enter the name, address, and contact information of your vendor or technology team.
8. Enter the name and description of your solution.
9. (Optional) Enter additional submission dates and times for any follow-up submissions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Page 1)**:
	+ **Contact Information (Contact Information, text, required)**: Enter your name, email, and phone number to enable us to contact you effectively.
* **Solution Details (Page 1)**:
	+ **Solution Description (Solution Description, text, required)**: Briefly describe your cloud solution, highlighting its key features and benefits.
* **Supporting Documentation (Page 1)**:
	+ **Supporting Documentation (Supporting Documentation, text, required)**: Attach relevant documentation such as user manuals, technical guides, and other resources that support your solution.
* **Submission Date 1 (Page 2)**:
	+ **Submission Date 1 (Submission Date 1, date, required)**: Enter the date when you submitted your solution for certification.
* **Submission Time 1 (Page 2)**:
	+ **Submission Time 1 (Submission Time 1, time, required)**: Enter the time when you submitted your solution for certification.
* **Submitter (Page 2)**:
	+ **Submitter (Submitter, select_one, required)**: Indicate who submitted your solution (you or someone from your team).
* **Solution Category (Page 2)**:
	+ **Solution Category (Solution Category, select_multiple, required)**: Choose the category of your solution (Cloud, Non-Cloud, Hybrid).
* **Solution Status (Page 2)**:
	+ **Solution Status (Solution Status, select_one, required)**: Choose the status of your solution (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Solution Type (Page 2)**:
	+ **Solution Type (Solution Type, select_multiple, required)**: Choose the type of your solution (Retail, Non-Retail).
* **Submission Type (Page 2)**:
	+ **Submission Type (Submission Type, select_one, required)**: Choose the type of submission (Full, Incremental, None).
* **Vendor Information (Page 3)**:
	+ **Vendor Name (Vendor Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the vendor that developed your solution.
	+ **Vendor Address (Vendor Address, text, required)**: Enter the address of the vendor that developed your solution.
	+ **Vendor Contact (Vendor Contact, text, required)**: Enter the contact information for the vendor that developed your solution.
* **Technology Team (Page 4)**:
	+ **Technology Team Name (Technology Team, text, required)**: Enter the name of your technology team.
	+ **Technology Team Address (Technology Team Address, text, required)**: Enter the address of your technology team.
	+ **Technology Team Contact (Technology Team Contact, text, required)**: Enter the contact information for your technology team.
* **Solution Details 2 (Page 4)**:
	+ **Solution Name (Solution Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of your solution.
	+ **Solution Description (Solution Description, text, required)**: Enter a detailed description of your solution.
* **Additional Submission Information (Page 5)**:
	+ **Submission Date 2 (Submission Date 2, date, optional)**: Enter any additional submission dates for follow-up submissions.
	+ **Submission Time 2 (Submission Time 2, time, optional)**: Enter any additional submission times for follow-up submissions.
	+ **Submission Date 3 (Submission Date 3, date, optional)**: Enter any additional submission dates for follow-up submissions.
	+ **Submission Time 3 (Submission Time 3, time, optional)**: Enter any additional submission times for follow-up submissions.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Attach all supporting documentation required for a thorough evaluation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form is for certification purposes only, and it will help us assess your solution's compatibility and feasibility for our customers.
